Software Engineer - Department of Computer Science
We are seeking a highly capable and motivated Software Engineer to design and develop a state-of-the-art scalable infrastructure for digital narratives.

This work is in connection with research on Digital Narratives (ELIAS) at the Pioneer Centre for AI in Copenhagen.

Place of employment
Pioneer Centre for Artificial Intelligence (P1) at the Department of Computer Science, University of Copenhagen, located in the Observatory, Øster Voldgade 3.

P1 gathers international multidisciplinary talent offering a creative and stimulating working environment.

Subject
The project involves developing an annotation frontend platform for social media narratives, alongside a backend ontology database.

Details
The candidate will be responsible for developing a software infrastructure constituting three components:

Annotator’s view: a website (and app) for data collection and annotation.
Researcher’s view: a dashboard for data aggregation and monitoring.
Backend: a fast robust safe database with flexible ontologies.
Advising
Ioannis Siglidis (Postdoc; P1), Maria Antoniak (Assistant Professor; CU Boulder), Serge Belongie (Director; P1)

Profile
We are looking for a highly motivated candidate with following competencies and experience:

Master’s Degree in digital humanities with demonstrated computer engineering skills. or
Master’s Degree in computer science or engineering with demonstrated interest in digital humanities.
General Interests: Web/Platform Design, Narrative Research, Deep Learning, Academic thinking.
Fluent in English, written and spoken.
