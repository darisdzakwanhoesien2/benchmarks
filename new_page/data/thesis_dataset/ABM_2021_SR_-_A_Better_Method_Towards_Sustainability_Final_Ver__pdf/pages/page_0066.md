Gross market-based energy indirect Scope 2 GHG emissions | ABM menggunakan listrik dari Perusahaan Listrik Negara (PLN) untuk seluruh area operasional kami di Indonesia. Karena PLN adalah penyedia tunggal dari energi listrik di Indonesia, maka perhitungan market-based indirect scope 2 GHG emission nilainya sama dengan location-based indirect scope 2 GCG emission.

ABM uses electricity from the Government-Owned Electricity Company (PLN) for all of our operational areas in Indonesia. Since PLN is the only provider of electrical energy in Indonesia, the market-based indirect GHG emission calculations are the same as the location-based GCG scope 2 emissions.  |
