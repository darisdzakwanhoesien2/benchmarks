# Evaluasi Pendekatan Manajemen [GRI 103-3]

Kebijakan pengendalian emisi GRK dievaluasi secara berkala dengan melakukan perbandingan dari tahun ke tahun. Tanggung jawab dilakukan oleh fungsi Safety, Health and Environmental (SHE) dan dievaluasi Direksi melalui mekanisme penilaian pelaporan berkala kepada pihak-pihak berwenang serta pencapaian Key Performance Indicator (KPI).

# Management Approach Evaluation [GRI 103-3]

GHG emission control policies are evaluated periodically by making comparisons from year to year. These responsibilities are carried out by the Occupational Safety, Health and Environment (SHE) function and evaluated by the Board of Directors on a regular basis through an assessment mechanism, which is recorded to the authorities and the achievement of Key Performance Indicators (KPI).
