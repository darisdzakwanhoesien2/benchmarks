# Lokasi Operasi [GRI 102-4]

Seluruh Kegiatan operasi dan usaha ABM berada di Indonesia dan tidak memiliki wilayah operasi dan usaha di luar Indonesia.

# Location of Operations [GRI 102-4]

All of ABM's operations and business activities are located in Indonesia and does not have any operating and business areas outside Indonesia.

# Pasar yang dilayani [GRI 102-6]

Untuk pemasaran batubara campuran, ABM memasarkan kepada perusahaan pembangkit listrik di pasar domestik maupun luar negeri seperti Tiongkok, Thailand, Vietnam, dan Filipina. Sedangkan pemasaran dalam skala komersial, ABM memasarkan di pasar domestik dan luar negeri seperti India, Tiongkok dan beberapa negara di Asia Tenggara. Untuk produk jasa dan suplai bahan bakar, ABM melayani pasar dalam negeri.

# Markets served [GRI 102-6]

For blended coal, ABM coal to power generation companies in domestic and foreign markets such as China, Thailand, Vietnam, and the Philippines. While marketing on a commercial scale, ABM markets it in domestic and foreign markets such as India, China and several countries in Southeast Asia. For fuel supply services product, ABM serve domestic market only.
