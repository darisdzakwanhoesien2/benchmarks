# BIDANG EKONOMI ECONOMY SECTOR

## Pertanian Binaan

Dalam bidang Pemberdayaan Masyarakat, CSR PT Sentul City Tbk juga melakukan binaan terhadap petani yang memanfaatkan lahan Sentul City.

Salah satunya ialah pertanian cabai di Cijayanti yang bekerja sama dengan BUMDesa setempat dengan lahan seluas lebih dari 5000 meter persegi. Pada kegiatan Bogor Keliling Senin 23 Mei 2023, Plt. Bupati Bogor Iwan Setiawan sempat meninjau panen raya pertanian cabai ini.

## Community Empowerment in Agriculture

In the field of Community Empowerment, CSR PT Sentul City Tbk also provides assistance to farmers utilizing land within Sentul City.

One of them is chili farming in Cijayanti, which collaborates with the local Village-Owned Enterprises (BUMDesa) on a land area of more than 5000 square meters. During the Bogor Keliling activity on Monday, May 23, 2023, the Acting Regent of Bogor, Iwan Setiawan, visited the chili farming harvest.

## Pelatihan Kreasi Baki Hantaran

Dalam rangka Hari Kartini dan mengisi waktu di bulan Ramadan, CSR Sentul City mengadakan Pelatihan Kreasi Baki Hantaran bagi orangtua siswa PAUD Sentul Maju Saluyu di Sudi (14 April 2023) dan Cibimbin (15 April 2023).

Kegiatan ini diikuti oleh sekitar 60 ibu-ibu di 2 lokasi tersebut. Bunda Santi (guru PAUD SMS Cibimbin) menjadi pelatih untuk Kreasi Baki Hantaran. Diharapkan melalui pelatihan ini, kaum ibu dapat menambah penghasilan untuk kebutuhan rumah tangga.

## Tray Decoration Training

In commemoration of Kartini Day and to fill the time during the month of Ramadan, CSR Sentul City organized Tray Decoration Training for parents of PAUD Sentul Maju Saluyu students in Sudi (April 14, 2023) and Cibimbin (April 15, 2023).

Around 60 mothers participated in this activity at the two locations. Bunda Santi (teacher at PAUD SMS Cibimbin) served as the trainer for Tray Decoration. It is hoped that through this training, mothers can increase their income for household needs.
