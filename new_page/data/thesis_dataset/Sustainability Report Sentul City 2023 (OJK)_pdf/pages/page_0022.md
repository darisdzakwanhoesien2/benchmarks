a. Policies to respond to challenges in meeting sustainability strategies

1. Sustainability values owned by the Company Since the beginning of the planning and development of the Sentul City area, it has been reflected and directed to maintain a green area by planting and cultivating various types of plants in order to keep and at the same time guard a beautiful, green and fresh environmental condition throughout the year. Maintaining and guarding environmental conditions in a sustainable manner has become a Company's commitment.

The development of the Sentul City area which includes the construction of housing, hotels, Trade Centers and Tourist Areas as well as other Supporting Facilities carried out by the Company continues to provide social facilities and public facilities in the form of roads, facilities and green open spaces with an area of approximately 40% to 60% of the built area.

Developing the area while still paying attention to the percentages mentioned above will have a positive impact on addressing climate change, which is one of the Sustainable Development Goals. The contribution to addressing climate change will be very significant if all stakeholders are aware of and willing to voluntarily implement a sustainable culture consistently.

The Company in developing the Sentul City area always refers to the mission, namely Developing Sentul City with quality and innovative housing that is in harmony with the natural environment. The Company sees that the Mission is appropriate and in line with the Sustainable Development Goals. Despite this, implementing the Company's mission requires hard work from planning to execution in the field.
