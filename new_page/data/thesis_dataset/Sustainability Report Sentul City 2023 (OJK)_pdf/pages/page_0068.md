## Number of Products Recall

Throughout the reporting period, there were no products recalled by the Company. As explained in relation to the Commitment to Providing Equivalent Product Services to Consumers, the Company provides a 100-day building maintenance guarantee from the signing of the Handover Deed for Land and Buildings. If during this period, the consumer finds damage and/or discrepancies between the building and the Building Layout Drawing and Technical Specifications, the consumer has the right to complain and request repairs without being charged.

Currently, the Company provides enhanced services for equivalent products to consumers. For new products, such as the Centronia Ruko cluster, Centronia Rumah, Parkville, and Spring Valley Extension, a 24-month building warranty is provided. This means that the Company offers a 2-year Building Maintenance Guarantee from the signing of the Handover Report for Land and Buildings. If, within this period, consumers find any damage and/or discrepancies between the building and the Building Layout Plan and Technical Specifications, consumers have the right to complain and request repairs at no cost. The provision of Equivalent Product Services to Consumers is offered for the purchase of the same product. Consumers who purchase the Company's products will receive buildings with the same specifications.
