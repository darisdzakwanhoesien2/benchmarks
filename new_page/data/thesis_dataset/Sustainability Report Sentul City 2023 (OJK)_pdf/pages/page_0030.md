TATA KELOLA KEBERLANJUTAN SUSTAINABILITY GOVERNANCE

Tata Kelola keberlanjutan memuat informasi terkait komitmen perusahaan dalam menjalankan tata kelola keberlanjutan dengan memperhatikan aspek ekonomi, lingkungan hidup, dan sosial.

1. Penanggung Jawab Penerapan Keuangan Berkelanjutan

Penanggungjawab penerapan keberlanjutan Perseroan dirangkap oleh Direktur yang membawahi bidang Perijinan dan Corporate Secretary yang bertanggung jawab dalam kebijakan keberlanjutan mengkoordinir praktik keberlanjutan yang dilaksanakan oleh divisi terkait dan mengelola lalu lintas data dan informasi terkait keberlanjutan. Dalam melaksanakan tugasnya Direktur yang membawahi bidang Perijinan dan Corporate Secretary dibantu oleh bagian terkait dengan keberlanjutan yang bertanggungjawab pada Direktur Perijinan dan Corporate Secretary.

Mengingat masalah keberlanjutan merupakan kegiatan lintas sektor Perseroan mengeluarkan kebijakan agar unit kerja yang terkait kegiatan dan masalah keberlanjutan memberikan dukungan penuh termasuk memberikan data yang diperlukan kepada unit kerja Corporate Secretary yang bertugas menyusun Laporan Keberlanjutan. Seiring dengan kampanye keberlanjutan yang dilakukan oleh Pemerintah dan pihak lainnya, diharapkan dari waktu ke waktu sumber daya manusia di bawah organisasi Perseroan memiliki pengetahuan dan budaya keberlanjutan yang terus meningkat dengan muara akhir memberi kontribusi terkait percepatan Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan.

2. Pengembangan Kompetensi Terkait Keuangan Berkelanjutan

Secara terencana dan berkesinambungan anggota Direksi dan anggota Dewan Komisaris Perseroan belum mengikuti pengembangan kompetensi di bidang keberlanjutan. Namun begitu sebagian anggota Direksi dan anggota Dewan Komisaris telah terlibat pembicaraan dan diskusi terkait bidang keberlanjutan serta memiliki perhatian yang cukup tinggi atas pengembangan bidang keberlanjutan. Pada tahun 2023 bagian Corporate Secretary mengikuti berbagai pelatihan terkait penerapan bidang Keberlanjutan dan Laporan Keberlanjutan yang diselenggarakan oleh berbagai institusi.

Sustainability Governance contains information related to the company's commitment to implementing sustainable governance by taking into account economic, environmental and social aspects.

1. Responsible for the Implementation of Sustainable Finance

The person in charge of implementing the Company's sustainability is concurrently the Director in charge of Licensing and the Corporate Secretary who is responsible for the sustainability policy, coordinating the sustainability practices implemented by the relevant divisions and managing the traffic of data and information related to sustainability. In carrying out its duties, the Director in charge of Licensing and Corporate Secretary is assisted by the section related to sustainability who is responsible to the Licensing Director and Corporate Secretary.

Considering that sustainability issues are a cross-sectoral activity of the Company, policies are issued to ensure that the work units related to sustainability activities and issues provide full support, including providing necessary data to the Corporate Secretary unit tasked with compiling the Sustainability Report. Along with the sustainability campaign conducted by the Government and other parties, it is expected that over time the human resources under the Company's organization will have continuously increasing knowledge and culture of sustainability, which will ultimately contribute to accelerating the achievement of Sustainable Development Goals.

2. Competency Development Related to Sustainable Finance

Planned and sustainable development of competencies in the field of sustainability has not been undertaken by members of the Board of Directors and members of the Board of Commissioners of the Company. However, some members of the Board of Directors and members of the Board of Commissioners have been involved in discussions and discussions related to sustainability and have a high level of attention to the development of sustainability. In 2023, the Corporate Secretary department participated in various training related to the implementation of sustainability and Sustainability Reports held by various institutions.
