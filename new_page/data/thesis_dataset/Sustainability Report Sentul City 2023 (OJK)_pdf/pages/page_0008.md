# Sosial Aspect

Perseroan dari waktu ke waktu berupaya untuk mendukung dan mengembangkan program sosial kemasyarakatan di sekitar Perseroan beroperasi dan tempat lain baik bekerjasama dengan pihak ketiga maupun secara swadaya.

The Company has been striving from time to time to support and develop community social programs in and around the area where The Company operates and other places, either by collaborating with third parties or voluntarily.

Perseroan juga memberikan perhatian terhadap pemberdayaan masyarakat sekitar. Berkenaan dengan hal tersebut Perseroan telah menyelenggarakan berbagai kegiatan sosial yang bersentuhan langsung dengan warga masyarakat sekitar wilayah Babakan Madang dan tempat lain. Kegiatan sosial kemasyarakatan yang didukung Perseroan antara lain menyangkut bidang Pendidikan, Pemberdayaan Perempuan dan Lingkungan.

The Company also pays attention to empowering the surrounding community. In this regard, the company has organized various social activities that directly involve the residents of the Babakan Madang area and other places. The community social activities supported by The Company include education, women's empowerment, and environmental issues.

Untuk meminimalisir dampak negatif kegiatan Perseroan, secara bertahap Perseroan terus melakukan berbagai perbaikan terakat pengelolaan limbah. Pengelolaan limbah yang dilakukan Perseroan telah melibatkan tenaga kerja lokal disekitar tempat Perseroan beroperasi khususnya disekitar tempat pengelolaan limbah. Selain dari pada itu Perseroan juga telah banyak mempekerjakan tenaga kerja lokal di sekitar tempat Perseroan beroperasi, yang ditempatkan pada berbagai unit kerja.

Selama periode pelaporan Perseroan tidak terdapat laporan pelanggaran terhadap peraturan lingkungan hidup.

To minimize the negative impact of the company's activities, The Company continues to gradually make various improvements related to waste management. The waste management carried out by The Company has involved local workers around the company's operating area, especially around the waste management area. In addition, The Company has also employed many local workers around its operating area, who are placed in various work units.

During the reporting period, there were no reports of violations of environmental regulations by The Company.
