# BIDANG SOSIAL SOCIAL SECTOR

## Bakti Sosial – Korban Banjir

Pada Rabu 7 Juni 2023, PT Sentul City Tbk mengadakan bakti sosial pembagian sembako di Kantor Desa Katang Tengah, untuk meringankan beban korban bencana tanah longsor dan banjir lumpur.

Bantuan diserahkan kepada pengurus Desa maupun warga, terdampak, oleh Tim CSR Sentul City dan perwakilan Divisi Land.

## Social Service – Flood Victims

On Wednesday, June 7, 2023, PT Sentul City Tbk held a social service event distributing groceries at the Katang Tengah Village Office to alleviate the burden of landslide and mud flood victims.

The assistance was handed over to the village officials and affected residents by the Sentul City CSR Team and representatives from the Land Division.

## Bakti Sosial – Korban Kebakaran

Pada Selasa 27 Juni 2023, PT Sentul City Tbk menyalurkan santunan kepada keluarga bapak Kemal (salah satu staf) yang menjadi korban bencana kebakaran, di Sukaraja.

Pada 2 Agustus 2023 telah terjadi kebakaran yang menimpa 3 rumah keluarga Dedi Setiawan, Toha, dan Moh. Ridwan Rifai di Kp. Gunung Batu, Desa Bojong Koneng.

Bantuan berupa bahan pokok makanan, pakaian, dan uang tunai diserahkan oleh Tim CSR dan perwakilan dari Pimpinan Divisi Land Bapak Yuli Dwi Kusmadi, langsung di lokasi tempat kejadian dengan tujuan untuk meringankan beban penderitaan mereka.

## Social Service – Fire Victims

On Tuesday, June 27, 2023, PT Sentul City Tbk provided assistance to the family of Mr. Kemal (one of the staff members) who were victims of a fire disaster in Sukaraja.

On August 2, 2023, a fire broke out affecting the homes of three families: Dedi Setiawan, Toha, and Moh. Ridwan Rifai in Gunung Batu Village, Bojong Koneng.

The aid, consisting of basic food supplies, clothing, and cash, was delivered by the CSR Team and representatives from the Land Division led by Mr. Yuli Dwi Kusmadi, directly at the scene of the incident, with the aim of alleviating their suffering.
