## 2. Prestasi dan Peristiwa Penting Pada Periode Pelaporan

Sepenjang tahun 2023 Perseroan berhasil melaksanakan Launching penjualan antara lain untuk kluster Centronia Residence, Parkville, Spring Valley Extension dan Spring Residence yang memperoleh tanggapan positif dari konsumen. Diharapkan dengan Launching - Launching tersebut memberikan dampak positif bagi Perseroan untuk terus berupaya mempersembahkan dan mengembangkan produk yang baik, kekinian dan sesuai kebutuhan konsumen serta pasar properti.

## 2. Achievements and Important Events in the Reporting Period

Throughout 2023, the Company successfully launched sales for several clusters, including Centronia Residence, Parkville, Spring Valley Extension, and Spring Residence, which received a positive response from consumers. It is hoped that these launches will have a positive impact on the Company's continued efforts to present and develop products that are good, contemporary, and meet the needs of consumers and the property market.
