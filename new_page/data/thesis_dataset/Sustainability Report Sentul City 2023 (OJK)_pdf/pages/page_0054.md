## Upah Minimum Regional

Upah adalah hak karyawan yang diterima dan dinyatakan dalam bentuk uang sebagai imbalan dari pengusaha atau pemberi kerja kepada Karyawan yang ditetapkan dan dibayarkan menurut suatu perjanjian kerja, kesepakatan, atau peraturan perundang-undangan, termasuk tunjangan bagi pekerja atas suatu pekerjaan dan/atau jasa yang telah dilakukan.

Karyawan yang bekerja pada Perseroaan menerima upah yang sesuai dengan golongan, jabatan, pangkat dan prestasi kerja. Elemen utama dalam menentukan tingkat upah jabatan tertentu adalah kualifikasi yang dibutuhkan, tingkat pendidikan, tanggung jawab, kondisi pekerjaan, perbandingan dengan kompetitor industri sejenis di pasar kerja. Berkenaan dengan hal tersebut Perseroaan mengenal tingkat kepangkatan dan golongan yang diatur dalam Surat Keputusan Direksi.

## Regional Minimum Wage

Wages are an employee's right to receive compensation in the form of money from their employer. This compensation is determined and paid according to an employment agreement, contract, or statutory regulations. Wages also include allowances for workers for work and/or services that have been performed.

Employees who work for the company receive a wage or basic salary according to their rank and position. The main element in determining the wage level of a position is the required qualifications, level of education, responsibility, job conditions, and comparison with similar industry competitors in the labor market. In this regard, the company recognizes the rank and grade levels as stipulated in the Director's Decree.

## Components of Employee Wages:

1. Basic Salary is the fundamental wage paid to an employee based on their job level or type of work.
2. Position Allowance is a structural or functional allowance provided to employees holding specific positions. Position allowances may be discontinued when an employee no longer holds the relevant position.
3. Transportation Allowance is a monetary compensation provided to employees to cover their commuting expenses between their residence and workplace.
