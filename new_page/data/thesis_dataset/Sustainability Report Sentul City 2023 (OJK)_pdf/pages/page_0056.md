## Pelaksanaan Cuti Bersalin

Perseroan mengatur terkait Cuti Bersalin dan Gugur Kandungan sesuai ketentuan yang berlaku yang antara lain mengatur sebagai berikut :

1. Pekerja wanita yang melahirkan diberi hak cuti/istirahat selama :
a. $1 \frac{1}{2}$ bulan, sebelum saatnya melahirkan anak, dan $1 \frac{1}{2}$ bulan, sesudah saatnya melahirkan anak, atau;
b. Akumulasi 3 bulan baik sebelum atau sesudah melahirkan, sesuai kesepakatan antara Perseroan dengan karyawan.
2. Dalam hal pekerja wanita yang mengalami keguguran kandungan diberikan hak cuti/istirahat sesuai dengan surat keterangan dokter atau bidan yang merawatnya, maksimal selama $1 \frac{1}{2}$ (satu setengah) bulan.
3. Cuti bersalin sebagaimana ketentuan-ketentuan tersebut diatas tetap mendapat upah penuh.
4. Apabila Karena alasan medis diperlukan waktu lebih dari yang ditentukan dapat diberikan berdasarkan surat keterangan dokter dan dalam masa perpanjangan cuti ini berlaku ketentuan upah selama sakit.

## Maternity Leave Implementation

The company regulates Maternity Leave and Miscarriage Leave in accordance with applicable provisions, including the following:

1. Female workers who give birth are entitled to a leave/rest for:
a. $1 \frac{1}{2}$ months before giving birth, and $1 \frac{1}{2}$ months after giving birth; or
b. Accumulation of 3 months before or after giving birth, as agreed between the company and the employee.
2. In the event of a miscarriage, female workers are entitled to a leave/rest in accordance with a doctor's or midwife's certificate, for a maximum of $1 \frac{1}{2}$ months.
3. Maternity leave as stipulated above is still entitled to full pay.
4. If a longer time is needed due to medical reasons, it can be granted based on a doctor's certificate, and during the extension of leave, sick pay rules apply.
5. Extension of maternity leave without a doctor's certificate will result in a deduction from annual leave entitlement or, if there is no remaining annual leave entitlement, will be considered as unpaid leave.
