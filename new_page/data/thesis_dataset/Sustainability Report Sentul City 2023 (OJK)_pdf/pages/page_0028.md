## 3. Penjelasan situasi eksternal

Perseroan secara bertahap telah menelaah strategi untuk mengatasi dampak negatif atas faktor eksternal terkait fluktuasi harga komoditas, perubahan nilai tukar, penurunan daya beli masyarakat, peningkatan biaya energi dan lain-lain.

Berkenaan dengan pengembangan usaha, Perseroan berupaya menghadirkan dan menawarkan produk yang sangat diperlukan dan diminati konsumen dengan harga yang menarik, agar produk dapat dengan cepat terserap habis oleh pasar dalam waktu yang singkat. Hal tersebut mengingat produk yang tidak cepat terserap habis oleh pasar akan menimbulkan persoalan baru yaitu pemeliharaan produk agar tidak rusak dan menurun nilainya. ## 3. Explanation of external situation

Gradually, the Company has been examining strategies to overcome negative impacts from external factors such as commodity price fluctuations, currency exchange rate changes, declining purchasing power of the society, increasing energy costs, and others.

Regarding business development, the Company strives to introduce and offer products that are highly needed and demanded by consumers at attractive prices, so that the products can be quickly absorbed by the market in a short time. This is because products that are not quickly absorbed by the market will create new problems, namely the maintenance of products to prevent damage and decrease in value. Regarding the maintenance of unsold products, it will incur new costs. Therefore, products that are sold quickly will prevent the Company from incurring maintenance costs.
