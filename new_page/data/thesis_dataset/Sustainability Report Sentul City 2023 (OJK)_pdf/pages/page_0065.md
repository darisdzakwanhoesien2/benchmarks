## Hari Peduli Sampah Nasional

Dalam rangka Hari Peduli Sampah Nasional 2023, CSR Sentul City bersama dengan Pemerintah Kecamatan Babakan Madang serta unsur desa Babakan Madang dan Citaringgul melakukan Bersih Sungai Babakan Madang. Citaringgul pada Jumat 24 Februari 2023.

Kegiatan ini diikuti oleh sekitar 150 personil dari SGC Sentul City, Pol PP Linmas, Koramil, Polsek, Serta desa. Dimulai dengan Apel Pag yang dipimpin oleh Sekcam Bapak Iskandar. Sementara itu, Camat Raden Imam Nahrawi juga hadir meninjau pelaksanaan kegiatan ini.

## National Waste Care Day

In commemoration of National Waste Care Day 2023, CSR Sentul City, together with the Babakan Madang Sub-District Government and representatives from Babakan Madang and Citaringgul villages, conducted a Clean-Up of the Babakan Madang River in Citaringgul on Friday, February 24, 2023.

Approximately 150 personnel from SGC Sentul City, Civil Service Police Unit (Pol PP Linmas), District Military Command (Koramil), Police Sector (Polsek), as well as representatives from the villages participated in this activity. It began with a Morning Assembly led by Sub-District Head Mr. Iskandar. Meanwhile, District Head Raden Imam Nahrawi also attended to oversee the implementation of this activity.

## Bantuan Air Bersih

Pada pertengahan bulan Agustus, kemarau mulai dirasakan oleh masyarakat. Banyak sumur kering, bahkan air gunung pun tidak mengalir dengan debit seperti biasanya.

Perseroan melalui tim sosial telah melakukan distribusi air bersih yang diperlukan di Kampung Cicerewed (Cijayanti) pada 16 Agustus 2023, Pasir Maung (Cijayanti) pada 22 Agustus 2023, dan Malingping (Babakan Madang) pada 30 Agustus 2023.

## Clean Water Assistance

In mid-August, the dry season began to be felt by the community. Many wells dried up, and even mountain water did not flow as usual.

The Company through its social team, distributed much-needed clean water in Cicerewed Village (Cijayanti) on August 16, 2023, Pasir Maung (Cijayanti) on August 22, 2023, and Malingping (Babakan Madang) on August 30, 2023.
