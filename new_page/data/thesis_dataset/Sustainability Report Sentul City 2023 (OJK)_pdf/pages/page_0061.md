# BIDANG PENDIDIKAN EDUCATION SECTOR

Peresmian Gedung PAUD

Pada tanggal 7 Desember 2023, pihak Sentul City bersama pihak instansi pemerintahan yaitu: Carnat Babakan Madang, Kadiv Pendidikan, Kepala Desa Bojong Koneng bersama Staf, Penilik, Tokoh Agama dan Santri melakukan kegiatan peresmian Gedung Paud Sentul Maju Saluyu yang terletak di Ds. Bojongkoneng, Kp. Sudi.

Acara ini terdiri beberapa kegiatan yaitu: Pemotongan tumpeng, persembahan penampilan dari perwakilan anak murid berupa tarian, dan setelah itu di lanjutkan dengan melihat keadaan ruangan yang sudah dibangun.

Inauguration of Early Childhood Education Building

On December 7, 2023, Sentul City, together with government agencies such as the Subdistrict Chief of Babakan Madang, the Education Division Head, the Head of Bojong Koneng Village along with staff, supervisors, religious leaders, and students, conducted the inauguration of the Sentul Maju Saluyu Early Childhood Education Building located in Bojongkoneng Village, Sudi Hamlet.

The event comprised several activities, including the cutting of the tumpeng (cone-shaped rice dish), performances by student representatives in the form of dances, followed by an inspection of the newly constructed rooms.

Makan Sehat Hari Gizi dan Makanan Nasional

Untuk mengedukasi dan memberi asupan sehat bagi anak-anak PAUD, maka secara rutin setiap bulan diadakan kegiatan Makan Sehat di semua PAUD Sentul Maju Saluyu.

Bulan Januari 2023, Makan Sehat dilaksanakan bertepatan dengan Hari Gizi dan Makanan Nasional yaitu pada tanggal 25 untuk PAUD SMS Sudi dan Cibimbin tanggal 30 untuk PAUD Tegaluhur.

Untuk bulan ini, menu makanan sehat adalah Bubur Kacang Hijau. Makanan sederhana ini mengandung gizi dan nutrisi yang baik untuk tumbuh kembang anak.

Healthy Eating on Nutrition and National Food Day

To educate and provide healthy nutrition for preschool children, a Healthy Eating activity is routinely held every month at all Sentul Maju Saluyu Early Childhood Education (PAUD) centers.

In January 2023, Healthy Eating was held to coincide with Nutrition and National Food Day on the 25th for PAUD SMS Sudi and Cibimbin, and on the 30th for PAUD Tegaluhur.

For this month, the healthy food menu is Green Bean Porridge. This simple food contains nutrients that are good for children's growth and development.
