# BIDANG PENDIDIKAN EDUCATION SECTOR

Kunjungan Ke POLSEK - Polisi Sahabat Anak Pada Rabu 14 Juni 2023, siswa PAUD SMS Cibimbin dan Sudi mengikuti kegiatan Polisi Sahabat Anak, yaitu pengenalan profesi polisi di Mapolsek Babakan Madang.

Lebih dari 40 siswa mengikuti kegiatan yang disambut langsung oleh Kapolsek Babakan Madang AKP Susilo Tri Wibowo.

Dalam kegiatan tersebut, dikenalkan profesi polisi serta rambu-rambu lalu lintas. Siswa yang berseragam seperti polisi mengikuti dengan sangat antusias.

Visit to the POLSEK - Children's Police Friends On Wednesday, June 14, 2023, students from the Cibimbin and Sudi Early Childhood Education (PAUD) schools participated in the Children's Police Friends activity, which introduced them to the police profession at the Babakan Madang Police Sector Office (POLSEK).

More than 40 students attended the event, which was welcomed by the Head of the Babakan Madang Police Sector, AKP Susilo Tri Wibowo.

During the activity, the police profession and traffic signs were introduced. Students dressed in police uniforms participated with great enthusiasm.

# BIDANG KESEHATAN HEALTH SECTOR

Program Donor Darah Sepanjang tahun 2023 Perseroan melaksanakan 4 kali kegiatan Donor Darah yaitu tanggal 17 Januari, 20 Maret, 19 Juni dan 1 November 2023

Program Donor Darah Keluarga Besar Sentul City menjadi kegiatan rutin yang diadakan dengan interval lebih kurang 75 hari. Program ini bekerjasama dengan PMI Kabupaten Bogor untuk menjaga ketersediaan darah bilamana ada orang yang memerlukan bantuan darah untuk keperluan tindakan medis.

Kegiatan donor darah sukarela ini diikuti oleh jajaran direksi, manajemen, staff, masyarakat warga Sentul City bahkan pelaku usaha yang ada di kawasan komersial Sentul City.

Blood Donation Program Throughout the year 2023, the Company conducted 4 blood donation activities on January 17, March 20, June 19, and November 1, 2023.

The Blood Donation Program of the Sentul City Extended Family has become a routine activity held approximately every 75 days. This program collaborates with the Indonesian Red Cross (PMI) of Bogor Regency to ensure the availability of blood for those in need of medical treatment.

The voluntary blood donation activities were participated in by the board of directors, management, staff, residents of Sentul City, and even business owners in the commercial areas of Sentul City.
