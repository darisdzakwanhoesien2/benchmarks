## 4. Performance Achievements

Amidst various dynamics, the national economic growth of 5.05% in 2023 is a cause for gratitude.

- The Company managed to record a net profit attributable to owners of the parent company of Rp. 332.3 billion.
- In the environmental field, the Company continues to pay great attention to maintaining and preserving various types of trees to keep the Sentul City area green and have fresh air throughout the year.
- In the social field, the Company has succeeded in carrying out various activities to empower the community around the Company's operations. The Company has successfully established partnerships with various parties related to the management of undeveloped land that has not yet been developed by the Company. The implementation of this partnership has created jobs, especially for residents around the location of the land being partnered with.
