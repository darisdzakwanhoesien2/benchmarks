# 3. Komitmen pimpinan dalam menerapkan Keuangan Berkelanjutan

Perseroan berkomitmen dan berupaya agar dalam menjalankan kegiatan usahanya senantiasa sejalan dengan penerapan Keuangan Berkelanjutan. Sehubungan dengan hal tersebut secara bertahap diharapkan diperoleh kesesuaian dan sinergi antara pengembangan bisnis Perseroan dengan Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan sehingga karenanya Perseroan dapat semakin berkontribusi dan mempercepat tercapainya Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan.

Komitmen tersebut telah diwujudkan oleh Perseroan dalam bentuk pemberdayaan masyarakat sekitar dan kerjasama kemitraan atas pengelolaan lahan Perseroan yang belum dikembangkan baik dengan warga ataupun Usaha Mikro Kecil dan Menengah. Dampak positif dari pemberdayaan dan kerjasama kemitraan tersebut adalah mitra-warga sekitar memperoleh manfaat secara ekonomi dan Perseroan memperoleh manfaat secara sosial dan lingkungan hidup secara berkelanjutan.

The development of the Sentul City area, including the surrounding region, along with the growth of businesses and Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises, has opened up significant employment opportunities. The availability of job opportunities and the absorption of local labor have made a positive contribution, reducing disparities and creating decent work and economic growth. Reducing inequality, decent work, and economic growth are some of the Sustainable Development Goals that require adequate attention from the Company.

The partnership program, including community empowerment and the absorption of local labor, can be used as a means of cementing good relations between the Company and the surrounding community. Therefore, potential social problems can be managed well and avoided as early as possible. As a company with a vast amount of undeveloped land, the Company recognizes and endeavors to maintain good relations with the government and the surrounding community where the Company operates.

# 3. Leadership commitment in implementing Sustainable Finance

The Company is committed and strives to ensure that its business activities are always in line with the implementation of Sustainable Finance. In this regard, it is expected that gradually there will be alignment and synergy between the Company's business development and the Sustainable Development Goals, so that the Company can increasingly contribute and accelerate the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals.

This commitment has been realized by the Company in the form of community empowerment and partnership collaborations for the management of the Company's undeveloped land, with both the local community and Small and Medium Enterprises. The positive impact of this empowerment and partnership collaboration is that the partners and the surrounding community receive economic benefits, while the Company benefits socially and environmentally in a sustainable manner.
