# ASPEK EMISI

## Upaya Pengurangan Emisi yang Dilakukan

Perseroan secara terencana dan terprogram belum memiliki upaya yang komprehensif terkait pengurangan Emisi. Namun begitu Perseroan telah melakukan kegiatan Penghijauan yang berkesinambungan sejak awal pengembangan kawasan sampai dengan saat ini. Penanaman dan perawatan berbagai jenis pohon di kawasan Sentul City dapat memperlambat pelepasan Emisi Karbon.

Selain hal tersebut di atas Perseroan secara berkala melakukan pemeliharaan seluruh kendaraan operasional untuk mengurangi emisi dan meningkatkan efisiensi bahan bakar.

# EMISSION ASPECT

## Efforts to Reduce Emissions

The Company in a planned and programmed manner does not yet have comprehensive efforts related to emission reduction. However, the Company has carried out sustainable reforestation activities since the beginning of the development of the area until now. Planting and caring for various types of trees in the Sentul City area can slow down the release of Carbon Emissions.

In addition to the above, the Company regularly maintains all operational vehicles to reduce emissions and improve fuel efficiency.
