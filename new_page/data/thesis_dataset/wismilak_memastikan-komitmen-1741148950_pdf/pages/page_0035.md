# Pelibatan Pemangku Kepentingan

[102-40] [102-42] [102-43] [102-44]

Pemangku kepentingan merupakan penentu keberlanjutan usaha Perseroan. Wismilak telah mengidentifikasi pemangku kepentingan internal dan eksternal berdasarkan penilaian dan pertimbangan faktor peran, kebutuhan, pengaruh, dan dampak. Hubungan profesional, harmonis, dan strategis merupakan kunci utama Wismilak membangun kepercayaan dan interaksi dengan seluruh pemangku kepentingannya.

# Stakeholder Engagement

[102-40] [102-42] [102-43] [102-44]

Stakeholders are the determinants of the Company's business sustainability. Wismilak has identified internal and external stakeholders based on the assessment and consideration of role, need, influence, and impact factors. A professional, harmonious, and strategic relationship is the main key for Wismilak to build trust and interaction with all its stakeholders.

# Pemegang Saham

Shareholder

Komitmen Perseroan dan tanggung jawab dalam pelaksanaan kinerja usaha

Company's commitment and responsibility in implementing business performance:

- Kinerja usaha dan proyeksi usaha ke depan
- Aksi Korporasi Perseroan
- Business performance and future business projections
Corporate actions

Minimal satu kali dalam setahun:

- Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Tahunan
- Paparan Umum Perseroan

At least once a year:

- Annual Shareholders' General Meeting
- Company's General Exposure

# Investor

Investor

Komitmen dan tanggung jawab dalam keterbukaan informasi

Commitment and responsibility in transparency

- Kinerja usaha dan proyeksi usaha ke depan
- Aksi Korporasi Perseroan
- Business performance and future business projections
Corporate actions

Sesuai Kebutuhan:

- Tatap Muka Investor
- Keterbukaan informasi melalui channel komunikasi resmi Perseroan (situs web)

As Needed:

- Face-to-face Interaction with Investors
- Transparency through Company's official communication channel (website)

# Masyarakat

Community

- Pengembangan masyarakat
- Kontribusi positif terhadap lingkungan sekitar Perseroan
- Community development
Positive contribution to the society

- Tanggung Jawab Sosial dan Lingkungan Perseroan
- Lapangan pekerjaan bagi masyarakat lokal
- Company's Social and Environmental Responsibility
- Jobs for the local community

Minimal satu kali dalam setahun:

- Pelaksanaan program tanggung jawab sosial perusahaan
- Pelibatan masyarakat di sekitar lingkungan Perseroan dalam kegiatan sosial

Sesuai kebutuhan:

Perekrutan sumber daya manusia dari wilayah sekitar area operasional Perseroan sesuai dengan persyaratan

At least once a year:

- Implementation of Corporate Social Responsibility program
- Involving the community around the Company in social activities

As needed:

Recruitment of human resources from the Company's surrounding areas in accordance with requirements
