# Recruitment Process and Employee Turnover

Seluruh proses rekrutmen telah diatur dalam Kebijakan HR-Recruitment 001 serta dilaksanakan sesuai dengan peraturan perundangan yang berlaku dan transparan. Perseroan mengedepankan prinsip kesetaraan dalam memberikan kesempatan bekerja dalam segala aspek. Sepanjang tahun pelaporan, Wismilak mencatat 231 karyawan baru yang terdiri dari 203 Karyawan pria dan 28 Karyawan wanita. Tingkat perputaran karyawan mencapai  $8,6\%$  (dengan proyek),  $8,5\%$  (tanpa proyek). [401-1]

The entire recruitment process is governed by HR-Recruitment Policy 001 and is conducted in accordance with applicable regulations, ensuring transparency. The Company prioritizes the principle of equality in providing employment opportunities in all aspects. During the reporting year, Wismilak hired 231 new employees, comprising 203 male and 28 female employees. The employee turnover rate reached  $8.6\%$  (with projects) and  $8.5\%$  (without projects). [401-1]
