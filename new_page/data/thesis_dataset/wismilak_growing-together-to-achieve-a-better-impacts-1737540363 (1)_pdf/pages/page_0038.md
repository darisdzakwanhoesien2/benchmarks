# Penggunaan Air [F.8] [303-1] [303-3] [303-5]

Water Consumption

Selama tahun pelaporan, Wismilak mencatat penggunaan air terbanyak bersumber dari pihak ketiga yaitu Perusahaan Daerah Air Minum (PDAM). Adapun, air tangki yang berasal dari vendor eksternal digunakan sebagai alternatif jika terjadi gangguan suplai air dari PDAM.

During the reporting year, Wismilak recorded the highest water usage sourced from third parties, namely the Regional Drinking Water Company (PDAM). Additionally, tank water from external vendors was utilized as an alternative in case of water supply disruptions from PDAM.
