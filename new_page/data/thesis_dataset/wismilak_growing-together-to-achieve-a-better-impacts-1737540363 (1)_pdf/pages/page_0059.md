# Occupational Health and Safety (OHS)

Dalam menjalankan kegiatan operasional, kami mengacu pada Sistem Manajemen Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja (SMK3) untuk memastikan seluruh peralatan dan standar operasional prosedur (SOP) pekerjaan telah dievaluasi dan selalu dilaksanakan di bawah supervisi tim sub-departement Safety.

Perseroan melakukan sosialisasi secara berkala untuk meningkatkan kesadaran pekerja akan pentingnya K3 serta identifikasi atas pekerjaan dengan risiko tinggi menggunakan metode Failure Mode and Effect Analysis (FMEA). Sosialisasi yang dilakukan antara lain meliputi sosialisasi prosedur dan mitigasi dampak K3. Dana yang dialokasikan untuk mendukung kegiatan sosialisasi dan pelatihan adalah sebesar Rp56.948.750 sebanyak 119 karyawan telah mengikuti pelatihan dan simulasi K3 sepanjang tahun 2023.

Lebih lanjut, guna mencegah dan memitigasi dampak-dampak keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja, Perseroan telah menyediakan fasilitas kesehatan berupa klinik pratama di Buntaran dan Occupational Health di Bojonegoro. [403-7]

In conducting our operational activities, we adhere to the Occupational Health and Safety Management System (OHSMS) to ensure that all equipment and standard operating procedures (SOP) have been evaluated and are consistently implemented under the supervision of the Safety sub-department team.

The Company conducts periodic socialization to enhance workers' awareness of the importance of OHS, including the identification of high-risk jobs using the Failure Mode and Effect Analysis (FMEA) method. Socialization includes OHS procedures and impact mitigation. An allocated budget of Rp56,948,750 has been used to support socialization and training activities, with 119 employees participating in OHS training and simulations throughout 2023.

Furthermore, to prevent and mitigate occupational safety and health impacts, the Company has provided health facilities in the form of a primary clinic in Buntaran and an Occupational Health clinic in Bojonegoro. [403-7]
