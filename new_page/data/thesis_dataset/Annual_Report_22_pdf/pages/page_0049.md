# Signing of Distribution Agreement between PPG and SMR &amp; Meccaya

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) was appointed and trusted by PT Sarwa Manggalla Raya (SMR) and MECCAYA to distribute products (with brands that have long been known in the market: Salep Kulit 88, Krim 88 Anti Jamur, Meccaderm, and Lukajel), effective from 1st September 2022.
