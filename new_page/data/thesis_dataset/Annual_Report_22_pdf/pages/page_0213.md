# Overview of Sustainability Aspects

Gambaran Umum Aspek Keberlanjutan

## Environment Friendly Innovation

We implement some green technology in carrying out our business processes. The application of green technology applied includes maximizing the use of extracted waste to become compost which is used in the curcuma garden at the SOHO Center of Excellence in Herbal Research (SCEHR) as organic fertilizer, SCEHR is certified as organic practitioner by the Indonesian Organic Farming Certification.

We also started shifting the use of organic solvents to water-based solvents for product coating. Further, we reduced the aluminium foil content of some of our packaging materials, this did not significantly impact the quality of the packaging.

## Partnership with Local Stakeholders

SOHO Global Health is committed to contributing to the socio-economic aspects of farmers and communities. We do this through the development of science and technology with academia through various extraction, pre-clinical (toxicity and efficacy) research and clinical trials and actively provide a resource person support to the Kampus Merdeka learning program, a program by the Ministry of Education and Culture which gives Universities the ability to provide courses and activities that would suit the interests of students outside of the standard curriculum. Several academics and institutions that have become research partners and Kampus Merdeka programs include the Faculty of Pharmacy and the Department of Chemical Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Gadjah Mada University, Yogyakarta, Faculty of Pharmacy, University of Indonesia, Depok, School of Pharmacy, Bandung Institute of Technology, Bogor Agricultural University, Udayana University Faculty of Medicine, as well as the National Research and Innovation Agency (BRIN). Through these various researches, several innovative products have been produced as well as scientific publications in both national and international journals.

We also collaborate with communities around the SOHO Center of Excellence in Herbal Research (SCEHR), such as the Dahlia Women Farmers Group. We are also committed to increasing farmers' income, including through the implementation of Smart Farming 4.0 which was initiated in 2020 and has been applied to all planted areas in the 2021 and 2022 planting year. We also encourage farmers to apply intercropping with sweet corn plants to help the farmers' have more income from crops. We conducted a research related to the cultivation of intercropping during the curcuma cultivation process, where the method of intercropping with sweet corn did not affect the quality of the curcuma produced. In fact intercropping was able to provide a positive impact for farmers, repeated land cultivation enriches the soil so that it gives a positive impact on curcuma cultivation.

## Inovasi Ramah Lingkungan

Kami menerapkan beberapa teknologi hijau dalam menjalankan proses bisnis kami. Penerapan teknologi hijau yang diterapkan antara lain memaksimalkan pemanfaatan limbah hasil ekstraksi menjadi kompos yang digunakan di kebun temulawak di SOHO Center of Excellence in Herbal Research (SCEHR) sebagai pupuk organik, SCEHR bersertifikat praktisi organik oleh Sertifikasi Pertanian Organik Indonesia.

Kami juga mulai mengalihkan penggunaan pelarut organik ke pelarut berbasis air untuk pelapisan produk. Selanjutnya, kami mengurangi kandungan aluminium foil dari beberapa bahan kemasan kami, hal ini tidak berdampak signifikan terhadap kualitas kemasan.

## Kemitraan dengan Pemangku Kepentingan Lokal

SOHO Global Health berkomitmen untuk berkontribusi pada aspek sosial ekonomi petani dan masyarakat serta terlibat dalam pengembangan ilmu pengetahuan dan teknologi bersama akademisi melalui berbagai riset ekstraksi, pre klinik (toksisitas dan efikasi) serta uji klinik dan aktif menjadi narasumber pada program pembelajaran Kampus Merdeka, sebuah program oleh Kementerian Pendidikan dan Kebudayaan yang memberikan hak kepada Universitas untuk menyediakan kursus dan kegiatan yang sesuai dengan minat siswa di luar standar kurikulum. Beberapa akademisi dan institusi yang menjadi mitra riset dan program Kampus Merdeka antara lain Fakultas Farmasi dan Departemen Teknik Kimia Fakultas Teknik Universitas Gadjah Mada Yogyakarta, Fakultas Farmasi Universitas Indonesia Depok, Sekolah Farmasi Institut Teknologi Bandung, Institut Pertanian Bogor, Fakultas Kedokteran Universitas Udayana, serta Badan Riset dan Inovasi Nasional (BRIN). Melalui berbagai riset tersebut telah dihasilkan beberapa produk inovatif serta publikasi ilmiah baik pada jurnal nasional maupun internasional.

Kami juga bekerja sama dengan masyarakat sekitar SOHO Center of Excellence in Herbal Research (SCEHR) dalam hal ini dengan Kelompok Wanita Tani Dahlia. Kami juga berkomitmen untuk meningkatkan pendapatan petani, salah satunya dengan implementasi Smart Farming 4.0 yang telah dirintis pada tahun 2020 dan telah diterapkan di seluruh area tanam pada tahun tanam 2021 dan 2022. Kami juga mendorong petani untuk menerapkan tumpang sari dengan tanaman jagung manis untuk membantu petani mendapatkan lebih banyak pendapatan dari tanaman. Kami melakukan penelitian terkait budi daya tumpang sari selama proses budi daya temulawak, dimana metode tumpang sari dengan jagung manis tidak mempengaruhi kualitas temulawak yang dihasilkan. Bahkan tumpang sari tidak hanya mampu memberikan dampak positif bagi petani, penggarapan lahan secara berulang mampu menyuburkan tanah sehingga juga dapat memberikan dampak positif bagi budidaya temulawak.
