Qualification or Certification as an Internal Audit Professional

The Head of the Internal Audit Unit is a Certified Public Accountant and a Certified Indonesia Internal Audit Practitioner (IIAP). His certification as Public Accountant was obtained from and issued by the Board of Accountancy in the Philippines. Whilst his certification of Indonesia Internal Audit Practitioner (IIAP) was obtained from and issued by the Institute of Internal Auditors in Indonesia. He is also a member of Institute of Internal Auditors and Association of Certified Fraud Examiners.

Kualifikasi atau Sertifikasi sebagai Profesi Audit Internal
Kepala Unit Audit Internal, telah memiliki sertifikasi sebagai Akuntan Publik Bersertifikat (Certified Public Accountant) dan sertifikasi Indonesia Internal Audit Practitioner (IIAP). Sertifikasi sebagai Akuntan Publik Bersertifikat tersebut beliau peroleh dari dan diterbitkan oleh Board of Accountancy di Filipina. Sedangkan sertifikasi Indonesia Internal Audit Practitioner beliau peroleh dari dan diterbitkan dari Institute of Internal Auditors di Indonesia. Beliau juga merupakan anggota dari Institute of Internal Auditors dan Association of Certified Fraud Examiners.
