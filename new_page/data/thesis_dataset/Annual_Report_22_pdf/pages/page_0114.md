Consumer Health segment recorded a decrease in net sales of -18.5% by year end to Rp 511.7 billion and contributed to 7% of group's total net sales. Gross Profit Margin decreased to 70.7% in 2022 compared to 72.0% last year, driven by declining Imboost sales contribution.

Net revenues from the Alliance segment increased by 19.9% compared to 2021. The increase was mainly due to increased sales of products such as Pramifrol and Soroquin, as well as several new product launches in 2022, such as Almantine.

Net revenues from the segment Others decreased by 14.1% mainly due to a decrease in revenue of Medical Devices and Unihealth products.

Segment Kesehatan Konsumen mencatat penurunan pertumbuhan penjualan bersih sebesar -18.5% pada akhir tahun menjadi Rp 511,7 miliar dan berkontribusi sekitar 7% dari total penjualan bersih grup. Margin Laba Kotor turun menjadi 70.7% pada tahun 2022 dibandingkan 72.0% tahun lalu, didorong oleh kontribusi penjualan Imboost yang menurun.

Pendapatan neto dari segmen Alliance meningkat 19,9% dibandingkan dengan tahun 2021. Kenaikan ini terutama karena kenaikan penjualan ditahun 2022 untuk produk seperti Pramifrol dan Soroquin, serta adanya produk baru yang diluncurkan di tahun 2022 seperti Almantine.

Pendapatan neto dari Segmen Lainnya mengalami penurunan sebesar 14,1% terutama karena adanya penurunan pendapatan di penjualan Alat Kesehatan dan produk-produk Unihealth.

Cost of revenues for the Professional Product and the Consumer Health segments decreased following a significant decrease in sales. In these segments, cost of revenues mainly represents cost of raw materials used in production.

Increase in cost of revenues in the Others segment was mainly due to higher production variances as a result of decreasing production volume.
