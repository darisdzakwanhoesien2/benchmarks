|  17. | The Board of Directors shall have a resignation policy for its members in the event of any financial fraud.

Direksi mempunyai kebijakan terkait pengunduran diri anggota Direksi apabila terlibat dalam kejahatan keuangan. | Recommendations have been implemented.
Policies regarding the resignation of members of the Board of Directors due to involvement in financial crimes or other violations of law have been stated in the Company's Board of Directors Charter and the Company's Articles of Association. The Board of Directors' Charter is available on the Company's Website.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.
Kebijakan terkait pengunduran diri anggota Direksi karena terlibat dalam kejahatan keuangan maupun pelanggaran hukum lainnya telah tercantum dalam Piagam Direksi Perseroan dan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan. Piagam Direksi telah tersedia di dalam Situs Web Perseroan.  |

|  18. | Public Companies shall have policies to address insider trading.

Perusahaan Terbuka memiliki kebijakan untuk mencegah terjadinya insider trading. | Recommendations have been implemented.
The Company already has a Stock and/or Insider Trading Transaction Policy Number P12/R00.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.
Perseroan telah memiliki Kebijakan Transaksi Perdagangan Saham dan/atau Efek Orang Dalam (Insider Trading) Nomor P12/R00.  |
|  19. | Public Companies shall have anti-corruption and anti-fraud policies.

Perusahaan Terbuka memiliki kebijakan anti korupsi dan anti fraud. | Recommendations have been implemented.
The Company has an Anti-Bribery & Corruption Policy Number P01/R02 and has published a Code of Business Ethics and Conduct Book (Towards Good Corporate Governance) which has also been socialized to all employees of the Company.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.
Perseroan telah memiliki Kebijakan Anti-Bribery & Corruption Nomor P01/R02 serta menerbitkan Buku Kode Etik dan Perilaku Bisnis (Menuju Tata Kelola Perusahaan Yang Baik) yang juga telah disosialisasikan kepada seluruh karyawan Perseroan.  |
|  20. | Public Companies shall have policies on vendor selection and capability improvement.

Perusahaan Terbuka memiliki kebijakan tentang seleksi dan peningkatan kemampuan pemasok atau vendor. | Recommendations have been implemented.
The Company already has a Procurement Policy Number P23/R02 and Standard Operating Procedures regarding the Procurement of Goods and Services Number SOP 7-04-01/R04, which also regulates the availability of vendor qualification questionnaires, vendor qualification audits, and also the obligations for vendors to sign the Statement of Compliance and fill out the due diligence questionnaire.

Perseroan telah memiliki Kebijakan Procurement Nomor P23/R02 serta Standar Operasional Prosedur mengenai Pengadaan Barang dan Jasa Nomor SOP 7-04-01/R04, termasuk juga di dalamnya mengatur mengenai adanya kuesioner kualifikasi vendor, audit kualifikasi vendor, dan juga adanya kewajiban bagi para vendor untuk menandatangani Pernyataan Kepatuhan dan mengisi kuesioner uji kelayakan (Due Dilligence).  |
|  21. | Public Companies shall have policies on the fulfillment of creditors' rights.

Perusahaan Terbuka memiliki kebijakan tentang pemenuhan hak-hak kreditur. | Recommendations have been implemented.
The Company already has a Policy on Fulfillment of Creditor Rights. The policy also regulates policy principles, including the Company's obligations in terms of implementing cooperation plans and/or agreements with creditors.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.
Perseroan telah memiliki Kebijakan tentang Pemenuhan Hak-Hak Kreditur. Dalam kebijakan tersebut juga mengatur mengenai prinsip-prinsip kebijakan, termasuk juga kewajiban Perseroan dalam hal pelaksanaan rencana kerjasama dan/atau kesepakatan dengan kreditur.  |
