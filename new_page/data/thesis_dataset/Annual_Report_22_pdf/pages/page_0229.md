After the primary VGR program was completed, SGH continued with the third dose vaccination, known as vaccine booster. The booster vaccination began on March 26, 2022 by walking-in directly to Kimia Farma Clinics located in Jakarta, Tangerang, Bogor, and Bandung.

In this VGR program, nearly 1000 person (employees and their spouses) became primary vaccine recipients; and more than 800 recipients of the vaccine booster. By the end of December 2022, out of the approximately 2500 employees, almost 100% have received the primary vaccine, and more than 87% have received the vaccine booster. Employees who haven't receive the vaccine are only those who have comorbidities.
