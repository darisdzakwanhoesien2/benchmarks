The company is also committed to respecting labor rights as a manifestation of human rights for employees, namely:

a. Fulfilling employment rights
b. Respect the work relationship with Employees, especially in terms of clarity of Employee status, job placement, acceptance of new employees, mutation, rotations, promotions, and demotions
c. Respect the right of Employees to have good health, clear working days and hours, opportunities for sick leave, menstrual leave, maternity leave, miscarriage, collective leave, and annual leave
d. Providing equal and non-discriminatory opportunities, as well as respecting diversity and inclusion
e. Respect the rights of employees to freedom of association and gathering, and negotiation through unions or workers representatives
f. Provide social facilities to all employees based on the company's capabilities
g. Provide a workplace that is free from all forms of harassment and intimidation, as well as facilitating the employees to submit and resolve grievances
h. Ensure the health and safety of Employees by providing a safe, healthy, and productive workplace

The Company also expects external parties and other stakeholders to participate in the company's commitments regarding human rights and encourage them to implement the same policies within their respective companies.
