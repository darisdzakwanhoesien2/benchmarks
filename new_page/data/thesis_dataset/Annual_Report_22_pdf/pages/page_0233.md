# Employee Gathering and Engagement

After nearly 3 years of the Company limiting employee activities during the COVID-19 pandemic, at the end of 2022 when the Government began allowing joint activities while still implementing health protocols, SGH Group once again held employee activities which were packaged in recreational events with a fun atmosphere aimed to enhance employee engagement, fostering a sense of togetherness and solidarity, as well as maintaining employee mental health, which ultimately aims to increase productivity at work.

Among the employee activities being referred to includes employee gathering held by the Distribution business unit of PT. Parit Padang Global (PPG) during the PPG Branch Manager National Meeting on July 23, 2022 in Bogor, themed "Boost Together, Boost Stronger". This meeting was attended by all PPG leaders, Branch Managers from all PPG branch offices along with representatives from other divisions/departments. The employee gathering consists of several fun games that aimed to bond all participants, increase togetherness and strengthen communication in the workplace.

# Pertemuan dan Keterlibatan Karyawan

Setelah selama hampir 3 tahun Perusahaan membatasi aktivitas kebersamaan karyawan sehubungan dengan situasi pandemi COVID-19, di penghujung tahun 2022 di saat Pemerintah mulai mengijinkan aktivitas kebersamaan dengan tetap menerapkan protokol kesehatan, SGH Group mulai mengadakan kembali aktivitas kebersamaan karyawan yang dikemas dalam acara rekreasi dengan suasana menyenangkan dengan tujuan untuk meningkatkan keterlibatan karyawan, menumbuhkan rasa kebersamaan dan solidaritas, serta menjaga kesehatan mental karyawan, yang pada akhirnya bertujuan untuk meningkatkan produktivitas dalam bekerja.

Aktivitas yang dimaksud antara lain berupa employee gathering yang diadakan oleh unit bisnis Distribution PT. Parit Padang Global (PPG) dalam PPG Branch Manager National Meeting pada tanggal 23 Juli 2022 di Bogor, dengan mengusung tema "Boost Together Boost Stronger". Selain diikuti oleh seluruh pimpinan PPG, employee gathering ini juga diikuti oleh para Kepala Cabang dari seluruh kantor cabang PPG di Indonesia beserta perwakilan dari beberapa divisi/departemen lainnya. Employee gathering terdiri dari beberapa fun games, yang bertujuan mengakrabkan seluruh peserta, meningkatkan kekompakan dan memperhatikkan komunikasi di lingkungan kerja.

Legal, Compliance, Internal Audit, Corporate Secretary, &amp; Human Resources (LCIACS &amp; HR) business unit also held an employee gathering on November 25, 2022 at Lembang, West Java. During this gathering, various bonding activities were carried out in through fun games &amp; competitions. Apart from games and competitions, the participants also had the opportunity to get their adrenaline pumping by playing games such as flying fox, line bridge, and rappelling while enjoying the view and cool weather in Lembang.

Unit bisnis Legal, Compliance, Internal Audit, Corporate Secretary, &amp; Human Resources (LCIACS &amp; HR) juga mengadakan employee gathering pada tanggal 25 November 2022, bertempat di Lembang, Jawa Barat. Pada sesi gathering ini, berbagai kegiatan kebersamaan dilakukan dalam bentuk permainan dan kompetisi yang menyenangkan. Selain permainan dan kompetisi, para peserta juga berkesempatan menguji adrenalin dengan bermain di ketinggian seperti flying fox, line bridge, dan turun tebing, sambil menikmati pemandangan dan udara sejuk di Lembang.
