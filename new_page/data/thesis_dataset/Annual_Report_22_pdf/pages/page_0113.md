Equity

In 2022, the equity of the Company reached Rp 2.4 trillion, indicating a 10% increase. The increase in equity was attributed to the total comprehensive income of Rp 378.3 billion, offset by the cash dividend payment of Rp 149.7 billion and the difference in transaction value with non-controlling interests of Rp 3 billion.

Representing 82.1% of the total, the Distribution segment is still the largest contributor to the Company's net revenues. Net revenues from this segment increased by Rp 523.9 billion or 9.6% compared to previous year. This performance was mainly due to the increased of principal's sales which rebounded post-Covid-19 as well as a few new principal additions. However, the gross profit margin remained strong at 8.7% due to a well-managed product portfolio mix.

Net Sales of the Professional Products division in 2022 grew -27.9% lower than in 2021, due to the end of the Covid-19 pandemic, sales of immune-boosting products in general also experienced a decline. The ban by BPOM related to the syrup issue also had an impact on decreasing sales of all syrup products in the Professional Products division, both for existing products such as Imboost Force syrup, Curvit syrup, and new products such as Gastin Force syrup. Even though its sales have decreased, Imboost Force is still the product with the largest contribution to the Professional Products business.

Mewakili 82,1% dari total, segmen Distribusi masih memberikan kontribusi terbesar bagi pendapatan neto Perseroan. Pendapatan neto dari segmen ini meningkat sebesar Rp 523,9 milyar atau 9,6% dibandingkan dengan tahun lalu. Kinerja ini terutama disebabkan oleh peningkatan penjualan produk prinsipal yang pulih pasca-Covid-19 serta beberapa penambahan prinsipal baru. Namun, margin laba kotor tetap kuat di angka 8,7% karena portofolio produk yang dikelola dengan baik.

Penjualan Bersih divisi Produk Professional pada tahun 2022 tumbuh lebih rendah -27.9% dibandingkan tahun 2021, dikarenakan dengan berakhirnya pandemi Covid-19, penjualan untuk produk penambah daya tahan tubuh secara umum juga mengalami penurunan. Pelarangan oleh BPOM terkait dengan isu sirup, juga berdampak terhadap penurunan penjualan semua produk sirup divisi Produk Professional, baik untuk produk eksisting seperti Imboost Force sirup, Curvit sirup, maupun produk baru seperti Gastin Force sirup. Walaupun sales nya mengalami penurunan namun Imboost Force masih menjadi product dengan kontribusi terbesar bagi bisnis Produk Professional.
