In 2022, SOHO Global Health will again begin to develop the Seed to Patient concept for products made from Echinacea purpurea (L.) Moench. Although Echinacea is natively an herb from Europe with subtropical to temperate climates, the extensive use of Echinacea outside European countries including Indonesia, makes Echinacea one of the herbs that has been naturalized to become an endogenous Indonesian herb. By applying the concept of Academic, Business, Government and Community (ABGC) collaboration, we have collaborated with the Spice and Medicinal Plants Research Institute (Balittro) to obtain superior Echinacea seeds in Indonesia and also with the GAPOKTAN Dahlia farming community for their cultivation. As such, SOHO has succeeded in carrying out Cultivation of Echinacea purpurea on SCEHR land which is estimated to be carried out 2-3 times each growing season. Each growing season, the amount of Echinacea simplicia produced ranges from 0.25-0.5 kg per m². The Echinacea simplicia produced by SCEHR also has superior quality with active biomarker levels that have met or even exceeded the Echinacea simplicia quality standards set by the European Medicines Agency (EMA).

Further collaboration with academics in testing the effectiveness of extracts as antioxidants, tests are carried out by determining the IC₅₀ value of each extract. IC₅₀ itself is defined as the concentration of antioxidant compounds needed to reduce radical activity by 50%, where a smaller value indicates higher activity. The test results showed that Echinacea purpurea extract from SCEHR had the best antioxidant effect compared to other Echinacea extract samples.

In producing its products, SIP is committed to using materials that are locally sourced. This is evidenced by the TKDN (Total Domestic Component) certification issued by the Ministry of Industry. TKDN is the percentage value of domestic materials and/or services that comprise a product. TKDN certificate is the proof prioritizing the use of domestic products for the production or provision of goods or services. Currently PT Soho has TKDN certificates for 66 products, and the TKDN certification process is still ongoing for other products.

Dalam memproduksi produk-produknya, SIP berkomitmen untuk menggunakan bahan-bahan yang berasal dari dalam negeri. Hal ini dibuktikan dengan sertifikasi TKDN (Total Komponen Dalam Negeri) yang dikeluarkan oleh Kementerian Perindustrian. TKDN adalah persentase nilai komponen produksi yang dibuat di Indonesia pada suatu produk barang dan jasa atau gabungan barang dan jasa dalam negeri. Sertifikat TKDN dapat digunakan sebagai bukti penggunaan produk dalam negeri untuk memproduksi dan memberikan barang atau jasa. Saat ini PT Soho telah memiliki sertifikat TKDN untuk 66 produk, dan proses sertifikasi TKDN masih terus berlangsung untuk produk-produk lainnya.
