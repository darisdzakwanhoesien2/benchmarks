|  13. | Decision on the composition of the Board of Directors should consider diversity in the required expertise, knowledge and experience.

Penentuan komposisi anggota Direksi memperhatikan keberagaman keahlian, pengetahuan, dan pengalaman yang dibutuhkan. | Recommendations have been implemented.
The determination of the composition of the members of the Company's Board of Directors has taken into account the diversity of expertise, knowledge and experience required by the Company. This can be seen in the profiles and work history of the members of the Company's Board of Directors which have been described in this Company's Annual Report for the 2022 Financial Year.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.
Penentuan komposisi anggota Direksi Perseroan telah memperhatikan keberagaman keahlian, pengetahuan dan pengalaman yang dibutuhkan oleh Perseroan. Hal ini dapat terlihat pada profil dan riwayat kerja dari para anggota Direksi Perseroan yang telah diuraikan dalam Laporan Tahunan Perseroan Tahun Buku 2022 ini.  |
|  14. | Board of Directors in charge of accounting or finance shall have the expertise and/or knowledge in accounting.

Anggota Direksi yang membawahi bidang akuntansi atau keuangan memiliki keahlian dan/atau pengetahuan di bidang akuntansi. | Recommendations have been implemented.
The Company's Finance Director, Mr. Piero Brambati, has strong expertise and experience in accounting and finance. This can be seen in his profile and work history in the Profile of the Company's Board of Directors in this Company's Annual Report for the 2022 Financial Year.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.
Direktur Keuangan Perseroan, yakni Bapak Piero Brambati, memiliki keahlian dan pengalaman yang mumpuni dalam bidang akuntansi dan keuangan. Hal ini dapat terlihat pada profil dan riwayat kerja beliau pada bagian Profil Direksi Perseroan dalam Laporan Tahunan Perseroan Tahun Buku 2022 ini.  |

|  15. | The Board of Directors shall have a self assessment policy to measure the performance of the Board of Directors.

Direksi mempunyai kebijakan penilaian sendiri (self assessment) untuk menilai kinerja Direksi. | Recommendations have been implemented.
The Company currently has a Policy for Assessment of the Performance of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors, the policy also explains aspects of the assessment and performance appraisal system of the Board of Directors.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.
Perseroan saat ini telah memiliki Kebijakan Penilaian atas Kinerja Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi Perseoran, dalam kebijakan tersebut juga menjelaskan perihal aspek-apek penilaian dan sistem penilaian kinerja Direksi.  |
