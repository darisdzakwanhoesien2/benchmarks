PT Soho Industri Pharmasi (“SIP”) (lanjutan)

Berdasarkan Akta Notaris Jimmy Tanal, S.H., M.Kn., No. 220 tanggal 30 Juni 2022, PT Soho Global Investment selaku pemilik 1 saham Seri A dengan nilai nominal Rp140.000 (nilai penuh) per saham di SIP, telah menjual saham tersebut ke PPG sebesar nilai wajar. Berdasarkan laporan penilaian yang dilakukan oleh KJPP Wawat Jatmika dan Rekan, penilai independen, nilai wajar saham SIP pada tanggal 31 Desember 2021 adalah sebesar Rp3.868.687.000 (nilai penuh) per saham. Setelah transaksi ini, kepemilikan Perusahaan dalam SIP mengalami peningkatan dari semula 99,64% menjadi 100,00%, melalui kepemilikan langsung dan kepemilikan tidak langsung melalui PPG. Selisih antara nilai nominal dan nilai wajar sebesar Rp3.868.547.000 (nilai penuh) diakui pada “Selisih Transaksi dengan Pihak Nonpengendali” dalam laporan keuangan konsolidasian pada tanggal 31 Desember 2022.

PT Parit Padang Global (“PPG”)

Pada tanggal 31 Oktober 2022, PPG menyetujui pembagian dividen kas interim untuk tahun buku 2022, kepada masing-masing pemegang saham dengan nilai maksimum Rp131 (nilai penuh) per lembar saham untuk seluruh seri saham. Dividen sebesar Rp100.346 telah dibayarkan kepada seluruh pemegang saham yang tercatat pada November 2022.

PT Soho Industri Pharmasi (“SIP”) (continued)

Based on Notarial Deed No. 220 of Jimmy Tanal, S.H., M.Kn., dated June 30, 2022, PT Soho Global Investment as a shareholder of 1 Series A share with nominal value of Rp140,000 (full amount) per share on SIP, has sold the share to PPG at fair value. Based on the appraisal report, performed by KJPP Wawat Jatmika and Partners, independent appraiser, the fair value of SIP share as of December 31, 2021 amounted to Rp3,868,687,000 (full amount) per share. After the transaction, the Company's ownership in SIP increased from 99.64% to 100.00%, through direct ownership and indirect ownership through PPG. The difference between selling price and fair value amounting to Rp3,868,547,000 (full amount) was recorded as “Differences in value of transactions of non-controlling interests” in consolidated financial statements as of December 31, 2022.

PT Parit Padang Global (“PPG”)

On October 31, 2022, PPG agreed distribution of interim cash dividend for the financial year 2022, to each shareholder of PPG up to maximum Rp131 (full amount) per share for any series of shares. Dividends of Rp100,346 have been paid to all registered shareholders in November 2022.
