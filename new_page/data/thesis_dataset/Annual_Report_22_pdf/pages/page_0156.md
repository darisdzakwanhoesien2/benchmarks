## Meetings of the Board of Directors

The Board of Directors has held internal meetings and joint meetings which involves the Board of Commissioners. The meetings of the Board of Directors is a forum and mechanism for decision-making by the Board of Directors collectively.

Before the beginning of the current financial year, the Company first set up a Corporate Calendar that contained the schedule of Board of Directors meetings.

Based on the Company's Articles of Association, the Board of Directors of the Company has a stipulation to hold regular internal meetings at least 1 (one) time per month, but may hold other meetings at any time if deemed necessary. Meanwhile, joint meetings between the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners are held regularly at least 1 (one) time in 4 (four) months.

## Rapat Direksi

Direksi telah melaksanakan rapat internal maupun rapat gabungan yang melibatkan Dewan Komisaris. Rapat Direksi merupakan suatu forum dan mekanisme atas pengambilan keputusan Direksi secara kolektif/bersama-sama.

Sebelum awal tahun buku berjalan, Perseroan terlebih dahulu menyusun Corporate Calendar yang memuat jadwal rapat Direksi.

Berdasarkan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan, Direksi Perseroan memiliki ketetapan untuk mengadakan rapat internal secara berkala paling kurang 1 (satu) kali setiap bulan, namun dapat mengadakan rapat lainnya setiap saat apabila dipandang perlu. Sementara, rapat gabungan antara Direksi bersama dengan Dewan Komisaris diadakan secara berkala paling kurang 1 (satu) kali dalam 4 (empat) bulan.
