# 02 Second Agenda

Approval of the use of the Company's profits for the financial year ended at December 31, 2021

## Approved Resolution

- Declare that the Company's net profit for the financial year ending on December 31, 2021 is IDR 550,176,285,981 (five hundred fifty billion one hundred seventy six million two hundred eighty five thousand nine hundred and eighty one Rupiah).
- Approve that after deducting the payment of 2021 interim cash dividend to Shareholders in the amount of IDR 250,026,143,083 (two hundred fifty billion twenty six million one hundred forty three thousand eighty three Rupiah) then the remaining net profit of the Company for the 2021 Financial Year which is IDR 300,150,142,898 (three hundred billion one hundred fifty million one hundred forty two thousand eight hundred ninety eight Rupiah) will be recorded as retained earnings which can be used for the Company's potential investment plans in the future.
