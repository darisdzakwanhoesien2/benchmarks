|  02. | All members of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners of the Public Company shall attend the Annual GMS.

Seluruh anggota Direksi dan anggota Dewan Komisaris Perusahaan Terbuka hadir dalam RUPS Tahunan. | Recommendations have been implemented but have not been fully fulfilled due to the COVID-19 pandemic.

The Company always tries to present the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners at each Annual GMS. The Company's Annual GMS for Fiscal Year 2021 was attended by 3 (three) of 5 (five) members of the Board of Directors and 1 (one) of 3 (three) members of the Board of Commissioners. The absence of members of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners occurred due to:

01. There are members of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners who are domiciled outside the territory of the Republic of Indonesia;
02. There are important conditions or schedule conflicts that prevent some members of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners from attending the Annual GMS;
03. In order to support the Government's efforts to prevent potential transmission of COVID-19 and comply with the Guidelines for the Prevention and Control of COVID-19 as issued by the Ministry of Health of the Republic of Indonesia; as well as
04. There is risk to the safety and health of the members of the Board of Directors and members of the Board of Commissioners if the members of the Board of Directors and members of the Board of Commissioners physically attend the GMS of the Company, considering that some members of the Board of Directors and members of the Board of Commissioners who are absent are classified in the category of elderly. They have a higher risk when exposed to the COVID-19 virus.

Rekomendasi telah diterapkan, namun belum dapat seluruhnya terpenuhi karena masih terkendala adanya kondisi pandemi COVID-19.

Perseroan selalu berusaha menghadirkan Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris pada setiap RUPS Tahunan. RUPS Tahunan Perseroan untuk Tahun Buku 2021 dihadiri oleh 3 (tiga) dari 5 (lima) anggota Direksi dan 1 (satu) dari 3 (tiga) anggota Dewan Komisaris. Ketidakhadiran anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris tersebut terjadi dikarenakan:

01. Terdapat anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris yang berdomisili di luar wilayah Republik Indonesia;
02. Terdapat kondisi penting atau benturan jadwal yang membuat beberapa anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris tidak dapat hadir pada RUPS Tahunan;
03. Dalam rangka mendukung upaya Pemerintah untuk mencegah potensi penularan COVID-19 serta mematuhi Pedoman Pencegahan dan Pengendalian COVID-19 sebagaimana diterbitkan oleh Kementerian Kesehatan Republik Indonesia; serta
04. Terdapat risiko terhadap keamanan dan kesehatan anggota Direksi dan anggota Dewan Komisaris tersebut apabila anggota Direksi dan anggota Dewan Komisaris tersebut menghadiri secara fisik RUPS Perseroan, mengingat beberapa anggota Direksi dan anggota Dewan Komisaris yang tidak hadir tersebut termasuk dalam kategori lanjut usia (lansia) yang memiliki risiko lebih tinggi apabila terpapar virus COVID-19.  |
|  03. | Summary of GMS minutes should be available in the Public Company's website for at least 1 (one) year.

Ringkasan risalah RUPS tersedia dalam Situs Web Perusahaan Terbuka paling sedikit selama 1 (satu) tahun. | The recommendation has been implemented, whereby the obligation to disclose the summary of the minutes of the GMS on the Company's Website has been carried out by the Company after the holding of the Company's Annual GMS for the 2021 Fiscal Year on June 30, 2022.

Rekomendasi telah diterapkan, dimana kewajiban pengungkapan ringkasan risalah RUPS pada Situs Web Perseroan telah dilaksanakan oleh Perseroan setelah penyelenggaraan RUPS Tahunan Perseroan untuk Tahun Buku 2021 pada tanggal 30 Juni 2022.  |
