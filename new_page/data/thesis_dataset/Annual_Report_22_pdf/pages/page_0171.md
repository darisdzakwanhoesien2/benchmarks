Composition of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee Membership

As at December 31, 2022, there was no change in the composition of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee members. The Decree of the Company's Board of Commissioners Number 08/SK-Dekom/SGH/VI/2020 dated June 19, 2020 concerning the Establishment of the Guidelines for the Company's Nomination and Remuneration Committee as referred to OJK Regulation No. 34/POJK.04/2015 regarding the Nomination and Remuneration Committee of Issuers or Public Companies stipulates that the number of members of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee consists of at least 3 (three) members with the following conditions:

- 1 (one) Chairman who is also a member, namely the Independent Commissioner of the Company; and
- The other 2 (two) members may come from members of the Board of Commissioners, parties from outside the Company, or parties holding managerial positions under the Board of Directors that is in charge of human resources.
