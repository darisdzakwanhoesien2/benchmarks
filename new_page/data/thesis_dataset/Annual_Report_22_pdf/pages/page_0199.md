# 3. Sinar Kencana Multilestari ("SKML")

|  Case Status
Status Perkara | PPG is a Concurrent Creditor of SKML. PPG has receivables that are due and payable to SKML for the pharmaceutical products supplied by PPG and SKML is unable to pay the receivables. The bankruptcy application of SKML was applied by another creditor of SKML.

PPG adalah Kreditur Konkuren SKML. PPG memiliki piutang yang telah jatuh tempo dan terutang kepada SKML atas produk farmasi yang dipasok oleh PPG dan SKML tidak mampu membayar piutang tersebut. Permohonan pailit SKML diajukan oleh kreditur SKML yang lain.  |
| --- | --- |
|  Risks Faced by the Company and Their Impacts on the Company
Risiko yang Dihadapi dan Pengaruhnya terhadap Perseroan | PPG faces the risk of inability to recover its receivables fully, which amounts to IDR 401,647,825.

PPG berisiko tidak akan memperoleh pengembalian tagihan secara penuh yang jumlahnya sebesar Rp 401.647.825.  |
|  Follow-up
Tindak Lanjut | SKML has been declared Bankrupt based on the decision of the Commercial Court at Central Jakarta District Court No. 230/Pdt.Sus.PKPU/2021/PN Niaga, dated July 1, 2021. Currently PPG is waiting for the settlement process from the liquidation team to get payment from PPG's receivables (if any).

SKML telah dinyatakan pailit berdasarkan putusan Pengadilan Niaga pada Pengadilan Negeri Jakarta Pusat No. 230/Pdt.Sus.PKPU/2021/PN Niaga, tanggal 1 Juli 2021. Saat ini PPG sedang menunggu proses penyelesaian dari tim likuidasi untuk mendapatkan pembayaran dari piutang PPG (jika ada).  |
