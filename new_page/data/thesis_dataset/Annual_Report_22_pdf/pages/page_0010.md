# Kronologi Pencatatan Saham

# YEAR 2020

The Company has submitted a Registration Statement in connection with the Public Offering to the Financial Services Authority under Number 396/SGH-OJK/VII/2020 dated June 25, 2020 regarding the Cover Letter for the Registration Statement in the context of the Company's Initial Public Offering and the latest amendments and/or additional information had been submitted through Letter Number 529/SGH-OJK/VIII/2020 dated August 28, 2020 regarding the Cover Letter for Changes and/or Additional Documents and/or Information on the Registration Statement in the context of the Company's Initial Public Offering in accordance with the requirements stipulated in the Law No. 8 of 1995 concerning the Capital Market, which is contained in the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia No. 64 of 1995, Supplement to the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia No. 3608 and its implementing regulations ("UUPM") and their amendments include OJK Regulation No. 7/POJK.04/2017 concerning Registration Statement Documents for Public Offering of Equity Securities, Debt Securities, and/or Sukuk.

The Company officially conducted an Initial Public Offering (IPO) on the Indonesia Stock Exchange on September 8, 2020 and became the  $42^{\text{nd}}$  issuer in 2020 or the  $705^{\text{th}}$  of the total issuers. The Company has set an offering price of IDR 1,820 per share and has gone through a public offering process on 2 - 3 September 2020.

# TAHUN 2020

Perseroan telah menyampaikan Pernyataan Pendaftaran sehubungan dengan Penawaran Umum kepada Otoritas Jasa Keuangan dengan Nomor 396/SGH-OJK/VII/2020 tanggal 25 Juni 2020 perihal Surat Pengantar untuk Pernyataan Pendaftaran dalam rangka Penawaran Umum Perdana Saham Perseroan dan perubahan dan/atau tambahan informasi yang terakhir disampaikan melalui Surat Nomor 529/SGH-OJK/VIII/2020 tanggal 28 Agustus 2020 perihal Surat Pengantar untuk Perubahan dan/atau Tambahan Dokumen dan/atau Informasi atas Pernyataan Pendaftaran dalam rangka Penawaran Umum Perdana Saham Perseroan sesuai dengan persyaratan yang ditetapkan dalam Undang-Undang No. 8 Tahun 1995 tentang Pasar Modal, yang dimuat dalam Lembaran Negara Republik Indonesia No. 64 Tahun 1995, Tambahan Lembaran Negara Republik Indonesia No. 3608 dan peraturan pelaksanaannya ("UUPM") serta perubahan-perubahannya antara lain Peraturan OJK No. 7/POJK.04/2017 tentang Dokumen Pernyataan Pendaftaran Dalam Rangka Penawaran Umum Efek Bersifat Ekuitas, Efek Bersifat Utang, dan/atau Sukuk.

Perseroan melakukan Penawaran Umum Perdana Saham (IPO) secara resmi di Bursa Efek Indonesia pada tanggal 8 September 2020 dan menjadi emiten ke-42 di tahun 2020 atau ke-705 dari total emiten. Perseroan telah menetapkan harga penawaran Rp 1.820 per saham dan telah melalui proses penawaran umum pada 2 - 3 September 2020.
