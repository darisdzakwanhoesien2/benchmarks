# Fixed assets, net

The Company's fixed assets as of December 31, 2022 amounted to Rp 383.5 billion, which represents a decrease of 1% compared to last year's Rp 386 billion. This decline was due to the normal annual depreciation of the fixed assets.

# Liabilities

The Company recorded total liabilities of Rp 2 trillion at the end of 2022, consisting of 93% short-term liabilities and 7% long-term liabilities. The amount of liabilities increased by Rp 233.4 billion or 13% compared to 2021. The increase in total liability value was mainly due to an increase in short-term liabilities.

# Current liabilities

In 2022, the Company's short-term liabilities amounted to Rp 1.9 trillion, representing a 15% increase. The increase in short-term liabilities was mainly due to an increase in trade payables, partly offset by a decrease in tax liabilities.

# Aset tetap, neto

Aset tetap Perseroan pada tanggal 31 Desember 2022 adalah sebesar Rp 383,5 milyar, mengalami penurunan sebesar 1% dibanding tahun lalu sebesar Rp 386 milyar. Penurunan ini merupakan depresiasi normal tahunan dari aset tetap tersebut.

# Liabilitas

Perseroan membukukan jumlah liabilitas di akhir tahun 2022 sebesar Rp 2 trillion yang terdiri dari 93% liabilitas jangka pendek dan 7% liabilitas jangka panjang. Jumlah liabilitas mengalami kenaikan sebesar Rp 233,4 milyar atau 13% dibanding tahun 2021. Kenaikan nilai total liabilitas tersebut terutama disebabkan oleh kenaikan liabilitas jangka pendek.

# Liabilitas jangka pendek

# Trade payables

The Company's trade payables at the end of 2022 amounted to Rp 1.6 trillion, representing an increase of 25%. The increase in trade payables was mainly due to the increase in the value of inventory purchases for the distribution business segment.

# Tax payables

As of December 31, 2022, the company recorded tax payable of Rp 10.8 billion, a decrease of 82% compared to the previous year. This reduction in tax liability was partly a result of the decline in the operational profits in 2022.

# Non-current liabilities

In 2022, the long-term liabilities of the Company amounted to Rp 148.1 billion, which experienced a 7% decrease. This decline was due to the reduction in lease liabilities and employee benefit obligations.

# Utang usaha

Utang usaha Perseroan pada tahun akhir tahun 2022 adalah sebesar Rp 1.6 trillion atau naik sebesar 25%. Naiknya utang usaha sebagian besar disebabkan oleh kenaikan nilai pembelian persediaan untuk segmen bisnis distribusi.

# Utang pajak

Pada tanggal 31 Desember 2022, Perseroan membukukan utang pajak sebesar Rp 10,8 milyar, turun sebesar 82% dibandingkan tahun lalu. Penurunan ini sebagian besar dikarenakan menurunnya keuntungan dari operasi tahun 2022.

# Liabilitas Jangka panjang

Pada tahun 2022, liabilitas jangka panjang Perseroan adalah sebesar Rp 148,1 milyar atau mengalami penurunan sebesar 7%, disebabkan oleh penurunan liabilitas sewa dan liabilitas imbalan kerja karyawan.
