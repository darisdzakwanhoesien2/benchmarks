# Certificate of Appreciation: Occupational Safety and Health (OSH) Management System for PT Parit Padang Global (PPG)

In May 2022 PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) received Certificate of Appreciation: Occupational Safety and Health (OSH) Management System from Minister of Manpower of the Republic of Indonesia. The certificate is valid for 3 years from the date of issue.

This certificate is obtained after a series of audits conducted by the government, carried out various parameters such as the establishment of OSH policies, OSH planning, OSH implementation, OSH monitoring &amp; evaluation as well as OSHMS performance review &amp; improvements.

Pada bulan Mei 2022 PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) menerima Sertifikat Penghargaan: Sistem Manajemen Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3) dari Menteri Ketenagakerjaan Republik Indonesia. Sertifikat berlaku selama 3 tahun sejak tanggal dikeluarkan.

Sertifikat ini diperoleh setelah serangkaian audit yang dilakukan oleh pemerintah, atas berbagai parameter seperti penetapan kebijakan K3, perencanaan K3, implementasi K3, pemantauan &amp; evaluasi K3 serta peninjauan &amp; peningkatan kinerja SMK3.
