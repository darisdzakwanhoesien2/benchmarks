# Good Corporate Governance Tata Kelola Perusahaan

The Company realizes that the implementation of good corporate governance will be able to support accountability and increase the success of the Company's business. The implementation of good corporate governance is also expected to increase value and create a good and superior image for the Company in the future. Therefore, the Company is committed to implementing good corporate governance thoroughly in every aspect of the Company's functions with reference to all relevant regulations.

Based on Law No. 40 of 2007 concerning Limited Liability Companies, the corporate governance mechanism regulates the pattern of relationships and structures between the Company's main organs consisting of the GMS, the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, as well as other supporting organs, namely the Audit Committee, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Company's Audit Unit, and the Corporate Secretary.

This structure is established to ensure the implementation of the corporate governance principles in a systematic manner by clearly defining the roles and responsibilities of each organ of the Company.

The GMS is the highest organ in the corporate governance structure. The GMS is a company organ with exclusive authority that cannot be delegated to other organs, as long as its roles and decisions are still in accordance with the Limited Liability Company Law and/or the Articles of Association of the Company. The GMS is a forum for shareholders to formulate important decisions by taking into account the interests of the Company, as well as considering the provisions in the Company's Articles of Association and all applicable rules and regulations.

Meanwhile, the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors are jointly responsible for the continuity of the Company's business in the long term. The management of the Company is carried out by the Directors, while the Board of Commissioners is responsible for supervising the performance of the management of the Company conducted by the Directors. Therefore, the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors must have the same perception of the Company's vision, mission and values to be achieved.

The implementation of the tasks and responsibilities of the two bodies are supported by their respective supporting bodies. The Board of Commissioners is assisted by the Audit Committee, and Nomination and Remuneration Committee. While the Board of Directors is assisted by the Corporate Secretary and Internal Audit Unit.

Perseroan menyadari bahwa penerapan tata kelola perusahaan yang baik akan mampu mendukung akuntabilitas dan peningkatan keberhasilan usaha Perseroan. Adanya penerapan tata kelola perusahaan yang baik diharapkan juga dapat meningkatkan nilai dan menciptakan citra yang baik dan unggul bagi Perseroan di masa akan datang. Oleh karenanya, Perseroan berkomitmen untuk menerapkan tata kelola perusahaan yang baik secara menyeluruh di dalam setiap aspek fungsi Perseroan dengan mengacu pada seluruh peraturan yang terkait.

Berdasarkan Undang-Undang No. 40 Tahun 2007 tentang Perseroan Terbatas, mekanisme tata kelola perusahaan mengatur pola hubungan dan struktur antara organ utama Perseroan yang terdiri dari RUPS, Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, dan juga organ pendukung lainnya, yakni Komite Audit, Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi, Unit Audit Perseroan, dan Sekretaris Perusahaan.

Struktur ini ditetapkan guna memastikan pelaksanaan prinsip tata kelola perusahaan secara sistematis dengan ditentukannya secara jelas mengenai peran dan tanggung jawab masing-masing organ Perseroan.

RUPS merupakan organ tertinggi dalam struktur tata kelola Perseroan. RUPS menjadi organ perusahaan dengan kewenangan eksklusif yang tidak dapat dilimpahkan kepada organ lain, sepanjang peran dan keputusannya masih sesuai dengan Undang-Undang Perseroan Terbatas dan/atau Anggaran Dasar Perseroan. RUPS merupakan forum bagi para pemegang saham untuk memformulasikan keputusan-keputusan penting dengan memperhatikan kepentingan Perseroan, serta mempertimbangkan ketentuan-ketentuan dalam Anggaran Dasar Perseroan dan seluruh ketentuan dan peraturan yang berlaku.

Sementara itu, Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi secara bersama-sama bertanggung jawab atas kelangsungan usaha Perseroan dalam jangka panjang. Pengelolaan Perseroan dilakukan oleh Direksi, sementara Dewan Komisaris bertanggung jawab untuk melakukan pengawasan terhadap kinerja pengelolaan Perseroan oleh Direksi. Oleh karena itu, Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi harus memiliki kesamaan persepsi terhadap visi, misi, dan nilai-nilai Perseroan yang ingin dicapai.

Pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab kedua organ tersebut didukung oleh organ pendukung masing-masing. Dewan Komisaris dibantu oleh Komite Audit dan Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi. Sedangkan Direksi dibantu oleh Sekretaris Perusahaan dan Unit Audit Internal.
