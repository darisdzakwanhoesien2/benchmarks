# Signing Ceremony of Partnership Between PPG and 3M Indonesia

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) has officially become 3M Indonesia's distributor for Nexcare products. PPG was appointed and trusted by 3M Indonesia to distribute 3M Consumer products (such as Nexcare, Micropore, etc.) as of April 1st, 2022.

Mrs. Olin (Salome Cahya), as Country Sales Lead - Consumer Business Group 3M Indonesia signed a letter of distribution agreement between the two parties and handed it over to Mr. Edwin Vega Dharma as PPG's VP Sales &amp; Marketing at the signing ceremony on April 4th, 2022.

The ceremonial event marking the beginning of the cooperation between the two parties, was held in the lobby of PT Parit Padang Global Head Office (HO) in Pulogadung. The event was carried out with the humble symbolic meaning of cutting "tumpeng rice", while breaking the fast for Moslem attendance, with strict health protocols compliance to prevent the spread of Covid-19 virus.

The appointment of PPG as 3M Indonesia's distributor partner demonstrates the trust this multinational principal places in PPG's reputation.

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) resmi menjadi distributor 3M Indonesia untuk produk Nexcare. PPG ditunjuk dan dipercaya oleh 3M Indonesia untuk mendistribusikan produk-produk Consumer 3M (seperti Nexcare, Micropore, dll) per 1 April 2022.

Ibu Olin (Salome Cahya), selaku Country Sales Lead - Consumer Business Group 3M Indonesia menandatangani surat penunjukan distributor dan menyerahkan kepada Bapak Edwin Vega Dharma selaku VP Sales &amp; Marketing PPG pada acara seremoni penandatanganan perjanjian kerjasama pada tanggal 4 April 2022.

Acara seremonial tanda awal kerjasama kedua pihak dilangsungkan di lobi kantor pusat PT Parit Padang Global di Pulogadung. Acara dilakukan secara sederhana dengan makna simbolik pemotongan nasi tumpeng sembari berbuka puasa, dengan tetap memperhatikan protokol kesehatan pencegahan penyebaran virus Covid-19.

Penunjukan PPG sebagai mitra distribusi 3M Indonesia menunjukkan kepercayaan yang diberikan prinsipal multinasional ini atas reputasi PPG.

# Signing of Cooperation Agreement between PPG and DKT Indonesia

04

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) appointed and trusted by PT Dharmendra Kumar Tiyagi (DKT) Indonesia to distribute DKT's OTC and Consumer products as of June 1st, 2022.

This partnership between PPG and DKT Indonesia aims to increase distribution of leading brands such as Sutra condoms, Fiesta condoms and other DKT popular brands.

Bapak Dimos Sakellaridis as President Director of DKT Indonesia signed a letter of appointment of Distributor and handed it over to Bapak Rogelio C La O' Jr as President Director of PT Soho Global Health, Tbk and CEO of PPG at the signing ceremony of the agreement at Hotel Borobudur on 24th June 2022.

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) ditunjuk dan dipercaya oleh PT Dharmendra Kumar Tiyagi (DKT) Indonesia untuk mendistribusikan produk-produk OTC dan Consumer DKT per 1 Juni 2022.

Kemitraan PPG dan DKT Indonesia ini bertujuan untuk meningkatkan distribusi merek-merek terkemuka seperti kondom Sutra, kondom Fiesta dan merek-merek populer DKT lainnya.

Bapak Dimos Sakellaridis selaku Presiden Direktur DKT Indonesia menandatangani surat penunjukan Distributor dan menyerahkan kepada Bapak Rogelio C La O' Jr selaku Presiden Direktur PT Soho Global Health, Tbk dan CEO PPG pada acara seremoni penandatanganan perjanjian kerjasama di Hotel Borobudur pada tanggal 24 Juni 2022.
