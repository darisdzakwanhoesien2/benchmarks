## Piero Brambati

### Director / Direktur

Responsible for Finance, Business Development, Business Technology, and Procurement Divisions, including among others:

- To direct, control, assign and provide necessary support and advice in every implementation of the Company's business operations carried out by all business units and support units, so that all of the Company's operational activities run in accordance with the vision, mission, business objectives, strategies, policies, plans and work programs that have been determined;
- To determine strategic directions and provide internal advisors needed in the implementation of the Company's operations; and
- To determine the design, implementation and financial supervision of the Company (the Company group of companies and their subsidiaries).

Bertanggung jawab atas Divisi Keuangan, Pengembangan Bisnis, Teknologi Bisnis, dan Pengadaan, termasuk di antaranya:

- Mengarahkan, mengendalikan, menugaskan dan memberikan dukungan dan saran yang diperlukan dalam setiap pelaksanaan operasional bisnis Perseroan yang dilakukan oleh seluruh unit bisnis dan unit pendukung, agar seluruh kegiatan operasional Perseroan berjalan sesuai dengan visi, misi, sasaran usaha, strategi, kebijakan, rencana, dan program kerja yang ditetapkan;
- Menetapkan arahan strategis dan penyediaan penasihat internal yang diperlukan dalam pelaksanaan operasional Perseroan; dan
- Menetapkan desain, implementasi, dan pengawasan keuangan Perseroan (kelompok perusahaan Perseroan beserta anak perusahaannya).

## Yuliana

### Director / Direktur

Responsible for Legal, Compliance, Corporate Secretary, and Human Resources Division, including among others:

- To establish strategic directions and provide legal services (including provision of internal advisors), as well as ensure compliance with laws and regulations and risk management processes;
- To determine the design, implementation, supervision and enforcement of compliance as well as the audit program of the Company (the Company's group of companies and their subsidiaries);
- To coordinate the implementation of operational tasks in the field of implementation of the internal audit and secretariat of the Company;
- To ensure the implementation and implementation of GCG in the Company;
- To coordinate, control and evaluate the internalization of GCG principles and ethical standards consistently within the Company.

Bertanggung jawab atas Divisi Hukum, Kepatuhan, Sekretaris Perusahaan, dan Sumber Daya Manusia, termasuk di antaranya:

- Menetapkan arahan strategis dan penyediaan pelayanan hukum (termasuk penyediaan penasihat internal), serta memastikan kepatuhan terhadap hukum dan regulasi serta proses manajemen risiko;
- Menetapkan desain, implementasi, pengawasan, dan penegakan kepatuhan serta program audit Perusahaan (kelompok perusahaan Perseroan beserta anak Perusahaannya);
- Mengkoordinasikan pelaksanaan tugas operasional di bidang pelaksanaan audit internal dan kesehretariatan Perseroan;
- Memastikan pelaksanaan dan implementasi GCG di Perseroan;
- Mengkoordinasikan, mengendalikan dan mengevaluasi internalisasi prinsip-prinsip GCG dan Standar Etika secara konsisten dalam Perseroan.
