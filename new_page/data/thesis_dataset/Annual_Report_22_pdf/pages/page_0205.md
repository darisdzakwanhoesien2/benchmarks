|  07. | Decision on the composition of the Board of Commissioners should consider diversity in the required expertise, knowledge and experience

Penentuan komposisi anggota Dewan Komisaris memperhatikan keberagaman keahlian, pengetahuan, dan pengalaman yang dibutuhkan. | Recommendations have been implemented.
The determination of the composition of the members of the Board of Commissioners has taken into account the diversity of expertise, knowledge and experience required by the Company. This can be seen in the profiles and work history of members of the Company's Board of Commissioners which have been described in this Company's Annual Report for the 2022 Financial Year.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.
Penentuan komposisi anggota Dewan Komisaris telah memperhatikan keberagaman keahlian, pengetahuan dan pengalaman yang dibutuhkan oleh Perseroan. Hal ini dapat terlihat pada profil dan riwayat kerja anggota Dewan Komisaris Perseroan yang telah diuraikan dalam Laporan Tahunan Perseroan Tahun Buku 2022 ini.  |

Principles:
Improving the Quality of the Execution of the Duties and Responsibilities of the Board of Commissioners

Prinsip:
Meningkatkan Kualitas Pelaksanaan Tugas dan Tanggung Jawab Dewan Komisaris

|  08. | The Board of Commissioners shall have a self assessment policy to measure the performance of the Board.

Dewan Komisaris mempunyai kebijakan penilaian sendiri (self assessment) untuk menilai kinerja Dewan Komisaris. | Recommendations have been implemented.
The Company currently has a Policy for Assessment of the Performance of the Board of Commissioners, the policy also explains aspects of the assessment and performance appraisal system of the Board of Commissioners.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.
Perseroan saat ini telah memiliki Kebijakan Penilaian atas Kinerja Dewan Komisaris Perseoran, dalam kebijakan tersebut juga menjelaskan perihal aspek-apek penilaian dan sistem penilaian kinerja Dewan Komisaris.  |
|  09. | Policy on self assessment to measure the performance of the Board of Commissioners shall be reported in the Public Company's Annual Report.

Kebijakan penilaian sendiri (self assessment) untuk menilai kinerja Dewan Komisaris, diungkapkan melalui Laporan Tahunan Perusahaan Terbuka. | Recommendations have been implemented.
The self-assessment policy to assess the performance of the Board of Commissioners has been disclosed in the Public Company Annual Report.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.
Kebijakan penilaian sendiri (self assessment) untuk menilai kinerja Dewan Komisaris telah diungkapkan melalui Laporan Tahunan Perusahaan Terbuka.  |
|  10. | The Board of Commissioners shall have a resignation policy for its members in the event of any financial fraud.

Dewan Komisaris mempunyai kebijakan terkait pengunduran diri anggota Dewan Komisaris apabila terlibat dalam kejahatan keuangan. | Recommendations have been implemented.
Policies regarding the resignation of members of the Board of Commissioners due to involvement in financial crimes or other legal violations have been stated in the Company's Board of Commissioners Charter and the Company's Articles of Association. The Board of Commissioners' Charter is available on the Company's Website.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.
Kebijakan terkait pengunduran diri anggota Dewan Komisaris karena terlibat dalam kejahatan keuangan maupun pelanggaran hukum lainnya telah tercantum dalam Piagam Dewan Komisaris Perseroan dan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan. Piagam Dewan Komisaris telah tersedia di dalam Situs Web Perseroan.  |
