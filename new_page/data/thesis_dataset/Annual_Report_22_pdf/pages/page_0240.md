# Recognizing and Respecting Employees' Rights
Mengakui dan Menghormati Hak-Hak Karyawan

## Recruitment Process

Employee recruitment is a process of searching, finding, and selecting candidates to meet the needs of human resources in a company. The function of recruitment is to ensure that human resource needs are met in accordance with the qualifications for the position so as to support smooth work processes in all business units.

Recruitment begins with employee requests from business units, which become a basis for the recruiter to start the candidates sourcing, both internally and externally. In the process of candidates sourcing, the SGH recruitment team does not discriminate against candidates based on ethnicity, religion and race; citizenship; gender; age; disability; political views; and other personal information, but prioritizes the skills and competencies needed in the intended field of work.

SGH uses several methods in candidates sourcing, including walk-in interviews and virtual job fairs. In 2022, we have conducted walk-in interviews in Jakarta, Semarang and Surabaya. The purpose of this activity is not only to speed up the recruitment process, but also to introduce SGH to the public, so that the Company's needs for resources can be met immediately. We also have an internship program and accepted the "Professional Work Practice Programs for Pharmacists" in collaboration with several universities in Indonesia. This certainly provides an opportunity for students to be able to get to know the work environment, so that when they have completed their studies they are expected to be able to apply their knowledge during internship and work practices program.

Another method of candidates sourcing is using social media. In addition to sharing information about job vacancies, we have also posted people activities and various information from the Company that are intended for the public.

## Proses Rekrutmen

Perekrutan karyawan adalah proses mencari, menemukan, dan menyeleksi kandidat untuk memenuhi kebutuhan Sumber Daya Manusia (SDM) di sebuah Perusahaan. Fungsi rekrutmen adalah memastikan kebutuhan SDM terpenuhi sesuai kualifikasi jabatan sehingga mendukung kelancaran proses kerja di seluruh unit bisnis.

Rekrutmen dimulai dengan permintaan kebutuhan karyawan dari unit bisnis, yang menjadi dasar bagi team rekrutmen memulai proses pencarian kandidat, baik internal maupun eksternal. Dalam proses pencarian kandidat, team rekrutmen SGH tidak membeda-bedakan kandidat berdasarkan Suku, Agama dan Ras (SARA), kewarganegaraan, jenis kelamin, usia, disabilitas, pandangan politik, maupun informasi personal lainnya, namun mengutamakan keterampilan dan kompetensi yang diperlukan di bidang pekerjaan yang dituju.

SGH menggunakan beberapa metode dalam proses pencarian kandidat, di antaranya walk-in interview dan virtual job fair. Pada tahun 2022, kami melakukan walk-in interview di Jakarta, Semarang dan Surabaya. Tujuan dari aktivitas ini selain untuk mempercepat proses rekrutmen, juga untuk memperkenalkan SGH ke masyarakat, sehingga kebutuhan SDM Perusahaan dapat segera terpenuhi. Kami juga memiliki program pemagangan dan menerima Praktek Kerja Profesi Apoteker (PKPA) yang bekerjasama dengan beberapa Universitas di Indonesia. Hal ini tentunya memberikan kesempatan kepada pelajar dan mahasiswa untuk dapat mengenal dunia kerja, sehingga saat sudah menyelesaikan studinya, mereka diharapkan dapat mengaplikasikan ilmu yang telah diperoleh sebelumnya selama program pemagangan dan praktek kerja.

Metode lainnya dalam pencarian kandidat adalah memanfaatkan media sosial. Selain membagikan informasi mengenai lowongan pekerjaan, kami juga membagikan aktivitas Karyawan serta berbagai informasi dari Perusahaan kepada publik lewat media sosial.
