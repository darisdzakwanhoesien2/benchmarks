# Supply &amp; Operations Townhall Meeting and Continuous Improvement Competition 2022

01

Continuous Improvement Competition 2022 that has been renamed into "IMBOOST FORCE: IMPROVEMENT BOOSTER FOR EXCELLENCE" Competition 2022 carrying the theme "Spotlight Our Performance" has generated great employees' enthusiasm and 700+ implemented improvement ideas resulted in substantial savings. This year the awarding announcement of IMBOOST FORCE Competition was presented in the Town Hall Meeting Supply &amp; Operations 2022 held during 14-17 November 2022.

The Continuous Improvement Competition aims to stimulate good practices related to continuous improvement. The awards provide recognition to individual's contributions as well as team collaborations in identifying and implementing continuous improvement projects that benefit the company/customer or the wider society through individual project category called QCI (Quality Control Initiative), and group project through QCC (Quality Control Circle) and QCP (Quality Control Project).

This annual competition, reaching in 2022 its 12th edition, once again showcased successful continuous improvement ideas. Since inception in 2011, improvement ideas in our manufacturing site, ranging from reducing energy usage (such as electricity, fuel, gas and water consumption) to improving system and processes, had successfully delivered substantial savings.

Kompetisi Continuous Improvement 2022 yang telah di rename menjadi "IMBOOST FORCE: IMPROVEMENT BOOSTER FOR EXCELLENCE" Competition 2022 mengusung tema "Spotlight Our Performance" telah membangkitkan antusiasme karyawan yang luar biasa dan 700+ ide perbaikan yang diterapkan menghasilkan penghematan yang substansial. Tahun ini pengumuman penganugerahan IMBOOST FORCE Competition dipresentasikan di Town Hall Meeting Supply &amp; Operations 2022 yang diselenggarakan pada 14 - 17 November 2022.

Kompetisi Continuous Improvement bertujuan untuk menstimulasi praktik dan proses bisnis yang terkait dengan perbaikan berkelanjutan. Penghargaan ini memberikan pengakuan atas kontribusi individu serta kolaborasi tim dalam mengidentifikasi dan menerapkan proyek perbaikan berkelanjutan yang bermanfaat bagi perusahaan/pelanggan atau masyarakat luas melalui kategori Proyek Individual yang disebut QCI (Quality Control Initiative), dan kategori Proyek Grup melalui QCC (Quality Control Circle) dan QCP (Quality Control Project).

Kompetisi tahunan yang telah diselenggarakan untuk ke-12 kalinya pada tahun 2022 ini, sekali lagi membuktikan keberhasilan ide-ide continuous improvement. Sejak diinisiasi di tahun 2011, berbagai gagasan perbaikan di lokasi produksi, mulai dari pengurangan penggunaan energi (seperti konsumsi listrik, bahan bakar, gas dan air) hingga perbaikan sistem dan proses, telah berkontribusi untuk penghematan biaya perusahaan secara substansial.
