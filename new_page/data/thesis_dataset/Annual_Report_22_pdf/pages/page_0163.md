In addition, the Board of Directors performance appraisal is also stipulated in the Board of Directors Performance Assessment Policy, namely:

01. All members of the Board of Directors have performance goals based on the duties and responsibilities of each member
02. Performance appraisal of the Board of Directors is conducted every year by the President Director
03. Performance appraisal results of the Board of Directors is reported to the Board of Commissioners through the Nomination and Remuneration Committee.

The Board of Directors' Performance Assessment Policy also regulates the aspects of the Board of Directors' assessment. These aspects include:

01. Lead, manage, and control the Company in accordance with the purpose of the Company
02. Make effort to increase the efficiency and effectiveness of the Company
03. Prepare an annual work plan containing the annual budget of the Company and submit the plan to the Board of Commissioner to obtain approval before the beginning of the following fiscal year
04. Perform his/ her duties and responsibilities pursuant to the provisions of the Articles and the resolutions of the General Meeting of Shareholders
05. Comply with the Articles of Association and the prevailing laws and regulations, and be obliged to perform duties based on principles of professionalism, efficiency, transparency, independency, accountability, responsibility and fairness, i.e. to convey the meeting of the Board of Directors in accordance with the applicable regulations.
