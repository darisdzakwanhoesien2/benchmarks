## 2. Henny Puspawati

### Case Status

Status Perkara

PPG has signed Perjanjian Sewa Menyewa (Lease Agreement) with Mrs. Henny for lease of PPG's Warehouse as stated in the Deed No. 1 dated October 1, 2015 ("Lease Agreement"). Pursuant to the Lease Agreement, PPG is required to pay rent for the first five-year lease period in amount IDR 1,775,000,000,-. This obligation has been carried out by PPG, but in its implementation it was discovered that the Warehouse's IMB is not in accordance with its designation, so that the Warehouse could not be used by PPG, and PPG had asked Mrs. Henny to return the rental fee that had been paid by PPG in the amount of IDR 1,175,000,000. This amount has been agreed by PPG and Mrs. Henny as stated in the Perjanjian Pengembalian Uang Sewa dated April 2, 2019 ("Rental Fee Repayment Agreement"). It is stated in the Rental Fee Repayment Agreement that Mrs. Henny will return the rental fee once Mrs. Henny's villa in Bali has been sold. However PPG has not received the rental fee until date since Mrs. Henny's villa in Bali has not been sold.

PPG telah menandatangani Perjanjian Sewa Menyewa (Perjanjian Sewa) dengan Ibu Henny untuk sewa Gudang PPG sebagaimana tercantum dalam Akta No. 1 tanggal 1 Oktober 2015 ("Perjanjian Sewa"). Berdasarkan Perjanjian Sewa, PPG diharuskan membayar sewa untuk masa sewa lima tahun pertama sebesar Rp 1.775.000.000,-. Kewajiban ini sudah dilaksanakan oleh PPG, namun dalam pelaksanaannya ditemukan IMB Gudang yang tidak sesuai dengan peruntukannya, sehingga Gudang tersebut tidak dapat digunakan oleh PPG, dan PPG telah meminta kepada Ibu Henny untuk mengembalikan biaya sewa tersebut yang telah dibayarkan oleh PPG sebesar Rp 1.175.000.000. Jumlah ini telah disepakati oleh PPG dan Ibu Henny sebagaimana tercantum dalam Perjanjian Pengembalian Uang Sewa tanggal 2 April 2019 ("Perjanjian Pembayaran Kembali Biaya Sewa"). Dalam Perjanjian Pelunasan Biaya Sewa disebutkan bahwa Ibu Henny akan mengembalikan biaya sewa setelah vila Ibu Henny di Bali telah terjual. Namun PPG belum menerima biaya sewa hingga saat ini karena vila Ibu Henny di Bali belum terjual.

### Risks Faced by the Company and Their Impacts on the Company

Risiko yang Dihadapi dan Pengaruhnya terhadap Perseroan

PPG will receive a full refund of the invoice amounting to IDR 1,175,000,000 if the villa in Bali has been sold.

PPG akan memperoleh pengembalian tagihan secara penuh yang jumlahnya sebesar Rp 1.175.000.000 jika vila di Bali sudah terjual.

Based on Appeal Decision No. 71/PDT/2022/PT YYK from Pengadilan Tinggi Yogyakarta which has been a final and binding decision, Mrs. Henny was ordered inter alia to repayment the rental fee to PPG in amount IDR 1,775,000,000; and stating the security seizure ("sita jaminan") of Mrs. Henny's villa in Bali to be valid and effective. However, PPG has received an objection for the stipulation of security seizure from Mr. Gatot Widjaja Tjipta (ex-husband of Mrs. Henny Puspawati).

Berdasarkan Putusan Banding No. 71/PDT/2022/PT YYK dari Pengadilan Tinggi Yogyakarta yang telah menjadi keputusan final dan mengikat, Ibu Henny diperintahkan antara lain untuk membayar kembali biaya sewa ke PPG sebesar Rp 1.775.000.000,-; dan menyatakan jaminan sita (sita jaminan) vila Ibu Henny di Bali sah dan efektif. Namun PPG telah menerima keberatan atas penetapan sita jaminan dari Bapak Gatot Widjaja Tjipta (mantan suami Ibu Henny Puspawati).
