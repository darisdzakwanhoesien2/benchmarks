Reports of Nomination and Remuneration Committee:
Throughout 2022, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee has carried out the following:

01. Provide recommendations to the Board of Commissioners regarding the amount of salary and honorarium increases for members of the Board of Commissioners and members of the Board of Directors of the Company

02. Evaluate the Company's remuneration and nomination policies

03. Supervise the implementation of talent and succession programs for the employees of the Company and Subsidiaries

04. Propose training programs to improve competencies and skills for employees of the Company and Subsidiaries

05. Review the appropriateness of the remuneration policy and facilities provided for the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors and employees of the Company and Subsidiaries, as well as providing recommendations for necessary improvements/changes

Independence of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee
During year 2022, each member of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee has acted independently and fulfilled aspects of independence in carrying out their duties, in accordance with the terms and conditions that apply as stated in the Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 34/POJK.04/2015 concerning the Nomination and Remuneration Committee of Issuers or Public Companies and the Decree of the Board of Commissioners of the Company Number 08/SK-Dekom/SGH/VI/2020 dated June 19, 2020 concerning the Establishment of Guidelines for the Company's Nomination and Remuneration Committee dated June 19, 2020 concerning Establishment of the Company's Nomination and Remuneration Committee Guidelines.

Laporan Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi
Sepanjang 2022, Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi telah melaksanakan hal-hal sebagai berikut:

01. Memberikan rekomendasi kepada Dewan Komisaris mengenai besaran kenaikan gaji dan honorarium bagi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan anggota Direksi Perseroan

02. Mengevaluasi kebijakan remunerasi dan nominasi Perseroan

03. Melakukan pengawasan terhadap pelaksanaan program talenta dan suksesi bagi para karyawan Perseroan dan Entitas Anak

04. Mengusulkan program pelatihan untuk meningkatkan kompetensi dan keterampilan bagi para karyawan Perseroan dan Entitas Anak

05. Mengkaji kelayakan kebijakan remunerasi dan fasilitas-fasilitas yang disediakan bagi Dewan Komisaris, Direksi dan para karyawan Perseroan dan Entitas Anak, serta memberikan rekomendasi perbaikan/perubahan yang diperlukan

Independensi Komite Nominasi Dan Remunerasi
Sepanjang tahun 2022, setiap anggota Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi telah bertindak independen dan memenuhi aspek independensi dalam menjalankan tugasnya, sesuai dengan syarat dan ketentuan yang berlaku sebagaimana tercantum dalam Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 34/POJK.04/2015 tentang Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik dan Keputusan Dewan Komisaris Perseroan Nomor 08/SK-Dekom/SGH/VI/2020 tanggal 19 Juni 2020 tentang Pembentukan Pedoman Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi Perseroan tanggal 19 Juni 2020 tentang Pembentukan Pedoman Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi Perseroan.
