The Company's shareholders periodically evaluate the performance of the Board of Commissioners at least once a year. Performance appraisal is carried out on the implementation of the duties and responsibilities of the Board of Commissioners in supervising and managing the Company as stated in the laws and regulations and the Company's Articles of Association.

In addition, based on the Policy on Performance Appraisal of the Board of Commissioners applicable in the Company, the following are the provisions for the Board of Commissioners performance assessment:

01. Performance appraisal of the Board of Commissioners is performed using self-assessment every year collegially
02. Performance appraisal of each member of the Board of Commissioners is performed by the President Commissioner every year based on role and responsibilities of the Board of Commissioners
03. Each member of the Board of Commissioners conducts a self-assessment, then the results are consolidated for joint review by all members of the Board of Commissioners in order to determine the effectiveness of the Board of Commissioners and the areas that need improvement
04. The Board of Commissioners prepares the corrective actions, and the President Commissioner ensures the implementation of the corrective actions
05. Performance appraisal of the Board of Commissioners is conducted by considering several components, which include structure and composition of the Board of Commissioners, strategy implementation and company management, the effectiveness of the committee, risk management implementation and internal control, and the implementation of Good Corporate Governance

The Policy on Performance Appraisal of the Board of Commissioners also regulates the aspects of the Board of Commissioners' assessment. These aspects of the assessment are among others:

01. Supervise the management of the Company performed by the Board of Directors
02. Review, examine, and approve the Annual Work Plan of the Company
03. Carry out special tasks assigned to the member(s) of the Board of Commissioners in accordance with the Articles of Association, applicable laws and regulations and/or resolutions of the General Meeting of Shareholders
04. Perform his/her duties and responsibilities in accordance with the provisions of the Articles of Association and the resolutions of the General Meeting of Shareholders
05. Comply with the Articles of the Association and the prevailing laws and regulations and be obliged to perform duties based on principles of professionalism, efficiency, transparency, independency, accountability, responsibility and fairness, among others to convey the BOC meeting in accordance with the applicable regulations

Pemegang saham Perseroan secara berkala melakukan evaluasi atas kinerja Dewan Komisaris setidaknya satu kali dalam setahun. Penilaian kinerja dilakukan terhadap pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab Dewan Komisaris dalam mengawasi dan mengelola Perseroan sebagaimana yang tercantum dalam peraturan perundang-undangan dan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan.

Di samping itu, berdasarkan Kebijakan Penilaian atas Kinerja Dewan Komisaris yang berlaku di Perseroan, maka di bawah ini merupakan ketentuan atas penilaian kinerja Dewan Komisaris:

01. Penilaian Kinerja Dewan Komisaris dilakukan dengan metode self-assessment setiap tahun secara kolegial
02. Penilaian kinerja Anggota Dewan Komisaris dilakukan oleh Komisaris Utama setiap tahun berdasarkan tugas dan tanggung jawab Dewan Komisaris
03. Masing-masing anggota Dewan Komisaris melakukan self-assessment, kemudian hasilnya dikonsolidasi untuk ditinjau bersama oleh seluruh anggota Dewan Komisaris guna menetapkan efektivitas dari Dewan Komisaris beserta area-area yang perlu dilakukan perbaikan
04. Dewan Komisaris menyusun rencana tindak lanjut perbaikan dan Komisaris Utama memastikan pelaksanaan rencana perbaikan tersebut
05. Penilaian kinerja Dewan Komisaris dilakukan dengan mempertimbangkan beberapa komponen, antara lain: struktur dan komposisi Dewan Komisaris, pelaksanaan strategi dan pengelolaan perusahaan, efektivitas pelaksanaan program kerja komite, penerapan manajemen risiko dan pengendalian internal, serta penerapan prinsip Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik

Kebijakan Penilaian atas Kinerja Dewan Komisaris juga mengatur perihal aspek-aspek penilaian Dewan Komisaris. Aspek-aspek penilaian tersebut antara lain:

01. Mengawasi pengurusan Perseroan yang dilaksanakan oleh Direksi
02. Meninjau, memeriksa, dan menyetujui Rencana Kerja Tahunan Perseroan
03. Melakukan tugas khusus yang diberikan kepada anggota Dewan Komisaris sesuai dengan Anggaran Dasar, hukum dan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku dan/atau keputusan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham
04. Melakukan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya sesuai dengan ketentuan-ketentuan Anggaran Dasar dan keputusan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham
05. Mematuhi Anggaran Dasar serta hukum dan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku dan wajib melaksanakan tugasnya sesuai dengan prinsip-prinsip profesionalisme, efisiensi, transparansi, independensi, akuntabilitas, pertanggungjawaban dan kewajaran, antara lain melaksanakan Rapat Dewan Komisaris sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku
