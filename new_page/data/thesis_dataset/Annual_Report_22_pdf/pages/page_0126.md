# Implementation of Good Corporate Governance Principles

# Penerapan atas Tata Kelola Perusahaan

The Company's commitment in implementing GCG is reflected in its business activities which are always based on the fulfilment of the following GCG principles:

Komitmen Perseroan dalam penerapan GCG tercermin dalam pelaksanaan kegiatan usaha yang selalu didasari atas pemenuhan asas-asas GCG sebagai berikut:

|  Principle Asas | Description Penjelasan | Implementation Penerapan  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Transparency Transparansi | Providing material information that is relevant, precise, clear, accurate, easily accessible and easily understood by shareholders and stakeholders. The decision-making process is carried out in a transparent manner in order to protect the interests of all stakeholders. Menyediakan informasi materiil yang relevan, tepat, jelas, akurat, mudah diakses, dan mudah dipahami oleh pemegang saham dan para pemangku kepentingan. Proses pengambilan keputusan dilakukan secara transparan dalam rangka melindungi kepentingan seluruh pemangku kepentingan | 01. Compilation of Annual Company Work Plans and Budgets 02. Compilation of Annual Reports, Sustainability Report and Good Corporate Governance Reports 03. Publication of Financial Statements (annual, semester, and quarterly) 04. Documenting every decision-making process during the meetings of the Board of Directors, Board of Commissioners and Committees of the Company 01. Menyusun Rencana Kerja dan Anggaran Perseroan Tahunan 02. Membuat Laporan Tahunan, Laporan Keberlanjutan, dan Laporan Tata Kelola 03. Mempublikasikan Laporan Keuangan (tahunan, semesteran, ataupun triwulanan) 04. Mendokumentasikan setiap proses pengambilan keputusan selama Rapat Direksi, Dewan Komisaris maupun Komite Perusahaan  |
|  Accountability Akuntabilitas | Providing clarity of functions, structure, systems, and accountability of each organ of the Company on implementation of its duties and responsibilities in order for the operational activities to be carried out effectively. Managing the Company in a proper and measurable manner, ensuring alignment with the interest of shareholders and other stakeholders. Working with high accountability and be responsible for all actions. Memberikan kejelasan fungsi, struktur, system, dan pertanggungjawaban setiap organ Perseroan atas pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawabnyaagar pengelolaan kegiatan operasional Perseroan terlaksana secara efektif. Mengelola Perseroan secara tepat dan terukur, memastikan keselarasan dengan kepentingan pemegang saham dan pemangku kepentingan lainnya. Bekerja dengan akuntabilitas tinggi dan bertanggungjawab atas semua tindakan yang dilakukan | 01. Availability of an organizational structure, Committee Charter, standard operating procedures, guidelines and manuals that contain clear functions, implementation and responsibilities of Shareholders, Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, Committees, Corporate Secretary and business units of the Company 02. Implementation of 3 levels of accountability in each activity, including: • Corporate Accountability • Team Accountability • Individual Accountability 03. Implement a check and balance mechanism in relation to the authority and role of each Company organ in managing the Company. 04. There are clearly defined Key Performance Indicators (KPI) and operational targets. 05. Preparation of financial reports which involving external audits (public accounting firms/“KAP”) which are carried out by taking into account the aspects of independence in terms of personnel, processes and audit results.  |
