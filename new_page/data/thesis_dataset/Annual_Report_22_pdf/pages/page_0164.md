Shareholders and the Company's Board of Commissioners periodically evaluate the performance of the Board of Directors, which shall be at least once a year. Performance assessment is carried out on the implementation of the duties and responsibilities of the Board of Directors in supervising and managing the Company as stated in the laws and regulations and the Company's Articles of Association.

Based on the guidelines as stipulated in the Board of Directors Charter and the Policy for Assessment of the Performance of the Directors, the Company conveys that throughout the 2022 period, the Company has complied with the provisions, aspects, and assessments of the Board of Directors.

The Board of Directors' assessment of the Committees that support the implementation of the duties of the Board of Directors':

Every year, the Company's Board of Directors evaluates the performance of its supporting organs, including the Corporate Secretary and the Internal Audit Unit. The assessment is carried out using a self-assessment system, peer evaluation, or with other systems decided in an internal meeting of the Board of Directors.

In year 2022, the Board of Directors assesses that the supporting organs have carried out their respective functions well, in accordance with the purpose of their formation.

01. The Corporate Secretary has carried out the duties, responsibilities, and authorities related to the corporate secretary in accordance with the Decree of the Board of Directors of the Company regarding the Appointment of the Company's Corporate Secretary. During 2022, the attendance rate of the Corporate Secretary in meetings reached 100%.

02. The Internal Audit Unit has carried out the duties, responsibilities, and authorities related to the internal audit unit in accordance with the Decree of the Company's Board of Directors regarding the Company's Internal Audit Unit Charter. During 2022, the attendance rate of members of the Internal Audit Unit in meeting reached 100%, as explained in more detail in the section on the Internal Audit Unit.

Remuneration for the Board of Directors

Remuneration reviews were based on the Company's business development and salary surveys. The total salaries and benefits (excluding bonus) paid to the Board of Directors of Group Companies amounted to IDR 27,983,156,452 (twenty seven billion nine hundred eighty three million one hundred fifty six thousand four hundred fifty two Rupiah) in year 2022.

Pemegang saham dan Dewan Komisaris Perseroan secara berkala melakukan evaluasi atas kinerja Direksi, setidaknya satu kali dalam setahun. Penilaian kinerja dilakukan terhadap pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab Direksi dalam mengawasi dan mengelola Perseroan sebagaimana yang tercantum dalam peraturan perundang-undangan dan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan.

Atas pedoman sebagaimana diatur dalam Piagam Direksi (Board of Directors Charter) dan Kebijakan Penilaian atas Kinerja Direktur, maka Perseroan menyampaikan bahwa sepanjang periode tahun 2022, Perseroan telah memenuhi ketentuan-ketentuan, aspek, dan penilaian terhadap Direksi.

Penilaian Direksi terhadap Komite yang mendukung pelaksanaan tugas Direksi:

Setiap tahun, Direksi Perseroan melakukan penilaian atas kinerja organ pendukungnya, termasuk juga Sekretaris Perusahaan dan Unit Audit Internal. Penilaian tersebut dilakukan menggunakan sistem penilaian sendiri, peer evaluation, atau dengan sistem lain yang diputuskan dalam rapat internal Direksi.

Pada tahun 2022, Direksi menilai organ pendukung tersebut telah menjalankan fungsinya masing-masing dengan baik, sesuai dengan tujuan pembentukannya.

01. Sekretaris Perusahaan telah melaksanakan tugas, tanggung jawab, dan wewenang terkait sekretaris perusahaan sesuai dengan Surat Keputusan Direksi Perseroan perihal Pengangkatan Sekretaris Perusahaan (Corporate Secretary) Perseroan. Sepanjang tahun 2022, tingkat kehadiran Sekretaris Perusahaan dalam meeting mencapai 100%.

02. Unit Audit Internal telah melaksanakan tugas, tanggung jawab, dan wewenang terkait unit audit internal sesuai dengan Surat Keputusan Direksi Perseroan perihal Piagam Unit Audit Internal Perseroan. Sepanjang tahun 2022, tingkat kehadiran anggota Unit Audit Internal dalam meeting mencapai 100%, sebagaimana lebih rinci dijelaskan pada bagian Unit Audit Internal.
