# Distribution and Logistic Division
Divisi Distribusi dan Logistik

## Overview

We established our distribution business in 1956, giving us a long track record as a successful pharmaceutical and consumer goods distribution partner. As the distribution arm of SOHO Global Health (the "Group"), Parit Padang Global (PPG) distributes both our own products and products for over 40 third-party partners ("PPG Principals"). We are distributors for Indonesian Pharmaceutical &amp; MedTech companies, such as Yarindo, Fahrenheit, Dipa Pharmalab, Global Dispomedika (GDM), Berno, Pharos Indonesia, Kalbe Farma, Hexpharm Jaya, Sari Enesis, and multinational companies, such as Bayer, Johnson &amp; Johnson, Mundipharma, L'Oreal, Haleon, BBraun, Eisai, IDS Med, and 3M. We represent more than two-thirds of our PPG portfolio of products on an exclusive basis, which can vary in scope and range from nationwide exclusivity to market channel or product exclusivity.

Distribution through PPG is a key enabler for our Professional Products and Consumer Health businesses. Our extensive distribution network ensures that any new products we launch are available across Indonesia through all of our distribution channels, from hospitals to minimarkets.

Our logistic network provides extensive coverage across the entire Indonesian archipelago, with a reach of approximately 95% of hospitals and pharmacies in Indonesia, as well as many modern and traditional outlets. This is made possible through our 25 branches and three sales offices, as well as over 30 third-party sub-distributors.

Our platform is supported by over 500 outsourced intermediate and last-mile delivery vehicles, as well as a cold storage logistics chain for products that require 2-8 degree Celsius warehousing. Our distribution network has the capacity to handle over 27,900 pallets across all branches, including our central warehouse. We are proud to be one of the few distributors in Indonesia awarded the International Good Storage and Distribution Practice Certificate from the Societe Generale de Surveillance, as well as the ISO 9001:2015 certificate for quality management systems, the Certificate of Method of Good Distribution of Medicines (CDOB) from BPOM RI, and the Certificate of Method of Good Distribution of Healthcare Devices (CDAKB).

## 2022 Highlights

The Distribution and Logistics Division registered close to double-digit net sales growth in 2022, rising from IDR 5,464 billion to IDR 5,987 billion, which represents a 9.6% increase. This performance was mainly due to the success of principals which rebounded post-Covid-19 as well as a few new principal additions. However, the gross profit margin remained strong at 8.7% due to well managed product portfolio mix.

## Gambaran Umum

Kami telah mendirikan bisnis distribusi sejak tahun 1956, dan hingga kini telah menunjukkan rekam jejak yang panjang sebagai mitra distribusi yang sukses untuk produk farmasi dan produk konsumen. Sebagai kepanjangan tangan distribusi dari SOHO Global Health ("Grup"), Parit Padang Global (PPG) mendistribusikan produk-produk milik Grup SGH dan produk untuk lebih dari 40 mitra pihak ketiga ("Prinsipal PPG"). Kami adalah distributor untuk perusahaan farmasi &amp; alat kesehatan lokal, seperti Yarindo, Fahrenheit, Dipa Pharmalab, Global Dispomedika (GDM), Berno, Pharos Indonesia, Kalbe Farma, Hexpharm Jaya, Sari Enesis, dan perusahaan multinasional, seperti Bayer, Johnson &amp; Johnson, Mundipharma, L'Oreal, Haleon, BBraun, Eisai, IDS Med, dan 3M. Sebanyak dua per tiga dari produk dalam portofolio produk PPG kami distribusikan secara eksklusif, yang memiliki ruang lingkup berbeda-beda dari eksklusivitas secara nasional hingga eksklusivitas saluran pasar atau eksklusivitas produk.

Distribusi melalui PPG merupakan pendorong utama pertumbuhan bisnis Produk Profesional dan Kesehatan Konsumen kami. Jaringan distribusi kami yang luas memastikan bahwa semua produk baru yang kami luncurkan tersedia di seluruh Indonesia melalui semua saluran distribusi PPG, dari rumah sakit hingga minimarket.

Jaringan logistik kami menyediakan cakupan yang luas di seluruh kepulauan Indonesia, dengan jangkauan sekitar 95% rumah sakit dan apotek di Indonesia, serta gerai-gerai modern dan tradisional. Hal ini dimungkinkan melalui cakupan 25 cabang dan tiga (3) kantor penjualan kami, serta lebih dari 30 subdistributor pihak ketiga.

Platform kami didukung oleh lebih dari 500 armada pengiriman, serta ruang penyimpanan pendingin (cold storage) untuk produk yang memerlukan penyimpanan suhu 2-8 derajat Celsius. Jaringan distribusi kami memiliki kapasitas untuk menangani lebih dari 27.900 palet di seluruh cabang, termasuk gudang pusat kami. Kami bangga menjadi salah satu distributor di Indonesia yang mendapatkan Sertifikat International Good Storage and Distribution Practice dari Societe Generale de Surveillance, serta sertifikasi ISO 9001:2015 untuk sistem manajemen mutu, Sertifikat Cara Distribusi Obat yang Baik (CDOB) dari BPOM RI, dan Sertifikat Cara Distribusi Alat Kesehatan yang Baik (CDAKB).

## Pencapaian 2022

Divisi Distribusi dan Logistik mencatat pertumbuhan penjualan bersih yang hampir mencapai dua digit pada tahun 2022, naik dari IDR 5.464 miliar menjadi IDR 5.987 miliar, atau mengalami peningkatan sebesar 9,6%. Kinerja ini terutama disebabkan oleh keberhasilan prinsip-prinsipal yang pulih pasca pandemi Covid-19 serta beberapa tambahan prinsip baru. Namun, marjin laba kotor tetap kuat pada rasio 8,7% karena portofolio produk yang dikelola dengan baik.
