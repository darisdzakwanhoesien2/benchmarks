## Indonesia economic outlook 2023 - 2024
### Outlook perekonomian Indonesia 2023 - 2024

Despite persistent global economic turmoil, the Indonesian economy remains resilient with a promising outlook. Bank Indonesia projects solid national economic growth in Indonesia in 2023 in the 4.5-5.3% range before accelerating to 4.7-5.5% in 2024, underpinned by private consumption, investment and positive export performance against a backdrop of global economic moderation. Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation is projected to track a downward trend and return to the 3.0%±1% target in 2023 and 2.5%±1% in 2024.
