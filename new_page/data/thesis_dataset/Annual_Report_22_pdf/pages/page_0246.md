# Training and Development

In the midst of the COVID-19 pandemic situation, company remains committed to improving employee knowledge and skills, instilling values and maintaining a work environment that encourages employee creativity. Company believes that employee development must be carried out from time to time, therefore various trainings covering technical and soft skills, as well as mental health topic are still being carried-out. Development programs with digital approach i.e. e-learning and webinars become an adaptive learning method in the midst of the pandemic.

During 2022, SGH total training duration reached 11,083 hours. The average training hours per employee at the end of 2022 was 4.6 hours per employee per year, as follows:

|   | 2020 | 2021 | 2022  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  Total Training Hours / Total Jam Pelatihan | 3,159 | 8,146 | 11,083  |
|  Employee Number / Jumlah Karyawan | 868 | 2,477 | 2,584  |

HRD conducted the "SGH Webinar Series", a series of webinars that were held online and attended by employees, both located at the head office and branch offices, even during the implementation of working from home (WFH) set-up. The company encourages SGH leaders to share knowledge with employees, with the aim of adding insight regarding certain topics or new skills that can be applied both at work and in daily life. In addition, employees get to know SGH's leaders more closely, where they can also learn a lot from the experiences of these leaders.

On certain topic such as mental health and health talks, we have invited professional speakers from several institutions. Various topics were chosen to broaden employees' insight into maintaining their mental health and to pay more attention to personal and family health, so that they remain fit and productive in their activities and are able to contribute to the Company and society.

# Pelatihan dan Pengembangan

Di tengah situasi pandemi COVID-19, Perusahaan tetap berkomitmen meningkatkan pengetahuan dan keterampilan karyawan, menanamkan nilai-nilai Perusahaan serta memelihara lingkungan kerja yang mendorong kreativitas. Perusahaan percaya bahwa pengembangan karyawan harus dilakukan dari waktu ke waktu, oleh karenanya berbagai pelatihan yang mencakup topik teknis dan soft skill, serta topik yang membahas tentang kesehatan mental tetap dilakukan. Pelatihan dengan pendekatan digital berupa e-learning dan webinar menjadi metode pembelajaran yang adaptif di tengah situasi pandemi.
