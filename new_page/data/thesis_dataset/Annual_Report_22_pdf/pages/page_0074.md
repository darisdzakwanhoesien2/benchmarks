Head of Internal Audit Unit Profile
Profil Kepala Unit Audit Internal

The Internal Audit Unit is led by a Head of the Internal Audit Unit who is appointed by the Board of Directors through a Decree of the Board of Directors and reports to the President Director and acts independently in carrying out his duties and responsibilities.

Unit Audit Internal dipimpin oleh seorang Kepala Unit Audit Internal yang ditunjuk oleh Direksi melalui suatu Surat Keputusan Direksi dan bertanggung jawab kepada Presiden Direktur serta bertindak secara independen dalam melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya.

![img-81.jpeg](img-81.jpeg)

The Internal Audit Unit is formed based on the Decree of Board of Directors Number 05/SK-Dir/SGH/VI/2020 concerning the Establishment of the Company's Internal Audit Unit dated June 19, 2020.

The Internal Audit Unit is responsible to the President Director and acts independently in carrying out its duties and responsibilities. In carrying out its responsibilities, the Internal Audit Unit also cooperates and coordinates with the Audit Committee. The Internal Audit Unit submits a report on the results of the audits it has carried out as well as objective suggestions and recommendations for improvement to the President Director and the Audit Committee.

Mr. Patrick Lian Abuan Go was appointed as Head of the Internal Audit Unit based on the Decree of the Board of Directors of the Company Number 07/SK-Dir/SGH/VI/2020 concerning the Appointment of Patrick Lian Abuan Go as Head of the Internal Audit Unit of the Company dated June 19, 2020.

Unit Audit Internal dibentuk berdasarkan Keputusan Direksi Perseroan Nomor 05/SK-Dir/SGH/VI/2020 tentang Pembentukan Unit Audit Internal Perseroan tertanggal 19 Juni 2020.

Unit Audit Internal bertanggung jawab kepada Presiden Direktur dan bertindak secara independen dalam melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya. Dalam melaksanakan tanggung jawabnya tersebut, Unit Audit Internal juga bekerjasama dan berkoordinasi dengan Komite Audit. Unit Audit Internal menyampaikan laporan hasil audit yang telah dilakukannya serta saran dan rekomendasi perbaikan yang objektif kepada Presiden Direktur dan Komite Audit.
