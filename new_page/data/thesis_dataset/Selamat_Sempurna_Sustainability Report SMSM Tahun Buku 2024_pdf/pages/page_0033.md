## PROFIL PERUSAHAAN [2-1]

PT Selamat Sempurna Tbk merupakan Perseroan terkemuka yang telah lama berkiprah dalam menyediakan solusi otomotif dan suku cadang. Selain Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM), Original Equipment Supplier (OES) dan aftermarket, Perseroan memproduksi serta menjual filter, radiator, alat pengangkat dan komponen kendaraan lainnya, yang meliputi pembuatan karoseri, dump truck, tangki, box, trailer dan dump hoist, serta produk komponen seperti tangki bahan bakar, knalpot dan pipa rem. Perseroan juga memiliki dua merek dagang utama yang telah terdaftar di lebih dari 130 negara, yaitu SAKURA Filter dan ADR Radiator.

Sampai akhir tahun 2024, Perseroan memiliki 5 (lima) entitas anak dan 2 (dua) entitas asosiasi, serta kerja sama perusahaan internasional melalui perjanjian bantuan teknik dan lisensi. PT Adrindo Intiperkasa merupakan entitas induk terakhir dari Perseroan. [2-1, 2-2]

## COMPANY PROFILE [2-1]

PT Selamat Sempurna Tbk is a leading company with a long-standing presence in providing automotive solutions and spare parts. In addition to Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM), Original Equipment Supplier (OES) and aftermarket, the Company manufactures and sells filters, radiators, lifting equipment and other vehicle components, including the production of bodywork, dump trucks, tanks, boxes, trailers and dump hoists, as well as component products such as fuel tanks, exhausts and brake pipes. The Company also owns two main trademarks registered in over 130 countries, namely SAKURA Filter and ADR Radiator.

As of the end of 2024, the Company has 5 (five) subsidiaries and 2 (two) associated entities, as well as international partnerships through technical assistance and licensing agreements. PT Adrindo Intiperkasa is the ultimate parent entity of the Company. [2-1, 2-2]
