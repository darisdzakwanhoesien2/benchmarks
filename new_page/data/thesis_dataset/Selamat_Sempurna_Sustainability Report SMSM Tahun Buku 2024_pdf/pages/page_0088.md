# Penggunaan Air

Air merupakan kebutuhan sangat penting bagi makhluk hidup, yang apabila tidak digunakan dengan sebaik mungkin, dapat memberikan risiko dampak tidak baik bagi lingkungan. Kebutuhan air bersih Perseroan dipenuhi melalui pembelian dari perusahaan air minum swasta PT Aetra Air Tangerang dan pemanfaatan air tanah yang sudah memiliki izin SIPA (Surat Izin Pengambilan Air). Perseroan menggunakan air bersih untuk kegiatan produksi, termasuk pencucian fasilitasnya, mengelola limbah padat dengan incinerator, cooling tower dan keperluan karyawan di MCK (Mandi, Cuci, Kakus).

Di samping itu, Perseroan tetap berupaya untuk melakukan efisiensi penggunaan air guna kelestarian lingkungan hidup yang berkelanjutan. Konservasi air juga dilakukan dengan pembuatan lubang biopori dan sumur resapan, serta mengolah air limbah hingga menggunakan sistem sirkulasi pada cooling tower untuk mendinginkan mesin atau mold.

# Water Usage

Water is a vital resource for all living organisms, and if not used properly, it can pose risks of negative environmental impacts. The Company's clean water needs are met through purchases from the private water utility company PT Aetra Air Tangerang and the use of groundwater for which the Company holds a Water Withdrawal Permit (SIPA). The clean water is used for production activities, including facility cleaning, managing solid waste with an incinerator, operating cooling towers and for employee needs at the sanitation facilities (bathrooms, washing and toilets).

In addition, the Company continues to make efforts to improve water usage efficiency for the sustainability of the environment. Water conservation is also carried out through the creation of biopore holes and infiltration wells, as well as treating wastewater and implementing a circulation system in the cooling towers to cool machines or molds.
