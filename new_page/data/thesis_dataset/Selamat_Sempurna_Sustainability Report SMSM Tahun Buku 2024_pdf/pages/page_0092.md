## Freedom of Association

Employees are given the freedom to express their opinions and aspirations with good ethics and in accordance with the applicable regulations in the Company. The Company has a Collective Labor Agreement (PKB) that was updated on March 9, 2023, which contains the terms of employment, rights and obligations of both parties based on the applicable laws and regulations. The PKB is the result of negotiations between the Company and the Labor Union, which is periodically reviewed and updated every three years.
