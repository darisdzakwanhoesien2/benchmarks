# Tata Kelola Keberlanjutan

Sustainability Governance

Perseroan berkomitmen untuk menerapkan pengendalian gratifikasi dan anti penyuapan guna mendukung upaya pemberantasan korupsi di lingkungan Perseroan dan melakukan sosialisasi kebijakan anti korupsi kepada seluruh karyawan dan manajemen Perseroan. Perseroan memanfaatkan berbagai kesempatan untuk menyampaikan sosialisasi ini, termasuk saat pelaksanaan program orientasi kepada karyawan baru, Direksi dan/atau Dewan Komisaris baru dan saat menjelang periode hari raya.

Dalam proses penerapannya, perwujudan dari komitmen tersebut tertuang dalam kebijakan Perseroan berupa:

1. Kode Etik;
2. Pedoman Penanganan Gratifikasi yang disahkan berdasarkan Keputusan Direksi Nomor: 009/DU/ADRG/VIII/2015 tanggal 18 Agustus 2015, yang selaras dengan Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan (Corporate Governance Guidelines) dan Pedoman Perilaku (Code of Conduct), serta nilai-nilai Perseroan.

The Company is committed to implementing gratuity control and anti-bribery to support efforts to eradicate corruption within the Company and disseminate anti-corruption policies to all employees and the Company's management. The Company took advantage of various opportunities to convey this outreach, including during the orientation program of new-employees, new-onboard of Directors and/or Commissioners of the Company and when approaching the festive periods.

In the process of implementation, the embodiment of this commitment is contained in the Company's policy in the form of:

1. Code of Conduct;
2. Gratification Handling Guidelines which have been approved based on the Board of Directors Decree Number: 009/DU/ADRG/VIII/2015 dated August 18, 2015. The Gratification Handling Guidelines are in line with the Corporate Governance Guidelines and Code of Conduct, as well as the values that apply in the Company.
