# Komunikasi K3 [403-4]

Perseroan secara konsisten melakukan komunikasi di awal atau akhir waktu kerja dari kantor pusat hingga area operasional mengenai kebijakan, program dan pencegahan terjadinya kecelakaan kerja. Selain itu, di setiap tempat kerja juga diberlakukan safety talk atau pertemuan keselamatan. Komunikasi dinilai sebagai cara yang sangat membantu Perseroan karena efisiensi dan keefektifannya.

Panitia Pembina Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (P2K3) bertanggung jawab untuk membina, mengawasi dan mengevaluasi pelaksanaan SMK3 dan investigasi insiden yang terjadi.

# OSH Communication [403-4]

The Company consistently communicates at the beginning or end of working hours from the headquarters to operational areas regarding policies, programs and accident prevention measures. Additionally, safety talks or safety meetings are held at every workplace. Communication is considered a very helpful method for the Company due to its efficiency and effectiveness.

The Occupational Safety and Health Committee (P2K3) is responsible for mentoring, monitoring and evaluating the implementation of the OSHMS (Occupational Safety and Health Management System) and investigating incidents that occur.
