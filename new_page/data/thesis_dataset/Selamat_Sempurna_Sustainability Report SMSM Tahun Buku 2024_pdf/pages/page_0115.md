Dalam mencegah dan memitigasi risiko yang timbul, Perseroan mengimplementasikan Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) pada sistem manajemen persediaan. Lebih dari itu, setiap penanggung jawab dapat melihat ketersediaan barang di gudang, jumlah barang masuk dan keluar, serta kapasitas gudang secara real time melalui program System Applications and Products in Data Processing (SAP).

Hingga akhir tahun 2024, Perseroan tidak mencatat adanya tumpahan, kecelakaan, atau insiden lain yang menyebabkan kerusakan lingkungan selama proses distribusi.

In order to prevent and mitigate emerging risks, the Company implements an Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system in its inventory management. Furthermore, each responsible party can monitor the availability of goods in the warehouse, track the inflow and outflow of goods, and assess the warehouse capacity in real-time through the System Applications and Products in Data Processing (SAP) program.

By the end of 2024, the Company has not recorded any spills, accidents, or other incidents that caused environmental damage during the distribution process.
