# Pengembangan Kompetensi

Karyawan merupakan aset penting bagi Perseroan, sehingga kualitas karyawan akan menjadi salah satu aspek yang dikembangkan. Program pengembangan kompetensi karyawan telah dilaksanakan sesuai kebutuhan Perseroan, salah satunya ialah melalui pelatihan. Selain itu, untuk karyawan yang berpotensi khusus juga akan dikembangkan melalui ADR Supervisory Development Program, Management Development Program, atau Management Trainee dan Pelatihan Otomasi.

Program pelatihan diberikan secara regular kepada seluruh karyawan sesuai pekerjaan yang menjadi tanggung jawab, bagian, jabatan, maupun bidang kerjanya. Pelatihan yang diberikan antara lain Internal Training, In-House Training dan External Training.

# Competency Development

Employees are a crucial asset to the Company, and their quality is one of the key aspects to be developed. Employee competency development programs have been implemented based on the Company's needs, including training programs. Additionally, employees with special potential will be developed through programs such as the ADR Supervisory Development Program, Management Development Program, or Management Trainee and Automation Training.

Training programs are regularly provided to all employees based on their roles, responsibilities, positions, or work areas. The training includes Internal Training, In-House Training and External Training.
