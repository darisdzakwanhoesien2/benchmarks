# Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja

Dalam memenuhi komitmen memberikan lingkungan kerja yang aman, Perseroan berupaya untuk terus menerapkan standar Sistem Manajemen K3 (SMK3) dengan mengacu pada Peraturan Pemerintah No. 50 tahun 2012 tentang Sistem Manajemen K3 (SMK3) dan ISO 45001:2018. Implementasi SMK3 merupakan tanggung jawab setiap tenaga kerja di Perseroan (100%), termasuk pemangku kepentingan dan wajib patuh atas pelaksanaan K3, guna mencapai target nihil insiden serta mencegah segala risiko K3 selama proses operasional. [403-1, 403-8]

Terdapat beberapa kegiatan yang dilakukan Perseroan untuk mencapai kinerja optimal K3, antara lain:

- Kegiatan memperingati bulan K3 nasional;
- Simulasi tanggap darurat;
- Sosialisasi pasca kecelakaan kerja;
- Rapat rutin Panitia Pembina Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (P2K3);
- Audit K3 dan audit K3L; dan
- Pelatihan petugas P3K dan petugas damkar tersertifikasi Kemenaker.

# Occupational Safety and Health

To fulfill its commitment to providing a safe working environment, the Company strives to continuously implement Occupational Health and Safety Management System (SMK3) standards referring to Government Regulation No. 50 of 2012 on the Occupational Health and Safety Management System (SMK3) and ISO 45001:2018. The implementation of SMK3 is the responsibility of all workers in the Company (100%), including stakeholders, who must comply with the OSH regulations to achieve the target of zero incidents and prevent any OSH risks during operations. [403-1, 403-8]

Several activities are carried out by the Company to achieve optimal OSH performance, including:

- National OSH month celebration;
- Emergency response simulations;
- Post-accident work safety briefings;
- Regular meetings of the OSH Committee (P2K3);
- OSH audits and environmental audits (K3L); and
- First Aid and fire safety training with Kemenaker certification.
