## Pengelolaan Limbah [306-1, 306-2]

Perseroan tentunya menghasilkan limbah baik dari aktivitas kantor maupun proses produksi berupa limbah cair (efluen), limbah padat non-B3 dan limbah padat B3. Sebagai bentuk peduli lingkungan, Perseroan memiliki kebijakan pengelolaan limbah non-B3, limbah B3 dan limbah elektronik. Kebijakan tersebut diterapkan pada seluruh operasional bisnis guna menjaga kelestarian lingkungan.

Air limbah yang dihasilkan tentunya akan diolah terlebih dahulu sebelum dibuang ke badan permukaan air untuk memastikan air limbah tersebut telah memenuhi nilai ambang batas baku mutu sesuai persyaratan perundangan yang berlaku, dengan pengujian kualitas air limbah secara berkala. Instalasi Pengolahan Air Limbah (IPAL) telah dimiliki setiap unit usaha Perseroan dan seluruh limbah cair dari proses produksi akan dialirkan ke IPAL dengan metode koagulasi dan flokulasi. Sementara, limbah cair dari aktivitas domestik di proses pada Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) secara aerob dan anerob.

## Waste Management [306-1, 306-2]

The Company naturally generates waste from both office activities and the production process, including liquid waste (effluent), non-hazardous solid waste, and hazardous solid waste. As a form of environmental concern, the Company has a policy for managing non-hazardous waste, hazardous waste and electronic waste. This policy is applied to all business operations to maintain environmental sustainability.

The wastewater generated is treated before being discharged into surface water bodies to ensure that the effluent meets the required quality standards in accordance with applicable regulations, with periodic effluent quality testing. Each of the Company's business units is equipped with a Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) and all wastewater from the production process is directed to the WWTP using coagulation and flocculation methods. Meanwhile, wastewater from domestic activities is processed in a Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) through both aerobic and anaerobic processes.
