## Program Sosialisasi K3 Tahun 2024

Sepanjang tahun 2024, Perseroan telah aktif menjalankan kegiatan terkait K3, yakni:
- Simulasi Tanggap Darurat;
- Rapat rutin P2K3;
- Audit K3 dan Audit K3L;
- Pemeriksaan perlengkapan tanggap darurat (Kotak P3K, APAR, Hydrant);
- Pemeriksaan alarm system;
- Pemeriksaan pipa gas;
- Pemeriksaan penyalur petir; dan
- Verifikasi kecelakaan kerja dan hasil pengawasan K3.

## Fasilitas K3 [403-3, 403-6]

Dalam mendukung manajemen K3 yang ideal, Perseroan berupaya untuk terus memberikan fasilitas K3 yang terbaik. Selain memenuhi kebutuhan darurat seperti Alat Pemadam Api Ringan (APAR), hydrant, alarm, mobil pemadam kebakaran, ambulans, tangga darurat, jalur dan tanda jalur evakuasi, P3K dan lainnya, Perseroan juga memberikan fasilitas kesehatan yang dapat digunakan oleh seluruh karyawan. Setiap karyawan dapat menikmati fasilitas gelanggang olahraga (GOR), pusat kebugaran (gym/fitness), BPJS Kesehatan dan Ketenagakerjaan, poliklinik, serta dokter.

Perseroan telah bekerja sama dengan Rumah Sakit di Indonesia. Golongan karyawan ke atas, mendapatkan kesempatan medical check-up rutin bila diperlukan. Lebih dari itu, karyawan golongan 4, 5, 6 dan 7 mendapatkan anggaran biaya plafon rawat jalan dengan limit yang berbeda. Sedangkan fasilitas asuransi kesehatan akan didapatkan oleh karyawan golongan 5 ke atas. Khusus karyawan Entitas Anak yang berada di luar negeri, akan mendapatkan fasilitas kesehatan dari Central Provident Fund (CPF), Social Security Organization (SOSCO), atau Pertubuhan Keselamatan Sosial (PERKESO).

## OSH Socialization Program in 2024

Throughout 2024, the Company has actively carried out activities related to (OSH), including:
- Emergency Response Simulation;
- Regular P2K3 Meetings;
- OSH Audit and Security, Safety, Health &amp; Environment (K3L) Audit;
- Inspection of emergency equipment (First Aid Kits, Fire Extinguishers, Hydrants);
- Alarm system inspection;
- Gas pipe inspection;
- Lightning rod inspection; and
- Verification of work accidents and OSH monitoring results.

## Facilities of OSH [403-3, 403-6]

In supporting ideal OSH management, the Company strives to continuously provide the best OSH facilities. In addition to meeting emergency needs such as Portable Fire Extinguishers (APAR), hydrants, alarms, fire trucks, ambulances, emergency ladders, evacuation routes and signs, first aid kits and others, the Company also provides health facilities available for all employees. Every employee can enjoy sports facilities (GOR), fitness centers (gym/fitness), BPJS Health and Employment, clinics, and doctors.

The Company has partnered with hospitals in Indonesia. Employees in higher categories are given the opportunity for routine medical check-ups when needed. Furthermore, employees in categories 4, 5, 6 and 7 receive a budget for outpatient care with varying limits. Health insurance facilities are provided to employees in category 5 and above. For employees of Subsidiaries located abroad, they will receive healthcare facilities from the Central Provident Fund (CPF), the Social Security Organization (SOSCO), or Pertubuhan Keselamatan Sosial (PERKESO).
