KATEGORI SOSIAL KEMASYARAKATAN<br/>CATEGORY OF THE SOCIAL COMMUNITY

|  Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja | Kecelakaan kerja meningkatkan downtime dan biaya kompensasi. | Reputasi jangka panjang terganggu jika terjadi kecelakaan fatal. | Budaya kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja meningkatkan produktivitas. | Zero fatality memperkuat citra employer of choice.  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  Occupational Health and Safety | Workplace accidents increase downtime and compensation costs. | Long-term reputation is disrupted if fatal accidents occur. | Occupational health and safety culture increases productivity. | Zero fatalities strengthen the image as an employer of choice.  |
|  Pelatihan dan Pendidikan | Kurangnya pelatihan menghambat adaptasi terhadap teknologi baru. | Kualitas sumber daya manusia stagnan menghambat inovasi. | Pelatihan teknis meningkatkan efisiensi operasional. | Sumber daya manusia kompeten menjadi aset strategis untuk pertumbuhan.  |
|  Training and Education | Lack of training hinders adaptation to new technologies. | Stagnant human resource quality hinders innovation. | Technical training improves operational efficiency. | Competent human resources become strategic assets for growth.  |
|  Hubungan Karyawan dan Keberagaman | Konflik industrial menurunkan moral dan produktivitas. | Budaya kerja eksklusif menurunkan daya saing talenta. | Lingkungan kerja inklusif mendorong retensi sumber daya manusia. | Keberagaman memperkaya perspektif dan inovasi perusahaan.  |
|  Employee Relations and Diversity | Industrial conflicts reduce morale and productivity. | An exclusive work culture weakens talent competitiveness. | An inclusive work environment supports human resource retention. | Diversity enriches corporate perspectives and innovation.  |
|  Kepatuhan Hukum terkait Pengelolaan Ketenagakerjaan | Pelanggaran aturan tenaga kerja menimbulkan sanksi dan citra buruk. | Ketidakpatuhan berulang menghambat akses ke pasar global. | Kepatuhan meningkatkan hubungan industrial yang harmonis. | Integritas ketenagakerjaan memperkuat daya saing global.  |
|  Legal Compliance in Workforce Management | Violations of labor regulations result in sanctions and damage to corporate image. | Repeated non-compliance hinders access to global markets. | Compliance fosters harmonious industrial relations. | Labor integrity strengthens global competitiveness.  |
|  Ketenagakerjaan dan Pengadaan Lokal | Kurangnya kontribusi terhadap ekonomi lokal menimbulkan penolakan sosial. | Ketergantungan pada tenaga kerja luar mengurangi dukungan komunitas. | Pemberdayaan lokal memperkuat hubungan dengan masyarakat. | Ekosistem lokal yang kuat menciptakan rantai nilai berkelanjutan.  |
|  Employment and Local Procurement | Insufficient contribution to the local economy leads to social rejection. | Dependence on external labor reduces community support. | Local empowerment strengthens relationships with communities. | A strong local ecosystem creates a sustainable value chain.  |
|  Hubungan Masyarakat | Ketidakterlibatan masyarakat menimbulkan resistensi terhadap proyek. | Ketimpangan manfaat jangka panjang merusak kepercayaan sosial. | Program pengembangan dan pemberdayaan masyarakat menciptakan dampak sosial langsung. | Hubungan jangka panjang menciptakan lisensi sosial untuk beroperasi yang stabil.  |
|  Community Relations | Lack of community engagement generates resistance to projects. | Inequitable long-term benefits erode social trust. | Community development and empowerment programs create direct social impact. | Long-term relationships establish a stable social license to operate.  |

KATEGORI LINGKUNGAN HIDUP<br/>CATEGORY OF THE ENVIRONMENT

|  Penggunaan Energi dan Emisi Gas Rumah Kaca | Ketergantungan pada energi fosil meningkatkan emisi dan biaya. | Regulasi emisi yang ketat menghambat operasional jika tidak diantisipasi. | Efisiensi energi menurunkan biaya dan emisi. | Transisi energi memperkuat posisi sebagai tambang hijau.  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  Energy Use and Greenhouse Gas Emissions | Dependence on fossil energy increases emissions and costs. | Stringent emissions regulations may hinder operations if not anticipated. | Energy efficiency reduces costs and emissions. | Energy transition strengthens positioning as a green mining.  |
|  Penggunaan Air | Krisis air lokal dapat menghambat operasi pabrik. | Penurunan kualitas dan kuantitas air menyebabkan konflik dengan komunitas. | Sistem daur ulang air meningkatkan efisiensi dan penerimaan sosial. | Praktik air berkelanjutan meningkatkan nilai lingkungan perusahaan.  |
|  Water Use | Local water crises can disrupt plant operations. | Declining water quality and quantity lead to conflicts with communities. | Water recycling systems improve efficiency and social acceptance. | Sustainable water practices enhance the company's environmental value.  |
|  Rencana Pascatambang | Keterlambatan reklamasi menimbulkan sanksi dan kerusakan lingkungan. | Gagalnya pemulihan lahan merusak keanekaragaman hayati. | Reklamasi progresif membangun kepercayaan regulator. | Keberhasilan restorasi menjadi model industri dan reputasi hijau.  |
|  Post-Mining Plan | Delays in reclamation result in sanctions and environmental damage. | Failure in land restoration harms biodiversity. | Progressive reclamation builds regulator trust. | Successful restoration becomes an industry model and strengthens a green reputation.  |
|  Tailing dan Pengelolaan Limbah | Pengelolaan limbah yang buruk menimbulkan pencemaran dan keluhan. | Akumulasi limbah yang tidak terkendali menyebabkan kerusakan ekologis. | Pemanfaatan kembali limbah mengurangi biaya dan volume buangan. | Sistem limbah sirkular memperkuat keberlanjutan jangka panjang.  |
|  Tailings and Waste Management | Poor waste management causes pollution and complaints. | Uncontrolled waste accumulation leads to ecological damage. | Waste reuse reduces costs and disposal volumes. | Circular waste systems strengthen long-term sustainability.  |
