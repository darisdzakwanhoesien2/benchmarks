The pandemic does not deter the Company from performing the business and operational activities that are oriented towards the environment. The large-scale business and operational activities also require large amounts of energy. Therefore, we continue to carry out efficient production without risking our product quality.

The pandemic also affects Indonesia's economic stability. Wismilak experiences market pressure risk and SKT excise rate increase that necessitate efficiency to suppress the operational cost.
