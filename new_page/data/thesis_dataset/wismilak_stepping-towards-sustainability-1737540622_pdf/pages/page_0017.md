# Pelibatan Pemangku Kepentingan

Kinerja keberlanjutan Wismilak dipengaruhi dan memengaruhi para pemangku kepentingan. Wismilak telah mengidentifikasi pemangku kepentingan internal dan eksternal berdasarkan penilaian dan pertimbangan faktor peran, kebutuhan, pengaruh, dan dampak. Hubungan profesional, harmonis, dan strategis merupakan kunci utama Wismilak membangun kepercayaan dan interaksi dengan seluruh pemangku kepentingannya.

# Stakeholder Engagement

Wismilak's sustainability performance is influenced and affects stakeholders. The Company has identified internal and external stakeholders based on the assessment and consideration of role, need, influence, and impact factors. Professional, harmonious, and strategic relationships are the main key for Wismilak in building trust and interaction with the entire stakeholders.

## Investor

### Investor

- Komitmen dan tanggung jawab dalam keterbukaan informasi
- Commitment and responsibility in information disclosure
- Kinerja usaha dan proyeksi usaha ke depan
- Aksi Korporasi Perseroan
- Future business performances and projections
- Corporate Action

### Sesuai Kebutuhan:

- Pertemuan Investor secara daring maupun melalui telekonferensi
- Keterbukaan informasi melalui channel komunikasi resmi Perseroan (situs web)

On an as-needed basis:

- Investor meeting online or via teleconference
- Transparency through Company's official communication channel (website)

## Masyarakat

### Community

- Keterlibatan dalam pengembangan masyarakat
- Kontribusi positif terhadap lingkungan sekitar Perseroan
- Being involved in community development
- Giving positive contribution to the environment surrounding the Company

- Tanggung jawab sosial dan lingkungan Perseroan
- Lapangan pekerjaan bagi masyarakat lokal
- Literasi Kewirausahaan
- Kepedulian kepada masyarakat terkait pandemi COVID-19
- The Company's social and environmental responsibility
- Employment opportunities for local community
- Entrepreneurship Literacy
- Awareness towards the public related to the COVID-19 pandemic

### Minimal satu kali dalam setahun:

- Pelaksanaan program tanggung jawab sosial perusahaan
- Pelibatan masyarakat di sekitar lingkungan Perseroan dalam kegiatan sosial
- Pelaksanaan literasi kewirausahaan untuk mendukung kemajuan wirausaha Indonesia

At a minimum once a year:

- Implementing corporate social responsibility program
- Engaging the community which resides around the Company in social activities
- Implementation of entrepreneurial literacy to support the progress of Indonesian entrepreneurs

### Sesuai kebutuhan:

- Perekrutan sumber daya manusia dari wilayah sekitar area operasional Perseroan sesuai dengan persyaratan
- On an as-needed basis:
- Recruiting human resources from around the Company's operational areas in accordance with the requirements

## Pemasok

### Supplier

- Keterkaitan terhadap kualitas produk berdasarkan kualitas bahan baku
- Significance of product quality based on raw materials quality
- Seleksi pemasok sesuai peraturan
- Evaluasi terhadap vendor (kualitas, kuantitas, ketetapan waktu, pengiriman, penawaran harga, dan pelayanan)
- Selecting suppliers according to regulations
- Evaluating vendors (quality, quantity, timeliness, delivery, bidding, and services)

- Evaluasi pemasok dilakukan sesuai kebutuhan
- Evaluation on suppliers to be conducted on an as-needed basis
