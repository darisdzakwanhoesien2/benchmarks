## Initiative for Greenhouse Emissions Reduction and the Results

Atas berbagai inisiatif pengurangan emisi GRK, Wismilak mencatat pengurangan emisi sebesar 296 ton CO₂ eq per tahun.

Due to various greenhouse emissions reduction initiatives, Wismilak recorded an emissions reduction of 296 tons of CO₂ eq per year.
