Laporan Keberlanjutan 2022 Sustainability Report

PT Archi Indonesia Tbk

# PENDIDIKAN DAN PELATIHAN [OJK F.22]

Kayawan yang sangat terampil dan termotivasi sangat penting bagi organisasi mana pun untuk mengoptimalkan kinerjanya. Archi berinvestasi pada pengembangan SDM dan membantu mereka tumbuh sebagai profesional yang terampil dengan kompetensi yang diperlukan dan siap menghadapi tantangan di masa depan. Archi turut berkomitmen untuk menyediakan program pelatihan dan pengembangan profesional yang menjembatani kesenjangan antara persyaratan pekerjaan dan kompetensi saat ini.

Pendidikan dan pelatihan yang dilakukan Perseroan meliputi pemenuhan standar kompetensi di setiap posisi maupun pengembangan melalui lembaga-lembaga eksternal. Briefing, coaching, dan pelatihan-pelatihan dilakukan guna peningkatan skill baik soft skill maupun hard skill.

Selain itu, terdapat program magang (on the job training) bagi lulusan fresh-graduate yang berasal dari masyarakat di sekitar wilayah operasi dan lulusan fresh-graduate secara nasional untuk lulusan-lulusan yang masih belum tersedia dari perguruan tinggi lokal. Dari program magang ini, Perseroan kemudian merekrut posisi-posisi yang tersedia.

Selama 2022, Perseroan telah memberikan pendidikan, pelatihan, dan sertifikasi ke beberapa posisi yang berhubungan dengan kegiatan produksi, lingkungan, serta Keselamatan dan K3. Adapun pelatihan yang diberikan sebagai berikut: [GRI 404-2]

- Sertifikat Izin Operator ("SIO") Juru Ikat
- Pengawas Operasional Pertama ("POP")
- SIO Telehandler
- Pengawas Operasional Utama ("POU")
- Penanggung Jawab Pengelolahan Limbah B3
- Diklat Implementasi Sistem Manajemen Keselamatan Pertambangan ("SMKP")
- Petugas Proteksi Radiasi

# EDUCATION AND TRAINING [OJK F.22]

Highly skilled and motivated employees are always essential for companies to optimize performance. Archi invests in HC development and helps them grow as qualified professionals with competence needed and ready to face challenges in the future. Archi is committed to providing professional training and development programs to bridge the gap between current work requirements and competency.

The education and training conducted by the Company includes meeting the competency standards in each position as well as developing them through external institutions. Briefings, coaching, and trainings are carried out to improve skills, both soft skills and hard skills.

In addition, the Company also conducts an internship program (on the job training) for fresh graduates who originate from nearby communities and domestic fresh graduates for those who are still not available from local universities. From this internship program, the Company then recruits those for vacant positions.

During the year 2022, the Company provided education, training, and certification for several positions related to production, environment, and OHS. The trainings provided are as follows: [GRI 404-2]

- Operator License ("SIO") Rigger
- First Operation Supervisor ("POP")
- SIO Telehandler
- Main Operation Supervisor ("POU")
- Person-in-Charge of B3 Waste Management
- Mining Safety Management System ("SMKP") Implementation Education &amp; Training
- Radiation Protection Officer
