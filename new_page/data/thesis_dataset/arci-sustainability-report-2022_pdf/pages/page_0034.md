Apart from practicing responsible mining, we also provide the best employment practices to our employees. Archi is committed to always providing the best employment practices that uphold the principle of equality by embracing all employees regardless of ethnicity, race, religion, gender, physical and other differences. The Company also provides adequate and competitive remuneration and other employee benefits. The lowest wage that Archi's permanent employees receive is 3.17% higher than the Provincial Minimum Wage ("UMP") standard for North Sulawesi.
