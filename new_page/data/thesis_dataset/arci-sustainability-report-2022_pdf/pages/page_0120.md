Sampai akhir 2022, Perseroan masih melanjutkan komitmennya dalam melaksanakan program-program yang bertujuan untuk penguatan mutu sekolah dan program-program pengembangan. Seperti program beasiswa untuk pendidikan tinggi di wilayah nasional dan internasional, penguatan dan pengembangan program Pendidikan Anak Usia Dini ("PAUD") dan Taman Kanak-Kanak ("TK"), serta pengembangan sekolah unggul-berbasis STEAM.

## Program Beasiswa

Archi memberikan program beasiswa kepada 59 mahasiswa pada tahun pembelajaran 2021-2022. Beasiswa diberikan kepada mahasiswa untuk belajar di universitas nasional seperti Universitas Sam Ratulangi, Universitas Negeri Manado, Universitas Klabat Manado, Universitas Kristen Indonesia Tomohon, dan Politeknik Negeri Manado. Selain itu, beasiswa internasional juga diberikan kepada para mahasiswa untuk berkesempatan belajar di universitas internasional seperti Jiangsu Agri-animal Husbandry Vocational College di Cina, Taiwan Shoufu University (TSU) di Taiwan, dan pascasarjana Drew University di Amerika Serikat.

Until the end of 2022, the Company continues to commit to implementing the programs intended to strengthen the school's quality and development programs. This includes scholarship programs for national and international higher education, strengthening and developing the Early Childhood Education ("PAUD") and Kindergarten ("TK"), and the development of STEAM-based schools of excellence.

## Scholarship Program

Archi provides scholarship programs to 59 students in the academic year 2021-2022. Scholarships are given to students to study at national universities such as Sam Ratulangi University, Manado State University, University Klabat Manado, Indonesian Christian University Tomohon, and Manado State Polytechnic. In addition, international scholarships were also given to students for the opportunity to study at international universities such as Jiangsu Agri-animal Husbandry Vocational College in China, Taiwan Shoufu University (TSU) in Taiwan, and postgraduate at Drew Universities in the United States.

## Pendidikan Anak Usia Dini: Taman Kanak-kanak dan Kelompok Bermain

Selama tahun 2022, tim CSR bidang pendidikan melakukan pendampingan dan pemantauan kegiatan di 19 PAUD TK dan Kelompok Bermain ("KB") yang berlokasi di 13 desa sekitar wilayah operasi tambang Koridor Timur dan satu desa di wilayah eksplorasi TTN Koridor Barat. Perseroan membantu pelaksanaan kegiatan belajar mengajar rutin di taman kanak-kanak tersebut. Selain itu, Archi memfasilitasi sekolah-sekolah untuk mendapatkan status persetujuan Gereja Masehi Injili di Minahasa ("GMIM") Sion Tinerungan PAUD TK dari Yayasan GMIM.

## Early Childhood Education: Kindergarten and Play Group

During 2022, the CSR team in the education field-assisted and monitored activities in 19 PAUD TK located in 13 villages around the Eastern Corridor mining operations and one village in the exploration area of TTN West Corridor. The Company helped with the implementation of routine learning activities in these kindergartens. Moreover, Archi facilitated the approval status from the Masehi Injili Church in Minahasa ("GMIM") Sion Tinerungan PAUD TK from the GMIM Foundation.
