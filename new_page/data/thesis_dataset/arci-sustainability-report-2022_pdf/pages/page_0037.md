# Melaksanakan Tata Kelola yang Baik dan Berkelanjutan

Implementation of Sustainable Good Corporate Governance

Pelaksanaan tata kelola perusahaan yang dijalankan Archi mengandung prinsip-prinsip keterbukaan (transparency), akuntabilitas (accountability), responsibilitas (responsibility), independensi (independence), dan kewajaran (fairness) dan kesetaraan (equality) menjadi kekuatan Perseroan menuju keberlanjutan.

The corporate governance implemented by Archi contains the principles of transparency, accountability, independence, fairness, and equality that make up the Company's strength going forward to sustainability.
