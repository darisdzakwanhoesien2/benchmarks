# MEMBERIKAN MANFAAT KEPADA MASYARAKAT

Providing Benefits to the Community

---

Archi telah dan akan terus membangun hubungan berkelanjutan yang telah terjalin dengan masyarakat lokal, lembaga pemerintahan daerah dan nasional, serta seluruh masyarakat sekitar wilayah operasional, untuk mencapai kesejahteraan sekarang dan di masa depan.

Archi has and will continue to build sustainable relationships which have been established with the local community, regional and national government institutions, as well as with the entire community around its operational areas, to achieve prosperity now and in the future.
