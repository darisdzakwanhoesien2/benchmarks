Archi memiliki komitmen untuk menjalankan praktik pertambangan yang berkelanjutan dengan memperhatikan aspek lingkungan sesuai dengan kaidah Good Mining Practice. Archi berupaya meminimalkan dampak lingkungan dari aktivitas penambangan dan operasional pendukung lainnya melalui berbagai kebijakan dan inisiatif. Perseroan juga berusaha untuk melestarikan sumber daya dan bahan baku untuk menjaga planet bumi dari ancaman perubahan iklim.

Untuk mendukung komitmen tersebut, Archi memiliki kebijakan pengelolaan lingkungan berdasarkan pada identifikasi, penilaian, dan pengendalian risiko terhadap bahan baku di seluruh fase bisnis Perseroan, mulai dari eksplorasi hingga pengembangan, operasi, dan penutupan tambang. Kebijakan-kebijakan yang dibuat juga telah didukung oleh tinjauan rutin internal, tinjauan rutin pihak ketiga, serta sistem manajemen lingkungan yang selaras dengan ISO 14001:2015 untuk mempertahankan akuntabilitas kinerja pengelolaan lingkungan di seluruh operasi. [GRI 3-3]

Penerapan kebijakan dilakukan dengan berbagai inisiatif pengelolaan lingkungan. Inisiatif ini dibagi dalam fokus-fokus area yakni kepatuhan pada lingkungan, keanekaragaman hayati, pengelolaan konsumsi energi dan emisi, pengelolaan air, pengelolaan limbah, serta reklamasi dan penutupan tambang.

Archi is committed to implementing sustainable mining practices by paying close attention to environmental aspects in accordance with the principles of Good Mining Practices. Archi strives to minimize the environmental impact of its mining and other supporting operational activities through various policies and initiatives. The Company also makes efforts to conserve resources and raw materials in order to protect the planet earth from the risks of climate change.

To support such commitment, the Company has issued environmental management policies based on the identification, assessment, and risk control of raw materials in all stages during the Company's business activities, starting from exploration to development, operation, and mine closure. The policies made have also been supported by regular internal reviews, regular third party reviews, and an environmental management system that is in alignment with ISO14001:2015 to maintain accountability of the environmental management performance in all operations. [GRI 3-3]

Policies are implemented through various environmental management initiatives. Categorized into different focuses, namely environment compliance, biodiversity, energy and emission consumption management, water management, waste management, as well as reclamation and mine closure.
