# Menjalankan Praktik Pertambangan yang Ramah Lingkungan Implementing Environmentally-Friendly Mining Practices

## Archi Dukung Polres Minut Tanam 10.000 Bakau di Likupang
Archi Supported Minut District Police with the Planting of 10,000 Mangroves in Likupang

Dalam mendukung kegiatan pelestarian alam, Archi memberikan dukungan kepada Polres Minut dalam pencanangan penanaman 10.000 bakau di Desa Maen, Kecamatan Likupang Timur. Pencanangan penanaman bibit bakau ini dilakukan untuk menyambut HUT ke-76 Bhayangkara sekaligus Hari Lingkungan Hidup Sedunia yang diadakan Polda Sulawesi Utara dan Archi.

Keberadaan bakau sangat penting bagi kelangsungan hidup biota laut dan dapat mencegah terjadinya abrasi, bahkan bencana lain yang ditimbulkan oleh laut. Penanaman 10.000 bakau ini diharapkan dapat membawa dampak positif bagi kelangsungan ekosistem lainnya.

In support of natural preservation activities, Archi provided assistance to the District Police of Minut in the declaration of the planting of 10,000 mangroves in the Village of Maen, Subdistrict of East Likupang. The declaration of planting the mangrove seeds was held in celebration of the 76th Bhayangkara Anniversary in unison with the World Environment Day organized by the North Sulawesi Regional Police and Archi.

The presence of mangroves is significant for the life sustainability of marine biota and helps prevent abrasion and even other disasters that may emerge due to the sea. Planting 10,000 mangroves are hoped to positively impact the sustainability of the other ecosystems.

## Mengembangkan 400 Bibit Eboni Growing 400 Ebony Seeds

Archi secara mandiri mempunyai inisiatif untuk turut mengembalikan kejayaan kayu Eboni yang saat ini nyaris punah dengan mengembangkan bibit di fasilitas pembibitan Perseroan dengan metode cabutan bibit alam dan memelihara keberadaan pohon induk sebagai sumber bibit eboni. Sampai dengan saat ini, Archi berhasil mengembangkan bibit alam sebanyak 400 bibit eboni dimana saat ini telah ditanam di area reklamasi dan perkembangannya dipantau secara berkala.

Archi independently took the initiative to restore the existence of the Ebony wood, which is now almost extinct, by growing the seeds in the Company's nursery by a method of natural seed extraction and maintaining the original parent tree as the source of the ebony seeds. Until now, Archi has succeeded in growing 400 ebony natural seeds, which it is now planted in the reclamation area, and their growth is monitored on a regular basis.
