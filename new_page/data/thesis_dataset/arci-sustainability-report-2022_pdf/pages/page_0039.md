# Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik

Good Corporate Governance

Pelaksanaan tata kelola perusahaan merupakan dasar dan acuan dalam pengelolaan Perseroan. Penerapan Good Corporate Governance diharapkan dapat meningkatkan integritas Perseroan yang berdampak pada peningkatan kinerja keberlanjutan. Archi berkomitmen untuk selalu meningkatkan kualitas penerapan GCG, terutama pada kegiatan operasional Perseroan.

Penerapan GCG di lingkungan Perseroan mengacu pada Undang-Undang No. 40 tahun 2007 tentang Perseroan Terbatas, Pedoman Umum Penerapan GCG yang dikeluarkan oleh Komite Nasional Kebijakan Governansi ("KNKG") tahun 2006, serta peraturan perundangan-undangan lain yang relevan. Selain itu, Perseroan juga mengadopsi praktik terbaik yang berlaku di lingkungan industri.

Pelaksanaan tata kelola perusahaan yang dijalankan Archi mengandung prinsip-prinsip keterbukaan (transparency), akuntabilitas (accountability), responsibilitas (responsibility), independensi (independence), dan kewajaran (fairness) dan kesetaraan (equality) menjadi kekuatan Perseroan menuju keberlanjutan.

## DASAR PENERAPAN GCG

Pelaksanaan GCG di lingkungan Perseroan mengacu pada sejumlah regulasi serta peraturan perundangan-undangan, yang antara lain:

1. Undang-Undang No. 40 tahun 2007 tentang Perseroan Terbatas.
2. Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan ("OJK") No. 21/POJK.04/2015 tentang Penerapan Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan Terbuka.
3. Surat Edaran OJK No. 32/SEOJK.04/2015 tentang Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan terbuka.
4. Kebijakan KNKG tahun 2006 tentang Pedoman Umum Good Corporate Governance Indonesia.

The implementation of Good Corporate Governance ("GCG") is the basis and reference in Company management. Application of Good Corporate Governance is expected to improve the Company's integrity that impacts sustainable performance. Archi is committed to continually improving the quality of its GCG implementation, especially in the Company's operational activities.

GCG implementation with in the Company's environment refers to Law No. 40 year 2007 regarding Limited Liability Companies, the General Guidance of GCG Implementation issued by the National Committee of Governance Policy ("KNKG") year 2006, as well as other relevant laws and regulations. In addition, the Company also adopts prevailing best practices in the industrial environment.

Archi's implementation of corporate governance contains the principles of transparency, accountability, independence, fairness, and equality which become the Company's strengths towards sustainability.

## BASIS OF GCG IMPLEMENTATION

GCG implementation within the Company's environment refers to a number of rules and regulations, including the following:

1. Law No. 40 year 2007 on Limited Liability Company.
2. Financial Services Authority ("OJK") Regulation No. 21/POJK.04/2015 on the Implementation of Governance Guidelines in Public Companies.
3. OJK Circular Letter No. 32/SEOJK.04/2015 on Governance Guidelines of Public Companies.
4. KNKG Policy year 2006 regarding the General Guidelines of Indonesia's Good Corporate Governance.
