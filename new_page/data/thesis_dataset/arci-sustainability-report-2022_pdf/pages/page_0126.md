# Pertanian Terpadu Berkelanjutan

Pertanian terpadu berkelanjutan bertujuan meningkatkan kesejahteraan petani di sekitar wilayah operasional. Program ini mengembangkan pertanian terpadu berkelanjutan yang difokuskan pada budidaya jagung varietas JH 37, budi daya padi dan tanaman hortikultura, termasuk pengendalian hama terpadu dan bantuan pupuk untuk seluruh lahan pertanian. Hingga akhir tahun 2022, program yang dibantu oleh Perseroan ini telah mencakup di lebih dari 44 (empat puluh empat) hektar penanaman jagung konsumsi, satu hektar sawah di Desa Wineru, dua hektar sawah Desa Winuri dan tiga hektar sawah di Desa Pulisan dan 14 hektar sawah di Desa Maen. Selanjutnya, program pertanian Perseroan juga telah mencakup penanaman serta panen berbagai cabai melalui kesatuan hortikultura di Desa Pinenek dan Desa Maen.

# Sustainable Integrated Farming

Sustainable Integrated Farming aims to improve the welfare of farmers around the operational area. This program develops integrated sustainable agriculture focused on cultivating the JH 37 variety of maize and rice and horticultural crops, including integrated pest control and fertilizer assistance for all agricultural land. Until the end of 2022, the Company-assisted program covered more than 44 (forty-four) hectares of planting corn for consumption, one hectare of rice fields in Wineru Village, two hectares of rice fields in Winuri Village, and three hectares of rice fields in Pulisan Village, and 14 hectares of rice fields in Maine Village. Furthermore, the Company's agricultural program also included the planting and harvesting of various chilies through the horticulture unit in Pinenek Village and Maen Village.

# Pendampingan Kelompok Tani Cabai Mentoring Chili Farmers Group

"Perseroan melakukan kegiatan pendampingan dan pemantauan penanaman cabai pada beberapa kelompok tani, yakni Kelompok Tani Samarusu, Kelompok Tani Araren Jaya di Desa Pinenek, Kelompok Tani Anugerah dan Felix Cs di Desa Maen. Kelompok tani ini menanam cabai varietas hibrida yakni Cabai Dewata dan cabai lokal."

Kegiatan ini juga bekerja sama dengan pemerintah melalui Dinas Pertanian dan Perkebunan Provinsi Sulawesi Utara seperti melakukan verifikasi kesesuaian lahan hortikultura untuk buah jeruk dan kelengkeng pontianak. Kegiatan lainnya berupa kunjungan dan sosialisasi tanaman hortikultura oleh Dinas Pertanian dan Perkebunan Kabupaten Minahasa Utara kepada kelompok tani binaan Perseroan

"The Company conducted mentoring and monitoring of chili planting activities for several farmer groups, namely the Samarusu Farmer Group, Araren Jaya Farmer Group in Pinenek Village, Anugerah Farmer Group and Felix Cs in Maen Village. These farmer groups planted chili of hybrid varieties namely Dewata chilies and local chilies."

This activity also collaborated with the government through the Agriculture and Plantation Office of North Sulawesi Province, such as verifying the suitability of horticultural land for Pontianak citrus and longan fruits. Other activities included visits and socialization of horticultural crops by the Department of Agriculture and Plantation of North Minahasa Regency to the Company's fostered farmer groups.
