# Meningkatkan Produktivitas, Memberikan Keamanan dan Kenyamanan Increasing Productivity, Providing Security And Comfort

Pencapaian Archi sangat bergantung pada kinerja yang baik dari karyawan karena setiap SDM yang terlibat merupakan kunci keberhasilan bagi pertumbuhan Perseroan. Oleh sebab itu, Archi terus berkomitmen untuk membekali karyawan dengan program pengembangan potensi, minat, dan bakat, hingga pengembangan kapabilitas SDM baik dari segi teknis maupun non-teknis, termasuk professionalisme, integritas, dan dedikasi.

Archi juga berupaya memberikan praktik ketenagakerjaan terbaik yang sesuai dengan undang-undang dan peraturan yang berlaku, serta memastikan keamanan, kesehatan, dan kenyamanan di tempat kerja. Hal-hal tersebut merupakan kunci sukses dalam mengembangkan SDM agar dapat berperan optimal dalam perjalanan Perseroan mencapai tujuan keberlanjutan.

Komitmen Archi dalam pengelolaan SDM diwujudkan melalui berbagai kebijakan. Pengelolaan SDM berada di bawah tanggung jawab Departemen SDM. Departemen SDM bertanggung jawab terhadap proses seleksi karyawan, proses induksi, proses penempatan pada departemen terkait, melaksanakan program pelatihan dan pengembangan, hingga evaluasi rutin penilaian kinerja setiap enam bulan sekali. [GRI 3-3]

# PRAKTIK KETENAGAKERJAAN

Praktik ketenagakerjaan memberikan gambaran tentang komitmen Archi dalam mengelola staf atau SDM. SDM memainkan peran sentral dalam memberikan kinerja terbaik bagi Perseroan. Archi berkomitmen untuk selalu memberikan praktik ketenagakerjaan terbaik yang menjunjung tinggi prinsip kesetaraan dengan mengikutsertakan seluruh karyawan tanpa membedakan suku, ras, agama, jenis kelamin, fisik, dan perbedaan lainnya. [OJK F.18]

Archi memberikan kesempatan yang sama bagi seluruh karyawan untuk bekerja dan mengembangkan kariernya. Perseroan menentang segala bentuk diskriminasi yang mungkin terjadi di lingkungan kerja Perseroan. Pada tahun pelaporan 2022, Perseroan tidak mengidentifikasi dan menerima laporan adanya perilaku diskriminatif di lingkungan kerja Perseroan maupun rantai bisnisnya. [GRI 3-3][GRI 406-1]

Archi's accomplishments highly depend on the good performance of the employees as each individual of HC involved is key to the success of the Company's growth. Therefore, Archi is unceasingly committed to providing employees with potential, interest, and talent development programs to enhance HC capabilities in technical and non-technical aspects, including professionalism, integrity, and dedication.

Archi also strives to provide the best manpower in accordance with prevailing laws and regulations, as well as to ensure security, health, and comfort at the workplace. These matters are key to success in developing HC so that they can take on optimal roles in the Company's journey to achieving its sustainable goals.

Archi's commitment to managing HC is manifested through various policies. HC management is the responsibility of the HC Department. The HC Department is responsible for the employee selection process, induction process, placement process in related departments, training and development programs, to routine evaluation of performance assessment once every six months. [GRI 3-3]

# LABOR PRACTICES

Labor practices represent Archi's commitment to managing its staff or human capital. HC plays a central role in providing the best performance for the Company. Archi's commitment is always to provide the best labor practices that uphold high the principle of equality by involving all employees with no discrimination of ethnicity, race, religion, gender, physique, and other differences. [OJK F.18]

Archi provides equal opportunities for all employees to work and develop their careers. The Company is against all forms of discrimination that may occur within the Company's workplace. In the reporting year of 2022, the Company did not identify nor receive any report on discriminative acts in the Company's working environment as well as in their business chain. [GRI 3-3] [GRI 406-1]
