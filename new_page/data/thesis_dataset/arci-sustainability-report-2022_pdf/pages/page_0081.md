# PENGELOLAAN LIMBAH PADAT

Kegiatan usaha Perseroan menghasilkan timbulan limbah padat baik bersifat Bahan Berbahaya Beracun ("B3") maupun non-B3. Untuk mengelola timbulan limbah tersebut, Archi menetapkan komitmen dan kebijakan pengelolaan limbah dengan pendekatan prinsip 3R (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle). Archi juga bekerja sama dengan pihak ketiga berizin untuk mengelola limbah B3. [OJK F.14][GRI 3-3]

Dalam rangka pemanfaatan limbah padat, Archi telah melaksanakan kegiatan penelaahan dan perencanaan serta persiapan untuk program pemanfaatan oli bekas sebagai campuran emulsi bahan peledak batuan penambangan, bekerja sama dengan kontraktor peledakan. Perizinan awal berupa Persetujuan Teknik telah diterbitkan oleh KLHK dan direncanakan untuk dilanjutkan pembangunan instalasi pemanfaatan limbah oli bekas pada tahun berikutnya.

# SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT

The Company's business activities generated solid waste of Hazardous Toxic Materials ("B3") and non-B3. Archi has made its commitment and waste management policy to manage such waste with the 3R principle (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle) approach. Archi also works together with licensed third parties to manage B3 waste. [OJK F.14][GRI 3-3]

In the effort the utilization of solid waste, Archi conducted analysis and planning activities as well as preparation for the program to utilize used oil as an emulsion mixture for mining rock explosives, working together with blasting contractors. The initial permit was the Technical Agreement issued by the KLHK and was planned to be continued with the development of used oil waste installation in the following year.
