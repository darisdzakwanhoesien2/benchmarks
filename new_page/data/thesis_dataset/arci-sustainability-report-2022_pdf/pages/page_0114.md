## DAMPAK OPERASIONAL TERHADAP MASYARAKAT SEKITAR [OJK F.23][GRI 3-3][GRI 413-2]

Melihat sifat kegiatan usaha yang memanfaatkan sumber daya mineral, Perseroan memiliki sejumlah potensi dampak negatif bagi masyarakat sekitar. Namun, potensi dampak negatif tersebut telah diimbangi dengan komitmen, kebijakan, dan kegiatan-kegiatan CSR, serta pembukaan lapangan kerja di mana masyarakat dapat menjadi karyawan Perseroan maupun karyawan kontraktor. Hal ini dapat meningkatkan pendapatan yang berdampak pada meningkatnya kualitas hidup mereka.

## DINAMIKA SOSIAL [GRI 413-2]

Hidup berdampingan dengan masyarakat berdampak pada hubungan yang dinamis. Berbagai dinamika terjadi dalam menjalin hubungan dengan masyarakat dan menjadi hal yang umum bagi setiap kegiatan usaha. Selama tahun 2022, terdapat beberapa masalah seperti urusan lahan dan penggunaan blasting yang berdampak pada masyarakat sekitar. Melalui berbagai macam pendekatan, masalah tersebut dapat diselesaikan dengan baik dan secara kekeluargaan. Salah satunya melalui santunan kepedulian dan program PPM.

## SALURAN PENGADUAN MASYARAKAT [OJK F.24]

Perseroan selalu terbuka terhadap saran, kritik, keluhan, dan pengaduan dari masyarakat mengenai kegiatan operasional yang dianggap mengganggu masyarakat sekitar. Untuk menerima saran, kritik, keluhan, dan pengaduan tersebut, Archi menyediakan mekanisme keluhan agar semua pemangku kepentingan dapat menyampaikan keluhan. Seluruh keluhan dan pengaduan yang diajukan melalui mekanisme keluhan akan ditangani dalam jangka waktu yang sesuai dan dilakukan investigasi dimana hasil temuannya akan dilaporkan secara transparan.

## IMPACT OF OPERATIONS TO THE SURROUNDING COMMUNITIES [OJK F.23][GRI 3-3][GRI 413-2]

Because of the characteristics of the business activities that take advantage of mineral resources, the Company has several potentials negative impact to surrounding communities. Nevertheless, these potential negative impacts are balanced by CSR commitments, policies, and activities, as well as the opening of job opportunities where local community members can become part of the Company employees and contractors’ employees. This can help increase earnings that impact the improvement of their quality of life.

## SOCIAL DYNAMICS [GRI 413-2]

Living side by side with the community brings impact on dynamic relations. Various dynamics occur in maintaining relations with the community and become a common issue for business activities. During 2022, several issues, such as land matters and the use of blasting, that affected surrounding community. Such problems can be resolved through various approaches with good and amicable means. One of them is by providing compensation of care and a PPM program.

## PUBLIC COMPLAINT CHANNEL [OJK F.24]

The Company is always open to suggestions, criticisms, reports, and complaints from the community on operational activities that are considered disruptive to surrounding society. To receive such suggestions, criticisms, reports, and complaints, Archi provides a complaint mechanism so that all stakeholders can have their complaints submitted through the mechanism and handled appropriately and investigate where findings will be reported transparently.
