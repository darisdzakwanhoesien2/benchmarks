# Archi terus memegang komitmen ESG (Environment, Social, &amp; Governance) untuk aktif mendukung pencapaian Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan di Indonesia. Komitmen tersebut diwujudkan dalam praktik penambangan yang bertanggung jawab di seluruh area operasi Perseroan, maupun dalam pelaksanaan program-program tanggung jawab sosial dan lingkungan bagi masyarakat lingkar tambang, dan menjadi penanda-jalan dalam perjalanan Perseroan menuju keberlanjutan.

Archi continues to uphold the ESG (Environment, Social, &amp; Governance) commitment to actively support the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals in Indonesia. This commitment is manifested in responsible mining practices throughout the Company's operational areas, as well as in the implementation of social and environmental responsibility programs for the near-mine communities, and serves as a road marker on the Company's journey towards sustainability.
