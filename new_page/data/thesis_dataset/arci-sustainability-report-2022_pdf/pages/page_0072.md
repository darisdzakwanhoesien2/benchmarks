# KEPATUHAN LINGKUNGAN [OJK F.16][GRI 2-27]

Dalam menjalankan praktik pertambangan dan kegiatan pendukung lainnya, Archi berkomitmen untuk mematuhi seluruh peraturan dan perundang-undangan yang berlaku serta standar perlindungan lingkungan dan kaidah teknik pertambangan yang baik. Archi juga memiliki kebijakan pengelolaan lingkungan yang didukung oleh Sistem Manajemen Lingkungan ("EMS"). Kebijakan EMS telah diselaraskan dengan ISO 14001:2015 yang menjadi standar lingkungan terkemuka serta mendapat tinjauan rutin dari internal dan pihak ketiga.

Adapun implementasi pengelolaan dampak lingkungan di seluruh area kerja Archi berpedoman pada:

- Dokumen Analisis Mengenai Dampak Lingkungan ("AMDAL")
- Rencana Pengelolaan Lingkungan ("RKL")
- Rencana Pemantauan Lingkungan ("RPL")
- Upaya Pengelolaan Lingkungan ("UKL")
- Upaya Pemantauan Lingkungan ("UPL")
- Rencana Reklamasi Tahap Operasi
- Rencana Pascatambang
- Perizinan Perlindungan dan Pengelolaan Lingkungan Hidup ("PPLH")

Selama tahun 2022, Archi tidak memiliki ketidakpatuhan terhadap hukum dan tidak mendapatkan denda atau hukuman terkait masalah lingkungan. Kami juga tidak menerima pengaduan dari publik yang berdampak signifikan terhadap lingkungan dari masyarakat sekitar.

# ENVIRONMENTAL COMPLIANCE [OJK F.16][GRI 2-27]

In implementing environmental practices and other supporting activities, Archi is committed to complying with all prevailing laws and regulations as well as environmental protection standards and rules of good mining techniques. Archi also retains its environmental management policy supported by the Environmental Management System ("EMS"). The EMS policy is consistent with ISO 14001:2015, which serves as the leading environmental standard and receives internal and third-party routine reviews.

Implementation of environmental impact management in all areas of Archi's operation refers to the following:

- Analysis Document on Environmental Impact ("AMDAL")
- Environmental Management Plan ("RKL")
- Environmental Monitoring Plan ("RPL")
- Environmental Management Work Plan ("UKL")
- Environmental Monitoring Work Plan ("UPL")
- Reclamation Plan on Operation Stage
- Post-Mining Plan
- Environmental Protection and Management ("PPLH") Permit

During 2022, Company received no monetary sanctions related to incidents of non-compliance with the environment. We also received no complaints from the public regarding environmental issues. We also did not receive any complaints from the public that significantly impacted the environment from the surrounding communities.
