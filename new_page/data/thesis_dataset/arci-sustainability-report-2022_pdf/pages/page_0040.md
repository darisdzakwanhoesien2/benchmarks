# STRUKTUR TATA KELOLA (GRI 2-9)

Struktur tata kelola perusahaan terdiri dari Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham ("RUPS"), Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, serta organ-organ pendukung lainnya seperti Sekretaris Perusahaan serta komite-komite seperti Komite Pengeluaran, Komite Investasi, Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi, Komite Audit, Komite Etik, dan Audit Internal.

# - Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham

Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham merupakan organ tertinggi dalam struktur tata kelola perusahaan. RUPS memiliki hak dan kewenangan yang tidak dimiliki Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris dalam batasan yang ditentukan dalam ketentuan peraturan perundangan dan Anggaran Dasar PT Archi Indonesia Tbk.

# GREENNANCE STRUCTURE (GRI 2-9)

The corporate governance structure consists of General Meeting of Shareholders ("GMS"), the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors as well as such other supporting organs as the Corporate Secretary and committees, namely Expenditure Committee, Investment Committee, Nomination and Remuneration Committee, Audit Committee, and Internal Audit.

# General Meeting of Shareholders

The General Meeting of Shareholders is the highest organ in the Company's governance structure. The GMS has the rights and authority not owned by the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners within determined limits as stipulated in the provisions set under the laws and regulations as well as the Articles of Association of PT Archi Indonesia Tbk.

![img-31.jpeg](img-31.jpeg)

Dalam pelaksanaan RUPS, sesuai dengan Undang-Undang No. 40 tahun 2007 tentang Perseroan Terbatas, pemegang saham berhak memperoleh keterangan yang berkaitan dengan Perseroan dari Direksi dan/atau Dewan Komisaris. Keterangan dimaksud sepanjang berhubungan dengan mata acara rapat dan tidak bertentangan dengan kepentingan Perseroan.

In the implementation of the GMS, in accordance with Law No. 40 year 2007 on Public Companies, the shareholders have the right to obtain information related to the Company from the Board of Directors and/or the Board of Commissioners. The information referred to may be on any topic as long as it relates to the meeting agenda and does not conflict with the interests of the Company.
