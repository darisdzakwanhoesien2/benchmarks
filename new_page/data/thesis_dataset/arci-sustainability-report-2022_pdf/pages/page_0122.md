# Penerapan Perpustakaan Digital di Sekolah Berbasis STEAM
## Implementation of Digital Library in STEAM-Based Schools

"Untuk mendukung penguatan kualitas sumber daya manusia di sekitar area pertambangan, Archi juga mendukung pelaksanaan program-program peningkatan kualitas pendidikan. Salah satunya adalah melalui implementasi Program Pengembangan Model Sekolah Unggul berbasis STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Art and Math) pada 2022 hingga 2026."

Salah satu pengembangan program sekolah unggul berbasis STEAM adalah penerapan Perpustakaan Digital di Sekolah Dasar ("SD") dan Sekolah Menengah Pertama ("SMP"). Tim CSR Perseroan telah melakukan sosialisasi implementasi dan fungsi Perpustakaan Digital di enam sekolah penerima program STEAM, yakni:

- Area MSM: SDN Inpres Maen, di Desa Maen; SD GMIM Winuri, di Desa Winuri; SMPN 2 Likupang, di Desa Winuri; dan SMPN 1 Likupang, di Desa Likupang.
- Area TTN: SDN Satap Pinasungkulan, di Desa Pinasungkulan dan SMPN 8 Bitung, di Desa Batuputih Bawah.

Setelah implementasi Perpustakaan Digital, Perseroan menyiapkan kegiatan pelatihan kepada para guru dan tenaga pendidik yang terlibat dalam program ini. Koordinasi dan diskusi telah dilakukan melalui pertemuan daring.

"To support the strengthening of human capital quality from areas around the mining sites, Archi also encourages the implementation of programs to improve the quality of education. One of the programs is through the implementation of the Development Program of the STEAM-Based Schools of Excellence (Science, Technology, Engineering, Art, and Math) for the years 2022 to 2026."

One of the development programs of the STEAM-based schools of excellence is the implementation of Digital Libraries in SD and Junior High Schools ("SMP"). The Company's CSR team has socialized the implementation and functions of the Digital Libraries in six STEAM program beneficiary schools, namely:

- MSM Area: Maen State Elementary School, in Maen Village; GMIM Winuri Elementary School, in Winuri Village; SMPN 2 Likupang State Junior High School, in Winuri Village; and SMPN 1 Likupang State Junior High School, in Likupang Village.
- TTN Area: Satap Pinasungkulan State Elementary School, in Pinasungkulan Village and SMPN 8 Bitung State Junior High School, in Batuputih Bawah Village.

Following the implementation of the Digital Library, the Company prepared training activities for teachers and educators involved in this program. Coordination and discussions were carried out through online meetings.

# Ekonomi

Sebagai sarana untuk meningkatkan pendapatan masyarakat di sekitar wilayah operasi tambang, Perseroan telah berkontribusi dalam melaksanakan program pengembangan Usaha Mikro Kecil dan Menengah ("UMKM") dan agri bisnis-agro industri di beberapa bidang.

## Pengembangan UMKM

Archi terus mendukung para pelaku usaha dalam peningkatan kualitas produk, kemudahan pengurusan perizinan, serta sarana dan prasarana produksi guna membantu para pelaku UMKM di desa-desa sekitar wilayah operasi tambang.

## Economy

As the facility to increase the income of the community around the mining operational areas, the Company has contributed to the implementation of development programs for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises ("MSMEs") and the agri-business-agro-industry in several fields.

## Development of MSMEs

Archi continues to support businesses in improving product quality, facilitating licensing arrangements, as well as production facilities and infrastructure to assist MSMEs players in villages around the mining operation area.
