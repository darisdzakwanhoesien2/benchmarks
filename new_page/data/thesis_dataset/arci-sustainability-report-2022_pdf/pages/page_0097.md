## PENILAIAN KINERJA
Archi mendorong pengembangan karier karyawan dengan menerapkan sistem manajemen SDM berbasis kompetensi selama bekerja di Perseroan. Penilaian kinerja dilakukan sebanyak dua kali dalam setahun pada pertengahan dan akhir tahun sesuai dengan amanat Perjanjian Kerja Bersama. Penilaian kinerja karyawan menggunakan perangkat dasar yaitu formulir Penilaian Kinerja yang menilai Key Performance Indicator ("KPI") untuk staf dan skala penilaian yang didasarkan kehadiran untuk non-staf. Pada 2022, Perseroan sudah melakukan penilaian kinerja kepada seluruh karyawan termasuk karyawan kontrak. [GRI 404-3]

## PERFORMANCE ASSESSMENT
Archi encourages employee career development by implementing the competency-based HC management system. Performance assessment is evaluated twice a year, at mid-year and year-end, as mandated through the Collective Labor Agreement. Employee performance assessment uses the basic tools of the Performance Assessment Form that evaluates the Key Performance Indicator ("KPI") for staff and evaluation scale based on non-staff attendance. In 2022, the Company conducted a performance assessment on all employees, including contract employees. [GRI 404-3]
