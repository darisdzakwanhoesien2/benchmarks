# Wismilak Resmikan Office Block Divisi Filter, Perkuat Efisiensi Operasional

Surabaya, 27 Januari 2027 – PT Wismilak Inti Makmur Tbk (Perseroan) meresmikan Office Block Divisi Filter yang berlokasi di Jl. Buntaran No.18 sebagai bagian dari upaya perusahaan dalam memperkuat efisiensi operasional, melakukan sentralisasi fungsi kerja, meningkatkan kolaborasi lintas divisi, serta mendukung pertumbuhan bisnis Divisi Filter secara berkelanjutan.

Office Block Divisi Filter tersebut dibangun di atas lahan seluas ±1.080 m² dengan total luas bangunan ±1.872 m² yang terdiri dari 2 (dua) lantai. Fasilitas ini difungsikan sebagai pusat kegiatan perkantoran Divisi Filter yang mencakup antara lain fungsi Quality Control (QC), Research &amp; Development (R&amp;D), Production Planning &amp; Control (PPC), Finance &amp; Accounting, serta fungsi pendukung operasional lainnya.

Direktur PT Wismilak Inti Makmur Tbk, Krisna Tanimihardja, menyampaikan bahwa peresmian Office Block Divisi Filter tersebut dilakukan dalam rangka penyediaan fasilitas kerja yang lebih terintegrasi serta penyesuaian terhadap kebutuhan operasional Perseroan seiring dengan perkembangan kegiatan usaha. Fasilitas ini diharapkan dapat mendukung peningkatan efisiensi operasional Perseroan ke depan.

# Wismilak Inaugurates Filter Division Office Block, Strengthening Operational Efficiency

Surabaya, 27 January 2027 – PT Wismilak Inti Makmur Tbk (the “Company”) officially inaugurated the Filter Division Office Block located at Jl. Buntaran No. 18 as part of the Company's efforts to strengthen operational efficiency, centralize work functions, enhance cross-division collaboration, and support the sustainable growth of the Filter Division's business.

The Filter Division Office Block was constructed on a land area of approximately ±1.080 m² with a total building area of approximately ±1.872 m², consisting of two (2) floors. This facility serves as the central office for the Filter Division, accommodating, among others, Quality Control (QC), Research &amp; Development (R&amp;D), Production Planning &amp; Control (PPC), Finance &amp; Accounting, as well as other operational support functions.

Director of PT Wismilak Inti Makmur Tbk, Krisna Tanimihardja, stated that the inauguration of the Filter Division Office Block was carried out to provide more integrated working facilities and to align with the Company's operational needs in line with the development of its business activities. The facility is expected to support improvements in the Company's operational efficiency going forward.
