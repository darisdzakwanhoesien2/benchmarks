# TATA KELOLA PERUSAHAAN CORPORATE GOVERNANCE

Perseroan menjunjung tinggi komitmen terhadap integritas dan transparansi sebagai landasan utama dalam penerapan Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik (Good Corporate Governance/ GCG). GCG merupakan pilar strategis yang memperkokoh daya saing Perseroan di era perubahan industri yang dinamis. Prinsip-prinsip GCG, yaitu transparansi, akuntabilitas, tanggung jawab, independensi, kewajaran, dan kesetaraan, menjadi pedoman Perseroan dalam setiap pengambilan keputusan dan tindakan.

Perseroan meyakini bahwa penerapan GCG yang efektif tidak hanya berperan dalam mencapai target-target bisnis, tetapi juga melindungi hak seluruh Pemangku Kepentingan. Keseimbangan antara kepentingan ekonomi dan sosial, antara individu dan kelompok, serta antara Pemegang Saham dan Pemangku Kepentingan lainnya, menjadi fokus utama Perseroan. Prinsip-prinsip GCG menjadi landasan moral dan strategis Perseroan.

Penerapan GCG bukan hanya merupakan kewajiban hukum bagi Perseroan, tetapi juga merupakan komitmen strategis dalam pengelolaan operasional bisnis. Perseroan senantiasa berupaya untuk patuh terhadap Undang-Undang Nomor 40 Tahun 2007, regulasi Otoritas Jasa Keuangan, dan ketentuan Bursa Efek Indonesia. Kepatuhan ini merupakan wujud komitmen Perseroan untuk meningkatkan nilai Perseroan, melindungi Pemangku Kepentingan, dan memperkuat akuntabilitas di seluruh lapisan kegiatan operasional.

Dengan menjunjung tinggi standar Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik dalam setiap aspek bisnis, Perseroan tidak hanya melindungi kepentingan Pemangku Kepentingan, tetapi juga memperkuat posisinya dalam menghadapi dinamika persaingan yang semakin kompleks. Keyakinan Perseroan terhadap nilai GCG sebagai landasan utama, menjadikan Perseroan lebih adaptif dan mampu meraih sasaran usaha dengan kemajuan yang signifikan dan berkelanjutan.

# Dasar Hukum Pedoman GCG

Seluruh kebijakan dan prosedur GCG Perseroan dicantumkan dalam Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik (GCG Code) yang mengacu pada peraturan dan ketentuan yang berlaku di Indonesia, antara lain:

1. Undang-Undang Republik Indonesia No. 40 tahun 2007 tentang "Perseroan Terbatas"
2. Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 33/POJK.04/2014 tentang "Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris Emiten atau Perseroan Publik"
3. Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 34/POJK.04/2014 tentang "Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi Emiten atau Perseroan Publik"
4. Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 35/POJK.04/2014 tentang "Sekretaris Perseroan Emiten atau Perseroan Publik"
5. Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 21/POJK.04/2014 tentang "Penerapan Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan Terbuka"
6. Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 55/POJK.04/2015 tentang "Pembentukan dan Pedoman Kerja Komite Audit"
7. Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 56/POJK.04/2015 tentang "Pembentukan dan Pedoman Penyusunan Piagam Unit Audit Internal"
8. Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 29/POJK.04/2016 tentang "Laporan Tahunan Emiten atau Perseroan Publik"
9. Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 15/POJK.04/2020 tentang "Rencana dan Penyelenggaraan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Perseroan Terbuka".
10. Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 16/POJK.04/2020 tentang "Pelaksanaan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Perseroan Terbuka Secara Elektronik" dan
11. Surat Edaran Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 16 tahun 2021 tentang "Bentuk dan Isi Laporan Tahunan Emiten atau Perseroan Publik"

The Company upholds a strong commitment to integrity and transparency as the primary foundation in implementing Good Corporate Governance (GCG). GCG is a strategic pillar that strengthens the Company's competitiveness in an era of dynamic industry change. The principles of GCG, namely transparency, accountability, responsibility, independence, fairness, and equality, are the Company's guidelines in every decision and action.

The Company believes that the effective implementation of GCG not only plays a role in achieving business targets but also protects the rights of all Stakeholders. The balance between economic and social interests, between individuals and groups, and between Shareholders and other Stakeholders, is the Company's main focus. The principles of GCG are the Company's moral and strategic foundation.

The implementation of GCG is not only a legal obligation for the Company but also a strategic commitment in managing business operations. The Company always strives to comply with Law No. 40 of 2007, Financial Services Authority regulations, and Indonesia Stock Exchange regulations. This compliance is a manifestation of the Company's commitment to increasing the Company's value, protecting Stakeholders, and strengthening accountability at all levels of operational activities.

By upholding the standards of Good Corporate Governance in every aspect of the business, the Company not only protects the interests of Stakeholders but also strengthens its position in facing increasingly complex competitive dynamics. The Company's belief in the value of GCG as a primary foundation makes the Company more adaptive and able to achieve business goals with significant and sustainable progress.

# Legal Basis of GCG Guidelines

All of the Company's GCG policies and procedures are listed in the Good Corporate Governance Guidelines (GCG Code) which refers to the regulations and provisions in force in Indonesia, among others:

1. Republic of Indonesia Law No. 40 of 2007 concerning "Limited Liability Companies"
2. Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 33/POJK.04/2014 concerning "Directors and Boards of Commissioners of Issuers or Public Companies"
3. Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 34/POJK.04/2014 concerning "Nomination and Remuneration Committees of Issuers or Public Companies"
4. Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 35/POJK.04/2014 concerning "Corporate Secretary of Issuers or Public Companies"
5. Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 21/POJK.04/2014 concerning "Implementation of Guidelines for Good Corporate Governance of Public Companies"
6. Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 55/POJK.04/2015 concerning "Formation and Work Guidelines of Audit Committees"
7. Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 56/POJK.04/2015 concerning "Formation and Guidelines for the Preparation of Internal Audit Unit Charters"
8. Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 29/POJK.04/2016 concerning "Annual Reports of Issuers or Public Companies"
9. Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 15/POJK.04/2020 concerning "Plans and Implementation of General Meetings of Shareholders of Public Companies".
10. Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 16/POJK.04/2020 concerning "Implementation of General Meetings of Shareholders of Public Companies Electronically" and
11. Financial Services Authority Circular Letter No. 16 of 2021 concerning "Forms and Contents of Annual Reports of Issuers or Public Companies"
