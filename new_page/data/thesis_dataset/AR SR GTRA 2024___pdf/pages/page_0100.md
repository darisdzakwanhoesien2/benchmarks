# UNIT AUDIT INTERNAL
## INTERNAL AUDIT UNIT

Unit Audit Internal (UAI) adalah bagian integral dari tata kelola perusahaan yang berperan penting dalam memberikan keyakinan dan konsultasi yang independen serta objektif. UAI berdedikasi untuk meningkatkan nilai perusahaan dan kinerja operasional melalui pendekatan sistematis. Hal ini dicapai dengan mengevaluasi dan meningkatkan efektivitas manajemen risiko, pengendalian internal, serta proses tata kelola perusahaan secara keseluruhan. Dalam menjalankan fungsinya, UAI menjalin kerja sama erat dengan Komite Audit, memastikan sinergi dalam mencapai tujuan perusahaan. Berikut profil Kepala Audit Internal saat ini:

### Implementation of the Corporate Secretary's Duties

Throughout 2024, the Corporate Secretary has performed their duties and responsibilities, which include:

1. Facilitating effective communication between the Company and its Shareholders, the Financial Services Authority (OJK), and other Stakeholders.
2. Ensuring the timely submission of periodic reports to the OJK in accordance with established deadlines.
3. Managing the organization and comprehensive documentation of all meetings of the Board of Commissioners and/or the Board of Directors.

The Internal Audit Unit (IAU) is an integral part of the Company's governance structure, playing a vital role in providing independent and objective assurance and consulting services. The IAU is dedicated to enhancing the Company's value and operational performance through a systematic approach. This is achieved by evaluating and improving the effectiveness of risk management, internal controls, and overall corporate governance processes. In carrying out its functions, the IAU works closely with the Audit Committee, ensuring synergy in achieving the Company's objectives.
