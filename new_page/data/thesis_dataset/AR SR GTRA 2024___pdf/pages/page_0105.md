2024 Annual Report

# Risiko Likuiditas

Perseroan menunjukkan bahwa penerimaan jangka pendek tidak cukup menutupi pengeluaran jangka pendek. Kebutuhan likuiditas Perseroan secara historis timbul dari kebutuhan untuk membiayai investasi dan pengeluaran barang modal terkait dengan program perluasan usaha. Perseroan membutuhkan modal kerja yang substansial untuk menjalankan proyek-proyek baru dan untuk mendanai operasional.

Dalam mengelola risiko likuiditas, Perseroan memantau dan menjaga tingkat kas yang dianggap memadai untuk membiayai operasional Perseroan dan untuk mengatasi dampak dari fluktuasi arus kas. Perseroan juga secara rutin mengevaluasi proyeksi arus kas dan arus kas aktual, termasuk jadwal jatuh tempo pinjaman, dan terus menelaah kondisi pasar keuangan untuk memelihara fleksibilitas pendanaan dengan cara menjaga ketersediaan komitmen fasilitas kredit.

Tujuan utama dari pengelolaan permodalan Perusahaan adalah untuk memastikan bahwa dipertahankannya peringkat kredit yang kuat dan rasio modal yang sehat agar dapat mendukung kelancaran usahanya dan memaksimalkan nilai dari pemegang saham. Perusahaan mengelola struktur modalnya dan membuat penyesuaian-penyesuaian sehubungan dengan perubahan kondisi ekonomi dan karakteristik dari risiko usahanya. Agar dapat menjaga dan menyesuaikan struktur modalnya, Perusahaan akan menyesuaikan jumlah dari pembayaran dividen kepada para pemegang saham atau tingkat pengembalian modal atau menerbitkan surat saham. Tidak ada perubahan dalam tujuan, kebijakan dan proses dan sama seperti penerapan tahun-tahun sebelumnya.

Perusahaan memonitor struktur modalnya dengan menggunakan rasio utang terhadap modal, dimana total utang dibagi dengan total modal.

# Liquidity Risk

Liquidity risk is defined as the risk when the Company's cash flow position indicates that short-term receipts are insufficient to cover short-term disbursements. Historically, the Company's liquidity needs have arisen from the need to finance investments and capital expenditures related to business expansion programs. The Company requires substantial working capital to run new projects and fund operations.

In managing liquidity risk, the Company monitors and maintains cash levels deemed adequate to finance operations and to address the impact of cash flow fluctuations. The Company also regularly evaluates cash flow projections and actual cash flows, including the maturity schedules of loans, and continuously reviews financial market conditions to maintain funding flexibility by ensuring the availability of committed credit facilities.

The main objective of the Company's capital management is to ensure the maintenance of strong credit ratings and healthy capital ratios to support its business operations and maximize shareholder value. The Company manages its capital structure and makes adjustments in response to changes in economic conditions and the risk characteristics of its business. To maintain and adjust its capital structure, the Company may adjust the amount of dividend payments to shareholders, the level of capital returns, or issue new shares. There have been no changes in the objectives, policies, and processes, which remain consistent with those applied in previous years.

The Company monitors its capital structure using a debt-to-equity ratio, calculated by dividing total debt by total equity.

# Effectiveness of Risk Management Policies and Systems

Throughout 2023, the Company continuously enhanced the effectiveness of its Risk Management system through monitoring by the Risk Management Committee. Continuous improvement was a primary focus, encompassing enhancements to risk governance, strategies and frameworks, information flows, and the processes for risk identification, measurement, monitoring, and control. The absence of significant insurance claims this year serves as an indicator of the success of the strategic and operational risk controls implemented.

The Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners, based on reviews and discussions involving Management, the Audit Committee, the Independent Auditor, and related divisions, have evaluated the Company's internal control and risk management systems.
