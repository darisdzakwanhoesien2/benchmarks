## Tingkat Kecelakaan Kerja

Sepanjang tahun 2024, Perseroan telah berhasil menerapkan sistem Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3) yang komprehensif di seluruh kegiatan operasional. Penerapan ini terbukti efektif dalam meminimalkan risiko kecelakaan kerja, dengan seluruh insiden yang terjadi dapat ditangani secara tuntas. Upaya proaktif Perseroan dalam mengurangi risiko kecelakaan kendaraan, termasuk melalui perawatan kendaraan rutin, perekrutan pengemudi kompeten, pelatihan berkala, dan penerapan kebijakan istirahat yang memadai, telah berkontribusi signifikan terhadap pencapaian ini.

## Occupational Accident Rate

Throughout 2024, the Company successfully implemented a comprehensive Occupational Safety and Health (OSH) system across all operational activities. This implementation proved effective in minimizing occupational accident risks, with all incidents being handled thoroughly. The Company's proactive efforts in reducing vehicle accident risks, including through routine vehicle maintenance, recruitment of competent drivers, periodic training, and the implementation of adequate rest policies, have significantly contributed to this achievement.

## Impact of Operations on the Community

The Company's primary mission is to contribute positively to the local community. This is realized through the enhancement of economic and social welfare, achieved through job creation and the implementation of various sustainable community development and empowerment programs.

## Customer Complaint Mechanism

The Company upholds the principles of transparency and accountability, including in the management of labor and OSH issues. Therefore, the Company opens complaint channels for all stakeholders to submit complaints or feedback regarding labor and OSH conditions in the workplace. Information obtained from these complaints will be analyzed comprehensively to evaluate the effectiveness of implemented programs and to identify areas requiring improvement. The Company has a responsive mechanism in place to address all incoming reports, which are handled by the relevant Divisions/Departments.
