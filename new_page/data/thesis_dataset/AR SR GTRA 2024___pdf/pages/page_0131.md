## Manajemen Risiko

Perseroan secara aktif menerapkan manajemen risiko untuk menjaga kesinambungan operasional dan melindungi nilai bisnis. Dalam industri logistik, risiko utama mencakup ketidakstabilan permintaan (order), kekurangan tenaga pengemudi yang kompeten, serta risiko kecelakaan kendaraan. Untuk memitigasi hal ini, Perseroan membangun sistem pelatihan safety driving yang dilakukan secara berkala, penetapan jalur operasional aman (dengan melarang kendaraan melalui wilayah berisiko tinggi seperti Jonggol, Cipanas, dan Lembang), serta memastikan pemeliharaan kendaraan secara rutin.

Turnover rendah pada pengemudi dan karyawan juga menjadi indikator efektivitas sistem manajemen risiko yang diterapkan. Pengelolaan disiplin, insentif berbasis performa, serta hubungan kerja yang transparan menjadi kunci dalam menciptakan stabilitas tenaga kerja. Selain itu, Perseroan menerapkan kebijakan tidak ada penolakan order dari pengemudi mitra sebagai upaya menjaga kepastian layanan pelanggan dan efisiensi distribusi.

## Pemanfaatan Peluang dan Prospek Bisnis

Perseroan terus menjajaki peluang ekspansi dan pertumbuhan usaha melalui pendekatan strategis yang berbasis kebutuhan pelanggan. Dalam ekosistem logistik yang semakin menuntut kecepatan dan akurasi, penggunaan armada khusus—misalnya untuk produk makanan, plastik, dan barang bernilai tinggi—memberikan nilai tambah yang membedakan Perseroan dari pesaing. Pengalaman panjang dalam melayani perusahaan besar seperti Mayora, Mondelez, dan Kapal Api menunjukkan kepercayaan pasar terhadap profesionalisme dan fleksibilitas layanan Perseroan.

Branding kendaraan operasional yang masif di jalan raya juga menjadi sarana promosi efektif yang memperluas eksposur publik, khususnya untuk calon pelanggan segmen business-to-business (B2B). Relasi yang terbina dengan para pemangku keputusan di industri manufaktur dan distribusi telah membuka pintu masuk bagi kolaborasi jangka panjang dan penyediaan layanan logistik yang lebih kompleks di masa mendatang.

Dengan kesiapan armada, sistem pengawasan berbasis GPS, serta kemampuan adaptasi terhadap kebutuhan pelanggan, prospek bisnis Perseroan pada tahun 2025 dipandang sangat positif, terutama dalam sektor pengangkutan barang konsumsi, kemasan, dan bahan baku industri ringan.

On the other hand, the Company continues to optimize opportunities through the integration of digital technology into its business processes. GPS-based vehicle tracking systems and intelligent route management have been implemented to improve delivery accuracy, operational efficiency, and service transparency for customers.

Strengthening relationships with strategic customers is also a top priority. A long-term partnership approach focused on service reliability, operational flexibility, and logistics value-added has proven to drive business growth. The reputation built over the years serves as a gateway to reach new customers and expand service coverage in the future.

## Risk Management

The Company actively implements risk management to maintain operational continuity and protect business value. In the logistics industry, key risks include order instability, a shortage of competent drivers, and vehicle accident risks. To mitigate these, the Company has established a regular safety driving training system, the designation of safe operational routes (by prohibiting vehicles from passing through high-risk areas such as Jonggol, Cipanas, and Lembang), and ensures routine vehicle maintenance.

Low turnover among drivers and employees is also an indicator of the effectiveness of the implemented risk management system. Discipline management, performance-based incentives, and transparent working relationships are key to creating workforce stability. Additionally, the Company implements a policy of no order refusal from partner drivers as an effort to maintain customer service certainty and distribution efficiency.

The Company continues to explore opportunities for business expansion and growth through a strategic approach predicated on customer needs. Within the increasingly demanding logistics ecosystem, characterized by the need for speed and accuracy, the deployment of specialized fleets—for instance, catering to food products, plastics, and high-value goods—provides a differentiated value proposition that distinguishes the Company from its competitors. The Company's extensive experience in serving prominent corporations such as Mayora, Mondelez, and Kapal Api demonstrates market confidence in its professionalism and service flexibility.

The extensive branding of operational vehicles on public roads also serves as an effective promotional channel, broadening public exposure, particularly to potential business-to-business (B2B) segment clients. Established relationships with key decision-makers in the manufacturing and distribution industries have facilitated entry points for long-term collaborations and the provision of more complex logistics services in the future.

With fleet readiness, a GPS-based monitoring system, and the ability to adapt to client requirements, the Company's business prospects for 2025 are viewed as highly positive, especially within the sectors of consumer goods transportation, packaging, and raw materials for light industry.
