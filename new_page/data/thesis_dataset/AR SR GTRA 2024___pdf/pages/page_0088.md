# Pelatihan

Sepanjang tahun 2024, Perseroan masih melakukan kajian atas program pelatihan terintegrasi yang relevan atas dinamika ekosistem bisnis.

# Penilaian Kinerja Direksi

Direksi Perseroan menjalankan tugasnya berdasarkan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan. Tanggung jawab atas sistem pengendalian internal Perseroan, khususnya yang berkaitan dengan manajemen keuangan, didelegasikan kepada Unit Audit Internal oleh Dewan Direksi. Sepanjang tahun 2024, Unit Audit Internal, bekerja sama dengan Dewan Direksi, telah melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya dalam pengelolaan keuangan Perseroan dengan baik.

# Penilaian (Self Assessment) Terhadap Kinerja Direksi

Penilaian kinerja Direksi dilakukan setiap tahun dan merupakan tanggung jawab Dewan Komisaris. Proses ini berfokus pada evaluasi pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab anggota Direksi dalam mengelola Perseroan, sesuai dengan amanat Anggaran Dasar Perseroan, Piagam Direksi, dan rencana kerja anggaran tahunan. Hasil penilaian kinerja Direksi disampaikan pada Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS) Tahunan. Momen ini menjadi krusial untuk mengevaluasi dan meningkatkan kinerja Direksi demi mencapai tujuan Perseroan.

# Penilaian Kinerja Komite di Bawah Direksi dan Dasar Penilaiannya

Dalam menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya, Direksi didukung oleh Sekretaris Perseroan dan Unit Audit Internal. Seluruh unit kerja di bawah Direksi telah menunjukkan kinerja yang baik sepanjang tahun 2024.

# Remunerasi Direksi

Gaji dan tunjangan yang dibayarkan kepada Dewan Komisaris Perseroan yang berakhir pada tanggal 31 Desember 2024 adalah sebesar Rp2.304.000.000.

# Training

Throughout 2024, the Company continued to review integrated training programs relevant to the dynamics of the business ecosystem.

# Board of Directors Performance Assessment

The Company's Board of Directors performs its duties in accordance with the Company's Articles of Association. Responsibility for the Company's internal control system, particularly concerning financial management, is delegated to the Internal Audit Unit by the Board of Directors. Throughout 2024, the Internal Audit Unit, in cooperation with the Board of Directors, has performed its duties and responsibilities in the Company's financial management effectively.

# Self-Assessment of the Board of Directors Performance

The performance assessment of the Board of Directors is conducted annually and is the responsibility of the Board of Commissioners. This process focuses on evaluating the performance of the duties and responsibilities of the Board of Directors members in managing the Company, in accordance with the mandate of the Company's Articles of Association, the Board of Directors Charter, and the annual budget work plan. The results of the Board of Directors performance assessment are presented at the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders (AGMS). This moment is crucial for evaluating and improving the performance of the Board of Directors to achieve the Company's objectives.

# Performance Assessment of Committees Under the Board of Directors and its Assessment Basis

In carrying out its duties and responsibilities, the Board of Directors is supported by the Corporate Secretary and the Internal Audit Unit. All work units under the Board of Directors have shown good performance throughout 2024.

# Remuneration of the Board of Director

Salaries and benefits paid to the Board of Commissioners of the Company for the year ended December 31, 2024 amounted to Rp2.304.000.000.
