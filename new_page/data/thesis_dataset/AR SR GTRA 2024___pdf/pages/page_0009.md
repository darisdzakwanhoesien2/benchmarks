Until the end of December 2024, the Company did not undertake any corporate actions, such as stock splits, stock mergers, stock dividends, bonus shares, changes in the nominal value of shares, issuance of convertible securities, or capital increases and decreases.

# INFORMASI PENGHENTIAN SEMENTARA DAN/ ATAU SANKSI PERDAGANGAN SAHAM SERTA PENGHAPUSAN PENCATATAN SAHAM STOCK TRADE SUSPENSION AND/OR SANCTION AND STOCK DELISTING

Pada tanggal 19 Februari 2024, Bursa Efek Indonesia (BEI) melakukan suspensi sementara terhadap perdagangan saham GTRA di Pasar Reguler dan Pasar Tunai akibat penurunan harga yang signifikan dalam waktu singkat. Suspensi ini dilakukan sebagai langkah cooling down untuk melindungi investor dan menjaga stabilitas pasar. Seiring dengan perkembangan yang ada, suspensi tersebut dicabut pada 20 Februari 2024 dan perdagangan saham GTRA kembali dibuka pada sesi I hari yang sama.

On February 19, 2024, the Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX) temporarily suspended trading of GTRA shares on the Regular and Cash Markets due to a significant decline in the share price within a short period. The suspension was implemented as a cooling-down measure to protect investors and maintain market stability. Following subsequent developments, the suspension was lifted on February 20, 2024, and trading of GTRA shares resumed in the first session of the same day.
