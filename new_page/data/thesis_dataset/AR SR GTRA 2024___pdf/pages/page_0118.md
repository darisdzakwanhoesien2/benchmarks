|  D1
D1 Planning | Mengakhiri kemiskinan dalam segala bentuk di manapun.
End poverty in all its forms everywhere. | Dukungan terhadap rumah singgah anak jalanan dan pemberian upah layak kepada seluruh karyawan.
Support for shelters for street children and the provision of fair wages to all employees.  |
|  D4
Quality Education | Menjamin kualitas pendidikan yang inklusif dan merata serta meningkatkan kesempatan belajar sepanjang hayat untuk semua.
Ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all. | Penyediaan akses pembelajaran bagi anak-anak jalanan dan pelatihan berkelanjutan bagi karyawan.
Provision of access to learning for street children and continuous professional development for employees.  |
|  D5
Educating | Mencapai kesetaraan gender dan memberdayakan kaum perempuan.
Achieve gender equality and empower all women and girls. | Persentase karyawan perempuan menunjukkan keterwakilan yang cukup besar dalam struktur organisasi serta tidak adanya praktik diskriminatif dalam ketenagakerjaan.
The percentage of female employees reflects significant representation within the organizational structure and indicates the absence of discriminatory practices in employment.  |
|  D8
Educating Management | Mengurangi ketimpangan di dalam dan antar negara.
Reduce inequality within and among countries. | Penerapan praktik ketenagakerjaan inklusif tanpa diskriminasi, kerja paksa, dan pekerja anak.
Implementation of inclusive employment practices without discrimination, forced labor, or child labor.  |
|  D9
Educating Action | Mengambil tindakan cepat untuk mengatasi perubahan iklim dan dampaknya.
Take urgent action to combat climate change and its impacts. | Penanaman mangrove di wilayah pesisir dan upaya efisiensi energi melalui perawatan kendaraan dan pelatihan safety driving.
Mangrove planting in coastal areas and energy efficiency initiatives through vehicle maintenance and safety driving training.  |
|  D14
Educating Women | Melestarikan dan memanfaatkan secara berkelanjutan sumber daya kelautan dan samudera untuk pembangunan berkelanjutan.
Conserve and sustainably use the oceans, seas and marine resources for sustainable development. | Pelaksanaan program konservasi ekosistem pesisir melalui penanaman mangrove.
Implementation of coastal ecosystem conservation programs through mangrove planting.  |
