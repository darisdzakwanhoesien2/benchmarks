# Struktur dan Kedudukan Unit Audit Internal

Struktur dan kedudukan Unit Audit Internal dalam Perseroan sebagai berikut:

1. Unit Audit Internal dipimpin oleh seorang Kepala Unit Audit Internal;
2. Kepala Unit Audit Internal diangkat dan diberhentikan oleh Direktur Utama atas persetujuan Dewan Komisaris;
3. Direktur Utama dapat memberhentikan Kepala Unit Audit Internal, setelah mendapat persetujuan Dewan Komisaris, jika Kepala Unit Audit Internal tidak memenuhi persyaratan sebagai Unit Audit Internal sebagaimana yang diatur dalam peraturan perusahaan dan/atau gagal atau tidak cakap menjalankan tugas;
4. Kepala Unit Audit Internal bertanggung jawab kepada Direktur Utama; serta
5. Auditor yang duduk dalam Unit Audit Internal bertanggung jawab secara langsung kepada Kepala Unit Audit Internal.

# Tugas dan Tanggung Jawab Unit Audit Internal

Informasi mengenai tugas dan tanggung jawab Unit Audit Internal diuraikan sebagai berikut:

1. Menguji dan mengevaluasi pelaksanaan pengendalian internal dan sistem manajemen risiko sesuai dengan kebijakan Perseroan;
2. Melakukan pemeriksaan dan penilaian atas efisiensi dan efektivitas di bidang keuangan, akuntansi, operasional, sumber daya manusia, pemasaran, teknologi informasi, dan kegiatan lainnya;
3. Memberikan saran perbaikan dan informasi yang objektif tentang kegiatan yang diperiksa pada seluruh tingkat manajemen;
4. Membuat laporan hasil audit dan menyampaikan laporan tersebut kepada Direktur Utama dan Dewan Komisaris;
5. Memantau, menganalisis, dan melaporkan pelaksanaan tindak lanjut perbaikan yang telah disarankan;
6. Bekerja sama dengan Komite Audit;
7. Menyusun program untuk mengevaluasi mutu kegiatan audit internal yang dilakukannya; serta
8. Melakukan pemeriksaan khusus apabila diperlukan.

# Dasar Hukum Penunjukkan

Keputusan Tertulis Direksi tentang Pembentukan dan Pengangkatan Kepala Unit Audit Internal tanggal 19 Agustus 2022.

# Piagam Audit Internal

Pelaksanaan tugas Unit Audit Internal didasarkan pada Keputusan Tertulis Direksi tertanggal 20 Juni 2024, yang mengatur tentang Pembentukan dan Pengangkatan Kepala Unit Audit Internal. Keputusan ini disusun dengan merujuk pada Peraturan OJK No. 56/POJK.04/2015 tentang Pembentukan dan Pedoman Penyusunan Piagam Unit Audit Internal.

# Independensi Unit Audit Internal

Dalam menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya, Unit Audit Internal senantiasa menjunjung tinggi prinsip independensi dan kehati-hatian. Perseroan berkomitmen untuk memastikan bahwa kepala dan seluruh anggota Unit Audit Internal yang bertugas saat ini terbebas dari segala bentuk intervensi, baik yang berasal dari Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, maupun Pemegang Saham. Selain itu, Unit Audit Internal juga sepenuhnya independen dari benturan kepentingan serta pengaruh atau tekanan dari pihak manapun yang tidak selaras dengan peraturan perundang-undangan, nilai-nilai etika, prinsip-prinsip dan praktik tata kelola perusahaan yang sehat, serta kebijakan Perseroan yang mungkin timbul selama pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab Unit Audit Internal.

# Structure and Position of the Internal Audit Unit

The structure and position of the Internal Audit Unit within the Company are as follows:

1. The Internal Audit Unit is headed by a Head of the Internal Audit Unit.
2. The Head of the Internal Audit Unit is appointed and dismissed by the President Director with the approval of the Board of Commissioners.
3. The President Director may dismiss the Head of the Internal Audit Unit, after obtaining approval from the Board of Commissioners, if the Head of the Internal Audit Unit does not meet the requirements as an Internal Audit Unit as stipulated in the company regulations and/or fails or is incapable of carrying out their duties.
4. The Head of the Internal Audit Unit is accountable to the President Director.
5. Auditors within the Internal Audit Unit are directly accountable to the Head of the Internal Audit Unit.

# Duties and Responsibilities of the Internal Audit Unit

Information regarding the duties and responsibilities of the Internal Audit Unit is described as follows:

1. To examine and evaluate the implementation of internal controls and risk management systems in accordance with the Company's policies.
2. To conduct audits and assessments of efficiency and effectiveness in the areas of finance, accounting, operations, human resources, marketing, information technology, and other activities.
3. To provide recommendations for improvement and objective information on the activities examined at all levels of management.
4. To prepare audit reports and submit these reports to the President Director and the Board of Commissioners.
5. To monitor, analyze, and report on the implementation of corrective actions that have been recommended.
6. To cooperate with the Audit Committee.
7. To develop a program to evaluate the quality of its internal audit activities.
8. To conduct special audits when necessary.

# Legal Basis for Appointment

The Written Decision of the Board of Directors concerning the Formation and Appointment of the Head of the Internal Audit Unit is dated August 19, 2022.

# Internal Audit Charter

The execution of the Internal Audit Unit's duties is based on the Written Decision of the Board of Directors dated June 20, 2024, which governs the Formation and Appointment of the Head of the Internal Audit Unit. This decision is prepared with reference to OJK Regulation No. 56/POJK.04/2015 concerning the Formation and Guidelines for the Preparation of the Internal Audit Unit Charter.

# Independence of the Internal Audit Unit

In carrying out its duties and responsibilities, the Internal Audit Unit always upholds the principles of independence and prudence. The Company is committed to ensuring that the head and all members of the currently serving Internal Audit Unit are free from any form of intervention, whether from the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, or Shareholders. In addition, the Internal Audit Unit is also fully independent of conflicts of interest and influence or pressure from any party that is not in line with laws and regulations, ethical values, sound corporate governance principles and practices, and Company policies that may arise during the performance of the duties and responsibilities of the Internal Audit Unit.
