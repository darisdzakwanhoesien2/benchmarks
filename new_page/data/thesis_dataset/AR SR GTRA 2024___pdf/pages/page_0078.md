# RAPAT UMUM PEMEGANG SAHAM (RUPS) GENERAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS (GMS)

Sebagai forum komunikasi utama antara Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, dan para Pemegang Saham, RUPS memiliki peran sentral dalam tata kelola Perseroan. RUPS merupakan forum pengambilan keputusan tertinggi Perseroan, di mana Pemegang saham dapat menyampaikan informasi, memberikan pengesahan, serta menyampaikan pendapat terkait pengelolaan Perseroan selama tahun buku yang berjalan, dan juga rencana pengelolaan Perseroan untuk tahun yang akan datang.

Pelaksanaan RUPS mengacu pada ketentuan yang tertuang dalam Anggaran Dasar Perseroan, Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 15/POJK.04/2020 tentang Rencana dan Penyelenggaraan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Perusahaan Terbuka, serta Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 16/POJK.04/2020 tentang Pelaksanaan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Perusahaan Terbuka Secara Elektronik.

Sesuai dengan regulasi tersebut, RUPS Tahunan wajib diselenggarakan setiap tahun, paling lambat 6 bulan setelah tahun buku Perseroan berakhir. Selain RUPS Tahunan, Perseroan juga dapat menyelenggarakan RUPS Luar Biasa yang bersifat kondisional, atau diadakan sewaktu-waktu sesuai dengan kebutuhan dan kepentingan Perseroan.

Perseroan senantiasa berupaya untuk memfasilitasi partisipasi aktif seluruh pemegang saham dalam setiap penyelenggaraan RUPS. Kami percaya bahwa komunikasi yang terbuka dan partisipatif antara Perseroan dan Pemegang saham adalah kunci untuk mencapai kesuksesan dan pertumbuhan yang berkelanjutan.

# Pelaksanaan RUPS Tahun 2024

Guna memenuhi ketentuan Pasal 49 ayat (1) dan Pasal 51 ayat (1) Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No.15/POJK.04/2020 tentang Rencana dan Penyelenggaraan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Perusahaan Terbuka ("POJK 15/2020"). Perseroan menyelenggarakan RUPS Tahunan pada hari Rabu, 29 Mei 2024 di Hotel Santika Premiere, Ruang Mawar, Jalan AIPDA KS Tubun, Slipi, Jakarta. 3. Supervisory and Compliance Committee
4. Conflict of Interest Committee
5. Good Corporate Governance Committee

7. Risk Management Committee
8. Corporate Secretary
9. Functions and Independence

Each part of the Company's organizational structure carries out its functions and responsibilities in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations, the Company's Articles of Association, and other provisions based on the principle of independence. This is to ensure that each part can carry out its duties, functions, and responsibilities professionally and independently for the benefit of the Company.

As the primary communication forum between the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, and the Shareholders, the GMS plays a central role in the Company's governance. The GMS serves as the Company's highest decision-making forum, where Shareholders can convey information, provide approvals, and express opinions regarding the Company's management during the past fiscal year, as well as the Company's management plans for the coming year.

The implementation of the GMS refers to the provisions set forth in the Company's Articles of Association, Financial Services Authority (OJK) Regulation No. 15/POJK.04/2020 concerning the Plan and Implementation of the General Meeting of Shareholders of Public Companies, and Financial Services Authority (OJK) Regulation No. 16/POJK.04/2020 concerning the Implementation of General Meetings of Shareholders of Public Companies Electronically.

In accordance with these regulations, the Annual GMS is required to be held every year, no later than 6 months after the Company's fiscal year ends. In addition to the Annual GMS, the Company may also hold an Extraordinary GMS which is conditional, or held at any time in accordance with the needs and interests of the Company.

The Company always strives to facilitate the active participation of all Shareholders in every GMS implementation. We believe that open and participatory communication between the Company and its Shareholders is key to achieving sustainable success and growth.
