1. GENERAL (Continued)

c. Initial Public Offering

On March 17, 2023, the Company obtained an effective statement from the Board of Commissioner of the Financial Services Authority ("OJK") in letter No. S-76/D.04/2023 to conduct an Initial Public Offering ("IPO") of 378,875,000 shares of Registered Shares with nominal value of Rp100 per share with offering price Rp150 per share to public. The shares offered during the IPO were listed in the Indonesian Stock Exchange on March 30, 2023.
