Limbah Non-B3 terdiri dari limbah domestik perkantor (sampah, kardus, kertas) dan limbah dari kegiatan perawatan (ban bekas). In an effort to reduce GHG emissions and contribute to environmental sustainability, the Company has implemented various measurable programs. These programs include:

- Optimization of electricity usage to reduce consumption.
- Implementation of periodic emission tests on operational vehicles to ensure compliance with environmental standards.
- Procurement of a fleet of newer vehicles with more advanced engine and emission management technology.
- Implementation of continuous greening programs throughout the Company's operational areas.

The Company's operational activities generate waste classified into Non-Hazardous and Toxic (Non-B3) and Hazardous and Toxic (B3) waste, according to their potential hazard levels. Non-B3 waste consists of office domestic waste (garbage, cardboard, paper) and waste from maintenance activities (used tires). B3 waste, generated from maintenance activities, workshops, and warehouses, includes used batteries and used lubricants/oil.
