## DEWAN KOMISARIS BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS

Dewan Komisaris, sebagai organ tata kelola perusahaan yang menjalankan fungsi pengawasan sesuai dengan ketentuan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan, memiliki peran krusial dalam memastikan pengelolaan Perseroan berjalan dengan baik. Pengawasan ini diwujudkan melalui pemantauan terhadap tindakan pengelolaan yang dilakukan oleh Direksi, serta pemberian nasihat yang konstruktif kepada Direksi. Selain itu, Dewan Komisaris bertanggung jawab untuk memastikan penerapan prinsip-prinsip Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik (Good Corporate Governance/GCG) di seluruh lini organisasi Perseroan.

Anggota Dewan Komisaris diangkat dan diberhentikan oleh Pemegang Saham melalui mekanisme Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS) yang diadakan setiap 5 tahun sekali. Namun, periode ini tidak mengurangi hak RUPS untuk menentukan jangka waktu yang berbeda. Dalam proses pemilihan anggota Dewan Komisaris, aspek integritas, kompetensi, dan reputasi menjadi pertimbangan utama, untuk memastikan kesesuaian dengan kebutuhan bisnis Perseroan.

## Komposisi Dewan Komisaris

Komposisi dan jumlah anggota Dewan Komisaris ditetapkan melalui Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS) dengan masa jabatan 5 tahun dan dapat diangkat kembali. Pengangkatan kembali mempertimbangkan elektabilitas calon anggota Dewan Komisaris dan kompleksitas Perseroan.

To receive the accountability report on the realization of the use of proceeds from the Company's Public Offering, where the proceeds from the Initial Public Offering received by the Company, after deducting all emission costs related to the Public Offering, will be fully utilized for working capital.

The Board of Commissioners, as a Company's corporate governance body that performs supervisory functions in accordance with the provisions of the Company's Articles of Association, plays a crucial role in ensuring the Company's management is properly run. This oversight is realized through monitoring the management actions taken by the Directors, as well as providing constructive advice to the Directors. In addition, the Board of Commissioners is responsible for ensuring the implementation of Good Corporate Governance (GCG) principles throughout the Company's organization.

Members of the Board of Commissioners are appointed and dismissed by the Shareholders through the mechanism of the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) held every 5 years. However, this period does not diminish the GMS's right to determine a different term. In the process of selecting members of the Board of Commissioners, aspects of integrity, competence, and reputation are primary considerations, to ensure suitability with the Company's business needs.

## Composition of the Board of Commissioners

The composition and number of members of the Board of Commissioners are determined through the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) with a term of office of 5 years and may be reappointed. Reappointment takes into account the electability of prospective members of the Board of Commissioners and the complexity of the Company.
