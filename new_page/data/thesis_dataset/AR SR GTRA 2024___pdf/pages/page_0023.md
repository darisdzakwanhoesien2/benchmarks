2024 Annual Report

# Perbandingan Antara Hasil dan Target Kinerja

Graha Trans menargetkan pendapatan sebesar Rp418 miliar dan laba bersih Rp40 miliar pada tahun 2024. Proyeksi ini didasarkan pada optimalisasi armada yang diperoleh pada tahun 2023 serta pertumbuhan permintaan dari pelanggan utama. Target ini juga berpotensi meningkat seiring dengan permintaan tambahan unit dari pelanggan. Melalui kinerja yang optimal dari setiap unit bisnis, Perseroan berhasil melampaui target pendapatan tahun 2024 sebesar 2,61%, dengan realisasi mencapai Rp428,93 miliar dibandingkan target awal sebesar Rp418 miliar. Namun demikian, laba bersih tercatat sedikit lebih rendah dari target, yaitu sebesar Rp39,84 miliar atau menurun 1,30% dibandingkan target laba bersih sebesar Rp40 miliar.

# Tantangan yang Dihadapi Perseroan

Pada tahun 2024, Perseroan menghadapi berbagai tantangan yang kompleks, baik dari dinamika perekonomian nasional maupun perkembangan industri transportasi. Meskipun perekonomian Indonesia menunjukkan pertumbuhan yang stabil, ancaman resesi global dan ketidakpastian rantai pasok internasional akibat ketegangan geopolitik tetap menjadi faktor yang mempengaruhi operasional Perseroan.

Di tingkat industri, sektor transportasi nasional juga mengalami tekanan yang tidak ringan. Laporan dari Masyarakat Transportasi Indonesia (MTI) menyoroti berbagai pekerjaan rumah yang masih harus diselesaikan, mulai dari ketimpangan infrastruktur antarwilayah, regulasi yang belum mengikuti perkembangan teknologi, hingga peningkatan kualitas pelayanan publik. Kondisi ini memberikan tantangan tersendiri bagi Perseroan dalam menjaga standar layanan yang prima dan memenuhi ekspektasi pelanggan serta Pemangku Kepentingan lainnya.

Selain tantangan makro dan struktural, Perseroan juga harus menghadapi hambatan operasional di lapangan. Salah satu isu eksternal yang cukup berdampak adalah kebijakan pembatasan pembelian solar bersubsidi menggunakan sistem barcode. Sayangnya, sistem ini masih kerap mengalami gangguan teknis yang menyebabkan keterlambatan pengisian bahan bakar dan berdampak langsung pada efisiensi operasional. Akibatnya, waktu tempuh distribusi menjadi tidak optimal dan overhead cost meningkat.

Di sisi lain, belum rampungnya pembangunan sejumlah pool juga menjadi kendala tersendiri. Dalam kondisi ini, Perseroan terpaksa mengandalkan pihak ketiga untuk layanan operasional di wilayah-wilayah tertentu, yang berdampak pada kenaikan biaya operasional secara keseluruhan. Meski demikian, Perseroan tetap berupaya menjaga kualitas layanan sambil mempersiapkan percepatan penyelesaian infrastruktur internal.

Dalam menghadapi tantangan-tantangan tersebut, Perseroan berkomitmen untuk terus meningkatkan kualitas pengelolaan keberlanjutan dalam setiap aktivitas bisnisnya. Penerapan standar kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja (K3) secara konsisten menjadi prioritas utama, mengingat operasional Perseroan yang melibatkan aktivitas distribusi barang dengan risiko tinggi. Dengan strategi yang adaptif dan responsif terhadap perubahan, Perseroan optimis dapat mengatasi berbagai tantangan sepanjang tahun 2024 dan terus memberikan nilai tambah bagi seluruh Pemangku Kepentingan.

# Comparison Between Performance Results and Targets

Graha Trans targets revenues of IDR 418 billion and a net profit of IDR 40 billion in 2024. This projection is based on the optimization of the fleet acquired in 2023 and the growth in demand from key customers. This target also has the potential to increase in line with additional unit requests from customers. Through the optimal performance of each business unit, The Company successfully exceeded its 2024 revenue target by 2.61%, achieving Rp428.93 billion compared to the initial target of Rp418 billion. However, net profit was slightly below target, amounting to Rp39.84 billion, or a decrease of 1.30% compared to the net profit target of Rp40 billion.

# Challenges Faced by the Company

In 2024, the Company faced various complex challenges, stemming from both national economic dynamics and developments in the transportation industry. Although the Indonesian economy showed stable growth, the threat of a global recession and uncertainty in the international supply chain due to geopolitical tensions remained factors influencing the Company's operations.

At the industry level, the national transportation sector also experienced significant pressure. Reports from the Indonesian Transportation Society (MTI) highlighted various outstanding issues that still need to be addressed, ranging from infrastructure disparities between regions and regulations that have not kept pace with technological advancements to the improvement of public service quality. This situation presents unique challenges for the Company in maintaining prime service standards and meeting the expectations of customers and other Stakeholders.

In addition to macro and structural challenges, the Company also had to face operational obstacles in the field. One significant external issue was the policy on limiting subsidized diesel fuel purchases using a barcode system. Unfortunately, this system frequently experienced technical disruptions that caused delays in refueling and directly impacted operational efficiency. Consequently, distribution travel time became suboptimal, and overhead costs increased.

On the other hand, the incomplete construction of several pools also posed a challenge. In this situation, the Company was forced to rely on third parties for operational services in certain regions, which resulted in an overall increase in operational costs. Nevertheless, the Company continued to strive to maintain service quality while preparing for the accelerated completion of internal infrastructure.

In addressing these challenges, the Company is committed to continuously improving the quality of sustainability management in every aspect of its business activities. The consistent implementation of occupational health and safety (OHS) standards is a primary priority, given the Company's operations involving the distribution of goods with high risks. With an adaptive and responsive strategy to changes, the Company is optimistic about overcoming various challenges throughout 2024 and continuing to provide added value to all Stakeholders.
