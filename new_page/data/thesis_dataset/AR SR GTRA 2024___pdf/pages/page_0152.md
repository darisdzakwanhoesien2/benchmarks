## Mekanisme Pengaduan Pelanggan

Sebagai wujud komitmen terhadap kepuasan pelanggan, Perusahaan menyediakan platform yang komprehensif untuk menerima pengaduan dan keluhan. Media ini berfungsi sebagai instrumen penting dalam mengevaluasi kinerja layanan dan merumuskan strategi peningkatan berkelanjutan. Pelanggan dapat menyampaikan laporan melalui berbagai saluran komunikasi atau kepada petugas/pengemudi di lapangan. Setiap laporan akan direspons dan ditangani secara profesional oleh Divisi/Departemen yang relevan. Perusahaan dengan bangga melaporkan bahwa selama tiga tahun terakhir, tidak ada pengaduan signifikan yang diterima terkait layanan logistik dan transportasi, mencerminkan kualitas layanan yang tinggi.

## Komitmen terhadap Mitra Usaha

Perseroan menempatkan komitmen kuat pada pertumbuhan dan penguatan ekonomi masyarakat di sekitar wilayah operasional. Hal ini diwujudkan melalui kebijakan rekrutmen tenaga kerja lokal serta prioritas kerja sama dengan pemasok barang dan jasa dari masyarakat setempat. Dalam hal pemasok lokal belum dapat memenuhi kebutuhan, Perseroan akan beralih ke pemasok nasional, yang berdomisili dan beroperasi di Indonesia. Kerja sama dengan pemasok internasional hanya dilakukan apabila pemasok nasional tidak dapat memenuhi kebutuhan atau dalam kondisi tertentu.

## Seleksi Mitra Usaha secara Adil dan Bertanggung Jawab

Perseroan menerapkan proses seleksi pemasok yang ketat, berlandaskan pada reputasi, rekam jejak, dan integritas calon mitra usaha. Prosedur pemilihan yang terstandardisasi ini memastikan bahwa setiap kerja sama yang terjalin bebas dari potensi benturan kepentingan, sehingga melindungi kepentingan Perseroan secara optimal.

## Hubungan dengan Mitra Usaha

Perseroan senantiasa menjalin kemitraan strategis dengan para mitra usaha, yang didasari oleh prinsip kerja sama yang saling menguntungkan. Komitmen kami tercermin dalam pemenuhan kewajiban pembayaran secara tepat waktu.

## Customer Complaint Mechanism

As a manifestation of its commitment to customer satisfaction, the Company provides a comprehensive platform to receive complaints and grievances. This medium serves as an important instrument in evaluating service performance and formulating continuous improvement strategies. Customers can submit reports through various communication channels or to officers/Drivers in the field. Each report will be responded to and handled professionally by the relevant Division/Department. The Company is proud to report that over the past three years, no significant complaints have been received regarding logistics and transportation services, reflecting the high quality of service.

## Commitment to Business Partners

The Company places a strong commitment on the growth and strengthening of the community's economy in the vicinity of its operational areas. This commitment is realized through local workforce recruitment policies and priority collaboration with suppliers of goods and services from the local community. In the event that local suppliers are unable to meet the needs, the Company will turn to national suppliers, domiciled and operating in Indonesia. Cooperation with international suppliers is only conducted if national suppliers cannot fulfill the requirements or under specific circumstances..

## Fair and Responsible Selection of Business Partners

The Company implements a stringent supplier selection process, grounded in the reputation, track record, and integrity of potential business partners. This standardized selection procedure ensures that every partnership established is free from potential conflicts of interest, thereby optimally protecting the Company's interests.

## Relationship with Business Partners

The Company consistently fosters strategic partnerships with its business partners, based on the principle of mutually beneficial cooperation. Our commitment is reflected in the timely fulfillment of payment obligations.

## Business Partner Complaint Mechanism

The Company has established dedicated communication channels to provide a platform for business partners to submit complaints or grievances. This mechanism is designed to facilitate continuous evaluation of existing collaborations and serve as a basis for future strategic decision-making. Each report received will be thoroughly addressed by the relevant division or department. Over the past three years, the Company has recorded no significant or material complaints from business partners, reflecting the effectiveness of the established collaborations.
