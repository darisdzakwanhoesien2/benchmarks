## Tumpahan Limbah

Perseroan menyadari potensi risiko lingkungan yang terkait dengan pengelolaan limbah B3 dari perawatan kendaraan operasional. Oleh karena itu, kami menerapkan sistem pengelolaan limbah B3 yang ketat sesuai dengan peraturan yang berlaku, guna mencegah potensi tumpahan dan dampaknya terhadap tanah, air, udara, serta kesehatan masyarakat. Upaya proaktif ini telah membuahkan hasil, dengan tidak adanya insiden tumpahan limbah B3 atau non-B3 yang signifikan di seluruh lokasi operasi Perseroan hingga tahun 2024.

## ASPEK PENGADUAN TERKAIT LINGKUNGAN HIDUP ENVIRONMENTAL COMPLAINTS ASPECT

Dalam rangka meningkatkan akuntabilitas dan responsivitas terhadap isu lingkungan, Perseroan telah menyediakan platform pengaduan yang mudah diakses oleh pemangku kepentingan dan masyarakat. Platform ini dirancang untuk menampung masukan dan keluhan terkait program pengelolaan lingkungan, yang akan digunakan sebagai dasar evaluasi dan perbaikan berkelanjutan. Laporan dapat disampaikan melalui saluran komunikasi yang telah ditetapkan, dan setiap laporan akan ditindaklanjuti secara profesional oleh Divisi/Departemen yang relevan.

Berdasarkan catatan Perseroan, tidak ada laporan signifikan dan material terkait masalah lingkungan yang diterima selama tiga tahun terakhir. Pada tahun 2024, Perseroan juga tidak menerima pengaduan dalam bentuk apapun terkait pelanggaran etika, integritas, norma, atau dugaan pelanggaran peraturan yang berpotensi merusak lingkungan hidup, menunjukkan komitmen Perseroan terhadap kepatuhan dan praktik bisnis yang bertanggung jawab.

## Waste Spills

The Company acknowledges the potential environmental risks associated with the management of hazardous and toxic waste (B3) from operational vehicle maintenance. Therefore, we implement a stringent B3 waste management system in compliance with applicable regulations to prevent potential spills and their impact on soil, water, air, and public health. These proactive efforts have yielded results, with no significant B3 or non-B3 waste spill incidents occurring across the Company's operational sites up to 2024.

To enhance accountability and responsiveness to environmental issues, the Company has provided an easily accessible complaints platform for stakeholders and the public. This platform is designed to accommodate feedback and complaints related to environmental management programs, which will be used as a basis for evaluation and continuous improvement. Reports can be submitted through established communication channels, and each report will be professionally followed up by the relevant Division/Department.

Based on the Company's records, no significant and material reports related to environmental issues have been received over the past three years. In 2024, the Company also did not receive any complaints in any form related to ethical, integrity, or regulatory violations that could potentially harm the environment, demonstrating the Company's commitment to compliance and responsible business practices.

The Company reaffirms its commitment to continuously provide significant social contributions to all stakeholders. This is realized through strategic initiatives in human resource management, community development, customer service enhancement, and business partnership strengthening. Corporate social responsibility programs are designed and implemented specifically, tailored to the unique needs of each stakeholder group, both internal and external.
