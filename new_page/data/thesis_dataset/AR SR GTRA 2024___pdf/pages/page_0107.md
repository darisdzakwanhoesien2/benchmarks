# Penanganan Pengaduan

Setiap laporan pelanggaran akan ditindaklanjuti melalui proses penyelidikan yang cermat dan pengumpulan bukti. Apabila laporan terbukti tidak benar, maka proses akan dihentikan. Namun, jika laporan terbukti benar, Perseroan akan memberikan sanksi sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku, dan jika terdapat indikasi tindak pidana, akan diteruskan kepada pihak berwenang.

## Handling of Complaints

Every report of violation will be followed up through a careful investigation process and evidence gathering. If the report is proven untrue, the process will be stopped. However, if the report is proven true, the Company will impose sanctions in accordance with applicable regulations, and if there are indications of a criminal act, it will be forwarded to the authorities.
