2024 Annual Report

# Independencea Anggota Komite

Anggota Komite Audit wajib memenuhi kriteria dan kompetensi yang telah ditetapkan, mencakup integritas serta pengetahuan dan pengalaman di bidang audit, perbankan, keuangan, atas akuntansi. Keanggotaan dari pihak independen mensyaratkan pertimbangan atas kompetensi, kriteria, independensi, kerahasiaan, dan penerapan kode etik dalam pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab. Independensi anggota tercermin dari tidak adanya hubungan keuangan, kepengurusan, kepemilikan saham, atau hubungan keluarga dengan Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, atau Pemegang Saham Pengendali, guna memastikan kemandirian dalam bertindak.

# Rapat Komite Audit

Komite Audit secara rutin menyelenggarakan rapat untuk membahas rencana keuangan dan kinerja, yang bertujuan untuk mendukung fungsi pengawasan Perseroan. Selain rapat internal, Komite Audit juga mengadakan rapat bersama Dewan Komisaris dan/atau Direksi, auditor internal, dan auditor eksternal. Rapat ini berfungsi untuk menyelaraskan pemikiran dan gagasan terkait seluruh aspek operasional Perseroan.

# Kebijakan Rapat Komite Audit

Kebijakan terkait penyelenggaraan rapat diatur dalam Piagam Komite Audit. Berdasarkan piagam tersebut, rapat Komite Audit wajib diadakan secara berkala, paling sedikit satu kali dalam tiga bulan. Rapat dapat dilaksanakan apabila dihadiri oleh lebih dari setengah jumlah anggota Komite Audit. Selain itu, rapat tambahan dapat diadakan sewaktu-waktu jika diperlukan untuk membahas isu-isu tertentu. Setiap keputusan yang diambil dalam rapat Komite Audit didasarkan pada musyawarah mufakat.

# Frekuensi Rapat dan Tingkat Kehadiran

Sesuai dengan Piagam Komite Audit, rapat Komite Audit dilaksanakan minimal satu kali dalam tiga bulan, dan dapat diadakan lebih sering jika dianggap perlu. Sepanjang tahun 2023, Komite Audit telah menyelenggarakan 4 (empat) kali rapat dengan tingkat kehadiran anggota mencapai 100%.

# Audit Committee Member Independence

Audit Committee members must meet the established criteria and competencies, including integrity and knowledge and experience in the fields of audit, banking, finance, or accounting. Membership from independent parties requires consideration of competence, criteria, independence, confidentiality, and application of the code of ethics in carrying out their duties and responsibilities. The independence of members is reflected in the absence of financial, managerial, share ownership, or family relationships with the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, or Controlling Shareholders, to ensure independence in acting.

# Audit Committee Meetings

The Audit Committee routinely holds meetings to discuss financial plans and performance, which aims to support the Company's supervisory function. In addition to internal meetings, the Audit Committee also holds meetings with the Board of Commissioners and/or Directors, internal auditors, and external auditors. These meetings serve to align thoughts and ideas regarding all aspects of the Company's operations.

# Audit Committee Meetings Policy

The policy regarding the conduct of meetings is regulated in the Audit Committee Charter. Based on the charter, Audit Committee meetings must be held periodically, at least once every three months. Meetings may be held if attended by more than half of the Audit Committee members. In addition, additional meetings may be held at any time if needed to discuss specific issues. Every decision taken in the Audit Committee meeting is based on deliberation for consensus.

# Meeting Frequency and Attendance Rate

In accordance with the Audit Committee Charter, Audit Committee meetings are held at least once every three months, and may be held more frequently if deemed necessary. Throughout 2023, the Audit Committee held 4 (four) meetings with a 100% member attendance rate.
