Nilai Keberlanjutan

Berlandaskan visi dan misi sejak didirikan pada tahun 2004, Perseroan terus mengutamakan kualitas layanan, efisiensi operasional, serta kepuasan pelanggan sebagai prioritas utama. Dengan memegang teguh prinsip-prinsip ini, Direksi optimis bahwa Perseroan akan terus berkembang secara berkelanjutan, memberikan manfaat bagi pelanggan, mitra bisnis, dan masyarakat.

### Respons Perseroan terhadap Isu Keberlanjutan

Sebagai bagian dari komitmen terhadap keberlanjutan, Perseroan telah menerapkan berbagai inisiatif yang mencerminkan tanggung jawab sosial dan lingkungan. Pada tahun 2024, program Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) difokuskan pada kegiatan penanaman 200 bibit pohon bakau di Kawasan Ekowisata Mangrove, Pantai Indah Kapuk. Selain itu, Perseroan memperkuat aspek ketenagakerjaan dengan menjaga tingkat turnover yang rendah dan mengutamakan praktik kerja yang adil, termasuk sistem rekrutmen yang transparan dan mendukung kesetaraan gender (70% karyawan adalah perempuan). Tidak ada karyawan di bawah upah minimum regional, serta seluruh karyawan telah terdaftar dalam program BPJS.

### Komitmen Perseroan dalam Keberlanjutan

Strategi jangka panjang difokuskan pada modernisasi armada transportasi, perawatan berkala kendaraan setiap 10.000 km, dan pelaksanaan uji KIR setiap 6 bulan untuk memastikan emisi kendaraan tetap terkendali. Perseroan juga menerapkan kebijakan pengemudi yang disiplin melalui pelatihan safety driving, pemeriksaan kendaraan sebelum keberangkatan, serta penetapan titik istirahat setiap 4 jam dalam rute operasional jarak jauh. Pada aspek sosial, Perseroan menekankan pentingnya pelibatan karyawan dan pemberian hak cuti yang adil dan fleksibel.

### Pencapaian Kinerja Keberlanjutan

Sebagai perusahaan yang telah melantai di Bursa Efek Indonesia, Perseroan terus menunjukkan kinerja yang positif dan berkomitmen terhadap penguatan armada logistik. Upaya penguatan ini dilakukan melalui investasi strategis untuk modernisasi kendaraan operasional yang disesuaikan dengan kebutuhan pelanggan. Langkah ini menunjukkan keseriusan Perseroan dalam meningkatkan efisiensi layanan logistik serta mendukung pertumbuhan yang berkelanjutan.

### Tantangan dalam Kebijakan Berkelanjutan

Tantangan utama yang dihadapi Perseroan adalah pengelolaan dampak lingkungan dari operasional logistik serta keterbatasan dalam penerapan teknologi kendaraan rendah emisi (seperti kendaraan listrik) akibat biaya yang masih sangat tinggi. Sebagai kompensasi, Perseroan melakukan kegiatan penghijauan dan terus meningkatkan efisiensi operasional.

### The Value of Sustainability

Rooted in its vision and mission since its establishment in 2004, the Company consistently prioritizes service quality, operational efficiency, and customer satisfaction as its core objectives. By steadfastly upholding these principles, the Board of Directors is optimistic that the Company will continue to grow sustainably, delivering benefits to customers, business partners, and the wider community.

### The Company's Response to Sustainability Issues

As part of its commitment to sustainability, the Company has implemented various initiatives that reflect its social and environmental responsibility. In 2024, the Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) program focused on the planting of 200 mangrove seedlings in the Mangrove Ecotourism Area, Pantai Indah Kapuk. Additionally, the Company has strengthened its labor practices by maintaining a low employee turnover rate and prioritizing fair working conditions, including a transparent recruitment system that supports gender equality (70% of employees are female). No employees are compensated below the regional minimum wage, and all employees are enrolled in the BPJS social security program.

### The Company's Commitment to Sustainability

The long-term strategy focuses on the modernization of the transportation fleet, regular vehicle maintenance every 10,000 kilometers, and the mandatory vehicle safety inspection (KIR) every 6 months to ensure controlled vehicle emissions. The Company also enforces a disciplined driver policy through safety driving training, pre-departure vehicle inspections, and the establishment of mandatory rest stops every 4 hours on long-haul operational routes. In the social aspect, the Company emphasizes the importance of employee engagement and the provision of fair and flexible leave entitlements.

### Sustainable Performance Achievements

As a publicly listed company on the Indonesia Stock Exchange, the Company continues to demonstrate positive performance and is committed to strengthening its logistics fleet. This strengthening effort is carried out through strategic investments in the modernization of operational vehicles tailored to customer needs. This step demonstrates the Company's dedication to enhancing the efficiency of logistics services and supporting sustainable growth.

### Challenges in Sustainability Policy

The primary challenges faced by the Company are managing the environmental impact of logistics operations and the limitations in implementing low-emission vehicle technology (such as electric vehicles) due to the currently prohibitive costs. As a compensatory measure, the Company undertakes reforestation initiatives and continuously improves operational efficiency.
