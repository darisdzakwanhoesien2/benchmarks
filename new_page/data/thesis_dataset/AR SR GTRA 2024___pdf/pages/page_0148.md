PT Grahaprima Suksesmandiri secara konsisten melaksanakan program Defensive Driving Training (DDT) sebagai wujud komitmen perusahaan terhadap keselamatan. Program ini dirancang untuk meningkatkan keterampilan dan kesadaran keselamatan para pengemudi operasional, membekali mereka dengan teknik berkendara yang aman dan efisien, serta kemampuan mengantisipasi risiko di jalan. Melalui DDT, perusahaan menanamkan budaya disiplin dan tanggung jawab dalam berkendara, dengan tujuan utama meminimalkan kecelakaan dan menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang aman bagi karyawan dan masyarakat.

PT Grahaprima Suksesmandiri consistently implements the Defensive Driving Training (DDT) program as a form of the company's commitment to safety. The program is designed to improve the skills and safety awareness of operational drivers, equipping them with safe and efficient driving techniques, as well as the ability to anticipate risks on the road. Through DDT, the company instills a culture of discipline and responsibility in driving, with the main objective of minimizing accidents and creating a safe working environment for employees and the community.
