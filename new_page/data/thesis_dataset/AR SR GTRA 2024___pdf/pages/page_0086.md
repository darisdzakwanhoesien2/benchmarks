# Prosedur Pelaksanaan Penilaian Kinerja

Hasil evaluasi kinerja ini digunakan sebagai acuan dalam menentukan kompensasi bagi Dewan Komisaris, serta menjadi bahan pertimbangan bagi Pemegang Saham dalam proses pemilihan ulang atau penggantian anggota Dewan Komisaris. Prosedur Pelaksanaan Penilaian Kinerja Hasil evaluasi kinerja Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris disampaikan kepada Pemegang Saham dalam Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS) dan ditinjau berdasarkan indikator yang relevan. Indikator dasar penilaian kinerja Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi meliputi:

1. Pelaksanaan tugas dan fungsi pengawasan dan pengelolaan seperti diatur dalam Anggaran Dasar;
2. Tingkat kepatuhan terhadap peraturan yang berlaku;
3. Tingkat kehadiran dalam rapat;
4. Keikutsertaan dalam penugasan khusus.

# Penilaian (Self Assessment) Terhadap Kinerja Dewan Komisaris

Evaluasi kinerja Dewan Komisaris didasarkan pada pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya, termasuk pengawasan terhadap kebijakan Perseroan, pemberian nasihat dan rekomendasi kepada Direksi demi kepentingan Perseroan, serta pelaksanaan tugas khusus sesuai dengan Anggaran Dasar dan peraturan Pasar Modal. Pemegang Saham juga berperan dalam menilai pencapaian Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi melalui RUPST. Proses penilaian ini mencerminkan pentingnya transparansi, akuntabilitas, dan keterlibatan Pemangku Kepentingan dalam menjaga kualitas Tata Kelola Perusahaan secara menyeluruh.

# Komite di Bawah Dewan Komisaris

Untuk membantu Dewan Komisaris dalam menjalankan fungsi audit dan remunerasi, Perseroan membentuk Komite Audit dan Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi. Kedua komite ini bertanggung jawab kepada Dewan Komisaris. Sepanjang tahun 2024, Komite Audit dan Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi telah melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya dengan baik.

# Prosedur dan Dasar Penetapan Remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi

Remunerasi bagi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi ditetapkan melalui prosedur sebagai berikut:

1. Berdasarkan permintaan Dewan Komisaris, Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi menyampaikan usulan besaran gaji dan tunjangan untuk anggota baru atau calon anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi kepada Dewan Komisaris.
2. Setelah mendapatkan persetujuan dari RUPST, Dewan Komisaris menerbitkan surat keputusan mengenai besaran gaji dan tunjangan untuk anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, serta memberikan persetujuan atas surat usulan dari Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi.

# Performance Assessment Procedures

The results of this performance evaluation serve as a reference for determining compensation for the Board of Commissioners and as a consideration for Shareholders in the re-election or replacement process of Board of Commissioners members. The performance evaluation results of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners are presented to the Shareholders at the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders (AGMS) and reviewed based on relevant indicators. The basic performance assessment indicators for the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors including:

1. Implementation of supervisory and management duties and functions as stipulated in the Articles of Association;
2. Level of compliance with applicable regulations;
3. Attendance rate at meetings;
4. Participation in special assignments.

# Self-Assessment of the Board of Commissioners Performance

The performance evaluation of the Board of Commissioners is based on the execution of their duties and responsibilities, including oversight of the Company's policies, providing advice and recommendations to the Board of Directors for the benefit of the Company, and carrying out special assignments in accordance with the Articles of Association and Capital Market regulations. Shareholders also play a role in assessing the achievements of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors through the AGMS. This assessment process reflects the importance of transparency, accountability, and Stakeholder involvement in maintaining the quality of Corporate Governance as a whole.

# Committees Under the Board of Commissioners

To assist the Board of Commissioners in carrying out audit and remuneration functions, the Company has established an Audit Committee and a Nomination and Remuneration Committee. Both committees are accountable to the Board of Commissioners. Throughout 2024, the Audit Committee and the Nomination and Remuneration Committee have performed their duties and responsibilities effectively.

# Procedures and Basis for Determining Remuneration of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors

Remuneration for members of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors is determined through the following procedures:

1. Upon request from the Board of Commissioners, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee submits a proposal for the amount of salaries and benefits for new or prospective members of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors to the Board of Commissioners.
2. After obtaining approval from the AGMS, the Board of Commissioners issues a decree regarding the amount of salaries and benefits for members of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors and grants approval for the proposal letter from the Nomination and Remuneration Committee.
