Aspek Ekonomi

Perseroan terus melakukan investasi strategis dalam peningkatan kapasitas dan kualitas armada sebagai upaya utama untuk mendukung efisiensi distribusi, menjaga ketepatan waktu pengiriman, dan merespons dinamika kebutuhan pelanggan. Modernisasi armada tidak hanya difokuskan pada jumlah kendaraan, tetapi juga pada sistem pemeliharaan rutin dan pemantauan berbasis teknologi untuk memastikan performa optimal dalam setiap proses logistik. Strategi ini turut memperkuat daya saing Perseroan dalam menjalin hubungan jangka panjang dengan perusahaan besar, serta memastikan keberlanjutan operasional di tengah kompetisi industri yang semakin ketat.

Aspek Sosial

Perseroan menunjukkan komitmen kuat terhadap tanggung jawab sosial melalui pelaksanaan berbagai program CSR yang mendukung pencapaian Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan (TPB), khususnya TPB 1 (Tanpa Kemiskinan), TPB 4 (Pendidikan Berkualitas), TPB 5 (Kesetaraan Gender), dan TPB 10 (Mengurangi Ketimpangan). Salah satu program yang pernah dijalankan adalah dukungan terhadap rumah singgah anak jalanan, yang menyediakan ruang aman dan akses pembelajaran bagi anak-anak rentan secara sosial.

Selain itu, aspek ketenagakerjaan dikelola secara adil dan inklusif. Perseroan mencatat tingkat turnover yang rendah dan proporsi karyawan perempuan mencapai 70%, mencerminkan praktik ketenagakerjaan yang mendukung kesetaraan gender. Tidak ada praktik diskriminatif, kerja paksa, maupun pekerja anak. Seluruh karyawan menerima upah sesuai atau di atas UMR dan telah terdaftar dalam program jaminan sosial (BPJS). Budaya kerja Perseroan menekankan nilai integritas dan disiplin, didukung oleh sistem pengawasan internal serta pelatihan dan pengembangan SDM yang berkelanjutan.

Aspek Lingkungan

Sebagai bagian dari komitmen terhadap lingkungan, Perseroan melaksanakan program penanaman mangrove di kawasan pesisir untuk mendukung pelestarian ekosistem, mitigasi perubahan iklim, serta perlindungan terhadap wilayah pesisir. Inisiatif ini secara langsung berkontribusi terhadap TPB 13 (Penanganan Perubahan Iklim) dan TPB 14 (Ekosistem Lautan).

Selain itu, pengelolaan operasional juga diarahkan pada pengurangan emisi dan efisiensi energi, seperti melalui perawatan berkala kendaraan setiap 10.000 km dan pelaksanaan uji KIR setiap 6 bulan. Perseroan juga memberikan pelatihan safety driving, menerapkan titik istirahat wajib untuk pengemudi setiap 4 jam, serta menetapkan jalur operasi yang aman guna menekan risiko kecelakaan dan dampak lingkungan.

Aspek Tata Kelola

Perseroan berkomitmen untuk menerapkan prinsip Good Corporate Governance (GCG) secara konsisten dalam seluruh lini bisnis guna memastikan transparansi, akuntabilitas, dan kepatuhan terhadap regulasi yang berlaku. Praktik tata kelola ini dijalankan melalui penguatan fungsi pengawasan internal, termasuk pelaksanaan audit rutin dan audit mendadak oleh tim internal audit yang independen.

Salah satu nilai utama yang dipegang teguh adalah integritas, yang dijadikan standar dalam proses rekrutmen, evaluasi kinerja, dan pengambilan keputusan. Perseroan memiliki kebijakan toleransi nol terhadap praktik kecurangan (fraud), serta menegakkan disiplin kerja melalui pemberian peringatan tertulis bagi pelanggaran etika atau SOP.

Economic Aspect

The Company continues to make strategic investments in enhancing the capacity and quality of its fleet as a primary effort to support distribution efficiency, maintain delivery timeliness, and respond to the dynamic needs of customers. Fleet modernization is not only focused on the number of vehicles but also on routine maintenance systems and technology-based monitoring to ensure optimal performance in every logistics process. This strategy further strengthens the Company's competitiveness in establishing long-term relationships with major corporations and ensures operational sustainability amidst increasingly intense industry competition.

Social Aspect

The Company demonstrates a strong commitment to social responsibility through the implementation of various CSR programs that support the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), particularly SDG 1 (No Poverty), SDG 4 (Quality Education), SDG 5 (Gender Equality), and SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities). One of the programs implemented was support for shelters for street children, providing safe spaces and access to learning for socially vulnerable children.

Furthermore, labor aspects are managed fairly and inclusively. The Company records a low employee turnover rate, and the proportion of female employees reaches 70%, reflecting labor practices that support gender equality. There are no discriminatory practices, forced labor, or child labor. All employees receive wages at or above the regional minimum wage and are enrolled in the social security program (BPJS). The Company's work culture emphasizes the values of integrity and discipline, supported by an internal control system and continuous human resource training and development.

Environmental Aspect

As part of its commitment to the environment, the Company implements a mangrove planting program in coastal areas to support ecosystem preservation, climate change mitigation, and the protection of coastal regions. This initiative directly contributes to SDG 13 (Climate Action) and SDG 14 (Life Below Water).

In addition, operational management is also directed towards emission reduction and energy efficiency, such as through regular vehicle maintenance every 10,000 kilometers and the mandatory vehicle safety inspection (KIR) every 6 months. The Company also provides safety driving training, implements mandatory rest stops for drivers every 4 hours, and establishes safe operating routes to minimize the risk of accidents and environmental impact.

Corporate Governance Aspects

The Company is committed to consistently implementing the principles of Good Corporate Governance (GCG) across all business lines to ensure transparency, accountability, and compliance with prevailing regulations. These governance practices are executed through the reinforcement of the internal oversight function, including the execution of routine and ad-hoc audits by an independent internal audit team.

A core value that is firmly upheld is integrity, which serves as the standard in recruitment processes, performance evaluations, and decision-making. The Company maintains a zero-tolerance policy towards fraudulent practices and enforces work discipline through the issuance of written warnings for breaches of ethics or Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
