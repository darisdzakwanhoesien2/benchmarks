# Tugas, Wewenang, dan Tanggung Jawab Dewan Komisaris

Berdasarkan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan, Dewan Komisaris memiliki tugas, wewenang, dan tanggung jawab yang meliputi:

1. Pengawasan dan Nasihat: Dewan Komisaris bertugas mengawasi kebijakan pengurusan dan jalannya pengurusan Perseroan secara umum, baik yang berkaitan dengan Perseroan maupun usaha Perseroan. Selain itu, Dewan Komisaris juga memberikan nasihat kepada Direksi dalam menjalankan kegiatan operasional Perseroan. Dalam melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya, Dewan Komisaris wajib bertindak dengan itikad baik, penuh tanggung jawab, dan kehat-hatian.
2. Penyelenggaraan RUPS: Dewan Komisaris berwenang untuk menyelenggarakan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS) sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku.
3. Pembentukan Komite: Guna mendukung efektivitas pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya, Dewan Komisaris wajib membentuk Komite Audit, Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi, serta komite lain yang dipersyaratkan oleh peraturan perundang-undangan di bidang pasar modal.
4. Fungsi Nominasi dan Remunerasi: Dewan Komisaris bertanggung jawab untuk melaksanakan fungsi nominasi dan remunerasi bagi anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris.
5. Evaluasi Kinerja Komite: Pada setiap akhir tahun buku, Dewan Komisaris melakukan evaluasi terhadap kinerja komite-komite yang dibentuk untuk membantu pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya.

# Piagam Dewan Komisaris

Dewan Komisaris menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya berdasarkan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan yang telah disahkan melalui Akta No. 31 Tahun 2023. Penyusunan Anggaran Dasar ini berlandaskan pada prinsip-prinsip Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik (Good Corporate Governance/GCG) yang berlaku umum, peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku, serta Anggaran Dasar Perseroan itu sendiri.

Duties, Authorities, and Responsibilities of the Board of Commissioners

Based on the Company's Articles of Association, the Board of Commissioners has the duties, authorities, and responsibilities that include:

1. Oversight and Advice: The Board of Commissioners is responsible for overseeing themanagement policies and the conduct of the Company's management in general, both related to the Company and the Company's business. In addition, the Board of Commissioners also provides advice to the Board of Directors in carrying out the Company's operational activities. In carrying out its duties and responsibilities, the Board of Commissioners is obligated to act in good faith, with full responsibility, and prudence.
2. Organization of GMS: The Board of Commissioners is authorized to hold the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) in accordance with applicable regulations.
3. Formation of Committees: To support the effectiveness of carrying out its duties and responsibilities, the Board of Commissioners is required to form an Audit Committee, a Remuneration and Nomination Committee, as well as other committees required by the prevailing laws and regulations in the capital market sector.
4. Nomination and Remuneration Functions: The Board of Commissioners is responsible for carrying out the nomination and remuneration functions for members of the Board of Director and Board of Commissioners.
5. Committee Performance Evaluation: At the end of each fiscal year, the Board of Commissioners conducts an evaluation of the performance of the committees formed to assist in carrying out their duties and responsibilities.

# Board of Commissioners Charter

The Board of Commissioners carries out its duties and responsibilities based on the Company's Articles of Association which were ratified through Deed No. 31 of 2023. The drafting of these Articles of Association is based on the principles of Good Corporate Governance (GCG) that are generally applicable, prevailing laws and regulations, as well as the Company's Articles of Association themselves.
