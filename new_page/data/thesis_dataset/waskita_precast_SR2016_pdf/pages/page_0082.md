Adapun rincian penyaluran dana CRS pada tahun 2016, yang dimulai sejak Januari-31 Desember, selengkapnya sebagai berikut:

a. Program Pendidikan
1. Pembangunan kembali ruang kelas pasca kebakaran
2. Bantuan dana kelengkapan sarana SLB Kusumo Asih Jakarta Timur
3. Sosialisasi peraturan pemerintah No. 2 Tahun 2016 tentang tata cara penyampaian data informasi oleh instansi pemerintah dan/atau lembaga swasta dalam pencegahan dan pemberantasan tindak pidana pencucian uang.
4. Pembangunan sekolah SMA 81

b. Program Sosial
1. Wakaf 1000 Al-quran
2. Pembangunan Asrama &amp; MCK Yatim Piatu &amp; Dhuafa Banten
3. Pembangunan dan Renovasi Panti Asuhan

6. Pembangunan/ Renovasi rumah ibadah
7. Bantuan bencana alam

c. Program Kesehatan
1. Khitanan massal
2. Donor darah

The details of the disbursement of CSR funds in 2016, which began in January-December 31, are as follows:

a. Education program
1. The reconstruction of post-fire classrooms
2. Financial aid for facilities of Kusumo Asih Special School, East Jakarta
3. Socialization of government regulation No. 2 of 2016 regarding the procedure of delivering data and information by government agencies and/or private institutions in the prevention and combating of money laundering.
4. Construction of 81 Senior High School

b. Social program
1. Endowments of 1000 Al Quran
2. Construction of Boarding School with Public Bathing, Washing, and Toilet Facilities for Orphans &amp; Dhuafa at Banten
3. Construction and renovation of orphanages
4. Distribution of sacrificial animal of Eid al-Adha
5. Social events, distribution of nine basic foods, and donation for education
6. Construction / renovation of worship places
7. Donation for natural disaster

c. Health program
1. Mass circumcision
2. Blood donor
