Dengan adanya Peraturan Pemerintah (PP) No. 50 Tahun 2012 tentang Penerapan SMK3 (Sistem Manajemen Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja) yang merupakan peningkatan dari Sistem SMK3 yang ada sebelumnya, Perseroan telah juga menerapkan PP tersebut melalui audit oleh badan sertifikasi PT. Sucofindo. Selain memenuhi ketentuan Peraturan Pemerintah No. 50 Tahun 2012 seperti yang tersebut di atas, Perseroan juga menerapkan OHSAS 18001:2007.

Untuk memenuhi Standar Pelaksanaan K3 Perseroan mewajibkan kepada seluruh petugas yang bertanggung jawab atas K3 Perseroan, memiliki sertifikat ahli K3 Umum. Proses sertifikasi bekerjasama dengan Lembaga Sertifikasi Ahli K3 yang terdaftar di Kementrian Ketenagakerjaan dan Transmigrasi. Program sertifikasi ini sangat penting untuk menciptakan budaya "zero accident" di Perseroan.

Upaya menuju zero accident merupakan komitmen yang tidak bisa ditawar-tawar. Walau begitu, dalam proses untuk menuju angka kecelakaan nol tersebut, selama tahun 2016, tercatat ada kecelakaan kerja sebagai berikut: G4-LA6

Selain melakukan investigasi tentang penyebab terjadinya insiden kerja, Perseroan juga mencatat ihwal pekerja yang sering terkena dampak atau berisiko tinggi terkena penyakit yang terkait dengan pekerjaannya. Data Perseroan menunjukkan bahwa pekerja di bagian produksi tercatat paling rentan terkena penyakit atau berisiko tinggi terkena penyakit akibat pekerjaan yang digelutinya. G4-LA7

Untuk meminimalkan insiden kecelakaan kerja, sosialisasi ihwal pentingnya keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja terus dilakukan oleh Perseroan. Apalagi, hal itu sudah diatur dalam Peraturan Perusahaan PT. Waskita Beton Precast, yang sudah disahkan melalui Surat Keputusan Direktur Jenderal Pembinaan Hubungan Industrial dan Jaminan Sosial Tenaga Kerja Nomer Kep.587/PHUSK-PKKAD/PP/V/2015 tanggal 19 Mei 2015.

Ketentuan tentang Keselamatan &amp; Kesehatan Kerja, Lingkungan, Mutu dan Pengamanan (K3LMP) diatur dalam Bab VI, pasal 35. Di dalamnya, antara lain, mengatur tentang kewajiban Perusahaan menyediakan alat-alat keselamatan &amp; kesehatan kerja dan lingkungan (K3LMP), dan pegawai wajib memakai dan memelihara alat-alat K3LMP yang disediakan oleh Perusahaan. With the issuance of Government Regulation (PP) No. 50 Year 2012 on Implementation of SMK3 (Management System of Occupational Health and Safety), which is an improvement from previous existing SMK3 System, the Company has also implemented the regulation through audit by certification bodies PT. Sucofindo, in addition to fulfilling the provisions of Government Regulation No. 50 Year 2012, as mentioned above the Company also implemented the OHSAS 18001:2007.

To meet the Standards of K3 Implementation, the Company requires that all officers responsible for the Company K3, have certificates General K3 Expert. The certification process is in collaboration with the Institute of Certification of K3 Expert that are registered in the Ministry of Manpower and Transmigration. This certification program is intended to create a culture of "zero accident" in the Company.

Efforts towards zero accident are a commitment that cannot be bargained. Even so, in the process towards the zero accident rate, during 2016, the following work accidents were recorded: G4-LA6

In addition to conducting an investigation on causes of workplace incidents, the Company also recorded particular workers who are affected or at high risk of disease associated with their job. Company data shows that employees in the production are recorded most often affected by the disease or at high risk of disease due to the work that they do. G4-LA7

To minimize the incidence of work-related accidents, socialization regarding the importance of occupational health and safety continue to be made by the Company. Moreover, it is set in the Company Regulations of PT. Waskita Beton Precast, which has been approved by the Decree of the Director General of Industrial Relations and Labor Social Security Number Kep.587/PHUSK-PKKAD/PP/V/2015 dated May 19, 2015.

The provisions on Health &amp; Safety, Environment, Quality and Security (K3LMP) are set out in Chapter VI, Article 35. Among other things, it regulates the liability of the Company to provide tools of occupational and environmental safety and health (K3LMP), and employees shall wear and take care of K3LMP tools provided by the Company. In addition, there are also provisions that employees must take an active part in prevention and mitigation of accidents/fires, environmental pollution and safety in the work environment.
