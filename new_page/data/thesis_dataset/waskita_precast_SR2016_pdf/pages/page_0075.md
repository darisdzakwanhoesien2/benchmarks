LAPORAN KABULAN

Tahun 2016 merupakan tahun yang sangat menjanjikan bagi pelaku industri yang bergerak di bidang infrastruktur, termasuk PT. Waskita Beton Precast. Hal itu tidak terlepas dari semangat pemerintah Indonesia untuk melakukan percepatan pembangunan infrastruktur. Dalam hal ini, pemerintah meyakini bahwa pembangunan infrastruktur yang lebih merata di Tanah Air akan memperkuat konektivitas antar wilayah, yang pada gilirannya memperkecil kesenjangan dan ketimpangan sosial.

Ada beragam infrastruktur yang dibangun pemerintah. Untuk infrastruktur logistik meliputi pembangunan jalan, jalan bebas hambatan (tol), pelabuhan, bandara dan rel kereta api. Sedangkan infrastruktur strategis, pembangunan yang dikebut pemerintah adalah pembangkit listrik, telekomunikasi, irigasi dan perumahan rakyat.

Sekadar gambaran, dalam dua tahun terakhir (2014-2015), pemerintah telah mempercepat pembangunan jalan nasional sepanjang 2.225 kilometer, jalan tol sepanjang 132 kilometer, dan jembatan sepanjang 16.246 meter, atau sebanyak 160 jembatan. Sementara itu, pada tahun 2016, target pembangunan jalan nasional sepanjang 703 km dan jembatan sepanjang lebih dari 8.452 meter.

Khusus pembangunan jalur kereta api, pemerintah tidak hanya berfokus di pulau Jawa, tapi juga membangun di wilayah lain, seperti di Pulau Sumatra, Kalimantan, dan Sulawesi. Saat ini, jalur kereta api yang beroperasi telah mencapai sepanjang 5.200 Kilometer Spoor (Km'sp). Pada tahun 2015, tercatat 179,33 Km'sp telah selesai dibangun, dan 271,5 Km'sp sedang dalam proses pembangunan. Tak hanya rel kereta api yang dikebut pembangunannya, pemerintah juga bersemangat mewujudkan kereta untuk transportasi perkotaan, seperti Mass Rapid Transportation (MRT), Light Rail Train (LRT), serta commuter line.

Sulit dimungkiri bahwa geliat pembangunan infrastruktur tersebut membawa angin segar bagi Waskita Beton Precast. Kontrak demi kontrak pembangunan infrastruktur di berbagai belahan bumi Indonesia diraih. Pada kurun waktu Januari-Desember 2016 misalnhya, Perseroan telah mengantongi kontrak baru senilai Rp12,226 triliun. Jumlah itu sudah melewati target kontrak baru pada tahun 2016 sebesar Rp8 triliun. Kontrak baru yang didapat Perseroan, antara lain, untuk sejumlah proyek jalan tol, seperti ruas Cimanggis-Cibitung senilai Rp2,3 triliun, Pemalang-Batang Rp500 miliar, Batang-Semarang Rp1,4 triliun. Bogor-Ciawi-Sukabumi seksi 2, 3 dan 4 sebesar Rp770 miliar, Manado-Bitung Rp200 miliar dan sebagainya.

2016 was a very promising year for players in the infrastructure industry, including PT. Waskita Beton Precast. This was not separated from the spirit of the Indonesian government to accelerate the infrastructure development. In this case, the government believes that evenly infrastructure development is throughout the country will strengthen inter-regional connectivity, which in turn reducing disparities and social inequalities.

There are a variety of infrastructures built by the government. Logistical infrastructures include construction of roads, highways (toll road), ports, airports, and railways. While strategic infrastructures that were accelerated by the government is construction of power plants, telecommunication, irrigation, and housing.

As an illustration, in the last two years (2014-2015), the government has accelerated the construction of 2,225 kilometers of national roads, 132 kilometers of toll roads, 6,246 meters bridges or as many as 160 bridges. Meanwhile, in 2016, the construction target for national road was 703 km and for bridges was more than 8,452 meters.

Particularly for the construction of railways, the government was not only focused on Java island, but also in other areas, such as on Sumatra, Kalimantan, and Sulawesi Island. Currently, the railway that operates have reached 5,200 kilometer spoor (Km'sp). In 2015, construction of 179,33 Km'sp have been completed, and 271,5 Km'sp are in the process of development. The acceleration is not only applied on the railways construction, the government is also eager to embody the trains for urban transport, such as Mass Rapid Transportation (MRT), Light Rail Train (LRT), and commuter line.

It is undeniable that the rampant infrastructure development has brought a very refreshing air for Waskita Beton Precast. Contracts after contracts of infrastructure construction in various parts of Indonesia were obtained. In the period from January to December 2016, for example, the Company got new contracts with contract value of Rp 12.226 trillion. This figure had already surpassed the target of new contracts in 2016 amounting to Rp 8 trillion. The new contracts include several toll road projects, such as Cimanggis-Cibitung with contract value of Rp2.3 trillion, Pemalang-Batang of Rp500 billion, Batang-Semarang of Rp1.4 trillion. Bogor-Ciawi-Sukabumi section 2, 3 and 4 of Rp770 billion, Manado-Bitung of Rp200 billion, and so on.
