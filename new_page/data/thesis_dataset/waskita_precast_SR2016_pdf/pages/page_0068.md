## LARANGAN:

Bagi pegawai Waskita Beton Precast berlaku larangan sebagaimana diatur dalam Peraturan Perusahaan yang masih berlaku.

- Penerapan dan Pelanggaran Etika (Whistle Blower Policy)

Penerapan etika di lingkungan PT. Waskita Beton Precast sangat dipengaruhi oleh:

1. Komitmen pimpinan, di kalangan Komisaris, Direksi, Manajemen, maupun kelompok kerja pegawai.
2. Penggerak penerapan etika dan pengembangannya oleh Bagian Bagian SDM &amp; Umum
3. Sosialisasi kepada setiap lapisan pegawai.

4. Employees shall hold secrets related to his/her position, i.e. secrets related to his/her duties and/or position, either in the form of written documents, sound recordings, or oral instruction/ statement from superiors.

Employees with superiors and subordinates in the Company:

1. Employees shall foster positive and productive cooperation, mutual acceptance and respect based on sincerity and good faith.
2. Superiors as role model, guide, mentor and are responsible for the behavior and performance of subordinates within the company.
3. Any employee who became a superior shall foster and provide an example to the employees in their environment.
4. Employees are required to work extra hours on the orders of authorized superiors, if the work must be done outside the Company's working hours.
5. Employees must report to their superiors any event of theft or loss of property owned by the Company no later than 1 x 24 hours.

Employees with fellow employee

1. Show mutual respect, encourage, and foster cooperation in each tasks and responsibilities.
2. Improve the integrity, openness, and a harmonious relationship.

## PROHIBITION:

For employee of Waskita Beton Precast applies the prohibition as stipulated in the existing Company Regulation.

- Implementation and Ethical Violations (Whistle Blower Policy)

Implementation of ethics within PT. Waskita Beton Precast is greatly influenced by:

1. Commitment of the leaders, among Commissioners, Directors, Management, as well as employee's working unit.
2. The driving force of the implementation and development of ethics is the Human Resources &amp; General Affairs Department.
3. Socialization to every employee at all level.
