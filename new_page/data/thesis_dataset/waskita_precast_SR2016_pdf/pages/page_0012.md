## Tumbuh Pesat

Tahun 2016 merupakan tahun yang penuh tantangan. Sejalan dengan masih melambatnya pertumbuhan ekonomi dunia, pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia pun masih kurang menggembirakan. Pertumbuhan ekonomi pada tahun 2016 tidak mampu memenuhi target sebesar 5,5-5,6%, seperti yang disampaikan pada awal tahun 2016. Badan Pusat Statistik (BPS) menyatakan, pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia pada tahun 2016 hanya mencapai angka 5,02%. Walau tak mencapai target, pencapaian sebesar 5% tetap patut disyukuri. Sebab, angka itu sudah melampaui angka perkiraan IMF, yakni 4,9%.

Kami patut bersyukur bahwa di tengah kondisi perekonomian global dan nasional yang kurang menggembirakan itu, kinerja Waskita Beton Precast justru sangat menggembirakan. Hal itu tak lepas dari semangat pemerintah Indonesia untuk mempercepat pembangunan infrastruktur dalam berbagai bentuk, seperti pembangunan jalan, jalan bebas hambatan, pelabuhan, bandara dan rel kereta api.

Sepanjang Januari-Desember 2016, Waskita Beton Precast telah mengantongi kontrak baru senilai Rp 12,226 triliun. Angka itu melewati target kontrak baru pada tahun 2016 sebesar Rp8 triliun.

## Grow Rapidly

2016 was a year full of challenges. In line with the world economic growth that are still experiencing a slowdown, Indonesia's economic growth was also less encouraging. Economic growth in 2016 was not able to meet its target of 5.5-5.6%, as stated at the beginning of 2016. The Central Statistics Agency (BPS) stated that Indonesia's economic growth in 2016 only reached 5.02%. Although not reaching the target, the achievement of 5% is something that we need to be grateful of because that figure already exceeded the IMF forecast, i.e. 4.9%.

We should be grateful that in the midst of less favorable global and national economic conditions, Waskita Beton Precast was able to show a very encouraging performance. This achievement was inseparable from the spirit of the Indonesian government to accelerate infrastructure development in various forms, such as the construction of roads, toll roads, ports, airports and railways.

The acceleration and equitable development of infrastructure by the government was a blessing for Waskita Beton Precast. Contracts by contracts for infrastructure development were achieved by the Company. From January to December 2016, Waskita Precast Concrete has pocketed new contracts worth Rp 12.226 trillion. That figure exceeded the set new contract target for 2016 amounting to Rp 8 trillion.

In addition to the achievement of contract values that were exceeded the target, the Company's positive performance was also demonstrated by great increase in a number of indicators. For example Operating Revenues. As of December 31, 2016 it was amounted to Rp 4.72 trillion, or reaching 178.39% compared to 2015 amounting to Rp2,64 trillion. Accordingly, the Company's profit also grew significantly. As of December 31, 2016, Net Profit was recorded at Rp634.82 billion, or 189.86% compared to 2015 amounting to Rp334.37 billion.
