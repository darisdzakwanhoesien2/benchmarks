# Aspek Material dan Boundary Teridentifikasi

Sebelum menentukan aspek-aspek yang material, dari 42 aspek yang ada dalam "Lembar Uji Materialitas" terlebih dahulu dilakukan formulasi ulang. Pertimbangannya, ada beberapa indikator yang bisa dikelompokkan menjadi satu. Dari formulasi ulang itu, kemudian didapat 16 aspek untuk diuji dan diolah.

Hasil uji materialitas menunjukkan ada 10 dari 16 aspek yang dinilai material/kategori penting dan sangat penting untuk disampaikan melalui Laporan Keberlanjutan ini. Dari sisi boundary (batasan), dari antara 10 aspek itu ada yang dampaknya terjadi di dalam organisasi, di luar organisasi, maupun terjadi di dalam maupun di luar organisasi.

## Material Aspects and Identified Boundary

Prior to determining the material aspects, we performed re-formulation of the 42 aspects of the "Materiality Test Sheet". This was done based on the consideration that there are some indicators that can be grouped into one. From the reformulation, there were 16 aspects to be tested and analyzed.

Materiality test results showed that there were 10 out of 16 aspects, which are considered material and very important to be delivered through this Sustainability Report. In terms of boundary (limit), of the 10 aspects, the impacts are occurring inside the organization, outside the organization, as well as inside and outside the organization.
