# Penerapan Prinsip Kehati-hatian G4-14
The Prudence Principle G4-14

Dalam menjalankan roda organisasi, Perseroan berkomitmen untuk menerapkan prinsip kehati-hatian guna menghindarkan dari berbagai risiko. Hal itu dilakukan antara lain dengan mengaktifkan kerja Unit Audit Internal, Komite Audit, Komite Risiko dan Asuransi. Selain itu, Perseroan juga mengadopsi standar international dalam sistem manajemennya dengan menerapkan, SMK3 (K3), dan ISO 9001:2008 (MUTU).

In running the organization, the Company is committed to apply the prudence principle in order to avoid risks. This is done partly by activating the Internal Audit Unit, Audit Committee, Risk and Insurance Committee. In addition, the Company also participated in various international certification standards. For example, applying the OHSAS 18001: 2007 (K3), SMK3 (K3), ISO 14001: 2004 (Environment), and ISO 9001: 2008 (Quality).

# Keanggotaan dalam Asosiasi G4-16
Membership in the Association G4-16

Sepanjang tahun 2016, Perseroan sudah bergabung dengan asosiasi yang memiliki kesamaan usaha utama, yakni industri manufaktur beton precast dan ready mix. Di antaranya AP3I (Asosiasi Perusahaan Pracetak Prategang Indonesia)

Throughout 2016, the Company had joined with associations that have the same main business, namely the precast and ready mix concrete manufacturing industry. Among others are AP3I (Indonesian Association of Precast Company), and IAPPI (Indonesian Association of Prestressed Precast)
