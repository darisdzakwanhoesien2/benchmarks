## STRUKTUR TATA KELOLA G4-34

Perseroan memiliki struktur tata kelola yang terdiri atas Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham, Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, disertai dengan organ-orang pendukung, seperti Komite Risiko dan Asuransi, serta Komite Audit.

- **Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham**

Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham, yang selanjutnya disebut RUPS, adalah Organ Perseroan yang mempunyai wewenang yang tidak diberikan kepada Direksi atau Dewan Komisaris dalam batas yang ditentukan dalam Undang-Undang ini dan/atau Anggaran Dasar.

Sesuai Anggaran Dasar Perseroan, RUPS terdiri atas RUPS Tahunan dan RUPS lainnya (RUPS Luar Biasa). RUPS tahunan wajib diselenggarakan dalam jangka waktu paling lambat 6 (enam) bulan setelah tahun buku berakhir, sedangkan RUPS lainnya dapat diselenggarakan pada setiap waktu berdasarkan kebutuhan untuk kepentingan Perseroan.

Penyelenggaraan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Tahunan dan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham lainnya diadakan Direksi atas permintaan Dewan Komisaris Perseroan, atau atas permintaan pemegang saham. Dalam RUPS Tahunan, Direksi menyampaikan:

a. Laporan Tahunan sebagaimana dimaksud pada Pasal 21 ayat 3 Anggaran Dasar ini.
b. Usulan penggunaan Laba Perseroan jika Perseroan mempunyai saldo laba yang positif;
c. Usulan Penunjukan Akuntan Publik yang terdaftar di OJK.

Selama tahun 2016, Perseroan telah menyelenggarakan RUPS Tahunan pada 14 Maret 2016 di Jakarta. Sedangkan RUPS Luar Biasa telah diselenggarakan pada 8 Juni 2016 di Jakarta. Hasil rapat antara lain menyetujui perubahan anggaran dasar dalam rangka menjadi Perseroan Tertutup menjadi Perseroan Terbuka, serta menetapkan susunan Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi.

- **Direksi**

Direksi adalah Organ Perseroan yang berwenang dan bertanggung jawab penuh atas pengurusan Perseroan untuk kepentingan Perseroan, sesuai dengan maksud dan tujuan Perseroan serta mewakili Perseroan, baik di dalam maupun di luar pengadilan sesuai dengan ketentuan anggaran dasar. ## GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE G4-34

The Company has a governance structure that is composed of the General Meeting of Shareholders, the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors, accompanied by the supporting organs, such as the Risk and Insurance Committee, and the Audit Committee.

- **General Meeting of Shareholders**

General Meeting of Shareholders, hereinafter called the GMS, is the Company's organ that has authority not granted to the Board of Directors or Board of Commissioners within the limits prescribed in this Act and/or the Articles of Association.

In accordance with the Company's Articles of Association, GMS consists of Annual GMS and other GMS (Extraordinary GMS). Annual GMS shall be held within a maximum period of 6 (six) months after the end of the financial year, while the other AGM can be held at any time based on the need for the interest of the Company.

The Annual General Meeting of Shareholders and the other General Meeting of Shareholders are held by the Board of Directors at the request of the Board of Commissioners, or at the request of shareholders. In the Annual GMS, the Board of Directors present:

a. The Annual Report referred to Article 21 paragraph 3 of the Articles of Association.
b. The proposal for the use of Company's Profit Company if the Company has retained positive earnings;
c. The proposal for appointing Public Accountant whom is registered at the FSA.

During 2016, the Company has held the Annual GMS on March 14, 2016 in Jakarta, while the Extraordinary GMS was held on June 8, 2016 in Jakarta. Results of the meeting among others were to approve alterations in the articles of association in order to change from closed company into publicly listed company, as well as establish the composition of the Boards of Commissioners and Board of Directors.

- **The Board of Directors**

The Board of Directors is the Company's organ that is authorized and fully responsible for management of the Company for the benefit of the Company, pursuant to the Company's purposes and objectives and represents the Company, both in and out of court in accordance with the provisions of the articles of association.
