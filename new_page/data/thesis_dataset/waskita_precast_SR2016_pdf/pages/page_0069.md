## Sanksi atas pelanggaran Etika:

1. Setiap pegawai yang melakukan pelanggaran Prosedur Waskita Precast di Bidang Etika dan Perilaku akan diberi sanksi sesuai dengan berat/ ringan, sifat, dan seringnya pelanggaran dilakukan.
2. Prosedur pemberian sanksi sesuai dengan Peraturan Perusahaan PT. Waskita Beton Precast Tbk

## Keharusan pelaporan terhadap pelanggaran etika:

1. Setiap pegawai wajib melaporkan terjadinya pelanggaran Prosedur Waskita Precast di Bidang Etika dan Perilaku ke Bagian SDM &amp; Umum
2. Setiap pegawai yang melaporkan mengenai dugaan pelanggaran etika harus mengungkapkan identitasnya dengan jelas.
3. Kerahasiaan identitas pelapor harus dijaga, kecuali apabila diperlukan dalam tindak lanjut laporannya sesuai kebijakan perusahaan.
4. Tidak ada hukuman yang dijatuhkan kepada pelapor manakala pelanggaran tersebut benar terjadi, kecuali apabila yang bersangkutan juga terlibat dalam pelanggaran dan/atau laporannya tidak benar.
5. Bagian Bagian SDM &amp; Umum harus menindaklanjuti laporan tersebut sesuai batas kewenangannya.
6. Bagian SDM &amp; Umum harus melaporkan kepada Direksi untuk diambil tindakan sesuai Kebijakan Perusahaan.

Selama tahun pelaporan, tidak ada pelanggaran terhadap etika dan perilaku, baik di kalangan Komisaris, Direksi maupun pegawai.

## Kebijakan Gratifikasi

Perseroan berkomitmen untuk mewujudkan praktik tata kelola yang baik, termasuk bersih dari praktik pemberian gratifikasi atau uang hadiah kepada pegawai di luar gaji yang telah ditentukan. Gratifikasi dilarang sepenuhnya oleh Perseroan karena sangat potensial memicu terjadinya konflik kepentingan serta mengurangi obyektivitas dan profesionalitas insan Perseroan. Sosialisasi larangan tentang gratifikasi serta pentingnya integritas atau kejujuran terus dilakukan oleh Perseroan, termasuk mengenai sanksi yang akan dijatuhkan apabila ada yang terbukti menerima gratifikasi.

## Sanctions for violations of the Ethics:

1. Any employee who violates Waskita Precast Procedures in the field of Ethics and Conduct will be sanctioned in accordance with the weight, nature, and the frequency of the violation.
2. Procedure for imposing sanctions pursuant to Company Regulation of Waskita Beton Precast Tbk

## The obligation to report any violations of ethics:

1. Every employee is required to report violations of Waskita Precast Procedure in the field of Ethics and Conduct to Human Resources &amp; General Affairs Department
2. Any employee who reported the alleged violation of ethics must reveal his identity.
3. Confidentiality of the reporter's identity will be kept, except as necessary in follow-up report according to the Company policy.
4. There is no punishment that will be given to the complainant if the reported violation does occur, except if the person concerned is also involved in the offense and/or the report was not true.
5. Human Resources &amp; General Affairs shall follow up the report in accordance with their scope of authority.
6. Human Resources &amp; General Affairs Department shall report to the Board of Directors to be taken actions according to the Company policy.

During the reporting period, there was no violation of ethics and conduct, both among the Commissioners, Directors and employees.

## Gratification Policy

The Company is committed to embody the good corporate governance practices, including free from the practice of giving gratification or reward money to employees other than salary that has been determined. Gratification is banned completely by the Company because it would most likely trigger conflict of interest and reduce the objectivity and professionalism of the personnel of Company. Socialization on the prohibition of gratification as well as the importance of integrity or honesty continue to be executed by the Company, including the sanctions to be imposed if proven to accept gratification.
