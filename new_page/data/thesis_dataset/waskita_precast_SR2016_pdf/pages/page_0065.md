Dalam hubungannya dengan Direksi, Komisaris berkewajiban untuk:

## LARANGAN:

Komisaris dilarang melakukan transaksi yang mempunyai benturan kepentingan dan mengambil keuntungan pribadi baik secara langsung maupun tidak langsung dari PT. Waskita Beton Precast selain penghasilan yang sah.

### Etika dan Perilaku Direksi

1. Dalam hubungannya dengan penerapan prinsip-prinsip Good Corporate Governance (GCG), Direksi harus:
2. Memahami prinsip-prinsip GCG dan melaksanakannya sebagai contoh perilaku bagi karyawan.
3. Menghindari timbulnya benturan kepentingan baik secara langsung maupun tidak langsung.

Dalam hubungan dengan tugas pokok, maka Direksi berkewajiban:

1. Mengusahakan dan menjamin terlaksananya usaha dan kegiatan perusahaan sesuai dengan maksud dan tujuan serta kegiatan usahanya.
2. Menyiapkan pada waktunya Rencana Jangka Panjang Perusahaan, Rencana Kerja dan Anggaran Perusahaan, termasuk rencana-rencana lainnya yang berhubungan dengan pelaksanaan usaha dan kegiatan perusahaan serta menyampaikannya kepada Komisaris dan Pemegang Saham untuk mendapatkan pengesahan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham.
3. Memelihara risalah rapat serta menyelenggarakan pembukuan perusahaan sesuai dengan kelaziman yang berlaku bagi suatu perusahaan.

3. Report immediately to the General Meeting of Shareholders if there are symptoms of declining performance of the Company.

In conjunction with the Board of Directors, Commissioners is obligated to:

1. Review and analyze the periodic reports and annual reports that have been prepared by the Board of Directors and sign them.
2. Supervise and provide advice on the management of the company.
3. Conduct an assessment of the performance of the Board of Directors.

## PROHIBITION:

Commissioners are prohibited from making any transactions that have conflict of interests and gaining personal benefit either directly or indirectly from PT. Waskita Beton Precast, other than their legitimate income.

### Ethics and Conduct Board for Directors

1. Related with the application of the principles of Good Corporate Governance (GCG), Directors must:
2. Understand the GCG principles and implement them as an exemplary for employees.
3. Avoid the emergence of direct or indirect conflicts of interest.

Related with their main duties, the Board of Directors is obliged to:

1. Promote and ensure the implementation of Company's business and activities are pursuant to the purpose and objectives as well the business activities.
2. Prepare the Company's Long Term Plan, Work Plan and Budget, including plans related with the Company's business and activities of the enterprise, and submit them to the Board of Commissioners and the Shareholders to obtain approval from the General Meeting of Shareholders.
3. Maintain the minutes of meeting as well as the Company's bookkeeping in accordance with the usual standards in a company.
