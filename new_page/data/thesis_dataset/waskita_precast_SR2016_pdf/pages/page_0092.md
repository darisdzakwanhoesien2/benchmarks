Pelatihan ini ditujukan bagi pegawai baru maupun pegawai yang dirotasi dan/atau dipromosikan ke pekerjaan/jabatan baru, melalui praktik langsung yang dilakukan sambil bekerja dengan sistem mentor.

- Pendidikan Lanjutan
- Pelatihan ini merupakan fasilitas pendidikan untuk menunjang bidang pekerjaan pegawai.
- Pelatihan Pra-Purna Bakti
- Pelatihan ini ditujukan bagi pegawai yang akan memasuki masa pensiun.

Khusus mengenai pelatihan pra-purna bakti, selama tahun 2016, Perseroan telah melakukan pelatihan berupa Tip Menjadi Barista dan Peluang Usahanya; Usaha Peternakan Sapi, Kamping, Ayam dan Ikan; serta Usaha Budidaya Jamur. Pelatihan dilakukan di Ciawi, Bogor, pada 29 Agustus-02 September 2016, dan diikuti oleh 3 pegawai. G4-LA10

Pelatihan dan pengembangan karyawan yang dilakukan Perseroan bisa diperinci sebagai berikut:

1. Pendidikan
Merupakan kegiatan pembelajaran untuk meningkatkan kompetensi pegawai melalui pendidikan bergelar (S1, S2 dan S3) di dalam maupun di luar negeri dengan beasiswa dari Perseroan, sponsorship, maupun biaya sendiri. Hingga akhir tahun 2016 sebanyak 7 pegawai Perseroan telah menyelesaikan pendidikan S2 di dalam dan luar negeri. Jumlah itu turun dibanding tahun 2015, sebanyak 10 karyawan.

- Tugas Belajar
- a. Pendidikan bergelar S2 di dalam negeri, beasiswa dari Perseroan dan dilakukan diluar waktu kerja.
- b. Pendidikan bergelar S2 di luar negeri, beasiswa dari Perseroan ataupun dari pemberi beasiswa lainnya, pegawai dibebaskan dari pekerjaan

- Ijin Belajar
- a. Pendidikan Bergelar (S1 atau S2) di dalam negeri, diluar waktu kerja dengan menggunakan biaya sendiri.
- b. Pendidikan Bergelar S2 di luar negeri, harus mengajukan cuti diluar tanggungan perusahaan dan sudah bekerja di Perseroan minimal 2 tahun.

The training is intended for new employees as well as employees who are rotated and/or promoted to a new job/position, through direct practice that is done while working with a mentor.

- Advanced Education
This training is an educational facility to support the employee's work field.

- Pre-post employment Training
This training is intended for employees who will retire.

Especially with regard to pre-post employment training, in 2016 the Company has conducted training in the form of Tips for Becoming a Barista and its Business Opportunities; Cattle, Goat, Chicken, and Fisch Farming Business; and Mushroom Cultivation. Trainings were executed in Ciawi, Bogor, on August 29-September 2, 2016 and followed by 3 employees. G4-LA10

Employee training and development conducted by the Company can be itemized as follows:

1. Education
Is learning activities to improve the competence of employees through education (S1, S2 and S3) inside and outside the country on a scholarship from the Company, sponsorship, as well as own expense. Until the end of 2016, as many as 7 (seven) employees of the Company who is currently completing S2 education at home and abroad. The number was lower than in 2015, as many as 10 employees.

- On Leave study
- a. S2 degree education in the country, a scholarship from the Company and outside working hours.
- b. S2 degree education abroad, scholarships from the Company or from other scholarship providers, employees were released from work.

- Permission for Study
- a. Education with degree (S1 or S2) within the country, outside of working time by using their own expense.
- b. Education of Master degree abroad, must request leave of absence beyond the responsibility of the company and has been working in the Company a minimum of 2 years.
