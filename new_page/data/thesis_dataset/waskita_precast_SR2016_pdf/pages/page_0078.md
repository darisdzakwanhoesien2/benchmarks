BERKEMBANG BERUAMA UNTUK KEBERLAKUNTASI
MEWUJUDKAN PERUSANAAN KAWAN LINGKUNGAN

Hingga 31 Desember 2016, dari total sebanyak 912 pegawai, sebanyak 95 orang direkrut dari masyarakat di sekitar plant. Mereka menduduki posisi sebagai Staff Teknik, dan staff non teknik Upaya merekrut pegawai dari orang lokal merupakan komitmen Perseroan untuk mengurangi tingkat pengangguran, sekaligus memberdayakan potensi tenaga lokal. Dengan terbukanya lapangan kerja di Waskita Beton Precast, maka kehidupan para pekerja tersebut akan meningkat.

Sementara itu, untuk pemasok bahan baku material, sepanjang tahun 2016, Perseroan menjalin kerjasama dengan 8 pemasok di lokasi operasional plant. Bahan baku yang dipasok berupa PC Bar, Iron Wire, Besi Beton, PC Strand, Shear Concelor. Bahan baku material dari para pemasok itu menyumbang 56% dari total kebutuhan bahan baku Perseroan. G4-EC9

Untuk program CSR, sepanjang tahun 2016, Perseroan mengalokasikan dana sebesar Rp1,415 miliar. Waskita Precast mengalokasikan dana tersebut sebagai perwujudan dan tanggung jawabnya dalam menjalankan amanat undang-undang seperti Undang-Undang No. 25 Tahun 2007 tentang Penanaman Modal, UU No. 40 Tahun 2007 tentang Perseroan Terbatas, serta ISO 26000 yang telah diratifikasi oleh Pemerintah pada tahun 2010 dan dijadikan Standar Nasional (SNI) pada 2012.

Kepedulian terhadap masyarakat di sekitar kantor maupun plant diwujudkan melalui program Tanggung Jawab Sosial Perusahaan dalam bentuk, antara lain: G4-EC7, G4-EC8

- Pemeriksaan kesehatan massal untuk masyarakat sekitar sarana produksi
- Pembangunan dan perbaikan sarana ibadah
- Perbaikan akses jalan lingkungan
- Sosialisasi kesehatan di sarana pendidikan disekitar sarana produksi
- Menyediakan sarana olahraga untuk karang taruna.

Until December 31, 2016, of a total of 912 employees, as many as 95 person were recruited from the communities around the plant. They occupied position as Staff Engineering and non-engineering staff. Efforts to recruit employees from local people is the Company's commitment to reduce the unemployment rate, as well as empowering the potential of local people. With the employment opportunities in Precast Concrete Waskita, the lives of these workers are expected to increase.

Regarding raw material suppliers, throughout 2016 the Company has formed a partnership with eight suppliers in the location of plant operations. The supplied raw materials were in the form of PC Bar, Iron Wire, Iron Concrete, PC Strand, Shear Concelor. These raw materials from suppliers contributed to 56% of total raw material needs of the Company. G4-EC9

For CSR program, throughout 2016, the Company allocated funds amounting to Rp1,809 billion. The allocation of these funds is the embodiment and responsibility of Waskita Beton Precast in carrying out the mandate of laws, such as Law No. 25 of 2007 on Investment, Law No. 40 Year 2007 on Limited Liability Company, as well as ISO 26000 which has been ratified by the government in 2010 and made the National Standard (SNI) in 2012.

The Company's concern for the community around the office or plant is realized through Corporate Social Responsibility program in the form of, among others: G4-EC7, G4-EC8

- Medical examination for the community around the production area
- Construction and repair of worship places
- Road repair
- Health socialization in educational institutions around the production area
- Build sports facilities for the youth.
