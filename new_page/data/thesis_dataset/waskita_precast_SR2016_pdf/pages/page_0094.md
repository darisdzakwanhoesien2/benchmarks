MEWILKODKAN PERUSANAAN KAWAR UNGKUNGAN

Pelaksanaan Workshop New Strategic Marketing, Teknisi Laboratorium Beton, Transfer Pengetahuan Mesin, Training Software Akuntansi, Pelatihan General Affairs Professional Certification (CGAP), Pelatihan QMS ISO 9001:2008 Interpretation &amp; Internal Audit, Pelatihan Basic Financial Modelling, Good Governance – Excellent Performance, Pelatihan Sipil Geoteknik dan Software Plaxis, Gathering Finance &amp; Leadership Development.

Selain itu, juga ada Pelatihan Surat Izin Operasional (SIO) Batching Plant &amp; Concrete Pump, Pelatihan dan Sertifikasi Pengawas Konstruksi Beton Pracetak Bangunan Gedung, Preparing ISO 9001:2015 Worskhop, Strategi Memenangkan Tender Pemerintah Melalui Sistem Elektronik (E-Procurement), Geo Talk I, Seminar Merancang Program Pelatihan &amp; Pengembangan Collection, Training Understanding &amp; Designing SOP dan Penugasan Seminar Pembebasan Lahan.

Total pelatihan selama tahun 2016 yang diikuti oleh pegawai tetap dan tidak tetap Waskita Beton Precast, berdasarkan People Development Ratio Desember 2016, adalah 6,1 jam/orang/tahun. Peserta yang telah mengikuti pelatihan sebanyak 438 orang, naik 38,17% dibanding tahun 2015 sebanyak 317 peserta. Peserta pelatihan berdasarkan level struktur organisasi terdiri dari 387 staf, 4 orang general manajer/Kepala SPI/Sekretaris Perusahaan, 33 orang Manajer Unit Kerja/Produksi/Supporting, 10 orang Kepala Seksi, dan 4 orang Staf Ahli.

Workshop on New Strategic Marketing, Transfer of Machinery Knowledge, Training on Accounting Software, Training of General Affairs Professional Certification (CGAP), Training of QMS ISO 9001:2008 Interpretation &amp; Internal Audit, Basic Financial Modelling Training, Good Governance - Excellent Performance, Training of Civil Geotechnical and Software Plaxis, Gathering Finance &amp; Leadership Development.

Besides that, there were also Training on Permit Operations (SIO) of Batching Plant and Concrete Pump, Training and Certification of Supervision of Precast Concrete Construction of Building, Preparing ISO 9001:2015 Worskhop, Strategy of Winning Government Tender Through Electronic Systems (E-Procurement), Geo Talk I, Seminar on Designing Training Program &amp; Development of Collection, Understanding and Designing SOP, and Seminar on Land Acquisition.

Total trainings during 2016 that were followed by permanent and temporary employees of Waskita Beton Precast, based on People Development Ratio in December 2016, was 6.1 hours/person/year. Participants who have attended training as many as 438 people, up 38.17% compared to 2015 as many as 317 participants. Participants based on the level of the organizational structure consists of 387 staff, 4 general manager/Head of Internal Audit/Corporate Secretary, 33 Manager of Working/Production/Supporting Unit, 10 Section Head, and 4 Expert Staff.
