# Ketercakupan dalam Perjanjian Kerja Bersama G4-11

Coverage in the Collective Labour Agreement G4-11

Perseroan menghargai kebebasan bagi karyawan dalam berserikat dan berkumpul, termasuk membuka peluang berdirinya serikat pekerja karyawan. Hingga saat ini, Perseroan belum memiliki Serikat Pekerja, meskipun dalam Peraturan Perusahaan di perusahaan lain berupa Perjanjian Kerja Bersama dimungkinkan untuk itu. Peraturan Perusahaan mengatur berbagai hal yang berkaitan dengan hubungan antara pegawai dengan perusahaan sehingga mengikat kedua pihak.

Walau Perseroan belum memiliki serikat pekerja, namun hak mereka untuk berkumpul dan menyampaikan pendapat sudah diatur dalam Peraturan Perusahaan yang mengikat bagi seluruh karyawan dan manajemen Perseroan. Hal itu, diatur dalam Perjanjian Kerja Bersama, diantaranya mengatur tentang keluh kesah pegawai dan cara penyampaiannya.

The Company appreciates the freedom for employees to join an union and assemble, including the opportunity to establish employee unions. Until now, the Company has not had an Employee Union, although the Company Regulations -or in other companies is in the form of Collective Labour Agreement- allows it. The Company Regulations organize various issues related to the relationship between an employee with the Company, and it is binding for both parties.

Although the Company does not have an Employee Union yet, but employee's right to assemble and express opinions has been set in the Company Regulations, and are binding for all employees and management of the Company. This is stated in the Collective Labor Agreement, among others regulates on employee complaints and media to submit the complaint.
