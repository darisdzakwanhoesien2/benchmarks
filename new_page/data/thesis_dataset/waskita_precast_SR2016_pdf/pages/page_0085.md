# Berkembang Bersama Untuk Keberlanjutan
Growing Together For Sustainability

PT. Waskita Beton Precast sedang tumbuh. Kinerjanya terus meningkat, kontrak demi kontrak kerja diraih. Bahkan, hingga beberapa tahun ke depan, Perseroan tidak akan kekurangan proyek. Bisa dibilang, industri manufaktur beton precast dan ready mix memang sedang berada di zaman keemasan.

Di balik performa dan kinerja yang kian kinclong itu, ada sejumlah tanggung jawab yang diemban oleh Perseroan agar usaha yang dijalani terus berlanjut. Antara lain, berkaitan dengan praktik ketenagakerjaan, penghargaan pada hak asasi manusia dan hak masyarakat lokal di sekitar lokasi perusahaan, serta tanggung jawab produk. Di antara keempat hal itu saling berkaitan, antara satu dengan yang lain saling mempengaruhi.

## Praktik Ketenagakerjaan dan Kenyamanan Bekerja

Sumber Daya Manusia (SDM) merupakan aset utama Perseroan dan memiliki peran penting dalam menentukan keberhasilan kegiatan usaha Perseroan. Menyadari hal tersebut, Perseroan berkeyakinan bahwa untuk dapat mencapai misi Perseroan, mutlak diperlukan usaha-usaha yang dapat menunjang pengembangan dan peningkatan kualitas sumber daya manusia sehingga pendayagunaan sumber daya manusia dapat dilakukan secara optimal.

Per tanggal 31 Desember 2016, Perseroan memiliki 912 karyawan. Berdasarkan komposisi jenjang pendidikan, Perseroan memiliki 344 karyawan Strata 1/Strata 2, 83 karyawan Diploma 3, dan 485 karyawan Sekolah Menengah Umum/Sederajat. Sejalan dengan proyek yang ditangani dan target yang ditetapapkan Perseroan, jumlah karyawan tersebut akan terus bertambah. G4-LA1

Sepanjang tahun 2016, Perseroan merekrut 440 karyawan, atau naik 93,22% dibanding tahun 2015, yakni 472 karyawan. Rekrutmen dilakukan di Kantor Pusat maupun di daerah-daerah tempat Waskita Beton Precast beroperasi. Hingga akhir Desember 2016, Perseroan mengoperasikan 10 pabrik (plant) precast, 41 pabrik ready mix/batching plant dan 3 stone crusher (quarry) yang memiliki lokasi yang strategis di dekat lokasi proyek-proyek yang dikerjakan Waskita Beton Precast.

Selain bertambahnya jumlah karyawan baru yang masuk, ada juga di antara karyawan Perseroan yang berhenti atau keluar dari pekerjaannya. Per 31 Desember 2016, sebanyak 47 karyawan keluar.

PT. Waskita Beton Precast is growing. Its performance continues to rise, contract-by-contract are achieved. In fact, until few years ahead, the Company will not experience project shortage. Thus it can be concluded that the manufacturing industry of precast and ready mix concrete is in its golden age.

Behind the increasingly brilliant performance, lies a number of responsibilities that must be carried out by the Company in order to maintain its business. Among other things are issues related to labor practices, respect for human rights, and the rights of local communities in the vicinity of the company, as well as product liability. These four issues are related and influence each other.

## Employment Practices and Working Comfort

Human Resources (HR) is a major asset of the Company and has an important role in determining the success of the Company's business activities. Realizing this, the Company believes that in order to achieve the mission of the Company, necessary efforts to support the development and improvement of the quality of human resources is absolute, so that utilization of human resources can be performed optimally.

As of December 31, 2016, the Company had 912 employees. Based on the composition of education level, the Company has 344 employees of bachelor/master degree, 83 employees of Diploma 4/Diploma 3 degree, and 485 employees of Senior High School/equivalents degree. In line with the project being handled and the target set by the Company, the number of employees will continue to grow. G4-LA1

Throughout 2016, the Company recruited 440 employees, an increase of 93.22% compared to 2015 of 472 employees. Recruitment was performed at the Head Office and in areas where Waskita Beton Precast operates. Until the end of December 2016, the Company is operating 10 precast factory (plant), 41 ready mix factory/batching plant, and 3 stone crusher (quarry) which has a strategic location near the location of the projects undertaken by Waskita Beton Precast.

In addition to increasing the number of new employees, there were also Company's employees who quit or resigned. As of December 31, 2016, as many as 47 employees resigned.
