- Etika dan Perilaku Pegawai pegawai dalam Perusahaan:
1. Pegawai PT. Waskita Beton Precast harus senantiasa menjunjung tinggi etika bisnis dalam melaksanakan tugasnya sehari-hari.
2. Pegawai wajib mendahulukan kepentingan Perusahaan daripada kepentingan golongan, pribadi, keluarga, kerabat dan/atau pihak lain.
3. Setiap pegawai wajib menjaga harta milik dan nama baik perusahaan.
4. Pegawai wajib bersikap/berperilaku baik dan menjaga kesopanan di dalam ataupun di luar Perusahaan.
5. Pegawai wajib mematuhi isi Peraturan Perusahaan.
6. Pegawai wajib mentaati aturan-aturan yang berlaku di perusahaan.

Pegawai dengan Jabatannya dalam Perusahaan:
1. Pegawai wajib mengerahkan segala daya dan upaya dalam melaksanakan tugas- jabatan yang diserahkan kepadanya.
2. Pegawai wajib menerima penempatan tugas, melaksanakan perjalanan dinas, mutasi dan detasir, berdasarkan Perintah/Keputusan Direksi atau Atasan yang berwenang.
3. Pegawai wajib berada di tempat tugas dan melaksanakan tugasnya pada hari-hari dan jam kerja yang ditentukan, kecuali sedang menjalankan tugas Perusahaan di luar tempat tugasnya.

2. The Board of Directors of PT. Waskita Beton Precast is prohibited from KKN (corruption, collusion, and nepotism) in connection with the company.
3. The Board of Directors is not allowed to have a position as director at other companies.
4. The Board of Directors is not allowed to own shares of competing companies or companies that act as suppliers.

- Ethics and Code of Conduct for Employee
1. Employees of PT. Waskita Beton Precast must always uphold business ethics in carrying out their daily duties.
2. Employees shall prioritize the interests of the Company rather than interests of group, personal, family, relatives and/or other parties.
3. Each employee shall maintain the Company's property and good name.
4. Employees shall act/behave properly and maintain decorum inside or outside the Company.
5. Employees shall comply with the contents of the Company Regulations.
6. Employees shall obey the rules that apply in the company.

Employees with his/her position in the Company:
1. Employees shall mobilize all resources and efforts in carrying out the duties of his/her position.
2. Employees are required to accept all assignments, carrying out business travel, transfer and placement, based on the Instruction/Decision of the Board of Directors or the authorized supervisor.
3. Employees shall be at the job site and perform their duties in the specified days and hours, except on duty outside his/her job site.
