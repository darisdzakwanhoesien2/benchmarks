In determining the ECL, the Group utilizes modelling methodologies which are reliant on internal and external data as well as a number of estimates. These include:

- assessment of significant increase in credit risk ("SICR");
- expectations of forward-looking macro-economic factors; and
- the model assumptions, including the post model adjustment.

In view of these factors, we identified this as a key audit matter.

b. Individual assessment of ECL on loans

The individual assessment of ECL involves the use of judgments and estimates, including management's key assumptions applied on the expected future cash flows of the borrower, including amounts and timing of recoveries and collateral valuations. In view of these factors, we identified this as a key audit matter.
