## Limitations

Decar Verite Asia planned and performed the engagement to obtain sufficient and appropriate evidence to provide a basis for a conclusion at a Moderate Level of Assurance. In accordance with a Type 1 assurance engagement under AA1000AS v3, procedures included reviewing and evaluating evidence on a risk-based sampling basis to assess the Bank's adherence to the AA1000 Accountability Principles (2018). The engagement did not include assurance over the reliability of specific sustainability performance data. However, the conformity of disclosures with the applicable sustainability reporting criteria was assessed. These processes enabled Decar Verite Asia to reduce the risk of error in its conclusions but does not reduce the risk to zero.

## Adherence to AA1000 Accountability Principles (2018)

Conclusions in regard to the AA1000 Accountability Principles (2018) are presented below:

## Inclusivity

The Bank has applied the principle of Inclusivity by identifying stakeholder groups significant to its operations, including customers, investors, employees, business partners, regulators, academics, communities, and nongovernmental organizations (NGOs). Appropriate engagement activities were conducted to obtain stakeholder feedback, support the improvement of sustainable products and services, and understand stakeholder needs and expectations. Stakeholders were also involved in the assessment of the Bank's material topics.

## Materiality

In 2024, the Bank identified and prioritized its most relevant sustainability topics with reference to MUFG, industry peers, rating agencies, and applicable standards and frameworks, incorporating expert judgment and stakeholder input. A double materiality approach was applied, considering impacts on both the Bank and its stakeholders. In 2025, the Bank reviewed the established material topics and determined that no revisions were required, as no significant changes occurred in its operations or value chain. Accordingly, Decar Verite Asia concludes that the principle of Materiality has been applied.

## Responsiveness

The Bank demonstrates responsiveness through targeted programs addressing social and environmental impacts, including the integration of climate considerations into its sustainable financing products and the promotion of financial inclusion initiatives for underserved communities. In collaboration with MUFG, the Bank has established a decarbonization strategy with defined targets and initiatives toward net zero emissions, aligned with applicable regulatory requirements and industry expectations. The Bank also maintains grievance and whistleblowing mechanisms and integrates material sustainability topics into its governance and decision-making processes to ensure systematic and effective responses.

## Impact

The Bank has applied the principle of Impact by identifying, measuring, and managing the economic, environmental, and social effects of its activities. Environmental impacts are addressed through measurable targets, including reductions in energy use and greenhouse gas emissions, assessment of financed emissions (Scope 3, Category 15), and ecosystem restoration initiatives. The Bank also evaluates the outcomes of its social programs and integrates sustainability performance, including sustainable finance targets, into governance mechanisms through Board-level KPIs, while continuing to expand financing toward sustainable business activities.
