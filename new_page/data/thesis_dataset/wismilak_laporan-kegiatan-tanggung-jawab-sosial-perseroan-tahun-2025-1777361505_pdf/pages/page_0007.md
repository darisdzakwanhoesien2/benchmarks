## Penilaian Dampak

Impact Assessment

Mempromosikan budaya lokal dan mendorong pertumbuhan ekonomi lokal.

Promotes local culture and supports regional economic growth.

## Capaian

Achievement

Program Pelestarian budaya tradisional madura melibatkan Pemerintah daerah, komunitas pelestarian seni budaya, UMKM serta menjangkau lebih dari 26.000 pengunjung.

The Traditional Madurese Cultural Preservation Program involved local government, cultural preservation communities, and MSMEs, reaching more than 26,000 visitors.

## Uraian Kegiatan

Activity Description

Wismilak mendukung penyelenggaraan Karapan Sapi Piala Presiden 2025 di Bangkalan sebagai upaya mempromosikan budaya lokal Madura. Melalui partisipasi tersebut, Wismilak turut mendorong pengembangan ekonomi daerah dengan menarik kunjungan wisatawan, sekaligus memberikan edukasi kepada generasi muda mengenai pentingnya menjaga dan melestarikan warisan budaya.

Wismilak supported the organization of the Karapan Sapi President's Cup 2025 in Bangkalan as part of its efforts to promote Madurese local culture. Through this participation, the Company also contributed to regional economic development by attracting tourist visits while educating younger generations on the importance of preserving cultural heritage.
