# Pemberdayaan Masyarakat
## Community Empowerment

Perseroan melaksanakan program pemberdayaan masyarakat sebagai bagian dari kontribusi terhadap pembangunan sosial di sekitar wilayah operasional. Setiap program dirancang melalui proses identifikasi kebutuhan dan survei awal guna memahami kondisi, potensi, serta tantangan yang dihadapi masyarakat. Pendekatan ini memastikan bahwa inisiatif yang dijalankan tidak bersifat umum atau seragam, melainkan disesuaikan dengan karakteristik dan kebutuhan penerima manfaat. Melalui perencanaan berbasis kebutuhan tersebut, Perseroan berupaya memastikan bahwa program pemberdayaan memberikan dampak yang lebih tepat sasaran, relevan, dan berkelanjutan bagi masyarakat yang terlibat.

The Company carries out community empowerment programs as part of its contribution to social development in the areas surrounding its operations. Each program is designed through a needs identification process and preliminary surveys to understand the conditions, potential, and challenges faced by the community. This approach ensures that the initiatives implemented are not generic or uniform, but rather tailored to the characteristics and needs of the beneficiaries. Through this needs-based planning, the Company seeks to ensure that its empowerment programs create impacts that are more targeted, relevant, and sustainable for the communities involved.
