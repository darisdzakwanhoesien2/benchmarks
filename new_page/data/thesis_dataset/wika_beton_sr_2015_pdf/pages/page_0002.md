Laporan Berkelanjutan 2015 PT Wijaya Karya Beton Tbk disusun untuk memberikan informasi secara menyeluruh tentang tujuan, kinerja, serta dampak ekonomi, sosial, dan lingkungan bagi seluruh pemangku kepentingan. Melalui laporan ini, Perseroan menyajikan informasi tentang pengaruh penting atas kinerja Perseroan dalam aspek-aspek bisnis yang berkelanjutan terhadap lingkungan dan masyarakat.

2015 Sustainability Report of PT Wijaya Karya Beton Tbk is prepared to give information regarding the Company's goal, performance, as well as the impact of economy, social, and environment on all stakeholders. This report also contains information regarding the important influence of the Company's performance in the aspects of sustainable business towards the environment and community.
