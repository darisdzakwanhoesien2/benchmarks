# Aspek Sosial
Social Aspect

## PermataBankers
Komitmen kami untuk memberikan kesempatan berkembang bagi sumber daya manusia ditunjukkan dengan pengembangan kompetensi dan kepemimpinan PermataBankers. Keberagaman dan kesetaraan kami wujudkan dalam memberikan kesempatan yang sama bagi semua PermataBankers, dari 7.776 total karyawan PermataBank, sebanyak 50% di antaranya adalah perempuan yang menduduki level manajerial.

## PermataHati
Sejak tahun 2017, PermataBank menginisiasi BRAVE: Because eveRyone is Able &amp; CreatiVE di mana kami membina dan mengembangkan difabel agar semakin berdaya. BRAVE melengkapi komitmen PermataBank memberikan kesempatan yang setara bagi difabel untuk berprestasi. Hingga kini sebanyak 27 karyawan difabel di PermataBank (18 karyawan tunanetra di bagian telesales. 2 karyawan tuna daksa di bagian telesales dan mortgage, serta 7 karyawan tuna daksa yang mengikuti program Disabled Associate Program).

PermataBank memberikan kesempatan kepada PermataBankers untuk ikut serta dalam kegiatan pemberdayaan masyarakat melalui Employee Volunteering. Pada tahun 2018, sebanyak 1.000 Employee Volunteers yang ikut dalam kegiatan CERITA Permata di 50 kota di seluruh Indonesia. [GRI 102-15]

## PermataBankers
Our commitment to providing opportunities for our human resources to grow is demonstrated by the PermataBankers competency and leadership development. We manifest diversity and equality in providing equal opportunities for all PermataBankers, from 7,776 employees of PermataBank, 50% are women at the managerial level.

## PermataHati
Since 2017, PermataBank initiated BRAVE: Because eveRyone is Able &amp; CreatiVE, which is where we foster and develop people with disabilities to be more empowered. BRAVE complements PermataBank's commitment to provide equal opportunities for people with disabilities to excel. Until recently, there are 27 people with disabilities who are employees of PermataBank (18 visually impaired employees in the telesales department, 2 people with physical disabilities in the telesales and mortgage department, as well as 7 people with physical disabilities who are participating in the Disabled Associate Program).

PermataBank provides opportunities for PermataBankers to participate in community empowerment activities through Employee Volunteering. In 2018, a total of 1,000 Employee Volunteers have participated in the CERITA Permata activity in 50 cities throughout Indonesia. [GRI 102-15]
