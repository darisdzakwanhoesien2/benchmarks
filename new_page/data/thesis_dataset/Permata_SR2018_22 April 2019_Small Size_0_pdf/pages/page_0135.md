# Kode Etik Perilaku

Code of Conduct

Kode Etik Perilaku Karyawan (Code of Conduct) merupakan panduan bagi PermataBankers dalam berinteraksi dengan pemangku kepentingan tentang cara bersikap dan bertingkah laku yang sesuai dengan prinsip-prinsip moral atau nilai-nilai mengenai hal yang dianggap baik dan tidak baik, serta hal-hal yang benar dan yang tidak benar.

PermataBank mengharuskan PermataBankers untuk selalu bersikap hati-hati, cermat dan cerdas saat menghadapi hal-hal yang berpotensi menimbulkan risiko yang dapat merugikan perusahaan atau memiliki Risk Awareness. Dengan penerapan Kode Etik dan risk awareness yang tinggi, kesalahan dalam pengambilan keputusan dapat dihindari dan mencegah kerugian bagi perusahaan, maupun berimplikasi pada reputasi, integritas dan kehormatan karyawan itu sendiri.

Kode Etik dan Budaya Kepatuhan diperkenalkan kepada setiap PermataBankers dan wajib ditandatangani sejak pertama kali karyawan bergabung. Selama bekerja, sosialisasi dan pelatihan melalui e-learning terkait Kode Etik dan Budaya Kepatuhan, dan prosedur anti korupsi dilakukan secara berkala. PermataBankers juga diwajibkan melakukan penyegaran Kode Etik melalui e-learning setiap tahun.

Dari waktu ke waktu, PermataBank mengevaluasi Kode Etik Perilaku Karyawan serta melakukan monitoring atas pemenuhan pelaksanaan e-learning. Hasil pemantauan dan evaluasi dilaporkan kepada Direktur Kepatuhan serta melalui Laporan Profil Risiko.

The Employee Code of Conduct is the guidelines for PermataBankers in interacting with the stakeholders on how to act and behave in accordance with the moral principles or values on matters that are considered good and bad, as well as matters that are right and not right.

PermataBank requires PermataBankers to always be careful, cautious, and smart when dealing with matters that have the potential to create risk that may be detrimental to the company or in other words to have risk awareness. With the implementation of the Code of Ethics and high risk awareness, mistakes in decision making can be avoided and prevent losses for the company, as well as implications to the reputation, integrity, and respect of the employees themselves.

The Code of Conduct and the Compliance Culture are introduced to each PermataBanker and shall be signed since the first time an employee joins. During their tenure, dissemination and training related to the Code of Conduct and Compliance Culture as well as anti corruption procedures are conducted periodically. PermataBankers are also required to have Code of Conduct refreshment through e-learning annually.

From time to time, PermataBank evaluates the Employee Code of Conduct and monitors the fulfillment of e-learning implementation. The monitoring and evaluation results are reported to the Compliance Director as well as through the Risk Profile Report.
