# Layanan Digital untuk Semua

Digital Services for All

Dengan meningkatnya kebutuhan nasabah akan layanan yang sederhana, cepat, dan andal (simple, fast, and reliable), PermataBank meluncurkan rangkaian inovasi produk dan layanan keuangan bagi nasabah. PermataBank mengembangkan perbankan digital untuk memberikan kemudahan dalam melakukan transaksi perbankan dan pembukaan rekening, termasuk komunikasi dengan nasabah melalui electronic channel (e-channel).

## INOVASI DIGITAL PERMATABANK

Digitalisasi layanan keuangan bisa meningkatkan inklusi keuangan di Indonesia karena dapat dimanfaatkan untuk menjangkau sistem keuangan.

**PermataMobile X**, aplikasi mobile banking terbaru yang memberikan lebih dari 200 fitur, seperti single customer view, account opening, wealth management transaction, history transaksi selama 12 bulan dan lain-lain.

**Buka Rekening Saham** bekerja sama dengan IndoPremier Sekuritas sebagai penyedia Rekening Dana Nasabah (RDN) hadir dengan terobosan terbaru menyederhanakan proses pembukaan rekening efek saham dan reksa dana secara digital, tanpa memerlukan tanda tangan basah calon investor.

**Beli SBR Online** cukup melalui situs web dan aplikasi ponsel pintar yang dibantu oleh mitra distribusi.

**Transaksi Mutual Fund dan Bond** dapat dilakukan melalui PermataMobile X. Transaksi pembelian atau penjualan Reksa Dana, Surat Utang, Time Deposit, serta Surat Berharga Negara dapat dilakukan secara online melalui PermataMobile X.

**Pembukaan rekening tabungan, pengajuan Kartu Kredit, serta pengajuan KTA** dapat dilakukan secara online melalui PermataMobile X.

Menerapkan artificial intelligence (AI) bernama **VoiceID**, yang berguna untuk melakukan otentifikasi identitas nasabah melalui suaranya, mempercepat verifikasi menjadi 45 detik.

With the increasing demands of customers for services that are simple, fast, and reliable, PermataBank has launched a series of product innovations and financial services for our customers. PermataBank has developed digital banking to provide convenience in conducting banking transactions and opening accounts, including communicating with customers through electronic channel (e-channel).

## PERMATABANK DIGITAL INNOVATION

Digitizing financial services can increase financial inclusion in Indonesia because it can be used to access the financial system.

**PermataMobile X**, the latest mobile banking application, which provides more than 200 features, such as single customer view, account opening, wealth management transactions, and transaction history for 12 months and others.

**Stock Account Opening** in collaboration with IndoPremier Sekuritas as the provider of customer fund accounts introduced the latest breakthrough of digital securities account opening process. With this process, customer's wet signature is no longer needed for the stock and mutual fund securities account opening.

**SBR Online Purchase** can be conducted through the website and smartphone application, assisted by the distribution partners.

**Mutual Funds and Bonds** Transactions can be conducted through PermataMobile X. Buying and selling transactions of Mutual Funds, Bonds, Time Deposits, as well as Government Securities can be conducted online through PermataMobile X.

**Opening of savings accounts, Credit Card applications, as well as Unsecured Loan applications** can be conducted through PermataMobile X.

Implementing artificial intelligence (AI) called **VoiceID**, which is useful to authenticate the identity of our customers through their voice, has increased the verification speed to 45 seconds.
