Dengan adanya kegiatan ini, para peserta berkesempatan untuk berhenti sejenak dari kesibukan sehari-hari, melihat kembali ke dalam diri, menemukan kembali kekuatan dan potensi masing-masing, dan setelah itu menerapkannya dalam kolaborasi dan pengembangan organisasinya masing-masing.

Rangkaian selanjutnya, terbagi menjadi empat jenis kegiatan untuk umum, yaitu konferensi, diskusi panel, kelas mini dan sustainability expo. Dengan berdonasi sebesar Rp50.000 pengunjung dapat mengikuti seluruh kegiatan konferensi dan diskusi panel. Seluruh hasil donasi yang terkumpul akan digunakan untuk pengembangan program pendidikan bersama Sanggar Anak Akar sebagai partner CSR PermataHati.

Sesi konferensi diisi oleh pembicara-pembicara yang memiliki perhatian penuh terhadap pendidikan. Sebut saja seperti Reky Martha (Hoshizora Foundation), Tyovan Adi Widagdo (Bahaso), Andri Rizki Putra (Yayasan Pemimpin Anak Bangsa) dan Yoris Sebastian (OMG Creative Consulting) yang berbagi tentang pentingnya menjadi seorang agen perubahan. Untuk diskusi panel, beberapa komunitas dan lembaga swadaya masyarakat juga diajak saling berkoneksi dan bersinergi sesuai dengan tema atau isu yang diangkat seputar youth, women, dan disability seperti:

- Creative Teaching Method: Komunitas Guru Bhumi, Shinta VR dan Bumi Kardus.
- Empowering Communities with Disabilities: Komunitas Thisable Enterprise dan Precious One.
- Women Entrepreneur: Komunitas Seratus Kapas dan Binar Academy.
- Reaching the Unreached Pockets: Komunitas Nara Kreatif, Red Nose Foundation dan Sanggar Anak Akar.
- Empowering Women: Komunitas Toraja Melo dan Circa Handmade.
- Empowering Community through Technology: Komunitas Gandeng Tangan, Arkademy dan Impact Byte.
- Empowering Local Resources: Komunitas Suka Chitta, Melookmel dan Suwe Ora Jamu.

Through this activity, the participants had the opportunity to pause from their daily activities, take an introspective look at themselves to rediscover their strengths and potential, and afterwards apply them in further collaboration and development of their respective organizations.

The next series was divided into four types of activities for the public, which are Conferences, Panel Discussions, Mini Classes, and a Sustainability Expo. With a small donation of Rp50,000, visitors can take part in all conference and panel discussion activities. All donation proceeds will be used to develop an education program with Sanggar Anak Akar as PermataHati's CSR partner.

The conference sessions were filled with speakers who pay special attention to education. Names such as Reky Martha (Hoshizora Foundation), Tyovan Adi Widagdo (Bahaso), Andri Rizki Putra (Pemimpin Anak Bangsa Foundation), and Yoris Sebastian (OMG Creative Consulting) who share the importance of being an agent of change. For the panel discussions, several communities and Non-Governmental Organizations were also invited to connect and collaborate in accordance with themes or issues raised around youth, women, and disability including:

- Creative Teaching Methods: Guru Bhumi, Shinta VR and Bumi Kardus community.
- Empowering Communities with Disabilities: Thisable Enterprise and Precious One Community.
- Women Entrepreneurs: Seratus Kapas and Binar Academy Community.
- Reaching the Unreached Pockets: Nara Kreatif, Red Nose Foundation and Sanggar Anak Akar Community.
- Empowering Women: Toraja Melo and Circa Handmade Community.
- Empowering Community through Technology: Gandeng Tangan, Arkademy and Impact Byte Community.
- Empowering Local Resources: Suka Chitta, Melookmel and Suwe Ora Jamu Community.
