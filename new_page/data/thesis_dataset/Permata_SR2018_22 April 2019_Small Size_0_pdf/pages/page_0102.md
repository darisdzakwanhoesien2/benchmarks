# Operasional Perbankan Ramah Lingkungan

Environmentally Friendly Banking Operations

Secara tidak langsung PermataBank memberi dampak lingkungan dan sosial yang baik. Untuk mengelola dampak tersebut, aspek lingkungan dan kepatuhan dipantau secara berkala sebagai bagian dari pengendalian risiko.

PermataBank berkomitmen untuk mengelola dampak lingkungan yang langsung timbul dari aktivitas perbankan yaitu penggunaan energi, air, dan kertas. Tujuan akhir pengelolaan lingkungan operasional perbankan adalah untuk mencegah pencemaran dan efisiensi sumber daya alam.

Dalam pengelolaan dampak penting lingkungan, PermataBank telah menetapkan aspek-aspek lingkungan yang harus dikelola beserta indikator dan target dalam pengelolaan dampak lingkungan. Indikator ini dievaluasi pencapaiannya secara periodik oleh Departemen, Security &amp; HSE Management di bawah Divisi, Corporate Real Estate Services, Direktorat Technology &amp; Operations (T&amp;O). [GRI 103-2] [GRI 103-3]

Indirectly PermataBank may give good environmental and social impacts. To manage these impacts, the environmental aspects and compliance are periodically monitored as part of risk control.

PermataBank is committed to managing the environmental impacts deriving directly from its banking activities, including the use of energy, water, and paper. The ultimate goal of banking operational environmental management is to prevent pollution as well as the efficient use of natural resources.

In the management of important environmental impacts, PermataBank has determined the environmental aspects that must be managed along with the indicators and targets in the management of environmental impacts. These indicators are periodically evaluated by the HSE Management &amp; Security Department under Corporate Real Estate Service Division, Technology &amp; Operations Directorate (T&amp;O). [GRI 103-2] [GRI 103-3]
