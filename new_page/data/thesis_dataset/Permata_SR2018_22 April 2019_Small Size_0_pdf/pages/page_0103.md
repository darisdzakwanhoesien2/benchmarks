# EARTH HOUR

Sebagai bentuk dukungan dan komitmen dalam mewujudkan lingkungan yang sehat dan berkelanjutan, PermataBank secara rutin berpartisipsi dalam gerakan Earth Hour yang digagas oleh World WWF (World Wildlife Fund). Pada saat aksi Earth Hour yang dilaksanakan tanggal 24 Maret 2018, PermataBank turut mematikan lampu selama satu jam (pukul 20.30 – 21.30 waktu setempat). Selain itu, PermataBank juga turut mengkampanyekan gerakan Earth Hour kepada PermataBankers melalui pemasangan PC Wallpaper dari tanggal 19-23 Maret 2018, serta melalui kuis yang dikirimkan ke surat elektronik PermataBankers.

As a demonstration of our support and commitment to create a Healthy and Sustainable Environment, PermataBank routinely participates in the Earth Hour movement that was initiated by the WWF (World Wildlife Fund). During the Earth Hour movement that was held on 24 March 2018, PermataBank also turned off its lights for one hour (20:30 – 21:30 local time). In addition, PermataBank also campaigns the Earth Hour movement to PermataBankers through the installation of PC Wallpaper from 19-23 March 2018, as well as through quizzes sent through email to PermataBankers.
