About This Report

PermataBank mendidik dan merekrut tenaga kerja difabel untuk berkarier di perbankan. Hingga kini terdapat 27 karyawan difabel di PermataBank (18 karyawan tunanetra di bagian telesales, 2 karyawan tuna daksa di bagian telesales dan mortgage, serta 7 karyawan tuna daksa yang mengikuti program Disabled Associate Program).

Sejak tahun 2017, inisiatif ini bertransformasi menjadi PermataBRAVE (Because eveRyone is Able and creatIVE) yang merupakan wadah bagi komunitas disabilitas untuk belajar, berbagi pengetahuan dan pengalaman baru dengan sesama komunitas lainnya.

Program partisipatif tersebut terus bergulir di awal tahun 2018, bersinergi dengan KartuNet dalam penyelenggaraan Kursus Online I'M (Internet Marketing) BRAVE yang diluncurkan sekaligus dalam rangka Hari Braille di bulan Januari dan 12 tahun KartuNet. Selain peluncuran dan demo dari platform I'M BRAVE ini, digelar juga talkshow inspiratif bertema "Berani Sukses dengan Online" dengan menghadirkan narasumber yang ahli di bidangnya.

Sejumlah 150 penyandang disabilitas khususnya tunanetra mengikuti kursus online I'M (Internet Marketing) BRAVE secara gratis. Mereka tidak hanya berasal dari Jakarta dan sekitarnya saja, namun juga berbagai provinsi lainnya di seluruh Indonesia. Kursus online ini akan berlangsung di website Kartunet dengan alamat https://kursus.kartunet.com selama tiga bulan dengan materi pengenalan internet marketing, dasar copywriting, membuat blog, financial literacy, hingga sukses menjual produk di e-commerce. Format materi telah disesuaikan agar dapat diakses dan dipahami oleh para tunanetra pengguna screen reader, seperti: teks artikel, audio tutorial, dan live streaming webinar.

![img-50.jpeg](img-50.jpeg)

PermataBank educates and recruits people with disabilities to provide them with a career in banking. Up to the present time, there are 27 people with disabilities who are employees of PermataBank (18 visually impaired employees in the telesales department, 2 people with physical disabilities in the telesales and mortgage department, as well as 7 people with physical disabilities who are participating in the Disabled Associate Program).

Since 2017, this initiative has transformed into PermataBRAVE (Because eveRyone is Able and creatIVE), which is a forum for the disabled community to learn as well as share new knowledge and experience with other fellow communities.

The participatory program continued to be rolled out in 2018, in synergy with KartuNet in the implementation of I'M (Internet Marketing) BRAVE Online Course, which was launched at the same time as the Braille Day in January and 12 years of KartuNet. In addition to the launch and demonstration of the I'M BRAVE platform, an inspirational talk show themed "Dare to Succeed Online" was also held, presenting speakers who are experts in their fields.

A total of 150 people with disabilities, especially blind people took the I'M (Internet Marketing) BRAVE online course for free. They are not only from Jakarta and surrounding areas, but also various other provinces throughout Indonesia. The online course took place on the Kartunet website https://kursus.kartunet.com for three months with modules consisting of introduction to internet marketing, basic copywriting, blogging, financial literacy, to how to succeed in selling products via e-commerce. The modules have been adjusted to be easily accessed and understood by visually impaired screen reader users, including: text articles, audio tutorials, and live streaming webinars.
