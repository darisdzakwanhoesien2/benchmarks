# Tentang PermataHati

About Permatahati

PermataHati adalah inisiatif tanggung jawab sosial PermataBank sebagai salah satu wujud nilai 'Caring' pada rangkaian nilai-nilai PermataBank, yaitu I-PRICE (Integrity, Partnership, Responsiveness, Innovation, Caring and Excellence), yang diwujudkan melalui gerakan relawan karyawan atau Employee Volunteerism. PermataHati resmi diluncurkan pada akhir 2010 dan berfokus pada pendidikan, khususnya literasi keuangan sejalan dengan arahan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) yaitu meningkatkan literasi keuangan di Indonesia sesuai dengan Peraturan Presiden Nomor 82 tahun 2016 tentang Strategi Nasional Keuangan Inklusif. [GRI 103-1]

Sebagai sebuah lembaga keuangan terpercaya, PermataBank menjalankan PermataHati melalui berbagai program yang bersifat jangka panjang dan berkelanjutan. PermataHati terdiri dari tiga pilar yang dikenal dengan 3E, yaitu Education, Enhancement, dan Empowerment. Rangkaian program PermataHati menjangkau tingkat sekolah dasar, sekolah menengah serta para guru. Sejak 2017, PermataHati menjadi program inklusif dengan merangkul komunitas disabilitas termasuk tunanetra, tunadaksa dan tunarungu. Modul edukasi literasi keuangan PermataHati dikembangkan oleh tim internal, para volunteer PermataBank serta pakar-pakar pendidikan, dan untuk pertama kalinya dibuat dalam bentuk braille. Tujuan mulia dari seluruh program PermataHati adalah menciptakan kualitas pendidikan dan kehidupan yang lebih baik bagi anak bangsa. Dengan slogan 'Senyummu untuk Masa Depan', PermataBank berharap agar program PermataHati dapat terus menciptakan manfaat di masa depan bagi seluruh pemangku kepentingan. [GRI 103-2] [GRI 103-3]

PermataHati is PermataBank's Corporate Social Responsibility initiative, which brings the Caring value of PermataBank's I-PRICE values (Integrity, Partnership, Responsiveness, Innovation, Caring and Excellence) to life through it Employee Volunteerism movement. PermataHati was officially launched at the end of 2010 and focuses on education, particularly in financial literacy, which is in line with the directives of the Financial Services Authority (OJK), being to improve financial literacy across Indonesia, in accordance with the Presidential Regulation Number 82 year 2016 regarding the National Strategy of Inclusive Finance. [GRI 103-1]

As a trusted financial institution, PermataBank implements PermataHati through a variety of long term and sustainable programs. PermataHati have three main pillars known as 3E, which are Education, Enhancement, and Empowerment. A series of PermataHati program that targets elementary schools, secondary schools, and teacher level. Since 2017, PermataHati has become an inclusive program by embracing communities with disabilities, including mainly the visually, hearing and physically impaired. PermataHati's financial literacy education module was developed through our internal team, volunteers of PermataBank, as well as experts in education, and for the first time, it was introduced in braille. The noble objective of the entire PermataHati program is to create a better quality of education and life for the children of the nation. With the slogan 'Your Smile for the Future', PermataBank carries the hope that the PermataHati program will continue to create future benefits for all its stakeholders. [GRI 103-2] [GRI 103-3]
