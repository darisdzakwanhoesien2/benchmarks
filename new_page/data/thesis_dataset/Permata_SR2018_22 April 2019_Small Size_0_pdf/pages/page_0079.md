# Tempat Belajar, Berkembang dan Membuat Perbedaan
Place to Learn, Grow and Make a Difference

Framework Learning &amp; Development terbaru dibagi menjadi 4 pilar yang terdiri dari:

1. Leadership Development Program.
Program pengembangan kepemimpinan dirancang sesuai dengan tingkat jabatan untuk meningkatkan efektivitas pembelajaran.

2. Functional Development Program.
Program pengembangan technical skill yang mencakup semua tingkatan proficiency.

3. Character Building, yang merupakan pengembangan area softskill dan VMV Internalization.

4. Acceleration Program yang dirancang untuk para Talent.

Untuk meningkatkan pengalaman dalam proses pembelajaran, unit HR juga mengembangkan berbagai sarana infrastruktur pendukung dan pengembangan sistem seperti QR scanning untuk registrasi peserta pelatihan, meningkatkan kualitas sarana pendukung dengan menciptakan ruang Enlightment Center pada Learning Center, membuat gamifikasi dalam proses belajar, dan mempersiapkan mobile learning yang direncanakan untuk diluncurkan pada tahun 2019.

Sebagai dukungan bagi program Corporate Social Responsibility, unit Learning &amp; Development ikut ambil bagian dalam program pemberdayaan difabel. Program terdiri dari proses rekrutmen, pembelajaran dalam kelas hingga penempatan ke unit kerja. Pada tahun 2018, tercatat 7 karyawan difabel telah bergabung dengan program ini.

Unit Learning &amp; Development juga secara rutin melakukan benchmark dan kunjungan ke berbagai perusahaan untuk mendapatkan insight dalam pengembangan manajemen dan infrastruktur Learning.

The most recent Learning &amp; Development Framework is divided into 4 pillars, consisting of:

1. Leadership Development Program.
The leadership development program that is designed according to the level of position to improve learning effectiveness.

2. Functional Development Program.
Program for developing technical skills that covers all levels of proficiency.

3. Character Building, which is the development of the soft skill area and VMV Internalization.

4. Acceleration Program, which is designed for Talents.

To improve the experience in the learning process, the HR unit has also developed a variety of supporting infrastructure facilities and systems, including QR scanning for the registration of training participants, improving the quality of supporting facilities by creating Enlightment Center rooms at the Learning Center, gamification in the learning process, as well as preparing mobile learning, which is planned to be launched in 2019.

As a support for the Corporate Social Responsibility program, the Learning &amp; Development unit takes part in the disabled empowerment program. This program consists of the recruitment process, classroom learning, and placement to working units. In 2018, 7 disabled employees joined this program.

The Learning &amp; Development Unit also routinely benchmarked and visited various companies to gain insight concerning management development and Learning infrastructure.
