# Upaya Pencegahan Fraud Internal

Internal Fraud Prevention Effort

Strategi dan kebijakan dalam mencegah dan memitigasi fraud selama tahun 2018 sebagai berikut:

1. Menerbitkan Kebijakan Benturan Kepentingan.
2. Melakukan tinjauan terhadap Kebijakan Pemberian Hadiah dan Jamuan.
3. Melakukan sosialisasi/refreshment perihal Kode Etik Perilaku Karyawan, Kebijakan Benturan Kepentingan, Kebijakan Pemberian Hadiah dan Jamuan, serta Kebijakan dan Prosedur Speak-Up.
4. Memberikan dukungan atas permintaan nasihat terkait Kode Etik dan Pemberian Hadiah dan Jamuan dari seluruh unit di PermataBank.
5. Melakukan monitoring atas pemenuhan pelaksanaan e-learning, yang selanjutnya dilaporkan melalui laporan Direktur Kepatuhan dan Laporan Profil Risiko.

Salah satu tantangan yang dihadapi PermataBank dalam melakukan upaya pencegahan fraud internal adalah jumlah PermataBankers yang perlu mendapatkan sosialisasi serta penyegaran yang berkesinambungan berkaitan dengan pencegahan dan mitigasi anti-fraud. [GRI 103-2]

## Pelatihan

Mengelola program e-learning, yakni model pelatihan/pembelajaran fraud awareness yang dikemas dalam bentuk animasi komputer disertai soal-soal pertanyaan, yang wajib diikuti oleh seluruh PermataBankers serta menyelenggarakan pelatihan pengenalan fraud bagi karyawan baru yang merupakan bagian dari New Employee Orientation Program (NEOP). Selain melalui e-learning, pelatihan tersebut juga dilakukan melalui tatap muka dengan PermataBankers.

## Blokir Rekening

Kebijakan blokir internal rekening giro dan tabungan nasabah terindikasi fraud untuk mencegah penggunaan rekening untuk menampung hasil kejahatan.

Strategy and policy in preventing and mitigating fraud in 2018 as follows:

1. Issuance of Conflict of Interest Policy.
2. Reviewed the provision of Gifts and Hospitality Policy.
3. Carried out socialization/refreshment on Code of Conduct, Conflict of Interest Policy, Provision of Gifts and Hospitality Policy, and Speak-Up Policy and Procedure.
4. Provided supports for advice request related to the Code of Conduct and Provision of Gifts and Hospitality Policy from all units at PermataBank.
5. Conducted monitoring on fulfillment of e-learning implementation, which is then reported through the Compliance Director's report and Risk Profile Report.

One of the challenges in preventing internal fraud is the total PermataBankers that required to have continuously socialization and refreshment related with anti-fraud prevention and mitigation. [GRI 103-2]

## Training

Managing the e-learning program, which is the fraud awareness training/learning model that is in the form of computer animation accompanied by questions, which must be followed by all PermataBankers as well as conducting the fraud recognition training for new employees (induction training) as part of New Employee Orientation Program (NEOP). Other than through e-learning, the training is also conducted through face-to-face with PermataBankers.

## Blocking Policy

The internal blocking policy of customers' checking and savings accounts that have indicated fraud in order to prevent the use of the accounts to collect any proceeds of crime.
