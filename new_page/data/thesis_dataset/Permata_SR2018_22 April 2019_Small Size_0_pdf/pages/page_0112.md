b. Training and refreshment programs for all PermataBankers in form of HSE e-learning consist of explanation on potential risks of health and safety at workplace and how to mitigate the risks in accordance with prevailing HSE standards. In 2018, a total of 6,860 PermataBankers (91.96% from total of PermataBankers) has finished HSE e-learning program.

c. Refreshment and training programs for PermataBankers as First Aiders of branches in form of First Aider Refreshment e-learning. In 2018, total of First Aiders passed the training through e-learning is 251 people from 272 First Aiders (92.28%).

d. In 2018, PermataBank has conducted two times drill in one year. In first semester, earthquake drill was conducted at Tower III Bintaro on 30 April 2018, fire drill at Tower I WTC 2 on 26 July 2018 and Tower II Hayam Wuruk on 2 May 2018. Other than being held during working hours, drill was also conducted during night time (twilight drill) which was followed by PermataBankers with night working shift at Tower II Hayam Wuruk on 17 March 2018. In second semester, fire drill was conducted at Tower II Hayam Wuruk on 24 October 2018, and Tower III Bintaro on 19 November 2018.
