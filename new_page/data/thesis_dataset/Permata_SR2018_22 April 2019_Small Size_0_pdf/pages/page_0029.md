# Ikhtisar Keberlanjutan PermataBank

Sustainability Highlights of PermataBank

## Aspek Ekonomi

Economic Aspect

PermataBank mencapai pertumbuhan kredit yang positif sebesar Rp107,41 trilion per 31 Desember 2018 (konsolidasi) dibandingkan periode yang sama tahun lalu sebesar Rp98,6 trilion. Pertumbuhan kredit ini dikontribusikan oleh seluruh segmen, meliputi Retail Banking dan Wholesale Banking masing-masing tumbuh sebesar 9,0% dan 12,6% dibandingkan dengan tahun lalu. Laba bersih setelah pajak meningkat menjadi sebesar Rp901,25 miliar. [GRI 102-15]

PermataBank has achieved positive credit growth amounting to Rp107.41 trillion as of 31 December 2018 (consolidated) compared to the same period in the previous year, which amounted to Rp98.6 trillion. The growth in credit was contributed to by all segments, with Retail Banking and Wholesale Banking posting growth of 9.0% and 12.6%, respectively, compared with the previous year. The Net income after tax increased to Rp901.25 billion. [GRI 102-15]
