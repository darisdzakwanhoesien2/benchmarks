# Less Paper Office
Less Paper Office

PermataBank mengurangi intensitas pemakaian kertas melalui dua pendekatan, pendekatan pertama adalah mengurangi penggunaan kertas untuk proses internal PermataBank dengan komunikasi elektronik, pendekatan kedua melalui penyediaan layanan digital bagi nasabah menggantikan solusi kertas pada transaksi perbankan.

Inovasi digital untuk meningkatkan layanan yang sekaligus juga mengurangi dampak lingkungan, di antaranya:
- PermataMobile X
- E-billing
- E-form di situs web PermataBank [GRI 103-2]

PermataBank is reducing the intensity of paper use through two approaches, first approach is by reducing the reliance on paper in PermataBank's internal operations using electronic communication, second approach is through digital services for customers which replace paper in banking transactions.

Digital innovations designed to improve our services, which also reduce environmental impacts were, among others:
- PermataMobile X
- E-billing
- E-form in PermataBank website [GRI 103-2]

# Skema Gedung Ramah Lingkungan
Environmentally Friendly Building Scheme

Sejak 2009, PermataBank menerapkan sistem rating bagi gedung-gedung kantor PermataBank yaitu Building Environmental Assessment Standard (BEAS) yang ditetapkan oleh Standard Chartered Bank sebagaimana telah diterapkan di 30 negara. BEAS mengukur dampak operasional dari gedung-gedung GEMS PermataBank. Termasuk dalam kriteria BEAS adalah pengelolaan emisi GRK, konsumsi listrik dan konsumsi air.

Selain itu, sesuai dengan peraturan izin lingkungan di Indonesia, PermataBank memastikan dilakukannya laporan pengelolaan dan pemantauan lingkungan pada gedung-gedung yang dimiliki dan dikelola oleh PermataBank. Hal ini untuk memastikan dampak lingkungan kegiatan PermataBank terhadap lingkungan sesuai dengan persyaratan peraturan perundangan.

Since 2009, PermataBank has implemented a rating system for the buildings of the PermataBank office, which is the Building Environmental Assessment Standard (BEAS), which was established by Standard Chartered Bank as it has been implemented in 30 countries. BEAS measures the operational impact of PermataBank's GEMS buildings. Included in the BEAS criteria are the management of GHG emissions, electricity consumption and water consumption.

Additionally, in accordance with environmental permit regulations in Indonesia, PermataBank ensures that environmental management and monitoring reports are carried out on buildings owned and managed by PermataBank. This is conducted to ensure that the environmental impact of PermataBank's activities on the environment is in accordance with regulatory requirements.
