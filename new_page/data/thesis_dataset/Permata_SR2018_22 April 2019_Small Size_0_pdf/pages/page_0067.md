# Permata PhotoJournalist Grant

Permata PhotoJournalist Grant

Memasuki tahun kedelapan, Permata Photojournalist Grant (PPG) telah menginspirasi pewarta foto nusantara untuk terus menghasilkan karya cemerlang. Beberapa dari mereka telah menerbitkan buku foto, menggelar pameran, memenangi berbagai lomba foto dan aktif membedayakan komunitas melalui keahliannya.

PPG diinisiasi oleh PermataBank sejak tahun 2010, bekerja sama dengan lembaga bergengsi dunia, Pannafoto Institute dan didukung oleh Kedutaan Besar Belanda dan Erasmus Huis hingga kini. Tema besar PPG 2018 adalah 'Diversity' yang secara resmi dimulai pada 11 Desember 2018.

Kegiatan PPG meliputi workshop intensif, forum editor foto, training for trainers, hingga presentasi hasil akhir dalam bentuk pameran foto dan diskusi. Total ada 12 sesi workshop yang dipandu fotografer profesional kenamaan seperti Eric Santosa seorang Psikolog Budaya, mentor dari PannaFoto Institute, Sasa Kralj (Kroasia), Jenny Smets (Director of Photography majalah Vrij, Nederland) dan alumni PPG.

Di akhir program setiap tahun, PPG memilih seorang pewarta foto untuk memperoleh kesempatan memperdalam ilmu dan keterampilannya di bidang photo journalistik di Amsterdam melalui program "Erasmus Huis Fellowship to Amsterdam". Now entering its eighth year, the Permata Photo-journalist Grant (PPG) has inspired Indonesian's photo journalists to produce brilliant works. Some of them have published photo books, held exhibitions, won various photo competitions, and actively empowered the community through their expertise.

The PPG was initiated by PermataBank since 2010, in collaboration with a prestigious world institution, the Pannafoto Institute and supported by the Dutch Embassy and Erasmus Huis until now. The main theme of the 2018 PPG was 'Diversity', which officially started on 11th December, 2018.

PPG activities include intensive workshops, photo editor's forum, training of trainers, and a presentation of the final results in the form of photo exhibitions and discussions. In total, there were 12 workshop sessions guided by well-known professional photographers, including Eric Santosa, a Cultural Psychologist, mentoring from PannaFoto Institute, Sasa Kralj (Croatia), Jenny Smets (Director of Photography of Vrij magazine, Netherlands), and PPG alumni.

At the end of the program every year, PPG selects one photojournalist to gain opportunity to deepen his/her knowledge and skills in the field of photo journalism in Amsterdam through the "Erasmus Huis Fellowship to Amsterdam" program.
