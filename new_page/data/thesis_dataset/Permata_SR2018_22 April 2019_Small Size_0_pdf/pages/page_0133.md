# Manajemen Risiko

Risk Management

PermataBank menerapkan manajemen risiko adalah aktivitas secara terpadu, dari tahap awal hingga akhir, guna mengendalikan dan mengoptimalkan profil risiko terhadap pendapatannya. Penerapan manajemen merupakan aktivitas bank wide yang merupakan cerminan komitmen PermataBank terhadap prinsip kehati-hatian.

Di PermataBank, Manajemen Risiko dikelola melalui Risk Management Committee (RMC) dan Asset Liability Committee (ALCO) di tingkat Direksi dan Risk Monitoring Committee (RMoC) di tingkat Dewan Komisaris.

PermataBank menggunakan Tipe Risiko untuk memastikan proses identifikasi risiko konsisten dan komprehensif, di mana pun risiko tersebut muncul. Setiap Tipe Risiko dikelola oleh Risk Control Owner (RCO) yang ditunjuk oleh Direksi. Tipe Risiko yang dikelola oleh RCO adalah: Kredit, Pasar, Likuiditas Jangka Pendek, Likuiditas Struktural, Lintas Batas Negara, Permodalan, Reputasi, Strategik, Pensiun dan Operasional, Imbal Hasil pada Unit Usaha Syariah, Investasi Ekuitas pada Unit Usaha Syariah dan Transaksi Intra-Grup terkait dengan Konglomerasi Keuangan. RCO bertanggung jawab untuk menentukan standar pengendalian minimum dan memastikan tujuan pengendalian dapat terpenuhi.

PermataBank juga melaporkan pengelolaan sebelas jenis risiko berdasarkan peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan, yaitu: (1) Risiko Kredit, (2) Risiko Pasar, (3) Risiko Operasional, (4) Risiko Likuiditas, (5) Risiko Hukum, (6) Risiko Strategik, (7) Risiko Kepatuhan, (8) Risiko Reputasi, (9) Risiko Imbal Hasil, (10) Risiko Investasi, dan (11) Risiko Transaksi Intra-Grup.

The implementation of PermataBank's risk management is an integrated activity, from the first to the last phase, in order to control and optimize the risk profile against income. The implementation of risk management is a bank wide activity, which is a reflection of PermataBank's commitment to the principle of prudence.

At PermataBank, Risk Management is managed through the Risk Management Committee (RMC) and Asset Liability Committee (ALCO) at the Board of Directors level and Risk Monitoring Committee (RMoC) at the Board of Commissioners level.

PermataBank uses Risk Types to ensure the risk identification process is consistent and comprehensive, wherever the risk arises. Each Risk Type is managed by the Risk Control Owner (RCO). The RCO is appointed by the Board of Directors. Risk Types managed by the RCO are: Credit, Market, Short-Term Liquidity, Structural Liquidity, Cross Country Border, Capital, Reputation, Strategic, Pension and Operational, Rate of Return of the Shariah Business Unit, Equity Investment of the Shariah Business Unit, and Intra-Group Transactions related to Financial Conglomerate. The RCO is responsible for determining the minimum control standards and ensuring that the control objectives are met.

PermataBank also reports the 11 risk types based on OJK regulations, which are: (1) Credit Risk, (2) Market Risk, (3) Operational Risk, (4) Liquidity Risk, (5) Legal Risk, (6) Strategic Risk, (7) Compliance Risk, (8) Reputation Risk, (9) Rate of Return Risk, (10) Investment Risk, and (11) Intra-Group Transaction Risk to the management.
