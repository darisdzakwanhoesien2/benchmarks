Komitmen Kesehatan, Keselamatan Kerja, dan Lingkungan (K3L) PermataBank
Health, Occupational Safety, and Environmental (HSE) Commitment of PermataBank

# Strategi Pengelolaan K3L

HSE Management Strategy

PermataBank memiliki
departemen penuh waktu
yang mengurusi aspek
K3L di bawah Direktorat
Technology and Operations
(T&amp;O) di mana Direktur
T&amp;O bertindak sebagai
ketua Country HSE
Committee. Departemen
K3L ini menjalankan fungsi
kebijakan, pemantauan,
dan pengawasan di tataran
strategis PermataBank.

PermataBank has a dedicated full
time department to maintain HSE
under the Directorate of Technology
and Operations (T&amp;O), in which the
T&amp;O Director acts as the Chairman
of the Country HSE Committee. The
HSE Department operates the policy,
monitoring, and supervision functions
at the strategic level.
