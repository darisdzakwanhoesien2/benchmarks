# Komunikasi K3L

HSE Communication

PermataBank mengkomunikasikan K3L secara terus menerus untuk menjaga tingkat kesadaran PermataBankers atas risiko kesehatan, keselamatan kerja dan lingkungan di tempat kerja serta mendorong perilaku yang aman setiap waktu.

PermataBank provides HSE communication in a continuous manner to maintain the awareness level of PermataBankers regarding health, safety and environmental risks in the workplace as well as to encourage safe behavior at all times.

|  Media Komunikasi K3L
HSE Communication Media | HSE Info
Penyampaian informasi melalui surat elektronik yang dikirimkan kepada seluruh PermataBankers melalui media CRES News untuk meningkatkan kepedulian di bidang K3L.

Delivery of information through CRES News e-mails to all PermataBankers to raise awareness in the HSE sector. | HSE Notice
Media poster yang dipasang di setiap kantor cabang, di antaranya mengenai himbauan penghematan air, penghematan energi, penghematan bahan bakar minyak dan sebagainya.

Posters are distributed to each branch office, promoting concepts like water saving, energy savings, fuel oil savings among others.  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  HSE Talkshow
Media sosialisasi melalui kegiatan seminar untuk PermataBankers di kantor pusat dan kantor cabang utama.

Media socialization through seminars for PermataBankers at the head office and main branch offices. | Kampanye Digital K3L (CRES HSE Digital Communication)
Media televisi di kantor cabang utama Tower III Bintaro yang memuat materi panduan menghadapi kebakaran, panduan menghadapi gempa bumi, serta himbauan menghemat penggunaan air.

Television media in the main branch office of Tower III Bintaro, which presents guideline materials concerning fire anticipation, guidelines for earthquake anticipation, as well as the appeal to reduce water use. | Earth Hour Campaign
Pemasangan Earth Hour Campaign sebagai wallpaper untuk PC untuk jangka waktu tertentu yang berisi himbauan menjaga kelestarian bumi di antaranya dengan mengurangi penggunaan kertas atau mengurangi penggunaan listrik.

Setting up Earth Hour Campaign as a PC wallpaper for a certain period of time which contains a plea to protect the earth by reducing paper use or reducing electricity usage.  |
