# PermataGreen Movement

PermataBank yang tergabung dalam Grup Astra Financial Service menyelenggarakan kegiatan tahunan yang bertujuan mempromosikan pelestarian lingkungan bersama publik. Pada kegiatan tahun 2018, Grup Astra Financial Service melaksanakan program penghijauan lewat penanaman 1.500 bibit pohon dari berbagai jenis yang mulai langka, seperti Kawista, Kasturi, Rao, Mersawa, Meranti Merah, Meranti Kuning. Acara ini dilaksanakan di Kampus Universitas Indonesia, Depok.

PermataBank as part of Astra Financial Services Group held an annual event aimed at promoting environmental preservation with the public. In the 2018 activity, the Astra Financial Services Group implemented a reforestation program through the planting of 1,500 tree seedlings of various types, which are beginning to be endangered, such as Kawista, Kasturi, Rao, Mersawa, Meranti Merah, Meranti Kuning. The event was held at the University of Indonesia Campus, Depok.

![img-115.jpeg](img-115.jpeg)

Kegiatan penghijauan pertama kali yang dilakukan secara bersama-sama oleh 11 perusahaan, dihadiri oleh Director in Charge Astra Financial Services Suparno Djasmin dan 11 Direktur dari 11 perusahaan yang tergabung dalam Group Astra Financial serta civitas academica Universitas Indonesia.

The first reforestation activities carried out jointly by 11 companies, was attended by the Director in Charge Astra Financial Services Suparno Djasmin and 11 Directors from 11 companies who are members of the Astra Financial Group and the University of Indonesia academic community.
