Throughout 2018, PermataBank improved the implementation of governance practices throughout all levels of the organization, one of which was by reviewing the PRICE value system, with the addition of the main value system that must be strongly adhered to, namely "Integrity". The management believes that it is very important to add this new value at the beginning of PermataBank's PRICE values, because Integrity must be the main foundation. Thus, the new values of PermataBank become "I-PRICE" (Integrity, Partnership, Responsiveness, Innovation, Caring dan Excellence).

In terms of operations, the policy of PermataBank to implement a green banking operation remains the same. We have succeeded in reducing the utilization of natural resources in the form of energy, water, and the reduction of Greenhouse Gas Emissions by 2.79%.

The contribution of PermataBank to the surrounding community is reflected through the PermataHati programs that are engaged in three pillars of activities, which are Education, Enhancement, and Empowerment. PermataHati is also a means to improve the financial literacy of the community, this is performed through community education.

We pay attention to people with disabilities by developing a special program for them, which is PermataBRAVE (Because eveRyone is Able &amp; CreatIVE), as an interaction and learning forum among the drivers and the disability communities. In 2018, PermataBank is the first bank in Indonesia to support the activities of Balai Besar Rehabilitasi Vokasional Bina Daksa (BBRVD), which is managed by the Ministry of Social Affairs. Together with the BBRVD, EVO of PermataBankers empowered 85 people with disabilities for three months. We have mutually learned that limitations shall not reduce the spirit to gain achievement and they have the opportunity to continue to develop their vocational skills.
