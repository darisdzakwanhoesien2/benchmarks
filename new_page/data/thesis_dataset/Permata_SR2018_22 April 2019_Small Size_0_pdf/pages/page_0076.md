# 3. Pengembangan Karyawan dan Kepemimpinan yang Efektif serta Pengelolaan Hubungan Industrial yang Harmonis

## Pengembangan Karyawan dan Kepemimpinan

Di tahun 2018, HR meluncurkan berbagai terobosan dalam pengembangan kepemimpinan, antara lain Captain Forum sebagai pencanangan Organization Agility di PermataBank kepada para pimpinan di level one-down Direksi bekerja sama dengan Tim Astra International. Program ini meliputi Organization Agility Assessment yaitu mengukur tingkat kemampuan PermataBank untuk menjadi organisasi yang agile. Proses assessment tersebut menggunakan metode pendekatan kualitatif dan kuantitatif, yang didukung dengan pengambilan data dan sample melalui survey, evidence check dan wawancara kepada karyawan tetap dengan kriteria yang sesuai. Proses tersebut diselenggarakan pada Agustus-Oktober 2018. Hasil assessment diterjemahkan menjadi rencana tindak lanjut yang akan dilanjutkan pada tahun 2019.

Tahun 2018 ini, PermataBank kembali melakukan survey untuk mengukur efektivitas karyawan melalui pengukuran tingkat engagement dan enablement.

# 2. Alignment of the Value Systems, Culture and Management with the Vision, Mission and Strategy

2018 was also marked by the alignment of the Value, Culture and Management Systems with the new Vision, Mission, and Strategy, as well as implementing a comprehensive program that allows leaders and talents to develop behavior that fits the culture and values of PermataBank. Several initiatives related to this initiatives are:

- Launch of Vision, Mission and Values (VMV) as well as VMV Champion as VMV ambassador of PermataBank, which will disseminate the VMV internalization process in all layers of PermataBank organization. Throughout 2018, PermataBank also held regular work visits to various companies for benchmarking and explored insights concerning the management of values and work culture.
- Launch the new talent management system and framework by mapping competency characteristics, expected performance and sharper differences in determining potential employee involvement and leadership.

# 3. Effective Employee and Leadership Development as well as Harmonious Industrial Relations Management

## Employee Development and Leadership

In 2018, HR launched various breakthroughs in leadership development, including the Captain Forum as the launching of Organization's Agility at PermataBank for leaders at the one-down level of the Board of Directors in collaboration with the Astra International Team. This program includes the Organization's Agility Assessment, which measures PermataBank's ability to become an agile organization. The assessment process uses qualitative and quantitative approaches that are supported by data and sample collection through surveys, evidence checks, and interviews with permanent employees within certain criteria. The process was organized from August to October 2018. The assessment results are translated into follow-up plans that will be continued in 2019.
