# Penerapan Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Berkelanjutan

Implementation of Sustainable Corporate Governance

Penerapan Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik atau GCG (Good Corporate Governance) berperan penting dalam memelihara kepercayaan dan memberikan nilai tambah bagi para pemangku kepentingan. Dewan Komisaris, Direksi dan seluruh PermataBankers berkomitmen dalam mewujudkan sebuah organisasi yang transparan, akuntabel, bertanggung jawab, independen dan kewajaran, guna memastikan PermataBank menjadi Bank yang terpercaya.

Tujuan penerapan GCG di lingkungan PermataBank:

- Memaksimalkan nilai-nilai PermataBank dengan menerapkan lima prinsip GCG.
- Mewujudkan pengelolaan PermataBank yang profesional dan independen.
- Menciptakan proses pengambilan keputusan yang berlandaskan pada nilai moral yang tinggi dan sesuai dengan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku di semua elemen PermataBank.
- Melaksanakan tanggung jawab sosial perusahaan terhadap pemangku kepentingan terkait.
- Berkontribusi dalam meningkatkan iklim investasi yang kondusif di Indonesia khususnya di sektor keuangan dan perbankan.

Serangkaian upaya penyempurnaan GCG dan manajemen risiko terus dilakukan guna mengantisipasi dan menekan potensi risiko bisnis sehingga fundamental PermataBank terjaga dengan baik. Selama tahun 2018, PermataBank telah memperbaiki kualitas penerapan praktik-praktik GCG terbaik di seluruh lapisan organisasi melalui:

1. Perubahan susunan Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris PermataBank.
2. PermataBank meluncurkan Visi, Misi dan Values yang baru.
3. PermataBank tetap fokus pada penerapan budaya risiko serta penerapan fungsi kontrol internal di seluruh level: first, second maupun third line of defense.

The successful implementation of Good Corporate Governance (GCG) plays a major role in maintaining confidence and providing added value to the stakeholders. The Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, and all PermataBankers are committed to demonstrating transparency, accountability, responsibility, independence, and fairness, to ensure that PermataBank continues to strengthen its reputation as a trusted Bank.

The objectives of GCG implementation within PermataBank are as follows:

- To maximize the values of PermataBank by implementing the five principles of GCG.
- Establishing professional and independent management of PermataBank.
- Creating a decision making process that is based on high moral values, which is in accordance with all prevailing regulations that are applicable at every level of PermataBank.
- Implementing corporate social responsibility towards all relevant stakeholders.
- To contribute in improving a favorable investment climate in Indonesia, particularly in the financial and banking sectors.

A series of efforts designed to improve GCG and risk management continue being performed to anticipate and reduce potential business risks, allowing PermataBank's fundamentals to be well maintained. Throughout 2018, PermataBank has improved the quality of GCG practices implementation across all levels of the organization through:

1. Changes in the composition of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors of PermataBank.
2. PermataBank has launched its new Vision, Mission, and Values.
3. PermataBank continues to focus on the implementation of risk management and the implementation of internal control functions across all levels: first, second, and third line of defense.
