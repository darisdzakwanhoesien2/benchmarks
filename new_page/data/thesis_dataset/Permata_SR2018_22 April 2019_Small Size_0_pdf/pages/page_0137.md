# Manajemen Risiko Fraud

Fraud Risk Management

Risiko fraud merupakan salah satu tantangan penting dalam integritas bisnis, terutama dalam menyediakan produk dan layanan keuangan. Terjadinya fraud mengakibatkan dampak merugikan bagi nasabah, kerugian, mempengaruhi reputasi dan melukai kepercayaan yang telah dibangun PermataBank. [GRI 103-1]

PermataBank telah menerapkan Fraud Risk Management Framework (FRMF) yang meliputi aktivitas pencegahan, pendeteksian, pemberian respons dan efek jera tersebut ke dalam strategi yang lengkap untuk mengelola risiko fraud di PermataBank. FRMF dikelola unit kerja Fraud Risk Management (FRM) di bawah koordinasi Direktorat Hukum dan Kepatuhan. [GRI 103-2]

Dalam penerapan kebijakan FRMF, PermataBank melaksanakan Fraud Risk Assessment (FRA), yang merupakan salah satu komponen utama pengelolaan risiko fraud, pada unit/produk/proses/aktivitas tertentu yang memiliki risiko fraud yang cukup tinggi, baik yang terpantau dari kenaikan insiden fraud maupun penambahan volume yang signifikan. Tujuan FRA adalah memperoleh kajian berkelanjutan untuk dapat mengetahui keefektifan pencegahan, pendeteksian dan respons terhadap setiap potensi skema dan kejadian fraud. [GRI 103-3]

Fraud risk is one of the most important challenges in business integration, particularly in providing financial products and services. The occurrence of fraud has resulted in adverse impacts for our customers including losses, affecting our reputation and hurting the trust that PermataBank has built. [GRI 103-1]

PermataBank has implemented the Fraud Risk Management Framework (FRMF) that includes prevention, detection, response, and deterrent effect into a comprehensive strategy designed to manage Fraud Risk at PermataBank. FRMF is managed by the Fraud Risk Management (FRM) unit under the coordination of the Legal and Compliance Directorate. [GRI 103-2]

In the implementation of the FRMF policy, PermataBank implements the Fraud Risk Assessment (FRA), which is one of the main components of fraud risk management on particular units/products/processes/activities with a high risk of fraud, monitored to have both an increasing number of fraud incidents or significant increase in volume. The objective of the FRA is to acquire a continuous study in order to determine the effectiveness of prevention, detection, and response to any potential fraud schemes and occurrences. [GRI 103-3]
