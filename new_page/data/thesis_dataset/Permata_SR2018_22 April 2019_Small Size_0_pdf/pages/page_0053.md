About Permatahati

Pada semester kedua 2018, PermataBank terus mengembangkan program ini dengan mendirikan PermataBRAVE HUB, kurikulum perbankan dan literasi keuangan melalui kemitraan strategis dengan Balai Besar Rehabilitasi Vokasional Penyandang Disabilitas (BBRVPD) yang dikelola oleh Kementerian Sosial di Cibinong serta Institut Perbanas.

PermataBRAVE HUB diikuti oleh 85 penerima manfaat usia setara SMK dan berjalan selama 3 bulan (24 Agustus - 31 Oktober 2018). Acara kelulusannya dilaksanakan bertepatan dengan kelulusan penerima manfaat di BBRVPD. Usai mengikuti program ini, PermataBank juga memberikan kesempatan kerja bagi 7 lulusan terpilih dari BBRVPD.

In the second semester of 2018, PermataBank further developed its program for people with disabilities through the establishment of PermataBRAVE HUB, a dedicated banking and financial literacy curriculum in strategic partnership with the Vocational and Rehabilitation Centre for People with Disabilities (BBRVPD) under the Social Welfare Ministry in Cibinong, as well as with Perbanas Banking Institute.

PermataBRAVE HUB was followed by 85 beneficiaries equivalent to the Vocational School level and operated for 3 months (24 August - 31 October 2018). The graduation event was held to so as coincide with the graduation of the BBRVPD beneficiaries. After participating in this program, PermataBank also provided employment opportunities for 7 selected BBRVPD graduates.

Melalui PermataBRAVE Hub, PermataBank menjadi bank pertama yang menjadi mitra Balai Besar Rehabilitasi Vokasional Penyandang Disabilitas (BBRVPD) untuk memberikan pelatihan dan edukasi secara intensif. PermataBank bekerja sama dengan Perbanas Institute mengembangkan modul Perbankan dan ASTRA International untuk modul kewirausahaan serta EVO sebagai penggerak utama kegiatan ini.

Through PermataBRAVE Hub, PermataBank has now become the first bank to partner with the National Vocational Rehabilitation Center (BBRVPD) to provide intensive training and education. PermataBank cooperates with the Perbanas Institute in order to develop the Banking modules and ASTRA International for entrepreneurship modules, as well as our EVO as the main drivers of this activity.
