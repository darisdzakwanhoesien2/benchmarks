# PermataBankir CIlik
Young PermataBankers

Program PermataBankir Cilik merupakan salah satu program unggulan PermataHati yang telah terlaksana sejak tahun 2015 yang ditujukan untuk menciptakan "Duta Menabung" dengan harapan anak-anak sejak usia tingkat sekolah dasar dapat menanamkan dan menyeberlajukan kebiasaan menabung sejak usia dini. PermataBankir Cilik adalah program edukasi literasi keuangan dan perbankan yang diberikan dalam bentuk rangkaian pelatihan dan pembekalan selama tiga bulan kepada pelajar Sekolah Dasar Negeri, Sekolah Dasar Luar Biasa atau yang setara.

Tahun ini, 90 siswa/i usia 10-11 tahun terpilih mewakili 21 sekolah di area Tangerang Selatan. Selain melibatkan sekolah dan Sekolah Luar Biasa, di tahun ke-4 pelaksanaannya PermataBankir Cilik juga melibatkan sekolah sepak bola dan sekolah kejar paket agar lebih banyak lagi anak Indonesia yang melek literasi keuangan. Para peserta juga berkesempatan untuk mengetahui proses pembukaan tabungan, yaitu dengan membuka tabungan SimPel (Simpanan Pelajar) dari OJK.

Selain penambahan kategori sekolah, tahun ini PermataBankir Cilik juga secara aktif melibatkan guru-guru dari setiap sekolah sebagai guru pendamping program. Sejumlah 15 guru sebelumnya mengikuti Teacher's Training mengenai literasi keuangan dan modul "7 Habit of Highly Effective People" selama dua hari bekerja sama dengan Dunamis. Usai mengikuti pelatihan, para guru selanjutnya mengimplementasikan pengetahuan tersebut kepada peserta terpilih PermataBankir Cilik yang telah melalui seleksi.

PermataBankir Cilik dimulai dengan proyek "7 Habits of Highly Effective People" selama 21 hari bersama guru pendamping, kegiatan bootcamp selama dua hari diikuti proyek 30 hari "Duta Menabung", serta berbagai agenda menarik seperti field trip ke Museum Bank Indonesia dan kantor PermataBank untuk mengenal sejarah uang dan perbankan Indonesia. Mereka juga berkesempatan mengikuti berbagai workshop seperti lenong, fotografi, band dan vocal group. Ada juga kelas pelatihan menulis yang dibawakan oleh praktisi jurnalistik untuk melatih dan merangsang kreativitas mereka sebagai persiapan untuk menjadi Duta Menabung.

The PermataBankir Cilik program is a part of PermataHati flagship programs since 2015 aimed at creating "Savings Ambassadors" in the hopes young elementary students will be the drivers in instilling and spreading the habit of saving to their communities from an early age. PermataBankir Cilik is a financial and banking literacy education program provided in series of training and workshops for three months to the students of Public Elementary Schools, Special Needs Schools or the equivalent.

This year, 90 students aged 10-11 years of age were selected, representing 21 schools in South Tangerang area. In addition to involving Schools and Special Needs Schools, in its 4th year of implementation, PermataBankir Cilik also involved soccer schools and acceleration schools, to allow more Indonesian children to develop their financial literacy. The participants also had the opportunity to experience about the process of opening a savings account, facilitated through SimPel/Simpanan Pelajar (9Student Savings) powered by OJK.

In addition to the school category, this year PermataBankir Cilik also actively involving teachers from each school as the program facilitator. A total of 15 teachers have previously joining the Teacher's Training regarding financial literacy and "7 Habits of Highly Effective People" module for two days in collaboration with Dunamis. After attending the training, the teachers then implemented the knowledge with the selected PermataBankir Cilik participants who had successfully gone through the selection process.

PermataBankir Cilik was started by the "7 Habits of Highly Effective People" project for 21 days with the accompanying teacher, bootcamp activities for two days, then followed by the 30-day "Savings Ambassadors" project, and various other interesting agendas, including a field trip to the Bank Indonesia Museum and PermataBank office to learn about Indonesian money and banking history. They are also equipped with various creative workshops such as lenong, photography, band, and vocal groups. There is also a writing class hosted by journalist designed to train and stimulate their creativity in preparation for becoming a Saving Ambassador.
