# Building Risk Assessment (BRA)

Building Risk Assessment (BRA)

Penilaian Risiko Gedung (Building Risk Assessment) di seluruh kantor operasional PermataBank untuk memastikan bahwa risiko-risiko operasional yang terkait dengan isu kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja dapat diidentifikasi, dikelola serta ditindaklanjuti.

Pada tahun 2018, BRA diadakan di 325 kantor bank terdapat sebanyak 519 temuan risiko kesehatan, keselamatan kerja dengan total 489 isu yang ditemukan telah diselesaikan secara efektif.

Building Risk Assessment at all PermataBank operational offices to ensure that operational risks related to occupational health and safety issues could be identified, managed and followed-up.

In 2018, the BRA was conducted at 325 branch offices and identified 519 occupational health and safety risk findings and 489 identified issues have been effectively resolved.

# Kompetensi K3 PermataBankers

PermataBankers OHS Competency

PermataBank mengadakan pelatihan internal maupun eksternal di bidang K3. Pelatihan ini ditujukan untuk meningkatkan kompetensi dan kesadaran PermataBankers dalam mencegah dan mengelola risiko-risiko kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja di tempat kerjanya serta kemampuan untuk mengantisipasi keadaan darurat.

Sepanjang tahun 2018 telah dilakukan pelatihan K3 dengan data sebagai berikut:

a. 8 kegiatan pelatihan Pertolongan Pertama Pada Kecelakaan yang diikuti oleh 155 PermataBankers dengan rincian:

- First Aid Training level 1 sebanyak 6 kali pertemuan di Jakarta dan Medan.
- First Aid Training level 2 sebanyak 1 kali pertemuan di Jakarta.
- First Aid Refreshment Training sebanyak 1 kali pertemuan di Jakarta.

PermataBank conducts internal and external training in the field of OHS. The training is intended to improve the competence and awareness of PermataBankers in preventing and managing occupational health and safety risks in the workplace as well as having the ability to overcome emergency occurrences.

Throughout 2018 has been conducted OHS trainings as follows:

a. 8 First Aid trainings followed by 155 PermataBankers:

- Level 1 First Aid Training, 6 batches conducted at Jakarta and Medan.
- Level 2 First Aid Training, 1 batch at Jakarta.
- First Aid Refreshment Training, 1 batch at Jakarta.
