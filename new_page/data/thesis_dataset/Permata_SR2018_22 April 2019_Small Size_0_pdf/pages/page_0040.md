About The Report

Sebagai pelaksanaan RAKB, PermataBank berupaya untuk meningkatkan portofolio pembiayaan kepada segmen Usaha Kecil Menengah (UKM) dan mikro produktif, termasuk di dalamnya adalah pembiayaan kegiatan-kegiatan yang memenuhi kriteria kegiatan usaha yang berkelanjutan. PermataBank menuangkan rencana pengembangan portofolio keuangan berkelanjutan ini dalam RAKB yang relevan dengan masing-masing unit bisnis.

As the implementation of RAKB, PermataBank strives to increase the financing portfolio to the productive and micro small and medium enterprises (SMEs) segment, including financing activities included in the sustainable business activities criteria. PermataBank sets out the sustainable financial portfolio development plan in the RAKB that is relevant to each business unit.

- Pertumbuhan portofolio ini dilakukan melalui optimalisasi hubungan dengan nasabah-nasabah inti yang telah ada, serta secara selektif menambah calon nasabah baru dan potensial pada sektor industri yang memiliki prospek baik dengan risiko yang lebih rendah.

The growth of this portfolio is carried out through optimizing relationships with our existing core customers, as well as selectively adding new and potential prospective customers to the industrial sector with good prospects and lower risks.

In connection with the current Bank Business Plan and future development, Retail banking (RB) has the products, services and initiatives that are related to the process of sustainable service.

1. Channeling cooperation with one of the microfinance institutions in order to assist in microfinance for people in the informal economy and the impact of the payment financial industry.
2. Financing for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) for businesses related to the economic sector of non-metal goods recycling and businesses related to other metal and block collection processes, sector.
3. Development of digital banking for account opening and banking transactions, including communication with customers through electronic channels that aim to provide services which are simple, fast and reliable to customers, and will significantly reduce paper usage at the same time.

The Shariah Business Unit establishes PermataBank's business strategy plan for sustainable finance through products and services that can be utilized to meet the social aspects in addition to meeting the demands of the customers for financial products and services.
