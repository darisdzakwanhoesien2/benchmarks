# E-learning

E-learning

PermataBankers menggunakan platform pelatihan e-learning untuk menjangkau PermataBankers yang cukup banyak dan tersebar di hampir ke seluruh Indonesia. E-learning pelatihan dan sosialisasi yang berkaitan dengan etika dan integritas di PermataBank mencakup fraud awareness, budaya kepatuhan, dan Kode Etik Perilaku Karyawan. [GRI 205-5]

PermataBankers use the e-learning training platform to reach the large number and geographically spread out team of PermataBankers across Indonesia. E-learning training and socialization relating to ethics and integrity at PermataBank includes fraud awareness, compliance culture, and Employee Code of Conduct. [GRI 205-5]
