# Melayani dengan Tanggung Jawab
Serving with Responsibility

PermataBank memberikan informasi produk dan jasa secara utuh dan transparan. Setiap penjualan produk, PermataBank menjelaskan manfaat, risiko serta biaya atas produk atau layanan kepada nasabah agar nasabah memperoleh produk atau layanan yang memang dibutuhkannya.

PermataBank juga telah menerapkan kebijakan yang sangat ketat untuk melindungi kerahasiaan data nasabah, sejalan dengan peraturan OJK. Untuk melindungi kerahasiaan, PermataBank menggunakan pendekatan teknologi dan pendekatan proses untuk memastikan tidak terjadi pelanggaran atas kerahasiaan data nasabah. [GRI 103-2]

Selama masa pelaporan tahun 2018, tidak terjadi pelanggaran terkait pelabelan produk dan jasa keuangan, pelanggaran atas privasi pelanggan dan penarikan produk. [GRI 417-2, 418-1]

## Pelayanan Nasabah

Layanan pelanggan yang efektif dan berkualitas tinggi dan manajemen keluhan sebagai bagian dari manajemen risiko reputasi. Untuk itu PermataBank memberikan kemudahan bagi para pemangku kepentingan untuk mengakses berbagai informasi mengenai PermataBank melalui situs web www.permatabank.com, seperti informasi tentang finansial dan perusahaan, publikasi (press release), produk dan aksi korporasi.

Untuk mendukung peningkatan kualitas layanan dan keunggulan operasional, bank memiliki unit Customer Care, Service Quality, dan Contact Center.

PermataTel adalah layanan Contact Center 24 jam sehari, 7 hari seminggu untuk memenuhi kebutuhan nasabah akan informasi produk, layanan perbankan dan program marketing PermataBank, permintaan/pengajuan layanan dan keluhan/pengaduan kualitas produk/layanan melalui telepon, surat elektronik serta media sosial. [GRI 103-2]

PermataBank provides information regarding its products and services in a full and transparent manner. For every product sale, PermataBank explains the benefits, risks, and costs of the products or services to the customers, allowing the customers to obtain the products or services that they really require.

PermataBank has also implemented a very strict policy to protect the confidentiality of customer data, in line with OJK regulations. To protect confidentiality, PermataBank uses a technological approach and process approach to ensure that there is no breach of confidentiality of customer data. [GRI 103-2]

Throughout the 2018 reporting period, there was no infringement of financial product and services labelling, infringement of customer privacy, and product recall. [GRI 417-2, 418-1]

## Customer Service

Effective and high quality customer service as well as complaint management is part of our reputation risk management. Therefore, PermataBank provides convenience to the stakeholders to access various information regarding PermataBank through the website www.permatabank.com, including financial and corporate information, publications and press releases, products, and corporate actions.

To support the improvement of service quality and operational excellence, the bank has Customer Care, Service Quality, and Contact Center units.

PermataTel is a 24 hours a day, 7 days a week Contact Center service provided to meet the customers' demands for PermataBank's product information, banking services and marketing programs, request/submit services, as well as complaints concerning product/services quality through telephone, electronic mail, and social media. [GRI 103-2]
