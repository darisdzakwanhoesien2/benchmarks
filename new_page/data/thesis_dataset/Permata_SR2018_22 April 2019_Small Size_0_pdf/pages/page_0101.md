# Astra Green Company

Astra Green Company

Pengelolaan K3L di PermataBank tidak terlepas dari kebijakan dan arahan pemegang saham utama yaitu PT Astra International Tbk dan Standard Chartered Bank. Dengan demikian terdapat inisiatif-inisiatif dari keduanya yang diterapkan di seluruh kantor PermataBank untuk mencapai tujuan bersama di bidang K3L. [GRI 103-1]

Salah satu cara untuk mengukur efektivitas pengelolaan K3L, PermataBank menggunakan Astra Green Company (AGC) yang ditetapkan oleh PT Astra International Tbk. Audit AGC memeriksa penerapan standar pengelolaan K3L dikombinasikan dengan peraturan dan persyaratan K3L di Indonesia. Hasil pemeriksaan AGC ditetapkan dalam rating yang meliputi hitam, merah, biru, hijau, dan emas. [GRI 103-3]

HSE Management at PermataBank cannot be separated from the policies and directives of the major shareholders, namely PT Astra International Tbk and Standard Chartered Bank. Thus, there are initiatives from both, which are implemented throughout PermataBank's office to achieve the common goals in the field of HSE. [GRI 103-1]

PermataBank uses the Astra Green Company (AGC) set by PT Astra International Tbk as one of the methods to measure the effectiveness of HSE management. AGC's audit examines the application of HSE management standards combined with the HSE regulations and requirements in Indonesia. The AGC inspection results are set in ratings, which include black, red, blue, green and gold. [GRI 103-3]
