# PermataYouthPreneur [GRI 413-1]
PermataYouthPreneur [GRI 413-1]

![img-67.jpeg](img-67.jpeg)

Usaha rintisan (Startup) berbasis Financial Technology (FinTech) di Indonesia kian marak dan menjadi salah satu landasan bagi wirausaha muda masa depan. Di era digital seperti sekarang ini, bisnis FinTech menjadi daya tarik tersendiri bagi anak-anak generasi millennial, sejalan dengan makin berkembangnya e-commerce dan situs portal media yang telah menjadi gaya hidup kekinian.

PermataBank melihat bahwa potensi ini juga akan membutuhkan wirausaha muda yang dapat memanfaatkan pertumbuhan usaha rintisan tersebut ke tingkatan selanjutnya. Di sinilah PermataHati mengambil peran meluncurkan program inovatif PermataYouthPreneur (PYP) - program pelatihan kewirausahaan bagi generasi "Jaman Now", khususnya siswa/i SMK jurusan RPL (Rekayasa Perangkat Lunak) selama periode tiga bulan pembelajaran.

Financial Technology (FinTech) based startups in Indonesia are increasingly prevalent and are developing into one of the foundations for future young entrepreneurs. In this digital era, FinTech businesses becoming the main attraction for the millennial generation, in line with the growth of e-commerce and media portal sites that have promoted a more contemporary lifestyle.

PermataBank identifies that this potential will also require young entrepreneurs who can take part on bringing the Indonesian startup growth further. This is where PermataHati took the role of launching the innovative PermataYouthPreneur (PYP) program - an entrepreneurship training program for the "Generation NOW" particularly vocational students majoring in Software Engineering during three-months period.
