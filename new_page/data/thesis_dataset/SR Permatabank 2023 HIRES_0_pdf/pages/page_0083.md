# KESELAMATAN &amp; KESEHATAN KERJA

PermataBank terus berkolaborasi menciptakan cara-cara kerja baru sehingga kami dapat terus memberikan standar pelayanan terbaik kepada nasabah. Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3) menjadi prioritas tertinggi kami untuk meningkatkan kinerja. Melalui Departemen Security, Safety &amp; Support Management (SSSM) yang berada di bawah Divisi Corporate Real Estate Services (CRES), Direktorat Technology &amp; Operations (T&amp;O), kami mengelola kegiatan K3 sehingga dapat memberikan lingkungan kerja yang aman dan nyaman bagi PermataBankers, nasabah, mitra, dan pemangku kepentingan lainnya.

PermataBank menetapkan visi K3 sebagai "Perseroan yang sehat, aman dan ramah lingkungan untuk mencapai Bisnis Berkelanjutan".

Tujuan pengelolaan K3 sebagai berikut:

a. Mencegah insiden dan kecelakaan kerja, menjaga kesehatan, dan keselamatan kerja.
b. Mencegah pencemaran lingkungan.
c. Memastikan penggunaan konsumsi sumber daya alam (penggunaan energi dan air) dengan efektif dan optimal.
d. Meningkatkan kualitas lingkungan.
e. Mematuhi peraturan kesehatan, keselamatan dan lingkungan yang berlaku.

# OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH &amp; SAFETY

PermataBank remains committed to working collaboratively to develop new ways of working to improve our service standards and provide the highest level of quality to our valued customers. Our primary concern for performance improvement is Occupational Safety &amp; Health (OHS). As a result, we diligently oversee our OHS activities through the Security, Safety, and Support Management (SSSM) Department, which is part of the Division of Corporate Real Estate Services (CRES), the Directorate of Technology &amp; Operations (T&amp;O), to create a safe and conducive working environment for PermataBankers, customers, partners, and other stakeholders.

The OHS vision of PermataBank is to become "a healthy, safe, and environmentally friendly company to achieve Sustainable Business."

The following are the objectives of OHS management in the Bank:

a. Preventing workplace incidents and accidents, while maintaining occupational health and safety.
b. Mitigating environmental pollution.
c. Ensuring effective and optimal use of natural resource consumption (energy and water use).
d. Enhancing environmental quality.
e. Adhering to relevant health, safety, and environmental regulations.
