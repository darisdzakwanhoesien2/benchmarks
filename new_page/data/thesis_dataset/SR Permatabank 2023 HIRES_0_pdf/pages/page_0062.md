## 7. Manajemen Risiko Pihak Ketiga

Berkaitan dengan adanya risiko ancaman keamanan informasi dari Pihak Eksternal, saat ini PermataBank memiliki proses penilaian risiko keamanan informasi untuk Pihak Ketiga (Third Party Security Assessment/TPSA) yang dilakukan secara berkala. Hal ini dilakukan untuk mengenali profil keamanan informasi terhadap organisasi Pihak Ketiga tersebut meliputi people, process, dan technology. Penilaian risiko pihak ketiga ini dilakukan melalui metode pengisian kuesioner, interview, dan/atau site visit.

PermataBank telah memiliki kebijakan yang mengatur mekanisme kerja sama dengan pihak ketiga dan pengaturan hak akses pihak ketiga serta telah menggunakan format kontrak kerja sama yang sudah mencakup klausul kerahasiaan. Sebelum PermataBank mengirimkan dokumen kepada pihak ketiga, pihak ketiga harus menandatangani dokumen Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

## Security Awareness

Kesadaran akan pentingnya keamanan siber ditanamkan kepada karyawan melalui penerapan kebijakan keamanan informasi, pengingat berkala atas kebijakan keamanan informasi, pengumuman berita keamanan informasi serta sharing session/seminar mengenai keamanan informasi umum.

PermataBank secara rutin mengirimkan security awareness ke seluruh PermataBankers melalui media surat elektronik, e-Learning, online training yang dilakukan dengan berkoordinasi dengan unit HR. Sosialisasi mengenai information security juga dilakukan secara berkala dan hasil pencapaiannya dilaporkan ke forum komite risiko.

## 7. Third-party Risk Management

PermataBank currently employs a periodic information security risk assessment process for Third Parties (TPSA) to address potential information security threats posed by external entities. This process aims to identify Third Party organizations' information security profile, which includes people, processes, and technology. Questionnaires, interviews, and/or on-site visits are used in the assessment.

PermataBank has established a policy governing third-party collaboration mechanisms, outlining third-party access rights and utilizing a cooperation contract format that includes a confidentiality clause. Before sharing documents with third parties, the third parties must sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

## Security Awareness

The importance of cybersecurity is instilled in employees through the implementation of information security policies, regular reminders of these policies, announcements about information security news, and sharing sessions or general information security seminars.

PermataBank consistently disseminates security awareness among all employees through electronic mail, e-Learning, and online training in collaboration with HR unit. There are also periodic information security socialization events, the results of which are reported to the risk committee forum.

## Capacity Building for HR in Cybersecurity

PermataBank prioritizes cybersecurity literacy among its employees by requiring mandatory Information Security Awareness training, which is delivered via e-learning and reinforced by e-mail communications on Information Security Awareness.

The comprehensive cybersecurity training program, which is accessible through PermataBank's e-learning portal, is available to all staff members, including the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners. Employees in cybersecurity-related work units receive training that is tailored to their specific roles and responsibilities or certifications.
