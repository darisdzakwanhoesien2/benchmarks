## Employee Engagement Survey

PermataBank conducts regular assessments of their engagement levels to evaluate the effectiveness of their management strategy. This is accomplished through the annual Change for Better Survey, which is conducted on a regular basis by an external global consultant. In 2023, 95% of PermataBankers responded to the survey, yielding an engagement score of 85%. Within this score, 94% of employees expressed pride in being a part of PermataBank, and 89% agreed that the Bank provides an environment conducive to making meaningful contributions. Notably, the overall employee engagement survey score increased by 3% over the previous year.

## Work-life Balance

Penerapan work-life balance di PermataBank dilakukan melalui program olahraga Permata Olympics; yang merupakan upaya meningkatkan efektivitas dan produktivitas PermataBankers, serta penerapan budaya hidup sehat dan seimbang. Seluruh PermataBankers diharapkan dapat lebih optimal dalam menjalankan setiap aktivitas, baik dalam lingkungan kerja maupun kehidupan pribadi. Pada tahun 2023, program Permata Olympics diikuti lebih dari 1.400 PermataBankers.

## Serikat Pekerja dan Perjanjian Kerja Bersama

PermataBank menghormati hak-hak PermataBankers untuk berserikat dan membentuk serikat pekerja di lingkungan Bank. Bank memiliki Serikat Pekerja Bank Permata (SPBP) yang mewakili PermataBankers untuk melaksanakan komunikasi dua arah dengan manajemen Bank. Setiap dua bulan, manajemen Bank bersama Serikat Pekerja membahas isu terkait ketenagakerjaan dalam rangka menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang kondusif.

Manajemen Bank dan Serikat Pekerja Bank telah bersama-sama menyepakati Perjanjian Kerja Bersama (PKB) yang mengatur hak dan kewajiban PermataBank dan PermataBankers secara hukum. PKB PermataBank telah mencakup seluruh (100%) PermataBankers.

## Work-life Balance

Through the Permata Olympics sports program, PermataBank promotes work-life balance. This initiative aims to increase the effectiveness and productivity of PermataBankers while also promoting a healthy and balanced lifestyle. All PermataBankers are encouraged to improve their professional and personal performance. Over 1,400 PermataBankers participated in the Permata Olympics program in 2023.

## Trade Unions and Collective Labour Agreements

PermataBank respects PermataBankers' rights to associate and form labor unions within the Bank. Permata Bank Workers Union (SPBP) represents PermataBankers and facilitates two-way communication with the Bank's management. The Bank's management and the Workers' Union meet every two months to discuss labor-related issues in order to create a pleasant working environment.

The Bank's management and the Labour Union have reached an agreement on a Collective Labour Agreement (CLA) that governs PermataBank and PermataBankers' legal rights and obligations. PermataBank's PKB has reached 100% of PermataBankers.
