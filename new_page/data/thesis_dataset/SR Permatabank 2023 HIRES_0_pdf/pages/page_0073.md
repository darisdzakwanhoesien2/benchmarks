Untuk memfasilitasi proses pembelajaran PermataBankers, PermataBank memiliki platform digital learning, yaitu Permata Learning (PEARL) yang mengintegrasikan event pelatihan/ seminar, materi video, materi gamification, proses coaching, mentoring, dan pengalaman belajar PermataBankers ke dalam satu aplikasi. Selain itu, PermataBank terus memperbaiki kemampuan dalam penggunaan platform online, seperti Instagram Live, Instagram TV, dan learning studio.

PermataBank menyelenggarakan acara Permata Learning Week pada bulan Agustus 2023 dengan mengusung tema 'Creating Impact Together'. Acara ini dilaksanakan setiap tahun dengan terus mengembangkan topik pelatihan dan infrastruktur pendukungnya. Total peserta yang mengikuti acara ini adalah sebanyak 11.318 staf, meningkat 371 staf (3,4%) dibandingkan tahun lalu.

PermataBank's digital learning platform, Permata Learning (PEARL), facilitates PermataBankers in their learning process. This platform unifies training events, seminars, video materials, gamification content, coaching, mentoring, and the overall learning experience. Furthermore, PermataBank is honing its skills in the use of online platforms such as Instagram Live, Instagram TV, and the learning studio.

PermataBank organized the Permata Learning Week event in August 2023, with the theme 'Creating Impact Together.' This annual event seeks to advance training topics while also improving supporting infrastructure. The total number of participants who took part in this event was 11,318 staff, an increase of 371 staff (3.4%) compared to last year.

## Biaya Pelatihan

PermataBank menyediakan anggaran pelatihan yang jumlahnya ditentukan berdasarkan kebutuhan dengan tetap mempertimbangkan kemampuan keuangan Bank setiap tahunnya. Di tahun 2023, Bank mengeluarkan biaya program pelatihan dan pengembangan sebesar Rp55.305 juta atau meningkat 11,2% dari tahun sebelumnya yang sebesar Rp49.754 juta.

## Training Expenses

PermataBank allocates a training budget, the amount of which is determined annually, taking into account identified needs as well as the Bank's financial capacity. In 2023, he Bank incurred training and development program expenses amounting to IDR55,305 million or an increase of 11.2% from the previous year which amounted to IDR49,754 million.
