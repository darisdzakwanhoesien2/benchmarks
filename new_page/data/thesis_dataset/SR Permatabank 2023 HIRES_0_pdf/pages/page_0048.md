## INOVASI DIGITAL

Inovasi digital adalah salah satu inisiatif penting dalam aksi keuangan berkelanjutan PermataBank, karena melalui produk dan layanan keuangan digital, masyarakat Indonesia berpeluang lebih baik untuk mendapatkan layanan keuangan inklusif, formal, berkeadilan, dan bertanggung jawab. Upaya ini diwujudkan oleh PermataBank dengan berinvestasi dalam kapabilitas digital baik di bidang ritel, UKM, wholesale dan syariah untuk memenuhi brand promise sebagai Bank yang simple, fast, dan reliable.

Dalam aksi keuangan berkelanjutan, PermataBank mengembangkan produk dan layanan keuangan digital untuk nasabah individu, bisnis, dan ekosistem bank digital. Produk bank digital yang diperuntukkan untuk memperluas akses layanan keuangan dilakukan melalui pengembangan produk QR Pay, API banking, mobile banking, internet banking PermataNet, digital value chain, model branch, serta super app PermataMobile X yang memiliki lebih dari 200 fitur unggulan.

PermataBank applies effective risk management to control and optimize the risk profile of income every time it decides on a loan decision. PermataBank and its loan distribution partners have committed to reducing NPLs through a restructuring and more selective approach consistent with the bank's policies and procedures.

## DIGITAL INNOVATION

Digital innovation is an important initiative in PermataBank's sustainable financial actions because financial products and services provide Indonesians with inclusive, formal, fair, and responsible financial services. PermataBank is demonstrating this effort by investing in digital capabilities in the retail, SME, wholesale, and sharia sectors in order to fulfill the Bank brand promise of being simple, fast, and reliable.

PermataBank develops digital financial products and services for individual customers, businesses, and the digital banking ecosystem as part of its commitment to sustainable financial action. Through the development of QR Pay products, banking API, mobile banking, PermataNet internet banking, digital value chain, branch models, and the PermataMobile X super app, digital bank products broaden access to financial services.
