In collaboration with PiBo, (an Indonesian children's digital library platform), throughout the program PermataHati introduced participating students to financial journaling using the simple 3-in money management concept (Tabung-in, Jajan-in, and Bagi-in), and instilling the practice of consistent saving in their daily lives, while taking part in engaging and interactive classes. Students are immersed in insightful experiences as part of the program, such as virtual tours of the Bank Indonesia Museum and PermataBank branches, which provide a deeper understanding of money and banking in Indonesia.

The curriculum also includes classes that promote creativity and confidence, such as Creative Thinking and Public Speaking. Online book reading challenges on bacapibo.com platform, featuring a leaderboard, motivate participants to complete their reading assignments and engage in healthy competition.

PermataBankir Cilik introduced new environmental material to students in 2023. The program, developed in collaboration with Bumi Buddies, a youth organization dedicated to environmental education, included an introduction to waste management classes, as well as a virtual tour of a waste bank.

The online implementation of the PermataBankir Cilik 2023 program continued, attracting 150 students from 16 public elementary schools and special schools in Bandung, Semarang, Surakarta, Jogjakarta, Surabaya, Malang, Jombang, Bojonegoro, and Denpasar.
