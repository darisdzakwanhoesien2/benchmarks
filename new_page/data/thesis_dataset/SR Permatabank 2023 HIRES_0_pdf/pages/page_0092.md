# MEMBERDAYAKAN MASYARAKAT

Sebagai salah satu penyedia layanan keuangan di Indonesia, PermataBank memiliki peranan penting untuk menciptakan pembangunan nasional yang berkeadilan demi memeratakan kesejahteraan bagi seluruh lapisan masyarakat. Melalui inisiatif tanggung jawab sosial, PermataBank turut membantu masyarakat mencapai tujuan ini secara berkelanjutan melalui rangkaian program, sekaligus mewujudkan nilai 'Caring' atau 'Peduli' yang merupakan bagian dari tata nilai PermataBank melalui partisipasi aktif para karyawannya.

# PERMATAHATI

Program-program pemberdayaan masyarakat PermataBank dilaksanakan melalui 'PermataHati', yang merupakan wujud dari komitmen Bank dalam meningkatkan literasi dan inklusi keuangan di Indonesia dengan membangun ekosistem yang berkelanjutan, suportif, dan kolaboratif bagi seluruh lapisan masyarakat, termasuk penyandang disabilitas.

Dikelola oleh Unit Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) yang bertanggung jawab dalam perencanaan strategis, anggaran, dan kemitraan setiap tahunnya.

Program-program PermataHati dijalankan melalui tiga pilar utama, yaitu Pendidikan (Education), Pemberdayaan (Empowerment), dan Peningkatan Fasilitas dan Inklusi (Enhancement). Kegiatan yang dijalankan mencakup berbagai penerima manfaat, seperti siswa dari tingkat pendidikan dasar, menengah/kejuruan, dan sekolah luar biasa; guru, generasi muda, perempuan, serta penyandang disabilitas. Para penerima manfaat diharapkan dapat semakin berdaya, siap bersaing, dan cerdas secara finansial.

PermataHati sepanjang tahun membuka peluang bagi karyawan PermataBank atau 'PermataBankers' untuk mengekspresikan kepeduliannya terhadap sesama dengan menjadi relawan atau Employee Volunteers (EVO) dalam berbagai aktivitas CSR. Di tahun 2023, PermataHati dilaksanakan dalam dua kegiatan besar, yaitu Program Inklusi dan Pemberdayaan Perempuan, serta program Banking for Students yang berfokus pada penyebaran edukasi literasi keuangan untuk siswa/i sekolah pada berbagai jenjang. Selain itu, program lainnya yang dijalankan meliputi pemberian beasiswa, donasi kemanusiaan, serta kegiatan advokasi lainnya bekerja sama dengan beragam mitra dan pemangku kepentingan.

# EMPOWERING COMMUNITIES

PermataBank, as a prominent financial service provider in Indonesia, plays an important role in fostering inclusive national development to improve the well-being of all segments of society. By implementing Corporate Social Responsibility initiatives, PermataBank is striving to achieve this goal through various programs, while also manifesting the 'Caring' value, one of PermataBank's core principles, through employees' active participation in the programs.

# PERMATAHATI

PermataBank's community empowerment initiatives are realized through 'PermataHati,' a tangible manifestation of the Bank's commitment to improving financial literacy and inclusion in Indonesia by way of developing a long-term, supportive, and collaborative ecosystem for all societal strata, including people with disabilities.

Managed by the Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Unit, which is in charge of strategic planning, budgeting, and annual partnerships.

PermataHati operates through three main pillars: Education, Empowerment, and Enhancement of Facilities and Inclusivity. The various activities serve a wide range of beneficiaries, including students in primary, secondary/vocational, and special education, as well as teachers, young people, women, and people with disabilities. The overarching goal is to empower these beneficiaries, preparing them to compete and become financially savvy.

Throughout the year, PermataHati offers PermataBank employees or PermataBankers the opportunity to demonstrate their compassion by serving as volunteers or Employee Volunteers (EVO) in a variety of CSR initiatives. PermataHati successfully completed two significant activities in 2023: the Women's Inclusion and Empowerment program, and the Banking for Students program, both of which focused on the dissemination of financial literacy education to students of various educational levels. Furthermore, other programs include scholarships, humanitarian contributions, and advocacy activities carried out in collaboration with diverse partners and stakeholders.
