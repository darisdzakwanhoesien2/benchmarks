# 3. Employee Volunteers (EVO)

Selain melalui program CERITA, sepanjang tahun PermataBankers berkesempatan untuk berkontribusi terhadap kegiatan CSR dengan menjadi Employee Volunteers (EVO). PermataBankers bisa menjadi EVO dengan terlibat langsung dalam pengajaran literasi keuangan dasar dan memberikan bantuan perbaikan fasilitas di sekolah-sekolah yang membutuhkan. Mereka juga bisa ikut dalam sejumlah kegiatan relawan di aktivitas-aktivitas pengajaran mitra PermataHati.

Pada tahun 2023, PermataBankers memberikan pelatihan literasi keuangan kepada total 1.700 penerima manfaat di seluruh Indonesia.

CERITA activities take place on the same day and date in each city where PermataBank has a presence, with Employee Volunteers (EVO) serving as key facilitators. # 3. Employee Volunteers (EVO)

In addition to the CERITA program, PermataBankers can participate in CSR activities throughout the year by becoming Employee Volunteers (EVO). PermataBankers can serve as EVO by actively participating in the teaching of fundamental financial literacy and providing assistance to schools in need. They are also encouraged to participate in various volunteer initiatives related to the educational activities of PermataHati partners.

PermataBankers delivered financial literacy training to 1,700 beneficiaries nationwide by 2023.
