## 2. Direksi

Direksi bertanggung jawab penuh atas pengurusan PermataBank untuk kepentingan dan tujuan PermataBank serta mewakili PermataBank sesuai dengan ketentuan Anggaran Dasar PermataBank dan peraturan yang berlaku.

## 3. Komite-komite di bawah Dewan Komisaris

Komite-komite yang dibentuk di bawah Dewan Komisaris bertugas untuk membantu fungsi pengawasan oleh Dewan Komisaris terdiri atas Komite Audit, Komite Pemantau Risiko, dan Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi.

## 4. Komite-komite di bawah Direksi

Komite-komite yang dibentuk di bawah Direksi bertugas untuk membantu Direksi dalam mengimplementasikan visi dan misi PermataBank serta membantu Direksi dalam melakukan pengurusan PermataBank terdiri atas Komite Manajemen Risiko, Komite Kredit, Komite Asset &amp; Liability, Komite Pengarah Teknologi Informasi, Komite Pensiun, dan Komite Keberlanjutan.

PermataBank telah mengintegrasikan keuangan keberlanjutan dalam organ tata kelola dengan membentuk dua organ penting yang fokus untuk mempersiapkan, menerapkan, memantau keuangan berkelanjutan di PermataBank. Pada 2023, PermataBank memperkuat pelaksanaan keuangan berkelanjutan dengan membentuk Unit Keberlanjutan yang langsung berada di bawah Direktorat Direktur Utama serta Komite Keberlanjutan yang beranggotakan seluruh anggota Direksi PermataBank. Komite Keberlanjutan mendukung Direksi dalam menetapkan arahan strategis keberlanjutan PermataBank termasuk Rencana Aksi Keuangan Berkelanjutan serta pengambilan keputusan penting saat mengintegrasikan aspek-aspek ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) dalam bentuk kebijakan Bank.

## 2. Board of Directors

The Board of Directors bears complete responsibility for the management of PermataBank, safeguarding the interests and objectives of the institution. It acts as the official representative of PermataBank in accordance with the provisions outlined in the PermataBank's Articles of Association and adheres to prevailing regulations.

## 3. Committees under the Board of Commissioners

The committees formed under the Board of Commissioners are tasked with assisting the supervisory function of the Board of Commissioners consist of Audit Committee, Risk Monitoring Committee, and Remuneration and Nomination Committee.

## 4. Committees under the Board of Directors

The committees formed under the Board of Directors are tasked with assisting the Board of Directors in implementing PermataBank's vision and mission and assisting the Board of Directors in managing PermataBank cosist of Risk Management Committee, Credit Committee, Asset &amp; Liability Committee, Information Technology Steering Committee, Pension Committee, as well as Sustainability Committee.

PermataBank has incorporated sustainable finance into its governance structure by establishing two vital bodies dedicated to the formulation, implementation, and oversight of the Bank's sustainable finance practices. In 2023, PermataBank strengthened its commitment to sustainable finance by establishing a dedicated Sustainability Unit directly under the President Director's Directorate and a Sustainability Committee comprised of all members of the PermataBank Board of Directors. The Sustainability Committee collaborates with the Board of Directors to define PermataBank's strategic vision for sustainability, including the creation of the Sustainable Finance Action Plan and in making critical decisions regarding the integration of Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) aspects into the Bank's policies.
