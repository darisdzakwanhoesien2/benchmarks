PermataBank telah memiliki kajian manajemen risiko yang lengkap atas semua aspek operasional bank, termasuk penilaian risiko atas aspek penerapan keberlanjutan yang dilakukan PermataBank. Melalui manajemen risiko, PermataBank berupaya mengidentifikasi, mengukur, memantau, dan mengendalikan kegiatannya dalam upaya meminimalkan efek negatif atau risiko terkait usahanya.

Rincian pelaksanaan manajemen risiko PermataBank disampaikan dalam Laporan Tahunan PermataBank 2023 pada bagian Manajemen Risiko.

## ANTI-FRAUD

Penerapan anti-fraud bagi perbankan sangatlah penting, karena kasus-kasus penyimpangan operasional pada kegiatan perbankan dapat mengakibatkan kerugian finansial bagi Bank ataupun kepada nasabahnya baik secara langsung maupun tidak langsung, terlebih lagi bagi reputasi Bank. Selain itu, Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) menaruh perhatian penting kepada perbankan di Indonesia agar menerapkan strategi anti-fraud yang menyeluruh mulai dari pencegahan, deteksi, investigasi, pelaporan, dan sanksi serta pemantauan, evaluasi, dan tindak lanjut yang harus diterapkan oleh bank umum termasuk PermataBank.

Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris PermataBank berkomitmen penuh untuk mencegah kerugian kepada pemangku kepentingan akibat fraud dengan menerapkan kebijakan, kerangka kerja manajemen risiko, dan strategi anti-fraud. Kebijakan anti-fraud PermataBank meliputi aktivitas pencegahan, pendeteksian, pemberian tanggapan, proses investigasi dan efek jera yang dijabarkan dalam strategi anti-fraud.

Pengelolaan fraud merupakan salah satu kebijakan strategis Bank yang dilakukan dengan menetapkan strategi dan kerangka kerja dalam mengelola resiko fraud yang dapat membantu PermataBank mengurangi risiko kerugian akibat fraud, termasuk membangun budaya berintegritas di seluruh jajaran PermataBankers.

PermataBank conducts a comprehensive risk management assessment that covers all aspects of the Bank's operations, including an assessment of the risks associated with the implementation of sustainability initiatives. PermataBank aims to identify, measure, monitor, and control its activities through rigorous risk management practices in order to mitigate adverse effects or risks associated with its business.

The Risk Management section of the PermataBank 2023 Annual Report contains detailed information on PermataBank's risk management implementation.

## ANTI-FRAUD

Maintaining anti-fraud measures is crucial in the banking sector because irregularities in the operational conduct of banking activities have the potential to result in financial losses for both the Bank and its customers, thereby directly influencing the Bank's overall reputation. It is also worth noting that the Financial Services Authority (OJK) closely monitors Indonesian banks' implementation of comprehensive anti-fraud strategies, which include prevention, detection, investigation, reporting, and sanctions, as well as monitoring, evaluation, and follow-up procedures that all commercial banks, including PermataBank, must follow.

PermataBank's Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners are completely committed to protecting stakeholders from losses caused by fraud. This commitment is reflected in the implementation of policies, risk management frameworks, and anti-fraud strategies. According to the anti-fraud strategy, PermataBank's comprehensive anti-fraud policy includes prevention, detection, response, investigation, and deterrent activities.

At PermataBank, fraud management is a strategic policy that is implemented through the development of strategies and frameworks for dealing with fraud risks. This initiative aims to assist PermataBank in mitigating the risk of losses due to fraud while also fostering an integrity culture at all levels of PermataBank.
