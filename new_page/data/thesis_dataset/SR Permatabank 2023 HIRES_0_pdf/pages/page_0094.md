## 2. PROGRAM BANGGA - Pemberdayaan UMKM Perempuan

Program pemberdayaan juga diberikan kepada perempuan penyandang disabilitas yang memiliki Usaha Mikro, Kecil, dan Menengah (UMKM) melalui kegiatan pelatihan dan pendampingan intensif online untuk meningkatkan kinerja usaha mereka dalam program BANGGA. Program ini

Thousands of young applicants were initially drawn to the program in its early stages, with over 3,000 candidates representing 1,241 institutions across 36 provinces, including high schools, vocational schools, polytechnic and universities. Following several phases and assessments, 100 participants were chosen to attend classes and receive training until they were able to develop a prototype of their innovative product.

The Grand Final was held at the PermataBank office in Jakarta, with 40 selected participants divided into ten teams. They presented their digital innovations to a jury comprised of representatives from PermataBank, Skilvul, the Financial Services Authority (OJK), and the Directorate of Partnership and Business and Industry Alignment (Mitras DUDI) of the Ministry of Education, Culture, Research and Technology.

The presentations were evaluated by the jury, and three winning teams were chosen, with an additional winning team chosen through public voting on the YouTube platform.

The three winning teams were entitled to receive prizes in the form of PermataBank savings and an opportunity to win scholarship for UI/UX bootcamp at Skilvul.
