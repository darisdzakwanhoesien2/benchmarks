# Realization of OHS Program

Di tahun 2023, program-program K3 yang telah direalisasikan oleh PermataBank adalah sebagai berikut:

PermataBank has implemented the following OHS programs as of 2023:

|  No | Program | Penjelasan Explanation | Pencegahan Achievement  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  1 | Building Risk Assessment (BRA) | Melakukan pengelolaan HSE berupa proses identifikasi bahaya dan ancaman di tempat kerja, penilaian risiko serta penanganan risiko HSE, keamanan (Security) serta kelangsungan usaha (Business Continuity Planning/BCP). | Dilakukan di 14 Kantor Cabang Utama dengan jumlah temuan sebanyak 34 dan 34 temuan telah diselesaikan per Desember 2023.  |
|   |   |  Conduct HSE management in the form of hazard and threat identification process in the workplace, risk assessment and HSE risk management, security and Business Continuity Planning (BCP). | Conducted in 14 Main Branch Offices with a total of 34 findings and 34 findings have been resolved as of December 2023.  |
|  2 | HSE Management Audit (HMA) |  | Dilakukan di 14 Kantor Cabang Utama.
Conducted in 14 Main Branch Offices.  |
|  3 | Investigasi dan Laporan Insiden/Kecelakaan Kerja | Proses investigasi dan menyusun laporan insiden/kecelakaan untuk mendapatkan akar penyebab kejadian dan memberikan rekomendasi untuk pencegahan. | 2 laporan investigasi terkait kejadian HSE.  |
|   |  Incident/Accident Investigation and Reporting | The process of investigating and compiling incident/accident reports to establish the root cause of the incident and provide recommendations for prevention. | 2 HSE incident-related investigation reports.  |
|  4 | Asesmen Struktur | Melaksanakan asesmen struktur untuk cabang milik sesuai periode jatuh tempo. | • Dilakukan pada 19 lokasi/kantor cabang milik yang sudah berumur di atas 25 tahun.
• Perbaikan hasil Asesmen Struktur pada 11 lokasi kantor cabang.  |
|   |  Structure Assessment | Conducted Structure Assessment for owned branches as per maturity period. | • Conducted on 19 locations/owned branches that are over 25 years old.
• Improvement of Structure Assessment at 11 branch locations.  |
|  5 | P2K3 | Mengelola P2K3 mulai dari menyusun program, melaksanakan forum, penyelesaian isu, dan membuat serta mengirim laporan ke Disnaker. | P2K3 di 10 Kantor Cabang Utama.  |
|   |   |  Managing the P2K3 starting from setting up programs, conducting forums, resolving issues, to preparing and submitting reports to the Manpower Office. | P2K3 in 10 Main Branch Offices.  |
|  6 | Tanggap Darurat | Simulasi/latihan evakuasi/fire drill, pelatihan/sharing terkait HSE dan tanggap darurat. | Simulasi latihan evakuasi secara terbatas dan pelatihan Tanggap Darurat dilakukan di 219 Kantor Cabang.  |
|   |  Emergency Response | Evacuation/fire drill simulation/training, training/sharing related to HSE and emergency response. | Limited evacuation drill simulations and Emergency Response training were conducted in 219 Branch Offices.  |
|  7 | Contractor Safety Management System | Pengelolaan kontraktor/vendor terkait pekerjaan berbahaya di area masing-masing untuk mencegah kecelakaan kerja. | Penerapan standar HSE di lokasi proyek pembangunan atau renovasi kantor.  |
|   |   |  Management of contractors/vendors related to hazardous work in their respective areas to prevent work accidents. | Implementation of HSE standards at office construction or renovation project sites.  |
