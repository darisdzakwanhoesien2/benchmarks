# KOMUNIKASI DAN KEPUASAN NASABAH

Untuk memastikan kualitas layanan terbaik bagi nasabah, PermataBank memiliki Divisi Kualitas Layanan dan Keunggulan Operasional yang menaungi Contact Center (PermataTel) dan Customer Care Center (CCC). PermataTel bertugas untuk melayani kebutuhan nasabah dan non nasabah terhadap informasi, permintaan, maupun keluhan terkait produk dan layanan perbankan melalui telepon, surat elektronik, dan media sosial. Sementara CCC bertugas untuk melayani kebutuhan dan keluhan nasabah maupun non nasabah terkait produk dan layanan perbankan dengan memberikan penyelesaian yang cepat dan tepat, serta melakukan survei kepuasan nasabah atas produk dan layanan milik Bank.

PermataBank berkomitmen untuk menyelesaikan keluhan nasabah sesuai dengan Service Level Agreement (SLA), yaitu dua sampai dengan sepuluh hari kerja. Jika batas tersebut terlewati, Bank akan memberikan Surat Perpanjangan Jangka Waktu Penyelesaian kepada nasabah.

Apabila nasabah merasa bahwa solusi yang diberikan oleh PermataBank masih tidak sesuai dengan yang diharapkan, nasabah dapat mengajukan keluhan kembali atau menggunakan penyelesaian melalui External Dispute Resolution (EDR) dengan pilihan, yaitu:

a. Fasilitasi/mediasi perbankan (terbatas) oleh OJK;
b. Penyelesaian di luar pengadilan, yaitu melalui Lembaga Alternatif Penyelesaian Sengketa Perbankan Indonesia (LAPSPI).

# COMMUNICATION AND CUSTOMER SATISFACTION

PermataBank has established a Service Quality and Operational Excellence Division to oversee both the Contact Center (PermataTel) and the Customer Care Center (CCC) in order to provide the highest level of service to its customers. PermataTel is committed to responding to customer and non-customer inquiries, requests, and complaints about banking products and services through various channels such as phone, e-mail, and social media. Simultaneously, the CCC Department is committed to resolving the needs and complaints of customers and non-customers regarding banking products and services in a timely and accurate manner. This department also conducts customer satisfaction surveys to gather information about the Bank's products and services.

PermataBank is committed to resolving customer complaints within two to ten working days, in accordance with the Service Level Agreement (SLA). If the limit is exceeded, the Bank will issue the customer a Letter of Extension of Settlement Period.

If the customer is dissatisfied with the resolution provided by PermataBank, they can file another complaint or seek resolution through External Dispute Resolution (EDR) with the following options:

a. Banking facilitation/mediation (limited) by OJK;
b. Out-of-court settlement, namely through the Indonesian Banking Dispute Resolution Alternative Institution (LAPSPI).
