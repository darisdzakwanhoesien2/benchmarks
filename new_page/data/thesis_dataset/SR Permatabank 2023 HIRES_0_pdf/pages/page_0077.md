## Cuti Karyawan

PermataBank memberikan hak cuti tahunan dan cuti khusus kepada PermataBankers sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku. Cuti melahirkan diberikan kepada karyawan perempuan selama tiga bulan tanpa menghilangkan atau mengurangi hak dan posisi karyawan yang bersangkutan di tempat kerja. Sedangkan untuk karyawan laki-laki akan mendapatkan hak cuti selama lima hari untuk mendampingi istrinya saat melahirkan.

Bank juga memberikan cuti di luar tanggungan (unpaid leave) dengan pertimbangan tertentu tanpa mengurangi manfaat asuransi kesehatan dan lainnya; serta reward non-cash berupa cuti ulang tahun, di mana karyawan berhak untuk mengambil cuti satu hari.

Sepanjang tahun 2023, terdapat 414 orang PermataBankers yang mengajukan cuti melahirkan dan cuti ayah; yang terdiri dari 233 orang karyawan wanita dan 181 orang karyawan laki-laki. Seluruhnya telah kembali bekerja di Bank setelah masa cuti berakhir.

## Program Pensiun

Program pensiun di PermataBank diterapkan dengan skema iuran dengan kontribusi tetap, di mana kewajiban iuran premi pensiun yang dibayarkan PermataBank adalah 9% dari gaji pokok karyawan. Bank menyediakan sistem dan mekanisme yang memungkinkan PermataBankers untuk mengelola dana pensiun kontribusinya secara mandiri dan reformasi dalam mekanisme pemberian pinjaman karyawan sebagai salah satu manfaat inti sebagai PermataBankers.

Untuk PermataBankers yang akan memasuki masa pensiun normal, PermataBank memberikan program manfaat pensiun, antara lain kesempatan untuk mengikuti pelatihan menjelang

The Bank implements a Pay for Performance remuneration policy, taking into account:

- The Company's financial performance;
- Employee competence and performance (meritocracy);
- The principle of feasibility and appropriateness in accordance with the position/job title;
- Remuneration practices that apply in the market (market competitiveness);
- Internal equity.

Remuneration is allocated in accordance with business objectives and distributed in line with the achievements of the Bank, its directorate, and each individual PermataBanker.

## Employee Leave

PermataBank complies with applicable regulations by providing PermataBankers with annual and special leave. Maternity leave is granted to female employees for three months without jeopardizing their rights or standing at work. Male employees are entitled to five days of paid time off to assist their wives during childbirth.

In addition, the Bank provides unpaid leave with specific considerations that do not reduce health and other insurance benefits. Employees also benefit from non-cash incentives such as birthday leave, which allows them to take a day off on their birthdays.

There were 414 PermataBankers who applied for maternity and paternity leave in 2023, with 233 female employees and 181 male employees. After their leave period expired, they all returned to work at the Bank.

## Retirement Program

PermataBank's pension program is based on a fixed contribution scheme, with PermataBank paying 9% of the employee's basic salary as the pension premium contribution obligation. The Bank provides systems and mechanisms that enable PermataBankers to manage their pension contributions independently, as well as reforms to the mechanism for providing employee loans, which are among the core benefits of being a PermataBanker.
