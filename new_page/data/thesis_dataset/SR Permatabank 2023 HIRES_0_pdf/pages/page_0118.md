# Tugas, Wewenang, dan Tanggung Jawab Keuangan Berkelanjutan
## Dunes, Authorities, and Responsibilities of Sustainable Finance

**Komite Keberlanjutan**
Sustainability Committee

Komite Keberlanjutan yang terdiri dari anggota tetap yaitu seluruh anggota Direksi PermataBank serta anggota tidak tetap tanpa hak suara dari berbagai unit sesuai dengan agenda yang akan dibicarakan.

**Komite Keberlanjutan dibentuk dengan tujuan untuk:**
- Mengawasi dan mempromosikan inisiatif keberlanjutan di PermataBank;
- Mengintegrasikan aspek ESG ke dalam strategi, kebijakan, dan praktik bisnis Bank; dan
- Memastikan kebutuhan terhadap peraturan yang berlaku, kesesuaian dengan standar industri dan praktik umum terkait keberlanjutan di perbankan.

**Komite Keberlanjutan bertanggung jawab untuk:**
- Memantau dan merekomendasikan kerangka dan kebijakan keberlanjutan serta perubahannya dan memastikan keselarasan dengan strategi bisnis bank secara keseluruhan;
- Mengawasi dan merekomendasikan strategi, inisiatif, dan target keberlanjutan;
- Memantau risiko dan menilai peluang bisnis utama yang muncul dari aspek ESG;
- Memantau kinerja keberlanjutan Bank;
- Mengawasi efektivitas dari penerapan Kebijakan Keberlanjutan di PermataBank dan merekomendasikan penyempurnaan prases penerapan prinsip keberlanjutan secara berkala;
- Menyusun dan memantau implementasi Rencana Aksi Keuangan Berkelanjutan (RAKB);
- Mengawasi dan/atau mengkaji penyimpangan dari Kebijakan Keberlanjutan; dan
- Mengkaji Term of Reference (TOR) Komite Keberlanjutan paling sedikit dua tahun sekali dan memberikan rekomendasi perubahan (jika diperlukan).

The Sustainability Committee is comprised of permanent members, which include all members of PermataBank's Board of Directors, as well as non-permanent members from various units who participate in discussions without voting rights in accordance with the agenda under consideration.

The Sustainability Committee was established with the objectives of:
- Oversee and promote sustainability initiatives at PermataBank;
- Integrate ESG aspects into the Bank's strategies, policies and business practices; and
- Ensure compliance with applicable regulations, conformity with industry standards and general practices related to sustainability in banking.

The Sustainability Committee is responsible for:
- Monitoring and recommending the sustainability framework and policies and their amendments and ensuring alignment with the Bank's overall business strategy;
- Oversee and recommend sustainability strategies, initiatives and targets;
- Monitor risks and assess key business opportunities arising from ESG aspects;
- Monitor the Bank's sustainability performance;
- Oversee the effectiveness of the implementation of the Sustainability Policy at PermataBank and recommend improvements to the process of implementing sustainability principles on a regular basis;
- Develop and monitor the implementation of the Sustainable Finance Action Plan;
- Monitor and/or review deviations from the Sustainability Policy; and
- Review the Terms of Reference (TOR) of the Sustainability Committee at least every two years and recommend changes (if necessary).

**Unit Keberlanjutan**
Sustainability Unit

Unit Keberlanjutan berperan sebagai koordinator dalam proses penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan serta penerapan aspek ESG di PermataBank.

Unit Sustainability berkolaborasi dengan unit bisnis atau unit kerja lainnya dalam melakukan inisiatif-inisiatif keuangan berkelanjutan yang telah ditetapkan dalam RAKB. Unit-unit pelaksana tersebut antara lain:
1. Corporate Banking
2. Commercial Banking
3. Treasury
4. Retail Banking
5. Unit Usaha Syariah (UUS)
6. International Business Development &amp; Advisory (IBDA)
7. Human Resource (Learning &amp; Development dan Talent Acquisition)
8. Risk (MERM, WS CRM, Retail CRM, dan Credit)
9. IT &amp; Operations (CRES)
10. Finance (Procurement &amp; Vendor Management)
11. Corporate Communication (Corporate Social Responsibility)

The Sustainability Unit acted as the coordinator in the implementation of sustainable finance and ESG aspects at PermataBank.

The Sustainability Unit, in collaboration with various business units or work units, ensures the implementation of the RAKB's sustainable finance initiatives. The participating units are as follows:
1. Corporate Banking
2. Commercial Banking
3. Treasury
4. Retail Banking
5. Syariah Business Unit (UUS)
6. International Business Development &amp; Advisory (IBDA)
7. Human Resource (Learning &amp; Development and Talent Acquisition)
8. Risk (MERM, WS CRM, Retail CRM, and Credit)
9. IT &amp; Operations (CRES)
10. Finance (Procurement &amp; Vendor Management)
11. Corporate Communication (Corporate Social Responsibility)
