## Manajemen Risiko Kredit

Otoritas Jasa Keuangan telah mengamanatkan lembaga jasa keuangan di Indonesia untuk menerapkan keuangan berkelanjutan, yaitu layanan keuangan yang memperhatikan aspek-aspek LST. Berdasarkan amanat tersebut, PermataBank telah menerapkan kebijakan dan persyaratan kredit kepada nasabah yang mencakup persyaratan lingkungan dan sosial sebelum penyaluran pembiayaan dilakukan.

Risiko lingkungan dan sosial yang dapat timbul dari penyaluran kredit kepada industri, antara lain risiko pencemaran air dan udara, kontaminasi lahan, praktik ketenagakerjaan, dan praktik bisnis yang beretika. PermataBank senantiasa memastikan adanya manajemen risiko kredit yang meliputi idektifikasi, penilaian, dan mitigasi untuk mengoptimalkan profil risiko lingkungan dan sosial terhadap pendapatan.

Kebijakan kredit PermataBank tertuang dalam WB Credit Policy yang mengatur bahwa semua proposal kredit untuk nasabah-nasabah yang terkait dengan bidang usaha atau bergerak di bidang industri tertentu harus memenuhi persyaratan Environment and Social Requirement Assessment (ESRA). Berdasarkan kebijakan tersebut, proposal kredit yang disetujui harus memenuhi persyaratan (lolas) ESRA (Environment and Social Requirement Assessment/Penilaian Kebutuhan Lingkungan dan Sosial), khususnya untuk nasabah-nasabah yang terkait dengan bidang usaha/bergerak di industri tertentu yang bersinggungan secara langsung dengan lingkungan sekitar.

Selain itu, sebagai bagian dari proses pemberian kredit, PermataBank juga menggunakan penilaian kategori Taksonomi Hijau Indonesia (THI) terhadap debitur. THI adalah klasifikasi aktivitas ekonomi yang mendukung upaya perlindungan dan pengelolaan lingkungan hidup serta mitigasi dan adaptasi terhadap perubahan iklim sesuai dengan panduan yang dikeluarkan oleh OJK.

Nonetheless, the Bank is indirectly vulnerable to the negative effects of its debtors' businesses. These effects can manifest themselves in a variety of ways, including labor disputes, environmental pollution, irresponsible resource exploitation, and other potential damages. The Bank addresses these issues by conducting a comprehensive and systematic assessment of prospective debtors prior to loan granting in order to minimize or even prevent negative impacts that are counter-productive to sustainability and violate the Bank's values.

## Credit Risk Management

The Financial Services Authority has mandated Indonesian financial institutions to implement sustainable finance, also known as financial services that consider ESG factors. PermataBank has issued a credit policy based on this mandate that requires all loan applicants to meet environmental and social requirements before receiving a loan.

Water and air pollution, land contamination, unhealthy employment, and unethical business practices are examples of environmental and social risks that may arise from industrial loans. PermataBank always maintains a solid credit risk management system that includes risk identification, assessment, and mitigation in order to optimize the environmental and social risk profile on income.

PermataBank's credit policy is specified in the WB Credit Policy, which states that all loan applications from customers operating in specific industrial sectors must meet the Environmental and Social Requirement Assessment (ESRA) prerequisites. According to the policy, loan applications must pass the ESRA (Environmental and Social Requirements Assessment) prerequisites in order to be approved, especially for customers who are related to business sectors/have operations in certain industries that have direct contact with the surrounding environment.

In addition, PermataBank also uses the Indonesian Green Taxonomy (THI) category assessment for debtors as part of the loan process. THI is a classification of economic activities that support efforts to protect and manage the environment as well as mitigate and adapt to climate change in accordance with guidelines issued by the OJK.
