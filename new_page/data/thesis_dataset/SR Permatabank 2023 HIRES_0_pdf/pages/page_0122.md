Tentang Laporan Keberlanjutan About Sustainability Report

Untuk memastikan budaya berintegritas selalu terjaga setiap waktu, tim Fraud Risk Management (FRM) menyelenggarakan training, diskusi, sharing, dan sosialisasi Fraud Management, Kode Etik, dan Budaya Kepatuhan kepada unit-unit kerja di tingkat regional, cabang-cabang dan unit-unit kerja yang terkait dengan pengelolaan risiko fraud baik melalui tatap muka maupun secara virtual. Tim FRM juga menyusun materi-materi pembelajaran tentang kasus-kasus fraud yang sedang trend di industri dan bagaimana mencegah agar tidak terjadi di PermataBank.

FRM melakukan Fraud Risk Assessment (FRA) melalui kajian berkelanjutan untuk mengevaluasi pencegahan, pendeteksian dan tanggapan terhadap setiap potensi skema dan kejadian fraud terhadap unit/produk/proses/aktivitas yang memiliki risiko fraud yang cukup tinggi, baik yang terpantau dari kenaikan insiden fraud maupun penambahan volume yang signifikan atau dari kejadian fraud yang terjadi di industri perbankan saat ini.

Secara periodik, tim FRM menyampaikan hasil-hasil pelaksanaan kegiatan budaya berintegritas serta hasil kajian FRA kepada Direktur Kepatuhan dan melalui Laporan Profil Risiko dan Laporan Semesteran ke OJK.

Lebih luas lagi, PermataBank telah membentuk unit kerja yang khusus menangani penyuapan dan korupsi yaitu Divisi Bribery &amp; Anti - Corruption. Kegiatan Divisi ini meliputi penerapan ISO 37001 tentang Sertifikasi Manajemen Anti Penyuan di PermataBank, bekerja sama dengan tim Financial Crime Control dalam mendeteksi dan investigasi risiko penyuan dan korupsi serta akan berhubungan dengan tim Litigasi dan tim Pemantauan serta Pelatihan Kepatuhan setiap kali terdapat indikasi hal yang memerlukan tindakan hukum lebih lanjut atau pelaporan Speak-Up.

# SPEAK-UP

Speak-Up adalah sarana/media bagi karyawan PermataBank, nasabah, maupun pihak ketiga, untuk melaporkan pelanggaran-pelanggaran dan malpraktek, baik berupa fraud maupun pelanggaran kode etik (untuk selanjutnya disebut "pelanggaran"), termasuk kecurigaan atas tindakan pelanggaran. Setiap pelaporan atau pengaduan melalui Speak-Up diperlakukan secara rahasia, hanya Administrator Speak-Up, PIC Speak-Up, dan Speak-Up Council yang mempunyai wewenang untuk mengetahui isi laporan.

Setiap pelaporan atau pengaduan melalui Speak-Up dicatat dalam register dan diklasifikasikan oleh Administrator berdasarkan kategori laporan yang meliputi: dugaan fraud, pelanggaran kode etik, dan laporan umum lain seperti keluh kesah atau saran.

PermataBank menjamin semua pihak pelapor mendapatkan perlindungan dari PermataBank apabila dari laporannya mengakibatkan sesuatu hal yang mengancam keberadaan pelapor serta laporan-laporan mereka tidak akan memengaruhi

The Fraud Risk Management (FRM) Team organizes training, discussions, sharing sessions, and awareness campaigns on Fraud Management, the Code of Ethics, and Compliance Culture to consistently maintain an integrity culture. These sessions are held in both face-to-face and virtual formats for regional work units, branches, and other units involved in fraud risk management. Furthermore, the FRM team provides educational materials on emerging fraud cases in the industry, as well as insights on how to prevent such incidents at PermataBank.

The FRM team conducts ongoing Fraud Risk Assessments (FRAs) to evaluate the prevention, detection, and response to potential fraud schemes and events within units, products, processes, or activities with a high risk of fraud. This assessment is based on factors such as an increase in fraud incidents or a significant increase in volume, as well as the current fraud events in the banking industry landscape.

The FRM team reports to the Compliance Director on a regular basis on the outcomes of the integrity culture activities and the findings of the FRA study. Furthermore, this information is conveyed to OJK through the Risk Profile Report and the Semesterly Report.

In a broader sense, PermataBank has established the Bribery &amp; Anti-Corruption Division, which is responsible for preventing bribery and corruption. The Division's initiatives include PermataBank's implementation of ISO 37001 for Anti-Bribery Management Certification. It collaborates closely with the Financial Crime Control team to identify and investigate bribery and corruption risks. Furthermore, the division interacts with the Litigation team and the Compliance Monitoring as well as Training team whenever there are indications of issues that require legal action or trigger the Speak-Up reporting mechanism.

# SPEAK-UP

Speak-Up is a tool/media for PermataBank's employees, customers, and third parties, to report violations and malpractices, either in fraud or code of conduct violations (hereinafter referred as "violations"), including suspicions of violations. Each Speak Up report is treated confidentially, with only the Speak-Up Administrator, Speak-Up PIC, and Speak-Up Council having access to the contents.

Every report or complaint submitted through Speak Up is meticulously recorded in a register and classified by the Administrator based on the nature of the report, which includes suspected fraud, code of ethics violations, and general reports such as complaints or suggestions.
