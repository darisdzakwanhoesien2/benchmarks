PermataBank memiliki Unit Kerja Information Security Risk Management yang bertanggung jawab dalam penentuan dan pengembangan materi, pelaksanaan, dan pemantauan pelaksanaan pelatihan terkait keamanan siber. Beberapa program pelatihan pegawai yang diinisiasi yaitu:

a. Pelatihan softskill atau hardskill secara berkala.
b. Pelatihan dan sertifikasi antara lain: CISA, CISM, CRISC, CISSP, ISO 27001, CEH, CHFI, CTIA, CRTP, ECPPT, CRTA, dan lain-lain.
c. Security awareness melalui surat elektronik, e-learning, digital signage, desktop wallpaper.
d. Phishing simulation yang dilakukan secara berkala.

## Audit Keamanan Informasi

Satuan Kerja Audit Internal (SKAI) sebagai lini pertahanan ketiga melakukan penilaian secara independen untuk memastikan manajemen telah mengidentifikasi risiko siber dan memastikan risiko siber telah dikelola sejalan dengan proses yang telah ditetapkan. SKAI juga melakukan penilaian secara independen terhadap kecukupan rancangan kontrol dan efektivitas manajemen risiko siber yang merujuk kepada ketentuan Internal Audit.

Cybersecurity merupakan salah satu entitas audit, sehingga secara berkala SKAI melakukan audit atas penyelenggaraan TI termasuk manajemen risiko siber.

Rencana Kerja Tahunan SKAI disetujui oleh Direktur Utama, Komite Audit, dan Dewan Komisaris.

## Pengembangan Berkelanjutan

PermataBank senantiasa melakukan perbaikan untuk meningkatkan ketahanan dan keamanan siber. Saat ini Bank telah meraih sertifikasi ISO 27001 – Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Informasi serta telah melakukan penilaian tingkat maturitas keamanan siber dengan mengacu pada Kertas Kerja SEOJK nomor 29/SEOJK.03/2022 tentang Ketahanan dan Keamanan Siber Bagi Bank Umum dan dikinkan setiap satu tahun sekali. Hasil penilaian tingkat maturitas keamanan siber ini akan dilaporkan kepada OJK setiap tahunnya.

The Information Security Risk Management Unit, which is responsible for developing, implementing, and overseeing cybersecurity training initiatives, has implemented a number of employee training programs. These are some examples:

a. Regular softskill and hardskill training.
b. Training and certifications such as CISA, CISM, CRISC, CISSP, ISO 27001, CEH, CHFI, CTIA, CRTP, ECPPT, CRTA, and many others.
c. Security awareness promoted through channels like electronic mail, e-learning, digital signage, and desktop wallpaper.
d. Periodic phishing simulations further enhance employee preparedness.

## Information Security Audit

The Internal Audit (SKAI) serves as the third line of defense, conducting independent assessments to ensure that management has identified and effectively managed cyber risks in accordance with established processes. In addition, using the internal audit policy, the Internal Audit independently assesses the adequacy of control design and the effectiveness of cyber risk management by referring to Internal Audit Guidelines.

The cybersecurity is one of audit entity, therefore Internal Audit periodically conduct audit on IT operations, including cyber risk management.

The Annual Audit Plan is approved by President Director, Audit Committee and Board of Commissioners.

## Continuous Improvement

PermataBank is constantly improving its cyber resilience and security. The Bank has currently obtained ISO 27001 - Information Security Management System certification and has conducted a cybersecurity maturity level assessment using SEOJK Working Paper number 29/SEOJK.03/2022 concerning Cyber Resilience and Security for Commercial Banks, which updated once a year. OJK will be informed of the results of this cybersecurity maturity level assessment on an annual basis.
