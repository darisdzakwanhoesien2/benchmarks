Responsible Financial Services

Keterlibatan Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dalam pengawasan pengelolaan keamanan informasi dilakukan melalui forum Komite Risiko Operasional, Komite Manajemen Risiko, dan Komite Pemantauan Risiko yang diadakan secara rutin. Peninjauan pengelolaan keamanan informasi juga dapat dipicu oleh perubahan material dalam situasi yang timbul baik dari lingkungan internal maupun eksternal.

Direksi menetapkan Risk Framework terkait dengan Risiko Operasional termasuk kemanan siber dan Audit Internal memastikan seluruh kebijakan/prosedur telah dijalankan oleh semua pihak terkait. Direksi juga menetapkan Kode Etik Perilaku Karyawan serta Kebijakan Tanggung Jawab Karyawan yang di dalamnya mencakup tanggung jawab karyawan untuk menjaga kerahasiaan data demi memastikan keamanan siber dan informasi. Direksi menetapkan struktur organisasi, perencanaan kuantitas karyawan, pembagian tugas dan tanggung jawab, serta memberikan tinjauan dan persetujuan dalam program pelatihan dan sertifikasi staf keamanan siber.

Pemahaman strategi, tingkat risiko terkait keamanan siber yang akan diambil (risk appetite) dan toleransi risiko (risk tolerance) terkait keamanan siber, serta kerangka manajemen risiko terkait keamanan siber yang telah ditetapkan oleh Direksi dan disetujui oleh Dewan Komisaris melalui dokumen:

a. Enterprise Risk Management Framework (ERMF); dan
b. Risk Type Framework (RTF) Risiko Keamanan Informasi.

Pelaksanaan monitoring keamanan siber dilakukan melalui berbagai komite risiko operasional dan komite risiko.

# 2. Identifikasi

PermataBank menerapkan program manajemen risiko, proses penilaian risiko dan fungsi audit untuk mengelola risiko dan menilai efektivitas dari kontrol utama. Proses Manajemen Risiko Keamanan informasi bertujuan untuk memastikan penerapan proses manajemen risiko yang efektif sesuai dengan Risk Appetite Bank. Mengacu pada kerangka manajemen risiko PermataBank, siklus manajemen risiko adalah sebagai berikut:

A. Identifikasi Risiko

Mengidentifikasi risiko inheren terkait keamanan informasi pada proses bisnis yang ada termasuk produk, kegiatan operasional, dan inisiatif atau proyek baru serta menjelaskan hasil identifikasi kepada unit bisnis terkait.

B. Pengukuran Risiko

Mengukur tingkat keparahan Risiko Keamanan informasi yang mencakup risiko bruto (inheren) dan risiko pasca-mitigasi (residual) dengan mengikuti Operational Risk Grading Matrix, Responsible Function Officer (RFO) bertanggung jawab untuk memastikan kesadaran lini pertama dan penerapan kerangka kerja manajemen risiko yang tepat.

Through regular forums such as the Operational Risk Committee, Risk Management Committee, and Risk Monitoring Committee, the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors actively participate in supervising information security management. Furthermore, significant changes in the internal or external environment may necessitate an examination of information security management.

The Board of Directors establishes the Risk Framework for Operational Risk, which includes cybersecurity. Internal Audit is in charge of ensuring that all relevant policies and procedures are followed by all parties involved. Furthermore, the Board of Directors developed the Code of Conduct for Employees and the Employee Responsibility Policy, which include employees' responsibility to maintain data confidentiality for cyber and information security assurance. In addition, the Board of Directors establishes the organizational structure, plans the number of employees, defines duties and responsibilities, and reviews as well as approves cybersecurity staff training and certification programs.

An understanding of the strategy, the level of cybersecurity-related risk to be taken (risk appetite), and risk tolerance related to cybersecurity, as well as the cybersecurity risk management framework established by the Board of Directors and approved by the Board of Commissioners through documents:

a. Enterprise Risk Management Framework (ERMF); and
b. Risk Type Framework (RTF) Information Security Risk.

The implementation of cybersecurity monitoring is handled by various operational risk and risk committees.

# 2. Identification

PermataBank implements a comprehensive risk management program that includes a risk assessment process and an audit function to manage risks and assess the effectiveness of the main controls. The Information Security Risk Management Process is intended to ensure the implementation of an effective risk management process that is consistent with the Bank's risk appetite. The risk management cycle unfolds as follows, according to PermataBank's risk management framework:

A. Identification of Risks

Identify the inherent risks of information security in existing business processes, including products, operational activities, and new initiatives or projects. Inform relevant business units about the results of this identification process.

B. Measurement of Risks

Using the Operational Risk Grading Matrix, assess the severity of Information Security Risks, including both gross (inherent) risks and post-mitigation (residual) risks. The Responsible Function Officer (RFO) is responsible for ensuring first-line awareness of the risk management framework and its proper implementation.
