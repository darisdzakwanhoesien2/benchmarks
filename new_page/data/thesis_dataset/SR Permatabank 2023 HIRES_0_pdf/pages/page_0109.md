# 2. Penggunaan Material Ramah Lingkungan

PermataBank tidak melakukan pengolahan bahan baku dalam kegiatan bisnisnya. Material yang digunakan PermataBank adalah material pendukung kegiatan operasional perbankan,seperti kertas dan Alat Tulis Kantor (ATK). Bank tidak menggunakan bahan baku daur ulang untuk kegiatan operasional.

# 2. Use of Environmentally Friendly Materials

PermataBank's business activities do not include the processing of raw materials. PermataBank's materials, which include items such as paper and stationery, are primarily used to support operational banking activities. It is important to note that the Bank does not use recycled raw materials in its operations.

# 3. Pengelolaan Limbah

PermataBank menghasilkan limbah berupa limbah beracun dan berbahaya (B3) dan limbah non B3. Limbah B3 yang dihasilkan dari kegiatan operasional Bank berupa limbah elektronik, baterai, cartridge, dan sebagainya. Sedangkan untuk limbah non B3 yang dihasilkan Bank berupa sampah, kertas, alat tulis bekas, dan limbah cair dari kegiatan domestik.

Semua limbah padat yang dihasilkan PermataBank diserahkan dan dikelola oleh pihak ketiga, yaitu pengelola gedung dan pengelola limbah B3. Sementara itu, pengelolaan limbah cair dilakukan dengan menggunakan Instalasi Pengolahan Air Limbah (IPAL). Hingga akhir tahun 2023, PermataBank tidak melakukan pencatatan untuk penghitungan limbah yang dihasilkan dikarenakan seluruhnya dikelola langsung oleh pihak ketiga. Tidak terdapat insiden tumpahan limbah Bank ke lingkungan maupun pengaduan terkait lingkungan hidup dari masyarakat maupun pemangku kepentingan lainnya selama periode pelaporan.

# 4. Energi dan Emisi

PermataBank memanfaatkan energi berupa bahan bakar minyak dan listrik dalam kegiatan operasionalnya. Untuk menekan penggunaan energi listrik, PermataBank melakukan berbagai upaya, diantaranya dengan menggunakan panel surya di kantin Kantor Cabang Bintaro, alat-alat elektronik yang hemat listrik, mematikan alat-alat elektronik yang tidak terpakai, menggunakan lampu LED untuk sebagian besar titik lampu di Kantor Pusat, pengaturan lembur, mematikan AC secara otomatis di kantor pusat mulai pukul 17:30 WIB dan mematikan lampu secara otomatis mulai pukul 19:00 WIB.

Paper consumption decreased in 2023 compared to the previous year, owing to ongoing digitalization initiatives in PermataBank's business processes. Paper is no longer used for customer account opening, teller transactions, monthly transaction reports/statements, or internal administrative processes at the Bank. As part of its commitment to responsible paper usage, the Bank ensures that paper is used sparingly when necessary.

# 3. Waste Management

PermataBank generates waste that is both toxic and hazardous (B3) as well as non-B3. Electronic waste, batteries, cartridges, and similar items are examples of B3 waste generated by the Bank's operational activities. Non-B3 waste, on the other hand, includes general waste, paper, used stationery, and liquid waste from domestic activities.

PermataBank entrusted the management of all solid waste generated to third parties, namely building managers and licensed hazardous and toxic waste managers. The building manager operates the Waste Water Treatment Plant (WWTP), which treats liquid waste. PermataBank did not keep a record of the waste generated as of the end of 2023 because it is entirely managed directly by third-party entities. During the reporting period, there were no reported incidents of waste spillage into the environment or environmental complaints from the general public or other stakeholders.

# 4. Energy and Emissions

PermataBank supports its operational activities with energy in the form of both fuel oil and electricity. PermataBank implements several initiatives to reduce electricity consumption, including the use of energy-efficient electronic devices, the shutdown of unused electronic equipment, the installation of LED lights, effective overtime management, and the automated cessation of the air conditioning system at the head office from 17:30 WIB onwards, as well as automatically deactivated lighting beginning at 19:00 WIB.
