Sepanjang periode pelaporan, tidak terdapat insiden diskriminasi terhadap PermataBankers maupun calon PermataBankers di lingkungan kerja Bank.

If the informal discussion fails to yield a solution, PermataBankers can pursue the complaint through the formal procedure for dealing with employee grievances, which can be accessed through Whistleblowing System in the forms of e-mail, phone, PO BOX, and e-form in PermataBank's website.

The Bank consistently strives to create an inclusive and professional work environment free of discrimination and harassment. PermataBank has a comprehensive workplace harassment policy that defines harassment, outlines procedures for dealing with it, and provides the necessary handling protocols. PermataBankers who are subjected to workplace harassment are encouraged to report such incidents as soon as possible using the established procedures.

Throughout the reporting period, there were no reported incidents of discrimination against PermataBankers or prospective PermataBankers.
