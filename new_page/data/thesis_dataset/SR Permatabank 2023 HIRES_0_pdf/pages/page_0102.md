# PERMATABANK DAN TUJUAN PEMBANGUNAN BERKELANJUTAN

PermataBank berkomitmen untuk mendukung pencapaian Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) melalui program PermataHati yang bersifat jangka panjang dan terarah. # PERMATABANK AND SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT GOALS

Through the long-term and targeted PermataHati program, PermataBank is committed to assisting in the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). PermataBank contributes to six SDGs, including Goal 1: No Poverty, Goal 4: Quality Education, Goal 5: Gender Equality, Goal 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth, Goal 9: Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure, and Goal 10: Reduced Inequality.
