Bank melakukan verifikasi atas data
nasabah atau perwakilannya
The bank verifies the customer or
representative data

![img-65.jpeg](img-65.jpeg)

Nasabah atau perwakilan memberikan informasi nomor rekening, nomor
kartu, nomor pinjaman dan/atau jenis transaksi keuangan, tanggal
transaksi, dan permasalahan yang disampaikan beserta identitas
nasabah

The customer or representative gives the information, such as account
numbers, card numbers, loan numbers and / or types of financial
transactions, transaction dates, and problem raised along with the
customer's identity

![img-66.jpeg](img-66.jpeg)

Bank akan menyampaikan hasil penyelesaian pengaduan
kepada nasabah melalui email, telepon, surat, atau SMS.
The bank will submit the results of the complaint settlement
to the customer via email, telephone, letter or SMS.

![img-67.jpeg](img-67.jpeg)

Karyawan bank akan memasukan pelaporan ke
dalam sistem pengaduan nasabah beserta informasi
nomor tiket atau nomor registrasi pengaduan, tanggal
penerimaan pengaduan, jangka waktu penyelesaian dan
nomor telepon unit kerja layanan pengaduan.

Bank's employee will input the report into the customer
complaints system together with the ticket number
or registration number of the complaint, the date of
receipt of the complaint, the period of resolution and the
telephone number of the complaint service working unit.
