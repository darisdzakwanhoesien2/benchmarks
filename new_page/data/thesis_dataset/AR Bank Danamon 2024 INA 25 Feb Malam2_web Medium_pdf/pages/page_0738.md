e. Operational risk

Bank wide Operational Risk Management Policy is used as a main reference for all line of businesses, support functions and Subsidiary in managing their operational risk.

The Bank's objective in managing operational risk is to prevent or minimize the impact of the failure or inadequate internal process, people, systems or from external events, which could impact the financial losses, and damage the Bank's reputation.
