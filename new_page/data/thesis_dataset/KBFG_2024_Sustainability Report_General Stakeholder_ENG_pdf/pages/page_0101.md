## Help Overcome Low Birth Rate

KB Financial Group is carrying out various support activities to "overcome the low birth rate crisis," which is a national task. We are contributing to easing financial burden by providing financial support in relation to childbirth/childcare, support for infertility treatment costs, and customized services for small business owners.

## Support for Overcoming Low Birth Rate for Small Business Owners

KB Financial Group signed an agreement with major local governments across the nation as part of measures to respond to low birth for small business owners. By providing childcare services and supporting personnel costs for workers replacing those who took childcare leave for small business owners who find it difficult to take childbirth leave or childcare leave, we are easing their livelihood burden and building a stable care environment. In addition, KB Insurance became the industry's first to launch an insurance that covers temporary business-closing costs for small business owners, thus covering losses in case of suspension of business due to care and health.
