# Management of Material Issues Associated with Corporate Value Creation

Of the 14 material issues, selected based on the 2024 materiality assessment results, KB Financial Group chose "sustainable finance," "protection of financial consumers," and "climate change strategy" as key management issues and manage them in alignment with the KPIs and performance of top management, including the executive director.
