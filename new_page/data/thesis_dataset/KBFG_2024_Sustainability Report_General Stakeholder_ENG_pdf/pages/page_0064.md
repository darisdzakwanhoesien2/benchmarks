# ACTIVITIES FOR FINANCIAL CONSUMER PROTECTION

## Systematic Management of Complaints

KB Financial Group operates an integrated complaint management support system to streamline the complaint handling process.

### Complaint handling activities

|  Subsidiary | Details  |
| --- | --- |
|  KB Kookmin Bank | • Establish an integrated complaint management interface and functions
• Continuous prevention activities such as preemptive measures against malicious complaints, post-checks, and sharing of consultant cases,
• Automate complaint prevention tasks using AI models
• Year-on-year reduction of 60% in the number of KB Life Mobile complaints  |
|  KB Securities | • Share complaint content and results of complaint-handling through the improvement of the complaint-handling system; and have adopted an alert function that provides information on the complaint-handling period
• Incorporate complaint-handling period into branch KPIs
• Received the Grand Prize at the “2024 Consumer Complaint Evaluation Awards”  |
|  KB Insurance | • Established the “Standards on Handling Complaint-related Affairs” and “Standards on Managing Consumer Protection” to specify detailed guidelines on complaints response
• Record and manage the results of monitoring the entire process of complaint handling and the follow-up process in the VOC system  |
|  KB Kookmin Card | • Constantly monitor internal complaints and respond within two hours through the “Prompt Complaint-Handling Team”
• Provide outsourced training to complaint investigators to strengthen consumer protection capabilities and expertise
• 4.54 cases of complaints per 100 thousand members based on disclosed data of the Credit Finance Association, indicating stable complaint management  |
|  KB Life Insurance | • Financial review of products with frequent complaints and strengthened accountability for those responsible for complaints
• Reduce time through digital transformation (DT) of complaint receipt and cancellation handling  |

## Financial Consumer Protection Training

KB Financial Group provides diverse training on financial consumer protection to enhance relevant employee capabilities.

### Employee training on financial consumer protection

|  Subsidiary | Details  |
| --- | --- |
|  Common for KB | • Training on financial consumer protection and complaint prevention
• 6 major sales principles of the Act on the Protection of Financial Consumers and financial product training per duty  |
|  KB Kookmin Bank | • Training on financial consumer protection and sales process for employees handling household loans
• Training on financial consumer protection and execution of the duty of explanation for deposit products
• Training on financial consumer protection for employees who sell card products
• Customer opinion-based customized training for the regional sales group, regional office, and branches  |
|  KB Securities | • Training on complaint prevention and customer service
• Customer service training for employees who provide customer counseling  |
|  KB Insurance | • Internal control training in relation to protection of financial consumers  |
|  KB Kookmin Card | • Operate the Financial Consumer Day every month to provide training on internal control regarding protection of financial consumers and matters to comply with during door-to-door sales
• Establish consumer protection standards by providing cyber training on consumer protection to all employees  |
|  KB Savings Bank | • Field training for branch employees on protection of financial consumers  |
