# CLIMATE RISK MANAGEMENT

KB Financial Group included the climate risk area in the enterprise risk management system for integrated management of climate change issues. We established the "Environmental and Social Risk Management (ESRM)" Framework to preemptively manage risks from environmental/social impact, in addition to traditional risk management. For climate risk, we have established a climate risk management system, based on the TCFD framework, under which we identify climate risks, establish response strategies, and implement them.
