## Management of Material Issues that Have Impact on External Stakeholders

For material issues that have a significant impact on external stakeholders, we derive the result index that measures direct results on local communities and the environment that are caused by corporate management activities and the impact index that quantitatively evaluates the impact on stakeholders to identify and manage impact on the external environment.
