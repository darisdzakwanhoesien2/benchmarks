# SUSTAINABILITY MANAGEMENT FOCUS

KB, growing together with citizens through care and co-prosperity

In 2024, KB Financial Group has established a new social contribution strategy system and implements "KB's Together with Others" project. Accordingly, we categorize project areas into "care" and "co-prosperity" and are steadily carrying out diverse social value creation activities.

## Core

KB Financial Group is realizing the value of care to create a society where all generations can live with peace of mind.

Based on national/social issues, we categorize the caring area into the four key areas of low birth rate, childcare, education, and safety &amp; well-being, and support caring that is connected to citizens' life cycle.
