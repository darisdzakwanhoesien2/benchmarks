# INCLUSIVE FINANCE SYSTEM

## Execution Areas of Inclusive Finance

KB Financial Group realizes finance's social responsibility and builds a sustainable financial ecosystem. We run financial inclusion programs in the three major areas of livelihood finance, financial accessibility, and corporate growth, and expand financial inclusion through practical financial support, consulting, customized education, and innovative company support.
