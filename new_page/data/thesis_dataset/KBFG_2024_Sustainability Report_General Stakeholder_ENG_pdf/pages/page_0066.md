# DIGITAL INNOVATION MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

KB Financial Group operates the AI/Digital Division and seven organizations exclusively in charge of digital technology under the "Digital Segment." In 2025, we merged the AI Division and DT Division and reformed the organization into the AI/Digital Division, and also created the "Digital Innovation Department"—a control tower of digital platforms, AI, and all areas of data—to establish the Group's digital finance strategies. In addition, the Financial AI Center was expanded into Center 1 and 2 to secure AI capabilities and fully implement generative AI business strategies.

The Chief Digital Officer (CDO) and top management in charge of digital technology by subsidiary discuss pending digital-related issues at the Group level at the "Digital Segment Strategy Meeting" and review relevant business. In addition, each subsidiary has a management-led council, including KB Kookmin Bank's "Star Banking Steering Committee" and KB Kookmin Card's "KB Pay Operation Council."
