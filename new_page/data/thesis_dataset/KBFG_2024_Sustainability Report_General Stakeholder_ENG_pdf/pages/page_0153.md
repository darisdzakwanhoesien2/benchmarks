## Materiality Assessment Result

KB Financial Group derived a total of 14 material issues in accordance with the priority of materiality assessment results. Of the material issues, five issues had a high financial materiality assessment result priority. For these five issues, we issued a separate report from the investor perspective that applied IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards S1 and S2.
