## Internal Control System

KB Financial Group has established and operates an internal control system with the Group Chief Compliance Officer at the center.

The Group Chief Compliance Officer oversees an on-site inspection on all subsidiaries that is conducted at least once a year, and the Group’s internal control activity results are regularly reported to the Internal Control Committee.
