# Security Inspection and Training for Subcontractors

KB Financial Group signed a security management agreement that includes technical measures to protect personal information processing information, physical security such as entry control, and administrative procedures. We also provide regular training and conduct onsite inspections on subcontractors. If a vulnerability is discovered in the inspection process, we assign an improvement period and re-examine whether measures are complete, after which a report is made to the BOD.
