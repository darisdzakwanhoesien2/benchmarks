Accordingly, KB Securities makes a three-year deferred payment for 40–60% of the total remuneration amount. This system is aimed at preventing the side effects of short-term performance-centered decision-making. The deferred bonus system was adopted to avoid excessive compensation that is based on temporary performance and to promote sustainable performance generation. In case of a loss or illegal conduct or increased risk during the deferred payment period, there is a regulation that adjusts the performance compensation for the deferred bonus.
