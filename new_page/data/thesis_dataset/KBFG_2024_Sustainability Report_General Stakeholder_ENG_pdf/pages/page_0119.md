## Environmental Management System

### ESG Information Disclosure System

KB Financial Group is building the “Group ESG Information Disclosure System” to more appropriately respond to global ESG disclosure standards’ requirements by systematizing sustainability data that was previously dispersed and by raising credibility and efficiency.

Key functions of the ESG Information Disclosure System

|  Financed emissions measurement  |
| --- |
|  • Automatically measure and monitor financed emissions by collecting data per subsidiary  |
|  Climate change scenarios analysis and financial impact analysis  |
|  • Analyze the Group’s climate change scenarios and monitor results
• Handle system-based integrated management of the results of analyzing financial impact caused by climate change  |
|  Measurement and monitoring of GHG emissions  |
|  • Handle integrated management of the Group’s GHG emissions information  |
|  Integrated management of other sustainability data  |
|  • Centralize and automate data (quantitative, qualitative, SASB) by each sustainability item
• Enhance consistency by standardizing basic information and performance data of sustainability financial products  |

## Environmental Information Management System

KB Financial Group operates the “Environmental Information Management System” to measure and handle integrated management of the Group’s major GHG emissions—direct emissions, indirect emissions, external emissions. We also use the system to monitor the “execution of net-zero strategies” and “reduction of carbon emissions.”
