### Installation of traffic lights

Traffic lights are essential to arrange road users and to avoid road users from traffic accidents. WIKA Beton has installed three traffic lights and other supporting public facilities in the crossing road near WIKA Beton's CPP at Boyolali.

The installation of traffic lights is the result of coordination between WIKA Beton with Resort Police of Boyolali and Department of Transportation of Boyolali. The location of the traffic lights installation is prone to traffic accidents.
