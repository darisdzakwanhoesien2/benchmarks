# Reducing Energy Consumption

Energy from various sources is used to support the company's operations. As part of QSHE's performance, energy usage is monitored by the QSHE &amp; Management System Bureau.

WIKA Beton manages energy consumption effectively and efficiently so that its utilization is optimal while contributing to emissions reduction. Energy efficiency efforts that have been implemented in the work environment include:

- Gradually maximize the use of energy-saving lamps in office environments;
- Efficiency of oil fuel consumption for operational cars.
