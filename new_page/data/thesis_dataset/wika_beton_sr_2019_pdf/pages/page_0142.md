WIKA Beton has several types of vendors in meeting the supply of goods and services. Involvement of local parties, namely local suppliers based on the geographical approach of each working unit, which is located around the WIKA Beton operating area throughout Indonesia. National suppliers, which are suppliers of strategic material which can fulfill the needs of the entire working units of WIKA Beton. International suppliers, which are suppliers domiciled in foreign countries which supply materials and tools that cannot be supplied by vendors in Indonesia. [204-1]
