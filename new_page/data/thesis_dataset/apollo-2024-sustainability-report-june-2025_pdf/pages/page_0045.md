# Expanding Opportunity

We aim to attract the best, most talented people to our business. We believe in the power of people — in all their lived experiences, perspectives and character traits — to drive our business forward.

Apollo's Expanding Opportunity strategy puts the full force of our network behind driving positive change across our Company and beyond. Expanding Opportunity prioritizes three distinct pillars: our workplace, the marketplace in which we operate and the communities where we work and live. We ask our people to live out Expanding Opportunity by contributing their talents and leadership in at least one focus area where they are passionate.
