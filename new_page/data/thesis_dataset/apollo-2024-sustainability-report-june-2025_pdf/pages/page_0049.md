# Philanthropy

Each AAM employee receives a Citizenship Grant of $2.5K (or local equivalent) that they may use in any combination of matching gifts and/or volunteer rewards on an annual basis.

- Through Matching Gifts, Apollo matches personal charitable contributions made by employees 1:1.
- Through Volunteer Rewards, the Firm contributes $25 for each hour an employee volunteers at an eligible nonprofit.
