# Apollo Opportunity Foundation

AOF seeks to expand opportunity in the communities where we live and work by contributing capital and engaging Apollo employees to invest in career education, economic empowerment and workforce development.

We launched AOF in 2022 with a commitment to invest more than $100M over the next decade to employee-nominated organizations around the globe that help to expand opportunity.
