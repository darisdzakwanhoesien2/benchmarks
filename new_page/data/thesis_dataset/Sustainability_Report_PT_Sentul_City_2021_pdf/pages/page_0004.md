## KINERJA SOSIAL / SOCIAL PERFORMANCE

Perseroan dari waktu ke waktu berupaya untuk mengambangkan program sosial kemasyarakatan di sekitar Perseroan beroperasi dan tempat lain baik bekerjasama dengan pihak ketiga maupun secara swadaya.

The Company from time to time strives to develop social programs in the vicinity of the Company's operations and other places, both in cooperation with third parties and independently.

Perseroan juga sangat peduli terhadap pemberdayaan masyarakat sekitar. Berkenaan dengan hal tersebut Perseroan telah menyelenggarakan berbagai kegiatan sosial yang bersentuhan langsung dengan warga masyarakat sekitar wilayah Babakan Madang dan tempat lain baik bekerjasama dengan pihak ketiga maupun secara swadaya.

Perseroan telah melakukan pengelolaan limbah dengan melibatkan tenaga kerja lokal. Selain dari pada hal tersebut di atas Perseroan juga telah banyak mempekerjakan tenaga kerja lokal di sekitar tempat Perseroan beroperasi, yang ditempatkan pada berbagai unit kerja.

Selama periode pelaporan Perseroan tidak terdapat laporan pelanggaran terhadap peraturan lingkungan hidup.

The Company is also very concerned about empowering the surrounding community. In this regard, the Company has organized various social activities that are in direct contact with local residents around the Babakan Madang area and other places, both in collaboration with third parties and independently.

The Company has carried out waste management by involving local workers. Apart from the above, the Company has also employed many local workers in the vicinity where the Company operates, which are placed in various work units.

During the Company's reporting period, there were no reports of violations of environmental regulations.
