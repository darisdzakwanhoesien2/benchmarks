## Wilayah Operasional

Perseroan melaksanakan kegiatan operasional di wilayah Desa Babakan Madang, Desa Sumur Batu, Desa Cijayanti, Desa Citaringgul, Desa Bojong Koneng, Desa Cipambuan, Desa Kadumangu, Desa Karang Tengah, Kecamatan Babakan Madang dan Desa Cadasngampar, Kecamatan Kedunghalang, Kabupaten Bogor.

## Produk Layanan dan Kegiatan Usaha

Maksud dan tujuan Perseroan adalah berusaha dalam bidang konstruksi, real estate, perdagangan dan jasa. Produk yang dihasilkan adalah bangunan rumah, apartemen, gedung perkantoran, gedung pertokoan dan lain-lain.

## Kegiatan Usaha

Menjalankan usaha real estate dan properti, antara lain meliputi perdagangan yang berhubungan dengan usaha real estate, yaitu penjualan bangunan-bangunan rumah, gedung perkantoran, gedung pertokoan, unit-unit ruangan apartemen, ruangan kantor, ruangan pertokoan dan lain-lain;

## Keanggotaan Pada Asosiasi

1. Perseroan tercatat sebagai anggota Asosiasi Emiten Indonesia (AEI) sejak tanggal 28 Juli 1997 hingga saat ini.
2. Perseroan juga sebagai anggota Persatuan Perusahaan Realestat Indonesia (REI), sejak tanggal 1 Agustus 1992 hingga saat ini.
3. Perseroan juga tercatat sebagai anggota Asosiasi Sekretaris Perusahaan Indonesia atau Indonesia Corporate Secretary Association (ICSA) hingga saat ini.

## Operational Area

The Company carries out operational activities in the areas of Babakan Madang Village, Sumur Batu Village, Cijayanti Village, Citaringgul Village, Bojong Koneng Village, Cipambuan Village, Kadumangu Village, Karang Tengah Village, Babakan Madang Sub-district and Cadasngampar Village, Kedunghalang Sub-district, and Bogor Regency.

## Product Services and Business Activities

The purpose and objective of the Company is to engage in the fields of construction, real estate, trading and services. The products produced are houses, apartments, office buildings, shopping buildings and others.

## Business activities

Engage in a real estate and property business, including trading related to the real estate business, namely the sale of houses, office buildings, shop buildings, apartment units, office spaces, shop rooms and others;

## Membership in the Association

1. The Company is registered as a member of the Association of Indonesian Issuers/ Asosiasi Emiten Indonesia (AEI) since July 28, 1997 until now.
2. The Company is also a member of the Indonesian Real Estate Company Association/ Persatuan Perusahaan Realestat Indonesia (REI), since August 1, 1992 until now.
3. The Company is also registered as a member of the Indonesian Corporate Secretary Association (ICSA) until now.
