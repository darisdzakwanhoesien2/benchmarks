## Environment

The use of electrical energy by the Company in 2021 is 441,613.38 compared to 2020 which is 340,849.60, which is greater due to the addition of areas that require lighting.

To continue to improve efficiency, the Company is currently gradually using energy-saving lamps.

Apart from this, in the process of building and developing the area, the Company also utilizes local materials in the form of andesite stone which are very suitable for foundations. The stone is obtained during the grading or cut &amp; fill process.
