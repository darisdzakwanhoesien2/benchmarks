## c. Strategi pencapaian target

### 1. Informasi pengelolaan risiko

Pembangunan keberlanjutan akan dapat dilaksanakan bilamana berbagai risiko dapat dikelola dengan baik. Untuk mengelola risiko keuangan, ekonomi, lingkungan, maupun risiko sosial dengan baik diperlukan pemetaan dan identifikasi risiko secara berkesinambungan.

Untuk memperkecil risiko sosial, Perseroan berupaya menjalin hubungan dan kerjasama yang baik dengan tokoh masyarakat sekitar termasuk memberikan kesempatan kerja kepada tenaga kerja lokal.

Deed No.124, dated, drawn up before Buntario Tigris Darmawa Ng, S.H., S.E., M.H., Notary in Jakarta.

The purposes &amp; objectives of the agreement are: to produce a number of Land and Buildings and Plots that are ready to be marketed to the public, equipped with Public Facilities and Other Facilities and to obtain profits in the future which will be divided between the Parties proportionally according to the profit sharing percentage as stipulated in the agreement.

It is hoped that this collaboration will have a positive influence on the business development of the Company and related parties.

## c. Target achievement strategy

### 1. Risk management information

Sustainable development can be implemented if various risks can be managed properly. To properly manage financial, economic, environmental and social risks, it is necessary to map and identify risks on an ongoing basis.

To minimize social risks, the Company seeks to establish good relationships and cooperation with local community leaders, including providing job opportunities to local workers.
