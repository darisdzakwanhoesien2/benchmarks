Pengembangan Kompetensi Keuangan Berkelanjutan [FS4]
Sustainable Finance Competency Development

Danamon provides all employees with trainings, with a particular focus on the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, Sustainability Committee, and Sustainability Team as the parties responsible for the management and oversight of sustainable finance implementation.
