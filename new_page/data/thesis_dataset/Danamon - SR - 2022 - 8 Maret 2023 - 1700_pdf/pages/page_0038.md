### General Information

Dibangun pertama kali pada tahun 1956 sebagai Bank Kopra Indonesia, kini PT Bank Danamon Indonesia Tbk telah melalui berbagai transformasi hingga menjadi lembaga bank terkemuka di Indonesia. Sejak tahun 2019, Bank secara mayoritas dimiliki oleh MUFG Bank.

With a rich history since its founding in 1956 as Bank Kopra Indonesia, PT Bank Danamon Indonesia Tbk has undergone numerous transformations and has emerged as a leading banking institution among Indonesian customers. Since 2019, the Bank has been majority-owned by MUFG Bank.
