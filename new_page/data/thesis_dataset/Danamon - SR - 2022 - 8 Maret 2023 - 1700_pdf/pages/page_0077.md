# Pilar Ekonomi
Economic Pillar

![img-94.jpeg](img-94.jpeg)

Pilar ekonomi berfokus pada kegiatan peningkatan kapasitas masyarakat yang mendorong kontribusi Danamon terhadap pencapaian TPB.

The economic pillar focuses on activities related to capacity building in the communities that encourages Danamon's contribution to the achievement of SDGs.
