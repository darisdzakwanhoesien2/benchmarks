Dalam laporan keuangan konsolidasian ini, laba kena pajak hasil rekonsiliasi menjadi dasar dalam pengisian Surat Pemberitahuan Tahunan (SPT) Pajak Penghasilan (PPh) Badan. Jumlah taksiran rugi fiskal masih berdasarkan perhitungan sementara, karena SPT PPh Badan tahun 2025 belum dilaporkan sampai dengan tanggal penyelesaian laporan keuangan konsolidasian.

In these consolidated financial statements, the taxable income resulting from the reconciliation serves as the basis for the preparation of the Corporate Income Tax Annual Return (SPT PPh Badan). The estimated fiscal loss is based on a provisional calculation, as the 2025 SPT PPh Badan has not yet been filed as of the completion date of the consolidated financial statements.
