# PANDANGAN ATAS PROSPEK USAHA

Dewan Komisaris menyambut baik proyeksi kinerja Perseroan yang merupakan gambaran prospek usaha yang dituangkan Direksi dalam Rencana Anggaran tahun 2026, dengan menetapkan sejumlah target yang akan dicapai dalam tahun 2026 beserta langkah-langkah strategis yang akan dijalankan.

Memasuki tahun 2026, perekonomian global semakin masuk kedalam ketidakpastian strategis fragmentasi geopolitik, politisasi rantai pasok global, serta percepatan transformasi digital dan transisi energi menciptakan wajah ekonomi yang cukup kompleks, penuh dengan risiko.

Kami berpandangan bahwa prospek usaha yang disampaikan beserta rencana strategi dan target usaha cukup optimistis. Prospek usaha tersebut sejalan dengan proyeksi kondisi perekonomian nasional yang diperkirakan dapat bertahan di tengah gejolak perekonomian global yang penuh ketidakpastian. Berbekal kinerja yang bagus di beberapa tahun terakhir, BNBR serta seluruh unit usahanya memiliki peluang yang baik khususnya dalam mendukung sektor manufaktur dan

receives support from various Committees, including the Audit Committee, Nomination and Remuneration Committee, Corporate Governance Committee, and the Investment and Risk Management Committee. These Committees assist in overseeing critical areas such as corporate governance implementation, internal control and risk management effectiveness, financial reporting compliance and reliability, and human resource requirements fulfilment.

In supervising the Board of Directors' management of the Company, the Board of Commissioners offers guidance and input to aid in formulating and executing the Company's strategy, considering economic, business, and industry dynamics. In 2025, The Board of Commissioners has directed the Board of Directors to focus on efforts to acquire the Cimanggis - Cibitung (CCT) toll road, in addition to implementing strategies to boost sales of goods and services, pursuing new product and business opportunities, and being vigilant in seizing every business opportunity while optimizing risk management practices. Additionally, they emphasized the importance of improving internal control and supervision across all business units, prioritizing Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) aspects to ensure business sustainability, and intensifying efforts in trade receivables collection and optimal utilization of Company assets.

# VIEWS ON BUSINESS PROSPECTS

The Board of Commissioners welcomes the Company's performance projections which are an illustration of the business prospects outlined by the Board of Directors in the 2026 Budget Plan, by setting a number of targets to be achieved in 2026 along with strategic steps to be implemented.

As we enter 2026, the global economy is increasingly facing strategic uncertainty. Geopolitical fragmentation, the politicization of global supply chains, and the acceleration of digital transformation and the energy transition are shaping a highly complex economic landscape fraught with risks.

We are of the view that the business prospects presented along with the strategic plans and business targets are quite optimistic. The business outlook is in line with the projected condition of the national economy, which is expected to survive the global economic turmoil. With good performance in the past few years, BNBR and all of its business units have good opportunities, especially in supporting manufacturing and infrastructure sectors and sustainable business.
