# OCCUPATIONAL SAFETY [GRI 403-2]

The Zero Accidents target has become the primary focus of BNBR's occupational safety management and all its subsidiaries. Industry standards are referenced in designing, operating, and maintaining all facilities to ensure their optimal and safe functioning. Risk prevention is implemented through strict supervision and routine monitoring of safety indicators. Comprehensive reviews of each OHS incident and enhanced awareness among all personnel regarding accredited safety procedures are continuously conducted as part of ongoing safety policy improvements.
