# IMPLEMENTASI ISO 26000
## IMPLEMENTATION OF ISO 26000 [SEOJK A.1]

Integrasi nilai CSR di seluruh lini bisnis semakin kuat sejak ISO 26000 ditetapkan sebagai acuan utama. Penguatan tata kelola organisasi menjadi langkah awal yang kami dahulukan, kemudian diikuti penegasan berbagai isu sosial prioritas yang relevan dengan perkembangan kebutuhan masyarakat. Standar ini juga menetapkan tujuh core subject tanggung jawab sosial sebagai fokus utama pelaksanaan.

1. Tata Kelola Organisasi
2. Hak Asasi Manusia
3. Praktik Ketenagakerjaan
4. Lingkungan Hidup
5. Praktik Operasi yang Adil
6. Masalah Konsumen
7. Keterlibatan dan Pengembangan Masyarakat

Seluruh pendekatan tersebut kami jalankan untuk memastikan praktik usaha berlangsung secara etis, bertanggung jawab, dan selaras dengan prinsip yang sejak awal menjadi dasar nilai perusahaan.

The integration of CSR values throughout all business lines has become stronger since ISO 26000 was adopted as the primary reference. The initial step involves strengthening organizational governance, subsequently followed by emphasizing various priority social issues relevant to the evolving needs of the community. This standard also sets seven core subjects of social responsibility as the main focus of implementation.

1. Organizational Governance
2. Human Rights
3. Labor Practices
4. Environment
5. Fair Operating Practices
6. Consumer Issues
7. Community Involvement and Development

We implement all of these approaches to ensure that business practices are conducted ethically, responsibly, and in accordance with the principles that have been the foundation of the company's values from the beginning.
