# A. ARDIANSYAH BAKRIE

WAKIL DIREKTUR UTAMA &amp; CO – CEO

VICE PRESIDENT DIRECTOR &amp; CO – CEO

Anindra Ardiansyah Bakrie, Warga Negara Indonesia, lahir di Jakarta pada tahun 1979. Memperoleh gelar Master of Business Administration (MBA) dari Bentley, McCallum Graduate School of Business, Waltham, Massachusetts, USA pada tahun 2005 dan gelar Bachelor of Science (BSc) Business Administration dari Georgetown University, McDonough School of Business, Washington, DC, USA pada tahun 2001.

Beliau menjabat sebagai Wakil Direktur Utama PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk sejak Mei 2018. Saat ini beliau juga menjabat sebagai Direktur Utama &amp; CEO PT Bakrie Indo Infrastructure, CEO PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk, dan Komisaris Utama di PT Southeast Asia Pipe Industries, PT Cimanggis Cibitung Tollways, PT Multi Kontrol Nusantara dan juga sebagai Komisaris di PT Bakrie Metal Industries dan PT Bakrie Power. Selain itu, beliau menjabat sebagai Direktur PT Bakrie Global Ventura sejak April 2012, Komisaris Utama PT Cakrawala Andalas Televisi (ANTV) sejak Oktober 2019, Komisaris Utama PT Lativi Mediakarya (tvOne) sejak Maret 2017, Komisaris Utama PT Viva Media Baru (vivanews.com) sejak Maret 2017, dan Komisaris Utama PT Intermedia Capital Tbk sejak September 2025.

Sebelumnya beliau menjabat sebagai Direktur Utama dan CEO PT Viva Media Baru (November 2013 – Maret 2017), Direktur Utama dan CEO PT Lativi Mediakarya (Agustus 2011 – Maret 2017), Komisaris PT Asia Global Media (September 2009 – Mei 2012), Komisaris PT Viva Media Baru (November 2008 – Mei 2012) dan Komisaris PT Intermedia Capital Tbk (Juni 2017 – Maret 2020).

Anindra Ardiansyah Bakrie, an Indonesian nationality, born in Jakarta in 1979. Obtained Master of Business (MBA) degree from Bentley, McCallum Graduate School of Business, Waltham, Massachusetts, USA in 2005 and Bachelor of Science (BSc) Business Administration degree from Georgetown University, McDonough School of Business, Washington, DC, USA, on 2001.

A. Ardianyah Bakrie holds the position of Vice President Director of PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk since May 2018. Currently he also holds the position as President Director &amp; CEO of PT Bakrie Indo Infrastructure, CEO PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk, and President Commissioner of PT Southeast Asia Pipe Industries, PT Cimanggis Cibitung Tollways, PT Multi Kontrol Nusantara, and also as Commissioner at PT Bakrie Metal Industries and PT Bakrie Power. He also serves as Director of PT Bakrie Global Ventura since April 2012, President Commissioner of PT Cakrawala Andalas Televisi (ANTV) since October 2019, President Commissioner of PT Lativi Mediakarya (tvOne) since March 2017, President Commissioner of PT Viva Media Baru (vivanews.com) since March 2017, and President Commissioner of PT Intermedia Capital Tbk since September 2025.

Previously he held the position of President Director and CEO of PT Viva Media Baru (November 2013 – March 2017), President Director and CEO of PT Lativi Mediakarya (August 2011 – March 2017), Commissioner of PT Asia Global Media (September 2009 – May 2012), Commissioner of PT Viva Media Baru (November 2008 – May 2012) and Commissioner of PT Intermedia Capital Tbk (June 2017 – March 2020).
