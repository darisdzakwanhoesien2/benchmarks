## AIR

Ketersediaan air bersih bagi operasional maupun masyarakat sekitar menjadi perhatian utama, sehingga efisiensi pemakaian terus diperkuat di seluruh aktivitas. Melalui strategi manajemen dan konservasi yang dilengkapi kebijakan serta program pendukung, Grup menekan konsumsi air sekaligus menjaga keberlanjutan sumber daya.

Upaya tersebut dijalankan lewat pemasangan sistem kontrol dan distribusi untuk mencegah kebocoran serta kelebihan pasokan, termasuk pembangunan fasilitas pengolahan air limbah guna mendukung proses daur ulang. Kesadaran karyawan juga diperkuat melalui panduan visual seperti poster dan stiker yang mendorong penggunaan sumber daya secara bertanggung jawab. Konsistensi penerapan di seluruh fasilitas menjaga konsumsi tetap stabil di kisaran 851.825 m³ atau 0,23 m³ per satu juta revenue selama tiga tahun terakhir, dan sepanjang 2025 tidak ada keluhan masyarakat terkait kualitas maupun ketersediaan air bersih di sekitar wilayah operasional. [SEOJK F.8] [GRI 303-3]

## WATER

The availability of clean water for operations and the surrounding community remains a primary concern; consequently, the efficiency of water use is persistently emphasized across all activities. Through management and conservation strategies, supported by policies and programs, the Group reduces water consumption while maintaining the sustainability of water resources.

These efforts are implemented through the installation of control and distribution systems to prevent leaks and over-supply, including the construction of wastewater treatment facilities to facilitate recycling processes. Employee awareness is further reinforced through visual aids such as posters and stickers that promote responsible resource use. Consistent implementation across all facilities has maintained consumption at approximately 851,825 m³ or 0.23 m³ per one million in revenue over the past three years. Throughout 2025, no complaints have been received from the community regarding the quality or availability of clean water within the operational areas. [SEOJK F.8] [GRI 303-3]
