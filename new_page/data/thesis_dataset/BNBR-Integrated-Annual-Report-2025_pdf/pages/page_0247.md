# KPI DEWAN KOMISARIS [SEOJK E.1] [GRI 2-12] [GRI 2-14]

Penetapan parameter kinerja untuk Dewan Komisaris dilakukan sesuai dengan tugas dan tanggung jawab sesuai kewenangan Dewan Komisaris berdasarkan ketentuan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan dan Peraturan Perundangan yang berlaku. Berikut adalah tabel parameter self-assessment kinerja Dewan Komisaris:

3. Result of evaluation on the performance of the BOC as a whole and performance of each member of BOC as an individual will be an integral part in the compensation and incentives giving scheme for members of BOC. Performance evaluation results of each member of BOC as an individual act as a rationale for shareholders to dismiss and/or re-appoint concerned members of BOC. The aforementioned performance evaluation result is a means of evaluating and increasing the effectiveness of the BOC, and as a foundation for continuous improvement in the execution of its supervisory functions.
