## TENTANG CCT

PT Cimanggis Cibitung Tollways (CCT) merupakan Badan Usaha Jalan Tol yang mengelola Ruas Cimanggis-Cibitung sepanjang 26,184 km dengan masa konsesi selama 45 tahun yang akan berakhir pada tanggal 10 Agustus 2061.

Ruas Jalan Tol Cimanggis-Cibitung ini sangat strategis, karena merupakan jalur alternatif untuk mengurai kemacetan di ruas tol lain, terutama Tol Jakarta-Cikampek, serta menjadi bagian penting dalam meningkatkan konektivitas dan efisiensi transportasi, serta pertumbuhan kawasan industri dan ekonomi di sekitar Jabodetabek.

Jalan Tol Cimanggis-Cibitung terdiri dari dua seksi. Seksi 1, segmen Cimanggis–Jatikarya sepanjang 2,75 km, beroperasi sejak 26 Oktober 2020. Seksi 2, segmen Jatikarya–Cibitung sepanjang 23,43 km, yang terdiri dari Seksi 2A segmen Jatikarya–Cikeas sepanjang 3,78 km, beroperasi sejak 16 Juni 2023, dan Seksi 2B segmen Cikeas–Cibitung sepanjang 19,657 km, beroperasi sejak tanggal peresmian 9 Juli 2024.

## PURPOSE OF TRANSACTION

The main consideration for this transaction is to strengthen the Company's Business Group's position in the national infrastructure sector, in line with the Company's long-term business strategy that focuses on infrastructure and manufacturing development.

The Company is expected to optimize business synergies, improve operational and strategic control over the toll road assets, and encourage sustainable and significant revenue contributions to the future consolidated performance of the Company's Business Group.

## ABOUT CCT

PT Cimanggis Cibitung Tollways (CCT) is a Toll Road Business Entity that manages the 26.184 km Cimanggis-Cibitung section with a 45-year concession period that will end on August 10, 2061.

The Cimanggis-Cibitung Toll Road section is highly strategic, as it serves as an alternative route to alleviate congestion on other toll roads, particularly the Jakarta-Cikampek Toll Road, and plays a crucial role in enhancing transportation connectivity and efficiency, as well as fostering industrial and economic growth in the Jabodetabek region.

The Cimanggis-Cibitung Toll Road consists of two sections. Section 1, the Cimanggis–Jatikarya segment, spans 2.75 km and has been operational since October 26, 2020. Section 2, the Jatikarya–Cibitung segment, spanning 23.43 km, consists of Section 2A, the Jatikarya–Cikeas segment, spanning 3.78 km, which has been operational since June 16, 2023, and Section 2B, the Cikeas–Cibitung segment spanning 19.657 km, has been operational since its inauguration on July 9, 2024.
