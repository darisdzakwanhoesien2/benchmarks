# PROGRAM CERDAS UNTUK NEGERI

Pendidikan kami tempatkan sebagai fondasi utama kemajuan bangsa melalui program Cerdas untuk Negeri yang bertujuan menyiapkan generasi masa depan yang mandiri dan unggul. Berbagai peluang disediakan, termasuk program magang bagi mahasiswa dan siswa setara SMA di unit BNBR, BMI, dan BA, pemberian beasiswa kepada putra-putri karyawan BA berprestasi, serta partisipasi aktif karyawan sebagai dosen dan guru tamu di Universitas Bakrie maupun sekolah di sekitar area pabrik. Sejumlah kegiatan Program Cerdas untuk Negeri telah terlaksana sepanjang tahun 2025, seperti:

- Kolaborasi BPI dalam kegiatan edukasi yang melibatkan 209 siswa dan 124 mahasiswa, serta keterlibatan anak yatim dan masyarakat sekitar
- Program beasiswa bagi siswa berprestasi peringkat 1, 2, dan 3 oleh BUMM untuk siswa SMPN 31 Tangerang
- Penyaluran buku bacaan Taman Iqro kepada masyarakat di sekitar BUMM
- Pemberian bantuan sembako dan dukungan biaya pendidikan kepada Panti Asuhan Guardian Holy Angel di Kota Bekasi oleh CCT
- Pelaksanaan program bantuan pendidikan melalui CCT Goes To School di SDN Jatikarya III
- Program praktik kerja lapangan (PKL) dan pemberian beasiswa bagi siswa SMK N 1 Bakauheni jurusan Teknik Pengelasan binaan SEAPI
- Program PKL bagi mahasiswa dari ITERA, UNILA, dan POLTEKKES Tanjung Karang
- Program pemagangan Teknik Pengelasan bekerja sama dengan Dinas Tenaga Kerja Kabupaten Lampung Selatan
- Program pemagangan bersama Kementerian Ketenagakerjaan Republik Indonesia (Batch 2 dan 3) oleh SEAPI

Education is positioned as the primary foundation for our national advancement through the Cerdas Untuk Negeri program, which aims to cultivate an independent, excellent future generation. Diverse opportunities are provided, including internship programs for university students and students equivalent to high school at BNBR, BMI, and BA units; scholarships for the children of BA employees who demonstrate academic excellence; as well as active involvement of employees as lecturers and guest instructors at Bakrie University and local schools around the factory area. Several activities under the Cerdas Untuk Negeri Program have been implemented throughout 2025, such as:

- BPI’s collaboration in educational initiatives involving 209 students and 124 university students, as well as engagement with orphans and surrounding communities
- Scholarship program for high-achieving students ranked 1st, 2nd, and 3rd by BUMM for students of SMPN 31 Tangerang
- Distribution of Taman Iqro reading books to communities surrounding BUMM
- Provision of basic necessities and educational financial support to Guardian Holy Angel Orphanage in Bekasi by CCT
- Implementation of educational support through the CCT Goes To School program at SDN Jatikarya III
- Internship (PKL) and scholarship programs for students of SMK N 1 Bakauheni majoring in Welding Engineering under SEAPI
- Internship programs for students from ITERA, UNILA, and POLTEKKES Tanjung Karang
- Apprenticeship program in Welding Engineering in collaboration with the South Lampung Manpower Office
- Apprenticeship program with the Ministry of Manpower of the Republic of Indonesia (Batch 2 and 3) conducted by SEAPI
