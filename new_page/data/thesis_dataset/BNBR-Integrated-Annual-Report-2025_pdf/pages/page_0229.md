# PENYELENGGARAAN RAPAT UMUM PEMEGANG SAHAM LUAR BIASA DI TAHUN 2025

Sesuai Anggaran Dasar, Perseroan telah menyelenggarakan RUPS Luar Biasa yang diselenggarakan pada 10 September 2025 bertempat di Ruang Nusantara, Bakrie Tower lantai 36, Jakarta. Sesuai dengan Peraturan Perundangan, sebelum pelaksanaan RUPS Perseroan telah menyampaikan Pemberitahuan Rencana kepada Otoritas Jasa Keuangan, melalui Surat Direksi No. 068/BNBR/CS-OJK/VII/25 tertanggal 31 Juli 2025, serta mengumumkan Rencana Rapat ini juga pada 31 Juli 2025 melalui situs web Perseroan, situs web Bursa Efek Indonesia dan situs web Kustodian Sentral Efek Indonesia.

Perseroan juga menyampaikan Pemanggilan Rencana kepada Otoritas Jasa Keuangan, melalui Surat Sekretaris Perusahaan No. 072/BNBR/CS-OJK/VIII/25 tertanggal 15 Agustus 2025 serta mengumumkan Panggilan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Luar Biasa ini pada 15 Agustus 2025 melalui situs web Perseroan, situs web Bursa Efek Indonesia dan situs web Kustodian Sentral Efek Indonesia.

Perseroan kemudian menyampaikan Perubahan Jadwal RUPS Luar Biasa kepada Bursa Efek Indonesia, melalui Surat Sekretaris Perusahaan No. 084/BNBR/CS-BEI/IX/25 tertanggal 5 September 2025 serta mengumumkan Panggilan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Luar Biasa ini pada 5 September 2025 melalui situs web Perseroan, situs web Bursa Efek Indonesia dan situs web Kustodian Sentral Efek Indonesia.

RUPS Luar Biasa pada tanggal 10 September 2025 tersebut di atas dihadiri oleh pemegang saham yang mewakili 139.778.035.241 lembar saham atau 80,60% dari seluruh pemegang saham. Dengan demikian rapat dinyatakan kuorum dan telah sesuai dengan ketentuan Pasal 43 ayat (a) POJK No.15/2020 dan Pasal 13 ayat 2.5 a Anggaran Dasar Perseroan, bahwa RUPSLB sah apabila dihadiri/diwakili lebih dari 3/4 (tiga per empat) bagian dari jumlah seluruh saham dengan hak suara yang sah yang telah dikeluarkan Perseroan.

# PERHITUNGAN SUARA PADA RAPAT UMUM PEMEGANG SAHAM LUAR BIASA

Pada pelaksanaan RUPS Luar Biasa Perseroan di tanggal 10 September 2025, Perseroan telah menunjuk pihak-pihak independen untuk melakukan perhitungan suara dan/atau melakukan validasi dalam rapat dari Biro Administrasi Efek PT Electronic Data Interchange Indonesia (EDII) dan Notaris Humberg Lie, SH, SE, Mkn.

# EXECUTION OF EXTRAORDINARY GENERAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS IN 2025

Pursuant to the Articles of Association, the Company has held the Extraordinary GMS on September 10, 2025, in Nusantara Room, Bakrie Tower 36th floor, Jakarta. Based on the regulations, prior to the GMS the Company has informed the Plan Announcement to the Financial Services Authority, through Corporate Secretary's Letter No. 068/BNBR/CS-OJK/VII/25 dated July 31, 2025, and announced the Meeting Plan also on July 31, 2025 through Company website, Indonesia Stock Exchange website and Indonesia Central Securities Depository website.

The Company has informed the Plan Notice to the Financial Services Authority, through Corporate Secretary's Letter No. 072/BNBR/CS-OJK/VIII/25 dated August 15, 2025, and also announced the Invitation of Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders on August 15, 2025, through Company website, Indonesia Stock Exchange website and Indonesia Central Securities Depository website.

The Company then informed Change in Schedule for Extraordinary GMS to the Indonesian Stock Exchange, through Corporate Secretary's Letter No. 084/BNBR/CS-BEI/IX/25 dated September 5, 2025, and also announced the Invitation of Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders on September 5, 2025, through Company website, Indonesia Stock Exchange website and Indonesia Central Securities Depository website.

Extraordinary GMS on September 10, 2025 was attended by shareholders that represent 139,778,035,241 shares or 80.60% from the total shareholders. Thereby the meeting was declared a quorum and was in accordance with the provisions of Article 43 letter (a) POJK No.15/2020 and Article 13 paragraph 2.5 aof the Company's Articles of Association, that the Extraordinary GMS is valid if attended / represented by more than 3/4 (three-fourth) of the total number of shares with valid voting rights issued by the Company.

# COUNTING OF VOTES AT THE EXTRAORDINARY GENERAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS

At the Company's Extraordinary GMS on September 10, 2025, the Company appointed independent parties to count the votes and/or carry out validation at the meeting from the Securities Administration Bureau of PT Electronic Data Interchange Indonesia (EDII) and Notary Humberg Lie, SH, SE, Mkn.
