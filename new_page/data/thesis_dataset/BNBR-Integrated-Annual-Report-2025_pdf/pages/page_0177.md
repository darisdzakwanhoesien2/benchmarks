VKTR then switched its business focus to electric vehicles (EV) trading &amp; manufacturing, assembly and carrosserie of BYD electric busses (E-bus), and EV spare parts trading which will be carried out with business partners. In the future, VKTR may consider expansion towards activities such as manufacturing of bus and truck bodies, producing EV spare parts, battery &amp; module packs, retrofit Internal Combustion Engine (ICE) vehicle to EV, and building EV charging station &amp; battery swap.

Currently VKTR's main sales are components for commercial vehicles, which contributed up to 87% of total sales tonnage in 2025. Manufacturing of components for commercial vehicles is done through subsidiary, that is PT Bakrie Autoparts (BA) which has been trusted as component supplier for two major OEMs in Indonesia, namely Mitsubishi and Hino. This reinforced BA's performance in commercial vehicle segment.

The commodity market which has been the backbone of commercial vehicle segment has experienced volatility in 2025 as a result of geopolitical crisis, and this was reflected in the performance of BA sales volume. However, BA is able to maintain their revenue by diversifying its product portfolios with increasing complexity and have high selling prices, in addition, BA strives to sell products with higher margins. BA also continued its efforts to tap into the passenger vehicle and aftersales market in order to support its business sustainability.

Moreover, BA will also boost its sales in non-automotive segment (general casting) in the coming years. BA's current customer profile also includes various agricultural and heavy equipment producers, right now BA is actively prospecting partnership with other producers in order to expand this market segment.

Given its aggressive business expansion and initiatives, we believe VKTR is poised to experience strong multiyear earnings growth ahead.
