# BMI

## Kunjungan Industri Management Trainee BMI

Industrial Visit by BMI Management Trainee

Management Trainee Grup PT Bakrie Metal Industries (BMI) Angkatan X melaksanakan kunjungan industri ke PT Krakatau Steel, PT Bakrie Construction, dan PT Southeast Asia Pipe Industries. Kunjungan ini berlangsung selama dua hari dari 31 Juli 2025 hingga 1 Agustus 2025.

Kegiatan ini memberikan kesempatan untuk melihat langsung proses produksi, pemanfaatan teknologi, dan praktik manajemen di industri manufaktur dan konstruksi. Selain memperluas wawasan teknis, kunjungan ini juga memperkuat pemahaman terhadap rantai nilai industri baja nasional serta mendorong kolaborasi antar unit dalam Grup BMI.

Management Trainee of PT Bakrie Metal Industries (BMI) Group Class X carried out an industrial visit to PT Krakatau Steel, PT Bakrie Construction, and PT Southeast Asia Pipe Industries. This visit lasted for two days, from July 31, 2025 until August 1, 2025.

This activity provides an opportunity to see firsthand the production processes, technology utilization and management practices in the manufacturing and construction industries. Apart from broadening technical insight, this visit also strengthened understanding of the national steel industry value chain and encouraged collaboration between units within the BMI Group.

# HELIO

## Instalasi EV Charging Unit di Stasiun DAMRI Ciputat oleh Helio

EV Charging Unit Installation at DAMRI's Ciputat Station by Helio

PT Helio Synar Energi (Helio) menyelesaikan instalasi 24 EV Charging Unit di Stasiun DAMRI Ciputat yang merupakan pusat penyediaan daya bagi bus listrik DAMRI yang beroperasi pada layanan Transjakarta. Stasiun Ciputat merupakan charging station DAMRI ke-4 dan berperan penting dalam memastikan kesiapan bus listrik DAMRI tetap optimal, peranan ini semakin didukung dengan spesifikasi EV charger yang hanya membutuhkan waktu 2 jam pengisian daya untuk menjamin jarak tempuh bus listrik DAMRI hingga 250 km.

PT Helio Synar Energi (Helio) has completed the installation of 24 EV charging units at the DAMRI Ciputat Station, which serves as a power supply hub for DAMRI electric buses operating on the Transjakarta route. The Ciputat Station is DAMRI's fourth charging station and plays a crucial role in ensuring the optimal readiness of DAMRI's electric buses; this role is further supported by the EV chargers' specifications, which require only 2 hours of charging to ensure a range of up to 250 km for DAMRI's electric buses.
