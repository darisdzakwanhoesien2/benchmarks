## PENAMBAHAN MODAL TANPA HAK MEMESAN EFEK TERLEBIH DAHULU TAHAP KEEMPAT KONVERSI KETIGA

Sehubungan dengan hasil keputusan RUPSLB tanggal 21 November 2018, pada tanggal 8 Desember 2023 PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk telah melaksanakan penambahan jumlah modal disetor dan ditempatkan dengan mengeluarkan saham biasa seri E sejumlah 38.445.133.000 saham dengan nilai nominal Rp64 per saham melalui mekanisme PMTHMETD. Pihak yang mengambil saham adalah Fountain City Investment Ltd.

Penambahan saham ini dilakukan Perseroan dalam rangka restrukturisasi utang Perseroan kepada para krediturnya. Sebelum pelaksanaan PMTHMETD, jumlah saham yang disetor dan ditempatkan Perseroan adalah sebesar 121.612.324.509 saham dan setelah pelaksanaan PMTHMETD menjadi sebesar 160.057.457.509 saham dengan nilai nominal Rp 64 per saham.

## CAPITAL INCREASE WITH NON PREEMPTIVE RIGHTS ("NPR") FOURTH STAGE CONVERSION THREE

In accordance with resolution of EGMS held on November 21, 2018, on December 8, 2023 PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk has executed increase of issued and fully paid capital by issuing ordinary shares E series totaling at 38,445,133,000 shares with par value of Rp64 per share through capital increase with NPR. The parties that absorbed the shares were Fountain City Investment Ltd.

This addition of shares was carried out by the Company to restructure the Company's debt to its creditors. Before the execution of capital increase with NPR the Company issued and fully paid capital was as much as 121,612,324,509 shares and after the execution of capital increase with NPR its total increased to 160,057,457,509 shares with par value of Rp64 per share.
