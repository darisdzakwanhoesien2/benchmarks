# HELIO

Instalasi EV Charger di Pool Sinarjaya oleh Helio

EV Chargers Installation at Sinarjaya Pool by Helio

PT Helio Synar Energi (Helio) menyelesaikan instalasi 7 unit VECTRA EV Charger (2 x 100 kW) di pool Sinarjaya untuk mendukung operasional armada bus listrik. Instalasi dilakukan langsung dengan supervise tim Helio dan dilengkapi layanan Full Maintenance Contract (FMC).

PT Helio Synar Energi (Helio) has completed the installation of seven VECTRA EV Chargers (2 x 100 kW) at the Sinarjaya depot to support the operation of Sinarjaya's electric bus fleet. The installation was carried out under the direct supervision of the Helio team and includes a Full Maintenance Contract (FMC).

# BNBR

Bakrie Middle Management Development Program (BMMDP)

Bakrie Middle Management Development Program (BMMDP)

Grup BNBR mengadakan Bakrie Middle Management Development Program (BMMDP) yang diikuti oleh 25 peserta di lingkup Grup BNBR. Pada kesempatan ini, para peserta mulai melakukan presentasi improvement project di hadapan panelis yang terdiri dari senior manajemen dan Akademisi Senior Universitas Bakrie (UB).

Dari manajemen BNBR diwakili oleh Bapak Okder Pendrian selaku Chief Human Capital &amp; Office Support, dan dari UB diwakili oleh Bapak Prof. M. Taufiq Amir, S.E., M.M., Ph.D. dan Bapak Arief Bimantoro Suharko, BSEE, M.Sc.Eng, Ph.D.

Presentasi ini dibagi dalam beberapa sesi yang dimulai di bulan Februari hingga Juli 2025. Bagi peserta yang menyelesaikan seluruh rangkaian program dengan hasil baik, akan masuk ke dalam talent pool Grup BNBR dan memiliki kesempatan mengembangkan karir di posisi-posisi strategis.

BNBR Group held Bakrie Middle Management Development Program (BMMDP) which was attended by 25 participants within the BNBR Group. On this occasion, the participants began presenting their improvement projects in front of panelists consisting of senior management and Senior Academics at Bakrie University (UB).

From BNBR management, Mr. Okder Pendrian as Chief Human Capital &amp; Office Support, and from UB represented by Mr. Prof. M. Taufiq Amir, SE, MM, Ph.D. and Mr. Arief Bimantoro Suharko, BSEE, M.Sc.Eng, Ph.D.

This presentation was divided into several sessions starting from February to July 2025. Participants who complete the entire series of programs with good results will enter the BNBR Group talent pool and obtained the opportunity to develop a career in strategic positions.
