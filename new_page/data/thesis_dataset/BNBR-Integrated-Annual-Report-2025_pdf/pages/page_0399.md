# PELATIHAN DAN PENGEMBANGAN [SEOJK F.22] [GRI 404-2]

Penguatan kompetensi sumber daya manusia terus kami dorong sebagai bagian dari strategi menjaga keberlanjutan usaha. Kesempatan yang setara kami berikan kepada setiap karyawan untuk mengasah keahlian melalui beragam program pelatihan dan pendampingan, sehingga pengembangan kapasitas individu berjalan selaras dengan kebutuhan organisasi dan target keberlanjutan Perusahaan. Upaya ini sekaligus memastikan setiap talenta mampu berkembang optimal serta berkontribusi nyata terhadap pertumbuhan bisnis.

Program pelatihan disusun dalam dua kategori utama, yaitu Program Pelatihan Manajerial dan Kepemimpinan serta Program Pelatihan Keterampilan Khusus. Dalam merancang program jangka panjang yang berkelanjutan, BNBR bekerja sama dengan Bakrie Learning Center (BLC). Perencanaan program tersebut dilaksanakan melalui tahapan berikut:

1. Penilaian dan identifikasi bakat.
2. Implementasi Rencana Suksesia Bakrie
3. Pengembangan program talent leadership melalui Bakrie Leadership Development Program
4. Formulasi dan Sosialisasi Bakrie Values atau Trimatra Bakrie
5. Melaksanakan Bakrie Engagement Program

Informasi rinci mengenai keseluruhan program pelatihan dan pengembangan karyawan dapat dilihat pada bagian Sumber Daya Manusia dalam laporan ini.

# TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT [SEOJK F.22] [GRI 404-2]

We continuously promote the strengthening of human resource competencies as a fundamental element of our strategic approach to ensure business sustainability. We provide equal opportunities to all employees to develop their skills through diverse training and mentoring programs, so that individual capacity development is aligned with organizational needs and the company's sustainability targets. This effort also ensures that all talent can achieve optimal development and make a tangible contribution to business growth.

The training programs are organized into two main categories: Managerial and Leadership Training Programs and Specialized Skills Training Programs. In the development of long-term sustainable programs, BNBR collaborates with Bakrie Learning Center (BLC). The planning process for these programs is conducted through the following stages:

1. Assessment and identification of talents.
2. Implementation of the Bakrie Succession Plan.
3. Development of a talent leadership program through the Bakrie Leadership Development Program.
4. Formulation and socialization of Bakrie Values or the Bakrie Trimatra.
5. Executing the Bakrie Engagement Program.

Detailed information about the entire employee training and development program can be found in the Human Resources section of this report.
