4. Mengawasi, mengevaluasi, dan memberikan saran/rekomendasi atas proses persetujuan proposal investasi, divestasi, dan pembiayaan, serta kegiatan terkait lainnya. Dalam memberikan saran/rekomendasi atas proses persetujuan proposal investasi / divestasi / pembiayaan, Komite mempertimbangkan risiko strategis (termasuk risiko reputasi dan risiko hukum / legal / regulasi), risiko finansial (termasuk risiko likuiditas), risiko pasar / komersial, dan risiko operasional atas rencana suatu kegiatan investasi, divestasi, dan pembiayaan, serta risiko-risiko lingkungan, sosial, serta potensi risiko perubahan iklim. Selain itu, penilaian risiko atas penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan dianggap sebagai langkah strategis yang esensial. BNBR mengakui bahwa integrasi pertimbangan lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola (ESG) ke dalam praktek keuangan Perseroan tidak hanya memitigasi risiko jangka panjang, tetapi juga mengidentifikasi peluang baru untuk pertumbuhan berkelanjutan. Melalui evaluasi komprehensif terhadap potensi risiko pasar, risiko operasional, dan risiko kredit, serta penerapan strategi mitigasi yang efektif, Perseroan berkomitmen untuk memastikan keberlanjutan finansial yang bertanggung jawab, mendukung inisiatif lingkungan dan sosial, serta memberikan nilai tambah bagi pemangku kepentingan. [SEOJK E.3] [GRI 2-13]

In giving input/recommendation on approval process of investment / divestment / funding proposal, the Committee considers strategic risks (including reputation risk and law / legal / regulation risk), financial risk (including mismatch risk), market / commercial risk, and operation risk affecting the investment, divestment, and funding plan, also the risks related to environmental and social impacts, as well as the risk related to climate change. Moreover, the risk assessment for the implementation of sustainable finance is considered an essential strategic step. BNBR recognizes that the integration of environmental, social, and governance (ESG) considerations into the company's financial practices not only mitigates long-term risks but also identifies new opportunities for sustainable growth. Through comprehensive evaluation of potential market risks, operational risks, and credit risks, along with the application of effective mitigation strategies, the company is committed to ensuring responsible financial sustainability, supporting environmental and social initiatives, and delivering added value to stakeholders.
