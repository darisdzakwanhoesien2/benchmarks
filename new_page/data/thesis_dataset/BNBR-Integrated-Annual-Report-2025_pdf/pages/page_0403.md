To strengthen the evaluation mechanism, BNBR implements the Bakrie Performance Contract (BPC) system, which is designed to be transparent and aligned with job descriptions and objectives.

The assessment composition in the BPC assigns 80% weight to KPIs and 20% to competencies, so it not only focuses on target achievement but also on employee capacity development. This approach helps ensure a comprehensive evaluation process while promoting each individual's professional growth. By the end of 2025, all employees will have participated in performance evaluations and career development processes, demonstrating the implementation of a comprehensive evaluation system across all BNBR units. # CSR DI BIDANG PENGEMBANGAN SOSIAL DAN KEMASYARAKATAN
## CSR IN SOCIAL AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT

Komitmen terhadap pengembangan sosial dan kemasyarakatan diwujudkan Grup BNBR melalui inisiatif Bakrie Untuk Negeri yang menaungi empat program utama: Cerdas untuk Negeri, Sehat untuk Negeri, Peduli untuk Negeri, dan Kemitraan untuk Negeri. Melalui rangkaian program tersebut, kami berupaya meningkatkan kualitas hidup masyarakat Indonesia dengan menitikberatkan pada aspek kesehatan, pendidikan, dan ekonomi. Perhatian terhadap pemberdayaan masyarakat sekitar area operasional juga senantiasa menjadi bagian dari tanggung jawab perusahaan dalam mendorong pembangunan manusia serta peningkatan kesejahteraan sosial.

# PEMBERDAYAAN MASYARAKAT SEKITAR PERUSAHAAN [SEOJK F.25]

Dukungan terhadap masyarakat di sekitar wilayah operasional terus kami perkuat melalui berbagai inisiatif pemberdayaan yang dirancang selaras dengan kebutuhan lokal. Fokus ini kami jalankan sebagai bagian dari komitmen berkelanjutan untuk memperluas manfaat kehadiran perusahaan, sekaligus memastikan kontribusi nyata terhadap peningkatan kesejahteraan sosial masyarakat.

BNBR Group's commitment to social and community development is realized through the Bakrie for the Nation initiative, which comprises four main programs: Smart for the Nation, Healthy for the Nation, Caring for the Nation, and Partnership for the Nation. These programs focus on improving the quality of life for Indonesian society by focusing on health, education, and the economy. Additionally, the company's ongoing commitment to empowering communities within its operational areas remains a key responsibility, committed to fostering human development and advancing social welfare.

# COMMUNITY EMPOWERMENT AROUND THE COMPANY [SEOJK F.25]

Our support for the communities surrounding our operational areas is consistently reinforced through diverse empowerment initiatives tailored to align with local needs. This focus is pursued as part of our continuous commitment to expand the benefits of the company's presence, while also ensuring tangible contributions toward enhancing the social welfare of the community.
