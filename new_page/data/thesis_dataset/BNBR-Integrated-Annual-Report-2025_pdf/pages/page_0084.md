# BPI

Halal Bi Halal dan Upacara Bendera BPI
BPI's Halal Bi Halal and Flag-Raising Ceremony

Dalam rangka merayakan Idul Fitri 1446 H, PT Bakrie Pipe Industries (BPI) menyelenggarakan kegiatan Halal Bihalal dan Upacara Bendera pada Selasa, 15 April 2025. Kegiatan ini diikuti oleh Direksi, Manajemen, serta seluruh karyawan BPI.

In order to celebrate Eid al-Fitr 1446 H, PT Bakrie Pipe Industries (BPI) is holding a Halal Bihalal activity and a Flag Ceremony on Tuesday, April 15 2025. This activity was attended by the Directors, Management, and all employees of BPI.

# BPI

Kunjungan IISIA ke BPI
IISIA Visit to BPI

PT Bakrie Pipe Industries (BPI) menerima kunjungan silaturahmi dan plant tour dari Sekretariat The Indonesian Iron &amp; Steel Industry Association (IISIA) sebagai bagian dari upaya mempererat hubungan antar anggota serta mendorong pertumbuhan industri baja nasional yang berkelanjutan.

PT Bakrie Pipe Industries (BPI) received a friendly visit and plant tour from the The Indonesian Iron &amp; Steel Industry Association (IISIA) Secretariat as part of efforts to strengthen relations between members and encourage the sustainable growth of the national steel industry.

# BNBR

Sosialisasi Internal Audit oleh Internal Audit Grup BNBR di Kantor BMI
Socialization of Internal Audit by BNBR Group's Internal Audit at BMI Office

Tim Internal Audit PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk (BNBR) mengadakan sosialisasi aspek GCG, business ethics protocol, dan pelaporan penerimaan gratifikasi kepada Grup PT Bakrie Metal Industries (BMI).

Acara ini diikuti 105 karyawan dari PT Bakrie Pipe Industries (BPI), PT Bakrie Construction (BCons), dan PT Southeast Asia Pipe Industries (SEAPI) guna memperkuat daya saing industri di tengah dinamika global.

The Internal Audit Team of PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk (BNBR) held outreach on aspects of GCG, business ethics protocol, and reporting on gratification receipts to PT Bakrie Metal Industries (BMI) Group.

This event was attended by 105 employees from PT Bakrie Pipe Industries (BPI), PT Bakrie Construction (BCons), and PT Southeast Asia Pipe Industries (SEAPI).
