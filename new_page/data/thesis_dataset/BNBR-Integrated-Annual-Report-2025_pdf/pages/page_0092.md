# BNBR

## Strategic Meeting BNBR

BNBR Strategic Meeting

Pada penghujung tahun 2025 grup PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk (BNBR), Grup PT Bakrie Global Ventura (BGV), dan Grup PT Visi Media Asia Tbk (VIVA) mengikuti arahan dari Direktur Utama &amp; CEO BNBR Bapak Anindya Bakrie yang dikemas dengan tajuk "BNBR Group, BGV Group, VIVA Group Strategic Briefing 2025". Acara ini dihadiri seluruh Senior Management Team (SMT) dari ketiga perusahaan.

Dalam pidatonya, Bapak Anindya Bakrie menyampaikan pentingnya Grup Bakrie untuk terus bertumbuh, berkembang, dan maju menjadi perusahaan global dengan berfokus pada transisi menuju green industry dan mengadopsi perkembangan teknologi artificial intelligence (AI).

Dalam kesempatan ini, Komisaris Independen BNBR Bapak Adrian T.P. Panggabean memaparkan lansekap ekonomi terkini di ranah domestik dan global. Dilanjutkan dengan peneliti Bandung Fe Institute dan AI Research Center di Institute Technology Del Bapak Prof. Hokky Situngkir, yang mempresentasikan tentang urgensi adopsi teknologi AI dalam mendorong pertumbuhan bisnis untuk memperkuat strategi efisiensi dan inovasi.

At the end of 2025 PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk (BNBR) Group, PT Bakrie Global Ventura (BGV) Group, and PT Visi Media Asia Tbk (VIVA) Group received direction from the President Director &amp; CEO of BNBR, Mr. Anindya Bakrie, which was presented in the event "BNBR Group, BGV Group, VIVA Group Strategic Briefing 2025". This event was attended by all Senior Management Teams (SMT) from the three companies.

In his speech, Mr. Anindya Bakrie conveyed the importance of the Bakrie Group to continue to grow, develop and progress into a global company. Namely, by focusing on the transition to a green industry and adopting developments in artificial intelligence (AI) technology.

On this occasion, the Independent Commissioner of BNBR, Mr. Adrian T.P. Panggabean, presented the latest economic landscape in the domestic and global domains. Followed by researchers from the Bandung Fe Institute and the AI Research Center at the Institute Technology Del Mr. Prof. Hokky Situngkir, who presented the urgency of adopting AI technology in driving business growth to strengthen efficiency and innovation strategies.
