# BINA USAHA MANDIRI MIZUSAWA

Sertifikat Apresiasi dari Komatsu Group Indonesia QC Supplier Convention ke-7 tahun 2025

Appreciation Certificate from 7th Komatsu Group Indonesia QC Supplier Convention year 2025

SEPTEMBER 2025 | SEPTEMBER 2025

Pada 16 September 2025 Bedil Team dari PT Bina Usaha Mandiri Mizusawa memperoleh sertifikat apresiasi untuk inisiatif bertopik "Menurunkan Reject Swelling di Part Ring D6S" dari ajang Komatsu Group Indonesia QC Supplier Convention ke-7 tahun 2025 yang diadakan oleh PT Komatsu Indonesia.

On September 16, 2025 Bedil Team from PT Bina Usaha Mandiri Mizusawa received appreciation certificate for its initiative with the topic titled "Decreasing Reject Swelling on Part Ring D6S" from 7th Komatsu Group Indonesia QC Supplier Convention year 2025 event carried out by PT Komatsu Indonesia.

# MULTI KONTROL NUSANTARA

Penghargaan Kecelakaan Nihil (Zero Accident)

Appreciation of Zero Accident

FEBRUARI 2025 | FEBRUARY 2025

Pada 26 Juni 2025, Gubernur Kalimantan Timur memberikan piagam penghargaan Kecelakaan Nihil (Zero Accident) Tahun 2024 kepada PT Multi Kontrol Nusantara Site KPC Sangatta atas prestasi dalam melaksanakan Program Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3) sehingga mencapai 763.304 jam kerja orang tanpa kecelakaan kerja, terhitung sejak 9 September 2021 hingga 31 Desember 2024.

On June 26, 2025, The Governor of East Kalimantan gave an appreciation charter of Zero Accident in 2024 to PT Multi Kontrol Nusantara Site KPC Sangatta for its achievements in implementing the Occupational Safety and Health (K3) Program so that it achieved 763,304 working hours without work accidents, starting from 9 September 2021 to 31 December 2024

On June 26, 2025, The Governor of East Kalimantan gave an appreciation charter to PT Multi Kontrol Nusantara Site KPC Sangatta for its achievements in implementing the HIV &amp; AIDS Prevention and Control Program in the workplace year 2024 with Platinum category.
