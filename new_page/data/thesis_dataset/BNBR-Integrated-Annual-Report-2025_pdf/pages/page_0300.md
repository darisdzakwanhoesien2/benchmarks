# PENGENDALIAN BISNIS DALAM SISTEM PENGENDALIAN INTERNAL

Sebagai bagian dari penguatan sistem pengendalian internal, BNBR membentuk Divisi Pengendalian Bisnis untuk memastikan efektivitas pemantauan terhadap perkembangan kinerja keuangan, operasional, dan bisnis unit usaha di bawah BNBR.

Divisi Pengendalian Bisnis berperan dalam:

1. Monitoring dan Evaluasi Kinerja
» Mengawasi pencapaian target keuangan dan operasional setiap unit usaha.
» Mengidentifikasi potensi risiko bisnis yang dapat mempengaruhi pencapaian kinerja.

2. Analisis dan Pelaporan
» Menyediakan laporan berkala kepada Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris mengenai kondisi keuangan, operasional, serta aspek strategis unit usaha.
» Menganalisis tren dan memberikan rekomendasi perbaikan berbasis data untuk mendukung pengambilan keputusan.

3. Kepatuhan dan Pengendalian Risiko
» Memastikan setiap unit usaha mematuhi kebijakan internal dan regulasi eksternal yang berlaku.
» Berkoordinasi dengan departemen Risk Management dan Audit Internal dalam mengidentifikasi serta memitigasi risiko bisnis yang berpotensi menghambat pertumbuhan usaha.

# BUSINESS CONTROL IN INTERNAL CONTROL SYSTEM

As part of reinforcement of the internal control system, BNBR established the Business Control Division to ensure effective monitoring of the development of financial, operational and business performance of business units under BNBR.

The Business Control Division plays a role in:

1. Performance Monitoring and Evaluation
» Overseeing the achievement of financial and operational targets of each business unit.
» Identifying potential business risks that may affect performance achievement.

2. Analysis and Reporting
» Provide regular reports to the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners on the financial condition, operations, and strategic aspects of business units.
» Analyze trends and provide data-based improvement recommendations to support decision making.

3. Compliance and Risk Control
» Ensure each business unit complies with applicable internal policies and external regulations.
» Coordinate with Risk Management and Internal Audit department in identifying and mitigating business risks that could potentially hamper business growth.
