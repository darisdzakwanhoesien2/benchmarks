Di luar sektor manufaktur, BNBR terus memajukan inisiatif infrastruktur strategis melalui PT Bakrie Indo Infrastructure (BIIN). Pada tahun 2025, Perseroan menyelesaikan akuisisi 100% kepemilikan PT Cimanggis Cibitung Tollways (CCT), pemegang konsesi jalan tol Cimanggis–Cibitung sepanjang 26,4 km di Jawa Barat. Pencapaian ini memperkuat posisi BNBR dalam pengembangan konektivitas nasional serta menyediakan sumber pendapatan jangka panjang yang stabil dan berbasis aset, mengingat potensi CCT yang sangat besar dengan lokasi strategisnya yang melewati berbagai pemukiman padat, serta rencana pengembangan jaringan jalan tol Jakarta-Cikampek yang akan melintasi CCT sehingga berpotensi menambah peningkatan lalu lintas CCT.

Selain itu, BIIN tetap terlibat dalam proyek infrastruktur energi, termasuk pengembangan pipa gas Kalija di koridor utara Jawa hingga Kalimantan, guna mendukung distribusi energi dan ekspansi industri di berbagai wilayah strategis.

BNBR juga berkontribusi dalam pembangunan infrastruktur dengan memanfaatkan teknologi digital serta mendukung peningkatan konektivitas nasional melalui anak usaha PT Multi Kontrol Nusantara (MKN). Pada tahun 2025, MKN mencatat pertumbuhan pendapatan sekitar 14,7%, meningkat menjadi Rp 358 miliar dari Rp 312 miliar pada tahun 2024. Kinerja ini didorong oleh meningkatnya permintaan atas sistem kontrol digital, infrastruktur jaringan, serta solusi teknologi terintegrasi pada berbagai proyek industri dan infrastruktur. Peningkatan tersebut menegaskan peran MKN dalam mendukung transformasi digital dan pembangunan infrastruktur cerdas, sekaligus membuka peluang seiring pesatnya pertumbuhan ekonomi digital Indonesia.

Melalui keterlibatannya dalam berbagai program strategis nasional yang berfokus pada pembangunan infrastruktur, pengembangan industri, dan kemajuan teknologi, Perseroan tetap berkomitmen untuk mendukung Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan (SDGs), khususnya Tujuan 9 dan 11. Beyond the manufacturing sector, BNBR continues to advance strategic infrastructure initiatives through PT Bakrie Indo Infrastructure (BIIN). During 2025, the Company completed the acquisition of 100% ownership in PT Cimanggis Cibitung Tollways (CCT), the concession holder of the 26.4 km Cimanggis–Cibitung toll road in West Java. This milestone strengthens BNBR's position in national connectivity development and provides a stable, long-term asset-backed revenue stream, considering CCT's enormous potential with its strategic location which passes through various densely populated residential areas, as well as the development plan of Jakarta-Cikampek toll road network which will intersect CCT, thus giving potential of increased traffic in CCT.

In addition, BIIN remains involved in energy infrastructure projects, including the Kalija gas pipeline development along the northern corridor of Java and into Kalimantan, supporting energy distribution and industrial expansion across key regions.

BNBR also contributes to infrastructure development by leveraging digital technologies and supporting initiatives to strengthen Indonesia's digital connectivity through its subsidiary, PT Multi Kontrol Nusantara (MKN). In 2025, MKN recorded revenue growth of approximately 14.7%, increasing to Rp 358 billion from Rp 312 billion in 2024. This improvement reflects expanding demand for digital control systems, network infrastructure, and integrated technology solutions across industrial and infrastructure projects. The positive performance highlights MKN's role in supporting digital transformation and smart infrastructure development, positioning the Company to capture opportunities arising from Indonesia's rapidly growing digital economy.

Through its participation in strategic national initiatives focused on infrastructure, industrial development, and technological advancement, the Company remains committed to contributing to the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), particularly Goals 9 and 11. These priorities emphasize resilient infrastructure, inclusive industrialization, innovation, and the creation of sustainable urban environments. By aligning its business activities with these global objectives, BNBR seeks to generate long-term economic value while supporting environmentally responsible growth and improved quality of life for communities. The Company believes that integrating sustainability principles into core operations is essential to building a resilient and future-ready business.
