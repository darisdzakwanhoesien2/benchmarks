# SISTEM PENGELOLAAN LIMBAH

Pengelolaan limbah di lingkungan Grup dilakukan dengan membagi limbah ke dalam dua kategori utama, yaitu limbah Bahan Berbahaya dan Beracun (B3) serta limbah non-B3 dari aktivitas produksi dan pendukung lainnya. Seluruh proses mengacu pada prinsip 3R (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle) yang dilengkapi prosedur penanganan dan pembuangan sesuai ketentuan. [SEOJK F.14] [GRI 306-2]

Untuk limbah B3, pengawasan diterapkan sejak tahap penyimpanan, pengumpulan, hingga pengangkutan, sebelum diserahkan kepada mitra pengelola limbah yang telah bekerja sama dengan perusahaan. Limbah cair, termasuk dari proses produksi, diolah melalui Instalasi Pengelolaan Air Limbah (IPAL) dengan sistem sirkulasi atau sedimentasi ke tangki (sludge retrieval) guna memastikan efektivitas pengolahan. [SEOJK F.13] [GRI 306-3] [GRI 306-4] [GRI 306-5]

Hasil pengolahan limbah cair dipantau dan diuji secara berkala oleh laboratorium bersertifikat, kemudian dilaporkan kepada Dinas Lingkungan Hidup di masing-masing wilayah operasional. Sepanjang tahun pelaporan, tidak terdapat pelepasan polutan signifikan maupun insiden tumpahan besar di area operasional perusahaan dan entitas anak. [SEOJK F.15]

# WASTE MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS

Waste management within the Group environment is conducted by categorizing waste into two main types: Hazardous and Toxic Materials (B3) waste and non-hazardous (non-B3) waste arising from production activities and other supporting operations. The entire process complies with the 3Rs principle (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle), complemented by handling and disposal procedures in accordance with regulations. [SEOJK F.14] [GRI 306-2]

For B3 waste, supervision is implemented from the storage, collection, to transportation prior to transfer to the waste management partners collaborating with the company. Liquid waste, including that generated from production processes, is treated via a Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) with a circulation or sedimentation system within a tank (sludge retrieval) to ensure treatment efficacy. [SEOJK F.13] [GRI 306-3] [GRI 306-4] [GRI 306-5]

The results of liquid waste treatment are monitored and periodically tested by a certified laboratory, then reported to the Environmental Agency within each operational region. Throughout the reporting year, there have been no significant pollutant discharges or major spill incidents within the company's operational areas and subsidiaries. [SEOJK F.15]
