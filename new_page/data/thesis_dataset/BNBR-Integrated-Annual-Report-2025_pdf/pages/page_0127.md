The Company's continued efforts to sharpen its business—through efficiency programs and selective investments that strengthen BNBR's long-term platform—remained evident in 2025. Operating profit was recorded at Rp 116.86 billion, compared with Rp 282.87 billion in 2024, reflecting tighter margins and higher operating costs. Nevertheless, net profit increase to Rp 502.74 billion in 2025 compared Rp 336.05 billion in 2024, supported mainly by non-recurring gains related to strategic transactions, including gains on bargain purchase and remeasurement of equity, and disciplined funding structure execution.

Meanwhile, on the balance sheet, the Company recorded an increase in current assets in 2025 to Rp 4.41 trillion from Rp 3.63 trillion in 2024. This expansion was mainly driven by restricted cash in banks, which rose to Rp 700.61 billion, alongside cash and cash equivalents of Rp 236.65 billion. Non-current assets increased sharply to Rp 19.15 trillion in 2025 from Rp 3.20 trillion in 2024, primarily reflecting consolidation of the Cimanggis—Cibitung toll road through the recognition of toll road concession rights of Rp 14.998 trillion. Other non-current assets also rose in line with supporting investment advances and long-term project requirements. As of year-end 2025, total assets reached Rp 23.57 trillion, a marked increase from Rp 6.83 trillion in 2024.

On the liabilities side, total liabilities rose to Rp 18.90 trillion in 2025 from Rp 2.92 trillion in 2024, mainly reflecting structured financing to support the CCT acquisition and consolidation, and related long-term investments.

The Company has continued to strengthen its capital structure, and during the 2016–2024 period successfully restructured debt amounting to Rp 25.66 trillion, including via Non-Preemptive Rights (NPR) capital increases and Mandatory Convertible Bonds (MCB) conversions into Company shares. This restructuring program was completed by the end of 2024, reducing financial pressure and improving balance-sheet flexibility. In 2025, that stronger base enabled BNBR to pursue strategic portfolio expansion with disciplined funding decisions, while maintaining prudent oversight on leverage and liquidity management.
