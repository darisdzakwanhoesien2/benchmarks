# ENERGI DAN TRANSISI MENUJU ENERGI TERBARUKAN

Pemanfaatan energi baru dan terbarukan (EBT) terus diperluas sebagai bagian dari komitmen efisiensi energi yang selaras dengan peta jalan pemerintah. Integrasi prinsip konservasi energi dilakukan melalui sistem pemantauan konsumsi listrik dan bahan bakar yang melibatkan seluruh karyawan.

Kesadaran akan besarnya kontribusi energi fosil terhadap perubahan iklim mendorong penerapan teknologi ramah lingkungan, instalasi yang sesuai kebutuhan, serta sistem manajemen energi untuk mengevaluasi penggunaan secara berkala. Listrik dimanfaatkan untuk operasional pabrik dan kantor, sedangkan bahan bakar solar digunakan bagi alat heavy-duty, kendaraan operasional, dan diesel generator.

Recognition of this performance is reflected in obtaining certification through the Corporate Performance Rating Program in Environmental Management (PROPER), a government initiative aimed at improving environmental management standards in accordance with Minister of Environment Regulation No. 6 of 2013. Furthermore, multiple other environmental certification standards have also been implemented.

1. Bakrie Autoparts (BA) – ISO 14001:2015 from TÜV SÜD Indonesia
2. Bakrie Constructions (BCONs) – ISO 14001:2015 from Care Certification Private Limited accredited by International Accreditation (IAS)
3. Bakrie Metal Industries (BMI) – ISO 14001:2015 from Care Certification Private Limited accredited by International Accreditation (IAS)
4. Bakrie Pipe Industries (BPI) – ISO 14001:2015 from TÜV Rheinland
5. Bakrie Power (BP) - ISO 14001:2015 from Tafa Sertifikasi Indonesia
6. Bina Usaha Mandiri Mizusawa (BUMM) – ISO 14001:2015 from TÜV SÜD Indonesia
7. Braja Mukti Cakra (BMC) – ISO 14001:2015 from BSI, ANAB, IAF, KAN
8. Multi Kontrol Nusantara (MKN) - ISO 14001:2015 from Business Systems Certification Pty. Ltd
9. South East Asia Pipe Industries (SEAPI) – ISO 14001:2015 from TÜV Rheinland

Efforts towards continuous improvement are being optimized to enhance environmental management efficiency while also providing long-term impacts. By the end of 2025, there are no environmental complaints, and all regulatory requirements have been fulfilled. [SEOJK F.16]
