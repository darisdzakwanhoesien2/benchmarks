## KESEMPATAN KERJA YANG ADIL [SEOJK F.18] [GRI 405-1]

Sinergi yang tumbuh dari keberagaman menjadi kekuatan utama yang terus BNBR bangun dalam lingkungan kerja. Prinsip “Kebersamaan” kami tempatkan sebagai fondasi untuk memastikan proses rekrutmen di BNBR dan seluruh unit anak usaha berjalan terbuka tanpa diskriminasi, baik melalui rekrutmen internal guna mempercepat mobilitas karier maupun rekrutmen eksternal untuk menjaring talenta berkualitas. Evaluasi berkala dilakukan pada setiap tahapan seleksi dengan mengukur parameter kesetaraan, keragaman, dan inklusi agar data yang dihasilkan mampu menjadi dasar identifikasi sekaligus perbaikan atas potensi kesenjangan.

Lingkungan kerja yang adil dan inklusif kami wujudkan melalui penerapan kebijakan ketenagakerjaan yang setara di seluruh lini. Untuk mendukung produktivitas operasional, Grup BNBR menerapkan skema perjanjian kerja yang berlaku setara bagi karyawan tetap maupun kontrak, mencakup aspek K3L, kesetaraan remunerasi, serta pengembangan diri dan pelatihan kompetensi. Pendekatan ini memastikan setiap individu memperoleh peluang yang sama untuk berkembang secara optimal sekaligus memperkuat keberlanjutan kinerja organisasi.

## KESETARAAN REMUNERASI

Pemenuhan hak kompensasi yang setara bagi seluruh karyawan menjadi bagian penting dari komitmen Grup BNBR terhadap praktik ketenagakerjaan yang adil. Karena itu, tidak ada perbedaan perlakuan dalam pemberian remunerasi antara karyawan wanita dan pria. Sistem pengupahan dirancang adil dan kompetitif sebagai bentuk apresiasi terhadap kinerja sekaligus pendorong produktivitas, dengan gaji yang selalu memenuhi regulasi Upah Minimum Regional (UMR) di setiap wilayah operasional. Penetapan remunerasi dan bonus dilakukan berdasarkan penilaian kinerja tahunan, kompetensi individu, serta biaya hidup di masing-masing area operasional anak perusahaan. [GRI 405-2] [SEOJK F.20]

## EQUAL EMPLOYMENT OPPORTUNITY [SEOJK F.18] [GRI 405-1]

The synergy that grows from diversity has become the main strength that BNBR continually cultivates within the workplace. We uphold the principle of ‘Togetherness’ as the foundation to ensure that the recruitment process at BNBR and all subsidiary units is carried out in a transparent manner and free from discrimination, whether through internal recruitment to accelerate career progression or external recruitment to attract high-calibre talent. Regular evaluations are performed at each selection stage by assessing parameters related to equality, diversity, and inclusion, with the collected data serving as a basis for identifying and addressing potential gaps.

We realize a fair and inclusive workplace environment through the implementation of equal employment policies across all lines. To enhance operational productivity, the BNBR Group applies a work agreement scheme that is uniformly applicable to both permanent and contract employees, encompassing aspects of K3L (Health, Safety, and Environment), remuneration equality, as well as self-development and competency training. This approach ensures that all individuals have equal opportunities for optimal development while also strengthening the sustainability of organizational performance.

## REMUNERATION EQUALITY

Remuneration Equality

Ensuring equitable compensation rights for all employees is an important part of BNBR Group’s commitment to fair labor practices. Consequently, there is no distinction in remuneration treatment between female and male employees. The wage system is designed to be equitable and competitive, serving as a form of performance-based appreciation and an incentive for productivity, with salaries consistently aligned with the Regional Minimum Wage (RMW) requirements in each operational area. Remuneration and bonuses are determined based on annual performance assessments, individual competencies, and the cost of living in each subsidiary’s operational area. [GRI 405-2] [SEOJK F.20]
