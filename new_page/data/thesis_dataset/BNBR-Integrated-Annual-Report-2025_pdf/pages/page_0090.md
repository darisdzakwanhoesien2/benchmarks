# BNBR

## Kunjungan Manajemen BNBR ke CCT

## BNBR Management's Visit to CCT

Manajemen PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk (BNBR) melakukan kunjungan kerja ke jalan tol Cimanggis Cibitung pada 3 Desember 2025. Hadir dalam acara tersebut A. Ardiansyah Bakrie (Wakil Direktur Utama &amp; Co-CEO), Roy Hendrajanto M. Sakti (Direktur), Kartini Sally (Direktur), dan R.A. Sri Dharmayanti (Direktur).

Selain itu, hadir pula R. Atok Hendrayanto (Chief Industrialization Officer), Okder Pendrian (Chief Human Capital &amp; Office Support), Didit Ardyanto (Chief Technology Officer), dan A. Amri Aswono Putro (Chief Business Development Officer).

Acara diawali dengan Town Hall Meeting yang diisi pemaparan tentang profil PT Cimanggis Cibitung Tollways (CCT), oleh Direktur Utama CCT Y. Widi Suharyanto, dan ditutup dengan site visit ke jalan tol Cimanggis Cibitung KM 63, lokasi yang akandigunakan untuk membangun Rest Area.

Site visit juga dilakukan ke Interchange Burangkeng yang bakal menjadi persimpangan antara jalan tol Cimanggis Cibitung dan jalan tol Jakarta Cikampek Selatan atau Japek II.

Management of PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk (BNBR) paid a working visit to the Cimanggis Cibitung Toll Road on December 3, 2025. Present at the event was A. Ardiansyah Bakrie (Deputy President Director &amp; Co-CEO), Roy Hendrajanto M. Sakti (Director), Kartini Sally (Director), and RA. Sri Dharmayanti (Director).

Also present were R. Atok Hendrayanto (Chief Industrialization Officer), Okder Pendrian (Chief Human Capital &amp; Office Support), Didit Ardyanto (Chief Technology Officer), and A. Amri Aswono Putro (Chief Business Development Officer).

The event began with a Town Hall Meeting which included a presentation about the profile of PT Cimanggis Cibitung Tollways (CCT) by the President Director of CCT Y. Widi Suharyanto, and concluded with a site visit to the Cimanggis Cibitung Toll Road KM 63, the location that will be used to build Rest Area.

Site visits were also carried out to the Burangkeng Interchange which will be the junction between the Cimanggis Cibitung Toll Road and the Jakarta Cikampek Selatan Toll Road or Japek II.

# BPI

## Kunjungan PT PLN (Persero) dalam Percepatan Pengiriman Tiang Listrik Baja untuk Pemulihan Bencana Sumatera

## Visit of PT PLN (Persero) in Accelerating the Delivery of Steel Electric Poles for Sumatra Disaster Recovery

Management PT PLN (Persero), Bapak Sumarno (EVP Manajemen Rantai Pasok) dan Bapak Lambas Richard Pasaribu (EVP Pengembangan Listrik Desa), bersama jajaran manajemen PLN melakukan kunjungan ke pabrik PT Bakrie Pipe Industries (BPI) pada 10 Desember 2025.

Kunjungan ini dilakukan untuk meninjau kesiapan pengiriman tiang baja listrik guna pemulihan kelistrikan di wilayah bencana Sumatera.

Kunjungan ini disambut hangat oleh Bapak Iskandar Ihsan Daulay (CCO), Bapak Deddy Kurnia (COO), serta manajemen BPI. Agenda meliputi peninjauan area produksi dan pabrikasi tiang baja, paparan kesiapan pengiriman, hingga pelepasan tiang baja menuju daerah terdampak.

BPI berkomitmen menghadirkan tiang listrik berkualitas untuk mendukung PLN dalam pemulihan kelistrikan pasca bencana.

Management of PT PLN (Persero), Mr Sumarno (EVP Supply Chain Management) and Mr Lambas Richard Pasaribu (EVP Village Electricity Development), together with PLN management visited the PT Bakrie Pipe Industries (BPI) factory on December 10, 2025.

This visit was carried out to review the readiness to send electric steel poles for electrical recovery in the Sumatra disaster area.

This visit was warmly welcomed by Mr. Iskandar Ihsan Daulay (CCO), Mr. Deddy Kurnia (COO), and BPI management. The agenda includes reviewing the production and manufacturing area for steel poles, exposure to shipping readiness, and releasing steel poles to affected areas.

BPI is committed to providing quality electricity poles to support PLN in electricity recovery after disasters.
