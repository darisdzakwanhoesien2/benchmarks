# MEMPERCEPAT TRANSFORMASI TRANSPORTASI MENUJU ERA KENDARAAN LISTRIK

Komitmen mendorong transisi transportasi rendah emisi kami wujudkan melalui pengembangan ekosistem kendaraan listrik bersama PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk (VKTR). Kolaborasi dengan berbagai pihak diarahkan untuk mendukung target transisi transportasi DKI Jakarta menuju 100% pada 2030 serta rencana induk Jawa Barat dalam penerapan angkutan umum berbasis listrik. Upaya ini sekaligus memperkuat kontribusi Perseroan terhadap peningkatan Tingkat Konten Dalam Negeri melalui solusi teknologi transportasi yang andal dan bebas emisi.

Pertumbuhan kinerja VKTR tercermin dari pendapatan VKTR yang meningkat signifikan sebesar 61,89% hingga akhir 2025, dengan penjualan bus listrik sebagai kontributor utama senilai Rp215,6 miliar dari 53 unit. Setelah tercatat di Bursa Efek Indonesia dan memperluas fasilitas pabrik perakitan bus serta truk listrik di Magelang, Jawa Tengah, VKTR kini menjajaki kerja sama strategis dengan sejumlah perusahaan otomotif global untuk pengembangan EV Mining Truck dan EV truck, sekaligus memperluas jaringan distribusi melalui kemitraan dengan dealer lokal di berbagai kota besar Indonesia.

Produksi kendaraan listrik juga diperkuat melalui perusahaan patungan PT VKTR Sakti Industries (VKTS) di Magelang yang memproduksi bus listrik dan truk listrik secara Completely Knock-Down (CKD). Untuk mempercepat elektrifikasi transportasi nasional, Perseroan turut menandatangani joint cooperation dengan perusahaan original equipment manufacturer (OEM) tier 0.5—penyedia solusi platform listrik yang merupakan bagian dari produsen baterai terbesar di dunia—sebagai langkah strategis mendukung target Net Zero Emission 2060.

# ACCELERATING TRANSPORTATION TRANSFORMATION TOWARDS THE ELECTRIC VEHICLE ERA

Our commitment to advancing the transition to low-emission transportation is realized through the development of an electric vehicle ecosystem in collaboration with PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas (VKTR). Collaborations with various parties are intended to support DKI Jakarta's transportation transition target to achieve 100% by 2030, as well as West Java's master plan for the implementation of electric-based public transportation. This effort also strengthens the Company's contribution to increasing the Domestic Content Level through reliable and emission-free transportation technology solutions.

VKTR's performance growth is reflected in its revenue, which has increased significantly by 61.89% by the end of 2025, with sales of electric buses as the main contributor, amounting to Rp215.6 billion from 53 units. Following its listing on the Indonesia Stock Exchange and the expansion of its electric bus and truck assembly facilities in Magelang, Central Java, VKTR is now exploring strategic partnerships with several global automotive companies for the development of EV Mining Trucks and EV trucks, while simultaneously expanding its distribution network through partnerships with local dealers in major cities across Indonesia.

Electric vehicle production is also strengthened through the joint venture PT VKTR Sakti Industries (VKTS) in Magelang, which manufactures electric buses and trucks via the Completely Knock-Down (CKD) method. To accelerate national transportation electrification, the Company has also signed a joint cooperation agreement with a tier 0.5 original equipment manufacturer (OEM)—a provider of electric platform solutions that is part of the world's largest battery manufacturer—as a strategic step to support the 2060 Net Zero Emission target.
