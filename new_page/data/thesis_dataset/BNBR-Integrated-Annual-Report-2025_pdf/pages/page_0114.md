Adrian Toho Parada Panggabean, Warga Negara Indonesia, lahir di Jakarta pada tahun 1966. Beliau memperoleh gelar Sarjana Ekonomi dari Universitas Indonesia, Jakarta, Indonesia pada tahun 1989, gelar Master of Science di bidang Development Finance dari University of Birmingham, Inggris, pada tahun 1992, dan gelar Doctor of Philosophy di bidang Public Finance dari University of Birmingham, Inggris, pada tahun 1997.

Beliau menjabat sebagai Komisaris Independen sejak September 2025. Saat ini beliau juga menjabat sebagai Direktur Independen, CIMB Investment Bank Bhd, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia dan sebagai anggota CIMB Group Board Risk and Compliance Committee untuk CIMB Bank, CIMB Islamic, CIMB Investment Bank, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.

Beliau memiliki pengalaman lebih dari 35 tahun di bidang industri finansial dan pernah bekerja di delapan negara berbeda. Sebelum berkarier secara profesional di bidang finansial dan investasi, beliau merupakan dosen bidang ekonomi di Universitas Indonesia, Jakarta, Indonesia (1988 – 2007). Sejak beralih karier beliau telah menjalani hampir seluruh segmen di industri keuangan: merchant banking, international development banking, investment banking, fund management, dan direktorat treasury perbankan komersial.

Pada periode Mei 2021 hingga saat ini, beliau pernah menjabat sebagai technical advisor / konsultan di berbagai lembaga keuangan, di antaranya Asian Development Bank; BRI Research Institute di Jakarta, Indonesia; Climate Policy Initiative (Indonesia); KfW Bankengruppe (Bank Investasi Jerman yang berbasis di Frankfurt).

Beliau menjabat sebagai penasihat ekonomi di Departemen Perencanaan Ekonomi Kantor Perdana Menteri Brunei Darussalam (2005-2010). Sebelum itu, beliau bekerja sebagai economist di Asian Development Bank di Manila, Filipina (2001-2007), Nomura Singapura (1999-2001), dan United Nations Development Programme, Jakarta (1998-1999). Di Indonesia, beliau memiliki pengalaman sebagai konsultan dalam manajemen keuangan publik dan hubungan fiskal antar pemerintah untuk Kementerian Keuangan Republik Indonesia (1997-1999).

Beliau juga pernah menjabat sebagai direktur di beberapa perusahaan manajemen investasi di Indonesia. Selain itu, beliau juga pernah menjabat sebagai Chief Economist sekaligus Treasury strategist dan Executive Vice President di PT Bank CIMB Niaga Tbk.

Adrian Toho Parada Panggabean, an Indonesian nationality, was born in Jakarta in 1966. He obtained his Bachelor degree in Economics from University of Indonesia, Jakarta, Indonesia in 1989, Master of Social Science degree in the field of Development Finance from University of Birmingham, United Kingdom, in 1992, and Doctor of Philosophy degree specializing in Public Finance from University of Birmingham, United Kingdom in 1997.

He holds the position as Independent Commissioner since September 2025. He currently also serves as Independent Director of CIMB Investment Bank Bhd, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, and as a member of the CIMB Group Board Risk and Compliance Committee for CIMB Bank, CIMB Islamic, and CIMB Investment Bank, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.

He has over 35 years of experience in the financial industry and has worked in eight different countries. Before pursuing a professional career in finance and investment, he was a lecturer in economics at University of Indonesia, Jakarta, Indonesia (1988-2007). Since switching careers, he has worked in almost every segment of the financial industry: merchant banking, international development banking, investment banking, fund management, and treasury directorate of commercial banking.

From May 2021 to the present, he has served as a technical advisor/consultant at various foreign and local financial institutions, including the Asian Development Bank; BRI Research Institute in Jakarta, Indonesia; Climate Policy Initiative (Indonesia); KfW Bankengruppe (Frankfurt-based German Investment Bank).

He was economic adviser in the Economic Planning Department of Brunei Darussalam's Prime Minister's Office (2005-2010). Prior to that, he was economist with the Asian Development Bank in Manila, Philippines (2001-2007), Nomura Singapore (1999-2001), and United Nations Development Programme, Jakarta (1998-1999). In Indonesia, he had experience as a consultant in public financial management and intergovernmental fiscal relations for the Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Indonesia (1997-1999).

He has also held directorship positions in several fund management firms in Indonesia. In addition, he has also served as Chief Economist-cum-Treasury strategist and Executive Vice President at PT Bank CIMB Niaga Tbk.
