# KARTINI SALLY

DIREKTUR &amp; CHIEF INVESTMENT OFFICER

DIRECTOR &amp; CHIEF INVESTMENT OFFICER

Kartini Sally, Warga Negara Indonesia, lahir di Jakarta pada tahun 1965. Beliau memperoleh gelar Sarjana dari Universitas Indonesia, gelar Master dari PPM School of Management, dan gelar Doktor dari Universitas Gadjah Mada. Beliau juga menjalani berbagai program pendidikan executive leadership, dari universitas dan institusi ternama di Eropa dan Amerika Serikat.

Beliau menjabat sebagai Direktur dan Chief Investment Officer sejak Juli 2022. Beliau pernah menjabat sebagai Anggota Komite Audit dan Risk Management PT Bank HSBC Indonesia (2021 – 2025).

Sebelumnya beliau menjabat sebagai Direktur Commercial Banking PT Bank Mandiri (Persero) Tbk, kemudian menjabat sebagai Direktur Hubungan Kelembagaan PT Bank Mandiri (Persero) Tbk dan sekaligus bertugas sebagai Direktur Pembina yang mensupervisi PT Bank Syariah Mandiri serta DPLK (Dana Pensiun Lembaga Keuangan) Bank Mandiri hingga akhir Maret 2018. Beliau juga menjabat sebagai Komisaris PT Mandiri Sekuritas (2011 – 2015).

Sebelum bergabung dengan BNBR, beliau memiliki pengalaman perbankan dan investasi di berbagai institusi.

Kartini Sally, an Indonesian nationality, was born in Jakarta in 1965. She earned a Bachelor degree University of Indonesia, Master degree from PPM School of Management, and Doctoral degree from Gadjah Mada University. She also underwent various executive leadership education programs from a number of university and institution in Europe and USA.

She holds the position of Director and Chief Investment Officer since July 2022. She held the position of Member of Audit and Risk Management Committee of PT Bank HSBC Indonesia (2021 – 2025).

Previously she served as Director of Commercial Banking Director of PT Bank Mandiri (Persero) Tbk, she then served as Director of Institutional Relation of PT Bank Mandiri (Persero) Tbk and at the same time served as Director of Trustees who supervised PT Bank Syariah Mandiri and DPLK (Financial Institution Pension Fund) Bank Mandiri until the end of March 2018. She has also served as Commissioner of PT Mandiri Sekuritas (2011 – 2015).

Prior to joining Bakrie Group, she had banking and investment experience in various institutions.
