## BNBR

## Corporate Legal Forum Grup Bakrie
Bakrie Group's Corporate Legal Forum

Direktorat Legal PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk (BNBR) bersama Bakrie Learning Center (BLC) menyelenggarakan Bakrie Group Corporate Legal Forum 2025.

Kegiatan ini difasilitasi oleh narasumber dari RS &amp; Partners Lawfirm dan MAPS Lawfirm dan diikuti oleh para peserta dari perusahaan di lingkup Grup Bakrie, dilanjutkan dengan sesi seminar selama dua hari.

Acara ini merupakan respons terhadap dinamika dan tantangan profesi Legal di Era 5.0. Dengan fokus agar fungsi Legal perusahaan di lingkup Grup Bakrie dapat beradaptasi terhadap perubahan teknologi seperti AI, peraturan terkait informasi dan transaksi elektronik.

Legal Directorate of PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk (BNBR) together with Bakrie Learning Center (BLC) held the Bakrie Group Corporate Legal Forum 2025.

This activity was facilitated by resource persons from RS &amp; Partners Lawfirm and MAPS Lawfirm and attended by participants from companies within the Bakrie Group, followed by a 2-day seminar session.

This event is a response to the dynamics and challenges of the Legal profession in Era 5.0. With a focus on making the company's legal functions within the Bakrie Group adapt to technological changes such as AI, regulations related to information and electronic transactions.

## VKTR

## Peluncuran 20 Bus Listrik CKD untuk Transjakarta melalui Sinarjaya
Launch of 20 CKD electric buses for Transjakarta via Sinarjaya

PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas, Tbk (VKTR) melakukan seremoni peluncuran 20 unit bus listrik berbasis Completely Knocked Down (CKD) pertama untuk Transjakarta melalui operator Sinarjaya bersama PT Laksana Bus Manufaktur selaku mitra karoseri. Seluruh bus listrik ini memiliki Tingkat Kandungan Dalam Negeri (TKDN) di atas 40 persen, sebuah bukti nyata dedikasi perusahaan dalam mendukung kemandirian industri otomotif nasional.

PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas, Tbk (VKTR) held a ceremony to launch the first 20 Completely Knocked Down (CKD) based electric buses for Transjakarta through the Sinarjaya operator together with PT Laksana Bus Manufaktur as bodywork partner. All of these electric buses have Domestic Content Levels (TKDN) above 40 percent, a clear proof of the company's dedication to supporting the independence of the national automotive industry.
