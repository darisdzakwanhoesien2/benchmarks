# FONDASI BNBR MENUJU BISNIS BERKELANJUTAN – KINERJA KEUANGAN

Pada tahun 2025, ketidakpastian global masih menekan aktivitas ekonomi, namun Indonesia tetap menunjukkan ketahanan yang relatif kuat dengan mempertahankan momentum pertumbuhan dan meningkatkan kepercayaan melalui inflasi yang stabil, permintaan domestik yang terjaga, serta pengelolaan kebijakan makroekonomi yang prudent di tengah tekanan eksternal.

Pada akhir tahun buku 2025, Perseroan membukukan pendapatan bersih sebesar Rp3,74 triliun, dibandingkan Rp3,87 triliun pada tahun 2024. Aktivitas manufaktur dan infrastruktur tetap menjadi kontributor terbesar, didukung oleh pengiriman produk dan permintaan pelanggan di berbagai lini usaha. Namun penurunan pendapatan dari pelanggan di sektor terkait minyak dan gas serta otomotif berdampak terhadap pencapaian pendapatan Perseroan. Sementara itu, segmen Perdagangan, Jasa, dan Investasi terus memperluas perannya melalui peningkatan pelaksanaan operasional dan jangkauan komersial. BNBR is also expanding into digital and smart manufacturing businesses to remain competitive in an increasingly technology-driven landscape. Through modernization of its manufacturing subsidiaries and adoption of advanced technologies, the Group aims to enhance operational efficiency, product quality, and sustainability performance. Digital transformation initiatives—ranging from automation and data-driven decision-making to infrastructure-related digital services—are being integrated across business units. These efforts complement BNBR’s participation in both public and private infrastructure projects, ensuring that its industrial capabilities remain relevant in supporting Indonesia’s economic transformation.

Looking ahead, BNBR is committed to strengthening its portfolio through strategic investments, technology integration, and human capital development. The Company seeks to position itself as a forward-looking infrastructure and industrial platform that contributes to sustainable growth in line with the Golden Indonesia 2045 vision. With a solid industrial heritage, expanding infrastructure assets, and a focus on innovation-driven solutions, BNBR aims to play a pivotal role in advancing national progress through resilient infrastructure development, clean technologies, and smart manufacturing excellence.

# BNBR FOUNDATION TOWARDS SUSTAINABLE BUSINESS – FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE

In 2025, global uncertainty continued to pressure economic activity, yet Indonesia remained relatively resilient, sustaining growth momentum and strengthening confidence through stable inflation, steady demand, and sound macroeconomic policy management, despite external headwinds.

At the end of the 2025 financial year, the Company recorded net revenues of Rp 3.74 trillion, compared with Rp 3.87 trillion in 2024. Manufacturing and infrastructure-related activities remained the largest contributor, supported by delivery and customer demand across products and services. However, decrease in revenue from customers related to oil &amp; gas as well as automotive sector has impacted the Company’s achieved revenues.
