## BISNIS YANG MENDORONG PEMBANGUNAN INDONESIA

Sebagai konglomerasi nasional terkemuka, PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk (BNBR) terus berevolusi dengan komitmen kuat untuk mendukung pembangunan jangka panjang Indonesia. Didirikan pada tahun 1942 sebagai perusahaan perdagangan hasil pertanian, Grup secara bertahap berekspansi ke sektor industri di bawah visi almarhum Achmad Bakrie untuk berkontribusi pada pembangunan bangsa melalui industrialisasi. Visi tersebut terwujud pada tahun 1959 dengan pendirian PT Bakrie Pipe Industries, yang menandai masuknya BNBR ke industri manufaktur baja. Selama beberapa dekade berikutnya, BNBR melakukan diversifikasi ke konstruksi, pengerjaan logam, bahan bangunan, infrastruktur, serta komponen otomotif, sehingga memperkokoh posisinya sebagai kontributor utama pertumbuhan industri dan ekonomi Indonesia.

Dalam beberapa tahun terakhir, Grup mempercepat transformasinya menuju sektor infrastruktur, keberlanjutan, dan industri masa depan. Tonggak penting dicapai melalui akuisisi strategis jalan tol Cimanggis–Cibitung (CCT), yang memperkuat platform infrastruktur BNBR sekaligus mendukung konektivitas nasional dan efisiensi logistik. Aset ini menyediakan sumber pendapatan jangka panjang yang stabil serta mempertegas peran Grup dalam agenda pembangunan infrastruktur Indonesia. Pada saat yang sama, BNBR terus mengembangkan inisiatif yang sejalan dengan target Net Zero Emissions (NZE) 2060, termasuk pengembangan mobilitas listrik melalui PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk, yang didukung kapabilitas manufaktur domestik dan upaya peningkatan kandungan lokal.

Bakrie Power continues to advance renewable energy development through PT Helio Synar Energi (Helio), including the construction of solar power installations and participation in the emerging electric vehicle charging infrastructure ecosystem. These initiatives support Indonesia's transition toward cleaner energy while creating new avenues for sustainable business growth. In parallel, the building materials segment is promoting environmentally responsible solutions through PT Modula Sustainability Indonesia (Modula), which develops cost-effective 3D-printed housing designed to address the nation's housing backlog. Together, these efforts demonstrate the Group's integrated approach to sustainability, combining energy transition, technological innovation, and social impact.

## BUSINESSES THAT FUELS INDONESIA'S DEVELOPMENT

As a leading national conglomerate, PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk (BNBR) continues to evolve with a strong commitment to supporting Indonesia's long-term development. Founded in 1942 as an agricultural trading company, the Group gradually expanded into industrial sectors under the vision of the late Achmad Bakrie to contribute to nation-building through industrialization. This vision materialized in 1959 with the establishment of PT Bakrie Pipe Industries, marking BNBR's entry into steel manufacturing. Over subsequent decades, BNBR diversified into construction, metalworks, building materials, infrastructure, and automotive components, establishing itself as a key contributor to Indonesia's industrial and economic growth.

In recent years, the Group has accelerated its transformation toward infrastructure, sustainability, and future-oriented industries. A significant milestone was the strategic acquisition of the Cimanggis–Cibitung toll road (CCT), strengthening BNBR's infrastructure platform while supporting national connectivity and logistics efficiency. This asset provides stable, long-term revenue streams and reinforces the Group's role in Indonesia's infrastructure development agenda. At the same time, BNBR continues to advance initiatives aligned with the Net Zero Emissions (NZE) 2060 target, including the development of electric mobility through PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk, supported by domestic manufacturing capabilities and localization efforts.
