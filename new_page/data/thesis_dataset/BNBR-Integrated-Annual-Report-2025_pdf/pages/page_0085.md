## BPI, BMI

Seminar dan Plant Visit BPI dan BMI bersama AKI
BPI and BMI Seminar and Plant Visit in Collaboration with AKI

PT Bakrie Pipe Industries (BPI) dan PT Bakrie Metal Industries (BMI) bersama Asosiasi Kontraktor Indonesia (AKI) menggelar seminar dan plant visit bertema "Steel Products Specification Knowledge" pada 27 Mei 2025.

Kegiatan ini bertujuan memperkuat pemahaman teknis seputar spesifikasi produk baja serta membuka ruang diskusi antara pelaku industri manufaktur dan kontraktor, dengan rangkaian mulai dari penyampaian materi, sesi tanya jawab, plant tour, hingga permainan dan penyampaian umpan balik yang berlangsung penuh antusiasme.

PT Bakrie Pipe Industries (BPI) and PT Bakrie Metal Industries (BMI) together with the Indonesian Contractors Association (AKI) held a seminar and plant visit with the theme "Steel Products Specification Knowledge" on May 27 2025.

This activity aims to strengthen technical understanding regarding steel product specifications and open up space for discussion between manufacturing industry players and contractors, with activities ranging from material delivery, question and answer sessions, plant tours, to games and feedback deliveries that were full of enthusiasm.

## VKTR

Peresmian Fasilitas Perakitan Kendaraan Listrik Berbasis CKD VKTS
Inauguration of CKD VKTS Based Electric Vehicle Assembly Facility

PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk (VKTR) mengadakan acara peresmian fasilitas perakitan kendaraan listrik berbasis CKD milik anak usahanya, PT VKTR Sakti Industries yang berlokasi di Magelang, Jawa Tengah. Fasilitas ini merupakan pusat perakitan Kendaraan Bermotor Listrik Berbasis Baterai (KBLBB) berstandar internasional, dilengkapi dengan teknologi mutakhir pada lini perakitan dan fasilitas pengecekan. Fasilitas ini memiliki kapasitas produksi hingga 3.000 unit per tahun dan menjadi pusat perakitan berbagai kendaraan listrik, termasuk bus listrik ukuran 12 meter yang telah memenuhi persyaratan Tingkat Komponen Dalam Negeri (TKDN) lebih dari 40%.

PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk (VKTR) held an inauguration event for the CKD-based electric vehicle assembly facility located in Magelang, Central Java, owned by its subsidiary PT VKTR Sakti Industries. This facility is an assembly center for international standard Battery Based Electric Motor Vehicles (KBLBB), equipped with the latest technology in assembly lines and checking facilities. This facility has a production capacity of up to 3,000 units per year and is the assembly center for various electric vehicles, including 12 meter electric buses which have met the Domestic Component Level (TKDN) requirements of more than 40%.
