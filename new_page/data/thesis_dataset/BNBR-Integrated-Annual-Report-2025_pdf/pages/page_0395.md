Konsistensi implementasi kebijakan kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja di seluruh lini dijaga melalui pembentukan Panitia Pembina Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja (P2K3) di tingkat Grup BNBR. Komite ini beranggotakan perwakilan karyawan dari setiap unit usaha yang bertugas mengawasi sekaligus menjalankan program K3. Tanggung jawab penerapan seluruh aspek K3, mulai dari penyediaan Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) hingga pembentukan Komite Manajemen K3, tetap berada pada masing-masing anak perusahaan sesuai ketentuan dalam Perjanjian Kerja Bersama (PKB). The consistency of health and safety policy implementation across all lines is maintained through the establishment of the Occupational Health and Safety Supervisory Committee (P2K3) at the BNBR Group level. This committee comprises employee representatives from each business unit, who are responsible for overseeing and implementing OHS programs. The responsibility to implement all aspects of OHS, including providing Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) and establishing the OHS Management Committee, resides with each subsidiary in accordance with the stipulations of the Collective Labour Agreement (CLA). Detailed information regarding the positions, duties, and responsibilities of P2K3 can be found in the following CLA:

- CLA from PT Bakrie Autoparts (BA) Chapter VIII Article 55
