## DIVIDEND DISTRIBUTION

Referring to the Company's business plan for the coming fiscal year as well as the provisions of Article 70 and Article 71 of the Limited Liability Company Law and the requirements in BAPEPAM Circular Letter No. S-2057/PM/2003 which regulates, among others, the distribution of profit in the form of dividends can be made if the Company has positive retained earnings and total equity.

# REALISASI PENGGUNAAN DANA HASIL PENAWARAN UMUM PERDANA (IPO)

REALIZATION OF INITIAL PUBLIC OFFERING (IPO) FUNDS

Pada tanggal 28 Agustus 1989, Perseroan melakukan Penawaran Umum Perdana (IPO) kepada masyarakat atas sejumlah saham Perseroan sebanyak 2.850.000 saham dengan nilai nominal sebesar Rp 1.000 per saham. Seluruh saham Perseroan tersebut dicatatkan di Bursa Efek Jakarta (BEJ) yang sekarang dikenal sebagai Bursa Efek Indonesia (BEI). Sejak saat itu hingga tahun pelaporan Perseroan tidak pernah melakukan aksi korporasi dengan menawarkan saham kepada publik dalam rangka menggalang dana.

On August 28, 1989, the Company conducted an Initial Public Offering (IPO) of 2,850,000 shares at par value of Rp 1,000 per share. All of the Company's shares were listed on Jakarta Stock Exchange (JSX), now known as Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX). Since then, up to the current reporting year the Company has never conducted any corporate action involving the offering of shares to the public in order to raise funds.
