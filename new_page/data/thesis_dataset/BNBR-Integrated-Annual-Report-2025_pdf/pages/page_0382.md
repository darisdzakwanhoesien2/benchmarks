The achieved efficiency results from ongoing programs, including the installation of heavy equipment fuel monitoring systems, the deployment of smart sensors in production facilities and buildings, and the implementation of Variable Speed Drive (VSD) systems on motor systems. Concerning clean energy development, PT Bakrie Power (BP) actively develops geothermal, solar, wind, and biomass projects.

The total capacity of renewable energy projects by the end of 2025 is projected to reach 198 MW, with energy usage recorded at a stable level of approximately 14,400,333 gigajoules or 3.85GJ per one million rupiah of revenue.
