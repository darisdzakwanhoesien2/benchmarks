# KEGIATAN CORPORATE SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY YANG BERSIFAT KARITATIF

## CHARITABLE CORPORATE SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY ACTIVITIES [GRI 203-3]

Melalui Yayasan Bakrie Amanah, BNBR menyenergikan pelaksanaan program CSR di seluruh entitas Grup Bakrie, termasuk Perseroan. Operasional kegiatan sosial tersebut dikoordinasikan di bawah Badan Pengelola Bakrie Untuk Negeri (BP BUN), dengan dukungan kolaborasi perusahaan afiliasi dalam berbagai aksi karitatif. Yayasan ini resmi berdiri pada 17 Agustus 2010, dan realisasi kontribusi sosial tercermin pada tabel pengeluaran dana berikut. [SEOJK F.25]

Through the Bakrie Amanah Foundation, BNBR coordinates the implementation of CSR programs across all entities within the Bakrie Group, including the Company. The operational activities of these social programs are managed by the Bakrie for the Nation Management Agency (BP BUN), with support from collaborative initiatives by affiliated companies across various social activities. The Foundation was formally established on August 17, 2010, and the use of social contribution funds is presented in the following fund expenditure table. [SEOJK F.25]
