# PELATIHAN KESEHATAN DAN KESELAMATAN KERJA

Kebijakan Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja (K3) di lingkungan BNBR dan anak usaha masih dipengaruhi oleh pengalaman penanganan pandemi COVID-19 yang terjadi beberapa tahun lalu. Risiko kecelakaan kerja dan kesehatan karyawan diantisipasi melalui penerapan kebijakan OHS BNBR, kepatuhan terhadap standar internasional ISO serta OHSAS (Occupational, Health, and Safety Assessment Series), dan penguatan protokol kesehatan. Upaya tersebut juga diwujudkan melalui pelaksanaan pelatihan dan sosialisasi rutin terkait kriteria ISO dan OHSAS. [SEOJK F.21] [GRI 403-1]

Berbagai program menjadi sarana evaluasi sekaligus penguatan implementasi keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja di lingkungan Perusahaan. Keterlibatan BNBR dalam Program Lokakarya ISO 9001:2015, Program Pertolongan Pertama, Program Sosialisasi Pencegahan Penyakit, Program Penanganan Bahan Berbahaya dan Beracun (B3), serta Program Pemadaman Kebakaran menunjukkan konsistensi tersebut. Dukungan tambahan pada tahun 2025 diwujudkan melalui pelaksanaan kegiatan berikut: [GRI 403-5]

- Lokakarya dan implementasi Sistem Manajemen K3 berbasis ISO 45001:2018
- Penggunaan Alat Pelindung Diri (APD) dan keselamatan kerja, termasuk bekerja di ketinggian
- Peningkatan kewaspadaan dan pengendalian lingkungan kerja melalui program Safety Awareness, simulasi tanggap darurat, dan kegiatan P2K3
- Pengelolaan bahan berbahaya (B3), termasuk pemahaman Material Safety Data

- Identification and evaluation of aspects and potential hazards related to occupational health and safety, along with the determination of controls to mitigate risks and their impacts to an acceptable level.
- Commitment to comply with laws, regulations, and other pertinent requirements.
- Prevention of work-related accidents, injuries, illnesses in the workplace, and pollution.
- Reduction of waste and energy consumption.
- Recording and socialization of OHS performance.
- Ensuring the management of hazardous conditions and behaviours that could lead to accidents or injuries on the job.
- Continuous improvement in the implementation of OHS.

# OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH AND SAFETY TRAINING

The Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) policies within BNBR and its subsidiaries continue to be influenced by the experiences gained during the COVID-19 pandemic, which transpired several years earlier. The risk of work-related accidents and employee health are addressed through the implementation of BNBR's OHS policies, compliance with international standards such as ISO and OHSAS (Occupational, Health, and Safety Assessment Series), and the enhancement of health protocols. These efforts are further supported by regular training sessions and socialization related to ISO and OHSAS criteria. [SEOJK F.21] [GRI 403-1]

Various programs serve as tools to evaluate and strengthen occupational safety and health implementation within the company environment. BNBR's participation in the ISO 9001:2015 Workshop Program, First Aid Program, Disease Prevention Socialization Program, Hazardous and Toxic Materials (B3) Handling Program, and Fire Extinguishing Program exemplifies this consistency.
