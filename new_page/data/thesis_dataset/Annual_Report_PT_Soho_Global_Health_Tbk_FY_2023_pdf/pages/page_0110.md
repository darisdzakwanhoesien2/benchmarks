Healthcare Industry

Industri Kesehatan

Indonesia total healthcare market looks stagnant in terms of MAT 4Q23, held back by Vaccine with few products. If J07-Vaccine is excluded, MAT 4Q23 still showing positive outlook, +1.5% MAT growth. Total pharma market in MAT 4Q23 decreased by -0.1% driven by non-Rx (OTC) category market declined by -7.1% due to the impact of the high use of Multivitamin products in MAT 4Q22, while Rx category grew by +3.6% due to chronic disease products in BPJS.

Pasar kesehatan Indonesia terlihat stagnan dalam hal MAT 4Q23, ditekan oleh Vaksin dengan sedikit produk. Jika J07-Vaksin dikecualikan, MAT 4Q23 masih menunjukkan prospek positif, pertumbuhan MAT +1,5%. Total pasar farmasi MAT 4Q23 mengalami penurunan sebesar -0,1% yang didorong oleh pasar kategori non-Rx (OTC) yang turun sebesar -7,1% akibat dampak penggunaan tinggi produk Multivitamin pada MAT 4Q22, sementara kategori Rx tumbuh sebesar +3,6% karena produk penyakit kronis di BPJS.
