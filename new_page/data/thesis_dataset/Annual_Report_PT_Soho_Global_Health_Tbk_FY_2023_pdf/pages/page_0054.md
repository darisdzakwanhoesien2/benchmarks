# The Signing of Distribution Cooperation Between PPG and SNS for Distribution Expansion of Diapet Products Owned by SIP

Penandatangan Kerjasama Distribusi antara PPG dan SNS untuk Perluasan Distribusi Produk Diapet Milik SIP

![img-56.jpeg](img-56.jpeg)

Soho Global Health (SGH), through one of its subsidiaries, PT SOHO Industri Pharmasi (SIP), as well as PT Parit Padang Global (PPG), sees the need for a strong partnership to expand the distribution of one of its flagship products, DIAPET, in the General Trade (GT) sector, including small shops, convenience stores, and drugstores across Indonesia.

Soho Global Health (SGH), melalui salah satu anak usahanya PT SOHO Industri Pharmasi (SIP) dan juga PT Parit Padang Global (PPG), memandang perlunya sebuah partnership yang kuat untuk ekspansi distribusi dari salah satu produk unggulannya yaitu DIAPET di sektor General Trade (GT) seperti warung, toko klontong dan toko obat di seluruh Indonesia.

Therefore, after thorough exploration and a lengthy process, an agreement has been reached for a collaboration with PT Sinar Niaga Sejahtera (SNS) for the distribution of Diapet products. SNS is one of the largest national distributors in Indonesia with the widest network for distribution in the consumer retail channel.

Untuk itu, setelah melalui penjajakan dan proses yang panjang, telah terjadi kesepakatan kerjasama dengan PT Sinar Niaga Sejahtera (SNS), untuk pendistribusian produk Diapet. SNS merupakan salah satu distributor nasional terbesar di Indonesia dengan jaringan terbanyak untuk distribusi di channel consumer retail.

The symbolic signing of the agreement took place on October 4, 2023, with Edwin Vega Darma, Vice President Sales and Marketing of PPG, signing on behalf of PPG, and Ruli Setiawan Tobing, Managing Director of SNS, signing on behalf of SNS. The signing was witnessed by Sylvia Rizal, VP Sales and Marketing Consumer Health of SIP. As the Principal, Sylvia Rizal welcomed and supported PPG's collaboration with SNS as a distribution partner for DIAPET products, noting SNS's well-known track record for having extensive distribution coverage in the Consumer Retail channel throughout Indonesia.

Penandatanganan kesepakatan secara simbolis dilakukan pada tanggal 4 Oktober 2023 oleh Edwin Vega Darma, selaku Vice President Sales and Marketing PPG dengan Ruli Setiawan Tobing, selaku Managing Director SNS. Penandatanganan tersebut disaksikan oleh Sylvia Rizal, VP Sales and Marketing Consumer Health SIP. Sebagai Principal, Sylvia Rizal, menyambut baik dan mendukung langkah PPG bekerjasama dengan SNS sebagai mitra distribusi untuk produk DIAPET, melihat track record SNS selama ini terkenal memiliki coverage distribusi yang rapat di channel Consumer Retail di seluruh Indonesia.
