# Human Capital

## Sumber Daya Manusia

SGH Group consistently prioritizes the human resources development as the company's main asset. The Company believes that achieving strategic goals and a successful operational is an integral part of employee dedication which requires skills and creativity of our extraordinary human resources.

Human resources management at SGH Group begins with the recruitment process. From this very first stage, the Company is fully committed to human rights, particularly in protecting children's rights by establishing general requirements for candidates, which include a minimum age of 18 (eighteen) years. This provision has also been regulated in detail in the Collective Labor Agreement and Company Regulations as well as SOP of Recruitment Management.

SGH Group is strongly committed to structured and definite policies, ensuring that every employee, regardless of gender, race, religion, ethnicity, or particular social groups, has equal opportunities in terms of development, remuneration, career development and employee remuneration.

SGH Group secara konsisten memprioritaskan pengembangan sumber daya manusia sebagai aset utama perusahaan. Perusahaan percaya bahwa keberhasilan operasional dan pencapaian tujuan strategis tidak terlepas dari dedikasi, keterampilan, dan kreativitas sumber daya manusia kami yang luar biasa.

Pengelolaan sumber daya manusia di SGH Group diawali dengan tahap rekrutmen. Dalam tahap ini, perusahaan sepenuhnya berkomitmen untuk melindungi Hak Asasi Manusia khususnya Hak Asasi Anak dengan menetapkan persyaratan umum bagi calon karyawan, yang mencakup usia minimal 18 (delapan belas) tahun. Ketentuan ini juga telah diatur secara rinci dalam Perjanjian Kerja Bersama dan Peraturan Perusahaan serta SOP Manajemen Rekrutmen.

SGH Group berkomitmen kuat dengan kebijakan yang jelas dan terstruktur, menjamin bahwa setiap karyawan, tanpa memandang jenis kelamin, ras, agama, etnis, atau kelompok sosial tertentu, memiliki kesempatan yang setara dalam hal pengembangan, remunerasi, pertumbuhan karir, dan remunerasi Karyawan.
