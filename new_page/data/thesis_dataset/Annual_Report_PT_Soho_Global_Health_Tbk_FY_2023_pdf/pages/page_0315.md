PT Parit Padang Global ("PPG") (lanjutan)

Berdasarkan Akta Notaris Jimmy Tanal, S.H., M.Kn., No. 218 tanggal 30 Juni 2022, PT Soho Global Investment selaku pemilik 1 saham Seri A dengan nilai nominal Rp1.000.000 (nilai penuh) per saham di PPG, telah menjual saham tersebut ke UHN sebesar nilai wajar. Berdasarkan laporan penilaian yang dilakukan oleh KJPP Wawat Jatmika dan Rekan, penilai independen, nilai wajar saham PPG pada tanggal 31 Desember 2021 adalah sebesar Rp2.103.000 (nilai penuh) per saham. Setelah transaksi ini, kepemilikan Perusahaan dalam PPG mengalami peningkatan dari semula 99,99% menjadi 100,00%, melalui kepemilikan langsung dan kepemilikan tidak langsung melalui UHN. Selisih antara nilai nominal dan nilai wajar sebesar Rp1.103.000 (nilai penuh) diakui pada "Selisih Transaksi dengan Pihak Nonpengendali" dalam laporan keuangan konsolidasian.

PT Parit Padang Global ("PPG") (continued)

Based on Notarial Deed No. 218 of Jimmy Tanal, S.H., M.Kn., dated June 30, 2022, PT Soho Global Investment as a shareholder of 1 Series A share with nominal value of Rp1,000,000 (full amount) per share on PPG, has sold the share to UHN at fair value. Based on the appraisal report, performed by KJPP Wawat Jatmika and Partners, independent appraiser, the fair value of PPG share as of December 31, 2021 amounted to Rp2,103,000 (full amount) per share. After the transaction, the Company's ownership in PPG increased from 99.99% to 100.00%, through direct ownership and indirect ownership through UHN. The difference between selling price and fair value amounting to Rp1,103,000 (full amount) was recorded as "Differences in value of transactions of non-controlling interests" in consolidated financial statements.
