Our first material topic is about keeping our employees engaged and satisfied which we want to achieve through continuous people development through formal and informal trainings, providing a conducive and safe working environment and providing competitive employee compensation and benefits. We put time and resources to develop our employees to prepare them to be ambassadors of our companies inside and outside of the work area.

Our second material topic is about making our customers happy and satisfied. We ensure products we produce are safe and will promote better health. We ensure that our products undergo a series of diligent quality testing and checks, both internally and by any necessary external parties, including regulators.

Our third material topic is about innovating products that would cater to what customer needs. Since lifestyle and other factors that affect the health or our customers are constantly changing, we identify and adopt to these factors in order for us to provide our customers what they need in this dynamic society.

Our fourth material topic is about our responsibility on the use and protection of our environment. We want to make as minimal environmental footprint in our activity by reducing our waste and utility usage which ultimately results to reduced carbon emissions which is contributing to climate change, the biggest environment threat that we faced today. Thus we are and will continue to do initiatives to monitor and reduce our Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions, with 2022 as our base year. We have established an ESG Committee, directly reporting to the Board which will assist the Board in managing the implementation and realization of our sustainability objectives, which includes achieving the Science Based reduction targets determined from the result of our Scope 1, 2 and 3 emission inventories.

Our fifth material topic is about having a strong corporate governance. We educate and train all our employees about Code of Ethics and Business Conduct in order to prepare them and how to properly act in the face of moral dilemmas. We provide a support platform (whistleblowing hotline) where actual and suspected violations can be reported without worry of retaliation. We set policy and procedures on how to handle violators.

These are the brief overview of our strategy in achieving our sustainability objectives. More details will be presented in the succeeding pages.

Kami meluangkan waktu dan sumber daya untuk mengembangkan karyawan kami guna mempersiapkan mereka menjadi duta perusahaan kami di dalam dan di luar tempat kerja.

Topik materi kami yang kedua adalah tentang membuat pelanggan senang dan puas. Kami memastikan produk yang kami hasilkan aman dan akan meningkatkan kesehatan. Kami memastikan bahwa produk kami menjalani serangkaian pengujian dan pemeriksaan kualitas yang cermat, baik secara internal maupun oleh pihak eksternal yang diperlukan, termasuk regulator.

Topik materi ketiga kami adalah tentang inovasi produk yang dapat memenuhi kebutuhan pelanggan. Karena gaya hidup dan faktor-faktor lain yang mempengaruhi kesehatan atau pelanggan kami terus berubah, kami mengidentifikasi dan mengadopsi faktor-faktor ini agar kami dapat menyediakan apa yang pelanggan kami butuhkan dalam masyarakat yang dinamis ini.

Topik materi kami yang keempat adalah tentang tanggung jawab kami terhadap pemanfaatan dan perlindungan lingkungan. Kami ingin meminimalkan jejak lingkungan dalam aktivitas kami dengan mengurangi limbah dan penggunaan utilitas yang pada akhirnya menghasilkan pengurangan emisi karbon yang berkontribusi terhadap perubahan iklim, ancaman lingkungan terbesar yang kita hadapi saat ini. Topik kami yang kelima adalah tentang tata kelola perusahaan yang kuat. Kami mendidik dan melatih seluruh karyawan kami tentang Kode Etik dan Perilaku Bisnis untuk mempersiapkan mereka dan bagaimana bertindak dengan benar dalam menghadapi dilema moral. Kami menyediakan platform dukungan (hotline pelaporan pelanggaran) dimana pelanggaran aktual dan dugaan pelanggaran dapat dilaporkan tanpa khawatir akan adanya tindakan pembalasan.
