# Recruitment &amp; Selection

## Proses Rekrutmen &amp; Seleksi

Fulfilment of Human Resources (HR) with qualified competencies is very much needed to increase productivity and develop a strong team in the Company. Since the recruitment and selection process, SGH does not tolerate any form of discrimination based on race, skin colour, gender, age, language, religion, ethnicity, and social background.

The complex SGH organization with various Business Units ranging from R&amp;D, Manufacturing, Marketing and Sales, Distribution, along with all supporting units such as Business Development, Finance, Human Resources, Legal, Internal Audit, Compliance, Information Technology, and others requires a very diverse range of candidates, with various educational backgrounds and work experience.

In meeting HR needs, the Company actively recruits new graduates and professionals with the aim of leading to the company's success. Apart from that, the Company also selectively re-employs retirees as experts in certain fields. This strategy is also one of the actions taken by the Company in implementing the principles of inclusion in the Company.

Before starting the selection process for prospective employees, the recruitment team first prepares a plan for workforce needs within a certain period, maps it every year. The recruitment process involves identifying and selecting appropriate candidates for job positions based on qualifications, skills, and suitability to the Company's culture and goals.

Sejak proses rekrutmen dan seleksi, SGH tidak mentoleransi segala bentuk diskriminasi berdasarkan ras, warna kulit, jenis kelamin, usia, bahasa, agama, etnis, dan latar belakang sosial.

Organisasi SGH yang kompleks dengan berbagai Unit Bisnis mulai dari R&amp;D, Manufaktur, Pemasaran dan Penjualan, Distribusi, beserta seluruh unit pendukung seperti Bagian Pengembangan Bisnis, Keuangan, Sumber Daya Manusia, Legal, Kepatuhan, Audit Internal, Informasi Teknologi, dan lainnya membutuhkan kandidat yang sangat beragam dengan berbagai latar belakang pendidikan serta pengalaman kerja.

Dalam pemenuhan kebutuhan SDM, Perusahaan secara aktif merekrut lulusan baru maupun profesional dengan tujuan yang mengarah pada keberhasilan perusahaan. Selain itu, Perusahaan juga secara selektif mempekerjakan kembali para pensiunan sebagai tenaga ahli di bidang-bidang tertentu. Strategi ini juga merupakan salah satu tindakan yang diambil Perusahaan dalam penerapan prinsip-prinsip inklusi di Perusahaan.

Sebelum memulai proses seleksi calon karyawan, tim rekrutmen terlebih dahulu menyusun perencanaan kebutuhan tenaga kerja dalam kurun waktu tertentu dan dipetakan setiap tahun. Proses rekrutmen melibatkan identifikasi dan pemilihan kandidat yang tepat untuk posisi pekerjaan berdasarkan kualifikasi, keterampilan, dan kesesuaian terhadap budaya dan tujuan Perusahaan.
