## Supply &amp; Operations Townhall Meeting and Continuous Improvement Competition 2023

Supply &amp; Operations Townhall Meeting dan Continuous Improvement Competition 2023

Continuous Improvement Competition: "IMBOOST FORCE: IMPROVEMENT BOOSTER FOR EXCELLENCE" Competition 2023 carrying the theme "Success Through Synergy" has generated great employees' enthusiasm and 600+ implemented improvement ideas resulted in substantial savings. This year, the grand final of the IMBOOST FORCE Competition took place from 13 - 16 November 2023, and the announcement of the winners was made during the Town Hall Meeting for Supply &amp; Operations on December 15, 2023.

The Continuous Improvement Competition aims to stimulate good practices related to continuous improvement. The awards provide recognition to individual's contributions as well as team collaborations in identifying and implementing continuous improvement projects that benefit the company/customer or the wider society through individual project category called QCI (Quality Control Initiative), and group project through QCC (Quality Control Circle) and QCP (Quality Control Project).

Kompetisi Continuous Improvement: "IMBOOST FORCE: IMPROVEMENT BOOSTER FOR EXCELLENCE" Competition 2023 mengusung tema "Success Through Synergy" berhasil menciptakan antusiasme yang luar biasa di kalangan karyawan. Hal ini terbukti dengan adanya 600+ ide perbaikan yang telah diimplementasikan sehingga menghasilkan penghematan bagi perusahaan. Tahun ini grand final IMBOOST FORCE Competition dilaksanakan pada 13 - 16 November 2023 dan penganugerahan pemenang dilakukan pada Town Hall Meeting Supply &amp; Operations, 15 Desember 2023.

Kompetisi Continuous Improvement bertujuan untuk menstimulasi praktik dan proses bisnis yang terkait dengan perbaikan berkelanjutan. Penghargaan ini memberikan pengakuan atas kontribusi individu serta kolaborasi tim dalam mengidentifikasi dan menerapkan proyek perbaikan berkelanjutan yang bermanfaat bagi perusahaan/pelanggan atau masyarakat luas melalui kategori Proyek Individual yang disebut QCI (Quality Control Initiative), dan kategori Proyek Grup melalui QCC (Quality Control Circle) dan QCP (Quality Control Project).

This annual competition, reaching in 2023 its 13th edition, once again showcased successful continuous improvement ideas. Since inception in 2011, improvement ideas in our manufacturing site, ranging from reducing energy usage (such as electricity, fuel, gas and water consumption) to improving system and processes, had successfully delivered substantial savings.

![img-49.jpeg](img-49.jpeg)

Kompetisi tahunan yang telah diselenggarakan untuk ke-13 kalinya pada tahun ini, sekali lagi membuktikan keberhasilan ide-ide improvement. Sejak diinisiasi di tahun 2011, berbagai gagasan perbaikan di Supply &amp; Operations, mulai dari otomatisasi proses produksi, pengurangan penggunaan energi (listrik, bahan bakar, gas, dan air), optimasi proses, hingga digitalisasi sistem, telah berkontribusi untuk penghematan biaya perusahaan secara substansial.
