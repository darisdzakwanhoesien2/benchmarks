# 04 PPG Signed a Distribution Territory Expansion Agreement with PT Johnson &amp; Johnson Indonesia (JNJ)

PPG Menandatangani Perjanjian Kesepakatan Penambahan Wilayah Distribusi dengan PT Johnson &amp; Johnson Indonesia (JNJ)

![img-60.jpeg](img-60.jpeg)

![img-61.jpeg](img-61.jpeg)

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) has been entrusted by JNJ Indonesia to distribute the Consumer Health product line in the distribution areas of Banjarmasin, Solo, and Yogyakarta. Due to its excellent performance and effective collaboration, JNJ Indonesia has extend its trust to PPG with the expansion of distribution areas, including Jakarta, Tangerang, Bogor, Semarang, Samarinda, Pontianak, and Banjarmasin in the Medical Channel segment.

The signing of the distribution area expansion agreement with JNJ Indonesia took place on May 24, 2023, at the PPG headquarters in Pulogadung.

JNJ Indonesia focuses on Consumer Health products that are well-known among Indonesian people. These include Listerine, Combantrin, Mylanta, Daktarin, Benadryl, and J&amp;J Baby.

The appointment of PPG as a distribution partner for the expansion of this strategic distribution area is expected to strengthen and sustain the business of JNJ Indonesia. This is especially significant considering their longstanding partnership and the fact that PPG has proven to be the right and fitting partner.

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) selama ini telah dipercaya oleh JNJ Indonesia untuk mendistribusikan lini produk Consumer Health di wilayah distribusi Banjarmasin, Solo dan Yogyakarta. Karena kinerja yang mumpuni dan kolaborasi yang efektif diantara keduanya, JNJ Indonesia kembali mempercayakan penambahan wilayah distribusi kepada PPG mencakup wilayah Jakarta, Tangerang, Bogor, Semarang, Samarinda, Pontianak, dan Banjarmasin pada area Medical Channel.

Penandatanganan kerja sama penambahan wilayah distribusi dengan JNJ Indonesia ini dilaksanakan pada 24 Mei 2023 bertempat di kantor pusat PPG di Pulogadung.

JNJ Indonesia berfokus pada produk Consumer Health yang telah dikenal masyarakat luas, diantaranya Listerine, Combantrin, Mylanta, Daktarin, Benadryl, dan J&amp;J Baby.

Penunjukkan PPG sebagai mitra distribusi untuk penambahan wilayah distribusi strategis ini diharapkan dapat membangun bisnis JNJ Indonesia semakin kuat dan berkelanjutan mengingat keduanya telah bermitra sangat panjang sebelumnya dan PPG telah menjadi the right and fit partner.
