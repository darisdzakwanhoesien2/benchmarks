Pada kompetisi 2023 ini, PT SIP mendaftarkan proyek berjudul "Integrated Change Control System (ICCS)". Melalui proyek ICCS ini, tim yang beranggotakan Yoanda Alisa Putri (Quality Compliance), Siany Ayu Olivia (Quality Compliance), Raka Arif Budiman (R&amp;D), dan Frederiko Eduwardo (Continuous Improvement) berhasil membawa pulang penghargaan Bronze Winner untuk kategori Manufacturing, sekaligus memenangkan penghargaan "Favorite Team/Team of The Year" sebagai tim dengan performa terbaik.

The ICCS project has had a positive impact on the internal workings of the company, as the Change Control submission process can be expedited. This acceleration allows for the faster implementation of improvements within the company, leading to increased productivity among employees. Moreover, the project also stands as a pioneer in the extensive digitalization process that has taken place at PT SIP in recent years. Through this achievement, it is hoped that the Continuous Improvement culture within the SGH will continue to enhance company's performance.
