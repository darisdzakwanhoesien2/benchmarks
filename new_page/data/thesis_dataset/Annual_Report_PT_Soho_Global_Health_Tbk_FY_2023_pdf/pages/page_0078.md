## Concurrent Positions

He has been serving on the Board of Commissioners of the Company since 2020 to date.

He is also serving as a Commissioner in the Company's subsidiaries, namely PT. Universal Health Network and PT. Parit Padang Global from 2009 to date. In addition, he is also serving as the Commissioner in the Company's affiliate, namely PT. Soho Global Investment from 2010 to date.

Apart from the above, Mr. Eng Liang Tan does not have other concurrent positions in the Company or other companies in Indonesia.

## Affiliate Relations

He has an affiliation relationship with the Company, because Mr. Eng Liang Tan is the main ultimate beneficial owner of the Company through his ownership of the Company's shares by Cascade Creek Pty Ltd. and Tiberias 96 Pty Ltd.
