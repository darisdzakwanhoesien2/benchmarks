# 05 PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) Signed a Distribution Cooperation Agreement with PT Martina Berto, Tbk (MBTO)

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) Tandatangani Perjanjian Kerjasama Distribusi dengan PT Martina Berto, Tbk (MBTO)

![img-63.jpeg](img-63.jpeg)

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG), a subsidiary of PT Soho Global Health, Tbk, has agreed to enter into a distribution cooperation agreement with PT Martina Berto, Tbk (MBTO) in the General Trade channel.

The agreement was formalized through the signing of the cooperation agreement by the respective company leaders on May 29, 2023, at the PPG headquarters in Pulogadung.

PT Martina Berto, Tbk is one of the business units of Martha Tilaar Group, a leading cosmetics, skincare, and herbal manufacturer in Indonesia. It draws inspiration from the natural wealth and cultural heritage of Indonesia. One of the pillars embraced by Martha Tilaar Group is women empowerment.

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG), anak perusahaan PT Soho Global Health, Tbk sepakat melakukan perjanjian kerja sama distribusi dengan PT Martina Berto, Tbk (MBTO) untuk saluran distribusi General Trade.

Kesepakatan tersebut dikukuhkan melalui penandatanganan perjanjian kerja sama yang dilakukan oleh masing-masing pimpinan perusahaan pada tanggal 29 Mei 2023 bertempat di kantor pusat PPG di Pulogadung.

PT Martina Berto, Tbk merupakan salah satu unit bisnis dari Martha Tilaar Group, produsen kosmetik, skincare, herbal terkemuka di Indonesia yang terinspirasi dari kekayaan alam dan budaya Indonesia dengan salah satu pilar yang diusung oleh Martha Tilaar Group adalah women empowerment.

# 06 Signing of Distribution Cooperation Agreement between PPG and PT Medikon Prima Laboratories (MPL)

Penandatanganan Perjanjian Kerjasama Distribusi PPG dan PT Medikon Prima Laboratories (MPL)

![img-64.jpeg](img-64.jpeg)

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) has been appointed and entrusted by PT Medikon Prima Laboratories (PML) to distribute its well-known health products that are already popular among Indonesian society.

Parit Padang Global (PPG) ditunjuk dan dipercaya oleh PT Medikon Prima Laboratories (MPL) untuk mendistribusikan produk-produk kesehatan milik MPL yang sudah banyak dikenal oleh masyarakat Indonesia.
