# Deferred tax assets, net

As of December 31, 2023, the Company's deferred tax assets, net, amounted to IDR 117.6 billion. This represents an increase of 8.6%, mainly attributable to deferred tax from unrealized profit on intercompany sales.

# Fixed assets, net

The Company's fixed assets as of December 31, 2023 amounted to IDR 384.3 billion, which represents a slight increase of 0.2% compared to last year's IDR 383.5 billion. This increase was mainly due to the addition of office equipment, net of the normal annual depreciation of the fixed assets.

# Liabilities

The Company recorded total liabilities of IDR 2.3 trillion at the end of 2023, consisting of 93% short-term liabilities and 7% long-term liabilities. The amount of liabilities increased by IDR 300.8 billion or 14.7% compared to 2022, primarily due to an increase in short-term liabilities.

# Current liabilities

In 2023, the Company's short-term liabilities amounted to IDR 2.2 trillion, representing a 15.1% increase. The increase in short-term liabilities was mainly due to an increase in trade payables, partly offset by a decrease in accrued expenses.

# Aset pajak tangguhan

Pada tanggal 31 Desember 2023, aset pajak tangguhan bersih Perusahaan berjumlah Rp 117,6 milyar. Ini merupakan peningkatan sebesar 8,6%, terutama disebabkan oleh pajak tangguhan atas laba yang belum direalisasi pada penjualan antar perusahaan.

# Aset tetap, neto

Aset tetap Perseroan pada tanggal 31 Desember 2023 berjumlah Rp 384,3 milyar, yang menunjukkan peningkatan kecil sebesar 0,2% dibanding tahun lalu sebesar Rp 383,5 milyar. Peningkatan ini terutama disebabkan oleh penambahan peralatan kantor, dikurangi dengan depresiasi tahunan normal dari aset tetap.

# Liabilitas

Perseroan membukukan jumlah liabilitas sebesar Rp 2,3 triliun pada akhir tahun 2023, terdiri dari 93% liabilitas jangka pendek dan 7% liabilitas jangka panjang. Jumlah liabilitas mengalami kenaikan sebesar Rp 300,8 milyar atau 14,7% dibandingkan dengan tahun 2022, terutama disebabkan oleh peningkatan liabilitas jangka pendek.

# Liabilitas jangka pendek

# Trade payables

The Company's trade payables at the end of 2023 amounted to IDR 1.9 trillion, representing an increase of 18.5%. The increase in trade payables was mainly due to the increase in the value of inventory purchases for the distribution business segment.

# Tax payables

As of December 31, 2023, the Company recorded tax payable of IDR 11.4 billion, an increase of 5.9% compared to the previous year. This rise in tax liability was mainly attributable to withholding taxes.

# Utang usaha

Utang usaha Perseroan pada akhir tahun 2023 adalah sebesar Rp 1.9 triliun, mengalami kenaikan sebesar 18,5%. Naiknya utang usaha sebagian besar disebabkan oleh kenaikan nilai pembelian persediaan untuk segmen bisnis distribusi.

# Utang pajak

Pada tanggal 31 Desember 2023, Perseroan membukukan utang pajak sebesar Rp 11,4 milyar, naik sebesar 5,9% dibandingkan tahun lalu. Kenaikan ini terutama disebabkan oleh kenaikan pajak penghasilan potong-pungut.
