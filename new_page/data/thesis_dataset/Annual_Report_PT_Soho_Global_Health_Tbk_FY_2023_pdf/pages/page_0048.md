# Penghargaan Karya Anak Bangsa 2023

In the National Health Day (HKN) 2023 moment, SOHO received the "Karya Anak Bangsa 2023" award from the Ministry of Health of the Republic of Indonesia in the pharmaceutical industry category. The award was presented by the Minister of Health of Indonesia at the Jakarta Convention Center (JCC), Senayan, on November 9, 2023.

SOHO achieved this recognition due to its innovative natural-based products registered in the Innovation e-catalogue. Notably, 37 SOHO natural-based products are listed in the Innovation e-catalogue.

Same award was previously received in 2017, thanks to SOHO's innovation in the development of Temulawak at the SOHO Center of Excellence in Herbal Research (SCEHR), using the Seed to Patient approach.

Di momen Hari Kesehatan Nasional (HKN) 2023, SOHO mendapatkan penghargaan "Karya Anak Bangsa 2023" dari Kementerian Kesehatan Republik Indonesia untuk kategori industri farmasi. Penghargaan ini diserahkan oleh Menteri Kesehatan RI di Jakarta Convention Center (JCC), Senayan pada tanggal 9 November 2023.

SOHO meraih penghargaan ini berkat inovasi produk-produk berbasis bahan alami yang sudah terdaftar di e-katalog Inovasi. Tidak kurang dari 37 produk SOHO bahan alami yang sudah terdaftar di e-katalog Inovasi.

Penghargaan yang sama juga pernah diiraih di tahun 2017 berkat inovasi SOHO dalam pengembangan Temulawak di SOHO Center of Excellence in Herbal Research (SCEHR), dengan pendekatan Seed to Patient.

# Bronze Winner and Team of The Year – OPEXCON 2023

In October 2023, the highly significant operational excellence competition titled Indonesia Operational Excellence Conference &amp; Award (OPEXCON) 2023 was held for business practitioners in Indonesia. This event serves as the peak of the annual competition organized by SHIFT Indonesia in collaboration with SSCX International, a consulting company specializing in operational excellence, productivity improvement, and business process development.

This competition was participated in by various private and government agencies, as well as state-owned enterprises (BUMN). The selection and judging process involved a panel of professional judges, including academics, government officials, and Continuous Improvement practitioners. The event is expected to serve as a platform for companies to consistently innovate and engage in continuous improvement practices.

Pada bulan Oktober 2023 digelar perhelatan kompetisi operational excellence yang sangat penting bagi para pelaku bisnis di Indonesia bertajuk Indonesia Operational Excellence Conference &amp; Award (OPEXCON) 2023. Acara ini merupakan acara puncak kompetisi tahunan yang diadakan oleh SHIFT Indonesia yang bermitra dengan SSCX International, perusahaan konsultan yang fokus dalam operational excellence, peningkatan produktivitas, dan pengembangan proses bisnis.

Kompetensi ini diikuti oleh berbagai instansi swasta dan instansi pemerintah, maupun BUMN, dimana proses seleksi &amp; penjuriannya melibatkan dewan juri profesional, baik dari akademisi, intansi pemerintah, hingga praktisi Continuous Improvement. Kegiatan ini diharapkan bisa menjadi wadah bagi perusahaan untuk secara konsisten melakukan inovasi dan menjalankan improvement secara berkelanjutan.
