Kelompok Usaha memiliki sebidang tanah dengan Hak Guna Bangunan ("HGB") di Jakarta, Bogor, Palembang, Bandung, Medan dan Sukabumi dengan keseluruhan masing-masing sejumlah 140.486 meter persegi pada tanggal 31 Desember 2023 dan 2022. Manajemen Kelompok Usaha berpendapat tidak terdapat masalah dengan perpanjangan hak atas tanah karena seluruh tanah diperoleh secara sah dan didukung dengan bukti kepemilikan yang memadai.

Additions in 2023 and 2022 include reclassification from advances amounting to Rp7,573 and Rp4,099, respectively (Note 29).

As of December 31, 2023 and 2022, the Group has advance for purchase of fixed assets amounted Rp8,087 and Rp7,573, respectively.

In 2023 and 2022, the Group has written-off fixed assets with net book value amounting to Rp35 and Rp1, respectively.

The Group owns parcels of land with Building Use Right ("HGB") in Jakarta, Bogor, Palembang, Bandung, Medan and Sukabumi with a total of 140,486 square meters as of December 31, 2023 and 2022. The HGB will expire between 2023 up to 2042. Management of the Group believes that there will be no difficulty in the extension of land rights since all the land rights were acquired legally and supported with appropriate ownership evidence.
