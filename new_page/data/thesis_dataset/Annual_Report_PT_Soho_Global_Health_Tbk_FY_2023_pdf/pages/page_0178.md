Board of Directors Charter

Piagam Direksi

In managing the Company, the Board of Directors follows the guidelines from the Board of Directors Charter which was inaugurated on June 9, 2015, which contains the work guidelines and rules, authority, duties, responsibilities, obligations, division of tasks, meetings, provisions on conflicts of interest, share ownership, mechanism arrangements and division of labor between members of the Board of Directors which are not regulated in the Company's articles of association or applicable laws and regulations. With the existence of the Board of Directors Charter, it is hoped that the performance of the Board of Directors will be more focused and in synergy with one another.

The Board of Directors Charter has been formulated based on the prevailing rules and regulations in Indonesia and is evaluated periodically to ensure its suitability with the development of the Company and the business environment.

Dalam mengelola Perseroan, Direksi berpedoman pada Piagam Direksi yang diresmikan pada tanggal 9 Juni 2015, yang memuat pedoman dan tata tertib kerja, wewenang, tugas, tanggung jawab, kewajiban, pembagian tugas, rapat, ketentuan benturan kepentingan, kepemilikan saham, pengaturan mekanisme dan pembagian kerja antar para anggota Direksi yang tidak diatur di dalam anggaran dasar Perseroan maupun ketentuan perundang-undangan yang berlaku. Dengan adanya Piagam Direksi, diharapkan kinerja Direksi lebih terarah dan bersinergi satu sama lain.

Piagam Direksi tersebut telah diformulasikan berdasarkan ketentuan dan peraturan yang berlaku di Indonesia, serta dievaluasi secara periodik untuk memastikan kesesuaiannya dengan perkembangan Perseroan dan lingkungan usaha.

# Assessment of the performance of the Board of Directors

# Penilaian terhadap kinerja Direksi

Referring to the Board of Directors Charter, the evaluation of the performance of the members of the Board of Directors is carried out by the Board of Commissioners and shareholders of the Company. The Company also has a Policy on Performance Appraisal of the Board of Directors, which this policy is prepared as a guideline that can be used to assess the effectiveness of the implementation of the duties and responsibilities of the Board of Directors and also as a form of accountability for the performance assessment of the Board of Directors of the Company.

The Board of Directors Charter contains an explanation of the method for evaluating the performance of members of the Board of Directors, which includes the following:

1. The Board of Commissioners will determine the Key Performance Indicators (KPI)
2. The Board of Commissioners will evaluate the performance achievements of the members of the Board of Directors which will then be submitted to the shareholders at the General Meeting of Shareholders ("GMS")
3. The performance evaluation of the members of the Board of Directors will be carried out based on the following KPIs:

a. Achievement of the Board of Directors' performance (collectively) by looking at the Company's achievements based on the Business Plan and Annual Operational Plan, and/or other criteria determined by the Board of Commissioners.
b. Achievement of the performance of the Board of Directors (individually) in achieving the Company's performance as stated in the Business Plan and Annual Operational Plan, Management Contract, and/or other criteria determined in the Board of Commissioners meeting.
c. Implementation of Good Corporate Governance principles, both individually and collectively.

Mengacu pada Piagam Direksi (Board of Directors Charter), penilaian atas kinerja anggota Direksi dilakukan oleh Dewan Komisaris dan pemegang saham Perseroan. Perseroan juga telah memiliki Kebijakan Penilaian atas Kinerja Direksi, yang mana kebijakan ini disusun sebagai pedoman yang dapat digunakan untuk menilai efektivitas pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab Direksi dan juga sebagai bentuk akuntabilitas atas penilaian kinerja Direksi Perseroan.

Piagam Direksi (Board of Directors Charter) memuat penjelasan mengenai metode evaluasi/penilaian kinerja atas anggota Direksi, yang mana di antaranya adalah sebagai berikut:

1. Dewan Komisaris akan menetapkan Indikator Kinerja Utama/ Key Performance Indicator (KPI)
2. Dewan Komisaris akan melakukan evaluasi atas pencapaian kinerja dari anggota Direksi yang mana selanjutnya akan disampaikan pada pemegang saham dalam Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham ("RUPS")
3. Evaluasi kinerja anggota Direksi tersebut akan dilakukan berdasarkan KPI sebagai berikut:

a. Pencapaian kinerja Direksi (secara bersama-sama) dengan melihat pencapaian Perseroan berdasarkan Rencana Bisnis dan Rencana Operasional Tahunan, dan/atau kriteria lainnya yang ditetapkan oleh Dewan Komisaris.
b. Pencapaian kinerja Direksi (secara individu) dalam mencapai kinerja Perusahaan Perseroan sebagaimana tercantum dalam Rencana Bisnis dan, Rencana Operasional Tahunan, Kontrak Manajemen, dan atau kriteria lainnya yang ditetapkan dalam rapat Dewan Komisaris.
c. Implementasi prinsip-prinsip Tata Kelola yang Baik (Good Corporate Governance), baik secara individu maupun kolektif.
