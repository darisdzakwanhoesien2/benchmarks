# 5. PT Garuda Indonesia (Persero) Tbk. ("Garuda")

## Case Status

Status Perkara

PPG is a Concurrent Creditor of Garuda. PPG has receivables that are past due and owed to Garuda for the pharmaceutical products supplied by PPG and Garuda is unable to pay these receivables. Garuda's bankruptcy application of Garuda was submitted by other creditors of Garuda.

PPG adalah Kreditur Konkuren Garuda. PPG memiliki piutang yang telah jatuh tempo dan terutang kepada Garuda atas produk farmasi yang dipasok oleh PPG dan Garuda tidak mampu membayar piutang tersebut. Permohonan pailit Garuda diajukan oleh kreditur Garuda yang lain.

## Risks Faced by the Company and Their Impacts on the Company

Risiko yang Dihadapi dan Pengaruhnya terhadap Perseroan

None, because Garuda has paid off all PPG receivables amounting to IDR 201,650,332.-.

Tidak ada, karena Garuda sudah melunasi semua piutang PPG sebesar Rp201.650.332,-.

## Follow-up

Tindak Lanjut

The case was closed by PPG because it was fully settled.

Kasus ditutup oleh PPG karena telah diselesaikan sepenuhnya.

# 6. Gatot Widjaja Tjipta

## Case Status

Status Perkara

Mr. Gatot is Mrs. Henny's ex-husband. He has filed an objection to the Sleman District Court regarding to determination of the collateral confiscation of the Villa in Bali as described in the previous case of Mrs. Henny above. Mr. Gatot has requested the Chairman of the Sleman District Court to cancel the decision to confiscate the guarantee for the Villa in Bali on the basis that the Villa in Bali is the joint property acquired during the marriage period between Mrs. Henny and Mr. Gatot.

Pak Gatot adalah mantan suami Ibu Henny. Beliau telah mengajukan keberatan kepada Pengadilan Negeri Sleman terkait penetapan sita jaminan atas Villa di Bali sebagaimana yang diuraikan pada kasus Ibu Henny sebelumnya di atas. Bapak Gatot telah meminta kepada Ketua Pengadilan Negeri Sleman untuk membatalkan penetapan sita jaminan Villa di Bali dengan pertimbangan bahwa Villa di Bali tersebut merupakan harta bersama yang diperoleh selama masa perkawinan antara Ibu Henny dan Bapak Gatot.

## Risks Faced by the Company and Their Impacts on the Company

Risiko yang Dihadapi dan Pengaruhnya terhadap Perseroan

PPG is at risk of not getting a full refund of its claim amounting to IDR 1,175,000,000 from the sale of the Villa in Bali.

PPG berisiko tidak akan memperoleh pengembalian atas tagihannya secara penuh yang jumlahnya sebesar Rp. 1.175.000.000 dari penjualan Villa di Bali tersebut.

## Follow-up

Tindak Lanjut

The current status is that Pak Gatot has withdrawn his lawsuit through the Sleman District Court based on Decree Number 233/Pdt.Bth/2022/PN Smn dated March 21, 2023. However, PPG is currently still undergoing the process related to Mrs. Henny's application for Judicial Review as previously described above.

Status terkini adalah Pak Gatot telah mencabut gugatannya melalui Pengadilan Negeri Sleman berdasarkan Penetapan Nomor 233/Pdt.Bth/2022/PN Smn tertanggal 21 Maret 2023. Namun demikian, PPG saat ini masih menjalani proses terkait pengajuan Peninjauan Kembali oleh Ibu Henny sebagaimana diuraikan sebelumnya di atas.

# 7. PT Maesindo Indonesia

## Case Status

Status Perkara

PPG and PT Maesindo Indonesia have signed a distribution agreement ("Distribution Agreement"), whereby in 2022 PPG decided to terminate the Distribution Agreement. Based on the Distribution Agreement, PT Maesindo Indonesia is obliged to buy back all the products that are still available at PPG or to inform the new distributor of PT Maesindo Indonesia to buy back the products. However, in fact, Maesindo did not agree to buy back all the remaining products nor instruct its new distributor to buy back all the remaining products.

PPG dan PT Maesindo Indonesia telah menandatangani perjanjian distribusi ("Perjanjian Distribusi"), dimana pada tahun 2022, PPG memutuskan untuk mengakhiri Perjanjian Distribusi tersebut. Berdasarkan Perjanjian Distribusi, PT Maesindo Indonesia wajib membeli kembali seluruh produk yang masih tersedia di PPG atau memberitahukan kepada distributor baru PT Maesindo Indonesia untuk membeli kembali produk tersebut. Namun kenyataannya, Maesindo tidak menerima untuk membeli kembali seluruh sisa produk tersebut atau memerintahkan distributor barunya untuk membeli kembali seluruh sisa produk tersebut.
