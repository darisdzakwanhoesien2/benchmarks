The company is also committed to respecting labor rights as a manifestation of human rights for employees, namely:

a. Fulfilling employment rights.
b. Respect the work relationship with employees, especially in terms of clarity of employee status, job placement, acceptance of new employees, mutation, rotations, promotions, and demotions.
c. Respect the right of employees to have good health, clear working days and hours, opportunities for sick leave, menstrual leave, maternity leave, miscarriage, collective leave, and annual leave.
d. Providing equal and non-discriminatory opportunities, as well as respecting diversity and inclusion.
e. Respect the rights of employees to freedom of association and gathering, and negotiation through unions or workers representatives.
f. Provide social facilities to all employees based on the company's capabilities.
g. Provide a workplace that is free from all forms of harassment and intimidation, as well as facilitating the employees to submit and resolve grievances.
h. Ensure the health and safety of employees by providing a safe, healthy, and productive workplace.

The Company also expects external parties and other stakeholders to participate in the company's commitments regarding human rights and encourage them to implement the same policies within their respective companies.

## Employee selection and benefits

We hire and incentivize people based on sole skills and capabilities. Ethnicity, religion, weight, appearance and gender are never a factor in our hiring and compensation criteria. Further, we have a strict policy and background check to filter hiring minors.

All company rights and obligations are stated in the employment agreement. We support and keep an open dialogue with the employee labor union. Further, all regulatory rights due to employees are awarded.

## Ecosystem and Environmental Preservation

SOHO environmental management policy complies with laws and environmental protection commitments, as stipulated in its Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE Policy). Environmental management practices and implementation are also regulated in the HSE Manual.

To ensure its environmental performance is in accordance with the predetermined policies, SOHO encourages business entities, especially production operation units, to implement the Environmental Management System in accordance with ISO 14001:2015.

Perusahaan juga berkomitmen dalam menghormati hak-hak ketenagakerjaan sebagai salah satu perwujudan HAM bagi karyawan, yaitu:

a. Memenuhi hak ketenagakerjaan.
b. Menghormati hubungan kerja dengan karyawan, khususnya dalam hal kejelasan status karyawan, penempatan kerja, penerimaan karyawan baru, mutasi, rotasi, promosi, dan demosi.
c. Menghormati hak karyawan untuk memiliki kesehatan yang baik, hari dan jam kerja yang jelas, kesempatan untuk cuti sakit, cuti haid, melahirkan, keguguran, cuti bersama, dan cuti tahunan.
d. Memberikan kesempatan yang setara dan non-diskriminatif serta menghargai keberagaman dan perbedaan.
e. Menghormati hak karyawan untuk berserikat dan berkumpul, serta bernegosiasi melalui serikat pekerja atau wakil pekerja.
f. Memberikan fasilitas sosial kepada seluruh karyawan sesuai kemampuan perusahaan.
g. Menyediakan tempat kerja yang bebas dari segala bentuk pelecehan dan intimidasi, dan memfasilitasi karyawan dalam menyampaikan dan menyelesaikan keluh kesah.
