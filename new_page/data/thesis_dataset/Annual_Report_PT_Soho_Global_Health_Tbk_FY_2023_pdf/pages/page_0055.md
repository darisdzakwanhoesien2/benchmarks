# 01 Signing of the PPG and Haleon Distribution Cooperation Agreement

Penandatanganan Perjanjian Kerjasama Distribusi PPG dan Haleon

![img-57.jpeg](img-57.jpeg)

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) officially becomes the distributor of Haleon Indonesia effective January 1, 2023.

PPG was appointed and trusted by Haleon Indonesia to distribute its well-known brands such as Panadol, Sensodyne, Polident, Actifed, Triaminic, Scott's Emulsion, and Voltaren which are widely known globally.

The signing ceremony for this cooperation agreement was held at the PPG head office in Pulogadung on February 16, 2023.

Haleon Indonesia was officially launched as an independent company that is 100% focused on consumer health after completing the demerger process from GSK in July 2022, and this is the first time Haleon Indonesia appointed a new distribution partner, namely PPG.

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) resmi menjadi distributor Haleon Indonesia efektif 1 Januari 2023.

PPG ditunjuk dan dipercaya oleh Haleon Indonesia untuk mendistribusikan brand ternama seperti Panadol, Sensodyne, Polident, Actifed, Triaminic, Scott's Emulsion, dan Voltaren yang sudah dikenal luas oleh masyarakat global.

Acara penandatanganan perjanjian kerjasama ini dilakukan di kantor pusat PPG di Pulogadung pada tanggal 16 Februari 2023.

Haleon Indonesia resmi diluncurkan sebagai perusahaan independen yang 100% berfokus pada kesehatan konsumen usai menyelesaikan proses pemisahan (demerger) dari GSK pada Juli 2022, dan ini adalah pertama kalinya Haleon Indonesia menunjuk mitra distribusi baru yaitu PPG.
