## PT. SOHO INDUSTRI PHARMASI ("SIP")

### 1. Directorate General of Taxes / Direktur Jendral Pajak

|  **Case Status**
Status Perkara | The appeal process for Corporate Income Tax and Value Added Tax for the 2015 fiscal year has been completed. The Tax Court decided to partially grant the demands of the SIP tax dispute case.

Proses banding atas Pajak Penghasilan Badan dan Pajak Pertambahan Nilai untuk tahun pajak 2015 telah selesai. Pengadilan Pajak memutuskan untuk mengabulkan sebagian tuntutan perkara sengketa pajak SIP.  |
| --- | --- |
|  **Risks Faced by the Company and Their Impacts on the Company**
Risiko yang Dihadapi dan Pengaruhnya terhadap Perseroan | Based on Tax Court's decision, on July 2023, the East Jakarta Madya Tax Office refunded to SIP the overpayment Corporate Income Tax amounting to IDR 4.454.782.240 and the overpayment Value Added Tax amounting to IDR 292.898.285. SIP will apply the fiscal corrections that are still maintained by the Tax Court Decision on transactions of the same nature in subsequent tax years.

Berdasarkan putusan Pengadilan Pajak, pada Juli 2023, Kantor Pajak Madya Jakarta Timur mengembalikan kelebihan bayar Pajak Penghasilan Badan sebesar Rp 4.454.782.240 dan Pajak Pertambahan Nilai sebesar Rp 292.898.285. SIP akan menerapkan koreksi fiskal yang masih dipertahankan Keputusan Pengadilan Pajak pada transaksi yang sifatnya sama di tahun-tahun pajak selanjutnya.  |
|  **Follow-up**
Tindak Lanjut | SIP agrees with the Tax Court's decision
SIP menyetujui hasil putusan Pengadilan Pajak.  |

## PT. Parit Padang Global ("PPG")

### 1. PT. Asuransi Himalaya Pelindung

|  **Case Status**
Status Perkara | PPG is a policyholder of Himalaya Insurance. PPG has submitted a claim for product damages to Himalaya Insurance from August 1, 2016 to January 22, 2018. However, PPG has not yet received payment for the claim. PPG, through its attorney, has filed a complaint with the OJK regarding the submission of this claim. Based on the Complaint Response Letter from the OJK, it was stated that Himalaya Insurance had had its operational license revoked and therefore had to be dissolved. A liquidation team has been formed to resolve claims submitted by PPG.

PPG merupakan pemegang polis Asuransi Himalaya. PPG telah mengajukan klaim atas kerusakan produk kepada Asuransi Himalaya sejak tanggal 1 Agustus 2016 sampai 22 Januari 2018. Namun demikian, PPG sampai saat ini belum juga menerima pembayaran terhadap klaim tersebut. PPG melalui kuasa hukumnya telah melakukan pengaduan ke OJK terkait pengajuan klaim ini. Berdasarkan Surat Tanggapan Pengaduan dari OJK, dinyatakan bahwa Asuransi Himalaya sudah dicabut izin operasionalnya sehingga harus dibubarkan.
