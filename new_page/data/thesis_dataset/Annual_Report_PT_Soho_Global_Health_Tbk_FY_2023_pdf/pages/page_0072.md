# Audit Committee Profile

## Profil Komite Audit

The Company's Audit Committee is appointed based on the decision of the Board of Commissioners and is responsible to the Board of Commissioners in assisting and carrying out the duties and functions of the Board of Commissioners. The company's Audit Committee acts independently in carrying out its duties and responsibilities.
