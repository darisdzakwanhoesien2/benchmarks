Implementation of GCG in the Company is based on the applicable laws and regulations, including:

Penerapan GCG di Perseroan berlandaskan pada perundang-undangan dan peraturan lain yang berlaku, antara lain:

01 The 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia
Undang-Undang Dasar Negara Republik Indonesia Tahun 1945

02 Law No. 40/2007 on Limited Liability Companies

05 Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 16/POJK.04/2020 on Implementation of Electronic General Meeting of Shareholders of Public Companies
Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 16/POJK.04/2020 tentang Pelaksanaan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Perusahaan Terbuka Secara Elektronik

06 Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 33/POJK.04/2014 on Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners of Issuers or Public Companies
Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 33/POJK.04/2014 tentang Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik

07 Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 34/POJK.04/2014 on the Nomination and Remuneration Committee of Issuers or Public Companies

Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 35/POJK.04/2014 tentang Sekretaris Perusahaan Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik

09 Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 55/POJK.04/2015 on the Establishment and Guidelines for the Work Implementation of the Audit Committee
Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 55/POJK.04/2015 tentang Pembentukan dan Pedoman Pelaksanaan Kerja Komite Audit

10 Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 56/POJK.04/2015 on the Establishment and Guidelines for the Preparation of Internal Audit Unit Charter
Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 56/POJK.04/2015 tentang Pembentukan dan Pedoman Penyusunan Piagam Unit Audit Internal

11 Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 29/POJK.04/2016 on Annual Report of Issuers or Public Companies and Financial Services Authority Circular Letter No. 30/SEOJK.04/2016 on Form and Content of Issuers or Public Companies' Annual Report, as amended by Circular Letter of the Financial Services Authority of the Republic of Indonesia No. 16/SEOJK.04/2021 concerning the Form and Content of the Annual Report of Issuers or Public Companies
Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 29/POJK.04/2016 tentang Laporan Tahunan Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik dan Surat Edaran Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 30/SEOJK.04/2016 tentang Bentuk dan Isi Laporan Tahunan Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik, sebagaimana diubah dengan Surat Edaran Otoritas Jasa Keuangan Republik Indonesia No. 16/SEOJK.04/2021 tentang Bentuk Dan Isi Laporan Tahunan Emiten Atau Perusahaan Publik

Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan Nomor 21/POJK.04/2015 tentang Penerapan Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan Terbuka dan Surat Edaran Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 32/SEOJK.04/2015 tentang Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan Terbuka
