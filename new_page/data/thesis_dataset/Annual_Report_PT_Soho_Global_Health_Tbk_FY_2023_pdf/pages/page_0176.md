Meetings of the Board of Directors

Rapat Direksi

The Board of Directors has held internal meetings and joint meetings which involve the Board of Commissioners. The meetings of the Board of Directors are a forum and mechanism for decision-making by the Board of Directors collectively.

Based on the Company's Articles of Association, the Board of Directors of the Company will hold regular internal meetings at least 1 (one) time per month but may hold other meetings at any time, if deemed necessary. Meanwhile, joint meetings between the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners are held regularly at least 1 (one) time in 4 (four) months.

During the year 2023, the Board of Directors has held 12 (twelve) Meetings of the Board of Directors and 4 (four) Joint Meetings of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners, with details of attendance rate as follows:

Direksi telah melaksanakan rapat internal maupun rapat gabungan yang melibatkan Dewan Komisaris. Rapat Direksi merupakan suatu forum dan mekanisme atas pengambilan keputusan Direksi secara kolektif/bersama-sama.

Berdasarkan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan, Direksi Perseroan akan mengadakan rapat internal secara berkala paling kurang 1 (satu) kali setiap bulan, namun dapat mengadakan rapat lainnya setiap saat apabila dipandang perlu. Sementara, rapat gabungan antara Direksi bersama dengan Dewan Komisaris diadakan secara berkala paling kurang 1 (satu) kali dalam 4 (empat) bulan.

Sepanjang tahun 2023, Direksi telah melaksanakan sebanyak 12 (dua belas) kali Rapat Direksi dan 4 (empat) kali Rapat Gabungan Direksi bersama Dewan Komisaris, dengan rincian tingkat kehadiran sebagai berikut:
