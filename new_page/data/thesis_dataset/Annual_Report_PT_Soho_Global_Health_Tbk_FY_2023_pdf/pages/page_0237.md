In 2022 and continuing to 2023, SOHO Global Health developed the Seed to Patient concept for products made from Echinacea purpurea (L.) Moench. Although Echinacea is natively an herb from Europe with subtropical to temperate climates, the extensive use of Echinacea outside European countries including Indonesia, makes Echinacea one of the herbs that has been naturalized to become an endogenous Indonesian herb. By applying the concept of Academic, Business, Government and Community (ABGC) collaboration, we have collaborated with the Spice and Medicinal Plants Research Institute (Balittro) to obtain superior Echinacea seeds in Indonesia and also with the GAPOKTAN Dahlia farming community for their cultivation. As such, SOHO has succeeded in carrying out Cultivation of Echinacea purpurea on SCEHR land which is estimated to be carried out 2-3 times each growing season. Each growing season, the amount of Echinacea simplicia produced ranges from 0.25-0.5 kg per m². The Echinacea simplicia produced by SCEHR also has superior quality with active biomarker levels that have met or even exceeded the Echinacea simplicia quality standards set by the European Medicines Agency (EMA). Further collaboration with academics in testing the effectiveness of extracts as antioxidants, tests are carried out by determining the IC50 value of each extract. IC50 itself is defined as the concentration of antioxidant compounds needed to reduce radical activity by 50%, where a smaller value indicates higher activity. The test results showed that Echinacea purpurea extract from SCEHR had the best antioxidant effect compared to other Echinacea extract samples.

In 2023, we continued the Seed to Patient concept by conducting extraction experiments on Echinacea purpurea dry herbs to obtain a standardized dry extract which will further be used in our new products. As an initial stage, the results of the experiments that we conducted show prospective results. This experiment will continue in the following year until expected result are obtained.

In 2023, SOHO Global Health expanded its Academic, Business, Government, and Community (ABGC) collaboration by obtaining RISPRO (Innovative Research Production) 2023 funding from the LPDP (Education Fund Management Institution) - Ministry of Finance. The RISPRO program aims to generate breakthrough research innovations that emphasize in increasing national competitiveness, potentially increasing intellectual wealth and economic value, as well as practical applications of renewable technologies, ultimately contributing to the well-being of the Indonesian society.

SOHO Global Health is recognized as qualified by LPDP in terms of technological readiness, innovative ideas, and human resource capabilities thus we were awarded a RISPRO research grant for the development of innovative immunostimulant products based on native Indonesian herbs. For this RISPRO program, SOHO Global Health collaborates with Bogor Agricultural Institute (IPB) as a partner in conducting research, and farmers and local suppliers for the availability of raw materials.

Pada tahun 2022 dan berlanjut sampai tahun 2023, SOHO Global Health mengembangkan konsep proses pembibitan sampai ke pengguna untuk produk-produk berbahan Echinacea purpurea (L.) Moench. Echinacea walaupun secara native merupakan herbal dari Eropa yang beriklim subtropis hingga sedang, pemanfaatan Echinacea yang luas di luar negara-negara Eropa termasuk Indonesia, menjadikan Echinacea sebagai salah satu herbal yang telah dinaturalisasi menjadi herbal endogenous Indonesia. Dengan menerapkan konsep kolaborasi Academic, Business, Government, dan Community (ABGC), kolaborasi dengan Balai Penelitian Tanaman Rempah dan Obat (Balittro) untuk memperoleh bibit unggulan Echinacea yang ada di Indonesia dan juga dengan komunitas petani GAPOKTAN Dahlia untuk kultivasinya. Hasil pengujian menunjukan ekstrak Echinacea purpurea dari SCEHR memiliki efek antioksidan paling baik dibandingkan dengan sampel ekstrak Echinacea lainnya.
