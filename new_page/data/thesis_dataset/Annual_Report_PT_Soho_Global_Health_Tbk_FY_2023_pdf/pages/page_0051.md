# New Product Launch: Imboost Cough
Peluncuran Produk Baru: Imboost Cough

In October 2023, SOHO Global Health proudly launched Imboost Cough, the latest innovation from Imboost. Imboost Cough comes in two (2) variants, one for adults (Imboost Cough) with a soothing mint flavour and one for children (Imboost Cough Kids) with a cherry flavour that kids love. This herbal cough syrup contains a combination of extracts from echinacea, ivy leaves, thyme, and ginger, which can help alleviate coughs and support immune health.

Pada Oktober 2023, SOHO Global Health dengan bangga meluncurkan Imboost Cough, inovasi terbaru dari Imboost. Imboost Cough hadir dengan dua (2) varian, untuk dewasa (Imboost Cough) dengan rasa Mint yang melegakan, dan untuk anak (Imboost Cough Kids) dengan rasa ceri yang disukai anak-anak. Sirup obat batuk herbal ini mengandung kombinasi dari ekstrak echinacea, daun ivy, biji timi, dan jahe, yang dapat membantu redakan batuk dan jaga daya tahan tubuh.

In line with the government's program to empower productive communities and drive "Indonesia Maju", people are becoming more productive again after the pandemic. Mobility and direct interaction with colleagues have increased. Immune strength has become essential to ensure that activities are not disrupted due to susceptibility to illnesses, especially in the current extreme hot weather and pollution, which can easily weaken the immune system.

Immune strength can vary from person to person. For individuals with a weaker immune system, one of the signs of a weakened immune system is the presence of a cough. When a cough appears, people often treat it on its own. However, by boosting the immune system, it can accelerate recovery and prevent the onset of more severe illnesses.

On October 9, 2023, Imboost Cough was launched with a Health Talk event titled "Imboost Innovation in Cough Relief" at Penang Bistro, Kebon Sirih, Jakarta. The Imboost Cough launch event featured three (3) speakers: Dr. Farhan Zubedi (Doctor and Digital Content Creator for Health Education), Titi Kamal (Actress, Mother of two, and Imboost Brand Ambassador), and Dr. Ir. Raphael Aswin, ST, MSi, CIP, IPU (VP Research &amp; Development and Regulatory &amp; Medical Affairs at PT. Soho Global Health). The event was attended by media and was celebrated with enthusiasm.

Selaras dengan program pemerintah untuk masyarakat produktif menuju "Indonesia Maju" masyarakat kembali produktif pasca pandemi. Mobilitas dan interaksi langsung dengan rekan kerja menjadi meningkat. Daya tahan tubuh jadi kunci agar aktivitas tidak terganggu karena rentan terhadap penyakit. Terlebih saat cuaca panas dan polusi saat ini yang menyebabkan daya tahan tubuh mudah menurun.

Daya tahan tubuh dapat berbeda pada tiap orang. Pada orang dengan daya tahan tubuh rendah, salah satu tanda munculnya penurunan daya tahan tubuh adanya batuk. Ketika muncul batuk, masyarakat kerap mengobati batuknya saja. Padahal dengan meningkatkan daya tahan tubuh, akan mempercepat pemulihan dan juga mencegah munculnya penyakit yang lebih berat.
