Following up on the Inmendagri, SGH Group has played an active role in supporting the Government's program in dealing with air pollution in the East Jakarta area.

SGH Group's support among others in providing tabebuya (tacyba bebuya) and terminalia catappa trees which were able to absorb air pollution. The trees were handed over on September 11, 2023, through the East Jakarta Mayor's office.

![img-150.jpeg](img-150.jpeg)

On September 29, 2023, SGH Group attended the invitation from the Mayor of East Jakarta for a tree planting event in order to contribute to reducing air pollution in Jakarta, particularly in the East Jakarta Administrative City.

The tree planting attended by Mayor of East Jakarta, East Jakarta Regional District Officials, Subdistrict Heads, village heads and representatives from the Management of PT SOHO Industri Pharmasi located on Kali Buaran Inspection Road and Reservoir site C Jakarta Garden City (JGC).

![img-151.jpeg](img-151.jpeg)

Apart from planting trees, SGH Group also participated in the installation of a water mist generator which was held on October 24, 2023, at the SGH Group head office. The event was attended by East Jakarta city government officials from:

1. Environmental Department
2. Fire and Rescue Department
3. Civil Service Police Unit
4. Rawa Terate Village
5. Cakung District

Menindaklanjuti Inmendagri tersebut, SGH Group turut berperan aktif mendukung program Pemerintah dalam menangani pencemaran udara di area Jakarta Timur.

Dukungan SGH Group antara lain diberikan dalam bentuk partisipasi menyediakan pohon tabebuya dan ketapang kencana yang mampu menyerap udara yang tercemar. Pohon-pohon tersebut diserahkan pada tanggal 11 September 2023 melalui kantor Walikota Jakarta Timur.

![img-152.jpeg](img-152.jpeg)

Pada tanggal 29 September 2023, SGH Group menghadiri undangan dari Walikota Jakarta Timur dalam acara penanaman pohon dalam rangka berkontribusi mengurangi pencemaran udara di Provinsi DKI Jakarta umumnya dan di Kota Administrasi Jakarta Timur khususnya.

Penanaman pohon tersebut berlokasi di Jalan Inspeksi Kali Buaran dan Waduk site C Jakarta Garden City (JGC) yang dihadiri oleh Walikota Jakarta Timur, Pejabat Kodya Jakarta Timur, Camat, Lurah dan perwakilan dari Pimpinan Perusahaan PT SOHO Industri Pharmasi.
