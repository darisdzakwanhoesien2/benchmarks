As part of SGH's CSR objectives to support children's literacy in Indonesia, particularly in Eastern Indonesia, since 2022 and continuing into 2023 and beyond, SGH is committed to supporting the improvement of children's literacy in Sumba through collaboration with the EGK Sumba Foundation via the Share the Books: Hope for Sumba hope for Indonesia program.

## Hope for Sumba Hope for Indonesia program: SGH x English Goes to Kampung (EGK) Sumba together fights for children literacy

SGH supports English learning activities for school-age children in Sumba organized by the English Goes to Kampung (EGK) Sumba community in Lamboya village, Laboya Bawa, West Sumba through the Share the Books program: Hope for Sumba Hope for Indonesia.

In the midst of the challenging conditions in West Sumba, where schools are poorly equipped and access to books is limited, the donations and acts of kindness from Soho Global Health (SGH) colleagues mean a great deal to the local children. Reading books, stationery, learning tools, and monetary donations are all greatly appreciated. Books serve as a window to the world, helping children at EGK Sumba learn about the world and build their dreams.

The children and youth of Sumba are the future generations of the island, particularly in the tourism sector, which relies heavily on foreign visitors. Therefore, it is crucial to provide English language learning resources to the youth in Sumbanese villages. In 2015, a humanitarian and literacy activist named Roswita Asti Kulla initiated English Goes to Kampung (EGK) Sumba, which has since reached over 1,000 Sumbanese children. However, there is still a long way to go to achieve the foundation's mission of making education accessible on Sumba Island.

## Hope for Sumba Hope for Indonesia program: SGH x English Goes to Kampung (EGK) Sumba berjuang bersama untuk literasi

SGH mendukung kegiatan belajar bahasa Inggris bagi anak-anak usia sekolah di Sumba yang diselenggarakan oleh komunitas English Goes to Kampung (EGK) Sumba di desa Lamboya, Laboya Bawa, Sumba Barat melalui program Share the Books: Hope for Sumba Hope for Indonesia.

Di tengah kondisi Sumba Barat yang sulit dijangkau, kondisi sekolah yang kurang memadai dan keterbatasan terhadap akses buku bacaan, donasi dan kepedulian rekan-rekan Soho Global Health (SGH) yang berbagi buku bacaan, alat tulis, alat bantu belajar, dan donasi dana sungguh sangat berarti bagi mereka. Sungguh buku adalah jendela dunia, melalui buku, anak-anak di EGK Sumba mulai mengenal dunia, dan memberanikan diri membangun impian.

Anak-anak dan pemuda Sumba, menjadi generasi masa depan Sumba, khususnya di sektor pariwisata yang mengandalkan wisatawan manca negara. Ada kebutuhan mendasar untuk menyediakan sumber daya belajar bahasa Inggris bagi para pemuda desa di Sumba. Sejak tahun 2015, English Goes to Kampung (EGK) Sumba diinisiasi oleh seorang penggerak kemanusiaan dan penggiat literasi, Roswita Asti Kulla, memulai dari kelas kecil di pesisir pantai. Hari ini, Yayasan ini telah menjangkau lebih dari 1.000 anak Sumba. Namun perjuangan masih panjang untuk mewujudkan misi komunitas ini: membuat pendidikan dapat diakses secara menyeluruh di Pulau Sumba.
