|  04. | Public Company shall have policies on communication with shareholders or investors.

Perusahaan Terbuka memiliki suatu kebijakan komunikasi dengan pemegang saham atau investor | Recommendation has been implemented.

The Company has a policy related to communication with shareholders and investors as stated in the Company Policy. The policy regulates communication guidelines, procedures for the process of disclosing information, media and other forms of communication with investors.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.

Perseroan memiliki kebijakan terkait komunikasi dengan pemegang saham dan investor yang tertuang dalam Kebijakan Perusahaan. Kebijakan tersebut mengatur mengenai pedoman komunikasi, tata cara proses pengungkapan informasi, media serta bentuk-bentuk komunikasi yang dilakukan dengan investor.  |
|  05. | Public Company shall announce its policies on communication with the shareholders and investors on its website.

Perusahaan Terbuka mengungkapkan kebijakan komunikasi Perusahaan Terbuka dengan pemegang saham atau investor dalam Situs Web. | Recommendation has been implemented.

The Company has disclosed the Public Company's communication policy with shareholders or investors in the Company's Policy on the Company's Website.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.

Perseroan telah mengungkapkan kebijakan komunikasi Perusahaan Terbuka dengan pemegang saham atau investor dalam Kebijakan Perseroan di Situs Web Perseroan.  |

|  06. | Decisions on the number of members of the Board of Commissioners should consider the condition of the respective Public Company.

Penentuan jumlah anggota Dewan Komisaris mempertimbangkan kondisi Perusahaan Terbuka. | Recommendation has been implemented.

The Company has a Board of Commissioners Charter which regulates the number of members of the Board of Commissioners, where the composition and number of members of the Board of Commissioners is determined by the GMS and must be in accordance with the complexity of the Company's business taking into account the purposes and objectives of the Company as regulated in the Company's Articles of Association, in order to enable appropriate decision making to be effective, precise and fast and act independently.  |
