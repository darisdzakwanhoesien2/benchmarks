# Consumer Health Division
Divisi Produk Kesehatan Konsumen

## Overview

The Consumer Health Division (CH) specializes in over-the-counter (OTC) products sold through retail outlets and online. We are the leader in natural and herbal OTC supplement/multivitamin products marketed directly to consumers in Indonesia, with several flagship brands such as Imboost, Curcuma Plus, Fitkom, Diapet, Laxing, and others.

Net sales growth for CH decreased by -15.8% compared to 2022. This decline in sales is primarily due to Imboost being affected by changes in consumer behavior regarding the consumption of vitamins after the end of the Covid-19 pandemic. Other reasons for the decrease include cases of EG-DEG contamination in some syrup medications produced by competitors, resulting in acute kidney failure cases in children. The Ministry of Health has prohibited the sale of syrup medications and supplements from mid-October 2022 until they receive safe verification from BPOM. The syrup issue persisted until the first semester of 2023.

According to IQVIA, Indonesia Total Market MAT 4Q23, the Imboost Franchise (comprising Imboost Regular-CH and Imboost Force-PP) ranked 5th among the top 20 best-selling pharmaceutical products in Indonesia, both in prescription and over-the-counter categories.

Consumer Health (CH) products are marketed directly to consumers through offline and online retailers across Indonesia. Our products are easily accessible through various sales channels, including retail pharmacies, modern trade pharmacies, minimarkets, supermarkets, convenience stores, and e-commerce channels. Most of our Consumer Health products are manufactured by the SGH Group, with some products produced by local suppliers for the SGH Group's brand names.

## 2023 Highlights

Soho Consumer Health recorded a decrease in net sales of -15.8% by the end of 2023, amounting to IDR 430.6 billion and contributing approximately 5.3% to SGH's total net sales. Gross profit margin decreased to 68.8% in 2023 compared to 70.7% the previous year, driven by the declining sales contribution from Imboost due to the end of the Covid-19 pandemic and national syrup issues.

The marketing strategy and priorities for 2023 are focused on two main areas:

1. Driving growth and market share for Imboost as the top priority brand by adapting and optimizing investments due to the sharp decline in demand for vitamin categories in the post-pandemic era. Furthermore, preparing for future growth by expanding the Imboost brand into new categories through the launch of the new Imboost Cough syrup product for children and adults, in parallel with the launch of Imboost Force Cough Kids for doctor's prescriptions.

## Gambaran Umum

Divisi Kesehatan Konsumen (CH) mengkhususkan diri pada produk OTC yang dijual melalui gerai ritel dan online. Kami adalah pemimpin dalam produk suplemen/multivitamin OTC alami dan herbal yang dipasarkan langsung ke konsumen di Indonesia dengan beberapa brand unggulan seperti Imboost, Curcuma Plus, Fitkom, Diapet, Laxing, dll.

Pertumbuhan Penjualan Bersih CH turun sebesar -15.8% dibandingkan tahun 2022. Penurunan sales ini terutama disebabkan oleh Imboost yang terdampak oleh perubahan perilaku konsumen atas konsumsi vitamin setelah berakhirnya pandemi Covid-19. Penyebab penurunan lainnya karena adanya kasus tercemarnya EG-DEG pada beberapa obat sirup produksi kompetitor yang mengakibatkan kasus gagal ginjal akut pada anak. Kemenkes melarang penjualan obat-obatan dan suplemen bentuk sirup dari pertengahan Oktober 2022 sampai mendapatkan verifikasi aman dari BPOM. Isu sirup ini masih berlangsung sampai dengan Semester 1 2023.

Menurut IQVIA, Indonesia Total Market MAT 4Q23, Imboost Franchise (gabungan Imboost Regular-CH dan Imboost Force-PP) menduduki peringkat ke-5 di antara top 20 produk farmasi terlaris di Indonesia baik dalam kategori resep maupun OTC.

Produk CH dipasarkan langsung ke konsumen melalui perantara offline and online retailer di seluruh Indonesia. Produk kami sangat mudah diakses di seluruh saluran penjualan, di apotek ritel, modern trade apotek, Minimarket, supermarket, toko kelontong, hingga e-commerce channel. Sebagian besar produk Kesehatan Konsumen kami diproduksi oleh Grup SGH, dengan beberapa produk diproduksi oleh pemasok lokal untuk nama merek Grup SGH.
