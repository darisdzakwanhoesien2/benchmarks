Communication with Regulator

Komunikasi dengan Regulator

As a public company, the Company shall submit certain reports periodically or incidentally to the Financial Services Authority and the Indonesia Stock Exchange. These reports include financial and non-financial information that is presented completely, accurately, and in accordance with the transparency principle.

During the year 2023, there were no delays and warnings from the Financial Services Authority or the Indonesia Stock Exchange to the Company. It evidences that the Company has complied and fulfilled all provisions of the regulations of the Financial Services Authority and the Indonesia Stock Exchange.
