Approval of Stock Split.

Approved Resolutions:

Approve the split of the nominal value of shares (stock split) which was originally IDR 500 (five hundred Rupiah) per share to become IDR 50 (fifty Rupiah) per share.

Hasil Keputusan:

Menyetujui pemecahan nilai nominal saham (stock split) yang semula sebesar Rp. 500,- (lima ratus Rupiah) per saham menjadi sebesar Rp. 50,- (lima puluh Rupiah) per saham.

2. Second Agenda

Approval of the Amendment to Article 4 paragraph (1) dan paragraph (2) of the Company's Article of Association concerning the Company's Capital.

Approved Resolutions:

A Approve to amend Article 4 paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) of the Company's Articles of Association in connection with changes in the nominal value of shares (stock split), therefore the Article 4 paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) of the Company's Articles of Association shall be written and read as follows:

CAPITAL (Article 4)

1. The authorized capital of the Company is IDR 1,431,756,078,000 (one trillion four hundred thirty one billion seven hundred fifty six million seventy eight thousand Rupiah), divided into 28,635,121,560 (twenty eight billion six hundred thirty five million one hundred twenty one thousand five hundred and sixty) shares, each share having a nominal value of IDR 50 (fifty Rupiah).
2. Based on the authorized capital, has been issued and paid-up capital in the amount of 44.32% (forty four point thirty two percent) or 12,691,682,390 (twelve billion six hundred ninety one million six hundred eighty two thousand three hundred ninety) shares, with the total nominal value of IDR 634,584,119,500, (six hundred thirty four billion five hundred eighty four million one hundred nineteen thousand five hundred Rupiah) by the shareholders who have subscribed for the shares with details and the total nominal value of shares which will be mentioned below.

Status Tindak Lanjut:

Telah direalisasikan sepenuhnya.

2. Mata Acara Kedua

Persetujuan atas Perubahan Pasal 4 ayat (1) dan ayat (2) Anggaran Dasar Perseroan tentang Modal Perseroan.

Hasil Keputusan:

A Menyetujui untuk mengubah Pasal 4 ayat (1) dan ayat (2) Anggaran Dasar Perseroan sehubungan dengan perubahan nilai nominal saham (stock split), yang selanjutnya Pasal 4 ayat (1) dan ayat (2) Anggaran Dasar Perseroan ditulis dan berbunyi menjadi sebagai berikut:

MODAL (Pasal 4)

1. Modal dasar Perseroan berjumlah Rp. 1.431.756.078.000,- (satu triliun empat ratus tiga puluh satu miliar tujuh ratus lima puluh enam juta tujuh puluh delapan ribu Rupiah) yang terbagi atas 28.635.121.560 (dua puluh delapan miliar enam ratus tiga puluh lima juta seratus dua puluh satu ribu lima ratus enam puluh) saham, masing-masing saham dengan nilai nominal sebesar Rp. 50,- (lima puluh Rupiah).
2. Dari modal dasar tersebut, telah ditempatkan dan disetor sebesar 44,32% (empat puluh empat koma tiga dua persen) atau sejumlah 12.691.682.390 (dua belas miliar enam ratus sembilan puluh satu juta enam ratus delapan puluh dua ribu tiga ratus sembilan puluh) saham, dengan nilai nominal seluruhnya sebesar Rp. 634.584.119.500,- (enam ratus tiga puluh empat miliar lima ratus delapan puluh empat juta seratus sembilan belas ribu lima ratus Rupiah) oleh para pemegang saham yang mengambil bagian saham dengan rincian serta total nilai nominal saham yang akan disebutkan di bawah ini.
