|  12. | Decisions on the number of members of the Board of Directors shall consider the condition of the respective Public Company and the effectiveness of the decision-making process. Penentuan jumlah anggota Direksi mempertimbangkan kondisi Perusahaan Terbuka serta efektifitas dalam pengambilan keputusan. | Recommendation has been implemented. The Company has a Board of Directors Charter which regulates the number of members of the Board of Directors, where the composition and number of members of the Board of Directors is determined by the GMS with due regard to the purposes and objectives of the Company as stipulated in the Company's Articles of Association, thus enabling effective, precise and fast decision making in the daily management of the Company. The determination of the number of members of the Board of Directors is also regulated in Article 18 paragraph 1 of the Company's Articles of Association, the number of which is adjusted to the requirements of the prevailing laws and regulations in the Capital Market sector. Both the Board of Directors Charter and the Company's Articles of Association are available on the Company's Website. Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan. Perseroan telah memiliki Piagam Direksi yang mengatur mengenai jumlah anggota dari Direksi, dimana komposisi dan jumlah anggota Direksi ditetapkan oleh RUPS dengan memperhatikan maksud dan tujuan Perseroan sebagaimana diatur dalam Anggaran Dasar Perseroan, sehingga memungkinkan pengambilan keputusan yang efektif, tepat dan cepat dalam pengurusan Perseroan sehari-hari. Penentuan jumlah anggota Direksi juga diatur dalam Pasal 18 ayat 1 Anggaran Dasar Perseroan yang mana jumlahnya disesuaikan dengan persyaratan dalam peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku di bidang Pasar Modal. Baik Piagam Direksi maupun Anggaran Dasar Perseroan telah tersedia dalam Situs Web Perseroan.  |

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan. Penentuan komposisi anggota Direksi Perseroan telah memperhatikan keberagaman keahlian, pengetahuan dan pengalaman yang dibutuhkan oleh Perseroan.
