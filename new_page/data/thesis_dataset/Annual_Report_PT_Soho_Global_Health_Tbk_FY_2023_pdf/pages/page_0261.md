- Commemoration of the 78th Independence Day of the Republic of Indonesia

In commemoration of Indonesia's 78th Independence Day, PT SOHO Industri Pharmasi Labor Union held an August 2023 Festive Event.

This event is filled with sports competitions in individual and team categories, as well as several group games which aim to strengthen cohesiveness, cooperation, and work discipline between employees in-line with the Company's goals.

This competition was attended by SGH Group employees whose work location is in the Pulogadung area. There were several competitions held, including:

- Indoor football
- Badminton
- Table tennis
- Chess
- Fishing
- Exciting group competitions (yell-yell, ping-pong ball relay, marble relay, ball train, hooked hat, and other fun games)

- Music Performance in the Manufacturing site

PT SOHO Industri Pharmasi Labor Union also initiated music performance by employees. This activity is held once a month in the manufacturing area which aims to facilitate employees' interest and talent in music as a positive activity.

- Peringatan Hari Kemerdekaan Republik Indonesia ke-78

Dalam rangka memperingati Hari Kemerdekaan Indonesia yang ke-78, Serikat Pekerja PT SOHO Industri Pharmasi mengadakan acara Gelora Bulan Agustus.

Acara ini diisi dengan perlombaan olah raga perseorangan dan beregu, juga beberapa permainan kelompok yang bertujuan untuk memperkuat kekompakan, kerja sama, serta disiplin kerja antar karyawan yang selaras dengan tujuan Perusahaan.

Perlombaan ini diikuti oleh karyawan SGH Group yang lokasi kerjanya di area Pulogadung. Terdapat beberapa perlombaan yang diadakan, antara lain:

- Futsal
- Bulutangkis
- Tenis meja
- Catur
- Memancing ikan
- Lomba kelompok seru (yel-yel, estafet bola ping-pong, estafet kelereng, kereta bola, topi berkait, dan permainan seru lainnya)
