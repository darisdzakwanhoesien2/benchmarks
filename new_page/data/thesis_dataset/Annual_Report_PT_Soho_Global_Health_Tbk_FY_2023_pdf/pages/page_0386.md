The notification of changes to the articles of association and the data updates have been received and recorded in the database of the Legal Entity Administration System of the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia, as stated in Letter Number AHU-AH.01.03-0134270 dated October 27, 2023.
