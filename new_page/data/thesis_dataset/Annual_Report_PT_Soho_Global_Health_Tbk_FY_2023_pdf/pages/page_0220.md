# 3. PT. Sinar Kencana Multilestari ("SKML")

## Case Status

Status Perkara

PPG is a Concurrent Creditor of SKML. PPG has receivables that are past due and owed to SKML for the pharmaceutical products supplied by PPG and SKML is unable to pay these receivables. The SKML's bankruptcy application was submitted by other SKML creditors.

PPG adalah Kreditur Konkuren SKML. PPG memiliki piutang yang telah jatuh tempo dan terutang kepada SKML atas produk farmasi yang dipasok oleh PPG dan SKML tidak mampu membayar piutang tersebut. Permohonan pailit SKML diajukan oleh kreditur SKML yang lain.

## Risks Faced by the Company and Their Impacts on the Company

Risiko yang Dihadapi dan Pengaruhnya terhadap Perseroan

PPG is at risk of not receiving full payment for its receivables amounting to IDR 401,647,825.

PPG berisiko tidak akan memperoleh pembayaran atas piutangnya secara penuh yang jumlahnya sebesar Rp. 401.647.825.

## Follow-up

Tindak Lanjut

SKML has been declared bankrupt based on the Commercial Court decision at Central Jakarta District Court No. 230/Pdt.Sus.PKPU/2021/PN Niaga dated July 1, 2021. Until now, PPG is still waiting for the settlement process from the liquidation team to get payment for PPG's receivables (if any).

SKML telah dinyatakan pailit berdasarkan putusan Pengadilan Niaga pada Pengadilan Negeri Jakarta Pusat No. 230/Pdt.Sus.PKPU/2021/PN Niaga tertanggal 1 Juli 2021. Hingga saat ini, PPG masih menunggu proses penyelesaian dari tim likuidasi untuk mendapatkan pembayaran atas piutang PPG (jika ada).

# 4. PT. Tozy Sentosa ("Centro")

## Case Status

Status Perkara

PPG is a Concurrent Creditor of Centro. PPG has receivables that are past due and owed to Centro for the pharmaceutical and OTC products supplied by PPG and Centro is unable to pay these receivables. Centro's bankruptcy application was submitted by other creditors of Centro.

PPG adalah Kreditur Konkuren Centro. PPG memiliki piutang yang telah jatuh tempo dan terutang kepada Centro atas produk farmasi dan OTC yang dipasok oleh PPG dan Centro tidak mampu membayar piutang tersebut. Permohonan pailit Centro diajukan oleh kreditur Centro yang lain.

## Risks Faced by the Company and Their Impacts on the Company

Risiko yang Dihadapi dan Pengaruhnya terhadap Perseroan

PPG did not receive payment for its receivables in full, which initially amounted to IDR 152,250,504,-, and only received payment of around 20 percent, namely IDR 32,216,202.53.

PPG tidak memperoleh pembayaran atas piutangnya secara penuh yang awalnya sebesar Rp 152.250.504,-, dan hanya memperoleh pembayaran sekitar 20 persen-nya, yakni Rp 32.216.202,53.

## Follow-up

Tindak Lanjut

Based on the Notification of List of Bankruptcy Assets of PT Tozy Sentosa (In Bankruptcy) and Request for Account Information No. 260/Pailit-TOZY/VI/2022 dated June 14, 2022, PPG only received payments of IDR 32,216,303.53.

Berdasarkan Pemberitahuan Daftar Pembagian Harta Pailit PT Tozy Sentosa (Dalam Pailit) dan Permintaan Informasi Rekening No. 260/Pailit-TOZY/VI/2022 tertanggal 14 Juni 2022, PPG hanya menerima pembayaran sebesar Rp. 32.216.303,53.
