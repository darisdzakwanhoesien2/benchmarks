2. Henny Puspawati

Case Status
Status Perkara

PPG has signed a Lease Agreement with Mrs. Henny for warehouse rental as stated in the Deed No. 1 dated October 1, 2015 ("Lease Agreement"). Pursuant to the Lease Agreement, PPG is required to pay rent for the first five-year lease period in the amount of IDR 1,775,000,000,-. This obligation has been carried out by PPG, but in its implementation, it was found that the Warehouse IMB was not in accordance with its intended purpose, so the Warehouse could not be used by PPG. PPG has requested Mrs. Henny to refund the rental fee paid by PPG in the amount of IDR 1,175,000,000. This amount has been agreed upon by PPG and Mrs. Henny as stated in the Rental Fee Repayment Agreement dated April 2, 2019 ("Rental Fee Repayment Agreement"). It is stated in the Rental Fee Repayment Agreement that Mrs. Henny will return the rental fee once Mrs. Henny's villa in Bali has been sold. However, to date, PPG has not received the rental fee because Mrs. Henny's villa in Bali has not been sold.

PPG telah menandatangani Perjanjian Sewa Menyewa dengan Ibu Henny untuk sewa gudang sebagaimana tercantum di dalam Akta No. 1 tanggal 1 Oktober 2015 ("Perjanjian Sewa"). Berdasarkan Perjanjian Sewa, PPG diharuskan membayar sewa untuk masa sewa lima tahun pertama sebesar Rp. 1.775.000.000,-. Kewajiban ini sudah dilaksanakan oleh PPG, namun dalam pelaksanaannya ditemukan IMB Gudang yang tidak sesuai dengan peruntukannya, sehingga Gudang tersebut tidak dapat digunakan oleh PPG. PPG telah meminta kepada Ibu Henny untuk mengembalikan biaya sewa yang telah dibayarkan oleh PPG, yakni sebesar Rp. 1.175.000.000. Jumlah ini telah disepakati oleh PPG dan Ibu Henny sebagaimana tercantum di dalam Perjanjian Pengembalian Uang Sewa tanggal 2 April 2019 ("Perjanjian Pembayaran Kembali Biaya Sewa"). Dalam Perjanjian Pembayaran Kembali Biaya Sewa disebutkan bahwa Ibu Henny akan mengembalikan biaya sewa tersebut setelah vila Ibu Henny di Bali telah terjual. Namun PPG belum menerima biaya sewa hingga saat ini karena vila Ibu Henny di Bali belum terjual.

Risks Faced by the Company and Their Impacts on the Company
Risiko yang Dihadapi dan Pengaruhnya terhadap Perseroan

PPG will only get a full refund of its claim in the amount of IDR1,175,000,000 if the Villa in Bali has been sold.

PPG hanya akan memperoleh pengembalian tagihan secara penuh yang jumlahnya sebesar Rp. 1.175.000.000 jika Villa di Bali sudah terjual.

Follow-up
Tindak Lanjut

Yogyakarta High Court Decision No 71/PDT/2022/PT.Yyk. dated September 22, 2022, which has permanent legal force, has decided, among other things, to sentence Mrs. Henny to pay a refund of the rent amounting to IDR 1,175,000,000 to PPG and stated that the security confiscation of land and villa buildings in Bali was valid and valuable based on Certificate of Ownership No. 2309 ("Villa") ("High Court Decision"). Based on the High Court Decision, PPG has submitted an auction for the villa. However, Ms. Henny submitted a Judicial Review of the High Court Decision and therefore, PPG has also submitted a Contra Memorandum of Judicial Review on December 18, 2023.

Putusan Pengadilan Tinggi Yogyakarta No 71/PDT/2022/PT.Yyk. tanggal 22 September 2022 yang telah berkekuatan hukum tetap, telah memutuskan antara lain menghukum Ibu Henny untuk membayar pengembalian biaya uang sewa sebesar Rp. 1.175.000.000 kepada PPG dan menyatakan sah serta berharga sita jaminan terhadap tanah dan bangunan vila di Bali berdasarkan Sertifikat Hak Milik No. 2309 ("Vila") ("Putusan PT"). Atas Putusan PT tersebut, PPG telah mengajukan lelang atas Vila tersebut. Namun demikian, Ibu Henny mengajukan Peninjauan Kembali atas Putusan PT dan oleh karenanya, PPG juga telah mengajukan Kontra Memori Peninjauan Kembali pada tanggal 18 Desember 2023.
