# Duties and Authorities of the Board of Directors
## Tugas dan Wewenang Direksi

The implementation of the duties and authorities of the Board of Directors are subject to the provisions stipulated in the Articles of Association and the prevailing laws and regulations.

The Board of Directors has the duties and responsibilities in accordance with the Articles of Association of the Company as follows:

01 The Board of Directors is in charge of carrying out and being responsible for the management of the Company in all aspects, and for the best interests of the Company in accordance with the purposes and objectives of the Company as stipulated in the Articles of Association. In carrying out the duties and responsibilities of managing the Company, the Board of Directors is supervised by the Board of Commissioners.

02 In carrying out the duties and responsibilities for management, the Board of Directors is required to hold an Annual GMS and other GMS as stipulated in the laws and regulations and the Articles of Association and submit the accountability report on its management to the Shareholders through such GMS.

03 Each member of the Board of Directors must carry out their duties and responsibilities in good faith, full of responsibility and prudence.

04 The Board of Directors is obliged to secure, manage and strive to improve the Company's valuable assets.

Pelaksanaan tugas dan wewenang Direksi tunduk pada ketentuan yang diatur dalam Anggaran Dasar dan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku.

Direksi memiliki tugas dan wewenang Direksi sesuai Anggaran Dasar Perseroan yakni sebagai berikut:

01 Direksi bertugas menjalankan dan bertanggung jawab atas pengurusan Perseroan di segala aspek, dan untuk kepentingan terbaik Perseroan sesuai dengan maksud dan tujuan Perseroan yang ditetapkan dalam Anggaran Dasar. Dalam menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawab atas pengurusan Perseroan, Direksi diawasi oleh Dewan Komisaris.

02 Direksi wajib menyelenggarakan RUPS Tahunan dan RUPS lainnya sebagaimana diatur dalam peraturan perundang-undangan dan Anggaran Dasar, dan menyampaikan laporan pertanggungjawaban atas pengurusannya kepada para Pemegang Saham melalui RUPS tersebut.

03 Setiap anggota Direksi wajib melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya dengan itikad baik, penuh tanggung jawab, dan kehati-hatian.

04 Direksi wajib mengamankan, mengelola, dan berupaya meningkatkan aset-aset berharga Perseroan.
