# Assessment of the Performance of the Board of Commissioners

Penilaian terhadap Kinerja Dewan Komisaris

Referring to the Board of Commissioners Charter, the evaluation of the performance of the members of the Board of Commissioners is carried out by the Company's shareholders. The Company also has a Policy on Performance Appraisal of the Board of Commissioners, which this policy is prepared as a guideline that can be used to assess the effectiveness of the implementation of the duties and responsibilities of the Board of Commissioners and also as a form of accountability for the assessment of the performance of the Board of Commissioners of the Company.

The Company's shareholders periodically evaluate the performance of the Board of Commissioners at least once a year. Performance appraisal is carried out on the implementation of the duties and responsibilities of the Board of Commissioners in supervising and managing the Company as stated in the laws and regulations and the Company's Articles of Association.

In addition, based on the Policy on Performance Appraisal of the Board of Commissioners applicable in the Company, the following are the provisions for the Board of Commissioners performance assessment:

1. Performance appraisal of the Board of Commissioners is performed using self-assessment every year collegially.
2. Performance appraisal of each member of the Board of Commissioners is performed by the President Commissioner every year based on the role and responsibilities of the Board of Commissioners.
3. Each member of the Board of Commissioners conducts a self-assessment, then the results are consolidated for joint review by all members of the Board of Commissioners in order to determine the effectiveness of the Board of Commissioners and the areas that need improvement.
4. The Board of Commissioners prepares the corrective actions, and the President Commissioner ensures the implementation of the corrective actions.
5. Performance appraisal of the Board of Commissioners is conducted by considering several components, which include the structure and composition of the Board of Commissioners, strategy implementation and company management, the effectiveness of the committee, risk management implementation and internal control, and the implementation of Good Corporate Governance.

Mengacu pada Piagam Dewan Komisaris (Board of Commissioners Charter), penilaian atas kinerja anggota Dewan Komisaris dilakukan oleh pemegang saham Perseroan. Perseroan juga telah memiliki Kebijakan Penilaian atas Kinerja Dewan Komisaris, yang mana kebijakan tersebut disusun sebagai pedoman yang dapat digunakan untuk menilai efektivitas pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab Dewan Komisaris dan juga sebagai bentuk akuntabilitas atas penilaian kinerja Dewan Komisaris Perseroan.

Pemegang saham Perseroan secara berkala melakukan evaluasi atas kinerja Dewan Komisaris setidaknya satu kali dalam setahun. Penilaian kinerja dilakukan terhadap pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab Dewan Komisaris dalam mengawasi dan mengelola Perseroan sebagaimana yang tercantum dalam peraturan perundang-undangan dan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan.

Di samping itu, berdasarkan Kebijakan Penilaian atas Kinerja Dewan Komisaris yang berlaku di Perseroan, maka di bawah ini merupakan ketentuan atas penilaian kinerja Dewan Komisaris:

1. Penilaian Kinerja Dewan Komisaris dilakukan dengan metode self-assessment setiap tahun secara kolegial.
2. Penilaian kinerja Anggota Dewan Komisaris dilakukan oleh Komisaris Utama setiap tahun berdasarkan tugas dan tanggung jawab Dewan Komisaris.
3. Masing-masing anggota Dewan Komisaris melakukan self-assessment, kemudian hasilnya dikonsolidasi untuk ditinjau Bersama oleh seluruh anggota Dewan Komisaris guna menetapkan efektivitas dari Dewan Komisaris beserta area-area yang perlu dilakukan perbaikan.
4. Dewan Komisaris menyusun rencana tindak lanjut perbaikan dan Komisaris Utama memastikan pelaksanaan rencana perbaikan tersebut.
5. Penilaian kinerja Dewan Komisaris dilakukan dengan mempertimbangkan beberapa komponen, antara lain: struktur dan komposisi Dewan Komisaris, pelaksanaan strategi dan pengelolaan perusahaan, efektivitas pelaksanaan program kerja komite, penerapan manajemen risiko dan pengendalian internal, serta penerapan prinsip Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik.
