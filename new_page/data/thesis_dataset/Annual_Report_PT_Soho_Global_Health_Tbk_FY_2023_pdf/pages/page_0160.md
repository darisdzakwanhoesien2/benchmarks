# Meeting Frequency and Meeting Attendance

Frekuensi Rapat dan Kehadiran Rapat

The Board of Commissioners has held internal meetings and joint meetings, which involve the Board of Directors as part of the implementation of its supervisory duties. The implementation of meetings of the Board of Commissioners has taken into account the provisions as regulated in the Company's Articles of Association and applicable OJK regulations.

During the year 2023, the Board of Commissioners has held the following meetings:

- Two (2) times the Board of Commissioners meetings,
- Four (4) times Joint Meetings between the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors, and
- Four (4) times Joint Meetings between Board of Commissioners and Committees,
