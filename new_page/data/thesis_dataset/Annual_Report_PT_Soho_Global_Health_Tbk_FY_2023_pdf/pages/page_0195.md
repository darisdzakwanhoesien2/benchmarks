Internal Audit Unit Reporting
Laporan Unit Audit Internal

Throughout the year 2023, the Internal Audit Unit carried out general audits based on the annual audit work plan submitted and approved by the Board of Directors. The Internal Audit Unit also conducted special (ad-hoc) audits based on the results of discussions or requests from the management and/or business units.

The audit plan was prepared using a risk-based approach, where the audit focus is made on areas with high-risk exposure. Further, the audit plan was continuously adjusted based on the changing audit environment and conditions.

Audit results were reported monthly by the Internal Audit Unit to the Board of Directors and the Audit Committee. A summary of audit results and highlights was reported and presented during quarterly meetings with the Board of Commissioners and Audit Committee.

Sepanjang tahun 2023, Unit Audit Internal melakukan audit umum berdasarkan rencana kerja audit tahunan yang disampaikan ke dan disetujui oleh Direksi. Unit Audit Internal juga melakukan audit khusus (ad-hoc) berdasarkan hasil pembahasan atau permintaan dari manajemen dan/atau unit bisnis.

Rencana audit disusun dengan menggunakan pendekatan berbasis risiko, di mana fokus audit dibuat untuk area dengan eksposur risiko yang tinggi. Selanjutnya, rencana audit terus disesuaikan dengan perubahan lingkungan dan kondisi audit.

Hasil audit dilaporkan setiap bulannya oleh Unit Audit Internal kepada Direksi dan Komite Audit. Ringkasan hasil audit dan sorotan telah dilaporkan dan dipresentasikan dalam rapat triwulanan dengan Dewan Komisaris dan Komite Audit.

# Qualification or Certification as an Internal Audit Professional
Kualifikasi atau Sertifikasi sebagai Profesi Audit Internal

The Head of the Internal Audit Unit is a Certified Public Accountant and a Certified Indonesia Internal Audit Practitioner (IIAP). His certification as Public Accountant was obtained from and issued by the Board of Accountancy in the Philippines. Whilst his certification as Indonesia Internal Audit Practitioner (IIAP) was obtained from and issued by the Institute of Internal Auditors in Indonesia.

He is also a member of the Institute of Internal Auditors and the Association of Certified Fraud Examiners.
