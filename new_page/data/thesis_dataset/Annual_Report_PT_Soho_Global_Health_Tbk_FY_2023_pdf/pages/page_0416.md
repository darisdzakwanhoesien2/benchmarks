## 34. PERISTIWA SETELAH PERIODE PELAPORAN

**Parit Padang Global ("PPG")**

Pada tanggal 18 Maret 2024, PPG telah melakukan pembelian Obligasi FR0081 dari PT Bank Central Asia Tbk ("BCA") yang belaku efektif tanggal 20 Maret 2024 hingga 15 Juni 2025.

Berdasarkan Surat Konfirmasi Transaksi dari BCA tanggal 18 Maret 2024, obligasi memiliki nilai nominal sebesar Rp100.000 dengan harga Rp100.350 (100,35%) dan kupon berjalan sebesar Rp1.705. Pada saat jatuh tempo PPG akan menerima imbal hasil sebesar 6,19%.

## 34. EVENT AFTER THE REPORTING PERIOD

**Parit Padang Global ("PPG")**

On March 18, 2024, PPG has made a purchase of Bond FR0081 from PT Bank Central Asia Tbk ("BCA") which is effective from March 20, 2024 to June 25, 2025.

Based on Transaction Confirmation Letter from BCA dated March 18 2024, bond has nominal value Rp100,000 with price Rp100,350 (100.35%) and accrued interest amounting Rp1,705. On the maturity date PPG will receive return amounting 6.19%.
