# HUMAN RESOURCE

Since our success to a large extent is dependent on our people we need to make sure that we attract, recruit, develop and retain excellent people. We put a lot of effort in developing our employees and aim to be a leader in Human Resource Development. A safe workplace is more than just policies and standards, it requires active participation. To create a safe working environment requires the uncompromising commitment of all of our people to demonstrate leadership in safety.
