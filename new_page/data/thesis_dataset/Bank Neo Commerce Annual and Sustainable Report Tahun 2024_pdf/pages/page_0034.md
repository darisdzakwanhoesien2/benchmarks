# Pengawasan terhadap Perumusan dan Implementasi Strategi dan Kebijakan Bank

Supervision of the Formulation and Implementation of the Bank's Strategies and Policies

Terdapat batasan dari tanggung jawab dan peran yang dijalankan baik oleh Dewan Komisaris maupun Direksi. Direksi berperan mengelola operasional dan bisnis Perseroan, sedangkan Dewan Komisaris bertugas melakukan pengawasan atas jalannya pengurusan oleh Direksi.

Dewan Komisaris juga senantiasa mengoptimalkan fungsi pengawasannya yang dilakukan melalui mekanisme rapat Dewan Komisaris, rapat Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, rapat komite-komite di bawah Dewan Komisaris; pemberian arahan, pendapat atau keputusan Dewan Komisaris; serta pelaksanaan kunjungan kerja Dewan Komisaris termasuk untuk memastikan terselenggaranya penerapan prinsip dan praktik GCG pada seluruh tingkatan dalam Perseroan.

Dalam menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawab pengawasan khususnya atas pengelolaan Perseroan, dilakukan selaras dengan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan dan perundang-undangan yang berlaku, termasuk pengawasan terhadap perumusan dan penerapan strategi serta kebijakan Perseroan.

Secara berkelanjutan, Dewan Komisaris senantiasa memperhatikan serta fokus mengawasi perumusan dan penerapan strategi dan kebijakan Perseroan sepanjang tahun 2024, dalam rangka menghadapi tantangan ekonomi serta tetap membawa Perseroan mencapai kinerja yang baik. Pengawasan dilakukan terutama namun tidak terbatas pada evaluasi terhadap Corporate Action Perseroan dan Rencana Bisnis Bank (RBB), penunjukan Auditor Eksternal, melakukan fungsi pengawasan terhadap pelaksanaan pengendalian internal di Perseroan. Dalam merancang dan merumuskan RBB, Dewan Komisaris berperan untuk memastikan bahwa aspirasi pemangku kepentingan, telah terserap dengan baik.

Lebih lanjut, Dewan Komisaris juga melakukan evaluasi atas kinerja keuangan dan operasional bisnis Bank, pengembangan kapasitas dan kapabilitas bisnis digital dan keamanan siber, pengembangan sumber daya manusia, penerapan teknologi digital perbankan, perbaikan kualitas aset dengan strategi perkreditan yang mengedepankan prinsip kehati-hatian, penghimpunan data pihak ketiga, produktivitas dan efisiensi serta pengawasan terhadap penerapan sistem manajemen risiko.

Dewan Komisaris mengapresiasi inisiatif dan kebijakan strategis yang telah dilakukan Direksi selama tahun 2024.

There are limits to the responsibilities and roles performed by both the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors. The Board of Directors plays a role in managing the Company's operations and business, while the Board of Commissioners is tasked with supervising the management by the Board of Directors.

The Board of Commissioners continues to optimize its supervisory function through various mechanisms, including Board of Commissioners meetings, joint meetings with the Board of Directors, meetings of committees under the Board of Commissioners, the provision of guidance, opinions, or decisions, and site visits. These efforts are also aimed at ensuring the implementation of Good Corporate Governance (GCG) principles at all levels of the Company.

In carrying out its supervisory duties and responsibilities, particularly over the management of the Company, the Board of Commissioners ensures alignment with the Company's Articles of Association and prevailing laws and regulations. This includes supervision of the formulation and implementation of the Company's strategies and policies.

Throughout 2024, the Board of Commissioners consistently paid close attention to and supervised the development and implementation of the Company's strategies and policies. This was done in response to economic challenges and to support the Company in achieving strong performance. The supervision focused, among others, on evaluating the Company's Corporate Actions and Bank Business Plan (RBB), the appointment of the External Auditor, and the execution of internal control functions. In designing and formulating the RBB, the Board of Commissioners plays a role in ensuring that the aspirations of stakeholders have been properly absorbed.

Furthermore, the Board also evaluated the Bank's financial and business performance, the development of digital business capacity and cybersecurity, human resource development, the adoption of digital banking technology, asset quality improvement through prudent credit strategies, third-party data collection, productivity and efficiency, and the implementation of risk management systems.

The Board of Commissioners appreciates the initiatives and strategic policies implemented by the Board of Directors throughout 2024.
