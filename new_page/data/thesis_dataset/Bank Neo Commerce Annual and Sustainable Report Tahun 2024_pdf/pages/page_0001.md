# Tentang Laporan Tahunan 2024
## About Annual Report 2024

Selamat datang pada Laporan Tahunan PT Bank Neo Commerce Tbk tahun buku 2024 dengan tema "Synergizing Strength for Common Growth". Tema tersebut dipilih berdasarkan analisis dan kajian yang mendalam berdasarkan fakta dan perkembangan bisnis sepanjang 2024 serta masa depan keberlanjutan bisnis PT Bank Neo Commerce Tbk.

Laporan Tahunan PT Bank Neo Commerce Tbk diterbitkan untuk meningkatkan keterbukaan informasi di lingkup internal kepada pemegang saham, para pemangku kepentingan dan otoritas terkait. Laporan Tahunan ini menjadi sumber dokumentasi komprehensif yang berisikan informasi kinerja PT Bank Neo Commerce Tbk selama satu tahun.

Penyebutan satuan mata uang "Rupiah" atau "IDR" merujuk pada mata uang resmi Republik Indonesia, sedangkan "Dolar AS" atau "USD" merujuk pada mata uang resmi Amerika Serikat. Semua informasi keuangan disajikan dalam mata uang Rupiah sesuai dengan Standar Akuntansi Keuangan Indonesia.

Laporan Tahunan ini memuat istilah "BNC," "Bank Neo Commerce," dan "Perseroan," yang merujuk pada PT Bank Neo Commerce Tbk yang menjalankan kegiatan usaha di bidang perbankan. Adakalanya kata "Bank" juga digunakan atas dasar kemudahan untuk menyebut PT Bank Neo Commerce Tbk secara umum.

Welcome to PT Bank Neo Commerce Tbk's 2024 Annual Report, themed "Synergizing Strength for Common Growth." This theme was selected based on in-depth analysis and evaluation of facts and business developments throughout 2024, as well as the future sustainability of PT Bank Neo Commerce Tbk.

The Annual Report of PT Bank Neo Commerce Tbk is published to enhance information disclosure within the internal scope to shareholders, stakeholders, and relevant authorities. This Annual Report serves as a comprehensive source of documentation containing information on the performance of PT Bank Neo Commerce Tbk over the past year.

The currency unit of "Rupiah" or "IDR" refers to the official currency of the Republic of Indonesia, while "US Dollars" or "USD" refers to the official currency of the United States. All financial information is presented in Rupiah in accordance with Indonesian Financial Accounting Standards.

This Annual Report includes the term of "BNC," "Bank Neo Commerce," and "Company," which refers to PT Bank Neo Commerce Tbk engaged in banking sector activities. At times, the term "Bank" is also employed for convenience in referring to PT Bank Neo Commerce Tbk in general.
