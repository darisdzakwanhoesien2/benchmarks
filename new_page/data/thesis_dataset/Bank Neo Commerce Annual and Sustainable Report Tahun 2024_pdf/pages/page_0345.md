Per 31 Desember 2024 Dewan Komisaris Perseroan beranggotakan 3 (tiga) orang yang terdiri dari 1 (satu) Komisaris Utama Independen, 1 (satu) Komisaris Independen dan 1 (satu) Komisaris. Seluruh anggota Dewan Komisaris merupakan para profesional di bidangnya masing-masing dengan latar belakang pendidikan, pengalaman kerja dan keahlian yang berbeda.

As of December 31, 2024, the Board of Commissioners of the Company consists of 3 (three) members, consisting of 1 (one) Independent President Commissioner, 1 (one) Independent Commissioner, and 1 (one) Commissioner. All members of the Board of Commissioners are professionals in their respective fields, with diverse educational backgrounds, work experience, and expertise.

Komposisi Dewan Komisaris Perseroan telah memenuhi persyaratan OJK tentang Penerapan Tata Kelola Bagi Bank Umum, dan juga Peraturan Bank Indonesia tentang Pelaksanaan Good Corporate Governance Bagi Bank Umum yaitu Bank wajib memiliki anggota Dewan Komisaris dengan jumlah paling sedikit 3 (tiga) orang dan paling banyak sama dengan jumlah anggota Direksi. Ketentuan Bank Indonesia dan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan yang mengatur tentang persyaratan jumlah anggota Komisaris Independen, paling kurang 50% dari seluruh anggota Komisaris Perseroan.

The composition of the Board of Commissioners of the Company has met the requirements set by OJK regarding the Implementation of Governance for Commercial Banks, as well as Bank Indonesia Regulations on the Implementation of Good Corporate Governance for Commercial Banks, which state that the Bank must have at least 3 (three) members of the Board of Commissioners and no more than the number of members of the Board of Directors. The regulations from Bank Indonesia and the Financial Services Authority (OJK) also require that the number of Independent Commissioners be at least 50% of the total number of Commissioners in the Company.
