# Kesetaraan

## Equality

Perseroan menempatkan kesetaraan sebagai salah satu prinsip utama dalam pengelolaaan sumber daya manusia. Dalam menjalankan operasionalnya, Perseroan memberikan kesempatan yang sama bagi semua individu yang ingin berkarier tanpa memandang latar belakang suku, agama, ras, golongan, jenis kelamin, atau aspek diskriminatif lainnya. Setiap proses rekrutmen, seleksi, dan pengembangan karyawan dilakukan secara adil dan transparan, berlandaskan pada kompetensi serta kebutuhan organisasi.

Selain memastikan proses yang setara dalam rekrutmen, Perseroan juga mengimplementasikan prinsip kesetaraan dalam pemberian manfaat kepada karyawan. Beragam fasilitas, seperti remunerasi, jaminan kesehatan, pelatihan pengembangan kompetensi, sarana kantor, dan promosi jabatan, diberikan berdasarkan tanggung jawab dan kinerja masing-masing karyawan. Dengan pendekatan ini, Perseroan mendukung terciptanya lingkungan kerja yang adil dan mendorong karyawan untuk berkontribusi secara maksimal.

Kesetaraan gender juga menjadi perhatian penting di Perseroan, yang tercermin dalam distribusi posisi strategis di berbagai level organisasi. Pada tahun 2024, komposisi karyawan laki-laki tercatat sebesar 51,32%, sedangkan karyawan perempuan sebesar 48,68%. Sebagai perbandingan, pada tahun 2023, karyawan laki-laki berkontribusi sebesar 51,30%, dan karyawan perempuan mencapai 48,70%, sedangkan pada tahun 2022 karyawan laki-laki tercatat sebesar 50,59%, dan karyawan perempuan mencapai 49,41%. Data ini menunjukkan bahwa Perseroan terus berupaya menjaga keseimbangan gender dalam tenaga kerjanya, sejalan dengan komitmen untuk menciptakan keberagaman yang inklusif.

Dengan langkah ini, Perseroan berupaya membangun lingkungan kerja yang setara dan inklusif, memastikan bahwa setiap karyawan dapat berkembang sesuai potensi masing-masing dan memberikan kontribusi terbaik untuk mendukung keberlanjutan bisnis.

The Company places equality as one of the main principles in human resource management. In carrying out its operations, the Company provides equal opportunities for all individuals who want to pursue a career regardless of ethnic background, religion, race, class, gender, or other discriminatory aspects. Every employee recruitment, selection, and development process is carried out fairly and transparently, based on competency and organizational needs.

Besides ensuring an equal process in recruitment, the Company also implements the principle of equality in providing benefits to employees. Various facilities, such as remuneration, health insurance, competency development training, office facilities, and job promotions, are provided based on the responsibility and performance of each employee. With this approach, the Company supports the creation of a fair work environment and encourages employees to contribute optimally.

Gender equality is also an important concern in the Company, which is reflected in the distribution of strategic positions at various levels of the organization. In 2024, the composition of male employees was recorded at 51.32%, while female employees were 48.68%. In comparison, in 2023, male employees contributed 51.30%, and female employees reached 48.70%, while in 2022 male employees were recorded at 50.59%, and female employees reached 49.41%. This data shows that the Company continues to strive to maintain gender balance in its workforce, in line with its commitment to creating inclusive diversity.

With this step, the Company seeks to build an equal and inclusive work environment, ensuring that each employee can develop according to their respective potential and provide the best contribution to support business sustainability.
