# Informasi Buy Back Shares &amp; Obligasi, serta Penambahan Modal melalui Right Issue

## Information on Share and Bond Buybacks, and Capital Increase through Rights Issue

Pada tahun 2024, Bank telah melakukan aksi korporasi *right issue* dengan melakukan Penawaran Umum Terbatas ("PUT") kepada para pemegang saham dengan skema PMHMETD VII yang efektif per tanggal 12 Juli 2024 dengan jumlah sebanyak 1.311.666.667 (satu miliar tiga ratus sebelas juta enam ratus enam puluh enam ribu enam ratus enam puluh tujuh) saham baru atas nama dengan nilai nominal Rp100 setiap saham. Jumlah dana yang akan diterima Perseroan melalui *right issue* ini mencapai Rp393.500.000.100 (tiga ratus sembilan puluh tiga miliar lima ratus juta seratus Rupiah). Dana dari *right issue* akan dialokasikan untuk memperkuat struktur permodalan dan pertumbuhan yang berkelanjutan.

In 2024, the Bank has conducted a right issue corporate action by conducting a Limited Public Offering ("Rights Issue") to shareholders with the PMHMETD VII scheme which was effective on July 12, 2024 with a total of 1.311,666,667 (one billion three hundred eleven million six hundred sixty six thousand six hundred sixty seven) new shares with a nominal value of Rp100 per share. The total funds to be received by the Company through this rights issue will reach Rp393,500,000,100 (three hundred ninety-three billion five hundred million one hundred Rupiah). Proceeds from the rights issue will be allocated to strengthen the Company's capital structure and sustainable growth.
