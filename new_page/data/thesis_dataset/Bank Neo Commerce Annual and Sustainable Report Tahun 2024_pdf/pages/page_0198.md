Pemisahan fungsi tersebut dapat membantu memastikan sistem pengendalian internal secara menyeluruh yang dilakukan oleh Satuan Kerja Audit Intern (SKAI) sehingga dapat melaksanakan fungsinya dalam melakukan pemeriksaan atas semua transaksi, laporan-laporan, serta kinerja dari masing-masing unit kerja yang melaksanakan aktivitas operasional dan unit kerja pelaksana maupun divisi/satuan kerja yang independen terhadap unit pelaksana/ pengambil risiko, yaitu unit kerja yang membawahi fungsi Kepatuhan, fungsi Manajemen Risiko, dan Satuan Kerja Audit Intern.

Sebagai bagian dari penerapan manajemen risiko, Bank memiliki struktur organisasi yang mencakup Satuan Kerja Manajemen Risiko (SKMR) yang fungsinya dijalankan oleh Divisi Credit Risk Management and Governance dan Divisi IT &amp; Operational Risk Management serta pelaporan melalui Komite Manajemen Risiko (KMR) sebagai komite yang dijalankan oleh Direksi. Sebagai bentuk pengawasan oleh Dewan Komisaris atas risiko-risiko pada Bank, Bank telah membentuk Komite Pemantau Risiko yang telah dijalankan sesuai dengan regulasi yang berlaku. Pada tahun 2024, rapat Komite Manajemen Risiko telah dilaksanakan sebanyak 4 (empat) kali dan Komite Pemantau Risiko telah dilaksanakan rapat sebanyak 12 (dua belas) kali.

Bank juga telah menetapkan Risk Appetite Statement and Risk Tolerance yang berfungsi sebagai bagian dari penerapan Manajemen Risiko dalam pengelolaan risiko agar rencana bisnis Bank dapat tercapai dan menjaga tingkat kesehatan Bank tetap terjaga dengan baik. Risk Appetite Statement and Risk Tolerance akan menjadi pedoman pelaksanaan aktivitas Bank berdasarkan prinsip kehati-hatian dan praktek tata kelola perbankan yang sehat (Good Corporate Governance).

Separation of functions can help ensure a comprehensive internal control system carried out by the Internal Audit Function (SKAI) so that it can carry out its function in examining all transactions, reports, and performance of each work unit that carries out operational activities and implementing work units and divisions / work units that are independent of the implementing / risk taking unit, namely the work unit in charge of the Compliance function, Risk Management function, and the Internal Audit Function.

As part of the implementation of risk management, the Bank has an organizational structure that includes a Risk Management Work Unit (SKMR) whose functions are carried out by the Credit Risk Management and Governance Division and the IT &amp; Operational Risk Management Division as well as reporting through the Risk Management Committee (RMC) as a committee run by the Board of Directors. As a form of supervision by the Board of Commissioners over risks in the Bank, the Bank has established a Risk Oversight Committee which has been carried out in accordance with applicable regulations. In 2024, the Risk Management Committee meeting was held 4 (four) times and the Risk Oversight Committee met 12 (twelve) times.

The Bank has also established a Risk Appetite Statement and Risk Tolerance which serves as part of the implementation of Risk Management in managing risks so that the Bank's business plan can be achieved and maintain the Bank's soundness level is well maintained. The Risk Appetite Statement and Risk Tolerance will serve as guidelines for the implementation of the Bank's activities based on prudential principles and sound banking governance practices (Good Corporate Governance).

# Penerapan Manajemen Risiko pada 8 (Delapan) Risiko Bank

Implementation of Risk Management on 8 (Eight) Bank Risks

Perseroan telah menerapkan manajemen risiko yang mencakup pengelolaan atas 8 (delapan) jenis risiko yang telah ditetapkan sesuai dengan Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan Nomor 18/POJK.03/2016 tentang Penerapan Manajemen Risiko Bagi Bank Umum, sebagai berikut:

1. Risiko Kredit;
2. Risiko Pasar;
3. Risiko Likuiditas;
4. Risiko Operasional;
5. Risiko Hukum;
6. Risiko Strategis;
7. Risiko Kepatuhan; dan
8. Risiko Reputasi.

The Company has implemented risk management that covers the management of 8 (eight) types of risks that have been determined in accordance with the Financial Services Authority Regulation Number 18/POJK.03/2016 concerning the Implementation of Risk Management for Commercial Banks, as follows:

1. Credit Risk;
2. Market Risk;
3. Liquidity Risk;
4. Operational Risk;
5. Legal Risk;
6. Strategic Risk;
7. Compliance Risk; and
8. Reputation Risk.
