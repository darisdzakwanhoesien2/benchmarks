Based on these economic projections, in 2025, the Company will focus on quality credit distribution growth to maintain a healthy NPL ratio, expand the market, and diversify customer segments. Marketing Strategy

Ketahanan perbankan tetap terjaga baik, yang tercermin dari permodalan yang kuat dan risiko kredit yang rendah.
