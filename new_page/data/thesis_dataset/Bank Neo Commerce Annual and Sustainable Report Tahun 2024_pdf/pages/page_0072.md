# 2020

- Perseroan melakukan Penawaran Umum Terbatas III (PUT III) kepada para Pemegang Saham dalam rangka Hak Memesan Efek Terlebih Dahulu (HMETD) dan menjadikan Perseroan memperoleh status sebagai Bank Umum Kegiatan Usaha (BUKU) II.
- Perseroan berkomitmen menjadi Bank Retail berbasis Digital untuk memenuhi kebutuhan nasabah, terutama di segmen Milenial.
- Nama Perseroan diubah dari PT Bank Yudha Bhakti Tbk menjadi PT Bank Neo Commerce Tbk (BNC) untuk merepresentasikan transformasi menjadi Bank dengan layanan digital.
- The Company conducted the Limited Public Offering III (PUT III) to Shareholders through a Rights Issue (HMETD), which enabled the Company to attain the status of a Commercial Bank with Business Activities (BUKU) II.
- The Company is committed to becoming a digital-based retail bank to meet customer needs, particularly in the Millennial segment.
- The Company's name was changed from PT Bank Yudha Bhakti Tbk to PT Bank Neo Commerce Tbk (BNC) to represent its transformation into a bank with digital services.

# 2021

- Bank Neo Commerce memindahkan kantor pusatnya ke Treasury Tower, SCBD, Jakarta, sebagai langkah strategis mendukung pengembangan bisnis.
- Aplikasi Neobank diluncurkan dan berhasil menarik 13 juta pengguna hingga akhir 2021, mencerminkan keberhasilan inovasi digital Perseroan.
- Perseroan melakukan PUT IV dan PUT V dalam rangka HMETD, dalam rangka pemenuhan ketentuan modal inti minimum.
- PT Akulaku Silvrr Indonesia ditetapkan sebagai Pemegang Saham Pengendali untuk memperkuat struktur kepemilikan Bank Neo Commerce.
- Bank Neo Commerce relocated its head office to Treasury Tower, SCBD, Jakarta, as a strategic move to support business expansion.
- The Neobank application was launched and successfully attracted 13 million users by the end of 2021, reflecting the Company's success in digital innovation.
- The Company conducted the Limited Public Offerings IV (PUT IV) and Limited Public Offerings V (PUT V) through Rights Issues (HMETD) to comply with the minimum core capital requirements.
- PT Akulaku Silvrr Indonesia was appointed as the Controlling Shareholder to strengthen the ownership structure of Bank Neo Commerce.

# 2022

Bank Neo Commerce berhasil memenuhi ketentuan modal inti minimum sebesar Rp3 triliun melalui mekanisme Penambahan Modal dengan Hak Memesan Efek Terlebih Dahulu (PMHMETD) VI.

Bank Neo Commerce has successfully met the minimum core capital requirement of IDR 3 trillion through the Capital Increase with Pre-emptive Rights (PMHMETD) VI mechanism.

# 2024

Melakukan penambahan modal melalui PMHMETD VII, guna menunjang pengembangan bisnis Perseroan.

Conducting additional capital through PMHMETD VII, to support the Company's business development.
