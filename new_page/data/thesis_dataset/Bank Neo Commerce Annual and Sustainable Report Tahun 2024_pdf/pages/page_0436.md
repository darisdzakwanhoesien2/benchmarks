Perseroan juga menetapkan kewajiban untuk menandatangani "Pakta Integritas" sejak tanggal 8 Mei 2015 yang wajib dilakukan oleh segenap jajaran di lingkungan Perseroan (Direksi, Dewan Komisaris, Karyawan). Pakta Integritas menjadi sebuah perwujudan komitmen mengenai kewajiban kepatuhan terhadap pokok-pokok Kode Etik bagi para profesional/pekerja yang bekerja di bidang Perbankan.

## Sanksi Pelanggaran Kode Etik

Sanctions for Code of Ethics Violations

Perseroan telah menetapkan sanksi kepada karyawan yang melakukan pelanggaran Kode Etik. Penetapan sanksi ini telah dipahami oleh seluruh karyawan karena mekanismenya diatur dalam Pakta Integritas serta sosialisasi.

Pada tahun 2024, terdapat 36 sanksi yang diberikan. Penerapan sanksi ini merupakan bagian dari komitmen perusahaan dalam menjaga disiplin kerja serta memastikan setiap karyawan mematuhi ketentuan yang berlaku.

Furthermore, the Company has mandated the signing of an "Integrity Pact" effective from May 8, 2015, which is compulsory for all individuals within the organization (Board of Directors, Board of Commissioners, and Employees). The Integrity Pact serves as a tangible commitment to comply with the core principles of the Code of Ethics for professionals working in the banking sector.

The Company has established sanctions for employees who violate the Code of Ethics. These sanctions have been clearly communicated and are well understood by all employees, as the enforcement mechanisms are outlined in the Integrity Pact and through regular socialization efforts.

In 2024, a total of 36 sanctions were imposed. The application of these sanctions is a manifestation of the Company's commitment to maintaining workplace discipline and ensuring that every employee adheres to the applicable rules and regulations.
