## Beban Bunga

Interest Expenses

Pada tahun 2024, Beban Bunga Perseroan sebesar Rp871 miliar, mengalami penurunan sebesar Rp74 miliar atau 7,78% dibandingkan dengan tahun 2023 yang sebesar Rp945 miliar.

Beban Bunga didominasi oleh simpanan nasabah sebesar 99% dari jumlah beban bunga. Penurunan beban bunga atas Simpanan Nasabah ini sejalan dengan inisiatif penurunan suku bunga deposito yang dilakukan Bank pada tahun 2024.

The composition of Interest Income was dominated by loans, accounting for 87.07%, and marketable securities, accounting for 12.32% of the total Interest Income.

The decrease in Interest Income was in line with the decline in the loan portfolio, current accounts with Bank Indonesia, current accounts with other banks, as well as placements with Bank Indonesia and other banks.

In 2024, the Company's Interest Expense amounted to Rp871 billion, a decrease of Rp74 billion or 7.78% compared to Rp945 billion in 2023.

Interest Expense was dominated by deposits from customers by 99% from total Interest Expense. The decrease in interest expense on deposits from customers was in line with the Bank's initiative to lower deposit interest rates in 2024.
