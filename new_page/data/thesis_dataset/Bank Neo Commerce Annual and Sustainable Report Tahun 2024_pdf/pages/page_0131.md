# Rencana Pengembangan TI Tahun 2025

IT Development Plan for 2025

Perseroan memahami pentingnya peran teknologi informasi (TI) dalam mendukung perkembangan bisnis jangka panjang, pencapaian target strategis, serta pemberian layanan optimal kepada nasabah. Berdasarkan hal tersebut, Direktorat TI telah menyusun Rencana Pengembangan Teknologi Informasi (RPTI) Tahun 2025. RPTI ini diselaraskan dengan Rencana Bisnis Bank (RBB) untuk memastikan peran TI dapat mendukung tercapainya target bisnis Perseroan.

RPTI bertujuan untuk mendukung transformasi digital secara berkesinambungan, sejalan dengan komitmen Perseroan dalam pengembangan sumber daya manusia (SDM), peningkatan teknologi informasi, serta penguatan pengendalian terhadap ketahanan siber (cyber security). Perseroan terus mengembangkan TI dari sisi infrastruktur maupun aplikasi untuk meningkatkan kualitas layanan, kenyamanan, dan keamanan dalam transaksi keuangan nasabah.

Berikut langkah-langkah strategis pengembangan TI yang tertuang dalam RPTI Tahun 2025:

1. Pengembangan core banking.
2. Pengembangan mobile banking.
3. Pengembangan corporate internet banking.
4. Pengembangan surrounding application &amp; tools.
5. Pengembangan payment system.
6. Pengembangan Big Data &amp; Analytics.
7. Pengembangan open-API.
8. Pengembangan Reporting System.
9. Peningkatan kapasitas infrastruktur dan Jaringan Komunikasi.
10. Sistem Keamanan TI dan Siber.

The Company understands the importance of the role of information technology (IT) in supporting long-term business development, achieving strategic targets, and providing optimal service to customers. Based on this, the IT Directorate has prepared the 2025 Information Technology Development Plan (RPTI). This RPTI is aligned with the Bank Business Plan (RBB) to ensure that the role of IT can support the achievement of the Company's business targets.

RPTI aims to support continuous digital transformation, in line with the Company's commitment to developing human resources (HR), improving information technology, and strengthening control over cyber security. The Company continues to develop IT from the infrastructure and application side to improve the quality of service, convenience, and security in customer financial transactions.

These are the strategic steps for IT development contained in the RPTI Year 2025:

1. Development of core banking.
2. Development of mobile banking.
3. Development of corporate internet banking.
4. Development of surrounding application &amp; tools.
5. Payment system development.
6. Big data &amp; analytics development.
7. Open-API development.
8. Reporting System Development.
9. Improvement of infrastructure capacity and Communication Network.
10. IT and Cyber Security System.
