|  Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan Nomor 13 Tahun 2024 tentang Transparansi dan Publikasi Suku Bunga Dasar Kredit bagi Bank Umum Konvensional (POJK SBDK BUK). Financial Services Authority Regulation Number 13 of 2024 on Transparency and Publication of the Basic Interest Rate for Conventional Commercial Banks (POJK SBDK BUK) | POJK ini berlaku bagi seluruh bank umum konvensional termasuk kantor cabang bank yang berkedudukan diluar negeri. Bank wajib:
a. Mengumumkan Laporan Publikasi SBDK terkini pada situs web BUK dan setiap kantor BUK dan dilakukan setiap adanya perubahan, berlaku sejak 1 Oktober 2024.
b. Menyampaikan Laporan Rincian SBDK kepada OJK secara bulanan dan dilaporkan paling lambat 7 November 2024.
c. Bagi BUK yang memiliki kanal digital dan/atau media elektronik lainnya, wajib mengumumkan Laporan Publikasi SBDK terkini dimedia tersebut.
d. Wajib memelihara pengumuman Laporan Publikasi SBDK di web paling sedikit untuk 5 (lima) tahun terakhir
This regulation applies to all conventional commercial banks, including branch offices of banks established abroad. Banks are required to:
a. Publish the latest SBDK Publication Report on the bank's website and each bank branch, and do so whenever there is a change, effective from October 1, 2024.
b. Submit the detailed SBDK Report to OJK on a monthly basis, with the first report due no later than November 7, 2024.
c. For banks that have digital channels and/or other electronic media, they must also publish the latest SBDK Publication Report on those media.
d. Banks must maintain the SBDK Publication Report on their website for at least the last 5 (five) years. | Menyesuaikan komponen Laporan Publikasi SBDK dan Laporan Rincian SBDK sesuai POJK
Penyesuaian pada ketentuan internal terkait Laporan SBDK.
Adjusting the components of the SBDK Publication Report and the Detailed SBDK Report in accordance with the POJK
Adjusting internal provisions related to the SBDK Report.  |
