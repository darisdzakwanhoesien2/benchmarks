Fungsi pengawasan Dewan Komisaris terhadap Direksi dilakukan dalam hubungan kerja yang saling menghormati. Sepanjang 2024 telah dilakukan rapat-rapat dengan Direksi mendiskusikan berbagai hal sehubungan dengan bisnis operasional Bank dan rapat Komite dibawah Dewan Komisaris untuk membantu Dewan Komisaris dalam fungsi pengawasan dan memberikan saran kepada Direksi khususnya pada aspek keuangan, manajemen risiko, dan tata kelola perusahaan.

Dalam rapat tersebut ada kalanya melibatkan pejabat Bank yang terkait dengan materi rapat. Dewan Komisaris dibantu oleh Komite dibawah Dewan Komisaris juga melakukan pengawasan langsung secara berkala demi memastikan kecukupan pengawasan serta meninjau kesesuaian proses dan hasil kerja dengan kualifikasi yang ditetapkan. Tidak hanya dalam forum rapat, fungsi pengawasan juga dilakukan dengan komunikasi dengan Direksi yang terbuka dan juga dengan Pejabat-pejabat Eksekutif terkait dari waktu ke waktu mengingat bisnis Perseroan yang sangat dinamis.

Adapun ruang lingkup laporan pengawasan Dewan Komisaris meliputi penilaian termasuk namun tidak terbatas pada:

1. Pelaksanaan Rencana Bisnis berupa penilaian aspek kuantitatif maupun kualitatif terhadap realisasi Rencana Bisnis termasuk Rencana Aksi Keuangan Berkelanjutan;
2. Faktor-faktor yang mempengaruhi kinerja secara umum, khususnya terkait faktor permodalan (capital), rentabilitas (earnings), profil risiko terutama risiko kredit, risiko pasar, risiko likuiditas, dan tata kelola yang baik; dan
3. Upaya memperbaiki kinerja Bank, dalam hal kinerjanya terdapat kemungkinan penurunan.

The supervisory role of the Board of Commissioners over the Board of Directors is conducted within a mutually respectful working relationship. Throughout 2024, joint meetings were held between the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors to discuss various matters related to the Bank's operational activities. Committee meetings under the Board of Commissioners were also conducted to support the Board in its supervisory role and to provide recommendations to the Board of Directors, particularly in areas of finance, risk management, and corporate governance.

These meetings sometimes involved relevant Bank officials depending on the subject matter discussed. The Board of Commissioners, with the assistance of its supporting Committees, also carried out regular direct supervision to ensure sufficient oversight and to review the alignment of processes and outcomes with the established standards. In addition to formal meetings, the supervisory function is also performed through open communication with the Board of Directors and relevant Executive Officers from time to time, given the dynamic nature of the Company's business.

The scope of the Board of Commissioners' supervisory report includes assessments of, but is not limited to:

1. Implementation of the Business Plan, including both quantitative and qualitative evaluations of its realization, as well as the Sustainable Finance Action Plan;
2. Factors affecting overall performance, particularly those related to capital, earnings, and risk profile—especially credit risk, market risk, and liquidity risk—and good governance practices; and
3. Efforts to improve the Bank's performance, especially in the event of a potential performance decline.

# Penilaian Dewan Komisaris terhadap kondisi perekonomian terkini

The Board of Commissioners' Assessment of the Current Economic Conditions

Kondisi perekonomian global pada tahun 2024 ditandai dengan volatilitas pasar keuangan ditengah berbagai gejolak geopolitik, seperti konflik dibeberapa kawasan dan terpilihnya pemimpin negara yang membawa haluan baru. Di sisi lain pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia tahun 2024 terbilang cukup resilien, yakni sebesar 5,03%, sedikit melambat dibandingkan tahun 2023 yang mencapai 5,05%, namun tetap di atas rata-rata pertumbuhan ekonomi global.

Bank Indonesia (Bi) mencatat pertumbuhan kredit perbankan di sepanjang 2024 sebesar 10,39 persen secara tahunan (yoy) atau berada dalam kisaran prokiraan sebesar 10-12 persen. Pertumbuhan kredit dipengaruhi oleh terjaganya minat penyaluran kredit perbankan, berlanjutnya realokasi alat likuid ke kredit oleh perbankan, tersedianya dukungan pendanaan dari pertumbuhan DPK, serta positifnya dampak Likuiditas Makroprudensial ("KLM") Bank Indonesia.

The global economic situation in 2024 has been marked by financial market volatility amid various geopolitical tensions, including conflicts in several regions and the election of new national leaders with different policy directions. On the other hand, Indonesia's economic growth in 2024 remained relatively resilient at 5.03%, slightly lower than the 5.05% recorded in 2023, yet still above the global average.

Bank Indonesia (Bi) reported that bank credit growth in 2024 reached 10.39% year-on-year (YoY), within the forecast range of 10-12%. This growth was supported by continued lending interest from banks, the reallocation of liquid assets to loans, increased funding from deposit growth, and the positive impact of Bank Indonesia's Macroprudential Liquidity Policy (KLM).
