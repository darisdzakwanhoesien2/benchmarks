# Satuan Kerja Audit Intern (SKAI)

Internal Audit Function (SKAI)

Satuan Kerja Audit Intern (SKAI) dibentuk untuk meningkatkan dan melindungi Bank melalui pemberian jasa assurance dan konsultasi yang berbasis risiko dan objektif, serta berperan sebagai mitra strategis manajemen dalam usaha mencapai sasaran dan tujuan dan membantu terciptanya tata kelola yang baik. SKAI sebagai lini ketiga dalam Three Lines Model, secara periodik melakukan komunikasi dan kolaborasi dengan fungsi lini lainnya serta saling berkontribusi dalam menciptakan dan menjaga nilai yang selaras dengan kepentingan stakeholders, melalui pelaksanaan fungsi assurance dan konsultasi.

Pelaksanaan fungsi assurance mencakup pelaksanaan audit berbasis risiko (Risk Based Audit) dan untuk pelaksanaan fungsi konsultasi dilakukan secara formal maupun informal yang berupa penugasan khusus atas permintaan manajemen. SKAI terus melakukan penyempurnaan metodologi audit dan inovasi dalam penggunaan teknologi agar pelaksanaan assurance dan konsultasi selaras dengan perkembangan bisnis, sehingga dapat mendorong perbaikan kualitas dan efektivitas tata kelola, pengelolaan risiko serta kecukupan dan efektivitas pengendalian internal.

Internal Audit Function (SKAI) was established to enhance and protect the Bank through the provision of risk-based and objective assurance and consulting services, as well as to act as a strategic partner of management in achieving goals and objectives and to assist in the creation of good governance. SKAI as the third line in the Three Lines Model, periodically communicates and collaborates with other line functions and contributes to creating and maintaining value that is aligned with the interests of stakeholders, through the implementation of assurance and consulting functions.

The implementation of the assurance function includes the implementation of risk-based audits and the implementation of consulting functions is carried out formally and informally in the form of special assignments at the request of management. SKAI continues to improve audit methodologies and innovations in the use of technology so that the implementation of assurance and consulting is in line with business developments, so as to encourage improvements in the quality and effectiveness of governance, risk management and the adequacy and effectiveness of internal control.

# Piagam Audit Intern

Internal Audit Charter

Audit Intern memiliki Piagam yang ditinjau dan diperbarui secara berkala oleh Perseroan. Piagam Audit Intern disahkan oleh Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris yang terakhir kali diperbarui pada tanggal 8 Desember 2023. Piagam Audit Intern disusun berdasarkan POJK No. 1/POJK.03/2019 tentang Penerapan Fungsi Audit Intern pada Bank Umum dan Global Internal Audit Standards.

Internal Audit has a Charter that is regularly reviewed and updated by the Company. The Internal Audit Charter was approved by the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners and was last updated on December 8, 2023. The Internal Audit Charter was prepared based on POJK No. 1/POJK.03/2019 concerning the Implementation of Internal Audit Function in Commercial Banks and Global Internal Audit Standards.

# Kedudukan dan Struktur SKAI

Position and Structure of SKAI

SKAI bertanggung jawab langsung kepada Direktur Utama serta dapat berkomunikasi secara langsung dengan Dewan Komisaris melalui Komite Audit. SKAI memiliki 3 (tiga) Departemen yang disesuaikan dengan strategi bisnis Perseroan, yaitu General Audit Department, IT Audit Department dan Project &amp; Audit Methodology Department yang dipimpin oleh Head of Satuan Kerja Audit Intern. Berikut Struktur Organisasi SKAI:

SKAI is directly responsible to the President Director and can communicate directly with the Board of Commissioners through the Audit Committee. SKAI has 3 (three) Departments that are adjusted to the Company's business strategy, namely the General Audit Department, IT Audit Department and Project &amp; Audit Methodology Department led by the Head of Internal Audit Function.
