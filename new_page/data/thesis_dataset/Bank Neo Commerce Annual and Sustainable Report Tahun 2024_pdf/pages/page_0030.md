# Laporan Dewan Komisaris
## Board of Commissioners' Report

Para Pemegang Saham dan Pemangku Kepentingan yang Terhormat,

Dalam kesempatan ini, perkenankan saya mewakili Dewan Komisaris PT Bank Neo Commerce Tbk ("Perseroan" atau "BNC") menyampaikan laporan pengawasan terhadap pengelolaan usaha Perseroan untuk tahun buku 2024.

Kami memanjatkan puji dan syukur kepada Tuhan Yang Maha Esa, bahwa karena karuniaNya, kami, bersama-sama dengan dengan Direksi dan seluruh karyawan BNC (Neobankers) mampu menghadapi tahun 2024 yang penuh dengan kompleksitas dan dinamika bisnis, tantangan dan ketidakpastian disertai dengan tensi geopolitik yang belum mereda dengan baik dan meraih pencapaian kinerja yang positif.

Sepanjang tahun 2024, Dewan Komisaris senantiasa menjalankan fungsi dan tugas pengawasan kepada Direksi, termasuk melakukan pengawasan terhadap pelaksanaan Rencana Bisnis Bank (RBB) dan Rencana Aksi Keuangan Berkelanjutan (RAKB). Dewan Komisaris juga mengawasi perumusan dan implementasi strategi dan kebijakan yang dilakukan oleh Direksi guna memastikan Perseroan tetap berada di jalur yang tepat, memberikan pandangan atas prospek usaha yang disusun oleh Direksi berikut pertimbangannya serta pengawasan terdahap penerapan tata Kelola Perusahaan dan kinerja struktur organ tata kelola.

Dear Shareholders and Stakeholders,

In this opportunity, please allow me on behalf of the Board of Commissioners of PT Bank Neo Commerce Tbk (the "Company" or "BNC") to submit a supervisory report on the management of the Company's business for the financial year 2024.

We praise and thank God Almighty, that by His grace, we together with the Board of Directors and all BNC employees (Neobankers) are able to faced the year 2024 which is full of complexity and business dynamics, challenges and uncertainties along with geopolitical tensions that have not subsided well and achieve positive performance achievements.

Throughout 2024, the Board of Commissioners has consistently carried out its supervisory duties over the Board of Directors, including monitoring the implementation of the Bank's Business Plan (RBB) and the Sustainable Finance Action Plan (RAKB). The Board of Commissioners also oversees the formulation and implementation of strategies and policies by the Board of Directors to ensure that the Company remains on the right path. We provide input on business outlooks prepared by the Board of Directors, along with relevant considerations, and monitor the implementation of good corporate governance and the performance of the governance structure.

# Fungsi Pengawasan dan Hubungan Kerja dengan Direksi
The Function of Supervision and Working Relationship with the Board of Directors

Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi merupakan organ Perseroan yang dibentuk dalam rangka mematuhi ketentuan peraturan dan perundang-undangan yang berlaku. Dewan Komisaris memiliki tugas melakukan pengawasan dan pemberian nasihat kepada Direksi atas pengurusan Perseroan. Dewan Komisaris senantiasa proaktif dalam melaksanakan fungsi pengawasan Perseroan, baik pada proses perumusan rencana strategi Perseroan, penyusunan dan implementasi rencana bisnis, pemantauan kinerja, serta penerapan manajemen risiko dan tata kelola.

Pengawasan yang dilakukan Dewan Komisaris antara lain dimaksudkan agar tercipta kesesuaian dan konsistensi pelaksanaan kegiatan usaha Perseroan, dengan rencana bisnis, serta implementasi prinsip Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang baik atau Good Corporate Governance (GCG) pada seluruh unit organisasi.

The Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors are organs of the Company established in accordance with prevailing laws and regulations. The Board of Commissioners is responsible for supervising and providing advice to the Board of Directors regarding the management of the Company. The Board of Commissioners remains proactive in carrying out its supervisory role, including in the formulation of strategic plans, preparation and implementation of business plans, performance monitoring, as well as risk management and governance practices.

The supervision carried out by the Board of Commissioners is intended to ensure alignment and consistency between the Company's business activities and its business plans and to promote the implementation of Good Corporate Governance (GCG) principles across all organizational units.
