## Pengungkapan Tagihan dan Pencadangan Berdasarkan Wilayah

1. Tagihan adalah nilai aset keuangan yang tercatat dalam laporan posisi keuangan sebelum dikurangi CKPN (gross).
2. Definisi penurunan nilai dan perhitungan CKPN aset keuangan mengacu pada standar akuntansi keuangan yang berlaku.
3. Pembagian wilayah dilakukan berdasarkan kebijakan masing-masing Bank, sesuai laporan manajemen. Pembagian wilayah ditetapkan paling sedikit 3 (tiga) wilayah. Bank harus mengungkapkan dalam laporan, rincian dari masing-masing wilayah.
4. Penentuan wilayah dilakukan berdasarkan lokasi proyek dari debitur.

## Disclosure of Claims and Allowance by Region

1. Claims refer to the value of financial assets recorded in the statement of financial position before deducting allowances for impairment losses (gross).
2. The definition of impairment and the calculation of the allowance for financial assets refer to the applicable financial accounting standards.
3. Regional classification is based on the policy of each Bank, in accordance with management reports. A minimum of 3 (three) regions must be defined. The Bank must disclose in the report the details of each region.
4. The determination of regions is based on the project location of the debtor.
