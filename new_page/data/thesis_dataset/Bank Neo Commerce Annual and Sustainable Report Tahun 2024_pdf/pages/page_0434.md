# Penilaian Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi terhadap Pelaksanaan Manajemen Risiko

Board of Commissioners and Directors Assessment of Risk Management Implementation

Peran aktif Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dalam penerapan manajemen risiko dan sistem pengendalian internal sangat penting. Dilihat dari tingkat risiko usaha di tahun 2024, tingkat risiko usaha Perusahaan tergolong Moderate. Perseroan berupaya mengantisipasi risiko usaha dan menjalankan usaha di sepanjang tahun buku secara normal tanpa ada isu yang memengaruhi secara signifikan.

Berdasarkan hasil penilaian (self assessment) profil risiko Bank untuk periode penilaian yang berakhir pada 31 Desember 2024, peringkat risiko komposit Bank dinilai Moderate dengan Risiko Inheren dinilai Moderate dan kualitas penerapan manajemen risiko dinilai Fair.

The active role of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors in the implementation of risk management and internal control systems is very important. Judging from the level of business risk in 2024, the Company's business risk level is classified as Moderate. The Company seeks to anticipate business risks and conduct business throughout the financial year normally without any issues that significantly affect it.

Based on the results of the Bank's risk profile self-assessment for the assessment period ending December 31, 2024, the Bank's composite risk rating is rated Moderate with Inherent Risk rated Moderate and the quality of risk management implementation rated Fair.
