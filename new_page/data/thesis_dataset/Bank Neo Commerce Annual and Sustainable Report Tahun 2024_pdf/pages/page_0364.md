# Wewenang Direksi

Board of Directors' authority

Dalam melaksanakan tugasnya, Direksi memiliki wewenang untuk:

1. Direksi berhak mewakili Perseroan di dalam dan di luar pengadilan tentang segala hal;
2. Direktur Utama berhak dan berwenang bertindak untuk dan atas nama Direksi serta mewakili Perseroan;
3. Direktur Utama berhak menunjuk salah satu Direktur bertindak untuk dan atas nama Direksi serta mewakili Perusahaan, bila Direktur Utama sakit, berpergian atau berhalangan karena sebab lain, hal mana tidak perlu dibuktikan kepada pihak ketiga;
4. Direksi berhak dan berwenang mengikat Perseroan dengan pihak lain atau pihak lain dengan Perseroan, serta menjalankan segala tindakan baik yang mengenai kepengurusan dan/atau kepemilikan akan tetapi tidak menghilangkan ketentuan yang berlaku pada Anggaran Dasar Perseroan serta peraturan perundang-undangan; dan
5. Direksi berhak mengatur sendiri pembagian pekerjaan di antara para anggota Direksi secara bersama-sama, secara musyawarah mufakat dan asas kekhususannya.

In implementing its duties, the Board of Directors has the authority to:

1. The Board of Directors is entitled to represent the Company in and out of court on all matters;
2. The President Director is entitled and authorized to act for and on behalf of the Board of Directors and represent the Company;
3. The President Director is entitled to appoint one of the Directors to act for and on behalf of the Board of Directors and represent the Company, if the President Director is sick, traveling or absent for other reasons, which does not need to be proven to any other person;
4. The Board of Directors has the right and authority to commit the Company with other parties or other parties with the Company, as well as to carry out all actions both regarding management and/or ownership but without eliminating the applicable provisions in the Company's Articles of Association and laws and regulations; and
5. The Board of Directors has the right to regulate the division of work among the members of the Board of Directors jointly, by deliberation and consensus and the principle of specificity.

# Kewajiban Direksi

Board of Directors' Obligations

Direksi Perseroan memiliki kewajiban untuk:

1. Mengusahakan dan menjamin terlaksananya usaha dan kegiatan Perseroan sesuai dengan maksud dan tujuan serta kegiatan usahanya;
2. Menyiapkan pada waktunya Rencana Jangka Panjang Perseroan, Rencana Kerja dan Anggaran Tahunan Perseroan, dan rencana kerja lainnya serta perubahannya untuk disampaikan kepada Dewan Komisaris dan mendapatkan persetujuan Dewan Komisaris;
3. Membuat Daftar Pemegang Saham, Daftar Khusus, Risalah RUPS, dan Risalah Rapat Direksi;

The Board of Directors of the Company has the obligation to:

1. To organize and ensure the implementation of the Company's business and activities in accordance with its purposes and objectives and business activities;
2. Prepare in due time the Company's Long Term Plan, the Company's Annual Work Plan and Budget, and other work plans and amendments to be submitted to the Board of Commissioners and obtain approval from the Board of Commissioners;
3. To create a Register of Shareholders, Special Register, Minutes of the GMS, and Minutes of the Board of Directors Meeting;
