# Informasi Perubahan Komposisi Dewan Komisaris Tahun 2024

Information on Changes in the Composition of the Board of Commissioners in 2024

Di tahun 2024, terdapat perubahan pada komposisi Dewan Komisaris Perseroan. Per 31 Desember 2024 anggota Dewan Komisaris Perseroan berjumlah 3 (tiga) orang yang terdiri 1 (satu) orang Komisaris Utama Independen, 1 (satu) orang anggota Komisaris Non Independen, dan 1 (satu) orang Komisaris Independen. Seluruh anggota Dewan Komisaris merupakan para profesional dengan latar belakang pendidikan, keahlian dan pengalaman yang berbeda.

In 2024, there were changes in the composition of the Company's Board of Commissioners. As of December 31st, 2024, the Company's Board of Commissioners consist of 3 (three) members consisting of 1 (one) Independent President Commissioner, 1 (one) Non-Independent Commissioner, and 1 (one) President Commissioner (Independent). All members of the Board of Commissioners are professionals with different educational backgrounds, expertise, and experience.
