Berdasarkan strategi yang telah diterapkan, Perseroan telah menjalankan program pengelolaan dan pengembangan SDM melalui aktivitas program sebagai berikut:

1. Menyelenggarakan Town Hall secara berkala untuk memperkuat komunikasi dan sinergi internal.
2. Melaksanakan kegiatan pelatihan kepemimpinan bagi Kepala Divisi dan Kepala Cabang melalui pelatihan Objective Key Result dan Balance Scorecard.
3. Menyelenggarakan kegiatan wellness development program dalam bentuk kegiatan olahraga dan pelatihan motivasi sebagai bentuk pengembangan kompetensi non-teknis bagi seluruh karyawan.
4. Melakukan kegiatan Culture Immersive Training (pelatihan mengenai core value dari Bank Neo Commerce) yang diikuti oleh staff &amp; leader.
5. Menyelenggarakan pelatihan kemampuan menjual produk dan melayani nasabah, termasuk product knowledge, selling &amp; marketing skills, market analysis, communication skills, presentation skills, negotiation skills, dan service excellence.
6. Mengadakan pelatihan sistem dan prosedur guna meningkatkan efisiensi pelayanan dan memperkecil zero defect, mencakup pengetahuan sistem dan prosedur, kompetensi digital dan informasi produk aspek kegiatan operasional.

a. Training Anti Pencucian Uang dan Pencegahan Pendanaan Terorisme
b. Training Business Continuity Plan
c. Training Anti Fraud Awareness
d. Training Cyber Security Awareness
8. Dalam rangka peningkatan kompetensi karyawan khususnya mandatory certification dilakukan training bidang-bidang terkait:
a. Training pada Risk Management
i. Mengikutsertakan karyawan pada Sertifikasi Manajemen Risiko sesuai dengan level jabatan.
ii. Menyelenggarakan training pengembangan kompetensi, baik melalui pelatihan eksternal maupun internal.
iii. Meningkatkan kesadaran risiko (Risk Awareness) bagi seluruh karyawan.
b. Training pada Wealth Management
i. Mengikutsertakan tenaga pemasar pada sertifikasi WAPERD (Wakil Agen Penjual Efek Reksa Dana), WPPE-P (Wakil Perantara Pedagang Efek - Pemasaran), AAUI (Asosiasi Asuransi Umum Indonesia), dan AAJI (Asosiasi Asuransi Jiwa Indonesia).

Based on the strategies that have been implemented, the Company has implemented HR management and development programs through the following program activities:

1. Holding regular Town Halls to strengthen internal communication and synergy.
2. Conducting leadership training activities for Division Heads and Branch Heads through Objective Key Result and Balance Scorecard training.
3. Organizing wellness development program activities in the form of sports activities and motivational training as a form of non-technical competency development for all employees.
4. Conducting Culture Immersive Training activities (training on core values of Bank Neo Commerce) which were attended by staff &amp; leaders.
5. Organizing product sales and customer service skills training, including product knowledge, selling &amp; marketing skills, market analysis, communication skills, presentation skills, negotiation skills, and service excellence.
6. Conducting system and procedure training to improve service efficiency and reduce zero defects, including knowledge of systems and procedures, digital competencies and product information on operational aspects.
7. Organizing mandatory non-certification training which includes:
a. Anti Money Laundering and Countering Terrorism Financing Training
b. Training Business Continuity Plan
c. Training Anti Fraud Awareness
d. Training Cyber Security Awareness
8. In order to improve employee competency, especially mandatory certification, training is carried out in related fields:
a. Training on Risk Management
i. Involve employees in Risk Management Certification according to job level.
ii. Organizing competency development training, both through external and internal training.
iii. Increase risk awareness for all employees.
b. Training on Wealth Management
i. Involve marketing staff in WAPERD (Mutual Fund Securities Sales Agent Representative), WPPE-P (Securities Trader Representative - Marketing), AAUI (Indonesian General Insurance Association), and AAJI (Indonesian Life Insurance Association) certification.
