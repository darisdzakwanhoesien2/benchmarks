## Penilaian terhadap Kinerja Dewan Komisaris

Assessment of the BOC Performance

Penilaian Kinerja Dewan Komisaris dilakukan sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku dalam mekanisme Kebijakan Perseroan. Secara umum, Dewan Komisaris menyiapkan laporan pengawasan setiap tahun untuk disampaikan kepada Pemegang Saham dalam RUPS.

The performance assessment of the Board of Commissioners is conducted in accordance with the applicable provisions of the Company Policy mechanism. In general, the BOC prepares a supervisory report every year to be submitted to the Shareholders in the GMS.

Hasil penilaian terhadap kinerja Dewan Komisaris di tahun 2024 berada pada level baik. Seluruh Dewan Komisaris Perseroan dinilai kompeten dan profesional, dengan berbagai keahlian, pengalaman, dan latar belakang masing-masing anggota yang dapat mendukung pengawasan operasional Perseroan. Dewan Komisaris juga memiliki tanggung jawab dan komitmen yang kuat untuk memastikan secara berkesinambungan Perseroan beroperasional dengan mengedepankan prinsip-prinsip GCG dan juga peraturan yang berlaku di industri perbankan, pasar modal, dan regulasi lainnya yang sesuai dengan Perseroan. Dewan Komisaris juga memiliki hubungan yang solid antar anggotanya dengan mengedepankan kolaboratif, serta terbuka untuk berdiskusi demi kepentingan Perseroan.

The results of the assessment of the performance of the Board of Commissioners in 2024 were at a good level. The entire Board of Commissioners of the Company is considered competent and professional, with various expertise, experience, and background of each member that can support the supervision of the Company's operations, the Board of Commissioners also has a strong responsibility and commitment to ensure that the Company continuously operates by prioritizing GCG principles and also applicable regulations in the banking industry, capital markets, and other regulations that are in accordance with the Company. The Board of Commissioners also has a solid relationship between its members by prioritizing collaboration, and is open to discussion for the benefit of the Company.
