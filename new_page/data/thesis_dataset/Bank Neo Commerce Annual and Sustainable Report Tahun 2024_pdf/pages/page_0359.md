# Kelengkapan Organ Dewan Komisaris

Composition of the Board of Commissioners

Dalam mendukung kelancaran pelaksanaan tugasnya, Dewan Komisaris dibantu oleh Komite-komite di bawah Dewan Komisaris, yakni:

1. Komite Audit;
2. Komite Pemantau Risiko;
3. Komite Nominasi &amp; Remunerasi.

Uraian lengkap ketiga komite di atas terdapat pada bagian Organ Pendukung Dewan Komisaris pada bab ini.

Penilaian terhadap kinerja ketiga Komite dilakukan secara individual atau kolektif secara berkala menggunakan metode self-assessment berdasarkan sistem yang telah disepakati bersama antara Dewan Komisaris dan Komite.

Hasil penilaian terhadap para ketiga Komite di atas menjadi acuan bagi setiap anggota untuk memperbaiki kinerjanya atau memperpanjang masa bakti anggota untuk periode berikutnya. Kriteria penilaian terhadap anggota Komite mencakup kualitas hasil rekomendasi, rapat-rapat, keaktifan dalam berkomunikasi dan memberikan rekomendasi, serta kriteria lainnya.

Selama tahun 2024, Berdasarkan hasil self-assessment, Dewan Komisaris menilai komite-komite di bawah Dewan Komisaris cukup baik, karena telah membantu Dewan Komisaris dalam melaksanakan tugas pengawasan, dan telah menjalankan tanggung jawabnya sesuai dengan pedoman masing-masing komite.

In supporting the performance of its duties, the Board of Commissioners is assisted by Committees under the Board of Commissioners, namely:

1. Audit Committee;
2. Risk Oversight Committee;
3. Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee.

A full description of the three committees above is contained in the Supporting Organs of the Board of Commissioners section of this chapter.

Assessment of the performance of the three Committees is conducted individually or collectively on a regular basis using the self-assessment method based on a system that has been mutually agreed between the Board of Commissioners and the Committee.

The results of the assessment of the three Committees above serve as a reference for each member to improve their performance or extend their service period for the next period. Criteria for assessment of Committee members This includes the quality of recommendations, meetings, active communication and recommendations, and other criteria.

During 2024, based on the self-assessment results, the Board of Commissioners assessed the committees under the Board of Commissioners as good enough, because they have assisted the Board of Commissioners in carrying out supervisory duties, and have carried out their responsibilities in accordance with the guidelines of each committee.

# Mekanisme Pemberhentian dan Pengunduran Diri Dewan Komisaris

Mechanism of Dismissal and Resignation of the Board of Commissioners

Pemberhentian anggota Dewan Komisaris berlaku ketentuan sebagai berikut:

1. Anggota Dewan Komisaris dapat diberhentikan sewaktu-waktu berdasarkan keputusan RUPS dengan menyebutkan alasannya.
2. Keputusan untuk memberhentikan anggota Dewan Komisaris sebagaimana dimaksud poin 1 diambil setelah yang bersangkutan diberi kesempatan untuk membela diri dalam RUPS.
3. Dalam hal keputusan untuk memberhentikan anggota Dewan Komisaris sebagaimana dimaksud poin 2 di atas dilakukan dengan keputusan di luar RUPS, anggota Dewan Komisaris yang bersangkutan diberitahu terlebih dahulu tentang rencana pemberhentian dan diberikan kesempatan untuk membela diri sebelum diambil keputusan pemberhentian.
4. Pemberian kesempatan untuk membela diri sebagaimana dimaksud poin 2 di atas tidak diperlukan dalam hal yang bersangkutan tidak berkeberatan atas pemberhentian tersebut.

The following provisions apply to the dismissal of members of the Board of Commissioners:

1. A member of the BOC may be dismissed at any time based on a resolution of the GMS by stating the reasons therefore.
2. The decision to dismiss a member of the Board of Commissioners as referred to in point 1 is taken after the person concerned has been given the opportunity to defend himself/herself in the GMS.
3. In a decision to dismiss a member of the Board of Commissioners as referred to in point 2 above is made by a resolution outside the GMS, the member of the Board of Commissioners concerned shall be notified in advance of the dismissal plan and given an opportunity to defend himself/herself before the dismissal decision is made.
4. The provision of an opportunity to defend himself/herself as referred to in point 2 above shall not be required in the event that the member concerned does not object to the dismissal.
