# Frekuensi dan Tata Cara Pemberian Nasihat kepada Direksi

Frequency and Procedures for Providing Advice to the Board of Directors

Dewan Komisaris melaksanakan rapat secara berkala, sebagai sarana dalam memberikan nasihat, saran, arahan, serta rekomendasi kepada Direksi. Rapat Dewan Komisaris wajib diadakan secara berkala paling kurang 1 (satu) kali dalam 2 (dua) bulan dan/atau dapat diadakan setiap waktu bilamana dianggap perlu.

Anggaran Dasar Perseroan juga mengatur bahwa Dewan Komisaris wajib pula mengadakan Rapat Dewan Komisaris bersama dengan Direksi secara berkala paling kurang 1 (satu) kali dalam 4 (empat) bulan.

Pemberian nasihat dan saran dari Dewan Komisaris kepada Direksi dapat juga dilakukan melalui rapat komite-komite di bawah Dewan Komisaris, arahan dan pendapat tertulis, serta kunjungan kerja Dewan Komisaris.

Selain itu, pemberian nasihat juga dapat dilakukan secara non formal berdasarkan kebutuhan tertentu yang memerlukan keputusan cepat atau terdapat perubahan besar dalam kebijakan Bank, selaras dengan peraturan dan ketentuan yang berlaku.

Dewan Komisaris mengoptimalkan fungsi pengawasan dan memberikan saran yang dibutuhkan dalam pengelolaan Perseroan oleh Direksi. Seluruh rapat yang diselenggarakan telah terdokumentasikan dengan baik. Pada tahun 2024, telah diselenggarakan sebanyak 7 (tujuh) kali rapat gabungan Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, dan 9 (sembilan) kali rapat Dewan Komisaris.

The Board of Commissioners holds regular meetings as a forum to provide advice, guidance, direction, and recommendations to the Board of Directors. According to the Company's Articles of Association, meetings of the Board of Commissioners must be held at least once every two (2) months and/or at any time deemed necessary.

In addition, the Articles of Association stipulate that joint meetings between the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors must be held at least once every four (4) months.

Advice and recommendations from the Board of Commissioners to the Board of Directors may also be conveyed through committee meetings under the Board of Commissioners, written directives and opinions, as well as through site visits by the Board of Commissioners. Furthermore, informal advice may be given when needed—particularly in situations requiring prompt decisions or in response to significant changes in the Bank's policies, in line with prevailing regulations.

The Board of Commissioners continuously optimizes its supervisory function and provides the necessary advice to support the management of the Company by the Board of Directors. All meetings held are well-documented. In 2024, a total of 7 (seven) joint meetings between the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors were held, along with 9 (nine) meetings of the Board of Commissioners.

# Penilaian Komite Dibawah Dewan Komisaris

Assessment of Committees Under the Board of Commissioners

Dalam rangka membantu dan mendukung pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab Dewan Komisaris serta dalam hal mengawasi pengelolaan Perseroan oleh Direksi, Dewan Komisaris membentuk komite-komite yang terdiri dari Komite Audit, Komite Pemantau Risiko dan Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi.

Komite-Komite di bawah Dewan Komisaris tersebut berperan aktif dalam membantu dan mendukung kelancaran tugas Dewan Komisaris antara lain dengan mengadakan rapat berkala paling kurang 1 (satu) kali dalam 1 (satu) bulan untuk komite audit, 1 (satu) kali dalam 1 (satu) bulan untuk komite pemantau risiko dan 1 (satu) kali dalam 3 (tiga) bulan untuk komite nominasi dan remunerasi.

In order to support and facilitate the execution of the duties and responsibilities of the Board of Commissioners, particularly in supervising the management of the Company by the Board of Directors, the Board of Commissioners has established several committees: the Audit Committee, the Risk Oversight Committee, and the Nomination and Remuneration Committee.

These committees play an active role in assisting and supporting the Board of Commissioners by conducting regular meetings—at least once a month for the Audit Committee, once a month for the Risk Oversight Committee, and once every three months for the Nomination and Remuneration Committee.
