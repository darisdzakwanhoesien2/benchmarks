# Komite Pengarah Teknologi Informasi

Information Technology Steering Committee (ITCS)

Komite Pengarah Teknologi Informasi dibentuk Direksi dengan tujuan membantu memberikan rekomendasi kepada Direksi terkait dengan penetapan Rencana Strategis Teknologi Informasi (RSTI), kebijakan dan prosedur penggunaan Teknologi Informasi, serta terkait hal TI lainnya.

Penilaian terhadap kinerja Divisi dan Komite dilakukan baik secara individual atau kolektif secara berkala. Penilaian dilakukan secara self-assessment berdasarkan sistem yang telah disepakati bersama.

The Information Technology Steering Committee was established by the Board of Directors with the purpose of assisting in providing recommendations to the Board of Directors related to the establishment of the Information Technology Strategic Plan (RSTI), policies and procedures for the use of Information Technology, as well as other IT related matters.

Assessment of Division and Committee performance is conducted either individually or collectively on a regular basis. The assessment is conducted by self-assessment based on a mutually agreed system.
