In every agenda point of the Meeting, the shareholders and/or their proxies are given the opportunity to raise questions and/or give opinions related to the agenda point of the Meeting being discussed.  |
|  Mekanisme pengambilan keputusan Rapat Mechanism of Meeting Decision-Making | Semua keputusan diambil dengan cara musyawarah untuk mufakat. Apabila musyawarah mufakat tidak tercapai, maka dilakukan pemungutan suara dengan cara menghitung jumlah saham yang setuju, abstain maupun yang tidak setuju. All decisions are made by consensus. If consensus cannot be reached, a vote is taken by counting the number of shares that vote agree, abstain or disagree.  |
