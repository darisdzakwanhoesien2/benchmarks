6. The Board of Commissioners must carry out its duties and responsibilities independently and with good faith, full responsibility, and caution.

7. The Board of Commissioners must allocate sufficient time to optimally carry out its duties and responsibilities.

8. The Board of Commissioners must notify OJK within 7 (seven) business days of the discovery of:
a. Violations of financial and banking regulations; and
b. Circumstances or potential situations that could endanger the Bank's business continuity.

9. In support of the effective execution of duties and responsibilities, the Board of Commissioners must establish at least the following committees:
a. Audit Committee;
b. Risk Oversight Committee;
c. Remuneration and Nomination Committee;
d. Other committees required by regulations.

10. The Board of Commissioners must supervise the committees mentioned in point 9 to ensure they carry out their tasks effectively and must evaluate and/or assess the performance of these committees at least at the end of each fiscal year.

11. The Board of Commissioners must actively supervise the Compliance Function by:
a. Evaluating the implementation of the Bank's compliance function at least twice a year;
b. Providing recommendations to improve the quality of the Bank's compliance function.
