## Mengelola Turnover Karyawan

Peristiwa keluar-masuknya karyawan merupakan hal yang lumrah terjadi di perusahaan manapun, termasuk Perseroan. Kendati begitu, tingkat perputaran (turnover) karyawan yang tinggi tetap bisa menimbulkan beberapa masalah bagi perusahaan, seperti mengganggu stabilitas produktivitas dan menurunkan kualitas output. Pada jangka panjang, tingkat turnover yang tidak terkendali bahkan bisa menimbulkan beban kerugian secara finansial bagi Perseroan.

Karena itu, Perseroan terus berusaha menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang kondusif dan harmonis dengan menerapkan beberapa strategi pengelolaan SDM yang berorientasi pada kebutuhan karyawan serta menjalankan program retensi untuk mempertahankan talenta-talenta terbaik yang dimiliki agar tetap loyal mendukung kemajuan Perseroan. Dengan hadirnya lingkungan kerja yang baik, maka tingkat turnover karyawan Perseroan relatif terkendali setiap tahun dan bahkan cenderung rendah.

Per 31 Desember 2024, tingkat perputaran karyawan Perseroan tercatat sebesar 30,11%, mengalami penurunan jika dibandingkan dengan tahun 2023 yang mencapai 73,13%. Berikut ini adalah rumus yang dipakai Perseroan untuk menghitung tingkat turnover karyawan:

To support the effective implementation of all HR competency development programs in accordance with the 2024 business plan, the Company has incurred training investment costs of Rp7.079.451.738, a decrease of Rp1.804.548.247 when compared to the previous year which amounted to Rp8.884.000.000. The decrease in costs was influenced by the bank's focus on efficient use of employee competency development costs for 2024.

Information regarding training activities and employee competency development during 2024 has been disclosed in Human Resources Chapter, page 482.

## Employee Turnover Management

Employee turnover is a common occurrence in any company, including the Company. However, a high employee turnover rate can still cause several problems for the Company, such as disrupting productivity stability and reducing output quality. In the long run, an uncontrolled turnover rate can even cause financial losses for the Company.

Therefore, the Company continues to strive to create a conducive and harmonious work environment by implementing several HR management strategies that are oriented to the needs of employees and running retention programs to retain the best talents to remain loyal to support the Company's progress. With the presence of a good working environment, the Company's employee turnover rate is relatively controlled every year and even tends to be low.

As of December 31, 2024, the Company's employee turnover rate was recorded at 30.11%, a decrease compared to 2023 which reached 73.13%. The following is the formula used by the Company to calculate the employee turnover rate:
