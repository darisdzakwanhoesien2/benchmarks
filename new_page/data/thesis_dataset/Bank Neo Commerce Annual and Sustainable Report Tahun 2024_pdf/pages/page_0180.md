## Target Jangka Pendek

1. Meningkatkan aktivitas akuisisi nasabah baru baik individu, UMKM, dan korporasi dengan terus mengembangkan produk layanan seperti Neo Loan dan menciptakan produk-produk milik Bank sendiri.
2. Melakukan kolaborasi dengan mitra daring dan luring dalam pengembangan usaha penyaluran kredit.

In addition, the Company has also set targets in the short term, medium term, and the main strategy, while taking into account the financial and non-financial conditions, as well as the Company's business development plans. The Company's plans and strategies for 2025 are as follows:

## Short-Term Company Targets

1. Increase the acquisition of new customers, including individuals, MSMEs, and corporations, by continuously developing service products such as Neo Loan and creating new products of the Bank.
2. Collaborate with both online and offline partners in the development of credit distribution.
