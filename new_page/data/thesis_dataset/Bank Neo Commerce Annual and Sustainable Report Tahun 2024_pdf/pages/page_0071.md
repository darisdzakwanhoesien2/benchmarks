# Milestones

|  1990 | Perseroan didirikan dengan nama PT Bank Yudha Bhakti atas inisiatif Dephankam, Perum ASABRI, Pepabri, dan para pengembang, dengan latar belakang kebijakan PAKTO 27/1988. Berdasarkan Persetujuan Prinsip Menteri Keuangan pada 14 Agustus 1989, Bank ini resmi beroperasi pada 9 Januari 1990.

The company was established under the name of PT Bank Yudha Bhakti on the initiative of the Ministry of Defense, Perum ASABRI, Pepabri, and developers, with the background of the PAKTO 27/1988 policy. Based on the Principle Approval of the Minister of Finance on August 14th, 1989, the Bank officially operated on January 9, 1990.  |
| --- | --- |
|  1998 | Di tengah krisis moneter yang melanda Indonesia pada tahun 1997, Bank Yudha Bhakti berhasil bertahan dan memanfaatkan peluang yang ada. Pada awalnya, kepemilikan Bank berada di bawah Induk Koperasi dan Pusat Koperasi di lingkungan TNI/POLRI serta Dephankam. Keberhasilan tersebut membawa Bank Yudha Bhakti meraih pengakuan sebagai Bank kategori "A".

In the midst of the monetary crisis that hit Indonesia in 1997, Bank Yudha Bhakti managed to survive and take advantage of the opportunities that existed. Initially, the Bank's ownership was under the Parent Cooperative and Cooperative Center within the TNI/POLRI and Dephankam. This success brought Bank Yudha Bhakti recognition as a category "A" Bank.  |
|  2001 | Perseroan memulai ekspansi berkelanjutan dengan membuka Kantor Cabang dan Kantor Cabang Pembantu di berbagai wilayah di Jawa dan Sumatera untuk memperluas jangkauan layanan perbankan.

The Company began continuous expansion by opening Branch Offices and Sub-Branch Offices in various regions in Java and Sumatra to expand the reach of banking services.  |
|  2015 | Pada 13 Januari 2015, Perseroan resmi menjadi perusahaan terbuka dengan mencatatkan sahamnya di Bursa Efek Indonesia dan melaksanakan Penawaran Umum Perdana (Initial Public Offering/IPO).

On January 13th, 2015, the Company officially became a public company by listing its shares on the Indonesia Stock Exchange and carrying out an Initial Public Offering (IPO).  |
|  2016 | Perseroan melakukan Penawaran Umum Terbatas I (PUT I) kepada para Pemegang Saham dalam rangka Hak Memesan Efek Terlebih Dahulu (HMETD).

The Company conducted the Limited Public Offering I (PUT I) to the Shareholders through a Rights Issue.  |
|  2019 | • Perseroan memulai era baru setelah lebih dari tiga dekade melayani masyarakat dengan produk perbankan konvensional.
• PT Akulaku Silvrr Indonesia masuk menjadi Pemegang Saham Perseroan melalui Penambahan Modal Tanpa Hak Memesan Efek Terlebih Dahulu (PMTHMETD).
• Perseroan melakukan Penawaran Umum Terbatas II (PUT II) kepada para Pemegang Saham dalam rangka Hak Memesan Efek Terlebih Dahulu (HMETD).
• The Company began a new era after more than three decades of serving the community with conventional banking products.
• PT Akulaku Silvrr Indonesia became a shareholder of the Company through a Capital Increase Without Pre-emptive Rights (PMTHMETD).
• The Company conducted the Limited Public Offering II (PUT II) to Shareholders through a Rights Issue (HMETD).  |
