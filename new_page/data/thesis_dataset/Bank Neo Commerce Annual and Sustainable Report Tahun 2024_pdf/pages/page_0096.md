Laporan Keberlanjutan Sustainability Report

Pada 11 Mei 2016, Bursa Efek Indonesia menyetujui pencatatan 1.509.096.000 lembar saham baru Perseroan dengan harga penawaran Rp125 per saham, sebagaimana tertuang dalam surat No. S-03016/BELPPI/05-2016. Sebelumnya, pada 2 Mei 2016, OJK Pasar Modal telah memberikan pemberitahuan resmi mengenai efektifnya pernyataan pendaftaran melalui surat No. S-215/D.04/2016.

Dalam pencatatan ini, Perseroan juga menerbitkan Waran Seri I sebanyak 754.548.000 Waran. Setiap 6 saham baru hasil pelaksanaan HMETD disertai dengan 3 Waran Seri I, yang memberikan hak kepada pemegang untuk membeli saham baru dengan harga pelaksanaan Rp135 per saham. Masa pelaksanaan Waran Seri I berlangsung dari 18 November 2016 hingga 18 Mei 2018, dengan ketentuan bahwa 1 Waran dapat digunakan untuk membeli 1 saham baru Perseroan.

On May 11, 2016, the Indonesia Stock Exchange approved the listing of 1,509,096,000 new shares of the Company at an offering price of Rp125 per share, as stated in letter No. S-03016/BELPPI/05-2016. Previously, on May 2nd, 2016, the Capital Market OJK had provided official notification regarding the effectiveness of the registration statement through letter No. S-215/D.04/2016.

In this listing, the Company also issued 754,548,000 Series I Warrants. Every 6 new shares resulting from the exercise of HMETD are accompanied by 3 Series I Warrants, which entitle the holder to purchase new shares at an exercise price of Rp135 per share. The exercise period of Series I Warrants runs from November 18th, 2016 to May 18th, 2018, with the provision that 1 Warrant can be used to purchase 1 new share of the Company.

# Penawaran Umum Terbatas II

Limited Public Offering II

Pada 27 Juni 2018, melalui RUPSLB para pemegang saham telah menetapkan untuk menyetujui penerbitan sebanyak-banyaknya 3.000.000.000 lembar saham baru melalui mekanisme Penambahan Modal dengan Hak Memesan Efek Terlebih Dahulu (HMETD). Persetujuan ini kemudian diikuti dengan pernyataan efektif dari OJK pada 28 Mei 2019 melalui surat No. S-64/D.04/2019, yang mengesahkan pelaksanaan Penawaran Umum Terbatas II (PUT II).

Melalui PUT II, Perseroan menawarkan sebanyak 499.603.954 lembar saham baru dengan nilai nominal Rp100 per saham dan harga pelaksanaan Rp338 per saham. Seluruh saham tersebut, yang merupakan 8,11% dari modal ditempatkan dan disetor penuh setelah PUT II, diterbitkan dari portapel Perseroan dan resmi dicatatkan di Bursa Efek Indonesia.

On June 27th, 2018, through the EGMS, the shareholders have decided to approve the issuance of a maximum of 3,000,000,000 new shares through the Capital Increase mechanism with Pre-emptive Rights (HMETD). This approval was then followed by an effective statement from the OJK on May 28, 2019 through letter No. S-64/D.04/2019, which authorized the implementation of the Limited Public Offering II (PUT II).

Through PUT II, the Company offers 499,603,954 new shares with a nominal value of Rp100 per share and an exercise price of Rp338 per share. All of these shares, which constitute 8.11% of the issued and fully paid-up capital after PUT II, were issued from the Company's portfolio and officially listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange.

# Penawaran Umum Terbatas III

Limited Public Offering III

Pada 28 Juni 2019, Perseroan memperoleh persetujuan RUPSLB untuk menerbitkan hingga 3.000.000.000 lembar saham baru. Selanjutnya, pernyataan efektif untuk pelaksanaan Penawaran Umum Terbatas III (PUT III) diterbitkan oleh OJK melalui surat No. S-66/D.04/2020 pada 18 Juni 2020. Dalam PUT III, Perseroan menawarkan sebanyak 1.320.381.878 lembar saham baru dengan nilai nominal Rp100 per saham dan harga pelaksanaan Rp300 per saham.

Rasio dalam penerbitan saham PUT III, ditetapkan bahwa setiap pemegang 14 saham berhak memperoleh 3 HMETD, di mana setiap 1 HMETD memberikan hak untuk membeli 1 saham baru. Sebanyak 500.013.138 lembar saham baru yang diterbitkan dari portapel Perseroan dalam PUT III telah resmi dicatatkan di Bursa Efek Indonesia.

On June 28th, 2019, the Company obtained approval from the EGMS to issue up to 3,000,000,000 new shares. Furthermore, an effective statement for the implementation of Limited Public Offering III (PUT III) was issued by the OJK through letter No. S-66/D.04/2020 on June 18th, 2020. In PUT III, the Company offered 1,320,381,878 new shares with a nominal value of Rp100 per share and an exercise price of Rp300 per share.

The ratio in the issuance of shares of PUT III, is set that every holder of 14 shares is entitled to obtain 3 HMETD, where every 1 HMETD gives the right to buy 1 new share. A total of 500,013,138 new shares issued from the Company's portfolio in PUT III have been officially listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange.
