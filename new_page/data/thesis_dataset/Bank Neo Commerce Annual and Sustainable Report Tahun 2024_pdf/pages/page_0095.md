# Pencatatan Saham Perdana

Initial Public Offering

Pada 31 Desember 2014, Perseroan memperoleh pernyataan efektif dari Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) melalui surat No. S-584/D.04/2014 terkait proses Penawaran Umum Perdana (IPO). Selanjutnya, pada 13 Januari 2015, saham Perseroan secara resmi tercatat di Bursa Efek Indonesia, menjadikannya perusahaan pertama yang mencatatkan saham di lantai bursa pada tahun tersebut.

Perseroan menerbitkan sebanyak 300.000.000 (tiga ratus juta) lembar saham biasa atas nama yang berasal dari portopel, setara dengan 11,93% (sebelas koma sembilan tiga persen) dari modal ditempatkan dan disetor penuh setelah IPO. Saham tersebut memiliki nilai nominal Rp100 (seratus rupiah) per saham dan ditawarkan kepada publik dengan harga Rp115 (seratus lima belas rupiah) per saham.

On December 31th, 2014, the Company obtained an effective statement from the Financial Services Authority (OJK) through letter No. S-584/D.04/2014 regarding the Initial Public Offering (IPO) process. Furthermore, on January 13, 2015, the Company's shares were officially listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange, making it the first company to list its shares on the stock exchange that year.

The Company issued 300,000,000 (three hundred million) ordinary shares in the name of those originating from the portfolio, equivalent to 11.93% (eleven point nine three percent) of the issued and fully paid-up capital after the IPO. The shares have a nominal value of Rp100 (one hundred rupiah) per share and are offered to the public at a price of Rp115 (one hundred and fifteen rupiah) per share.
