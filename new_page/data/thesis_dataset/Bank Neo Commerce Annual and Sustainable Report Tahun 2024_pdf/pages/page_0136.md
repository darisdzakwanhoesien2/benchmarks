# Tinjauan Makro Segmen Industri
## Industry Segment Macro Overview

Menjelang penutupan tahun 2024, Bank Indonesia memutuskan untuk mempertahankan suku bunga acuannya (BI-Rate) pada level 6,00%, dengan suku bunga Deposit Facility tetap di 5,25% dan suku bunga Lending Facility di 6,75%. Keputusan ini sejalan dengan strategi kebijakan moneter yang ditempuh secara konsisten guna menjaga inflasi tetap dalam sasaran serta mendukung kesinambungan pertumbuhan ekonomi nasional. Selain itu, kebijakan ini turut diperkuat oleh langkah-langkah stabilisasi nilai tukar melalui intervensi di pasar valuta asing, penerapan operasi moneter yang pro-pasar, serta posisi cadangan devisa yang tetap mencukupi.

Sementara itu, Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK), dalam rilisnya tanggal 31 Desember 2024, menegaskan bahwa industri perbankan Indonesia terus menunjukkan ketahanan meski dihadapkan pada ketidakpastian global dan tekanan ekonomi domestik. Kinerja positif ini dicapai melalui pendekatan yang inovatif dan responsif, memungkinkan sektor perbankan untuk menjaga kestabilan sistem keuangan, memperkuat aktivitas ekonomi, serta mempertahankan kepercayaan publik.

Berdasarkan data per Desember 2024, pertumbuhan kredit perbankan umum tercatat sebesar 10,39% (yoy), sedikit meningkat dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya yang berada di angka 10,38% (yoy). Kredit investasi mencatat pertumbuhan tertinggi, yaitu 13,62%, diikuti oleh kredit konsumsi sebesar 10,61%, dan kredit modal kerja sebesar 8,35%. Sektor perdagangan besar dan eceran, serta sektor pertanian, memberikan kontribusi dominan terhadap pertumbuhan kredit tersebut. Di sisi lain, Dana Pihak Ketiga (DPK) perbankan juga tumbuh positif, sebesar 4,48% (yoy) menjadi Rp8.837,2 triliun pada tahun 2024, lebih tinggi dibandingkan pertumbuhan pada tahun 2023 yang sebesar 3,73%. Kondisi likuiditas perbankan tetap sehat, tercermin dari rasio AL/NCD sebesar 112,87% dan AL/DPK sebesar 25,59%, yang keduanya jauh di atas ambang batas ketentuan masing-masing 50% dan 10%.

Selain itu, permodalan bank umum juga tetap solid dengan rasio CAR sebesar 26,69%. Kualitas kredit juga membaik, tercermin dari penurunan rasio NPL gross menjadi 2,08%, serta stabilnya NPL net di level 0,74%.

Toward the end of 2024, Bank Indonesia decided to maintain its benchmark interest rate (BI Rate) at 6.00%, with the Deposit Facility rate at 5.25% and the Lending Facility rate at 6.75%. This decision aligns with a consistently implemented monetary policy strategy aimed at keeping inflation within target and supporting sustainable national economic growth. Additionally, the policy is reinforced by exchange rate stabilization measures, including foreign exchange market interventions, pro-market monetary operations, and a sufficient level of foreign exchange reserves.

Meanwhile, the Financial Services Authority (OJK), in its release dated December 31, 2024, affirmed that Indonesia's banking industry continued to demonstrate resilience despite global uncertainty and domestic economic pressures. This positive performance was achieved through innovative and responsive approaches, enabling the banking sector to maintain financial system stability, support economic activity, and preserve public confidence.

As of December 2024, credit growth in the commercial banking sector reached 10.39% (yoy), slightly increasing from 10.38% (yoy) in the previous year. Investment loans recorded the highest growth at 13.62%, followed by consumer loans at 10.61%, and working capital loans at 8.35%. Significant contributions to this growth came from the wholesale and retail trade sectors, as well as the agriculture sector. On the other hand, Third-Party Funds (TPF) also grew positively by 4.48% (yoy), reaching Rp8,837.2 trillion in 2024, higher than the 3.73% growth recorded in 2023. This contributed to a healthy banking liquidity condition. Liquidity ratios remained strong, with the AL/NCD ratio at 112.87% and the AL/TPF ratio at 25.59%, both well above the regulatory thresholds of 50% and 10%, respectively.

In addition, commercial banks maintained solid capital adequacy with a Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) of 26.69%, while credit quality improved, as reflected in the decline of the gross Non-Performing Loan (NPL) ratio to 2.08%, and the stability of the net NPL ratio at 0.74%.
