Melanjutkan program tahun sebelumnya terkait pelaksanaan Penerapan Anti Pencucian Uang, Pencegahan Pendanaan Terorisme, dan Pencegahan Pendanaan Proliferasi Senjata Pemusnah Massal (APU PPT dan PPPSPM) sesuai dengan POJK No. 8 Tahun 2023 tentang Penerapan Program Anti Pencucian Uang, Pencegahan Pendanaan Terorisme, dan Pencegahan Pendanaan Proliferasi Senjata Pemusnah Massal di Sektor Jasa Keuangan. 7. Melakukan kajian terhadap ketentuan internal Bank lainnya yang memiliki keterkaitan dengan proses penerapan APU PPT dan PPSPM agar sesuai dengan POJK No. 8 Tahun 2023 tentang Penerapan Program Anti Pencucian Uang, Pencegahan Pendanaan Terorisme, dan Pencegahan Pendanaan Proliferasi Senjata Pemusnah Massal di Sektor Jasa Keuangan.

8. Melakukan pengembangan Sistem Informasi sebagai upaya penyediaan data secara otomatis untuk pelaporan kepada PPATK. Pada periode semester II tahun 2024 Bank melakukan pengembangan dengan menambahkan beberapa fungsi didalam aplikasi Web APU PPT yaitu penambahan menu monitoring transaksi Loan (Loan Early Repaid, Loan Overpayment, dan Loan Drawdown Repayment Difference).

9. Melakukan pemeliharaan berkala terhadap daftar nama/list nama yang tercantum dalam DTTOT, PPSPM, UN List, UK-US Sanction List, OFAC dan sebagainya. Unit kerja APU PPT saat ini telah melakukan pemeliharaan secara rutin terkait dengan daftar nama/list nama yang tercantum dalam DTTOT, PPSPM, UN List, UK Sanction List, OFAC dan sebagainya, berkoordinasi dengan Divisi Digital Operation dan Anti Fraud. Pada Semester II tahun 2024 juga telah ditambahkan data pihak yang diduga terkait aktivitas judi online baik yang diperoleh dari OJK maupun diperoleh dari hasil siber patrol secara mandiri kedalam list sebagai salah satu upaya Bank memitigasi risiko penyalahgunaan fasilitas Bank untuk aktivitas tindak pidana perjudian.

10. Melakukan pelatihan mengenai APU PPT kepada karyawan BNC, khususnya kepada first line of defense dan unit terkait lainnya. Pelatihan APU PPT dan PPSPM telah dilakukan oleh Unit Kerja APU PPT kepada karyawan Kantor Cabang dan Kantor Pusat.

11. Meningkatkan upaya kesadaran terkait Program Anti Suap dan Anti Korupsi pada Unit Kerja di lingkungan pada Struktur Organisasi Bank. Satuan Kerja Kepatuhan (SKK) telah berupaya meningkatkan kesadaran terkait Program Anti Suap dan Anti Korupsi pada Unit Kerja di lingkungan pada Struktur Organisasi Bank, dengan mengeluarkan Core Inside (Compliance Regulatory Information and Compliance Awareness Highlight).

6. Continuing the previous year's program related to the implementation of Anti-Money Laundering, Prevention of Financing of Terrorism, and Prevention of Financing of Proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction (AML/CFT and PPPSPM) in accordance with POJK No. 8 of 2023 concerning the Implementation of Anti-Money Laundering Programs, Prevention of Financing of Terrorism, and Prevention of Financing of Proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction in the Financial Services Sector. Including developing the AML/CFT Web application to support the provision of reporting data to regulators.

7. Conduct a review of other internal Bank provisions that have a relationship with the AML/CFT and PPSPM implementation process to be in accordance with POJK No. 8 of 2023 concerning the Implementation of Anti-Money Laundering Programs, Prevention of Financing of Terrorism, and Prevention of Financing of Proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction in the Financial Services Sector.

8. Developing the Information System as an effort to provide data automatically for reporting to PPATK. In the second semester period of 2024, the Bank developed several functions in the AML/CFT Web application, namely the addition of a Loan transaction monitoring menu (Loan Early Repaid, Loan Overpayment, and Loan Drawdown Repayment Difference).

9. Conduct periodic maintenance of the list of names listed in DTTOT, PPSPM, UN List, UK-US Sanction List, OFAC and so on. The AML/CFT work unit has currently carried out routine maintenance related to the list of names listed in DTTOT, PPSPM, UN List, UK Sanction List, OFAC and so on, in coordination with the Digital Operation and Anti Fraud Division. In Semester II of 2024, data on parties suspected of being related to online gambling activities, both obtained from OJK and obtained from the results of cyber patrols independently, have also been added to the list as one of the Bank's efforts to mitigate the risk of misuse of Bank facilities for gambling criminal activities.

10. Conduct training on AML/CFT to BNC employees, especially to the first line of defense and other related units. AML/CFT and PPSPM training has been conducted by the AML/CFT Work Unit to Branch Office and Head Office employees.

11. Increasing awareness efforts related to the Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption Program in the Work Units within the Bank's Organizational Structure. The Compliance Working Unit (SKK) has made efforts to increase awareness related to the Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption Program in the Work Units within the Bank's Organizational Structure, by issuing Core Inside (Compliance Regulatory Information and Compliance Awareness Highlight).
