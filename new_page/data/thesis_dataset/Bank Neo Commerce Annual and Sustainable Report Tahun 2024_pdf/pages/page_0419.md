# Pengendalian Mutu Audit

Audit Quality Control

Program pengendalian (Quality Assurance) dan peningkatan (Improvement) mutu audit didesain untuk kesesuaian aktivitas Internal Auditor dengan standar dan evaluasi penerapan kode etik, antara lain dalam bentuk pengembangan metodologi dan penyempurnaan audit program agar sesuai dengan perkembangan bisnis dan best practice. Pengendalian mutu aktivitas audit intern dilakukan oleh pihak internal dan eksternal. Pengendalian mutu yang dilakukan oleh pihak internal dilakukan dengan dibentuk Development &amp; Quality Assurance Department, dengan tugas antara lain melakukan kaji ulang terhadap kualitas pelaksanaan dan hasil audit serta memastikan penerapan kode etik auditor telah dipatuhi.

Pengendalian mutu eksternal yang dilakukan oleh pihak independen dilakukan 3 (tiga) tahunan. Review terakhir pada 15 Agustus 2023 dilakukan oleh KPMG.

3. Confidentiality

Internal auditors respect the value and ownership of the information they receive and do not disclose such information without proper authorization, unless required by law or the profession.

a. Internal auditors must be careful in using and safeguarding information obtained in the course of their duties.
b. They should not use information for personal gain, or in any way, that is contrary to the law or detrimental to legitimate and ethical corporate objectives.

4. Competence

a. Internal auditors apply the necessary knowledge, skills and experience in providing Internal Audit services.
b. Internal auditors only engage in the provision of services that require their knowledge, skills and experience.
c. Must provide Internal Audit services in accordance with the IPPF.
d. Must continuously improve the expertise, effectiveness and quality of their services.

Quality assurance and improvement programs are designed to conform Internal Auditor activities to standards and evaluate the implementation of the code of ethics, among others in the form of methodology development and improvement of audit programs in accordance with business developments and best practices. Quality control of internal audit activities is carried out by internal and external parties. Quality control carried out by internal parties is carried out with the establishment of the Development &amp; Quality Assurance Department, with the task of, among others, conducting a review of the quality of the implementation and results of the audit and ensuring the application of the auditor's code of ethics has been complied with.

External quality control conducted by an independent party is carried out for 3 (three) years. The last review on August 15, 2023 was conducted by KPMG.
