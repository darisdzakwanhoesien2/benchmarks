## Business Outlook

Tingkat inflasi pada tahun 2025 diperkirakan tetap terkendali dalam kisaran $2,5\% \pm 1\%$ (yoy). Bank Indonesia terus mengoptimalkan kebijakan moneternya guna menjaga stabilitas ekonomi serta mendorong pertumbuhan ekonomi yang berkelanjutan.

Dalam Rapat Dewan Gubernur Bank Indonesia pada bulan Maret 2025, diputuskan untuk mempertahankan suku bunga acuan (BI-Rate) pada level $5,75\%$, suku bunga Deposit Facility sebesar $5,00\%$, dan suku bunga Lending Facility sebesar $6,50\%$. Kebijakan ini diambil sebagai respons terhadap dinamika perekonomian global yang masih penuh ketidakpastian, dengan tujuan menjaga stabilitas nilai tukar Rupiah sesuai dengan fundamental ekonomi, mengendalikan inflasi, serta mendukung momentum pertumbuhan ekonomi nasional.

Inflation in 2025 is expected to remain under control within the range of $2.5\% \pm 1\%$ (yoy). Bank Indonesia continues to optimize its monetary policy to maintain economic stability and encourage sustainable economic growth.

In the Board of Governors meeting in March 2025, it was decided to maintain benchmark interest rate (BI-Rate) at $5.75\%$, the Deposit Facility rate at $5.00\%$, and the Lending Facility rate at $6.50\%$. This policy decision was made in response to the dynamics of the global economy, which remains highly uncertain, with the goal of maintaining the stability of the Rupiah exchange rate in line with economic fundamentals, controlling inflation, and supporting the momentum of national economic growth.
