GCG merupakan kunci penting untuk memastikan tercapainya kinerja bisnis yang berkelanjutan. Untuk itu, Dewan Komisaris mendukung penerapan prinsip-prinsip GCG dalam setiap kegiatan Perseroan.

Berkenaan hal tersebut, Dewan Komisaris berpendapat Perseroan telah menerapkan prinsip dan rekomendasi yang diatur dalam Pedoman Tata Kelola serta Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan yang terkait serta telah berupaya melakukan perbaikan-perbaikan dan penyempurnaan penerapan GCG pada seluruh tingkatan organisasi Perseroan. Penerapan GCG di lingkungan Perseroan terus mengalami perbaikan. Hal ini menjadi cerminan komitmen Direksi untuk dapat menerapkan prinsip dan praktik GCG di seluruh elemen operasi dan bisnis Bank.

Salah satu aspek penting dalam keberhasilan penerapan prinsip Tata Kelola adalah sinergi dan kolaborasi yang baik antara organ pendukung Dewan Komisaris dan organ pendukung Direksi. Melalui keberadaan organ-organ pendukung tersebut, setiap aspek dalam penerapan GCG dapat terlaksana dan terpantau, baik itu kepatuhan, manajemen risiko, audit, hingga perkembangan SDM dan Teknologi Informasi sebagai salah satu fondasi mewujudkan akuntabilitas Bank.

Dalam rangka mendukung pelaksanaan prinsip GCG di Perseroan, saat ini komposisi anggota Komisaris Independen Perseroan mencapai lebih dari 50%. Jumlah Komisaris Independen yang mayoritas dan cukup besar tersebut dapat mendorong Dewan Komisaris untuk bertindak objektif, aktif dan konstruktif, serta mampu melindungi kepentingan Perseroan dan seluruh pemangku kepentingan.

Strong GCG is crucial to achieving sustainable business performance. The Board of Commissioners supports the implementation of GCG principles across all Company activities.

In this regard, the Board of Commissioners is of the opinion that the Company has implemented the principles and recommendations outlined in the Corporate Governance Guidelines and the relevant regulations issued by the Financial Services Authority (OJK). The Company has also made continuous improvements to enhance GCG implementation at all levels of the organization. The steady progress in GCG practices reflects the Board of Directors' commitment to applying good governance principles throughout the Bank's operational and business units.

One of the key success factors in implementing good governance principles lies in the strong synergy and collaboration between the supporting organs of the Board of Commissioners and those of the Board of Directors. Through the work of these supporting organs, every aspect of GCG implementation—such as compliance, risk management, audit, human resource development, and information technology—can be effectively carried out and monitored, thereby laying a strong foundation for the Bank's accountability.

To support the implementation of GCG principles within the Company, more than 50% of the Board of Commissioners currently consists of Independent Commissioners. This majority and significant representation of Independent Commissioners enables the Board of Commissioners to act objectively, actively, and constructively, as well as to safeguard the interests of the Company and all stakeholders.

# Risk Management

The Board of Commissioners recognizes that internal control systems and risk management are critical factors in conducting banking business. The Board views that the Company has established an effective internal control system that aligns with the Bank's risk profile. These controls have been implemented effectively and serve as a key component in the Bank's management, forming a strong foundation for sound and secure banking operations.

Throughout 2024, the Board of Commissioners exercised oversight over the implementation of internal control systems, including the approval of the Liquidity Risk Management Policy, Contingency Funding Plan, Risk Management Implementation Policy, and the Company's Risk Appetite and Risk Tolerance.
