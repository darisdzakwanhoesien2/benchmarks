# Dewan Komisaris

# Board Of Commissioners

Dewan Komisaris merupakan organ utama yang berperan dalam melakukan pengawasan terhadap kebijakan pengurusan dan jalannya pengelolaan Perseroan yang dilakukan oleh Direksi. Dewan Komisaris berwenang untuk memberikan nasihat kepada Direksi untuk kepentingan Perseroan, sesuai dengan maksud dan tujuan Perseroan.

Dewan Komisaris diangkat melalui RUPS setelah mendapat persetujuan dari OJK (fit and proper test) dan juga dapat diberhentikan sewaktu-waktu melalui RUPS tanpa mengurangi hak RUPS. Dewan Komisaris terdiri dari Komisaris Utama, Komisaris Independen dan Komisaris. Usulan pengangkatan dan/atau penggantian anggota Dewan Komisaris kepada RUPS dilakukan dengan memperhatikan rekomendasi Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi.

The Board of Commissioners is the main organ responsible for overseeing the policies of the management and the running of the Company's operations carried out by the Board of Directors. The Board of Commissioners has the authority to provide advice to the Board of Directors for the benefit of the Company, in accordance with the Company's objectives and purposes.

The Board of Commissioners is appointed through the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) after obtaining approval from the Financial Services Authority (OJK) (fit and proper test) and can also be dismissed at any time through the GMS without diminishing the rights of the GMS. The Board of Commissioners consists of the President Commissioner, Independent Commissioners, and Commissioners. The proposal for the appointment and/or replacement of members of the Board of Commissioners to the GMS is made with consideration of the recommendations from the Nomination and Remuneration Committee.

# Piagam Dewan Komisaris

# Board of Commissioners Charter

Piagam Dewan Komisaris (BOC Charter) merupakan pedoman dan tata tertib kerja Dewan Komisaris dalam menjalankan tugasnya. Disusun dengan mengacu pada Anggaran Dasar Perseroan, peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku, serta praktik-praktik tata kelola yang baik. BOC Charter memuat panduan pelaksanaan tugas, tanggung jawab dan wewenang Dewan Komisaris.

Piagam Dewan Komisaris Perseroan diperbaharui di tahun 2023 serta dapat diakses oleh publik melalui situs web Perseroan.

The Board of Commissioners Charter (BOC Charter) is the guideline and code of conduct for the Board of Commissioners in carrying out their duties. It is developed with reference to the Company's Articles of Association, applicable laws and regulations, and best governance practices. The BOC Charter outlines the implementation of the duties, responsibilities, and authority of the Board of Commissioners.

The Company's BOC Charter was updated in 2023 and is accessible to the public through the Company's website.
