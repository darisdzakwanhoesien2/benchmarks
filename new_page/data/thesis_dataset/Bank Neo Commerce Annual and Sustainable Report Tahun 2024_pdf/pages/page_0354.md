g. Meeting invitations must be signed by the President Commissioner, other members of the Board of Commissioners, or the Corporate Secretary, including the date, time, place, and agenda of the meeting.
h. Decisions of the meeting are made based on consensus. If consensus cannot be reached, decisions are made by voting, with the majority vote determining the outcome.
i. Meeting minutes must be prepared by the Corporate Secretary or another party designated by the Board of Commissioners and documented appropriately.
j. If a meeting is conducted via teleconference, the meeting minutes must be followed up by being signed by all members of the Board of Commissioners who attended.
k. The meeting minutes must be signed by all members of the Board of Commissioners who attended and sent to all members of the Board of Commissioners.
l. If a member of the Board of Commissioners does not sign the meeting minutes, they must provide a written explanation separately attached to the minutes.
m. If dissenting opinions arise during the meeting, these must be clearly recorded in the meeting minutes, including the reasons for the dissent.
n. The Board of Commissioners may also make valid decisions without holding a meeting, provided that all serving members of the Board of Commissioners agree by signing the proposal. A decision made in this way has the same validity as one made in a meeting.
