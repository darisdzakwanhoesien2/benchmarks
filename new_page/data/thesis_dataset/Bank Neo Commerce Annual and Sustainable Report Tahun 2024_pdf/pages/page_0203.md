Company Profile

Perseroan mengelola risiko strategik Perseroan secara langsung melalui kebijakan dan strategi Perseroan yang dituangkan dalam Rencana Bisnis Bank dan disampaikan setiap tahunnya kepada Otoritas Jasa Keuangan untuk mendapatkan persetujuan.

Perseroan secara berkala melakukan pengukuran, penilaian, dan pemantauan secara menyeluruh terhadap risiko strategis melalui analisis terhadap kesesuaian penetapan sasaran strategis dengan kondisi lingkungan bisnis, posisi Perseroan di pasar, efisiensi dalam melaksanakan kegiatan usaha, kondisi makro ekonomi serta jumlah kompetitor di mana Perseroan melaksanakan kegiatan usaha.

Berdasarkan hasil penilaian mandiri (self-assessment) Perseroan untuk posisi 31 Desember 2024, Risiko Strategik secara komposit dinilai Moderate.

## Risiko Kepatuhan

Risiko kepatuhan merupakan potensi kerugian yang timbul akibat Perseroan tidak mematuhi dan/atau tidak melaksanakan peraturan perundang-undangan dan ketentuan yang berlaku.

Perseroan memastikan kebijakan, ketentuan, sistem, dan prosedur serta kegiatan usaha yang dilakukan telah sesuai dengan ketentuan Regulator dan peraturan perundang-undangan, memastikan kepatuhan terhadap komitmen yang dibuat oleh Perseroan kepada Regulator lain yang berwenang serta mewujudkan terlaksananya Budaya Kepatuhan pada semua tingkatan organisasi dan kegiatan usaha Perseroan.

Perseroan secara berkala juga melakukan pengukuran, penilaian dan pemantauan terhadap risiko kepatuhan melalui penilaian terhadap jenis, signifikansi dan frekuensi pelanggaran yang dilakukan oleh Perseroan terhadap regulasi dan peraturan perundang-undangan lainnya yang berlaku.

Berdasarkan hasil penilaian mandiri (self-assessment) Perseroan untuk posisi 31 Desember 2024, Risiko Kepatuhan secara komposit dinilai Moderate.

## Risiko Reputasi

Risiko reputasi merupakan potensi kerugian yang timbul akibat menurunnya tingkat kepercayaan stakeholder akibat persepsi negatif terhadap Perseroan.

Unit yang membawahi fungsi pelayanan dan pengaduan nasabah melakukan pengelolaan risiko reputasi dengan cara menangani setiap pengaduan dan keluhan nasabah dengan tepat waktu dan merespon setiap pengaduan dan keluhan yang dapat menimbulkan dampak negatif bagi Perseroan. Hal tersebut merupakan salah satu upaya Perseroan untuk memberikan pelayanan terbaik kepada nasabah dan meningkatkan kepercayaan pada masyarakat.

The company manages strategic risk directly through its policies and strategies as outlined in the Bank's Business Plan that annually submitted OJK for approval.

The Company periodically conducts comprehensive measurement, assessment and monitoring of strategic risks through suitability analysis between strategic target with business environment condition, the Company's position in the market, efficiencies in conducting business activities, macroeconomic conditions, and competitors analysis on where the Company carries out business activities.

According to the results of the Company's risk profile self assessment for the position of December 31, 2024, the composite Strategic Risk is rated Moderate.

## Compliance Risk

Compliance risk is a potential loss arising from the Company's failure to comply with and/or not implementing the prevailing laws and regulations.

The Company ensures that policies, systems, and procedures as well as business activities carried out are in accordance with the provisions of the Regulator and laws and regulations, ensures compliance with commitments made by the Company to authorized Regulators and realizes the implementation of a Compliance Culture at all levels of the Company's organization and business activities.

The Company also periodically measures, assesses and monitors compliance risk by assessing the type, significance and frequency of violations committed by the Company against regulations and other applicable laws and regulations.

Based on the results of the Company's self-assessment for the position of December 31, 2024, the composite Compliance Risk is rated Moderate.

## Reputation Risk

Reputation risk is a potential loss arising from a decrease in the level of stakeholder trust due to negative perceptions of the Company.

Units responsible for customer service and complaints manage reputation risk by handling all customer complaints and grievances in a timely manner and responding to any complaints and grievances that may have a negative impact on the Company. This is one of the Company's commitment to provide the best service to customers and increase trust in the community.
