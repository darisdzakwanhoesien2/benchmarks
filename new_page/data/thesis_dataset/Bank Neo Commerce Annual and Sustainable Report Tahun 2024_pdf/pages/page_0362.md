# Program Pengenalan Perseroan bagi Direksi Baru

Introduction Program for New Directors

Perseroan menyelenggarakan program pengenalan Perseroan bagi anggota Direksi baru. Tujuan program ini adalah untuk memberikan pengetahuan dan pemahaman tentang Perseroan dan lingkup pekerjaan yang dijalankan. Program pengenalan tersebut mencakup profil, kondisi terkini, kinerja usaha, serta hal-hal lain mengenai Perseroan yang dianggap perlu. Program Pengenalan Direksi Baru dilakukan kepada Bapak Eri Budiono yang diangkat sebagai Direktur Utama Perseroan berdasarkan RUPSLB tanggal 30 April 2024.

The Company held an introduction to the Company program for new members of the Board of Directors. The purpose of this program is to provide knowledge and understanding of the Company and its scope of work. The introduction program covers the Company's profile, current conditions, business performance, as well as other matters concerning the Company deemed necessary. The New Directors Introduction Program was conducted for Mr. Eri Budiono who was appointed as President Director of the Company based on the EGMS dated 30 April 2024.
