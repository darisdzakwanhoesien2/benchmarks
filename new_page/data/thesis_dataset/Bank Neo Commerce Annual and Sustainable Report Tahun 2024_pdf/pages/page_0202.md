# Risiko Operasional

Risiko Operasional merupakan potensi kerugian akibat ketidakcukupan dan/atau tidak berfungsinya proses internal, kesalahan manusia, kegagalan sistem, dan/atau adanya kejadian-kejadian eksternal yang mempengaruhi operasional Perseroan.

Dalam mengelola risiko operasional, Perseroan mewajibkan setiap unit kerja agar bertanggung jawab untuk mengelola risiko yang melekat pada kegiatan operasional sehari-hari dengan mengacu kepada kebijakan dan prosedur internal dan eksternal. Selain itu, pengelolaan risiko operasional juga meliputi hal-hal yang terkait dengan pengembangan produk, sistem (teknologi sistem informasi), sumber daya manusia, dan kejadian eksternal yang berpotensi dapat mengganggu operasional Perseroan. Selanjutnya, untuk melaksanakan pemantauan Risiko Operasional, Perseroan menggunakan perangkat Manajemen Risiko Operasional seperti Loss Event Database (LED) dan Risk Control Self-Assessment (RCSA).

Berdasarkan hasil penilaian mandiri (self-assessment) Perseroan untuk posisi 31 Desember 2024, Risiko Operasional secara komposit dinilai Moderate to High.

# Risiko Hukum

Risiko hukum adalah potensi kerugian yang disebabkan oleh adanya tuntutan hukum dan/atau kelemahan pada aspek yuridis. Perseroan mengelola kemungkinan risiko hukum dengan memastikan bahwa seluruh aktivitas dan hubungan kegiatan usaha Perseroan dengan semua pihak telah sesuai dan didasarkan pada aturan dan persyaratan yang dapat melindungi kepentingan Perseroan dari segi hukum.

Perseroan telah memiliki unit kerja yang bertugas untuk mengelola risiko hukum dengan melaksanakan pengelolaan risiko hukum yang bersifat preventif dan proaktif terhadap aktivitas-aktivitas yang berhubungan dengan kegiatan usaha Perseroan sehingga dapat meminimalisir dampak finansial yang signifikan bagi Perseroan. Unit kerja dimaksud melakukan pengukuran, penilaian, dan pemantauan secara keseluruhan terhadap risiko hukum melalui analisis yang mendalam atas faktor litigasi, kelemahan perikatan, dan ketiadaan/perubahan perundang-undangan dari setiap produk atau transaksi yang telah dilaksanakan oleh Perseroan.

Berdasarkan hasil penilaian mandiri (self-assessment) Perseroan untuk posisi 31 Desember 2024, Risiko Hukum secara komposit dinilai Low to Moderate

# Risiko Strategis

Risiko strategik merupakan potensi kerugian akibat ketidaktepatan dalam pengambilan dan/atau pelaksanaan suatu keputusan strategis serta kegagalan dalam mengantisipasi perubahan lingkungan bisnis.

# Operational Risk

Operational Risk is the potential loss due to inadequate internal processes, human error, system failure, and/or external events that affect the Company's operations.

To manage operational risk, the Company requires each working unit within the Company to be responsible for managing the inherent risk rating of daily operational activities by referring to internal and external policies and procedures. In addition, operational risk management also covers matters related to product development, systems (information system technology), human resources, and external events that may potentially disrupt Company's business continuity. As part of Operational Risk monitoring, the Company utilizes Operational Risk Management tools such as Loss Event Database (LED) and Risk Control Self-Assessment (RCSA).

Based on the results of the Company's risk profile self assessment for the position of December 31, 2024, the composite Operational Risk is rated Moderate to High.

# Legal Risk

Legal risk is a potential loss caused by lawsuits and/or weaknesses in juridical aspects. The Company manages the possibility of legal risk by ensuring that all activities and relationships of the Company's business activities with all parties are in accordance and based on the regulations and laws that can protect the Company's interests from a legal perspective.

The Company has an appointed legal unit in charge of managing legal risk, to carry out preventive and proactive legal risk management for activities related to the Company's business activities so as to minimize significant financial impacts for the Company. The work unit conducts measurement, assessment, and overall monitoring of legal risk through in-depth analysis of litigation factors, weaknesses of the engagement, and the absence/change of legislation from each product or transaction that has been implemented by the Company.

Based on the results of the Company's self-assessment for the position of December 31, 2024, the composite Legal Risk is rated Low to Moderate.

# Strategic Risk

Strategic risk is the potential loss due to inaccuracy in making and/or implementing a strategic decision and failure to anticipate changes in the business environment.
