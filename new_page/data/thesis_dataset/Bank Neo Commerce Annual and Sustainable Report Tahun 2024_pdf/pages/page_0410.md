# Komite Kredit Bermasalah

# Non-Performing Loan Committee

Komite Kredit Bermasalah dibentuk Direksi guna penerapan prinsip Good Corporate Governance dalam pengambilan keputusan terkait penanganan Kredit Bermasalah termasuk dalam mengevaluasi dan memutuskan langkah-langkah penanganan penyelesaian kredit bermasalah.

The Non-Performing Credit Committee was established by the Board of Directors to implement the principles of Good Corporate Governance in making decisions related to the handling of Non-Performing Loans, including in evaluating and deciding on steps to resolve non-performing loans.

# Tugas dan Tanggung Jawab

Duties and Responsibilities

Tugas dari Komite Kredit Bermasalah adalah sebagai berikut:

1. Restrukturisasi pinjaman dalam rangka penyelamatan kredit
2. Penanganan Kredit Bermasalah termasuk namun tidak terbatas pada cash settlement, asset settlement/AYDA
3. Penjualan/Transfer kredit, surat berharga dan AYDA (asset disposal)
4. Hapus buku dan Hapuh tagih
5. Menerima update terkait debitur yang ditangani oleh Fungsi SAM maupun debitur yang masuk dalam program restrukturisasi kredit
6. Memutuskan memo persetujuan lainnya (antara lain seperti perubahan atas struktur pinjaman; jumlah pinjaman; jangka waktu; terms &amp; conditions, agunan/jaminan; pricing; dan hal lainnya yang terkait)

Tanggung Jawab dari Komite Kredit Bermasalah adalah sebagai berikut:

1. Memastikan bahwa penanganan kredit bermasalah telah sesuai dengan kebijakan yang berlaku.
2. Melaksanakan tugas terkait penanganan kredit bermasalah berdasarkan kemahiran profesionalnya secara jujur, obyektif, cermat, konsekuen dan konsisten
3. Menolak permintaan dan/atau pengaruh dari pihak-pihak yang berkepentingan dan/atau pihak-pihak yang berpotensi memiliki konflik kepentingan, terkait dengan pengajuan proposal persetujuan penanganan kredit bermasalah.

The duties of the Non-Performing Loans Committee are as follows:

1. Loan restructuring in the context of credit rescue
2. Troubled Loans handling including but not limited to cash settlement, asset settlement/YDA
3. Sale/Transfer of loans, securities and AYDA (asset disposal)
4. Write-off.
5. Receive updates related to debtors handled by the SAM Function and debtors included in the credit restructuring program
6. Decide on other approval memos (such as changes to loan structure; loan amount; term; terms &amp; conditions, collateral/collateral; pricing; and other related matters)

The Responsibilities of the Non-Performing Loan Committee are as follows:

1. Ensure that the handling of non-performing loans is in accordance with applicable policies.
2. Carry out tasks related to the handling of non-performing loans based on their professional skills honestly, objectively, carefully, consequently and consistently.
3. Reject requests and/or influence from interested parties and/or parties with potential conflicts of interest, related to the submission of proposals for approval of non-performing loan handling.
