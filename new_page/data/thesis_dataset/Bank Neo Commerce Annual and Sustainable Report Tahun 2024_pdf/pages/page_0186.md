Regulation of the Indonesia Deposit Insurance Corporation Number 2 of 2024 on Resolution Plans for Commercial Banks | Dalam PLPS ini mengatur antara lain:
a. Bank wajib menyusun dan menyampaikan Rencana Resolusi kepada LPS setiap 2 (dua) tahun sekali.
b. Bank menyusun Rencana Resolusi
c. Bank wajib menyampaikan kepada LPS:
1) rencana aksi pemulihan yang telah disetujui oleh OJK paling lambat 15 (lima belas) hari kalender sejak Bank menerima persetujuan dari OJK; dan
2) data dan informasi serta dokumen lainnya yang diperlukan LPS.
This regulation governs, among other things:
a. Banks must prepare and submit a Resolution Plan to the Deposit Insurance Corporation (LPS) every 2 (two) years.
b. Banks must prepare the Resolution Plan.
c. Banks are required to submit to the LPS:
1) The recovery action plan that has been approved by OJK, no later than 15 (fifteen) calendar days after the bank receives approval from OJK; and
2) Data, information, and other documents required by the LPS. | Menyusun dan menyampaikan Rencana Resolusi kepada LPS Banks are required to prepare and submit a Resolution Plan to the LPS.  |
