# Komite Aset &amp; Liabilitas (ALCO)

## Asset &amp; Liability Committee (ALCO)

Komite pendukung Direksi dengan tugas menganalisa dan mengevaluasi pengelolaan aset dan liabilitas, serta pengambilan keputusan yang terkait melalui perumusan kebijakan, strategi dan sasaran untuk mengelola aset dan liabilitas Bank secara terintegrasi. Mengurangi dan meminimalisir risiko yang terjadi pada perbankan terutama Risiko Likuiditas, Suku Bunga dan Nilai Tukar.

A supporting committee of the Board of Directors with the task of analyzing and evaluating the management of assets and liabilities, as well as making related decisions through the formulation of policies, strategies and objectives to manage the Bank's assets and liabilities in an integrated manner. Reducing and minimizing the risks that occur in banking, especially Liquidity, Interest Rate and Exchange Rate Risks.

# Tugas dan Tanggung Jawab Komite Aset &amp; Liabilitas (ALCO)

Duties and Responsibilities of Asset &amp; Liability Committee (ALCO)

1. Menentukan strategi pengelolaan aset dan kewajiban serta tingkat suku bunga agar bank dapat memperoleh tingkat pengembalian (return) yang optimal sesuai dengan risk appetite bank.
2. Melakukan analisa dan koordinasi terkait pengelolaan aset dan liabilitas dalam rangka memaksimalkan income bagi bank.
3. Melakukan evaluasi atas tinjauan ekonomi terakhir serta proyeksinya, berkaitan dengan antisipasi atas dampaknya terhadap kondisi aset liabilities Bank.

1. Determine asset and liability management strategies and interest rates so that the bank can obtain an optimal rate of return in accordance with the bank's risk appetite.
2. Conduct analysis and coordination related to asset and liability management in order to maximize income for the bank.
3. Evaluate the latest economic outlook and projections, in anticipation of its impact on the Bank's asset and liability condition.
