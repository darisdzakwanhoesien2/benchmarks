# Komite Pemantau Risiko

Risk Oversight Committee

Komite Pemantau Risiko merupakan Komite yang dibentuk dan bertanggung jawab kepada Dewan Komisaris untuk membantu tugas dan fungsi Dewan Komisaris terkait dengan fungsi pengawasan dan pemberian rekomendasi kepada Direksi agar penerapan manajemen risiko Perseroan memenuhi unsur-unsur kecukupan prosedur dan metodologi pengelolaan risiko, sehingga risiko Perseroan dapat terkendali pada batas/limit yang telah ditetapkan.

The Risk Oversight Committee is a Committee formed and responsible to the Board of Commissioners to assist the duties and functions of the Board of Commissioners related to the supervisory function and providing recommendations to the Board of Directors, so that the committee oversee the company's risk management implementation has meet the with required methodologies to ensure the identified risk can be controlled as per predetermined limit.

# Pedoman dan Tata Tertib (Charter) Komite Pemantau Risiko

Charter of the Risk Oversight Committee

Komite Pemantau Risiko memiliki Pedoman dan Tata Tertib (Charter) yang berisikan antara lain struktur keanggotaan, tugas, tanggung jawab dan wewenang, rapat komite, etika kerja, waktu kerja, serta masa tugas. Pedoman kerja tersebut dikaji secara berkala untuk memastikan cakupan sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku dan perkembangan bisnis Perseroan. Pedoman terakhir disetujui oleh Komite Pemantau Risiko pada bulan November 2023.

The Risk Oversight Committee is governed through a charter that contains, membership structure, duties, responsibilities and authorities, committee meetings, work ethics, working hours, and term of service. The Charter is reviewed periodically to ensure its coverage is in line with the prevailing regulations and the Company's business development. The latest guidelines were approved by the Risk Oversight Committee in November 2023.
