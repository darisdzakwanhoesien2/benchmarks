Sustainability Report

Hingga akhir tahun 2024, ROA dan ROE Perseroan masing-masing tercatat sebesar 0,10% dan 0,59%, mengalami peningkatan jika dibanding tahun 2023 yang sebesar minus 2,99% dan minus 17,56%. Sementara, NIM Perseroan tercatat sebesar 17,30% di tahun 2024, turun dibanding tahun 2023 yang sebesar 18,39%. Kemudian, BOPO Perseroan tercatat sebesar 99,34%, menurun dari 112,27% di tahun 2023. Pencapaian ini menunjukkan bahwa usaha perseroan melakukan ekspansi bisnis disertai dengan efisiensi biaya dapat berjalan dengan baik seperti terlihat dalam kinerja profitabilitas Perseroan.

By the end of 2024, the Company's Return on Assets (ROA) and Return on Equity (ROE) were recorded at 0.10% and 0.59%, respectively, an improvement compared to 2023 of -2.99% and -17.56% respectively. Meanwhile, the Company's Net Interest Margin (NIM) was recorded at 17.30% in 2024, a decrease compared to 2023 of 18.39%. The Company's Cost-to-Income Ratio (BOPO) stood at 99.34%, down from 112.27% in 2023. This achievement demonstrates that the Company's business expansion, coupled with cost efficiency, has been well executed, as reflected in the Company's profitability performance.
