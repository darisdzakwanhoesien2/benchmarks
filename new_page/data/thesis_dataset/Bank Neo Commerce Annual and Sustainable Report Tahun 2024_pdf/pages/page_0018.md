# Kilas Peristiwa 2024
## 2024 Event Highlights

### 9 Januari 2024
### January 9, 2024

**BNC Anniversary 34 tahun &amp; Ekspansi Kantor**
**BNC 34th Anniversary &amp; Office Expansion**

![img-18.jpeg](img-18.jpeg)

Bank Neo Commerce semakin memperkuat komitmennya dalam memberikan pelayanan yang optimal kepada nasabah. Memasuki usia ke 34 tahun, BNC terus meningkatkan digitalisasi proses dan layanannya, dan memperluas jaringan dengan menambah Kantor Fungsional Non Operasional yang berlokasi di SCBD, Jakarta Selatan.

Bank Neo Commerce has solidified its commitment to providing optimal service to customers. Entering its 34th year, BNC continues to improve the digitization of its processes and services, and expands its network by adding a Non-Operational Functional Office located in SCBD, South Jakarta.

### 31 Januari 2024
### January 31, 2024

**Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Luar Biasa**
**Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders**

![img-19.jpeg](img-19.jpeg)

Bank Neo Commerce (BNC) melaksanakan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Luar Biasa (RUPSLB) pertama di tahun 2024, dimana dalam RUPSLB tersebut, Pemegang Saham menyetujui pengunduran diri Bapak Pamitra Wineka dari jabatannya sebagai Komisaris PT Bank Neo Commerce Tbk. RUPSLB ini diadakan di Kantor Pusat BNC, Jakarta, dihadiri oleh jajaran manajemen BNC, baik jajaran Komisaris dan Direksi, serta perwakilan dari para pemegang saham Perseroan.

Bank Neo Commerce (BNC) held the first Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders (EGMS) in 2024, in the EGMS, Shareholders approved the resignation of Mr. Pamitra Wineka from his position as a Commissioner of PT Bank Neo Commerce Tbk. This EGMS was held at the Head Office of BNC, Jakarta, and attended by BNC's management, including the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors, as well as representatives of the Company's shareholders.
