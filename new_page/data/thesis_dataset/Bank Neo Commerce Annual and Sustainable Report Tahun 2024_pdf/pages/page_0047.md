# Peran Direksi dalam Penerapan Kebijakan Strategis

Role of the Board of Directors in the Implementation of Strategic Policies

Direksi memiliki peran penting dalam perumusan strategi dan kebijakan strategis, dimana secara berkala dalam setiap tahunnya telah tertuang dalam Rencana Bisnis Bank (RBB) yang kemudian dilaporkan kepada Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK).

Penyusunan RBB dilakukan Direksi dengan pengawasan dan arahan yang diberikan oleh Dewan Komisaris, serta mengacu pada aspirasi pemegang saham, baik pemegang saham mayoritas maupun pemegang saham minoritas melalui optimalisasi fungsi investor relations.

Hasil rumusan kebijakan strategis Perseroan wajib dijalankan oleh seluruh unit terkait dengan tugas pokok dan fungsi masing-masing unit. Sebagaimana tertuang dalam Pedoman dan Tata Tertib Kerja, Direksi berperan aktif untuk memastikan bahwa setiap unit memahami tugas pokok dan fungsi yang dijalankan agar rencana kerja yang telah ditetapkan dapat tercapai dengan baik. Di samping itu, Direksi juga mengambil peran sebagai pengambil keputusan strategis khususnya terkait beberapa hal yang membutuhkan jenjang pengambilan keputusan tertinggi, baik pengambilan keputusan pada aspek pengembangan produk, bisnis, pemasaran dan penjualan, hingga manajemen risiko dan aspek Tata Kelola Perusahaan. Peranan utama Direksi dalam perumusan strategi di antaranya menganalisis situasi internal dan eksternal perusahaan untuk memahami lingkungan bisnis dan tantangan yang harus dihadapi, serta menentukan tujuan strategis Bank yang menjadi dasar dalam perumusan strategi dan kebijakan Bank.

Seluruh proses perumusan strategi dan keterlibatan Direksi dalam kebijakan strategis tersebut diharapkan dapat membuktikan komitmen Perseroan untuk menjunjung tinggi prinsip kehati-hatian dan penerapan Good Corporate Governance (GCG).

The Board of Directors plays a crucial role in formulating strategies and strategic policies, which are outlined annually in the Bank's Business Plan (RBB) and subsequently reported to the Financial Services Authority (OJK).

The preparation of the RBB is carried out by the Board of Directors under the supervision and guidance of the Board of Commissioners, while also considering the aspirations of the shareholders, both majority and minority shareholders, through the optimization of investor relations.

The results of the Company's strategic policy formulation must be carried out by all units related to the main duties and functions of each unit. As stated in the Code of Conduct, the Board of Directors plays an active role in ensuring that each unit understands its main duties and functions so that the work plan that has been set can be achieved properly. In addition, the Board of Directors also takes on the role of strategic decision maker, especially in relation to matters that require the highest level of decision making, both decision making on aspects of product development, business, marketing and sales, to risk management and aspects of Corporate Governance. The main role of the Board of Directors in strategy formulation includes analyzing the internal and external situation of the company to understand the business environment and challenges that must be faced, as well as determining the Bank's strategic objectives which form the basis for the formulation of the Bank's strategies and policies.

The entire process of strategy formulation and the involvement of the Board of Directors in strategic policies is expected to prove the Company's commitment to upholding the principles of prudence and the implementation of Good Corporate Governance (GCG).
