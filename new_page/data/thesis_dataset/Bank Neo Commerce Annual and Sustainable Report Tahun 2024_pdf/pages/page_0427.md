Throughout 2024, there were 12 (twelve) new and/or revised regulations consisting of 6 (six) Bank Indonesia (BI) Regulations, 5 (five) Financial Services Authority (OJK) Regulations, and 1 (one) other Laws and Regulations impacting the Bank that have been socialized to the management and related work units. The impact assessment of new and/or revised regulations is conducted by the compliance unit together with the relevant work units and the follow-up and fulfillment are jointly monitored by the compliance unit together with the relevant work units.
