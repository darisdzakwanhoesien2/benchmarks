# Penerapan Manajemen Risiko pada Bank yang Mencakup Pengelolaan Risiko Produk dan Aktivitas Baru

Implementation of Risk Management in Banks Covering Risk Management of New Products and Activities

Perseroan telah menerapkan pengelolaan manajemen risiko pada rencana pengembangan produk/aktivitas baru guna meminimalisir potensi kerugian yang mungkin terjadi. Salah satu metode dalam pengelolaan risiko yang diterapkan yakni berupa pengkajian oleh lini kedua Perseroan atas komponen, proposal, dan penilaian risiko secara mandiri yang dajukan oleh unit pemilik atas pengembangan produk/aktivitas baru yang dilakukan berdasarkan ketentuan internal maupun eksternal yang berlaku, adapun pengembangan produk/aktivitas baru disusun oleh unit-unit yang melekat dengan produk/aktivitas pada Perseroan. Hal tersebut merupakan bagian penting yang tidak terpisahkan dari tahap pengembangan produk/aktivitas baru.

Pelaksanaan pengelolaan risiko pada rencana pengembangan produk dan/atau aktivitas baru di Perseroan mencakup kegiatan, antara lain:

1. Tahap penetapan produk atau aktivitas dikategorikan sebagai produk atau aktivitas baru diusulkan oleh pemrakarsa produk (Divisi terkait) dan disampaikan kepada Divisi Kepatuhan, Divisi Credit Risk Management &amp; Governance dan/atau Divisi IT &amp; Operational Risk Management, dan unit kerja lainnya untuk dimintakan opini terkait kelayakan dan kesesuaian atas pengembangan produk dan/atau aktivitas baru.
2. Tahap Risk Assessment dan penyampaian permintaan penilaian kecukupan pengelolaan risiko kepada Divisi/unit kerja terkait Divisi Kepatuhan, Divisi Credit Risk Management &amp; Governance dan/atau Divisi IT &amp; Operational Risk Management dan/atau Divisi Legal dan unit kerja lainnya.

The Company has implemented risk management in the new product/activity development initiative to minimize potential losses that may occur. One of the risk management methods implemented by the Company is through risk review conducted by the second line on the components, proposal, and risk self-assessment for new product/activity development by the related unit, in accordance with applicable internal and external regulations. This process constitutes an essential part of risk governance, whereby the development of new products/activities is carried out by the respective units directly responsible for those products/activities within the Company. This is an important and integral part of the new product/activity development stage.

The implementation of risk management in the Company's plans for new product and/or activity development includes, among others, the following activities

1. The step of determining whether a product or activity is categorized as a new product or activity is proposed by the product proponent (relevant Division) and submitted to the Compliance Division, Credit Risk Management &amp; Governance Division and/or IT &amp; Operational Risk Management Division, and other working units to seek opinions regarding the feasibility and suitability of developing new products and/or activities.
2. The Risk Assessment stage and the submission of requests for risk management adequacy assessment to the Compliance Division, Credit Risk Management &amp; Governance Division and/or IT &amp; Operational Risk Management Division and/or Legal Division and other work units.
