## Kepemilikan Saham Anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi Secara Langsung Maupun Tidak Langsung
Share Ownership of Members of the Board of Commissioners and Directors Directly or Indirectly

Pada periode 1 Januari 2024 hingga 31 Desember 2024, tidak ada anggota Dewan komisaris dan Direksi yang memiliki saham BNC secara langsung, maupun tidak langsung.

Sesuai dengan Peraturan OJK, Perseroan memiliki kebijakan mengenai kepemilikan saham Direksi yang mengatur bahwa anggota Direksi diwajibkan untuk mengungkapkan kepemilikan sahamnya paling lambat 3 (tiga) hari kerja setelah terjadinya kepemilikan atas saham.

In the period from January 1st, 2024 to December 31st, 2024, no member of the Board of Commissioners and Directors owned BNC shares directly or indirectly.

In accordance with OJK Regulations, the Company has a policy regarding the Board of Directors' share ownership which stipulates that members of the Board of Directors are required to disclose their share ownership no later than 3 (three) working days after the share ownership occurs.
