## Pengawasan Aktif Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris

Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi bertanggung jawab atas efektivitas penerapan manajemen risiko, memastikan penerapan manajemen risiko telah memadai sesuai dengan karakteristik, kompleksitas, profil risiko dan memahami dengan baik jenis tingkat risiko yang melekat pada kegiatan bisnis Perseroan.

Direksi melakukan koordinasi dengan tim terkait melalui rapat dalam melaksanakan manajemen risiko di lingkungan Perseroan, seperti rapat Asset Liabilities Committee (ALCO), rapat Direksi, rapat penyelesaian kredit bermasalah, rapat Komite Produk, rapat Komite Manajemen Risiko, rapat Komite Kebijakan Perkreditan Bank, serta rapat-rapat lainnya.

Dewan Komisaris berperan dalam melakukan pengawasan atas penerapan manajemen risiko di Perseroan dengan melakukan pengawasan terhadap kebijakan strategis, pengendalian internal, kepatuhan terhadap ketentuan yang berlaku, kebijakan investasi dan divestasi, target kinerja, kebijakan SDM, pelaksanaan program anti pencucian uang dan pencegahan pendanaan terorisme serta Undang-Undang Tindak Pidana Pencucian Uang (UU-TPPU), kebijakan manajemen risiko, serta pelaksanaan prinsip tata kelola perusahaan dalam setiap kebijakan usaha Perseroan.

Pelaksanaan dan penerapan manajemen risiko Perseroan dilakukan secara berkala oleh Direksi melalui rapat Komite Manajemen Risiko, dan pengawasan atas penerapan manajemen risiko dilakukan secara berkala oleh Dewan Komisaris melalui rapat Komite Pemantau Risiko. ## Active Supervision by Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners

The Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors are responsible for the effectiveness of risk management implementation, ensuring that risk management implementation is adequate in accordance with the characteristics, complexity, risk profile and have understanding of the types and levels of the inherent risk within the Company business activities.

The Directors coordinate with related teams through meetings in implementing risk management within the Company, such as Asset Liabilities Committee (ALCO) meetings, Non-Performing Credit meeting, Product Commiittee meeting, Risk Management Committee meetings, Bank Credit Policy Committee meetings, and other meetings.

The Board of Commissioners oversights the implementation of risk management in the Company by overseeing the execution of the strategic policies, internal controls, Company's compliance towards regulations, investment and divestment policies, performance achievement, human resources policies, the implementation of anti-money laundering programs and terrorism financing prevention, as well as the Anti-Money Laundering Law (AML Law), risk management policies, and the execution of corporate governance principles in all of the Company's business policies.

The Board of Directors held the Risk Management Committee on periodic basis as part of the Company's practice and implementation of Risk Management, while the Board of Commissioners oversight the implementation of of Risk Management Committee and Risk Management implementation in the Company through Risk Oversight Committee.
