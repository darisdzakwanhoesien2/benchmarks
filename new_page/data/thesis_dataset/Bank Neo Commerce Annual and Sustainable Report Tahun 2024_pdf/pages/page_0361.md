# Direksi

## Board of Directors

Direksi adalah organ tata kelola Perseroan yang berperan penting dalam menjalankan pengelolaan usaha Perseroan. Direksi menjalankan tugas secara bersama dan masing-masing sesuai pembidangannya, dengan tujuan mencapai visi dan misi Perseroan, serta meningkatkan nilai tambah bagi pemangku kepentingan dan memastikan keberlanjutan usaha.

Anggota Direksi juga wajib mematuhi Anggaran Dasar Perseroan dan peraturan perundang-undangan serta menjunjung tinggi prinsip-prinsip profesionalisme, efisiensi, transparansi, kemandirian, akuntabilitas, pertanggungjawaban serta kewajaran. Setiap anggota Direksi wajib melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawab dengan itikad baik, penuh tanggung jawab, dan kehati-hatian.

Board of Directors is the Company's governance organs that plays an important role in managing the Company's business. The Board of Directors performs its duties jointly and individually in accordance with its duties, with the aim of achieving the Company's vision and mission, as well as increasing added value for stakeholders and ensuring business sustainability.

Members of the BOD must also comply with the Company's Articles of Association and laws and regulations and uphold the principles of professionalism, efficiency, transparency, independence, accountability, responsibility and fairness. Each member of the BOD shall carry out their duties and responsibilities in good faith, with full responsibility and prudence.

## Piagam Direksi

Board of Directors' Charter

Perseroan memiliki Pedoman dan Tata Tertib Kerja Direksi atau Piagam Direksi (BOD Charter), berisi panduan pelaksanaan tugas, tanggung jawab dan wewenang Direksi. Piagam Direksi Perseroan (BOD Charter) tertuang dalam Surat Keputusan Direksi No. SKEP/013/SET/BNC/V/2021 tanggal 11 Mei 2021 tentang Pedoman dan Tata Tertib Kerja Direksi. Piagam Direksi disusun berdasarkan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan, peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku, dan praktik-praktik terbaik GCG yang digunakan sebagai panduan pelaksanaan tugas, tanggung jawab, dan wewenang Direksi.

The Company has a BOD Charter, which guides the implementation of the duties, responsibilities and authorities of the BOD. The Company's BOD Charter is stipulated in BOD Decree No. SKEP/013/SET/BNC/V/2021 dated May 11, 2021 on the BOD Charter. The BOD Charter is prepared based on the Company's Articles of Association, prevailing laws and regulations, and GCG best practices that are used as guidelines for the implementation of the duties, responsibilities, and authorities of the BOD.
