Sustainability Report

Komposisi Dewan Komisaris dilakukan dengan mempertimbangkan kebutuhan dan kompleksitas Perseroan. Komposisi Dewan Komisaris ditentukan berdasarkan pada keahlian, pengalaman profesional, dan latar belakang masing-masing anggota untuk mendukung efektivitas pelaksanaan tugas Dewan Komisaris, tanpa membedakan jenis kelamin, suku, ras, dan agama.

Perseroan telah menyelenggarakan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Luar Biasa (RUPSLB) pada 30 April 2024 untuk menyetujui:

- Pengangkatan kembali Pramoda Dei Sudarmo sebagai Komisaris Independen Perseroan.
- Pengunduran diri Tjandra M. Gozali sebagai Komisaris Non Independen Perseroan.
- Pengangkatan Kreisna D. Gozali sebagai Komisaris Non Independen Perseroan. Pengangkatan ini telah mendapatkan persetujuan dari Otoritas Jasa Keuangan melalui Keputusan Anggota Dewan Komisioner Otoritas Jasa Keuangan Nomor KEPR-130/D.03/2024 tanggal 30 Oktober 2024 dan telah dikukuhkan kembali dalam Akta Nomor 19 tanggal 4 November 2024.

Perseroan dengan ini menyampaikan penghargaan dan ucapan terima kasih setinggi-tingginya atas pemikiran, kerja keras, serta jasa-jasa Tjandra M. Gozali untuk kemajuan Perseroan.

Perseroan telah menerima pernyataan efektif untuk Inkawan D. Jusi sebagai Komisaris Utama Independen Perseroan dari Otoritas Jasa Keuangan melalui Keputusan Anggota Dewan Komisioner Otoritas Jasa Keuangan Nomor KEPR-10/D.03/2024 tertanggal 5 Februari 2024.

Dengan demikian, susunan anggota Dewan Komisaris Perseroan per 31 Desember 2024 adalah sebagai berikut:

The composition of the Board of Commissioners is determined based on the Company's needs and complexity. It is structured to ensure the effectiveness of the Board's duties, taking into account the members' expertise, professional background, and experience, without discrimination based on gender, ethnicity, race, or religion.

The Company held an Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders (EGMS) on April 30, 2024, which approved the following matters:

- Reappointment of Pramoda Dei Sudarmo as the Company's Independent Commissioner.
- Resignation of Tjandra M. Gozali from his position as Non-Independent Commissioner.
- Appointment of Kreisna D. Gozali as the Company's Non-Independent Commissioner. This appointment was approved by the Financial Services Authority (OJK) through the Decision of the Board of Commissioners of OJK Number KEPR-130/D.03/2024 dated October 30, 2024, and was reaffirmed through Deed Number 19 dated November 4, 2024.

The Company expresses its highest appreciation and gratitude to Tjandra M. Gozali for his invaluable contributions, dedication, and services to the development of the Company.

Additionally, the Company received an effective statement for the appointment of Inkawan D. Jusi as Independent President Commissioner from the Financial Services Authority (OJK) through the Decision of the Board of Commissioners of OJK Number KEPR-10/D.03/2024 dated February 5, 2024.

Accordingly, as of December 31, 2024, the composition of the Company's Board of Commissioners is as follows:

|  Nama Nama | Jabatan Position  |
| --- | --- |
|  Inkawan D. Jusi | Komisaris Utama Independen Independent President Commissioner  |
|  Pramoda Dei Sudarmo | Komisaris Independen Independent Commissioner  |
|  Kreisna D. Gozali | Komisaris Non Independen Non-Independent Commissioner  |

Perubahan susunan ini diharapkan dapat terus memaksimalkan fungsi pengawasan dan dapat memberikan nasihat yang lebih baik lagi. Komposisi Dewan Komisaris di atas telah memenuhi ketentuan Bank Indonesia tentang persyaratan jumlah anggota Komisaris Independen, paling kurang 50% dari seluruh anggota Komisaris Perseroan, serta memenuhi ketentuan OJK mengenai Komisaris Independen.

This change in composition is expected to continue maximizing the supervisory function and provide better advice. The above composition of the Board of Commissioners meets the requirements set by Bank Indonesia regarding the number of Independent Commissioners, which must be at least 50% of the total members of the Board of Commissioners, as well as the regulations of the Financial Services Authority (OJK) concerning Independent Commissioners.
