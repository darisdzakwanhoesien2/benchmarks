Penetapan komposisi Dewan Komisaris dilakukan dengan mempertimbangkan kebutuhan dan kompleksitas Perseroan. Komposisi Dewan Komisaris ditentukan berdasarkan pada keahlian, pengalaman profesional, dan latar belakang masing-masing anggota untuk mendukung efektivitas pelaksanaan tugas Dewan Komisaris, tanpa membedakan jenis kelamin, suku, ras, dan agama.

The determination of the composition of the Board of Commissioners is based on the needs and complexity of the Company. The composition is determined based on the expertise, professional experience, and backgrounds of each member to support the effectiveness of the Board of Commissioners' duties, without discrimination based on gender, ethnicity, race, or religion.

# Program Pengenalan Perseroan bagi Komisaris Baru

Introduction Program for New Commissioners

Perseroan menyelenggarakan program pengenalan Perseroan bagi anggota Dewan Komisaris baru. Tujuan program ini adalah untuk memberikan pengetahuan dan pemahaman tentang Perseroan dan lingkup pekerjaan yang dijalankan. Program pengenalan tersebut mencakup profil, kondisi terkini, kinerja usaha, serta hal-hal lain mengenai Perseroan yang dianggap perlu. Program pengenalan Komisaris baru ini dilaksanakan setiap terdapat anggota baru melalui sejumlah rapat dan/atau materi informasi lainnya.

Program Pengenalan Perseroan bagi Komisaris Baru dilakukan kepada Bapak Inkawan D. Jusi selaku Komisaris Utama Independen dan Bapak Kreisna Dewantara Gozali selaku Komisaris Non Independen.

The Company conducts an introduction program for new members of the Board of Commissioners. The purpose of this program is to provide knowledge and understanding about the Company and the scope of work carried out. The introduction program includes the Company's profile, current conditions, business performance, and other matters deemed necessary. This introduction program is held whenever there is new member through a series of meetings and/or other informational materials.

The Introduction Program for new members was conducted for Mr. Inkawan D. Jusi as the Independent President Commissioner and Mr. Kreisna Dewantara Gozali as the Non-Independent Commissioner.

# Kepemilikan Saham Dewan Komisaris

Shares Ownership by the Board of Commissioners

Perseroan memiliki kebijakan mengenai kepemilikan saham Dewan Komisaris yang mengatur bahwa anggota Dewan Komisaris diwajibkan untuk mengungkapkan kepemilikan sahamnya paling lambat 3 (tiga) hari kerja setelah terjadinya kepemilikan atas saham Perseroan. Kebijakan tersebut telah diimplementasikan melalui pelaporan yang efektif dengan prinsip keterbukaan.

The Company has a policy regarding the ownership of shares by the Board of Commissioners, which stipulates that members of the Board of Commissioners are required to disclose their share ownership no later than 3 (three) business days after acquiring shares in the Company. This policy has been implemented through effective reporting in accordance with the principle of transparency.
