## Manajemen Risiko Pasar

Perseroan menjalankan manajemen risiko pasar dengan melakukan proses identifikasi, pengukuran, pemantauan dan pengendalian risiko pasar melalui analisis terhadap struktur neraca, perkembangan suku bunga pasar, serta analisis pergerakan suku bunga di masa mendatang.

Dalam proses identifikasi risiko pasar, Perseroan melakukan analisa produk dan aktivitas yang terkait aktivitas tresuri melalui proses identifikasi risiko pasar yang melekat pada setiap produk, untuk kemudian dipertimbangkan dan dimitigasi sebelum atau setelah produk diluncurkan. Untuk mendukung proses identifikasi risiko pasar yang kuat, Perseroan memiliki Kebijakan Manajemen Risiko Pasar yang mengatur mengenai proposal, kaji ulang, dan persetujuan atas produk dan aktivitas baru.

## Manajemen Risiko Pasar – Trading Book

Perseroan belum memiliki aktivitas dan produk kategori Trading Book, sehingga tidak ada eksposur atas risiko pasar – Trading Book sampai dengan tanggal 31 Desember 2024.

## Manajemen Risiko Pasar – Banking Book

Pengelolaan risiko pasar pada banking book dilakukan melalui analisis terhadap struktur neraca untuk melihat besaran gap yang terjadi antara Rate Sensitive Asset (RSA) terhadap Rate Sensitive Liabilities (RSL) yang akan mempengaruhi pendapatan dan nilai ekonomis modal Bank. Perhitungan eksposur risiko suku bunga di Banking Book (Interest Rate Risk in the Banking Book/IRRBB) dilakukan mengacu pada SEOJK No.12/SEOJK.03/2018 tentang Penerapan Manajemen Risiko dan Pengukuran Risiko Pendekatan Standar Untuk Risiko Suku Bunga dalam Banking Book bagi Bank Umum.

Perseroan melakukan proses mark to market berdasarkan harga pasar setiap akhir hari. Perseroan juga melakukan monitoring eksposur risiko pasar secara harian sebagai bagian dari mitigasi risiko serta memastikan eksposur tersebut tidak melebihi limit yang telah ditetapkan oleh Direksi dan Komisaris dalam Risk Appetite Bank.

## Market Risk Management

The Company carries out market risk management by identifying, measuring, monitoring and controlling market risk through analysis of the balance sheet structure, development of market interest rates, and analysis of future interest rate movements.

In the process of market risk identification, the Company analyzes products and activities related to treasury activities through a process of identifying inherent market risk in each product, to be considered and mitigated before or after the product is launched. To support a robust market risk identification process, the Company has developed Market Risk Policy, which governed the proposal, review and approval process.

## Market Risk Management – Trading Book

Company does not have Trading Book category activities and products, therefore there is no exposure to market risk – Trading Book as of December 31, 2024.

## Management of Market Risk – Banking Book

Market risk management in the banking book is carried out through analysis of the balance sheet structure to see the amount of gap that occurs between Rate Sensitive Asset (RSA) and Rate Sensitive Liabilities (RSL) which will affect the income and economic value of the Bank's capital. The calculation of interest rate risk exposure in the Banking Book (IRRBB) is carried out in reference to SEOJK No.12/SEOJK.03/2018 regarding Implementation of Risk Management and Standardised Approach to Risk Assessment of Interest Rate Risk in Banking Book for Commercial Banks.

The Company performs a mark to market process based on market prices at the end of each day. The Company also monitors market risk exposure on a daily basis as part of risk mitigation and ensures that the exposure does not exceed the limits set by the Board of Directors and Commissioners in the Bank's Risk Appetite.
