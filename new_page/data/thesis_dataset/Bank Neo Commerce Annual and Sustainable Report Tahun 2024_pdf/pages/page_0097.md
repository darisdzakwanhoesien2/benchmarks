# Penawaran Umum Terbatas IV

Limited Public Offering IV

Pada 30 September 2020, Perseroan mendapatkan persetujuan dari RUPSLB untuk menerbitkan hingga 5.000.000.000 lembar saham baru melalui skema Hak Memesan Efek Terlebih Dahulu (HMETD). Pernyataan efektif untuk pelaksanaan Penawaran Umum Terbatas IV (PUT IV) diterbitkan oleh OJK pada 31 Mei 2021 melalui surat No. S-76/D.04/2021. Dalam PUT IV, Perseroan menawarkan sebanyak 832.724.404 lembar saham baru dengan nilai nominal Rp100 per saham dan harga pelaksanaan Rp300 per saham.

Rasio penerbitan saham dalam PUT IV ditetapkan bahwa setiap pemegang 8 saham memiliki 1 HMETD, di mana setiap 1 HMETD memberikan hak untuk membeli 1 saham baru. Saham yang diterbitkan dalam pelaksanaan PUT IV sebanyak 832.724.404 lembar saham berasal dari portopel Perseroan, mencerminkan modal ditempatkan dan disetor penuh setelah PUT IV, dan seluruh saham tersebut telah resmi dicatatkan di Bursa Efek Indonesia.

On September 30th, 2020, the Company received approval from the EGMS to issue up to 5,000,000,000 new shares through the Pre-emptive Rights (HMETD) scheme. The effective statement for the implementation of the Limited Public Offering IV (PUT IV) was issued by the OJK on May 31, 2021 through letter No. S-76/D.04/2021. In PUT IV, the Company offered 832,724,404 new shares with a nominal value of Rp100 per share and an exercise price of Rp300 per share.

The ratio of share issuance in PUT IV is set that every holder of 8 shares has 1 HMETD, where every 1 HMETD gives the right to buy 1 new share. Shares issued in the implementation of PUT IV amounting to 832,724,404 shares come from the Company's portfolio, reflecting the issued and fully paid capital after PUT IV, and all of these shares have been officially listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange.

# Penawaran Umum Terbatas V

Limited Public Offering V

Pada 28 Mei 2021, Perseroan memperoleh persetujuan dari RUPSLB untuk menerbitkan hingga 5.000.000.000 lembar saham baru. Persetujuan ini diikuti dengan pernyataan efektif dari OJK pada 18 November 2021 melalui surat No. S-208/D.04/2021, yang mengesahkan penerbitan sebanyak 1.927.162.193 lembar saham baru dengan nilai nominal Rp100 per saham dan harga pelaksanaan Rp1.300 per saham.

Rasio penerbitan saham dalam PUT V adalah setiap pemegang 35 saham memperoleh 9 HMETD, di mana setiap 1 HMETD memberikan hak untuk membeli 1 saham baru. Saham hasil pelaksanaan PUT V sebanyak 1.927.162.193 lembar seluruhnya berasal dari portopel Perseroan dan telah resmi dicatatkan di Bursa Efek Indonesia.

On May 28th, 2021, the Company obtained approval from the EGMS to issue up to 5,000,000,000 new shares. This approval was followed by an effective statement from the OJK on November 18, 2021 through letter No. S-208/D.04/2021, which authorized the issuance of 1,927,162,193 new shares with a nominal value of Rp100 per share and an exercise price of Rp1,300 per share.

The ratio of share issuance in PUT V is that every holder of 35 shares receives 9 HMETD, where every 1 HMETD gives the right to buy 1 new share. Shares resulting from the implementation of PUT V amounting to 1,927,162,193 sheets are all from the Company's portfolio and have been officially listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange.
