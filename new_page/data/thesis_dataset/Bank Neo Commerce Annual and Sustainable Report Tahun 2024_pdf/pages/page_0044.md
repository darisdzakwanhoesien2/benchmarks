# Analisa Makro Ekonomi dan Industri Perbankan

Macroeconomic Analysis and Banking Industry

Perekonomian global sepanjang 2024 masih ditandai dengan gejolak geopolitik yang diikuti dengan beberapa fenomena. Walaupun demikian Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) dalam siaran persnya menyatakan bahwa kondisi perekonomian global terjaga sejalan dengan meredanya tekanan di pasar keuangan global terutama setelah kepastian kembali terpilihnya Trump sebagai Presiden Amerika Serikat (AS), serta pelonggaran kebijakan moneter di berbagai negara utama sebagai respons tekanan inflasi yang melambat. Laju penurunan inflasi global yang terus berlanjut khususnya di negara maju seperti AS, mendorong beberapa bank sentral melakukan pemangkasan suku bunga. Sepanjang tahun 2024, The Fed telah memangkas suku bunganya atau FFR sebesar 100 bps terhitung sejak September 2024.

Tentu kondisi ekonomi global yang bergejolak membawa dampak di sisi pasar keuangan, yaitu kondisi likuiditas yang cenderung ketat dan penurunan suku bunga acuan (BI-rate) yang sifatnya lebih terbatas seiring tekanan nilai tukar Rupiah. Lebih lanjut, ketidakpastian geopolitik sepanjang 2024 membuat investor dari berbagai negara berada dalam moda waspada (wait and see) dalam melakukan ekspansi ekonomi.

Throughout 2024, the global economy continued to be marked by geopolitical turbulence, accompanied by several other phenomena. However, the Financial Services Authority (OJK) stated in its press release that the global economic conditions remained stable, particularly with the easing of pressure in global financial markets, especially following the confirmation of Donald Trump's re-election as President of the United States (US), as well as the easing of monetary policies in key countries in response to slowing inflation. The global inflation rate continued to decrease, particularly in advanced economies like the US, prompting several central banks to lower interest rates. Throughout 2024, the Federal Reserve (The Fed) reduced its Federal Funds Rate (FFR) by 100 basis points (bps), starting in September 2024.

Of course, the volatile global economic condition has an impact on the financial market side, namely liquidity conditions that tend to be tight and a more limited reduction in the benchmark interest rate (BI-rate) in line with the pressure on the Rupiah exchange rate. Furthermore, geopolitical uncertainty throughout 2024 has put investors from various countries in wait-and-see mode for economic expansion.

Indonesia's economy, on the other hand, remained stable at the end of 2024 despite rising uncertainties and global challenges such as geo-economic fragmentation, geopolitical tensions, and a global economy projected to slow down. Indonesia's economic growth in 2024 was recorded at 5.03%, slightly slower than the previous year at 5.05%. Growth was seen in all expenditure components, especially in exports of goods and services.
