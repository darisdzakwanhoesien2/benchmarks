# Struktur Organ Tata Kelola Perusahaan Waskita / Corporate Governance Structure of Waskita

![img-158.jpeg](img-158.jpeg)

# ORGAN PERUSAHAAN

Pada struktur tata kelola perseroan terdapat organ-organ utama, yang terdiri dari RUPS, Board of Commissioners dan Board of Directors. Masing-masing organ tersebut memiliki fungsi, tugas, dan tanggung jawab yang jelas, diatur sesuai Anggaran Dasar serta peraturan/undang-undang yang berlaku. Organ-organ pendukung Board of Commissioners secara khusus menjalankan peran dukungan bagi Board of Commissioners dalam pelaksanaan fungsi pengawasan pengelolaan Perseroan. Sementara organ-organ pendukung Board of Directors menjalankan peran dukungan bagi Board of Directors dalam melaksanakan pengelolaan operasional Perseroan.

Waskita memiliki fungsi khusus sebagai penanggung jawab dalam penerapan dan pemantauan Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik, yang telah ditetapkan Surat Keputusan Board of Directors PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk No. 35/SK/WK/2021 tanggal 29 Oktober 2021 tentang Perubahan Struktur Organisasi Corporate Office &amp; Business Unit PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk. Berdasarkan Surat Keputusan tersebut, Social and Environmental (SER) Unit memiliki fungsi khusus sebagai penanggung jawab penerapan dan pemantauan Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik. Social and Environmental Unit secara khusus bertanggung jawab kepada Director of Finance Perseroan.

# CORPORATE BODIES

In the corporate governance structure, the main bodies consist of the GMS, the Board of Commissioners, and the Board of Directors. Each of these bodies is assigned with clear functions, duties, and responsibilities, regulated in accordance with the Articles of Association and applicable regulations/laws. The Board of Commissioners supporting organs are specifically assigned to support the Board of Commissioners in carrying out the supervisory function of the management of the Company, while the Board of Directors supporting organs assists the Board of Directors in carrying out the operational management of the Company.

Waskita has dedicated a function as a party in charge of implementing and monitoring Good Corporate Governance, which is stipulated through the Decree of the Board of Directors of PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk No. 02/SK/WK/2020 dated December 22, 2020 concerning Revision of Changes in Organizational Nomenclature and Positions in the Organizational Structure of PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk. In accordance with the Decree, the Social and Environmental (SER) Unit has a special function in charge of implementing and monitoring Good Corporate Governance. The Social and Environmental (SER) Unit is directly responsible to the Company's Director of Finance.
