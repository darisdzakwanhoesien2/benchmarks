Sustainable Governance
WASKITA

Dalam rangka menciptakan prinsip korporasi yang sehat, Perseroan mengoptimalkan kerangka dan sistem GCG untuk mewujudkan kegiatan bisnis yang adil dan jujur, melalui kebijakan Anti Korupsi dan Gratifikasi, Kode Etik Perilaku (Code of Conduct), dan Sistem Pelaporan Pelanggaran (Whistleblowing System). Ketiga sistem tersebut diperkuat dengan kerangka pengelolaan dan mitigasi risiko yang sesuai dengan profil risiko Perseroan, berikut kajian terhadap penerapan pengendalian internal yang dilakukan oleh Auditor Eksternal secara berkala. Organ tata kelola dalam Perseroan telah berperan dalam mendukung penerapan pengendalian internal dan manajemen risiko secara berkesinambungan, antara lain Risk Management Division Korporat yang mengkoordinir dan memantau upaya mitigasi risiko. Internal Audit bertugas mengevaluasi penerapan audit internal dan manajemen risiko, serta Komite Dewan Komisaris mengawasi penerapannya.

In creating sound corporate principles, the Company optimized its GCG system and framework to realize a fair and honest business activities through Anti-Corruption and Gratification, Code of Conduct, and Whistleblowing System. Those three systems are strengthened by the management and mitigation framework that is in line with the Company's risk profile. The review on the internal prevention implementation is conducted by an External Auditor continuously. The Company's governance organs which have acted in support of implementation of internal prevention and risk management continuously are namely the corporate Risk Management Division that coordinated and monitored the efforts of risk mitigations, the Internal Audit that evaluated the implementation of internal audit and risk management, as well as the Committee of the Board of Commissioners that supervised the implementation.

# RANTAI PASOKAN

Supply Chain

[3-3]

Untuk mewujudkan tujuan Perusahaan, kegiatan operasional Waskita dilakukan secara bekerja sama dengan pemasok/vendor baik barang maupun jasa melalui prosedur pengadaan barang dan jasa yang berlaku. Perseroan berupaya menjunjung prinsip adil, transparan, dan profesional dalam proses pengadaan barang dan jasa yang dilakukan, baik langsung maupun tidak langsung. Perseroan juga menghindari adanya praktik suap dan gratifikasi pada pengadaan barang dan jasa guna mencegah terjadinya pelanggaran dan kecurangan yang dapat merugikan Perseroan. Adapun ruang lingkup rantai pasokan Waskita meliputi pengadaan material dan jasa, logistik, pelaporan dan kepatuhan, dan jasa-jasa lainnya.

In achieving the Company's objectives, Waskita cooperates with suppliers/vendors of products and services through applicable procurement procedures. The Company upholds the principles of fairness, transparency, and professionalism in both direct and indirect procurement process. The Company also avoid the practices of bribery and gratuities in its procurement to prevent any offenses and frauds that could bring disadvantages to the Company. Waskita's scope of supply chain consists of material and service procurement, logistics, reporting and compliance, and other services.
