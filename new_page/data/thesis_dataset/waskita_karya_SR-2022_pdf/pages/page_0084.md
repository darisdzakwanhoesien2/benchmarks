# INISIATIF EKSTERNAL

External Initiatives

Waskita memegang teguh komitmen untuk senantiasa mematuhi ketentuan yang berlaku dan standar mutu yang berlaku sesuai bidang usaha Perseroan, baik standar yang berlaku di Indonesia maupun standar secara internasional. Beberapa sertifikasi telah diperoleh oleh Waskita dalam rangka menjaga standar mutu antara lain Sertifikat ISO 9001:2015 (Sistem Manajemen Mutu), OHSAS 18001:2007 yang bermigrasi ke ISO 45001:2018 dan SNI ISO 37001:2016 (Sistem Manajemen Anti Penyuaapan), serta ISO 14001:2015 (Sistem Manajemen Lingkungan).

Waskita is committed to complying with all applicable regulations and quality standards regarding the Company's line of business, both national and international standards. A number of certifications have been obtained by Waskita in order to maintain quality standards, including ISO 9001: 2015 Certificate (Quality Management System), OHSAS 18001: 2007 which migrated to ISO 45001: 2018 and SNI ISO 37001: 2016 (Anti-Bribery Management System), as well as ISO 14001:2015 (Environmental Management System)
