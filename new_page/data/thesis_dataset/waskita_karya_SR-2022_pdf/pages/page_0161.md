# PENGELOLAAN RISIKO TERKAIT ASPEK POLITIK, EKONOMI, SOSIAL, TEKNOLOGI, LINGKUNGAN HIDUP DAN HUKUM

Risk Management related to Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental and Legal Aspects

[E-3]

Pengelolaan risiko yang dijalankan Perseroan dijalankan secara menyeluruh, meliputi aspek politik, ekonomi, sosial, teknologi, lingkungan hidup, dan hukum. Perseroan melakukan pengelolaan risiko yang berkaitan dengan aspek politik, seperti pergantian pejabat pemerintahan, kondisi lingkungan politik, baik di Indonesia, maupun di dunia yang dapat mempengaruhi kondisi bisnis Perusahaan secara signifikan.

Risiko yang berkaitan dengan aspek ekonomi secara umum dipengaruhi oleh pertumbuhan ekonomi, inflasi, nilai tukar rupiah terhadap mata uang asing, suku bunga, situasi pasar, serta ketidakstabilan moneter yang terjadi di dunia. Perusahaan menjalankan pengelolaan risiko ekonomi dengan melakukan pengembangan usaha baru melalui proses penyusunan feasibility Study yang kuat sebagai upaya menghadirkan value dan return secara maksimal bagi Perseroan.

Selain itu, Waskita mengupayakan perintisan proyek lain di luar target sebagai cadangan upaya memperluas peluang pasar konstruksi, termasuk sejumlah proyek dalam Master Percepatan dan Perluasan Pembangunan Ekonomi Indonesia (MP3EI), mencari alternatif-alternatif skema investasi, melakukan inventarisasi aset secara berkala, hingga melaksanakan roadshow terkait pelaksanaan divestasi kepada calon investor.

Sementara pengelolaan risiko terkait aspek sosial secara umum dilatarbelakangi oleh harapan terhadap peningkatan kondisi lingkungan sekitar, peningkatan tenaga lokal, hingga pemberdayaan sumber daya lokal di sekitar area operasional. Perusahaan terus-menerus melakukan mitigasi risiko aspek sosial, yang meliputi peningkatan kualitas komunikasi dan sosialisasi terkait lingkungan, pelibatan tenaga kerja dan mitra kerja lokal pada pekerjaan penunjang demi mengurangi tingkat disparitas sosial yang dapat menimbulkan potensi risiko.

The risk management carried out by the Company is carried out in a comprehensive manner covering political, economic, social, technological, environmental and legal aspects. The Company carries out risk management related to political aspects, such as changes in government officials, political environmental conditions, both in Indonesia and in the world, which can significantly affect the Company's business conditions.

Risks related to economic aspects are generally influenced by economic growth, inflation, the rupiah exchange rate against foreign currencies, interest rates, market conditions, and global monetary instability. The company Carries out economic risk management by developing new businesses through preparing a strong feasibility study as an effort to provide maximum value and return for the Company.

Waskita also strives to pioneer other projects outside the target as a backup effort to expand construction market opportunities, including carrying out a number of projects in the Master for the Acceleration and Expansion of Indonesian Economic Development (MP3EI), seeking for alternative investment schemes, conducting regular asset inventories, and carrying out roadshows related to the implementation of divestments to potential investors.

Meanwhile, risk management on social and environmental aspects is generally motivated with expectations for improving the condition of the local environment, increasing local labor, and empowering local resources around the operational area. The Company continuously mitigates the social risk through improving the quality of communication and outreach related to the environment, involving the local labor and business partners in supporting work to reduce social disparity that can cause potential risks.
