In the fourth quarter of 2022, Waskita Karya focused on increasing operational productivity, including several projects in the National Capital City (IKN) which were won by the Company in the tender process. The Company targets an acceleration of the existing project progress. Waskita's commitment is not only for generating profits but also to maintaining the trust that has been given by the Government and service users by paying attention to the quality and time of work according to the contract.

With the improved performance, Waskita is expected to be able to maintain the trust of all stakeholders and become a SOE with sound financial performance. The Company also encourages all Company personnel to consistently carry out the transformation process, refocusing resources, implementing digitization in all business processes, and strengthening Good Corporate Governance (GCG) as a shared commitment.

On the other hand, in 2022 the Company has carried out its compliance with tax-related laws and regulations. The Company has paid taxes in a timely manner. This also serves as a real contribution of the Company to the country's development. The Company has made tax payments of Rp2,873,677,149,493 or an increase compared to the previous year of $307.26\%$. The tax payments include Value Added Tax (VAT), Employee and Corporate Income Tax, Land and Building Tax, Regional Tax as well as BBN and Vehicle Tax. In addition, the Company has also carried out its obligations to shareholders by paying dividends and contributing to regional development through a number of infrastructure projects undertaken by the Company.
