## STRUKTUR TATA KELOLA

Untuk mendukung penerapan Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik, Perusahaan memiliki struktur tata kelola Perseroan yang merujuk pada Undang-Undang No. 40 tahun 2007 tentang Perseroan Terbatas. Adapun struktur tata kelola perseroan terdiri atas 3 (tiga) organ utama, yaitu Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS), Board of Commissioners, dan Board of Directors. Board of Commissioner dalam menjalankan fungsinya dibantu oleh organ-organ pendukung, yakni Board of Commissioners Committee. Sementara Board of Directors dibantu oleh organ-organ pendukung seperti Corporate Secretary dan Internal Audit.

## GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE

In the implementation of Good Corporate Governance, the Company's governance structure is prepared pursuant to the applicable laws, including Law No. 40 of 2007 concerning Limited Liability Companies. The Company's governance structure consists of 3 (three) main bodies, namely the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS), the Board of Commissioners, and the Board of Directors. The Board of Commissioners is assisted by supporting organs in carrying out its functions, namely the Board of Commissioners Committees, while the Board of Directors is assisted by supporting organs such as the Corporate Secretary and Internal Audit.
