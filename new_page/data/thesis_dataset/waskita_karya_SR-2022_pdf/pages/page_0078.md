WASKITA

The Company has prepared an organizational structure through a comprehensive review to ensure that the organizational structure is in line with the company's vision and mission. In addition, the assessment of the process of preparing the organizational structure has also taken into account the development needs of the Company's organization and the dynamics of the industry it is facing. The Company determines the organizational structure through the approval of the Board of Commissioners and the approval of the Board of Directors, which was last amended and stipulated through the Decree of the Board of Directors of PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk No.34/SK/WK/2022 dated August 1, 2022 concerning Changes in the Organizational Structure and Positions in the Organizational Structure of PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk Company.
