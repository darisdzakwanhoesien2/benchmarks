## STRATEGI KEBERLANJUTAN MEWUJUDKAN PEMBANGUNAN BERKELANJUTAN

Untuk dapat menjaga kelangsungan bisnis, PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk berkomitmen untuk menerapkan strategi keberlanjutan secara konsisten. Dengan menerapkan komitmen tersebut secara sungguh-sungguh, Perseroan dapat mempertahankan keberadaannya dan terus tumbuh menjadi Perusahaan yang andal, terpercaya, dan terdepan di bidang konstruksi terintegrasi dan investasi. Perseroan menerapkan kebijakan strategi keberlanjutan dengan memperkuat Tata Kelola, Risk, dan Culture yang mengintegrasikan Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) dalam setiap proses bisnis, serta dijalankan dalam Grand Strategy yang terbagi menjadi 5 (lima) aspek strategi, yaitu diversifikasi pendapatan, efisiensi biaya, pengelolaan pendapatan, peningkatan kapabilitas internal, serta pengelolaan risiko.

Untuk menjaga kualitas kinerja sekaligus mewujudkan zero accident, Perseroan secara berkala mendorong seluruh insan Waskita Karya untuk meningkatkan kompetensinya di bidang konstruksi. Melalui upaya ini, Perseroan dapat meraih pertumbuhan yang positif seiring dengan upaya peningkatan kebijakan investasi demi memaksimalkan pencapaian kinerja Perseroan. Berbagai upaya strategi keberlanjutan yang dijalankan Perseroan di sepanjang tahun 2022 diharapkan dapat mewujudkan kinerja keberlanjutan secara optimal yang meliputi aspek ekonomi, sosial, lingkungan, dan tata kelola. Untuk itu, Waskita Karya bertekad untuk menjaga komitmennya dalam peningkatan penerapan Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik (GCG) &amp; manajemen risiko di setiap lini Perusahaan untuk memperkuat fundamental Perseroan.

On this occasion, the Company would like to present the Sustainability Report for the 2022 fiscal year which has been prepared according to the GRI Standard and the provisions in POJK No. 51/POJK.03/2017 concerning the Implementation of Sustainable Finance for Financial Services Institutions, Issuers and Public Companies. This Sustainability Report is also a concrete manifestation of the Company's commitment to fulfilling its responsibility for information disclosure to all stakeholders, particularly through delivering economic, social and environmental performance. In addition, through this report the Company presents economic, social, and environmental performance for 2022, as well as description of the Company's contribution to infrastructure development in the country.

## SUSTAINABILITY STRATEGY IN REALIZING SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT

In maintaining business continuity, PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk is committed to consistently implementing sustainability strategy. Through this commitment, the Company is able to maintain its existence and continue to grow to become a reliable, trustworthy, and leading company in integrated construction and investment. The Company implements a sustainability strategy policy by integrating Governance, Risk, and Culture (GRC) in every business process in a Grand Strategy which is divided into 5 (five) strategic aspects, namely income diversification, cost efficiency, revenue management, internal capability improvement, and risk management.

To maintain the quality of performance while also realizing zero accidents, the Company regularly encourages all Waskita Karya personnel to improve their competence in the construction sector. Through these efforts, the Company can achieve positive growth in line with efforts to improve investment policies in order to maximize the achievement of the Company's performance. The various sustainability strategy efforts carried out by the Company throughout 2022 are expected to be able to realize optimal sustainability performance which includes economic, social, environmental and governance aspects. For this reason, Waskita Karya is determined to maintain its commitment to increasing the implementation of Good Corporate Governance (GCG) &amp; risk management in every line of the Company to strengthen the Company's fundamentals.
