# Portfolio &amp; Innovation

Strategi ini diharapkan dapat meningkatkan perolehan proyek konstruksi konvensional (non-turnkey) melalui peningkatan bid win rate atas tender yang diikuti serta perluasan pangsa pasar ke luar negeri dan peningkatan kapabilitas di champion segment Waskita (Water Infrastructure &amp; Airport). Selain itu, Perusahaan juga berusaha mengoptimalkan portofolio investasi yang dikelola melalui portfolio rebalancing. This strategy is expected to increase the acquisition of conventional (non-turnkey) construction projects by increasing the tender winning rate for participating tenders as well as expanding market share overseas and increasing capability in the Waskita (Water Infrastructure &amp; Airport) champion segment. In addition, the Company also seeks to optimize the investment portfolio which is managed through portfolio rebalancing.

# Lean

Strategi ini bertujuan untuk memastikan capaian margin laba sesuai target/rencana dan meningkatkan daya saing melalui sentralisasi pengadaan, optimalisasi sistem procurement &amp; supply chain management, design &amp; engineering, penerapan lean construction serta implementasi claim &amp; change management. This strategy aims to ensure the achievement of profit margins according to targets/plans and increase competitiveness through procurement centralization, optimization of procurement &amp; supply chain management systems, design &amp; engineering, application of lean construction and implementation of claims &amp; change management.

# Digital

Strategi ini berfokus dalam optimalisasi teknologi guna mendukung core competency sehingga dapat berkontribusi dalam meningkatkan daya saing dan kinerja Perusahaan. Optimalisasi digital ini antara lain dilakukan dengan mengembangkan sistem/aplikasi/teknologi/digital security program serta inovasi dalam proses bisnis Perusahaan.

This strategy focuses on optimizing technology to support core competency to increase competitiveness and performance of the Company. This digital optimization is carried out, among others, by developing systems/applications/technology/digital security programs as well as innovations in the Company's business processes.

# Financial

Strategi ini bertujuan untuk memperbaiki fundamental keuangan Perusahaan dan menerapkan perencanaan serta pelaksanaan pengelolaan keuangan yang terstruktur, akurat &amp; terkendali. Beberapa langkah dalam memperbaiki fundamental keuangan Perusahaan melalui restrukturisasi keuangan Waskita Grup, dukungan Pemerintah melalui PMN dan penjaminan Pemerintah terkait Obligasi/Sukuk dan KMK, serta divestasi atas kepemilikan jalan tol, serta didukung dengan percepatan kolektibilitas piutang pekerjaan. This strategy aims to improve the Company's financial fundamentals and implement structured, accurate &amp; controlled planning and implementation of financial management. Measures taken to improve the Company's financial fundamentals through Waskita Group financial restructuring, include Government support through PMN and Government guarantees related to Bonds/Sukuk and KMK, as well as divestment of toll road ownership, and supported by accelerating the collectibility of work receivables.

# Risk Management

Strategi ini bertujuan untuk mengoptimalkan pengendalian risiko sebagai pertimbangan utama dalam pengambilan keputusan yang dapat diukur melalui peningkatan skor Risk Maturity Index. Beberapa langkah dalam optimalisasi Risk Management antara lain melalui pengembangan framework Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) serta meningkatkan kesadaran &amp; kapabilitas seluruh elemen di Perusahaan terkait risiko. This strategy aims to optimize risk control as a major consideration in decision making which can be measured through an increase in the Risk Maturity Index score. Measures in optimizing Risk Management include developing an Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) framework and raising awareness &amp; capability of all elements in the Company regarding risk.

# Talent Engine

Merupakan strategi yang bertujuan untuk mengembangkan talenta SDM dan memastikan ketersediaan talent di seluruh lini bisnis Perusahaan melalui penyesuaian struktur organisasi agar menjadi lebih fleksibel, efisien &amp; resilient terhadap perubahan situasi, pengembangan kapabilitas &amp; pemenuhan kapasitas SDM yang dapat mendukung proses bisnis, serta pengembangan sistem pengelolaan SDM yang terintegrasi. This strategy aims to develop HC talent and ensure the availability of talent in all business lines of the Company through adjusting the organizational structure to make it more flexible, efficient &amp; resilient to changing situations, develop HC capabilities &amp; fulfill HC capacity to support business processes, as well as develop an integrated HR management system.
