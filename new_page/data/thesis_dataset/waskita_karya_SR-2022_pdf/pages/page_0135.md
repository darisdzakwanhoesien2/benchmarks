Peningkatan konsumsi air dan energi disebabkan, antara lain pemberlakuan kebijakan WFO di tahun 2022, penambahan jumlah SDM dan unit kerja/pengembangan organisasi, penempatan kembali sejumlah pegawai yang sebelumnya menyebar pada unit-unit bisnis/anak perusahaan, serta pelaksanaan rapat-rapat dan kegiatan Perusahaan telah dilakukan secara offline. Adapun jumlah karyawan Corporate Office di kantor pusat di tahun 2022 terjadi peningkatan yang mencapai 1.921 orang, dari sebelumnya di tahun 2021 sebanyak 1.895 orang.

The increase in water consumption was due to, among others, the implementation of the WFO policy in 2022, increase in human capital and work units/organizational development, reassignment of a number of employees who were previously spread across business units/subsidiaries, and offline meetings and activities of the Company. In additions, Corporate Office employees at the head office increased in 2022 to 1.921 employees from 1.895 employees in 2021.

Material Recycling

Waskita is committed to using recyclable materials through repeated use of materials for manufacturing processes, such as the manufacture of precast concrete.
