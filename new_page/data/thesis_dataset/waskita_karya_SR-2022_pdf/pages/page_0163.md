Laporan Kieberlanjutan 2022 Sustainability Report | PT Waskita Karya (Perseroi Tbk)

Perseroan. Melalui pelaksanaan Pedoman Perilaku tersebut, diharapkan dapat menjaga sekaligus memastikan Perseroan ataupun pegawai untuk dapat bertindak sesuai nilai-nilai dan standar etika yang berlaku sebagai budaya perusahaan (corporate culture) Waskita.

Pelaksanaan kebijakan penerapan kode etik yang dijalankan Perseroan mengacu pada Pedoman Etika dan Perilaku (Code of Conduct) Insan Waskita No. 85.1/SK/WK/2020 tanggal 22 September 2020 tentang Prosedur Waskita di Bidang Etika dan Perilaku. Pedoman tersebut mengatur hubungan antara Perseroan dengan unsur pada lingkungan internal yang mencakup Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, dan pegawai, serta lingkungan eksternal meliputi pemegang saham, pelanggan, pemasok, subkontraktor, pemberi jasa, pesaing, media masa, penyelenggara negara, dan masyarakat.

Waskita senantiasa melakukan optimalisasi pencegahan pelanggaran Code of Conduct. Upaya tersebut direalisasikan melalui Sistem Pelaporan Pelanggaran (Whistleblowing System) yang mengacu pada penerapan prinsip transparansi, dengan pelaksanaan yang menjamin perlindungan terhadap pelapor. Kebijakan Whistleblowing System Waskita telah diatur melalui Surat Keputusan Board of Directors No. 03.6/SK/WK/2019 tanggal 26 Maret 2019, tentang Pedoman Sistem Pelaporan Pelanggaran (SPP)/Whistleblowing System (WBS) PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk. Perusahaan berharap, seluruh pegawai hingga sejumlah unsur pemangku kepentingan lain Perseroan turut berkontribusi memanfaatkan sistem pelaporan pelanggaran ini.

Dalam perjalanan bisnisnya, Perseroan selalu berupaya meminimalisasi sekaligus mencegah terjadinya risiko terhadap reputasi di samping menjalankan langkah peningkatan kepercayaan masyarakat. Upaya tersebut dilakukan dengan senantiasa menindak respons cepat, tanggap, dan tepat terhadap pengaduan pelanggaran yang disampaikan oleh pemangku kepentingan sesuai ketentuan yang berlaku. Di samping itu, Perseroan secara konsisten melaksanakan pengawasan terhadap efektivitas mekanisme pengaduan pelanggaran dan memastikan pengawasan terhadap seluruh elemen penerapan sistem pelaporan pelanggaran berjalan baik, melalui sosialisasi dan komunikasi secara berkala. Sebagai bentuk penghargaan bagi pelapor yang terbukti, Perusahaan memberikan penghargaan tertentu sebagai reward, menjamin perlindungan bagi pelapor, memantau efektivitas dan perbaikan program, serta secara berkala melakukan maintain and ensure that the Company or its employees are able to act in accordance with the values and ethical standards of Waskita's corporate culture.

The Company's code of conduct implementation policy refers to the Waskita Personnel Code of Conduct No. 85.1/SK/WK/2020 dated September 22, 2020 concerning Waskita Procedures in Ethics and Behavior. These guidelines regulate the relationship between the Company and elements in the internal environment, including the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, and employees, as well as the external parties including shareholders, customers, suppliers, subcontractors, service providers, competitors, mass media, state administrators, and the public.

Waskita always optimizes the prevention of Code of Conduct violations through the Whistleblowing System in accordance with the principle of transparency that ensures the protection of whistleblowers. Waskita's Whistleblowing System policy is regulated in the Decree of the Board of Directors No. 03.6/SK/WK/2019 dated March 26, 2019, concerning Guidelines for the Whistleblowing System (WBS) of PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk. All employees and other stakeholders are invited to utilized the whistleblowing system.

In its business, the Company continuously strives to minimize potential risks of its other than making efforts to increase public trust, by always maintaining a fast, responsive, and precise response to complaints of violations submitted by stakeholders in accordance with applicable regulations. Furthermore, the Company consistently monitors the effectiveness of the violation complaint mechanism in addition to ensuring safeguards for all elements of the implementation of the whistleblowing system through regular dissemination and internalization. The Company provides rewards for proven whistleblowers, guarantees protection for whistleblowers, monitors program effectiveness and improvement, and periodically carries out benchmarking to measure the quality of Waskita's Whistleblowing System implementation compared to systems of other companies in the same industry on an equivalent level.
