The Company has equipped the office area with a number of support devices, such as body temperature detection devices, QR code scanning for the PeduliLindungi application, hand sanitizers in a number of rooms, and hand washing facilities in easily accessible places.

The Company also enforces WFH and WFO policies by adjusting employee attendance schedules without reducing work productivity. This program is carried out by taking into account the Public Restriction (PPKM) policy. In addition, Waskita requires every Waskita employee with virus symptoms or physical contact with Covid-19 patients to take antigen swab test. To monitor the situation of the spread of the virus while ensuring the health of employees, the Company requires every employee to periodically fill out health questionnaires and use the PeduliLindungi application when entering the office area.

Waskita also encouraged all of the Company's personnel to be vaccinated as an effort to prevent the spread of Covid-19, in collaboration with the local Puskesmas. Other precautions carried out by the Company include providing vitamin supplements to increase immunity in preventing Covid-19.
