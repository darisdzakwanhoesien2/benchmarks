# BIAYA PENGEMBANGAN KOMPETENSI DAN DURASI WAKTU PELATIHAN

Di TAHUN 2022,
REALISASI BIAYA PELAKSANAAN PROGRAM PENGEMBANGAN KOMPETENSI SDM YANG DIKELUARKAN OLEH PERSEROAN SEBESAR RP12.101.817.664,-, ATAU MENINGKAT DIBANDING TAHUN SEBELUMNYA YANG MENCAPAI RPS.512.588.651,-. HAL INI DIPENGARUHI OLEH PENINGKATAN SERTIFIKASI PEGAWAI TERKAIT KEBUTUHAN KEGIATAN OPERASIONAL PERUSAHAAN.

Sementara itu, di tahun 2022 Perseroan merealisasikan jam pelatihan sejumlah 55,60 jam/orang/tahun. Jumlah tersebut diketahui lebih tinggi dibanding realisasi pada tahun 2021, yang sebesar 55,50 jam/orang/tahun. Peningkatan ini disebabkan peningkatan sertifikasi pegawai terkait kebutuhan kegiatan operasional Perusahaan.

## PENILAIAN PEGAWAI DAN PENGEMBANGAN KARIER

UPAYA PERSEROAN MENCIPTAKAN SDM BERKUALITAS JUGA DIWUJUDKAN MELALU PROGRAM PENILAIAN TERHADAP SOFT COMPETENCY INDIVIDU BAGI SELURUH KARYAWAN DAN PROSES EVALUASI SECARA OBJEKTIF TERHADAP KINERJA YANG DIHASILKAN. ADAPUN PENILAIAN SOFT COMPETENCY MENCAKUP ASPEK KEJUJURAN, KEDISIPLINAN, TANGGUNG JAWAB, PENGETAHUAN, INISIATIF, KECAKAPAN, KEPEMIMPINAN, HUBUNGAN DENGAN REKAN KERJA, KEPATUHAN, HINGGA OUTPUT YANG DIHASILKAN.

## COMPETENCY DEVELOPMENT COSTS AND TRAINING HOURS

[404-1]

In 2022, the Company's HC competency development program has realized a total cost of Rp12,101,817,664, an increase compared to the realization in the previous year which was Rp5.51 billion. This was due to an increase in employee certifications on the Company's operational needs.

In addition, the training hours realized by the Company in 2022 is a total of 55.60 hours/person/year, an increase compared to the realization in 2021 of 55.50 hours/person/year. This increase was due to an increase in employee certifications on the Company's operational needs.

## EMPLOYEE ASSESSMENT AND CAREER DEVELOPMENT

[404-3]

The Company's efforts to create quality human capital are also realized through assessment program of individual soft competency for all employees and an objective evaluation process of performance. The soft competencies assessed include aspects of honesty, discipline, responsibility, knowledge, initiative, skills, leadership, relationships with colleagues, compliance, to output result.
