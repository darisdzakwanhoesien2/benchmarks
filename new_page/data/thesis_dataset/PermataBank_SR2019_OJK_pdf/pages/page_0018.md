# Komitmen Penerapan Keberlanjutan PermataBank

PermataBank telah menerapkan keuangan berkelanjutan per 1 Januari 2019 sesuai dengan ketentuan yang dimuat dalam POJK 51/2017. Strategi penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan ini dituangkan dalam RAKB yang memuat program 1 tahun dan 5 tahun.

Pada tahun 2019, prioritas penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan adalah pengembangan produk dan/atau jasa keuangan berkelanjutan termasuk peningkatan portofolio pembiayaan, investasi atau penempatan pada instrumen keuangan atau proyek yang sejalan dengan penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan. Terdapat 6 program yang diuraikan dalam RAKB tersebut yaitu:

1. Peningkatan penyaluran keuangan inklusif.
2. Pembiayaan bagi Usaha Kecil Menengah (UKM) yang sejalan dengan penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan.
3. Peningkatan transaksi dan distribusi produk jasa keuangan secara elektronik.
4. PermataTabungan iB Haji.
5. Layanan Satu Atap Haji.
6. Kebijakan/prosedur yang mendukung pengembangan produk dan/atau jasa keuangan berkelanjutan.

- Social Values: accomplished through the creation of positive impacts on society through financial literacy education and sustainable empowerment, supported by the Employee Volunteerism (EVO) program.
- Environmental Values: accomplished through the application of accountable financing regulation for companies that implement good corporate governance and meeting the applicable norms and regulations.

PermataBank has policies related to lending and financing that cover the social and environmental aspects in them. Approved loan proposals must pass the Environment and Social Requirement Assessment (ESRA), particularly for customers in certain businesses/industries.

# PermataBank's Commitment for Sustainability Implementation

PermataBank has implemented sustainable finance as of 1 January 2019 in accordance with the provisions in POJK 51/2017. The sustainable finance implementation strategy is outlined in the RAKB that contains the 1-year and 5-year programs.

In 2019, the priority for sustainable finance implementation is the development of sustainable financial products and services, including increasing the portfolio of financing, investment or placement in financial instruments or projects that are in line with the implementation of sustainable finance. There are 6 programs described in the RAKB, namely:

1. Increase in financial inclusion disbursement.
2. Financing for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) that is in line with the implementation of sustainable finance.
3. Increase in electronic transactions and distributions of financial services products.
4. PermataTabungan iB Haji.
5. Hajj One Stop Services.
6. Policies/procedures that support the development of sustainable finance products and services.
