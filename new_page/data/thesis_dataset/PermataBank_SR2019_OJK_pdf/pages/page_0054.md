Selain program PermataBRAVE Hub, PermataBank turut memberikan bantuan usaha serta pelatihan/pendampingan kewirausahaan kepada berbagai komunitas difablepreneur. Komunitas-komunitas tersebut juga menjadi mitra PermataHati yang dilibatkan mulai dari sebagai penerima manfaat hingga turut memberdayakan melalui berbagai kesempatan usaha. Komunitas tersebut antara lain Precious One, Thisable.id, Kopituli, Dekarkdekor, dan KitoRato yang dikelola oleh para lulusan program PermataBRAVE Hub tahun 2018.

Sebagai bank yang inklusif, PermataBank telah mendidik dan merekrut tenaga kerja disabilitas untuk berkarier di industri perbankan. Hingga kini terdapat 26 tenaga kerja disabilitas di PermataBank (20 karyawan tunanetra di bagian telesales, 6 karyawan tuna daksa di bagian Syariah, Technical &amp; Operations, dan Retail Banking).

Santoso – Founder of KopiTuli, Ikhwan Tariqo – Digital Marketer, as well as Ratnawati Soetedjo – Founder of Precious One.

In addition to the PermataBRAVE HUB program, PermataBank also provides business and entrepreneurship training/assistance to various disablepreneur communities. These communities are also partners of PermataHati who are involved as beneficiaries and even empowering themselves through various business opportunities. These communities include Precious One, Thisable.id, Kopituli, Dekarkdekor, and Kitorato that are managed by graduates of the PermataBRAVE HUB program in 2018.

As an inclusive bank, PermataBank has contributed in education and recruitment for people with disabilities to have a career opportunities in banking industry. Until now, there are 26 people with disabilities employed at PermataBank (20 visually impaired employees at the telesales department, 6 people with physical disabilities in the Sharia, Technical &amp; Operations, and Retail Banking directorate).
