# Pencapaian Kinerja Keuangan Berkelanjutan

Nilai keberlanjutan PermataBank dituangkan dalam strategi untuk mengadopsi prinsip-prinsip keuangan berkelanjutan melalui tiga area utama yaitu ekonomi, sosial, dan lingkungan. Di bidang ekonomi PermataBank meningkatkan portofolio produk keuangan berkelanjutan melalui peningkatan portofolio Kredit Usaha Rakyat (KUR) dan mengembangkan kerja sama channeling dengan lembaga keuangan mikro.

Dari segi transformasi digital, PermataBank terus mengembangkan layanan pembukaan rekening dan transaksi perbankan seperti produk unsecured lending termasuk kartu kredit dan Kredit Tanpa Agunan (KTA) melalui mobile banking serta peningkatan transaksi perbankan melalui e-channel seperti eMutual Fund dan eBond. Tersedianya layanan keuangan digital memungkinkan produk-produk dan layanan keuangan dapat dinikmati masyarakat yang lebih luas serta memberikan kemudahan dalam melakukan transaksi perbankan.

Di bidang sosial, PermataBank memberikan dampak positif bagi masyarakat melalui edukasi literasi keuangan dan pemberdayaan yang berkelanjutan bagi pemangku kepentingan yang lebih luas. Kegiatan tanggung jawab sosial dan lingkungan PermataBank yang bertajuk PermataHati diterapkan secara berkelanjutan melalui dengan tiga pilar 3E yaitu Education, Enhancement, dan Empowerment. PermataBankers turut terlibat dalam berbagai program PermataHati melalui Employee Volunteerism sebagai wujud salah satu nilai "Caring" kepada masyarakat dan komunitas sekitar.

Di tahun 2019, sebagai bagian dari rangkaian program PermataBRAVE, PermataBank mengajak penyandang disabilitas dari Balai Besar Rehabilitasi Vokasional Penyandang Disabilitas (BBRVPD) Cibinong berkunjung dan belajar literasi keuangan di kantor PermataBank. Melalui program ini pula, sebanyak 120 penyandang disabilitas peserta program pelatihan vokasi di BBRVPD Cibinong mendapatkan modul program berupa literasi keuangan dan perbankan, kewirausahaan, dan pengembangan diri.

# Performance Achievement of Sustainable Finance

PermataBank sustainability values are set out in the strategy to adopt the principles of sustainable finance through three main areas, which are economic, social, and environmental. In the economic field, PermataBank is increasing its sustainable financial product portfolio by increasing the Microfinance (KUR) portfolio and developing channeling cooperation with microfinance institutions.

In terms of digital transformation, PermataBank continues to develop account opening and banking transaction services, such as unsecured lending, including credit cards and unsecured loans (KTA) through mobile banking, as well as increasing banking transactions through e-channels, such as eMutual Funds and eBonds. The availability of digital financial services allows for financial products and services to be enjoyed by the wider community as well and provide convenience in banking transactions.

In the social field, PermataBank provides positive impacts on the community through financial literacy education and sustainable empowerment for expanding the stakeholder base. PermataBank's social and environmental responsibility activities named PermataHati is implemented in a sustainable manner with the 3E pillars, namely Education, Enhancement, and Empowerment. PermataBankers are involved in various PermataHati programs through Employee Volunteerism as a manifestation of one of the values of "Caring" to the surrounding communities.

In 2019, as part of the PermataBRAVE series program, PermataBank invited people with disabilities from the Cibinong Vocational and Rehabilitation Center for People with Disabilities (BBRVPD) to visit and learn financial literacy at the PermataBank office. Through this program, 120 people with disabilities who participated in the vocational training program at Cibinong BBRVPD received program modules regarding financial and banking literacy, entrepreneurship, and self-development.
