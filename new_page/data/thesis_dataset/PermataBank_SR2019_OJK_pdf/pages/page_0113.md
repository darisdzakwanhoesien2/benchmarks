## EARTH HOUR

Sebagai bentuk dukungan dan komitmen dalam mewujudkan lingkungan yang sehat dan berkelanjutan, PermataBank secara rutin berpartisipsi dalam gerakan Earth Hour yang digagas oleh World Wildlife Fund (WWF). Pada saat aksi Earth Hour yang dilaksanakan tanggal 30 Maret 2019, PermataBank turut mematikan lampu selama satu jam (pukul 20.30 – 21.30 waktu setempat). Selain itu, PermataBank juga turut mengkampanyekan gerakan Earth Hour kepada PermataBankers melalui pemasangan wallpaper pada PC dari tanggal 25-29 Maret 2019, serta melalui kuis yang dikirimkan ke surat elektronik PermataBankers.

As a form of the support and commitment in realizing a healthy and sustainable environment, PermataBank routinely participates in the Earth Hour movement that was initiated by World Wildlife Fund (WWF). During the Earth Hour movement that was held on 30 March 2019, PermataBank turned off its lights for one hour (20.30-21.30 local time). In addition, PermataBank also campaigns the Earth Hour movement to PermataBankers through the installation of wallpaper on PC from 25-29 March 2019, as well as through quizzes sent to the email of PermataBankers.
