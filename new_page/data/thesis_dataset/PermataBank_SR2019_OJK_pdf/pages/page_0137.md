# Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik

Good Corporate Governance

PermataBank menerapkan Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik atau Good Corporate Governance (GCG) untuk melindungi kepentingan serta memberikan nilai tambah bagi seluruh pemangku kepentingan. Dewan Komisaris, Direksi dan seluruh PermataBankers berkomitmen dalam mewujudkan sebuah organisasi yang transparan, akuntabel, bertanggung jawab, independen dan wajar. Hal ini bertujuan untuk memastikan PermataBank menjadi Bank yang terpercaya.

Tujuan penerapan GCG di lingkungan PermataBank:

- Memaksimalkan nilai-nilai PermataBank dengan menerapkan lima prinsip GCG.
- Mewujudkan pengelolaan PermataBank yang profesional dan independen.
- Menciptakan proses pengambilan keputusan yang berlandaskan pada nilai moral yang tinggi dan sesuai dengan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku di semua elemen PermataBank.
- Melaksanakan tanggung jawab sosial perusahaan terhadap pemangku kepentingan terkait.
- Berkontribusi dalam meningkatkan iklim investasi yang kondusif di Indonesia khususnya di sektor keuangan dan perbankan.

PermataBank selalu meningkatkan pelaksanaan GCG dan manajemen risiko untuk mengantisipasi dan menekan potensi risiko bisnis sehingga fundamental PermataBank dapat terjaga dengan baik. Selama tahun 2019, PermataBank telah memperbaiki kualitas penerapan praktik-praktik GCG terbaik di seluruh lapisan organisasi melalui:

- Perubahan susunan Direksi PermataBank yaitu pengangkatan Direktur Unit Usaha Syariah, Direktur Retail Banking, dan Direktur Sumber Daya Manusia.
- Penerbitan kebijakan perdagangan saham anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direktur.
- Penerapan manajemen risiko di PermataBank tetap berfokus pada fungsi kontrol internal di seluruh level, yaitu first, second maupun third line of defense.

PermataBank implements Good Corporate Governance (GCG) to maintain the interests as well as to provide added value to all stakeholders. The Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, and all PermataBakers are committed to realize a transparent, accountable, responsible, independent, and fair organization. This is to ensure that PermataBank becomes a trusted Bank.

The objectives of GCG implementation within PermataBank are to:

- Maximize the values of PermataBank by implementing the five principles of GCG.
- Establish professional and independent management of PermataBank.
- Create a decision-making process that is based on high moral values, which are in accordance with all prevailing regulations that are applicable at every level of PermataBank.
- Implement corporate social responsibility towards all relevant stakeholders.
- Contribute in improving a favorable investment climate in Indonesia, particularly in the financial and banking sectors.

PermataBank continues to improve the implementation of GCG and risk management to anticipate and reduce potential business risks, allowing PermataBank's fundamentals to be well maintained. During 2019, PermataBank improved the quality of GCG practices across all levels of the organization through:

- Changes to the composition of the Board of Directors of PermataBank, namely the appointment of the Sharia Business Unit Director, Retail Banking Director, and Human Resources Director.
- Issuance of policies on share trading for members of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors.
- Implementation of risk management at PermataBank remains focused on internal control functions at all levels, namely first, second, as well as third line of defense.
