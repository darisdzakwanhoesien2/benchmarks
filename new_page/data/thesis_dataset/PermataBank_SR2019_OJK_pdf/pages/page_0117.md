# Operasional Perbankan Ramah Lingkungan

Environmentally Friendly Banking Operations

- Pendaftaran produk unsecured lending seperti kartu kredit dan KTA melalui mobile banking.
- Konversi dari statement dalam bentuk kertas ke e-statement terkait dengan produk-produk seperti CASA, TD, kartu kredit, dan mortgage.
- Pendaftaran atau layanan transaksi seperti eMutual Fund, eBond, serta KTA melalui ATM dan e-form melalui situs web.
- Media komunikasi kepada nasabah menggunakan SMS dan surat elektronik.

# SKEMA GEDUNG RAMAH LINGKUNGAN

Sejak 2009, PermataBank menerapkan sistem peringkat bagi gedung-gedung kantor PermataBank yaitu Building Environmental Assessment Standard (BEAS) yang ditetapkan oleh Standard Chartered Bank sebagaimana telah diterapkan di 30 negara. BEAS mengukur dampak operasional dari gedung-gedung GEMS PermataBank. Termasuk dalam kriteria BEAS adalah pengelolaan emisi GRK, konsumsi listrik dan konsumsi air.

Selain itu, sesuai dengan peraturan izin lingkungan di Indonesia, PermataBank memastikan dilakukannya laporan pengelolaan dan pemantauan lingkungan pada gedung-gedung yang dimiliki dan dikelola oleh PermataBank. Hal ini untuk memastikan dampak lingkungan kegiatan PermataBank terhadap lingkungan sesuai dengan persyaratan peraturan perundangan.

- Registration of unsecured lending products such as credit card and KTA through mobile banking.
- Conversion from statement in paper form to e-statement related with products such as CASA, TD, credit card and mortgage.
- Registration or transaction services such as eMutual Fund, eBond, and KTA trough ATM and e-form trough website.
- Communication media to customers using SMS and email.

# ENVIRONMENTALLY FRIENDLY BUILDING SCHEME

Since 2009, PermataBank has implemented a rating system for PermataBank office buildings, which is the Building Environmental Assessment Standard (BEAS). This was established by Standard Chartered Bank and has been implemented in 30 countries. BEAS measures the operational impact of PermataBank's GEMS buildings. Included in the BEAS criteria are the management of GHG emissions, electricity consumption and water consumption.

Additionally, in accordance with environmental permit regulations in Indonesia, PermataBank ensures that environmental management and monitoring reports are carried out on buildings owned and managed by PermataBank. This is conducted to ensure that the environmental impact of PermataBank's activities on the environment is in accordance with regulatory requirements.
