## Saryadi Guyatno, S.T., M.B.A

Kepala Subdili Penyelarasan Kejuruan dan Kerja sama Industri PSMK Kementerian Pendidikan dan Kebudayaan.
Head of Sub-Directorate of Vocational Alignment and PSMK Industrial Cooperation of the Ministry of Education and Cultural Affairs.

“Kementrian Pendidikan dan Kebudayaan menyambut baik program PYP yang secara nyata memberikan penguatan kompetensi teknis dan kewirausahaan dibidang digital kepada siswa SMK. Kami memiliki keyakinan bahwa siswa SMK akan mampu menjawab tantangan perubahan teknologi digital yang muncul seiring dengan adanya revolusi industri 4.0. Kami berharap melalui program PYP akan muncul banyak talent digital dan sekaligus para digitalpreneur yang berasal dari SMK.”

“The Ministry of Education and Cultural Affairs welcomes the PYP program, which provides technical and entrepreneurship strengthening in the digital field to vocational school students in a tangible manner. We are confident that vocational school students will be able to address the challenges of changing digital technology that emerge along with the 4.0 industrial revolution. We hope that through the PYP program there will be a lot of digital talent and at the same time digitalpreneur from vocational schools.”

2019 Sustainability Report
