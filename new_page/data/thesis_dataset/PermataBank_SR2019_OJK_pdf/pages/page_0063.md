Unite For Education (UFE) merupakan sebuah gerakan advokasi yang mengajak segenap pemangku kepentingan untuk meningkatkan kualitas pendidikan di Indonesia. UFE bertujuan menjadi tempat bagi komunitas untuk mengembangkan ekosistem pendidikan berkelanjutan yang saling menginspirasi serta mendorong adanya kolaborasi antara para pemangku kepentingan yang peduli dan memiliki semangat yang sama untuk kemajuan pendidikan di Indonesia. Seperti tahun sebelumnya, UFE dijalankan dalam 2 rangkaian kegiatan, yaitu UFE Boot Camp yang diperuntukkan bagi para komunitas PermataHati dan UFE Sustainability Forum bagi aktivis, praktisi komunitas dan pendidikan serta masyarakat umum.

UFE Boot Camp 2019 mengajak 20 founder beserta pengelola dari 10 komunitas PermataHati yaitu Sanggar Anak Akar, Arkademy, Hoshizora Foundation, Red Nose Foundation, Precious One, NARA Kreatif, Thisable, Gandeng Tangan, Circa Handmade, Inspiration Factory. Di sini dihadirkan sejumlah pembicara untuk memberikan materi dan membagi pengalaman mengelola organisasi hingga kemampuan komunikasi sehingga organisasi tersebut dapat tumbuh dan berjalan dengan sukses.

Unite for Education (UFE) is an advocacy movement that invites multiple stakeholders to improve the quality of education in Indonesia. UFE aims to be a place for communities to develop a sustainable education ecosystem that inspires each other and encourages collaborations between stakeholders who have the same mission and passion for a better education in Indonesia. Similar to the previous year, UFE was presented in 2 series of activities, namely UFE Boot Camp intended for PermataHati communities, and UFE Sustainability Forum for activists, community, education practitioners, and the general public.

The 2019 UFE Boot Camp invites 20 founders and successors of 10 PermataHati communities, include Sanggar Anak Akar, Arkademy, Hoshizora Foundation, Red Nose Foundation, Precious One, NARA Kreatif, Thisable, Gandeng Tangan, Circa Handmade, and Inspiration Factory. In this event, speakers were presented to provide materials and share experiences in managing an organization up to communication skills allowing the organization to grow and move forward.

The Unite for Education – Sustainability Forum main event took the theme 'Women as Change Agent' that emphasizes the important role of women in driving the economy of a nation.
