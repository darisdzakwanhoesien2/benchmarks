# Tantangan Penerapan Keuangan Berkelanjutan

Tantangan yang dihadapi dalam hal pelaksanaan program RAKB terkait dengan portofolio kredit adalah peningkatan penyaluran kredit dan upaya untuk memperbaiki kualitas kredit dengan mempertahankan tingkat Non-Performing Loan (NPL) tidak lebih dari 5% terhadap portofolio fasilitas kredit. Hal yang dilakukan PermataBank dalam mengatasi tantangan tersebut dengan menerapkan strategi bisnis yang bertujuan untuk meningkatkan pendapatan, menekan biaya, dan pengendalian risiko untuk mencegah kerugian.

Tantangan eksternal yang dihadapi adalah kondisi perekonomian global dan nasional serta kebijakan makro yang dapat mempengaruhi keberlanjutan PermataBank. Kami juga mencermati kompetisi dari perusahaan fintech yang dapat menyalurkan pembiayaan kepada masyarakat, sehingga masyarakat semakin banyak memiliki pilihan untuk melakukan transaksi keuangan. Faktor sosial eksternal lainnya yang dapat memengaruhi PermataBank termasuk isu ketenagakerjaan seperti ketentuan upah minimum yang memengaruhi pengambilan keputusan di dunia usaha.

PermataBank also held the "CERITA Permata (Love and Empathy from Us) simultaneously in 50 cities and involving approximately 3,000 Employee Volunteers, an activity in line with the mission to expand financial access and literacy for all members of the community. We also organized the PermataYouthPreneur (PYP) program, which is an inspiring start-up training program with a comprehensive curriculum in the entrepreneurship for Vocational High School students majoring in Software Engineering for two months of learning. During 2019, PYP was attended by 130 students with the theme "Financial Technology Innovation Towards Industry 4.0".

In the environmental field, PermataBank consistently manages the environmental impacts of its business activities, which include reducing Greenhouse Gas emissions, energy, and water usage in all bank offices. The initiative to convert paper-based statements to e-statements and the use of SMS and electronic mail as communication media, is part of the effort to reduce the environmental footprint in financial services to the customers.

# Challenges in Sustainable Finance Implementation

The challenges in the implementation of the RAKB program related to loan portfolio are increasing loan disbursements and the efforts to improve credit quality while maintaining the Non-Performing Loan (NPL) level under 5% of the loan facility portfolio. What PermataBank has done to address these challenges is to implement a business strategy to increase revenues, reduce costs, and control risks in order to prevent losses.

External challenges include the global and national economic conditions and those macro policies that may affect the sustainability of PermataBank. We also pay close attention to competition from fintech companies that can channel financing to the public, enabling the public to have more options to conduct financial transactions. Other external social factors that can influence PermataBank include employment issues, such as minimum wage, which influences decision-making across the business world.
