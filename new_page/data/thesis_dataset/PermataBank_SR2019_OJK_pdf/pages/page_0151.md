# Manajemen Risiko Fraud dan APU-PPT

Management of Fraud and AML-CTF Risks

Mekanisme Sistem Whistleblowing (Speak-Up) di PermataBank, dilakukan dengan memperhatikan prinsip - prinsip berikut:

- Sifat laporan adalah rahasia. Hanya administrator dan komite Speak-Up yang telah ditunjuk manajemen yang memiliki wewenang untuk mengetahui isi laporan.
- Pelapor dapat memilih 1 dari 3 prinsip anonim, yaitu: full disclosure (mencantumkan identitas), partial anonymity (identitas hanya disampaikan kepada administrator) atau anonymous (tidak mencantumkan identitas).
- Pelapor berhak mendapatkan perlindungan terhadap ancaman yang mungkin terjadi, baik dari dalam maupun dari luar PermataBank.
- Pelapor dibebaskan dari segala sanksi jika laporannya tidak terbukti.
- PermataBank menjamin bahwa laporan yang disampaikan pelapor tidak akan mempengaruhi penilaian kinerja dan karier mereka di PermataBank.
- Pelapor harus menyampaikan informasi secara mendetil agar dapat ditindaklanjuti.

The Whistleblowing System (Speak-Up) mechanism at PermataBank is carried out with due regard to the following principles:

- The nature of the report is confidential. Only administrators and the Speak-Up committee appointed by the management have the authority to know the contents of the report.
- The informant can choose 1 of the 3 anonymity principles, namely: full disclosure (disclose identity), partial anonymity (identity only disclose to administrators) or anonymous (does not disclose identity).
- The informant is entitled to have protection against threats that may occur, both from within and from outside PermataBank.
- The informant is freed from all sanctions in the event the report is unproven.
- PermataBank guarantees that reports submitted by the informant will not affect their performance and career evaluations at PermataBank.
- The informant must submit the information in detail so that it can be followed up.

Selama tahun 2019, Administrator Speak-Up menerima 18 laporan, dengan 6 laporan umum yang bersifat "keluh kesah", dan 12 laporan berindikasi pelanggaran ketentuan Kode Etik. Seluruh laporan tersebut telah ditindaklanjuti.

Throughout 2019, the Speak-Up Administrator received 18 reports, with 6 general reports or grievances, and 12 reports of indicated violations of the Code of Conduct. These reports have all been followed up.
