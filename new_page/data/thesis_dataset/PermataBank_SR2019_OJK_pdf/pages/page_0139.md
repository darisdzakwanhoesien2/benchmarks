# Struktur Tata Kelola PermataBank

Governance Structure of PermataBank

Sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku serta best practice, untuk mendukung implementasi GCG yang konsisten dan efektif, PermataBank memiliki organ-organ tata kelola sebagai berikut:

- Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS)
- Dewan Komisaris
- Direksi
- Komite-komite di bawah fungsi Dewan Komisaris:
- Komite Audit
- Komite Pemantau Risiko
- Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi
- Komite Tata Kelola Terintegrasi
- Audit Internal
- Komite-komite yang berada di bawah fungsi Direksi

Pursuant to the applicable regulations and best practice, to support a consistent and effective GCG implementation, PermataBank has established the following governance organs:

- General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS)
- Board of Commissioners
- Board of Directors
- Committees under the functions of the Board of Commissioners:
- Audit Committee
- Risk Monitoring Committee
- Remuneration and Nomination Committee
- Integrated Governance Committee
- Internal Audit
- Committees under the functions of the Board of Directors
