# Komitmen PermataBank bagi Keuangan Berkelanjutan

Commitment of PermataBank to Sustainable Finance

Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan (TPB)/(SDG – Sustainable Development Goals) adalah pengentasan kemiskinan yang dilakukan sejalan dengan strategi peningkatan kesehatan dan pendidikan, mengurangi ketidaksetaraan, dan mendorong pertumbuhan ekonomi serta pelestarian lingkungan. Komitmen ini juga dibarengi dengan komitmen Pemerintah dalam Kesepakatan Paris untuk bersama-sama mengatasi dampak perubahan iklim.

Pemerintah Indonesia telah menetapkan komitmen untuk melakukan upaya-upaya tercapainya SDGs dan upaya mitigasi serta adaptasi perubahan iklim salah satunya dengan penerbitan POJK 51/2017. Melalui POJK 51/2017, industri jasa keuangan diharuskan berperan aktif dalam menyediakan produk dan layanan keuangan bagi pelaku ekonomi serta menyalurkan investasi sosial yang selanjutnya dapat memberikan kontribusi kepada TPB.

PermataBank berkomitmen penuh untuk mendukung pencapaian TPB bersama para pemangku kepentingan salah satunya dengan menerapkan keuangan berkelanjutan. Komitmen tersebut diwujudkan oleh PermataBank melalui pengembangan produk dan jasa keuangan berkelanjutan sesuai dengan kebutuhan masyarakat serta melalui kontribusi langsung kegiatan CSR. [GRI 102-15]

One objective of Sustainable Development Goals is the alleviation of poverty that is carried out in line with the strategy of health and education improvement, reduce inequality, and encourage economic growth, as well as environmental preservation. This commitment is also complemented with the government's commitment in the Paris Agreement to collectively address the impacts of climate change.

The Government of Indonesia has established a commitment to accomplish the SDGs as well as mitigation and adaptation efforts towards climate change, one of which is through the issuance of POJK 51/2017. Through POJK 51/2017, the financial services industry is required to play an active role in providing financial products and services for economic agents as well as channeling social investment that will ultimately contribute to SDGs.

PermataBank is fully committed to support the achievement of SDGs with stakeholders, one of which is by implementing sustainable finance. The commitment is realized by PermataBank through the development of sustainable finance products and services in accordance with the needs of the community and through direct CSR activity contribution. [GRI 102-15]
