# B.R.A.V.E

"Because EveRyone is Able &amp; CreatiVE"

Sejak tahun 2010 PermataBank memulai inisiatif untuk menjadi inklusif bagi penyandang disabilitas dengan PermataBRAVE (Because EveRyone is Able and CreatiVE), inisiatif ini merupakan wadah bagi komunitas disabilitas untuk belajar, berbagi pengetahuan dan pengalaman baru dengan sesama komunitas. Pada tahun 2018, inisiatif ini ditingkatkan dengan mendirikan PermataBRAVE HUB yang merupakan program pelatihan dan pembinaan selama tiga bulan bagi para penyandang disabilitas dalam bidang literasi keuangan &amp; perbankan, kewirausahaan dan keterampilan kerja.

Program PermataBRAVE HUB tahun 2019 diselenggarakan pada bulan Agustus hingga bulan Oktober 2019 diikuti oleh 120 penyandang disabilitas dari Balai Besar Rehabilitas Vokasional Penyandang Disabilitas (BBRVPD), Cibinong. Kegiatan tersebut diisi dengan literasi keuangan dan perbankan, kewirausahaan, serta pengembangan diri dan kunjungan langsung ke kantor PermataBank. Seluruh kegiatan PermataBRAVE difasilitasi oleh EVO, Perbanas Institute, Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) dan para pemangku kepentingan lainnya.

Melalui program ini, peserta mendapatkan berbagai pengetahuan mengenai tabungan dan investasi, peluang usaha di koperasi, tips menjadi telesales yang handal langsung dari salah satu telesales tuna netra terbaik di PermataBank, serta merasakan pengalaman seru membuka tabungan PermataME lewat PermataMobile X.

Untuk modul kewirausahaan, PermataBank menggandeng beberapa sociopreneur panutan dan telah lama terjun di dunia usaha sambil turut membantu pemberdayaan penyandang disabilitas, antara lain Angkie Yudistia – Founder Thisable.id, Putri

Since 2010, PermataBank has taken the initiative to become inclusive for people with disabilities with PermataBRAVE (Because EveRyone is Able and CreatiVE), this initiative aims to be a forum for the disability community to learn as well as share new knowledge and experience with fellow communities. In 2018, this initiative was enhanced through the establishment of the PermataBRAVE HUB, which is a three-month training and mentorship program for people with disabilities, in the fields of financial literacy &amp; banking, entrepreneurship, and work skills.

In 2019, the PermataBRAVE HUB program was held from August to October 2019 and attended by 120 persons with disabilities from the Vocational and Rehabilitation Center for People with Disabilities (BBRVPD), Cibinong. The activity was filled with financial literacy and banking, entrepreneurship, as well as self-development and direct visit to PermataBank offices. All PermataBRAVE activities were facilitated by EVO, Perbanas Institute and OJK representatives.

Through this program, participants acquired a variety of knowledge about savings and investment, business opportunities in cooperatives, tips to become a reliable telesales directly from one of the best visually impaired telesales in PermataBank, as well as exciting experience when opening a PermataME savings through PermataMobile X.

For the entrepreneurship module, PermataBank engaged with a number of role-model sociopreneurs that have been involved in business for a long time while empowering people with disabilities, among others Angkie Yudistia – Founder of Thisable.id, Putri
