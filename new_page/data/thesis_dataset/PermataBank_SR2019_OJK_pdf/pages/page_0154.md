# Komitmen kepada Pemangku Kepentingan

Commitment to the Stakeholders

PermataBank menyadari bahwa keberhasilan bisnis yang berkelanjutan juga bergantung kepada pelibatan yang efektif dengan para pemangku kepentingan. Melalui berbagai metode, kami memperoleh masukan yang berharga dari setiap pemangku kepentingan untuk meningkatkan kinerja, pengelolaan risiko, serta pengembangan produk dan jasa PermataBank.

Berdasarkan pengaruhnya pada kinerja dan reputasi, PermataBank menilai bahwa terdapat lima pemangku kepentingan utama yaitu nasabah, karyawan, masyarakat, pemegang saham, dan regulator. [GRI 102-40] [GRI 102-42] [GRI 102-43] [GRI 102-44]

PermataBank is aware that the success of a sustainable business also depends on an effective engagement of stakeholders. Through various methods, we obtained valuable inputs from each stakeholder to improve performance, risk management, as well as the development of products and services of PermataBank.

Based on the impact to performance and reputation, there are five key stakeholders of PermataBank, which are the customers, employees, communities, shareholders, and regulators. [GRI 102-40] [GRI 102-42] [GRI 102-43] [GRI 102-44]
