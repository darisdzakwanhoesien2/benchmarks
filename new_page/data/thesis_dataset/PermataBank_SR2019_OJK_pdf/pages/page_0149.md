# Manajemen Risiko Fraud dan APU-PPT

Management of Fraud and AML-CTF Risks

Fraud di perbankan sangat berkuitan dengan risiko kredit dan risiko reputasi. Akibat dari fraud dapat menimbulkan dampak yang merugikan bagi nasabah serta merusak reputasi serta kepercayaan yang telah dibangun oleh PermataBank. Untuk mengelola fraud, PermataBank menerapkan Fraud Risk Management Framework (FRMF) yang meliputi aktivitas pencegahan, pendeteksian, pemberian respon dan efek jera ke dalam strategi yang lengkap untuk mengelola risiko fraud di PermataBank. FRMF dikelola unit kerja Fraud Risk Management (FRM) di bawah koordinasi Direktorat Hukum dan Kepatuhan. [GRI 103-1] [GRI 103-2]

PermataBank melaksanakan Fraud Risk Assessment (FRA) yaitu kajian berkelanjutan untuk mengevaluasi pencegahan, pendeteksian dan respons terhadap setiap potensi skema dan peristiwa fraud. Tujuan dari FRA adalah untuk memperhatikan area yang memiliki risiko fraud pada unit/produk/proses/aktivitas yang ditentukan dan membantu unit kerja untuk merancang strategi kontrol yang tepat untuk memitigasi risiko fraud tersebut. [GRI 103-3]

# INTERNAL FRAUD

PermataBank melakukan berbagai upaya pencegahan terhadap kemungkinan terjadinya internal fraud, di antaranya dengan cara:

a) Menerbitkan fraud alert berupa tulisan dan disebarkan melalui surat elektronik yang menginformasikan kepada seluruh karyawan tentang tren/modus kejahatan fraud yang berkembang dengan tujuan membangun kepedulian dan kesadaran terhadap risiko fraud.
b) Mengelola program e-learning fraud, yakni model pelatihan/ pembelajaran fraud awareness yang dikemas dalam bentuk animasi komputer disertai soal-soal pertanyaan, yang wajib diikuti oleh seluruh karyawan PermataBank.
c) Menyelenggarakan pelatihan pengenalan fraud bagi karyawan baru (induction training).
d) Kebijakan blokir internal rekening giro dan tabungan nasabah terindikasi fraud untuk mencegah penggunaan rekening untuk menampung hasil kejahatan. [GRI 205-2]

Fraud in banking is closely related to credit risk and reputation risk. The consequences of fraud can lead to detrimental impact on the customers as well as damaging the reputation and confidence that have been built by PermataBank. To manage fraud, PermataBank implemented Fraud Risk Management Framework (FRMF) that covers prevention, detection, response, and deterrent activities into a comprehensive strategy to manage the fraud risk in PermataBank. FRMF is managed by the Fraud Risk Management unit under the coordination of the Legal and Compliance Directorate. [GRI 103-1] [GRI 103-2]

PermataBank implements Fraud Risk Assessment (FRA), which is a continuous study to evaluate the prevention, detection, and response to any potential fraud schemes and occurrences. The objective of the FRA is to examine areas with fraud risk on units/products/activities that have been determined as well as assisting the work unit to formulate the appropriate control strategy to mitigate the fraud risk. [GRI 103-3]

# INTERNAL FRAUD

PermataBank takes all necessary efforts to prevent potential internal fraud, including through:

a) Publishing fraud alerts, which are written and distributed via e-mail to inform all employees about the growing fraud trends/ modus with an aim to build awareness to fraud risks.
b) Launching fraud e-learning program, a model of training/ learning regarding fraud awareness in the form of scored computer animation games/quizzes that are mandatory for all of PermataBank employees.
c) Providing fraud introduction training for new employees (induction training).
d) Implementing policy to internally block current and deposit accounts of customers, which are indicating alleged involvement in fraud to prevent accounts from being used to save illegal funds. [GRI 205-2]
