# BUILDING RISK ASSESSMENT (BRA)

Penilaian Risiko Gedung (Building Risk Assessment) di seluruh kantor operasional PermataBank untuk memastikan bahwa risiko-risiko operasional yang terkait dengan isu kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja dapat diidentifikasi, dikelola serta ditindaklanjuti.

Pada tahun 2019, BRA diadakan di 51 kantor bank dimana terdapat sebanyak 97 temuan risiko kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja dengan total 86 isu yang ditemukan telah diselesaikan secara efektif.

# KOMPETENSI K3 PERMATABANKERS

PermataBank mengadakan pelatihan internal maupun eksternal di bidang K3. Pelatihan ini ditujukan untuk meningkatkan kompetensi dan kesadaran PermataBankers dalam mencegah dan mengelola risiko-risiko kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja di tempat kerjanya serta kemampuan untuk mengantisipasi keadaan darurat.

Sepanjang tahun 2019 telah dilakukan pelatihan K3 dengan data sebagai berikut:

a. 2 kegiatan pelatihan Pertolongan Pertama Pada Kecelakaan yang diikuti oleh 22 PermataBankers dengan rincian:
- First Aid Refreshment Training sebanyak 2 kali pertemuan di Jakarta.

b. Program pelatihan serta penyegaran untuk seluruh PermataBankers dalam bentuk e-learning HSE yang berisi penjelasan mengenai potensi risiko kesehatan dan keselamatan di lingkungan tempat kerja serta bagaimana cara melakukan mitigasi risiko sesuai dengan standar HSE yang berlaku. Selama tahun 2019, sebanyak 6.860 PermataBankers (90,73% dari total PermataBankers) telah menyelesaikan program e-learning HSE.

# BUILDING RISK ASSESSMENT (BRA)

Building Risk Assessment at all PermataBank operational offices to ensure that operational risks related to occupational health and safety issues could be identified, managed, and followed-up.

In 2019, the BRA was conducted at 51 branch offices which has identified 97 occupational health and safety risk findings with those 86 identified issues were effectively resolved.

# PERMATABANKERS OHS COMPETENCY

PermataBank conducts internal and external training in the field of OHS. The training is intended to improve the competence and awareness of PermataBankers in preventing and managing occupational health and safety risks in the workplace as well as having the ability to overcome emergency occurrences.

Throughout 2019 conducted OHS trainings as follows:

a. 2 First Aid trainings attended by 22 PermataBankers:
- 2 First Aid Refreshment Trainings held in Jakarta.

b. Training and refreshment programs for all PermataBankers in form of HSE e-learning consisted of explanation on potential risks of health and safety at workplace and how to mitigate the risks in accordance with prevailing HSE standards. In 2019, a total of 6,860 PermataBankers (90.73% from total of PermataBankers) finished HSE e-learning program.

c. Refreshment and training programs for PermataBankers as First Aiders for branch offices in form of First Aider Refreshment e-learning. In 2019, total of First Aiders passed the training through e-learning was 246 people from 268 First Aiders (91.79%).

d. During 2019, PermataBank conducted two times drill in one year. In first semester, fire drill was conducted at Tower III Bintaro on 18 April 2019, in evacuation drill at Tower I WTC 2 on 25 July 2019, and fire drill at Tower II Hayam Wuruk on 19 June 2019.
