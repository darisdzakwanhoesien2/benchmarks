## Highlight Pelatihan di 2019

- 1.065 karyawan telah mengikuti sertifikasi WPPE Pemasaran, dimana pejabat PermataBank yang melakukan fungsi pemasaran wajib tersertifikasi WPPE Pemasaran.
- 664 partisipan telah mengikuti Bancassurance Training, untuk meningkatkan skill dan kemampuan Relationship Manager (RM) dalam memberikan referensi produk unit link.
- 649 partisipan telah mengikuti Intensive Service Enhancement Program (INSEP), untuk para staff frontliner dalam memahami PermataMobileX.
- 618 partisipan telah mengikuti Learning Lounge, dengan tema yang dipilih adalah tentang Wellness dan Kesehatan, seperti Energy Management, Self-Healing, dan Mental Hack for Professional.
- 371 partisipan telah mengikuti Lead with Values, untuk menanamkan corporate value dan implementasi dari Eight Leadership Competency.
- 291 partisipan telah mengikuti New Me Program – Renewing Mind and Rebuilding Life, merupakan pelatihan dasar untuk pengembangan spiritualitas dan kapasitas diri dengan fokus pada pembentukan karakter, sikap mental positif, nilai-nilai kerja.
- 166 partisipan telah mengikuti My Perfect Life, sebuah program yang dipersiapkan bagi karyawan PermataBank yang akan memasuki masa persiapan pensiun.

## 2019 Training Highlight

- 1,065 employees have attended the WPPE Marketing certification, in which PermataBank officials in the marketing functions must have WPPE Marketing certification.
- 664 participants attended the Bancassurance Training, to improve the Relationship Manager (RM) skill and ability in providing unit link product references.
- 649 participants attended the Intensive Service Enhancement Program (INSEP), for front-liner staffs to understand PermataMobileX.
- 618 participants attended the Learning Lounge, the theme selected was Wellness and Health, such as Energy Management, Self-Healing, and Mental Hack for Professionals.
- 371 participants attended Lead with Values, to instill corporate values and as the implementation of the Eight Leadership Competency.
- 291 participants attended the New Me Program – Renewing Mind and Rebuilding Life, which is a basic training for the development of spirituality and self-capacity with a focus on character building, positive mental attitude, work values.
- 166 participants attended in My Perfect Life, a program prepared for PermataBank employees entering the retirement preparation period.
