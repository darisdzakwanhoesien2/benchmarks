# Komitmen Kesehatan, Keselamatan Kerja, dan Lingkungan (K3L) PermataBank

PermataBank's Occupational Health, Safety, and Environmental Commitment

## KOMUNIKASI K3L

PermataBank mengkomunikasikan K3L secara terus menerus untuk menjaga tingkat kesadaran PermataBankers atas risiko kesehatan, keselamatan kerja dan lingkungan di tempat kerja serta mendorong perilaku yang aman setiap waktu.

## HSE COMMUNICATION

PermataBank provides HSE communication in a continuous manner to maintain the awareness level of PermataBankers regarding health, safety, and environmental risks in the workplace as well as to encourage safe behavior at all times.

### Media Komunikasi K3L HSE Communication Media

### HSE Info

Penyampaian informasi melalui surat elektronik yang dikirimkan kepada seluruh PermataBankers melalui media CRES News untuk meningkatkan kepedulian di bidang K3L.

Delivery of information through email to all PermataBankers through the CRES News to near awareness in the field of HSE.

### HSE Notice

Media poster yang dipasang di setiap kantor cabang, di antaranya mengenai himbauan penghematan air, penghematan energi, penghematan bahan bakar minyak dan sebagainya.

Posters installed in each branch office, regarding the encouragement to reduce water usage, energy usage, fuel consumption, and others.

### HSE Talkshow

Media sosialisasi melalui kegiatan seminar untuk PermataBankers di kantor pusat dan kantor cabang utama.

Introduction media through seminars for PermataBankers conducted at the heart of office and main branch offices.

### Kampanye Digital K3L (CRES HSE Digital Communication)

Media televisi di kantor cabang utama Tower III Bintaro yang memuat materi panduan menghadapi kebakaran, panduan menghadapi gempa bumi, serta himbauan menghemat penggunaan air.

Television media at the main branch office at Tower III Bintaro containing possible materials for anticipating fire, earthquake, as well as the appeal to reduce water usage.

### Earth Hour Campaign

Pemasangan Earth Hour Campaign sebagai wallpaper untuk PC untuk jangka waktu tertentu yang berisi tembauan menjaga kelestarian bumi di antaranya dengan mengurangi penggunaan kertas atau mengurangi penggunaan listrik.

Installation of Earth Hour Campaign as a PC wallpaper during a consecutive period containing encouragement to reduce the water usage, others by reducing paper usage or reducing operating usage.

## ASTRA GREEN COMPANY

Pengelolaan K3L di PermataBank tidak terlepas dari kebijakan dan arahan pemegang saham utama yaitu PT Astra International Tbk dan Standard Chartered Bank. Dengan demikian terdapat inisiatif-inisiatif dari keduanya yang diterapkan di seluruh kantor PermataBank untuk mencapai tujuan bersama di bidang K3L. Salah satu cara untuk mengukur efektivitas pengelolaan K3L, PermataBank menggunakan Astra Green Company (AGC) yang ditetapkan oleh PT Astra International Tbk. Audit AGC memeriksa penerapan standar pengelolaan K3L dikombinasikan dengan peraturan dan persyaratan K3L di Indonesia. Hasil pemeriksaan AGC ditetapkan dalam rating yang meliputi hitam, merah, biru, hijau, dan emas. [GRI 103-3]

## ASTRA GREEN COMPANY

HSE management at PermataBank is inseparable from the policies and directives of the main shareholders, namely PT Astra International Tbk and Standard Chartered Bank. Accordingly, there are initiatives from both that are implemented throughout PermataBank's offices to accomplish the common objectives in terms of HSE. PermataBank uses the Astra Green Company (AGC) that was established by PT Astra International Tbk. AGC Audit examines the implementation of HSE management standards combined with the HSE regulations and requirements in Indonesia. The AGC assessment results are determined in ratings, which include black, red, blue, green, and gold. [GRI 103-3]
