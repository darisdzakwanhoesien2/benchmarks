# Kode Etik

Code of Conduct

Pengenalan Kode Etik Perilaku Karyawan dilakukan sejak karyawan baru bergabung dan dilakukan penyegaran secara periodik selama karyawan masih bekerja di PermataBank. Sosialisasi kode etik dilakukan dengan pelatihan melalui e-learning terkait Kode Etik dan Budaya Kepatuhan dan prosedur anti korupsi. Pemantauan pelaksanaan kode etik dilaporkan dan dievaluasi oleh Direktur Kepatuhan dalam Laporan Profil Risiko.

Selain Kode Etik Perilaku Karyawan, PermataBank juga memiliki Kode Etik untuk Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, dan Pendukung Organ. Sesuai dengan Kode Etik tersebut, dalam melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya, anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi wajib melandasi diri dengan serangkaian etika sebagai berikut:

- Etika berkaitan dengan keteladanan.
- Etika berkaitan dengan kepatuhan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan.
- Etika berkaitan dengan keterbukaan dan kerahasiaan informasi.
- Etika berkaitan dengan peluang.
- Etika berkaitan dengan benturan kepentingan di mana anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi wajib bersikap profesional.
- Etika menghindari berkompromi karena hadiah dan hiburan (entertainment).
- Etika berkaitan dengan informasi orang dalam.
- Etika berkaitan dengan penyuapan dan korupsi.
- Etika berkaitan pencegahan pencucian uang.
- Etika untuk memperlakukan karyawan dengan adil.
- Etika berkaitan dengan aktivitas berpolitik.

Kode Etik tersebut disosialisasikan secara rutin dan telah dimuat dalam situs web PermataBank.

The introduction to the Employee Code of Conduct is delivered to new employees upon commencement and periodically refreshed during their tenure at PermataBank. The socialization of the code of conduct is carried out with training through e-learning regarding the Code of Conduct and the Compliance Culture, as well as anti-corruption procedures. Oversight of the code of conduct implementation is reported and evaluated by the Compliance Director in the Risk Profile Report.

In addition to the Employee Code of Conduct, PermataBank has also established the Code of Conduct for the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, and Supporting Organs. Pursuant to the Code of Conduct, in conducting their duties and responsibilities, the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors must ground themselves with the following series of ethics:

- Model ethics.
- Ethics related to compliance with laws and regulations.
- Ethics related to disclosure and confidentiality of information.
- Ethics related to opportunity.
- Ethics related to conflicts of interest, whereas members of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors are required to act professionally.
- Ethics related to avoid compromise due to gift and entertainment.
- Ethics related to insider information.
- Ethics related to bribery and corruption.
- Ethics related to the prevention of money laundering.
- Ethics to treat employees fairly.
- Ethics related to political activities.

The Code of Conduct is regularly disseminated and has been uploaded to PermataBanks' website.
