# Tugas dan Tanggung Jawab Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dalam Keuangan Berkelanjutan

Terkait dengan penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan, Dewan Komisaris memiliki tanggung jawab dalam menyetujui Rencana Aksi Keuangan Berkelanjutan (RAKB) yang disusun oleh Direksi. Sedangkan Direksi memiliki tanggung jawab untuk melaksanakan RAKB yang telah disetujui serta melakukan pengawasan atas pelaksanaan program RAKB secara berkala paling sedikit setiap enam bulan sekali. Unit kerja terkait yang mengembangkan produk dan/atau jasa keuangan berkelanjutan, wajib memastikan RAKB telah diimplementasikan sesuai dengan indikator yang ditetapkan.

Dalam hal RAKB tidak dapat dilaksanakan dengan baik atau tidak mencapai tujuan yang diinginkan, Direksi akan meninjau kembali keseluruhan program khususnya program jangka panjang serta melihat kesesuaian program tersebut dengan strategi PermataBank secara bankwide dan indikator keberhasilan program. Selain itu, Direksi juga memiliki tanggung jawab melaporkan secara berkala implementasi RAKB kepada Dewan Komisaris.

The Board of Directors is fully responsible for the management of PermataBank for the interests and objectives of PermataBank and represents PermataBank, both inside and outside the Court, in accordance with the provisions of PermataBank's Articles of Association and the applicable regulations.

More detailed implementation of duties and responsibilities of the governance organs can be viewed in the 2019 Annual Report of PermataBank in the Governance section on page 446.

# Duties and Responsibilities of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors in Sustainable Finance

In relations to the implementation of sustainable finance, the Board of Commissioners is responsible for approving the Sustainable Finance Action Plan (RAKB) prepared by the Board of Directors. The Board of Directors is responsible for implementing the approved RAKB as well as periodically monitoring the implementation of RAKB at least every six months. Related units that prepare sustainable finance products and/or services must ensure that the RAKB has been implemented in accordance with the determined indicators.

In the event that the RAKB cannot be properly implemented or does not accomplish the set objectives, the Board of Directors will review the entire program, particularly the long-term program while examining the suitability of the program with the bankwide PermataBank's strategy as well as the indicators of the program's success. In addition, the Board of Directors is also responsible for periodically reporting the RAKB implementation to the Board of Commissioners.
