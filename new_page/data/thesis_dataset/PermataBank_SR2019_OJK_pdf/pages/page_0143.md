# Manajemen Risiko

Risk Management

PermataBank menerapkan manajemen risiko secara terpadu, dari tahap awal hingga akhir, guna mengendalikan dan mengoptimalkan profil risiko terhadap pendapatannya. Penerapan manajemen risiko merupakan aktivitas bankwide yang merupakan cerminan komitmen PermataBank terhadap prinsip kehati-hatian. Di PermataBank, manajemen risiko dikelola melalui Risk Management Committee (RMC) dan Asset Liability Committee (ALCO) di tingkat Direksi dan Risk Monitoring Committee (RMoC) di tingkat Dewan Komisaris.

PermataBank menggunakan Tipe Risiko untuk memastikan identifikasi risiko yang dan komprehensif dan konsisten, di mana pun risiko tersebut muncul. Setiap Tipe Risiko dikelola oleh RCO yang ditunjuk oleh Direksi. Adapun tipe risiko yang dikelola oleh RCO adalah sebagai berikut: Kredit, Pasar, Likuiditas Jangka Pendek, Likuiditas Struktural, Lintas Batas Negara, Permodalan, Reputasi, Strategik, Pensiun, Operasional, Imbal Hasil pada Unit Usaha Syariah, Investasi Ekuitas pada Unit Usaha Syariah dan Transaksi Intra-Grup terkait dengan Konglomerasi Keuangan. Mengingat luasnya lingkup Risiko Operasional, terdapat beberapa Sub Tipe Risiko Operasional. RCO juga bertanggung jawab untuk mengelola Sub Tipe Risiko Operasional: External Rules &amp; Regulations, Liability, Legal Enforceability, Damage or Loss of Physical Assets, Safety &amp; Security, Internal Fraud or Dishonesty, External Fraud, Information Security, Processing Failure, Model dan Technology Management.

PermataBank melaporkan pengelolaan sebelas jenis risiko berdasarkan peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK), yaitu: (1) Risiko Kredit, (2) Risiko Pasar, (3) Risiko Operasional, (4) Risiko Likuiditas, (5) Risiko Hukum, (6) Risiko Strategik, (7) Risiko Kepatuhan, (8) Risiko Reputasi, (9) Risiko Imbal Hasil, (10) Risiko Investasi, dan (11) Risiko Transaksi Intra-Grup.

PermataBank implements risk management in an integrated manner, from the first to the last phase, in order to control and optimize the risk profile against income. The implementation of risk management is a bankwide activity, which is a reflection of PermataBank's commitment to the principle of prudence. At PermataBank, risk management is managed through the Risk Management Committee (RMC) and Asset Liability Committee (ALCO) at the Board of Directors level and Risk Monitoring Committee (RMoC) at the Board of Commissioners level.

PermataBank uses Risk Types to ensure a comprehensive and consistent risk identification, wherever the risk arises. Risk Types managed by the RCO is as follows: Credit, Market, Short-term Liquidity, Structural Liquidity, Cross Country Border, Capital, Reputation, Strategic, Pension, Operations, Rate of Return of the Sharia Business Unit, Equity Investment of the Sharia Business Unit, and Intra-Group Transaction related to Financial Conglomerate. Considering the broad scope of Operational Risk, there are a number of Operational Risk Sub Types. The RCO is also responsible for managing the Operational Risk Sub Types: External Rules &amp; Regulations, Liability, Legal Enforceability, Damage or Loss of Physical Assets, Safety &amp; Security, Internal Fraud or Dishonesty, External Fraud, Information Security, Processing Failure, Model and Technology Management.

PermataBank reports the management of eleven risk types based on the regulation of the Financial Services Authority (OJK), namely: (1) Credit Risk, (2) Market Risk, (3) Operational Risk, (4) Liquidity Risk, (5) Legal Risk, (6) Strategic Risk, (7) Compliance Risk, (8) Reputation Risk, (9) Rate of Return Risk, (10) Investment Risk, and (11) Intra-Group Transaction Risk.
