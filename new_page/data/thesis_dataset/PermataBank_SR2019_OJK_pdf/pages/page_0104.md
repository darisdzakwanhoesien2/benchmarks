Social Economic Contribution of PermataBank

Kredit perbankan dimanfaatkan oleh pengusaha untuk membiayai kegiatan dan investasi sehingga dapat menyerap tenaga kerja, di samping itu dampak ekonomi juga timbul pengeluaran yang dilakukan nasabah yang dibiayai PermataBank. Kontribusi ekonomi PermataBank ke dalam perkonomian Indonesia juga diwujudkan dalam bentuk penyerapan tenaga kerja, pembayaran pajak dan retribusi serta pembelian barang dan jasa.

Selain dari kegiatan usaha, PermataBank memberikan kontribusi kepada masyarakat melalui program CSR PermataHati. PermataBank telah melaksanakan tanggung jawab sosial dan lingkungan sejak tahun 2002 dengan fokus kegiatan edukasi literasi perbankan terutama melalui kegiatan yang berkaitan dengan dunia pendidikan.

Banking loans are utilized by entrepreneurs to finance activities and investments, subsequently absorbing employment, in addition, economic impacts also arise through expenditures made by customers who are financed by PermataBank. PermataBank's economic contribution to the Indonesian economy is also manifested in the form of employment, payment of taxes and fees, as well as the purchase of goods and services.

In addition to business activities, PermataBank contributes to the communities through the PermataHati CSR programs. PermataBank has implemented corporate social responsibility since 2002 with a focus on banking literacy education activities, particularly through activities related to education.
