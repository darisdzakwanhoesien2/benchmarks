Sustainability Report Supporting Information

Selama tahun 2019, PermataBank terus melakukan peningkatan kinerja untuk memberikan manfaat kepada seluruh pemangku kepentingan dan menerapkan keuangan berkelanjutan sesuai dengan strategi jangka panjang yang telah kami tetapkan, melalui pengembangan perbankan digital, penyaluran kredit yang inklusif serta menerapkan pengelolaan Lingkungan, Sosial, dan Tata Kelola (LST).

During 2019, PermataBank continued to improve its performance to provide benefits to the stakeholders and implemented sustainable finance in accordance with the long-term strategy that we have established, through the development of digital banking, inclusive loan disbursement, as well as the implementation of Environment, Social, and Governance (ESG) management.
