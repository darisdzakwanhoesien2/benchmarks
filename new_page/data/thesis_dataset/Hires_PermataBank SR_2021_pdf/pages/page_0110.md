# Program Vaksin Gotong Royong

Dalam rangka mendukung Pemerintah dalam penanganan virus COVID-19, PermataBank melaksanakan program Vaksin Gotong Royong (VGR) yang ditujukan untuk kerugawan PermataBank beserta dengan keluarga karyawan (pasangan dan anak yang berusia di atas 18 tahun). Program VGR diadakan secara bertahap di bulan Juni untuk karyawan yang berada di wilayah Jabodetabek yang kemudian dilanjutkan dengan pemberian dosis kedua di bulan Juli secara serentak di Istora Senayan.

- Routine disinfectant at the Head Office and Branch Offices in need.
- Cleaning of Air Conditioner (AC) filter at the Head Office, which is performed once a week.
- Paging of COVID-19 health protocol reminders at the Head Office twice a day.
- Body temperature checks prior to entering work area buildings.
- Provision of BCP rooms as needed.

In addition to the abovestated programs, PermataBank also conducts COVID-19 transmission prevention programs, such as:
- COVID Awareness Staff Communication Series, through the Director Message and President Director Message.
- New COVID Center of PermataBank that can be accessed via cellular phones.
- Webinar on Safe Guide for Self-Isolation by the COVID Center of PermataBank, which was held on 15 July 2021.
- Distribution of the Self-Isolation Guideline e-book.
- About COVID through the HR Info series.
- COVID-19 Self-Assessment using Google Form for those who are WFO.
- COVID-19 Survivor Plasma Donor Program with PermataClub.
- Employee Assistance Program, consultation service aimed at maintaining the mental health of employees.

# Gotong Royong Vaccine Program

In the context of supporting the Government in the handling of the COVID-19 virus, PermataBank implemented the Gotong Royong Vaccine (VGR) program, which is aimed at PermataBank employees and their families (spouse and children over the age of 18). The VGR program was carried out in stages in June for employees in the Greater Jakarta area, subsequently followed-up with the provision of the second dose in July simultaneously at Istora Senayan.
