## Pendirian Dua (2) Unit Bank Wakaf Mikro (BWM)

PermataBank melalui Unit Usaha Syariah bekerja sama dengan Yayasan Bangun Sejahtera Mitra (BSM) Umat mendirikan 2 unit Bank Wakaf Mikro di kota Tasikmalaya dan Lampung. Yayasan BSM Umat sendiri merupakan organisasi yang telah direkomendasikan OJK dan memiliki pengalaman luas dalam melakukan pendampingan terhadap 60 Lembaga Keuangan Mikro Syariah.

Dalam hal ini, PermataBank menyalurkan dana CSR sebesar Rp16.000.000.000,- (enam belas miliar Rupiah) untuk pendirian 2 unit bank yang selanjutnya dikelola oleh Yayasan BSM Umat dengan Pengawasan dari OJK. Departemen Koperasi Usaha Kecil Menengah, juga Pesantren dan Tokoh Agama/Masyarakat setempat. Pendirian 2 unit bank tersebut di Pondok Pesantren Ormas Nahdhatul Ulama (NU) di Pondok Pesantren Cipasung di Singapura, Tasikmalaya dan di Pondok Pesantren Ormas Persyarikatan Muhammadiyah di Lampung. [GRI 413-1]

## Establishment of Two (2) Micro Waqf Banks (BWM)

Through its Sharia Business Unit, PermataBank together with Bangun Sejahtera Mitra (BSM) Umat Foundation established 2 Micro Waqf Banks in the cities of Tasikmalaya and Lampung. The BSM Umat Foundation itself is an organization that has been recommended by OJK and has extensive experience in providing assistance to 60 Sharia Microfinance Institutions.

In this case, PermataBank distributed Rp16.000.000.000 (sixteen billion Rupiah) in CSR funds for the establishment of 2 banks which were then managed by BSM Umat Foundation with supervision from OJK. Department of Small and Medium Enterprises Cooperatives, as well as Islamic Boarding Schools and Religious/ Community Leaders. The establishment of the 2 banks at the Nahdhatul Ulama (NU) Islamic Boarding School in Cipasung Islamic Boarding School in Singapura, Tasikmalaya and at the Ormas Persyarikatan Muhammadiyah Islamic Boarding School in Lampung.
