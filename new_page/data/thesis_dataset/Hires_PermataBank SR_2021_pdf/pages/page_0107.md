Responsible Financial Services

Mengingat risiko K3 tertinggi dalam kegiatan operasional perbankan normal adalah risiko yang melekat pada keselamatan gedung, Departemen HSE Management menerapkan program-program K3 antara lain:

Given that the highest OHS risk in normal banking operations is the risk attached to building safety, the HSE Management Department implements the following building OHS programs:

|  No | Program Kerja Work Program | Penjelasan Description | Pencapaian Achievement  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  1 | Initial Building Assessment (IBA) | Penilaian awal gedung terhadap persyaratan HSE pada lokasi baru dan relokasi yang akan dijadikan kantor operasional. Initial assessment of the building against HSE requirements at new locations as well as relocations that will be used as operational offices. | Dilakukan IBA untuk setiap penentuan kantor baru. IBA is carried out in the determination of each new office.  |
|  2 | Layout Approval | Proses persetujuan terhadap desain renovasi, refurbishment, kantor cabang baru atau relokasi. Approval process for renovation, refurbishment, new branch office or relocation designs. | Layout Approval untuk semua design layout. Layout Approval for all design layout.  |
|  3 | HSE Design Review | Proses evaluasi dan rekomendasi terhadap gambar rancangan/layout kantor yang dikirimkan oleh tim Project Management dengan menambahkan perlengkapan dan persyaratan HSE yang dibutuhkan. The process of evaluating and recommending office design/layout drawings submitted by the Project Management team by adding the required HSE equipment and requirements. | Gambar rancangan cabang atau renovasi sudah memasukkan perlengkapan HSE yang dibutuhkan. The branch design or renovation drawings have included the required HSE equipment.  |
|  4 | Asesmen Struktur Structure Assessment | Kegiatan yang bertujuan untuk mengidentifikasi adanya kerusakan-kerusakan pada struktur bangunan yang dapat mengurangi kekuatan struktur bangunan dan membahayakan keselamatan penghuni termasuk memberikan rekomendasi perbaikannya. The activity that aims to identify any damage to the building structure that can reduce the strength of the building structure and endanger the safety of the occupants, including providing repair recommendations. | Asesmen Struktur untuk 14 kantor cabang yang telah berumur > 25 tahun. Structural Assessment for 14 branch offices that are > 25 years old.  |
|  5 | Building Risk Assessment (BRA) & Branch Security Assessment (BSA) | Proses pemeriksaan yang dilakukan untuk mengidentifikasi bahaya dan ancaman di tempat kerja, melakukan analisa, pengelolaan HSE dan penilaian risiko di kantor cabang. Inspection process carried out to identify hazards and threats in the workplace, conduct analysis, management of HSE and risk assessment at branch offices. | Telah dilakukan pada 11 kantor cabang utama. Dengan jumlah temuan 39 dan 38 diantaranya sudah diselesaikan (per Desember 2021). Has been conducted at 11 main branch offices. With a total of 39 findings and 38 of them have been completed (as of December 2021).  |
|  6 | Network Quality Office Contest (NQOC) | Pemeriksaan lapangan yang dilakukan langsung oleh tim Network (Branch) untuk identifikasi bahaya dan kegiatan operasional. Field inspections carried out directly by the Network (Branch) team for hazard identification and operational activities. | Dilakukan tiap kuartal untuk seluruh kantor cabang. Conducted every quarter for all branch offices.  |
