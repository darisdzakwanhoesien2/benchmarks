# Pelatihan K3

PermataBank menyelenggarakan pelatihan K3 yang bersifat mandatory untuk setiap PermataBankers dan karyawan yang ditunjuk dalam organisasi dan komite K3 di masing-masing kantor Bank. Topik-topik pelatihan K3 telah dikembangkan menjadi modul E-Learning yang dapat diakses oleh PermataBankers melalui portal intranet PermataBank, salah satu modul yaitu HSE E-learning. Modul baru hasil pengembangan telah dimanfaatkan sejak Juni 2021 dan modul P3K E-learning sedang dalam tahap pengembangan.

Pada 2021, sebanyak 6.863 PermataBankers (92,61% dari total PermataBankers) telah menyelesaikan program E-learning HSE dan jumlah petugas first aider yang telah lulus pelatihan melalui E-learning sebanyak 246 orang dari 268 petugas P3K (90,32%). PermataBank juga menyelenggarakan program Sertifikasi Kompetensi bidang LK3 antara lain training sertifikasi Ahli K3 Umum. Pada tahun 2021, lima orang karyawan PermataBank mengikuti pelatihan yang menjadi persyaratan peraturan ini.

Selain melalui pelatihan formal, kesadaran PermataBankers tentang keselamatan dan kesehatan juga terus ditingkatkan melalui komunikasi K3, antara lain PC Wallpaper dengan tema Kampanye Bulan K3 Nasional serta materi terkait HSE ke seluruh Indonesia untuk mendukung kampanye bulan K3 Nasional pada bulan Januari – Februari 2021.

# Insiden terkait K3

Kronologis insiden yang terjadi sebagai berikut:

- Pada tanggal 19 Agustus 2021 terjadi insiden kebakaran di salah satu kantor cabang PermataBank.
- Bagian depan (sign wall) kantor cabang Kaliurang – Yogyakarta tersambar api karena kebakaran dari bangunan di sebelah kantor cabang tersebut.
- Mitigasi yang dilakukan pada saat itu adalah pemadaman oleh pemadam kebakaran setempat, mematikan aliran listrik, pemeriksaan listrik oleh petugas PLN, serta penjagaan oleh petugas satpam.
- Kantor cabang sudah kembali normal beroperasi pada tanggal 23 Agustus 2021 dan telah selesai dilakukan perbaikan facade yang terbakar. [GRI 403-2]

# OHS Training

PermataBank organizes mandatory OHS training for every PermataBankers and employees appointed in the OHS organization and committee in each of the Bank's office. OHS training topics have been developed into E-Learning modules that can be accessed by PermataBankers through the PermataBank intranet portal, one of which is the HSE E-learning. The new developed module has been in use since June 2021 and the First Aid E-learning module is currently under development.

In 2021, 6,863 PermataBankers (92.61% of the total PermataBankers) have completed the HSE E-learning program and the number of first aider officers who have passed the training through E-learning is 246 people out of 268 First Aid officers (90.32%). PermataBank also organizes a Competency Certification program in the field of LK3, including training for General OHS Expert certification. In 2021, five PermataBank employees have attended the training that is a requirement of this regulation.

Apart from formal training, the awareness of PermataBankers' to health and safety is also continuously improved through OHS communication, including PC Wallpaper with the National OHS Month Campaign theme as well as materials related to HSE throughout Indonesia to support the National OHS Month campaign in January – February 2021.

# OHS Incident

The chronology of the incident as follows:

- On 19 August 2021, there was a fire incident at one of PermataBank's branch offices.
- The front (sign wall) of the Kaliurang – Yogyakarta branch office caught fire due to a fire from the building next to the branch office.
- Mitigation carried out at that time was blackouts by the local fire department, turning off electricity, checking electricity by PLN officers, as well as guard duty by security officers.
- The branch office returned to normal operation on 23 August 2021. Furthermore, repairs have also been made to the burnt facade. [GRI 403-2]
