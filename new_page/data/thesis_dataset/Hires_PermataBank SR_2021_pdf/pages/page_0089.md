In collaboration with ThisAble and its partners, in 2021 PermataHati was able to empower people with disabilities to become ready-to-work professionals by providing intensive training and internship for ten (10) people as Baristas; for 10 people in the field of Hospitality; for nine (9) people as Telesales; and for eight (8) people as Make Up Artists; as well as by granting educational scholarships to 15 beneficiaries at the University level. This program also provides training for four (4) disabled entrepreneurs with various types of businesses, and supports them with capital assistance.

PermataHati also collaborated with Precious One in providing assistance program for thirty seven (37) MSME with disabilities from Jakarta and a number of cities on the islands of Java and Sumatra. This program consists of general classes and intensive mentoring on business development materials presented by practitioners with various specializations, such as Sales and Marketing; Branding; Product Design; to Financial and Investment Management.

In partnership with DARE Foundation, PermataHati also empowered 10 people with disabilities through the granting of prosthetic legs. These 10 beneficiaries consist of 2 children and 8 adults who act as the breadwinner in their families. The prosthesis aid serves as a tool of assistance for their mobilization, allowing them to actively participate in society, as well as leveraging their quality of lives in terms of education and financial condition.
