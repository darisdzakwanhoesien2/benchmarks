# PermataBankir Cilik

Melalui program PermataBankir Cilik 2021, sebuah program berdurasi satu bulan bagi anak-anak sekolah dasar, PermataHati kembali berkolaborasi dengan Pibo (Perpustakaan anak digital pertama di Indonesia - www.bacapibo.com) untuk memberikan pembelajaran serta menanamkan kebiasaan menabung sejak dini melalui berbagai aktivitas dan kelas daring yang kreatif. Pada tahun ini, PermataBankir Cilik diikuti 150 peserta dari 15 Sekolah Dasar (SD) di Jakarta, Tangerang, Tangerang Selatan, Bandung, Yogyakarta, dan Malang, termasuk Sekolah Luar Biasa (SLB). Kali ini PermataHati bersama Pibo menggandeng Kinarya Gagas—sebuah komunitas pelatihan bagi pengajar yang berbasis di Bandung—untuk memberikan workshop bagi para guru pendamping. Materi pelatihan yang diberikan mencakup strategi pengajaran atraktif secara daring untuk anak-anak, serta panduan bagi para guru agar dapat lebih membimbing para siswa dalam mengikuti program PermataBankir Cilik.

Semua peserta PermataBankir Cilik 2021 mengikuti kelas tantangan membaca di www.bacapibo.com, membuat laporan keuangan sederhana, mempelajari pengelolaan keuangan dengan konsep 3-in (Tabung-in, Jajan-in, Bagi-in), mengikuti kelas menulis cerita pendek (cerpen), mengikuti kelas story telling, serta mengenal seluk beluk mengenal bank dengan mengikuti "Virtual Tour" ke kantor cabang PermataBank di Bogor dan Jakarta, dan berkunjung ke Museum Bank Indonesia. Di akhir program, para peserta dinyatakan lulus dan dinobatkan menjadi Duta Menabung 2021, serta berhak mendapatkan tabungan Simpanan Pelajar (SimPel) dari PermataBank. [GRI 413-1, FS16]

# PermataBankir Cilik

Through the 2021 PermataBankir Cilik program, a one-month program for elementary school students, PermataHati once again collaborated with Pibo (Indonesia's first digital children's library - www.bacapibo.com) to build awareness and instill the habit of saving from an early age through various creative activities and online classes for children. This year, PermataBankir Cilik engaged 150 students from 15 elementary schools (SD) in Jakarta, Tangerang, South Tangerang, Bandung, Yogyakarta, and Malang, including Special Schools (SLB). This time, PermataHati and Pibo collaborated with Kinarya Gagas—a training community for teachers that is based in Bandung—to arrange workshops for assistant teachers. The training materials provided include online teaching strategies, as well as practical tips for teachers to enable them to better guide their students in participating in PermataBankir Cilik program.

All participants of the 2021 PermataBankir Cilik were required to take a reading challenge class at www.bacapibo.com, make simple financial reports, learn financial management with the 3-in concept (Tabung-in, Jajan-in, Bagi-in which translates to Save-it, Spend-it, Share-it), take short story writing and story-telling classes, and take a "Virtual Tour" of PermataBank branch offices in Bogor and Jakarta, as well as visit to the Bank Indonesia Museum. At the end of the program, students graduated and were officially appointed as 2021 Savings Ambassadors, and were entitled to receive Simpanan Pelajar (Student Savings/ SimPel) savings from PermataBank. [GRI 413-1, FS16]
