# Survei Efektivitas PermataBanker

Untuk memahami tingkat engagement dan keefektifan pengelolaan sumber daya manusia, PermataBank melakukan survei tahunan Change for Better Survey yang diselenggarakan oleh konsultan global eksternal. Pada survei yang dilakukan pada 2021, 92% dari karyawan telah menyatakan kebanggaan mereka terhadap PermataBank serta 95% menyatakan PermataBank tempat yang baik untuk berkontribusi. Indeks Efektivitas Karyawan secara keseluruhan meningkat 0,5% dibandingkan tahun lalu atau 15% dibandingkan tahun 2019. Efektivitas diukur dengan dua masukan utama, yaitu indeks Keterikatan dan Pemberdayaan Karyawan. Skor tertinggi didapat untuk dimensi terkait dengan arahan strategi bisnis dan kualitas pelayanan kepada nasabah. Peningkatan indeks tercermin di hampir seluruh Direktorat, di seluruh dimensi, serta diatas seluruh norma yang dijadikan acuan. [GRI 103-3]

Moreover, Permatabank also implements Flexible Benefit which provides PermataBankers the flexibility to choose and obtain the best benefits according to their personal needs. For PermataBankers who are entering normal retirement age, PermataBank provides a pension benefit program. The programs include the opportunity to take part in training before retirement, taking "day off" a maximum of 1 (one) week per month as well as additional retirement benefits.

# PermataBanker Effectiveness Survey

To understand the level of engagement and effectiveness of human resource management, Permata Bank conducts Change for Better Survey on yearly basis which are carried out by an external global consultant. In the survey conducted in 2021, 92% of employees have expressed their pride in Permata Bank and 95% stated that PermataBank is a good place to contribute. The overall Employee Effectiveness Index increased by 0.5% compared to last year, reaching 15% compared to 2019. Effectiveness is measured by two main inputs, namely the Employee Engagement and Empowerment index. The highest score was obtained for the dimensions related to the direction of business strategy and quality of service to customers. The increase in the index occurred in almost all Directorates, in all dimensions, and at above all reference indicators. [GRI 103-3]
