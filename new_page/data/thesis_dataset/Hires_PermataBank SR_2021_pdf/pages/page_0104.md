Tentang Laporan Keberlanjutan About Sustainability Report

Management di bawah Divisi Corporate Real Estate Services (CRES), Direktorat Technology &amp; Operations (T&amp;O). PermataBank menetapkan visi K3 yaitu menjadi "Perusahaan yang sehat, aman &amp; ramah lingkungan untuk mencapai Bisnis Berkelanjutan." Tujuan pengelolaan K3 adalah mencegah insiden dan kecelakaan, kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja, mencegah pencemaran lingkungan, mematuhi peraturan dan persyaratan yang terkait di bidang K3.

Sesuai dengan ketentuan peraturan, PermataBank membentuk Panitia Pembina Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (P2K3) atau Komite K3 di 13 kantor dengan jumlah di atas 100 orang. Komite K3 merupakan wadah kerja sama antara pengusaha dan tenaga kerja untuk mengembangkan kerja sama dan partisipasi efektif dalam penerapan K3 di lingkungan Perusahaan. Komite K3 mengadakan forum/pertemuan untuk membahas mengenai LK3 secara rutin setiap kuartal dan mengirimkan pelaporan ke Disnaker setempat. [GRI 103-2]

PermataBank memeriksa keefektifan sistem manajemen K3 secara periodik melalui audit tahunan. Pada tahun 2021, PermataBank telah melakukan HSE Management Audit (HMA) untuk memeriksa HSE di 11 Kantor Cabang Utama. [GRI 103-3]

Management under the Corporate Real Estate Services (CRES) Division, Directorate of Technology &amp; Operations (T&amp;O). PermataBank has established its OHS vision, namely to become a "Healthy, safe, and environmentally-friendly Company in order to achieve Sustainable Business". The objective of OHS management is to prevent occupational health and safety incidents and accidents, prevent environmental pollution, and comply with the applicable regulations and requirement in the field of OHS.

Pursuant to regulatory provisions, PermataBank has established the Occupational Health and Safety Development Committee (P2K3) or OHS Committee in 13 offices with more than 100 employees. The OHS Committee is a forum for collaboration between employer and workers to develop effective cooperation and participation in OHS implementation within the Company. The OHS Committee regularly holds forums/meetings to discuss LK3 every quarter and submit reports to the local Manpower office. [GRI 103-2]

PermataBank periodically reviews the effectiveness of OHS management through annual audits. In 2021, PermataBank has performed the HSE Management Audit (HMA) to inspect HSE in 11 Main Branch Offices. [GRI 103-3]
