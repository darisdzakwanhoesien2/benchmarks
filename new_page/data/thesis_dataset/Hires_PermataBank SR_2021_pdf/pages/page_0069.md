Responsible Financial Services

Sebagai tempat kerja yang inklusif, peluang yang setara juga kami berikan kepada penyandang disabilitas untuk bekerja di PermataBank. Saat ini terdapat 21 tenaga kerja disabilitas yang bekerja di bagian telesales, divisi bisnis hingga fungsional. Kami memberikan perhatian khusus bagi penyandang disabilitas dengan mendukung pengembangan mereka melalui program Permata BRAVE dan bekerja sama dengan lembaga vokasi disabilitas untuk meningkatkan kemampuan dan kemandirian mereka. Informasi lebih rinci tentang bagaimana kami membantu penyandang disabilitas dapat dibaca di bagian 'Mendukung Masyarakat'.

PermataBank mewujudkan keadilan di tempat kerja dengan memberikan peluang yang sama untuk semua orang dalam proses rekrutmen, pengembangan kompetensi, evaluasi kinerja, remunerasi, maupun karier, tidak memandang perbedaan suku, agama, dan ras seseorang. Kinerja karyawan dinilai secara obyektif, transparan, dan timbal balik sedangkan pengembangan individu disesuaikan dengan kinerja yang dinilai secara obyektif dan sejalan dengan kebutuhan dan strategi PermataBank.

As an inclusive workplace, we also provided equal work opportunities for people with disabilities to work at PermataBank. Currently, there are 21 workers with disabilities filling in various positions from telesales, business, to functional divisions. We pay special attention to people with disabilities by supporting their development through the Permata BRAVE program and collaborate with disability vocational institutions to improve their abilities and independence. More detailed information on how we assist people with disabilities can be found in the 'Supporting Communities' section.

PermataBank realizes equality in the workplace by providing equal opportunities for everyone in the process of recruitment, competency development, performance evaluation, remuneration, and career, regardless of ethnicity, religion, and race. Employee performance is assessed in an objective, transparent, and reciprocal manner, while individual development is adjusted to performance, which is assessed objectively and in line with PermataBank's needs and strategies.
