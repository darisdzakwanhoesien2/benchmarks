# Komunikasi Nasabah

## Customer Communication

Untuk mendukung peningkatan kualitas layanan bagi nasabah dan keunggulan operasional, PermataBank memiliki Divisi Kualitas Layanan dan Keunggulan Operasional yang menaungi Departemen Contact Center (PermataTel) dan Departemen Customer Care Center (CCC). Departemen Contact Center (PermataTel) merupakan departemen yang melayani kebutuhan nasabah maupun non nasabah terhadap informasi, permintaan ataupun keluhan terkait produk perbankan maupun fasilitas pinjaman (Kartu Kredit, Personal Loan, dan KPR) melalui telepon, surat elektronik, dan media sosial. Sedangkan departemen Customer Care Center merupakan departemen yang melayani kebutuhan nasabah maupun non nasabah terhadap keluhan terkait produk dan layanan perbankan dengan memberikan penyelesaian yang cepat dan tepat dan juga departemen yang melakukan survei atas kepuasan nasabah atas penanganan pengaduan serta survei kepuasan nasabah atas produk dan layanan perbankan.

Pada akhirnya keseluruhan inisiatif dan kemampuan mengatasi tantangan tersebut berhasil meningkatkan kinerja kedua departemen. Departemen Contact Center, misalnya, berhasil meningkatkan jumlah kasus yang dapat diselesaikan dalam kurun waktu 24 jam dan &lt;2 hari dibandingkan tahun lalu dimana kasus yang dapat diselesaikan dalam kurun 24 jam meningkat 7,81% dan &lt;2 hari meningkat 4,48%. Departemen CCC berhasil menyelesaikan pengaduan nasabah kurang dari 24 jam sebesar 64,31%, kurang dari dua hari kerja sebesar 76,63% dan kurang dari lima hari kerja sebesar 89,28%.

PermataBank mengelola setiap keluhan nasabah dengan seksama sesuai dengan Service Level Agreement (SLA). Setiap keluhan nasabah akan diselesaikan dalam dua sampai dengan dua puluh hari kerja, jika perlu nasabah dapat mengajukan keluhan kembali atau menggunakan penyelesaian melalui External Dispute Resolution (EDR). To support the improvement of service quality for customers and operational excellence, PermataBank has established the Service Quality and Operational Excellence Division, which oversees the Contact Center Department (PermataTel) and the Customer Care Center (CCC) Department. The Contact Center Department (PermataTel) is a department that serves the needs of customers and non-customers for information, requests or complaints related to banking products and loan facilities (Credit Card, Personal Loan, and Mortgage) via telephone, email, and social media. Moreover, the Customer Care Center department is a department that serves the needs of customers and non-customers for complaints related to banking products and services by providing fast and appropriate resolutions and also serves as the department that conducts surveys on customer satisfaction for the handling of complaints and surveys on customer satisfaction for banking products and services.

Ultimately, the overall initiative and ability to overcome these challenges managed to improve the performance of both departments. The Contact Center Department, for example, succeeded in increasing the number of cases that could be resolved within 24 hours and &lt;2 days compared to the previous year, in which cases that could be resolved within 24 hours increased by 7.81% and &lt;2 days increased by 4.48%. The CCC Department succeeded in resolving customer complaints in less than 24 hours by 64.31%, in less than two working days by 76.63% and in less than five working days by 89.28%.

PermataBank manages every customer complaint thoroughly, in accordance with the Service Level Agreement (SLA). Each customer complaint will be resolved within two to twenty working days, if necessary, the customer may file another complaint or use a resolution through External Dispute Resolution (EDR).
