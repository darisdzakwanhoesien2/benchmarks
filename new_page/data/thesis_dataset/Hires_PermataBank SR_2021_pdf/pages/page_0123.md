# Manajemen Risiko Fraud

Fraud merupakan salah satu risiko penting dalam kegiatan perbankan, terjadinya fraud dapat mengakibatkan kerugian finansial berkaitan dengan risiko keuangan dan kerugian non-finansial dari risiko reputasi yang dapat mengurangi kepercayaan publik kepada Bank. Kerangka kerja manajemen risiko fraud termuat dalam Kebijakan Strategi Anti-Fraud yang meliputi aktivitas pencegahan, pendeteksian, pemberian tanggapan, proses investigasi dan efek jera yang dijabarkan dalam strategi yang lengkap untuk mengelola risiko fraud di PermataBank dalam rangka mencapai level tertinggi dalam hal integritas bisnis. Tanggung jawab manajemen risiko fraud berada di bawah unit kerja Fraud Risk Management (FRM), Direktorat Hukum dan Kepatuhan. [GRI 103-1]

Pencegahan kemungkinan terjadinya fraud di PermataBank, di antaranya dilakukan dengan cara:

a) Menerbitkan fraud alert berupa tulisan yang disebarkan melalui surat elektronik berisi informasi kepada seluruh karyawan tentang tren/ modus kejahatan fraud yang berkembang dengan tujuan membangun kepedulian dan kesadaran terhadap risiko fraud.
b) Melalui webinar dan e-learning Anti-Fraud, yakni salah satu pelatihan daring untuk meningkatkan kepedulian terhadap fraud dalam bentuk diskusi, animasi disertai pertanyaan, yang wajib diikuti oleh seluruh karyawan PermataBank.
c) Pelatihan/sharing session internal yang dilakukan oleh unit kerja FRM untuk pembelajaran risiko dan bentuk/tren fraud perbankan. [GRI 103-2]

# Fraud Risk Management

Fraud is one of the key risks in banking activities, fraud occurrence may lead to financial losses in relations to financial risk and non-financial losses in relations to reputation risk, which will reduce the confidence level of the public towards the Bank. The framework of fraud risk management is contained in the Anti-Fraud Strategy Policy, covering the activities of prevention, detection, response, investigation process, and deterrent effect, which is described in a comprehensive strategy to manage fraud risk in PermataBank in order to reach the highest level in terms of business integrity. The responsibility for fraud risk management is under the Fraud Risk Management (FRM) unit, Legal and Compliance Directorate. [GRI 103-1]

The prevention of possibilities of fraud in PermataBank is conducted by several measures, including:

a) Issuing fraud alerts in writing form, that are distributed via email, containing information to all employees regarding the trends/modes of fraud that are developing with the aim of building recognition and awareness to fraud risk.
b) Through Anti-Fraud webinar and e-learning, which is an online training aimed at increasing fraud awareness, which is delivered in the form of discussion, animation followed by a quiz, which is mandatory for all employees of PermataBank.
c) Internal training/sharing session conducted by the FRM unit for the education on risks and forms/trends of fraud in banking. [GRI 103-2]
