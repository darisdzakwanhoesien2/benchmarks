# Sebuah komitmen PermataBank untuk pembangunan berkelanjutan

## A commitment of PermataBank for sustainable development

PermataHati merupakan wujud komitmen tanggung jawab sosial atau Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) PermataBank. Melalui berbagai inisiatif dan kolaborasi berkelanjutan di seluruh nusantara, PermataHati berkomitmen untuk meningkatkan edukasi dan inklusi finansial, memperbaiki kualitas hidup masyarakat, memperluas akses pendidikan, serta turut berperan dalam pemberdayaan komunitas, khususnya bagi penyandang disabilitas agar mereka mendapatkan kesempatan untuk meraih sukses yang sama. [GRI 103-1]

PermataHati is a manifestation of PermataBank's commitment to Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR). Through a variety of continuous initiatives and collaborations throughout the archipelago, PermataHati is committed to increasing financial education and inclusion, improving people's quality of life, expanding access to education, and empowering communities, particularly people with disabilities to allow them the same opportunities to achieve success. [GRI 103-1]

## PermataHati

Sejak 2010, PermataHati menjalankan program-program berkelanjutan melalui tiga pilar utama, yaitu 3E: Education (Pendidikan), Empowerment (Pemberdayaan), dan Enhancement (Peningkatan Fasilitas dan Inklusivitas). PermataHati mewujudkan nilai-nilai dari ketiga pilar tersebut melalui berbagai aktivitas yang dijalankan dengan menyasar pada beberapa level pendidikan, mulai dari tingkat sekolah dasar, sekolah menengah/kejuruan, dan sekolah luar biasa, serta mencakup berbagai penerima manfaat lainnya, seperti guru-guru sekolah, generasi muda, hingga wanita dan para penyandang disabilitas, agar mereka semakin berdaya, cerdas finansial, dan siap bersaing. [GRI 103-2]

## PermataHati

Since 2010, PermataHati drives sustainable programs through three main pillars, namely 3E: Education, Empowerment, and Enhancement (Improvement of Facilities and Inclusivity). PermataHati incorporates the values of these three pillars through activities that are carried out by targeting several education levels, starting from primary school, secondary/vocational school, and special school, including various other beneficiaries, such as school teachers, the younger generation, to women and people with disabilities, allowing them to become more empowered, financially knowledgeable, and ready to compete. [GRI 103-2]
