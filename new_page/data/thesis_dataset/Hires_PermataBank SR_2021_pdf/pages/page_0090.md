# PermataYouthPreneur

PermataYouthPreneur merupakan program pendampingan digitalpreneur yang diperuntukkan bagi siswa/i SMK/SMA untuk belajar dari PermataBank sebagai salah satu bank digital terdepan di Indonesia. Dalam penyelenggaraannya, PermataHati bekerja sama dengan mitra strategis, Arkademy-sebuah akademi Teknologi, Programming, Coding, dan penempatan kerja di bidang teknologi. Program PermataYouthPreneur memberikan pembekalan bagi peserta untuk mengembangkan ide startup Teknologi Finansial (FinTech) yang mereka miliki. Modul pengembangan ide bisnis yang diberikan meliputi Customer Validation, Product Validation, hingga materi literasi finansial dan soft skills.

Pada tahun 2021, PermataYouthPreneur yang memasuki tahun ke-4 penyelenggaraannya diadakan sepenuhnya secara daring dan mengangkat tema "Membantu Pertumbuhan UMKM melalui Teknologi Finansial (FinTech)". Program ini diikuti oleh 471 peserta dari berbagai wilayah di Indonesia, mulai dari Pulau Jawa; Sumatera; Kalimantan; hingga Papua. Kemudian, 45 tim terpilih mengikuti pembelajaran selama satu bulan didampingi mentor-mentor dari PermataBank; Kementerian Pendidikan, Kebudayaan, Riset dan Teknologi Republik Indonesia (RI); Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK); serta beberapa pemimpin FinTech dan generasi muda dari berbagai institusi yaitu Telkomsel; Tokopedia; dan Flip ID. [GRI 413-1, FS16]

# PermataYouthPreneur

PermataYouthPreneur is a digitalpreneur mentoring program intended for high school/ vocational school students to learn from PermataBank as one of the leading digital banks in Indonesia. In its implementation, PermataHati collaborates with a strategic partner, Arkademy- a Technology, Programming, Coding, and job placement center. The program provides training for participants to develop their Financial Technology (FinTech) startup ideas including business idea development modules such as Customer Validation, Product Validation, financial literacy, as well as soft skills.

In 2021, PermataYouthPreneur, in its 4th year, was held entirely online with the theme "Helping MSME's Growth through Financial Technology (FinTech)". This program was attended by 471 participants from various regions in Indonesia, from the island of Java; Sumatra; Kalimantan; to Papua. Subsequently, 45 teams were selected to participate in a one-month study accompanied by mentors from PermataBank; Ministry of Education, Culture, Research and Technology of the Republic of Indonesia (RI); Financial Services Authority (OJK); as well as several FinTech leaders and young generation leaders from various institutions, namely Telkomsel; Tokopedia; and Flip ID.
