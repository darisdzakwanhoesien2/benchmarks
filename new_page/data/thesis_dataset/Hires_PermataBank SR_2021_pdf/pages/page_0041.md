# Persyaratan Lingkungan dan Sosial

PermataBank telah menerapkan kebijakan dan persyaratan kredit kepada nasabah yang mencakup persyaratan lingkungan dan sosial sebelum penyaluran dilakukan. Kebijakan dan prosedur WB Credit Policy mengatur bahwa semua proposal kredit untuk nasabah-nasabah yang terkait dengan bidang usaha/bergerak di bidang industri tertentu harus memenuhi persyaratan ESRA (Environment and Social Requirement Assessment/Penilaian Persyaratan Lingkungan dan Sosial), kecuali dinyatakan lain karena tidak berlaku untuk beberapa debitur tertentu.

Dari sisi risiko lingkungan aspek utama yang dapat timbul dari pemberian kredit terhadap industri tertentu tersebut seperti kontaminasi tanah, pencemaran air, pencemaran udara, serta proyek berskala besar yang merusak lingkungan hidup dan sosial. Sedangkan dari sisi risiko sosial yang utama yang dapat timbul seperti peraturan setempat, praktek penggunaan tenaga kerja, serta praktik bisnis yang beretika.

Berdasarkan kebijakan tersebut, industri yang diwajibkan untuk memenuhi penilaian ESRA generic (umum) yang prosedurnya harus dipenuhi setiap waktu adalah industri yang termasuk dalam kategori berisiko tinggi dan industri berisiko menengah dan rendah sesuai dengan penilaian/skor dari segi land contamination, waste management, emissions, reputational, dan sosial untuk industri seperti forestry, logging, manufacture, dan konstruksi. Sedangkan bagi industri pertambangan dan kelapa sawit wajib memenuhi persyaratan ESRA khusus (ESRA-kelapa sawit dan ESRA-pertambangan).

# Social and Environmental Requirements

PermataBank has implemented credit policies and requirements for customers that include social and environmental requirements prior to disbursement. The WB Credit Policy and procedure stipulate that all loan proposals for customers that are engaging in certain business fields/industries must meet the ESRA (Environmental and Social Requirement Assessment) requirements, unless stated otherwise as it does not apply to certain debtors.

In terms of environmental risk, the main aspect that can arise from lending to these certain industries, such as land contamination, water pollution, air pollution, as well as large-scale projects damaging the environment and social. Meanwhile, in terms of social risk, the main issues that can arise include local regulation, employment practices, as well as ethical business practices.

Based on this policy, the industries that are required to meet the generic ESRA assessment whose procedures must be met at all times are industries that are included in the high-risk category, as well as medium and low risk industries, according to the assessment/score in terms of land contamination, waste management, emissions, reputational, and social for industries engaging in forestry, logging, manufacturing, and construction activities. Meanwhile, mining and palm oil industries are required to meet the specific ESRA (ESRA-palm oil and ESRA-mining).
