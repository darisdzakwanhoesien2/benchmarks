Tentang Laporan Keberlanjutan About Sustainability Report

Penyandang Disabilitas (BBRVPD) yang berada di bawah naungan Kementerian Sosial (Kemensos) Republik Indonesia. Modul literasi finansial dalam program PermataBRAVE Hub mencakup pengenalan terhadap fungsi bank; peran Bank Indonesia (BI) dan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK); pengelolaan keuangan dasar; hingga pengetahuan mengenai berinvestasi.

Penyelenggaraan PermataBRAVE Hub tahun 2021 dilaksanakan secara hybrid dengan memberlakukan protokol kesehatan yang ketat, dan diikuti oleh 25 peserta dari sejumlah kota di Jawa Barat.

Perluasan Program - Program PermataBRAVE telah diperluas dan dikembangkan pada kuartal ke-tiga tahun 2021, demi memperbanyak cakupan penyandang disabilitas yang diberdayakan. Melalui perluasan program ini, PermataBRAVE berfokus pada ketiga pilar PermataHati CSR, yakni: Pendidikan - pelatihan vokasional sesuai minat, dan pendidikan literasi keuangan; Pemberdayaan - pendampingan keterampilan wirausaha, modal usaha bagi pelaku UMKM baru dan berpotensi, peluang penempatan kerja profesional, serta beasiswa pendidikan; dan Peningkatan Fasilitas - pemberian alat bantu mobilitas bagi yang membutuhkan.

PermataHati bekerja sama dengan 3 mitra dalam menyelenggarakan inisiatif ini, yakni ThisAble yang merupakan yayasan sosial yang aktif memberikan pelatihan kesiapan kerja, serta sebagai penyalur tenaga profesional penyandang disabilitas; Precious One yang merupakan sebuah yayasan sosial

Vocational for People with Disabilities (BBRVPD), which runs under the supervision of the Ministry of Social Affairs (Kemensos) of the Republic of Indonesia. The financial literacy module in the PermataBRAVE Hub program ranges from introduction to banking functions; roles of Bank Indonesia (BI) and Financial Services Authority (OJK); basic financial management; to investment knowledge.

The implementation of PermataBRAVE Hub in 2021 was conducted in hybrid format with strict health protocols, and attended by 25 participants from a number of cities in West Java.

Program Enhancement - The scope of the PermataBRAVE program was further broadened and strengthened in the third quarter of 2021 in order to increase the coverage and empowerment of people with disabilities. Under the new scope, PermataBRAVE provides further focus on the 3 key pillars: Education - vocational training, and financial literacy education; Empowerment - mentoring, entrepreneurial skills, business capital for aspiring entrepreneurs of MSMEs, and professional job placement as well as scholarships; and Enhancement - mobility aids for the physical, hearing and vision impaired.

PermataHati has 3 strategic partners supporting this initiative, namely ThisAble, a social foundation that actively provides job readiness training, as well as acting as a supplier of professionals with disabilities; and Precious One, a social foundation that empowers people with disabilities by providing classes as well
