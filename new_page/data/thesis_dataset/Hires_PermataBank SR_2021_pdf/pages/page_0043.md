# Produk Keuangan Inklusif

Inclusive Financial Product

Melalui perbankan ritel, PermataBank memberikan dukungan pada program PEN dengan cara membina kemitraan dan menciptakan nilai bermakna bagi nasabah UMKM.

Through the retail banking segment, PermataBank has provided support for the PEN program by fostering partnerships as well as generating significant values for its MSME customers.

Akses pembiayaan dari lembaga jasa keuangan formal yang merata atau inklusif sangat penting bagi seluruh lapisan masyarakat untuk meningkatkan perekonomian serta melindungi mereka dari kejahatan keuangan. PermataBank mendukung penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan yang dicanangkan oleh OJK dengan memberikan pembiayaan kepada usaha/nasabah yang layak namun belum memiliki akses pembiayaan dari bank yang diarahkan untuk pengembangan bisnis berkelanjutan. [GRI 103-1, 103-2]

Equal or inclusive access to financing from formal financial service institutions is crucial for all levels of the community in order to improve the economy as well as protect them from financial crimes. PermataBank supports the implementation of sustainable finance that was initiated by the OJK, by providing financing to eligible businesses/ customers but do not have access to financing from banks that are directed towards sustainable business development. [GRI 103-1, 103-2]
