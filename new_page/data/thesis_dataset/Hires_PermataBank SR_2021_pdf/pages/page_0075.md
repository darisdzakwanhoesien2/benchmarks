Responsible Financial Services

Nilai imbalan yang diterima PermataBanker akan bergantung kepada capaian Bank, Direktorat, dan masing-masing individu. Hal ini sesuai dengan aturan regulator dan prinsip tata kelola yang baik. Dalam penerapannya, PermataBank menetapkan kebijakan remunerasi dan kesejahteraan karyawan dengan mempertimbangkan:

- Kinerja keuangan Perusahaan.
- Kompetensi dan kinerja karyawan (meritocracy).
- Asas kelayakan dan kepantasan sesuai dengan posisi/jabatan.
- Praktik remunerasi yang berlaku di pasar (market competitiveness).
- Kesetaraan internal (internal equity).

Penghormatan pada hak-hak karyawan dan norma tenaga kerja, diwujudkan dalam penyelenggaraan hubungan industrial antara manajemen dan serikat pekerja yang mewakili karyawan yaitu Serikat Pekerja Bank Permata (SPBP). Komunikasi dua arah manajemen dan serikat pekerja amatlah penting terutama dalam suasana penuh ketidakpastian akibat pandemi dan ekonomi selama dua tahun terakhir, setiap dua bulan forum ini membahas isu terkait ketenagakerjaan dalam rangka penciptaan lingkungan kerja yang kondusif. Manajemen dan Serikat Pekerja juga telah bersama-sama menyepakati Perjanjian Kerja Bersama (PKB) dengan sukses tanpa adanya isu yang signifikan. Perundingan PKB terselesaikan dalam satu kali tahap perundingan, dimana PermataBank menjadi bank pertama yang sukses melaksanakan perundingan dengan Serikat Pekerja melalui media virtual. Terkait adanya Undang-Undang Cipta Kerja, PermataBank juga merupakan bank pertama yang menerapkan pasal hybrid terkait pensiun.

[GRI 102-41]

The value of rewards received by PermataBankers depends on the achievement of the Bank, Directorate, and each individual. This is in accordance with the rules of regulators and the principles of good corporate governance. In practice, PermataBank applies remuneration and employee welfare policy based on:

- The Company's financial performance.
- The employee's competency and performance (meritocracy).
- The employee's position and rank.
- The applicable remuneration practice in the banking industry (market competitiveness).
- Internal equity.

Recognition for employee rights and labor norms is manifested in the implementation of industrial relations between management and the workers union representing the employees, namely the Bank Permata Employee Union (SPBP). Two-way communication between management and workers union is key, especially in an atmosphere full of uncertainty due to the pandemic and the economy over the past two years, this forum discusses issues related to employment every two months in order to create a favorable work environment. Management and the Workers Union have also jointly agreed on a Collective Labor Agreement (CLA) successfully without any significant issues. The CLA negotiations were completed in one negotiation stage, where PermataBank became the first bank to successfully carry out negotiations with the Workers Union through virtual media. Regarding the Employment Creation Act, PermataBank is also the first bank to implement a hybrid article related to retirement.
