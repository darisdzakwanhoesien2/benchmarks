Konsistensi PermataBank mengelola K3 di tempat kerja memperoleh aspirasi dari regulator yaitu Kementerian Ketenagakerjaan dan Transmigrasi yang memberikan Penghargaan Kecelakaan Nihil 2020 (Zero Accident Award 2020). Pada tahun 2021 kantor cabang Tunjungan dan kantor cabang Panglima Sudirman telah mendapatkan penghargaan ini sebanyak tiga belas kali dan dua belas kali berturut-turut.

## Mitigasi Kondisi Pandemi COVID-19

Pandemi global akibat COVID-19 masih terus berlangsung selama tahun 2021 membuat kegiatan operasional PermataBank tetap dijalankan dengan mematuhi langkah-langkah pencegahan dan penanganan penularan COVID-19. PermataBank mempertahankan program dan protokol kesehatan di tempat kerja untuk pencegahan dan penanganan pandemi COVID-19 antara lain:

- Penyediaan dan penggunaan alat pelindung diri seperti masker, sarung tangan, dan penggunaan hand sanitizer, sarana kerja yang sehat dan bersih serta memberikan jarak atau sekat antar meja/ kursi.
- Meneruskan dan menyesuaikan penerapan Prosedur Kelangsungan Bisnis (Business Continuity Plan/BCP) dalam menghadapi pandemi dan Organisasi Satuan Tugas Penanganan COVID-19 dan Command Center.
- Pengaturan kerja (Working Arrangement) untuk proses-proses kerja yang merupakan Mission Critical (bila tidak dilakukan mengganggu pelayanan pada nasabah), dan Business Critical (bila tidak dilakukan mengganggu bisnis bank secara signifikan) serta mengatur karyawan berdasarkan itu untuk menetapkan Work From Home (WFH) dan Work From Office (WFO).
- Pengaturan perjalanan dinas maupun pribadi baik domestik maupun mancanegara.
- Penggunaan pertemuan virtual untuk meeting, training, gathering dan sejenisnya.
- Meneruskan prosedur testing dan contact tracing pada setiap kondisi.
- Memberdayakan Kapten Lantai dan petugas keamanan melakukan pengawasan terhadap pelaksanaan Protokol COVID (5M – Menjaga jarak, Menggunakan masker, Mencuci tangan pakai sabun, Menjauhi kerumunan, serta Membatasi mobilisasi dan interaksi).

The consistency of PermataBank in managing OHS in the workplace has received due recognition from the regulator, namely the Ministry of Manpower and Transmigration, which awarded the 2020 Zero Accident Award (Zero Accident Award 2020). In 2021 the Tunjungan branch office and the Panglima Sudirman branch office have respectively received this award thirteen and twelve times, consecutively.

## Mitigation of COVID-19 Pandemic Conditions

The global pandemic due to COVID-19 continued throughout 2021, and as such PermataBank's operations continue to be carried out by adhering to COVID-19 transmission prevention and handling measures. PermataBank maintains the programs and health protocols in the work place for the prevention and handling of the COVID-19 pandemic, including:

- Provision and use of personal protective equipment, such as masks, gloves, and use of hand sanitizers, healthy and clean work facilities, as well as ensuring distance or providing partitions between desks/chairs.
- Continue and adjust the implementation of Business Continuity Plan (BCP) in response to the pandemic and COVID-19 Handling Task Force Organization and Command Center.
- Working Arrangement for Mission Critical processes (will disrupt services to customers if not carried out), and Business Critical processes (will disrupt the bank's business if not carried out), as well as arranging the working hours of employees based on that to determine Work From Home (WFH) and Work From Office (WFO).
- Arrangement of business and personal travel, both domestic and overseas.
- Use of virtual meetings for meetings, training, gatherings, and others.
- Continue testing and contact tracing procedures in every condition.
- Empowering Floor Captains and security officers to supervise the implementation of the COVID Protocol (5M – Maintaining distance, Wearing masks, Washing hands with soap, Staying away from crowds, and Limiting mobilization and interaction).
