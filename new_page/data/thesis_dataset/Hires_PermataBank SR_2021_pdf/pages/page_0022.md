## Pencapaian Keuangan Berkelanjutan

Pencapaian inisiatif keuangan berkelanjutan di bidang ekonomi melalui kerja sama loan channeling dengan P2P (peer to peer) lending fintech dan Bank Perkreditan Rakyat (BPR) telah mencatatkan total kredit sebesar Rp175,8 miliar dan total kredit untuk Kredit Usaha Rakyat (KUR) senilai Rp18,4 miliar. Sampai dengan 31 Desember 2021, total penyaluran dana yang memenuhi kriteria kegiatan usaha berkelanjutan mencapai Rp508,6 miliar atau meningkat sebesar 16,30% dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya sebesar Rp437,3 miliar.

Pada tahun 2021, PermataBank menorehkan milestone baru dalam mendorong perekonomian daerah melalui penyaluran pembiayaan dan pemberdayaan masyarakat yang belum mendapatkan layanan keuangan formal yaitu lingkungan pesantren. Pada 8 Juni 2021, dengan diresmikan oleh Wakil Presiden Republik Indonesia Bapak Ma'ruf Amin, PermataBank menyalurkan dana sebesar Rp16 miliar untuk pendirian dua unit Bank Wakaf Mikro, yang ke depannya diharapkan menjadi pusat pertumbuhan ekonomi baru yang inklusif dan berbasis syariah di lingkungan pesantren.

## Sustainable Finance Achievements

The sustainable finance initiatives in the economic sector through loan channeling collaboration with P2P (peer to peer) lending fintech and Rural Banks (Bank Perkreditan Rakyat/BPR) have recorded total credit of Rp175.8 billion and total credit for the microcredit program (Kredit Usaha Rakyat/KUR) worth Rp18.4 billion. As of 31 December 2021, total loan disbursement for sustainable business activities reached Rp508.6 billion, a 16.30% increase from Rp437.3 billion in the previous year.

In 2021, PermataBank set a new milestone in boosting the regional economy through financing distribution and empowerment of people who have not received formal financial services, such as Islamic boarding schools. Inaugurated by the Vice President of the Republic of Indonesia Mr. Ma'ruf Amin on 8 June 2021, PermataBank has disbursed Rp16 billion for the establishment of two units of Micro Waqf Bank, which in the future are expected to become the growth center for an inclusive sharia-based economy in the boarding school environment.

The digitization of banking services and products has also benefited larger stakeholders. The Application Programming Interface (API) that the Bank developed for Islamic Financial Institutions has been received positive response where Sharia Rural Banks (Bank Perkreditan Rakyat Syariah/BPRS) using this product to improve their performance.
