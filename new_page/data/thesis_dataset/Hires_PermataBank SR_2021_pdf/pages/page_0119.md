# Pengembangan Kompetensi Keuangan Berkelanjutan

Untuk menerapkan keuangan berkelanjutan secara efektif, PermataBank ikut serta dalam kegiatan pengembangan kompetensi yang diselenggarakan oleh regulator dan penyelenggara eksternal lainnya, antara lain:

1. Seminar "GRI-DCP Beginner Seminar", diadakan oleh PT Bursa Efek Indonesia bersama GRI, CDP, BAPPENAS, dan ICSA, tanggal 2-3 Juni 2021.
2. Seminar "GRI-DCP Advanced Seminar", diadakan oleh PT Bursa Efek Indonesia bersama GRI, CDP, BAPPENAS, dan ICSA, tanggal 29-30 Juni 2021.
3. Webinar "Mengukur Kemajuan Korporasi terhadap Kebijakan Keberlanjutan di Indonesia", diselenggarakan oleh Collective Consulting Group (CCG) bersama dengan Carbon Disclosure Project (CDP), tanggal 19 Agustus 2021.
4. Seminar "Examining stakeholder needs for global sustainability reporting standards - what is needed to make them fit for purpose?", diadakan oleh GRI, tanggal 27 September 2021.
5. Seminar "IDX-GRI-CDP-Advanced Seminar Preparing Corporate Action Plan on TCFD dan SDGs", diadakan oleh PT Bursa Efek Indonesia dan GRI, tanggal 22 Oktober 2021.
6. Webinar "Pengenalan Aspek ESG Sebagai Salah Satu Penunjang Bisnis Berkelanjutan", penyelenggara ICSA, tanggal 19 November 2021.
7. Webinar "GRI Cross-country Learning Session for Business: Climate Change, Gender Equity &amp; Inclusion, Water &amp; Circular Economy, and OHS", diadakan oleh GRI dan PT Bursa Efek Indonesia, tanggal 9, 16, 23, dan 30 November 2021.

# Sustainable Finance Competency Development

In order to implement an effective sustainable finance, PermataBank participates in competency development programs that are organized by regulators or other external organizers, among others:

1. "GRI-DCP Beginner Seminar", organized by PT Bursa Efek Indonesia in collaboration with GRI, CDP, BAPPENAS, and ICSA, on 2-3 June 2021.
2. "GRI-DCP Advanced Seminar", organized by PT Bursa Efek Indonesia in collaboration with GRI, CDP, BAPPENAS, and ICSA, on 29-30 June 2021.
3. "Measuring Corporate Progress towards Indonesian Sustainability Policy" Webinar, organized by the Collective Consulting Group (CCG) in collaboration with Carbon Disclosure Project (CDP), on 19 August 2021.
4. "Examining stakeholder needs for global sustainability reporting standards - what is needed to make them fit for purpose?" seminar, organized by the GRI, on 27 September 2021.
5. "IDX-GRI-CDP-Advanced Seminar Preparing Corporate Action Plan on TCFD dan SDGs" seminar, organized by PT Bursa Efek Indonesia and GRI, on 22 October 2021.
6. "Introduction to ESG Aspects as One of the Supports for Sustainable Business", Webinar, organized by ICSA, on 19 November 2021.
7. "GRI Cross-country Learning Session for Business: Climate Change, Gender Equity &amp; Inclusion, Water &amp; Circular Economy, and OHS" webinar, organized by GRI and PT Bursa Efek Indonesia, on 9, 16, 23, and 30 November 2021.
