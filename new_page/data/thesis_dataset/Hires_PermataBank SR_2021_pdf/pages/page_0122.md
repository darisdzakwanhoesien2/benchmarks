# Speak-Up

Speak-Up merupakan sistem whistleblowing PermataBank untuk melaporkan pelanggaran atau potensi pelanggaran, baik berupa fraud, korupsi, konflik kepentingan, perlakuan tidak etis, maupun pelanggaran Kode Etik, termasuk kecurigaan atas tindakan pelanggaran. Setiap laporan Speak-Up yang diterima dikelola oleh Administrator Speak-Up, PIC Speak-Up, dan Speak-Up Council yang ditunjuk oleh PermataBank.

PermataBank menyediakan jalur pelaporan Speak-Up melalui:

- Situs Web: http://bit.ly/pb-speak-up
- Surat Elektronik: speak.up@permatabank.co.id
- Telepon, SMS atau WhatsApp: 0811-2937-133
- PO BOX: 4890 JKTM 12700

Mekanisme Sistem Whistleblowing (Speak-Up) di PermataBank dilakukan dengan memperhatikan prinsip-prinsip berikut:

- Laporan bersifat rahasia. Hanya Administrator Speak-Up, PIC Speak-Up, dan Speak-Up Council yang mempunyai wewenang untuk mengetahui isi laporan.
- Pelapor dapat memilih 1 dari 3 prinsip anonim, yaitu: full disclosure, partial anonymity, atau anonymous.
- Pihak pelapor berhak mendapatkan perlindungan dari PermataBank apabila dari laporannya mengakibatkan sesuatu hal yang mengancam keberadaan pelapor.
- Pihak pelapor dibebaskan dari segala sanksi bila ternyata laporannya tidak dapat dibuktikan kebenarannya.
- PermataBank menjamin semua pihak pelapor bahwa laporan-laporan mereka tidak akan mempengaruhi penilaian kinerja dan/atau perjalanan karier mereka di PermataBank.
- Dengan jaminan tersebut di atas, diharapkan pihak pelapor bersedia memberikan informasi yang lebih terperinci kepada manajemen terkait tindak lanjut atas laporan mereka.

Selama tahun 2021, Administrator Speak-Up menerima 17 laporan, di mana 7 laporan bersifat umum atau keluh kesah, 8 laporan berindikasi pelanggaran ketentuan Kode Etik, dan 2 laporan berindikasi fraud. Laporan-laporan tersebut telah ditindaklanjuti oleh PIC Speak-Up dan dilaporkan secara berkala kepada Speak-Up Council dan Direksi PermataBank. Seluruh laporan tersebut telah ditindaklanjuti.

# Speak-Up

Speak-Up is PermataBank's whistleblowing system to report violations or potential violations, whether in the form of fraud, corruption, conflicts of interest, unethical treatment, or violation of the Code of Conduct, including suspicion of violations. Each Speak-Up report received is managed by the Speak-Up Administrator, Speak-Up PIC, and Speak-Up Council appointed by PermataBank.

PermataBank provides its Speak-Up channels via:

- Website: http://bit.ly/pb-speak-up
- E-mail: speak.up@permatabank.co.id
- Phone, SMS or WhatsApp: 0811-2937-133
- PO BOX: 4890 JKTM 12700

The Whistleblowing System mechanism (Speak-Up) at PermataBank is implemented with due observance of the following principles:

- Confidential report. Only the Speak-Up Administrators, Speak-Up PIC, and Speak-Up council have the authority to access the contents of the report.
- A whistleblower may choose 1 from 3 principles of anonymity, namely: full disclosure, partial anonymity, or anonymous.
- The reporting party is entitled to protection from PermataBank if the report leads to potential threats to the whistleblower.
- The whistleblower will not be held accountable if his/her report is unproven.
- PermataBank guarantees that the report submitted by a whistleblower will not affect his/her performance evaluation/ career in PermataBank.
- With such guarantee, the whistleblower is expected to provide detailed information to the management related to follow-ups concerning their reports.

During 2021, the Speak-Up Administrator received 17 reports, 7 of which were general reports or grievances, 8 reports were concerning the indication of the Code of Conduct violations, and 2 reports were concerning fraud indications. These reports have been followed-up by the Speak-Up PIC and periodically reported to the Speak-Up Council and the Board of Directors of PermataBank. All reports have been followed-up.
