Aspek lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola telah diintegrasikan dalam kebijakan kredit wholesale banking, selain persyaratan yang berlaku umum seperti perizinan dan dokumen lingkungan.

PermataBank ensures that the capacity of its supporting infrastructure, including digital services, always meets the needs of users. PermataBank also makes sure that system stability is well maintained for optimal performance and security.

In the social sector, PermataHati has provided more than 2,300 students at primary and secondary education levels with better financial literacy through a virtual gamification approach. PermataHati's flagship program that focuses on people with disabilities is PermataBRAVE. In 2021, the program has helped more than 50 people with disabilities to start and develop their own businesses. Through this, we have learned that disability is not a deficiency that requires special treatment, but a motivation to work and be independent with equal opportunities and rewards.

The pandemic that is still suppressing economic growth and causing health risks are areas of concern for PermataBank. Together with Perbanas and 49 banks in Indonesia, PermataBank has been involved in the Gotong Royong Vaccination (GRV) program initiated by the Government of Republic of Indonesia for the private sector. The total of PermataBank's employee who have received the complete COVID-19 vaccine through GRV program has reached 99.67% and their family members as well as PermataBank outsources also included in this GRV program. In addition, together with the crowdfunding Kitabisa platform, PermataBank has raised more than Rp120 million, which have been donated to health workers and MSMEs affected by the pandemic in DKI Jakarta, Java, and Bali.
