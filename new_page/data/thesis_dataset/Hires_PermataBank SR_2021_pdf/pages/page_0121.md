Selama tahun 2021, beberapa inisiatif yang dilakukan untuk meningkatkan budaya kepatuhan sekaligus untuk meningkatkan awareness terhadap Kode Etik Perilaku Karyawan di antaranya adalah:

- Director's Message melalui surat elektronik perihal Kebijakan Pemberian Hadiah dan Jamuan (pada saat mendekati perayaan keagamaan), terkait Communication Policy and Social Media Guidelines, serta terkait kebijakan Speak-Up.
- Serangkaian Code of Conduct Series seperti penggunaan peralatan dan fasilitas kantor, memperlakukan karyawan dengan adil, tidak menyalahgunakan insider information, dan lain-lain.
- Speak-Up Campaign.
- Mewajibkan seluruh karyawan untuk mengikuti e-learning Kode Etik, e-learning Budaya Kepatuhan, dan e-learning Kebijakan Benturan Kepentingan serta melakukan penyegaran e-learning tersebut setiap tahun.

PermataBank juga menetapkan Kode Etik untuk Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, dan Organ Pendukung. Sesuai dengan Kode Etik tersebut, dalam melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya, anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi wajib melandasi diri dengan serangkaian etika sebagai berikut:

- Etika berkaitan dengan keteladanan.
- Etika berkaitan dengan kepatuhan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan.
- Etika berkaitan dengan keterbukaan dan kerahasiaan informasi.
- Etika berkaitan dengan peluang.
- Etika berkaitan dengan benturan kepentingan di mana anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi wajib bersikap profesional.
- Etika menghindari berkompromi karena hadiah dan hiburan (entertainment).
- Etika berkaitan dengan informasi orang dalam (insider information).
- Etika berkaitan dengan penyuapan dan korupsi.
- Etika berkaitan pencegahan pencucian uang.
- Etika untuk memperlakukan karyawan dengan adil.
- Etika berkaitan dengan aktivitas berpolitik. [GRI 102-16]

During 2021, several initiatives were carried out to improve the compliance culture as well as to increase awareness to the Code of Conduct, among others:

- Director's Message via e-mail regarding the Policy on Gifts and Entertainment (when approaching religious celebrations), regarding the Communication Policy and Social Media Guidelines, as well as matters related to the Speak-Up policy.
- Code of Conduct Series, such as the use of office equipment and facilities, treating employees fairly, not abusing insider information, and others.
- Speak-Up Campaign.
- Requiring all employees to attend the Code of Conduct e-learning, Compliance Culture e-learning, and Conflicts of Interest Policy e-learning, as well as the refreshment e-learning every year.

PermataBank has also established the Code of Conduct for the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, and Supporting Organs. In accordance with the Code of Conduct, in carrying out their duties and responsibilities, members of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors must adhere to the following ethics:

- Ethics relating to exemplification.
- Ethics relating to compliance with laws and regulations.
- Ethics relating to the disclosure and confidentiality of information.
- Ethics relating to opportunity.
- Ethics relating to conflict of interest in which members of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors must behave professionally.
- Ethics to avoid compromises on gifts and entertainment.
- Ethics relating to insider information.
- Ethics relating to bribery and corruption.
- Ethics relating to the prevention of money laundering.
- Ethics to treat employees fairly.
- Ethics relating to political activities. [GRI 102-16]
