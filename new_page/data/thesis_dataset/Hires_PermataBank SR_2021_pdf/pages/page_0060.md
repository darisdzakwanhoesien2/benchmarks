# Perlindungan Nasabah

## Customers Security

PermataBank melakukan perlindungan terhadap nasabah sejak seseorang atau sebuah badan hukum masih menjadi calon nasabah. PermataBank menyediakan informasi produk dan layanan Bank melalui beragam kanal yaitu di kantor cabang, melalui situs web PermataBank, PermataMobile X, call center, serta media sosial PermataBank.

PermataBank berkomitmen untuk memberikan layanan yang setara bagi konsumen dengan menyediakan berbagai sarana media komunikasi yang mudah untuk diakses oleh nasabah dan/atau masyarakat yang paling kurang meliputi surat, telepon, surat elektronik, faksimili, dan situs web. [GRI 102-2]

Pada proses penjualan produk atau jasa keuangan, PermataBank menerapkan kebijakan dan prosedur yang mengatur tentang transparansi produk dan/atau layanan Bank kepada calon nasabah, terutama mengenai manfaat, risiko dan biaya-biaya serta syarat dan ketentuan yang melekat pada produk dan/atau layanan mencakup produk yang diterbitkan oleh Bank maupun produk dari lembaga keuangan lain yang dipasarkan melalui Bank kepada masyarakat.

## Prinsip perlindungan nasabah PermataBank:

1. Transparansi;
2. Perlakuan yang Adil yaitu perlakuan Konsumen secara adil dan tidak diskriminatif;
3. Keandalan;
4. Kerahasiaan dan keamanan data/informasi Konsumen; dan
5. Penanganan pengaduan serta penyelesaian sengketa Konsumen secara sederhana, cepat, dan biaya terjangkau. [GRI 102-2, 417-1]

PermataBank protects its customers since a person or a legal entity is still a potential customer. PermataBank provides information on the Bank's products and services through various channels, namely at branch offices, through PermataBank's website, PermataMobile X, call center, as well as PermataBank's social media.

PermataBank is committed to providing equal services to consumers by providing various means of communication media that are easy to access by customers and/or the public, which at least includes letters, telephones, email, facsimiles, and websites. [GRI 102-2]

In the process of selling financial products or services, PermataBank applies policies and procedures that regulate the transparency of the Bank's products and/or services to prospective customers, particularly regarding the benefits, risks, and costs, as well as the terms and conditions inherent to products and/or services, including products issued by the Bank as well as products of other financial institutions that are marketed through the Bank to the public.

## PermataBank customer protection principles:

1. Transparency;
2. Fair Treatment, namely the treatment of consumers in a fair and non-discriminatory manner;
3. Reliability;
4. Confidentiality and security of Consumer data/information; and
5. Handling of complaints as well as resolution of consumer disputes in a simple and fast manner, at an affordable cost. [GRI 102-2, 417-1]
