### Penjelasan singkat mengenai produk, layanan, dan kegiatan usaha yang dijalankan
Brief description of the products, services, and business activities

PT Bank Permata Tbk ("PermataBank/Perseroan") memiliki bidang usaha sebagai Bank Umum, yang menawarkan produk dan jasa perbankan untuk membantu masyarakat serta perusahaan agar berhasil menciptakan kesejahteraan dan pertumbuhan bisnis melalui segmen usaha dari Retail Banking, Wholesale Banking, SME, serta produk, dan jasa berbasis Syariah.

Selain itu, PermataBank juga memiliki layanan berbasis digital seperti PermataMobileX dan PermataNet untuk melakukan transaksi perbankan.

PT Bank Permata Tbk ("PermataBank/Company") has a line of business as a Commercial Bank, which offers banking products and services to assist communities and companies to succeed in creating prosperity and business growth through business segments from Retail Banking, Wholesale Banking, SME, and other products, as well as Sharia-based services.

In addition, PermataBank also has digital-based services, such as PermataMobileX and PermataNet for conducting banking transactions.
