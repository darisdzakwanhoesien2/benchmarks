# Vaksinasi COVID-19

PermataBank bekerja sama dengan Perbanas untuk memberikan vaksin kepada Karyawan. Pemberian vaksin berjalan dengan baik dimana lebih dari 99% Karyawan beserta keluarga telah menerima vaksin, yang turut berpengaruh terhadap penurunan kasus positif COVID-19 di PermataBank, selaras dengan penurunan kasus di Indonesia. Kami menghadapi pandemi bersama-sama untuk memastikan keberlangsungan layanan perbankan.

# COVID-19 Vaccination

Permata Bank collaborated with Perbanas to provide vaccines to employees. Vaccination went well where more than 99% of employees and their families have received the vaccine, which also contributed to the decrease in positive cases of COVID-19 at Permata Bank, in line with the decline in cases in Indonesia. We deal with the pandemic together to ensure the continuity of banking services.
