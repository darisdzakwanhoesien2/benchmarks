# Penghargaan

PermataBank melalui PermataHati menerima penghargaan "Insan Berkemampuan Khusus (IBK) Award" dari Cagar Foundation dalam rangka Hari Disabilitas Internasional yang jatuh pada tanggal 3 Desember 2021. Cagar Foundation merupakan sebuah yayasan sosial yang fokus dalam memberdayakan komunitas penyandang disabilitas, dan dipimpin oleh Deka Kurniawan, salah satu anggota Komisi Nasional Disabilitas yang diangkat oleh Presiden Republik Indonesia. Penghargaan ini diserahkan kepada PermataBank sebagai apresiasi atas komitmen dan kontribusi PermataBank dalam memberdayakan komunitas penyandang disabilitas di Indonesia melalui program flagship PermataBRAVE.

# Awards

PermataBank through PermataHati received "Insan with Special Ability (IBK) Award" from Cagar Foundation in the International Disability Day on 3 December 2021. Cagar Foundation is a social foundation that focuses on empowering people with disabilities, and is led by Deka Kurniawan, a member of the National Commission for Disabilities, who was appointed by the President of the Republic of Indonesia. This award was presented to PermataBank as an appreciation for its commitment and contribution in empowering the communities of people with disabilities in Indonesia through its flagship program, PermataBRAVE.
