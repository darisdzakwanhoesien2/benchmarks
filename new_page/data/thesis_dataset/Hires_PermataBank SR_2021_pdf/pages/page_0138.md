Pada periode pelaporan 2020-2021, terdapat perubahan pada topik kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja berkenaan dengan terjadinya pandemi COVID-19. Topik ini menjadi masih menjadi prioritas utama pada tahun pelaporan 2021 yang berkaitan dengan risiko kesehatan bagi PermataBankers dan keluarganya, nasabah, penyediaan produk dan jasa serta keberlangsungan bisnis Bank secara keseluruhan. Walaupun pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia cenderung membaik di akhir 2021 serta berjalanya program vaksinasi COVID-19 yang dilakukan Pemerintah, PermataBank tetap menjalankan prosedur kesehatan secara konsisten dan berkelanjutan. [GRI 102-49]

In the 2020-2021 reporting period, there were changes to the occupational health and safety topic in relations to the COVID-19 pandemic. This topic remains the top priority in the 2021 reporting year related to the health risk for PermataBankers and their families, customers, provision of products and services, as well as the overall Bank's business sustainability. Even though Indonesia's economic growth tends to improve at the end of 2021, as well as the implementation of the COVID-19 vaccination program carried out by the Government, PermataBank continues to carry out health procedures in a consistent and sustainable manner. [GRI 102-49]
