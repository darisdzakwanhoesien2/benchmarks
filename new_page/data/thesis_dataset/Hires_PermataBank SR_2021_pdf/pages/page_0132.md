| --- | --- | --- |
|  Kepada PermataBankers, Bank berkomitmen untuk fokus dalam membuat perubahan (Making a Difference). To PermataBankers, the Bank is committed to focus on Making a Difference. | • Penyediaan fasilitas kesehatan, kesejahteraan dan pemenuhan hak-hak PermataBankers sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku.
Providing health and welfare facilities, as well as fulfilling PermataBankers' rights in accordance with prevailing regulations.
• Budaya kerja PermataBankers yang sesuai dengan nilai-nilai I-PRICE.
Aligning PermataBankers' work culture with I-PRICE values.
• Program pelatihan untuk meningkatkan kompetensi.
Providing training programs to improve competence.
• Mempertahankan sumber daya manusia unggul terbaik.
Retaining the most excellent human resources. | Pelibatan yang dilakukan setiap saat meliputi:
Methods of engagement at any time include:
• Melalui forum internal seperti town hall. Seperti tahun sebelumnya masih diselenggarakan secara daring.
Through internal forums such as town halls. Similar to previous year, town halls are still held virtually.
• Wadah Serikat Pekerja PermataBank. PermataBank Worker Union forum.
• Program-program pelatihan dan pengembangan kompetensi yang selama masa pandemi COVID-19 ini dilakukan secara virtual melalui e-learning ataupun melalui webinar.
Training and competency development programs, which were carried out virtually through e-learning or through webinars during the COVID-19 pandemic.
• Penyampaian informasi baik berita maupun pengumuman serta sosialisasi melalui surat elektronik dan portal internal (PermataWorld). Distribution of information, both news and announcements and socialization via email and internal portal (PermataWorld).
• Survey rutin yang ditujukan ke karyawan. Regular surveys addressed to employees.  |
|  Kepada masyarakat PermataBankers bertanggung jawab dan berkomitmen pada anggota masyarakat serta mendukung pengembangan masyarakat. To the community, PermataBankers will be responsible and committed to community members and support community development. | • Program CSR yang berfokus pada pendidikan, khususnya edukasi literasi keuangan. CSR program that focuses on education, particularly financial literacy education.
• Program CSR yang dapat memiliki nilai tambah bagi penerima manfaat. CSR programs with added value to the beneficiaries.
• Program CSR yang juga merangkul komunitas penyandang disabilitas. CSR programs that embrace people with disabilities.
• Selama pandemi COVID-19, melalui program bantuan untuk komunitas yang terdampak dengan COVID-19.
During the COVID-19 pandemic, through assistance programs for communities affected by COVID-19. | Pelibatan yang dilakukan setiap saat meliputi:
Methods of engagement at any time include:
• Melalui Employee Volunteer.
Through Employee Volunteer.
• Kerja sama dengan komunitas-komunitas sekitar serta lembaga-lembaga terkait.
Collaboration with surrounding communities as well as related institutions.  |
|  Kepada pemegang saham PermataBankers berkomitmen untuk memberikan kinerja dan manfaat yang baik sekaligus bertanggung jawab kepada pemegang saham. To shareholders, PermataBankers is committed to provide good performance and benefits as well as to be responsible to shareholders. | • Pemegang saham memperoleh informasi material atas kondisi perusahaan termasuk informasi keuangan perusahaan yang akuntabel, transparan, mudah diakses dan mudah dipahami oleh pemegang saham dan juga masyarakat umum. Shareholders obtain material information regarding the conditions of the company including the company's financial information that is accountable, transparent, easily accessible, and easily understood by shareholders and the general public.
• Kualitas keterbukaan informasi serta keakuratan informasi yang disampaikan kepada pemegang saham.
The quality of information disclosure and the accuracy of information submitted to shareholders.
• Tata kelola perusahaan yang baik yang diakui baik secara lokal maupun internasional.
Good corporate governance that is recognized both locally and internationally. | Pelibatan yang dilakukan pada periode tertentu meliputi:
Methods of engagement at certain times include:
• Melalui Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS) Tahunan dan RUPS Luar Biasa.
Through the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) and Extraordinary GMS.
• Keterbukaan informasi melalui situs web PermataBank dan situs web Bursa Efek Indonesia.
Disclosure of information through PermataBank's website and the website of Bursa Efek Indonesia.
• Publikasi Laporan Keuangan dan Laporan Tahunan yang dapat diakses melalui situs web PermataBank dan situs web Bursa Efek Indonesia.
Publication of the Financial Statements and Annual Report that can be accessed through PermataBank's website and the website of Bursa Efek Indonesia.
• Paparan publik (public expose) tahunan dan investor meeting.
Annual public expose and investor meeting.  |
