# Work-life Balance

## Work-life Balance

Serikat Pekerja Bank Permata dan PermataClub melalui program olahraga Virtual Olympics. Program ini merupakan upaya meningkatkan efektivitas dan produktivitas karyawan, penerapan budaya hidup sehat dan seimbang, sehingga karyawan dapat lebih optimal dalam menjalankan setiap aktivitas, baik dalam lingkungan kerja maupun kehidupan pribadi. Pada tahun 2021, program Virtual Olympics mempertandingkan 3 cabang olahraga yang diikuti lebih dari 600 karyawan.

PermataBank memandang keluarga memiliki arti penting bagi setiap PermataBanker, Perseroan memberikan hak cuti tahunan dan cuti khusus kepada karyawan sesuai ketentuan agar mereka bisa menikmati waktu yang berharga dengan keluarganya. Untuk karyawan perempuan, cuti melahirkan diberikan selama tiga bulan tanpa menghilangkan atau mengurangi hak dan posisi yang bersangkutan di tempat kerja sedangkan untuk para ayah memperoleh hak cuti selama 5 hari jika istrinya melahirkan, sebagai dukungan kami kepada keluarga agar bisa menyiapkan proses kelahirannya dengan baik.

Selama tahun 2021, jumlah pekerja perempuan yang mengajukan cuti melahirkan ada sebanyak 249 orang dan sebanyak 100% kembali bekerja di posisi semula. Sementara itu, untuk pekerja pria yang mengambil cuti karena istrinya melahirkan tercatat sebanyak 176 orang. PermataBank juga memberikan Cuti Di Luar Tanggungan (unpaid leave) dengan pertimbangan tertentu tanpa mengurangi manfaat asuransi kesehatan dan lainnya, selain itu terdapat reward non-cash yaitu Cuti Ulang Tahun, di mana karyawan berhak untuk mengambil cuti 1 hari.

Employee Union Bank Permata and PermataClub through the Virtual Olympics sports program. This program is an effort to increase the effectiveness and productivity of employees, implement a healthy and balanced living culture, allowing employees to be more optimal in carrying out every activity, both in the work environment and personal life. In 2021, the Virtual Olympics program competed 3 sports with more than 600 employees participating in the event.

PermataBank views family as an important part of every PermataBanker, the Company provides the rights for annual leave and special leaves for employees according to the provisions, allowing them to enjoy valuable time with their families. For female employees, a three months maternity leave is provided without eliminating or reducing the rights and positions in the workplace, while fathers receceive 5 days leave in the event their wife gives birth, as our support to the family, enabling them to prepare for a proper maternity process.

During 2021, the number of female workers who applied for maternity leave was 249 people and a total of 100% returned to work to their original positions. Meanwhile, a total of 176 male workers took a leave because their wife gave birth. PermataBank also provides Unpaid Leave with certain considerations without reducing health insurance benefits and others, in addition there is a non-cash reward, namely Birthday Leave, where employees are entitled to take a 1 day leave.

PermataBank also implements a pension program with a fixed pension contribution scheme, in which 9% of the pension premium is paid by PermataBank from the employee's basic salary. PermataBank also provides a system and mechanism that allows employees to manage their contribution pension
