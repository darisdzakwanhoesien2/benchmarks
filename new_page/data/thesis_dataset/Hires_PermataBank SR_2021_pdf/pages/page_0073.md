## Penilaian kinerja yang adil

PermataBank menyelenggarakan proses penilaian kinerja yang dilakukan secara obyektif, transparan, dan meminimalkan bias. Proses penilaian kinerja yang dilakukan secara adil mencegah timbulnya perlakuan tidak adil yang menimbulkan ketidaknyamanan dan kehilangan kepercayaan di tempat kerja serta pengembangan individu yang tidak efektif yang merugikan kedua belah pihak. Sebaliknya penilaian kinerja yang dilakukan secara konstruktif dapat meningkatkan motivasi kerja dan mendorong produktivitas tim dan Perseroan.

PermataBank has also developed a Future Learning Engine that was developed via online platforms (Instagram Live, Instagram TV), and learning studio. The Digital Learning Platform has also been developed to facilitate unlimited access for PermataBankers training, anywhere and anytime. With the adoption of technology, Permata Learning Week has reached more than 10,000 participants from all over Indonesia in 3 days of the program.

In 2021, teaching and learning activities, which are mostly conducted online, have been experienced by 98% of PermataBankers with an average of 4.73 days of learning per person, compared to an average of 4.55 days/person in 2020. In the midst of limited training implementation due to the pandemic, the satisfaction level of training participant has increased to a score of 4.5 compared to 4.2 in the previous year.

## Fair performance assessment

PermataBank implements a performance assessment process that is objective, transparent, and minimizes bias. A fair performance assessment process prevents unfair treatment that will lead to discomfort and loss of trust in the workplace as well as ineffective individual development, which is detrimental to both parties. On the other hand, a performance assessment that is carried out in a constructive manner will increase work motivation and encourage the productivity of the team and the Company.
