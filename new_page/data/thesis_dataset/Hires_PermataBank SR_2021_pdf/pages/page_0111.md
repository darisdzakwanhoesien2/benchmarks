Responsible Financial Services

Program VGR pada tahap selanjutnya diadakan di bulan Agustus ditujukan untuk karyawan dan keluarganya yang berada di luar Jabodetabek serta dilakukan secara serentak di 6 kota besar bertempat di kantor cabang PermataBank Bintaro, PermataBank Merdeka (Bandung), PermataBank Tunjungan (Surabaya), PermataBank Zainul Arifin (Medan), PermataBank Pandanaran (Semarang), dan PermataBank Dewi Sartika (Denpasar).

PermataBank kembali mengadakan program VGR di bulan September di berbagai tempat yaitu kantor cabang PermataBank Bintaro, kantor cabang PermataBank Zainul Arifin (Medan), kantor cabang PermataBank Tunjungan (Surabaya), Sentra Vaksinasi Plant Kimia Farma (Bandung), Klinik Kimia Farma Sutomo (Semarang), dan Klinik Kimia Farma Diponegoro (Bali).

The next stage of the VGR program was held in August for employees and their families outside the Greater Jakarta area, and was also held simultaneously in 6 major cities, located at the branch offices of PermataBank Bintaro, PermataBank Merdeka (Bandung), PermataBank Tunjungan (Surabaya), PermataBank Zainul Arifin (Medan), PermataBank Pandanaran (Semarang), and PermataBank Dewi Sartika (Denpasar).

PermataBank held another VGR program in September in various locations, namely PermataBank Bintaro branch office, PermataBank Zainul Arifin (Medan) branch office, PermataBank Tunjungan (Surabaya) branch office, Kimia Farma Plant Vaccination Center (Bandung), Kimia Farma Sutomo Clinic (Semarang), and Kimia Farma Diponegoro Clinic (Bali).
