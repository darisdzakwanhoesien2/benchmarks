Responsible Financial Services

PermataBank terus berinvestasi dalam kapabilitas digital yang terdepan di pasar - PermataAPI, PermataMobile X, PermataNet, Permata-eBusiness, PermataQR, di bidang Ritel, UKM, Wholesale dan Syariah- untuk memenuhi brand promise PermataBank yakni simple, fast and reliable. Selain pengembangan produk keuangan, percepatan transformasi digital juga dilakukan dengan membangun kemitraan yang kuat dan efektif dengan pihak ketiga serta dengan banyak pemain utama dalam ekosistem Bank dan ekonomi digital.

PermataBank juga mencatatkan pencapaian transaksi digital dari semua digital channel. Pada penggunaan PermataMobile X dan PermataNET, keduanya mengalami pertumbuhan transaksi signifikan yakni sebesar dua kali lipat dibandingkan tahun lalu.

# Mendukung UMKM digital

PermataBank mendukung UMKM untuk naik kelas dengan pemanfaatan transaksi digital yang juga menjadi program Bank Indonesia untuk mendigitalisasi 12 juta merchant melalui QRIS di tahun 2021. Dengan produk PermataQR Merchant, Bank berharap dapat memudahkan para pedagang khususnya UMKM untuk bertransaksi secara digital dengan beragam manfaat seperti meningkatkan penjualan dan menurunkan biaya pengelolaan uang tunai atau kecil.

Selama 2021 terdapat peningkatan dalam hal transaksi secara digital di mana jumlah pengguna PermataMobile X meningkat 61% year on year, sedangkan jumlah transaksi meningkat 63% year on year. Jumlah mitra dan transaksi API naik 60% dan 236% year on year. Jumlah transaksi EDC pun meningkat 26% year on year. Jumlah QR merchant mencapai ±391 ribu hanya dalam waktu satu tahun sejak diluncurkan.

PermataBank continues to invest for the leading digital capabilities on the market – PermataAPI, PermataMobileX, PermataNet, Permata-eBusiness, PermataQR, in retail, SME, Wholesale, and Sharia – to meet its brand promise, namely simple, fast and reliable. In addition to development of financial products, acceleration of digital transformation is also carried out by establishing solid and effective partnerships with third parties as well as with various major players in the Bank's ecosystem and digital economy.

PermataBank also achieved record highs in digital transactions across all digital channels. Significantly, PermataMobile X and PermataNET both recorded double the number of transactions as compared with the previous year.

# In support of digital MSMEs

PermataBank supports MSMEs to advance by utilizing digital transactions, which is also Bank Indonesia's program to digitalize 12 million merchants through QRIS in 2021. With the PermataQR Merchant product, the Bank hopes to facilitate traders, particularly MSMEs, to conduct transactions digitally with various benefits, such as increasing sales and lowering the cost of managing cash or bank notes and small change.

During 2021 there was an increase in the number of digital transactions, where the number of PermataMobile X users increased by 61% year on year, while the number of transactions increased by 63% year on year. The number of API partners and transactions increased by 60% and 236% year on year, respectively. The number of EDC transactions also increased by 26% year on year. The number of QR merchants reached ±391 thousand in just one year since its launch.
