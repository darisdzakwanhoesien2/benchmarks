# Memperluas Akses Layanan Keuangan

Saat ini, PermataBank terus melengkapi ekosistem digital inovatif milik PermataBank yaitu - QR Pay, API banking, mobile banking, internet banking, digital value chain, model branch, serta super app PermataMobile X yang memiliki lebih dari 200 fitur unggulan serta memperbarui platform internet banking PermataNET, yang merupakan transformasi penggunaan standar baru dalam pengalaman internet banking sesuai digital architecture roadmap sebagai upaya Bank yang selalu mendengar nasabah dan memperbaiki layanan.

Di tahun 2021, PermataBank beroperasi dengan didukung oleh 286 Kantor Cabang (264 Konvensional dan 22 Syariah), 29 Payment Point Syariah dan 873 ATM, yang tersebar di 62 kota di Indonesia. Jaringan Cabang PermataBank yang luas mampu menyediakan layanan kepada lebih dari 6,1 juta nasabah.

Selain itu untuk memperluas akses bagi masyarakat, PermataBank juga bekerja sama dengan Indomaret menyediakan akses tarik tunai gratis bagi nasabah PermataBank di lebih dari 18.700 lokasi Indomaret di seluruh Indonesia. [GRI 203-2]

# Expanding the Access to Financial Services

Currently, PermataBank continues to complement PermataBank's innovative digital ecosystem, namely - QR Pay, API banking, mobile banking, internet banking, digital value chain, branch model, as well as the PermataMobile X super app, which has more than 200 flagship features, as well as updating the PermataNET internet banking platform, which is a transformation by using new standards in the internet banking experience according to the digital architecture roadmap as an effort by the Bank to always listen to customers and improve services.

In 2021, PermataBank operates with the support of 286 Branch Offices (264 Conventional and 22 Sharia), 29 Sharia Payment Points and 873 ATMs, spread across 62 cities in Indonesia. PermataBank's extensive branch network is able to provide services to more than 6.1 million customers.

In addition, to expand access for the public, PermataBank also cooperates with Indomaret to provide access to free cash withdrawals for PermataBank customers at more than 18,700 Indomaret locations throughout Indonesia. [GRI 203-2]
