# Pengelolaan PermataBanker tidak hanya mencakup kapasitas dan kapabilitas tetapi juga mencakup penghargaan atas perbedaan, membangun budaya, engagement antar individu, dan hubungan industrial yang harmonis.

The management of PermataBanker does not only cover capacity and capability but also includes respecting differences, building a culture, engagement between individuals, and harmonious industrial relations.

Perjalanan yang dilalui sepanjang 2020-2021 adalah pengalaman berharga di mana PermataBank menghadapi tantangan ganda yaitu pandemi global yang belum pernah kita alami sebelumnya dan proses peralihan PermataBank bersama pemegang saham pengendali baru yaitu Bangkok Bank. Namun demikian pada akhir 2021, PermataBank tetap mampu mempertahankan kinerja terbaiknya dalam menyediakan layanan keuangan terpercaya di Indonesia dan hal ini tidak terlepas dari kemampuan Bank mengelola sumber daya yang paling utama yaitu PermataBanker. [GRI 103-1]

The journey through 2020-2021 was a valuable experience, in which PermataBank confronted a double challenge, namely the global pandemic that we had never experienced before and the transition process of PermataBank with its new controlling shareholder, namely Bangkok Bank. However, at the end of 2021, PermataBank was still able to maintain its best performance in providing reliable financial services in Indonesia and this was inseparable from the Bank's ability to manage its most important resources, namely PermataBanker. [GRI 103-1]
