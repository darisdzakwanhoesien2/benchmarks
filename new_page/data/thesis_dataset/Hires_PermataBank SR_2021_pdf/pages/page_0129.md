Responsible Financial Services

Proses pelaporan manajemen risiko kepada Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi disampaikan secara berkala, minimal sekali dalam sebulan, termasuk pelaporan pemenuhan terhadap risk tolerance untuk semua risiko utama yang dikelola oleh PermataBank, terutama eksposur risiko kredit, pasar, likuiditas, operasional, dan keamanan informasi. Satuan Kerja Manajemen Risiko (SKMR) juga menyampaikan laporan profil risiko yang meliputi seluruh risiko utama yang dikelola oleh Bank setiap kuartal. Laporan disetujui oleh Direksi dan dievaluasi oleh Dewan Komisaris melalui rapat bulanan Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, serta rapat Komite Risiko yang diadakan pada tingkat Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris.

The process of reporting risk information to the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors is submitted regularly, at a minimum on a monthly basis, which includes reporting on the fulfillment of compliance with risk tolerance of all principal risks managed by PermataBank, especially credit, market, liquidity, operational, and information security risk exposures. In addition, the Risk Management Unit (RMU) also submits a risk profile report covering all principal risks managed by the Bank on a quarterly basis. The report is approved by the Board of Directors and evaluate by the Board of Commissioners through monthly meetings of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors, as well as through Risk Committee meetings that held periodically both at the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners level.

Hasil dari proses evaluasi oleh Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi akan ditindaklanjuti oleh seluruh unit terkait dan pemenuhan atas rekomendasi yang diberikan oleh Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi akan dimonitor secara berkala. PermataBank memastikan seluruh laporan yang dikirim selalu berdasarkan komunikasi yang jelas dan terbuka dengan stakeholder utama.

The results of evaluation process by the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors will be followed up by all relevant units and the fulfillment of recommendations given by the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors will be monitored periodically. PermataBank ensures that all reports submitted always based on clear and transparent communication with key stakeholders.

Informasi lebih rinci tentang pelaksanaan manajemen risiko dapat dibaca dalam Laporan Tahunan PermataBank 2021 di bagian Manajemen Risiko.

More detailed information on the implementation of risk management can be viewed in the 2021 Annual Report of PermataBank in the Risk Management section.
