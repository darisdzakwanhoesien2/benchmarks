## Dewan Komisaris

Sesuai dengan Anggaran Dasar PermataBank, Dewan Komisaris memiliki tugas utama yaitu melakukan pengawasan terhadap pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab Direksi serta pengawasan atas kebijakan pengurusan dan memberikan nasihat kepada Direksi. Dalam pelaksanaan tugasnya sebagai fungsi pengawasan, Dewan Komisaris juga dibantu oleh komite-komite yang dibentuk di bawah Dewan Komisaris seperti Komite Audit, Komite Pemantau Risiko, Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi, serta Komite Tata Kelola Terintegrasi.

## Direksi

Direksi bertanggung jawab penuh atas pengurusan PermataBank untuk kepentingan dan tujuan PermataBank serta mewakili PermataBank. Direksi juga mewakili PermataBank baik di dalam maupun di luar Pengadilan, sesuai dengan ketentuan Anggaran Dasar PermataBank dan peraturan yang berlaku.

Direksi dibantu oleh komite-komite yang dibentuk di bawah Direksi untuk mendukung Direksi dalam mengimplementasikan visi dan misi PermataBank serta membantu Direksi dalam melakukan pengurusan PermataBank.

Rincian pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab organ tata kelola PermataBank dapat dibaca dalam Laporan Tahunan PermataBank 2021 bagian Tata Kelola Perusahaan.

## Board of Commissioners

Pursuant to the Articles of Association of PermataBank, the Board of Commissioners has the main task of supervising the implementation of the duties and responsibilities of the Board of Directors as well as supervising the management policy and providing advice to the Board of Directors. In the implementation of its duties as a supervisory function, the Board of Commissioners also assisted by committees established under the Board of Commissioners, such the Audit Committee, Risk Monitoring Committee, Remuneration and Nomination Committee, as well as Integrated Governance Committee.

## Board of Directors

The Board of Directors is fully responsible for the management of PermataBank for the interests and objectives of PermataBank, as well as representing PermataBank both inside and outside the Court, with due observance of the provisions of PermataBank's Articles of Association and prevailing regulations.

The Board of Directors assisted by committees established under the Board of Directors to support the Board of Directors in implementing the vision and mission of PermataBank as well as assist the Board of Directors in managing PermataBank.

Details of the implementation of duties and responsibilities of PermataBank's governance structures can be viewed in the 2021 Annual Report of PermataBank, in the Corporate Governance section.
