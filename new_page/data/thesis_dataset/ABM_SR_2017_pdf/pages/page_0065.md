Sustainable Governance

# Pengungkapan Independensi

Seluruh anggota Komite Audit yang berasal dari pihak independen tidak memiliki saham Perseroan, tidak memiliki hubungan usaha dengan Perusahaan, dan tidak memiliki hubungan keluarga dengan Pemegang Saham Utama, Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi; serta mempunyai pengetahuan dan pengalaman sesuai dengan yang ditetapkan dalam peraturan OJK.

# Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi

## Dasar Hukum Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi

Komite Nominasi &amp; Remunerasi ABM dibentuk melalui Surat Keputusan Dewan Komisaris ABM No. 018/ABM-RES-BOC/V/2012 tanggal 3 Mei 2012. Komite Nominasi &amp; Remunerasi telah membuat dan memberlakukan Piagam Komite Nominasi &amp; Remunerasi sebagai landasan kerja Komite Nominasi &amp; Remunerasi yang dituangkan dalam Surat Keputusan Dewan Komisaris ABM No. 033/ABM-RES-BOC/VIII/2015 tanggal 19 Agustus 2015 tentang Pemberlakuan Piagam Komite Nominasi &amp; Remunerasi. Keberadaan dan landasan kerja Komite Nominasi &amp; Remunerasi telah sesuai dengan Peraturan OJK No. 34/POJK.04/2014.

## Keanggotaan Komite Nominasi &amp; Remunerasi

Anggota Komite Nominasi &amp; Remunerasi harus memahami kegiatan usaha ABM dan Grup ABM, memiliki pengetahuan yang memadai mengenai peraturan perundang-undangan pasar modal, operasional perusahaan, ketenagakerjaan serta hubungan industrial, dan mempunyai pengetahuan dan pengalaman yang memadai sesuai dengan latar belakang pendidikannya serta mampu berkomunikasi dengan baik.

Susunan keanggotaan Komite Nominasi &amp; Remunerasi sekurang-kurangnya terdiri dari seorang Komisaris Independen sebagai ketua komite dan setidaknya satu anggota lainnya, dapat berasal dari dalam atau luar perusahaan. Untuk anggota yang berasal dari luar perusahaan, tidak diperkenankan merangkap menjadi anggota komite lain. Masa kerja anggota Komite Nominasi &amp; Remunerasi paling lama 3 (tiga) tahun dan dapat diangkat kembali untuk masa jabatan berikutnya.

# Disclosure of Independence

All members of the Audit Committee from independent parties do not own shares in the Company, have no business relationship with the Company, and have no family relationship with the Ultimate Shareholders, Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors; as well as have the knowledge and experience as stipulated in the OJK regulation.

# Nomination and Remuneration Committee

## Nomination and Remuneration Committee Legal Basis

The ABM Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee was established through the Board of Commissioners' Decree No. ABM. 018/ABM-RES-BOC/V/2012 dated May 3, 2012. The Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee has established and enacted a Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee Charter as the working platform for the Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee as set forth in the ABM Board of Commissioners' Decree No. 033/ABM-RES-BOC/VIII/2015 dated 19 August 2015 concerning the enactment of the Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee Charter. The existence and working platform for the Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee is in accordance with OJK Regulation No. 34/POJK.04/2014.

## Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee Membership

Members of the Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee must understand the ABM business activities and the ABM Group, have sufficient knowledge of the capital market laws and regulations, company operations, employment and industrial relations, and have sufficient knowledge and experience in accordance with their educational background and be able to communicate well.

The Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee comprises at least one Independent Commissioner as Committee Chairman, and at least one other member who may come from within or outside the Company. For members who come from outside the company, they are not allowed to be members of other committees. The term in office for the Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee shall be no longer than 3 (three) years and they may be reappointed for a following term of office.
