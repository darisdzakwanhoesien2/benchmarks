# COMPANY OVERVIEW

PT ABM Investama Tbk ("Company" or "ABM") was established on June 1, 2006 under the name of PT Adiratna Bani Makmur, later renamed PT ABM Investama in 2009. On December 6, 2011, ABM made history by registering 550.6 million shares on the Indonesia Stock Exchange under the code ABMM, and changed its name to PT ABM Investama Tbk. (102-1, 102-5)

ABM was an integrated energy company that made strategic investments in energy related sectors. Its business portfolio spanned the areas of resources, services and infrastructure, wholly synergized to support the achievement of the Company's 2020 Vision, through a Balanced Portfolio.
