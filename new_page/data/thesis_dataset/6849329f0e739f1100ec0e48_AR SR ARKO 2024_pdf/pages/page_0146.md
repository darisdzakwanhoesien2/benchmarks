# STRUKTUR DAN BESARAN REMUNERASI DIREKSI DAN DEWAN KOMISARIS

Selama tahun 2024, Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris telah menerima sejumlah remunerasi. Remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi yang telah ditetapkan mencakup beberapa komponen, antara lain:

1. Honorarium
2. Tunjangan
3. Fasilitas
4. Insentif Kinerja

Perseroan tidak memberikan remunerasi dalam bentuk opsi saham kepada Dewan Komisaris maupun Direksi.

Mengacu pada ketentuan tersebut, remunerasi yang diberikan kepada anggota Dewan Komisaris di tahun 2024 sebesar Rp379.000.000 (tiga ratus tujuh puluh sembilan juta Rupiah). Sementara remunerasi yang diberikan kepada anggota Direksi di tahun 2024 sebesar Rp6.150.000.000 (enam miliar seratus lima puluh juta Rupiah).

# Structure and Amount of Remuneration of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners

In 2024, the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners have received a number of remunerations with details as follows:

1. Honorarium
2. Allowances
3. Facilities
4. Performance Incentive

The Company does not provide remuneration in the form of stock options to the Board of Commissioners or the Board of Directors.

Referring to these provisions, the remuneration given to members of the Board of Commissioners in 2024 amounted to Rp379,000,000 (three hundred seventy nine million Rupiah). Meanwhile the remuneration given to members of the Board of Directors in 2024 amounted to Rp6,150,000,000 (six billion one hundred and fifty million Rupiah).
