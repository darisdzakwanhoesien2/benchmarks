Annual &amp; Sustainability Report
2024

# Operasional Yaentu

- Proyek Tomoni berhasil mendapatkan pembiayaan dari PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur (Persero) pada tanggal 15 Agustus 2024.
- Proyek Yaentu telah beroperasi secara komersial pada tanggal 16 Oktober 2024.

# Yaentu Operational

- Tomoni Project received financing from PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur (Persero) on August 15, 2024.
- Yaentu Project has been operating commercially.

# 2023

## Penerbitan Green Bonds

- Perseroan menerbitkan Obligasi Berwawasan Lingkungan "Green Bonds" dan telah tercatat pada Bursa Efek Indonesia pada 9 Agustus 2023.
- Proyek Kukusan 2 mendapatkan fasilitas pembiayaan konstruksi PLTA dari PT Indonesia Infrastructure Finance.
- Perseroan melalui anak perusahaanannya, PT Arkora Hydro Malili telah menandatangani PPA dengan PLN untuk mengembangkan Proyek Tomoni dengan kapasitas produksi 10 MW.

## Green Bonds Issuance

- The Company issued Environmentally Friendly Bond "Green Bonds" and was listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange on August 9, 2023.
- Kukusan 2 Project received funding from PT Indonesia Infrastructure Finance.
- The Company, through its subsidiary PT Arkora Hydro Malili, has signed a Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) with PLN to develop the Tomoni Project with 10 MW production capacity.

# 2022

## Go-Public

- Perseroan resmi terdaftar di Bursa Efek Indonesia dengan kode saham "ARKO" pada 8 Juli 2022.
- Perseroan melakukan penandatangan PPA Proyek Kukusan 2 (5.4 MW) di Lampung, Sumatra Selatan dan memulai konstruksi.

## Go-Public

- The Company was officially listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange with ticker code "ARKO" on July 8, 2022.
- The Company signed the PPA for Kukusan 2 Project (5.4 MW) in Lampung, South Sumatra and commenced construction.

# 2021

## Pembiayaan Yaentu

- Perseroan memulai konstruksi Proyek Yaentu.
- Proyek Yaentu mendapatkan pembiayaan dari PT Indonesia Infrastructure Finance.

## Yaentu Financing

- The Company started the construction of Yaentu Project.
- Yaentu Project received funding from PT Indonesia Infrastructure Finance.
