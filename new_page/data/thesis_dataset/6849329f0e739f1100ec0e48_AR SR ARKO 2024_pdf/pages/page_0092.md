The dam is situated at an altitude of 590 meters above sea level, allowing water to be diverted into a pipeline leading to a sediment basin, then channeled through an open waterway before entering the penstock.

Pada tahun 2024, pendapatan dari operasional Proyek Tomasa berjumlah Rp7,75 miliar, menurun 23,57% dibanding pendapatan tahun 2023 sebesar Rp10,15 miliar. Hal ini diakibatkan oleh dampak dari El Nino tahun 2023 yang masih terasa hingga semester pertama tahun 2024.

In 2024, revenue from the operations of the Tomasa Project amounted to Rp7.75 billion, a decrease of 23.57% compared to the 2023 revenue of Rp10.15 billion. This was due to the El Nino impact in 2023 which still felt until the first semester of 2024.

Beban pokok pendapatan tahun 2024 tercatat sebesar Rp7,52 miliar dan beban usaha sebesar Rp8,45 miliar. Terjadi penurunan pada beban pokok pendapatan dan peningkatan pada beban usaha masing-masing sebesar -2,92% dan 251,55% dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya. Secara garis besar, beban pada masing-masing pos terjadi untuk kebutuhan operasional dan perawatan pembangkit.

The cost of revenue in 2024 was recorded at Rp7.52 billion, while operating expenses amounted to Rp8.45 billion. There was a decrease in the cost of revenue and an increase in operating expenses by -2.92% and 251.55%, respectively, compared to the previous year. Overall, the expenses in each category were incurred for operational needs and power plant maintenance.
