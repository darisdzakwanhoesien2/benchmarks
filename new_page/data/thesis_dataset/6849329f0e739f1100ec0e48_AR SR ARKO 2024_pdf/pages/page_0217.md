# Pelatihan dan Pengembangan Karyawan [F.22] Employee Training and Development [F.22]

Secara total, jumlah jam pelatihan karyawan mencapai 16 jam dengan total biaya mencapai Rp7.500.000

The Company places great importance on the continuous management and development of its employees. Every year, the Company organizes training activities on various topics to support the enhancement of employee competencies.

In total, the number of training hours for employees reached 16 hours, with a total cost of Rp7.500.000

# Dampak Operasional Bagi Masyarakat [F.23] Operational Impact on the Community [F.23]

Aktivitas operasional atau jalan akses menuju proyek berada di daerah yang dekat dengan pemukiman penduduk. Perseroan berupaya untuk memberikan dampak positif bagi masyarakat melalui berbagai program mencakup pendidikan, kesehatan, pengembangan ekonomi lokal, budaya hingga lingkungan. Program-program yang dilakukan Perseroan membantu meningkatkan kualitas masyarakat sekitar, meningkatkan kesejahteraan mereka, dan memperbaiki tingkat kesehatan masyarakat. Kehadiran Perseroan juga memberikan kesempatan bagi masyarakat untuk meningkatkan ekonomi mereka, salah satunya dengan membuka lapangan pekerjaan, membiarkan mereka bergabung untuk menjadi tenaga pendukung operasional proyek.

Dari hasil produksi, yaitu energi listrik yang didistribusikan, membawa dampak positif bagi masyarakat. Listrik yang dihasilkan Perseroan juga tergolong nihil emisi (zero emission) sehingga berdampak positif bagi lingkungan. [F.28]

Dampak positif yang dihasilkan Perseroan selama tahun 2024 melalui aktivitas operasional dan program-program sosial dan lingkungan melindungi Perseroan dari risiko sosial dari masyarakat sekitar. Selama tahun 2024, tidak terdapat keluhan dari operasional proyek yang berasal dari masyarakat. [F.24]

The operational activities or access roads to the hydropower plant are located near residential areas. The Company aims to have a positive impact on the community through various programs, including education, health, local economic development, culture, and the environment. These programs help improve the quality of life for the surrounding community, enhance their welfare, and improve public health. The Company's presence also provides opportunities for the community to improve their economy, such as by opening up job opportunities and allowing them to join as supporting staff for the hydropower plant operations.

The produced electricity, which is distributed, has a positive impact on the community. The electricity generated by the Company is also considered zero-emission, which has a positive effect on the environment. [F.28]

The positive impacts generated by the Company in 2024 through its operational activities and social and environmental programs have protected the Company from social risks posed by the surrounding community. Throughout 2024, there were no complaints from the community regarding the hydropower plant's operations. [F.24]
