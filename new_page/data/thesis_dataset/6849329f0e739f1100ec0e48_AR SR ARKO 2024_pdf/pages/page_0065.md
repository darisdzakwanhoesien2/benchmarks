# DEMOGRAFI KARYAWAN
## HUMAN RESOURCES

Sumber Daya Manusia (SDM) merupakan elemen yang sangat penting dalam upaya mewujudkan visi dan misi dan Perseroan memberikan perhatian yang sangat besar terhadap pengelolaan dan pengembangan karyawan.

Per 31 Desember 2024, jumlah karyawan Perseroan di kantor pusat, di luar dari karyawan entitas anak, tercatat sebanyak 8 orang, mengalami penurunan dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya sebanyak 83 orang.

Human Resources (HR) is an important element in realizing the vision and mission, and the Company is very concerned with the management and development of its employees.

As of December 31, 2024, the number of employees at the Company's headquarters, excluding employees of subsidiaries, was recorded at 8, a decrease compared to 83 employees last year.
