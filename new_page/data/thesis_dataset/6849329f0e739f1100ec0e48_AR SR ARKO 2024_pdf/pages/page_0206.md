# UPAYA DAN PENCAPAIAN EFISIENSI ENERGI DAN PENGGUNAAN ENERGI TERBARUKAN [F.7]

# Energy Efficiency Efforts and Achievements and Renewable Energy Consumption [F.6]

Dalam menjalankan kegiatan operasional, Perseroan memerlukan 2 (dua) jenis energi utama, yaitu listrik dan BBM. Kedua jenis energi ini dikonsumsi untuk operasional proyek.

Energi listrik yang digunakan di powerhouse, termasuk area kantor di dalamnya, dipasok dari hasil produksi listrik Perseroan. BBM yang digunakan untuk kendaraan operasional berasal dari PT Pertamina. Produksi listrik dan BBM yang digunakan Perseroan berasal dari sumber energi tak terbarukan berbahan baku fosil sehingga Perseroan berkomitmen untuk lebih cermat dalam mengonsumsi energi, terutama BBM.

In carrying out its operational activities, the Company requires 2 (two) main types of energy, namely electricity and fuel. These two types of energy are consumed for the operation of the projects.

The electricity used in the powerhouse, including the office areas within it, is supplied from the Company's own electricity production. The fuel used for operational vehicles is sourced from PT Pertamina. The electricity production and fuel used by the Company come from non-renewable energy sources with fossil raw materials, so the Company is committed to being more careful in energy consumption, especially fuel.
