# PENGGUNAAN AIR [F.8]

# Water Consumption [F.8]

Perseroan menggunakan air untuk memenuhi kebutuhan operasional proyek. Data yang diungkapkan merupakan jumlah penggunaan air di site, tempat proyek beroperasi, termasuk anak usaha. Secara total, data penggunaan air diambil dari 3 proyek yang beroperasi.

Sumber air yang digunakan untuk aktivitas operasional di proyek adalah air permukaan yang berasal dari sungai. Sesuai prinsip operasional proyek run-of-river, air permukaan yang telah digunakan untuk kegiatan operasional tidak berubah menjadi air limbah karena hanya dimanfaatkan energinya secara fisik tanpa ada penambahan material kimia. Perseroan tidak mengungkapkan penggunaan air di kantor pusat yang tidak berhubungan langsung dengan pengoperasian proyek.

The Company's efforts to reduce electricity consumption are mainly done by installing energy-efficient LED lights compared to conventional lights. Lights and electronic devices are turned off when not in use. The Company also encourages employees to avoid turning on lights while natural daylight is still sufficient.

The Company also strives to reduce the use of operational vehicles by prioritizing online meetings whenever possible. This way, fuel consumption for operational vehicles can be made more efficient.

The Company uses water to support the operational needs of its projects. The disclosed data represents the amount of water usage at the sites where the projects operate, including its subsidiaries. In total, water usage data is collected from 3 projects.

The water source used for operational activities at the projects is surface water from rivers. In accordance with the operational principles of run-of-river hydropower plants, the surface water used for operations does not turn into wastewater, as it is only utilized for its physical energy without any chemical additives. The Company does not disclose water usage at the head office, as it is not directly related to projects operations.
