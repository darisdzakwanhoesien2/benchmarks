# Lingkungan Bekerja yang Layak dan Aman [F.21]
## Safe and Decent Working Environment [F.21]

Perseroan senantiasa berkomitmen untuk menciptakan lingkungan bekerja yang layak dan aman bagi seluruh karyawannya. Perseroan memiliki berbagai fasilitas seperti toilet, ruang makan, tempat ibadah, dan tempat parkir. Semua fasilitas dipastikan aman, bersih, dan nyaman.

Secara khusus, mengingat bidang usaha Perseroan yang mengoperasikan proyek, terdapat ketentuan khusus mengenai Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3). Program kerja K3 menjadi penting, terutama untuk menjamin keselamatan para karyawan di site dan memastikan PLTA tetap beroperasi tanpa ada gangguan.

Pada tahun 2024, program K3 yang telah dilakukan Perseroan dan telah berjalan secara konsisten dari tahun-tahun sebelumnya adalah:

1. Melakukan identifikasi risiko K3;
2. Menyediakan sarana dan prasarana keselamatan kerja, terutama di proyek;
3. Menyediakan alat pemadam api;
4. Melakukan inspeksi rutin terkait kondisi tempat kerja termasuk alat pelindung diri (APD);
5. Pertemuan (meeting) berkala antara site dengan kantor pusat;
6. Menyediakan asuransi BPJS Kesehatan dan Ketenagakerjaan.

Hasil pelaksanaan program K3 sepanjang tahun 2024 adalah nihil kecelakaan kerja.

Perseroan juga menjalankan sejumlah program secara konsisten untuk memastikan implementasi K3 berjalan efektif. Program-program tersebut mencakup pelatihan berbasis kompetensi, terutama bagi personil keamanan (security) dan personil yang memiliki tanggung jawab khusus di bidang K3.

The Company is committed to creating a decent and safe working environment for all its employees. The Company provides various facilities such as restrooms, a dining area, places of worship, and parking spaces. All facilities are ensured to be safe, clean, and comfortable.

In particular, given the nature of the Company's business in operating projects, there are specific provisions regarding Occupational Health and Safety (OHS). The OHS program is essential, especially to ensure the safety of employees at the site and to ensure the hydropower plant operates smoothly without disruptions.

In 2024, the OHS programs implemented by the Company, which have been consistently running from previous years, include:

1. Identifying OHS risks;
2. Providing work safety facilities, especially at the projects;
3. Providing fire extinguishers;
4. Conducting routine inspections of the workplace conditions, including personal protective equipment (PPE);
5. Holding regular meetings between the site and the head office;
6. Providing BPJS Health and Employment insurance.

As a result of the OHS program implementation throughout 2024, there were no work-related accidents.

The Company also consistently runs several programs to ensure the effective implementation of OHS. These programs include competency-based training, especially for security personnel and those with specific responsibilities in the OHS field.
