# 17. BANK LOAN

On February 28, 2023, the Company obtained long term credit facility from PT Bank Central Asia (BCA) with maximum amount Rp 11,300,000,000, and maximum period in February 2025. The interest payment within the same month with withdrawal, based on an interest rate per annum of $7.7\%$ for the first 2 years and floating interest rate applicable where BCA will review with following the monetary changes. This loan is used to purchase of 1 (one) office space unit which recorded as building improvement and represent as collateral for bank loan (Note 9).
