# Evaluasi Pendekatan Manajemen

Evaluasi dilakukan Direksi melalui mekanisme penilaian pelaporan berkala kepada pihak-pihak berwenang, serta pencapaian KPI. Dari hasil evaluasi tahun 2022 diketahui Perusahaan telah dapat mengelola pemakaian air serta efluen sesuai peraturan yang berlaku.

# Evaluation of the Management Approach

Evaluation is conducted by the Board of Directors through a periodic reporting assessment mechanism to the authorities, as well as KPI achievements. From the results of the 2022 evaluation, it is known that the Company has been able to manage water and effluent use in accordance with applicable regulations.
