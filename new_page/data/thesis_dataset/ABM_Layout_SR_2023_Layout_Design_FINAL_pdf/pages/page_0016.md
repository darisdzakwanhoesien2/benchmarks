# Lokasi Operasi
## Operational Location [GRI 2-1]

Semua operasi dan usaha ABM sepenuhnya berpusat di Indonesia dan tidak mencakup wilayah operasi atau kegiatan bisnis di luar negeri.

All of ABM's operations and business are centered in Indonesia and include no overseas operations or business.
