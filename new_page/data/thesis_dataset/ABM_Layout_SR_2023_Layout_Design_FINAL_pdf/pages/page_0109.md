# Evaluasi Pendekatan Manajemen [GRI 2-7]

Kebijakan dan prosedur Perusahaan terkait pengelolaan pekerja temporer secara berkala dievaluasi oleh tim Corporate Human Capital dengan mempertimbangkan berbagai input dan insights, antara lain Strategic Man Power Planning Perusahaan yang disesuaikan dengan strategic direction perusahaan, baik dalam jangka pendek, menengah, dan panjang, termasuk kebutuhan untuk personil dengan spesifikasi khusus untuk berbagai rencana Perusahaan ke depan.

# Evaluation of the Management Approach [GRI 2-7]

The Company's policies and procedures regarding the management of temporary workers are regularly evaluated by the Corporate Human Capital team by considering various inputs and insights, including the Company's Strategic Man Power Planning which is adjusted to the company's strategic direction, both in the short, medium and long term, including the need to personnel with special specifications for the Company's various future plans.
