Legal Basis of GCG Implementation

2. Undang-Undang Republik Indonesia Nomor 8 Tahun 1995 tentang Pasar Modal;
3. Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan Nomor 21/POJK.04/2015 tentang Penerapan Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan Terbuka;

6. Kode Etik dan Kebijakan Standar Perilaku Perseroan.

3. Financial Services Authority Regulation Number 21/POJK.04/2015 regarding the Implementation of Public Company Governance Guidelines;
4. Circular Letter of the Financial Services Authority Number 32/SEOJK.04/2015 regarding Guidelines for the Governance of Public Companies;
5. Articles of Association of the Company; and
6. The Company's Code of Ethics and Standard of Conduct Policy.
