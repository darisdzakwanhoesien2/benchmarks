Enforcing Environmentally-Friendly Operations

Bibli Tiket berkomitmen untuk meminimalkan dampak pada lingkungan melalui penerapan ekonomi sirkular dan mengurangi jejak karbon melalui berbagai inisiatif ramah lingkungan.

Bibli Tiket is dedicated to minimizing the Company's environmental impact. Bibli Tiket implements a circular economy in its operation and strives to reduce the carbon footprint through various green initiatives.
