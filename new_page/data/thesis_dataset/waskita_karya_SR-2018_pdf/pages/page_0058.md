## PEDOMAN TATA KELOLA PERUSAHAAN (GRI 102-24)

Waskita memiliki pedoman tata kelola perusahaan yang tertuang dalam Prosedur Waskita Bidang Tata Kelola Perusahaan sesuai Surat Keputusan (SK) Direksi No. 39/SK/WK/2017 tanggal 20 Desember 2017, yang menjadi acuan bagi organ tata kelola perusahaan dalam mengatur hubungan kerja, norma dan etika serta nominasi dan seleksi terhadap organ tata kelola perusahaan di Waskita.

## CORPORATE GOVERNANCE CODE (GRI 102-24)

Waskita has guidelines for corporate governance as contained in the Waskita Procedure for Corporate Governance in accordance with the Decree (SK) of Board of Directors No. 39/SK/WK/2017 dated December 20, 2017, which becomes a reference for corporate governance instruments in regulating work relations, norms and ethics, as well as nominations and selection of corporate governance instruments in Waskita.
