WAKI

Waskita menjalankan kegiatan usaha dalam bidang industri konstruksi, industry pabrikasi, jasa penyewaan, investasi, pekerjaan terintegrasi (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction: EPC), serta layanan jasa peningkatan kemampuan di bidang konstruksi dengan menerapkan prinsip-prinsip Perseroan Terbatas.

Waskita runs business activity in construction industry, manufacturing industry, leasing service, investment, integrated works (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction/EPC), and services in capability improvement in construction field by implementing Limited Liability Company principles.
