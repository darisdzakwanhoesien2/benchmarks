# SUMBER DAYA MANUSIA YANG UNGGUL DAN KOMPETITIF

Waskita memiliki para pegawai yang handal dan unggul di industri konstruksi yang hadir melalui sistem pengelolaaan Sumber Daya Manusia (SDM) yang terukur serta komprehensif dengan mengaplikasi sistem Manpower Planning. Berdasarkan roadmap yang telah disusun, pada tahun 2018, Waskita saat ini memasuki fase pengembangan menuju pengembangan berkelanjutan. Dimana pada fase pengembangan menitikberatkan pada memelihara dan meningkatkan program berjalan dengan mengoptimalkan Talent Management System, sistem kepegawaian, dan SIM SDM dalam rangka mempersiapkan calon potensial untuk setiap kelas jabatan. Optimalisasi tersebut untuk mencapai target strategis yakni menciptakan value bagi para stakeholder.

Hingga Desember 2018, pegawai Waskita tercatat sebanyak 2.018 yang terdiri dari 1.812 pegawai laki-laki dan 206 pegawai perempuan. Dalam penerapannya, pengelolaaan SDM di Waskita memberikan kesempatan tanpa melihat perbedaan gender, baik pada kesempatan berkarir, remunerasi serta peningkatan kompetensi.

Dari sisi pemberian remunerasi, Waskita menerapkan skema remunerasi berdasarkan kompetensi orangnya (people), posisi jabatannya (position), dan kinerjanya (performance) serta disesuaikan dengan pasar tenaga kerja yang mengacu pada Key Performance Indicator (KPI) sesuai dengan pay for performance. Selain nominalnya selalu berada di atas UMR, Waskita memiliki kebijakan berupa pemberian upah untuk Pegawai baru tanpa membedakan jenis kelamin, pria atau wanita. Selain Remunerasi, para pegawai Waskita juga mendapatkan fasilitas kesehatan dan kesejahteraan lainya.

Dari sisi pengembangan kompetensi, Waskita telah menyusun program pelatihan dan pengembangan karir yang dilaksanakan dalam setiap tahunnya antara lain, Leadership Development Program for Director &amp; Commissioner, Leadership Development Program for General Manager, Leadership Development Program For Middle Manager, Leadership Development Program For Project Manager and Superintendent, Leadership Development Program For Junior Manager, Leadership Development Program for Finance and Human Capital. Pada Tahun 2018, realisasi biaya pengembangan dan pelatihan SDM sebesar Rp12,6 miliar meningkat dibandingkan tahun 2017 sebesar Rp8,5 miliar. Realisasi jumlah jam pelatihan per orang per tahun pada tahun 2018 mencapai 55 jam/ orang/tahun lebih banyak dibandingkan pada tahun 2017 yakni 53,98 jam/ orang/tahun. Perseroan berkomitmen meningkatkan kualitas pengembangan kompetensi SDM guna menjawab tantangan bisnis yang semakin dinamis dimasa-masa mendatang.

# EXCELLENT AND COMPETITIVE HUMAN RESOURCES

Waskita has reliable and excellent employees in the construction industry who are present through a measured and comprehensive Human Resources (HR) management system by applying the Manpower Planning system. Based on the prepared roadmap, in 2018, Waskita is currently entering the development phase towards sustainable development, where the development phase focuses on maintaining and improving the running program by optimizing the Talent Management System, staffing system, and HR SIM in order to prepare potential candidates for each position class. The optimization is to achieve the strategic target of creating value for stakeholders.

As of December 2018, there were 2,018 Waskita employees consisting of 1,812 male employees and 206 female employees. In its implementation, HR management at Waskita provides an opportunity regardless of gender differences, both in career opportunities, remuneration and increased competence.

In terms of remuneration, Waskita implemented a remuneration scheme based on the competence of people, position, and performance and adjusted to the labor market that refers to the Key Performance Indicator (KPI) in accordance with pay for performance. In addition to the nominal value which is always above the UMR, Waskita has a policy in the form of wages for new employees regardless of gender, male or female. Besides Remuneration, Waskita employees also get other health and welfare facilities.

In terms of competency development, Waskita has developed training and career development programs that are carried out every year, including Leadership Development Program for Director &amp; Commissioner, Leadership Development Program for General Manager, Leadership Development Program for Middle Manager, Leadership Development Program for Project Manager and Superintendent, Leadership Development Program For Junior Manager, Leadership Development Program for Finance and Human Capital. In 2018, the realization of HR development and training costs amounted to Rp12.6 billion, increased compared to 2017 at Rp8.5 billion. Realization of the number of training hours per person per year in 2018 reached 55 hours/person/year, more than that of 2017 which was 53.98 hours/person/year. The Company is committed to improving the quality of HR competency development in order to respond to the increasingly dynamic business challenges in the future.
