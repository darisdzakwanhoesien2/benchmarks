# EVALUATION ON GCG PERFORMANCE (GRI 102-28)

In addition, Waskita also uses the Company Corporate Governance Scorecard (CCGS) criteria issued by the Ministry of SOE as criteria used in Waskita's GCG assessment in 2018.
