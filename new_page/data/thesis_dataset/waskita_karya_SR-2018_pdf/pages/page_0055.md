- Direksi

# STRUKTUR TATA KELOLA BERLANJUTAN WASKITA KARYA (GRI 102-18) (102-22)

Waskita memperhatikan pemetaan fungsi atau kegiatan usaha dengan standar etika bisnis serta memastikan mekanisme pengawasan dan keseimbangan dapat bekerja secara efektif dalam membangun struktur tata kelola Waskita. Struktur tersebut meliputi struktur organ dan kebijakan Waskita, yaitu Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham, Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, serta Organ pendukung lainnya seperti Satuan Pengawasan Intern, Sekeretaris Perusahaan, Sistem Pengendalian Internal, Manajemen Risiko, serta ketaatan terhadap ketentuan yang berlaku.

- Board of Directors
Waskita's instrument that is authorized and fully responsible for Waskita's management for Waskita's interests, in accordance with Waskita's purpose and objectives and representing Waskita, both inside and outside the court in accordance with the provisions of the Articles of Association.

# SUSTAINABLE GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE OF WASKITA KARYA (GRI 102-18) (GRI 102-22)

In establishing corporate governance structure, the Company takes account of the alignment of function or business activities mapping with business ethics standards to ensure that effective check and balance mechanism is in place. The Company's corporate governance structure includes its organs and policies, to name a few, General Meeting of Shareholders, the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors and other supporting organs such as Corporate Secretary, internal control, risk management and compliance with the applicable regulations.
