## Mekanisme Penyelesaian Keluhan Pelanggan [G4-PR8]

Waskita akan memberikan respon yang cepat atas pengaduan yang masuk dan penyelesaian secara bijak melalui pemusatan seluruh pengaduan. Mekanisme pengelolaan pengaduan sebagai berikut

- Waskita memiliki standar dalam merespon atas pengaduan pelanggan 1 x 24 jam.
- Pengaduan yang masuk melalui saluran aduan via website, fax, hotline dan email Waskita diterima oleh Sekretariat Perusahaan.
- Sekretariat Perusahaan akan memberikan jawaban sebagai respon terhadap keluhan kepada pelanggan bahwa keluhan telah diterima dan akan segera diproses oleh unit yang terkait dengan materi keluhan.

## Customer Complaints Center

Waskita has established a customer complaint center. This customer complaint center can be accessed through several channels including:

- Waskita Website via www.waskita.co.id;
- Fax (021) 8508506;
- Hotline (021) 8508510; and
- email: waskita@waskita.co.id

## Customer Complaints Settlement Mechanism [G4-PR8]

The Company will respond promptly to incoming complaints and solve wisely through the centralization of all complaints. Grievance management mechanisms are as follows:

- The Company has a standard in responding to customer complaints 1 x 24 hours.
- Complaints entered through the complaint channel via website, fax, hotline and email of the Company are received by the Corporate Secretary unit.
- The Corporate Secretary will respond in response to a complaint to the customer that the complaint has been received and will be processed immediately by the unit associated with the complaint material.
