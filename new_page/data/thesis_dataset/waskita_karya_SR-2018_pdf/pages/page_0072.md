# GO GREEN CONSTRUCTION

## Green Building Waskita

Waskita berkomitmen mendukung program *Sustainable Development* yang mensinergikan aspek bisnis dengan kinerja keberlanjutan dalam meningkatkan kualitas pengelolaan lingkungan hidup. Hal tersebut ditujukan dengan aktivitas Waskita yang mengutamakan prinsip ramah lingkungan dan penghematan. Komitmen pada ramah lingkungan diapresiasi dengan mendapatkan sertifikasi Green Building Waskita dari pihak GBCI (Green Building Council Indonesia). Sertifikat tersebut merupakan salah bentuk pengakuan atas kepedulian Waskita terhadap kelestarian lingkungan. Sertifikasi gedung tersebut akan menjadi pengingat, sekaligus pemicu semua insan Waskita agar selalu hidup dengan spirit pro-lingkungan.

## Komitmen Waskita Pada Green Construction (GRI 103-2)

Komitmen Waskita dalam pelaksanaan proyek konstruksi berwawasan lingkungan/ramah terhadap lingkungan, antara lain, diselenggarakan melalui upaya-upaya berikut:

1. Waskita telah memenuhi kewajiban pembuatan dokumen lingkungan (AMDAL, Dokumen Pengelolaan dan Pemantauan Lingkungan (DPPL)) sesuai dengan amanat Undang-undang No.32 Tahun 2009 tentang Perlindungan dan Pengelolaan Lingkungan Hidup
2. Waskita telah membuat Laporan Pengelolaan Lingkungan (RKL) dan Laporan Pemantauan Lingkungan (RPL) yang dilaporkan secara perodik setiap 6 (enam) bulan sekali kepada instansi terkait, seperti Kementerian Lingkungan Hidup (bLH). Komponen yang dikelola dan dipantau sebagai berikut;
- Kualitas udara dan kebisingan
- Kualitas air bersih dan air permukaan
- Limbah padat/sampah
- Pengelolaan kesehatan masyarakat
- Pengelolaan sosial ekonomi
3. Waskita memiliki izin untuk menyimpan secara sementara Limbah bahan berbahaya dan beracun (b3) dari Pemerintah Provinsi DKI Jakarta yang dituangkan melalui keputusan Gubernur Provinsi DKI Jakarta Nomor 62/2010 Tentang izin Penyimpanan sementara Limbah bahan berbahaya Dan beracun (b3).
4. Aktivitas penghijauan dan penanaman pohon

- The Corporate Secretary submits complaints from customers to related units.
- Subsequent customer complaints are processed and completed by the related unit. Related units can directly communicate to customers in the complaint resolution process.

# GO GREEN CONSTRUCTION

## Green Building Waskita

Waskita is committed to supporting a Sustainable Development program that synergizes business aspects with sustainability performance in improving the quality of environmental management. This was aimed at Waskita's activities which prioritized environmentally-friendly policies and savings. Commitment to environmental friendliness was appreciated by obtaining the Green Building Waskita certification from GBCI (Green Building Council Indonesia). The certificate is a form of recognition of Waskita's concern for environmental sustainability. The building certification will be a reminder, as well as the trigger for all Waskita personnel to always live with the spirit of pro-environment.

## Waskita's Commitment to Green Construction (GRI 103-2)

Waskita commitment in conducting green construction/ eco-friendly project is implemented through the following efforts:

1. Waskita has fulfilled the obligations on making environmental documents (AMDAL, Environmental Management and Monitoring Documents (DPPL)) based on the Law No. 32 Year 2009 on Environmental Protection and Management.
2. Waskita has made Environmental Management Report (RKL) and Environmental Monitoring Report (RPL) which are periodically reported in every 6 months to the related institution, such as Ministry of Environment (BLH). Components which are managed and monitored are as follows:
- Air and Noise Quality
- Clean water and surface water quality
- Solid waste/garbage
- Society health management
- Social economy management
3. Waskita has the license to temporarily keep the hazardous and toxic waste materials (B3) issued by DKI Jakarta provincial government No 61/2010 on License of Keeping the Hazardous and Toxic Waste (B3) Temporarily
4. Greening and tree planting activities.
