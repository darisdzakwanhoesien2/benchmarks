# Infrastruktur Berdaya Saing Tinggi

Sebagai kontraktor BUMN terbesar di Indonesia, PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk telah menjadi yang terdepan dalam membangun infrastruktur negeri. Pada tahun 2018, Waskita kembali mendapatkan kepercayaan untuk menyelesaikan dan mengerjakan sejumlah proyek pembangunan infrastruktur berstatus Proyek Strategis Nasional (PSN). Waskita merupakan perusahaan konstruksi yang juga mengembangkan usaha pada bidang rekayasa, investasi infrastruktur dan property/realty. Waskita diakui sebagai perusahaan konstruksi yang memiliki keunggulan kompetitif yang membawa kepercayaan para pelanggan untuk bekerja sama dalam menyelesaikan berbagai proyek.

Overall, in the 2018 fiscal year, the Company successfully achieved the set targets, especially in the Statement of Financial Position, where all the components succeeded in exceeding the set target. In 2018, the Company also succeeded in realizing the profit for the year which reached 103.18% of the predetermined target. This was the result of an increase in Revenue of 7.91% compared to the previous year and accompanied by an increase in efficiency in managing Operating Expenses so that the component was able to be reduced by 69.50% in 2018.

# High Competitiveness Infrastructure

As the largest SOE contractor in Indonesia, PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk has become the leader in building the country's infrastructure. In 2018, Waskita regain the trust to complete and work on a number of infrastructure development projects with the status of National Strategic Projects (PSN). Waskita is a construction company that also develops business in the fields of engineering, infrastructure investment and property/realty. Waskita is recognized as a construction company that has a competitive advantage that brings the trust of customers to work together in completing various projects.
