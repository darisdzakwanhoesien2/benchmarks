- Kedua, dari hasil umpan balik tersebut, tim penyusun Laporan Keberlanjutan melakukan Focus Group Discussion (FGD) dengan perwakilan berbagai unit dan divisi internal Perseroan, khususnya unit dan divisi yang memiliki keputusan strategis serta ketersediaan data atas berbagai topik material yang direkomendasikan oleh pemangku kepentingan. FGD dilakukan pada tanggal 30 Januari 2024.

- Secondly, based on the feedback received, then Sustainability Report drafting team conducted a Focus Group Discussion (FGD) with representatives from various units and divisions within the Company, especially units and divisions with strategic decision-making and data availability on various material topics recommended by stakeholders. The FGD was held on January 30, 2024.
