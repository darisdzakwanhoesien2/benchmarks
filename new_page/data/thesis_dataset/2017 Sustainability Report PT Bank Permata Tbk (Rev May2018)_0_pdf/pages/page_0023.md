# Sekilas Tentang PermataBank

![img-24.jpeg](img-24.jpeg)

PermataBank mendukung penuh pelaksanaan keuangan berkelanjutan yang sejalan dengan aspirasi pemangku kepentingan untuk menjalankan bisnis secara berkelanjutan dan bertanggung jawab secara sosial serta berpartisipasi dalam upaya perlindungan lingkungan.

PermataBank fully supports the implementation of sustainable finance in line with the aspirations of stakeholders to run the business in a responsible and socially responsible manner, as well as participating in environmental protection efforts.

PT Bank Permata Tbk ("PermataBank/ Company/ Bank") was originally established under the name PT Bank Persatuan Dagang Indonesia in Indonesia by Deed of Establishment number 228 dated 17 December 1954 and ratified as a legal entity by the Minister of Justice of the Republic of Indonesia (now Minister of Justice and Rights Human Rights) on 4 January 1955. Obtained the bank's business license based on Decision Letter of the Minister of Finance No. 19371/U.M.II dated 19 February 1957 and a license to engage in foreign exchange activities based on Decision Letter of the Monetary Council of Bank Indonesia No. Sekr/D.M./97 dated 8 May 1956.
