# GCG Structure of PermataBank

# Struktur GCG PermataBank

Struktur GCG PermataBank untuk mendukung penerapan GCG secara maksimal sebagai berikut: [102-18]
- Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS)
- Dewan Komisaris
- Direksi
- Komite-komite di bawah fungsi Dewan Komisaris:
- Komite Audit
- Komite Pemantau Risiko
- Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi
- Komite Tata Kelola Terintegrasi
- Audit Internal
- Komite-komite yang berada di bawah fungsi Direksi

The GCG structure of PermataBank to support the implementation of GCG maximally is as follows: [102-18]
- General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS)
- Board of Commissioners
- Board of Directors
- Committees under the Board of Commissioners:
- Audit Committee
- Risk Monitoring Committee
- Remuneration and Nomination Committee
- Integrated Governance Committee
- Internal Audit
- Committees under the Board of Directors
