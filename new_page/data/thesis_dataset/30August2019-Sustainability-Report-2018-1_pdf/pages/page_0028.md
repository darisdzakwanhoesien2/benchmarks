# BNBR Sustainability in Determining Content and Boundaries of BNBR Sustainability Reports

Determination of Contents of BNBR's Sustainability Reports is processed through several stages, namely:

1
IDENTIFY RELEVANT
SUSTAINABILITY ASPECTS
AND BOUNDARIES OF EACH
ASPECT ACCORDING TO
INDUSTRY CHARACTERISTICS
AND THEIR IMPACT
ON STAKEHOLDERS

2
PRIORITIZE ASPECTS
AND SUSTAINABILITY
ISSUES TO DETERMINING
THE MATERIALITY
OF EACH ASPECT

3
VALIDATE EVERY ASPECT
OF EACH MATERIAL

4
REVIEW PREVIOUS
REPORTS TO IMPROVE
THE QUALITY OF THIS
SUSTAINABILITY REPORT.
