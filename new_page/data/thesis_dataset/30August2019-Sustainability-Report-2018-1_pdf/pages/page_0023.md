# BAKRIE PIPE INDUSTRIES

## Certificate of Appreciation – Occupational Safety and Health Management System

July 2017 – July 2020

Certificate of Appreciation for Occupational Safety and Health Management System was awarded by The Minister Of Employment Of The Republic Of Indonesia, M. Hanif Dhakiri. The certificate is based on the Decree Of The Minister Of Employment Of The Republic Of Indonesia no. 201, year 2017 to certify that PT Bakrie Pipe Industries has implemented an Occupational Safety and Health Management System with an audit result of 96.38% for advanced level category. Certificate was obtained on July 14, 2017 and is valid until July 14, 2020.
