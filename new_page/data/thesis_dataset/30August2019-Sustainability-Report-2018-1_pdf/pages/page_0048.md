Through its business unit, PT Bakrie Autopart, BNBR deepens the automotive world by establishing cooperation with the leading electric bus manufacturer in China, BYD Auto Co.LTD. BNBR believes the world of transportation will continue to grow and in the future BNBR will not only sell electric bus vehicles but will also offer transportation systems for cities in Indonesia.

This year, BNBR has conducted a test drive for electric bus units in Bali. In this trial, BNBR provided 2 units of electric buses which were used as shuttle buses at the International Monetary Fund &amp; World Bank event. This electric bus received positive responses from users as well as special attention from the Indonesian government. The electric bus is expected to be developed further in Indonesian transportation industry in the future.
