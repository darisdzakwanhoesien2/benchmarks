Board of Directors Explanation

Komitmen Bank Aladin Syariah untuk mengembangkan bisnis perbankan yang berkelanjutan harus diikuti dengan kesadaran seluruh insan tentang pentingnya perwujudan keberlanjutan.

Bank Aladin Syariah's commitment to developing a sustainable banking business must be followed by awareness of all people about the importance of realizing sustainability.
