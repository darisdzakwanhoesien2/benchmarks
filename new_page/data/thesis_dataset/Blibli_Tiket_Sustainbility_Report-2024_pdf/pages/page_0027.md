# Action for Our Planet

## Target &amp; Realitas/2024 (1-3)
2024 Target &amp; Realization (4-6)

1. Mempertahankan penggunaan kardus baru yang 100% bersertifikasi FSC.
Muntam the use of 100% FSC-certified new cardboard.

2. Meningkatkan proporsi limbah yang dipakai kembali dan didaur ulang sebanyak 5%.
Increase the proportion of waste reused and recycled by 5%.

3. Mengurangi jumlah emisi dari kegiatan operasional (cakupan 1 &amp; 2).
Reduce the number of emissions from operational activities (scope 1 &amp; 2).

# Action for People

## Action for Excellence

1. Mempertahankan kepuasan pelatihan dan pengembangan karyawan.
Maintain employee training and development satisfaction.

2. Menumbuhkan 10% proporsi karyawan yang menerima pelatihan terkait keberlanjutan.
Increase the proportion of employees receiving sustainability-related training by 10%.

3. Meningkatkan jumlah penerima manfaat menjadi 15.000 orang dari program pemberdayaan masyarakat.
Expand the number of beneficiaries of community employment programs to 15.000 people.

# Action for Excellence

## Action for Practice

1. Mempertahankan dan bertransisi ke Sertifikasi ISO/IEC 27001:2022 untuk Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Informasi.
Maintain and transition to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Certification for Information Security Management System.

2. Mendapatkan Sertifikasi ISO/IEC 27701:2019 untuk Privasi Data.
Obtain ISO/IEC 27701:2019 Certification for Data Privacy.

3. Nihil kasus terkait pelanggaran data dan privasi pengguna.
Zero cases related to data breaches and user privacy.

# Action for Our Planet

## Pendekatan untuk mencapai target
Approach to achieving the target

- Implementasi prosedur pengadaan kardus bersertifikasi FSC untuk pemenuhan pasanan.
Implement FSC-certified cardboard procurement procedures for order fulfillment.

- Bekerja sama dengan mitra bisnis untuk mendaur ulang limbah perusahaan dari kemasan hingga limbah kantor.
Collaborate with business partners to recycle company waste from packaging to office waste.

- Melakukan efisiensi di seluruh proses rantai pasokan.
Optimize efficiency across the supply chain process.

# Action for People

## Action for People

## Action for Excellence

- Meningkatkan kualitas modul pelatihan dan pengembangan yang sesuai dengan kebutuhan karyawan.
Improve the quality of training and development modules to suit employee needs.

Laverage the Blibli Tiket ecosystem to increase employee participation in sustainability education programs.

Engage more stakeholders in implementing community empowerment programs.

Implement comprehensive ethics, business compliance, and data security.

- Mengembangkan kualitas infrastruktur dari edukasi secara berkala untuk meningkatkan keamanan data.
Continuously enhance infrastructure quality and education to strengthen data security.
