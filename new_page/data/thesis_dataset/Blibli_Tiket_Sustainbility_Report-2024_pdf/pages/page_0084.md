# BUILDING RESILIENT COMMUNITIES

The Company collaborates with various communities, foundations, and non-profit organizations to enhance quality of life through the development and empowerment programs. Throughout 2024, Blibli Tiket successfully delivered initiatives across multiple communities as outlined below:

![img-172.jpeg](img-172.jpeg)
Semangat Berkolaborasi untuk Sumbawa
Spirit of Collaboration for Sumbawa

tiket.com berkolaborasi dalam kegiatan seminar dan lokarya bagi komunitas lokal di Kabupaten Sumbawa Barat, Nusa Tenggara Barat. Melalui komunitas tiket Empowered Network of Expertise (ten-v), tiket.com menyampaikan topik pariwisata berkelanjutan, pemanfaatan media sosial sebagai saluran promosi, serta digital marketing untuk meningkatkan penjualan, dengan penekanan pada digitalisasi dan kolaborasi. Sebanyak 70 peserta dari 13 komunitas lokal mengikuti kegiatan ini, mulai dari pelaku UMKM, kelompok sadar wisata, hingga komunitas olahraga.

tiket.com collaborated for a seminar and workshop for local communities in West Sumbawa Regency, West Nusa Tenggara. Through the tiket Empowered Network of Expertise (ten-v) community, tiket.com delivered topics on sustainable tourism, the use of social media as a promotional channel, and using digital marketing to increase sales, with an emphasis on digitalization and collaboration. A total of 70 participants from 13 local communities attended this activity, ranging from MSMEs, tourism awareness groups, to sports communities.

# Sahabat Ibu Pintar (SIP)
Smart Mother's Companion (SIP)

![img-173.jpeg](img-173.jpeg)

Berkolaborasi dengan Milklife, Realfood, dan Hansaplast di Festival Merah Putih, Blibli menyelenggarakan kegiatan ibu bersama si kecil yang diikuti oleh 277 peserta. Adapun beberapa kegiatan yang dilakukan yaitu fashion show dan talkshow edukasi parenting. Selain itu, komunitas ini juga berpartisipasi pada acara Langkah Membumi Festival 2024 dengan mengadakan kegiatan sensory play bekerja sama dengan tempat edukasi sensori.

Collaborating with Milklife, Realfood, and Hansaplast at the Red and White Festival, Blibli held a mother and children activity attended by 277 participants. The activities carried out included a fashion show and a parenting education talk show. Additionally, the community participated in the Langkah Membumi Festival 2024 by hosting a sensory play activity in partnership with a dedicated sensory education center.
