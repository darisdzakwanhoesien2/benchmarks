# Manajemen Pengelolaan Effluent [303-2][303-4]

Effluent Treatment Management

Perusahaan menerapkan sistem Instalasi Pengolahan Air Limbah (IPAL) di gudang Surabaya dan Marunda. Di gudang Surabaya, air limbah disalurkan melalui IPAL milik kawasan SIER (Surabaya Industrial Estate Rungkut) dan terlebih dahulu melalui lubang sampling masing-masing tenant. Uji laboratorium atas limbah cair dilakukan oleh pengelola kawasan. Sementara itu, di gudang Marunda, pengelolaan air dilakukan secara mandiri, dimulai dari aeration tank, dilanjurkan ke unit biotech, dan kemudian diolah kembali (recycle) untuk kebutuhan flushing toilet serta penyiraman tanaman. Uji laboratorium terhadap air limbah telah dilakukan pada tahap konstruksi, sementara pengujian lanjutan akan dilakukan pada akhir tahun 2025, bersamaan dengan dimulainya fase operasional penuh. Dalam pelaksanaannya, Blibli Tiket berpedoman pada PP No.22 tahun 2021 tentang Penyelenggaraan Perlindungan dan Pengelolaan Lingkungan Hidup, serta kriteria Green Building terkait kebutuhan daur ulang air, dan sumber air alternatif.

The Company implements Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) systems at its Surabaya and Marunda warehouses. At the Surabaya warehouse, wastewater is channeled through the WWTP managed by the SIER (Surabaya Industrial Estate Rungkut) industrial area, preceded by a sampling point for each tenant. Laboratory testing of the wastewater is conducted by the estate management. Meanwhile, at the Marunda warehouse, water is managed independently, starting from the aeration tank, followed by the biotech unit, and then further treated and recycled for toilet flushing and plant irrigation. Laboratory testing was conducted during the construction phase, with follow-up testing scheduled for the end of 2025, in line with the start of full operational activities. In its implementation, Blibli Tiket adheres to ISO Government Regulation No. 22 of 2021 on Environmental Protection and Management, as well as Green Building criteria concerning water recycling and alternative water sources.
