# MEKANISME PENGADUAN MASYARAKAT [F16][F24][2-25]

Untuk meningkatkan transparansi dan menjaga hubungan dengan para seluruh pihak, Blibli Tiket membuka saluran pengaduan bagi para pemangku kepentingan termasuk pelanggan dan masyarakat melalui layanan customer service 24 jam (Customer Care Blibli &amp; Customer Care tiket.com). Customer Care Blibli Tiket senantiasa responsif akan keluhan dan pertanyaan pelanggan serta mengedepankan personalisasi layanan dengan didukung inovasi teknologi penggunaan AI dan ML sehingga lebih optimal dan cepat.

Pelanggan maupun masyarakat dapat menyampaikan pengaduan terkait produk, layanan, kebijakan Perseroan, maupun lingkungan hidup. Pengelolaan pengaduan atau laporan yang diterima menjadi tanggung jawab Departemen Blibli Care (Customer Care Blibli) dan Departemen Customer Care Operations (Customer Care tiket.com). Setiap pengaduan selanjutnya akan dievaluasi dan diputuskan tindakan penyelesaiannya. Hingga akhir tahun 2024, Blibli Tiket tidak mendapatkan pengaduan dari masyarakat terkait lingkungan hidup.

# PUBLIC GRIEVANCE MECHANISM [F16][F24][2-25]

To foster transparency and strengthen connections with all stakeholders, Blibli Tiket has opened a dedicated 24-hour complaint channel accessible to both customers and the public through Customer Care Blibli and tiket.com. This service remains responsive to customer feedback and questions at all times. Blibli Tiket also emphasizes personalized assistance, supported by innovations in artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML), enabling faster and more effective resolutions to every concern.

Customers and the public may submit complaints regarding products, services, company policies, or environmental concerns. The management of all reports is the responsibility of the Blibli Care Department (Customer Care Blibli) and the Customer Care Operations Department (Customer Care tiket.com). Each complaint is evaluated and resolved accordingly. As of the end of 2024, Blibli Tiket has not received any environmental complaints from the public.
