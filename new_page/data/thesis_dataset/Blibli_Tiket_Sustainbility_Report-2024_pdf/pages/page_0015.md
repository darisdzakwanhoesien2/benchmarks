Through this omnichannel ecosystem, customers can shop and conduct transactions via Click &amp; Collect, Blibli InStore, Blibli OMG, Trade-in, and physical stores both online and offline. Blibli has also developed order fulfilment, logistics, and delivery infrastructure to end consumer that can reach all customers in Indonesia while providing digital marketing technology services.

The company's core activities encompass retail trading business of other goods through orders, where the goods will be sent to the buyer based on to the goods ordered through catalogues, models, telephones, TV, internet, mass media, and the like. It also includes the sale of essential goods, mainly food, beverages, or tobacco, as well as non-food items such as household furniture, children's toys, and clothing at minimarkets or supermarkets or hypermarkets. Additionally, it operates web portals and/or digital platforms for commercial purposes. Meanwhile, its supporting business activities include the development of e-commerce applications.

Blibli has expanding its product and service coverage across multiple categories, including smartphones and gadgets, electronic equipment, fashion and beauty, sports equipment, hobbies and games, automotive, daily necessities, and digital products such as utility bill payments, credit top-ups, shopping and game vouchers purchases, and other. Meanwhile, tiket.com runs an online travel agent (OTA) platform, which provides transportation ticket booking services, accommodation, attractions tickets, concerts, tourism activities, and other value-added travel and accommodation services to meet customer lifestyle needs.

Furthermore, the company's subsidiary, PT Supra Boga Lestari Tbk, operates in the modern retail sector, focuses on providing high-quality food and fresh products. On the other hand, Dekoruma is engaged in the home and living retail and interior design and has developed its own interior design automation software called Thudio, to helps customers customise their home interiors design based on their needs and preferences. For more detailed information regarding Blibli Tiket's services and business activities, please refer to the 2024 Annual Report of PT Global Digital Niaga Tbk.
