## Policies for Addressing Challenges [D.1.a]

In 2024, we initiated innovative strategic policies and initiatives, focusing on expanding our product portfolio, enhancing customer service, developing AI and ML-based technologies, advancing integrated omnichannel innovations, improving operational cost efficiency, and strengthening synergies within the Blibli Tiket ecosystem.

To enhance the customer shopping experience, optimize business performance, and create sustainable value for all stakeholders, the Company implemented a series of strategic initiatives. These included optimizing the product mix for higher margins, completing a warehouse in Marunda to strengthen distribution and logistics, and acquiring PT Dekoruma Lastari (DIL) to expand its home &amp; living product category. Additionally, the Company launched the Unified Membership feature, providing seamless access and a unified login for all ecosystem users, and introduced the Blibli Affiliate program to boost user transactions. Blibli Tiket also expanded its presence by opening new physical stores across Indonesia to strengthen its market reach.

## Action for Excellence

Blibli Tiket terus memperkuat tata kelola perusahaan dengan menerapkan sistem manajemen keamanan informasi yang terintegrasi. Pendekatan ini mencakup evaluasi komprehensif dan penilaian risiko yang meliputi aspek organisasi, sumber daya manusia, proses, dan teknologi. Sebagai hasilnya, Perseroan berhasil mempertahankan serta bertransisi ke sertifikasi ISO/IEC 27001: 2022 untuk sistem manajemen keamanan informasi, sekaligus memperoleh sertifikasi ISO/IEC 27701: 2019 untuk privasi data. Selain itu, Blibli Tiket menjadi platform perdagangan omnichannel pertama yang memiliki Cyber Security Incident Response Team (CSIRT). Berkat langkah-langkah ini, hingga akhir tahun 2024, Perseroan mencatat nihil pelanggaran data dan privasi pengguna.

Blibli Tiket juga secara konsisten menerapkan prinsip-prinsip tata kelola perusahaan yang baik (good corporate governance/GCG) dengan mengelola risiko. Hal ini dilakukan dengan menjalankan model pertahanan tiga lapis dan memastikan pelaksanaan evaluasi dan pengawasan yang menyeluruh atas tata kelola keberlanjutan.

## Action for Excellence

Blibli Tiket is dedicated to strengthening its corporate governance by implementing an integrated information security management system. This strategy encompasses a thorough evaluation and risk assessment that addresses all critical aspects, including organisational structure, human capital, processes, and technology. This approach has led the Company to achieved certification for its information security management systems under ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and has obtained ISO/IEC 27701:2019 certification for data privacy. Furthermore, Blibli Tiket has positioned itself as the first omnichannel trading platform to establish a Cyber Security Incident Response Team (CSIRT). As a result of these initiatives, the company recorded no data or user privacy breaches by the conclusion of 2024.

In its operational activities, Blibli Tiket consistently adheres to good corporate governance (GCG) and risk management principles. This is implemented through a three-line defence model, ensuring a comprehensive evaluation and supervision of sustainability governance.

## Action for People

To build customer trust and nurture a responsible future workforce, Blibli Tiket is dedicated to investing in employee training and the development of communities in which it operates in. By promoting equality, inclusivity, and non-discrimination, the Company fosters a supportive work environment. Additionally, the Company prioritizes workplace health and safety, ensuring that all employees feel valued and secure.

Employee satisfaction with the training and development provided by the Company reached a score of 4.8 for Blibli and 4.6 for tiket.com. To strengthen this commitment, the company launched the BOLD Learning Festival, a learning program featuring classroom sessions and hands-on experiments, delivered by more than 20 experts across six key topics. Additionally, Blibli Tiket introduced specialised sustainability training, engaging 646 employees in the CollaborAction program to encourage active participation in sustainability efforts.
