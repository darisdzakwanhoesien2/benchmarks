# Ngopi (Ngobrol Hepi) dan Lunch &amp; Learn

![img-126.jpeg](img-126.jpeg)

Pembelajaran melalui podcast dengan durasi satu jam, yang dapat didengarkan langsung oleh seluruh karyawan. Bibli dan rekaman sesi dapat diakses melalui platform Billearning. Sepanjang 2024, terdapat 8 Ngopi dengan ragam topik dari optimasi pekerjaan, kesehatan mental hingga kesehatan finansial dengan pembicara dari internal dan eksternal. 5 Lunch &amp; Learn dengan sharing knowledge terkait automation, public speaking, digital marketing, dan SEO; dan 6 sharing session melalui talkshow dan workshop terkait kesehatan, mental health hingga dari peserta Operation Management Development Program (Ops MDP).

In 2024, Bibli enhanced employee learning through a range of innovative formats. Live one-hour podcasts—accessible both in real time and on demand via the Billearning platform—fostered dynamic knowledge sharing. Additionally, 8 "Ngopi" sessions covered topics from work optimization and mental health to financial well-being, featuring insights from both internal and external experts; 5 Lunch &amp; Learn sessions provided expertise on automation, public speaking, digital marketing, and SEO; and 6 sharing sessions, delivered as talk shows and workshops, addressed health and mental wellness, including contributions from participants in the Operation Management Development Program (Ops MDP).

# tiket Academy

![img-127.jpeg](img-127.jpeg)

Payung program pelatihan dan pengembangan karyawan di tiket.com yang bertujuan menyediakan peluang sekaligus membangun lingkungan bagi karyawan untuk dapat belajar, berkembang, menggali potensi, dan berkontribusi terhadap pembangunan berkelanjutan. Melalui pendekatan 3E (Experience, Empowerment, and Education), perancangan program pelatihan disesuaikan dengan kebutuhan masing-masing unit bisnis dan tingkatan karyawan berdasarkan tiga pilar utama, yaitu Employee Development, Community Development, dan Youth Development. tiket Academy juga menyediakan platform berbasis situs sebagai one-stop learning solution yang dapat diakses oleh seluruh karyawan, kapan saja dan di mana saja.

tiket.com's employee training and development umbrella program is designed to provide opportunities while fostering an environment where employees can learn, grow, unlock their potential, and actively contribute to sustainable development goals. Through the 3E approach—Experience, Empowerment, and Education—each training initiative is tailored to suit the specific needs of different business units and employee levels. These programs are structured around three key pillars: Employee Development, Community Development, and Youth Development. To support continuous learning, tiket Academy also provides a web-based platform as a one-stop learning solution, enabling all employees to access learning materials anytime and anywhere.

# DEAL Hour (Drop Everything and Learn)

Pembelajaran karyawan tiket.com setiap bulan di hari Jumat minggu ke-4 selama 1 jam untuk belajar mandiri atau sesi diskusi internal. Pada tahun 2024, total jam pembelajaran mencapai 491 jam dengan 442 peserta.

Every month, on the fourth Friday, tiket.com employees participate in a one-hour session dedicated to self-directed learning or internal discussion. In 2024, these sessions accumulated a total of 491 learning hours, engaging 442 participants.

# ten-x (tiket Empower Network of Xpertise)

![img-129.jpeg](img-129.jpeg)

Komunitas pembelajaran peer-to-peer untuk karyawan tiket.com dengan mengembangkan metode pembelajaran tanpa batasan guna berbagi pengetahuan dan keahlian sebagai wadah pengembangan diri. Program ini telah diselenggarakan sebanyak 107 sesi, melibatkan total 8.077 peserta baik internal maupun eksternal, termasuk t-fams, mahasiswa, dan rekan profesional. Pada tahun 2024, ten-x mengalami perkembangan yang signifikan dengan melibatkan 38 ahli dan Indonesia, Vietnam, dan India yang berkolaborasi meningkatkan kapabilitas karyawan dan berbagi pengetahuan lintas negara. Melalui upaya ini, ten-x berhasil mempertahankan skor feedback yang mengesankan, yaitu 4,6 dari 5.

At Bibli tiket, the peer-to-peer learning community supports open and continuous learning, allowing employees to exchange insights and skills freely as a means of self-development. Since its launch, this program has held 107 sessions and involved a total of 8.077 participants from both internal and external circles—ranging from t-fams to university students and professionals. In 2024, the ten-x program saw major progress by collaborating with 38 experts across Indonesia, Vietnam, and India. These cross-country partnerships helped strengthen employee capabilities and promoted international knowledge sharing. Thanks to these efforts, ten-x successfully maintained an excellent feedback rating of 4.6 out of 5.
