Pada tahun 2024, Blibli Tiket telah melaksanakan berbagai program pelatihan dan pengembangan, baik secara terintegrasi seperti BOLD Festival maupun secara terpisah sesuai kebutuhan dan kebijakan masing-masing. Untuk meningkatkan efektivitas, Perseroan terus berupaya mengintegrasikan program-program tersebut.

Adapun untuk memastikan keberjalanannya efektif dan sesuai progress kariernya masing-masing, para leaders secara rutin melakukan coaching bulanan kepada karyawan. Selain itu, leaders juga secara aktif mengidentifikasi kebutuhan pelatihan karyawan dengan mengisi training plan departemen terkait serta self-development plan.

Seluruh pelaksanaan program pelatihan dan pengembangan ini berada di bawah tanggung jawab Departemen Human Capital Development untuk Blibli, dan Divisi People Strategy di bawah payung program tiket Academy untuk tiket.com. Departemen tersebut akan mengevaluasi pelaksanaan program secara berkala setiap enam bulan untuk Blibli dan tiga bulan untuk tiket.com. Hasilnya, Blibli akan melaporkan kepada Chief Operating Officer (COO) dan tiket.com kepada Chief People Officer (CPO).

In 2024, Blibli Tiket has implemented various training and development programs, both in an integrated manner such as BOLD Festival and separately according to their respective needs and policies, to increase effectiveness, the Company continues to integrate these programs.

To ensure effective implementation and alignment with individual career progression, our leaders conduct monthly coaching sessions with employees. Additionally, they actively identify training needs by completing the relevant departmental training plans and self-development plans.

The implementation of all training and development programmes falls under the responsibility of the Human Capital Development Department for Blibli and the People Strategy Division for tiket.com. These departments conduct regular evaluations of the programmes—every six months for Blibli and every three months for tiket.com. The outcomes of these evaluations are reported to the Chief Operating Officer (COO) at Blibli and to the Chief People Officer (CPO) at tiket.com.

# Al and Automation Training

Sepanjang tahun 2024, 1.018 karyawan Blibli Tiket telah mengikuti pelatihan Al dan automasi dengan total jam pelatihan 3.944 jam untuk memudahkan berbagai automasi proses kerja. Hasilnya, karyawan mampu menciptakan 286 proyek yang langsung diaplikasikan dalam kegiatan operasional yang tidak hanya meningkatkan efisiensi kerja namun mampu menghemat biaya sebesar Rp2,9 miliar per tahun.

In 2024, a total of 1.018 Blibli Tiket employees participated in Al and automation training, accumulating 3.944 training hours aimed at simplifying various work processes. As a result, employees successfully created 286 projects that were directly applied in day-to-day operations—initiatives that not only boosted work efficiency but also resulted in annual cost savings of IDR 2.9 billion.
