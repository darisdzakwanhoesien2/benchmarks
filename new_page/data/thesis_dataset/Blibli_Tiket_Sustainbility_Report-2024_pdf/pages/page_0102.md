Subject: Clarification on Nomenclature in the Independent Assurance Statement of the 2024 Sustainability Report of PT Global Digital Niaga Tbk

Dear Management,

PT Sejahtera Rambah Asia (“SRAI”) hereby issues this letter to provide formal clarification regarding the GRI Standards reference stated in the Independent Assurance Statement for the 2024 Sustainability Report of PT Global Digital Niaga Tbk, issued on April 4th, 2025.

During the assurance process, SRAI reviewed the final draft of the Sustainability Report, which included references to both “in accordance with the GRI Standards” and “with reference to the GRI Standards” in different sections of the report. Following subsequent confirmation with PT Global Digital Niaga Tbk reporting team, it has been formally established that the 2024 Sustainability Report is prepared “in accordance with the GRI Standards”. This clarification is therefore issued to align the assurance statement with the reporting entity’s final and confirmed disclosure approach.

To support this reporting position, Blibli Tiket has incorporated several additional GRI disclosures required under the “in accordance” option such as GRI 223, 224, 2074, 3013, 3025, 3031, 3032, 3043, 3044, 3056, and 3057. These disclosures have been duly reviewed and verified in accordance with our established assurance procedures.

We confirm that this clarification does not affect the methodology, assurance level, or the findings and recommendations presented in the originally issued assurance statement. All conclusions previously stated remain valid and unchanged.

Consistent with our assurance practices, a separate Management Letter containing the full details of recommendations will be provided directly to the management of PT Global Digital Niaga Tbk. This ensures a clear separation between clarification of reporting alignment and broader improvement guidance.
