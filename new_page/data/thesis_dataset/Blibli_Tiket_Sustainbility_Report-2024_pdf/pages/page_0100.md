- The Company does not produce emissions of ozone-depleting substances or significant air emissions such as nitrogen oxides or sulfur oxides in its operations. Based on the characteristics of current business activities, operations are not considered to contribute significantly to emissions that impact air quality or the environment. However, Blibli Tiket has calculated emissions from the use of ODS-containing equipment, such as air conditioners, fire extinguishers, water dispensers, refrigerators, and generator sets. These are included in the Scope 1 emissions, totaling 157 tons CO2e in 2024.
