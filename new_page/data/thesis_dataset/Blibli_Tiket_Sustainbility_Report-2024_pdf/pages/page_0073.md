In addition, Blibli Tiket has adopted the Net Promoter Score (NPS) as an employee feedback mechanism and implements a comprehensive 360-degree behavior evaluation—incorporating self-assessments, peer reviews, subordinate feedback, and managerial evaluations. All employees (100%) have undergone performance evaluations and participated in our full suite of training and competency development initiatives, with over 90% of tiket.com employees engaging in these programs.

After assessments are completed, the results are aggregated based on predetermined weightings and calibrated across divisions, departments, and the board. These consolidated findings are then discussed with employees during evaluation sessions—referred to as performance coaching at Blibli—which also identify performance gaps and career aspirations to guide further competency and career development initiatives.

# HAPPY Award

![img-133.jpeg](img-133.jpeg)

tiket.com memperkenalkan penghargaan tahunan pada tahun 2023 sebagai bentuk apresiasi karyawan yang memberikan kontribusi luar biasa. Pada tahun 2024, penghargaan ini diperluas dengan tujuh kategori, yang menyasar anggota tim, pemimpin, dan proyek yang memberikan dampak terbesar. Dengan lebih dari 300 nominator unik, tiket.com mengevaluasi 561 nominasi dari 11 fungsi bisnis. Pemenang dipilih melalui proses pemungutan suara karyawan yang transparan, dan menghasilkan 14 individu dan proyek yang terbaik.

In 2023, tiket.com introduced an annual awards programme as a form of appreciation for employees who have made outstanding contributions. In 2024, the scope of this initiative was expanded to include seven categories, recognising team members, leaders, and projects that delivered the most significant impact. With over 300 unique nominees, tiket.com reviewed 561 nominations across 11 business functions. Winners were selected through a transparent employee voting process, resulting in the recognition of 14 outstanding individuals and projects.

# Blibli Star

Program pengembangan intensif yang dirancang mempersiapkan pemimpin masa depan perusahaan. Program ini menyelaksi karyawan dengan kinerja unggul dan berpotensi yang telah melewati proses wawancara panel dengan Chief Executive Officer (CEO) dan Head of Department. Pada tahun 2024, 17 karyawan dari 34 kandidat yang sebelumnya telah terpilih di tahun 2023 menjalani berbagai program pengembangan yang telah dirancang untuk memperkaya kompetensi kepemimpinan dan profesional mereka. Program ini mencakup kelas pembelajaran terkait pemahaman bisnis, kepemimpinan, dan keterampilan strategis; pengalaman di dunia kerja yakni rotasi lintas fungsi dan proyek strategis; serta path to leadership yaitu program mentoring dan coaching dan para pemimpin perusahaan.

An intensive development program was designed to prepare the next generation of leaders within the company. The program selects high performing, high-potential employees through a panel interview process led by the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and Heads of Department. In 2024, 17 employees, originally shortlisted from 34 candidates in 2023, took part in a series of tailored learning and growth experiences to sharpen their leadership and professional skills. The program includes classroom-based sessions covering business understanding, leadership development, and strategic thinking; practical work experience through cross-functional role rotations and key project involvement; also, a leadership journey that includes mentoring and coaching from senior company leaders.
