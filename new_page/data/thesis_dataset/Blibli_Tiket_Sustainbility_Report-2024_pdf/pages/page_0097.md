# Komitmen Kebijakan [2-23]

Policy Commitments

Hingga saat ini, Biblii Tiket belum memiliki kebijakan tertulis yang spesifik terkait kebijakan keberlanjutan maupun kebijakan Hak Asasi Manusia (HAM). Meskipun demikian, Perusahaan telah menanamkan nilai-nilai HAM dalam kegiatan operasional seperti kesejahteraan karyawan dan kebebasan berekspresi yang tercantum dalam kebijakan Perusahaan.

Notes: The number of outgoing employees includes employees who resigned voluntarily (voluntary leave) and terminated employment by the Company (involuntary leave).

As of now, Biblii Tiket has not established a specific written policy regarding sustainability or human rights. Nevertheless, the Company has embedded human rights values into its operational activities, including employee welfare and freedom of expression, as reflected in the Company's Code of Conduct.
