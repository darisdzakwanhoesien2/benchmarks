SUSTAINABLE PERFORMANCE

Guna memperkuat tata kelola serta pengawasan kinerja keberlanjutan, Direksi telah membentuk unit kerja di bawah koordinasi Departemen Environment, Health, Safety, and Security &amp; Environmental, Social, and Governance (EHSS &amp; ESG) melalui Surat Keputusan Rotasi Jabatan 1652/II/SK-P/t/X/2023. Unit ini bertanggung jawab atas penerapan kebijakan dan strategi keberlanjutan, mengukur dan menganalisis kinerja keberlanjutan, serta mengkomunikasikan hasilnya kepada pemangku kepentingan. Kinerja Departemen EHSS &amp; ESG dilaporkan kepada Chief Operating Officer (COO) dan dalam rapat Direksi setiap bulan, serta disampaikan kepada Dewan Komisaris setiap dua bulan sekali. Perseroan telah memiliki key performance indicator (KPI) kinerja keberlanjutan di bawah pengawasan Direksi. Salah satu KPI ini adalah meningkatkan efisiensi di seluruh kegiatan operasional, pencapaian target dalam indikator kinerja utama (KPI) yang telah ditetapkan akan dievaluasi secara berkala. Pengungkapan informasi proses dan hasil penilaian tersebut disampaikan dalam Laporan Tahunan PT Global Digital Niaga Tbk 2024. [E.1][2-13][2-18][2-24]

Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris secara aktif terlibat dalam pengembangan dan pembaruan target dan kinerja keberlanjutan. Selain itu, Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris turut meninjau capaian kinerja keberlanjutan, yang kemudian disampaikan setiap tahun kepada pemangku kepentingan lainnya melalui laporan keberlanjutan. Peninjauan kinerja ini dilakukan secara independen dan transparan untuk memastikan keselarasan strategi keberlanjutan perusahaan dengan tujuan perusahaan, menghindari konflik kepentingan internal, serta memperkuat akuntabilitas dalam pengambilan keputusan.

Perseroan berhasil menjaga kepatuhan terhadap hukum dan peraturan yang berlaku, yakni hingga akhir tahun 2024. Perseroan tidak menghadapi kasus terkait konflik kepentingan dan tidak mendapatkan sanksi denda maupun hukum lainnya atau tuntutan material baik dari lembaga berwenang, pihak ketiga, maupun pihak regulator lain. [2-12][2-15][2-27]

Terkait informasi lebih lanjut mengenai RUPS, keberagaman dan independensi Dewan, peran Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris beserta fungsi-fungsi di bawahnya, konflik kepentingan, pencalonan dan pemilihan badan tata kelola tertinggi, proses nominasi dan seleksi, diskusi isu kritis, kebijakan dan proses menentukan remunerasi, serta besaran rasio kompensasi total tahunan Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris disampaikan dalam laporan tahunan 2024. [2-10][2-11][2-15][2-16][2-19][2-20][2-21]

To strengthen governance and oversight of sustainability performance, the Board of Directors has established a dedicated unit under the coordination of the Environment, Health, Safety, and Security &amp; Environmental, Social, and Governance (EHSS &amp; ESG) Department through the Job Rotation Decree 1652/II/SK-P/t/X/2023. This unit is responsible for implementing sustainability policies and strategies, measuring and analyzing sustainability performance, and communicating the results to stakeholders. The EHSS &amp; ESG Department reports to the Chief Operating Officer (COO) and presents its performance in monthly Board of Directors meetings, as well as to the Board of Commissioners every two months. The Company has established key performance indicators (KPIs) for sustainability performance under the supervision of the Board of Directors. One of these KPIs is to improve efficiency in all operational activities, the achievement of targets in the key performance indicators (KPIs) that have been set will be evaluated regularly. Disclosure of information on the process and results of the assessment is submitted in the Annual Report of PT Global Digital Niaga Tbk 2024. [E.1][2-13][2-18][2-24]

The Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners play an active role in setting and updating sustainability targets while reviewing performance achievements which are then submitted to other stakeholders through annual sustainability reports. This performance review has been conducted independently and transparently to ensure alignment of the company's sustainability strategy with the company's objectives, avoid internal conflicts of interest, and strengthen accountability in decision-making.

The Company complies with applicable laws and regulations, and by so doing until the end of 2024 did not face any cases related to conflicts of interest and did not receive any fines or other legal sanctions or material demands from authorized institutions, third parties, or other regulators. [2-12][2-15][2-27]

Further information about the GMS, the Boards' diversity and independence, the role of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners and their subordinate functions, conflicts of interest, nomination and election of the highest governance body, nomination and selection process, discussion of critical issues, policies and processes for determining remuneration, and the annual compensation ratio of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners can be found in the 2024 annual report. # MANAJEMEN RISIKO KEBERLANJUTAN [E.3]

Bibli Tiket telah mengidentifikasi, menilai, dan mengelola risiko keberlanjutan di industri retail mengacu pada ISO 31000:2018 dan COSO. Dalam memitigasi risiko, Perseroan juga menerapkan model pertahanan tiga lapis (three lines of defense). Integrasi manajemen risiko keberlanjutan dalam seluruh kegiatan operasional membantu mengoptimalkan kinerja melalui alokasi sumber daya yang lebih efektif dan efisien. Pengelolaan risiko keberlanjutan ini menjadi tanggung jawab Unit Audit Internal dan Departemen EHSS &amp; ESG.

# SUSTAINABILITY RISK MANAGEMENT [E.3]

Bibli Tiket has identified, assessed, and managed sustainability risks in the retail industry by adhering to ISO 31000:2018 and COSO. To mitigate these risks, the Company applies a three-line of defense model. The integration of sustainability risk management across all operational activities enhances performance by enabling more effective and efficient resource allocation. The Internal Audit Unit and the EHSS &amp; ESG Department are responsible for overseeing sustainability risk management.
