# Tumbuh Bersama Masyarakat

EMPOWERING GROWTH WITH COMMUNITY

Blibli Tiket bertumbuh bersama masyarakat melalui kolaborasi dalam menjalankan program tanggung jawab sosial dan lingkungan di antaranya kegiatan pelatihan, pendampingan, dan pengembangan usaha untuk meningkatkan nilai tambah dan memperluas dampak positif guna mendorong pertumbuhan ekonomi secara inklusif dan berkelanjutan.

Blibli Tiket fosters growth alongside the community through collaborative social and environmental responsibility programs—including training, mentorship, and business development initiatives. These efforts are designed to enhance value and broaden positive impacts, ultimately driving inclusive and sustainable economic growth.

Perseroan menjalankan program tanggung jawab sosial dan lingkungan (TJSL) yang berdampak luas bagi masyarakat. Melalui sinergi bersama pemerintah, mitra usaha, komunitas, dan organisasi, Blibli Tiket fokus pada pemberdayaan masyarakat, peningkatan ekonomi tidak langsung, serta kompetensi dan proses digitalisasi pelaku UMKM. Upaya ini bertujuan meningkatkan kesejahteraan masyarakat, termasuk mengelola dampak positif maupun negatif dari operasional perusahaan seperti rantai pasokan serta infrastruktur fisik dan digital. Selain itu, implementasi program TJSL juga mempertimbangkan respons terhadap isu global dan mitigasi risiko, sehingga mampu berkontribusi mendukung pencapaian TPB. [F.23][F.28]

Demi memastikan program yang diselenggarakan berdampak luas bagi masyarakat, Blibli Tiket melakukan pengukuran dampak program melalui monitoring dan evaluasi. Pengukuran ini dilakukan secara berkala oleh Departemen Seller Development untuk Blibli serta Divisi People Strategy dan Divisi Public Relation untuk tiket.com. Hasilnya akan dilaporkan kepada Direktur masing-masing untuk ditindaklanjuti.

The Company implements a comprehensive Corporate Social and Environmental Responsibility (CSER) program that significantly impacts the community. In partnership with government agencies, business partners, communities, and organizations, Blibli Tiket focuses on empowering local communities, fostering indirect economic development, and advancing the digital transformation and capacity of MSMEs. These initiatives aim to enhance community well-being by effectively managing both the positive and negative impacts of our operations—ranging from supply chain dynamics to physical and digital infrastructure. Furthermore, the program addresses global issues and risk mitigation, thereby contributing to the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals. [F.23][F.28]

To ensure a broad community impact, Blibli Tiket employs rigorous monitoring and evaluation protocols. These assessments are conducted periodically by the Seller Development Department for Blibli and by the People Strategy and Public Relations Divisions for tiket.com, with the outcomes reported directly to the respective Directors.
