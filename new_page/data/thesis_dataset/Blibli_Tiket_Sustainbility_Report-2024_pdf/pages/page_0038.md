# Strengthening Warehouse and Logistics Networks

Blibli has developed a modern warehouse and logistics infrastructure that streamlines the entire process, from receiving goods and conducting quality control to storage, order processing, and packaging before delivery. The company's warehousing system incorporates AI and the Internet of Things (IoT) to monitor inventory effectively. In 2024, to enhance its logistics and supply chain capabilities, Blibli completed the construction of a new warehouse in the Marunda industrial and warehousing area of West Java. With this new facility, the Company is optimistic that it can lead the implementation of smart logistics and supply chain management in fulfilling customer orders.

The Marunda warehouse is designed with a green building concept in mind and comes equipped with comprehensive tech-driven solutions that enhance Blibli's operational efficiency and fulfillment capabilities. Serving as the central hub of Blibli's supply chain, this facility will receive products from most suppliers in the Jabodetabek area before they are distributed across Blibli's extensive warehouse network.

All Blibli warehouses implement the Halal Assurance System (HAS 23000), which ensures a thorough curation of halal products through regular inspections and audits. Special packaging is also utilized to maintain compliance with halal requirements. The

Dalam menciptakan ekosistem halal, Blibli juga telah bersinergi dengan Badan Penyelenggara Jaminan Produk Halal (BFJPH) untuk mengakselerasi terciptanya ekosistem perdagangan halal melalui bank data produk halal bagi mitra seller dalam mendapatkan sertifikasi halal produknya. Selain 23 hubs, seluruh gudang juga terdapat armada pengiriman sendiri yaitu BES Paket (mitra 1PL) yang bekerja sama dengan 20+ mitra logistic (mitra 3PL) untuk memberikan pilihan metode pengiriman yang lebih luas kepada pelanggan. Kramat Jati and Batu Caper warehouses have received halal certification, demonstrating that their standard operating procedures contribute to the development of a halal ecosystem in Indonesia.

In its halal ecosystem, Blibli synergises with the Halal Product Guarantee Agency (BFJPH) to accelerate the creation of a halal trading ecosystem using a halal product database for seller partners to obtain halal certification for their products. As a result, Blibli became the first credible e-commerce site to provide a wide range of halal products, with trustworthy seller partners, halal warehouse services, and special audit teams to oversee its halal products.

Within its logistics network, Blibli Tiket operates a hub-and-spoke system featuring 13 warehouses spanning over 202,000 m² across Jabodetabek, Bandung, Yogyakarta, Solo, Semarang, Surabaya, Medan, and Makassar. These warehouses are supported by 23 distribution centers (hubs), and a network of main distribution centers, distribution centers, mini distribution centers, and mobile distribution centers, serving as spokes to facilitate efficient delivery to end consumers.

In addition to the 23 hubs, all warehouses have their own delivery fleet, known as BES Paket (1PL partner). They are also supported by more than 20 logistics partners (3PL partners) that provide customers with a wide range of shipping options. By 2024, BES Paket has employed 600 staff members, with 400 personnel serving 47 cities and regencies across Indonesia. Furthermore, logistics partners offer a two-hour delivery service available in 45 cities throughout the country.
