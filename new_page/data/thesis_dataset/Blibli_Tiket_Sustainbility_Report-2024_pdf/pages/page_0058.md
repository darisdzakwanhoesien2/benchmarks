Perseroan secara aktif mendorong aksi iklim melalui penyelenggaraan program Langkah Membumi Festival (LMF) dan penanaman pohon. # COLLABORATION FOR CLIMATE RESILIENCE

The Company encouraged climate action through the Langkah Membumi Festival (LMF) program and tree planting programs. Although none of Blibli Tiket's operational locations, including office buildings, warehousing, logistics, e-commerce, and online travel agencies, are in protected areas or areas with high biodiversity, these programs are still implemented to increase sustainability awareness and commitment. # 1. Langkah Membumi Festival

Langkah Membumi Festival

Program tahunan di bawah Blibli Tiket Action, yang mencerminkan komitmen Blibli terhadap keberlanjutan dan pemberdayaan para pemangku kepentingaan, termasuk masyarakat untuk bersama-sama menjalankan aksi kolektif dalam menciptakan dampak positif di sektor ekonomi, sosial, dan lingkungan.

An annual program under Blibli Tiket Action, reflecting Blibli's commitment to sustainability and empowerment of stakeholders including the communities to jointly carry out collective action to create a positive impact in the economic, social, and environmental sectors.

Memasuki tahun ketiganya, Festival Langkah Membumi 2024 kembali berkolaborasi dengan Ecoxytem yang berfokus pada pengembangan eksprenur dan solusi iklim. Mengangkat tema "CollaborAction for the Earth", festival ini digelar pada 2-3 November di Senayan Park sebagai wadah InterAction, ExplorAction, dan CollaborAction untuk menciptakan ide dan solusi berkelanjutan. Kegiatannya mencakup talkshow, workshop, ecopreneur market, music festival, olahraga, cek kesehatan, test drive mobil listrik, dan lainnya.

Sebagai festival keberlanjutan pertama di Indonesia yang mengukur dampak secara holistik, LMF mengacu pada standar internasional ISO 14040/44 Life Cycle Assessment. Berdasarkan hasil pengukuran dampak, festival ini menawarkan solusi inovatif yang menginspirasi masyarakat untuk mengadopsi praktik ramah lingkungan dalam kehidupan sehari-hari. Melalui festival ini, Blibli Tiket memperkuat pencapaian TPB 17.

In its third year, the 2024 Langkah Membumi Festival once again collaborated with Ecoxytem, and focused on developing ecopreneurs and climate solutions. With the theme "CollaborAction for the Earth", this festival was held on November 2-3 at Senayan Park as a forum for InterAction, ExplorAction, and CollaborAction, for stakeholders to jointly create innovative sustainable ideas and solutions. There were different activities including talk shows, workshops, ecopreneur markets, music festivals, sports activities, health checks, electric car test drives and others.

As the first sustainability festival in Indonesia that measures impact holistically, LMF refers to the international ISO 14040/44 Life Cycle Assessment standards. Based on the impact measurement results, this festival offered innovative solutions to inspire people to adopt eco-friendly practices in their everyday life. Through this festival, Blibli Tiket reinforced achievement of SDG 17.
