# Internship Program Melalui Magang Bersertifikat Kampus Merdeka (MBKM)

Internship Program Through the Independent Campus Certified Internship (MBKM)

Program magang yang diselenggarakan bekerja sama dengan Kementerian Pendidikan, Kebudayaan, Riset, dan Teknologi (Kemendikbudristek), memberikan kesempatan bagi mahasiswa dari berbagai universitas di seluruh Indonesia untuk mendapatkan pengalaman kerja nyata di Bibli. Sepanjang 2024, total mahasiswa yang mengikuti program ini sebanyak 458 orang.

In collaboration with the Ministry of Education, Culture, Research, and Technology (Kemendikbudristek) the internship program offers students from universities across Indonesia valuable real-world work experience at Bibli. In 2024, 458 students participated, fostering talent development and contributing to a sustainable future workforce.
