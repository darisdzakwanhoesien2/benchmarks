# KINERJA EKONOMI

Pada tahun 2024, Blibli Tiket berhasil mencatatkan pertumbuhan positif dengan peningkatan pendapatan mencapai Rp16.716 miliar atau naik 14% dibandingkan tahun 2023 yang sejalan dengan peningkatan kinerja profitabilitas Perseroan. Hingga akhir tahun 2024, Blibli Tiket telah mengoperasikan 204 toko fisik elektronik konsumen, 60 gerai supermarket premium, dan 32 home and living experience centers di seluruh Indonesia.

Ke depan, Perseroan fokus pada strategi usaha dan kebijakan strategis yaitu ditekankan pada perluasan pilihan produk, penguatan layanan, pengembangan teknologi, inovasi omnichannel, kepemimpinan biaya serta pengembangan sinergi potensial di dalam ekosistem Blibli Tiket yang terintegrasi. Di sisi operasional, Perseroan juga telah mulai menerapkan otomasi di berbagai proses, termasuk pemanfaatan kecerdasan buatan (artificial intelligence).

# ECONOMIC PERFORMANCE

As of 2024, Blibli Tiket successfully recorded positive growth with an increase in revenue reaching IDR16.716 billion or up 14% compared to 2023 which is in line with the improvement in the Company's profitability performance. By the end of 2024, Blibli Tiket has operated 204 physical consumer electronics stores, 60 premium supermarket outlets, and 32 home and living experience centers across Indonesia.

Looking forward, the Company will focus its business strategies and strategic policies on emphasizing expanding product offerings, strengthening services, advancing technology, innovating the omnichannel, enhancing cost leadership and developing potential synergies within an unified Blibli Tiket ecosystem. On the operational front, the Company has implemented several otomation initiatives, including the use of artificial intelligence.
