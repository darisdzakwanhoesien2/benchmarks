# Blibli Rising Innovator Skills Enhancement (Blibli RISE)

![img-163.jpeg](img-163.jpeg)

Program mageng unik yang dirancang untuk siswa Sekolah Menengah Atas (SMA) dengan memberikan keterampilan dan pengetahuan yang dibutuhkan untuk sukses di dunia perdagangan omnichannel yang dinamis. Melalui program ini siswa mendapatkan pengalaman langsung di dunia kerja sejak dini. Peserta akan menjalani rotasi di berbagai fungsi dalam perusahaan, memungkinkan mereka memahami bagaimana setiap departemen berkontribusi terhadap keberhasilan Blibli secara keseluruhan. Di 2024, kegiatan ini dilakukan di Jakarta dengan total peserta sebanyak 10 orang.

This one-of-a-kind internship program is specially tailored for high school students, aiming to equip them with practical skills and foundational knowledge to excel in today's fast-paced omnichannel commerce environment. Through this initiative, students are exposed to real-world work experiences early on. Participants go through a rotation across multiple departments within the company, giving them the opportunity to explore various roles and gain a deeper understanding of how each team supports Blibli's broader operations and goals. In 2024, the program took place in Jakarta and welcomed a total of 10 student participants.

# Internship Gateway to Nurture Future Talent (Blibli Ignite)

![img-164.jpeg](img-164.jpeg)

Program magang yang dirancang khusus untuk memberikan pengalaman kerja nyata bagi siswa Sekolah Menengah Kejuruan (SMK) dalam bidang operasional dan logistik. Melalui program ini, peserta akan menjalani Fraktik Kerja Lapangan (PKL) di warehouse Blibli, yakni mereka dapat mengasah keterampilan teknis dan memahami langsung proses kerja di industri e-commerce. Dengan bimbingan dari para profesional, siswa SMK akan mendapatkan wawasan mendalam tentang manajemen gudang, distribusi, serta efisiensi operasional, yang akan menjadi bekal berharga untuk karier mereka di masa depan. Pada tahun 2024, Blibli IGNITE diimplementasikan kepada SMK Industri Kreatif dan SMKN IT Bekasi pada Mei 2024, dengan total peserta sebanyak 91 orang.

Blibli's internship initiative is designed to offer real-world work experience to vocational high school (SMK) students, focusing on operations and logistics. Through this program, students take part in Field Work Practices (PKL) at Blibli's warehouse, where they can sharpen their technical abilities and observe e-commerce workflows up close. Under the guidance of experienced professionals, participants gain in-depth knowledge of warehouse operations, distribution systems, and how to improve operational efficiency. In May 2024, Blibli IGNITE was carried out in collaboration with SMK Industri Kreatif and SMKN IT Bekasi, involving a total of 91 student participants.

# Blibli Care Uplifting Bright Minds Building Learning Experience (BUBBLE)

![img-165.jpeg](img-165.jpeg)

Program keterlibatan sosial dari BlibliCare yang dibuat untuk berbagi kebahagiaan dan pengalaman belajar yang menyenangkan bersama anak-anak marginal. Dalam semangat memperkuat budaya #WeCare, kami mendorong karyawan belajar dan bermain bersama, serta berbagi sebagian dari yang kita miliki. Kegiatan yang dilakukan meliputi pengalaman belajar yang menyenangkan seperti mendongong dan mewarnai untuk anak-anak sekolah dasar, serta vision-boarding dan permainan berbasis mindset layanan untuk anak-anak sekolah menengah. Selain itu, terdapat donasi berupa pakaian, calana, buku, dan lainnya, serta makan siang bersama. Program ini melibatkan agen Blibli Care sebagai relawan dan lebih dari 60 anak.

BlibliCare's community outreach program was created to spread joy and provide meaningful learning experiences for marginalized children. Embracing the #WeCare spirit, our employees take part in activities that allow them to learn, play, and share with the children. Fun and educational sessions include storytelling and coloring for elementary school kids, along with vision-boarding and service-oriented games for junior high students. The program also includes donations such as clothes, pants, books, and other useful items, as well as enjoying lunch together. This initiative is carried out by BlibliCare volunteers and has involved more than 60 children.

# Innovation Challenge Masterclass

![img-166.jpeg](img-166.jpeg)

Blibli berpartisipasi dalam Innovation Challenge Masterclass, sebuah program yang ditujukan bagi mahasiswa pemilik usaha rintisan (startup) untuk mengembangkan keterampilan bisnis mereka. Dalam program ini, Blibli bekerja sama dengan Innovative Academy Universitas Gadjah Mada berbagi wawasan mengenai pengembangan Business Model Canvas dan strategi pemasaran kepada 100 peserta. Melalui sesi ini, peserta mendapatkan pemahaman mendalam tentang bagaimana membangun model bisnis yang solid serta strategi pemasaran yang efektif agar startup mereka dapat berkembang dan bersaing di pasar.

Blibli took part in the Innovation Challenge Masterclass, a program aimed at supporting university students who are building their own startups by strengthening their business skills. In collaboration with the Innovative Academy of Universitas Gadjah Mada, Blibli shared valuable insights with 100 participants, focusing on how to develop a strong Business Model Canvas and craft effective marketing strategies. Through this session, participants gained a comprehensive understanding of how to create a solid business foundation and apply marketing approaches that can help their startups grow and remain competitive in the market.
