# EKOSISTEM OMNICHANNEL

[F.26]

Blibli Tiket terus meningkatkan sinergi antar platform untuk menjadi omnichannel commerce and lifestyle terbaik. Perseroan menghadirkan pengalaman belanja dan bertransaksi bagi pelanggan secara seamless baik daring maupun luring melalui ekosistem omnichannel dengan berbagai pilihan serta fleksibilitas. Dengan integrasi ini, pelanggan kini dapat menikmati berbagai kemudahan melalui inovasi fitur terbaru keanggotaan terpadu (Unified Membership). Pelanggan kini dapat menikmati kemudahan login yang lebih cepat, aman, dan nyaman dengan kredensial yang sama di seluruh platform, sekaligus memperoleh lebih banyak manfaat dan personalisasi sebagai kelanjutan dari inovasi integrasi ekosistem.

On a quarterly basis, the Company disseminates its unaudited financial results, while annual audited financial statements are provided to financial institutions. Blibli Tiket adheres to all regulatory requirements, inclusive of tax obligations and various levies such as Taxes and Non-Tax State Revenues (PNBP). The Company actively engages stakeholders in the development of its tax, legislative, and administrative frameworks to mitigate any potential impact on its reputation and trustworthiness. The Finance Director oversees tax compliance activities, maintaining communication and collaboration with the local Tax Office. In 2024, Blibli Tiket ensured adherence to regulations by disbursing employee service fees accordingly and did not receive any financial assistance from the government. [201-4][207-1][207-2][207-3]

# OMNICHANNEL ECOSYSTEM

[F.26]

Blibli Tiket continues to enhance the synergy between platforms, aiming to become the best commerce and lifestyle omnichannel. The company provides customers with a seamless shopping and transaction experience, both online and offline, by offering a flexible and diverse omnichannel ecosystem. Through this integration, customers can now experience various conveniences through the latest Unified Membership feature innovation. Customers can now enjoy faster, safer, and more convenient login with the same credentials across platforms, while gaining more benefits and personalization as a continuation of the ecosystem integration innovation.
