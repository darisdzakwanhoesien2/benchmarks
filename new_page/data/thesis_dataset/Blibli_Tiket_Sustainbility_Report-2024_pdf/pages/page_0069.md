## PELATIHAN DAN PENGEMBANGAN KOMPETENSI KARYAWAN [E.2][F.22][3-3]

Blibli Tiket terus berupaya membangun tim yang kompeten sejak perekrutan, pengembangan kapasitas, karakter, dan teknologi supaya mampu menghasilkan kinerja dan solusi yang unggul bagi kebutuhan para pelanggan. Dalam pengembangan kapasitas, Perseroan memberikan program pelatihan dan pengembangan untuk seluruh karyawan baik tetap maupun alih daya.

Sepanjang 2024, telah dilaksanakan training, workshop, sharing session, community learning, serta penggunaan learning management system seperti Blilearning dan tiketAcademy untuk karyawan tetap dan kontrak. Sementara untuk karyawan alih daya terdapat program onboarding, pelatihan regular, leadership dan self-development training. Melalui berbagai program pelatihan dan pengembangan tersebut, Blibli Tiket berhasil mencapai tingkat kepuasan pelatihan 4,8 untuk Blibli dan 4,6 untuk tiket.com. [2-17]

## EMPLOYEE COMPETENCY TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT [E.2][F.22][3-3]

Blibli Tiket is committed to cultivating a highly competent team through strategic recruitment, capacity building, character development, and technological advancement—ensuring superior performance and innovative customer solutions. The Company offers comprehensive training programs for both permanent and contract employees to continuously enhance workforce capabilities.

During 2024, training, workshops, sharing sessions, community learning, and the use of learning management systems such as Blilearning and tiketAcademy was organized for permanent and contract employees. While, for outsourced employees, onboarding programs, regular training, leadership and self-development training were held. During these training and development programs, Blibli Tiket achieved a training satisfaction level of 4.8 for Blibli and 4.6 for tiket.com. [2-17]
