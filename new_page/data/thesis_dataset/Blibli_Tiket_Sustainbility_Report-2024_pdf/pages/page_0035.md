Adapun dari segi inovasi layanan dan pemanfaatan teknologi, Blibli Tiket berupaya memberikan pengalaman terbaik dan setara bagi seluruh pelanggan dengan mengoptimalkan pemanfaatan artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning (ML), dan proprietary data analytics. Salah satu target inovasi tersebut adalah meningkatkan kemampuan kurasi, penawaran produk serta layanan yang lebih personalized dan convenience kepada masing-masing pelanggan. Keunggulan ini mampu meningkatkan customer journey dari awal berinteraksi dengan platform Blibli Tiket, mulai dari pencarian produk, pembayaran, pemenuhan pesanan, hingga layanan purna jual.

Pada tahun 2024, penggunaan teknologi AI/ML meningkat secara signifikan didukung area teknologi baru seperti GenAI, LLM, dan Vector Search yang dimanfaatkan secara efektif. Hal ini memberikan dampak positif termasuk dalam pengalaman penjual dan pelanggan, efisiensi biaya operasional, penargetan pelanggan, rekomendasi produk, dan pencarian.

On service innovations and technology side, Blibli Tiket provides an exceptional and equitable experience for all customers through optimizing the use of artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning (ML), and proprietary data analytics. One of the targets of these innovations is to improve curation capabilities, offer a variety of products and services that are more personalized and convenient to each customer. These advantages are able to improve the customer journey from the beginning of interaction with the Blibli Tiket platform, from product search, payment, order fulfillment, to after-sales service.

In 2024, the use of AI and machine learning (ML) technology grew significantly, aided by advancements in new technologies like Generative AI (GenAI), large language models (LLM), and vector search. This surge positively affected both seller and customer experiences, leading to greater operational cost efficiency, improved customer targeting, enhanced product recommendations, and more effective search capabilities.
