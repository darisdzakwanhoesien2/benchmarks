# PENYELENGGARAAN RAPAT UMUM PEMEGANG SAHAM LUAR BIASA KEDUA DI TAHUN 2024

Sesuai Anggaran Dasar, Perseroan telah menyelenggarakan RUPS Luar Biasa yang diselenggarakan pada 28 November 2024 bertempat di Ruang Nusantara, Bakrie Tower lantai 36, Jakarta. Sesuai dengan Peraturan Perundangan, sebelum pelaksanaan RUPS Perseroan telah menyampaikan Pemberitahuan Rencana kepada Otoritas Jasa Keuangan, melalui Surat Direksi No. 081/BNBR/CS-OJK/X/24 tertanggal 22 Oktober 2024, serta mengumumkan Rencana Rapat ini juga pada 22 Oktober 2024 melalui situs Perseroan, situs Bursa Efek Indonesia dan situs Kustodian Sentral Efek Indonesia.

# EXECUTION OF THE SECOND EXTRAORDINARY GENERAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS IN 2024

Pursuant to the Articles of Association, the Company has held the Extraordinary GMS on November 28, 2024, in Nusantara Room, Bakrie Tower 36th floor, Jakarta. Based on the regulations, prior to the GMS the Company has informed the Plan Announcement to the Financial Services Authority, through Corporate Secretary's Letter No. 081/BNBR/CS-OJK/X/24 dated October 22, 2024, and announced the Meeting Plan also on October 22, 2024 through Company website, Indonesia Stock Exchange website and Indonesia Central Securities Depository website.
