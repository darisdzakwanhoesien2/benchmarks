Inflation fell gradually as food and energy prices declined, with annual Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation falling to 1.5% in November 2024. This was due to increased agricultural production and lower global oil prices¹. Realized inflation in December 2024 was 1.57% (yoy), down from 2.61% (yoy) in 2023².

Entering 2025, the World Bank projects Indonesia's economic growth to reach 5.1%. Indonesia's financial potential in 2025 is expected to remain promising, driven by policy reforms, adoption of digitalization, and investment in strategic sectors. However, the global economy is still expected to face a number of challenges, including slowing global economic growth. The International Monetary Fund (IMF) projects global economic growth in 2025 to reach 3.3%, a slight increase from the 2024 projection of 3.1%. Risks affecting global conditions include the weakening economies of China and the United States, continued geopolitical tensions, the impact of climate change, uncertainty over global interest rates, and the latest developments related to the implementation of import tariffs by the United States to all countries in the world.

The Board of Commissioners considers that in the midst of uncertain global economic conditions, the Board of Directors has taken careful steps in determining the Company's business direction. Regarding the Company's financial condition, the Board of Directors has successfully revitalized the balance sheet through debt restructuring efforts that have been ongoing for the past few years, followed by a quasi reorganization corporate action that became effective on August 22, 2024, and in December 2024 the Company also completed the final stage of corporate action in the form of a private placement. This series of corporate actions has resulted in a much leaner and healthier balance sheet posture.

With the completion of this corporate action, the Company's debt to assets ratio at the end of 2024 decreased to 43% from 63% at the end of 2023. Meanwhile, the Company's debt to equity ratio also dropped significantly to 75% from 167% at the end of 2023. As of December 31, 2024, the Company managed to record retained earnings of Rp328 billion as a result of the quasi reorganization corporate action that has succeeded in eliminating a deficit at the beginning balance of 2024 as much as Rp19.5 trillion.
