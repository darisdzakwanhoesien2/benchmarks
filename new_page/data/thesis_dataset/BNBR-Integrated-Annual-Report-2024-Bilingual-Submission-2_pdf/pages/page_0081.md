# Penghargaan Achmad Bakrie 2024

Achmad Bakrie Award 2024

Penghargaan Achmad Bakrie kembali digelar pada tahun 2024 dengan memberikan penghargaan kepada lima orang yang berprestasi di bidangnya masing-masing yang mampu memberikan manfaat kepada masyarakat luas, sesuai amanah H. Achmad Bakrie tentang kelndonesiaan dan kemanfaatan

Kelima penerima Penghargaan Achmad Bakrie (PAB) XX 2024 adalah Jusuf Wanandi untuk bidang Pemikiran Sosial, D. Zawawi Imron untuk bidang Sastra, Afriyanti Sumboja untuk bidang Sains dan Teknologi, Harapan untuk bidang Kesehatan, dan penghargaan khusus untuk Grandprix Thomryes Marth Kadja untuk bidang Ilmuwan Muda.

Penerima penghargaan tahun ini dipilih melalui seleksi ketat tim juri yang terdiri dari tim Freedom Institute, perwakilan Grup Bakrie, dan pakar atau konsultan independen. Malam penganugerahan dibuka dengan kata sambutan dari Direktur Utama / CEO BNBR Anindya Bakrie dan Wakil Presiden Republik Indonesia Ma'ruf Amin yang menyampaikan pandangannya tentang "Upaya Memajukan Bangsa".

The Achmad Bakrie Award was held again in 2024 by giving awards to five people who excelled in their respective fields and were able to provide benefits to the wider community, in accordance with H. Achmad Bakrie's mandate regarding Indonesian-ness and usefulness.

The four recipients of the Achmad Bakrie Award (PAB) XX 2024 were Jusuf Wanandi for Social Thoughts field, D. Zawawi Imron for Literature field, Afriyanti Sumboja for Science and Technology field, Harapan for Health field, and special recognition for Grandprix Thomryes Marth Kadja for Young Scientist field.

This year's award recipients were selected through a strict selection by a jury team consisting of the Freedom Institute team, representatives of the Bakrie Group, and independent experts or consultants. The awarding night was opened with remarks from BNBR President Director/CEO Anindya Bakrie and Vice President of the Republic of Indonesia Ma'ruf Amin who delivered his views on "Advancing the Nation".

# VKTR TEKNOLOGI MOBILITAS, HELIO SYNAR ENERGI, MODULA TIGA DIMENSI

# Kuliah Umum FSRD ITB

General Lecture of FSRD ITB

Grup BNBR hadir sebagai narasumber di Kuliah Awal Semester Program Studi Desain Produk Fakultas Seni Rupa dan Desain (FSRD) Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB) yang diadakan di Design Centre FSRD ITB.

Grup BNBR diwakili oleh tiga perusahaan hijaunya, yaitu PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk (VKTR) yang bergerak di bidang kendaraan listrik, PT Helio Synar Energi (Helio) yang bergerak di bidang energi baru dan terbarukan, dan PT Modula Tiga Dimensi (Modula3D) yang bergerak di bidang 3D printing konstruksi.

The BNBR Group was present as guest speakers at the Beginning of Semester Lecture of the Product Design Study Program of the Faculty of Art and Design (FSRD) of the Bandung Institute of Technology (ITB) held at the Design Center FSRD ITB.

The BNBR Group was represented by three of its green companies, namely PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk (VKTR) which is engaged in electric vehicles, PT Helio Synar Energi (Helio) which is engaged in new and renewable energy, and PT Modula Tiga Dimensi (Modula3D) which is engaged in 3D printing construction.
