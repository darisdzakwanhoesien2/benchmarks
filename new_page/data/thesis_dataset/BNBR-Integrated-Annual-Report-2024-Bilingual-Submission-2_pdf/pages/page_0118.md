# ENVIRONMENT

Tanggung jawab terhadap lingkungan tertanam kuat dalam filosofi bisnis BNBR dan anak perusahaannya. Berkomitmen terhadap keberlanjutan, Perseroan secara aktif mengikuti sertifikasi seperti ISO 14001 untuk unit-unit manufakturnya, memastikan peningkatan kinerja lingkungan yang berkelanjutan melalui pengelolaan sumber daya yang lebih baik, efisiensi energi, pengendalian emisi, konservasi air, dan pengurangan limbah.

Secara paralel, BNBR memperluas perannya dalam pengembangan energi bersih melalui PT Bakrie Power (BP) dan anak perusahaannya PT Helio Synar Energi (Helio). Selain mengembangkan pembangkit listrik tenaga surya (PLTS), BP juga menjajaki panas bumi, angin, dan energi terbarukan lainnya. Helio telah memasang sistem tenaga surya atap di Braja Mukti Cakra dan Bakrie Pipe Industries. Hingga saat ini, inisiatif energi terbarukan BNBR-baik yang telah selesai maupun yang sedang berjalan-telah mencapai kapasitas gabungan sebesar 197 MW. Melalui upaya-upaya ini, BNBR mendukung transisi Indonesia menuju Net-Zero Emissions, sejalan dengan tujuan iklim global dan komitmen yang dibuat di bawah COP28.

# SOCIAL

BNBR menjalankan inisiatif tanggung jawab sosial perusahaan (CSR) untuk memberdayakan masyarakat di sekitar wilayah operasinya, meningkatkan kualitas hidup, dan melindungi lingkungan. Program-program sosial ini dilaksanakan dengan kolaborasi bersama perusahaan-perusahaan Grup Bakrie lainnya melalui yayasan Bakrie Amanah. Pada tahun 2024, BNBR, melalui Bakrie Amanah, menyalurkan bantuan sebesar Rp 1,74 miliar, memberikan manfaat kepada lebih dari 2.000 individu.

With a firm belief that ESG is key to long-term value creation, BNBR is dedicated to enhancing performance across all ESG dimensions. This ongoing commitment ensures that the Company not only generates sustainable business outcomes but also delivers lasting benefits to society and all stakeholders.

# ENVIRONMENT

Environmental responsibility is deeply embedded in the business philosophy of BNBR and its subsidiaries. Committed to sustainability, the Company actively pursues certifications such as ISO 14001 for its manufacturing units, ensuring continuous improvement in environmental performance through better resource management, energy efficiency, emissions control, water conservation, and waste reduction.

In parallel, BNBR is expanding its role in clean energy development through PT Bakrie Power (BP) and its subsidiary PT Helio Synar Energi (Helio). Beyond developing solar power plants (PLTS), BP is exploring geothermal, wind, and other renewable energy. Helio has already installed rooftop solar systems at Braja Mukti Cakra and Bakrie Pipe Industries. To date, BNBR's renewable energy initiatives—both completed and in progress—have reached a combined capacity of 197 MW. Through these efforts, BNBR supports Indonesia's transition to Net-Zero Emissions, in line with global climate goals and the commitments made under COP28.

# SOCIAL

BNBR carries out its corporate social responsibility (CSR) initiatives to empower communities around its operations, improve quality of life, and protect the environment. These socially driven programs are implemented in collaboration with other Bakrie Group companies through the Bakrie Amanah foundation. In 2024, BNBR, through Bakrie Amanah, distributed assistance amounting to Rp 1.74 billion, benefiting over 2,000 individuals.
