# FONDASI BNBR MENUJU BISNIS BERKELANJUTAN – KINERJA KEUANGAN

Pada tahun 2024 berbagai himpitan dari faktor eksternal di dunia membayangi perekonomian Indonesia, meski demikian Indonesia terbilang berhasil mempertahankan momentum pertumbuhannya dan bahkan membuat momen titik balik di tahun 2024.

Pada akhir tahun buku 2024 Perseroan mencatatkan pendapatan bersih sejumlah Rp 3,87 triliun, meningkat sebesar 2,9% dibandingkan tahun 2023. Segmen Manufaktur dan Infrastruktur, yang merupakan sumber pendapatan terbesar Perseroan, meningkat sebanyak 0,3% pada tahun 2024 menjadi sejumlah Rp 3,48 triliun. Konsistensi unit usaha dalam meraih peluang dalam segmen usahanya berdampak positif terhadap pertumbuhan kinerja unit usaha manufaktur. Di segmen Perdagangan, Jasa dan Investasi Perusahaan mencatatkan peningkatan sebanyak 13,6% menjadi sejumlah Rp 136,46 miliar pada tahun 2024, peningkatan ini antara lain merupakan dampak peningkatan penjualan bus listrik dan kendaraan listrik lainnya oleh VKTR.

BNBR’s manufacturing subsidiaries continue to play vital roles in their respective sectors. Yet, as the business landscape evolves, revitalizing and modernizing these units remains essential to maintain competitiveness. BNBR remains confident in the manufacturing sector’s promise—buoyed by strong governmental support—and continues to contribute meaningfully to national progress through partnerships in both public and private infrastructure initiatives.

Looking ahead, the Company is dedicated to strengthening its subsidiaries through advanced technology integration and strategic investment in human and capital resources. These efforts are aimed at reinforcing BNBR’s role in fostering sustainable growth in alignment with the Golden Indonesia 2045 vision. With a solid industrial foundation, a future-focused approach, and a skilled workforce, BNBR is committed to propelling Indonesia’s progress through innovation-driven infrastructure development and smart manufacturing excellence.

# BNBR FOUNDATION TOWARDS SUSTAINABLE BUSINESS – FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE

In 2024 various pressures from external factors in the world overshadowed the Indonesian economy, even so Indonesia was considered successful in maintaining its growth momentum and even created a turnaround moment in 2024.

At the end of the 2024 financial year, the Company recorded net revenues of Rp 3.87 trillion, an increase of 2.9% compared to 2023. The Manufacturing and Infrastructure segment, which is the Company’s largest source of revenue, increased by 0.3% in 2024 to become Rp 3.48 trillion. The consistency of the business unit in seizing opportunities in its business segment has a positive impact on the growth of the manufacturing business unit’s performance. In the Trade, Services and Investment segment, the Company recorded an increase of 13.6% to Rp 136.46 billion in 2024. This decrease was partly due to increased sales of VKTR’s electric buses and other electric vehicles.
