# PEREKONOMIAN INDONESIA: PERTUMBUHAN YANG TANGGUH DI TENGAH KETIDAKPASTIAN GLOBAL

Pada tahun 2024, ekonomi Indonesia menunjukkan ketahanan yang stabil di tengah ketidakpastian global yang terus berlanjut dan perubahan kondisi eksternal. Kerangka makroekonomi negara ini berhasil menghadapi tantangan dari tingginya suku bunga global serta berakhirnya siklus boom komoditas, sehingga tetap mendukung lingkungan pertumbuhan yang stabil (Laporan Ekonomi BI, 2024; BPS Indonesia, 2025).

PDB Indonesia tumbuh sebesar 5,03% pada tahun 2024 berdasarkan perbandingan tahunan, dengan sektor manufaktur dan jasa tetap menjadi pendorong utama pertumbuhan ekonomi. Secara keseluruhan, output ekonomi mencapai sekitar IDR 22.139,0 triliun, sementara PDB per kapita meningkat menjadi IDR 78,6 juta (sekitar USD 4.960,3)—menunjukkan kemajuan meskipun masih menghadapi tantangan struktural yang berkelanjutan (BPS Indonesia, 2025).

Despite early signs of resilience, overall economic activity remains below pre-pandemic projections. In 2024, this shortfall is compounded by structural challenges such as ageing populations, entrenched market rigidities, and the digital transformation, all of which have contributed to persistently high inflation coupled with slower productivity growth (World Bank, 2024).

To counter these challenges, policymakers are calling for ambitious structural reforms aimed at relieving constraints in product and labor markets, strengthening investments, and boosting workforce participation and skills development. Additionally, accelerating the transition toward decarbonization through enhanced green and digital infrastructure investment, improved environmental standards, and broader carbon pricing is viewed as essential to foster a more sustainable economic future (OECD, 2024).

# INDONESIA ECONOMIC: RESILIENT GROWTH AMID GLOBAL UNCERTAINTY

In 2024, Indonesia's economy demonstrated steady resilience amid persistent global uncertainties and shifting external conditions. The country's macroeconomic framework effectively navigated headwinds from higher global interest rates and the winding down of the commodity cycle, supporting a stable growth environment (BI Economic Report, 2024; BPS Indonesia, 2025).

Indonesia's GDP grew by 5.03% on a comparable basis in 2024, with the manufacturing and services sectors continuing as key growth drivers. Overall economic output reached approximately IDR 22,139.0 trillion, and GDP per capita advanced to IDR 78.6 million (approximately USD 4,960.3), illustrating progress despite enduring structural challenges (BPS Indonesia, 2025).
