# SEMANGAT PEMBARUAN - FOKUS PADA ESG

Selain memperkuat fondasi keuangan dan bisnisnya, BNBR bangga dengan kemajuannya dan tetap berkomitmen untuk mengembangkan operasinya dengan penekanan kuat pada prinsip-prinsip Lingkungan, Sosial, dan Tata Kelola (Environmental, Social, and Governance - ESG). Menyadari potensi

Furthermore, the Indonesian Government's strong dedication to reducing carbon emissions and advancing renewable energy is in harmony with these initiatives. In the past few years, Indonesia has introduced several initiatives to accelerate the adoption of electric vehicles (EVs) and promote renewable energy, aligning with its commitment to reduce carbon emissions. Among those is the enactment of Presidential Regulation No. 79 of 2023 amends previous policies to accelerate the adoption and production of Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) in Indonesia.

Apart from the manufacturing sector, BNBR is also still developing various infrastructure projects through PT Bakrie Indo Infrastructure (BIIN). The two main projects currently being handled are the 26.4 km Cimanggis-Cibitung toll road in West Java, as well as the 200 km Kalija gas pipeline for phase 1 off the north coast of Java Island from Kepodang to Tambak Lorok, and phase 2 stretching from East Kalimantan to South Kalimantan.

BNBR also participates in infrastructure development projects by harnessing advancements in digital technology and supporting initiatives to enhance Indonesia's digital connectivity through one of its subsidiaries, PT Multi Kontrol Nusantara (MKN). In 2024, MKN experienced a decrease in revenue by 23.0% compared to 2023, declining from Rp 405.6 billion to Rp 312.2 billion.

Through involvement in various national programs aimed at infrastructure development and fostering industries for the future, the Company remains actively engaged in advancing the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), with particular emphasis on SDG goals 9 and 11. These goals encompass infrastructure, industry, and innovation, as well as sustainable cities and communities.

# REJUVENATION SPIRIT - FOCUS ON ESG

Beyond strengthening its financial and business foundations, BNBR takes pride in its progress and remains deeply committed to evolving its operations with a strong emphasis on Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles. Recognizing the untapped potential of ESG investments in Indonesia and the ASEAN region, BNBR continuously refines its practices to align with global standards and contribute meaningfully to local and national Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
