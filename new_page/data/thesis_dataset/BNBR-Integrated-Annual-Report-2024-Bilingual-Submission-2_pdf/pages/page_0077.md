# VKTR TEKNOLOGI MOBILITAS

Penandatanganan Kemitraan Strategis antara VKTR dengan Indomobil

Strategic Partnership Signing between VKTR and Indomobil

PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk (VKTR) mengumumkan kemitraan strategis dengan Indomobil melalui PT IMG Sejahtera Langgeng.

Kolaborasi ini menandai era baru dalam industri otomotif, dengan fokus pada pengembangan kendaraan listrik penumpang dan komersial melalui pendirian perusahaan joint venture.

VKTR membawa keunggulan teknologi kendaraan listrik, sementara Indomobil menyumbangkan pengalaman mendalam di industri otomotif dan jaringan distribusi yang mapan. Kemitraan ini mempertegaskomitmen kedua perusahaan untuk mendukung target Net Zero Emissions 2060 dan memperluas penetrasi teknologi EV di Indonesia.

PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk (VKTR) announced a strategic partnership with Indomobil through PT IMG Sejahtera Langgeng.

This collaboration marks a new era in the automotive industry, focusing on the development of passenger and commercial electric vehicles through the establishment of a joint venture company.

VKTR brings technological excellence in electric vehicles, while Indomobil contributes deep experience in the automotive industry and an established distribution network. This partnership emphasizes both companies' commitment to support the Net Zero Emissions 2060 target and expand the penetration of EV technology in Indonesia.

# VKTR TEKNOLOGI MOBILITAS

Groundbreaking Pembangunan Fasilitas Kendaraan Listrik Komersil VKTR

Groundbreaking of VKTR Commercial Electric Vehicle Facility Construction

PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk (VKTR) resmi memulai pembangunan fasilitas kendaraan listrik komersial berbasis Completely Knock Down (CKD) pertama di Indonesia, melalui anak perusahaanannya PT VKTR Sakti Industries (VKTS) di Magelang, Jawa Tengah.

Acara ini disaksikan oleh Menteri Perhubungan Republik Indonesia Budi Karya Sumadi, perwakilan Kementerian Perindustrian, pemerintah daerah, serta tokoh dan tamu undangan.

Fasilitas ini bertujuan menjadi pusat transfer teknologi, penelitian, dan pengembangan kendaraan listrik (EV), sekaligus menciptakan lapangan kerja hijau.

PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk (VKTR) officially started the construction of the first Completely Knock Down (CKD) based commercial electric vehicle facility in Indonesia, through its subsidiary PT VKTR Sakti Industries (VKTS) in Magelang, Central Java.

The event was witnessed by the Minister of Transportation of the Republic of Indonesia Budi Karya Sumadi, representatives of the Ministry of Industry, local government, as well as invited figures and guests.

The facility aims to be a center for technology transfer, research, and development of electric vehicles (EVs), while creating green jobs.
