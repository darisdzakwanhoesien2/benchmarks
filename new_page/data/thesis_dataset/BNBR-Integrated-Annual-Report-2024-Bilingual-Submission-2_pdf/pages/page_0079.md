# BAKRIE &amp; BROTHERS

Penandatanganan MOU antara Grup BNBR dengan Pertamina untuk Pengembangan Infrastruktur Riset di IKN

MOU Signing between BNBR Group and Pertamina for the Development of Shared Hub Infrastructure in IKN

Grup BNBR menandatangani nota kesepahaman (MoU) dengan Pertamina untuk pengembangan Infrastruktur Shared Hub di Ibu Kota Nusantara (IKN) yang merupakan bagian dari Nusantara Knowledge Hub IKN. Kesepakatan ini sekaligus menjadi kelanjutan dari penandatanganan Joint Statement of Collaboration pada November 2023, dimana Universitas Stanford di Amerika Serikat akan mengadakan penelitian terkait sektor keberlanjutan.

MoU ini ditandatangani oleh Direktur Utama / CEO BNBR Anindya Bakrie, dan Direktur Utama Pertamina Nicke Widyawati. Turut menyaksikan, Wakil Direktur Utama / Co-CEO BNBR Ardiansyah Bakrie, dan Kepala Otoritas Ibu Kota Nusantara (OIKN) Bambang Susantono.

Pada kesempatan yang sama, Subholding Pertamina New Renewable Energy (PNRE) dan PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk (VKTR) menjalin kerja sama dimana kedua pihak akan mengembangkan e-Maas (electric Mobility as a Service) sebagai inovasi transisi menuju ekosistem EV skala besar.

BNBR Group signed a memorandum of understanding (MoU) with Pertamina for the development of Shared Hub Infrastructure in the Nusantara Capital City (IKN) which is part of the Nusantara Knowledge Hub IKN. This agreement is also a continuation of the signing of the Joint Statement of Collaboration in November 2023, where Stanford University in the United States will conduct research related to the sustainability sector.

This MoU was signed by BNBR President Director / CEO Anindya Bakrie, and Pertamina President Director Nicke Widyawati. Also witnessing, BNBR Vice President Director / Co-CEO Ardiansyah Bakrie, and Head of the Nusantara Capital Authority (OIKN) Bambang Susantono.

On the same occasion, Pertamina New Renewable Energy (PNRE) Subholding and PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk (VKTR) established a cooperation where both parties will develop e-Maas (electric Mobility as a Service) as a transition innovation towards a large-scale EV ecosystem.

# BAKRIE PIPE INDUSTRIES

BPI Raih Penghargaan CSR dari Pemerintah Daerah Jawa Barat
BPI Received a CSR Award from West Java Regional Government

PT Bakrie Pipe Industries (BPI) berhasil meraih penghargaan Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) dari Pemerintah Daerah Provinsi Jawa Barat atas kontribusi sosial BPI yang telah berjalan selama 9 tahun (2015-2023). Penghargaan ini ditandatangani oleh Pj. Gubernur Jawa Barat Bey Muchmudin, dan diterima oleh Head of HR &amp; Administration BPI Baihaki Muslim.

PT Bakrie Pipe Industries (BPI) received Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) award from the West Java Provincial Government for BPI's social contribution that has been running for 9 years (2015-2023). This award was signed by Acting Governor of West Java Bey Muchmudin, and received by BPI Head of HR &amp; Administration Baihaki Muslim.
