# HUBUNGAN KERJA DEWAN KOMISARIS DAN DIREKSI

Dewan Komisaris bertanggung jawab untuk mengawasi pengelolaan Perseroan yang dilakukan oleh Direksi dan jajarannya. Untuk itu Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi wajib memelihara hubungan kerja yang harmonis dan sinergis sesuai dengan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan. Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi diwajibkan oleh Anggaran Dasar Perseroan untuk menyelenggarakan rapat gabungan sedikitnya 3 (tiga) kali dalam setahun. # WORKING RELATIONS OF THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS AND DIRECTORS

The Board of Commissioners has the responsibility to supervise the Company's management by the Board of Directors and its management. To that end, the Board of Commissioners and Directors shall maintain harmonious and synergic working relations, in accordance with the Company's Articles of Association. The Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors are stipulated by the Company's Articles of Association to hold a joint meeting at least 3 (three) times per year.
