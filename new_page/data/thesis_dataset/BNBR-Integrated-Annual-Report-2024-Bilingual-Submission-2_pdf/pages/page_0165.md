# BAKRIE INDO INFRASTRUCTURE

Rencana Pembangunan Jangka Menengah Nasional (RPJMN) 2025 – 2029 yang telah ditetapkan Pemerintah Republik Indonesia merupakan tahap awal penguatan fondasi transformasi menuju pencapaian sasaran Indonesia Emas 2045. RPJMN menetapkan 8 (delapan) Prioritas Nasional dengan swasembada energi dan kesinambungan pengembangan infrastruktur sebagai bagian dari prioritas tersebut.

Dalam aspek swasembada energi, Pemerintah telah mencanangkan penguatan implementasi transisi energi berkeadilan sebagai salah satu Kegiatan Prioritas yang diampu oleh Kementerian Energi dan Sumber Daya Mineral (ESDM). Kegiatan Prioritas ini memiliki sasaran dan target yang akan dicapai selama kurun waktu 2025 – 2029 berupa:

The National Medium-Term Development Plan (RPJMN) 2025-2029, which has been established by the Government of the Republic of Indonesia, is the initial stage of strengthening the foundation for the transformation towards achieving the Golden Indonesia 2045 vision. The RPJMN sets 8 (eight) National Priorities with energy self-sufficiency and sustainable infrastructure development as part of these priorities.

In the aspect of energy self-sufficiency, the Government has launched strengthening the equitable energy transition implementation as one of the Priority Activities carried out by the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources (ESDM). This Priority Activity has targets and objectives that will be achieved during the period 2025 - 2029 which are as follows:
