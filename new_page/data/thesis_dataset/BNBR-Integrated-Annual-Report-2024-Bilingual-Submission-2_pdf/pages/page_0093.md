# PENGAWASAN ATAS IMPLEMENTASI STRATEGI

Dalam mengawasi pelaksanaan strategi dan pengelolaan Perusahaan, Dewan Komisaris menggunakan mekanisme berupa rapat gabungan Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi. Dewan Komisaris meminta penjelasan dari manajemen mengenai hal-hal tertentu yang memerlukan perhatian dan tindak lanjut, untuk memastikan kepatuhan terhadap strategi, kebijakan, dan program yang telah ditetapkan. Selain itu, Dewan Komisaris mendapat dukungan dari berbagai Komite, antara lain Komite Audit, Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi, Komite Tata Kelola Perusahaan, serta Komite Investasi dan Manajemen Risiko. Komite-komite ini membantu mengawasi bidang-bidang penting seperti penerapan tata kelola perusahaan, pengendalian internal dan efektivitas manajemen risiko, kepatuhan dan keandalan pelaporan keuangan, serta pemenuhan persyaratan sumber daya manusia.

Dalam melakukan pengawasan terhadap pengelolaan Perseroan oleh Direksi, Dewan Komisaris memberikan arahan dan masukan untuk membantu merumuskan dan melaksanakan strategi Perseroan dengan mempertimbangkan dinamika perekonomian, bisnis, dan industri. Pada tahun 2024, Dewan Komisaris mengarahkan Direksi untuk fokus pada restrukturisasi utang dan penyehatan neraca keuangan Perseroan, menyempurnakan strategi untuk meningkatkan penjualan barang dan jasa, mengejar peluang produk dan bisnis baru, serta waspada dalam memanfaatkan setiap peluang bisnis dengan tetap mengoptimalkan praktik manajemen risiko. Selain itu, mereka juga menekankan pentingnya peningkatan pengendalian dan pengawasan internal di seluruh unit bisnis, mengedepankan aspek Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) untuk menjamin keberlanjutan usaha, serta mengintensifkan upaya penagihan piutang usaha dan pemanfaatan aset Perusahaan secara optimal.

Hence, the Board of Commissioners extends gratitude to the Board of Directors for their unwavering dedication in fulfilling their responsibilities, driving production and sales growth through strategic business initiatives, and pioneering sustainable business breakthroughs. With such solid achievements, the Board of Commissioners anticipates that the Company will build upon this foundation for robust business expansion in the future.

# SUPERVISION OF STRATEGY IMPLEMENTATION

In overseeing the implementation of the Company's strategy and management, the Board of Commissioners employs a mechanism involving joint meetings of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors. The Board of Commissioners seek explanations from management on specific matters requiring attention and follow-up, ensuring adherence to the established strategy, policies, and programs. Furthermore, the Board of Commissioners receives support from various Committees, including the Audit Committee, Nomination and Remuneration Committee, Corporate Governance Committee, and the Investment and Risk Management Committee. These Committees assist in overseeing critical areas such as corporate governance implementation, internal control and risk management effectiveness, financial reporting compliance and reliability, and human resource requirements fulfilment.

In supervising the Board of Directors' management of the Company, the Board of Commissioners offers guidance and input to aid in formulating and executing the Company's strategy, considering economic, business, and industry dynamics. In 2024, the Board of Commissioners directed the Board of Directors to focus on restructuring the Company's debt and balance sheet, enhancing strategies to boost goods and services sales, pursuing new product and business opportunities, and being vigilant in seizing every business opportunity while optimizing risk management practices. Additionally, they emphasized the importance of improving internal control and supervision across all business units, prioritizing Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) aspects to ensure business sustainability, and intensifying efforts in trade receivables collection and optimal utilization of Company assets.
