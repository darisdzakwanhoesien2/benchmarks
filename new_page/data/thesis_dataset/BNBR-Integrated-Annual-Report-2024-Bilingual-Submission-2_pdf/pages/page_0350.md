# KUASI REORGANISASI
## QUASI REORGANIZATION

## LATAR BELAKANG KUASI REORGANISASI

PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk ("Perseroan") telah mencapai kinerja keuangan yang positif selama tiga tahun kebelakang (2021-2023). Peningkatan kinerja Perseroan yang didukung oleh pertumbuhan positif dihampir seluruh lini bisnis Perseroan.

Oleh karena kondisi tersebut, Perseroan memiliki inisiatif untuk mengimplementasikan kuasi reorganisasi sesuai dengan ketentuan yang diatur dalam Peraturan Badan Pengawas Pasar Modal dan Lembaga Keuangan ("Bapepam-LK") No. IX.L.1, Lampiran Keputusan Ketua Bapepam-LK No. Kep-718/BL/2012 tentang Kuasi Reorganisasi ("Peraturan IX.L.1").

Dengan penerapan kuasi reorganisasi tersebut, diharapkan dapat merestrukturisasi struktur ekuitas Perseroan dan akan memberikan nilai tambah kepada pemegang saham.

## TUJUAN KUASI REORGANISASI

Adapun tujuan dan manfaat dilaksanakannya Rencana Kuasi Reorganisasi oleh Perseroan adalah sebagai berikut:

1. Perseroan dapat memulai awal yang baru (fresh start) dengan neraca keuangan yang menunjukan saldo laba tanpa dibebani defisit masa lampau;
2. Memperbaiki struktur ekuitas Perseroan dengan mengeliminasi akumulasi rugi (defisit) dengan menggunakan komponen ekuitas lain seperti agio saham, selisih transaksi dengan pihak nonpengendali, dan penurunan modal saham;
3. Dengan kondisi neraca keuangan yang menunjukan nilai sekarang tanpa dibebani defisit masa lampau, Perseroan diharapkan akan lebih mudah memperoleh pendanaan, jika diperlukan, dalam rangka pengembangan usaha;

## QUASI REORGANIZATION BACKGROUND

PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk (the "Company") has achieved positive financial performance over the past three years (2021-2023). The Company's performance improvement is supported by positive growth in almost all of the Company's business lines.

Due to these conditions, the Company has taken the initiative to implement a quasi reorganization in accordance with the provisions stipulated in the Capital Market and Financial Institutions Supervisory Agency ("Bapepam-LK") Regulation No. IX.L.1, Attachment to the Decree of the Chairman of Bapepam-LK No. Kep-718/BL/2012 regarding Quasi Reorganization ("Regulation IX.L.1").

With the implementation of the quasi reorganization, it is expected to restructure the Company's equity structure and will provide added value to shareholders.

## PURPOSE OF QUASI REORGANIZATION

The objectives and benefits of implementing the Quasi Reorganization Plan by the Company are as follows:

1. The Company can start fresh with a balance sheet that shows the balance of profits without being burdened by past deficits;
2. Improve the Company's equity structure by eliminating accumulated losses (deficits) by using other equity components such as paid-in capital in excess of par values, differences in transactions with non-controlling parties, and decreases in share capital;
3. With the condition of the balance sheet that shows the present value without being burdened by past deficits, the Company is expected to more easily obtain funding, if needed, in the context of business development;
