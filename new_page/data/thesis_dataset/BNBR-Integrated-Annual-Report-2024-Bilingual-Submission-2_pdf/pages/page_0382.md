INTEGRATED ANNUAL REPORT 2024

# 1. Kepatuhan terhadap Pemenuhan Perizinan Pengelolaan Lingkungan Hidup

Dalam rangka mendukung pengelolaan lingkungan yang bertanggung jawab, sejumlah unit usaha BNBR, seperti BMI, BCons, BPI, SEAPI, BMC, BP, dan BBI, telah melengkapi dokumen Analisis Mengenai Dampak Lingkungan (AMDAL) serta Upaya Pengelolaan dan Pemantauan Lingkungan Hidup (UKL-PKL) yang menjadi prasyarat penting dalam pengambilan keputusan terkait penyelenggaraan usaha atau kegiatan. Selain itu, untuk menjamin keberlanjutan akses terhadap sumber daya air, unit-unit seperti BPI, SEAPI, BUMM, BMC, BP, dan BBI telah memperoleh Surat Izin Pengusahaan Air Tanah (SIPA) guna memenuhi kebutuhan operasional, dan untuk memastikan keamanan kesehatan masyarakat serta kelestarian lingkungan. Beberapa unit usaha, seperti BMI, BCons, BPI, SEAPI, BA, BUMM, dan BMC, juga telah menerapkan kebijakan pengelolaan limbah bahaya yang ketat. Langkah-langkah tersebut mencerminkan komitmen BNBR dalam mematuhi regulasi lingkungan dan mengoptimalkan pengelolaan sumber daya alam secara berkelanjutan.

# 2. Kepatuhan terhadap Pengendalian Pengelolaan Air

Sebagai upaya melindungi dan mengelola mutu air, unit-unit usaha BNBR berkomitmen untuk mematuhi regulasi dengan memenuhi sejumlah indikator penting, antara lain penerapan sistem manajemen lingkungan untuk mencegah pencemaran air, penyediaan fasilitas pengolahan air limbah, serta pelaksanaan pemantauan dan pengukuran mutu air secara berkala. Unit-unit seperti BMI, BCons, BPI, SEAPI, BA, BUMM, dan BMC telah mengimplementasikan sistem manajemen lingkungan pencegahan pencemaran air dan secara rutin melakukan pemantauan mutu air guna mendukung pengelolaan lingkungan yang berkelanjutan.

# 3. Kepatuhan Terhadap Pengendalian Pengelolaan Udara

Unit usaha BNBR diharapkan memiliki sistem manajemen pencegahan pencemaran udara, fasilitas pengolahan limbah udara, dan melakukan pemantauan mutu udara secara berkala. Misalnya, BPI tidak memiliki fasilitas pengolah limbah udara karena proses galvanisnya hanya menghasilkan penguapan tanpa pencemaran, meskipun uji emisi tetap dilakukan. Demikian pula, SEAPI tidak memerlukan fasilitas tersebut karena limbah udaranya relatif kecil. Sementara itu, BA telah menginstalasi mesin dust collector dan sedang dalam proses instalasi mesin fume extraction, sedangkan BUMM sedang dalam proses instalasi mesin fume extraction. BMC rutin menguji emisi setiap enam bulan, sedangkan berdasarkan regulasi VKTR tidak memerlukan fasilitas pengolah limbah udara di pabriknya.

# 1. Compliance with Environmental Management Permits

To support responsible environmental management, several BNBR business units, such as BMI, BCons, BPI, SEAPI, BMC, BP, and BBI, have completed Environmental Impact Analysis (AMDAL) and Environmental Management and Monitoring Efforts (UKL-PKL) documents, which are essential prerequisites for business-related decision-making. Additionally, to ensure sustainable access to water resources, units like BPI, SEAPI, BUMM, BMC, BP, and BBI have obtained Groundwater Utilization Permits (SIPA) to meet operational needs and ensure public health and environmental sustainability. Several units, including BMI, BCons, BPI, SEAPI, BA, BUMM, and BMC, have also implemented strict hazardous waste management policies. These steps reflect BNBR's commitment to complying with environmental regulations and optimizing sustainable natural resource management.

# 2. Compliance with Water Management Control

To protect and manage water quality, BNBR business units are committed to complying with regulations by meeting key indicators, including implementing environmental management systems to prevent water pollution, providing wastewater treatment facilities, and conducting regular water quality monitoring. Units such as BMI, BCons, BPI, SEAPI, BA, BUMM, and BMC have implemented water pollution prevention systems and regularly monitor water quality to support sustainable environmental management.

# 3. Compliance with Air Management Control

BNBR business units are expected to have air pollution prevention systems, air waste treatment facilities, and conduct regular air quality monitoring. For example, BPI does not require air waste treatment facilities as its galvanizing process only produces vapor without pollution, although emission testing is still conducted. Similarly, SEAPI does not need such facilities due to minimal air waste. Meanwhile, BA has installed dust collector machine and currently in the process of installing fume extraction machines, while BUMM is in the process of installing fume extraction machines. BMC conducts emission tests every six months, while based on regulation VKTR does not require air waste treatment facilities in its factory.
