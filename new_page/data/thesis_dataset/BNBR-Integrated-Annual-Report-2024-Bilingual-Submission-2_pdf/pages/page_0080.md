# BAKRIE &amp; BROTHERS

Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Tahunan dan Luar Biasa BNBR 2024
BNBR Annual and Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders 2024

PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk (BNBR) mengadakan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Tahunan (RUPST) dan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Luar Biasa (RUPSLB) pada 21 Juni 2024. Di dalam RUPST tersebut Perseroan menyampaikan di antaranya kinerja usaha dan kondisi finansial Perseroan yang kemudian disetujui oleh para pemegang saham.

Di dalam RUPSLB Perseroan juga mendapatkan persetujuan untuk menjalankan aksi korporasi kuasi reorganisasi yang memungkinkan Perseroan untuk lebih lincah dalam meraih kesempatan di masa depan.

PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk (BNBR) held an Annual General Meeting of Shareholders (AGMS) and Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders (EGMS) on June 21, 2024. At the AGMS, the Company presented, among other things, business performance and the Company's financial condition which were then approved by the shareholders.

At the EGMS, the Company also received approval to carry out a quasi reorganization corporate action that will allow the Company to be more agile in seizing future opportunities.

# BAKRIE &amp; BROTHERS

Peresmian Jalan Tol Cimanggis Cibitung
Cimanggis Cibitung Toll Road Inauguration

Jalan tol Cimanggis Cibitung yang menghubungkan Depok, Bogor dan Bekasi telah diresmikan oleh Wakil Presiden Republik Indonesia Ma'ruf Amin, dengan disaksikan oleh Direktur Utama / CEO BNBR Anindya Bakrie, Wakil Direktur Utama / Co-CEO BNBR Ardiansyah Bakrie, dan Chief Business Officer BNBR Amri Aswono Putro. Hadir pula perwakilan dari Pemerintah Republik Indonesia, yaitu Menteri PUPR Basuki Hadimuljono, Pj. Gubernur Jawa Barat Bey Triadi Machmudin, Pj. Bupati Bogor Asmawa Tosepu.

Peresmian ini dilakukan di Jembatan Sungai Cileungsi KM 57+400.

The Cimanggis Cibitung toll road connecting Depok, Bogor and Bekasi was inaugurated by Vice President of the Republic of Indonesia Ma'ruf Amin, witnessed by BNBR President Director/CEO Anindya Bakrie, BNBR Vice President Director/Co-CEO Ardiansyah Bakrie, and BNBR Chief Business Officer Amri Aswono Putro. Also present were representatives from the Government of the Republic of Indonesia, namely PUPR Minister Basuki Hadimuljono, Acting Governor of West Java Bey Triadi Machmudin, Acting Bogor Regent Asmawa Tosepu.

The inauguration was held at Cileungsi River Bridge KM 57+400.
