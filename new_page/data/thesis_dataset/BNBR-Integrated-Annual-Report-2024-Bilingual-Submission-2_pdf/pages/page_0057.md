## BRAJA MUKTI CAKRA

On September 29, 2024, Harapan Jaya Subdistrict, Bekasi City gave an award to PT Braja Mukti Cakra for their support and aid in the 2024 Sport and Art Week (Porseni) activity of Harapan Jaya subdistrict.
