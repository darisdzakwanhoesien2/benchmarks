# KETENAGAKERJAAN, KESEHATAN, DAN KESELAMATAN KERJA

LABOR PRACTICES AND OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH &amp; SAFETY

BNBR mengakui bahwa karyawan adalah aset utama dalam mencapai kemajuan dan keberlanjutan usaha. Sebagai wujud tanggung jawab terhadap karyawan, kami telah melaksanakan program Ketenagakerjaan, Kesehatan, dan Keselamatan Kerja (K3) berdasarkan kerangka kerja OHS Management Program. [SEOJK F.21] [GRI 403-1]

Dalam pelaksanaan K3, pedoman kami merujuk pada Company Health and Safety Policy yang disahkan pada 25 Januari 2013. Kebijakan tersebut dirancang untuk menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang aman, sehat, dan bebas dari dampak negatif terhadap lingkungan. Berbagai upaya telah kami lakukan guna mencapai standar kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja yang ideal meliputi: [GRI 403-2]

- Identifikasi dan evaluasi terhadap aspek serta potensi ancaman kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja serta menentukan pengendalian terhadap risiko dan dampaknya dalam tingkat yang dapat diterima.
- Komitmen untuk mematuhi peraturan perundang- undangan dan persyaratan lainnya.
- Pencegahan kecelakaan kerja, cidera, sakit di tempat kerja serta polusi.
- Pengurangan limbah dan konsumsi energi.
- Pencatatan serta sosialisasi kinerja kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja.
- Memastikan pengendalian terhadap kondisi dan perilaku yang tidak aman dan dapat mengakibatkan kecelakaan atau cedera di saat kerja.
- Perbaikan terus menerus dalam penerapan K3L.

# PELATIHAN KESEHATAN DAN KESELAMATAN KERJA

Walaupun pandemi COVID-19 sudah berlalu empat tahun, namun dampaknya masih terasa dalam kebijakan K3L di lingkungan BNBR dan anak usaha. Untuk melindungi karyawan dari risiko kecelakaan dan menjaga kesehatan, selain menerapkan kebijakan OHS BNBR dan mematuhi standar internasional ISO serta OHSAS (Occupational, Health, and Safety Assessment Series), kami terus memperketat protokol kesehatan. Sebagai bagian dari upaya tersebut, BNBR secara rutin mengadakan pelatihan dan sosialisasi terkait kriteria ISO. [SEOJK F.21] [GRI 403-1]

BNBR recognizes that employees are the primary asset in achieving business progress and sustainability. As part of our responsibility toward employees, we have implemented Labor, Health, and Occupational Safety (OHS) programs based on the OHS Management Program framework. [SEOJK F.21] [GRI 403-1]

In implementing OHS, our guidelines refer to the Company Health and Safety Policy ratified on January 25, 2013. This policy is designed to create a safe, healthy, and environmentally friendly workplace. Various efforts have been made to achieve ideal occupational health and safety standards, including: [GRI 403-2]

- Identification and evaluation of occupational health and safety aspects and potential threats, as well as determining controls for risks and their impacts at acceptable levels.
- Commitment to comply with applicable laws and other requirements.
- Prevention of workplace accidents, injuries, illnesses, and pollution.
- Reduction of waste and energy consumption.
- Recording and socialization of occupational health and safety performance.
- Ensuring control over unsafe conditions and behaviors that could lead to accidents or injuries at work.
- Continuous improvement in the implementation of K3L (Occupational Health, Safety, and Environment).

# OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH AND SAFETY TRAINING

Although the COVID-19 pandemic has passed four years ago, its impact is still felt in the K3L policies within BNBR and its subsidiaries. To protect employees from accident risks and maintain their health, in addition to implementing BNBR's OHS policy and adhering to ISO and OHSAS (Occupational Health and Safety Assessment Series) standards, we continue to tighten health protocols. As part of these efforts, BNBR regularly conducts training and socialization related to ISO criteria. [SEOJK F.21] [GRI 403-1]
