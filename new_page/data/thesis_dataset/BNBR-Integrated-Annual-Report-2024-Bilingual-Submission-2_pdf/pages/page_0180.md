# REWARD MANAGEMENT [SEOJK F.20][GRI 202-1]

Driven by the spirit of fostering a strong work ethic and performance-oriented culture, BNBR has implemented a remuneration scheme based on the principles of *internal fairness* and *external competitiveness*, encouraging employees to continuously improve productivity.

The Company applies a *reward &amp; punishment* policy as part of its collective commitment to achieving agreed-upon objectives.

High-performing employees are rewarded, while those who do not meet targets are subject to proportional sanctions in accordance with applicable regulations.

As part of its compliance with labor regulations, the Company’s *reward management policy* adheres to all applicable laws and consistently meets the regional minimum wage standards in all operational areas. The average ratio of entry-level employee wages to the local minimum wage is 1.02:1 for both female and male employees.

## HUMAN CAPITAL MANAGEMENT POLICIES [SEOJK F.18] [SEOJK F.19]

Sebagai perusahaan dengan fokus kegiatan di bidang manufaktur dan infrastruktur, BNBR terus berupaya untuk menyempurnakan Kebijakan dan Prosedur SDM (KSDM) Perseroan. Perseroan telah menata kembali KSDM Perseroan yang mengatur tentang kebijakan terkait hal-hal berikut:

- Penlaku Bisnis
- Organisasi
- Rekrutmen dan Seleksi

- Mutasi antar perusahaan dalam grup Bakrie

- Pelatihan dan Pengembangan
- Kompensasi dan Tunjangan

- Business Conduct
- Organization

- Inter-company in Bakrie group employee transfer
- Performance Evaluation System
- Training and Development
- Compensation and Allowances
