# BISNIS YANG MENDORONG PEMBANGUNAN INDONESIA

Sebagai konglomerat nasional terkemuka, PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk (BNBR) terus memajukan bisnisnya dengan komitmen yang mengakar pada pembangunan Indonesia. Sejak didirikan pada tahun 1942 sebagai pedagang produk pertanian, mendiang Achmad Bakrie memiliki visi untuk berperan lebih luas dalam pembangunan bangsa melalui industrialisasi. Visi ini terwujud pada tahun 1959 dengan pendirian PT Bakrie Pipe Industries, yang menandai masuknya BNBR ke dalam industri baja. Selama beberapa dekade berikutnya, BNBR berekspansi ke bidang konstruksi baja, industri logam, bahan bangunan, dan komponen otomotif-memantapkan posisinya dalam pertumbuhan industri nasional.

Sejalan dengan agenda Net Zero Emissions (NZE) 2060, BNBR menegaskan kembali komitmen keberlanjutannya dengan mendirikan PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk. Awalnya berfokus

beyond economic returns. Its initiatives are grounded in a deep sense of responsibility—to promote not only financial strength, but also long-term value that serves both humanity and the environment.

In response to this challenge, diverse sectors are being explored, particularly in alignment with Indonesia's ambition to implement decarbonization and attain the Net Zero Emission (NZE) target by 2060. A notable initiative is the founding of PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk (VKTR), which seeks to expedite the realization of sustainable transportation by electrifying transportation in Indonesia. VKTR's objectives are fortified by the extensive expertise of its subsidiary, PT Bakrie Autoparts, which has a robust background in manufacturing within the automotive segment, a development that transpired at the conclusion of 2022.

Bakrie Power has made significant strides in the realm of renewable energy by inaugurating numerous solar power plants under PT Helio Synar Energi (Helio). At the same time, in the building materials sector, the Company also made the transition towards sustainable practices through the establishment of PT Modula Sustainability Indonesia (Modula) which is a provider of cost-effective and environmentally friendly 3d printed houses. This is one of the alternative solutions to address the housing shortage in Indonesia.

# BUSINESSES THAT FUELS INDONESIA'S DEVELOPMENT

As a leading national conglomerate, PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk (BNBR) continues to advance its business with a deep-rooted commitment to Indonesia's development. Since its beginnings in 1942 as a trader of agricultural products, the late Achmad Bakrie envisioned a broader role in nation-building through industrialization. This vision took shape in 1959 with the establishment of PT Bakrie Pipe Industries, marking BNBR's entry into steel manufacturing. Over the following decades, BNBR expanded into steel construction, metalworks, building materials, and automotive components—solidifying its place in the nation's industrial growth.

In line with Indonesia's Net Zero Emissions (NZE) 2060 agenda, BNBR reaffirmed its sustainability commitment by founding PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk. Initially focused on distributing vehicle and heavy equipment parts, VKTR transitioned into electric mobility in 2022, supported by its acquisition of PT Bakrie Autoparts and its long-standing expertise in automotive manufacturing.
