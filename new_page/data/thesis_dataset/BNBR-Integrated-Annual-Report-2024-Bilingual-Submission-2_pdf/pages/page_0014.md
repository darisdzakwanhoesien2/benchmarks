## 1962

- Pioneered Indonesia's steel manufacturing industry.

## 1989

- Pendirian PT Bakrie Tasanjaya yang kemudian berganti nama menjadi PT Bakrie Autoparts (BA).
- IPO PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk (BNBR).
- Expanding the company's business field to steel structure construction, metal fabrication and other infrastructure projects.
- Establishment of PT Bakrie Building Industries.
- Establishment of PT Bakrie Tasanjaya which later changed its name to PT Bakrie Autoparts (BA).
- IPO of PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers ("BNBR").

## 1990
## 1996

- Obtaining a fixed wireless service operating license.
