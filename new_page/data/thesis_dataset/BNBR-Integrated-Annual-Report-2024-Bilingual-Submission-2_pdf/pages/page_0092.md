Selain itu, Perseroan secara proaktif menjajaki usaha bisnis yang berkelanjutan dengan menyelaraskan inovasi teknologi dengan tanggung jawab terhadap lingkungan. Inisiatif utama meliputi distribusi kendaraan listrik, seperti bus listrik untuk TransJakarta, serta truk listrik dan forklift listrik untuk penggunaan di sektor swasta.

Melalui unit usahanya, PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk (VKTR), Perseroan juga melanjutkan komitmennya dalam mendukung pembangunan Indonesia dengan langkah industrialisasi yang dilakukan melalui penyelesaian pembangunan fasilitas perakitan kendaraan listrik komersial berbasis Completely Knocked Down (CKD) pertama di Indonesia yang berlokasi di Magelang, Jawa Tengah pada awal tahun 2025. Komitmen tinggi

The excellent performance in the aspect of improving the Company's balance sheet posture was also complemented by the achievements of the Company's business units, thus at the end of 2024 the Company managed to record a positive performance.

In fiscal year 2024, the Company recorded revenue of Rp 3.87 trillion, an increase of 2.9% compared to 2023. The good performance resulted in a net profit of Rp 336.05 billion, an increase of 27.07% compared to net profit in 2023. The increase in net profit income was also supported by the Company's other income related to debt settlement and deduction of financial charges. Such achievements underscore the Company's adeptness in formulating and executing business strategies to swiftly address prevailing challenges and exploit emerging business prospects. The Company has even succeeded in maintaining positive performance for three consecutive years after the pandemic. On behalf of the Board of Commissioners, I extend appreciation to the Board of Directors for their steadfast performance in 2024.

The Board of Commissioners extends its appreciation to the Board of Directors for their continued dedication to driving innovation that strengthens the Company's business capabilities and competitiveness in a dynamic environment. This commitment is reflected in the Company's strategic efforts to capitalize on opportunities in automotive market and infrastructure development, as seen in the improved demand for steel related products. Additionally, the Company is proactively exploring sustainable business ventures by aligning technological innovation with environmental responsibility. Key initiatives include the distribution of electric vehicles, such as electric buses for TransJakarta, as well as EV trucks and EV forklifts for private sector use.

Through its business unit, PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk (VKTR), the Company also continues its commitment in supporting Indonesia's development with industrialization steps taken through the completion of the construction of Indonesia's first Completely Knocked Down (CKD) based commercial electric vehicle assembly facility located in Magelang, Central Java in early 2025. This high commitment to industrialization makes VKTR the first company in Indonesia to successfully meet the 40% Local Content Level (TKDN) for a 12-meter electric bus.
