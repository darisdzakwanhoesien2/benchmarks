# PENILAIAN KINERJA KARYAWAN

BNBR menerapkan sistem penilaian kinerja berbasis Key Performance Indicators (KPI) guna mengukur kinerja operasional perusahaan dan kinerja individu karyawan secara objektif, adil, dan terukur. Melalui KPI, Perusahaan juga menentukan jenjang karir dan jabatan bagi seluruh karyawan. Sebagai bagian dari mekanisme evaluasi, BNBR mengadopsi sistem Bakrie Performance Contract (BPC) yang dirancang untuk menilai kinerja secara sistematis, transparan, adil, dan sesuai dengan deskripsi serta objektif pekerjaan. Sistem BPC ini tidak hanya membantu karyawan mencapai target kerja yang telah ditetapkan, tetapi juga mendukung pengembangan karir mereka melalui penilaian dengan bobot 80% untuk KPI dan 20% untuk kompetensi. Hingga akhir tahun 2024, seluruh karyawan telah mengikuti proses penilaian kinerja dan pengembangan karir, menandakan penerapan sistem evaluasi yang komprehensif di seluruh unit BNBR. [GRI 404-3]

To maintain business integrity and ethics, BNBR firmly rejects all forms of forced labor and child labor. We enforce a minimum working age policy of 18 years, based on Law No. 13 of 2003 on Labor, and demand the same commitment from all business partners, including in the supply chain. To ensure compliance with these standards, BNBR and its business partners have strengthened monitoring and control systems. Throughout 2024, no cases of underage labor or forced labor were detected in BNBR's supply chain or among our partners. [SEOJK F.19] [GRI 408-1][GRI 409-1]

# EMPLOYEE PERFORMANCE EVALUATION

BNBR implements a Key Performance Indicator (KPI)-based performance evaluation system to objectively, fairly, and measurably assess both company operational performance and individual employee performance. Through KPIs, the company also determines career progression and positions for all employees. As part of the evaluation mechanism, BNBR adopts the Bakrie Performance Contract (BPC) system, designed to assess performance systematically, transparently, fairly, and in line with job descriptions and objectives. The BPC system not only helps employees achieve set work targets but also supports their career development through evaluations weighted 80% on KPIs and 20% on competencies. By the end of 2024, all employees had undergone performance evaluation and career development processes, indicating the comprehensive implementation of the evaluation system across all BNBR units. [GRI 404-3]
