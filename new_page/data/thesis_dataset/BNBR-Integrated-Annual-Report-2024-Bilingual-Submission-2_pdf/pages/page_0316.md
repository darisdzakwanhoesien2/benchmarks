RISIKO DI UNIT USAHA MANUFAKTUR
RISKS AT MANUFACTURING-BASED BUSINESS UNITS

|  NO | KATEGORI CATEGORY | RISIKO RISKS | MITIGASI MITIGATIONS  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  1. | Risiko Strategis
Strategic Risk | Penurunan jumlah pesanan dari pelanggan.
Decrease in customer order volume. | Menyusun dan melaksanakan rencana cadangan yang meliputi penambahan produk potensial dan memperluas basis pelanggan.
Formulate and execute a strategic contingency plan inclusive of diversifying potential products and expanding the customer base.  |
|  2. | Risiko Strategis
Strategic Risk | Fluktuasi harga dan kelangkaan bahan baku.
Volatility in the price and availability of raw materials. | 1. Melakukan pembelian bahan baku untuk buffer stock di saat harga terbilang rendah.
2. Menyampaikan perkiraan kebutuhan bahan baku kepada vendor guna menjaga kepastian harga dan stok.
1. Procure raw materials for buffer inventory at opportune market valuations.
2. Distribute raw material demand forecasts to vendors to safeguard price and supply continuity.  |
|  3 | Risiko
Operasional
Operational Risk | Peningkatan rejection rate atas output produksi.
Escalation in production output rejection rates. | 1. Menerapkan draft H-7 jadwal servis bulanan.
2. Mempertahankan level terendah atas critical utility stock parts.
3. Melakukan peningkatan keahlian sumber daya manusia.
4. Segera berkoordinasi dengan supplier terkait material reject yang melebihi target;
1. Implement a draft H-7 monthly service schedule;
2. Sustain minimal levels of critical utility stock parts;
3. Elevate workforce competencies;
4. Engage in immediate coordination with suppliers on exceeding target rejection rates.  |
|  4. | Risiko
Operasional
Operational Risk | Keterlambatan pengantaran produk kepada pelanggan.
Delays in product delivery to clients. | 1. Mempercepat jadwal produksi dan menyederhanakan proses logistik.
2. Melakukan kontrol yang ketat terhadap persediaan barang jadi.
3. Membentuk armada pengiriman cadangan untuk meningkatkan keandalan distribusi.
1. Expedite production timelines and streamline logistical processes.
2. Exercise stringent control over the inventory of finished goods.
3. Establish a reserve delivery fleet for enhanced distribution reliability.  |
|  5. | Risiko Hukum
Legal Risk | Pelanggaran regulasi dan/atau pencemaran lingkungan hidup.
Breach of environmental compliance | 1. Pembuatan SOP pengelolaan limbah.
2. Pelaporan pemanfaatan limbah B3.
3. Melakukan pemutakhiran dokumen lingkungan.
4. Perbaikan infrastruktur pengelolaan limbah dan peralatan pendukung lainnya.
1. Development of Waste Management Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
2. Reporting on the utilization of hazardous and toxic waste (B3 Waste).
3. Updating environmental documentation.
4. Improvement of waste management infrastructure and other supporting equipment.  |
