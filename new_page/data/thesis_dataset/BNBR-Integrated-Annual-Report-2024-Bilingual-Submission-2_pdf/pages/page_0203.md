# PETA JALAN IMPLEMENTASI GCG

GCG IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP

## 2013-2014

- Piagam Audit.
- Pengkajian Committee Charter.
- Penyempurnaan Kebijakan &amp; Prosedur.
- Menjadikan pengelolaan risiko sebagai budaya yang melekat hingga ke Unit Usaha.
- Pengkajian Roadmap GCG disesuaikan dengan Roadmap GCG OJK.
- Pemantapan kerangka kerja Corporate Internal Audit, Manajemen Risiko, Human Resources Management.
- Mengangkat Komisaris Independen dan Direktur Independen.
- Pengkajian dan penandatanganan kembali Kode Etik, Kebijakan Perilaku Bisnis, dan Pakta Integritas.

- Audit Charter.
- Review Committee Charter.
- Improvement of Policies &amp; Procedures.
- Make risk management a culture that extends to the Business Unit.
- Review of GCG Roadmap with reference to OJK GCG Roadmap.
- Strengthening the framework of Corporate Internal Audit, Risk Management, Human Resources Management.
- Appointed Independent Commissioner and Independent Director.
- Review and re-signing of Code of Ethics, Code of Conducts, and Integrity Pact.

## 2015-2016

- Pemantapan kerangka kerja Corporate Internal Audit, Manajemen Risiko, Human Resources Management.
- Pengkajian Board Manual dan Corporate Governance Manual.
- Menjadikan pengelolaan risiko sebagai budaya yang melekat hingga ke Unit Usaha.
- Tetap berkomitmen untuk melakukan asesmen GCG secara independen hingga ke Unit Usaha yang terkonsolidasi.
- Kebijakan &amp; Prosedur Antifraud dan Penerimaan Gratifikasi.
- Penerapan ASEAN CG Scorecard untuk asesmen GCG dan Laporan Tahunan.

- Strengthening the framework of Corporate Internal Audit, Risk Management, Human Resources Management.
- Review of Board Manual and Corporate Governance Manual.
- Making risk management a culture that extends to the Business Units.
- Remain committed to carrying out independent GCG assessments down to the consolidated Business Units.
- Policy &amp; Procedure concerning Antifraud and Gratuities Receiving.
- Implementation of ASEAN CG Scorecard for GCG and Annual Report assessment.
