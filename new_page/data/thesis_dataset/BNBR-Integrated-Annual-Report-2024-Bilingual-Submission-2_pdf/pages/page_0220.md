## PERHITUNGAN SUARA PADA RAPAT UMUM PEMEGANG SAHAM LUAR BIASA

Pada pelaksanaan RUPS Luar Biasa Perseroan di tanggal 28 November 2024, Perseroan telah menunjuk pihak-pihak independen untuk melakukan perhitungan suara dan/atau melakukan validasi dalam rapat dari Biro Administrasi Efek PT. Electronic Data Interchange Indonesia (EDII) dan Notaris Humberg Lie, SH, SE, Mkn.

The Company has informed the Plan Notice to the Financial Services Authority, through Corporate Secretary's Letter No. 088/BNBR/CS-OJK/XI/24 dated November 6, 2024, and also announced the Invitation of Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders on November 6, 2024, through Company website, Indonesia Stock Exchange website and Indonesia Central Securities Depository website.

Extraordinary GMS on November 28, 2024 was attended by shareholders that represent 141,122,123,515 shares or 88.17% from the total shareholders. Thereby the meeting was declared a quorum and was in accordance with the provisions of Article 41 paragraph 1 letter a POJK No.15/2020 and Article 13 paragraph 2.1.a of the Company's Articles of Association, that the meeting is valid if attended / represented by more than ½ (one-half) of the total number of shares with valid voting rights issued by the Company.

## COUNTING OF VOTES AT THE EXTRAORDINARY GENERAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS

At the Company's Extraordinary GMS on November 28, 2024, the Company appointed independent parties to count the votes and/or carry out validation at the meeting from the Securities Administration Bureau of PT. Electronic Data Interchange Indonesia (EDII) and Notary Humberg Lie, SH, SE, Mkn.
