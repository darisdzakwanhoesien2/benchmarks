# KARTINI SALLY

DIREKTUR &amp; CHIEF INVESTMENT OFFICER
DIRECTOR &amp; CHIEF INVESTMENT OFFICER

Kartini Sally, Warga Negara Indonesia, lahir di Jakarta pada tahun 1965. Beliau memperoleh gelar Sarjana dari Universitas Indonesia, Jakarta, dan gelar Pasca Sarjana dari PPM School of Management, Jakarta. Beliau juga menjalani berbagai program pendidikan executive leadership, seperti ASEAN Leadership Program di Cambridge University - Inggris (2017), Executive Leadership Program di London Business School – Inggris (2015), Executive Program di Darden Business School, University of Virginia – Amerika Serikat (2014), Executive Risk Management Program, INSEAD, Fountainebleau – Prancis (2009), dan Executive Program di Kellog Business School, University of Northwestern Chicago – Amerika Serikat (2008).

Beliau menjabat sebagai Direktur dan Chief Investment Officer sejak Juli 2022. Saat ini beliau juga menjabat sebagai Anggota Komite Audit dan Risk Management PT Bank HSBC Indonesia. Sebelumnya beliau menjabat sebagai Direktur Commercial Banking PT Bank Mandiri (Persero) Tbk, kemudian menjabat sebagai Direktur Hubungan Kelembagaan PT Bank Mandiri (Persero) Tbk dan sekaligus bertugas sebagai Direktur Pembina yang mensupervisi PT Bank Syariah Mandiri serta DPLK (Dana Pensiun Lembaga Keuangan) Bank Mandiri hingga akhir Maret 2018. Sebelum bergabung dengan BNBR, beliau memiliki pengalaman perbankan dan investasi di berbagai institusi.

Kartini Sally, an Indonesian nationality, was born in Jakarta in 1965. She earned a Bachelor degree University of Indonesia, Jakarta, and Post Graduate degree from PPM School of Management, Jakarta. She also underwent various executive leadership education programs, such as the ASEAN Leadership Program at Cambridge University - UK (2017), Executive Leadership Program at London Business School - UK (2015), Executive Program at Darden Business School, University of Virginia - USA (2014), Executive Risk Management Program, INSEAD, Fountainebleau - France (2009), and Executive Program at Kellog Business School, University of Northwestern Chicago - United States (2008).

She holds the position of Director and Chief Investment Officer since July 2022. She currently also holds the position of Member of Audit and Risk Management Committee of PT Bank HSBC Indonesia. Previously she served as Director of Commercial Banking Director of PT Bank Mandiri (Persero) Tbk, she then served as Director of Institutional Relation of PT Bank Mandiri (Persero) Tbk and at the same time served as Director of Trustees who supervised PT Bank Syariah Mandiri and DPLK (Financial Institution Pension Fund) Bank Mandiri until the end of March 2018. Prior to joining Bakrie Group, she had banking and investment experience in various institutions.
