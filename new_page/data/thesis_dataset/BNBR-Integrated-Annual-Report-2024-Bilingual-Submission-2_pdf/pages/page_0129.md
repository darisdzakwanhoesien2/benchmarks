# R.A. SRI DHARMAYANTI

DIRECTOR &amp; CHIEF LEGAL OFFICER

Sri Dharmayanti, Warga Negara Indonesia, lahir di Jakarta pada tahun 1962 dan memperoleh gelar Magister Hukum dari Universitas Indonesia. Beliau memegang posisi Direktur Perseroan sejak tahun 2008. Saat ini, beliau juga menjabat sebagai Direktur PT Bumi Resources Tbk ("BUMI"), Komisaris PT Arutmin Indonesia, dan Komisaris PT Kaltim Prima Coal. Sebelumnya, beliau menjabat sebagai Chief Legal Officer di BUMI.

Beliau juga ikut serta sebagai Badan Pengurus Asosiasi Pertambangan Indonesia (API-IMA), Dewan Penasihat Asosiasi Pertambangan Batubara Indonesia (APBI-ICMA), Dewan Pembina Indonesian Corporate Secretary Association (ICSA), anggota Tim Pengkajian Asosiasi Emiten Indonesia (AEI), dan anggota Perhimpunan Ahli Pertambangan Indonesia (PERHAPI).

Sri Dharmayanti, an Indonesian nationality, was born in Jakarta in 1962 and obtained her Master degree in Law from University of Indonesia. She holds the position of Director since 2008. Currently, she also serves as Director of PT Bumi Resources Tbk ("BUMI"), Commissioner of PT Arutmin Indonesia, and Commissioner of PT Kaltim Prima Coal. Previously she held the position of Chief Legal Officer at BUMI.

She also participates on the Board of the Indonesian Mining Association (API-IMA), Advisory Board of the Indonesian Coal Mining Association (APBI-ICMA), Advisory Board of the Indonesian Corporate Secretary Association (ICSA), as a member of the Assessment Team of Indonesia Public Listed Companies Association (AEI), and as a member of Association of Indonesian Mining Professional (PERHAPI).
