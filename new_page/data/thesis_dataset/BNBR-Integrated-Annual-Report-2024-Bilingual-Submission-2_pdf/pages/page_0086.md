# VKTR TEKNOLOGI MOBILITAS

VKTR Raih Penghargaan dari Ajang "Indonesia Best Public Company Awards 2024"

VKTR Received an Award from "Indonesia Best Public Company Awards 2024" Event

PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk (VKTR) menerima piagam penghargaan "The Fastest Growing New Public Company 2024 in Automotive Component Industry for The Capacious Market Capitalization for Sustainability Business" untuk sektor "Indonesia Best Public Company Award 2024" yang diadakan oleh Warta Ekonomi Research and Consulting.

PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk (VKTR) received a certificate of appreciation as "The Fastest Growing New Public Company 2024 in Automotive Component Industry for The Capacious Market Capitalization for Sustainability Business" in the Sustainability Business sector carried out by Warta Ekonomi Research and Consulting.

# VKTR TEKNOLOGI MOBILITAS

Pengiriman Pertama EV Bus Kolaborasi
VKTR, Laksana, Sinarjaya, dan TransJakarta
First Delivery of EV Bus Collaboration between
VKTR, Laksana, Sinarjaya, and TransJakarta

PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk (VKTR), PT Laksana Bus Manufaktur (Laksana), dan Sinarjaya merayakan pencapaian penting melalui acara pengiriman pertama (First Delivery), yang menandai pengiriman perdana bus listrik (EV bus) untuk PT Transportasi Jakarta (TransJakarta) sebagai konsumen utama.

Acara yang diadakan di garasi Laksana, Ungaran, ini merupakan hasil dari kemitraan strategis yang mendukung komitmen bersama menuju transportasi ramah lingkungan dan berkelanjutan di Indonesia.

PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk (VKTR), PT Laksana Bus Manufaktur (Laksana), and Sinarjaya celebrated an important milestone with the First Delivery event, which marked the first delivery of an EV bus to PT Transportasi Jakarta (TransJakarta) as the main customer.

The event, held at Laksana's Ungaran garage, was the result of a strategic partnership that supports a shared commitment towards environmentally friendly and sustainable transportation in Indonesia.
