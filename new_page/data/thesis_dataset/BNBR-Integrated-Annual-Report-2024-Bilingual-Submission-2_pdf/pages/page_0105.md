# EKONOMI GLOBAL 2024 – MENAVIGASI KETAHANAN DI TENGAH KETIDAKPASTIAN DAN TRANSFORMASI

Pada tahun 2024, dunia terus menghadapi dampak lanjutan dari pandemi COVID-19—luka yang, meskipun mulai pulih, masih memengaruhi perilaku ekonomi—serta serangkaian tantangan baru yang muncul. Lanskap ekonomi ditandai oleh realignment geopolitik yang semakin berkembang, dengan meningkatnya ketegangan antara kekuatan besar yang semakin memperumit arus perdagangan dan investasi global. Selain itu, kemajuan pesat dalam teknologi digital serta dorongan mendesak untuk praktik yang berketahanan terhadap perubahan iklim menghadirkan peluang sekaligus tantangan. # 2024 GLOBAL ECONOMY: NAVIGATING RESILIENCE AMID UNCERTAINTY AND TRANSFORMATION

In 2024, the world continued to navigate the aftereffects of the COVID-19 pandemic—a scar that, while healing, still influenced economic behavior—and encountered a new set of challenges along the way. The landscape was marked by renewed geopolitical realignments, as evolving tensions among major powers further complicated international trade and investment flows. On top of this, rapid advancements in digital technologies and the urgent drive for climate-resilient practices added both opportunities and hurdles. Persistent supply chain vulnerabilities, coupled with sporadic conflicts and trade friction, underscored a complex environment where old challenges merged with new policy imperatives.

Overall, global GDP growth stabilized at 3.2% in 2024¹, reflecting a measured rebound amid pervasive uncertainty. Advanced economies continued to struggle with headwinds—including lingering energy market volatility and cautious policy adjustments—resulting in marginal gains.
