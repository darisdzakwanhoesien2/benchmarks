# PROSEDUR DAN DASAR HUKUM PENGANGKATAN KETUA AUDIT INTERNAL

Divisi Internal Audit PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk dipimpin oleh seorang Kepala Unit Audit Internal yang diangkat dan diberhentikan oleh Direktur Utama &amp; CEO atas persetujuan Dewan Komisaris serta bertanggung jawab kepada Direktur Utama &amp; CEO. Dasar hukum pengangkatan kepala unit audit internal berdasarkan surat keputusan Direktur Utama Perseroan Nomor 003/SK/BOD-BGU/VII/2017 tanggal 10 Juli 2017.

# PROCEDURE AND LEGAL BASIS FOR APPOINTMENT OF INTERNAL AUDIT HEAD

PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk’s Internal Audit Division is led by the Head of Internal Audit appointed and terminated by the President Director &amp; CEO following the approval of the Board of Commissioners, and is responsible to the President Director &amp; CEO. The legal basis for the appointment of the head of the internal audit unit is based on the Decree of the President Director of the Company Number 003/SK/BOD-BGU/VII/2017 dated July 10, 2017.
