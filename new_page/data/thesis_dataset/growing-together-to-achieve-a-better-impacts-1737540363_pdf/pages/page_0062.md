Waktu Kegiatan: Activity Time:

Juni | June 2023

Nama Kegiatan: Event:

Literasi Digital Pelatihan Koding dan Robotika untuk sekolah dasar di Surabaya Digital Literacy Coding and Robotics Training for Elementary Schools in Surabaya

Uraian Kegiatan: Activity Description: Guna meningkatkan literasi digital sekolah dasar di Surabaya, Wismilak melakukan program literasi melalui pelatihan koding dan robotika. Wismilak memberikan paket pelatihan kepada sekolah dasar di Surabaya dan program lanjutan beasiswa ke murid yang berprestasi. Program ini diberikan agar mereka bisa memiliki daya saing di masa depan. Setiap tahunnya Wismilak bekerja sama dengan Koding Akademi yang merupakan alumni Program kewirausahaan : Wismilak Diplomat Success Challenge.

To enhance digital literacy in elementary schools in Surabaya, Wismilak has implemented a literacy program through coding and robotics training. Wismilak provides training packages to elementary schools in Surabaya and offers advanced scholarship programs to outstanding students. This initiative aims to equip students with competitive skills for the future. Annually, Wismilak collaborates with Koding Akademi, an alumni of the Wismilak Diplomat Success Challenge entrepreneurship program.
