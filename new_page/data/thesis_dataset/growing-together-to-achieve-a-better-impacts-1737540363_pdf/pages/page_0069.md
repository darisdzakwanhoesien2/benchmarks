# Struktur Tata Kelola Keberlanjutan [E.1][2-9][2-11][2-12][2-13][2-14]

Sustainability Governance Structure

![img-108.jpeg](img-108.jpeg)

Tata kelola keberlanjutan Wismilak merujuk pada tata kelola perusahaan yang baik (GCG). Struktur organisasi tata kelola sesuai Undang-Undang (UU) No. 40 Tahun 2007 Tentang Perseroan Terbatas, terdiri atas Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS) sebagai organ tata kelola tertinggi, Direksi sebagai pengurus Perseroan, dan Dewan Komisaris sebagai pengawas yang dibantu oleh komite. Nominasi dan pengangkatan Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi menjadi kewenangan Pemegang Saham yang disahkan pada saat RUPS. Perseroan meyakini tidak terdapat benturan kepentingan di antara Direksi, Dewan Komisaris maupun Pemegang Saham, yang dapat mempengaruhi setiap keputusan yang dibuat. Detail mengenai RUPS, Direksi, Dewan Komisaris, beserta fungsi-fungs di bawahnya, proses nominasi dan seleksi, serta rasio remunerasi dan evaluasi kinerja Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris terdapat di dalam Laporan Tahunan Wismilak 2023. [2-9][2-10][2-15][2-18] [2-19][2-21]

Wismilak's sustainability governance refers to good corporate governance (GCG). The governance organizational structure is in accordance with Law No. 40 of 2007 on Limited Liability Companies, consisting of the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) as the highest governance body, the Board of Directors as the management of the Company, and the Board of Commissioners as supervisors assisted by various committees. The nomination and appointment of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors fall within the authority of the Shareholders, as ratified during the GMS. The Company believes no conflicts of interest exist among the Board of Directors, the Board of Commissioners, and the Shareholders, which may influence any decisions made. Details on the GMS, the Board of Directors, the Board of Commissioners, their functions, the nomination and selection processes, as well as the remuneration ratios and performance evaluations of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners, are contained in the Wismilak 2023 Annual Report.
