# Penilaian Kinerja dan Pengembangan Karier [404-3]

Performance Appraisal and Career Development

Selama tahun 2023, terdapat 332 karyawan yang mendapatkan promosi. Seluruh penilaian kinerja diakomodir melalui aplikasi Talent Management System. [404-3]

During 2023, 332 employees were promoted. All performance appraisals are accommodated through the Talent Management System application. [404-3]

Wismilak memiliki Kebijakan dan Prosedur Performance Appraisal (Penilaian Kinerja) yang terdapat di dalam MOD-OD 004. Penilaian kinerja seluruh karyawan dilakukan melalui aplikasi Performance Appraisal Online yang dilakukan oleh atasan secara daring, dengan memadukan telah penilaian berbasis kuantitatif dan kualitatif berdasarkan pencapaian kinerja timnya. Setiap atasan bertanggung jawab gunna menentukan dan mengomunikasikan telah penilaian kinerja timnya serta perbaikan yang diharapkan untuk periode kinerja selanjutnya melalui proses evaluasi kinerja. Selain itu, atasan juga perlu menyampaikan objektif Departemen kepada tim yang selaras dengan objektif Perseroan.

Lebih lanjut, evaluasi kinerja karyawan dapat juga dilakukan melalui aplikasi Perform dengan mekanisme personal feedback yang merupakan masukan bagi proses Tinjauan Karyawan atas pencapaian objektif (Key Performance Indicator/KPI), Leadership Model, pengembangan karier, dan Perencanaan pengembangan yang akan datang. Seluruh telah penilaian kinerja digunakan oleh Perseroan sebagai dasar melakukan pemetaan kompetensi, kebutuhan pengembangan, dan promosi terutama pada level Staf, Supervisor, dan Manager.

Wismilak has a Performance Appraisal Policy and Procedure outlined in MOD-OD 004. The performance appraisal for all employees is conducted remotely through the Online Performance Appraisal application by supervisors. The assessment combines quantitative and qualitative performance achievements based on each team's performance. Each supervisor is responsible for determining and communicating the performance appraisal results and expected improvements for the next performance period through the performance evaluation process. Additionally, supervisors must communicate department objectives to the team, aligning them with the Company's objectives.

Furthermore, employee performance evaluation can also be conducted through the Perform application, incorporating personal feedback as input for the Employee Review process, covering achievements in Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), Leadership Model, career development, and future development planning. The Company utilizes all performance assessment results as a basis for competency mapping, development needs, and promotions, especially at the Staff, Supervisor, and Managerial levels.
