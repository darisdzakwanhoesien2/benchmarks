# Pekerja Lain Bukan Karyawan Wismilak

Other Workers Not Employees of Wismilak

Di tempat kami juga terdapat pekerja lain yang bukan karyawan Wismilak. Mereka adalah pekerja vendor/ pemasok yang bekerja berdasarkan kontrak yang telah disepakati. Namun demikian, laporan ini belum menyertakan jumlah pekerja lain yang bukan karyawan Wismilak, mengingat dinamisnya jumlah pergerakan di antara mereka. [2-8]

In our facilities, there are also non-Wismilak employees, namely vendor/supplier workers operating under agreed contracts. However, this report does not currently include the specific number of other workers who are not Wismilak employees due to the dynamic nature of their movements. [2-8]

# Kebijakan Ketenagakerjaan

Employment Policy [F.18] [F.19] [2-30]

Wismilak telah mengatur kebijakan mengenai ketenagakerjaan yang dirangkum dalam buku Kebijakan HR-Recruitment 001 dan telah berlaku sejak tanggal 1 Juli 2020. Selain itu, Perseroan juga menerbitkan Peraturan Perusahaan (PP) yang telah disahkan oleh Menteri Ketenagakerjaan (Menaker) dan Perjanjian Kerja Bersama (PKB) yang disosialisasikan kepada seluruh karyawan. Pada periode pelaporan, seluruh  $(100\%)$  karyawan tercakup dalam Peraturan Perusahaan yang diberlakukan di PT Gawih Jaya dan PT Wismilak Inti Makmur, sedangkan Perjanjian Kerja Bersama diberlakukan di PT Gelora Djaja.

Wismilak established an employment policy as summarized in HR-Recruitment Policy Book 001, effective July 1, 2020. Additionally, the Company has issued Company Regulations (PP), which have been approved by the Minister of Manpower (Menaker), and Collective Labor Agreements (CLA) that have been disseminated to all employees. During the reporting period, all  $(100\%)$  employees were covered by the Company Regulations enforced in PT Gawih Jaya and PT Wismilak Inti Makmur, while the Collective Labor Agreement was enforced in PT Gelora Djaja.
