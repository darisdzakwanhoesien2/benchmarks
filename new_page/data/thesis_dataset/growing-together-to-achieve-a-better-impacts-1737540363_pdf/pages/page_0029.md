# Target Kinerja | Performance Targets

- Konsisten menciptakan ekosistem kemitraan berbasis ekonomi bersama masyarakat sekitar melalui program Wismilak Success Partner (WSP)
Consistent in creating an economic-based partnership ecosystem with surrounding communities through the Wismilak Success Partner (WSP) program
- Terus memperkuat iklim kewirausahaan yang berkelanjutan beserta daya dukungannya melalui program kewirausahaan Indonesia, Diplomat Success Challenge (DSC)
Continuously strengthening the sustainable entrepreneurial environment and its support through the Indonesian entrepreneurship program, Diplomat Success Challenge (DSC)

# Pencapaian | Achievements

- Menjangkau 18.460 ekosistem kemitraan bersama masyarakat melalui program Wismilak Success Partner (WSP)
Reached 18,460 community partnership ecosystems through the Wismilak Success Partner (WSP) program
- 29.780 Pelaku wirausaha Indonesia berpartisipasi dalam program Diplomat Success Challenge (DSC)
29,780 Indonesian entrepreneurs have participated in the Diplomat Success Challenge (DSC) program

# Evaluasi yang Dilakukan | Evaluation Conducted

Evaluasi dilakukan di setiap akhir pelaksanaan program dan juga pada saat persiapan pelaksanaan program di tahun berikut nya. Selain itu, Perseroan juga melakukan proses pelibatan pemangku kepentingan dengan adanya diskusi maupun penyebaran survei kepada pemangku kepentingan terkait
Evaluation is conducted at the end of each program implementation and also during the preparation for the next year's program. Additionally, the Company engages stakeholders through discussions and surveys
