# Tanggapan Umpan Balik Laporan Tahun Sebelumnya [G.3]

Feedback on the Previous Year's Report

Hingga akhir tahun 2023, kami belum menerima umpan balik dari pemangku kepentingan atas Laporan Keberlanjutan tahun sebelumnya. Namun, kami tetap terbuka untuk menerima masukan dan umpan balik guna meningkatkan kualitas laporan di masa yang akan datang.

# Penentuan Isi dan Kualitas Laporan [3-1]

Determination of Report Content and Quality

Laporan Keberlanjutan Wismilak 2023 disampaikan berdasarkan prinsip: akurasi, keseimbangan, kejelasan, perbandingan, kelengkapan, konteks keberlanjutan, ketepatan waktu, dan dapat diverifikasi. Rangkaian penentuan isi dan proses penyusunan dilakukan secara internal dan telah mendapat persetujuan dari Komite Unit Keberlanjutan dan Direktur Utama yang bertanggung jawab atas seluruh kegiatan keuangan berkelanjutan.

As of the end of 2023, we have not received any feedback from stakeholders on the previous year's Sustainability Report. However, we remain open to receiving input and feedback to improve the quality of future reports.

The Wismilak 2023 Sustainability Report is presented based on the principles of accuracy, balance, clarity, comparability, completeness, sustainability context, timeliness, and verifiability. The determination of content and the preparation process are conducted internally and have received approval from Sustainability Unit Committee and President Director who is responsible for all sustainable finance activities.
