## Operating Areas and Markets Served

PT Wismilak Inti Makmur Tbk beroperasi di sektor produksi dan pemasaran rokok kretek premium. Perseroan memiliki kantor pusat di Surabaya, Jawa Timur, Indonesia. Hingga akhir tahun 2023, Perseroan memiliki 4 fasilitas produksi, 4 sentra logistik regional, dan 22 fasilitas distribusi yang tersebar di seluruh Indonesia. Produk-produk yang dipasarkan meliputi berbagai merek rokok kretek, seperti Wismilak Diplomat, Galan Slim, Wismilak Diplomat Mild Menthol, dan Diplomat Evo serta merek lainnya yang dapat dilihat di https://www.wismilak.com/id/produk-kami. Selain Indonesia, kami juga memasarkan produk di pasar luar negeri untuk produk *filter rods* di Malaysia, Taiwan, Singapore, Filipina dan Cina.

PT Wismilak Inti Makmur Tbk operates in the production and marketing of premium clove cigarettes. The Company has its head office in Surabaya, East Java, Indonesia. As of the end of 2023, the Company has 4 production facilities, 4 regional logistics centers, and 22 distribution facilities spread throughout Indonesia. The products marketed include various brands of clove cigarettes, such as Wismilak Diplomat, Galan Slim, Wismilak Diplomat Mild Menthol, and Diplomat Evo as well as other brands, which can be viewed at https://www.wismilak.com/id/produk-kami. In addition to Indonesia, we also market our filter rod products overseas in Malaysia, Taiwan, Singapore, Philippines and China.
