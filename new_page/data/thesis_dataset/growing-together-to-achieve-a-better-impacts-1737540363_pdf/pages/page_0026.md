# Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja (K3)

Occupational Health and Safety (OHS)

# Signifikansi Topik | Topic Significance

Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3) menjadi topik yang penting dalam menjalankan kegiatan operasional dikarenakan Perseroan bertanggung jawab untuk mewujudkan kondisi andal dan aman di seluruh kegiatan operasional, baik di level Perseroan maupun entitas anak

Occupational Safety and Health (OHS) is an important topic in conducting operational activities as the Company is responsible for ensuring reliable and safe conditions throughout its operational activities, both at the Company and subsidiary levels

Kebijakan | Policies

Kebijakan Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja di PT Gelora Djaja yang diterbitkan oleh Presiden Direktur PT Gelora Djaja Pada Tanggal 24 Oktober 2016

Occupational Health and Safety Policy at PT Gelora Djaja issued by the President Director of PT Gelora Djaja on October 24, 2016

# Evaluasi yang Dilakukan | Evaluation Conducted

1. Identifikasi Bahaya, penilaian risiko dan pengendalian risiko Hazard identification, risk assessment, and risk control
2. Inspeksi harian safety Daily safety inspections
3. Pengawasan pekerjaan berisiko tinggi Supervision of high-risk work
4. Pelaporan kecelakaan kerja Reporting of workplace accidents
5. Investigasi kecelakaan kerja dan keadaan darurat Investigation of workplace accidents and emergencies
6. Perijinan pekerjaan berisiko tinggi Permitting for high-risk work
7. Penanganan dan pengelolaan limbah B3 Handling and management of hazardous (B3) waste
8. Pemantauan dan pengujian lingkungan kerja Monitoring and testing of the work environment
9. Pemantauan dan pengujian air limbah Monitoring and testing of wastewater
10. Pemantauan dan pemeriksaan alat pemadam api ringan Monitoring and inspection of light fire extinguishers
11. Pemantauan dan pemeriksaan P3K Monitoring and inspection of first aid kits
