Program pelatihan ini dirancang dengan cermat untuk memperkaya pengetahuan dan keterampilan personal karyawan, sejalan dengan evolusi kebutuhan perusahaan.

Perseroan menggelar program pelatihan secara berkala, melibatkan sejumlah inisiatif internal dan eksternal. # Employee Competency Development

Human resources of superior quality are the main key to the company's sustainability and business growth. In an effort to ensure that employees have the competencies and abilities necessary to meet growing business demands, the company actively provides opportunities for them to take part in training programs. This training program is carefully designed to enrich employees' personal knowledge and skills, in line with the evolution of company needs.

The company holds regular training programs, involving a number of internal and external initiatives. Internally, the company organizes training sessions that are tailored to the company's specific needs and cover various aspects, from improving technical skills to leadership development. In addition, the company also provides opportunities for employees to take part in external training held by leading training institutions.

This training, which is held regularly and in a variety of ways, reflects the company's commitment to continuously improving employee capacity and competency. By involving employees in holistic training programs, the company not only invests in individual development but also ensures that the company's human resources remain relevant and competitive in a dynamic market.
