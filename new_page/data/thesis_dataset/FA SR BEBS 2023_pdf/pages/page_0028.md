KBLI 43909 - Konstruksi Khusus lainnya Kelompok ini mencakup kegiatan konstruksi khusus lainnya yang belum diklasifikasikan dalam kelompok 43901 s.d. 43905 yang memerlukan keahlian atau perlengkapan khusus, seperti kegiatan pengerjaan penahan lembab dan air, dehumidifikasi (pelembaban) bangunan, shaft sinking, pembengkokan baja, pemasangan batu dan batu bata, pemasangan dan pembongkaran tangga (scaffold dan platform) kecuali penyewaannya, pemasangan cerobong asap dan oven untuk keperluan industri dan pekerjaan yang memerlukan keahlian memanjat dan penggunaan perlengkapan yang berkaitan, misalnya bekerja pada gedung gedung yang tinggi. Termasuk pekerjaan di bawah permukaan tanah, konstruksi kolam renang di luar ruangan, pembersihan dengan uap, penyemburan pasir untuk membersihkan tembok dan kegiatan sejenisnya untuk eksterior bangunan dan penyewaan derek dengan menggunakan operator;

6. KBLI 08101 – Penggalian Batu Hias dan Batu Bangunan

Mencakup usaha penggalian batu hias dan batu bangunan, seperti batu pualam atau marmer, batu split (batu gajah, base course, batu pecah), paras, obsidian, andesit dan granit. Kegiatan pemecahan, pembersihan, pengangkutan dan penjualan, yang tidak dapat dipisahkan secara administratif dari usaha penggalian batu hias dan bangunan;

framed articles with mineral or cellulosic substance; mineral insulating materials, such as slag wool, rock wool and other types of wool; exfoliated vermiculite, an expanded clay and heat-insulating and sound-absorbing material; articles of various mineral substances, such as mica and articles of mica, articles of peat as a burning material, articles of graphite (electrical goods); articles of asphalt or similar materials, for example adhesives based on asphalt, coal tar and so on; and carbon and graphite fibers and their derivatives (except electrodes and electrical equipment);

5. KBLI 43909 - Other Special Construction

This category includes other special construction activities not classified in 43901 to 43905, and requires special skills or equipment, such as activities for holding moisture and water, dehumidification of buildings, shafts sinking, steel bending, masonry and bricklaying, installation and dismantling of stairs (scaffold and platform) except for leasing, installation of chimneys and ovens for industrial use and jobs requiring climbing skills and the use of related equipment, like working in tall buildings. It also includes underground work, construction of outdoor swimming pools, steam cleaning, sand blasting to clean walls and similar activities for building exteriors and crane rental using an operator;

6. KBLI 08101 – Excavation of Decorative Stone and Building Stone

It includes excavating decorative stones and building stones, such as marble, split stone (elephant stone, base course, crushed stone), sand, obsidian, andesite and granite. It also includes activities of breaking, cleaning, transporting and selling, which cannot be separated administratively from the excavation of decorative stone and buildings;
