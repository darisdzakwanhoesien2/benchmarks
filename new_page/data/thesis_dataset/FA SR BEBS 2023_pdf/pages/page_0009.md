# RENCANA STRATEGI BISNIS
BUSINESS STRATEGY PLAN

Rencana strategis Perseroan dibuat untuk mencapai Visi dan Misi Perseroan. Implementasi dari Visi dan Misi tersebut dilakukan melalui kegiatan operasional dan bisnis yang terintegrasi dengan aspek ekonomi, sosial, dan lingkungan.

The company's strategic plan is created to achieve the company's vision and mission. Implementation of the vision and mission is carried out through operational and business activities that are integrated with economic, social, and environmental aspects.

Dalam cakupan kegiatan operasional dan bisnis, Perseroan memiliki beberapa fokus dalam melakukan kegiatan-kegiatan yang bertujuan tercapainya keberlanjutan seperti mengurangi penggunaan energi listrik secara efisien agar dapat menghemat sumber daya dan mengurangi dampak lingkungan, penggunaan air secara efisien dalam kegiatan operasional guna menjaga ketersediaan air bersih dan melindungi sumber daya air.

Within the scope of operational and business activities, the company has several focuses on carrying out activities aimed at achieving sustainability, such as reducing the use of electrical energy efficiently in order to save resources and reduce environmental impacts and using water efficiently in operational activities to maintain the availability of clean water and protect water resources.
