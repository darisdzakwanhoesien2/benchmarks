In ensuring the effective implementation of training and development programs, the leaders conduct routine coaching to Blibli employees once a month and continue to actively ensure the fulfilment of every employee's training needs by completing the relevant department training plan and self-development plan. Blibli Tiket will guarantee that employees can develop their competencies according to their career progress.

Pada tahun 2023, beberapa penyelenggaraan program pelatihan dan pengembangan di Blibli Tiket dilaksanakan secara terintegrasi salah satunya BOLD Festival, serta lainnya dilakukan terpisah sesuai kebutuhan dan kebijakan masing-masing. Untuk ke deparnya, Perseroan akan terus meningkatkan integrasi pelaksanaan program pelatihan dan pengembangan. Seluruh implementasi program pelatihan dan pengembangan Blibli menjadi tanggung jawab Departemen Human Capital Development, sedangkan untuk tiket.com di bawah pengawasan Divisi People Strategy, Departemen ini akan mengevaluasi pelaksanaan program secara berkala setiap enam bulan untuk Blibli dan tiga bulan untuk tiket.com. Adapun hasil penilaian Blibli akan dilaporkan kepada COO dan tiket.com diberikan kepada Chief People Officer (CPO).

In 2023, Blibli Tiket held some integrated training and development programs, such as the BOLD Festival, while others were held separately according to their respective needs and policies. The Company will continue integrating more training and development programs in the future. The Human Capital Development Department implements Blibli's training and development programs, while at tiket.com, it is supervised by the People Strategy Division. These departments periodically evaluate the program implementation every six months for Blibli and three months for tiket.com. The results of Blibli's assessment will be reported to the COO and tiket.com's to the Chief People Officer (CPO).

### BRIGHT
(Biblionears Insight)

![img-123.jpeg](img-123.jpeg)

Program pembelajaran untuk karyawan Blibli yang diberikan oleh narasumber expert dan eksternal. Para narasumber berbagi pengetahuan dan pengalaman selama satu jam di setiap sesi. Pada tahun 2023, terdapat 2 sesi BRIGHT dengan topik tips pengembangan diri dan pandangan bisnis.

A learning program for Blibli employees provided by external expert speakers. The speakers share their knowledge and experience in one-hour sessions. In 2023, there were 2 BRIGHT sessions with self-development tips and business outlook topics.

Program pembelajaran karyawan tiket.com setiap hari Jumat minggu ke-4 setiap bulannya dengan mengelokasikan waktu 1 jam dari hari kerja untuk belajar mandiri atau sesi diskusi internal. Sepiargang tahun 2023, total jam pembelajaran pada program ini yaitu sebanyak 260 jam dengan 555 peserta.

An employee learning program is held by tiket.com every Friday of the 4th week of each month by allocating 1 hour of the workday for self-study or internal discussion sessions. Throughout 2023, the total learning hours in this program reached 260 hours with 555 participants.
