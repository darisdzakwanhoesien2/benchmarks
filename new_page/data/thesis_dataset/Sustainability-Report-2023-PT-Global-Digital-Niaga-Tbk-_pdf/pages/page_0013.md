The sustainability report discloses material topics that are relevant, have a significant impact on the Company, and are of concern to the Company's stakeholders. There are no changes in material topics in this report compared to the last report due to the absence of significant business changes in the Company. The Company has not involved an independent party in conducting verification (assurance) for this report, but all data and information have been approved by the Board of Directors and related divisions. [G.1][2-4][2-5]

The scope of information and data comes from PT Global Digital Niaga Tbk, which is included in the consolidated statements but excludes PT Supra Boga Lastan Tbk. Meanwhile, economic performance data is consolidated financial data and independently audited by a Public Accounting Firm. [2-2]
