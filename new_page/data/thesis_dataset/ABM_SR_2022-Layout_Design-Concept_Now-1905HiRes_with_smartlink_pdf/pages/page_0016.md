## Location of Operations [GRI 2-1]

Kegiatan operasi dan usaha ABM seluruhnya berada di Indonesia dan tidak memiliki wilayah operasi dan usaha di luar Indonesia.

All of ABM's operations and business activities are located in Indonesia and does not have any operating and business areas outside Indonesia.
