Reduction of GHG emissions | • ABM telah mengidentifikasi potensi reduksi emisi dari area rehabilitasi
Daerah Aliran Sungai di Blok Tiwingan dan Blok Kalaan oleh anak
perusahaan. Selain itu, potensi reduksi emisi juga dihasilkan dari entitas
bisnis perusahaan di sektor energi baru dan terbarukan.
• Total potensi reduksi ini mencapai 165.324 ton CO_{2}eq.
• Sampai dengan akhir tahun 2022, ABM masih melakukan proses validasi
atas potensi reduksi emisi tersebut.
• ABM has identified the potential for reducing emissions from the watershed
rehabilitation area in the Tiwingan Block and Kalaan Block by its
subsidiaries. In addition, the emission reduction potential is also generated
from the company's business entities in the field of new and renewable
energy. This reduction potential amounts to 165,324 tons of CO_{2}eq.
• Until the end of 2022, ABM is still validating
the emission reduction potential.  |
