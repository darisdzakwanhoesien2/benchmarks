Untuk mengukuhkan pengelolaan risiko, prinsip pencegahan juga dilakukan Perusahaan dengan mengaktifkan kerja Unit Audit Internal, Komite Audit, serta Komite Pemantau Risiko dan Tata Kelola Terintegrasi yang dimiliki Perusahaan. To minimize the impact of these various risks, WSBP has carefully mapped the risks along with their mitigation steps and policies by implementing a Risk Management System based on the Three Lines Model.
