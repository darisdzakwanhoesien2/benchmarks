# Penyusunan Rencana Program Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja

Berdasarkan telah pemetaan risiko di setiap wilayah kerja di atas, selanjutnya WSBP menyusun rencana program K3 yang terdiri dari 5 sektor program yaitu: [GRI 403-?]

1. People Development
Mapping personel HSE, melakukan pelatihan kepada awareness kepada personel

2. Improvement
Pelaporan dari unit bisnis secara real time dengan digitalisasi, awareness dan pengembangan kompetensi pegawai

3. Compliance &amp; Implementation
Workshop &amp; refreshment, serta pemenuhan dan monitoring legislasi yang ada.

4. Monitoring
Monitoring program-program HSE pada unit bisnis, Implementasi kesesuaian dan monitoring kinerja HSE pada Unit Operasional.

5. Reporting
Reporting ketidaksesuaian dan pelaporan P2K3 ke dinas terkait.

# Preparation of Occupational Safety and Health Program Plans

Based on the results of risk mapping in each work area above, WSBP prepared OHS program plans consisting of 5 program sectors: [GRI 403-?]

1. People Development
Mapping HSE personnel, conducting training or awareness for personnel

2. Improvement;
Reporting from business units in real time with digitalization, employees awareness and competency development

3. Compliance &amp; Implementation
Workshop &amp; refreshment, as well as compliance and monitoring of existing legislation.

4. Monitoring
Monitoring HSE programs in business units, implementing conformity and monitoring HSE performance in Operational Units.

5. Reporting
Reporting non-conformities and reporting of OSH to related agencies.
