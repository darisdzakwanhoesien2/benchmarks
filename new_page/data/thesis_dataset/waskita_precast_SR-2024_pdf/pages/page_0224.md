# KINERJA LINGKUNGAN
## ENVIRONMENTAL PERFORMANCE

Komitmen WSBP terhadap lingkungan diwujudkan melalui adopsi teknologi hijau dan inovasi dalam proses produksi antara lain penggunaan Ash hasil sampingan pembakaran batu bara untuk campuran beton. WSBP's commitment to the environment is realized through the adoption of green technology and innovation in the production process, including the use of ash from coal combustion by products for concrete mixes.
