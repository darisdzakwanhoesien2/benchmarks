Perubahan Susunan Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris Perseroan. / Composition changes in the Company's Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners

## Hasil Keputusan / Resolutions

1. Mengangkat Bapak Iwan Hadianto re tebagai Komisaris Perseroan dan Bapak Nicko Widjaja sebagai Komisaris Independen Perseroan. / Appointed Mr. Iwan Hadianto re the Company's Commissioner and Mr. Nicko Widjaja as the Company's Independent Commissioner
