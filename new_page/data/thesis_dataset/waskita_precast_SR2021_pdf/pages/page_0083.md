On these findings, all of the Company's personnel are committed to maintaining and even improving the implementation of GCG so that a higher score can be obtained for the implementation in 2022, which will be assessed in 2023. In line with that, the Company will also optimally carry out all recommendations from the assessor team of GCG implementation in fiscal year 2021.
