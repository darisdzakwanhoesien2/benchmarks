# KEGIATAN MEMBANGUN BUDAYA BERKELANJUTAN
POJKS1.F1
SUSTAINABLE CULTURE BUILDING ACTIVITIES

Dalam rangka meningkatkan pemahaman dan kesadaran seluruh organ akan budaya berkelanjutan, Perseroan secara berkala melakukan sosialisasi terkait penerapan prinsip berkelanjutan. Sosialisasi tersebut dilaksanakan dalam kegiatan rutin di internal ataupun melalui rapat-rapat gabungan antar divisi/unit. Selain itu, Perseroan secara berkala melaksanakan kegiatan pengembangan kompetensi terkait keberlanjutan, yang ditujukan kepada Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, dan seluruh karyawan.

In order to increase the understanding and awareness of all organs of sustainable culture, the Company regularly conducts socialization related to the implementation of sustainable principles. The socialization is carried out in routine internal activities or through joint meetings between divisions/units. In addition, the Company regularly conducts competency development activities related to sustainability, aimed at the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, and all employees.

# TANGGUNG JAWAB SOSIAL
SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY

Perseroan senantiasa menjaga dan meningkatkan manfaat sosial bagi karyawan, masyarakat sekitar, serta mitra usaha dan pelanggan. Inisiatif pemenuhan tanggung jawab tersebut disesuaikan dengan kebutuhan masing-masing pemangku kepentingan.

The Company always maintains and improves social benefits for employees, surrounding communities, as well as business partners and customers. Initiatives to fulfill these responsibilities are tailored to the needs of each stakeholder.
