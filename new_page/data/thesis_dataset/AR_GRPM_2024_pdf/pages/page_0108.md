Wewenang Komite Audit diuraikan sebagai berikut.

1. Mengakses dokumen, data, dan informasi Perseroan tentang karyawan, dana, aset, dan sumber daya yang diperlukan.

3. Melibatkan pihak independen di luar Komite Audit yang diperlukan untuk membantu pelaksanaan tugasnya (jika diperlukan).

# Authority

The authority of the Audit Committee is described as follows.

1. Accessing the Company's documents, data, and information on employees, funds, assets, and necessary resources.
2. Communicate directly with employees, including the Board of Directors and those who carry out internal audit, risk management, and accountant functions related to the duties and responsibilities of the Audit Committee.
3. Involve independent parties outside the Audit Committee as necessary to assist in the performance of its duties (if required).
