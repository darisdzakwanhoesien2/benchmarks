# Kolektibilitas Piutang

# Collectability of Accounts Receivables

Accounts receivable collectability is the Company's ability to collect accounts receivable from customers or business partners. To calculate the collectability of receivables, the Company calculates the average collection period approach which is calculated by dividing trade receivables by net sales, then multiplied by the total days for 1 year.
