# 01 PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) Signs Distribution Agreement with PT Mitra Mas Sentosa (Brand: Cool-Vita)

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) Tandatangani Perjanjian Kerjasama Distribusi dengan PT Mitra Mas Sentosa (brand: Cool-Vita)

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG), a subsidiary of PT Soho Global Health, Tbk, has entered into a distribution partnership agreement with PT Mitra Mas Sentosa. The agreement was formalized through a signing ceremony attended by the leaders of both companies, Edwin Vega Darma, VP Sales &amp; Marketing of PPG, and Wang Junjie, CEO of PT Mitra Mas Sentosa. The event was also witnessed by Pandy Harianto, VP Operations &amp; Supply Chain of PPG, as well as Michelle Chen, Indonesia Country Manager, and Sulya Fei, VP General Management from PT Mitra Mas Sentosa.

The signing ceremony took place on January 24, 2025, in the lobby of PT Soho Global Health's office in Pulogadung. The event was attended by Vicki Goutama, Head of Marketing at PPG, along with the Directors of PT Mitra Mas Sentosa and key team members from both companies, all of whom are committed to working together in this partnership.

PT Mitra Mas Sentosa is a producer of vitamin supplements under the Cool-Vita brand. The brand is already present in the Indonesian market with seven product categories approved by BPOM (Indonesia's Food and Drug Authority) and certified Halal.

In his remarks, Wang Junjie, CEO of PT Mitra Mas Sentosa, expressed his excitement about this fresh start in 2025 to ensure Cool-Vita's growth in key channels. "We hope that PPG can support Cool-Vita's growth aspirations," said Wang Junjie.

PPG has over 65 years of experience and is recognized as one of the top five health product distribution and logistics companies in Indonesia. "This partnership reflects PPG's interest and confidence in Cool-Vita's growth, and we are excited to achieve mutual success through effective collaboration," stated Pandy Harianto.

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG), anak perusahaan PT Soho Global Health, Tbk sepakat melakukan perjanjian kerja sama distribusi dengan PT Mitra Mas Sentosa. Kesepakatan tersebut dikukuhkan melalui penandatanganan perjanjian kerja sama yang dilakukan oleh masing-masing pimpinan perusahaan, yaitu Edwin Vega Darma Selaku VP Sales &amp; Marketing PPG dan Wang Junjie, CEO PT Mitra Mas Sentosa, serta disaksikan oleh Pandy Harianto, VP Operations &amp; Supply Chain PPG dan dari pihak PT Mitra Mas Sentosa turut menyaksikan Ibu Michelle Chen, Indonesia Country Manager dan Ibu Sulya Fei, VP General Management.

Penandatanganan kerjasama ini dilaksanakan pada tanggal 24 Januari 2025 bertempat di lobby PT Soho Global Health, berlokasi di Pulogadung. Dalam acara ini turut hadir Vicki Goutama Selaku Head of Marketing PPG dan jajaran Direksi PT Mitra Mas Sentosa beserta seluruh team yang berperan penting dari masing-masing perusahaan untuk berkomitmen menjalankan kemitraan bersama.

PT Mitra Mas Sentosa merupakan produsen suplemen vitamin yang mengusung brand Cool-Vita. Brand ini telah hadir di pasar Indonesia dengan 7 (tujuh) produk kategori yang sudah disetujui oleh BPOM serta bersertifikasi Halal.

Pada sambutannya, CEO PT Mitra Mas Sentosa menyatakan bahagia dengan awal baru di tahun 2025 ini untuk memastikan Cool-Vita tumbuh di channel yang penting. "Kami berharap PPG dapat mendukung aspirasi pertumbuhan Cool-Vita," ujar Wang Junjie.

PPG telah berpengalaman dan dipercaya lebih dari 65 tahun sebagai top 5 perusahaan distribusi dan logistik produk kesehatan di Indonesia. "Kerjasama ini menunjukkan ketertarikan dan keyakinan PPG terhadap pertumbuhan Cool-Vita dan PPG bersemangat untuk menyongsong kesuksesan bersama melalui kolaborasi efektif keduanya," ujar Pandy Harianto.
