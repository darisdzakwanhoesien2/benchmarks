# Assessment of the Performance of the Board of Directors

## Penilaian terhadap Kinerja Direksi

The evaluation of the performance of the members of the Board of Directors is carried out by the Board of Commissioners and shareholders of the Company with reference to the Board of Directors Charter. The Company also has a Policy on Performance Appraisal of the Board of Directors, which serves as a guideline for assessing the effectiveness of the implementation of the duties and responsibilities of the Board of Directors and also as a form of accountability for the performance assessment of the Board of Directors of the Company.

The Board of Directors Charter contains an explanation of the method for evaluating the performance of members of the Board of Directors, which includes the following:

1. The Board of Commissioners will determine the Key Performance Indicators (KPI);
2. The Board of Commissioners will evaluate the performance achievements of the members of the Board of Directors which will then be submitted to the shareholders at the GMS;
3. The performance evaluation of the members of the Board of Directors will be carried out based on the following KPIs:
- Achievement of the Board of Directors' performance (collectively) by looking at the Company's achievements based on the Business Plan and Annual Operational Plan, and/or other criteria determined by the Board of Commissioners.
- Achievement of the performance of the Board of Directors (individually) in achieving the Company's performance as stated in the Business Plan and Annual Operational Plan, Management Contract, and/or other criteria determined in the Board of Commissioners meeting.
- Implementation of GCG principles, both individually and collectively.

Penilaian atas kinerja anggota Direksi dilakukan oleh Dewan Komisaris dan pemegang saham Perseroan dengan mengacu pada Piagam Direksi. Perseroan juga telah memiliki Kebijakan Penilaian atas Kinerja Direksi, yang mana kebijakan ini disusun sebagai pedoman untuk menilai efektivitas pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab Direksi dan juga sebagai bentuk akuntabilitas atas penilaian kinerja Direksi Perseroan.

Piagam Direksi memuat penjelasan mengenai metode evaluasi/penilaian kinerja atas anggota Direksi, yang mana di antaranya adalah sebagai berikut:

1. Dewan Komisaris akan menetapkan Indikator Kinerja Utama/ Key Performance Indicator (KPI);
2. Dewan Komisaris akan melakukan evaluasi atas pencapaian kinerja dari anggota Direksi yang mana selanjutnya akan disampaikan pada pemegang saham dalam RUPS;
3. Evaluasi kinerja anggota Direksi tersebut akan dilakukan berdasarkan KPI sebagai berikut:
- Pencapaian kinerja Direksi (secara bersama-sama) dengan melihat pencapaian Perseroan berdasarkan Rencana Bisnis dan Rencana Operasional Tahunan, dan/atau kriteria lainnya yang ditetapkan oleh Dewan Komisaris.
- Pencapaian kinerja Direksi (secara individu) dalam mencapai kinerja Perseroan sebagaimana tercantum dalam Rencana Bisnis dan Rencana Operasional Tahunan, Kontrak Manajemen, dan/atau kriteria lainnya yang ditetapkan dalam rapat Dewan Komisaris.
- Implementasi prinsip-prinsip GCG, baik secara individu maupun kolektif.
