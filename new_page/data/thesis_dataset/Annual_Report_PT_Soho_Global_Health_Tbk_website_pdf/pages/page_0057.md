# Memorandum of Understanding Signing Ceremony between RSUP Dr. Sardjito and PT SOHO Global Health Tbk. Establishing Strategic Collaboration in Education, Research, and Pharmaceuticals

Acara Penandatanganan Nota Kesepakatan RSUP Dr. Sardjito dan PT. SOHO Global Health Tbk. Jalin Kerja Sama Strategis dalam Bidang Pendidikan, Penelitian, dan Farmasi

On June 4, 2025 memorandum of understanding was signed between Dr. Sardjito Central General Hospital (RSUP Dr. Sardjito) and PT. SOHO Global Health Tbk. The event took place in Yogyakarta and was attended by the board of directors and management from both parties.

The collaboration covers the areas of education, research, and pharmaceuticals. This partnership is expected to serve as a strategic step in strengthening synergy between the healthcare service institutions and the pharmaceutical industry, particularly in supporting the advancement of the national healthcare sector.

This collaboration reflects a shared commitment from both parties to promote sustainable development in the health sector and to open up broader opportunities for future cooperation.

Pada 4 Juni 2025 telah dilaksanakan penandatanganan nota kesepakatan antara Rumah Sakit Umum Pusat (RSUP) Dr. Sardjito dengan PT. SOHO Global Health Tbk. Kegiatan ini berlangsung di Yogyakarta dan dihadiri oleh jajaran direksi serta manajemen dari kedua belah pihak.

Kerja sama ini mencakup bidang pendidikan, penelitian, dan farmasi. Kolaborasi ini diharapkan dapat menjadi langkah strategis dalam memperkuat sinergi antara dunia industri dan institusi pelayanan kesehatan, khususnya dalam mendukung kemajuan dunia kesehatan nasional.

Kerja sama ini menjadi salah satu bentuk komitmen kedua belah pihak untuk mendorong kemajuan sektor kesehatan secara berkelanjutan, serta membuka ruang kolaborasi yang lebih luas di masa depan.
