In addition to being conducted at the corporate level, the Town Hall Meeting was also held within the Supply &amp; Operations Business Unit on December 12, 2025. Carrying the theme "REJUVE: Reignite Before Jumping into a New Venture", the event served as a platform for management to communicate the Company's performance achievements, business developments, as well as its strategic direction and targets for the upcoming year.

On this occasion, management also expressed its appreciation for the collaboration and contributions of all teams throughout 2025, while conveying expectations for improved and more sustainable performance in 2026. The event was enlivened by various activities, including a Wonderful Indonesia dance performance, interactive quizzes, door prize distribution, and an Awarding Ceremony featuring the Continuous Improvement Award, Safety Awareness Award, Star Talent Award, and Long Service Awards. Through this initiative, SOHO Global Health aims to further strengthen synergy, enhance motivation, and foster a collaborative spirit among all employees in supporting the achievement of sustainable performance.

Overall, the implementation of the Town Hall Meeting has had a positive impact on strengthening the Company's organizational culture. Through initiatives that extend to the business unit level, SOHO Global Health reaffirms its commitment to promoting active employee engagement in supporting sustainable transformation and driving excellent performance across all levels of the organization.
