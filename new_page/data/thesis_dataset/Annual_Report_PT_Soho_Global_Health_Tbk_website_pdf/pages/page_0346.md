## E. The 15th Work Unit Deliberation Meeting of the Labor Union

The Pharmaceutical and Health Reform Workers' Union Unit of PT SOHO Industri Pharmasi (SP Farkes-R PT SIP) convened its 15th Work Unit Deliberation on 6 December 2025 at the Pulogadung Industrial Estate. This event represents a triennial organizational agenda conducted as part of the union's internal governance mechanism, aimed at presenting the management accountability report and electing new leadership.

The work unit deliberation process encompassed the nomination of chairman candidates, the distribution of ballots to all members, and the conduct of plenary sessions on the day of the event. The management accountability report was formally accepted by the participants, with the meeting attended by 93 members as well as representatives from the federation.

Based on the voting results, Mr. Sigit Rahmadi was re-elected as Chairman of SP Farkes-R PT SIP for the 2025-2028 term. Subsequently, through the formatur session, the composition of the management board was established as follows:

- Chairman: Sigit Rahmadi
- Vice Chairman 1: Andreas Tri Setyo W.
- Vice Chairman 2: Joni Asipu
- Vice Chairman 3: Agus Pramono
- Secretary General: Agus Supriatman
- Secretary 1: M. Alif Reza
- Secretary 2: Santy Utami
- Secretary 3: Subur Mardiyono
- Treasurer: Agus Farkhan Gozali
- Treasurer 1: Riyan Hidayat
- Treasurer 2: Gathot Setiarto
- Head of Youth Affairs: Tomy

## E. Musyawarah Unit Kerja (Musnik) ke-15 Serikat Pekerja

Serikat Pekerja Farmasi dan Kesehatan Reformasi Unit Kerja PT SOHO Industri Pharmasi (SP Farkes-R PT SIP) menyelenggarakan Musyawarah Unit Kerja (Musnik) ke-XV pada 6 Desember 2025 di Kawasan Industri Pulogadung, Jakarta Timur. Kegiatan ini merupakan agenda organisasi yang dilaksanakan setiap tiga tahun sebagai bagian dari mekanisme tata kelola internal, yang bertujuan untuk menyampaikan laporan pertanggungjawaban pengurus serta memilih kepengurusan baru.

Pelaksanaan Musnik mencakup proses penjaringan calon ketua, distribusi surat suara kepada seluruh anggota, serta pelaksanaan sidang pleno pada hari kegiatan. Laporan pertanggungjawaban pengurus diterima oleh peserta Musnik, yang dihadiri oleh 93 anggota serta perwakilan organisasi federasi.

Berdasarkan hasil pemungutan suara, Bapak Sigit Rahmadi kembali terpilih sebagai Ketua Umum SP Farkes-R PT SIP untuk periode 2025-2028. Selanjutnya, melalui sidang formatur, ditetapkan susunan kepengurusan sebagai berikut:

- Sekretaris 3: Subur Mardiyono
- Bendahara Umum: Agus Farkhan Gozali
- Bendahara 1: Riyan Hidayat
