# Dividend policy

The Company pays dividends in an equitable and timely manner. All shareholders are treated equally and paid within 30 days after the dividends are declared.

In addition to prioritizing fundamental performance growth, the Company is also committed to delivering added value to shareholders through the distribution of cash dividends. The dividend amount is determined by taking into account the Company's earnings, financial condition, operational requirements, the obligation to establish reserve funds in accordance with applicable regulations, as well as future business development plans.

As a demonstration of consistency with this commitment, the Company maintains a dividend policy with a distribution range of 50%–70% of net profit as cash dividends. This policy remains adaptive and subject to review in line with developments in the Company's financial condition and expansion strategy.

In July 2025, an annual cash dividend of IDR 300.8 billion was paid to shareholders for the 2024 financial year.

In January 2026, an interim dividend of IDR 420.1 billion was paid to shareholders for the 2025 financial year.

# Kebijakan pembagian deviden

Perseroan membayar dividen secara adil dan tepat waktu. Semua pemegang saham diperlakukan sama dan dibayar dalam waktu 30 hari setelah dividen diumumkan.

Selain mengedepankan pertumbuhan kinerja fundamental, Perseroan juga berkomitmen memberikan nilai tambah bagi pemegang saham melalui pembagian dividen tunai. Besaran dividen ditentukan dengan memperhatikan capaian laba, kondisi keuangan Perseroan, kebutuhan operasional, kewajiban pembentukan dana cadangan sesuai ketentuan perundang-undangan, serta rencana pengembangan usaha ke depan.

Sebagai wujud konsistensi atas komitmen tersebut, Perseroan mempertahankan kebijakan dividen dengan kisaran pembagian 50%–70% dari laba bersih sebagai dividen tunai. Kebijakan ini tetap bersifat adaptif dan dapat ditinjau kembali sesuai dengan perkembangan kondisi keuangan maupun strategi ekspansi Perseroan.

Pada bulan Juni 2025, dividen tunai tahunan sebesar Rp300,8 milyar telah dibayarkan kepada para pemegang saham untuk tahun buku 2024.

Pada bulan Januari 2026, dividen interim sebesar Rp420,1 milyar telah dibayarkan kepada para pemegang saham untuk tahun buku 2025.
