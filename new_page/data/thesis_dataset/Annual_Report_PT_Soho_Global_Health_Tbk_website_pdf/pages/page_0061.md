# PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) Signs Distribution Cooperation Agreement with PT Sea Makeup Beauty

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) Tandatangani Perjanjian Kerjasama Distribusi dengan PT Sea Makeup Beauty (brand: The Originote)

![img-72.jpeg](img-72.jpeg)

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG), a subsidiary of PT Soho Global Health, Tbk, has agreed to enter into a distribution cooperation agreement with PT Sea Makeup Beauty. The agreement was formalized through the signing of a cooperation contract by the respective company leaders: Edwin Vega Darma as VP of Sales &amp; Marketing of PPG and the Director and Legal Management from PT Sea Makeup Beauty. The signing was also witnessed by Pandy Harianto, VP of Operations &amp; Supply Chain at PPG, and Josie Jayasaputra Kusmayadi, Legal Manager of PT Soho Global Health.

The signing ceremony took place on April 15, 2025, in the lobby of PT Soho Global Health, located in Pulogadung. The event was attended by Vicki Goutama, Head of Marketing at PPG, along with the Board of Directors of PT Sea Makeup Beauty and key team members from both companies, all committed to jointly carrying out the collaboration.

The Originote brand, brought by PT Sea Makeup Beauty, has been present in the Indonesian market since 2022, with one of its flagship products being the Hyalucera Moisturizer. The brand carries the motto "Affordable Skincare for All," emphasizing safety for all ages and skin types.

In his speech, the Director of PT Sea Makeup Beauty expressed happiness with the partnership: "The moment we start our partnership with PPG marks the moment we become serious about expanding our business in Indonesia. This phase is a crucial milestone for us in developing offline channels, especially in modern retail outlets. We hope for a stable business environment, and with the mutual trust we build together, I believe both parties will achieve success. In the future, we hope more Sea Makeup Beauty products, in addition to The Originote, can be part of this collaboration with PPG," said the Director of PT Sea Makeup Beauty.

PPG has more than 65 years of experience and is trusted as one of the top 5 distribution and logistics companies for health products in Indonesia. "This collaboration shows PPG's interest and confidence in the growth of the skincare industry in Indonesia, and in The Originote brand, which is rapidly growing in the national skincare scene. PPG is proud to be a part of PT Sea Makeup Beauty's journey, and we are excited to achieve success together through effective collaboration," said Edwin Vega.

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG), anak perusahaan PT Soho Global Health, Tbk sepakat melakukan perjanjian kerja sama distribusi dengan PT Sea Makeup Beauty. Kesepakatan tersebut dikukuhkan melalui penandatanganan perjanjian kerja sama yang dilakukan oleh masing-masing pimpinan perusahaan, yaitu Edwin Vega Darma selaku VP Sales &amp; Marketing PPG dan Director dan Legal management dari PT Sea Makeup Beauty, serta disaksikan oleh Pandy Harianto, VP Operations &amp; Supply Chain PPG, Josie Jayasaputra Kusmayadi, Legal Manager PT Soho Global Health.

Penandatanganan kerjasama ini dilaksanakan pada tanggal 15 April 2025 bertempat di lobby PT Soho Global Health, berlokasi di Pulogadung. Dalam acara ini turut hadir Vicki Goutama selaku Head of Marketing PPG dan jajaran Direksi PT Sea Makeup Beauty beserta seluruh team yang berperan penting dari masing-masing perusahaan untuk berkomitmen menjalankan kemitraan bersama.

Brand The Originote yang dibawa oleh PT Sea Makeup Beauty ini telah hadir di pasar Indonesia sejak tahun 2022 dengan salah satu produk unggulan nya Hyalucera Moisturizer dan memiliki moto "Affordable Skincare for All" yang aman digunakan untuk semua usia dan jenis kulit.

Pada sambutannya Direktur PT Sea Makeup Beauty menyatakan bahagia dengan kemitraan ini. "Momen dimana kami memulai kemitraan dengan PPG adalah momen dimana kami mulai serius mengembangkan bisnis di Indonesia. Tahap ini adalah milestone yang penting bagi kami untuk mengembangkan offline channel khususnya di modern outlet channel. Harapannya dimana bisnis stabil, dan dengan mutual trust yang dibangun bersama-sama maka saya berharap kesuksesan akan diraih oleh kedua belah pihak, ke depannya akan lebih banyak produk Sea Makeup Beauty selain The Originote yang bisa dikolaborasikan dengan PPG," ujar Direktur PT Sea Makeup Beauty.

PPG telah berpengalaman dan dipercaya lebih dari 65 tahun sebagai top 5 perusahaan distribusi dan logistik produk kesehatan di Indonesia. "Kerjasama ini menunjukkan ketertarikan dan keyakinan PPG terhadap pertumbuhan industri skincare di Indonesia dan brand the Originote yang tumbuh sangat pesat di industri skincare nasional. PPG bangga bisa jadi bagian dari journey PT Sea Makeup Beauty dan kami bersemangat untuk menyongsong kesuksesan bersama melalui kolaborasi efektif keduanya," ujar Edwin Vega.
