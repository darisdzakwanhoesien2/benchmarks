# The Role of the Board of Directors in Formulating Corporate Strategy and Strategic Policies

Peran Direksi dalam Perumusan Strategi dan Kebijakan Strategi Perseroan

The Board of Directors plays a central role in formulating the Company's strategic direction and policies, which serve as the primary guidelines for all business activities. Each year, the strategy-formulation process begins with the preparation of the Company's Annual Work Plan and Budget (RKAP), which translates the Company's long-term aspirations into measurable programs and targets. In this process, the Board of Directors is responsible for identifying growth opportunities, anticipating market dynamics, and establishing strategic priorities that align with shareholder expectations. Furthermore, the Board of Directors ensures that the principles of sustainability and innovation are embedded in every strategic policy, so that the Company's growth is not only focused on financial performance but also on creating long-term value for patients, customers, and other stakeholders.

Throughout the 2025 Financial Year, the Company's strategy was formulated based on three key pillars. First, strengthening the core business and the Company's portfolio of leading brands to ensure they remain the primary drivers of growth. Second, fostering innovation through continuous investment in research, the launch of new products, and the development of product licensing collaborations with strategic partners to meet the evolving healthcare needs. Third, enhancing operational efficiency through cost discipline, digitalization initiatives, and the reinforcement of the national distribution network.

These three pillars were then translated into specific strategies for each business segment, namely:

- Consumer Health, focusing on enhancing brand awareness, driving new product innovation, expanding healthcare professional reach, increasing penetration in hospitals and clinics, expanding distribution coverage, and strengthening e-commerce channels the retail sector, particularly pharmacies.
- Distribution, through PT Parit Padang Global (PPG), by implementing direct and sub-distributor expansion strategies, digitalizing the supply chain, and adding new principals.
- Alliance (Licensing), through portfolio renewal, accelerated product registration processes, and exploration of strategic partnership opportunities to expand capabilities and product offerings.
- International Business, by strengthening the commercialization of innovative products through existing distributors and exploring new market partnerships.

Furthermore, the Board of Directors, both individually and together with the President Director, is responsible for designing, developing, implementing, and monitoring the Company's short-term and long-term business strategies. These responsibilities include strategic planning, business development, marketing strategy, sales functions, and managing relationships with external stakeholders. The Board of Directors also conducts continuous performance monitoring, reviews the effectiveness of strategy implementation, and ensures that all initiatives are aligned with good corporate governance (GCG) principles and applicable policies.

Direksi memegang peran sentral dalam merumuskan arah strategis dan kebijakan Perseroan yang menjadi pedoman utama bagi seluruh kegiatan usaha. Setiap tahun, proses perumusan strategi diawali melalui penyusunan Rencana Kerja dan Anggaran Perseroan (RKAP) yang menerjemahkan aspirasi jangka panjang perusahaan ke dalam program kerja dan target yang terukur. Dalam perumusan ini, Direksi bertanggung jawab untuk mengidentifikasi peluang pertumbuhan, mengantisipasi dinamika pasar, serta menetapkan prioritas yang selaras dengan harapan pemegang saham. Selain itu, Direksi menanamkan prinsip keberlanjutan dan inovasi ke dalam setiap kebijakan strategis agar pertumbuhan Perseroan tidak hanya berorientasi pada kinerja finansial tetapi juga pada penciptaan nilai jangka panjang bagi pasien, pelanggan, dan pemangku kepentingan lainnya.

Sepanjang Tahun Buku 2025, strategi Perseroan dirumuskan berdasarkan tiga pilar utama. Pertama, memperkuat bisnis inti dan portofolio merek unggulan Perseroan agar tetap menjadi penggerak utama pertumbuhan. Kedua, mendorong inovasi melalui investasi berkelanjutan dalam riset dan pengembangan, peluncuran produk baru, serta pengembangan kerja sama lisensi produk dengan mitra strategis guna memenuhi kebutuhan kesehatan yang terus berkembang. Ketiga, meningkatkan efisiensi operasional melalui disiplin biaya, digitalisasi, serta penguatan jaringan distribusi nasional.

Ketiga pilar tersebut kemudian diturunkan ke dalam strategi spesifik pada masing-masing segmen usaha, yaitu:

- Consumer Health, dengan fokus pada peningkatan brand awareness, inovasi produk baru, perluasan jangkauan tenaga kesehatan, peningkatan penetrasi di rumah sakit dan klinik, penguatan, perluasan distribusi, dan penguatan kanal e-commerce serta sektor ritel khususnya apotek.
- Distribusi, melalui PT Parit Padang Global (PPG), dengan strategi ekspansi distribusi langsung dan sub-distributor, digitalisasi rantai pasok, serta penambahan prinsipal baru.
- Alliance (Licensing), melalui pembaruan portofolio transaksi, percepatan proses pendaftaran produk, serta eksplorasi peluang kemitraan strategis untuk memperluas kapabilitas dan portofolio produk.
- International Business, dengan memperkuat komersialisasi produk inovatif melalui distributor yang ada serta menjajaki kerja sama di negara baru.

Lebih lanjut, Direksi, baik secara individual maupun bersama dengan Presiden Direktur, bertanggung jawab untuk merancang, mengembangkan, melaksanakan, serta memantau strategi bisnis jangka pendek dan jangka panjang Perseroan. Tanggung jawab ini mencakup perencanaan strategis, pengembangan bisnis, strategi pemasaran, fungsi penjualan, serta hubungan dengan pemangku kepentingan eksternal. Direksi juga melakukan pemantauan kinerja secara berkesinambungan, meninjau efektivitas implementasi strategi, serta memastikan seluruh inisiatif selaras dengan tata kelola yang baik (GCG) dan kebijakan yang berlaku.
