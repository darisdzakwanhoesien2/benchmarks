# Ignasius Jonan

## President Commissioner and Independent Commissioner / Presiden Komisaris dan Komisaris Independen

### Concurrent Positions

- Independent Commissioner – PT Unilever Indonesia Tbk, from 2020 to date;
- Chairman and Independent Commissioner – PT Anabatic Technologies Tbk, from 2021 to 2026***;
- Chairman – Marsh Indonesia from 2022 to date;
- Independent Commissioner – PT United Tractors Tbk, from 2025 to date.

Other than the position(s) described above, Mr. Ignasius Jonan does not hold any concurrent positions at PT Soho Global Health Tbk or in any other companies in Indonesia.

### Affiliate Relations

Mr. Ignasius Jonan has no financial, management or family relationships with other members of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners, as well as the Main Beneficial Owner of PT. Soho Global Health Tbk.

### Ownership of the Company's Shares

Mr. Ignasius Jonan does not hold any shares in PT Soho Global Health, Tbk.

***Mr. Ignasius Jonan has resigned from his position as President Commissioner and Independent Commissioner of PT Anabatic Technologies Tbk, and such resignation has been accepted at the Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders (EGMS) of PT Anabatic Technologies Tbk held on 27 March 2026. However, the resignation shall become effective at the nearest Annual General Meeting of Shareholders (AGMS) of PT Anabatic Technologies Tbk.
