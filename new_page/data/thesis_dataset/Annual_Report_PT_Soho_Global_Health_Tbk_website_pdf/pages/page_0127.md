# f. On-the-Job Training
## On-the-Job Training

The Company conducts on-the-job training as part of its strategy to develop employees' competencies in a practical and application-oriented manner. On-the-job training provides employees with the opportunity to learn their duties and responsibilities directly within the workplace, under the guidance of supervisors, mentors, or more experienced colleagues.

The on-the-job training program enables employees to gain a thorough understanding of work processes, apply knowledge acquired through formal training, and tangibly enhance their technical and operational skills.

Through on-the-job training, the Company aims to accelerate the adaptation of new employees, improve operational efficiency, and strengthen the competencies required to support the sustainable achievement of its business objectives

Perusahaan menyelenggarakan on-the-job training sebagai bagian dari strategi pengembangan kompetensi Karyawan secara praktis dan aplikatif. On-the-job training memberikan kesempatan kepada Karyawan untuk mempelajari tugas dan tanggung jawab mereka secara langsung di lingkungan kerja, dengan bimbingan dari atasan, mentor, atau rekan yang lebih berpengalaman.

Program on-the-job training memungkinkan Karyawan untuk memahami proses kerja secara mendalam, menerapkan pengetahuan yang diperoleh dari pelatihan formal, serta meningkatkan keterampilan teknis dan operasional secara nyata.

Melalui on-the-job training, Perusahaan bertujuan untuk mempercepat adaptasi Karyawan baru, meningkatkan efisiensi operasional, serta memperkuat kompetensi yang diperlukan untuk mendukung pencapaian tujuan bisnis secara berkelanjutan.

# g. Knowledge Sharing Sessions

The Company also actively conducts knowledge-sharing sessions as a means of collaborative learning and the exchange of knowledge among employees.
