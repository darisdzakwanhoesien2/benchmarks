Furthermore, we have reinforced our Cyber Security Code Review as a mandatory protocol for all SGH applications. By integrating security assessments directly into the development lifecycle, we identify and remediate vulnerabilities at the source, ensuring our digital platforms are "secure by design" before they reach our users and partners.

## Human Resilience

Finally, we recognize that human awareness is the ultimate shield. Every SGH employee is required to complete and pass our annual cybersecurity training. Achieving a 100% completion rate not only fosters a culture of vigilance but also contributes to our strong Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) performance, ensuring that SGH remains a resilient and trusted leader in the industry.

## Whistleblowing System [GRI 2-26]

We have established platforms for anonymously reporting non-compliance and violations of our core values. This system assures no retaliation to the reporting party and appreciation is communicated for reporting any actual or potential violations.

The cases reported are filtered as some matters should be handled by other business units (i.e. HRD). Updates are provided to the reporting party regarding processed cases to show action about the report raise.

## Code of Ethics and Conduct Training

The Company conducts an annual mandatory training program on the Code of Ethics and Business Conduct for all employees. The program includes a scored assessment to ensure that employees understand and are able to apply the principles set out in the Code.

The training covers key compliance and integrity topics, including conflicts of interest, anti-bribery and anti-corruption, mutual respect in the workplace, personal data protection, health and safety, and ethical marketing practices. Through this program, the Company seeks to strengthen awareness and reinforce a culture of integrity and responsible conduct across the organization.

Participation in this training is mandatory for all employees to ensure consistent understanding and adherence to the Company's ethical standards.

In addition, the Company expects its business partners and counterparties to acknowledge and comply with the principles outlined in the Company's Code of Ethics and Business Conduct as part of maintaining responsible and ethical business relationships. We ensure business partners comply with our Code of Ethics and Business Conduct by including binding clauses in our contractual agreements, which triggers penalties, sanctions and/or business termination upon breach.

## Pelatihan Kode Etik dan Perilaku

Perusahaan menyelenggarakan program pelatihan tahunan yang wajib diikuti oleh seluruh karyawan mengenai Kode Etik dan Perilaku Bisnis. Program ini dilengkapi dengan evaluasi berbasis penilaian untuk memastikan bahwa karyawan memahami serta mampu menerapkan prinsip-prinsip yang tercantum dalam Kode Etik tersebut.

Materi pelatihan mencakup berbagai topik penting terkait kepatuhan dan integritas, antara lain benturan kepentingan, anti-penyuapan dan anti-korupsi, saling menghormati di tempat kerja, perlindungan data pribadi, kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja, serta praktik pemasaran yang beretika. Melalui program ini, perusahaan berupaya meningkatkan kesadaran serta memperkuat budaya integritas dan perilaku yang bertanggung jawab di seluruh organisasi.

Partisipasi dalam pelatihan ini bersifat wajib bagi seluruh karyawan guna memastikan adanya pemahaman yang konsisten serta kepatuhan terhadap standar etika yang ditetapkan oleh perusahaan.

Selain itu, perusahaan juga mengharapkan seluruh mitra usaha dan pihak rekanan untuk memahami, mengakui, serta mematuhi prinsip-prinsip yang tercantum dalam Kode Etik dan Perilaku Bisnis perusahaan sebagai bagian dari upaya menjaga hubungan bisnis yang bertanggung jawab dan beretika.
