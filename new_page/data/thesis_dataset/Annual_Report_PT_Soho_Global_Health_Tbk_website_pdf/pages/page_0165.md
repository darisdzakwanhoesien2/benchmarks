|  Independency
Independensi | Managing the Company independently with an appropriate balance of power and in such a manner that no single Company organ shall dominate the others.

Ensuring that every decision-making process and the implementation of duties and responsibilities are carried out independently, objectively, professionally, and without any conflict of interest and intervention or pressure from any party, so that every decision shall be made solely to the full benefit of the Company.

Mengelola Perseroan secara mandiri dengan keseimbangan kekuatan yang sesuai dan sedemikian rupa sehingga tidak ada satu organ Perseroan pun yang dapat mendominasi yang lain.

Memastikan setiap proses pengambilan keputusan serta pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab dilakukan secara mandiri, objektif, profesional, dan tanpa ada benturan kepentingan dan intervensi atau tekanan dari pihak mana pun, sehingga setiap keputusan harus diambil semata-mata sepenuhnya untuk kepentingan Perseroan. | i) Creating professionalism in managing the Company;
ii) Respecting the rights, obligations, duties, authority and responsibilities of all Company organs; and
iii) Avoid or minimize the occurrence of conflicts of interest in decision making by the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors and all employees, and upholding the principle of independency for the best interests of the Company in every decision making.

i) Menciptakan profesionalisme dalam mengelola Perseroan;
ii) Menghormati hak, kewajiban, tugas, wewenang serta tanggung jawab seluruh organ Perseroan; dan
iii) Menghindari atau meminimalkan terjadinya benturan kepentingan dalam pengambilan keputusan oleh Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, dan seluruh karyawan, dan menjunjung tinggi prinsip independensi untuk kepentingan terbaik Perseroan dalam setiap pengambilan keputusan.  |
|  Fairness
Kewajaran | Always consider the interests of the shareholders and stakeholders based on the principles of fairness and equality and in accordance with applicable laws and regulations, which includes ensuring the fulfillment of the rights and interests of stakeholders, as well as providing fair and equal treatment for all stakeholders, including providing opportunities for all stakeholders to share their input and opinions.

Senantiasa memperhatikan kepentingan pemegang saham dan pemangku kepentingan berdasarkan prinsip keadilan dan kesetaraan serta sesuai dengan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku, termasuk menjamin pemenuhan hak dan kepentingan dari para pemangku kepentingan, serta memberikan perlakuan yang adil dan setara bagi seluruh pemangku kepentingan, termasuk memberikan kesempatan bagi seluruh pemangku kepentingan untuk memberikan masukan dan pendapat. | i) Provide opportunities for shareholders to attend and share their input during the General Meeting Shareholder (“GMS”) in accordance with the applicable regulations;
ii) Respect the rights of minority shareholders in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations;
iii) Treat all business partners in a fair and transparent manner;
iv) Implement the principles of equality and fairness in fulfilling the rights of each stakeholder, both arising under agreements and applicable laws and regulations;
v) Conduct an Annual Employee Engagement Survey which aims to explore the employees’ perception about factors that strengthen employee relationships with the Company, where the survey results will be followed up with improvements in the organization; and
vi) Foster a conducive atmosphere and working environment for all employees.

i) Memberikan kesempatan kepada pemegang saham untuk menghadiri dan memberikan pendapat di dalam Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (“RUPS”) sesuai peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku;
ii) Menghormati hak pemegang saham minoritas sesuai dengan ketentuan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku;
iii) Memperlakukan seluruh mitra kerja secara adil dan transparan;  |
