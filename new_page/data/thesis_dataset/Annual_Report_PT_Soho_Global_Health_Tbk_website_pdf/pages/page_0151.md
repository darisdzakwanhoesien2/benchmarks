# Other payables

As of 31 December 2025, the Company recorded other payables of Rp 509.4 billion, a significant increase of 560.5% compared to the prior year. This increase was primarily attributable to the recognition of a liability for the interim dividend distribution for the financial year 2025 amounting to Rp 420 billion, which was declared in December 2025 and subsequently paid in January 2026.

# Non-current Liabilities

In 2025, the Company's long-term liabilities amounted to Rp198.1 billion, experiencing a 16.4% increase. This rise was primarily due to an increase in employee benefit obligations.
