## Responsible Supply Chain Management [GRI 308-1 &amp;2, 414-1&amp;2]

The Company implements responsible supply chain practices in line with GRI 308 (Environmental Assessment of Suppliers) and GRI 414 (Social Assessment of Suppliers). All suppliers are required to sign a Sustainability Code of Conduct and are contractually bound by environmental, labor, and human rights clauses embedded within supplier agreements. As of 2025, 90.8% of active suppliers have signed the Sustainability Code of Conduct.

Employees within the procurement function receive training on sustainability issues in the supply chain to support the consistent application of responsible sourcing practices. The Company conducts environmental and social assessments of suppliers through structured questionnaires as part of its supplier screening and evaluation process. These assessments enable the identification of potential environmental and social risks and inform follow-up actions where necessary.
