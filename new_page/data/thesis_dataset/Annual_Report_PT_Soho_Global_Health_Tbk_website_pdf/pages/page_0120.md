# Employee Training and Development

## Pelatihan &amp; Pengembangan Karyawan

Training and development represent a long-term investment that plays a vital role in strengthening individual capabilities as well as the organization's overall competitiveness. By continuously updated skills, employees are able to work more effectively, adapt to organizational changes, respond swiftly to increasingly competitive market dynamics, and contribute to the Company's long-term sustainability.

At SGH Group, training and development initiatives are not limited to the employees itself, but also extend to freelancers, interns, fieldwork and on-the-job training participants, as well as outsourced personnel.

Throughout 2025, the Company conducted training and development programs for its workforce with a total duration of 16,834 hours. Based on these total training hours, by the end of 2025 each individual received on average, training and development opportunities amounting to 5.5 hours per individual per year.

Pelatihan dan pengembangan merupakan investasi jangka panjang yang berperan penting dalam meningkatkan kemampuan individu maupun daya saing organisasi. Dengan keterampilan yang diperbarui secara konsisten, karyawan akan mampu bekerja lebih efektif, adaptif terhadap perubahan organisasi, sigap menghadapi dinamika pasar yang semakin kompetitif, serta menjaga keberlanjutan Perusahaan.

Pelatihan dan pengembangan di SGH Group tidak hanya ditujukan bagi karyawan, namun juga bagi pekerja lepas, peserta magang dan peserta praktik kerja lapangan, hingga tenaga alih daya.

Selama tahun 2025, Perusahaan telah menyelenggarakan pelatihan dan pengembangan bagi tenaga kerja dengan total durasi mencapai 16.834 jam. Berdasarkan total jam pelatihan tersebut, setiap individu di akhir 2025, rata-rata mendapatkan kesempatan untuk mengikuti pelatihan dan pengembangan sebesar 5,5 jam per individu per tahun.

Training and development programs are designed based on the specific needs of each business unit in a respective year. Various training activities are conducted internally by the Human Resources Department in collaboration with the leaders of each Business Unit, as well as in partnership with external parties. Internal training is generally carried out as part of a knowledge and transfer of knowledge process from leaders or more experienced employees to those who require further development. External training is intended for employees who require specialized competencies or professional knowledge and expertise that cannot yet be facilitated internally. In this regard, the Company collaborates with external partners or professional training service providers to ensure that the materials delivered are relevant, up to date, and aligned with the needs of the respective period.

The types of training and development programs offered are also diverse, ranging from the enhancement of technical skills such as presentation skills, complaint handling, and the utilization of technology, to specialized training that supports leadership development. In addition, the Company promotes a culture of self-learning through access to digital learning platforms, webinars, and employee knowledge-sharing sessions, ensuring that learning takes place not only in formal settings but also as part of daily work activities.

Pelatihan dan pengembangan di-desain berdasarkan kebutuhan masing-masing unit bisnis pada tahun tertentu. Berbagai pelatihan dilakukan secara internal oleh Departemen Sumber Daya Manusia berkolaborasi dengan para pimpinan dari masing-masing Unit Bisnis, dan juga bekerjasama dengan pihak eksternal. Pelatihan internal pada umumnya dilakukan sebagai bagian dari proses mentransfer pengetahuan dan pengalaman kerja dari pimpinan atau karyawan yang lebih ahli kepada yang masih membutuhkan pelatihan. Pelatihan eksternal ditujukan bagi karyawan yang membutuhkan kompetensi khusus ataupun pengetahuan dan keahlian profesional yang belum dapat difasilitasi secara internal. Dalam hal ini Perusahaan bekerja sama dengan mitra eksternal atau penyedia jasa pelatihan profesional guna memastikan materi yang diberikan relevan, mutakhir, dan sesuai dengan kebutuhan pada periode yang dimaksud.

Jenis pelatihan dan pengembangan yang diselenggarakan juga beragam, mulai dari penguatan keterampilan teknis seperti presentasi; penanganan keluhan; pemanfaatan teknologi; hingga pelatihan khusus yang mendukung kepemimpinan. Selain itu, Perusahaan juga mendorong budaya belajar mandiri melalui akses ke kanal pembelajaran digital, webinar, dan sesi berbagi pengetahuan antar karyawan, sehingga pembelajaran tidak hanya berlangsung di ruangan tetapi juga dalam keseharian pekerjaan.
