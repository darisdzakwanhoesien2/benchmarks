Through this diversified structure, the Company ensures that each member of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors is motivated to achieve strategic targets without disregarding the principles of prudence and sound risk management. In addition, the remuneration procedures, basis of determination, and remuneration structure are established entirely based on the results of the annual performance evaluation of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors.

Melalui struktur yang terdiversifikasi ini, Perusahaan memastikan bahwa setiap anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi termotivasi untuk mencapai target strategis tanpa mengabaikan prinsip kehati-hatian dan manajemen risiko yang sehat. Selain itu, prosedur remunerasi, dasar penentuan, serta struktur remunerasi tersebut ditetapkan sepenuhnya berdasarkan hasil evaluasi kinerja tahunan Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi.
