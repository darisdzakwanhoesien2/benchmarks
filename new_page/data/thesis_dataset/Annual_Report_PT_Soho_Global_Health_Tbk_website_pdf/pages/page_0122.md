The SGH Webinar Series is implemented flexibly, either through fully online sessions to reach employees across multiple locations, or through hybrid formats that combine in-person and virtual participation. This initiative demonstrates the Company's commitment to fostering a dynamic work environment in which every individual is given the opportunity to grow and contribute their best to the Company.
