# The Process undertaken by the Board of Directors to ensure the Implementation of the Company's Strategy

Proses yang dilakukan Direksi untuk Memastikan Implementasi Strategi Perseroan

To ensure that the strategies formulated can be executed consistently and effectively, the Board of Directors implements structured control and monitoring mechanisms. The strategies and policies outlined in the Company's Annual Work Plan and Budget (RKAP) are further translated into detailed work programs at the divisional and operational levels, each equipped with clear performance indicators. The achievement of these programs is measured through the Personal Scorecards of each member of the Board of Directors, which include key performance indicators aligned with the Company's objectives.

The Board of Directors conducts regular monthly reviews of financial and operational results, as well as quarterly evaluations of strategic priority achievements. The evaluation results are discussed collegially to assess alignment between strategy and realization, while also determining corrective actions in the event of any deviation. In addition, the internal audit and risk management functions provide independent assessments to ensure that strategy implementation remains consistent with established policies and complies with prevailing laws and regulations.

The strategy implementation process also includes annual business strategy reviews, continuous monitoring and tracking of the Company's performance, strategic analyses, and the execution of business development initiatives. The Board of Directors, both individually and in collaboration with the President Director, works together to ensure that the established strategies are executed consistently across all key functions, including finance, operations, marketing, and business development.

Furthermore, the Board of Directors maintains ongoing communication with stakeholders—including employees, business partners, healthcare professionals, and regulators—to ensure that the adopted strategies are implemented comprehensively throughout the organization. Untuk memastikan strategi yang telah dirumuskan dapat dijalankan secara konsisten dan efektif, Direksi menerapkan mekanisme pengendalian dan pemantauan yang terstruktur. Strategi dan kebijakan yang dituangkan dalam RKAP dijabarkan ke dalam program kerja rinci di tingkat divisi dan operasional, masing-masing dilengkapi dengan indikator kinerja yang jelas. Pencapaian program tersebut diukur menggunakan Personal Scorecard masing-masing anggota Direksi, yang memuat indikator kinerja utama sesuai dengan sasaran Perseroan.

Direksi secara rutin melakukan tinjauan bulanan atas hasil keuangan dan operasional serta evaluasi triwulanan terhadap pencapaian prioritas strategis. Hasil evaluasi dibahas secara kolegial untuk menilai kesesuaian antara strategi dan realisasi, sekaligus menetapkan langkah korektif bila terdapat deviasi. Selain itu, fungsi audit internal dan manajemen risiko memberikan penilaian independen guna memastikan pelaksanaan strategi tetap konsisten dengan kebijakan yang telah ditetapkan serta sesuai dengan peraturan perundang-undangan.

Proses implementasi strategi juga dilakukan melalui tinjauan tahunan strategi bisnis, pemantauan dan pelacakan kinerja Perseroan, analisis strategis, serta pelaksanaan inisiatif pengembangan bisnis. Direksi, baik secara individu maupun bersama Presiden Direktur, berkolaborasi untuk memastikan strategi yang ditetapkan terlaksana secara konsisten di seluruh fungsi utama, meliputi keuangan, operasional, pemasaran, maupun pengembangan bisnis.

Selain itu, Direksi juga menjaga komunikasi berkelanjutan dengan para pemangku kepentingan, termasuk karyawan, mitra usaha, tenaga kesehatan, dan regulator, untuk memastikan bahwa strategi yang ditetapkan dapat terimplementasi secara menyeluruh di seluruh organisasi.
