# 2026 Strategies

In 2026, the Company will continue to strengthen the Consumer Health segment through the following strategic initiatives:

- Optimizing targeted engagement with healthcare professionals while expanding sales channel coverage.
- Strengthening digital marketing and e-commerce capabilities to enhance brand awareness, consideration, and product trial through collaborations with influencers, marketplaces, modern trade partners, online pharmacies, and reseller networks.
- Strengthening growth in retail and pharmacy channels as key drivers of OTC product sales.
- Expanding both direct and indirect distribution to enhance performance in pharmacy channels while strengthening presence in modern trade.
- Optimizing the launch and commercialization of new products, particularly through digital and e-commerce channels.

# Strategi 2026

Untuk tahun 2026, Perseroan akan terus memperkuat pengembangan segmen Kesehatan Konsumen melalui beberapa inisiatif strategis berikut:

- Mengoptimalkan cakupan tenaga profesional kesehatan secara lebih terarah sekaligus memperluas jangkauan saluran penjualan.
- Memperkuat digital marketing dan e-commerce guna meningkatkan brand awareness, consideration, dan trial produk melalui kolaborasi dengan influencer, marketplace, mitra modern trade, apotek online, serta jaringan reseller.
- Memperkuat pertumbuhan bisnis di kanal ritel dan apotek sebagai pendorong utama penjualan produk OTC.
- Memperluas distribusi langsung dan tidak langsung guna meningkatkan kinerja di kanal farmasi sekaligus memperkuat kehadiran di modern trade.
- Mengoptimalkan peluncuran dan komersialisasi produk baru, terutama melalui kanal digital dan e-commerce.
