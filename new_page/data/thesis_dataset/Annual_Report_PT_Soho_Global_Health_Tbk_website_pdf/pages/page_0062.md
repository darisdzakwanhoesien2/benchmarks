# PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) Signs Distribution Cooperation Agreement with PT Ichiya Indonesia

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) Tandatangani Perjanjian Kerjasama Distribusi dengan PT Ichiya Indonesia (brand: Okamoto)

![img-73.jpeg](img-73.jpeg)

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG), a subsidiary of PT Soho Global Health, Tbk, has agreed to enter into a distribution partnership with PT Ichiya Indonesia. The agreement was formalized through the signing of a cooperation contract by the respective company leaders, namely Edwin Vega Darma, VP Sales &amp; Marketing of PPG, and Ms. Phinnywati Chandra, Director of Ichiya Indonesia. The signing was also witnessed by Pandy Harianto, VP Operations &amp; Supply Chain of PPG, and Josie Jayasaputra Kusmayadi, Legal Manager of PT Soho Global Health.

The signing ceremony took place on April 28, 2025, in the lobby of PT Soho Global Health, located in Pulogadung. The event was also attended by Vicki Goutama, Head of Marketing PPG, along with the Board of Directors of PT Ichiya Indonesia and key team members from both companies who played important roles and committed to working together.

The Okamoto brand, distributed by PT Ichiya Indonesia, has been present in the Indonesian market since 2009, with one of its flagship products being OKAMOTO 003 PLATINUM 2's.

In her remarks, the Director of PT Ichiya Indonesia expressed her confidence that the distribution partnership with PPG would enhance opportunities for mutual growth and development. "We believe that this cooperation marks an important milestone for the Okamoto brand to grow even faster in Indonesia," said Ms. Phinnywati Chandra, Director of PT Ichiya Indonesia.

PPG has more than 65 years of experience and is recognized as one of the top 5 distribution and logistics companies for healthcare products in Indonesia. "This partnership reflects PPG's interest and confidence in the growth of the pharmaceutical distribution industry. We are proud to distribute the premium Okamoto brand. In the future, this brand will complement its product portfolio in the market, which will further enhance its growth potential," said Edwin Vega, VP Sales &amp; Marketing of PPG.

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG), anak perusahaan PT Soho Global Health, Tbk sepakat melakukan perjanjian kerja sama distribusi dengan PT Ichiya Indonesia. Kesepakatan tersebut dikukuhkan melalui penandatanganan perjanjian kerja sama yang dilakukan oleh masing-masing pimpinan perusahaan, yaitu Edwin Vega Darma Selaku VP Sales &amp; Marketing PPG dan Ibu Phinnywati Chandra, Director Ichiya Indonesia serta disaksikan oleh Pandy Harianto, VP Operations &amp; Supply Chain PPG, Josie Jayasaputra Kusmayadi, Legal Manager PT Soho Global Health.

Penandatanganan kerjasama ini dilaksanakan pada tanggal 28 April 2025 bertempat di lobby PT Soho Global Health, berlokasi di Pulogadung. Dalam acara ini turut hadir Vicki Goutama Selaku Head of Marketing PPG dan jajaran Direksi PT Ichiya Indonesia beserta seluruh team yang berperan penting dari masing-masing perusahaan untuk berkomitmen menjalankan kemitraan bersama.

Brand Okamoto yang dibawa oleh PT Ichiya Indonesia ini telah hadir di pasar Indonesia sejak tahun 2009 dengan salah satu produk unggulannya OKAMOTO 003 PLATINUM 2's.

Pada sambutannya Direktur PT Ichiya Indonesia menyatakan yakin dengan kemitraan distribusi dengan PT PPG ini akan meningkatkan peluang tumbuh dan berkembang bersama PT PPG dan PT Ichiya. "Kami percaya titik awal kerjasama ini menjadi milestone yang penting bagi brand Okamoto untuk berkembang lebih pesat di Indonesia," ujar Ibu Pinnywati Chandra, Direktur PT Ichiya Indonesia.

PPG telah berpengalaman dan dipercaya lebih dari 65 tahun sebagai top 5 perusahaan distribusi dan logistik produk kesehatan di Indonesia. "Kerjasama ini menunjukkan ketertarikan dan keyakinan PPG terhadap pertumbuhan industri Distribusi Farmasi dan kami bangga mendistribusikan premium brand Okamoto. Brand ke depan akan melengkapi range product portofolio nya di market sehingga potensi pertumbuhan nya pun makin besar jika portofolio nya dilengkapi," ujar Edwin Vega, VP Sales &amp; Marketing PPG.
