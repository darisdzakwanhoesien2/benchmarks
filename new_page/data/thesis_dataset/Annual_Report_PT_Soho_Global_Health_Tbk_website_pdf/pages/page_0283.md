# Sustainability Governance

## Tata Kelola Keberlanjutan

## Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners duties on sustainable finance [POJK E.2, GRI 2-24]

Soho Global Health is committed to implementing strong and integrated corporate governance that incorporates Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) considerations into the Company's strategy and operations. The Board of Directors oversees the implementation of sustainability initiatives and ensures that ESG considerations are integrated into the Company's business processes and decision-making. The Board of Commissioners provides strategic oversight and monitors the effectiveness of the Company's sustainability governance framework.

To support the implementation of our ESG strategy, the Company has established an internal ESG governance structure consisting of the ESG Executive Committee and the ESG Functional Committee.

The ESG Executive Committee, led by the Chief Executive Officer, provides strategic direction and supervision over the implementation of the Company's ESG Management System. The committee also reviews the adequacy of resources allocated to achieve the Company's ESG objectives and sustainability targets.

Reporting to the ESG Executive Committee is the ESG Functional Committee, which is led by four ESG Officers representing relevant business functions. The ESG Functional Committee is responsible for coordinating, monitoring, and implementing ESG-related initiatives across the organization, ensuring alignment with the Company's sustainability strategy and commitments.

The ESG Functional Committee holds monthly meetings to monitor the progress of ESG initiatives and address operational matters. The ESG Executive Committee convenes quarterly meetings to review progress, align strategic priorities, and evaluate the implementation of sustainability initiatives. Updates and key developments from these committees are subsequently reported to the Board as part of the Company's governance and oversight process.

Through this governance structure, Soho Global Health ensures that sustainability considerations are systematically integrated into its operations while maintaining accountability, transparency, and effective oversight at all levels of the organization.

## Tugas Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris di bidang keuangan berkelanjutan [POJK E.2, GRI 2-24]

Soho Global Health berkomitmen untuk menerapkan tata kelola perusahaan yang kuat dan terintegrasi dengan memasukkan pertimbangan Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) ke dalam strategi dan operasional perusahaan. Direksi bertanggung jawab untuk mengawasi pelaksanaan inisiatif keberlanjutan serta memastikan bahwa aspek ESG terintegrasi dalam proses bisnis dan pengambilan keputusan perusahaan. Dewan Komisaris menjalankan fungsi pengawasan strategis terhadap implementasi kebijakan keberlanjutan serta memantau efektivitas kerangka tata kelola keberlanjutan perusahaan.

Untuk mendukung pelaksanaan strategi ESG, perusahaan membentuk struktur tata kelola internal yang terdiri dari ESG Executive Committee dan ESG Functional Committee.

ESG Executive Committee dipimpin oleh Chief Executive Officer dan bertanggung jawab untuk memberikan arahan strategis serta melakukan pengawasan terhadap implementasi ESG Management System perusahaan. Komite ini juga meninjau kecukupan sumber daya yang dialokasikan untuk mencapai tujuan dan target keberlanjutan perusahaan.

Di bawah ESG Executive Committee terdapat ESG Functional Committee yang dipimpin oleh empat ESG Officers yang mewakili fungsi-fungsi utama perusahaan. ESG Functional Committee bertugas untuk mengoordinasikan, memantau, serta melaksanakan berbagai inisiatif ESG di seluruh organisasi, guna memastikan keselarasan dengan strategi dan komitmen keberlanjutan perusahaan.

ESG Functional Committee menyelenggarakan rapat bulanan untuk memantau perkembangan pelaksanaan inisiatif ESG serta membahas berbagai hal operasional terkait. Sementara itu, ESG Executive Committee menyelenggarakan rapat triwulanan untuk meninjau kemajuan program, menyelaraskan prioritas strategis, serta mengevaluasi implementasi berbagai inisiatif keberlanjutan. Hasil pembahasan dan perkembangan penting dari kedua komite tersebut selanjutnya dilaporkan kepada Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris sebagai bagian dari mekanisme pengawasan dan tata kelola perusahaan.

Melalui struktur tata kelola ini, Soho Global Health memastikan bahwa pertimbangan keberlanjutan terintegrasi secara sistematis dalam operasional perusahaan, sekaligus menjaga akuntabilitas, transparansi, dan efektivitas pengawasan di seluruh tingkat organisasi.
