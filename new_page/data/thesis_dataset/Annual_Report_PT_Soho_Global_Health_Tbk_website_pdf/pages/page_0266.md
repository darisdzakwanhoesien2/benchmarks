|  4. | Public Company shall have policies on communication with shareholders or investors.

Perusahaan Terbuka memiliki suatu kebijakan komunikasi dengan pemegang saham atau investor. | The recommendation has been implemented.

The Company has a policy related to communication with shareholders and investors as stated in the Company Policy. The policy regulates communication guidelines, procedures for the process of disclosing information, media and other forms of communication with investors.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.

Perseroan memiliki kebijakan terkait komunikasi dengan pemegang saham dan investor yang tertuang dalam Kebijakan Perusahaan. Kebijakan tersebut mengatur mengenai pedoman komunikasi, tata cara proses pengungkapan informasi, media serta bentuk-bentuk komunikasi yang dilakukan dengan investor.  |
|  5. | Public Company shall announce its policies on communication with the shareholders and investors on its website.

Perusahaan Terbuka mengungkapkan kebijakan komunikasi Perusahaan Terbuka dengan pemegang saham atau investor dalam Situs Web. | The recommendation has been implemented.

The Company has disclosed the Public Company's communication policy with shareholders or investors in the Company's Policy on the Company's Website.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.

Perseroan telah mengungkapkan kebijakan komunikasi Perusahaan Terbuka dengan pemegang saham atau investor dalam Kebijakan Perseroan di Situs Web Perseroan.  |

# Principles :

Strengthening the Membership and Composition of the Board of Commissioners

# Prinsip :

Memperkuat Keanggotaan dan Komposisi Dewan Komisaris

|  No | Recommendations
Rekomendasi | Remarks
Keterangan  |

|  6. | Decisions on the number of members of the Board of Commissioners should consider the condition of the respective Public Company.

Penentuan jumlah anggota Dewan Komisaris mempertimbangkan kondisi Perusahaan Terbuka. | The recommendation has been implemented.

The Company has a Board of Commissioners Charter which regulates the number of members of the Board of Commissioners, where the composition and number of members of the Board of Commissioners is determined by the GMS and must be in accordance with the complexity of the Company's business taking into account the purposes and objectives of the Company as regulated in the Company's Articles of Association, in order to enable appropriate decision making to be effective, precise and fast and act independently.

Furthermore, the Board of Commissioners Charter also stipulates that the number of Independent Commissioners must be at least 30% (thirty percent) of the total members of the Board of Commissioners.

The determination of the number of members of the Board of Commissioners is also regulated in Article 21 paragraph 1 of the Company's Articles of Association, the number of which is adjusted to the requirements of the prevailing laws and regulations in the Capital Market sector. The Board of Commissioners Charter and the Company's Articles of Association are available on the Company's Website.  |
