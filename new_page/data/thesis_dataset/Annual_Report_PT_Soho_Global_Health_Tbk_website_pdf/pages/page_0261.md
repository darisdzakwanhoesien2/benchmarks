Risks Faced by the Company and the Impact on the Company
Risiko yang Dihadapi dan Pengaruhnya terhadap Perseroan

PPG is at risk of not receiving the full payment of the invoices amounting to IDR 304,028,170 (three hundred four million twenty eight thousand one hundred seventy Rupiah).

PPG berisiko tidak akan memperoleh pembayaran atas tagihan tersebut secara penuh sebesar Rp 304.028.170,- (tiga ratus empat juta dua puluh delapan ribu seratus tujuh Rupiah).

Follow-up
Tindak Lanjut

The judge at the Brebes District Court decided that Apotek Ben Waras had made a default which harmed PPG so that Apotek Ben Waras was obliged to pay PPG amount of IDR 304,028,170.

Based on this decision PPG is in the process of filing an Application for Execution before the Brebes District Court.

Hakim di Pengadilan Negeri Brebes memutuskan bahwa Apotek Ben Waras telah melakukan wanprestasi yang merugikan PPG sehingga Apotek Ben Waras wajib membayar PPG sejumlah Rp 304.028.170.

Berdasarkan keputusan ini, PPG sedang dalam proses mengajukan Permohonan Eksekusi di Pengadilan Negeri Brebes.

## 10. PT. Arindipharma Selaras Medika ("AMK")

Case Status
Status Perkara

AMK is one of PPG's customers which purchases PPG products for resale to end customers. Since September of 2023, AMK has stopped making payments for the invoices which has been due and payable to PPG.

AMK merupakan salah satu pelanggan PPG yang membeli produk PPG untuk dijual kembali ke pelanggan akhir. Sejak bulan September tahun 2023, AMK telah berhenti melakukan pembayaran atas tagihan yang telah jatuh tempo dan terutang kepada PPG.

Risks Faced by the Company and the Impact on the Company
Risiko yang Dihadapi dan Pengaruhnya terhadap Perseroan

PPG is at risk of not receiving the full payment of the invoices amounting to IDR 450,830,532 (four hundred fifty million eight hundred thirty thousand five hundred thirty two Rupiah).

PPG berisiko tidak akan memperoleh pembayaran atas tagihan tersebut secara penuh sebesar Rp 450.830.532,- (empat ratus lima puluh juta delapan ratus tiga puluh ribu lima ratus tiga puluh dua Rupiah).

Follow-up
Tindak Lanjut

PPG has submitted a lawsuit to the Bekasi District Court which was resolved through Mutual Settlement (Kesepakatan Perdamaian) signed by both parties on December 6, 2023. However, to date, AMK has only made a few installment payments pursuant to the Mutual Settlement. Currently, PPG is in the process of enforcing the settlement of the remaining receivables.

PPG telah mengajukan gugatan ke Pengadilan Negeri Bekasi dan diselesaikan melalui Kesepakatan Perdamaian yang ditandatangani kedua belah pihak pada 6 Desember 2023. Namun, hingga saat ini, AMK baru melunasi beberapa angsuran sesuai dengan Kesepakatan Perdamaian. Saat ini, PPG sedang dalam proses menindaklanjuti penagihan pembayaran angsuran yang tersisa.
