# Nomination and Remuneration Committee Profile

Profil Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi

## PREAMBLE

The Nomination and Remuneration Committee is appointed based on the Board of Commissioners' decree and is responsible to the Board of Commissioners also acts independently in carrying out their duties and responsibilities.
