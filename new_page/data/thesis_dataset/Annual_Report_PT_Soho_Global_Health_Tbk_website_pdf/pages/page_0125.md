## c. Soft Skills Training

### Pelatihan Soft Skill

The Company also conducted various soft skills training programs as part of its comprehensive human resources development efforts. These programs are aimed at strengthening employees' interpersonal skills, communication abilities, and professional attitudes in order to support work effectiveness, team collaboration, and leadership quality within the Company.

The soft skills training programs are designed to address the needs of a dynamic organization and to foster a productive and integrity-driven workplace culture. The soft skills training programs implemented throughout 2025 included, among others:

a. Becoming Excellent Leader in the Digital Era for employees of the Consumer Healthcare unit
b. Presentation Skills for Star and Potential Talent of the Supply &amp; Operations unit
c. Strategic Leadership for leaders of the Supply &amp; Operations unit
d. Situational Leadership for Leaders of Labor Union Work Units
e. Eye on the Ball, Leadership Training for Responsible Pharmacists, Branch Accounting Supervisors, and Branch Logistics Supervisors
f. Creative Thinking for Star and Potential Talent of the Supply &amp; Operations unit
g. Handling Customer Complaints for employees of the Human Resources Division
h. Communicating with Impact Using the DISC Model for Sales Managers in the Medical Healthcare unit

Perusahaan juga menyelenggarakan berbagai program pelatihan soft skill sebagai bagian dari upaya pengembangan sumber daya manusia secara menyeluruh. Pelatihan ini bertujuan untuk memperkuat kemampuan interpersonal, komunikasi, serta sikap profesional Karyawan dalam mendukung efektivitas kerja, kolaborasi tim, dan kualitas kepemimpinan di lingkungan Perusahaan.

Program pelatihan soft skill dirancang untuk menjawab kebutuhan organisasi yang dinamis dan mendorong terciptanya budaya kerja yang produktif dan berintegritas. Pelatihan soft skill yang dilaksanakan selama tahun 2025, antara lain:

a. Menjadi Pemimpin Unggul di Era Digital bagi Karyawan unit Consumer Healthcare
b. Keterampilan Presentasi bagi Karyawan Unggulan dan Potensial di unit Supply &amp; Operations
c. Kepemimpinan Strategis bagi Pimpinan unit Supply &amp; Operations
d. Kepemimpinan Situasional bagi Pimpinan Unit Kerja Serikat Pekerja
e. Fokus Pada Tujuan, Pelatihan Kepemimpinan bagi Apoteker Penanggung Jawab, Branch Accounting Supervisor, dan Branch Logistics Supervisor
f. Berpikir Kreatif bagi Karyawan Unggulan dan Potensial di unit Supply &amp; Operations
g. Menangani Keluhan Pelanggan bagi Karyawan divisi Sumber Daya Manusia
h. Berkomunikasi dengan Efektif menggunakan Model DISC bagi Sales Manager di unit Medical Healthcare
