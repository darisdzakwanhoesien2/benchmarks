## a. Minimum Wage
## Upah Minimum

SGH Group demonstrates a strong commitment to complying with the regulations of the Government of Indonesia, particularly those concerning the Provincial Minimum Wage (UMP) and the City/Regency Minimum Wage (UMK). The Company ensures that all employees receive remuneration that is not lower than the stipulated UMP/UMK and consistently adheres to annual increases in UMP/UMK in accordance with the respective Provincial Governor Regulations across Indonesia.

SGH Group also implements a Wage Structure and Scale in compliance with applicable laws and regulations. This remuneration structure is based on job level, competency, and individual performance. The Company consistently ensures internal equity and alignment.

The Company is actively committed to periodically reviewing overall wage compliance to ensure adherence to the provisions set by the Government of Indonesia and to maintain fair and competitive remuneration practices. In addition, SGH Group does not differentiate remuneration between male and female employees.

Due to the confidentiality of the remuneration structure, during this reporting period the Company does not disclose the ratio between the highest annual compensation received by an individual and the average total annual compensation of all employees.

SGH Group menunjukkan komitmen tinggi untuk mematuhi peraturan Pemerintah Indonesia, khususnya mengenai Upah Minimum Provinsi (UMP) dan Upah Minimum Kota (UMK). Perusahaan memastikan seluruh karyawan menerima imbal jasa tidak di bawah UMP/UMK yang telah ditetapkan, serta secara konsisten mengikuti kenaikan UMP/UMK dari tahun ke tahun sesuai dengan Peraturan Gubernur masing-masing Provinsi di Indonesia.

SGH Group juga menerapkan Struktur dan Skala Upah sesuai dengan ketentuan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku. Struktur remunerasi ini didasarkan pada tingkat jabatan, kompetensi, dan kinerja masing-masing karyawan. Perusahaan selalu memastikan keadilan dan keselarasan internal.

Perusahaan berkomitmen secara aktif dan berkala meninjau kesesuaian upah secara keseluruhan agar memenuhi ketentuan yang ditetapkan oleh Pemerintah Indonesia dan menjaga praktik remunerasi yang adil dan kompetitif. Selain itu, SGH Group tidak membedakan remunerasi antara karyawan laki-laki dan perempuan.

Karena alasan kerahasiaan struktur remunerasi, pada periode ini Perusahaan tidak melaporkan rasio kompensasi tahunan tertinggi yang diterima individu dibandingkan dengan rata-rata total kompensasi tahunan seluruh karyawan.
