The Board of Directors' assessment of the Committees that support the implementation of the duties of the Board of Directors:

Penilaian Direksi terhadap Komite yang mendukung pelaksanaan tugas Direksi:

Every year, the Company's Board of Directors evaluates the performance of its supporting organs, including the Corporate Secretary and the Internal Audit Unit. The assessment is carried out using a self-assessment system, peer evaluation, or with other systems decided in an internal meeting of the Board of Directors.

In the Year 2025, the Board of Directors assessed that the supporting organs had carried out their respective functions well and in accordance with the purpose of their formation, with details as follows:

1. The Corporate Secretary has carried out the duties, responsibilities, and authorities related to the Corporate Secretary's functions in accordance with the Decree of the Board of Directors of the Company regarding the Appointment of the Corporate Secretary of the Company. During the Year 2025, the attendance rate of the Corporate Secretary in the meetings reached 100%.
2. The Internal Audit Unit has carried out the duties, responsibilities, and authorities related to the Internal Audit Unit functions in accordance with the Decree of the Company's Board of Directors regarding the Company's Internal Audit Unit Charter.
3. During the Year 2025, the attendance rate of members of the Internal Audit Unit in the meetings reached 100%, as explained in more detail in the section on the Internal Audit Unit.

Setiap tahun, Direksi Perseroan melakukan penilaian atas kinerja organ pendukungnya, termasuk juga Sekretaris Perusahaan dan Unit Audit Internal. Penilaian tersebut dilakukan menggunakan sistem penilaian sendiri, evaluasi rekan sejawat, atau dengan sistem lain yang diputuskan dalam rapat internal Direksi.

Pada Tahun 2025, Direksi menilai organ pendukung tersebut telah menjalankan fungsinya masing-masing dengan baik dan sesuai dengan tujuan pembentukannya, dengan rincian sebagai berikut:

1. Sekretaris Perusahaan telah melaksanakan tugas, tanggung jawab, dan wewenang terkait fungsi Sekretaris Perusahaan sesuai dengan Surat Keputusan Direksi Perseroan perihal Pengangkatan Sekretaris Perusahaan Perseroan. Sepanjang Tahun 2025, tingkat kehadiran Sekretaris Perusahaan di dalam rapat mencapai 100%.
2. Unit Audit Internal telah melaksanakan tugas, tanggung jawab, dan wewenang terkait fungsi Unit Audit Internal sesuai dengan Surat Keputusan Direksi Perseroan perihal Piagam Unit Audit Internal Perseroan.
3. Sepanjang Tahun 2025, tingkat kehadiran anggota Unit Audit Internal di dalam rapat mencapai 100%, sebagaimana lebih rinci dijelaskan pada bagian Unit Audit Internal.

Remuneration for the Board of Directors

Remunerasi untuk Direksi

Remuneration reviews were based on the Company's business development and salary surveys. The total salaries and benefits (excluding bonus) paid to the Board of Directors of Group Companies amounted to IDR 31,978,353,682 (thirty-one billion nine hundred seventy-eight million three hundred fifty-three thousand six hundred eighty-two rupiah) in the Year 2025.

Tinjauan remunerasi didasarkan pada pengembangan bisnis Perseroan dan survei gaji. Total gaji dan tunjangan (tidak termasuk bonus) yang dibayarkan kepada Direksi Grup Perseroan berjumlah Rp.31.978.353.682,- (tiga puluh satu miliar sembilan ratus tujuh puluh delapan juta tiga ratus lima puluh tiga ribu enam ratus delapan puluh dua rupiah) pada Tahun 2025.
