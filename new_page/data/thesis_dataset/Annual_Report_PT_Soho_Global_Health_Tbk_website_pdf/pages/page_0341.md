As part of its efforts to improve access to healthcare services, the Company also provides online health consultation services with the Company Doctor. This facility enables employees to obtain medical information and advice quickly and conveniently. To support independent health monitoring, the Company's polyclinic is also equipped with a Body Mass Index (BMI) measurement device, which helps employees better understand their physical health condition and raises awareness of the importance of maintaining an ideal body weight.

The Company has also introduced an innovative initiative through the Medical Talk Series, a health education video program that discusses various medical topics relevant to daily life. This program is a collaboration between the Medical Affairs Department and the Marketing Department and is disseminated to employees through various internal communication channels. The videos are also published on the Company's YouTube channel as part of an effort to broaden access to health information and enhance health literacy within the workplace.

As a preventive measure, SOHO Global Health consistently conducts annual medical check-ups for employees. In addition, the Company organizes mini medical check-ups in collaboration with external partners. These initiatives aim to support the early detection of potential health risks and ensure that employees' health conditions are monitored effectively.

Perusahaan juga menghadirkan inovasi melalui Seri Medical Talk, yaitu video edukasi kesehatan yang membahas berbagai topik medis yang relevan dengan kehidupan sehari-hari. Program ini merupakan hasil kolaborasi antara Departemen Medical Affairs dan Departemen Marketing, dan disebarluaskan kepada karyawan melalui berbagai kanal komunikasi internal. Video tersebut juga dipublikasikan melalui kanal YouTube perusahaan sebagai bagian dari upaya memperluas akses informasi kesehatan serta meningkatkan literasi kesehatan di lingkungan kerja.

Sebagai langkah preventif, SOHO Global Health secara konsisten melaksanakan pemeriksaan kesehatan tahunan bagi karyawan. Inisiatif ini bertujuan untuk mendukung deteksi dini terhadap potensi risiko kesehatan serta memastikan kondisi kesehatan karyawan dapat terpantau secara optimal.
