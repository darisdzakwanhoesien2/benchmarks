|  Yuliana | Director / Direktur
Responsible for Legal, Compliance, Corporate Secretary, and Human Resources Division, amongst others:
(i) to establish strategic directions and provide legal services (including provision of internal advisors), as well as ensure compliance with laws and regulations and risk management processes;
(ii) to determine the design, implementation, supervision and enforcement of compliance as well as the audit program of the Company (the Company's group of companies and their subsidiaries);
(iii) to coordinate the implementation of operational tasks in the field of implementation of the internal audit and the secretariat of the Company;
(iv) to ensure the implementation of GCG in the Company; and
(v) to coordinate, control and evaluate the internalization of GCG principles and ethical standards consistently within the Company.

Bertanggung jawab atas Divisi Hukum, Kepatuhan, Sekretaris Perusahaan, dan Sumber Daya Manusia, termasuk di antaranya:
(i) menetapkan arahan strategis dan penyediaan pelayanan hukum (termasuk penyediaan penasihat internal), serta memastikan kepatuhan terhadap hukum dan regulasi serta proses manajemen risiko.
(ii) menetapkan desain, implementasi, pengawasan, dan penegakan kepatuhan serta program audit Perseroan (kelompok perusahaan Perseroan beserta entitas anaknya);
(iii) mengordinasikan pelaksanaan tugas operasional di bidang pelaksanaan audit internal dan kesekretariatan Perseroan;
(iv) memastikan pelaksanaan dan implementasi GCG di Perseroan; dan
(v) mengordinasikan, mengendalikan dan mengevaluasi internalisasi prinsip-prinsip GCG dan standar etika secara konsisten dalam Perseroan.  |
