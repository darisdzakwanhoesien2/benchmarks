## A. SGH Product Donations

The eradication of malnutrition (stunting) remains one of the main priorities of the Indonesian Government. SGH supports this initiative by giving special attention to children's health, particularly those living in areas surrounding the Company's operations. This commitment is demonstrated through the donation of vitamins via the Integrated Health Service Post (Posyandu) in Jatinegara, Cakung, East Jakarta. The donation was distributed through the Head of RW 010 to support the monthly Posyandu activities for children in the local community.

The Company also continued its collaboration with Nagrak District, Sukabumi, an area near the SCEHR research and plantation facilities. SGH's contribution was showcased through the provision of stunting intervention assistance, including processed supplementary food (PMT), donations of Curcuma Plus milk, and health education support.

In addition to vitamin donations, the Company's support also includes donations of SGH products, namely Curcuma Lysine syrup. These were distributed through several of the Company's social responsibility partners, including Yayasan Kawula Peduli, the Bakam Bercerita Community Service Program from Gadjah Mada University (UGM) in Bangka, and the Viriya Dhamma Buddhist Student Family (KMBVD) of Atma Jaya Catholic University, as part of their social outreach efforts.
