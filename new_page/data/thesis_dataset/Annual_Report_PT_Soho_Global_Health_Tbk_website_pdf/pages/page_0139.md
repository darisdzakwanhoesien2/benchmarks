# Business Review

Tinjauan Usaha [POJK B.1]

## Consumer Health Segment

Divisi Produk Kesehatan Konsumen

## Overview

The Consumer Health segment comprises a broad portfolio of products that do not require a doctor's prescription and are marketed directly to consumers through multiple channels, including retail stores, pharmacies, and e-commerce platforms. In addition, these products are also promoted through healthcare professionals and healthcare facilities such as hospitals and clinics. Key brands in this segment include Imboost Force and Imboost Regular, Curcuma Force, Noroid, Asthin, Curcuma Plus Vitamin, Fitkom, Diapet, and Laxing.

## 2025 Achievements

In 2025, the Consumer Health segment delivered strong growth, with net sales reaching Rp1.15 trillion, representing a 24.6% increase compared to 2024 and contributing 10.7% to SGH's total net sales. Gross profit margin improved to 75.5% from 73.8% in the previous year, reflecting a stronger mix of higher-value products and continued optimization of distribution channels.

Throughout the year, Imboost remained the primary contributor to the segment's performance and maintained its position as one of the leading brands in the immune supplement category. Growth was further supported by strong performance in the e-commerce channel, particularly from Asthin, Noroid, and Curcuma Plus Vitamin, which continue to strengthen their presence across digital platforms. Meanwhile, hospital, clinic, and retail channels continued to provide stable sales contributions, supported by products such as Curcuma Force, Boost D, Fitkom, and Diapet.

Based on IQVIA Indonesia Total Market data for Q4-2025, Imboost ranked 5th among the top 10 best-selling pharmaceutical products in Indonesia across both prescription and OTC categories, and ranked 1st in the vitamin category (excluding ready-to-drink products). This achievement further reinforces the Company's strong position in Indonesia's consumer healthcare market.

## Gambaran Umum

Segmen Kesehatan Konsumen mencakup portofolio produk yang luas yang tidak memerlukan resep dokter dan dipasarkan secara langsung kepada konsumen melalui berbagai saluran, termasuk toko ritel, apotek, dan platform e-commerce. Selain itu, produk-produk dalam segmen ini juga dipromosikan melalui tenaga kesehatan serta fasilitas layanan kesehatan seperti rumah sakit dan klinik. Merek-merek utama dalam segmen ini meliputi Imboost Force dan Imboost Regular, Curcuma Force, Noroid, Asthin, Curcuma Plus Vitamin, Fitkom, Diapet, dan Laxing.

## Pencapaian 2025

Pada tahun 2025, Segmen Kesehatan Konsumen mencatatkan pertumbuhan yang kuat dengan penjualan bersih mencapai Rp1,15 trillion, meningkat 24,6% dibandingkan tahun 2024, serta memberikan kontribusi sebesar 10,7% terhadap total penjualan bersih SGH. Margin laba kotor meningkat menjadi 75,5% dari 73,8% pada tahun sebelumnya, mencerminkan komposisi portofolio produk bernilai tambah tinggi serta optimalisasi pengelolaan kanal distribusi.

Sepanjang tahun, Imboost tetap menjadi kontributor utama bagi kinerja segmen dan mempertahankan posisinya sebagai salah satu merek terkemuka di kategori suplemen daya tahan tubuh. Pertumbuhan juga didorong oleh kinerja yang kuat di kanal e-commerce, terutama dari merek Asthin, Noroid, dan Curcuma Plus Vitamin yang semakin memperkuat kehadirannya di platform digital. Di sisi lain, kanal rumah sakit, klinik, dan ritel tetap memberikan kontribusi penjualan yang stabil, didukung oleh produk-produk seperti Curcuma Force, Boost D, Fitkom, dan Diapet.

Berdasarkan data IQVIA Indonesia Total Market Q4-2025, Imboost menempati peringkat ke-5 di antara 10 produk farmasi terlaris di Indonesia baik dalam kategori resep maupun OTC, serta peringkat pertama dalam kategori vitamin (di luar ready-to-drink). Pencapaian ini semakin memperkuat posisi Perseroan sebagai salah satu pemain utama di pasar consumer healthcare di Indonesia.
