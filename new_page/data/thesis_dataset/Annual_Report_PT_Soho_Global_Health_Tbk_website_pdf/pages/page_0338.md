# X. Town Hall Meeting SOHO Global Health and Supply &amp; Operations / Town Hall Meeting SOHO Global Health dan Supply &amp; Operations

The Town Hall Meeting is a strategic agenda regularly conducted by SOHO Global Health as part of the Company's commitment to promoting transparency and strengthening two-way communication between management and employees. Through this forum, management shares updates on Company performance, strategic direction, and priorities, while also providing an open platform for employees to express their feedback and aspirations. This initiative also serves as an important medium to foster a sense of unity and strengthen collaboration across all levels of the organization.

On February 21, 2025, the Town Hall Meeting of SOHO Global Health was held at the Company's head office located in Pulogadung Industrial Estate, East Jakarta. During the event, the Chief Financial Officer &amp; Chief Operating Officer delivered a Business Update covering the Company's performance in 2024 and its strategic priorities for 2025, including updates on organizational structure. The session also featured an interactive Q&amp;A and open discussion, enabling direct engagement between management and employees.

In addition, business updates from various business units were presented by representatives of Consumer Health, Distribution, and Alliance. These presentations provided employees with a more comprehensive understanding of the Company's business developments and direction, while also enhancing employee engagement in supporting the achievement of the Company's strategic objectives.

Town Hall Meeting merupakan salah satu agenda strategis yang secara berkala diselenggarakan oleh SOHO Global Health sebagai wujud komitmen perusahaan dalam mendorong keterbukaan informasi serta memperkuat komunikasi dua arah antara manajemen dan karyawan. Melalui forum ini, manajemen menyampaikan perkembangan kinerja perusahaan, arah dan prioritas strategis, serta membuka ruang dialog bagi karyawan untuk menyampaikan masukan dan aspirasi secara langsung. Kegiatan ini juga menjadi sarana penting dalam memperkuat rasa kebersamaan dan menumbuhkan semangat kolaborasi di seluruh lini organisasi.

Pada 21 Februari 2025, Town Hall Meeting SOHO Global Health diselenggarakan di kantor pusat perusahaan yang berlokasi di Kawasan Industri Pulogadung, Jakarta Timur. Dalam kesempatan tersebut, Chief Financial Officer &amp; Chief Operating Officer menyampaikan Business Update yang mencakup beberapa topik utama, antara lain kinerja perusahaan sepanjang tahun 2024 serta prioritas strategis untuk tahun 2025, pembaruan struktur organisasi, serta sesi tanya jawab dan diskusi terbuka dengan karyawan.

Selain itu, pembaruan bisnis dari masing-masing unit usaha juga disampaikan oleh perwakilan unit terkait, yaitu Consumer Health, Distribusi, dan Aliansi. Penyampaian ini memberikan pemahaman yang lebih komprehensif kepada karyawan mengenai perkembangan dan arah bisnis Perusahaan, sekaligus memperkuat keterlibatan karyawan dalam mendukung pencapaian tujuan strategis Perusahaan.
