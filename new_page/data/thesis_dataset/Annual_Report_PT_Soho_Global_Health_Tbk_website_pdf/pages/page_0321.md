C. Team-Building Activities by Business Unit

Throughout 2025, the Company continued to demonstrate its commitment to fostering an inclusive, collaborative, and empowering workplace through a variety of employee engagement initiatives. These initiatives are designed to enhance employee morale and productivity while strengthening a positive and sustainable organizational culture. A range of programs were implemented, including personal development activities, employee appreciation programs, and initiatives aimed at strengthening cross-team collaboration and cultivating a strong sense of togetherness across all levels of the organization. These activities were carried out through team-building programs organized by business units and work locations, including:

- Consumer Health Business Unit
- Alliance Business Unit
- Finance and Accounting, Tax, Business Development, Business Technology, and Procurement Business Units
- Legal, Compliance, Internal Audit, Corporate Secretary, and Human Resources Business Units
- Distribution Business Unit – Head Office
- Distribution Business Unit – Branch Offices
- Distribution Business Unit – National Meeting of Responsible Pharmacists, Accounting Supervisors, and Logistics Section Heads
- Distribution Business Unit – National Meeting of Sales Supervisors
- Distribution Business Unit – National Meeting of Branch Managers

# Consumer Health Business Unit
Unit Bisnis Consumer Health

As in previous years, the Consumer Health Business Unit held its National Sales Conference at the beginning of the year, from January 15 to 18 2025 in Bandung, under the theme "Innovate to Elevate!". As part of the Company's efforts to strengthen team motivation and capabilities, Mr. Adriano Giovanni, a public speaker and training facilitator, delivered a training session titled "Becoming an Outstanding Leader in the Digital Era." The session aimed to equip participants with the skills needed to lead effectively amid rapid technological advancements, evolving market dynamics, and the changing expectations of the digital generation.

C. Pelaksanaan Team Building per Unit Bisnis

Sepanjang tahun 2025, Perusahaan terus menunjukkan komitmennya dalam menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang inklusif, kolaboratif, dan memberdayakan melalui berbagai aktivitas keterlibatan karyawan. Inisiatif ini bertujuan untuk meningkatkan semangat dan produktivitas kerja, sekaligus membangun budaya organisasi yang positif dan berkelanjutan. Beragam program telah dijalankan, mulai dari kegiatan pengembangan diri, program apresiasi karyawan, hingga aktivitas yang memperkuat kolaborasi lintas tim dan menumbuhkan semangat kebersamaan di seluruh lapisan organisasi, berupa team building per Unit Bisnis dan per lokasi kerja, di antaranya:

- Unit Bisnis Kesehatan Konsumen
- Unit Bisnis Aliansi
- Unit Bisnis Keuangan dan Akuntansi, Pajak, Pengembangan Bisnis, Teknologi Bisnis, dan Pengadaan
- Unit Bisnis bidang Hukum, Kepatuhan, Audit Internal, Sekretaris Korporasi, dan Sumber Daya Manusia
- Unit Bisnis Distribusi – Kantor Pusat
- Unit Bisnis Distribusi – Kantor Cabang
- Unit Bisnis Distribusi – Pertemuan Nasional Apoteker Penanggung Jawab, Supervisor Akuntansi Kantor Cabang, dan Kepala Seksi Logistik
- Unit Bisnis Distribusi – Pertemuan Nasional Supervisor Penjualan
- Unit Bisnis Distribusi – Pertemuan Nasional Kepala Cabang

Seperti tahun-tahun sebelumnya, unit bisnis Consumer Health mengadakan Konferensi Penjualan Nasional pada awal tahun, tepatnya pada tanggal 15 sampai dengan 18 Januari 2025 bertempat di Bandung, dengan tema "Innovate to Elevate!". Dalam upaya meningkatkan motivasi dan keterampilan tim, Bapak Adriano Giovanni, seorang pembicara publik dan fasilitator pelatihan membawakan sesi pelatihan bertema "Menjadi Pemimpin Unggul di Era Digital", untuk membekali peserta dengan kemampuan untuk memimpin secara efektif di tengah perubahan teknologi, dinamika pasar, dan ekspektasi generasi digital.
