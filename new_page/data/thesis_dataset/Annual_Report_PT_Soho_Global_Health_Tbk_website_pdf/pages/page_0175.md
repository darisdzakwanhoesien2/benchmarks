# General Shareholders Meeting Year 2025

# Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Tahun 2025

During the year 2025, the Company held three (3) GMS, consisting of 2 (two) Extraordinary GMS and 1 (one) Annual GMS. In conducting the GMS, the Company has complied with the provisions of Article 12 of the Company's Articles of Association and adhered to the provisions of Article 8, Article 10, and Article 17 of POJK No.15/2020.

Selama tahun 2025, Perseroan telah menyelenggarakan tiga (3) kali RUPS, yakni 2 (dua) kali RUPS Luar Biasa dan 1 (satu) kali RUPS Tahunan. Dalam menyelenggarakan RUPS tersebut, Perseroan telah memenuhi ketentuan Pasal 12 Anggaran Dasar Perseroan serta memperhatikan ketentuan Pasal 8, Pasal 10, dan Pasal 17 POJK No.15/2020.
