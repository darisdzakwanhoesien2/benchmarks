# Employee Statistics

## Statistik Karyawan

In implementing a sustainable business strategy, human resources are the Company main asset and a key driver of success. Throughout 2025, The Company consistently enhanced its human capital capabilities through various competency development initiatives, including competency Development programs, improvement to performance management systems, and the creation of an inclusive and productive work environment.

The Company is strongly committed to systematic and structured policies to ensure that every employee is provided with equal opportunities. This commitment compasses career development, employee welfare, social security protection, occupational health and safety, as well as the freedom of association and assembly, all of which are implemented without regard to gender, race, religion, ethnicity, or socio-cultural backgrounds.

SGH Group's commitment to gender equality is reflected in the representation of women in strategic positions. The company also ensures equality of rights and obligations between male and female employees, as stipulated in the Board of Directors' Decree on the Policy of Equality in the Provision of Allowances and Benefits. Dalam menjalankan strategi bisnis yang berkelanjutan, Sumber Daya Manusia (SDM) menjadi aset utama Perusahaan dan merupakan salah satu kunci keberhasilan. Sepanjang tahun 2025, Perusahaan secara konsisten mengembangkan kapabilitas SDM melalui berbagai inisiatif pengembangan kompetensi, perbaikan sistem manajemen kinerja, serta penciptaan lingkungan kerja yang inklusif dan produktif.

Perusahaan berkomitmen tinggi terhadap kebijakan yang sistematis dan terstruktur guna memastikan bahwa setiap karyawan memiliki kesempatan yang setara. Hal ini mencakup aspek pengembangan karir, kesejahteraan, perlindungan jaminan sosial, keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja, serta kebebasan berserikat dan berkumpul, yang dilakukan tanpa memandang jenis kelamin, ras, agama, etnis, atau latar belakang sosial budaya lainnya.

Komitmen SGH Group terhadap kesetaraan gender tercermin melalui representasi tenaga kerja perempuan dalam posisi strategis. Perusahaan juga menjamin kesetaraan hak dan kewajiban antara tenaga kerja laki-laki dan perempuan, sebagaimana tertuang dalam Surat Keputusan Direksi mengenai Kebijakan Kesetaraan dalam Pemberian Tunjangan dan Bantuan.
