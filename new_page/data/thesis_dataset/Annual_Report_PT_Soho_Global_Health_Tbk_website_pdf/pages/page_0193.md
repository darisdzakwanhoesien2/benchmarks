# Board of Commissioners Charter

## Piagam Dewan Komisaris

In supervising the Company's management, the Board of Commissioners follows the guidelines from the Board of Commissioners Charter which contains their duties, responsibilities, and authorities.

Dalam mengawasi pengelolaan Perseroan, Dewan Komisaris berpedoman pada Piagam Dewan Komisaris yang memuat tugas, tanggung jawab dan wewenang Dewan Komisaris.

# Performance Assessment of the Board of Directors

## Penilaian atas Kinerja Direksi

The Board of Commissioners highly appreciate the performance of the Board of Directors and all Company personnel for the positive performance achievements amidst various challenges faced. The Board of Commissioners considers that the Board of Directors has given its best efforts and strategies to maintain the stability and continuity of the Company's business.

The Board of Commissioners has conducted an assessment of the performance of the Board of Directors in managing the Company throughout the 2025 Financial Year. A comprehensive evaluation was carried out to assess the effectiveness of the Directors' leadership in steering the Company's strategy amid the dynamics of the highly competitive healthcare industry. The Board of Commissioners views that the Board of Directors has demonstrated strong leadership as well as consistent commitment to the Company's Vision and Objectives.

Under the leadership of the Board of Directors, the Company successfully recorded solid financial performance with consistent growth compared to the previous year. The Company closed the 2025 Financial Year with a net profit after tax of IDR 600.32 billion, representing an increase of 29.76% compared to IDR 462.65 billion in 2024. This improvement in performance was also accompanied by higher returns for shareholders. The Board of Commissioners views this achievement as a reflection of the Board of Directors' capability to formulate and implement effective strategies, manage risks prudently, and maintain operational efficiency.

In overseeing the formulation of strategy, the Board of Commissioners has reviewed and provided input on the Company's Annual Work Plan and Budget (RKAP) to ensure that the Company's strategic direction aligns with shareholders' aspirations, regulatory requirements, and long-term sustainability objectives. Through this process, the Board of Commissioners actively conveyed considerations related to innovation, risk management, and value creation, which were subsequently integrated into the Company's strategic agenda.

In overseeing the implementation of strategy, the Board of Commissioners carried out its role through structured and intensive engagement with the Board of Directors. Throughout the 2025 Financial Year, the Board of Commissioners consistently held internal meetings, joint meetings with the Board of Directors, as well as meetings with the Committees under the Board of Commissioners. These forums served as the primary platform for the Board of Commissioners to review periodic performance reports, discuss market dynamics and strategic priorities, and provide input and direction on business development, in-licensing programs, merger and acquisition opportunities, sustainability, human resources, compliance, internal audit, and financial performance.

Dewan Komisaris sangat mengapresiasi kinerja Direksi dan seluruh jajaran Perseroan atas pencapaian kinerja positif yang berhasil dicapai di tengah berbagai tantangan yang muncul. Dewan Komisaris menilai bahwa Direksi telah mengerahkan upaya dan strategi terbaik demi mempertahankan stabilitas dan kelangsungan bisnis Perseroan.

Dewan Komisaris telah melakukan penilaian atas kinerja Direksi dalam mengelola Perseroan sepanjang Tahun Buku 2025. Evaluasi menyeluruh dilakukan untuk menilai efektivitas kepemimpinan Direksi dalam mengarahkan strategi Perseroan di tengah dinamika pasar industri kesehatan yang kompetitif. Dewan Komisaris menilai bahwa Direksi menunjukkan kepemimpinan yang kuat sekaligus komitmen yang konsisten terhadap Visi dan Tujuan Perseroan.

Di bawah kepemimpinan Direksi, Perseroan berhasil mencatatkan kinerja keuangan yang solid dengan pertumbuhan yang konsisten dari tahun sebelumnya. Perseroan menutup tahun buku 2025 dengan laba bersih setelah pajak sebesar Rp 600,32 miliar, meningkat 29,76% dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya yaitu sebesar Rp 462,65 miliar pada tahun 2024. Peningkatan kinerja ini juga disertai dengan imbal hasil yang lebih baik kepada para pemegang saham. Dewan Komisaris memandang bahwa capaian tersebut mencerminkan kemampuan Direksi dalam merumuskan dan mengimplementasikan strategi yang efektif, mengelola risiko secara bijak, serta menjaga efisiensi operasional.

Dalam pengawasan atas perumusan strategi, Dewan Komisaris telah menelaah dan memberikan masukan terhadap Rencana Kerja dan Anggaran Tahunan Perseroan (RKAP), guna memastikan arah strategis Perseroan sejalan dengan aspirasi pemegang saham, ketentuan regulator, serta tujuan keberlanjutan jangka panjang. Melalui proses ini, Dewan Komisaris secara aktif menyampaikan pertimbangan terkait inovasi, manajemen risiko, dan penciptaan nilai yang kemudian diintegrasikan ke dalam agenda strategis Perseroan.

Dalam pengawasan atas implementasi strategi, Dewan Komisaris melaksanakan fungsinya melalui keterlibatan yang terstruktur dan intensif dengan Direksi. Sepanjang Tahun Buku 2025, Dewan Komisaris secara konsisten menyelenggarakan rapat internal, rapat gabungan dengan Direksi, serta rapat bersama Komite-Komite di bawah Dewan Komisaris. Forum-forum ini menjadi sarana utama bagi Dewan Komisaris untuk menelaah laporan kinerja berkala, membahas dinamika pasar dan prioritas strategis, serta memberikan masukan dan arahan terkait pengembangan usaha, program in-licensing, peluang merger dan akuisisi, keberlanjutan, sumber daya manusia, kepatuhan, audit internal, dan kinerja keuangan.
