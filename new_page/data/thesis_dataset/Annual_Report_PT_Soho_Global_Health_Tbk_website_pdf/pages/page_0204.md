# Pandy Harianto*

# Director / Direktur

Responsible for the Operations Division of the Company, including among others:

(i) to control and ensure the implementation of all operational activities of the Company and subsidiaries, including production, supply chain, and distribution, so that they are carried out effectively, efficiently, and in alignment with the Company's vision, mission, strategies, policies, plans, and work programs;
(ii) to establish and implement operational strategies to enhance productivity, cost efficiency, service quality, and the Company's operational excellence;
(iii) to lead the Company's digital market penetration and product marketing initiatives, as well as to ensure operational readiness and reliability in supporting the development of digital distribution channels;
(iv) to act as an internal advisor in operational matters and the implementation of digital transformation to support the achievement of the Company's strategic objectives.

Bertanggung jawab atas Divisi Operasional Perseroan, termasuk di antaranya:

(i) mengendalikan, dan memastikan pelaksanaan seluruh kegiatan operasional Perseroan dan entitas anak, termasuk produksi, rantai pasok, dan distribusi, agar berjalan efektif, efisien, dan selaras dengan visi, misi, strategi, kebijakan, rencana, dan program kerja Perseroan;
(ii) menetapkan dan mengimplementasikan strategi operasional untuk meningkatkan produktivitas, efisiensi biaya, kualitas layanan, serta keunggulan operasional Perseroan;
(iii) memimpin penetrasi pasar digital dan pemasaran produk Perseroan serta memastikan kesiapan dan keandalan operasional dalam mendukung pengembangan kanal distribusi digital;
(iv) berperan sebagai penasihat internal dalam aspek operasional dan implementasi transformasi digital guna mendukung pencapaian tujuan strategis Perseroan.
