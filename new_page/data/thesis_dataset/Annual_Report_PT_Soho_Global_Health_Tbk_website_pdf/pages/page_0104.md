Control of the Company is exercised by Cascade Creek Pty Ltd together with Tiberias 96 Pty Ltd, both of which are under the ownership and control of the same ultimate party, namely Mr. Eng Liang Tan, as the ultimate beneficial owner.

Mr. Eng Liang Tan is the ultimate beneficial owner as well as the ultimate controlling party of the Company through his indirect shareholding in the Company. The ownership structure is as follows:

i) Mr. Eng Liang Tan exercises full control over Cascade Creek Pty Ltd and Tiberias 96 Pty Ltd.
ii) Cascade Creek Pty Ltd and Tiberias 96 Pty Ltd are each shareholders of the Company.
iii) Accordingly, the shareholdings in the Company held by Cascade Creek Pty Ltd and Tiberias 96 Pty Ltd constitute Mr. Eng Liang Tan's indirect ownership in the Company.

Based on the ownership structure as illustrated below, Mr. Eng Liang Tan indirectly controls the Company's shares through Cascade Creek Pty Ltd and Tiberias 96 Pty Ltd, and therefore qualifies as the ultimate beneficial owner as well as the controlling party of the Company in accordance with the prevailing capital market laws and regulations.

Pengendalian Perseroan berada pada Cascade Creek Pty Ltd bersama dengan Tiberias 96 Pty Ltd, yang berada di bawah kepemilikan dan pengendalian pihak yang sama, yaitu Bapak Eng Liang Tan selaku ultimate beneficial owner.

Bapak Eng Liang Tan merupakan ultimate beneficial owner sekaligus pengendali akhir Perseroan melalui kepemilikan tidak langsung atas saham Perseroan. Alur kepemilikan tersebut adalah sebagai berikut:

i) Bapak Eng Liang Tan memiliki pengendalian penuh atas Cascade Creek Pty Ltd dan Tiberias 96 Pty Ltd.
ii) Cascade Creek Pty Ltd dan Tiberias 96 Pty Ltd masing-masing merupakan pemegang saham Perseroan.
iii) Dengan demikian, kepemilikan saham Perseroan oleh Cascade Creek Pty Ltd dan Tiberias 96 Pty Ltd merupakan kepemilikan tidak langsung Bapak Eng Liang Tan atas Perseroan.

Berdasarkan struktur kepemilikan sebagaimana digambarkan di bawah ini, Bapak Eng Liang Tan secara tidak langsung menguasai saham Perseroan melalui Cascade Creek Pty Ltd dan Tiberias 96 Pty Ltd, sehingga memenuhi kualifikasi sebagai pemilik manfaat akhir (ultimate beneficial owner) sekaligus pengendali Perseroan sebagaimana dimaksud dalam peraturan perundang-undangan di bidang Pasar Modal.
