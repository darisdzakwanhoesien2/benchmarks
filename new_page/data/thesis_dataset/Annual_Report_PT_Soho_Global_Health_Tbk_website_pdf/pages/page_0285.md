To ensure effective implementation, the Company's Internal Audit Division conducts regular assessments of compliance with these policies and procedures as part of its internal control function. Internal Audit reports its findings to the Board through periodic reporting on a monthly, quarterly, and annual basis. The reports include observations on the effectiveness of internal controls, areas for improvement, and recommended corrective actions, which are reviewed by the Board for further direction and oversight.

As part of its governance framework, the Company has established a number of key policies that support ethical conduct, responsible business practices, and risk management. These include policies on anti-bribery and corruption, insider trading, information technology and infrastructure security, personal data protection, ethical marketing practices in interactions with healthcare professionals and institutions, and human rights. These policies guide the conduct of employees and business partners in maintaining high standards of integrity and compliance.

In addition, the Company conducts periodic risk assessments to identify potential operational, regulatory, and sustainability-related risks. The results of these assessments are communicated to the Board to support informed decision-making and to prioritize mitigation actions for areas identified as having higher risk exposure.

Through these monitoring and control mechanisms, the Company seeks to ensure the effective implementation of its sustainable finance and ESG commitments while maintaining strong governance and accountability across the organization.
