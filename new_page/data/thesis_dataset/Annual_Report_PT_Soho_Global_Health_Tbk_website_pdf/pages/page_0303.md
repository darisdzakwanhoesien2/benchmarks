IV. Employee Turnover / Tingkat Pergantian Karyawan

Throughout 2025, a total of 170 employees left the Company due to various reasons, including voluntary and involuntary resignations, contract expiration, unsuccessful completion of probation, retirement, and death. This figure represents 7.1% of the annual employee turnover rate as of the end of December 2025.

The Company continues to make efforts to retain its best talents to ensure sustainable business growth, including re-engaging retired employees who may be considered as experts or consultants for the Company. In addition, the Company remains committed to enhancing employees' knowledge and competencies through various training and development programs, which will be further elaborated in the following section.

Sepanjang tahun 2025, sebanyak 170 karyawan berhenti bekerja dari Perusahaan dengan berbagai alasan, antara lain pengunduran diri secara sukarela maupun karena pengakhiran hubungan kerja (PHK), berakhirnya masa kontrak, tidak lulus masa percobaan, pensiun, serta meninggal dunia. Jumlah tersebut setara dengan 7,1% dari tingkat pergantian karyawan tahunan hingga akhir Desember 2025.

Perusahaan terus berupaya untuk mempertahankan talenta terbaik yang dimiliki guna memastikan pertumbuhan bisnis yang berkelanjutan, termasuk merekrut kembali karyawan yang telah pensiun untuk dipertimbangkan sebagai tenaga ahli atau konsultan bagi Perusahaan. # V. Regional Minimum Wage / Upah Minimum Regional [POJK F.20]

SGH Group demonstrates a strong commitment to complying with the regulations of the Government of Indonesia, particularly those concerning the Provincial Minimum Wage (UMP) and the City/Regency Minimum Wage (UMK). The Company ensures that all employees receive remuneration that is not lower than the stipulated UMP/UMK and consistently adheres to annual increases in UMP/UMK in accordance with the respective Provincial Governor Regulations across Indonesia.

SGH Group also implements a Wage Structure and Scale in compliance with applicable laws and regulations. This remuneration structure is based on job level, competency, and individual performance. The Company consistently ensures internal equity and alignment.

The Company is actively committed to periodically reviewing overall wage compliance to ensure adherence to the provisions set by the Government of Indonesia and to maintain fair and competitive remuneration practices. In addition, SGH Group does not differentiate remuneration between male and female employees.
