# Suspension or Delisting

## Suspensi atau Delisting

The Company experienced a temporary trading suspension imposed by the Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX) on 27 October 2025 pursuant to IDX Letter No. Peng-SPT-00341/BEI.WAS/102-5 dated 24 October 2025. The suspension was implemented in the Regular Market and Cash Market as a cooling down measure following a significant cumulative increase in the Company's share price in the market. Furthermore, on October 27, 2025 the IDX also requested the Company to conduct an Incidental Public Expose in connection with the increase in the Company's cumulative share price as referred to in the previous paragraph, as well as the Company's current financial performance and business conditions. The Company conducted the Incidental Public Expose on November 10, 2025.

Trading of the Company's shares resumed on 14 November 2025 following the convention of the Incidental Public Expose. However, due to the temporary suspension of trading of the Company's shares from October 27 to November 13, the Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX) has added the Company to the Special Monitoring List which is effective from November 14, 2025 through Exchange Announcement No. Peng-PK-00094/BEI.PLP/11-2025 dated November 13, 2025. While on the Special Monitoring List, the Company's shares are traded using an auction mechanism. Furthermore, based on Exchange Announcement No. Peng-CK-00101/BEI.PLP/11-2025 dated November 24, 2025 regarding the Revocation of Equity Securities from Special Monitoring, the Company's shares have been revoked by the IDX from the Special Monitoring List effective from November 25, 2025.

Perseroan mengalami penghentian sementara perdagangan saham oleh Bursa Efek Indonesia (BEI) pada tanggal 27 Oktober 2025 berdasarkan Surat BEI Nomor Peng-SPT-00341/BEI.WAS/102-5 tertanggal 24 Oktober 2025. Penghentian sementara tersebut dilakukan di Pasar Reguler dan Pasar Tunai dalam rangka cooling down sehubungan dengan terjadinya peningkatan harga kumulatif saham Perseroan secara signifikan di pasar. Selain itu, pada tanggal 27 Oktober 2025 BEI juga meminta Perseroan untuk melaksanakan Paparan Publik Insidentil (Incidental Public Expose) sehubungan dengan peningkatan harga kumulatif saham Perseroan sebagaimana dimaksud serta menyampaikan informasi mengenai kinerja keuangan dan kondisi bisnis terkini Perseroan. Perseroan telah melaksanakan Paparan Publik Insidentil tersebut pada tanggal 10 November 2025.

Perdagangan saham Perseroan kembali dibuka pada tanggal 14 November 2025 setelah dilaksanakannya Paparan Publik Insidentil tersebut. Namun dikarenakan adanya penghentian sementara perdagangan saham Perseroan dari tanggal 27 Oktober hingga 13 November, maka Bursa Efek Indonesia (BEI) telah menambahkan Perseroan ke dalam Daftar Pemantauan Khusus yang berlaku efektif sejak tanggal 14 November 2025 melalui Pengumuman Bursa No. Peng-PK-00094/BEI.PLP/11-2025 tertanggal 13 November 2025. Selama berada dalam Daftar Pemantauan Khusus tersebut, saham Perseroan diperdagangkan dengan mekanisme auction. Selanjutnya, berdasarkan Pengumuman Bursa No. Peng-CK-00101/BEI.PLP/11-2025 tertanggal 24 November 2025 mengenai Pencabutan Efek Bersifat Ekuitas dari Pemantauan Khusus, saham Perseroan telah dicabut oleh BEI dari Daftar Pemantauan Khusus efektif sejak tanggal 25 November 2025.
