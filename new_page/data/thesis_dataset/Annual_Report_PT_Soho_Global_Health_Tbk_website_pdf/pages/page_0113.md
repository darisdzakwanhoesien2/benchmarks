# Empowerment of Women Workers

## Pemberdayaan Pekerja Perempuan

The evolving role and participation of women in the professional sphere have made women's empowerment and gender equality in the workplace matters of significant importance.

At SGH Group, we believe that the empowerment of women is not only a part of social responsibility, but also a key strategy in fostering innovation and driving business growth within the organization. The Company is committed to implementing gender equality and supporting the empowerment of women in the workplace in order to create an inclusive and sustainable working environment.

In upholding the principle of equality in the workplace, we implement comprehensive policies covering recruitment, performance evaluation, remuneration, career development, and carrying out tasks professionally without discrimination based on gender, ethnicity, religion, race, social group, or physical condition.

Throughout 2025, we continued to strengthen our commitment to gender equality across all levels of the organization. As of the end of 2025, women accounted for 28% of the Company's total workforce.

Perkembangan peran dan partisipasi perempuan dalam bidang profesional membuat pemberdayaan perempuan dan kesetaraan gender di lingkungan kerja menjadi hal yang sangat diperhatikan.

Di SGH Group, kami meyakini bahwa pemberdayaan perempuan bukan hanya bagian dari tanggung jawab sosial, tetapi juga strategi penting dalam menciptakan inovasi dan pertumbuhan bisnis di organisasi. Perusahaan berkomitmen mengimplementasikan kesetaraan gender dan mendukung pemberdayaan perempuan di tempat kerja dalam menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang inklusif dan berkelanjutan.

Dalam menjunjung tinggi prinsip kesetaraan di lingkungan kerja, kami menerapkan beragam kebijakan yang menyeluruh mulai dari penerimaan, penilaian kinerja, remunerasi, pengembangan karier, serta melaksanakan tugas secara profesional tanpa membedakan jenis kelamin, suku, agama, ras, golongan, dan kondisi fisik.

Sepanjang tahun 2025, kami terus memperkuat komitmen terhadap kesetaraan gender di seluruh lini organisasi. Pada akhir tahun 2025, jumlah tenaga kerja perempuan mewakili 28% dari total tenaga kerja di Perusahaan.
