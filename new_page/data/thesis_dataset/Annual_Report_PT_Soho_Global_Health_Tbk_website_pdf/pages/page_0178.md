# Implementation of the Extraordinary GMS

Pelaksanaan RUPS Luar Biasa

The Company conducted its Extraordinary GMS on September 16, 2025, at the Company's Head Office, Ruang Training Logistik, Lantai 3, Jl. Rawa Sumur II Kav. BB No. 4A-4B, Kawasan Industri Pulogadung, Kel. Jatinegara, Kec. Cakung, Jakarta Timur, 13930, DKI Jakarta. The Extraordinary GMS was attended by 10,333,343,110 shares or 81.41% of all fully paid-up shares of the Company.

The agenda discussed and resolved in the Extraordinary GMS were in accordance with the agendas stated in the Invitation of Extraordinary GMS dated August, 25 2025 delivered by the Board of Directors of the Company to all shareholders.

The Independent Parties who carry out the vote counting process and carry out the validation are:
- PT Datindo Entrycom, as Securities Administration Bureau
- Jimmy Tanal, S.H., M.Kn, Notary in South Jakarta

Perseroan telah menyelenggarakan RUPS Luar Biasa pada tanggal 16 September 2025 di Kantor Pusat Perseroan, Ruang Training Logistik, Lantai 3, Jl. Rawa Sumur II Kav. BB No. 4A-4B, Kawasan Industri Pulogadung, Kel. Jatinegara, Kec. Cakung, Jakarta Timur, 13930, DKI Jakarta. RUPS Tahunan dihadiri oleh 10.333.343.110 saham atau sebesar 81,41% dari seluruh saham Perseroan yang telah disetor penuh.

Agenda yang dibahas dan diputuskan dalam RUPS Luar Biasa telah sesuai dengan mata acara yang tertuang di dalam Pemanggilan atas RUPS Luar Biasa tertanggal 25 Agustus 2025 yang disampaikan oleh Direksi Perseroan kepada seluruh para pemegang saham.

Pihak Independen yang melakukan proses perhitungan suara dan melakukan validasi adalah:
- PT Datindo Entrycom, selaku Biro Administrasi Efek
- Jimmy Tanal, S.H., M.Kn, Notaris di Jakarta Selatan

The resolutions approved in the Extraordinary GMS held on September 16, 2025, are as follows:

Approval of the proposed changes to the composition of the Board of Directors of the Company.
