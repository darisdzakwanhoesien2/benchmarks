## Piero Brambati

## Director / Direktur

Responsible for the Finance, Business Development, Business Technology, and Procurement Divisions, including among others:

(i) to direct, control, and oversee the Company's financial functions, including financial planning, budgeting, cash flow management, as well as supervision of the financial performance of the Company and its subsidiaries;
(ii) to formulate and implement the Company's commercial strategies, including sales, marketing, and market development, in order to achieve revenue growth and profitability targets;
(iii) to lead the Company's business development, including the identification of new business opportunities, strategic partnerships, as well as business expansion and product portfolio development;
(iv) to direct the development and utilization of business technology, including systems and digital platforms that support commercial activities, marketing, and data-driven decision-making;
(v) to establish and oversee policies and governance of procurement of goods and services to ensure efficiency, transparency, and alignment with the Company's commercial and operational strategies;
(vi) to conduct market analysis, industry trends, and consumer behavior as a basis for the Company's strategic decision-making;
(vii) to direct, coordinate, and provide strategic support and necessary guidance in the implementation of the Company's business operations carried out by all business units and support units, in order to ensure that all operational activities are aligned with the Company's vision, mission, business objectives, strategies, policies, plans, and work programs;
(viii) to ensure the integration of commercial strategies, financial capabilities, and technological support in order to drive sustainable growth; and
(ix) to act as an internal advisor in financial, commercial, and business development matters to support the achievement of the Company's strategic objectives.

Bertanggung jawab atas Divisi Keuangan, Pengembangan Bisnis, Teknologi Bisnis, dan Pengadaan, termasuk di antaranya:

(i) mengarahkan, mengendalikan, dan mengawasi fungsi keuangan Perseroan, termasuk perencanaan keuangan, pengelolaan anggaran, arus kas, serta pengawasan kinerja keuangan Perseroan dan entitas anak;
(ii) merumuskan dan mengimplementasikan strategi komersial Perseroan, termasuk penjualan, pemasaran, dan pengembangan pasar guna mencapai target pertumbuhan pendapatan dan profitabilitas;
(iii) memimpin pengembangan bisnis Perseroan, termasuk identifikasi peluang usaha baru, kemitraan strategis, serta ekspansi usaha dan portofolio produk;
(iv) mengarahkan pengembangan dan pemanfaatan teknologi bisnis, termasuk sistem dan platform digital yang mendukung kegiatan komersial, pemasaran, dan pengambilan keputusan berbasis data;
(v) menetapkan dan mengawasi kebijakan serta tata kelola pengadaan barang dan jasa untuk memastikan efisiensi, transparansi, dan sinergi dengan strategi komersial dan operasional Perseroan;
(vi) melakukan analisis pasar, tren industri, dan perilaku konsumen sebagai dasar penetapan strategi Perseroan;
(vii) mengarahkan, mengoordinasikan, dan memberikan dukungan strategis serta arahan yang diperlukan dalam pelaksanaan kegiatan operasional bisnis Perseroan oleh seluruh unit bisnis dan unit pendukung, guna memastikan seluruh kegiatan operasional Perseroan berjalan selaras dengan visi, misi, sasaran usaha, strategi, kebijakan, rencana, dan program kerja yang telah ditetapkan;
(viii) memastikan integrasi antara strategi komersial, kapabilitas keuangan, dan dukungan teknologi guna menciptakan pertumbuhan yang berkelanjutan; dan
(ix) berperan sebagai penasihat internal dalam aspek keuangan, komersial, dan pengembangan bisnis guna mendukung pencapaian tujuan strategis Perseroan.
