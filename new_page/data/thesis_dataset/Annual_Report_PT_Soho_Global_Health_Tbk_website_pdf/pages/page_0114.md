## Benefit Karyawan

The Company is committed to creating a productive work environment, supported by fair labor practices and competitive remuneration. The principles of equality and non-discrimination form the foundation of all employment practices, particularly in the areas of compensation and benefits.

The principle of non-discrimination encompasses equality regardless of ethnicity, cultural background, religion, race, gender, or other personal factors. This commitment is clearly reflected in the remuneration structure of SGH Group, where benefits are granted based on individual competence, qualifications, and capabilities, ensuring that all employees are fairly rewarded according to their actual contributions and potential.

The rights and obligations of the Company and its employees are clearly stipulated in employment agreements. SGH Group actively promotes open dialogue with labor unions to enhance employee welfare. These constructive discussions reflect shared aspirations toward a harmonious and progressive working environment.

To maintain its position as an employer of choice within the industry, SGH Group implements a proactive remuneration strategy that is responsive to market dynamics. The Company actively participates in remuneration surveys, conducts benchmarking against comparable companies, and continuously seeks to understand employee aspirations through employee engagement surveys, HR service satisfaction surveys, consultations with Human Resources Business Partners (HRBP) through the HR Clinic, exit interviews, and anonymous reporting mechanisms.

As part of its ongoing efforts to enhance employee health and well-being, in early 2025 SGH Group introduced a new program, "Be Healthy," as part of the Company Wellness Program. The program provides a 50% subsidy for employees' purchases of SGH Group products, as well as reimbursement for gym membership fees.
