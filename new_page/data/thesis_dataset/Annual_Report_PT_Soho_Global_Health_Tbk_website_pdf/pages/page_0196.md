# View on the Implementation of the Company's Corporate Governance

## Pandangan atas penerapan tata Kelola Perseroan

The Company affirms that strong corporate governance is a fundamental foundation for safeguarding performance, reputation, and long-term sustainability. The principles of Good Corporate Governance (GCG) are implemented through transparency, accountability, responsibility, independence, and fairness. Through the consistent application of these principles, strategic decision-making across the organization is carried out in a consistent and equitable manner and aligned with the interests of shareholders and other stakeholders.

Throughout the 2025 Financial Year, the Board of Commissioners is of the view that the Company has consistently implemented these governance principles. Transparency is realized through the timely and accurate disclosure of information in the Annual Report, Sustainability Report, Corporate Governance Report, as well as periodic financial reporting. Accountability is strengthened through a clear organizational structure, well-defined roles and responsibilities, and a system of checks and balances supported by independent external audits. Responsibility is reflected in compliance with prevailing laws and regulations, sound management practices, and the fulfillment of social and environmental responsibilities. Independence is maintained through objective decision-making processes free from conflicts of interest, while fairness is manifested through the protection of shareholders' rights (including minority shareholders), equal treatment of all stakeholders, and enhanced employee engagement through annual surveys.

The implementation of such governance practices is also supported by the active roles of the Company's supporting organs. The Audit Committee as well as the Nomination and Remuneration Committee assist the Board of Commissioners in carrying out its supervisory functions, while the Corporate Secretary and the Internal Audit Unit support the Board of Directors in the execution of management functions. The Board of Commissioners is of the view that this governance framework has operated effectively throughout the 2025 Financial Year in safeguarding accountability, compliance, and stakeholder trust.

Perseroan menegaskan bahwa tata kelola perusahaan yang kuat merupakan fondasi utama dalam menjaga kinerja, reputasi, dan keberlanjutan jangka panjang. Prinsip Good Corporate Governance (GCG) dijalankan melalui transparansi, akuntabilitas, responsibilitas, independensi, dan kewajaran (fairness). Dengan penerapan prinsip-prinsip tersebut, pengambilan keputusan strategis di seluruh organisasi dijalankan secara konsisten, adil, serta selaras dengan kepentingan pemegang saham dan pemangku kepentingan lainnya.

Sepanjang Tahun Buku 2025, Dewan Komisaris berpendapat bahwa Perseroan telah secara konsisten menerapkan prinsip-prinsip tata kelola tersebut. Transparansi diwujudkan melalui keterbukaan informasi yang tepat waktu dan akurat dalam Laporan Tahunan, Laporan Keberlanjutan, Laporan Tata Kelola Perusahaan, serta pelaporan keuangan secara berkala. Akuntabilitas diperkuat melalui struktur organisasi yang jelas, pembagian peran yang terdefinisi, serta mekanisme check and balance yang didukung oleh audit eksternal independen. Responsibilitas tercermin dalam kepatuhan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan, praktik manajemen yang sehat, serta pelaksanaan tanggung jawab sosial kepada masyarakat dan lingkungan. Independensi dijaga melalui proses pengambilan keputusan yang objektif dan bebas dari benturan kepentingan, sementara kewajaran diwujudkan melalui perlindungan hak-hak pemegang saham (termasuk pemegang saham minoritas), perlakuan setara terhadap seluruh pemangku kepentingan, serta peningkatan keterikatan karyawan melalui survei tahunan.

Penerapan tata kelola tersebut juga didukung oleh peran aktif organ pendukung Perseroan. Komite Audit serta Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi yang membantu Dewan Komisaris dalam menjalankan fungsi pengawasan, sementara Sekretaris Perusahaan dan Unit Audit Internal mendukung Direksi dalam pelaksanaan fungsi pengelolaan. Dewan Komisaris menilai bahwa kerangka tata kelola ini telah berfungsi secara efektif sepanjang Tahun Buku 2025 dalam menjaga akuntabilitas, kepatuhan, dan kepercayaan pemangku kepentingan.
