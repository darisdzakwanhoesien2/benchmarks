# Advances for Right of Use Assets

As of December 31, 2025, advances for right of use assets amounted to IDR 41.9 billion. The IDR 7.8 billion decrease during 2025 was primarily due to rent expense realization.

# Fixed Assets

The Company's fixed assets as of 31 December 2025 amounted to Rp400.4 billion, which represents a decrease of 0.5% compared to last year due to the normal annual depreciation of the fixed assets.

# Deferred Tax Assets

As of 31 December 2025, the Company's deferred tax assets amounted to Rp124 billion. This represents a decrease of 2.2%, mainly attributable to deferred tax from unrealized profit on intercompany sales.

# Liabilities

The Company recorded total liabilities of Rp3.6 trillion at the end of 2025, consisting of 94% short-term liabilities and 6% long-term liabilities. The amount of liabilities increased by Rp877.7 billion or 32.5% compared to 2024, primarily due to an increase in short-term liabilities.

# Current Liabilities

In 2025, the Company's short-term liabilities amounted to Rp3.4 trillion, representing a 33.6% increase. The increase in short-term liabilities was mainly due to an increase in trade payables and other payables.

# Uang Muka Aset Hak Guna

Pada tanggal 31 Desember 2025, Uang muka aset hak guna berjumlah Rp41,9 miliar. Penurunan sebesar Rp7,8 milyar ditahun 2025 utamanya merupakan realisasi biaya sewa.

# Aset Tetap

Aset tetap Perseroan pada tanggal 31 Desember 2025 berjumlah Rp400,4 milyar, menunjukkan penurunan sebesar 0,5% dibanding tahun lalu karena depresiasi tahunan normal dari aset tetap.

# Aset Pajak Tangguhan

Pada tanggal 31 Desember 2025, aset pajak tangguhan Perusahaan berjumlah Rp124 miliar. Ini merupakan penurunan sebesar 2,2%, terutama disebabkan oleh pajak tangguhan atas laba yang belum direalisasi pada penjualan antar perusahaan.

# Liabilitas

Perseroan membukukan jumlah liabilitas sebesar Rp3,6 triliun pada akhir tahun 2025, terdiri dari 94% liabilitas jangka pendek dan 6% liabilitas jangka panjang. Jumlah liabilitas mengalami kenaikan sebesar Rp877,7 milyar atau 32,5% dibandingkan dengan tahun 2024, terutama disebabkan oleh peningkatan liabilitas jangka pendek.

# Liabilitas Jangka Pendek

# Trade Payables

The Company's trade payables at the end of 2025 amounted to Rp2.6 trillion, representing an increase of 19.2%. The increase in trade payables was mainly due to the increase in the value of inventory purchases for the distribution business segment.

# Utang Usaha

Utang usaha Perseroan pada akhir tahun 2025 adalah sebesar Rp2,6 triliun, mengalami kenaikan sebesar 19,2%. Naiknya utang usaha sebagian besar disebabkan oleh kenaikan nilai pembelian persediaan untuk segmen bisnis distribusi.
