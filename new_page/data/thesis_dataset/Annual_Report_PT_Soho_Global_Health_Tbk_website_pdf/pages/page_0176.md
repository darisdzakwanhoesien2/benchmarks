# Implementation of the Extraordinary GMS

## Pelaksanaan RUPS Luar Biasa

The Company conducted its Extraordinary GMS on February 04, 2025, at the Company's Head Office, Ruang Training Logistik, Lantai 3, Jl. Rawa Sumur II Kav. BB No. 4A-4B, Kawasan Industri Pulogadung, Kel. Jatinegara, Kec. Cakung, Jakarta Timur, 13930, DKI Jakarta. The Extraordinary GMS was attended by 10,333,340,610 shares or 81.41% of all fully paid-up shares of the Company.

The agenda discussed and resolved in the Extraordinary GMS were in accordance with the agenda stated in the Invitation of Extraordinary GMS dated January 10, 2025 delivered by the Board of Directors of the Company to all shareholders.

The Independent Parties who carried out the voting counting process and carried out the voting validation are:

- PT Datindo Entrycom, as the Securities Administration Bureau
- Jimmy Tanal, S.H., M.Kn, Notary in South Jakarta

The resolutions approved in the Extraordinary GMS held on February 04, 2025 are as follows:

### 1. First Agenda

Approval of the proposed changes to the composition of the Board of Directors of the Company.

#### Approved Resolutions:

**I) Accept and approve the resignation of the following members of the Board of Directors:**

a. Rogelio Paulino Jr. Castillo Lao, as President Director of the Company;
b. Henryk Klakurka, as Director of the Company;
c. Tan Ting Luen, as Director of the Company; and
d. Wonbae Lee, as Director of the Company

Effective as of the closing of the Meeting, and hereby grant full release and discharge (acquit-et-de-charge) to the aforementioned members of the Board of Directors from their respective responsibilities as President Director or Director of the Company, and for all actions that have been reasonably and accurately disclosed in the Company's books and records as well as audited accounts during their tenure.

**II) Approve and ratify the acceptance of the resignation of the members of the Board of Directors as mentioned in point (I) above, to be effective earlier than the 90 (ninety) days after the submission of their resignation request, as stipulated in Article 18 paragraph (16) of the Company's Articles of Association.**

- PT Datindo Entrycom, selaku Biro Administrasi Efek
- Jimmy Tanal, S.H., M.Kn, Notaris di Jakarta Selatan

Hasil keputusan yang disetujui di dalam RUPS Luar Biasa tanggal 04 Februari 2025 tersebut adalah sebagai berikut

### 1. Mata Acara Pertama

Persetujuan atas rencana perubahan susunan Direksi Perseroan.

#### Hasil Keputusan:

**I) Menerima dan menyetujui pengunduran diri anggota Direksi sebagai berikut :**

a. Rogelio Paulino Jr. Castillo Lao, sebagai Presiden Direktur Perseroan;
b. Henryk Klakurka, sebagai Direktur Perseroan;
c. Tang Ting Luen, sebagai Direktur Perseroan;
d. Wonbae Lee, sebagai Direktur Perseroan.

Terhitung sejak ditutupnya Rapat, dan dengan ini memberikan pelepasan dan pembebasan penuh (acquit-et-de-charge) kepada anggota Direksi Perseroan tersebut di atas dari tanggung jawabnya masing-masing sebagai Presiden Direktur atau Direktur Perseroan, dan untuk segala tindakan yang telah diungkapkan secara wajar dan benar dalam buku-buku dan catatan-catatan Perseroan serta akun yang telah diaudit selama masa jabatannya.

**II) Menyetujui dan mengesahkan untuk menerima pengunduran diri anggota Direksi yang mengundurkan diri sebagaimana disebutkan dalam poin (I) di atas berlaku efektif lebih cepat sebelumnya dari 90 (sembilan puluh) hari setelah diajukannya permohonan pengunduran diri anggota Direksi tersebut sebagaimana diatur dalam Pasal 18 ayat (16) Anggaran Dasar Perseroan.**
