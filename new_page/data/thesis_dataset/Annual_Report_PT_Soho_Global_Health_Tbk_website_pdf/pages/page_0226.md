# Reports of Nomination and Remuneration Committee

## Laporan Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi

Throughout the Year 2025, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee has carried out the following tasks:

1. Provide recommendations to the Board of Commissioners regarding the amount of salary and honorarium increases for members of the Board of Commissioners and members of the Board of Directors of the Company.
2. Evaluate the Company's remuneration and nomination policies.
3. Supervise the implementation of talent and succession programs for the employees of the Company and subsidiaries.
4. Propose training programs to improve competencies and skills for employees of the Company and its subsidiaries.
5. Review the appropriateness of the remuneration policy and facilities provided for the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors and employees of the Company and subsidiaries, as well as providing recommendations for necessary improvements/changes.
6. Provide updates to the Board of Commissioners regarding the latest policies and regulations that have an impact on employee remuneration.

Sepanjang Tahun 2025, Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi telah melaksanakan hal-hal sebagai berikut

1. Memberikan rekomendasi kepada Dewan Komisaris mengenai besaran kenaikan gaji dan honorarium bagi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan anggota Direksi Perseroan.
2. Mengevaluasi kebijakan remunerasi dan nominasi Perseroan.
3. Melakukan pengawasan terhadap pelaksanaan program talenta dan suksesi bagi para karyawan Perseroan dan entitas anak.
4. Mengusulkan program pelatihan untuk meningkatkan kompetensi dan keterampilan bagi para karyawan Perseroan maupun entitas anak.
5. Mengkaji kelayakan kebijakan remunerasi dan fasilitas-fasilitas yang disediakan bagi Dewan Komisaris, Direksi dan para karyawan Perseroan dan entitas anak, serta memberikan rekomendasi perbaikan/perubahan yang diperlukan.
6. Memberikan pembaruan/update kepada Dewan Komisaris terkait kebijakan dan peraturan terbaru yang berdampak terhadap remunerasi karyawan.

# Independency of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee

## Independensi Komite Nominasi Dan Remunerasi

During the Year 2025, each member of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee has acted independently and fulfilled aspects of independency in carrying out their duties, in accordance with the terms and conditions that apply as stated in the Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 34/POJK.04/2015 concerning the Nomination and Remuneration Committee of Issuers or Public Companies and the Decree of the Board of Commissioners of the Company Number 08/SK-Dekom/SGH/VI/2020 dated June 19, 2020 regarding the Establishment of Guidelines for the Company's Nomination and Remuneration.

Sepanjang Tahun 2025, setiap anggota Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi telah bertindak independen dan memenuhi aspek independensi dalam menjalankan tugasnya, sesuai dengan syarat dan ketentuan yang berlaku sebagaimana tercantum dalam Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 34/POJK.04/2015 tentang Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik dan Keputusan Dewan Komisaris Perseroan Nomor 08/SK-Dekom/SGH/VI/2020 tanggal 19 Juni 2020 tentang Pembentukan Pedoman Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi Perseroan.
