|  2. | All members of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners of the Public Company shall attend the Annual GMS.

Seluruh anggota Direksi dan anggota Dewan Komisaris Perusahaan Terbuka hadir dalam RUPS Tahunan. | The recommendations have been implemented but cannot be fully carried out due to consideration of the existence of members of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners who are domiciled outside the territory of the Republic of Indonesia.

The Company always strive to present the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners at every GMS held by the Company. The Company's Annual GMS for the Financial Year 2025 was attended by 4 (four) of the 6 (six) members of the Board of Directors and 1 (one) of the 3 (three) members of the Board of Commissioners.

The absence of members of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners occurred due to:
1. there are members of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners who are domiciled outside the territory of the Republic of Indonesia; and
2. there are important conditions or schedule conflicts that prevent several members of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners from attending the Annual GMS.

Rekomendasi telah diterapkan, namun belum dapat seluruhnya terpenuhi karena mempertimbangkan adanya anggota Direksi maupun Dewan Komisaris yang berdomisili di luar wilayah Republik Indonesia.

Perseroan senantiasa berusaha menghadirkan Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris pada setiap RUPS yang diselenggarakan oleh Perseroan. RUPS Tahunan Perseroan untuk Tahun Buku 2025 dihadiri oleh 4 (empat) dari 6 (enam) anggota Direksi dan 1 (satu) dari 3 (tiga) anggota Dewan Komisaris.

Ketidakhadiran anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris tersebut terjadi dikarenakan:
1. terdapat anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris yang berdomisili di luar wilayah Republik Indonesia; serta
2. terdapat kondisi penting atau benturan jadwal yang membuat beberapa anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris tidak dapat hadir pada RUPS Tahunan tersebut.  |
|  3. | A summary of GMS minutes should be available on the Public Company's website for at least 1 (one) year.

Ringkasan risalah RUPS tersedia dalam Situs Web Perusahaan Terbuka paling sedikit selama 1 (satu) tahun. | The recommendation has been implemented, where the obligation to disclose the summary of the GMS minutes on the Company's Website has been carried out by the Company after holding the Company's Annual GMS for the Financial Year 2025 on May 26, 2025.

Rekomendasi telah diterapkan, dimana kewajiban pengungkapan ringkasan risalah RUPS pada Situs Web Perseroan telah dilaksanakan oleh Perseroan setelah penyelenggaraan RUPS Tahunan Perseroan untuk Tahun Buku 2025 pada tanggal 26 Mei 2025.  |
