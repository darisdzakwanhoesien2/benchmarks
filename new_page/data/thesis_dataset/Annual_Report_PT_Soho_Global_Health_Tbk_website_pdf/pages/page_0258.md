## 4. PT. Rajawali Nusindo ("RNI")

Case Status
Status Perkara

PPG is a concurrent creditor of RNI. PPG has past-due receivables owed by RNI for products supplied by PPG. RNI is unable to fulfil its payment obligations to PPG. A Suspension of Debt Payment Obligations (PKPU) application has been submitted by other creditors of RNI. The PKPU process is currently still in progress.

PPG adalah kreditur konkuren RNI. PPG memiliki piutang yang telah jatuh tempo dan terutang kepada RNI atas produk yang dipasok oleh PPG dan RNI tidak mampu membayar piutang tersebut. Permohonan Penundaan Kewajiban Pembayaran Utang (PKPU) RNI telah diajukan oleh kreditur lain RNI. Hingga saat ini, proses PKPU masih berjalan.

Risks Faced by the Company and the Impact on the Company
Risiko yang Dihadapi dan Pengaruhnya terhadap Perseroan

PPG is at risk of not receiving the full payment for its receivables amounting to IDR 1,375,139,430 (one billion three hundred seventy five million one hundred thirty nine thousand four hundred and thirty Rupiah).

PPG berisiko tidak akan menerima pembayaran atas piutangnya secara penuh sebesar Rp 1.375.139.430,- (satu miliar tiga ratus tujuh puluh lima juta seratus tiga puluh sembilan ribu empat ratus tiga puluh Rupiah).

Follow-up
Tindak Lanjut

The debtors have agreed to and executed the settlement proposal, under which PPG's debt shall be settled by PT Rajawali Nusindo within period as stated in the settlement agreement dated 17 April 2025.

Para debitur telah menyetujui dan melaksanakan proposal penyelesaian, di mana utang PPG akan diselesaikan oleh PT Rajawali Nusindo dalam jangka waktu sebagaimana ditetapkan dalam perjanjian perdamaian tertanggal 17 April 2025.
