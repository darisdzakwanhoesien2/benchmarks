# Outsourced Employees

## Karyawan Alih Daya

In order to maintain operational efficiency while ensuring that the Company's remain focused on its core business activities, the Company implements an outsourcing scheme through partnerships with third-party service providers. This scheme is utilized to support non-core functions, such as security, cleaning services, administrative support, and other general operation activities.

By the end of 2025, the number of outsourced personnel supporting the Company's operations was recorded at 676 workers, spread across various business units and operational areas.

The Company ensures that its outsourcing service providers comply with applicable labor laws and regulations in Indonesia, including the fulfillment of workers' statutory rights, as well as adherence to quality standards, and Occupational Safety, and Health (OSH).

Through structured management of outsourced personnel, the Company was able to maintain service quality, enhance efficiency, and remain focused on the development of internal competencies in support of the achievement of its long-term strategic objectives.

Dalam rangka menjaga efisiensi operasional sekaligus memastikan fokus Perusahaan pada aktivitas bisnis intinya, Perusahaan menerapkan skema alih daya melalui kemitraan dengan perusahaan penyedia layanan pihak ketiga.
