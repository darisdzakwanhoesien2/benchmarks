# Administrative Sanction

## Sanksi Administratif

On March 17, 2025, the Financial Services Authority ("OJK") issued Letter No. S-544/PM.212/2025 through the Electronic Reporting System (SPE) regarding the delay in the submission and announcement of the Company's Information Disclosures No. 001/SOHO/KI/OJK-BEI/I/2025, 002/SOHO/KI/OJK-BEI/I/2025, and 003/SOHO/KI/OJK-BEI/I/2025 dated January 6, 2025 concerning changes the Board of Directors.

The Company has submitted its response through letter No. 220/SGH-OJK/III/2025 on March 18, 2025, which essentially explains that the delay did not result from the Company's negligence, but occurred during the transitional period of the implementation of the regulation, during which the Company only obtained official information regarding the enactment of POJK No. 45 of 2024 on January 20, 2025. The Company has also submitted an objection to the imposition of such sanction.

However, considering that the objection was submitted after the deadline stipulated under the prevailing OJK regulations, the Company was still subject to administrative sanctions imposed by OJK as set out in Letters No. S-442/PM.2113/2025, S-443/PM.2113/2025, and S-444/PM.2113/2025, each dated July 24, 2025, in the form of a fine of Rp2,000,000 per day for 4 (four) days for each controlled company (PT UHN, PT PPG, and PT SIP), resulting in a total fine of Rp8,000,000 per company (aggregate total of Rp24,000,000).

All fines have been fully paid by the Company on August 25, 2025.

Pada tanggal 17 Maret 2025, Otoritas Jasa Keuangan ("OJK") melalui Sistem Pelaporan Elektronik (SPE) menerbitkan Surat No. S-544/PM.212/2025 terkait keterlambatan penyampaian dan pengumuman Keterbukaan Informasi Perseroan No. 001/SOHO/KI/OJK-BEI/I/2025, 002/SOHO/KI/OJK-BEI/I/2025, dan 003/SOHO/KI/OJK-BEI/I/2025 tanggal 6 Januari 2025 mengenai perubahan Direksi.

Perseroan telah menyampaikan tanggapan melalui Surat No. 220/SGH-OJK/III/2025 tanggal 18 Maret 2025, yang pada pokoknya menjelaskan bahwa keterlambatan tersebut bukan merupakan kelalaian Perseroan, melainkan terjadi dalam masa transisi penerapan peraturan, di mana Perseroan baru memperoleh informasi resmi mengenai pemberlakuan POJK No. 45 Tahun 2024 pada tanggal 20 Januari 2025. Perseroan juga telah menyampaikan keberatan atas pengenaan sanksi tersebut.

Namun demikian, mengingat pengajuan keberatan tersebut dilakukan setelah batas waktu yang ditentukan dalam ketentuan OJK yang berlaku, Perseroan tetap dikenakan sanksi administratif oleh OJK sebagaimana tertuang dalam Surat No. S-442/PM.2113/2025, S-443/PM.2113/2025, dan S-444/PM.2113/2025 masing-masing tertanggal 24 Juli 2025, berupa denda sebesar Rp2.000.000 per hari selama 4 (empat) hari untuk masing-masing perusahaan terkendali (PT UHN, PT PPG, dan PT SIP), sehingga total denda sebesar Rp8.000.000 per perusahaan (total keseluruhan Rp24.000.000).

Seluruh denda tersebut telah dibayarkan oleh Perseroan pada tanggal 25 Agustus 2025.
