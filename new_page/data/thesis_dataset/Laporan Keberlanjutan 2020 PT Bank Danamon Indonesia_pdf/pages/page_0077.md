## Pilar Sosial

### Tanggap Bencana (TB)

Tanggap Bencana (TB) merupakan salah satu program Pilar Sosial sebagai upaya kepedulian Danamon terhadap masyarakat yang terdampak bencana yang terjadi di Indonesia. Akibat pandemi COVID-19, program TB tahun 2020 dibagi menjadi dua kegiatan, yaitu bantuan yang berfokus pada masyarakat terdampak COVID-19 dan bantuan non-COVID-19.

### 1. Bantuan Pandemi COVID-19

Danamon bekerja sama dengan Pemerintah Pusat, Pemerintah Daerah/Satuan Gugus Tugas, dan Yayasan/Lembaga Swadaya Masyarakat (LSM) dalam memberikan bantuan kepada tenaga medis, pekerja sektor informal, dan masyarakat yang terdampak oleh pandemi ini. ## Social Pillar

### Disaster Response (DR)

Disaster Response (DR) is one of the Social Pillar programs as an efforts of Danamon concerns towards the community affected by disasters in Indonesia. Due to the COVID-19 pandemic, the 2020 DR program was divided into two activities, one focusing on assistance to communities affected by COVID-19, and the second for non-COVID-19 assistance.

### 1. COVID-19 Pandemic Assistance

Danamon is working closely with the Central Government, Local Governments / Task Force Units, and Foundations/Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs) to assist medical personnel, informal sector workers, and communities affected by this pandemic.
