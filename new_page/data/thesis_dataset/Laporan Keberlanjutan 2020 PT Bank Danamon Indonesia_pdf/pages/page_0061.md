Kinerja Pemberian Kredit [F56]

Total pinjaman selama tahun 2020 mencapai Rp134.161 miliar. Kredit Danamon berdasarkan sektor ekonomi masih didominasi oleh sektor rumah tangga, pembiayaan konsumen, dan sewa pembiayaan yaitu sebesar 37% dari total kredit.

Loan Portfolio Performance [F56]

Total loan during 2020 reached Rp134,161 billion. Danamon's loan by economic sector is still dominated by the household, consumer financing, and finance leases sectors, amounting to 37% of total loan.
