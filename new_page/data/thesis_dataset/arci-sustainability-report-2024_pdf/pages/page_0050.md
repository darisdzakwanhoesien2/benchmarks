Penanggung Jawab Utama Keberlanjutan ([GRI 2-14] Key Persons Responsible for Sustainability

|  Uraian Description | Tegas dan Tanggung Jawab Duties and Responsibilities  |
| --- | --- |
|  Dewan Komisaris | Bertanggung jawab dalam melaksanakan pengawasan dan pemberian nasihat atas pengelolaan aspek keberlanjutan. Pelaksanaan fungsi ini dibantu oleh Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi, Komite Audit, dan Komite Etik.  |
|  Board of Commissioners | Responsible for supervisory and advisory duties on the management of sustainability aspects. The implementation of this function is assisted by Nomination and Remuneration Committee, Audit Committee, and Ethics Committee.  |
|  Direksi | Bertanggung jawab penuh untuk mengoordinasikan kebijakan dan pengelolaan aspek keberlanjutan. Pelaksanaan fungsi ini akan dibantu oleh Komite dan unit kerja di bawah Direksi, yaitu Komite Pengeluaran, Komite Investasi, Sekretaris Perusahaan, dan Unit Audit Internal.  |
|  Board of Directors | Full responsibility for coordinating policies and managing sustainability aspects. The implementation of this function is assisted by committees and work units under the Board of Directors, namely Expenditure Committee, Investment Committee, Corporate Secretary, and Internal Audit Unit.  |
