# KOMPAS TUJUAN PEMBANGUNAN BERKELANJUTAN (TPB)

WIKA Beton continues to strengthen its efforts in achieving sustainability by referring to the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) that are prioritized by the Company. Throughout 2024, various initiatives and tangible achievements have been realized as part of its commitment to sustainable development.
