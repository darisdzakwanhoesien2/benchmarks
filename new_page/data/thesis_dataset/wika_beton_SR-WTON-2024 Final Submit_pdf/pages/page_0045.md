# Manajemen Topik yang Berdampak terhadap Pemangku Kepentingan Eksternal

Management of Topics Impacting External Stakeholders

WIKA Beton berkomitmen untuk berkontribusi secara nyata terhadap keberlanjutan sosial dan lingkungan melalui operasional, produk, dan layanan yang dihasilkan. Fokus perusahaan tercermin dalam dua topik material prioritas, yakni emisi dan masyarakat lokal, yang mencerminkan komitmen perusahaan terhadap keberlanjutan sosial dan lingkungan.

WIKA Beton is committed to making a tangible contribution to social and environmental sustainability through its operations, products, and services. This commitment is reflected in the company's two priority material topics, namely emissions and local communities, which underscore its dedication to social and environmental sustainability.
