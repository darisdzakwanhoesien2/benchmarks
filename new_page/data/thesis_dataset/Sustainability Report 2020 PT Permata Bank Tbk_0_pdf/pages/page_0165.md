# Manajemen Risiko

Risk Management

PermataBank menerapkan manajemen risiko secara terpadu, dari tahap awal hingga akhir, guna mengendalikan dan mengoptimalkan profil risiko terhadap pendapatannya. Penerapan manajemen risiko merupakan aktivitas bankwide yang merupakan cerminan komitmen PermataBank terhadap prinsip kehati-hatian. Di PermataBank, manajemen risiko dikelola melalui Komite Manajemen Risiko (RMC) dan Komite Aset &amp; Liabilitas (ALCO) dan Komite Manajemen Risiko Terintegrasi (KMRT) di tingkat Direksi serta Komite Pemantau Risiko (RMoC) di tingkat Dewan Komisaris.

Sesuai dengan Kebijakan Manajemen Risiko Perusahaan, PermataBank mengelola 14 (empat belas) tipe risiko utama, yaitu risiko kredit, pasar, operasional, likuiditas jangka pendek, likuiditas struktural, hukum, kepatuhan, reputasi, strategik, intra-grup, permodalan, keamanan informasi, imbal hasil, dan risiko investasi. Sesuai dengan Peraturan OJK, PermataBank telah melaporkan profil risiko yang disusun untuk 11 risiko utama yaitu risiko kredit, pasar, operasional, likuiditas, hukum, kepatuhan, reputasi, strategik, intra-grup, imbal hasil, dan risiko investasi. Laporan profil risiko PermataBank secara individual, konsolidasi, dan Unit Usaha Syariah tersebut disampaikan kepada OJK secara triwulanan dan untuk profil risiko terintegrasi dilaporkan secara semesteran.

## Implementasi Kebijakan, Prosedur, dan Limit Eksposur

PermataBank menggunakan kerangka pengendalian sebagai pedoman yang jelas dan tegas mengenai batasan dalam pengambilan risiko. Tiga komponen kunci dalam kerangka pengendalian risiko, yang secara kolektif menyediakan mekanisme untuk mengatur dan menyesuaikan batasan dalam pengambilan risiko adalah kebijakan, limit eksposur, dan kerangka delegasi wewenang risiko.

To control and optimize the risk profile against its income, PermataBank implements an integrated risk management, from the initial stage to the end. The implementation of risk management is a bank-wide activity as a reflection of PermataBank's commitment to the principle of prudence. In PermataBank, the risk management is exercised by the Risk Management Committee (RMC), Asset &amp; Liability Committee (ALCO) and Integrated Risk Management Committee (IRMC) at the level of the Board of Directors also Risk Monitoring Committee (RMoC) at the level of the Board of Commissioners.

In accordance with the Enterprise Risk Management Policy, PermataBank manages 14 principal risk types, which are credit, market, operational, short-term liquidity, structural liquidity, legal, compliance, reputation, strategy, intra-group, capital, information security, rate of return, and investment risks. In accordance with the prevailing regulations, PermataBank has reported risk profile for 11 principal risks which are credit, market, operational, liquidity, legal, compliance, reputation, strategy, intra-group, rate of return, and investment risks. PermataBank's individual risk profile, PermataBank's individual risk profile, consolidation, and Sharia Business Unit report are submitted to OJK on a quarterly basis and integrated risk profiles are submitted to OJK on semi-annual basis.

## Implementation of Policies, Procedures, and Exposure Thresholds

PermataBank utilizes a control framework as a clear and firm guideline regarding thresholds in risk taking. The three key components of risk control framework, which collectively provide a mechanism to regulate and adjust thresholds on risk taking, are policies, exposure thresholds, and frameworks for delegating risk authority.
