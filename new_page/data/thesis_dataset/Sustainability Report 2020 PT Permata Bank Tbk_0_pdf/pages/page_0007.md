# Tonggak Sejarah Keberlanjutan 2020

2020 Sustainability Milestones

1. Pengambilalihan 89,12% saham PermataBank dari PT Astra International Tbk dan Standard Chartered Bank oleh Bangkok Bank Public Company Limited.
Acquisition of 89.12% of PermataBank's shares from PT Astra International Tbk and Standard Chartered Bank by Bangkok Bank Public Company Limited.

2. Integrasi Bangkok Bank Public Company Limited, cabang Jakarta, cabang pembantu Medan, dan cabang pembantu Surabaya ke PermataBank di mana PermataBank bertindak sebagai bank penerima integrasi.
Integration of Bangkok Bank Public Company Limited Jakarta branch, Medan, and Surabaya sub-branches into PermataBank in which PermataBank was the receiving bank in the integration.
