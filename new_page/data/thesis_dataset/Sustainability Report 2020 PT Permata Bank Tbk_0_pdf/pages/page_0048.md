PermataHati menginginkan kualitas edukasi dan kehidupan yang lebih baik bagi generasi muda Indonesia. Dengan slogan yang berbunyi "Senyummu untuk Masa Depan", PermataHati berkomitmen membawa perubahan bagi komunitas-komunitas, termasuk penyandang disabilitas, melalui beragam program yang berfokus pada edukasi literasi finansial, peningkatan kualitas kehidupan, serta pemberdayaan yang berkelanjutan.

PermataHati envisions a better quality of education and life for Indonesian youth. Through its slogan "Your Smile For the Future", PermataHati is committed to making a difference for its communities including people with disabilities, through programs focused on financial literacy education, life enhancement as well as long term empowerment.
