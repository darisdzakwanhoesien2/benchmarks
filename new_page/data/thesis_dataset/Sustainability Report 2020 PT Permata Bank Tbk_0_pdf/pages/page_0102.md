## Program Kesehatan Karyawan [403-3]

Menjaga kesehatan diri dan keluarga adalah tanggung jawab setiap individu. Dalam rangka kesejahteraan karyawan, Bank memberikan bantuan kesehatan kepada karyawan. Bank memberikan bantuan pengobatan dan perawatan melalui fasilitas BPJS Kesehatan (selanjutnya disebut "BPJS") dan/atau Asuransi Kesehatan (selanjutnya disebut "Asuransi"). BPJS dan Asuransi berlaku bagi karyawan dan keluarga yang berhak sesuai kebijakan PermataBank.

## Employee Health Program [403-3]

Maintaining one's own health and their family's is the responsibility of every individual. To promote employee welfare, the Bank provides health benefits to its employees. The Bank covers medication and treatment through BPJS Kesehatan (hereafter is "BPJS") and/or Health Insurance (hereafter is "Insurance"). BPJS and Insurance can be claimed by the Bank's employees and their families according to PermataBank's policy.
