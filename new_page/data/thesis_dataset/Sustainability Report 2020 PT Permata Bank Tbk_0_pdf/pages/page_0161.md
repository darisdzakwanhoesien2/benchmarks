Governance Structure of PermataBank

## Dewan Komisaris [102-22, 102-23]

Dewan Komisaris PermataBank melakukan tugas utama mengawasi kebijakan Direksi dalam menjalankan pengurusan PermataBank serta memberikan nasihat kepada Direksi. Dalam pelaksanaan tugasnya, Dewan Komisaris juga dibantu oleh komite-komite.

Dewan Komisaris menyampaikan laporan pertanggungjawaban pengawasan atas pengelolaan PermataBank serta pencapaian target-target kinerja dalam Key Performance Indicator (KPI) yang dilakukan bersamasama dengan/oleh Direksi kepada RUPS dalam rangka memperoleh pembebasan dan pelunasan tanggung jawab (acquit et de charge) dari RUPS.

Dewan Komisaris diangkat dan mempertanggung jawabkan palaksanaan tugasnya melalui RUPS. Komposisi Dewan Komisaris per tanggal 31 Desember 2020 terdiri atas 1 orang Komisaris Utama, empat orang Komisaris Independen dan tiga orang Komisaris. Pelaksanaan tugas Dewan Komisaris dijalankan dengan berpedoman kepada Board Manual Dewan Komisaris.

## Board of Commissioners [102-22, 102-23]

The Board of Commissioners of PermataBank performs the core duties of overseeing policies made by the Board of Directors in managing PermataBank, and providing advice to the Board of Directors. In exercising its duties, the Board of Commissioners is also assisted by committees.

The Board of Commissioners submits a supervisory accountability report on the management of PermataBank and the achievement of performance targets in the Key Performance Indicator (KPI) carried out jointly with/by the Board of Directors to the GMS in order to obtain release and discharge of responsibility (acquit et de charge) from the GMS.

The Board of Commissioners is appointed and accounts for the exercise of its duties through the GMS. The composition of the Board of Commissioners as of 31 December 2020 constitutes of one President Commissioner, four Independent Commissioners and three Commissioners. The duties of the Board of Commissioners is exercised by referring to the Board Manual of the Board of Commissioners.
