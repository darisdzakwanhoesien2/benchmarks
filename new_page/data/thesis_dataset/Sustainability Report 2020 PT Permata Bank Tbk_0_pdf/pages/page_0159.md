# Struktur Tata Kelola PermataBank

Governance Structure of PermataBank

Sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku, best practice yang dilaksanakan oleh berbagai organisasi berskala global, serta untuk mendukung penerapan praktik terbaik GCG yang konsisten dan efektif, struktur tata kelola PermataBank terdiri atas beberapa organ tata kelola utama, sebagai berikut:

- Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS)
- Dewan Komisaris
- Direksi
- Komite-komite di bawah fungsi Dewan Komisaris:
- Komite Audit
- Komite Pemantau Risiko
- Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi
- Komite Tata Kelola Terintegrasi
- Internal Audit
- Komite-komite yang berada di bawah Fungsi Direksi

Pursuant to prevailing regulations, and best practices exercised by various global scale organizations, as well as to support the consistent and effective implementation of GCG best practice, the governance structure of PermataBank constitutes the following corporate governance organs:

- General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS)
- Board of Commissioners
- Board of Directors
- Committees under the functions of the Board of Commissioners:
- Audit Committee
- Risk Monitoring Committee
- Remuneration and Nomination Committee
- Integrated Governance Committee
- Internal Audit
- Committees under the functions of the Board of Directors
