## Speak-Up

Speak-Up merupakan sistem whistleblowing berupa sarana/media bagi PermataBankers untuk melaporkan pelanggaran-pelanggaran dan malpraktek, baik berupa fraud maupun pelanggaran Kode Etik, termasuk kecurigaan atas tindakan pelanggaran. Speak-Up diperkenalkan di PermataBank sejak tahun 2008.

## Mekanisme Speak-up

Semua karyawan PermataBank dapat melaporkan setiap indikasi terjadinya pelanggaran (fraud) dan diwajibkan melaporkan setiap pelanggaran yang dilakukan oleh karyawan lain kepada manajemen melalui saluran Speak-Up yang telah disediakan berupa surat elektronik, layanan pesan singkat (SMS), telepon, dan surat (PO Box). Manajemen PermataBank akan melakukan evaluasi dan analisis terhadap semua laporan yang diperoleh melalui Speak-Up, dan selanjutnya melakukan tindakan lanjut yang melibatkan unit kerja terkait, seperti Fraud Risk Management, Internal Audit, Legal, Human Resources ataupun pihak lain, jika diperlukan.

Mekanisme Sistem Whistleblowing (Speak-Up) di PermataBank, dilakukan dengan memperhatikan prinsip-prinsip berikut:

- Laporan bersifat rahasia. Hanya administrator dan Speak-Up Council yang telah ditunjuk manajemen yang memiliki wewenang untuk mengetahui isi laporan.
- Pelapor dapat memilih 1 dari tiga prinsip anonim, yaitu: full disclosure (mencantumkan identitas), partial anonymity (identitas hanya disampaikan kepada administrator) atau anonymous (tidak mencantumkan identitas).
- Pihak pelapor dibebaskan dari segala sanksi bila ternyata laporannya tidak dapat dibuktikan kebenarannya.
- PermataBank menjamin semua pihak pelapor bahwa laporan-laporan mereka tidak akan mempengaruhi penilaian kinerja dan/atau perjalanan karier mereka di PermataBank.

## Speak-Up

Speak-Up is a whistleblowing system in the form of a facility/media for PermataBankers to report infringements and malpractices, whether they are fraud or violations against the Code of Conduct, including suspicion of violations. PermataBank has introduced Speak-Up since 2008.

## Speak-up Mechanism

All PermataBank's employees may report any indication of violation (or fraud) and are required to report any infringements committed by other employees to the company's management via the Speak-Up channel provided in the form of electronic mail, short message service (SMS), telephone and mail (PO Box). PermataBank's management will evaluate and analyze all reports obtained through Speak-Up, and then carry out follow-up actions involving relevant working units, such as Fraud Risk Management, Internal Audit, Legal, Human Resources or other parties, if necessary.

The Whistleblowing System mechanism (Speak-Up) at PermataBank is implemented by paying attention to the following principles:

- All reports are confidential. Only Speak-Up administrators and council, who is previously appointed by the management, have the authority to know the contents of the report.
- The whistleblowers may select one of three principles of anonymity, i.e.: full disclosure (identity is included), partial anonymity (identity is only submitted to the administrator) or anonymous (identity in not included).
- The informant will be not be held accountable if his/her report is unproven.
- PermataBank guarantees that the report submitted by an informant will not affect his/her performance evaluation/career in PermataBank.
