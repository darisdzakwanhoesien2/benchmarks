## 2. Retail Banking

a. Apply inclusive financing through loan channeling partnership with P2P lending fintech and Rural Banks and distribute Microfinancing to help MSMEs and provide financial solutions for retirees in the informal sector and the bottom of the pyramid.

b. Provide financing for SMEs that are in the retail industry, trading industry, processing industry, and service industry. PermataBank also supports businesses that help the government to prudently protect and manage the environment, for example by financing SME customers involved in non-metal recycling sector and freshwater biota conservation, among others.

c. Develop digital banking for account opening and transactions, including for communication with customers through e-channels to provide simple, fast, and reliable services while reducing paper use significantly.

## 3. Sharia Business Unit

To improve digital services to facilitate sharia banking transactions among customers of PermataBank. This digital service improvement not only aims to reduce paper use but also provides convenience for customers to perform banking transactions.
