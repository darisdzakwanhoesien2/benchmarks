# PERMATA YOUTHPRENEUR

![img-69.jpeg](img-69.jpeg)

PermataYouthPrenuer (PYP) adalah program pelatihan di bidang kewirausahaan digital dan literasi keuangan yang ditujukan bagi para siswa/i tingkat SMA/K dan yang setaranya selama periode tiga bulan pembelajaran. Di tahun 2020 ini, dengan mengangkat tema "Challenging Future Fintech Leaders", PermataBank bekerjasama dengan Arkademy dan didukung oleh Kementerian Pendidikan dan Kebudayaan mengemas program PYP dalam rangkaian diskusi dan pelatihan, modul kurikulum online, mentoring, agar peserta mampu mengembangkan ide produk fintech masing-masing.

Selama bulan April hingga Juni 2020 peserta disuguhi oleh modul seperti Business Validation, Product Validation, Software Development, Financial Literacy, hingga soft skills dalam bentuk Presentation dan Pitching Skill yang merupakan hasil kerjasama PermataBank dengan Arkademy dan didukung oleh Kementerian Pendidikan dan Kebudayaan, Direktorat Jenderal Pendidikan Dasar dan Menengah, Direktorat Pembinaan Sekolah Menengah Kejuruan.

Usai mengikuti program, peserta terpilih akan mendapatkan berbagai kesempatan pengembangan diri mulai dari beasiswa sekolah coding hingga magang di perusahaan startup. Melalui program ini diharapkan para peserta dapat mengembangkan kemampuannya menuju startup digital dan dapat menginspirasi generasi muda Indonesia untuk tumbuh menjadi calon pemimpin bisnis digital yang sukses, siap menghadapi tantangan global dan berguna bagi masyarakat.

PermataYouth Preneur (PYP) is a three-month training program on digital entrepreneurship and digital literacy intended for vocational school students or their equivalents. In 2020, with the theme "Challenging Future Fintech Leaders", PermataBank collaborated with Arkademy and gained the support from Ministry of Education and Culture to hold a PYP program that included discussion and training, online curriculum module, and mentoring, with the goal of providing insights to participants to develop their own fintech products.

From April to June 2020, the participants were given modules on Business Validation, Product Validation, Software Development, Financial Literacy, and soft skills training on Presentation and Pitching Skill. The activity was conducted as a partnership between PermataBank and Arkademy and with the support from Education and Culture Ministry, the Elementary and Middle Education Directorate General, and the Vocational School Development Directorate.

After being enrolled in the program, shortlisted participants will get various opportunities to personal development ranging from coding scholarship to internship at start-up companies. Through this program, it is expected that the participants can develop their skills to face the era of digital startup and inspire Indonesian youths to become successful leaders in the digital business who are ready to tackle global challenges and contribute to society.
