## Program Pensiun

Selain paket kesejahteraan tersebut, PermataBank juga menyelenggarakan program pensiun dengan skema iuran pensiun dengan kontribusi tetap, di mana kewajiban iuran premi pensiun dibayarkan PermataBank dengan persentase tertentu dari gaji pokok karyawan.

Untuk PermataBankers yang akan memasuki masa pensiun normal, PermataBank memberikan program manfaat pensiun. Adapun program-program tersebut antara lain kesempatan untuk mengikuti pelatihan menjelang pensiun, pengambilan "day off" maksimal 1 (satu) minggu per bulan serta tambahan uang manfaat pensiun.

## Hidup Berkeseimbangan

PermataBank memiliki program rutin yang dijalankan dalam rangka meningkatkan efektivitas dan produktivitas karyawan, serta penerapan budaya hidup sehat dan seimbang. Program tersebut bernama PermataClub yang dikelola oleh Direktorat HR.

With the above maternity leave policy, during 2020, the number of female employees who had applied maternity leave was 297 employees. Meanwhile, the number of male employees who took leave due to their wives' giving birth was 187 employees. [103-3, 401-3]

In addition to maternity leave, PermataBank also offers Unpaid Leave on a case-by-case basis on the recommendation and approval of relevant officials. During the time when an employee takes Unpaid Leave, their rights to health benefits and other benefits are intact. In 2020, the number of employees who took Unpaid Leave was 4 employees.

## Pension Program

Besides the welfare package, PermataBank also implements a pension program with fixed contribution in which the premium is paid by PermataBank at certain percentage of the employees' salary.

For PermataBankers who are about to enter a normal retirement period, PermataBank offers pension benefits programs. These programs include the opportunity to be enrolled in a pre-retirement training, a week's worth of "day off" per month and pension allowance.

## Balanced Life

PermataBank has an ongoing routine program that aims to improve employee effectiveness and productivity and encourage healthy and balanced lifestyle. This program is called PermataClub which is run by the HR Directorate.
