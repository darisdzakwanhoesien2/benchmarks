# Aksi PermataHati untuk Covid-19
## PermataHati for Covid-19 [103-2, 103-3]

- Berbagai inisiatif dari Bisnis dan Divisi yakni Wholesale Banking, Retail Banking, Network, Technology &amp; Operations serta UPZ PermataSyariah juga telah dilakukan selama masa pandemi, melalui program-program produk segmen klien maupun karyawan. Bantuan yang diberikan untuk pemangku kepentingan eksternal ditujukan kepada masyarakat yang terdampak secara ekonomi serta para petugas kesehatan, pelaku industri perhotelan hingga masyarakat termarginalkan.

- Gerakan penggalangan dana karyawan #DenganHati Challenge: 08.05, sebuah aksi donasi online serentak dari karyawan PermataBank yang dilakukan #DiRumahAja ini diselenggarakan pada 08.05 yaitu tanggal 8 Mei selama 8 jam, diwujudkan dalam 5 aksi kebaikan, menyentuh 5 target penerima manfaat, serentak di 50 kota di Indonesia. Hasil sumbangan sebesar Rp890 juta berhasil terkumpul dan didistribusikan langsung kepada lima penerima manfaat yang terdampak dan membutuhkan bantuan selama masa pandemi Covid-19, yakni Tenaga Medis, Pengemudi Taksi dan Ojek Online, Penyandang Disabilitas, Petugas Jenazah dan Penggali Jenazah, serta Guru Honorer dan Pelaku UKM. Pengumpulan donasi online dalam Gerakan #DenganHati Challenge: 08.05 juga didukung dengan pemanfaatan inovasi digital PermataBank yang diterapkan para karyawan melalui PermataMobile X.

- Dukungan bagi mitra-mitra PermataHati CSR selama masa pandemi berupa paket data bagi para murid dan guru dari sekolah SD, SMP, SMA, dan SLB untuk mendukung kegiatan belajar dan memastikan kegiatan belajar tidak terputus.

- Edukasi melalui Media Sosial dan Internal Pendidikan masih menjadi fokus utama PermataHati. Melalui akun resmi Twitter, Facebook, dan Instagram @PermataHatiCSR, PermataHati secara rutin memberikan edukasi, tips produktif beraktivitas #DiRumahAja, serta ajakan kepada masyarakat akan pentingnya mengikuti anjuran social distancing untuk memutus rantai penyebaran virus Covid-19. Mereka memberikan contoh yang baik selama menjalankan aktivitasnya sehari-hari, baik bekerja dari rumah

- Several initiatives by Businesses and Divisions such as Wholesale Banking, Retail Banking, Network, Technology &amp; Operations and UPZ PermataSyariah had also been executed during the pandemic, through client-segmented or employee-segmented programs. The aid for external stakeholders was given to economically impacted communities and healthcare workers, the hotel industry, and underprivileged communities.

- #DenganHati Challenge (Employee Fund-Raising Program): 08.05, was an online donation program initiated by PermataBank employees with the hashtag #diRumahAja on 08.05 – the 8th of May – for 8 hours, realized in 5 good deeds, for 5 beneficiaries, simultaneously in 50 cities in Indonesia. The raised amount of Rp890 million was distributed directly to five beneficiaries who were affected by and in need of help during the Covid-19 pandemic. These five beneficiaries included Healthcare Workers, Taxi Drivers and Online Transport Drivers, People with Disabilities, Morticians and Cemetery Workers, and Honorary Teachers and SMEs. The donation program #DenganHati Challenge: 08.05 was also supported by PermataBank’s digital innovation implemented by employees via PermataMobile X.

- Support for PermataHati CSR partners during the pandemic was given to students and teachers of Elementary School, Middle School, High School, and Extraordinary School in the form of internet quota to facilitate teaching and learning process.

- Education via Social and Internal Media
Education is still at the core of PermataHati. Through Twitter, Facebook, dan Instagram accounts @PermataHatiCSR, PermataHati routinely educate, give tips and invite the public to comply with social distancing recommendation to curb the spread of the Covid-19 viruses. PermataHati provide good examples on how to manage daily activities, ranging from working from home to serving customers at the office, living healthily, and maintaining hygiene
