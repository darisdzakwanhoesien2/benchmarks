Segmen keuangan inklusif PermataBank adalah dengan menyalurkan pembiayaan untuk membantu keuangan UMKM dan memberikan solusi finansial untuk para pensiunan bagi masyarakat melalui kerja sama loan channelling dan menyalurkan Kredit Usaha Rakyat (KUR). Kerja sama loan channelling dilakukan PermataBank dengan P2P lending fintech yang telah mendapat ijin dari OJK dan Badan Perkreditan Rakyat dimana dalam memberikan fasilitas kredit, mitra bank ini wajib mematuhi peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku.

PermataBank's inclusive finance segment provides funding to help MSMEs and financial solutions for retirees and communities through loan channelling partnership and Microfinancing. The loan channelling partnership is between PermataBank and P2P lending fintech that has been approved by the OJK and Rural Bank. In providing loan facilities, the partners shall comply with the prevailing laws and regulations.

## B. Financing for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) in line with the Implementation of Sustainable Finance

PermataBank focuses on nurturing the growth of SMEs that are in the retail industry, trading industry, processing industry, and service industry. PermataBank also fosters businesses that support the government in protecting and managing the environment more responsibly, for example by financing SME customers involved in non-metal recycling sector and in freshwater biota conservation, among others.
