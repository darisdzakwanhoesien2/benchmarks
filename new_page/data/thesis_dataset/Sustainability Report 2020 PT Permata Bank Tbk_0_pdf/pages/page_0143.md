# HSE Management Audit

Pengelolaan K3L tidak terlepas dari penerapan kebijakan yang dimiliki oleh PermataBank dalam mencapai tujuan bersama di bidang K3L. Salah satu cara untuk mengukur efektifitas pengelolaan K3L, PermataBank menerapkan HSE Management Audit dengan beberapa rating penilaian meliputi hitam, merah, biru, hijau, dan emas. [103-2]

# HSE Management Audit

HSE management cannot be separated from the implementation of policies by PermataBank in achieving HSE common goals. One way to measure the effectiveness of HSE management is that PermataBank performs an HSE Management Audit with several ratings consisting of black, red, blue, green, and gold. [103-2]

# Skema Gedung Ramah Lingkungan

Sejak 2009, PermataBank menerapkan sistem rating bagi gedung-gedung kantor PermataBank yaitu Building Environmental Assessment Standard (BEAS). BEAS mengukur dampak operasional dari gedung-gedung GEMS PermataBank. Termasuk dalam kriteria BEAS adalah pengelolaan emisi Gas Rumah Kaca (GRK), konsumsi listrik, dan konsumsi air.

# Environmentally Friendly Building Scheme

Since 2009, PermataBank have used a rating system to assess the offices of PermataBank called Building Environmental Assessment Standard (BEAS). BEAS measures the operational impacts of PermataBank's GEMS buildings. Included in the BEAS criteria are the Greenhouse Gas (GHG), electricity consumption, and water consumption aspects.
