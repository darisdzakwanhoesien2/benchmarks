Program-program PermataHati 2020

2020 PermataHati Programs

Mengindahkan Peraturan Pemerintah mengenai Pembatasan Sosial Berskala Besar (PSBB), PermataBank menginisiasi kegiatan CSR ini dalam bentuk donasi online, menyalurkan bantuannya tanpa harus turun ke lapangan. Hasil sumbangan yang terkumpul didistribusikan langsung melalui para mitra penyalur bantuan, kepada lima profesi yang terdampak dan membutuhkan bantuan selama masa pandemi Covid-19, yakni Tenaga Medis, Pengemudi Taksi dan Ojek Online, Penyandang Disabilitas, Petugas Jenazah dan Penggali Jenazah, serta Guru Honorer dan Pelaku UKM.

Terkumpul sebanyak Rp889.577.590 selama 8 jam sejak dibuka dari jam 8 pagi, yang merupakan hasil kontribusi lebih dari 4.500 karyawan PermataBank. Pengumpulan donasi online dalam Gerakan #DenganHati Challenge: 08.05 juga didukung dengan pemanfaatan inovasi digital PermataBank yang diterapkan para karyawan melalui PermataMobile X, sehingga aksi kebaikan bersama ini dapat terlaksana walaupun para karyawan yang berdonasi tetap di rumah saja.

Due to the Large-Scale Social Restriction (PSBB) initiated by the government, PermataBank held its CSR activities online without direct involvement in the field. The donation was distributed directly to five beneficiaries who were affected by and in need of help during the Covid-19 pandemic. These five beneficiaries included Healthcare Workers, Taxi Drivers and Online Transport Drivers, People with Disabilities, Morticians and Cemetery Workers, and Honorary Teachers and SMEs.

The total donation of Rp889,577,590, which was raised in 8 hours beginning from 8 A.M., was possible because of the contribution from more than 4,500 PermataBank employees. The online donation #DenganHati Challenge: 08.05 was also supported by PermataBank's digital innovation implemented by employees through PermataMobile X, which made it possible for the employees to run this activity while staying home.
