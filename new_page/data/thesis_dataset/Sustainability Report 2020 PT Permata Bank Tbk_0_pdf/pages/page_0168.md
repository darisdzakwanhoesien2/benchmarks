# Kode Etik

## Code of Conduct

Kode Etik Perilaku Karyawan (Code of Conduct) merupakan panduan bagi PermataBankers dalam bersikap dan bertingkah laku sesuai dengan prinsip-prinsip moral atau nilai-nilai mengenai hal-hal yang dianggap baik dan tidak baik, serta hal-hal yang benar dan tidak benar. Melalui Kode Etik Perilaku Karyawan diharapkan PermataBankers dapat selalu bersikap hati-hati, cermat dan cerdas saat menghadapi hal-hal yang berpotensi menimbulkan risiko yang dapat merugikan perusahaan (memiliki Risk Awareness).

Kode Etik Perilaku Karyawan PermataBank berisikan etika perilaku karyawan untuk:

- Mematuhi peraturan internal PermataBank, peraturan Bank Indonesia dan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan serta peraturan perundangan lainnya yang berlaku.
- Menolak tindakan penyuapan dan korupsi.
- Menghindari berkomporomi karena hadiah dan hiburan (entertainment).
- Speak-up.
- Mencegah pencucian uang dan fraud.
- Menghindari benturan kepentingan.
- Tidak bertransaksi ketika memiliki informasi orang dalam.
- Tidak melakukan mis-sell atau misrepresent produk dan jasa PermataBank.
- Cepat dan tanggap dalam menangani keluhan nasabah.
- Menjaga kerahasiaan dan perlindungan informasi dan data.
- Memperlakukan karyawan dengan adil.
- Terbuka dan jujur kepada regulator.
- Sikap dan perilaku.
- Penggunaan peralatan dan fasilitas PermataBank.
- Aktivitas berpolitik.

## Pengenalan dan Sosialisasi Kode Etik [102-17]

Pengenalan Kode Etik Perilaku Karyawan dilakukan sejak karyawan baru bergabung dan dilakukan penyegaran secara periodik selama karyawan masih bekerja di PermataBank. Sosialisasi kode etik dilakukan dengan pelatihan melalui e-learning terkait Kode Etik dan Budaya Kepatuhan dan prosedur anti korupsi.

Employee Code of Conduct is a guideline for PermataBankers in behaving and acting pursuant to the moral principles or values related to matters considered good and bad, as well as true and false. Through the Employee Code of Conduct, PermataBankers are expected to always act prudently, carefully and intelligently when dealing with matters which potentially can cause risks to the company (having Risk Awareness).

PermataBank's Employee Code of Conduct contains ethical behavior for employees to:

- Comply with PermataBank's internal regulations, Bank Indonesia and Financial Services Authority regulations as well as other prevailing laws and regulations.
- Reject bribery and corruption.
- Avoid compromises on gifts and entertainment.
- Speak-up.
- Prevent money laundering and fraud.
- Avoid conflict of interest.
- Not transact while obtaining insider information.
- Not mis-sell or misrepresent PermataBank's products and services.
- Be quick and responsive in handling customer complaints.
- Maintain the confidentiality and protection of information and data.
- To treat employees fairly.
- Be open and honest with regulators.
- Behave and act appropriately.
- Utilize PermataBank's equipment and facilities.
- Not take part in practical political activities.

## Introduction and Socialization of Code of Conduct [102-17]

The introduction of the Code of Conduct is carried out at the beginning when the new employee joins the company and is periodically refreshed while he/she is still working at PermataBank. The socialization of the Code of Conduct is carried out in training through e-learning about the Code of Conduct and Compliance.
