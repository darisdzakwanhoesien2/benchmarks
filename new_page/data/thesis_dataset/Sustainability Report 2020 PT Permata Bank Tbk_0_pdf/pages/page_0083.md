# Meningkatkan produktivitas dalam krisis

Produktivitas karyawan meningkat di mana pendapatan per karyawan naik 11,6% dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya. Hal ini dicapai melalui pertimbangan yang cermat dalam mengalokasikan tenaga kerja, sejalan dengan penerapan teknologi baru dan peningkatan skala bisnis pada saat yang bersamaan.

# Laser-sharp focus pada hal yang penting

Selain melindungi diri dan nasabah dari pandemi, dan menjaga agar proses integrasi dilakukan tepat waktu, seluruh karyawan tetap fokus untuk mencapai hasil dari bisnis utama Bank. Sesi komunikasi jarak jauh secara rutin dilakukan dengan memanfaatkan teknologi untuk menyelaraskan seluruh PermataBankers pada prioritas utama. Sesi interaktif on-line telah menghubungkan secara langsung 2.000 PermataBankers dengan manajemen. Strategi, rencana, dan target untuk tahun 2020 diuji, dikomunikasikan, diselaraskan, dan disampaikan secara rutin, terus menerus dan teliti.

# Improving productivity in a crisis

Employee productivity has improved as revenue per employee rose by 11.6% compared to previous year. This was achieved through studious and careful considerations in allocating workforce in line with the implementation of new technologies and scaling the business at the same time.

We increased stability by successfully managing attrition risks and secured a record low voluntary attrition rate of 8.21%. Proactive actions were executed by all line management levels to identify and creatively find new ways in delivering better quality and higher volumes by maintaining resources at a steady level. We were able to internally source more than two thirds of our senior leadership positions.

# Laser-sharp focus on what is important

Aside from protecting ourselves and our customers from the pandemic, and securing on-time integration process, we kept everyone focused on key business output. Far reaching and frequent communication sessions were enabled through technology to align all PermataBankers on key priorities. Interactive on-line sessions were able to connect 2,000 PermataBankers directly with the management in one go. Strategy, plans and targets for 2020 were tested, communicated, aligned and cascaded with continuous rigor.
