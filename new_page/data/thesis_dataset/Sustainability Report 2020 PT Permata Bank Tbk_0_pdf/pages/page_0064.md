Dari 105 penerima manfaat BBRVPD di Semester I tahun 2020, PermataBRAVE Hub menyeleksi hingga 40 peserta untuk mengikuti seluruh rangkaian program selama tiga bulan bekerja sama dengan Perbanas Institute serta para pakar di bidangnya.

Di akhir program, peserta terbaik diharapkan dapat melanjutkan Praktek Belajar Kerja (PBK) di PermataBank sebagai karyawan ataupun di mitra-mitra bisnis binaan PermataBank sebagai pelaku usaha.

# Kurikulum PermataBRAVE 2020

- Motivation.
- Banking &amp; Financial Institution.
- Regulatory &amp; Complaint Handling.
- Digital Banking.
- Financial Literacy &amp; English Class with EVO.
- Telesales session &amp; Corporate Culture.
- Work Readiness &amp; English Class.
- Brave day in PermataBank (Job Shadow).

From 105 beneficiaries of BBRVPD in the first Semester of 2020, PermataBRAVE Hub selected 40 people to participate in the three-month program which was facilitated by experts in their respective fields collaborating with Perbanas Institute.

At the end of the program, the best participants were given the opportunity to continue Work Learning Practices (WLP) at PermataBank as employees or at PermataBank's business partners as entrepreneurs.
