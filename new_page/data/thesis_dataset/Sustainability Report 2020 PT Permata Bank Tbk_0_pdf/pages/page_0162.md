# Struktur Tata Kelola PermataBank Governance Structure of PermataBank

# Komite-komite Dewan Komisaris

Dalam menjalankan tugasnya, Dewan Komisaris didukung oleh komite-komite yaitu Komite Audit, Komite Pemantau Risiko, Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi, serta Komite Tata Kelola Terintegrasi (Integrated Governance Committee/IGC). Keempat komite tersebut, sesuai spesifikasi di bidangnya, bertugas memberikan rekomendasi kepada Dewan Komisaris dalam menjalankan fungsi pengawasanya. Rekomendasi dimaksud menyangkut penetapan strategis saran di bidang ekonomi, sosial maupun partisipasi pada pelestarian lingkungan yang dinyatakan dalam KPI, rekomendasi pengawasan jalanya kegiatan operasional, penilaian kinerja bidang-bidang dimaksud termasuk rekomendasi penetapan remunerasi Dewan Komisaris maupun Direksi, sesuai capaian kinerja PermataBank yang telah ditetapkan sebelumnya dan sesuai kemampuan keuangan PermataBank.

# Direksi [102-22, 102-23]

Direksi bertanggung jawab penuh atas pengurusan PermataBank untuk kepentingan dan tujuan PermataBank serta mewakili PermataBank, baik di dalam maupun di luar pengadilan, sesuai dengan ketentuan Anggaran Dasar PermataBank dan peraturan yang berlaku.

Kinerja Direksi dinilai berdasarkan ukuran-ukuran tertentu yang telah ditetapkan dalam KPI, yang juga meliputi kinerja pada aspek ekonomi, sosial, partisipasi pada pelestarian lingkungan, maupun penerapan tata kelola perusahaan. KPI ditetapkan oleh Dewan Komisaris, dengan sepengetahuan Direksi. Uraian rinci mengenai tugas dan tanggung jawab Direksi PermataBank dapat dibaca dalam Laporan Tahunan PermataBank 2020 di bagian Tata Kelola Perusahaan.

# Committees of the Board of Commissioners

In exercising its duties, the Board of Commissioners is assisted by the committees, namely Audit Committee, Risk Monitoring Committee, Remuneration and Nomination Committee, and Integrated Governance Committee (IGC). The four committees, according to respective responsibilities, are in charge of providing recommendations to the Board of Commissioners in conducting its supervisory function. The recommendation is related to the determination of strategic targets in the economic, social and environmental conservation sectors provided in the KPI, supervision of the implementation of operational activities, and performance assessment in such sectors, including recommendations for determining the remuneration of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors, according to PermataBank's predetermined performance achievements and its financial capacity.

# Board of Directors [102-22, 102-23]

The Board of Directors is fully responsible for the management of PermataBank for the interests and objectives of PermataBank and represents the company, both inside and outside the court, in accordance with the provisions of PermataBank's Articles of Association and prevailing laws and regulations.

The performance of the Board of Directors is assessed based on certain measures set out in the KPI, which also include performance in the economic, social and environmental conservation sectors, and corporate governance. The KPI is determined by the Board of Commissioners, with the knowledge of the Board of Directors. A detailed description of the duties and responsibilities of the Board of Directors of PermataBank is provided in the 2020 PermataBank's Annual Report in the Corporate Governance.
