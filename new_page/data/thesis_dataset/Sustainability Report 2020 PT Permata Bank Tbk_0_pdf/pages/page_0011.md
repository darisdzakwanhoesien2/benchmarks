A Foreword from the President Director

Dalam membantu para debitur dari dampak kesulitan ekonomi akibat pandemi, PermataBank meluncurkan program restrukturisasi kredit bagi seluruh golongan usaha, baik korporasi, perusahaan menengah hingga Usaha Mikro, Kecil dan Menengah (UMKM). Untuk kelompok debitur UMKM, kami merealisasikan program subsidi bunga sesuai arahan OJK. Hal ini sejalan dengan Program Pemulihan Ekonomi Nasional yang diluncurkan pemerintah Indonesia.

Selain itu, kami juga melakukan mitigasi pandemi Covid-19 terhadap sumber daya manusia PermataBank. Sesuai dengan arahan pemerintah kami telah menerapkan Work From Home (WFH) serta pengaturan kerja yang selain untuk melindungi karyawan PermataBank, juga untuk menjaga kualitas layanan kepada nasabah. Kami juga membentuk Satuan Tugas Penanganan Covid-19 dan Command Center, serta melakukan sosialisasi terus-menerus kepada karyawan mengenai risiko penularan virus Covid-19 serta pentingnya penerapan pola hidup bersih dan sehat.

Sementara itu, sebagai bagian dari tiga pilar utama program CSR PermataHati, kami meluncurkan beragam program bantuan untuk membantu masyarakat terdampak. Program tersebut adalah:

- Penggalangan dana eksternal "Cegah Corona #DenganHati". Suatu kegiatan penggalangan dana dan edukasi 'menjaga jarak' dari para pemangku kepentingan yang berkolaborasi dengan lembaga kemanusiaan Aksi Cepat Tanggap (ACT).
- Gerakan penggalangan dana karyawan #DenganHati Challenge: 08.05. Suatu aksi donasi daring serentak dari karyawan PermataBank yang dilakukan #DiRumahAja. Hasil penggalangan dana sejumlah Rp890 juta didonasikan langsung kepada lima kelompok penerima manfaat, yakni: tenaga medis, pengemudi taksi dan ojek online, penyandang disabilitas, petugas jenazah dan penggali jenazah, guru honorer serta pelaku usaha kecil menengah.
- Bantuan bagi mitra PermataHati. Kegiatan CSR selama masa pandemi berupa paket data yang diberikan bagi para murid dan guru dari sekolah SD, SMP, SMA, dan SLB, yang digunakan untuk mendukung kegiatan belajar secara daring selama penerapan kebijakan pembatasan sosial berskala besar.

To help alleviate the difficulties experienced by debtors due to the pandemic, PermataBank launched a credit restructuring program for all types of businesses from corporation and medium corporations to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs). For MSMEs, we realized a program of interest rate subsidy under the OJK recommendation. This was in line with the Indonesian government's National Economic Recovery Program.

Moreover, we also protected our human resources from the effects of the Covid-19 pandemic. Under the government's instructions, we had imposed a 'Work From Home' (WFH) policy and managed work accordingly to protect PermataBank employees and also to maintain service quality for customers. We also formed a Covid-19 Task Force and a Command Center and regularly educated our employees about the risks of Covid-19 virus and the importance of clean and healthy living.

Meanwhile, as part of the three main pillars of the PermataHati CSR program, we launched a series of support programs to help the affected communities. These programs were:

- External fundraising program named "Cegah Corona #DenganHati", which was a fundraising and education program on social distancing held by stakeholders in collaboration with the humanitarian organization Aksi Cepat Tanggap (ACT).
- Fundraising initiated by PermataBank employees called #DenganHati Challenge: 08.05, an online donation program run by PermataBank employees with the hashtag #DiRumahAja. A total of Rp890 million from this fundraising was directly donated to five beneficiaries: medical professionals, online taxi drivers and transport drivers, people with disabilities, morticians and cemetery workers, honorary teachers, also small and medium businesses.
- Support for PermataHati partners. The CSR program during the pandemic in a form of internet quota for students and teachers of primary schools, junior high schools, senior high schools, and extraordinary schools used for online teaching and learning during the large-scale social restriction.
