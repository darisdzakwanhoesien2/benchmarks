# E. Banking For Student

Mengawali tahun 2020, 450 karyawan direktorat Wholesale Banking PermataBank bergerak memberikan edukasi keuangan kepada para siswa SD Negeri Kutuh 2, Badung, Bali. Dengan menggunakan MODul FinansiAL (MODAL),sebuah materi edukasi keuangan yang menarik dan komprehensif. Kegiatan ini merupakan upaya meningkatkan akses kepada edukasi keuangan dan keuangan inklusif sejak usia dini. Selain edukasi perbankan, dilakukan juga pembersihan pantai yang bertempat di Pantai Patra - Bali.

Dalam acara in PermataBank juga memberikan donasi kepada pihak SDN Kutuh 2 untuk membantu penerapan literasi keuangan dan proses pembelajaran di sekolah. Kegiatan CSR PermataHati ini merupakan rangkaian kickoff meeting direktorat Wholesale Banking PermataBank.

At the beginning of 2020, 450 employees of PermataBank's Wholesale Banking directorate gave financial education to students of Kutuh 2 State Elementary School, Badung, Bali using the MODul FinansiAL (MODAL), a comprehensive module on financial education designed in a creative way. This initiative was an effort to provide more access to financial education and financial inclusion at an early age. Other than banking education, the initiative also initiated the cleaning of Patra Beach - Bali.

In this activity, PermataBank also gave donation to Kutuh 2 State Elementary School for establishing a financial literacy curriculum and the learning process in the school. This PermataHati CSR was part of a series of kick-off meetings run by the Wholesale Banking directorate of PermataBank.
