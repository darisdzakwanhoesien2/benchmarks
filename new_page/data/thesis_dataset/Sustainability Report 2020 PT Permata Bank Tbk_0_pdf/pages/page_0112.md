Data BI menunjukkan posisi cadangan devisa Indonesia pada akhir Desember 2020 sebesar US$135,9 miliar, meningkat US$6,7 miliar dari posisi Desember 2019 yang tercata US$129,2 miliar. Jumlah cadangan devisa tersebut setara dengan pembiayaan 10,2 bulan impor atau 9,8 bulan impor dan pembayaran utang luar negeri pemerintah, serta berada di atas standar kecukupan internasional sekitar 3 bulan impor. Generally, macroeconomic conditions remained stable as shown by stable inflation, current account deficit, and high level of foreign exchange reserves.

Data from Bank Indonesia shows foreign exchange reserves in Indonesia as of December 2020 were US$135.9 billion, rising by US$6.7 billion from US$129.2 billion from December 2019. The foreign exchange reserves were equal to 10.2 or 9.8 months of import financing and foreign debt financing and were above the international adequacy standard of 3-month import financing. The increase in the foreign exchange reserves reflected the Indonesian Balance of Payments (BOP) amidst the current account deficit in 2020.
