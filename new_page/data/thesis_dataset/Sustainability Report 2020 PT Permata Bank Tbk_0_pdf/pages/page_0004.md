# Aspek Pengelolaan Sumber Daya Manusia
## Human Resources Management

- Perempuan di jajaran Manajemen sebanyak 24%
- Indeks Efektivitas Karyawan secara keseluruhan meningkat 21%
- Pendapatan per karyawan meningkat 11,6%
- Rata-rata hari pelatihan 4.55 hari/karyawan

- The percentage of female in the Management was 24%
- Employee Effectiveness Index rose by 21% overall
- Salary rose by 11.6% for each employee
- The average training days were 4.55 days/employee

# Aspek Tanggung Jawab Produk
## Product Responsibility

- Total penyelesaian pengaduan nasabah dalam waktu kurang dari dua hari mencapai 74,57%
- Total penyelesaian pengaduan nasabah dalam waktu kurang dari lima hari mencapai 92,44%
- The percentage of less-than-two-days complaints resolution reached 74.57%
- The percentage of less-than-five-days complaints resolution reached 92.44%
