Learn, Develop and Grow with PermataBank

Untuk menindaklanjuti tantangan pandemi COVID-19 di tahun 2020, Direktorat HR telah menerbitkan beberapa kebijakan dan strategi terkait keselamatan dan kesehatan karyawan sebagai berikut:

- Pengaturan kerja WFH (Work From Home), WFO (Work From Office) serta split operations sesuai dengan kebutuhan unit kerja masing-masing.
- Menindaklanjuti arahan Manajemen dalam pengelolaan Command Center yang bertugas menangani kasus COVID-19.
- Pemberian tunjangan sementara untuk membantu pemilihan sarana transportasi, pengadaan alat pelindung diri pribadi, vitamin dan lain-lain, serta untuk membantu melancarkan pekerjaan dari rumah (WFH).
- Bantuan untuk akses screening COVID-19 test untuk Karyawan dan keluarga.
- Sosialisasi berkala untuk edukasi Protokol Kesehatan.
- Penegakan Protokol Kesehatan dalam pengaturan kerja dan fasilitas pendukung kerja dengan tetap mematuhi ketentuan yang berlaku. Di antaranya dengan melakukan pembatasan perjalanan dinas, mendorong online meeting, pembatasan kunjungan ke nasabah, pelatihan online, penyediaan laptop, dan aktifasi BCP site.

Sejalan dengan Business Continuity Plan (BCP), tempat-tempat kerja satelit diberlakukan, khususnya untuk memastikan kegiatan operasional dapat tetap berjalan bila salah satu tempat kerja terkena dampak. Hal ini sekaligus dapat mengurangi beban kepadatan di tempat kerja. Dalam hal pengaturan kerja, fungsi kerja yang dapat dilakukan dari jauh diupayakan dengan memaksimalkan penggunaan teknologi dan infrastruktur. Pertemuan fisik dibatasi digantikan dengan pertemuan virtual, termasuk kegiatan-kegiatan pelatihan, sosialisasi, townhall, dan lain-lain.

Secara keseluruhan PermataBank dapat melalui masa pandemi COVID-19 dengan baik pada tahun 2020.

To handle the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020, the HR Directorate had issued several policies and strategies with respect to the safety and health of its employees detailed as follows:

- The policy on WFH (Work From Home), WFO (Work From Office) and split operations is managed according to the needs of each work unit.
- The Command Center is responsible for handling the COVID-19 cases.
- Providing temporary allowance for employees which covers a choice of transport, PPE, vitamins, and other benefits to facilitate work from home (WFH).
- Providing COVID-19 screening tests for employees and their families.
- Regular socialization of the importance of abiding by the Health Protocols.
- Imposing the Health Protocols at work and supporting facilities according to applicable provisions. Business trips are limited, online meetings are encouraged, client visits are restricted, online trainings are made, laptops are provided and BCP site is activated.

In line with the Business Continuity Plan (BCP), satellite offices are established specially to ensure the running of operations if one of the offices is affected. This can also help reduce crowded areas in the workplace. The work can be arranged to be performed remotely by using technology and infrastructure. Physical contact is limited and is replaced with virtual contact, including for training, education, townhall, and other activities.

All things considered, PermataBank managed to get through the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020.

# Employee Leave

PermataBank provides maternity leave for female employees for 3 months so they may prepare their maternity well. The leave duration is 1.5 month before giving birth and 1.5 month after giving birth. After taking maternity leave, a female employee may resume work with the same position as the one she holds before
