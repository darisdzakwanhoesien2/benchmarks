# Kebijakan Keuangan Berkelanjutan dan Nilai Keberlanjutan PermataBank

Sebagai lembaga jasa keuangan yang menjalankan fungsi sebagai agen pembangunan, PermataBank berkomitmen penuh untuk melaksanakan keuangan berkelanjutan di Indonesia dan mendukung pemerintah dalam mewujudkan Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan sebagaimana telah ditetapkan dalam Peraturan Presiden Nomor 59 tahun 2017.

Bagi PermataBank, keberlanjutan bertujuan untuk memberikan manfaat jangka panjang kepada pemangku kepentingan baik di bidang ekonomi, sosial maupun lingkungan. Secara khusus, kami telah menetapkan tiga nilai keberlanjutan untuk mencapai tujuan tersebut, yaitu:

- Nilai Ekonomi, diterapkan dalam pelaksanaan program keuangan berkelanjutan, yaitu inklusi keuangan melalui peningkatan portofolio Kredit Usaha Rakyat (KUR) dan mengembangkan kerja sama penyaluran (channeling) dengan lembaga keuangan mikro.

- Education via social media and internal media on tips on productive activities during the #DiRumahAja period and on the importance of social distancing to intercept the transmission of Covid-19.

The result from CSR PermataHati program implementation, in 2020 PermataBank received Padmamitra Award at a national level from the Social Ministry of the Republic of Indonesia for its consistency in conducting corporate social responsibilities targeting the community of people with disabilities.

At the same time, we continued to deliver our commitment to the achievement of Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) in Indonesia, through specific programs established in the SFAP. The year 2020 was the second period of the program.

# Sustainable Finance Policy and PermataBank's Sustainability Value

As a financial service institution with a function as an agent of development, PermataBank is fully committed in implementing sustainable finance in Indonesia and supporting the government to realize Sustainable Development Goals as stipulated in Presidential Decree Number 59 of 2017.

For PermataBank, sustainability is aimed at providing long-term economic, social and environmental benefits to stakeholders. Specifically, we have established three sustainable values to achieve those goals:

- Economics Value, which is implemented in the context of sustainable finance, took the form of finance inclusion through microfinancing portfolio enhancement and channeling partnership with micro financial institutions.
