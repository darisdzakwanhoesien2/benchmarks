Program-program PermataHati 2020
2020 PermataHati Programs

# Periode Program

Berlangsung selama tiga bulan (April – Juni 2020) dengan serangkaian kegiatan sebagai berikut:

1. Seleksi online.
2. Modul online, terbagi menjadi:
- Kelas Customer Validation.
- Kelas Hustler (Modul Business Validation).
- Kelas Hipster (Modul Product Validation).
- Kelas Hacker (Modul Software Development).
- Kelas Pitching dan Presentation.
3. Mentoring online mingguan.
4. Modul soft skill, terbagi menjadi:
- Financial literacy.
- Presentation skill.
- Expert sharing session.
5. Grand Final dan Graduation.

# Period of the Program

The period lasted for three months (April – June 2020) with the following timetable:

1. Online selection.
2. Distribution of online modules, consisting of:
- Customer Validation Class.
- Hustler (Modul Business Validation) Class.
- Hipster (Modul Product Validation) Class.
- Hacker (Modul Software Development) Class.
- Pitching and Presentation Class.
3. Weekly online mentoring.
4. Modules on soft skills, consisting of:
- Financial literacy.
- Presentation skill.
- Expert sharing session.
5. Grand Final and Graduation.
