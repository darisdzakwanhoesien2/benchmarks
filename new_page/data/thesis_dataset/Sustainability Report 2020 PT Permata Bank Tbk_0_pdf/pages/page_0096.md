The last pillar is the Acceleration Program, a program designed to develop the talents of PermataBankers. This program has 4 levels, i.e. EDP (Executive Development Program), MMAP (Middle Management Acceleration Program), ELAP (Emerging Leader Acceleration Program), and FAP (Functional Acceleration Program).

PermataBank had also built a Future Learning Engine through program branding and sharing HRLD News on online platforms (Instagram Live, Instagram TV), and created a learning studio to facilitate online training. [103-2]

In 2020, PermataBankers' average training days were 4.55 days/person. Meanwhile, the number of PermataBankers participating in training in 2020 was 99%.
