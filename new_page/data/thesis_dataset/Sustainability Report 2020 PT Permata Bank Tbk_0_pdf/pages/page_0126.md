C. Produk Wholesale

Keberadaan Wholesale Banking PermataBank telah membantu banyak nasabah korporasi, institusi, dan komersial dalam mencari solusi ekspansi bisnis. Wholesale Banking PermataBank senantiasa mengedepankan inovasi pada produk dan layanan untuk memastikan kebutuhan para nasabah selalu terpenuhi. Saat ini, produk dan layanan Wholesale Banking PermataBank sangat beragam dan semakin lengkap. Mulai dari cash management, trade finance, supply chain, pembiayaan usaha, treasury solution, hingga capital markets.

Pada tahun 2020 PermataBank memperluas jaringan distribusi dengan membuka 1 kantor cabang konvensional, 5 kantor cabang syariah, 1 Payment Point Syariah, dan 28 ATM baru. Selain itu, pada tahun 2020 PermataBank meluncurkan new model branch yang dirancang untuk memberikan pengalaman terbaik bagi nasabah. Dengan demikian, jumlah kantor cabang PermataBank hingga akhir tahun 2020 mencapai 301 yang tersebar di 62 kota Indonesia. Selain itu, jaringan PermataBank juga didukung oleh 4 mobile branch, 23 Payment Point Syariah, dan 925 jaringan ATM.

Selama masa pelaporan tahun 2020, tidak terjadi pelanggaran terkait pelabelan produk dan jasa keuangan, pelanggaran atas privasi pelanggan dan penarikan produk. Selama masa pelaporan juga tidak ada produk yang dihentikan penjualan ataupun ditarik dari peredaran. [417-2]

C. Wholesale Products

PermataBank's Wholesale Banking has helped many corporate customers, institutions, and commercial customers find solutions to their needs for business expansion. PermataBank's Wholesale Banking always prioritizes product and service innovation to ensure the fulfillment of customers' needs. Currently, the products and services of Wholesale Banking are varied and more comprehensive including cash management, trade finance, supply chain, business financing, treasury solution, and capital markets.

In 2020 PermataBank expanded its distribution network by opening 1 conventional branch office, 5 sharia branch offices, 1 Sharia Payment Point, and 28 new ATMs. Moreover, in 2020 PermataBank launched a new model branch designed to provide the best experience to customers. As such, PermataBank total branch offices by the end of 2020 reached 301 offices spread across 62 cities in Indonesia. In addition, PermataBank network was also supported by 4 mobile branches, 23 Sharia Payment Points, and 925 ATMs.

During the 2020 financial reporting, there were no violations related to the labelling of financial product and financial service, violation of customer privacy, and product recalls. Also during the year, there was not any discontinued or revoked product. [417-2]
