## Periode dan Siklus Laporan [102-50, 102-51, 102-52]

Laporan ini memuat program dan kegiatan operasional bank yang berkomitmen mendukung tercapainya SDGs dengan menyeimbangkan antara kinerja bidang ekonomi, sosial, dan lingkungan. Pelaporan hasil realisasi program tersebut dilengkapi dengan data-data yang relevan dengan pilihan topik material selama dua tahun terakhir, pada periode 1 Januari 2020 sampai dengan 31 Desember 2020 dan perbandinganya untuk periode 2019. PermataBank merencanakan penerbitan Laporan secara reguler setiap tahun.

Data dan kegiatan yang dicantumkan dalam Laporan ini merupakan data PermataBank beserta entitas anak, sebagaimana data keuangan dalam Laporan Tahunan yang merupakan Laporan Keuangan Konsolidasian dengan entitas anak yang telah diudit. [102-45]

The transparency indicator of the GRI Standard Core applied is presented with an index in specially-colored parentheses on each relevant page and is summarized in the Standard Core Index on page 190. [102-55]

The non-financial sustainability data in this report have been internally verified by PermataBank but have not been externally assured. The data and information on financial performance in this report are from PermataBank's Financial Statements for the 31 December 2020 period, which had been audited by the Public Accounting Firm of Tanudiredja, Wibisana, Rintis &amp; Partners as presented in PermataBank's 2020 Annual Report. [102-56]

## Reporting Period and Cycle [102-50, 102-51, 102-52]

This report contains programs and operations of the bank that reflected the attainment of the SDGs in terms of economic, social, and environmental aspects. The results of such programs are supported by relevant data with material topics for the last two years, namely those starting from 1 January 2020 until 31 December 2020 and a comparison with the data from the period of 2019. PermataBank submits a Report regularly every year.

The data and activities listed in this report are obtained from PermataBank and its subsidiaries in the same way that the financial data in the Annual Report are obtained from the Consolidated Financial Report of the audited subsidiaries. [102-45]
