2020 PermataHati Programs

# Profil Peserta Angkatan 2020

Peserta PermataBRAVE Hub tahun ini berjumlah 105 siswa, terdiri dari:

- 65 peserta tuna daksa.
- 37 peserta tuna rungu wicara.
- 3 peserta tuna netra.
- Usia 18-35 tahun dari seluruh Indonesia.
- Jenis pelatihan keterampilan:
- Komputer dan call center.
- Desain grafis.
- Otomotif.
- Logam.
- Elektro.
- Penjahitan.

Kegiatan PermataBRAVE ini hanya sempat berjalan hingga Februari 2020 akibat adanya pandemi Covid-19. Meskipun begitu, kegiatan akan kembali dilanjutkan setelah kondisi dikatakan aman oleh Pemerintah.

# Participants of Batch 2020 Profile

The participants of PermataBRAVE Hub this year numbered 105 students, consisting of:

- 65 people with physical impairment.
- 37 people with hearing impairment.
- 3 with visual impairment.
- People of age 18-35 across Indonesia.
- Types of skills training:
- Computer and call center.
- Graphic design.
- Automotive (repair).
- Metalworking.
- Electricity.
- Tailor apprenticeship.

This PermataBRAVE program implemented until February 2020 due to the Covid-19 pandemic. Nevertheless, the program will run again once the government declares that it is safe to conduct business as usual.
