## Mitigasi Kondisi Pandemi

Sejak awal Februari 2020, PermataBank sudah mempersiapkan diri guna menghadapi pandemi Covid-19 diantaranya dengan menerapkan Prosedur Kelangsungan Bisnis (BCP) dalam menhadapi pandemi, mengadakan dan menerapkan alat pelindung diri seperti masker, sarung tangan, dan penggunaan hand sanitizer, sarana kerja yang sehat dan bersih serta memberikan jarak atau sekat antar meja/kursi.

## Mitigation during the Pandemic

Since the beginning of February 2020, PermataBank had been preparing to mitigate the impact of the Covid-19 pandemic by implementing Business Continuity Procedure (BCP) in addressing the pandemic, procuring and distributing personal protective equipment such as masks, gloves, hand sanitizers, setting up hygienic and clean work infrastructures and giving space between each table/chair.
