PYP 2020 was attended by 522 High/Vocational School students and equivalents (45 teams) who passed online selection process in the previous month. They came from various regions in Indonesia from Sumatera, Java, Kalimantan to Bali. The best 10 teams had the opportunity to make visual presentations on their beta financial technology (fintech) products they had developed in front of juries and Dr. Ir. M. Bakrun M.M (Vocational School Director and Acting Official of the Partnership and DUDI Adjustment Directorate of the Indonesian Education and Culture Ministry), Dr. Ahmad Saufi, S.Si., M.Sc.
