# Kompetensi K3 PermataBankers

## PermataBankers OHS Competency

- Kebakaran pada panel listrik (MDP) yang berada di lantai satu kantor cabang Urip Sumoharjo-Solo;
- Nasabah tersandung oleh besi pembatas parkir di depan cabang Metro Pondok Indah;
- Karyawan tersandung dan jatuh pada pembatas parkir atau penutup salah satu sumur resapan di kantor cabang Tower III Bintaro; dan
- Karyawan tersandung trotoar lobi belakang kantor cabang Tower III Bintaro.

- Fire on electric panel (MDP) located on first floor of branch office Urip Sumoharjo-Solo;
- Customer stumbled on iron parking barrier located in front of branch office Metro Pondok Indah;
- Employee stumbled on parking barrier or cover of the infiltration wells located in branch office Tower III Bintaro; and
- Employee stumbled on back lobby sidewalk located in branch office Tower III Bintaro.
