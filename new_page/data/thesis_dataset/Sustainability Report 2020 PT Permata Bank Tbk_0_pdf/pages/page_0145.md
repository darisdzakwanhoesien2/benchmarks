# Building Risk Assessment (BRA)

Penilaian Risiko Gedung (Building Risk Assessment/BRA) di seluruh kantor operasional PermataBank untuk memastikan bahwa risiko-risiko operasional yang terkait dengan isu kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja dapat diidentifikasi, dikelola serta ditindaklanjuti.

Pada tahun 2020, BRA diadakan di 63 kantor bank dimana terdapat 158 temuan risiko kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja, yang telah diselesaikan secara efektif. [403-2]

# Building Risk Assessment (BRA)

Building Risk Assessment (BRA) on all PermataBank's operational offices is conducted to ensure that operational risks related to occupational health and safety could be identified, managed and followed up.

In 2020, the BRA was conducted on 63 branch offices and there were 158 findings related to occupational health and safety risks, that had been effectively resolved. [403-2]
