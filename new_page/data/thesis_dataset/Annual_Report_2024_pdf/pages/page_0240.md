|  22. | Public Company shall have policies on whistleblowing system.
Perusahaan Terbuka memiliki kebijakan sistem whistleblowing. | The recommendation has been implemented.
The Company has a Whistleblowing System Policy Number P08/R04 which is a code of conduct that applies for the Board of Commissioners, Committees, Board of Directors and all employees of the Company. This policy is the basis for all employees to report violations or suspected violations of the Code of Ethics and Business Conduct and/or violations of laws and regulations and operational policies of the Company and the Company's group.
Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.
Perseroan telah memiliki Kebijakan Sistem Whistleblowing Nomor P08/R04 yang merupakan pedoman perilaku yang berlaku bagi Dewan Komisaris, Komite, Direksi dan seluruh karyawan Perseroan. Kebijakan tersebut menjadi dasar bagi seluruh karyawan untuk melaporkan pelanggaran atau dugaan pelanggaran terhadap Kode Etik dan Perilaku Bisnis dan/atau pelanggaran atas hukum maupun peraturan dan kebijakan operasional Perseroan maupun grup Perseroan.  |

|  24. | Public Company shall utilize information technology beyond its website as a medium for information transparency.
Perusahaan Terbuka memanfaatkan penggunaan teknologi informasi secara lebih luas selain Situs Web sebagai media keterbukaan informasi. | The recommendations have been implemented and will continue to be developed.
