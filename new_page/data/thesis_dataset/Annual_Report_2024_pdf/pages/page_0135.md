# Advances for Right of Use Asset

As of 31 December 2024, advances for right of use asset amounted to Rp 49.7 billion, representing an increase of Rp 41.8 billion during 2024. This increase was primarily due to advance payments made to the industrial estate management for land use rights within the estate area, covering the period from 2026 – 2056.

# Fixed Assets, net

The Company's fixed assets as of 31 December 2024 amounted to Rp 402.4 billion, which represents an increase of 4.7% compared to last year's Rp 384.3 billion. This increase was mainly due to the addition of office equipment, net of the normal annual depreciation of the fixed assets.

# Deferred Tax Assets, net

As of 31 December 2024, the Company's deferred tax assets, net, amounted to Rp 126.8 billion. This represents an increase of 7.8%, mainly attributable to deferred tax from unrealized profit on intercompany sales.

# Liabilities

The Company recorded total liabilities of Rp 2.7 trillion at the end of 2024, consisting of 94% short-term liabilities and 6% long-term liabilities. The amount of liabilities increased by Rp 348.9 billion or 14.9% compared to 2023, primarily due to an increase in short-term liabilities.

# Current Liabilities

In 2024, the Company's short-term liabilities amounted to Rp 2.5 trillion, representing a 15.6% increase. The increase in short-term liabilities was mainly due to an increase in trade payables.

# Uang Muka Aset Hak Guna

Pada tanggal 31 Desember 2024, uang muka aset hak guna berjumlah Rp 49,7 miliar, peningkatan sebesar Rp 41,8 miliar ditahun 2024 utamanya merupakan pembayaran dimuka kepada pengelola kawasan industri lokasi Perseroan untuk izin penggunaan lahan dalam kawasan industri untuk periode 2026 – 2056.

# Aset Tetap, neto

Aset tetap Perseroan pada tanggal 31 Desember 2024 berjumlah Rp 402,4 miliar, yang menunjukkan peningkatan sebesar 4,7% dibanding tahun lalu sebesar Rp 384,3 miliar. Peningkatan ini terutama disebabkan oleh penambahan peralatan kantor, dikurangi dengan depresiasi tahunan normal dari aset tetap.

# Aset Pajak Tangguhan, neto

Pada tanggal 31 Desember 2024, aset pajak tangguhan bersih Perseroan berjumlah Rp 126,8 miliar. Ini merupakan peningkatan sebesar 7,8%, terutama disebabkan oleh pajak tangguhan atas laba yang belum direalisasi pada penjualan antar perusahaan.

# Liabilitas

Perseroan membukukan jumlah liabilitas sebesar Rp 2,7 triliun pada akhir tahun 2024, terdiri dari 94% liabilitas jangka pendek dan 6% liabilitas jangka panjang. Jumlah liabilitas mengalami kenaikan sebesar Rp 348,9 miliar atau 14,9% dibandingkan dengan tahun 2023, terutama disebabkan oleh peningkatan liabilitas jangka pendek.

# Liabilitas Jangka Pendek

# Trade Payables

The Company's trade payables at the end of 2024 amounted to Rp 2.2 trillion, representing an increase of 16.1%. The increase in trade payables was mainly due to the increase in the value of inventory purchases for the distribution business segment.

# Utang Usaha

Utang usaha Perseroan pada akhir tahun 2024 adalah sebesar Rp 2,2 triliun, mengalami kenaikan sebesar 16,1%. Naiknya utang usaha sebagian besar disebabkan oleh kenaikan nilai pembelian persediaan untuk segmen bisnis distribusi.
