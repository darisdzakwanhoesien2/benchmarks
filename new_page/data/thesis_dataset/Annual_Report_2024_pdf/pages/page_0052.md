# Signing of the Research Grant MoU between LPDP, SOHO, and IPB

Penandatanganan MoU Research Grant LPDP – SOHO – IPB

![img-50.jpeg](img-50.jpeg)

The Ministry of Health of the Republic of Indonesia, in collaboration with the Education Fund Management Institution (Lembaga Pengelolaan Dana Pendidikan – “LPDP”), has launched the Innovative Productive Research Program (Riset Inovatif Produktif – “RISPRO”), a research funding program specifically for the health sector. RISPRO aims to produce breakthrough research innovations that focus on enhancing national competitiveness, with the potential to increase intellectual property and economic value, as well as develop new, applicable technologies that can improve the welfare of the Indonesian people.

PT SOHO Industri Pharmasi (SIP/“SOHO”) welcomes this initiative by applying for research funding to develop herbal-based pharmaceutical products aimed at boosting the immune system (OHT Immunostimulant). The RISPRO application process involves substance selection and intensive discussions with LPDP, as well as proposal reviews by BPOM, academic representatives, and health practitioners to assess the feasibility of the submitted research proposal. LPDP determined that SOHO was eligible to receive a research grant based on technological readiness, innovative ideas, and human resource capabilities, with IPB as the research partner.

On January 31, 2024, the proposal was finalized with the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between SOHO and LPDP as the funder, and SOHO and IPB as the research partner. The MoU was signed by Dr. Raphael Aswin Susilowidodo, Vice President of Research and Development, Regulatory and Medical Affairs at PT SOHO Industri Pharmasi, representing SOHO; Wisnu Soenarso, Director of Research Facilities at LPDP, representing LPDP; and Prof. Sugeng, Director of Research and Innovation at IPB, representing IPB.

Kementerian Kesehatan Republik Indonesia, bekerja sama dengan Lembaga Pengelola Dana Pendidikan (LPDP), meluncurkan Program Riset Inovatif Produktif (RISPRO), yaitu program pendanaan riset khusus di bidang kesehatan. RISPRO bertujuan menghasilkan terobosan inovasi riset yang berfokus pada peningkatan daya saing nasional, berpotensi meningkatkan kekayaan intelektual dan nilai ekonomis, serta menciptakan teknologi terbaru yang aplikatif dan dapat meningkatkan kesejahteraan masyarakat Indonesia.

PT SOHO Industri Pharmasi (SIP/“SOHO”) menyambut baik inisiatif ini dengan mengajukan pendanaan riset untuk pengembangan sediaan farmasi berbasis herbal sebagai peningkat sistem imun (OHT Imunostimulant). Pengajuan RISPRO mencakup seleksi substansi dan diskusi intensif dengan LPDP serta penguji proposal dari BPOM, akademisi, dan praktisi kesehatan untuk menilai kelayakan proposal riset yang diajukan. LPDP menilai SOHO layak menerima research grant berdasarkan kesiapan teknologi, ide inovatif, serta kemampuan SDM, dengan IPB sebagai mitra riset.

Pada 31 Januari 2024, proposal ini difinalisasi melalui penandatanganan MoU antara SOHO dan LPDP sebagai pemberi dana, serta SOHO dengan IPB sebagai mitra riset. Penandatanganan MoU dilakukan oleh Dr. Raphael Aswin Susilowidodo, selaku Vice President Research and Development, Regulatory and Medical Affairs PT SOHO Industri Pharmasi, mewakili SOHO; Bapak Wisnu Soenarso, selaku Direktur Fasilitas Riset LPDP, mewakili LPDP; dan Prof. Sugeng, selaku Direktur Riset dan Inovasi IPB, mewakili IPB.
