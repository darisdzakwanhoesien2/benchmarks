# SOHO Supports BPOM: Foster Parent Program for MSMEs in Natural Medicine

SOHO Dukung BPOM: Program Orang Tua Angkat UMKM Obat Bahan Alam

On Friday, August 2, 2024, the National Agency of Drug and Food Control (BPOM) launched the Foster Parent Program for MSMEs in Natural Medicine (OBA) at Central Park Mall as part of the opening of WellFest 2024: Natural Beauty and Wellness. This program is a partnership between BPOM and the natural medicine industry, aimed at providing guidance to MSME actors, accelerating business development, and ensuring compliance with standards.

PT Soho Industri Pharmasi (SIP) has committed to becoming one of the Foster Parents for MSMEs in OBA. The commitment was represented by Dr. Ir. Raphael Aswin Susilowidodo, ST, M.Si, CIP, IPU, Vice President of R&amp;D, Regulatory, and Medical Affairs. The signing from BPOM was represented by Dr. Dra. L. Rizka Andalusia, Apt., M.Pharm., MARS, Acting Head of BPOM, and Mohamad Kashuri, S.Si, Apt., M.Farm, Deputy II for the Supervision of Traditional Medicines, Health Supplements, and Cosmetics at BPOM.

Pada Jum’at, 2 Agustus 2024, BPOM meluncurkan Program Orang Tua Angkat UMKM Obat Bahan Alam (OBA) di Mall Central Park sebagai bagian dari pembukaan WellFest 2024: Natural Beauty and Wellness. Program ini merupakan kemitraan antara BPOM dan industri obat bahan alam yang bertujuan untuk memberikan pendampingan kepada pelaku UMKM OBA, mempercepat pengembangan usaha, serta memastikan kepatuhan terhadap standar.

PT Soho Industri Pharmasi (SIP) berkomitmen sebagai salah satu Orang Tua Angkat UMKM OBA, diwakili oleh Dr. Ir. Raphael Aswin Susilowidodo, ST, M. Si, CIP, IPU, selaku Vice President of R&amp;D, Regulatory and Medical Affairs. Penandatangan dari BPOM diwakili oleh Dr. Dra. L. Rizka Andalusia, Apt., M.Pharm., MARS, selaku Plt. Kepala BPOM dan Mohamad Kashuri, S.Si, Apt., M.Farm, selaku Deputi II Bidang Pengawasan Obat Tradisional, Suplemen Kesehatan dan Kosmetik BPOM.
