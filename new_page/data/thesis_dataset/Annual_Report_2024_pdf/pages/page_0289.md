# f. Commemoration of the 79th Independence Day of the Republic of Indonesia

In commemoration of the 79th Indonesian Independence Day, PT SOHO Industri Pharmasi Labor Union initiated a competition event with the theme "The Vibrant Spirit of SOHO Together with the Work Unit Leadership of PT SOHO Industri Pharmasi". This activity consisted of a fishing competition and other games which were participated in by employees from SOHO Global Health Group located in Pulogadung, East Jakarta. This activity aims to strengthen relationships among employees, increase the spirit of togetherness, and create a pleasant atmosphere in commemoration of historical moments for the Indonesian nation.

With high enthusiasm from the participants, this event provided a memorable experience while strengthening solidarity among employees.

# Company Activities with Labor Union

A harmonious industrial relations between the Company and all employees and Labor Union are important in supporting the achievement of various Company targets. The Company realizes that the Labor Union also play an important role as representatives of employees as well as a partner of the Company. Therefore, it is very important for the officials and members of the Labor Union to have strong organizational skills and consistently improve their competence.

# 1. Training for Union Leaders and Members

One of the Company's acts in supporting harmonious industrial relations with the Labor Union is by supporting self-development programs for the officials and members of the Labor Union through regular training from year to year. In 2024, a training session on the topic "Win-Win Negotiation" was held on February 2, to February 3, 2024 located at Cisarua, Bogor, with 22 participants consisting of officials and members of the Labor Union. This training was facilitated by Mr. M. Zakir (PP Sales Force Effectiveness &amp; Training Senior Manager) and Mr. R. Rispriyatna (Distribution Sales Force Effectiveness Senior Manager). The focus of the training is to teach participants effective negotiation skills. On the second day, the activity continued with team building which started with fun games to raise the enthusiasm of the participants. Then each group was assigned to complete 6 games at different stations to earn as many points as possible. The aim of this team building is to sharpen team cohesion and improve cooperation among team members.

# Aktivitas Perusahaan bersama Serikat Pekerja

Hubungan industrial yang harmonis antara Perusahaan dengan seluruh Karyawan dan Serikat Pekerja merupakan hal yang penting dalam menunjang pencapaian berbagai target Perusahaan. Perusahaan menyadari bahwa Serikat Pekerja juga memiliki peranan penting yakni sebagai perwakilan Karyawan sekaligus sebagai mitra Perusahaan. Oleh karenanya sangatlah penting bagi Pimpinan dan Anggota Serikat Pekerja memiliki kemampuan berorganisasi yang mumpuni serta konsisten meningkatkan kompetensi diri.

# 1. Pelatihan-pelatihan bagi Pengurus dan Anggota Serikat Pekerja

Salah satu langkah Perusahaan dalam mendukung hubungan industrial yang harmonis dengan Serikat Pekerja antara lain dengan mendukung program pengembangan diri bagi Pengurus dan Anggota Serikat Pekerja dengan memberikan pelatihan secara berkala dari tahun ke tahun. Di tahun 2024 ini, telah diadakan pelatihan bertopik "Win-Win Negotiation" pada tanggal 2 sampai dengan 3 Februari 2024 bertempat di Cisarua, Bogor, dengan peserta sebanyak 22 orang terdiri dari unsur Pengurus dan Anggota Serikat Pekerja. Pelatihan ini difasilitasi oleh Bapak M. Zakir (PP Sales Force Effectiveness &amp; Training Senior Manager) dan Bapak R. Rispriyatna (Distribution Sales Force Effectiveness Senior Manager). Fokus pelatihan adalah untuk mengajarkan kepada peserta keterampilan bernegosiasi secara efektif. Pada hari kedua, kegiatan dilanjutkan dengan team building yang dimulai dengan fun games untuk membangkitkan semangat peserta. Kemudian setiap kelompok ditugaskan untuk menyelesaikan 6 permainan di pos-pos yang berbeda untuk mendapatkan poin sebanyak mungkin. Tujuan dari team building ini antara lain untuk mempertajam kekompakan tim dan meningkatkan kerjasama di antara anggota tim.
