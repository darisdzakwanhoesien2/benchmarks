c. Informasi Entitas Anak (lanjutan)

PT Universal Health Network ("UHN")

Berdasarkan Akta Notaris Jimmy Tanal, S.H., M.Kn., No. 216 tanggal 30 Juni 2022, PT Soho Global Investment selaku pemilik 1 saham Seri A dengan nilai nominal Rp1.000.000 (nilai penuh) per saham di UHN, telah menjual saham tersebut ke SIP sebesar nilai wajar. Berdasarkan laporan penilaian yang dilakukan oleh KJPP Wawat Jatmika dan Rekan, penilai independen, nilai wajar saham UHN pada tanggal 31 Desember 2021 adalah sebesar Rp7.442.000 (nilai penuh) per saham. Setelah transaksi ini, kepemilikan Perusahaan dalam UHN mengalami peningkatan dari semula 99,98% menjadi 100,00%, melalui kepemilikan langsung dan kepemilikan tidak langsung melalui SIP. Selisih antara nilai nominal dan nilai wajar sebesar Rp6.442.000 (nilai penuh) diakui pada "Selisih Transaksi dengan Pihak Non pengendali" dalam laporan keuangan konsolidasian.

c. Information of Subsidiaries (continued)

PT Universal Health Network ("UHN")

Based on Notarial Deed No. 216 of Jimmy Tanal, S.H., M.Kn., dated June 30, 2022, PT Soho Global Investment as a shareholder of 1 Series A share with nominal value of Rp1,000,000 (full amount) per share on UHN, has sold the share to SIP at fair value. Based on the appraisal report, performed by KJPP Wawat Jatmika and Partners, independent appraiser, the fair value of UHN share as of December 31, 2021 amounted to Rp7,442,000 (full amount) per share. After the transaction, the Company's ownership in UHN increased from 99.98% to 100.00%, through direct ownership and indirect ownership through SIP. The difference between selling price and fair value amounting to Rp6,442,000 (full amount) was recorded as "Differences in value of transactions of non-controlling interests" in consolidated financial statements.
