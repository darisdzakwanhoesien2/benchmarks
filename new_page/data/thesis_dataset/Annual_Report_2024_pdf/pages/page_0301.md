In 2024, we participated in the Foster Parent program for Natural Medicine for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSME) of Herbal Medicines. This program is a partnership program between BPOM and the Herbal Medicine Industries to help accelerate the fulfillment of licensing and certification requirements for MSME players. Soho is committed to becoming a foster parent for Minyak Nusantara, located in Surakarta, Central Java. The signing of the commitment as a foster parent was carried out on October 16, 2024. By becoming a foster parent, Soho will assist the Minyak Nusantara in terms of helping them obtain a Certificate of Good Manufacturing Practice on Traditional Medicine (CPOTB) in stages, providing training related to CPOTB and product manufacturing, and assisting in the product registration process to obtain a marketing authorization. From the assistance that we have provided, it is hoped that Minyak Nusantara can meet the standards for facilities and product quality so that they are able to produce safe, efficacious and high-quality products that are competitive.
