# Fulfillment of Recommendation of SEOJK No.32/SEOJK.04/2015

Pemenuhan Rekomendasi SEOJK No.32/SEOJK.04/2015

The Company always pays attention to and implements GCG principles in accordance with the Guidelines for Public Company Governance which has been regulated in the Circular Letter of the Financial Services Authority No.32/SEOJK.04/2015.

The implementation of these guidelines is described as follows, which in principle the Company will always implement the recommendations and improve its quality from time to time:

## Principles:

Improving the Value of General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) Implementation

Perseroan senantiasa memperhatikan dan menerapkan prinsip-prinsip GCG sesuai dengan Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan Terbuka yang telah diatur dalam Surat Edaran Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No.32/SEOJK.04/2015.

|  1. | Public Company should be equipped with either open or closed voting technical procedures, which focus on independency, and the interest of shareholders.

Perusahaan Terbuka memiliki cara atau prosedur teknis pengumpulan suara (voting) baik secara terbuka maupun tertutup yang mengedepankan independensi, dan kepentingan pemegang saham. | The recommendation have been implemented.

The Company has regulated the procedures and technical procedures for voting in each GMS. The procedures and technical procedures are prepared based on the provisions of the Company's Articles of Association and the applicable laws and regulations.

The procedures and technical procedures are notified to all shareholders in the invitation letter for the GMS and contained in the rules for the GMS, which are distributed to each shareholder present.

The technical procedures and procedures also include regulations regarding voting conducted in private through voting cards given to shareholders, as well as voting through a system provided by KSEI (KSEI eASY).

Information related to these procedures is also available on the Company's Website, as will always be updated from time to time, in the event that the Company's GMS will be held.  |
