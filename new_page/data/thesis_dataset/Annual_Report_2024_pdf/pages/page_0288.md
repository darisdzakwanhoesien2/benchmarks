3. Discussion of proposals, suggestions, and input from members regarding cooperative management.

The annual meeting is usually an important moment to strengthen relations between members and management, as well as strengthen joint commitment to realize the welfare of cooperative members.

## e. Music Performance in the Manufacturing area

Music performances in the Soho manufacturing area are one of the activities initiated by the Labor Union in the form of monthly musical performances involving employees located at the Head Office, Jalan Pulogadung No. 6 Jakarta Industrial Estate Pulogadung, and employees from other locations who are willing to come to that location.

This activity is routinely held every month in the manufacturing area aiming to provide space for employees to express their interest and talent in music. Apart from being a means of entertainment, this performance is also an opportunity to strengthen the spirit of togetherness and create a more harmonious work atmosphere.

Through this initiative, the Labor Union hopes to contribute positively to the emotional well-being and motivation of employees, while strengthening interpersonal relationships in the workplace.

## e. Pertunjukan Musik di area Manufacturing

Pertunjukan musik di area manufakturing Soho merupakan salah satu kegiatan yang diinisiasi oleh Serikat Pekerja berupa pertunjukan musik bulanan yang melibatkan Karyawan yang berlokasi di Kantor Pusat Jalan Pulogadung No. 6 Kawasan Industri Pulogadung, dan juga Karyawan dari lokasi lainnya yang bersedia hadir ke lokasi tersebut.

Selain sebagai sarana hiburan, pertunjukan ini juga menjadi ajang untuk memperkuat semangat kebersamaan dan menciptakan suasana kerja yang lebih harmonis.

Melalui inisiatif ini, Serikat Pekerja berharap dapat memberikan kontribusi positif terhadap kesejahteraan emosional dan motivasi Karyawan, sekaligus mempererat hubungan antar individu di lingkungan kerja.
