General Shareholders Meeting Year 2024

Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Tahun 2024

During the year 2024, the Company held one (1) GMS, namely the Annual GMS. In conducting the GMS, the Company has complied with the provisions of Article 12 of the Company's Articles of Association and adhered to the provisions of Article 8, Article 10, and Article 17 of POJK No. 15/2020.

Selama tahun 2024, Perseroan telah menyelenggarakan satu (1) kali RUPS, yakni RUPS Tahunan. Dalam menyelenggarakan RUPS tersebut, Perseroan telah memenuhi ketentuan Pasal 12 Anggaran Dasar Perseroan serta memperhatikan ketentuan Pasal 8, Pasal 10, dan Pasal 17 POJK No. 15/2020.

## Annual GMS
## RUPS Tahunan

The Board of Directors of the Company has conducted the following:

1. Sent a letter to the Financial Services Authority regarding the plan to hold a GMS along with details of the GMS agenda through letter Number 289/SGH-IDX/V/2024, dated May 13, 2024 regarding Submission of the Agenda for the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders.
2. Announced the Annual GMS on May 22, 2024, with the following details:
- in the Company's official website (in Indonesian and English versions);
- in the Indonesia Stock Exchange website, through the SPE (in Indonesian and English versions); and
- eASY.KSEI website.
3. Issued the Invitation for the Annual GMS on June 6, 2024, with the following details:
- in the Company's website (in Indonesian and English versions);
- in the Indonesia Stock Exchange website, through the SPE (in Indonesian and English versions); and
- eASY.KSEI website.
4. Issued the Corrected Invitation for the Annual GMS on June 26, 2024, with the following details:
- in the Company's website (in Indonesian and English versions);
- in the Indonesia Stock Exchange website, through the SPE (in Indonesian and English versions); and
- eASY.KSEI website.

## Implementation of the Annual GMS
## Pelaksanaan RUPS Tahunan

The Company conducted its Annual GMS on June 28, 2024, at the Company's Head Office, Ruang Training Logistik, Lantai 3, Jl. Rawa Sumur II Kav. BB No. 4A-4B, Kawasan Industri Pulogadung, Kel. Jatinegara, Kec. Cakung, Jakarta Timur, 13930, DKI Jakarta. The Annual GMS was attended by 10,333,339,310 shares or 81.41% of all fully paid-up shares of the Company.

Direksi Perseroan telah melakukan hal-hal sebagai berikut:

1. Mengirimkan surat kepada OJK tentang rencana akan diselenggarakannya RUPS beserta dengan rincian mata acara RUPS melalui surat Nomor 289/SGH-IDX/V/2024 tanggal 13 Mei 2024 perihal Penyampaian Mata Acara Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Tahunan.
2. Melakukan Pengumuman RUPS Tahunan pada tanggal 22 Mei 2024, dengan rincian sebagai berikut:

- di situs Bursa Efek Indonesia melalui SPE (dalam versi bahasa Indonesia dan bahasa Inggris); dan
- situs eASY.KSEI.

- di situs resmi Perseroan (dalam versi bahasa Indonesia dan bahasa Inggris);

- situs eASY.KSEI.
4. Melakukan Pemanggilan Koreksi atas RUPS Tahunan pada tanggal 26 Juni 2024, dengan rincian sebagai berikut:
- di situs resmi Perseroan (dalam versi bahasa Indonesia dan bahasa Inggris);

- situs eASY.KSEI.
