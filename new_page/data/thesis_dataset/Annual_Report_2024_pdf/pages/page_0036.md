Changes in the Composition of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners of the Company and its Subsidiaries Perubahan Susunan Direksi dan Komisaris Perseroan dan Entitas Anak

In 2025, the Company and its Subsidiaries implemented changes in the composition of the Board of Commissioners and/or Board of Directors, with the following details:

- Amendments were made in the Subsidiaries, with the details as follows:

(i) Based on the Notarial Deed made before Notary Jimmy Tanal, S.H., M.Kn., No. 18 dated January 7, 2025, SIP's shareholders approved the amendment of composition of SIP's Commissioner and Board of Directors become as follows:

**Board of Commissioners**
- Commissioner: Andy Nugroho Purwohardono

**Board of Directors**
- President Director: Ery Yunasri, S.H., LLM
- Director: Rogelio Paulino Jr. Castillo Lao
- Director: Piero Brambati
- Director: Kalista Utama
- Director: Ir. Raphael Aswin Susilowidodo, M.Si.

(ii) Based on the Notarial Deed made before Notary Jimmy Tanal, S.H., M.Kn., No. 20 dated January 7, 2025, PPG's shareholders approved the amendment of composition of PPG's Commissioner and Board of Directors become as follows:

**Board of Commissioners**
- Commissioner: Eng Liang Tan

**Board of Directors**
- President Director: Ery Yunasri, S.H., LLM
- Director: Rogelio Paulino Jr. Castillo Lao
- Director: Piero Brambati
- Director: Edwin Vega Darma
- Director: Pandy Harianto

(iii) Based on the Notarial Deed made before Notary Jimmy Tanal, S.H., M.Kn., No. 72 dated April 16, 2025, UHN's shareholders approved the amendment of the composition of UHN's Commissioner and Board of Directors to become as follows:

**Board of Commissioners**
- Commissioner: Eng Liang Tan

**Board of Directors**
- President Director: Ery Yunasri, S.H., LLM
- Director: Rishy S Hedar

(a) the aforementioned changes became effective for PT Soho Industri Pharmasi and PT Parit Padang Global as of January 2, 2025 as disclosed by the Company in the public information disclosure on January 6, 2025, with the respective report numbers as follows: (i) Report on Material Information of Facts Number 001/SOHO/KI/OJK-BEI/I/2025 for changes in PT Soho Industri Pharmasi and (ii) Report on Material Information of Facts Number 002/SOHO/KI/OJK-BEI/I/2025 for changes in PT Parit Padang Global.
