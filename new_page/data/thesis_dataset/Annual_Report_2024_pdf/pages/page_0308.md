# Public Complaint [POJK 6.c]

We provide platforms that are easy for the public/consumer to submit complaints to us through our website www.sohoglobalhealth.com, email address public.relations@sohoglobalhealth.com, and social media (Facebook: SOHO Natural; Instagram: @sohonatural and YouTube: Soho Natural).

As part of our commitment to product safety and in compliance with regulatory requirements, we have a pharmacovigilance reporting system. All reports of adverse events received must be reported to the pharmacovigilance team within 24 hours verbally and/or in writing via email at pv@sohoglobalhealth.com. The Company will follow up on complaints from the public/consumers and monitor the status of the settlement.

The number of adverse event reports received from products are as follows:

|  2022 | 2023 | 2024  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  12 | 12 | 5  |

These complaints have been investigated and followed up according to applicable procedures, these complaints were generally non-serious events related to hypersensitivity reaction, bradycardia, and tinnitus. After stopping the use of the product, the negative reactions gradually disappeared, and the patient's condition improved.

# Safety Evaluated Products/Services for Customers [POJK 6.f]

We are committed to providing and offering good quality and safe products for our customers. Before being distributed to the market, all products have gone through various stages of very strict evaluation by the National Agency of Drug and Food Control (NA-DFC) so that these products are guaranteed in terms of safety, efficacy, and quality. We ensure that every product to be distributed has passed the evaluation and has obtained a marketing authorization from the NA-DFC. Each production batch that is circulated has met the requirements for testing, monitoring, and product release carried out by our Quality Assurance (QA) Division. In addition, we also carry out routine pharmacovigilance activities for products that have been circulated to the market. Pharmacovigilance activities aim to monitor side effects and adverse events that may occur due to the use of the product.

# Product Recall [POJK 6.f]

In 2024, we conducted 2 product recalls for 2 products and we decided not to renew the marketing license. There were no incidents nor impact to safety before and after the recalls.

# Produk/Jasa yang Sudah Dievaluasi Keamanannya bagi Pelanggan [POJK 6.f]

Kami berkomitmen untuk menyediakan dan menawarkan produk yang berkualitas baik dan aman bagi para pelanggan kami. Sebelum diedarkan ke pasar, semua produk telah melalui berbagai tahapan evaluasi yang sangat ketat oleh Badan POM sehingga produk tersebut terjamin secara keamanan, efikasi, dan mutu. Kami memastikan bahwa setiap produk yang akan diedarkan telah lulus evaluasi dan telah memperoleh surat izin edar dari Badan POM. Setiap bets produksi yang diedarkan telah memenuhi persyaratan untuk pengujian, pengawasan, dan perilisan produk yang dilakukan oleh Divisi Pemastian Mutu (Quality Assurance, QA). Selain itu, kami turut melakukan kegiatan farmakovigilans secara rutin untuk produk-produk yang telah diedarkan ke pasar. Aktivitas farmakovigilans bertujuan untuk melakukan monitoring efek samping dan kejadian tidak diinginkan yang mungkin terjadi akibat penggunaan produk.

# Produk yang Ditarik Kembali [POJK 6.f]
