# Signing of the PPGOS Subdistributor Partnership with PT Pingloka Distrinaga

Penandatanganan Kerjasama PPGOS Subdistributor dengan PT Pingloka Distrinaga

![img-51.jpeg](img-51.jpeg)

![img-52.jpeg](img-52.jpeg)

PPGOS, the B2B digital eCommerce platform owned by PT. Parit Padang Global, continues to expand its distribution reach through PPGOS Subdistributors. This expansion was marked by the signing of Agreement on the Use of the PPGOS Application between PT. Parit Padang Global (PPG) and PT. Pingloka Distrinaga Yogyakarta.

PPGOS, platform eCommerce B2B digital milik PT. Parit Padang Global, kembali memperluas dukungannya untuk penetrasi distribusi melalui PPGOS Subdistributor. Hal ini ditandai dengan penandatanganan Berita Acara Kesepakatan Penggunaan Aplikasi PPGOS antara PT. Parit Padang Global (PPG) dan PT. Pingloka Distrinaga Yogyakarta.

In his remarks during the signing ceremony on May 23, 2024, Edwin Vega (VP of Sales &amp; Marketing PPG) expressed hope that this implementation would improve the distribution quality of both companies by providing customers with easier access to purchase products. This statement was agreed upon by Kuncara Aji Kusuma (Managing Director of PT. Pingloka Distrinaga) on the same occasion.

![img-53.jpeg](img-53.jpeg)

Dalam sambutannya pada acara penandatanganan kerjasama tanggal 23 Mei 2024, Edwin Vega (VP Sales &amp; Marketing PPG) berharap implementasi ini dapat meningkatkan kualitas distribusi kedua perusahaan dengan memberikan kemudahan bagi pelanggan dalam melakukan pembelian produk. Pernyataan ini disepakati oleh Kuncara Aji Kusuma (Managing Director PT. Pingloka Distrinaga) pada kesempatan yang sama.
