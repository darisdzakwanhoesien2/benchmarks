# Profile of Head of Internal Audit Unit

## Profil Kepala Unit Audit Internal

The Internal Audit Unit is led by a Head of the Internal Audit Unit who is appointed by the Board of Directors through a Decree of the Board of Directors. The Internal Audit Unit is responsible to the President Director and acts independently in carrying out its duties and responsibilities. In carrying out its responsibilities, the Internal Audit Unit also collaborates and coordinates with the Audit Committee.

The Internal Audit Unit was formed based on the Decree of the Company's Directors Number 05/SK-Dir/SGH/VI/2020 concerning the Establishment of the Company's Internal Audit Unit dated June 19, 2020.

Mr. Devy Heriyanto was appointed as the Head of the Company's Internal Audit Unit as of February 21, 2025, replacing Mr. Patrick Lian Abuan Co., based on the Company's Board of Directors Decree Number 01/SK-DIR/SOHO/II/2025.

The Internal Audit Unit submits a report on the results of the audits a has carried out as well as objective suggestions and recommendations in improvement to the President Director and the Audit Committee.

Unit Audit Internal dipimpin oleh seorang Kepala Unit Audit Internal yang ditunjuk oleh Direksi melalui suatu Surat Keputusan Direksi. Unit Audit Internal bertanggung jawab kepada Presiden Direktur dan bertindak secara independen dalam melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya. Dalam melaksanakan tanggung jawabnya tersebut, Unit Audit Internal juga bekerjasama dan berkoordinasi dengan Komite Audit.

Unit Audit Internal dibentuk berdasarkan Keputusan Direksi Perseroan Nomor 05/SK-Dir/SGH/VI/2020 tentang Pembentukan Unit Audit Internal Perseroan tertanggal 19 Juni 2020.

Tuan Devy Heriyanto ditunjuk sebagai Kepala Unit Audit Internal Perseroan per 21 Februan 2025 menggantilah Tuan Patrick Lian Abuan Co. berdasarkan Surat Keputusan Direksi Perseroan Nomor 01/SK-DIR/SOHO/II/2025.

Unit Internal Audit menyampaikan laporan hasil audit yang telah diakukannya serta saran dan rekomendasi perbaikan yang dapat kepada Presiden Direktur dan Komite Audit.
