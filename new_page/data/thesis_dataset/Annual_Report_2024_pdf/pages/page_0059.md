## Work Experience

- Director of PT. Astrazeneca Indonesia since 1978 to January 2025;

- Commissioner of PT. Parit Padang Global, a PT Soho Global Health Tbk's subsidiary, from 2009 to date;

## Affiliate Relations

He has an affiliate relationship with PT. Soho Global Health, Tbk, as Mr. Eng Liang Tan is the ultimate beneficial owner of PT. Soho Global Health, Tbk through the share ownership of PT. Soho Global Health, Tbk by Cascade Creek Pty Ltd. and Tiberias 96 Pty Ltd.

## Ownership of the Company's Shares

Mr. Eng Liang Tan indirectly holds shares in PT. Soho Global Health, Tbk through the share ownership of PT. Soho Global Health, Tbk by:

- Tiberias 96 Pty Ltd, where he is the 100% shareholder of Tiberias 96 Pty Ltd; and
- Cascade Creek Pty Ltd, where he indirectly holds 100% of the shares in Cascade Creek Pty Ltd.
