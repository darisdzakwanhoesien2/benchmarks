# Communication with Regulator

## Komunikasi dengan Regulator

As a public company, the Company must submit certain reports periodically or incidentally to the Financial Services Authority and the Indonesia Stock Exchange. These reports include financial and non-financial information that is presented completely, accurately, and in accordance with the transparency principle.

During the Year 2024, there were no delays identified by nor warnings from the Financial Services Authority or the Indonesia Stock Exchange sent to the Company. This demonstrates that the Company has complied and fulfilled all provisions of the regulations of the Financial Services Authority and the Indonesia Stock Exchange.
