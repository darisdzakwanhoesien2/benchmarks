As part of our purpose, mission, and business strategy, we address critical medical needs while expanding access to our products and services in both the prescription and over-the-counter sectors. Through various programs conducted throughout the year, we strive to enhance healthcare access and improve people's health and well-being.

- Health education for the public. We regularly educate the public in collaboration with health experts and the media. These educational initiatives are conducted both online (via webinars) and offline.
- Product donations to support stunting intervention. We regularly donate products to local communities and organizations, including orphanages, to help combat malnutrition. Our efforts include providing children with vitamins and educating communities and organizations on stunting prevention.

Our recent collaboration with the Community Health Center (Puskesmas) Pulogadung in East Jakarta, supports the Government's National Stunting Rate Reduction Target. We participated in a program that distributes vitamins and health supplements to hundreds of children in the East Jakarta area, near our head office and plant.

SGH also continued its collaboration with Nagrak District, Sukabumi, an area near the SCEHR research and plantation facilities. SGH's contribution was showcased through the provision of stunting intervention assistance, including processed supplementary food (PMT), donations of Curcuma Plus milk, and health education support.

Sebagai bagian dari tujuan, misi, dan strategi bisnis kami, kami menangani kebutuhan medis yang penting sambil memperluas akses ke produk dan layanan kami di sektor resep dan obat bebas. Melalui berbagai program yang dilakukan sepanjang tahun, kami bertujuan untuk meningkatkan akses ke layanan kesehatan serta meningkatkan kesehatan dan kesejahteraan masyarakat.

- Edukasi kesehatan untuk masyarakat. Kami secara rutin mengedukasi masyarakat bekerja sama dengan pakar kesehatan dan media. Edukasi dilakukan secara online (webinar) dan offline.
- Donasi produk untuk mendukung intervensi stunting. Kami juga secara rutin mendonasikan produk kepada komunitas lokal dan organisasi, termasuk panti asuhan, di mana kami berupaya mengatasi masalah gizi buruk dengan memberikan vitamin kepada anak-anak serta mengedukasi komunitas dan organisasi tentang pencegahan stunting.

Kolaborasi terkini kami dengan Puskesmas Pulogadung, Jakarta Timur, mendukung Target Penurunan Angka Stunting Nasional Pemerintah dengan berpartisipasi dalam program pendistribusian vitamin dan suplemen kesehatan kepada ratusan anak di wilayah Jakarta Timur, lokasi yag dekat dengan kantor pusat dan pabrik kami.

SGH juga melanjutkan kolaborasi dengan Kecamatan Nagrak, Sukabumi, wilayah yang berdekatan dengan fasilitas penelitian dan perkebunan SCEHR. Kontribusi SGH ditunjukkan melalui pemberian bantuan intervensi stunting, seperti pemberian makanan tambahan (PMT), donasi susu Curcuma Plus, dan dukungan edukasi kesehatan.

In 2023 and 2024, the total value of products donated amounted to IDR 265 million and IDR 340 million, respectively. Building on its commitment to a healthier Indonesia, SGH prioritized addressing stunting in 2023-2024 by providing intervention assistance, including processed supplementary food (PMT) and donations of Curcuma Plus vitamins and milk.

Pada tahun 2023 dan 2024, total produk yang didonasikan SGH senilai IDR 265 juta dan IDR 340 juta, berturut-turut. Dalam komitmennya untuk mewujudkan Indonesia yang lebih sehat, SGH memprioritaskan penanganan stunting sejak tahun 2023-2024 dengan memberikan bantuan intervensi stunting, termasuk pemberian makanan tambahan (PMT) serta donasi vitamin dan susu Curcuma Plus.
