# 01 PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) Signed a Distribution Cooperation Agreement with PT Fresenius Medical Care Indonesia (FME)

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) Menandatangani Perjanjian Kerja Sama Distribusi dengan PT Fresenius Medical Care Indonesia (FME)

# Ceremonial of New Join Cooperation

PT. Parit Padang Global &amp; PT. Fresenius Medical Care Indonesia

![img-61.jpeg](img-61.jpeg)

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG), a subsidiary of PT Soho Global Health Tbk, has entered into a distribution partnership with PT Fresenius Medical Care Indonesia (FME). The agreement was formalized through a contract signing by the respective company leaders on Thursday, June 6, 2024, at the main lobby of PT Soho Global Health.

PT Fresenius Medical Care Indonesia is a leading global manufacturer of medical equipment and provider of dialysis services, offering flagship products such as hemodialysis, hemodiafiltration, CAPD, and CRRT, which are widely recognized both in Indonesia and internationally.

"We are proud and delighted to collaborate with PPG once again," said dr. Parulian Simandjuntak, President Director of Fresenius Medical Care Indonesia. Rogelio Cooey, Director of PT Soho Global Health, warmly welcomed the partnership, stating, "All the best and welcome home, our valuable partner!"

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG), anak perusahaan PT Soho Global Health Tbk, sepakat menjalin perjanjian kerja sama distribusi dengan PT Fresenius Medical Care Indonesia (FME). Kesepakatan ini dikukuhkan melalui penandatanganan perjanjian oleh masing-masing pimpinan perusahaan pada Kamis, 6 Juni 2024, bertempat di Lobby Utama PT Soho Global Health.

PT Fresenius Medical Care Indonesia merupakan produsen peralatan dan penyedia layanan dialisis terkemuka di dunia, dengan produk unggulan seperti hemodialisis, hemodiafiltrasi, CAPD, dan CRRT yang dikenal luas di Indonesia maupun secara global.

"Merupakan kebanggaan dan kebahagiaan dapat kembali bekerja sama dengan PPG," ujar dr. Parulian Simandjuntak, Presiden Direktur Fresenius Medical Care Indonesia. Direktur PT Soho Global Health, Rogelio Cooey, menyambut dengan hangat, "All the best and welcome home, our valuable partner!"
