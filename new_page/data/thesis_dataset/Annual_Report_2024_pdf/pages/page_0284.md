## vii. Medical Healthcare

To conclude the year 2024, the Medical Healthcare Business Unit also conducted a National Sales Conference as a routine annual agenda. The conference was held in Yogyakarta from December 12, to December 14, 2024 with the theme "Gaspolli – Lit 2025" which means bringing speed and bright enthusiasm in responding to business opportunities and challenges in 2025. This conference was attended by Sales Managers, Medical Representatives, as well as teams from the Head Office and Branches in the Yogyakarta, Solo and Semarang areas.

The event also included "Coaching Technique – iGROW Model" training for Sales Managers to develop their personal potential, which was facilitated by Mrs. Rumiris F. Simaremare, SKM, MKKK, CPC, CHRM (SGH HR Management &amp; Organization Development Senior Manager), a Certified Professional Coach.

Apart from training, there was also a costume competition with racing theme which encouraged employees to think creatively and showcase their best outfits, as well as team building "The Amazing Race" with various exciting games that strengthened team unity. This aimed to train teams to work together effectively towards sustainable common goals.
