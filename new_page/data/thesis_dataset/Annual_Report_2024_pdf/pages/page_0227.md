# 5. PT. Tozy Sentosa ("Centro")

Case Status
Status Perkara

PPG is a concurrent creditor of Centro. PPG has past-due receivables owed by Centro for the pharmaceutical and OTC products supplied by PPG to Centro. Centro is unable to fulfil its payment obligations to PPG. Centro's bankruptcy application has been submitted by other creditors of Centro.

PPG adalah kreditur konkuren Centro. PPG memiliki piutang yang telah jatuh tempo dan terutang kepada Centro atas produk farmasi dan OTC yang dipasok oleh PPG dan Centro tidak mampu membayar piutang tersebut. Permohonan pailit Centro telah diajukan oleh kreditur Centro lain.

Risks Faced by the Company and the Impact on the Company
Risiko yang Dihadapi dan Pengaruhnya terhadap Perseroan

PPG is at risk of not receiving payment for its receivables in full, amounting to IDR 152,250,504 (one hundred fifty two million two hundred fifty thousand five hundred and four Rupiah).

PPG berisiko tidak memperoleh pembayaran atas piutangnya secara penuh sebesar Rp 152,250,504,- (seratus lima puluh dua juta dua ratus lima puluh ribu lima ratus empat Rupiah).

Follow-up
Tindak Lanjut

Based on the Notification of List of Bankruptcy Assets of PT Tozy Sentosa (In Bankruptcy) and Request for Account Information No. 260/Pailit-TOZY/VI/2022 dated June 14, 2022, PPG only received payments of IDR 32,216,303.53 (thirty two million two hundred sixteen thousand three hundred three point fifty three Rupiah). PPG is still awaiting the realization of the payment.

Berdasarkan Pemberitahuan Daftar Pembagian Harta Pailit PT Tozy Sentosa (Dalam Pailit) dan Permintaan Informasi Rekening No. 260/Pailit-TOZY/VI/2022 tertanggal 14 Juni 2022, PPG hanya menerima pembayaran sebesar Rp 32,216,303,53,- (tiga puluh dua juta dua ratus enam belas ribu tiga ratus tiga koma lima tiga Rupiah). PPG masih menunggu realisasi pembayaran tersebut.

# 6. PT Maesindo Indonesia ("Maesindo")

Case Status
Status Perkara

PPG and PT Maesindo Indonesia entered into a Distribution Agreement ("Distribution Agreement"). In 2022, PPG decided to terminate the Distribution Agreement. Under its terms, an event of termination would require Maesindo to repurchase all remaining products at PPG's warehouses or instruct the new distributor to repurchase the products. However, Maesindo refused to repurchase all the remaining products and did not instruct its new distributor to do so either.

PPG dan Maesindo telah menandatangani sebuah Perjanjian Distribusi ("Perjanjian Distribusi"). Pada tahun 2022, PPG memutuskan untuk mengakhiri Perjanjian Distribusi tersebut. Berdasarkan ketentuan Perjanjian Distribusi, dalam hal Perjanjian Distribusi diakhiri, Maesindo wajib membeli kembali seluruh produk yang masih tersisa di gudang PPG atau memerintahkan distributor baru Maesindo untuk membeli kembali produk tersebut. Namun, Maesindo menolak untuk membeli kembali seluruh produk yang tersisa tersebut dan juga tidak memerintahkan distributor barunya untuk melakukan hal tersebut.
