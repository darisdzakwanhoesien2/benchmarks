# Board of Commissioners Charter

## Piagam Dewan Komisaris

In supervising the Company's management, the Board of Commissioners follows the guidelines from the Board of Commissioners Charter which contains their duties, responsibilities, and authorities.

Dalam mengawasi pengelolaan Perseroan, Dewan Komisaris berpedoman pada Piagam Dewan Komisaris yang memuat tugas, tanggung jawab dan wewenang Dewan Komisaris.

# Performance Assessment of the Board of Directors

## Penilaian atas Kinerja Direksi

The Board of Commissioners highly appreciate the performance of the Board of Directors and all Company personnel for the positive performance achievements amidst various challenges faced. The Board of Commissioners considers that the Board of Directors has given its best efforts and strategies to maintain the stability and continuity of the Company's business.

Dewan Komisaris sangat mengapresiasi kinerja Direksi dan seluruh jajaran Perseroan atas pencapaian kinerja positif yang berhasil dicapai di tengah berbagai tantangan yang muncul. Dewan Komisaris menilai bahwa Direksi telah mengerahkan upaya dan strategi terbaik demi mempertahankan stabilitas dan kelangsungan bisnis Perseroan.

# Assessment of the Performance of the Board of Commissioners

## Penilaian terhadap Kinerja Dewan Komisaris

Referring to the Board of Commissioners Charter, the evaluation of the performance of the members of the Board of Commissioners is carried out by the Company's shareholders. The Company has also established a Policy on Performance Appraisal of the Board of Commissioners, which serves as a guideline for assessing the effectiveness of the implementation of the duties and responsibilities of the Board of Commissioners and also as a form of accountability for the performance assessment of the Board of Commissioners of the Company.

The Company's shareholders periodically evaluate the performance of the Board of Commissioners at least once a year. This evaluation assesses the Board of Commissioners' effectiveness in supervising and managing the Company as stated in the prevailing laws and regulations and the Company's Articles of Association.

In addition, based on the Policy on Performance Appraisal of the Board of Commissioners, the following are the provisions for the Board of Commissioners performance assessment:

1. Performance appraisal of the Board of Commissioners is performed using a self-assessment method every year collegially.
2. Performance appraisal of each member of the Board of Commissioners is performed by the President Commissioner every year based on the role and responsibilities of the Board of Commissioners.
3. Each member of the Board of Commissioners conducts a self-assessment, to which then the results are consolidated for a joint review by all members of the Board of Commissioners in order to determine the effectiveness of the Board of Commissioners and the areas that need improvement.
4. The Board of Commissioners prepares the corrective actions, and the President Commissioner ensures the implementation of the corrective actions.

Mengacu pada Piagam Dewan Komisaris, penilaian atas kinerja anggota Dewan Komisaris dilakukan oleh pemegang saham Perseroan. Perseroan juga telah menetapkan Kebijakan Penilaian atas Kinerja Dewan Komisaris, yang bertindak sebagai pedoman dalam menilai efektivitas pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab Dewan Komisaris dan juga sebagai bentuk akuntabilitas atas penilaian kinerja Dewan Komisaris Perseroan.

Pemegang saham Perseroan secara berkala melakukan evaluasi atas kinerja Dewan Komisaris setidaknya satu kali dalam setahun. Evaluasi ini menilai efektivitas Dewan Komisaris dalam melakukan pengawasan dan pengurusan Perseroan sebagaimana yang tercantum dalam peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku dan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan.

Di samping itu, berdasarkan Kebijakan Penilaian atas Kinerja Dewan Komisaris, maka di bawah ini merupakan ketentuan atas penilaian kinerja Dewan Komisaris:

1. Penilaian Kinerja Dewan Komisaris dilakukan dengan metode self-assessment setiap tahun secara kolegial.
2. Penilaian kinerja Anggota Dewan Komisaris dilakukan oleh Komisaris Utama setiap tahun berdasarkan tugas dan tanggung jawab Dewan Komisaris.
3. Masing-masing anggota Dewan Komisaris melakukan self-assessment, kemudian hasilnya dikonsolidasi untuk ditinjau bersama oleh seluruh anggota Dewan Komisaris guna menetapkan efektivitas dari Dewan Komisaris beserta area-area yang perlu dilakukan perbaikan.
4. Dewan Komisaris menyusun rencana tindak lanjut perbaikan dan Komisaris Utama memastikan pelaksanaan rencana perbaikan tersebut.
