# The Board of Commissioners' assessment of the Committees that support the implementation of the duties of the Board of Commissioners

Penilaian Dewan Komisaris terhadap Komite-Komite yang mendukung pelaksanaan tugas Dewan Komisaris

The Board of Commissioners can only function with the support of its committees. Overall, the committees supporting the Board of Commissioners have fulfilled the Year 2024 work plan, as explained below:

1. The Audit Committee understands and has mastered the problems faced by the Company in coordination with the Board of Directors' supporting units and appointed external auditors. The Audit Committee makes important and significant contributions to the Company by providing suggestions for improvements to the Company's management in order to improve the Company's performance throughout the year 2024. The Board of Commissioners has received the results of the review and performance report of the Audit Committee.

During the Year 2024, the attendance rate of members of the Audit Committee at Committee Meetings reached 67%, as explained in more detail in the Audit Committee section.

2. The Nomination and Remuneration Committee has carried out its duties, responsibilities and authorities for the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors in accordance with the Nomination and Remuneration Committee Charter.

During the Year 2024, the attendance rate of members of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee at Committee Meetings reached 93%, as explained in more detail in the Nomination and Remuneration Committee section.

Pelaksanaan fungsi Dewan Komisaris tidak lepas dari dukungan dari komite-komite yang dimiliki. Secara umum, komite-komite pendukung Dewan Komisaris telah memenuhi rencana kerja Tahun 2024, sebagaimana dijelaskan berikut:

1. Komite Audit memahami dan telah menguasai masalah yang dihadapi Perseroan serta telah mengkoordinasikannya dengan unit-unit pendukung Direksi maupun auditor eksternal yang ditunjuk oleh Perseroan. Komite Audit memberikan kontribusi yang penting dan signifikan bagi Perseroan dalam memberikan usulan-usulan perbaikan kepada manajemen Perseroan dalam rangka untuk meningkatkan kinerja Perseroan di sepanjang tahun 2024. Hasil telaahan dan laporan kinerja Komite Audit telah Dewan Komisaris terima dengan baik.

Sepanjang Tahun 2024, tingkat kehadiran anggota Komite Audit di dalam Rapat Komite mencapai 67%, sebagaimana lebih rinci dijelaskan pada bagian Komite Audit.

2. Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi telah melaksanakan tugas, tanggung jawab, dan wewenangnya terkait nominasi dan remunerasi bagi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi sesuai dengan Piagam Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi.

Sepanjang Tahun 2024, tingkat kehadiran anggota Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi di dalam Rapat Komite mencapai 93%, sebagaimana lebih rinci dijelaskan pada bagian Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi.

# Remuneration for the Board of Commissioners

Remunerasi untuk Dewan Komisaris

In Year 2024, the Company paid IDR 400,000,000 (four hundred million Rupiah) for the remuneration of the Board of Commissioners of the Group Companies.

The remuneration procedure, basis of determination, and remuneration structure are determined based on the Board of Commissioners' annual performance.

Pada Tahun 2024, Perseroan membayar Rp 400.000.000- (empat ratus juta Rupiah) untuk remunerasi Dewan Komisaris Grup Perseroan.

Prosedur remunerasi, dasar penentuan, dan struktur remunerasi ditentukan berdasarkan kinerja tahunan Dewan Komisaris.
