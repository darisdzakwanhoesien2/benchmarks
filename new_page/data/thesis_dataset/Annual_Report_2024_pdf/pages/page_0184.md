Responsible for the Finance, Business Development, Business Technology, and Procurement Divisions, including among others:
(i) to direct, control, assign and provide necessary support and advice in every implementation of the Company's business operations carried out by all business units and support units, so that all of the Company's operational activities run in accordance with the vision, mission, business objectives, strategies, policies, plans and work programs that have been determined;
(ii) to determine strategic directions and provide internal advisors needed in the implementation of the Company's operations; and
(iii) to determine the design, implementation and financial supervision of the Company (the Company group of companies and their subsidiaries).

Bertanggung jawab atas Divisi Keuangan, Pengembangan Bisnis, Teknologi Bisnis, dan Pengadaan, termasuk di antaranya:
(i) mengarahkan, mengendalikan, menugaskan dan memberikan dukungan dan saran yang diperlukan dalam setiap pelaksanaan operasional bisnis Perseroan yang dilakukan oleh seluruh unit bisnis dan unit pendukung, agar seluruh kegiatan operasional Perseroan berjalan sesuai dengan visi, misi, sasaran usaha, strategi, kebijakan, rencana, dan program kerja yang ditetapkan;
(ii) menetapkan arahan strategis dan penyediaan penasihat internal yang diperlukan dalam pelaksanaan operasional Perseroan; dan
(iii) menetapkan desain, implementasi, dan pengawasan keuangan Perseroan (kelompok perusahaan Perseroan beserta anak perusahaannya).  |
