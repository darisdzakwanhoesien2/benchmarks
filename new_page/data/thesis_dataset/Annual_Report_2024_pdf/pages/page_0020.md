## 2024 Performance in Review
Kajian Kinerja 2024

The year 2022/2024 marked the post-COVID period following the global pandemic of 2020–2021. For SOHO Group, this was a particularly challenging time, especially due to shifts in consumer purchasing behavior toward our products—particularly Imboost—which had previously experienced a sharp increase in sales during the pandemic.

Despite challenging business conditions, we have continued to make steady progress in positioning our key businesses for the future. In 2024, our net sales grew by 22.7% compared to the previous year. However, it is important to note that this growth was primarily driven by strong sales from third-party principals. Parit Padang Global (PPG), our distribution business, recorded a total net revenue increase of 23.9% compared to the previous year.

Looking back at the past year, we have much to be proud of. According to the latest IQVIA data for MAT Q4 2024, SOHO Group continues to be among the top 20 healthcare companies in Indonesia, the top 5 OTC companies, and among the top 2 in the Vitamin category. Imboost continues to be among the top 5 most valuable pharmaceutical brands/products in Indonesia across both prescription and OTC categories. These are strong milestones, and we should continue working hard to deliver success for SOHO Group. Furthermore, we have a robust pipeline of product innovation, and a cash-rich balance sheet to complement organic growth with any inorganic opportunities that may arise.

Tahun 2022/2024 menandai periode pasca-COVID setelah pandemi global pada tahun 2020–2021. Bagi SOHO Grup, ini merupakan masa yang sangat menantang, terutama akibat perubahan perilaku pembelian konsumen terhadap produk-produk kami—khususnya Imboost—yang sebelumnya mengalami lonjakan penjualan yang tajam selama masa pandemi.

Meskipun kondisi bisnis menantang, kami terus mencatat kemajuan yang stabil dalam mempersiapkan bisnis utama kami untuk masa depan. Pada tahun 2024, penjualan bersih kami tumbuh sebesar 22,7% dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya. Namun, penting untuk dicatat bahwa pertumbuhan ini terutama didorong oleh penjualan yang kuat dari prinsipal pihak ketiga. Parit Padang Global (PPG), unit bisnis distribusi kami, mencatat peningkatan pendapatan bersih sebesar 23,9% dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya.

Melihat kembali tahun yang telah berlalu, kami memiliki banyak pencapaian yang patut dibanggakan.
