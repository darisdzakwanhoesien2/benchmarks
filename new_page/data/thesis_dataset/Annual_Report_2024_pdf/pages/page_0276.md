## SGH Group Wellness Program

The Company believes that physical fitness is important to support employee health, so they remain fit at work. Therefore, on April 26, 2024 the Company launched a health program called the "SGH Wellness Program", located at the SGH Group Head Office, Jakarta. Mr. Rogelio Paulino Jr. Castillo Lao as President Director &amp; CEO said that regular exercise is important to maintain and increase heart rate so that the body's metabolism is well maintained.

The launch event of SGH Wellness Program was attended and supported by all levels of SGH's Group Management and also attended by employee representatives from each Department/Division/Unit located at the Head Office.

## Program Kesehatan SGH Grup

Perusahaan percaya bahwa kebugaran tubuh penting untuk menunjang kesehatan Karyawan agar tetap prima dalam bekerja. Oleh karena itu, pada tanggal 26 April 2024 Perusahaan telah meluncurkan program kesehatan yang diberi nama "SGH Wellness Program", bertempat di Kantor Pusat SGH Grup, Jakarta. Bapak Rogelio Paulino Jr. Castillo Lao selaku Presiden Direktur &amp; CEO menyampaikan bahwa olahraga rutin penting dilakukan untuk menjaga kestabilan detak jantung sehingga metabolisme tubuh terjaga dengan baik.

Acara peluncuran SGH Wellness Program ini mendapatkan dukungan dari seluruh jajaran Manajemen SGH Grup dan dihadiri pula oleh perwakilan Karyawan dari setiap Departemen/Divisi/Unit yang berlokasi di Kantor Pusat.
