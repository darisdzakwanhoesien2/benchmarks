# 02 The Signing of the Distribution Cooperation Agreement between PPG and PT Keva Cosmetics International

Penandatanganan Perjanjian Kerja Sama Distribusi PPG dan PT Keva Cosmetics International

![img-64.jpeg](img-64.jpeg)

PT Parit Padang Global Tandatangani Perjanjian Kerjasama Distribusi dengan PT Keva Cosmetics International (ESQA)

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) has been appointed and entrusted by PT Keva Cosmetics International to distribute the popular cosmetic brand ESQA, which is already well-known among Indonesian consumers.

PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) ditunjuk dan dipercaya oleh PT Keva Cosmetics International untuk mendistribusikan brand kosmetika ternama ESQA yang sudah banyak dikenal oleh masyarakat Indonesia.

The signing of this partnership took place on Thursday, August 8, 2024, at the lobby of PPG's headquarters in Pulogadung, East Jakarta.

Penandatanganan kerjasama ini dilaksanakan pada hari Kamis, 8 Agustus 2024 bertempat di lobi kantor pusat PPG di Pulogadung, Jakarta Timur.

PT Keva Cosmetics International is the first certified vegan cosmetics manufacturer in Indonesia, promoting the ESQA brand. Eight years after its founding in 2016, ESQA has grown into a leading brand, becoming a top brand at Watsons and Sociolla. Its various products have also received multiple awards from medias and positive reviews from consumers.

PT Keva Cosmetics International merupakan produsen kosmetik dengan sertifikasi vegan pertama di Indonesia yang mengusung brand ESQA. Delapan (8) tahun berselang sejak didirikan tahun 2016, ESQA telah berkembang menjadi brand unggulan, diantaranya dengan menjadi top brands di Watsons dan Sociolla dan beragam produknya sudah mendapatkan beragam penghargaan dari media dan juga dari review konsumen.

![img-65.jpeg](img-65.jpeg)

In her speech, Cindy Angelina, CEO &amp; Co-Founder of PT Keva Cosmetics International, expressed her confidence that PPG's distribution of ESQA products would support the brand's next level of growth. "The signing of this distribution partnership marks an important milestone, as this is the first time ESQA has appointed an exclusive distributor to expand its reach across Indonesia," said Cindy Angelina.

Pada sambutannya, Cindy Angelina, CEO &amp; Co-Founder PT Keva Cosmetic International menyatakan keyakinannya tentang pendistribusian produk ESQA oleh PPG yang diharapkan akan mendukung next level of growth dari ESQA. Penandatanganan kerjasama distribusi ini menjadi milestone penting dimana untuk pertama kalinya ESQA menunjukkan exclusive distributor untuk memperluas jangkauan distribusi ke seluruh Indonesia," ujar Cindy Angelina.
