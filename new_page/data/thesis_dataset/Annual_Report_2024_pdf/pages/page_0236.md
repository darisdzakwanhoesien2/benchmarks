|  9. | Policy on self-assessment to measure the performance of the Board of Commissioners shall be reported in the Public Company's Annual Report.

Kebijakan penilaian sendiri (self-assessment) untuk menilai kinerja Dewan Komisaris, diungkapkan melalui Laporan Tahunan Perusahaan Terbuka. | The recommendation has been implemented.

The self-assessment policy to assess the performance of the Board of Commissioners has been disclosed in the Public Company Annual Report.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.

Kebijakan penilaian sendiri (self-assessment) untuk menilai kinerja Dewan Komisaris telah diungkapkan melalui Laporan Tahunan Perusahaan Terbuka.  |
|  10. | The Board of Commissioners shall have a resignation policy for its members in the event of any financial fraud.

Dewan Komisaris mempunyai kebijakan terkait pengunduran diri anggota Dewan Komisaris apabila terlibat dalam kejahatan keuangan. | The recommendation has been implemented.

Policies regarding the resignation of members of the Board of Commissioners due to involvement in financial crimes or other legal violations have been stated in the Company's Board of Commissioners Charter and the Company's Articles of Association.

The Board of Commissioners' Charter is available on the Company's Website.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.

Kebijakan terkait pengunduran diri anggota Dewan Komisaris karena terlibat dalam kejahatan keuangan maupun pelanggaran hukum lainnya telah tercantum dalam Piagam Dewan Komisaris Perseroan dan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan.

Piagam Dewan Komisaris telah tersedia di dalam Situs Web Perseroan.  |
|  11. | The Board of Commissioners or the Committee in charge of executing the Nomination and Remuneration function shall prepare succession policy for the Nomination of members of the Board of Directors.

Dewan Komisaris atau Komite yang menjalankan fungsi Nominasi dan Remunerasi menyusun kebijakan suksesi dalam proses Nominasi anggota Direksi. | The recommendation has been implemented.

The Company's Nomination and Remuneration Committee is in charge of compiling the composition and nomination process for members of the Board of Directors and/or Board of Commissioners, as well as formulating policies and criteria in the nomination process for candidates for members of the Board of Directors and/or Board of Commissioners. This is stated in the Company's Nomination and Remuneration Committee Guidelines which are also available on the Company's Website.

The succession plan is discussed annually and the implementation of succession is carried out as needed.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.

Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi Perseroan bertugas menyusun komposisi dan proses nominasi anggota Direksi dan/atau Dewan Komisaris, serta menyusun kebijakan dan kriteria dalam proses nominasi calon anggota Direksi dan/atau Dewan Komisaris. Hal tersebut tercantum dalam Pedoman Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi Perseroan yang juga telah tersedia dalam Situs Web Perseroan.

Rencana suksesi dibahas setiap tahun dan pelaksanaan suksesi dijalankan sesuai dengan kebutuhan.  |
