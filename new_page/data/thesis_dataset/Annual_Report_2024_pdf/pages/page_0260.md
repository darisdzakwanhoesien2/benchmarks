The research and development department continues to carry out innovative initiatives to develop environmentally friendly products. Such as removing leaflets and just putting all the necessary product information on the product packaging. This initiative has been implemented for several new products since its launching. This had a big impact in terms of reducing paper use, including the amount of waste produced. We also ensure that the secondary packaging (box) that we used also has an FSC (Forest Stewardship Council) certificate, which means it is sourced from sustainable raw materials and managed responsibly.

The Production Department has made improvements in the process of filling tablets into bottles by replacing sensors and sharpening the knife that cuts the desiccant packaging, thereby reducing the occurrence of machine stops during the bottle-filling process.

Improvement efforts were also made in the process of installing envelopes on the strip by retrofitting the envelope machine and adjusting the envelope dimensions to minimize rejects and increase product yields.

Other departments, such as Quality Control and Quality Assurance are also contributing to support waste reduction. Simplification of the analysis process to speed up analysis time and reduce the use of reagents (less waste) and energy usage.

Other improvements include reducing the use of paper by developing digitalization systems, such as Change Control System (ICCS - Integrated Change Control System), Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS), Calibration and Reagent Inventory Management System (Quality Laboratory Inventory System - Q-LIS) and Deviation Report Integrated System.

Departemen penelitian dan pengembangan terus melakukan inisiatif inovatif mengembangkan produk ramah lingkungan. Seperti melepas brosur dan hanya mencantumkan semua informasi produk yang diperlukan pada kemasan produk. Inisiatif ini telah diterapkan pada beberapa produk baru sejak peluncurannya. Hal ini berdampak besar dalam hal pengurangan penggunaan kertas, termasuk jumlah sampah yang dihasilkan. Kami juga memastikan bahwa kemasan sekunder (box) yang kami gunakan juga memiliki sertifikat FSC (Forest Stewardship Council) yang artinya bersumber dari bahan baku yang berkelanjutan dan dikelola secara bertanggung jawab.

Departemen Produksi telah melakukan perbaikan dalam proses pengisian tablet ke dalam botol dengan mengganti sensor dan menajamkan pisau untuk memotong kemasan dessicant sehingga mengurangi kejadian mesin berhenti saat proses filling botol.

Upaya perbaikan juga dilakukan pada proses pemasangan amplop pada strip dengan cara retrofit mesin envelope dan pengaturan dimensi envelope agar dapat meminimalisir reject dan meningkatkan rendemen produk.

Departemen lain, seperti Quality Control dan Quality Assurance juga memiliki kontribusi untuk mendukung pengurangan limbah. Penyederhanaan proses analisis juga dapat mempercepat waktu analisis dan mengurangi penggunaan reagen (lebih sedikit limbah) dan penggunaan energi.

Perbaikan lainnya juga mengurangi penggunaan kertas melalui pengembangan sistem digitalisasi, seperti Sistem Pengendalian Perubahan (ICCS - Integrated Change Control System), Sistem Manajemen Informasi Laboratorium (LIMS), Kalibrasi dan Manajemen Inventori Reagen (Q-LIS) dan Sistem Pelaporan Penyimpangan.
