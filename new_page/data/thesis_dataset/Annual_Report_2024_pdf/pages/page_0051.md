# Continuous Improvement – Supply &amp; Operations 2024

Perbaikan Berkelanjutan – Supply &amp; Operations 2024

![img-49.jpeg](img-49.jpeg)

Continuous Improvement Competition "IMBOOST FORCE: Improvement BOOSTer FOR ExcellenCE" Competition 2024 carrying the theme 'Electrify your Idea!', successfully created extraordinary enthusiasm among the employees of Supply &amp; Operations at PT Soho Industri Pharmasi. This is evident from the 400+ improvement ideas implemented, resulting in substantial savings for the company. This year, the award ceremony was held during the Employee Gathering - Supply &amp; Operations on December 13, 2024.

The Imboost Force Competition aims to stimulate practices and business processes related to continuous improvement. Each category is evaluated based on two main aspects: SPOT (Service and Productivity Optimization) and LIGHT (Level-Up Cost Saving Through Improvement). Specifically, starting in 2024, the competition introduced an additional evaluation criteria focused on carbon emission reduction (tCO2e), emphasizing energy management and environmental sustainability, in line with the company's efforts to support eco-friendly initiatives.

Awards for the winners aim to recognize individual contributions as well as team collaboration in identifying and implementing continuous improvement projects that benefit the company/customers or the wider community. These are categorized into Individual Projects, called QCI (Quality Control Initiative), and Group Projects through QCC (Quality Control Circle) and QCP (Quality Control Project).

This annual competition, held for the 14th time this year, once again demonstrates the success of improvement ideas. Since its initiation in 2011, various improvement ideas in Supply &amp; Operations, ranging from production process automation and energy usage reduction (electricity, fuel, gas, and water) to process optimization and system digitalization, have made substantial contributions to cost savings for the company.

Kompetisi Continuous Improvement: "IMBOOST FORCE: Improvement BOOSTer FOR ExcellenCE" Competition 2024 mengusung tema "Electrify your Idea!" berhasil menciptakan antusiasme yang luar biasa di kalangan karyawan Supply &amp; Operations PT Soho Industri Pharmasi. Hal ini terbukti dengan adanya 400+ ide perbaikan yang diimplementasikan sehingga menghasilkan penghematan bagi perusahaan. Tahun ini puncak acara penghargaan dilaksanakan pada Employee Gathering - Supply &amp; Operations yang digelar tanggal 13 Desember 2024.

Kompetisi Continuous Improvement bertujuan untuk menstimulasi praktik dan proses bisnis yang terkait dengan perbaikan berkelanjutan. Setiap kategori dinilai berdasarkan dua aspek utama: SPOT (Service and Productivity Optimization) dan LIGHT (Level-Up Cost Saving Through Improvement). Khusus sejak tahun 2024 ini, ada yang membedakan penilaian kompetisi kali ini yaitu adanya penilaian tambahan terkait pengurangan emisi karbon (tCO2e) yang berfokus pada pengelolaan energi dan keberlanjutan lingkungan, sesuai dengan upaya perusahaan untuk mendukung inisiatif ramah lingkungan.

Penghargaan bagi para pemenang ditujukan untuk memberikan pengakuan atas kontribusi individu serta kolaborasi tim dalam mengidentifikasi dan menerapkan proyek perbaikan berkelanjutan yang bermanfaat bagi perusahaan/pelanggan atau masyarakat luas melalui kategori Proyek Individual yang disebut QCI (Quality Control Initiative), dan kategori Proyek Grup melalui QCC (Quality Control Circle) dan QCP (Quality Control Project).

Kompetisi tahunan yang telah diselenggarakan untuk ke-14 kalinya pada tahun ini, sekali lagi membuktikan keberhasilan ide-ide improvement. Sejak diinisiasi di tahun 2011, berbagai gagasan perbaikan di Supply &amp; Operations, mulai dari otomatisasi proses produksi, pengurangan penggunaan energi (listrik, bahan bakar, gas, dan air), optimasi proses, hingga digitalisasi sistem, telah berkontribusi untuk penghematan biaya perusahaan secara substansial.
