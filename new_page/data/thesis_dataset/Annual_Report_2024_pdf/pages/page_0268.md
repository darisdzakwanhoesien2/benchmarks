This program aims to establish a sustainable, environmentally friendly ecosystem in schools and homes. To achieve this, teachers must receive comprehensive training to understand and effectively implement the green school concept. Therefore, the Befriend with Nature program series begins with teachers training.

This program involves 150 teachers from 150 schools across various regions in West Java. The Teachers Training program took place throughout November 2024, with participants consisting of early childhood education teachers from Cirebon, Garut, and West Bandung Regency. From each of these regions, 50 teachers from 50 schools invited to participate in the training sessions at Eco Camp, Bandung.

Program ini berupaya untuk menciptakan ekosistem berkelanjutan yang ramah lingkungan di sekolah dan rumah. Untuk mewujudkannya, para guru perlu dibekali dengan pelatihan yang komprehensif agar dapat memahami dan meng-implementasikan konsep sekolah hijau secara efektif. Maka dari itu, rangkaian program Berkawan dengan Alam 2024/2025 dimulai dengan pelatihan bagi para guru atau Teachers Training.

Kegiatan ini melibatkan 150 guru dari 150 sekolah yang tersebar di berbagai daerah di Jawa Barat. Program Teachers Training berlangsung sepanjang November 2024, dengan peserta yang terdiri dari guru pendidikan usia dini dari wilayah Cirebon, Garut, dan Kabupaten Bandung Barat. Dari masing-masing wilayah, sebanyak 50 guru dari 50 sekolah diundang untuk mengikuti pelatihan yang diselenggarakan di Eco Camp, Bandung.

After the three-days training series, teachers received support in implementing the Green School project at their respective schools. During this project, they were also provided with online guidance, including progress mentoring and in-depth material sessions. The training topics covered for participants included Green Sustainable, Zero Waste, Green Repentance, Clean and Neat, and From Recycle to Refuse.

The Befriend with Nature 2024/2025 event series will conclude with the Big Bang agenda. This event will serve as a platform for sharing experiences, lessons, and inspiration among all involved parties, including teachers, students, and parents. The main activities of Big Bang will include reports on Green School projects, an Environmental Education Festival, Creativity Competitions, Green School Exhibitions, and Zero Waste Workshops. During the Environmental Education Festival, several attractions will take place, including the Green Festival, Green Health, Green Games, and Green Gathering. This entire series of activities is expected to serve as a momentum to strengthen the collective commitment to fostering a sustainable culture of environmental care.

Setelah rangkaian pelatihan selama tiga hari, para guru mendapat dukungan untuk menjalankan proyek penghijauan sekolah (Sekolah Hijau) di sekolahnya masing-masing. Selama proyek Sekolah Hijau berlangsung, para guru juga diberikan pendampingan secara daring, termasuk mentoring perkembangan dan pendalaman materi. Topik pelatihan yang diberikan kepada peserta meliputi Lestari Hijau, Zero Waste (Minim Sampah), Pertobatan Hijau, Bersih dan Rapi, dan From Recycle to Refuse.

Rangkaian acara Berkawan dengan Alam 2024/2025 akan ditutup dengan agenda Big Bang. Kegiatan ini akan menjadi wadah berbagi pengalaman, pembelajaran, dan inspirasi bagi semua pihak yang terlibat, termasuk guru, siswa, dan orang tua. Aktivitas utama dalam Big Bang meliputi laporan proyek Sekolah Hijau, Festival Edukasi Lingkungan, Lomba Kreativitas, Pameran Sekolah Hijau, dan Zero Waste Workshop. Pada Festival Edukasi Lingkungan, terdapat beberapa wahana yaitu wahana Green Festival, Green Health, Green Games, dan Green Gathering. Seluruh rangkaian kegiatan ini diharapkan dapat menjadi momentum untuk memperkuat komitmen bersama dalam menciptakan budaya peduli lingkungan yang berkelanjutan.
