# iii. Finance &amp; Accounting, Tax, Business Development, Business Technology &amp; Procurement

The Finance &amp; Accounting, Tax, Business Development, Business Technology and Procurement Business Unit held a team building from July 12 to 13, 2024 in Bogor, themed "Wonderful and Happiness, Together with Us in Bogor (WAH Batagor)".

The event started with a "bonfire" which was held in a large garden area with a cool natural atmosphere, which made employees feel fresher. In this session, employees show their creativity through the clothing and accessories they wear. Apart from that, the event was also enlivened with outbound activities, fun quizzes and door prizes.

This employee gathering was designed in a pleasant atmosphere as a refreshment for employees after carrying out busy routines so that they can return to work more refreshed and productive, as well as increasing togetherness, employee involvement, solidarity and more reliable teamwork.
