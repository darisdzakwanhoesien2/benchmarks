## President Director / Presiden Direktur

Responsible for coordinating all operational activities of the Company, including among others:

(i) Design, coordinate, direct, control, supervise and evaluate the tasks of the Company's corporations and business units, so that all of the Company's operational activities run in accordance with the vision, mission, business targets, strategies, policies, plans and work programs which has been decided;

(ii) Aligning all internal initiatives within the Company, and also ensuring the increment of the Company's competitive ability;

(iii) Ensuring that all information related to the Company is always available when it's needed by the Board of Commissioners or shareholders;

(iv) Organizing and leading regular meetings of the Board of Directors according to the decision of the Board of Directors or if deemed necessary according to the recommendation of the Board of Directors; and

(v) Delegating work to other members of the Board of Directors or appointing other members of the Board of Directors to act on behalf of the Board of Directors and represent the Company.

Bertanggung jawab untuk mengkoordinasikan semua kegiatan operasional Perseroan, termasuk di antaranya:

(i) Merencanakan, mengkoordinasikan, mengarahkan, mengendalikan, mengawasi dan mengevaluasi tugas korporasi Perseroan dan unit bisnis, agar seluruh kegiatan operasional Perseroan berjalan sesuai dengan visi, misi, sasaran usaha, strategi, kebijakan, rencana, dan program kerja yang ditetapkan;

(ii) Menyelaraskan seluruh inisiatif-inisiatif di internal Perseroan, serta memastikan adanya peningkatan dalam kemampuan bersaing Perseroan;

(iii) Memastikan segala informasi yang terkait dengan Perseroan selalu tersedia bila diperlukan oleh Dewan Komisaris atau pemegang saham;

(iv) Menyelenggarakan dan memimpin Rapat Direksi secara berkala sesuai ketetapan Direksi atau apabila dipandang perlu sesuai usulan Direksi; dan

(v) Mendelegasikan pekerjaan kepada anggota Direksi lainnya atau menunjuk anggota Direksi lain untuk bertindak atas nama Direksi dan mewakili Perseroan.

## Director / Direktur

(i) Carry out the Company's management duties in accordance with the Company's aims and objectives and always strive to improve the Company's efficiency and effectiveness;

(ii) Plan, coordinate, direct, control, supervise and evaluate the corporate duties of the Company and its business units and supporting units, so that all operational activities of the Company run in accordance with the vision, mission, business targets, strategies, policies, plans and work programs determined by the Company;

(iii) Determine the strategic direction that will be implemented by the Company;

(iv) Responsible for implementing the Company's overall business strategy and operations;

(v) Leading the executive management team in carrying out the Company's vision and mission;

(vi) Supervise the Company's operational and financial performance so that it meets the set targets;

(vii) Develop and implement the Company's business strategy policies and make important decisions for the company; and

(viii) Manage business risks and opportunities to ensure the Company's growth.

(i) Melaksanakan tugas kepengurusan Perseroan sesuai dengan maksud dan tujuan Perseroan dan senantiasa berusaha meningkatkan efisiensi dan efektivitas Perseroan;

(ii) Merencanakan, mengkoordinasikan, mengarahkan, mengendalikan, mengawasi dan mengevaluasi tugas korporasi Perseroan dan unit bisnis serta unit pendukung, agar seluruh kegiatan operasional Perseroan berjalan sesuai dengan visi, misi, sasaran usaha, strategi, kebijakan, rencana, dan program kerja yang ditetapkan oleh Perseroan;

(iii) Menetapkan arahan strategis yang akan dijalankan oleh Perseroan;

(iv) Bertanggung jawab atas pelaksanaan strategi bisnis dan operasional perusahaan secara keseluruhan;
