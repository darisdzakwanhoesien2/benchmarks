Employee engagement refers to an Employee's emotional commitment and dedication to the job, the Company, and its goals. The Company continues to strive to create a more positive and productive work culture, by investing in various Employee engagement activities, including training and team building which have been carried out consistently every year and have been running for more than 5 years. Apart from that, the Company also provides awards as a form of appreciation for high performing employees, which is expected to increase employee loyalty to continue working for the Company.

This is the Company's commitment to increasing employee engagement. When employees feel "emotionally attached" to the Company, they will provide their optimal potential which will ultimately have a positive impact on the Company. In 2024, training, team building, and awards were carried out at national conferences in each business unit.

## a. Team Building

### i. Consumer Healthcare

Starting 2024, the Consumer Healthcare Business Unit has held a National Sales Conference from January 08 to 11, 2024 located in Bogor. The theme was "Smart Act, Big Impact" with a background of space astronauts which is interpreted as a smart strategy that will bring big impact and bigger results. To increase the team's motivation and skills, Mr. Parlindungan Marpaung, a public speaker and training facilitator, was invited to present a training session on the topic "How to be a Great Leader". Apart from that, the event was also enlivened with a best costume competition that honed employee creativity, followed by an awards night as an appreciation for the teams who had given their best contribution to the Company, and of course team building. Team building can be an activity that encourages each team member to socialize and get to know each other. Good personal relationships between each team member will have a positive impact on the atmosphere in the work environment. At the same time, they can collaborate better and work more efficiently.
