# 2024 Awards &amp; Recognitions

Prestasi dan Penghargaan di Tahun 2024

Bronze Winner and Team of The Year – OPEXCON 2024

Pemenang Medali Perunggu dan Team of The Year – OPEXCON 2024

![img-48.jpeg](img-48.jpeg)

On November 21, 2024, the annual Operational Excellence competition, a significant event for business practitioners in Indonesia, was held under the title Indonesia Operational Excellence Conference &amp; Award (OPEXCON) 2024. The event is the highlight of the annual competition organized by SHIFT Indonesia in partnership with SSCX International. It serves as a prestigious platform to celebrate the achievements of innovative companies in Indonesia that have made tangible contributions through process improvement efforts and operational efficiency.

This competition was participated in by various private institutions, government agencies, and state-owned enterprises. The selection and judging process involved a panel of professional judges, including academics, government officials, and Continuous Improvement practitioners. This prestigious annual competition once again showcases a variety of outstanding projects that demonstrate the industrial sector's commitment to operational efficiency.

Building on the success of the previous year, PT Soho Industri Pharmasi once again achieved remarkable recognition at the OPEXCON 2024 competition, securing two prestigious awards: Winner in the Bronze Category and Team of the Year 2024. The project submitted this year, titled "Optimize Packaging Process for Diapet Caps 10s," was led by a dedicated team comprising Christophorus Aditya (Solid Production Manager), Jeremia Mehaga (Continuous Improvement Specialist), Heru Kurniawan (Maintenance Manager), and Cecep Subarnas (Solid Production Operator).

This project has significantly benefited the company. Through the innovations implemented, the Solid Production team successfully reduced man-hours in the secondary packaging process for Diapet capsules, lowered operational costs, and enhanced production line efficiency. The production process became faster, with a substantial reduction in runtime, resulting in increased dosage output. This achievement reinforces the hope that the Continuous Improvement culture within SGH will continue to drive enhanced company performance.

Pada tanggal 21 November 2024 digelar perhelatan tahunan kompetisi operational excellence yang sangat penting bagi para pelaku bisnis di Indonesia bertajuk Indonesia Operational Excellence Conference &amp; Award (OPEXCON) 2024. Acara ini merupakan acara puncak kompetisi tahunan yang diselenggarakan oleh SHIFT Indonesia bermitra dengan SSCX International. Acara ini menjadi ajang bergengsi untuk merayakan pencapaian perusahaan-perusahaan inovatif di Indonesia yang telah memberikan kontribusi nyata melalui upaya perbaikan proses dan efisiensi operasional.

Kompetisi ini diikuti oleh berbagai instansi swasta dan instansi pemerintah, maupun BUMN, dimana proses seleksi &amp; penjuriannya melibatkan dewan juri profesional, baik dari akademisi, instansi pemerintah, hingga praktisi Continuous Improvement. Kompetisi tahunan bergengsi ini kembali menampilkan beragam proyek unggulan yang menunjukkan komitmen dunia industri terhadap efisiensi operasional.

Mengulang sukses tahun sebelumnya, pada kompetisi OPEXCON 2024 ini, PT Soho Industri Pharmasi kembali membawa pulang dua penghargaan sekaligus: The Winner Bronze Category dan Team of The Year 2024. Adapun proyek yang diajukan tahun ini bertajuk "Optimize Packaging Process for Diapet Caps 10s" dengan tim yang beranggotakan Christophorus Aditya (Solid Production Manager), Jeremia Mehaga (Continuous Improvement Specialist), Heru Kurniawan (Maintenance Manager), dan Cecep Subarnas (Solid Production Operator).

Proyek ini memberikan dampak positif yang signifikan bagi perusahaan. Berkat inovasi yang diterapkan, tim Solid Production berhasil mengurangi manhour pada proses pengemasan sekunder Diapet kapsul, menekan biaya operasional, serta meningkatkan efisiensi lini produksi. Proses produksi menjadi lebih cepat dengan penurunan waktu proses (run time) yang signifikan, sehingga output dosage juga meningkat. Melalui prestasi ini, diharapkan budaya Continuous Improvement di lingkup SGH menjadi budaya kerja yang dapat terus meningkatkan kinerja perusahaan.
