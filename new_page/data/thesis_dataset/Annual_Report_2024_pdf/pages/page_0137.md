Net Revenue of the Professional Products Division in 2024 increased by 21.6% compared to 2023, driven primarily by the strong performance of Imboost Force (an immune-boosting supplement), as the public increasingly sought to maintain their health by consuming immunity-enhancing supplements. This growth was also supported by a shift in consumer behavior following the COVID-19 pandemic, with greater awareness and attention to personal health. This trend positively impacted the sales of products within the Professional Products Division, including skincare products, which contributed to the sales growth of Noroid and Asthin. Both products benefited from robust support through e-commerce services and sales channels in 2024. Additionally, in 2024, the division recorded incremental sales from newly launched products, namely Imboost Force Cough Syrup for Adults and Boost Protekid for children. The division also expanded its Noroid product range by introducing more economical packaging in smaller sizes.

The Consumer Health segment recorded a net revenue increase of 13.9% at year-end, reaching Rp 490.6 billion and contributing approximately 4.9% to the Company's total net revenue. This growth was primarily driven by the strong performance of Imboost, which gained increasing recognition among consumers through television advertisements and digital marketing efforts. Imboost is widely trusted to enhance the immune system, especially with the innovative Imboost Extra variant, which is enriched with vitamins C and D and available in both tablet and effervescent forms. Another factor supporting sales growth in this segment was the positive performance of Curcuma Plus Vitamin, which benefited from expanded distribution in retail pharmacies as well as increased online sales through e-commerce platforms.

Net revenue from the Alliance segment increased by 13.8% compared to 2023. This growth was primarily supported by the launch of new products such as Mycophen and Tacsograf, as well as higher sales of Pramifrol and Soroquin, which were listed in the national e-catalogue. Additionally, the performance of Flutison also contributed positively to the segment's overall sales growth.

Net revenues from the Others segment decreased by 0.4%, mainly due to a decrease in revenue from sales of Unihealth products.
