## 9. Apotek Ben Waras

Case Status
Status Perkara

Apotek Ben Waras is one of PPG's customers which purchases PPG products for resale to end customers. Since June of 2024, Apotek Ben Waras has stopped making payments for invoices which has been due and payable to PPG.

Apotek Ben Waras merupakan salah satu pelanggan PPG yang membeli produk untuk dijual kembali ke pelanggan akhir. Sejak bulan Juni tahun 2024, Apotek Ben Waras telah berhenti melakukan pembayaran atas tagihan yang telah jatuh tempo dan terutang kepada PPG.

Risks Faced by the Company and the Impact on the Company
Risiko yang Dihadapi dan Pengaruhnya terhadap Perseroan

PPG is at risk of not receiving the full payment of the invoices amounting to IDR 304,028,170 (three hundred four million twenty eight thousand one hundred seventy Rupiah).

PPG berisiko tidak akan memperoleh pembayaran atas tagihan tersebut secara penuh sebesar Rp 304.028.170,- (tiga ratus empat juta dua puluh delapan ribu seratus tujuh puluh Rupiah).

Follow-up
Tindak Lanjut

PPG has submitted a lawsuit to the Brebes District Court which is currently still in progress.

PPG telah mengajukan gugatan ke Pengadilan Negeri Brebes dan masih dalam proses.
