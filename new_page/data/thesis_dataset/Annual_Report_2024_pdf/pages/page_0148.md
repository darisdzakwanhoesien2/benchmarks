# Implementation of Good Corporate Governance Principles

## Penerapan atas Tata Kelola Perusahaan

The Company's commitment to implement GCG is reflected in its business activities which are always based on the fulfillment of the following GCG principles:

Komitmen Perseroan dalam penerapan GCG tercermin dalam pelaksanaan kegiatan usaha yang selalu didasari atas pemenuhan asas-asas GCG sebagai berikut:

|  Principle
Asas | Description
Penjelasan | Implementation
Penerapan  |

|  Transparency
Transparansi | Providing material information that is relevant, precise, clear, accurate, easily accessible and easily understood by shareholders and stakeholders.

The decision-making process is carried out in a transparent manner in order to protect the interests of all stakeholders.

Menyediakan informasi materiil yang relevan, tepat, jelas, akurat, mudah diakses, dan mudah dipahami oleh pemegang saham dan para pemangku kepentingan.

Proses pengambilan keputusan dilakukan secara transparan dalam rangka melindungi kepentingan seluruh pemangku kepentingan. | i) Compilation of Annual Company Work Plans and Budgets;
ii) Compilation of Annual Reports, Sustainability Report and Good Corporate Governance Reports;
iii) Publication of Financial Statements (annual, semester, and quarterly);
iv) Documenting every decision-making process during the meetings of the Board of Directors, Board of Commissioners and Committees of the Company.

i) Menyusun Rencana Kerja dan Anggaran Perseroan Tahunan;
ii) Membuat Laporan Tahunan, Laporan Keberlanjutan, dan Laporan Tata Kelola;
iii) Mempublikasikan Laporan Keuangan (tahunan, semesteran, ataupun triwulanan);
iv) Mendokumentasikan setiap proses pengambilan keputusan selama Rapat Direksi, Dewan Komisaris maupun Komite Perseroan.  |
|  Accountability
Akuntabilitas | Providing clarity of functions, structure, systems, and accountability of each organ of the Company regarding the implementation of its duties and responsibilities for operational activities to be carried out effectively.

Managing the Company accurately and measurably, ensuring alignment with the interest of shareholders and other stakeholders.

Working with a sense of high accountability and being responsible for all actions.

Memberikan kejelasan fungsi, struktur, sistem, dan pertanggungjawaban setiap organ Perseroan atas pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya agar pengelolaan kegiatan operasional Perseroan terlaksana secara efektif.

Mengelola Perseroan secara tepat dan terukur, memastikan keselarasan dengan kepentingan pemegang saham dan pemangku kepentingan lainnya.

Bekerja dengan akuntabilitas tinggi dan bertanggung jawab atas semua tindakan yang dilakukan. | i) Availability of an organizational structure, Committee Charter, standard operating procedures, guidelines and manuals that contain clear functions, implementation and responsibilities of shareholders, Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, Committees, Corporate Secretary and business units of the Company;
ii) Implementation of three (3) levels of accountability in each activity, including:
• Corporate accountability;
• Team accountability; and
• Individual accountability.

iii) Implement a check and balance mechanism in relation to the authority and role of each Company organ in managing the Company;
iv) There are clearly defined Key Performance Indicators (KPI) and operational targets;
v) Preparation of financial reports involving external audits (public accounting firms/'KAP') which are carried out by taking into account the aspects of independency in terms of personnel, processes and audit results.  |
