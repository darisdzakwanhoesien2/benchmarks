In accordance with Law Number 17 of 2023 concerning Health, and Circular Letter of the Minister of Health Number HK.02.01/MENKES/1048/2023 concerning the Implementation of Hospital Accreditation, Class D Primary Hospitals, Community Health Centers and Clinics, as well as Reporting National Quality Indicators for Places Independent Practice for Doctors and Independent Practice for Dentists, it is stated that every health service facility is obliged to continuously and sustainably improve the quality of health services. Therefore, the Company is committed to taking part in the PT SOHO Industri Pharmasi Primary Clinic accreditation survey through the Online Health Facilities Data (DFO) application which was created and managed by the Ministry of Health.

The accreditation survey is an assessment to measure the achievement and implementation of accreditation standards while determining accreditation status is the granting of an accreditation certificate based on the results of the accreditation survey. Accreditation Standards are guidelines that contain levels of achievement that must be met by health service facilities to improve service quality and patient safety.

The accreditation survey was carried out using 2 (two) methods, including hybrid or offline and online methods, and was carried out on May 13, and May 15, 2024. As a result, the PT SOHO Industri Pharmasi Primary Clinic received Primary Clinic Accreditation with the title "Plenary" from the Ministry of Health of the Republic of Indonesia. Plenary Accreditation is the highest recognition based on an assessment of health facilities, standards and ongoing service quality.

The representatives of the Board of Directors and the Doctor in-charge of the Primary Clinic of PT SOHO Industri Pharmasi and all clinic staff are committed to continuously maintaining and improving facilities, service quality and patient safety.
