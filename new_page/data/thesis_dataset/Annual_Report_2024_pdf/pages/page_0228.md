Risks Faced by the Company and the Impact on the Company
Risiko yang Dihadapi dan Pengaruhnya terhadap Perseroan

PPG is at risk of not receiving the full payment for the remaining stock of products which has a valuation of IDR 4,764,196,844 (four billion seven hundred sixty four million one hundred ninety six thousand eight hundred forty four Rupiah).

PPG berisiko tidak akan memperoleh pembayaran secara penuh atas sisa stok produk tersebut yang memiliki valuasi sebesar Rp 4.764.196.844 (empat miliar tujuh ratus enam puluh empat juta seratus sembilan puluh enam ribu delapan ratus empat puluh empat Rupiah).

Follow-up
Tindak Lanjut

PPG's lawsuit was initially rejected/deemed inadmissible by the Bantul District Court at February 29, 2024. Following this, PPG filed an appeal with the Yogyakarta High Court, which ruled in PPG's favor in its decision No. 45/PDT/2024/PT YYK dated May 13, 2024. Currently, Maesindo has submitted an appeal to the Supreme Court (cassation). PPG is still following and monitoring the progress of the cassation submission process.

Gugatan PPG ditolak/dinyatakan tidak dapat diterima oleh Pengadilan Negeri Bantul pada 29 Februari 2024. Kemudian, PPG mengajukan banding di Pengadilan Tinggi Yogyakarta, yang mengabulkan gugatan PPG dalam putusan No. 45/PDT/2024/PT YYK tertanggal 13 Mei 2024. Saat ini, Maesindo sedang mengajukan kasasi ke Mahkamah Agung. PPG masih mengikuti dan memonitor perkembangan proses pengajuan kasasi tersebut.

# 7. PT Irma Pertiwi ("Irma Pertiwi")

Case Status
Status Perkara

Irma Pertiwi is one of PPG's customers which purchases PPG products for resale to end customers. Since 2022, Irma has stopped making payments for the invoices which has been due and payable to PPG.

Irma Pertiwi merupakan salah satu pelanggan PPG yang membeli produk PPG untuk dijual kembali ke pelanggan akhir. Sejak tahun 2022, Irma Pertiwi telah berhenti melakukan pembayaran atas tagihan yang telah jatuh tempo dan terutang kepada PPG.

Risks Faced by the Company and the Impact on the Company
Risiko yang Dihadapi dan Pengaruhnya terhadap Perseroan

PPG is at risk of not receiving the full payment of the invoices amounting to IDR 698,595,848 (six hundred ninety eight million five hundred ninety five thousand eight hundred forty eight Rupiah).

PPG berisiko tidak akan memperoleh pembayaran secara penuh atas tagihan tersebut sebesar Rp 698.595.848 (enam ratus sembilan puluh delapan lima ratus sembilan puluh lima delapan ratus empat puluh delapan Rupiah).

Follow-up
Tindak Lanjut

In its decision No. 643/Pdt.G/2023/PN JKT.TIM dated March 6, 2024, the East Jakarta District Court ruled that Irma Pertiwi was in default, causing harm to PPG, and ordered Irma Pertiwi to pay damages amounting to IDR 698,595,848 (six hundred ninety eight million five hundred ninety five thousand eight hundred forty eight Rupiah). However, to date, Irma Pertiwi has not made any payments to PPG.

Dalam putusan No. 643/Pdt.G/2023/PN JKT.TIM tertanggal 6 Maret 2024, Pengadilan Negeri Jakarta Timur memutuskan Irma Pertiwi telah melakukan wanprestasi yang merugikan PPG sehingga Irma Pertiwi wajib membayar kerugian sebesar Rp 698.595.848,- (enam ratus sembilan puluh delapan juta lima ratus sembilan puluh lima ribu delapan ratus empat puluh delapan Rupiah). Hingga saat ini, Irma Pertiwi belum melakukan pembayaran apapun kepada PPG.
