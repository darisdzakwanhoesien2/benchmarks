# Inauguration of the PPG DC Logistics Warehouse, C&amp;R Warehouse, PPG Jakarta 3 Branch, and SOHO Warehouse

Peresmian Gudang PPG DC Logistik, Gudang C&amp;R, PPG Cabang Jakarta 3, dan Gudang SOHO

![img-54.jpeg](img-54.jpeg)

On Wednesday, January 10, 2024, PT Soho Industri Pharmasi (SIP) and PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) marked a significant step in strengthening their synergy. A ceremonial inauguration and blessing service were held as an expression of gratitude to God for the successful relocation of the Additional DC Logistics Warehouse and C&amp;R Warehouse from Pulo Kambing to the Rawa Sumur BB 1 Warehouse at the beginning of 2024. Additionally, the relocation of PPG Jakarta 3 Branch and SOHO Warehouse to Pulo Gadung 8 was also completed during this period.

The event began with a blessing ceremony involving the sprinkling of holy water, followed by visits to several locations, including the Rawa Sumur DC, the Additional DC and C&amp;R Warehouses, the Jakarta 3 Branch, and the SOHO Warehouse. The inauguration was symbolized by a ribbon-cutting ceremony led by the management, marking SGH's continuous growth and its commitment to providing even better service to its customers.

Pada Rabu, 10 Januari 2024, PT Soho Industri Pharmasi (SIP) dan PT Parit Padang Global (PPG) menandai langkah penting dalam memperkuat sinergi antara keduanya. Acara peresmian dan misa pemberkatan digelar sebagai ungkapan syukur kepada Tuhan atas selesainya proses perpindahan Gudang Tambahan DC Logistik dan Gudang C&amp;R dari Pulo Kambing ke Gudang Rawa Sumur BB 1 di awal tahun 2024. Selain itu, perpindahan Cabang PPG Jakarta 3 dan Gudang SOHO ke Pulo Gadung 8 juga turut dilaksanakan dalam momentum ini.

Rangkaian acara diawali dengan pemberkatan gudang melalui percikan air suci, diikuti dengan kunjungan ke berbagai lokasi, yaitu DC Rawa Sumur, Gudang Tambahan DC dan Gudang C&amp;R, Cabang Jakarta 3, dan Gudang SOHO. Acara peresmian ditandai dengan pemotongan pita oleh manajemen, simbol pertumbuhan SGH yang berkelanjutan dan komitmen untuk memberikan pelayanan yang semakin baik bagi pelanggan.
