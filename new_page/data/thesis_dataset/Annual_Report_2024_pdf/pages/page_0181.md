# Duties and Authorities of the Board of Directors

## Tugas dan Wewenang Direksi

The implementation of the duties and authorities of the Board of Directors are subject to the provisions stipulated in the Articles of Association and the prevailing laws and regulations.

The Board of Directors has the duties and responsibilities in accordance with the Articles of Association of the Company as follows:

1. The Board of Directors is in charge of carrying out and being responsible for the management of the Company in all aspects, and for the best interests of the Company in accordance with the purposes and objectives of the Company as stipulated in the Articles of Association. In carrying out the duties and responsibilities of managing the Company, the Board of Directors is supervised by the Board of Commissioners.

2. In carrying out the duties and responsibilities for management, the Board of Directors is required to hold an Annual GMS and other GMS as stipulated in the laws and regulations and the Articles of Association and submit the accountability report on its management to the Shareholders through such GMS.

3. Each member of the Board of Directors must carry out their duties and responsibilities in good faith, full of responsibility and prudence.

4. The Board of Directors is obliged to secure, manage and strive to improve the Company's valuable assets.

5. The Board of Directors has the right to represent the Company inside and outside the Court on all matters and in all events, bind the Company to other parties and other parties to the Company, and carry out all actions regarding decision and ownership, but with the limitation that for certain matters, the Board of Directors must obtain prior approval from the Company's Board of Commissioners, namely:

a. borrowing or lending money on behalf of the Company (excluding withdrawing the Company's money from the banks) which amount is in excess of the limit as determined by the Board of Commissioners from time to time;

b. establishing a business entity or participating in other companies, both in Indonesia or outside Indonesia;

c. binding the Company as guarantor; and

d. purchase or dispose of the Company's assets for a value equal to or above 50% (fifty percent) of the Company's total equity.

However, the involvement of the Board of Commissioners does not negate the responsibility of the Board of Directors for the management of the Company.

6. The Board of Directors must meet the requirements specified in the Capital Market regulations in carrying out actions that constitute material transactions and/or conflict of interests as referred to in the applicable Capital Market regulations.

7. Members of the Board of Directors are not authorized to represent the Company if there is a case in court between the Company and the member of the Board of Directors concerned.

Pelaksanaan tugas dan wewenang Direksi tunduk pada ketentuan yang diatur dalam Anggaran Dasar dan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku.

Direksi memiliki tugas dan wewenang Direksi sesuai Anggaran Dasar Perseroan yakni sebagai berikut:

1. Direksi bertugas menjalankan dan bertanggung jawab atas pengurusan Perseroan di segala aspek, dan untuk kepentingan terbaik Perseroan sesuai dengan maksud dan tujuan Perseroan yang ditetapkan dalam Anggaran Dasar. Dalam menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawab atas pengurusan Perseroan, Direksi diawasi oleh Dewan Komisaris.

2. Direksi wajib menyelenggarakan RUPS Tahunan dan RUPS lainnya sebagaimana diatur dalam peraturan perundang-undangan dan Anggaran Dasar, dan menyampaikan laporan pertanggungjawaban atas pengurusannya kepada para Pemegang Saham melalui RUPS tersebut.

3. Setiap anggota Direksi wajib melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya dengan itikad baik, penuh tanggung jawab, dan kehati-hatian.

4. Direksi wajib mengamankan, mengelola, dan berupaya meningkatkan aset-aset berharga Perseroan.

5. Direksi berhak mewakili Perseroan di dalam dan di luar Pengadilan tentang segala hal dan dalam segala kejadian, mengikat Perseroan dengan pihak lain dan pihak lain dengan Perseroan, serta menjalankan segala tindakan baik yang mengenai kepengurusan maupun kepemilikan, akan tetapi dengan pembatasan bahwa untuk hal-hal tertentu, Direksi harus memperoleh persetujuan terlebih dahulu dari Dewan Komisaris Perseroan, yakni:

a. meminjam atau meminjamkan uang atas nama Perseroan (tidak termasuk pengambilan uang Perseroan di bank-bank) yang jumlahnya melebihi batasan sebagaimana ditetapkan oleh Dewan Komisaris dari waktu ke waktu;

b. mendirikan suatu usaha atau turut serta pada perusahaan lain baik di dalam maupun di luar negeri;

c. mengikat Perseroan sebagai penjamin; dan

d. membeli atau melepaskan aset Perseroan untuk nilai setara atau di atas 50% (lima puluh persen) dari total ekuitas Perseroan.

Namun demikian, keterlibatan Dewan Komisaris tersebut tidak meniadakan tanggung jawab Direksi atas pelaksanaan kepengurusan Perseroan.

6. Direksi harus memenuhi persyaratan yang ditentukan dalam peraturan Pasar Modal dalam melaksanakan tindakan yang merupakan transaksi material dan/atau benturan kepentingan yang dimaksud dalam peraturan Pasar Modal yang berlaku.

7. Anggota Direksi tidak berwenang mewakili Perseroan apabila terdapat perkara di pengadilan antara Perseroan dengan anggota Direksi yang bersangkutan.
