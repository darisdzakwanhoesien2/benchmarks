c. Employee Training in the Legal, Compliance, Internal Audit, Corporate Secretary &amp; Human Resources Department

Employees of the Legal, Compliance, Internal Audit, Corporate Secretary &amp; Human Resources Business Units also held training for their entire team as an employee development program. This training raised the topic "Communication Skill" which was held offline and facilitated by Mrs. Rumiris F. Simaremare (HR Management &amp; Organization Development Senior Manager). The training was divided into 2 sessions, the first session for the Human Resources Division which was held on May 3, 2024 and the second session for the Legal, Compliance, Internal Audit and Corporate Secretary Divisions on July 2, 2024.

In addition to communication skills training, the Human Resources Division also held "Training of Trainers" on July 5, 2024, as a refreshment session for the entire team which will facilitate orientation training for New Employees.

d. Employee Training in the Medical Healthcare Department

As a self-development opportunity for Sales Managers in the Medical Healthcare Business Unit, a "Coaching Technique-iGROW Model" training was held on December 12, 2024, facilitated by Mrs. Rumiris F. Simaremare, SKM, MKKK, CPC, CHRM (HR Management &amp; Organizations Development Senior Manager), a Certified Professional Coach. This training is one of the agenda for the 2024 National Sales Conference took place in Yogyakarta.

Subsequently, on December 13, 2024, Mrs. Rumiris also presented a refreshment session on "Corporate Values" for all National Sales Conference participants, consisted of Sales Managers, Medical Representatives, as well as teams from the Head Office and Branches in the Yogyakarta, Solo and Semarang areas.

c. Pelatihan Karyawan di Departemen Hukum, Kepatuhan, Audit Internal, Sekretaris Perusahaan &amp; Sumber Daya Manusia

Karyawan yang tergabung pada Unit Bisnis Hukum, Kepatuhan, Audit Internal, Sekretaris Perusahaan &amp; Sumber Daya Manusia juga mengadakan pelatihan bagi seluruh timnya sebagai program pengembangan Karyawan. Pelatihan ini mengangkat topik "Keterampilan Berkomunikasi" yang diadakan secara tatap muka, dengan pembicara Ibu Rumiris F. Simaremare (HR Management &amp; Organization Development Senior Manager). Pelatihan terbagi menjadi 2 sesi, yaitu sesi pertama untuk Divisi Sumber Daya Manusia yang diadakan pada tanggal 03 Mei 2024 dan sesi kedua untuk Divisi Hukum, Kepatuhan, Audit Internal, dan Sekretaris Perusahaan pada tanggal 02 Juli 2024.

Selain pelatihan keterampilan berkomunikasi, Divisi Sumber Daya Manusia juga mengadakan "Pelatihan bagi Pengajar" pada tanggal 05 Juli 2024, sebagai sesi penyegaran bagi seluruh tim yang akan memfasilitasi pelatihan orientasi bagi Karyawan Baru.

d. Pelatihan Karyawan di Departemen Medical Healthcare

Sebagai pengembangan potensi diri bagi Manajer Penjualan pada Unit Bisnis Medical Healthcare, telah diadakan pelatihan dengan topik "Coaching Technique - iGROW Model" pada tanggal 12 Desember 2024, yang difasilitasi oleh Ibu Rumiris F. Simaremare, SKM, MKKK, CPC, CHRM (HR Management &amp; Organization Development Senior Manager), seorang Certified Professional Coach. Pelatihan ini menjadi salah satu agenda pada Konferensi Penjualan Nasional 2024 yang bertempat di Yogyakarta.

Setelahnya, pada tanggal 13 Desember 2024, Ibu Rumiris juga membawakan sesi penyegaran mengenai Nilai-nilai Perusahaan yang berjudul "Corporate Values" bagi seluruh peserta Konferensi Penjualan Nasional, yang terdiri dari para Manajer Penjualan, Medical Representative, serta tim dari Kantor Pusat dan Cabang di area Yogyakarta, Solo, dan Semarang.
