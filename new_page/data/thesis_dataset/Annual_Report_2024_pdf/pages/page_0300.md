### Local Communities [ORI 413-1]

Collaboration with communities around the SOHO Center of Excellence in Herbal Research (SCEHR), in this case with the Dahlia Women Farmers Group, is still ongoing, including implementation of Smart Farming 4.0 which was initiated in 2020 and has been applied to all planted areas. We also encourage farmers to apply intercropping with sweet corn plants to help the farmers have more income from crops. We conducted research related to the cultivation of intercropping during the curcuma cultivation process, where the method of intercropping with sweet corn did not affect the quality of the curcuma produced. Intercropping was able to provide a positive impact on farmers, repeated land cultivation, enriches the soil so that it has a positive impact on curcuma cultivation. We also provide training support to increase farmers' knowledge and skills by sharing knowledge regarding "Procedures for Planting Curcuma and Using Organic Fertilizer" and "Occupational Safety and Health".

### Masyarakat Lokal [ORI 413-1]

Kerjasama dengan masyarakat sekitar SOHO Center of Excellence in Herbal Research (SCEHR) dalam hal ini dengan Kelompok Wanita Tani Dahlia masih tetap dilakukan, termasuk implementasi Smart Farming 4.0 yang telah dirintis pada tahun 2020 dan telah diterapkan di seluruh area tanam. Bahkan tumpang sari tidak hanya mampu memberikan dampak positif bagi petani, penggarapan lahan secara berulang mampu menyuburkan tanah sehingga juga dapat memberikan dampak positif bagi budidaya temulawak.
