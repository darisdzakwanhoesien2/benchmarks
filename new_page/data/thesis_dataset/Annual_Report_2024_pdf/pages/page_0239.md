|  19. | Public Company shall have anti-corruption and anti-fraud policies.

Perusahaan Terbuka memiliki kebijakan anti korupsi dan anti fraud. | The recommendation has been implemented.

The Company has an Anti-Bribery & Corruption Policy Number P01/R03 and has published a Code of Business Ethics and Conduct Book (Towards Good Corporate Governance) which has also been socialized to all employees of the Company.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.

Perseroan telah memiliki Kebijakan Anti-Bribery & Corruption Nomor P01/R03 serta menerbitkan Buku Kode Etik dan Perilaku Bisnis (Menuju Tata Kelola Perusahaan Yang Baik) yang juga telah disosialisasikan kepada seluruh karyawan Perseroan.  |
|  20. | Public Company shall have policies on vendor selection and capability improvement.

Perusahaan Terbuka memiliki kebijakan tentang seleksi dan peningkatan kemampuan pemasok atau vendor. | The recommendation has been implemented.

The Company already has a Procurement Policy Number P23/R03 and Standard Operating Procedures regarding the Procurement of Goods and Services Number SOP 7-04-01/R05, which also regulates the availability of vendor qualification questionnaires, vendor qualification audits, and also the obligations for vendors to sign the Statement of Compliance and fill out the due diligence questionnaire.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.

Perseroan telah memiliki Kebijakan Procurement Nomor P23/R03 serta Standar Operasional Prosedur mengenai Pengadaan Barang dan Jasa Nomor SOP 7-04-01/R05, termasuk juga di dalamnya mengatur mengenai adanya kuesioner kualifikasi vendor, audit kualifikasi vendor, dan juga adanya kewajiban bagi para vendor untuk menandatangani Pernyataan Kepatuhan dan mengisi kuesioner uji kelayakan (due diligence).  |
|  21. | Public Company shall have policies on the fulfillment of creditors' rights.

Perusahaan Terbuka memiliki kebijakan tentang pemenuhan hak-hak kreditur. | The recommendation has been implemented.

The Company already has a Policy on Fulfillment of Creditor Rights. The policy also regulates policy principles, including the Company's obligations in terms of implementing cooperation plans and/or agreements with creditors.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.

Perseroan telah memiliki Kebijakan tentang Pemenuhan Hak-Hak Kreditur. Dalam kebijakan tersebut juga mengatur mengenai prinsip-prinsip kebijakan, termasuk juga kewajiban Perseroan dalam hal pelaksanaan rencana kerjasama dan/atau kesepakatan dengan kreditur.  |
