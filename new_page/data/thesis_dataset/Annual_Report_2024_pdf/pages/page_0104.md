## Upah Minimum Regional

SGH Group is also highly committed to complying with Indonesian Government regulations, especially those governing Regional Minimum Wages (UMR) and Provincial Minimum Wages (UMP). The Company ensures that no employee receives compensation below the UMR/UMP that has been determined, including following increases in the UMR/UMP from year to year in accordance with the Regulations of the Governors of each Province in Indonesia.
