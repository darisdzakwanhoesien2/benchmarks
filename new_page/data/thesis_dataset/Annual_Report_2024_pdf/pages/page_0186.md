# Meetings of the Board of Directors

## Rapat Direksi

The Board of Directors has held internal meetings and joint meetings which involve the Board of Commissioners. The meetings of the Board of Directors are a forum and mechanism for decision-making by the Board of Directors collectively.

Based on the Company's Articles of Association, the Board of Directors of the Company will hold regular internal meetings at least 1 (one) time each month but may hold other meetings at any time if deemed necessary. Meanwhile, joint meetings between the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners are held regularly at least 1 (one) time every 4 (four) months.

Berdasarkan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan, Direksi Perseroan akan mengadakan rapat internal secara berkala paling kurang 1 (satu) kali setiap bulan, namun dapat mengadakan rapat lainnya setiap saat apabila dipandang perlu.
