Follow-up
Tindak Lanjut

Yogyakarta High Court in its Decision No. 71/PDT/2022/PT YYK dated September 22, 2022 which has acquired permanent legal force, ruled, among other things: (i) to order Mrs. Henny to fully refund to PPG the warehouse lease fee of IDR 1,175,000,000 (one billion one hundred seventy five million Rupiah); and (ii) affirm the validity and enforceability of the security confiscation of the Villa ("High Court Decision"). Based on this ruling, PPG has initiated an auction for the Villa. However, Mrs. Henny Puspawati filed a judicial review of the High Court Decision, which was subsequently rejected by the Supreme Court. PPG is currently awaiting the auction results for the Villa.

Putusan Pengadilan Tinggi Yogyakarta No. 71/PDT/2022/PT YYK tanggal 22 September 2022 yang telah berkekuatan hukum tetap, telah memutuskan antara lain, untuk memerintahkan Ibu Henny Puspawati untuk membayar pengembalian biaya uang sewa sebesar Rp 1,175.000.000 (satu milyar seratus tujuh puluh lima juta Rupiah) kepada PPG dan menyatakan sah serta berharga sita jaminan terhadap Vila ("Putusan PT"). Atas putusan tersebut, PPG telah mengajukan lelang atas Vila tersebut. Namun, Ibu Henny Puspawati mengajukan peninjauan kembali atas Putusan PT, namun peninjauan kembali tersebut telah ditolak oleh Mahkamah Agung. Hingga saat ini, PPG masih menunggu hasil lelang atas Vila tersebut.
