Currently, Permata Bank is working to reduce emissions by establishing a baseline in 2024, supported by emission reduction programs targeting both internal operations and debtor activities. To this end, the Bank has set short, medium, and long term targets for energy and emission reductions.

# INISIATIF PENGURANGAN KONSUMSI ENERGI DAN EMISI GRK YANG DIHASILKAN

Initiatives to Reduce Energy Consumption and GHG Emissions Generated

![img-53.jpeg](img-53.jpeg)

Permata Bank pun berupaya meminimalisir penggunaan energi listrik melalui berbagai upaya sehingga dapat mengurangi emisi yang dihasilkan. Pada tahun 2024, Bank berhasil mengurangi penggunaan energi listrik sebesar 2.715,16 GJ dan emisi cakupan 2 yang dihasilkan sebesar 678,13 Ton CO₂eq. Upaya yang dilakukan, antara lain:

- Instalasi 114 panel surya di kantin Kantor Bintaro dengan luas 205,2 m² yang menghasilkan energi listrik sebesar 4.306,32 kWh/bulan atau ± 30% dari total konsumsi listrik gedung kantin Kantor Bintaro. Melalui penggunaan panel surya, pada tahun 2024, Bank berhasil mengurangi penggunaan energi listrik sebesar 215,08 GJ;
- Menggunakan alat-alat elektronik yang hemat listrik;
- Melanjutkan program penggantian lampu konvensional menjadi lampu LED di Kantor Bintaro sebanyak ±5.900 lampu dan di Kantor Hayam Wuruk sebanyak ±1.700 lampu;
- Melakukan pengaturan jam lembur di kantor;
- Mematikan alat-alat elektronik yang tidak terpakai;
- Mematikan AC secara otomatis di Kantor Pusat mulai pukul 17:30 WIB;
- Mematikan lampu secara otomatis mulai pukul 19:00 WIB;
- Digitalisasi sistem perbankan melalui internet banking dan mobile banking, sehingga mengurangi kebutuhan kunjungan fisik nasabah ke Bank;
- Kampanye penghematan energi dan Sustainability Journey melalui media komunikasi internal ke seluruh PermataBankers;
- Himbauan bagi PermataBankers untuk berpartisipasi dalam program Earth Hour pada tanggal 21-27 Maret 2024 untuk mematikan lampu sign wall dan sign pole selama +/- 1 jam (dari pukul 20.30-21.30 waktu setempat); dan
- Mendukung program pemerintah untuk pembiayaan proyek hijau dan pinjaman ramah lingkungan.

Permata Bank is also striving to minimize electricity consumption through various efforts to reduce emissions. In 2024, the Bank succeeded in reducing electricity usage by 2,715.16 GJ and scope 2 emissions generated by 678.13 Tons CO₂eq. These initiatives include:

- Installing 114 solar panels in the cafeteria of the Bintaro Office, covering an area of 205.2 m² generating 4,306.32 kWh/month, equivalent to approximately 30% of total electricity consumption of the Bintaro Office canteen building. Through the use of solar panels, by 2024, the Bank managed to reduce the use of electric energy by 215.08 GJ;
- Using energy-efficient electronic devices;
- Continuing the program to replace conventional lights with LED lights, at the Bintaro Office there are ±5,900 lights and at the Hayam Wuruk Office there are ±1,700 lights;
- Regulating office overtime hours;
- Turning off unused electronic devices;
- Automatically turning off air conditioning at the Headquarters starting at 5:30 PM WIB;
- Automatically turning off lights starting at 7:00 PM WIB;
- Digitizing banking systems through inter-net banking and mobile banking to reduce the need for physical customer visits to the Bank;
- Running energy-saving campaigns and Sustainability Journey initiatives through internal communication channels for all PermataBankers;
- Permata Bank invited all PermataBankers to participate in Earth Hour from 21-27 March 2024 to switch off sign wall and sign pole lights for approximately one hour (8:30 PM-9:30 PM local time); and
- Supporting government programs for green projects and environmentally friendly loans.
