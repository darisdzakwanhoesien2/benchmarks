**Dasar Hukum Pendirian**
**Legal Basis of Establishment**

Akta Pendirian No. 228 tanggal 17 Desember 1954, dibuat di hadapan Eliza Pondaag, Notaris pengganti dari Raden Mas Soerojo. Akta tersebut telah disetujui oleh Menteri Kehakiman Republik Indonesia dengan suratnya No. J.A. 5/2/2 tanggal 4 Januari 1955 dan diumumkan dalam BNRI No. 22 tanggal 18 Maret 1955, TBNRI No. 292.
The establishment of Permata Bank is legally grounded in the Deed of Establishment No. 228, dated 17 December 1954, notarized by Eliza Pondaag, a substitute notary for Raden Mas Soerojo. The Minister of Justice of the Republic of Indonesia legalized it as a legal entity through his decree No. J.A. 5/2/2, dated 4 January 1955. The ministerial decree was subsequently promulgated in the State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia No. 22, dated 18 March 1955, and the Supplementary State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia No. 292.
