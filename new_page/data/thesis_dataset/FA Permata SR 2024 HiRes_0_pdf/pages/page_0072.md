Work-life Balance
Permata Bank juga berupaya mengimplementasikan work-life balance bagi seluruh karyawannya melalui program olahraga Permata Olympics. Melalui program ini, Bank mendukung PermataBankers untuk dapat meningkatkan efektivitas dan produktivitas dalam bekerja, serta menerapkan gaya hidup sehat dan seimbang, baik di lingkungan kerja maupun kehidupan pribadi. Pada tahun 2024, sebanyak 1.756 PermataBankers ikut serta dalam program Permata Olympics.

Promosi Kesehatan [403-7]
Permata Bank memberikan Flexible Benefit bagi PermataBankers untuk memilih dan memperoleh benefit kesehatan secara fleksibel sesuai dengan kebutuhan masing-masing. Sebanyak 1.485 karyawan dengan total 4.438 peserta asuransi telah menyesuaikan manfaat kesehatannya. Bank juga berupaya untuk meningkatkan akses kesehatan karyawan melalui peningkatan limit asuransi serta pengaplikasian Telehealth untuk konsultasi kesehatan.

Kami juga memberikan berbagai fasilitas kesehatan, antara lain tas dan obat-obatan P3K di seluruh kantor cabang, serta layanan dokter melalui klinik, alat P3K (seperti tandu, tabung oksigen, dan kursi roda), dan ruang menyusui yang tersedia di tiga Kantor Cabang Utama (Hayam Wuruk, WTC, dan Bintaro). Kami juga memberikan fasilitas Medical Check-Up (MCU) bagi karyawan tetap dengan masa kerja minimal 1 tahun. Pada tahun 2024, terdapat 6.124 karyawan yang menerima fasilitas MCU.

2. Healthy Canteen Program
This program included hygiene and healthy food sanitation workshops for food vendors at the canteens in the Bintaro (South Tangerang) and Hayam Wuruk Office (Jakarta). Each location hosted two sessions throughout 2024.

Work-life Balance
Permata Bank also strives to implement work-life balance for all its employees through the Permata Olympics sports program. This program supports PermataBankers in enhancing work effectiveness and productivity while adopting a healthy and balanced lifestyle both at work and in their personal lives. In 2024, a total of 1,756 PermataBankers participated in the Permata Olympics program.

Health Promotion [403-7]
Permata Bank provides a Flexible Benefit program, enabling PermataBankers to select and customize health benefits according to their individual needs. In 2024, 1,485 employees, encompassing a total of 4,438 insurance participants, adjusted their health benefits. The Bank also enhanced healthcare access for employees by increasing insurance limits and implementing Telehealth services for health consultations.

Permata Bank also offers comprehensive health facilities, including first-aid kits and supplies at all branch offices, as well as doctor services through clinics, first-aid equipment (such as stretchers, oxygen tanks, and wheelchairs), and nursing rooms located at three main branch offices (Hayam Wuruk, WTC, and Bintaro). Permanent employees with at least one year of service are also entitled to Medical Check-Up (MCU) facilities. In 2024, a total of 6,124 employees utilized this benefit.
