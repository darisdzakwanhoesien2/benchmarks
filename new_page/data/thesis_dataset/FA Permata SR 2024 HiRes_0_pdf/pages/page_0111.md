Di tahun 2024, Permata Bank juga dikenai sanksi denda oleh regulator akibat pelanggaran dalam pelaporan. Permata Bank segera menindaklanjuti dan kemudian menganalisis akar penyebab (root cause) kesalahan tersebut. Berdasarkan hasil analisis, Bank akan menyusun langkah mitigasi dan rencana perbaikan untuk menghindari potensi kesalahan pelaporan berulang. Pada periode pelaporan, terdapat 15 kasus yang mengakibatkan Bank dikenai sanksi finansial.

## Antikorupsi dan Anti-Fraud[205-1]

Divisi Bribery &amp; Anti-Corruption merupakan pihak yang bertanggung jawab untuk menangani penyuapan dan korupsi melalui kerja sama dengan Divisi Fraud Risk Management untuk mendeteksi dan investigasi risiko penyuapan dan korupsi. Selain itu, Divisi Bribery &amp; Anti-Corruption juga akan melibatkan Divisi Litigasi dan Divisi Compliance Monitoring &amp; Training apabila terdapat indikasi yang memerlukan tindakan hukum lanjutan atau pelaporan melalui Speak-Up.

Permata Bank telah mengimplementasikan kebijakan dan prosedur internal yang telah disetujui oleh Direksi, yang melarang praktik korupsi dan fraud antara lain Kebijakan Anti Penyuapan, Kebijakan Strategi Anti-Fraud, Kode Etik Perilaku Karyawan, Kebijakan Benturan Kepentingan, serta Kebijakan Pemberian Hadiah dan Jamuan. Bank juga telah memperoleh sertifikasi ISO 37001:2016 Sistem Manajemen Anti Penyuapan (SMAP) yang menunjukkan komitmen Bank dalam penerapan Antikorupsi dan Anti-Fraud. Penerapan kebijakan tersebut merupakan bagian dari kerangka kerja manajemen risiko dan strategi untuk mencegah, mendeteksi, menginvestigasi, dan menindaklanjuti setiap insiden yang terjadi.

In 2024, Permata Bank was also subjected to fines by regulators due to reporting violations. The Bank promptly addressed the issue and conducted a root cause analysis to identify the underlying errors. Based on the analysis results, the Bank will develop mitigation measures and corrective plans to prevent the recurrence of similar reporting errors. During the reporting period, there were 15 cases that resulted in financial penalties for the Bank.

## Anti-Corruption and Anti-Fraud [205-1]

The Bribery &amp; Anti-Corruption Division is responsible for handling bribery and corruption issues in collaboration with the Fraud Risk Management Division to detect and investigate bribery and corruption risks. Additionally, the Bribery &amp; Anti-Corruption Division will engage the Litigation Division and the Compliance Monitoring &amp; Training Division if there are indications requiring further legal action or reporting through the Speak-Up channel.

Permata Bank has implemented internal policies and procedures approved by the Board of Directors, prohibiting corruption and fraud practices. These include the Anti-Bribery Policy, Anti-Fraud Strategy Policy, Employee Code of Ethics, Conflict of Interest Policy, and Gifts and Hospitality Policy. The Bank has also obtained the ISO 37001:2016 Anti-Bribery Management System (SMAP) certification, demonstrating its commitment to Anti-Corruption and Anti-Fraud practices. The implementation of these policies is part of the Bank's risk management framework and strategy to prevent, detect, investigate, and address any incidents that may arise.
