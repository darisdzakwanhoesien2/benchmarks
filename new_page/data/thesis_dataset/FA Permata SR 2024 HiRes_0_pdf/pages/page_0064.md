## Survei Keterikatan Karyawan

Untuk mengevaluasi efektivitas strategi pengelolaan PermataBankers, Permata Bank secara rutin melakukan kajian tingkat keterikatan melalui survei tahunan, yaitu Change for Better Survey, yang diselenggarakan oleh konsultan global eksternal. Pada tahun 2023, hasil survei keterikatan pegawai mencapai 85,0%, meningkat dibandingkan tahun 2022 yang sebesar 82,0%. Namun demikian, pada tahun 2024, survei keterikatan pegawai tidak dilakukan karena Bank sedang dalam proses perancangan ulang alat ukur untuk meningkatkan kemampuan pengukuran tingkat engagement. Adapun survei dengan format baru akan dilaksanakan pada tahun 2025.

## Pengaduan Masalah Ketenagakerjaan [2-16][2-26]

Permata Bank berupaya untuk membangun kenyamanan bekerja, salah satunya tercermin melalui prosedur Penanganan Keluh Kesah yang bertujuan untuk memastikan permasalahan karyawan ditangani secara langsung, adil, dan objektif. PermataBankers dapat menyampaikan keluh kesahnya terkait masalah ketenagakerjaan melalui mekanisme pengaduan secara informal melalui diskusi langsung dengan atasan. Apabila keluhan yang disampaikan tidak dapat diselesaikan dengan atasan secara langsung, karyawan dapat meneruskan kepada Direktorat Human Resources dan/atau melalui SPBP untuk disampaikan kepada Direktorat Human Resources. Selama proses penyelesaian berjalan, SPBP dapat memberikan advokasi sesuai dengan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku. ## Employee Engagement Survey

Permata Bank regularly evaluates the effectiveness of its PermataBankers management strategies by assessing employee engagement levels through the Change for Better Survey, an annual survey conducted by an external global consultant. In 2023, the survey results showed an 85.0% engagement level, marking an improvement from 82.0% in 2022. However, in 2024, the employee engagement survey was not conducted as the Bank was in the process of redesigning the measurement tool to enhance engagement assessment capabilities. The newly formatted survey is scheduled for implementation in 2025.

## Labor-Related Grievances [2-16][2-26]

Permata Bank is committed to fostering a comfortable working environment, as reflected in its Grievance Handling Procedure, designed to ensure that employee issues are addressed promptly, fairly, and objectively. PermataBankers can raise employment-related concerns through an informal complaint mechanism, such as direct discussions with their supervisor. If the issue cannot be resolved at this level, employees can escalate their grievances to the Human Resources Directorate and/or seek assistance from SPBP to present the matter to the Human Resources Directorate. During the resolution process, the Workers Union may provide advocacy in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. The protocol for PermataBankers to report concern is through the Whistleblowing System (WBS), accessible via: [FN-C8-510a.2]
