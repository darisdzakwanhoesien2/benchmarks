2024 Sustainability Report PT Bank Permata Tbk

# 2014

Penyertaan saham pada PT Astra Sedaya Finance sebesar 25%.

Marked the Bank's 25% equity participation in PT Astra Sedaya Finance.

# 2018

- Divestasi saham di PT Astra Sedaya Finance.
- Peluncuran PermataMobile X.
- Peluncuran Visi, Misi dan nilai-nilai I-PRICE.
- Divestment of shares in PT Astra Sedaya Finance.
- Launched PertamaMobile X.
- Introduced the Vision, Mission, and values of I-PRICE.

# 2020

- Melalui proses akuisisi, Bangkok Bank Public Company Limited (Bangkok Bank) resmi menjadi pemegang saham pengendali Permata Bank setelah mengambil alih 89,12% saham Permata Bank dari seluruh jumlah saham yang telah ditempatkan dan disetor oleh Standard Chartered Bank dan PT Astra International Tbk.
- Sesuai dengan arahan dari Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK), pada 21 Desember 2020, Bangkok Bank mengintegrasikan Bangkok Bank Public Company Limited cabang Jakarta, cabang pembantu Medan, dan cabang pembantu Surabaya (Bangkok Bank Indonesia atau BBI) ke Permata Bank di mana Permata Bank bertindak sebagai bank penerima integrasi.

- Through an acquisition process, Bangkok Bank Public Company Limited (Bangkok Bank) officially became the controlling shareholder of Permata Bank by acquiring 89.12% of Permata Bank's issued and paid-up shares from Standard Chartered Bank and PT Astra International Tbk.
- In accordance with directives from the Financial Services Authority (OJK), on 21 December 2020, Bangkok Bank integrated Bangkok Bank Public Company Limited branches in Jakarta, Medan (sub-branch), and Surabaya (sub-branch) (Bangkok Bank Indonesia or BBI) into Permata Bank, with Permata Bank acting as the receiving bank for the integration.

# 2019

Peluncuran Model Branch yang dirancang untuk memberikan pengalaman terbaik bagi nasabah baik secara luring (dengan bantuan karyawan Permata Bank) maupun secara daring (self service).

Launched the Model Branch, designed to provide the best customer experience both offline (with assistance from Permata Bank employees) and online (self-service).

# 2023

- Permata Bank memperoleh sertifikat ISO 37001:2016 Sistem Manajemen Anti-Penyuapan.
- Permata Bank meluncurkan Permata Bank Institute for Economic Research (PIER). PIER menyediakan analisis dan riset terhadap perkembangan tren pasar terkini serta isu-isu ekonomi di Indonesia dan global.
- Permata Bank attained the ISO 37001:2016 Anti-Bribery Management System certification.
- Permata Bank established the Permata Bank Institute for Economic Research (PIER), tasked with analyzing and researching contemporary market trends and economic issues, both within Indonesia and on a global scale.

# 2024

- Perubahan logo Permata Bank untuk memperkuat identitas Permata Bank dan menyelaraskan dengan identitas Bangkok Bank sebagai Pemegang Saham Utama dengan mengusung semangat untuk tumbuh bersama atau Growing Together.
- Permata Bank melakukan transformasi aplikasi mobile banking PermataMobile X menjadi Permata ME.
- Changed Permata Bank's logo to strengthen its identity and align with Bangkok Bank's identity as the majority shareholder, embodying the spirit of Growing Together.
- Permata Bank transformed the PermataMobile X mobile banking application into Permata ME.
