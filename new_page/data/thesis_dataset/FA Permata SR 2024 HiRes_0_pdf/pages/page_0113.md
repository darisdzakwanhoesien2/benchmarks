The Bank ensures that reporting does not impact the performance evaluation and/or career progression of the whistleblower. Reports are only accessible to authorized parties, namely the Speak-Up Administrator, Speak-Up PIC, and the Speak-Up Council. The Speak-Up Administrator records each report in a register and categorizes it based on suspected fraud, code of conduct violations, or other general reports such as grievances or suggestions.

Every three months, Speak-Up PICs present the results of case investigations to the Speak-Up Council during the Speak-Up Council meeting. In these meetings, the Speak-Up Council provides guidance on the effectiveness of investigations and the potential expansion of investigation areas. Additionally, a summary of Speak-Up investigation results is presented in the Board of Directors Meeting every six months.

# Permasalahan terhadap Penerapan Keuangan Berkelanjutan [E 5]

## Issues in Sustainable Finance Implementation

Sejak penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan di tahun 2019, Permata Bank menghadapi berbagai tantangan akibat perkembangan peraturan dan persyaratan, serta perubahan kondisi ekonomi, sosial, dan lingkungan. Pada tahun 2024, tantangan yang harus dihadapi oleh Bank, di antaranya:

- Peningkatan jumlah transaksi digital dapat meningkatkan risiko keamanan data (information security) Bank dan nasabah;
- Pemahaman terhadap pengertian dan implementasi prinsip keuangan berkelanjutan maupun LST, baik untuk karyawan ataupun nasabah masih belum merata;
- Pemahaman mengenai dokumen-dokumen lingkungan terkait persyaratan pemberian kredit serta pemahaman mengenai Risiko Iklim yang perlu ditingkatkan secara internal; dan

- The increasing volume of digital transactions, which heightens the risk of data security (information security) for the Bank and its customers;
