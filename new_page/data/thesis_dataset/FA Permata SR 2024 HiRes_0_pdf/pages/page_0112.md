# Pelatihan Antikorupsi dan Anti-fraud (205-2)

Permata Bank menyadari bahwa korupsi dan fraud berpotensi menyebabkan kerugian finansial bagi Bank dan nasabah, baik secara langsung maupun tidak langsung. Permata Bank memiliki unit kerja Fraud Risk Management yang bertugas menangani penerapan Strategi Anti-Fraud di Bank, termasuk di dalamnya terkait pilar pencegahan dengan melakukan penyusunan dan sosialisasi Anti-Fraud kepada unit kerja dan cabang yang ada di Permata Bank. Selain itu, FRM juga melakukan kajian berkelanjutan sebagai bentuk Fraud Risk Assessment (FRA). Melalui FRA, Permata Bank mengevaluasi pencegahan, pendeteksian dan tanggapan terhadap setiap potensi skema dan kejadian fraud terhadap unit/produk/proses/aktivitas yang memiliki risiko fraud yang cukup tinggi, baik yang terpantau dari kenaikan insiden fraud maupun penambahan volume yang signifikan. Kegiatan budaya berintegritas dan hasil kajian FRA akan dilaporkan kepada Direktur Kepatuhan melalui Laporan Profil Risiko dan Laporan Semesteran ke OJK.

Permata Bank telah memberikan sosialisasi terkait kebijakan Antikorupsi, Anti-Fraud, dan kode etik kepada Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, dan seluruh karyawan, termasuk karyawan baru, karyawan tetap, karyawan tidak tetap/pekerjaan alih daya, serta pihak ketiga (vendor) yang bekerja sama dengan Permata Bank. Sosialisasi kepada internal Permata Bank dilakukan melalui refreshment kode etik, kesadaran Anti-Fraud, program roadshow, e-mail blast, desktop wallpaper, serta melalui kewajiban untuk penyelesaian e-learning terkait Sistem Manajemen Anti Penyuapan (SMAP), Anti-Fraud awareness, dan kode etik. Sebagai salah satu upaya Anti-Fraud dalam mencegah tindakan korupsi, Bank juga telah melakukan sosialisasi Antikorupsi secara offline maupun online kepada pihak ketiga yang memiliki kerja sama dengan Permata Bank. Sosialisasi dihadiri oleh 187 vendor yang menandai bahwa para vendor tersebut sudah terinfo mengenai kebijakan Antikorupsi dan Anti-Fraud yang tertuang dalam klausul terkait anti-penyuapan. # Anti-Corruption and Anti-Fraud Training (205-2)

Permata Bank recognizes that corruption and fraud have the potential to cause direct and indirect financial losses to the Bank and its customers. To address this, Permata Bank has a Fraud Risk Management unit responsible for implementing the Anti-Fraud Strategy at the Bank, which includes the prevention pillar by preparing and socializing Anti-Fraud measures to the work units and branches within Permata Bank. In addition, FRM conducts ongoing evaluations as part of its Fraud Risk Assessment (FRA). Through the FRA, Permata Bank evaluates the prevention, detection, and response to potential fraud schemes and incidents within units/products/processes/activities with high fraud risks, including those observed through increased fraud incidents or significant volume growth. The results of the FRA and integrity culture initiatives are reported to the Compliance Director via the Risk Profile Report and semi-annual reports to the Financial Services Authority (OJK).

Permata Bank has conducted socialization programs on Anti-Corruption, Anti-Fraud, and code of ethics policies for the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, and all employees, including new hires, permanent employees, temporary employees/contract workers, and third parties (vendors) collaborating with the Bank. Internal socialization at Permata Bank is conducted through various initiatives, including code of conduct refreshers, Anti-Fraud awareness programs, roadshows, e-mail blasts, desktop wallpapers, and mandatory completion of e-learning modules on the Anti-Bribery Management System (SMAP), Anti-Fraud awareness, and the code of conduct. As part of its Anti-Fraud efforts to prevent corruption, the Bank has also conducted Anti-Corruption socialization sessions, both offline and online, for third parties collaborating with Permata Bank. These sessions were attended by 187 vendors, ensuring they were informed about the Bank's Anti-Corruption and Anti-Fraud policies, which are outlined in anti-bribery clauses included in agreements with the vendors.
