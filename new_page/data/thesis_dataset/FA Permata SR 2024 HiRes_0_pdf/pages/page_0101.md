# Tata Kelola Keberlanjutan [2-9][2-12][2-13]

## Sustainability Governance

Sehubungan dengan penerapan POJK No. 17 Tahun 2023 tentang Penerapan Tata Kelola bagi Bank Umum, Permata Bank melakukan beberapa penyesuaian kebijakan dan prosedur sebagai wujud kepatuhan dan juga upaya memperkuat tata kelola yang mengedepankan prinsip keterbukaan, akuntabilitas, tanggung jawab, independensi, dan kewajaran, dengan mengintegrasikan aspek keberlanjutan. Selain POJK No. 17/2023, Permata Bank juga melaksanakan tata kelola dengan mengadopsi standar internasional ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS) 2023, best practice, serta peraturan lainnya yang berlaku.

Setiap tahunnya, Bank telah melaksanakan self-assessment untuk memastikan kepatuhan Bank terhadap regulasi yang berlaku serta penerapan Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik (GCG). Hasil rekomendasi self-assessment telah diterapkan oleh Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris. [2-11]

Permata Bank menyadari dari pemberian kredit, secara langsung maupun tidak langsung juga terpapar risiko lingkungan, termasuk kerusakan hutan, hilangnya keanekaragaman hayati, kerusakan lingkungan laut dan sumber air, serta risiko sosial seperti pelanggaran hak asasi manusia dan hak-hak tenaga kerja. Oleh sebab itu, untuk memperkuat fungsi Keberlanjutan di tahun 2024, terdapat penyesuaian struktur organisasi dengan memindahkan Departemen Keberlanjutan ke dalam Divisi Corporate Secretary &amp; Sustainability serta penyesuaian pada Terms of Reference (TOR) dan keanggotaan pada Komite Keberlanjutan.

Komite Keberlanjutan terdiri dari anggota tetap dengan hak suara yaitu Direktur Utama, Direktur Risiko, dan Direktur Keuangan, serta anggota tetap tanpa hak suara yang berasal dari Chief dan Kepala Divisi yang terkait seperti Corporate Banking, Commercial Banking, International Business Development &amp; Advisory, Treasury, Corporate Secretary &amp; Sustainability, Corporate Communication, dan Consumer Lending. Komite Keberlanjutan dapat mengundang Kepala Divisi Permata Bank lainnya berdasarkan topik yang akan didiskusikan di rapat.

Komite Keberlanjutan bertanggung jawab untuk mengawasi dan mempromosikan inisiatif keberlanjutan di Permata Bank; mengintegrasikan aspek LST ke dalam strategi, kebijakan, dan praktik bisnis Bank; serta memastikan kepatuhan Bank terhadap peraturan yang berlaku, kesesuaian standar industri dan praktik umum terkait keberlanjutan di perbankan.

In line with the implementation of Financial Services Authority Regulation (POJK) No. 17 of 2023 concerning the Governance Implementation for Commercial Banks, Permata Bank has undertaken several improvements on policies and procedures as a form of compliance and an effort to improve the principles of transparency, accountability, responsibility, independency, and fairness while at the same time integrating sustainability aspects. In addition to POJK No. 17/2023, Permata Bank has adopted the 2023 ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS) international standards, best practices, and other applicable regulations.

Each year, the Bank conducts a self-assessment to ensure compliance with applicable regulations and the implementation of Good Corporate Governance (GCG). The recommendations from the self-assessment have been implemented by the Board of Directors along with the Board of Commissioners. [2-11]

Permata Bank is aware that by providing credit, it is directly or indirectly exposed to environmental risks, including forest destruction, loss of biodiversity, damage to the marine environment and water sources, as well as social risks such as violations of human rights and labor rights. To strengthen the Sustainability function in 2024, adjustments were made to the organizational structure, including relocating the Sustainability Department into the Corporate Secretary &amp; Sustainability Division and revising the Terms of Reference (TOR) as well as membership of the Sustainability Committee.

The Sustainability Committee comprises permanent members with voting rights: the President Director, the Risk Director, and the Finance Director. Additionally, the Committee has non-voting permanent members from Chiefs and Division Heads of relevant divisions such as Corporate Banking, Commercial Banking, International Business Development &amp; Advisory, Treasury, Corporate Secretary &amp; Sustainability, Corporate Communication, and Consumer Lending. The Sustainability Committee may also invite other Division Heads of Permata Bank to attend meetings based on the topics to be discussed.

The Sustainability Committee is responsible for overseeing and promoting Permata Bank's sustainability initiatives; integrating ESG aspects thoroughly into the Bank's strategies, policies, and business practices; and ensuring the Bank's compliance with relevant regulations, industry standards, and general practices related to sustainability in the banking sector.
