In 2024, Permata Bank received 3,425 customer complaints, all of which (100,00%) were resolved. Additionally, the Bank did not receive any complaints related to environmental or social issues from the public. [F.16]

## Survei Kepuasan Pelanggan [F.30]
Permata Bank melakukan survei kepuasan nasabah yang dilakukan oleh pihak ketiga dengan menggunakan metode Net Promoter Score (NPS). Dengan demikian, Bank dapat mengetahui tingkat kepuasan nasabah terhadap produk dan layanan yang ditawarkan.

## Customer Satisfaction Survey [F.30]
Permata Bank conducts customer satisfaction surveys through a third party using the Net Promoter Score (NPS) method. This approach enables the Bank to gauge customer satisfaction with its products and services.
