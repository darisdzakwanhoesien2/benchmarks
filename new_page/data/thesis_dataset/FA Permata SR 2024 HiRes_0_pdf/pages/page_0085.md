Pada tahun 2024, Permata Hati mengadakan dan mendukung program pengayaan kompetensi guru dalam memberikan edukasi kepada murid. Dukungan Permata Hati dilakukan dalam dua kegiatan, yaitu:

### a) Pra-program Permata Bankir Cilik

Sebelum pelaksanaan program Permata Bankir Cilik, Bank juga memberikan edukasi guna mempersiapkan guru sebagai agen perubahan peningkatan literasi keuangan di sekolah. During the program, participants were encouraged to maintain a financial journal using the simple 3-in money management concept (Save-in, Spend-in, and Share-in), while also learning to save consistently in their daily lives. Participants were introduced to money and banking in Indonesia through a virtual tour of the Bank Indonesia Museum and an in-person visit to Permata Bank branch offices. These visits aimed to provide insights into banking functions and activities at branch offices, such as the role of ATMs, Customer Service, and Tellers.

In collaboration with PiBo—a children's literacy platform and pioneer of Indonesia's digital library for children—Permata Hati designed a comprehensive and interactive curriculum. This curriculum focuses on enhancing financial literacy, reading and writing skills, digital literacy, communication abilities, and environmental awareness. Students are also encouraged to develop modern skills, including creativity, communication, critical thinking, and collaboration.

The Permata Bankir Cilik 2024 program was attended by 170 students from 15 public elementary schools and 2 special-needs schools across West Java, Central Java, DI Yogyakarta, East Java, and North Sumatra. Conducted online, each school sent 8-12 student representatives from grades 4 and 5, accompanied by one teacher. All participants, both students and teachers, received a Starter Kit containing worksheets, informational guides, uniforms, and a subscription to PiBo's digital library for use throughout the program.
