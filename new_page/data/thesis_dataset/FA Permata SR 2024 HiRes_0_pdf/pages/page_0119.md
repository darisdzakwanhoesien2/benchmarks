# Adherence to AA1000AP (2018) and GRI Universal Standards

**Inclusivity** – The Bank has implemented inclusivity principles through structured engagement mechanisms, such as annual general meetings, public disclosures, employee town halls, vendor socialization sessions, and regulatory compliance reporting. The Bank’s stakeholder engagement activities are implemented by taking into account the diverse perspectives of customers, employees, regulators, vendors, and local communities. The Bank has also integrated inclusivity by conducting employee well-being programs, financial inclusion initiatives, sustainable business training for vendors, and anti-corruption compliance sessions. Through continuous dialogue via digital platforms and social medias, the Bank ensures that stakeholder feedback is acknowledged.

**Materiality** – The Bank has implemented a structured approach to identifying and prioritizing material topics for its sustainability reporting, which includes a Focus Group Discussion (FGD) and input from key governance bodies, ensuring that material topics align with the Bank’s performance results and stakeholder expectations. Material topics were identified based on insights from Board of Directors interviews and considerations. However, involving external stakeholders, such as customers and investors, in the materiality assessment process would provide a broader perspective and enhance the relevance of selected topics.

**Responsiveness** – The Bank has established mechanisms for addressing stakeholder concerns, including grievance procedures for employees and customer complaint resolution systems. The Bank manages employee grievances through discussions with supervisors and formal channels such as the Whistleblowing System (WBS) to ensure transparency and fairness. For customer complaints, the Bank follows a Service Level Agreement (SLA) to resolve issues in a timely manner and has integrated digital platforms for efficient complaint handling. While the Bank has demonstrated a commitment to addressing stakeholder concerns, improvements could be made by disclosing the progress made in response to customer complaints to show how concerns are addressed.

**Impact** – The Bank has realized its commitment to sustainability through various initiatives that generate impacts. The Bank conducted financial literacy programs in its effort to enhance financial management skills among students and underserved communities, supporting broader economic empowerment. The Bank has a zero data privacy breach as a result of its data security governance and compliance with personal data protection regulations. In environmental aspect, the Bank has increased its green financing. In social aspects, the Bank has conducted various initiatives, including entrepreneurship training for persons with disabilities, empowerment for small and medium enterprises, and conservation projects, and contribution to social and environmental benefits.
