# Pengembangan Kompetensi terkait Keuangan Berkelanjutan [E.2][2-17]

Permata Bank telah menetapkan Key Performance Indicator (KPI) Keberlanjutan bagi Divisi Corporate Secretary &amp; Sustainability yang harus dipenuhi setiap tahunnya. Untuk mencapai KPI tersebut, sepanjang tahun 2024, Permata Bank mengikutsertakan Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, dan seluruh karyawan dalam pengembangan kompetensi dan pelatihan terkait keberlanjutan yang seluruhnya diselenggarakan oleh Permata Bank, dengan tujuan untuk meningkatkan pemahaman dan kompetensi terhadap praktik keberlanjutan di Permata Bank.

# Sustainable Finance Training and Development [E.2][2-17]

Permata Bank has established sustainability KPIs for the Corporate Secretary &amp; Sustainability Division, which will be assessed annually. As part to achieve the KPI, throughout 2024 Permata Bank enrolled its Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, and all employees to training and development programs on sustainability topics arranged by Permata Bank itself. These training and development programs are aimed to enhance comprehensive understanding and competency regarding sustainability practice within Permata Bank.
