## 2. Penyaluran Pembiayaan Inklusif

Permata Bank telah mengambil peran aktif dalam mendorong inklusi keuangan di Indonesia melalui penyaluran pembiayaan inklusif kepada UMKM dan Perorangan Berpenghasilan Rendah (PBR). Pembiayaan inklusif menjadi salah satu instrumen yang efektif dalam memberikan akses keuangan yang lebih luas bagi masyarakat, mendorong pertumbuhan ekonomi, meningkatkan kesejahteraan masyarakat, serta memperkuat stabilitas perekonomian nasional.

Bank pun telah berhasil memenuhi ketentuan Rasio Pembiayaan Inklusif Makroprudensial (RPIM) yang ditetapkan oleh pemerintah. Hal ini dibuktikan dengan pencapaian RPIM pada akhir tahun 2024 yang berada di atas batas minimum yang dipersyaratkan, yaitu sebesar 11,92% untuk konvensional dan 10,73% untuk syariah.

Sejak tahun 2016, Permata Bank telah berkomitmen terhadap pembiayaan inklusif dengan menyalurkan dana melalui mitra atau loan channeling kepada Peer to Peer (P2P) lending fintech dan Bank Perkreditan Rakyat (BPR) Dana Raya. Kerja sama dengan P2P lending fintech

Permata Bank consistently strives to expand its KKUB portfolio through various initiatives. One of the endeavours involves offering loans for the purchase of eco-friendly properties and financing to green projects. Additionally, the Bank provides financing for low-income individuals to promote financial inclusion. The Bank also prioritizes customer accessibility by digitizing services through the development of applications/platforms. Internally, the Bank enhances understanding and knowledge of sustainable finance through inter-unit collaboration such as the Sustainability Department, Corporate Banking, Commercial Banking, Consumer Banking, Treasury, and Risk.

## 2. Inclusive Financing Distribution

Permata Bank actively promotes financial inclusion in Indonesia by providing inclusive financing to MSMEs and Low-Income Individuals (PBR). Inclusive financing serves as an effective tool to broaden financial access for the community, drive economic growth, enhance societal welfare, and strengthen national economic stability.

The Bank has successfully met the Macroprudential Inclusive Financing Ratio (RPIM) requirements set by the government. As of the end of 2024, RPIM achievements exceeded the minimum thresholds, reaching 11.92% for conventional banking and 10.73% for sharia banking.
