# Produk, Layanan, dan Kegiatan Usaha yang Dijalankan [C.4][2-6]

Products, Services, and Business Activities

Permata Bank melaksanakan kegiatan usahanya di bidang bank umum, mencakup kegiatan usaha utama dan kegiatan usaha pendukung, sebagaimana yang tertuang dalam Pasal 3 Anggaran Dasar Permata Bank sebagaimana yang termaktub dalam Akta No. 94 Tanggal 27 September 2024 dan telah diberitahukan kepada Menteri Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia berdasarkan suratnya No. AHU-AH.01.03-0196980 tanggal 1 Oktober 2024 dan telah diumumkan dalam BNRI No. 5 tanggal 17 Januari 2025, TBNRI No. 1167. Permata Bank menawarkan berbagai layanan perbankan yang terdiri kegiatan usaha utama dan kegiatan usaha penunjang, meliputi segmen Konsumer, Syariah, Komersial, serta Korporasi.

Permata Bank conducts its business activities in the field of commercial banking, encompassing primary and supporting business activities as stipulated in Article 3 of Permata Bank's Articles of Association, as outlined in Deed No. 94 dated 27 September 2024, which has been duly notified to the Minister of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia under letter No. AHU-AH.01.03-0196980 dated 1 October 2024 and was announced in BNRI No. 5 dated 17 January 2025, TBNRI No. 1167. Permata Bank offers a variety of banking services comprising primary and supporting business activities, covering Consumer, Sharia, Commercial, and Corporate segments.

# Produk dan Layanan Permata Bank

## Permata Bank Products and Services

Dalam menjalankan kegiatan usaha dalam layanan perbankan, Permata Bank melakukan penghimpunan dan menyediakan dana masyarakat serta memberikan suku bunga yang bersaing. Beberapa produk Permata Bank antara lain pembiayaan usaha, pemberian modal usaha, tabungan, giro, deposito, kartu kredit, pembiayaan rumah, dan lain sebagainya.

In conducting its banking services, Permata Bank collects and provides public funds while offering competitive interest rates. Some of Permata Bank's products include business financing, working capital loans, savings accounts, current accounts, term deposits, credit cards, home financing, and others.

Informasi lebih lengkap mengenai kegiatan usaha serta produk dan layanan yang ditawarkan oleh Permata Bank dapat dilihat pada Laporan Tahunan Permata Bank 2024 halaman 84-85.

Further detailed information about Permata Bank's business activities, as well as the products and services offered, can be found in the 2024 Permata Bank Annual Report on page 84-85.
