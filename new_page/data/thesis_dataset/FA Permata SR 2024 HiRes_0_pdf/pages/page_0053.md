Sementara untuk pengolahan data emisi GRK cakupan 3, Bank mengalikan data penggunaan taxi online, kereta api, dan pesawat terbang dengan faktor emisi yang ditetapkan oleh OJK. Di samping itu, Bank menggunakan Carbon Emission Calculator dari ICAO untuk menghitung emisi yang dihasilkan dari pesawat terbang.

Perhitungan emisi cakupan 3 belum termasuk perhitungan Financed Emission. Perhitungan terkait dengan Financed Emission hanya dilakukan untuk keperluan Climate Risk Stress Test (CRST) dan hanya untuk tahun 2023. Saat ini, Permata Bank dalam tahap pengembangan metodologi perhitungan Financed Emission yang lebih aplikatif. Sampai akhir periode pelaporan, Bank belum melakukan perhitungan untuk emisi zat perusak ozon maupun emisi non-GRK signifikan lainnya. [305-6][305-7][FN-CB-410b.4]

For scope 3 GHG emissions, the Bank multiplies data on the use of online taxis, trains, and airplanes by emission factors established by OJK. Additionally, the Bank utilizes the Carbon Emission Calculator from ICAO to calculate emissions generated from air travel.

The calculation of scope 3 emissions does not yet include Financed Emissions. Financed Emissions calculations were conducted solely for the Climate Risk Stress Test (CRST) and only for the year 2023. Currently, Permata Bank is in the process of developing a more practical methodology for calculating Financed Emissions. As of the end of the reporting period, the Bank has not conducted calculations for ozone-depleting substances or other significant non-GHG emissions. [305-6][305-7][FN-CB-410b.4]

- The data provided includes the Head Office and all branch offices.
