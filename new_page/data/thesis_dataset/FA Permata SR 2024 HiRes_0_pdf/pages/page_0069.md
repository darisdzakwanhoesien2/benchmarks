# Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3) [F.21]

## Occupational Health &amp; Safety (OHS)

### Sistem Manajemen Kesehatan dan Keselamatan K3 (SMK3) [403-1][403-8]

Permata Bank telah memiliki dan menerapkan Sistem Manajemen Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja (SMK3) yang berlaku untuk seluruh (100%) PermataBankers, sebagaimana tercantum dalam Kebijakan Kesehatan, Keselamatan Kerja dan Lingkungan (K3L). Penerapan SMK3 ini bertujuan untuk menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang layak, aman, dan nyaman bagi PermataBankers, nasabah, mitra, maupun pemangku kepentingan lainnya. Pelaksanaan SMK3 dikelola oleh Departemen Security, Safety &amp; Support Management (SSSM) yang berada di bawah Divisi Corporate Real Estate Services (CRES) dalam Direktorat Finance &amp; Sharia Business Unit untuk mengimplementasikan SMK3.

### Identifikasi Risiko K3 [403-2]

Sejalan dengan Kebijakan K3L yang berlaku, Permata Bank secara proaktif melakukan pengelolaan K3 berupa proses identifikasi bahaya dan ancaman di tempat kerja, penilaian risiko serta penanganan risiko K3, keamanan (security), serta kelangsungan usaha (Business Continuity Planning/BCP). Pada tahun 2024, identifikasi bahaya dilakukan melalui Building Risk Assessment (BRA) dan HSE Management Audit (HMA) di 16 kantor cabang utama. Dari hasil penilaian dan audit, terdapat 54 temuan yang seluruhnya telah diselesaikan. Upaya ini dilakukan Bank untuk mencegah terjadinya kecelakaan kerja, penyakit akibat kerja, serta kerusakan lingkungan.

### Occupational Health and Safety Management System (OHSMS) [403-1][403-8]

Permata Bank has implemented an Occupational Health and Safety Management System (OHSMS) applicable to all (100%) PermataBankers, as outlined in the Health, Safety, and Environmental (HSE) Policy. The OHSMS aims to create a proper, safe, and comfortable working environment for PermataBankers, customers, partners, and other stakeholders. The implementation of the OHSMS is managed by the Security, Safety &amp; Support Management (SSSM) Department, which operates under the Corporate Real Estate Services (CRES) Division within the Finance &amp; Sharia Business Unit Directorate.

### OHS Risk Identification [403-2]

In alignment with the HSE Policy, Permata Bank proactively manages OHS through hazard and threat identification processes in the workplace, risk assessment, and OHS risk mitigation. This includes security measures and Business Continuity Planning (BCP). In 2024, hazard identification was conducted through Building Risk Assessment (BRA) and HSE Management Audit (HMA) at 16 main branches. The assessment and audit identified 54 findings which were resolved. These efforts enable the Bank to prevent workplace accidents, occupational diseases, and environmental damage.
