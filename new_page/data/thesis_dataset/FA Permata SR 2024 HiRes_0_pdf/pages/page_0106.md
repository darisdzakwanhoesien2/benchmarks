# Pengelolaan Risiko Iklim [2-13][FN-CB-550a.2]

Pengelolaan Risiko Iklim di Permata Bank dilakukan di bawah pengawasan Direktur Risiko selaku Risk Framework Owner (RFO) dari Risiko Iklim, yang dalam aktivitas kesehariannya didukung oleh RFO PIC yang terdiri dari Division Head Market and Enterprise Management (MERM), Division Head Corporate Commercial &amp; Emerging Business Banking Credit Risk Management (CCE CRM), serta Division Head Corporate Secretary &amp; Sustainability. Sejalan dengan penambahan Risiko Iklim sebagai risiko utama, Permata Bank juga telah membentuk Kerangka Kerja Tipe Risiko Iklim (Climate Risk Type Framework) serta menerapkan Taksonomi Hijau Indonesia (THI) yang kemudian diperkuat menjadi Taksonomi untuk Keuangan Berkelanjutan Indonesia (TKBI) untuk memastikan penerapan prinsip keuangan berkelanjutan yang efektif dan mengatasi isu-isu terkait iklim. Departemen Climate Risk Analytics juga telah dibentuk pada tahun 2024 di bawah Divisi MERM dengan fokus pada pengembangan Climate Risk Stress Test (CRST) dan perhitungan Financed Emission.

Pada tahun 2024, Permata Bank telah melakukan CRST pertama kalinya sesuai dengan persyaratan regulasi dan telah disetujui oleh Direktur Risiko dan Direktur Keuangan &amp; Unit Usaha Syariah, serta disajikan kepada Komite Pemantau Risiko (KPR) dan Komite Manajemen Risiko (KMR) untuk memberikan update mengenai identifikasi risiko dan dampaknya kepada Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris. Hasil CRST dapat digunakan sebagai pertimbangan pengambilan pengambilan keputusan maupun penerapan strategi keberlanjutan.

Frekuensi pelaksanaan CRST akan sejalan dengan persyaratan regulasi, mengingat regulasi Risiko Iklim adalah dokumen yang terus berkembang. Pendekatan yang dilakukan CSRT telah mengikuti panduan OJK dengan menerapkan skenario-skenario yang ditentukan (Net Zero 2050, Delay Transition, Current Policy) terhadap Risiko Transisi dan Risiko Fisik.

Secara garis besar, debitur diklasifikasikan ke dalam sub-sektor prioritas yang telah ditentukan tingkat risikonya (High, Medium, dan Low), kemudian dilakukan uji ketahanan atas debitur, baik untuk jangka pendek (2024-2026) maupun jangka panjang (2030-2050). Uji ketahanan dilakukan dengan melihat dampak skenario terhadap perubahan Credit Risk Grading (CRG) debitur dan dampak finansial terhadap Permata Bank secara keseluruhan melalui proyeksi Loan Impairment (LI), Risk Weighted Asset (RWA), dan Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR). Selain dampak pada Risiko Kredit, CRST secara umum juga diaplikasikan untuk mengukur dampak terhadap Risiko Pasar, Risiko Likuiditas, dan Risiko Operasional.

Melalui pilot bottom-up CRST di tahun 2024 terhadap 50% portofolio, Permata Bank mengidentifikasi dampak dari Risiko Transisi dan Risiko Fisik terhadap penurunan Profit After Tax (PAT) serta penurunan Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) dalam jangka pendek (2024-2026) menjadi sebesar 30%-31% dan dalam jangka panjang (2030-2050) menjadi sebesar 32%-33% dibandingkan Desember 2023 yang mencapai 38%. # Climate Risk Management [2-13][FN-CB-550a.2]

Climate Risk management at Permata Bank is overseen by the Risk Director as the Risk Framework Owner (RFO) for Climate Risk, supported in daily operations by RFO PICs, including the Division Head Market and Enterprise Management (MERM), Division Head Corporate Commercial &amp; Emerging Business Banking Credit Risk Management (CCE CRM), and Division Head Corporate Secretary &amp; Sustainability. By recognizing Climate Risk as a significant concern, Permata Bank has established a Climate Risk Type Framework and introduced the Indonesian Green Taxonomy (THI), which has been further strengthened into the Indonesian Sustainable Finance Taxonomy (TKBI) to ensure the effective implementation of sustainable finance principles and address climate-related issues. Additionally, the Climate Risk Analytics Department was established in 2024 under the MERM Division, focusing on the development of the Climate Risk Stress Test (CRST) and the calculation of Financed Emissions.

In 2024, Permata Bank conducted its first CSRT in compliance with regulatory requirements, approved by the Risk Director and the Finance &amp; Sharia Business Unit Director. The results were presented to the Risk Management Committee (RMC) and Risk Monitoring Committee (RMOC) to update the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners on risk identification and its impacts. The CRST results can be used as a consideration for decision-making and the implementation of the Bank's sustainability strategy.

The frequency of CRST implementation will align with regulatory requirements, as Climate Risk regulations are continuously evolving. The CRST approach adheres the OJK guidelines, applying specified scenarios (Net Zero 2050, Delay Transition, Current Policy) to assess Transition and Physical Risks.

In general, debtors are classified into priority sub-sectors based on their risk levels (High, Medium, and Low), followed by resilience testing for both the short term (2024-2026) and the long term (2030-2050). Resilience testing evaluates the impact of scenarios on changes in debtor Credit Risk Grading (CRG) and financial impacts on Permata Bank, including projections of Loan Impairment (LI), Risk-Weighted Assets (RWA), and Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR). Beyond Credit Risk, CRST is also applied to measure impacts on Market Risk, Liquidity Risk, and Operational Risk.

In the short term (2024-2026), these declined to 30%-31%, and in the long term (2030-2050) to 32%-33%, compared to December 2023 levels of 38%.
