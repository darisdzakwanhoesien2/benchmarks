## Pelatihan K3 (403-5)

Pemahaman dan kesadaran akan pentingnya K3 juga ditingkatkan melalui pelatihan terkait K3 bagi PermataBankers serta adanya beberapa pelatihan yang bersifat wajib bagi beberapa petugas sesuai penunjukan di masing-masing kantor Bank. Bank pun telah mengembangkan topik-topik pelatihan K3 dalam modul e-learning yang dapat diakses melalui Portal PermataBankers dan PEARL. Pada tahun 2024, Permata Bank juga telah melaksanakan simulasi latihan evakuasi secara terbatas serta pelatihan Tanggap Darurat yang dilakukan di 194 kantor utama dan kantor cabang.

PermataBankers can also report work-related hazards using an incident reporting form.

As part of its commitment to creating a safe and healthy work environment, the Bank has established an Advisory Committee of Occupational Health and Safety (P2K3), consisted of 108 members across nine main offices and was led by the Management. P2K3 serves as a platform for management and employees to actively collaborate in raising awareness and implementing OHS measures. Quarterly meetings conducted by P2K3 provide a forum for PermataBankers to participate and consult on the development, implementation, and evaluation of the OHSMS. The discussions and outcomes of these meetings are regularly reported to the relevant authorities. The P2K3 ensures that the Bank's OHS programs are implemented in compliance with established procedures. (403-4)

## OHS Training (403-5)

Understanding and awareness of the importance of OHS for PermataBankers and there are several mandatory training courses for several officers as appointed at each Bank office. The Bank has also developed OHS training topics into e-learning modules, accessible via the PermataBankers Portal and PEARL. In 2024, Permata Bank conducted limited evacuation drills and Emergency Response Training across 194 main and branch offices.
