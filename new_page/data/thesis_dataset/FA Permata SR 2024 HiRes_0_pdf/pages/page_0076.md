## Evaluasi Keamanan Produk [F 27][F 29][416-1]

Bank memiliki mekanisme untuk memastikan bahwa seluruh (100%) produk dan layanan digital yang dijual telah dievaluasi keamanannya serta sesuai dengan peraturan dan persyaratan yang ditetapkan oleh regulator. Evaluasi dilakukan melalui proses penilaian risiko (risk assessment), audit keamanan TI, pengujian keamanan (penetration testing), evaluasi proses dan prosedur operasional standar, serta Security Operation Center (SOC) yang beroperasi secara berkelanjutan selama 24/7. Adapun penerapan ujian keamanan TI menggunakan jasa pihak ketiga yang independen dan telah menjadi anggota Self-Regulatory Organization (SRO) atau Asosiasi Sistem Pembayaran Indonesia (ASPI) yang ditunjuk oleh Bank Indonesia (BI).

Dalam menangani produk atau layanan baru, Permata Bank telah mengacu pada standar keamanan untuk perizinan BI dan OJK yang mewajibkan agar setiap produk dan layanan Bank harus memiliki laporan perizinannya terlebih dahulu. Salah satu syaratnya adalah tidak ada temuan penetration test dan audit TI yang masih open. Jika layanan tersebut sudah mendapatkan izin dari BI atau OJK, layanannya dimonitor sesuai prosedur pengelolaan SLA layanan TI. Hal ini juga diatur mekanisme change management; di mana layanan TI harus lolos uji security acceptance test (compliance check dan vulnerability assessment) terlebih dahulu sebelum digunakan atau dilakukan perubahan di lingkungan produksi. Dengan demikian selama tahun 2024, tidak terdapat produk dan layanan keuangan yang ditarik kembali dari program penjualan dengan alasan apapun, serta tidak adanya insiden ketidakpatuhan terhadap peraturan yang membahayakan keselamatan dan kesehatan nasabah. ## Product Safety Evaluation [F 27][F 29][416-1]

The Bank has a mechanism to ensure that all (100%) of its digital products and services are evaluated for security and comply with regulations and requirements set by regulators. This evaluation is conducted through risk assessments, IT security audits, penetration testing, standard operating procedure evaluations, and a 24/7 Security Operation Center (SOC). IT security testing is performed by independent third parties who are members of the Self-Regulatory Organization (SRO) or the Indonesian Payment System Association (ASPI), as designated by Bank Indonesia (BI).

For new products or services, Permata Bank adheres to security standards required for BI and OJK licensing, ensuring that all products and services have an approved licensing report. One key requirement is that there are no unresolved findings from penetration tests or IT audits. Once the product or service receives BI or OJK approval, it is monitored according to IT service SLA management procedures. This process is also governed by change management mechanisms, where IT services must pass a security acceptance test (compliance check and vulnerability assessment) before being deployed or modified in the production environment. As a result, throughout 2024, no financial products or services were withdrawn from sales for any reason, and there were no incidents of regulatory non-compliance that jeopardized the safety and health of customers.
