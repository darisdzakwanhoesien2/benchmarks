Hingga akhir tahun 2024, Permata Bank menghadapi permasalahan hukum. Namun demikian, seluruh permasalahan hukum tersebut tidak bersifat material dan tidak mempengaruhi status, kedudukan, kelangsungan kegiatan usaha, dan keadaan keuangan Bank. Permasalahan hukum yang dimaksud adalah perkara yang melibatkan Permata Bank, baik sebagai tergugat atau turut tergugat, yakni gugatan perdata dari debitur atau pihak ketiga lainnya, terkait dengan eksekusi jaminan oleh Permata Bank atas barang jaminan yang diberikan sebagai jaminan pembayaran kembali utang debitur.

The Legal Advisory Division also ensures that the Bank adheres to applicable laws and regulations in its operations. As outlined in the Legal Advisory Procedure, the division performs the following:

1. Providing legal reviews and issuing opinions on transactions, new products, and services to be introduced by the Bank.
2. Preparing legal document standards for new and/or developed products and services issued by the Bank.
3. Conducting periodic reviews of standard legal documents to ensure compliance with prevailing laws and regulations.
4. Creating and updating the Legal Policy.
5. Reviewing/providing legal opinions on policies/procedures from other units upon request.
6. Assisting related units in handling cases arising from credit or non-credit issues, service provision, and fraud-related matters.
7. Providing legal information and knowledge to related units on a regular basis.
8. Supporting related units in conducting legal assessments and issuing opinions on corporate actions undertaken by the Bank.
9. Drafting legal documents related to credit/financing facilities, both conventional and sharia, associated with credit/financing initiated by the business unit.

As of the end of 2024, Permata Bank faced legal matters. Notably, none of these were material or impacted the Bank's status, position, business continuity, or financial condition. The legal matters referred to are civil lawsuits involving Permata Bank as a defendant or co-defendant, typically arising from third-party or debtor lawsuits related to Permata Bank's execution of collateral provided as security for the repayment of debtor obligations.
