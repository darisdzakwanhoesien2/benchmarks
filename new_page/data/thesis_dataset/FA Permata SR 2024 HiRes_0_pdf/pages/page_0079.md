# Peningkatan Security Awareness dan Kapasitas SDM terkait Keamanan Siber

Permata Bank berupaya membekali seluruh PermataBankers, termasuk Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris, dengan pengetahuan keamanan siber yang memadai. Oleh sebab itu, Bank memberikan publikasi materi serta pelatihan terkait Information Security Awareness. Pelatihan tersebut merupakan mandatory e-learning bagi seluruh karyawan dan disampaikan melalui e-learning portal Permata Bank. Permata Bank menunjuk Unit Kerja Information Security Risk Management sebagai pihak yang bertanggung jawab untuk merumuskan, melaksanakan, dan memantau program peningkatan security awareness bagi seluruh karyawan. Beberapa program security awareness yang diberikan adalah sebagai berikut:

- Publikasi materi security awareness melalui surat elektronik, digital signage, dan desktop wallpaper.
- Modul e-Learning Security Awareness dan modul khusus phishing awareness.
- Phishing simulation yang dilakukan secara berkala.

Di samping itu, Bank juga mengikutsertakan seluruh karyawan di unit kerja terkait keamanan siber pada pelatihan ataupun sertifikasi yang sesuai tugas dan tanggung jawab masing-masing. Beberapa program pelatihan yang diberikan adalah sebagai berikut:

- Pelatihan soft skill atau hard skill sesuai Individual Learning and Development Plan (ILDP) secara berkala.
- Pelatihan dan sertifikasi sesuai kebutuhan di masing-masing unit kerja, seperti CISA, CISM, CRISC, CISSP, ISO 27001, CEH, CHFI, CTIA, CRTP, ECPPT, CRTA, dan lain-lain.

# Audit Keamanan Informasi

Sebagai lini pertahanan ketiga, Satuan Kerja Audit Internal (SKAI) bertanggung jawab untuk menilai secara independen efektivitas sistem pengendalian internal Bank, termasuk pengelolaan risiko siber. SKAI memeriksa apakah manajemen telah mengidentifikasi, menilai, dan mengelola risiko sesuai dengan kebijakan dan regulasi yang berlaku. Selain itu, SKAI juga mengacu pada ketentuan Internal Audit dalam mengevaluasi kecukupan rancangan kontrol dan efektivitas manajemen risiko. Sebagai salah satu entitas audit, SKAI pun melakukan audit atas penyelenggaraan Teknologi Informasi (TI) yang di dalamnya mencakup manajemen risiko siber. Rencana Kerja Tahunan SKAI disetujui oleh Direktur Utama, Komite Audit, dan Dewan Komisaris.

# Keamanan Data dan Privasi Nasabah [FN-CB-230a.1][FN-CB-230a.2]

Pertumbuhan pesat produk dan layanan perbankan digital serta perkembangan teknologi tentunya telah membawa sejumlah tantangan baru, termasuk meningkatnya risiko keamanan data dan informasi. Permata Bank menyadari bahwa risiko ini dapat memberikan berbagai dampak negatif pada reputasi Bank dan kepercayaan nasabah, serta menimbulkan potensi kerugian finansial bagi Bank dan nasabah. Di samping itu, risiko keamanan data yang dihadapi pun semakin beragam, salah satunya ancaman dengan menggunakan media phishing. Oleh karena itu, Bank telah menerapkan keamanan berlapis, baik dari sisi teknologi, proses, maupun SDM untuk menghadapi ancaman phishing.

# Enhancing Security Awareness and Employee Capacity in Cybersecurity

Permata Bank equips all PermataBankers, including the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners, with sufficient knowledge of cybersecurity. To achieve this, the Bank provides Information Security Awareness materials and training as mandatory e-learning courses for all employees via the Permata Bank e-learning portal. The Information Security Risk Management Unit is tasked with the responsibilities of designing, implementing, and monitoring security awareness programs for all employees. Key security awareness initiatives include:

- Dissemination of security awareness materials through e-mail, digital signage, and desktop wallpapers.
- E-learning modules on Security Awareness and phishing awareness.
- Regular phishing simulations.

Additionally, the Bank ensures that all employees in cybersecurity-related units participate in relevant training and certification programs tailored to their duties and responsibilities. Training programs provided include:

- Regular soft skills and hard skills training as part of the Individual Learning and Development Plan (ILDP).
- Specialized training and certifications based on unit needs, such as CISA, CISM, CRISC, CISSP, ISO 27001, CEH, CHFI, CTIA, CRTP, ECPPT, CRTA, and others.

# Information Security Audit

As the third line of defense, the Internal Audit Unit (SKAI) is responsible for independently assessing the effectiveness of the Bank's internal control systems, including cybersecurity risk management. SKAI evaluates whether management has identified, assessed, and managed risks in accordance with applicable policies and regulations. Additionally, SKAI follows Internal Audit guidelines to assess the adequacy of control designs and the effectiveness of risk management. As part of its audit responsibilities, SKAI conducts audits on Information Technology (IT) operations, including cybersecurity risk management. The SKAI Annual Work Plan is approved by the President Director, Audit Committee, and Board of Commissioners.

# Data Security and Customer Privacy [FN-CB-230a.1][FN-CB-230a.2]

The rapid growth of digital banking products and services, along with technological advancements, has introduced new challenges, including increased risks to data and information security. Permata Bank recognizes that these risks can negatively impact the Bank's reputation and customer trust, while also posing potential financial losses for both the Bank and its customers. Additionally, data security risks are becoming increasingly diverse, including threats such as phishing attacks. To address these threats, the Bank has implemented multi-layered security measures across technology, processes, and human resources to mitigate phishing risks.
