# Recommendation

1. To provide details on how stakeholder input is used in key corporate decision-making and sustainability strategies in its stakeholder engagement disclosures.
2. To disclose targets for each material topic, ensuring that efforts are measurable and progress can be effectively monitored in addressing sustainability challenges.
3. To improve disclosures of its action plan based on the results of employee and customer satisfaction surveys to demonstrate their feedback is used for continuous improvement.
4. To disclose measurable outcomes of its social and environmental programs, such as increase in savings and financial knowledge from financial literacy program, quantitative data on greenhouse gas emissions reduction, and quantification the societal impact of community investments.
5. To enhance compliance with GRI 201-2 and OJK F.9, improve ESRA to measure biodiversity impacts, including palm oil and mining industries, using GIS (a location database from regulators, i.e. Ministry of Environment &amp; Forestry or Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources), manual analysis of client documents or other simple mapping tools, report data on high-risk clients adjacent to conservation areas and denied credits based on CRMS insights, and include biodiversity risks in ERMF and ESRA with clear financial estimates, strengthening climate risk management and transparency.
