## Kecelakaan Kerja [403-9]

Hingga akhir tahun 2024, tidak ada kecelakaan kerja fatal yang terjadi di lingkup Permata Bank. Akan tetapi, terdapat tiga insiden kecelakaan kerja yang seluruhnya bersifat ringan. Sebagai langkah preventif, Bank telah melakukan evaluasi menyeluruh untuk mengidentifikasi penyebab serta memperoleh rekomendasi tindakan perbaikan guna mencegah terjadinya insiden serupa di masa mendatang.

In addition, the Bank launched the Peduli Karyawan Permata (PEKA) program, a comprehensive initiative aimed at improving physical and psychological well-being, with a focus on mental health. This program includes the Employee Assistance Program (EAP) for mental health consultations, financial literacy programs to equip employees with financial management skills, and psychological support initiatives to help employees maintain mental well-being in the workplace. In 2024, 65 employees benefited from the EAP.

## Work Accidents [403-9]

Until the end of 2024, there were no fatal work accidents within Permata Bank. However, there were three workplace incidents, all of which were minor. As a preventive measure, the Bank conducted a thorough evaluation to identify the causes and obtain recommendations for corrective actions to prevent similar incidents in the future.

# Rantai Pasok yang Bertanggung Jawab

## Responsible Supply Chain

### Pelibatan Pemasok Lokal [204-1]

Permata Bank melibatkan banyak pemasok lokal dalam rantai pasoknya. Pemasok lokal merupakan pemasok yang beroperasional di Indonesia, atau wilayah tertentu dalam negeri. Permata Bank juga melakukan internalisasi aspek LST di dalam proses pengadaan dengan cara memperkenalkan aspek LST kepada vendor melalui pelaksanaan webinar bertema keberlanjutan kepada vendor di tanggal 26 dan 28 November 2024. Bank melakukan pengkinian Letter of Integrity (LOI) dan Code of Conduct (CoC) yang menambahkan aspek standar LST secara umum per tanggal 3 Juni 2024. Bank juga secara aktif berkomunikasi kepada vendor untuk penyelesaian sign off LOI dan CoC terbaru di bulan Desember 2024.

### Local Supplier Engagement [204-1]

Permata Bank engages a significant number of local suppliers in its supply chain. Local suppliers are defined as those operating within Indonesia or specific regions domestically. Permata Bank has also incorporated ESG aspects into its procurement process by introducing these principles to vendors through implementation of a webinar on sustainability for vendors on 26 and 28 November 2024. As of 3 June 2024, Permata Bank updated its Letter of Integrity (LOI) and Code of Conduct (CoC) to include general ESG standards. The Bank actively communicated with vendors to complete the sign-off of the updated LOI and CoC by December 2024.
