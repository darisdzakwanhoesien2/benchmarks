# Realisasi Investasi Sosial Permata Bank 2024

Sepanjang tahun 2024, Permata Bank telah melaksanakan berbagai program sosial dengan total dana yang disalurkan sebesar Rp5,95 miliar. Bank berharap program-program ini dapat memberikan nilai manfaat bagi seluruh pemangku kepentingan.

# Permata Bank Social Investment Realization 2024

Throughout 2024, Permata Bank implemented various social programs, with a total disbursed fund of Rp5.95 billion. The Bank hopes that these initiatives will create meaningful value for all stakeholders.

# Survei Kepuasan Penerima Manfaat dan Employee Volunteer (EVO)

Permata Bank berupaya mengukur efektivitas pelaksanaan program Permata Hati dengan secara rutin mengumpulkan umpan balik dari penerima manfaat dan EVO di akhir program. Hasil pengukuran tersebut menjadi bahan evaluasi untuk perbaikan pelaksanaan program di masa mendatang.

# Beneficiary Satisfaction Survey and Employee Volunteer (EVO)

Permata Bank strives to measure the effectiveness of the Permata Hati program by regularly collecting feedback from beneficiaries and EVO at the end of each program. The results of these evaluations serve as a basis for improving program implementation in the future.
