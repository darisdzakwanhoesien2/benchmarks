# Permata Learning Week (PLW)

Permata Bank juga kembali mengadakan acara tahunan Permata Learning Week (PLW) dengan berbagai peningkatan pada topik pelatihan dan infrastruktur pendukungnya. Acara tersebut dilaksanakan pada bulan Agustus 2024 dan telah diikuti oleh hampir 12.681 karyawan dalam 15 sesi yang berbeda, meningkat 12,0% dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya. Pada tahun 2024, PLW berfokus pada topik "Shaping the Future Together" sebagai tema utama dengan berbagai topik pelatihan yang diselenggarakan, meliputi keberlanjutan, Artificial Intelligence (AI), serta well-being. Selain sesi pembelajaran, PLW juga menyelenggarakan berbagai kegiatan menarik, seperti AI Photobooth, Aura Reading, Tarot, Art &amp; Crafts, Cooking Class, serta standing booth. Selama acara berlangsung, lebih dari 800 goodie bag, hadiah, dan berbagai doorprize diberikan kepada para peserta. Untuk membantu karyawan dalam mempelajari sesi yang dibahas, seluruh sesi selama PLW telah diunggah ke platform pembelajaran digital PEARL.

# Sustainability Learning Series

Permata Bank berupaya untuk meningkatkan kapasitas dan kompetensi PermataBankers mengenai keuangan berkelanjutan, antara lain terkait karakteristik produk dan/atau jasa keuangan berkelanjutan serta pengelolaan risikonya. Peningkatan kompetensi dilakukan dengan pelaksanaan pelatihan/webinar dan/atau program pembelajaran lainnya melalui Sustainability Learning Series. Adapun webinar yang dilaksanakan di tahun 2024 antara lain:

- Webinar pada acara launching Sustainability Learning Series dengan tema "Best Practice Pertamina Sustainability" pada tanggal 5 Juni 2024 yang diikuti oleh 410 peserta.
- Webinar pada acara Permata Learning Week dengan tema "Integrating Sustainability into Corporate Lending (Renewable Energy Industry)" pada tanggal 6 Agustus 2024 yang diikuti oleh 459 peserta.
- Webinar dengan tema "The Green Shift: How Sustainable Finance is Changing the Game" pada tanggal 7 Agustus 2024 yang diikuti oleh 433 peserta.
- Webinar dengan tema "Sustainability Journey" pada tanggal 7 Agustus 2024 yang diikuti oleh 325 peserta.

Kami juga memberikan pelatihan luring kepada PermataBankers terkait perhitungan emisi yang dilaksanakan di Permata Learning Center pada tanggal 8-9 Oktober 2024.

Bank juga menyediakan perangkat self-learning mengenai keuangan berkelanjutan secara umum dan lebih spesifik mengenai penerapan aspek LST bagi seluruh karyawan melalui digital learning platform. Hal ini dilakukan dengan merilis lima video e-learning keuangan berkelanjutan di PEARL pada tahun 2024, yaitu tentang Sustainability Awareness, Navigating Climate Change: Strategies for Business Resilience, Sustainable Finance Roadmap, Understanding Indonesia Sustainable Finance Taxonomy, dan Pelaksanaan TJSL #DenganHati untuk Bukit Tigapuluh with WWF. Selain itu, Bank juga telah mengirimkan 30 e-mail blast Sustainability Journey tentang keuangan berkelanjutan kepada karyawan.

# Permata Learning Week (PLW)

Permata Bank successfully organized the annual Permata Learning Week (PLW) in August 2024, featuring enhanced training topics and improved supporting infrastructure. This year's event attracted participation from nearly 12,681 employees across 15 sessions, representing a 12.0% increase compared to the previous year. The 2024 PLW adopted the theme "Shaping the Future Together", focusing on key areas such as sustainability, Artificial Intelligence (AI), and well-being. In addition to training sessions, the event offered a variety of interactive activities, including an AI Photobooth, Aura Reading, Tarot, Art &amp; Crafts, and Cooking Classes, along with information booths. During the event, more than 800 goodie bags, gifts, and door prizes were distributed to participants. To ensure employees could access and benefit from the knowledge shared during the sessions, all learning materials and session recordings were made available on the PEARL digital learning platform.

# Sustainability Learning Series

Permata Bank strives to enhance the capacity and competence of PermataBankers regarding sustainable finance, including the characteristics of sustainable financial products and/or services and their associated risk management. Competency development is carried out through training/webinars, and/or other learning programs under the Sustainability Learning Series. The webinars conducted in 2024 include:

- Webinar during the launching of the Sustainability Learning Series with the theme "Best Practice Pertamina Sustainability", held on 5 June 2024 and attended by 410 participants.
- Webinar during Permata Learning Week with the theme "Integrating Sustainability into Corporate Lending (Renewable Energy Industry)", held on 6 August 2024 and attended by 459 participants.
- Webinar with the theme "The Green Shift: How Sustainable Finance is Changing the Game", held on 7 August 2024 and attended by 433 participants.
- Webinar with the theme "Sustainability Journey", held on 7 August 2024 and attended by 325 participants.

We also conducted offline training for PermataBankers on emissions calculations at the Permata Learning Center on 8-9 October 2024.

In addition, the Bank provides self-learning tools on sustainable finance, both general and specific to the implementation of ESG aspects, for all employees via its digital learning platform. In 2024, the Bank released five e-learning videos on sustainable finance through the PEARL platform, covering the following topics: Sustainability Awareness, Navigating Climate Change: Strategies for Business Resilience, Sustainable Finance Roadmap, Understanding Indonesia Sustainable Finance Taxonomy, and CSER Implementation #DenganHati untuk Bukit Tigapuluh with WWF. Additionally, the Bank issued 30 Sustainability Journey e-mail blasts to employees, focusing on sustainable finance education.
