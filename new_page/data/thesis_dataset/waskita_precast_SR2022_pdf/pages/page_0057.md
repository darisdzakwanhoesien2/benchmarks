WSBP organizational structure as of December 31, 2022, has been stipulated through Decree No. 99/SK/WSBP/PEN/2022 dated September 15, 2022 concerning Revisions to Changes in the Organizational Structure of Corporate Offices and Business Units within PT Waskita Beton Precast Tbk.
