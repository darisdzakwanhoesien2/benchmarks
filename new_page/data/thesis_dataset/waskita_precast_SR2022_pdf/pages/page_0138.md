2. Skill allowance

Skill allowance is a reward granted to employees in the form of allowance on a monthly basis for their professional skills as indicated by the possession of a Skill Certificate (SKT) issued by an Accredited Professional Association, or recognition by WSBP for special skills possessed by the employees. As such with the expertise allowance, the amount of skill allowance is regulated in the Decision of Director in charge of HCM.
