# Tentang Laporan Keberlanjutan

About the Sustainability Report

Laporan Keberlanjutan merupakan bentuk keterbukaan Perseroan atas pengelolaan dampak dan capaian kinerja lingkungan, sosial, tata kelola (LST), dan ekonomi kepada pemangku kepentingan.

Sustainability Report represents the Company's transparency towards stakeholders on the management of impacts and achievements in the environmental, social, governance (ESG), as well as economic performance.
