## Topik Signifikan Pertemuan dan Kebutuhan

1. Pengurusan izin usaha dan regulasi;
2. Koordinasi terkait tata niaga, ekspor/impor.

## Upaya Pemenuhan Kebutuhan

1. Mengikuti kegiatan yang dijadwalkan pemerintah;
2. Kepatuhan pada peraturan;
3. Memberikan laporan kinerja secara berkala.

## Meetings/Means of Engagement and Frequency

Hearing

## Significant Topic of Meetings and Needs

1. Management of business license and regulations;
2. Coordination regarding trade, export/import.

## Efforts to Fulfill Needs

1. Participating in government scheduled activities;
2. Compliance with regulations;
3. Providing periodic performance reports.
