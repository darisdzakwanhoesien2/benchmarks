As a follow-up to the OHS training, the Company, through P2K3, conducts an evaluation of the effectiveness of employees' understanding of the training material through surveys/questionnaires. Meanwhile, the evaluation of OHS culture awareness is based on the level of compliance of employees with K3-related regulations and the number of work accidents.
