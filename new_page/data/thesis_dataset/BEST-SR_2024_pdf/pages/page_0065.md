The stages of handling complaints submitted by tenants can be described as follows:

a. Tenants submit complaints and/or needs to Customer Service through the BEFA Helpdesk application or contact BeFa Team by creating a complaint ticket
b. Customer Service provides responses to complaints and/or needs submitted by tenants
c. Customer Service forwards complaints and/or needs submitted by tenants to the relevant PIC
d. The Implementing PIC follows up and resolves complaints and/or tenant needs
e. Customer Service provides information to tenants if complaints and/or needs have been resolved
f. Tenant receives complaint resolution reports and provides responses to the resolution of complaints and/or their needs

# Number of Complaints in 2024

Throughout 2024, BeFa's Customer Service team received 70 complaints and 229 service requests, all of which have been duly addressed and resolved. This reflects BeFa's commitment to delivering efficient and responsive tenant support, ensuring a seamless operational experience
