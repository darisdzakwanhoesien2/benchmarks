## 10. Sisi

## 11. Emisi dihitung dari data jarak dan pengeluaran pengiriman barang internal GoTo dari vendor dan pengiriman barang merchant antar gudang Tokopedia.

Emissions are derived from a combination of initial and base/parang/and-based data for shipment of GoTo internal goods from vendors and shipment of merchant goods between Tokopedia's headquarters.

## 12. Memahas emisi dari transportasi karyawan dari aktivitas terkait bisnis (termasuk perjalanan udara, darat, kondisi api, dan laut), serta akomodasi karyawan selama perjalanan terkait bisnis selama tahun pelaporan. Data jarak dan pengeluaran tidak digunakan.

Includes emissions from the transportation of employees from business-related activities (including air, road, rail, and marine travel), as well as the accommodations of employees during business-related travel during the reporting year. Both distance-based and hybrid-based data were used.

## 13. Data umat digunakan untuk mengatur perjalanan (banyak) karyawan dari rumah ke kantor atau gudang.

Every data was used by measures like: day employee commute from their home to our office or month week.

## 14. Emisi dari distribusi dan transportasi trili dihitung menggunakan data jarak dan pengeluaran (daya untuk pengiriman Tokopedia. Emisi dari pengiriman Tokopedia yang diselesaikan menggunakan laporan (layar pada GoKIM) dimasarkan ke dalam kategori 11.

Downstream transportation and distribution emissions were calculated using distance-based and hybrid-based data from shipping of Tokopedia deliveries. Emissions from Tokopedia were calculated using layer and air (i.e., outside layer) based on Category 11.

## 15. Emisi dari penggunaan produk yang dijual oleh GoTo termasuk emisi tetap penggunaan langsung dari layanan mobilitas (layar (buildback), GoCar, GoTaxi, GoFood, GoShop, GoMark, GoKIM, GoSent, GoBio, dan sebar/sebar) GoCar hemat, GoCar ktok, etc). Emisi fasil penggunaan tidak langsung dari produk yang dijual oleh kami.

Emissions from the sale of work products must be used to the program emissions from layer 11 multiple, services (buildbacks), GoCar, GoTaxi, GoFood, GoShop, GoMark, GoKIM, GoSent, GoBio, dan sebar/sebar (oil/air/heat). The amount of use of the product was not calculated.

## 16. Termasuk emisi dari pembuangan dan pengolahan produk yang dijual oleh GoTo. Emisi untuk kategori 11 (banyak dari data) terbasis pengeluaran dari toosti untuk barang komasan di Tokopedia.

Includes emissions from waste disposal and treatment of products sold by GoTo. Emissions for this category were derived from space-based and weight-based data for packaging goods in Tokopedia.

## 17. Total konsumsi bahan bakar dari sumber terbarukan mewakili energi yang disensurnai oleh panel rurus di kantor pusat Tokopedia, dan tidak termasuk energi matahari yang hilang selama konsensi menjadi listrik.

Total fuel consumption from mewakile sources represents the energy consumed by solar panels at Tokopedia headquarters, and does not include the solar energy lost during conversion into electricity.
