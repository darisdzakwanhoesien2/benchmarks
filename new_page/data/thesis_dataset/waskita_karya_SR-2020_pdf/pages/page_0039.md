## Building the Nation through Environmental Performance [4.b.1]

Waskita's commitment to preserving the environment includes ensuring that every project work does not have negative impact on the environment and is in accordance with environmental criteria as well as applicable laws and regulations. Before working on a project, the Company conducts an Environmental Impact Analysis (AMDAL) and periodically audits the project handled by both Internal and External Auditors. This is the Company's commitment to realizing Go Green Construction, which is not only oriented towards profit but also towards environmental preservation in every project as well as environment around the operational locations.
