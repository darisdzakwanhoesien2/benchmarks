## Mekanisme Nominasi Badan Tata Kelola Tertinggi [102-24]

Waskita sebagai BUMN yang sahamnya mayoritas dimiliki oleh negara, mekanisme nominasi anggota Board of Commissioners dan Board of Directors telah diatur melalui Peraturan Menteri BUMN No. PER-02/MBU/02/2015 tanggal 17 Februari 2015 tentang Persyaratan dan Tata Cara Pengangkatan dan Pemberhentian Anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Dewan Pengawas Badan Usaha Milik Negara serta Peraturan Menteri BUMN No. PER-03/MBU/02/2015 tanggal 17 Februari 2015 tentang Persyaratan dan Tata Cara Pengangkatan dan Pemberhentian Anggota Direksi Badan Usaha Milik Negara. Dalam pedoman tersebut telah diatur mengenai mekanisme nominasi dan penilaian atas kepatutan dan kelayakan (fit and proper test) bagi calon Anggota Board of Commissioners dan Board of Directors.

Pemegang Saham/RUPS telah melakukan penilaian terhadap nama-nama calon anggota Board of Commissioners dan Board of Directors Perseroan yang diperoleh melalui proses penjaringan sesuai dengan ketentuan yang telah ditetapkan. Seluruh bakal calon yang disetujui oleh Pemegang Saham/RUPS telah diundang untuk mengikuti uji kelayakan dan kepatutan (fit and proper test).

## Nomination Mechanism of Supreme Governance Bodies [102-24]

Waskita as an SOE whose majority shares are owned by the state, the nomination mechanism for members of Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors has been regulated through the Minister of SOE Regulation No. PER-02/MBU/02/2015 dated February 17, 2015 concerning Requirements and Procedures for the Appointment and Dismissal of Members of Board of Commissioners and Supervisory Board of State-Owned Enterprises and the Minister of SOE Regulation No. PER-03/MBU/02/2015 dated February 17, 2015 concerning Requirements and Procedures for the Appointment and Dismissal of Members of Board of Directors of State-Owned Enterprises. These guidelines have stipulated the nomination and proper test mechanism for candidate of Members of Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors.

The Shareholders/GMS assesses the names of candidate members of Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors of the Company who are obtained through a selection process in accordance with the stipulated conditions. All prospective candidates approved by the Shareholders/GMS are then invited to take part in the fit and proper test.
