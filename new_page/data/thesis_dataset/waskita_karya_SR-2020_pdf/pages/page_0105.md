# Penilaian Pegawai &amp; Pengembangan Karir [404-3]

Perseroan melakukan penilaian kepada seluruh pegawai terkait soft competency individu dan kinerja. Beberapa aspek penilaian pegawai difokuskan pada kejujuran, kedisiplinan, tanggungjawab, pengetahuan, inisiatif, kecakapan, kepemimpinan, hubungan dengan rekan kerja, kepatuhan dan hasil pekerjaan.

Program pengembangan karir diberikan kepada setiap pegawai untuk mencapai tingkat karir yang maksimal sesuai dengan perkembangan bisnis Perseroan dan memberikan kesempatan kepada setiap pegawai untuk berkontribusi secara maksimal kepada Perseroan. Perseroan juga menjamin persamaan kesempatan bagi seluruh pegawai dalam mengembangkan karirnya sesuai dengan perkembangan bisnis Perseroan secara adil dan transparan. Hal ini diharapkan dapat meningkatkan efisiensi dan efektivitas dalam mencapai tujuan Perseroan.

# Employee Assessment &amp; Career Development [404-3]

The Company assesses all employees related to individual soft competency and performance. Several aspects of employee appraisal are focused on honesty, discipline, responsibility, knowledge, initiative, skills, leadership, relationships with colleagues, compliance and work results.

Career development program is given to each employee to achieve the maximum career level in accordance with Company's business development and provide opportunities for each employee to contribute optimally to the Company. The Company also guarantees equal opportunities for all employees in developing their careers in accordance with the Company's business development in a fair and transparent manner. This is expected to increase efficiency and effectiveness in achieving the Company's goals.
