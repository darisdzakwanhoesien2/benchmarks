## Penghematan Energi &amp; Air [302-4, 302-5, 303-3]

Komitmen Perseroan dalam menjaga kelestarian alam khususnya di kantor pusat telah dilakukan dengan program penghematan energi baik penggunaan listrik maupun air. Upaya penghematan listrik yang telah dilakukan diantaranya dengan mengganti seluruh lampu dengan jenis LED yang hemat energi, pemakaian AC yang diatur menggunakan sistem otamatis (timer) dari jam 05.00 s.d. 17.00 serta melakukan kampanye hemat listrik kepada pengguna gedung Waskita Heritage. Sedangkan upaya penghematan air yang telah dilakukan Perseroan adalah dengan mengganti semua keran wastafel dengan jenis autostop, merecycle limbah STP untuk digunakan flushing toilet serta melakukan kampanye hemat air kepada pengguna gedung.

## Konsumsi Energi Tahun 2019-2020

The Company also periodically conducts emission tests on smoke-producing equipment such as generators and operational vehicles to find out that the exhaust emission levels are below the threshold so as to reduce global warming and select environmentally friendly production equipment and vehicles [305-1,305-5,305-6].

Waskita's commitment to realizing Green Building has been proven by obtaining Waskita Green Building certificate from the Green Building Council Indonesia (GBCI) and the absence of sanctions related to non-compliance with environmental laws and regulations [307-1].

## Energy &amp; Water Saving [302-4, 302-5, 303-3]

The Company's commitment to preserving nature, especially at the Head Office, has been carried out by means of energy saving programs, both electricity and water use. Efforts to save electricity that have been made include replacing all lamps with energy-efficient LED types, use of air conditioning which is regulated using an automatic system (timer) from 05.00 am until 05.00 pm and conducting a campaign to save electricity for Waskita Heritage building users. Meanwhile, the effort to save water that has been carried out by the Company is by replacing all sink taps with auto stop type, recycling STP waste for use in flushing toilets and conducting water-saving campaigns for building users.
