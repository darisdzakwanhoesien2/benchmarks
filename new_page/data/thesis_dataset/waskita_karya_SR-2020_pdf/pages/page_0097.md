## Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3)

Waskita menempatkan Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3) sebagai prioritas utama di dalam bekerja dan beraktivitas. Pencapaian semua target usaha Perseroan tidak sempurna jika aspek keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja tidak diperhatikan. Untuk itu, Waskita berkomitmen mendukung dan melaksanakan aspek keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja dalam semua kegiatan baik operasional di kantor maupun di proyek. Seluruh kegiatan operasional dan proyek mengacu pada Sistem Manajemen Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (SMK3) dan wajib dipatuhi oleh seluruh Insan Waskita.

Penerapan SMK3 di Perseroan berpedoman pada sistem Standar Manajemen Mutu ISO 9001:2015; ISO 14001:2015; ISO 45001:2018 serta Sistem Manajemen Pengamanan sesuai regulasi yang diberlakukan sejak tahun 2007. Untuk mempertahankan keberlanjutan sertifikat QHSE (ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001), Perseroan melakukan beberapa program antara lain memaksimalkan penerapan PDCA pada CO, BU dan proyek, melakukan inspeksi rutin dan non rutin (koordinasi dengan BU masing-masing proyek) dan membuat kontrak dengan vendor dan menuangkan ketentuan terkait batas waktu maksimal penerbitan sertifikat baru (paling lambat 1 bulan sebelum sertifikat lama expired).

Perseroan juga melakukan review Prosedur Pengamanan dan melakukan koordinasi dengan Auditor (Mabes POLRI) dan CORSEC untuk memastikan kesiapan proses Audit. Hal ini dilakukan agar Perseroan dapat memperoleh sertifikat terkait Pengamanan (Perpol No. 7 Tahun 2019) yang berguna bagi aktivitas operasional Wasktia.

Perseroan sangat memperhatikan kasus fatality, insiden pencemaran lingkungan skala nasional dan kegagalan konstruksi di setiap proyek Waskita. Untuk mewujudkan hal tersebut, Perseroan melakukan monitoring terhadap hasil temuan dari Management Walk Through (MWT) Top Management kemudian menindaklanjuti dan melakukan pencegahan menyeluruh terhadap proyek lainnya dan mengadakan MWT yang dilakukan oleh Board of Directors ke beberapa proyek Perseroan terutama yang memiliki tingkat kompleksitas tinggi.

## Occupational Health and Safety (OHS)

Waskita places Occupational Safety and Health (OHS) as the top priority in doing work and activities. The achievement of all of Company's business targets is imperfect if the safety and health aspects are not considered. For this reason, Waskita is committed to supporting and implementing occupational safety and health aspects in all activities both in office and project operations. All operational activities and projects refer to the Occupational Health and Safety Management System (OHSMS) and must be obeyed by all Waskita Personnel.

The implementation of OHSMS in the Company is guided by ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management Standard system; ISO 14001:2015; ISO 45001:2018 and Security Management System in accordance with regulations that have been in effect since 2007. To maintain the sustainability of QHSE certificate (ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001), the Company has carried out several programs, including maximizing the application of PDCA on CO, BU and projects, conducting routine and non-routine inspections (in coordination with BU of each project), make a contract with vendors and set out the provisions regarding the maximum deadline for issuing new certificate (no later than 1 month before the old certificate expires).

The Company also reviews Security Procedures and coordinates with the Auditor (National Police Headquarters) and the CORSEC to ensure the readiness of Audit process. This is done so that the Company can obtain a certificate related to Security (Perpol No.7 of 2019) which is useful for Wasktia's operational activities.

The Company pays close attention to cases of fatality, incidents of national scale environmental pollution and construction failures in every Waskita project. To achieve this, the Company monitors the findings of Management Walk Through (MWT), in which Top Management then follows up and takes complete prevention of other projects and conducts MWTs carried out by Board of Directors for several Company projects, especially those with a high level of complexity.
