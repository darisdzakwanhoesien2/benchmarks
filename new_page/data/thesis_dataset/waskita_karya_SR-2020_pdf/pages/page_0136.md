# Pengelolaan Risiko Terkait Aspek Ekonomi, Sosial dan Lingkungan Hidup [102-29]

Dalam pengelolaan risiko, Waskita sangat memperhatikan aspek ekonomi, sosial dan lingkungan hidup. Risiko terkait aspek ekonomi biasanya disebabkan oleh peraturan perpajakan dan pungutan, mekanisme pengurusan perizinan, situasi pasar, ketidakstabilan moneter, pergantian pejabat di lingkungan pemerintahan. Perseroan melakukan pengelolaan risiko ekonomi tersebut dengan melakukan pengembangan usaha baru yang didahului dengan kajian feasibility study untuk memberikan value dan return semaksimal mungkin bagi Perseroan, melakukan perintisan proyek lain diluar

The implementation of risk management in the Company includes:

1. Preparation and implementation of Risk Management Policies;
2. Compilation of risk appetite and risk tolerance;
3. Formulation of the types of risks faced by the Company which are divided into 4 types of risk (Business and Financial Risks, Operational and Technical Risks, Legal Risks and QHSE &amp; Performance Risks);
4. Use of Waskita Risk Management Application (WaRM) for periodic risk reporting;
5. Preparation of a bribery risk assessment;
6. Preparation of risk assessment on corporate actions;
7. Evaluation of Waskita Risk Management System in 2020 has been running effectively. The results become recommendation that must be followed up to improve Waskita Risk Management System.

The lack of a risk awareness culture in the Company and the inadequate use of a risk approach in decision making becomes a challenge in implementing ERM as a whole. In addition, the implementation process of WARM (Waskita Risk Management) application that is integrated with SAP as a whole in the Company still needs to be done carefully because it will affect the speed and accuracy of risk information that will be used in the management's decision making. Therefore, the Company strives to mitigate the risk of these constraints so that risk management can be carried out thoroughly and a strong risk awareness culture is created within the Company. The Company conducts a risk study which includes downside risk and opportunity risk study to increase the achievement level of established strategic targets.
