Sebagai salah satu komitmen Perseroan dalam mewujudkan tata kelola perusahaan yang baik, proses rekrutmen pegawai dilakukan mengacu pada ketentuan dan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku dan memberikan kesempatan yang sama kepada seluruh masyarakat Indonesia untuk mengikuti proses rekrutmen. Rekrutmen dilakukan secara transparan dan tidak ada perlakuan diskriminasi terkait unsur SARA (Suku, Agama, Ras dan Antar Golongan), jenis kelamin dan disabilitas serta memperhatikan batas usia minimum bagi calon pekerja agar memastikan tidak adanya eksploitasi anak dibawah umur. Selain itu, Perseroan juga melarang penerapan kerja paksa sehingga pegawai yang direkrut dapat melaksanakan pekerjaan dengan penuh kesadaran dan tanggungjawab. Perseroan memperhatikan aspek pemberdayaan masyarakat lokal berdasarkan domisili pegawai pada lokasi proyek sesuai kebutuhan dan lokasi proyek.

Pada aspek kebijakan remunerasi, Perseroan memperhatikan aspek kompetitif berdasarkan ketentuan yang berlaku. Penerapan skema remunerasi mengacu pada kompetensi yang dimiliki oleh setiap individu, posisi jabatan dan kinerja. Penetapan skema remunerasi diatur berdasarkan pasar tenaga kerja yang mengacu pada Key Performance Indicator (KPI) dan disesuaikan dengan Pay for Performance.

Perseroan berkomitmen dan berupaya untuk meningkatkan kesejahteraan seluruh Insan Waskita sebagai bentuk apresiasi dan mendorong kinerja serta meningkatkan loyalitas pegawai terhadap Perseroan. Dengan kompensasi yang kompetitif, Perseroan berharap para pegawai dapat mengoptimalkan kemampuannya sesuai dengan tugas dan tanggung jawab yang telah diberikan. Oleh karena itu, dalam menetapkan pemberian upah, Perseroan memperhatikan ketentuan perundang-undangan terkait ketenagakerjaan yang mengatur pemberian upah pegawai baru tanpa membedakan jenis kelamin sesuai dengan Upah Minimum Regional (UMR) berdasarkan lokasi operasional Perseroan.

Perseroan menjamin setiap pegawai mendapat kesejahteraan yang layak dan sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku. Perseroan memberikan tunjangan dan fasilitas kesejahteraan kepada setiap pegawai di luar dari remunerasi yang diterima setiap bulannya dan melakukan program persiapan bagi pegawai yang akan memasuki masa pensiun selain program peningkatan kompetensi selama masih aktif bekerja. Perseroan juga memberikan jaminan pensiun kepada setiap pegawai yang akan mengalami masa purna tugas dan memberikan pembekalan kepada pegawai yang akan pensiun agar mampu melakukan perencanaan pasca pensiun.

As one of the Company's commitments in realizing good corporate governance, the employee recruitment process is carried out in accordance with prevailing laws and regulations and provides equal opportunities for all Indonesian people to participate in the recruitment process. The recruitment is done transparently, without any discrimination related to the elements of SARA (ethnicity, religion, race and inter-group), gender and disability as well as paying attention to the minimum age limit for prospective workers to ensure that there is no exploitation of minors. In addition, the Company also prohibits the application of forced labor so that employees who are recruited can carry out their work with full awareness and responsibility. The Company pays attention to empowering local communities based on the domicile of employees at the project location according to the needs and location of project.

With due regard to the remuneration policy, the Company pays attention to competitive aspect based on applicable regulations. The remuneration scheme refers to the competencies possessed by each individual, position and performance, and is determined based on the labor market which refers to the Key Performance Indicator (KPI) and is adjusted to Pay for Performance.

The Company is committed and strives to improve the welfare of all Waskita People as a form of appreciation and aimed at encouraging performance and increasing employee loyalty to the Company. With competitive compensation, the Company hopes that employees can optimize their capabilities according to respective assigned duties and responsibilities. Therefore, in determining the remuneration, the Company takes into account laws and regulations related to employment, which regulate the provision of wages for new employees without differentiating gender according to the Regional Minimum Wage (UMR) based on the Company's operational location.

The Company guarantees that every employee receives a decent welfare and is in accordance with applicable regulations. The Company provides allowances and welfare facilities to each employee apart from monthly remuneration, and carries out a preparation program for employees who are about to enter retirement in addition to competency development programs while still actively working. The Company also provides pension guarantees to every employee who is about to retire and prepares its retiring employees with skills and knowledge to support them in executing their retirement planning.
