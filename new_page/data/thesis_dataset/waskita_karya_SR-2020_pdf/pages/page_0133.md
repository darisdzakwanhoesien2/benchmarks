# Komitmen Anti Korupsi [205-1]

Sebagai bentuk komitmen Waskita dalam pencegahan korupsi, seluruh insan Perseroan telah menandatangani pernyataan kepatuhan terhadap Pedoman Perilaku (Code of Conduct) yang tertuang dalam bentuk Piagam Pakta Integritas sebagai bentuk komitmen seluruh Insan Waskita, baik Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, Committee, seluruh pegawai, maupun siapa saja yang bekerja di lingkungan Perseroan untuk mematuhi Pedoman Perilaku (Code of Conduct) Perseroan.

Perseroan telah mengembangkan kebijakan anti korupsi dan menjunjung tinggi standar etika. Untuk memperkuat komitmen dalam penegakan anti korupsi, Waskita telah membuat beberapa pedoman yang sebagai berikut:

- Kebijakan Laporan Harta Kekayaan Penyelenggara Negara (LHKPN) berdasarkan Surat Keputusan Board of Directors No. 23/SK/WK/2019 tanggal 29 November 2019 tentang Tata Cara Pendaftaran, Pengumuman dan Pemeriksaan Laporan Harta Kekayaan Penyelenggara Negara (LHKPN) di Lingkungan PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk;
- Kebijakan Anti Gratifikasi berdasarkan Surat Edaran Waskita No. 06/SE/WK/2017 tanggal 30 Maret 2017 tentang Pedoman dan Batasan Gratifikasi;
- Sistem Pelaporan Pelanggaran (Whistleblowing System) berdasarkan Surat Keputusan Board of Director No. 03.6/SK/WK/2019 tanggal 26 Maret 2019 tentang Pedoman Sistem Pelaporan Pelanggaran (SPP)/Whistleblowing System (WBS) PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk.

Komunikasi dan pelatihan tentang kebijakan dan prosedur antikorupsi tersebut dilakukan melalui lokarkarya, workshop dan pemasangan standing banner dan poster di lingkungan kantor pusat. Setiap insan Waskita wajib untuk menjunjung standar etika yang tinggi dan mengkampanyekan budaya Perseroan sehingga dapat meminimalisir tindakan korupsi yang dapat merugikan keuangan dan reputasi Perseroan.

Untuk meningkatkan kesadaran pentingnya perilaku anti korupsi pada setiap insan Waskita, Perseroan memfasilitasi kegiatan workshop penerapan Good Corporate Governance dengan mengundang pembicara dari eksternal perusahaan untuk memberikan materi dan pemahaman terkait upaya pencegahan korupsi dan dampaknya bagi personal dan perusahaan [205-2].

# Anti-Corruption Commitment [205-1]

As a form of Waskita's commitment to preventing corruption, all Company personnel have signed a statement of compliance with the Code of Conduct which is stated in the Integrity Pact Charter as a form of commitment of all Waskita employees, including Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, Committee, and all employees, as well as anyone who works in the Company to comply with the Company's Code of Conduct.

The Company has prepared anti-corruption policy and upholds ethical standards. To strengthen its commitment to anti-corruption enforcement, Waskita has made several guidelines as follows:

- State Official Wealth Report (LHKPN) based on the Decree of Board of Directors No. 23/SK/WK/2019 dated November 29, 2019 concerning Procedures for Registration, Announcement and Examination of State Official Wealth Report (LHKPN) within PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk;
- Anti-Gratification Policy based on Waskita Circular Letter No. 06/SE/WK/2017 dated March 30, 2017 concerning Guidelines and Limits of Gratuities;
- Whistleblowing System based on the Decree of Board of Directors No. 03.6/SK/WK/2019 dated March 26, 2019 concerning Guidelines for PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk's Whistleblowing System (WBS).

Communication and training on anti-corruption policies and procedures is carried out through trainings, workshops and the installation of standing banners and posters at the head office. Every Waskita people is obliged to uphold high ethical standards and campaign for the Company's culture so as to minimize acts of corruption that can harm the Company's finances and reputation.

To increase awareness of the importance of anti-corruption behavior in every Waskita people, the Company facilitates workshop on Good Corporate Governance by inviting external speakers to give materials and understanding regarding efforts to prevent corruption and its impact on individuals and the company [205-2].
