Perseroan juga senantiasa melakukan pengawasan terhadap efektivitas saluran pengaduan pelanggaran dan menjaga seluruh elemen dalam penerapan sistem pelaporan pelanggaran dengan melakukan sosialisasi dan komunikasi secara berkala, memberikan penghargaan bagi pelapor yang terbukti kebenarannya, memantau efektivitas dan perbaikan program serta melakukan benchmarking untuk mengukur efektivitas pelaksanaan sistem pelaporan pelanggaran dibandingkan dengan perusahaan lain.

# Membangun Budaya Berkelanjutan

Waskita membangun budaya secara berkelanjutan yang diawali dengan komitmen dari Top Management yang kemudian didiseminasikan kepada seluruh pegawai Waskita. Selanjutnya untuk mendorong implementasi, Perseroan melaksanakan program-program budaya yang terdiri dari programmatic dan customized program yang dilaksanakan di setiap Unit dengan melibatkan seluruh pegawai. Selain itu, untuk memastikan implementasi budaya, Perseroan melakukan penilaian perilaku budaya perusahaan serta menunjuk pegawai terpilih untuk menjadi change agent yang membantu dalam implementasi budaya perusahaan secara menyeluruh dan berkelanjutan.

The Company has facilitated the Whistleblowing System to prevent practices that are contrary to Waskita's Code of Conduct. The whistle blowing system is applied by prioritizing the principle of transparency and providing security assurance for whistleblowers. The policy regarding the Whistleblowing System has been regulated in the Decree of Board of Directors No. 03.6/SK/WK/2019 dated March 26, 2019, regarding PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk's Guidelines for the Whistleblowing System (WBS).

To minimize and prevent the likelihood of reputational risks and to increase public trust in Waskita, the Company tries to respond quickly, responsively and appropriately and follow up on all complaints of violations from stakeholders in accordance with applicable procedures. The Company also continuously monitors the effectiveness of channel for complaints of violations and maintains all elements in the application of the whistleblowing system by conducting regular outreach and communication, giving awards to whistleblowers who are proven to be true, monitoring the effectiveness and improvement of program and then conducting benchmarking to measure the effectiveness of the whistleblowing system through comparison with other companies.

Throughout 2020, there were 12 (twelve) incoming reports that have been followed up (closed). These violations are still within reasonable limits and have no impact on the reputation and performance of the Company.

# Building a Sustainable Culture

Waskita builds a culture in a sustainable manner that begins with a commitment from Top Management and then disseminated to all Waskita employees. Furthermore, to encourage the implementation, the Company organizes cultural programs consisting of programmatic and customized programs in each unit by involving all employees. In addition, to ensure the culture is implemented, the Company assesses the behavior of corporate culture and appoints selected employees to become agent of change and is tasked to assist in the implementation of corporate culture in a comprehensive and sustainable manner.
