## Management Walkthrough di Surabaya
Management Walkthrough in Surabaya

Waskita menggelar Management Walkthrough di Surabaya, Jawa Timur, yang diikuti oleh 52 pegawai dari lima proyek Waskita di Jawa Tengah dan Jawa Timur yaitu Jalan Tol KLBM (Krian-Legundi-Bunder-Manyar), renovasi Bandara Juanda, Jalan Tol Pasuruan-Probolinggo, Jalan Tol Colomadu, dan Jalan Tol Probolinggo-Banyuwangi.

Waskita held a Management Walkthrough in Surabaya, East Java, which was attended by 52 employees from five Waskita projects in Central Java and East Java, namely the KLBM (Krian-Legundi-Bunder-Manyar) Toll Road, renovation of Juanda Airport, Pasuruan-Probolinggo Toll Road, Colomadu Toll Road, and Probolinggo-Banyuwangi Toll Road.
