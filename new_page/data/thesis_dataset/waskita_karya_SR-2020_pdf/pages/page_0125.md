# STRUKTUR TATA KELOLA DAN ORGAN PERUSAHAAN [102-18] [102-22]

Governance Structure and Corporate Bodies [102-18] [102-22]

![img-192.jpeg](img-192.jpeg)

## Struktur Tata Kelola [102-18]

Berdasarkan Undang Undang No. 40 tahun 2007 tentang Perseroan Terbatas, Struktur Tata Kelola terdiri dari Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS), Board of Commissioners, dan Board of Directors. Board of Commissioners dibantu oleh organ pendukung seperti Board of Commissioners Committee dan Board of Directors dibantu oleh organ pendukung seperti Corporate Secretary dan Internal Audit.

## Governance Structure [102-18]

Based on Law No. 40 of 2007 concerning Limited Liability Companies, the Governance Structure consists of General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS), Board of Commissioners, and Board of Directors. Board of Commissioners is assisted by supporting bodies such as Board of Commissioners Committee and Board of Directors assisted by supporting bodies such as Corporate Secretary and Internal Audit.
