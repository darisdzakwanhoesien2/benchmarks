# Strategi Keberlanjutan untuk Membangun Negeri [1]

Komitmen Perseroan untuk turut berpartisipasi dalam membangun negeri telah kami canangkan dalam strategi keberlanjutan usaha agar dapat menjawab peluang dan tantangan yang dihadapi sepanjang tahun 2020 yang memiliki dampak dan manfaat terhadap kinerja Perseroan baik ekonomi, sosial dan lingkungan [4.a.1].

Strategi Keberlanjutan tersebut kami manifestasikan dengan memperkuat Tata Kelola, Risk, dan Culture yang mengintegrasikan Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) dalam setiap proses bisnis melalui pemantauan indikator yang bersifat high risk serta melakukan perbaikan pada proses inti dan penguatan culture, competencies and capabilities secara berkelanjutan [4.c.1].

# Membangun Negeri Melalui Kinerja Ekonomi

Pertumbuhan kinerja yang berkelanjutan merupakan prestasi Waskita dalam mempertahankan reputasinya sebagai perusahaan besar di bidang konstruksi. Hal ini tentunya menjadi prestasi yang menggembirakan ditengah kondisi perekonomian global dan nasional yang lemah akibat dampak pandemi Covid-19 yang terjadi, Perseroan masih dapat mencatatkan pertumbuhan yang positif selama tahun 2020 dan mampu mempertahankan posisinya sebagai market leader dalam industri konstruksi nasional [4.a.5] [4.c.3].

Di tengah kondisi pandemi Covid-19 yang melanda tanah air, Perseroan mampu mempertahankan reputasinya sebagai perusahaan besar di bidang konstruksi walaupun dampaknya sangat dirasakan oleh Perseroan atau setiap perusahaan. Pendapatan Usaha Perseroan pada tahun 2020 turun sebesar 48,42% atau setara dengan Rp15.196,93 miliar dan aset turun sebesar 13,87% atau setara dengan Rp17.000,30 miliar. [4.a.4] [4.b.1]

Untuk mempertahankan pertumbuhan kinerja secara berkelanjutan, Perseroan terus mengembangkan portofolio bisnisnya dan meningkatkan daya saing di pasar eksternal. Pada tahun 2020, Perseroan mengikuti lelang sebanyak 105 buah proyek dengan nilai total sebesar Rp39,38 triliun dan berhasil memenangkan 37 lelang proyek dengan mencatatkan nilai proyek baru sebesar Rp21,46 triliun. Hal ini mencerminkan daya saing Perseroan yang cukup baik dalam satuan nilai proyek sebesar 54,59%.

# Sustainability Strategy for Building the Nation [1]

The Company's commitment to participate in building the nation has been prepared in a business sustainability strategy, aiming to answer the opportunities and challenges faced throughout 2020 and will have an impact and benefit on the Company's economic, social and environmental performance [4.a.1].

We manifest this Sustainability Strategy by strengthening Governance, Risk, and Culture that integrates Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) in every business process through monitoring high risk indicators as well as making improvements to core processes and strengthening culture, competencies and capabilities in a sustainable manner [4.c.1].

# Building the Nation through Economic Performance

Continuous performance growth is one of Waskita's achievements in maintaining its reputation as a major construction company. This is certainly an encouraging achievement, given that amidst the weak global and national economic conditions due to the impact of the Covid-19 pandemic, the Company was still able to record positive growth during 2020 and managed to maintain its position as a market leader in the national construction industry [4.a.5] [4.c.3].

In the midst of the Covid-19 pandemic that hit the country, the Company was able to maintain its reputation as a large company in the construction sector, even though the impact was deeply felt by the Company or other companies. The Company's revenues in 2020 decreased by 48.42% or Rp15,196.93 billion and assets decreased by 13.87% or Rp17,000.30 billion. [4.a.4] [4.b.1]

To maintain sustainable performance growth, the Company continues to develop its business portfolio and increase competitiveness in external markets. In 2020, the Company participated in the auction of 105 projects with a total value of Rp39.38 trillion and won 37 project auctions by recording new project value of Rp21.46 trillion. This reflects the Company's good competitiveness in terms of project value at 54.59%.
