## Perubahan Signifikan pada Organisasi [GRI 102-10] [3.f]

Perseroan melakukan perubahan struktur organisasi berdasarkan Surat Keputusan Board of Directors PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk No. 02/SK/WK/2020 tanggal 22 Desember 2020 tentang Revisi Perubahan Nomenklatur Organisasi dan Jabatan pada Struktur Organisasi Perusahaan PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk. Hingga akhir tahun 2020, Perseroan tidak melakukan perubahan terhadap lokasi usaha dan struktur modal saham, namun demikian Waskita sebagai Holding Company telah melakukan penataan aktivitas operasional seluruh Anak Perusahaan dan Bisnis Unit dengan menempatkannya di Waskita Rajawali Tower yang merupakan kerjasama dengan PT Rajawali Nusantara Indonesia (RNI) sebagai pemilik lahan. Dengan demikian Gedung Waskita Heritage diperuntukan hanya untuk kegiatan operasional Kantor Pusat.

## Pendekatan Atau Prinsip Pencegahan [102-11]

Dalam rangka mewujudkan pengelolaan bisnis yang sehat dan mencapai pertumbuhan berkelanjutan, Perseroan melakukan kajian terhadap penerapan pengendalian internal yang dilakukan oleh Auditor Eksternal secara periodik untuk memberikan pertimbangan atas penelaahan pengendalian internal dan merumuskan upaya mitigasi terhadap berbagai risiko yang dihadapi Perseroan. Risk Management Division Korporat mengkoordinir dan memantu upaya mitigasi risiko dan proses evaluasinya dilakukan oleh Internal Audit dan diawasi oleh Committee yang berada di bawah Board of Commissioners.

## Significant Change in Organization [GRI 102-10] [3.f]

The Company made some changes to its organizational structure based on the Decree of Board of Directors of PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk No. 02/SK/WK/2020 dated December 22, 2020 concerning the Revision of Changes to Organization Nomenclature and Position in the Company Organizational Structure of PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk. Until the end of 2020, the Company has not made changes to its business location and share capital structure, however, Waskita as a Holding Company has arranged the operational activities of all Subsidiaries and Business Units by placing them in Waskita Rajawali Tower which is a collaboration with PT Rajawali Nusantara Indonesia (RNI) as the land owner. Therefore, Waskita Heritage Building is intended only for operational activities of Head Office.

## Prevention Approaches or Principles [102-11]

In order to realize sound business management and achieve sustainable growth, the Company conducts periodic reviews of the implementation of internal control by External Auditor to provide consideration for the internal control review and formulate mitigation efforts against various risks faced by the Company. The Corporate Risk Management Division coordinates and assists risk mitigation efforts and the evaluation process is carried out by Internal Audit and is supervised by a Committee under Board of Commissioners.
