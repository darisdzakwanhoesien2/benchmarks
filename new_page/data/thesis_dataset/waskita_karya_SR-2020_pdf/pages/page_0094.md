## Kebijakan Remunerasi &amp; Upah Layak [102-35, 202-1, 405-2]

Kebijakan remunerasi Waskita memperhatikan aspek kompetitif berdasarkan ketentuan yang berlaku. Penerapan skema remunerasi mengacu pada kompetensi yang dimiliki oleh setiap individu (people), posisi jabatan (position), dan kinerja (performance). Penetapan skema remunerasi diatur berdasarkan pasar tenaga kerja yang mengacu pada Key Performance Indicator (KPI) dan disesuaikan dengan Pay for Performance.

Perseroan memperhatikan dan berupaya untuk meningkatkan kesejahteraan seluruh Insan Waskita sebagai bentuk apresiasi dan mendorong kinerja serta meningkatkan loyalitas pegawai terhadap Perseroan. Dengan kompensasi yang kompetitif, Perseroan berharap para pegawai dapat mengoptimalkan kemampuannya sesuai dengan tugas dan tanggung jawab yang telah diberikan.

Oleh karena itu, dalam menetapkan pemberian upah, Waskita memperhatikan ketentuan perundang-undangan terkait ketenagakerjaaan yang mengatur pemberian upah pegawai baru tanpa membedakan jenis kelamin sesuai dengan Upah Minimum Regional (UMR) berdasarkan lokasi operasional Perseroan. Perseroan memberikan upah kepada pegawai di atas angka UMR. Rasio Upah Minimum Regional (UMR) DKI dibanding upah yang diterima pegawai baru Waskita di DKI Jakarta adalah 1:1,49% dari UMR.

## Remuneration &amp; Living Wages Policy [102-35, 202-1, 405-2]

Waskita's remuneration policy pays attention to competitive aspects based on applicable regulations. The remuneration scheme refers to the competencies possessed by each People, Position and Performance. The remuneration scheme is determined based on the labor market which refers to the Key Performance Indicator (KPI) and is adjusted to Pay for Performance.

The Company considers and strives to improve the welfare of all Waskita People as a form of appreciation and to encourage performance as well as to increase employees' loyalty to the Company. With competitive compensation, the Company hopes that employees can optimize their abilities according to their assigned duties and responsibilities.

Therefore, in determining the payment of wages, Waskita takes into account the provisions of laws and regulations related to employment which regulate the provision of wages for new employees regardless of gender according to the Regional Minimum Wage (UMR) based on the Company's operational location. The Company's employee wages are above the UMR. The ratio of Regional Minimum Wage (UMR) to the wages received by new Waskita employees in DKI Jakarta is 1: 1.49% of the UMR.
