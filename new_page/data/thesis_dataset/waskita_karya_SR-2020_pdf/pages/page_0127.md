# Organ Perusahaan [102-23]

Organ utama perusahaan yang terdiri dari RUPS, Board of Commissioners dan Board of Directors memiliki peran utama di dalam menjalankan penerapan tata kelola di Perseroan. Dengan demikian antar organ Perseroan memiliki fungsi, tugas dan tanggung jawab yang jelas dan diatur sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku dan Anggaran Dasar Waskita. Sedangkan organ pendukung Board of Commissioners memiliki peran dalam membantu Board of Commissioners dalam menjalankan fungsi pengawasan dan organ pendukung Board of Directors memiliki peran membantu Board of Directors dalam menjalankan operasional perusahaan.

Perseroan telah menunjuk seorang anggota Board of Directors sebagai penanggung jawab dalam penerapan dan pemantauan Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik melalui Surat Keputusan Board of Directors PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk No. 02/SK/WK/2020 tanggal 22 Desember 2020 tentang Revisi Perubahan Nomenklatur Organisasi dan Jabatan pada Struktur Organisasi Perusahaan PT Waskita Karya (Persero).

# Corporate Bodies [102-23]

The Corporate main bodies, which consist of the GMS, Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors, has a major role in carrying out the implementation of governance in the Company. Thus, the Corporate bodies have clear functions, duties and responsibilities and are regulated in accordance with the prevailing provisions and Waskita's Articles of Association. Meanwhile, Board of Commissioners supporting bodies have a role in assisting Board of Commissioners in carrying out the supervisory function and Board of Directors supporting bodies have the role of assisting Board of Directors in carrying out the Company's operations.

The Company has appointed a member of Board of Directors as the person in charge of the implementation and monitoring of Good Corporate Governance through the Decree of Board of Directors of PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk No. 02/SK/WK/2020 dated December 22, 2020 concerning the Revision of Changes in Organization Nomenclature and Position in the Company Organizational Structure of PT Waskita Karya (Persero).

|  Organ Utama Perseroan Corporate Main Organs | Tugas dan Tanggungjawab Duties and Responsibilities  |
| --- | --- |
|  Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham General Meetings of Shareholders | Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham merupakan organ Perseroan yang mempunyai wewenang yang tidak diberikan kepada Board of Commissioners maupun Board of Directors. Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham mengambil keputusan terkait dengan kebijakan Perseroan dan penilaian terhadap Board of Commissioners dan Board of Directors melalui Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS). The General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) is the Corporate body having the authority not granted to Board of Commissioners or Board of Directors. General Meeting of Shareholders takes decisions related to Corporate policies and assessments of Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors through the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS).  |
|  Board of Commissioners | Board of Commissioners merupakan organ Perseroan yang bertugas dan bertanggung jawab secara kolektif untuk melakukan pengawasan dan memberikan nasihat kepada Board of Directors serta memastikan bahwa perusahaan melaksanakan GCG secara konsisten. Board of Commissioners is the Corporate body that is collectively assigned and responsible for supervising and providing advice to Board of Directors and ensuring that the Company implements GCG consistently.  |
|  Board of Directors | Board of Directors merupakan organ Perseroan yang bertugas dan bertanggung jawab atas pengurusan Waskita untuk kepentingan Perseroan sesuai dengan maksud dan tujuan Perseroan yang ditetapkan dalam Anggaran Dasar. Dalam melaksanakan tugasnya, Board of Directors bertanggung jawab kepada RUPS. Pertanggungjawaban Board of Directors kepada RUPS merupakan perwujudan akuntabilitas pengelolaan perusahaan sesuai dengan prinsip-prinsip GCG. Board of Directors is the Corporate body that is assigned and responsible for the management of Waskita for the benefit of the Company in accordance with its purposes and objectives as stipulated in the Articles of Association. In carrying out its duties, Board of Directors is responsible to the GMS. The responsibility of Board of Directors to the GMS is a manifestation of the Company's management accountability in accordance with the GCG principles.  |
