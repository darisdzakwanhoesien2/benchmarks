# Mewujudkan Green Office dan Green Building Waskita [103-2]

Green Office dan Green Building Waskita merupakan komitmen Perseroan dalam mendukung program Sustainable Development melalui pengutamaan prinsip ramah lingkungan dan penghematan energi. Upaya untuk menciptakan Green Office telah dilakukan oleh Perseroan dengan berupaya menerapkan berbagai program penghematan, seperti hemat kertas penghematan listrik serta hemat air melalui sosialisasi dan himbauan kepada seluruh pegawai.

Waskita khususnya kantor pusat telah melakukan pengelolaan limbah/sampah dengan memisahkan sesuai jenisnya yaitu organik, non organik dan B3. Limbah/sampah organik dan non organik diangkut ke TPA bekerja sama dengan perusahaan yang memiliki izin pengangkutan sampah. Sedangkan limbah B3, pengelolaannya bekerja sama dengan perusahaan yang memiliki izin pengelolaan limbah B3. Limbah/sampah organik dan non organik diangkut setiap satu hingga dua hari sekali, sedangkan limbah B3 diangkut setiap enam bulan sekali [306-2] [306-4].

Waskita juga melakukan beberapa tindakan dalam mengupayakan penggunaan material yang ramah lingkungan antara lain dengan tidak menggunakan chloro fluoro carbon dan hallon untuk bahan Alat Pemadam Api Ringan (APAR), tidak menggunakan pembungkus makanan dari styrofoam, tidak menggunakan bahan asbes untuk pembuatan atap kantor dan barak pekerja, memaksimalkan penggunaan produk bahan yang sudah memiliki (terserifikasi) ISO 9001 &amp; ISO 14001, mengurangi pemakaian air minum dalam kemasan serta menggunakan freon AC dengan ODP=0 agar tidak merusak lapisan ozon.

The components that are managed and monitored are related to air and noise quality, clean and surface water quality, solid waste/garbage, public health management and socio-economic management.

As a form of Waskita's commitment to realizing environmentally sound project works, the Company has a permit from the Provincial Government of DKI Jakarta to temporarily store Hazardous and Toxic Waste (B3) based on the Decree of Governor of DKI Jakarta Province No. 62/2010 concerning Temporary Storage Permits for Hazardous and Toxic Waste (B3) and carrying out reforestation and tree planting activities in each project environment. The Company also selects new suppliers using environmental criteria as a form of Waskita's concern in realizing environmentally friendly suppliers [308-1].

# Creating Waskita Green Office and Green Building [103-2]

Waskita Green Office and Green Building are the Company's commitment in supporting the Sustainable Development program by prioritizing environmentally friendly and energy saving principles. Efforts to create a Green Office have been carried out by the Company by trying to implement a number of savings programs, such as saving paper, saving electricity and saving water through outreach and appeals to all employees.

Waskita, especially the Head Office, has carried out waste management by separating according to its type, namely organic, non-organic and B3. Organic and non-organic waste is transported to the landfills in collaboration with a company that has a waste transport permit. Meanwhile, B3 waste is managed in collaboration with a company that has a B3 waste management permit. Organic and non-organic waste is transported every one to two days, while B3 waste is transported once every six months [306-2] [306-4].

Waskita has also taken several actions in striving for the use of environmentally friendly materials, including not using chloro fluoro carbon and hallon for light fire extinguishers (APAR), not using food packaging made from styrofoam, not using asbestos materials for making office roofs and workers' barracks, maximizing the use of material products that already have (certified) ISO 9001 &amp; ISO 14001, reducing the use of bottled drinking water and using AC Freon with ODP = 0 so as not to damage the ozone layer.
