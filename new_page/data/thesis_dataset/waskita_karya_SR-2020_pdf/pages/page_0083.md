# ECONOMIC PERFORMANCE FOR SUSTAINABILITY

![img-175.jpeg](img-175.jpeg)

## Kinerja Perusahaan

Di tengah kondisi pandemi Covid-19 yang melanda tanah air, Perseroan mampu mempertahankan reputasinya sebagai perusahaan besar di bidang konstruksi walaupun dampaknya sangat dirasakan oleh Perseroan atau setiap perusahaan. Pendapatan Usaha Perseroan pada tahun 2020 turun sebesar 48,42% atau setara dengan Rp15.196,93 miliar dan aset turun sebesar 13,87% atau setara dengan Rp17.000,30 miliar.

## Company Performance

In the midst of the Covid-19 pandemic that hit the country, the Company was able to maintain its reputation as a large company in the construction sector, even though the impact was deeply felt by the Company or other companies. The Company's revenues in 2020 decreased by 48.42% or Rp15.196.93 billion and assets decreased by 13.87% or Rp17,000.30 billion.
