## Para Pemangku Kepentingan yang Terhormat,

Tahun 2020 menjadi tantangan bagi kita bersama, dimana pandemi Covid-19 yang terjadi menjadikan Perseroan harus merubah rencana kerja dan aktivitas usaha agar bisnis tetap bisa berjalan secara berkelanjutan. Hal ini tentunya menjadi tantangan bagi Perseroan agar mampu mewujudkan program NAWACITA untuk menciptakan konektivitas nasional dan pertumbuhan ekonomi yang berkelanjutan melalui pembangunan infrastruktur yang komprehensif. Untuk itu, kami terus melakukan transformasi melalui perluasan pasar konstruksi dalam rangka meningkatkan nilai dan daya saing Perseroan dengan mengedepankan keseimbangan antara aspek sosial (people), bisnis (profit), dan lingkungan (planet).

Kami bersyukur di tengah kondisi pandemi yang masih berlangsung, Perseroan tetap dapat menyajikan Laporan Keberlanjutan yang disusun berdasarkan GRI Standar dan POJK Nomor 51/POJK.03/2017 tentang Penerapan Keuangan Berkelanjutan bagi Lembaga Jasa Keuangan, Emiten, dan Perusahaan Publik. Laporan ini merupakan bentuk komitmen keterbukaan informasi Perseroan sebagai perusahaan terbuka dalam menyampaikan kinerja Ekonomi, Sosial dan Lingkungan kepada para pemangku kepentingan sekaligus kontribusi Perseroan dalam membangun negeri melalui pembangunan infrastruktur di tanah air [4.a.2]. [4.a.3].

## Our Honorable Stakeholders,

2020 was a challenging year for all of us, due to the occurrence of the Covid-19 pandemic that has forced the Company to change its work plans and business activities so that the business can continue to run in a sustainable manner. This is certainly a challenge for the Company, to be able to realize the NAWACITA program to create national connectivity and sustainable economic growth through comprehensive infrastructure development. To that end, we continue to transform through the expansion of construction market in order to increase the value and competitiveness of the Company by prioritizing a balance between social (people), business (profit) and environment (planet) aspects.

We are grateful that in the midst of ongoing pandemic, the Company still managed to present a Sustainability Report prepared based on GRI Standards and POJK 51/POJK.03/2017 concerning the Implementation of Sustainable Finance for Financial Service Institutions, Issuers and Public Companies. This report is a form of Company's commitment in disclosing information as a public company, to deliver its Economic, Social and Environmental performance to stakeholders as well as its contribution to building the nation through national infrastructure [4.a.2].
