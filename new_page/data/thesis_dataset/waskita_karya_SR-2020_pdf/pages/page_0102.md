## Membangun SDM yang Berkualitas [103-2, 103-3]

Perseroan berkomitmen untuk menciptakan SDM yang berkualitas dengan melakukan program pengembangan kompetensi SDM melalui 4 (empat) fase yakni fase persiapan, lepas landas, pengembangan, dan fase pengembangan berkelanjutan.

## Building High-Quality Human Capital [103-2, 103-3]

The Company is committed to creating high-quality Human Capital by carrying out the human capital competency development programs through 4 (four) phases, namely preparation, take-off, development and sustainable development phases.
