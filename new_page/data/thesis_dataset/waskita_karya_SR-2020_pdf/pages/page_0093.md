Dalam proses rekrutmen, Perseroan memperhatikan batas usia minimum bagi calon pekerja agar memastikan tidak adanya eksploitasi anak dibawah umur. Selain itu, Perseroan juga melarang penerapan kerja paksa sehingga pekerja harus melaksanakan pekerjaan dengan penuh kesadaran dan kemauan diri [408-01].

Perseroan menerapkan larangan keras terhadap pekerja anak. Perseroan memberikan kesempatan bagi Pelajar di tingkat SMK untuk melakukan program magang selama 3 (tiga) bulan di area perkantoran. Program magang tersebut merupakan kerjasama Perseroan dengan sekolah untuk memberikan kesempatan bagi pelajar mengenal kegiatan kerja di Perseroan.

In the recruitment process, the Company pays attention to the minimum age limit for prospective workers to ensure that there is no minors exploitation. In addition, the Company also prohibits the application of forced labor so that employees must carry out their work with full awareness and self-determination [408-01].

The Company enforces strict prohibition on child labor. The Company opens opportunities for students at Vocational High School level for 3 (three) months apprenticeship program in office areas. The apprenticeship program is a collaboration between the Company and schools to provide opportunities for students to get to know work activities in the Company.
