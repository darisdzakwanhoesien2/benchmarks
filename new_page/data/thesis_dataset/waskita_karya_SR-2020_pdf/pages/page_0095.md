## Kesejahteraan Pegawai

Perseroan menjamin setiap pegawai mendapat kesejahteraan yang layak dan sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku. Perseroan memberikan tunjangan dan fasilitas kesejahteraan kepada setiap pegawai di luar dari remunerasi yang diterima setiap bulannya, antara lain [401-2]:

- Tunjangan Hari Raya (THR);
- Tunjangan cuti;
- Bantuan uang duka bila pegawai atau keluarga mengalami musibah meninggal dunia;
- Jamsostek (jaminan kematian);
- Jaminan kecelakaan kerja;
- Jaminan hari tua;
- Medical check up;
- Family gathering;
- Jasa produksi;
- Cuti;
- Perawatan kesehatan;
- Penggantian kaca mata;
- Bantuan uang makan.

## Masa Purna Tugas [404-2]

Perseroan melakukan program persiapan bagi pegawai yang akan memasuki masa pensiun selain program peningkatan kompetensi selama masih aktif bekerja. Perseroan juga memberikan jaminan pensiun kepada setiap pegawai. Bagi pegawai yang akan mengalami masa purna tugas, Perseroan memberikan memberikan pembekalan kepada pegawai yang akan pensiun untuk mempersiapkan karir kedua para pegawai.

## Program Pensiun [201-3]

Perseroan menetapkan batas usia produktif pegawai sampai dengan usia 55 tahun. Batas usia tersebut merupakan usia masa kontrak kerja pegawai sejak awal masuk sebagai Pegawai Tidak Tetap (PTT) dan Pegawai Tetap (PT) sampai dengan memasuki usia Masa Persiapan Pensiun (MPP). MPP diberikan secara proporsional mulai 3, 6, 9 dan 12 bulan sesuai dengan masa kerja masing-masing.

## Employee Welfare

The Company guarantees that every employee gets sufficient welfare and is in accordance with applicable regulations. The Company provides allowances and welfare facilities to each employee apart from their monthly remuneration, among others [401-2]:

- Religious Holiday Allowance (THR);
- Leave Allowance;
- Funeral money assistance if an employee or family passed away;
- Jamsostek (Death Security (JKM));
- Work Accident Insurance;
- Old Age Security;
- Medical check up;
- Family gathering;
- Production services;
- Leave;
- Health care;
- Glasses reimbursement;
- Meal allowance.

## Retirement Period [404-2]

The Company carries out preparation program for employees who are about to enter retirement, in addition to competency improvement program while still actively working, the Company also provides pension guarantees to every employee. For employees who are about to enter retirement period, the Company provides a preparation for retiring employees to prepare for their second career.

## Pension Program [201-3]

The Company sets a limit for the employee productive age up to 55 years old. The age limit is the age of employee's work contract from their joining time as Non-Permanent Employee (PTT) and Permanent Employee (PT) until they enter into Pre-retirement Period (MPP). MPP is given proportionally from 3, 6, 9 and 12 months according to their respective tenure.
