## Rekrutmen dan Tingkat Turnover [401-1]

### Rekrutmen

Sebagai salah satu komitmen Perseroan dalam mewujudkan tata kelola perusahaan yang baik, pelaksanaan rekrutmen pegawai mengacu pada ketentuan dan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku. Perseroan memberikan kesempatan yang sama kepada seluruh masyarakat Indonesia untuk mengikuti proses rekrutmen. Rekrutmen dilakukan secara transparan dan tidak ada perlakuan diskriminasi terkait unsur SARA (Suku, Agama, Ras dan Antar Golongan), jenis kelamin dan disabilitas.

Rekrutmen yang dilakukan oleh Perseroan terdiri dari rekrutmen eksternal dan internal. Rekrutmen eksternal merupakan proses rekrutmen yang sumbernya dari luar Perseroan seperti rekrutmen Fresh Graduates/Management Trainee (MT) untuk mengisi jabatan entry level dan rekrutmen internal yang sumbernya dari dalam Perseroan seperti mutasi pegawai yang mencakup transfer dan promosi jabatan serta pengkaryaan pegawai kembali.

Program Management Trainee merupakan program rekrutmen yang dilakukan secara berkala untuk menjaring calon pegawai yang memiliki keahlian khusus dan kompetensi serta memiliki jiwa leadership.

The Company carries out manpower planning to support its performance. The manpower planning process is carried out by ensuring the short, medium and long-term human capital needs are in line with Waskita's vision and mission as well as the Corporate Long-Term Plan (RJPP).

## Recruitment and Turnover Rate [401-1]

### Recruitment

As one of the Company's commitments in realizing Good Corporate Governance, the employee recruitment refers to applicable laws and regulations. The Company provides equal opportunities to all Indonesian people to participate in the recruitment process. Recruitment is carried out in a transparent manner, with no discrimination related to the elements of SARA (Ethnicity, Religion, Race and Intergroup), gender and disability.

The recruitment carried out by the Company consists of external and internal recruitment. External recruitment is recruitment process uses sources from outside the Company such as Fresh Graduates/Management Trainees (MT) to fill the Entry Level Positions and Internal recruitment is recruitment process uses sources from inside the Company such as employee transfers and promotions as well as Employee re-employment.

The Management Trainee Program is a recruitment program that is carried out regularly to recruit prospective employees who have special skills and competencies and have a leadership spirit.
