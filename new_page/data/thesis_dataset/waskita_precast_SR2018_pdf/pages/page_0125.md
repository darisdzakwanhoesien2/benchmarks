## Effluent and Waste

The waste produced by the Company is liquid effluent production. The waste is produced from the spinning spun piles, batching plant washing process, and mixer truck. In the management of liquid waste, the Company is committed not to dispose of carelessly. Therefore, for handling liquid waste from the production process in the plant, the Company builds a liquid waste storage tank which functions as an WWTP with a pH control system, coagulation / clumping and neutralization. The water stored in the reservoir will be used again using a water pump to water the material in the stock yard. (103-2)

The drainage system in the plant consists of trenches, tubs of control, and public sewers whose size adapts to existing buildings. In addition to functioning as a drain, drainage is also used to prevent flooding in the activity area. ## Effluent dan Limbah

Limbah yang dihasilkan Perseroan adalah air limbah (effluent) produksi yang berbentuk cair. Limbah tersebut dihasilkan dari proses spinning spun piles, proses pencucian batching plant, dan truck mixer. Dalam pengelolaan limbah cair, Perseroan berkomitmen untuk tidak membuang sembarangan. Sebab itu, untuk penanganan limbah cair dari proses produksi di plant, Perseroan membangun bak penampung limbah cair yang berfungsi sebagai IPAL dengan sistem pengaturan pH, koagulasi/penggumpalan dan penetralan. Air yang ditampung dalam bak penampung akan digunakan kembali menggunakan pompa air untuk penyiraman material di stock yard. (103-2)

Sistem drainase di plant terdiri dari parit, bak kontrol, dan selokan umum yang ukurannya menyesuaikan dengan bangunan yang ada.
