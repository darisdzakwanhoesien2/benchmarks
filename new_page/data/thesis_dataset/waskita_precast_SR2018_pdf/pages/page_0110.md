GIVING THE BEST SERVICE TO THE NATION

- Pelatihan Mengenai Anti Korupsi
Pada tahun 2018, Perusahaan mengikuti sosialisasi mengenai pengendalian gratifikasi di lingkungan Perusahaan yang diselenggarakan oleh PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk bekerja sama dengan Komisi Pemberantasan Korupsi (KPK). Ini dilaksanakan dalam rangka program kegiatan Internalisasi Budaya Perusahaan serta meningkatkan pelaksanaan Good Corporate Governance (GCG) di lingkungan Perusahaan.

- Kegiatan lainnya
Berikut kegiatan lainnya yang dilakukan di tahun 2018 dalam bidang sosial dan kemasyarakatan, antara lain:
1. Bantuan buku untuk Paguyuban Perantauan Generasi Cacat
2. Bakti sosial "Peduli Kasih kesehatan Ibu"
3. Bantuan bencana kebakaran di Kembangan Utara, Jakarta Barat
4. Santunan anak yatim di Kantor Pusat dan Unit Produksi
5. Pengecoran lantai bangunan lapang tembak KostrAD
6. Pembangunan Taman Median Coastal Road
7. Bantuan Barrier untuk Pembangunan PAM
8. Bantuan kepada korban bencana gempa bumi dan tsunami di Palu, Sigi, dan Donggala
9. Bantuan kepada korban bencana gempa bumi di Lombok

- Anti-Corruption Training
In 2018, the Company participated in the socialization of the control of gratuities within the Company organized by the holding company, namely PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk in collaboration with the Corruption Eradication Commission (KPK). This socialization was carried out in the framework of the program to internalize the Corporate Culture and improve the implementation of Good Corporate Governance (GCG) within the Company.

- Other activities
The following are other activities carried out in 2018 in the social and community fields, including:
1. Book assistance for Disabled Migration Societies
2. Social service of "Peduli Kasih Kesehatan Ibu" ("Caring for Mother's Health")
3. Fire disaster assistance in Kembangan Utara, West Jakarta
4. Compensation of orphans at the Head Office and Production Unit
5. Casting the floor of the Kostrad firing field
6. Construction of the Coastal Road Median Park
7. Barrier Assistance for PAM Development
8. Assistance to victims of the earthquake and tsunami in Palu, Sigi and Donggala
9. Assistance to victims of the earthquake in Lombok
10. Mass circumcision of orphans
11. A free social service for cataract surgery
