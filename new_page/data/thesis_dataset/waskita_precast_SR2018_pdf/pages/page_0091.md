WHISTLEBLOWER POLICY SYSTEM (103-2, 285-1)

Adapun mekanisme penyampaian pelaporan pengaduan dapat dilayangkan, baik melalui Short Message Service (SMS) melalui nomor khusus yang telah disediakan dan secara tertulis dalam surat dengan alamat khusus. Laporan yang disampaikan pelapor sekurang-kurangnya memuat informasi mengenai data diri pelapor (nama, alamat, nomor telepon, faksimili, e-mail, satuan kerja).

## Sanksi Atas Pelanggaran Etika:

1. Setiap pegawai yang melakukan pelanggaran Prosedur Waskita Precast (PWP) di Bidang Etika dan Perilaku akan diberi sanksi sesuai dengan berat/ ringan, sifat, dan seringnya pelanggaran dilakukan.
2. Prosedur pemberian sanksi sesuai dengan Peraturan Perusahaan PT Waskita Beton Precast Tbk

## Keharusan Pelaporan terhadap Pelanggaran Etika:

1. Setiap pegawai wajib melaporkan terjadinya pelanggaran Prosedur Waskita Precast (PWP) di Bidang Etika dan Perilaku ke Departemen SDM &amp; Umum
2. Setiap pegawai yang melaporkan mengenai dugaan pelanggaran etika harus mengungkapkan identitasnya dengan jelas.
3. Kerahasiaan identitas pelapor harus dijaga, kecuali apabila diperlukan dalam tindak lanjut laporannya sesuai kebijakan perusahaan.
4. Tidak ada hukuman yang dijatuhkan kepada pelapor manakala pelanggaran tersebut benar terjadi, kecuali apabila yang bersangkutan juga terlibat dalam pelanggaran dan/atau laporannya tidak benar.
5. Departemen SDM &amp; Umum harus menindaklanjuti laporan tersebut sesuai batas kewenangannya.
6. Departemen SDM &amp; Umum harus melaporkan kepada Direksi untuk diambil tindakan sesuai Kebijakan Perusahaan.

As for the mechanism for reporting, complaints can be sent either through Short Message Service (SMS) to a special number provided and in writing in a special address. The report submitted by the reporter contains at least information about the reporter's personal data (name, address, telephone number, facsimile, e-mail, work unit).

## Sanctions for violations of Ethics:

1. Any employee that violates Waskita Precast Procedures in Ethics and Conduct will be sanctioned in accordance with the major/minor, nature, and the frequency of the violation.
2. Procedure for imposing sanctions is pursuant to the Company Regulation of Waskita Beton Precast Tbk.

## The obligation to report any violations of ethics:

1. Every employee is required to report any violations of Waskita Precast Procedure in Ethics and Conduct to Human Capital &amp; General Affairs Department
2. Any employee that reports the alleged violation of ethics must reveal his identity.
3. Confidentiality of the reporter's identity will be maintained, except as necessary in the follow-up report in accordance with the Company's policy.
4. There is no punishment that will be imposed on the whistleblower in the event that the reported violation did occur, except if the person concerned is also involved in the violation and/or the report was not true.
5. Human Capital &amp; General Affairs Department shall follow up the report in accordance with their scope of authority.
6. Human Capital &amp; General Affairs Department shall report to the Board of Directors to be subsequently dealt with according to the Company policy.
