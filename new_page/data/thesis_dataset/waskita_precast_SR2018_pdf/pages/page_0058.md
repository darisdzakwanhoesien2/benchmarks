# Informasi Mengenai Karyawan (102-8)
## Employee Composition (102-8)

Per 31 Desember 2018, jumlah pegawai Perusahaan tercatat sebanyak 1.508 orang, naik dibandingkan jumlah pegawai per 31 Desember 2017, yang tercatat sebanyak 1.503 orang.

Berdasarkan statusnya, karyawan sebanyak 1.508 orang tersebut di atas terbagi menjadi tiga kategori, yakni pegawai tetap, tidak tetap dan outsourcing; sedangkan berdasarkan wilayah kerjanya terbagi dalam dua kelompok besar, yakni mereka yang bekerja di Kantor Pusat (PK) dan Kantor di Daerah (KP). Dalam operasional sehari-hari, Perusahaan mempekerjakan pekerja pria dan wanita dan tidak membedakan pekerjaan berdasarkan jenis kelamin.

- Demografi Pegawai Berdasarkan Status dan Jenis Kelamin 2017-2018

As of December 31, 2018, the number of Company employees was 1,508, increase from the number of employees as of December 31, 2017, which was recorded at 1,503.

Based on its status, the 1,480 employees mentioned above are divided into three categories, namely permanent, temporary employees and outsourcing; while based on its working area is divided into two major groups, namely those who work in the Head Office (PK) and Regional Offices (KP). In daily operations, the Company employs male and female workers and does not distinguish work by gender.
