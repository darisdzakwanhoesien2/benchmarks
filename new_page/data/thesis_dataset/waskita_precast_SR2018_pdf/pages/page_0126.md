Sementara itu, limbah domestik dari aktivitas perkantor dan pekerja produksi dari buangan closet, peturasan, dan bidet, akan dialirkan ke dalam septic tank yang dilengkapi dengan sumur resapan. Adapun limbah cair dari floor drain kamar mandi dan wastafel, limbah akan dialirkan ke saluran pengumpul untuk kemudian dialirkan ke saluran drainase. (103-2)

Menyangkut pengelolaan sampah domestik dan limbah padat, Perseroan senantiasa menyediakan tempat pembuangan sampah yang cukup. Anjuran agar membuang sampah pada tempatnya tak pernah lupa terus disosialisasikan. Dengan demikian, maka area tapak dan perkantor akan tetap terjaga kebersihannya. Pada gilirannya, kebersihan lokasi kerja tersebut akan mendukung lingkungan kerja yang sehat dan nyaman. Tempat sampah yang tersedia seoptimal mungkin memudahkan karyawan dan pengunjung untuk memisahkan antara sampah organik dan non-organik. (103-2)

Sejalan dengan kinerja positif Perseroan, Waskita Beton Precast tak kendor semangat untuk semakin peduli dan berkontribusi dalam menjaga lingkungan. Untuk itu, Perseroan telah meraih ISO 14001 2015 - sertifikasi internasional untuk sistem manajemen lingkungan pada Desember 2018. Sertifikasi ini penting untuk diraih karena memiliki beragam manfaat bagi Perseroan. Misalnya, meningkatkan citra organisasi, meningkatkan kinerja lingkungan organisasi, meningkatkan penaatan terhadap ketentuan perundang-undangan pengelolaan lingkungan, mengurangi risiko usaha, dan meningkatkan daya saing.

The company also produces hazardous waste (B3), which is used oil, used batteries, used lights and others. To avoid negative impacts on the environment, B3 waste is transported by transporters who have obtained permission from the Ministry of Environment and the Ministry of Transportation to be delivered to beneficiaries and destruction of B3 waste that has obtained management permits from the Ministry of Environment. (103-3, 306-4)

Meanwhile, domestic waste from office activities and production workers from closet, drainage and bidet discharges, will be channeled into septic tanks equipped with infiltration wells. As for liquid waste from the floor drain of bathroom and sink, the waste will be channeled to the collection channel and then flowed to the drainage channel. (103-2)

Regarding the management of domestic waste and solid waste, the Company always provides sufficient landfills. The suggestion that the garbage is disposed of in its place never forgets to be socialized. Thus, the site and office area will be kept clean. In turn, the cleanliness of the work site will support a healthy and comfortable work environment. The available bins are as optimal as possible making it easier for employees and visitors to separate between organic and non-organic waste. (103-2)

In line with the Company's positive performance, Waskita Beton Precast has not relaxed its enthusiasm to care more and contribute to maintaining the environment. To that end, the Company has achieved ISO 14001 2015 - international certification for the environmental management system in December 2018. This certification is important to achieve because it has various benefits for the Company. For example, improving the image of the organization, improving the environmental performance of the organization, increasing compliance with the provisions of legislation on environmental management, reducing business risks, and increasing competitiveness.
