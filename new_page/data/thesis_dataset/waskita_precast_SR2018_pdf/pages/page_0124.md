2018 Sustainability Report
MEMBANGUN KEPEDULIAN TERHADAP KELESTARIAN LINGKUNGAN
BUILDING CARE FOR ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY

Penghematan juga dilakukan dalam penggunaan air tanah. Sebab air tanah merupakan sumber daya air yang keberadaannya terbatas, dan pengambilan tanpa perhitungan akan mengakibatkan kerusakan lingkungan. Spirit penghematan air, baik air PDAM maupun air tanah, dilakukan di plant maupun di kantor-kantor operasional Waskita Beton Precast.

Dengan upaya tersebut, penggunaan air PDAM selama tahun 2018 di batching plant tercatat sebanyak 150.630.709 liter. Pada tahun pelaporan, Perseroan tidak bisa menampilkan data pembanding tahun sebelumnya karena data tidak tersedia. Perbandingan akan disajikan pada Laporan tahun berikutnya, yakni tahun 2018 dan 2019.

Savings are also made in the use of ground water. Because ground water is a water resource whose existence is limited, and taking without calculation will result in environmental damage. The spirit of water saving, both PDAM water and ground water, is carried out at the plant and at the operational offices of Waskita Beton Precast.

With these efforts, the use of PDAM water during 2018 in the batching plant was recorded at 150,630,709 liter. In the reporting year, the Company was unable to display comparable data of the previous years due to the unavailability of such data. The comparison will be presented in the following year's report, which is 2018 and 2019.
