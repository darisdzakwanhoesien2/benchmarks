## Dampak Ekonomi Tidak Langsung

Keberadaan Waskita Beton Precast dalam pembangunan infrastruktur di Indonesia tidak sekadar mencari laba sebesar-besarnya. Dalam hal ini, Perseroan ingin agar keberadaannya juga bermanfaat bagi masyarakat di sekitarnya. Manfaat itu, antara lain, masyarakat lokal memiliki kesempatan untuk menjadi pekerja di lokasi-lokasi plant yang dimiliki Waskita Beton Precast, menjadi pemasok bahan baku material, atau mendapatkan bantuan melalui program tanggung jawab sosial perusahaan (Corporate Social Responsibility). (103-2)

Hingga 31 Desember 2018, Waskita Beton Precast merekrut tenaga kerja lokal sebanyak 3.752 orang. Mereka menduduki posisi sebagai staf teknik, dan staf non-teknik. Upaya merekrut pegawai dari orang lokal merupakan komitmen Perseroan untuk mengurangi tingkat pengangguran, sekaligus memberdayakan potensi tenaga lokal. Dengan terbukanya lapangan kerja di Waskita Beton Precast, maka kehidupan para pekerja tersebut akan meningkat.

Sementara itu, untuk pemasok bahan baku material, sepanjang tahun 2018, Perseroan menjalin kerjasama dengan 197 pemasok lokal dan 1 pemasok luar negeri pemasok di lokasi operasional plant. Bahan baku yang dipasok berupa pasir, semen, split, besi, PC bar, PC strand, iron wire dan lain-lain. Bahan baku material dari para pemasok itu menyumbang 99,51% dari total kebutuhan bahan baku Perseroan. (103-3, 204-1)

Untuk program CSR, sepanjang tahun 2018, Perseroan mengalokasikan dana sebesar Rp1,64 miliar. Waskita Beton Precast mengalokasikan dana tersebut sebagai perwujudan dan tanggung jawabnya dalam menjalankan amanat undang-undang, seperti Undang-Undang No. 25 Tahun 2007 tentang Penanaman Modal, UU No. 40 Tahun 2007 tentang Perseroan Terbatas, serta ISO 26000 yang telah diratifikasi oleh Pemerintah pada tahun 2010 dan dijadikan Standar Nasional (SNI) pada 2012.

In order to create healthy competition, the Company also has a Code of Conduct that contains Waskita Beton Precast's attitude that supports the principles of fair business competition in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. The commitment of all the Company's employees to the Company's Code of Conduct results in the absence of legal action or sanctions from the Business Competition Supervisory Commission (KPPU) as an authority authorized to deal with alleged monopolistic practices and/or unfair business competition. (103-3, 206-1)

## Indirect Economic Impact

The existence of Waskita Beton Precast in infrastructure development in Indonesia is not merely seeking maximum profit. In this case, the Company wants its existence to also benefit the surrounding community. These benefits, among others, local people have the opportunity to become workers in plant locations owned by Waskita Beton Precast, become suppliers of raw materials, or get assistance through Corporate Social Responsibility programs. (103-2)

As of December 31, 2018, Waskita Beton Precast recruited 3,752 local workers. They occupy positions as technical staff, and non-technical staff. The effort to recruit employees from local people is the Company's commitment to reduce unemployment, while empowering the potential of local workers. With the opening of recruitments at Waskita Beton Precast, the welfare of these workers shall increase.

Meanwhile, for suppliers of raw materials, throughout 2018, the Company formed a partnership with 197 local suppliers and 1 foreign supplier at the plant's operational location. The raw materials supplied are in the form of sand, cement, split, iron, PC bars, PC strands, iron wire and others. Raw materials from these suppliers contribute 99,51% of the Company's total raw material needs. (103-3, 204-1)

For CSR programs, throughout 2018, the Company allocates funds of Rp1.64 billion. Waskita Beton Precast allocates these funds as a manifestation and responsibility in carrying out twining Investment, Law No. 40 of 2007 concerning Limited Liability Companies, as well as ISO 26000 which was ratified by the Government in 2010 and formed as the National Standard (SNI) in 2012.
