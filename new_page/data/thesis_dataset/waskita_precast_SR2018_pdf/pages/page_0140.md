## Program Management Trainee (MT)

Perseroan akan melakukan proses program Management Trainee (MT) untuk memenuhi kebutuhan karyawan jangka panjang dengan menyeleksi lulusan baru dari universitas/institusi pendidikan lainnya yang memiliki reputasi dan track record baik di dalam negeri dan di luar negeri. Program ini diselenggarakan secara terpusat dan dikoordinasikan oleh Departemen SDM, Sistem &amp; TI.

Dalam pelaksanaannya, peserta MT akan menempuh tahapan-tahapan orientasi dan pembelajaran materi yang ada di semua lingkup bisnis proses Perseroan, dan selanjutnya dilakukan penilaian atas kemampuan adaptasi dan implementasi. Total waktu yang diperlukan dalam masa MT ini adalah 6 (enam) bulan. Sampai dengan saat ini, Perseroan sudah bekerjasama dengan WASKITA dalam melakukan seleksi MT.

## e. Leadership Development Program for Junior Manager

## f. Leadership Development Program for Finance and Human Capital

Provides insight of management aspects of the plant/ batching plant related to the field of HC and Finance as well as how to apply those in the plant/ batching plant to be able to achieve the objectives of the Company. ## Management Trainee (MT) Program

The Company will conduct the process of Management Trainee (MT) program to fulfill the needs of long-term employees by selecting new graduates from universities/ other educational institutions that have a good reputation and track record both domestic and abroad, held centrally and coordinated by the Department of Human Capital, Systems &amp; IT.

In its implementation, participants will take the MT orientation stages and learning materials in all aspects of the business processes of the Company, and their capability to adapt and to implement will be evaluated. Total time required in this MT period is six (6) months. Until today, the Company has been working with WASKITA in the selection of MT.

## Labor Management

The Company is also responsible for the management of labor in terms of the implementation of government regulation regarding the Regional/City Minimum Wage ("UMR/UMK") and the Company also provides Death &amp; Occupational Accidents Insurance. In addition, the Company also provides Religious Holiday Allowance ("THR") and Production Incentive.
