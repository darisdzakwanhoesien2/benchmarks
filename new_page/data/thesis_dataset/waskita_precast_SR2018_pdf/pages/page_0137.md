Selama tahun 2018 pelatihan yang diselenggarakan adalah Teknisi Laboratorium Beton, Leading and Motivating with NLP, FINON, Safety Driving For Heavy Vehicle, Supply Chain Management, Maintenance &amp; Peralatan Batching Plant, Audit Intern Tingkat Dasar 2 dan lain-lain. Program pendidikan dan pelatihan terbuka bagi seluruh karyawan, baik laki-laki maupun perempuan, jenjang manajemen maupun staf, pegawai tetap maupun tidak tetap. Adapun total pelatihan selama tahun 2018 berdasarkan People Development Ratio Desember 2018, adalah 38,5 jam/orang/tahun. Berdasarkan jenis kelamin, jam pelatihan karyawan laki-laki adalah 34,7 jam/orang/tahun, lebih tinggi dibandingkan jam pelatihan karyawan perempuan, yang tercatat sebanyak 3,8 jam/orang/tahun. Sedangkan berdasarkan level jabatan, jam pelatihan staf adalah 34,7 jam/orang/tahun, lebih rendah dibandingkan level manajemen, yang tercatat sebanyak 3,8 jam/orang/tahun. (103-3, 404-1)

In addition, there is also a Training on Operational Permit (SIO) Batching Plant &amp; Concrete Pump, Training and Certification for Supervision of Building Prefabricated Concrete Construction, Preparing ISO 9001: 2015 Workshop, Strategy to Win Government Tender Through Electronic Systems (E-Procurement), Geo Talk I, Designing Training &amp; Development Collection Seminar, SOP Understanding &amp; Designing Training and Land Acquisition Seminar Assignment

During 2018 the trainings held were Concrete Laboratory Technician, Leading and Motivation with NLP, FINON, Safety Driving for Heavy Vehicles, Supply Chain Management, Maintenance &amp; Batching Plant Equipment, Basic Internal Audit 2 and others. The education and training program is open to all employees, both male and female, levels of management and staff, permanent and non-permanent employees. The total training during 2018 based on the December 2018 People Development Ratio, is 38.5 hours/person/year. Based on gender, the training hours of male employees are 34.7 hours/person/year, higher than the training hours of female employees, which are recorded at 3.8 hours/person/year. While based on position level, staff training hours are 34.7 hours/person/year, lower than management level, which is recorded as 3.8 hours/person/year. (103-3, 404-1)
