# Perekrutan dan Pergantian Karyawan

Per 31 Desember 2018, Perseroan memiliki 1.480 karyawan, turun dibanding tahun sebelumnya dengan jumlah karyawan sebanyak 1.503 orang. Jumlah tersebut merupakan akumulasi dari hasil rekrutmen dikurangi dengan jumlah karyawan yang meninggalkan Perseroan dengan berbagai alasan yang diperbolehkan undang-undang (turnover). (103-2)

Selama tahun pelaporan, Perseroan merekrut karyawan sebanyak 146 orang. Rekrutmen dilakukan di Kantor Pusat maupun di daerah-daerah tempat Waskita Beton Precast beroperasi. Hingga akhir tahun 2018, Perseroan mengoperasikan 11 pabrik (plant) precast, 73 pabrik ready mix/batching plant dan 5 stone crusher (quarry) yang memiliki lokasi yang strategis di dekat lokasi proyek-proyek yang dikerjakan Waskita Beton Precast. Rekrutmen terbuka untuk siapapun, tanpa mempertimbangkan suku, agama, ras maupun jenis kelamin.

# Employee Recruitment and Turnover

As of December 31, 2018, the Company had 1,480 employees, down from the previous year with 1,503 employees. This amount represents the accumulation of recruitment results minus the number of employees leaving the Company for various reasons allowed by the law (turnover). (103-2)

During the reporting year, the Company recruited 146 employees. Recruitment is carried out at the Head Office and in areas where Waskita Beton Precast operates. Until the end of 2018, the Company operated 11 precast plants, 73 ready mix / batching plant plants and 5 stone crusher (quarry) which have a strategic location near the projects' location carried out by Waskita Beton Precast. Recruitment is open to anyone, regardless of ethnicity, religion, race or gender.
