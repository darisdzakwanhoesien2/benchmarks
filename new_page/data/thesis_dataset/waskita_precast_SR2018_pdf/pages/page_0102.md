# Peran Waskita Beton Precast

Komitmen pemerintah menjadikan infrastruktur sebagai prioritas memberikan peluang besar bagi Waskita Beton Precast untuk turut serta dalam pembangunan tersebut. Kontrak demi kontrak berhasil diiraih Perseroan dan nilainya mampu mencapai target yang telah ditetapkan pada tahun 2018, yakni sebesar Rp6,6 triliun. Adapun total kontrak yang dikelola (order book) senilai Rp17,34 triliun, termasuk kontrak bawaan (carry over) tahun 2017 sebesar Rp 10,68 triliun. Terhadap kontrak-kontrak yang telah didapat, Perseroan berkomitmen untuk menyelesaikannya dengan sebaik mungkin sebagai bentuk pelayanan terbaik bagi bangsa. (103-2)

Target sebesar Rp6,6 triliun merupakan hasil revisi sebanyak dua kali yang dilakukan Perseroan pada tahun 2018. Pada awalnya, Waskita Beton Precast menargetkan kontrak baru sebesar Rp11,52 triliun. Namun, target itu diturunkan menjadi Rp8,3 triliun. Setelah itu, target kembali direvisi menjadi Rp8,3 triliun, dan terakhir dipatok sebesar Rp6,6 triliun. Revisi dilakukan sejalan dengan tender beberapa proyek utama yang diundur, seperti proyek jalan tol Probolinggo-Banyuwangi, jembatan Penajam Balikpapan, dan Proyek jalan tol Singosari-Batu.

Berdasarkan proyek yang ditangani, kapasitas produksi Perseroan selama tahun 2018 tercatat sebesar 3,5 juta ton, meningkat 7,7 persen dibanding tahun 2017, yang tercatat sebesar 3,25 juta ton. Selain menambah kapasitas produksi, Waskita Beton Precast juga melakukan pengembangan produk baru, antara lain, berupa rumah precast, tiang listrik beton, dan bantalan kereta api. (103-2)

Perolehan kontrak baru berasal dari proyek internal sebesar 63%, yaitu jalan tol Cibitung-Cilincing dan proyek lainnya. Sedangkan proyek yang berasal dari eksternal sebesar 37%, antara lain, proyek jalan tol Pekanbaru-Dumai, Bandara Kulonprogo, Pelabuhan Pattimban dan proyek lainnya. Sementara itu, proyek yang sudah dirampungkan di antaranya proyek tol Becakayu, yang merupakan proyek turnkey pertama Perseroan. Proyek turnkey memiliki margin yang lebih besar dibandingkan non-turnkey. Namun demikian, sebagai kompensasinya, kontraktor harus siap memberikan pendanaan sampai proyek selesai. Selain proyek tol Becakayu, proyek lain yang sudah selesai pada tahun 2018 adalah LRT Palembang, Tol Cimanggis Cibitung Seksi 1, Tol Bekasi cawan Kampung melayu seksi 1, Tol Pejagan Pemalang, dan Tol Solo Kertosono.

# The Role of Waskita Beton Precast

The government's commitment to establish infrastructure as a priority provides a great opportunity for Waskita Beton Precast to participate in the development. The contract after contract was successfully achieved by the Company and its value was able to reach the target set in 2018, which amounted to Rp.6.6 trillion. As for the total managed contracts (order book) worth IDR 17.34 trillion, including carry-over contracts in 2017 of IDR 10.68 trillion. Regarding the contracts that have been obtained, the Company is committed to completing it as well as possible as the best form of service for the nation. (103-2)

The target of Rp.6.6 trillion is a revised result twice as done by the Company in 2018. At first, Waskita Beton Precast targeted a new contract of Rp11.52 trillion. However, the target was reduced to IDR 8.3 trillion. Then, the target was revised to Rp.8.3 trillion, and the last was pegged at Rp.6.6 trillion. The revision was carried out in line with the tenders of several major projects which were postponed, such as the Probolinggo-Banyuwangi toll road project, the Balikpapan Penajam bridge and the Singosari-Batu toll road project.

Based on the projects handled, the Company's production capacity during 2018 was recorded at 3.5 million tons, an increase of 7.7 percent compared to 2017, which was recorded at 3.25 million tons. In addition to increase production capacity, Waskita Beton Precast also developed new products, among others, in the form of precast houses, concrete electric poles, and railway bearings. (103-2)

The acquisition of new contracts comes from internal projects of 63%, namely the Cibitung-Cilincing toll road and other projects. While external projects amounted to 37%, including the Pekanbaru-Dumai toll road project, Kulonprogo Airport, Port of Pattimban and other projects. Meanwhile, the completed projects include the Becakayu toll road project, which is the Company's first turnkey project. Turnkey projects have a greater margin than non-turnkey. However, as compensation, the contractor must be prepared to provide funding until the project is completed. In addition to the Becakayu toll road project, other projects that have been completed in 2018 are Palembang LRT, Cimanggis Cibitung Section 1 Toll Road, Bekasi Toll Road, Kampung Melayu section 1, Pejagan Pemalang Toll Road, and Kertosono Solo Toll Road.
