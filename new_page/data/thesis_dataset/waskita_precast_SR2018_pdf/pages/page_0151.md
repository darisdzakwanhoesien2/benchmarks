# Memberikan Yang Terbaik Untuk Pelanggan

Giving The Best For Customers

Sistem struktur beton pracetak merupakan salah satu alternatif teknologi dalam perkembangan konstruksi di Indonesia. Dibanding sistem beton konvensional, sistem ini memiliki sejumlah kelebihan yakni mendukung efisiensi waktu, efisiensi energi dan lebih ramah lingkungan.

Sejalan dengan proyek yang ditangani, Waskita Beton Precast sebagai salah satu produsen beton precast, terus berupaya untuk meningkatkan kapasitas produksi dan melakukan pengembangan atau inovasi produk sehingga mampu memenuhi kebutuhan pelanggan. Per 31 Desember 2018, kapasitas produksi Perseroan tercatat sebesar 3,5 juta ton, meningkat 7,7 persen dibanding tahun 2017, yang tercatat sebesar 3,25 juta ton. Sementara itu, jumlah produknya ada sebanyak 24 jenis produk, termasuk empat produk baru, yakni sprig WP, RC Pipe, Railway Sleeper dan Electric Pole. (103-2)

The precast concrete structure system is one of the technological alternatives in the development of construction in Indonesia. Compared to conventional concrete systems, this system has a number of advantages that support time efficiency, energy efficiency and are more environmentally friendly.

In line with the projects handled, Waskita Beton Precast as one of the precast concrete manufacturers, continues to strive to increase production capacity and carry out product development or innovation so as to be able to meet customer needs. As of December 31, 2018, the Company's production capacity was 3.5 million tons, an increase of 7.7 percent compared to 2017, which was recorded at 3.25 million tons. Meanwhile, there are 24 types of products, including four new products, namely WP WP, RC Pipe, Railway Sleeper and Electric Pole. (103-2)
