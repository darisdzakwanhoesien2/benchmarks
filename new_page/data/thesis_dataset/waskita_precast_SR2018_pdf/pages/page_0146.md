# Angka Kecelakaan Kerja

Untuk memenuhi Standar Pelaksanaan K3, Waskita Beton Precast mewajibkan kepada seluruh petugas yang bertanggung jawab atas K3 Perseroan, memiliki sertifikat ahli K3 Umum. Proses sertifikasi bekerjasama dengan Lembaga Sertifikasi Ahli K3 yang terdaftar di Kementrian Ketenagakerjaan dan Transmigrasi. Program sertifikasi ini sangat penting untuk menciptakan budaya "zero accident" di Perseroan.

Upaya menuju zero accident merupakan komitmen yang tidak bisa ditawar-tawar. Untuk mewujudkan hal itu, sekaligus mencegah agar tidak terjadi insiden atau kecelakaan kerja, Perseroan menetapkan rencana aksi berupa penetapan sistem Safety Performance Indicator. Sistem ini akan menjadi acuan bagian unit kerja sebagai indikator pengelolaan keselamatan, kesehatan dan lingkungan hidup. Untuk mencapai indicator tersebut, Perseroan menetapkan langkah-langkah konkrit, seperti menerapkan sistem reward and punishment, safety observation program, safety leadership dan lain-lain. (103-2)

Untuk meminimalkan insiden kecelakaan kerja, sosialisasi ihwal pentingnya K3 juga terus dilakukan oleh Perseroan. Apalagi, hal itu sudah diatur dalam Peraturan Perusahaan PT Waskita Beton Precast, yang sudah disahkan melalui Surat Keputusan Direktur Jenderal Pembinaan Hubungan Industrial dan Jaminan Sosial Tenaga Kerja Nomor Kep.587/PHIJSK-PKKAD/PP/V/2015 tanggal 19 Mei 2015. Ketentuan tentang Keselamatan &amp; Kesehatan Kerja, Lingkungan, Mutu dan Pengamanan (K3LMP) diatur dalam Bab VI, pasal 35. Di dalamnya, antara lain, mengatur tentang kewajiban Perusahaan menyediakan alat-alat keselamatan &amp; kesehatan kerja dan lingkungan (K3LMP), dan pegawai wajib memakai dan memelihara alat-alat K3LMP yang disediakan oleh Perusahaan.

# Work Accidents Rate

To meet the OSH Implementation Standards, Waskita Beton Precast requires all officers responsible for the Company's OSH to have a General OSH expert certificate. The certification process is in collaboration with the OSH Expert Certification Institute registered with the Ministry of Manpower and Transmigration. This certification program is very important to create a "zero accident" culture in the Company.

The effort towards zero accident is a commitment that cannot be negotiated. In order to realize zero accident, while preventing work accidents or accidents from occurring, the Company sets out an action plan in the form of establishing a Safety Performance Indicator system. This system will become a reference for work unit parts as indicators of safety, health and environment management. To achieve these indicators, the Company sets concrete steps, such as implementing a system of reward and punishment, observation safety programs, safety leadership and others. (103-2)

To minimize the incidence of work accidents, the Company continued to socialize the importance of OSH. Moreover, this has been regulated in the Company Regulation of PT Waskita Beton Precast, which has been ratified through a Decree of the General of Industrial Relations Development and Labor Social Security Director Number Kep.587 / PHIJSK-PKKAD / PP / V / 2015 dated May 19, 2015. Provisions concerning Occupational Safety &amp; Health, Environment, Quality and Safety (K3LMP) regulated in Chapter VI, article 35. Consisted, among other things, regulates the Company's obligation to provide safety and health and environmental (K3LMP) tools, and employees must wear and maintain K3LMP tools provided by the Company.
