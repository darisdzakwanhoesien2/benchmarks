Perseroan juga mengembangkan produk baru, antara lain Double Capsule Filter, Dual Hollow Capsule, dan Dual Hollow Mono yang telah dirancang dan diuji sejak 2024 dan direncanakan untuk diluncurkan pada periode mendatang. Selain itu, Wismilak Filter melakukan investasi pada teknologi produksi berteknologi tinggi, seperti laser perforation technology. Inisiatif ini merupakan bagian dari strategi diversifikasi portofolio, penguatan kapabilitas produksi, serta upaya merespons dinamika pasar dan kebutuhan konsumen, sekaligus memperluas jangkauan pasar ekspor, khususnya ke kawasan Eropa dan Kanada.

The Company is also developing new products, including Double Capsule Filter, Dual Hollow Capsule, and Dual Hollow Mono, which have been designed and tested since 2024 and are planned to be launched in the coming period. In addition, Wismilak Filter has invested in advanced production technologies, such as laser perforation technology. These initiatives form part of the Company's strategy to diversify its product portfolio, strengthen production capabilities, and respond to evolving market dynamics and consumer needs, while also expanding its export market reach, particularly to Europe and Canada.
