# Layanan Pengaduan (F.2a)
## Public Grievance Mechanism

Perseroan membuka kanal komunikasi bagi publik untuk menyampaikan pengaduan dan aspirasi. Tindak lanjut atas setiap laporan dilakukan secara terkoordinasi melalui unit Suara Konsumen dan ditindak lanjut oleh Departemen Marketing serta Departemen Sales &amp; Field Marketing. Pada 2025, Perseroan menerima sejumlah 129 pengaduan dan seluruhnya telah ditindaklanjuti sesuai dengan prosedur yang berlaku.

The Company provides communication channels for the public to submit complaints and feedback. Follow-up on each report is carried out in a coordinated manner through the Consumer Voice unit and is handled by the Marketing Department and the Sales &amp; Field Marketing Department. In 2025, the Company received 129 complaints, all of which were followed up in accordance with applicable procedures.
