Dalam kinerja tata kelola, Perseroan memperkuat mekanisme pengawasan melalui Komite Keberlanjutan yang bertugas memastikan integrasi aspek ESG di seluruh unit kerja dapat berjalan dengan baik. Adapun kinerja komite secara berkala dievaluasi oleh Direksi guna memastikan target keberlanjutan Perseroan dapat tercapai. Selain itu, Wismilak juga telah memperbarui Kode Etik Perusahaan yang kini turut merefleksikan komitmen terhadap keberlanjutan sebagai bagian integral dari pedoman perusahaan. Di sisi lain, Perseroan juga memperkuat praktik pengadaan melalui asesmen Supplier Ethical Data Exchange-Sedex Member Ethical Trade Audit (SEDEX-SMETA) sebagai upaya meningkatkan transparansi, kepatuhan terhadap etika bisnis, serta pengelolaan risiko ESG dalam rantai pasok.

In addition to contributing to society, the Company strives to create a work environment that is inclusive, fair, and free from discrimination. We also understand the importance of developing adaptive and capable human resources through both internal and external training programs. Throughout 2025, 3,170 employees participated in various training programs, with average training hours per employee recorded at 4.4 hours.

In governance performance, the Company strengthened its oversight mechanism through the Sustainability Committee, which is tasked with ensuring that ESG integration across all work units is carried out effectively. The Committee's performance is evaluated periodically by the Board of Directors to ensure that the Company's sustainability targets are achieved. In addition, Wismilak updated its Code of Conduct, which now also reflects the Company's commitment to sustainability as an integral part of its corporate guidelines. This update strengthens Wismilak's efforts to integrate sustainability principles into its operations, products, and work culture, while also building a foundation for long-term value creation and future resilience. In line with this update, awareness efforts were also carried out for all employees. On the other hand, the Company also strengthened its procurement practices through the Supplier Ethical Data Exchange-Sedex Member Ethical Trade Audit (SEDEX-SMETA) assessment as part of its efforts to improve transparency, compliance with business ethics, and ESG risk management in the supply chain.
