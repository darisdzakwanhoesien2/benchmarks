# Respons terhadap Perubahan Iklim
# Climate Change Response

## Signifikansi Topik | Topic Significance

Perubahan iklim dapat mempengaruhi keberlangsungan operasional, rantai pasok, dan biaya produksi. Oleh karena itu, Perseroan perlu mengelola risiko iklim dan menjalankan inisiatif mitigasi serta adaptasi guna menjaga stabilitas kinerja dan keberlanjutan usaha.

Climate change may affect operational continuity, supply chains, and production costs. Therefore, the Company needs to manage climate-related risks and implement mitigation and adaptation initiatives to maintain performance stability and business sustainability.

## Kebijakan | Policies

- Kebijakan PT. Wismilak Inti Makmur tentang komitmen Perseroan untuk menjalankan keberlanjutan sehingga berkontribusi terhadap tujuan pembangunan berkelanjutan (People, planet, profit).
- Kebijakan lingkungan PT. Gelora Djaja.

- PT Wismilak Inti Makmur Tbk's policy on the Company's commitment to sustainability in order to contribute to the sustainable development goals (People, Planet, Profit).
- PT Gelora Djaja's environmental policy.

## Target Kinerja | Performance Targets

Pengembangan energi terbarukan
Development of renewable energy

## Pencapaian | Achievements

Melanjutkan pengembangan PLTS ke fase 2 dengan penambahan kapasitas sebesar 328 kWp. Hingga akhir 2025, total kapasitas terpasang mencapai 454 kWp dengan instalasi panel surya seluas 2.122 m² yang tersebar di 18 area.

Continued the development of the solar power system into Phase 2, with an additional capacity of 328 kWp. As of the end of 2025, the total installed capacity reached 454 kWp, with solar panels installed across 2,122 m² in 18 areas.

## Evaluasi yang Dilakukan | Evaluation Conducted

Evaluasi berkala atas pengelolaan risiko iklim dan inisiatif mitigasi mencakup pengukuran dampak terhadap operasional, rantai pasok, dan biaya produksi, melibatkan Departemen General Factory Sub Departemen Safety di bawah arahan Direktur Teknik dan Operasional.

Periodic evaluation of climate risk management and mitigation initiatives covers assessment of operational impacts, supply chain, and production costs, involving the General Factory Department – Safety Sub-Department under the supervision of the Technical and Operations Director.
