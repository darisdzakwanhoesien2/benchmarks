Managing with Responsibility

Perseroan menerapkan kesetaraan kesempatan kerja tanpa diskriminasi pada seluruh proses ketenagakerjaan, mulai dari rekrutmen, pengembangan, penilaian kinerja, hingga kesempatan karier. Proses rekrutmen dilaksanakan secara transparan dan objektif sesuai Kebijakan HR-Recruitment 001 yang berlaku sejak 1 Juli 2020. Hubungan kerja diatur melalui Peraturan Perusahaan yang telah disahkan oleh Menteri Ketenagakerjaan dan Perjanjian Kerja Bersama (PKB) yang berlaku sesuai entitas, serta kebijakan pencegahan pelecehan seksual dan non-diskriminasi yang tertuang dalam Kode Etik. Seluruh ketentuan tersebut berlaku bagi 100% karyawan. [2-30]

Hingga akhir 2025, jumlah karyawan Perseroan mencapai 3.173 orang berdasarkan data konsolidasi PT Wismilak Inti Makmur Tbk, PT Gelora Djaja, dan PT Gawih Jaya. Hingga periode pelaporan, tidak terdapat laporan maupun temuan diskriminasi dan pelanggaran HAM di lingkungan operasional. [406-1]

The Company applies equal employment opportunity without discrimination throughout all employment processes, from recruitment, development, and performance evaluation to career opportunities. The recruitment process is carried out transparently and objectively in accordance with HR-Recruitment Policy 001, which has been in effect since July 1, 2020. Employment relationships are governed by Company Regulations approved by the Minister of Manpower and Collective Labor Agreements (CLA) applicable to each entity, as well as policies on the prevention of sexual harassment and non-discrimination as set out in the Code of Conduct. All of these provisions apply to 100% of employees. [2-30]

As of the end of 2025, the Company's total workforce stood at 3,173 employees, based on the consolidated data of PT Wismilak Inti Makmur Tbk, PT Gelora Djaja, and PT Gawih Jaya. As of the end of the reporting period, there were no reports or findings of discrimination or human rights violations within the operational environment. [406-1]
