# Praktik Persaingan Usaha yang Sehat, Kepatuhan, dan Etika Bisnis [2-27][096-1]

# Fair Competition, Compliance, and Business Ethics Practices

Wismilak menerapkan praktik dan standar etika bisnis yang berlaku secara nasional dan global. Perseroan menjalankan pemasaran produk dengan menjunjung prinsip persaingan usaha yang adil dan sehat. Integritas dan etika bisnis menjadi dasar dalam pelaksanaan kegiatan usaha. Perseroan memastikan kepatuhan terhadap ketentuan hukum serta tanggung jawab sosial dan lingkungan melalui praktik bisnis yang transparan dan akuntabel. Penerapan tata kelola usaha mengacu pada Undang-Undang Republik Indonesia Nomor enam Tahun 1999 tentang Larangan Praktik Monopoli dan Persaingan Usaha Tidak Sehat serta Kebijakan Pengadaan Berkelanjutan Wismilak. Sepanjang 2025, Perseroan tidak menerima ataupun terlibat dalam tindakan hukum yang berkaitan dengan perilaku antikompetitif, anti-trust, dan monopoli. Perseroan juga tidak dihadapi dengan denda terkait pelanggaran regulasi persaingan usaha.

Wismilak upholds business practices and ethical standards that are recognized both nationally and globally. The Company markets its products while upholding the principles of fair and healthy competition. Integrity and business ethics form the foundation of its business activities. The Company ensures compliance with legal requirements, as well as social and environmental responsibility, through transparent and accountable business practices. Its business governance practices refer to Law of the Republic of Indonesia No. 5 of 1999 on the Prohibition of Monopolistic Practices and Unfair Business Competition, as well as Wismilak's Sustainable Procurement Policy. Throughout 2025, the Company was not involved in and did not receive any legal action related to anti-competitive behavior, antitrust, or monopoly practices. The Company also did not face any fines related to violations of competition regulations.
