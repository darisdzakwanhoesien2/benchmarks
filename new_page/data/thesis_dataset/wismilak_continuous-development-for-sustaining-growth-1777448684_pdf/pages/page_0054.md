## Perhitungan Emisi GRK [F11] Calculation of GHG Emissions

Perseroan secara rutin melakukan pemantauan dan pelaporan emisi GRK kepada Kementerian Lingkungan Hidup sebagai bentuk kepatuhan terhadap regulasi yang berlaku. Perhitungan emisi GRK untuk PT Wismilak Inti Makmur Tbk diklasifikasikan ke dalam cakupan 1, cakupan 2, dan cakupan 3. Emisi langsung (cakupan 1) berasal dari kegiatan operasional, termasuk penggunaan kendaraan. Emisi tidak langsung (cakupan 2) bersumber dari konsumsi energi yang dibeli, seperti listrik dari PT Perusahaan Listrik Negara (PLN) dan energi dari PT Perusahaan Gas Negara (PGN), sedangkan emisi tidak langsung lainnya (cakupan 3) berasal dari aktivitas dalam rantai pasokan pihak ketiga. Sementara itu, PT Gelora Djaja saat ini melakukan perhitungan emisi GRK dalam bentuk gabungan cakupan 1 dan cakupan 2, yang mencakup emisi $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$, $\mathrm{CH}_4$, dan $\mathrm{N}_2\mathrm{O}$ dari kegiatan operasional boiler dan konsumsi listrik. Perhitungan tersebut belum dipisahkan secara rinci antara cakupan 1 dan cakupan 2. Hingga akhir tahun 2025, Perseroan juga belum melakukan perhitungan emisi zat perusak ozon (ODS) dan emisi udara signifikan lainnya. [305-6] [305-7]

This second-phase development remained focused on the Wismilak Filter business unit. The solar power system (PLTS) supports the operation of production machinery and, overall, contributes to an emissions reduction of $186\mathrm{tCO}_{2}\mathrm{e}$. Going forward, the Company also plans to expand the use of PLTS to its other business units.

The Company routinely monitors and reports its GHG emissions to the Ministry of Environment as part of its compliance with applicable regulations. GHG emissions for PT Wismilak Inti Makmur Tbk are classified into Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3. Direct emissions (Scope 1) arise from operational activities, including vehicle use. Indirect emissions (Scope 2) originate from purchased energy consumption, such as electricity from PT Perusahaan Listrik Negara (PLN) and energy from PT Perusahaan Gas Negara (PGN), while other indirect emissions (Scope 3) derive from activities within the third-party supply chain. Meanwhile, PT Gelora Djaja currently calculates its GHG emissions as a combined total of Scope 1 and Scope 2, covering $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$, $\mathrm{CH}_4$, and $\mathrm{N}_2\mathrm{O}$ emissions from boiler operations and electricity consumption. These emissions have not yet been separately disclosed between Scope 1 and Scope 2. As of the end of 2025, the Company has not yet calculated emissions of ozone-depleting substances (ODS) or other significant air emissions. [305-6][305-7]
