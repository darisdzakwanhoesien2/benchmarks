Marketing and Communication

# Signifikansi Topik | Topic Significance

Pemasaran dan komunikasi yang efektif berperan penting dalam mendorong kinerja dan menjaga reputasi Wismilak. Melalui strategi yang tepat, Perseroan dapat membangun kepercayaan pemangku kepentingan dan meminimalkan risiko misinformasi maupun reputasi.

Effective marketing and communication play an important role in driving performance and maintaining Wismilak's reputation. Through the right strategy, the Company can build stakeholder trust and minimize the risk of misinformation and reputational issues.

# Kebijakan | Policies

Peraturan Bursa Efek Indonesia Nomor I-E tentang Kewajiban Penyampaian Informasi.

Indonesia Stock Exchange Regulation No. I-E on the Obligation to Submit Information.

# Target Kinerja | Performance Targets

- Melakukan public expose tahunan minimal 1 kali dalam setahun sebagai bagian dari keterbukaan informasi terhadap publik;
- Tercapainya efektivitas strategi, target penjualan, efisiensi biaya, serta peningkatan Sales Revenue, dan Customer Retention.

- Conducting at least one annual public expose as part of information disclosure to the public;
- Achieving the effectiveness of strategies, sales targets, cost efficiency, as well as improvements in sales revenue and customer retention.

# Pencapaian | Achievements

- Kenaikan 34% penjualan dari tahun sebelumnya
- Survey Acceptance product Wismilak yang dilakukan tercatat sesuai dan diterima konsumen
- New Product Launching (NPL) di setiap brand diterima oleh konsumen dan terimplikasi pada perolehan omzet Perseroan.

- 34% increase in sales compared to the previous year
- The product acceptance survey for Wismilak indicates that the products are well received by consumers
- New Product Launches (NPL) across each brand are well received by consumers and contribute to the Company's revenue growth

# Evaluasi yang Dilakukan | Evaluation Conducted

Evaluasi dilakukan secara periodik oleh Departemen Marketing Analisis, Riset dan Pengembangan, serta Marketing dalam strategi pemasaran.

The evaluation is conducted periodically by the Marketing Analysis, Research and Development, and Marketing Departments as part of the marketing strategy.
