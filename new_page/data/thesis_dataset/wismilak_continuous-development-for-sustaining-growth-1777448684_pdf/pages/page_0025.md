# Penentuan Topik Material[1-1][1-2] Material Topics Determination

Wismilak melakukan peninjauan topik material setiap tahun guna memastikan prospek bisnis, strategi, dan inisiatif keberlanjutannya tetap selaras dengan kebutuhan dan ekspektasi pemangku kepentingan. Perseroan melakukan Identifikasi risiko, peluang, strategi usaha, dan menganalisis dampaknya. Dalam proses tersebut, Perseroan juga mempertimbangkan acuan topik material yang tercantum dalam standar GRI dan International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS).

Pada 2025, Wismilak memperkuat proses penilaian materialitas dengan menerapkan pendekatan double materiality (DMA). Pendekatan dianalisis melalui materialitas dampak (impact materiality) yang memerhatikan dampak aktivitas Perseroan terhadap lingkungan dan sosial, serta materialitas finansial (financial materiality) yang memetakan dampak kinerja ESG terhadap kondisi keuangan. Topik material dirumuskan ulang melalui diskusi internal yang dipandu oleh tim akademisi selaku pihak independen dan tim ahli. Proses ini melibatkan perwakilan dari berbagai fungsi di Wismilak. Pelaksanaan diskusi tetap mempertimbangkan masukan dari pemangku kepentingan internal dan eksternal. Hasil diskusi tersebut kemudian diajukan kepada Direksi dan telah memperoleh pengesahan serta penetapan.

# Topik Material [1-2] Material Topics

Setelah disetujui Direksi, Laporan Keberlanjutan Wismilak 2025 mengungkapkan enam topik material sebagai prioritas pengungkapan informasi. Dengan demikian, terdapat perubahan atas topik material yang disampaikan pada Laporan Keberlanjutan periode sebelumnya. Perubahan tersebut dipengaruhi oleh perbedaan metode penentuan topik material serta isu-isu keberlanjutan yang berkembang selama 2025.

Wismilak reviews its material topics annually to ensure that its business prospects, strategy, and sustainability initiatives remain aligned with stakeholder needs and expectations. The Company identifies risks, opportunities, and business strategies, and analyzes their impacts. In this process, the Company also considers references to material topics set out in the GRI Standards and the International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS).

In 2025, Wismilak strengthened its materiality assessment process by adopting a double materiality approach (DMA). Under this framework, the Company assessed impact materiality, which considers the effects of its activities on the environment and society, alongside financial materiality, which evaluates the influence of ESG performance on its financial condition. The material topics were subsequently refined through internal discussions facilitated by an independent academic team, with support from a team of experts. Representatives from various functions across Wismilak participated in the process, which also incorporated input from both internal and external stakeholders. The results of the discussion were subsequently submitted to the Board of Directors and have obtained approval and formal endorsement.

Following approval by the Board of Directors, the 2025 Wismilak Sustainability Report discloses six material topics as disclosure priorities. Accordingly, there were changes to the material topics presented in the previous reporting period's Sustainability Report. These changes were influenced by differences in the method used to determine material topics, as well as sustainability issues that developed during 2025.
