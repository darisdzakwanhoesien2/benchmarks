Mengelola dengan Tanggung Jawab
Managing with Responsibility

Perseroan menjalankan kegiatan usaha dengan mengacu pada prinsip tata kelola yang baik, integritas, serta kepatuhan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku. Komitmen tersebut dituangkan dalam Kode Etik (CoC) yang menjadi pedoman perilaku bagi seluruh insan. Perseroan dalam menjalankan aktivitas usaha secara bertanggung jawab. Kode Etik Perseroan mengintegrasikan prinsip-prinsip keberlanjutan yang mencakup aspek lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola (ESG). Di dalamnya tercantum berbagai ketentuan mengenai etika bisnis, kepatuhan terhadap hukum, penghormatan terhadap hak asasi manusia, praktik ketenagakerjaan yang adil, perlindungan lingkungan, serta pencegahan praktik korupsi dan benturan kepentingan. Kode Etik dapat diakses oleh pemangku kepentingan melalui situs resmi Perseroan pada tautan berikut: https://www.wismilak.com/uploads/laporan/pdf/standar-perilaku-perusahaan-1767061780.pdf.

Perseroan memastikan penerapan Kode Etik melalui berbagai mekanisme internal, termasuk sosialisasi kebijakan, pelatihan kepada karyawan, serta integrasi prinsip-prinsip tersebut dalam prosedur operasional dan kegiatan bisnis sehari-hari. Selain itu, Perseroan menyediakan mekanisme pelaporan yang memungkinkan karyawan maupun pemangku kepentingan untuk menyampaikan dugaan pelanggaran terhadap kebijakan yang berlaku. Setiap laporan akan ditindaklanjuti sesuai dengan prosedur yang telah ditetapkan guna memastikan penerapan standar etika dan kepatuhan berjalan secara konsisten dalam seluruh kegiatan usaha Perseroan.

The Company conducts its business activities in accordance with the principles of good governance, integrity, and compliance with applicable laws and regulations. This commitment is set out in the Code of Conduct (CoC), which serves as a behavioral guideline for all Company personnel in carrying out business activities responsibly. The Company's CoC integrates sustainability principles covering environmental, social, and governance (ESG) aspects. It includes provisions on business ethics, legal compliance, respect for human rights, fair labor practices, environmental protection, and the prevention of corruption and conflicts of interest. The CoC can be accessed by stakeholders through the Company's official website at the following link: https://www.wismilak.com/uploads/laporan/pdf/standar-perilaku-perusahaan-1767061780.pdf.

The Company ensures the implementation of CoC through various internal mechanisms, including policy dissemination, employee training, and the integration of these principles into operational procedures and daily business activities. In addition, the Company provides a reporting mechanism that allows employees and stakeholders to report suspected violations of applicable policies. Each report is followed up in accordance with established procedures to ensure the consistent implementation of ethical standards and compliance across all of the Company's business activities.
