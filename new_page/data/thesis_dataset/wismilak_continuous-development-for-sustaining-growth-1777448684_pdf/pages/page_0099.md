# Struktur Tata Kelola Keberlanjutan
# Sustainability Governance Structure

Tata kelola keberlanjutan Wismilak dijalankan dengan mengacu pada prinsip tata kelola perusahaan yang baik (GCG). Struktur tata kelola Perseroan terdiri atas Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS) sebagai organ tertinggi, Direksi sebagai pengelola Perseroan, serta Dewan Komisaris yang menjalankan fungsi pengawasan dengan dukungan komite di bawahnya. Nominasi dan pengangkatan anggota Direksi maupun Dewan Komisaris merupakan kewenangan Pemegang Saham yang ditetapkan melalui RUPS. Perseroan memastikan tidak terdapat benturan kepentingan yang dapat mempengaruhi objektivitas pengambilan keputusan. Informasi lebih lanjut mengenai struktur, peran dan tanggung jawab, mekanisme nominasi dan seleksi, serta remunerasi dan evaluasi kinerja Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris tersedia dalam Laporan Tahunan Wismilak 2025.

Tanggung jawab atas aspek keberlanjutan terintegrasi dalam seluruh fungsi tata kelola perusahaan. Untuk memperkuat pengawasan, Perseroan membentuk Komite Keberlanjutan yang bertugas memastikan efektivitas pelaksanaan kinerja keberlanjutan. Kinerja Komite tersebut berada di bawah pengawasan dan evaluasi langsung oleh Direksi.

Wismilak's sustainability governance is carried out in accordance with the principles of good corporate governance (GCG). The Company's governance structure consists of the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) as the highest governing body, the Board of Directors as the managing body, and the Board of Commissioners as the supervisory body, supported by committees under its oversight. The nomination and appointment of members of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners are the authority of the Shareholders as determined through the GMS. The Company ensures that there are no conflicts of interest that may affect the objectivity of decision-making. Further information on the structure, roles and responsibilities, nomination and selection mechanisms, as well as the remuneration and performance evaluation of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners is available in Wismilak's 2025 Annual Report. [2-9][2-10][2-19][2-19][2-20][2-21]

Responsibility for sustainability aspects is integrated across all corporate governance functions. To strengthen oversight, the Company has established a Sustainability Committee tasked with ensuring the effective implementation of sustainability performance. The Committee's performance is subject to direct oversight and evaluation by the Board of Directors.
