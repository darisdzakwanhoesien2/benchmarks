# Upaya dan Pencapaian Efisiensi Energi [F7][202-4] Energy Efficiency Initiatives and Achievements

Langkah-langkah efisiensi energi di Wismilak dilaksanakan berdasarkan tiga kategori inisiatif yang mendapat persetujuan manajemen. Seluruh capaian efisiensi dipantau secara berkala melalui pencatatan sistematis atas hasil absolut. Implementasi upaya efisiensi energi pada 2025 telah berkontribusi pada penurunan penggunaan energi sebesar 12,5% dan penghematan biaya energi sebesar 4%.

Energy efficiency measures at Wismilak are carried out based on three categories of initiatives approved by management. All efficiency achievements are monitored regularly through systematic recording of absolute results. The implementation of energy efficiency measures in 2025 contributed to a 12.5% reduction in energy use and 4% energy cost savings.

## Inisiatif Efisiensi Energi [F7]

Energy Efficiency Initiatives

### Low-Cost Efficiency

1. Memasang atap transparan untuk pencahayaan di siang hari untuk menurunkan konsumsi daya listrik.
2. Melakukan perawatan mesin produksi secara berkala dengan mengganti spare part yang sudah tidak layak pakai.
3. Menggunakan efluen yang telah melalui proses pemurnian kembali sebagai sumber air dalam perawatan tanaman.
4. Melakukan monitoring konsumsi steam boiler melalui aplikasi.

1. Installed transparent roofing to provide daylight illumination and reduce electricity consumption.
2. Performed regular maintenance on production machinery by replacing spare parts that were no longer fit for use.
3. Reused effluent that had undergone further treatment as a water source for plant maintenance.
4. Monitored steam boiler consumption through an application.

### Medium Cost Efficiency

1. Optimalisasi operasional kompresor secara bergantian sesuai dengan kebutuhan mesin produksi.
2. Optimalisasi konsumsi LNG untuk bahan bakar utama dari boiler.
3. Mengganti pompa air dengan mekanisme gravitasi untuk distribusi air bersih dari tandon ke kamar mandi.
4. Mengganti lampu penerangan jalan umum (PJU) dari lampu konvensional menjadi lampu jenis LED.

1. Optimized compressor operations on a rotating basis according to production machine requirements.
2. Optimized LNG consumption as boiler primary fuel.
3. Replaced water pumps with a gravity-based mechanism for distributing clean water from storage tanks to restrooms.
4. Replaced conventional public street lighting with LED lights.

### High-Cost Efficiency

1. Optimalisasi penggunaan single packet heating tunnel (SPHT) untuk menggantikan shrinkage tunnel dalam proses produksi.
2. Melakukan peningkatan teknologi dan penambahan mesin.
3. Melaksanakan pembangkit listrik tenaga surya (PLTS) Fase 2

1. Optimized the use of the single packet heating tunnel (SPHT) to replace the shrinkage tunnel in the production process.
2. Upgraded technology and added machinery.
3. Implemented Phase 2 of the solar power system (PLTS).
