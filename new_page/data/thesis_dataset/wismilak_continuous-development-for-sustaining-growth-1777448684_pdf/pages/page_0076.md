# Pekerja Lain Bukan Karyawan Wismilak [2-9] Other Workers Who Are Not Wismilak Employees

Selain karyawan tetap, kegiatan operasional Wismilak melibatkan tenaga kerja alih daya dari vendor atau pemasok yang bekerja berdasarkan perjanjian kontrak sesuai kebutuhan kegiatan usaha. Hingga akhir 2025, jumlah tenaga alih daya yang bekerja untuk mendukung operasional mencapai 3.183 orang, atau 50,1% dari total karyawan secara keseluruhan. Tenaga alih daya tersebut bekerja di bawah pengelolaan pihak ketiga dan mayoritas ditempatkan pada fungsi Produksi/Factory.

In addition to permanent employees, Wismilak's operational activities involve outsourced workers from vendors or suppliers who work under contractual arrangements based on business needs. As of the end of 2025, the number of outsourced workers supporting operations reached 3,183 people, representing 50.1% of the total workforce. These outsourced workers are managed by third parties, with the majority assigned to Production/Factory functions.

# Perputaran Karyawan Wismilak [401-1] Wismilak Employee Turnover

Perseroan menjalankan proses rekrutmen secara terbuka dan patuh terhadap kebijakan internal serta regulasi ketenagakerjaan. Selama 2025, Perseroan merekrut 291 karyawan baru, yang terdiri dari 254 pria dan 37 wanita. Adapun tingkat perputaran karyawan pada periode tersebut tercatat sebesar 9,1%.

The Company conducts its recruitment process openly and in compliance with internal policies and labor regulations. During 2025, the Company recruited 291 new employees, consisting of 254 men and 37 women. The employee turnover rate for the period was recorded at 9.1%.
