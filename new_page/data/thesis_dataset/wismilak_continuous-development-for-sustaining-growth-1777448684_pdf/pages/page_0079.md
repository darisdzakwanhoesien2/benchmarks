Perseroan menyediakan pelatihan yang dapat diakses secara daring maupun luring. Selama periode pelaporan, sebanyak 3.170 karyawan, yang terdiri dari tenaga alih daya dan karyawan tetap, telah mengikuti program pelatihan yang diselenggarakan Perseroan. Setiap karyawan menerima rata-rata 4,4 jam pelatihan dalam satu tahun pelaporan sebagai bagian dari pengembangan kompetensi yang terukur. [404-1]

The Company provides training that can be accessed both online and offline. During the reporting period, a total of 3,170 personnel, consisting of outsourced workers and permanent employees, participated in training programs organized by the Company. On average, each participant received 4.4 hours of training during the reporting year as part of measurable competency development. [404-1]
