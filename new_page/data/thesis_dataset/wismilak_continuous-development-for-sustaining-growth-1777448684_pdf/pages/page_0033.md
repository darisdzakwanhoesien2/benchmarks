# Lingkungan, Kesehatan, dan Keselamatan Kerja
Environment, Health, and Safety

## Signifikansi Topik | Topic Significance

Lingkungan, Kesehatan, dan Keselamatan Kerja menjadi bagian penting dalam strategi operasional yang memastikan keamanan dan kelayakan lingkungan kerja, di tingkat Perseroan maupun di seluruh entitas anak.

Occupational Health and Safety is an important part of the Company's operational strategy, ensuring the safety and suitability of the work environment at both the parent company and across all subsidiaries.

## Kebijakan | Policies

Kebijakan Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja di PT Gelora Djaja yang diterbitkan oleh Presiden Direktur PT Gelora Djaja pada Tanggal 24 Oktober 2016.

Occupational Health and Safety Policy of PT Gelora Djaja, issued by the President Director of PT Gelora Djaja on October 24, 2016.

## Target Kinerja | Performance Targets

- Nol Kecelakaan.
- Meningkatkan persentase karyawan fit to work menjadi ≥ 80%.

- Zero accidents.
- Increase the percentage of employees who are fit to work to ≥ 80%.

## Pencapaian | Achievements

- Nol kecelakaan kerja.
- 3.900.561 Jam Kerja Selamat.
- Peningkatan persentase karyawan fit to work sebesar 2%.

- Zero workplace accidents.
- 3,900,561 safe working hours.
- 2% increase in the percentage of employees who were fit to work.

## Evaluasi yang Dilakukan | Evaluation Conducted

1. Identifikasi bahaya, penilaian risiko, dan pengendalian risiko.
2. Inspeksi harian safety.
3. Pengawasan pekerjaan berisiko tinggi.
4. Pelaporan kecelakaan kerja.
5. Investigasi kecelakaan kerja dan keadaan darurat.
6. Perizinan pekerjaan berisiko tinggi.
7. Penanganan dan pengelolaan limbah B3.
8. Pemantauan dan pengujian lingkungan kerja, air limbah, alat pemadam api ringan, P3K.

1. Hazard identification, risk assessment, and risk control.
2. Daily safety inspections.
3. Supervision of high-risk work.
4. Workplace accident reporting.
5. Investigation of workplace accidents and emergency situations.
6. Work permits for high-risk work.
7. Handling and management of hazardous and toxic waste.
8. Monitoring and testing of the work environment, wastewater, fire extinguishers, and first aid facilities.
