# Penggunaan Material yang Ramah Lingkungan (F3)[101-2]
## Use of Environmentally Friendly Materials

Wismilak mengintegrasikan praktik keberlanjutan dalam operasionalnya melalui pemanfaatan material daur ulang, antara lain limbah debu, tembakau, dan limbah kertas, guna mengurangi timbulan limbah dan meningkatkan efisiensi penggunaan sumber daya.

Wismilak integrates sustainability practices into its operations through the use of recycled materials, including tobacco dust, waste, and paper waste, to reduce waste generation and improve resource-use efficiency.

# Dukungan terhadap Lingkungan Hidup
## Support for the Environment

### Inisiatif Penghijauan (F6)[F10][F16][204-1]
### Greening Initiatives

Wilayah operasional Perseroan tidak berlokasi di dalam atau dekat kawasan konservasi dengan keanekaragaman hayati tinggi. Sebagai bentuk komitmen lingkungan, Perseroan melakukan kegiatan penghijauan di sekitar area pabrik guna mendukung konservasi keanekaragaman hayati dan meningkatkan serapan emisi. Selama periode pelaporan, tidak terdapat pengaduan terkait lingkungan hidup.

The Company's operational areas are not located within or near conservation areas with high biodiversity value. As part of its environmental commitment, the Company carried out greening activities around its factory areas to support biodiversity conservation and increase emissions absorption. During the reporting period, there were no environmental complaints.
