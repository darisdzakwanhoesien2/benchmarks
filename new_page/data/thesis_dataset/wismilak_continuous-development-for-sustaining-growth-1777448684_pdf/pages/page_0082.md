# Memberikan Fasilitas Terbaik
Providing the Best Facilities

Perseroan menerapkan prinsip kesetaraan dalam pemberian remunerasi kepada seluruh karyawan. Setiap karyawan menerima imbalan sesuai kompetensi dan level jabatan tanpa diskriminasi berdasarkan gender, ras, golongan, atau faktor lainnya. Pengaturan remunerasi, kompensasi, tunjangan, dan bentuk apresiasi diatur dalam Kebijakan Human Resources Compensation and Benefit (HR-C&amp;B). Penetapan upah dilakukan melalui evaluasi kinerja dengan mengacu pada Upah Minimum Regional yang ditetapkan Pemerintah, dengan pembayaran sekurang-kurangnya sebesar 100% dari UMR di masing-masing provinsi. Pada tingkat entry level, rasio gaji pokok antara karyawan pria dan wanita adalah setara, yaitu 1:1. (F.20)[202-1][405-2]

The Company applies the principle of equality in providing remuneration to all employees. Each employee receives compensation based on competence and job level without discrimination based on gender, race, group, or other factors. Remuneration, compensation, benefits, and other forms of recognition are governed under the Human Resources Compensation and Benefit Policy, HR C and B. Wages are determined through performance evaluation with reference to the Regional Minimum Wage set by the Government, with payment of no less than 100% of the applicable provincial minimum wage. At the entry level, the ratio of base salary between male and female employees is equal at 1:1.(F.20)[202-1][405-2]
