# Performance Evaluation

Perseroan melaksanakan evaluasi kinerja berdasarkan Kebijakan dan Prosedur Performance Appraisal yang tertuang dalam MODOD 004. Penilaian dilakukan secara daring melalui aplikasi Performance Appraisal Online dengan pendekatan kuantitatif dan kualitatif oleh atasan langsung. Atasan menyampaikan hasil evaluasi, memberikan arahan perbaikan, dan memastikan pemahaman sasaran departemen agar selaras dengan tujuan strategis Perseroan.

Perseroan juga menggunakan aplikasi Perform sebagai sarana personal feedback untuk menilai pencapaian KPI, efektivitas kepemimpinan, dan rencana pengembangan karier. Hasil evaluasi menjadi dasar pemetaan kompetensi, perencanaan pengembangan individu, dan promosi jabatan melalui Talent Management System. Pada periode pelaporan, sebanyak 351 karyawan memperoleh promosi berdasarkan hasil penilaian kinerja.

The Company conducts performance evaluations based on the Performance Appraisal Policy and Procedure set out in MODOD 004. Assessments are conducted online through the Performance Appraisal Online application using both quantitative and qualitative approaches by direct supervisors. Supervisors communicate the evaluation results, provide guidance for improvement, and ensure that department objectives are understood and aligned with the Company's strategic goals.

The Company also uses the Perform application as a personal feedback tool to assess KPI achievement, leadership effectiveness, and career development plans. The evaluation results serve as the basis for competency mapping, individual development planning, and promotions through the Talent Management System. During the reporting period, 351 employees received promotions based on their performance appraisal results.
