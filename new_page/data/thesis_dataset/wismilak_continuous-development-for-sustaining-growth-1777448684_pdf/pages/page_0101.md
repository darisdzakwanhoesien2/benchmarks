# Menghindari Benturan Kepentingan [1-11][2-15]
Avoiding Conflicts of Interest

Perseroan menerapkan pengendalian untuk mencegah terjadinya benturan kepentingan melalui penerapan prinsip profesionalitas dan integritas oleh seluruh pejabat, mulai dari Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, Kepala Unit Kerja, hingga jajaran manajerial. Perseroan juga memastikan bahwa Ketua organ tata kelola tertinggi tidak merangkap jabatan sebagai eksekutif senior di Perseroan. Hingga akhir 2025, Perseroan tidak mencatat adanya peristiwa benturan kepentingan yang melibatkan Dewan Komisaris maupun Direksi.

The Company applies controls to prevent conflicts of interest through the implementation of professionalism and integrity by all officials, from the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, and Heads of Work Units to the managerial level. The Company also ensures that the Chair of the highest governance body does not concurrently serve as a senior executive within the Company. As of the end of 2025, the Company had not recorded any conflicts of interest involving the Board of Commissioners or the Board of Directors.

# Kegiatan Internal Komite Keberlanjutan
Internal Activities of the Sustainability Committee

Sepanjang 2025, Komite Keberlanjutan mengadakan empat kali pertemuan dengan Direksi guna membahas kinerja keberlanjutan kuartal IV 2024 serta kuartal I-III 2025. Di samping itu, Komite menjalankan sejumlah kegiatan lainnya, di antaranya:

- Melakukan penerapan strategi keberlanjutan Perseroan;
- Melakukan monitoring pelaksanaan inisiatif keberlanjutan Perseroan;
- Melakukan pengelolaan isu lingkungan;
- Melakukan pendampingan proses pemeringkatan EcoVadis untuk unit usaha Wismilak Filter;
- Melaksanakan sosialisasi budaya keberlanjutan;
- Melakukan penyusunan Laporan Keberlanjutan Perseroan tahun buku 2024;
- Melakukan penyusunan Performance Report tahun buku 2024 untuk unit usaha Wismilak Filter;
- Melakukan pendampingan pelaksanaan program Quality Control Competition (QCC).

Throughout 2025, the Sustainability Committee held four meetings with the Board of Directors to discuss sustainability performance for the fourth quarter of 2024 and the first through third quarters of 2025. In addition, the Committee carried out a number of other activities, including:

- Implemented the Company's sustainability strategy;
- Monitored the implementation of the Company's sustainability initiatives;
- Managed environmental issues;
- Provided assistance in the EcoVadis rating process for the Wismilak Filter business unit;
- Conducted sustainability culture awareness programs;
- Prepared the Company's 2024 Sustainability Report;
- Prepared the 2024 Performance Report for the Wismilak Filter business unit;
- Provided assistance in implementing the Quality Control Competition (QCC) program;
