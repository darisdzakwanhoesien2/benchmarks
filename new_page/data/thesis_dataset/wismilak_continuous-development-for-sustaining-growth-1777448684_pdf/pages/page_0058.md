# Pengelolaan Effluent (F14)[306-2][306-4][306-5] Effluent Management

Seluruh eflien hasil kegiatan operasional diolah melalui Instalasi Pengolahan Air Limbah (IPAL), dan limbah cair domestik dikelola agar dapat digunakan kembali. Penerapan baku mutu air limbah mengacu pada Peraturan Gubernur Jawa Timur No. 72 Tahun 2013 tentang Baku Mutu Air Limbah Industri. Pada 2025, tidak terdapat insiden tumpahan eflien maupun cairan berbahaya lainnya. (F15)

All effluent generated from operational activities is treated through a Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP), and domestic wastewater is managed for reuse. The application of wastewater quality standards refers to East Java Governor Regulation No. 72 of 2013 on Industrial Wastewater Quality Standards. In 2025, there were no incidents involving effluent spills or other hazardous liquid spills. (F15)
