# Area Operasional dan Pasar Terlayani (C-4)(2-1)(2-4)

## Operating Areas and Markets Served

Hingga akhir periode pelaporan, Perseroan menjalankan kegiatan usaha pada sektor produksi dan pemasaran rokok kretek premium, cerutu, dan filter rod. Kegiatan operasional Perseroan dikelola melalui kantor pusat di Surabaya dan kantor perwakilan di Jakarta.

Sepanjang 2025, Perseroan didukung oleh 4 fasilitas produksi dan 4 sentra logistik regional yang berperan dalam mendukung proses produksi, penyimpanan, dan distribusi produk. Distribusi dilakukan melalui 23 jaringan distribusi yang menjangkau seluruh wilayah Indonesia.

Perseroan melayani pasar domestik serta pasar internasional, khususnya untuk lini produk filter rod, yang mencakup China, Singapura, dan Vietnam. Hingga akhir 2025, Perseroan memiliki 12 brand dengan total 64 produk dan menghadirkan produk baru sebanyak 29 SKU. Portofolio produk Perseroan mencakup berbagai merek, antara lain Wismilak Kretek, Wismilak Diplomat, dan Diplomat Evo, serta merek lainnya yang tercantum pada situs resmi Perseroan https://wismilak.com/produk-kami.

As of the end of the reporting period, the Company operates in the production and marketing of premium kretek cigarettes, cigars, and filter rods. The Company's operations are managed through its head office in Surabaya and a representative office in Jakarta.

Throughout 2025, the Company was supported by 4 production facilities and 4 regional logistics centers that played a role in supporting the production, storage, and distribution of its products. Distribution was carried out through 23 distribution networks serving all regions of Indonesia.

The Company serves both domestic and international markets, particularly for its filter rod product line, which includes China, Singapore, and Vietnam. As of the end of 2025, the Company has 12 brands with a total of 64 products and introduced 29 new SKUs. The Company's product portfolio includes various brands, including Wismilak Kretek, Wismilak Diplomat, and Diplomat Evo, as well as other brands listed on the Company's official website https://wismilak.com/produk-kami.
