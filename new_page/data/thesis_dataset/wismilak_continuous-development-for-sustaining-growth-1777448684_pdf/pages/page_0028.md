# Praktik Pengadaan

Procurement Practices

|  Governance | Strategy | Risk Management | Metric & Target  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  • Penanggung Jawab: Purchasing Manager dan Marketing Service Manager.
• Di bawah arahan: Direktur Teknis & Operasional, dan Direktur Pemasaran.
• Responsible parties: Purchasing Manager and Marketing Service Manager.
• Under the direction of: Director of Engineering & Operations, and Marketing Director. | • Memastikan kepatuhan terhadap kode etik dalam proses pengadaan.
• Melakukan penilaian kualitas vendor.
• Ensure compliance with the code of ethics in the procurement process.
• Conduct vendor quality assessments. | Risiko gangguan dalam ketersediaan pasokan bahan baku yang dapat memengaruhi kelancaran operasional.
Risk of disruption in the availability of raw material supplies that may affect operational continuity. | • Memastikan 100% kepatuhan terhadap kode etik pengadaan.
• Meningkatkan persentase vendor yang memenuhi vendor quality assessment dari 80% menjadi 85%.
• Ensure 100% compliance with the procurement code of ethics.
• Increase the percentage of vendors meeting the vendor quality assessment from 80% to 85%.  |

# Lingkungan, Kesehatan, dan Keselamatan Kerja

Environment, Health, and Safety

|  Governance | Strategy | Risk Management | Metric & Target  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  • Penanggung Jawab: Factory Manager melalui P23K dan bekerja sama dengan HR Manager.
• Di bawah arahan: Direktur Teknis & Operasional.
• Responsible party: Factory Manager through P23K, in collaboration with the HR Manager.
• Under the direction of: Director of Engineering & Operations. | • Mengendalikan kondisi lingkungan kerja agar tetap aman dan kondusif.
• Meningkatkan kesiapan kerja (fit to work) bagi seluruh pekerja.
• Maintain workplace conditions that are safe and conducive.
• Improve fit-to-work readiness for all employees. | Risiko kecelakaan kerja yang berpotensi memengaruhi keselamatan pekerja serta berdampak pada penurunan produktivitas kerja.
Risk of workplace accidents that may affect employee safety and reduce productivity. | • Mencapai nol kecelakaan kerja (Zero accident).
• Meningkatkan persentase fit to work sebesar 5% (dari 75% menjadi 80%).
• Achieve zero workplace accidents.
• Increase the fit-to-work percentage by 5%, from 75% to 80%.  |
