# Pencapaian Kinerja Keberlanjutan
## Sustainability Performance Achievements

Penjualan bersih Perseroan di akhir 2025 mencapai Rp6.381,7 miliar, meningkat sebesar 34,33% dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya sejumlah Rp4.750,9 miliar. Sementara itu, laba usaha tercatat sebesar Rp556,8 miliar naik 48% dari tahun 2024 sebesar Rp376,4 miliar.

Pada kinerja lingkungan, kami melanjutkan pengembangan Pembangkit Listrik Tenaga Surya (PLTS) fase kedua dengan menambah kapasitas sebesar 382 kWp. Dengan penambahan ini, total kapasitas menjadi 454 kWp. Dari total daya tersebut, panel surya yang berhasil terpasang hingga akhir 2025 tercatat seluas 2.122 m² yang tersebar di 18 area. Di sisi lain, guna mengurangi timbulan limbah, Wismilak memanfaatkan 155 ton limbah kertas menjadi bahan baku kertas daur ulang dan 40 ton limbah debu tembakau dikirim kepada pihak ketiga.

Sementara pada kinerja sosial, Perseroan tetap melanjutkan berbagai program pemberdayaan masyarakat yang telah menjadi bagian dari komitmen jangka panjang. Program AksiLiterasi Wismilak terus mendorong peningkatan literasi membaca dan digital bagi pelajar dan sebanyak lebih dari 40.000 pelajar telah mendapatkan manfaat dari kegiatan ini.

Program kewirausahaan Diplomat Success Challenge (DSC) memberikan kesempatan bagi ribuan wirausaha Indonesia untuk mengembangkan usaha mereka melalui akses pembinaan dan jaringan bisnis. Pada akhir 2025, DSC menjangkau 43.281 peserta dari seluruh wilayah di Indonesia. The Company's net sales at the end of 2025 reached Rp6,381.7 billion, representing an increase of 34.33% compared to the previous year's Rp4,750.9 billion. Meanwhile, operating profit amounted to Rp556.8 billion, up by 48% from Rp376.4 billion in 2024.

In environmental performance, we continued the second phase of our Solar Power Plant (PLTS) development by adding 382 kWp of capacity. With this addition, total installed capacity reached 454 kWp. Of this total, solar panels installed by the end of 2025 covered 2,122 m² across 18 areas. At the same time, in an effort to reduce waste generation, Wismilak utilized 155 tons of paper waste as raw material for recycled paper and sent 40 tons of tobacco dust waste to third parties.

On the social performance side, the Company continued various community empowerment programs that have become part of its long-term commitment. The AksiLiterasi Wismilak program continued to promote reading and digital literacy among students, and more than 40,000 students have benefited from the program.

The Diplomat Success Challenge (DSC) entrepreneurship program provided opportunities for thousands of Indonesian entrepreneurs to develop their businesses through access to mentoring and business networks. By the end of 2025, DSC had reached 43,281 participants from across Indonesia. Wismilak also provided a Sustainability Report mentoring program for DSC participants through collaboration with Parahyangan Catholic University. The program used the LESTARI Guide, which was also developed with the participation of members of Wismilak's Sustainability Committee, to help MSMEs identify and communicate the economic, social, and environmental impacts of their businesses as part of responsible and sustainable business practices.
