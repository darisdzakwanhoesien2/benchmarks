# Manajemen Energi dan Emisi Energy and Emissions Management

## Jumlah dan Intensitas Energi yang Digunakan [F-5][302-1] Energy Consumption and Intensity

Selama periode pelaporan, Perseroan menggunakan sumber energi yang berasal dari pasokan listrik PT PLN (Persero), gas alam cair, dan solar. Total konsumsi energi dihitung berdasarkan akumulasi pemakaian energi untuk kegiatan operasional kantor dan pabrik, yang selanjutnya dikonversi ke dalam satuan Gigajoule (GJ).

Perhitungan konsumsi energi dalam laporan ini hanya mencakup aktivitas operasional di dalam Perseroan. Hingga akhir periode pelaporan, Perseroan belum melakukan perhitungan konsumsi energi yang berasal dari aktivitas di luar Perseroan. Pada 2025, PT Wismilak Inti Makmur Tbk mencatat intensitas energi sebesar 3,3 GJ/Ton, meningkat dibandingkan tahun 2024 yang mencapai 2,9 GJ/Ton. Di sisi lain, PT Gelora Djaja mencatat intensitas energi sebesar 6,0 GJ/Ton, menurun dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya yang mencapai 6,3 GJ/Ton. [302-2]

During the reporting period, the Company used energy sourced from electricity supplied by PT PLN (Persero), liquefied natural gas, and diesel fuel. Total energy consumption was calculated based on the cumulative energy used for office and factory operations, which was then converted into Gigajoules (GJ).

The energy consumption calculation in this report only covers operational activities within the Company. As of the end of the reporting period, the Company had not yet calculated energy consumption from activities outside the Company. In 2025, PT Wismilak Inti Makmur Tbk recorded an energy intensity of 3.3 GJ/ton, an increase compared to 2.9 GJ/ton in 2024. Meanwhile, PT Gelora Djaja recorded an energy intensity of 6.0 GJ/ton, a decrease compared to 6.3 GJ/ton in the previous year. [302-2]
