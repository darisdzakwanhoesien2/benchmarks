# Inisiatif Pengelolaan Effluent [F13][F14][303-4][306-3]

## Effluent Management Initiatives

Effluent generated from employees' domestic activities is managed by Wismilak for reuse through two categories of initiatives:

1. Low cost: Air limbah yang telah diolah dimanfaatkan kembali untuk perawatan tanaman.
2. High cost: Air limbah yang berasal dari kondensat diolah dan dimanfaatkan kembali untuk air umpan boiler.

1. Low cost: Treated wastewater is reused for plant irrigation.
2. High cost: Wastewater from condensate is treated and reused as boiler feedwater.

# Pengelolaan Limbah Waste Management

Jenis limbah yang dihasilkan Wismilak meliputi limbah B3 padat dan cair, serta limbah padat non-B3. Seluruh data dan pemantauan limbah dikelola melalui LogBook dan Neraca Limbah B3. [306-5][306-2]

The types of waste generated by Wismilak include solid and liquid hazardous waste (B3 waste), as well as non-hazardous solid waste. All waste data and monitoring are managed through the Hazardous Waste Logbook and Hazardous Waste Balance Sheet. [306-5][306-2]
