## Message from the President Director

![img-32.jpeg](img-32.jpeg)

“Keberlanjutan merupakan proses yang terus kami jalankan sesuai komitmen Wismilak dalam melakukan praktik usaha berkelanjutan serta membawa dampak positif bagi masyarakat, lingkungan, dan masa depan. Sepanjang 2025, Wismilak terus mendorong inovasi, memperkuat kapabilitas sumber daya manusia, dan meningkatkan efisiensi operasional untuk mendukung pertumbuhan usaha yang lebih tangguh, adaptif, dan berdaya saing.”

Sustainability is an ongoing process that we continue to pursue in line with Wismilak's commitment to implementing sustainable business practices and creating a positive impact on society, the environment, and the future. Throughout 2025, Wismilak continued to drive innovation, strengthen human capital capabilities, and improve operational efficiency to support business growth that is more resilient, adaptive, and competitive.

# Para Pemangku Kepentingan yang Terhormat, Dear Stakeholders,

Di tengah dinamika industri dan kondisi ekonomi sepanjang 2025, Wismilak terus meningkatkan kesadaran untuk menerapkan prinsip keberlanjutan dalam menjalankan strategi bisnis. Langkah ini kami perkuat dengan mempertajam fokus kami pada inovasi dan kolaborasi. Kami terus melakukan integrasi antar fungsi untuk memperkuat sinergi pengelolaan kinerja lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola (ESG), serta meneruskan pengukuran dampak kegiatannya secara bertahap.

Amid industry dynamics and economic conditions throughout 2025, Wismilak continued to strengthen its commitment to implementing sustainability principles in executing its business strategy. We reinforced this effort by sharpening our focus on innovation and collaboration. We continue to integrate functions across the organization to strengthen the synergy of environmental, social, and governance (ESG) performance management, while gradually continuing to measure the impacts of these activities.

# Strategi Keberlanjutan dan Kebijakan Merespons Tantangan
## Sustainability Strategy and Policy in Responding to Challenges

Transformasi keberlanjutan dalam dunia usaha mendorong Wismilak untuk terus memperkuat komitmen terhadap pengelolaan ESG. Dengan berpegang pada nilai Bersama Meraih Sukses, Memiliki Integritas, dan Semangat Continuous Improvement, Perseroan berupaya memastikan bahwa setiap kegiatan operasional dilakukan secara bertanggung jawab dan memberikan manfaat bagi masyarakat dan lingkungan.

The sustainability transformation taking place in the business world has encouraged Wismilak to continuously strengthen its commitment to ESG management. Guided by the values of Achieving Success Together, Upholding Integrity, and Maintaining a Spirit of Continuous Improvement, the Company strives to ensure that every operational activity is carried out responsibly and delivers benefits to society and the environment.
