# Tantangan atas Penerapan Keberlanjutan [E-3][2-25] Challenges in the Implementation of Sustainability

Seiring perubahan perilaku konsumen yang mempengaruhi tingkat konsumsi domestik, Wismilak menghadapi tantangan dalam menjaga kinerja usaha di industri tembakau. Perubahan ini mendorong Perseroan menyesuaikan strategi bisnis dan operasional agar tetap selaras dengan preferensi pasar serta perkembangan regulasi dan sosial.

Sebagai respons, Perseroan memperkuat strategi usaha melalui pengelolaan dampak operasional, pengembangan program masyarakat, serta peningkatan kualitas produk dan efektivitas distribusi. Sepanjang 2025, Perseroan juga menerapkan prinsip kehati-hatian dan memperluas kolaborasi dengan pemangku kepentingan. Pendekatan ini mendukung ketahanan dan daya saing usaha di tengah perubahan pola konsumsi dan kondisi pasar.

# Dampak Perubahan Iklim [201-2] Climate Change Impacts

Perseroan mengidentifikasi perubahan iklim ekstrem sebagai risiko yang berpotensi mempengaruhi proses produksi serta ketersediaan bahan baku utama, termasuk tembakau dan cengkeh, yang dapat berdampak pada kinerja operasional dan keuangan. Untuk mengelola risiko tersebut, Perseroan menerapkan strategi pengelolaan bahan baku yang terintegrasi dengan Fungsi Manajemen Risiko, termasuk pengendalian pasokan dan mutu bahan baku.

Hingga 2025, Perseroan tidak mencatat dampak keuangan yang signifikan akibat perubahan iklim terhadap kegiatan operasional. Ke depan, Perseroan akan terus menerapkan proses produksi ramah lingkungan serta memastikan penggunaan bahan baku berkualitas melalui pengendalian mutu sesuai standar yang ditetapkan.

As consumer behavior shifts and affects the level of domestic consumption, Wismilak faces challenges in maintaining business performance in the tobacco industry. These changes have prompted the Company to adjust its business and operational strategies to remain aligned with market preferences, as well as regulatory and social developments.

In response, the Company strengthened its business strategy through the management of operational impacts, the development of community programs, and improvements in product quality and distribution effectiveness. Throughout 2025, the Company also adopted a precautionary approach and expanded collaboration with stakeholders. This approach supported business resilience and competitiveness amid changing consumption patterns and market conditions.

The Company identified extreme climate change as a risk that may affect production processes and the availability of key raw materials, including tobacco and cloves, which may in turn affect operational and financial performance. To manage this risk, the Company implemented a raw material management strategy integrated with the Risk Management Function, including controls over raw material supply and quality.

As of 2025, the Company had not recorded any significant financial impact from climate change on its operations. Going forward, the Company will continue to adopt environmentally friendly production processes and ensure the use of quality raw materials through quality control in accordance with established standards.
