## Capaian
Achievement

Program ini hadir dalam memberikan dukungan kepada 20 Taman Kanak-Kanak di Jawa Timur.

The program provided support to 20 kindergartens in East Java.

## Uraian Kegiatan
Activity Description

Bekerja sama dengan Perempuan Indonesia Maju (PIM) DPD Jawa Timur dan Junior Chamber International Indonesia Chapter East Java, Wismilak melanjutkan program penguatan literasi membaca yang telah dimulai sejak 2016. Kolaborasi ini memberikan dukungan kepada sejumlah anak-anak di Jawa Timur untuk meningkatkan minat dan kemampuan membaca sejak usia dini.

In collaboration with Perempuan Indonesia Maju (PIM) East Java Regional Board and Junior Chamber International Indonesia Chapter East Java, Wismilak continues its reading literacy strengthening program, which has been in place since 2016. This collaboration supports children in East Java in building interest in and ability to read from an early age.
