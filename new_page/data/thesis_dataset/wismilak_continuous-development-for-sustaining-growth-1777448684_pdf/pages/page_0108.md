# Praktik Pengadaan Procurement Practices

Pada 2025, Perseroan meningkatkan kualitas praktik pengadaan melalui penguatan implementasi Sedex Member Ethical Trade Audit (SMETA). Inisiatif ini merupakan bagian dari upaya berkelanjutan untuk meningkatkan transparansi, kepatuhan etika bisnis, serta pengelolaan risiko ESG dalam rantai pasok.

In 2025, the Company improved the quality of its procurement practices by strengthening the implementation of the Sedex Member Ethical Trade Audit (SMETA). This initiative forms part of the Company's ongoing efforts to improve transparency, business ethics compliance, and ESG risk management in the supply chain.
