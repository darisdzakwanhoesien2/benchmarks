# Employee Training and Development

Perseroan meningkatkan kualitas, kapabilitas, dan kesiapan karyawan untuk merespons dinamika industri dan perubahan kebutuhan kompetensi. Pengembangan dilakukan pada seluruh tahapan siklus kerja guna mendukung kinerja dan arah usaha Perseroan. Program yang dijalankan mencakup persiapan generasi penerus, pengelolaan transisi antargenerasi, serta peningkatan dan penyesuaian kompetensi melalui upskilling dan reskilling.

The Company improves employee quality, capabilities, and readiness to respond to industry dynamics and changing competency needs. Development is carried out across all stages of the employment cycle to support the Company's performance and business direction. The programs implemented include succession preparation, the management of intergenerational transition, and competency enhancement and adjustment through upskilling and reskilling.
