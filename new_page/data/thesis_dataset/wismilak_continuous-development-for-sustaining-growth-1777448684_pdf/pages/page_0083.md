Perseroan mengikutsertakan karyawan dalam program Jaminan Hari Tua dan Jaminan Pensiun melalui BPJS Ketenagakerjaan sesuai ketentuan peraturan perundang-undangan. Iuran dibayarkan oleh Perseroan dan karyawan secara proporsional, dengan akses informasi kepesertaan melalui aplikasi JMO.

Selain jaminan sosial, Perseroan menyediakan tunjangan dan kompensasi nonfinansial untuk mendukung kesejahteraan karyawan. Fasilitas tersebut mencakup dukungan telekomunikasi, konsumsi, transportasi, dan manfaat lain yang relevan dengan kebutuhan kerja. Perseroan juga memberikan penghargaan atas pencapaian kinerja, membuka peluang pengembangan karier, serta menyediakan fasilitas pendukung lainnya.

The Company enrolls employees in the Old Age Security and Pension Security programs through BPJS Ketenagakerjaan in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. Contributions are paid proportionally by the Company and employees, with membership information accessible through the JMO application.

In addition to social security, the Company provides non-financial benefits and compensation to support employee well-being. These facilities include telecommunication support, meals, transportation, and other benefits relevant to work needs. The Company also provides recognition for performance achievements, offers career development opportunities, and provides other supporting facilities.

# Cuti Melahirkan [401-3]

## Parental Leave

Hak cuti melahirkan bagi seluruh karyawan, baik wanita maupun pria. Sesuai dengan kebijakan yang berlaku, karyawan wanita berhak mendapatkan cuti melahirkan selama 3 bulan, sedangkan karyawan pria diberikan cuti khusus selama 2 hari saat istrinya melahirkan.

Parental leave is granted to all eligible employees. In accordance with applicable policies, female employees are entitled to 3 months of maternity leave, while male employees are granted 2 days of special leave upon the birth of their child.
