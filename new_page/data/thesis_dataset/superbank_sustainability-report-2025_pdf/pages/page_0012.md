# Sustainability Journey

At Superbank, our sustainability journey represents more than just a commitment, it is the way we create sustainable value for our stakeholders and the communities we serve in the long term. As we continue to grow, we recognize the importance of integrating ESG considerations into our business operations. By enhancing our sustainable finance strategy and transparent reporting, we reaffirm our responsibility to drive positive economic, social, and environmental impacts.

## November - December 2024

- Preparation of the first standalone Sustainability Report in 2024 and alignment with the Sustainable Finance Action Plan (RAKB)
- The first materiality assessment to identify key sustainability focuses based on stakeholder relevance as well as Superbank's strengths and potential

## January - December 2025

- Launch of SuperGROW as Superbank's primary sustainability pillar, including the publication of our inaugural standalone 2024 Sustainability Report and the launch of a dedicated sustainability section on our website.
- Capacity-building programs across all SuperGROW pillars to deepen employee awareness and competency.
- Execution of the Bank's inaugural Climate Risk Management &amp; Scenario (CRMS) stress test, strengthening our readiness in climate-related risk governance.
- Issuance of the Sustainability Policy as a governance umbrella to reinforce a consistent approach across all sustainability initiatives.
- The Bank's IPO, marking a significant step in strengthening Superbank's governance, transparency, and sustainability, while simultaneously increasing capital capacity to support our progression as a KBMI 2 bank.

## April 2026

Superbank has published its 2025 Sustainability Report, marking a significant milestone in the Bank's sustainability journey.

## May 2026 - Onwards

Superbank continues to explore and strengthen both its short-term and long-term frameworks within the Bank's sustainability journey through more structured processes and deeper engagement across the entire organization.

# Sustainability Governance

## Parties Accountable for Sustainable Finance

At Superbank, our dedication to integrity, transparency, and sustainable value creation is deeply rooted in our sustainability governance framework. By strategically embedding ESG principles across the organization, we elevate sustainability from a mere compliance obligation into a fundamental business imperative. This governance framework is designed to drive ethical behavior, ensure regulatory adherence, and maximize stakeholder value, thereby solidifying our commitment to building a resilient and inclusive financial ecosystem.

Our sustainability efforts are guided by robust oversight and structured governance. The Board of Directors provides strategic direction and ensures compliance with sustainability commitments, while the Board of Commissioners, through the Risk Oversight Committee, monitors risk mitigation and compliance. Dedicated cross-functional teams collaborate to formulate, implement, and monitor sustainability initiatives, ensuring alignment with global best practices and the evolving regulatory landscape.

To ensure rigorous oversight of our sustainability aspirations, Superbank has established a specialized cross-functional unit called the "Sustainability Squad." Comprised of members from various departments, this team fosters continuous collaboration to oversee and report on sustainability progress in the coming years. The Sustainability Squad has a direct reporting line to the Board of Directors and to the Board of Commissioners via the Risk Oversight Committee every six months. ESG-related matters, including sustainability plans, progress, and key developments, are discussed within the respective functional governance meetings.

# Sustainable Finance Competency Development

Competency development for the parties accountable for Sustainable Finance implementation at Superbank is a fundamental element in ensuring the effective integration of sustainability principles into business operations. Equipped with a comprehensive understanding of ESG aspects, Superbank is able to formulate strategic policies that are not only aligned with regulatory requirements but also relevant to global best practice standards.

Through a capacity-building approach—encompassing structured training, certifications, and collaboration with stakeholders—we continue to strengthen the organization's capability to navigate the increasingly complex dynamics of sustainability. This commitment serves as a catalyst for innovation in our sustainable finance strategy, ensuring Superbank's optimal contribution toward inclusive and environmentally friendly economic growth.
