# President Director's Remarks and Explanation on the Sustainability

![img-18.jpeg](img-18.jpeg)

## Dear Valued Stakeholders,

The year 2025 marks the beginning of a transformative new era for Superbank. Our successful debut on the Indonesia Stock Exchange as a public company, coupled with our elevation to the KBMI 2 bank category, stands as a testament to our evolving business maturity. To us, attaining public company status is more than a corporate achievement; it is a mandate to uphold high standards of transparency and accountability. This transformation serves as a primary catalyst for us to solidify our comprehensive Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) framework, ensuring that every stride of future growth is resilient, responsible, and creates long-term value for all our stakeholders.

The implementation of sustainable finance at Superbank is built upon a foundation of mature risk governance and compliance with regulatory expectations. In 2025, Superbank successfully completed its inaugural Climate Risk Management &amp; Scenario (CRMS) stress test to ensure the resilience of the Bank's business model against various transition and physical risk scenarios.

Through this initial implementation, we have gained critical insights into how climate scenarios may impact our portfolio resilience, credit quality, and long-term financial performance. This achievement strengthens our foundation in climate-related risk governance and aligns our practices with OJK's expectations and emerging global standards.

## 1. Policies to Address Challenges in Achieving the Sustainability Strategy

In responding to the dynamics of the 2025 sustainability landscape, marked by global macroeconomic complexities and escalating climate risks, Superbank has taken strategic steps by proactively strengthening its risk management architecture. To mitigate the increasingly tangible impacts of both physical and transition risks, the Bank responsively completed its Climate Risk Management &amp; Scenario (CRMS) stress test to precisely assess credit portfolio resilience and secure long-term business model sustainability amidst environmental uncertainty.

Meanwhile, Superbank's transformation into a public company is embraced as strategic momentum to accelerate organizational governance and accountability standards to a higher level. Addressing the urgent need for internal competency in this new era, the Bank moved swiftly to align human resource capabilities through intensive and targeted ESG capacity-building programs across various functional lines. This strategic response is institutionally reinforced through the establishment of the Sustainability Policy and the integration of the SuperGROW framework, ensuring that sustainability principles are not merely compliance activities but are deeply embedded as strategic pillars in every operational decision-making process of the Bank.

## 2. Sustainable Finance Implementation

To fulfill our mission of creating a 'Lasting Impact' for society and the environment, Superbank adopts a holistic approach to implementing Sustainable Finance principles. We realize this commitment through our SuperGROW strategy, a sustainability aspiration ecosystem designed to address global challenges while meeting local needs. Through this strategy, we ensure that sustainability is not merely a theoretical concept but is deeply embedded in every service interaction and business decision we make.
