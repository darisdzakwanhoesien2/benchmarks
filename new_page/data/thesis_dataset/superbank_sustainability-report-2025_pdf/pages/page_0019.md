## Occupational Health and Safety (OHS)

Workplace safety is fundamental to fostering a productive and healthy environment where employees feel secure and valued. This commitment demands the implementation of rigorous measures to mitigate workplace accidents, injuries, and occupational hazards. Key actions include providing adequate training, ensuring the availability of safety equipment, and maintaining an organized workspace. By upholding strict safety standards and regulations, the organization protects its workforce while reducing operational disruptions associated with workplace incidents. Generally, these safety procedures encompass various aspects, such as:

## Building Access

1. Restricted access for third parties/guests, with a requirement for a designated person-in-charge to meet and escort guests throughout their stay in the office.
2. Installation of monitoring devices (such as CCTV).
3. Periodic reviews of physical building access and workplace environmental controls to ensure safety, including fire detection and suppression, electrical data adjustments, air conditioning (HVAC) regulation, humidity control, and other related measures.

## Implementation of Occupational Safety

1. All work equipment must be stored in a secure location.
2. Prohibition of eating, drinking, and smoking in areas containing information storage facilities or computer centers (server rooms).
3. Provision of regularly updated emergency contact numbers, including the police, building security, fire department, ambulance, nearest hospitals, National Electricity Company (PLN), Regional Disaster Management Agency (BPBD), and others.
4. Fire risk protection by providing and maintaining fire suppression systems, water sprinklers, smoke detectors, and Portable Fire Extinguishers (APAR) in easily accessible areas, establishing disaster protection and office evacuation procedures in accordance with Business Continuity Management (BCM) and other relevant protocols, and storing flammable materials outside computer and communication rooms. With the support of the building management where we operate, fire drills are conducted regularly to ensure all employees can respond quickly and effectively in fire emergencies.
5. Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) are compiled to provide information for all employees regarding potential hazards (health, fire, reactivity, and environmental impact) as well as guidance for working safely with chemical products.

Our dedication to providing a safe and healthy workplace is formalized through Superbank's comprehensive commitment to OHS compliance. We ensure this through ongoing adherence to regulatory mandates, meticulous implementation of safety protocols, and the promotion of a work culture that prioritizes awareness and accountability for the well-being of all personnel, contractors, and relevant stakeholders.

## Social Aspects

### Impact of Operations on Local Communities

As a bank that provides digital services whose operations are conducted without the need for physical branch offices (branchless), the negative impacts of Superbank's operational activities on local communities are relatively limited. An operational model that prioritizes digital services helps minimize the potential environmental and social impacts typically associated with large-scale physical operations, such as noise, traffic congestion, disruption of community activities, or changes in regional spatial planning.

On the other hand, digital technology-based operations provide indirect positive impacts for the community through the provision of financial services that are more accessible, efficient, and inclusive. The utilization of digital platforms allows the public to access banking services without depending on the physical presence of branch offices, thereby increasing convenience, time efficiency, and service reach across various levels of society.

To anticipate potential impacts that the public might experience from digital technology-based operations, Superbank continuously carries out digital financial literacy and inclusion efforts. This initiative aims to enhance public understanding of using digital financial services safely and responsibly, supported by strengthened governance, system security, and consumer protection to ensure that the benefits of digital operations can be felt broadly and sustainably.

### Community Grievances Received by Superbank

The Bank provides communication channels for the public to submit positive feedback or constructive grievances regarding its operational and/or sustainability activities via email at sustainability@superbank.id. As of the end of 2025, the Bank has received no environmental-related community grievances concerning Superbank's operational activities.
