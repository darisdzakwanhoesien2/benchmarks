## Material Aspects

Superbank adopts a responsible approach in selecting operational support materials. We prioritize the use of eco-friendly materials for all office furniture, ensuring that our workspaces are not only ergonomic but also have a low environmental impact. We recognize that environmental responsibility extends beyond mere compliance; it encompasses proactive efforts in managing resources, energy, and emissions for long-term sustainability.

In line with our digital business model, we promote a paperless culture across all internal and external operational aspects. The transformation from paper-based administrative processes to a fully digital documentation system, including the customer onboarding process, has significantly reduced our reliance on paper materials.

## 2025 Performance

**235 kg papers**

Total paper consumption is recorded as highly efficient, reflecting the success of the bank's digitalized workflows.

## Energy Aspect

Superbank's energy management strategy focuses on two main pillars: technological infrastructure optimization and energy-efficient behavior. As a technology-based service provider, we strategically utilize cloud server infrastructure. This step has proven effective in maximizing resource utilization while significantly cutting energy consumption compared to conventional data centers.

Within the workspace, the smart green office concept is implemented through regular energy monitoring and internal campaigns that encourage employees to switch off lights and electronic devices when not in use for extended periods.

## 2025 Performance

**193,534.31 kWh**

An increase of 12.8% from the previous year is in line with business operational expansion and the growth in headcount, yet it remains managed within a framework of strict efficiency.

## Water Aspect

Superbank strives for a prudent approach to clean water management across all office facilities. Simple yet impactful initiatives, such as replacing bottled plastic drinking water with glass carafes and water dispensers in meeting rooms, not only reduce waste but also optimize water consumption.

### 2025 Performance

**268 m3**

The recording of water consumption reflects the Bank's efforts to manage water usage more efficiently as part of our commitment to resource conservation and sustainable operational practices.

### 2025 Performance

**400 Trees**

This partnership has resulted in tree planting initiatives that represent Superbank's tangible contribution to Indonesia's climate action agenda, as well as our efforts to maintain ecosystem balance and biodiversity.

## Emission Aspect

The year 2025 marked a historic milestone with the completion of our first Climate Risk Management System (CRMS) stress test. Using the 2024 Scope 2 emissions database (recorded at 146.86 tCO₂e) as a benchmark, we continue to monitor our emissions profile to anticipate the impact of climate change on the resilience of our portfolio and Bank operations. The utilization of cloud technology in partnership with global providers committed to carbon-free energy remains our primary strategy for mitigating Greenhouse Gas (GHG) emissions.

## Waste and Effluent Aspect

Superbank combats plastic waste through our monthly 'Plastic-Free Weeks' program. Under this initiative, employees are encouraged to reduce their use of single-use plastics.

### 2025 Performance

**Reduced &gt; 500 bottles/month**

This program has successfully diverted more than 500 plastic bottles from potential waste each month within the office environment. Furthermore, the digitalization of documents has minimized paper waste, creating a cleaner and more efficient operational cycle.

The Bank recorded a total of 14.64 tons of waste disposed on an annualized basis, calculated using a limited data population.

## Environmental Grievance Mechanisms

Superbank consistently complies with all prevailing environmental laws and regulations. We provide transparent grievance mechanisms for stakeholders to raise concerns regarding the environmental impact of our operations.

## 2025 Performance

Throughout the reporting period, there were no environmental-related grievances received, and no monetary or non-monetary sanctions were imposed on the Bank for non-compliance with environmental regulations.
