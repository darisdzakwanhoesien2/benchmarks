# Company Profile

PT Super Bank Indonesia Tbk ("Superbank") focuses on providing banking products and services powered by the effective utilization of digital technology. By integrating technology across all operations and customer touchpoints, Superbank continuously enhances service quality, operational efficiency, and the overall customer experience in alignment with evolving financial needs.

Over the past three years, Superbank's growth has been bolstered by an increasingly robust shareholder structure and strategic partnerships with various parties. In 2025, Superbank entered a new chapter as a public company and ascended to the KBMI 2 category, strengthening its capital position, governance framework, and capacity to support sustainable and inclusive growth.

## VISION

To be a Bank that empowers all people with simple, transparent, and flexible financial products by leveraging appropriate technology.

## MISSION

To provide digital financial solutions that more effectively meet the financial service needs of MSME and retail customers.
