In 2025, Superbank underwent a significant transformation in its status and structure through an Initial Public Offering (IPO), marking its transition into a public company listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange. This change carries material implications for heightened standards of transparency, accountability, and public disclosure obligations.

Additionally, Superbank was reclassified as a KBMI 2 bank following an increase in core capital exceeding Rp6 trillion. This reclassification has strengthened the bank's capital structure and enhanced its ability to manage risks and support sustainable business growth.

Building on this foundation, Superbank's 2025 sustainability strategy focuses on integrating Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles across all operational lines. Superbank consistently aligns business priorities with sustainable practices through prudent governance and disciplined risk management, ensuring readiness to navigate regulatory dynamics and meet the expectations of customers, investors, the public, and regulators.

# SuperGROW: From Aspiration to Culture

The SuperGROW aspiration remains the primary compass of our sustainability journey. This theme is not merely a slogan, but a tangible embodiment of the company's core value, "Make an Impact." We believe that true sustainability is born when innovation and social responsibility converge in every business decision.

To that end, we are committed to continuing and maximizing the implementation of SuperGROW across the Bank's activities. Through ongoing internalization programs, we ensure that the principles of People, Profit, and Planet are understood and practiced by every stakeholder at Superbank. Consequently, sustainability is no longer a separate initiative but a standard of conduct that defines how we work and serve, in order to create meaningful long-term value for a better future.

# Super-Green

Promoting sustainable digital banking by developing climate governance and risk management within the banking portfolio, eco-friendly operations, and smart green offices, by reducing our environmental impact while simultaneously promoting responsible finance.

# Super-Reliable and Safe

Ensuring data privacy and digital security, delivering a secure and regulatory-compliant digital banking experience, and supporting the creation of a safe, healthy, and supportive work environment for all employees.

# Super-Opportunity

Empowering the underbanked community and MSME players through accessible banking services, offering inclusive financial solutions, tailored loans, and seamless digital transactions to drive economic growth and sustainable financial independence.

# Super-Women

Supporting women's leadership by nurturing female leaders and empowering women entrepreneurs in the MSME sector through tailored financing solutions.
