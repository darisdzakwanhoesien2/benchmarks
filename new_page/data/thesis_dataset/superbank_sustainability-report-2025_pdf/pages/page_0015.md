## Sustainable Finance Performance Review

Solid economic performance serves as the foundation for Superbank to expand service access for underserved communities. Through a customer-centric approach supported by digital innovation, we strive to transform accessibility challenges into long-term economic opportunities for our customers.

This commitment is realized through our flagship product, 'Pinjaman Atur Sendin' (PAS), an unsecured credit facility that offers financial management flexibility for users. The high adoption rate of this product not only contributes to the surge in customer numbers within the Superbank ecosystem but also serves as tangible evidence of our success in driving inclusive economic growth, where every individual has an equal opportunity to thrive.

The following are several overviews of Superbank's sustainable economic performance achievements in 2025:

- The total number of individuals, including underbanked groups, who received unsecured loans through PAS increased by more than 306% YrY.
- Through a measured financing strategy, Superbank successfully increased its inclusive financing reach, growing by 49% YrY to Rp 4.0 Trillion. This success is reflected in the Inclusive and Macroprudential Financing Ratio (RPIM) of 41.6%, in line with Superbank's vision to promote an inclusive and sustainable economy.
- In line with our effective acquisition strategy, Superbank's customer base has continued to grow consistently and is now approaching the 6 million customer mark.

This comprehensive achievement reinforces Superbank's position as a next-generation bank that provides digital banking services that focuses not only on innovative financial solutions but also prioritizes security, relevance, and sustainable value creation for all stakeholders.
