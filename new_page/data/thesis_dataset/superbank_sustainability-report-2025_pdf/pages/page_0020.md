# Responsibility for Sustainable Product Development and Financial Services

Superbank believes that the banking products of the future must not only be relevant and accessible but also socially and environmentally responsible. Through our SuperGROW strategy, we ensure that every product and service innovation launched has undergone a comprehensive impact assessment, upholds the principles of consumer protection, and contributes positively to national financial inclusion. In line with this, Superbank is committed to realizing our Super-Reliable and Safe aspiration by prioritizing the security of the banking ecosystem. We integrate reliable security systems throughout the entire business cycle to guarantee transparent and integrated services.

# Innovation and Development of Sustainable Products and Financial Services

As a digital-first bank, innovation is at the heart of our sustainability strategy. Superbank develops sustainable financial products and services with Social and Economic Aspects as our primary approach:

1. Loan Products: We introduced the Pinjaman Atur Sendiri (PAS) – a 'Self-Adjusting Loan' product specifically designed to serve the underbanked segment. This product utilizes alternative credit scoring based on data analytics, enabling individuals who previously lacked access to formal banking to obtain fair and transparent financing.
2. Savings Products:

Main Savings Account: An account that is automatically created when a customer successfully registers as a Superbank customer. Customers can use the Main Savings Account for sending and receiving transfers from other banks.

Saku by Superbank: Additional savings account designed to help allocate customers' money according to their specific needs. Saku can also be named to match the customer's allocation preferences.

OVO Nabung: Enables one-click account opening for Superbank's digital savings within the OVO app, offering interest rates of up to 5% p.a. with seamless transactions.

Celengan by Superbank: A type of savings account that enables automatic saving. There are two available methods for saving: Auto-Fill or Non-Auto-Fill. The amount saved will be adjusted based on the chosen deposit frequency by the customer. Customers have the opportunity to earn higher interest rate of up to 10% p.a. and the balance can be withdrawn at any time.

Deposito by Superbank: Savings product in the form of a deposit that can be accessed through the application, offering benefits with interest rates of up to 7.5% p.a. where the term can start as early as 7 days and can be withdrawn earlier before the maturity date without any administrative fees.

Superbank x Grab Integration: Integrates digital banking services into the Grab ecosystem through direct debit and in-app onboarding, enabling automatic payments, instant account opening within the Grab app, and same-day settlement for partners/merchants to drive the adoption of Superbank's services.

Lucky Pocket: Offers customers daily cash rewards by saving once and selecting a Lucky Pocket each day, with prizes credited directly to the main savings account and additional chances for customers who participate consistently for 7 consecutive days.
