# Risk Assessment of Sustainable Finance Implementation

Superbank implements a systematic and structured sustainable finance monitoring mechanism to ensure the integration of sustainability principles across all operational lines and business strategies. This oversight process is under the direct supervision of the Board of Directors, with support from relevant business units responsible for ensuring policy implementation and the fulfilment of sustainability aspects in alignment with OJK regulations and global ESG standards.

Evaluations of implementation effectiveness are conducted periodically through sustainability performance measurements, internal reporting mechanisms, and audits of ongoing initiatives. The scope of this monitoring includes a comprehensive identification of sustainability risks and opportunities to maintain long-term business resilience. In this regard, Superbank has mapped the potential risks faced and formulated relevant strategic mitigation measures, as follows:

|  No. | Type of Risk | Risk Mitigation  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  1. | Credit Risk | The Bank mitigates credit risk through a holistic oversight mechanism across all aspects of the credit portfolio. We apply strict prudent banking principles in every lending decision, supported by highly selective debtor and issuer screening strategies. Furthermore, the Bank proactively reviews alternative fund allocation diversification to minimize risk concentration and maintain the quality of earning assets.  |
|  2. | Market Risk | Market risk management is focused on resilience against external volatility. The Bank mitigates this risk by conducting regular market risk sensitivity analyses and implementing a comprehensive market risk management policy framework. This measure ensures that market risk management operates effectively in responding to interest rate dynamics and exchange rate fluctuations that could impact the Bank's performance.  |
|  3. | Liquidity Risk | To maintain a healthy liquidity position, the Bank implements a disciplined fund management strategy through in-depth cash flow analysis and maturity profiling. The Bank also routinely performs stress tests to measure readiness in facing liquidity pressure scenarios. All these efforts are reinforced by a series of solid liquidity risk management policies, ensuring the Bank's ability to meet both short-term and long-term obligations in a timely manner.  |
|  No. | Type of Risk | Risk Mitigation  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  4. | Operational Risk | The Bank mitigates operational risk by implementing a disciplined risk identification and measurement framework according to established frequencies. Operational risk impact management is conducted systematically through the utilization of risk management tools such as Risk Control Self-Assessment (RCSA), the IT Risk Register—which is crucial to our digital ecosystem—and the Loss Event Database (LED). These measures ensure that the Bank's operational resilience remains intact amidst dynamic business activities.  |
|  5. | Reputational Risk | Maintaining public trust is Superbank's top priority. Reputational risk mitigation is carried out through responsive customer aspiration and complaint handling mechanisms, alongside integrated recording of any events that could potentially affect the Bank's image. These efforts are supported by adequate reputational risk management policies to ensure that positive stakeholder perceptions are maintained.  |
|  6. | Legal Risk | The Bank mitigates legal risk through thorough and periodic reviews of all agreement documents and cooperation contracts. Management is committed to ensuring legal certainty and maintaining the Bank's strong juridical position in every business engagement to protect the interests of both the Bank and its customers.  |
|  7. | Strategic Risk | Strategic risk management is focused on intensive monitoring of the alignment between actual performance and the Bank Business Plan (RBB). The Bank mitigates this risk by identifying potential deviations early and periodically measuring strategic risk impacts. This is reinforced by adaptive strategic risk management policies, ensuring that the Bank's business direction remains relevant to its long-term vision.  |
|  8. | Compliance Risk | As a bank operating with a digital service-based business model, Superbank pays special attention to compliance risk mitigation. This includes proactive identification processes for risks that may arise from digital innovation, rigorous reviews of new product launches, and the continuous strengthening of internal control system mechanisms.  |

# Stakeholder Engagement

Superbank views stakeholder engagement as a fundamental pillar in ensuring business sustainability and long-term value creation. We believe that transparent, constructive, and collaborative communication is key to aligning the expectations of various parties, ranging from regulators, customers, business partners, and employees to the wider community, within an ever-evolving industrial ecosystem.

Through active and participatory dialogue, Superbank proactively identifies opportunities for service innovation while simultaneously mitigating risks to ensure compliance with applicable regulations and sustainability standards. This inclusive approach not only strengthens stakeholder trust but also serves as a catalyst in realizing Superbank's aspirations as an innovative and sustainable digital banking service.
