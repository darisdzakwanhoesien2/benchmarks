# Security Evaluation of Products and Services

Superbank places customer security as its top priority by ensuring that 100% of our products and services have undergone thorough security impact evaluations before being launched. We implement a multi-layered protection approach that encompasses both cybersecurity and financial integrity. All products operate under the ISO 27001:2022 standard, featuring advanced data encryption and independent security testing (penetration testing) to guarantee privacy and service availability. Furthermore, transactional integrity is maintained through the AML/CFT and CPF (APU PPT PPPSPM) programs, which integrate digital biometric verification and AI-based real-time fraud detection systems. This comprehensive evaluation ensures that every innovation we introduce is not only solution-oriented but also secure from cyber risks and financial crimes, in compliance with prevailing regulations.

# Impact of Products and Services

Superbank's products and services are designed to create a positive multiplier effect for the grassroots economy:

## Rapid and Widespread Public Adoption of Products and Services::

The success of Superbank's product penetration strategy is reflected in our growing customer base, which is now nearing 6 million users. This proves that our products are highly relevant and deliver a significant impact for the broader community.

## Prevention of Over-Indebtedness::

The use of an alternative credit scoring engine for PAS (Pinjaman Atur Sendiri) allows us to assess a customer's repayment capacity more accurately by tailoring it to their profile, capability, and needs. This approach prevents over-extension of credit, which could potentially trap customers in a cycle of debt.

# Number of Product Recalls

Throughout 2025, there were no banking products and/or services withdrawn from circulation due to system defects, regulatory violations, or security risks. All launched products have obtained formal approval and registration from the Financial Services Authority (OJK) and Bank Indonesia.
