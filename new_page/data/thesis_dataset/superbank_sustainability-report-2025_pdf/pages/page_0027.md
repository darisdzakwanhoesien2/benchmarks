# Superbank's Super-GROW Supports the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

Superbank's Sustainability Aspiration, Super-GROW, is committed to driving sustainability by aligning its efforts with the SDGs. Through innovative practices and responsible operations, we aim to create lasting value for our stakeholders while contributing to address critical global challenges such as climate change, social equity, and economic growth. By integrating sustainability principles into our strategies, products, and community initiatives, we strive to contribute meaningfully to a more inclusive and sustainable future for all.

|  Sustainability Aspirations | SDG | Approach  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Super-Green | • SDG 7 - Affordable and Clean Energy
• SDG 13 - Climate Action | As a bank that provides digital baking services, Superbank is deeply committed to promoting sustainability through environmentally friendly operations. We have adopted a paperless approach to support a green office, significantly reducing paper waste and fostering a more sustainable workplace. Additionally, we have taken steps to minimize the use of plastic bottles from our consumption, further reducing our environmental footprint.

We have also streamlined our operations by reducing the number of physical branches, which not only enhances operational efficiency but also contributes to lowering transportation-related emissions. Furthermore, by promoting a hybrid working arrangement, we support flexible work options that help reduce gas consumption and decrease the environmental impact of daily commuting.  |
|  Super-Reliable and Safe | • SDG 16 - Promote peaceful and inclusive societies for sustainable development, provide access to justice for all and build effective, accountable and inclusive institutions at all levels. | To safeguard data and ensure privacy, security, and transparency for our customers, Superbank prioritize strong ethical standards and robust internal controls within our organization.

We provide comprehensive ethics and anti-money laundering training to all employees, ensuring that they are equipped to uphold the highest standards of integrity and security. Equally important, we also ensure adherence to our Code of Ethics and Code of Conduct across all levels, reinforcing our commitment to ethical behavior and responsible management of sensitive information. Additionally, our compliance officer conducts regular internal control reviews, with detailed reports submitted to the board, ensuring that data security and integrity are maintained at all times.

These measures not only protect against cybercrime and potential breaches but also promote transparency and accountability, fostering trust in our operations and ensuring the responsible management of both institutional and public data.  |
