## D.1 Building an Inclusive and Responsive Work Environment

We believe that an inclusive work environment begins by recognizing and supporting the unique needs of every individual. Our strategy goes beyond mere regulatory compliance, focusing on providing fair remuneration and benefits as well as holistic wellbeing programs.

## D.2 Remuneration Equality and Flexible Benefits

Superbank implements a fair compensation policy for all employees, regardless of gender or employment status. In addition to standard benefits in accordance with applicable regulations, we pay special attention to employee health through various flexible supplementary benefits.

To support work-life balance, we provide the Flexi-Benefit program, which can be tailored to the personal needs of employees:

a. **Flexi Spending Account**: Funds that can be used for self-development, out-of-pocket healthcare costs, immediate family care, and work-related activity support.

b. **Flexi Wellbeing Account**: Includes a Mental Wellbeing Account and a Preventive Healthcare Account, which are specific allocations for early detection and health protection (medical check-ups and vaccinations) as well as psychological support, enabling employees to proactively prioritize their physical and mental health.

## D.3 Super Women and Allies Club (SWCA): A Space to Grow Together

The year 2025 marked the official launch of the Super Women and Allies Club (SWCA). This forum is not only intended for women but also embraces male colleagues (allies) to create a collaborative ecosystem. SWCA serves as a platform for employees to learn, share perspectives, and support one another in their professional journeys. Throughout the year, this commitment was realized through a series of activities ranging from speed mentorship and external coaching to internal social gatherings.

## D.4 2025 Super-Women Activities

Throughout 2025, Superbank held various strategic initiatives to celebrate and empower the role of women:

a. Launch of SuperGROW &amp; SWCA: In celebration of International Women's Day, Superbank launched its SuperGROW sustainability aspirations and officially inaugurated the Super Women and Allies Club (SWCA). The event featured the debut of an inspirational speaker series sharing stories of resilience and purpose.

b. "Modal, Mimpi, dan Masa Depan" Talk Show: An in-depth discussion on the power of access to capital and education for women. Featuring Katrina Inandia (Amartha), Yudhi Pradaria (B Market Builder), and Superbank's CFO, Melisa Hendrawati, the event highlighted how inclusive finance and practical financial literacy can create a tangible impact for female entrepreneurs.

c. Kartini Day Celebration: Under the theme "Honoring Kartini's Legacy in Today's World," this event featured an interactive dialogue with three Superbank leaders: Rustini Dewi (Audit Committee), Cut Azia (Chief of Internal Audit), and Qiyam Padriansy (Head of Treasury). The discussion focused on strategies for balancing assertiveness and empathy in leadership, mental health, and the harmonization of professional and family roles.

d. Self-Development &amp; Literacy: Closing the year with a book review of "Anak Kantoran" (The Office Kid) combined with a meditation session and a potluck, strengthening community bonds while supporting employee mental health.

## 3. Strategy for Achieving Targets

Superbank's sustainability aspirations for the 2025-2027 period will continue to be guided by the SuperGROW pillars. This theme reflects our commitment to continuous growth and innovation through four core aspirations:

- Super-Green (G): Driving sustainable digital banking through the gradual establishment of governance structures and climate risk management frameworks, creating eco-friendly and efficient operations and office environments.
- Super-Reliable and Safe (R): Ensuring data security, digital privacy, and strict anti-money laundering compliance, while striving for a healthy workplace culture and environment.
- Super-Opportunity (O): Expanding financial inclusion and empowering underbanked communities and MSMEs through innovative digital solutions.
- Super-Women (W): Increasing female leadership in senior management and providing financial access for women entrepreneurs.

This strategy is aligned with our core value, "Make an Impact," which aims to create meaningful sustainable growth for all people across Indonesia.
