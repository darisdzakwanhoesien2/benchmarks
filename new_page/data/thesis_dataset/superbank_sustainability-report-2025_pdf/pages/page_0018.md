# Commitment to the Prevention of Forced Labor

Superbank holds a steadfast commitment preventing forced labor and ensuring full compliance with labor regulations to protect employee rights, particularly regarding flexible working arrangements and leave entitlements. To effectively uphold these standards, we have established comprehensive corporate policies that provide clear guidance on the following:

- Fair and regular working hours, ensuring the equitable distribution of tasks and responsibilities.
- Leave entitlements, guaranteeing employees' rights to rest, personal time, and well-being.

By implementing these policies, Superbank fosters a healthy, balanced, and legally compliant work environment.

# Commitment to the Prohibition of Child Labor

In strict adherence to all legal requirements, Superbank ensures that every employee has reached the legal working age (17 years), which is confirmed through verified identification during the recruitment process. The institution strictly prohibits and does not employ child labor, maintaining full compliance with all relevant laws governing the employment of minors.

# Compliance with Regional Minimum Wage Standards

Superbank's remuneration policy guarantees fair compensation for all employees through strict compliance with minimum wage regulations across every operational region (Jakarta, Bandung, and Yogyakarta). Superbank's dedication to fair wages is demonstrated through two primary practices: setting the base salary for permanent staff in entry-level roles above the Regional Minimum Wage (UMK), and ensuring absolute pay equity regardless of gender. These standards are continuously monitored to maintain competitive and equitable compensation for our team.

# Decent and Safe Working Environment

At Superbank, we believe that a safe workplace is a culture where everyone feels comfortable, respected, and supported. Superbank encourages open conversations regarding boundaries, respectful communication, and ways to support colleagues when concerns arise. This approach ensures that workplace safety is not only practically implemented but truly experienced and felt by our employees. Aligned with the Company's aspiration to foster a safe work environment, Superbank's commitment to employee well-being has earned external recognition through the Bronze Award for Best Employee Wellness Strategy at the Employee Experience Awards Indonesia 2025. This achievement reflects the Company's ongoing efforts to cultivate a workplace that protects both the physical and mental wellbeing of its employees, promotes psychological safety, and supports a healthy work-life balance as an integral part of its sustainability agenda.

# Training and Capability Development

In supporting operational activities, the Bank is committed to enhancing Human Resources (HR) capabilities through training mechanisms, coaching and mentoring, and strategic assignments. Mandatory training is conducted consistently to ensure all Bank personnel possess the necessary competencies and skills. In line with digital transformation, the Bank makes strategic investments in future-ready skills through 15 training sessions related to Data Protection &amp; Security and AI. Furthermore, the Bank proactively monitors the fulfillment of mandatory professional certifications, such as Risk Management Certification (SMR), Treasury Dealer, and SK-SP, to ensure organizational readiness in facing the dynamic challenges of the financial industry.

# Internal Capacity Building on Sustainability

As part of its training and development, Superbank prioritizes the introduction and deepening of sustainability as a core element of organizational capacity building under the SuperGROW framework. Our sustainability approach is not treated as a standalone topic rather, it is a learning initiative deliberately embedded through various internal activities to integrate ESG into our mindset, culture, and decision-making processes.

![img-35.jpeg](img-35.jpeg)

Throughout 2025, sustainability-related learning activities covered aspects of financial inclusion and/or financial literacy, product knowledge and/or development, green initiatives, risk (including climate risk), data protection and governance, digital security, cybersecurity, asset protection, consumer protection, AML-CFT-CPF (Anti-Money Laundering, Counter-Financing of Terrorism, and Counter-Proliferation Financing of Weapons of Mass Destruction), compliance, safe workspaces and environments, as well as leadership training. Beyond knowledge sharing, these sessions provided a space to critically explore how sustainability principles intersect with business strategy, risk awareness, and long-term value creation.
