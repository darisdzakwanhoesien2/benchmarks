# A. Super-Green: Commitment to Green and Sustainable Operations

The Super-Green pillar reflects Superbank's dedication to realizing a digital banking service that is not only technologically advanced but also environmentally responsible. We implement smart green office concepts to maximize energy efficiency and promote the prudent use of resources. Beyond internal efficiency, we minimize our carbon footprint through digital technology innovations that enable financial services without physical boundaries. This commitment is further strengthened by the development of a green portfolio and sustainable finance practices focused on climate-conscious investments, serving as an environmental risk mitigation effort for long-term sustainability.

## A.1 Environmental Risk Analysis &amp; Climate Governance

The year 2025 marks a new milestone in Superbank's sustainability journey with the successful completion of our inaugural risk stress test (CRMS). Using 2024 as the baseline year, this stress test recorded a Scope 2 emissions efficiency performance of 146.86 tCO2e. This achievement provides a robust data foundation to anticipate the impacts of climate change on the resilience of our credit portfolio and future financial performance. We view this inaugural CRMS initiative as the first step in a long-term journey toward climate governance maturity. Moving forward, Superbank is committed to:

a. Continuously refining methodologies and data quality for climate risk analysis to align with global standards and OJK regulations.
b. Deeply integrating climate considerations into the Bank's strategic planning and risk management.
c. Identifying long-term energy efficiency opportunities to support the transition toward low-emission operations.

## A.2 Innovation for Sustainable Eco-Friendly Business

We actively expand our financial support for environmentally oriented businesses, including through strategic participation in the Grab Ventures Velocity program. Through a collaboration with Jejak in, an environmental technology platform, we have realized the planting of 400 verified trees as a tangible contribution to carbon offset initiatives. This partnership reinforces Superbank's vision to integrate technology, social impact, and sustainability, while continuing to open financing opportunities for green businesses in the future. Furthermore, the Bank held an inspiring knowledge-sharing session, titled "Path to Green and Inclusive Products," in collaboration with Grab Indonesia's sustainability team, which shared practical strategies for community empowerment through technology and sustainable initiatives.

## A.3 Smart Green Office: Cultivating a Sustainable Mindset

We believe that green transformation begins with daily habits. Therefore, Superbank implements various initiatives to foster environmentally conscious behavior among employees, including:

a. Plastic-Free Weeks: A monthly program that successfully reduced the use of more than 500 plastic bottles per month, supported by replacing bottled water with glass pitchers in meeting rooms.
b. Energy Efficiency: Encouraging an energy-saving culture by turning off unused electronic devices and implementing regular monitoring of electricity consumption.

## A.4 Eco-Friendly Operations: The Advantage of Digital Banking Services

As a technology-based institution, we leverage digital innovation to significantly reduce our environmental impact through three primary approaches:

a. **Branchless Operations**: Our digital technology enables customers to transact anytime without the need to visit a physical location. This directly reduces carbon emissions from customer travel and eliminates the energy consumption associated with conventional brick-and-mortar branch buildings.
b. **Paperless Culture**: We have optimally transitioned from paper-based administrative processes to a digital documentation ecosystem. This initiative spans from digital customer onboarding to internal documentation, aimed at reducing paper waste while simultaneously enhancing data security and workflow efficiency.
c. **Sustainable Cloud Infrastructure**: Superbank strategically utilizes cloud infrastructure instead of conventional physical servers. By partnering with global cloud providers committed to carbon-free energy, we ensure IT operations that are adaptive, secure, and significantly more energy-efficient than conventional data centers.

# B. Super-Reliable and Safe: Uncompromising Integrity and Security

The Super-Reliable and Safe pillar is a manifestation of Superbank's priority in maintaining security and operational integrity. This strategic foundation is built upon robust data protection, a comprehensive AML-CFT (Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Financing of Terrorism) program, and the creation of a safe and conducive working environment. Through the implementation of high-standard security systems and proactive illegal activity detection, we ensure a transparent, secure, and trusted banking environment for all our customers.

## B.1 Data Security and Protection: A Holistic Approach

Superbank provides digital banking services that place financial control in the hands of the customer, backed by a robust security infrastructure that protects data privacy in every transaction. Our integrated security approach is embedded across the entire business cycle, reflected in our operational governance, product development, human capital capabilities, and technology.

a. **Governance and Product Development**: Our commitment to responsible data management is enshrined in our transparent internal governance architecture (detailed in the 2024 Sustainability Report). In product development, we implement a "Shift Left Security" strategy, where security considerations are integrated from the initial design phase to mitigate risks as early as possible.
b. **Human Capital Capabilities**: We recognize that the best technology requires reliable operators. Therefore, Superbank invests heavily in international certifications for our information security team to navigate the evolving cyber threat landscape. Our team holds the following certifications:

- Management &amp; Audit: CISSP, CISA, CISM, ISO 27001 Lead Auditor &amp; Implementer.
- Technical &amp; Operational: CEH, OSCP, Red Team Operator, Certified DevSecOps Professional.
- Data Privacy: CIPM, CIPP/E, and Certified Indonesian Personal Data Protection Officer (CIPDPO).

Furthermore, a strong digital security culture is fostered through mandatory employee training and periodic phishing simulations in compliance with OJK regulations, ensuring organization-wide vigilance.

c. **Technological and Cyber Resilience**: Superbank employs a defense-in-depth framework to prevent, detect, and respond to threats. Our infrastructure is protected by a 24/7 Security Operations Centre (SOC) utilizing advanced platforms (SIEM &amp; SOAR) and cloud technology secured through VPC isolation and Web Application Firewalls. All customer data is protected using industry-standard encryption (AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.2+ in transit). This commitment is validated by our ISO 27001:2022 certification, confirming the implementation of international standards in information and cyber security.
d. **Customer Awareness and Due Diligence**: Security is a shared responsibility. We actively educate customers through regular campaigns on account protection and fraud trends. Internally, we apply rigorous due diligence principles, including both CDD (Customer Due Diligence) and EDD (Enhanced Due Diligence), in our customer onboarding and partner management processes to prevent financial crime and maintain the integrity of the banking ecosystem.
