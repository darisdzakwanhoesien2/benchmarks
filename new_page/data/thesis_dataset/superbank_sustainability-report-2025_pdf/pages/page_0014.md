## Society:

As part of our social responsibility, Superbank is committed to delivering a positive impact on the wider community, particularly for unbanked and underbanked segments who lack access to formal banking services.

Engagement is conducted on a tentative basis through Social and Environmental Responsibility (TJSL) programs, financial literacy activities, and collaborations with Non-Governmental Organizations or local communities.

## Challenges in Implementing Sustainable Finance

The year 2025 marks a monumental transition period for Superbank, where our change in status to a public company and a KBMI 2 bank brings the logical consequence of increased expectations from both regulators and stakeholders. In this acceleration phase, the primary challenge we face is balancing the pace of digital innovation with the required rigor in implementing Sustainable Finance. We recognize that integrating ESG aspects into a technology-based banking business model requires complex infrastructure adaptation, particularly in the collection and validation of accurate emission data for reporting and climate risk analysis purposes. The limited availability of historical data and information, especially from the retail and MSME segments that are our focus, remains a technical constraint in developing a precise decarbonization strategy.

Beyond technical aspects, other challenges lie within organizational capability and culture. The implementation of new standards, such as the Climate Risk Management Scenario (CRMS) in 2025, demands specific competency levels that are still in the developmental stage within the national banking industry, including internally at Superbank. Building a uniform understanding of the urgency of sustainability across all levels of the organization—from management to daily operations—is an ongoing process that requires time and consistency. We are also faced with external challenges in the form of varying levels of sustainability literacy among customers, which influences the adoption rate of our initiated products.

Nevertheless, Superbank views these dynamics not as obstacles, but as catalysts to strengthen our business foundation. We believe that every identified challenge opens up space for Superbank to innovate further, both in leveraging big data technology for climate risk measurement and in designing creative educational programs for customers. By adhering to the SuperGROW strategy and supported by a solid capital structure and governance post-IPO, we are optimistic about our ability to transform these challenges into strategic opportunities. Our commitment remains steadfast: to continue learning, adapting, and moving forward to realize a digital banking ecosystem that is not only financially resilient but also delivers a sustainable positive impact.
