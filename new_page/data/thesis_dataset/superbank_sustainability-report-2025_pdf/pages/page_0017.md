## Statement on Equal Access to Products for Customers

As a Bank committed to inclusion, Superbank ensures its banking application is accessible to anyone, anywhere, as long as they have an internet connection. Our primary focus is serving the underbanked population by providing equitable access, particularly for communities that have yet to be optimally reached by banking services. We implement unsecured lending to reach individuals who are often excluded from conventional banking, thereby upholding financial inclusion while maintaining steadfast compliance with prudent risk management practices. Equal access to products is bolstered by the implementation of reliable security and data protection systems. Throughout 2025, the Bank recorded zero incidents or customer grievances regarding data breaches, reflecting our commitment to maintaining customer trust and supporting sustainable and responsible banking practices.

## Labor Aspects

### Employee Demographic

As of the end of December 2025, Superbank employed 665 individuals, with female employees accounting for approximately 37% of the total workforce, distributed across various organizational levels, including managerial positions. Superbank upholds the principle of equal employment opportunity, ensuring that recruitment, development, performance appraisal, and promotion processes are conducted fairly and based on merit, without gender discrimination. As the organization grows, Superbank continues to foster an inclusive work environment that provides equal space for women to thrive and advance their careers. In support of this, we established the 'Super-Women Club and Allies' as a forum for empowerment and capacity building for our female employees. Moving forward, Superbank remains committed to strengthening inclusive and sustainable labor practices.

## Commitment to Equal Employment Opportunity

To uphold equality and non-discrimination in recruitment, Superbank enforces a fair hiring policy that prohibits the consideration of personal identity factors (e.g., gender, age, disability status, ethnicity, religion, or other forms of self-expression) during the evaluation process. This crucial step fosters a diverse, inclusive, and entirely merit-based recruitment process, ensuring equal opportunities for all candidates. Superbank's recruitment policy is strategically designed to guarantee fair, merit-based evaluations, focusing on candidates' skills, potential, and cultural alignment with the institution's values. Assessment criteria encompass essential qualifications, including educational background, organizational experience, professional achievements, and work history. Furthermore, the process emphasizes work potential and self-development, particularly in assessing learning capacity and growth trajectory. Candidates are also evaluated based on character and personal qualities that optimize job performance, alongside specialized knowledge and skills that contribute directly to organizational success. Most importantly, alignment with Superbank's corporate image, values, and culture ensures that new hires are fully in sync with the organization's mission and work environment. Generally, the recruitment process consists of:

- Administrative screening based on CVs and documents submitted by candidates.
- Basic competency testing for specific positions.
- Interviews, discussions, or other assessment methods to evaluate candidate competencies.
- Pre-employment screening to evaluate the integrity and credibility of candidates through external sources with relevant information. This screening includes, but is not limited to, credit checks, Anti-Money Laundering (AML) checks, background checks, candidate verification, and identification of family relationships with existing employees.
