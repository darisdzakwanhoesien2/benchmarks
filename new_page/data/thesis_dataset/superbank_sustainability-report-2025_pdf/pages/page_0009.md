# B.2 Anti-Money Laundering, Counter-Financing of Terrorism, and Counter-Proliferation Financing (AML, CFT, and CPF)

Superbank strengthens its AML-CFT and CPF programs through a risk-based approach. This governance is executed via the "Three Lines of Defense" model:

a. First Line: AML Operations Unit under the Operations Director.
b. Second Line: AML Compliance Unit under the Compliance Director.
c. Third Line: Internal Audit Department providing independent oversight.

System Development and Strategic Plan: In 2025, we consistently enhanced our internal systems for transaction profile monitoring and regulatory reporting to PPATK (Indonesian Financial Transaction Reports and Analysis Center), while performing risk-based customer data updates (Risk-Based Approach).

# B.3 A Safe and Conducive Working Environment

For Superbank, a safe workplace means creating a culture where every individual feels respected and supported both physically and psychologically. This commitment received external recognition through a Bronze Award in the Best Employee Wellness Strategy category at the Employee Experience Awards Indonesia 2025.

We foster a positive work environment through various concrete initiatives throughout the year:

a. Anti-Harassment Education: "Creating a Safe &amp; Respectful Workplace" sessions held with Bullyid to reinforce a "speak-up" culture and prevent discrimination.
b. Mental &amp; Physical Well-being: A series of events for World Mental Health Day and Breast Cancer Awareness, including mindfulness yoga sessions, health seminars (Health Talks) on cancer, free health screenings, and emotional resilience workshops and book reviews.

# C. Super-Opportunity: Unlocking Access, Empowering Growth

The Super-Opportunity pillar reaffirms Superbank's commitment to being a catalyst for financial inclusion in Indonesia. We focus on providing access to financing for underbanked segments and Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) that have traditionally faced barriers within the conventional banking system.

To expand our sustainability impact, we strategically integrate banking services into leading digital ecosystems, including Grab, OVO, and KakaoBank. This collaboration allows us to serve a broader segment of society through seamless and rapid transactions. This commitment is bolstered by a solid network of loan partners, ensuring equitable capital distribution to create a sustainable economic impact.

# C.1 Empowering Communities and MSMEs

Financial inclusion is more than just access, it is the foundation of economic resilience. Through inclusive financial solutions and tailored lending, we empower individuals and business owners to achieve financial independence. Our empowerment strategy focuses on four dimensions of impact:

a. Economic Stability: Providing access to affordable credit for those marginalized from the formal system, helping them break the cycle of poverty and build a solid financial foundation.
b. Livelihood Improvement: Equipping MSMEs with financial tools that simplify operations, allowing entrepreneurs to focus on business expansion and create employment opportunities.
c. Technology-Driven Growth: Access to financing enables MSMEs to invest in technology and infrastructure, transforming small businesses into competitive entities.

d. Bridging the Gap: Ensuring banking services reach all layers of society regardless of background, creating an ecosystem where economic opportunities are equally distributed.

# C.2 Innovative Financing Solutions through Strategic Partnerships

Our alliances with loan partners are the keys of Superbank's social impact strategy. We provide financing products tailored to the specific needs of our customers:

a. Asset-Based Financing (Used Car Ecosystem): Collaborating with automotive fintech platforms to provide working capital for dealers (MSMEs) and purchase financing for end-customers.
b. Inclusive Multipurpose Loans: Providing flexible access to cash for underbanked individuals for both productive and personal needs.
c. Focus on Underserved Segments: Expanding our reach to the unbanked population, strengthening their ability to participate actively in the formal economic system.

# C.3 Financial Literacy: Building a Financially Savvy Generation

In 2025, Superbank's financial literacy initiatives reached 930 participants through both online and offline sessions. These initiatives involve Superbank employees directly educating the community on financial management and responsible digital banking.

Key Literacy Initiatives:

a. Dangdut Academy Partnership: A unique breakthrough by sponsoring a popular talent search show to reach the mass market segment. This program provided dedicated education sessions for the show's audience, introducing the benefits of digital banking in accessible language.
b. Youth Focus: Recognizing that the younger generation are digital natives, we organized interactive sessions for elementary, middle school, university students, and interns. The goal is to instill a financially savvy mindset from an early age so they are prepared to grow alongside Indonesia's digital economy.
