# Risk Management in Sustainability Implementation

The Audit Committee oversees corporate risk management, while the Board of Commissioners monitors the implementation of the Risk Management strategy by the Board of Directors. The Audit Committee is responsible for evaluating risk management policies, ensuring the accountability of the Board of Directors, and approving transactions that require approval from the Board of Commissioners. DSNG regularly identifies risks affecting performance using the Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) methodology. Further details are available in the 2024 Annual Report.

To support sustainability, DSNG also identifies Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) risks that could impact operations. These are documented within the Environmental and Social Management System (ESMS), initially developed in 2021 and updated in 2023.
