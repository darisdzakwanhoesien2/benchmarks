# Climate Action Plan

Our efforts to address climate change align with the Group Sustainability Policy, which focuses on three key areas: forests, climate, and society. The DSNG Sustainability Policy serves as the main policy roadmap that integrates climate-related issues into the company's goals and business strategies.

In pursuit of its aspiration to achieve Net Zero by reducing emissions, adapting to climate change risks, as well as aligning with the Indonesian Government's

efforts to meet its Nationally Determined Contribution (NDC) commitments, DSNG has developed a Climate Action Plan and a transition roadmap toward a low-carbon and climate-resilient future. The DSNG Climate Action Plan outlines strategic steps for climate change mitigation and adaptation, focusing on a 44% reduction in greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions by 2030, compared to the 2019 baseline year.
