# Supply Chain in the Agro-Palm Oil Business Unit

DSNG is committed to implementing the NDPE policy as a core principle across all its operations. This policy encompasses the protection of forests and peatlands, ensuring responsible business practices that do not exploit employees, communities, or other stakeholders involved in the supply chain. NDPE applies not only to assets owned and directly managed by DSNG but also to plasma plantations, subsidiaries, joint ventures, and all suppliers and contractors.
