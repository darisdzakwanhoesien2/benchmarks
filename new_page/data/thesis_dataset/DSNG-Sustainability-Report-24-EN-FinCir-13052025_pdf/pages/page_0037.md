# Traceability

As of 2024, the Company operates 1 unit of Kernel Crushing Plant (KCP) and 12 units of palm oil mill, with 1 unit of palm oil mill is undergoing major maintenance and will not operate since January 2024. Three mills received Fresh Fruit Bunches (FFB) solely from core and partnership plantations, while the remaining mills accept FFB from core, partnership, and external plantations. External plantations include those from Independent Plantation Companies (IPC), independent farmers through cooperatives, and independent farmers through agents. The supply of FFB to the Company's mills is illustrated in the following chart:

![img-88.jpeg](img-88.jpeg)

The Company's policy stipulates that the implementation of NDPE also extends to primary suppliers, specifically those supplying FFB to the Company's mills. Therefore, NDPE applies to all supplies. Internal supply consists of core and partnership plantations, while external supplies comprise IPC, cooperatives, and agents.
