Our Progress towards our 2030 Emissions Target

DSNG absolute emission 2019 vs 2022
2019 2022

DSNG has conducted a triennial emission calculation in collaboration with an independent institution to monitor the achievement of its emission reduction target of 44% by 2030, compared to the 2019 baseline. Several interventions lead to the absolute decrease in Scope 1 and 2 emission by 13%.

![img-151.jpeg](img-151.jpeg)
DSNG emission intensity (ton CO₂e/ton CPO)

In 2024, the emission intensity is 1.06 tons CO₂e per ton of CPO. This represents a 9.4% decrease since 2022, when it was 1.17 tons CO₂e per ton of CPO. The decline was due to the implementation of methane capture plant operations and improved diesel and biomass efficiency through the use of biogas and Bio-CNG. The annual emission intensity in the oil palm sub-unit is calculated using the RSPO Palm GHG Calculator 4.0.

![img-152.jpeg](img-152.jpeg)
Emission Intensity (tCO₂e/million rupiah revenue)

The overall increase in emissions, primarily driven by Scope 3, is mainly due to increased production and the need for external FFB supply. This correlates with a 20% reduction in emission intensity relative to the company's revenue.

![img-153.jpeg](img-153.jpeg)
Comparison of emission intensity against several companies (ton CO₂e/ton CPO)

We compared emissions in the palm oil sub-unit with several peer companies in the palm oil industry. The graph shows that DSNG's emission intensity of 1.06 tons CO₂e per ton of CPO in 2024 is between the other companies, that is 0.88 and 1.47 tons CO₂e per ton of CPO. This figure is even significantly lower than the average for RSPO-certified companies. The comparative graph was compiled using the RSPO Palm GHG Calculator methodology for 2024 based on each company's SR publication. The average value of RSPO certified companies in 2016 is based on the publication by Schmidt and De Rosa in 2020.
