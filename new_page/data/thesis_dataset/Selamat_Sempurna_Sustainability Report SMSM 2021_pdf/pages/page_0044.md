Permasalahan, Perkembangan Kinerja Keberlanjutan, dan Pengaruhnya terhadap Perseroan

Permasalahan, kendala, tantangan, isu signifikan terkait kinerja keberlanjutan tentunya sangat berpengaruh terhadap kinerja Perseroan. Pada tabel di bawah ini, kami telah memetakan beberapa permasalahan dan perkembangan kinerja keberlanjutan yang berpengaruh terhadap Perseroan.

Problems, Sustainability Performance Development, and Its Impact on the Company

Problems, constraints, challenges, significant issues related to sustainability performance are certainly very influential to the Company's performance. In the table below, we have mapped some of the problems and developments in sustainability performance that affect the Company.

# Lingkungan | Environment

## Tantangan | Challenges

- Kebijakan pemerintah dengan adanya peraturan baru terkait perubahan izin lingkungan menjadi persetujuan lingkungan.
- Petugas WWTP dan Incinerator belum memiliki sertifikat kompetensi dari BNSP (Badan Nasional Sertifikasi Profesi).
- Letak Perseroan dilewati oleh aliran air sungai.
- Government policy with new regulations related to changing environmental permits to environmental approvals.
- WWTP and Incinerator officers have not yet obtained a certificate of competence from BNSP (National Professional Certification Agency).
- The Company's location is bypassed by river water flow.

## Pengaruh terhadap Perseroan | Impact to the Company

- Potensi mendapat sanksi dari pemerintah apabila tidak melakukan pelaporan, untuk itu Perseroan melakukan Pelaporan Lingkungan ke Dinas Lingkungan Hidup (DLH).
- Citra negatif atau mendapatkan sanksi dari badan sertifikasi atau dinas lingkungan hidup apabila jadi temuan saat ada inspeksi mendadak/audit. Perseroan akan melakukan koordinasi dengan LPK (Lembaga Pelatihan Kerja) untuk mendapatkan sertifikat kompetensi dari BNSP.
- Potensi pencemaran air sungai oleh perusahaan lain yang ada di hulu, sehingga dilakukan pengelolaan lingkungan seperti rekondisi waste water treatment dan pengurusan perizinan pembuangan limbah cair.
- The potential to be sanctioned by the government is to be sanctioned by the Company conducts Environmental Reporting to the Environment Agency (DLH).
- Negative image or getting sanctioned by a certification body or environmental agency is it becomes a finding when there is a sudden inspection/audit. The Company will coordinate with LPK (Job Training Institute) to obtain a certificate of competence from BNSP.
- Potential pollution of river water by other companies upstream, so that environmental management such as waste water treatment reconditioning and managing liquid waste disposal permits.
