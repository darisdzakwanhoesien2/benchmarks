# TATA KELOLA KEBERLANJUTAN SUSTAINABILITY GOVERNANCE

Perseroan berkomitmen menerapkan prinsip-prinsip tata kelola perusahaan yang baik (Good Corporate Governance/GCG), mengacu pada Surat Edaran Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) No.32/SEOJK.04/2015 tentang Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan Terbuka; Undang-Undang No.40/2007 tentang Perseroan Terbatas; Undang-Undang No.8/1995 tentang Pasar Modal; Peraturan OJK No.21/POJK.04/2015 tentang Penerapan Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan Terbuka; serta Indonesia Corporate Governance Roadmap yang diterbitkan OJK pada bulan Januari 2014. Komitmen tersebut kami terapkan secara konsisten pada seluruh perencanaan, keputusan, dan kegiatan operasional bisnis Perseroan. Selain itu, implementasi GCG Perseroan juga didasarkan pada parameter ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS).

Setiap tahunnya, Perseroan melakukan penilaian terhadap kinerja GCG. Pada tahun 2021, Perseroan mendapatkan total nilai 89,25 terhadap GCG berdasarkan ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS) 2021, meningkat apabila dibandingkan tahun 2020. Hasil tersebut termasuk dalam predikat "Good" atau Level 3 (80-89,99), yang berarti implementasi tata kelola korporasi Perseroan sudah melampaui kepatuhan terhadap persyaratan minimal dan mengadopsi sebagian standar internasional yang disyaratkan oleh ACGS.

The Company is committed to implementing the principles of good corporate governance (GCG), referring to the Circular Letter of the Financial Services Authority (OJK) No.32/SEOJK.04/2015 concerning Guidelines for Public Corporate Governance; Law No.40/2007 concerning Limited Liability Companies; Law No.8/1995 on Capital Markets; OJK Regulation No.21/POJK.04/2015 concerning the Implementation of Guidelines for Public Company Governance; and the Indonesia Corporate Governance Roadmap issued by OJK in January 2014. We apply this commitment consistently to all planning, decisions, and operational activities of the Company's business. In addition, the Company's GCG implementation is also based on the parameters of the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS).

Every year, the Company conducts an assessment of the performance of GCG. In 2021, the Company received a total value of 89.25 to GCG based on the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS) 2021, an increase when compared to 2020. The results are included in the predicate "Good" or Level 3 (80-89.99), which means that the implementation of the Company's corporate governance has exceeded compliance with minimum requirements and adopted some of the international standards required by ACGS.
