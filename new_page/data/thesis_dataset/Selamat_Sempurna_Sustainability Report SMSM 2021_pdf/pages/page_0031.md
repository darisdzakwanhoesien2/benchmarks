Perseroan melakukan manajemen persediaan (inventory management) di Gudang penyimpanan dengan software Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP). Penerapan sistem ini merupakan salah satu upaya Perseroan untuk memitigasi potensi risiko dalam proses distribusi. Perseroan telah mengimplementasikan ERP dalam sistem manajemen persediaan. Selain itu juga, Perseroan telah menggunakan program System Applications and Products in Data Processing (SAP) secara penuh, yang memungkinkan setiap penanggung jawab dapat melihat ketersediaan barang di gudang, jumlah barang masuk atau keluar, serta kapasitas gudang. Sistem ini memungkinkan penanggung jawab untuk memantau kondisi gudang dari jarak jauh dan kapan saja (real time).

The Company conducts inventory management in storage warehouses with Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) software. The implementation of this system is one of the Company's efforts to mitigate potential risks in the distribution process. The Company has implemented ERP in the inventory management system. In addition, the Company has used the System Applications and Products in Data Processing (SAP) program in full, which allows each person in charge to see the availability of goods in the warehouse, the number of goods entering or exiting, and warehouse capacity. This system allows the person in charge to monitor the condition of the warehouse remotely and at any time (real time).
