## Keamanan Distribusi

Perseroan berkomitmen untuk menjaga keamanan distribusi baik domestik maupun ekspor, sesuai dengan Standar Operasional Prosedur (SOP) yang berlaku. Perseroan juga memastikan untuk menjalin hubungan yang baik dengan para distributor, meliputi perlindungan keamanan dan kesehatan kerja (K3), kesadaran HAM, serta menerapkan persyaratan yang mencakup pengelolaan K3 dan pengelolaan risiko lingkungan, seperti persyaratan uji emisi dan kelayakan jalan.

Pada proses distribusi produk secara lokal, pelanggan dapat menunjuk sendiri pihak pengangkut yang merupakan pihak ketiga, sesuai persyaratan yang dimiliki oleh masing-masing pelanggan. Sementara mekanisme distribusi ekspor dilakukan sesuai dengan kontrak yang telah disepakati bersama dengan pelanggan. Perseroan juga berkomitmen untuk menjaga keamanan dan kualitas produk melalui product liability insurance, yang melindungi pelanggan dari risiko terkait dengan produk Perseroan, seperti risiko penarikan produk dari peredaran.

Dalam kegiatan distribusi produk, baik domestik maupun ekspor, Perseroan menjamin kesempatan yang sama bagi setiap mitra yang telah memenuhi persyaratan untuk melaksanakan distribusi, serta adanya peraturan terkait K3 dan safety sign pada distribusi. Risiko tumpahan mungkin kurang relevan bagi Perseroan, namun kerusakan produk akibat jatuh dalam proses distribusi sangat mungkin terjadi. Oleh karena itu, Perseroan memiliki SOP yang cukup ketat dan mampu memitigasi berbagai risiko yang mungkin terjadi, dari proses pengemasan produk hingga pemilihan mitra yang melakukan distribusi.

## Distribution Safety

The Company is committed to maintaining the security of distribution both domestically and exports, in accordance with the applicable Standard Operating Procedures (SOP). The Company also ensures to establish good relations with distributors, including occupational safety and health protection (OHS), human rights awareness, and implementing requirements that include OHS management and environmental risk management, such as emission test requirements and road feasibility.

In the process of distributing products locally, customers can appoint their own carriers who are third parties, according to the requirements owned by each customer. While the export distribution mechanism is carried out in accordance with the contract that has been mutually agreed with the customer. The Company is also committed to maintaining the safety and quality of products through product liability insurance, which protects customers from risks associated with the Company's products, such as the risk of product recall from circulation.

In product distribution activities, both domestic and export, the Company guarantees equal opportunities for every partner who has met the requirements to carry out distribution, as well as regulations related to OHS and safety signs on distribution. Spill risk may be less relevant to the Company, but product damage due to falls in the distribution process is very highly likely. Therefore, the Company has a fairly strict SOP and is able to mitigate various risks that may occur, from the product packaging process to the selection of partners who distribute.
