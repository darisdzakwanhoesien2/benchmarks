# PROFIL SINGKAT PERUSAHAAN
## COMPANY'S IN BRIEF

![img-27.jpeg](img-27.jpeg)

PT Selamat Sempurna Tbk berdiri pada 19 Januari 1976 dan telah terdaftar sebagai perusahaan publik sejak 1996. Perseroan bergerak di bidang manufaktur dan distribusi alat-alat atau perlengkapan (spare parts) kendaraan bermotor, alat-alat berat, dan mesin terkait lainnya. Perseroan telah memberikan solusi otomotif dan pengadaan suku cadang untuk pelanggan Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM), Original Equipment Supplier (OES), serta aftermarket selama lebih dari 40 tahun. Merek dagang produk Perseroan, yaitu Sakura Filter dan Radiator, telah terdaftar di lebih dari 100 negara. Perseroan memiliki 5 entitas anak dan 3 entitas asosiasi, serta kerja sama perusahaan internasional melalui perjanjian bantuan teknik dan lisensi. [102-2]

PT Selamat Sempurna Tbk was established on January 19, 1976 and has been registered as a public company since 1996. The Company is engaged in manufacturing and distribution of motor vehicle equipment or equipment (spare parts), heavy equipment, and other related machinery. The Company has been providing automotive solutions and procurement of spare parts for Customers of Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM), Original Equipment Supplier (OES), and aftermarket for more than 40 years. The Company's product trademarks, Sakura Filter and Radiator, have been registered in more than 100 countries. The Company has 5 subsidiaries and 3 associate entities, as well as international corporate cooperation through engineering and licensing assistance agreements. [102-2]
