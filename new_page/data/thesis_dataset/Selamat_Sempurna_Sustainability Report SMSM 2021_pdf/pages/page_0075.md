Sepanjang tahun 2021, Perseroan secara aktif melaksanakan kegiatan-kegiatan terkait K3, antara lain:
Throughout 2021, the Company actively carried out OHS related activities, including:

![img-77.jpeg](img-77.jpeg)

Safety Excellence, yaitu pelatihan untuk memperkuat pemahaman kebijakan keselamatan pada karyawan setiap penerimaan karyawan atau staf baru.
Safety Excellence, which is training to strengthen the understanding of safety policies in employees with every admission of new employees or staff.

Simulasi tanggap darurat, berupa latihan evakuasi, pertolongan korban kecelakaan, pemadaman api, dan pembersihan tumpahan B3 di area kerja.
Simulation of emergency response, in the form of evacuation exercises, accident victim assistance, fire suppression, and B3 spill cleaning in the work area.

![img-78.jpeg](img-78.jpeg)

![img-79.jpeg](img-79.jpeg)

Peningkatan keterampilan karyawan dalam penerapan standar global Safety to Win, diantaranya pelatihan pemadam kebakaran baik penggunaan APAR ataupun hydrant, sertifikasi kepatuhan, dan pelatihan kesiapan tanggap darurat.
Improving employee skills in the implementation of global Safety to Win standards, including firefighting training both the use of APAR and hydrants, compliance certification, and emergency response readiness training.

Pelatihan tim tanggap darurat untuk respon terhadap bencana alam gempa bumi.
Emergency response team training for response to earthquake natural disasters.

![img-80.jpeg](img-80.jpeg)

![img-81.jpeg](img-81.jpeg)

Pemeriksaan sistem hydrant, sistem alarm, dan alat pemadam api ringan (APAR).
Inspection of hydrant systems, alarm system, and light fire extinguishers (Fire Extinguishers).

![img-82.jpeg](img-82.jpeg)

Pemeriksaan pemadam kebakaran, ambulans, dan kotak P3K.
Inspection of fire departments, ambulances and first aid box.

![img-83.jpeg](img-83.jpeg)

Safety Patrol (unsafe action dan unsafe condition) setiap empat bulan sekali.
Safety Patrol (unsafe action and unsafe condition) every four months.

Pengecekan suhu tubuh karyawan dan tamu, serta penyemprotan disinfektan untuk mencegah penyebaran virus COVID-19.
Checking the body temperature of employees and guests, as well as spraying disinfectants to prevent the spread of the COVID-19 virus.

![img-84.jpeg](img-84.jpeg)

Penambahan area cuci tangan lengkap dengan sabun cuci tangan.
The addition of a handwashing area complete with handwashing soap.

Penambahan poster/spanduk protokol kesehatan pencegahan COVID-19 di area kantin karyawan dan area lingkungan.
The addition of posters/banners of the COVID-19 prevention health protocols in the employee canteen area and environmental area.
