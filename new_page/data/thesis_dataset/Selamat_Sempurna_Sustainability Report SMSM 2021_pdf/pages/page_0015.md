# Peluang dan Prospek Usaha

Di masa mendatang, kami yakin bahwa industri otomotif nasional dapat terus berkembang dan berkontribusi pada perekonomian nasional. Sepanjang tahun 2021, industri otomotif tumbuh luar biasa mencapai 17,82% dengan penyerapan tenaga kerja, baik langsung maupun tidak langsung sekitar 1,5 juta orang di sepanjang mata rantai nilai industri. Terkait nilai investasi, industri otomotif tercatat merealisasikan sebesar Rp22,5 triliun pada tahun 2021, naik 220% jika dibandingkan capaian penanaman modal tahun 2020.

Perseroan terus berinovasi untuk mengembangkan teknologi otomotif ramah lingkungan, seiring dengan penerapan Standar Emisi Euro IV dan kebijakan kendaraan rendah emisi karbon (LCEV), serta program percepatan kendaraan listrik di Indonesia. Selain itu, perkembangan industri otomotif juga dipengaruhi oleh perkembangan menuju Industri 4.0, seperti otomasi, pemanfaatan, dan pertukaran data dalam proses manufaktur. Kami senantiasa berpartisipasi untuk membantu mewujudkan peta jalan Industri 4.0 Indonesia (Making Indonesia 4.0), melalui penguasaan kemampuan manufaktur kendaraan listrik dan baterai listrik sesuai dengan tren global.

# Business Opportunities and Prospects

In the future, we are confident that the national automotive industry can continue to develop and contribute to the national economy. Throughout 2021, the automotive industry grew tremendously by 17.82% with the absorption of labor, both directly and indirectly around 1.5 million people along the industrial value chain. Regarding the value of investment, the automotive industry was recorded to realize Rp22.5 trillion in 2021, an increase of 220% when compared to the achievement of investment in 2020.

The Company continues to innovate to develop environmentally friendly automotive technologies, along with the implementation of the Euro IV Emission Standard and low carbon vehicle policy (LCEV), as well as the electric vehicle acceleration program in Indonesia. In addition, the development of the automotive industry is also influenced by developments towards Industry 4.0, such as automation, utilization, and data exchange in manufacturing processes. We are always participating to help realize the Roadmap of Industry 4.0 Indonesia (Making Indonesia 4.0), through mastering the manufacturing capabilities of electric vehicles and electric batteries in accordance with global trends.
