## Lingkungan Kerja Layak dan Aman

Perseroan telah memenuhi peraturan perundangan terkait Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja (K3) dan standar internasional yang berlaku. Di samping itu, Perseroan juga melakukan perbaikan berkelanjutan terhadap sistem manajemen dan kinerja K3 Perseroan, guna memastikan lingkungan kerja yang layak dan aman bagi karyawan. Kami juga melibatkan karyawan untuk ikut bertanggung jawab terhadap K3 anak buah dan pihak ketiga dalam kontrol mereka, serta wajib mempraktikkan cara bekerja yang aman. Selain itu, aspek K3 ini kami tuangkan juga ke dalam Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), sehingga setiap personel memiliki tanggung jawab untuk berperan serta untuk mencapai target zero accident.

Upaya-upaya lain yang Perseroan lakukan untuk memenuhi aspek K3 sepanjang tahun 2021 adalah:

1. Penguatan Materi Training K3 Dasar dan Emergency Response Plan kepada karyawan dan staf baru.
2. Penambahan sistem alarm kebakaran di area gudang bahan baku dan gudang barang jadi.
3. Pengadaan sepatu safety kepada karyawan yang mempunyai resiko kejatuhan benda berat seperti area produksi Presshop, Maintenance, Workshop, Engineering Process dan Building.

## Decent and Safe Working Environment

The Company has complied with laws and regulations related to Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) and applicable international standards. In addition, the Company also makes continuous improvements to the Company's OHS management and performance system, in order to ensure a decent and safe working environment for employees. We also involve employees to take responsibility for their OHS and third parties in their control, and are obliged to practice safe ways of working. In addition, this aspect of OHS we pour also into Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), so that every personnel has a responsibility to participate in achieving the zero accident target.

Other efforts made by the Company to meet the OHS aspect throughout 2021 are:

1. Strengthening Basic OHS Training Materials and Emergency Response Plan to new employees and staff.
2. Addition of fire alarm system in raw material warehouse area and finished goods warehouse.
3. Procurement of safety shoes to employees who have the risk of falling heavy objects such as Presshop production areas, Maintenance, Workshops, Engineering Process and Building.
