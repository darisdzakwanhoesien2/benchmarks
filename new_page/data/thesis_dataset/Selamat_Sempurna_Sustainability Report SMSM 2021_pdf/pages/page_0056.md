Krisis energi dipengaruhi oleh adanya peningkatan aktivitas ekonomi seiring dengan pemulihan ekonomi sehingga permintaan gas alam untuk kegiatan produksi meningkat cukup tajam. Namun, hal tersebut tidak diimbangi dengan supply gas alam yang memadai. Kondisi ini menyebabkan peningkatan permintaan terhadap barang substitusi gas alam yaitu komoditas lain seperti batu bara, sehingga mengakibatkan peningkatan harga pada komoditas lain termasuk peningkatan harga bahan baku utama Perseroan seperti baja.

Seperti yang telah digambarkan sebelumnya, kondisi tahun 2021 memberikan tantangan yang cukup berat bagi Perseroan. Untuk itu, Perseroan melakukan sejumlah kebijakan strategis agar tetap dapat mempertahankan kinerjanya, di antaranya dengan melakukan penerapan operational excellence yang berkelanjutan hingga upaya efisiensi dengan terus melakukan penurunan biaya usaha dan biaya pokok produksi melalui penerapan continuous improvement serta memperkuat implementasi aspek-aspek ESG.

The energy crisis is influenced by an increase in economic activity along with the economic recovery so that the demand for natural gas for production activities increases quite sharply. However, this is not balanced with an adequate supply of natural gas. This condition led to an increase in demand for natural gas substitution goods, i.e. other commodities such as coal, resulting in an increase in prices in other commodities including an increase in the price of the Company's main raw materials such as steel.

As previously described, the conditions in 2021 present quite severe challenges for the Company. For this reason, the Company conducts a number of strategic policies in order to maintain its performance, including by implementing sustainable operational excellence to efficiency efforts by continuing to reduce business costs and production costs through the implementation of continuous improvement and strengthening the implementation of ESG aspects.
