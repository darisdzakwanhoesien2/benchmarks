Perseroan belum menunjukkan pejabat dan/atau fungsi khusus untuk bertanggung jawab terhadap perencanaan, pelaksanaan, pemantauan, dan evaluasi terhadap kinerja keberlanjutan Perseroan. Seluruh aspek terkait keberlanjutan dijalankan secara bersama-sama oleh seluruh fungsi di bawah tanggung jawab Direksi dan pengawasan Dewan Komisaris. Struktur tata kelola Perseroan secara lebih rinci dapat dilihat pada Laporan Tahunan yang disajikan terpisah dari laporan ini. [102-18] [102-19]

Direktur Utama bertanggung jawab atas pengambilan keputusan tertinggi terkait isu-isu ekonomi, lingkungan, dan sosial, yang dibantu oleh Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris. Direktur Utama mendelegasikan tanggung jawab atas kinerja lingkungan kepada Divisi General Affairs. Tanggung jawab atas kinerja sosial terkait sumber daya manusia internal Perseroan, berada di bawah Divisi Human Capital. Sementara tanggung jawab atas kinerja ekonomi, termasuk memperhitungkan risiko finansial atas dampak perubahan iklim yang dapat mengganggu proses produksi dan keberlanjutan Perseroan, dilaksanakan oleh Direktur Keuangan. The Company has not appointed officials and/or special functions to be responsible for planning, implementing, monitoring, and evaluating the Company's sustainability performance. All aspects related to sustainability are carried out jointly by all functions under the responsibility of the Board of Directors and the supervision of the Board of Commissioners. The Company's governance structure in more detail can be seen in the Annual Report presented separately from this report. [102-18] [102-19]

The President Director is responsible for the highest decision making related to economic, environmental, and social issues, assisted by the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners. The President Director delegates responsibility for environmental performance to the General Affairs Division. Responsibility for social performance related to the Company's internal human resources, falls under the Human Capital Division. While responsibility for economic performance, including taking into account financial risks to the impact of climate change that can disrupt the Company's production and sustainability processes, is carried out by the Director of Finance. [102-20]
