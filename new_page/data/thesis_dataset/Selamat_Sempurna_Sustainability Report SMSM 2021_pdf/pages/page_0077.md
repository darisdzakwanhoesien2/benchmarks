• Create Hazard Identification Risk Assessment & Determination Control (HIRADC), which is a form used to identify risks and hazard levels in the work area in accordance with Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) ADR-SHE-SOP-016 (HIRADC).
• Create Standard Operational Procedure (SOP) emergency response as a preventive measure in case of fire and emergency.
• Conduct investigations related to work accidents, fires, or emergencies.
• Create a first aid SOP as a guide for first aid officers in the field to speed up the first aid process.
• Making Safety Sign SOP as a guide of information that is an appeal, warning, or prohibition in the work area with the established standards.
• Create a Fire Alarm System SOP as a work area mapping guide for the installation of alarm detectors. | • SHE
