|  7 | Pengelolaan Sumber Daya Manusia, termasuk tenaga kerja lokal. | Ketidakmampuan untuk mengidentifikasi, mengembangkan sumber daya manusia (SDM) yang memadai, menarik, atau mempertahankan personil yang berkualifikasi, dapat menyulitkan pengelolaan bisnis dan dapat berdampak negatif pada operasi dan hasil keuangan. | Sebagai upaya mengatasi risiko tersebut Perseroan memastikan pengelolaan Sumber Daya Manusia (SDM) terlaksana dengan baik, mulai dari perekrutan hingga pengembangan karier karyawan. Di samping itu, Perseroan membuka kesempatan bagi masyarakat lokal untuk bekerja. Dengan merekrut tenaga kerja lokal, Perseroan berharap akan berkontribusi pada peningkatan kondisi ekonomi masyarakat lokal, meningkatkan keharmonisan hubungan Perseroan dengan masyarakat sekitar, serta meminimalkan potensi risiko sosial.

Perseroan telah memiliki program rekrutmen karyawan baru secara berkesinambungan, yakni program MDP. Selain itu, Perseroan memiliki proses pengembangan manajemen terpadu, yang meliputi penilaian kinerja rutin terkait perilaku kepemimpinan, keterampilan, dan kompetensi. Penilaian kinerja dilaksanakan untuk mengidentifikasi kebutuhan keterampilan dan kemampuan di masa depan, mengembangkan jalur karier, dan mengidentifikasi bakat dan pemimpin di masa depan di antara karyawan.

Pada tahun 2021, fokus pengelolaan karyawan dilaksanakan melalui pengembangan Platform ADR Digital Learning untuk meningkatkan kualitas karyawan. Peningkatan kualitas Sumber Daya Manusia (SDM) diharapkan dapat mendorong peningkatan kesejahteraan karyawan.  |
|   | Human Resource Management, including local labor. | The inability to identify, develop adequate human resources (HR), attract, or retain qualified personnel, can complicate business management and can negatively impact operations and financial results. | In an effort to overcome these risks, the Company ensures that human resources (HR) management is carried out properly, ranging from recruitment to employee career development. In addition, the Company opens opportunities for local people to work. By recruiting local workers, the Company hopes that it will contribute to improving the economic condition of local communities, improving the harmony of the Company's relationship with the surrounding community, and minimizing potential social risks.

The Company already has a new employee recruitment program on an ongoing basis, i.e. the MDP program. In addition, the Company has an integrated management development process, which includes routine performance assessments related to leadership behavior, skills, and competencies. Performance assessments are implemented to identify future skills and abilities needs, develop career paths, and identify future talents and leaders among employees.

In 2021, the focus of employee management was implemented through the development of the ADR Digital Learning Platform to improve employee quality Improving the quality of Human Resources (HR) is expected to encourage the improvement of employee welfare.  |
