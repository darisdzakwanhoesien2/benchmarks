Perseroan, melalui salah satu direksi membentuk Satuan Tugas (Satgas) Penanggulangan COVID-19 yang beranggotakan petugas P2K3 dari berbagai divisi. Satgas Penanggulangan COVID-19 Perseroan bertugas mengawasi, mengendalikan, menanggulangi, dan mengantisipasi penyebaran virus dengan langkah-langkah nyata. Satgas Penanggulangan COVID-19 telah menerbitkan pedoman Standar Operasional Prosedur (SOP) yang mengatur perilaku seluruh karyawan selama masa pandemi.

## Pemberdayaan Masyarakat

Keberadaan Perseroan dan kegiatan operasionalnya memberikan dampak positif ataupun negatif terhadap masyarakat sekitar. Secara tidak langsung, kami memberikan dampak ekonomi kepada masyarakat yang berada di lingkungan Perseroan. Kami mempekerjakan 328 orang masyarakat di sekitar lokasi pabrik yang terletak di Indonesia. Dari jumlah tersebut, 94 orang merupakan karyawan tetap dan 234 orang merupakan karyawan tidak tetap. Secara keseluruhan, Perseroan dan entitas anak menyerap 11% tenaga kerja lokal. [203-2]

Selain itu, kegiatan operasional pabrik turut membuka kesempatan usaha bagi masyarakat setempat, seperti warung makan dan usaha penitipan sepeda motor. Perseroan turut memfasilitasi 16 kios kantin untuk dikelola masyarakat. Di sisi lain, kegiatan pabrik menimbulkan kebisingan dan penurunan kualitas udara di lingkungan sekitar. Oleh karena itu, Perseroan bersama dengan Dinas Lingkungan Hidup melalui Laporan Upaya Pengelolaan dan Upaya Pemantauan Lingkungan (UKL-UPL), berupaya mengendalikan dan mengoordinasikan isu tersebut setiap 6 bulan sekali.

Perseroan melaksanakan kegiatan-kegiatan kemasyarakatan, sebagai upaya meningkatkan nilai bagi masyarakat yang ada di sekitar wilayah operasional dan meminimalkan dampak negatif yang ditimbulkan. Kami memiliki empat sektor program sosial dan pengembangan masyarakat, yakni perbaikan akses dan kualitas pendidikan, kualitas layanan kesehatan, sosial, dan keagamaan. The Company, through one of the directors formed a Task Force on COVID-19 Countermeasures consisting of first aid workers from various divisions. The Company's COVID-19 Countermeasures Task Force is in charge of supervising, controlling, tackling, and anticipating the spread of the virus with concrete measures. The COVID-19 Countermeasures Task Force has issued Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) guidelines that regulate the behavior of all employees during the pandemic.

## Community Empowerment

The existence of the Company and its operational activities have a positive or negative impact on the surrounding community. Indirectly, we have an economic impact on the people in the Company. We employ 328 people around the factory site located in Indonesia. Of these, 94 were permanent employees and 234 were non-permanent employees. Overall, the Company and its subsidiaries absorb 11% of the local workforce. [203-2]

In addition, the factory's operational activities also open business opportunities for the local community, such as food stalls and motorcycle maintenance businesses. The Company also facilitates 16 canteen stalls to be managed by the community. On the other hand, factory activities cause noise and decrease air quality in the surrounding environment. Therefore, the Company together with the Environment Agency through the Environmental Monitoring Efforts and Management Efforts Report (UKL-UPL), seeks to control and coordinate the issue every 6 months.

The Company carries out community activities, as an effort to increase value for the communities around the operational area and minimize the negative impact caused. We have four sectors of social programs and community development, i.e. improving access and quality of education, quality of health, social, and religious services.
