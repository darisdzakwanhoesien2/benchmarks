Pada aspek lingkungan, Perseroan memastikan setiap aspek operasional telah memenuhi komitmen terhadap lingkungan. Selain itu fasilitas produksi Perseroan telah mendapatkan sertifikat ISO 14001:2015 tentang Sistem Manajemen Lingkungan. Kami berkomitmen untuk menaati peraturan di bidang lingkungan, mencegah pencemaran, dan konsisten terhadap perbaikan berkelanjutan. Perseroan konsisten mengelola limbah B3 hasil kegiatan operasional melalui dua cara, yakni pengolahan secara mandiri dan penyerahan kepada pihak ketiga yang memiliki izin sesuai dengan Peraturan Menteri Lingkungan Hidup. Oleh karena itu, selama periode 2021 Perseroan tidak menghadapi masalah terkait lingkungan dan mampu melaksanakan kegiatan operasional dengan kinerja positif.

Selama tahun 2021, Perseroan telah merealisasikan biaya tanggung jawab di bidang lingkungan sebesar Rp120 juta. Kami berkomitmen untuk mengoptimalkan penggunaan energi secara efektif dan efisien, sehingga dapat berkontribusi mengurangi emisi. Pada tahun 2021, kami mampu menurunkan rata-rata penggunaan energi sebesar 22,73% dengan substitusi material adhesive yang bersifat non-curing (tanpa menggunakan oven listrik). Penurunan konsumsi energi akan berkontribusi pada penurunan emisi GRK yang dihasilkan. Selain itu, intensitas penggunaan energi pada tahun 2021 juga mengalami penurunan, karena berkurangnya kegiatan operasional incinerator yang memengaruhi penurunan pemakaian gas yang cukup signifikan.

Perseroan berhasil menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang layak dan aman bagi karyawan, melalui penerapan sistem manajemen K3 sesuai perundangan dan standar internasional yang berlaku. Selain itu, kami meraih pencapaian zero fatality di tahun 2021 serta kembali meraih penghargaan dari pihak eksternal terkait Panitia Pembina Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (P2K3). In the environmental aspect, the Company ensures that every operational aspect has fulfilled its commitment to the environment. In addition, the Company's production facilities have obtained ISO 14001:2015 certificate on Environmental Management System. We are committed to complying with environmental regulations, preventing pollution, and being consistent with continuous improvements. The Company consistently manages B3 waste from operational activities through two ways, i.e. independent processing and handover to third parties who have permits in accordance with the Regulation of the Minister of Environment. Therefore, during the 2021 period the Company did not face environmental-related problems and was able to carry out operational activities with positive performance.

During 2021, the Company has realized the cost of environmental responsibility of Rp120 million. We are committed to optimizing energy use effectively and efficiently, so as to contribute to reducing emissions. In 2021, we were able to reduce our average energy use by 22.73% by the substitution of non-curing adhesive materials (without the use of electric ovens). A decrease in energy consumption will contribute to a decrease in the resulting GHG emissions. In addition, the intensity of energy use in 2021 also decreased, due to the reduced operational activities of incinerators which affected a significant decrease in gas usage.

The Company succeeded in creating a decent and safe working environment for employees, through the implementation of the OHS management system in accordance with applicable laws and international standards. In addition, we achieved zero fatality achievement in 2021 and again won awards from external parties related to the Occupational Safety and Health Supervisory Committee (P2K3).
