# Kode Etik dan Perilaku Anti-Persaingan

Seluruh karyawan wajib menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawab secara profesional, sesuai dengan Kode Etik Perseroan yang berlaku. Selain itu, Kode Etik Perseroan juga mengatur kepatuhan Perseroan terhadap prinsip-prinsip persaingan usaha, salah satunya etika usaha terkait hubungan dengan pesaing. Perseroan menjalankan usaha berdasarkan prinsip-prinsip persaingan usaha yang sehat sesuai dengan perundang-undangan yang berlaku. Kami menjadikan perusahaan pesaing sebagai pembanding (benchmark), karena kami memandang persaingan sebagai tantangan untuk meningkatkan kinerja Perseroan.

# Code of Ethics and Anti-Competitive Conduct

All employees are obliged to carry out their duties and responsibilities professionally, in accordance with the Applicable Code of Ethics of the Company. In addition, the Company's Code of Ethics also regulates the Company's compliance with the principles of business competition, one of which is business ethics related to relationships with competitors. The Company conducts business based on the principles of healthy business competition in accordance with applicable legislation. We make competing companies as benchmarks, because we view competition as a challenge to improve the Company's performance.
