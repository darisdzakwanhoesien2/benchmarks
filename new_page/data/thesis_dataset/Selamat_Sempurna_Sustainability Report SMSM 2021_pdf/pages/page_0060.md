# CAPAIAN KINERJA LINGKUNGAN
## ENVIRONMENTAL PERFORMANCE ACHIEVEMENTS

Perseroan menjadikan ISO 14001 (Sistem Manajemen Lingkungan) sebagai acuan dalam memastikan setiap aspek operasional beserta produk yang dihasilkan telah memenuhi komitmen terhadap lingkungan. Selain itu, kami juga terus berupaya mematuhi peraturan di bidang lingkungan, pencegahan pencemaran, serta berkomitmen terhadap perbaikan berkelanjutan.

Sepanjang tahun 2021, tidak terdapat pelanggaran terhadap regulasi lingkungan hidup maupun konsekuensi hukum yang diterima Perseroan atau pemasok, yang mengakibatkan kegiatan operasional terhenti. [307-1]

The Company makes ISO 14001 (Environmental Management System) as a reference in ensuring that every aspect of operations and products produced has met its commitment to the environment. In addition, we also continue to strive to comply with regulations in the field of environment, pollution prevention, and are committed to continuous improvement.

Throughout 2021, there were no violations of environmental regulations or legal consequences received by the Company or suppliers, resulting in operational activities being stopped. [307-1]
