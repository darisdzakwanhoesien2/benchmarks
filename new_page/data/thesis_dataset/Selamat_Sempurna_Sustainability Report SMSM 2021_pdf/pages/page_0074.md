# Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja (K3)

Perseroan menerapkan Sistem Manajemen K3 (SMK3) dan regulasi yang berlaku sebagai dasar penerapan K3. Divisi Safety, Health, and Environment (SHE) bertanggung jawab atas penerapan SMK3 di lingkungan Perseroan melalui upaya identifikasi risiko kerja dalam rangka menekan angka kecelakaan kerja. SMK3 Perseroan mencakup seluruh area pabrik dan kantor, serta seluruh karyawan (100%) pada seluruh tingkat jabatan. [403-1] [403-3]

Perseroan memastikan seluruh jajaran manajemen dan staf diingatkan kembali untuk melaksanakan Komitmen K3L, yang berisi:

- Setiap jajaran manajemen dan staf mengeliminasi hal-hal yang dapat mengakibatkan kecelakaan, cedera dan penyakit akibat kerja, serta pencemaran lingkungan guna melestarikan alam dan penggunaan energi ke arah perbaikan yang berkelanjutan.
- Selalu mendukung program dan kebijakan K3L serta mensosialisasikannya ke seluruh karyawan dan manajemen sesuai peraturan yang berlaku.

Untuk memenuhi komitmen tersebut, Perseroan membentuk Panitia Pembina Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (P2K3) yang disahkan oleh Dinas Tenaga Kerja Provinsi Banten. P2K3 bertugas untuk mengaplikasikan program-program K3 dalam mencegah kecelakaan dan penyakit akibat kerja. Pada tahun 2021, Perseroan mendapatkan penghargaan dari Pihak Eksternal terkait P2K3.

# Occupational Health and Safety (OHS)

The Company implements the OHS Management System (SMK3) and applicable regulations as the basis for implementing OHS. The Safety, Health, and Environment (SHE) Division is responsible for the implementation of SMK3 in the Company's environment through efforts to identify work risks in order to reduce the number of work accidents. SMK3 of the Company covers all areas of factories and offices, as well as all employees (100%) at all levels of positions. [403-1] [403-3]

The Company ensures that all management and staff are reminded to carry out the K3L Commitment, which contains:

- Each management and staff eliminates things that can cause accidents, injuries and diseases due to work, as well as environmental pollution to preserve nature and energy use towards sustainable improvement.
- Always support K3L programs and policies and socialize them to all employees and management in accordance with applicable regulations.

To fulfill this commitment, the Company established a Committee for Occupational Safety and Health Management (P2K3) authorized by the Banten Provincial Manpower Office. P2K3 is tasked to apply OHS programs in preventing accidents and diseases due to work. In 2021, the Company received an award from external parties related to P2K3.
