## Komitmen Layanan Setara kepada Pelanggan

Perseroan menerapkan sistem manajemen mutu terpadu sebagai bagian dari pengendalian kualitas produk terhadap konsumen. Selain itu, proses produksi dan distribusi yang kami laksanakan mengacu pada standar internasional serta standar industri komponen otomotif, yakni ISO/TS 16949. Dengan menerapkan hal-hal tersebut secara berkesinambungan, Perseroan berkomitmen untuk memberikan layanan setara kepada pelanggan serta memastikan kepuasan pelanggan.

Sejak tahun 2018, Perseroan telah mengadopsi standar ISO di seluruh area produksi serta memperoleh sertifikasi ISO 14001:2015 untuk lingkup Desain dan Pembuatan Penyaring (Udara, Oli, Bahan Bakar, Hidraulik). Kami juga berupaya meningkatkan nilai bagi pelanggan melalui Polis Asuransi Jaminan atas Produk Utama Perseroan, yang mencakup risiko terkait dengan produk, misalnya risiko penarikan produk dari peredaran.

## Equal Service Commitment to Customers

The Company implements an integrated quality management system as part of product quality control for consumers. In addition, the production and distribution process that we carry out refers to international standards and automotive component industry standards, i.e. ISO/TS 16949. By implementing these things continuously, the Company is committed to providing equal service to customers and ensuring customer satisfaction.

Since 2018, the Company has adopted ISO standards in all production areas and obtained ISO 14001:2015 certification for the scope of Filter Design and Manufacture (Air, Oil, Fuel, Hydraulics). We also strive to increase value for customers through the Insurance Policy on the Company's Main Product, which includes risks related to the product, such as the risk of withdrawing products from circulation.

## Employee Remuneration

The Company ensures that employee remuneration is above regional wage standards and applicable regulations. The provision of employee wages at the Head Office is in accordance with the rules of the Provincial Minimum Wage (UMP) of DKI Jakarta Raya.
