## Evaluasi Keamanan Produk

Perseroan secara berkala mengevaluasi keamanan produk, terutama untuk memastikan bahwa produk tidak mengandung material berbahaya. Kami telah mengevaluasi performa dan ketahanan seluruh produk filter (100%) yang telah kami luncurkan. Perseroan telah memastikan bahwa produk-produk tersebut telah memenuhi ketentuan dan kebijakan keamanan di masing-masing negara tujuan ekspor. Selain itu, kami juga menyertakan surat pernyataan bersamaan dengan barang yang diproduksi dan dikirim tidak mengandung material berbahaya.

Perseroan mengevaluasi keamanan produk melalui beberapa cara, yaitu substance of concern untuk produk electro plating, REACH Agreement dan International Material Data System (IMDS) untuk memantau adanya substansi berbahaya pada produk ketika ekspor, serta audit produk. Selain itu, keamanan rantai pasok produk kami telah tersertifikasi Authorized Economic Operator (AEO) dari Kementerian Keuangan Republik Indonesia Direktorat Jendral Bea dan Cukai (DJBC).

Sertifikasi AEO merupakan program yang dibangun berdasarkan dasar kerja sama internasional antar negara anggota Organisasi Kepabeanan Dunia (World Customs Organization/WCO) untuk meningkatkan fasilitas dan pengamanan perdagangan global yang mengacu kepada kerangka standar Secure and Facilitate Global Trade (SAFE) yang dibentuk oleh WCO. Lewat program AEO, negara-negara di dunia mulai menyeleksi perusahaan yang memiliki reputasi baik dan yang tidak baik, terutama dalam perdagangan dunia. Perusahaan yang memiliki reputasi baik akan diberikan sertifikat AEO yang diakui oleh negara-negara anggota WCO di dunia sehingga perusahaan tersebut akan difasilitasi kegiatan perdagangannya di seluruh dunia.

## Product Safety Evaluation

The Company periodically evaluates product safety, especially to ensure that the product does not contain hazardous materials. We have evaluated the performance and durability of all filter products (100%) that we have launched. The Company has ensured that these products have met the provisions and security policies in each export destination country. In addition, we also include affidavits along with goods produced and shipped do not contain harmful materials.

The Company evaluates product safety through several ways, i.e. substance of concern for electro plating products, REACH Agreement and International Material Data System (IMDS) to monitor the presence of harmful substances in products when exporting, as well as product audits. In addition, the security of our product supply chain has been certified authorized economic operator (AEO) from the Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Indonesia Directorate General of Customs and Excise (DJBC).

AEO certification is a program built on the basis of international cooperation between member countries of the World Customs Organization (WCO) to improve facilities and safeguards of global trade that refers to the Secure and Facilitate Global Trade (SAFE) standard framework established by the WCO. Through the AEO program, countries in the world began to select companies that have a good reputation and which are not good, especially in world trade. A reputable company will be awarded an AEO certificate recognized by the WCO member countries of the world so that the company will be facilitated its trading activities around the world.
