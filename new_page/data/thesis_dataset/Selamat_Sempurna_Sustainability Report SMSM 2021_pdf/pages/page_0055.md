Selain dinamika perkembangan pandemi, berbagai tantangan lain yang dihadapi pada tahun 2021 adalah disrupsi rantai pasok global dan krisis energi global yang pada akhirnya menjadi salah satu tantangan bagi kegiatan ekspor Perseroan, walaupun tantangan yang dirasakan tidak sebesar di tahun 2020. Pandemi COVID-19 menyebabkan fluktuasi substansial pada permintaan kontainer yang mengganggu pasokan logistik kontainer global. Pembatasan dan lock-down yang diberlakukan oleh sebagian besar negara di awal pandemi menurunkan volume perdagangan dan permintaan kontainer. In addition to the dynamics of pandemic development, various other challenges faced in 2021 are disruption of the global supply chain and the global energy crisis which ultimately becomes one of the challenges for the Company's export activities, although the perceived challenges are not as great as in 2020. The COVID-19 pandemic caused substantial fluctuations in container demand that disrupted global container logistics supplies. Restrictions and lock-downs imposed by most countries at the beginning of the pandemic decreased the volume of trade and demand for containers. Companies engaged in logistics respond to this by adjusting the operation of container transport ships, and redirecting or parking them in some places and canceling sailing (empty sailing) for certain ships. This then led to the disruption of the global supply chain in early 2021 due to the increase in world demand after the recovery of community activities and economic activities, not offset by the readiness of shipping goods due to the abnormal activity of freight forwarding companies. The increase in demand for goods and the abnormality of shipping activities led to an increase in shipping costs.
