Untuk limbah cair domestik, Perseroan mengolahnya dengan menggunakan Sewage Treatment Plan (STP) dengan menggunakan bakteri secara aerob dan anaerob yang menghasilkan lumpur dan air limbah. Lumpur yang dihasilkan dari proses STP dikelola oleh pihak ketiga penyedia jasa sedot lumpur dan air limbah, kemudian dilakukan pengecekan terhadap kualitasnya sebelum dibuang ke aliran sungai.

The Company has a reciprocating incinerator that can be used to process waste periodically with minimal fuel use and pollution production. Furthermore, the burning ash is accommodated at TPS B3 and then handed over to third parties carriers (transporters) and licensed B3 waste processors. [306-2]

For non-B3 waste, the Company processes it by handing over the waste to a third party or collector for further processing. Liquid waste (effluent) resulting from production activities (painting process) is carried out using Waste Water Treatment (WWT) with coagulation and flocculation processing methods.

Every month, the Company checks water quality (pH) to ensure water safety when it will be consumed and when water will be discharged into the river. The Company ensures that the water waste has been separated from the sludge which will be treated separately through a press machine and stored at TPS B3 for further handover to third parties. [306-1] [306-2]

For domestic liquid waste, the Company processes it using the Sewage Treatment Plan (STP) using aerobic and anaerobic bacteria that produce sludge and wastewater. The sludge generated from the STP process is managed by a third party provider of sludge and wastewater suction services, then checking its quality before being discharged into the river flow.

In 2021, we produced 170.4 Tons of B3 waste with a B3 waste intensity of 3.05 grams/production unit and 7,963 Tons of non-B3 waste with a non-B3 waste intensity of 142.99 grams/production unit.
