## Keanekaragaman Hayati

Wilayah operasional beserta pabrik Perseroan berada di pusat industri, sehingga tidak berada di sekitar atau berdekatan dengan area yang dilindungi atau yang memiliki keanekaragaman hayati tinggi. Begitu pula dengan jalur distribusi produk yang melalui darat, laut, dan udara, namun tidak bersinggungan langsung dengan area yang dilindungi. Meskipun demikian, Perseroan tetap mendukung upaya konservasi keanekaragaman hayati di sekitar lokasi operasional maupun di kawasan lainnya melalui beberapa program lingkungan hidup, yaitu:

The Company also seeks to reduce energy for the production process through gas and electricity savings by replacing adhesive materials that use gas-fired ovens (curring ovens) using non-curing adhesive materials (without the use of electric ovens). In addition, the Company reduces the operating hours of waste material treatment equipment (incinerators) that use gas and electricity fuel by transferring some materials handed over to licensed waste managers. These efforts succeeded in reducing gas use by 1,032 BTU/Pcs, electricity by 0.01619 kWh/Pcs, and diesel by 0.13 Milliliters/Pcs compared to 2020.

## Biodiversity

The Company's operational areas and factories are located in the industrial center, so they are not around or adjacent to protected areas or those with high biodiversity. Similarly, product distribution channels are by land, sea, and air, but do not intersect directly with protected areas.
