## Survei Kepuasan Pelanggan

Perseroan melibatkan pelanggan untuk memberikan penilaian melalui survei kepuasan pelanggan yang dilakukan oleh PIC Pemasaran Lokal dan Ekspor melalui metode kuesioner. Hal tersebut kami lakukan untuk memperbaiki dan meningkatkan kualitas produk dan jasa yang kami berikan, serta menciptakan inovasi-inovasi baru yang bermanfaat di masa mendatang.

Skor kepuasan dari Pelanggan Ekspor Filter dan Radiator tahun 2021 mengalami peningkatan dari tahun 2020, akibat meningkatnya pelunasan atas backorder dan on-time delivery.

As of the end of 2021, no products have been recalled and banned by the authorities, for example due to the use of certain materials or energy sources that are prohibited.

## Customer Satisfaction Survey

The Company engages customers to provide assessments through customer satisfaction surveys conducted by Local Marketing and Export PIC through questionnaire methods. We do this to improve the quality of the products and services we provide, and create new innovations that are useful in the future.

The satisfaction score of Filter and Radiator Export Customers in 2021 increased from 2020, due to increased repayment of backorders and on-time delivery.
