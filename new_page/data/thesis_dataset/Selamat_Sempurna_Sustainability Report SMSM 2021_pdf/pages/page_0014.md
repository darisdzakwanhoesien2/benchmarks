Pada tahun 2021, pandemi COVID-19 masih menjadi tantangan bagi Perseroan. Kami tetap memberikan hak-hak karyawan secara penuh. Kami melaksanakan pertemuan K3 setiap 2 minggu sekali, baik secara daring maupun luring, serta memperketat protokol kesehatan dengan memeriksa suhu karyawan sebelum memasuki lokasi kerja, menjaga jarak, serta memberikan manfaat tambahan seperti vitamin dan alat pelindung diri (APD), yakni masker dan face shield. Segala upaya tersebut dilakukan agar karyawan tetap terlindungi kesehatannya selama masa pandemi.

Kami terus berupaya untuk menciptakan nilai bagi masyarakat, melalui program-program pengembangan masyarakat yang berfokus pada bidang sosial, lingkungan, pendidikan, keagamaan, dan kesehatan. Hingga akhir tahun 2021, Perseroan merealisasikan dana untuk kegiatan sosial mencapai Rp24,16 juta.

Sepanjang tahun 2021, Perseroan secara konsisten menerapkan prinsip tata kelola perusahaan yang baik (Good Corporate Governance/GCG). Berdasarkan hasil pengukuran GCG berdasarkan standar ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS) 2021 oleh Indonesia Institute for Corporate Directorship (IICD), Skor ACGS Perseroan adalah 89,25 (Predikat "Good") lebih tinggi dibandingkan tahun 2020 yang mencapai 79,18. Skor ACGS tersebut menunjukkan implementasi GCG sudah melampaui kepatuhan terhadap persyaratan minimal dan mengadopsi sebagian standar internasional yang disyaratkan oleh ACGS. Mengacu pada hasil penilaian terhadap 100 emiten dengan kapitalisasi pasar terbesar atau BigCap100 dan Emiten pada kapitalisasi pasar menengah Mid Cap pada penilaian ACGS 2020, skor Perseroan berada jauh di atas rata-rata skor secara keseluruhan MidCap100 (65,38) dan BigCap100 (73,63).

ADR Digital Learning platform to provide training and education for each employee. In 2021, we have carried out 333 trainings attended by 4,927 participants with an average of 4 hours of training hours per employee per year.

In 2021, the COVID-19 pandemic is still a challenge for the Company. We continue to provide full employee rights. We hold OHS meetings every 2 weeks, both online and offline, and tighten health protocols by checking employee temperatures before entering the work site, maintaining distance, and providing additional benefits such as vitamins and personal protective equipment (PPE), i.e. masks and face shields. All these efforts are made so that employees remain protected for their health during the pandemic.

We continuously strive to create value for society, through community development programs that focus on social, environmental, educational, religious, and health fields. Until the end of 2021, the Company realized funds for social activities reaching Rp24.16 million.

Throughout 2021, the Company has consistently implemented the principles of good corporate governance (GCG). Based on the results of GCG measurements based on the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS) 2021 standard by the Indonesia Institute for Corporate Directorship (IICD), the Company's ACGS Score is 89.25 ("Good" Predicate) higher than in 2020 which reached 79.18. The ACGS score shows that GCG implementation has exceeded compliance with minimum requirements and adopted some of the international standards required by ACGS. Referring to the results of the assessment of the 100 issuers with the largest market capitalization or BigCap100 and issuers in mid-cap mid-market capitalization at the ACGS 2020 valuation, the Company's score is well above the average overall score of MidCap100 (65.38) and BigCap100 (73.63).
