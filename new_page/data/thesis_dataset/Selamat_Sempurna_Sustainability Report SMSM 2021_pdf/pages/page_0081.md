Pada tahun 2021, pandemi COVID-19 masih menjadi tantangan, sehingga Perseroan masih fokus menjaga karyawan agar tidak terpapar virus atau meminimalisir potensi penularan virus. Kami melaksanakan pertemuan K3 setiap 2 minggu sekali, baik secara daring maupun luring, serta memperketat protokol kesehatan dengan memeriksa suhu karyawan sebelum masuk, menjaga jarak, dan membagikan masker kain untuk karyawan yang bekerja di kantor atau pabrik. Perseroan juga menyediakan fasilitas bagi karyawan yang terpapar COVID-19, yakni berupa fasilitas isolasi mandiri, pembayaran tes swab PCR, dan tetap membayarkan gaji secara penuh.

In 2021, the COVID-19 pandemic is still a challenge, so the Company is still focused on keeping employees from being exposed to the virus or minimizing the potential for virus transmission. We hold OHS meetings every 2 weeks, both online and offline, and tighten health protocols by checking employee temperature before entering, maintaining distance, and distributing cloth masks to employees working in offices or factories. The Company also provides facilities for employees exposed to COVID-19, i.e. in the form of self-isolation facilities, PCR swab test payments, and still paying salaries in full.
