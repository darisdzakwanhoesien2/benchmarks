## Implikasi Finansial terkait Perubahan Iklim dan Pascapandemi COVID-19 [201-2]

Perubahan iklim yang terjadi akibat pemanasan global dapat berdampak bagi Perseroan, baik secara langsung maupun tidak langsung. Kami berupaya untuk mengantisipasi dampak bencana alam akibat perubahan iklim dengan cara menempatkan investasi finansial untuk mengasuransikan bangunan

The Company has established new strategies for managing market challenges and opportunities, such as efficiency and streamlining of business implementation, to face new challenges in the future. The Company continues to consider aspects of risk management, prioritizes the principle of prudence in strengthening financial conditions and asset optimization efforts, discipline in cost management, increasing efficiency and cost effectiveness, and actively conducting exploration and initiatives that increase added value and provide synergy for the Company.
