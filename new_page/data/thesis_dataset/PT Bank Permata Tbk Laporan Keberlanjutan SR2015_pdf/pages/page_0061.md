# TATA KELOLA UNTUK KEBERLANJUTAN
Tata Kelola untuk Keberlanjutan

Manajemen PermataBank, setelah melakukan kajian dan analisa atas laporan yang diperoleh dari karyawan, akan melakukan tindak lanjut yang melibatkan pihak-pihak terkait meliputi Fraud Risk Management, Internal Audit, Legal, Human Resources, PIC Speak Up, maupun pihak-pihak lain jika diperlukan.

Kebijakan Speak-Up dibuat dengan mengacu kepada prinsip-prinsip dibawah ini:

1. Laporan bersifat rahasia. Hanya Administrator Speak-Up dan Speak-Up Council yang mempunyai wewenang untuk mengetahui isi laporan.
2. Pihak pelapor dapat memilih 1 dari 3 prinsip anonim di bawah ini:
- Full Disclosure
- Partial anonymity
- Anonymous
3. Pihak pelapor berhak mendapatkan perlindungan dari PermataBank bila akibat dari laporannya mengakibatkan sesuatu hal yang mengancam keberadaan pelapor di dalam maupun di luar PermataBank.
4. Pihak pelapor dibebaskan dari segala sanksi bila ternyata laporannya tidak dapat dibuktikan kebenarannya.
5. PermataBank menjamin semua pihak pelapor bahwa laporan-laporan mereka tidak akan mempengaruhi penilaian kinerja dan/atau perjalanan karir mereka di PermataBank.
6. Dengan jaminan tersebut di atas, pihak pelapor diharapkan bersedia untuk memberikan informasi yang lebih terperinci kepada manajemen terkait tindak lanjut atas laporan mereka.

## Penggunaan dan Ouput Speak-Up

Selama tahun 2015, laporan yang telah diterima melalui saluran Speak Up berjumlah sebanyak 11 laporan dan seluruhnya telah ditindaklanjuti.

PermataBank's management will follow through, review, and analyze every report. The management's follow-up involves relevant parties, such as Fraud Risk Management, Internal Audit, Legal, Human Resources, PIC Speak Up, and other parties as deemed necessary.

The following principles inform Speak-Up policies:

1. All reports are strictly confidential. Only Speak Up Administrator and Council have the authority to access reports' content.
2. A person who makes the report can choose one of the three degrees of anonymity:
- Full Disclosure
- Partial anonymity
- Anonymous
3. A person who makes the report has the right to obtain PermataBank's protection if the act of reporting leads to threatening situations inside or outside of PermataBank.
4. A person who makes the report shall not be held liable if the report cannot be substantiated.
5. PermataBank provides assurance that the reports will not affect performance assessment and/or career development of any person making the report.
6. Having such guarantee, a person who makes a report is encouraged to provide as detailed information as possible to the management for the benefit of further investigation on the report.

## Use of Speak-Up and Output

During the course of 2015, there were a total of 11 reports received through Speak Up channels. All reports have been followed through.
