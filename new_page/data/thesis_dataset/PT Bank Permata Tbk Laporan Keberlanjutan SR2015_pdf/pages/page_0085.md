# SAMBUT HARI OLAHRAGA NASIONAL PERMATABANK SELENGGARAKAN KOMPETISI OLAHRAGA DIKOMBINASIKAN LITERASI KEUANGAN

Celebrating National Sports Day PermataBank Held Sports Competition and Financial Literacy Event 04-301 FS16

Dalam rangka memperingati Hari Olahraga Nasional (9/9), PermataBank menggelar kompetisi olahraga &amp; literasi keuangan - 'Permata FITS Tournament 2015' pada hari Minggu, 6 September 2015 di Lapangan Futsal Cilandak Sports Center.

Kegiatan ini dihadiri oleh Achmad K. Permana – Direktur PermataBank Syariah yang mewakili pimpinan korporasi, dan melibatkan 60 anak dari Yayasan Rama-Rama dan PermataClub Futsal Kids. Usai pertandingan, dilanjutkan dengan kompetisi berhitung dari modul Financial Literacy Through Sports (FITS) yang menjadi nilai tambah dalam penentuan pemenang pada turnamen tersebut.

Celebrating National Sports Day (9/9), PermataBank held sports &amp; financial literacy event - 'Permata FITS Tournament 2015' – on Sunday, 6 September 2015, at Futsal Field of Cilandak Sports Center.

Permata FITS Tournament 2015 was attended by Achmad K. Permana – Director of PermataBank Syariah, representing corporate leadership, and 60 children from Rama-Rama Foundation and PermataClub Futsal Kids. The match was followed by math competition taken from Financial Literacy Through Sports (FITS) module, in which scores were added to determine the winner of the tournament.
