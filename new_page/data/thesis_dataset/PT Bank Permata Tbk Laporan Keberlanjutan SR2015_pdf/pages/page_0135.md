## Skema Gedung Ramah Lingkungan

Sejak 2009, PermataBank menerapkan sistem rating bagi gedung-gedung kantor PermataBank yaitu Building Environmental Assessment Standard (BEAS) yang ditetapkan oleh Standard Chartered Bank dan telah diterapkan di 30 negara. BEAS mengukur dampak operasional dari gedung-gedung GEMS PermataBank. Termasuk dalam kriteria BEAS adalah pengelolaan emisi GRK, konsumsi listrik dan konsumsi air. Pada 2015, seluruh kantor cabang PermataBank yang masuk dalam kategori GEMS telah dievaluasi dan dipastikan pemenuhannya terhadap kriteria BEAS.

Selain itu, sesuai dengan peraturan izin lingkungan di Indonesia, PermataBank memastikan dilakukannya laporan pengelolaan dan pemantauan lingkungan pada gedung-gedung yang dimiliki dan dikelola oleh PermataBank. Pengelolaan, pemantauan, dan pelaporan dilakukan sesuai dengan dokumen izin lingkungan yang relevan pada masing-masing gedung, termasuk di dalamnya adalah pemantauan kualitas air dan kualitas udara lingkungan. Hal ini untuk memastikan dampak lingkungan kegiatan PermataBank terhadap lingkungan sesuai dengan persyaratan peraturan perundangan. PermataBank juga ingin menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang sehat, aman, dan nyaman bagi para PermataBankers.

## Green Building Scheme

Since 2009, PermataBank has been applying Building Environmental Assessment Standard (BEAS) rating system for PermataBank's office buildings. This system is also applied by Standard Chartered Bank and has been implemented in 30 countries. BEAS measures operational impacts of PermataBank GEMS buildings. BEAS criteria includes GHG emission management and electricity and water consumption. In 2015, all PermataBank GEMS buildings were evaluated and their conformity to BEAS criteria were confirmed.

In addition to that, in accordance with applicable environmental permit regulation in Indonesia, PermataBank ensures that there is proper and continuous reporting of environmental management and monitoring practices in buildings managed and owned by PermataBank. The management, monitoring, and reporting – including on water and air quality – conform with environmental permits relevant to each building. This measure is taken in order to ensure that environmental impacts resulted by PermataBank's activities are within regulatory corridor. Further, it is also part of PermataBank's endeavors to create healthy, safe, and comfortable workplace for PermataBankers.
