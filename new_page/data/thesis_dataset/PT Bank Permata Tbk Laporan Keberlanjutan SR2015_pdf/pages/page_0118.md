Selain itu, PermataBank juga melaksanakan pengelolaan talenta (SDM unggul) secara terstruktur dan sistematis, melalui tahapan identifikasi dan pengembangan khusus. Program pengembangan khusus termasuk pendidikan/pelatihan, coaching/mentoring, pengnasaan mengelola proyek dan rotasi sesuai kebutuhan pengembangan masing-masing individu. Pendidikan dan pelatihan khusus yang diberikan termasuk program peningkatan pendidikan formal dan program pengembangan eksekutif bekerja sama dengan institusi pendidikan INSEAD, NUS, dan BINUS. Pada tahun 2015, 76% kekosongan pada posisi pejabat eksekutif dapat dipenuhi dari kader yang telah dikembangkan sendiri.

PermataBank also carries out structured and systematic management of its most talented human capital (leading employees). They are identified and receive special development programs, which include education/training, coaching/mentoring, project management assignment, and employee rotation tailored to their individual development needs. Special education and training programs include formal education upscale and executive development programs in collaboration with leading educational institutions INSEAD, NUS, and BINUS. In 2015, 76% of vacant executive positions were successfully filled by home-grown talents.
