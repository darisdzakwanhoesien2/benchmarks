# Pekan Sehat Permata

# Permata Health Week

![img-134.jpeg](img-134.jpeg)

Pekan Sehat Permata digelar kembali untuk kedua kalinya di tahun 2015 ini. Seluruh PermataBankers menyambut dengan antusias keseluruhan rangkaian acara yang diadakan di Tower I WTC 2 pada tanggal 26-30 Oktober 2015 dan di Tower III Bintaro pada tanggal 2-7 November 2015.

Rangkaian acara Pekan Sehat Permata 2015 meliputi Health Talk (seminar dan talkshow kesehatan yang mengundang dokter ahli dari Rumah Sakit Siloam Group, perwakilan Yayasan Jantung Indonesia, dan penyintas kanker dari Komunitas Love Pink Indonesia), Health Check (pemeriksaan kesehatan dan konsultasi dokter umum &amp; ahli gizi dari Rumah Sakit Siloam Group), Health Fair (pameran alat-alat kesehatan), Mini Gym (kelas senam seperti yoga, zumba, senam jantung, dan body combat dengan instruktur dari Gold's Gym Indonesia dan Yayasan Jantung Indonesia), Blood Donation (donor darah bersama Palang Merah Indonesia), CPR Training (pelatihan resusitasi jantung paru bersama PRO Emergency), Bike To Work (gerakan naik sepeda ke kantor), No Tobacco Day (gerakan hari tanpa asap rokok) dan Health Quiz (kuis yang menguji pengetahuan tentang topik kesehatan).

Permata Health Week was back in 2015. Conducted for the second time, all PermataBankers welcomed this occasion enthusiastically. The series of events were organized at Tower I WTC 2 on 26-30 October 2015 and at Tower III Bintaro on 2-7 November 2015.

Permata Health Week 2015 included events such as Health Talk (health seminar and talkshow, inviting speakers from Siloam Hospitals Group, Yayasan Jantung Indonesia, and cancer survivors from Love Pink Indonesia community), health check (check-up and medical consultation with general practitioners &amp; nutritionists from Siloam Hospitals Group), health fair (medical equipment exhibition), mini gym (exercise classes, e.g. yoga, zumba, heart exercise, and body combat with instructors from Gold's Gym Indonesia and Yayasan Jantung Indonesia), blood donation (with Indonesian Red Cross), CPR Training (heart and lung resuscitation training with PRO Emergency), Bike to Work (movement that promotes cycling to the workplace), No Tobacco Day, and health quiz (quiz around health topics).
