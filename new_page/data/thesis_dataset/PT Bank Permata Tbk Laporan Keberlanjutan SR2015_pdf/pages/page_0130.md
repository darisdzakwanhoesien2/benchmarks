# MEWUJUDKAN OPERASIONAL RAMAH LINGKUNGAN

Dalam menciptakan operasi yang ramah lingkungan, PermataBank memfokuskan pada efisiensi energi, air, sampah kertas, pengelolaan gedung ramah lingkungan, kepatuhan pada izin lingkungan, dan promosi lingkungan hidup yang melibatkan PermataBankers. Sehubungan dengan banyaknya bangunan yang dikelola oleh PermataBank, saat ini difokuskan pada upaya pengelolaan di bangunan-bangunan utama yang memiliki luas lebih dari 20.000 kaki persegi atau 1.850 m² yang memenuhi kriteria Global Environmental Management System (GEMS Building). Ke depannya, pemantauan akan dilakukan ke kantor cabang di seluruh Indonesia secara bertahap. GEMS Building mewakili 30% dari seluruh portofolio kantor PermataBank.

# REALIZING ENVIRONMENTALLY FRIENDLY OPERATIONS

To create environmentally friendly operations, PermataBank focuses on efficient energy, water, paper, and building management use and practices as well as compliance with environmental permits and promoting environmental awareness to PermataBankers. In terms of building management, considering the large number of building under PermataBank's management, the focus currently is on the main buildings that are of over 20,000 sq.ft, or 1,850 square meter. These buildings conform with Global Environmental Management Systems (GEMS) criteria. This standard will be gradually rolled-out to other branch offices in Indonesia. As of today, GEMS compliant buildings represent 30% of the total PermataBank's building portfolio.
