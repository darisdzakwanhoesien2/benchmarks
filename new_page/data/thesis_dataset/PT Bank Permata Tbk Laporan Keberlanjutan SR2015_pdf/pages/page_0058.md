## Implementasi Kebijakan, Limit Eksposur dan Kerangka Delegasi Wewenang Risiko 04-16

PermataBank menggunakan kerangka pengendalian sebagai pedoman yang jelas dan tegas mengenai batasan dalam pengambilan risiko. Terdapat tiga komponen kunci dalam kerangka pengendalian risiko, yang secara kolektif menyediakan mekanisme untuk mengatur dan menyesuaikan batasan dalam pengambilan risiko yaitu:

- Kebijakan menetapkan aturan dan standar yang jelas atas semua aktivitas bisnis. Kebijakan menggambarkan tujuan pengendalian PermataBank dan menginformasikan strategi operasional, organisasi dan infrastruktur PermataBank.
- Limit eksposur memberikan batasan yang jelas untuk risiko finansial. Limit diaplikasikan pada area di mana pengukuran dan pemantauan eksposur risiko dapat diandalkan, terhadap seluruh variabel risiko. Untuk memastikan konsentrasi eksposur yang sifatnya material telah dipertimbangkan kapanpun eksposur tersebut timbul, limit dapat ditetapkan pada beberapa tingkatan seperti transaksi perorangan, nasabah, portofolio, produk/proses, lini bisnis, geografis, dan sebagainya. Pada tingkat teratas, penentuan batasan toleransi risiko disesuaikan dengan Risk Appetite dan tingkat toleransi stakeholder utama.

In 2015, PermataBank's Annual Report recognized 10 risk types that have been identified pursuant to the regulations of the Financial Services Authority, namely: (1) Credit Risk, (2) Market Risk, (3) Operational Risk, (4) Liquidity Risk, (5) Legal Risk, (6) Strategic Risk, (7) Compliance Risk, (8) Reputational Risk, (9) Return Risk, and (10) Investment Risk.

## Policy, Exposure Limit, and Risk Authority Delegation 04-16

PermataBank applies clear and rigorous guidelines to limit risk taking. There are three components in risk control framework that collectively provides a mechanism to regulate and adjust the limits of risk taking. These components are:

- Policies, that establish clear rules and standard of all business activities. Policies articulate the objectives of PermataBank's control and inform PermataBank's operational strategies, organization, and infrastructure.
- Exposure Limits provide clear-cut boundaries for financial risks. Limits are applied in areas where PermataBank can reliably measure and monitor risk exposures across all risk variables. To ensure that material exposures have been taken into account whenever they occur, limits can be set at a number of levels, such as individual transaction, customer, portfolio, products/processes, business lines, geography, and so on. At the highest level, risk tolerance limit conforms with Risk Appetite and tolerance of key stakeholders.
