# Appreciation Day for HSE Volunteers

Demi mewujudkan lingkungan kerja yang aman dan sehat, PermataBankers terpilih menjalankan tugas tambahan sebagai Kapten Lantai, Petugas P3K, dan Anggota P2K3 atau disebut juga dengan HSE Champions. Atas dedikasi yang luar biasa itulah, manajemen PermataBank memberikan apresiasi kepada para HSE Champions tersebut dalam bentuk acara yang bernama Appreciation Day for HSE Champions.

# Appreciation Day for HSE Volunteers

To realize safe and healthy workplace, selected PermataBankers had additional role as Floor Warden, First Aid Responder, and member of HSE Committee. These employees are also known as HSE Champions. Acknowledging their dedication, PermataBank management regularly organizes Appreciation Day for HSE Champions.

![img-139.jpeg](img-139.jpeg)

Acara Appreciation Day for HSE Champions diselenggarakan pada Senin sore, tanggal 21 Desember 2015 di Tower III Bintaro Lantai 18 Ruang Executive dengan tema "To Infinity &amp; Beyond". Di tahun 2015, ini terdapat ratusan PermataBankers yang hadir pada sore itu dan beberapa diantaranya mendapatkan penghargaan lebih yakni sebagai The Best Floor Warden, The Best First Aider, dan The Best P2K3 Member.

Selain itu, juga terdapat penyerahan penghargaan kepada pemenang CRES 6S Awards di tingkat bankwide. CRES 6S Awards adalah penghargaan yang diberikan untuk direktorat/cabang yang paling menerapkan 6S di lingkungan kerjanya, yakni Sort, Set In Order, Shine, Standardize, Sustain, dan Safety.

Appreciation Day for HSE Champions in 2015, themed "To Infinity &amp; Beyond", was held on the evening of Monday, 21 December, at Executive Room Tower III Bintaro, 18th floor. In 2015, hundreds of PermataBankers attended the event; some of them obtained the highest appreciation as Best Floor Warden, Best First Aider, and Best P2K3 Member.

In addition, there were also awards for winners of bankwide CRES 6S Awards. CRES 69 Awards is given to a directorate/branch that is best in implementing the 6S principles in their workplace, namely Sort, Set In Order, Shine, Standardize, Sustain, and Safety.
