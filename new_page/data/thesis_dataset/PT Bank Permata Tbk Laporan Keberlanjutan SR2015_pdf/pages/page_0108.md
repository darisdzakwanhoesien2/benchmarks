Everyone in PermataBank, from Board Of Commissioners, Board Of Directors, to employees, must abide by the code of conduct and work culture.

PRICE is continually disseminated and instilled to all PermataBankers. For PermataBankers, these values need to be understood and serve as thinking basis and behavior reference in their daily activities, including when interacting with our stakeholders. To every new employee, hired as fresh graduate or part of pro-hire program, work culture is disseminated through an induction program. G4-56 G4-504

# NILAI BUDAYA KERJA PERMATABANKERS

## Partnership
Kami saling memahami dan bersama-sama membangun hubungan yang kokoh dengan pihak internal dan eksternal berlandaskan rasa saling menghormati.

## Responsiveness
Kami bekerja dengan cepat, akurat, dan efektif dalam memberikan layanan yang tepat waktu.

## Innovation
Kami selalu berpikir inovatif untuk meningkatkan cara kita bekerja, membuatnya lebih mudah, lebih baik dan lebih cepat.

## Caring
Kami menaruh perhatian dan menghargai nasabah, rekan kerja, masyarakat, investor dan regulator.

## Excellence
Kami memberikan layanan prima kepada nasabah dan memicu kinerja yang prima dalam pekerjaan sehari-hari.

# PERMATABANKERS WORK CULTURE

## Partnership
We understand each other and build strong relationships based on mutual respect internally and externally.

## Responsiveness
We work quickly, accurately, and effectively to deliver prompt service.

## Innovation
We continuously think out of the box to improve the way we work, making it simpler, better and faster.

## Caring
We are attentive and respect our customers, colleagues, communities, investors and regulators.

## Excellence
We deliver excellent performance to customers and drive excellent performances in day to day job.
