# ASOSIASI G4-15 G4-16

PermataBank turut aktif dalam berbagai asosiasi di sektor industri perbankan dan jasa keuangan. Kami terdaftar sebagai anggota dan turut berperan aktif dalam berbagai diskusi yang membantu pemerintah dan masyarakat untuk menciptakan jasa keuangan dan perbankan yang dapat memberikan manfaat luas bagi para pemangku kepentingan. Asosiasi-asosiasi tersebut antara lain adalah:

1. Asosiasi Emiten Indonesia (AEI);
2. Perhimpunan Bank Nasional (Perbanas);
3. Indonesian Corporate Secretary Association (ICSA).

# ASSOCIATION G4-15 G4-16

PermataBank is an active part in several banking and financial services associations. PermataBank is registered as member of these associations and is actively involved in various discussions aimed to help the government and the society creating financial and banking services that would generate the greatest benefit for all stakeholders. The associations are:

1. Indonesian Listed Companies Association (AEI);
2. National Banking Association (Perbanas);
3. Indonesian Corporate Secretary Association (ICSA).
