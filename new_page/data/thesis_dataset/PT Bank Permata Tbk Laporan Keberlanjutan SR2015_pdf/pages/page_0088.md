PERMATAHATI UNTUK MASYARAKAT
PermataHati for The Community

Pada tahun 2015, PermataHati melakukan program pelatihan Guruku Permataku bagi 1.300 guru yang tersebar di seluruh Indonesia bersinergi dengan:

- UPZ (Unit Pelayanan Zakat) PermataBank Syariah, terdapat 400 guru PAUD/TK-SD di Jakarta, Tangerang, dan Solo, serta 300 guru SMP-SMA di Bandung.
- FIFGroup dan AAB, sebagai bagian dari financial services Grup Astra, bersinergi dalam mengadakan pelatihan kepada 600 guru SMP-SMA di Lubukpakam, Mataram dan Palembang.

In 2015, PermataHati organized Guruku Permataku training program, which covered 1,300 teachers across Indonesia. The program was held in synergy with:

- UPZ (Zakat Service Unit) of PermataBank Syariah. As many as 400 early education, kindergarten, and elementary school teachers in Jakarta, Tangerang, and Solo participated as well as 300 junior high and senior high school teachers in Bandung.
- FIFGroup and AAB, as part of financial services Astra Group in synergy held training for 600 junior and senior high school teachers in Lubukpakam, Mataram, and Palembang.
