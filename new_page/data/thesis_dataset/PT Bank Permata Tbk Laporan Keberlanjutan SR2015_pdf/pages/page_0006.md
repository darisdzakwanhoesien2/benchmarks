Embedding the 3Ps principle (Profit, People, Planet) across all operational activities is important in order to achieve PermataBank business sustainability.

Through this principle and its programs, PermataBank aims to giving back to the communities. We are not solely focused on generating profit, but are also responsible to the ecosystem, which comprises communities (people) and environment (planet), where PermataBank is present. The sustainability aspect is no longer become the goal but also became the tool in fulfilling our responsibility, today and in the future.

As an entity in the financial services industry, we believe that having strong relationship with the communities is paramount in ensuring sustainable business growth.
