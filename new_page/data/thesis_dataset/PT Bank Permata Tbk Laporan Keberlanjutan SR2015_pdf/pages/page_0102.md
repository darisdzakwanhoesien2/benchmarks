This program is supported by Putera Sampoerna Foundation and several other corporations. This year marked the fifth UFE. Presenting a different theme of “Goodness Ville”, this year’s UFE invited families to have a picnic and together contributed to the education sector through exciting traditional and educational games. Participants of UFE 2015 “Goodness Ville” could directly contribute to education, where their donation was channeled to “Save a Teen” program for disadvantaged students who otherwise would join the dropout rate. These students would continue their education in the Akademi Siswa Bangsa Internasional (ASBI).
