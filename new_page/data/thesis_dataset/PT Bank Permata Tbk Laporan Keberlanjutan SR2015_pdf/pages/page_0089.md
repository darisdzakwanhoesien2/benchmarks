PermataHati for The Community

# PENGEMBANGAN SARANA DAN PRASARANA

Pilar kedua PermataHati, yaitu Facility Enhancement (Pengembangan Sarana dan Prasarana), bertujuan untuk meningkatkan fasilitas dalam mendukung proses belajar dan mengajar. Perbaikan fasilitas sekolah tidak hanya terkait dengan kegiatan akademik, tapi juga non-akademik serta fasilitas umum yang ada di sekolah. Perbaikan fasilitas akademik seperti laboratorium komputer dan perpustakaan, sedangkan fasilitas non akademik seperti arena kesenian, sanitasi, fasilitas olah raga, dan fasilitas umum seperti kantin, koperasi serta sarana pendukung lainnya.

# FACILITY ENHANCEMENT

The second pillar of PermataHati is Facility Enhancement, which goal is to improve learning facilities not only in the classrooms, but also non-academic facilities and other facilities around the school environment. Academic facility enhancement covers, among other, computer lab and library, while non-academic facilities covers sports, arts, sanitation, sport facilities and the general facilities such as cafeteria, cooperative unit and so on.
