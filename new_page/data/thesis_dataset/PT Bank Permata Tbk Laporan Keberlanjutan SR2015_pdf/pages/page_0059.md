## Sistem Pengendalian Internal G4-14

PermataBank memiliki internal audit yang berfungsi untuk memberikan assurance yang independen terhadap efektivitas atas proses pengendalian risiko. Internal Audit bertanggung jawab terhadap proses pengendalian risiko pada aktivitas bisnis (lini pertama) dan proses yang dikelola oleh Petugas Pengendali Risiko (lini kedua) sesuai dengan persyaratan yang telah ditetapkan pada Kerangka Kerja Manajemen Risiko.

- Delegated Risk Authority Framework ensures that decision-making concerning main risks can only be taken by certain individuals or committees who are equipped with the necessary competence, judgment, and perspectives to ensure that PermataBank's control standard and risk-returns objectives are met.

Complete report of PermataBank's risk management is available in Corporate Governance Chapter of PermataBank's Annual Report 2015.

## Internal Control System G4-14

PermataBank has internal audit unit that works to provide independent assurance regarding risk control effectiveness. Internal Audit unit is responsible for risk control process that takes place within business activities (first line) and the process that is carried out by Risk Control Officers (second line), in accordance with the requirements established under Risk Management Framework.
