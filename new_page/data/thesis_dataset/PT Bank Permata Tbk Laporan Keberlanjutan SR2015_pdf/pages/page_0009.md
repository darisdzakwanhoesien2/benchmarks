# TENTANG LAPORAN INI

About This Report

Dalam operasional kami sehari-hari, PermataBank turut dibantu oleh para pemasok barang maupun jasa (outsourcing). Hal ini mendorong PermataBank untuk melaksanakan proses seleksi dan evaluasi para pemasok barang dan jasa khususnya para pemasok tenaga kerja diantaranya dalam bidang security, sales and promotion, transportasi dan cleaning service. Proses seleksi dan evaluasi tersebut dilakukan berdasarkan kesadaran kami bahwa kinerja mereka turut mempengaruhi reputasi dan nama baik PermataBank, maka dari itu laporan keberlanjutan kami juga mencakup kebijakan para pemasok. 64-71

In the daily operations, PermataBank engages suppliers of goods and services (outsource). For that purpose, PermataBank has set up selection and evaluation process for suppliers, especially manpower suppliers in the areas of security, sales and promotion, transportation, and cleaning services. The selection and evaluation measures are carried out as PermataBank realizes that the performance of the suppliers affect PermataBank's reputation and name. Our Sustainability Report also entails a section that explains our supplier policy. 64-71

# PEDOMAN DAN STANDAR PELAPORAN

Laporan ini disusun mengikuti panduan internasional laporan keberlanjutan Global Reporting Initiatives (GRI) dengan kriteria G4 CORE OPTION berikut pilihan indikator khusus sektor keuangan sesuai dengan Financial Service Sector Supplement – Global Reporting Initiatives.

Laporan keberlanjutan PermataBank mencantumkan indikator GRI G4 disetiap bagian laporan untuk memudahkan para pembaca memahami relevansi isi laporan dengan indikator GRI G4.

# GUIDELINES AND REPORTING STANDARD

This report conforms with the international guideline for sustainable report, the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI). We follow the G4 CORE OPTION and include a number of specific indicators for financial sector in accordance with Financial Service Sector Supplement – Global Reporting Initiatives.

PermataBank Sustainability Report cites the GRI G4 indicator in each of the report's section so that readers can easily identify the relevance between the report's content and GRI G4 indicators.
