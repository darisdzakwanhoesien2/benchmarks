Health, Safety, and Environment Management

## PROGRAM PENANAMAN POHON

Sebagai bagian dari keluarga Grup Astra, PermataBank turut berpartisipasi dalam program penanaman 1.300 pohon di Sentul Eco Edu Tourism Forest, Bogor. Program ini merupakan bentuk perwujudan komitmen PermataBank di bidang lingkungan berupa partisipasi aktif dalam konservasi lingkungan melalui gerakan penanaman pohon di Astra Forest dan program edukasi lingkungan bagi anak-anak. Acara peresmian penanaman pohon dilakukan di Gedung Tower III Bintaro pada tanggal 7 November 2015 yang melibatkan direksi, manajemen senior, serta putra-putri karyawan PermataBank bersamaan dengan acara Permata Family Fun Walk &amp; Run. 64 (2015)

## PERMATAGREEN MOVEMENT

Dalam sebuah kegiatan keberlanjutan, karyawan menjadi faktor penentu keberhasilan berbagai inisiatif lingkungan sebuah perusahaan. PermataBank telah berusaha mewujudkan efisiensi dan kepedulian lingkungan yang dilakukan secara konsisten di lingkungan PermataBank. PermataBank juga memfasilitasi PermataBankers untuk melakukan kegiatan dalam menciptakan lingkungan yang lebih dan lestari.

PermataGreen Movement merupakan acara tahunan yang diselenggarakan PermataBank untuk mewujudkan komitmen yang tinggi di bidang lingkungan berupa partisipasi aktif dalam konservasi lingkungan melalui gerakan penanaman pohon.

## TREE PLANTING

As part of Astra Group family, PermataBank participated in planting 1,300 trees in Sentul Eco Edu Tourism Forest, Bogor. This program was part of PermataBank's environmental commitment; PermataBank is determined to actively take part in environmental conservation through tree planting action in Astra Forest as well as by giving environmental education to young children. Tree planting ceremony was held at Tower III Bintaro on 7 November 2015, attended by Board of Directors, senior management, and children of PermataBankers. The event was held in conjunction with Permata Family Fun Walk &amp; Run. 64 (2015)

## PERMATAGREEN MOVEMENT

In any sustainability movement, employees play a pivotal role that determines the success of the company's environmental initiatives. PermataBank continually endeavors to realize efficiency and environmental awareness, and facilitates PermataBankers to partake in activities that will contribute to a better environment.

PermataGreen Movement is an annual event organized by PermataBank. It realizes the Bank's strong commitment to the environment, in which the Bank demonstrates active participation in tree planting program as part of environment conservation efforts.

Ceremony of PermataGreen Movement in 2015 took place at Tower III Bintaro, attended by Board of Directors, senior management, and children of PermataBankers. Representative from PT Astra International Tbk was also present in this occasion. The ceremony was a symbolic handover of trees from President Director of PermataBank to PT Astra International Tbk.
