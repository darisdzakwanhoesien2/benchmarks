A number of photo journalists with international reputation, such as Sasa Kralj (Croatia) and Kadir van Lohuizen, co-founder NOOR photo agency, as well as the Advisory Board World Press Photo extended their full support to PPG. PPG also had resident facilitators from PannaFoto Institute, certified by World Press Photo, such as Edy Purnomo and Ahmad 'Deny' Salman.

At the end of the program, one photo journalist received Permata Photojournalist Grant- Erasmus Huis Fellowship to Amsterdam 2015 as PPG's best graduate. Under the fellowship, the grantee joined NOOR Photo Agency and attended the World Press Photo Awards Days in Amsterdam.
