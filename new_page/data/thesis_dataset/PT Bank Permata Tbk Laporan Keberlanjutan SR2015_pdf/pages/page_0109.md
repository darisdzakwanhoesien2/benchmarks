# THE PERMATABANKERS

The PermataBankers

Selama tahun 2015, penanaman nilai-nilai dan budaya kerja PermataBank terus dilakukan melalui program Orientasi Karyawan Baru, berbagai program pengembangan kepemimpinan dan coaching, serta aplikasinya dimasukkan ke dalam modul-modul teknis yang telah diperbaharui seperti Program Foundation &amp; Incubation (untuk RM/P, CS-Teller, RM SME, RO), RM Acceleration Program, Enhancement / Maintenance Program, Need Based Learning Program untuk meningkatkan kinerja, dan Certification Program yang terdiri External, Internal &amp; Professional Certification. M.SO.

The efforts to inculcate PermataBank's values and work culture continued throughout 2015 through New Employee Orientation Program and various leadership development and coaching programs. Practical examples have been incorporated in updated technical modules for programs such as Foundation and Incubation (RM/P, CS-Teller, RM SME, RO), RM Acceleration, Enhancement/ Maintenance, Need Based Learning to improve performance, and Certification Program that consists of External, Internal, and Professional Certification. M.SO.

# C. PROFIL DAN DISTRIBUSI KARYAWAN

Hingga akhir tahun 2015, karyawan PermataBank berjumlah 9.038 orang, terdiri dari 7.854 karyawan tetap dan 1.184 karyawan kontrak (termasuk Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris) yang tersebar di 62 kota di seluruh Indonesia. G4-10

# C. EMPLOYEE PROFILE AND DISTRIBUTION

As at the end of 2015, PermataBank had a total of 9,038 employees. This included 7,854 permanent employees and 1,184 contract employees (including the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners) who work in 62 cities throughout Indonesia. G4-10
