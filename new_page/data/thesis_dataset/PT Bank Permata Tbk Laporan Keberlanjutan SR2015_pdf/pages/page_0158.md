PermataBank melayani nasabah institusi, dari korporasi berskala menengah hingga besar melalui Direktorat Wholesale Banking. PermataBank senantiasa berusaha untuk memahami bisnis nasabah secara menyeluruh melalui pendekatan total relationship. Dengan begitu, PermataBank akan memberikan solusi inovatif multi-produk yang disesuaikan dengan kondisi dan kebutuhan khusus nasabah dengan mendayagunakan sinergi dan kerja sama antar unit bisnis maupun divisi di Wholesale Banking PermataBank.

Di samping itu, kami juga memiliki Retail Banking PermataBank yang memfokuskan untuk memberikan layanan finansial yang menyeluruh dan terbaik untuk pemenuhan kebutuhan nasabah individu dan keluarga kelas menengah dan atas (mass and mass affluent), serta nasabah di sektor UKM (Usaha Kecil dan Menengah) atau biasa dengan SME (Small &amp; Medium Enterprise). Desain produk dan layanan yang kami sediakan menggunakan pendekatan yang berpusat pada nasabah (customer-centric) dengan target geografi berpusat pada kota-kota besar dan menengah di Indonesia.

Untuk meningkatkan layanan bagi nasabah ritel, PermataBank memberikan berbagai kemudahan agar transaksi nasabah menjadi lebih efektif dan efisien. Salah satunya adalah melalui penggunaan aplikasi elektronik. PermataBank memiliki layanan Permata e-Form yang dapat diakses dari perangkat iPad yang telah tersedia di cabang-cabang tertentu maupun dari laptop atau tablet milik nasabah. Permata e-Form merupakan produk inovatif pertama di Indonesia saat diperkenalkan pada tahun 2012. Layanan Permata e-Form terus dikembangkan dan saat ini telah dapat menangani transaksi pembukaan rekening, setoran, transfer serta formulas aplikasi untuk fasilitas KPR dan KTA.

As a business entity in the financial services sector, our bond with customers is built through our services that are inclusive for all.

PermataBank serves customers from institutional sector, medium to large scale corporates that are catered by our Wholesale Banking Directorate. We want to always understand the business of our customers through our total relationship approach. That way, PermataBank is able to offer innovative and comprehensive solutions that are tailored to a customer's unique condition and needs. To that end, we tap into the synergy and collaboration between business units and divisions in PermataBank Wholesale Banking.

PermataBank also has Retail Banking directorate that focuses on all-round and best financial services for individual as well as mass and mass affluent segment, including customers from small and medium enterprise (SME) sector. Our products and services are developed using customer-centric approach, where geographically we target major and middle cities in Indonesia.

To enhance services for retail customers, PermataBank offers multiple platforms to ensure that customers can enjoy effective and efficient transaction processes. One of these platforms is electronic application, where PermataBank has prepared Permata e-Form that is accessible from mobile device such as iPad, which is available at certain branch offices, or from customers' personal laptop or tablet. When launched in 2012, Permata e-Form was the first of its kind in Indonesia. This service has been continually developed and its current features include account opening, money deposit, money transfer, and loan application for home and unsecured loans.
