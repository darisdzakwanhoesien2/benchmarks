# PARTISIPASI EARTH HOUR 2015

Earth Hour merupakan gerakan perlindungan bumi yang dijalankan oleh WWF pada tahun 2007 dan kini telah diikuti lebih dari 7.000 kota di seluruh dunia. Sebagai bentuk dukungan dan komitmen dalam mewujudkan lingkungan yang sehat dan keberlanjutan, PermataBank berpartisipasi dalam Gerakan Earth Hour 2015. PermataBank turut mematikan lampu penerangan di internal dan eksternal seluruh kantor cabang PermataBank pada 28 Maret 2015 pada pukul 20.30 –21.30. Imbauan juga disampaikan kepada seluruh karyawan untuk berpartisipasi Earth Hour di rumah masing-masing melalui email, poster, wallpaper di PC, dan notifikasi/pengingat ke telepon seluler karyawan yang menerima fasilitas Blackberry. 04-23127

# PERMATABANK, SUPPORTING MOTHER EARTH FROM THE HEART

Various activities took place in 2015. They were carried out to inspire care for nature and to promote efforts around environmental sustainability for the sake of our planet.

# EARTH HOUR 2015

The Earth Hour environmental movement was initiated by WWF in 2007. By now, more than 7,000 cities around the world have participated. To show the support and commitment to realizing sustainable environment, PermataBank took part in Earth Hour 2015 by turning off internal and external lightings of all of its branch offices on 28 March 2013 from 20.30 to 21.30. PermataBank also voiced this movement to to employees and encouraged them to participate in Earth Hour at home. This campaign was communicated using email, posters, PC wallpaper, dan notification/reminder to employees with Blackberry facility. 04-23127
