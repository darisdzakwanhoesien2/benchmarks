# STRUKTUR GCG
Struktur GCG di PermataBank untuk mendukung penerapan GCG terdiri atas komponen-komponen sebagai berikut: G4-34

- RUPS
- Dewan Komisaris
- Direksi
- Komite Audit
- Komite Pemantau Risiko
- Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi
- Audit Internal
- Komite-komite yang berada di bawah fungsi Direksi

# GCG STRUCTURE
PermataBank's GCG structure that is designed to support GCG implementation consists of the following components: G4-34

- General Meeting of Shareholders
- Board of Commissioners
- Board of Directors
- Audit Committee
- Risk Monitoring Committee
- Remuneration and Nomination Committee
- Internal Audit
- Committees under the Board of Directors
