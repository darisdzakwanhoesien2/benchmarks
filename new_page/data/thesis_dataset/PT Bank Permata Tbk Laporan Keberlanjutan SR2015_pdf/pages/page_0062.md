# PENCEGAHAN DAN PENGELOLAAN FRAUD

Pencegahan dan pengelolaan fraud di PermataBank dilaksanakan dibawah kendali Komite Pengawasan Fraud (FOC) yang mempunyai tugas untuk memastikan penanganan permasalahan atau fraud secara tepat dan memastikan penerapan kebijakan-kebijakan yang ada dapat dilaksanakan secara efektif. Keanggotaan FOC terdiri atas Direktur Utama, Wakil Direktur Utama, Direktur Kepatuhan, Direktur Sumber Daya Manusia, Direktur Risiko, dan Head Financial Crime Risk sebagai sekretaris komite.

Berbagai upaya dan program yang bertujuan untuk meminimalisasi terjadi internal fraud diantaranya adalah:

G4-14 G4-SO4

- Menerbitkan Fraud Alert, yakni tulisan melalui email/ surel yang menginformasikan kepada seluruh karyawan tren/ modus kejahatan fraud yang berkembang dengan tujuan membangun kepedulian dan kesadaran terhadap risiko fraud.
- Mengelola program E-learning Fraud, yakni model pelatihan/ pembelajaran fraud awareness yang dikemas dalam bentuk animasi komputer disertai soal-soal pertanyaan, yang wajib diikuti oleh seluruh karyawan Bank Permata. Sejak diluncurkan di April 2011 sampai dengan akhir Desember 2015 jumlah karyawan yang telah mengikuti e-learning sebanyak 7.622 karyawan (93% dari total seluruh karyawan). Presentase karyawan yang belum mengikuti e-learning adalah karyawan baru, dengan kewajiban menyelesaikan e-learning dalam 90 hari sejak tanggal masuk kerja.
- Menyelenggarakan pelatihan pengenalan fraud bagi karyawan baru (induction training).
- Kebijakan blokir internal rekening giro dan tabungan nasabah terindikasi fraud untuk mencegah penggunaan rekening untuk menampung hasil kejahatan.

# FRAUD PREVENTION AND MITIGATION

Fraud prevention and mitigation activities in PermataBank are carried out under the leadership of Fraud Oversight Committee (FOC). FOC is responsible to ensure that appropriate measures have been taken to address fraud and that existing policies are properly enforced. FOC comprises President Director, Vice President Director, Compliance Director, Human Resources Director, Risk Director, and Head Financial Crime Risk as the committee secretary.

Measures and programs designed to minimize internal fraud cases among others are:

G4-14 G4-SO4

- Publish Fraud Alert in writing, through email to inform all employees the up dated trend and modus of fraud, in purpose to build concern and awareness toward risk of fraud.
- Provide Fraud E-learning program, which is a fraud awareness training/learning model which must be followed by all employees of Bank Permata. The program is available in the form of computer animation equipped with questions. Since launched in April 2011 until end of December 2015, total participants has reached as many as 7.622 employees (93% of the total employees). The rest percentage is from new employees, with the obligation to complete the e-learning in 90 working days from the first working date.
- Conduct training fraud recognition for new employees (induction training).
- Internal policy to block current and savings account of the customer indicated to commit fraud to prevent from misuse of bank account to keep proceeds crime.
