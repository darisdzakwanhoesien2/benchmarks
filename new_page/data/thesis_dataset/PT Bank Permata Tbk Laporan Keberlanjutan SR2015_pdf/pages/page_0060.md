Tata Kelola untuk Keberlanjutan

- Setiap karyawan baru selalu menerima buku Kode Etik Perilaku Karyawan PermataBank. Karyawan baru harus menandatangani formulir pernyataan telah membaca serta berjanji untuk mematuhi kode etik tersebut.
- Kode etik PermataBank menjadi salah satu bagian dari "Right Start", suatu buku panduan 90 hari pertama bekerja di PermataBank yang dibagikan kepada karyawan baru di hari pertama mereka bergabung.
- Seluruh karyawan wajib mengikuti e-learning mengenai kode etik yang harus diperbaharui setiap tahun.

- All new employees receive PermataBank Code of Conduct book. New employees need to sign a statement form verifying that they have read and pledged their commitment to comply with the book's content.
- PermataBank's Code of Conduct is included as a part of the "Right Start", a guidebook for the first 90 days joining PermataBank. This book is distributed to employees on their first day of work.
- E-learning program on code of conduct is mandatory for all employees and they need to refresh their participation annually.

# SISTEM WHISTLEBLOWING (SPEAK-UP)

## Keberadaan Speak-Up

Untuk mendukung terciptanya Good Corporate Governance di PermataBank, khususnya menciptakan budaya pengelolaan risiko, selain memiliki perangkat sistem dan prosedur, dibutuhkan partisipasi aktif seluruh karyawan dalam bentuk tindakan nyata untuk menjaga lingkungan kerja yang bebas dari tindakan fraud.

Whistleblowing System di PermataBank dikenal dengan nama Speak-Up yang telah diperkenalkan sejak tahun 2008, merupakan sarana bagi seluruh karyawan untuk melaporkan pelanggaran-pelanggaran dan malpraktik, termasuk kecurigaan atas tindakan tersebut.

## Mekanisme Speak-Up

Setiap karyawan PermataBank memiliki hak dan tanggung jawab untuk melaporkan setiap indikasi pelanggaran dan diwajibkan menyampaikan sesegera mungkin adanya tindakan pelanggaran yang dilakukan oleh karyawan lain kepada manajemen PermataBank melalui saluran Speak-Up yang telah disediakan.

# WHISTLEBLOWING SYSTEM (SPEAK-UP)

## Speak-Up

To further GCG practices in PermataBank, especially concerning risk management culture, active participation from all employees is also required. Beyond having the necessary systems and procedures in place, real participation will contribute to safeguard work environment from fraudulence.

Whistleblowing System in PermataBank, known as Speak Up, has been introduced since 2008. This is a platform for employees to report violations or improper conduct, including suspicions thereof.

## Speak Up Mechanism

Every employee at PermataBank has the right and responsibility to report every indication of violation. They are required to report immediately violations committed by other employees to PermataBank's management through available Speak-Up channels.
