Pengelolaan Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja serta Lingkungan (K3L) ini dijalankan penuh waktu oleh departemen khusus di bawah Direktorat Teknologi dan Operasi dengan total HSE Officer sebanyak 11 orang yang mengelola dan mengorganisasi jalannya program K3L di seluruh kantor PermataBank. G4-LA3

Dua pemegang saham utama PermataBank, PT Astra International Tbk dan Standard Chartered Bank, telah memberikan arahan dan kriteria untuk melakukan pengelolaan aspek lingkungan serta kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja yang komprehensif untuk operasi perbankan. PT Astra International Tbk yang berpengalaman dalam mengelola dampak lingkungan di industri otomotif dan Standard Chartered Bank yang berpengalaman dalam mengelola dampak lingkungan di industri perbankan secara global, telah menjadi kelebihan tersendiri bagi PermataBank. Persyaratan dan pengalaman keduanya telah kami adopsi untuk menjadi kebijakan yang berlaku di seluruh kantor PermataBank di Indonesia.

The benefit of comprehensive OHS management is two-fold; first, it ensures the sense of safety among employees and secondly, at the same time, the Bank also manages its own operational risk.

Health, Safety and Environment (HSE) management is carried out by a full-time, dedicated unit under the Directorate of Technology and Operations. In total, there are 11 HSE Officers who manage and execute HSE programs throughout all PermataBank's offices.

G4-LA5

PermataBank's two main shareholders, PT Astra International Tbk and Standard Chartered Bank, provide direction and criteria of comprehensive HSE management for banking operations. The long-standing experience of PT Astra International Tbk in managing environmental impacts in the auto industry, and Standard Chartered Bank in the global banking environment, are advantageous for PermataBank. Requirements that both owners adhere to and their experiences have been adopted by PermataBank as policies that are applicable for all PermataBank's offices in Indonesia.
