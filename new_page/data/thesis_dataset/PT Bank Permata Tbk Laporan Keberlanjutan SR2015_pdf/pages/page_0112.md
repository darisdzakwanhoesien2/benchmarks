# D. PEREKRUTAN TALENTA BARU

Strategi Sumber Daya Manusia (SDM) PermataBank adalah meningkatkan kemampuan organisasi melalui peningkatan kemampuan para manajer dalam mengelola sumber daya manusia, mengembangkan talenta, mengelola dan memperbaiki proses kerja yang pada gilirannya akan berdampak pada kinerja PermataBank secara keseluruhan. Tantangan utama keterbatasan SDM perbankan yang handal menyebabkan tingginya turnover karyawan.

Oleh karena itu, PermataBank konsisten melakukan kaderisasi melalui perekrutan dan penyelenggaraan pengembangan kader melalui fresh graduate program. Selama tahun 2015, Permatabank telah merekrut 84 lulusan baru untuk dikembangkan menjadi ahli perbankan melalui:

- Banking Associate Program (BAP)
- Graduate Management Associate Program (GMAP)
- Class Program (CS/Teller dan Mortgage Sales Specialist)

Selain itu, PermataBank melaksanakan pengelolaan talenta secara terstruktur dan sistematis.

# D. RECRUITING NEW TALENTS

PermataBank's Human Resource (HR) strategy is to enhance organizational capabilities by bringing up the capacities of managers in managing human resource, developing talents, and managing and improving work processes – which, eventually, will impact the overall performance of PermataBank. However, with skilled banking human resource in high demand, employee turnover rate can be a challenge.

To address that, PermataBank consistently refreshes its pool of talents by recruiting and carrying out talent development through fresh graduate program. In 2015, PermataBank hired a total of 84 fresh graduates. They would be trained as bankers through:

- Banking Associate Program (BAP)
- Graduate Management Associate Program (GMAP)
- Class Program (CS/Teller and Mortgage Sales Specialist)

In addition to that, PermataBank also manages structured and systematic talent management programs.
