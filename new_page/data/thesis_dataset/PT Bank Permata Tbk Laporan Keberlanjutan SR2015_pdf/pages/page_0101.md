Unite for Education (UFE) merupakan gerakan partisipatif untuk menyuarakan kepedulian tentang isu-isu pendidikan di Indonesia. UFE didirikan sejak 2011, didalamnya terdapat 3.000-4.000 insan peduli pendidikan dan ikut serta dalam penyelenggaraan kegiatan olahraga yang sarat informasi pendidikan, olahraga, dan hiburan.

Unite for Education (UFE) is a participatory movement to raise awareness on educational issues in Indonesia. Established in 2011, UFE accommodates around 3,000 to 4,000 individuals who care about education and participate in sports activities rich with educational information as well as entertainment.

![img-111.jpeg](img-111.jpeg)
