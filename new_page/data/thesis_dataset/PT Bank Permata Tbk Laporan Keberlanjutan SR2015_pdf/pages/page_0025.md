# JEJAK LANGKAH

## 1955
- PT Bank Bali Tbk mulai beroperasi secara komersial sebagai bank swasta di Indonesia.
- PT Bank Bali Tbk commenced its commercial operations as a private bank in Indonesia.

## 1990
- Penawaran umum perdana saham di Bursa Efek Jakarta dan Bursa Efek Surabaya.
- Initial public offering in the Jakarta Stock Exchange and Surabaya Stock Exchange.

## 2000
- Badan Penyehatan Perbankan Nasional (BPPN) merekapitalisasi PT Bank Bali Tbk, dan Pemerintah RI menjadi pemegang saham utama dengan kepemilikan 59,3%.
- Indonesia Banking Restructuring Agency (IBRA) recapitalized PT Bank Bali Tbk, and the Government of Indonesia became the main shareholder with a 59.3% ownership.

## 2002
- PT Bank Universal Tbk, PT Bank Patriot, PT Bank Prima Express, PT Bank Artamedia dan PT Bank Bali Tbk bergabung menjadi PT Bank Permata Tbk.
- Merger of PT Bank Universal Tbk, PT Bank Patriot, PT Bank Prima Express, PT Bank Artamedia and PT Bank Bali Tbk forming PT Bank Permata Tbk.

## 2004
- Penggabungan nilai nominal saham.
- Divestasi 51,72% saham Pemerintah RI kepada Standard Chartered Bank (SCB) dan PT Astra International Tbk (AI), masing-masing 25,86%. Pada akhir tahun, kepemilikan SCB dan AI meningkat menjadi 63,1%, atau masing-masing 31,55%.
- Reverse stock split.
- The Government divested 51.72% to Standard Chartered Bank (SCB), and PT Astra International Tbk (AI), 25.86% respectively. At the end of the year, combined SCB and AI ownership increased to 63.1%, or 31.55% each, respectively.

## 2006
- SCB dan AI meningkatkan kepemilikan menjadi 89,01%.
- Penawaran Obligasi Subordinasi I kepada publik sebesar Rp500 miliar dengan tenor 10 tahun dan opsi beli setelah tahun ke-5 (10NCS). Didaftarkan di Bursa Efek Jakarta.
- SCB and AI increased their combined ownership to 89.01%.
- Public Offering of Subordinated Bonds I of Rp500 billion with 10-years tenor and buyback option after five years (10NCS). Listed on Jakarta Stock Exchange.

## 2009
- Penerbitan Obligasi Subordinasi sebesar US$100 juta dengan tenor 12 tahun dan opsi beli setelah tahun ke-7 (12NC7) melalui mekanisme private placement. PT Astra International Tbk dan Standard Chartered Bank bertindak sebagai pembeli siaga.
- Issuance of Subordinated Bonds of US$100 million with a tenor of 12 years and buyback option after seven years (12NC7) through a private placement mechanism. PT Astra International Tbk and Standard Chartered Bank acted as standby buyers.

## 2010
- Penerbitan Obligasi Subordinasi sebesar Rp700 miliar dengan tenor 10 tahun melalui mekanisme private placement, Standard Chartered Bank bertindak sebagai pembeli tunggal.
- Penawaran Umum Terbatas IV dengan Hak Memesan Efek Terlebih Dahulu sebesar Rp2 triliun. Standard Chartered Bank dan PT Astra International Tbk meningkatkan sahamnya sekaligus bertindak selaku pembeli siaga. Kepemilikan bersama Standard Chartered Bank dan PT Astra International Tbk meningkat menjadi 89,03%, atau masing-masing 44,515%.
- Akusisi PT GE Finance Indonesia.
- Untuk pertama kalinya mencapai angka Rp1 triliun untuk laba bersih.
- Issuance of Subordinated Bonds amounting to Rp700 billion with a tenor of 10 years through a private placement mechanism. Standard Chartered Bank as the single buyer.
- Rights Issue IV amounting Rp2 trillion. Standard Chartered Bank and PT Astra International Tbk took up their share and acted as the standby buyers. The ownership of Standard Chartered Bank and PT Astra International Tbk jointly increased to 89.03%, or 44.515% respectively.
- Acquisition of PT GE Finance Indonesia.
- For the first time reached Rp1 trillion in terms of net profit.
