# KOMITMEN PERMATABANK TERHADAP PEMANGKU KEPENTINGAN

PermataBank senantiasa memperoleh masukan yang berharga dari setiap pemangku kepentingan untuk meningkatkan kinerja layanan, mengembangkan produk, pengelolaan risiko maupun untuk meningkatkan reputasi PermataBank. Bagi kami, pelibatan seluruh pemangku kepentingan dalam menjalankan kegiatan operasional PermataBank adalah salah satu kunci keberlanjutan perusahaan.

Terdapat lima pemangku kepentingan PermataBank yang kami identifikasi berdasarkan potensi dampak operasional kami terhadap mereka dan mereka terhadap operasional, kinerja dan reputasi PermataBank. Selain itu kami juga turut mempertimbangkan prinsip-prinsip penentuan pemangku kepentingan dari panduan Global Reporting Initiative GRI G4, yakni: Responsibility, Influence, Dependency, Proximity, dan Representation.

# PERMATABANK'S COMMITMENT TO STAKEHOLDERS

PermataBank continuously receives valuable input from all stakeholders to improve service performance, develop products, manage risks, and to enhance PermataBank's reputation. For PermataBank, stakeholder engagement in its operations is one of the keys to sustainability.

PermataBank has identified five stakeholder groups based on the potential of PermataBank's operational impacts to these groups and, conversely, the groups' impacts to PermataBank's operations, performance, and reputation. In addition, PermataBank also considers GRI G4 principles in determining stakeholder groups, namely Responsibility, Influence, Dependency, Proximity, and Representation. G4.25
