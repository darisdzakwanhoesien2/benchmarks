# KETERLIBATAN DAN ANTUSIASME EMPLOYEE VOLUNTEER (EVO) HASILKAN COMMUNITY CENTER DI CIKALONG WETAN

The creation of Community Center in Cikalong Wetan as the result of Employee Volunteer (EVO) Engagement and Enthusiasm

G4-ECZ G4-SO1

Awal tahun 2015, PermataHati memulai kampanye CSR yang baru yaitu "All You Need is Love" yang bertujuan untuk merangkul lebih banyak lagi Employee Volunteer (EVO) PermataBank agar terlibat aktif dalam melakukan kegiatan sosial.

Kampanye ini ditandai dengan gerakan partisipatif seluruh karyawan PermataBank yang dihimbau untuk mengenakan kaos volunteer PermataHati pada satu hari yang sama, yaitu tanggal 13 Februari 2015. Gerakan ini dimulai dengan tantangan "I Will If Challenge" – yaitu apabila minimal 500 karyawan PermataBank menggunakan kaos volunteer PermataHati, maka jajaran Direksi dan senior leader PermataBank memberikan donasi guna pembangunan Community Center di Desa Mandala Mukti, Kampung Pangheotan, RT 01, RW 016, Kec. Cikalong Wetan, Kab. Bandung Barat.

Kampung Pangheotan ini berada di perbukitan, berbatasan langsung dengan hutan Negara. Bangunan terdahulu tempat masyarakat dan anak-anak PAUD serta Madrasah beraktifitas sudah tidak dapat digunakan lagi, dindingnya sudah rapuh dan retak-retak, bahkan sudah tidak layak untuk direnovasi. Selain itu, kondisi tanah tempat bangunan lama berdiri pun sangat mengkhawatirkan untuk dibangun dengan bangunan baru karena berada di tanah yang miring dan rawan longsor.

Beranjak dari permasalahan inilah PermataHati bersamasama dengan seluruh karyawan PermataBank saling bahu membahu melakukan serangkaian kegiatan CSR dan berdonasi melalui kampanye All You Need is Love. Tantangan "I will IF Challenge" di tanggal 13

In early 2015, PermataHati begun the new CSR campaign, namely 'All You Need is Love' that aims to invite more Employee Volunteer (EVO) of PermataBank to actively engage in social activity.

The campaign was marked by participative movement of all PermataBank employees that are invited to use PermataHati volunteer shirts in one day, on 13 February 2015. The movement was commenced with the challenge program "I Will If Challenge" – which was if a minimum of 500 PermataBank employees are using PermataHati volunteer shirt, then the Board of Directors and senior leaders of PermataBank will extend donation for the construction of Community Center in Mandala Mukti Village, Pangheaton District, RT 01, RW016, Sub-District Cikalong Wetan, West Bandung Regency.

Pangheotan village is located on the hill, directly adjacent to the border of the State forest. The previous establishment for the community and early childhood children including prayer facility were damaged, the walls are brittled and cracked and cannot be renovated. Moreover, the soil condition on which the building were constructed is not feasible for a new construction as it is located on sloping ground and prone to landslides.
