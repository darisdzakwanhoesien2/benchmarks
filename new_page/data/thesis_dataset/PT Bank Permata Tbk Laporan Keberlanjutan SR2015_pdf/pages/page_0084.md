# FINANCIAL LITERACY THROUGH SPORTS (FITS) BERSAMA EVO DAN KELUARGA

Financial Literacy Through Sports (FITS) With Your Family

Setiap hari Sabtu selama bulan April dan Mei 2015, para EVO beserta keluarganya secara rutin ikut terlibat dalam program Financial Literacy Through Sports (FITS) yang berperan sebagai facilitator dalam mendampingi para peserta untuk belajar di dalam kelas (indoor) maupun coaching clinic (outdoor). Keterlibatan mereka membuat program FITS ini menjadi semakin seru, karena anak-anak kampung Dadap Serpong dapat bersosialisasi dan berinteraksi secara langsung dengan anak-anak EVO yang ikut bermain sambil belajar.

Kegiatan ini tidak hanya bermanfaat bagi para pesertanya, tapi juga memberi arti tersendiri dan pengalaman yang tak terlupakan bagi EVO dan anggota keluarganya.

Every Saturday in April and May 2015, several EVOs with their family routinely involved in Financial Literacy Through Sports (FITS) program and took the role as facilitators in accompanying the participants to learn indoor or outdoor (coaching clinic). Their involvement sparked excitement in FITS event, especially since the children of Dadap Serpong village were able to interact with children of the EVOs who came with their parents to learn and to play.

This program not only benefit the participants, but also gives its own meaning and unforgettable experience to EVO and their family.
