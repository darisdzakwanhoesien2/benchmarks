# PERMATA STUDENT ENTREPRENEURSHIP PROGRAM (PSEP):
JOB SHADOW DAY 2015
75 ANAK SMK MELIHAT 'DAPUR OPERASI' PERMATABANK

Permata Student Entrepreneurship Program (PSEP): Job Shadow Day 2015
75 Vocational Students Visiting PermataBank's "Kitchen"

![img-100.jpeg](img-100.jpeg)

Job Shadow Day merupakan salah satu kegiatan dari Permata Student Entrepreneurship Program (PSEP) yang dijalankan untuk mengenalkan dunia kerja kepada siswa/i binaan PermataHati (SMK Farmasi DITKESAD, SMKN 5, dan SMK Bina Informatika). 75 siswa/i dari Perusahaan Siswa yang mewakili 3 sekolah tersebut berkunjung ke kantor pusat PermataBank untuk melihat kegiatan di dealing room dan banking hall serta bertatap muka secara langsung dengan para Senior Manager PermataBank untuk belajar bagaimana mengelola perusahaan, memahami tugas dan tanggung jawab beberapa posisi di perusahaan, dan memperlihatkan secara langsung dunia kerja sesungguhnya.

![img-101.jpeg](img-101.jpeg)

PermataBank and Prestasi Junior Indonesia (PJI) collaborated to organize Job Shadow activity on 21 January 2015. This program aimed to prepare students of vocational schools assisted by PermataHati, namely SMK Farmasi DITKESAD, SMKN 5, and SMK Bina Informatika, for the professional world. As part of the Permata Student Entrepreneurship Program (PSEP), the 100 students from Student Companies, representing 3 schools, visited PermataBank's head office to see first hand the dealing room, banking hall, and to face to face with PermataBank's Senior Managers. They were learning on how to organize a company and understand the roles and responsibilities of several positions in PermataBank.
