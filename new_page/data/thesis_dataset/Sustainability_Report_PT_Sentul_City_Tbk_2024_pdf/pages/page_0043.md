## ASPEK UMUM

### Biaya Lingkungan Hidup

Perseroan senantiasa berupaya mencegah dampak buruk dari kegiatan usaha yang dilakukan, dengan mengeluarkan kebijakan yang sejalan dengan tujuan pembangunan berkelanjutan. Sampai dengan saat ini Perseroan tetap konsisten pada upaya memelihara vegetasi, menanam berbagai jenis pohon serta mempertahankan kawasan Sentul City tetap hijau. Dampak dari konsistensi pelaksanaan kebijakan Perseroan tersebut diharapkan memberikan kontribusi terkait kesehatan warga penghuni kawasan Sentul City dan warga sekitar. Pada tahun yang baru berakhir biaya perawatan yang dikeluarkan oleh Perseroan sebesar Rp 11.733.600.000, dibandingkan biaya perawatan tahun 2023 mengalami kenaikan sekitar Rp 388 juta. Biaya perawatan tersebut meliputi Taman Lingkungan, Taman Spine Road, Play Set, Rumput Bahu Jalan, Rumput Ruang Terbuka Hijau, Pohon Jalan, Pohon Penghijauan, Penyapuan, Jogging Track dan Saluran.

## GENERAL ASPECTS

### Environmental Costs

The Company always strives to prevent adverse impacts from its business activities by issuing policies aligned with sustainable development goals. To date, the Company has remained consistent in its efforts to maintain vegetation, plant various types of trees and keep the Sentul City area green. The impact of the consistent implementation of the Company's policy is expected to contribute to the health of residents of the Sentul City area and surrounding communities. In the recently ended year, the maintenance costs incurred by the Company amounted to Rp 11,733,600,000, reflecting an increase of approximately Rp 388 million compared to maintenance costs in 2023. These costs cover the maintenance of Neighborhood Park, Spine Road Park, Play Set, Road Shoulder Grass, Green Open Space Grass, Street Trees, Greening Trees, Sweeping, Jogging Track and Drains.
