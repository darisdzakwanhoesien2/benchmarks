# Aspek Sosial Social Aspect

Perseroan dari waktu ke waktu berupaya untuk mendukung dan mengembangkan program sosial kemasyarakatan di sekitar Perseroan beroperasi dan tempat lain, baik bekerja sama dengan pihak ketiga maupun secara swadaya.

The Company continuously strives to support and develop community social programs around the Company's operational areas and other places, either by collaborating with third parties or voluntarily.

|  Biaya program sosial kemasyarakatan yang dikeluarkan Perseroan Costs of community social programs incurred by the Company | Rp 404.860.000 | Rp 312.772.000 | Rp 1.098.230.869  |

Perseroan juga memberikan perhatian terhadap pemberdayaan masyarakat sekitar. Berkenaan dengan hal tersebut, Perseroan telah menyelenggarakan berbagai kegiatan sosial yang bersentuhan langsung dengan warga masyarakat sekitar wilayah Babakan Madang dan tempat lain. Kegiatan sosial kemasyarakatan yang didukung Perseroan antara lain menyangkut bidang Pendidikan, Pemberdayaan Perempuan, dan Lingkungan.

The Company also pays attention to the empowerment of surrounding communities. In this regard, various social activities have been conducted, involving direct interaction with communities in the Babakan Madang area and other locations. The community social activities supported by the Company focus on education, women's empowerment, and environmental issues.

Untuk meminimalisir dampak negatif kegiatan Perseroan, secara bertahap Perseroan terus melakukan berbagai perbaikan terkait pengelolaan limbah. Pengelolaan limbah yang dilakukan Perseroan telah melibatkan tenaga kerja lokal di sekitar tempat Perseroan beroperasi, khususnya di sekitar tempat pengelolaan limbah. Selain daripada itu Perseroan juga telah banyak mempekerjakan tenaga kerja lokal di sekitar tempat Perseroan beroperasi, yang ditempatkan pada berbagai unit kerja.

Selama periode pelaporan Perseroan tidak terdapat laporan pelanggaran terhadap peraturan lingkungan hidup.

To minimize the negative impact of its activities, the Company continues to implement various improvements in waste management. The Company's waste management efforts have involved local workers from areas surrounding its operations, particularly around the waste processing sites. Additionally, the Company has employed a significant number of local workers across different work units near its business operations.

During the reporting period, there were no reports of violations of environmental regulations by the Company.
