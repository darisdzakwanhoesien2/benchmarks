# Bidang Kesehatan
Health Sector

![img-45.jpeg](img-45.jpeg)

Perseroan bekerja sama dengan PMI Kabupaten Bogor melaksanakan kegiatan Donor Darah.

Sepanjang tahun 2024 Perseroan melaksanakan 3 kali kegiatan Donor Darah yaitu:

1. Tanggal 13 Februari 2024, diikuti oleh 62 pendonor;
2. Tanggal 2 Mei 2024, diikuti oleh 55 pendonor;
3. Tanggal 13 Agustus 2024, diikuti oleh 64 pendonor.

The Company partnered with the Indonesian Red Cross (PMI) of Bogor Regency to organize blood donation events.

Throughout 2024, the Company conducted three blood donation events:

1. February 13, 2024, participated by 62 donors;
2. May 2, 2024, participated by 55 donors;
3. August 13, 2024, participated by 64 donors.

![img-46.jpeg](img-46.jpeg)

Perseroan bekerja sama dengan Puskesmas Cijayanti melaksanakan kegiatan POSYANDU, di PAUD Sudi dan PAUD Cibimbin, terkait Deteksi Dini Tumbuh Kembang Anak.

Kegiatan dilaksanakan pada tanggal 26 &amp; 27 Maret 2024, yang diikuti oleh 42 anak peserta didik usia dini.

The Company collaborated with the Cijayanti Community Health Center (Puskesmas) to conduct an Integrated Health Service (Posyandu) at Sudi Early Childhood Education and Development (PAUD) and Cibimbin Early Childhood Education and Development, focusing on Early Detection of Child Growth and Development.

The activity took place on March 26 &amp; 27, 2024, with 42 early childhood students participating.
