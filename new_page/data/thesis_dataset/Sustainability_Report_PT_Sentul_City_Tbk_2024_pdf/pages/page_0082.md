Perseroan membagi Paket Bahan Makanan Pokok di Kecamatan Babakan Madang dan sekitarnya dalam Program Ladang Pahala.

Sepanjang tahun 2024 Perseroan melaksanakan Program Ladang Pahala sebanyak 9 kali yaitu:

1. Tanggal 1 Maret 2024, di Kampung Jampang Tangkil, Desa Cadas Ngampar;
2. Tanggal 5 April 2024, di sekitar area Pasar Ahpoong Desa Cadas Ngampar;
3. Tanggal 31 Mei 2024, di Kampung Cibarengkok, Desa Sumur Batu;
4. Tanggal 21 Juni 2024, di Kampung Cicadas, Desa Babakan Madang;
5. Tanggal 5 Juli 2024, di Kampung Cijulang, Desa Cadas Ngampar;
6. Tanggal 16 Agustus 2024, di Kampung Cicadas, Desa Babakan Madang;
7. Tanggal 6 September 2024, Kampung Pasir Maung, Desa Citaringgul;
8. Tanggal 4 Oktober 2024, di Kampung Pasir Maung, Desa Cijayanti;
9. Tanggal 1 November 2024, di Kampung Cicadas, Desa Babakan Madang.

Pada hari Raya Idul Adha tahun 2024, Perseroan menyerahkan 25 ekor Kambing ke beberapa Desa di Kecamatan Babakan Madang.

Penyerahan 25 Kambing pada hari Raya Kurban dilaksanakan pada tanggal 18 Juni 2024.

The Company distributed Basic Food Packages in Babakan Madang District and surrounding areas through the "Ladang Pahala" (Field of Blessings) Program.

Throughout 2024, the program was implemented 9 times:

1. March 1, 2024, in Jampang Tangkil Village, Cadas Ngampar Subdistrict;
2. April 5, 2024, near Ahpoong Market area, Cadas Ngampar Subdistrict;
3. May 31, 2024, in Cibarengkok Village, Sumur Batu Subdistrict;
4. June 21, 2024, in Cicadas Village, Babakan Madang Subdistrict;
5. July 5, 2024, in Cijulang Village, Cadas Ngampar Subdistrict;
6. August 16, 2024, in Cicadas Village, Babakan Madang Subdistrict;
7. September 6, 2024, in Pasir Maung Village, Citaringgul Subdistrict;
8. October 4, 2024, in Pasir Maung Village, Cijayanti Village;
9. November 1, 2024, in Cicadas Village, Babakan Madang Subdistrict.

On Eid al-Adha 2024, the Company donated 25 goats to several villages in Babakan Madang District.

The handover of 25 goats for the Eid al-Adha qurban took place on June 18, 2024.
