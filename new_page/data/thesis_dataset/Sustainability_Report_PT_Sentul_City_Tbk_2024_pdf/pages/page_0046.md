# Upaya Efisiensi Energi

Perseroan berupaya untuk melakukan efisiensi energi dengan cara antara lain;

1. Secara bertahap mengganti atau menggunakan lampu hemat energi untuk penerangan jalan umum di kawasan Sentul City dan mengurangi durasi lampu nyala.
2. Secara bertahap mengganti atau menggunakan lampu hemat energi untuk kantor Perseroan dan mematikan lampu kantor yang tidak dipergunakan atau tidak diperlukan.
3. Secara bertahap mengganti AC kantor dengan AC hemat energi.

# ASPEK EMISI

Perseroan secara terencana dan terprogram belum memiliki upaya yang komprehensif terkait pengurangan Emisi. Namun begitu Perseroan telah melakukan kegiatan Penghijauan yang berkesinambungan sejak awal pengembangan kawasan sampai dengan saat ini. Upaya Penanaman dan perawatan berbagai jenis pohon di kawasan Sentul City diharapkan dapat memperlambat pelepasan Emisi Karbon.

Selain hal tersebut di atas Perseroan secara berkala melakukan pemeliharaan seluruh kendaraan operasional untuk mengurangi emisi dan meningkatkan efisiensi bahan bakar.

# Efforts in Energy Efficiency

The Company strives for energy efficiency by:

1. Gradually replacing or using energy-saving lamps for public street lighting in the Sentul City area and reducing the duration of lighting operations.
2. Gradually replacing or using energy-saving lamps in the Company's offices and turning off unused or unnecessary office lights.
3. Gradually replacing office air conditioners with energy-saving models.

# WATER ASPECT

Based on the data above, it indicates that the use of water in the area in 2024 has decreased compared to 2023, as well as 2022.

# EMISSION ASPECT

The Company has not yet implemented a comprehensive effort to reduce emissions in a planned and programmed manner. However, the Company has conducted continuous reforestation activities since the beginning of the area's development up to the present. Planting and maintaining various types of trees in the Sentul City area is expected to slow down the release of carbon emissions.

In addition, the Company regularly maintains all operational vehicles to reduce emissions and improve fuel efficiency.
