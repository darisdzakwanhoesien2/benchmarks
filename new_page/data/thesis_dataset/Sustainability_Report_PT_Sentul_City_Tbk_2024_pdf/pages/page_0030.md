## C. Strategi Pencapaian Target

### 1. Informasi pengelolaan risiko

Pembangunan keberlanjutan akan dapat dilaksanakan bilamana berbagai risiko dapat dikelola dengan baik. Untuk mengelola risiko keuangan, ekonomi, lingkungan, maupun risiko sosial dengan baik diperlukan pemetaan dan identifikasi risiko secara berkesinambungan.

Untuk memperkecil risiko sosial, Perseroan berupaya menjalin hubungan dan kerja sama yang baik dengan tokoh masyarakat sekitar termasuk memberikan kesempatan kerja kepada tenaga kerja lokal, melaksanakan pemberdayaan dan kemitraan bagi warga termasuk dengan Usaha Mikro Kecil dan Menengah sekitar Perseroan beroperasi.

Mengingat pentingnya pengelolaan risiko sosial hal-hal sebagaimana di bawah ini diharapkan akan memberikan kontribusi positif dan dapat meminimalisir risiko sosial antara lain:

a. Terus bertambahnya tenaga kerja lokal yang bekerja pada Perseroan termasuk Entitas Anak.
b. Semakin besarnya kontribusi Perseroan dan Entitas Anak pada pemberdayaan masyarakat sekitar.
c. Semakin besarnya keuntungan ekonomi masyarakat sekitar dan Usaha Mikro Kecil dan Menengah sekitar atas kerja sama dan kemitraan yang dikembangkan Perseroan dan Entitas Anak.

It is expected that the development of these products will have a positive impact on the Company, enabling it to continuously strive to present high-quality, modern products that meet the needs of consumers and the property market. Products that are well-absorbed and well-received by the property market can significantly contribute to and boost the Company's financial performance in the short, medium, and long term.

## C. Target Achievement Strategy

### 1. Risk management information

Sustainable development can only be achieved if various risks are managed effectively. To properly manage financial, economic, environmental, and social risks, continuous risk mapping and identification are required.

To mitigate social risks, the Company strives to establish good relationships and collaborate with local community leaders. This include providing job opportunities for local workers and implementing community empowerment and partnership programs with Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) in the Company's operational areas.

Given the importance of social risk management, the following factors are expected to contribute positively and minimize social risks:

a. The increasing employment of local workers by the Company and its Subsidiaries.
b. The growing contribution of the Company and its Subsidiaries to community empowerment efforts.
c. The rising economic benefits for the surrounding community and Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises through partnerships established by the Company and its Subsidiaries.
