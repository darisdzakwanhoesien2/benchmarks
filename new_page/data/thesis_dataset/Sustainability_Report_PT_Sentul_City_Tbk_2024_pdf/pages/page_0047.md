## Mekanisme Pengelolaan Limbah dan Efluen
### Sistem Pengelolaan air limbah di Sentul City

#### 1. Sistem Wastewater Reticulation
Berupa jaringan perpipaan yang menampung air limbah dari closet kamar mandi dari masing-masing rumah, yang kemudian dikoneksikan dengan Sewage Treatment Plan (STP) yang berada di 5 titik. Air limbah tersebut kemudian ditampung dan diolah dalam STP dengan sistem anaerob, overflow hasil pengolahan dialirkan ke jaringan drainase kota.

## Mechanism for Waste and Effluent Management
### Wastewater Management System in Sentul City

#### 1. Wastewater Reticulation System
This system consists of a pipeline network that collects wastewater from bathroom closets in each house, which is then connected to the Sewage Treatment Plan (STP) located at 5 points. The wastewater is collected and treated in the STP using an anaerobic system, and the overflow from the treatment process is discharged into the city drainage network.
