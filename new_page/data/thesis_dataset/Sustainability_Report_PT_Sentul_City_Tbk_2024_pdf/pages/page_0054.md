2. Sistem Biofil/Septic Tank Individual

Sistem ini berupa pemasangan biofil tank individual di setiap unit rumah yang disesuaikan dengan kapasitas per unit rumah. Untuk unit komersial diakomodir dengan penyediaan biofil tank atau septic individual di masing-masing area komersial. Overflow hasil pengolahan dialirkan ke jaringan drainase kota.

2. Individual Biofil/Septic Tank System

This system involves installing an individual biofil tank in each residential unit, adjusted to the capacity of each house. For commercial units, wastewater is managed through the provision of biofil tanks or individual septic tanks in each commercial area. Overflow from the treatment is discharged into the city drainage network.
