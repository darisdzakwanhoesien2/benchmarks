## PROGRAM ORIENTASI DAN ON THE JOB TRAINING BAGI PEGAWAI BARU

Untuk dapat mengenal sistem, prosedur, dan budaya yang ada di WSBP, Perusahaan memberikan pembekalan bagi seluruh Insan WSBP baru yang telah dinyatakan lolos kualifikasi. Pembekalan tersebut diberikan dalam bentuk Program Orientasi dan On The Job Training. Setelah mengikuti program pembekalan, para Insan WSBP baru akan langsung ditempatkan di Unit Kerja/Bisnis dan proyek-proyek WSBP yang sedang berjalan.

Selama tahun 2025, hanya dilakukan Program Orientasi bagi pegawai baru yang berstatus OS dan tidak ada pelaksanaan On the Job Training bagi pegawai baru.

Berdasarkan berbagai program pengembangan kompetensi yang disebutkan di atas, total pendidikan dan pelatihan selama tahun 2025 berdasarkan People Development Ratio adalah 31.124 jam pelatihan dengan rerata jam pelatihan setiap Insan WSBP adalah 51,7 jam/orang/tahun. Jumlah jam dan rata-rata jam pelatihan tersebut naik dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya, yaitu 17.384 jam pelatihan dengan rerata jam pelatihan setiap pekerja 3,66 jam/orang/tahun. Uraian mengenai pelatihan selengkapnya disampaikan dalam tabel berikut: [GRI 404-1]

## ORIENTATION AND ON-THE-JOB TRAINING PROGRAM FOR NEW EMPLOYEES

To familiarize new Insan WSBP with the systems, procedures, and culture at WSBP, the Company provides orientation for all new personnel who have passed the qualification process. This orientation is provided in the form of an Orientation and On-The-Job Training Program. After completing the orientation program, new Insan WSBP will be directly assigned to Operational/ Business Units and ongoing WSBP projects.

Throughout 2025, only Orientation Programs were conducted for new employees with OS status, and no On the Job Training was provided for new hires.

Based on the various competency development programs mentioned above, the total education and training hours during 2025, based on the People Development Ratio, was 31,124 training hours, with the average training hours per Insan WSBP being 51.7 hours/person/year. The total hours and average training hours increased compared to the previous year, which were 17,384 training hours with an average of 3.66 training hours per employee/year.
