# Kebijakan Evaluasi Kinerja Direksi

Penilaian kinerja Direksi WSBP dilaksanakan dengan mengacu pada pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab yang tercantum dalam peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku, Anggaran Dasar Perusahaan, serta penugasan yang diberikan oleh RUPS. Evaluasi kinerja masing-masing anggota Direksi dirancang berdasarkan target dari Aspirasi Pemegang Saham (APS) yang telah disepakati dalam perjanjian penunjukan (Kontrak Manajemen). Proses evaluasi ini dilakukan secara berkala dan hasilnya disampaikan dengan transparan kepada seluruh anggota Direksi sejak mereka diangkat. Penilaian ini dijadikan sebagai salah satu dasar pertimbangan bagi RUPS untuk menunjuk kembali anggota Direksi yang berkinerja baik serta sebagai salah satu dasar dalam mengembangkan sistem remunerasi bagi Direksi. Penilaian kinerja Direksi sesuai dengan sistem evaluasi Direksi yang telah disusun oleh Dewan Komisaris. Sistem penilaian kinerja Direksi harus mencantumkan indicator untuk menilai kinerja Direksi, diajukan kepada RUPS agar disahkan, dan dilaksanakan setahun sekali.

# Board of Directors Performance Evaluation Policy

The performance assessment of the WSBP Board of Directors is carried out based on the implementation of the duties and responsibilities stipulated in applicable laws and regulations, the Company's Articles of Association, and assignments assigned by the GMS. The performance evaluation of each member of the Board of Directors is designed based on the targets set forth in the Shareholder Aspiration (APS), as agreed upon in the appointment agreement (Management Contract). This evaluation process is conducted periodically, and the results are transparently communicated to all members of the Board of Directors upon their appointment. This assessment serves as one of the considerations for the GMS to reappoint members of the Board of Directors who have performed well and as one of the foundations for developing a remuneration system for the Board of Directors. The performance assessment of the Board of Directors is in accordance with the Board of Directors evaluation system established by the Board of Commissioners. The Board of Directors performance assessment system must include indicators for assessing the Board of Directors' performance, be submitted to the GMS for approval, and be implemented annually.
