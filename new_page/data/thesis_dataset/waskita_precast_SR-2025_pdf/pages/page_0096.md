# STRUKTUR TATA KELOLA [GRI 2-9, 2-11]

Berdasarkan Undang-Undang Republik Indonesia No. 40 tahun 2007 tentang Perseroan Terbatas (UU PT), struktur tata kelola Perusahaan meliputi Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS), Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi. Untuk system kepengurusan tata kelola, sesuai dengan Anggaran Dasar Perusahaan, WSBP menganut system dua badan (two tier system), yaitu Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi yang memiliki wewenang dan tanggung jawab yang jelas sesuai fungsinya masing-masing sebagaimana diamanatkan dalam Anggaran Dasar Perusahaan dan peraturan perundang-undangan. Di WSBP, Direksi sekaligus merupakan eksekutif senior yang dipilih langsung oleh pemegang saham dan bertanggung jawab terhadap kepengurusan Perusahaan. [GRI 2-11]

Dalam menjalankan fungsinya, Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi didukung sejumlah organ. Dewan Komisaris dibantu oleh Sekretaris Dewan Komisaris, Komite Audit, Komite Pemantau Risiko dan Tata Kelola Terintegrasi, serta Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi. Komite-komite tersebut bertugas membantu Dewan Komisaris dalam mengambil keputusan mengenai topik-topik ekonomi, lingkungan dan sosial di Perusahaan dengan terlebih dahulu menyelenggarakan Rapat Komite dengan Direksi dan Manajemen. Sementara itu, Direksi didukung oleh unit fungsional yang terdiri dari Fungsi Corporate Secretary, Fungsi Internal Audit, Fungsi Kepatuhan dan Manajemen Risiko, serta Fungsi Legal. [GRI 2-9]

Based on the 2025 assessment results, the assessment of GCG implementation at WSBP has improved compared to 2024. This achievement encourages WSBP to continue enhancing GCG implementation and optimally address all recommendations from the GCG implementation assessment team for the 2025 fiscal year, in order to maintain long-term business sustainability.

# GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE [GRI 2-9, 2-11]

Based on Law of the Republic of Indonesia No. 40 of 2007 concerning Limited Liability Companies (UU PT), the Company's governance structure includes the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS), the Board of Commissioners, and the Board of Directors. For governance management, in accordance with the Company's Articles of Association, WSBP adheres to a two-tier system: the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors, each with clear authority and responsibilities according to their respective functions, as mandated by the Company's Articles of Association and laws and regulations. At WSBP, the Board of Directors is also a senior executive directly elected by shareholders and is responsible for the management of the Company. [GRI 2-11]

In carrying out their functions, the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors are supported by several organs. The Board of Commissioners is assisted by the Secretary to the Board of Commissioners, the Audit Committee, the Integrated Risk Monitoring and Governance Committee, and the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. These committees are tasked with assisting the Board of Commissioners in making decisions on economic, environmental, and social topics within the Company by first holding Committee Meetings with the Board of Directors and Management. Meanwhile, the Board of Directors is supported by functional units consisting of the Corporate Secretary Function, Internal Audit Function, Compliance and Risk Management Function and Legal Function. [GRI 2-9]
