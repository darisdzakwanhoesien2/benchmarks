\text{Emisi GRK}_{\text{GRK Emission}} \left(\frac{\mathrm{kg}}{\text{thn}}\right) = \frac{\text{Konsumsi Energi}}{\text{Energi Consumption}} \left(\frac{\mathrm{TJ}}{\text{thn}}\right) \times \frac{\text{Faktor Emisi}}{\text{Emission Factor}} \left(\frac{\mathrm{kg}}{\mathrm{TJ}}\right)
