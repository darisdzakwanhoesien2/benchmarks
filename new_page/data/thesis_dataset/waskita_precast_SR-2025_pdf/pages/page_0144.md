# PERMASALAHAN TERHADAP PENERAPAN KEUANGAN/KEGIATAN BERKELANJUTAN [OJK E.5]

Komitmen WSBP menjalankan pemenuhan Tanggung Jawab Sosial dan Lingkungan (TJSL) tidak hanya merupakan bentuk kepatuhan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan, namun juga sebagai upaya menciptakan pertumbuhan ekonomi berkelanjutan dengan menyelaraskan kepentingan ekonomi, sosial, dan lingkungan hidup. Dalam menjalankan penerapan kegiatan berkelanjutan selama tahun 2025, Perusahaan dihadapkan dengan berbagai tantangan, antara lain:

1. Meningkatnya tuntutan terhadap praktik bisnis yang ramah lingkungan
2. Adanya persepsi negatif terhadap perusahaan manufaktur yang dinilai menghasilkan emisi dalam jumlah besar

# ISSUES RELATED TO THE IMPLEMENTATION OF SUSTAINABLE FINANCE/ACTIVITIES [OJK E.5]

WSBP's commitment to fulfilling its Corporate Social and Environmental Responsibility (CSR) is not only a form of compliance with laws and regulations, but also an effort to create sustainable economic growth by aligning economic, social, and environmental interests. In implementing sustainable activities through 2025, the Company faced various challenges, including:

1. Increasing demand for environmentally friendly business practices
2. Negative perceptions of manufacturing companies perceived as producing large amounts of emissions

Perusahaan melakukan upaya-upaya solutif dalam mengatasi tantangan-tantangan tersebut, antara lain:

1. Pelaksanaan WSBP Inspiring Kindness: Piles of Sustainability
2. Peraihan sertifikasi Green Label Indonesia di Batching Plant WSBP Pegangsaan
3. Peraihan Penghargaan Industri Hijau Tahun 2025 di Precast Plant WSBP Karawang, Subang, dan Prambon
4. Pengaplikasian inovasi produk beton ramah lingkungan di berbagai proyek strategis nasional seperti Mortar Foam, Fly Ash, Spun Pile, SPRigWP, dan masih banyak lagi

The Company is implementing solutions to address these challenges, including:

1. Implementation of WSBP Inspiring Kindness: Piles of Sustainability
2. Achieving Green Label Indonesia certification at the WSBP Pegangsaan Batching Plant
3. Receiving the 2025 Green Industry Award at the Precast Plant WSBP Karawang, Subang and Prambon
4. Application of innovative environmentally friendly concrete products in various national strategic projects such as Mortar Foam, Fly Ash, Spun Pile, SPRigWP, and many more
