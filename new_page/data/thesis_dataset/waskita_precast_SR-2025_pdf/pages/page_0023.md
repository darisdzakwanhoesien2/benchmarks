Tata Kelola Perusahaan Berkelanjutan

Sustainable Corporate Governance

Perusahaan secara berkala melakukan pembaruan Pedoman Etika dan Perilaku (Code of Conduct) sebagai landasan bagi seluruh Insan WSBP dalam menjalankan kegiatan usaha yang berintegritas serta selaras dengan prinsip-prinsip GCG. Selain itu, Perusahaan terus memperkuat implementasi tata kelola melalui penerapan prinsip transparansi, akuntabilitas, responsibilitas, independensi, dan kewajaran dalam seluruh aktivitas operasional dan pengambilan keputusan.

Komitmen terhadap tata kelola yang baik juga diwujudkan melalui ke patuhan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku serta kebijakan internal Perusahaan, yang didukung oleh penguatan manajemen risiko dan system pengendalian internal. Perusahaan juga mengoptimalkan fungsi pengawasan oleh Dewan Komisaris, penerapan Whistleblowing System (WBS) sebagai sarana pelaporan dugaan pelanggaran, serta penguatan budaya integritas dan ke patuhan di seluruh lini organisasi gunna menjaga keberlanjutan usaha secara bertanggung jawab.

Sebagai bagian dari mekanisme pengelolaan dan pengawasan perusahaan, Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris secara rutin melaksanakan rapat sesuai dengan rencana kerja yang telah ditetapkan. Selain itu, Perusahaan memastikan setiap laporan yang diterima melalui Whistleblowing System (WBS) dikelola dan ditindaklanjuti sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku untuk menjaga integritas dan penerapan etika bisnis di lingkungan Perusahaan.

Atas berbagai upaya dalam penerapan Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik, Perusahaan mencatatkan capaian skor GCG sebesar 85,440 dengan predikat "Sangat Baik". Skor tersebut diperoleh dari hasil asesmen yang dilakukan oleh Pusat Pengembangan Akuntansi &amp; Keuangan (PPA&amp;K).

Komitmen Perusahaan dalam menjalankan kinerja keberlanjutan secara terarah membawa dampak positif. Pada tahun 2025 Perusahaan berhasil meraih sejumlah penghargaan terkait keberlanjutan, antara lain:

- Penghargaan Program Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3) Periode 1 November 2020 s/d 13 Desember 2024 Precast Plant WSBP Prambon oleh Gubernur Jawa Timur

The Company periodically updates its Code of Conduct as the foundation for all WSBP personnel in conducting business activities with integrity and in alignment with GCG principles. Furthermore, the Company continues to strengthen governance implementation through the application of the principles of transparency, accountability, responsibility, independence, and fairness across all operational activities and decision-making processes.

The commitment to good corporate governance is also demonstrated through compliance with applicable laws and regulations as well as the Company's internal policies, supported by the strengthening of risk management and the internal control system. The Company also optimizes the supervisory function of the Board of Commissioners, the implementation of the Whistleblowing System (WBS) as a means of reporting alleged violations, and the strengthening of a culture of integrity and compliance across all levels of the organization, in order to maintain responsible business sustainability.

As part of the Company's management and oversight mechanism, the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners regularly conduct meetings in accordance with the established work plan. In addition, the Company ensures that every report received through the Whistleblowing System (WBS) is managed and followed up in accordance with applicable provisions, in order to uphold integrity and the application of business ethics within the Company.

In recognition of its various efforts in implementing good corporate governance, the Company recorded a GCG score of 85.440 with a predicate of "Excellent." This score was obtained from an assessment conducted by the Center for Accounting and Finance Development (PPA&amp;K).

The Company's commitment to pursuing sustainability performance in a focused and structured manner has yielded positive outcomes. In 2025, the Company successfully received a number of sustainability-related awards, including:

- Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) Program Award for the Period of November 1, 2020 to December 13, 2024 for WSBP Prambon Precast Plant, conferred by the Governor of East Java
