1. Ketua: Direktur Utama
2. Pelaksana Harian: Direktur Operasi
3. Sekretaris: Kepala Divisi QSHE
4. Komite Pencegahan HIV &amp; AIDS: Dokter Perusahan
5. Anggota: Seluruh Kepala Divisi dan Beberapa Perwakilan Insan WSBP

In accordance with applicable regulations, the OHS Committee is responsible for identifying potential work risks, formulating mitigation measures, and overseeing compliance with OHS standards.

The establishment of the OHS Committee not only supports the implementation of the OHS Management System, but it also strengthens a participatory work safety culture, where all Insan WSBP actively contribute to creating a safe and healthy work environment. The existence of the OSH Committee is regulated in the Decree of the Board of Directors No. 182/SK/WBP/PEN/2024 concerning the Establishment of Occupational Health and Safety Committee of PT Waskita Beton Precast Tbk. OSH Committee members include the elements of the employer and employees, consisting of the Chairman, Secretary, and Members. This organization has the task of providing advice and considerations, whether requested or not, to employers or management regarding occupational health and safety issues. At WSBP, the OSH Committee is under the authority of the President Director, who is also the Chairman of the OSH Committee. The complete organizational structure of the OSH Committee is as follows: [GRI 403-4]

1. Head: President Director
2. Daily Executive: Director of Operations
3. Secretary: Head of QSHE Division
4. HIV &amp; AIDS Prevention Committee: Company Doctor
5. Members: All Vice President and Selected Representatives of Insan WSBP

The functions of the OSH Committee in WSBP are as follows:

1. Duties
Provide advice and recommendations, whether requested or not, to the Company regarding Quality, Health, Safety, and Environment (QHSE) issues.

2. Authorities
a. Enter the workplace as stipulated in the appointment decision.
b. Request information and/or details regarding the implementation of quality, health, safety, and environment (QHSE) requirements in the workplace, according to the scope of their appointment.
