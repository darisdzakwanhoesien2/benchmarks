Meanwhile, on the social aspect, WSBP presents the WSBP Inspiring Kindness: Belajar Beton Goes to School program, which aims to educate students on the concrete manufacturing industry, construction, and infrastructure development. Through this program, WSBP also provides support for school learning activities by supplying practical equipment placed in school laboratories, thereby enhancing students' understanding and skills in the field of construction from an early age.

Furthermore, through the Girls on Site program, WSBP in collaboration with Srikandi WSBP, advocates for the importance of women empowerment in the manufacturing and construction industries by inspiring female senior high school and vocational school students in the vicinity of WSBP's operational areas to have the confidence to pursue careers in these sectors. WSBP recognizes the importance of inclusivity, particularly the role of women, in the manufacturing and construction industries in supporting long-term sustainability performance.

WSBP also regularly conducts Religious Holiday Celebrations and Welfare Assistance activities as a form of corporate social responsibility, by providing assistance in the form of financial aid and goods to those in need, thereby strengthening the harmonious relationship between the Company and the surrounding community.

From the governance perspective, in support of sustainable development, the Company continuously strengthens the implementation of good corporate governance through the application of the principles of transparency, accountability, responsibility, independence, and fairness. The Company ensures compliance with applicable laws and regulations as well as internal company policies, supported by the strengthening of risk management policies and internal controls.
