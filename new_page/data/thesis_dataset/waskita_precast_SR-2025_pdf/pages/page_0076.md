Adjustments were made to the supply chain in 2025 as part of efforts to increase efficiency and optimize partnerships by strengthening the quality and performance of more selective suppliers. A total of 448 suppliers were recorded, a 10% decrease from 493 in the previous year. Accordingly, the contract value in 2025 reached Rp1,334,136 million, reflecting the company's strategic move to manage its contract portfolio more selectively and effectively compared to 2024.
