The following are the components of benefits/facilities provided by the Company to employees:

1. Expertise allowance
A monetary award given to Insan WSBP for professional expertise demonstrated by the possession of a Certificate of Expertise (SKA) issued by an Accredited Professional Association, or Company recognition of an employee's special expertise. The fields of expertise and skills eligible for the allowance and its amount are regulated in a Director's Decree in charge of HCM.

2. Proficiency allowance
A monthly monetary award given to employees for professional proficiency demonstrated by the possession of a Certificate of Proficiency (SKT) issued by an Accredited Professional Association, or Company recognition of a Insan WSBP's special proficiency. Similar to the expertise allowance, the amount of the proficiency allowance is regulated in a Director's Decree in charge of HCM.
