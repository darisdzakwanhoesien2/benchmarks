3. Inovasi Produk/Solusi yang Mendukung Pembangunan Berkelanjutan

WSBP mengembangkan produk dan layanan yang menjawab kebutuhan proyek, termasuk ketahanan mutu, kecepatan pemasangan, efisiensi sumber daya, serta potensi pengurangan dampak lingkungan terhadap siklus hidup proyek.

4. Pengelolaan Air dan Pencegahan Pencemaran

WSBP melakukan pengendalian penggunaan air dalam proses, pemeliharan sarana pengolahan air/limbah cair, serta pemantauan kualitas untuk memastikan kepatuhan dan pencegahan dampak terhadap lingkungan sekitar.

5. Pengelolaan Material dan Rantai Pasok Berkelanjutan

WSBP mendorong penguatan rantai pasok yang efektif dan bertanggung jawab melalui standardisasi kualitas material, evaluasi pemasok, serta penguatan aspek kepatuhan, termasuk dukungan terhadap peningkatan Tingkat Komponen Dalam Negeri (TKDN).

6. Kepatuhan, K3, dan Penguatan Budaya Operasi yang Bertanggung Jawab

WSBP mengedepankan operasi yang aman, patuh terhadap regulasi, dan berorientasi pencegahan, termasuk penguatan inspeksi, pelatihan, serta perbaikan berkelanjutan untuk menjaga keselamatan pekerja dan keberlanjutan operasional,

7. Upaya Reduksi Emisi atas Produksi melalui Program WSBP Inspiring Kindness: Piles of Sustainability

WSBP melaksanakan program penghijauan melalui penanaman pohon sebagai bagian dari upaya kontribusi terhadap pengurangan emisi karbon serta peningkatan kualitas lingkungan di sekitar wilayah operasional.

8. Upaya Kesinambungan Industri, Membangun Minat dan Keterampilan Pelaku Industri Generasi Muda (BelajarBeton, 3DBIM, WSBP Inspiring Kindness: Girls on Site)

WSBP mendorong pengembangan kapasitas generasi muda melalui program edukasi dan pengenalan industri konstruksi guna menumbuhkan minat, pengetahuan, serta keterampilan yang relevan bagi keberlanjutan sektor konstruksi di masa depan.

3. Product Innovation/Solutions Supporting Sustainable Development

WSBP develops products and services that address project requirements, including quality durability, installation speed, resource efficiency, and the potential for reducing environmental impacts across the project life cycle.

4. Water Managemet and Pollution Prevention

WSBP implements controls over water usage in its processes, maintains water and liquid waste treatment facilities, and conducts quality monitoring to ensure compliance and prevent impacts on the surrounding environment.

5. Material Management and Sustainable Supply Chain

WSBP promotes the strengthening of an effective and responsible supply chain through material quality standardization, supplier evaluation, and the reinforcement of compliance aspects, including support for the improvement of the Local Content Level (TKDN).

6. Compliance, Occupational Health and Safety (OHS), and the Strengthening of a Responsible Operational Culture

WSBP prioritizes safe, regulation-compliant, and prevention-oriented operations, including the strengthening of inspections, training, and continuous improvement to safeguard worker safety and operational sustainability.

7. Emission Reduction Efforts from Production through the WSBP Inspiring Kindness: Piles of Sustainability Program

WSBP implements a reforestation program through tree planting as part of its contribution to reducing carbon emissions and improving environmental quality in the vicinity of its operational areas.

8. Efforts for Industry Continuity: Building Interest and Skills Among the Next Generation of Industry Practitioners (BelajarBeton, 3DBIM, WSBP Inspiring Kindness: Girls on Site)

WSBP promotes capacity development among the younger generation through education programs and introductions to the construction industry, with the aim of cultivating relevant interest, knowledge, and skills for the future sustainability of the construction sector.
