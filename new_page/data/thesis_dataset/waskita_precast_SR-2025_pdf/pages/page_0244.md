# Emissions Management

As part of efforts to realize a quality environment, WSBP is committed to reducing Greenhouse Gas (GHG) emissions generated from production activities due to the use of electrical energy, fuel oil, and business air travel. The Company collaborates with stakeholders, including other corporations. [OJK F.23]

WSBP strives to manage emissions responsibly as an effort to mitigate the negative impact of operational activities on the environment. The Company measures GHG emissions across Scope 1, 2, and 3. In this report, the emissions reported are direct GHG emissions (Scope 1) generated from the use of fossil fuels (gasoline and diesel), indirect GHG emissions (Scope 2) from electricity consumption, and other indirect GHG emissions (Scope 3) from business air travel.
