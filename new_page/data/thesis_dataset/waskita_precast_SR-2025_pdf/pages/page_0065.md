# Peta Wilayah Operasional dan Pemasaran (OJK C.3)

## Map of Operational and Marketing Area [OJK C.3]

Sebagai perusahaan manufaktur manufaktur Beton Precast, Beton Readymix, Quarry, Jasa Konstruksi, dan Post Tension dengan kapasitas produksi Beton Precast mencapai 3,7 juta ton/tahun dengan 9 Precast Plant WSBP, di antaranya terletak di Gasing, Bojonegara, Cibitung, Karawang, Sadang, Kalijati, Subang, Klaten, dan Prambon, serta 14 Batching Plant WSBP dan 2 Quarry WSBP aktif dengan total sebanyak 4 Stone Crusher, serta 5 Area Penjualan WSBP yang tersebar di seluruh wilayah Indonesia, WSBP mampu menyasar pasar internal dan eksternal dari berbagai proyek infrastruktur di Indonesia. Selain itu, WSBP juga memiliki 1 Workshop Alat Berat yang terletak di Cikopo dan 1 Lab &amp; Admixture yang terletak di Karawang.

As a manufacturer of Precast Concrete, Readymix Concrete, Quarry, Construction Services and Post Tension with a Precast Concrete production capacity of 3.7 million tons/year with 9 WSBP Precast Plants, a Precast Concrete production capacity of 3.7 million tons/year with 9 Precast Plants WSBP, located at Gasing, Bojonegara, Cibitung, Karawang, Sadang, Kalijati, Subang, Klaten and Prambon, as well as 14 Batching Plants WSBP and 2 active Quarries WSBP with total of 4 Stone Crushers and 5 Sales Areas WSBP spread throughout Indonesia, WSBP is able to target internal and external markets from various infrastructure projects in Indonesia. In addition, WSBP also has 1 Heavy Equipment Workshop located in Cikopo and 1 Lab &amp; Admixture located in Karawang.
