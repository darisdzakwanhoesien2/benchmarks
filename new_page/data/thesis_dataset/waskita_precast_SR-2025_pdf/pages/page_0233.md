# Mewujudkan Industri Hijau demi Masa Depan

Realizing a Green Industry for the Future

WSBP memastikan seluruh proses produksinya mematuhi standar mutudan lingkungan melalui penerapan system terintegrasi yang berpedoman pada Standar Industri Hijau (SIH) dengan melakukan pengendalian emisi, hingga pengelolaan air limbah yang diproses menggunakan instalasi pengolahan air limbah (IPAL) di seluruh pabrik

WSBP ensures that all of its production processes comply with quality and environmental standards through the implementation of an integrated system based on the Green Industry Standards (SIH), including emission control and wastewater management using wastewater treatment plants (WWTPs) at all of its factories.
