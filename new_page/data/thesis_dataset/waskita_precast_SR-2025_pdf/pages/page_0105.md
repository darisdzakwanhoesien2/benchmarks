# Rapat Direksi

Di sepanjang tahun 2025, Direksi telah melaksanakan rapat internal dengan rincian rapat Direksi adalah sebagai berikut:

1. Rapat Koordinasi sebanyak 1 (satu) kali
2. Rapat Direksi Terbatas sebanyak 27 (dua puluh tujuh) kali
3. Rapat Direksi sebanyak 10 (sepuluh) kali

Rapat tersebut dilaksanakan secara konsisten sesuai rencana kerja yang telah ditetapkan, dan realisasinya mencapai 100%.

# Organ Pendukung di Bawah Direksi

Untuk mengoptimalkan kinerjanya, Direksi dibantu oleh organ pendukung, yaitu Divisi Corporate Secretary dan Divisi Internal Audit.

# Divisi Corporate Secretary

WSBP membentuk Divisi Corporate Secretary berdasarkan Peraturan Menteri BUMN No. PER-02/MBU/03/2023 tentang Pedoman Tata Kelola dan Kegiatan Korporasi Signifikan Badan Usaha Milik Negara dan Peraturan OJK No. 35/POJK.04/2014 tentang Sekretaris Perusahaan Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik.

Divisi Corporate Secretary memiliki tanggung jawab esensial dalam menjalin komunikasi yang efektif antara perusahaan, pemegang saham, regulator, serta pemangku kepentingan lainnya. Fungsi utama Divisi Corporate Secretary adalah memastikan bahwa seluruh aktivitas perusahaan berjalan sesuai dengan ketentuan hukum yang berlaku, serta mendukung keterbukaan informasi yang transparan dan akurat. Divisi Corporate Secretary dipimpin oleh seorang Kepala Divisi yang memiliki peran strategis dalam mengelola hubungan eksternal, memperkuat kepercayaan publik, dan menjaga kepatuhan terhadap regulasi pasar modal yang dinamis.

# Board of Directors Meetings

Throughout 2025, the Board of Directors held internal meetings with the following breakdown:

1. 1 (one) time Coordination Meeting
2. 27 (twenty-seven) times Limited Board of Directors Meeting
3. 10 (ten) times Board of Directors Meeting

These meetings were conducted consistently in accordance with the established work plan, with a realization rate of 100%.

# Supporting Organs Under the Board of Directors

To optimize its performance, the Board of Directors is assisted by supporting organs, namely Corporate Secretary Division and Internal Audit Division.

# Corporate Secretary Division

WSBP established Corporate Secretary Division in accordance with the Minister of State-Owned Enterprises Regulation No. PER-02/MBU/03/2023 on Guidelines for Governance and Significant Corporate Activities of State-Owned Enterprises, and OJK Regulation No. 35/POJK.04/2014 concerning Corporate Secretary of Issuers or Public Companies.

Corporate Secretary Division plays a vital role in facilitating effective communication between the Company, shareholders, regulators, and other stakeholders. Its primary function is to ensure that all Company activities comply with applicable laws and to support transparent and accurate information disclosure. Corporate Secretary Division is led by a Vice President who holds a strategic role in managing external relations, strengthening public trust, and ensuring compliance with evolving capital market regulations.
