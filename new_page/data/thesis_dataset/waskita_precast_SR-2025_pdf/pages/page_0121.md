3. Komite Audit
Komite Audit sebagai Organ Pengelola Risiko di bawah Dewan Komisaris memiliki fungsi Internal Audit dengan wewenang, tugas dan tanggung jawab:

a. Mengakses seluruh informasi yang relevan tentang Perusahaan terkait dengan tugas dan fungsi Komite Audit;
b. Memantau dan mengkaji efektivitas pelaksanaan Audit Intern dan Audit Ekstern;
c. Memastikan objektivitas dan independensi Auditor Internal dan Auditor Eksternal;
d. Memastikan kredibilitas dan objektivitas laporan keuangan Perusahaan yang akan diterbitkan untuk pihak eksternal dan badan pengawas, termasuk penindaklanjutan keluhan dan/atau catatan

ii) Ensure the Internal Audit Division is informed about developments, initiatives, projects, products, and operational changes, as well as identified and anticipated risks;
iii) Ensure appropriate corrective actions are taken promptly to address all findings and recommendations of the Internal Audit;
iv) Ensure the Vice President of Internal Audit has the necessary resources and budget to carry out his duties and functions in accordance with the annual audit plan, within the Company's financial capabilities;
v) Ensure the Company and its subsidiaries (if any) have an Internal Audit Division; and
vi) Carry out other Internal Audit functions in accordance with laws and regulations, the Company's Articles of Association, and/or GMS resolutions.

c. Integrated Governance
i) Develop Integrated Governance policies;
ii) Direct, monitor, and evaluate the implementation of Integrated Governance policies;
iii) Follow-up on directions of advice from the Board of Commissioners for the improvement of the Integrated Governance policies; and
iv) Carry out other Integrated Governance functions in accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations, Company's Articles of Association, and/or resolutions of the GMS.

3. Audit Committee
The Audit Committee, as the Risk Management Body under the Board of Commissioners, has the function of internal audit, with the following authority, duties, and responsibilities:

a. Access all relevant information about the Company related to the duties and functions of the Audit Committee;
b. Monitor and review the effectiveness of the implementation of Internal and External Audits;
c. Ensure the objectivity and independence of Internal and External Auditors;
d. Ensure the credibility and objectivity of the Company's financial reports to be issued to external parties and supervisory bodies, including following up on complaints and/or reports of irregularities
