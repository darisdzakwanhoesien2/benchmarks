# SEKILAS KONDISI EKONOMI NASIONAL DAN GLOBAL

Perekonomian global sepanjang tahun 2025 bergerak dinamis dan tidak pasti. Perang dagang kembali menjadi alat tekanan geopolitik, dan arus perdagangan global semakin terfragmentasi. Kebijakan moneter di negara maju yang kerap berubah, adanya fluktuasi arus modal, serta konflik geopolitik turut memperbesar volatilitas pasar keuangan global.

Di tengah situasi global yang penuh tantangan, ekonomi Indonesia sepanjang 2025 masih menunjukkan ketahanan yang relatif kuat. Meski tidak tumbuh tinggi, ekonomi Indonesia konsisten meningkat dan membaik di tiap triwulannya.

# BRIEF OVERVIEW OF GLOBAL AND NATIONAL ECONOMY

Throughout 2025, the global economy stirred dynamically and uncertainly. Trade wars once again became a tool of geopolitical pressure, and global trade flows became increasingly fragmented. Frequent changes in monetary policies in developed countries, fluctuations in capital flows, and geopolitical conflicts contributed to increasing volatility in global financial markets.

The Indonesian economy still demonstrated relatively strong resilience amid the challenging global situation throughout 2025. Although not experiencing high growth, the Indonesian economy consistently increased and improved each quarter.
