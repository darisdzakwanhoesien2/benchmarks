## Kebijakan Untuk Merespons Tantangan Dalam Pemenuhan Strategi Keberlanjutan

Isu perubahan iklim dan pemanasan global merupakan permasalahan global yang memerlukan penanganan secara serius dan kolaboratif. Apabila tidak diantisipasi secara tepat, dampak yang ditimbulkan berpotensi mengancam keberlangsungan ekosistem serta kehidupan makhluk hidup di masa depan. Sebagai perusahaan BUMN yang bergerak di bidang manufaktur Beton Precast, Beton Readymix, dan Jasa Konstruksi, WSBP memahami peran strategisnya dalam mendukung upaya pengendalian perubahan iklim serta pengelolaan dampak lingkungan yang timbul dari aktivitas operasional perusahaan.

Komitmen ini diwujudkan melalui berbagai inisiatif yang mendukung penerapan prinsip industri hijau serta pengembangan produk ramah lingkungan sebagai bagian dari strategi keberlanjutan Perusahaan. Penerapan prinsip tersebut bertujuan untuk meningkatkan efisiensi penggunaan energi dan sumber daya, mengurangi potensi emisi serta limbah dari proses produksi, serta memastikan bahwa aktivitas operasional Perusahaan dilaksanakan dengan memperhatikan aspek perlindungan lingkungan.

These various measures, the Company endeavors to ensure that all business activities are conducted in a transparent, accountable, and responsible manner, in order to maintain long-term business sustainability and deliver added value to all stakeholders.

Through these various initiatives, WSBP strives to make a tangible contribution to mitigating environmental impacts while simultaneously supporting the creation of positive social value and the application of good corporate governance, allowing the Company's existence and activities to deliver sustainable benefits to its stakeholders and society at large.

## WSBP's Policy to Address Challenges in Achieving Sustainableity Strategy

The issues of climate change and global warming are global issues that require serious and collaborative action. If not properly anticipated, the impacts they generate have the potential to threaten the sustainability of ecosystems and the lives of living beings in the future. Therefore, synergy is required among the government, the industrial sector, the scientific community, and society at large to reduce greenhouse gas emissions, protect ecosystems, and promote the creation of more sustainable development. As a state-owned enterprise (SOE) operating in the sectors of Precast Concrete, and Readymix Concrete manufacture and Construction Services, WSBP recognizes its strategic role in supporting efforts to address climate change and manage the environmental impacts arising from the Company's operational activities.

In responding to these challenges, WSBP implements policies focused on optimizing production processes through a more efficient, innovative, and sustainable approach. This commitment is realized through various initiatives supporting the application of green industry principles and the development of green products as part of the Company's sustainability strategy. The application of these principles aims to improve energy and resource use efficiency, reduce potential emissions and waste from production processes, and ensure that the Company's operational activities are carried out with due regard for environmental protection.
