# PENILAIAN PEGAWAI DAN JENJANG KARIER [GRI 404-3]

Dalam rangka mendorong motivasi pegawai untuk terus Produktif, WSBP memberikan kesempatan yang setara bagi seluruh Insan WSBP untuk menempuh jenjang karier yang jelas dan berkelanjutan. Perusahaan menyediakan system pengembangan karier berbasis kompetensi dan kinerja, sehingga setiap Insan WSBP memiliki peluang untuk berkembang sesuai dengan potensi dan kontribusinya. Adapun proses pengembangan karier pegawai telah diatur dalam Prosedur Pemenuhan Tenaga Kerja (PWP-HC-01), Penilaian Kinerja Pegawai (PWP-HC-03), Promosi Pegawai (PWP-HC-11).

Dalam melakukan penilaian kinerja, WSBP menerapkan system penilaian kinerja pegawai yang transparan, obyektif, dan tanpa diskriminasi. Penilaian dilakukan kepada seluruh pegawai (100%) untuk mewujudkan keadilan, dukungan, serta menetapkan target pencapaian kepada seluruh pegawai. Penilaian dilakukan secara adil kepada semua pegawai berdasarkan prestasi kerja tanpa membedakan Jenis kelamin. [GRI 3-3]

# EMPLOYEE APPRAISAL AND CAREER PATH [GRI 404-3]

To encourage employee motivation to continue to be productive, WSBP provides equal opportunities for all Insan WSBP to pursue a clear and sustainable career path. The Company provides a competency and performance-based career development system, thus providing every Insan WSBP with the opportunity to develop according to their potential and contribution. The Insan WSBP career development process has been regulated in the Manpower Fulfillment Procedure (PWP-HC-01), Employee Performance Assessment (PWP-HC-03), Employee Promotion (PWP-HC-11).

In conducting performance assessments, WSBP applies a transparent, objective, and non-discriminatory employee performance assessment system. The assessment is conducted for all employees (100%) to ensure fairness, support, and the establishment of achievement targets for every employee. The appraisal is done in a fair manner to all employees based on their work performance, regardless of gender. [GRI 3-3]
