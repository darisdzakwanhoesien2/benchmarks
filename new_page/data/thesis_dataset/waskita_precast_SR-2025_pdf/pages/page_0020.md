Sebagai bentuk tanggung jawab sosial perusahaan kepada masyarakat, WSBP merealisasikan berbagai kegiatan melalui program WSBP Inspiring Kindness dengan total realisasi dan Tanggung Jawab Sosial dan Lingkungan (TJSL) sebesar Rp317,86 juta sepanjang tahun 2025. Melalui program ini, WSBP melaksanakan berbagai kegiatan sosial, antara lain Donor Darah yang dilaksanakan sebanyak satu kali, Berbagi Takjil sebanyak 15 kali kegiatan, serta pemberian santunan kepada kaum dhufa di sekitar area unit operasional.

Selain itu, WSBP juga menjalankan program Belajar Beton Goes to School yang diselenggarakan sebanyak tiga kali kegiatan dan diikuti oleh lebih dari 300 mahasiswa dan siswa untuk memberikan wawasan mengenai industri konstruksi kepada para pelajar. WSBP juga melaksanakan program Girls on Site, yaitu program yang mendorong kesetaraan gender di sektor konstruksi dengan mengundang siswisiwiekoklah kejuran untuk berkunjung ke Precast Plant WSBP. Program ini dilaksanakan satu kali dengan melibatkan sebanyak 25 siswi.

Di bidang pengembangan kompetensi, WSBP secara konsisten menyelenggarakan berbagai program pendidikan dan pelatihan, baik yang bersifat teknis maupun nonteknis. Sepanjang tahun 2025, total pelaksanaan pendidikan dan pelatihan berdasarkan People Development Ratio mencapai 31.124 jam pelatihan, dengan rata-rata 51,7 jam pelatihan per orang per tahun.

Dalam upaya menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang sehat dan aman, WSBP terus memperkuat penerapan aspek Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3) di seluruh area operasional. Sepanjang tahun 2025, Perusahaan mencatatkan zero fatality di seluruh area kerja, baik di Precast Plant, Batching Plant &amp; Quarry, Workshop, maupun pada proyek-proyek yang dikelola WSBP.

Berbagai upaya yang dilakukan WSBP juga diarahkan untuk memastikan terpenuhinya hak-hak pegawai serta menjamin kesetaraan bagi seluruh Insan WSBP. Hasil Survei Keterikatan Pegawai pada tahun 2025 tercatat sebesar 65%, mengalami penurunan dibandingkan tahun 2024. Hasil ini mencerminkan adanya dinamika persepsi Pegawai terhadap beberapa aspek organisasi. Temuan tersebut menjadi perhatian Manajemen untuk terus memperkuat komunikasi internal, meningkatkan transparansi, serta mengembangkan berbagai inisiatif yang dapat mendukung keterlibatan dan kepercayaan Pegawai terhadap keberlanjutan Perusahaan.

As a form of corporate social responsibility to the community, WSBP realized various activities through the WSBP Inspiring Kindness program, with a total realization of Corporate Social and Environmental Responsibility (CSR) funds amounting to Rp317.86 million throughout 2025. Through this program, WSBP carried out various social activities, including a one Blood Drive program, Iftar Sharing (Berbagi Takjil) conducted on 15 occasions, and the provision of welfare assistance to underprivileged communities in the vicinity of operational unit areas.

In addition, WSBP also conducted the Belajar Beton Goes to School program, which was held on three occasions and attended by more than 300 college and high school students, to provide insights into the construction industry to learners. WSBP also conducted the Girls on Site program, a program that promotes gender equality in the construction sector by inviting female vocational school students to visit WSBP's Precast Plant. This program was held once, involving a total of 25 female students.

On the competency development front, WSBP consistently organizes various education and training programs, both technical and non-technical in nature. Throughout 2025, the total implementation of education and training based on the People Development Ratio reached 31,124 training hours, with an average of 51.7 training hours per person per year.

In its efforts to create a healthy and safe working environment, WSBP continues to strengthen the implementation of Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) aspects across all operational areas. Throughout 2025, the Company recorded zero fatalities across all work areas, including Precast Plants, Batching Plants &amp; Quarries, Workshops, and projects managed by WSBP.

The various efforts undertaken by WSBP are also directed toward ensuring the fulfillment of employee rights and guaranteeing equality for all WSBP personnel. The Employee Engagement Survey results for 2025 were recorded at 65%, a decrease compared to 2024. These results reflect the dynamics of employees' perceptions regarding certain organizational aspects. The findings have motivated the Management to continuously strengthen internal communication, improve transparency, and develop various initiatives that can support employee engagement and confidence in the Company's sustainability.
