## DUKUNGAN WSBP TERHADAP PEMBANGUNAN BERKELANJUTAN

Dalam rangka memperkuat kontribusi Perusahaan terhadap mitigasi dampak Lingkungan, Sosial, dan Tata Kelola (Environmental, Social, and Governance/ESG), WSBP secara konsisten mengimplementasikan berbagai program dan kebijakan yang bertujuan untuk memastikan bahwa seluruh aktivitas operasional dilaksanakan secara bertanggung jawab dan berkelanjutan. Komitmen ini diwujudkan melalui penerapan langkah-langkah strategis yang tidak hanya berfokus pada pengelolaan dampak lingkungan, tetapi juga pada peningkatan nilai sosial serta penerapan tata kelola perusahaan yang baik.

Komitmen tersebut juga sejalan dengan berbagai kebijakan dan regulasi pemerintah yang mendorong penerapan praktik pembangunan berkelanjutan di sektor industri dan korporasi. Pemerintah Indonesia telah menetapkan kebijakan terkait pelaksanaan Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan (TPB) melalui Peraturan Presiden Nomor 59 Tahun 2017 tentang Pelaksanaan Pencapaian Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan, yang menjadi pedoman bagi pemerintah, dunia usaha, dan masyarakat dalam mendukung agenda pembangunan berkelanjutan di Indonesia. Selain itu, pengelolaan lingkungan hidup di Indonesia juga diatur melalui Undang-Undang Nomor 32 Tahun 2009 tentang Perlindungan dan Pengelolaan Lingkungan Hidup, yang menegaskan kewajiban setiap pelaku usaha untuk menjaga kelestarian lingkungan serta mencegah terjadinya pencemaran dan kerusakan lingkungan. Di sisi lain, penerapan praktik bisnis berkelanjutan juga didorong melalui Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (POJK) No. 51/POJK.03/2017 tentang Penerapan Keuangan Berkelanjutan bagi Lembaga Jasa Keuangan, Emiten, dan Perusahaan Publik, yang mendorong perusahaan untuk mengintegrasikan aspek lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola dalam strategi bisnis serta pelaporan keberlanjutan.

The development of infrastructure projects underway in 2025 will impact the value of new contracts obtained by the Company. In 2025, the Company recorded a New Contract Value (NKB) of Rp1.53 trillion and Revenue of Rp1.57 trillion. WSBP targets NKB growth of 10.16% and an increase in Revenue of up to 16.06% compared to 2024.

## WSBP'S SUPPORT FOR SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT

In order to strengthen the Company's contribution to the mitigation of Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) impacts, WSBP consistently implements various programs and policies aimed at ensuring that all operational activities are carried out in a responsible and sustainable manner. This commitment is realized through the implementation of strategic measures that focus not only on managing environmental impacts, but also on enhancing social value and applying good corporate governance practices.

This commitment is also aligned with various government policies and regulations that promote the adoption of sustainable development practices in the industrial and corporate sectors. The Government of Indonesia has established policies pertaining to the implementation of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) through Presidential Regulation No. 59 of 2017 concerning the Implementation of the Achievement of Sustainable Development Goals, which serves as a guideline for the government, the business community, and society in supporting the sustainable development agenda in Indonesia. Furthermore, environmental management in Indonesia is also governed by Law No. 32 of 2009 concerning Environmental Protection and Management, which affirms the obligation of every business entity to preserve the environment and prevent environmental pollution and degradation. Additionally, the adoption of sustainable business practices is further encouraged through the Financial Services Authority Regulation (POJK) No. 51/POJK.03/2017 concerning the Implementation of Sustainable Finance for Financial Services Institutions, Issuers, and Public Companies, which encourages the Company to integrate environmental, social, and governance aspects into their business strategies and sustainability reporting.
