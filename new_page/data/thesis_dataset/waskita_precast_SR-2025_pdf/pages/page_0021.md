### Environmental Aspect Performance

In conducting its operational activities, WSBP is committed to implementing environmentally-friendly business practices through the application of Green Industry principles across all production processes. This commitment is realized through various initiatives, including the implementation of Green Label Products, the strengthening of energy management, and the execution of integrated environmental management encompassing hazardous and toxic waste (B3) management, wastewater management, and non-B3 solid waste management. In addition, the Company also conducts carbon footprint calculations, environmental quality measurements, and periodic environmental management reporting in accordance with applicable environmental documents, as part of its efforts to ensure compliance with environmental regulations.

WSBP's commitment to environmental preservation is also demonstrated through the development of various more environmentally friendly concrete products. These products are designed not only to meet construction strength and quality standards, but also to support more sustainable infrastructure development. Among the products produced are Spun Pile, CCSP, Readymix, PC-I Girder, and Full Slab, which have been utilized in various infrastructure projects such as toll roads, flyovers, and other infrastructure development works.

In addition to product innovation, the Company also optimizes the use of more environmentally friendly materials in its production processes.
