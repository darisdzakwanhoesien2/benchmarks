Tata Kodak Persaikasan Berkelanjutan
Sustainable Corporate Governance

Komitmen Perusahaan terhadap pelestarian lingkungan juga diwujudkan melalui berbagai program penghijauan. Program ini dilakukan WSBP melalui TJSL WSBP Inspiring Kindness: Piles of Sustainability. Upaya Perusahaan menjaga keberlanjutan ekosistem lingkungan membawa Perusahaan meraih sejumlah penghargaan. Precast Plant WSBP Karawang, Precast Plant WSBP Subang, dan Precast Plant WSBP Prambon memperoleh piagam penghargaan Standar Industri Hijau (SIH) dari Kementerian Perindustrian. Prestasi ini menandakan efisiensi energi serta penerapan proses ramah lingkungan area produksi yang dijalankan Perusahaan.

Komitmen WSBP yang memprioritaskan aspek lingkungan dalam kegiatan bisnisnya juga diwujudkan melalui produksi beton yang ramah lingkungan. Produk Beton Readymix WSBP dari Batching Plant WSBP Pegangsaan resmi meraih sertifikasi Green Label Indonesia (GLI) 2025 Level Gold. Pencapaian tersebut menjadikan WSBP sebagai produsen pertama dan satu-satunya di Indonesia yang meraih pengakuan tertinggi untuk kategori ini.

Verifikasi dilakukan oleh lembaga internasional IAPMO, dengan audit menyeluruh untuk Batching Plant WSBP Pegangsaan. Produk WSBP dinyatakan lolos dari seluruh aspek penilaian: efisiensi energi, pengurangan emisi, manajemen limbah, dan inovasi proses.

Beton Readymix bersertifikasi Green Label ini telah digunakan dalam proyek strategis LRT Jakarta Fase 1B: Velodrome-Manggarai, membuktikan bahwa mutu dan keberlanjutan bisa berjalan berdampingan.

Dengan mengintegrasikan prinsip keberlanjutan ke dalam strategi Perusahaan, WSBP tidak hanya memenuhi kewajiban regulasi lingkungan nasional dan global, namun juga berkontribusi program pemerintah mewujudkan target Net Zero Emission 2060.

Komitmen dan Kebijakan Pengelolaan Lingkungan Dalam melakukan pengelolaan lingkungan, WSBP mengacu pada berbagai peraturan perundang-undangan beserta aturan-aturan pelaksanaannya di antaranya: [GRI 3-3]

1. Undang-Undang Republik Indonesia No. 6 Tahun 2023 tentang Penetapan Peraturan Pemerintah Pengganti Undang-Undang No. 2 Tahun 2022 tentang Cipta Kerja.

The Company's commitment to environmental conservation is also demonstrated through various greening programs. These initiatives are carried out by WSBP under its CSR program, WSBP Inspiring Kindness: Piles of Sustainability. The Company's efforts to maintain ecosystem sustainability have earned it several awards. WSBP's Precast Plants in Karawang, Subang, and Prambon have received the Green Industry Standard (SIH) certificate from the Ministry of Industry. This achievement reflects the Company's energy efficiency and the implementation of environmentally friendly processes in its production areas.

WSBP's commitment to prioritizing environmental aspects in its business activities is further demonstrated through the production of environmentally friendly concrete. WSBP's Readymix Concrete product from Batching Plant WSBP Pegangsaan has officially received the Green Label Indonesia (GLI) 2025 Gold Level certification, making WSBP the first and only producer in Indonesia to attain the highest recognition in this category.

The verification was conducted by the international institution IAPMO, with a comprehensive audit of WSBP's Pegangsaan Batching Plant. WSBP's products were confirmed to have passed all assessment aspects, including energy efficiency, emission reduction, waste management, and process innovation.

This Green Label-certified Readymix concrete has been utilized in the strategic LRT Jakarta Phase 1B: Velodrome-Manggarai project, demonstrating that quality and sustainability can go hand in hand.

By integrating sustainability principles into its corporate strategy, WSBP not only meets national and global environmental regulatory obligations but also contributes to the government's program to achieve the 2060 Net Zero Emission target.

1. Law of the Republic of Indonesia No. 6 of 2023 concerning the Stipulation of Government Regulation in Lieu of Law No. 2 of 2022 concerning Job Creation.
