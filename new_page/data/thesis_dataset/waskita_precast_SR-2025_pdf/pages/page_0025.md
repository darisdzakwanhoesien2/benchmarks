# STRATEGI PENCAPAIAN TARGET

Untuk mencapai target kinerja keberlanjutan, WSBP menerapkan strategi yang terintegrasi dengan rencana bisnis Perusahaan serta pelaksanaan Business Resilience &amp; Growth Program. Strategi tersebut dijalankan melalui penguatan tata kelola, efektivitas operasional, inovasi produk, serta pengelolaan dampak lingkungan secara terukur di seluruh lini operasi. Strategi pencapaian target keberlanjutan WSBP dilaksanakan melalui beberapa langkah utama sebagai berikut:

1. Integrasi Keberlanjutan ke dalam Proses Bisnis dan Pengambilan Keputusan

Perusahaan mengintegrasikan aspek keberlanjutan ke dalam perencanaan, penganggaran, dan pelaksanaan operasional, sehingga target lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola menjadi bagian dari pengelolaan kinerja.

2. Efisiensi Proses Produksi Berbasis Lean dan Pengendalian Pemborosan (Waste)

Perusahaan mengoptimalkan proses produksi untuk meningkatkan produktivitas dan efisiensi biaya sekaligus menurunkan pemborosan material, energi, dan waktu proses. Pendekatan ini mendukung konsistensi mutu, ketepatan waktu pemenuhan pesanan, serta pengurangan dampak lingkungan dari aktivitas produksi.

# TARGET ACHIEVEMENT STRATEGY

To achieve its sustainability performance targets, WSBP implements a strategy that is integrated with the Company's business plan and the execution of the Business Resilience &amp; Growth Program. This strategy is carried out through the strengthening of governance, operational effectiveness, product innovation, and the measurable management of environmental impacts across all lines of operation. WSBP's sustainability target achievement strategy is implemented through the following key steps:

1. Integration of Sustainability into Business Processes and Decision-Making

The Company integrates sustainability aspects into planning, budgeting, and operational execution, ensuring that environmental, social, and governance targets become an integral part of performance management.

2. Lean-Based Production Process Efficiency and Waste Control

The Company optimizes its production processes to improve productivity and cost efficiency while simultaneously reducing material, energy, and process time waste. This approach supports quality consistency, order fulfillment timeliness, and the reduction of environmental impacts from production activities.
