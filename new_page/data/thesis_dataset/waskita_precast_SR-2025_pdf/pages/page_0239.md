# Persyaratan Kualitas Bahan Baku Utama

Perusahaan menerapkan prinsip kehat-hatian dan pengendalian mutu yang tinggi dalam memilih dan menyediakan bahan baku. Upaya ini dilakukan untuk menjamin kualitas produksi yang dihasilkan sesuai dengan standard yang berlaku sehingga bisa memenuhi harapan konsumen/pelanggan. Beberapa persyaratan kualitas bahan baku utama yang diterapkan oleh Perusahaan antara lain:

1. Semen

Jenis semen yang digunakan sebagai bahan baku pembuatan beton disesuaikan dengan karakteristik dari tipe beton dengan kualitas semen yang distandardisasi di Indonesia.

2. Air

Persyaratan penggunaan air untuk proses produksi beton harus memenuhi Persyaratan Umum Bahan Bangunan di Indonesia, antara lain:

a. Air harus bersih;
b. Tidak mengandung lumpur, minyak, dan benda terapung lainnya yang dapat dilihat secara visual;

The Company endeavors to establish cooperation with suppliers or third parties to procure production raw materials. Supplier or third-party partners must meet specific requirements, including that the managed location/land complies with environmental impact analysis regulations and holds an Production Business License (IUP).

In addition to collaborating with third parties, WSBP meets its raw material needs through its own quarries located in Bojonegara, Cilegon, Banten Province, and Lumbang, East Java. The procurement of goods and services at WSBP refers to the latest policy dated January 28, 2022, as stipulated in the President Director's Decree No. 03.ISO9001.2022.01 concerning the Procurement Policy of PT Waskita Beton Precast Tbk. This policy includes provisions that prioritize the use of domestically produced goods, national design and engineering, and the expansion of opportunities for small enterprises, provided that quality, price, and purpose can be justified.

# Quality Requirements for Main Raw Materials

The Company applies the principles of prudence and strict quality control in selecting and providing raw materials. This effort is undertaken to guarantee that the quality of the produced output complies with applicable standards, thereby meeting customer expectations. Some of the main raw material quality requirements implemented by the Company include:

1. Cement

The type of cement used as a raw material for concrete production is adjusted to the characteristics of the concrete type, with cement quality standardized in Indonesia.

2. Water

The requirements for water usage in the concrete production process must comply with the General Requirements for Building Materials in Indonesia, including:
