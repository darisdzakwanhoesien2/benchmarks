## PENGEMBANGAN KOMPETENSI PEGAWAI [OJK F.22]

Sebagai bagian dari strategi meningkatkan produktivitas dan daya saing, WSBP berkomitmen untuk terus mengembangkan kompetensi Insan WSBP. Perusahaan menyelenggarakan pengembangan kompetensi melalui berbagai program pelatihan, baik yang bersifat teknis maupun manajerial, untuk memastikan setiap pegawai memiliki keahlian dan pengetahuan yang relevan dengan tuntutan industri konstruksi yang dinamis. Selain itu, WSBP juga menyediakan peluang untuk mengikuti sertifikasi profesional guna mendukung karier jangka panjang pegawai.

Program pengembangan pegawai yang dijalankan Perusahaan sejalan dengan Manpower Planning dan dirancang secara berkelanjutan sehingga Insan WSBP memiliki competitive advantage yang mampu mendorong tercapainya target-target yang ditetapkan dalam RKAP. Program pengembangan kompetensi dan talent WSBP terbagi menjadi 5 (lima) program, yaitu Program Pendidikan, Program Sertifikasi, Program Pelatihan, Program Pengembangan, dan Program Orientasi.

## PROGRAM PENDIDIKAN

Merupakan kegiatan pembelajaran untuk meningkatkan kompetensi Pegawai melalui pendidikan bergelar (S1, S2 dan S3) di dalam maupun di luar negeri dengan beasiswa dari Perusahaan, sponsorship, maupun biaya sendiri.

1. Tugas Belajar

a. Pendidikan bergelar S2 di dalam negeri, beasiswa dari Perusahaan dan dilakukan di luar waktu kerja.

Furthermore, the policy on clear working hours, including regulations on overtime, is in line with Law of the Republic of Indonesia No. 19 of 1999 concerning Ratification of ILO Convention No. 105 Concerning The Abolition of Forced Labour. [GRI 3-3]

WSBP's seriousness in implementing clear minimum age and working hour restrictions has resulted in zero incidents/ findings related to child labor and forced labor practices throughout 2025. Consequently, WSBP has not received any fines/sanctions for non-compliance with regulations concerning child labor and forced labor. [GRI 2-27]

## EMPLOYEE COMPETENCY DEVELOPMENT [OJK F.22]

As part of the strategy to enhance productivity and competitiveness, WSBP is committed to continuously developing the competencies of Insan WSBP. The Company conducts competency development through various training programs, both technical and managerial, to ensure every employee possesses the skills and knowledge relevant to the demands of the dynamic construction industry. Furthermore, WSBP also provides opportunities for employees to participate in professional certifications to support employees' long-term careers.

The employee development programs run by the Company are aligned with Manpower Planning and designed sustainably so that Insan WSBP have a competitive advantage capable of driving the achievement of targets set in the RKAP. WSBP's competency and talent development programs are divided into 5 (five) programs: the Education Program, Certification Program, Training Program, Development Program, and Orientation Program.

## EDUCATION PROGRAM

This is a learning activity to enhance employee competencies through degree-granting education (Bachelor's, Master's, and Doctoral) domestically or abroad with Company scholarships, sponsorship, or self-funding.

1. Study Assignment

a. Master's degree education domestically, with scholarship from the Company, conducted outside working hours.
