# SIKLUS, PERIODE DAN CAKUPAN LAPORAN [GRI 2-2, 2-3]

Sebagai bentuk kepatuhan terhadap regulasi, WSBP menerbitkan Laporan Keberlanjutan setiap tahun, bersamaan dengan Laporan Keuangan dan Laporan Tahunan Perusahaan, yakni 1 Januari 2025 sampai dengan 31 Desember 2025. Laporan ini memuat aktivitas bisnis Kantor Pusat dan seluruh Unit Operational (Precast Plant, Batching Plant, Quarry, Workshop), Proyek, dan Area Penjualan milik WSBP yang tersebar di beberapa area di Indonesia. Laporan ini mengungkapkan kinerja ekonomi, sosial dan lingkungan sebagai bagian dari komitmen terhadap keberlanjutan.

# PENENTUAN TOPIK MATERIAL [GRI 3-1, 3-2, 3-3]

Pada Laporan Keberlanjutan 2025 tidak terdapat perubahan terkait topik material dari tahun sebelumnya. Perusahaan telah melakukan peninjauan topik material melalui proses identifikasi dan menilai dampak secara berkesinambungan. Dari proses tersebut, Perusahaan memastikan bahwa topik material dalam laporan ini mewakili dampak paling signifikan selama tahun pelaporan. Peninjauan dilakukan melalui diskusi secara online pada 14 Januari 2026 yang diikuti oleh pemangku kepentingan internal WSBP, yaitu manajemen dan Insan WSBP lintas divisi, termasuk para penanggung jawab penyusunan laporan. [GRI 3-1, 3-2, 3-3]

# REPORT CYCLE, PERIOD, AND SCOPE [GRI 2-2, 2-3]

As a form of compliance with regulations, WSBP is publishing a Sustainability Report annually, simultaneously with the Company's Financial Statements and Annual Report, during the period of January 1-December 31, 2025. This report covers all operational activities of the Head Office and all Operational Units (Precast Plant, Batching Plant, Quarry, Workshop), Projects and Sales Areas owned by WSBP, which are spread in several areas in Indonesia. This report reveals economic, social and environmental performance as part of the commitment to sustainability.

# DETERMINATION OF MATERIAL TOPIC [GRI 3-1, 3-2, 3-3]

In the 2025 Sustainability Report, there were no changes regarding material topics compared to the previous year. The Company has reviewed the material topics by identifying and assessing impacts on an ongoing basis. From that process, the Company ensures that the material topics within this report represent the most significant impact during the reporting year. The reviewing process was conducted through an online discussion in January 14, 2026, and attended by internal WSBP stakeholders, namely management and Insan WSBP across divisions, including those responsible for preparing the report. [GRI 3-1, 3-2, 3-3]
