## b. Internal Audit

## c. Tata Kelola Terintegrasi

i) Melakukan evaluasi dan persetujuan kebijakan Tata Kelola Terintegrasi;
ii) Mengawasi penerapan Tata Kelola Terintegrasi pada Perusahaan agar selaras dengan kebijakan Manajemen Risiko PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk sebagai Induk Perusahaan;
iii) Mengawasi pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab Direksi Perusahaan, serta memberikan arahan atau nasihat kepada Direksi Perusahaan atas pelaksanaan kebijakan Tata Kelola Terintegrasi;

iii) Supervising and providing advice on the implementation of Risk Management functions in accordance with laws and regulations, the Company's Articles of Association, and/or GMS decisions.

## b. Internal Audit

i) Ensure that the Company's Board of Directors has an Internal Audit Division that carries out the Internal Audit function;
ii) Approve the appointment and dismissal of the Vice President of Internal Audit proposed by the Board of Directors;
iii) Ensure that the Internal Audit Division has access to information and/or data regarding the State-Owned Enterprise (BUMN) necessary to carry out its duties;
iv) Approve the Internal Audit Charter proposed by the Board of Directors, taking into account the proposals from the Internal Audit Division;
v) Review the effectiveness and efficiency of the Internal Control System based on information obtained from the Internal Audit Division at least once a year;
vi) Appoint a professional external quality control officer to conduct a quality assurance review of the Internal Audit performance at least once every three years; and
vii) Supervise and provide advice on the implementation of other Internal Audit functions in accordance with statutory provisions, the Company's Articles of Association, and/or GMS decisions.
