# Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi

Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi dibentuk sesuai ketentuan POJK No. 34/POJK.04/2014. Pembentukan komite ditetapkan melalui Surat Keputusan Dewan Komisaris No. 11/SK/WBP/DK/2023 tanggal 12 Oktober 2023, yang memuat pengangkatan ketua dan anggota, susunan komite, serta penetapan honorarium dan tunjangan.

Pada tahun 2025, Dewan Komisaris melakukan pembaruan komposisi Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi melalui Surat Keputusan No. 14/SK/WBP/DK/2025 tanggal 22 Desember 2025, mengenai pemberhentian dan pengangkatan anggota serta penetapan susunan Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi yang baru.

# Komite Pemantau Risiko dan Tata Kelola Terintegrasi

Berdasarkan Peraturan Menteri BUMN No. PER-3/MBU/03/2023 tentang Organ dan Sumber Daya Manusia Badan Usaha Milik Negara, Komite Pemantau Risiko dan Tata Kelola Terintegrasi dibentuk untuk memperkuat pengawasan Dewan Komisaris terhadap pelaksanaan manajemen risiko dan penerapan tata kelola secara terintegrasi. Komite ini merupakan pengembangan dari Komite Pemantau Manajemen Risiko.

Perubahan nomenklatur dan susunan komite ditetapkan melalui Surat Keputusan Dewan Komisaris No. 10/SK/WBP/DK/2023 tanggal 12 Oktober 2023. Selanjutnya, Dewan Komisaris kembali memperbarui komposisi komite melalui Surat Keputusan Dewan Komisaris No. 13/SK/WBP/DK/2025 tanggal 22 Desember 2025, yang mencakup pemberhentian dan pengangkatan ketua serta anggota komite.

Komite ini bertugas memberikan rekomendasi kepada Dewan Komisaris terkait efektivitas pelaksanaan kebijakan manajemen risiko dan kualitas pengawasan tata kelola perusahaan.

# DIREKSI [GRI 2-9]

Direksi adalah Organ Perusahaan yang memiliki wewenang dan tanggung jawab terhadap kepengurusan Perusahaan untuk kepentingan Perusahaan, sesuai dengan maksud dan tujuan Perusahaan serta mewakili Perusahaan, baik di dalam maupun di luar pengadilan sesuai dengan ketentuan Anggaran Dasar Perusahaan.

# Nomination and Remuneration Committee

The Nomination and Remuneration Committee was formed in accordance with OJK Regulation No. 34/POJK.04/2014. The committee's establishment was formalized by Board of Commissioners Decree No. 11/SK/WBP/DK/2023 dated October 12, 2023, detailing the appointment of the chair and members, the committee's composition, and the honorarium and benefits settlements.

In 2025, the Board of Commissioners updated the composition of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee through Decree No. 14/SK/WBP/DK/2025 dated December 22, 2025, concerning the dismissal and appointment of members and the establishment of the new committee structure.

Integrated Risk Monitoring and Governance Committee Based on Minister of State-Owned Enterprises Regulation No. PER-3/MBU/03/2023 on Organization and Human Resources of State-Owned Enterprises, the Integrated Risk Monitoring and Governance Committee was formed to strengthen the Board of Commissioners' oversight of risk management and the implementation of integrated governance. This committee is a development of the former Risk Management Monitoring Committee.

Changes in the nomenclature and structure of the committee were formalized through Board of Commissioners Decree No. 10/SK/WBP/DK/2023 dated October 12, 2023. Subsequently, the Board of Commissioners updated the committee's composition through Decree of the Board of Commissioners No. 13/SK/WBP/DK/2025 dated December 22, 2025, which included the dismissal and appointment of the chair and committee members.

This committee is tasked with providing recommendations to the Board of Commissioners regarding the effectiveness of risk management policies and the quality of corporate governance oversight.

# BOARD OF DIRECTORS [GRI 2-9]

The Board of Directors is the Company's organ with the authority and responsibility to manage the Company for the benefit of the Company, in accordance with the Company's purposes and objectives, and to represent the Company both inside and outside the courts, in accordance with the provisions of the Company's Articles of Association.
