Based on Regulation of the Financial Services Authority (OJK) Number 12/POJK.03/2021 dated 30 July 2021 on Commercial Banks, there is a simplification of network recording with the OJK Regulator. The categorization of bank offices under branch offices is simplified into sub-branch offices and functional offices. To that end, the company equalizes the status of cash offices to sub-branch offices and payment points to functional offices.
