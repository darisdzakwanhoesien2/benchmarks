# Customer Complaints Mechanism

The company realizes that resolving consumer complaints quickly and accurately is part of service excellence. To that end, the Company has established Special Policy No. KK9-K Dated 29 November 2022 on Customer Complaints. Guided by these regulations, the company opens customer complaint channels in all bank work units. Customers can access it anytime and anywhere, regardless of the office where the customer first registered. # Complaint Flow

The following is the flow of handling customer complaints at BTN

1. Customers File Complaints
Customers file complaints via the channels provided by BTN.

2. Verification of Customer Data
BTN verifies customer data in accordance with the established procedures.

3. Customer Gets Complaint Number
BTN receives and records and provides complaint ticket numbers to customers.

4. Follow-up Process by BTN
BTN will follow up and resolve customer complaints within the specified time.

5. Complaint Process Resolved
If the customer agrees with the results of the settlement, the complaint is considered resolved.

6. The Customer is Dissatisfied and Proceeds LAPS SIK
Customers who do not agree with the settlement results can continue the process at the Alternative Financial Services Sector Dispute Resolution Institution (LAPS SJK)
