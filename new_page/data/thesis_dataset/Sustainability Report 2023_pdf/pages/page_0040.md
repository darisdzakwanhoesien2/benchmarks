# Mission

- To actively support the government in advancing the welfare of the Indonesian people through home ownership;
- To realize the life that millions of Indonesians dream of by providing decent housing;
- To become One of the Home of Indonesia's Best Talent;
- To increase shareholder value by focusing on sustainable profitability growth as a blue-chip company with solid risk management principles; and
- To become a financial partner for stakeholders in the housing ecosystem by providing comprehensive solutions and the best services through digital innovation.

# Review of Vision and Mission by the BoC and the BoD

The company periodically reviews its vision and mission by considering changes in internal and external environmental conditions. The results of the vision and mission review by the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors carried out in 2021 and the recommendations from McKinsey stated that the formulation of the new vision and mission was in accordance with changes in internal and external environmental conditions. The new vision and mission have been established in the company's Corporate Plan 2021—2025 and have been outlined in the bank's Business Plan 2022—2025.
