# Career Development

The company provides various paths in managing employee career development. Each career path offers different levels of advancement and opportunities. Career path mapping is carried out based on an evaluation of the talents and potential of each employee. Each employee is identified in a potential matrix formulated by considering aspects of performance and potential, as shown below:

![img-119.jpeg](img-119.jpeg)

In addition to potential mapping, the company also implements a Job Posting program, which is a process of openly searching for candidates for certain positions. The aim of this process is to provide extensive opportunities for employees to develop their respective careers.

![img-120.jpeg](img-120.jpeg)

# ANNOUNCEMENT

The company will give an announcement to all employees regarding vacancies for certain positions, accompanied by the criteria for the desired candidates.

![img-121.jpeg](img-121.jpeg)

# SELECTION

The company carries out a selection process based on suitability of position criteria with the applicant. Selection is on-desk or face-to-face (interviews, simulations, etc.)

![img-122.jpeg](img-122.jpeg)

# DECISION

The Board of Directors will decide on the employee selected from the selection results to fill the position.

![img-123.jpeg](img-123.jpeg)
