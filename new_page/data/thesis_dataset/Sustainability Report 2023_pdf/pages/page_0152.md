# GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE [GRI 2-9, 2-10, 2-11]

The Company's governance structure follows the provisions of Law No. 40 of 2007 concerning Limited Liability Companies. This law stipulates that "the Company's organs consist of the General Meeting of Shareholders, the Board of Directors, and the Board of Commissioners." The establishment of these Company organs aims to ensure that corporate governance principles can operate effectively through clear roles and responsibilities, creating a mechanism for checks and balances. The General Meeting of Shareholders, the Board of Directors, and the Board of Commissioners work with mutual respect for their respective tasks, responsibilities, and authorities in accordance with laws and articles of association, thus creating effective corporate governance.
