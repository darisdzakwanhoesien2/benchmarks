# Risk Management Unit

Risk Management Unit, is a working unit responsible for identifying, measuring, monitoring, and controlling risks to assist the tasks and responsibilities of the Board of Directors;
Compliance Unit, is a working unit responsible for carrying out compliance function tasks and preventive measures to assist the tasks and responsibilities of the Board of Directors;
Internal Audit Unit, is a working unit responsible for ensuring internal controls to assist the tasks and responsibilities of the Board of Directors; and
Policy Unit, is a working unit responsible for managing policies and procedures to assist the tasks and responsibilities of the Board of Directors.
