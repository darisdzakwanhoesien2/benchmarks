# Kinerja Perseroan

Kami percaya dengan visi dan misi yang sejalan antara Direksi, Komisaris, dan seluruh stakeholder, Perseroan dapat mencapai kinerja terbaiknya dan mengoptimalkan potensi ekosistem digital yang dimiliki. Sejak menawarkan 156.558.200 lembar saham di Bursa Efek Indonesia (BEI) dengan kode saham WIFI, perseroan berkembang dan berfokus kepada tiga pilar usaha, yakni: pemenuhan konektivitas melalui jaringan serat optik handal dan aman, media periklanan dan hiburan yang atraktif, pengembangan teknologi, serta warehouse management.

Saat ini perseroan berfokus dalam menjalankan operasional infrastruktur fiber optik yang handal, dan berkapasitas besar di sepanjang 4.000 km jalur rel kereta di Pulau Jawa bekerjasama dengan PT Kereta Api Indonesia (Persero), yang kami harapkan dapat membantu pemenuhan kebutuhan akan konektivitas internet yang handal dan terjangkau oleh seluruh lapisan masyarakat di pulau Jawa. Selain itu perseroan juga mengoperasikan jaringan infrastruktur serat optik pada jalan provinsi serta jalan tol Pulau Jawa, sehingga keseluruhan jaringan yang dioperasikan perseroan secara mencapai 5.724 km.

Kehandalan konektivitas yang dibangun perseroan dengan mengkombinasikan jaringan serat optik pada rel kereta-jalan tol-jalan provinsi menjadi kunci utama bagi perseroan dalam memenangkan persaingan bisnis ke depan yang menghadirkan solusi konektivitas yang handal dan efisien. # Company Performance

We believe that with a vision and mission aligned between the Board of Directors, Board of Commissioners, and all stakeholders, the Company can achieve its best performance and optimize its digital ecosystem potential. Since offering 156,558,200 shares on the Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX) with the stock code WIFI, the Company has developed and focused on three business pillars: providing connectivity through reliable and secure fiber optic networks, attractive advertising and entertainment media, technology development, and warehouse management.

Currently, the Company is focused on operating a reliable and large-capacity fiber optic infrastructure along a 4,000 km railway line in Java in cooperation with PT Kereta Api Indonesia (Persero), which we hope will help meet the need for reliable and affordable internet connectivity for all layers of society in Java. In addition, the Company also operates a fiber optic infrastructure network on provincial and toll roads in Java, so that the overall network operated by the Company reaches 5,724 km.

The reliability of connectivity built by the company by combining Fiber Optic Networks on railway-highway-provincial roads is the key to winning future business competition by offering reliable and efficient connectivity solutions. This reliability of connectivity also supports the company's other business pillars such as advertising and digital media, fulfillment center management, and other digital services. Synergy between all business pillars is absolutely necessary in presenting a business model combination that can be quickly adopted with unlimited growth potential.
