Target 2023

2023 Target

Strategi ini mencakup persiapan sejumlah konsep berbasis solusi wirausaha untuk mengoptimalkan percepatan adopsi penggunaan internet sekaligus mendukung transformasi digital di Indonesia. Perseroan merancang suatu ekosistem digital terintegrasi akan mampu mempercepat transformasi digital dan mendorong potensi ekonomi di tengah pandemi, sehingga memberikan manfaat bagi masyarakat. Perseroan juga merancang ekosistem digital melalui pengembangan konsep offline-to-online aset media, serta pengembangan aplikasi smartphone yang didukung jaringan infrastruktur internet yang dijalankan secara terpadu dan mandiri.

This strategy includes the preparation of a number of entrepreneurial solution-based concepts to optimize the acceleration of internet usage adoption while supporting digital transformation in Indonesia. The Company designed an integrated digital ecosystem that will be able to accelerate digital transformation and boost economic potential amid the pandemic, thus providing benefits to society. The Company also designs digital ecosystems through the development of offline-to-online media asset concepts, as well as the development of smartphone applications supported by internet infrastructure networks that are run in an integrated and independent manner.
