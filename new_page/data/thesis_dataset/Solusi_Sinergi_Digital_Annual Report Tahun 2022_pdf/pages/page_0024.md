## Tinjauan Industri

Menurut data Asosiasi Pengusaha Jasa Internet Indonesia (APJI), pengguna internet di Indonesia terus bertumbuh dari waktu ke waktu. Jumlah pengguna internet pada tahun 2022 telah mencapai 215,6 juta pengguna atau setara 78,19% dari total populasi Indonesia yang sebanyak 275,77 juta jiwa. Jumlah pengguna internet ini meningkat 2,67% dibandingkan pada tahun 2021. Penetrasi digital diyakini masih akan berlanjut dengan pertumbuhan yang cukup agresif karena gaya hidup setelah pandemi. ## Market Review

According to data from the Indonesian Internet Service Providers Association (APJI), internet usage in Indonesia continues to grow over time. The number of internet users in 2022 has reached 215.6 million users, or equivalent to 78.19% of Indonesia's total population of 275.77 million people. This number of internet users increased by 2.67% compared to 2021. This trend will continue to increase in line with the increasing digital needs of society from year to year.

Digital penetration is believed to continue with aggressive growth due to post-pandemic lifestyle. The consumption of data by society is also increasing in line with the improving digital adoption of society. Various diverse needs of society demand better digital infrastructure utilization to achieve efficiency and effectiveness in the economy. In an effort to accelerate digital transformation, the Ministry of Communication and Information Technology collaborates with stakeholders, including various telecommunications industry players, to expand internet infrastructure.
