# Kegiatan Usaha Fulfillment Center - Warehouse Ecosystem

Business Activities: Fulfillment Center - Warehouse Ecosystem

Surge menyadari semakin pesatnya pertumbuhan jumlah UMKM di Indonesia termasuk di pulau Jawa, tidak sebanding dengan pengelolaan pendistribusiannya, sehingga Perseroan dalam memperluas bisnisnya bekerjasama dengan beberapa mitra dalam pengembangan ekosistem gudang, Surge menghadirkan solusi aplikasi online dengan internet berkecepatan tinggi untuk mendukung rantai pasokan dan sistem logistik berteknologi tinggi. Terdapat 13.800 gudang di Indonesia dan 168.000 warung yang terkoneksi dengan ekosistem digital Perseroan.

Surge realizes that the rapid growth in the number of UMKM in Indonesia, including on the island of Java, is not proportional to the management of its distribution, so that the Company in expanding its business collaborates with several partners in developing a warehouse ecosystem, present online application solutions with high-speed internet infrastructure to support high-tech supply chain and logistics systems. There were 13,800 warehouses spread throughout Indonesia and there were 168,000 kiosks connected to Company's Digital Ecosystem.
