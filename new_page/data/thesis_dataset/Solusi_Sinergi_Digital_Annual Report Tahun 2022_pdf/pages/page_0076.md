Prospek infrastruktur konektivitas di Indonesia ke depan cukup menjanjikan, upaya pemerintah untuk memperbaiki infrastruktur digital dan meningkatkan akses internet, seiring dengan tren yang berkembang menuju transformasi digital dan pertumbuhan penggunaan mobile dan e-commerce, menjadi faktor yang akan berkontribusi pada pertumbuhan bisnis konektivitas yang berkelanjutan di tanah air. Terlepas dari potensi pertumbuhannya, masih ada tantangan yang harus dihadapi di pasar fixed broadband di Indonesia. Salah satu tantangan signifikan adalah keterjangkauan layanan internet, terutama di daerah pedesaan. Pemerintah dan perusahaan swasta perlu bekerja sama untuk mengembangkan strategi agar layanan internet lebih terjangkau dan dapat diakses oleh masyarakat di seluruh pelosok tanah air.

There are currently around 11 million households in Indonesia that enjoy unlimited fast internet based on Fixed Broadband out of a total of more than 70 million households in Indonesia. This Fixed Broadband internet penetration is the lowest in Southeast Asia when compared to other neighboring countries such as Vietnam (95%), Malaysia (45%), and Thailand (59%). This shows the potential for a high increase in Fixed Broadband internet users in the coming years as people's connectivity needs and digital activities increase.

The outlook for connectivity infrastructure in Indonesia going forward is promising, as the government's efforts to improve digital infrastructure and increase internet access, along with the growing trend towards digital transformation and the growth of mobile usage and e-commerce, are factors that will contribute to the continued growth of the connectivity business in the country. Despite its growth potential, there are still challenges to be faced in the fixed broadband market in Indonesia. One significant challenge is the affordability of internet services, especially in rural areas. The government and private companies need to work together to develop strategies to make internet services more affordable and accessible to people in all corners of the country.
