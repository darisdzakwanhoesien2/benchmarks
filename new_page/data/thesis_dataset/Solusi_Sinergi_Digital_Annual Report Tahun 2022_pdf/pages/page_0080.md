Dalam rangka menjaga keberlangsungan dan perkembangan bisnis ke depan, Perseroan akan meneruskan integrasi dan sinergi yang tercipta antar unit bisnis maupun antar Entitas Anak yang akan bertujuan untuk menselaraskan terbentuknya ekosistem digital pada segala aspek bisnis Perseroan. Perseroan akan membuka berbagai peluang baru bagi pengembangan bisnis seperti, pengembangan fiberisasi menara, local internet exchange maupun Internet desa yang bekerjasama dengan berbagai perusahaan penyelenggara jasa internet. Perseroan akan mengoptimalisasi kemitraan yang sudah terjalin dan membuka peluang-peluang untuk kemitraan baru guna menegaskan posisi perusahaan sebagai enabler ekosistem digital di Indonesia.

Store, Sobat Tani, Asuransi Kita, MyVerse, as well as Free Wi-Fi Access facilities that have been spread across 89 Jabodetabek Commuterline train stations and at inter-city train stations and in various Jakarta market locations.

In order to maintain the continuity and development of the business going forward, the Company will continue the integration and synergy created between business units and between Subsidiaries which will aim to align the formation of a digital ecosystem in all aspects of the Company's business. The company will open up various new opportunities for business development such as the development of tower fiberization, local internet exchanges and village Internet in collaboration with various internet service provider companies. The company will optimize existing partnerships and open opportunities for new partnerships to emphasize the company's position as an enabler of the digital ecosystem in Indonesia.
