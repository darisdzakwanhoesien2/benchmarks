In terms of financial performance, the Board of Commissioners assessed that the Directors successfully recorded an improvement in the Company's and subsidiary entities' performance in 2022 by recording net operating revenue of Rp 461.2 billion, an increase of 18% compared to 2021, which was recorded at Rp 390.9 billion.

The Board of Commissioners always coordinates with all parties involved in the operation of the Company, especially the Directors, to optimize the performance of the Company and its human resources. This coordination is in the form of oversight of the Company's business, as well as by providing input, views and constructive criticism directly in the joint meeting of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors held every quarter.

Throughout 2022, the Board of Commissioners provided opinions, suggestions, and advice to support the implementation of the duties and responsibilities of the Directors in managing the Company, which were conveyed in coordination meetings. These meetings are held regularly every quarter to discuss the Company's business strategy and financial reports in accordance with market practices, with the aim of maintaining integrity and transparency.
