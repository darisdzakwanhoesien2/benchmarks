# Program Kepemilikan Saham
## Oleh Pegawai Dan Direksi

Program pemberian saham jatah pasti kepada pegawai oleh Perseroan, dengan mengalokasikan saham sebanyak-banyaknya 0,5% (nol koma tiga puluh satu persen) dari jumlah Saham Yang Ditawarkan dalam Penawaran Umum atau sebanyak 1.615.180 (satu juta enam ratus lima belas ribu seratus delapan puluh) lembar saham berdasarkan Surat Keputusan Direksi No. 002/SK-ESA/SSDIX/2020 tanggal 25 September 2020 tentang Program Alokasi Saham Pegawai (Employee Stock Allocation atau ESA) yang memberikan persetujuan atas persyaratan peserta dan pelaksanaan Program ESA.

# Sistem Pelaporan Pelanggaran

Sistem Pelaporan Pelanggaran, atau Whistleblowing System (WBS), adalah mekanisme yang diterapkan dalam organisasi secara mandiri dengan tujuan memberikan saluran bagi pelapor terkait pelanggaran yang terjadi di lingkup Perusahaan. Pelanggaran dapat dikategorikan secara umum, termasuk pelanggaran terhadap prinsip-prinsip GCG, nilai-nilai etika, serta peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku.

Sistem WBS Perseroan telah dibentuk dan saat ini pelaporan dapat melalui email whistleblower@surge.co.id yang tercantum di website perseroan. Sekretaris Perusahaan, yang akan menangani whistleblowing, melapor secara berkala kepada Komite Audit dan segera menginformasikan kepada Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris mengenai kasus-kasus yang serius.

# Stock Ownership Program by Employees And Directors

The program for the provision of fixed allotment shares to employees by the Company, by allocating a maximum of 0.5% (zero point thirty-one percent) of the total Shares Offered in a Public Offering, or a maximum of 1.615.180 (one million six hundred fifteen thousand one hundred eighty) shares based on the Decree of the Board of Directors No. 002 / SK-ESA / SSDIX / 2020 dated 25 September 2020 concerning the Employee Stock Allocation (ESA) Program which provides approval for participant requirements and the implementation of the ESA Program.

# Violation Reporting System

The Whistleblowing System (WBS) is a mechanism that is implemented independently within the organization with the purpose of providing a medium for reporting related violations that occur within the Company. Violations can be categorized in general, including violations of GCG principles, ethical values, and prevailing laws and regulations.

The Company's WBS system has been established and currently reporting can be through email whistleblower@surge.co.id listed on Company's Website. The Corporate Secretary, which handles the Board of Commissioners about any severe cases.
