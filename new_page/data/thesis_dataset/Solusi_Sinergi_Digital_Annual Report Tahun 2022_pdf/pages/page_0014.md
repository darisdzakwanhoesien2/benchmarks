# Ikhtisar Saham

Perseroan tercatat di Bursa Efek Indonesia pada tanggal 30 Desember 2020 dengan kode WIFI. Sepanjang tahun 2022 tercatat rata-rata volume perdagangan saham harian sebesar 137.795.993 lembar saham dengan harga saham penutupan pada 30 Desember 2022 sebesar Rp. 130 / lembar saham. Sementara total saham yang beredar sampai dengan 31 Desember 2022 adalah sejumlah 2.255.321.287 lembar saham dengan total kapitalisasi pasar sebesar Rp. 293.191.767.310

The company was listed in Indonesia Stock Exchange with ticker code WIFI on December 30, 2020. In 2022, the average of daily trading volume of shares traded was recorded at 137.795.993 shares with the closing price (31 December 2022) at IDR 130 per share. Meanwhile, the total shares available until 31 December 2022 was recorded at 2.255.321.287 shares with a total market capitalization of IDR 293.191.767.310.
