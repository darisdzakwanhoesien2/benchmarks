# Sanksi Administratif

Perusahaan dan entitas anak sepanjang tahun 2022 tidak dikenakan sanksi administratif yang material.

# Budaya Perseroan

Perseroan tidak secara khusus mendefinisikan Budaya Perseroan. Nilai-nilai Perseroan dan Kode etik Perseroan dianggap telah merangkum dan mengatur etika berbisnis dan berkomunikasi di dalam Perseroan dengan cukup jelas.

# Kode Etik Perseroan

Perseroan menyadari pentingnya menjaga reputasi dalam menjalankan bisnisnya yang terbentuk melalui komitmen Manajemen dan Karyawan dalam berperilaku dan bertindak sesuai dengan etika dan budaya Perseroan dengan tetap memperhatikan hukum dan peraturan yang berlaku. Untuk itu, Perseroan telah memiliki Kode Etik sebagai panduan bagi seluruh Manajemen dan Karyawan dalam bersikap dan berperilaku sesuai norma-norma yang berlaku.

Kode Etik memandu cara kami menyeimbangkan kepentingan perseroan, pemegang saham, karyawan, klien, mitra bisnis dan masyarakat umum.

# Administrative Sanctions

The company and its subsidiaries during 2022 were not subject to any material administrative sanctions.

# Company Culture

The Company's values and code of conduct are considered to have summarized and clearly governed the business ethics and communication within the Company.

# Company Code Of Conduct

The Company acknowledges the importance of maintaining its reputation in running its business, which is formed through the commitment of Management and Employees to behave and act in accordance with the Company's Code of Conduct and culture while still paying attention to prevailing laws and regulations. For this reason, the Company has a Code of Conduct as a guide for all Management and Employees in behaving in compliance with prevailing norms.

The Code guides the way in which surge balances the interests of the Company, Shareholders, Employees, Client, Business Partners and general public.
