# Kegiatan Usaha Jaringan Serat Optik

# Optical Fiber Network Business Activities

Infrastruktur jaringan Serat Optik menjadi pendorong utama kinerja Surge, sejak 2019 telah berkerjasama dengan PT. KAI (Persero) melalui Perusahan anak, yaitu "Weave", menggelar, mengoperasikan dan mengelola jaringan serat optik yang dipasang sepanjang jalur kereta di pulau Jawa, berkat kemampuan kami untuk meningkatkan pengembangan produk dan layanan di tengah percepatan penerapan transformasi digital yang terus berkelanjutan, Surge berhasil mencatat total serat optik yang dibangun serta dioperasikan sebanyak 5.237 Km jaringan serat optik yang mengkombinasikan jalur kereta api, jalan tol, dan jalan raya pada akhir tahun 2022.

Fiber Optic network infrastructure became the main driver of Surge's performance, since 2019 it has been working with PT. KAI (Persero) through a subsidiary company is "Weave", deploying, operating and managing fiber optic networks installed along the train line on the Java island, as we managed to improve product and service development in the midst of accelerating the implementation of digital transformation that continues to be sustainable, Surge managed to record a total of 5,237 Km of fiber optic networks built and operated combining railways, toll roads and highways by the end of 2022.

# Kegiatan Usaha Periklanan

# Advertising Business Activities

Sejak tahun 2018, Perseroan dan Perusahaan Anak, yaitu IMT dan AMI, menyediakan jasa periklanan dalam bentuk Digital Out-of-home Advertising dan digital advertising, baik di kereta api dan fasilitasnya (railway media) maupun di luar kereta api dan fasilitasnya (non-rail media). Dalam memperluas kegiatan usaha periklanan, Perseroan pada tahun 2022 bekerjasama dengan perusahaan transportasi umum berbasis bus untuk mengelola pengembangan bisnis dan menjalankan kegiatan periklanan seperti bus branding, coverageat dan kegiatan jasa iklan lainnya.

Since 2018, the Company and its subsidiaries, are IMT and AMI, provide advertising services in the form of Digital Out-of-home Advertising and digital advertising, both on trains and their facilities (railway media) and outside trains and their facilities (non-rail media). In expanding advertising business activities, the Company in 2022 established cooperation with bus-based public transportation companies to manage business development and carry out advertising activities such as bus branding, coverage at and other advertising service activities.
