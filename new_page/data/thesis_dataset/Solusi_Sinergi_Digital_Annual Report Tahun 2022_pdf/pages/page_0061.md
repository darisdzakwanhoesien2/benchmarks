# Makro Ekonomi

Perekonomian Indonesia tumbuh kuat di tengah risiko perlambatan ekonomi global. Keberlanjutan pemulihan ekonomi serta peran APBN juga terefleksi pada peningkatan kesejahteraan masyarakat. Pemulihan ekonomi yang masih terpantau kuat didukung oleh pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia pada triwulan III 2022 tercatat sebesar 5,7 persen (yoy), dan permintaan domestik yang kuat (tumbuh 5,4 persen yoy) serta investasi (tumbuh 5,0 persen yoy). Kinerja perekonomian tersebut juga turut didukung oleh tingkat inflasi yang relatif terjaga sebesar 5,4 persen yoy pada bulan November 2022, serta tingkat pengangguran dan kemiskinan menurun ke 5,86 persen dan 9,54 persen. Hingga sepanjang tahun 2022, pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia tercatat mencapai 5.0 persen (yoy), kepada tumbuh 5,3 persen.

Untuk penyelesaian masalah-masalah ekonomi global, Pemerintah mengadakan forum G20 dimana akselerasi transformasi digital menjadi salah satu prioritas di Indonesia. Ekonomi digital menjadi pilar transformasi digital yang menjadi penggerak baru pemulihan ekonomi global.

T Indonesia's economy is growing strong amid the risks of a global economic slowdown. The sustainability of economic recovery and the role of the State Budget are also reflected in improving people's welfare. The strong observed economic recovery is supported by Indonesia's economic growth in the third quarter of 2022 which was recorded at 5.7 percent (yoy), and strong domestic demand (growing 5.4 percent yoy) and investment (growing 5.0 percent yoy). This economic performance was also supported by the inflation rate which was relatively maintained at 5.4 percent yoy in November 2022, and the unemployment and poverty rates decreased to 5.86 percent and 9.54 percent. Until 2022, Indonesia's economic growth was recorded at 5.0 percent (yoy), or grew by 5.3 percent.

To resolve global economic problems, the Government held the G20 forum where accelerating digital transformation is one of Indonesia's priorities. The digital economy is a pillar of digital transformation which is a new driver of global economic recovery.
