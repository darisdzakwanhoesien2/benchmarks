# Penerapan Strategi di 2023

Fokus Perseroan untuk tahun 2023 adalah optimalisasi ekosistem digital dalam mencapai pertumbuhan yang berkelanjutan. Dengan beroperasinya jaringan serat optik Java Backbone secara penuh, perseroan berfokus untuk menghadirkan konektivitas yang handal dan terjangkau, Perseroan bekerjasama dengan berbagai stakeholder di industri telekomunikasi seperti Internet Service Provider (ISP), Cloud Provider, Tower Company, Telco Provider, maupun lainnya.

# Strategy Implementation in 2023

The focus of the company for 2023 is to optimize its digital ecosystem to achieve sustainable growth. The company aims to provide reliable and affordable connectivity by fully operating its Java Backbone fiber optic network, while also collaborating with various stakeholders in the telecommunications industry such as ISPs, cloud providers, tower companies, telco providers, and others.

The company has prepared several business models to accelerate the adoption of connectivity services and the development of the digital ecosystem in Indonesia.

# Business Outlook

The prospects of the digital economy in Indonesia are expected to be significant, especially with the recovery of economic activities post-pandemic and the shift in people's lifestyles from offline to online and vice versa. The fixed broadband internet market in Indonesia has a great potential to grow and develop in the future, given the high demand for digital services. The current fixed broadband penetration rate in Indonesia is around 15%, which is relatively low compared to other Southeast Asian countries.
