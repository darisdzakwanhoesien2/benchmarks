# PUBLIC EXPOSE

The Company is committed to managing its business in a transparent and professional manner, one of which is by providing an open dialog space through public expose activities for shareholders, the public, and the media. This step is a form of the Company's compliance with the Listing Rules of the Indonesia Stock Exchange No. I-E regarding Obligations to Submit Information, as well as IDX Circular Letter No. SE-00003/BEI/05-2020 which regulates the procedures for implementing Public Expose electronically.
