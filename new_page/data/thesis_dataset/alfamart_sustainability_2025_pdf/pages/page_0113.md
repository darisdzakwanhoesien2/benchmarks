# Investment of Capital Expenditure

Sepanjang tahun 2024, Perseroan dan Entitas Anak telah merealisasikan belanja modal sebesar Rp5.067.144 juta. Pengeluaran untuk belanja modal tersebut bertujuan untuk penambahan jumlah gerai, pengembangan gudang dan kantor cabang. Adapun dana yang digunakan untuk merealisasikan belanja modal tersebut berasal dari kas internal perusahaan.

Throughout 2024, the Company and Subsidiaries made capital expenditures amounted to Rp5,067,144 million. The purpose of the capital expenditures were intended to increase the number of stores, develop warehouses, and branch offices. The funds used in the capital expenditures were mainly from the Company's internal cash.
