Kinerja Lingkungan Environmental Performance

Perusahaan mengimplementasikan kinerja lingkungan melalui upaya penjagaan terhadap ekosistem di sekitar wilayah operasional serta meminimalisasi dampak buruk aktivitas operasi terhadap habitat flora dan fauna di wilayah operasional. [GRI 304-2] Komitmen Perseroan atas hal tersebut antara lain terwujud melalui kegiatan konservasi terumbu karang yang berada di sekitar operasi pelabuhan bongkar muat batu bara, yaitu pelabuhan North Pulau Laut Coal Terminal (NPLCT).

Pada site Sebuku dilakukan upaya konservasi hayati melalui program revegetasi. [GRI 304-3] Melalui program tersebut, Perseroan melalui PT Bahari Cakrawala Sebuku (BCS) ikut berkontribusi bagi penjagaan terhadap kondisi vegetasi yang terpelihara guna menghasilkan kondisi yang baik, erosi yang dapat dicegah, kondisi lahan bekas tambang yang secara menyeluruh telah diperbaiki, hingga turun andil dalam proses pengurangan gas karbon melalui penghijauan.

Perusahaan melalui MP bersama Al telah membangun rehabilitasi lahan Daerah Aliran Sungai (DAS). Melalui upaya ini, Perseroan bersama Al melakukan penanaman di area-area kritis kawasan hutan dalam rangka rehabilitasi DAS serta rehabilitasi di luar area operasional penambangan. Area rehabilitasi DAS tersebut berlokasi di Kabupaten Banjar, Tanah Laut dan Tanah Bumbu Provinsi Kalimantan Selatan.

Selain itu, Perusahaan melalui PT Mitratama Perkasa (MP) site Kintap melakukan pemantauan terhadap keberadaan flora dan Fauna untuk mengetahui keberadaan flora yang berada di wilayah/lokasi antara CPP dan underpass dan Desa Mekarsari. Perusahaan juga melakukan upaya revegetasi, yakni melakukan penanaman pohon karet (Hevea brasiliensis) dan trembesi (Samanea saman) pasca kegiatan penambangan. Untuk mendukung terwujudnya pelestarian lingkungan, MP site Kintap mewajibkan karyawan untuk tidak mengganggu satwa liar, mengingat keberadaan satwa liar memiliki peran dalam menjaga keseimbangan atau stabilitas lingkungan. Perseroan senantiasa berupaya untuk terus menjaga pelestarian keanekaragaman hayati di area operasional kami.

The Company implements environmental performance through efforts to protect the ecosystem around the operational area and minimize the negative impacts of operational activities on the flora and fauna habitat in the operational area. [GRI 304-2] The Company's commitment to this is realized, among others, through coral reef conservation activities around the coal loading and unloading port operations, namely the North Pulau Laut Coal Terminal (NPLCT) port.

At the Sebuku site, biological conservation efforts are carried out through a revegetation program. [GRI 304-3] Through this program, the Company, through PT Bahari Cakrawala Sebuku (BCS), contributes to maintaining the condition of maintained vegetation to produce good conditions, prevent erosion, improve the condition of ex-mining land as a whole, and participate in the process of reducing carbon gas through reforestation.

Through its subsidiary, Al, the Company are engaged in rehabilitating river basin areas (DAS). Through this effort, the Company together with Al carries out planting in critical areas of forest areas in the context of DAS rehabilitation and rehabilitation outside the mining operational area. The DAS rehabilitation area is located in Banjar, Tanah Laut and Tanah Bumbu Regencies, South Kalimantan Province.

The Company, through PT Mitratama Perkasa (MP) site Kintap, monitors the presence of flora and fauna in the area between the CPP and the underpass, as well as Mekarsari Village. As part of its environmental preservation efforts, the Company engages in revegetation initiatives, including the planting of rubber trees (Hevea brasiliensis) and rain trees (Samanea saman) following mining activities. To further support biodiversity preservation, MP Kintap site requires employees to avoid disturbing wildlife, as these animals play a vital role in maintaining environmental balance. The Company always strives to preserve biodiversity in all its operational areas.
