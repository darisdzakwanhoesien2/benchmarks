# Tata Kelola Keberlanjutan
## Sustainability Governance

Penerapan Tata Kelola Perusahaan (GCG) membantu mengarahkan pengelolaan perusahaan secara profesional berdasarkan prinsip-prinsip transparansi, akuntabilitas, tanggung jawab, independen, kewajaran dan kesetaraan. Memahami hal itu, Kami berkomitmen kuat untuk menerapkan prinsip-prinsip GCG secara menyeluruh pada seluruh lini bisnisnya.

Dengan menjalankan prinsip-prinsip GCG, Perseroan tidak hanya dapat mewujudkan aktivitas usaha yang transparan, akuntabel, bertanggung jawab, dan sehat. Lebih dari itu, Astrindo dapat meningkatkan kepercayaan para pemangku kepentingan dan investor, sehingga berdampak terhadap pembentukan citra yang positif pada Perseroan.

Lebih lanjut, Perseroan berupaya secara sungguh-sungguh menghadirkan aktivitas bisnis yang bertanggung jawab melalui pemenuhan peraturan perundang-undangan dan standar etika. Perseroan juga melakukan pemenuhan terhadap Tanggung Jawab Sosial dan Lingkungan, yang merupakan perwujudan kepedulian Perseroan terhadap lingkungan dan masyarakat sekaligus juga bentuk kontribusi Perseroan terhadap pencapaian Sustainable Development Goals.

## STRUKTUR TATA KELOLA KEBERLANJUTAN
### [GRI 2-9] [GRI 2-11]

Merujuk pada Undang-Undang No. 40 tahun 2007 tentang Perseroan Terbatas, Perseroan memiliki struktur tata kelola Perseroan yang terdiri dari 3 (tiga) organ utama yaitu Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS), Dewan Komisaris, serta Direksi. [GRI 2-9] Perseroan telah membentuk organ pendukung guna menunjang pelaksanaan tugas Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, yaitu Komite Audit, Satuan Audit Internal, serta Sekretaris Perusahaan.

The implementation of Good Corporate Governance (GCG) directs the management of the Company in a professional manner, based on the principles of transparency, accountability, responsibility, independence, fairness, and equality. Understanding this, we are is firmly committed to implementing GCG principles across all its business lines.

By applying the GCG principles, the Company not only ensures transparent, accountable, responsible, and healthy business practices, but also enhances the trust of stakeholders and investors, contributing to the creation of a positive image for the Company.

Additionally, the Company is committed to conducting responsible business activities by complying with laws and regulations as well as ethical standards. The Company also demonstrates its commitment to Social and Environmental Responsibility, reflecting the Company's commitment to the environment and society and its contribution to achieving the Sustainable Development Goals.

## SUSTAINABILITY GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE
### [GRI 2-9] [GRI 2-11]

In accordance with Law No. 40 of 2007 concerning Limited Liability Companies, the Company has established a good corporate governance structure consisting of 3 (three) main organs: the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS), the Board of Commissioners, and the Board of Directors. [GRI 2-9] To support the implementation of the duties of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors, the Company has formed additional supporting organs, namely the Audit Committee, the Internal Audit Unit, and the Corporate Secretary.
