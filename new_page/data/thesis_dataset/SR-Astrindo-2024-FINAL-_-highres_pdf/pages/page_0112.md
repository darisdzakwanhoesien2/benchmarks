Perseroan melalui JMB mengamati bahwa mayoritas warga lokal berprofesi sebagai petani. ## Agriculture and Animal Husbandry Sector

The Company, through JMB, has observed that most local residents work as farmers. In light of this, JMB offers support for community empowerment in agriculture and animal husbandry to enhance farmers' capacity and promote sustainable farming practices. JMB provides assistance in procuring food and fruit plant seeds, as well as implementing a greening program to promote food security within the community. Additionally, the Company supports the operations of the Jadi Makmur Bersama Farmers Group and contributes to the maintenance of HPL land and the rental of equipment.
