# Kinerja Lingkungan
Environmental Performance

Perseroan melalui PT Jembayan Muarabara (JMB) melakukan upaya konservasi lingkungan. Perseroan memiliki area Arboretum seluas ±100 Ha sebagai wujud komitmen pengelolaan lahan pasca-tambang. Upaya ini dilakukan dengan target utama mengembalikan area pasca-tambang sesuai fungsinya. Selain sebagai tempat koleksi beragam jenis tanaman serta tempat berlindung dan mencari makan bagi satwa-satwa liar dengan didukung lokasi yang dekat dengan vegetasi asli, Arboretum merupakan habitat bagi beberapa hewan yang dilindungi. [GRI 304-3]

Arboretum dibangun di areal bekas tambang PIT 203 dan PIT 210 yang telah menyelesaikan kegiatan penambangan dan proses reklamasi. Pada area tersebut, telah dilakukan penanaman vegetasi lokal, baik pohon kayu keras maupun buah-buahan lokal, serta beberapa tanaman lokal yang dilindungi.

The Company through PT Jembayan Muarabara (JMB) carries out environmental conservation efforts. The Company has an Arboretum area of ±100 Ha as a form of commitment to post-mining land management. This effort is carried out with the main target of restoring the post-mining area according to its function. In addition to being a place to collect various types of plants and a place to shelter and find food for wild animals supported by a location close to native vegetation, the Arboretum is a habitat for several protected animals. [GRI 304-3]

The arboretum is situated in former mining areas designated as PIT 203 and PIT 210, where mining activities and the reclamation process have concluded. In this location, local species, including hardwoods and fruit trees, as well as several protected plants, have been planted.

Pemantauan juga dilakukan oleh entitas anak lainnya. JMB telah melaksanakan pemantauan secara periodik terhadap area reklamasi sejak tahun 2010, yaitu setiap 2 (dua) tahun sekali. Pemantauan dilakukan terhadap tingkat kesuburan tanah, pengukuran laju erosi, petak ukur permanen, perhitungan carbon stock, dan keanekaragaman hayati.

Other subsidiaries also conduct monitoring. JMB has carried out periodic assessments of the reclamation area since 2010, at intervals of every 2 (two) years. This monitoring includes evaluations of soil fertility, measurements of erosion rates, establishment of permanent measurement plots, calculations of carbon stocks, and assessments of biodiversity.
