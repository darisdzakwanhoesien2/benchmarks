Aspek Sosial dan Kemasyarakatan
Social and Community Aspects

# Bidang Lingkungan

Di bidang lingkungan JMB melakukan studi tiru pembangunan green house, pembangunan tempat pembuangan sampah Desa Mulawarman, dan normalisasi sungai.

# Bidang Ekonomi

Melakukan peningkatan kapasitas dan akses masyarakat terhadap UKM, seperti memberikan pelatihan pembuatan tempe hemat air dan kecap, bantuan dana gelar TTG ke-10, mendukung kegiatan Posyantek Desa Suka Maju Bank Sampah Mandiri, serta pengangkutan core box ke Bank Sampah Mandiri Suka Maju.

# Bidang Kesehatan

JMB memberikan bantuan BPJS Kesehatan kepada warga tidak mampu, bantuan air bersih, jasa geolistrik (pengecekan titik air), operasional instruktur senam, memberikan bantuan peralatan medis untuk Puskesmas, serta pengadaan tandon air bersih. Selain itu, JMB menyelenggarakan kegiatan donor darah secara berkala.

# Bidang Sosial-Budaya

JMB memberikan dukungan di bidang sosial budaya, antara lain berkontribusi untuk kegiatan keagamaan di desa-desa binaan serta perayaan hari besar nasional dan kegiatan-kegiatan budaya, baik tingkat desa maupun provinsi, antara lain bantuan hari raya Natal, Idul Fitri dan Idul Adha, hari raya umat Hindu, bantuan kegiatan keagamaan, mebel gereja, dan pembangunan mushola.

# Bidang Kelembagaan Masyarakat

Pada bidang kemasyarakatan, JMB melakukan pengembangan lembaga-lembaga masyarakat khususnya yang ada di desa, mencakup memberikan kontribusi untuk lembaga-lembaga non Pemerintah-Koperasi dan atau Badan Usaha milik Desa Karang Taruna, Kelompok Tani dan lain sebagainya, seperti pengadaan komputer dan laptop, bantuan operasional tim CSR, serta pembangunan MCK.

# Environmental Sector

In the environmental sector, JMB organized a study tour related to greenhouse construction, established a waste disposal site in Mulawarman Village, and engaged in river normalization efforts.

# Economic Sector

JMB build capacity and improved community access to small and medium enterprises (SMEs) by providing training in tempeh production, water conservation, soy sauce making, and financial assistance for the 10th TTG title. Furthermore, JMB supports the Posyantek activities of the Suka Maju Village Bank Sampah Mandiri and helped transport core boxes to their location.

# Health Sector

In the health sector, JMB offered BPJS Kesehatan to underprivileged residents, provided clean water access, geoelectric services to check water points, operational gymnastics instructors, medical equipment assistance for Puskesmas, and clean water tanks. Additionally, JMB organized regular blood donation activities.

# Socio-Cultural Sector

JMB supported socio-cultural initiatives by contributing to religious activities in fostered villages, as well as national and cultural celebrations at both the village and provincial levels. This includes assistance for Christmas, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Hindu holidays, and funding for the furnitures of churches and prayer rooms.

# Community Institutional Sector

In the community sector, JMB developed community institutions in villages by supporting non-governmental organizations, cooperatives, and village-owned business entities. Contributions include procuring computers and laptops, providing operational assistance for the CSR team, and constructing toilets.
