Sustainability Governance

# KEBIJAKAN REMUNERASI [GRI 2-19]

Pada tahun 2024 pemegang saham melalui RUPS telah menetapkan untuk memberikan kewenangan kepada Dewan Komisaris untuk menentukan struktur dan nominal remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dengan tetap mempertimbangkan rekomendasi yang diberikan oleh pemegang saham. Informasi mengenai Kebijakan Remunerasi dapat dilihat pada Laporan Tahunan Bab Tata Kelola Sub Bab Kebijakan Remunerasi Dewan Komisaris Dan Direksi.

# Proses Menentukan Remunerasi [GRI 2-20]

Pada proses menentukan remunerasi, Dewan Komisaris menetapkan indikator-indikator utama dalam proses penetapan remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, sesuai dengan anggaran dasar yang berlaku.

Adapun indikator dalam penetapan remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi adalah sebagai berikut:

1. Remunerasi yang berlaku pada industri sejenis.
2. Pencapaian target atau kinerja Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi.
3. Kemampuan finansial Perseroan.

Informasi mengenai Kebijakan Remunerasi Dewan Komisaris Dan Direksi dapat dilihat pada Laporan Tahunan Bab Tata Kelola Subbab Kebijakan Remunerasi Dewan Komisaris Dan Direksi.

Terkait remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dapat dilihat dalam Laporan Tahunan Perusahaan.

# TANTANGAN PENERAPAN TATA KELOLA BERKELANJUTAN [OJK E.5]

Sepanjang tahun 2024 Perseroan dihadapkan dengan berbagai tantangan yang memiliki dampak signifikan bagi penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan, yaitu turunnya harga batu bara, menurunnya permintaan batu bara, dan permasalahan rantai pasok. Selain itu, Perusahaan juga harus meningkatkan kinerja keberlanjutan secara konsisten, mengingat operasional yang dihasilkan memiliki dampak pada lingkungan.

# REMUNERATION POLICY [GRI 2-19]

In 2024, the shareholders, through the GMS, delegated authority to the Board of Commissioners to determine the structure and amount of remuneration for both the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors, while also considering the recommendations provided by the shareholders. Information related to the Remuneration Policy can be found in the Annual Report, within the Governance Chapter, specifically in the sub-chapter on Remuneration Policy for the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors.

# Remuneration Determination Process [GRI 2-20]

When determining remuneration for the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors, the Board of Commissioners identifies key indicators in alignment with the applicable articles of association.

The following indicators are considered in this assessment:

1. Remuneration practices within similar industries.
2. Achievement of performance targets by the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors.
3. The Company's financial capability.

Information regarding the Remuneration Policy for the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors can also be found in the Annual Report, specifically in the Governance Chapter and the sub-chapter on Remuneration Policy.

The remuneration of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors in 2024 is available in the Company's Annual Report.

# CHALLENGES OF IMPLEMENTING SUSTAINABLE GOVERNANCE [OJK E.5]

Throughout 2024, the Company faced several significant challenges that impact the implementation of sustainable finance. These challenges include falling coal prices, declining coal demand, and supply chain issues. Moreover, the Company must consistently enhance its sustainability performance, as its operations have an effect on the environment.
