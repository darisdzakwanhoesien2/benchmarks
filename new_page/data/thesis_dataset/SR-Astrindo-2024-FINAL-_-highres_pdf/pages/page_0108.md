# Lingkup Pemenuhan Aspek K3 [GRI 403-2] [GRI 403-7]

Untuk mencegah terjadinya kecelakaan kerja, Perseroan dan entitas anaknya mengimplementasikan aspek K3 mencakup sejumlah lingkup perumusan dan pemenuhan, yaitu:

- Operasional bebas insiden Kecelakaan kerja;
- Sertifikasi aspek K3;
- Budaya Sadar K3;
- Pelatihan K3;
- Pengawasan Aspek K3; dan
- Konseling K3.

# OHS Aspect Fulfillment Scope [GRI 403-2] [GRI 403-7]

To prevent workplace accidents, the Company and it's subsidiaries implements OHS aspects that address several key areas, including:

- Operations free from workplace incident occurrences;
- Certification of OHS aspects;
- OHS awareness culture;
- OHS training;
- Supervision of OHS practices; and
- OHS counseling services.

# Kecelakaan Kerja [GRI 403-9] [GRI 403-10]

Untuk mengukur efektivitas praktik K3 di lingkungan Perseroan, Astrindo menerapkan metode monitoring terhadap perkembangan setiap potensi insiden kecelakaan kerja, serta melakukan pencatatan dan perhitungan berkenaan dengan insiden kecelakaan kerja. Untuk memperkuat praktik K3, Perseroan juga menentukan tingkat kecelakaan. Perhitungan jam kerja, tingkat kecelakaan kerja, serta hal-hal lain terkait hal tersebut dilakukan sesuai panduan yang dikeluarkan International Labor Organization (ILO). Perseroan berkomitmen kuat untuk terus memperkuat pelaksanaan K3, mempertahankan nihil kecelakaan kerja, serta mencegah munculnya penyakit akibat kerja sebagai pemenuhan bagi masing-masing karyawan.

# Work Accidents [GRI 403-9] [GRI 403-10]

To assess the effectiveness of Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) practices within the company, Astrindo employs a monitoring method to track the development of potential work accident incidents. This includes recording and calculating the frequency of work accidents. To reinforce OHS practices, the company also establishes an accident rate. The calculations related to working hours, accident rates, and other relevant aspects are conducted in accordance with guidelines set forth by the International Labor Organization (ILO). The company is firmly committed to enhancing OHS implementation, maintaining a goal of zero work accidents, and preventing work-related diseases for the benefit of every employee.
