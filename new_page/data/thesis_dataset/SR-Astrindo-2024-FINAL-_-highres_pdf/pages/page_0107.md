# Aspek Ketenagakerjaan
## Employment Aspect

Di samping itu, kami juga memiliki berbagai program kesehatan yang ditujukan untuk menjamin terjaganya kesehatan karyawan, dengan uraian sebagai berikut: [GRI 403-3][GRI 403-6]

- Pelaksanaan medical check-up karyawan baru dan berkala untuk karyawan.
- Pelatihan dan pengarahan kesehatan karyawan.
- Inspeksi kesehatan dan kebersihan oleh tim medis lapangan.
- Kegiatan clinic rescue di daerah operasional.
- Sosialisasi kesehatan melalui berbagai media komunikasi, seperti meeting, poster, spanduk, dan sebagainya.

Untuk memperkuat pelaksanaan K3 di lingkungan Perseroan melalui JMB, Astrindo mendorong seluruh karyawan, terutama yang pekerjaannya memiliki risiko tinggi untuk mengikuti pelatihan K3. Selama tahun 2024 karyawan JMB telah mengikuti pelatihan K3 sebagai berikut: [GRI 403-5]

- Pelatihan Pembinaan Petugas P3K
- Resertifikasi K3 Listrik
- Pelatihan Induksi K3 dan re-induksi bagi karyawan lama atau pulang cuti
- Melakukan pengetesan kemampuan mengendarai alat oleh trainer
- Memproses dan mengeluarkan izin kerja dan izin mengendarai (ID/Kimper)
- Melakukan patrol rutin
- Monitoring fit for work bagi driver dump truck melalui program BANDARA
- Melakukan inspeksi kecepatan unit
- Fire Extinguisher inspection
- Vehicle inspection
- Management Inspection
- Area Inspection
- Conduct Safety Talk PSM
- Conduct Fatigue Check for Operators
- Health Patrol
- Convoy Check
- Hazard Report
- Perform: Fatigue Check (Health Patrol) and implementation Fit to Work Statement

Additionally, we offer various health programs to ensure the well-being of our employees. The following activities are included: [GRI 403-3][GRI 403-6]

- Implementation of both initial and periodic medical check-ups for employees.
- Health training and informational sessions for staff.
- Health and hygiene inspections conducted by our field medical team.
- Emergency medical support in operational areas.
- Health awareness campaigns using various communication tools, such as meetings, posters, banners, and more.

To enhance OHS practices, the Company, through JMB, encourages all employees, particularly those in high-risk roles, to participate in OHS training. Throughout 2024, JMB employees participated in the following OHS training sessions: [GRI 403-5]

- First Aid Officer Development Training
- Electrical OHS Recertification
- OHS Induction Training and re-induction for old employees or those returning from leave
- Conducting Equipment driving ability tests by Trainers
- Processing and issuing work permits and driving permits (ID/Kimper)
- Conducting routine patrols
- Monitoring fit for work for dump truck drivers through the AIRPORT program
- Conducting unit speed inspections
- Fire Extinguisher inspection
- Vehicle inspection
- Management Inspection
- Area Inspection
- Conducting safety talk/PSM
- Conducting Fatigue Check for operators
- Health Patrol
- Convoy Check
- Hazard Report
- Perform: Fatigue Check (Health Patrol) and Implementation Fit to work statement
