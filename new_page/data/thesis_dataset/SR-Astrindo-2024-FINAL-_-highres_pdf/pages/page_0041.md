PT Jembayan Muarabara meraih piagam penghargaan dalam pengelolaan lingkungan hidup pertambangan mineral dan batu bara untuk kelompok pemegang izin usaha pertambangan dalam rangka penanaman modal dalam negeri komoditas batu bara periode penilaian tahun 2023. Penghargaan ini diberikan oleh Kementerian Energi dan Sumber Daya Mineral (ESDM) Republik Indonesia.

PT Jembayan Muarabara received an award certificate in environmental management of mineral and coal mining for the group of mining business permit holders for domestic investment in coal commodities for the 2023 assessment period. This award was granted by the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources (ESDM) of the Republic of Indonesia.

PT Jembayan Muarabara mendapatkan penghargaan dari Gubernur Kalimantan Timur dengan peringkat emas melalui Program Penilaian Peringkat Kinerja Perusahaan dalam Pengelolaan Lingkungan Hidup Provinsi Kalimantan Timur.

PT Jembayan Muarabara received an award from the Governor of East Kalimantan with a gold rating through the Company Performance Rating Assessment Program in Environmental Management of East Kalimantan Province.

PT Jembayan Muarabara Site Separi Kabupaten Kutai Kartanegara meraih piagam penghargaan Pencegahan &amp; Penanggulangan HIV AIDS di tempat kerja, serta Nihil Kecelakaan (Zero Accident). Piagam ini diberikan oleh Gubernur Kalimantan Timur dalam Program Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3) di Tempat Kerja dan Masyarakat Kalimantan Timur.

PT Jembayan Muarabara Site Separi Kutai Kartanegara Regency received a certificate of appreciation for Prevention &amp; Handling of HIV AIDS in the workplace, as well as Zero Accident. This certificate was given by the Governor of East Kalimantan in the Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) Program in the Workplace and Community of East Kalimantan.
