# Kegiatan Usaha Perseroan [OJK C.4]
Business Activity of the Company [OJK C.4]

Mengacu pada Anggaran Dasar Perseroan Pasal 3, Perseroan menjalankan kegiatan aktivitas kantor pusat dan aktivitas konsultasi manajemen lainnya di bidang pertambangan, jasa, pembangunan, dan perdagangan. Pada posisi akhir 2024, Perseroan menjalankan kegiatan-kegiatan usaha berikut:

1. Jasa Penunjang Pertambangan: Jasa infrastruktur pertambangan batu bara yang terintegrasi.
2. Eksplorasi dan Produksi: Pertambangan batu bara.

Based on Article 3 of the Company's Articles of Association, the Company carries out head office activities and other management consulting activities in the mining, services, development and trade sectors. At the end of 2024, the Company carries out the following business activities:

1. Mining Support: Integrated coal mining infrastructure services.
2. Exploration and Production: Coal mining.

# JASA PELABUHAN DAN PENANGANAN BATU BARA

1. Perseroan melaksanakan kegiatan usaha penyediaan jasa batu bara terintegrasi berupa penyewaan pelabuhan batu bara dan fasilitas penghancur batu bara melalui entitas anak, yaitu PT Mitratama Perkasa (MP). [GRI 2-2]

Pada 31 Desember 2024, MP memiliki 5 (lima) aset operasional yang seluruhnya telah beroperasi penuh dan memberikan kontribusi kepada Perseroan, yaitu:

a. Pelabuhan Asam-asam.
b. Pelabuhan Mulia Barat.
c. CPP dan OLC Asam-asam.
d. CPP dan OLC Mulia Barat.
e. CBU North Pulau Laut Coal Terminal.

[GRI 2-2]

Pada 31 Desember 2024, NTP memiliki 5 (lima) aset yang telah beroperasi penuh, yaitu:

c. OLC &amp; TBCT Duplication.

e. Barge Loading Facilities (BLF) Extension.

# PORT SERVICES AND COAL HANDLING

1. The Company conducts business activities to provide integrated coal services by renting coal ports and coal crushing facilities through its subsidiary, PT Mitratama Perkasa (MP). [GRI 2-2]

As of December 31, 2024, MP has 5 (five) operational assets, all of them are fully operating and have contributed to the Company, namely:

a. Asam-Asam Coal Handling Port.
b. West Mulia Coal Handling Port.
c. Asam-Asam CPP and OLC.
d. West Mulia CPP and OLC.
e. CBU North Pulau Laut Coal Terminal

2. The Company through PT Nusa Tambang Pratama (NTP) conducts business activities in the coal network infrastructure project development, including coal crushers, Coal Preparation Plant (CPP) and Overland Conveyor (OLC) as well as providing integrated coal distribution services. [GRI 2-2]

As of December 31, 2024, NTP has 5 (five) assets that are fully operating, namely:

a. Lubuk Tutung Coal Handling Port.
b. Sangatta Coal Crusher.
c. OLC &amp; Tanjung Barat Coal Terminal (TBCT) Duplication.
d. Melawan CPP and Western OLC.
e. Barge Loading Facilities (BLF) Extension.
