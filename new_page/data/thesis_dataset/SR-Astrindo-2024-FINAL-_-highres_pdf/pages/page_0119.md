# Implementation of the CSER Program at PT Bahari Cakrawala Sebuku (BCS)

In 2024, PT Bahari Cakrawala Sebuku (BCS) conducted its CSR and Community Development activities in Sebuku site, as follows:

|  Kegiatan / Activities  |
| --- |
|  Penyerapan Tenaga Kerja Lokal 24 orang tenaga kerja dari beberapa Desa Binaan / Absorption of 24 local workers from several fostered villages  |
|  Membantu pengadaan listrik dan air bersih untuk operasional Poskesdes Desa Sekapung, SMP sekapung dan SMA Sekapung / Assisting in the provision of electricity and clean water for the operation of the Sekapung Village Health Post, Sekapung Middle School and Sekapung High School  |
|  Melakukan perbaikan AC di Poskedes (Pos Kesehatan Desa) Sekapung / Repairing the AC at the Sekapung Village Health Post  |
|  Membantu meja kerja (2 buah) dan tempat sampah (4 buah) untuk operasional Puskesmas Kecamatan Sei Bali Diberikan sesuai permintaan dan ketersediaan perusahaan (bukan barang baru) / Providing work desks (2 units) and trash bins (4 units) for the operation of the Sei Bali District Health Center Provided according to the Company's request and availability (not new items)  |
|  Memberikan bantuan bibit ikan (5000 ekor) dan pakan ikan kepada Kelompok Tani Maju Bersama desa Sekapung / Providing assistance in the form of fish seeds (5,000) and fish feed to the Maju Bersama Farmers Group in Sekapung Village.  |
|  Membantu perbaikan atap ruang tunggu dan tempat parkir sepeda motor di Pelabuhan Serakaman / Assisting in repairing the roof of the waiting room and motorbike parking area at the Seraman Port  |

|  Memberikan bantuan dalam rangka perayaan HUT RI tanggal 17 Agustus 2024 kepada Camat Pulau Sebuku / Providing assistance in the context of celebrating the Indonesian Independence Day on August 17, 2024 to the Head of Sebuku Island Sub-district. For events in the sub-district  |
|  Memberikan bantuan untuk operasional Koramil, berupa 2 unit meja kerja, 1 meja, 1 kursi, karpet, kulkas / Providing assistance for Koramil operations, in the form of 2 work desks, 1 table, 1 chair, carpet, refrigerator  |
|  Memberikan bantuan untuk operasional polsek, berupa AC, kasur dan penyambungan listrik PLN ke Rumah Dinas Kapolsek / Providing assistance for police station operations, in the form of AC, mattresses and PLN electricity connections to the Police Chief's Official Residence  |
