Aspek Sosial dan Kemasyarakatan
Social and Community Aspects

## Bidang Infrastruktur

JMB menyalurkan bantuan renovasi Pondok Pertemuan Dusun Pulau Mas, melakukan perbaikan plafon Gereja Elsahddai Bukit Pariaman, pemasangan sambungan listrik baru Dusun Berambai, bantuan dana pembangunan pagar rumah PRR, rehabilitasi Polsek Teluk Dalam, perbaikan jembatan Desa Mulawarman, pemasangan listrik di Desa Mulawarman dan Desa Desa Bhuana Jaya, perbaikan pintu air Dusun Pulau Mas, serta memberikan bantuan perbaikan rumah warga Bhuana Jaya.

## Infrastructure Sector

JMB has provided assistance for several infrastructure projects, including renovating the Pulau Mas Hamlet Meeting House, repairing the ceiling of the Elsahddai Church in Bukit Pariamanm installing new electricity connections in Berambai Hamlet, providing financial assistance for the construction of PRR house fences, rehabilitation of the Teluk Dalam Police station, repairing the Mulawarman Village bridge, installing electrical connections in Mulawarman Village and Bhuana Jaya Village, repairing the Pulau Mas Hamlet water gate, abd assisting in the repair of houses for Bhuana Jaya residents.
