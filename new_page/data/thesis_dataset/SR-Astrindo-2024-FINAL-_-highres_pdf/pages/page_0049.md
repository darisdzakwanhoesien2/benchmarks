Sustainability Governance

# PENANGGUNG JAWAB PENERAPAN TATA KELOLA BERKELANJUTAN [GRI 2-12] [GRI 2-13] [GRI 2-14] [OJK E.1]

Untuk mencapai target kinerja keberlanjutan, Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi turut terlibat dalam pelaksanaan kinerja keberlanjutan. Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dibantu oleh pejabat dan/atau unit kerja, hingga seluruh karyawan di bawah tanggung jawab Sekretaris Perusahaan. Sekretaris Perusahaan melaporkan pelaksanaan kinerja keberlanjutan kepada Direksi. Di samping itu, Sekretaris Perusahaan membantu Direksi dalam penyusunan Laporan Keberlanjutan. Direksi bertanggung jawab dalam penyusunan dan publikasi Laporan Keberlanjutan kepada pemangku kepentingan. [GRI 2-14]

Lebih lanjut, pelaksanaan praktik keuangan berkelanjutan tidak hanya dilakukan oleh Perusahaan, tetapi juga dijalankan di tingkat entitas anak, yaitu pada site Jembayan yang dilaksanakan oleh Unit CSR di bawah Departemen Support Services PT Jembayan Muarabara serta pada area Sebuku yang dilaksanakan di bawah Departemen Environment PT Bahari Cakrawala Sebuku.

# MANAJEMEN RISIKO PERUSAHAAN

Untuk menjaga keberlangsungan usaha dalam jangka panjang secara sehat dan berintegritas, Perusahaan menerapkan pengelolaan manajemen risiko dalam aktivitas operasional sehari-hari. Pengelolaan manajemen risiko dilaksanakan secara terintegrasi dan sistematis, melalui mitigasi serta tindak lanjut yang tepat guna mengurangi potensi hadirnya dampak yang dapat mengancam usaha Perusahaan.

Untuk mendukung pengelolaan risiko berjalan optimal, Perseroan mengimplementasi budaya sadar risiko (risk culture) dengan kuat melalui seluruh karyawan. Saat berperan sebagai risk taker dalam proses pengambilan keputusan, para karyawan melakukan analisis yang memadai terkait eksposur risiko yang berpotensi terjadi. Pelaksanaan budaya risiko tersebut ternyata memberikan dampak yang positif terhadap penerapan aspek manajemen risiko pada semua tingkatan dan tahapan.

# PERSON RESPONSIBLE FOR IMPLEMENTING SUSTAINABLE GOVERNANCE [GRI 2-12] [GRI 2-13] [GRI 2-14] [OJK E.1]

The Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors are both involved in the implementation of sustainability performance in order to achieve the targets. The Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors are supported by officials and work units, as well as all employees under the responsibility of the Corporate Secretary. The Corporate Secretary reports the sustainability performance implementation to the Board of Directors. Additionally, the Corporate Secretary assists the Board of Directors in preparing the Sustainability Report, which the Board of Directors is responsible for publishing to stakeholders. [GRI 2-14]

Moreover, the implementation of sustainable financial practices extends beyond the Company to its subsidiaries. These practices are carried out in the Jembayan site by the CSR Unit under the Support Services Department of PT Jembayan Muarabara, and in the Sebuku area by the Environmental Department of PT Bahari Cakrawala Sebuku.

# ENTERPRISE RISK MANAGEMENT

To ensure long-term business continuity in a healthy and integrated manner, the Company implements risk management as part of its daily operations. This risk management process is carried out in an integrated and systematic way, through mitigation and appropriate follow-up actions to minimize potential impacts that could threaten the Company's business.

To support optimal risk management, the Company implements a strong risk culture through all employees. When acting as risk takers in the decision-making process, employees conduct adequate analysis related to potential risk exposures. The implementation of this risk culture has had a positive impact on the application of risk management aspects at all levels and stages.
