# MEMPERKUAT KONTRIBUSI MELALUI PELESTARIAN LINGKUNGAN HIDUP DAN KEANEKARAGAMAN HAYATI

Kami terus memperkuat upaya pelestarian lingkungan secara menyeluruh dalam rangka mengurangi dampak operasional terhadap lingkungan hidup. Hal ini diwujudkan melalui efisiensi penggunaan air dan energi, hingga pengelolaan limbah yang dihasilkan dari aktivitas operasional pada site Perseroan. Kami memastikan aktivitas operasional senantiasa dilakukan melalui praktik-praktik bertanggung jawab dan terus menginisiasi penggunaan sumber energi baru terbarukan. Selaras dengan itu, pemantauan dan pengurangan atas emisi yang dihasilkan dari aktivitas operasional terus kami lakukan.

Untuk memperkuat kontribusi kami terhadap lingkungan, Perseroan melalui entitas anaknya melakukan upaya konservasi lingkungan melalui penanaman vegetasi lokal, baik pohon kayu keras maupun buah-buahan lokal, serta beberapa tanaman lokal yang dilindungi. Upaya ini dilakukan dengan tujuan utama mengembalikan area pasca-tambang sesuai fungsinya dan mengembangkan habitat hewan-hewan yang dilindungi. [GRI 304-3]

# STRENGTHENING CONTRIBUTION THROUGH ENVIRONMENTAL CONSERVATION AND BIODIVERSITY

We continue to strengthen our comprehensive environmental conservation efforts in order to reduce the impact of our operations on the environment. This includes optimizing our use of water and energy, as well as managing waste generated by our activities at operational sites. We ensure that our operations are conducted responsibly while actively promoting the use of new &amp; renewable energy sources. Additionally, we continuously monitor and work to reduce emissions associated with our operations.

To strengthen our contribution to the environment, the Company through its subsidiaries carries out environmental conservation efforts by planting local vegetation, both local hardwood and fruit trees, as well as several protected local plants. These efforts are carried out with the main objective of restoring post-mining areas to their proper function and developing protected animal habitats. [GRI 304-3]
