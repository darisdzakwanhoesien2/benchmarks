# Upaya Konservasi Keanekaragaman Hayati [OJK F.10]

Keanekaragaman hayati berperan penting dalam menjaga keseimbangan ekosistem. Atas dasar itu, Perseroan memberikan perhatian terhadap keanekaragaman hayati itu melalui upaya konservasi. Hal ini dijalankan selain sebagai bentuk tanggung jawab Perseroan terhadap lingkungan, juga merupakan pemenuhan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan, terutama yang berkaitan dengan perlindungan dan pelestarian lingkungan. Melalui proses tersebut, kami mewujudkan komitmen untuk tidak beroperasi di kawasan yang memiliki keanekaragaman hayati tinggi serta tidak mengubah kondisi ekosistem lingkungan hidup di sekitar area operasi.

# Biodiversity Conservation Efforts [OJK F.10]

Biodiversity is essential for maintaining ecosystem balance. In light of this, the Company prioritizes biodiversity through conservation efforts. This is carried out not only as a form of the Company's responsibility to the environment, but also as a fulfillment of laws and regulations, especially those related to environmental protection and preservation. Through this process, we realize our commitment not to operate in areas with high biodiversity and not to change the condition of the environmental ecosystem around the operating area.
