# DEWAN KOMISARIS

Dewan Komisaris merupakan organ di dalam struktur tata kelola yang melaksanakan fungsi pengawasan dan pemberian nasihat atas pengurusan Pengelolaan oleh Direksi. Melalui fungsi tersebut, Dewan Komisaris sekaligus memastikan bahwa pengurusan Perseroan telah dilaksanakan sesuai dengan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan, peraturan perundang-undangan, serta prinsip-prinsip GCG.

# DIREKSI

Direksi merupakan organ yang berwenang dan bertanggung jawab atas pengurusan Perseroan, dilakukan semata demi kepentingan Perseroan sesuai dengan maksud dan tujuan Perseroan. Direksi dapat mewakili Perseroan, baik di dalam maupun di luar pengadilan, sesuai ketentuan yang diatur di dalam Anggaran Dasar dan peraturan perundang-undangan.

Untuk mendukung pelaksanaan kinerja operasional berjalan optimal, Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi mengadakan rapat secara berkala. Pada tahun 2024 Dewan Komisaris menyelenggarakan rapat internal sebanyak 6 kali dan rapat gabungan dengan Direksi sebanyak 6 kali. Sementara Direksi telah melakukan rapat sebanyak 12 kali.

Upaya lain yang dilakukan Perseroan untuk mendukung optimalisasi kinerja Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, Perseroan memiliki kebijakan keberagaman Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi. Latar belakang, pengalaman, dan keterampilan yang berbeda-beda yang dimiliki Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dapat berdampak terhadap peningkatan kualitas kinerja Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi. Informasi lebih rinci mengenai keberagaman serta independensi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dapat dilihat pada Laporan Tahunan Perusahaan.

# KEBIJAKAN NOMINASI DEWAN KOMISARIS DAN DIREKSI [GRI 2-10]

Perseroan memiliki kebijakan dan prosedur nominasi bagi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, yakni melalui RUPS sesuai keputusan para pemegang saham yang dilakukan berdasarkan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan dan peraturan

# THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS

The Board of Commissioners is a body within the governance structure that carries out supervisory and advisory functions on the Company's management by the Board of Directors. Through this function, the Board of Commissioners also ensures that the management of the Company is carried out in accordance with the Company's Articles of Association, laws and regulations and GCG principles.

# THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS

The Board of Directors is an organ that is authorized and responsible for the management of the Company, carried out solely for the interests of the Company in accordance with the aims and objectives of the Company. The Board of Directors can represent the Company, both inside and outside the court, in accordance with the provisions stipulated in the Articles of Association and laws and regulations.

The Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors hold regular meetings to support the implementation of optimal operational performance. In 2024, the Board of Commissioners held 6 internal meetings and 6 joint meetings with the Board of Directors. Meanwhile, the Board of Directors has held 12 meetings.

The Company carries out another effort to support the performance optimization of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors in the form of a policy of diversity of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors. The different backgrounds, experiences, and skills possessed by the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors can impact the performance quality improvement of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors. More detailed information on the diversity and independence of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors can be seen in the Company's Annual Report.
