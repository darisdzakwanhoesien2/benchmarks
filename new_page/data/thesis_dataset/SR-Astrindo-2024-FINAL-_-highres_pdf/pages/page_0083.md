Investasi Pelestarian Keanekaragaman Hayati [OJK F.4]

Pada 2024 Perseroan melalui JMB telah merealisasikan anggaran untuk investasi program keanekaragaman hayati, sejumlah USD16,5 ribu.

## EMISI

Perseroan mendukung dengan komitmen pemerintah yang termuat di dalam Peraturan Presiden Republik Indonesia No. 61 Tahun 2011 tentang Rencana Aksi Nasional Penurunan Emisi Gas Rumah Kaca "RAN-GRK". Perseroan berupaya untuk menghadirkan kontribusi optimal terhadap upaya perlindungan dan pelestarian lingkungan, demi menekan perubahan iklim berupa cuaca ekstrem yang berlangsung pada skala global.

Astrindo mengelola dampak kegiatan operasional secara terstruktur. Perseroan melakukan efisiensi penggunaan energi, baik energi BBM maupun listrik untuk menjaga keberlangsungan lingkungan.

Biodiversity Conservation Investment [OJK F.4]

In 2024, the Company through JMB allocated a budget for its biodiversity program investment totaling USD16.5 thousand.

## EMISSION

The Company supports the government's commitment outlined in Presidential Regulation of the Republic of Indonesia No. 61 of 2011, which pertains to the National Action Plan for Reducing Greenhouse Gas Emissions, known as "RAN-GRK." The Company strives to make significant contributions to environmental protection and preservation efforts aimed at mitigating climate change and the extreme weather events occurring globally.

Astrindo adopts a structured approach to managing the impact of its operational activities. The Company focuses on the efficient use of energy, including both fuel and electricity, to ensure environmental sustainability.
