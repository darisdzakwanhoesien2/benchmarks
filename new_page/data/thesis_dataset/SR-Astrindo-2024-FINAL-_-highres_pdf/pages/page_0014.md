## BUSINESS OUTLOOK

The Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources (ESDM) has set a target for coal production in 2025 to reach 735 million tons, which marks a 3.52% increase over the 2024 production target of 710 million tons. Approximately 230 million tons of this total will be allocated for domestic needs, accounting for about 30.3% of total coal production.

Given these conditions, the Company is optimistic about achieving better growth in the coming year. The Company will continue to strengthen its capabilities to support the achievement of its targets and ensure business sustainability.
