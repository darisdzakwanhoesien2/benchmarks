# MENGEVALUASI KINERJA BADAN TATA KELOLA TERTINGGI [GRI 2-18]

Sebagai bentuk wujud nyata pelaksanaan prinsip-prinsip GCG, Perusahaan memiliki kebijakan Penilaian kinerja Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi. Penilaian dilaksanakan setiap tahun dengan menggunakan metode penilaian mandiri (self-assessment) yang dilakukan oleh masing-masing anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi.

Pada metode self-assessment tersebut, Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi melakukan evaluasi mandiri terhadap kinerja dan kontribusi yang telah diberikan kepada organ-organ pendukung Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, efektivitas pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab sebagaimana tercantum dalam Pedoman Direksi atau Board Manual, serta pencapaian target-target KPI Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi. Selanjutnya, hasil penilaian mandiri tersebut diinterpretasikan ke dalam laporan.

Pada tahapan selanjutnya, laporan penilaian kinerja tersebut disampaikan di dalam RUPST sebagai bentuk pertanggungjawaban Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi kepada pemegang saham. Informasi mengenai evaluasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dapat dilihat pada Laporan Tahunan bab Tata Kelola Perusahaan.

# EVALUATING THE PERFORMANCE OF THE HIGHEST GOVERNANCE BODY [GRI 2-18]

As a concrete manifestation of the implementation of GCG principles, the Company has established a policy for the Performance Assessment of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors. The assessment is conducted annually through a self-assessment method by each member of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors.

In the self-assessment method, the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors conduct an independent evaluation of the performance and contributions made to the supporting organs of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors, the effectiveness of the implementation of duties and responsibilities as stated in the Board Manual, and the achievement of KPI targets of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors. Furthermore, the results of the self-assessment are compiled into a report.

Subsequently, this performance assessment report is presented at the AGMS as a means of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors' accountability to the shareholders. Detailed information regarding the evaluation of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors can be found in the Annual Report, specifically in the Good Corporate Governance chapter.
