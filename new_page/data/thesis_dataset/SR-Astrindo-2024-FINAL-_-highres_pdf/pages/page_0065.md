# KOMITMEN PERSEROAN TERHADAP PELESTARIAN LINGKUNGAN

PT Astrindo Nusantara Infrastruktur Tbk sebagai perusahaan pertambangan batu bara berkomitmen penuh memenuhi tanggung jawab lingkungan melalui upaya pelestarian lingkungan hidup yang dijalankan secara konsisten dan terukur. Perseroan menerapkan praktik-praktik operasi berwawasan lingkungan dalam kegiatan operasionalnya sebagai langkah konkret pengurangan dampak buruk bagi lingkungan hidup di sekitar area operasional.

Perseroan menerapkan praktik-praktik operasi berwawasan lingkungan melalui serangkaian kegiatan yang bertujuan untuk mendukung upaya pemanfaatan, penataan, pemeliharaan, pengawasan, pengendalian, pemulihan dan pengembangan lingkungan hidup selaras dengan peningkatan kualitas lingkungan yang berada di wilayah operasi Perseroan. Perseroan memastikan limbah yang dihasilkan dari aktivitas operasional senantiasa dikelola secara baik, dalam rangka meminimalisasi dampak buruk terhadap lingkungan.

Di samping itu, kami juga memastikan kegiatan operasional dijalankan dengan memperhatikan upaya mitigasi terhadap risiko lingkungan, melalui manajemen risiko yang komprehensif. Perseroan juga mempersiapkan dan mengalokasikan anggaran secara berkala bagi program-program pelestarian lingkungan hidup, sesuai skala usaha dan target yang ditetapkan. [0JK F.4]

Seiring hal itu, Perseroan memastikan kegiatan operasionalnya senantiasa memenuhi ketentuan atau izin yang disyaratkan, berupa rekomendasi Upaya Pengelolaan Lingkungan Hidup, dokumen Analisis Mengenai Dampak Lingkungan Hidup (AMDAL) serta Upaya Pengelolaan Lingkungan Hidup dan Upaya Pemantauan Lingkungan Hidup (UKL-UPL). Untuk memperkuat komitmen terhadap pelestarian lingkungan, Perseroan memiliki kebijakan internal, yang secara khusus mengatur upaya pencegahan pencemaran dan kontaminasi lingkungan.

# THE COMPANY'S CONTRIBUTION TO ENVIRONMENTAL CONSERVATION

PT Astrindo Nusantara Infrastruktur Tbk, as a coal mining company, is fully committed to fulfilling its environmental responsibilities through consistent and measurable environmental conservation efforts. The Company implements environmentally conscious operational practices as a concrete step to reduce the negative impact on the surrounding environment.

The Company implements these environmentally conscious practices through a series of activities aimed at utilizing, organizing, maintaining, supervising, controlling, restoring, and developing the environment, all in line with improving the quality of the areas where the Company operates. The Company ensures that the waste generated from its operational activities is managed properly to minimize any negative effects on the environment.

Additionally, we ensure that our operational activities are carried out with a focus on environmental risk mitigation through comprehensive risk management. The Company also prepares and allocates a periodic budget for environmental conservation programs based on the scale of its business and the specific targets set.[0JK F.4]

In alignment with these efforts, the Company ensures that all operational activities meet the required regulations and permits, including recommendations for Environmental Management Efforts, Environmental Impact Analysis (AMDAL) documents, and Environmental Management and Monitoring Efforts (UKL-UPL). To strengthen its commitment to environmental preservation, the Company has established an internal policy specifically designed to prevent environmental pollution and contamination.
