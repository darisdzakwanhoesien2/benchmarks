# Pengelolaan dan Pengolahan Limbah

## Waste Management and Treatment

Respon pada perubahan iklim juga dilakukan dengan melakukan pengelolaan dan pengolahan limbah, yang ditimbulkan dari kegiatan pertambangan dan pendukungnya. Pengungkapan informasi pengelolaan limbah berdasarkan Standar GRI edisi 2020. Pengelolaan dan pengolahan limbah dilakukan sesuai bentuk dan karakteristik limbah, baik dilakukan sendiri maupun diserahkan kepada pihak ketiga. Melalui pengelolaan limbah, Perusahaan juga berupaya mengurangi potensi dampak terhadap lingkungan. [306-1, 306-2] [POJK51-6.e.5.b] [MM2]

The climate change response also involves managing and treating waste generated from the mining and support activities. The waste management information disclosed is based on the 2020 GRI Standards. Waste management and treatment is carried out by the Company, or is sent to a third party, based to the shape and characteristics of the waste. By managing its waste the Company is looking to reduce any potential impact on the environment. [306-1, 306-2] [POJK51-6.e.5.b] [MM2]
