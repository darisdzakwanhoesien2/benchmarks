PT Tunas Inti Abadi (TIA), Penghargaan Platinum Indonesian CSR Award (ICA) 2020

PT Tunas Inti Abadi (TIA), Platinum Indonesian CSR Award (ICA) 2020

Penghargaan diberikan atas upaya rehabilitasi dan pelestarian ekosistem kawasan lahan kritis di Daerah Aliran Sungai (DAS) Taman Hutan Raya Sultan Adam, Kalimantan Selatan, melalui skema pemberdayaan masyarakat setempat.

The award was in recognition of the efforts made to rehabilitate and conserve critical land ecosystems in the Sultan Adam Forest Park Watershed (DAS), South Kalimantan, through a local community empowerment scheme.
