# Daftar Topik Material

Tidak ada perubahan material dari Laporan Keberlanjutan periode sebelumnya, yang ditetapkan berdasarkan kajian dengan pemangku kepentingan internal. Hal ini didasari situasi yang dihadapi Perusahaan pada periode pelaporan, tidak terlalu berbeda dengan periode sebelumnya. Kami hanya menambahkan pengungkapan informasi terkait penanganan pandemi COVID-19. [102-47]

# Material Topics

There were no material changes from the previous period's Sustainability Report, which was determined based on a study with internal stakeholders, and were based on the situation facing by the Company in the reporting period, which not changed significantly from the previous period. We have however added disclosure of information related to handling the COVID-19 pandemic. [102-47]
