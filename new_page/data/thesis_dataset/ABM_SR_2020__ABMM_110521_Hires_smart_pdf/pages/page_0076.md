# Operasi Pertambangan yang Berkeadilan
## Equitable Mining Operations

"PT ABM Investama Tbk berkomitmen menjalankan kegiatan operasi pertambangan yang berkeadilan sehingga mendatangkan kesejahteraan bagi pekerja dan masyarakat di sekitar area operasi. Di masa pandemi COVID-19 Perusahaan turut menjaga kesejahteraan pekerja, dan masyarakat melalui bantuan tanggung jawab sosial perusahaan (CSR)."

"PT ABM Investama Tbk is committed to running mining operations that are just so they bring prosperity to workers and the communities around the operation areas. During the COVID-19 pandemic, the Company helped maintain the welfare of workers and the communities through its corporate social responsibility (CSR) assistance."

2020 Sustainability Report
