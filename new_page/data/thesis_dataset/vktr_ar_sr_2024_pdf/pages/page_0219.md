# CHANGES TO ACCOUNTING POLICIES

Effective January 1, 2024, the Company has adopted new standards and amendments to Financial Accounting Standards (PSAK), as follows:

&gt; Amendment to PSAK 201 (formerly PSAK 1), "Presentation of Financial Statements", concerning non-current liabilities with covenants;
&gt; Amendment to PSAK 207 (formerly PSAK 2), "Statement of Cash Flows", and PSAK 107 (formerly PSAK 60), "Financial Instruments: Disclosures", concerning supplier financing arrangements; and
&gt; Amendment to PSAK 116 (formerly PSAK 73), "Leases", concerning leaseback transactions.

The adoption of these amendments did not have an impact on the Company's consolidated financial statements.
