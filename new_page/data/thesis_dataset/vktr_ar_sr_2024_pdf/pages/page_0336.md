KEBIJAKAN ANTI KORUPSI DAN SOSIALISASINYA ANTI-CORRUPTION POLICY AND ITS DISSEMINATION

Perseroan telah menyusun serta menerapkan kebijakan dan prosedur terkait anti-fraud serta pelaporan penerimaan gratifikasi. Selain itu, Perseroan terus berupaya menciptakan persaingan usaha yang sehat dan transparan, sekaligus mencegah tindakan atau perilaku yang dapat memicu konflik kepentingan maupun praktik korupsi, kolusi, dan nepotisme (KKN).

Setiap pelanggaran terhadap ketentuan dan kebijakan tersebut akan ditindaklanjuti dengan tegas, terutama jika tindakan tersebut berpotensi menimbulkan kerugian finansial maupun non-finansial bagi Perseroan, termasuk yang dapat merusak nama baik dan citra perusahaan.

Perseroan tidak menerima adanya pelaporan pelanggaran terkait tindakan korupsi, kolusi dan nepotisme maupun fraud serta gratifikasi di lingkungan usaha Perseroan selama tahun 2024.

The Company has established and implemented anti-fraud policies and procedures, along with gratification reporting mechanisms. Additionally, the Company remains committed to fostering fair and transparent business competition, while preventing actions or behaviors that could lead to conflicts of interest or corrupt, collusive, and nepotistic (KKN) practices.

Any violation of these policies and regulations will be addressed decisively, particularly if the actions result in financial or non-financial losses to the Company, including those that could damage its reputation and corporate image.

The Company did not receive any reports of violations related to acts of corruption, collusion and nepotism as well as fraud and gratification in the Company's business environment during 2024.
