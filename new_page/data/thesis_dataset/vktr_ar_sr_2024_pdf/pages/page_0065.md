## Penilaian Kinerja Komite di Bawah Direksi

Hingga 31 Desember 2024, Perseroan belum membentuk komite khusus di bawah Direksi. Namun demikian, Direksi didukung oleh sejumlah fungsi pendukung yang berperan penting dalam memastikan pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya berjalan sesuai dengan ketentuan peraturan perundangan dan prinsip GCG. Di antaranya adalah Sekretaris Perusahaan yang berperan dalam menjembatani komunikasi antara Perseroan dengan pemangku kepentingan, serta Komite Audit Internal yang membantu Direksi dalam pengawasan dan pemantauan efektivitas pengendalian internal dan manajemen risiko. Kedua fungsi ini telah menjalankan perannya secara positif dan mendukung terciptanya tata kelola yang transparan dan akuntabel.

## Perubahan Komposisi Direksi

Tahun 2024 menjadi tonggak penting dalam perjalanan VKTR dengan terjadinya perubahan signifikan dalam susunan Direksi, sebagai bagian dari strategi Perseroan untuk memperkuat kepemimpinan dan merespons secara adaptif terhadap dinamika industri kendaraan listrik yang terus berkembang.

Pada bulan Desember 2024, Perseroan dengan antusias menyambut Bapak V. Bimo Kurniatmoko sebagai anggota baru Direksi. Beliau membawa rekam jejak profesional yang kuat di sektor otomotif dan teknologi, termasuk pengalaman kepemimpinan di sejumlah perusahaan terkemuka. Pengangkatan ini mencerminkan komitmen VKTR dalam menghadirkan ethics. Throughout 2024, the Company did not receive any reports through the implemented WBS.

In addition, risk management is a crucial element in the Company's strategic decision-making process, addressing uncertainties that may impact the achievement of long-term objectives. To strengthen its risk governance, the Company applies the Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) framework. The effectiveness of the risk management system is regularly evaluated by the Audit Committee with support from the Internal Audit Unit, and the results are used as the basis for recommendations submitted to the Board of Directors. Based on the latest evaluation, the Board of Commissioners concluded that the risk management system in place is adequate and effective in supporting operational continuity and preventing potential business disruptions.

## Performance Assessment of the Committee Under the Board of Directors

As of 31 December 2024, the Company had not established any dedicated committees under the Board of Directors. Nevertheless, the Board is supported by key functions that serve a vital role in ensuring that its duties and responsibilities are fulfilled in accordance with applicable laws and regulations, as well as the principles of GCG. These include the Corporate Secretary, who facilitates communication between the Company and its stakeholders, and the Internal Audit Committee, which assists the Board in overseeing and monitoring the effectiveness of internal control and risk management. Both functions have carried out their roles positively, contributing to the establishment of transparent and accountable governance.

## Changes In the Board of Directors Composition

The year 2024 marked a pivotal moment for VKTR with a significant change in the composition of the Board of Directors, part of the Company's broader strategy to reinforce leadership capabilities and respond proactively to the evolving challenges and opportunities within the electric vehicle industry.

In December 2024, we were pleased to welcome Mr. V. Bimo Kurniatmoko as the newest member of VKTR's Board of Directors.
