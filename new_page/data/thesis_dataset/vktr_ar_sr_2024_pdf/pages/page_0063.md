# PENERAPAN TATA KELOLA PERUSAHAAN

Perseroan bersama entitas anak berkomitmen penuh untuk menerapkan Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik (Good Corporate Governance/GCG) sebagai fondasi utama dalam menjalankan dan mengembangkan bisnis. Penerapan prinsip-prinsip GCG memungkinkan Perseroan untuk lebih adaptif terhadap dinamika pasar, merespons tantangan secara tepat waktu, serta merancang strategi berkelanjutan guna meningkatkan daya saing dan mendorong pertumbuhan jangka panjang.

Pelaksanaan GCG yang konsisten berperan penting dalam menciptakan sistem operasional yang transparan, akuntabel, bertanggung jawab, independen, dan adil. Prinsip-prinsip ini tidak hanya memperkuat integritas dan efisiensi Perseroan, tetapi juga meningkatkan kepercayaan para pemangku kepentingan, termasuk investor, pelanggan, mitra strategis, dan regulator. Looking ahead to 2025, the Company is focused on a sustainable growth strategy, with top priorities on boosting sales and marketing activities to expand the electric vehicle market share in Indonesia. In the 1st quarter, the Company successfully delivered 20 (twenty) units of CKD electric buses for the first time to Transjakarta which will be operated by Sinarjaya. In the 2nd quarter, the Company has also successfully obtained PO 80 (eighty) units of CKD electric buses to Perum DAMRI for Transjakarta. In terms of strengthening the Company's operations and finances, VKTR will also optimize financial management by enhancing operational efficiency and expanding access to funding through the capital market, in support of the activation and operation of its expanded assembly facility. As part of this strategy, the Company aims to gradually increase production capacity to meet the growing demand from both government and corporate sectors.

With these strategies, VKTR aims to solidify its position as a leader in Indonesia's sustainable mobility industry and contribute meaningfully to the achievement of the country's energy transition targets.

# CORPORATE GOVERNANCE PRACTICES

The Company and its subsidiaries are fully committed to implementing Good Corporate Governance (GCG) as a fundamental cornerstone in managing and expanding the business. The application of GCG principles enables the Company to remain responsive to market dynamics, address challenges promptly, and design sustainable strategies that enhance competitiveness and drive long-term growth.

Consistent implementation of GCG serves a vital role in building an operational system grounded in transparency, accountability, responsibility, independence, and fairness. These principles not only strengthen the Company's integrity and efficiency but also foster greater trust among stakeholders, including investors, customers, strategic partners, and regulators.
