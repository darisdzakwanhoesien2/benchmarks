## Spare Parts and Accessories Trading

### Marketing through Direct Sales

The marketing strategy for spare parts and accessories is conducted through direct engagement with automotive companies and metal industries that require these components. Upon receiving a specific request, the Company manufactures spare parts according to inspection results and customer requirements for commercial vehicles.

In addition to direct sales, the Company also offers digital platforms such as an official website and an electronic catalog, allowing customers to browse available products at https://vktr.id.
