Untuk kemudahan penyampaian, istilah "Perseroan" merujuk pada PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk, yang berfokus pada perdagangan besar kendaraan bermotor listrik berbasis baterai, suku cadang, aksesori kendaraan, serta industri karoseri dan sepeda motor listrik roda dua dan tiga. This Sustainability Report has not undergone verification by an independent assurance service provider. However, VKTR has conducted a thorough internal verification process to ensure that all information presented in this report is accurate, reliable, and truly reflects the actual conditions.
