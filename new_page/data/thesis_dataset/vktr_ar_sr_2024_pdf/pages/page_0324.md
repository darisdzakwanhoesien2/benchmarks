## PELAKSANAAN KEGIATAN AUDIT INTERNAL TAHUN 2024

Pada tahun 2024, Departemen Audit Internal telah melaksanakan kegiatan audit dan konsultasi sebagai berikut:

1. Jasa Consulting atas Permasalahan Pertanggungjawaban Uang Muka.
2. Reviu atas SOP Penggunaan Kendaraan Operasional, Transportasi Berbasis Aplikasi dan Mileage.
3. Pengawasan dan Evaluasi Proyek Magelang Plant Q1.
4. Evaluasi Proses Bisnis dan Internal Control Kegiatan Riset.
5. Pengawasan dan Evaluasi Proyek Magelang Plant Q2.
6. Reviu SOP Tentang Gratifikasi dan Whistle Blowing System.
7. Pengawasan Tindak Lanjut Hasil Audit.

## ACTIVITIES OF THE INTERNAL AUDIT IN 2024

In 2024, the Internal Audit Department has carried out audit and consulting activities as follows:

1. Consulting services related to issues concerning advance payment accountability.
2. Review of SOPs for operational vehicle usage, app-based transportation, and mileage.
3. Monitoring and evaluation of the Magelang Plant Project – Q1.
4. Evaluation of business processes and internal controls for research activities.
5. Monitoring and evaluation of the Magelang Plant Project – Q2.
6. Review of SOPs on Gratification and the Whistleblowing System.
7. Monitoring of follow-up actions on audit findings.
