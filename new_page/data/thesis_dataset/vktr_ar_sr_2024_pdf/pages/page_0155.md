## KONDISI UMUM PEREKONOMIAN GLOBAL

Perekonomian dunia pada tahun 2024 tumbuh melambat dengan risiko ketidakpastian yang meningkat. Dinamika tersebut disebabkan beragam faktor antara lain fragmentasi ekonomi dan perdagangan antarnegara yang semakin meningkat, ketegangan geopolitik yang berlanjut seperti perang Rusia dan Ukraina serta di kawasan Timur Tengah, dan perubahan iklim ekstrem.

Ekonomi negara maju seperti Amerika Serikat (AS) menunjukkan pemulihan seiring dengan membaiknya permintaan domestik, khususnya pada sektor jasa dan investasi di teknologi informasi serta jasa keuangan. Selain itu jumlah tenaga kerja yang terserap di dunia kerja meningkat.

## GENERAL CONDITION OF THE GLOBAL ECONOMY

The global economy in 2024 is experiencing a slowdown with increasing uncertainty. This dynamic is driven by various factors, including rising economic and trade fragmentation between countries, ongoing geopolitical tensions such as the Russia-Ukraine war and conflicts in the Middle East, and extreme climate change.

Advanced economies, such as the United States (US), are indicating signs of recovery, supported by improved domestic demand, particularly in the services sector, as well as investments in information technology and financial services. In addition, employment levels have increased as more workers enter the labor market.
