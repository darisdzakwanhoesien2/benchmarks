4. Strategic Partnerships with Regulators, Bus Operators, and Industry Partners

In conducting its business operations, the Company has established strategic partnerships with regulators, bus operators, and other industry partners. As part of its collaboration with regulators, the Company is in the process of signing a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Jasa Sarana and Transjakarta, a Regional Government-Owned Enterprise (BUMD) specializing in public transportation infrastructure management.

In its industrial partnerships, the Company collaborates with various strategic partners, including BYD and other principals, in the development of electric buses and other VKTR electric vehicle products. Through its collaboration with BYD, the Company successfully sold electric buses to Mayasari Bakti, which are now operating on several Transjakarta routes.
