5. The Investigation Team and/or the independent external party conducts verification and validation of the violation report, as well as of the results of the investigation that has been carried out.

6. After completing the verification and validation, the Investigation Team and/or the independent external party prepares an investigation report and submits it to the CEO, who then forwards it to the Ethics &amp; Compliance Committee for further processing. The Investigation Report (LHI) must at minimum include the facts/findings of the reported violation, the conclusions of the investigation supported by both preliminary and supporting evidence, as well as witness statements.

7. Based on the investigation report received, the Ethics &amp; Compliance Committee conducts a review and analysis of the investigation results as the basis for decision-making.
» If the violation is not proven, the Ethics &amp; Compliance Committee does not impose any sanctions, and the case is declared closed.
» If the violation is proven, the Ethics &amp; Compliance Committee proceeds to the next process.

8. If the violation is proven:
a. The Ethics &amp; Compliance Committee imposes a sanction and issues a Decision Letter on Violation Report Sanction Determination for the Reported Party.
b. The Ethics &amp; Compliance Committee submits the Decision Letter on Violation Report Sanction Determination to the CEO for follow-up.
c. The Ethics &amp; Compliance Committee may refer the case to the Corporate Legal Department for any violations of laws and regulations to be followed up with the relevant authorities.

9. The CEO processes the follow-up of the Decision Letter on Violation Report Sanction Determination and delivers it to the Reported Party.
