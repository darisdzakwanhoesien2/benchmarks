However, in line with investment realization for the expansion of electric bus production facilities and the provision of after-sales services for electric buses currently operated by Transjakarta, there was an increase in several operating expense components. These included advertising and promotion costs, business travel expenses, employee salaries and benefits, as well as professional service fees. The increase in various expense components led to a 4.11% increase in operating expenses, from Rp162.09 billion in 2023 to Rp168.74 billion, resulting in an operating profit of Rp9.40 billion, a significant decline of 81.67% from Rp51.30 billion in 2023.

The increase in operating expenses and other costs resulted in a profit before income tax of Rp13.44 billion in 2024, a decline of 68.18% from Rp42.25 billion in 2023. After deducting an income tax expense of Rp1.15 billion, net profit stood at Rp12.29 billion, decreased by 58.42% from Rp29.56 billion in 2023.

From a financial position perspective, the Company recorded a contraction due to the realization of investment in the assembly facility in Magelang, Central Java. Total assets decreased by 3.58% from Rp1,668.36 billion in 2023 to Rp1,608.60 billion. Current assets declined by 29.36% to Rp729.98 billion. Non-current assets reached Rp878.63 billion in 2024, an increase of 38.37% compared to Rp635.00 billion in 2023. The decline in current assets was mainly attributed to a decrease in Cash and Cash Equivalents by 89.79% to Rp75.12 billion. In addition, there was a decrease in Net Inventory value by 1.77% to Rp139.91 billion.

VKTR's total liabilities were recorded at Rp452.68 billion in 2024, a decrease of 13.03% compared to Rp520.50 billion in 2023. The decrease in liabilities was due to a 48.57% reduction in non-current liabilities, which declined to Rp160.27 billion.

The decrease in long-term liabilities was mainly due to an 82.70% drop in related party payables to Rp31.99 billion, a 28.31% decrease in lease liabilities to Rp6.66 billion, and a 3.02% reduction in employee benefit liabilities to Rp104.57 billion in 2024. Meanwhile, the Company's equity stood at Rp1,155.93 billion in 2024, growing by 0.70% compared to Rp1,147.86 billion in 2023.
