# MENCIPTAKAN SUMBER DAYA MANUSIA YANG UNGGUL [POJK F.18]

Sumber Daya Manusia (SDM) merupakan pilar utama bagi keberlanjutan dan pertumbuhan bisnis VKTR. Perseroan menyadari bahwa pengelolaan dan pengembangan SDM yang unggul sangat penting untuk mendukung visi dan misi Perseroan di tengah dinamika pasar yang kompetitif. Oleh karena itu, pengembangan talenta dan kompetensi karyawan selalu menjadi prioritas dalam strategi Perseroan.

Pada tahun 2024, Perseroan telah menetapkan sejumlah kebijakan untuk pengelolaan SDM yang efektif dan berkelanjutan. Fokus utama kebijakan ini mencakup pengembangan kompetensi, pemberian benefit yang memadai bagi karyawan, serta penerapan prinsip keberagaman dan inklusi di lingkungan kerja. Untuk mendukung tujuan tersebut, Perseroan juga mengimplementasikan teknologi seperti Human Capital Management (HCM) yang terintegrasi dengan Enterprise Resource Information System (ERIS) dan platform pelatihan daring Satukelas, guna meningkatkan efisiensi dan pengalaman kerja karyawan. [POJK F.17]

## Kebijakan Pengelolaan SDM 2024:

1. Pengembangan Kompetensi: Perseroan fokus pada peningkatan keterampilan, pengetahuan, dan kemampuan karyawan melalui pelatihan berkelanjutan, pengembangan karier, dan evaluasi kinerja.
2. Benefit Karyawan: Menyediakan program kesehatan, kebugaran, serta kompensasi dan tunjangan yang mendukung kesejahteraan karyawan, termasuk program perjalanan dinas.
3. Keberagaman &amp; Inklusi: Mengedepankan penerimaan dan kesetaraan melalui kebijakan equal opportunity employment dan menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang aman dan sehat.
4. Penerapan Teknologi: Memanfaatkan teknologi untuk mendukung fleksibilitas dan efisiensi dalam pengelolaan SDM, termasuk dengan sistem HCM yang terintegrasi.

# DEVELOPING EXCELLENT HUMAN RESOURCES [POJK F.18]

Human Resources (HR) are the main pillar of sustainability and business growth at VKTR. The Company recognizes that the management and development of outstanding HR are essential to support its vision and mission amidst a dynamic and competitive market environment. As such, talent development and employee competency enhancement are always a priority in the Company's strategic move.

In 2024, the Company established several policies for effective and sustainable HR management. The main focus of these policies includes competency development, providing adequate employee benefits, and implementing the principles of diversity and inclusion in the workplace. To support these goals, the Company also adopted technologies such as the Human Capital Management (HCM) system integrated with the Enterprise Resource Information System (ERIS) and the Satukelas online training platform, aimed at improving both operational efficiency and employee experience. [POJK F.17]

## HR Management Policy 2024:

1. Competency Development: The Company focuses on enhancing employees' skills, knowledge, and abilities through continuous training, career development, and performance evaluations.
2. Employee Benefits: Providing health and wellness programs, as well as compensation and allowances that support employee well-being, including business travel programs.
3. Diversity &amp; Inclusion: Promoting acceptance and equality through equal opportunity employment policies and fostering a safe and healthy work environment.
4. Technology Implementation: Utilizing technology to support flexibility and efficiency in HR management, including an integrated HCM system.

The Company encounters various external challenges, including economic shifts, technological developments such as automation and artificial intelligence (AI), and changes in government regulations affecting employment.
