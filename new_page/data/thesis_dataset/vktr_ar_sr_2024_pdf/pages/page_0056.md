## Kinerja Operasional Segmen Usaha

Secara operasional, bisnis Perseroan terbagi ke dalam dua segmen utama, yaitu Perdagangan dan Manufaktur, yang masing-masing memainkan peran strategis dalam mendukung pertumbuhan berkelanjutan dan penguatan industri kendaraan listrik di Indonesia. Tinjauan operasional mencakup strategi bisnis dan pemasaran, kinerja penjualan KBLBB, serta efisiensi proses manufaktur.

Melalui anak perusahaan, VKTR memperluas penetrasi ke pasar kendaraan penumpang dengan mengamankan proyek pengembangan model baru dan memfokuskan produksi pada segmen Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM). Ke depan, Perseroan juga akan meningkatkan kapasitas produksi untuk Original Equipment Supplier (OES) atau pasar suku cadang (aftermarket) sebagai bagian dari strategi diversifikasi, guna mengurangi ketergantungan pada penjualan Agen Tunggal Pemegang Merek (ATPM) dan menciptakan aliran pendapatan yang lebih stabil.

Sepanjang tahun 2024, kinerja segmen perdagangan VKTR tetap kuat dengan penjualan kendaraan listrik yang masih didominasi oleh segmen B2G, khususnya melalui kerja sama strategis dengan Transjakarta. Perseroan juga mulai memperluas pangsa pasar ke segmen B2B, termasuk melalui penjualan dengan total 8 unit bus listrik dimana 1 unit telah sukses dikirimkan pada tahun 2023 dan sisanya pada tahun 2024 ke PT RAPP. Dalam rangka memperluas jangkauan distribusi kendaraan listrik komersial di Indonesia, VKTR menjalin kemitraan dengan Indomobil melalui PT IMG Sejahtera Langgeng. Selain kendaraan, VKTR juga aktif memperdagangkan suku cadang seperti brake drum dan disc brake, baik untuk kendaraan konvensional maupun berbasis listrik.

## Business Segment Operational Performance

Operationally, VKTR manages its business through two main segments, Trade and Manufacturing, each serving a strategic role in driving sustainable growth and supporting the broader development of the electric vehicle sector in Indonesia. The operational review includes business and marketing strategy execution, BEV sales performance, and manufacturing process efficiency.

Through its subsidiary, the Company expanded its reach into the passenger vehicle segment by securing a new vehicle model development project, with production efforts focused on the Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) segment. Looking ahead, VKTR plans to increase its capacity for Original Equipment Supplier (OES) or aftermarket production as part of its diversification strategy. This move is designed to reduce dependency on sales through brand-holding sole agents (ATPM) and to create more stable and sustainable business opportunities.

Throughout 2024, VKTR's trading segment remained strong, with electric vehicle sales still dominated by the B2G segment, particularly through strategic collaboration with Transjakarta. The Company also began expanding its market share into the B2B segment, including through the sale of a total of 8 electric buses, with 1 unit successfully delivered in 2023 and the remaining units in 2024 to PT RAPP. To further extend the distribution of commercial electric vehicles across Indonesia, VKTR partnered with Indomobil through PT IMG Sejahtera Langgeng. In addition to vehicles, VKTR was actively involved in trading spare parts such as brake drums and disc brakes for both conventional and electric vehicles.

Through its subsidiary PT Bakrie Autoparts and its line of sub-subsidiaries, VKTR strengthened its presence in the passenger vehicle segment by securing a new model development project for OEM products, with plans to expand into the OES market as part of its diversification strategy.
