MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION &amp; ANALYSIS

Pengisian Kendaraan Listrik Umum (SPKLU) oleh PT PLN (Persero). Pada tahun 2024, jumlah SPKLU meningkat hingga sebesar 299% menjadi 3.233 unit dibandingkan tahun 2023 sejumlah 1.081 unit. Jumlah SPKLU telah tersebar di 2.306 lokasi di seluruh Indonesia. Peningkatan jumlah SPKLU paling signifikan terjadi di sepanjang ruas jalur Trans Jawa dan Trans Sumatra.

Selain itu, jumlah fasilitas Home Charging Services (HCS) juga meningkat sebesar 302%, dari 9.393 unit di 2023 menjadi total 28.356 unit di 2024. PLN juga meningkatkan jumlah Stasiun Pengisian Listrik Umum (SPLU) yang naik menjadi 9.956 unit di 2024.

Peningkatan SPKLU berdampak pada naiknya jumlah transaksi di SPKLU sebesar 337% menjadi 402.509 kali transaksi dibandingkan di 2023 yang sebesar 119.600 kali transaksi.

Jumlah kendaraan listrik di Indonesia terus meningkat. Mengutip data Gabungan Industri Kendaraan Bermotor Indonesia (Gaikindo), pada 2024, penjualan mobil secara nasional mencapai 865.723 unit. Dari jumlah tersebut, mobil listrik yang terjual sebanyak 43.188 unit, atau meraih pangsa pasar sebesar 5%. Penjualan mobil listrik pada 2024 mengalami lonjakan signifikan dibandingkan 2023, di mana pangsa pasar BEV masih sebesar 1,7 persen atau sebanyak 17.051 unit dari total penjualan 1.005.802 unit.

Electric Vehicle Charging Station (SPKLU) network, managed by PT PLN (Persero). In 2024, the number of SPKLUs increased by 299%, reaching 3,233 units, compared to 1,081 units in 2023. These charging stations have been distributed across 2,306 locations throughout Indonesia, with the most significant increases occurring along the Trans-Java and Trans-Sumatra routes.

Furthermore, the number of Home Charging Services (HCS) increased by 302%, from 9,393 units in 2023 to a total of 28,356 units in 2024. PLN also expanded the number of Public Electric Charging Stations (SPLU), reaching 9,956 units in 2024.

The expansion of SPKLU stations has led to a 337% increase in transactions, reaching 402,509 transactions in 2024, compared to 119,600 transactions in 2023.

The number of electric vehicles in Indonesia continues to grow. According to data from the Association of Indonesian Automotive Industries (Gaikindo), in 2024, national car sales reached 865,723 units. Of this total, electric vehicle sales amounted to 43,188 units, securing a 5% market share. EV sales in 2024 experienced a significant surge compared to 2023, when battery electric vehicles (BEVs) accounted for only 1.7% of the market, with 17,051 units sold out of a total of 1,005,802 vehicles.
