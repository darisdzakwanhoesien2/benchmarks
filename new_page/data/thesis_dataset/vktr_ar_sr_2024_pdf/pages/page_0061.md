Another challenge faced is the substantial investment required for the development of CKD-based electric vehicle production facilities and the battery ecosystem, which demands careful long-term planning and financing. From a policy perspective, government incentives are needed to accelerate the electrification of commercial vehicles at the national level. In addition, an in-depth analysis of raw material subsidy utilization should also be conducted, with reference to benchmarking practices from countries that are more advanced in implementing electrification.

In response, VKTR implemented a range of strategic initiatives. The Company diversified its raw material sources and automotive components to reduce reliance on any single supplier, while also expanding its product lines to reach broader market segments. Workforce readiness was addressed through continuous training programs aimed at strengthening operational capabilities and maintaining product quality standards.

To address regulatory challenges, the Company has strengthened its governance and legal compliance systems by updating internal policies and establishing a more adaptive organizational structure in response to changes in external regulations. VKTR also continues to carry out advocacy and education efforts across various levels of stakeholders, as well as foster and expand strategic partnerships with various stakeholders to enhance its business network and reduce the risk of dependency on a single partner.

From an infrastructure and technology perspective, the development of Indonesia's first CKD-based EV assembly facility marked a milestone in strengthening the national EV industry. VKTR also embraced digital technologies, including Data Loss Protection (DLP), cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence (AI), to support comprehensive digital transformation and operational excellence.
