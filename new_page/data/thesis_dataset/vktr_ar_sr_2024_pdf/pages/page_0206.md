TINJAUAN KEUANGAN FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE

# Penjualan

Komponen penjualan Perseroan terdiri dari perdagangan komponen suku cadang serta penjualan Kendaraan Bermotor Listrik Berbasis Baterai (KBLBB).

Penjualan komponen suku cadang sebesar Rp862,22 miliar di tahun 2024, turun sebesar 10,21% dibandingkan tahun 2023 Rp960,29 miliar. Penjualan KBLBB sebesar Rp144,24 miliar di tahun 2024, naik sebesar 35,87% dibandingkan tahun 2023 sebesar Rp106,16 miliar.

Setelah dikurangi retur dan diskon penjualan sebesar Rp2,80 miliar, total penjualan neto pada tahun 2024 tercatat sebesar Rp1.003,66 miliar, menurun sedikit sebesar 5,48% dibandingkan tahun 2023 sebesar Rp1.061,86 miliar,

# Beban Pokok Penjualan

Komponen Beban Pokok Penjualan terdiri dari beban manufaktur dan beban perdagangan. Beban manufaktur mencakup biaya bahan baku, proses fabrikasi, tenaga kerja langsung, serta barang dalam penyelesaian. Sedangkan beban perdagangan mencerminkan berbagai biaya terkait aktivitas perdagangan.

Beban Pokok Penjualan tercatat sebesar Rp825,51 miliar di tahun 2024, menurun sebesar 2,71% dibandingkan tahun 2023 sebesar Rp848,47 miliar. Penurunan disebabkan berkurangnya beban manufaktur sebesar 8,88% menjadi Rp695,13 miliar di tahun 2024. Sedangkan biaya perdagangan meningkat sebesar 48,61% menjadi Rp135,48 miliar di tahun 2024.

Tidak terdapat pemasok dengan total pembelian kumulatif individu yang melebihi 10% dari total penjualan neto.

# Laba Bruto

Laba bruto Perseroan tercatat sebesar Rp178,15 miliar di tahun 2024, menurun sebesar 16,52% dibanding tahun 2023 sebesar Rp213,39 miliar.

# Beban Usaha

Beban usaha terdiri dari beban penjualan dan pemasaran, serta beban umum dan administrasi. Jumlah beban usaha sebesar Rp168,74 miliar di tahun 2024.

# Sales

The Company's sales components consist of trading in spare parts components and the sale of Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs).

Sales of spare parts components amounted to Rp862.22 billion in 2024, a decrease of 10.21% compared to Rp960.29 billion in 2023. Sales of BEVs reached Rp144.24 billion in 2024, an increase of 35.87% compared to Rp106.16 billion in 2023.

After deducting returns and sales discounts of Rp2.80 billion, total net sales in 2024 stood at Rp1,003.66 billion, representing a slight decrease of 5.48% compared to Rp1,061.86 billion in 2023.

# Cost of Goods Sold

The components of Cost of Goods Sold consist of manufacturing costs and trading costs. Manufacturing costs include raw material expenses, fabrication processes, direct labor, and work-in-progress. Meanwhile, trading costs reflect various expenses related to trading activities.

Cost of Goods Sold was recorded at Rp825.51 billion in 2024, a decrease of 2.71% compared to Rp848.47 billion in 2023. The decline was driven by an 8.88% decrease in manufacturing costs to Rp695.13 billion in 2024. On the other hand, trading costs increased by 48.61% to Rp135.48 billion in 2024.

There were no suppliers with individual cumulative purchases exceeding 10% of total net sales.

# Gross Profit

The Company's gross profit was recorded at Rp178.15 billion in 2024, a decrease of 16.52% compared to Rp213.39 billion in 2023.

# Operating Expenses

Operating expenses consist of selling and marketing expenses, as well as general and administrative expenses. Total operating expenses was Rp168.74
