## Alur Prosedur Whistleblowing System

Whistleblowing System di Perseroan merupakan sistem dan prosedur yang dirancang untuk mengelola (menerima, menelaah dan menindaklanjuti) pengaduan indikasi pelanggaran, seperti perilaku melawan hukum, perbuatan tidak etis/tidak semestinya, diadukan secara rahasia, anonim dan mandiri (independen) yang digunakan untuk mengoptimalkan peran serta pihak internal Perseroan dan pihak eksternal lainnya dalam mengungkapkan pelanggaran yang terjadi di lingkungan Perseroan.

Perseroan telah menerapkan WBS yang terstruktur dan transparan untuk memastikan penanganan laporan pelanggaran secara tepat. Prosedur pengelolaan pelaporan pelanggaran mencakup aspek-aspek Prosedur Pelaporan Pelanggaran yang Dilakukan oleh Karyawan; serta Prosedur Pelaporan Pelanggaran yang Dilakukan oleh Dewan Komisaris/Direksi/IA. Informasi lebih lengkap mengenai alur prosedur WBS tersedia di Bab Tata Kelola Perusahaan dalam Laporan Tahunan ini.

The WBS is part of a broader commitment by the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, and the entire management team to foster a culture of integrity that is applied in a structured, systematic, and comprehensive manner. Through the implementation of the WBS, the Company aims to build employee loyalty while creating a safe, open, and conducive work climate. This ensures that every employee feels valued and has the space to report issues that may threaten the sustainability of good governance, without fear or concern about negative repercussions for themselves.

## Whistleblowing System Workflow

The Company's Whistle Blowing System is a system and procedure designed to manage (receive, review, and follow up on) reports of suspected violations, such as unlawful conduct, unethical or improper behavior, which are submitted confidentially, anonymously, and independently. This system is used to optimize the participation of internal parties within the Company as well as other external parties in disclosing violations that occur within the Company's environment.

The Company has established a structured and transparent WBS to ensure the proper handling of reports related to violations. The procedure for managing whistleblowing reports covers the following aspects: the Whistleblowing Reporting Procedure for Employees and the Whistleblowing Reporting Procedure for the Board of Commissioners/Directors/IA. More detailed information on the WBS procedure flow is available in the Corporate Governance chapter of this Annual Report.
