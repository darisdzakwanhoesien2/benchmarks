# TATA KELOLA KEBERLANJUTAN

# SUSTAINABILITY GOVERNANCE

![img-236.jpeg](img-236.jpeg)

Perseroan berkomitmen untuk mengintegrasikan secara bertahap prinsip-prinsip keberlanjutan dalam setiap aspek operasionalnya, baik dari sisi lingkungan, sosial, maupun Tata Kelola Perusahaan (ESG). Perseroan memastikan transparansi dan akuntabilitas melalui penerapan prinsip-prinsip Good Corporate Governance (GCG).

Perseroan memiliki struktur organisasi yang jelas dengan Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi yang berkompeten dalam bidangnya, serta komite audit yang bertugas menjaga integritas laporan keuangan dan memastikan kepatuhan terhadap regulasi yang berlaku. Perseroan juga melakukan pelaporan terkait inisiatif keberlanjutan secara transparan, untuk memastikan bahwa setiap langkah yang diambil Perseroan selalu berorientasi pada keberlanjutan jangka panjang.

The Company is committed to gradually integrating sustainability principles into every aspect of its operations, covering environmental, social, and governance (ESG) aspects. The Company ensures transparency and accountability through the implementation of Good Corporate Governance (GCG) principles.

The Company has a clear organizational structure, with a Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors who are competent in their respective fields, as well as an audit committee responsible for safeguarding the integrity of financial reporting and ensuring compliance with applicable regulations. The Company also reports its sustainability initiatives in a transparent manner to ensure that every step taken is consistently aligned with long-term sustainability goals.
