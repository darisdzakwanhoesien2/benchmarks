# Capaian dan Kepatuhan Lingkungan [POJK F.16]

Hingga penghujung tahun 2024, Perseroan tidak tercatat melakukan pelanggaran terhadap regulasi di bidang pengelolaan limbah dan lingkungan hidup. Komitmen ini tercapai melalui serangkaian pengujian yang dilakukan secara terukur dan berkala, memastikan bahwa seluruh operasional Perseroan berjalan dengan memperhatikan dampak lingkungan yang minimal dan sesuai dengan peraturan yang berlaku. Selain itu, Perseroan juga tidak mencatat adanya tumpahan limbah yang terjadi di tahun 2024.

Implemented Waste Management Measures:

- Liquid Waste: Liquid waste is managed by the Department of Housing and Settlements, and wastewater quality is measured every six months by a third party for the Environmental Management and Monitoring Report (UKL-UPL).
- Domestic Wastewater Treatment: Ensuring that domestic wastewater discharged into the environment does not exceed established quality standards, and constructing sealed, watertight drainage systems.
- Hazardous Waste (B3) Management: B3 waste is handed over to officially licensed third parties and managed in accordance with regulations, with proper labeling applied.
- B3 Waste Storage: The Company has a hazardous waste (B3) storage facility that complies with safe storage procedures in accordance with prevailing regulations.
- Waste Segregation: Waste is sorted by type to minimize environmental impact, and adequate facilities are provided to support efficient waste management.
- Air and Noise Pollution: The Company monitors air quality and noise levels to ensure that operational activities do not exceed the limits set by regulations.
- Clinical Waste Management: Clinical waste is managed in collaboration with certified third parties that meet applicable medical waste management standards.

# Environmental Compliance and Achievements [POJK F.16]

As of the end of 2024, the Company recorded no violations of regulations related to waste and environmental management. This commitment was achieved through a series of measured and periodic testing processes to ensure that all Company operations are conducted with minimal environmental impact and in compliance with applicable laws. Furthermore, the Company did not record any waste spills during 2024.
