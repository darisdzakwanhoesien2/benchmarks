To achieve this target, the government actively promotes transportation electrification, including in the public transport sector, through the provision of various fiscal and non-fiscal incentives, both for industry players and the general public.

Supported by proactive policies and a clear regulatory direction, electric vehicle (EV) penetration in Indonesia has grown significantly. The market share of EVs increased from 1.7% in 2023 to around 5% in 2024, with projected sales of battery electric vehicles (BEVs) and hybrids reaching 45,000 and 65,000 units respectively in 2025. In the commercial EV segment, market potential remains vast, as its contribution currently accounts for less than 1% of total operating EVs. Transjakarta's target to operate 10,000 electric buses by 2030 and the Ministry of Transportation's support for public transport electrification serve as strong signals for continued growth in this sector.

As a pioneer in commercial electric vehicles, VKTR is seizing this market momentum by implementing a structured and long-term expansion strategy. The Company is preparing to commence operations of Indonesia's first CKD-based commercial EV assembly facility, located in Magelang, which is expected to begin operations in 2025. This facility will not only serve as a production center but also as a hub for technology transfer and electric vehicle research. It will also contribute to the creation of green jobs and support sustainable national economic growth.

From the product development side, VKTR has developed various types of electric vehicles, ranging from large buses, medium buses, trucks, to tractor heads. Prototypes of the large bus, medium bus, trucks, to tractor heads, and trucks (4×2 and 6×4) have obtained Type Test Certificates from the Ministry of Transportation. Meanwhile, the tractor head type is currently in the preparation stage for the vehicle certification process.
