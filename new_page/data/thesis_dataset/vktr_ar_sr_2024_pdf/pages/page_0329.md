Sehingga upaya yang dilakukan Perseroan dan Perusahaan Anak untuk meminimalisir risiko yang akan berdampak kepada kegiatan usaha Perseroan dan Perusahaan Anak adalah dengan melakukan investasi teknologi informasi yang tepat dan handal di seluruh kegiatan operasional Perseroan maupun Perusahaan Anak.

» Business Competition Risk
Due to the increasing competition in the Company's and its Subsidiaries' business segments, the Company and its Subsidiaries are enhancing services to provide greater convenience for customers and improving the quality of their resources. Additionally, they are introducing new innovations that add value to maintain their competitiveness in the industry.

» Risk of Technological Change
Technological changes and developments are inevitable for the Company and its Subsidiaries. Therefore, to mitigate risks that may impact their business activities, the Company and its Subsidiaries are investing in reliable and appropriate information technology across all operational activities.

» Risks related to Raw Material Supply
To address risks, particularly those related to the availability of raw material supplies, the Company and its Subsidiaries are diversifying their raw material sources from multiple suppliers. By implementing diversification, raw material supply will not be dependent on a single source.

» Risks related to Termination of Cooperation with Partners and Clients
To mitigate the risk of partnership and client termination, the Company and its Subsidiaries continuously strengthen relationships with existing partners while actively expanding collaborations with new partners.
