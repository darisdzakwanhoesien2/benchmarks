2. Peraturan Menteri Keuangan Nomor 135 Tahun 2024 tentang Pajak Penjualan atas Barang Mewah atas Impor dan/atau Penyerahan Barang Kena Pajak yang Tergolong Mewah Berupa Kendaraan Bermotor Listrik Berbasis Baterai Roda Empat Tertentu yang Ditanggung Pemerintah Tahun Anggaran 2025.

Melalui PMK 135/2024 tersebut, pemerintah memperpanjang pemberian insentif Pajak Penjualan atas Barang Mewah (PPnBM) kendaraan listrik hingga 31 Desember 2025. 2. Minister of Finance Regulation No. 135 of 2024 Regarding Luxury Goods Sales Tax on the Import and/or Delivery of Taxable Luxury Goods in the Form of Certain Four-Wheeled Battery Electric Vehicles Covered by the Government for the 2025 Fiscal Year

Through PMK 135/2024, the government has extended the Luxury Goods Sales Tax (PPnBM) incentive for electric vehicles until December 31, 2025. Previously, the PPnBM facility, which allowed the government to fully cover 100% of the tax owed, was set to expire on December 31, 2024, as regulated under PMK No. 9 of 2024. With the issuance of PMK No. 135 of 2024, PMK No. 9 of 2024 is no longer valid.
