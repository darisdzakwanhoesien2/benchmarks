# 2 Arief A. Dhani
Anggota Komite Audit
Member of the Audit Committee

Warga Negara Indonesia, 52 tahun, lahir tahun 1971 dan berdomisili di Jakarta. Meraih gelar Sarjana Akuntansi dari Universitas Indonesia (1995) dan Magister Manajemen Keuangan dari Universitas Pelita Harapan (2009). Beliau juga memiliki sertifikasi CPA (1998), sertifikasi Wakil Manajer Investasi (1998), dan CA (Chartered Accountant) (2013).

Indonesian citizen, 52 years old, born in 1971 and domiciled in Jakarta. He earned a Bachelor of Accounting from Universitas Indonesia (1995) and a Master of Financial Management from Universitas Pelita Harapan (2009). He also holds a CPA (1998), Investment Manager Representative Certification (1998), and CA (Chartered Accountant) designation (2013).

Beliau memulai karier sebagai Auditor di Drs Hadi Sutanto &amp; Partners (PricewaterhouseCoopers) (1994-2000), kemudian menjadi Partner di RSM AAJ Associates (2000-2009), Arman Dhani &amp; Partners (2009-2014), dan Arief Jauhari (sejak 2015). Pernah menjabat di Komite Audit PT Semen Baturaja Tbk, PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk, PT Wahana Ottomitra Multiartha Tbk, PT Samindo Resources Tbk, dan PT Bayan Resources Tbk, serta Komite Audit &amp; Manajemen Risiko PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk.

He began his career as an Auditor at Drs Hadi Sutanto &amp; Partners (PricewaterhouseCoopers) from 1994 to 2000, then served as Partner at RSM AAJ Associates (2000-2009), Arman Dhani &amp; Partners (2009-2014), and Arief Jauhari (since 2015). He has also held Audit Committee roles at PT Semen Baturaja Tbk, PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk, PT Wahana Ottomitra Multiartha Tbk, PT Samindo Resources Tbk, and PT Bayan Resources Tbk, and served in the Audit and Risk Management Committee at PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk.

# 3 A. Kristiyanto Wahyu Indriya
Anggota Komite Audit
Member of the Audit Committee

Warga Negara Indonesia, 62 tahun, lahir tahun 1962 dan berdomisili di Bekasi, Jawa Barat. Meraih gelar Sarjana Ekonomi dari Universitas Gadjah Mada (1989), Master of Science dari Universitas Indonesia (2002), dan Sarjana Hukum dari Universitas Bhayangkara (2009). Beliau juga memiliki Sertifikat Komite Audit (2016).

Beliau memulai karier sebagai Finance Controller di PT Bali Nirwana Resort (1998-1999), Finance Controller PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk (1999-2002), Audit Manager di Doli, Bambang, Sudarmadji &amp; Dadang (2002-2008), dan Audit Partner di Morison International (2008-2011) serta Crowe Indonesia (2011-2013). Beliau pernah menjabat sebagai anggota Komite Audit BPJS Ketenagakerjaan (2013-2017), Business Advisory Partner di CIBA (sejak 2013), Board Member di Tarakanita Education Institution (sejak 2018), anggota Komite Audit di PT Century Tokyo Leasing Indonesia (2021-2023), dan Rekan Audit di KAP Teramihardja, Pradhono, dan Chandra (Rodi &amp; Partner) sejak 2023.

Indonesian citizen, 62 years old, born in 1962 and domiciled in Bekasi, West Java. He earned a Bachelor of Economics from Universitas Gadjah Mada (1989), a Master of Science from Universitas Indonesia (2002), and a Bachelor of Law from Universitas Bhayangkara (2009). He also holds an Audit Committee Certificate (2016).

He began his career as Finance Controller at PT Bali Nirwana Resort (1998-1999), Finance Controller at PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk (1999-2002), served as Audit Manager at Doli, Bambang, Sudarmadji &amp; Dadang (2002-2008), and Audit Partner at Morison International (2008-2011) and Crowe Indonesia (2011-2013). He was Audit Committee Member at BPJS Employment (2013-2017), Business Advisory Partner at CIBA (since 2013), Board Member at Tarakanita Education Institution (since 2018), Audit Committee Member at PT Century Tokyo Leasing Indonesia (2021-2023), and Audit Partner at KAP Teramihardja, Pradhono, dan Chandra (Rodi &amp; Partner) since 2023.
