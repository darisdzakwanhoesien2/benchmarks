In response to external pressures, such as the decline in national automotive sales and slower GDP growth, Management adopted a disciplined financial strategy, cost efficiency measures, and capital expenditure optimization. This approach has preserved the Company's financial resilience and ensured continued operations in an increasingly challenging market environment.

The Board of Commissioners appreciates the proactive and adaptive policies of the Board of Directors, which remain focused on creating long-term value for all stakeholders and enhancing contributions to the green energy transition in the transportation sector. We also recognize the growing innovation from the Board of Directors and management in understanding client needs.

The Board of Commissioners supports the Board of Directors' approach, which emphasizes meeting the increasingly diverse needs of clients. This will be a key factor in building trust, accelerating adoption, and strengthening the Company's role in supporting a greener and more sustainable future.
