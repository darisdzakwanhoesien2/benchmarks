# PT Bakrie Autoparts (BA)

## Apresiasi &amp; Penghargaan yang diberikan oleh Appreciation and Award from:

1. Bakrie Amanah Kepada BA atas, ‘Partisipasi dan dukungan pada program Ramadhan Untuk Negeri 2024’
Bakrie Amanah to BA for, ‘Participation and support for Ramadhan Untuk Negeri program 2024’.

2. Hino Indonesia Manufacturer Club (HIMC) Kepada BA (Precision Team) atas, ‘Medali Perak dalam Event Konvensi QCC di Hino Innovation Convention (HICo) ke-7 2024’
Award and Medal from Hino Indonesia Manufacturer Club (HIMC) to BA for, ‘Silver Medal in QCC Convention event 7th Hino Innovation Convention (HICo) 2024’.

3. Hino Indonesia Manufacturer Club (HIMC) Kepada BA (Achmad Alfian) atas, ‘Medali Emas dalam Event Konvensi SS di Hino Innovation Convention (HICo) ke-7 2024’
Award and Medal from Hino Indonesia Manufacturer Club (HIMC) to BA (Achmad Alfian) for, ‘Gold Medal in SS Convention event 7th Hino Innovation Convention (HICo) 2024’.

4. Hino Indonesia Manufacturer Club (HIMC) kepada BA (Yudi Iswanto) atas, ‘Juara 2 Best 3 Kategori SS Liga 2 dalam Event Konvensi SS di Hino Innovation Convention (HICo) ke-7 2024’
Award and Medal from Hino Indonesia Manufacturer Club (HIMC) to BA (Yudi Iswanto) for, ‘2nd Winner of Best 3 SS Category League 2 in SS Convention event 7th Hino Innovation Convention (HICo) 2024’.
