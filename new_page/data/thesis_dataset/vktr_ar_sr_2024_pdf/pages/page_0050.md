VKTR, as a pioneer in the Battery Electric Vehicle (BEV) segment, stands at the forefront of this transformation, aligning its business model with the government's vision of a greener and low-emission economy.

The consistent growth of the transportation and warehousing sector in recent years has contributed to increasing demand for vehicle spare parts, both for passenger and commercial vehicles. As a producer of key components for various types of vehicles, VKTR is in a strategic position to capture this opportunity. On the other hand, the Government's commitment to developing the BEV ecosystem as part of the downstreaming of basic minerals further strengthens the demand for electric vehicles in various forms, from electric buses and light commercial vehicles to electric motorcycles.

The implementation of various strategic policies and incentives has successfully driven national EV market growth. According to Gaikindo data, total sales of electrified vehicles (xEV) in 2024 reached 99,701 units, marking a significant increase from previous years. This includes 42,889 units of Battery Electric Vehicles (BEV), 56,812 Hybrid Electric Vehicles (HEV), and a small number of Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEV). This growth reflects increasing consumer interest in low-emission vehicles and the market's readiness for energy transition in the transportation sector.

Policy support is also reflected in the Indonesian Government's ambitious targets through the National Energy Council, which aims to convert conventional vehicles to battery-based electric vehicles, up to 13 million motorcycles and 2 million cars by 2030. In addition, of the approximately 280,000 fossil fuel-powered buses in Indonesia, the majority have the potential to be converted (retrofitted) into electric vehicles or replaced with new electric buses in line with the growing demand for efficient and environmentally friendly public transportation.
