In major emerging markets like China and India, economic growth remained positive. China's economic recovery was supported in part by interest rate cuts and a ¥10 trillion economic stimulus package introduced by the Chinese government in September 2024. These measures helped sustain domestic economic growth in line with the 5% target.

Meanwhile, India's economy grew due to increased public infrastructure investment and higher household investments in real estate. On the supply side, growth was supported by a strong manufacturing sector and vibrant service activities.

The rising risks of global uncertainty were accompanied by extreme climate changes, leading to significant natural disasters. This added fiscal pressure on the affected countries. In 2024, global temperatures reached a new record high.

According to an analysis by the Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S), a European Union agency monitoring global warming, the global average temperature in 2024 surpassed 1.5 degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels. This exceeded the temperature rise limit set under the Paris Agreement, which aims to mitigate the worst effects of climate change.

The increase in temperature triggered disasters such as storms in the United States, wildfires in Canada, and droughts affecting the Amazon River. Beyond the Americas, heat waves also struck Asia, from India to Japan, where daily maximum temperatures remained 5 degrees Celsius above the climatological average for several consecutive days.
