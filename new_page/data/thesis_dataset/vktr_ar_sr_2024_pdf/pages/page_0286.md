# NOMINASI DAN REMUNERASI DEWAN KOMISARIS DAN DIREKSI

## NOMINASI ANGGOTA DEWAN KOMISARIS DAN DIREKSI

Proses pengangkatan dan pemberhentian anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dilakukan melalui mekanisme RUPS. Masa jabatan anggota Dewan Komisaris dan anggota Direksi ditetapkan selama 5 (lima) tahun, terhitung sejak tanggal penetapan oleh RUPS. Setelah masa jabatannya berakhir, anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dapat diangkat kembali oleh RUPS.

Selain itu, kriteria dan persyaratan bagi calon anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi disusun berdasarkan Undang-Undang Perseroan Terbatas, peraturan di bidang pasar modal, serta regulasi lain yang berkaitan dengan kegiatan usaha Perseroan.

## PROSEDUR PENETAPAN REMUNERASI DEWAN KOMISARIS DAN DIREKSI [GRI 2-19, 2-20]

Berdasarkan Undang-undang No. 40 Tahun 2007, penetapan remunerasi dan/atau tunjangan yang diberikan kepada anggota Dewan Komisaris dan anggota Direksi harus ditetapkan dalam keputusan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham. Kewenangan dalam menetapkan besaran remunerasi sepenuhnya dilimpahkan kepada Dewan Komisaris, yang memutuskan melalui rapat Dewan Komisaris, dengan mempertimbangkan rekomendasi serta saran dari Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi.

## STRUKTUR REMUNERASI DEWAN KOMISARIS DAN DIREKSI [GRI 2-21]

Struktur remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi untuk 2023/2024 telah mengacu pada hasil keputusan RUPS Tahunan tanggal 3 Juni 2024.

# NOMINATION AND REMUNERATION OF BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS AND BOARD OF DIRECTORS

## BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS AND DIRECTORS' MEMBERS NOMINATION

The appointment and dismissal of members of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors are carried out through the GMS mechanism. The tenure of Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors members is set for a period of five (5) years, effective from the date of appointment by the GMS. Upon the expiration of their term, members of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors may be reappointed by the GMS.

In addition, the criteria and requirements for candidates for the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors are established based on the Limited Liability Company Law, capital market regulations, and other relevant regulations governing the Company's business activities.

## REMUNERATION PROCEDURE FOR THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS AND BOARD OF DIRECTORS [GRI 2-19, 2-20]

Pertaining to Law No. 40 of 2007, the determination of remuneration and/or allowances for members of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors must be decided through a resolution of the General Meeting of Shareholders. The authority to determine the amount of remuneration is fully delegated to the Board of Commissioners, which makes decisions through Board of Commissioners' meetings, taking into account recommendations and advice from the Nomination and Remuneration Committee.

## BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS AND BOARD OF DIRECTORS REMUNERATION STRUCTURES [GRI 2-21]

The remuneration structure of the Board of Commissioners and Directors for the period of 2023/2024 has been referred to the Annual GMS resolution dated June 3, 2024.
