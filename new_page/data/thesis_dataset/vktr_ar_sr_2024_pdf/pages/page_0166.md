TINJAUAN PEREKONOMIAN ECONOMIC OVERVIEW

Menurut data Badan Pusat Statistik (BPS), dalam periode Januari–Desember 2024, jumlah penumpang penerbangan domestik mencapai 63,7 juta orang, meningkat 1,76% dibandingkan 2023, sementara penumpang penerbangan internasional mencapai 19,0 juta orang, mengalami lonjakan 21,46% dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya.

Sementara itu, jumlah penumpang angkutan laut secara kumulatif mencapai 25,9 juta orang, naik 20,46% dibandingkan 2023. Jumlah penumpang kereta api juga mengalami pertumbuhan signifikan, mencapai 504,6 juta orang, meningkat 17,81% dibandingkan tahun 2023.

Di sisi lain, sektor Restoran &amp; Hotel mengalami pertumbuhan yang kuat, didorong oleh meningkatnya aktivitas wisata, terutama selama libur sekolah dan hari raya keagamaan nasional. Peningkatan jumlah wisatawan domestik dan mancanegara berkontribusi terhadap tingginya tingkat okupansi hotel dan peningkatan konsumsi di sektor kuliner.

Investasi juga tumbuh positif yang tercermin pada beberapa indikator kelompok barang modal, terutama dari peningkatan investasi penanaman modal asing (PMA) dan penanaman modal dalam negeri (PMDN). Berdasarkan laporan Kementerian Investasi/Badan Koordinasi Penanaman Modal (BKPM), realisasi investasi selama tahun 2024 mencapai Rp1.712,4 triliun, naik sebesar 20,8% dibandingkan tahun 2023. Realisasi PMA sebesar Rp900,2 triliun, tumbuh sebesar 21,0% secara tahunan dan portofolio sebesar 52,5% dari total realisasi investasi.

Sedangkan realisasi PMDN sebesar Rp814,0 triliun, tumbuh sebesar 20,6% dari tahun 2023 dan portofolionya mencapai 47,5% dari total realisasi investasi. Aktivitas investasi tersebut menyerap tenaga kerja sebanyak 2.456.130 orang, meningkat 34,7% dari tahun sebelumnya.

Ditinjau dari sektor usaha, investasi tersebar di lima sektor utama, yaitu industri logam dasar, barang logam; bukan mesin dan peralatannya; transportasi; gudang

According to Statistics Indonesia (BPS), during the January–December 2024 period, domestic air passenger numbers reached 63.7 million, marking a 1.76% increase compared to 2023, while international air passengers surged to 19.0 million, a 21.46% jump from the previous year.

In addition, sea transportation recorded 25.9 million passengers, an increase of 20.46% compared to 2023. Rail transport also saw a significant increase, with 504.6 million passengers, reflecting a 17.81% increase from 2023.

Meanwhile, the Restaurants &amp; Hotels sector experienced strong growth, fueled by higher tourism activity, particularly during school holidays and major national religious celebrations. The increase in both domestic and international tourists contributed to higher hotel occupancy rates and greater spending in the food and beverage industry.

Investment also grew positively, as reflected in several indicators of capital goods, particularly the increase in foreign direct investment (FDI) and domestic investment (PMDN). According to a report from the Ministry of Investment/Indonesia Investment Coordinating Board (BKPM), total investment realization in 2024 reached Rp1,712.4 trillion, marking a 20.8% increase compared to 2023. FDI realization amounted to Rp900.2 trillion, grew 21.0% year-on-year, accounting for 52.5% of total investment realization.

Meanwhile, domestic investment (PMDN) realization reached Rp814.0 trillion, grew 20.6% from 2023, with a portfolio share of 47.5% of total investment realization. These investment activities created 2,456,130 jobs, an increase of 34.7% compared to the previous year.
