# HUBUNGAN DENGAN PEMANGKU KEPENTINGAN
[POJK E.4][GRI 2-29]

Perseroan secara proaktif melakukan identifikasi terhadap seluruh pemangku kepentingan yang terlibat dalam wilayah operasionalnya, dengan tujuan utama untuk memahami harapan mereka serta membangun hubungan yang konstruktif dan berkelanjutan. Dalam rangka ini, pemangku kepentingan dikelompokkan ke dalam dua kategori utama, yaitu Pemangku Kepentingan Internal dan Pemangku Kepentingan Eksternal. Proses klasifikasi ini tidak hanya mempertimbangkan siapa saja yang terlibat, tetapi juga menganalisis sejauh mana dampak yang ditimbulkan oleh masing-masing kelompok terhadap Perseroan, serta tingkat kepentingan mereka terhadap keberlanjutan dan kesuksesan Perseroan. Hal ini membantu Perseroan dalam menyusun pendekatan yang tepat dalam menjalin hubungan dengan setiap pemangku kepentingan berdasarkan kebutuhan, harapan, dan tingkat pengaruh mereka.

To manage risks effectively, the Company follows four key steps: risk identification, risk level assessment, development of mitigation plans to address identified risks, and periodic monitoring and evaluation to assess the effectiveness of the mitigation strategies implemented. This approach ensures that any emerging risks are addressed appropriately and in a measurable manner.

To support this risk management process, the Company has implemented the use of a Risk Register as a key tool in managing risks. Through this tool, each division within the Company is required to actively identify potential risks that may affect their operations. Once risks are identified, each division evaluates the potential economic impact on the Company and categorizes the risk levels into three categories: high, medium, and low. As a result, the Company's risk management is not only systematic but also adaptive, ensuring that every action taken helps maintain operational continuity and stability across all areas of the business.

# STAKEHOLDER ENGAGEMENT
[POJK E.4][GRI 2-29]

The Company proactively identifies all stakeholders involved within its operational scope, with the primary goal of understanding their expectations and building constructive and sustainable relationships. In this effort, stakeholders are grouped into two main categories: Internal Stakeholders and External Stakeholders. This classification process not only considers who is involved but also analyzes the level of impact each group has on the Company, as well as their degree of importance in relation to the Company's sustainability and success. This approach helps the Company formulate appropriate strategies to engage with each stakeholder based on their needs, expectations, and level of influence.
