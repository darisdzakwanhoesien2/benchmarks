# ASSETS

The Company recorded assets amounting to Rp1,608.60 billion in 2024, a decrease of 3.58% year-on-year compared to Rp1,668.36 billion in 2023. The decrease in assets was caused by a decline in current assets by 29.36% to Rp729.98 billion. Non-current assets amounted to Rp878.63 billion in 2024, an increase of 38.37% compared to Rp635.00 billion in 2023.

Current assets decreased by 29.36%, from Rp1,033.36 billion in 2023 to Rp729.98 billion in 2024, mainly due to a reduction in cash and cash equivalents by 89.79% to Rp75.12 billion. The decrease in cash and cash equivalents was due to the use for short-term investments amounting to Rp100.33 billion.

In addition, net inventory, which includes auxiliary materials and spare parts, finished goods, work in progress, and raw materials, also decreased by 1.77% from Rp142.44 billion in 2023 to Rp139.92 billion in 2024.
