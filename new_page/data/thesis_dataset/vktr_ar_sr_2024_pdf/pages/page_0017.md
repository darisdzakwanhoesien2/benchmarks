# VKTR menyelenggarakan Rapat pemegang Saham tahunan (RUPST)

VKTR Holds Annual General Meeting of Shareholders (AGMS)

Pada Senin, 3 Juni 2024, VKTR menggelar Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Tahunan (RUPST) yang dihadiri oleh jajaran direksi, komisaris, dan pemegang saham. Acara ini menjadi momen penting untuk merefleksikan kinerja positif sepanjang tahun 2023 dan menyusun strategi masa depan. Laporan tahunan 2023 menyoroti komitmen VKTR dalam menghadirkan solusi mobilitas ramah lingkungan melalui inovasi produk dan kemitraan strategis. Dalam rapat ini, disetujui pula penunjukan kantor akuntan publik untuk mengaudit laporan keuangan konsolidasian perseroan. VKTR menegaskan visinya untuk menjadi pemimpin industri kendaraan listrik di Indonesia, mendukung transisi energi terbarukan, dan mempercepat tercapainya Net Zero Emissions di Tanah Air.

On Monday, June 3, 2024, VKTR held its Annual General Meeting of Shareholders (AGMS), attended by the board of directors, commissioners, and shareholders. The event served as an important moment to reflect on the company's positive performance throughout 2023 and to formulate future strategies. The 2023 annual report highlighted VKTR's commitment to providing sustainable mobility solutions through product innovation and strategic partnerships. During the meeting, the appointment of a public accounting firm to audit the company's consolidated financial statements was also approved. VKTR reaffirmed its vision to become a leader in Indonesia's electric vehicle industry, supporting the renewable energy transition, and accelerating the achievement of Net Zero Emissions in the country.

# VKTR menjadi Speaker di Acara The International Battery Summit 2024

VKTR as a Speaker at The International Battery Summit 2024

VKTR di International Battery Summit 2024. Achmad Audrinazta, Head of Strategic Planning &amp; Business Development VKTR, tampil sebagai pembicara di International Battery Summit 2024, membahas perjalanan VKTR dalam mempelopori elektrifikasi berkelanjutan di Indonesia. Sebagai bagian dari Road to Indonesia International Sustainability Forum 2024, acara ini menghadirkan lebih dari 50 pembicara dari 20 negara yang berbagi wawasan tentang tren terbaru teknologi baterai. VKTR terus mendorong inovasi dan keberlanjutan dalam industri EV, berkomitmen untuk mendukung elektrifikasi yang lebih ramah lingkungan di Indonesia.

VKTR at the International Battery Summit 2024. Achmad Audrinazta, Head of Strategic Planning &amp; Business Development at VKTR, participated as a speaker at the International Battery Summit 2024, sharing insights on VKTR's journey in pioneering sustainable electrification in Indonesia. As part of the Road to Indonesia International Sustainability Forum 2024, the event featured more than 50 speakers from 20 countries, discussing the latest trends in battery technology. VKTR remains committed to driving innovation and sustainability in the EV industry, supporting a greener and more sustainable electrification transition in Indonesia.

# VKTR berpartisipasi di Acara Net Zero Summit 2024

VKTR Participates in the Indonesia Net-Zero Summit 2024

VKTR Berpartisipasi dalam Indonesia Net-Zero Summit 2024. Anindya N. Bakrie, Presiden Direktur Bakrie &amp; Brothers dan Komisaris Utama VKTR, akan hadir dalam Indonesia Net-Zero Summit 2024 untuk memberikan pandangannya mengenai peran sektor swasta dalam mendukung pencapaian target Net-Zero Emission Indonesia. Acara ini menjadi kesempatan penting untuk mendiskusikan langkah-langkah konkret yang dapat diambil oleh sektor swasta dalam mempercepat transisi menuju ekonomi rendah karbon, serta kontribusi VKTR dalam mendorong mobilitas berkelanjutan di Indonesia.

VKTR Participates in the Indonesia Net-Zero Summit 2024. Anindya N. Bakrie, President Director of Bakrie &amp; Brothers and President Commissioner of VKTR, will attend the Indonesia Net-Zero Summit 2024 to share his insights on the role of the private sector in supporting Indonesia's Net Zero Emissions target. This event serves as a crucial platform to discuss concrete actions that the private sector can take to accelerate the transition toward a low-carbon economy, as well as VKTR's contributions in advancing sustainable mobility in Indonesia.
