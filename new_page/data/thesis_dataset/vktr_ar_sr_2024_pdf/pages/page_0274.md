DEWAN KOMISARIS
BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS

Berdasarkan Keputusan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Luar Biasa (RUPSLB) pada 6 Desember 2024, Rapat menyetujui pengunduran diri Bapak Yukki Nugrahawan Hanafi dari jabatannya sebagai Komisaris Independen Perseroan. Dengan demikian, per 31 Desember 2024, komposisi Dewan Komisaris Perseroan terdiri dari tiga anggota, yaitu 1 (satu) Presiden Komisaris, 1 (satu) Komisaris, dan 1 (satu) Komisaris Independen. Dua dari Komisaris berdomisili di Indonesia. Dengan satu Komisaris Independen dari total tiga anggota, maka proporsi Komisaris Independen dalam Dewan Komisaris adalah 33%.

Based on the resolution of the Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders (EGMS) on December 6, 2024, the meeting approved the resignation of Mr. Yukki Nugrahawan Hanafi from his position as Independent Commissioner of the Company. Hence, as of December 31, 2024, the composition of the Company's Board of Commissioners consists of 3 (three) members: 1 (one) President Commissioner, 1 (one) Commissioner, and 1 (one) Independent Commissioner. Two of the Commissioners are domiciled in Indonesia. With one Independent Commissioner out of a total of three members, Independent Commissioners represent 33% of the Board of Commissioners.

## MASA JABATAN DEWAN KOMISARIS

Sesuai Anggaran Dasar Perseroan, satu periode masa jabatan anggota Dewan Komisaris paling lama 5 (lima) tahun atau sampai dengan penutupan RUPS pada akhir 1 (satu) periode masa jabatan dimaksud, dengan tidak mengurangi hak RUPS untuk memberhentikan anggota Dewan Komisaris tersebut sewaktu-waktu sebelum masa jabatannya berakhir.

## BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS TERM OF OFFICE

Pursuant to the Company's Articles of Association, tenure for members of the Board of Commissioners is a maximum of 5 (five) years or until the closure of the General Meeting of Shareholders at the end of the specified term of office, without prejudice to the rights of the GMS to dismiss the member of the Board of Commissioners at any time prior to end of term.
