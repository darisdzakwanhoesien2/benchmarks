# PERUBAHAN KOMPOSISI DEWAN KOMISARIS
[OJK C.6]
CHANGES IN THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS COMPOSITION
[OJK C.6]

Berdasarkan Keputusan RUPS Luar Biasa tanggal 6 Desember 2024, Rapat mengesahkan pengunduran diri Bapak Yukki Nugrahawan Hanafi, dari jabatannya sebagai Komisaris Independen Perseroan. Sehingga setelah dilakukannya perubahan susunan Dewan Komisaris Perseroan, maka susunan Dewan Komisaris Perseroan per 31 Desember 2024, menjadi sebagai berikut:

- Komisaris Utama: Anindya Novyan Bakrie
- Komisaris: The Lord Aamer Ahmad Sarfraz
- Komisaris Independen: Dr. Dino Patti Djalal

Based on the resolution of the Extraordinary GMS on December 6, 2024, the meeting approved the resignation of Mr. Yukki Nugrahawan Hanafi from his position as Independent Commissioner of the Company. Following the changes in the composition of the Board of Commissioners, the structure of the Company's Board of Commissioners as of December 31, 2024, is as follows:

- President Commissioner: Anindya Novyan Bakrie
- Commissioner: The Lord Aamer Ahmad Sarfraz
- Independent Commissioner: Dr. Dino Patti Djalal
