# PENGEMBANGAN KOMPETENSI KARYAWAN
[POJK F.22] [GRI 3-3]

SDM yang kompeten dan unggul adalah aset utama bagi kesuksesan Perseroan. Untuk itu, Perseroan berkomitmen untuk terus membina dan mengembangkan SDM guna memenuhi standar kompetensi yang mendukung kelancaran operasional Perseroan. Pengembangan ini sejalan dengan kebijakan Perseroan yang menekankan kesetaraan bagi seluruh karyawan, mencakup proses rekrutmen, pelatihan, pengembangan, serta penilaian kinerja yang adil dan transparan.

Berikut adalah cara perusahaan mengidentifikasi dan mengembangkan SDM bertalenta serta program khusus yang dapat memfasilitasi jalur karir yang jelas bagi karyawan:

1. Mengidentifikasi SDM Bertalenta
a. Penilaian Kinerja dan Potensi
b. 360-Degree Feedback
c. Penilaian Potensi Kepemimpinan
d. Program Talent Pool

2. Mengembangkan SDM Bertalenta
a. Program Pengembangan Kepemimpinan
b. Program Pelatihan dan Sertifikasi
c. Mentoring dan Coaching
d. Rotasi Pekerjaan

3. Jalur Karir yang Jelas bagi Karyawan Berbakat
a. Rencana Pengembangan Karir (Career Pathing)
b. Jalur Karir yang Fleksibel
c. Sistem Penilaian dan Penghargaan Berkelanjutan

Pada tahun 2024, Perseroan telah melaksanakan 185 pelatihan, diikuti oleh 154 karyawan, dengan rata-rata 11 jam per karyawan.

# JENIS DAN CAKUPAN PROGRAM PENGEMBANGAN KOMPETENSI DAN KEAHLIAN [GRI 404-2]

Perseroan berfokus pada pengembangan kompetensi karyawan melalui berbagai program pelatihan dan sertifikasi yang bekerja sama dengan lembaga pendidikan. Program ini bertujuan untuk meningkatkan keterampilan teknis, interpersonal, dan kepemimpinan karyawan, yang mendukung kinerja perusahaan secara keseluruhan.

# EMPLOYEE COMPETENCY DEVELOPMENT
[POJK F.22] [GRI 3-3]

Competent and high-performing HR are key assets to a company's success. To that end, the Company is committed to continuously nurturing and developing its workforce to meet the competency standards required to support seamless business operations. This development aligns with the Company's policy that emphasizes equality for all employees, covering recruitment, training, development, and fair and transparent performance evaluation processes.

The following are the Company's approaches to identifying and developing talented employees, as well as special programs that facilitate clear career paths:

1. Identifying Talented Employees
a. Performance and Potential Assessment
b. 360-Degree Feedback
c. Leadership Potential Evaluation
d. Talent Pool Program

2. Developing Talented Employees
a. Leadership Development Programs
b. Training and Certification Programs
c. Mentoring and Coaching
d. Job Rotation

3. Clear Career Paths for Talented Employees
a. Career Pathing Plans
b. Flexible Career Tracks
c. Continuous Performance Assessment and Reward Systems

In 2024, the Company conducted 185 training programs, attended by 154 employees, with an average of 11 training hours per employee.

# TYPES AND SCOPE OF COMPETENCY AND SKILL DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMS [GRI 404-2]

The Company focuses on developing employee competencies through various training and certification programs in collaboration with educational institutions. These programs aim to enhance employees' technical, interpersonal, and leadership skills, thereby supporting the Company's overall performance.
