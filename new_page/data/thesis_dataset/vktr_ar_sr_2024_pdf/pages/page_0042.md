# PANDANGAN ATAS PENERAPAN TATA KELOLA

Perseroan terus berupaya meningkatkan kualitas dan efektivitas implementasi tata kelola, baik dari sisi struktur, proses dan hasil tata kelola. Pencapaian kinerja operasional dan finansial yang bertumbuh serta pelaksanaan inisiatif keberlanjutan yang konsisten mencerminkan keberhasilan penerapan tata kelola di VKTR selama tahun 2024.

Sebagai bagian dari pelaksanaan tata kelola, Perseroan telah melaksanakan kebijakan anti-korupsi dan strategi anti-fraud. Perseroan secara konsisten menerapkan program kepatuhan dan pengendalian internal guna memastikan tidak adanya praktik korupsi dan fraud dalam setiap lini bisnis. Sepanjang tahun 2024, berbagai langkah pencegahan, deteksi, dan penanganan fraud telah dilakukan, termasuk penguatan sistem pengendalian internal, dan peningkatan kesadaran karyawan melalui pelatihan.

Dalam hal efektivitas, Dewan Komisaris melihat bahwa strategi yang diterapkan telah memberikan dampak positif dalam memitigasi risiko fraud. Deteksi dini melalui audit internal serta adanya jalur pelaporan yang transparan telah meningkatkan respons cepat dalam menangani potensi penyimpangan.

Terkait dengan implementasi Whistleblowing System (WBS) di Perseroan, Dewan Komisaris menilai pelaksanaannya telah berfungsi dengan baik sebagai sarana dalam menampung laporan dan keluhan terkait pelanggaran peraturan perusahaan, hukum, maupun etika. Dewan komisaris menilai sistem WBS dirancang dengan baik ditandai dengan kemudahan akses, jaminan kerahasiaan, independensi, transparansi, dan perlindungan terhadap pelapor maupun saksi. Sepanjang tahun 2024, tidak terdapat laporan yang diterima melalui sistem Whistleblowing.

# VIEW ON GOVERNANCE IMPLEMENTATION

The Company strives to improve the quality and effectiveness of governance implementation in terms of structure, processes, and outcomes. The achievement of growing operational and financial performance, along with the consistent implementation of sustainability initiatives, reflects the successful application of governance at VKTR throughout 2024.

As part of its governance practices, the Company has implemented anti-corruption policies and anti-fraud strategies. VKTR consistently enforces compliance programs and internal controls to ensure that no corruption or fraud occurs in any business line. Throughout 2024, various measures for the prevention, detection, and handling of fraud were undertaken, including strengthening internal control systems and raising employee awareness through training programs.

In terms of effectiveness, the Board of Commissioners observes that the strategies implemented have had a positive impact on mitigating the risk of fraud. Early detection through internal audits and the presence of transparent reporting channels have enhanced the speed of response in addressing potential irregularities.

Regarding the implementation of the Whistleblowing System (WBS) in the Company, the Board of Commissioners assesses that it has been functioning well as a platform for receiving reports and complaints related to violations of company regulations, laws, or ethics. The Board of Commissioners views the WBS as well-designed, as indicated by its ease of access, confidentiality assurance, independence, transparency, and protection for both whistleblowers and witnesses. Throughout 2024, no reports were received through the Whistleblowing System.
