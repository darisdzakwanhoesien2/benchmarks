# Penghargaan

Sepanjang tahun 2024, VKTR berhasil meraih berbagai penghargaan bergengsi yang mencerminkan pengakuan atas kontribusi dan kiprahnya dalam pengembangan industri kendaraan listrik di Indonesia. Beberapa penghargaan yang diterima antara lain:

- The Fastest Growing New Public Company 2024 dalam kategori Automotive Component Industry for The Capacious Market Capitalization for Sustainability Business (Sector: Consumer Cyclicals) dari Warta Ekonomi Research and Consulting,
- 2nd Implementation &amp; Development EV Ecosystem Innovation Award 2024 dalam ajang 2nd Annual EV Industry Summit Indonesia 2024, dan
- Terbaik 1 Mitra dengan Padanan Terbanyak Tahun 2021–2024 dalam ajang Vokasi Berinovasi 2024.

Selain penghargaan untuk entitas induk, anak perusahaan VKTR juga turut menerima sejumlah penghargaan atas pencapaian dalam inovasi produk, performa bisnis, dan kualitas operasional. Secara keseluruhan, VKTR dan entitas anaknya telah membukukan lebih dari 20 penghargaan dari berbagai institusi kredibel. Prestasi ini menjadi simbol nyata atas komitmen dan dedikasi Perseroan dalam mempercepat elektrifikasi transportasi dan membangun ekosistem kendaraan listrik yang berkelanjutan di Indonesia.

# PERBANDINGAN ANTARA HASIL DAN TARGET

Pada tahun 2024, Perseroan menghadapi tantangan signifikan yang berdampak terhadap pencapaian target yang telah ditetapkan. Ketidakpastian global yang dipicu oleh fragmentasi ekonomi, konflik geopolitik, dan fluktuasi perdagangan internasional turut menjadi faktor utama stagnasi ekonomi secara global. Di tengah kondisi tersebut, total penjualan neto Perseroan tercatat sebesar Rp1.003,66 miliar atau 40,16% dari target Rp2.498,86 miliar. Tekanan terhadap daya beli dan pasar kendaraan komersial turut memengaruhi kinerja penjualan sepanjang tahun.

Dari sisi profitabilitas, laba neto Perseroan hanya mencapai Rp12,29 miliar atau 9,45% dari target sebesar Rp130,00 miliar. Meskipun terdapat pertumbuhan dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya, hasil ini belum mencerminkan potensi yang diharapkan seiring dengan strategi ekspansi yang tengah dijalankan. Kinerja keuangan juga turut dipengaruhi oleh peningkatan biaya investasi awal serta penyesuaian operasional dalam proses peningkatan kapasitas produksi kendaraan listrik berbasis CKD.

# Awards

Throughout 2024, VKTR earned numerous prestigious awards, reflecting broad recognition of its contributions and leadership in the development of Indonesia's electric vehicle industry. The awards received include:

- The Fastest Growing New Public Company 2024 in the Automotive Component Industry for The Capacious Market Capitalization for Sustainability Business (Sector: Consumer Cyclicals) from Warta Ekonomi Research and Consulting,
- 2nd Implementation &amp; Development EV Ecosystem Innovation Award 2024 at the 2nd Annual EV Industry Summit Indonesia 2024, and
- First Place in Partner with the Padanan Fund from 2021–2024 at the Vokasi Berinovasi 2024 event.

In addition to recognition for the parent company, VKTR's subsidiaries also received various awards for achievements in product innovation, business performance, and operational quality. In total, VKTR and its subsidiaries have secured over 20 awards across multiple categories from reputable institutions. These accomplishments stand as a testament to the Company's ongoing commitment and dedication to advancing electric mobility and developing a sustainable EV ecosystem in Indonesia.

# COMPARISON OF ACTUAL RESULTS AND TARGETS

In 2024, the Company faced significant challenges that impacted the achievement of its established targets. Global uncertainty triggered by economic fragmentation, geopolitical conflicts, and fluctuations in international trade emerged as key factors contributing to global economic stagnation. Amid these conditions, the Company's total net sales amounted to Rp1,003.66 billion, or 40.16% of the Rp2,498.86 billion target. Pressure on purchasing power and the commercial vehicle market also affected sales performance throughout the year.

In terms of profitability, the Company recorded a net profit of only Rp12.29 billion, or 9.45% of the Rp130.00 billion target. Although this reflects growth compared to the previous year, the result has yet to meet the anticipated potential aligned with the ongoing expansion strategy. Financial performance was also affected by an increase in initial investment costs as well as operational adjustments during the scale-up of CKD-based electric vehicle production capacity.
