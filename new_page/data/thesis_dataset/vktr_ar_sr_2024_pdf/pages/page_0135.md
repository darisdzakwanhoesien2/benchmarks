# Indah Permatasari Saugi

Chief of Corporate Affairs
Chief of Corporate Affairs

Indah Permatasari Saugi, Warga Negara Indonesia, berusia 41 tahun, adalah profesional dinamis dengan pengalaman lebih dari dua dekade dalam bidang komunikasi korporasi, kepatuhan, kesehretariatan Perusahaan, dan branding strategis, baik di Perusahaan publik maupun multinasional. Beliau meraih gelar Sarjana Hukum dari Universitas Pelita Harapan dan mengantongi berbagai sertifikasi profesi di bidang Corporate Secretary, Compliance &amp; Risk Management, PERADI sertifikasi.

Saat ini, Indah menjabat sebagai Chief Corporate Affairs dan Corporate Secretary di PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk sejak tahun 2023. Dalam perannya, beliau bertanggung jawab atas integrasi fungsi legal, governance, komunikasi strategis, serta sustainability, guna memperkuat transparansi, akuntabilitas, dan reputasi Perseroan di mata pemangku kepentingan nasional maupun global.

Sebelum bergabung dengan VKTR, Indah membangun rekam jejak kuat di berbagai Perusahaan ternama seperti PT Aviasi Pariwisata Indonesia (Injourney), PT AirAsia Indonesia Tbk, dan PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur (Persero). Di sana, beliau mengelola proyek strategis lintas sektor termasuk IPO, sustainability initiatives, dan transformasi GCG.

Dedikasi Indah dalam mendukung agenda keberlanjutan tercermin dari kiprahnya dalam pengembangan program-program berbasis ESG dan keterlibatan aktif dalam forum korporasi nasional seperti Asosiasi Emiten Indonesia (AEI) dan Indonesia Corporate Secretary Association (ICSA).

Dengan pendekatan kepemimpinan yang kolaboratif, ketajaman hukum, serta komitmen terhadap inovasi berkelanjutan, Indah membawa kontribusi penting dalam memperkuat posisi VKTR sebagai pemimpin dalam sektor kendaraan listrik dan mobilitas rendah karbon.

Indah Permatasari Saugi, Indonesian citizen, 41 years old, is a dynamic professional with over two decades of experience in corporate communication, compliance, corporate secretarial, and strategic branding, working across public and multinational companies. She holds a Bachelor of Law degree from Universitas Pelita Harapan and various professional certifications, including Corporate Secretary, Compliance &amp; Risk Management, PERADI Certification.

Currently, Indah serves as Chief Corporate Affairs and Corporate Secretary at PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk since 2023. In this role, she is responsible for overseeing and integrating the legal, corporate governance, strategic communication, and sustainability functions, aimed at strengthening transparency, accountability, and corporate reputation among national and international stakeholders.

Prior to joining VKTR, Indah built a strong track record at reputable companies such as PT Aviasi Pariwisata Indonesia (Injourney), PT AirAsia Indonesia Tbk, and PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur (Persero). She managed cross-sector strategic projects, including IPOs, sustainability initiatives, and corporate governance transformations.

Her commitment to advancing sustainability is demonstrated through the development of ESG-based programs and her active participation in national corporate forums such as the Indonesian Issuers Association (AEI) and the Indonesia Corporate Secretary Association (ICSA).

Through her collaborative leadership style, legal acumen, and commitment to continuous innovation, Indah serves a vital role in strengthening VKTR's position as a leader in the electric vehicle and low-carbon mobility sector.
