Untuk memastikan kelancaran serta keamanan dalam implementasi TI, Perseroan juga telah memperkuat system perlindungan siber dengan mengadopsi piranti lunak antivirus Eset Endpoint Security. Sistem keamanan ini tidak hanya berfungsi untuk mencegah serangan virus dan malware, tetapi juga menjadi benteng utama dalam menangkal potensi pencurian data serta ancaman siber lainnya yang dapat mengganggu kelangsungan bisnis Perseroan. Dengan pendekatan keamanan berlapis, Perseroan dapat memastikan bahwa system informasi tetap terlindungi, data pelanggan aman, dan seluruh transaksi digital berjalan dengan tingkat keamanan yang tinggi.

Perseroan telah mengadopsi Office 365 license dengan tujuan utama untuk memastikan bahwa seluruh data perusahaan tersimpan dengan aman di cloud server Microsoft. Langkah ini memungkinkan fleksibilitas dalam penyimpanan, keamanan data yang lebih baik, serta akses yang lebih mudah bagi karyawan dari berbagai lokasi. Selain itu, untuk meningkatkan keamanan jaringan internal, Perseroan telah mengimplementasikan SSID WiFi khusus bagi tamu. Langkah ini bertujuan untuk menghindari potensi file corruption akibat virus serta mencegah tamu atau pihak luar mengakses data internal perusahaan tanpa izin.

Program ini dirancang untuk memperkuat pemahaman karyawan mengenai system TI terbaru serta meningkatkan keahlian mereka dalam mengelola dan mengembangkan teknologi yang digunakan di Perseroan.

To ensure the seamless and secure implementation of IT systems, the Company has also strengthened its cybersecurity framework by adopting Eset Endpoint Security antivirus software. This security system not only helps prevent virus and malware attacks but also serves as the first line of defense against data breaches and other cyber threats that could disrupt business continuity. By implementing a multi-layered security approach, the Company ensures robust protection of its information systems, safeguards customer data, and maintains high security standards for all digital transactions.

The Company has adopted an Office 365 license to ensure that all corporate data is securely stored in Microsoft's cloud server. This initiative provides greater flexibility in storage, enhanced data security, and easier access for employees across different locations. In addition, to reinforce internal network security, the Company has implemented a dedicated SSID WiFi for guests. This measure aims to prevent potential file corruption caused by viruses and restrict unauthorized access to internal corporate data by external visitors.

Alongside IT infrastructure and system upgrades, the Company is committed to enhancing IT workforce competency through various training programs and knowledge-sharing sessions. These initiatives are designed to strengthen employees' understanding of the latest IT systems and enhance their ability to manage and develop the technology employed within the Company.

Throughout 2024, the Company has conducted various IT workforce development programs, including knowledge-sharing sessions on IT infrastructure.
