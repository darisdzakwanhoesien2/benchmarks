Industri Pengolahan menjadi motor utama pertumbuhan dengan kontribusi 0,9%, didorong oleh permintaan yang stabil dari pasar domestik dan internasional. Beberapa sub-sektor yang mencatat pertumbuhan signifikan antara lain industri makanan dan minuman yang meningkat sebesar 5,90%, industri logam dasar yang mengalami lonjakan 13,34%, serta industri barang logam, komputer, barang elektronik, optik, dan peralatan listrik yang tumbuh sebesar 6,16%. Kinerja positif sektor ini mencerminkan peningkatan kapasitas produksi dan inovasi di sektor manufaktur.

Sektor Perdagangan Besar dan Eceran; Reparasi Mobil dan Sepeda Motor mencatat pertumbuhan sebesar 5,59%, yang didorong oleh peningkatan produksi dalam sektor Pertambangan dan Industri Pengolahan, serta peningkatan volume impor. Hal ini mencerminkan pemulihan aktivitas ekonomi yang semakin kuat pasca pandemi, terutama dalam sektor perdagangan ritel dan otomotif.

Pada 2024, terdapat tiga sektor usaha dengan pertumbuhan tertinggi, yaitu Jasa Lainnya, Transportasi &amp; Pergudangan, serta Akomodasi &amp; Makan Minum. Berbagai event olahraga, konser musik, dan festival budaya juga turut berkontribusi terhadap pertumbuhan sektor ini, mendorong konsumsi dan penciptaan lapangan kerja di sektor jasa.

Sektor Transportasi &amp; Pergudangan juga mengalami ekspansi yang signifikan, didorong oleh peningkatan jumlah penumpang dan volume barang yang diangkut. Perbaikan infrastruktur transportasi dan peningkatan konektivitas antar wilayah menjadi faktor utama dalam mendorong pertumbuhan sektor ini.

The Manufacturing Industry emerged as the primary growth driver, contributing 0.9%, supported by stable domestic and international demand. Several subsectors described notable expansion, including the food and beverage industry, which grew by 5.90%, the basic metal industry, which surged by 13.34%, and the metal goods, computers, electronics, optics, and electrical equipment industry, which recorded a growth of 6.16%. This positive performance reflects increased production capacity and innovation in the manufacturing sector.

The Wholesale and Retail Trade; Repair of Motor Vehicles and Motorcycles sector recorded a 5.59% growth, supported by increased production in the Mining and Manufacturing sectors, as well as higher import volumes. This growth indicates a strong post-pandemic economic recovery, particularly in the retail trade and automotive sectors.

In 2024, the three fastest-growing sectors were Other Services, Transportation &amp; Warehousing, and Accommodation &amp; Food Services. The Other Services sector experienced rapid growth, driven by increased recreational and entertainment activities, in line with a surge in domestic and international tourist arrivals. Various sports events, music concerts, and cultural festivals contributed to this sector's growth, boosting consumer spending and job creation in the service industry.

The Transportation &amp; Warehousing sector also expanded significantly, fueled by higher passenger traffic and freight volume. Improved transportation infrastructure and enhanced regional connectivity served a major role in driving this sector's expansion.
