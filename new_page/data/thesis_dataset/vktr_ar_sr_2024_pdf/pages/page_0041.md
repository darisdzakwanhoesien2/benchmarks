# PELAKSANAAN TUGAS DAN TANGGUNG JAWAB DEWAN KOMISARIS

Dewan Komisaris telah melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawab utama fungsi pengawasan terhadap manajemen operasional yang dilakukan oleh Direksi selama tahun 2024. Dewan Komisaris juga telah memberikan saran, nasihat dan arahan yang bersifat strategis kepada Direksi sebagai bagian dari implementasi tata kelola terbaik di Perseroan.

Dalam melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawab Dewan Komisaris berpedoman kepada Piagam Dewan Komisaris Perseroan. Fungsi pengawasan Dewan Komisaris dilaksanakan melalui rapat internal sebanyak 10 (sepuluh) kali di tahun 2024 yang membahas agenda antara lain perkembangan inisiatif strategis dan keberlanjutan Perseroan; laporan audit; implementasi tata kelola, pengelolaan sumber daya manusia dan teknologi; dan makro ekonomi serta industri kendaraan listrik di tingkat global dan nasional.

Pelaksanaan fungsi pengawasan Dewan Komisaris selain melalui rapat internal juga dilakukan melalui rapat gabungan dengan Direksi secara berkala yang membahas isu-isu aktual dan tantangan yang dihadapi oleh Perseroan dalam menerapkan kebijakan strategis dan perkembangan pencapaian kinerja Perseroan.

Fungsi pengawasan Dewan Komisaris dilakukan sejak perumusan strategi Perseroan dengan memberikan saran dan persetujuan terhadap rencana kebijakan strategis yang diajukan oleh Direksi. Untuk meningkatkan kualitas fungsi pengawasan, Dewan Komisaris terus meningkatkan kompetensi melalui keikutsertaan dalam pelatihan, seminar, dan webinar agar selalu mendapatkan informasi terbaru terutama terkait dengan bidang usaha Perseroan.

Dalam melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawab Dewan Komisaris dibantu oleh Komite Audit dan Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi, serta komite lainnya yang telah berperan aktif dalam memastikan implementasi tata kelola dan mendukung pencapaian strategi bisnis Perseroan.

# IMPLEMENTATION OF DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS

The Board of Commissioners has carried out its main duties and responsibilities in overseeing the operational management conducted by the Board of Directors throughout 2024. The Board of Commissioners has also provided strategic advice, counsel, and direction to the Board of Directors as part of the implementation of best governance practices within the Company.

In performing its duties and responsibilities, the Board of Commissioners is guided by the Company's Board of Commissioners Charter. The oversight function was carried out through 10 (ten) internal meetings in 2024, discussing agendas such as the progress of the Company's strategic and sustainability initiatives; audit reports; implementation of governance; management of human resources and technology; and macroeconomic conditions as well as the electric vehicle industry at both global and national levels.

In addition to internal meetings, the Board of Commissioners also carries out its supervisory function through regular joint meetings with the Board of Directors, which discuss current issues and challenges faced by the Company in implementing strategic policies and achieving performance milestones.

The supervisory role of the Board of Commissioners begins from the formulation stage of the Company's strategies by providing advice and approval for strategic policy plans proposed by the Board of Directors. To enhance the quality of its supervisory function, the Board of Commissioners continuously improves its competencies by participating in trainings, seminars, and webinars to stay informed, especially in areas related to the Company's line of business.

In carrying out its duties and responsibilities, the Board of Commissioners is supported by the Audit Committee and the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, as well as other committees that have actively contributed to ensuring good governance implementation and supporting the Company's business strategies achievement.
