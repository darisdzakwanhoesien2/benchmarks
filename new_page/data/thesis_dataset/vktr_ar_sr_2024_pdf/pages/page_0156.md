The US economic recovery is accompanied by reduced inflationary pressures, leading to a decrease in the benchmark interest rate (Federal Funds Rate or FFR) by 50 basis points, from 5.5% to 5.0% in September 2024. Subsequently, the FFR was further lowered by 25 basis points in November and another 25 basis points in December 2024, reaching 4.5% by the end of the year.

The decline in the Federal Funds Rate (FFR) has led to a decrease in short-term U.S. Treasury yields, while medium- to long-term U.S. Treasury yields have tended to rise due to increasing fiscal deficits and the high debt burden of the U.S. government.

However, the outcome of the U.S. presidential election at the end of 2024, with Donald Trump being re-elected and implementing his "America First" policy, could bring significant changes to the global geopolitical and economic landscape.

President Trump is expected to impose high tariffs on countries with large trade surpluses with the U.S., including the European Union, the United Kingdom, China, Mexico, Canada, Japan, South Korea, and Vietnam.

Moreover, stricter immigration policies will be enforced against illegal foreign workers, and foreign policy decisions regarding NATO, the Russia-Ukraine conflict, and the Middle East could reshape the global geopolitical map.

Trump's victory also negatively impacted global financial markets in the fourth quarter of 2024 and is expected to further slow global economic growth, with increasing risks of trade and investment fragmentation.
