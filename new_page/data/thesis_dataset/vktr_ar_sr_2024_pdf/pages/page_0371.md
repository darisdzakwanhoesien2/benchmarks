# RANTAI PASOK DAN PEMASOK LOKAL [GRI 2-6, 3-3, 204-1]

Perseroan mengutamakan kerja sama erat dengan pemasok barang dan jasa, yang mendukung kelancaran operasional dan pertumbuhan mitra usaha, baik lokal maupun internasional. Proses seleksi pemasok dilakukan dengan ketat, mengacu pada standar teknis, administratif, dan etika yang telah ditetapkan.

Pemasok dipilih melalui prosedur yang transparan, termasuk verifikasi legalitas dokumen, tender terbuka, dan evaluasi kinerja. Semua langkah ini sesuai dengan Standar Operasional Prosedur (SOP) yang berlaku di Perseroan, menjamin proses pengadaan yang efisien dan akuntabel.

Proses ini mencakup verifikasi legalitas dokumen, benchmarking bersama pihak pengguna terkait teknis dan material produksi, serta input data legalitas ke dalam sistem Approval Vendor List (AVL) yang terverifikasi oleh tim IC dan Akuntansi. Selanjutnya, dilakukan proses tender terbuka atau tabulasi penawaran, audit, penilaian, evaluasi kinerja, dan pengembangan barang/jasa. Ketentuan tersebut telah tertuang dalam sejumlah kebijakan dan prosedur internal, antara lain SOP PROC-RP-04/02/2023, BMC/PP-PR-05 tentang Pengadaan Barang &amp; Jasa, PP-PR-05/IK-008 terkait Persyaratan Barang Vendor, BM/PR-04/PROC-RC/03 tentang Approval Supplier List (ASL), BM/PR-04/PROC-RC/04 mengenai Supplier Assessment Record, BM/PR-04/PROC-RC/18 tentang Supplier Performance Report, serta PP-PR-05/IK-002 mengenai Prosedur Seleksi, Evaluasi &amp; Pengembangan Supplier.

Pada tahun 2024, Perseroan mencatat 832 pemasok lokal (99,52% dari total pemasok).

This compliance not only reflects the Company's integrity but also supports the government's efforts in fostering progress and prosperity for all Indonesian people.

# SUPPLY CHAIN AND LOCAL SUPPLIERS [GRI 2-6, 3-3, 204-1]

The Company prioritizes close collaboration with suppliers of goods and services to ensure seamless operations and support the growth of both local and international business partners. The supplier selection process is carried out rigorously, based on established technical, administrative, and ethical standards.

Suppliers are selected through a transparent procedure, including document legality verification, open tenders, and performance evaluations. All of these steps are in accordance with the Company's applicable Standard Operating Procedures (SOP), ensuring an efficient and accountable procurement process.

This process includes the verification of document legality, benchmarking with end users regarding technical and production material specifications, and the input of legal data into the Vendor Approval List (AVL) system, which is verified by the IC and Accounting teams. It is followed by an open tender or bid tabulation process, audits, assessments, performance evaluations, and the development of goods/services. These requirements are outlined in various internal policies and procedures, including SOP PROC-RP-04/02/2023, BMC/PP-PR-05 on Procurement of Goods &amp; Services, PP-PR-05/IK-008 on Vendor Goods Requirements, BM/PR-04/PROC-RC/03 on Approval Supplier List (ASL), BM/PR-04/PROC-RC/04 on Supplier Assessment Record, BM/PR-04/PROC-RC/18 on Supplier Performance Report, and PP-PR-05/IK-002 on Supplier Selection, Evaluation &amp; Development Procedures.

In 2024, the Company recorded 832 local suppliers (99.52% of the total number of suppliers).
