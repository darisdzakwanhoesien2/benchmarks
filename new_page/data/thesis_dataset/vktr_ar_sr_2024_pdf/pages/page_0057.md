# Kinerja Keuangan

Pada aspek profitabilitas operasional, Perseroan mencatatkan nilai penjualan total sebesar Rp1.003,66 miliar di tahun 2024 menurun sedikit sebesar 5,48% dibandingkan tahun 2023 sebesar Rp1.061,86 miliar. Penjualan ini terdiri dari Penjualan komponen suku cadang sebesar Rp862,22 miliar di tahun 2024, turun sebesar 10,21% dibandingkan tahun 2023 Rp960,29 miliar. Penjualan KBLBB sebesar Rp144,24 miliar di tahun 2024, naik sebesar 35,87% dibandingkan tahun 2023 sebesar Rp106,16 miliar.

Perseroan mencatatkan penurunan komponen beban pokok penjualan sebesar 2,71% dari tahun growth vision, VKTR continued producing and delivering electric buses to various partners. In 2024, a total of 29 electric buses and chassis were delivered. By the end of 2024, VKTR has sold 84 electric buses, 14 electric trucks, and 7 electric forklifts, which are distributed across various regions in Indonesia.

At Manufacture Segment, in 2024 VKTR achieved a major milestone in the manufacturing segment with the construction of Indonesia's first commercial electric vehicle production facility based on the Completely Knocked Down (CKD) system, developed by its subsidiary PT VKTR Sakti Industries (VKTS) in Magelang. This facility is scheduled to begin operations in early 2025. VKTR through its subsidiaries also operates a spare parts plant focused on casting and machining processes to support EV component production. By the end of 2024, a total of 64 electric buses were already in operation without major issues, underscoring both product reliability and the readiness of the Company's CKD assembly infrastructure to serve the national EV industry.

To accelerate technology development and enhance local content, VKTR established strategic collaborations with global automotive manufacturers and expanded local partnerships with leading brands such as Hino, Mitsubishi Fuso, Suzuki, Daihatsu, Toyota, Yanmar, and Komatsu. VKTR's spare parts have met high-quality standards and fulfilled the 40% a Local Content Requirement (TKDN), including certification for its 12-meter bus. Despite the slowdown in the national economy, the component manufacturing segment for commercial and passenger vehicles continued to show a positive trend, driven by operational flexibility and adaptive strategies.

# Financial Performance

In terms of operational profitability, the Company recorded total sales of Rp1,003.66 billion in 2024, representing a slight decline of 5.48% compared to Rp1,061.86 billion in 2023. These sales comprised component sales amounting to Rp862.22 billion in 2024, a decrease of 10.21% from Rp960.29 billion in 2023. Meanwhile, sales of BEVs reached Rp144.24 billion in 2024, marking an increase of 35.87% from Rp106.16 billion in 2023.
