## SURVEI KEPUASAN KARYAWAN

Untuk mengevaluasi tingkat keterikatan dan kepuasan karyawan, Perseroan telah melaksanakan survei keterikatan karyawan (employee engagement survey) pada tahun 2024.

Survei ini mencakup beberapa area utama, seperti:
1. Keseimbangan kehidupan kerja dan jam kerja
2. Hubungan dengan manajer dan rekan kerja
3. Peluang pengembangan karir
4. Kondisi lingkungan kerja, termasuk fasilitas dan fasilitas kesehatan

Hasil survei ini memberikan wawasan berharga bagi Perseroan untuk meningkatkan kebijakan dan program yang lebih efektif dalam mendukung kesejahteraan dan kepuasan karyawan.

1. High-speed internet access
2. Employee shuttle service (home-office-home)
3. Comfortable workspaces equipped with computers and office supplies
4. Meeting rooms with adequate supporting facilities
5. Worship rooms (musholla, prayer rooms, etc.)
6. Nursing rooms
7. Open or green spaces
8. Pantry/kitchen area
9. Sports area
10. Childcare facilities, especially in plantation areas

However, despite various preventive efforts, the Company recorded several occupational accident incidents throughout 2024. The majority of incidents occurred in the plant area and involved minor injuries. A total of 6 (six) incidents were recorded in 2024.

## EMPLOYEE SATISFACTION SURVEY

To evaluate employee engagement and satisfaction levels, the Company conducted an employee engagement survey in 2024.

This survey covered several key areas, including:
1. Work-life balance and working hours
2. Relationships with managers and coworkers
3. Career development opportunities
4. Work environment conditions, including facilities and health services

The results of this survey provided valuable insights for the Company to improve policies and programs that more effectively support employee well-being and satisfaction.
