Amid these global headwinds, Indonesia remained resilient, recording a year-on-year economic growth of 5.03%, a slight decrease from 5.05% in 2023. This stability was supported by a seamless national leadership transition and strong performance in consumption, investment, and government spending. Oil and gas exports, along with non-oil exports, also contributed positively. Total investment reached Rp1,712.4 trillion, grew 20.8% year-on-year, with foreign direct investment accounting for more than half of that figure.

The manufacturing, transportation, and wholesale trade sectors posted positive growth. The basic metals industry grew by 13.34%, driven by increased export demand for iron and steel. Meanwhile, the transportation and warehousing sector recorded growth of 8.69%, supported by rising mobility and the growing trend of digital trade. Annual inflation was successfully contained at 1.57%, while the benchmark BI Rate showed a downward trend, reflecting strong coordination between fiscal and monetary policies in maintaining macroeconomic stability.

While the national automotive industry recorded a decline, with total car sales falling by 14% to 865,723 units, growth momentum was instead recorded in the electric vehicle segment. Sales of Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) rose significantly by 152% to 42,889 units in 2024, reflecting a growing public awareness and interest in low-emission vehicles. VKTR views this dynamic as a strategic opportunity to strengthen its position within the national electric vehicle ecosystem.
