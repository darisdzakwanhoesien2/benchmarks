KOMITE AUDIT
AUDIT COMMITTEE

Komite Audit dibentuk untuk meningkatkan efektivitas fungsi pengawasan Dewan Komisaris terhadap berbagai aspek penting dalam operasional Perseroan. Komite ini bertanggung jawab dalam mengawasi informasi keuangan, sistem pengendalian internal, efektivitas pemeriksaan oleh Auditor Eksternal dan Auditor Internal, implementasi manajemen risiko, serta kepatuhan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku.

## DASAR HUKUM

Komite Audit dibentuk Berdasarkan Surat Keputusan Dewan Komisaris Perseroan Tentang Pengangkatan Komite Audit No. 01/SK-DEKOM/VKTR/VIII/2022 tanggal 6 September 2022. Pembentukan Komite Audit telah sesuai dengan Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 55/POJK.04/2015 tanggal 23 Desember 2015 tentang Pembentukan dan Pedoman Pelaksanaan Kerja Komite Audit.

## KOMPOSISI &amp; PROFIL KOMITE AUDIT

Komite Audit Perseroan terdiri dari 3 (tiga) orang anggota, dimana 1 (satu) orang Komisaris Independen sebagai Ketua dan 2 (dua) orang anggota pihak independen, serta memiliki pemahaman yang memadai atas bisnis yang dijalani Perseroan. Keanggotaan Komite Audit diangkat dan diberhentikan oleh Dewan Komisaris.

Komposisi Komite Audit per 31 Desember 2024 adalah sebagai berikut:

The Audit Committee is established to enhance the effectiveness of the Board of Commissioners' oversight function in key operational areas of the Company. The committee is responsible for monitoring financial information, internal control systems, the effectiveness of audits conducted by External and Internal Auditors, risk management implementation, and compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

## LEGAL REFERENCES

The Audit Committee was established based on the Board of Commissioners' Decree on the Appointment of the Audit Committee No. 01/SK-DEKOM/VKTR/VIII/2022 dated September 6, 2022. The establishment of the Audit Committee is in line with Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 55/POJK.04/2015 dated December 23, 2015 concerning the Establishment and Guidelines for Implementing the Work of the Audit Committee.

## COMPOSITION &amp; PROFILE OF AUDIT COMMITTEE

The Company's Audit Committee consists of 3 (three) members, of which 1 (one) Independent Commissioner is the Chairman and 2 (two) independent party members, as well as have an adequate understanding of the Company's business. Members of Audit Committee are appointed and dismissed by Board of Commissioners.
