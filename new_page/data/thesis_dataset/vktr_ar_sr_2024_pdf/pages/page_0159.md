Inflasi dunia menurun dari 6,2% pada 2023 menjadi 5,2% pada 2024, namun diprakirakan masih tetap tinggi pada 2025 dan 2026, masing-masing 4,5% dan 4,2%, dengan kemungkinan terjadinya perang dagang antara AS dengan sejumlah mitra dagang utamanya, serta terganggunya mata rantai pasok perdagangan dunia. (Sumber: Bank Indonesia, dalam Laporan Perekonomian Indonesia, 2024).

Global inflation decreased from 6.2% in 2023 to 5.2% in 2024, but it is projected to remain high in 2025 and 2026, at 4.5% and 4.2%, respectively, with the possibility of a trade war between the U.S. and several of its major trading partners, as well as disruptions in global trade supply chains. (Source: Bank Indonesia, in the Indonesian Economic Report, 2024).
