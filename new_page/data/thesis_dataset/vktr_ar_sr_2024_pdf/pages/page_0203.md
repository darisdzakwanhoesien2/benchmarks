Aset tidak lancar Perseroan tercatat sebesar Rp878,63 miliar di tahun 2024, meningkat sebesar 38,37% dari tahun 2023 sebesar Rp635,00 miliar. Peningkatan ini terutama disebabkan naknya perolehan aset tetap sebesar 56,71% menjadi Rp624,52 miliar, yang meliputi antara lain bangunan dan prasarana, mesin dan peralatan, dan peralatan pengangkutan untuk mendukung aktivitas operasional Perseroan.

## LIABILITAS

Liabilitas Perseroan sebesar Rp452,68 miliar di tahun 2024, menurun sebesar 13,03% dibandingkan tahun 2023 sebesar Rp520,50 miliar. Penurunan liabilitas disebabkan berkurangnya liabilitas jangka panjang sebesar 48,57% menjadi Rp160,27 miliar.

## Liabilitas Jangka Pendek

### Current Liabilities

(Dalam Rp Juta | In Rp Million)

The Company's non-current assets stood at Rp878.63 billion in 2024, an increase of 38.37% from Rp635.00 billion in 2023. This increase was primarily due to the increase in the acquisition of fixed assets by 56.71% to Rp624.52 billion, which includes among others buildings and infrastructure, machinery and equipment, and transportation equipment to support the Company's operational activities.

## LIABILITIES

The Company's liabilities amounted to Rp452.68 billion in 2024, a decrease of 13.03% compared to Rp520.50 billion in 2023. The decrease in liabilities was caused by a reduction in non-current liabilities by 48.57% to Rp160.27 billion.
