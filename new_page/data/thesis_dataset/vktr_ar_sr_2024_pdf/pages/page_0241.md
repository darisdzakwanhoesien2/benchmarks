Direksi bertanggung jawab atas pengelolaan dan pengambilan keputusan harian, memastikan kegiatan operasional bisnis selaras dengan tujuan strategis Perseroan serta kepatuhan terhadap regulasi yang berlaku. Didukung oleh Sekretaris Perusahaan, Audit Internal, dan unit kerja operasional lainnya.

## Peran dan Tanggung Jawab

Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi memiliki peran serta tanggung jawab yang berbeda sesuai dengan Anggaran Dasar perusahaan dan peraturan yang berlaku. Dewan Komisaris berfokus pada fungsi pengawasan dan arahan strategis, sedangkan Direksi bertugas dalam pelaksanaan strategi operasional dan bisnis.

## Mekanisme Tata Kelola

Untuk menerapkan prinsip GCG secara efektif, Perseroan mengadopsi mekanisme tata kelola yang terstruktur dan didukung oleh infrastruktur tata kelola yang memadai. Mekanisme ini mencakup pendekatan soft-structure, yang terdiri dari regulasi dan kebijakan yang mengatur tanggung jawab setiap organ tata kelola serta hubungan kerja antar organ, termasuk keterlibatan mereka dengan pemangku kepentingan internal maupun eksternal.

&gt; Board of Commissioners
The Board of Commissioners supervises and advises the Board of Directors in managing the company, ensures the implementation of GCG, and may establish committees to support its oversight function.

&gt; Board of Directors
The Board of Directors is responsible for daily management and decision-making, ensuring business operations align with the Company's strategic objectives and regulatory compliance. Supported by the Corporate Secretary, Internal Audit, and other operational units.

## Roles and Responsibilities

The Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors each have distinct roles and responsibilities in accordance with the Company's Articles of Association and prevailing regulations. The Board of Commissioners focuses on oversight and strategic direction, while the Board of Directors executes operational and business strategies.

## Governance Mechanism

To implement GCG principles effectively, the Company adopts a structured governance mechanism supported by adequate governance infrastructure. This mechanism includes a soft-structure approach, which consists of regulations and policies that define the responsibilities of governance organs and the working relationships among them, as well as their engagement with both internal and external stakeholders.
