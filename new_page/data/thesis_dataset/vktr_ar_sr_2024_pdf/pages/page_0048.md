# TINJAUAN MAKROEKONOMI

Tahun 2024 kembali diwarnai oleh dinamika global dan nasional yang kompleks, memengaruhi kinerja Direksi VKTR dalam menetapkan arah strategis Perseroan. Dunia masih dibayangi oleh ketidakpastian akibat belum meredanya ketegangan geopolitik di sejumlah kawasan seperti Timur Tengah dan perang Rusia-Ukraina, yang memicu gangguan rantai pasok serta mendorong fragmentasi ekonomi dan perdagangan global. Transisi kepemimpinan di berbagai negara juga turut menambah ketidakpastian, termasuk dalam hal komitmen bersama menghadapi perubahan iklim. Various initiatives, such as the development of the e-Mobility as a Service (e-MaaS) scheme, the use of telematics technology, excellent after-sales service, and the strengthening of fleet management systems, reflect the Company's commitment to building a smart and environmentally friendly transportation ecosystem. By the end of 2024, VKTR has sold 84 electric buses, 14 electric trucks, and 7 electric forklifts across various regions in Indonesia. All VKTR electric vehicles in operation have cumulatively traveled over 8.4 million kilometers, contributing to an emissions reduction of approximately 8,000 tons of CO₂—equivalent to the positive impact of planting more than 360,000 trees.

This commitment to innovation and sustainability is also aligned with the Company's resilience in facing external challenges. Amid global geopolitical tensions, rising inflation, and supply chain fragmentation, VKTR has maintained solid performance through product diversification, market expansion, and collaboration with strategic partners. This resilience is further supported by an adaptive governance structure and agile operational systems, enabling the Company to respond to market changes accurately and proactively. With this foundation, VKTR is well-positioned to continue leading the transformation of sustainable mobility in Indonesia.

# MACROECONOMIC OVERVIEW

The year 2024 was marked by complex global and domestic dynamics that shaped the VKTR Board of Directors' performance and strategic direction. The world continued to encounter heightened uncertainty stemming from persistent geopolitical tensions in regions such as the Middle East and the ongoing Russia-Ukraine war, leading to supply chain disruptions and growing economic and trade fragmentation. Leadership transitions in several countries further intensified uncertainty, particularly around global climate commitments. The United States' withdrawal from the Paris Agreement dealt a serious blow to collective efforts to limit global warming to 1.5 degrees Celsius.
