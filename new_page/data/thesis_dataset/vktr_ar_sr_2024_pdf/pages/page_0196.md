# SEGMEN PERDAGANGAN
## TRADING SEGMENT

### Strategi Bisnis

Penjualan kendaraan listrik Perseroan saat ini masih didominasi oleh segmen Business-to-Government (B2G), terutama melalui penjualan bus listrik kepada mitra strategis operator bus Transjakarta. Selain itu, Perseroan juga berupaya memperluas portofolio pelanggan ke segmen Business-to-Business (B2B). Salah satu pencapaiannya adalah penjualan dengan total 8 unit bus listrik dimana 1 unit telah sukses dikirimkan pada tahun 2023 dan sisanya pada tahun 2024 kepada PT Riau Andalan Pulp and Paper (RAPP) pada tahun 2024. Di samping itu, VKTR memperkuat kemitraan strategis dengan Indomobil melalui PT IMG Sejahtera Langgeng, guna memperluas jaringan kendaraan listrik komersial di Indonesia.

Perseroan juga menjalankan perdagangan komponen suku cadang, termasuk brake drum, disc brake, serta berbagai komponen lainnya yang digunakan dalam kendaraan komersial, baik kendaraan berbahan bakar fosil maupun Kendaraan Bermotor Listrik Berbasis Baterai (KBLBB).

Lebih lanjut, melalui entitas anaknya, PT Bakrie Autoparts (BA) bersama anak usahanya Perseroan tengah memperluas pangsa pasar ke segmen kendaraan penumpang, dengan mengamankan proyek pengembangan model kendaraan baru.

BA bersama anak usahanya memprioritaskan produksi untuk produk Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM). Ke depannya, Perseroan berencana meningkatkan produksi untuk Original Equipment Supplier (OES) atau aftermarket dan sourcing parts, sebagai bagian dari strategi diversifikasi. Langkah ini bertujuan untuk mengurangi ketergantungan terhadap fluktuasi penjualan Agen Tunggal Pemegang Merek (ATPM) dan menciptakan peluang bisnis yang lebih stabil serta berkelanjutan.

### Business Strategy

The Company's electric vehicle sales are currently dominated by the Business-to-Government (B2G) segment, primarily through the sale of electric buses to its strategic partner, Transjakarta bus operators. In addition to B2G, the Company is actively expanding its customer portfolio in the Business-to-Business (B2B) segment. A significant milestone was achieved in 2024 with the sale of a total 8 electric buses of which 1 unit has been successfully delivered in 2023 and the rest in 2024 to PT Riau Andalan Pulp and Paper (RAPP). Furthermore, VKTR has strengthened its strategic partnership with Indomobil through PT IMG Sejahtera Langgeng, further expanding the commercial EV market in Indonesia.

The Company is also engaged in the trading of spare parts components, including brake drums, disc brakes, and various other components used in commercial vehicles, both for fossil fuel-powered vehicles and Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs).

Furthermore, through its subsidiary PT Bakrie Autoparts (BA) and its sub-subsidiaries, the Company is expanding its market share into the passenger vehicle segment by securing a new vehicle model development project.

BA and its subsidiaries prioritize production for Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) products. Moving forward, the Company plans to increase production for Original Equipment Supplier (OES) or aftermarket and sourcing parts products as part of its diversification strategy. This initiative aims to reduce reliance on fluctuations in sales from sole brand-holding agents (ATPM) while creating a more stable and sustainable business opportunity.
