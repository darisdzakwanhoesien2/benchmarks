VKTR Receives "The Fastest Growing New Public Company 2024" Award in Automotive Component Industry for The Capacious market Capitalization for Sustainability Business "Consumer Cyclicals" Sector"

Penghargaan "The Fastest Growing New Public Company 2024" VKTR. VKTR dengan bangga menerima penghargaan "The Fastest Growing New Public Company 2024 in Automotive Component Industry for The Capacious Market Capitalization for Sustainability Business (Sector: Consumer Cyclicals)" dari Warta Ekonomi Research and Consulting. Penghargaan ini mengukuhkan komitmen VKTR dalam menghadirkan inovasi berkelanjutan di industri otomotif, khususnya dalam sektor kendaraan listrik. Kami mengucapkan terima kasih kepada semua pihak yang telah memberikan dukungan dalam perjalanan kami, serta berharap dapat terus berkontribusi untuk masa depan yang lebih hijau dan berkelanjutan.

VKTR is proud to receive the "The Fastest Growing New Public Company 2024 in Automotive Component Industry for The Capacious Market Capitalization for Sustainability Business (Sector: Consumer Cyclicals)" award from Warta Ekonomi Research and Consulting. This recognition reaffirms VKTR's commitment to driving sustainable innovation in the automotive industry, particularly in the electric vehicle sector. We extend our gratitude to all stakeholders who have supported our journey and look forward to continuing our contributions toward a greener and more sustainable future.

First Delivery Launching Event for Electric Buses – Collaboration of Transjakarta, Sinarjaya, and Laksana Held at Laksana Garage, Ungaran

Acara First Delivery: Kolaborasi VKTR, Laksana, Sinarjaya, dan Transjakarta. Pada 3 Desember 2024, VKTR, PT Laksana Bus Manufaktur (Laksana), dan Sinarjaya merayakan pencapaian penting melalui acara First Delivery, yang menandai pengiriman perdana kendaraan listrik untuk Transjakarta sebagai konsumen utama. Acara ini merupakan hasil dari kemitraan strategis yang mendukung komitmen bersama menuju transportasi ramah lingkungan dan berkelanjutan di Indonesia. Ini adalah langkah besar dalam mewujudkan masa depan transportasi yang lebih hijau, yang sejalan dengan upaya pemerintah dalam mencapai target Net Zero Emissions 2060.

First Delivery Event: Collaboration of VKTR, Laksana, Sinarjaya, and Transjakarta. On December 3, 2024, VKTR, Laksana, and Sinarjaya celebrated a significant milestone through the First Delivery event, marking the initial delivery of electric vehicles for Transjakarta as the primary customer. This event is the result of a strategic partnership that supports a shared commitment to environmentally friendly and sustainable transportation in Indonesia. It represents a major step toward a greener future for transportation, aligning with the government's efforts to achieve the Net Zero Emissions 2060 target.
