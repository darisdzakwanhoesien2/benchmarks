Selain untuk impor mobil listrik, fasilitas ini juga berlaku untuk penyerahan mobil listrik yang diproduksi dari mobil Completely Knocked-Down (CKD). Fasilitas PPnBM untuk penyerahan mobil CKD diberikan dengan beberapa ketentuan, seperti:

As per the previous regulations, two requirements must be met by CBU electric vehicle importers to qualify for this facility. In addition to submitting an import declaration document, they must also prepare a report on the realization of the Luxury Goods Sales Tax (PPnBM) covered by the government.

This facility is not only applicable to imported electric vehicles but also to electric vehicles produced from Completely Knocked-Down (CKD) units. The PPnBM incentive for CKD electric vehicles is granted under the following conditions:

1. The taxable entrepreneur (PKP) selling the vehicle must issue a tax invoice in accordance with regulations.
2. The PKP seller must also prepare a report on the realization of PPnBM covered by the government.

The tax invoice issued for the delivery of CKD electric vehicles must include transaction code 01 and details such as the brand, type, variant, and chassis number. Additionally, it must contain the note: "PPnBM covered by the government in accordance with PMK No. 135 of 2024." Meanwhile, the report on the realization of PPnBM covered by the government for CKD electric vehicles may be in the form of a tax invoice reported in the VAT periodic tax return (SPT) by the PKP.

If the requirements are not met, taxpayers will not be able to take advantage of the PPnBM incentive. Taxable entrepreneurs (PKP) delivering CKD electric vehicles cannot benefit from the facility if they fail to issue a tax invoice in accordance with regulations and do not prepare a realization report. As a result, the import or delivery of these electric vehicles will still be subject to PPnBM according to applicable regulations.
