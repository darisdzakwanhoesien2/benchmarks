# Kode Etik Berlaku Di Seluruh Level Organisasi

Kode Etik Perseroan berlaku untuk semua lapisan karyawan termasuk Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris Perseroan. Semua karyawan memiliki kewajiban untuk melaksanakan Kode Etik dan mematuhi kebijakan dan prosedur Perseroan, peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku, termasuk peraturan di mana Perseroan dan anak perusahaan yang menjalankan bisnis.

Perseroan berpegang teguh pada standar etika yang tinggi dan mengharapkan mitra bisnis, pemasok, kontraktor, agen, dan pihak lain yang bekerja dengan atau atas nama Perseroan untuk mematuhi standar yang sama.

# Sanksi Pelanggaran Kode Etik

Setiap pelanggaran atau ketidakpatuhan terhadap kode etik Perseroan yang dilakukan oleh Dewan Komisaris, Direksi dan seluruh karyawan dapat dikenakan sanksi berupa teguran lisan, peringatan tertulis, ganti kerugian dan Pemutusan Hubungan Kerja (PHK) sesuai dengan peraturan dan ketentuan perundang-undangan yang berlaku.

# Code of Conduct Applicable in Organizational Levels

The Company's Code of Conduct applies to all employees at every level, including the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners. All employees are required to uphold the Code of Conduct and comply with the Company's policies and procedures, prevailing laws and regulations, as well as the regulations governing the jurisdictions where the Company and its subsidiaries operate.

The Company adheres to high ethical standards and expects its business partners, suppliers, contractors, agents, and other parties working with or on behalf of the Company to uphold the same standards.

# Sanctions for Code of Conduct Violations

Any violation or non-compliance with the Company's Code of Conduct by the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, or employees may result in sanctions, including verbal warnings, written warnings, compensation for damages, or termination of employment (PHK), in accordance with prevailing laws and regulations.
