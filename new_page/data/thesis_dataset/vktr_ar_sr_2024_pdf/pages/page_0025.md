# Piagam Penghargaan

# Certificate of Appreciaton

1. Pada Maret 2024, PT Mitsubishi Motors Krama Yudha Indonesia (MMKI) memberikan penghargaan kepada BUMM sebagai Good Delivery Supplier PT MMKI.

In March 2024, PT Mitsubishi Motors Krama Yudha Indonesia (MMKI) presented an award to BUMM as a Good Delivery Supplier of PT MMKI.

2. Very Valuable and Good Contribution

Pada 7 Maret 2024, PT Yanmar Diesel Indonesia (YADIN) memberikan penghargaan kepada BUMM atas kinerja dan dukungan yang luar biasa kepada PT YADIN.

Very Valuable and Good Contribution

On March 7, 2024, PT Yanmar Diesel Indonesia (YADIN) presented an award to BUMM for its outstanding performance and support to PT YADIN.

3. Kontribusi Terbaik

Pada 31 Maret 2024, PT Panasonic Manufacturing Indonesia (PMI) memberikan penghargaan kepada BUMM atas dukungan dan Kerjasama yang telah diberikan selama tahun fiskal 2023 kepada PT PMI.

Best Contribution

On March 31, 2024, PT Panasonic Manufacturing Indonesia (PMI) presented an award to BUMM for the support and cooperation that has been given during the 2023 fiscal year to PT PMI.

4. Piagam Penghargaan Partisipasi dan Peduli terhadap lingkungan

Pada 1 Agustus 2024, Kelurahan Alam Jaya Kecamatan Jatiuwung Kota Tangerang memberikan penghargaan kepada BUMM atas partisipasi dan peduli terhadap kegiatan kelurahan Alam Jaya di bidang lingkungan dan sosial masyarakat.

Participation and Care for the Environment Award Charter

On August 1, 2024, Alam Jaya Village, Jatiuwung District, Tangerang City presented an award to BUMM for its participation and concern for the activities of Alam Jaya Village in the environmental and social fields of the community.

5. Piagam untuk Kontribusi dan Pencapaian

Pada 18 September 2024, PT Komatsu Indonesia (KI) memberikan sertifikat penghargaan kepada BUMM atas kontribusi dan pencapaian pada Quality Control Supplier Convention  $6^{\text{th}}$  Komatsu Group Indonesia.

Certificate for Contribution and Achievement

On September 18, 2024, PT Komatsu Indonesia (KI) presented a certificate of appreciation to BUMM for its contribution and achievements to the  $6^{\text{th}}$  Komatsu Group Indonesia Quality Control Supplier convention.
