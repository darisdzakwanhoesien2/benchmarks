# Jenis Program Pelatihan yang Dilakukan:

1. Pelatihan Teknis: Meningkatkan keterampilan di bidang spesifik sesuai peran, seperti teknologi, manufaktur, keuangan, dan pemasaran.
2. Pelatihan Kepemimpinan dan Manajerial: Fokus pada keterampilan pengambilan keputusan, manajemen tim, dan pemecahan masalah.
3. Pelatihan Soft Skills: Mengembangkan keterampilan seperti komunikasi, negosiasi, dan manajemen waktu.
4. Sertifikasi Profesional: Memberikan sertifikasi yang relevan, seperti manajemen proyek atau analisis data.
5. Pelatihan Teknologi dan Inovasi: Fokus pada teknologi baru seperti cloud computing, big data, dan cybersecurity.

Perseroan menyadari bahwa mengukur efektivitas pelatihan sangat penting untuk mengetahui apakah investasi dalam pelatihan memberikan dampak yang positif terhadap kinerja karyawan dan perusahaan. Efektivitas pelatihan biasanya diukur melalui beberapa cara berikut:

1. Penilaian pra-dan pasca pelatihan
2. Umpan balik peserta pelatihan
3. Observasi jangka panjang
4. Analisis biaya-manfaat

Pelatihan yang efektif dapat membawa berbagai dampak positif terhadap kinerja karyawan dan perusahaan, antara lain:

a. Peningkatan Keterampilan dan Kompetensi sehingga meningkatkan produktivitas dan efisiensi dalam bekerja
b. Peningkatan Motivasi dan Kepuasan Kerja
c. Penurunan Kesalahan dan Peningkatan Kualitas Kerja
d. Pengembangan Karir dan Jalur Kepemimpinan
e. Retensi Karyawan

Saat ini Perseroan belum memiliki pelatihan persiapan pensiun, namun Perseroan akan bekerja sama dengan Bakrie Untuk Negeri (BUN) untuk mengadakan pelatihan tersebut di tahun-tahun mendatang. Walaupun demikian, berikut adalah strategi dan rencana pengembangan SDM untuk tahun 2025:

1. Peningkatan Kompetensi Teknologi: Fokus pada keterampilan digital, kendaraan listrik, dan kecerdasan buatan melalui pelatihan internal dan eksternal.

# Types of Training Programs Conducted:

1. Technical Training: Enhancing skills in specific areas according to roles, such as technology, manufacturing, finance, and marketing.
2. Leadership and Managerial Training: Focusing on decision-making, team management, and problem-solving skills.
3. Soft Skills Training: Developing skills such as communication, negotiation, and time management.
4. Professional Certification: Providing relevant certifications such as project management or data analysis.
5. Technology and Innovation Training: Focusing on emerging technologies such as cloud computing, big data, and cybersecurity.

The Company recognizes that measuring training effectiveness is essential to determine whether investments in training generate positive impacts on employee and corporate performance. Training effectiveness is typically measured through the following methods:

1. Pre- and post-training assessments
2. Participant feedback
3. Long-term observation
4. Cost-benefit analysis

Effective training can deliver several positive impacts on employee and Company performance, including:

a. Enhanced Skills and Competencies that improve productivity and work efficiency
b. Increased Motivation and Job Satisfaction
c. Reduced Errors and Improved Work Quality
d. Career Development and Leadership Pathways
e. Employee Retention

Currently, the Company does not yet offer pre-retirement training; however, it plans to collaborate with Bakrie Untuk Negeri (BUN) to organize such training in the coming years. Nevertheless, the following are the Company's HR development strategies and plans for 2025:

1. Enhancing Technological Competence: Focusing on digital skills, electric vehicles, and artificial intelligence through both internal and external training programs.
