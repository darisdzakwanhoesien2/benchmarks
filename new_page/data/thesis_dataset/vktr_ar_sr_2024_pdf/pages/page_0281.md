# Rapat Gabungan Dewan Komisaris dengan Direksi

Sesuai Anggaran Dasar Perseroan, Rapat Gabungan Dewan Komisaris dengan Direksi diselenggarakan secara berkala paling kurang 1 (satu) kali dalam 4 (empat) bulan, dengan agenda pembahasan di antaranya:

&gt; Persetujuan atas rencana kerja tahunan Perusahaan.
&gt; Penelaahan atas laporan tahunan dan laporan keuangan.
&gt; Pelaksanaan GCG termasuk pelaksanaan RUPS dan pelaksanaan kepatuhan (compliance).
&gt; Rencana transaksi dan strategi perusahaan terkait lainnya, termasuk rencana pembiayaan dan transaksi yang dilakukan perusahaan lainnya.

Rapat Gabungan Dewan Komisaris dengan Direksi tahun 2024 telah diagendakan sebelum tahun berjalan yang dituangkan dalam program kerja Dewan Komisaris tahun 2023.

Selama tahun 2024, Rapat Gabungan Dewan Komisaris dengan Direksi telah dilakukan sebanyak 10 (sepuluh) kali yang dihadiri oleh anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi.

# Board of Commissioners Joint Meetings with Directors

In accordance with the Company's Articles of Association, the Joint Meeting of the Board of Commissioners with the Board of Directors is held periodically at least once in 4 (four) months, with a discussion agenda among others:

&gt; Approval of the Company's annual work plan.
&gt; Review of the annual report and financial statements.
&gt; Implementation of GCG including the conduct of the GMS and compliance implementation.
&gt; Plans for transactions and other related company strategies, including financing plans and transactions conducted by other companies.

The 2024 Joint Meeting of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors has been scheduled prior to the current year as outlined in the 2023 work program of the Board of Commissioners.

During 2024, the Joint Meeting of the Board of Commissioners with the Board of Directors has been held 10 (ten) times which was attended by members of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors.
