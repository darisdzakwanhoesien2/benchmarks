# PENANGGUNG JAWAB KEBERLANJUTAN [POJK E.1]
[GRI 2-9, 2-11, 2-12, 2-13, 2-14]

Sebagai bagian dari komitmen terhadap tata kelola keberlanjutan, Perseroan telah mengadopsi ketentuan yang ditetapkan dalam Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (POJK) No. 51/POJK.03/2017 terkait dengan Penerapan Keuangan Berkelanjutan bagi Lembaga Jasa Keuangan, Emiten, dan Perusahaan Publik. Dalam penerapannya, seluruh pegawai, pejabat, dan unit kerja yang memiliki tanggung jawab atas keberlanjutan di Perseroan diwajibkan untuk berperan aktif dalam implementasi, pelaporan, dan pencapaian sasaran yang berkaitan dengan prinsip keberlanjutan. Hal ini bertujuan untuk memastikan bahwa setiap inisiatif keberlanjutan dilaksanakan secara konsisten dan terukur, sejalan dengan standar yang berlaku.

Meskipun Perseroan belum memiliki struktur organisasi yang khusus menangani tata kelola keberlanjutan, langkah konkret telah diambil dengan membentuk Tim Task Force yang terdiri dari perwakilan Sekretaris Perusahaan dan Divisi Business Development. Tim ini bertanggung jawab untuk mengarahkan dan mengimplementasikan kebijakan keberlanjutan yang selaras dengan prinsip-prinsip ESG, termasuk dalam penyusunan laporan keberlanjutan. Melalui pembentukan Tim Task Force ini, Perseroan memastikan adanya koordinasi yang efektif dalam mewujudkan kinerja keberlanjutan yang dapat memberikan dampak positif jangka panjang, sekaligus mendukung upaya Perseroan untuk tetap berdaya saing di tingkat global.

# KEBERAGAMAN DALAM MANAJEMEN [GRI 405-1] [GRI 3-3, 405-1]

Perseroan menghargai keberagaman dalam manajemen sebagai salah satu kekuatan utama fundamental basis for long-term business sustainability. By upholding strong GCG principles, the Company ensures that all aspects of its operations, including compliance with laws and regulations, are carried out with integrity and professionalism. The sustainability and progress of the Company are determined not only by its financial performance but also by how it conducts its business with consideration for the long-term impact on the environment and society.

# SUSTAINABILITY RESPONSIBILITY [POJK E.1] [GRI 2-9, 2-11, 2-12, 2-13, 2-14]

As part of its commitment to sustainability governance, the Company has adopted the provisions stipulated in the Financial Services Authority Regulation (POJK) No. 51/POJK.03/2017 concerning the Implementation of Sustainable Finance for Financial Service Institutions, Issuers, and Public Companies. In its implementation, all employees, officials, and work units responsible for sustainability within the Company are required to actively participate in the implementation, reporting, and achievement of sustainability-related goals. This aims to ensure that every sustainability initiative is carried out consistently and in a measurable manner, in line with applicable standards.

Although the Company has not yet established a dedicated organizational structure to manage sustainability governance, concrete steps have been taken through the formation of a Task Force Team comprising representatives from the Corporate Secretary and Business Development Division. This team is responsible for directing and implementing sustainability policies aligned with ESG principles, included in the preparation of sustainability reports. Through the establishment of this Task Force Team, the Company ensures effective coordination in achieving sustainability performance that delivers long-term positive impact, while also supporting the Company's efforts to remain competitive at the global level.
