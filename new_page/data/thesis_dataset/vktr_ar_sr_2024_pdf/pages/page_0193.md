In the commercial electric vehicle (EV) segment, future growth opportunities remain vast, as the number of commercial EVs in Indonesia currently accounts for less than 1% of the total EV population. New electric buses represent only 0.11%, while electric trucks account for a mere 0.004%, indicating that demand for commercial EVs is still significantly high. For instance, Transjakarta has set a target of deploying 10,000 EV buses by 2030, highlighting the enormous potential of this market.

The Indonesian government's commitment to electrifying public transportation has also contributed to the rising demand for electric buses. The Ministry of Transportation has outlined an ambitious plan to increase urban mass transport electrification to 90% by 2030, achieve full electrification by 2040, and fully transition all public transportation, including city transport, to EVs by 2045. This policy is accelerating Indonesia's shift toward a more sustainable transportation system.
