BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS &amp; DIRECTORS PROFILE

# V. Bimo Kurniatmoko

Direktur
Director

Warga negara Indonesia 45 tahun, beliau menjabat sebagai Direktur VKTR sejak tahun 2024.

Saat ini, Beliau juga menjabat sebagai Direktur Utama di PT Bakrie Autoparts dan PT Braja Mukti Cakra, Deputy Chief of Executive Officer di PT VTKR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk.

Sebelumnya beliau menjabat sebagai Head of Project Management Office PT Bakrie Global Ventura pada tahun 2016 hingga 2020, Director and CEO PT Inovasi Teknologi Nusantara (Bakrie Global subsidiary pada tahun 2015 hingga 2018. VP Technology Operations, PT Bakrie Telecom, Tbk 2013 hingga 2015 dan Senior Account Manager, PT Ericsson Indonesia pada 2007 hingga 2013.

Bimo meraih gelar sarjana Teknik Elektro di Universitas Institut Teknologi Bandung pada tahun 2002 dan Master of Engineering-Telecommunications Engineering di University of Melbourne pada tahun 2004.

Sepanjang kariernya, Bimo dikenal sebagai pemimpin yang adaptif dengan keunggulan dalam menyelesaikan tugas-tugas kompleks, merancang operasi bisnis yang ramping, dan membangun sistem kerja efektif di berbagai Perusahaan.

Dengan latar belakang teknis yang kuat dipadukan dengan wawasan bisnis yang tajam, Bimo membawa nilai tambah strategis bagi VKTR dalam mewujudkan visi sebagai pelopor mobilitas listrik komersial di Indonesia.

An Indonesian citizen, 45 years old, he has served as Director of VKTR since 2024.

He concurrently serves as President Director of PT Bakrie Autoparts dan PT Braja Mukti Cakra, Deputy Chief Executive Officer at PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk.

Previously, he held various positions including Head of the Project Management Office at PT Bakrie Global Ventura from 2016 to 2020, Director and CEO of PT Inovasi Teknologi Nusantara (a subsidiary of Bakrie Global) from 2015 to 2018, VP of Technology Operations at PT Bakrie Telecom Tbk from 2013 to 2015, and Senior Account Manager at PT Ericsson Indonesia from 2007 to 2013.

Bimo holds a Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering from the Bandung Institute of Technology (2002) and a Master of Engineering in Telecommunications Engineering from the University of Melbourne (2004).

Throughout his career, Bimo has been recognized as an adaptive leader with strengths in solving complex tasks, designing lean business operations, and building effective organizational systems across companies.

With a strong technical background combined with sharp business acumen, Bimo delivers strategic value to VKTR in realizing its vision as a pioneer of commercial electric mobility in Indonesia.
