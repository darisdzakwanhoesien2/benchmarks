Untuk tahun yang berakhir pada tanggal 31 Desember 2024 dan 2023, amortisasi yang dibebankan pada akun beban umum dan administrasi sebesar Rp92 juta dan Rp19 juta.

On May 15, 2024, the Company received a patent certificate for an invention titled "Process for Producing Nickel-Rich Positive Electrode Active Material for Lithium-Ion Batteries". The duration of protection for the simple patent is granted for ten (10) years, starting from the date of receipt.

For the years ended December 31, 2024 and 2023, the amortization charged to the general and administrative expense account amounted to Rp92 million and Rp19 million.
