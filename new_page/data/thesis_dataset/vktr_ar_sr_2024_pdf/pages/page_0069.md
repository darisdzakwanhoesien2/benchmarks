Program pelatihan ini tidak hanya meningkatkan kompetensi karyawan, tetapi juga mendukung visi pemerintah dalam mempercepat elektrifikasi transportasi dan mencapai target Net Zero Emission pada 2060. Dengan menciptakan lapangan kerja baru dan membangun kapabilitas lokal, VKTR menunjukkan kontribusi nyata dalam membangun ekosistem kendaraan listrik yang inklusif, berdaya saing, dan berkelanjutan bagi masyarakat Indonesia.

Selain itu, dari sisi kinerja tata kelola, Perseroan menempatkan tata kelola keberlanjutan sebagai fondasi strategis dalam mewujudkan pertumbuhan berkelanjutan. Untuk mendukung hal tersebut, Perseroan merencanakan pembentukan Komite dan Gugus Tugas Keberlanjutan pada tahun 2025, serta melaksanakan evaluasi efektivitas tata kelola melalui mekanisme self-assessment dan penilaian independen. Prinsip transparansi, akuntabilitas, tanggung jawab, independensi, dan kewajaran senantiasa dijunjung tinggi dalam setiap proses pengambilan keputusan. Komitmen ini juga tercermin melalui perolehan sertifikasi ISO 9001:2015 atas sistem manajemen mutu untuk proses manufaktur komponen kendaraan seperti brake drum, disc brake, dan bracket generator, yang berlaku hingga Juni 2027. Dengan tata kelola yang kuat, VKTR optimistis dapat memperkuat posisinya sebagai pemimpin mobilitas hijau nasional yang mampu menciptakan nilai jangka panjang secara berkelanjutan.

From the social aspect, the Company believes that industrial development must go hand in hand with the improvement of human resource quality. To support the operation of its electric vehicle assembly facility, the Company has recruited and trained skilled workers in both technical and operational fields. This training program not only enhances employee competencies but also supports the government's vision of accelerating transportation electrification and achieving the Net Zero Emission target by 2060. By creating new job opportunities and building local capabilities, VKTR demonstrates its tangible contribution to developing an inclusive, competitive, and sustainable electric vehicle ecosystem for the Indonesian community.

In terms of governance performance, the Company positions sustainability governance as a strategic foundation for achieving sustainable growth. To support this, the Company plans to establish a Sustainability Committee and Task Force in 2025, as well as conduct governance effectiveness evaluations through self-assessments and independent reviews. The principles of transparency, accountability, responsibility, independence, and fairness are consistently upheld in every decision-making process. This commitment is further demonstrated by the attainment of ISO 9001:2015 certification for the quality management system covering the manufacturing processes of vehicle components such as brake drums, disc brakes, and generator brackets, valid through June 2027. With strong governance practices, VKTR is optimistic about strengthening its position as a national leader in green mobility, capable of creating long-term sustainable value.
