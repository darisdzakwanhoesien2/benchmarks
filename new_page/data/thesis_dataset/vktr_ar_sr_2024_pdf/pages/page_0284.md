Penilaian kinerja Direksi dilakukan dengan mekanisme sebagai berikut:

a. Dewan Komisaris menetapkan kebijakan mengenai penilaian kinerja Direksi secara kolegial dan individual, dengan mempertimbangkan pencapaian KPI serta efektivitas pengelolaan Perseroan dalam menghadapi tantangan bisnis. Selain itu, penilaian juga dilakukan secara tidak langsung melalui pemberian saran, masukan, dan nasihat kepada Direksi sebagai bagian dari fungsi pengawasan.

b. Dewan Komisaris melakukan penilaian kinerja Direksi berdasarkan indikator kinerja utama yang telah ditetapkan sebelumnya, baik secara kolegial maupun individual, dengan mempertimbangkan realisasi pencapaian kinerja. Dalam proses ini, Dewan Komisaris juga memberikan arahan dan rekomendasi terkait aspek strategis, termasuk rencana pengembangan usaha, langkah-langkah mitigasi risiko, pengambilan keputusan dalam menghadapi tantangan bisnis, serta penyusunan anggaran tahunan dan laporan keuangan.

c. Dewan Komisaris menyampaikan hasil penilaian kinerja Direksi secara kolegial dan individual kepada RUPS dalam Laporan Tugas Pengawasan Dewan Komisaris secara tahunan, termasuk evaluasi terhadap efektivitas implementasi strategi yang telah dijalankan. Laporan ini juga mencakup penilaian terhadap aspek tata kelola, termasuk penunjukan kantor akuntan publik sebagai auditor eksternal guna memastikan transparansi dan akuntabilitas kinerja Perseroan.

The Board of Directors' performance assessment is carried out through the following mechanisms:

a. The Board of Commissioners establishes policies regarding the performance evaluation of the Board of Directors, both collectively and individually, taking into account KPI achievements and the effectiveness of the Company's management in addressing business challenges. Additionally, the Board of Commissioners assesses performance indirectly by providing advice, input, and guidance as part of its supervisory function.

b. The Board of Commissioners evaluates the performance of the Board of Directors based on predefined key performance indicators, both collectively and individually, while considering actual performance achievements. In this process, the Board of Commissioners also provides direction and recommendations on various strategic aspects, including business development plans, risk mitigation strategies, decision-making in addressing business challenges, as well as the preparation of the annual budget and financial statements.

c. The Board of Commissioners presents the performance evaluation results of the Board of Directors, both collectively and individually, to the GMS in the Annual Supervisory Report, including an assessment of the effectiveness of the implemented strategies. This report also covers corporate governance aspects, including the appointment of a public accounting firm as an external auditor to ensure transparency and accountability in the Company's performance.
