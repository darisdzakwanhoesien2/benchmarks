SUSTAINABILITY GOVERNANCE

- Kebijakan Nominasi dan Pemilihan Anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi [GRI 2-10]

Perseroan memiliki kebijakan yang mengatur proses nominasi dan pemilihan anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi yang dilakukan secara transparan dan berbasis pada prinsip profesionalisme serta integritas.

- Pemenuhan Hak Pemegang Saham Sesuai Ketentuan dan Peraturan yang Berlaku

Perseroan senantiasa menjaga hak-hak Pemegang Saham dengan mematuhi ketentuan perundang-undangan yang berlaku.

- Kebijakan Mengenai Kewajiban Direksi/Dewan Komisaris Untuk Mencegah Adanya Konflik Kepentingan [GRI 2-15]

Perseroan memiliki kebijakan yang mewajibkan setiap anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris untuk senantiasa menjaga integritas dan profesionalisme dalam menjalankan tugasnya, termasuk kewajiban utama untuk mencegah timbulnya konflik kepentingan dalam pengambilan keputusan.

- Nomination and Selection Policy for Members of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors [GRI 2-10]

The Company has a policy governing the nomination and selection process of members of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors, which is conducted transparently and based on the principles of professionalism and integrity.

- Fulfillment of Shareholders’ Rights in Accordance with Prevailing Laws and Regulations

The Company consistently upholds the rights of shareholders by complying with the prevailing laws and regulations.

- Policy on the Obligations of the Board of Directors/ Board of Commissioners to Prevent Conflicts of Interest [GRI 2-15]

The Company has a policy that requires every member of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners to maintain integrity and professionalism in carrying out their duties, including a fundamental obligation to prevent the emergence of conflicts of interest in the decision-making process.
