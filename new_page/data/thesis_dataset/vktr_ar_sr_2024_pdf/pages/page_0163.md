The ongoing synergy between monetary authorities, fiscal authorities, and the business sector has made Indonesia's economy resilient amid the global slowdown. According to the Central Statistics Agency (BPS), Indonesia's national economic growth reached 5.03% in 2024. Annual inflation stood at 1.57%, remaining within the target range of 2.5% ±1%.

The BI Rate was maintained at 6% throughout 2024 to ensure Rupiah exchange rate stability and support national economic growth.
