Beberapa faktor eksternal yang signifikan turut memengaruhi hasil akhir Perseroan. Selain itu, sektor otomotif nasional secara keseluruhan mengalami tekanan, dengan penurunan penjualan kendaraan komersial yang merupakan segmen utama VKTR. Perseroan tetap menunjukkan ketangguhan dengan memperluas pasar bus listrik, menyelesaikan pembangunan pabrik CKD, dan merealisasikan penjualan kepada berbagai sektor strategis. Penjualan BEV yang tumbuh pesat secara nasional menjadi sinyal positif untuk masa depan. Meskipun terdapat deviasi antara target dan hasil, Direksi meyakini bahwa fondasi yang dibangun selama tahun 2024 menjadi pijakan kuat untuk percepatan pertumbuhan dan pencapaian target yang lebih ambisi di tahun-tahun mendatang.

# KENDALA DAN UPAYA PENYELESAIAN

Tahun 2024 menjadi periode yang penuh tantangan bagi VKTR, seiring dengan dinamika ekonomi global yang diwarnai oleh ketidakpastian geopolitik, fragmentasi perdagangan, dan tekanan suku bunga global yang tinggi. Kondisi ini berdampak pada fluktuasi permintaan pasar kendaraan listrik serta menciptakan tekanan terhadap daya beli dan stabilitas operasional. Di tengah kompleksitas tersebut, Perseroan juga menghadapi risiko kelangkaan sumber daya serta ketergantungan terhadap entitas anak dalam mendukung kegiatan operasional utama.

Selain itu, kompleksitas peraturan yang terus berkembang, khususnya terkait lingkungan, keselamatan, dan ketenagakerjaan, menuntut Perseroan untuk senantiasa memastikan kepatuhan penuh terhadap seluruh regulasi yang berlaku. Di sisi

Meanwhile, the Company's financial position remained stable. Total assets reached Rp1,608.60 billion, or 62.97% of the target, while equity was recorded at Rp1,155.93 billion, or 92.62% of the target. This reflects a relatively solid capital structure. On the other hand, the Company's liabilities stood at Rp452.67 billion, or 34.64% of the target, indicating a conservative approach in managing debt and obligations. Despite the deviation from the initial targets, the Board of Directors remains optimistic that the foundation built in 2024 will support better achievements in the coming year.

Several significant external factors also influenced the Company's final performance. In addition, the national automotive sector as a whole experienced pressure, with declining sales in commercial vehicles, VKTR's main segment. Moreover, the political climate surrounding Indonesia's presidential election further affected the business environment and consumer purchasing power.

The Company continued to demonstrate resilience by expanding the electric bus market, completing the construction of the CKD plant, and realizing sales across various strategic sectors. The rapid growth of BEV sales at the national level serves as a positive signal for the future. Despite the deviation between targets and results, the Board of Directors believes that the foundation built throughout 2024 provides a strong platform for accelerated growth and the achievement of more ambitious targets in the years to come.

# CHALLENGES AND STRATEGIC RESOLUTIONS

The year 2024 was a challenging period for VKTR, marked by global economic dynamics shaped by geopolitical uncertainties, trade fragmentation, and sustained high global interest rates. These conditions impacted fluctuations in electric vehicle market demand and exerted pressure on purchasing power and operational stability. Amid this complexity, the Company also faced risks related to resource scarcity and a high dependence on its subsidiaries to support core operational activities.
