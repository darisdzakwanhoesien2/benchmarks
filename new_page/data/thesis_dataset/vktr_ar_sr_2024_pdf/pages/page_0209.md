## Arus Kas dari Aktivitas Investasi

Arus kas neto digunakan untuk aktivitas investasi tercatat negatif sebesar Rp607,60 miliar di tahun 2024. Kas neto untuk aktivitas investasi negatif disebabkan antara lain adanya kenaikan investasi jangka pendek, kenaikan aset neto tidak lancar lainnya, dan kenaikan perolehan aset tetap. Sedangkan kas neto dari investasi sebesar Rp40,74 miliar di tahun 2023.

## Arus Kas dari Aktivitas Pendanaan

Arus kas neto dari aktivitas pendanaan tercatat positif sebesar Rp69,04 miliar di tahun 2024, menurun dari tahun 2023 sebesar Rp712,94 miliar. Penurunan disebabkan karena telah diterimanya dan IPO Perseroan pada tahun sebelumnya.

## Kas dan Setara Kas Awal Tahun

Kas dan setara kas awal tahun 2024 tercatat sebesar Rp735,90 miliar, meningkat sebesar 938,01% dibanding awal tahun 2023 sebesar Rp70,89 miliar. Peningkatan dikarenakan Perseroan menerima pendanaan dari hasil melakukan penawaran perdana saham.

## Kas dan Setara Kas Akhir Tahun

Kas dan setara kas akhir tahun 2024 sebesar Rp75,12 miliar, menurun dibandingkan akhir tahun 2023 sebesar Rp735,90 miliar. Penurunan disebabkan oleh:

1. Penggunaan dana untuk realisasi CAPEX (Pembangunan Fasilitas Produksi baik Bangunan Pabrik, Mesin Produksi, dan Pembelian Kendaraan Uji Coba dan Prototipe).
2. Penggunaan dana untuk modal kerja.
3. Investasi Jangka Pendek berupa Deposito berjangka waktu lebih dari 3 bulan.
4. Establishing a production facility in Magelang to enhance product competitiveness and increase local content in every manufactured product.
5. Developing strategic collaborations with industry partners to explore new business opportunities and gain a competitive advantage.
6. Building with financial institutions to provide financing options to customers.

## Cash Flows from Investing Activities

Net cash used in investing activities was recorded at a negative Rp607.60 billion in 2024. The negative cash flow from investing activities was mainly due to an increase in short-term investments, an increase in other non-current assets, and higher acquisition of fixed assets. In comparison, net cash from investing activities amounted to Rp40.74 billion in 2023.

## Cash Flows from Financing Activities

Net cash from financing activities was recorded at a positive Rp69.04 billion in 2024, a decrease from Rp712.94 billion in 2023. The decrease was due to the receipt of proceeds from the Company's IPO in the previous year.

## Cash and Cash Equivalents at the Beginning of the Year

Cash and cash equivalents at the beginning of 2024 stood at Rp735.90 billion, an increase of 938.01% compared to Rp70.89 billion at the beginning of 2023. The increase was due to the Company receiving funding from the proceeds of its initial public offering.

## Cash and Cash Equivalents at the End of the Year

Cash and cash equivalents at the end of 2024 amounted to Rp75.12 billion, a decrease compared to Rp735.90 billion. The decline was due to:

1. Use of funds for the realization of CAPEX (Construction of Production Facilities including Factory Buildings, Production Machinery, and Purchase of Test and Prototype Vehicles).
2. Use of funds for working capital.
3. Short-term investments in the form of time deposits with maturities of more than 3 months.
