# RINGKASAN EKSEKUTIF
# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Perseroan menunjukkan kemajuan signifikan dalam inisiatif keberlanjutannya selama tahun 2024, mencapai pertumbuhan finansial dan kontribusi penting dalam aspek lingkungan dan sosial. Meskipun terjadi penurunan penjualan bersih akibat tantangan pasar eksternal, Perseroan berhasil mempertahankan stabilitas pertumbuhan ekuitas dan aset, serta meningkatkan profitabilitas. Fokus Perseroan dalam mengurangi konsumsi energi dan emisi, serta meningkatkan investasi sosial, menegaskan komitmennya terhadap pembangunan berkelanjutan jangka panjang.

The Company demonstrated significant progress in its sustainability initiatives throughout 2024, achieving financial growth while making meaningful contributions to environmental and social aspects. Despite a decline in net sales due to external market challenges, the Company managed to maintain stable equity and asset growth while improving profitability. The Company's focus on reducing energy consumption and emissions, as well as increasing social investments, underscores its commitment to long-term sustainable development.
