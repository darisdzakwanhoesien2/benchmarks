As part of this commitment, the government is actively promoting the adoption of electric vehicles (EVs), recognizing that the transportation sector serve a significant role in global warming. To accelerate the shift to EVs, various incentives have been introduced for EV importers and consumers, encouraging the adoption of emission-free vehicles across Indonesia.
