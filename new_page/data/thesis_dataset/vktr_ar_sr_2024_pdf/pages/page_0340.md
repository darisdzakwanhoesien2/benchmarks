» Apabila pelanggaran terbukti, Komite Etik &amp; Kepatuhan melanjutkan ke proses berikutnya.

9. Apabila pelanggaran terbukti:
» Komite Etik &amp; Kepatuhan menetapkan sanksi dan mengeluarkan Surat Keputusan Penetapan Sanksi Pelaporan Pelanggaran untuk Terlapor.
» Komite Etik &amp; Kepatuhan menyerahkan kepada Head Human Capital (HC) untuk tindak lanjut Surat Keputusan Penetapan Sanksi Pelaporan Pelanggaran.
» Komite Etik &amp; Kepatuhan dapat menyerahkan kepada Departemen Corporate Legal untuk setiap pelanggaran terhadap hukum dan peraturan perundang-undangan untuk ditindaklanjuti kepada pihak yang berwenang.

10. Head HC memproses tindak lanjut Surat Keputusan Penetapan Sanksi Pelaporan Pelanggaran dan memberikannya kepada Terlapor.

11. Apabila pelanggaran terbukti:
a. Komite Etik &amp; Kepatuhan menetapkan sanksi dan mengeluarkan Surat Keputusan Penetapan Sanksi Pelaporan Pelanggaran kepada Terlapor.
b. Komite Etik &amp; Kepatuhan menyerahkan kepada Fungsi HC untuk tindak lanjut Surat Keputusan Penetapan Sanksi Pelaporan Pelanggaran.
c. Komite Etik &amp; Kepatuhan dapat menyerahkan kepada Departemen Corporate Legal untuk setiap pelanggaran terhadap hukum dan peraturan perundang-undangan untuk ditindaklanjuti kepada pihak yang berwenang.

8. Based on the Investigation Report (LHI) received, the Ethics &amp; Compliance Committee conducts a review and analysis of the investigation results as the basis for decision-making.
» If the violation is not proven, the Ethics &amp; Compliance Committee does not impose any sanctions and the case is declared closed.
» If the violation is proven, the Ethics &amp; Compliance Committee proceeds to the next process.

9. If the violation is proven:
» The Ethics &amp; Compliance Committee imposes a sanction and issues a Decision Letter on Violation Report Sanction Determination for the Reported Party.
» The Ethics &amp; Compliance Committee submits the Decision Letter on Violation Report Sanction Determination to the Head of Human Capital (HC) for follow-up.
» The Ethics &amp; Compliance Committee may refer the case to the Corporate Legal Department for any violations of laws and regulations to be followed up with the relevant authorities.

10. The Head of Human Capital (HC) processes the follow-up of the Decision Letter on Violation Report Sanction Determination and delivers it to the Reported Party.

11. If the violation is proven:
a. The Ethics &amp; Compliance Committee imposes a sanction and issues the Decision Letter on Violation Report Sanction Determination to the Reported Party.
b. The Ethics &amp; Compliance Committee submits the Decision Letter on Violation Report Sanction Determination to the HC Function for follow-up.
c. The Ethics &amp; Compliance Committee may refer the case to the Corporate Legal Department for any violations of laws and regulations to be followed up with the relevant authorities.
