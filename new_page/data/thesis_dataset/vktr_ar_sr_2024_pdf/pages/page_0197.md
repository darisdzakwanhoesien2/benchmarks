# Keterangan:

&gt; Divisi penjualan menerima Purchase Order ("PO") dari pelanggan terkait dengan kebutuhan unit, spesifikasi dan jadwal kedatangan.
&gt; Divisi keuangan melakukan penagihan Down Payment ("DP") atas PO kepada pelanggan serta meminta proof of fund sebagai dasar pelunasan PO tersebut. Perseroan akan mengecek compliance (kelengkapan) seluruh dokumen, apakah dana telah diterima dan pelanggan dapat membuktikan proof of fund.
&gt; Divisi office support melakukan pemesanan produk ke principal.
&gt; OEM akan membuat jadwal pengiriman dan mengirimkan bus ke Perseroan.
&gt; Perseroan akan membayar pajak impor dan bea masuk. Jika permintaan unit dalam bentuk Completely Knocked Down (CKD), setelah barang diterima, Divisi Office Support akan menerbitkan PO ke karoseri. Sebelum unit diterima, baik CKD atau Completely Build Up (CBU), akan dilakukan Per Delivery Inspection (PDI) oleh teknisi. Setelah lolos uji PDI, unit akan dikirimkan ke BA sebelum dilakukan serah terima ke pelanggan.
&gt; Divisi Office Support akan menerbitkan PO kepada vendor yang akan melakukan proses Surat Tanda Nomor Kendaraan (STNK) dan Uji Kendaraan Secara Teknis (KEUR). Setelah proses STNK dan KEUR selesai, Divisi Finance akan melakukan penagihan atas pelunasan.
&gt; Bus dikirimkan kepada pelanggan.
&gt; Setelah pelunasan diterima, Divisi Sales Dan Teknisi Perseroan akan menyiapkan dokumen Berita Acara Serah Terima (BAST) dan melakukan serah terima unit kepada pelanggan.

# Description:

&gt; The Sales Division receives a Purchase Order (PO) from customers regarding unit requirements, specifications, and delivery schedules.
&gt; The Finance Division issues an invoice for the Down Payment (DP) based on the PO and requests a proof of fund from the customer as a basis for PO settlement. The Company will check compliance and completeness of all documents, ensuring that funds have been received and the customer can provide a valid proof of fund.
&gt; The office support division places product orders to the principal.
&gt; The OEM schedules delivery and ships the buses to the Company.
&gt; The Company processes import taxes and customs duties. If the unit order is in Completely Knocked Down (CKD) form, once the goods are received, the Office Support Division will issue a PO to the body manufacturer. Before receiving the unit, whether CKD or Completely Built-Up (CBU), a Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) will be conducted by technicians. After passing the PDI test, the unit is sent to BA before final handover to the customer.
&gt; The Office Support Division issues a PO to the vendor responsible for processing the Vehicle Registration Certificate (STNK) and Technical Vehicle Inspection (KEUR). Once STNK and KEUR processes are completed, the Finance Division will issue an invoice for the final payment.
&gt; The bus is delivered to the customer.
&gt; Upon receipt of the final payment, the Company's Sales and Technician Division prepares the Handover Report Document (BAST) and hands over the unit to the customer.
