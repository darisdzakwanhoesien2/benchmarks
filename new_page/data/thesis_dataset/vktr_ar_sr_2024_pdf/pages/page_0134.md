# Alex Kim

Chief Strategy Officer
Chief Strategy Officer

Alex Kim, Warga Negara Korea Selatan, berusia 47 tahun adalah eksekutif global berpengalaman yang saat ini menjabat sebagai Chief Strategy Officer di PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk sejak tahun 2023. Beliau memiliki latar belakang kuat dalam pengembangan korporasi, perencanaan strategis, restrukturisasi bisnis, dan fundraising di industri kendaraan listrik global.

Sebelum bergabung dengan VKTR, Alex mengemban berbagai posisi strategis seperti Senior Vice President di Arrival, General Manager di Hyundai Motors Company, serta Vice President di Morgan Stanley Private Equity dan Deutsche Bank Korea. Pengalamannya mencakup transaksi IPO, M&amp;A lintas negara, serta pendanaan korporasi berskala besar.

Di VKTR, Alex bertanggung jawab atas penyusunan strategi Perseroan, pelaksanaan business plan, penggalangan dana, serta mengawasi pengelolaan hubungan investor. Perannya krusial dalam memperkuat fondasi VKTR pasca-IPO dan memperluas eksistensi global Perseroan di sektor kendaraan listrik.

Alex meraih gelar Bachelor of Arts di bidang Ekonomi dari Moscow State University dan University of Colorado, serta merupakan kandidat Master of Science di bidang Corporate Finance dari London School of Economics. Penguasaannya terhadap bahasa Korea, Inggris, Jerman, dan Rusia mendukung efektivitas komunikasi lintas budaya.

Dengan pengalaman internasional yang luas dan keahlian mendalam dalam bidang keuangan strategis, Alex berperan penting dalam memperkuat posisi VKTR sebagai pemain global di industri mobilitas listrik.

Alex Kim, South Korean citizen, 47 years old, is a seasoned global executive currently serving as Chief Strategy Officer at PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk since 2023. He has a strong background in corporate development, strategic planning, business restructuring, and fundraising, particularly within the global electric vehicle sector.

Prior to joining VKTR, Alex held various strategic roles, including Senior Vice President at Arrival, General Manager at Hyundai Motors Company, and Vice President at Morgan Stanley Private Equity and Deutsche Bank Korea. His experience spans IPO transactions, cross-border M&amp;As, and large-scale corporate financings.

At VKTR, Alex is responsible for developing the Company strategies, executing business plans, managing fundraising activities, and overseeing investor relations. His role is critical in strengthening VKTR's foundation post-IPO and expanding the Company's global presence in the electric vehicle sector.

Alex earned a Bachelor of Arts in Economics from Moscow State University and the University of Colorado and was a candidate for a Master of Science in Corporate Finance at the London School of Economics. His fluency in Korean, English, German, and Russian enhances cross-cultural communication effectiveness.

With extensive international experience and deep expertise in strategic finance, Alex serves a vital role in strengthening VKTR's position as a global player in the electric mobility industry
