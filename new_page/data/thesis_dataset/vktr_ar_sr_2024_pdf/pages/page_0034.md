BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS REPORT

# 22

Tahun 2024 merupakan tonggak penting bagi VKTR dalam memperkuat posisinya sebagai pionir kendaraan listrik komersial di Indonesia. Melalui komitmen kuat menuju net zero emissions 2060, ekspansi kendaraan listrik berbasis CKD, serta penguatan tata kelola dan efisiensi, VKTR menunjukkan ketangguhan dalam menghadapi tantangan global dan memperkuat kontribusinya terhadap transisi energi bersih.

The year 2024 marked a pivotal milestone for VKTR in solidifying its position as a pioneer of commercial electric vehicles in Indonesia. With a firm commitment to achieving net zero emissions by 2060, the expansion of CKD-based electric vehicle production, and the strengthening of governance and efficiency, VKTR demonstrated resilience amidst global challenges and reinforced its contribution to the clean energy transition.
