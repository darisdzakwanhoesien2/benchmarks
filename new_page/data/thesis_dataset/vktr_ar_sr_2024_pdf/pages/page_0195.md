# TINJAUAN OPERASIONAL SEGMEN USAHA BUSINESS SEGMENT OPERATIONAL REVIEW

![img-180.jpeg](img-180.jpeg)

Perseroan menjalankan kegiatan utama di bidang perdagangan besar mobil baru, khususnya Kendaraan Bermotor Listrik Berbasis Baterai (KBLBB). Selain itu, Perseroan juga mengembangkan berbagai kegiatan pendukung, termasuk pelayanan purna jual yang unggul (after sales) bisnis komponen suku cadang, aksesori mobil, serta industri karoseri untuk kendaraan bermotor roda empat atau lebih, kendaraan Listrik berbasis baterai, serta sektor industri pengecoran besi dan baja. Perseroan juga terlibat dalam industri suku cadang dan aksesori kendaraan bermotor roda empat atau lebih, yang semakin memperkuat ekosistem bisnisnya.

Tinjauan operasional Perseroan mencakup strategi bisnis dan pemasaran, aktivitas penjualan KBLBB, serta proses manufaktur dan kinerjanya. Dalam operasionalnya, Perseroan membagi bisnisnya ke dalam dua segmen utama, yaitu Perdagangan dan Manufaktur, yang masing-masing memiliki peran strategis dalam mendukung pertumbuhan Perseroan dan industri kendaraan listrik di Indonesia.

The Company conducts its core business in the wholesale trade of new automobiles, specifically Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs). In addition, the Company also develops various supporting activities, including excellent after-sales services, the spare parts and accessories business, as well as the coachbuilding industry for four-wheeled or more motor vehicles, battery-based electric vehicles, and the iron and steel casting industry. The Company is also involved in the automotive spare parts and accessories industry for four-wheeled or more motor vehicles, further strengthening its business ecosystem.

The Company's operational review covers business and marketing strategies, BEV sales activities, manufacturing processes, and overall performance. The Company structures its business operations into two primary segments: Trading and Manufacturing, both of which serve a strategic role in supporting the Company's growth and the expansion of Indonesia's electric vehicle industry.
