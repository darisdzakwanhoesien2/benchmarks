# Pertumbuhan Bisnis dan Pencapaian Strategis VKTR di 2024

VKTR terus mencatat pertumbuhan stabil di setiap kuartal sepanjang tahun 2024, didorong oleh meningkatnya permintaan dan purchase order untuk berbagai lini produk. Tren positif ini semakin memperkuat optimisme Perseroan dalam mempertahankan ekspansi berkelanjutan di tahun-tahun mendatang.

Hingga akhir September 2024, VKTR berhasil memenangkan tender Transjakarta melalui operator untuk 20 unit bus listrik 12 meter Completely Knocked Down (CKD). Unit-unit ini ditargetkan untuk diserahterimakan kepada operator pada tahun 2024, berkontribusi terhadap upaya pengurangan emisi transportasi di Jakarta.

Di luar proyek Transjakarta, VKTR juga telah mengamankan purchase order (PO) dari instansi pemerintah dan Badan Usaha Milik Negara (BUMN), termasuk unit garbage compactor, dump truck, dan arm roll truck. Dua unit compactor listrik pertama VKTR bahkan telah beroperasi di Ibu Kota Nusantara (IKN) sejak Agustus 2024.

# VKTR's Business Growth and Strategic Momentums in 2024

VKTR continues to demonstrate stable growth in each quarter of 2024, driven by increasing demand and purchase orders across its diverse product lineup. This positive trajectory reinforces the Company's confidence in sustained expansion in the coming years.

As of the end of September 2024, VKTR secured a Transjakarta tender through operators for 20 units of 12-meter Completely Knocked Down (CKD) electric buses. These units are targeted for handover to operators by the end of 2024, contributing to Jakarta's efforts to reduce transportation emissions.

Beyond the Transjakarta project, VKTR has successfully secured multiple purchase orders from government agencies and state-owned enterprises (SOEs), including garbage compactor, dump trucks, and arm roll trucks. Notably, two of VKTR's first electric compactor trucks have been operational in Ibu Kota Nusantara (IKN) since August 2024.
