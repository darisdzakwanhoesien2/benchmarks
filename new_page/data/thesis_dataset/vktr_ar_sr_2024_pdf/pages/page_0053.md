The series of research, development, and innovation initiatives implemented by VKTR throughout 2024 reflect the Company's strong commitment to driving the transition toward sustainable transportation in Indonesia. By prioritizing a market-driven approach, unit trial strategies, product diversification, and innovative business models such as e-MaaS, VKTR has not only expanded its market reach but also reinforced its position as a key player in the national electric vehicle industry.

Cross-sector collaboration, brand strengthening, and continuous market research serve as the Company's strategic foundation in delivering reliable, relevant, and competitive electric mobility solutions. Moving forward, VKTR remains dedicated to ongoing innovation and adaptability to evolving technologies and market needs, in line with its long-term vision to be a catalyst for clean-energy-based transportation transformation in Indonesia.
