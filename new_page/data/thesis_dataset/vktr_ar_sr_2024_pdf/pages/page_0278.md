## KOMISARIS INDEPENDEN

Komisaris Independen Perseroan terdiri dari 2 (dua) anggota atau sebesar 33% dari jumlah anggota Dewan Komisaris Perseroan. Dengan demikian, Perseroan telah memenuhi ketentuan POJK 33/2014 tentang Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik, serta tercermin pada Anggaran Dasar Perseroan dan Pedoman Dewan Komisaris, di mana Perseroan wajib memiliki Komisaris Independen yang jumlahnya paling kurang 30% dari jumlah seluruh anggota Dewan Komisaris.

## INDEPENDENT COMMISSIONER

The Company's Independent Commissioners consist of two members, representing 33% of the total members of the Board of Commissioners. Accordingly, the Company has complied with OJK Regulation No. 33/2014 on the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners of Issuers or Public Companies. This compliance is also reflected in the Company's Articles of Association and the Board of Commissioners' Charter, which stipulate that the Company must have Independent Commissioners comprising at least 30% of the total members of the Board of Commissioners.
