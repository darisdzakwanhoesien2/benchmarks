# 3. Information Security

To safeguard data and mitigate cybersecurity threats, the Company has adopted an advanced Endpoint Security system as a preventive measure against viruses, malware, and phishing attacks. This security infrastructure includes real-time monitoring, early threat detection, and encryption technology to protect sensitive information. In addition, the Company has implemented multi-layered security policies, including multi-factor authentication (MFA), advanced firewalls, and cybersecurity training for employees to raise awareness and preparedness against digital threats. These measures ensure a robust and secure IT environment that protects business continuity.

# 4. Enhancing Customer Service

The Company continues to improve customer service by utilizing information technology to create a more interactive and responsive customer experience. One key initiative is the development of an AI-powered chatbot system that allows customers to access information and receive assistance quickly and efficiently. In addition, the Company has implemented a CRM system to manage customer interactions, enhance service personalization, and expedite request or complaint resolution. These innovations help improve customer satisfaction, build loyalty, and strengthen long-term relationships with stakeholders. By embracing digital transformation in customer service, the Company ensures a seamless and enhanced customer experience that aligns with modern business demands.

# 5. Human Resource Development

The Company recognizes that the success of digital transformation relies not only on technology but also on the competency of the human resources managing it. Therefore, the Company actively conducts knowledge-sharing programs among employees on the latest technologies, such as Veeam Backup, VMware, and Proxmox. These programs aim to enhance employees' knowledge and technical skills in IT system management, data management, and digital infrastructure
