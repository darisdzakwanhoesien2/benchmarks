# A. Ardiansyah Bakrie

Chief Executive Officer
Chief Executive Officer

Anindra Ardiansyah Bakrie, Warga Negara Indonesia, lahir di Jakarta pada tahun 1979 berusia 46 tahun. Beliau meraih gelar Master of Business Administration (MBA) dari Bentley, McCallum Graduate School of Business, Massachusetts, Amerika Serikat pada tahun 2005, dan Bachelor of Science (BSc) di bidang Business Administration dari Georgetown University, Washington, D.C., Amerika Serikat pada tahun 2001. Pendidikan internasional ini memperkuat landasan kepemimpinan dan visinya dalam membangun Perusahaan berorientasi masa depan.

Saat ini, beliau memegang jabatan sebagai Chief Executive Officer (CEO) PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk dan Direktur Utama PT Bakrie Indo Infrastructure sejak tahun 2022. Selain itu, beliau juga menjabat sebagai Komisaris Utama di berbagai Perusahaan dalam Grup Bakrie, termasuk PT Southeast Asia Pipe Industries dan PT Multi Kontrol Nusantara, mencerminkan peran sentralnya dalam pengembangan sektor energi, infrastruktur, dan teknologi.

Sebelum bergabung di VKTR, Ardiansyah berpengalaman panjang di industri media dan telekomunikasi, dengan jabatan strategis seperti Direktur Utama PT Viva Media Baru, PT Lativi Mediakarya, serta Komisaris Utama PT Asia Global Media. Kiprah tersebut membentuk keahlian beliau dalam pengelolaan Perusahaan berbasis inovasi dan transformasi digital.

Kepemimpinan beliau di VKTR menegaskan komitmennya untuk mendorong transformasi industri kendaraan listrik di Indonesia. Dengan pendekatan strategis dan inovatif, beliau membawa VKTR menjadi pelopor dalam sektor mobilitas berkelanjutan, sejalan dengan agenda dekarbonisasi nasional.

Sebagai bagian dari keluarga Bakrie, Ardiansyah memiliki hubungan afiliasi dengan anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi serta pemegang saham pengendali di lingkungan PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk, memperkuat jaringan kolaborasi lintas Perusahaan dalam mendukung visi pertumbuhan jangka panjang.

Anindra Ardiansyah Bakrie, an Indonesian citizen, was born in Jakarta in 1979, 46 years old. He obtained a Master of Business Administration (MBA) degree from Bentley, McCallum Graduate School of Business, Massachusetts, USA, in 2005, and a Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Business Administration from Georgetown University, Washington, D.C., USA, in 2001. This international education reinforced his leadership foundation and vision in building a future-oriented Company.

Currently, he serves as Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk and President Director of PT Bakrie Indo Infrastructure since 2022. He also holds key positions within the Bakrie Group, including President Commissioner roles at PT Southeast Asia Pipe Industries and PT Multi Kontrol Nusantara, highlighting his central role in advancing the energy, infrastructure, and technology sectors.

Prior to joining VKTR, Ardiansyah gained extensive experience in the media and telecommunications industries, holding strategic roles such as President Director of PT Viva Media Baru, PT Lativi Mediakarya, and President Commissioner of PT Asia Global Media. These roles shaped his expertise in leading innovation-driven companies and navigating digital transformation.

His leadership at VKTR underscores his commitment to driving the transformation of Indonesia's electric vehicle industry. Through strategic and innovative approaches, he positions VKTR as a pioneer in sustainable mobility, aligned with the national decarbonization agenda.

As part of the Bakrie family, Ardiansyah maintains close affiliations with members of the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, and controlling shareholders within PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk, reinforcing an extensive collaborative network to drive long-term corporate growth.
