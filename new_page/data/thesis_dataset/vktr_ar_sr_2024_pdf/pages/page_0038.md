Di tengah stagnasi global, komitmen penanganan perubahan iklim menghadapi tantangan besar setelah AS memutuskan keluar dari Perjanjian Paris yang merupakan pakta iklim yang diratifikasi hampir semua negara di dunia untuk mencegah suhu bumi naik sebesar 1,5 derajat celsius.

Di lingkup domestik, perekonomian nasional tumbuh positif sebesar 5,03% secara tahun pada 2024, lebih rendah dari realisasi pada periode yang sama tahun sebelumnya sebesar 5,05%. Transisi kepemimpinan nasional dan iklim politik yang kondusif turut berperan dalam menjaga kestabilan ekonomi.

Sektor konsumsi, investasi, dan belanja pemerintah menjadi penopang utama pertumbuhan ekonomi. Ekspor migas dan nonmigas juga meningkat di tengah stagnasi global. Investasi asing dan domestik tumbuh positif dengan realisasi investasi sebesar Rp1.712,4 triliun, meningkat sebesar 20,8% secara tahun pada 2024. Portfolio investasi asing mencapai lebih dari 50% dari total investasi dengan realisasi sebesar Rp900,2 triliun. Sedangkan realisasi investasi dalam negeri sebesar Rp814,0 triliun, tumbuh sebesar 20,6% dari tahun sebelumnya.

Seiring pertumbuhan yang positif, laju inflasi tahunan terkendali sebesar 1,57% disertai dengan suku bunga acuan BI Rate yang cenderung turun. Pemerintah melalui otoritas moneter dan fiskal terus berupaya untuk menjaga stabilitas nilai tukar rupiah di tengah tekanan menguatnya mata uang dollar AS.

Sektor usaha seperti industri pengolahan, transportasi, dan perdagangan besar tumbuh positif di tahun 2024. Amid global stagnation, the commitment to addressing climate change encounters significant challenges following the United States' decision to withdraw from the Paris Agreement, a climate pact ratified by nearly every country in the world to prevent global temperatures from rising by 1.5 degrees Celsius.

On the domestic front, the national economy grew positively by 5.03% year-on-year in 2024, slightly lower than the 5.05% recorded in the same period of the previous year. The national leadership transition and a conducive political climate also served a role in maintaining economic stability.

The consumption, investment, sectors and government spending were the main drivers of economic growth. Oil and gas, as well as non-oil and gas exports also increased amid global stagnation. Foreign and domestic investments posted positive growth, with realized investment of Rp1,712.4 trillion, a 20.8% increase year-on-year in 2024. Foreign portfolio investment accounted for more than 50% of total investment with a realization of Rp900.2 trillion. Meanwhile, domestic investment realization reached Rp814.0 trillion, grew by 20.6% from the previous year.

Align with positive growth, the annual inflation rate remained controlled at 1.57%, accompanied by a downward trend in the BI benchmark interest rate. The government, through monetary and fiscal authorities, continued its efforts to maintain the stability of the rupiah exchange rate amid pressure from the strengthening U.S. dollar.

Business sectors such as manufacturing, transportation, and wholesale trade posted positive growth in 2024. The Basic Metal Industry grew by 13.34%, in line with increased foreign demand, particularly for iron and steel. The transportation and warehousing sector grew by 8.69% year-on-year, driven by increased public mobility and the strengthening trend of e-commerce trade.

In the automotive industry, based on Gaikindo data, total wholesale car sales reached 865,723 units in 2024, a decrease of 14% year-on-year compared to 1,005,802 units in 2023. Vehicle sales in the commercial segment declined by 15%.
