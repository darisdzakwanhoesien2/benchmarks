Groundbreaking Pembangunan Fasilitas Kendaraan Listrik Komersil Berbasis CKD Pertama di Indonesia yang berlokasi di Magelang Jawa Tengah

Groundbreaking of Indonesia's First CKD-Based Commercial Electric Vehicle Facility in Magelang, Central Java

Pada 27 Februari 2024, VKTR resmi memulai pembangunan fasilitas kendaraan listrik komersial berbasis Completely Knocked Down (CKD) pertama di Indonesia, melalui anak perusahaanannya PT VKTR Sakti Industries (VKTS) di Magelang, Jawa Tengah. Acara ini disaksikan oleh Menteri Perhubungan Budi Karya Sumadi, perwakilan Kementerian Perindustrian, pemerintah daerah, serta tokoh dan tamu undangan. Fasilitas ini bertujuan menjadi pusat transfer teknologi, penelitian, dan pengembangan kendaraan listrik (EV), sekaligus menciptakan lapangan kerja hijau. Sebagai pionir EV komersial, VKTR berkomitmen mendukung ekosistem kendaraan listrik di Indonesia, termasuk perakitan merek EV global seperti BYD, untuk mendukung target Net Zero Emissions 2060.

On February 27, 2024, VKTR officially commenced the construction of Indonesia's first Completely Knocked Down (CKD)-based commercial electric vehicle facility through its subsidiary, PT VKTR Sakti Industries (VKTS), in Magelang, Central Java. The event was witnessed by Minister of Transportation Budi Karya Sumadi, representatives from the Ministry of Industry, local government officials, and distinguished guests. This facility is set to become a hub for technology transfer, research, and development in the electric vehicle (EV) sector while also creating green job opportunities. As a pioneer in commercial EVs, VKTR is committed to advancing Indonesia's EV ecosystem, including assembling global EV brands such as BYD, in support of the country's Net Zero Emissions 2060 target.

VKTR memperoleh award dari The 2nd Annual EV Industry Summit Indonesia 2024 dengan kategori "EV Bus &amp; Heavy Duty Manufacture Innovation"

VKTR Receives Award at The 2nd Annual EV Industry Summit Indonesia 2024 in the "EV Bus &amp; Heavy Duty Manufacture Innovation" Category

Pada 29 Februari 2024, Alex Kim, Chief Strategist Officer VKTR, mewakili Perseroan dalam acara The 2nd Annual EV Industry Summit Indonesia 2024. Dalam kesempatan tersebut, Kim membahas peluang dan tantangan penggunaan armada kendaraan listrik komersial di Indonesia. Pada acara yang sama, VKTR meraih penghargaan bergengsi dalam kategori "EV Bus &amp; Heavy Duty Manufacture Innovation", sekaligus dinobatkan sebagai penerima 2024 EV Ecosystem Innovation Award atas inovasi terdepan yang telah dilakukan. Semoga VKTR terus berinovasi lebih jauh dan mendukung pemerintah dalam mencapai target Net Zero Emissions tahun 2060 di masa mendatang.

On February 29, 2024, Alex Kim, Chief Strategist Officer of VKTR, represented the company at The 2nd Annual EV Industry Summit Indonesia 2024. During the event, Kim discussed the opportunities and challenges in adopting commercial electric vehicle fleets in Indonesia. At the same event, VKTR received a prestigious award in the "EV Bus &amp; Heavy Duty Manufacture Innovation" category and was also recognized as the recipient of the 2024 EV Ecosystem Innovation Award for its leading innovations. VKTR remains committed to continuous innovation and supporting the government in achieving the Net Zero Emissions 2060 target.

Penandatanganan Joint Development Agreement antara VKTR dengan Pertamina New &amp; Renewable Energi

Signing of Joint Development Agreement Between VKTR and Pertamina New &amp; Renewable Energy

Kemitraan Strategis VKTR dan PNRE untuk Dekarbonisasi Transportasi. VKTR dan Pertamina New &amp; Renewable Energy (PNRE) resmi menjalin kemitraan strategis melalui penandatanganan Joint Development Agreement untuk memajukan konsep e-Mobility as a Service (e-MaaS). Kolaborasi ini membentuk usaha patungan (JV) yang bertujuan menyediakan sekitar 1.000 kendaraan listrik komersial, termasuk EV Bus, untuk memenuhi kebutuhan Transjakarta dan perusahaan lainnya. Langkah ini mencerminkan komitmen bersama VKTR dan PNRE dalam mendukung target Net Zero Emission 2060 melalui solusi transportasi ramah lingkungan di Indonesia.

Strategic Partnership Between VKTR and PNRE for Transportation Decarbonization. VKTR and Pertamina New &amp; Renewable Energy (PNRE) have officially entered into a strategic partnership through the signing of a Joint Development Agreement to advance the concept of e-Mobility as a Service (e-MaaS). This collaboration establishes a joint venture (JV) aimed at providing approximately 1,000 commercial electric vehicles, including EV buses, to meet the needs of Transjakarta and other companies. This initiative reflects the shared commitment of VKTR and PNRE to support the Net Zero Emissions 2060 target through sustainable transportation solutions in Indonesia.
