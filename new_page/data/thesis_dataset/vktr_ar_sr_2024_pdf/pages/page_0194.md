Dari sisi pengembangan produk, VKTR telah mengembangkan berbagai jenis kendaraan listrik, mulai dari jenis bus besar, bus medium, bus kecil, truk hingga tracktor head. Prototipe jenis kendaraan bus besar, bus medium, bus kecil dan truck (4×2 dan 6×4) telah memiliki Sertifikat Uji Tipe dari Kementerian Perhubungan. Sedangkan untuk jenis Tracktor Head sedang tahap persiapan proses sertifikasi kendaraan.

Kapabilitas produksi kendaraan listrik komersial VKTR akan semakin meningkat setelah pendirian perusahaan joint venture dengan Pertamina New &amp; Renewable Energy (PNRE). Kemitraan strategis ini bertujuan untuk mempercepat penerapan konsep e-Mobility as a Service (e-MaaS) dengan menyediakan sekitar 1.000 unit kendaraan listrik yang masih dalam tahap evaluasi awal. Strategi ini dilakukan guna memenuhi kebutuhan Transjakarta dan berbagai perusahaan lainnya di Indonesia.

In terms of product development, VKTR has developed various types of electric vehicles, ranging from large buses, medium buses, and small buses to trucks and tractor heads. Prototypes of large buses, medium buses, small buses, and trucks (4×2 and 6×4) have obtained Type Test Certificates from the Ministry of Transportation. Meanwhile, the tractor head variant is currently in the preparation stage for vehicle certification.

VKTR's production capabilities for commercial electric vehicles are expected to increase further following the establishment of a joint venture company with Pertamina New &amp; Renewable Energy (PNRE). This strategic partnership aims to accelerate the implementation of the e-Mobility as a Service (e-MaaS) concept by providing around 1,000 electric vehicles, which are currently under initial evaluation. This strategy is intended to meet the needs of Transjakarta and various other companies across Indonesia.
