## Pabrik Perakitan

Pada tahun 2024, PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk melalui anak usahanya, PT VKTR Sakti Industries (VKTS), mengoperasikan fasilitas perakitan kendaraan listrik komersial berbasis *Completely Knocked Down (CKD)* di Magelang senilai Rp300 miliar. Fasilitas ini memiliki kapasitas produksi hingga 3.000 unit per tahun dan menjadi pusat perakitan berbagai kendaraan listrik unggulan, termasuk bus listrik berukuran 12 meter yang telah memenuhi persyaratan Tingkat Komponen Dalam Negeri (TKDN) lebih dari 40%. Pembangunan fasilitas ini melibatkan kolaborasi dengan mitra strategis dan institusi akademik, sebagai bentuk dukungan terhadap pengembangan industri kendaraan listrik nasional.

Hingga akhir tahun 2024, progres pembangunan fasilitas perakitan kendaraan listrik berbasis CKD milik VKTR di Magelang telah mencapai 99,4%. Fasilitas ini dilengkapi dengan berbagai sistem dan peralatan manufaktur modern, termasuk jalur perakitan bus dan truk, area sub-assembly untuk front &amp; rear axle, leaf spring, dan chassis, serta slat chain conveyor dan sistem rotary untuk memindahkan rangka ke jalur perakitan umum. Untuk menjamin kualitas dan keselamatan setiap unit, fasilitas ini juga menyediakan *Shower Room*, *Battery Station*, *Truck Sub Assembly Line*, *Wheel Alignment*, dan *Inspection Room*, yang memungkinkan dilakukannya proses pengujian menyeluruh sebelum kendaraan didistribusikan. Keberadaan fasilitas ini tidak hanya memperkuat posisi VKTR sebagai pelopor kendaraan listrik berbasis CKD di Indonesia, tetapi juga mendukung efisiensi operasional dan meningkatkan daya saing industri kendaraan listrik nasional.

# FACILITIES

The facilities in product manufacturing process are designed with advanced technology and an integrated system, by following matters:

## Assembly Plant

In 2024, PT VKTR Teknologi Mobilitas Tbk, through its subsidiary PT VKTR Sakti Industries (VKTS), operated a commercial electric vehicle assembly facility in Magelang based on the Completely Knocked Down (CKD) system, with an investment value of IDR 300 billion and a production capacity of up to 3,000 units per year. One of the key products assembled at this facility is the 12-meter electric bus, which has fulfilled the Local Content (TKDN) requirement more than 40%. Built in collaboration with strategic partners and academic institutions, the facility supports the development of Indonesia's national electric vehicle industry.

By the end of 2024, construction progress of VKTR's CKD-based electric vehicle assembly facility in Magelang had reached 99.4%. The facility is equipped with modern manufacturing systems and equipment, including bus and truck assembly lines, sub-assembly areas for front &amp; rear axles, leaf springs, and chassis, as well as slat chain conveyors and rotary systems to move chassis to the general assembly line. To ensure quality and safety, the facility also includes a *Shower Room*, *Battery Station*, *Truck Sub Assembly Line*, *Wheel Alignment*, and an *Inspection Room*, enabling comprehensive testing before vehicle deployment. This facility strengthens VKTR's role as a pioneer in CKD-based electric vehicles in Indonesia while also enhancing operational efficiency and the national EV industry's competitiveness.
