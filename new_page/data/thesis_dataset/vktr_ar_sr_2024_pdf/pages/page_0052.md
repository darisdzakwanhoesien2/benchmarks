# PENELITIAN &amp; PENGEMBANGAN SERTA INOVASI

## Diversifikasi Produk melalui Pengembangan Prototipe Berbasis Permintaan Pasar

Sepanjang tahun 2024, VKTR mengembangkan berbagai prototipe kendaraan listrik untuk memperkuat portofolio produk dalam menghadapi dinamika pasar yang terus berubah. Prototipe tersebut meliputi bus listrik 12 meter dan 8 meter, transporter, tractor head, light-duty dan heavy-duty truck, serta forklift elektrik. Keberadaan prototipe ini memungkinkan calon pelanggan melakukan uji coba langsung, mempercepat adopsi kendaraan listrik, sekaligus memberikan fleksibilitas lebih besar bagi tim penjualan dalam menyasar kebutuhan lintas industri.

## Strategi Trial Unit sebagai Pendekatan Penetrasi Pasar Proaktif

VKTR melaksanakan uji coba unit kendaraan di berbagai sektor industri sebagai bagian dari strategi penetrasi pasar proaktif. Melalui inisiatif ini, pelanggan potensial dapat mengevaluasi langsung kinerja, efisiensi, dan keandalan kendaraan listrik VKTR dalam mendukung operasional mereka. Hasil uji coba memperlihatkan kualitas dan daya saing produk VKTR di berbagai kondisi operasional nyata.

## Inovasi Skema e-Mobility as a Service (e-MaaS)

Untuk menjawab tantangan investasi awal kendaraan listrik, VKTR memperkenalkan inovasi skema e-MaaS di mana pelanggan hanya dikenakan biaya operasional tanpa perlu belanja modal (CAPEX) di awal. Skema ini membuka peluang baru bagi sektor industri yang ingin melakukan transisi kendaraan tanpa risiko finansial besar, sekaligus mendorong percepatan elektrifikasi kendaraan listrik komersial dan perluasan pasar nasional.

# RESEARCH &amp; DEVELOPMENT AND INNOVATION

## Product Diversification through Market-Driven Prototype Development

Throughout 2024, VKTR developed a wide range of electric vehicle prototypes to strengthen its product portfolio in response to the ever-changing market landscape. These prototypes include 12-meter and 8-meter electric buses, transporters, tractor heads, light-duty and heavy-duty trucks, as well as electric forklifts. The availability of these prototypes enables potential customers to conduct direct trials, accelerating electric vehicle adoption while giving the sales team greater flexibility in targeting cross-industry needs.

## Unit Trial Strategy as a Proactive Market Penetration Approach

VKTR conducted unit trials across various industrial sectors as part of a proactive market penetration strategy. Through this initiative, potential customers were able to directly assess the performance, efficiency, and reliability of VKTR's electric vehicles in supporting their operations. The trial results demonstrated the quality and competitiveness of VKTR's products under tangible operating conditions.

## e-Mobility as a Service (e-MaaS) Scheme Innovation

To address the challenge of upfront investment in electric vehicles, VKTR introduced the innovative e-MaaS scheme, under which customers are only charged operational costs without the need for initial capital expenditure (CAPEX). This model opens new opportunities for industries seeking to transition to electric vehicles without significant financial risks, while encouraging the acceleration of the electrification of commercial electric vehicles and the expansion of the national market.
