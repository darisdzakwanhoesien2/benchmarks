**Establishment of Subsidiaries (continued)**

PT Jaringan Infra Andalan ("JIA") (d/h PT Mitra Digital Ekosistem ("MDE")) (continued)

Based on the Deed of Circular Decision / Resolution Statement of the Shareholders of the Limited Company No. 45 dated January 29, 2020 from Notary Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., the Company sold shares with value of Rp 239,000,000, which decreased Company's ownership in JIA to 360 shares or equivalent to 60% ownership.

Based on the Deed of Statement of Decision/Circular Decision of Shareholders of the Limited Liability Company No. 59 dated May 28, 2024 from Notary Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., the Company purchased JIA shares worth Rp239,000,000 and experienced an increase in ownership in MDE to 599 shares or equivalent to 99% ownership, and changed Article 1 of the Articles of Association by changing the name from PT Mitra Digital Ekosistem to PT Jaringan Infra Andalan and has received approval for changes to the articles of association from the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decision Letter No. AHU-0031059.AH.01.02.Tahun 2024 dated May 29, 2024.

Based on the Deed of Statement of Decision/Circular Decision of Shareholders of the Limited Liability Company No. 78 dated September 25, 2024 from Notary Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., the Company amended Article 3 of the Articles of Association by changing the intent and purpose and business activities including: telecommunications activities with cables, telecommunications activities without cables, internet service providers and internet interconnection services (NAP) and this amendment has received approval for amendments to the articles of association from the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decision Letter No. AHU-0061261.AH.01.02.Year 2024 dated September 27, 2024.

Based on the Deed of Statement of Shareholders' Decision No. 02 dated November 5, 2024 from Notary Selo Selvieana, S.H., M.Kn., the Company conducted a stock split with a ratio of 1:1,000 with a nominal value of Rp 1,000 per share, so that the number of shares in circulation changed to 600,000 shares. This change has been approved by the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decision Letter No. AHU-AH.01.03-0207922 dated November 5, 2024.
