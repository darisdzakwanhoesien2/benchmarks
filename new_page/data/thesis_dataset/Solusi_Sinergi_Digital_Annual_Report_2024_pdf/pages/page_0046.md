## Kegiatan Usaha Periklanan:

Sejak tahun 2018, Perseroan dan Perusahaan Anak, yaitu IMT dan AMI, menyediakan jasa periklanan dalam bentuk Digital Out-of-home Advertising dan digital advertising, baik di kereta api dan fasilitasnya (railway media) maupun di luar kereta api dan fasilitasnya (non-rail media).

Dalam memperluas kegiatan usaha periklanan, Perseroan pada tahun 2022 bekerjasama dengan perusahaan transportasi umum berbasis bus untuk mengelola pengembangan bisnis dan menjalankan kegiatan periklanan seperti bus branding, coverageat dan kegiatan jasa iklan lainnya.

## Kegiatan Usaha Produk dan Layanan Digital:

Dengan berkembangnyapasa digital dan perkembangan tren teknologi bertumbuh begitu cepat dari tahun ke tahun, kami berfokus pada pengembangan bisnis Digital untuk mencapai pertumbuhan yang berkelanjutan. Perseroan memperluas kegiatan usahanya ke bidang produk dan layanan digital berupa penyediaan perangkat lunak (software) yang inovatif dan berkualitas tinggi. Langkah ini diambil sebagai respons terhadap perkembangan pesat dalam teknologi informasi dan permintaan pasar yang semakin meningkat terhadap solusi digital. Pembuatan software dilakukan melalui Perusahaan Anak, yaitu KKD yang telah beroperasi sejak 2019, yang telah menciptakan dan mengembangkan berbagai aplikasi yang bersinergi dengan berbagai mitra Perseroan.

## Advertising Business Activities:

Since 2018, the Company and its subsidiaries, IMT and AMI, have been providing advertising services in the form of Digital Out-of-Home (DOOH) Advertising and digital advertising, both within trains and their facilities (railway media) as well as outside railway facilities (non-rail media).

To further expand its advertising business activities, in 2022, the Company established a collaboration with a bus-based public transportation company to manage business development and carry out advertising activities such as bus branding, cover seat advertising, and other advertising services.

## Digital Products and Services Business Activities:

With the rapid expansion of the digital market and the continuous growth of technological trends year over year, the Company remains focused on developing its digital business to achieve sustainable growth. The Company has expanded its business activities into the digital products and services sector by providing innovative and high-quality software solutions. This strategic initiative was undertaken in response to the rapid advancements in information technology and the increasing market demand for digital solutions. Software development is carried out through the Company's subsidiary, KKD, which has been operating since 2019 and has successfully created and developed various applications in synergy with the Company's partners.
