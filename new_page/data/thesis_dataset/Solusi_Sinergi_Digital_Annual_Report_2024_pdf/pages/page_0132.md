|  Sekretaris Perusahaan dalam menjalankan peran sebagai penghubung antara Perseroan dan para pemangku kepentingan dinilai belum optimal. Namun demikian, Perseroan tetap memberikan dukungan agar Sekretaris Perusahaan dapat segera beradaptasi dan menjalankan fungsi-fungsi utamanya, termasuk dalam memastikan kelancaran penyampaian informasi penting serta pemenuhan prinsip-prinsip Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik (GCG) sesuai dengan ketentuan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku. | Corporate Secretary in carrying out his role as a liaison between the Company and stakeholders is considered not optimal. However, the Company continues to provide support so that the Corporate Secretary can immediately adapt and carry out its main functions, including in ensuring the smooth delivery of important information and the fulfilment of the principles of Good Corporate Governance (GCG) in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations.  |
|  UNIT AUDIT INTERNAL | INTERNAL AUDIT UNIT  |
|  Pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab Unit Audit Internal dalam melakukan pengawasan dan evaluasi atas penerapan sistem pengendalian internal, sistem manajemen risiko, serta prinsip-prinsip Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik (GCG) di seluruh unit kerja Perseroan masih dalam tahap awal. Perseroan terus mendukung proses adaptasi dan penguatan fungsi Unit Audit Internal agar dapat menjalankan perannya secara optimal ke depannya. | The implementation of the duties and responsibilities of the Internal Audit Unit in supervising and evaluating the implementation of internal control systems, risk management systems, and Good Corporate Governance (GCG) principles in all of the Company's work units is still in its early stages. The Company continues to support the process of adapting and strengthening the functions of the Internal Audit Unit in order to carry out its role optimally in the future.  |
