# TANGGUNG JAWAB SOSIAL BIDANG LINGKUNGAN HIDUP

Perseroan memandang penting untuk menerapkan inisiatif keberlanjutan lingkungan hidup yang selaras dengan tujuan dan maksud Perseroan dan sesuai dengan kebutuhan pemangku kepentingan internal dan eksternal.

Dalam Pelaksanaan Tanggung Jawab Sosial Bidang Lingkungan Hidup, Perseroan akan mewujudkan hal tersebut melalui penghematan energi, khususnya pada penggunaan listrik dan kebijaksanaan dalam menggunakan air. Lebih lanjut, Perseroan juga mengupayakan meminimalkan dampak negatif terhadap lingkungan dengan menargetkan peningkatan kontribusi dalam penghijauan dimana wilayah kerja Perseroan berada.

Adapun Perseroan juga secara konsisten bertahap dengan melakukan penggunaan material dan energi yang ramah lingkungan, guna mendukung upaya pelestarian lingkungan. Perseroan telah menerapkan inisiatif Go Green, 3R (Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle), dan dalam melakukan tugas administrasi dengan mereduksi penggunaan kertas (paperless).

# SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY IN THE ENVIRONMENTAL SECTOR

The Company considers it important to implement environmental sustainability initiatives that are aligned with the Company's goals and objectives and in accordance with the needs of internal and external stakeholders.

In the implementation of Social Responsibility for the Environment, the Company will realize this through energy savings, especially in the use of electricity and wisdom in using water. Furthermore, the Company also seeks to minimize negative impacts on the environment by targeting an increased contribution to reforestation where the Company's working areas are located.

Meanwhile, the Company also consistently phases in the use of environmentally friendly materials and energy, in order to support environmental conservation efforts. The Company has implemented the Go Green initiative, 3R (Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle), and in performing administrative tasks by reducing the use of paper (paperless).

# POJK F.1

# KOMITMEN MEMBANGUN BUDAYA KEBERLANJUTAN

Perseroan menyadari akan Perubahan iklim yang terjadi dan dalam jangka dapat berdampak bagi Operasional bisnis usaha Perusahaan. Tantangan yang berkaitan dengan iklim dapat menggangu operasional sektor swasta mulai dari ketersediaan air, hingga gangguan jaringan distribusi. Perseroan juga belajar dari dampak akibat pandemi COVID-19 telah mengekspos bagaimana bisnis dan negara sangat rentan terhadap bencana skala global. Terlebih di Indonesia, tantangan terkait iklim umumnya berbentuk dalam berbagai bencana hidrometeorologi seperti banjir, longsor, kenaikan muka air, atau kekeringan.

# COMMITMENT TO BUILDING A CULTURE OF SUSTAINABILITY

The Company is well aware of the climate change that is occurring and can impact the Company's business operations in the long run. Climate-related challenges can disrupt private sector operations ranging from water availability, to distribution network disruptions. The Company has also learned from the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic which has exposed how businesses and countries are highly vulnerable to global-scale disasters. Especially in Indonesia, climate-related challenges generally take the form of various hydrometeorological disasters such as floods, landslides, water table rise, or drought.
