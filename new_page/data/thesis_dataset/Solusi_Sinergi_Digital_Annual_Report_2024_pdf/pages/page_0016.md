## SURGE Resmikan Kerja Sama Strategis dengan NTT e-Asia untuk Perluas Infrastruktur Internet

Pada Juli 2024, PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk (SURGE) resmi menjalin kerja sama dengan NTT e-Asia Corporation, anak usaha dari NTT Group Jepang, melalui penandatangan kontrak layanan konsultasi Fiber To The Home (FTTH). Kolaborasi ini menjadi langkah strategis untuk mempercepat ekspansi jaringan internet cepat dan terjangkau bagi 25 juta rumah tangga di Pulau Jawa, serta memperkuat ekosistem digital SURGE yang mencakup backbone serat optik, Edge Data Center, dan layanan bandwidth berkapasitas besar.

## SURGE Establishes Strategic Partnership with NTT e-Asia to Expand Internet Infrastructure

In July 2024, PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk (SURGE) officially entered into a strategic partnership with NTT e-Asia Corporation, a subsidiary of Japan-based NTT Group, through the signing of a consultancy service agreement for Fiber To The Home (FTTH) development. This collaboration marks a key milestone in accelerating the expansion of fast and affordable internet access for 25 million households across Java Island and strengthens SURGE's digital ecosystem, which includes fiber optic backbone, Edge Data Centers, and high-capacity bandwidth services.
