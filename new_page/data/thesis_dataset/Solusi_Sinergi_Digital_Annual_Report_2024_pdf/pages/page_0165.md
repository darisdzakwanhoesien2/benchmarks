In the economic aspect, in practice, economic performance is the priority of the Company's business activities which are affected by economic, social and environmental risks. We prioritize the value of sustainability in every action and business decision. We believe that sustainability is not only a moral duty, but also the basis for innovative opportunities and long-term relationships with all stakeholders.

As a form of commitment and compliance in implementing Corporate Social Responsibility mandated in the Limited Liability Company Law and other applicable laws and regulations, the Company always actively participates in the implementation of Corporate Social Responsibility through various activities carried out by the Company. The Company is committed to always carry out Corporate Social Responsibility activities obligatorily, because the Company realizes that the sustainability of the Company's activities and business activities cannot be separated from the support of various parties and is a reciprocal relationship with various levels of society to achieve mutual benefits that will continue to have an impact on the wider community and for future generations. The Company strives to run its business responsibly which includes the implementation of corporate social responsibility. Not only does it affect the Company's brand, but the Company believes that this social responsibility program can indirectly contribute to the growth and development of Indonesia.
