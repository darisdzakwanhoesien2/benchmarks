SURGE Resmikan Kolaborasi Strategis dengan ICONVEST untuk Internet Terjangkau

Pada September 2024, PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk (SURGE) menjalin kerja sama strategis dengan PT Indonesia Connectivity Investasi (ICONVEST), anak usaha dari PLN Icon Plus, dalam rangka pengembangan layanan internet terjangkau bagi masyarakat. Kolaborasi ini ditandai dengan penandatanganan Nota Kesepahaman yang mencakup pengembangan portofolio bisnis internet rumah, berbagi kompetensi, dan kapasitas infrastruktur. Melalui sinergi jaringan ICONVEST yang telah mencakup 18amper 3 juta homepass dan backbone SURGE sepanjang 7.000 km, diharapkan kerja sama ini dapat memperkuat kinerja segmen telekomunikasi dan memperluas jangkauan konektivitas nasional secara efisien dan inklusif.

SURGE Establishes Strategic Collaboration with ICONVEST to Expand Affordable Internet Access

In September 2024, PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk (SURGE) entered into a strategic partnership with PT Indonesia Connectivity Investasi (ICONVEST), a subsidiary of PLN Icon Plus, to support the development of affordable internet services for the public. Marked by a Memorandum of Understanding, the collaboration includes joint efforts in developing internet business portfolios, sharing competencies, and optimizing infrastructure capacity. With ICONVEST's network reaching nearly 3 million homepasses and SURGE's 7,000 km fiber-optic backbone, the partnership aims to strengthen SURGE's telecommunications performance and expand nationwide internet coverage efficiently and inclusively.
