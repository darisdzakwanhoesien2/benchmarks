Pada 31 Desember 2023, jumlah Waran Seri I yang telah dilaksanakan menjadi saham adalah sebanyak 104.033.830 saham, namun baru diaktakan pada tanggal 2 Februari 2024

Berdasarkan RUPS Perusahaan diaktakan Notaris Rini Yulianti, S.H., No. 3 tanggal 2 Februari 2024, Perusahaan memeroleh peningkatan modal disetor melalui konversi waran menjadi saham sebanyak 104.033.830 saham sehingga modal yang ditempatkan dan disetor menjadi sebesar Rp 2.359.355.118 saham. Akta tersebut telah disetujui oleh Kementerian Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui Surat Keputusan No. AHU-AH.01.03-0032694 tanggal 5 Februari 2024.

Uang Muka Setoran Modal

Pada 31 Desember 2023, uang muka setoran modal merupakan penambahan modal saham dan tambahan modal disetor yang diperoleh atas transaksi Waran Seri I yang telah dilaksanakan sebanyak 104.033.830 saham.

Uang muka setoran modal pada tanggal 31 Desember 2023 terdiri dari modal saham dan tambahan modal disetor masing-masing sebesar Rp 10.403.383.100 dan Rp 61.379.948.490 yang belum diaktakan. Uang muka tersebut telah diaktakan Berdasarkan Akta Notaris Rini Yulianti, S.H, No. 3 tanggal 2 Februari 2024.

As of December 31, 2023, the number of Series I Warrants that have been exercised into shares is 104,033,830 shares, but it was only notarized on February 2, 2024.

Based on the Company's General Meeting of Shareholders notarized by Notary Rini Yulianti, S.H., No. 3 dated February 2, 2024, the Company obtained increase paid-in capital through warrant conversion of 104,033,830 shares so that the issued and paid-up capital becomes Rp 2,359,355,118 shares. The deed has been approved by the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decision Letter No. date AHU-AH.01.03-0032694 dated February 5, 2024.

Advance Stock Subscription

As of December 31, 2023, the advance for stock subscription represents additional share capital and additional paid-in capital obtained from Series I Warrants that have been exercised are 104,033,830 shares.

Advances for capital deposits as of December 31, 2023 consist of share capital and additional paid-in capital amounted to Rp 10,403,383,100 and Rp 61,379,948,490, respectively which has not been notarized. The advance has been notarized based on Deed of Notary Rini Yulianti, S.H, No. 3 dated February 2, 2024.
