# KEGIATAN USAHA

## BUSINESS ACTIVITIES

## Kegiatan Usaha Telekomunikasi:

Infrastruktur jaringan Serat Optik menjadi pendorong utama kinerja Surge, sejak 2019 telah berkerjasama dengan PT. KAI (Persero) melalui Perusahan anak, yaitu "Weave", menggelar, mengoperasikan dan mengelola jaringan serat optik yang dipasang sepanjang jalur kereta di pulau Jawa, berkat kemampuan kami untuk meningkatkan pengembangan produk dan layanan di tengah percepatan penerapan transformasi digital yang terus berkelanjutan, Surge berhasil mencatat total serat optik yang dibangun dan dioperasikan sebanyak 6.927 Km jaringan serat optik yang mengkombinasikan jalur kereta api, jalan tol, dan jalan raya pada akhir tahun 2023.

Pada tahun 2024 PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk Melalui Entitas Perseroan PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem berhasil merampungkan pembangunan 200 Ribu Homepass untuk Viberlink (ISP- Internet Service Provider). Serta telah melakukan penandatangan kerjasama dengan 50 (ISP-Internet Service Provider) Kerjasama ini mencakup penyediaan layanan telekomunikasi yang meliputi Backbone, penyewaan Core, kapasitas jaringan, dan Rak kolokasi yang disediakan oleh Weave di lokasi-lokasi strategis, terutama mendukung infrastruktur akses internet ke masyarakat. Hingga kini, Perusahaan telah mengoperasikan infrastruktur backbone serat optik sepanjang 6.927 km, di sepanjang jalur kereta dan jalur lain di Pulau Jawa. Selain itu, perusahaan telah berinvestasi dalam penyediaan bandwidth berkapasitas besar hingga 64.000 Gbps. Untuk mendukung pengembangan Cloud Computing, perusahaan juga telah membangun dan mengoperasikan 58 Edge Data Center (EDC) yang tersebar di berbagai kota di Pulau Jawa, dengan potensi peningkatan hingga 592 lokasi. Saat ini, Edge Data Center perusahaan digunakan oleh ISP untuk colocation dan solusi Content Delivery, Network (CDN) bagi penyedia layanan Cloud.

## Telecommunications Business Activities:

Fiber optic network infrastructure has become the main driver of Surge's performance. Since 2019, through its subsidiary "Weave," Surge has collaborated with PT Kereta Api Indonesia (Persero) ("KAI") to deploy, operate, and manage fiber optic networks installed along railway tracks across Java Island.

Thanks to our ability to enhance product and service development amidst the ongoing acceleration of digital transformation, Surge successfully recorded a total of 6,927 kilometers of fiber optic networks deployed and operated by the end of 2023, combining railway tracks, toll roads, and highways.

In 2024, PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk, through its subsidiary PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem, successfully completed the development of 200,000 homepasses for Viberlink (ISP - Internet Service Provider). The Company also signed cooperation agreements with 50 Internet Service Providers (ISPs). This collaboration encompasses the provision of telecommunications services, including backbone connectivity, core leasing, network capacity, and colocation racks supplied by Weave at strategic locations, primarily to support internet access infrastructure for the public. To date, the Company has operated a fiber optic backbone infrastructure spanning 6,927 kilometers along railway lines and other routes across Java Island. Additionally, the Company has invested in providing high-capacity bandwidth of up to 64,000 Gbps. To further support the growth of cloud computing, the Company has developed and operates 58 Edge Data Centers (EDCs) located across various cities in Java, with the potential to expand to 592 sites. Currently, the Company's Edge Data Centers are utilized by ISPs for colocation services and Content Delivery Network (CDN) solutions for cloud service providers.
