## Layanan Kolokasi Data (Colocation)

Perseroan menyediakan layanan colocation melalui Edge Data Center berkapasitas 6–10 rak (masing-masing 42U), dengan fasilitas penyewaan ruang, daya, sistem pendingin, dan konektivitas. Hingga 2023, telah beroperasi 3 lokasi dan 55 Edge Data Center lainnya selesai dibangun. Layanan ini ditujukan bagi ISP dan entitas bisnis yang memerlukan solusi hosting server tanpa membangun data center sendiri, dengan keunggulan keamanan tinggi, efisiensi biaya, serta akses jaringan berkecepatan tinggi. Pemesanan minimum adalah 1U, dengan opsi 1 rack (42U) dan promo harga tertentu.

## Layanan Content Delivery Network (CDN)

CDN adalah sistem server yang tersebar di berbagai lokasi dan dirancang untuk mengirimkan konten digital secara cepat dan andal kepada pengguna akhir. Layanan ini umum digunakan oleh penyedia cloud, penyedia konten, perusahaan e-commerce, dan lainnya untuk distribusi halaman web, gambar, video, dan audio. Dengan merutekkan permintaan konten ke server terdekat, CDN meningkatkan kecepatan, ketersediaan, dan pengalaman pengguna secara keseluruhan.

## Colocation Data Service (Colocation)

The Company provides colocation services through Edge Data Centers with a capacity of 6–10 racks (42U each), offering space, power, cooling, and connectivity. As of 2023, 3 sites were operational and 55 additional Edge Data Centers had been completed. This service targets ISPs and businesses needing server hosting without investing in their own facilities, offering high security, cost efficiency, and access to high-speed rail-based network infrastructure. The minimum order is 1U, with full rack (42U) options and promotional pricing available.

## Content Delivery Network (CDN) Service

A CDN is a distributed server system designed to deliver digital content quickly and reliably to end users. Commonly used by cloud providers, content providers, e-commerce companies, and others, it supports the distribution of web pages, images, videos, and audio. By routing content requests to the nearest server, CDN improves delivery speed, availability, and overall user experience.

## Tower Fiberization Service

This service involves installing fiber optic cables from the Company's railway network to telecommunications towers within a 1–5 km radius along Java Island to enhance high-speed internet connectivity. Connected towers are linked to data centers via the Company's fiber backbone. The service aims to accelerate tower fiberization, enable telecom operators to expand their network reach, reduce costs, and deliver more reliable connectivity in both urban and rural areas.
