# RIWAYAT PERUSAHAAN

## BRIEF HISTORY OF THE COMPANY

Perseroan didirikan berdasarkan hukum Negara Republik Indonesia dan berkedudukan di Jakarta Selatan. Perseroan pertama kali didirikan dengan "PT Lucaffe Indonesia" sebagaimana termaktub dalam Akta Pendirian Perseroan Terbatas PT Lucaffe Indonesia No. 21 tanggal 6 September 2012 yang dibuat dihadapan Humberg Lie, S.H., S.E., M.Kn., Notaris di Jakarta Utara. Kegiatan usaha Perseroan berfokus pada infrastruktur jaringan serat optic, produk dan layanan digital, Teknologi dan media periklanan.

Surge pertama kali menawarkan sahamnya kepada publik dan tercatat di Bursa Efek Indonesia sejak 30 Desember 2020 dengan kode saham "WIFI"

Melihat kebutuhan akan konektivitas yang terus meningkat seiring perkembangan ekosistem digital di Indonesia. Surge melalui anak usaha PT. Integrasi Ekosistem Jaringan ("Weave") bekerjasama dengan PT KAI (Persero) melalui proyek pengembangan jaringan serat optik sepanjang rel kereta di Pulau Jawa. Pengembangan infrastruktur ini diharapkan dapat memenuhi kebutuhan konektivitas bagi seluruh penduduk pulau Jawa dan kedepannya Perseroan juga akan melebarkan jangkauannya ke pulau Sumatera. Pada tahun 2019, KAI dan Weave menandatangani kontrak jangka panjang untuk pemasangan, pengoperasian dan pengelolaan serat optic sepanjang rel kereta milik KAI.

Perusahaan memperkuat komitmen untuk menumbuhkan bisnis yang berkelanjutan yang mana saat ini telah berkembang pada pembangunan jaringan serat optik pada jalan tol dan jalan raya sehingga total jaringan serat optik yang telah digelar Surge saat ini mencapai 6.927 km, Surge dapat membantu dan berkontribusi untuk Indonesia terutama dalam pengembangan ekosistem digital, peningkatan kualitas pendidikan dan layanan public, serta pengembangan ekonomi digital di Indonesia.

The Company was established under the laws of the Republic of Indonesia and is domiciled in South Jakarta. The Company was initially founded under the name "PT Lucaffe Indonesia," as stated in the Deed of Establishment of Limited Liability Company PT Lucaffe Indonesia No. 21 dated September 6, 2012, drawn up before Humberg Lie, S.H., S.E., M.Kn., a Notary in North Jakarta. The Company's business activities focus on fiber optic network infrastructure, digital products and services, technology, and advertising media.

Surge first offered its shares to the public and was listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX) on December 30, 2020, under the stock code "WIFI."

Recognizing the growing demand for connectivity in line with the expansion of Indonesia's digital ecosystem, Surge, through its subsidiary PT Integrasi Ekosistem Jaringan ("Weave"), entered into a collaboration with PT Kereta Api Indonesia (Persero) ("KAI") for a fiber optic network development project along railway tracks across Java Island. This infrastructure development is expected to meet the increasing connectivity needs of the population across Java and, in the future, expand coverage to Sumatra Island. In 2019, KAI and Weave signed a long-term contract for the installation, operation, and management of fiber optic networks along KAI's railway tracks.

The Company continues to strengthen its commitment to fostering sustainable business growth, which has expanded to the development of fiber optic networks along toll roads and highways. As a result, the total fiber optic network deployed by Surge has now reached 6,927 kilometers. Through this infrastructure, Surge contributes significantly to Indonesia by supporting the development of the digital ecosystem, enhancing the quality of education and public services, and promoting the growth of the digital economy across the country.
