Dalam beberapa tahun terakhir ---khususnya pasca pandemi Covid-19--- industri digital berbasis internet mengalami pertumbuhan dan ekspansi secara cepat hingga ke perdesaan. Bahkan, saat ini sebagian besar aktivitas masyarakat untuk memenuhi kebutuhan hidup pun lebih mengandalkan ketersediaan media maupun aplikasi digital berbasis internet. Lonjakan pemanfaatan teknologi berbasis internet ini pada akhirnya mendorong peningkatan permintaan terhadap akses internet maupun produk dan layanan digital. Menariknya lagi, belakangan ini semakin marak perusahaan yang membutuhkan layanan jasa periklanan berbentuk Digital Out-of-home Advertising (DOOH) maupun digital advertising.

In recent years - especially after the Covid-19 pandemic - the internet-based digital industry has experienced rapid growth and expansion to rural areas. In fact, currently most of the community's activities to fulfill their needs rely more on the availability of internet-based digital media and applications. This surge in the use of internet-based technology has ultimately driven an increase in demand for internet access and digital products and services. Interestingly, lately there are more and more companies that need advertising services in the form of Digital Out-of-home Advertising (DOOH) and digital advertising.
