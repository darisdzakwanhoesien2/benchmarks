**PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem ("IJE")**

Based on the Deed of Decision Statement / Circular Resolution of Limited Liability Company Shareholders No. 20 dated February 09, 2022 from Notary Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., IJE increased the issued capital / paid-up capital from Rp 133,000,000,000 to Rp 167,026,000,000, so the Company's share ownership in IJE becomes 150,356 shares or equivalent to 90.00% ownership.

Based on the Deed of Decision Statement / Circular Resolution of Limited Company Shareholders No. 94 dated August 22, 2022 from Notary Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., there was a sale of some of the IJE shares owned by PT Lintas Maju Maxima to the Company amounted to 15,030 shares, bringing the Company's share ownership in IJE to 165,356 shares or equivalent to 99.00% ownership.

Based on the Deed of Decision Statement / Circular Resolution of Limited Company Shareholders No. 22 dated April 10, 2023 from Notary Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., IJE increased the issued capital / paid-up capital from Rp 167,026,000,000 to Rp 229,026,000,000, bringing the Company's share ownership in IJE to 227,356 shares or equivalent to 99.27% ownership.

Based on the Deed of Decision Statement / Circular Resolution of Limited Company Shareholders No. 69 dated March 20,2024 from Notary Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., IJE increased the issued capital / paid-up capital from Rp 229,026,000,000 to Rp 262,026,000,000, bringing the Company's share ownership in IJE to 260,356 shares or equivalent to 99.27% ownership.

Based on the Deed of Decision Statement / Circular Resolution of Limited Company Shareholders No. 71 dated March 20,2024 from Notary Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., IJE increased the issued capital / paid-up capital from Rp 262,026,000,000 to Rp 562,026,000,000, bringing the Company's share ownership in IJE to 560,356 shares or equivalent to 99.70% ownership.
