## Ekonomi digital di Indonesia

Sementara itu pada kegiatan usaha jaringan serat optik, Surge melalui anak usahanya, PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem (IJE) alias Weave melakukan kerjasama dengan PT Kereta Api Indonesia (Persero) terkait penyediaan jaringan kabel serat optik di sepanjang rel kereta. Saat ini Perseroan sudah mengoperasikan infrastruktur backbone serat optik sepanjang 6,927 kilometer di sepanjang jalur kereta dan jalur lain di Pulau Jawa. Surge juga telah berinvestasi dalam penyediaan bandwidth berkapasitas besar hingga 64.000 Gbps, guna mendukung pengembangan cloud computing, serta membangun dan mengoperasikan 58 Edge Data Center (EDC) di Pulau Jawa. EDC milik Perseroan digunakan oleh penyedia layanan internet (ISP) untuk colocation dan solusi Content Delivery Network (CDN) bagi penyedia layanan cloud. Perseroan juga telah menjalin kemitraan investasi strategis dengan Nippon Telegraphan Telephone East Corporation (NTT E-ASIA) yang merupakan anak usaha inti dari NTT Group asal Jepang.

Meanwhile, in the fiber optic network business activity, Surge through its subsidiary, PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem (IJE), aka Weave, collaborated with PT Kereta Api Indonesia (Persero) regarding the provision of fiber optic cable networks along the train tracks. Currently, the Company has operated 6,927 kilometers of fiber optic backbone infrastructure along the railways and other routes in Java. Surge has also invested in the provision of large capacity bandwidth of up to 64,000 Gbps, to support the development of cloud computing, as well as building and operating 58 Edge Data Centers (EDC) on Java Island. The Company's EDCs are used by internet service providers (ISPs) for colocation and Content Delivery Network (CDN) solutions for cloud service providers. The Company has also established a strategic investment partnership with Nippon Telegraphan Telephone East Corporation (NTT E-ASIA), a core subsidiary of Japan's NTT Group.
