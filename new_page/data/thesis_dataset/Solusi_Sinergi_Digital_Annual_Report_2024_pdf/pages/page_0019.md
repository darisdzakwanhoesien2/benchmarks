SURGE dan NTT e-Asia Jalin Kolaborasi Strategis Pengembangan Infrastruktur ICT

Pada November 2024, PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk (SURGE) resmi menandatangani Nota Kesepahaman (MoU) dengan NTT e-Asia Corporation (NTTeA), anak perusahaan dari NTT Group Jepang, untuk memperkuat pengembangan infrastruktur ICT di Indonesia. Kolaborasi ini mencakup pengembangan fiber optic backbone (terestrial dan bawah laut), jaringan akses seperti FTTH dan Fixed Wireless Access, serta pendirian pusat pelatihan telekomunikasi. Inisiatif ini bertujuan untuk memperluas konektivitas digital secara merata dan mempercepat transformasi digital nasional melalui transfer teknologi, peningkatan kapabilitas SDM, dan efisiensi operasional bersama.

SURGE and NTT e-Asia Establish Strategic Collaboration for ICT Infrastructure Development

In November 2024, PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk (SURGE) signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with NTT e-Asia Corporation (NTTeA), a subsidiary of Japan's NTT Group, to strengthen ICT infrastructure development in Indonesia. The partnership covers the expansion of terrestrial and sub-marine fiber optic backbones, access networks such as FTTH and Fixed Wireless Access, and the establishment of a telecommunications training center. This collaboration aims to accelerate nationwide digital transformation through technology transfer, workforce development, and enhanced operational efficiency.
