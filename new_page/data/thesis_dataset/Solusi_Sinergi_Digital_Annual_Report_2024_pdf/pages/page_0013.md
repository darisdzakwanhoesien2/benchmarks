# INFORMASI MENGENAI OBLIGASI, SUKUK, ATAU OBLIGASI KONVERSI
INFORMATION ON BONDS, SUKUK, OR CONVERTIBLE BONDS

Sampai dengan akhir tahun 2024, Perseroan tidak menerbitkan efek lainnya, baik dalam bentuk obligasi, sukuk, maupun obligasi konversi. Namun melalui anak usahanya, PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem (IJE), menerbitkan obligasi perdana senilai Rp600 miliar. Dana dari obligasi ini digunakan untuk belanja modal dan modal kerja, terutama untuk memperluas infrastruktur konektivitas di Pulau Jawa.

As of the end of 2024, the Company did not issue any other securities, whether in the form of bonds, sukuk, or convertible bonds. However, through its subsidiary, PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem (IJE), the Company issued its inaugural bond amounting to IDR 600 billion. The proceeds from this bond issuance were utilized for capital expenditure and working capital, primarily to support the expansion of connectivity infrastructure across Java Island

## AKSI KORPORASI

Sampai dengan akhir tahun 2024, Perseroan terdapat aksi korporasi yaitu pembagian dividen tunai.

Pada 5 Juli 2024, Perseroan membagikan dividen tunai sebesar Rp2.501.000.000, atau Rp1,06 per saham. Pemegang saham yang tercatat dalam Daftar Pemegang Saham (DPS) pada 20 Juni 2024 berhak menerima dividen ini. Tanggal cum dan ex dividen di pasar reguler dan negosiasi adalah 14 dan 19 Juni 2024, sedangkan di pasar tunai adalah 20 dan 21 Juni 2024.

## CORPORATE ACTION

As of the end of 2024, the Company carried out a corporate action in the form of a cash dividend distribution.

On July 5, 2024, the Company distributed cash dividends amounting to IDR 2,501,000,000, or IDR 1.06 per share. Shareholders registered in the Shareholders Register (DPS) as of June 20, 2024, were entitled to receive this dividend. The cum and ex-dividend dates on the regular and negotiated markets were June 14 and June 19, 2024, respectively, while for the cash market, they were June 20 and June 21, 2024.

## SUSPENSION / DELISTING

Suspension dengan akhir tahun 2024, Saham perseroan tidak terdapat informasi mengenai penghentian sementara perdagangan saham (suspension) dan/atau pembatalan pencatatan saham (delisting)

## SUSPENSION / DELISTING

Until the end of 2024, the Company's shares have no information regarding the suspension of stock trading (suspension) and/or delisting
