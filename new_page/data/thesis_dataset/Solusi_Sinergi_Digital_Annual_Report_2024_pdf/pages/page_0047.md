## Layanan Penyewaan Core Serat Optik (Lease Core)

IJEE, melalui kerja sama dengan PT Kereta Api Indonesia (KAI) selama 10 tahun, menanam kabel serat optik berkapasitas 144 core di sepanjang jalur kereta Pulau Jawa. Layanan ini menyediakan konektivitas point-to-point berkecepatan tinggi dan latensi rendah yang ditujukan bagi perusahaan telekomunikasi, data center, dan penyedia layanan cloud. Pelanggan dapat menyewa minimal 1 pair (2 core) dengan durasi kontrak 5–10 tahun. Sejak beroperasi pada 2022, layanan ini telah dimanfaatkan oleh pelanggan strategis dan didukung SLA hingga 99,99%, berkat tingkat keamanan tinggi dari infrastruktur serat optik yang ditanam di jalur rel kereta.

## Jasa Penjualan Bandwidth (Leased Line)

IJEE menyediakan layanan leased line berupa koneksi jaringan dedicated point-to-point berkecepatan tinggi dan aman untuk penyelenggara layanan internet (ISP). Bandwidth disediakan melalui integrasi serat optik dengan perangkat DWDM dari Huawei dan Packetlight, serta didukung teknologi ASON untuk memastikan konektivitas tetap andal dan seamless dengan SLA tinggi.

## Fiber Optic Core Leasing Service (Lease Core)

IJEE's, through a 10-year collaboration with PT Kereta Api Indonesia (KAI), has deployed 144-core fiber optic cables along the railway corridor in Java. This service provides high-speed, low-latency point-to-point connectivity tailored for telecommunications companies, data centers, and cloud service providers. Customers can lease a minimum of 1 pair (2 cores) with contract durations ranging from 5 to 10 years. Since its launch in 2022, the service has been utilized by strategic clients and is supported by a Service Level Agreement (SLA) of up to 99.99%, enabled by the high security and low fiber breakage probability offered by the rail-based fiber infrastructure.

## Bandwidth Leasing Service (Leased Line/Managed Capacity)

IJEE's provides high-speed, secure, point-to-point dedicated network connectivity for Internet Service Providers (ISPs). Bandwidth is delivered through fiber optic infrastructure integrated with DWDM devices from Huawei and Packetlight, and enhanced with ASON technology to ensure seamless failover and high service reliability (SLA).
