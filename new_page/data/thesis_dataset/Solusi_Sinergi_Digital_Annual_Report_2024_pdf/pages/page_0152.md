## Statement of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors on the Adequacy of Risk Management System

The Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors consider that the implementation of the Company's risk management system throughout 2024 has been running well and effectively. This condition is demonstrated by the ability to prevent various risks faced by the Company through appropriate procedures and mitigation measures, without causing significant and other material impacts.
