Sustainability Report

# PELATIHAN DAN PENGEMBANGAN KARYAWAN

Pada tahun 2024, perusahaan melibatkan seluruh karyawan dalam berbagai kegiatan pelatihan internal dan eksternal serta pengembangan kompetensi dengan berbagai tema dan lingkup. Selama program orientasi karyawan, perusahaan juga melakukan pelatihan dan pengembangan teknis secara berkala sesuai dengan kebutuhan karyawan.

# PENGEMBANGAN KARIER DAN SUKSESI

Perseroan juga memberikan kesempatan bagi karyawan Perseroan untuk mengembangkan karirnya di Perseroan. Hal ini sejalan dengan komitmen Perseroan dalam mempertahankan talenta terbaik di dalam Perseroan. Adapun promosi karir ke jenjang yang lebih tinggi diberikan dengan mempertimbangkan hasil kinerja atau KPI, Kompetensi Teknis, keahlian serta perilaku hingga integritas karyawan.

# EMPLOYEE TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT

In 2024, the company involved all employees in various internal and external training and competency development activities with various themes and scopes. During the employee orientation program, the company also conducts regular technical training and development according to employee needs.

# CAREER DEVELOPMENT AND SUCCESSION

The Company also provides opportunities for its employees to develop their careers in the Company. This is in line with the Company's commitment to retain the best talent within the Company. Career promotions to higher levels are given by considering performance results or KPIs, Technical Competence, expertise and behavior to employee integrity.

# FABI REMUNERATION

In order to provide fair remuneration for the Company's employees. The Company also provides facilities to its employees including health care and treatment benefits, work accident benefits, non-work accident death benefits, weekly and daily breaks, maternity leave, work safety and work equipment, provision of Service Vehicle facilities for workers with certain positions.

# FASILITAS PENUNJANG KESEJAHTERAAN KARYAWAN

Selain memberikan fasilitas utama dan kompensasi, perusahaan dan entitas anak menyediakan berbagai fasilitas tambahan untuk meningkatkan kualitas hidup karyawan. Ini termasuk mushola, pantry, transportasi, dan fasilitas sosial lainnya untuk berkumpul dan bersosialisasi.

# EMPLOYEE WELFARE SUPPORT FACILITIES

In addition to providing primary facilities and compensation, the company and its subsidiaries provide various additional facilities to improve the quality of life of employees. These include prayer rooms, pantries, transportation, and other social facilities for gathering and socializing.
