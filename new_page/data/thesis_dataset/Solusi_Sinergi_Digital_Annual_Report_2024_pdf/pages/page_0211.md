# c. Initial Public Offering
On December 21, 2020, the Company received an Effective Statement from Otoritas Jasa Keuangan ("OJK") by Letter No. S-294/D.04/2020 to conduct an initial public offering of 156,558,200 shares with a nominal value of Rp 100 per share, with an offering price of Rp 530 per share, along with a debt conversion of 283,018,800 shares which were converted at an offering price of Rp 530 per share. On December 21, 2020, all these shares were listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange.
