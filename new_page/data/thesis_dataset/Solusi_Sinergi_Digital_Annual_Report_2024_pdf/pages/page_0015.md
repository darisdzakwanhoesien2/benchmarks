# Kemitraan Strategis SURGE dan Arsari Group dalam Percepatan Penyediaan Internet Terjangkau

Pada 24 Juni 2024, PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk (SURGE) melalui anak usahanya PT Jaringan Infra Andalan, menjalin kerja sama strategis dengan PT Arsari Sentra Data, bagian dari Arsari Group, yang ditandai dengan penandatangan Memorandum of Understanding (MoU). Arsari Group menyatakan minat untuk berinvestasi guna mempercepat pengembangan infrastruktur konektivitas yang menyasar 25 juta rumah tangga di Pulau Jawa. Kolaborasi ini diharapkan mampu mempercepat penetrasi layanan internet tetap (fixed broadband) yang handal dan terjangkau, sekaligus mendorong transformasi digital nasional, peningkatan kualitas pendidikan, akses kesehatan, dan pertumbuhan ekonomi digital masyarakat Indonesia.

# Strategic Partnership Between SURGE and Arsari Group to Accelerate Affordable Internet Access

On June 24, 2024, PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk (SURGE), through its subsidiary PT Jaringan Infra Andalan, entered into a strategic collaboration with PT Arsari Sentra Data, a subsidiary of Arsari Group, marked by the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU). Arsari Group expressed its interest to invest in supporting the acceleration of connectivity infrastructure development targeting 25 million households across Java Island. This collaboration is expected to significantly boost the penetration of reliable and affordable fixed broadband internet services, contributing to Indonesia's national digital transformation, and supporting improvements in education quality, healthcare access, and the growth of the digital economy.
