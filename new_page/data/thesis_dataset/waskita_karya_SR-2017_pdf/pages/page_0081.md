# SERTIFIKAT EMAS UNTUK GEDUNG WASKITA

Papan putih itu terpampang jelas di kiri pintu masuk Gedung Waskita, di Jalan Biru Laut, Jatinegara, Jakarta Timur. Isinya ditulis dengan cat hitam sehingga sangat kontras dengan warna dasar papan: GREENSHIP EXISTING BUILDING, Rating Certification. GOLD, 003/RP/EB/XI-2015. Inilah papan pengumuman yang menunjukkan bahwa Gedung Waskita Karya telah memenuhi syarat sebagai Green Building yang dikeluarkan oleh Green Building Council Indonesia (GBC).

Bukan urusan mudah bagi Waskita untuk memperoleh sertifikat emas dari GBC Indonesia. Sebab, untuk mendapatkannya, Waskita harus memenuhi berbagai syarat sebagai berikut:

Persyaratan Penilaian Sertifikasi Existing Green Building, seperti dimuat dalam buku "Panduan Pelaksanaan Pekerjaan – Bagian V- GREEN CONSTRUCTION Proyek Gedung dan Infrastruktur, +Plus Implementasi Green Building pada Gedung Waskita tahun 2016" adalah sebagai berikut:

1. Syarat Kelayakan (Eligibility)
Sebelum melalui proses sertifikasi, untuk proyek gedung Existing Green Building harus memenuhi penilaian kekelayakan (eligibility) yang ditetapkan oleh GBC Indonesia. Ada 4 persyaratan kelayakan dalam proses untuk memperoleh sertifikasi:
a. Upaya Pengelolaan Lingkungan (UKL) dan Upaya Pemantauan Lingkungan (UPL)
b. Persetujuan penggunaan data bangunan untuk database dan studi kasus Green Building.
c. Sertifikat Laik Fungsi (SLF)/Kelayakan Menggunakan Bangunan (KMB)
d. Izin Membangun Bangunan (IMB)

2. Kriteria Penilaian (Greenship EB Rating Tools V 1,0)
a. Kategori Penilaian
Greenship terbagi atas enam kategori penilaian, yang terdiri dari:
- Tepat Guna Lahan (Appropriate Site Development-ASD)
- Efisiensi dan Konservasi Energi (Energy Efficiency and Conservation-EEC)
- Konservasi Air (Water Conservation-WAC)
- Sumber dan Siklus Material (Material Resources and Cycle-MRC).
- Kesehatan dan Kenyamanan dalam Ruang (Indoor Health and Comfort -IHC)
- Manajemen Lingkungan Bangunan (Building Environment Management-BEM)

# GOLDEN CERTIFICATE FOR WASKITA BUILDING

A white board is clearly displayed on the left side of the Waskita Building, at Jalan Biru Laut, Jatinegara, East Jakarta. On it are written in black paint as a contrast to the basic color of the board: GREENSHIP EXISTING BUILDING, Rating Certification: GOLD, 003/RP/EB/XI-2015. This notice board shows that the Waskita Karya Building has qualified as a Green Building, as recognized by the Green Building Council Indonesia (GBC).

It was not easy for Waskita to gain a gold certificate from GBC Indonesia. Waskita had to meet the following requirements:

The Certification Requirements for Existing Green Building Certification, as stated in the book 'Work Implementation Guide - Part V- GREEN CONSTRUCTION Building and Infrastructure Project, + Plus Implementation of Green Building at Waskita Building 2016' was as follows:

1. Eligibility Requirements
Prior to going through the certification process, the Existing Green Building building project must meet eligibility assessments set by GBC Indonesia. There are 4 eligibility requirements in the process of obtaining certification:
a. Environmental Management Efforts (UKL) and Environmental Monitoring Efforts (UPL)
b. Consequently, the use of building data for the Green Building database and case study.
c. feasibility Function Certificate (SLF) / Building Feasibility (KMB)
d. Building Construction Permit (IMB)

2. Assessment Criteria (Greenship EB Rating Tools V 1.0)
a. Assessment Category
Greenship is divided into six assessment categories, consisting of:
- Appropriate Site Development (ASD)
- Energy Efficiency and Conservation (EEC)
- Water Conservation (WAC)
- Material Resources and Cycle-MRC.
- Indoor Health and Comfort (IHC)
- Building Environment Management (BEM)
