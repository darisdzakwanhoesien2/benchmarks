# Remunerasi dan Kesejahteraan Pegawai

Sistem Remunerasi yang diadopsi Perseroan menggunakan prinsip pay for performance. Sistem ini memungkinkan Perseroan untuk memberikan remunerasi bagi pegawai dengan kompetitif dibandingkan dengan strandar hidup layak dan standart market price yang berlaku di industri konstruksi. Pay for Performance merupakan sistem remunerasi yang dikaitkan dengan hasil penilaian kinerja pegawai.

Waskita memberlakukan sistem remunerasi yang kompetitif dan memperhatikan tingkat upah di industri sejenis, peraturan ketenagakerjaan, peraturan Upah Minimum Provinsi (UMP), serta kemampuan finansialnya. Waskita memastikan bahwa besaran upah minimum yang diberikan ke semua Pegawainya sesuai peraturan yang berlaku. Kebijakan remunerasi Perusahaan di semua tingkat dan posisi berlaku sama untuk semua Pegawai, tanpa membedakan jenis kelamin. Waskita menerapkan skema remunerasi berdasarkan kompetensi orangnya (people), posisi jabatannya (position), dan kinerjanya (performance) serta disesuaikan dengan pasar tenaga kerja. (405-2)

Hasil penilaian kinerja berdasarkan KPI pada setiap akhir tahun akan mempengaruhi tingkat grade yang diterimanya pada tahun berikutnya. Sedangkan tingkat grade pegawai dengan berbagai macam faktor pengali yang lain akan mempengaruhi remunerasi yang akan diterimanya. Pegawai yang berkinerja baik akan menerima remunerasi yang lebih baik dibandingkan dengan yang berkinerja kurang baik.

Kualitas dan kepuasan yang diberikan kepada pelanggan juga menjadi poin penting yang harus dicapai. Perseroan menjalankan reward and punishment, guna memberikan apresiasi atas kinerja pegawai yang mampu meraih kepuasan pelanggan.

Dari sisi kesejahteraan, selain penghasilan bulanan berupa gaji dan tunjangan transportasi, Pegawai Waskita memperoleh tunjangan lain seperti Tunjangan Hari Raya (THR), tunjangan cuti, dan bantuan uang duka bila pegawai atau keluarganya mengalami musibah meninggal dunia. Bentuk kesejahteraan pegawai lain di Perseroan meliputi:

- Jamsostek (jaminan kematian, jaminan kecelakaan kerja, jaminan hari tua);
- Medical check up;
- Family gathering;
- Jasa produksi;
- Cuti;
- Perawatan kesehatan;
- Penggantian kaca mata; dan
- Bantuan Uang Makan

# Employee Remuneration and Welfare

The Company's adopted remuneration system uses the principle of pay for performance. This system allows the Company to provide remuneration for employees competitively compared to the standard of living and the prevailing market price standard in the construction industry. Pay for performance is a remuneration system associated with performance appraisal results of employees.

Waskita enforces a competitive remuneration system and takes into account wage rates in similar industries, labor regulations, Provincial Minimum Wage (UMP) regulations, as well as financial capabilities. Waskita ensures that the minimum wage amount given to all its employees is in accordance with the applicable regulations. The Company's remuneration policy at all levels and positions apply equally to all employees, regardless of gender. Waskita applies remuneration schemes based on the competence of the people, position, and performance and adjusted to the labor market. (405-2)

The performance appraisal results based on the Performance Indicator (KPI) at the end of each year will affect the grade level it receives in the following year. While the level of employees with various other multiplier factors will affect the remuneration that will be received. Better performers will receive better remuneration compared to poor performers.

Quality and satisfaction given to customers also become important points to be achieved. The Company runs a reward and punishment, in order to give an appreciation for the performance of employees who are able to achieve customer satisfaction.

In terms of welfare, in addition to monthly income in the form of salary and transportation allowances, Waskita employees receive other benefits such as holiday allowance (THR), leave allowances, and help money mourning if the employee or his family suffered death. Other employee benefits in the Company include:

- Jamsostek (death cover, accident insurance, old age pension);
- Medical check-up;
- Family gathering;
- Production service;
- leave;
- Health care;
- Replacement of glasses; and
- Food coupon.
