## Perubahan Signifikan pada Organisasi dan Rantai Pasokan (102-10)

## Prinsip Pencegahan (102-11)

Dalam menjalankan usaha, Waskita dihadapkan pada potensi risiko operasional, keuangan dan lingkungan yang besar. Perseroan senantiasa melakukan identifikasi dan evaluasi atas risiko yang dilakukan melalui masing-masing divisi. Direksi bersama-sama dengan Satuan Pengawasan Internal dan dan Dewan Komisaris yang diwakili oleh Komite Audit melakukan kajian dan merumuskan strategi pengelolaan dan mitigasi yang diperlukan. Pada 2017, evaluasi terhadap sistem manajemen risiko Perseroan telah berjalan efektif.

## Inisiatif Eksternal (102-12)

Waskita berkomitmen untuk menerapkan standar baku yang berlaku dalam bisnis, maupun untuk mengukur memantau dan mengukur kinerjanya dengan mengikuti standar yang berlaku secara internasional, yakni ISO-9001, OSHAS-18001, GRI 4.0, serta IFRS.

## Keterlibatan dalam Organisasi dan Asosiasi (102-13)

## Significant Change to Organizations and Supply Chain (102-10)

Up to the end of 2017, there is no significant change in terms of location, stock capital structure, and supply chain.

## Prevention Principles (102-11)

In running the business, Waskita is faced with potential operational, financial and environmental risks. The Company constantly identifies and evaluates the risks made through each division. The Board of Directors together with the Internal Audit Unit and the Board of Commissioners represented by the Audit Committee shall review and formulate necessary management and mitigation strategies. In 2017, the evaluation of the Company's risk management system has been effective.

## External Initiatives (102-12)

Waskita is committed to implement applicable standards in business, as well as for measuring monitoring and measuring its performance in accordance with internationally accepted standards, ISO-9001, OSHAS-18001, GRI 4.0, and IFRS.
