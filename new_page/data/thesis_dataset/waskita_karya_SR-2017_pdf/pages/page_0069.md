## BERGERAK BERSAMA MEMBERDAYAKAN MASYARAKAT

Waskita meyakini bahwa tanggung jawab sosial perusahaan atau Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) memiliki peranan yang sangat penting untuk mewujudkan keberlangsungan usaha Perseroan. Bagi Waskita, CSR merupakan suatu komitmen berkelanjutan untuk bertindak etis dalam mengimplementasikan program tanggung jawab sosial perusahaan untuk bersama membangun kualitas kehidupan yang lebih baik. Sebab itu, Waskita memiliki komitmen jangka panjang untuk melaksanakan program CSR sebagai bagian dari tanggung jawab perusahaan terhadap masyarakat dan lingkungan.

Kebijakan Perseroan dalam pelaksanaan CSR tertuang dalam Keputusan Direksi PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk Nomor: 53/SK/WK/2003 tentang Pembentukan Unit Kemitraan dan Program Bina Lingkungan PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk. Implementasi atas kebijakan tersebut, Perseroan membuat PKBL. Kemudian, Perseroan mengembangkan PKBL dan mengembangkannya menjadi Program Kemitraan dengan bantuan pinjaman modal kerja, hibah, dan biaya operasional serta Program Bina Lingkungan. (203-1, 203-2, 413-1)

Per 31 Desember 2017, susunan Pengurus PKBL Waskita adalah sebagai berikut:
Pembina Unit PKBL : Direktur Keuangan &amp; Strategi, Tunggul Rajagukguk
Kepala Unit PKBL : Meiriawan, SE
Bendahara Unit PKBL : Andri Ramadhan

## COMMUNITY EMPOWERING

Waskita believes that Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) has a very important role to realize the business continuity of the Company. For Waskita, CSR is an ongoing commitment to act ethically in implementing corporate social responsibility programs to jointly build a better quality of life. Therefore, Waskita has a long-term commitment to implement CSR program as part of corporate responsibility to society and environment.

The Company's policy in the implementation of CSR is contained in the Decree of PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk Board of Directors No.53/SK/WK/2003 on the Establishment of Partnership Unit and Community Development Program of PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk. By the Implementation of the policy, the Company established PKBL. Subsequently, the Company developed the PKBL and expanded it into a Partnership Program with the help of working capital loans, grants, and operational costs as well as the Community Development Program. (203-1, 203-2, 413-1)

As of December 31, 2017, the composition of the Waskita PKBL Board is as follows:
PKBL Founder : Director of Finance &amp; Strategy, Tunggul Rajagukguk
Head of Unit PKBL : Meiriawan
Treasurer of PKBL Unit : Andri Ramadhan

## CSR Principles

Implementation of Waskita's CSR is based on the following rules and standards:
a. Circular Letter of the State Ministry of State-Owned Enterprises No.SE-07 / MBU / 2008 dated May 5, 2008 concerning Implementation of PKBL and Implementation of Article 74 of Law No.40 of 2007 concerning Limited Liability Companies and the latest amendments number Per-347 09 / MBU / 07/2015 dated July 3, 2015 on Partnership Program of SOEs with Small Business and Partnership Program.
b. Circular Letter of State Ministry of State-owned Enterprises No.SE-14 / MBU / 2008 dated 30 June 2008 on Optimization of Partnership Program Fund through Distribution Cooperation.
