## Pemulihan dan Perlindungan Lingkungan

Perseroan berkomitmen untuk terus memberikan kontribusi positif dalam pelestarian lingkungan. Seluruh kegiatan operasional Perseroan dalam bidang konstruksi senantiasa

For the materials with the increasing volume, it is due to the increasing number of projects Waskita had in 2017. The materials meant here are steel bars, steel structure, long pole, and etc.

The other important thing, in the use of material especially from the supplier, Waskita only takes them from the licensed sources. For examples, mining business permits for suppliers of sand and split stone materials.
