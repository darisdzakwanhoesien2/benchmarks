Langkah-langkah Mewujudkan Kecelakaan Kerja Nihil

Komitmen Waskita untuk mewujudkan kecelakaan kerja nilih dilakukan melalui upaya-upaya berikut:

1. Personnel K3 menjadi lebih fokus ke masalah keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja
a. Melepaskan personnel K3 dari tugas pengawasan mutu dan menunjuk personnel QC sebagai personnel pengawasan mutu.
b. Posisi personnel K3 dan QC dibuat independen yaitu berada di bawah kepala proyek pada struktur organisasi proyek.
c. Personnel K3 mempunyai garis komando langsung dengan unit bisnis melalui kepala bagian jaminan mutu.
c. Revision of Waskita Procedure
d. Socialization and training
e. Implementation of PW in accordance with ISO 9001 &amp; 14001 version 2015

2. Internal &amp; External Audit
a. Efforts to avoid fatal accidents
b. Sharing knowledge of K3L
c. Functional guidance of HSE system
d. Site visit project related to K3L
e. Monthly Safety Awareness

3. Efforts to prevent construction failure
a. Sharing quality knowledge
a. Functional guidance of the quality system
a. Collection of methods and work instructions
a. Site visit project related to quality

4. Efforts to avoid cases of environmental pollution on a large scale (national)
a. Environmental measurement
b. Recording and reporting of spill events

5. Provide health and safety facilities provided at the office and on site:
a. Health facilities at the office
- Waskita Clinic led by Head of Clinic who is Hiperkes certified
b. Health facilities on site
- First Aid facilities in accordance with Permenakertrans No. PER-15 / MEN / VIII / 2008 namely;
- First Aid Room
- First aid kit and contents
- Evacuation and transportation equipment
- Additional facilities in the form of personal protective equipment and / or special equipment in the workplace which has potential special hazards

# Steps to Achieve Zero Work Accidents

Waskita's commitment to realize zero workplace accidents is done through the following efforts:

1. K3 personnel become more focused on safety and health issues
a. Unleash K3 personnel from quality control duties and appoint QC personnel as quality control personnel.
b. Positions of K3 and QC personnel are made independent of being under the project head on the project organization structure.
c. K3 personnel have a direct command line with the business unit through the head of the quality assurance department.
