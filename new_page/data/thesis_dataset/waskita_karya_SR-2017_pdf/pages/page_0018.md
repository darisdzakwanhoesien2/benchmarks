## Kinerja Sosial Keberlanjutan

Sebagai bentuk pelaksanaan komitmen triple bottom line pada aspek people, Waskita berupaya mewujudkan kesejahteraan untuk pemangku kepentingan eksternal, terutama masyarakat, melalui pelaksanaan program pengembangan dan pemberdayaan masyarakat. Program itu kami lakukan dalam wadah Tanggung Jawab Sosial Perusahaan atau Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

Bagi Waskita, CSR merupakan suatu komitmen berkelanjutan untuk bertindak etis dalam mengimplementasikan program tanggung jawab sosial perusahaan untuk bersama membangun kualitas kehidupan yang lebih baik. Sebab itu, Waskita memiliki komitmen jangka panjang untuk melaksanakan program CSR sebagai bagian dari tanggung jawab perusahaan terhadap masyarakat dan lingkungan. Kebijakan Perseroan dalam pelaksanaan CSR tertuang dalam Keputusan Direksi PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk Nomor: 53/SK/WK/2003 tentang Pembentukan Unit Kemitraan dan Program Bina Lingkungan PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk.

## Sustainable Social Performance

As a form of implementation of triple bottom line commitment on the aspect of people, Waskita seeks to create prosperity for external stakeholders, especially the community, through the implementation of development programs and community empowerment. The program we do in the form of Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

For Waskita, CSR is an ongoing commitment to act ethically in implementing corporate social responsibility programs to jointly build a better quality of life. Therefore, Waskita has a long-term commitment to implement CSR program as part of corporate responsibility to society and environment. The Company's policy in the implementation of CSR is contained in the Decree of PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk Board of Directors No. 53 / SK / WK / 2003 on the Establishment of Partnership Unit and Community Development Program of PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk.

### a. Partnership Fund Loan

The Partnership Program allows the Company to provide loan funds to non-commercial partners. The loan becomes the capital for the assisted partners to increase their business as well as in the investment of tools that support their business (financial capital).
