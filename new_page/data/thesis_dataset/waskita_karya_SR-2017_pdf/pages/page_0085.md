# Rekrutmen Management Trainee 2017

Pada 2017, Perseroan merekrut Batch I masuk 164 orang pada April tahun 2017 dan Batch II masuk 174 orang pada September 2017 Sehingga total rekrutmen SDM 2017 sebanyak 338 orang pegawai. Rekrutmen SDM yang dilakukan Perseroan, didasarkan pada data manpower planing yang telah dijelaskan sebelumnya.

Proses rekrutmen senantiasa menjunjung asas keterbukaan, kewajaran, dan kesetaraan. Calon pegawai dijaring melalui pemasangan iklan lowongan kerja di website Perseroan, job fair, kerjasama dengan universitas-universitas ternama, serta seleksi bagi calon pegawai yang pernah melakukan praktek kerja lapangan di Perseroan. Secara khusus, Perseroan merekrut calon pegawai melalui management trainee untuk posisiposisi tertentu yang membutuhkan kompetensi, leadership, serta keahlian khusus.

Proses adaptasi dan kemampuan berkomunikasi mutlak diperlukan setiap pegawai baru untuk menunjang kinerjanya. Karena itu, Perseroan juga menekankan kepada pegawai baru untuk berpikir terbuka dan positif serta harus memahami tugas dengan baik sebagai langkah awal dalam bekerja.

Seluruh pegawai baru yang telah diterima akan diberikan pembekalan untuk langsung ditempatkan dalam proyek-proyek yang sedang berjalan. Mereka diberikan program orientasi dan on the job training untuk mengenal sistem, prosedur dan budaya yang ada di Perseroan. Program orientasi dilakukan secara in class maupun melalui out bound dan pengenalan disiplin secara militer. Selanjutnya mereka akan ditempatkan secara temporer di Unit Kerja/Bisnis selama kuarng lebih 5 bulan untuk melihat dan belajar sistem dan prosedur yang telah dipelajari dalam masa orientasi.

Perseroan juga melakukan pemantauan dan mentoring, serta evaluasi terhadap pegawai baru dalam on the job training. Selama masa orientasi dan on the job training, para pegawai baru tersebut diberikan penilaian kinerja untuk penetapan diangkat atau tidaknya sebagai pegawai Perseroan.

# Management Trainee Recruitment 2017

In 2017, the Company recruited Batch I into 164 people in April of 2017 and Batch II entered 174 people in September 2017 so that the total recruitment of HR 2017 of 338 employees. The recruitment of human capital conducted by the Company is based on the previously mentioned manpower planing data.

The recruitment process always upholds the principles of openness, fairness, and equality. Prospective employees are netted through job advertisements on the Company's website, job fair, cooperation with renowned universities, and selection of candidate employees who have conducted field work practices in the Company. In particular, the Company recruits prospective employees through management trainees for certain positions that require competence, leadership, and specialized skills.

The process of adaptation and communication skills is absolutely needed by every new employee to support its performance. Therefore, the Company also emphasizes to new employees to think openly and positively and must understand the task well as a first step in work.

All new recruited employees will be given debriefing to be directly placed in ongoing projects. They are given an orientation program and on the job training to familiarize themselves with the systems, procedures and cultures of the Company. Orientation program is done in class or through out bound and military discipline introduction. They will then be placed temporarily in the Work / Business Unit for more than 5 months to view and learn the systems and procedures learned during the orientation.

The Company also conducts monitoring and mentoring, as well as evaluation of new employees in on the job training. During the orientation and on the job training, the new employees are given performance appraisals for the appointment of whether or not they are appointed as employees of the Company.
