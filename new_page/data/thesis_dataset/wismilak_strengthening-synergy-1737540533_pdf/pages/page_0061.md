# Kesempatan yang Setara dan Keberagaman

Perseroan menghargai kesetaraan dan keberagaman dalam manajemen SDM dengan mengedepankan kompetensi dan keterampilan karyawan sesuai dengan kebutuhan divisi. Seluruh karyawan baik laki-laki dan perempuan maupun dari tingkat bawah sampai atas, Perseroan membuka kesempatan kerja yang begitu lebar tanpa adanya diskriminasi terhadap agama, ras, dan gender. Hal ini telah diatur dalam Kebijakan No. MOD-OD-003 mengenai Kebijakan dan Prosedur Promosi/Mutasi Jabatan dan Penetapan Job Class bagi seluruh karyawan.

Selain itu, Perseroan mendukung program Workforce Inclusive Development yang merupakan program kesetaraan dan keberagaman bagi kaum disabilitas di tempat kerja. Pada bulan Juli 2021, perwakilan dari Wismilak telah mengikuti Pelatihan Bahasa Isyarat Dasar yang diadakan secara daring dan diikuti seluruh perusahaan di Indonesia. Pelatihan ini dilaksanakan dalam 2 batch yang diselenggarakan oleh Asosiasi Pengusaha Indonesia (APINDO) dan Pelopor Peduli Disabilitas Situbondo. Dengan adanya pelatihan ini, diharapkan dapat membuka peluang, mendukung, dan memberdayakan kaum disabilitas.

# Equal Opportunity and Diversity

The Company appreciates equality and diversity in Human Capital management by prioritizing employee competency and skills in accordance with the needs of divisions. All employees, whether male or female, from the lowest level upwards, receive access to a considerably extensive job opportunity without discrimination against religion, race, and gender. This has been regulated in Policy No. MOD-OD-003 on Position Promotion/ Transfer Policies and Procedure and Job Class Establishment for all employees.

Furthermore, the Company supports the Workforce Inclusive Development program which is an equality and diversity program for the disabled at the workplace. In July 2021, representatives from Wismilak attended the Basic Sign Language Training which was held online and was attended by all companies in Indonesia. This training was carried out in 2 batches organized by the Indonesian Employers' Association (APINDO) and Situbondo Disability Care Pioneers. With this training, it is hoped that it will open up opportunities, support, and empower people with disabilities.
