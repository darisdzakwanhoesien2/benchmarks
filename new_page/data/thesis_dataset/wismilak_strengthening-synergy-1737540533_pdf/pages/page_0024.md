# Company Profile [102-1] [102-2] [102-5]

PT Wismilak Inti Makmur Tbk (Wismilak) was established on 14 December 1994. The legal form of Wismilak is a 'Limited Liability Company' that trades its shares on the exchange with the share code of 'WIIM'.

Wismilak produces cigarette flavouring, filter, and other cigarette supporting materials. Additionally, the Company also markets and sells cigarette products and their paraphernalia as well as invests in kretek cigarette producing companies. Wismilak products can be viewed on the company website, www.wismilak.com, menu "Our Products".
