## Topik Material: Pemasaran dan Pelabelan [103-1] [103-2] [103-3]

## Tantangan yang Dikelola

Dari sisi pelabelan, tidak ada tantangan yang signifikan terjadi di periode berjalan. Namun, dari sisi pemasaran, adanya pembatasan sosial berskala besar masih menjadi tantangan yang signifikan di tahun 2021. Perseroan harus membatasi kegiatan bahkan program-program dari Divisi Pemasaran yang membutuhkan terjadinya kontak secara langsung. Hal tersebut guna membantu Perseroan dalam melawan virus COVID-19.

## Pendekatan Manajemen pada Topik Pemasaran dan Pelabelan

Tanggung jawab terhadap produk mencakup proses monitoring dan evaluasi mulai dari awal produksi hingga produk siap dipasarkan, termasuk memastikan pelabelan yang memadai. Setiap produk dipastikan telah tertera label dengan informasi kandungan produk yang sesuai dengan standar kelayakan Wismilak. Seluruh kegiatan produksi rokok diawasi dan menjadi tanggung jawab dari Departemen Primary Production, Secondary Production, dan Quality Assurance. Wismilak terus meningkatkan kepatuhan pada regulasi guna memastikan telah menjalankan standar operasional terbaik untuk menghasilkan produk berkualitas.

Dari sisi pemasaran produk, Wismilak memerhatikan bagaimana produk diterima di tangan konsumen dengan kondisi yang masih baik dan sesuai dengan standar dan ketentuan yang ditetapkan, baik dari sisi pengiriman maupun komunikasi pemasaran yang jelas bagi konsumen. Perseroan menerima saran maupun pengaduan dari konsumen dan telah menyediakan saluran untuk menyampaikan penilaian atas barang/jasa yang disediakan. Kegiatan terkait pemasaran dan pelabelan berada di bawah tanggung jawab Divisi Pemasaran, Quality Assurance, serta Sales &amp; Field Marketing.

## Material Topics: Marketing and Labeling [103-1] [103-2] [103-3]

## Managed Challenges

From the labeling aspect, there were no significant challenges in the fiscal period. However, from the marketing aspect, the large-scale social restrictions were a considerable challenge in 2021. The Company had to limit activities, including programs from the Marketing Division that required direct contact. This was to assist the Company in fighting the COVID-19 virus.

## Management Approach in the Topic of Marketing and Labeling

The responsibility for the product encompasses the monitoring and evaluation processes from the beginning of production until the products are ready to be marketed, including ensuring sufficient labeling. All products are ensured to carry the label that displays the product content information in accordance with the Wismilak adequacy standards. All production activities of cigarettes are monitored and fall under the responsibility of the Primary Production, Secondary Production, and Quality Assurance Departments. Wismilak continues to improve compliance with regulations to ensure that it has implemented the best operational standards to produce quality products.

From the product marketing aspect, Wismilak pays attention to how the products are received in the hands of consumers in good condition and in accordance with established standards and stipulations, whether from the delivery aspect or clear marketing communication to consumers. The Company receives inputs and complaints from consumers and has prepared channels to convey assessment of provided goods/services. Activities pertaining to marketing and labeling fall under the responsibility of the Marketing, Quality Assurance, as well as Sales &amp; Field Marketing Divisions.
