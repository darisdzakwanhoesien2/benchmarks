# Pengembangan Kompetensi

Topik Material: Pelatihan dan Pendidikan [103-1] [103-2] [103-3]

## Tantangan yang Dikelola

Pengembangan kemampuan dan kompetensi karyawan tetap dilaksanakan meski dalam situasi pandemi. Selama pandemi, Wismilak tetap menjalankan pelatihan dan program bagi karyawan baik daring maupun luring. Koneksi jaringan dan adaptasi perubahan menjadi daring menjadi salah satu kendala saat pelaksanaan implementasi pelatihan secara daring. Protokol kesehatan yang ketat diterapkan bagi karyawan yang melakukan pelatihan di lapangan.

## Pendekatan Manajemen pada Topik Pelatihan dan Pendidikan

Perseroan meyakini bahwa kelangsungan bisnis di industri salah satunya dilihat dari sisi karyawan. Karyawan difasilitasi oleh Wismilak dengan pelatihan dan pengembangan baik berupa softskill, technical, maupun leadership. Hal ini dilakukan Perseroan untuk meningkatkan kualitas dan kompetensi dari karyawan agar dapat menunjang pengembangan bisnis saat ini dan masa yang mendatang. Melalui aplikasi KELAS, karyawan dapat mengakses materi pelatihan secara bebas, kapan pun dan di manapun, pelatihan-pelatihan berbasis daring yang sudah dikembangkan dan dapat terjangkau ke semua karyawan Perseroan.

Divisi Human Resources (HR) bertanggung jawab atas pelatihan dan pengembangan karyawan serta melakukan evaluasi dengan menggunakan indikator kinerja utama (key performance indicator/KPI) pelatihan. Terkait aktivitas pelatihan dan pengembangan, karyawan dapat melakukan pengaduan terkait pelatihan dan pengembangan melalui HR-Helpdesk yang selanjutnya akan ditindaklanjuti oleh Departemen Management Organization Development.

# Competency Development

Material Topic: Training and Education [103-1] [103-2] [103-3]

## Managed Challenges

The development of employee capability and competency continued to be implemented despite the pandemic situation. During the pandemic, Wismilak persisted with implementing training and program for employees, whether online or offline. Strict health protocols were implemented for employees undertaking training on the field.

## Management Approach in the Topic of Training and Education

The Company believes that one of the benchmarks of business continuity in the industry is the employee aspect. Employees are facilitated by Wismilak with training and development, whether soft skill, technical skill, or leadership. This is conducted by the Company to improve employee quality and competency to support current and future business development. Through the KELAS application, employees are able to freely access, whenever and wherever, online-based training which has been developed and accessible for all Company employees.

The Human Resources (HR) Division is responsible for employee training and development and performs evaluation using the key performance indicators of the training. Pertaining to training and development activities, employees can file reports regarding training and development via the HR-Helpdesk, which will be followed-up by the Management Organization Development Department.
