# Pengelolaan Air Limbah

Upaya penanganan air limbah industri dan domestik dilakukan di Instalasi Pengolahan Air Limbah (IPAL), guna menanggulangi dampak pencemaran dan kerusakan lingkungan. Pengelolaan dilangsungkan untuk memastikan air olahan IPAL yang kembali dibuang ke sungai atau parit, sesuai dengan peraturan dan mematuhi baku mutu industri tembakau dan cerutu.

# Wastewater Processing

Efforts to process industrial and domestic wastewater are conducted at the Wastewater Processing Installation (IPAL) to mitigate pollution impacts and environmental damage. Processing is undertaken to ensure that the IPAL processed water disposed to rivers or ditches is in accordance with regulations and meets the quality standards of the tobacco and cigar industry.
