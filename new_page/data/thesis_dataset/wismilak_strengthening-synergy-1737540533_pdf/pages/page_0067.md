# Pelaksanaan MCU

Perseroan bertugas untuk memfasilitasi karyawan perihal keamanan seperti alat pelindung diri (APD) untuk mengurangi kecelakaan akibat pekerjaan berisiko tinggi. Pekerjaan berisiko tinggi dapat diidentifikasi menggunakan metode Failure Mode and Effect Analysis (FMEA). Pada tahun 2021, Perseroan mencatatkan bahwa tidak ada kasus kematian yang terjadi, namun terdapat 62 kejadian kecelakaan kerja yang terdiri atas 49 kasus kecelakaan lalu lintas di luar lingkungan Perseroan, 12 kasus akibat unsafe action, dan 1 kasus akibat unsafe condition. # MCU implementation

The Company has the duty of facilitating employees pertaining to safety concerns, such as personal protective equipment (APD) to reduce the probability of an accident due to high-risk jobs. High-risk jobs can be identified using the Failure Mode and Effect Analysis (FMEA) method. In 2021, the Company recorded no fatalities; however, there were 62 work accidents consisting of 49 traffic accidents outside of the environment of the Company, 12 cases due to unsafe action, and 1 case due to unsafe condition.
