# Energy Efficiency Initiative [302-4]

In 2021, there were activities with low-cost, medium-cost, and high-cost approaches for energy efficiency which caused a decrease in energy intensity. The initiatives implemented by Wismilak were:

## LOW COST

- Pemasangan atap transparan untuk pencahayaan di siang hari secara berkelanjutan sehingga menurunkan penggunaan konsumsi daya listrik untuk penerangan
- Melakukan perawatan mesin produksi secara berkala dengan mengganti spare part sesuai standar umur pakai
- Melakukan penghematan air PDAM
- Installation of transparent roofing for sustainable natural lighting during the day to reduce electricity consumption for lighting
- Conducting periodic production machine maintenance by replacing spare parts in accordance with service life standards
- Conducting PDAM water saving

## MEDIUM COST

- Mengoptimalkan operasional kompresor secara bergantian sesuai dengan kebutuhan suplai angin ke mesin-mesin produksi
- Menggunakan air eflien untuk kebutuhan perawatan pohon dan tanaman perdu
- Mengoptimalkan pemakaian LNG sebagai bahan bakar utama dari boiler
- Mengganti pompa air dengan mekanisme gravitasi untuk distribusi air bersih dari tandon ke toilet
- Mengganti lampu PJU dari lampu konvensional menjadi tipe lampu LED
- Optimizing compressor operations alternately in accordance with the wind supply needs to production machines
- Using effluent water for watering trees and shrubberies
- Optimizing LNG consumption as the main boiler fuel
- Replacing water pumps with gravity mechanism for the distribution of clean water from the tank to the toilet
- Replacing PJU lamps from conventional lamps to LED type lights

## HIGH COST

- Mengoptimalkan penggunaan Single Packet Heating Tunnel (SPHT) untuk menggantikan shrinkage tunnel pada proses produksi
- Menurunkan nilai pressure steam pada boiler sehingga konsumsi air boiler juga mengalami penurunan
- Optimizing the use of Single Packet Heating Tunnel (SPHT) to replace shrinkage tunnel in the production process
- Decreasing the pressure steam value in the boiler to reduce boiler water consumption
