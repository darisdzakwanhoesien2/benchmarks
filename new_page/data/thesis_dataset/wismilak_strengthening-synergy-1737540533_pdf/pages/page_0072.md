# Peduli Pendidikan

Untuk meningkatkan literasi dan budaya membaca bagi anak-anak usia dini, melalui program Aksiliterasi Surabaya, Wismilak melakukan pembagian buku bacaan ke 21 sekolah di Surabaya yang dilakukan pada 25 November 2021 yang bertepatan pada Hari Guru Nasional. Program berhasil dilaksanakan berkat dukungan Pemerintah Kota Surabaya diwakili oleh Ibu Chusnur Ismiati Hendro Gunawan sebagai Ketua Dharma Wanita Persatuan Kota Surabaya serta pemerhati Literasi Membaca di Kota Surabaya.

# Peduli Wirausaha

Wismilak mempersembahkan kembali program kewirausahaan Diplomat Success Challenge (DSC) XII yang merupakan bagian dari ekosistem kewirausahaan yang diinisiasi Wismilak sejak 2010. Program kewirausahaan DSC XII diselenggarakan mulai 19 Juli 2021 – 21 Januari 2022 dengan mengusung tema #BIKINGEBRAKAN untuk memberikan peluang bagi kaum muda untuk menjadi wirausahawan yang sukses melalui networking, edukasi, kompetisi wirausaha, pemberian hibah modal usaha dan mentoring. Pada tahun 2022, program DSC akan menjadi salah satu program insersi keberlanjutan yang dilaksanakan berdasarkan diskusi dengan Global Reporting Initiatives (GRI).

Program kewirausahaan DSC XII tahun 2021 juga berjalan dengan lancar berkat kolaborasi yang didukung berbagai pihak eksternal, seperti NU Circle, LPNU (Lembaga Perekonomian Nahdlatul Ulama), Ansor, dan HIPSI (Himpunan Pengusaha Santri Indonesia), dan APINDO UMKM AKADEMI (AUA). Meski pandemi COVID-19 masih berlangsung, program kewirausahaan DSC XII tetap dilakukan secara daring, namun untuk kegiatan luring tetap dilaksanakan dengan mematuhi protokol kesehatan secara ketat.

# Care for Education

To improve the literacy of and the culture to read in early age children, Wismilak through Aksiliterasi Surabaya program distributed reading books to 21 schools in Surabaya, conducted on 25 November 2021, which coincided with the National Teachers' Day. The program was successfully carried out due to the support of the Surabaya City Government, represented by Mrs. Chusnur Ismiati Hendro Gunawan as Chairman of Dharma Wanita Persatuan of the Surabaya City and an observer of the Reading Literacy in Surabaya City.

# Care for Entrepreneurs

Wismilak held the Diplomat Success Challenge (DSC) XII entrepreneurship program as part of the entrepreneurship ecosystem initiated by Wismilak since 2010. The DSC XII entrepreneurship program was held from 19 July 2021 to 21 January 2022 with the #BIKINGEBRAKAN (#MAKINGABREAKTHROUGH) theme, to provide opportunities for young people to become successful entrepreneurs through networking, education, entrepreneurial competitions, granting business capital and mentoring. In 2022, the DSC program will be one of the sustainability insertion programs implemented based on discussions with the Global Reporting Initiatives (GRI).

The 2021 DSC XII entrepreneurship program ran smoothly due to the collaboration supported by various external parties, such as the NU Circle, LPNU (Nahdlatul Ulama Economic Institution), Ansor, and HIPSI (Association of Indonesian Santri Entrepreneurs), and APINDO UMKM AKADEMI (AUA). As the COVID-19 pandemic was still ongoing, the DSC XII entrepreneurship program was held online, and offline activities were carried out by complying with strict health protocols.
