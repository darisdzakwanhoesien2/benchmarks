# Pengadaan Barang dan Jasa

Serangkaian kegiatan dan proses produksi produk di Wismilak melibatkan pemasok lokal dalam operasionalnya. Pelibatan pemasok lokal mendapatkan respon baik serta dampak positif dengan membangun perekonomian lokal. Pelibatan dilangsungkan guna membantu pemenuhan kebutuhan dari sisi material untuk pembuatan produk, seperti tembakau tembakau asli daerah dari seluruh Indonesia.

# Procurement of Goods and Services

A series of production activities and processes at Wismilak engages local suppliers in their operations. The engagement of local suppliers received positive response and brought positive impacts by developing local economy. Engagement is also conducted to assist meeting the needs for material aspects in product manufacturing, such as native tobacco from all over Indonesia.
