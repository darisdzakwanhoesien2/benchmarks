# Wilayah Operasi dan Pasar Terlayani
[102-4] [102-6] [102-10]

Seluruh kegiatan operasional entitas induk dan entitas anak dari proses produksi, distribusi, dan pemasaran produk dikerjakan di Indonesia. Pada tahun 2021, terdapat penambahan wilayah operasional yaitu, Area Distribusi Tangerang. Distribusi terbesar untuk produk yaitu dari Indonesia sebagai pasar dalam negeri diikuti dengan pasar luar negeri, seperti Cina, Vietnam, Taiwan, dan Belize yang dikhususkan untuk ekspor produk filter rods.

# Operational Areas and Market Served
[102-4] [102-6] [102-10]

All operational activities of the parent entity and subsidiary entities from the production, distribution, and marketing processes of products are conducted in Indonesia. In 2021, there was an additional area of operation, which was the Tangerang Distribution Area. The largest distribution for the products is Indonesia as the domestic market, followed by overseas markets, such as China, Vietnam, Taiwan, and Belize, which are specialized for the filter rods product export.
