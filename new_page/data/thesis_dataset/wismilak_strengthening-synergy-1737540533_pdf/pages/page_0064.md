# Program Masa Persiapan Pensiun (MPP) [404-2]

Persiapan Wismilak dalam mengelola karyawan laki-laki dan perempuan yang memasuki usia pensiun, kini telah terwujudkan di tahun 2021. Beberapa program telah dipersiapkan oleh Perseroan untuk membina karyawan Wismilak yang memasuki umur 55 tahun keatas sebagai ketentuan umur pensiun.

Tahun ini, Wismilak, bekerja sama dengan Dinas Tenaga Kerja (Disnaker) Kota Surabaya, telah menjalankan Batch 1 untuk program MPP yang diadakan pada 24-25 Agustus 2021 dengan 1 partisipan. Dalam kegiatan ini, partisipan diberikan materi mengenai Siap dan Produktif Menghadapi Masa Pensiun, Mindset Kewirausahaan dan Peluang Usaha, dan Jenis-jenis Usaha yang cocok bagi Pensiunan dan Tips Eksekusi.

# Evaluasi dan Penilaian Kinerja [404-3]

Evaluasi dan penilaian kinerja diberlakukan terhadap seluruh karyawan setiap dua kali dalam satu tahun. Evaluasi dilakukan secara digital melalui portal e-KPI sebagai dasar penilaian terhadap kinerja karyawan setelah adanya hasil talent review dan performance appraisal. Setelah itu, evaluasi akan ditinjau kembali oleh manajemen untuk menentukan reward dan punishment sebagai respon atas hasil tinjauan. Selain reward dan punishment, Perseroan juga akan menerima bimbingan dan arahan untuk keefektifan kinerja karyawan.

Peninjauan atas potensi karyawan juga dinilai melalui sistem 9-box dan performance appraisal. Peninjauan potensi karyawan dilakukan minimal 6 bulan sekali setelah pengangkatan dan akan ditinjau satu tahun sekali. Akhir tahun 2021, 54,8% karyawan telah menerima evaluasi dan penilaian kinerja dan 11,7% karyawan telah menerima evaluasi dan penilaian potensi.

# Retirement Preparation Period (MPP) Program [404-2]

The preparation of Wismilak in managing male and female employees entering the retirement age has been realized in 2021. A number of programs were prepared by the Company to foster Wismilak employees entering 55 years of age upwards as the retirement age stipulation.

This year, Wismilak, in cooperation with the Surabaya City Manpower Department (Disnaker), has implemented Batch 1 for the MPP program held on 24-25 August 2021 with 1 participant. In the activity, the participant was given materials pertaining to Ready and Productive in Facing the Retirement Period, the Entrepreneurship Mindset and Business Opportunities, and Types of Businesses Suitable for Retirees and Tips for Execution.

# Performance Evaluation and Assessment [404-3]

Performance evaluation and assessment are enforced on all employees twice in one year. Evaluation is conducted digitally via the e-KPI portal as the basis for assessment of employee performance after the availability of talent review and performance appraisal results. Then the evaluation will be re-evaluated by management to determine reward and punishment as a response to evaluation results. Aside from reward and punishment, the Company also receives guidance and directives for employee performance effectiveness.

The review of employee potential is also assessed via the 9-box system and performance appraisal. The employee potential review is performed at a minimum of once every 6 months following appointment and will be reviewed once a year. At the end of 2021, 54.8% of employees have received performance evaluation and assessment and 11.7% of employees have received evaluation and potential assessment.
