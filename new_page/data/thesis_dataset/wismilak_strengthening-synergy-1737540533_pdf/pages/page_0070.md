# Tumbuh Bersama Masyarakat

Topik Material: Masyarakat Lokal [103-1] [103-2] [103-3]

## Tantangan yang Dikelola

Secara garis besar, tantangan yang dihadapi dipengaruhi oleh musibah COVID-19 sehingga adanya keadaan yang terbatas untuk berinteraksi antar individu secara langsung. Dalam penerapannya, Wismilak membatasi mobilitas seperti pertemuan secara langsung dan banyak program pemberdayaan masyarakat yang dilakukan secara daring maupun penggabungan luring dan daring (hybrid) sesuai protokol kesehatan dan peraturan yang berlaku.

## Pendekatan Manajemen pada Topik Masyarakat Lokal

Masyarakat sekitar merupakan salah satu elemen penting yang memberikan dampak terhadap operasional Perseroan. Wismilak terus melakukan inovasi dalam tanggung jawab sosial yang mencakup kegiatan peningkatan kesejahteraan dan potensi masyarakat sebagai bentuk perwujudan salah satu nilai Wismilak, yaitu “Bersama meraih Sukses”. Program pemberdayaan masyarakat tetap dilakukan meski di tengah kondisi COVID-19 tanpa melupakan protokol kesehatan, di mana seluruh kegiatan tanggung jawab sosial ini berada di bawah Departemen Hubungan Masyarakat. Pada tahun 2021, alokasi dana yang direalisasikan sebesar Rp16,6 miliar.

## Evaluasi Dampak Operasional bagi Masyarakat Sekitar [413-1] [413-2]

Guna menjaga hubungan dengan masyarakat sekitar yang tinggal di sekitar wilayah pabrik, Wismilak mengidentifikasi potensi dampak negatif mungkin terjadi akibat operasional pabrik. Hasil evaluasi dampak operasional yang mungkin menjadi potensi di antaranya berupa kebisingan, dan berbagai jenis material luaran atau limbah dari proses produksi yang diatasi dengan adanya pengelolaan limbah sesuai prosedur.

# Growing with the Community

Material Topic: Local Communities [103-1] [103-2] [103-3]

## Managed Challenges

Overall, the challenges faced were influenced by the COVID-19 disaster, which caused limited conditions to directly interact with other individuals. In its implementation, Wismilak restricted mobility, such as direct meetings and a majority of community empowerment programs were performed online or by combining offline and online (hybrid) as per prevailing health protocols and regulations.

## Management Approach in the Topic of Local Communities

The surrounding communities are one of the significant elements that impact the operations of the Company. Wismilak continues to innovate social responsibility that includes activities to improve community welfare and potential as a form of materialization of one of Wismilak values, “Achieving Success Together”. The community empowerment program continued to be implemented despite the COVID-19 condition while complying with health protocols, whereas all social responsibility activities were under the Public Relations Department. In 2021, the realized allocated funds were Rp16.6 billion.

## Evaluation of the Impact of Operations on the Surrounding Communities [413-1] [413-2]

To maintain relations with the surrounding communities living around factory areas, Wismilak identifies potentially occurring negative impacts due to factory operations. The evaluation results of potentially occurring impact of operations are noise, and various types of output material or waste from the production process, which are mitigated with waste management as per procedure.
