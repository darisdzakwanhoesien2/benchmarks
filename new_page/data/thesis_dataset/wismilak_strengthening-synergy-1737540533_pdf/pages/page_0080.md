Perseroan berkomitmen untuk menjaga kualitas produk yang memenuhi kebutuhan pasar dengan serangkaian proses bisnis dan pengawasan yang sangat ketat. Saat ini, terdapat 55 pemasok lokal yang terlibat dari seluruh Indonesia. Wismilak memastikan bahwa setiap kegiatan hingga proses yang dilakukan hingga sampai ke entitas anak sudah mematuhi peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku serta penerapan prinsip good manufacturing practices; kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja, keamanan, dan lingkungan; tata kelola Perseroan; dan kebijakan lainnya yang terkait. Seluruh proses bisnis yang terjadi dalam Perseroan bisa dilihat di situs web Perseroan, www.wismilak.com, menu "Tentang Kami".

# Topik Material: Praktik Pengadaan [103-1] [103-2] [103-3]

# Tantangan yang Dikelola

Meski terjadi beberapa kali Pembatasan Sosial Berskala Besar (PSBB), pergerakan dan pengelolaan rantai pasokan Perseroan tidak terganggu oleh dampak COVID-19 selama periode laporan. Perseroan memastikan selama masa pandemi kegiatan operasional baik dari produksi produk hingga pendistribusian berjalan dengan lancar serta memastikan kualitas dari produk yang diterima oleh pelanggan.

# Pendekatan Manajemen pada Topik Praktik Pengadaan

Pengelolaan rantai pasokan Wismilak dilakukan secara ketat untuk mendapatkan kinerja yang terbaik. Seluruh pemasok dan vendor dipilih berdasarkan proses seleksi sesuai dengan kebijakan Perseroan. Pemilihan pemasok dan vendor dilakukan atas pedoman Kebijakan dan Prosedur Pengelolaan Pembelanjaan dengan Pemasok yang dikelola oleh Departemen Procurement. Lewat kebijakan ini, Perseroan dapat melakukan pengawasan terhadap kualitas-kualitas material yang dipakai. Jauh sebelum COVID-19, Wismilak telah menjalin kerja sama dengan pemasok lokal untuk memasok bahan baku sebagai bahan material produk.

# Supply Chain [102-9]

The Company is committed to maintaining product quality that meets market needs with a series of business processes and strict monitoring. Presently, there are 55 engaged local suppliers from across Indonesia. Wismilak ensures that each activity and process conducted until the subsidiary entities have complied with prevailing regulatory laws as well as implementation of the principles of good manufacturing practices; occupational health and safety, security, and environment; corporate governance; and other relevant policies. All business process occurring within the Company can be viewed on www.wismilak.com, menu "About Us".

# Material Topic: Procurement Practices [103-1] [103-2] [103-3]

# Managed Challenges

Despite the occurrence of multiple instances of Large-Scale Social Restrictions (PSBB), the movement and management of the supply chain of the Company was not obstructed by the impacts of COVID-19 in the reporting period. The Company ensured that during the pandemic, the operational activities, whether from the product manufacturing to distribution went smoothly while ensuring the quality of the products received by consumers.

# Management Approach to the Topic of Procurement Practices

The Wismilak supply chain management is strictly conducted to obtain the best performance. All suppliers and vendors are chosen based on a selection process as per the policies of the Company. Selection of suppliers and vendors is performed under the guideline of the Expenditure Management Policy and Procedure with Suppliers which is managed by the Procurement Department. Through this policy, the Company was able to monitor the quality of materials used. Far prior to COVID-19, Wismilak had cultivated cooperation with local suppliers to supply raw materials as the materials for the products.
