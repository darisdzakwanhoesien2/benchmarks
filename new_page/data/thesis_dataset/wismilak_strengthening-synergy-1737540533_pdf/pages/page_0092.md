# Kepatuhan dan Etika Bisnis

Wismilak menjunjung tinggi integritas dan menjadikan etika bisnis sebagai dasar dalam menjalankan usaha. Terdapat kebijakan yang jelas dalam setiap proses bisnis yang wajib didukung dan diterapkan oleh seluruh karyawan, maupun pemasok/mitra kerja yang berada di lingkungan Perseroan. Selain menjalankan kebijakan internal, Wismilak juga patuh terhadap regulasi di Indonesia serta menerapkan norma-norma etika bisnis yang berlaku universal.

Perseroan menolak segala bentuk kecurangan, korupsi, maupun gratifikasi. Adanya Kode Etik yang berlaku bagi seluruh karyawan diharapkan dapat meningkatkan kesadaran untuk bekerja dengan bersih dan mendukung praktik antikorupsi di lingkungan perusahaan. Kebijakan antikorupsi dan antikecurangan tercantum dalam Board Manual Dewan Komisaris, Direksi Wismilak, dan juga Sistem Perilaku Perusahaan. Dalam Sistem Perilaku Perusahaan Wismilak, juga mencakup nilai-nilai kejujuran, kesopanan, dan integritas yang wajib dipatuhi oleh seluruh insan Wismilak. Sosialisasi mengenai kode etik dan standar perilaku kepada karyawan hendak dilakukan secara berkala melalui berbagai kanal komunikasi. Untuk mendapatkan informasi lebih mendalam, dapat dilihat di situs web perusahaan, www.wismilak.com, menu "Informasi Investor" sub menu "Praktik Tata Kelola". [102-16]

Perseroan menyediakan sistem pelaporan pelanggaran (whistleblowing system/WBS) yang dikelola oleh dikelola oleh Sekretaris Perusahaan dan Tim Wismilak Whistleblowing System (Tim WWBS), dan telah ditetapkan dalam Standar Perilaku Perusahaan. WBS juga menjadi sarana untuk melapor terkait pelanggaran terhadap peraturan Wismilak, Kode Etik, maupun pelanggaran hukum dan kegiatan ilegal lain yang berpotensi menimbulkan kerugian bagi Perseroan di masa depan. Setiap laporan dijamin akan ditindaklanjuti sesuai hasil temuan penyelidik dengan jaminan perlindungan terhadap pelapor. Pada tahun 2021, tidak ada pelaporan yang diterima Perseroan. Skema WBS dapat dilihat di situs web perusahaan, www.wismilak.com, menu "Informasi Investor" sub menu "Praktik Tata Kelola".

# Compliance with Business Ethics

Wismilak highly upholds integrity and establishes business ethics as a foundation in running its business. There are clear policies in every business process which are mandatory to be supported and implemented by all employees and suppliers/business partners in the environment of the Company. Aside from implementing internal policies, Wismilak also complies with regulations in Indonesia as well as implements universally applicable business ethics norms.

The Company strongly rejects all forms of fraud, corruption, and gratuity. The Code of Conduct, applicable to all employees, is expected to raise the awareness for clean work and support the anticorruption practices in the environment of the Company. The anticorruption and antifraud policies are included in the Board Manual of the Wismilak Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, as well as the Corporate Behavior System. In the Wismilak Corporate Behavior System, irregular values of honesty, courtesy, and integrity that must be complied with by all Wismilak Personnel. Socialization of the code of conduct and standards of behavior to employees is performed periodically via various communication channels. For more detailed information, please view on www.wismilak.com, menu "Investor Information" sub menu "Governance Practice". [102-16]

The Company provides a Whistleblowing System (WBS) managed by the Corporate Secretary and the Wismilak Whistleblowing System Team (WWBS Team), and has been established in the Standards of Behavior of the Company. The WBS is also a means to report pertaining to violations of Wismilak regulations, Code of Conduct, or violations of law and other illegal activities that potentially cause loss for the Company in the future. Each report is guaranteed to be followed-up in accordance with the findings of investigators, along with a protection guarantee for the whistleblower. In 2021, there were no reports received by the Company. The WBS System scheme can be viewed further on www.wismilak.com, menu "Investor Information" sub menu "Governance Practice".
