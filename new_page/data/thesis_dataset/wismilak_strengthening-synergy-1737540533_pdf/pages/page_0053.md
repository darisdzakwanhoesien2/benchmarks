# Program Akselerasi Karir

Untuk mempersiapkan kompetensi dan mempersiapkan lulusan baru (fresh graduate) agar dapat mengisi posisi-posisi manajerial khusus, diadakan program Akselerasi Karir. Pada tahun 2021, telah dilaksanakan 2 program akselerasi, yaitu Program Pendidikan Graduate Challenge Program (GCP) untuk posisi Brand Manager (BM), serta Program Pendidikan Sales and Marketing Supervisor (PPSMS) untuk posisi supervisor di bidang sales dan marketing. Tahun 2022 akan dilaksanakan program terbaru, yaitu Program Pendidikan Area Sales Manager (PPASM) yang ditujukan bagi lulusan baru yang ingin mengisi posisi manajer di bidang sales dan marketing.

# Career Acceleration Program

The Career Acceleration program is held to develop competency and prepare fresh graduates to fill specific managerial positions. 2 Akselerasi Karir programs were held in 2021, which were the Graduate Challenge Program (GCP) Education program for the Brand Manager (BM) position as well as the Sales and Marketing Supervisor Education Program (PPSMS) for the supervisor position in sales and marketing. A new program will be held in 2022, the Area Sales Manager Education Program (PPASM), intended for fresh graduates wanting to fill position in sales and marketing.

Selain beberapa program tersebut, Wismilak juga mengadakan program seperti Talent Mapping untuk meningkatkan kompetensi meningkatkan kompetensi karyawan sehingga tidak adanya kesenjangan antara jajaran manajemen dengan karyawan generasi milenial muda. Evaluasi kompetensi karyawan yang disiapkan Wismilak lewat tool 9-box juga merupakan salah satu sarana mekanisme evaluasi untuk melihat apakah ada kesesuaian kompetensi karyawan dengan posisi yang kosong. Secara berkala, evaluasi talenta dilakukan melalui program Individual Development Plan (IDP) untuk memastikan kesiapan (fit and proper) talenta-talenta yang ada dalam mengisi kekosongan posisi.

Aside from the aforementioned programs, Wismilak also holds programs such as Talent Mapping to improve employee competency and remove the gap between management and young millennial generation employees. The employee competency evaluation prepared by Wismilak via the 9-box tool is also a means of evaluation mechanism to observe the compatibility between employee competency and vacant positions. Periodically, talent evaluation is conducted via the Individual Development Plan (IDP) program to ensure the fit and proper of existing talents in filling vacant positions.
