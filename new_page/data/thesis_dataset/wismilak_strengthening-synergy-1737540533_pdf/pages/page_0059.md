Pengembangan kompetensi karyawan dilakukan dengan beberapa tata cara seperti pelatihan in-class, on-the-job training, off-the-job training, maupun e-learning. Program yang diikuti mulai dari karyawan, baik yang baru, posisi jabatan yang baru, maupun penggantian tanggung jawab pekerjaan.

Program yang diaplikasikan dalam Perseroan antara lain I'm In Program bagi karyawan baru dan HR Knowledge for New Manager bagi manajer baru. Program ini diadakan sebagai bentuk pengenalan visi, misi, dan nilai-nilai yang diadaptasikan oleh Perseroan serta peraturan dan budaya organisasi agar dapat menjalankan tugas dan kewajiban sesuai dengan tugas dan tanggung jawab masing-masing.

## Manajemen SDM yang Menghormati HAM Kebijakan Ketenagakerjaan [102-41]

Dalam upaya pemenuhan hak dan kewajiban tenaga kerja dengan Perseroan sesuai peraturan yang berlaku, Wismilak memiliki Peraturan Perusahaan (PP) dan Perjanjian Kerja Bersama (PKB). Dengan adanya pengelolaan sumber daya manusia (SDM), secara lebih sistematis dapat memastikan kesetaraan kesempatan kerja, peningkatan kualitas dan kapasitas karyawan, perlindungan di tempat kerja, serta keharmonisan dalam lingkup kerja. Semua kebijakan yang dibuat juga memuat informasi bahwa Perseroan tidak mempekerjakan pekerja di bawah umur dan tidak adanya pekerja paksa.

Employee competency development is conducted via several methods, such as in-class training, on-the-job training, off-the-job training, and e-learning. The programs are participated in by employees, whether newly hired, in new positions, or a shift in job responsibility.

The programs applied in the Company include the I'm In Program for new employees and HR Knowledge for New Manager for new managers. The programs are conducted as a form of introduction to the vision, mission, and values adopted by the Company as well as company regulations and organizational culture for employees to be able to perform their duties and responsibilities in accordance with their respective duties and responsibilities.

## Human Capital Management That Respects Human Rights Labor Policies [102-41]

In the effort to fulfill the rights and responsibilities of labor with the Company as per prevailing regulations, Wismilak has Company Regulations (PP) and Collective Labor Agreement (PKB). The existence of human capital (SDM) management can systematically ensure job opportunity equality, employee quality and capacity improvement, workplace protection, as well as harmony within the scope of the work. All formulated policies also contain information that the Company does not employ underage workers and there is no forced labor.
