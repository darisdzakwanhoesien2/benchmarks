Pada tahun 2024, PTBA merealisasikan dana Program Tanggung Jawab Sosial dan Lingkungan sebesar Rp70,68 miliar. Dana disalurkan melalui Program Pendanaan UMK yang bekerja sama dengan BRI dan Program Pembeian Bantuan dan/atau Kegiatan Lainnya.

In 2024, PTBA has realized up to Rp70.68 billion for the Social and Environmental Responsibility Program. The fund was distributed through the MSEs Financing Program in collaborate with BRI and the Assistance and/or Other Activities Program.
