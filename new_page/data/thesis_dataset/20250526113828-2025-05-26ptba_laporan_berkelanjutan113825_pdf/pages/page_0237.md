# Top 10 Hazardous and OHS Risk Identified through Identification Process and Risk Assessment in 2024

|  9 | Bekerja dengan panas (pengelasan yang terdapat atau dekat dengan bahan yang mudah meledak/terbakar atau pekerjaan yang di luar area workshop yang tidak dirancang untuk pekerjaan panas)
Working with heat (welding in or near explosive/flammable materials or work outside the workshop area that is not designed to be heat resistant) | Rekayasa:
Grounding alat terpasang baik, exhaust fan

Administrasi:
Kompeten pengelas, kompeten penanggulangan kebakaran, pengawas wajib POP

Praktik Kerja:
Otoritas kerja dengan panas, JSA, pengawasan melekat

APD:
Menggunakan APD yang sesuai

Engineering:
The grounding device is installed properly, exhaust fan

Administration:
Welder competence, fire prevention competence, POP mandatory supervisor

Work practice:
Authority to work with heat, JSA, Attached supervision

PPE:
Use appropriate PPE  |
|  10 | Bekerja di atas/dekat air kedalaman > 1 m atau belum diketahui
Working above/near water with 1m deep or unknown | Rekayasa:
Sarana walkway dipasang railing

Administrasi:
Minimal pekerja 2 orang, pengawas wajib POP, rambu keselamatan wajib pelampung, peringatan high risk, dumping limiter, rambu batas dan kedalaman penggalian, rambu dan lokasi evakuasi darurat

Praktik Kerja:
Otoritas kerja dekat air, pelatihan khusus bekerja dekat air, JSA

APD:
Selalu menggunakan pelampung

Engineering:
Walkway facilities are installed with railings

Administration:
Minimum of 2 workers, mandatory POP supervisors, mandatory safety signs for buoys, high risk warnings, dumping limiters, boundary signs and excavation depth, emergency evacuation signs and locations.

Work practice:
Authority for working near water, special training for working near water, JSA

PPE:
Always use life vest  |
