# Performance Evaluation of Governance Body [GRI 2-18]

Performance evaluation of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors has been regulated in the Work Guidelines for the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors (Board Manual), which explains the main performance measures (Key Performance Indicator) as certain measures which are targets that must be achieved by the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors.
