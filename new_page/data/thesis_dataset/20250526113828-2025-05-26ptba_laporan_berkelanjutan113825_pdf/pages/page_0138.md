For important documents, the paper is shredded using a paper shredder before disposal. Although PTBA uses recyclable and environmentally friendly paper materials, the Company does not use recycled paper products in its day-to-day operations. [GJK FS]

As of December 31, 2024, PTBA used 15.70 tons of paper, an increase compared to 2023 which reached 13.08 tons. The increase was influenced by the increase in the number of documents that still needed to be printed.
