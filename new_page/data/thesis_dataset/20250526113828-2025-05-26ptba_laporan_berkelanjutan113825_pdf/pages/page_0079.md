PTBA berhasil mencapai laba bersih Rp5,10 triliun. Pencapaian itu didukung oleh peningkatan penjualan 16% yoy dan bertambahnya nilai ekspor hingga 30% yoy, terutama ke negara-negara Asia Tenggara.

PTBA successfully achieved a net profit of Rp5.10 trillion. This achievement was supported by an increase in production of 4% year-on-year and an increase in export value of 30% year-on-year, especially to Southeast Asian countries.
