# Pengembangan Sumber Daya Manusia Berkelanjutan Sustainable Human Capital Development

Sumber Daya Manusia (SDM) merupakan aset penting yang memberikan kontribusi besar bagi Perseroan untuk meraih keunggulan dalam persaingan bisnis dan kinerja terbaik. SDM menjadi elemen penting kesuksesan kinerja Perseroan dalam memberikan pelayanan terbaik kepada seluruh pelanggan. Menyadari hal tersebut, Perseroan mengembangkan sistem Human Capital Management System yang memberdayakan SDM yang ada di Perseroan.

## Kekuatan SDM Waskita

Perseroan memiliki jumlah SDM pada 2016 sebanyak 1.509 orang. Jumlah ini mengalami peningkatan 15,10% dibandingkan pada 2015 sebanyak 1.311 orang. Peningkatan jumlah SDM tersebut sejalan dengan kebutuhan SDM dari sisi kuantitas, serta mempertimbangkan peningkatan produktivitas setiap pegawai.

Komposisi SDM Perseroan berdasarkan jenjang jabatan, tingkat pendidikan, usia, dan jenis kelamin sebagai berikut: [G4-10] [G4-9]

Human capital is the most important assets that give large contribution to the Company in its effort to achieve excellence in business competition and deliver the best performance. The Company always regards human capital as the most substantial assets and partner to reach the Company's goal. They are also a key to the Company's success in delivering the best services to customers. With this in mind, the Company develops Human Capital Management System to empower our workforce.

## Strength of Waskita's Human Capital

The Company had 1,509 employees in 2016. This number increased by 15.10% compared to that of 2015 which was 1,311 employees. Increase in the number of HC was in line with the needs of HC from quantity aspect, as well as considering the increase in productivity of each employee.
