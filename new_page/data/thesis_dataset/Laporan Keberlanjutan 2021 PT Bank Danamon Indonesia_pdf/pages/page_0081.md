Sejalan dengan komitmen Bank dalam pengembangan kualitas karyawan, Direktorat Human Capital mengadakan program Danamon Bankers Trainee (DBT) bagi lulusan S1/S2 terbaik dan berbakat. Peserta DBT dipersiapkan untuk calon pemimpin Bank di masa depan. Pada tahun 2021, diselenggarakan empat batch, mencakup dua angkatan DBT, dengan total peserta sebanyak 103 orang.

Sebagai kesinambungan program tahun lalu, Danamon menyelenggarakan sosialisasi dan pelatihan penerapan pedoman ESRA. Pelatihan ini dimaksudkan untuk memperkenalkan dan mendalami pemahaman terkait analisa risiko lingkungan dan sosial dalam proses credit underwriting. Danamon memastikan karyawan

In line with the Bank's commitment to developing employee's quality, the Human Capital Directorate conducted the Danamon Bankers Trainee (DBT) program for the best and most talented Bachelor/Master degree graduates. DBT participants are prepared as future leaders in the Bank, and in 2021, four batches were held, including two DBT batches, with a total of 103 participants.

As a continuity of last year's program, Danamon organized socialization and training on the ESRA Guideline to introduce and explore an understanding of environmental and social risk analysis in the Credit Underwriting process. Danamon ensured the employees had sufficient knowledge and capacity before ESRA
