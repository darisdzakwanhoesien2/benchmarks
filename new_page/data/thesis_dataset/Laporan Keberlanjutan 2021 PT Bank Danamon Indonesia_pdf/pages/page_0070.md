2021 Sustainability Report

# Kinerja Sosial

## Social Performance

Bank mengembangkan program tanggung jawab sosial (corporate social responsibility/“CSR”) secara berkesinambungan untuk pemangku kepentingan internal dan eksternal. Program tanggung jawab sosial internal ditujukan kepada karyawan Bank. Adapun program tanggung jawab sosial eksternal ditujukan kepada nasabah dan masyarakat.

Komitmen CSR Danamon kepada karyawan antara lain berupa menyediakan lingkungan kerja yang aman dan nyaman. Selain itu, secara konsisten, Bank berupaya mengembangkan dan meningkatkan potensi dan kapabilitas karyawan.

Komitmen CSR Danamon kepada nasabah dan masyarakat diwujudkan melalui pengembangan layanan dan produk perbankan yang setara dan inklusif berupa informasi program, serta informasi layanan dan produk fitur. Danamon juga memberikan kemudahan akses informasi melalui berbagai sarana komunikasi, seperti website, e-mail, brosur, maupun media lain.

## KETENAGAKERJAAN

Karyawan merupakan salah satu aset terpenting perusahaan yang harus dikelola secara berkesinambungan. Karyawan yang profesional, kompeten, dan berintegritas merupakan salah satu faktor penting kesuksesan Danamon. Divisi Learning, Engagement &amp; Corporate University bertanggung jawab dalam pengembangan kapasitas dan kapabilitas karyawan.

Danamon menjamin hak dan kewajiban seluruh karyawan sesuai dengan peraturan dan ketentuan Bank yang berlaku. Danamon menjamin kesetaraan kesempatan berkarir dan segala praktik non-diskriminatif dalam proses pengembangan karyawan. Peraturan ketenagakerjaan tertera dalam Kebijakan Human Capital.

The Bank has continued to develop Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) programs for internal and external stakeholders. Social Responsibility Programs for internal are aimed to employees, and external programs are for customers and the communities.

CSR Danamon’s commitment to employees includes providing a safe and comfortable work environment. In addition, the Bank consistently looks to develop and increase the employee’s potential and capability.

CSR Danamon’s commitment to customers and the communities is realized through developing equal and inclusive banking services and products, includes program information, moreover information on product services and features. Danamon also provides easy information communication access through websites, e-mails, brochures, and other media.

## EMPLOYMENT

Employee is one of the most important company assets and it must be managed sustainably. Professional, competent, and integrity employees are key factors for Danamon’s success. The Learning, Engagement &amp; Corporate University Division is responsible for developing the employee’s capacity and capability.

Danamon guarantees all employees the rights and obligations in accordance with applicable Bank rules and regulations. Danamon guarantees equal career opportunities and no discriminatory practices in the employee development process. Employment regulations are stated in the Human Capital Policy.
