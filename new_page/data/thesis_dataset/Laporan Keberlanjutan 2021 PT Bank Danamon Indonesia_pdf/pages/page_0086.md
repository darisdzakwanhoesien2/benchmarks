2021 Sustainability Report

Kegiatan literasi keuangan yang dilaksanakan tahun 2021 antara lain: [FN-CB-240a.4]

1. Danamon Financial Friday. Sepanjang 2021, ada 91 episode dengan berbagai topik seperti pengelolaan keuangan pribadi dan usaha, produk-produk investasi dan cara pemilihannya, hingga pemilihan aset potensial, yang ditayangkan melalui kanal Youtube Danamon.
2. Employee Financial Wellness Festival melibatkan MUFG dan Adira baik untuk narasumber maupun peserta. Selain ilmu investasi dan pengelolaan keuangan secara umum, peserta festival diperkenalkan secara mendetail produk dan jasa keuangan yang dikeluarkan oleh Danamon Grup. Festival ini juga diharapkan dapat meningkatkan product knowledge dan self-belonging karyawan.
3. Danamon juga berkolaborasi dengan salah satu organisasi nirlaba untuk memberikan pelatihan pengelolaan keuangan di bidang pertanian kepada 200 petani yang ada di tiga lokasi yaitu di Jawa Tengah, Sumatera Barat, dan Nusa Tenggara Barat. Tenggara Barat. [FS13]FS14
4. Di penghujung tahun Danamon menyelenggarakan Virtual Journalist Class untuk mitra-mitra jurnalis Danamon yang mengangkat tema "Danamon Berbagi Ilmu: Membangun Bankir Berkualitas dan Berjiwa Pemimpin."

Financial literacy activities carried out in 2021 included: [FN-CB-240a.4]

1. Danamon Financial Friday. During 2021, there were 91 episodes covering various topics such as personal and business financial management, investment products and selection methods, to selecting potential assets, all aired through the YouTube Danamon channel.
2. The Employee Financial Wellness Festival involving MUFG and Adira as speakers and participants. In addition to investment and financial management in general, festival participants were introduced to Danamon Group products and financial services. The festival was also expected to increase employees' product knowledge and self-belonging.
3. Danamon also collaborated with non-profit organizations to provide agriculture financial management training for 200 farmers in three locations in Central Java, West Sumatra and West Nusa Tenggara. [FS13]FS14
4. At the end of the year, Danamon organized Virtual Journalist Classes for Danamon journalist partners with the theme "Danamon Berbagi Ilmu: Membangun Bankir Berkualitas dan Berjiwa Pemimpin."
