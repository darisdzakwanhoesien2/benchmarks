# Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3)

Occupational Health and Safety (OHS)

# Pendekatan Manajemen [3-3]

Walaupun perbankan bukan merupakan industri dengan potensi risiko kecelakaan kerja tinggi, K3 karyawan tetap menjadi perhatian Danamon. Saat ini, Danamon belum memiliki system manajemen K3 serta komite khusus K3. Namun demikian, implementasi K3 Danamon mengacu pada kebijakan Pemerintah terkait isu kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja yang berlaku, yaitu UU No 1/1970;PP No. 50/2012; Peraturan BI No.11/25/PBI/2009; POJK No. 18/POJK.03/2016; POJK No. 51/POJK.03/2017. Bank telah memiliki ketentuan mengenai fasilitas kesehatan karyawan serta panduan menghadapi kondisi pandemi yang dituangkan dalam bentuk SOP COVID-19 Danamon.

# Management Approach [3-3]

Although banking is not an industry with a high potential risk of work accidents, employee's OHS remains a concern for Danamon. Currently, Danamon does not yet have an OHS management system or a special OHS committee. However, Danamon's OHS refers to the Government's policies related to applicable occupational health and safety issues, including Law No 1/1970; PP No. 50/2012; BI Regulation No.11/25/PBI/2009; POJK No. 18/POJK.03/2016; POJK No. 51/POJK.03/2017. The Bank has instigated provisions for employee health facilities and guidelines for facing pandemic conditions in Danamon COVID-19 SOP.
