# Kinerja Lingkungan Hidup
## Environmental Performance

### KINERJA LINGKUNGAN DALAM AKTIVITAS OPERASIONAL DANAMON

Dukungan dalam upaya pelestarian lingkungan dikembangkan dalam beberapa program. Bank mengembangkan program keberlanjutan di internal Bank. Hal ini selaras dengan kebijakan dan budaya keberlanjutan Bank. Insan Danamon diberi kesempatan untuk berpartisipasi aktif dalam program ini. Selain itu, upaya pelestarian lingkungan diimplementasikan di sekitar wilayah kerja.

Danamon secara konsisten mensosialisasikan kebijakan dan budaya keberlanjutan melalui berbagai media komunikasi internal, seperti surel (email blast), pelatihan daring, dan lain-lain. Materi sosialisasi tersebut mengenai Good Practices on the Responsible Consumption, Saving Energy, Water and Paper, Earth Day, Global Warming &amp; Climate Change. Sosialisasi terus dilakukan secara berkala. Program sosialisasi dikembangkan dan di bawah koordinasi Divisi Corporate Real Estate Management (CREM) dan Divisi Sustainability Finance (SF).

Sebagai sarana meningkatkan partisipasi aktif insan Bank, pada tahun 2021 diselenggarakan lomba photo internal. Tema lomba diselaraskan dengan budaya berkelanjutan. Lomba photo internal 2021 mengangkat tema “peduli lingkungan”. Hal ini juga dimaksudkan agar karyawan dapat lebih memahami nilai-nilai keberlanjutan dalam aktivitas keseharian. Diharapkan karyawan dapat mengaplikasikan nilai-nilai keberlanjutan dalam kegiatan operasional Bank, sehingga visi dan misi keberlanjutan Bank dapat tercapai. Selain melakukan kegiatan pelestarian lingkungan di sekitar wilayah kerja dalam mengimplementasikan TJSL, Danamon melakukan upaya keberlanjutan di lingkungan internal kantor. Bank mengelola efisiensi penggunaan energi, kertas, dan air, di bawah koordinasi dan tanggung jawab Divisi Corporate Real Estate Management (CREM) dan Divisi Sustainability Finance (SF).

### DANAMON'S OPERATIONAL ACTIVITIES ENVIRONMENTAL PERFORMANCE

Environmental conservation support has been developed in several programs. The Bank has developed an internal sustainability program that harmonized with the Bank's sustainability policy and culture. Danamon employees are given the opportunity to actively participate in this program. In addition, environmental conservation efforts are implemented in the working areas.

Danamon consistently socializes its sustainability policies and culture through internal communication media such as email blast, online training, and others. The socialization materials cover Good Practices on the Responsible Consumption, Saving Energy, Water and Paper, Earth Day, Global Warming &amp; Climate Change. The socialization is carried out regularly. The socialization program was developed and coordinated by the Corporate Real Estate Management Division (CREM) and the Sustainability Finance (SF) Division,

To increase the active participation of the Bank's employees, in 2021 an internal photo competition was held. The competition theme was aligned with sustainable culture, with the 2021 Internal Photo Competition theme being “environmentally caring”. It is also intended so employees can better understand the sustainability values in their daily activities. It is hoped that the employees can apply sustainability values in the Bank's operational activities so that the Bank's sustainability vision and mission can be achieved. In addition to carrying out environmental conservation activities around the working areas when implementing CSR, Danamon pursues sustainability efforts in its offices. The Bank manages energy, paper and water efficiencies under the coordination and responsibility of the Corporate Real Estate Management (CREM) division and the Sustainability Finance (SF) Division.
