## RISIKO TERKAIT PERUBAHAN IKLIM

Salah satu risiko perubahan iklim yang dihadapi Bank adalah risiko fisik. Berdasarkan *incident report* yang masuk di 2021, terdapat 26 kejadian bencana alam (banjir, gempa bumi, erupsi, badai tropis siklon). Kejadian banjir yang terjadi sempat menyebabkan beberapa kantor cabang tutup yaitu:

a. Cabang Nabire Papua tutup selama tiga hari;
b. Cabang Sintang Kalimantan Barat tutup selama dua hari;
c. Cabang Panakkukang Makassar tutup selama dua hari

Namun demikian, Bank mencatat tidak ada dampak kerugian finansial dari penutupan kantor cabang. Hal ini merupakan hasil mitigasi persiapan lokasi back up untuk critical unit untuk melanjutkan proses kegiatan operasional yang tertunda akibat insiden/bencana. Penutupan cabang-cabang karena bencana/insiden telah dilaporkan ke OJK oleh Tim BI License Danamon kantor pusat. [201-2]

## CLIMATE CHANGE RELATED RISKS

One of the climate change risks faced by the Bank is physical risk. Based on the 2021 incident report there were 26 natural disaster events (floods, earthquakes, eruptions, tropical cyclones). The floods caused several branch offices to close, including:

a. Nabire Papua branch for three days;
b. Sintang West Kalimantan branch for two days;
c. Panakkukang Makassar branch for two days

However, the Bank suffered no financial losses from the closing of Branch offices. This was due to mitigation steps in place in back up locations for critical units to continue any operational activities delayed due to incidents/disasters. Branch closures due to disasters/incidents were reported to OJK by the Danamon BI License Team at Head Office. [201-2]

## Sistem Pelaporan Pelanggaran (Whistleblowing System)

### Whistleblowing System

Sistem Pelaporan Pelanggaran (Whistleblowing System/WBS) merupakan sarana komunikasi bagi pemangku kepentingan internal maupun eksternal, untuk menyampaikan atau melaporkan tindakan pelanggaran atau fraud yang dilakukan pelaku internal Bank. WBS merupakan upaya Bank untuk meminimalkan dan menghindari penyalahgunaan dan pelanggaran yang dapat menghambat proses bisnis. Dengan WBS, pelanggaran atau fraud dapat terdeteksi dan dicegah sedini mungkin melalui pengungkapan pelapor (whistleblower).

Bank memiliki kebijakan sistem pelaporan pelanggaran atau Whistleblowing System. Kebijakan tersebut dituangkan dalam Kebijakan Penanganan Fraud no. 1.8.0.0/COM/RC/2/2021, yang menjadi acuan penerapan WBS.

The Whistleblowing System (WBS) is a means of communication for internal and external stakeholders to convey or report acts of violations or fraud committed by the Bank's employees. The WBS is the Bank's effort to minimize and avoid misuse and violations that can hinder the business processes. With WBS, violations or fraud can be detected and prevented as early as possible through whistleblower disclosure.

The Bank has a policy of violation reporting system or Whistleblowing System. This policy is stated in the Fraud Handling Policy No. 1.8.0.0/COM/RC/2/2021, that is used as the reference for implementing the WBS.
