b. Rumah Sakit Darurat COVID-19

Bekerja sama dengan organisasi berbasis Syariah (Dompet Dhuafa), Danamon mendirikan tiga Rumah Sakit Darurat di Jakarta yakni Rumah Sakit Polri, Rumah Sakit Taman Sari, dan Rumah Sakit Budhi Asih. Fasilitas yang terdapat pada Rumah Sakit Darurat COVID-19 tersebut antara lain berupa tenda, tempat tidur, ventilator, air conditioner, cairan infus, Alat Pelindung Diri (APD), multivitamin, kursi roda, serta sarana pendukung lainnya.

c. Pemberian Asuransi bagi Relawan COVID-19 BNPB

Di bawah koordinasi Badan Nasional Penanggulangan Bencana (BNPB), di 2021 Danamon menyediakan 13.073 premi asuransi bagi Relawan COVID-19 BNPB yang disalurkan melalui BP Jamsostek. Bantuan ini diharapkan dapat memberikan dukungan dan ketenangan bagi relawan dan keluarganya. Premi asuransi ini sebagai bentuk perlindungan dari risiko jaminan kematian dan kecelakaan kerja, meliputi biaya perawatan dan pengobatan, transportasi, asuransi Sementara Tidak Mampu Bekerja (STMB), santunan cacat, santunan kematian, biaya rehabilitasi, dan bantuan beasiswa.

2. Bantuan non-COVID-19

Danamon menyalurkan bantuan bagi masyarakat yang terdampak musibah bencana alam di berbagai wilayah Indonesia, yakni: banjir di Banjarmasin, Sintang, Palangkaraya, dan Garut, badai siklon di Kupang, serta erupsi Semeru di Lumajang. Jenis bantuan antara lain berupa sembako, paket kesehatan, terpal, selimut, dan obat-obatan.

b. COVID-19 Emergency Hospital.

In collaboration with Sharia-Based Organizations (Dompet Dhuafa), Danamon established three emergency hospitals in Jakarta, the National Police Hospital, Taman Sari Hospital, and Budhi Asih Hospital. The facilities contained in these COVID-19 emergency hospital included tents, beds, ventilators, air conditioners, infusion fluids, personal-protection equipment (APD), multivitamins, wheelchairs, and other supporting facilities.

c. Provision of insurance for COVID-19 BNPB volunteers.

Under the coordination of the National Disaster Management Agency (BNPB), in 2021 Danamon provided 13,073 insurance premiums for COVID-19 BNPB volunteers who were channeled through BP Jamsostek. This assistance provided support and tranquility for volunteers and their families as the insurance premium was a form of protection against the risk of death and work accidents, and included the cost of care and treatment, transportation, temporary insurance if not able to work (STMB), disability compensation, death compensation, rehabilitation costs, and scholarship assistance.

2. Non-COVID-19 Supports

Danamon distributed assistance for natural disaster in various regions of Indonesia, including floods in Banjarmasin, Sintang, Palangkaraya, and Garut, Hurricane Cyclones in Kupang, and Semeru eruption in Lumajang. The assistance contained basic necessities, health packages, tarpaulin, blankets, and medicines.
