2021 Sustainability Report

Kami juga mendukung kontribusi Bank Danamon terhadap target Net Zero GHG Emission MUFG atas operasinya sendiri pada tahun 2030. Dukungan ini tercermin melalui berbagai inisiatif pengurangan jejak lingkungan, seperti pemantauan penggunaan dan efisiensi sumber daya.

Di tengah pendemi COVID-19, kami mengapresiasi adaptasi cepat Bank Danamon dalam melindungi dan memberikan pelayanan terbaik bagi nasabah melalui serangkaian inovasi pada layanan berbasis teknologi, serta penguatan infrastruktur informasi teknologi untuk mendukung kebijakan bekerja dari rumah bagi karyawan.

Kontribusi aktif Bank Danamon kepada masyarakat juga dilanjutkan melalui program-program pengembangan masyarakat dalam berbagai bidang seperti literasi keuangan, pendidikan, lingkungan dan kesehatan.

Atas nama Dewan Komisaris, saya mengucapkan terima kasih kepada seluruh pemangku kepentingan yang terlibat dalam komitmen keberlanjutan. Saya berharap Bank Danamon dapat menjadi panutan dalam menerapkan prinsip keuangan berkelanjutan melalui berbagai inovasi dan kolaborasi, serta dengan terus memberikan nilai tambah kepada para pemangku kepentingan.

We also support Bank Danamon's contribution to MUFG's target of Net Zero GHG Emissions on its own operations by 2030. This support is reflected through various environmental footprint initiatives, such as monitoring the usage and efficiency of resources.

Amid the COVID-19 pandemic, we appreciate Bank Danamon's agile adaptation to protect its customers and provide them with the best services through a series of innovations in technology-based customer services, as well as enhancing its IT infrastructure to support work-from-home policies for employees.

Bank Danamon's active contribution to society has also been continued through community development programs in areas such as financial literacy, education, the environment, and health care.

On behalf of the Board of Commissioners, I would like to thank all stakeholders involved in the sustainability commitments. I hope Bank Danamon becomes a role model in applying sustainable finance principles through its innovations and collaborations, as well as by continuing to provide added value to stakeholders.
