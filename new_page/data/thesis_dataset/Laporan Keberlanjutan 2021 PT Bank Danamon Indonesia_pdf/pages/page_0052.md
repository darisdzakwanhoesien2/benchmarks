## 2. KESADARAN KARYAWAN DALAM MENERAPKAN KEUANGAN BERKELANJUTAN

Bank secara konsisten mengembangkan program peningkatan kapabilitas karyawan terkait literasi keuangan dan keuangan berkelanjutan melalui pelatihan dan sharing session. Danamon juga mengembangkan materi pelatihan internal terkait keuangan berkelanjutan. Hal ini dilakukan agar karyawan Danamon dapat memahami praktik terbaik dalam penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan secara inklusif.

## 2. EMPLOYEE AWARENESS IN APPLYING SUSTAINABLE FINANCE

The Bank consistently develops employee capability improvement programs related to financial literacy and sustainable finance through training and sharing sessions. Danamon also develops its sustainable finance internal training materials. This is to ensure Danamon employees understand the best practices in applying sustainable finance in an inclusive manner.
