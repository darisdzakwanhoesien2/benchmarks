# Merk, Produk, Jasa, Layanan, dan Pasar Terlayani [2-6]

Brand, Product, Services, and Market Served [2-6]

Danamon bergerak di sektor perbankan dengan produk antara lain tabungan, kredit modal kerja dan kredit investasi untuk korporasi serta UKM, investments &amp; retail treasury services, e-banking, kartu kredit, bancassurance, kredit kepemilikan rumah, kredit kendaraan bermotor, kredit tanpa agunan, trade finance, cash management, dan produk keuangan Syariah. Danamon memiliki rangkaian produk dan layanan perbankan yang dipersembahkan bagi nasabah dari berbagai kalangan.

Danamon memiliki 864 cabang yang terdiri dari kantor cabang konvensional, unit Syariah dan kantor-kantor cabang perusahaan anak, dalam rangka merespon perkembangan kebutuhan nasabah dan masyarakat. Informasi lebih detail terkait produk dan layanan Danamon dapat dibaca pada Laporan Tahunan 2021, yang juga dapat diakses melalui korporasi www.danamon.co.id/.

Danamon is engaged in the banking sector delivering products such as savings, working capital and investment loan for corporate and SME, investments &amp; retail treasury services, e-banking, credit cards, bancassurance, mortgages, automotive financing, unsecured loans, trade finance, cash management, and Sharia financial products. Danamon has a range of banking products and services that are offered to customers from all walks of life.

Danamon has 864 branches consisting of conventional branch offices, Sharia units and Subsidiary branch offices, to respond to the developing customer and community needs. More detailed information on Danamon's products and services can be found in the 2021 Annual Report, which can also be accessed through the corporate website www.danamon.co.id/.
