# KONTRIBUSI DAN DISTRIBUTSI EKONOMI

Pada 2021, Indonesia mulai mengalami perbaikan ekonomi dibanding tahun 2020, didukung oleh tingkat suku bunga yang rendah, berbagai stimulus dari pemerintah, harga komoditas ekspor yang naik dan juga gerakan vaksinasi massal. Pemulihan ekonomi ini terlihat dari pertumbuhan GDP sebesar $3,7\%$ YoY di tahun 2021. Permintaan atas kendaraan bermotor mengalami pemulihan dibanding tahun lalu, walaupun lebih kecil dari estimasi awal dikarenakan terjadinya kekurangan supply komponen elektronik secara global dan Pemberlakuan Pembatasan Kegiatan Masyarakat (PPKM) yang ketat di bulan Juli dan Agustus. Permintaan fasilitas kredit juga mengalami tren yang sama, dimana pemulihan yang terjadi sempat terhambat di pertengahan tahun akibat penyebaran varian Delta.

One of the performance management strategies is to collaborate with MUFG, where Danamon provides services to MUFG customers and their ecosystem in the areas of financial supply chain, payroll, and cash management.

By collaborating with MUFG and ADMF, and its digital investment, Danamon continues to deliver a good performance. Danamon has also continued its consumer customer segmentation initiatives with different value propositions for Danamon Privilege, Danamon Optimal (Emerging Affluent) and Middle Market customers.

Danamon allocates its resources to manage its economic performance, including information technology and HC. The Internal Audit Division (SKAI) consistently and periodically conducts internal audits of the divisions and work units, regional and branch offices to evaluate the strategies and processes as well as target achievements. In addition, external audits are carried out regularly by Public Accountants. External audit results can be seen on the Danamon website, www.danamon.co.id

# ECONOMIC CONTRIBUTION AND DISTRIBUTION

In 2021, Indonesia began to experience economic improvements compared to 2020, thanks to low interest rates, various stimulus from the Government, rising export commodity prices, and also the mass vaccination movement. This economic recovery can be seen in the GDP growth of $3.7\%$ YoY in 2021. Demand for motor vehicles also recovered compared to last year, although smaller than the initial estimate due to a global shortage of electronic components supplies and the strict Community Activity Restrictions (PPKM) in July and August. Demand for loans also experienced the same trend, where the recovery was hampered in the middle of the year due to the spread of the Delta variant.
