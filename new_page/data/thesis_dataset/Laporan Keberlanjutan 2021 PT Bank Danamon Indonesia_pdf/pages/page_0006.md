2021 Sustainability Report

Dengan mengacu pada Kebijakan Keberlanjutan dan Delapan Prinsip Keuangan Berkelanjutan, Danamon meneruskan penerapan lima (5) program prioritas yang tertuang dalam Rencana Aksi Keuangan Berkelanjutan (RAKB). RAKB dikaji ulang setiap tahun secara konsisten dan berkesinambungan sebagai acuan pelaksanaan keuangan berkelanjutan dan upaya mendukung pembangunan berkelanjutan di Indonesia.

By referring to the Sustainability Policy and the Eight Sustainable Finance Principles, Danamon has continued its five (5) priority programs contained in the Sustainable Finance Action Plan (SFAP). The SFAP is reviewed every year consistently and continuously as a guide for implementing sustainable finance and the efforts required to support sustainable development in Indonesia.

Pada Maret 2021, Danamon mulai mengimplementasikan pedoman dan kerangka kerja Manajemen Risiko Lingkungan dan Sosial (Environmental and Social Risk Management/“ESRM”) atau disebut Sustainability Credit, dan Analisa Risiko Lingkungan dan Sosial (Environmental and Social Risks Analysis/“ESRA”). Implementasi dilakukan untuk Enterprise Banking khususnya segmen Corporate dan Commercial. Pedoman disetujui melalui Rapat Direksi pada bulan November 2020. Pedoman dan kerangka kerja ESRM dan ESRA merupakan perwujudan komitmen Bank dalam meningkatkan portofolio kredit yang berwawasan Lingkungan, Sosial, dan Tata Kelola (LST).

In March 2021, Danamon started to implement the guideline and framework of Environmental and Social Risk Management ("ESRM") or called Sustainability Credit, and Environmental and Social Risks Analysis ("ESRA"). The implementation is done for Enterprise Banking especially Corporate and Commercial segments. The guidelines were approved through the Board of Directors' Meeting in November 2020. The ESRM and ESRA guidelines and framework demonstrates the Bank's commitment to increase the Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG-based) loan portfolio.
