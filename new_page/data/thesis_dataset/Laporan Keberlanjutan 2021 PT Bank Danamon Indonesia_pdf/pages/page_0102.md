Data penggunaan listrik berasal dari Menara Bank Danamon (MBD). Penyajian ulang data tahun 2019 dan 2020 terkait perubahan sumber standar konversi dengan merujuk International Energy Agency (IEA) https://www.iea.org/reports/unit-converter-and-glossary.
Electricity usage data comes from Menara Bank Danamon (MBD). Restatement of 2019 and 2020 data related to changes of the standard conversion source by referring to the International Energy Agency (IEA) https://www.ieaa.org/reports/unit-converter-and-glossary.

Program efisiensi listrik di Menara Bank Danamon (MBD) yang telah dilakukan selama ini, antara lain: [302-4]

1. Kampanye hemat listrik;
2. Pemasangan motion censor, lampu akan hidup ketika ada pergerakan. Upaya ini menghasilkan efisiensi penggunaan listrik sebesar 60.000 kWh;
3. Monitoring penggunaan chiller;
4. Penggunaan lampu LED;
5. Pola kerja dan pengaturan kerja selama pandemi COVID-19.

Divisi Corporate Real Estate Management (CREM) meninjau program efisiensi listrik secara berkala. Ke depan, program tidak hanya difokuskan di Menara Bank Danamon, namun akan diimplementasikan di kantor regional dan cabang. Implementasi dilakukan secara bertahap.

Electrical efficiency programs in Menara Bank Danamon (MBD) that have been made to date, include: [302-4]

1. Electricity-saving campaigns;
2. Installation of motion censors, where lights go on when there is a movement. This effort resulted in electricity usage efficiency of 60,000 kWh;
3. Monitoring the use of chillers;
4. Use of LED lights;
5. Work patterns and job settings during COVID-19 pandemic.

Corporate Real Estate Management (CREM) Division regularly reviews the electrical efficiency programs. In the future this program will not only apply in Menara Bank Danamon, but also will be gradually rolled-out to regional and branch offices.
