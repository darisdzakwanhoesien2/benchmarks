2021 Sustainability Report

Namun demikian, Bank tetap mendukung upaya pelestarian lingkungan dan keanekaragaman hayati, terutama melalui penerapan pedoman Sustainability Credit dan ESRA dalam proses credit underwriting.

## PENGADUAN LINGKUNGAN HIDUP

Danamon berupaya untuk selalu mematuhi regulasi terkait lingkungan hidup yang berlaku di Indonesia. Sepanjang tahun 2021, Danamon tidak menerima pengaduan terkait lingkungan hidup.

However, the Bank supports environmental and biodiversity conservation efforts, especially through Sustainability Credit and ESRA guidelines implementation in the credit underwriting process.

## ENVIRONMENTAL COMPLAINTS

Danamon complies with the prevailing environmental regulation in Indonesia. Throughout 2021, Danamon did not receive any complaints related to the environment.
