Danamon menyadari bahwa program-program yang dilakukan harus memiliki dampak yang berkelanjutan. Oleh karena itu Danamon dan mitra melakukan duala tahapan pada prosesnya, yakni Persiapan Komunitas dan Konstruksi. Dalam tahapan Persiapan Komunitas, mitra Danamon melakukan Survey and Assessment, Program Dissemination and Needs Identification, Establishment of the Local Committee, Finalize Beneficiaries List, dan Social Contract. Tahapan-tahapan tersebut dilakukan untuk mencapai tujuan program yaitu meningkatkan kesehatan, ketangguhan, dan kesejahteraan masyarakat sebagai penerima manfaat.

Danamon understands that these programs must have a sustainable impact. Therefore Danamon and its partners take a two-stage approach in the process, namely community preparation and construction. In the community preparation stage, Danamon Partners conducted a Survey and Assessment, Program Dissemination and Needs Identification, Establishment of the Local Committee, Finalize Beneficiaries List, and Social Contract. These stages are aimed for the program objectives, namely improving health, toughness, and community welfare in the beneficiaries.
