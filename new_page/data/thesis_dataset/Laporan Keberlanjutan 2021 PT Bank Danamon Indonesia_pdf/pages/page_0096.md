## Penanganan Pengaduan Terkait Data Nasabah

Danamon terbuka akan masukan dan pengaduan dari nasabah, masyarakat dan para pemangku kepentingan. Pengaduan keamanan data nasabah dapat bersifat umum maupun spesifik. Pengaduan dapat disampaikan melalui Hello Danamon atau datang langsung ke kantor cabang. Selama tahun 2021, tidak terdapat pengaduan terkait pengelolaan data nasabah. Danamon senantiasa melakukan pembaharuan untuk memastikan keamanan data terhindar dari potensi kebocoran data (data leakage). [418-1][FN-CB-230a.1]

## KEAMANAN PRODUK [416-1]

Informasi terkait layanan dan produk Bank dilakukan secara konsisten dan transparan, mencakup juga mengenai risiko. Edukasi produk dan layanan dilakukan melalui berbagai media komunikasi, seperti materi promosi, dan situs website Bank. Pemahaman akan produk dan layanan perbankan yang baik dapat meminimalkan kesalahan penggunaan.

Bank memastikan layanan dan produk perbankan yang akan diluncurkan ke masyarakat, telah melalui proses yang sesuai dengan kebijakan dan prosedur internal. Serta telah memenuhi regulasi dan atau mendapat persetujuan dari regulator.

b. Security/protection from data leakage, includes: (i) deactivating USB ports, (ii) running disk encryption, (iii) limiting access to external e-mail and internet access, and (iv) applying DLP (Data Leakage Prevention) that will send an alert If there is data delivery that does not follow the permissible rules.

## Handling of Complaints Related to Customer Data

Danamon is open to inputs and complaints from customers, community, and stakeholders. Customer data security complaints can be general or specific, and can be submitted via Hello Danamon or by directly going to the branch offices. In 2021, there were no complaints related to customer data management. Danamon updates its data security to ensure there is no data leakage. [418-1][FN-CB-230a.1]

## PRODUCT SAFETY [416-1]

Information related to the Bank's services and products is carried out consistently and transparently, including the risk. Products and service education is delivered through communication media, such as promotional material and the Bank's website. A good understanding of banking products and services can minimize errors.

The Bank ensures its banking services and products that will be launched to the community pass through processes that are in accordance with internal policies and procedures, and have fulfilled regulations and/or received approval from the regulator.
