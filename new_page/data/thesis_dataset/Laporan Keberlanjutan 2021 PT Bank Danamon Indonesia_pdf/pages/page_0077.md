# PROTOKOL KESEHATAN DI MASA ADAPTASI KEBIASAAN BARU

Untuk memastikan lingkungan kerja Bank Danamon aman dari paparan virus COVID-19, Crisis Command Center (CCC) Danamon melakukan tindakan pencegahan dan penanganan COVID-19 sebagai berikut: [403-4][403-6]

- Menerapkan split operation untuk menghindari penularan virus dalam satu area kerja sehingga operasional tetap dapat berjalan jika ada karyawan yang terpapar virus di salah satu lokasi di atas. Strategi split operation yaitu dengan membagi unit kerja dengan pilihan berikut:
- Bekerja di Kantor (WFO)
- Bekerja di lokasi utama
- Bekerja di lokasi back up/split operation
- Bekerja di Rumah (WFH)

- Menyediakan thermo scanner, thermo gun, barcode PeduliLindungi dan face recognition saat masuk kantor, khusus gedung kantor pusat dan regional

- Menyediakan alat kesehatan seperti hand sanitizer di area kantor
- Pembagian vitamin dan masker bagi seluruh karyawan
- Mewajibkan karyawan untuk menggunakan masker dan menggantinya selama 4 jam sekali
- Jaga jarak tempat kerja minimal 1,5 meter

- Tidak melakukan meeting tatap muka
- Melakukan penyemprotan disinfektan secara rutin/ pengadaan UV lamp disinfektan di gedung kantor
- Mewajibkan seluruh karyawan baik tetap, kontrak maupun mitra yang bekerja di Bank Danamon untuk mengisi survei harian dan mingguan melalui Aplikasi Danamon Care
- Menyediakan tes rapid/antigen/Genose/PCR kepada karyawan yang mengalami gejala COVID-19 atau ada kontak erat dengan orang terkonfirmasi positif baik di kantor maupun di luar kantor
- Menyediakan rawat inap di RS rujukan, hotel rekanan dan kampus Ciawi untuk isolasi mandiri bagi karyawan yang hasil PCR positif
- Menyediakan obat-obatan bagi yang melakukan isoman melalui tele medicine
- Menyelenggarakan vaksinasi untuk seluruh karyawan dan keluarga serta masyarakat umum dalam rangka mendukung program pemerintah

# HEALTH PROTOCOLS TO ADAPT TO THE NEW NORMAL PERIOD

To ensure Bank Danamon's work environment was safe from any exposure to the COVID-19 virus, Danamon's Crisis Command Center (CCC) instigated the following precautions for handling COVID-19: [403-4][403-6]

- Implemented a split operation to avoid transmission of the virus in one work area so that operations could continue if any employee is exposed to the virus in one of the locations. The work units' split operation strategy wer divided as follows:
- Work from Office (WFO)
- Work in prime locations
- Work in backup/split operation locations
- Work from Home (WFH)

- Providing thermal scanners, thermo guns, Care Protect barcodes and face recognition when entering the offices, specifically the head office buildings and regional offices
- Providing medical equipment such as hand sanitizer in the office area
- Distributing vitamins and masks for all employees
- Requiring employees to wear masks and replace them every 4 hours.
- Maintaining a minimum distance of 1.5 meters in the workplace
- No face-to-face meetings
- Regularly spraying disinfectant, procuring UV disinfecting lamps in office buildings
- Requiring all employees, whether permanent, contract or partners working at Bank Danamon, to complete daily and weekly surveys through the Danamon Care Application
- Providing rapid/antigen/Genose/PCR testing for employees who experience symptoms of COVID-19, or have had close contact with confirmed positive people both in the office and outside the office
- Providing inpatient care at referral hospitals, partner hotels and the Ciawi campus for self-isolation for employees whose PCR results were positive
- Providing medicine for those self-isolating through telemedicine
- Organizing vaccinations for all employees and their families as well as the general public to support the Government programs
