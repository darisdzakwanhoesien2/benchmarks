# Penilaian Kinerja Karyawan dan Persiapan Masa Pensiun

Penilaian kinerja karyawan berdasar pada prinsip objektivitas dan kesetaraan, serta dilakukan dengan menganalisa capaian Key Performance Indicator (KPI) setiap karyawan. Penilaian dilaporkan secara berjenjang sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku. Penilaian performa kerja dilaksanakan sebanyak dua kali dalam satu tahun. Evaluasi dan penilaian kerja merupakan salah satu sarana untuk memotivasi peningkatan performa kerja dari setiap karyawan. Penilaian kinerja menjadi salah satu pertimbangan dalam proses promosi. Pada tahun 2021, sebanyak 8.306 karyawan (atau 94% dari total karyawan) menjalani penilaian kinerja (performa appraisal). Selain itu sebanyak 614 karyawan mendapatkan promosi jabatan. 53% dari karyawan yang mendapatkan promosi jabatan adalah perempuan. [404-3]

Bank mengembangkan program persiapan masa purnabakti ditujukan bagi karyawan yang telah memasuki masa pensiun. Program berupa pembekalan dan pelatihan. Beberapa topik pelatihan, antara lain pengelolaan keuangan, peluang usaha setelah pensiun, dan kesehatan. Peserta pelatihan juga berkesempatan untuk berkunjung ke tempat usaha kecil. Selain program persiapan masa pensiun, Danamon memberikan fasilitas lain bagi karyawan, seperti asuransi BPJS Ketenagakerjaan, jaminan kecelakaan kerja, jaminan hari tua, dan jaminan kematian. # Employee Performance Assessment and Retirement Preparation

Twice-yearly employee performance appraisals are carried out based on the objectivity and equality principles, and analyze each employee's Key Performance Indicators (KPI) achievements. They are reported in stages in line with prevailing regulations. Job evaluations and appraisals help to motivate each employee's work performance improvement. The performance appraisals are considered in the promotion process. In 2021, 8,306 employees (94% of the total employees) underwent a performance appraisal, followed by 614 employees being promoted. 53% of the employees promoted were women. [404-3]

The Bank has developed a retirement preparation program, intended for employees reaching retirement age. The program involves debriefings and training, with the training topics covering financial management, business opportunities after retirement, and health. Training participants are also given the opportunity to visit small businesses. As well as the retirement preparation program, Danamon provides other facilities for employees, including BPJS Employment insurance, work accident insurance, retirement insurance, and death insurance.
