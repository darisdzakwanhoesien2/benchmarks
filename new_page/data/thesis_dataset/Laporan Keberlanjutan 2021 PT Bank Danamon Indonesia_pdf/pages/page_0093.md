Laporan Keberlanjutan 2021

Bank memberi perhatian atas masukan dan saran yang disampaikan masyarakat serta nasabah. Hal tersebut akan menjadi salah satu pertimbangan dalam pengembangan layanan dan produk perbankan Danamon.

Selama tahun 2021, terdapat 12.605 pengaduan yang diterima Bank dan seluruh pengaduan dapat terselesaikan dengan baik. [418-1]

The Bank pays attention to the community and customers' input and suggestions. This will be used in the development of Danamon's banking services and products.

During 2021, there were 12,605 complaints received by the Bank and all complaints were resolved properly. [418-1]
