# SURVEI KEPUASAN NASABAH

Survei kepuasan nasabah merupakan salah satu media interaksi dengan telah satu pemangku kepentingan. Danamon secara konsisten melaksanakan survei kepuasan nasabah. Survei dilakukan setiap tahun melalui Customer Engagement Survey. Nasabah dapat menyampaikan masukan, saran dan pendapat terkait dengan layanan dan produk Bank. Hasil survei dipergunakan sebagai acuan untuk meningkatkan standar layanan. Selain itu, hasil survei dapat membantu Bank untuk mengetahui kebutuhan dan kepuasan nasabah atas produk, layanan, serta loyalitas nasabah pada Danamon.

Customer Engagement Survey terdiri dari beberapa aspek penilaian. Danamon menggunakan Customer Engagement Score (CE Score) dengan skala skor 1 menunjukkan sangat tidak puas dan skor 5 menunjukkan sangat puas. Hasil Customer Engagement Survey 2021, Danamon memperoleh skor 4,17, mengalami penurunan  $2\%$  dari tahun lalu (skor: 4,24). Hal yang perlu ditingkatkan terutama pada aspek penilaian "Bank selalu memenuhi janji" dan "Bank sempurna untuk saya".

# CUSTOMER SATISFACTION SURVEY

Customer satisfaction survey is one of the interactive media with the stakeholders. Danamon regularly carries out customer satisfaction survey, at least once a year through Customer Engagement Survey. Customers can provide input, make suggestions and opinions related to Bank services and products. The survey result is used as a reference for improving service standards. In addition, the survey result can help the Bank to know the customers' needs and satisfaction with products, services and loyalty to Danamon.

Customer Engagement Survey covers several assessment aspects. Danamon uses a Customer Engagement Score (CE Score) with a score scale 1 as very dissatisfied, and a score 5 as very satisfied. The score of 2021 Danamon Customer Engagement Survey Result is 4.17, decrease by  $2\%$  from previous year (score: 4.24). Things that need to be improved is the assessment aspects of "The bank always fulfills its promises" and "The bank is perfect for me".
