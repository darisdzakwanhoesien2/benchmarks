# Pelaksanaan Pelatihan dan Pengembangan Karyawan

Metode pelaksanaan pelatihan dan pengembangan karyawan dilakukan dengan sistem belajar di dalam kelas, online learning (e-learning), on the job training, penugasan khusus, pendampingan, dan kesempatan belajar dari orang lain. Implementasi metode disesuaikan dengan kebutuhan dan tujuan pelatihan.

Menyikapi kondisi selama pandemi COVID-19, program pelatihan dan pengembangan dilaksanakan dengan mengoptimalisasi penerapan teknologi. Seluruh program pelatihan atau sebanyak 100% dilaksanakan secara daring melalui online training maupun e-learning. Tercatat sebanyak 778 pelatihan diselenggarakan sepanjang tahun 2021, dengan total dana yang diinvestasikan untuk program pelatihan mencapai Rp 85,63 miliar. Sebagai tambahan, pada tahun 2021, pengembangan e-learning mengenai Sustainability tahap kedua telah selesai.

# Employee Training and Development

The employee training and development are adjusted to the training needs and objectives and includes a classroom learning system, online learning (e-learning), on the job training, special assignments, mentoring, and learning from others. The method implementation is adjusted with training needs and goals.

In response to the COVID-19 pandemic conditions, training and development programs optimized by using the technology. 100% of the training was carried out online through online training and e-learning. In 2021, 778 training sessions were held, and the total funds invested for training was Rp 85.63 billion. In additions, the second phase of e-learning development of Sustainability was completed in 2021.
