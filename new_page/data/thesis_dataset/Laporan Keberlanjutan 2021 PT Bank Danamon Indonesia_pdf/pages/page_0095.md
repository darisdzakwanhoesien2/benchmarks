Laporan Keberlanjutan 2021

Seluruh karyawan Bank bertanggung jawab atas perlindungan kerahasiaan dan keamanan data nasabah maupun Bank secara keseluruhan, sebagaimana diatur di dalam ketentuan internal Bank yang mengacu pada ketentuan dari regulator.

Sosialisasi keamanan data nasabah dilakukan secara konsisten kepada segenap karyawan Bank. Edukasi dilakukan melalui beberapa sarana, seperti broadcast email, simulasi dan edukasi dengan e-learning. Hal ini sebagai upaya untuk memastikan pemahaman mengenai keamanan data nasabah dan penggunaan informasi yang benar.

Danamon mengalokasikan sumber daya manusia dan teknologi untuk mengelola kerahasiaan dan keamanan data, sebagai berikut:

1. Sumber Daya Manusia
a. Unit kerja IT Security, Control &amp; Governance Function dalam Divisi Information Technology (IT) yang bertanggung jawab menetapkan kebijakan IT, Standar Security dan Prosedur pengelolaan keamanan IT.
b. Unit kerja Information Risk Management dalam Divisi Risk Management Bank yang bertanggungjawab terhadap penetapan kebijakan keamanan informasi.
c. Divisi Operational Risk Management yang bertanggungjawab terhadap penetapan kebijakan risiko operasional Bank.
d. Divisi Compliance yang mengelola kepatuhan bank terhadap regulasi yang berasal dari regulator.
e. Satuan Kerja Audit Intern yang bertanggungjawab memeriksa kepatuhan unit kerja terhadap peraturan dan ketentuan terkait kerahasiaan dan keamanan data nasabah.

2. Teknologi
a. Mekanisme pembatasan pemberian akses data sesuai kebutuhan (least privilege) sehingga orang yang tidak memiliki otoritas tidak bisa mengakses sistem, fungsi, maupun data.

All Bank employees are responsible for protecting the confidentiality and security of customer data and the Bank as a whole, as stipulated in the Bank's internal provisions that refer to the regulator's provisions.

Socialization on customer data security is carried out consistently to all Bank employees. This education is done through several channels such as broadcast emails, simulations and education through e-learning. This is to ensure an understanding of customer data security and the correct use of information.

Danamon allocates human resources and technology to manage data confidentiality and security, as follows:

1. Human Resources
a. IT Security, Control &amp; Governance Working Unit in the Information Technology (IT) Division is responsible for establishing IT policies, Security standards and IT Security Management Procedures.
b. Information Risk Management Working Unit in the Bank's Risk Management Division is responsible for establishing the information security policies.
c. Operational Risk Management Division is responsible for establishing the Bank's operational risk policy.
d. Compliance Division manages the Bank's compliance with regulator regulations.
e. Internal Audit Working Unit is responsible for examining the working units' compliance with regulations and provisions related to confidentiality and security of customer data.

2. Technology
a. The mechanism for limiting data access as needed (least privilege) so that people who do not have the authority cannot access the system, function, and data.
