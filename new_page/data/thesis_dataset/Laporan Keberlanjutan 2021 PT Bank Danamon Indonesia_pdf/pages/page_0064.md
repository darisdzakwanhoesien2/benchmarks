Untuk meminimalkan dampak terkait portofolio produk atau kredit terkait LST, Danamon memiliki daftar industri yang dilarang, permintaan pemenuhan dokumen lingkungan dari debitur dan dilakukannya analisis risiko lingkungan dan sosial dalam proses credit underwriting untuk beberapa sektor industri tertentu.

# Pedoman Sustainability Credit dan Pedoman ESRA

Pengelolaan portofolio kredit berwawasan LST Danamon telah dilengkapi dengan Pedoman Sustainability Credit yang meliputi ESRM dan Pedoman Analisa Risiko Lingkungan dan Sosial (ESRA) dalam proses kredit. Pedoman ini disusun pada tahun 2020 dan mulai diimplementasikan pada Maret 2021 untuk Enterprise Banking (EB), khususnya segmen Corporate dan Commercial. Pedoman ini berlaku untuk pinjaman baru dan tambahan yang mempunyai risiko LST yang signifikan. Pedoman berisi serangkaian prosedur untuk mengidentifikasi, mengevaluasi, memitigasi, dan mengompensasi risiko LST inheren industri debitur. Langkah ini dilakukan untuk mengidentifikasi aktivitas/ proyek debitur berdasarkan risiko lingkungan dan sosial serta kepatuhan debitur terhadap regulasi lingkungan dan sosial. [SASB FN-CB-410a.1]

Currently, there have been no specific complaints received concerning the product portfolio or ESG loan aspects. However, complaints can be submitted to the bankwide customer complaint handling working unit.

The ESG-based loan portfolio management is the responsibility of all lines of business that work with the Credit Team and the Integrated Risk Directorate, and they are supervised by the Business Director, Credit Director and the Integrated Risk Director through the Sustainability Committee.

To minimize any impact from the ESG product or loan portfolio, Danamon maintains a list of prohibited industries, requests for fulfillments of environmental documents from debtors, and conducts environmental and social risk analysis on the credit underwriting processes for certain industrial sectors.

Sustainability Credit Guidelines and ESRA Guidelines Danamon's ESG-based loan portfolio management has been equipped by Sustainability Credit guidelines which include ESRM and Environmental and Social Risk Analysis (ESRA) Guideline for credit process. These guidelines were developed in 2020 and implementation started in March 2021 for Enterprise Banking (EB), in particular the Corporate and Commercial segments. The guidelines are applicable for new and additional loans with a significant ESG risk. The guidelines contain the procedures for identifying, evaluating, mitigating, and compensating for the debtor industry's inherent ESG risks. This step was taken to identify the debtor's activities/projects based on environmental and social risks, as well as the debtor's compliance with environmental and social regulations. [SASB FN-CB-410a.1]
