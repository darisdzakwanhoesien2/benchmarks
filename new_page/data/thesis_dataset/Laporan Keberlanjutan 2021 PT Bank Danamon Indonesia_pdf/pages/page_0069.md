Laporan Keberlanjutan 2021

## Pengungkapan Aset Terkait Karbon (Carbon-related asset) [FS11]

MUFG mengumumkan MUFG Carbon Neutrality Declaration pada Mei 2021. Sebagai salah satu perusahaan grup MUFG, Danamon menyelaraskan strateginya dan berkolaborasi dengan MUFG dalam aspek ini. Pada tahun 2021, Danamon mulai mengungkapkan carbon-related assets yang dibiayai oleh Danamon agar sejalan dengan pengungkapan MUFG.

Danamon melakukan analisa kredit dengan hati-hati dan mempertimbangkan aspek LST ketika memberikan kredit kepada industri yang termasuk dalam carbon-related assets.

## Disclosure of Carbon-related Assets [FS11]

MUFG announced the MUFG Carbon Neutrality Declaration in May 2021. As one of the MUFG group companies, Danamon aligns its strategies and collaborates with MUFG in this aspect. In 2021, Danamon commenced to disclose carbon-related assets financed by Danamon to be in line with the disclosure of MUFG.

Danamon conducted prudent credit analysis which consider ESG aspect when providing the loan to the industry included in carbon-related assets.
