# Inklusi Keuangan

Bank juga mendukung program keuangan inklusif yang diprakasi Pemerintah melalui program Laku Pandai. Saat ini, cakupan wilayah layanan Laku Pandai Danamon semakin luas. Per 31 Desember 2021, masyarakat di 19 (sembilan belas) kabupaten/kota di 5 (lima) provinsi (Jawa Barat, Jawa Tengah, Lampung, Yogyakarta, Sulawesi Selatan), dapat memperoleh layanan Laku Pandai Danamon. Diharapkan dengan pengembangan ini, masyarakat semakin dekat dan mudah mendapatkan layanan keuangan tanpa kantor.

Bank terus meningkatkan kapabilitas agen Laku Pandai Perorangan (Agen DanaKu). Diharapkan Agen DanaKu dapat memberikan pelayanan prima kepada nasabah. Untuk itu, Bank menyempurnakan sistem dan prosedur yang ada. Bank juga memiliki panduan rekrutmen agen. Rekrutmen agen dilakukan uji tuntas dan kunjungan ke lokasi calon agen. Danamon bekerjasama dengan Kemitraan Berdaya Adira (Keday Adira) dalam rekrutmen agen. Program ini dilakukan untuk meningkatkan keamanan, kenyamanan dan kepercayaan nasabah.

Sistem pendaftaran Laku Pandai Danamon menjadi semakin efisien dan efektif. Bank mengembangkan sarana pendaftaran yang mudah. Pendaftaran nasabah Laku Pandai Danamon cukup dengan SMS Banking USSD *141*12# yang terhubung dengan data Dinas Kependudukan dan Pencatatan Sipil (DUKCAPIL). Hal ini membantu agen dan Bank dalam proses Know Your Customer (KYC) dan proses pelaporan.

Di tengah kondisi pandemi yang penuh tantangan, per 31 Desember 2021, jumlah Agen Laku Pandai sebanyak 35 Agen yang terdiri atas 32 Agen Perorangan dan 3 Agen Badan Hukum. Nasabah Laku Pandai Danamon tercatat sebanyak 2.290 nasabah. Pada umumnya nasabah Laku Pandai Danamon adalah Ibu Rumah Tangga dan Petani. Total dana nasabah Laku Pandai Danamon mencapai Rp102.940.144 per 31 Desember 2021. [FS13][FS14][FN-CB-240a.3]

# Financial Inclusion

The Bank also supports the Government's financial inclusive program through its Laku Pandai program. Currently, the scope for the Danamon Laku Pandai is becoming increasingly broad. As of December 31, 2021, communities in 19 (nineteen) Regency / City in 5 (five) Provinces (West Java, Central Java, Lampung, Yogyakarta, South Sulawesi) received Danamon Laku Pandai. It is expected that with this development, the communities will get closer and find it easier to access financial services without offices.

The Bank continues to increase the capability of Laku Pandai Agents (Agen DanaKu), in the hope they can provide excellent service to customers. For this reason, the Bank is perfecting the existing system and procedures. The Bank also has an agent recruitment guide. Agent recruitment is carried out through thorough tests and visits to prospective agent locations. Danamon cooperates with Adira Empowered Partnership (Keday Adira) for the recruitment of agents. This program is carried out to improve customer security, comfort and trust.

Danamon's registration system is becoming more efficient and effective, the Bank developing easy registration facilities. Danamon's Laku Pandai Customer registration uses SMS Banking USSD * 141 * 12 # which is connected to the Department of Population and Civil Registration (Dukcapil). This helps agents and the Bank in the knowing your customer (KYC) and reporting process.

During the challenging pandemic conditions, as of December 31, 2021, the number of Agents of Laku Pandai is 35 Agents consisting of 32 Individual agents and 3 legal entity agents. The Danamon Laku Pandai customers amounted to 2,290 customers. In general, customers of Laku Pandai Danamon are Housewives and Farmers. The total funds of Laku Pandai Danamon reached Rp. 102,940,144 as of December 31, 2021. [FS13] [FS14][FN-CB-240a.3]
