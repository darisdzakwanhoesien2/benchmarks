# Risk Management

Kegiatan bisnis perbankan tidak terlepas dari berbagai jenis risiko. Pengelolaan risiko yang efektif diperlukan untuk memastikan Bank telah mengidentifikasi risiko dan eksposur yang dihadapi, memetakan prioritas mitigasi, dan mengelola risiko sehingga berada dalam tingkat yang dapat diterima. Danamon telah melaksanakan empat pilar penerapan manajemen risiko:

1. Pengawasan aktif Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris;
2. Kecukupan kebijakan, prosedur, dan penetapan limit manajemen risiko;
3. Kecukupan proses identifikasi, pengukuran, pemantauan, dan pengendalian risiko, serta system informasi manajemen risiko;
4. Sistem pengendalian internal.

Penerapan manajemen risiko Danamon telah mengintegrasikan aspek LST, baik dalam aktivitas operasional Bank maupun pengembangan produk dan layanan Bank. Danamon telah memiliki pedoman dan kerangka kerja ESRM dan ESRA dalam proses credit underwriting. Pedoman ini telah diterapkan sejak Maret 2021 untuk pinjaman baru dan tambahan pinjaman yang mempunyai risiko LST yang signifikan. Pada tahap pertama implementasi, pedoman ditujukan kepada Enterprise Banking, khususnya segmen Corporate dan Commercial.

Danamon secara konsisten melakukan kajian dan pengkinian penerapan manajemen risiko, yang mencakup kecukupan kebijakan, limit, dan prosedur. Divisi Satuan Kerja Audit Internal (SKAI) secara berkala mengaudit proses manajemen risiko. SKAI juga memantau perbaikan yang telah dilakukan atas hasil temuan audit. Audit eksternal dilakukan oleh Kantor Akuntan Publik (KAP). PQJK 51.5.C

Banking business has many types of risks. Effective risk management is needed to ensure the Bank has identified the risks and exposures it faces, mapped out mitigation priorities, and managed the risks at an acceptable level. Danamon has instigated four risk management pillars:

1. Active supervision by the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners;
2. Adequacy of risk management policies, procedures and limits setup;
3. Adequacy of risk identification, measurement, monitoring, control processes, and risk management information systems;
4. Internal control system.

Danamon's risk management implementation has integrated the ESG aspects, within the Bank's operational activities and in the development of the Bank's products and services. Danamon already has ESRM and ESRA guidelines and framework in the credit underwriting process. These guidelines were implemented in March 2021 for new loans and additional loans with a significant ESG risk. During the first phase, the guidelines were applied in Enterprise Banking, particularly in the Corporate and Commercial segments.

Danamon consistently reviews and updates its risk management, including the adequacy of policies, limits and procedures. The Internal Audit Division (SKAI) periodically audits the risk management process. SKAI also monitors improvements made to the audit findings. External audit is carried out by a Public Accounting Firm (KAP). PQJK 51.5.C
