2021 Sustainability Report

Penerapan dan pengawasan keuangan berkelanjutan merupakan kinerja kolektif antara Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, Komite Keberlanjutan, dan Tim Keberlanjutan. Kerja sama dan koordinasi tersebut diatur dalam Kebijakan Keberlanjutan Danamon. Tugas, wewenang, dan tanggung jawab dari masing-masing pihak dicantumkan di dalam Kebijakan Keberlanjutan. [2-11] [2-12] 2-13]

Secara berkala, Bank menyelenggarakan Rapat Komite Keberlanjutan sebagai sarana koordinasi. Dalam rapat tersebut dilakukan pembahasan progress implementasi dari RAKB serta hal-hal terkait keuangan berkelanjutan lainnya.

Sustainable finance implementation and supervision is a collective effort between the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, the Sustainability Committee, and the Sustainability Team. This cooperation and coordination is regulated in the Danamon Sustainability Policy. The duties, authorities, and responsibilities of each party are listed in the Sustainability Policy. [2-11] [2-12] 2-13]

Periodically, the Bank holds Sustainability Committee Meetings as a means of coordination. The meetings discuss the SFAP progress as well as other matters related to sustainable finance.

# Pengembangan Kompetensi Keuangan Berkelanjutan [FS4] Sustainable Finance Competency Development

Danamon senantiasa melakukan pengembangan karyawan, termasuk terkait keuangan berkelanjutan. Wawasan dan pemahaman keuangan berkelanjutan diperlukan seluruh jajaran insan Danamon, terlebih Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, Komite Keberlanjutan, dan Tim Keberlanjutan sebagai pengelola, penanggung jawab, dan pengawas implementasi keuangan berkelanjutan.

Danamon continues to conduct employee development related to sustainable finance. Sustainable finance insight and understanding is required at all Danamon levels, especially for the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, Sustainability Committee, and the Sustainability Team as the manager, person in charge, and supervisor of sustainable finance implementation.
