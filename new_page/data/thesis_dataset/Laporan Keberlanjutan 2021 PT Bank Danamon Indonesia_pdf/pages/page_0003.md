## Kebijakan Keberlanjutan [2-22][2-23]
## Sustainability Policy

Sejak tahun 2018, PT Bank Danamon Indonesia Tbk ("Danamon", "Bank") mengambil langkah strategis dengan menyusun visi misi keberlanjutan yang melengkapi visi misi korporasi. Pada tahun 2019, Danamon resmi menerbitkan Kebijakan Keberlanjutan, yang mengacu pada POJK 51/POJK.03/2017 tentang Penerapan Keuangan Berkelanjutan bagi Lembaga Jasa Keuangan, Emiten, dan Perusahaan Publik ("POJK 51/2017").

Kebijakan Keberlanjutan Danamon memuat penjelasan mengenai tata kelola keberlanjutan, prinsip keuangan berkelanjutan, Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan (TPB) yang menjadi prioritas dan komitmen terkait, serta tugas, wewenang, dan tanggung jawab penanggung jawab keuangan berkelanjutan. Kebijakan Keberlanjutan menjadi acuan penerapan prinsip keuangan berkelanjutan dalam kegiatan operasional Bank (pronomina Danamon), penyusunan Rencana Aksi Keuangan Berkelanjutan ("RAKB"), maupun laporan keberlanjutan. Kebijakan Keberlanjutan telah dikaji ulang di tahun 2021 dan disesuaikan dengan strategi Bank terkini.

Prinsip keuangan berkelanjutan Danamon yang tercantum dalam Kebijakan Keberlanjutan telah sesuai dengan POJK 51/2017. Penerapan prinsip keuangan berkelanjutan dilakukan secara bertahap sesuai dengan kondisi di Bank sebagai wujud komitmen Bank dalam upaya memaksimalkan manfaat positif dan meminimalkan dampak negatif atas bisnis. In 2018, PT Bank Danamon Indonesia Tbk ("Danamon", "Bank") took the strategic step of establishing a sustainability vision and mission to complement the corporate vision and mission. In 2019, Danamon officially issued its Sustainability Policy that refers to POJK 51/POJK.03/2017 concerning the Implementation of Sustainable Finance for Financial Services Institutions, Issuers, and Public Companies ("POJK 51/2017").

Danamon's Sustainability Policy explains the sustainability governance, sustainable finance principles, the prioritized Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and related commitments, as well as the duties, authorities and responsibilities of the person in charge of sustainable finance. The Sustainability Policy has become the reference for the implementation of sustainable finance principles in the Bank's operations, the preparation of the Sustainable Finance Action Plan ("SFAP"), as well as the sustainability report. In 2021, Sustainability Policy has been reviewed and updated to be aligned with current Bank's strategy.

Danamon's sustainable finance principles as stated in the Sustainability Policy are in accordance with POJK 51/2017. The sustainable finance principles have been carried out in stages according to the Bank's conditions as a form of the Bank's commitment to maximizing positive benefits and minimizing negative impacts on the business.
