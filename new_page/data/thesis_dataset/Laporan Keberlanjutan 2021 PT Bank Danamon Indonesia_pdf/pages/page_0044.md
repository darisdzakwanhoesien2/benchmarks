Laporan Keberlanjutan 2021

# PENANAMAN BUDAYA KEBERLANJUTAN

Danamon secara konsisten melakukan sosialisasi kebijakan dan budaya keberlanjutan kepada karyawan melalui berbagai media komunikasi internal,seperti surel (email blast), pelatihan daring, dan lain-lain. Materi sosialisasi tersebut antara lain mengenai praktik konsumsi yang bertanggung jawab, efisiensi energi, penggunaan air dan kertas, Hari Bumi, pemanasan global dan perubahan iklim. Program sosialisasi dikembangkan dan berada di bawah koordinasi Tim Keberlanjutan.

Sebagai sarana meningkatkan partisipasi aktif insan Bank, pada tahun 2021 Danamon menyelenggarakan lomba foto internal. Tema lomba diselaraskan dengan budaya keberlanjutan dan mengangkat tema peduli lingkungan. Hal ini juga dimaksudkan agar karyawan dapat lebih memahami nilai-nilai keberlanjutan dalam aktivitas keseharian. Lomba ini diharapkan dapat membantu karyawan mengaplikasikan nilai-nilai keberlanjutan dalam kegiatan operasional Bank dan mendukung tercapainya visi dan misi keberlanjutan Bank.

# PLANTING OF SUSTAINABILITY CULTURE

Danamon disseminates the sustainability policies and culture consistently to all employees through internal communication media, such as email (email blast), online training, and others. The socialization material includes responsible consumption practices, energy efficiency, water and paper usage, Earth Day, global warming and climate change. The socialization program is developed and is under the coordination of the Sustainability Team.

To increase all Bank personnel's active participation, in 2021 Danamon held an internal photo competition. The competition theme was aligned with a sustainable culture, and raised a theme of caring for the environment. This was also intended to help employees better understand the sustainability values in their daily activities. This competition helps employees apply sustainability values in the Bank's operational activities and supports the Bank's sustainability vision and mission achievement.
