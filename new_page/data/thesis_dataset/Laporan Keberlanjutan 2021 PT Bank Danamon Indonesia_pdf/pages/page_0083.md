# MASYARAKAT

Secara umum, kegiatan perbankan memberikan dampak positif kepada masyarakat, baik secara langsung maupun tidak langsung. Perbankan berperan memberikan kemudahan akses bagi masyarakat terhadap sumber pendanaan bagi usahanya serta turut berkontribusi dalam peningkatan roda perekonomian.

Namun demikian, hampir selalu setiap kegiatan usaha memiliki dampak negatif, begitupun institusi perbankan. Dengan meningkatnya roda perekonomian serta kemudahan untuk mengakses sumber keuangan berpengaruh pada meningkatnya perilaku konsumtif masyarakat. Danamon melihat dampak negatif ini – meski tidak signifikan – tetap memengaruhi pola kehidupan masyarakat. Selain itu, terdapat potensi kesenjangan sosio-ekonomi antara kelas masyarakat yang memiliki akses terhadap teknologi dan perbankan dengan masyarakat yang tidak memiliki akses tersebut.

Oleh karena itu, melalui kegiatan Tanggung Jawab Sosial dan Lingkungan (TJSL), Danamon berkontribusi kembali kepada masyarakat dengan harapan dapat memitigasi dan meminimalisasi dampak negatif dari keberadaan bank. Program TJSL Danamon dilakukan oleh Divisi Sustainability Finance (SF) yang terbagi dalam tiga unit yang saling bersinergi, yakni Unit Pengembangan Lingkungan Kemasyarakatan (Community Development/ Commdev), Unit HSE, serta Unit BCM. Dalam penyusunan program-program TJSL, Divisi Sustainability Finance mengacu pada tiga Pilar, yakni Pilar Ekonomi, Pilar Sosial, dan Pilar Lingkungan dan menyesuaikan dengan indikator TPB. [413-1]

Pelaksanaan program TJSL yang dilakukan di tahun 2021 merealisasikan biaya TJSL sebesar Rp 7,2 miliar dengan total penerima manfaat langsung sebanyak 146.317 orang, melibatkan 1.078 karyawan sebagai relawan (Employee Volunteering) dari seluruh wilayah kerja Danamon. Biaya realisasi TJSL tahun 2021 meningkat sebesar 44% dibandingkan tahun 2020.

# Communities

Generally the banking activities have had a direct and indirect positive impact on the communities. The Bank plays a role in providing easy access to the public for business funding sources, and contributes to driving the wheels of the economy.

However, almost all business activities can have a negative impact, including in banking institutions. The increase in driving the wheels of the economy and the ease of accessing financial sources has increased the communities' consumptive behavior. Danamon looks at this negative impact, although not significant, as still affecting the community life pattern. In addition, there is the potential for a socio-economic gap between the people who have access to technology and banking with people who do not have access.

Therefore, through its Corporate Social Responsibility activities (CSR), Danamon contributes to the public in the hope of mitigating and minimizing the negative impact of the Bank's existence. Danamon CSR program is carried out by the Sustainability Finance (SF) Division and is divided into three units that synergize with each other, namely the Community Development / Commdev Unit, HSE Unit, and the BCM Unit. In the preparation of the CSR programs, the Sustainability Finance Division refers to three pillars, which are the economic pillar, social pillar, and environmental pillar, and adapts them to the SDGs indicators. [413-1]

The cost of CSR programs conducted in 2021 amounted to Rp 7.2 billion for 146,317 people as direct beneficiaries, and involving 1,078 employees as volunteers (Employee Volunteering) from all Danamon working areas. The CSR cost in 2021 increase by 44% compared to 2020.
