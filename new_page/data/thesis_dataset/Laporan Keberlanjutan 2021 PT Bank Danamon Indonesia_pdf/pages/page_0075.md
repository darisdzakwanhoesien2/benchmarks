Program Kesehatan Karyawan [403-6] [401-3]

Kesehatan karyawan merupakan salah satu faktor penting terkait pencapaian kinerja. Danamon menyediakan fasilitas kesehatan bagi seluruh karyawan. Bank menyediakan program BPJS Kesehatan dan asuransi kesehatan yang mencakup manfaat biaya rawat inap, rawat jalan, dental, melahirkan, kacamata dan Medical Check Up. Pelaksanaan pemberian fasilitas kesehatan kepada karyawan diatur dalam Kebijakan Human Capital. Karyawan dapat mengakses fasilitas kesehatan yang diperlukan di rumah sakit/klinik/fasilitas kesehatan lain. Terkait pandemi COVID-19, sejak tahun 2020, Danamon tetap menyediakan beberapa fasilitas kesehatan secara khusus.

### Upaya Penanganan dan Mitigasi Pandemi COVID-19

Melalui Unit Business Continuity Management (BCM), Danamon menerbitkan panduan dalam menghadapi kondisi pandemi yang dituangkan dalam bentuk SOP COVID-19. Selain itu Unit BCM memandu pembuatan Business Continuity Procedure (BCP) bagi critical maupun non-critical unit/division termasuk cabang. BCP mengatur tentang penanganan dan mitigasi terjadinya insiden agar kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja karyawan serta operasional bisnis tetap terjamin. [POJK 51.6.c.2.c]

Selain menyiapkan infrastruktur dan fasilitas K3 di lingkungan kerja, informasi berkala diberikan melalui sejumlah kanal komunikasi internal (e-mail, whatsapp blast, telegram blast, announcement system, standing banner, dan HSE's update meeting). Danamon juga membekali karyawan dengan pengetahuan melalui webinar series terkait beragam topik kesehatan diri dan keluarga, K3, meningkatkan produktivitas kerja, serta mitigasi bencana termasuk di dalamnya isu COVID-19. Webinar ini menjadi bagian dari pelibatan aktif seluruh karyawan dalam isu K3 selain melalui survei wajib rutin (Daily Self Assessment/DSA dan Employee Health Survey/EHS) pada aplikasi Danamon Care. Pendaftaran aplikasi PeduliLindungi melalui QR Code juga dipasang

### Employee Health Program [403-6] [401-3]

Employee health is one of the most important factors for performance achievement. Danamon provides health facilities for all employees and provides BPJS Health, and Health Insurance programs that include benefits covering hospitalization, outpatient, dental, childbirth, glasses and Medical Check Up. These benefits can be accessed by employees in hospitals, clinics and other health facilities. The employees' health facilities are provided through Human Capital Policy. During the COVID-19 pandemic, since 2020, Danamon still provided specific health facilities.

### Pandemic Handling and COVID-19 Mitigation Efforts

Through the Business Continuity Management (BCM) Unit, Danamon has published guidelines to face the pandemic in the form of COVID-19 SOP. In addition, the BCM Unit guides developing of Business Continuity Procedures (BCP) for critical and non-critical units/divisions including branches. BCP regulates incident handling and mitigation so that the health and safety of employees and business operations are guaranteed. [POJK 51.6.c.2.c]

As well as preparing the OHS infrastructure and facilities in the work place, information is regularly provided through internal communication channels (e-mail, Whatsapp Blast, Telegram Blast, Announcement System, Standing Banner, and HSE's Update Meeting). Danamon also disseminates knowledge to employees through Webinar Series on their own and their families' health topics, OHS, increasing work productivity, as well as disaster mitigation including COVID-19. These webinars form part of the active involvement of all employees in OHS, in addition to routine mandatory surveys (Daily Self Assessment / DSA and Employee Health Survey / EHS) in the Danamon Care application.
