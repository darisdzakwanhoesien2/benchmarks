|  PORTOFOLIO KREDIT BERWAWASAN LST ESG-BASED LOAN PORTFOLIO | Perbankan dan institusi finansial menghadapi tuntutan yang semakin kuat terhadap pembiayaan yang bertanggung jawab dan berwawasan LST. Hal ini semakin relevan dengan adanya seruan mitigasi perubahan iklim. Keuangan berkelanjutan dan pengelolaan risiko perbankan menjadi komitmen Bank, salah satunya melalui pengembangan portofolio kredit berwawasan LST dan implementasi pedoman dan kerangka kerja ESRM dan ESRA sejak Maret 2021. Sebagai langkah awal implementasi, Bank memfokuskan kepada Enterprise Banking (segmen Corporate dan Commercial). | Banks and financial institutions face growing demands for responsible and ESG-based financing. This is becoming increasingly relevant with the call for climate change mitigation. Sustainable finance and banking risk management are the Bank's commitments, one of which is through the growth of ESG-based loan portfolio and the implementation of ESRM and ESRA guidelines and frameworks since March 2021. Danamon implements the guidelines on Enterprise Banking (Corporate and Commercial segments) as its initial step.  |

|  KESEHATAN DAN KESELAMATAN KERJA OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH AND SAFETY | Kondisi kesehatan karyawan sangat memengaruhi pencapaian kinerja dan keberlangsungan usaha Bank. Oleh karena itu, kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja karyawan menjadi perhatian Danamon. Hal ini semakin relevan di tengah kondisi pandemi COVID-19 yang masih berlangsung hingga akhir tahun 2021. Danamon memberikan fasilitas kesehatan kepada karyawan bank, serta senantiasa memastikan lingkungan kerja yang sehat dan aman bagi karyawan. Hal ini diatur dalam kebijakan sumber daya manusia (SDM). | Employees' health contributes significantly to the Bank's performance achievement and business continuity, and therefore their health and safety is a major concern for Danamon. This has become even more relevant during the COVID-19 pandemic, which was still continuing at the end of 2021. Danamon provides health facilities for its employees, and always ensures a healthy and safe working environment. This is regulated in the Human Capital (HC) policy.  |

|  DAMPAK EKONOMI TIDAK LANGSUNG INDIRECT ECONOMIC IMPACT | Salah satu sasaran utama dari dampak kegiatan Danamon adalah kontribusi dampak ekonomi positif bagi masyarakat. Selain kontribusi langsung melalui kinerja keuangan, kontribusi dampak ekonomi tidak langsung diberikan melalui kegiatan literasi dan inklusi keuangan serta bantuan untuk infrastruktur di masyarakat sekitar kantor operasional Danamon. Kontribusi ini diharapkan dapat menjadi faktor pengungkit bagi masyarakat untuk meningkatkan wawasan keuangan, mengelola uang dengan lebih bijak, dan mendukung aktivitas masyarakat dalam kegiatan ekonomi. | Contributing positively to the communities' economy is one of the main targets of Danamon's activities. In addition to direct contributions from financial performance, indirect economic contributions are also delivered through financial literacy and inclusion activities as well as infrastructure assistance for the communities surrounding Danamon's operational offices. These contributions are expected to be a lever for the communities to increase their financial insight, manage money more wisely, and support the community activities in the economy.  |
