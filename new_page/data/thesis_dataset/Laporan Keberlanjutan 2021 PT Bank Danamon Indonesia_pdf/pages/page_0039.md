# Keanggotaan Asosiasi [2-28]
## Association Membership

Danamon terlibat aktif dalam asosiasi industri di tingkat nasional. Keikutsertaan dalam asosiasi merupakan salah satu langkah strategis untuk dapat mengetahui perkembangan isu-isu terkini, baik informasi bisnis, perbankan, risiko, maupun peluang. Keikutsertaan dalam asosiasi juga mendorong kepatuhan Danamon terhadap regulasi perbankan.

Including insurance, IT development services, hardware and software, professional services, consultants, office supply providers, ATM machine providers, outsourced worker services, construction and facility companies, cash management service providers, et cetera.

Danamon encourages suppliers to support sustainable finance by complying with applicable laws and regulations regarding employment in relation to procurement practices. To avoid violations or fraud, Danamon provides Whistleblowing System (WBS) to report alleged of violations or fraud in the procurement process.

Danamon is actively involved in national industry associations. Participating in associations is a strategic step to understand development in the latest issues, including business information, banking, risks, and opportunities. Participating in associations also encourages Danamon's compliance with banking regulations.
