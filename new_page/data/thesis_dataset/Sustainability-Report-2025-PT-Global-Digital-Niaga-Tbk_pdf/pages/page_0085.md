# Sedekah Jadi Berkah

Dalam rangka merayakan ulang tahun Dekoruma ke-10, Dekoruma mengusung nilai gaya hidup Japandi melalui praktik decluttering sebagai wujud rasa syukur dan kepedulian sosial. Sejalan dengan tema anniversary "Gratitude to Grow and Move Forward", barang-barang yang terkumpul selama 3 minggu periode donasi disalurkan melalui program "Sedekah Jadi Berkah", hasil kolaborasi dengan Donasi Barang. Program ini melibatkan 100+ Dekorumates, sekaligus memperkuat budaya empati dan kontribusi kolektif, serta memberikan dukungan nyata bagi penerima donasi dengan memanfaatkan kembali barang layak pakai untuk meningkatkan kualitas hidup.

To celebrate Dekoruma's 10th anniversary, the Company embraced the Japandi lifestyle value of decluttering as an expression of gratitude and social responsibility. Aligned with the anniversary theme "Gratitude to Grow and Move Forward," items collected during the three-week donation period were distributed through the "Sedekah Jadi Berkah" program, in collaboration with Donasi Barang. The initiative engaged over 100 Dekorumates, fostering a culture of empathy and collective contribution, while providing meaningful support to donation recipients by giving pre-loved items a second life and improving everyday living conditions.

Rangkaian insiatif ini memberikan dampak ekonomi tidak langsung yang signifikan, termasuk peningkatan keterampilan, kesadaran keberlanjutan, peluang ekonomi lokal, dan akses pendidikan bagi komunitas rentan. Melalui pendekatan ini, Perseroan berperan sebagai mitra pembangunan sosial yang mendukung ketahanan komunitas dan pertumbuhan ekonomi yang inklusif dan berkelanjutan. (GRI 2011-2)

Dampak ekonomi tidak langsung tersebut sejalan dengan prioritas pemangku kepentingan serta agenda pembangunan yang lebih luas, termasuk penguatan UMKM, pengembangan sektor pariwisata, peningkatan keterampilan generasi muda, dan pencapaian Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan (TPB), khususnya TPB 4, 8, 10, dan 17. (GRI 2011-2)

# Mendukung Mitra Seller dan Wirausaha (GRI 413-1, 413-2)

Perseroan mendukung mitra seller dan wirausaha melalui layanan fulfillment dan logistik terintegrasi yang membantu memperluas jangkauan pasar, meningkatkan efisiensi operasional, serta memperkuat daya saing dalam ekosistem omnichannel Bibli Tiket. Dukungan ini memastikan proses pemeruhan pesanan berjalan andal sekaligus menciptakan nilai tambah bagi mitra usaha. (GRI 413-1)

These initiatives generated significant indirect economic impacts, including improved skills, increased sustainability awareness, expanded local economic opportunities, and enhanced access to education for vulnerable communities. Through this approach, the Company acts as a social development partner that supports community resilience and inclusive, sustainable economic growth. (GRI 2011-2)

These indirect economic impacts are aligned with stakeholder priorities and broader development agendas, including MSME strengthening, tourism-sector development, youth skills enhancement, and the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), particularly SDGs 4, 8, 10, and 17. (GRI 2011-2)

# Supporting Sellers and Entrepreneurs (GRI 413-1, 413-2)

The Company supports seller partners and entrepreneurs through integrated fulfillment and logistics services that expand market access, enhance operational efficiency, and strengthen competitiveness within the Bibli Tiket omnichannel ecosystem. This support ensures reliable order fulfillment while creating sustainable value for business partners. (GRI 413-1)

Tumbuh Bersama Komunitas

Grijering Together with Communities

Sejalan dengan aktivitas operasional tersebut, Perseroan menerapkan mekanisme keterlibatan masyarakat di seluruh lokasi yang berinteraksi dengan komunitas, termasuk pemetaan pemangku kepentingan, konsultasi lokal, dan penyediaan saluran pengaduan publik. Sepanjang 2025, khususnya di area gudang, 75% dari kegiatan operasional utama telah melibatkan masyarakat dan komunitas setempat sesuai kebutuhan lokal. Di Gudang Marunda, sebagian dari tenaga kerja operasional berasal dari warga sekitar, mencerminkan komitmen Perseroan dalam menciptakan manfaat ekonomi langsung dan hubungan jangka panjang dengan komunitas. (GRI 413-1)

Perseroan secara berkala melakukan pemetaan pemangku kepentingan, konsultasi dengan komunitas lokal, serta pemantauan potensi dampak sosial dan lingkungan dan kegiatan operasional. Hasil evaluasi tersebut menjadi dasar penyusunan program pengembangan masyarakat yang disesuaikan dengan kebutuhan komunitas. Selain itu, Perseroan menyediakan mekanisme pengaduan masyarakat yang dapat diakses publik serta forum komunikasi internal, termasuk komite keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja dan perwakilan pekerja, untuk mengidentifikasi dan menindaklanjut potensi dampak operasional. (GRI 413-1)

Pelaksanaan mekanisme tersebut dilakukan melalui proses partizpatif dengan komunitas lokal dan pemangku kepentingan yang relevan. Perseroan tidak melakukan penilaian dampak gender secara terpisah, namun kebutuhan kelompok rentan dipertimbangkan dalam konsultasi dan perancangan program. Hasil pemantauan sosial dan lingkungan digunakan sebagai dasar tindakan/untoperasional dan pengembangan program masyarakat, dan informasi yang relevan disampaikan melalui mekanisme komunikasi Perseroan. (GRI 413-1)

Hasil evaluasi menunjukkan bahwa operasional Perseroan tidak menimbulkan dampak negatif signifikan terhadap komunitas lokal. Risiko sosial dan lingkungan dinilai rendah karena fasilitas berlokasi di kawasan komersial atau industri serta tidak melibatkan aktivitas yang berisiko terhadap kesehatan, keselamatan, atau mata pencaharian masyarakat. Oleh karena itu, fokus insiatif diarahkan pada peningkatan kapasitas dan pemberdayaan ekonomi, termasuk dukungan terhadap UMKM melalui kolaborasi strategis, aktivasi digital, dan partisipasi dalam program nasional. (GRI 413-2)

In line with these operational activities, the Company implements community engagement mechanisms across all locations that interact with local communities, including stakeholder mapping, local consultations, and the provision of public grievance channels. Throughout 2025, particularly in warehouse areas, 75% of the Company's main operational activities involved local communities and community groups in accordance with local needs. At the Marunda Warehouse, a portion of the operational workforce is recruited from nearby residents, reflecting the Company's commitment to creating direct economic benefits and fostering long-term relationships with the community. (GRI 413-1)

The Company conducts periodic stakeholder mapping, consultations with local communities, and monitoring of potential social and environmental impacts arising from its operational activities. The results of these evaluations form the basis for community development initiatives tailored to local needs. In addition, the Company provides publicly accessible community grievance mechanisms and internal communication forums, including occupational health and safety committees and worker representation bodies, to identify and address potential operational impacts. (GRI 413-1)

These mechanisms are implemented through participatory processes involving local communities and relevant stakeholders. The Company does not conduct separate gender impact assessments, but the needs of vulnerable groups are taken into account by data collection and program design. The results of social and environmental monitoring are used as the basis for operational follow-up and community program development, and relevant information is communicated through the Company's communication mechanisms. (GRI 413-1)

The evaluation results indicate that the Company's operations do not generate significant negative impacts on local communities. Social and environmental risks are assessed as low, as the facilities are located in commercial or industrial areas and do not involve activities that pose risks to community health, safety, or livelihoods. Accordingly, the Company's initiatives focus on capacity building and economic empowerment, including support for micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) through strategic collaborations, digital activations, and participation in national programs. (GRI 413-2)
