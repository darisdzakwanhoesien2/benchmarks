# IDENTIFIKASI BAHAYA, PENILAIAN RISIKO, DAN PELATIHAN K3 [GRI:403-2, 403-5, 403-6, 403-7]

Bibli Tiket menerapkan kerangka kerja kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja (K3) yang komprehensif dan dikelola oleh unit Environment, Health, Safety &amp; Security (EHSS), yang melakukan identifikasi bahaya serta penilaian risiko secara rutin dan non-rutin, termasuk pada saat terjadi perubahan operasional maupun pembukaan lokasi baru. Aktivitas distribusi dan logistik diidentifikasi sebagai area dengan tingkat risiko lebih tinggi, sehingga menjadi prioritas dalam penerapan pengendalian dan mitigasi risiko. [GRI:403-2]

# HAZARD IDENTIFICATION, RISK ASSESSMENT, AND OHS TRAINING [GRI:403-2, 403-5, 403-6, 403-7]

Bibli Tiket implements a comprehensive occupational health and safety (K3) framework managed by the Environment, Health, Safety &amp; Security (EHSS) unit, which conducts routine and non-routine hazard identification and risk assessments, including during operational changes and the opening of new locations. Distribution and logistics activities are identified as higher-risk areas and as such, prioritized for risk control and mitigation measures. [GRI:403-2]

# Mengutamakan Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Karyawan
Prioritizing Our People's Health and Safety

Perseroan menugaskan Health and Safety Environment (HSE) Representatives dan HSE Officers di lokasi operasional untuk memantau kepatuhan, menindaklanjuti temuan, serta melaporkan kinerja K3 kepada manajemen. Seluruh karyawan diberikan akses terhadap saluran pelaporan yang mudah dijangkau untuk menyampaikan kondisi tidak aman tanpa rasa takut akan tindakan pembalasan, serta memiliki kewenangan untuk menghentikan pekerjaan apabila dinilai membahayakan keselamatan. [GRI:403-2, 403-4]

The Company assigns Health and Safety Environment (HSE) Representatives and Officers at operational sites to monitor compliance, follow up on findings, and report K3 performance to management. All employees are provided with accessible channels to report unsafe conditions without fear of retaliation and are empowered to stop work if it poses safety risks. [GRI:403-2, 403-6]

Upaya pencegahan dilakukan melalui penyediaan alat pelindung diri (APD), pelaksanaan briefing keselamatan sebelum operasional dimulai, serta penyelenggaraan pelatihan K3 secara berkala yang mencakup tanggap darurat, pertolongan pertama, ergonomi, dan keselamatan di area logistik. Mitra kerja di lokasi (orsite vendors) turut dilibatkan dalam pelatihan tanggap darurat, dan audit K3 dilaksanakan secara berkala untuk mengevaluasi efektivitas pengendalian risiko. [GRI:403-5, 403-7]

Preventive measures include the provision of personal protective equipment (PPE), pre-operation safety briefings, and regular K3 training covering emergency response, first aid, ergonomics, and logistics safety. Orsite vendors are included in emergency response training, and periodic K3 audits are conducted to evaluate the effectiveness of risk controls. [GRI:403-5, 403-7]

Untuk mendukung kesehatan pekerja secara holistik, Bibli Tiket menyediakan layanan kesehatan kerja dan program promosi kesehatan yang bersifat sukarela, termasuk pemeriksaan kesehatan, dukungan kesehatan mental, edukasi gaya hidup sehat, serta pelatihan literasi keuangan. Rangkaian upaya ini memperkuat budaya keselamatan, menurunkan risiko operasional, serta mendukung terciptanya lingkungan kerja yang aman, sehat, dan berkelanjutan bagi karyawan. [GRI:403-6]

To promote holistic worker health, Bibli Tiket provides occupational health services and voluntary health programs, including medical check-ups, mental health support, healthy lifestyle education, and financial literacy training. These efforts strengthen the safety culture, reduce operational risks, and support a safe, healthy, and sustainable working environment for employees. [GRI:403-6]

# OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH AND SAFETY PERFORMANCE AND INCIDENTS [GRI:403-9, 403-10]

Selama tahun 2025, Bibli Tiket mencatat 0 insiden kecelakaan kerja fatal di seluruh area perkantoran dan operasional. Pelaporan insiden dan audit keselamatan dilaksanakan secara rutin di seluruh lokasi kerja guna memastikan kepatuhan terhadap prosedur kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja. Hasil audit dimanfaatkan untuk memperkuat sistem kerja, meningkatkan pelatihan, serta menumbuhkan budaya keselamatan. Melalui berbagai upaya tersebut, Bibli Tiket berhasil mempertahankan nihil kecelakaan kerja dan nol fatalitas selama periode pelaporan, mencerminkan komitmen Perseroan dalam menjaga lingkungan kerja yang aman dan sehat. [GRI:403-9]

During 2025, Bibli Tiket recorded 0 fatal work-related incidents across its offices and operational areas. Regular incident reporting and safety audits were conducted across all sites to ensure compliance with occupational health and safety procedures, and audit results were used to strengthen work systems, training, and safety culture. Through these efforts, Bibli Tiket successfully maintained zero workplace incidents and zero fatalities during the reporting period, reflecting the Company's commitment to maintaining a safe and healthy working environment. [GRI:403-9]
