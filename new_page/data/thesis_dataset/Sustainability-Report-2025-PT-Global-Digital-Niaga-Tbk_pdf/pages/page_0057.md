# MENEGAKKAN INTEGRITAS: KOMITMEN KAMI MELAWAN KORUPSI DAN GRATIFIKASI [GRI 2-26, 205-1, 205-2, 205-3]

# UPHOLDING INTEGRITY: OUR STAND AGAINST CORRUPTION AND GRATIFICATION [GRI 2-26, 205-1, 205-2, 205-3]

Dalam rangka menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang sehat, Perseroan menegakkan praktik bisnis yang beretika dan berintegritas melalui penerapan Kode Etik Perseroan. Kode Etik ini memuat nilai-nilai inti, etika bisnis, etos kerja, serta komitmen terhadap Peraturan Perusahaan (PP). Selain itu, Kode Etik juga mengatur penerapan prinsip antikonupsi dalam kegiatan pengadaan berdasarkan asas keadilan, transparansi, dan integritas tinggi, termasuk dalam hal inovasi, efisiensi, hubungan dengan pemasok, serta penerimaan hadiah, keramahtamahan, atau bentuk bantuan lainnya. Informasi dalam Kode Etik juga mencakup isu pelecehan dan diskriminasi di tiket.com. Kode Etik ini berlaku bagi seluruh anggota Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, dan karyawan dalam menjalankan kegiatan usaha serta berinteraksi dengan para pemangku kepentingan. [GRI 205-2]

In order to foster a healthy work environment, the Company upholds ethical and integrity-based business practices through the implementation of the Company's Code of Conduct. The Code outlines the Company's core values, business ethics, work ethics, and commitment to the Company Regulations (PP). It also governs the application of anti-corruption principles in procurement activities based on fairness, transparency, and integrity, including matters related to innovation, efficiency, supplier relationships, and the acceptance of gifts, hospitality, or other forms of assistance. The Code of Conduct also addresses issues of harassment and discrimination at tiket.com. It applies to all members of the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, and employees in conducting business and engaging with stakeholders. [GRI 205-2]

Bibli Tiket telah melakukan penilaian risiko korupsi terhadap 100% kegiatan operasionalnya sebagai bagian dari penerapan manajemen risiko perusahaan dan tata kelola perusahaan yang baik. Kode Etik dan Kebijakan Antikonupsi Perseroan dikomunikasikan melalui laman resmi perusahaan serta platform pembelajaran internal, termasuk Billearning (Bibli) dan Daisy (tiket.com). [GRI 205-1, 205-2].

Bibli Tiket has assessed corruption risks across 100% of its operations as part of its enterprise risk management and good corporate governance framework. The Company's Code of Ethics and Anti-Corruption Policy are communicated through official websites and internal learning platforms, including Billearning (Bibli) and Daisy (tiket.com). [GRI 205-1, 205-2]
