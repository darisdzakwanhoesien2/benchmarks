# STRATEGI HUMAN CAPITAL UNTUK ORGANISASI YANG ADAPTIF DAN DIGITAL

Karyawan Blibli Tiket merupakan penggerak utama dalam mewujudkan visi Perseroan dan mendorong pertumbuhan bisnis yang berkelanjutan. Hingga akhir 2025, Perseroan memiliki 6775 karyawan penuh waktu, terdiri dari 3.688 karyawan tetap dan 3.087 karyawan tidak tetap, serta didukung oleh 3.093 pekerja alih daya di area operasional, logistik, keamanan, dan kebersihan.

Pada tahun 2025, strategi Human Capital difokuskan pada penyelarasan talenta, struktur, dan kapabilitas untuk mendukung agenda pertumbuhan dan transformasi digital Perseroan. Strategi ini dibangun di atas tiga pilar utama: penguatan kapabilitas strategis HC, pengembangan budaya yang berpusat pada karyawan, serta peningkatan keunggulan operasional. Melalui kolaborasi antara Human Capital Business Partner (HCBP) dan para pakar HC, Perseroan memastikan kebijakan dan proses SDM selaras dengan kebutuhan bisnis.

Secara spesifik di Blibli, strategi di turunkan menjadi penerapan yang menghasilkan sejumlah peningkatan, termasuk standarisasi proses rekrutmen, penggunaan alat seleksi berbasis digital seperti psychometric dan AI screening, serta pengembangan kapabilitas tim Talent Acquisition. Penguatan organisasi dilakukan melalui pelaksanaan organizational diagnostic, penyempurnaan Performance Management dengan memanfaatkan penilaian Net Promoter Score (NPS), yang digunakan sebagai mekanisme umpan balik karyawan yang melengkapi evaluasi perilaku 360 derajat, serta peningkatan kualitas program pengembangan talenta. Perseroan juga mempercepat adopsi platform digital, seperti low-code automation, integrasi HRIS, dan solusi berbasis AI, untuk meningkatkan efisiensi dan konsistensi layanan HC.

Perseroan mengutamakan pendekatan people-centered melalui pelatihan AI dan otomas; program kepemimpinan, perbaikan proses rekrutmen berbasis data, peningkatan mobilitas internal, serta komunikasi terbuka melalui Town Hall, Heads Up, Group Date, dan sesi coaching sebagai forum dialog berkala antara pimpinan dan tim untuk menjaga keterhubungan dan keterlibatan karyawan. Melalui pelaksanaan perubahan bertahap, pelatihan, dan dukungan pengguna yang konsisten, Perseroan berhasil meningkatkan adopsi digital dan menyatukan proses HC di seluruh ekosistem.

# HUMAN CAPITAL STRATEGY FOR AN ADAPTIVE AND DIGITAL ORGANIZATION

Blibli Tiket's employees are central to delivering the Company's vision and driving sustainable business growth. By the end of 2025, the Company employed 6775 full-time employees, including 3.688 permanent and 3.087 non-permanent staff, supported by 3.093 outsourced workers across operations, logistics, security, and facilities.

In 2025, the Human Capital strategy focused on aligning talent, structure, and capabilities with the Company's growth and digital transformation agenda. Built on three pillars, strengthening strategic HC capabilities, fostering a people-centric culture, and enhancing HC operational excellence, the strategy was implemented through close collaboration between Human Capital Business Partner (HCBP) and HC experts to ensure that people-related policies and processes remained aligned with business needs.

Specifically at Blibli, the strategy has been translated into implementation that resulted to several improvements, including standardization of recruitment process, the use of digital-based selection tools such as psychometric assessments and AI- screening, as well as Talent Acquisition team's capabilities development. Organizational strengthening is carried out through the implementation of organizational diagnostics, the enhancement of Performance Management by leveraging Net Promoter Score (NPS) insights, used to serve as an employee feedback mechanism, complementing 360-degree behavioral evaluations, and the improvement of talent development program quality, and improving the quality of talent development programs. The Company also accelerated the adoption of digital platforms, such as low-code automation, HRIS integration, and AI-driven solutions, to enhance HC service efficiency and consistency.

The Company prioritized a people-centered approach through AI and automation training, leadership programs, data-driven improvements to recruitment processes, enhanced internal mobility, and open communication through Town Halls, Heads Up sessions, Group Date, and coaching session as regular dialogue forums between leaders and teams to maintain connection and engagement. Through phased changes, continuous training, and consistent user support, the Company successfully strengthened digital adoption and aligned HC processes across the entire ecosystem.
