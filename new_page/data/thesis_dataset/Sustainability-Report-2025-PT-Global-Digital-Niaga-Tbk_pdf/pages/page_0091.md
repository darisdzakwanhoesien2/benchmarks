Recycle Village (RV) Upcycled Bags for Report Packaging

Sebagai bagian dari komitmen terhadap penggunaan produk, timah lingkungan dan pembendiralan (JMKA) BINI TIDAK dipegangkan oleh tas Upcycle Recycle Village (RV) yang diproduksi menjadi milik dan tidak diproduksi. Data tahun 2005, tas Upcycle RV digunakan sebagai demikian, Jodi Laporte, Turunan dan Laporan UMAH (sede ringkas di seluruh dunia), di seder 400 militer dan di 100 militer. In 2007, regmenterid nadi upcycle, dengan 1000 beran selegar, kg dan diproduksi sebenar 70 unit. Mereka inovasi ini, hepatiman nadi upcycle, memanfaatkan produk dan mengurangi produksi timah, sebaliknya, terlalu rapa memadai di pengembangan UMAH (100 militer) dan tak berarti, semakin banyak lebih.

As part of its commitment to environment, by forceps products and MOME measurement, BINI TIDAK peradopsi Recycle Village (RV) Upcycled Bags made from upcycled operational waste material. This influence was caused out in collaboration with Recycle Village (RV), Social MOME that serves an active role in producing value-added and responsibly made products.

In 2025, the Upcyclled RV Bags were used as packaging for the Company's Annual Report and Sustainability Report distributed to investors and selected stakeholders. The bags were produced using upcycled materials with a stop weight of approximately 7 kg, resulting in 20 units. Through this initiative, the Company extends material life cycles, reduces operational waste, and simultaneously supports local MOME development through inclusive circular economy practices.

# MENGURANGI DAN MENGELOLA LIMBAH

[SEOJK F13] [GRI 306-1, 306-2, 306-3, 306-4, 306-5]

Bibli Tiket berkomitmen mengurangi dan mengelola limbah secara bertanggung jawab sebagai bagian dari upaya menjaga efisiensi sumber daya dan meminimalkan dampak lingkungan di seluruh rantai nilai. Dampak limbah terutama berasal dari aktivitas operasional seperti pergudangan, pengemasan, pengiriman produk, serta aktivitas perkantoran dan toko fisik, yang menghasilkan berbagai aliran limbah termasuk limbah kemasan, limbah organik, dan limbah elektronik. Selain berasal dari kegiatan operasional internal, Perseroan juga menyadari bahwa aktivitas dalam rantai nilai, termasuk pemasok serta penggunaan produk oleh pelanggan, turut berkontribusi terhadap timbulan limbah sehingga memerlukan pendekatan pengelolaan yang menyeluruh. [GRI 306-1, SEOJK F13]

Untuk memitigasi dampak tersebut, Perseroan menerapkan pendekatan ekonomi sirkular dengan memprioritaskan pencegahan timbulan limbah, pemanfaatan kembali material, serta peningkatan tingkat daur ulang. Inisiatif ini diwujudkan melalui optimalisasi penggunaan kemasan ramah lingkungan, pemanfaatan kembali material dalam proses operasional, pemilahan limbah di sumber, serta penguatan program daur ulang bersama mitra pengelola limbah. Sebagai bagian dari pengelolaan yang sistematis, limbah diklasifikasikan ke dalam kategori limbah B3, limbah organik, limbah anorganik, dan limbah residu guna memastikan bahwa setiap jenis limbah dikelola sesuai dengan karakteristik dan metode penanganan yang tepat. [GRI 306-2]

Pengelolaan limbah non-B3 dilakukan melalui pemilahan dan pengiriman kepada pengelola limbah pihak ketiga yang memiliki (p) resmi untuk diproses melalui kegiatan pemulihan material, termasuk daur ulang. Sementara itu, limbah B3 seperti limbah elektronik, oli bekas, dan toner dikumpulkan di Tempat Penyimpanan Sementara (TPS) sebelum diserahkan kepada pengelola limbah tersertifikasi untuk diolah sesuai dengan peraturan yang berlaku. Kepatuhan mitra pengelola limbah terhadap standar pengelolaan lingkungan diverifikasi secara berkala oleh Departemen Environmental, Health, Safety, and Sustainability (EHSS &amp; ESG) melalui mekanisme kontrak kerja sama serta pemantauan rutin. [GRI 306-2, 306-5]

Sebagian besar proses pemulihan dan pembuangan limbah dilakukan secara offsite melalui pengelola limbah berizin, sementara beberapa kegiatan pengolahan limbah organik skala kecil, seperti pengomposan melalui sistem biopon, dilaksanakan secara onsite di fasilitas operasional tertentu.

# REDUCING AND MANAGING WASTE

[SEOJK F13] [GRI 306-1, 306-2, 306-3, 306-4, 306-5]

Bibli Tiket is committed to reducing and managing waste responsibly as part of its broader effort to promote resource efficiency and minimize environmental impacts across the value chain. Waste impacts primarily arise from operational activities such as warehousing, packaging, product delivery, as well as office and physical store operations, which generate several waste streams including packaging waste, organic waste, and electronic waste. In addition to internal operations, the Company recognizes that activities across the value chain, including suppliers and post-consumer use of products, also contribute to waste generation and therefore require a comprehensive waste management approach. [GRI 306-1, SEOJK F13]

To address these impacts, the Company adopts a circular economy approach by prioritizing waste prevention, material reuse, and improved recycling practices. These initiatives are implemented through environmentally responsible packaging optimization, reuse of materials within operational processes, waste segregation at source, and the strengthening of recycling programs in collaboration with licensed waste management partners. As part of a systematic management framework, waste is classified into hazardous waste, organic waste, inorganic waste, and residual waste to ensure that each type of waste is managed using the most appropriate handling method. [GRI 306-2]

Non-hazardous waste is managed through segregation and transferred to licensed third-party waste handlers for recovery processes, including recycling. Hazardous waste, such as electronic waste, used oil, and toner cartridges, is collected at temporary storage facilities before being transferred to certified waste management partners for treatment in accordance with applicable regulations. Compliance of waste management partners with environmental management standards is periodically verified by the Environmental, Health, Safety, and Sustainability (EHSS &amp; ESG) Department through contractual arrangements and routine monitoring. [GRI 306-2, 306-5]
