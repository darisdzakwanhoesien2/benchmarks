# MENGHUBUNGKAN PELANGGAN MELALUI PENGALAMAN TANPA HAMBATAN (SEOJK.FIT.F26)

Bibli Tiket secara konsisten memperkuat sinergi lintas platform untuk mempertahankan posisinya sebagai penyedia ekosistem omnichannel commerce and lifestyle terdepan di Indonesia. Melalui pengintegrasian teknologi, layanan daring dan luring kini menyatu dalam satu pengalaman yang lancar dan tepercaya, didukung oleh layanan Click &amp; Collect, pengiriman cepat 2 Jam Sampai, serta fitur Tukar Tambah yang menghubungkan transaksi digital dengan layanan fisik. (SEOJK F17)

Inovasi Keanggotaan Terpadu (Unified Membership) terus dikembangkan dengan pendekatan berbasis AI dan otomasi, memungkinkan pelanggan menikmati proses login yang lebih mudah dan personalisasi pengalaman yang lebih relevan. Sementara itu, fitur Bibli Tiket Rewards (Unified Loyalty Points) semakin diperluas untuk memberikan manfaat lintas platform, termasuk fitur instant refund dan cashback melalui BibliPay. (SEOJK FIT F26)

Selain itu, Bibli Tiket berkolaborasi dengan mitra hotel, operator wisata, dan pelaku UMKM untuk mempromosikan destinasi lokal melalui kampanye tematik seperti Bibli Tiket Travel Fair dan HarpeInas 2025, yang turut mendorong pertumbuhan ekonomi kreatif daerah. (SEOJK F26)

# CONNECTING CUSTOMERS THROUGH SEAMLESS EXPERIENCES (SEOJK.FIT.F26)

Bibli Tiket continues to strengthen cross-platform synergy to uphold its position as Indonesia's leading omnichannel commerce and lifestyle ecosystem. By integrating technology and services seamlessly, the Company delivers a unified, reliable, and convenient online-to-offline shopping experience, supported by features such as Click &amp; Collect, 2-Hour Delivery, and Trade-In services that bridge digital transactions with physical touchpoints. (SEOJK F17)

The Unified Membership innovation continues to be developed through an AI- and automation-driven approach, enabling customers to enjoy a more seamless login process and more relevant personalized experiences. At the same time, the Bibli Tiket Rewards fiture (Unified Loyalty Points) has been further expanded to deliver cross-platform benefits, including instant refund features and cashback through BibliPay. (SEOJK FIT F26)

In addition, Bibli Tiket partnered with hotels, tour operators, and MSMEs to promote local destinations through thematic campaigns such as the Bibli Tiket Travel Fair and National Customer Day (Harpelmas 2025), which supported regional creative economic growth. (SEOJK F26)
