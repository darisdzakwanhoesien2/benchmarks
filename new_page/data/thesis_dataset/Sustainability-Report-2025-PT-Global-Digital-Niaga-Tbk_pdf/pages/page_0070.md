Chapter 04

Karyawan Kami, Kekuatan Kami
Our People, Our Strength

Seluruh karyawan tetap terdaftar dalam program pensiun wajib melalui: BRJS Ketenagakerjaan, yang mencakup Jaminan Hari Tua (JHT) dan Jaminan Pensiun (JP). Untuk tiket.com, Ranch Market, dan Dekoruma, iuran JHT terdiri atas kontribusi 3,7% dari Perseroan dan 2% dari karyawan, serta iuran JP sebesar 2% dari Perseroan dan 1% dari karyawan. Sementara itu, untuk Blibli, seluruh iuran program tersebut sepenuhnya ditanggung oleh Perseroan. Hingga akhir tahun 2025, 100% karyawan tetap telah berpartisipasi dalam program pensiun tersebut. [GRI 201-3]

Selain program BRJS, Perseroan juga mengakui kewajiban imbalan pasca kerja jangka panjang sesuai dengan ketentuan ketenagakerjaan yang berlaku, yang dihitung menggunakan metode aktuana Projected Unit Credit dan didanai melalui sumber daya umum Perseroan, sebagaimana diungkapkan dalam laporan keuangan konsolidasian yang telah diaudit. Perseroan secara berkala meninjau kecukupan pendanaan dan asumsi aktuana, serta mempertimbangkan pengembangan pengaturan pensiun tambahan sebagai bagian dari strategi jangka panjang untuk meningkatkan kesejahteraan karyawan. [GRI 201-3]

Karyawan tidak tetap dari non-permanen memperoleh manfaat kerja sesuai dengan peraturan ketenagakerjaan dan ketentuan hubungan kerja yang berlaku, namun tidak termasuk dalam program pensiun, kepemilikan saham, maupun manfaat asuransi jiwa tambahan yang diperuntukkan bagi karyawan tetap. [GRI 401-2]

Pada tahun 2025, Perseroan memperkuat kesejahteraan emosional dan mental karyawan melalui kombinasi kampanye kesadaran, aktivitas refleksi, serta sesi pembelajaran dan dialog yang terintegrasi dalam program pengembangan kepemimpinan, pembelajaran berkelanjutan, dan komunikasi organisasi. Upaya ini didukung oleh penyediaan fasilitas kebugaran, komunitas hobi dan olahraga berbasis karyawan (employee-led communities), serta ruang kolaborasi yang mendorong interaksi sosial yang sehat. Pendekatan ini membantu karyawan menjaga keseimbangan emosional, membangun ketahanan diri, dan tetap terhubung secara positif di tengah dinamika pekerjaan dan transformasi organisasi.

# PEMANFAATAN HAK CUTI [GRI 401-3]

Perseroan menyediakan hak cuti yang komprehensif sebagai bagian dari kebijakan ketenagakerjaan untuk mendukung

All permanent employees are enrolled in mandatory retirement programs under BRJS Ketenagakerjaan, covering Jaminan Hari Tua (JHT) and Jaminan Pensiun (JP). For tiket.com, Ranch Market, and Dekoruma contributions comprise 3.7% by the employer and 2% by employees for JHT, and 2% by the employer and 1% by employees for JP. For Blibli, all contributions to these programs are fully borne by the Company. As of the end of 2025, 100% of permanent employees participated in these retirement schemes. [GRI 201-3]

In addition to BRJS programs, the Company recognizes long-term post-employment benefit obligations in accordance with applicable labor regulations, calculated using the Projected Unit Credit actuarial method and funded through the Company's general resources, as disclosed in the audited consolidated financial statements. The Company periodically reviews funding adequacy and actuarial assumptions and considers the development of additional pension arrangements as part of its long-term employee welfare strategy. [GRI 201-3]

Temporary and non-permanent workers receive benefits in accordance with labor regulations and their employment terms, but are not included in retirement programs, share ownership plans, or additional life insurance benefits designated for full-time employees. [GRI 401-2]

In 2025, the Company strengthened employees' emotional and mental well-being through a combination of awareness campaigns, reflective activities, and integrated learning and dialogue sessions embedded within leadership development, continuous learning, and organizational communication programs. These efforts were supported by the provision of wellness facilities, employee-led hobby and sports communities, and collaborative spaces that encourage healthy social interaction. This approach helps employees maintain emotional balance, build personal resilience, and remain positively connected amid workplace dynamics and organizational transformation.

Specifically, the Company provides parental leave entitlements to support employee well-being and work-life balance in accordance with applicable regulations. All permanent employees (100%) are eligible for parental leave. In 2025, parental leave was utilized by 144 female employees and 249 male employees.

Upon completion of parental leave, 95% of female employees and 100% of male employees returned to work. Of those who returned, 98% of female employees and 97% of male employees remained employed 12 months after returning to work, reflecting the Company's commitment to fostering a family-friendly and supportive workplace.

Parental leave provisions, including maternity and paternity leave, are implemented in line with statutory requirements and internal policies, with specific durations adjusted based on medical recommendations and applicable labor regulations.
