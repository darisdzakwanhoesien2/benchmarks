# MENGANTISIPASI RISIKO DAN MEMPERKUAT KETAHANAN BISNIS

[SEGJK E 3]

Bilbii Tiket menerapkan prosedur identifikasi, pengukuran, pemantauan, dan pengendalian risiko keberlanjutan yang mencakup aspek ekonomi, lingkungan, dan sosial dengan berpedoman pada ISO 31000: 2018 dan kerangka COSO. Proses ini dilakukan secara rutin melalui assemen risiko unit kerja, matriks peridaian risiko, serta pemantauan berkala untuk memastikan pengendalian berjalan efektif.

Pengelolaan risiko dilaksanakan melalui tiga lini pertahanan, di mana manajemen operasional bertanggung jawab atas kontrol harian, fungsi Risiko dan EHSS &amp; ESG memastikan monitoring serta kepatuhan, sementara Audit Internal mengevaluasi efektivitas sistem secara independen.

Direksi melakukan telaah berkala atas profil risiko dan efektivitas mitigasi, sedangkan Dewan Komisans mengawasi penerapan manajemen risiko serta memastikan proses mitigasi sesuai prinsip tata kelola. Pendekatan ini memastikan risiko keberlanjutan dikelola secara terintegrasi untuk mendukung ketahanan bisnis jangka panjang.

# ANTICIPATING RISKS AND STRENGTHENING BUSINESS RESILIENCE

[SEGJK E 3]

Bilbii Tiket applies structured procedures to identify, measure, monitor, and control sustainability risks across economic, environmental, and social aspects, guided by ISO 31000:2018 and the COSO framework. These processes are conducted regularly through risk assessments, scoring matrices, and ongoing monitoring to ensure effective controls.

Risk governance follows the three lines of defense model: operational management executes daily controls, the Risk and EHSS &amp; ESG functions oversee monitoring and compliance, and Internal Audit independently evaluates system effectiveness.

The Board of Directors conducts periodic reviews of key risks and mitigation performance, while the Board of Commissioners supervises the overall risk management effectiveness and alignment with good governance practices. This approach ensures sustainability risks are managed in an integrated manner to support long-term business resilience.
