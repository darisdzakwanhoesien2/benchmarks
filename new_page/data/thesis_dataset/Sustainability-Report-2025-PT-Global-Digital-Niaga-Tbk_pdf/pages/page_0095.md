# MENGOPTIMALKAN PENGGUNAAN DAN EFISIENSI ENERGI [SEOJ:K F7] [GRI 302-1, 302-2, 302-3, 302-4, 302-5]

Perseroan menggunakan energi listrik dan bahan bakar minyak (BBM) untuk mendukung kegiatan operasional, termasuk operasional fasilitas perkantoran, gudang, dan toko fisik, penggunaan genset, serta kendaraan pengantaran BES Paket. Selama periode pelaporan, Perseroan tidak menggunakan energi pemanas (heating), pendingin non-listrik (cooling), maupun uap (steam), serta tidak melakukan penjualan kembali energi kepada pihak lain. [GRI 302-1][SEOJ:K F6]

Sepanjang tahun 2025, total konsumsi energi Perseroan tercatat sebesar 248.570 GJ, yang terdiri dari konsumsi bahan bakar minyak (BBM) sebesar 14.580 GJ dan konsumsi listrik sebesar 233.990 GJ. Jumlah ini merupakan hasil konsolidasi konsumsi energi dalam kegiatan operasional Bibli Tiket. Perubahan jumlah antar-periode tidak sepenuhnya mencerminkan perubahan performa operasional, namun juga dipengaruhi oleh perubahan batas pelaporan, sehingga data reduksi energi belum tersedia. Perseroan akan terus melakukan penyempurnaan cakupan dan metodologi pelaporan secara bertahap guna meningkatkan kelengkapan dan akurasi data. [GRI 302-1, 302-4][SEOJ:K F6]

# OPTIMIZING ENERGY USE AND EFFICIENCY [SEOJ:K F7] [GRI 302-1, 302-2, 302-3, 302-4, 302-5]

The Company uses electricity and fuel to support its operations, including office, warehouse, and physical store activities, the operation of generators, as well as BES Paket delivery vehicles. During the reporting period, the Company did not utilize heating, non-electric cooling, or steam, nor did it sell energy to any third parties. [GRI 302-5][SEOJ:K F6]

Throughout 2025, the Company's total energy consumption amounted to 248,570 GJ, consisting of fuel consumption of 14,580 GJ and electricity consumption of 233,990 GJ. This figure represents the consolidated energy consumption across Bibli Tiket's operational activities. Changes between periods do not fully reflect operational performance, but are also influenced by changes in reporting boundaries; therefore, energy reduction data is not yet available. The Company will continue to gradually refine the scope and reporting methodology to improve the completeness and accuracy of the data. [GRI 302-1, 302-4][SEOJ:K F6]

Energy intensity is calculated by comparing total energy consumption against the Company's net revenue. In 2025, the Company's energy intensity was recorded at 1112 GJ/Rp billion. This figure reflects the level of energy use in Bibli Tiket's consolidated operational activities, although data on energy intensity reduction are not yet available. The Company continues to pursue energy efficiency in its operations, including through the application of the Kaizen approach for energy savings, such as optimizing distribution routes and monitoring the temperature stability of cooling systems at operational facilities.
