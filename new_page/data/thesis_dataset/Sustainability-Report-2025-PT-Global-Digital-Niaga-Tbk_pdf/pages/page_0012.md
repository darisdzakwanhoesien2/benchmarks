# Metodologi dan Lingkup Perhitungan Emisi Gas Rumah Kaca (GRK)

Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Emissions Calculation Methodology and Scope

Pada tahun 2025, Blibli Tiket terus memperbarui dan menyempurnakan metodologi perhitungan emisi GRK untuk memastikan akurasi, konsistensi, dan cakupan yang lebih komprehensif di seluruh aktivitas operasional. Pembaruan ini dilakukan sebagai bagian dari komitmen Perseroan untuk mengelola emisi secara transparan dan terukur guna mendukung target pengurangan emisi jangka panjang.

Perhitungan emisi gas rumah kaca (GRK) Perseroan dilakukan dengan mengacu pada pedoman gas rumah kaca yang diakui secara nasional dan internasional untuk memastikan akurasi, konsistensi, dan keterbandingan pelaporan dan tahun ke tahun. Penjelasan lebih rinci mengenai sumber data, faktor emisi, dan metodologi perhitungan disajikan pada bagian Data dan Metrik.

In 2025, Blibli Tiket continued to refine and enhance its Greenhouse Gas (GHG) emissions calculation methodology to ensure greater accuracy, consistency, and comprehensive coverage across all operational activities. This update reflects the Company's commitment to transparent and measurable emissions management in support of its long-term reduction goals.

The Company's greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions are calculated in accordance with nationally and internationally recognized GHG guidelines to ensure accuracy, consistency, and comparability of emissions reporting over time. Further details on data sources, emission factors, and calculation methodologies are provided in the Data and Metric section.

# Proses Assurance Independen untuk Menjamin Keandalan Laporan

Independent Assurance Process to Ensure Report Realibility [SEOJK G1][GRI 2-5]

Laporan Keberlanjutan ini telah melalui proses external assurance independen yang dilakukan oleh PT Sucofindo selaku penyedia jasa assurance. Proses assurance tersebut dilaksanakan berdasarkan AA1000 Assurance Standard (AA1000AS v3) dengan tingkat assurance Type 2 Level Moderate. Ruang lingkup assurance mencakup penelashan atas kesesuaian laporan dengan GRI Standards 2021, serta keakuratan dan keandalan data kinerja ekonomi, sosial, dan lingkungan yang disajikan, termasuk efektivitas proses pengumpulan dan validasi data yang mendasarinya. [SEOJK G1] [GRI 2-5]

Pernyataan Assurance Independen dapat dilihat pada Lampiran halaman 248 dalam Laporan ini. Pernyataan tersebut merupakan bagian yang tidak terpisahkan dari Laporan ini dan perlu dibaca bersama Laporan Keberlanjutan ini untuk memahami secara utuh ruang lingkup, metodologi, batasan, dan kesimpulan assurance yang diberikan. [SEOJK G1] [GRI 2-5]

This Sustainability Report has undergone an independent external assurance process conducted by PT Sucofindo as the assurance provider. The assurance engagement was carried out in accordance with the AA1000 Assurance Standard (AA1000AS v3) with an assurance level of Type 2 Level Moderate. The scope of the assurance included a review of the Report's alignment with the GRI Standards 2021, as well as the accuracy and reliability of the economic, social, and environmental performance data presented, including the effectiveness of the underlying data collection and validation processes. [SEOJK G1] [GRI 2-5]

The Independent Assurance Statement can be found in the Appendix on page 248 of this Report. The statement forms an integral part of this Report and should be read together with this Sustainability Report to obtain a complete understanding of the scope, methodology, limitations, and conclusions of the assurance provided. [SEOJK G1] [GRI 2-5]

Direksi bersama divisi terkait turut berperan dalam proses pemilihan penyedia assurance, penentuan ruang lingkup assurance, serta peninjauan hasil assurance sebelum Laporan ini diterbitkan. Meskipun demikian, pelaksanaan prosedur assurance, proses penilaian, dan penyusunan kesimpulan assurance tetap sepenuhnya menjadi tanggung jawab penyedia assurance independen. [GRI 2-5]

The Board of Directors, together with the relevant divisions, was involved in the process of selecting the assurance provider, determining the scope of the assurance, and reviewing the assurance results prior to the publication of this Report. However, the execution of the assurance procedures, the assessment process, and the preparation of the assurance conclusions remained entirely the responsibility of the independent assurance provider. [GRI 2-5]
