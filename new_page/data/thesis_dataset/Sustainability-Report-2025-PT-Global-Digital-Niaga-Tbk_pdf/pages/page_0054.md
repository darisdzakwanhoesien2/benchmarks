# Pengelolaan Risiko Keberlanjutan dan Penegakan Integritas

Sustainability Risk Management and Integrity Governance

## KOMITMEN DAN PENERAPAN KEBIJAKAN UNTUK BISNIS YANG BERTANGGUNG JAWAB (GRI 2-23, 2-24)

Biblii Tiket berkomitmen menjalankan kegiatan usaha secara bertanggung jawab, beretika, dan berintegritas sebagaimana dituangkan dalam Kode Etik dan Pedoman Perilaku yang berlaku bagi Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, karyawan, serta mitra seller. Kode Etik ini menjadi dasar penerapan prinsip kejujuran, keadilan, kepatuhan hukum, dan tata kelola perusahaan yang baik dalam seluruh aktivitas usaha dan hubungan bisnis Perseroan. (GRI 2-23)

Komitmen kebijakan tersebut diterapkan di seluruh tingkatan organisasi dan diintegrasikan ke dalam strategi, kebijakan operasional, serta prosedur kerja, termasuk dalam pengadaan barang dan jasa, pengelolaan risiko, dan pengelolaan hubungan dengan mitra seller. Direksi bertanggung jawab atas pengawasan penerapan kebijakan, dengan dukungan Departemen Legal, Human Capital, dan Internal Audit dalam pelaksanaan, pemantauan, dan pelaporan kepatuhan secara berkala. (GRI 2-24)

Biblii Tiket menegaskan larangan terhadap segala bentuk korupsi, gratifikasi, penyalahgunaan wewenang, dan diskriminasi, serta berkomitmen menghormati Hak Asasi Manusia (HAM) melalui praktik ketenagakerjaan yang adil, kesetaraan kesempatan kerja, dan perlindungan bagi kelompok rentan. Perseroan juga menerapkan mekanisme WBS sebagai sarana pelaporan pelanggaran yang menjamin kerahasiaan dan mendukung proses due diligence dalam mengidentifikasi dan memitigasi risiko ekonomi, sosial, dan lingkungan. (GRI 2-23, 2-24)

Untuk memastikan pemahaman dan penerapan yang konsisten, Perseroan menyelenggarakan pelatihan dan sosialisasi Kode Etik secara berkala kepada karyawan melalui platform internal dan sesi tatap muka. Kode Etik dan Pedoman Perilaku telah disahkan oleh Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris serta berlaku untuk seluruh kegiatan usaha dan hubungan bisnis Perseroan. Kode Etik dan Pedoman Perilaku Perseroan dapat diakses melalui tautan berikut: https://about.biblii.com/sl/about (GRI 2-23, 2-24)

## POLICY COMMITMENTS AND IMPLEMENTATION FOR RESPONSIBLE BUSINESS CONDUCT (GRI 2-23, 2-24)

Biblii Tiket is committed to conducting its business activities in a responsible, ethical, and integrity-driven manner, as set out in the Code of Ethics and Code of Conduct applicable to the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, employees, and seller partners. The Code of Ethics serves as the foundation for upholding principles of honesty, fairness, legal compliance, and good corporate governance across all business activities and relationships of the Company. (GRI 2-23)

These policy commitments are implemented across all levels of the organization and integrated into the Company's strategies, operational policies, and work procedures, including procurement, risk management, and the management of relationships with seller partners. The Board of Directors is responsible for overseeing the implementation of these policies, supported by the Legal, Human Capital, and Internal Audit Departments in carrying out, monitoring, and periodically reporting on compliance. (GRI 2-24)

Biblii Tiket strictly prohibits all forms of corruption, gratification, abuse of authority, and discrimination, and is committed to respecting human rights through fair labor practices, equal employment opportunities, and the protection of vulnerable groups. The Company also implements WBS as a reporting mechanism for violations that ensures confidentiality and supports due diligence processes in identifying and mitigating economic, social, and environmental risks. (GRI 2-23, 2-24)

To ensure consistent understanding and application, the Company conducts regular training and socialization of the Code of Ethics for employees through internal digital platforms and in-person sessions. The Code of Ethics and Code of Conduct have been approved by Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners and apply to all of the Company's business activities and relationships. The Company's Code of Ethics and Code of Conduct can be accessed at the following link: https://about.biblii.com/en/about. (GRI 2-23, 2-24)

## Kepatuhan terhadap Hukum dan Peraturan (GRI 2-27)

Biblii Tiket menjalankan seluruh kegiatan usaha sesuai dengan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku di Indonesia, termasuk keterituan di bidang pasar modal, ketenagakerjaan, perpajakan, dan perlindungan konsumen. Pengawasan atas kepatuhan dilakukan melalui fungsi yang relevan sesuai dengan ruang lingkupnya, yaitu kepatuhan pasar modal oleh Corporate Secretary, kepatuhan ketenagakerjaan oleh fungsi Industrial Relations, kepatuhan perpajakan oleh fungsi Tax, serta kepatuhan perlindungan konsumen oleh fungsi Legal and Compliance. Seluruh fungsi tersebut melaksanakan pemantauan secara berkala dan menyampaikan pelaporan kepada Direksi.

Sepanjang tahun 2025, tidak terdapat kasus ketidakpatuhan signifikan terhadap hukum dan peraturan yang berlaku, baik yang mengakibatkan sanksi denda maupun sanksi non-moneter. Dengan demikian, jumlah total kasus ketidakpatuhan signifikan adalah nihil (0), dengan nilai total denda sebesar Rp0.

Perseroan mendefinisikan ketidakpatuhan signifikan sebagai pelanggaran yang berpotensi menimbulkan dampak material terhadap operasional, keuangan, atau reputasi perusahaan. Mekanisme evaluasi dan tindakan korektif diterapkan apabila terdapat potensi pelanggaran, guna mencegah terulangnya kejadian serupa.

## Compliance with Laws and Regulations (GRI 2-27)

Biblii Tiket conducts all of its business activities in compliance with the prevailing laws and regulations in Indonesia, including those related to the capital market, employment, taxation, and consumer protection. Compliance oversight is carried out by the relevant functions in accordance with their respective scopes, namely capital market compliance by the Corporate Secretary, employment compliance by the Industrial Relations function, tax compliance by the Tax function, and consumer protection compliance by the Legal and Compliance function. All functions conduct periodic monitoring and report to the Board of Directors.

Throughout 2025, there were no significant instances of non-compliance with prevailing laws and regulations, whether resulting in monetary fines or non-monetary sanctions. Accordingly, the total number of significant non-compliance cases was zero (0), with a total value of fines amounting to Rp0.

The Company defines significant non-compliance as any violation that has the potential to result in material impacts on its operations, financial condition, or reputation. Evaluation mechanisms and corrective actions are implemented whenever potential non-compliance is identified, in order to prevent the recurrence of similar incidents.
