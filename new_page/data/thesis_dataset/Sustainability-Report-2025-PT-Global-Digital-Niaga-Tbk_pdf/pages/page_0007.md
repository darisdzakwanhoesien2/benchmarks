# Action for Our Planet

As part of its ongoing commitment, Bibli Tiket continues to strengthen this pillar through the implementation of circular economy principles based on reduce, reuse, and recycle, integrated across its business processes. This approach supports resource efficiency, the use of more environmentally friendly materials, and the application of automation to reduce emissions and operational waste.

Selain optimalisasi internal, Perseroan juga mendorong partisipasi pelanggan dalam praktik keberlanjutan melalui program pengembalian kemasan, opsi Green Delivery, dan fitur Misi Tanam Pohon. Upaya ini bertujuan membangun ekosistem keberlanjutan yang inklusif dan kolaboratif di seluruh lini bisnis.

Sebagai bentuk komitmen dalam mengelola penggunaan material, Perseroan melakukan beberapa inisiatif, termasuk penggunaan kardus bersertifikat FSC, shredded cardboard, serta optimalisasi penggunaan kembali kardus paket. Melalui program pengembalian kemasan, pelanggan dapat mengembalikan kemasan bekas kepada kurn untuk kemudian dipakai kembali atau dijual. Sepanjang tahun 2025, program ini berhasil mengumpulkan hampir 14.500 kemasan bekas sebagai bagian dari komitmen ekonomi sirkular Perseroan sekaligus mendukung misi penanaman satu pohon untuk setiap sepuluh kemasan bekas yang dikembalikan.

Dalam upaya mengelola dampak lingkungan dari aktivitas logistik, selain dengan pemakaian kendaraan listrik untuk armada logistik internal, Bibli Tiket memperluas kolaborasi dengan mitra bisnis yang memungkinkan pelanggan memilih opsi pengiriman Green Delivery yang lebih ramah lingkungan. Sepanjang tahun berjalan, sejauh lebih dari 181.000 km telah ditempuh menggunakan kendaraan listrik untuk pengantaran pesanan.

Pada tahun 2025, Bibli Tiket juga meluncurkan fitur keberlanjutan baru bernama Misi Tanam Pohon, yang memungkinkan pelanggan memberikan kontribusi penanaman pohon untuk transaksi produk fisik maupun produk digital. Sepanjang tahun berjalan, seluruh inisiatif penanaman pohon menghasilkan 16.000 pohon mangrove telah ditanam atau meningkat 2,3 kali dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya, sehingga total pohon yang ditanam sejak 2021 mencapai 31.000 pohon.

Edukasi keberlanjutan juga diwujudkan secara kolaboratif melalui Langkah Membumi Ecoground 2025, sebuah acara keberlanjutan yang menjadi wadah kolaborasi lintas sektor yang telah dimulai sejak 2022. Acara keberlanjutan ini menghadirkan berbagai kegiatan seperti tailshow, workshop, pasar ecopreneur, festival musik, olahraga, pemeriksaan kesehatan, dan aktivitas ramah lingkungan lainnya.

In addition to internal optimization efforts, the Company also encourages customer participation in sustainability practices through packaging return programs, Green Delivery options, and Misi Tanam Pohon feature. These efforts aim to build an inclusive and collaborative sustainability ecosystem across all business lines.

As part of its commitment to managing material usage, the Company has implemented several initiatives, including the use of FSC-certified boxes, shredded cardboard, and the optimization of factory packaging reuse. Through its reverse logistics program, customers are able to return used packaging to counters for reuse or resale. Throughout 2025, this program collected nearly 14,500 used packages as part of the Company's circular economy commitment and in support of its mission to plant one tree for every ten used packaging returned.

In its efforts to reduce environmental impact from logistic activities, in addition to the use of electric vehicles for its internal logistics fleet, Bibli Tiket expanded collaborations with business partners to enable customers to choose more environmentally friendly Green Delivery options. During the year, more than 181,000 km were traveled using electric vehicles for order deliveries.

In 2025, Bibli Tiket also launched a new sustainability feature called the Tree Planting Mission, which enables customers to contribute to tree planting through transaction of both physical and digital products. Throughout the year, all tree-planting initiatives resulted in the planting of 16,000 mangrove trees, representing a 2.3-fold increase compared with the previous year, bringing the cumulative total of trees planted since 2021 to 31,000.

Sustainability education was also delivered collaboratively through Langkah Membumi Ecoground 2025, a sustainability event that has served as a cross-sector collaboration platform since its inception in 2022. The event featured a range of activities, including talk shows, workshops, an ecopreneur market, music festivals, sports activities, health screenings, and other environmentally friendly initiatives.

In 2025, the event engaged more than 120 collaboration partners and through its implementation, achieved emissions reductions of 124.100, es compared to the previous year. All event activities embraced sustainability principles, including the reuse of materials for event and decoration purposes.
