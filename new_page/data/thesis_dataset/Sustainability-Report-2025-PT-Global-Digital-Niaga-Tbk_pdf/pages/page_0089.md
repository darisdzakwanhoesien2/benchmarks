# Tahapan Penerapan Ekonomi Sirkular

Penerapan konsep ekonomi sirkular dalam aktivitas logistik dilakukan dengan mengintegrasikan prinsip reuse, reduce, dan recycle (3R) di setiap tahap rantai pasok, mulai dari proses pengadaan, pemenuhan pesanan, pengiriman barang, hingga pengembalian produk. [SEGUK F14][GRI 3-3]

These three principles are applied consistently across all operations, including logistics activities.

# Stages of Circular Economy Implementation

The circular economy approach in logistics operations is carried out by integrating the reuse, reduce, and recycle (3R) principles into every stage of the supply chain, from procurement and order fulfillment to product delivery and return processes. [SEGUK F14][GRI 3-3]
