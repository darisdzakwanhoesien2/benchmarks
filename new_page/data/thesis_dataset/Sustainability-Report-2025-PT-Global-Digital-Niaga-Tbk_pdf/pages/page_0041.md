|  Sebagai bagian dari komitmen terhadap tata kelola yang transparan dan berkelanjutan, Bibli Tiket menerapkan pendekatan, perpajakan yang patuh, bertanggung jawab, dan selaras dengan strategi bisnis serta kontribusi terhadap perekonomian nasional. Pendekatan pajak Perseroan berlandaskan prinsip kepatuhan terhadap peraturan yang berlaku, baik dari sisi formal maupun substansi, serta menghindari praktik perpajakan agresif yang berpotensi menimbulkan risiko reputasi dan keuangan. [GRI 207-1] | As part of its commitment to transparent and sustainable governance, Bibli Tiket applies a tax approach that is compliant, responsible, and aligned with its business strategy as well as its contribution to the national economy. The Company's tax approach is grounded in adherence to applicable tax laws and regulations, both in form and in substance, and avoids aggressive tax practices that could give rise to reputational and financial risks. [GRI 207-1] |   |
|  Tata kelola perpajakan berada di bawah pengawasan Direktur Keuangan, dengan dukungan sistem, pengendalian internal, serta proses pemantauan yang memastikan kepatuhan dan pengelolaan risiko pajak secara berkelanjutan. Risiko perpajakan diidentifikasi, dikelola, dan dievaluasi secara berkala sebagai bagian dari kerangka manajemen risiko Perseroan. Pada tahun 2025, Perseroan mulai mengimplementasikan sistem Coretax untuk mendukung pelaporan dan pemotongan pajak secara lebih terstandar dan efisien. [GRI 207-2] | Tax governance is overseen by the Finance Director, supported by systems, internal controls, and monitoring processes that ensure ongoing compliance and effective tax risk management. Tax risks are identified, managed, and periodically evaluated as part of the Company's overall risk management framework. In 2025, the Company began implementing the Coretax system to support more standardized and efficient tax reporting and withholding processes. [GRI 207-2] |   |
|  Bibli Tiket secara aktif berinteraksi dengan otoritas pajak dan pemangku kepentingan terkait dalam rangka klarifikasi regulasi, kepatuhan administrasi, serta pengelolaan risiko. Perseroan tidak terlibat dalam advokasi kebijakan publik terkait pajak di luar kondor kepatuhan dan konsultasi yang sah. [GRI 207-3] | Bibli Tiket actively engages with tax authorities and relevant stakeholders for the purposes of regulatory clarification, administrative compliance, and tax risk management. The Company does not engage in public policy advocacy related to taxation beyond lawful compliance and formal consultation processes. [GRI 207-3] |   |
