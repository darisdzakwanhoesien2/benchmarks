# Blilearning

Platform belajar daring Blibli dengan kurikulum soft skill, technical skill, dan keberlanjutan (data security, kejahatan siber, gaya hidup hijau, operasi berkelanjutan). Sepanjang 2025, Blilearning digunakan oleh 1550 pengguna dalam format artikel, video, audio, dan infografis.

Blibli online learning platform with a curriculum covering soft skills, technical skills, and sustainability topics (data security, cybercrime prevention, green lifestyle, and sustainable operations). Throughout 2025, Blilearning was used by 1550 users in article, video, audio, and infographic formats.

# Blibli Talks

Sesi satu jam bersama pakar internal dan eksternal yang terbuka bagi karyawan maupun publik untuk berbagi praktik terbaik seputar inovasi, teknologi, dan kepemimpinan. Pada tahun 2025, terselenggara 5 sesi dengan berbagai topik, termasuk pengembangan soft skill, kepemimpinan, strategic mindset, dan empati untuk mendorong pertumbuhan bisnis.

A one-hour session with internal and external experts, open to employees and the public, to share best practices on innovation, technology, and leadership. In 2025, 5 sessions were conducted covering various topics, including soft skill development, leadership, strategic mindset, and empathy to drive business growth.

# tiket Academy

Payung pengembangan karyawan di tiket.com dengan pendekatan 3E (Experience, Empowerment, Education) di tiga pilar: Employee, Community, dan Youth Development. Tersedia platform situs sebagai one-stop learning yang bisa diakses kapan saja.

An employee development umbrella program at tiket.com applying the 3E approach (Experience, Empowerment, Education) across three pillars: Employee, Community, and Youth Development. Supported by a website-based platform as a one-stop learning resource accessible anytime.

# ten-x (tiket Empower Network of Xpertise)

Komunitas peer-to-peer lintas fungsi untuk berbagi keahlian tanpa batasan format. Hingga tahun 2025 telah berlangsung 78 sesi, melibatkan 4,735 peserta (internal/eksternal), menghadirkan 40 ahli lintas negara, dengan skor umpan balik 4,6/5.

A cross-functional peer-to-peer learning community that facilitates knowledge and skill sharing without format limitations. By 2025, 78 sessions had been conducted, involving 4,735 participants (internal and external), featuring 40 experts from different countries, and achieving a feedback score of 4.6/5.
