# HAPPY Award

Sebagai wujud apresiasi terhadap kontribusi luar biasa karyawan, tiket.com kembali mengadakan program penghargaan tahunan yang pertama kali diperkenalkan pada tahun 2023. Pada tahun 2025, penghargaan ini dilokuskan untuk mengapresiasi anggota tim, pemimpin, dan proyek yang memberikan dampak paling signifikan bagi perusahaan. Terdapat 487 nominasi dan 11 fungsi bisnis yang berbeda yang berpartisipasi dalam proses seleksi. Pemenang penghargaan ditetapkan melalui proses pemungutan suara karyawan yang transparan, dan menghasilkan 10 individu dan 3 kelompok terbaik di tahun 2025.

As part of its commitment to recognizing exceptional employee contributions, tiket.com once again held its annual awards program, which was first introduced in 2023. In 2025, the program focused on honoring team members, leaders, and projects that delivered the most significant impact to the company. There were 487 nominations across 11 business functions who participated in the selection process. The winners were selected through a transparent employee voting process, resulting in the recognition of 10 outstanding individuals and 3 best groups in 2025.

# Best Retail Offline Team

Dekoruma memberikan apresiasi atas pencapaian karyawan dalam fungsi Retail Offline melalui program penghargaan tahunan. Pada tahun 2025, terdapat 10 kategori penghargaan yang diberikan kepada 21 karyawan berprestasi. Para pemenang akan memperoleh hadiah berupa perjalanan wisata ke Jepang yang dijadwalkan pada tahun 2026 sebagai bentuk apresiasi atas kontribusi dan kinerja mereka.

Dekoruma recognizes the achievements of employees within the Offline Retail function through its annual award program. In 2025, a total of 10 award categories were presented to 21 outstanding employees. The winners will receive a complimentary trip to Japan, scheduled for 2026, as a token of appreciation for their contributions and performance.

# Tumbuh Bersama Komunitas

# Growing Together with Communities

[SEGUK.F25] [GRI 3-3, 413-1, 413-2]

Perseroan melaksanakan program Tanggung Jawab Sosial dan Lingkungan (TJSL) yang dirancang untuk memberikan dampak sosial yang inklusif dan berkelanjutan. Melalui kolaborasi dengan pemerintah, mitra seller, komunitas, dan berbagai organisasi, Blibli Tiket memfokuskan inisiatifnya pada pengembangan dan pembedayaan generasi muda, termasuk penyandang disabilitas; penguatan sektor panwisata melalui peningkatan kompetensi dan digitalisasi UMKM; serta pembedayaan komunitas melalui berbagai program bantuan sosial yang bertujuan meningkatkan kesejahteraan masyarakat. Inisiatif ini bertujuan untuk meningkatkan kesejahteraan sosial sekaligus mengelola dampak positif maupun negatif dan kegiatan operasional, termasuk rantai pasok serta infrastruktur fisik dan digital. Selain itu, pelaksanaan program TJSL juga memperhatikan isu global dan upaya mitigasi risiko agar dapat berkontribusi terhadap pencapaian TPB. [SEGUK.F23, F25-F28][GRI 3-3, 413-1]

Untuk memastikan setiap program memberikan manfaat yang optimal bagi masyarakat, Blibli Tiket melakukan pengukuran dampak secara berkala melalui kegiatan monitoring dan evaluasi. Proses ini dijalankan secara kolaboratif oleh beberapa fungsi terkait, termasuk Departemen Human Capital, ESG, dan Public Relations. Hasil evaluasi selanjutnya disampaikan kepada masing-masing Direktur untuk ditindaklanjuti sesuai dengan kewenangan dan kebutuhan organisasi. [GRI 3-3, 413-1]

Selain itu, Berdasarkan penilaian internal terhadap seluruh lokasi operasional, hingga akhir tahun pelaporan 2025, Perseroan tidak mengidentifikasi adanya dampak aktual maupun potensi dampak negatif yang signifikan terhadap masyarakat lokal yang timbul dan kegiatan operasional. Seluruh aktivitas usaha Blibli Tiket, termasuk kantor, gudang, pusat distribusi, serta infrastruktur digital, berlokasi di kawasan komersial dan perkotaan yang telah memiliki izin tata ruang dan tidak berada di wilayah dengan tingkat kerentanan sosial tinggi atau komunitas adat. Potensi dampak sosial yang mungkin timbul, seperti lalu lintas logistik dan hubungan tidak langsung dalam rantai pasok, dikelola melalui kepatuhan perizinan, standar operasional, serta koordinasi dengan mitra seller dan otoritas setempat. Separang periode pelaporan, Perseroan tidak menerima pengaduan masyarakat yang bersifat material dan tidak terdapat insiden yang memerlukan tindakan remediasi. [GRI 413-2]

The Company implements Social and Environmental Responsibility programs designed to deliver inclusive and sustainable social impact. Through collaboration with the government, sellers, communities, and various organizations, Blibli Tiket focuses its initiatives on the development and empowerment of youth, including persons with disabilities; strengthening the tourism sector through capacity building and digitalization of MSMEs; and empowering communities through various social assistance programs aimed at improving societal well-being. These initiatives aim to improve social/welfare while managing both the positive and negative impacts of operations, including supply chain activities and physical as well as digital infrastructure. Furthermore, the implementation of social and environmental responsibility programs also considers global issues and risk mitigation efforts to contribute to the achievement of SDGs. [SEGUK.F23, F25-F28][GRI 3-3, 413-1]

To ensure that each program delivers meaningful impact, Blibli Tiket conducts periodic monitoring and evaluation to measure its effectiveness. This process is carried out collaboratively by several relevant functions, including Human Capital, ESG and Public Relations departments. The evaluation results are subsequently submitted to the respective Board of Directors for follow-up in accordance with their roles and organizational needs. [GRI 3-3, 413-1]

In addition, based on internal assessments across all operational locations, as of the end of the 2025 reporting period, the Company identified no significant actual or potential negative impacts on local communities arising from its operations. All Blibli Tiket activities, including offices, warehouses, distribution centers, and digital infrastructure, are located in designated commercial and urban areas and do not operate in socially vulnerable areas or indigenous communities. Potential social impacts, such as logistics traffic and indirect relationships within the supply chain, are managed through regulatory compliance, operational standards, and coordination with business partners and local authorities. During the reporting period, no material community complaints were received and no incidents requiring remediation were recorded. [GRI 413-2]
