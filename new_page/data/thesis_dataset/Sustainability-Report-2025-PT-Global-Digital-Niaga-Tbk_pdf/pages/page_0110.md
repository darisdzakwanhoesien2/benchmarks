# Sumber Emisi dan Metodologi Perhitungan Emisi GRK

Summary of Emission Sources and GHG Calculation Methodology

|  Sumber Emisi Emission Source | Lingkap Tasque | Basis Perhitungan Activity Data Basis | Metode Perhitungan & Standar Calculation Method & Standards  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  Pembakaran Stasoner – Genset Stationary Combustion – Generators | Scope 1 | Konsumsi BBM genset berdasarkan catatan penggunaan di lokasi operasional. Fuel consumption of generators based on consumption records at operational sites. | Perhitungan konsumsi BBM mengikuti pedoman kalkulator hijau Bank Indonesia (2024) dengan pendekatan konsumsi BBM + faktor emisi. Fuel consumption calculated using the green calculator Bank Indonesia (2024) guidelines with a fuel consumption + emission factor approach.  |
|  Pembakaran Kendaraan – Mobil kantor, mobil logistik, motor kurir Mobile Combustion – Office cars, logistics vehicles, courier motorcycles | Scope 1 | Data pembelian BBM kendaraan operasional. Operational vehicle fuel purchase data. | Perhitungan emisi kendaraan mengikuti pedoman kalkulator hijau Bank Indonesia (2024). Vehicle emissions calculated using the green calculator Bank Indonesia (2024) guidelines.  |
|  Emisi Fugitive – Refrigerants & Fire Suppressants Fugitive Emissions – Refrigerants & Fire Suppressants | Scope 1 | Data pengisian dan penggunaan refrigeran serta fire suppressants disesuaikan dengan periode operasional lokasi. Refrigerant and fire suppressant consumption data adjusted to each location’s operational period. | Perhitungan berdasarkan jumlah refrigeran yang digunakan dikalikan nilai Global Warming Potential sesuai referensi internasional yang berlaku. Emissions calculated based on the amount of refrigerant used multiplied by the applicable Global Warming Potential from internationally recognized references.  |
|  Konsumsi Listrik Lokasi Operasional Electricity Consumption – Operational Sites | Scope 2 | Konsumsi listrik (kWh) kantor, gudang, hub logistik, dan toko fisik yang dikelola langsung. Electricity consumption (kWh) from offices, warehouses, logistics hubs, and directly managed physical stores. | Menggunakan faktor emisi listrik dari Direktorat Jenderal Ketenagalistrikan Kementerian ESDM (2019) dengan pendekatan kWh + faktor emisi. Calculated using electricity emission factors usually the Directorate General of Electricity, Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources (2019) with a kWh + emission factor approach.  |
|  Barang dan Jasa yang Dibek – Packaging Purchased Goods & Services – Packaging | Scope 3 | Volume dan jenis material kemasan berdasarkan data pengadaan pemasok. Volume and type of packaging materials based on supplier procurement data. | Perhitungan menggunakan pendekatan data aktivitas + faktor emisi berdasarkan faktor emisi material yang relevan. Calculated using an activity data + emission factor approach with relevant material emission factors.  |
|  Transportasi & Distribusi Hulu – Pemasok ke Gudang/Toko Upstream Transportation & Distribution – Supplier to Warehouse/Store | Scope 3 | Data pengiriman logistik dari pemasok ke gudang dan toko. Logistics data for supplier deliveries to warehouses and stores. | Perhitungan menggunakan jarak dan moda transportasi (ton-km + faktor emisi) sesuai faktor konversi DEFRA 2025. Calculated using distance and transport mode (ton-km + emission factor) in line with the DEFRA 2025 conversion factors.  |
