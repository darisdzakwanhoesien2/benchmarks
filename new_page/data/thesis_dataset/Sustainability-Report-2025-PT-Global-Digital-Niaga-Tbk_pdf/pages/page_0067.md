# MENDENGARKAN KARYAWAN DAN MEMPERKUAT KETERLIBATAN DI SELURUH EKOSISTEM

Pada tahun 2024, Perseroan melaksanakan survei Employee Satisfaction (ESAT) untuk memahami pengalaman dan kebutuhan karyawan di seluruh entitas. Temuan dari ESAT 2024 menunjukkan pertanyaan peningkatan arahan kepemimpinan, khususnya terkait perasaan karyawan yang belum sepenuhnya didengar atau dipahami. Sebagai tindak lanjut, sepanjang 2025 Perseroan memperkuat komunikasi dan kedekatan melalui berbagai inisiatif.

Untuk mengukur keterlibatan lintas generasi dan anak usaha, Perseroan mengkombinasikan survei tahunan, kanal umpan balik kualitatif, serta pemantauan tren melalui BOBA (Biblioneers Bot Assistant). Data pertanyaan dan pola umpan balik dianalisis melalui Google Data Studio, diperkuat dengan diskusi kelompok dan dialog tim untuk memahami konteks mendalam dari masing-masing kelompok karyawan.

Sepanjang 2025, Perseroan membangun budaya kerja yang lebih inklusif dan kolaboratif melalui peluncuran nilai budaya baru, Winning Principles: Customer First, Act Fast &amp; Practical, Be the Solution, dan Continuous Improvement. Nilai ini diterapkan melalui pendekatan proses bisnis dan pendekatan manusia. Pada proses bisnis, nilai diterapkan di seluruh perjalanan kerja karyawan, mulai dari employer branding, rekrutiner, berbasis nilai, onboarding, pengembangan, penilaian kinerja, engagement, hingga proses promosi dan offboarding.

Pendekatan manusia diwujudkan melalui lima Winning Orbit: Winning Ambassadors, Winning Community, Winning Missions, Winning Leaders, dan Winning Moments, yang mendorong kebiasaan positif, kolaborasi lintas tim, serta penguatan budaya secara konsisten. Melalui pendekatan menyeluruh ini, Perseroan memastikan keterlibatan, inklusivitas, dan kolaborasi terjaga kuat di seluruh ekosistem.

# LISTENING TO EMPLOYEES AND STRENGTHENING ENGAGEMENT ACROSS THE ECOSYSTEM

In 2024, the Company conducted an Employee Satisfaction (ESAT) survey to better understand the experiences and needs of employees across all entities. Insights from the 2024 ESAT indicated the need for stronger leadership direction, particularly related to employees feeling not fully heard or understood. As a follow-up, throughout 2025, the Company strengthened communication and connection through various initiatives.

To measure engagement across generations and subsidiaries, the Company combined annual surveys, qualitative feedback channels, and trend monitoring through BOBA (Biblioneers Bot Assistant). Inquiry data and feedback patterns were analyzed using Google Data Studio and further validated through focus group discussions and team dialogues to capture deeper context from different employee segments.

Throughout 2025, the Company fostered a more inclusive and collaborative work culture through the launch of new cultural values, Winning Principles: Customer First, Act Fast &amp; Practical, Be the Solution, and Continuous Improvement. These values are carried out through business-process and people-focused approaches. On the business-process, the values are embedded across the entire employee journey, from employer branding, value-based recruitment, onboarding, learning, performance assessment, engagement, promotion processes, and offboarding.

The people approach was conducted through five Winning Orbits: Winning Ambassadors, Winning Community, Winning Missions, Winning Leaders, and Winning Moments, to promote positive habits, cross-team collaboration, and consistent reinforcement of culture. Through this comprehensive approach, the Company ensured that engagement, inclusivity, and collaboration remained strong across the ecosystem.

# WAREHOUSE WONDERLAND: Explore the Wonders of Marunda

Bibli menggelar Warehouse Wonderland: Explore the Wonders of Marunda, sebuah program yang memberikan kesempatan bagi pengunjung untuk melihat secara langsung operasional warehouse Bibli melalui tur fasilitas, pengalaman teknologi otomasi dan robotik, serta berbagai aktivitas interaktif bagi keluarga.

Acara ini menghadirkan berbagai kegiatan, termasuk warehouse tour, area bermain anak, photo spots, kompetisi keluarga, serta sapan makanan dan minuman. Selain itu, peserta juga berkesempatan memenangkan berbagai hadiah menarik melalui program doorprize. Perseroan juga membuka kesempatan bagi relawan untuk berpartisipasi dalam mendukung kelancaran penyelenggaraan acara sekaligus memperoleh pengalaman dan jejaring baru.

Bibli organized Warehouse Wonderland: Explore the Wonders of Marunda, an initiative providing support with the opportunity to experience. Bibli's warehouse operations facilitated through facility tours, automated, and reliable field-average demonstrations and various interactive family activities.

The event featured a range of activities, including a warehouse tour, children's playground area, photo spots, family competitions, and food and beverage offerings. Participants also had the opportunity to see place few prizes through a door prize program. The Company also opened volunteer opportunities to support the event's implementation while enabling participants to gain new experiences and networks.

# t-Club (Employee-led Clubs)

t-Clubs merupakan inisiatif komunitas berbasis minat di tiket.com yang menjadi wadah bagi karyawan untuk menyalurkan hobi, mengembangkan keterampilan, membangun jejaring lintas fungsi, serta menjaga keseimbangan kerja dan kehidupan pribadi. Program ini dijalankan secara kolaboratif oleh 44 komite volunteer dan berbagai fungsi dan melibatkan 300+ karyawan aktif di Jakarta, Bali, Yogyakarta, dan Surabaya, dengan agenda rutin bulanan serta kegiatan kolaboratif seperti pertandingan pemanfaatan antar perusahaan. Saat ini, t-Clubs menaung berbagai kelompok minat, termasuk olahraga, gim, dan komunitas finansial, serta dirancang sebagai ruang bermain sekaligus ruang bertumbuh bagi t-Fans agar tetap terhubung, aktif, dan engaged.

t-Clubs is an interest-based community initiative at tiket.com that provides a platform for employees to pursue hobbies, develop skills, build cross-functional connections, and maintain a healthy work-life balance. The program is collaboratively run by 44 volunteer committee members across functions and engages over 300 active employees in Jakarta, Bali, Yogyakarta, and Surabaya, featuring regular monthly activities and collaborative events such as friendly matches with other companies. Encompassing various interest groups across sports, gaming, and finance, t-Clubs is designed as both a space to play and a space to grow, enabling t-Fans to stay connected, active, and engaged.
