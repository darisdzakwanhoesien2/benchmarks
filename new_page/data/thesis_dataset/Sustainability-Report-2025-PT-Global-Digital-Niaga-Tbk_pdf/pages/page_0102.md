Pengukuran Dampak Berbasis Ilmiah (ISO 14040/44 – LCA)

Dampak lingkungan kegiatan festival diukur secara ilmiah menggunakan pendekatan Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) sesuai standar ISO 14040/44, untuk memastikan transparansi dan akurasi pelaporan keberlanjutan.

Science-Based Impact Measurement (ISO 14040/44 – LCA)

The festival's environmental impacts were scientifically measured using a Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) approach in accordance with ISO 14040/44, supporting transparent and credible sustainability reporting.

Awareness &amp; Donasi Penanaman Pohon (Jajakin)

Festival mendorong kesadaran dan pengumpulan donasi penanaman pohon melalui kemitraan dengan Jajakin, menghubungkan partisipasi pengunjung dengan aksi iklim nyata.

Tree-Planting Awareness &amp; Donations (Jajakin)

The festival raised awareness and collected donations for tree planting through a partnership with Jajakin, linking visitor participation with tangible climate action.

Pengerolaan Limbah Terintegrasi (Sirsak)

Setelah limbah kegiatan festival diketika oleh mitra pengelolaan limbah Sirsak, memastikan pemilahan dan penanganan limbah dilakukan secara bertanggung jawab.

Integrated Waste Management (Sirsak)

All festival waste was managed by Sirsak as the official waste management partner, ensuring proper waste segregation and responsible handling.

Pemantastan Limbah untuk Fasilitas Acara (Pable)

Limbah terpilah dimanfaatkan kembali sebagai bagian dari fasilitas dan elemen pendukung acara melalui kolaborasi dengan Pable, mendukung praktik ekonomi sirkular.

Waste Upcycling for Event Facilities (Pable)

Selected waste materials were repurposed into event facilities and supporting elements through collaboration with Pable, reinforcing circular economy practices.

Water Refill Station (Modena)

Water refill stations were provided in collaboration with Modena, helping reduce the use of single-use plastic bottles throughout the event.

Edukai Keberlanjutan (Talkshow &amp; Workshop)

Edukaai keberlanjutan disampaikan melalui rangkaian talkshow dan workshop yang membahas isu lingkungan, gaya hidup berkelanjutan, dan inovasi hijau.

Sustainability Education (Talkshows &amp; Workshops)

Sustainability education was delivered through a series of talkshows and workshops addressing environmental issues, sustainable lifestyles, and green innovation.
