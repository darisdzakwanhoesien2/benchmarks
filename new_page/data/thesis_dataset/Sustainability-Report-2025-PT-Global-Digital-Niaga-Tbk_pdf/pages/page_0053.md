# EVALUASI DAN PEMBERIAN IMBALAN ATAS KINERJA TATA KELOLA [GRI 2-18, 2-19, 2-20, 2-21]

Bibli Tiket melaksanakan evaluasi kinerja tahunan terhadap Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi untuk menilai efektivitas tata kelola serta pengawasan atas dampak ekonomi, sosial, dan lingkungan. Evaluasi dilakukan secara independen berdasarkan kriteri yang telah ditetapkan, antara lain kompetensi, tingkat kehadiran, kontribusi strategis, serta kepatuhan terhadap prinsip tata kelola perusahaan yang baik. Hasil evaluasi dimanfaatkan untuk mendukung pengembangan kapasitas, penyempurnaan tata kelola, dan perencanaan suksesi. Secara umum, hasil penilaian menunjukkan bahwa Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, dan komite-komite di bawah Dewan Komisaris telah menjalankan fungsi pengawasan dan tata kelola secara efektif sepanjang tahun 2025. Uraian lebih lengkap mengenai hasil evaluasi tersebut disajikan dalam Laporan Tahunan Perseroan yang diterbitkan secara terpisah namun terintegrasi dengan Laporan Keberlanjutan ini yang dapat diakses melalui tautan berikut: https://about.blibli.com/id/investor-relations/annual-reports. [GRI 2-18]

Kebijakan remunerasi bagi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dirancang berdasarkan prinsip keadilan, kewajaran, dan kinerja, dengan mempertimbangkan pencapaian indikator kinerja utama, termasuk kinerja keberlanjutan. Struktur remunerasi terdiri atas komponen tetap dan variabel sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku. Proses penetapan remunerasi berada di bawah pengawasan Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi yang diketua oleh Komisaris Independen, yang memberikan rekomendasi untuk disetujui melalui Rapat Umum Pemegang Sahan. Sepanjang tahun 2025, Perseroan tidak melibatkan konsultan remunerasi eksternal. [GRI 2-19, 2-20]

Terkait pengungkapan rasio total kompensasi tahunan, Perseroan saat ini tengah memperkuat proses konsolidasio data dan metodologi perhitungannya. Oleh karena itu, pengungkapan rasio total kompensasi tahunan individu dengan penghasilan tertinggi terhadap median kompensasi tahunan seluruh karyawan, serta rasio persentase kenaikan tahunan, akan disampaikan pada periode pelaporan keberlanjutan berikutnya. [GRI 2-21]

# EVALUATING AND REWARDING GOVERNANCE PERFORMANCE [GRI 2-18, 2-19, 2-20, 2-21]

Bibli Tiket conducts annual performance evaluations of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors to assess the effectiveness of governance and oversight of economic, social, and environmental impacts. Evaluations are carried out independently based on predefined criteria, including competence, attendance, strategic contribution, and adherence to good corporate governance principles. The results are used to support capacity development, governance improvements, and succession planning. Overall, the evaluation results indicate that the Company's governance and oversight functions were carried out effectively throughout 2025 by the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, and the committees under the Board of Commissioners. More detailed information on the results of the evaluation is available in the Company's Annual Report, which is published separately but forms an integrated part of this Sustainability Report that can be accessed through this link: https://about.blibli.com/en/investor-relations/annual-reports. [GRI 2-18]

The remuneration policy for the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors is designed based on principles of fairness, reasonableness, and performance, taking into account the achievement of key performance indicators, including sustainability performance. Remuneration consists of fixed and variable components in accordance with applicable regulations. The Nomination and Remuneration Committee, chaired by an Independent Commissioner, oversees the remuneration process and provides recommendations for approval by the GMS. No external remuneration consultants were engaged in 2025. [GRI 2-19, 2-20]

With respect to the disclosure of annual total compensation ratios, the Company is in the process of strengthening internal data consolidation and calculation methodologies. As such, the disclosure of the ratio of annual total compensation of the highest-paid individual to the median annual compensation of all employees, as well as the ratio of annual percentage increases, will be presented in future sustainability reporting periods. [GRI 2-21]
