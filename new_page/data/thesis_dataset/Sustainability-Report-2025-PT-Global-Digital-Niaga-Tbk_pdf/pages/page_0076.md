## Leadership Pathway

Program pengembangan kepemimpinan di tiket.com dirancang untuk memberdayakan para pemimpin melalui pembelajaran yang terstruktur namun tetap fleksibel, dengan pendekatan lokakarya interaktif, pembelajaran rekan sejawat (peer learning), serta penerapan langsung di lingkungan kerja. Sepanjang tahun 2025, program ini melibatkan 243 dari 316 pemimpin, mencatat 2.780 jam pembelajaran dalam 53 sesi, serta memperoleh rata-rata persiapan 4,62 per sesi, yang mencerminkan relevansi praktis dan dampak langsung terhadap efektivitas kepemimpinan.

The leadership development programs at tiket.com are designed to empower leaders through structured yet flexible learning journeys, combining interactive workshops, peer learning, and direct on-the-job application. Throughout 2025, the programs engaged 243 out of 316 leaders, delivered 2.780 learning hours across 53 sessions, and achieved an average session rating of 4.62, reflecting strong practical relevance and a direct impact on leadership effectiveness.

## CollaborAction

Rangkaian edukasi keberlanjutan bagi seluruh karyawan dilaksanakan melalui tiga sesi sharing secara luring, dengan total partisipasi pada tahun 2025 mencapai 100 karyawan. Sesi-sesi tersebut mencakup kunjungan pembelajaran ke Gudang Marunda yang menerapkan konsep Warehouse Green Building, Kantor BCA Foresta yang telah memperoleh sertifikasi Green Building, serta Tempat Pemrosesan Akhir (TPA) Bantar Gebang.

A series of sustainability education initiatives were conducted for all employees, comprising three in-person sharing sessions. Total participation in 2025 reached 100 employees. These sessions included learning visits to the Marunda Warehouse, which implements Green Warehouse Building practices, the BCA Foresta Office, which has obtained Green Building certification, and the Bantar Gebang Final Waste Disposal Site.

## BOLD Festival

(GIR 2-17)

Festival pembelajaran tahunan ini menghadirkan rangkaian kelas dan diskusi mendalam yang dibawakan oleh para pembicara inspiratif, serta diselenggarakan secara hybrid, baik luring maupun daring. Pada tahun 2025, sesi luring dilaksanakan di Kantor Pusat Bibili dan dapat diakses secara daring oleh seluruh karyawan di ekosistem Bibili Tiket.

Sebanyak 19 pembicara berpartisipasi dalam festival ini, dengan total 6.314 karyawan, termasuk Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris yang turut mengikuti rangkaian kegiatan tersebut.

This annual learning festival featured a series of in-depth classes and discussions delivered by inspiring speakers and was conducted in a hybrid format, both offline and online. In 2025, the offline sessions were held at the Biblii Head Office and were accessible online to all employees across the Biblii Tiket ecosystem.

A total of 19 speakers participated in the festival, with 6,314 employees, including members of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners, taking part in the event.
