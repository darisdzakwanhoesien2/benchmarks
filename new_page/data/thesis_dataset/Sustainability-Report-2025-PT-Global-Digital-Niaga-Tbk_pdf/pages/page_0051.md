# STRUKTUR TATA KELOLA KEBERLANJUTAN (SIM 2-9)

Struktur tata kelola Bibli Tiket terdiri atas Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS) sebagai organ tata kelola tertinggi, Dewan Komisaris sebagai organ pengawas, dan Direksi sebagai organ pelaksana yang bertanggung jawab atas pengelolaan Perseroan. Dalam menjalankan fungsi pengawasannya, Dewan Komisaris didukung oleh Komite Audit serta Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi Pengawasan atas dampak ekonomi, lingkungan, dan sosial dilakukan oleh Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi sesuai fungsi masing-masing, dengan dukungan Komite Audit dalam aspek pengawasan pelaporan, pengendalian internal, kepatuhan, dan manajemen risiko, serta dukungan Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi dalam aspek komposisi, nominasi, evaluasi, dan remunerasi organ tata kelola. Pada tingkat operasional, integrasi pengelolaan dampak ekonomi, lingkungan, dan sosial didukung oleh Departemen EHSS &amp; ESG.

Hingga akhir tahun 2025, Dewan Komisaris terdiri dari 4 (empat) anggota, termasuk 2 (dua) Komisaris Independen, sedangkan Direksi terdiri dari 6 (enam) anggota. Ketua Dewan Komisaris merupakan anggota non-eksekutif dan tidak merangkap jabatan manajerial di Perseroan. Komite Audit terdiri dari 3 (tiga) anggota, termasuk 2 (dua) anggota independen, dan Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi terdiri dari 3 (tiga) anggota, yang diketuai oleh Komisaris Independen. Susunan ini mencerminkan pemisahan peran pengawasan dan eksekusi, serta mendukung independensi dalam tata kelola Perseroan.

Komposisi Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, dan komite-komite di bawah Dewan juga ditinjau dengan mempertimbangkan masa jabatan, jabatan signifikan lain dan komitmen yang dimiliki masing-masing anggota, keberagaman gender, keberadaan kelompok sosial yang kurang terwakili, kompetensi yang relevan dengan dampak Perseroan, serta representasi pemangku kepentingan.

Anggota Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, dan komite yang mendukung fungsi pengawasan memiliki kombinasi kompetensi dan pengalaman yang relevan dengan dampak Perseroan, termasuk di bidang teknologi, keuangan, operasional audit, pengendalian internal, manajemen risiko, tata kelola, sumber daya manusia, remunerasi, kepatuhan, serta keberlanjutan. Kompetensi tersebut menjadi dasar bagi organ tata kelola untuk menilai dan mengawasi dampak Perseroan terhadap ekonomi, lingkungan, dan masyarakat.

# SUSTAINABILITY GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE (SIM 2-9)

Bibli Tiket's governance structure comprises the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) as the highest governance body, the Board of Commissioners as the supervisory body, and the Board of Directors as the executive body responsible for managing the Company. In carrying out its oversight function, the Board of Commissioners is supported by the Audit Committee and the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. Oversight of the Company's economic, environmental, and social impacts is exercised by the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors in accordance with their respective roles, with support from the Audit Committee in matters relating to reporting oversight, internal control, compliance, and risk management, and from the Nomination and Remuneration Committee in matters relating to the composition, nomination, evaluation, and remuneration of the governance bodies. At the operational level, the integration of the management of economic, environmental, and social impacts is supported by the EHSS &amp; ESG Department.

As of the end of 2025, the Board of Commissioners consisted of 4 (four) members, including 2 (two) independent Commissioners, while the Board of Directors consisted of 6 (six) members. The Chair of the Board of Commissioners is a non-executive member and does not hold a managerial role within the Company. The Audit Committee consists of 3 (three) members, including 2 (two) independent members, and the Nomination and Remuneration Committee consists of 3 (three) members and is chaired by an independent Commissioner. This composition reflects the separation between oversight and executive functions and supports independence within the Company's governance structure.

The composition of the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, and the committees under the Board is also considered with reference to the tenure of members, other significant positions and commitments held by each member, gender diversity, the presence of under-represented social groups, competencies relevant to the Company's impacts, and stakeholder representation.

Members of the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, and the committees supporting the oversight function possess a combination of competencies and experience relevant to the Company's impacts, including in the areas of technology, finance, operations, audit, internal control, risk management, governance, HR, remuneration, compliance, and sustainability.
