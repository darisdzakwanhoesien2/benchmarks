Building sustainability begins with a focused strategy and a clear understanding of material issues. This chapter outlines Blibli Tiket's strategic approach to embedding sustainability into its business and details the process of identifying material topics that serve as priorities in creating long-term value for all stakeholders.

# Perjalanan Kami Menuju Pertumbuhan Berkelanjutan

Our Path Toward Sustainable Growth

[SEOJK A1] [GRI 3-3]

Sebagai bagian dari komitmen untuk menjalankan praktik usaha yang berkelanjutan, Blibli Tiket menetapkan arah strategis yang menjadi landasan dalam mewujudkan visi pertumbuhan jangka panjang melalui payung program Blibli Tiket Action. Program ini menjadi kerangka utama bagi seluruh program lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola (LST), sekaligus menegaskan fokus. Perseroan pada aksi nyata dan keterlibatan seluruh pemangku kepentingan dalam menghadirkan perubahan yang berarti.

Melalui payung program tersebut, Blibli Tiket terus berupaya mengintegrasikan praktik-praktik berkelanjutan ke seluruh kegiatan operasional Perseroan. Perseroan menelaah dan menindaklanjuti berbagai topik material LST sebagai prioritas strategis, menetapkan sasaran yang terukur, serta mengembangkan insisatif yang sejalan dengan kebijakan keberlanjutan. Setiap topik material kemudian diimplementasikan melalui tiga pilar Blibli Tiket Action, Action for Excellence, Action for People, dan Action for Our Planet, yang menjadi panduan Perseroan dalam menciptakan nilai jangka panjang.

Pendekatan terstruktur ini tidak hanya memastikan bahwa setiap topik material mendapat respons yang tepat, namun juga memperkuat kontribusi Blibli Tiket terhadap Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan (TPB) yang relevan. Dengan demikian, Perseroan mampu memberikan manfaat bagi seluruh pemangku kepentingan sekaligus menjaga ketahanan dan keberlanjutan bisnis di masa depan.

# PROSES PENENTUAN DAN PENGELOLAAN TOPIK MATERIAL

[GRI 2-14, 3-1, 3-2, 3-3]

Perseroan menetapkan topik material melalui proses yang terstruktur untuk mengidentifikasi dan memprioritaskan dampak aktual maupun potensial, baik positif maupun negatif, terhadap aspek ekonomi, sosial, dan lingkungan yang timbul dari kegiatan operasional maupun hubungan bisnis Perseroan. Proses ini mencakup analisis terhadap risiko, peluang, serta pengaruh signifikan yang dapat memengaruhi keberlanjutan jangka panjang Blibli Tiket dan para pemangku kepentingan. [GRI 3-1]

As part of its commitment to responsible and sustainable business practices, Blibli Tiket has established a clear strategic direction to support long-term growth through the Blibli Tiket Action program. This program serve as the overarching framework for all Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) efforts; this initiative reinforces the Company's focus on concrete actions and active stakeholder participation in driving meaningful and transformative change.

Through this overarching program, Blibli Tiket continues to integrate sustainable practices across all of the Company's operational activities. The Company identifies and manages key material ESG topics, sets measurable targets, and implements initiatives aligned with its sustainability policies. These material topics are further executed through the three pillars of Blibli Tiket Action, Action for Excellence, Action for People, and Action for Our Planet, which guide the Company in generating long-term value.

This structured approach ensures that each material topic is addressed appropriately while strengthening Blibli Tiket's contribution to the relevant Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). In doing so, the Company supports positive impacts for stakeholders and reinforces the resilience and sustainability of its business into the future.

# PROCESS FOR DETERMINING AND MANAGING MATERIAL TOPICS

[GRI 2-14, 3-1, 3-2, 3-3]

The Company determines its material topics through a structured process to identify and prioritize actual and potential, positive and negative impacts on economic, social, and environmental aspects arising from its operations and business relationships. This process includes an analysis of significant risks, opportunities, and impacts that may affect the long-term sustainability of Blibli Tiket and its stakeholders. [GRI 3-1]
