Gigering Together Through Learning

Sejalan dengan penguatan kepemimpinan, Blibli Tiket juga mempercepat pengembangan kapabilitas AI dan automasi untuk mendukung transformasi digital yang berkelanjutan. Sepanjang tahun 2025, Perseroan menyelenggarakan 25 sesi pelatihan automasi dengan total 66 jam pelatihan, dengan jumlah partisipasi 325 orang. Program ini mendorong penerapan automasi secara langsung di proses kerja, mencerminkan kontribusi nyata pengembangan kapabilitas digital terhadap produktivitas, efisiensi operasional, dan keberlanjutan kinerja bisnis. [SEOJK F22] [GRI 3-3,404-2]

Selain itu, Perseroan mengoperasionalkan Biblioneers Insight sebuah wadah berbagi pengetahuan antar pemimpin untuk memperkaya wawasan, berbagi pengalaman, serta memperkuat kapabilitas kepemimpinan kolektif. Pendekatan ini didukung oleh kerangka pengembangan yang komprehensif berbasis peran dan level, memastikan setiap karyawan memiliki kejelasan jalur karier dan akses yang setara terhadap peluang pengembangan sesuai kebutuhan fungsi dan tahap perkembangan mereka. [SEOJK F22] [GRI 404-2]

Sejalan dengan agenda transformasi digital, prioritas pembelajaran pada 2025 diarahkan pada peningkatan kemampuan otomasi, AI, dan keterampilan digital lainnya, serta penguatan kapabilitas teknis melalui pengembangan Subject Matter Expert (SME). Untuk memastikan efektivitas pembelajaran, Perseroan menerapkan kerangka evaluasi berbasis Kirkpatrick Model yang mencakup umpan balik peserta, peningkatan pengetahuan, perubahan perilaku, hingga kaitannya dengan peningkatan kinerja dan kesiapan promosi. Hasil pengembangan talenta kemudian dintegrakan ke dalam kerangka kompetensi perusahaan sebagai dasar perencanaan suksesi dan penilaian potensi. [GRI 3-3] [GRI 404-2]

Pembelajaran diperluas melalui Blilearning platform digital yang menyediakan akses fleksibel terhadap modul pembelajaran mandiri maupun kurasi konten sesuai kebutuhan bisnis. Data/partisipasi, akses konten, serta evaluasi Level 1 dan Level 2 dianalisis untuk memastikan relevansi materi dan penyempurnaan berkelanjutan. Melalui kombinasi penguatan kepemimpinan, peningkatan keterampilan digital dan teknis, serta sistem evaluasi yang terstruktur, Perseroan berhasil memperkuat fondasi talenta yang siap menghadapi tantangan masa depan dan mendorong pertumbuhan berkelanjutan. [SEOJK F22] [GRI 3-3,404-2]

In parallel with leadership development, Blibli Tiket accelerated AI and automation capability building to support its ongoing digital transformation. In 2025, the Company delivered 25 automation training sessions totaling 66 learning hours, with total 325 participants. These programs enabled the direct application of automation solutions across work processes and demonstrating the tangible contribution of digital capability development to productivity, operational efficiency, and long-term business sustainability. [SEOJK F22] [GRI 3-3,404-2]

Complementing the formal programs, the Company institutionalized Biblioneers Insight, a leadership-sharing platform that enables leaders to exchange best practices, insights, and lessons learned, strengthening collective leadership capability. This was supported by a comprehensive role- and level-based development framework that provides every employee with a clear career pathway and equitable access to structured development opportunities aligned with their functional requirements and growth stage. [SEOJK F22] [GRI 404-2]

Aligned with the Company's digital transformation agenda, learning priorities in 2025 emphasized automation. AI and broader digital skill development, as well as technical capability building through the refinement of the Subject Matter Expert (SME) framework. Learning effectiveness is regularly evaluated using the Kirkpatrick Model, capturing participant feedback, knowledge gain, behavioral change, and linkage to performance and promotion readiness. Talent development outcomes are integrated into the Company's competency framework to support succession planning and identify high-potential individuals for advancement. [GRI 3-3] [GRI 404-2]

Learning accessibility was further scaled through Blilearning, the Company's integrated digital learning platform, offering flexible self-paced and curated content aligned with evolving business priorities. Participation data, content usage, and Level 1-2 evaluations were used to refine learning offerings and ensure continuous improvement. Through strengthened leadership development, enhanced digital and technical capabilities, and a structured evaluation system, the Company continued to build a future-ready workforce capable of supporting long-term organizational growth. [SEOJK F22] [GRI 3-3,404-2]

# 1.

## Berlaku untuk Seluruh Karyawan Blibli Tiket Group

- BOLD Festival
- Collaboration
- Pelatihan umum/regular (keterampilan dasar, komunikasi kolaborasi)
- Pelatihan AI &amp; otomasi untuk kesiapan digital dan optimalisasi akar kerja

## Applicable to AS Blibli Tiket Group Employees

- BOLD Festival
- Collaboration
- General / regular training (core skills, communication, collaboration)
- AI &amp; automation training to strengthen digital readiness and workflow optimisation
- External training (certification and professional development)

# 2.

## Khusus untuk Karyawan Baru

- Program onboarding mencakup integrasi peran, perendemen budaya, dan pengenalan. Blinning Principles [khusus Blibli]
