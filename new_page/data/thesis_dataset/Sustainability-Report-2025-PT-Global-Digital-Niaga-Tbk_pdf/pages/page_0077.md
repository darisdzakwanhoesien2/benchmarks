# Pelatihan Automasi Automation Training

Sejak diluncurkan pada tahun 2024, program pelatihan automasi Blibli terus memperkuat kapabilitas digital karyawan dalam mendorong efisiensi dan inovasi proses kerja. Sepanjang tahun 2025, program ini menyelenggarakan 25 sesi pelatihan (66 jam) yang diikuti oleh 175 karyawan. Pelatihan mencakup berbagai topik, antara lain Power Automate, Robotic Process Automation (RPA), VBA-Macro, Office Script, Business Process Thinking, serta teknik automasi tingkat menengah yang relevan dengan kebutuhan operasional. Program ini selaras dengan Self-Development Program (SDP) untuk memastikan materi automasi sesuai dengan kebutuhan nyata karyawan dalam meningkatkan produktivitas dan efektivitas kerja. Mindset automasi juga dibangun sejak awal perjalanan karyawan. Pada tahun 2025, Blibli melalui Departemen Human Capital juga memberikan pelatihan kepada karyawan baru, guna menanamkan budaya inovasi dan transformasi digital sejak hari pertama.

Siswa its launch in 2024, Blibli's automation training program has continued to strengthen employees' digital capabilities in driving operational efficiency and process innovation. Throughout 2025, the program delivered 25 training sessions (66 hours) attended by 175 employees. The training covered various topics, including Power Automate, Robotic Process Automation (RPA), VBA-Macro, Office Script, Business Process Thinking, and intermediate automation techniques relevant to operational needs. The program was aligned with the Self-Development Program (SDP) to ensure that automation topics addressed employees' actual needs in enhancing productivity and work effectiveness. An automation mindset is also cultivated from the beginning of the employee journey. In 2025, Blibli through its Human Capital Department to provide training for new employees, following a culture of innovation and digital transformation from day one.

# MENGHARGAI DAN MENGAKUI KINERJA UNGGUL [681:404-3]

Perseroan menerapkan sistem penilaian kinerja dan pengembangan karier yang dilakukan secara berkala dan terstruktur untuk seluruh karyawan. Proses ini mencakup penetapan sasaran, sesi umpan balik dan coaching, serta kalibrasi kinerja lintas fungsi dan jenjang jabatan, yang menjadi dasar dalam pengembangan kompetensi, promosi, dan perniyauan remunerasi.

Sepanjang tahun 2025, seluruh karyawan (100%) telah menerima penilaian kinerja dan pengembangan karier secara rutin. Hasil evaluasi dikalibrasi antar divisi dan manajemen sebelum dikomunikasikan kepada karyawan melalui sesi evaluasi dan coaching, termasuk pembahasan kinerja, aspirasi karier, dan kebutuhan pengembangan.

Sebagai bagian dari komitmen terhadap kesetaraan kesempatan, Perseroan juga memantau implementasi sistem penilaian dan pengembangan karier secara berkelanjutan untuk memastikan penerapan yang adil dan konsisten bagi seluruh karyawan tanpa memandang gender maupun jabatan.

# RECOGNIZING AND REWARDING EXCELLENCE [681:404-3]

The Company implements a structured and regular performance and career development review system for all employees. This process includes goal setting, feedback and coaching sessions, as well as cross-functional and cross-level performance calibration, which serves as the basis for competency development, promotions, and remuneration reviews.

Throughout 2025, all employees (100%) received regular performance and career development reviews. The evaluation results were calibrated across divisions and management levels before being communicated to employees through evaluation and coaching sessions, including discussions on performance outcomes, career aspirations, and development needs.

As part of its commitment to equal opportunity, the Company continuously monitors the implementation of its performance and career development review system to ensure fair and consistent application for all employees, regardless of gender or position.

# Blibli Star

Blibli Star merupakan program apresiasi dan penghargaan bagi karyawan yang menunjukkan kompetensi unggul serta kontribusi signifikan terhadap perusahaan. Para penerima penghargaan akan mengikuti program pengembangan intensif yang dirancang untuk mempersiapkan calon pemimpin masa depan Perseroan.

Pada tahun 2025, sebanyak 48 kandidat mengikuti proses seleksi yang ketat. Kandidat dipilih melalui penilaian komprehensif terhadap kinerja dan potensi, termasuk proses wawancara panel bersama.

Blibli Star is an appreciation and award program for employees who demonstrate outstanding competence and significant contributions to the company. Award recipients participate in an intensive development program designed to prepare the Company's future leaders.

In 2025, a total of 48 candidates underwent a rigorous selection process. Candidates were chosen through a comprehensive assessment of performance and potential, including panel interviews.
