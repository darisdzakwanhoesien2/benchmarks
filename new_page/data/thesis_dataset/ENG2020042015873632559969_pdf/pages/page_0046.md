# MENDUKUNG PENCAPAIAN MILLENNIUM DEVELOPMENT GOALS

Pada tahun 2000 lalu, 168 negara termasuk Indonesia, berkumpul bersama di Markas Besar Perserikatan Bangsa Bangsa guna menegaskan kembali tujuan bersama dalam menciptakan perdamaian serta memastikan terjadinya peningkatan kesejahteraan masyarakat dunia. Ada lima landasan pembangunan yang ditegaskan, yaitu: kebebasan, persamaan, solidaritas, toleransi, penghargaan untuk alam dan berbagi tanggung jawab. Pertemuan tersebut kemudian menghasilkan kesepakatan pencapaian delapan target pembangunan seluruh bangsa di dunia, yang dikenal sebagai Millennium Development Goals, yakni:

consultation services, to ensure more efficient resources usage, improve productivity by minimize the social and environmental impacts.

Thus, it had been made clear for us that the business sustainability meant the maximum utilization of technology and innovation, to increase efficiency and productivity by reducing the operations impacts to the environment and social community of surrounding area. The essence of sustainability for us is to seek the balanced achievement between performances in economic, environmental preservation and improving social welfare.

# PROMOTING ACHIEVEMENT OF MILLENNIUM DEVELOPMENT GOALS

In 2008, 186 countries including Indonesia, were gathered in the Headquarter of United Nation, to reaffirm the collective goals in establishing peace and to ensure improved prosperity of the world's society. Five development foundations had been firmly stated, namely: freedom, equality, solidarity, tolerance, respect to the nature and joint responsibility. The meeting had reached to an agreement of achieving eight targets of development for all nations in the world, known as Millennium Development Goals, which are:

| Goal 1 | Memberantas Kemiskinan dan Kelaparan | Eradicate Extreme Poverty and Hunger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goal 2 | Mencapai Kesamaan Pendidikan Dasar | Achieve Universal Primary Education |
| Goal 3 | Mendukung Kesetaraan Gender dan Pemberdayaan Perempuan | Promote Gender Equality and Empower Women |
| Goal 4 | Mengurangi Tingkat Kematian Anak | Reduce Child Mortality |
