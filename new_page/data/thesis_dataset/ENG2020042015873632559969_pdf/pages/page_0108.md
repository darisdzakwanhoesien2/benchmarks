Distribusi perolehan ekonomi lainnya adalah kepada karyawan, dalam bentuk pembayaran gaji dan tunjangan lainnya, dengan nilai mencapai Rp287,3 miliar di tahun 2014, naik 25,6% dari nilai sebesar Rp228,7 miliar di tahun 2013. Kami juga mendistribusikan perolehan nilai ekonomi kepada para pemangku kepentingan lainnya, seperti negara dan masyarakat sekitar, berupa pembayaran pajak, retribusi dan lain-lain.

With such amount of economic value gain, the total economic value distributed was amounted to Rp5.51 trillion.

The biggest economic value was distributed to the suppliers, both in providing raw materials and relevant services to operations of the Company, with a value of Rp4,51 trillion, an increase of 12.9% from Rp3,99 trillion in 2013. The distribution value to suppliers included an approximately 81.7% of economic value gain of the Company in 2014.

A big portion of economic value gain also distributed to the funders, which was to fulfill financial liability of Rp599.0 billion. This value was decreased from the interest payments in 2013 of Rp1.13 trillion. The decreased of interest payment was the positive impact of the Company's success in restructuring its debts.

The economic value gain was also distributed to employees, in the form of salaries and other benefits, amounted to Rp287.3 billion in 2014, an increase of 25.6% from Rp228.7 billion in 2013. We also distributed economic value gain to other stakeholders, such as government and local communities, in the form of taxation, levies and others.
