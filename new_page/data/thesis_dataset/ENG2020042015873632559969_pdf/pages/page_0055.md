# KEBIJAKAN DAN PEDOMAN PELAKSANAAN TATA KELOLA

Kami telah memiliki berbagai kebijakan dan pedoman dalam menerapkan praktek terbaik tata kelola melalui implementasi seluruh prinsip-prinsip dasar GCG. Beberapa diantara kebijakan tersebut adalah Nilai-nilai Perusahaan (Trimatra Bakrie), Pakta Integritas, Pedoman Tata Kelola, Pedoman Etika Perusahaan, Board Charter, Charter Komite Penunjang Dewan Komisaris, Charter Internal Audit, Pedoman Kebijakan Manajemen Risiko, Piagam Kepatuhan, Whistleblowing System, serta kebijakan dan pedoman pelaksanaan kegiatan operasional lainnya.

Kebijakan dan Pedoman tersebut senantiasa kami kaji secara periodik untuk disesuaikan dengan kondisi dan perkembangan bisnis Perusahaan dan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku.

# BOARD CHARTER

Board Charter adalah petunjuk tata laksana kerja Dewan Komisaris, Direksi dan Manajemen yang menjelaskan tahapan kerja dan aktivitas Dewan

Directors covered 5 (five) main areas, namely, management, risk management, internal control, communications and social responsibility. The Board of Directors also in charge to represent the Company both in and out of court in accordance with the Articles of Association.

In order to maintain the interests and ensure business continuity, the Board of Directors shall prepare and carry out risk management in the Company. In addition, the Board of Directors has also designed the functions of supervisory and operations control through the internal control unit, and through fluent communication with the stakeholders. The Board of Directors, together with the Board of Commissioners hold the performance accountability in managing the Company before the GMS. The Board of Directors is periodically elected and appointed through the GMS.

# GOVERNANCE IMPLEMENTATION POLICY AND MANUAL

We had applied various policies and manual in conducting GCG best practices, through the implementation of all GCG basic principles. Among those policies are the Corporate Values (Trimatra Bakrie), Integrity Pact, Governance Manual, Code of Conduct, Board Charter, Board of Commissioners Supporting Committee Charter, Internal Audit Charter, Manual for Risk Management Policy, Compliance Charter, Whistleblowing System, as well as policy and manual for other operations activities.

The policy and manual are periodically reviewed to be adjusted in line with the condition and the Company's business development, as well as prevailing laws and regulations.
