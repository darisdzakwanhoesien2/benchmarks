## Program Pendidikan dan Pelatihan [G4-LA9]

Peningkatan kompetensi SDM Bakrie &amp; Brothers kami lakukan melalui dua jalur, yakni pelaksanaan Pendidikan Formal dan Pemberian Program Pelatihan berkala. Untuk program pendidikan formal, kami menyediakan bantuan biaya pendidikan bagi karyawan yang akan melanjutkan pendidikannya ke jenjang yang lebih tinggi. Kebijakan tersebut diatur dalam KSDM no. 3.6/KSDM/VI/2006

Sedangkan untuk meningkatkan kompetensi rutin, kami menyusun program pelatihan berkala kepada para karyawan dengan jenis pelatihan bersertifikasi maupun pelatihan teknik khusus maupun umum sesuai kebutuhan masing-masing anak usaha. Sepanjang tahun 2014 kami menyelenggarakan sebanyak 210 pelatihan dengan jumlah peserta pelatihan 2.576 orang karyawan.

## Educational and Training Program [G4-LA9]

The improvement of Bakrie &amp; Brothers HR competence has been conducted through two methods, namely Formal Education Program and Periodic Training Program. We provide fund assistance for the employee that are interested to pursue formal education to the higher levels. This policy has been stipulated in KSDM No.3.6/KSDM/VI/2006.

Whilst for the routine competence development, we set a periodic training program for employees, with certified training, or special skill training, as well as regular training, according to the needs. During 2014 We had organized 210 trainings, with number of attendants of 2,576 employees.
