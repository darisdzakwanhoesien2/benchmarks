## 2. Implementing Performance Management [G4-LA11]

As an integral part of the policy of quality HR management, we consistently implement objective performance appraisal system, thereby enable all employees to have an equal direction and a commitment in supporting the Company's business objective achievements. We implement performance appraisal system through the development of Key Performance Indicators (KPI) that become the basic of the Company's performance assessment as well as individual assessment, and become one of the measurement in defining career path for all employees.
