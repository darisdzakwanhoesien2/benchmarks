In a sustainable process of ERM, every risk owner was requested to conduct their own risk management process, from identification, assessment, proposal and control application of the risk management process, as well as mitigation toward those risks, or usually called Risk and Control Self Assessment (RCSA). The main goals are to minimize potential losses and most importantly to maximize the goal achievement in every function and structure of the Company.

In order to increase the quality of Corporate Risk Management, we organize several activities in 2014 concerning risk management, namely:

- Governance – Risk – Compliance (GRC) Day with the theme of “Indonesian Outlook 2015 and Beyond”
- Appointment of Risk Officer and Control Self Assessment (RCSA)

To perform effective and efficient risk management, through the implementation of ERM we identified the risks and prepare the mitigation for every main risk that included into high and extreme risks category. Several risks are thoroughly identified and mitigated in 2014, both in the corporate and subsidiaries level, which includes: Strategic Risk, Market Risk, Liquidity Risk, Insolvency Risk, Credit Risk, Discrepancy Risk and Operational Risk.
