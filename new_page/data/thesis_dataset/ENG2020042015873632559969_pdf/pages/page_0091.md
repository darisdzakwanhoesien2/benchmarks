## KESEHATAN KERJA (G4-LA7)

Kami juga berkomitmen penuh untuk menjaga kesehatan kerja para karyawan selain mencegah terjadinya kecelakaan kerja, dan kami wujudkan melalui ketersediaan pelayanan kesehatan kepada setiap karyawan, meliputi penyediaan fasilitas kesehatan dan biaya pengobatan. Untuk menjaga kesehatan karyawan, kami melakukan pemeriksaan

- Regular meetings: Daily meetings and weekly meetings.
- Visits by management.
- Safety Patrol (Daily inspection) for aspects: OHS facilities, equipment and work activities.
- Safety Induction.
- Completeness of OHS related Communication Facility.
- OHS Campaign.
- Education and training on accident treatments.
- Implementation of OHS audit by the authorized party.

Those consistent various programs had enabled BPI to record zero accident as targeted.
