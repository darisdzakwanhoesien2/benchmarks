## Bidang Ekonomi
- Merealisasikan pendistribusian perolehan nilai ekonomi.
- Meningkatkan kegiatan ekonomi daerah melalui pemberian peran pemasok lokal.

## Economy
- To realize the distribution of economic value creation.
- To improve local economic activities through the assignment of local suppliers.

## Layanan Pelanggan
- Meningkatkan kualitas layanan puma jual.
- Menyelesaikan seluruh keluhan pelanggan.
- Memperkenalkan produk-produk inovatif dan ramah lingkungan.

## Customer Services
- Increase quality of after-sales services.
- To settle all customers complaints.
- To introduce innovative and environmentally friendly products.

![img-5.jpeg](img-5.jpeg)

## Pengelolaan Sumber Daya Manusia dan Ketenagakerjaan
- Merealisasikan berbagai program pelatihan bersertifikat maupun non sertifikasi untuk meningkatkan kompetensi seluruh karyawan.
- Melaksanakan program penilaian kinerja reguler dan asesmen promosi jabatan terhadap karyawan.
- Merekrut dan mempersiapkan calon karyawan baru dalam rangka pengembangan usaha.
- Membangun budaya sadar K3 dan memenuhi seluruh standar K3 untuk meraih nihil-insiden kecelakaan kerja.
- Melengkapi seluruh Alat Keselamatan Diri dan Alat Pemadam Kebakaran.

## Human Capital and Manpower Management
- To realize various certified and non-certified training program to improve employees' competence.
- To implement the performance and promotion assessment program towards employees.
- To recruit and prepare employees' candidates due to business expansion.
- To develop a culture of awareness towards OHS and to fulfil it's standards in order to accomplish zero incidents at work.
- To finalize all emergency equipment and fire extinguisher.
