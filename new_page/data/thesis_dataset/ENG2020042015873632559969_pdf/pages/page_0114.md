- PT Bakrie Pipe Industries (BPI)
Perusahaan, melalui BPI menyediakan prosedur Customer Satisfaction yang secara garis besar digambarkan sebagai berikut:

- Hotline
Through Hotline service, customers can submit complaints directly to the Commercial Team that will conduct follow up to the complaint, either caused by utilization/ installation or other issues related to services.

- Direct Service
If the submitted complaints are required for further examination, representatives of Commercial and Quality Control Team will conduct spot checks to customers, to discover issues and determine solutions as well as necessary actions.

- Online Site
We also provide communication channel through online sites that allow the customers to submit questions/complaints. The facility is provided in the website: www.bakrie-building.com. Every submission will be forwarded to the Commercial Team for further actions.

- PT Bakrie Metal Industries (BMI)
As the manifestation of service and responsibility to customers, BMI performed Direct Service program, which is a periodic visit to the customers to obtain information and feedback, especially regarding the customers' satisfaction. BMI also conducted identification of customers needs and evaluate the products requirement and communication aspects with the customers in accordance with ISO 9001:2008.
