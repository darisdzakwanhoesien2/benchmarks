## Program Pengembangan Kepemimpinan Bakrie

Bekerjasama dengan Bakrie Solusi Strategis (BSS)/ Bakrie Learning Center (BLC), kami membangun sistem pengembangan kepemimpinan untuk setiap tingkatan di dalam organisasi Perusahaan, mulai dari level pemula (entry level) sampai dengan level eksekutif (executive level). Program pengembangan kepemimpinan tersebut terdiri atas: (1) Bakrie Induction Program, (2) Bakrie Basic Management Development Program (B BMDP), (3) Bakrie Middle Management Development Program (BMMDP), (4) Bakrie General Management Development Program (BGMDP), dan (5) Bakrie Executive Development Program (BEDP).

Dalam beberapa tahun terakhir BSS fokus dalam melakukan kajian atas keseluruhan program Bakrie Leadership Development Program, sehingga dapat menghasilkan pemimpin-pemimpin yang berkualitas di Kelompok usaha Bakrie.

## Bakrie Leadership Development Program

We cooperate with Bakrie Strategic Solution (BSS)/ Bakrie Learning Center (BLC) in developing leadership development system for each level in the organization, from entry level to the executive level. The program consists of (1) Bakrie Induction Program, (2) Bakrie Basic Management Development Program (B BMDP), (3) Bakrie Middle Management Development Program (BMMDP), (4) Bakrie General Management Development Program (BGMDP), and (5) Bakrie Executive Development Program (BEDP).

In the last several years BSS was focused on reviewing the overall Bakrie Leadership Development Program, in order to produce quality leaders for Bakrie Group.
