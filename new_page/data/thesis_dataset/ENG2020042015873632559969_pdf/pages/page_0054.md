# RAPAT UMUM PEMEGANG SAHAM

Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS) adalah organ tertinggi dalam struktur tata kelola perusahaan kami. RUPS merupakan forum internal tertinggi dimana para pemegang saham bersidang untuk membahas dan memutuskan arahan strategis Perusahaan disamping melaksanakan evaluasi atas kinerja Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi di bidang ekonomi, sosial maupun pengelolaan lingkungan dalam mengelola perusahaan. RUPS memegang wewenang yang tidak didelegasikan kepada Dewan Komisaris atau Direksi sesuai batasan yang diatur dalam Anggaran Dasar dan peraturan perundang-undangan.

Dalam RUPS para pemegang saham berhak menggunakan haknya sesuai jumlah saham yang dimiliki dan menetapkan sikap dalam proses pengambilan keputusan penting yang berkaitan dengan rencana strategis Perusahaan.

## RUPS terdiri atas:

- RUPS Tahunan yang diselenggarakan satu kali setelah penutupan tahun buku.
- RUPS Luar Biasa yaitu Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham yang diadakan sewaktu-waktu sesuai dengan kebutuhan.

# DEWAN KOMISARIS

Dewan Komisaris bertugas dan bertanggung jawab dalam mengawasi pelaksanaan tugas operasional yang dilakukan Direksi, mengawasi pelaksanaan keputusan RUPS, memastikan kepatuhan terhadap peraturan perundangan yang berlaku, memberikan nasihat kepada Direksi serta memastikan Perusahaan melaksanakan prinsip-prinsip GCG. Dewan Komisaris juga memiliki kewenangan khusus agar dapat menjalankan tugas pengawasannya.

# GENERAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS

General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) is the highest organ in our governance structure. GMS is the highest internal forum in which our shareholders convened to discuss and stipulate the Company's strategic directions, in addition to carrying out an evaluation upon the performance of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors, in economic, social and environmental aspects of the Company's management. GMS has the authority that shall not be delegated to the Board of Commissioners nor the Board of Directors in accordance to the limitation set out in the articles of association and regulatory.

In the GMS, the shareholders are entitled to exercise their rights in line with the number of shares owned, and to define their stands in the process of important decision making related to the strategic plan of the Company.

## GMS consisted of:

- The Annual General Meeting of Shareholders which is held once in a year or at least six months after the closing of the fiscal year.
- The Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders that can be held at any time necessary.

# BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS

The Board of Commissioners has the duties and responsibilities to supervise the implementation of operations duties performed by the Board of Directors, to oversee the implementation of GMS's resolutions, to ensure the compliance toward prevailing laws and regulations, to provide advice to the Board of Directors and to ensure the Company's implementation on GCG principles. The Board of Commissioners has the rights to require necessary information from the Board of Directors in carrying out their duties. The Board also has specific authority in order to carry out its supervisory duties.
