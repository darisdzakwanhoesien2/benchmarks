# PENGHARGAAN

Berbagai upaya dalam meningkatkan kualitas penerapan prosedur kerja sesuai kaidah K3 membuat Perusahaan, melalui entitas anak, mendapatkan beberapa penghargaan terkait praktek K3, mencakup:

- Penghargaan 8,2 juta jam kerja tanpa kecelakaan – BMI (unit BCons) dari Oktober 2009 hingga Desember 2014
- Penghargaan 3,3 juta jam tanpa kecelakaan – BPI periode 1 Januari 2011 hingga 31 Desember 2013
- Penghargaan 3 juta jam kerja tanpa kecelakaan – BMI (unit BFU) periode 14 Mei 2012 hingga 14 Mei 2013
- Penghargaan 7 juta jam kerja tanpa kecelakaan – BA periode 1 Januari hingga 31 Desember 2013

In addition to routine examination and treatment, we also organized counseling activities for employees, especially regarding the serious illness and illness caused by work. # AWARDS

We have carried out various efforts in improving quality of working procedures implementation, according to OHS principles. The efforts has enabled the Company, through its subsidiaries, to receive several awards related to OHS practices, as follows:

- Award of 8.2 million workhours without accident - BMI (unit BCons) from October 2009 until December 2014
- Award of 3.3 million workhours without accident - BPI period of 1 January 2011 until 31 December 2013
- Award of 3 million workhours without accident - BMI (unit BFU) period of 14 May 2012 until 14 May 2013
- Award of 7 million workhours without accident - BA period of 1 January 1 to 31 December 2013
