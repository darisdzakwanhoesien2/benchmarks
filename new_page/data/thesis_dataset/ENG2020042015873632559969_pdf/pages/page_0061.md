# PEDOMAN PERILAKU [G4-56]

Kami terus berupaya untuk membangun organisasi yang didukung insan-insan yang bekerja dengan mengedepankan kejujuran, integritas dan loyalitas, untuk menjamin keberlanjutan usaha. Upaya tersebut kami wujudkan dengan menyusun dan mengembangkan pedoman perilaku yang terdiri dari Kode Etik dan Kebijakan Perilaku Bisnis. Dengan penerapan pedoman perilaku, kami berharap profesionalisme dapat ditingkatkan baik pada jajaran manajemen maupun segenap karyawan PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk termasuk anak perusahaan.

Kode etik dan kebijakan perilaku bisnis mengandung prinsip-prinsip Perusahaan secara utuh serta merangkum nilai dan standar perilaku PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk. Kedua pedoman tersebut berlaku bagi seluruh Insan BNBR dalam seluruh level organisasi Perusahaan, dan harus selalu digunakan sebagai pedoman berperilaku dalam pekerjaan sehari-hari. Mengingat pentingnya pedoman perilaku, maka kami melakukan kajian secara berkala terhadap butir-butir aturan yang terkandung didalamnya agar senantiasa sesuai dengan perkembangan terkini dan tetap mencerminkan nilai-nilai Trimatra Bakrie

professionalism as the character of every Bakrie's people in conducting daily operations duties.

Trimatra Bakrie as Core Values, together with Instrumental Values become the basic of forming the Corporate Culture.

The implementation of this culture was a prerequisite so as to enable the Company to move and think in harmony internally, especially in the daily work activities. This culture is performed through providing the example and applied from top to bottom level, and was disseminated through various activities, meetings and periodic evaluations.

# CODE OF CONDUCT [G4-56]

We strive to develop organization that is supported by hardworking people that holds up honesty, integrity, and loyalty, to ensure business sustainability. These efforts were embodied through the development and preparation of Code of Conduct, which consisted of Code of Ethics and Business Conduct Policy. With the implementation of Code of Conduct, we expect to increase professionalism among the employees and Management of PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk, including its subsidiaries.

The code of ethics and business conducts policy contained the Corporate principles as a whole, including the summary of values and standard of conduct in PT Bakrie &amp; Brothers Tbk. Both manuals are applied to all levels of organization, and shall be used as guideline in conducting daily tasks. Considering the importance of Code of Conduct, we performed periodical review towards its regulatory articles, to up date with the recent developments and to reflect the values of Trimatra Bakrie as the Company's philosophy.
