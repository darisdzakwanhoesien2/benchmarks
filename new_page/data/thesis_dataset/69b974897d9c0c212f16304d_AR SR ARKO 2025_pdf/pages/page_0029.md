Direksi

Report of The Board of Directors

Direksi menetapkan strategi penguatan Perseroan sebagai pengembang dan operator pembangkit listrik tenaga air berbasis energi terbarukan melalui peningkatan kapasitas terkontrak, pengelolaan pipeline proyek berkelanjutan, penerapan prinsip ESG, serta optimalisasi kinerja pembangkit yang mendorong pertumbuhan pendapatan.

The Board of Directors set a strategy to strengthen the Company's position as a renewable hydropower plant developer and operator by increasing contracted capacity, managing a sustainable project pipeline, implementing ESG principles, and optimizing power plant performance to drive revenue growth.

Para Pemegang Saham dan Pemangku Kepentingan yang Terhormat,

Dengan penuh rasa syukur, kami menyampaikan Laporan Direksi atas kinerja Perseroan sepanjang tahun 2025. Perseroan terus menunjukkan pencapaian yang progresif dengan peningkatan pendapatan, profitabilitas, dan konsistensi pencapaian produksi. Selain itu, kami bangga menyampaikan bahwa telah mendapatkan kontrak baru untuk Proyek PLTA Pongbembe dengan kapasitas 20 MW. Perseroan terus berkembang dan memanfaatkan pertumbuhan industri energi terbarukan yang positif di Indonesia.

Dear Esteemed Shareholders and Stakeholders,

With sincere gratitude, we hereby present the Report of the Board of Directors on the Company's performance throughout 2025. The Company continued to demonstrate progressive achievements, marked by increases in revenue, profitability, and consistent production performance. In addition, we are proud to report that the Project Pongbembe Hydropower with a capacity of 20 MW has secured a new contract. The Company continues to grow and capitalize on the positive growth of Indonesia's renewable energy industry.

PT Arkora Hydro Tbk
