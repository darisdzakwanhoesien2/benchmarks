# Pencegahan Konflik Kepentingan [G-09]

Prevention of Conflict of Interest [G-09]

Pengungkapan hubungan afiliasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dilakukan untuk mencegah konflik kepentingan dalam tim manajemen yang memiliki pengaruh besar terhadap jalannya Perseroan. Setiap anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi didorong untuk dapat bekerja secara independen dan mengutamakan kepentingan Perseroan di atas kepentingan pribadi.

The disclosure of affiliated relationships among the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors is taken to prevent conflicts of interest within the management team, which holds significant influence over the Company's operations. Each member of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors is encouraged to work independently and to prioritize the interests of the Company above personal interests.
