# Tinjauan Operasional
## Operational Review

PT Arkora Hydro Tbk bergerak di bidang pengembangan, kepemilikan, dan pengoperasian fasilitas pembangkit listrik berbasis tenaga air yang memanfaatkan aliran sungai langsung (run-of-river). PLTA dengan jenis run-of-river merupakan jenis pembangkit listrik yang tidak memerlukan pembangunan bendungan besar untuk penyimpanan air, sehingga memiliki dampak lingkungan yang relatif lebih rendah, waktu pembangunan yang lebih singkat, serta lebih adaptif terhadap kondisi geografis di berbagai wilayah Indonesia.

Hingga akhir tahun 2025, Perseroan telah memiliki 3 (tiga) proyek yang telah beroperasi secara komersial dengan total kapasitas terpasang sebesar 27,4 MW. Selain itu, jika ditotal dengan kapasitas pembangkit yang telah dikontrakkan (contracted capacity), maka total kapasitas pembangkit yang dimiliki Perseroan mencapai 62,8 MW. Perseroan juga memiliki portofolio proyek dalam tahap pengembangan (pipeline projects) dengan total kapasitas lebih dari 300 MW yang tersebar di sejumlah wilayah dengan potensi sumber daya air yang memadai.

Pipeline proyek tersebut mencerminkan kesiapan dan kesinambungan pertumbuhan usaha Perseroan dalam jangka panjang. Perseroan senantiasa berfokus pada kegiatan pengembangan dan pengoperasian PLTA secara profesional, efektif, dan berkelanjutan.

The Company continuously maintains the operational reliability of its power plants and the quality of its electricity generation to ensure a stable and sustainable supply to PLN. Looking ahead, although the Company has not yet decided to actively seek new customers, it remains open to various collaboration opportunities with other parties, particularly from the private sector, provided that such opportunities are in line with the Company's business development strategy.

The Company also continues to closely monitor regulatory developments, the investment climate, and the potential development of new projects in the renewable energy sector as part of its efforts to expand its business portfolio and further strengthen its contribution to supporting sustainable national energy security.

PT Arkora Hydro Tbk operates in the development, ownership, and operation of hydropower generation facilities utilizing run-of-river systems. Run-of-river hydropower plants are a type of power generation that does not require the construction of large dams for water storage, thereby having a relatively lower environmental impact, shorter development time, and greater adaptability to the geographical conditions across various regions in Indonesia.

As of the end of 2025, the Company has 3 (three) projects that are commercially operational with a total installed capacity of 27.4 MW. In addition, when combined with the contracted capacity, the Company's total power generation capacity amounts to 62.8 MW. The Company also has a portfolio of projects under development (pipeline projects) with a total capacity of more than 300 MW, which are located across several regions with adequate hydropower potential.

All of the pipeline projects reflect the Company's preparedness and the sustainability of its long-term business growth. The Company remains consistently focused on the professional, effective, and sustainable development and operation of hydropower plants.
