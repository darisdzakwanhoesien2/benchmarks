# Pelatihan dan Pengembangan Karyawan [F.22] Employee Training and Development [F.22]

Perseroan meyakini bahwa peningkatan kualitas sumber daya manusia merupakan salah satu faktor kunci dalam memastikan keberlanjutan usaha dan mempertahankan daya saing di industri. Oleh karena itu, strategi pengembangan kompetensi karyawan dirancang untuk mendukung kebutuhan bisnis.

Rata-rata jam pelatihan karyawan pada tahun 2025 mencapai 17 jam yang dieselenggarakan oleh PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur ("Persero") sebagai lembaga pembiayaan proyek Perseroan. Terdapat 2 karyawan yang berpartisipasi atas 3% dari total keseluruhan karyawan yang dimiliki Perseroan tahun 2025. [S-05]

The Company also has a Human Rights (HAM) policy that regulates the respect, enforcement, and protection of human rights. This is likewise outlined in the Company Policy Related to Social and Environmental Aspects, applicable to the Company and all its subsidiaries. [S-09]

The Company believes that improving the quality of its human resources is one of the key factors in ensuring business sustainability and maintaining competitiveness in the industry. Therefore, the employee competency development strategy is designed to align with the business needs.

In average, the employee training hour in 2025 reached 17 hour, organized by PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur ("Persero") as the Company's project financing institution. There were 2 employees participated or 3% from the total employees the Company had in 2025. [S-05]

# Pengembangan Masyarakat Community Development

Aktivitas operasional dan akses jalan menuju proyek berada di wilayah yang berdekatan dengan pemukiman penduduk, sehingga keberadaan Perseroan memiliki keterkaitan langsung dengan kehidupan masyarakat sekitar.

Perseroan berupaya untuk mendukung pertumbuhan berkelanjutan masyarakat dengan memberikan dampak positif yang luas melalui penerapan strategi pengembangan masyarakat. Upaya berkelanjutan dilakukan untuk mendorong pertumbuhan yang kuat sekaligus menghasilkan manfaat langsung bagi masyarakat, sejalan dengan nilai-nilai Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan (TPB). Melalui pendekatan ini, Perseroan berupaya membangun fondasi yang kokoh untuk pertumbuhan yang inklusif dan berjangka panjang, serta memberikan kontribusi langsung bagi komunitas di sekitar wilayah operasional.

The operational activities and access roads to the hydropower plant are located near residential areas, which makes the Company aware of its close connection to the surrounding communities.

The Company strives to support communities in achieving sustainable growth by delivering broad positive impacts through the implementation of community development strategies. Continuous efforts are made to drive robust development while generating direct benefits for the community, in alignment with the values of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Through this approach, the Company seeks to establish a solid foundation for inclusive and long-term growth, contributing directly to the well-being of communities surrounding the operational areas.
