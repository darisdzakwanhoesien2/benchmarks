# Penanggung Jawab Penerapan Aspek Berkelanjutan [E.1]
## The Responsibility to Implement Sustainability Aspects [E.1]

Penerapan aspek keberlanjutan dalam operasional Perseroan menjadi tanggung jawab Direksi sepenuhnya. Direksi berperan dalam menentukan kebijakan keberlanjutan, melakukan koordinasi dan memimpin penerapan praktik keberlanjutan.

Dalam menerapkan aspek keberlanjutan di aktivitas operasional sehari-hari, Direksi dibantu oleh divisi-divisi terkait yaitu divisi ESG Specialist yang bertanggung jawab melaksanakan aspek keberlanjutan sesuai bidang tugas mereka. Direksi juga dibantu oleh Komite Audit untuk memantau dan mengevaluasi program keberlanjutan yang dimiliki oleh Perseroan.

The implementation of sustainability aspects in the Company's operations becomes the responsibility of the Board of Directors. The Board of Directors plays a key role in establishing sustainability policies, coordinating programs and activities, and leading the implementation of sustainability practices.

In carrying out sustainability aspects in daily operational activities, the Board of Directors is supported by the relevant division which is ESG Specialist division responsible to execute sustainability initiatives within their respective areas of expertise. Additionally, the Board of Directors is also assisted by the Audit Committee in monitoring and evaluating the Company's sustainability programs.

# Pelaksanaan Tugas Direksi 2025
## Implementation of the Duties of the Board of Directors In 2025

Sepanjang tahun 2025, Direksi telah melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya sesuai dengan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan dan serta peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku. Direksi bertanggung jawab atas pengelolaan operasional Perseroan, penyusunan dan pelaksanaan strategi bisnis, serta pengambilan keputusan strategis guna mendukung pencapaian kinerja dan keberlanjutan usaha Perseroan.

Dalam pelaksanaannya, Direksi fokus pada implementasi rencana kerja dan anggaran tahun 2025, penguatan kinerja operasional dan keuangan, serta upaya peningkatan efisiensi dan efektivitas kegiatan usaha. Direksi juga secara aktif merespons dinamika industri dan kondisi perekonomian dengan langkah-langkah strategis yang terukur untuk menjaga stabilitas dan pertumbuhan Perseroan.

Selain itu, Direksi berkomitmen terhadap penerapan Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik (Good Corporate Governance) secara konsisten, termasuk pemenuhan aspek kepatuhan, pengendalian internal, dan manajemen risiko. Direksi juga menjalin koordinasi dan komunikasi yang efektif dengan Dewan Komisaris, serta menindaklanjuti arahan dan rekomendasi yang diberikan sebagai bagian dari mekanisme pengawasan yang berkelanjutan.

In 2025, the Board of Directors has carried out its duties and responsibilities in accordance with the Company's Articles of Association and applicable laws and regulations. The Board of Directors is responsible for managing the Company's operations, formulating and implementing business strategies, and making strategic decisions to support the achievement of the Company's performance and business sustainability.

In its implementation, the Board of Directors focused on the implementation of the 2025 work plan and budget, strengthening operational and financial performance, and efforts to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of business activities. The Board of Directors also actively responded to industry dynamics and economic conditions with measured strategic steps to maintain the Company's stability and growth.

In addition, the Board of Directors is committed to the consistent implementation of Good Corporate Governance, including compliance, internal control, and risk management. The Board of Directors also establishes effective coordination and communication with the Board of Commissioners and follows up on the directions and recommendations given as part of a continuous monitoring mechanism.
