# Struktur dan Besaran Remunerasi Direksi Dan Dewan Komisaris

Selama tahun 2025, Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris telah menerima sejumlah remunerasi. Remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi yang telah ditetapkan mencakup beberapa komponen, antara lain:

1. Honorarium
2. Tunjangan
3. Fasilitas
4. Insentif Kinerja

Perseroan tidak memberikan remunerasi dalam bentuk opsi saham kepada Dewan Komisaris maupun Direksi.

Mengacu pada ketentuan tersebut, remunerasi yang diberikan kepada Dewan Komisaris secara kolektif pada tahun 2025 adalah sebesar Rp367.409.308 (tiga ratus enam puluh tujuh juta empat ratus sembilan ribu tiga ratus delapan Rupiah) per tahun.

The Board of Directors are entitled to receive remuneration with the following conditions:

2. The amount of remuneration for each member of the Board of Directors is determined by considering the performance and achievement of individual targets, the performance and financial condition of the Company, professional salary standards in the industrial sector and/or similar business scale, along with other relevant factors.
3. Stipulation of the remuneration of the Board of Directors can be delegated by the GMS to the Board of Commissioners while still considering the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee.

# Structure and Amount of Remuneration of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners

In 2025, the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners have received a number of remunerations with details as follows:

1. Honorarium
2. Allowances
3. Facilities
4. Performance Incentive

The Company does not provide remuneration in the form of stock options to the Board of Commissioners or the Board of Directors.

Referring to these provisions, the remuneration given to the Board of Commissioners collectively in 2025 amounted to Rp367,409,308 (three hundred sixty-seven million four hundred nine thousand three hundred eight Rupiah) per year.
