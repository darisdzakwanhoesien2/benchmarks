Laporan Tahunan &amp; Keberlanjutan 2025

# Laporan Dewan Komisaris

Report of The Board of Commissioners

Dari sisi operasional, Perseroan mencatatkan kinerja yang solid dengan kapasitas kontrak mencapai 62,8 MW, penambahan proyek PLTA Pongbembe 20 MW, serta realisasi produksi listrik sebesar 92,45% dari target tahunan, mencerminkan pencapaian yang patut diapresiasi.

From an operational perspective, the Company generated solid performance, achieving contracted capacity of 62.8 MW, securing the addition of the 20 MW Project Pongbembe Hydropower, and realizing 92.45% of its annual electricity production target, reflecting an accomplishment worthy of appreciation.

# Para Pemegang Saham dan Pemangku Kepentingan yang Terhormat,

Sepanjang tahun 2025, Perseroan telah menunjukkan kinerja yang solid dan progresif dari sisi pendapatan dan produksi listrik yang terjaga sepanjang tahun. Perseroan juga telah menambah satu Perjanjian Jual Beli Listrik (PJBL) baru yaitu Proyek Pongbembe dengan kapasitas 20 MW. Kami memberikan apresiasi atas upaya Direksi menghadapi dinamika pasar dan memanfaatkan peluang untuk mendorong pertumbuhan.

# Dear Esteemed Shareholders and Stakeholders,

Throughout 2025, the Company demonstrated solid and progressive performance in revenue growth and consistently maintained electricity production throughout the year. The Company also added one new project that has commenced its Commercial Operation Date (COD) and secured a Power Purchase Agreement (PPA), namely the Project Pongbembe with a capacity of 20 MW. We really appreciate what the Board of Directors has done in navigating market dynamics and capitalizing on opportunities to drive growth.
