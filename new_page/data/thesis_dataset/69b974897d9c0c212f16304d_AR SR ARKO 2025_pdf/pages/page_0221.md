## Pengelolaan Emisi
### Emission Management

Pengelolaan emisi gas rumah kaca (GRK) menjadi salah satu komitmen Perseroan dalam mendukung pengendalian dampak lingkungan. Pembangkit tenaga listrik berbasis energi baru dan terbarukan (EBT) yang dikelola Perseroan menjadi salah satu solusi yang diperlukan dunia saat ini untuk mengatasi emisi GRK.

Proyek yang dikelola Perseroan menghasilkan listrik dengan emisi yang sangat minim. Walaupun demikian, Perseroan berkomitmen untuk menekan tingkat emisi yang dihasilkan hingga seminimal mungkin.

Dalam Laporan Keberlanjutan ini, emisi yang dilaporkan adalah emisi GRK langsung (cakupan 1) yang dihasilkan dari penggunaan bahan bakar minyak berupa bensin dan diesel untuk kegiatan pendukung dan emisi GRK (cakupan 2) tidak langsung yang bersumber dari penggunaan listrik. Emisi GRK yang dihasilkan dari aktivitas operasional Perseroan berupa massa karbon dioksida ekuivalen (CO_{2}eq). Perseroan tidak menghasilkan emisi lain karena tidak ada proses pembakaran dalam proses produksi.

All operational projects are not located in conservation areas. However, the Company continues to implement biodiversity management efforts, as vegetation loss and potential habitat fragmentation may occur during the construction phase due to land acquisition activities. To mitigate these impacts, the Company has planned land revegetation in project areas, undertaken watershed rehabilitation, and provided both verbal and written education to employees and local communities.

Particularly in 2025, the environmental preservation activities carried out by the Company through its subsidiaries in charge of project operations were watershed rehabilitation, provision of hazardous waste storage infrastructure, waste management cooperation, and periodic environmental quality monitoring.

Management of Greenhouse Gas (GHG) emissions is one of the Company's commitment in supporting environmental impact control. The renewable energy-based power plants managed by the Company serve as one of the essential solutions needed today to address GHG emissions.

The project managed by the Company generates electricity with very minimal emissions. However, the Company remains committed to reducing emission levels as much as possible.

In this Sustainability Report, the emissions reported include direct GHG emissions (scope 1) resulting from the use of fuel such as gasoline and diesel for supporting activities, as well as indirect GHG emissions (scope 2) from electricity consumption. The GHG emissions produced from the Company's operational activities are in the form of mass carbon dioxide equivalent (CO_{2}eq). The Company does not generate other emissions since there is no combustion process in production.
