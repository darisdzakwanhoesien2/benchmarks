At the end of 2025, the cash and cash equivalents balance at the end of the year amounted to Rp99.33 billion. This value increased by 166% compared to the previous year, which was due to substantial proceeds in cashflow of financing activities.

# Rasio Likuiditas
# Liquidity Ratio

Rasio likuiditas mengacu pada rasio lancar, Perseroan melakukan penghitungan dengan membandingkan nilai aset lancar dengan liabilitas jangka pendek.

Per 31 Desember 2025, rasio rasio lancar Perseroan tercatat sebesar 65,8%. Terjadi penurunan dari tahun sebelumnya karena peningkatan signifikan pada liabilitas jangka pendek yang disebabkan oleh utang obligasi yang akan jatuh tempo dalam satu tahun.

Nilai rasio memperlihatkan bahwa Perseroan memiliki kemampuan yang cukup untuk memenuhi kewajiban jangka pendek.

The liquidity ratio refers to the current ratio, and the Company calculates it by comparing the value of current assets with current liabilities.

As of December 31, 2025, the Company's current ratio amounted to 65.8%. This was lower than the previous year due to significant increase in current liability due to bonds payable that is to be due within one year.

The ratio indicates that the Company has sufficient ability to meet its short-term obligations.
