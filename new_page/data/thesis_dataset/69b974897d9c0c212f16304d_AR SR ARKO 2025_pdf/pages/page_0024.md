# Pengawasan Terhadap Implementasi Strategi Perusahaan Supervision on The Implementation of The Company's Strategy

Dewan Komisaris menjalankan fungsi pengawasan terhadap seluruh perumusan dan implementasi strategi yang ditetapkan oleh Direksi, Proses tersebut dilakukan melalui rapat rutin dengan Direksi, evaluasi kinerja, dan pemanfaatan komite pendukung yaitu Komite Audit dan Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi. Kedua komite telah mendukung kami secara penuh, bertindak independen, serta memberikan pendapat dan rekomendasi yang akurat, terkait pelaporan keuangan, kinerja operasional, serta kepatuhan terhadap regulasi.

Sepanjang tahun 2025, kami telah mengadakan rapat gabungan untuk memonitor kinerja Direksi dan pencapaian Perseroan secara berkala. Total terdapat 6 kali rapat gabungan yang membahas update hasil keuangan, produksi listrik setiap proyek yang beroperasi dan pembangunan proyek baru.

Secara khusus, Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi memberikan perhatian terhadap sejumlah agenda strategis perihal evaluasi produksi listrik, pemantauan pembangunan proyek yang sedang berlangsung yaitu PLTA Tomoni (10 MW) dan PLTA Kukusan 2 (5,4 MW), serta persetujuan pengembangan proyek. Kami mendukung dan senantiasa memantau perkembangan pipeline proyek dengan ekspansi 300 MW dalam lima tahun ke depan untuk mendukung target Net Zero Emission 2060.

The Board of Commissioners carries out its supervisory function over the formulation and implementation of strategies established by the Board of Directors. This process is conducted through regular meetings with the Board of Directors, performance evaluations, and the utilization of supporting committees, namely the Audit Committee and the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. Both committees have fully supported the Board of Commissioners, acted independently, and provided accurate opinions and recommendations related to financial reporting, operational performance, and regulatory compliance.

Throughout 2025, joint meetings were held regularly to monitor the performance of the Board of Directors and the Company's achievements. A total of 6 joint meetings were conducted to discuss financial performance updates, electricity production from operating projects, and the development of new projects.

In particular, the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors focused on several strategic agendas, including the evaluation of electricity production, monitoring the construction of ongoing projects such as the Tomoni Hydropower Plant (10 MW) and the Kukusan 2 Hydropower Plant (5.4 MW), as well as approving project development initiatives. We support and continuously monitor the development of the project pipeline, targeting an expansion of 300 MW over the next five years to support the Net Zero Emission 2060 target.
