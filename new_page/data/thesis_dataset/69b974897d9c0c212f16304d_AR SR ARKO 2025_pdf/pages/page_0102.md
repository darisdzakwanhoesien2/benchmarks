The cost of revenue in 2025 was recorded at Rp124.56 billion. The cost of revenue and operating expenses increased by 99.69% and 30.12%, respectively, compared to the previous year. In general, the increase in expenses across these accounts occurred as the construction works had reached the finalization stage.

As a result, by the end of 2025, the Company recorded a profit of Rp3.31 billion from the Project Kukusan 2, representing a decrease of 89.8% compared to the previous year.

# Segment Operasi Proyek Tomoni (10 MW)
Operating Segment of Project Tomoni (10 MW)

Proyek Tomoni berlokasi di Poso. Pembangunan Proyek Tomoni dimulai pada kuartal kedua tahun 2024 dengan target COD pada tahun 2026. Proyek Tomoni memiliki kapasitas produksi pembangkit sebesar 10 MW dengan perkiraan output energi tahunan sebesar 56.940 MWh.

Proyek Tomoni memiliki debit air sebesar 11,9 m³/detik dengan tinggi net head mencapai 95 m yang dilengkapi dengan dua turbin Francis horizontal.

The Project Tomoni is located in Poso. Construction of the Project Tomoni began in the second quarter of 2024 with a target COD in 2026. The Tomoni Project has a generation capacity of 10 MW with an estimated annual energy output of 56,940 MWh.

The project has a water discharge of 11.9 m³/second with a net head of 95 meters, and is equipped with two horizontal Francis turbines.
