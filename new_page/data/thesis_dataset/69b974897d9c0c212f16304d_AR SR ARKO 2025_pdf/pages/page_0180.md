## Sertifikasi Profesi UAI
### Internal Audit Profession Certification

Audit Internal didorong untuk memiliki kompetensi teknis di bidang audit dan kompetensi umum yang dapat menunjang pelaksanaan tugas. Ketua beserta anggota Audit Internal merupakan orang yang terbaik, berpengalaman, dan berkompeten dalam bidang masing-masing. Hal tersebut bertujuan untuk memastikan bahwa proses audit pada Perseroan dapat terlaksana dengan benar dan bertanggung jawab.

## Pelaksanaan Tugas UAI
### Implementation of UAI Duties

Sepanjang tahun 2025, Unit Audit Internal telah melaksanakan tugas sebagai berikut:

1. Rencana kerja Audit Internal 2025 telah mendapatkan persetujuan Direktur Utama serta telah dilaksanakan sepenuhnya oleh Audit Internal.
2. Sistem pengendalian internal senantiasa dievaluasi untuk mengukur tingkat efektivitas pelaksanaannya setiap semester.
3. Melakukan evaluasi atas pelaksanaan kegiatan operasional pada masing-masing bidang, yaitu keuangan, akuntansi, sumber daya manusia, operasional, pemasaran, serta teknologi informasi;
4. Melaksanakan cash count dan rekonsiliasi bank guna memastikan kas terkontrol dengan baik;
5. Meninjau stock opname terhadap persediaan, baik itu barang jadi, bahan baku, bahan pembantu, atau suku cadang;
6. Mengkaji efisiensi atas penggunaan jumlah bahan baku terhadap output produksi;
7. Memantau dan mengevaluasi bahan bakar minyak;
8. Memeriksa penjualan/distribusi dan piutang usaha;
9. Memeriksa pembelian dan utang usaha; dan
10. Memeriksa Laporan Keuangan.

3. Hold regular and incidental meetings with the Board of Directors, Board of Commissioners and/or Audit Committee; also
4. Coordinate their activities with the activities of external auditors.

Internal Audit is encouraged to have competence in auditing and general competence that can support the implementation of its duties. The Chairman and members of Internal Audit are the best, experienced, and competent people in their respective fields. It is to ensure that the audit process at the Company can be carried out correctly and responsibly.

In 2025, Internal Audit Unit has performed the following duties:

1. The 2025 Internal Audit work plan has received approval from the President Director and has been fully implemented by Internal Audit.
2. The internal control system is constantly evaluated to measure the level of effectiveness of its implementation every semester.
3. Evaluate the implementation of operational activities in each field such as finance, accounting, human resources, operations, marketing and information technology;
4. Conduct cash counts and bank reconciliations to ensure cash is well controlled;
5. Review stock-taking of inventory, be it finished goods, raw materials, auxiliary materials, or spare parts;
6. Assess the efficiency of the raw materials use on production output;
7. Monitor and evaluate fuel oil;
8. Check sales/distribution and trade receivables;
9. Check purchases and accounts payable; and
10. Check Financial Reports.
