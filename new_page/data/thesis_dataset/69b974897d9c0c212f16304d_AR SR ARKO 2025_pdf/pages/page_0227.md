# Kesetaraan Kesempatan Bekerja [F.18] Equal Working Opportunities [F.18]

Semua kandidat memiliki kesempatan yang sama untuk bekerja di Perseroan, karena persyaratannya terutama didasarkan pada kualifikasi/profesional. Hal ini juga berlaku bagi para karyawan, terutama terkait promosi. Dalam melakukan penilaian, Perseroan memastikan prosesnya dilakukan secara obyektif dan transparan berdasarkan kualifikasi, kompetensi, pengalaman dan profesionalisme individu.

Baik karyawan laki-laki maupun perempuan memiliki kesempatan yang setara, namun beberapa posisi di site mayoritas ditempat oleh karyawan laki-laki. Hal ini disebabkan oleh sifat pekerjaan yang memerlukan kekuatan fisik dan kemauan bekerja pada lingkungan dengan tingkat mobilitas yang tinggi. Selain itu, minat bekerja di lapangan juga lebih banyak datang dari kandidat karyawan laki-laki.

Gambaran mengenai komposisi gender karyawan telah disajikan pada Bab Profil Perusahaan.

Komitmen terhadap kesetaraan kesempatan bekerja juga tercermin dalam keberpihakan Perseroan terhadap masyarakat lokal. Perseroan secara aktif memberikan peluang kerja bagi warga setempat yang tinggal di sekitar area operasional proyek. Upaya ini tidak hanya dimaksudkan untuk mendukung pertumbuhan ekonomi masyarakat lokal, tetapi juga memberikan manfaat strategis bagi Perseroan, antara lain efisiensi jarak tempuh, efektivitas waktu kerja, serta penguatan hubungan dengan komunitas sekitar.

The employee turnover rate of the Company over the last three years (2023-2025) is considered low, always remaining below 10%. This achievement demonstrates that the Company treats employees fairly, offers competitive remuneration, and maintains harmony in the work environment.

All candidates have equal opportunities to work at the Company, as the requirements are primarily based on professional qualifications. This also applies to all employees, especially in terms of promotion. When making assessment, the Company ensures the process is carried out objectively and transparently based on individual's qualifications, competence, experience and professionalism.

Both male and female employees have the same opportunities, however, some positions in the site are mostly filled by male employees. This is due to the nature of the work which requires physical strength and a willingness to work in an environment with a high level of mobility. In addition, interest in working at site also comes more from male candidates.

The overview of the employees' gender composition has been presented in the Company Profile Chapter.

The Company's commitment to equal employment opportunities is also reflected in its support for the local community. The Company actively provides job opportunities for local residents living around the project's operational area. This initiative is not only intended to support the economic growth of the local community but also offers strategic benefits for the Company, including reduced travel distance, improved work-time efficiency, and strengthened relationships with surrounding communities.
