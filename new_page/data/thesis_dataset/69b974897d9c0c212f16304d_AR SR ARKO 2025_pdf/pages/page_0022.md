# Pandangan Terhadap Situasi Ekonomi dan Industri
## Overview of The Economic and Industrial Situation

Tahun 2025 merupakan tahun yang menantang karena ekonomi global masih diwarnai ketidakpastian mulai dari perlambatan ekonomi dunia, ketegangan geopolitik, hingga fragmentasi perdagangan internasional. Situasi global juga berdampak pada Indonesia, namun seiring dengan selesainya proses pergantian pemerintah pada akhir 2024, kondisi Indonesia tetap stabil karena arah kebijakan menjadi lebih jelas dan membuat kepercayaan investor meningkat sehingga mendorong realisasi investasi di berbagai sektor. Hal ini terlihat dari pertumbuhan penanaman modal dalam negeri dan asing (PMDN dan PMA) yang tumbuh 9,74% (y-on-y) dan 12,66% (c-to-c) menurut Badan Koordinasi Penanaman Modal Indonesia.

The year 2025 was a challenging period as the global economy remained marked by uncertainty, including a global economic slowdown, geopolitical tensions, and fragmentation of international trade. These global conditions also affected Indonesia. However, following the completion of the government transition at the end of 2024, Indonesia's condition remained stable as policy direction became clearer, boosting investor confidence and encouraging investment realization across various sectors. This was reflected in the growth of domestic and foreign investment (PMDN and PMA), which increased by 9.74% (y-on-y) 12.66% (c-to-c), respectively, according to Indonesia Investment Coordinating Board.
