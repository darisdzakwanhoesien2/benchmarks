# 2024

## Operational Proyek Koro Yaentu

- Proyek Tomoni berhasil mendapatkan pembiayaan dari PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur (Persero) pada tanggal 15 Agustus 2024.
- Proyek Koro Yaentu telah beroperasi secara komersial pada tanggal 16 Oktober 2024.

## Project Koro Yaentu Operational

- Project Tomoni received financing from PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur (Persero) on August 15, 2024.
- Project Koro Yaentu has been operating commercially.

# 2022

## Go-Public

- Perseroan resmi terdaftar di Bursa Efek Indonesia dengan kode saham "ARKO" pada 8 Juli 2022.
- Perseroan melakukan penandatanganan PJBL Proyek Kukusan 2 (5.4 MW) di Tanggamus, Lampung dan memulai konstruksi.

## Go-Public

- The Company was officially listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange with ticker code "ARKO" on July 8, 2022.
- The Company signed the PPA for Project Kukusan 2 (5.4 MW) in Tanggamus, Lampung and commenced construction.

# 2025

## Penandatanganan PJBL Proyek Pongbembe dan Tahap Awal Konstruksi

Penandatangan kontrak PJBL untuk Proyek Pongbembe (20 MW) dan Perseroan memulai konstruksi Proyek Pongbembe.

## Signing of the PPA for the Pongbembe Project and Early Stage of Construction

Signing a PPA contract for Project Pongbembe (20 MW) and also the Company started the construction of Project Pongbembe.

# 2023

## Penerbitan Green Bonds

- Perseroan menerbitkan Obligasi Berwawasan Lingkungan "Green Bonds" dan telah tercatat pada Bursa Efek Indonesia pada 9 Agustus 2023.
- Proyek Kukusan 2 mendapatkan fasilitas pembiayaan konstruksi PLTA dari PT Indonesia Infrastructure Finance.
- Perseroan melalui anak perusahaannya, PT Arkora Hydro Mailili telah menandatangani PJBL dengan PLN untuk mengembangkan Proyek Tomoni dengan kapasitas produksi 10 MW.

## Green Bonds Issuance

- The Company issued Environmentally Friendly Bond "Green Bonds" and was listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange on August 9, 2023.
- Project Kukusan 2 received funding from PT Indonesia Infrastructure Finance.
- The Company, through its subsidiary PT Arkora Hydro Mailili, has signed a Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) with PLN to develop the Tomoni Project with 10 MW production capacity.

# 2021

## Pembiayaan Proyek Koro Yaentu

- Perseroan memulai konstruksi Proyek Koro Yaentu.
- Proyek Koro Yaentu mendapatkan pembiayaan dari PT Indonesia Infrastructure Finance.

## Project Koro Yaentu Financing

- The Company started the construction of Project Koro Yaentu.
- Project Koro Yaentu received funding from PT Indonesia Infrastructure Finance.
