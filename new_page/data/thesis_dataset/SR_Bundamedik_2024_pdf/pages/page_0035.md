## Business Activities [C.4][2-6]

Company operates in the healthcare sector, focusing on managing a of hospitals and clinics under the Bunda Hospital Group, which is spread across several cities. This network includes RSIA Bunda Jakarta, RSU Bunda Jakarta, RSU Bunda Margonda, RSIA Bunda Ciputat, RSU Bunda Padang, RSU Citra Harapan, RSIA Az Zahra Palembang, RSIA Pusura Tegalsari, RSIA Bunda Denpasar, RSJP Paramarta Bandung, and BIC Vida Bekasi Clinic.
