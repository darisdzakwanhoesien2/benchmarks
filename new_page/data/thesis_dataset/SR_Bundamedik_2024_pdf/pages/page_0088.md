Bersama Nudah Mudar

Di Yayasan Metta Mama dan Maggha, yang saat ini merawat lebih dari 61 bayi dan anak terlantar dengan bantuan 35 bidan, Perseroan memberikan edukasi kesehatan terkait perawatan bayi, pemberian MPASI, serta tumbuh kembang anak. Sementara itu, di Desa Padang Sambian Kaja, Perseroan mengadakan pelatihan penanganan pertama pada kegawatdaruratan bagi lebih dari 50 perangkat desa. Kegiatan ini ditutup di Panti Asuhan Sidhi Astu, dengan 70 anak usia sekolah dasar dan remaja diberikan edukasi tentang anatomi tubuh serta sistem reproduksi pada remaja. Perseroan juga memberikan pemeriksaan kesehatan gratis untuk seluruh penerima manfaat serta mendonasikan alat kesehatan, alat kebersihan dan sanitasi, alat ukur berat dan tinggi badan bayi dan balita, serta perlengkapan kebutuhan anak seperti susu dan popok.

At the Yayasan Metta Mama dan Maggha, which currently cares for more than 61 abandoned babies and children with the help of 35 midwives, Company provided health education related to baby care, complementary food, and child development. In Padang Sambian Kaja Village, Company held emergency first aid training for more than 50 village officials. The activities closed at the Sidhi Astu Orphanage, where 70 elementary students and teenagers were educated about body anatomy and the reproductive system of teenagers. Company also provided free medical check-up for all beneficiaries and donated medical equipment, cleaning and sanitation equipment, measuring tools for the weight and height of babies and toddlers, as well as children's necessities such as milk and diapers.

# Gadang Alang Laweh

Perseroan secara proaktif menghadirkan manfaat dan nilai tambah bagi komunitas masyarakat warga sekitar lingkungan Rumah Sakit Bunda Group. Pada 3 Oktober 2024, Perseroan menyelenggarakan kegiatan normalisasi Sungai Banda Gadang di Kelurahan Alang Laweh, yang berlokasi dekat dengan RSU Bunda Padang, salah satu jaringan Rumah Sakit Bunda Group. Kegiatan ini bertujuan mencegah potensi banjir saat musim hujan, yang dapat mengganggu aktivitas warga sekitar.

# Normalization the Banda Gadang

# Alang Laweh River

The Company proactively brings benefits and added value to the communities surrounding Bunda Group Hospital. On October 3, 2024, The Company supported Banda Gadang River normalization in Alang Laweh Village, which is located close to RSU Bunda Padang, one of the Bunda Group Hospitals. This activity aimed at preventing potential flooding during the rainy season, which can disrupt the activities of local residents.
