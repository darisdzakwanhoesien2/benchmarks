# Mendukung Masa Depan Anak dan Masyarakat Bali

Pada 29 Agustus 2024, kegiatan TJSL Kolaborasi Perseroan Group dilaksanakan di tiga lokasi di Bali, yaitu Yayasan Metta Mama Maggha, Desa Padang Sambian Kaja, serta Panti Asuhan Sidhi Astu. Program ini memberikan manfaat kepada lebih dari 200 peserta, yang terdiri dari bidan, perangkat desa, serta anak-anak (baik pada bayi, balita, usia sekolah dasar hingga remaja).

# Supporting the Future of Balinese Children and Society

On August 29, 2024, Company Group Joint CSR activities were carried out in three locations in Bali, at the Yayasan Metta Mama Maggha, Padang Sambian Kaja Village, and the Sidhi Astu Orphanage. This program benefited more than 200 participants, including midwives, village officials, and children (including infants, toddlers, elementary school-aged children, and teenagers).
