Sepanjang 2024, kami menilai bahwa Direksi telah mengelola Perseroan dengan baik melalui penerapan strategi yang efektif dalam menjalankan bisnis yang berkelanjutan. Kinerja tersebut tercermin dari laporan keuangan yang positif, dengan pendapatan sebesar Rp1,55 triliun atau mencapai 93% dari target Perseroan yang sebesar Rp1,66 triliun. Capaian laba tahun berjalan sebesar Rp18,9 miliar atau mencapai target pertumbuhan laba bersih sebesar double digit growth (FY24: 16% YoY). Sementara pada struktur modal, Perseroan menimbang struktur modal yang dimiliki telah seimbang, sehingga tidak terdapat perubahan target pada tahun berjalan.

Dari sisi operasional dan lingkungan, kami memberikan apresiasi atas upaya pengelolaan limbah dan konsumsi air secara bertanggung jawab. Dewan Komisaris juga mendukung penuh program peningkatan kesejahteraan karyawan, termasuk peluncuran Kebijakan Laktasi, serta program employee assistance untuk mendukung kesehatan mental karyawan. Peningkatan hasil survei kepuasan pelanggan yang mencapai 4,63 dan rata-rata jam pelatihan karyawan sebesar 52,24 jam/orang juga menjadi indikator bahwa manajemen berhasil menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang nyaman dan produktif.

Kontribusi sosial perusahaan dalam bentuk layanan kesehatan gratis, pelatihan untuk masyarakat, serta berbagai program donasi juga menunjukkan komitmen Perseroan dalam menciptakan dampak positif bagi masyarakat sekitar. Kami menyarankan agar kegiatan ini terus ditingkatkan dengan lebih memperhatikan dampak kemandirian dan penciptaan nilai jangka panjang.

Dewan Komisaris menilai bahwa seluruh kegiatan usaha senantiasa telah berjalan sesuai prinsip tata kelola perusahaan yang baik (GCG), serta mematuhi regulasi yang berlaku. Kami mendukung strategi berkelanjutan yang telah dirumuskan oleh manajemen, termasuk penerapan nilai 5C (Create Innovation, Compassion, Competence, Change Agility, dan Collaboration), yang menjadi fondasi budaya kerja di lingkungan Perseroan. Untuk itu, nilai 5C perlu ditanamkan sebagai budaya organisasi kepada setiap insan Perseroan.

Throughout 2024, our assessment shows that the Board of Directors has managed the Company effectively by implementing strategies that support sustainable business operations. This performance is reflected in the Company's positive financial results, with revenue of IDR 1.55 trillion, reaching 93% of the Company's target of IDR 1.66 trillion. The net profit for the year amounted to IDR 18.9 billion, meeting the target of double-digit growth in net profit (FY24: 16% YoY). Meanwhile, in terms of capital structure, the Company considers its existing capital structure to be balanced, and therefore there are no changes to the target for the current year.

From an operational and environmental perspective, we appreciate the Company's efforts in responsible waste and water consumption management. The Board of Commissioners also fully supports initiatives to enhancing employee well-being, including the launch of the Breastfeeding Policy and the employee assistance program to support mental health. The improvement in customer satisfaction survey results, which reached a score of 4.63, along with an average of 52.24 training hours per employee, further indicate that management has succeeded in fostering a comfortable and productive work environment.

The Company's social contribution in the form of free health services, community training, and donation programs demonstrates the Company's commitment to having a positive impact on the surrounding communities. We recommend that these initiatives continue to be enhanced with greater emphasis on fostering self-reliance and creating long-term value.

The Board of Commissioners is assess that all business activities have been conducted in accordance with the principles of good corporate governance (GCG) and in compliance with prevailing regulations. We fully support the sustainable strategies formulated by management, including the implementation of the 5C values (Create Innovation, Compassion, Competence, Change Agility, and Collaboration), which serve as the foundation of the work culture at the Company. Therefore, it is essential that the 5C values are instilled as a core organizational culture across all levels of the Company personnel.
