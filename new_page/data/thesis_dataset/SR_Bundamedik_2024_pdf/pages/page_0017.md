Kami memberikan apresiasi atas langkah manajemen dalam melakukan self assessment GCG dan pencapaian pemenuhan indikator PUGKI sebesar 79,01%. Penambahan Komite Procurement serta reorganisasi struktur tata kelola menunjukkan komitmen Perseroan dalam memperkuat sistem pengawasan dan akuntabilitas.

Peran Perseroan dalam mendukung pengembangan sumber daya manusia (SDM) yang unggul turut diwujudkan dengan turut ambil bagian dalam program rumah sakit pendidikan serta penyelengaraan Program Pendidikan Dokter Spesialis (PPDS). Selain itu, inovasi digital terus dikembangkan, termasuk integrasi sistem pembayaran dengan SatuSehat dan peningkatan penggunaan aplikasi OneBunda. Semua capaian ini menunjukkan bentuk transformasi yang relevan dengan tuntutan industri kesehatan modern.

## Pengawasan terhadap Strategi dan Tata Kelola

Dewan Komisaris perlu memastikan bahwa strategi perusahaan diarahkan untuk mencapai keberlanjutan bisnis yang optimal. Inisiatif keberlanjutan "IBU" harus menjadi bukti komitmen perusahaan dalam memberikan dampak positif bagi masyarakat dan lingkungan.

Strategi pertumbuhan yang berorientasi pada penguatan aset yang ada, kolaborasi antar entitas usaha, serta pengembangan center of excellence merupakan langkah tepat dalam menjawab tantangan industri kesehatan dan mengoptimalkan prospek usaha, khususnya bagi ibu dan anak. Standarisasi patient journey dengan layanan yang unggul dan mudah diakses, serta efisiensi rantai pasok melalui central procurement juga menjadi elemen penting dalam meningkatkan daya saing Perseroan. Dari sisi risiko, kami mencatat bahwa manajemen telah melakukan pemetaan risiko berbasis aspek lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola (LST).

Industri kesehatan mengalami perkembangan pesat, diikuti dengan meningkatnya kompetisi. Maka, adanya Center of Excellence (COE) yang unggul, didukung oleh tenaga medis yang kompeten dan inovasi menjadi sangat penting dalam memberikan layanan kesehatan yang optimal.

We appreciate management's steps taken to conduct a GCG self-assessment that showed 79.01% of the PUGKI indicators had been fulfilled. The addition of a Procurement Committee and the reorganization of the governance structure demonstrates the Company's commitment to strengthening the supervisory and accountability system.

The Company role for supporting the development of excellent human capital (HC) is being realized by taking part of teaching hospital programme, and the implementation of the Specialist Doctor Education Program (PPDS). In addition, digital innovations continue to be developed, including the integration of our payment systems with SatuSehat, and the increased use of the OneBunda application, which can be viewed as a form of transformation relevant to the demands of the modern health industry.

## Strategies and Governance Supervision

As the Board of Commissioners, we assure that the Company's strategies are directed towards achieving optimal business sustainability. The "IBU" sustainability initiatives have to be a proof of the Company's commitment to providing a positive impact on society and the environment.

Growth strategies oriented towards strengthening existing assets, collaboration between business entities, and development of centers of excellence are the right steps for responding to the health industry challenges and optimise business prospects, especially for mothers and children. Standardizing the patient journey using superior and easily accessible services based on community needs and supply chain efficiency through central procurement are important elements of increasing the Company's competitiveness. In terms of risk, we note that management has conducted risk mapping based on the environmental, social, and governance (ESG) aspects.

The healthcare industry is experiencing rapid growth, accompanied by increasing competition. In this context, the establishment of a strong Center of Excellence (COE), supported by competent medical professionals and continuous innovation, is essential to delivering optimal healthcare services.
