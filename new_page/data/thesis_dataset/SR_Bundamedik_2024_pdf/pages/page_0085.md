### Antenatal Care Training for Midwife Communities in Depok and Bekasi

To commemorate World Prematurity Day on November 17, 2024, RSU Bunda Margonda (Depok) and RSU Citra Harapan (Bekasi), held Antenatal Care Training and free medical check-up for 50 midwives in Depok and 55 midwives in Bekasi.
