# Nilai-Nilai Keberlanjutan (C1)
# Sustainability Values

Di tahun 2024, PT Bundamedik Tbk memperkuat internalisasi nilai - nilai dan budaya Perusahaan yaitu Corporate Values 5C (Compassion, Competence, Change Agility, Collaboration, Create Innovation). Untuk memperkuat, menghayati, dan mengimplementasikan budaya ini, Perseroan melaksanakan berbagai inisiatif dan upaya strategis. Beberapa di antaranya yaitu sebagai berikut:

1. Melakukan peninjauan dan penilaian proses implementasi Corporate Values (5C Check-in) di seluruh Unit dan Anak Usaha Perseroan.
2. Memberikan pelatihan mengenai nilai-nilai budaya Perseroan kepada seluruh karyawan.
3. Membuat Task Force Culture &amp; Employee Engagement serta menunjuk perwakilan karyawan sebagai 5C Champion.
4. Membuat theme song Bundamedik Group.
5. Menyelenggarakan town hall meeting mengenai Corporate Values 5C untuk seluruh manajemen dan pimpinan Perseroan.
6. Menyelenggarakan 5C Talks untuk para pimpinan Perseroan.
7. Memperkuat budaya perusahaan melalui berbagai artefak, program, dan kampanye mengenai Corporate Values 5C.

In 2024, PT Bundamedik Tbk strengthened the internalization of its corporate values and culture, known as the 5C (Compassion, Competence, Change Agility, Collaboration, Create Innovation). To embed, uphold, and implement this culture, the Company undertook various initiatives and strategic efforts. A few of them are as follows:

1. Reviewing and assessing the implementation of Corporate Values through the '5C Check-in' across all Company units and subsidiaries.
2. Providing training on corporate cultural values to all employees.
3. Establishing a Culture &amp; Employee Engagement Task Force and appointing employee representatives as 5C Champions.
4. Creating a theme song for Bundamedik Group.
5. Organizing a town hall meeting on the 5C Corporate Values for all Company management and leaders.
6. Hosting the 5C Talks for Company leaders.
7. Reinforcing corporate culture through various artifacts, programs, and campaigns centered on the 5C Corporate Values.
