# Tata Kelola Keberlanjutan Sustainability Governance

## Struktur Organisasi [E.1][2-9]
### Organizational Structure

Struktur organisasi di Perseroan melibatkan Direksi yang bertanggung jawab menjalankan kegiatan operasional Perseroan, termasuk kinerja LST. Selanjutnya Dewan Komisaris berwenang melakukan pengawasan kinerja Direksi dalam menjalankan kegiatan operasional. Pemegang saham, melalui Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS) memiliki kewenangan mengangkat dan memberhentikan anggota Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris. Dalam menjalankan tugasnya, Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris dibantu oleh komite-komite di bawahnya. [2-11]

Dalam mengelola kinerja dan dampak keberlanjutan, Direksi mendelegasikannya kepada unit-unit terkait. Kinerja ekonomi menjadi tanggung jawab jawab Direktorat Corporate Finance dan Direktorat Business Development &amp; Commercial. Kinerja sosial dan lingkungan menjadi tanggung jawab Direktorat Human Capital, Direktorat Medical Nursing &amp; Quality, Direktorat Corporate Secretary, serta masing-masing unit usaha rumah sakit dan klinik. Secara berkala, unit-unit tersebut melaporkan kinerja keberlanjutan kepada Direksi dan di bawah pengawasan Dewan Komisaris untuk disetujui, dikembangkan, dan diperbaharui sesuai dengan TPB. Kinerja dan dampak keberlanjutan ini juga kemudian akan ditinjau bersama pemangku kepentingan lainnya. [2-12][2-13][2-16][2-22]

The organizational structure of Company includes the Board of Directors who are responsible for managing the Company's operational activities, including ESG performance. Additionally, the Board of Commissioners is tasked with supervising the performance of the Board of Directors in executing these operational activities. Shareholders through a General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS), hold the authority to appoint and dismiss members of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners. To support their duties, both the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners are assisted by subordinate committees. [2-11]

To manage performance and sustainability impact, the Board of Directors delegates these responsibilities to the relevant units. Economic performance falls under the purview of the Corporate Finance Directorate and the Business Development &amp; Commercial Directorate. Social and environmental performance is the responsibility of the Human Capital Directorate, the Medical Nursing &amp; Quality Directorate, the Corporate Secretary Directorate, and each hospital and clinic business unit. These units periodically report their sustainability performance to the Board of Directors, with oversight from the Board of Commissioners for approval, development, and updates in alignment with the SDGs. This sustainability performance and impact is also reviewed by other stakeholders. [2-12][2-13][2-16][2-22]

# Penerapan Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik [2-9]
## Implementation of Good Corporate Governance (GCG)

Kami menerapkan praktik tata kelola berdasarkan prinsip tata kelola perusahaan yang baik (GCG), dan terus berupaya meningkatkan inisiatif terkait. Pada tahun 2024, kami melakukan uji mandiri (self-assessment) GCG dengan pendekatan "Apply/Explain". Dari hasil penilaian, menunjukkan bahwa Perseroan telah memenuhi 79,01% indikator PUGKI. Kami juga memastikan untuk selalu patuh terhadap undang-undang dan regulasi yang berlaku. Pada tahun 2024, tidak terdampak sanksi maupun denda yang diberikan kepada Perseroan, anggota Direksi, anggota Dewan Komisaris, baik dari lembaga berwenang maupun pihak regulator. [2-27]

We adhere to governance practices based on the principles of good corporate governance (GCG), and continue our efforts to improve related initiatives. In 2024, we conducted a GCG self-assessment utilizing the "Apply/Explain" approach. The assessment results indicate that the Company has met 79.01% of the PUGKI indicators. We also endeavor to comply with applicable laws and regulations. In 2024, no sanctions or fines imposed on the Company, its Board of Directors, or its Board of Commissioners, by any authorized institutions or regulators. [2-27]
