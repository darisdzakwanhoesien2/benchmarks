# Mekanisme Pengaduan Masyarakat [F.24][2-25]
## Public Complaints Mechanism

Perseroan memiliki saluran pengaduan yang dapat diakses masyarakat, pasien, maupun pemangku kepentingan lainnya untuk menyampaikan masukan, keluhan, maupun pengaduan melalui call center 1500799. Pengaduan yang masuk akan dikelola dan ditindaklanjuti oleh Customer Relations. Pada tahun 2024, kami menerima keluhan paling banyak terkait pelayanan rumah sakit. Namun demikian, seluruh (100%) pengaduan yang diterima telah direspons dan terselesaikan. Perseroan berupaya untuk terus memperbaiki dan meningkatkan pelayanan kami kepada seluruh pasien. Penyelesaian pengaduan diselesaikan berdasarkan kategori ringan (7x24 jam), sedang (3x24 jam), dan berat (1x24 jam).

The Company has a complaint channel that can be accessed by the public, patients, and other stakeholders to submit feedback, complaints, or concerns through call center 1500799. Complaints received will be managed and follow up by Customer Relations. In 2024, we received the most complaints related to the hospital services. However, all (100%) complaints received were responded and resolved. Company strives to continuously improve and enhance our services to all patients. Complaints were resolved based on its categories, which are minor (7x24 hours), moderate (3x24 hours) and severe (1x24 hours) categories.
