Penjelasan Dewan Komisaris
Message from the Board of Commissioners

Kami menyampaikan apresiasi yang tinggi atas pencapaian dan dedikasi Direksi dan seluruh jajaran manajemen dalam menjalankan kegiatan usaha yang berlandaskan nilai-nilai keberlanjutan, serta komitmen terhadap pelayanan kesehatan yang holistik, sejalan dengan semangat Because Family Matters.

We would like to express our highest appreciation for the achievements and dedication of the Board of Directors and all levels management for performing our business activities based on the sustainability values, with a commitment to holistic health services, in line with the spirit of Because Family Matters.

## Pemangku kepentingan yang kami hormati,

Pada kesempatan ini, Dewan Komisaris menyampaikan hasil tinjauan dan rekomendasi kepada Perseroan dalam mengelola aspek-aspek lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola (LST) guna memastikan optimalisasi penerapan prinsip-prinsip keberlanjutan.

## Penilaian Kinerja Direksi

Kondisi ketidakpastian perekonomian global, regulasi yang semakin ketat, dan perubahan teknologi yang pesat telah memengaruhi kondisi ekonomi global. Walaupun Indonesia tetap menunjukkan stabilitas ekonomi yang cukup baik, didukung oleh berbagai pemulihan nasional di beberapa sektor, namun kita tetap harus berhati-hati dalam mengambil setiap keputusan usaha. Untuk itu, kami menyarankan agar Perseroan terus meningkatkan ketangguhan dan mengimplementasikan strategi yang adaptif agar dapat menghadirkan layanan kesehatan yang berkualitas.

## Dear stakeholders,

On this occasion, the Board of Commissioners would like to present our review and recommendations to the Company regarding the management of environmental, social, and governance (ESG) aspects, to ensure the sustainability principles are applied optimally.

## Board of Directors' Performance Assessment

The uncertainties of the global economy, increasingly stringent regulations, and rapid technological advancements have significantly impacted global economic conditions. Although Indonesia continues to demonstrate relatively strong economic stability, supported by national recovery efforts across various sectors, prudent decision-making remains essential. In light of this, we recommend that the Company continue to strengthen its resilience and implement adaptive strategies to ensure the delivery of high-quality healthcare services.
