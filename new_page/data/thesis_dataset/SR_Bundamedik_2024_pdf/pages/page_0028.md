|   | Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja Karyawan & Pasien Occupational Health and Safety for Employee & Patient | Kesehatan dan keselamatan di tempat kerja sebagai aspek vital yang memengaruhi kualitas pelayanan kesehatan serta berkontribusi langsung terhadap kesehatan dan keselamatan pasien Occupational health and safety is a vital aspect that influences the quality of health services and contributes directly to patient health and safety | Perseroan memprioritaskan kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja (K3) bagi karyawan dan pasien gunna meningkatkan kualitas layanan. Perseroan menciptakan lingkungan rumah sakit yang bersih, nyaman, aman, dan sehat. Seluruh kegiatan usaha dijalankan dengan cermat, beretika, dan penuh kehati-hatian untuk memastikan keselamatan semua pihak. Company prioritizes occupational health and safety (OHS) for employees and patients to enhance service quality. The Company has established a clean, comfortable, safe, and healthy hospital environment. All business activities are conducted with care, ethics, and diligence to ensure the safety of all parties. Implementasi K3 di Perseroan mengacu pada Pasal 35 dan 47 Peraturan Perusahaan PT Bundamedik Tbk tahun 2023-2025, yang telah disahkan melalui Keputusan Kepala Dinas Tenaga Kerja, Transmigrasi, dan Energi Provinsi DKI Jakarta Nomor e-2196 Tahun 2023 tentang Pengesahan Peraturan Perusahaan Bundamedik. Setiap rumah sakit di bawah Perseroan memiliki unit kerja khusus yang mengelola Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja Rumah Sakit (K3RS), dengan personel yang telah lulus uji kompetensi dari Lembaga Sertifikasi Profesi (LSP) yang diakui oleh Badan Nasional Sertifikasi Profesi (BNSP). The implementation of OHS at Company is guided by Articles 35 and 47 of the Company Regulations of PT Bundamedik Tbk for 2023-2025, which were ratified through the Decree of the Head of the DKI Jakarta Provincial Manpower, Transmigration, and Energy Service Number e-2196 of 2023 regarding the Ratification of the Bundamedik Company Regulations. Each hospital under Company has a dedicated unit responsible for managing Hospital Occupational Safety and Health (HOSH), staffed by personnel who have passed competency tests from the Professional Certification Institute (LSP) recognized by the National Professional Certification Agency (BNSP). OHS performance is evaluated annually through the measurement of key performance indicators, with the results reported to the Hospital Director and forwarded to the Company.  |
