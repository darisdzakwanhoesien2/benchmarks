Dari kinerja sosial, kami terus berupaya untuk meningkatkan kenyamanan dan kesejahteraan karyawan. Kami meluncurkan Kebijakan Laktasi untuk mendukung karyawan perempuan dengan memberikan ruang dan fasilitas laktasi. Selain itu, Perseroan juga peduli terhadap kesehatan fisik dan mental karyawan. Kami memiliki Employee Assistance Program dengan fasilitas platform mental health dan konsultasi langsung dengan psikolog. Pada 2024, hasil survei tingkat keterikatan karyawan mencapai skor 78 dari skala 100.

Kami juga berupaya untuk memberikan dampak positif bagi masyarakat sekitar. Untuk meningkatkan kualitas kesehatan masyarakat, Perseroan memberikan pemeriksaan kesehatan gratis berupa pemeriksaan kesehatan umum, tumbuh kembang anak, dan kesehatan gigi. Pemeriksaan ini telah dilakukan 9 kali dengan total penerima manfaat kurang lebih 2.000 orang. Kami turut memberikan pelatihan perawatan kehamilan dan bayi untuk bidan, serta pelatihan pemberian pertolongan pertama pada anak di sekolah untuk para guru.

Pada 2024, kami melakukan normalisasi Sungai Banda Gadang di Kelurahan Alang Laweh, yang berlokasi dekat dengan RSU Bunda Padang, salah satu jaringan Rumah Sakit Bunda Group. Kegiatan ini bertujuan untuk mencegah potensi banjir saat musim hujan, yang dapat mengganggu aktivitas warga sekitar. Perseroan juga memberikan donasi perlengkapan rumah sakit, obat-obatan, pangan darurat, serta kebutuhan anak-anak untuk korban bencana alam, donasi buku dan pemeriksaan kesehatan gratis.

For our water consumption, in 2024 we used 98,345 m³, a decrease of 46.30% compared to 183,145 m³ in 2023. This decrease was due to the number of patients and the efforts made to save water by making signs about water use near the service area sinks, and making a schedule for watering plants.

For our social performance, we also strive to improve the comfort and welfare of all our employees. We launched the Breastfeeding Policy to support our female employees by providing lactation rooms and facilities. In addition, we care about the physical and mental health of employees, through an employee assistance program, where employees can access the mental health platform and consult directly with psychologists. In 2024, the employee engagement survey results reached a score of 78 on a scale of 100.

We also strive to make a positive impact on the surrounding communities. To improve the quality of public health, we provide free medical check-up in the form of general health checks, child development, and dental health. These programs were carried out 9 times for approximately 2,000 beneficiaries. We also provide training in antenatal and infant care for midwives, as well as training for teachers in providing first aid to students.

In 2024, we also conduct normalization the Banda Gadang River in Alang Laweh Village, which is located close to RSU Bunda Padang, part of the Bunda Group Hospital. This was undertaken to prevent potential flooding during the rainy season, which can disrupt the activities of local residents. We also donated medical equipments, emergency food, and children's needs for victims of natural disasters, as well as books and free medical check-up.
