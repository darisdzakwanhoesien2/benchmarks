## PEKAN SEHAT PERMATA

Demi mewujudkan PermataBankers yang peduli terhadap kesehatan diri, kami mencanangkan Program Pekan Sehat Permata. Tidak hanya dilakukan di kantor pusat, kantor cabang utama di luar kota juga melakukan program hal yang serupa. Kegiatan ini bertujuan membantu menerapkan gaya hidup sehat untuk PermataBankers dan meningkatkan kualitas kesehatan PermataBankers.

Pekan Sehat Permata merupakan rangkaian kegiatan yang terdiri dari: Health Check, Health Fair, Health Talk, Health Quiz, CPR Training, Blood Donation, Mini Gym, No Tobacco Day, Bike To Work, Photo Contest, Brain Buster, Social Action, Green Run &amp; Family Fun Walk, dan Health Campaign. Dalam pelaksanaannya, PermataBank bekerja sama dengan Koperasi Karyawan PermataBank, Garda Medika, PermataClub, Gold’s Gym, dan juga Serikat Pekerja Bank Permata.

## PERMATA HEALTHY WEEK

With the aim of guiding PermataBankers to care about their own health, we have designed the Permata Healthy Week Program. Held not only at head office but also at all main provincial offices. This activity aims to help create a lifestyle for PermataBankers to increase their quality.

Permata Healthy Week covers a range of activities comprising: Health Check, Health Fair, Health Talk, Health Quiz, CPR Training, Blood Donation, Mini Gym, No Tobacco Day, Bike To Work, Photo Contest, Brain Buster, Social Action, Green Run &amp; Family Fun Walk and Health Campaign. In its implementation, PermataBank also collaborated with PermataBank Employees Cooperative, Garda Medika, PermataClub, Gold’s Gym and the Permata Bank Labour Union.
