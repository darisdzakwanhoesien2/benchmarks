Corporate Governance for Sustainability

# STRUKTUR GCG
Untuk mendukung penerapan GCG, struktur GCG di PermataBank terdiri atas komponen-komponen sebagai berikut: G4-34[1]
- Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS)
- Dewan Komisaris
- Direksi
- Komite Audit
- Komite Pemantau Risiko
- Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi
- Audit Internal
- Komite-komite yang berada di bawah fungsi Direksi

# GCG STRUCTURE
In order to support the implementation of GCG, GCG structure at PermataBank is composed of the following components: [G4-34]
- General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS)
- Board of Commissioners
- Board of Directors
- Audit Committee
- Risk Monitoring Committee
- Remuneration and Nomination Committee
- Internal Audit
- Committees Beneath the Functions of the Board of Directors
