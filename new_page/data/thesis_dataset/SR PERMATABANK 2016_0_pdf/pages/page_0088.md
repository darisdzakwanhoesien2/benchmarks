# KOMITMEN PERMATABANK TERHADAP PEMANGKU KEPENTINGAN

PermataBank senantiasa memperoleh masukan yang berharga dari setiap pemangku kepentingan untuk meningkatkan kinerja layanan, mengembangkan produk, pengelolaan risiko maupun untuk meningkatkan reputasi PermataBank. Bagi kami, pelibatan seluruh pemangku kepentingan dalam menjalankan kegiatan operasional PermataBank adalah salah satu kunci keberlanjutan perusahaan.

Terdapat lima pemangku kepentingan PermataBank yang kami identifikasi berdasarkan potensi dampak operasional kami terhadap mereka dan mereka terhadap operasional, kinerja dan reputasi PermataBank. Selain itu kami juga turut mempertimbangkan prinsip-prinsip penentuan pemangku kepentingan dari panduan Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) G4, yakni: Responsibility, Influence, Dependency, Proximity, dan Representation. [G4-25]

Pemangku kepentingan tersebut adalah nasabah, karyawan, masyarakat, pemegang saham, dan regulator. Komunikasi PermataBank kepada setiap pemangku kepentingan dilakukan dengan berbagai cara, media, dan metode pelibatan. [G4-24]

# PERMATABANK'S COMMITMENT TO STAKEHOLDERS

PermataBank consistently solicits valuable input from all stakeholders in order to boost its service performance, develop product, manage risk and increase PermataBank's reputation. For us, the involvement of all stakeholders in PermataBank's operational activities is a key to our business sustainability.

We have identified five PermataBank stakeholders with whom our operations potentially impact, and who have a potential impact on PermataBank's operations, performance and reputation. We also take into account principles to determine stakeholders from the Global Reporting Initiatives (GRI) G4, namely: Responsibility, Influence, Dependency, Proximity and Representation. [G4-25]

These five stakeholders are customers, employees, public, shareholders and regulators. PermataBank communicates with stakeholders through a range of channels, media and means of involvement. [G4-24]
