# MANAJEMEN KESEHATAN, KESELAMATAN KERJA, DAN LINGKUNGAN (K3L)
Health, Occupational Safety, and Environment (HSE) Management

## Rencana K3L PermataBank Tahun 2017
Rencana PermataBank pada 2017 di bidang K3L yaitu:
- Melanjutkan program perbaikan Infrastruktur gedung untuk menjamin kesehatan dan keselamatan karyawan serta nasabah di Kantor Pusat dan kantor-kantor cabang, seperti perbaikan pada power sistem gedung dan jalur evakuasi darurat cabang sewa.
- Meningkatkan kualitas program kampanye K3L kepada seluruh karyawan dan vendor/rekanan PermataBank melalui kampanye K3L melalui media digital di Tower III Bintaro dan training HSE untuk vendor/rekanan PermataBank.

## PermataBank HSE Plans 2017
PermataBank’s plans for HSE in 2017 include:
- Continuing infrastructure improvement programs to guarantee health and safety for employees and customers at Head Office and branch offices, including improvements to build power systems and evacuation routes at rented branches.
- Improving the quality of HSE campaign programs for all employees and PermataBank vendors/partners through digital media training at Tower III Bintaro and HSE training for PermataBank vendors/partners.
