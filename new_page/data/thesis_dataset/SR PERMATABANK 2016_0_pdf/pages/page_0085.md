## PENCEGAHAN DAN PENGELOLAAN FRAUD

Manajemen PermataBank memiliki komitmen untuk menyelenggarakan program anti-fraud. Semua unit bisnis dan unit pendukung di dalam PermataBank berkomitmen atas pencegahan, pendeteksian, respon dan pemberian efek jera atas fraud.

Strategi Anti Fraud PermataBank tertuang dalam kebijakan Fraud Risk Management Framework (FRMF), yang merangkum aktivitas pencegahan, deteksi, pemberian respon dan efek jera dalam suatu strategi yang menyeluruh terkait pengelolaan risiko fraud demi menjaga integritas bisnis.

Selama tahun 2016, inisiatif yang dilakukan PermataBank untuk mengembangkan kultur, etik dan pencegahan fraud sebagai berikut: [G4-14] [G4-SO4]

- Menerbitkan Fraud Alert, yakni tulisan melalui email/surel yang menginformasikan kepada seluruh karyawan tren/modus kejahatan fraud yang berkembang dengan tujuan membangun kepedulian dan kesadaran terhadap risiko fraud.
- Mengelola program E-learning Fraud, yakni model pelatihan/pembelajaran fraud awareness yang dikemas dalam bentuk animasi komputer disertai soal-soal pertanyaan, yang wajib diikuti oleh seluruh karyawan PermataBank.
- Menyelenggarakan pelatihan pengenalan fraud bagi karyawan baru (induction training).
- Kebijakan blokir internal rekening giro dan tabungan nasabah terindikasi fraud untuk mencegah penggunaan rekening untuk menampung hasil kejahatan.

## FRAUD PREVENTION AND MANAGEMENT

PermataBank Management is committed in implementation of anti-fraud programs. All business units and supporting units within PermataBank are committed to preventing, detecting, responding to and deterring fraud.

PermataBank's anti-fraud strategy is stipulated in the Fraud Risk Management Framework (FRMF) covering prevention, detection, response and deterrence within a comprehensive strategy on fraud risk management in order to maintain business integrity.

In 2016, initiatives carried out by PermataBank to develop a culture, ethics and fraud prevention were as follows: [G4-14] [G4-SO4]

- Published Fraud Alert, which were written messages via e-mail to inform all employees about the trends / growing fraud crime modus with the aim to build awareness.
- Launched Fraud E-Learning program, a model of training / learning of fraud awareness packaged in form of scored computer animation games/ quizzes that were mandatory for all of Bank Permata employees.
- Provided fraud awareness induction training for new joiners.
- Implementation of policy to internally block a customer's current and deposit accounts who is indicated with fraud to prevent the accounts from being used to accumulate illegal funds.
