Program ini bertujuan agar mereka dapat mengimplementasikan materi FITS kepada murid-murid di sekolahnya masing-masing dan juga memberikan pembinaan materi kepada guru-guru dari sekolah lainnya, sehingga program ini dapat menjangkau lebih banyak lagi siswa/i SD di daerah Subang.

A mini tournament of 100 participants from 10 Subang elementary schools, marked the closing of the program, held on September 1st, to commemorate National Sports Day.

PermataHati also carried out special training of elementary-school teachers through Financial Literacy Through Sports - Training of Trainers (FITS ToT), in cooperation with the ASA Foundation for a period of five months, with the participation of 25 Subang elementary-school teachers. This program aimed to implement FITS material among students at their respective schools, as well as developing teachers at other schools, allowing the program to reach a larger number of elementary-school students in the Subang area.
