## PERMATABANK MENANAM 1.400 POHON

Pada tahun 2016, PermataBank berpartisipasi aktif dalam konservasi lingkungan melalui program penanaman 1.400 pohon di Astra Forest, Sentul Eco Edu Tourism Forest, Bogor. Sebagai salah satu program PermataBank yang bertujuan untuk mewujudkan komitmen kami dalam bidang lingkungan, di saat yang bersamaan PermataBank juga menyelenggarakan program edukasi lingkungan bagi anak-anak.

Acara seremoni penanaman pohon dilakukan di Gedung Tower III Bintaro pada tanggal 8 Oktober 2016 yang melibatkan Direksi, manajemen senior, putra-putri karyawan PermataBank, serta perwakilan dari manajemen senior PT Astra International Tbk.

Pada acara tersebut, dilakukan penyerahan simbolik 1.400 pohon dari Presiden Direktur PermataBank kepada Division Head Corporate Communication PT Astra International Tbk. [G4-EN13]

## PERMATABANK MENANAM 1.400 POHON

In 2016, PermataBank actively participated in environmental conservation through a program of planting 1,400 trees in Astra Forest, Sentul Eco Edu Tourism Forest, Bogor. As one of PermataBank's programs to implement our commitment in the environmental field, at the same time PermataBank held an environmental education program for children.

The tree-planting ceremony was held at Tower III Bintaro on 8 October 2016 which included the Board of Directors, senior management, PermataBank employees' children and representatives from the senior management of PT Astra International Tbk.

During the event, 1,400 trees were symbolically handed over by the President Director of PermataBank to the Head of the Corporate Communication Division of PT Astra International Tbk. [G4-EN13]

## PERMATAGREEN MOVEMENT

Dalam sebuah kegiatan keberlanjutan, karyawan menjadi faktor penentu keberhasilan berbagai inisiatif lingkungan sebuah perusahaan. PermataBank telah berusaha mewujudkan efisiensi dan kepedulian lingkungan yang dilakukan secara konsisten di lingkungan PermataBank. PermataBank juga memfasilitasi PermataBankers untuk melakukan kegiatan dalam menciptakan lingkungan yang lebih dan lestari.

PermataGreen Movement merupakan gerakan yang diselenggarakan PermataBank untuk mewujudkan komitmen yang tinggi di bidang lingkungan berupa partisipasi aktif dalam konservasi lingkungan melalui gerakan penanaman pohon dan gerakan ramah lingkungan lainnya.

Kegiatan PermataGreen Movement melalui Gerakan Penanaman Pohon di tahun 2016 dilakukan di Tower III Bintaro yang melibatkan jajaran Direksi, Manajemen Senior dan Putra-Putri Karyawan PermataBank serta dihadiri oleh perwakilan PT Astra International, Tbk. Dalam acara tersebut dilakukan serah terima pohon secara simbolis dari Direktur Utama PermataBank ke PT Astra International

## PERMATAGREEN MOVEMENT

In sustainability activities, employees are a determining factor for the success of a company's environmental initiatives. PermataBank strives to increase efficiency and environmental concern within PermataBank. PermataBank also facilitates PermataBankers to carry out activities to preserve the environment.

The PermataGreen Movement is a movement held by PermataBank to support its firm commitment to actively participate in environmental conservation through tree planting and other environmentally-friendly movements.

The PermataGreen Movement, through the Tree-Planting Movement, in 2016 was held at Tower III Bintaro which included the Board of Directors, Senior Managers and the children of PermataBank employees, and was also attended by representatives of PT Astra International Tbk. During the event, trees were symbolically handed over by PermataBank President Director to PT Astra International.
