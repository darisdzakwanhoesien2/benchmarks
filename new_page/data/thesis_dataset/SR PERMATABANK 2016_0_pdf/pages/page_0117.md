In connection with the large number of buildings managed by PermataBank, our environmental policy is now focused on managing our main buildings with an area covering more than 20,000 square feet or 1,850 m2, which meet the Global Environmental Management System (GEMS Building) criteria. In stages, monitoring is carried out at other branch offices across Indonesia. GEMS Building represents 30% of PermataBank's office portfolio.
