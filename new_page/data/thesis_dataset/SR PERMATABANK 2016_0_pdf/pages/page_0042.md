# MODAL (MODul FinansiAL)

MODAL (Modul Financial) merupakan modul terbaru PermataHati yang diluncurkan bersama dengan Program CERITA Permata, berupa materi edukasi literasi keuangan komprehensif bagi anak-anak di tahapan usia sekolah. MODAL ini disusun sesuai dengan financial literacy cycle needs disetiap tahapan usia sekolah dan kurikulum sekolah serta produk finansial yang sesuai dengan setiap jenjang usia. Modul ini dibagi menjadi beberapa jenjang usia: SD umur 6-8 tahun, 9-10 tahun, 11-12 tahun, untuk jenjang SMP 13-15 tahun dan SMA 16-18 tahun. MODAL ini diharapkan dapat menjadi pegangan bagi siswasiswi yang mendapat pengajaran literasi keuangan dan mengenal lebih jauh tentang produk dan layanan Bank.

# MODAL (MODul FinansiAL)

MODAL (Modul Financial) is the newest PermataHati Module launched alongside with the CERITA Permata Program, and contains comprehensive financial literacy education material for children at each stage of the school cycle. MODAL was formulated in line with the financial literacy cycle needs at each stage of the school cycle and curriculum, as well as financial products aimed at various ages. Each age range requires a different MODAL, in line with its particular needs. MODAL is divided into different age ranges: elementary school 6-8 years, 9-10 years and 11-12 years, middle school 13-15 years and high school 16-18 years. It is hoped that MODAL will serve to allow school students to obtain greater knowledge of financial matters, and especially as regards the Bank.
