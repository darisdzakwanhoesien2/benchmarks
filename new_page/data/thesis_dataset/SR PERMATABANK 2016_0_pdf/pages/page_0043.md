We hope, indeed, that in future, the Bank can help boost education at higher levels – elementary, middle and high schools all the way to higher education establishments, or even to the world of work or business. Once more, I thank PermataBank for its Bankir Cilik program; may similar programs be carried out by other financial institutions.
