## SKEMA GEDUNG RAMAH LINGKUNGAN

Sejak 2009, PermataBank telah menerapkan sistem rating bagi gedung-gedung kantor PermataBank yaitu Building Environmental Assessment Standard (BEAS) yang mengukur dampak operasional dari gedung-gedung GEMS PermataBank, dengan kriteria antara lain pengelolaan emisi GRK, konsumsi listrik, dan konsumsi air. Pada 2016, seluruh kantor cabang PermataBank yang masuk dalam kategori GEMS telah dievaluasi dan dipastikan pemenuhannya terhadap kriteria BEAS.

Selain itu, sesuai dengan peraturan izin lingkungan di Indonesia, PermataBank memastikan dilakukannya laporan pengelolaan dan pemantauan lingkungan pada gedung-gedung yang dimiliki dan dikelola oleh PermataBank. Pengelolaan, pemantauan, dan pelaporan dilakukan sesuai dengan dokumen izin lingkungan yang relevan pada masing-masing gedung, termasuk di dalamnya adalah pemantauan kualitas air dan kualitas udara lingkungan. Hal ini untuk memastikan dampak lingkungan kegiatan PermataBank terhadap lingkungan sesuai dengan persyaratan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku.

PermataBank develops various of technology-based innovations to improve services, as well as to reduce environmental impact, such as PermataMobile, PermataNet and PermataMobile Cash. Besides providing convenience, speed and continual access for customers, electronic banking services are able to reduce the environmental impact of PermataBank's office operations and customer transportation in comparison with conventional transactions.

The additional transaction volume seen by PermataBank in 2016 results in increased use of paper for PermataBank's operations. Increased consumption of photocopy paper at branch offices in Greater Jakarta reached 34.93%, equivalent to 28.87 tons of paper of various sizes.

## BUILDING ENVIRONMENTAL ASSESSMENT STANDARDS

Since 2009, PermataBank has applied a rating system for PermataBank office buildings: the Building Environmental Assessment Standards (BEAS), which assess the operational impact of PermataBank GEMS buildings, with criteria including Greenhouse Gas emissions management, electricity consumption and water consumption. In 2016, all PermataBank branch offices in the GEMS category were evaluated and to ensure its compliance with BEAS criteria.

Besides this, in line with Indonesia environmental regulations, PermataBank ensures that environmental monitoring and management reports are conducted at offices owned by PermataBank. Management, monitoring and reporting are conducted in line with relevant environmental licensing documents at each building, including monitoring of air and water quality. This is to ensure that the environmental impact of PermataBank's activities is in line with prevailing laws and regulations.
