PermataHati diluncurkan sejak Oktober 2010 sebagai identitas baru untuk nama CSR PermataBank yang mewakili semangat PermataBankers dalam berkontribusi dan memberikan dampak positif serta berkesinambungan bagi pendidikan di Indonesia. Fokus utama PermataHati adalah edukasi untuk literasi keuangan yang sejalan dengan program Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) yaitu "Edukasi Literasi Keuangan". Program ini juga mendukung gerakan yang dilaksanakan Bank Indonesia dalam program "Ayo Ke Bank" dalam meningkatkan akses edukasi keuangan bagi masyarakat terutama anak-anak usia dini. PermataHati memiliki 3 (tiga) Pilar yaitu Human Development (Pengembangan Manusia), Facility Enhancement (Pengembangan Sarana dan Prasarana Penunjang Pendidikan) dan Community Empowerment (Pemberdayaan Masyarakat). Dalam pelaksanaan program PermataHati, karyawan terlibat sebagai relawan EVO.

PermataHati was launched in October 2010 as a new identity for PermataBank CSR, representing PermataBankers' spirit to contribute and provide a positive, sustainable influence on education in Indonesia. PermataHati's main focus was on education for financial literacy, in line with the OJK program "Financial Literacy Education". The program also supports Bank Indonesia's efforts with the program "Ayo Ke Bank" aiming to improve access to financial education, especially for young children. PermataHati initiative stands on three pillars, namely Human Development, Facility Enhancement and Community Empowerment. In the PermataHati program, employees are involved as EVO volunteers.

As part of the management's commitment to support sustainability PermataHati programs, all employees involved as EVOs not only receive an additional one-day leave on top of their annual leave, but also get Poin Senyum in the amount according to the activity being carried out.
