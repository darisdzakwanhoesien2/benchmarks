# F. PEMBINAAN HUBUNGAN INDUSTRIAL

Seluruh karyawan PermataBank dilindungi hak dan kewajibannya melalui Perjanjian Kerja Bersama (PKB) antara Bank dan Serikat Pekerja sebagai wakil karyawan. PKB merupakan pedoman hak dan kewajiban karyawan secara keseluruhan, mengatur dan menyelesaikan permasalahan industrial serta mengembangkan dan memupuk hubungan kerja sama yang lebih erat antara Bank dan karyawan, dalam rangka memajukan dan mendukung jalannya usaha PermataBank serta mensejahterakan karyawan. [G4-10]

Pertemuan dengan Serikat Pekerja dilakukan secara rutin, dalam rangka menampung aspirasi, usulan, saran, dan kritik, serta terkait dengan continous improvement dalam proses pengelolaan karyawan. Setiap 2 (dua) tahun sekali wakil manajemen Bank dan Serikat Pekerja Bank Permata bersama-sama mengkaji ulang isi PKB.

# F. FOSTERING INDUSTRIAL RELATIONS

The rights and obligations of all PermataBankers are protected through the Collective Labor Agreement (CLA) between the Bank and Labor Union as the employees' representative. CLA serves as guidelines for the employees' overall rights and obligations, regulate and settle the industrial issues, as well as develop and foster closer cooperative relations between PermataBank and employees, in order to improve and promote PermataBank business progress as well as employees' welfare. [G4-10]

PermataBankers and Labor Union representatives hold routine meetings and communication forums. This is done to accommodate Permatabankers aspirations, proposals, recommendations and critics, that are related with continuous improvement in PermataBank's HR management processes. Every two years the Bank management and union review CLA contents.
