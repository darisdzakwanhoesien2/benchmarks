# PROGRAM PERBAIKAN FASILITAS &amp; INFRASTRUKTUR

Selama tahun 2016, PermataBank melaksanakan serangkaian program perbaikan fasilitas dan infrastruktur yang bertujuan untuk meningkatkan kualitas kesehatan, keselamatan dan kenyamanan karyawan dan nasabah PermataBank. Program perbaikan fasilitas dan infrastruktur yang dilakukan PermataBank sepanjang tahun 2016 adalah sebagai berikut:

1. Pembuatan tangga darurat di 3 cabang (Cabang Yos Sudarjo Cirebon, Merdeka Bandung, Zainul Arifin Medan).
2. Pemasangan fire alarm system di 2 cabang (Cabang Merdeka Bandung &amp; Zainul Arifin Medan).
3. Peremajaan/refreshment semua toilet dan pantry Tower III Bintaro.
4. Penggantian 11 unit AHU (Air Handling Unit) di Tower III Bintaro.
5. Penggantian genset &amp; trafo Gedung Tower III Bintaro.
6. Perbaikan dinding tampak muka bangunan (facade) Gedung Tower III Bintaro.

# KOMPETENSI K3 PERMATABANKERS

PermataBank secara konsisten mengadakan pelatihan internal maupun eksternal di bidang K3 sepanjang tahun 2016. Pelatihan ini bertujuan untuk meningkatkan kompetensi dan kesadaran karyawan dalam mencegah dan mengelola risiko-risiko K3 di tempat kerjanya. Terdapat 891 PermataBankers yang diikutsertakan dalam pelatihan-pelatihan K3.

# FACILITIES AND INFRASTRUCTURE IMPROVEMENT PROGRAMS

In 2016, PermataBank has carried out a range of programs to improve facilities and infrastructure, with the aim of improving the quality of health, safety and comfort for PermataBank's employees and customers. Facilities and infrastructure improvement programs carried out by PermataBank in 2016 were as follows:

1. Construction of emergency staircases at 3 branch offices (Yos Sudarjo Cirebon, Merdeka Bandung, Zainul Arifin Medan).
2. Installation of fire alarm systems at 2 branches (Merdeka Bandung &amp; Zainul Arifin Medan).
3. Refurbishment of all toilets and pantries at Tower III Bintaro.
4. Replacement of 11 AHU (Air Handling Units) at Tower III Bintaro.
5. Replacement of generators and electric circuit at Tower III Bintaro.
6. Improvement of facades at Tower III Bintaro.

# PERMATABANKERS HSE COMPETENCY

PermataBank consistently provided internal and external training on HSE throughout 2016. This training aimed to raise employees' competency and awareness in order to prevent and manage HSE risk in the workplace. There were 891 PermataBankers who took part in HSE training.
