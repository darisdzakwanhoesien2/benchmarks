## SADARI KESEHATAN MELALUI PROGRAM PEKAN SEHAT PERMATA

Menyambut ulang tahun Permata yang ke-14, PermataBank mengadakan Pekan Sehat Permata di kantor pusat Jakarta dan juga cabang utama di luar kota. Pekan Sehat Permata merupakan program internal PermataBank yang bertujuan untuk meningkatkan kesehatan PermataBankers melalui kegiatan-kegiatan yang diadakan selama 26 September – 8 Oktober 2016.

14 rangkaian acara Pekan Sehat Permata terdiri dari: Health Check, Health Fair, Health Talk, Health Quiz, CPR Training, Blood Donation, Mini Gym, No Tobacco Day, Bike To Work, Photo Contest, Brain Buster, Social Action, Green Run &amp; Family Fun Walk, dan Health Campaign. Dalam pelaksanaannya, PermataBank bekerja sama dengan Koperasi Karyawan PermataBank, Garda Medika, PermataClub, Gold's Gym, dan juga Serikat Pekerja Bank Permata.

Selain itu, bersama dengan PermataClub melaksanakan donor darah di WTC dan Bintaro, serta 11 kota lainnya. Seluruh PermataBankers juga dapat menambah pengetahuan sekaligus mendapatkan hadiah di program Health Quiz dan Photo Contest. Di kegiatan Social Action, dilaksanakan pemeriksaan kesehatan gratis untuk adik-adik di Yayasan Sahabat Yatim Bintaro dan penyerahan donasi dalam bentuk barang dan perlengkapan penunjang kesehatan.

## BOOSTING HEALTH AWARENESS THROUGH THE PERMATA HEALTHY WEEK

In commemoration of Permata's 14th anniversary, PermataBank held Permata Healthy Week at the Jakarta head office and main provincial offices. Permata Healthy Week is an internal PermataBank program aiming to increase PermataBankers health through activities held between 26 September -8 October 2016.

Permata Health Week consists of 14 events: Health Check, Health Fair, Health Talk, Health Quiz, CPR Training, Blood Donation, Mini Gym, No Tobacco Day, Bike To Work, Photo Contest, Brain Buster, Social Action, Green Run &amp; Family Fun Walk and Health Campaign. In its implementation, PermataBank collaborated with PermataBank Employees Cooperative, Garda Medika, PermataClub, Gold's Gym and the Permata Bank Labour Union.

We also held blood Donation in cooperation with PermataClub at WTC and Bintaro, as well as in 11 other cities. All PermataBankers were also able to increase their knowledge and win prizes through Health Quiz and Photo Contest. In the Social Action activity, there were free health checks for children at the Bintaro Sahabat Yatim Foundation and donations in the form of goods and health equipment.

## NO TOBACCO DAY

"Rokok ini dapat membunuhmu" himbauan akan bahaya rokok dalam setiap kemasan. Sadar akan hal itu, perusahaan menggagas program "No Tobacco Day". Kegiatan ini bertujuan mengkampanyekan gaya hidup sehat dengan menciptakan lingkungan bebas asap rokok. Program tersebut dilaksanakan kantor pusat dan cabang PermataBank seluruh Indonesia. Demi mensukseskan program, PermataBank melakukan diantaranya yaitu mengirimkan email mengenai "Himbauan untuk Tidak Merokok di Lingkungan Kantor PermataBank di Seluruh Kantor Pusat &amp; Cabang PermataBank", pemasangan spanduk himbauan "Hari Bebas Merokok", pemberian gimmick untuk karyawan yang mendonasikan uang belanja rokok.

## NO TOBACCO DAY

"This cigarette could kill you" is socialization on the dangers of smoking, in conjunction with the "No Tobacco Day" held by PermataBank. This activity was aimed to campaign healthy lifestyles to create a smoke-free environment. This program was held at head office and PermataBank branches across Indonesia. In order to make sure this socialization is succeeded, PermataBank has carried out few activities including sending emails on Socialization On Not To Smoke In PermataBank Office t at all Head Offices and PermataBank Branches, "Smoke-Free Day" posters, gimmicks for employees which had donated their cigarette money.
