1. UMUM (lanjutan)

c. Penawaran Umum Saham Perdana

Pada tanggal 21 Desember 2020, Perusahaan memperoleh Pernyataan Efektif dari Otoritas Jasa Keuangan ('OJK') dengan Surat No. S-294/D.04/2020 untuk melakukan penawaran umum saham perdana 156.558.200 lembar saham dengan nilai nominal Rp 100 per lembar saham, dengan harga penawaran Rp 530 per lembar saham, beserta konversi utang sebanyak 283.018.800 lembar saham yang di konversi pada harga penawaran Rp 530 per lembar saham. Pada tanggal 21 Desember 2020, seluruh saham tersebut telah dicatatkan di Bursa Efek Indonesia.

Penawaran Umum Saham Perdana tersebut disetujui oleh Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Luar Biasa dan dituangkan dalam Akta Notaris No. 12 oleh Rini Yulianti, S.H., tanggal 15 Juli 2020.

Berdasarkan RUPS Perusahaan diaktakan Notaris Rini Yulianti, S.H., No. 3 tanggal 2 Februari 2024, Perusahaan memperoleh peningkatan modal disetor melalui Penambahan Modal Tanpa Hak Memesan Efek Terlebih Dahulu ('PMTHMETD') sebanyak 323.035.700 lembar saham yang dilakukan oleh PT Investasi Sukses Bersama ('ISB'). Meningkatkan modal ditempatkan dan disetor setelah pelaksanaan PMTHMETD sehingga modal yang ditempatkan dan disetor menjadi sebesar 2.359.355.118 lembar saham. Akta tersebut telah disetujui oleh Menteri Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui Surat Keputusan No. AHU-AH.01.03-0032694 tanggal 5 Februari 2024.

Berdasarkan RUPSLB Perusahaan diaktakan Notaris Dr. Sugih Haryati, S.H., M.Kn., No. 99 tanggal 22 Juli 2025, para pemegang saham menyetujui pengeluaran saham dalam simpanan (portepel) Perseroan sehubungan dengan pelaksanaan Penambahan Modal dengan Hak Memesan Efek Terlebih Dahulu I ('PMHMETD I'). RUPSLB menyetujui penerbitan sebanyak-banyaknya 4.718.710.236 lembar saham biasa atas nama, dengan nilai nominal Rp100 per lembar saham. Modal disetor Perseroan telah bertambah menjadi sebanyak 5.308.549.015 lembar saham, dengan nilai nominal seluruhnya Rp 530.854.901.500. Akta tersebut telah disetujui oleh Menteri Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui Surat Keputusan No. AHU-0048100.AH.01.02 Tahun 2025 tanggal 22 Juli 2025.

1. GENERAL (continued)

c. Initial Public Offering

On December 21, 2020, the Company received an Effective Statement from Otoritas Jasa Keuangan ('OJK') by Letter No. S-294/D.04/2020 to conduct an initial public offering of 156,558,200 shares with a nominal value of Rp 100 per share, with an offering price of Rp 530 per share, along with a debt conversion of 283,018,800 shares which were converted at an offering price of Rp 530 per share. On December 21, 2020, all these shares were listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange.

The IPO was approved by the Extraordinary Shareholders' General Meeting held and notarized by Notarial Deed No. 12 of Rini Yulianti, S.H., dated July 15, 2020.

Based on the Company's General Meeting of Shareholders notarized by Notary Rini Yulianti, S.H., No. 3 dated February 2, 2024, the Company obtained increase paid-in capital through Capital Increase Without Pre-emptive Rights ('PMTHMETD') of 323,035,700 shares which was made by PT Investasi Sukses Bersama ('ISB'). Increase the issued and paid-up capital after PMTHMETD so that the issued and paid-up capital becomes 2,359,355,118 shares. The deed has been approved by the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decision Letter No. AHU-AH.01.03-0032694 dated February 5, 2024.

Based on the resolutions of the Company's RUPSLB, as notarized by Notary Dr. Sugih Haryati, S.H., M.Kn., under Deed No. 99 dated July 22, 2025, the shareholders approved the issuance of shares from the Company's portfolio in connection with the implementation of the Rights Issue I ('PMHMETD I'). The RUPSLB approved the issuance of up to 4,718,710,236 ordinary shares with a par value of Rp 100 per share. Following the implementation of the Rights Issue I, the Company's issued and paid-up capital increased to 5,308,549,015 shares, with a total par value of Rp 530,854,901,500. The deed has been approved by the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decision Letter No. AHU-0048100.AH.01.02 Year 2025 dated July 22, 2025.
