The rapid growth in subscriber numbers has also been driven by STARLITE's innovative community-based distribution model. Local Internet Service Providers (ISPs) and community groups are recruited to act as sales agents, frontline customer support representatives, and subscription fee collection partners. This decentralized approach accelerates market penetration while strengthening local engagement and service responsiveness. These local partners are entitled to receive 20% of the collected subscription fees as commission.
