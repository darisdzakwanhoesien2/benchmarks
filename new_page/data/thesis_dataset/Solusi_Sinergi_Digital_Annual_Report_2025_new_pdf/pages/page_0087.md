Bapak Fuad Rama Riyadi, 24 tahun, warga negara Indonesia, diangkat sebagai anggota Komite Audit berdasarkan Surat Keputusan Dewan Komisaris PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk tentang Perubahan dan Penunjukan Anggota Komite Audit Perseroan No. 005/SK-DEKOM/SSD/XII/2025 tanggal 22 Desember 2025.

Mr. Fuad Rama Riyadi, 24 years old, an Indonesian citizen, was appointed as a member of the Audit Committee based on the Decree of the Board of Commissioners of PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk concerning the Amendment and Appointment of the Company's Audit Committee Members No. 005/SK-DEKOM/SSD/XII/2025 dated December 22, 2025.

Bapak Fuad Rama Riyadi adalah seorang profesional di bidang akuntansi dan perpajakan dengan pengalaman dalam fungsi akuntansi dan pajak pada perusahaan nasional serta kantor konsultan pajak.

Mr. Fuad Rama Riyadi is an accounting and taxation professional with experience in accounting and tax functions at national companies as well as a tax consulting firm.

Beliau berpengalaman dalam hal tanggung jawab atas pelaporan dan pembayaran pajak melalui system Coretax, termasuk Pajak Penghasilan Pasal 21 karyawan, Pasal 23, dan Pasal 4 ayat (2); penyusunan pencatatan dan jurnal akuntansi bulanan; pengelolaan Pajak Pertambahan Nilai (PPN); pengoperasian system Accurate Online; pengarsipan dokumen perpajakan dan akuntansi; serta mengikuti pembaruan ketentuan perpajakan yang berlaku.

He has extensive experience in overseeing and being responsible for tax reporting and payment through the Coretax system, including Employee Income Tax Article 21, Income Tax Article 23, and Article 4 paragraph (2); preparing monthly accounting records and journal entries; managing Value Added Tax (VAT); operating the Accurate Online system; filing tax and accounting documents; and keeping abreast of updates to prevailing tax regulations.

Sebelumnya, beliau bekerja sebagai Accounting &amp; Tax di PT Nitto Alam Indonesia, Tangerang, selama periode Mei 2024 hingga Juli 2025. Tanggung jawab beliau meliputi penyusunan pencatatan dan jurnal akuntansi bulanan, pengelolaan Pajak Penghasilan Pasal 21, penerbitan e-Faktur Pajak Pertambahan Nilai (PPN), rekapitulasi dokumen kepabeanan (BC 2.3, 2.5, dan 3.0), serta rekapitulasi invoice pembelian.

Previously, he worked as Accounting &amp; Tax at PT Nitto Alam Indonesia, Tangerang, from May 2024 to July 2025. His responsibilities included monthly accounting and journal preparation, management of Income Tax Article 21, issuance of VAT e-Faktur, recapitulation of customs documents (BC 2.3, 2.5, and 3.0), and recapitulation of purchase invoices.

Bapak Fuad Rama Riyadi menyelesaikan program kerjanya di bidang Accounting &amp; Tax di PT BATS Internasional Group, Jakarta, dari bulan Agustus 2022 hingga April 2024, dengan turut serta dalam penyusunan pencatatan dan jurnal akuntansi bulanan, pelaksanaan opname aset, pengelolaan Pajak Penghasilan Pasal 21, Pasal 23, dan Pasal 4 ayat (2), serta pengelolaan Pajak Pertambahan Nilai (PPN).

Mr. Fuad Rama Riyadi completed his Accounting &amp; Tax at PT BATS Internasional Group, Jakarta, from August 2022 to April 2024, where he was involved in monthly accounting and journal preparation, asset stocktaking, management of Income Tax Articles 21, 23, and Article 4 paragraph (2), as well as Value Added Tax (VAT).

Pada tahap awal kariernya, beliau juga pernah mengikuti program kerja di bidang Finance pada PT Nitto Seiko Trading Indonesia, Tangerang, untuk periode bulan Agustus hingga November 2021.

Earlier in his career, he also undertook a Finance at PT Nitto Seiko Trading Indonesia, Tangerang, from August to November 2021.

Bapak Fuad Rama Riyadi meraih gelar Sarjana Akuntansi dari Universitas Islam Negeri Syarif Hidayatullah Jakarta di tahun 2024.

Mr. Fuad Rama Riyadi earned a Bachelor's degree in Accounting from Universitas Islam Negeri Syarif Hidayatullah Jakarta (2024).
