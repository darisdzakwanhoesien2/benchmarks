# Bidang Usaha Periklanan dan Media Digital

Digital Advertising and Media Business

Gambaran Umum Usaha Periklanan dan Media Digital Digital Advertising and Media Business Overview

![img-107.jpeg](img-107.jpeg)

Bidang Usaha Periklanan dan Media Digital Dikelola Langsung oleh Perseroan, serta Melalui Anak Usaha, PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem.

Melalui kemitraan dengan agensi-agensi periklanan pihak ketiga, Perseroan menawarkan layanan konsultansi periklanan dan penempatan media kepada para pelanggan korporasi di sektor FMCG, perbankan dan keuangan, e-commerce, fashion, dan sektor pemerintah.

The Company's Digital Advertising and Media Business Is Run Directly by The Company and Through Its Subsidiary, PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem.

In partnership with leading third-party advertising agencies, the Company provides advertising consultancy and media placement services to corporate clients across the FMCG, banking and finance, e-commerce, fashion, and government sectors.

Layanan juga meliputi penempatan media digital di berbagai perangkat. Perseroan mengelola portofolio aset media dalam ruang (indoor) dan luar ruang (outdoor), yang sebagian besar berlokasi di wilayah Jabodetabek.

Melalui kemitraannya dengan PT Kereta Api Indonesia (Persero) (PT KAI), WEAVE mengelola salah satu platform media dan periklanan terintegrasi paling canggih di sektor perkeretaapian. Platform tersebut meliputi aset layar digital yang terpasang di dalam rangkaian kereta dan stasiun, serta jaringan Wi-Fi gratis di area stasiun, yang menghasilkan pendapatan dari penempatan iklan bagi PT KAI maupun WEAVE.

The services offered also include digital media placement across various devices. In addition, it manages a portfolio of indoor and outdoor media assets, primarily located in the Greater Jakarta area.

Through its partnership with PT Kereta Api Indonesia (Persero) (PT KAI), WEAVE manages one of the most advanced integrated media and advertising platforms within the railway environment. The platform includes digital display assets installed inside trains and stations, as well as free Wi-Fi networks across station premises, generating revenues from advertising placements for both PT KAI and WEAVE.
