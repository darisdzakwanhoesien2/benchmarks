## Independensi

Dalam menjalankan tugas dan kewajibannya, Komite Audit memegang teguh prinsip-prinsip independensi dan kehati-hatian. Perseroan memastikan bahwa seluruh anggota Komite Audit yang sedang menjabat terbebas dari segala bentuk intervensi dan tidak memiliki hubungan keuangan, manajerial, kepemilikan, ataupun hubungan keluarga dengan anggota Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, atau Pemegang Saham, ataupun hubungan lain dengan Perseroan yang dapat mempengaruhi independensi mereka.

Komite Audit juga bebas dari segala bentuk konflik kepenting dan segala bentuk pengaruh atau tekanan dari pihak-pihak lainnya yang tidak sesuai dengan perundang-undangan dan peraturan yang berlaku, standar etika, praktik usaha yang sehat, atau kebijakan Perseroan selama pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya.

## Periode dan Masa Jabatan Anggota Komite Audit

Anggota Komite Audit diangkat untuk masa kerja 5 (lima) tahun dengan memperhatikan masa jabatan Dewan Komisaris sebagaimana diatur dalam Anggaran Dasar Perseroan. Periode tugas anggota Komite Audit yang berasal dari Dewan Komisaris tidak boleh lebih lama daripada masa jabatan Komisaris dan hanya bisa diangkat kembali hanya untuk 1 (satu) periode berikutnya.

## Independence

In carrying out its duties and responsibilities, the Audit Committee upholds the principles of independence and prudence. The Company ensures that all current Audit Committee members are free from any interference and do not have financial, managerial, ownership, or family relationships with members of the BoC, BoD, or Shareholders, nor any other relationship with the Company that could compromise their independence.

The Audit Committee is also free from conflicts of interest and from any influence or pressure from parties whose actions may conflict with applicable laws and regulations, ethical standards, sound business practices, or Company policies during the execution of its duties and responsibilities.

## Period and Terms of Office of Audit Committee Members

Members of the Audit Committee are appointed for a service period of 5 (five) years with due observance of the term of office of the BoC as stipulated in the Articles of Association of the Company. The term of office of an Audit Committee member doubling as a BoC member may not be longer than the term of office of the Commissioners, and can only be reappointed for the next 1 (one) period.

## Composition

There were a few changes in the composition of the Audit Committee in 2025. During the year, two members, namely Cutriwati and Dwi Afriilanto, resigned from their positions as members of the Audit Committee. On December 22, 2025, the Board of Commissioners appointed Anasthasia Rizky Rochiandani and Fuad Rama Riyadi as new members of the Audit Committee, based on Board of Commissioners Decree No. 005/SK-DEKOM/SSD/XII/2025 dated December 22, 2025, concerning Changes and Appointment of Audit Committee Members.
