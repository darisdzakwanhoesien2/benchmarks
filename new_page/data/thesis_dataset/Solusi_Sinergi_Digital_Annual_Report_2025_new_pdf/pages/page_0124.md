# PENGGUNAAN DANA HASIL PENAWARAN UMUM PERDANA SAHAM

Dana hasil penawaran umum tahun 2020 dengan hasil neto sebesar Rp79.443.458.072 telah digunakan seluruhnya untuk perluasan usaha dan investasi pada barang modal, termasuk namun tidak terbatas pada biaya sewa ruang iklan, perlengkapan penunjang usaha periklanan, serta biaya overhead. Oleh karena itu, pada tahun 2024 tidak terdapat informasi yang perlu diungkapkan terkait realisasi penggunaan dana hasil penawaran umum Perseroan.

# INVESTASI, EKSPANSI, DIVESTASI, AKUISISI, RESTRUKTURISASI UTANG/MODAL YANG MATERIAL

Berdasarkan Akta Pernyataan Keputusan/Resolusi Sirkuler Pemegang Saham Perseroan Terbatas No. 2 tanggal 5 Juni 2025 dari Notaris Delny Teoberto, S.H., M.Kn., terdapat perubahan maksud dan tujuan kegiatan PT Jaringan Infra Andalan ("JIA"). Perubahan tersebut telah disetujui oleh Menteri Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui Surat Keputusan No. AHU-0036822.AH.01.02 tanggal 5 Juni 2025.

Berdasarkan Akta Pernyataan Keputusan Pemegang Saham Perseroan Terbatas No. 3 tanggal 5 Agustus 2025 dari Notaris Delny Teoberto, S.H., M.Kn., yang telah memperoleh pengesahan dari Kementerian Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui Surat Keputusan No. AHU-AH.01.09-0320881 tanggal 5 Agustus 2025, pemegang saham PT Sentosa Bersama Global menjual seluruh kepemilikan sahamnya pada PT Investasi Jaringan Nusantara ("IJN") kepada JIA sebanyak 599.999 lembar saham dengan harga jual sebesar Rp599.999.000.

Berdasarkan Akta Notaris Delny Teoberto, S.H., M.Kn., No. 1 tanggal 13 Maret 2025, PT Solusi Pariwisata Digital melakukan perubahan nama menjadi PT Solusi Jaringan Nusantara. Perubahan tersebut telah disetujui oleh Menteri Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui Surat Keputusan No. AHU-00185513.AH.01.02 tanggal 14 Maret 2025.

Pada tanggal 29 September 2025, Perusahaan mendirikan PT Solusi Sinergi Borneo ("SSB") dengan kepemilikan sebanyak 175 lembar saham atau setara dengan 70% kepemilikan. Pendirikan SSB tersebut dituangkan dalam Akta Notaris No. 14 tanggal 29 September 2025 dari Notaris Delny Teoberto, S.H., M.Kn., dan telah memperoleh persetujuan dari Kementerian

Based on the Notarial Deed of Neilly Iralita Iswari, S.H., M.Si., M.Kn., No. 16 dated May 9, 2025, concerning the minutes of correction of Notarial Deed No. 5 dated June 6, 2024, the shareholders approved a correction to the dividend amount, from Rp2,501,000,000 to Rp2,500,916,425.

Based on the Notarial Deed of Rini Yulianti, S.H., No. 15 dated June 12, 2025, regarding the Minutes of the General Meeting of Shareholders, the shareholders approved the distribution of cash dividends for the profit of the 2024 fiscal year amounting to Rp4,718,710,236.

# USE OF IPO PROCEEDS

The proceeds from the Company's 2020 public offering, with net proceeds of Rp79,443,458,072 have been fully utilized for business expansion and investment in capital goods, including but not limited to rental payments for advertising space, advertising support equipment, and overhead costs. Accordingly, as of 2024, there is no remaining information to disclose regarding the realization of the use of proceeds from the Company's public offering.

# MATERIAL INVESTMENT, EXPANSION, DEBT/CAPITAL RESTRUCTURING

Based on the Deed of Decision Statement (Circular Resolution) of a Limited Liability Company No. 2 dated June 5, 2025, notarized by Delny Teoberto, S.H., M.Kn., there was a change in the purposes and objectives of PT Jaringan Infra Andalan ("JIA"). The change has been approved by the Minister of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decision Letter No. AHU-0036822.AH.01.02 dated June 5, 2025.

Based on the Deed of Shareholders' Resolution of a Limited Liability Company No. 3 dated August 5, 2025, notarized by Delny Teoberto, S.H., M.Kn., and approved by the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decree No. AHU-AH.01.09-0320881 dated August 5, 2025, the shareholders of PT Sentosa Bersama Global sold all of their share ownership in PT Investasi Jaringan Nusantara ("IJN") to JIA, totaling 599,999 shares, at a selling price of Rp599,999,000.

Based on Notarial Deed No. 1 dated March 13, 2025, of Delny Teoberto, S.H., M.Kn., PT Solusi Pariwisata Digital changed its name to PT Solusi Jaringan Nusantara. This amendment was approved by the Minister of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decision Letter No. AHU-00185513.AH.01.02 dated March 14, 2025.

On September 29, 2025, the Company established PT Solusi Sinergi Borneo ("SSB"), with ownership of 175 shares, representing 70% ownership. The establishment of SSB was documented in Notarial Deed No. 14 dated September 29, 2025, notarized by Delny Teoberto, S.H., M.Kn., and approved by the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia
