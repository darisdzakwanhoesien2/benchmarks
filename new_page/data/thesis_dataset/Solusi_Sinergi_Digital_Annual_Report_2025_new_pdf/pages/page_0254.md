## d. Entitas Anak (lanjutan)

### Akuisisi Entitas Anak (lanjutan)

#### PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem ("IJE") (lanjutan)

Berdasarkan Akta Pernyataan Keputusan / Resolusi Sirkuler Pemegang Saham Perseroan Terbatas No. 94 tanggal 22 Agustus 2022 dari Notaris Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., terjadi penjualan sebagian saham IJE milik PT Lintas Maju Maxima kepada JIA sebanyak 15.030 lembar saham, sehingga kepemilikan saham JIA di IJE menjadi 165.356 lembar saham atau ekuivalen dengan 99.00% kepemilikan.

Berdasarkan Akta Pernyataan Keputusan / Resolusi Sirkuler Pemegang Saham Perseroan Terbatas No. 22 tanggal 10 April 2023 dari Notaris Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., IJE meningkatkan modal ditempatkan / modal disetor yang semula Rp 167.026.000.000 menjadi Rp 229.026.000.000, sehingga kepemilikan saham JIA di IJE menjadi 227.356 lembar saham atau ekuivalen dengan 99,27% kepemilikan.

Berdasarkan Akta Pernyataan Keputusan / Resolusi Sirkuler Pemegang Saham Perseroan Terbatas No. 69 tanggal 20 Maret 2024 dari Notaris Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., IJE meningkatkan modal ditempatkan / modal disetor yang semula Rp 229.026.000.000 menjadi Rp 262.026.000.000, sehingga kepemilikan saham JIA di IJE menjadi 260.356 lembar saham atau ekuivalen dengan 99,27% kepemilikan.

Berdasarkan Akta Pernyataan Keputusan / Resolusi Sirkuler Pemegang Saham Perseroan Terbatas No. 71 tanggal 20 Maret 2024 dari Notaris Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., IJE meningkatkan modal ditempatkan / modal disetor yang semula Rp 262.026.000.000 menjadi Rp 562.026.000.000, sehingga kepemilikan saham JIA di IJE menjadi 560.356 lembar saham atau ekuivalen dengan 99,70% kepemilikan.

Berdasarkan Akta Notaris Rini Yulianti, S.H., No. 12 tanggal 17 Juli 2025, IJE telah melakukan Perubahan Anggaran Dasar yang meliputi peningkatan modal dasar dari semula sebesar Rp 800.000.000.000 menjadi sebesar Rp 5.598.219.764.000, serta peningkatan modal ditempatkan dan disetor dari semula sebesar Rp 713.020.000.000 menjadi sebesar Rp 1.399.554.941.000.

## d. Subsidiaries (continued)

### Acquisition of Subsidiaries (continued)

#### PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem ("IJE") (continued)

Based on the Deed of Decision Statement / Circular Resolution of Limited Company Shareholders No. 94 dated August 22, 2022 from Notary Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., there was a sale of some of the IJE shares owned by PT Lintas Maju Maxima to JIA amounted to 15,030 shares, bringing JIA share ownership in IJE to 165,356 shares or equivalent to 99.00% ownership.

Based on the Deed of Decision Statement / Circular Resolution of Limited Company Shareholders No. 22 dated April 10, 2023 from Notary Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., IJE increased the issued capital / paid-up capital from Rp 167,026,000,000 to Rp 229,026,000,000, bringing JIA share ownership in IJE to 227,356 shares or equivalent to 99.27% ownership.

Based on the Deed of Decision Statement / Circular Resolution of Limited Company Shareholders No. 69 dated March 20, 2024 from Notary Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., IJE increased the issued capital / paid-up capital from Rp 229,026,000,000 to Rp 262,026,000,000, bringing JIA's share JIA's in IJE to 260,356 shares or equivalent to 99.27% ownership.

Based on the Deed of Decision Statement / Circular Resolution of Limited Company Shareholders No. 71 dated March 20, 2024 from Notary Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., IJE increased the issued capital / paid-up capital from Rp 262,026,000,000 to Rp 562,026,000,000, bringing JIA share ownership in IJE to 560,356 shares or equivalent to 99.70% ownership.

Based on Notarial Deed of Rini Yulianti, S.H., No. 12 dated July 17, 2025, IJE amended its Articles of Association, which included an increase in the authorized capital from Rp 800,000,000,000 to Rp 5.598,219,764,000, as well as an increase in the issued and fully paid capital from Rp 713,020,000,000 to Rp 1,399,554,941,000.
