### Perjanjian antara Perusahaan dengan PT Lintas Daya Andalan ("LDA") (lanjutan)

Adapun ketentuan pembayaran yang dilakukan perusahaan kepada LDA sebagai berikut: (lanjutan)

- Sisa deposit yang belum ter-amortisasi bersifat dapat dikembalikan secara penuh (refundable) apabila target kerjasama tidak tercapai. LDA menjamin proses pengembalian dana dilakukan selambat-lambatnya 1 (satu) hari kerja setelah instruksi pengembalian diterbitkan oleh Perusahaan.
- Apabila nilai deposit telah habis terpakai, Perusahaan wajib melunasi tagihan berikutnya dalam jangka waktu 14 hari sejak dokumen penagihan dinyatakan lengkap. Perlu dicatat bahwa jumlah kuantitas dan nilai perjanjian bersifat tidak mengikat bagi Perusahaan jika pemasok tidak dapat memenuhi timeline pengiriman yang telah disepakati.

### Perjanjian Jual Beli Jasa Barang dan Jasa Layanan dengan PT ECS Indo Jaya

Pada tanggal 26 Maret 2025, Perusahaan menandatangani Perjanjian Jual Beli Barang dan Jasa Layanan No. 043/LGL/ECS-SSD/III/2025 dengan PT ECS Indo Jaya. Lingkup utama perjanjian ini mencakup pengadaan produk Teknologi Informasi dan Komunikasi (TIK), infrastruktur, serta penyediaan layanan terkait yang meliputi desain, konfigurasi, integrasi, dan instalasi.

Berdasarkan ketentuan perjanjian, skema pembayaran dibedakan menjadi dua kategori utama:

- Produk dan Layanan Huawei
Pembayaran dilakukan dalam jangka waktu 2 tahun (720 hari kalender) sejak serah terima barang (POD). Cicilan dimulai pada tahun kedua (hari ke-361) sebanyak 12 kali secara bulanan.
- Layanan Pihak Ketiga
Uang muka 30% setelah Surat Perintah Kerja (SPK) terbit, dan pelunasan 70% setelah Berita Acara Serah Terima (BAST) ditandatangani.

### Perjanjian Jual Beli Barang dan Jasa Layanan Proyek FWA

Pada bulan 24 Juli 2025, Perusahaan menandatangani Perjanjian Jual Beli Barang dan Jasa Layanan Proyek FWA No. 110/PKS/LGL/ECS-SSD/VI/2025 dengan PT ECS Indo Jaya. Lingkup utama perjanjian ini mencakup pengadaan produk Teknologi Informasi dan Komunikasi (TIK), infrastruktur TIK, serta layanan terkait yang meliputi perencanaan, desain, konfigurasi, integrasi, instalasi, dan pemeliharaan yang dilakukan oleh Huawei selaku produsen utama.

The Company (continued)

### Agreement between the Company and PT Lintas Daya Andalan ("LDA") (continue)

The payment terms between the Company and LDA are as follows: (continued)

- Any remaining deposit that has not yet been amortized shall be fully refundable if the cooperation target is not achieved. LDA guarantees that the refund process will be completed no later than 1 (one) business day after the refund instruction is issued by the Company.
- If the deposit balance has been fully utilized, the Company is required to settle the subsequent invoices within 14 days from the date the billing documents are declared complete. It should be noted that the quantity and contract value stipulated in the agreement are non-binding to the Company if the supplier fails to meet the agreed delivery timeline.

### Sales and Purchase Goods and Services Agreement with PT ECS Indo Jaya

On March 26, 2025, the Company entered into a Goods and Services Sales and Purchase Agreement No. 043/LGL/ECS-SSD/III/2025 with PT ECS Indo Jaya. The main scope of the agreement includes the procurement of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) products, infrastructure, and related services, including design, configuration, integration, and installation.

Based on the terms of the agreement, the payment scheme is divided into two main categories:

- Huawei Products and Services
Payments are made within a period of 2 years (720 calendar days) from the Proof of Delivery (POD). Installments begin in the second year (day 361) and are made in 12 monthly installments.
- Third-Party Services
A 30% down payment is made after the issuance of the Work Order (SPK), and the remaining 70% is paid after the Minutes of Handover (BAST) has been signed.

### Sales and Purchase Goods and Services Agreement FWA Project

On July 24, 2025, the Company entered into a Goods and Services Sales and Purchase Agreement for the FWA Project No. 110/PKS/LGL/ECS-SSD/VI/2025 with PT ECS Indo Jaya. The main scope of the agreement includes the procurement of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) products, ICT infrastructure, and related services, including planning, design, configuration, integration, installation, and maintenance, which are carried out by Huawei as the main manufacturer.
