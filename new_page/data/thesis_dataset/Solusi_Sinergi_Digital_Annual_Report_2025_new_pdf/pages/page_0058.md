# Dasar Hukum Pendirian
Legal Basis of Establishment [POJK 3.c.3.]

Perseroan didirikan dengan nama PT Lucaffe Indonesia, sebagaimana tercantum dalam Akta Pendirian Perseroan Terbatas No. 21 tanggal 6 September 2012, yang dibuat di hadapan Humberg Lie, S.H., S.E., M.Kn., Notaris di Jakarta Utara.

Berdasarkan Akta No. 12 tanggal 15 Juli 2020 juncto Akta Pernyataan Keputusan Para Pemegang Saham No. 15 tanggal 18 Agustus 2020, yang dibuat di hadapan Rini Yulianti, S.H., Notaris di Jakarta, Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Luar Biasa (RUPSLB) antara lain telah menyetujui Rencana Penawaran Umum; dan Perubahan atas seluruh Anggaran Dasar Perseroan sehubungan dengan perubahan status Perseroan menjadi Perseroan Terbatas Terbuka (Perusahaan Publik), dalam rangka penyesuaian dengan ketentuan Anggaran Dasar perusahaan publik sebagaimana diatur dalam Peraturan No. IX.J.1; Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) No. 15/POJK.04/2020; dan Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) No. 33/POJK.04/2014.

The Company was incorporated under the name of PT Lucaffee Indonesia, as stated in the Deed of Establishment of a Limited Liability Company No. 21 dated September 6, 2012, which was drawn up before Notary Humberg Lie, S.H., S.E., M.Kn., Notary in North Jakarta.

Based on Deed No. 20 dated July 15, 2020, in conjunction with the Deed of Shareholders' Resolution No. 15 dated August 18, 2020, drawn up before Rini Yulianti, S.H., Notary in Jakarta, the Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders ("EGMS") approved, among others the plan for the Initial Public Offering; and the amendment to the entire Articles of Association of the Company in connection with the change of the Company's status from a private limited liability company to a public limited liability company.

In order to comply with the provisions governing the articles of association of public companies as stipulated in Regulation No. IX.J.1; OJK Regulation No. 15/POJK.04/2020; and OJK Regulation No. 33/POJK.04/2014.
