Perusahaan (lanjutan)

PT KDB TIFA Finance Tbk (lanjutan)

Perusahaan dilarang melakukan tindakan-tindakan sebagai berikut:

(a) mengurangi, mengganti, mengubah barang (kecuali yang dinyatakan tegas dalam Perjanjian ini), menjual, mengalihkan hak, menyewa pembiayaan-kan, menggadaikan, menghiptikkan, membebankan, atau dengan cara apapun kepada pihak lain tanpa persetujuan tertulis terlebih dahulu dari PT KDB TIFA Finance Tbk; dan
(b) melakukan tindakan apa pun atau gagal melakukan tindakan yang dapat mengurangi nilai Jaminan.

Berdasarkan perjanjian No. JCKLS25110009 pada tanggal 3 Desember 2025, Perusahaan memperoleh fasilitas pembiayaan dari PT KDB Tifa Finance sebesar Rp 59.852.000.000. Pembiayaan ini dikenakan bunga sebesar 9,25% dan berlaku untuk jangka waktu 48 bulan.

PT Radana Bhaskara Finance Tbk ("RDB")

Jangka waktu pembiayaan tersebut sampai dengan 19 Desember 2024.

23. LOANS (continued)

The Company (continued)

PT KDB TIFA Finance Tbk (continued)

The Company is prohibited from taking following actions:

(a) Reducing, replacing, changing goods (except as expressly stated in this Agreement), selling, transferring rights, leasing, pawning, hypothecating, encumbering, or in any other way to another party without prior written approval from PT KDB TIFA Finance Tbk; and
(b) take any action or fails to take any action which may reduce the value of the Collateral.

Based on the agreement No. JCKLS25110009 on December 3, 2025, the Company obtained a financing facility from PT KDB TIFA Finance amounting to Rp 59,852,000,000. This financing bears interest of 9.25% and is valid for a period of 48 months.

PT Radana Bhaskara Finance Tbk ("RDB")

On August 6, 2024, RDB and the Company made an agreement as stated in the transfer of rights to receivables agreement No. 1012/LCS/SSD-CESSIE/VIII/2024 with the value of receivables transferred amounting to Rp 6,993,000,000 and factoring fees of 16.50% so that the total factoring financing amounted to Rp 5,040,000,000. The financing period is until December 21, 2024.

On August 6, 2024, RDB and the Company made an agreement as stated in the transfer of rights to receivables agreement No. 1014/LCS/SSD-CESSIE/VIII/2024 with the value of receivables transferred amounting to Rp 7,381,500,000 and factoring fees of 16.50% so that the total factoring financing amounted to Rp 5,320,000,000. The financing period is until December 19, 2024.

On August 23, 2024, RDB and the Company made an agreement as stated in the transfer of rights to receivables agreement No. 1107/LCS/SSD-CESSIE/VIII/2024 with the value of receivables transferred amounting to Rp 6,438,000,000 and factoring fees of 16.50% so that the total factoring financing amounted to Rp 4,640,000,000. The financing period is until January 13, 2025.
