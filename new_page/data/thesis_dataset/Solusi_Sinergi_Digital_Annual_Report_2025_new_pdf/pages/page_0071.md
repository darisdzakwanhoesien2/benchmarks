# Wilayah Operasional

Operational Area (POJK 3.c.4.)

![img-77.jpeg](img-77.jpeg)

Bidang usaha periklanan dan produk digital mengelola berbagai jenis aset media, yang dibangun dan beroperasi di berbagai wilayah di seluruh Indonesia.

Bidang usaha infrastruktur telekomunikasi Perseroan mengoperasikan sekitar 7.357 kilometer jaringan backbone serat optik yang membentang dari kota Cilegon hingga kota Banyuwangi di Pulau Jawa, di sepanjang jalur kereta api, jalan tol dan jalan-jalan provinsi, termasuk jaringan kabel bawah laut.

Ke depan, Perseroan akan membangun jaringan backbone serat optik di sepanjang koridor jalur kereta api yang berlokasi di Sumatra, serta membangun infrastruktur 5G Fixed Wireless Access (FWA) yang ekstensif di wilayah I, meliputi wilayah Jawa, Maluku, dan Papua.

The advertising and digital product business manages a broad portfolio of media assets, deployed and operated across various regions throughout Indonesia.

The Company's telecommunication infrastructure business operates some 7,357 kilometers of fiber optic backbone network that spans from Cilegon to Banyuwangi in Java Island, along railway lines, toll roads, and provincial roads, including submarine cable network.

In the future, the Company plans to build an additional fiber optic backbone network along the railway corridor located in Sumatra and deploy an extensive 5G Fixed Wireless Access (FWA) infrastructure across Region I, which covers Java, Maluku, and Papua.
