Corporate Governance

Pernyataan Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi atas Kecukupan Sistem Manajemen Risiko Perseroan

Setelah melakukan evaluasi yang menyeluruh, Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi mengambil kesimpulan bahwa sistem manajemen risiko telah membuktikan keandalannya dalam mencegah dampak dari berbagai risiko yang timbul dan proses manajemen risiko secara keseluruhan cukup andal dalam memitigasi potensi-potensi risiko yang dapat terjadi.

Statement of the BoC and BoD on the Adequacy of the Company's Risk Management System

Following a thorough evaluation, the BoC and the BoD concluded that the risk management system has demonstrated its ability to prevent the impact of any arising risks and the overall risk management processes are robust in mitigating potential risks that may arise.

# Perkara Hukum yang Melibatkan Perseroan, Anak-anak Usaha, Komisaris, dan/atau Direktur

Legal Matters Involving the Company, its Subsidiaries, Commissioners, and/or Directors

Sepanjang tahun 2025, Perseroan, anak-anak usahanya, anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi tidak menghadapi tuntutan hukum atau terlibat dan perkara litigasi sipil maupun kriminal.

Throughout 2025, the Company, its subsidiaries, and the members of the BoC and BoD were not subject to any legal indictments and were not involved in any civil or criminal litigation.

# Sanksi Administrasi yang Dikenakan Kepada Perseroan, Anak-anak Usaha, Komisaris dan/atau Direktur

Administrative Sanctions Imposed on the Company, its Subsidiaries, Commissioners and/or Directors

Sepanjang tahun 2025, Perseroan, anak-anak usahanya serta para anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi tidak dikenakan sanksi adminsitratif.

During 2025, the Company, its subsidiaries, and the members of the BoC and BoD were not subject to any administrative sanctions.
