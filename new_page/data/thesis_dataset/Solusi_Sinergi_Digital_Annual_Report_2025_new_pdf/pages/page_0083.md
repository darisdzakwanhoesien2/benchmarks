Bapak Mohammad Mustaghfirin, 58 tahun, Warga Negara Indonesia, berdomisili di Bekasi, pertama kali diangkat sebagai Direktur Perseroan berdasarkan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Luar Biasa yang diselenggarakan pada 26 September 2024.

Beliau merupakan profesional berpengalaman di bidang rekayasa jaringan, manajemen kinerja, dan operasional telekomunikasi.

Selain menjadi sebagai Direktur Perseroan, saat ini beliau juga menjadi sebagai Direktur PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem sejak 2024, serta Komisaris PT Jaringan Infra Andalan sejak 2025, yang merupakan anak usaha Perseroan.

Sebelumnya, Beliau menduduki berbagai posisi kepemimpinan senior di PT Telkomsel, antara lain sebagai Vice President Network Performance and Service Management untuk periode 2021-2023, Vice President Network Planning and Engineering tahun 2015-2019, Person in Charge ICT Network Management Lead untuk wilayah Jabodetabek, Banten, dan Jawa Barat periode 2013-2015, Vice President Radio Access Engineering untuk wilayah di luar Jawa-Bali tahun 2011-2013, Vice President Network Operations untuk wilayah di luar Jawa-Bali dari 2010-2011, serta Vice President Service Layer Engineering pada periode 2007-2010.

Bapak Mohammad Mustaghfirin meraih gelar Magister Manajemen Pemasaran dari Telkom University, Bandung, pada tahun 2021.

Bapak Mohammad Mustaghfirin tidak memiliki hubungan afiliasi dengan anggota Dewan Komisaris, anggota Direksi, maupun dengan pemegang saham utama dan pengendali Perseroan.

Mr. Mohammad Mustaghfirin, 58 years old, an Indonesian citizen and domiciled in Bekasi, was first appointed as Director of the Company based the resolution of the Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders held on September 26, 2024.

He is a seasoned expert in network engineering, performance management, and telecommunications operations.

In addition to his role as the Director of the Company, he currently serves as Director of PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem since 2024, and Commissioner of PT Jaringan Infra Andalan since 2025, which are subsidiaries of the Company.

Previously, he held various senior leadership positions at PT Telkomsel. These included Vice President of Network Performance and Service Management from 2021 to 2023, Vice President of Network Planning and Engineering from 2015 to 2019, Person in Charge of ICT Network Management Lead for Greater Jakarta, Banten, and West Java from 2013 to 2015, Vice President of Radio Access Engineering for regions outside Java-Bali from 2011 to 2013, Network Operations Vice President for regions outside Java-Bali from 2010 to 2011, and Vice President of Service Layer Engineering from 2007 to 2010.

Mr. Mohammad Mustaghfirin earned a Master's degree in Marketing Management from Telkom University, Bandung, in 2021.

Mr. Mohammad Mustaghfirin has no affiliation with members of the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, as well as the Company's major and controlling shareholders.
