## 2. Limited Public Offering Process

At this stage, the External Auditor acts as a coordinator for all activities, particularly in coordinating with relevant authorities. The scope of work at this stage includes:

- Assisting management in reviewing the prospectus and summary prospectus prepared by the Company's management.
- Assisting the Company's management in preparing the registration statement to be submitted to the Financial Services Authority (Otoritas Jasa Keuangan/OJK) and reviewing the completeness of the required supporting documents.
- Assisting the Company's management in reviewing the preparation of the public expose and providing input on the presentation materials prepared by management.
- Accompanying the Company's management in attending various technical meetings with the underwriter, officials of OJK, and the Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX).
- Assisting the Company's management in responding to questions during hearings and mini expose sessions conducted by OJK.
- Preparing a comfort letter in connection with the Limited Public Offering.

# Sistem Manajemen Risiko

## Risk Management System

Perseroan menyadari bahwa sistem manajemen risiko yang efektif merupakan hal penting agar dapat mengidentifikasi, mengevaluasi dan melakukan mitigasi atas berbagai risiko yang dapat timbul selama kegiatan usahanya. Untuk memperkuat sistem tersebut, Perseroan telah mengembangkan daftar risiko yang komprehensif yang mendokumentasikan seluruh potensi risiko yang relevan bagi kegiatan operasionalnya. Daftar risiko itu berfungsi sebagai pusat referensi untuk kegiatan pemantauan risiko, penetapan kemungkinan terjadi dan dampaknya, serta formulasi tindakan mitigasi yang tepat. Melalui pendekatan ini, Perseroan dapat secara proaktif melakukan pengelolaan atas risiko-risiko serta mendukung proses pengambilan keputusan berbasis informasi di seluruh tingkat organisasi.

## JENIS-JENIS RISIKO

### Risiko sebagai Perseroan Induk

Keputusan berinvestasi dalam bentuk proyek atau penyertaan pada Perseroan lain selalu didahului oleh proses studi yang mendalam dengan mempertimbangkan risiko, kesesuaian bisnis (visi, misi, dan strategi usaha), serta kemampuan Perseroan.

The Company recognizes that an effective risk management system is essential for identifying, assessing, and mitigating the various risks that may arise in its business activities. To strengthen this system, the Company has developed a comprehensive risk register that documents all potential risks relevant to its operations. This register serves as a central reference for monitoring risks, evaluating their likelihood and impact, and formulating appropriate mitigation strategies. Through this structured approach, the Company is able to manage risks proactively and support informed decision-making across the organization.

## RISK TYPES

### Holding Company Risk

Decisions to invest in a project or participate in another company are always preceded by an in-depth study process, taking into account potential risks, business suitability (vision, mission, and business strategy), and the Company's capabilities.
