## Perjanjian antara Perusahan dengan PT Lintas Daya Andalan ("LDA")

Pada tanggal 15 September 2025, Perusahaan menandatangani Perjanjian Jual Beli Nomor 069/PJB/SPKFF-FWA/IX/2025 dengan PT Lintas Daya Andalan untuk pengadaan material Fixed Wireless Access (FWA). Total nilai target pengadaan ini adalah sebesar Rp 1.072.500.000.000 untuk 1.000.000 unit perangkat N50 CPE SRT838CX 5G.

Adapun ketentuan pembayaran yang dilakukan perusahaan kepada LDA sebagai berikut:

- Perusahaan akan melakukan pembayaran uang muka kepada LDA sebesar Rp 500.000.000.000 guna memastikan proses produksi dan distribusi material. Setoran ini dapat diberikan dalam bentuk penyetoran tunai atau Bank Garansi Jaminan Pembayaran.
- Deposit akan diamortisasi secara berkala sesuai dengan nilai material FWA yang telah diterima oleh Perusahaan berdasarkan tagihan dan Berita Acara Serah Terima (BAST).

f. Rights and obligations:

### Rights of the Company

- The Company has the right to obtain services as stipulated in the Agreement properly and correctly, including topology, blueprints and/or other documents deemed necessary by the Company.
- The Company has the right to monitor the recruitment and termination of employees of PT Era Media Sejahtera Tbk, including the transfer of hard skills and soft skills knowledge to such employees in accordance with the competency standards set in the Agreement.
- The Company has the right to request replacement or adjustment of part or all of the topology, blueprint, configuration, and/or equipment to optimize its operational requirements.

### Obligations of the Company:

- The Company is obligated to make monthly payments of the transaction price to PT Era Media Sejahtera Tbk.
- To provide system access and supply the necessary content materials required for the implementation of the services.

### Agreement between the Company and PT Lintas Daya Andalan ("LDA")

On September 15, 2025, the Company entered into a Sale and Purchase Agreement No. 069/PJB/SPKFF-FWA/IX/2025 with PT Lintas Daya Andalan for the procurement of Fixed Wireless Access (FWA) materials. The total target procurement value amounts to Rp 1,072,500,000,000 for 1,000,000 units of N50 CPE SRT838CX 5G devices.

The payment terms between the Company and LDA are as follows:

- The Company shall provide an advance payment to LDA amounting to Rp 500,000,000,000 to ensure the production and distribution process of materials. Such advance may be provided either in the form of a cash deposit or a Bank Guarantee for Payment.
- The deposit will be amortized periodically in accordance with the value of FWA materials received by the Company based on the invoice and the Minutes of Handover (Berita Acara Serah Terima/BAST).
