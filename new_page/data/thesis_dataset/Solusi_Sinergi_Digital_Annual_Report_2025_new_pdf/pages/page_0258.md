## Akuisisi Entitas Anak (lanjutan)

### PT Telemedia Komunikasi Pratama

Berdasarkan Akta pernyataan keputusan sirkuler para pemegang saham No. 15 tanggal 13 Juni 2024 dari Dethisyah Agrimerinda, S.H., M.Kn., notaris di Jakarta, DSS melakukan pembelian saham TKP dengan jumlah nilai akuisisi sebesar Rp 20.599.000.000, yang terdiri dari 20.599 lembar saham, sehingga kepemilikan saham DSS di TKP menjadi 20.599 lembar atau ekuivalen dengan 99,99515%.

### Surge International Supply Chain Management Co., Limited ("SISCM")

Berdasarkan Keputusan Tertulis yang ditandatangani oleh Direktur Tunggal Perseroan berdasarkan Pasal 7 ayat (2) Anggaran Dasar Surge International Supply Chain Management Co. Ltd (SISCM) tertanggal 3 Desember 2025, memutuskan bahwa GMI memiliki 51 lembar saham SISCM dengan pembayaran tunai sebesar HK$ 1, sehingga GMI memiliki kepemilikan 52% di SISCM.

### Pelepasan Entitas Anak

#### PT Ini Kopi Indonesia ("IKI")

Berdasarkan Akta Pernyataan Keputusan Pemegang Saham Perseroan Terbatas No. 11 tanggal 24 September 2025 dari Notaris Delny Teoberto, S.H., M.Kn. dan telah mendapatkan pengesahan dari Kementerian Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui surat keputusan No. AHU-AH.01.09-0341852 tanggal 24 September 2025, KKD melepas seluruh kepemilikan saham IKI kepada pihak ketiga sebanyak 594 lembar, dengan harga jual sebesar Rp 594.000.000. Selisih antara harga pelepasan dengan nilai buku investasi IKI dicatat sebagai "Laba Pelepasan Entitas Anak" dan disajikan sebagai bagian dari penghasilan lain-lain pada laporan laba rugi dan penghasilan komprehensif lain konsolidasian.

## Acquisition of Subsidiaries (continued)

### PT Telemedia Komunikasi Pratama

Based on the Deed of circular decision statement of the shareholders of the limited company No. 15 dated June 13, 2024 of Dethisyah Agrimerinda, S.H., M.Kn., notary in Jakarta, DSS purchased TKP shares with a total acquisition value of Rp 20,599,000,000, consisting of 20,599 shares then DSS's share ownership in TKP became 20,599 shares or equivalent to 99.99515%.

### Surge International Supply Chain Management Co., Limited ("SISCM")

Based on the Written Decision signed by the Sole Director of the Company based on Article 7 paragraph (2) of the Articles of Association of Surge International Supply Chain Management Co. Ltd (SISCM) dated December 3, 2025, it was decided that GMI owns 51 shares of SISCM with a cash payment of HK$ 1, so that GMI has 52% ownership in SISCM.

## Divestment of Subsidiaries

### PT Ini Kopi Indonesia ("IKI")

Based on the Deed of Decision of the Shareholders of the Limited Company No. 11 dated September 24, 2025 from Notary Delny Teoberto, S.H., M.Kn. and has been approved by the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decree No. AHU-AH.01.09-0341852 dated September 24, 2025, KKD released all of its ownership in IKI to third parties with 594 shares, at a selling prices of Rp 594,000,000. The difference between the disposal price and the book value of IKI investment is recorded as part of "Gain on Divestment of Subsidiary" and presented as other income in the consolidated statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income.
