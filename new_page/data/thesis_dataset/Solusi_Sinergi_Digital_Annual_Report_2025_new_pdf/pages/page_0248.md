### Pendirian Entitas Anak (lanjutan)

#### PT Jaringan Infra Andalan ("JIA") (lanjutan)

Berdasarkan Akta Pernyataan Keputusan/Keputusan Sirkuler Pemegang Saham Perseroan Terbatas No. 45 tanggal 29 Januari 2020 dari Notaris Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., Perusahaan menjual saham JIA senilai Rp 239.000.000 dan mengalami penurunan kepemilikan di JIA menjadi 360 lembar saham atau setara dengan 60% kepemilikan.

Berdasarkan Akta Pernyataan Keputusan/Keputusan Sirkuler Pemegang Saham Perseroan Terbatas No. 59 tanggal 28 Mei 2024 dari Notaris Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., Perusahaan membeli saham JIA senilai Rp 239.000.000 dan mengalami peningkatan kepemilikan di JIA menjadi 599 lembar saham atau setara dengan 99,99% kepemilikan, serta mengubah Pasal 1 Anggaran Dasar dengan melakukan perubahan nama yang semula PT Mitra Digital Ekosistem menjadi PT Jaringan Infra Andalan dan telah mendapat persetujuan perubahan anggaran dasar dari Kementerian Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui Surat Keputusan No. AHU-0031059.AH.01.02.Tahun 2024 tanggal 29 Mei 2024.

### Establishment of Subsidiaries (continued)

#### PT Jaringan Infra Andalan ("JIA") (continued)

Based the Deed of Decision Statement / Circular Resolution of Limited Liability Company Shareholders No. 45 dated January 29, 2020 from Notary Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., the Company sold shares with value of Rp 239,000,000, which decreased Company's ownership in JIA to 360 shares or equivalent to 60% ownership.

Based on the Deed of Decision Statement / Circular Resolution of Limited Liability Company Shareholders No. 59 dated May 28, 2024 from Notary Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., the Company purchased JIA shares worth Rp 239,000,000 and experienced an increase in ownership in MDE to 599 shares or equivalent to 99,99% ownership, and changed Article 1 of the Articles of Association by changing the name from PT Mitra Digital Ekosistem to PT Jaringan Infra Andalan and has received approval for changes to the articles of association from the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decision Letter No. AHU-0031059.AH.01.02.
