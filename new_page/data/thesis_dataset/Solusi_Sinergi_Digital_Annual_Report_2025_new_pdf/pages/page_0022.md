SURGE Berhasil Memenangkan Lelang
Spektrum 1,4 GHz
SURGE was named as winner of the 1.4 GHz
Spectrum Auction

PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk mengumumkan kesiapan penggelaran jaringan 5G FWA "Internet Rakyat" dengan harga terjangkau di wilayah Indonesia, setelah keberhasilan salah satu anak usahanya dalam memenangkan lelang spektrum 1,4 GHz untuk Wilayah I (meliputi wilayah Jawa, Maluku, dan Papua).

PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk announced its readiness for the rollout of the "Internet Rakyat" 5G FWA network at affordable prices across the Indonesian region, following its subsidiary's successful bid in the 1.4 GHz spectrum auction for Region 1 (covering Java, Maluku, and Papua).
