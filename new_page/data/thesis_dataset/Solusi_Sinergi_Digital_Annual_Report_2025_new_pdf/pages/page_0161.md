# Pelaksanaan Tugas di Tahun 2025

Sepanjang tahun 2025, Sekretaris Perusahaan telah melaksanakan kegiatan penting berikut:

1. Berperan sebagai penghubung antara Perseroan dengan seluruh pemangku kepentingan.
2. Menyampaikan masukan kepada Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi perihal kepatuhan Perseroan terhadap peraturan pasar modal yang relevan.
3. Mengelola penyelenggaraan RUPS Tahunan dan Luar Biasa, kegiatan Paparan Publik, serta Rapat Dewan Komisaris, Rapat Direksi, Rapat Komite Audit, Rapat Komite Nominasi &amp; Remunerasi, dan Rapat Unit Audit Internal.
4. Memastikan transparansi dan pengungkapan informasi penting secara tepat waktu kepada pihak-pihak OJK, BEI, serta masyarakat.
5. Menyelenggarakan program orientasi untuk anggota BoC dan BoD yang baru menjabat dan memastikan bahwa anggota tersebut dapat dengan cepat mengenal kegiatan operasional Perseroan.

# Implementation of Duties in 2025

Throughout 2025, the Corporate Secretary has carried out the following key activities:

1. Acted as a liaison between the Company and all stakeholder.
2. Provided feedback to the BoC and the BoD on the Company's compliance with relevant capital market regulations.
3. Organized the Annual and Extraordinary GMS, Public Expose activities, as well as the BoC Meetings, BoD Meetings, Audit Committee Meetings, Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee Meetings, and the Internal Audit Unit Meetings.
4. Ensured transparent and timely disclosure of important information to the OJK, IDX, and the public.
5. Organized orientation programs for newly appointed BoC and BoD members and ensured that the new members can quickly adapt and be familiar with the Company's operational activities.

# Unit Audit Internal

## Internal Audit Unit

Unit Audit Internal merupakan fungsi tata kelola penting yang bertanggung jawab memberikan asuransi dan konsultatif yang independen dan objektif. Unit ini berfungsi meningkatkan nilai Perseroan dan menyempurnakan kinerja operasionalnya melalui evaluasi yang sistematis atas proses-proses manajemen risiko, pengendalian internal dan tata kelola. Dalam menjalankan tugasnya, Unit Audit Internal bekerjasama erat dengan Komite Audit.

## Piagam Audit Internal

Unit Audit Internal melaksanakan tugas-tugasnya atas dasar Piagam Unit Audit Internal sesuai yang telah disahkan oleh Dewan Komisaris Perseroan tanggal 17 Juli 2020. Piagam internal Audit berisikan pedoman tentang tujuan, kedudukan, wewenang, tanggung jawab dan ruang lingkup pekerjaan audit internal dalam menjalankan fungsi dan tugasnya, yang mengacu pada Peraturan OJK No. 56/POJK.04/2015 perihal Pembentukan dan Pedoman Penyusunan Piagam Unit Audit Internal.

## Independensi

Dalam melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya, Unit Audit Internal memegang teguh prinsip independensi dan kehati-hatian. Perseroan memastikan bahwa Kepala dan anggota Unit Audit Internal terbebas dari segala bentuk intervensi dari pihak Direksi, Dewan Komisaris, ataupun Pemegang Saham. Dalam melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya, Unit Audit Internal juga bebas dari konflik kepentingan dan segala bentuk pengaruh atau tekanan yang melanggar perundang-undangan dan peraturan, standar etika, praktik usaha yang sehat, ataupun berbagai kebijakan Perseroan.

The Internal Audit Unit is a key governance function responsible for providing independent and objective assurance and advisory services. Its purpose is to enhance the Company's value and improve operational performance through a systematic evaluation of risk management, internal control, and corporate governance processes. In performing its duties, the Internal Audit Unit works closely with the Audit Committee.

## Internal Audit Charter

The Internal Audit Unit carries out its duties based on the Internal Audit Charter which was approved by the BoC of the Company on July 17, 2020. Internal Audit Charter contains guidelines regarding the objectives, position, authority, responsibility, and scope of internal audit work in carrying out its functions and duties, which refers to OJK Regulation No. 56/POJK.04/2015 concerning the Establishment and Guidelines for the Preparation of the Internal Audit Unit Charter.

## Independency

In carrying out its duties and responsibilities, the Internal Audit Unit upholds the principles of independence and prudence. The Company ensures that the Head and members of the Internal Audit Unit are free from any form of intervention from the BoC, the BoD, or Shareholders. The Internal Audit Unit is also free from conflicts of interest and from any influence or pressure that may violate laws and regulations, ethical standards, sound business practices, or Company policies during the execution of its duties and responsibilities.
