Bapak Yune Marketatmo, 56 tahun, Warga Negara Indonesia, pertama kali diangkat sebagai Direktur Utama Perseroan berdasarkan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Luar Biasa yang diselenggarakan pada 2 Agustus 2023. Beliau berdomisili di Banten.

Beliau memiliki pengalaman yang luas di sektor telekomunikasi dan infrastruktur digital, dengan latar belakang yang kuat di bidang operasional jaringan, kepemimpinan teknologi, dan manajemen korporasi.

Selain menjabat sebagai Direktur Utama Perseroan, saat ini beliau juga menjabat sebagai Direktur Utama PT Kreasi Kode Digital dan Direktur PT Gawai Maju Indonesia sejak 2023, Komisaris Utama PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem sejak 2024 (setelah sebelumnya menjabat sebagai Direktur pada tahun 2023), Komisaris Utama PT Jejaring Digital Utama sejak 2024, Komisaris PT Telemedia Komunikasi Pratama dan PT Solusi Jaringan Nusantara, serta Komisaris Utama PT Solusi Sinergi Borneo sejak 2025, yang merupakan anak usaha Perseroan.

Sebelumnya, Beliau menduduki berbagai posisi kepemimpinan senior di Indosat Mega Media (IM2), antara lain sebagai Chief Technology and Information Officer (CTIO) sekaligus anggota Direksi pada periode 2018-2022, Pelaksana Tugas Chief Executive Officer pada 2019-2020, serta Chief Operating Officer sekaligus anggota Direksi tahun 2021. Beliau juga pernah menjabat sebagai Pelaksana Tugas Chief Technology Officer di Indosat Ooredoo tahun 2017, serta sebagai Senior Vice President dan Group Head of Network Planning di Indosat Ooredoo pada periode 2016-2018.

Bapak Yune Marketatmo menyelesaikan program Pascasarjananya di Royal Melbourne Institute of Technology, Australia, tahun 1997.

Bapak Yune Marketatmo tidak memiliki hubungan afiliasi dengan anggota Dewan Komisaris, anggota Direksi lainnya, maupun dengan pemegang saham utama dan pengendali Perseroan.

Mr. Yune Marketatmo, 56 years old, an Indonesian citizen, was first appointed as President Director of the Company pursuant to the resolution of the Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders held on August 2, 2023. He is domiciled in Banten.

He has extensive experience in the telecommunications and digital infrastructure sector, with a strong background in network operations, technology leadership, and corporate management.

In addition to his role as the President Director of the Company, he currently serves as President Director of PT Kreasi Kode Digital and Director of PT Gawai Maju Indonesia since 2023, President Commissioner of PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem since 2024 (after previously serving as Director in 2023), President Commissioner of PT Jejaring Digital Utama since 2024, Commissioner of PT Telemedia Komunikasi Pratama and PT Solusi Jaringan Nusantara, as well as President Commissioner of PT Solusi Sinergi Borneo since 2025, which are subsidiaries of the Company.

Prior to this, He held several senior leadership roles at Indosat Mega Media (IM2), including Chief Technology and Information Officer (CTIO) and member of the Board of Directors from 2018 to 2022, Acting Chief Executive Officer from 2019 to 2020, and Chief Operating Officer and member of the Board of Directors in 2021. He also served as Caretaker Chief Technology Officer of Indosat Ooredoo in 2017, and as Senior Vice President and Group Head of Network Planning at Indosat Ooredoo from 2016 to 2018.

Mr. Yune Marketatmo completed a Postgraduate program at the Royal Melbourne Institute of Technology, Australia, in 1997.

Mr. Yune Marketatmo has no affiliation with members of the Board of Commissioners, fellow members of the Board of Directors, as well as the Company's major and controlling shareholders.
