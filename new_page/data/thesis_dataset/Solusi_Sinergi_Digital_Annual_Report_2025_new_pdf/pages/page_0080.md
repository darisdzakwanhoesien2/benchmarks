Bapak Doni Satiaji Soetadi, 62 tahun, Warga Negara Indonesia, berdomisili di Jakarta, pertama kali diangkat sebagai Komisaris Independen Perseroan berdasarkan keputusan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Luar Biasa yang diselenggarakan pada 18 Mei 2022. Juga menjabat sebagai Ketua Komite Audit Perseroan berdasarkan Surat Keputusan Dewan Komisaris No. 001/SK-DEKOM/SSD/V/2022 tanggal 19 Mei 2022, serta Ketua Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi berdasarkan Surat Keputusan Dewan Komisaris 004/SK-DEKOM/SSD/VIII/2023 tanggal 24 Agustus 2023.

Berlias memiliki pengalaman yang luas dalam bidang penasihat strategis, keuangan korporasi, serta kepemimpinan senior di perusahaan-perusahaan konsumen, teknologi, dan keuangan.

Sebelumnya, Beliau pernah menjabat sebagai Strategic Partner di Ismaya Group pada periode 2004-2021. Juga pernah menjabat sebagai Strategic Advisor di WeWork pada 2015-2018, Principal untuk Indonesia di JUUL pada 2014-2017, serta Chairman Uber Indonesia pada 2011-2015. Pada tahap awal kariernya, beliau aktif terlibat dalam bidang Corporate Finance di Ciptadana Capital pada periode 2005-2019.

Bapak Doni Satiaji Soetadi meraih gelar Bachelor of Hotel Management dari Les Roches International School, Crans-Montana, Swiss, pada 1988.

Bapak Doni Satiaji Soetadi tidak memiliki hubungan afkiasi dengan anggota Dewan Komisaris lainnya, anggota Direksi, maupun dengan pemegang saham utama dan pengendali Perseroan.

Mr. Doni Satiaji Soetadi, 62 years old, an Indonesian citizen and domiciled in Jakarta, was first appointed as Independent Commissioner of the Company pursuant to the resolution of the Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders held on May 18, 2022. He also serves as the Company's Chairman of the Audit Committee based on the Board of Commissioners Decree No. 004/SK-DEKOM/SSD/VIII/2023 dated May 19, 2022, and Chairman of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee based on the Board of Commissioners Decree 004/SK-DEKOM/SSD/VIII/2023 dated August 24, 2023.

He has extensive experience across strategic advisory, corporate finance, and senior leadership roles in consumer, technology, and finance companies.

Previously, he was a Strategic Partner at Ismaya Group from 2004 to 2021. He also served as Strategic Advisor to WeWork from 2015 to 2018, Principal for Indonesia at JUUL from 2014 to 2017, and Chairman of Uber Indonesia from 2011 to 2015. Earlier in his career, he was involved in Corporate Finance at Ciptadana Capital from 2005 to 2019.

Mr. Doni Satiaji Soetadi earned a Bachelor of Hotel Management from Les Roches International School, Crans-Montana, Switzerland, in 1988.

Mr. Doni Satiaji Soetadi has no affiliation with other fellow members of the Board of Commissioners, members of the Board of Directors, nor with the Company's major and controlling shareholders.
