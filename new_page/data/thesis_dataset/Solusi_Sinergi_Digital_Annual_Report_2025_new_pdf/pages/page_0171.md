# Pernyataan Penerapan Pedoman Tata Kelola Bagi Perusahaan Terbuka

Statement on the Implementation of Corporate Governance Guidelines for Public Companies

Merujuk pada POJK No. 21/POJK.04/2015 tanggal 16 November 2015 perihal Penerapan Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik dan Surat Edaran OJK No. 32/SEOJK.04/2015 perihal Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan Terbuka, Perseroan berkewajiban mematuhi ke 5 (lima) Aspek, 8 (delapan) Prinsip dan 25 (dua puluh lima) Rekomendasi sesuai yang tercantum dalam peraturan-peraturan di atas. Penerapan Perseroan atas ketentuan-ketentuan tata kelola tersebut dijelaskan dalam tabel berikut:

In accordance with POJK No. 21/POJK.04/2015 dated November 16, 2015 on the Implementation of Good Corporate Governance, and OJK Circular Letter No. 32/SEOJK.04/2015 on Guidelines for the Governance of Public Companies, the Company is required to comply with 5 (five) Aspects, 8 (eight) Principles, and 25 (twenty-five) Recommendations as stipulated in these regulations. The Company's implementation of these corporate governance requirements is presented as follows:
