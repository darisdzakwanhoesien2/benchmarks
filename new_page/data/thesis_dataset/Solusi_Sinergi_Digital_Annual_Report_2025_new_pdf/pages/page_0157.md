## Independence

The Company ensures that each Committee member is an experienced professional who upholds transparency and demonstrates high integrity in carrying out their duties. They are committed to making decisions objectively and independently.

The Nomination and Remuneration Committee reports directly to the BoC.

## Term and Period of the Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee Members

Members of the Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee are appointed for a 5 (five) year term by taking into consideration the BoC's length of service as stipulated in the Company's Article of Association. The length of service of the Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee Members from the BoC may not be longer than the length of service of the Commissioners and can only be reappointed for the next 1 (one) term.
