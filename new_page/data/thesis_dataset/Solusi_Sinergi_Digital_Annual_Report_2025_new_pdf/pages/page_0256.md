PT Kreasi Kode Digital ("KKD") (anjutan)

Berdasarkan Akta Pernyataan Keputusan / Resolusi Sirkuler Pemegang Saham Perseroan Terbatas No. 29 tanggal 13 Maret 2023 dari Notaris Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., KKD meningkatkan modal dasar yang semula Rp 2.000.000.000 menjadi Rp 20.000.000.000, modal ditempatkan/ disetor yang semula Rp 600.000.000 menjadi Rp 10.600.000.000, sehingga kepemilikan saham Perusahaan di KKD menjadi 10.450 lembar saham atau ekuivalen dengan 98,58% kepemilikan.

PT Laju Inti Digital ("LIDI")

Berdasarkan Akta Notaris No. 27 dari Notaris Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., tanggal 11 Februari 2021, AMI membeli saham PT Laju Inti Digital (dahulu PT Kereta Teknologi Listrik) dari PT Lintas Maju Maxima sebanyak 200 lembar saham, sehingga total kepemilikan saham AMI atas LIDI menjadi 420 lembar saham atau ekuivalen dengan 70% kepemilikan. Akta ini telah mendapat persetujuan Menteri Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui Surat Keputusan No. AHU-AH.01.03-0111664 tanggal 22 Februari 2021.

PT Investasi Jaringan Nusantara ("IJN") (d/h Investasi Maju Makmur ("IMM"))

IJN didirikan berdasarkan Akta Notaris No. 65 tanggal 21 Agustus 2023 dari Notaris Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn dan telah mendapat pengesahan dari Menteri Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui Surat Keputusan No. AHU-0162834.AH.01.11 Tanggal 22 Agustus 2023 Tahun.

Berdasarkan Akta Pernyataan Keputusan / Resolusi Sirkuler Pemegang Saham Perseroan Terbatas No. 2 tanggal 4 Agustus 2025 dari Notaris Delny Teoberto, S.H., M.Kn., pemegang saham PT Sentosa Bersama Global menjual sebagian kepemilikan saham IJN kepada pihak JIA sebanyak 299.999 lembar saham, dengan harga jual sebesar Rp 299.999.000 dan terdapat perubahan nama Perusahaan dari sebelumnya PT Investasi Maju Makmur menjadi PT Investasi Jaringan Nusantara, perubahan susunan pemegang saham dan perubahan susunan dewan direksi dan komisaris. Perubahan tersebut telah disetujui oleh Menteri Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia dalam Surat Keputusan No. AHU-AH.01.09-0320565 tanggal 5 Agustus 2025.

Acquisition of Subsidiaries (continued)

PT Kreasi Kode Digital ("KKD") (continued)

Based on the Deed of Decision Statement / Circular Resolution of Limited Liability Company Shareholders No. 29 dated March 13, 2023 from Notary Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., KKD increased the original authorized capital Rp 2,000,000,000 to Rp 20,000,000,000, the original issued/paid-up capital Rp 600,000,000 becomes Rp 10,600,000,000, so the Company's share ownership in KKD becomes 10,450 shares or the equivalent of 98.58% ownership.

PT Laju Inti Digital ("LIDI")

Based on Notarial Deed No. 27 from Notary Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., dated February 11, 2021, AMI purchased shares of PT Laju Inti Digital (formerly PT Kereta Teknologi Listrik) from PT Lintas Maju Maxima totaling 200 shares, therefore the total shares ownership of AMI in LIDI is 420 shares, equivalent to 70% ownership. This deed has been approved by the Minister of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decision Letter No. AHU-AH.01.03-0111664 date February 22, 2021.

PT Investasi Jaringan Nusantara ("IJN") (formerly Investasi Maju Makmur ("IMM"))

IJN was established based on Notarial Deed No. 65 dated August 21, 2023 from the Notary Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn and was approved by the Minister of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decision Letter No. AHU-0162834.AH.01.11 dated August 22, 2023.

Based on the Deed of Statement of Decision / Circular Resolution of the Shareholders of the Limited Liability Company No. 2 dated August 4, 2025 issued by Notary Delny Teoberto, S.H., M.Kn., the shareholder PT Sentosa Bersama Global sold part of its shareholding in IJN to JIA in the amount of 299,999 shares, at a selling price of Rp 299,999,000. The deed also stipulates a change of the Company's name from PT Investasi Maju Makmur to PT Investasi Jaringan Nusantara, as well as changes to the composition of shareholders and to the composition of the Board of Directors and Commissioners. These changes have been approved by the Minister of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through the Decision Letter No. AHU-AH.01.09-0320565 dated August 5, 2025
