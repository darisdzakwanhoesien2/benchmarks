## IKLAN DAN MEDIA DIGITAL

Bisnis periklanan dan media digital Perseroan merupakan salah satu platform media periklanan terbesar dalam ekosistem transportasi di Indonesia. Melalui entitas anaknya, Perseroan telah mengembangkan portofolio solusi periklanan yang beragam, mencakup iklan digital dan layanan media Digital Out-Of-Home (DOOH), media iklan berbasis Wi-Fi gratis, serta berbagai format media digital terintegrasi berbasis teknologi yang dirancang untuk menghadirkan kampanye digital yang berkesan.

Layanan-layanan tersebut hadir di berbagai lokasi strategis, termasuk media digital di dalam dan di luar sarana transportasi publik, termasuk di dalam ruang kereta api dan stasiun, untuk menjangkau audiens yang luas.

## LAYANAN DAN APLIKASI DIGITAL

Didorong oleh akselerasi ekonomi digital serta perubahan teknologi yang berlangsung semakin cepat, Perseroan menempatkan transformasi digital sebagai salah satu pilar utama dalam strategi pertumbuhan jangka panjang. Fokus ini diarahkan untuk membangun kapabilitas bisnis digital yang mampu menciptakan nilai berkelanjutan.

Sebagai bagian dari strategi tersebut, Perseroan melakukan diversifikasi usaha ke sektor produk dan layanan digital dengan menghadirkan solusi perangkat lunak yang adaptif, inovatif, dan bernilai tambah tinggi. Inisiatif ini merupakan bentuk antisipasi terhadap meningkatnya adopsi teknologi informasi serta kebutuhan pasar yang terus berkembang akan solusi digital terintegrasi. Aktivitas pengembangan perangkat lunak dijalankan melalui Anak Perusahaan yang telah beroperasi sejak 2019 dan memiliki rekam jejak dalam mengembangkan berbagai aplikasi melalui kolaborasi strategis dengan mitra-mitra Perseroan.

Anak Perusahaan kami mengembangkan dan menawarkan berbagai aplikasi dan layanan digital, seperti aplikasi mobile dan solusi digital yang hadir untuk menjaring pengguna dan menciptakan digital ekosistem.

This achievement enables the rollout of 5G Fixed Wireless Access (FWA) services that feature faster network deployment and the ability to reach greenfield and non-fiberized areas. Complementing Starlite's FTTH offerings, the 5G FWA service enables us to target one of the nation's most strategic and populous regions.

Through the combined deployment of these wireless and wired fixed broadband solutions, Internet for the People program strives to leverage the best of fiber and 5G FWA technologies to bridge the digital divide, accelerate internet adoption, and enhance digital inclusion throughout Indonesia.

## DIGITAL ADVERTISING AND MEDIA

Our digital advertising and media business represents one of the largest advertising media platforms within Indonesia's transportation ecosystem. Through its subsidiaries, the Company has developed a diversified portfolio of advertising solutions, encompassing digital advertising and Digital Out-Of-Home (DOOH) media services, free Wi-Fi-based advertising, and other integrated, technology-enabled digital media formats designed to deliver high-impact campaigns.

These services are deployed across a wide range of locations, including digital displays both within and beyond public transportation venues, including inside trains and stations, enabling broad audience reach.

## DIGITAL SERVICES AND APPLICATIONS

Driven by the rapid growth of the digital economy and the accelerating pace of technological change, the Company has positioned digital transformation as a core pillar of its long-term growth strategy. This focus aims to build digital business capabilities that generate sustainable value.

As part of this strategy, the Company has diversified into digital products and services by delivering adaptive, innovative, and high-value software solutions. This initiative reflects a proactive response to the increasing adoption of information technology and the growing market demand for integrated digital solutions. Software development is carried out through the Subsidiary, which has been operating since 2019 and has a proven track record of creating and developing applications in collaboration with the Company's strategic partners.

Our subsidiaries develop and offer digital applications and services, such as mobile apps and digital solutions to engage users and generate a digital ecosystem.
