Pendirian Entitas Anak (lanjutan)

PT Jaringan Infra Andalan ("JIA") (lanjutan)

Berdasarkan Akta Pernyataan Keputusan Pemegang Saham Perseroan Terbatas No. 3 tanggal 5 Agustus 2025 dari Notaris Delny Teoberto, S.H., M.Kn., dan telah mendapatkan pengesahan dari Kementerian Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui surat keputusan No. AHU-AH.01.09-0320881 tanggal 5 Agustus 2025, pemegang saham PT Sentosa Bersama Global menjual seluruh kepemilikan saham IJN kepada pihak JIA sebanyak 599.999 lembar, dengan harga jual sebesar Rp 599.999.000.

PT Jejaring Digital Utama ("JDU")

JDU didirikan berdasarkan Akta Notaris No. 47 tanggal 23 Juni 2020 dari Notaris Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., dan telah mendapat pengesahan dari Menteri Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui Surat Keputusan No. AHU-0028949, AH.01.01 Tahun 2020 tanggal 24 Juni 2020. Sesuai akta tersebut, Perusahaan memiliki kepemilikan 99,90% di JDU.

Berdasarkan Akta Notaris Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., No. 24 tanggal 13 Mei 2024, PT Solusi Pembayaran Gerai melakukan perubahan nama menjadi PT Jejaring Digital Utama, Perubahan tersebut telah disetujui oleh Menteri Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia dalam Surat Keputusannya No. AHU-AH.01.09-0205790 tanggal 24 Mei 2024.

Berdasarkan Akta Pernyataan Keputusan/ Resolusi Sirkuler Pemegang Saham Perseroan Terbatas No. 24 tanggal 13 Mei 2024 dari Notaris Janty Lega, S.H., Perusahaan mengalihkan Sebagian saham kepada JDU sebanyak 250 lembar saham dan telah mendapat persetujuan perubahan anggaran dasar dari Kementerian Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui Surat Keputusan No. AHU-0100502.AH.01.11. Tahun 2024 tanggal 24 Mei 2024. Sehingga Perusahaan memiliki kepemilikan 70,00% di JDU.

PT Solusi Jaringan Nusantara ("SJN") (d/h Solusi Pariwisata Digital ("SPD"))

SJN didirikan berdasarkan Akta Notaris No. 15 tanggal 6 November 2019 dari Notaris Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., dan telah mendapat pengesahan dari Menteri Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui Surat Keputusan No. AHU-0058529.AH.01.01.Tahun 2019 tanggal 6 November 2019.

PT Jaringan Infra Andalan ("JIA") (continued)

Based on the Statement of Decision of the Shareholders of the Limited Liability Company No. 3 dated August 5, 2025, issued by Notary Delny Teoberto, S.H., M.Kn., and approved by the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decree No. AHU-AH.01.09-0320881 dated August 5, 2025, the shareholders of PT Sentosa Bersama Global sold all of their share ownership in IJN to JIA, totaling 599,999 shares, at a selling price of Rp 599,999,000.

PT Jejaring Digital Utama ("JDU")

JDU was established based on Notarial Deed No. 47 dated June 23, 2020 from the Notary Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., and was approved by the Minister of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decision Letter No. AHU-0028949, AH.01.01 of 2020 dated June 24, 2020. According to the deed, The Company has 99.90% ownership in JDU.

Based on Notarial Deed No. 24 of Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., dated May 13, 2024, PT Solusi Pembayaran Gerai changed its name into PT Jejaring Digital Utama. This amendment was approved by the Minister of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia in its Decision Letter No. AHU-AH.01.09-0205790 dated May 24, 2024

Based on the Deed of Decision Statement / Circular Resolution of the Limited Liability Company shareholders No. 24 dated May 13, 2024, issued by Notary Janty Lega, S.H., the Company distributed 250 shares to JDU and has received approval for amendments to the articles of association from the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decree No. AHU-0100502.AH.01.11.2024 dated May 24, 2024. As a result, the Company holds a 70.00% ownership interest in JDU.

PT Solusi Jaringan Nusantara ("SJN") (formerly Solusi Pariwisata Digital ("SPD"))

SJN was established based on Notarial Deed No. 15 dated November 6, 2019 by Notary Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., and has obtained approval from the Minister of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decision Letter No. AHU-0058529.AH.01.01.Year 2019 dated November 6, 2019.
