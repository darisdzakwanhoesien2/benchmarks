**Pelepasan Entitas Anak (lanjutan)**

**PT Integrasi Media Terkini ("IMT")**

Berdasarkan Akta Pernyataan Keputusan Pemegang Saham Perseroan Terbatas No. 12 tanggal 24 September 2025 dari Notaris Delny Teoberto, S.H., M.Kn., dan telah mendapatkan pengesahan dari Kementerian Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui Surat Keputusan No. AHU-AH.01.09-0341856 tanggal 24 September 2025, Perusahaan melepas seluruh kepemilikan saham IMT kepada pihak ketiga sebanyak 599 lembar saham, dengan harga jual sebesar Rp 599.000.000. Selisih antara harga pelepasan dengan nilai buku investasi IMT dicatat sebagai "Laba Pelepasan Entitas Anak" dan disajikan sebagai bagian dari penghasilan lain-lain pada laporan laba rugi dan penghasilan komprehensif lain konsolidasian.

**PT Aspek Media Indonesia ("AMI")**

Berdasarkan Akta Pernyataan Keputusan Pemegang Saham Perseroan Terbatas No. 10 tanggal 24 September 2025 dari Notaris Delny Teoberto, S.H., M.Kn., dan telah mendapatkan pengesahan dari Kementerian Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui Surat Keputusan No. AHU-AH.01.09-0341852 tanggal 24 September 2025, Perusahaan melepas seluruh kepemilikan saham AMI kepada pihak ketiga sebanyak 599 lembar, dengan harga jual sebesar Rp 599.000.000. Selisih antara harga pelepasan dengan nilai buku investasi AMI dicatat sebagai "Laba Pelepasan Entitas Anak" dan disajikan sebagai bagian dari penghasilan lain-lain pada laporan laba rugi dan penghasilan komprehensif lain konsolidasian.

**PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem ("IJE")**

Berdasarkan Akta Pernyataan Keputusan Pemegang Saham Perseroan Terbatas No. 5 tanggal 5 November 2024 dari Notaris Selo Selvieana, S.H., M.Kn., dan telah mendapat pengesahan dari Kementerian Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui Surat Keputusan No. AHU-01.09-0272211 tanggal 5 November 2024, Perusahaan melepas seluruh kepemilikan saham IJE kepada JIA sebanyak Rp 711.652.120.000.

**Divestment of Subsidiaries (continued)**

**PT Integrasi Media Terkini ("IMT")**

Based on the Deed of Decision of the Shareholders of the Limited Company No. 12 dated September 24, 2025 from Notary Delny Teoberto, S.H., M.Kn., and has been approved by the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decision Letter No. AHU-AH.01.09-0341852 dated September 24, 2025, the Company released all of its ownership in IMT to third parties with 599 shares, at a selling prices of Rp 599,000,000. The difference between the disposal price and the book value of IMT investment is recorded as part of "Gain on Divestment of Subsidiary" and presented as other income in the consolidated statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income.

**PT Aspek Media Indonesia ("AMI")**

Based on the Deed of Decision of the Shareholders of the Limited Company No. 10 dated September 24, 2025 from Notary Delny Teoberto, S.H., M.Kn., and has been approved by the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decree No. AHU-AH.01.09-0341852 date September 24, 2025, the Company released all of its ownership in AMI to third parties with 599 shares, at a selling prices of Rp 599,000,000. The difference between the disposal price and the book value of AMI investment is recorded as part of "Gain on Divestment of Subsidiary" and presented as other income in the consolidated statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income.

**PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem ("IJE")**

Based on the Deed of Statement of Shareholders' Resolution of the Limited Liability Company No. 5 dated November 5, 2024 from Notary Selo Selvieana, S.H., M.Kn., and has been approved by the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decree No. AHU-01.09-0272211 dated November 5, 2024, the Company released all IJE share ownership to JIA for Rp 711,652,120,000. profit or loss and other comprehensive income.
