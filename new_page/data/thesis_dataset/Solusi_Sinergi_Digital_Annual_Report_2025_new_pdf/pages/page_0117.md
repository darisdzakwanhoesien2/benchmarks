# Laporan Posisi Keuangan Konsolidasian

Consolidated Statement of Financial Position

## ASSET

Per 31 Desember 2025, jumlah aset meningkat sebesar 421,8%, mencapai Rp15,17 triliun dari Rp2,91 triliun per 31 Desember 2024.

Total aset lancar tercatat mencapai Rp8,69 triliun per 31 Desember 2025, naik 1.381,7% dari sebesar Rp586,35 miliar per 31 Desember 2024. Aset tidak lancar mencapai sebesar Rp6,48 triliun per 31 Desember 2025 dari Rp2,32 triliun per 31 Desember 2024.

## ASSETS

As of December 31, 2025, total assets increased by 421.8%, reaching Rp15.17 trillion from Rp2.91 trillion as per December 31, 2024.

Total current assets reached Rp8.69 trillion as of December 31, 2025, rose by 1,381.7% from Rp586.35 billion as of December 31, 2024. Non-current assets amounted to Rp6.48 trillion as of December 31, 2025, from Rp2.32 trillion as of December 31, 2024.
