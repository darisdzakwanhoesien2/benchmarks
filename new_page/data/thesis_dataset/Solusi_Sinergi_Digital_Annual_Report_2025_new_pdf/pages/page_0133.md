# Pedoman dan Kebijakan

## Guidelines and Policies

Penerapan GCG Perseroan didasarkan atas peraturan dan ketentuan perundang-undangan berikut yang berlaku di Indonesia, serta praktik-praktik terbaik di bidang tata kelola yang diakui secara internasional:

1. Undang-Undang Nomor 40 tahun 2007 tentang Perusahaan Terbatas.
2. Undang-Undang Nomor 8 tahun 1995 tentang Pasar Modal.
3. Surat Edaran Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) Nomor 16/SEOJK.04/2021 tentang Bentuk dan Isi Laporan Tahunan Emiten dan Perusahaan Publik.
4. Peraturan OJK No. 29/POJK.04/2016 tentang Laporan Tahunan Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik.
5. Peraturan OJK No. 21/POJK.04/2015 tentang Penerapan Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan Terbuka dan Surat Edaran OJK No. 32/SEOJK.04/2015 tentang Pedoman Tata Kelola Perusahaan Terbuka.
6. Peraturan OJK No. 15/POJK.04/2020 tentang Rencana dan Penyelenggaraan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Perusahaan Terbuka.
7. Peraturan OJK No. 16 tahun 2020 tentang Pelaksanaan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Perusahaan Terbuka Secara Elektronik.
8. Peraturan OJK No. 14 Tahun 2025 tentang Pelaksanaan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham, Rapat Umum Pemegang Obligasi dan Rapat Umum Pemegang Sukuk Secara Elektronik.
9. Peraturan OJK No. 42 tahun 2020 tentang Transaksi Afiliasi dan Transaksi Benturan Kepentingan.
10. Peraturan OJK No. 17 tahun 2020 tentang Transaksi Material dan Perubahan Kegiatan Usaha.
11. Peraturan OJK No. 33/POJK.04/2014 tentang Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik.
12. Peraturan OJK No. 55/POJK.04/2015 tentang Pembentukan dan Pedoman Pelaksanaan Kerja Komite Audit.
13. Peraturan OJK No. 34/POJK.04/2014 tentang Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik.
14. Peraturan OJK No. 35/PJOK.04/2014 tentang Sekretaris Perusahaan Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik.
15. Peraturan OJK No. 56/POJK.04/2015 tentang Pembentukan dan Pedoman Penyusunan Piagam Unit Audit Internal.
16. Pedoman Umum Governansi Korporat Indonesia 2021.
17. Anggaran Dasar Perseroan.
18. Peraturan-peraturan lainnya yang relevan.

The Company's GCG implementation adheres to the following prevailing laws and regulations in Indonesia, along with internationally recognized best practices in corporate governance:

1. Law Number 40 of 2007 on Limited Liability Companies.
2. Law Number 8 of 1995 on Capital Markets.
3. Financial Services Authority (OJK) Circular No. 16/SEOJK.04/2021 on the Form and Content of the Annual Report of Listed and Public Companies.
4. OJK Regulation No. 29/POJK.04/2016 concerning Annual Reports of Listed or Public Companies.
5. OJK Regulation No. 21/POJK.04/2015 on the Implementation of Corporate Governance Guideline for Public Companies and OJK Circular Letter No. 32/SEOJK.04/2015 on Guidelines for Public Company Governance.
6. OJK Regulation No. 15/POJK.04/2020 on the Plan and Implementation of the General Meeting of Shareholders of Public Companies.
7. OJK Regulation No. 16 of 2020 on the Electronic Implementation of the General Meeting of Shareholders of Public Companies.
8. POJK No. 14 of 2025 concerning the Implementation of Electronic General Meetings of Shareholders, General Meetings of Bondholders, and General Meetings of Sharia Bondholders.
9. OJK Regulation No. 42 of 2020 on Affiliated Transactions and Conflict of Interest Transactions.
10. OJK Regulation No. 17 of 2020 on Material Transactions and Changes in Business Activities.
11. OJK Regulation No. 33/POJK.04/2014 on Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners of Listed or Public Companies.
12. OJK Regulation No. 55/POJK.04/2015 on the Establishment and Work Guideline of the Audit Committee.
13. OJK Regulation No. 34/POJK.04/2014 on Nomination and Remuneration Committee of Listed or Public Companies.
14. OJK Regulation No. 35/PJOK.04/2014 on Corporate Secretary of Listed or Public Companies.
15. OJK Regulation No. 56/POJK.04/2015 on Internal Audit Unit Establishment and Charter Preparation Guideline.
16. The 2021 Indonesian General Guidelines for Corporate Governance.
17. The Company's Articles of Association.
18. Other relevant regulations.
