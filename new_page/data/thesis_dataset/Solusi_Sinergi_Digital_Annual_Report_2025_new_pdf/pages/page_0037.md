# Laporan Direksi

## Board of Directors' Report

Segmen infrastruktur komunikasi digital berhasil mempertahankan momentum pertumbuhannya dan semakin memperkuat posisinya sebagai faktor pertumbuhan utama Perseroan. Melalui pemanfaatan jaringan backbone serat optik di sepanjang koridor jalur kereta api bersama PT Kereta Api Indonesia, entitas anak PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem telah menghadirkan berbagai solusi konektivitas bagi para pelanggan korporasi.

Tingkat permintaan solusi konektivitas berkapasitas tinggi dari para pelanggan korporasi dan operator platform digital terus meningkat sepanjang tahun 2025, seiring pesatnya perkembangan digitalisasi kegiatan usaha. Sejalan dengan perkembangan tersebut, segmen layanan telekomunikasi business-to-business berhasil meraih pertumbuhan yang positif, didukung oleh perluasan basis pelanggan serta berlanjutnya optimalisasi pemanfaatan jaringan Perseroan.

Our digital communication infrastructure segment maintained strong growth momentum and is increasingly positioned as the Company's principal growth engine. Leveraging its extensive fiber-optic backbone deployed along railway corridors in partnership with PT Kereta Api Indonesia, our subsidiary PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem provides a comprehensive range of connectivity solutions for enterprise customers.

Demand for high-capacity connectivity from enterprise and digital platform operators continued to grow during the year, reflecting the accelerating digitalization of business activities. As a result, the business-to-business telecommunications services segment delivered solid growth supported by expanding client relationships and continued network monetization.

Sepanjang tahun 2025, kedua segmen usaha utama Perseroan, bidang usaha infrastruktur komunikasi digital serta layanan periklanan dan media digital, terus meraih kinerja yang positif.

Over the course of 2025, the Company's two primary business segments, digital communication infrastructure and digital advertising and media services, continued to demonstrate positive performance.

Sejalan dengan perkembangan tersebut, Perseroan juga terus memperluas kehadirannya di pasar fixed broadband bagi pelanggan residensial melalui layanan STARLITE Fiber-to-the-Home (FTTH). Sepanjang tahun 2025, STARLITE berhasil meraih tingkat penerimaan pasar yang tinggi, didukung oleh harga layanan yang terjangkau serta keandalan konektivitas serat optiknya. Pertumbuhan jumlah pelanggan didorong oleh upaya perluasan jangkauan jaringan serta penerapan model distribusi berbasis komunitas yang melibatkan para mitra lokal dalam kegiatan akuisisi pelanggan, instalasi, dan dukungan layanan.

In parallel, the Company continued to expand its presence in the residential fixed broadband market through the STARLITE Fiber-to-the-Home. Throughout 2025, STARLITE demonstrated strong market acceptance supported by affordable pricing and reliable fiber connectivity. Subscriber growth was driven by continued network expansion and a community-based distribution model that engages local partners in customer acquisition, installation, and service support.
