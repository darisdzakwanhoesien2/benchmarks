# Hasil Penilaian Kinerja Direksi
BoD Performance Assessment Results

Berdasarkan hasil penilaian di tahun 2025, kinerja Direksi menunjukkan hasil yang positif, serta telah memenuhi berbagai indikator kinerja yang ditetapkan. Direksi dinilai telah secara efektif melaksanakan tugas-tugas, tanggung jawab dan wewenangnya sesuai ketentuan dalam Anggaran Dasar Perseroan serta perundang-undangan dan peraturan yang berlaku. Strategi untuk pertumbuhan dan perkembangan usaha juga telah dilaksanakan dengan baik, sehingga Perseroan dapat meraih kinerja operasional dan keuangan yang positif.

Based on the 2025 assessment results, the performance of the Board of Directors was rated positively, having met the established performance indicators. The Board of Directors is considered to have effectively carried out its duties, responsibilities, and authorities in accordance with the Company's Articles of Association and applicable laws and regulations. The strategies for business growth and development were also implemented appropriately, enabling the Company to achieve strong operational and financial performance.

# Komite di Bawah Dewan Komisaris
Committees Under the BoC

Dewan Komisaris telah membentuk Komite Audit dan Komite Nominasi &amp; Remunerasi, dengan tugas membantu Dewan Komisaris dalam menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya.

## KOMITE AUDIT

Komite Audit dibentuk untuk membantu Dewan Komisaris dalam mengawasi pengelolaan Perseroan, melakukan pengawasan atas kualitas pelaporan keuangan, serta memastikan efektivitas dari sistem pengendalian internal.

Komite Audit secara langsung melaporkan kegiatannya kepada Dewan Komisaris dan menyampaikan penilaian dan rekomendasinya atas laporan yang disampaikan oleh Direksi. Komite Audit juga turut berpartisipasi dalam pemilihan dan penunjukan akuntan publik, melakukan penilaian atas rencana dan laporan audit internal maupun eksternal, serta menyampaikan pandangan yang independen perihal kepatuhan Perseroan terhadap perundang-undangan dan peraturan yang berlaku.

## Piagam Komite Audit

Komite Audit menjalankan tugas-tugasnya berdasarkan Piagam Komite Audit. Piagam tersebut disahkan pada tanggal 17 Juli 2020 sebagai acuan bagi Komite Audit dalam menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawab sesuai dengan Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan Nomor 55/POJK.04/2015 tentang Pembentukan dan Pedoman Pelaksanaan Kerja Komite Audit ("POJK 55/2015").

The BoC has established the Audit Committee and the Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee, tasked with assisting the BoC in performing its duties and responsibilities.

## AUDIT COMMITTEE

The Audit Committee was established to assist the BoC in overseeing the management of the Company, monitoring the quality of financial reporting, and ensuring the effectiveness of internal control systems.

The Audit Committee reports directly to the BoC and provides assessments and recommendations on reports submitted by the BoD. The Committee is also involved in the selection and appointment of public accountants, reviews internal and external audit plans and results, and provides an independent view on the Company's compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

## Audit Committee Charter

The Audit Committee performs its duties in accordance with its Audit Committee Charter. The Charter was formalized on July 17, 2020 as a reference for the Audit Committee in carrying out its duties and responsibilities following the Financial Services Authority Regulation Number 55/POJK.04/2015 concerning the Establishment and Work Guidelines for the Audit Committee ("POJK 55/2015").
