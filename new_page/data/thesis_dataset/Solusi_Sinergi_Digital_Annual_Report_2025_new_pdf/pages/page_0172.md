|  Prinzip 3 Memperkuat Keanggotaan dan Komposisi Dewan Komisaris
Principle 3 Strengthening The Membership and Composition of The Board of Commissioners  |   |   |   |
|  3.1. | Penentuan jumlah anggota Dewan Komisaris mempertimbangkan kondisi Perseroan. | Jumlah komposisi Dewan Komisaris Perseroan sudah sesuai dengan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan dan ketentuan komposisi yang disyaratkan dalam POJK No. 33/POJK.04/2014 tentang Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik. | Memenuhi Comply  |
|   | Determination of the number of members of the BoC considers the condition of the Company. | The number of BoC members is determined in accordance with the Company's Articles of Association and OJK Regulation No. 33/POJK.04/2014 on the BoD and BoC of Issuers or Public Companies. |   |
|  3.2. | Penentuan komposisi anggota Dewan Komisaris memperhatikan keberagaman keahlian, pengetahuan, dan pengalaman yang dibutuhkan. | Komposisi Dewan Komisaris Perseroan sudah memperhatikan keberagaman, keahlian, pengetahuan serta pengalaman yang dibutuhkan khususnya di industri infrastruktur telekomunikasi. | Memenuhi Comply  |
|   | The determination of the composition of the BoC considers the diversity of expertise, knowledge and experience required. | The composition of the Company's BoC has considered the needed diversity of skills, knowledge, and experience, particularly in the telecommunications infrastructure industry. |   |
|  Prinzip 4 Meningkatkan Kualitas Pelaksanaan Tugas dan Tanggung Jawab Dewan Komisaris
Principle 4 Strengthening The Quality of The Execution and Responsibilities of The Board of Commissioners  |   |   |   |
|  4.1. | Dewan Komisaris mempunyai kebijakan penilaian sendiri (self-assessment) untuk menilai kinerja Dewan Komisaris. | Dewan Komisaris telah memiliki kebijakan penilaian sendiri (self-assessment) untuk menilai kinerja Dewan Komisaris sebagai samar tercantum dalam Laporan Tahunan Perseroan. | Memenuhi Comply  |
|   | The BoC has policies on Self-Assessment to evaluate the performance of the BoC. | The BoC has a self-assessment policy to evaluate their performance as stated in the Company's Annual Report. |   |
|  4.2. | Kebijakan penilaian sendiri (self-assessment) untuk menilai kinerja Dewan Komisaris, diungkapkan melalui Laporan Tahunan Perseroan | Kebijakan penilaian sendiri untuk menilai kinerja Dewan Komisaris telah diungkapkan dalam Laporan Tahunan Perseroan. | Memenuhi Comply  |
|   | Policy on Self-Assessment to evaluate the performance of the BoC is stated in the Company's Annual Report. | Policy on self-assessment to assess the performance of the BoC has been disclosed in the Company's Annual Report. |   |
|  4.3. | Dewan Komisaris mempunyai kebijakan terkait pengunduran diri anggota Dewan Komisaris apabila terlibat dalam kejahatan keuangan. | Anggaran Dasar Perseroan mengatur bahwa jabatan Dewan Komisaris berakhir apabila tidak memenuhi persyaratan perundang-undangan. | Memenuhi Comply  |
|   | The BoC has a policy on the resignation of members of the BoC who are involved in any financial crime. | The Company's Article of Association stipulates that the term of a member of BoC shall end if such member fails to comply with the prevailing laws and regulations. |   |
|  4.4. | Dewan Komisaris atau Komite yang menjalankan fungsi Nominasi dan Remunerasi menyusun kebijakan suksesi dalam proses nominasi anggota Direksi. | Dewan Komisaris memberikan rekomendasi yang independen dan objektif tentang calon anggota prospektif untuk Direksi untuk diusulkan kepada pemegang saham. | Memenuhi Comply  |
|   | The BoC or Committee that carries out the Nomination and Remuneration function formulates a succession policy for the nomination of members of the BoD. | The BoC provides independent and objective recommendations for each prospective member for the BoD to be proposed to the shareholders. |   |
