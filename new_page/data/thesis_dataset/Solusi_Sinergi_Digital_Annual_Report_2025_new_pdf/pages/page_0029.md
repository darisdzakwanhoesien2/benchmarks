The Company also continued expanding its presence in the residential fixed broadband market through the Fiber-to-the-Home (FTTH) service. Since its launch in 2024, this service has demonstrated encouraging market acceptance supported by affordable pricing, reliable fiber connectivity, and a community-based operating model that engages local partners in customer acquisition, installation, and service support.

Another strategic milestone was achieved in October 2025, pursuant to the announcement of the selection results on October 15, 2025 and the Decree of the Minister of Communication and Digital Affairs No. 489 of 2025 dated November 24, 2025, PT Telemedia Komunikasi Pratama was designated as the winner of the 1.4 GHz Broadband Wireless Access spectrum auction for Regional I, covering Java, Maluku, and the Papua regions. This development represents an important step in expanding fixed broadband accessibility across Indonesia.

Dewan Komisaris mengapresiasi komitmen manajemen dan seluruh karyawan dalam menghadapi perkembangan dinamika industri dengan tetap mempertahankan prinsip tata kelola perusahaan yang baik, manajemen risiko yang berhati-hati, serta pelaksanaan strategi yang disiplin.

The Board of Commissioners acknowledges the commitment of management and employees in navigating this evolving industry landscape while maintaining sound governance, prudent risk management, and disciplined strategy execution.

Sebagai tindak lanjut dari pencapaian tersebut, Perseroan telah menghadirkan inisiatif IRA - Internet Rakyat, layanan broadband 5G Fixed Wireless Access yang menawarkan solusi konektivitas internet berkecepatan tinggi dengan harga terjangkau bagi konsumen residensial dan para pelaku usaha kecil di wilayah yang belum sepenuhnya terjangkau oleh layanan infrastruktur fixed broadband.

Following this achievement, the Company introduced the IRA - Internet Rakyat initiative, a 5G Fixed Wireless Access broadband service designed to deliver affordable and high-speed connectivity to households and small businesses in areas underserved by fixed broadband infrastructure.
