Pada 31 Desember 2023, jumlah Waran Seri I yang telah dilaksanakan menjadi saham adalah sebanyak 104.033.830 lembar saham, namun baru diaktakan pada tanggal 2 Februari 2024.

Berdasarkan RUPS Perusahaan diaktakan Notaris Rini Yulianti, S.H., No. 3 tanggal 2 Februari 2024, Perusahaan memperoleh peningkatan modal disetor melalui konversi waran menjadi saham sebanyak 104.033.830 lembar saham sehingga modal yang ditempatkan dan disetor menjadi sebesar 2.359.355.118 lembar saham, dengan nilai nominal seluruhnya sebesar Rp 235.935.511.800. Akta tersebut telah disetujui oleh Kementerian Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui Surat Keputusan No. AHU-AH.01.03-0032694 tanggal 5 Februari 2024.

Berdasarkan Akta Notaris Dr. Sugih Haryati, S.H., M.Kn., No. 99 tanggal 22 Juli 2025, Perusahaan memperoleh tambahan modal disetor melalui Penambahan Modal Dengan Melaksanakan Hak Memesan Efek Terlebih Dahulu I (PMHMETD I) sebanyak 4.718.710.236 lembar saham yang dilakukan oleh PT Investasi Sukses Bersama ('ISB'). Peningkatan modal ditempatkan dan disetor setelah pelaksanaan PMHMETD sehingga modal yang ditempatkan dan disetor menjadi sebesar 5.308.549.015 lembar saham, dengan nilai nominal seluruhnya sebesar Rp 530.854.901.500. Akta tersebut telah disetujui oleh Kementerian Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui Surat No. AHU-AH.01.03-0192292 tanggal 22 Juli 2025.

As of December 31, 2023, the number of Series I Warrants that have been exercised into shares is 104,033,830 shares, but it was only notarized on February 2, 2024.

Based on the Company's General Meeting of Shareholders notarized by Notary Rini Yulianti, S.H., No. 3 dated February 2, 2024, the Company obtained increase paid-in capital through warrant conversion of 104,033,830 shares so that the issued and paid-up capital becomes 2,359,355,118 shares, with a total nominal value of Rp 235,935,511,800. The deed has been approved by the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decision Letter No. date AHU-AH.01.03-0032694 dated February 5, 2024.

Based on Deed of Notary Dr. Sugih Haryati, S.H., M.Kn., under Deed No. 99 dated July 22, 2025, the Company obtained additional paid-in capital through Capital Increase by Exercising Pre-emptive Rights ("PMTHMETD") of 4,718,710,236 shares which was made by PT Investasi Sukses Bersama ('ISB'). Increase the issued and paid-up capital after PMHMETD so that the issued and paid-up capital becomes 5,308,549,015 shares, with a total nominal value of Rp 530,854,901,500. The deed has been approved by the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decision Letter No. date AHU-AH.01.03-0192292 July 22, 2025.
