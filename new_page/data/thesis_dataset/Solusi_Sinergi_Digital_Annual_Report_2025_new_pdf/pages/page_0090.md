Bapak Laurensius Pardede, 41 tahun, Warga Negara Indonesia, diangkat sebagai anggota Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi berdasarkan Surat Keputusan Dewan Komisaris PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk mengenai Pembentukan Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi No. 002/SK-DIR/SSD/V/2022 tanggal 19 Mei 2022.

Beliau adalah seorang profesional di bidang sumber daya manusia dengan pengalaman lebih dari sepuluh tahun dalam pengelolaan SDM, hubungan industrial, serta pengembangan organisasi.

Saat ini, beliau menjabat sebagai Head of Human Resources and General Affairs di PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk (SURGE) sejak Oktober 2018. Dalam peran tersebut, beliau bertanggung jawab atas perumusan dan pelaksanaan strategi SDM, termasuk rekrutmen, hubungan industrial, administrasi ketenagakerjaan, keterlibatan karyawan, penggajian, sistem remunerasi, pembelajaran dan pengembangan, serta pengelolaan anggaran fungsi SDM.

Sebelumnya, Bapak Laurensius Pardede menjabat sebagai HR Generalist (Assistant Manager) di Galeries Lafayette - Pacific Place, MAP Group pada periode 2016 hingga 2018, sebagai HR Recruitment Senior Executive and Welfare di PT AA International Inc. tahun 2016, dan sebagai HR Senior Supervisor di Galeries Lafayette - Pacific Place, MAP Group, dari tahun 2012 hingga 2015.

Di awal karir beliau, Bapak Laurensius Pardede bekerja sebagai HR Supervisor di PT Indo Tirta Suaka, Batam, dan di McDermott International Inc. (Batam) tahun 2007.

Bapak Laurensius Pardede meraih gelar Sarjana Fisika dari Universitas Sumatra Utara tahun 2006. Selain pendidikan formal, beliau juga memiliki berbagai sertifikasi di bidang ketenagakerjaan, keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja (K3), manajemen risiko, serta program Human Resources Business Partner.

Mr. Laurensius Pardede, 41 years old, an Indonesian citizen, was appointed as a member of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee based on the Decree of the Board of Commissioners of PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk regarding the Establishment of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee No. 002/SK-DIR/SSD/V/2022 dated May 19, 2022.

He is a human resource professional with more than ten years of experience in human capital management, industrial relations, and organizational development.

He currently serves as Head of Human Resources and General Affairs at PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk (SURGE), a position he has held since October 2018. In this role, he is responsible for formulating and implementing the Company's human resources strategy, including recruitment, industrial relations, workforce administration, employee engagement, payroll, remuneration systems, learning and development, as well as managing the HR function's budget.

Previously, Mr. Laurensius Pardede served as HR Generalist (Assistant Manager) at Galeries Lafayette - Pacific Place, MAP Group, from 2016 to 2018, as HR Recruitment Senior Executive and Welfare at PT AA International Inc. in 2016, and as HR Senior Supervisor at Galeries Lafayette - Pacific Place, MAP Group, from 2012 to 2015.

Farrier in his career, Mr. Laurensius Pardede worked as HR Supervisor at PT Indo Tirta Suaka, Batam, from 2008 to 2012 and at McDermott International Inc. (Batam) in 2007.

Mr. Laurensius Pardede earned a Bachelor's degree in Physics from Universitas Sumatra Utara in 2006. In addition to his formal education, he holds various professional certifications in manpower affairs, occupational health and safety (K3), risk management, and Human Resources Business Partner programs.
