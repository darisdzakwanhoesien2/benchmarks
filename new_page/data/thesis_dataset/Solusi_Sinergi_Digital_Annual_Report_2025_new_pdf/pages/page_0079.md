Bapak Hermansjah Haryono, 53 tahun, Warga Negara Indonesia, pertama kali diangkat sebagai Komisaris Utama Perseroan berdasarkan keputusan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Luar Biasa yang diselenggarakan pada 2 Agustus 2023. Juga menjabat sebagai anggota Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi Perseroan berdasarkan Surat Keputusan Dewan Komisaris 004/SK-DEKOM/SSD/VIII/2023 tanggal 24 Agustus 2023. Berdomisili di Jakarta.

Selain menjabat sebagai Komisaris Utama Perseroan, saat ini beliau juga menjabat sebagai Direktur Utama PT Jejaring Digital Utama sejak 2020, Direktur Utama PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem sejak 2021, Direktur PT Solusi Jaringan Nusantara dan PT Jaringan Infra Andalan sejak 2022, serta Komisaris PT Gawai Maju Indonesia dan PT Kreasi Kode Digital sejak 2023, yang merupakan anak usaha Perseroan.

Mr. Hermansjah Haryono, 53 years old, an Indonesian citizen, was first appointed as President Commissioner of the Company pursuant to the resolution of the Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders held on August 2, 2023. He also serves as a member of the Company's Nomination and Remuneration Committee based on the Board of Commissioners Decree 004/SK-DEKOM/SSD/VIII/2023 dated August 24, 2023. He is domiciled in Jakarta.

Bapak Hermansjah Haryono memiliki pengalaman yang luas di industri telekomunikasi dan layanan digital. Beliau pernah menjabat sebagai Direktur Utama Perseroan sejak 2020 sampai 2023, Chief Operation PT Digi Asia Bios sejak 2018 sampai 2020 dan Commercial Director Asia-Pacific di Digicel pada periode 2017-2018. Sebelum itu, beliau menduduki posisi mulai dari Product Marketing, Channel Strategy sampai Executive Vice President Enterprise, New Business, Modern Channel, dan International Business di Hutchison 3 Indonesia pada periode 2005-2017, dengan tanggung jawab meliputi strategi korporasi, inovasi produk, pengembangan kanal, serta inisiatif bisnis internasional. Pada tahap awal kariernya, beliau pernah menjabat sebagai Brand Manager di XL Axiata pada periode 2004-2005.

In addition to his role as the President Commissioner of the Company, he currently serves as the President Director of PT Jejaring Digital Utama since 2020, President Director of PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem since 2021, Director of PT Solusi Jaringan Nusantara and PT Jaringan Infra Andalan since 2022, as well as Commissioner of PT Gawai Maju Indonesia and PT Kreasi Kode Digital since 2023, which are subsidiaries of the Company.

Mr. Hermansjah Haryono brings extensive experience in the telecommunications and digital services industry. He previously served as President Director of the Company from 2020 to 2023, Chief Operation of PT Digi Asia Bios from 2018 to 2020 and Commercial Director Asia-Pacific at Digicel from 2017 to 2018. Prior to that, he held positions starting from Product Marketing and Channel Strategy to Executive Vice President for Enterprise, New Business, Modern Channel, and International Business at Hutchison 3 Indonesia from 2005 to 2017, where he was responsible for corporate strategy, product innovation, channel development, and international business initiatives. Earlier in his career, he served as Brand Manager at XL Axiata from 2004 to 2005.

Bapak Hermansjah Haryono meraih gelar Sarjana Teknik Mesin dari Universitas Trisakti, Jakarta, pada 1996, serta gelar Magister Administrasi Bisnis (MBA) dari Cleveland State University, Amerika Serikat, tahun 2000. Beliau juga telah menyelesaikan Executive Program in General Management di Universitas Macquarie, Sydney, di tahun 2008.

Mr. Hermansjah Haryono earned a Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering from Universitas Trisakti, Jakarta, in 1996, and a Master of Business Administration from Cleveland State University, United States, in 2000. He also completed the Executive Program in General Management at Macquarie University, Sydney, in 2008.

Bapak Hermansjah Haryono tidak memiliki hubungan afiliasi dengan anggota Dewan Komisaris lainnya dan anggota Direksi, namun memiliki hubungan afiliasi dengan pemegang saham utama dan pengendali Perseroan.

Mr. Hermansjah Haryono has no affiliation with other fellow members of the Board of Commissioners, members of the Board of Directors. However, he has an affiliated relationship with the Company's major and controlling shareholders.
