Bapak Shannedy Ong, 51 tahun, Warga Negara Indonesia, berdomisili di Jakarta, pertama kali diangkat sebagai Direktur Perseroan berdasarkan keputusan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Luar Biasa yang diselenggarakan pada 5 Desember 2024. Beliau juga menjabat sebagai Sekretaris Perusahaan Perseroan sejak 2 Desember 2024, berdasarkan Keputusan Direksi No. 017/SK-DIR/SSD/XI/2024.

Selain menjabat sebagai Direktur Perseroan, saat ini Beliau juga menjabat sebagai Direktur Utama PT Investasi Jaringan Nusantara sejak 2024, serta Direktur Utama PT Telemedia Komunikasi Pratama sejak 2025, yang merupakan anak usaha Perseroan.

Bapak Shannedy Ong merupakan profesional berpengalaman di sektor telekomunikasi dan teknologi, dengan latar belakang yang kuat di bidang rekayasa jaringan, kepemimpinan komersial, dan pengelolaan akun strategis. Beliau menjabat sebagai Country Managing Director Qualcomm di Jakarta pada periode 2015-2024. Sebelumnya, menduduki berbagai posisi kepemimpinan senior di Ericsson Indonesia, antara lain sebagai Vice President New Business pada 2014-2015, Vice President Carrier Customer Account pada periode 2012-2014, serta Head of Commercial and Sales - Carrier Customer Account tahun 2005-2012.

Pada tahap awal kariernya, Beliau pernah menjabat sebagai Senior Network Architect - Carrier Customer di Fujitsu Australia Pty Ltd or Ericsson Australia Pty Ltd untuk periode 2000-2005, di Sydney dan Melbourne, Australia. Mengawali karier profesionalnya sebagai Senior Transmission Engineer di PT NEC Indonesia pada 1996-2000.

Bapak Shannedy Ong meraih gelar Sarjana Teknik Elektro dan Komputer dari University of Technology Queensland, Brisbane, Australia, pada 1996.

Bapak Shannedy Ong tidak memiliki hubungan afiliasi dengan anggota Dewan Komisaris, anggota Direksi lainnya, maupun dengan pemegang saham utama dan pengendali Perseroan.

Mr. Shannedy Ong, 51 years old, an Indonesian citizen, is domiciled in Jakarta and was first appointed as Director of the Company based on the resolution of the Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders convened on December 5, 2024. He also serves as the Company's Corporate Secretary since December 2, 2024, based on the Decree of the BoD No. 017/SK-DIR/SSD/XI/2024.

In addition to his role as the Director of the Company, currently he has served as President Director of PT Investasi Jaringan Nusantara since 2024, and President Director of PT Telemedia Komunikasi Pratama since 2025, which are subsidiaries of the Company.

Mr. Shannedy Ong is an expert in the telecommunications and technology sector, with a strong background in network engineering, commercial leadership, and strategic account management. He served as Country Managing Director of Qualcomm in Jakarta from 2015 to 2024. Prior to that, he held several senior leadership roles at Ericsson Indonesia, including Vice President of New Business from 2014 to 2015, Vice President of Carrier Customer Account from 2012 to 2014, and Head of Commercial and Sales - Carrier Customer Account from 2005 to 2012.

Earlier in her career, he worked as a Senior Network Architect - Carrier Customer at Fujitsu Australia Pty Ltd or Ericsson Australia Pty Ltd from 2000 to 2005, based in Sydney and Melbourne, Australia. He began his professional career as a Senior Transmission Engineer at PT NEC Indonesia from 1996 to 2000.

Mr. Shannedy Ong earned a Bachelor's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Technology Queensland, Brisbane, Australia, in 1996.

Mr. Shannedy Ong has no affiliation with other members of the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, nor any affiliation with the Company's major and controlling shareholders.
