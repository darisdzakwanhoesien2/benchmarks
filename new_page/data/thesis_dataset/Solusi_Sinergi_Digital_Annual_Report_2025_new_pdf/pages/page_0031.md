# Laporan Dewan Komisaris
## Report of The Board of Commissioners

## PELAKSANAAN FUNGSI PENGAWASAN

Sepanjang tahun 2025, Dewan Komisaris telah menjalankan fungsi pengawasan sesuai dengan ketentuan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku serta kerangka tata kelola Perseroan.

Dewan Komisaris secara rutin menyelenggarakan rapat-rapat untuk menelaah kinerja operasional Perseroan, kondisi keuangan, praktik manajemen risiko, serta berbagai inisiatif strategis yang dijalankan manajemen. Rapat gabungan dengan Direksi juga dilaksanakan secara berkala untuk memastikan keselarasan antara fungsi pengawasan dan pelaksanaan strategi oleh manajemen.

Aspek Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) juga secara berkala dibahas untuk memastikan bahwa prinsip keberlanjutan telah terintegrasi dalam perencanaan strategis dan pengambilan keputusan operasional Perseroan.

## EXECUTION OF SUPERVISORY RESPONSIBILITIES

Throughout 2025, the Board of Commissioners performed its supervisory function in accordance with applicable regulations and the Company's governance framework.

The Board of Commissioners conducted regular meetings to review the Company's operational performance, financial condition, risk management practices, and strategic initiatives. Joint meetings with the Board of Directors were also held to ensure alignment between supervisory oversight and management execution.

Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) considerations were also regularly discussed, ensuring that sustainability principles remain integrated within the Company's strategic planning and operational decision-making.

Dalam menjalankan tugas-tugasnya, Dewan Komisaris didukung oleh Komite Audit serta Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi. Komite Audit bertugas mengawasi integritas pelaporan keuangan, sistem pengendalian internal, kepatuhan, serta manajemen risiko perusahaan. Sementara itu, Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi memberikan rekomendasi terkait struktur tata kelola, perencanaan suksesi, serta kebijakan remunerasi.

In carrying out its duties, the Board of Commissioners was supported by the Audit Committee and the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. The Audit Committee oversees financial reporting integrity, internal control systems, compliance, and enterprise risk management. Meanwhile, the Nomination and Remuneration Committee provides recommendations regarding governance structure, succession planning, and remuneration policies.
