# Organ di Bawah Direksi

Bodies Under the BoD

Hingga akhir tahun 2025, Direksi belum membentuk Komite di bawah Direksi. Meskipun demikian, dalam rangka mendukung pelaksanaan tugas, fungsi, dan tanggung jawab Direksi secara efektif dan sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku, Direksi senantiasa didukung oleh Sekretaris Perusahaan dan Unit Audit Internal. Sekretaris Perusahaan berperan dalam memastikan kepatuhan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan serta kelancaran komunikasi perusahaan, sementara Unit Audit Internal memberikan dukungan melalui fungsi pengawasan dan pengendalian internal untuk memastikan penerapan tata kelola perusahaan yang baik.

Until end of 2025, no committee had been established under the BoD. Nevertheless, in carrying out its duties, functions, and responsibilities, the BoD is supported by the Corporate Secretary and the Internal Audit Unit. The Corporate Secretary plays a key role in ensuring compliance with applicable laws and regulations as well as facilitating effective corporate communication, while the Internal Audit Unit provides independent assurance through its oversight and internal control functions to support the implementation of good corporate governance.
