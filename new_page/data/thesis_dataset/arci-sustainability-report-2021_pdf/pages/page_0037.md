# Tata Kelola yang Baik

Good Corporate Governance

![img-45.jpeg](img-45.jpeg)

Pelaksanaan tata kelola perusahaan yang dijalankan Archi mengandung prinsip-prinsip keterbukaan (transparency), akuntabilitas (accountability), responsibilitas (responsibility), independensi (independency), dan kewajaran dan kesetaraan (fairness) menjadi kekuatan perusahaan menuju keberlanjutan.

The implementation of corporate governance by Archi contains the principles of transparency, accountability, responsibility, independence, and fairness and equality which is a strength for the Company towards sustainability.
