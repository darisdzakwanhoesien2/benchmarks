Sustainability Report

Pada tahun 2024, sebanyak 1.146 karyawan mengikuti berbagai inisiatif pengembangan, dengan rata-rata durasi pembelajaran mencapai 19 jam per individu. Topik-topik utama yang diselenggarakan mencakup Sales Management, Customer Relationship Management, serta Production &amp; Technical Improvement, yang disesuaikan dengan kebutuhan karyawan. Aktivitas ini bertujuan mendukung operasional sehari-hari sekaligus memperkuat daya saing karyawan untuk menghadapi tantangan di masa depan.

Dalam upaya memperkuat keterampilan teknis dan manajerial di seluruh jenjang organisasi, Perseroan terus mengembangkan Training Academy sebagai program pengembangan utama. Jika sebelumnya program ini difokuskan pada Kepala Filling Station, kini cakupannya diperluas hingga level staff hingga manajer, memastikan setiap individu memiliki kompetensi yang relevan untuk mendukung operasional bisnis yang optimal. Sebagai bagian dari Training Academy, Perseroan juga fokus mengembangkan Subject Matter Expert (SME) Internal.

Selain itu, Perseroan memfasilitasi pelatihan bersertifikasi untuk kebutuhan spesifik, seperti Training K3 dan Sertifikat Angkut Produk B3 yang diwajibkan bagi sopir distribusi. Inisiatif ini tidak hanya bertujuan meningkatkan kompetensi individu, tetapi juga memastikan kepatuhan terhadap standar keselamatan dan regulasi yang berlaku.

In 2024, 1,146 employees participated in various development initiatives, with an average learning duration of 19 hours per individual. The main topics include Sales Management, Customer Relationship Management, and Production &amp; Technical Improvement, which are adjusted with employee needs. This activity has the purpose to support daily operations and improve employee competitiveness in dealing with future challenges.

In order to strengthen technical and managerial skills at all levels of the organization, the Company continues to develop the Training Academy as a main development program. If previously this program was focused on the Head of Filling Station, now its scope has been expanded to the staff level to managers, ensuring that each individual has relevant competencies to support optimal business operations. As part of the Training Academy, the Company also focuses on developing Internal Subject Matter Experts (SME).

In addition, the Company facilitates certified training for specific needs, such as OHS Training and B3 Product Transport Certificates required for distribution drivers. This initiative not only has the purpose to improve individual competency, but also ensures compliance with applicable safety standards and regulations.
