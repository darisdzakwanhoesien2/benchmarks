## Riwayat Pendidikan

Beliau meraih gelar Sarjana Ekonomi dari Universitas Islam
Indonesia Yogyakarta (1995) dan Program Pasca Sarjana
di Universitas Brawijaya Malang (2014).

## Riwayat Jabatan

Beliau mengawali kariernya sebagai Staf Auditor, Verifikasi,
Evaluasi, dan Pengendalian Transaksi Keuangan Bank
Danamon Indonesia (1996-2000). Selain itu, beliau merupakan
salah satu pendiri Lembaga Swadaya Masyarakat "ADEMOS"
atau Himpunan Demokrasi dan Kesejahteraan Sosial yang
bergerak di bidang sosial, pendidikan, pemberdayaan ekonomi
berkelanjutan, dan ekonomi kreatif, serta berpengalaman
sebagai Ketua Komisi Pemilihan Umum (KPU) Bojonegoro
(2014-2019).

## Rangkap Jabatan

Per 31 Desember 2024, beliau terlibat dalam Dewan Pengarah
Program Ekonomi Kreatif di Bojonegoro sebagai Moderator
dan Narasumber Industri Kreatif. Beliau juga menjabat sebagai
Direktur PT Samator LNG dan PT Samator Wase Gas.

## Hubungan Afiliasi

Beliau tidak memiliki hubungan afiliasi dengan anggota
Dewan Komisaris lainnya, anggota Direksi, serta Pemegang
Saham Utama dan/atau Pengendali.

## Educational Background

He holds a degree in Economics from the Islamic University of
Indonesia, Yogyakarta (1995), and a Postgraduate Degree from
Brawijaya University, Malang (2014).

## Career Background

He started his career as an Auditor, Verification, Evaluation,
and Control of Financial Transactions Staff at Bank Danamon
Indonesia (1996-2000). He is also one of the founders of the
Non-Governmental Organization "ADEMOS" or the Democracy
and Social Welfare Association, which is engaged in social,
educational, sustainable economic empowerment, and creative
economy. He also has experience as the Chairman of the
Bojonegoro Regional Election Commission (KPU Bojonegoro)
(2014-2019).

## Concurrent Position

As of December 31, 2024, he is also involved in the Steering
Committee for the Creative Economy Program in Bojonegoro
Moderator and Human Resources. He also serves as a Director
at PT Samator LNG and PT Samator Wase Gas.

## Affiliations

He has no affiliate relationship with other members of the
Board of Commissioners, members of the Board of Directors,
as well as Main and/or Controlling Shareholders.
