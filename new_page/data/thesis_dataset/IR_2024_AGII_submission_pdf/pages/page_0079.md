Dasar Hukum Pengangkatan Legal Basis of Appointment

Menjabat sebagai Komisaris Utama Perseroan sejak 2021 dan diangkat kembali berdasarkan Akta Pernyataan Keputusan Rapat No. 51 tertanggal 27 Juli 2022 yang dibuat di hadapan Fathiah Helmi, SH, Notaris di Jakarta, dan telah mendapatkan surat pemberitahuan dari Menteri Hukum dan HAM Republik Indonesia tertanggal 22 Agustus 2022.

Serves as the President Commissioner since 2021 and was reappointed based on the Meeting Decree No. 51 dated July 27, 2022, made before Fathiah Helmi, SH, Notary at Jakarta, and has received a notification letter from the Minister of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia dated August 22, 2022.

# Riwayat Pendidikan

Beliau menyelesaikan Pendidikan Sekolah Menengah Atas (SMA) di Petra 2 Surabaya. Pada 2015, beliau meraih gelar dalam Program Pengembangan Eksekutif bernama "Strategic Action Program" yang diadakan oleh Penelitian dan Pelatihan Ekonomik dan Bisnis (P2EB) Universitas Gadjah Mada Yogyakarta. Beliau juga aktif mengikuti konferensi nasional dan internasional di bidang manajemen, gas industri, dan gas medis.

# Riwayat Jabatan

Sebelum menjabat sebagai Komisaris Utama, beliau pernah mengemban tanggung jawab sebagai Direktur Utama Perseroan (2006-2018) dan Wakil Komisaris Utama Perseroan (2018-2021). Beliau juga pernah menjabat sebagai Direktur Utama PT Krakatau Samator (2013-2021). Lebih lanjut, beliau cukup aktif dalam beberapa organisasi, di antaranya menjadi Ketua II Pembinaan dan Prestasi (Binpres) dan Marketing dalam Persatuan Bola Voli Indonesia (PBVSI) (2021-2023), serta Anggota Dewan Pembina dalam Asosiasi Gas Industri Indonesia.

# Rangkap Jabatan

Per 31 Desember 2024, beliau menjabat sebagai Komisaris Utama PT Samator, PT Samabayu Mandala, dan PT Sandana; Komisaris PT Samator Gas Industri, PT Aneka Mega Energi, dan PT Ruci Gas; serta Direktur Utama PT Arohera, PT Samator Land, dan PT Samator LNG. Beliau juga menjabat sebagai Zonal Vice President Representing Southeastern Asian Volleyball Confederation (AVC), anggota Komisi Pengembangan Fédération Internationale de Volleyball (FIVB), serta Sekretaris Jenderal PBVSI (sejak 2023).

# Hubungan Afiliasi

Beliau memiliki hubungan afiliasi dengan anggota Dewan Komisaris lainnya, anggota Direksi, serta Pemegang Saham Utama dan/atau Pengendali.

# Educational Background

He completed his high school education at Petra 2 High School in Surabaya. In 2015, he received a degree for the Executive Development Program called "Strategic Action Program" organized by P2EB Gadjah Mada University in Yogyakarta. He also actively participates in national and international conferences in management, industrial gas, and medical gas sector.

# Career Background

Before serving the Company as the President Commissioner, he was Vice President Commissioner (2018-2021) and the President Director (2006-2018). He once served as the President Director of PT Krakatau Samator (2013-2021). In addition, he has been actively involved in several organizations, including serving as Vice Chairman II for Development and Performance (Binpres) and Marketing at the Indonesian Volleyball Association (PBVSI) (2021-2023), as well as member of the Advisory Board at the Indonesian Industrial Gas Association.

# Concurrent Position

As of December 31, 2024, he serves as President Commissioner of PT Samator, PT Samabayu Mandala, and PT Sandana; Commissioner of PT Samator Gas Industri, PT Aneka Mega Energi, and PT Ruci Gas; as well as the President Director of PT Arohera, PT Samator Land, and PT Samator LNG. He also serves as the Zonal Vice President representing the Southeastern Asian Volleyball Confederation (AVC), a member of the Development Commission of the Fédération Internationale de Volleyball (FIVB), and the General Secretary of PBVSI (since 2023).

# Affiliations

He is affiliated with other members of the Board of Commissioners, members of the Board of Directors as well as Main and Controlling Shareholders.
