# Apresiasi

Tantangan yang dihadapi sepanjang tahun 2024 telah memberikan pelajaran berharga bagi Perseroan dalam memperkuat daya saing dan keberlanjutan usaha. Dewan Komisaris mengapresiasi dedikasi Direksi dan seluruh jajaran yang telah menjalankan strategi secara optimal, serta menyampaikan terima kasih kepada Pemegang Saham atas kepercayaan yang diberikan. Penghargaan juga disampaikan kepada seluruh pemangku kepentingan atas dukungan yang senantiasa menguatkan fondasi pertumbuhan Perseroan. Dewan Komisaris mengharapkan agar sinergi yang telah terjalin dapat terus diperkuat untuk memastikan keberlanjutan nilai bagi seluruh pihak di masa mendatang.

# Appreciation

The challenges faced throughout 2024 provided valuable lessons for the Company in terms of strengthening competitiveness and business sustainability. The Board of Commissioners would like to appreciate the dedication of the Board of Directors and all management levels who optimally implemented the strategy, and expresses gratitude to the Shareholders for their trust in the Company. We would also like to express our appreciation to all stakeholders for their support, which invariably enhances the foundation of the Company's growth. The Board of Commissioners hopes that the synergy that has been established will continue to build, so as to ensure the sustainability of value for all parties in the future.
