## Keberagaman dan Kesetaraan (POJK F.18)

Perseroan menegaskan komitmen terhadap keberagaman dan kesetaraan di lingkungan kerja melalui penerapan prinsip anti-diskriminasi yang tercantum dalam Kode Etik. Kebijakan ini memastikan seluruh karyawan diperlakukan secara adil tanpa diskriminasi berdasarkan gender, suku, agama, ras, status sosial, maupun latar belakang lainnya. Implementasi kebijakan ini mencakup seluruh aspek pengelolaan SDM, seperti rekrutmen, pengembangan kompetensi, dan pemberian hak-hak kerja yang didasarkan pada kinerja dan kemampuan individu.

Sebagai wujud penghormatan terhadap hak asasi manusia, Perseroan menjunjung tinggi kesetaraan di tempat kerja yang selaras dengan Undang-Undang Ketenagakerjaan serta Konvensi International Labour Organization (ILO) tentang Discrimination in Employment and Occupation. Komitmen ini tidak hanya mendukung pengembangan karyawan secara individual tetapi juga berkontribusi terhadap pembangunan sosial yang berkelanjutan. [GRI 3-3] [ESG S-09]

Sebagai langkah untuk menjaga lingkungan kerja yang inklusif, Perseroan menyediakan mekanisme pelaporan melalui Sistem Pelaporan Pelanggaran (SPP) yang memungkinkan penanganan dugaan diskriminasi secara transparan. Selama tahun 2024, tidak ada laporan diskriminasi yang diterima, mencerminkan efektivitas langkah-langkah Perseroan dalam menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang kondusif dan inklusif. Hal ini mendukung karyawan dalam mengembangkan potensinya secara optimal. [GRI 406-1] [ESG S-07]

Komitmen terhadap keberagaman dan kesetaraan tercermin dalam data demografi karyawan. Pada tahun 2024, Perseroan memiliki 1.968 karyawan, mengalami penurunan dari 2.082 karyawan pada tahun sebelumnya.

## Diversity and Equality (POJK F.18)

The Company affirms its commitment to diversity and equality at workplace by applying anti-discrimination principles as specified in the Code of Conduct. This policy ensures that all employees are treated fairly without discrimination based on gender, ethnicity, religion, race, social status, or other backgrounds. The implementation of this policy covers all aspects of HR management, such as recruitment, competency development, and granting work rights based on individual performance and abilities.

As a form of respect for human rights, the Company upholds equality in the workplace in line with the Manpower Law and the International Labor Organization (ILO) Convention on Discrimination in Employment and Occupation. This commitment not only supports individual employee development but also contributes to sustainable social development. [GRI 3-3] [ESG S-09]

In order to maintain an inclusive work environment, the Company provides a reporting mechanism through the Whistleblowing System which allows for transparent handling of alleged discrimination. During 2024, no reports of discrimination were received, reflecting the effectiveness of the Company's measures in creating a conducive and inclusive work environment. This supports employees in developing their potential optimally. [GRI 406-1] [ESG S-07]

The commitment to diversity and equality is reflected in employee demographic data. In 2024, the Company had 1,968 employees or decreased than 2,082 employees recorded in the previous year.
