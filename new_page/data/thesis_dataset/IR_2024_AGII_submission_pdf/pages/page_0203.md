## Kebijakan dan Prosedur Penetapan Remunerasi
[GRI 2-19, 2-20]

Proses remunerasi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi Perseroan merupakan wewenang dari Fungsi Remunerasi yang dijalankan oleh Dewan Komisaris. Dalam menetapkan besaran remunerasi, Dewan Komisaris mengajukan usulan dalam rapat, dengan mempertimbangkan kinerja organ utama, pencapaian target, kondisi keuangan internal, beban tugas dan tanggung jawab, serta standar remunerasi di industri sejenis. Setelah usulan disepakati, hasilnya disampaikan kepada Pemegang Saham dalam RUPS untuk mendapatkan persetujuan.

Adapun prosedur penetapan remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi diungkapkan sebagai berikut.

1. Menyusun struktur remunerasi bagi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan/atau Direksi.

3. Menyusun besaran atas remunerasi bagi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan/atau Direksi.
4. Melakukan penilaian kinerja dengan kesesuaian remunerasi yang diterima masing-masing anggota Dewan Komisaris dan/atau Direksi.

## Remuneration Policy and Procedure [GRI 2-19, 2-20]

The remuneration process for members of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors falls under the authority of the Remuneration Function, which is carried out by the Board of Commissioners. In determining remuneration amount, the Board of Commissioners submits proposals during meetings, considering performance of key corporate organs, achievement of Company's targets, internal financial condition, workload and responsibilities, and industry remuneration standards. Once the proposal is agreed upon, the final decision is submitted to the Shareholders at the GMS for approval.

The remuneration determination procedures for the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors is described as follows.

1. Develop remuneration structure for members of the Board of Commissioners and/or Board of Directors.
2. Develop policy on remuneration for members of the Board of Commissioners and/or Board of Directors.
3. Develop the amount of remuneration for members of the Board of Commissioners and/or Board of Directors.
4. Conduct performance appraisals on the remuneration received by each member of the Board of Commissioners and/or Board of Directors.

## Remuneration Structure and Amount

The remuneration components for Board of Commissioners members consist of honorarium and allowances, while Board of Directors members receive salary and allowances. The remuneration percentage for the Board of Commissioners is as follows President Commissioner (10%), Vice President Commissioner (6%-8%), other Commissioners (2%-3%).
