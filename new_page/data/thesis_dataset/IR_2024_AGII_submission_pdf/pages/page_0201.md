# Penilaian Kinerja Direksi [GRI 2-18] [ESG G-04]

## Prosedur Penilaian

Penilaian kinerja Direksi dilakukan setiap tahun oleh Dewan Komisaris dengan menggunakan metode KPI yang telah ditetapkan. Proses ini bertujuan untuk mengukur pencapaian kinerja, mengevaluasi efektivitas kebijakan strategis, serta mengidentifikasi area yang perlu ditingkatkan. Hasil penilaian tersebut disampaikan kepada Pemegang Saham dalam RUPS untuk memastikan transparansi, akuntabilitas, dan perbaikan berkelanjutan dalam pengelolaan perusahaan.

## Pihak Penilai

Penilaian kinerja Direksi Perseroan dilakukan secara internal (self-assessment) yang dilaksanakan langsung oleh Dewan Komisaris. Selain itu, Pemegang Saham terlibat dalam penilaian kinerja Direksi melalui persetujuan atas Laporan Tahunan, Laporan Keuangan, dan keputusan strategis yang disampaikan dalam RUPS.

## Kriteria Penilaian

Kriteria penilaian Direksi mengacu pada:

1. Implementasi praktik GCG;
2. Kinerja secara keuangan, operasional, dan aspek-aspek lainnya yang berperan penting bagi keberlanjutan Perseroan;
3. Perbandingan antara target dan pencapaian aktual;
4. Keselarasan kinerja terhadap visi dan misi;
5. Strategi dan inovasi;
6. Pencapaian manajemen dalam meningkatkan nilai bagi Pemegang Saham; serta
7. Kinerja masing-masing Direktur secara individu.

## Hasil Penilaian

Direksi Perseroan telah melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya secara optimal sepanjang tahun 2024, yang tercermin dalam peningkatan kinerja operasional dan keuangan yang signifikan. Beberapa area yang perlu diperbaiki, seperti penguatan koordinasi antar departemen, telah diidentifikasi sebagai fokus utama untuk tahun mendatang.

# Board of Directors Performance Evaluation [GRI 2-18] [ESG G-04]

## Evaluation Procedure

The Board of Commissioners conducts an annual performance evaluation of the Board of Directors using predetermined Key Performance Indicators (KPIs). This process has a purpose to assess performance achievements, evaluate the effectiveness of strategic policies, and identify areas for improvement. The evaluation results are presented to the Shareholders at the GMS to ensure transparency, accountability, and continuous improvement in company management.

## Assessors

The performance evaluation of the Board of Directors is conducted internally (self-assessment) and carried out directly by the Board of Commissioners. Additionally, the Shareholders play a role in the evaluation process through their approval of the Annual Report, Financial Statements, and strategic decisions presented at the GMS.

## Evaluation Criteria

The Board of Directors' performance evaluation is based on the following criteria:

1. Implementation of GCG practices;
2. Financial, operational, and other key performance aspects essential for the Company's going concern;
3. Comparison between targets and actual achievements;
4. Alignment of performance with vision and mission;
5. Strategy and innovation initiatives;
6. Management's success in enhancing the Shareholders value; and
7. Individual performance of each Director.

## Evaluation Results

The Board of Directors has effectively fulfilled its duties and responsibilities throughout 2024, reflected in significant improvements in operational and financial performance. However, areas for improvement, such as enhancing interdepartmental coordination, have been identified as key focus areas for the upcoming year.

# Performance Assessment of Committees Under the Board of Directors

In 2024, the Company has not yet established any committees under the Board of Directors. However, the Board of Directors supervises several governance organs that support its functions, such as the Corporate Secretary and the Internal Audit Unit. Therefore, the Board of Directors perform an assessment the performance of these governance organs to ensure operational effectiveness and strong corporate governance.
