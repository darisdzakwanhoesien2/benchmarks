# Direksi Board of Directors

Direksi adalah organ perusahaan yang bertanggung jawab penuh atas pengurusan perusahaan untuk kepentingan dan tujuan Perusahaan. Direksi bertugas dan bertanggung jawab secara kolektif koligial dalam mengelola Perseroan demi tercapainya tujuan Perseroan dengan tetap tunda dan patuh terhadap setiap ketentuan yang berlaku. Masing-masing anggota Direksi melaksanakan tugas dan mengambil keputusan sesuai dengan pembagian tugas, wewenang, dan hal-hal lain yang terkait dengan jabatannya sesuai dengan Anggaran Dasar dan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku.

# Pedoman Kerja

Dalam melaksanakan tugas dan fungsinya, Direksi memiliki acuan kerja yang termuat dalam Pedoman Kerja Komisaris dan Direksi (Board Manual) yang ditandatangani oleh Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi. Board Manual menjadi acuan Direksi dalam melakukan pengambilan keputusan dan pelaksanaan tugas serta tanggung jawabnya. Evaluasi terhadap Piagam Direksi dilakukan secara berkala selaras dengan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku. Piagam Direksi terakhir kali diperbarui pada tanggal 17 April 2023.

Piagam Direksi mengatur hal-hal berikut.

1. Struktur Keanggotaan.
2. Masa Jabatan.
3. Persyaratan Umum.
4. Tugas, Tanggung Jawab, dan Wewenang.
5. Rapat.
6. Pelaporan dan Pertanggungjawaban.
7. Lain-lain (Rangkap Jabatan, Kepemilikan Saham, Nilai, Jam Kerja, dan Kode Etik Bisnis).

# Kriteria Penunjukan [ESG G-06]

Pemilihan anggota Direksi Perseroan mengacu pada Anggaran Dasar dan POJK 33/2014, dengan memperhatikan kriteria yang sesuai dengan kondisi dan kebutuhan Perseroan, sebagai berikut.

1. Mempunyai akhlak, moral, dan integritas yang baik.
2. Cakap melakukan perbuatan hukum.
3. Dalam 5 tahun sebelum pengangkatan dan selama menjabat tidak pernah:

a. Dinyatakan pailit;
b. Dinyatakan bersalah menyebabkan suatu Perseroan dinyatakan pailit;

The Board of Directors is the company's organ that fully responsible for managing the company in the Company's interests and objectives. The Board of Directors collectively and collegially carries out its duties and responsibilities to ensure the Company's goals are met while complying with all applicable laws and regulations. Each member of the Board of Directors performs their duties and makes decisions according to their designated roles, authorities, and responsibilities as outlined in the Articles of Association and applicable laws and regulations.

# Work Guidelines

In carrying out its duties and functions, the Board of Directors follows a Board Manual, which serves as a reference for decision-making and the execution of their responsibilities. This manual, signed by both the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors, ensures alignment in governance practices. The Board Manual is regularly reviewed and updated in accordance with prevailing laws and regulations. The most recent update was made on April 17, 2023.

The Board Manual covers the following aspects.

1. Membership Structure.
2. Term of Office.
3. General Requirements.
4. Duties, Responsibilities, and Authorities.
5. Meetings.
6. Reporting and Accountability.
7. Other Provisions (Concurrent Positions, Share Ownership, Values, Working Hours, and Business Code of Ethics).

# Appointment Criteria [ESG G-06]

The selection of the Board of Directors members refers to the Articles of Association and POJK 33/2014, taking into account criteria that align with the Company's conditions and needs, as follows.

1. Have good character, moral, and integrity.
2. Competent in performing legal acts.
3. During the past 5 years prior to his appointment and his tenure:

a. Has never been declared bankrupt;
b. Has never been declared guilty of causing a company bankruptcy;
