# Layanan Kesehatan Kerja [GRI 403-3]

Perseroan menyediakan fasilitas kesehatan berupa pemeriksaan kesehatan rutin bagi seluruh karyawan. Pemeriksaan ini disesuaikan dengan jenis pekerjaan, di mana pekerja kantor dan pekerja produksi menjalani pemeriksaan berdasarkan risiko dan bahaya kerja masing-masing. Hasil pemeriksaan dianalisis oleh dokter perusahaan, dikonsultasikan dengan karyawan terkait, dan ditinjau oleh Koordinator Kesehatan Kerja serta Departemen SDM.

Selain pemeriksaan kesehatan, Perseroan juga menawarkan layanan kesehatan lainnya, seperti:

1. Program BPJS Ketenagakerjaan; serta
2. Asuransi kesehatan dan rumah sakit yang mencakup seluruh karyawan beserta keluarganya.

# Promosi Kesehatan Kerja [GRI 403-4]

Unit HSE bersama Departemen SDM telah menginisiasi berbagai program untuk meningkatkan pemahaman terkait K3 di lingkungan kerja serta mendorong penerapan gaya hidup sehat di kalangan karyawan. Pada tahun 2024, kegiatan yang dilaksanakan meliputi:

1. Training Awareness tentang Ergonomi di Tempat Kerja
Pelatihan ini bertujuan membantu karyawan mengidentifikasi faktor risiko di lingkungan kerja serta menerapkan strategi dan teknik untuk mengurangi risiko cedera saat bekerja.

# Work Health Service [GRI 403-3]

The Company provides health facilities in the form of periodic medical check-ups for all employees. This medical check-up is tailored to the type of work, where office workers and production workers undergo examination based on their respective occupational risks and dangers. The results of the examination are analyzed by the Company's doctor, discussed with the concerned employees, and reviewed by the Occupational Health Coordinator and HR Department.

In addition to medical check-ups, the Company also offers other health services, such as:

1. BPJS Employment Program; and
2. Health insurance and hospital for all employees and their families.

# Work Health Promotion [GRI 403-4]

The HSE Unit with the HR Department has initiated various programs to increase awareness related to OHS in the work environment and promote the implementation of a healthy lifestyle among employees. In 2024, the activities carried out include:

1. Training Awareness about Ergonomics in the Workplace
This training aims to help workers identify risk factors in the workplace and apply strategies and techniques to reduce the risk of injury while on the job.
