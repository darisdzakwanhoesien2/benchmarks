# Pelaksanaan Tugas

The Internal Audit Unit has carried out its duties and responsibilities effectively and efficiently throughout 2024, including conducting internal audits in coordination with the Audit Committee; attending joint meetings with the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, and the Audit Committee; conducting training activities; and resolving 3 cases reported through the whistleblowing channel.

# Tata Kelola Teknologi Informasi

## Information Technology Governance

Perseroan membentuk sistem tata kelola teknologi informasi (TI) yang bertujuan meningkatkan kinerja proses bisnis. Selain itu, TI memiliki peran penting dalam melindungi informasi dan data Perseroan dari ancaman keamanan serta memastikan kepatuhan terhadap peraturan yang berlaku.

## Penerapan TI

Sepanjang tahun 2024, Perseroan telah melakukan pengembangan terhadap penerapan TI, di antaranya:

1. Menjalankan Go-Live implementasi sistem ERP Epicor - Phase 1 di Perseroan dan Entitas Anak, yang terhubung ke berbagai sistem teknologi pendukung kegiatan operasional, termasuk antara lain Cylinder Tracking System, Automatic Fleet Weighbridge System, serta otomasi pelaporan kegiatan plant melalui aplikasi Distributed Control System (DCS);
2. Menerapkan aplikasi Great Day sebagai platform karyawan untuk mengakomodasi kebutuhan dan pemenuhan informasi ketenagakerjaan, antara lain presensi, pengajuan cuti, dan benefit karyawan lainnya yang dapat diakses secara mobile melalui smartphone;
3. Memperluas penerapan Sistem Customer Reorder System melalui Teknologi Tank Telemetry (IoT) hingga mencakup lebih dari 1.100 tangki pelanggan skala besar; serta
4. Menginisiasi dan merilis kebijakan Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Informasi (SMKI) berbasis ISO 27001 serta Standar Operasional Prosedur (SOP) untuk memperkuat perlindungan aset informasi dan memastikan kepatuhan terhadap standar keamanan global. Untuk mendukung pemahaman dan implementasinya, Perseroan juga telah melakukan sosialisasi melalui brosur informatif dan sesi daring.

Selain itu, Perseroan menerapkan langkah-langkah untuk menghadapi gangguan, meningkatkan keamanan siber, dan mendukung pemulihan bencana guna memastikan seluruh

The Company established an Information Technology (IT) governance system with a purpose to improve business process performance. In addition, IT plays a crucial role in protecting the Company's information and data from security threats and ensuring compliance with applicable regulations.

## IT Implementation

Over the course of 2024, the Company has enhanced its IT implementation, including:

1. Ran the Go-Live implementation of the ERP Epicor system – Phase 1 in the Company and Subsidiaries, integrating with various operational support technologies, such as the Cylinder Tracking System, Automatic Fleet Weighbridge System, and automated plant activity reporting through the Distributed Control System (DCS) application;
2. Implementation of the Great Day application as an employee platform to facilitate workforce needs and access to employment-related information, such as attendance recording, leave applications, and employee benefits, accessible via mobile devices;
3. Expansion of the Customer Reorder System implementation using Tank Telemetry Technology (IoT), now covering over 1,100 large-scale customer tanks; and
4. Initiation and rollout of an Information Security Management System (ISMS) policy based on ISO 27001, along with Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) to strengthen information asset protection and ensure compliance with global security standards. To support understanding and implementation, the Company has conducted dissemination through informative brochures and online sessions.
