## Pelatihan

Pelatihan Unit Audit Internal bertujuan untuk meningkatkan kompetensi serta pemahaman anggota mengenai berbagai aspek, seperti tata kelola perusahaan yang baik, peraturan yang berlaku, standar industri, dan isu keberlanjutan. Pelatihan ini dapat dilakukan melalui berbagai metode, baik yang diselenggarakan oleh pihak internal Perseroan, pihak ketiga, maupun secara mandiri. Selama tahun 2024, Unit Audit Internal telah mengikuti berbagai pelatihan, yang akan diuraikan sebagai berikut.

## Training

The training for the Internal Audit Unit has the purpose to improve the competencies and understanding of its members on various aspects, such as good corporate governance, applicable regulations, industry standards, and sustainability issues. This training can be conducted through various methods, whether organized by the Company's internal parties, third parties, or independently. Throughout 2024, the Internal Audit Unit participated in various training sessions, which will be presented as follows.
