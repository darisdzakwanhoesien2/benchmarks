# Pernyataan dan Praktik Bad Corporate Governance

## Statement and Practices of Bad Corporate Governance

Perseroan menegaskan bahwa tidak ada tindakan atau kebijakan yang mencerminkan praktik tata kelola perusahaan yang buruk (bad corporate governance), sebagai wujud komitmen terhadap penerapan prinsip-prinsip GCG secara konsisten.

The Company affirms that there are no actions or policies that reflect bad corporate governance practices, as a manifestation of its commitment to consistently apply the GCG principles.
