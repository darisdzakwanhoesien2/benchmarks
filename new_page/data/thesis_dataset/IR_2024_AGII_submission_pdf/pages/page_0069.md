# Visi, Misi, dan Tata Nilai Perseroan [POJK C.1]
## Company Vision, Mission, and Values

Perseroan telah menyusun dan menetapkan visi, misi, serta tata nilai perusahaan dengan mengintegrasikan prinsip keberlanjutan. Langkah ini diambil untuk memastikan keseimbangan kinerja dalam aspek ekonomi, sosial, dan lingkungan, sekaligus memperkuat komitmen dalam menerapkan praktik terbaik good corporate governance (GCG).

The Company has developed and established its vision, mission, and values by integrating sustainability principles. This step was taken to ensure a balanced performance in the economic, social, and environmental aspects, while also reinforcing its commitment to implementing good corporate governance (GCG) best practices.

## Review on Vision and Mission
