# Bidang Usaha [GRI 2-6] [POJK C.4] Line of Business

## Kegiatan Usaha Berdasarkan Anggaran Dasar

Perseroan menjalankan kegiatan usaha berdasarkan Anggaran Dasar, dengan fokus utama pada produksi, pemasaran, dan penjualan berbagai jenis gas untuk industri serta produk terkait di Indonesia, baik dalam bentuk gas cair maupun gas padat. Untuk mendukung kegiatan usaha utama tersebut, Perseroan juga menjalankan kegiatan penunjang, antara lain menyediakan jasa terkait produk gas yang dihasilkan, seperti memperdagangkan produk gas dari produsen lain kepada pelanggan, termasuk specialty gas dan rare gas. Selain itu, Perseroan merancang konstruksi dan instalasi peralatan gas, serta menawarkan produk terkait lainnya kepada pabrik dan rumah sakit. Seluruh kegiatan usaha tersebut telah dijalankan oleh Perseroan selama tahun 2024 sesuai dengan ketentuan dalam Anggaran Dasar.

Sebagai bagian dari pengembangan usaha, pada tahun 2024 Perseroan menambahkan Klasifikasi Baku Lapangan Usaha Indonesia (KBLI) 43223, yang mencakup kegiatan instalasi minyak dan gas. Penambahan ini memperluas ruang lingkup kegiatan Perseroan, khususnya dalam layanan konstruksi, pemasangan, dan pengelolaan infrastruktur gas, sehingga mendukung kebutuhan pelanggan di sektor energi dan industri yang terus berkembang.

## Line of Business Based on Articles of Association

The Company engages its business activities based on its Articles of Association, with a primary focus on the production, marketing, and selling of wide range of gases for the industry and associated products in Indonesia, both in the form of liquid and solid gases. To support these core activities, the Company also engages in providing supporting services, including services related to the manufactured gas products such as trading gas products from other manufacturers to the Company's customers, including specialty gases and rare gases. In addition, the Company also designs the construction and installation of gas equipment and offers other related products to factories and hospitals. All of these business activities have been carried out by the Company during 2024 in accordance with the provisions of the Articles of Association.

As part of its business development, in 2024 the Company added the Indonesian Standard Business Classification (KBLI) 43223, which covers oil and gas installation activities. This addition expands the scope of the Company's activities, particularly in the construction, installation, and management of gas infrastructure services, thereby supporting the customer's needs in the continually evolving energy and industrial sectors.
