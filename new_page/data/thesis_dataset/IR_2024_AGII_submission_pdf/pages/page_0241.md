# Membangun Budaya Keberlanjutan [POJK F.1]

## Building a Sustainability Culture

Perseroan secara konsisten mengembangkan Budaya Keberlanjutan sebagai dasar utama dalam upaya mencapai visi dan misi perusahaan. Implementasi Budaya Keberlanjutan ini berfokus pada Tata Nilai Perseroan, yakni accountable (akuntabel), resilient (ketangguhan), innovative (inovatif), entrepreneurial (wirausaha), serta fun &amp; fearless (menikmati tantangan itu menyenangkan).

Budaya Keberlanjutan di Perseroan diinternalisasikan secara berkesinambungan melalui berbagai program dan kegiatan perusahaan. Hal ini memastikan bahwa penerapan strategi keberlanjutan dapat dilakukan secara konsisten di seluruh aspek operasional.

The Company consistently develops a Sustainability Culture as the main foundation in its efforts to achieve its vision and mission. The implementation of this Sustainability Culture focuses on the Company's Core Values, namely accountable, resilient, innovative, entrepreneurial, as well as fun &amp; fearless.

The Sustainability Culture is continuously internalized within the Company through a number of corporate programs and activities. This ensures that the implementation of sustainability strategies is carried out consistently across all operational aspects.

Diseminasi Budaya Keberlanjutan dilakukan kepada seluruh pemangku kepentingan, terutama karyawan, dimulai sejak proses rekrutmen dan diterapkan dalam segala aspek kegiatan sehari-hari.

Pada tahun 2024, Perseroan berhasil melibatkan 945 karyawan dalam pelatihan yang berkaitan dengan keberlanjutan. Selain itu, Perseroan juga mengadakan beberapa kegiatan internalisasi Budaya Keberlanjutan, antara lain:

1. Melaksanakan inisiatif pemilahan sampah untuk memudahkan pengolahan sebagai bagian dari kampanye pengurangan plastik Perseroan;
2. Menerapkan digitalisasi dalam proses operasional untuk mengurangi penggunaan kertas serta mendorong karyawan membawa tumbler dan/atau kotak makan sendiri sebagai pengganti botol plastik sekali pakai dan styrofoam guna mendukung inisiatif pengurangan plastik;
3. Menempatkan tanda/rambu untuk mengingatkan karyawan tentang inisiatif penghematan air dan listrik. Selain itu, di Kantor Pusat Surabaya juga diterapkan pemadaman AC setiap pukul 17.30 WIB;

The dissemination of the Sustainability Culture is directed toward all stakeholders, particularly employees, starting from the recruitment process and applied in all aspects of daily activities.

In 2024, the Company successfully engaged 945 employees in sustainability-related training. Additionally, the Company conducted several Sustainability Culture internalization initiatives, including:

1. Implemented a waste sorting initiative to facilitate waste processing as part of the Company's plastic reduction campaign;
2. Implemented digitalization operational processes to reduce paper usage and encouraging employees to bring their own tumblers and/or lunch boxes as substitutes for single-use plastic bottles and styrofoam, supporting the plastic reduction initiative;
3. Providing visual warnings to alert employees to conserve water and electricity. Additionally, at the Surabaya Head Office, air conditioning is turned off daily at 17:30 WIB;
