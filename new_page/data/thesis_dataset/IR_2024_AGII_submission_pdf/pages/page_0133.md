a. Pekanbaru, di mana telah melakukan renovasi serta perluasan area panggung pengisian;

The Company's expansion activities:

1. Expansion of new depo and filling stations in:
a. Pekanbaru, where renovations and expansion of the filling stage area have been completed;
b. Morowali, which equipped with oxygen and carbon dioxide gas filling units; and
c. Depo in Sumbawa, Muara Teweh and Kintap.
