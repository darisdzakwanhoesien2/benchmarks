# Kebijakan Kepemilikan Saham Perusahaan oleh Dewan Komisaris dan/atau Direksi

Company Share Ownership Policy by the Board of Commissioners and/or Board of Directors

Perseroan senantiasa mematuhi peraturan terkait pengungkapan informasi kepemilikan saham Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, dan Pemegang Saham dengan hak suara 5%, sebagaimana diatur dalam POJK 4/2024. Laporan terkait kepemilikan dan perubahan kepemilikan saham tersebut wajib disampaikan kepada Otoritas Jasa Keuangan dan Bursa Efek Indonesia selambat-lambatnya 5 hari kerja setelah terjadinya perubahan. Sejalan dengan peraturan ini, Perseroan telah menyampaikan laporan tepat waktu melalui e-reporting bulanan dengan judul "Laporan Bulanan Registrasi Pemegang Efek" dan mengunggah informasi tersebut di situs keterbukaan informasi BEI serta situs web Perseroan.

The Company always complies with regulations regarding the disclosure of share ownership by the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, and the Shareholders with 5% voting rights, as outlined in POJK 4/2024. Reports regarding share ownership and changes in ownership must be submitted to the Financial Services Authority and the Indonesia Stock Exchange no later than 5 working days after the change occurs. In accordance with this regulation, the Company has submitted timely reports through monthly e-reporting titled "Monthly Report on Securities Holder Registration" and uploaded the information on the IDX's public disclosure site as well as the Company's website.

# Larangan Transaksi Orang Dalam [ESG G-08] Insider Trading Prohibition

Perseroan belum memiliki kebijakan khusus terkait transaksi orang dalam (insider trading), namun ketentuan mengenai hal ini sudah diatur dalam Kode Etik Perseroan. Sebagai bagian dari komitmen untuk menjaga integritas dan transparansi, Perseroan melarang keras insider trading, yaitu penggunaan informasi material yang belum dipublikasikan untuk kepentingan pribadi, terutama dalam hal perdagangan saham.

Perseroan berupaya mencegah praktik tersebut dengan mengacu pada peraturan yang berlaku, seperti POJK 78/2017 dan Pasal 104 Undang-Undang No. 8 Tahun 1995 tentang Pasar Modal. Melalui penerapan peraturan tersebut, Perseroan berharap seluruh organ Perseroan, termasuk Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, dapat mematuhi ketentuan yang ada dan segera melaporkan setiap dugaan pelanggaran terkait insider trading.

The Company does not yet have a specific policy regarding insider trading; however, this matter is regulated in the Code of Conduct. As part of its commitment to maintaining integrity and transparency, the Company strictly prohibits insider trading, which is the use of unpublished material information for personal gain, especially in share trading.

The Company strives to prevent such practices by adhering to applicable regulations, such as POJK 78/2017 and Article 104 of Law No. 8 of 1995 concerning the Capital Market. Through the implementation of these regulations, the Company expects all of the Company's organs, including the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors, to comply with the existing provisions and promptly report any suspected violations related to insider trading.
