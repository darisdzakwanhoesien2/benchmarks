# Pernyataan Direksi dan/atau Dewan Komisaris atau Komite Audit atas Kecukupan Sistem Manajemen Risiko

Sepanjang tahun 2024, Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi telah melakukan peninjauan menyeluruh terhadap penerapan sistem manajemen risiko. Berdasarkan hasil peninjauan, sistem manajemen risiko dinilai telah diterapkan dengan efektif dan memadai dalam mengidentifikasi dan mengelola risiko yang berpotensi memengaruhi pencapaian tujuan Perseroan. Hal tersebut dibuktikan dengan tidak ditemukan adanya risiko yang berdampak signifikan, baik secara material maupun non-material. Meski demikian, Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi menyadari perlunya peningkatan berkelanjutan dalam pengelolaan risiko, khususnya dalam menghadapi tantangan dan ketidakpastian yang mungkin timbul di masa mendatang. Oleh karena itu, rekomendasi untuk memperkuat dan menyempurnakan sistem manajemen risiko akan terus dipertimbangkan dan diimplementasikan.

# Statement from the Board of Directors and/or Board of Commissioners or the Audit Committee on the Adequacy of the Risk Management System

Throughout 2024, the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors have conducted a thorough review of the implementation of the risk management system. Based on the review findings, the risk management system is considered to have been implemented effectively and adequately in identifying and managing risks that could potentially impact the achievement of the Company's goals. This is evidenced by the absence of significant risks, both material and non-material. However, the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors acknowledge the need for continuous improvement in risk management, especially in facing challenges and uncertainties that may arise in the future. Therefore, recommendations to strengthen and enhance the risk management system will continue to be considered and implemented.

# Litigasi dan Perkara Hukum [GRI 2-27] Litigations and Lawsuits

Sepanjang tahun 2024, tidak terdapat litigasi atau perkara hukum yang dihadapi oleh Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, manajemen, maupun karyawan Perseroan. Hal ini mencerminkan komitmen Perseroan terhadap praktik bisnis yang baik serta kepatuhan terhadap peraturan yang berlaku.

Throughout 2024, there were no litigations or lawsuits faced by the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, the management, or Company's employees. This reflects the Company's commitment to good business practices and compliance with applicable regulations.
