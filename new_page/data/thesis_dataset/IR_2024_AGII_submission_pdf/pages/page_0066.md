Berbekal pengalaman bisnis yang panjang, Perseroan telah berkontribusi secara aktif terhadap pertumbuhan ekonomi nasional melalui berbagai sektor, termasuk manufaktur, kesehatan, barang konsumsi, dan infrastruktur. Untuk terus memenuhi kebutuhan industri, Perseroan memperluas kegiatan usahanya dalam perdagangan produk gas dari produsen lain, seperti specialty gas, rare gas, dan produk sejenis, serta layanan konstruksi dan instalasi infrastruktur gas, penyediaan peralatan, teknologi, dan produk pendukung terkait. Keandalan Perseroan semakin diperkuat dengan kontribusinya selama pandemi Covid-19, di mana Perseroan menjadi garda terdepan dalam memenuhi kebutuhan gas oksigen yang mendesak di seluruh Indonesia, mencerminkan komitmen untuk menghadirkan solusi terbaik dalam industri gas nasional. Hingga saat ini, Perseroan mengoperasikan 58 pabrik dan 103 filling stations yang tersebar di 29 provinsi di Indonesia, menjadikannya pemimpin di industri gas nasional.

Dengan semangat inovasi, Perseroan terus berupaya mempertahankan pangsa pasar sekaligus mengejar peluang baru di masa depan. Untuk mendukung visi tersebut, Perseroan berkomitmen meningkatkan kompetensi melalui inovasi produk, pengelolaan sumber daya manusia (SDM) yang unggul, dan penerapan prinsip keberlanjutan dalam setiap aspek operasional. Pendekatan ini memastikan Perseroan tetap relevan dan kompetitif di tengah dinamika pasar yang terus berkembang.

With a long business history, the Company has actively contributed to national economic growth through a number of sectors, including manufacturing, healthcare service, consumer goods, and infrastructure. To continue meeting the industry's needs, the Company has expanded its business activities in the trading of gas products from other producers, such as specialty gases, rare gases, and similar products, as well as gas infrastructure construction and installation services, provision of equipment, technology, and related supporting products. The Company's reliability was further strengthened by its contribution during the Covid-19 pandemic, where the Company was at the forefront of meeting the urgent oxygen gas needs across Indonesia, reflecting its commitment to providing the best solutions in the national gas industry. To date, the Company operates 58 plants and 103 filling stations across 29 provinces in Indonesia, making it a leader in the national gas industry.

With a spirit of innovation, the Company continues to strive to maintain its market share while pursuing new opportunities in the future. To support this vision, the Company is committed to improving its competencies through product innovation, superior human resource (HR) management, and the implementation of sustainability principles in every aspect of its operations. This approach ensures the Company remains relevant and competitive amidst the ever-evolving market dynamics.
