# Proses dan Model Bisnis [IR-4C]
## Business Process and Model

Perseroan telah merancang dan menetapkan proses serta model bisnis yang terintegrasi, mencakup proses inti dan proses pendukung. Proses inti mencakup aktivitas utama yang berfokus pada penciptaan nilai bagi pelanggan, meliputi produksi, distribusi, dan layanan. Aktivitas-aktivitas ini dirancang untuk memastikan kualitas produk dan kepuasan pelanggan melalui alur operasional yang efisien.

The Company has designed and established an integrated process and business model, covering both core and supporting processes. The core processes involve key activities focused on creating value for the customers, including production, distribution, and services. These activities are designed to ensure product quality and customer satisfaction through efficient operational workflows.
