Legal Basis of Appointment
Beliau menjabat sebagai Direktur Utama Perseroan sejak 2018 dan diangkat kembali berdasarkan Akta Pernyataan Keputusan Rapat No. 51 tertanggal 27 Juli 2022 yang dibuat di hadapan Fathiah Helmi, SH, Notaris di Jakarta, dan telah mendapatkan surat pemberitahuan dari Menteri Hukum dan HAM Republik Indonesia tertanggal 22 Agustus 2022.

He serves as the President Director since 2018 and was reappointed based on the Meeting Decree No. 51 dated July 27, 2022, made before Fathiah Helmi, SH, Notary at Jakarta, and has received a notification letter from the Minister of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia dated August 22, 2022.

## Riwayat Pendidikan

Beliau meraih gelar Bachelor of Science dari Marquette University, Wisconsin, USA, [2003] dan Master of Business Administration dari University of Chicago, Booth School of Business, Amerika Serikat [2011]. Beliau juga mengikuti Program Pendidikan Reguler (PPRA) LIII yang diselenggarakan oleh LEMHANNAS [2015]. Selain itu, beliau menyelesaikan program pendidikan Manajemen ASEAN yang diadakan oleh Kansai Economic Federation [2017]. Beliau juga telah menyelesaikan program Executive Master of Business Administration Belt and Road People Bank of China School of Finance (PBSCSF) Tsinghua University.

## Riwayat Jabatan

Beliau telah berkarier di Perseroan selama lebih dari 22 tahun. Sebelumnya, beliau menjabat sebagai Direktur Komersial Perseroan [2004-2018]. Beliau aktif berorganisasi dan memiliki jabatan yang strategis di berbagai organisasi, di antaranya sebagai Wakil Ketua Umum Bidang Pengembangan Industri Strategis Kamar Dagang Indonesia (KADIN), Ketua DPD Ikatan Keluarga Alumni LEMHANNAS (IKAL) Provinsi Jawa Timur, Ketua B3 Dewan Pimpinan Pusat Organisasi Angkatan Darat (DPP Organda), Ketua Umum Organisasi Kepemudaan Majelis Pandita Buddha Maitreya Indonesia (PERSADA BUMI), Wakil Ketua Umum Persatuan Umat Buddha Indonesia (PERMABUDHI), Wakil Ketua Bidang Industri Manufaktur Asosiasi Pengusaha Indonesia (APINDO), Bendahara IKAL Strategic Center, dan anggota Entrepreneurship Advisory Board (EAB) Universitas Surabaya.

## Rangkap Jabatan

Per 31 Desember 2024, beliau juga menjabat sebagai Direktur Utama dan CEO PT Samator, Direktur Utama PT Samator Gas, Direktur Utama PT Ruci Gas, Komisaris Utama PT Aneka Mega Energi, dan Wakil Komisaris Utama PT Krakatau Samator. Beliau juga tercatat sebagai Konsul Kehormatan Republik Slovakia Surabaya (sejak Oktober 2021).

## Hubungan Afiliasi

Beliau memiliki hubungan afiliasi dengan anggota Dewan Komisaris, anggota Direksi, dan Pemegang Saham Utama dan/atau Pengendali.

## Educational Background

He obtained his Bachelor of Science from Marquette University, Wisconsin, USA [2003] and his Master of Business Administration from the University of Chicago, Booth School of Business, USA [2011]. He also completed the Regular Education Program (PPRA) LIII, organized by the National Resilience Institute (LEMHANNAS) of the Republic of Indonesia [2015]. He also completed the ASEAN Management program held by the Kansai Economic Federation [2017]. He also completed the Executive Master of Business Administration Belt and Road program at the People Bank of China School of Finance (PBSCSF) Tsinghua University.

## Career Background

He has been working in the Company for more than 22 years. Previously, he served as the Commercial Director [2004-2018]. He is actively involved in a number of organizations and holds strategic positions in several of them, including as Vice Chairman of the Strategic Industry Development Division at the Indonesian Chamber of Commerce and Industry (KADIN), Chairman of the DPD of the LEMHANNAS Alumni Family Association (IKAL) East Java Province, Chairman of B3 at the Central Leadership Council of the Indonesian Army Organization (DPP Organda), Chairman of the Youth Organization of the Pandita Buddha Maitreya of Indonesia (PERSADA BUMI), Vice Chairman of the Indonesian Buddhist Association (PERMABUDHI), Vice Chairman of the Manufacturing Division of the Indonesian Entrepreneurs Association (APINDO), Treasurer of the IKAL Strategic Center, and a member of the Entrepreneurship Advisory Board (EAB) at the University of Surabaya.

## Concurrent Position

As of December 31, 2024, he also serves as the President Director and CEO of PT Samator, the President Director of PT Samator Gas, President Director of PT Ruci Gas, the President Commissioner of PT Aneka Mega Energi, and a Vice President Commissioner of PT Krakatau Samator. He is also a Honorary Consul of the Republic of Slovakia in Surabaya (since October 2021).

## Affiliations

He has affiliate relationship with other members of the Board of Commissioners, members of the Board of Directors, and the Main and/or Controlling Shareholders.
