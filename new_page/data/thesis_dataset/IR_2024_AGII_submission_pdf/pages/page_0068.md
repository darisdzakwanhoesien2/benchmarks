# 2021

- Pendiri Grup Samator dan Komisaris Utama Perseroan (2006-2021), Almarhum Bapak Arief Harsono, menerima penghargaan dari Marketeers dalam acara MarkPlus Conference 2022 dengan nominasi penghargaan "Immortal Marketing Award 2021" karena beliau telah menjadi sosok inspirasional bagi pegiat marketing serta merupakan sosok pemimpin yang penuh kebajikan dan welas asih.
- Merealisasikan rencana transaksi pembelian aset tetap untuk 2 unit bisnis milik PT Samator yang masing-masing berlokasi di Cikande, Provinsi Banten dan di Gresik, Provinsi Jawa Timur di Indonesia. Saat ini, Perseroan telah mengoperasikan 54 pabrik dan 106 stasiun pengisian di 28 provinsi di Indonesia.
- Melakukan Penawaran Umum Obligasi Berkelanjutan II Aneka Gas Industri dan Sukuk Ijarah Berkelanjutan II Aneka Gas Industri (Tahap III dan Tahap IV).
- The Founder of Samator Group and President Commissioner of the Company (2006-2021), the late Mr. Arief Harsono, was honored with a nomination for the "Immortal Marketing Award 2021" from Marketeers at the MarkPlus Conference 2022, for being an inspirational figure for marketing activists, also a wise and compassionate leader.
- Completed acquisition transaction of fixed assets for 2 (two) business units owned by PT Samator, each located in Cikande, Banten Province, and Gresik, East Java Province in Indonesia. Currently, the Company operates 54 factories with 106 filling stations across 28 provinces in Indonesia.
- Conducted Public Offering of Aneka Gas Industri Sustainable Bond II and Aneka Gas Industri Sustainable Sukuk Ijarah II (Phase III and Phase IV).

# 2022

- PT Aneka Gas Industri Tbk resmi berganti nama menjadi PT Samator Indo Gas Tbk melalui Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS) Luar Biasa Desember 2022 untuk memperkuat branding sebagai bagian dari Samator Group.
- Penandatanganan MoU Program Net Zero Hub yang diinisiasi oleh Kamar Dagang dan Industri (KADIN) Indonesia, dalam rangka menyukseskan target pemerintah Net Zero Emission untuk tahun 2060.
- PT Aneka Gas Industri Tbk officially changed its name to PT Samator Indo Gas Tbk in December 2022 through the Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders (EGMS) to strengthen the Company's branding as part of the Samator Group.
- Signing of the MoU for the Net Zero Hub Program initiated by the Indonesian Chamber of Commerce and Industry (KADIN), in order to support the Government's target of achieving Net Zero Emissions by 2060.

# 2023

- Matrix Company Limited, sebuah perusahaan investasi induk yang dinasihati dan/atau dikelola oleh CVC Capital Partners telah membeli saham minoritas Perseroan.
- Melakukan Penawaran Umum Obligasi Berkelanjutan III Samator Indo Gas dan Sukuk Ijarah Berkelanjutan III Samator Indo Gas (Tahap I).
- Secara operasional, Perseroan telah melakukan beberapa ekspansi pabrik dan filling station di Jawa Tengah, Maluku, Lampung, Sulawesi Tengah, dan Kalimantan Utara.
- Matrix Company Limited, a holding investment company advised and managed by CVC Capital Partners, acquired a significant minority stake in the Company.
- Conducted Public Offering of Samator Indo Gas Sustainable Bond III and Samator Indo Gas Sustainable Sukuk Ijarah III (Phase I).
- Operation-wise, the Company has expanded several plants and filling stations at Central Java, Maluku, Lampung, Central Sulawesi, and North Kalimantan.
