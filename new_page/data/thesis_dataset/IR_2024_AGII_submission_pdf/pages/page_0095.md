# Perubahan Komposisi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi

Sepanjang tahun 2024, komposisi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi Perseroan tetap tidak mengalami perubahan. Stabilitas dalam manajemen ini mencerminkan komitmen Perseroan untuk menjaga kesinambungan kepemimpinan dalam mendukung kelancaran operasional dan pencapaian tujuan strategis.

Namun demikian, Perseroan telah menerima surat pengunduran diri dari Bapak Setyo Wahono selaku Wakil Komisaris Utama pada 20 Januari 2025, yang akan dimintakan persetujuannya dalam RUPS mendatang.

# Changes in the Composition of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors

Throughout 2024, the composition of the members of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors remained unchanged. This stability in the Management team reflects the Company's commitment to maintaining leadership continuity to support smooth operations and the achievement of strategic goals.

However, the Company has received a letter of resignation from Mr. Setyo Wahono as the Vice President Commissioner in January 20, 2025, which will be submitted for approval at the upcoming General Meeting of Shareholders.
