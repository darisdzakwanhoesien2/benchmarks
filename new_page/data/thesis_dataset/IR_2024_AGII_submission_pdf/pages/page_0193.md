# Hasil Penilaian

Pada tahun 2024, penilaian kinerja Dewan Komisaris menunjukkan bahwa masing-masing anggota telah menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya dengan baik dan telah memberikan kontribusi yang positif bagi kemajuan Perseroan.

# Penilaian Kinerja Komite di Bawah Dewan Komisaris

Dalam menjalankan fungsi, tugas, dan tanggung jawab pengawasan, Dewan Komisaris dibantu oleh komite pendukung, yaitu Komite Audit dan Komite ESG. Dewan Komisaris menilai Komite Audit dan Komite ESG telah menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya dengan baik di tahun 2024 serta memberikan rekomendasi-rekomendasi praktikal kepada Dewan Komisaris.

# Prosedur Penilaian

Dewan Komisaris melakukan penilaian kinerja Komite Audit dan Komite ESG secara berkala untuk mengevaluasi efektivitas pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab komite tersebut. Penilaian ini bertujuan untuk memastikan bahwa Komite Audit dan Komite ESG menjalankan fungsi pengawasan dengan baik, terutama terkait dengan Laporan Keuangan dan penerapan sistem pengendalian internal, serta kinerja ESG.

# Kriteria Penilaian

Kriteria penilaian kinerja Komite Audit dan Komite ESG meliputi:

1. Realisasi rencana kerja yang telah ditetapkan untuk tahun berjalan;
2. Tingkat kehadiran anggota Komite Audit dan Komite ESG dalam rapat yang diselenggarakan;
3. Kualitas pengawasan terhadap proses audit, Laporan Keuangan, dan sistem pengendalian internal; serta
4. Rekomendasi yang diberikan oleh Komite Audit dan Komite ESG kepada Dewan Komisaris untuk perbaikan dan peningkatan proses operasional.

# Hasil Penilaian

Sepanjang tahun 2024, Dewan Komisaris menilai bahwa Komite Audit dan Komite ESG telah melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya dengan baik dan profesional. Penilaian ini didasarkan pada efektivitas proses audit Laporan Keuangan yang dilakukan oleh Komite Audit, serta pengawasan yang baik terhadap penerapan GCG, sistem pengendalian internal, dan manajemen risiko. Selain itu, Komite Audit juga telah memberikan rekomendasi-rekomendasi yang praktikal dan konstruktif yang mendukung perbaikan dan penguatan tata kelola perusahaan. Demikian pula dengan Komite ESG, yang telah menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya secara efektif melalui penetapan Indikator Kinerja Utama (KPI) ESG serta strategi yang selaras dengan kebijakan ESG Perseroan.

# Evaluation Result

In 2024, the performance evaluation of the Board of Commissioners indicated that each member had effectively fulfilled their duties and responsibilities, contributing positively to the Company's growth.

# Performance Assessment of Committees Under the Board of Commissioners

In carrying out its supervisory functions, duties, and responsibilities, the Board of Commissioners is assisted by a supporting committee, the Audit Committee and ESG Committee. According to the Board of Commissioners' assessment, the Audit Committee and ESG Committee managed to carry out its duties and responsibilities optimally and provided practical recommendations throughout 2024.

# Assessment Procedure

The Board of Commissioners periodically assesses the performance of the Audit Committee and ESG Committee to assess the effectiveness of its duties and responsibilities. This assessment has a purpose to ensure that the Audit Committee and ESG Committee effectively carries out its supervisory function, particularly concerning Financial Statements and the implementation of the internal control system, and ESG performance.

# Assessment Criteria

The performance evaluation of the Audit Committee and ESG Committee is based on the following criteria:

1. Implementation of the approved work plan for the current year;
2. Attendance level of Audit Committee and ESG Committee members in meetings;
3. Quality of supervision in the audit process, Financial Statements, and internal control systems; and
4. Recommendations provided by the Audit Committee and ESG Committee to the Board of Commissioners for operational improvements and enhancements.

# Assessment Results

Throughout 2024, the Board of Commissioners deemed that the Audit Committee and ESG Committee had effectively and professionally fulfilled its duties and responsibilities. This assessment was based on the effectiveness of the Financial Statement audit process, as well as strong supervision of GCG implementation, internal control systems, and risk management. Additionally, the Audit Committee has provided practical and constructive recommendations that support continuous improvements and the strengthening of corporate governance. Similarly, the ESG Committee has effectively carried out its duties and responsibilities by establishing ESG Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and strategies aligned with the Company's ESG policies.
