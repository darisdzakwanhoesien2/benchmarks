## Kunjungan Presiden Republik Indonesia ke Pabrik Batang

Presiden Republik Indonesia, Bapak Joko Widodo bersama dengan Menteri Investasi, Wakil Menteri Badan Usaha Milik Negara, dan Wakil Menteri Badan Koordinasi Penanaman Modal (BKPM) mengunjungi lokasi pabrik baru Perseroan di Batang untuk meninjau kesiapan pabrik tersebut.

Visit of the President of the Republic of Indonesia to Batang Plant
The President of the Republic of Indonesia, Mr. Joko Widodo, along with the Minister of Investment, the Deputy Minister of State-Owned Enterprises, and the Deputy Minister of the Investment Coordinating Board (BKPM), visited the Company’s new plant in Batang to review its readiness.

## Peluncuran Logo dan Tema Samator 49: All In

Dalam rangka HUT ke-49 Samator Group, diluncurkan identitas korporat baru, “ALL IN”, dengan logo berbentuk kepalan tangan dalam kobaran api yang melambangkan komitmen dan semangat penuh untuk mencapai tujuan.

## Launch of the Samator 49 Logo and Theme: All In

In celebration of Samator Group’s 49th anniversary, a new corporate identity, “ALL IN”, was launched, featuring a logo in the form of a clenched fist in a blaze of fire, symbolizing commitment and full enthusiasm to achieve goals.

## Saham Perseroan Terindeks dalam SMC Composite 2024

Setelah periode Indeks SMC sebelumnya, saham AGII kembali terindeks dalam Indeks SMC untuk periode Agustus 2024-Februari 2025.

## The Company’s Shares Indexed in the SMC Composite 2024

After the previous SMC Index period, AGII shares have been re-indexed in the SMC Index for the period of August 2024-February 2025.
