Selain itu, beliau memiliki berbagai sertifikasi, seperti Certified Securities Analyst (CSA), Certified Sustainability Reporting Specialist (CSRS), Certified Risk Professional (CRP), dan Certification in Audit Committee Practices (CACP).

# Riwayat Jabatan

Beliau memiliki pengalaman kerja sebagai Strategic Planning Samator Group (2007-2018), Direktur Investasi Dana Pensiun Satya Wacana (2019-2021), dan Komite Audit PT Samator Indo Gas Tbk (2021-2022).

# Rangkap Jabatan

Per 31 Desember 2024, beliau juga menjabat sebagai Ketua Komite Audit Perseroan, Komite Audit PT Polytama Propindo, Komisaris PT Nusantara Sawit Sejahtera Tbk, serta Komisaris Independen PT Samuel Sekuritas Indonesia dan PT Goshen Anugerah Sejahtera. Beliau juga aktif mengajar sebagai Dosen Luar Biasa pada Program Magister Manajemen Universitas Diponegoro, Semarang, dan Magister Akuntansi Unika Soegijapranata, Semarang, serta Dosen pada Fakultas Ekonomika dan Bisnis Universitas Kristen Satya Wacana, Salatiga.

# Hubungan Afiliasi

Beliau tidak memiliki hubungan afiliasi dengan anggota Dewan Komisaris lainnya, anggota Direksi, serta Pemegang Saham Utama dan/atau Pengendali.

# Educational Background

He obtained his Bachelor Degree in Economics from Universitas Kristen Satya Wacana (1998), Master Degree in Financial Management from Universitas Diponegoro (2000), and 2 doctoral degrees, namely in Financial Management from Universitas Diponegoro (2015) and Environmental Science from Universitas Katolik Soegijapranata (2021). In addition, he holds various certifications, such as Certified Securities Analyst (CSA), Certified Sustainability Reporting Specialist (CSRS), Certified Risk Professional (CRP), and Certification in Audit Committee Practices (CACP).

# Career Background

He had experiences as a Strategic Planning at Samator Group (2007-2018), Investment Director at Dana Pensiun Satya Wacana (2019-2021), and Audit Committee of PT Samator Indo Gas Tbk (2021-2022).

# Concurrent Position

As of December 31, 2024, he also serves as the Chairman of the Audit Committee of the Company, member of the Audit Committee of PT Polytama Propindo, a Commissioner of PT Nusantara Sawit Sejahtera Tbk, and an Independent Commissioner of PT Samuel Sekuritas Indonesia and PT Goshen Anugerah Sejahtera. He is also actively teaching as a Lecturer in the Master Program in Management at Universitas Diponegoro, Semarang, and the Master Program in Accounting at Unika Soegijapranata, Semarang, as well as a Lecturer at the Faculty of Economics and Business at Universitas Satya Wacana Christian, Salatiga.
