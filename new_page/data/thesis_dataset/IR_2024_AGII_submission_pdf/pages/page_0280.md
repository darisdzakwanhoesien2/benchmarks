## Pekerja Anak dan Pekerja Paksa [POJK F.19] [ESG S-10]

Perseroan menerapkan kebijakan ketenagakerjaan sesuai peraturan pemerintah, mencakup batas usia minimum karyawan, yaitu 18 tahun, dan pengaturan jam kerja. Jam kerja di Head Office berlangsung Senin hingga Jumat pukul 08.00-17.00 WIB, sedangkan di cabang meliputi Senin hingga Jumat pukul 08.00-16.00 WIB dan Sabtu pukul 08.00-13.00 WIB. Untuk posisi tertentu, sistem kerja bergilir diberlakukan dengan 3 shift, yakni 07.00-15.00 WIB, 15.00-23.00 WIB, dan 23.00-07.00 WIB. Perseroan memastikan operasionalnya bebas dari pekerja anak dan kerja paksa sesuai regulasi yang berlaku.

## Kebijakan Terkait Pelecehan Seksual dan Non-Diskriminasi [ESG S-08]

Perseroan menjunjung tinggi nilai-nilai etika dan saling menghormati dalam lingkungan kerja. Meskipun belum terdapat pengaturan secara eksplisit mengenai pelecehan seksual dalam Kode Etik, prinsip-prinsip etika karyawan, standar perilaku, serta mekanisme penyampaian keluhan telah diatur dan menjadi dasar dalam mencegah tindakan yang merugikan, termasuk pelecehan maupun diskriminasi. Perseroan tidak mentoleransi segala bentuk diskriminasi atas dasar gender, agama, usia, latar belakang, atau kondisi pribadi lainnya, serta berkomitmen menjaga ruang kerja yang aman dan inklusif. Sebagai bagian dari perbaikan berkelanjutan, Perseroan senantiasa mengevaluasi kebijakan ketenagakerjaan guna memastikan perlindungan yang lebih kuat terhadap hak dan martabat seluruh karyawan.

## Child Labor and Forced Labor [POJK F.19] [ESG S-10]

The Company implements employment policies in accordance with Government regulations, including a minimum age limit for employees of 18 years and work hour arrangements. Working hours at the Head Office are Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM-5:00 PM West Indonesia Time [WIB], while at the branches, working hours are Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM-4:00 PM WIB and Saturday from 08.00-13.00 WIB. For certain positions, a rotating work system is implemented with 3 shifts, i.e., namely 07.00-15.00 WIB, 3:00-11:00 PM WIB, and 11:00 PM-7:00 AM WIB. The Company ensures that its operations are free from child labor and forced labor in accordance with applicable regulations.

## Policy Regarding Sexual Harassment and Non-Discrimination [ESG S-08]

The Company upholds ethical values and mutual respect in the work environment. Although there is no explicit regulation regarding sexual harassment in the Code of Conduct, employee ethics principles, standards of behaviour, and complaint mechanisms have been regulated and serve as a basis for preventing detrimental actions, including harassment and discrimination. The Company does not tolerate any form of discrimination on the basis of gender, religion, age, background, or other personal conditions, and is committed to maintaining a safe and inclusive workspace. As part of continuous improvement, the Company continuously evaluates its employment policies to ensure better protection of all employees' rights and dignity.
