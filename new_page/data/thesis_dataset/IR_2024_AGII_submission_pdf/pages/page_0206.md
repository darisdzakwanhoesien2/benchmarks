# Organ Tata Kelola di Bawah Dewan Komisaris
## Governance Organs Under the Board of Commissioners

## Komite Audit

Komite Audit Perseroan merupakan organ yang dibentuk untuk membantu Dewan Komisaris dalam menjalankan tugas pengawasan terhadap Laporan Keuangan, mengevaluasi kinerja Auditor Internal dan Eksternal, serta memastikan penerapan sistem pengendalian internal dan manajemen risiko Perseroan diterapkan secara efektif dan menyeluruh.

## Pedoman Kerja

Pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab Komite Audit mengikuti ketentuan yang tercantum dalam Piagam Komite Audit. Piagam ini diperbarui dan disempurnakan pada 27 Maret 2023 dan telah dipublikasikan di situs web pada laman Tata Kelola Perusahaan.

## Masa Jabatan

Anggota Komite Audit menjabat sesuai dengan masa jabatan Dewan Komisaris dan dapat dipilih kembali hanya untuk 1 periode tambahan.

## Komposisi

Komite Audit terdiri dari 3 anggota, yakni 2 Komisaris Independen, salah satunya menjabat sebagai ketua dan yang lainnya sebagai anggota, serta 1 anggota yang merupakan pihak independen. Berikut informasi terkait komposisi anggota Komite Audit Perseroan selama tahun 2024.

## Audit Committee

The Audit Committee is an organ established to assist the Board of Commissioners in carrying out its supervisory duties over the Financial Statements, evaluating the performance of both Internal and External Auditors, and ensuring the effective and comprehensive implementation of the Company's internal control system and risk management.

## Work Guidelines

The duties and responsibilities of the Audit Committee are carried out in accordance with the provisions outlined in the Audit Committee Charter. This Charter was updated and improved on March 27, 2023, and has been published on the Company's website on the Corporate Governance page.

## Term of Office

Audit Committee members serve in accordance with the term of office of the Board of Commissioners and may be re-elected for only 1 additional term.

## Composition

The Audit Committee consists of 3 members, 2 Independent Commissioners, one serving as the chairman and the other as a member, and 1 independent member. Information related to the composition of the Audit Committee members during 2024 is presented as follows.
