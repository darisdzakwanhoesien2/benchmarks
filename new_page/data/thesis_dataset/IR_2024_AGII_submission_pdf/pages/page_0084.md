# Riwayat Pendidikan

Gelar Sarjana Hukum diperoleh dari Universitas Proklamasi 45 Yogyakarta (1999) dan gelar Magister Hukum dari Universitas Kadiri (2025). Saat ini, beliau sedang menempuh pendidikan Magister Hukum di Universitas 17 Agustus 1945 (UNTAG) Surabaya. Beliau telah mengikuti Sekolah Pimpinan Kepolisian (Sespimpol) (2004) dan Program Pendidikan Reguler Angkatan (PPRA) di Lembaga Ketahanan Nasional (LEMHANNAS) (2015). Beliau juga pernah mengikuti pendidikan di International Law Enforcement Academy (ILEA) di Bangkok, Thailand (2002). Selain itu, beliau memiliki Sertifikat Kompetensi sebagai Qualified Risk Governance Professional (QRGP) (2024).

# Educational Background

He completed his education at the Police Academy (Akpol) Class of 1988 and continued at the Police Science University (PTIK) Class of 1995. He earned a Bachelor of Law degree from Universitas Proklamasi 45 Yogyakarta in 1999 and a Master of Law degree from Universitas Kadiri in 2025. At the moment, he is pursuing a Master of Law degree at Universitas 17 Agustus 1945 (UNTAG) Surabaya. He attended the Police Leadership School (Sespimpol) in 2004 and the Regular Education Program (PPRA) at the National Resilience Institute of the Republic of Indonesia (LEMHANNAS) in 2015. He also attended training at the International Law Enforcement Academy (ILEA) in Bangkok, Thailand, in 2002. In addition, he holds a Competency Certificate as a Qualified Risk Governance Professional (QRGP), obtained in 2024.

# Career Background

Before joining the Company, he served as the Head of Bangka Departmental Police Force at Babel Regional Police Force (2007-2008), Director of Intelligence and Security at Babel Regional Police Force (2008-2010), Head of Operations at Maluku Regional Police Force (2011-2013), Director of Intelligence and Security at Metro Jaya Regional Police Force (2013-2015), Director 35 at the National Intelligence Agency (BIN) (2015-2018), Expert Staff at the Ministry of Law and Human Rights (Kemenkumham) at the National Intelligence Agency (BIN) (2018-2020), Head of the Daily Executive of the Coordinating Agency for Counterfeit Money Eradication (Kalakhar Botasupal) (2018-2020), Deputy VIII at BIN (2020-2021), Deputy Head of the National Cyber and Encryption Agency (BSSN) (2021-2022), and Special Staff to the Head of BIN (2021-2024).

# Concurrent Position
