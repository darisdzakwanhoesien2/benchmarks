# Transparansi Informasi Mengenai Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi

Transparency on Information of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors

## Nominasi dan Remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi

[IR-4B] [GRI 2-10, 2-19, 2-20, 2-21]

## Kebijakan dan Prosedur Nominasi

Proses nominasi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi Perseroan merupakan kewenangan Fungsi Nominasi yang dijalankan oleh Dewan Komisaris. Usulan mengenai nominasi dan remunerasi kemudian diajukan kepada RUPS untuk memastikan bahwa calon yang diajukan memenuhi kriteria dan kebutuhan perusahaan. Apabila terdapat benturan kepentingan di antara anggota Dewan Komisaris terkait usulan nominasi, hal ini harus diungkapkan secara terbuka selama rapat nominasi.

Adapun prosedur nominasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi diuraikan sebagai berikut.

1. Menyusun komposisi jabatan anggota Direksi dan/atau Dewan Komisaris.
2. Menyusun kebijakan dan kriteria yang dibutuhkan dalam proses nominasi.
3. Kebijakan evaluasi kinerja bagi anggota Direksi dan/atau Dewan Komisaris.
4. Melakukan penilaian kinerja anggota Direksi dan/atau Dewan Komisaris berdasarkan tolok ukur yang telah disusun sebagai bahan evaluasi.

## Nomination and Remuneration of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors [IR-4B] [GRI 2-10, 2-19, 2-20, 2-21]

## Nomination Policy and Procedure

The nomination process for members of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors falls under the authority of the Nomination Function, which is carried out by the Board of Commissioners. Proposals regarding nomination and remuneration are then submitted to the GMS to ensure that the proposed candidates meet the Company's criteria and needs. If there is a conflict of interest among Board of Commissioners members regarding the nomination proposal, it must be openly disclosed during the nomination meeting.

The nomination procedures for the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors is described as follows.

1. Determine the composition of positions for members of the Board of Directors and/or Board of Commissioners.
2. Develop policies and criteria required in the nomination process.
3. Develop performance evaluation policies for members of the Board of Directors and/or Board of Commissioners.
4. Assess the performance of members of the Board of Directors and/or Board of Commissioners based on predetermined benchmarks as evaluation materials.
