# Evaluasi K3 [GRI 403-9, 403-10] [ESG 5-06]

Perseroan melakukan audit Hazard and Operability Study (HAZOP), Quantitative Risk Assessment (QRA), dan Environment, Health and Safety (EHS) di seluruh pabrik di wilayah Barat dan Timur guna memantau kinerja pengelolaan sarana dan keselamatan kerja. Audit ini dilaksanakan bersama Enviro Solutions and Consulting (ESC), konsultan terkemuka di bidang lingkungan, keselamatan, dan risiko di Asia.

Hasil audit memberikan gambaran detail tentang praktik dan kinerja di masing-masing aspek yang diaudit. Rekomendasi dari auditor digunakan sebagai dasar untuk meningkatkan area yang memerlukan perbaikan.

## OHS Evaluation [GRI 403-9, 403-10] [ESG 5-06]

The Company conducts audits of the Hazard and Operability Study (HAZOP), Quantitative Risk Assessment (QRA), and Environment, Health, and Safety (EHS) for all of its factories in the West and East regions to monitor the performance of facility management and workplace safety. These audits are conducted in collaboration with Enviro Solutions and Consulting (ESC), a leading consultancy in environmental, safety, and risk management in Asia.

The audit results provide a detailed overview of the practices and performance in each of the audited aspects. Recommendations from auditors are used as a basis for improving areas that require improvement.
