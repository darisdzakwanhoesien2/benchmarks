Serves as the Vice President Director since 2018 and was reappointed based on the Meeting Decree No. 51 dated July 27, 2022, made before Fathiah Helmi, SH, Notary at Jakarta, and has received a notification letter from the Minister of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia dated August 22, 2022.

## Riwayat Pendidikan

Beliau memperoleh gelar Insinyur dari Institut Teknologi Sepuluh November, Surabaya (1986) dan Magister Manajemen dari Institut Pengembangan Wiraswasta Indonesia (IPWI) (1996). Di samping itu, beliau telah mengikuti Program Pengembangan Eksekutif “Strategic Action Program” yang diadakan oleh P2EB Universitas Gadjah Mada, Yogyakarta (2015). Beliau juga telah menjalani Program Pemantapan Nilai-Nilai Kebangsaan untuk Kamar Dagang Indonesia LEMHANNAS Republik Indonesia (2019).

## Riwayat Jabatan

Sebelum bergabung dengan Perseroan, beliau pernah menjabat sebagai Direktur Teknik PT Samator (1997-1999) dan kemudian menjadi Direktur Research and Development PT Samator (1999-2001).

## Rangkap Jabatan

Per 31 Desember 2024, beliau juga menjabat sebagai Chief Operating Officer Perseroan, Direktur PT Samator, Direktur Utama PT Dwi Setia Gas dan PT Sandana Adi Prakarsa, Direktur PT Krakatau Samator, serta Komisaris PT Arohera. Beliau juga aktif berorganisasi dan merupakan anggota Steering Committee dan Dewan Pembina di Asosiasi Gas Industri Indonesia.

## Hubungan Afiliasi

Beliau tidak memiliki hubungan afiliasi dengan anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, namun terafiliasi dengan Pemegang Saham Utama dan/atau Pengendali.

## Educational Background

He obtained an Engineer title from Institut Teknologi Sepuluh November, Surabaya, in 1986 and obtained his Master in Management from Institut Pengembangan Wiraswasta Indonesia (IPWI) in 1996. In addition, he completed the “Strategic Action Program” of Executive Development Program organized by P2EB of Universitas Gadjah Mada, Yogyakarta (2015). He has also completed the Consolidation of National Values for the Indonesian Chamber of Commerce Program organized by the Indonesian National Resilience Institute (LEMHANNAS) (2019).

## Career Background

Before joining the Company, he served as the Technical Director of PT Samator (1997-1999) and later became the Director of Research and Development of PT Samator (1999-2001).

## Concurrent Position

As of December 31, 2024, he also serves as the Chief Operating Officer of the Company, a Director of PT Samator, the President Director of PT Dwi Setia Gas, the President Director of PT Sandana Adi Prakarsa, and a Director of PT Krakatau Samator. He is also active in organizations and is a member of the Steering Committee and Advisory Board at the Indonesian Industrial Gas Association.

## Affiliations

He has no affiliate relationship with members of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors, but has affiliation with the Main and/or Controlling Shareholders.
