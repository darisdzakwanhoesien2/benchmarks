# Pelaksanaan Tugas

Sepanjang tahun 2024, Direksi telah melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawab dengan baik dan efektif, di antaranya:

1. Menetapkan rencana jangka pendek, menengah, dan panjang Perseroan;
2. Menerima persetujuan dari Dewan Komisaris atas seluruh rencana strategis dan aksi korporasi, serta melaksanakan laporan kepada Dewan Komisaris;
3. Melaksanakan rapat internal sebanyak 12 kali dan rapat gabungan dengan Dewan Komisaris sebanyak 3 kali;
4. Melaksanakan RUPS Tahun buku 2023 pada tanggal 24 Juni 2024 sesuai dengan ketentuan dalam Anggaran Dasar Perseroan;
5. Menjalankan aktualisasi dampak sosial melalui kegiatan corporate social responsibility (CSR); serta
6. Memenuhi laporan-laporan berkala kepada regulator mencakup Laporan Penggunaan Dana Hasil Penawaran Umum, Laporan Keterbukaan Informasi terkait Aksi Korporasi, dan laporan-laporan lainnya dengan berkoordinasi bersama Sekretaris Perusahaan.

# Implementation of Duties

Throughout 2024, the Board of Directors has effectively and successfully carried out its duties and responsibilities, including:

1. Establishing the Company's short-term, medium-term, and long-term plans;
2. Obtaining approval from the Board of Commissioners for all strategic plans and corporate actions, as well as reporting to the Board of Commissioners;
3. Holding 12 internal meetings and 3 joint meetings with the Board of Commissioners;
4. Conducting the Annual GMS for the 2023 financial year on June 24, 2024, in accordance with the Articles of Association;
5. Implementing social impact initiatives through corporate social responsibility (CSR) programs; and
6. Submitting regular reports to regulators, including the Report on the Use of Public Offering Proceeds, Disclosure Reports on Corporate Actions, and other required reports, in coordination with the Corporate Secretary.
