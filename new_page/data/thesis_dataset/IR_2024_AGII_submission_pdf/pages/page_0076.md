# Perubahan Perseroan yang Bersifat Signifikan [POJK C.6]

## Significant Changes in the Company

Sepanjang tahun 2024, terjadi perubahan signifikan di internal Perseroan, yaitu:

1. Pembukaan stasiun pengisian di Morowali, Sulawesi Tengah; dan
2. Pembukaan pabrik ASP di Kawasan Industri Terpadu Batang, pabrik untuk produksi hidrogen rendah karbon di Batam, dan pabrik asetilen di Sofifi, Maluku Utara.

Throughout 2024, there were some significant internal changes within the Company:

1. The opening of a filling station at Morowali, Central Sulawesi; and
2. The opening of an ASP plant at Batang Integrated Industrial Zone, a low carbon hydrogen production plant in Batam, and an acetylene plant in Sofifi, North Maluku.
