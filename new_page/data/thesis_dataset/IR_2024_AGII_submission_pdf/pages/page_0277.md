## Biaya Pengembangan Kompetensi

Perseroan menetapkan anggaran pelatihan dan pengembangan berdasarkan evaluasi kebutuhan, dengan tetap mempertimbangkan kondisi keuangan. Pada tahun 2024, total biaya yang dikeluarkan untuk program ini tercatat sebesar Rp1,11 miliar, meningkat 3,07% dibandingkan tahun 2023 sebesar Rp1,08 miliar. Kondisi ini terjadi disebabkan peningkatan jumlah training luring (offline) dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya.

## Regenerasi

Perseroan melaksanakan reorganisasi dan konsolidasi SDM sebagai langkah strategis menghadapi dinamika bisnis yang terus berkembang serta memastikan kesiapan organisasi untuk pertumbuhan masa depan. Salah satu inisiatif utama yang dijalankan adalah program Personal Development Plan (PDP), yang dirancang guna mempersiapkan minimal 2 kandidat suksesor di setiap posisi kritikal, khususnya di level Supervisor ke atas.

PDP berfokus pada pengembangan individu yang diproyeksikan mampu mengisi posisi strategis dalam waktu 1 hingga 2 tahun. Pendekatan program ini mencakup 10% teori terkait unit kerja, 20% pelatihan melalui proses coaching untuk pengembangan keterampilan spesifik, dan 70% keterlibatan langsung dalam proyek operasional. Struktur ini bertujuan membangun kombinasi pemahaman teoritis dan kemampuan praktis melalui pengalaman langsung.

Melalui implementasi PDP selama 2 tahun, peserta diharapkan siap menjadi suksesor di posisi kritikal, mendukung keberlanjutan organisasi secara efektif. Pada tahun 2024, sebanyak 97 kandidat terlibat dalam program PDP, baik yang baru memulai maupun melanjutkan pelatihan dari periode sebelumnya, mencerminkan komitmen Perseroan dalam membangun SDM yang andal dan adaptif terhadap perubahan.

## Competency Development Costs

The Company sets the training and development budget based on needs evaluation, by considering financial conditions. In 2024, the total cost incurred for this program was recorded at Rp1.11 billion, an increase of 3.07% compared to Rp1.08 billion in 2023. This condition was mainly due to the increase in number of offline training compared to the previous year.

## Regeneration

The Company is implementing HR reorganization and consolidation as a strategic measure to deal with developing business dynamics and ensure the organization preparation for future growth. One of the main initiatives applied is the Personal Development Plan (PDP) program, which is designed to prepare at least 2 successor candidates for each critical position, especially at the Supervisor level and above.

PDP focuses on the development of individuals who are expected to fill strategic positions within 1 to 2 years. This program approach includes 10% theory related to work units, 20% training through a coaching process for specific skill development, and 70% direct involvement in operational projects. This structure aims to build a combination of theoretical understanding and practical skills through direct experience.

After participating in the PDP for 2 years, participants are expected to be ready to become successors in critical positions, supporting the sustainability of the organization effectively. In 2024, 97 candidates were involved in the PDP program, both those who were just starting and continuing training from previous period, thus reflecting the Company's commitment to building reliable and adaptive human resources to change.
