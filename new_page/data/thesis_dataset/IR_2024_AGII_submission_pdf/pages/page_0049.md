## Pandangan atas Penerapan Tata Kelola Perusahaan

Dewan Komisaris menilai bahwa penerapan tata kelola perusahaan yang baik (GCG) di Perseroan telah berjalan dengan prinsip yang kokoh. Direksi telah memastikan bahwa transparansi, akuntabilitas, tanggung jawab, kemandirian, serta kesetaraan dan kewajaran diterapkan secara konsisten di seluruh aspek operasional. Untuk memperkuat mekanisme pengawasan, Perseroan juga membentuk struktur tata kelola yang melibatkan Dewan Komisaris serta komite pendukung, yaitu Komite Audit. Langkah ini berperan dalam menjaga objektivitas pengambilan keputusan dan memastikan kepatuhan terhadap regulasi yang berlaku, termasuk standar internasional dalam pengelolaan keberlanjutan.

Dewan Komisaris juga mengapresiasi upaya Direksi dalam membangun budaya tata kelola yang berorientasi pada integritas dan keberlanjutan. Implementasi manajemen risiko yang terintegrasi, peningkatan transparansi, serta program pengembangan sumber daya manusia menjadi langkah strategis untuk memperkuat fondasi GCG. Selain itu, komitmen terhadap tanggung jawab sosial dan lingkungan terus diperkuat melalui berbagai inisiatif yang selaras dengan strategi bisnis jangka panjang. Ke depan, Dewan Komisaris mendorong Direksi untuk terus mengembangkan praktik tata kelola yang adaptif dan inovatif agar Perseroan dapat mempertahankan daya saing serta menciptakan nilai yang berkelanjutan bagi pemangku kepentingan.

## Views on the Implementation of Corporate Governance

It is the view of the Board of Commissioners that the implementation of good corporate governance (GCG) in the Company was carried out employing strong principles. The Board of Directors ensured that transparency, accountability, responsibility, independence, as well as equality and fairness were applied consistently across all operational aspects. To strengthen its supervisory mechanism, the Company also established a governance structure involving the Board of Commissioners and a supporting committee, namely the Audit Committee. This step has played a role in maintaining the objectivity of decision-making and ensuring compliance with applicable regulations, including international standards in sustainability management.

The Board of Commissioners also appreciates the Board of Directors' efforts in building a governance culture that is oriented towards integrity and sustainability. The implementation of integrated risk management, increased transparency, and human resource development programs are strategic steps to strengthen GCG foundation. In addition, the commitment to social and environmental responsibility continues to be reinforced through various initiatives, in line with long-term business strategies. Moving forward, the Board of Commissioners encourages the Board of Directors to continue developing adaptive and innovative governance practices so that the Company can maintain its competitiveness and create sustainable value for stakeholders.
