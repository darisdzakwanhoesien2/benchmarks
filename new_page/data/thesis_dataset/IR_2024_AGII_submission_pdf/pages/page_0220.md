# Pengembangan TI Tahun 2025

Perseroan telah menyusun rencana pengembangan TI di tahun 2025, yang diuraikan sebagai berikut.

1. Melakukan ekspansi (rollout) implementasi sistem ERP Epicor - Phase 2 ke 39 lokasi operasional Perseroan dan Entitas Anak, lengkap dengan berbagai integrasi untuk mendukung proses produksi dan distribusi produk.
2. Membentuk sistem Ordering Hotline/Call Center sebagai jalur pemesanan bagi pelanggan retail yang tersentralisasi, guna memberikan layanan yang lebih profesional, transparan, dan terstandarisasi.
3. Menghadirkan portal pemesanan produk secara self-service bagi pelanggan retail yang melakukan walk-in purchases ke lokasi distribusi, yang terhubung dengan automatic payment gateway untuk mempermudah proses pembelian secara non-tunai.
4. Melaksanakan seleksi akhir dan implementasi disaster recovery untuk Critical IT Assets, seperti sistem ERP dan beberapa aplikasi lainnya, dengan menggunakan Teknologi Cloud Replication guna menjamin keamanan serta keberlangsungan proses usaha di masa keadaan kahar.

By increasing employee awareness of best security practices including identity verification, strong password management, and caution against suspicious emails or links the Company significantly reduces the risk of cyberattacks.

# IT Development Plan for 2025

The Company has developed an IT expansion plan for 2025, which includes.

1. Rolling out the ERP Epicor system - Phase 2 across 39 operational locations of the Company and its Subsidiaries, integrating various functions to support production process and product distribution.
2. Establishing an Ordering Hotline/Call Center system to provide a centralized ordering channel for retail customers, ensuring professional, transparent, and standardized service.
3. Introducing a self-service product ordering portal for retail customers making walk-in purchases at distribution locations, integrated with an automatic payment gateway to facilitate cashless transactions.
4. Perform final selection and implementation of disaster recovery solutions for Critical IT Assets such as ERP systems and other key applications, utilizing Cloud Replication Technology to ensure security as well as business continuity during force majeure events.
