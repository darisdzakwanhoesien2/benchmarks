# Komitmen Terhadap Net Zero Emission (ESG E-06, E-07)

Perseroan berkomitmen untuk berperan aktif dalam pengendalian perubahan iklim global melalui target Net Zero Emission pada tahun 2060. Net Zero merupakan kondisi di mana emisi gas rumah kaca yang dilepaskan ke atmosfer sepenuhnya disembangkan dengan jumlah emisi yang diserap atau dieliminasi, sehingga tidak ada peningkatan bersih emisi. Untuk mendukung komitmen tersebut, Perseroan telah menyusun Decarbonization Roadmap yang mencakup pengurangan emisi Cakupan 1 (emisi langsung dari bahan bakar fosil) dan Cakupan 2 (emisi tidak langsung dari konsumsi listrik).

Sebagai bagian dari implementasi roadmap tersebut, Perseroan menetapkan rencana aksi yang terfokus pada tahun 2025, meliputi:

1. Pengurangan Emisi Cakupan 1
a. Transisi Armada Kendaraan
Perseroan akan mulai mengimplementasikan strategi green mobility melalui pengurangan ukuran armada, optimasi rute, serta penggantian kendaraan berbahan bakar fosil dengan kendaraan listrik (EV), yang dijadwalkan mulai berjalan pada Juni 2025.

This approach not only reduces greenhouse gas emissions but also provides an environmentally friendly solution for industrial waste management, while supporting operational efficiency and contributing to sustainable practices.

3. Clean Energy Transition
Gradually replacing diesel fuel with more eco-friendly deklite fuel.

4. Management of Electricity Consumption
a. Limiting the time of electricity use in its Head Offices and public street lighting; and
b. Electricity operations in the office are only on for 9 hours on weekdays and off 24 hours on weekdays.

5. Product Distribution Efficiency
a. Implementing Transportation Management System for Schedule and Route Optimization; and
b. Using distribution trucks with Euro 4 standard engines, which have lower emission levels.

6. Implementation of Enhanced Oil Recovery (EOR) Project
a. Exploring the implementation of EOR technology with PT Pertamina EP at CO₂ removal facilities in Subang and Cilamaya.
b. CO₂ gas from the separation process is collected using a methyl diethanolamine (MDEA)-based absorption system, reducing CO₂ content to less than 5%, which is to be processed into products with economic value for the industry.

With these measures, the Company is continuously committed to reducing environmental impacts, supporting operational sustainability, and providing a positive contribution to environmental conservation.

# Commitment to Net Zero Emissions (ESG E-06, E-07)

The Company is committed to actively participating in addressing global climate change by targeting Net Zero Emissions by 2060. Net Zero refers to a state in which greenhouse gas emissions released into the atmosphere are fully balanced by the amount of emissions absorbed or eliminated, resulting in no net increase in emissions. To support this commitment, the Company has developed a Decarbonization Roadmap covering reductions in Scope 1 emissions (direct emissions from fossil fuels) and Scope 2 emissions (indirect emissions from electricity consumption).

As part of the roadmap implementation, the Company has set out focused action plans for 2025, including:

1. Scope 1 Emission Reductions
a. Vehicle Fleet Transition
The Company will begin implementing a green mobility strategy by downsizing its fleet, optimizing routes, and replacing fossil-fueled vehicles with electric vehicles (EVs), scheduled to commence in June 2025.
