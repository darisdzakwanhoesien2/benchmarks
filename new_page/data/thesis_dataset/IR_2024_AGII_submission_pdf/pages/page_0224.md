# Sistem Manajemen Risiko
# Risk Management System

Perseroan berkomitmen untuk menerapkan sistem manajemen risiko yang merupakan bagian dari pengelolaan GCG yang komprehensif. Sistem ini dirancang untuk mengidentifikasi, menganalisis, dan mengelola risiko yang dapat memengaruhi pencapaian tujuan bisnis, dengan pendekatan proaktif untuk mengelola risiko finansial, operasional, dan reputasi secara efektif. Mengacu pada standar ISO 9001, sistem ini mencakup proses identifikasi, evaluasi, dan pengendalian risiko-risiko yang dapat menghambat aktivitas bisnis dan kelangsungan usaha Perseroan.

The Company is committed to implementing a risk management system as part of a comprehensive GCG management framework. This system is designed to identify, analyze, and manage risks that may affect the achievement of business objectives, with a proactive approach to effectively manage financial, operational, and reputational risks. Referring to the ISO 9001 standard, this system includes processes for identifying, evaluating, and controlling risks that could hinder business activities and the Company's sustainability.

# Risk Profile and Mitigation [IR-4D]

The Company has established a risk profile along with mitigation measures, which are presented as follows.
