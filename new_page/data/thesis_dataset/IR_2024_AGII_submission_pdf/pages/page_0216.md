Head of Internal Audit Unit

Selain itu, beliau pernah menjabat sebagai Assistant Manager of Special Audit Division Indonesia Financial Group (IFG) (2022-2023).

Professional Accountant, Certified Forensic Auditor (CFrA), Sertifikasi Auditor Muda, dan Certified Fraud Examiner (CFE) Candidate.
