# Sertifikasi Personil Unit Audit Internal

Sertifikasi yang dimiliki oleh personil Unit Audit Internal Perseroan hingga tahun 2024, antara lain:

1. Certified Professional Fraud Investigator (CPFI);
2. Certified Professional Information Technology Auditor (CPITA);
3. Certified Effective Communication Skills at Work (CECSW);
4. Financial Risk Management Certified Professional (FRMCP); serta
5. Certified Internal Control Specialist (CICS).

# Tugas dan Tanggung Jawab

Tugas dan tanggung jawab Unit Audit Internal diuraikan sebagai berikut.

1. Menyusun strategi dan rencana kerja audit tahunan serta rencana pengembangan kemampuan dan keterampilan auditor sehingga selalu update dan inline dengan perkembangan Perseroan sesuai masukan dari Direktur Utama dan Dewan Komisaris.
2. Mempersiapkan dan melaksanakan audit atas efektivitas sistem pengendalian internal dan sistem manajemen risiko sesuai dengan kebijakan Perseroan.
3. Mempersiapkan dan melaksanakan audit kepatuhan terhadap jalannya pengelolaan Perseroan untuk memastikan bahwa seluruh kegiatan Perseroan telah mematuhi peraturan yang berlaku.
4. Mempersiapkan dan melaksanakan audit operasional untuk mengukur tingkat efisiensi dan efektivitas semua kegiatan Perseroan.
5. Mempersiapkan dan melaksanakan audit khusus (investigative audit) atas permintaan dari Manajemen.
6. Membuat dan menyampaikan laporan audit, rekomendasi dan tindak lanjut yang belum, sedang, dan telah dilaksanakan kepada Direktur Utama.

# Independensi

Unit Audit Internal berkomitmen untuk bekerja secara independen, profesional, dan tanpa benturan kepentingan dalam melaksanakan tugasnya. Unit Audit Internal juga senantiasa mematuhi Standar Profesi dan Kode Etik yang tercantum dalam Piagam Audit Internal.

The certifications held by the Internal Audit Unit personnel as of 2024 include:

1. Certified Professional Fraud Investigator (CPFI);
2. Certified Professional Information Technology Auditor (CPITA);
3. Certified Effective Communication Skills at Work (CECSW);
4. Financial Risk Management Certified Professional (FRMCP); and
5. Certified Internal Control Specialist (CICS).

# Duties and Responsibilities

The duties and responsibilities of the Internal Audit Unit are described as follows.

1. Developing an annual audit strategy, work plan, and auditor's skills development to ensure they are up-to date and in line with the Company's development with inputs from the President Director and the Board of Commissioners.
2. Preparing and auditing the effectiveness of internal control and risk management following the Company policy.
3. Preparing and conducting compliance audits on the Company's management to ensure that all activities follow the Company's policies/rules and regulations and the applicable state legal regulations.
4. Preparing and carrying out operational audits to measure the efficiency and effectiveness of all the Company's activities.
5. Preparing and carrying out an investigative audit if requested by the Management.
6. Creating and submitting audit reports, recommendations, and follow-ups that have yet to be, are being, or have been implemented to the President Director.

# Independence

The Internal Audit Unit is committed to work independently, professionally, and without conflicts of interest in carrying out its duties. The Internal Audit Unit also consistently adheres to the Professional Standards and Code of Ethics outlined in the Internal Audit Charter.
