# Pengelolaan Limbah

Perseroan mengelola limbah yang dihasilkan secara bertanggung jawab untuk meminimalkan dampak negatif terhadap lingkungan, termasuk pencemaran tanah, air, dan udara, yang dapat memengaruhi kesehatan manusia serta mendukung mitigasi perubahan iklim. Limbah yang dihasilkan mencakup limbah padat dan cair, terdiri dari limbah non-B3, seperti sampah kantor, kertas, dan alat tulis (ATK), serta limbah B3 dari kegiatan maintenance, bengkel, produksi, kantor, workshop, dan gudang. Contoh limbah B3 meliputi baterai bekas, pelumas atau oli bekas, kemasan B3, limbah elektronik, filter udara, limbah karbida, dan majun bekas. Untuk memastikan pengelolaan limbah sesuai standar, Perseroan mematuhi regulasi yang berlaku dan bekerja sama dengan pihak berizin untuk pengolahan limbah, khususnya limbah B3, guna mendukung keberlanjutan dan mengurangi dampak lingkungan. [GRI 3-3, 306-1]

# Waste Management

The Company manages its waste in a responsible manner to minimize negative impacts on the environment, including soil, water, and air pollution, which may affect human health and support climate change mitigation. Its waste includes solid and liquid waste, consisting non-toxic and hazardous (non-B3) waste, such as office waste, paper, and stationery, as well as toxic and hazardous (B3) waste from maintenance, work areas, production, office, workshop, and warehouse activities. Examples of B3 waste include used batteries, lubricants or oil, non-B3 packaging, electronic waste, air filters, carbide waste, and used cotton. To ensure its waste management meets standards, the Company complies with applicable regulations and cooperates with licensed parties for waste processing, especially B3 waste, to support sustainability and reduce environmental impacts. [GRI 3-3, 306-1]
