# Tata Kelola Keberlanjutan

## Sustainability Governance

![img-133.jpeg](img-133.jpeg)

Perseroan menjadikan tata kelola berkelanjutan sebagai strategi utama untuk memperkuat keunggulan kompetitif dan mendorong pertumbuhan jangka panjang perusahaan. Strategi ini diimplementasikan melalui praktik terbaik Good Corporate Governance (GCG) serta prinsip Environment, Social, and Governance (ESG), yang memastikan pengelolaan dampak lingkungan dan sosial secara efektif. Dengan pendekatan ini, Perseroan berkomitmen memberikan nilai berkelanjutan bagi seluruh pemangku kepentingan.

## Penanggung Jawab Penerapan Strategi Keberlanjutan [GRI 2-14] [POJK E.1]

Perseroan telah menunjuk Direktur Produksi dan Teknik sebagai pemimpin utama dalam penerapan strategi keberlanjutan. Direktur ini bertanggung jawab mengimplementasikan inisiatif keberlanjutan serta menyusun laporan terkait, sementara setiap unit bisnis diwajibkan melaporkan secara berkala kepada Direktur Process Improvement &amp; Keberlanjutan untuk mengevaluasi progres penerapan keberlanjutan perusahaan. Selain itu, bersama Direksi lainnya, Direktur Process Improvement dan Keberlanjutan mengawasi proses uji tuntas (due diligence) untuk mengidentifikasi dan mengelola dampak ekonomi, lingkungan, dan sosial yang dihasilkan oleh kegiatan bisnis. Perseroan juga melibatkan pemangku kepentingan melalui survei dan layanan pengaduan dalam kegiatan due diligence ini. Hasil dari proses tersebut kemudian disampaikan kepada Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris dalam rapat rutin, memungkinkan pengambilan keputusan yang tepat terkait dampak bisnis Perseroan.

The Company adopts sustainable governance as a core strategy to strengthen its competitive advantage and drive long-term growth. This strategy is implemented through best practices in Good Corporate Governance (GCG) and the Environment, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles, ensuring effective management of environmental and social impacts. Through this approach, the Company is committed to delivering sustainable value to all stakeholders.

## Responsibility for Implementing the Sustainability Strategy [GRI 2-14] [POJK E.1]

The Company has appointed the Director of Production and Technical as the primary leader in implementing the sustainability strategy. This Director is responsible for executing sustainability initiatives and preparing related reports, while each business unit is required to report regularly to the Director of Process Improvement &amp; Sustainability to evaluate the progress of the Company's sustainability efforts. Additionally, in collaboration with other Board of Directors members, the Director of Process Improvement &amp; Sustainability supervises the due diligence process to identify and manage the economic, environmental, and social impacts of business activities. The Company also engages stakeholders through surveys and complaint mechanisms as part of this due diligence process. The findings from these processes are then reported to the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners in regular meetings, enabling informed decision-making regarding the Company's business impact.
