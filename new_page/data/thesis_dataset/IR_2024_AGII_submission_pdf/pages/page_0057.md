Kontribusi sosial juga menjadi perhatian utama, di mana Perseroan menyalurkan dana corporate social responsibility (CSR) sebesar Rp4,06 miliar, meningkat signifikan dibandingkan Rp1,77 miliar pada tahun 2023. From the operational side, sustainable product innovation continued being developed, with 15 environmentally friendly products successfully introduced to the market. Commitment to sustainability was also reflected in the procurement policy, where 98.17% of total suppliers were local suppliers, an increase compared to the previous year, which recorded 97.74%. In line with this, the Company ensured the sustainability of the workforce by maintaining 100% Indonesian citizens as employees, and implementing competency development in accordance with the direction of business development. The Company allocated training costs of Rp1.11 billion, an increase from Rp1.08 billion in the previous year, with a total of 1,146 employees receiving training.

Making a social contribution was also a major priority, and the Company distributed corporate social responsibility (CSR) funds of Rp4.06 billion, which was a significant increase in comparison to Rp1.77 billion in 2023. The main focus of this social program included the development of health infrastructure, as the Company supplied more than 10,000 medical gas cylinders and completed installations in more than 250 hospitals in Sumatra, Java, Kalimantan, Sulawesi, Bali, and Nusa Tenggara. With a total investment of Rp127.3 billion, this initiative is expected to strengthen access to healthcare services and support the resilience of the medical sector in various regions.

As part of its environmental responsibility, the Company succeeded in reducing the intensity of energy consumption to 0.0033 GJ/m³ from 0.0034 GJ/m³ in the previous year, and reducing the intensity of emissions to 0.0007 tonsCO₂eq/m³. The efficiency of water use has also been optimised, in line with the reduction of non-hazardous and toxic waste by 61.48%, from 511.42 kg to 196.99 kg, which was managed in collaboration with the local Sanitary Office. Additionally, greening efforts were enhanced by planting 149 trees in open green spaces in Banjarbaru, Jakarta, and Surabaya in the "Safari Ramadhan" program, as well as the revitalization program for the Jagir River and its surroundings. To ensure the sustainability of these initiatives, the Company allocated funds of Rp14.59 billion, an increase of 110.13% compared to the previous year.
