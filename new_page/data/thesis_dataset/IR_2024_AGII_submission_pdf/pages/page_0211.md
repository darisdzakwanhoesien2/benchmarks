# Pedoman Kerja

Pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab Komite ESG mengikuti ketentuan yang tercantum dalam Terms of Reference [TOR] ESG Committee. TOR telah disahkan dan disetujui oleh Direksi pada tanggal 18 November 2024.

# Komposisi

Komite ESG dibentuk berdasarkan Surat Keputusan Dewan Komisaris No. 98/SK-Dekom/SIG/XI/2024, yang terdiri dari 5 orang anggota. Direktur Utama menjabat sebagai Ketua Komite ESG, dengan 4 anggota lainnya berasal dari jajaran Direksi, yaitu Chief Commercial Officer, Director Technical &amp; Compliance Officer, Director of Production &amp; Technical, serta Director of Process Improvement.

# Working Guidelines

The duties and responsibilities of the ESG Committee are carried out in accordance with the provisions outlined in the ESG Committee Terms of Reference [TOR]. The TOR was ratified and approved by the Board of Directors on November 18, 2024.

# Composition

The ESG Committee was established based on the Board of Commissioners Decision No. 98/SK-Dekom/SIG/XI/2024, consisting of 5 members. The President Director serves as the Chairman of the ESG Committee, with the other 4 members comes from the Board of Directors: the Chief Commercial Officer, Director Technical &amp; Compliance Officer, Director of Production &amp; Technical, and Director of Process Improvement.

# Tugas dan Tanggung Jawab

Komite ESG bertugas mengembangkan dan merancang strategi ESG Perseroan, termasuk mengawasi sumber risiko dan peluang ESG yang paling material, tindakan-tindakan yang terkait, serta proses pelaporannya.

# Wewenang

Komite ESG juga memiliki wewenang yang telah diatur dalam TOR Komite ESG, antara lain:

1. Mencari informasi yang diperlukan dari setiap karyawan Perseroan, di mana seluruh karyawan telah diarahkan untuk bekerja sama dengan Komite ESG;
2. Melakukan kerja sama dan berkoordinasi seperlunya dengan seluruh komite pendukung Dewan Komisaris lainnya;
3. Menggunakan jasa lembaga eksternal serta memperoleh nasihat hukum atau profesional independen dari luar, jika diperlukan;
4. Mengakses sumber daya yang memadai untuk melaksanakan tugasnya;
5. Mengundang Direktur, pejabat Perseroan atau Entitas Anak, maupun pihak lain untuk menghadiri rapat Komite sebagai Invitee dari waktu ke waktu sesuai kebutuhan; serta

# Duties and Responsibilities

The ESG Committee is responsible for developing and designing the Company's ESG strategy, including supervising the most material ESG risks and opportunities, related actions, and the reporting process.

# Authority

The ESG Committee also holds the authority as outlined in the ESG Committee's TOR, including:

1. Requesting necessary information from any Company's employee, with all employees directed to cooperate with the ESG Committee;
2. Collaborating and coordinating as needed with all other supporting committees of the Board of Commissioners;
3. Engaging external institutions and obtaining independent legal or professional advice, if necessary;
4. Accessing adequate resources to perform its duties;
5. Inviting Directors, Company or Subsidiary officers, or other parties to attend Committee meetings as Invitees from time to time as needed; and
