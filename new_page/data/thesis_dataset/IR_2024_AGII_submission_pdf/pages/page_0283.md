Selain penerapan hierarki pengendalian risiko, Perseroan juga mengadopsi kebijakan tambahan untuk memitigasi risiko kecelakaan kerja secara proaktif. Salah satu kebijakan tersebut adalah mewajibkan penggunaan APD, seperti kacamata, helm, sepatu, dan sarung tangan, bagi seluruh pekerja, termasuk mereka yang bertugas mendistribusikan produk. Perseroan juga secara konsisten melakukan perawatan berkala terhadap seluruh sarana dan prasarana, serta menerapkan kebijakan ketat di lingkungan pabrik, yang meliputi:

1. Pelarangan merokok di seluruh area pabrik guna mencegah potensi bahaya kebakaran;
2. Pemeliharaan berkala terhadap peralatan pabrik untuk memastikan operasional berjalan dengan aman dan efisien;
3. Penerapan sistem kerja yang aman, yang dirancang untuk melindungi karyawan dan menjaga lingkungan sekitar;
4. Penyusunan program kerja yang bertujuan memastikan kesehatan, keselamatan kerja, dan lingkungan (K3L), serta mengidentifikasi dan mengendalikan potensi bahaya terkait seluruh proses produksi. Program ini diperbaiki secara berkesinambungan;
5. Pemeliharaan tempat kerja agar tetap aman dan bebas dari potensi bahaya yang dapat mengancam K3L;
6. Penyediaan APD yang sesuai bagi karyawan dan pengunjung, guna mendukung keselamatan mereka selama berada di lingkungan Perseroan;
7. Penerapan kebijakan kebersihan lingkungan yang terintegrasi dengan kebijakan K3L, dengan memastikan tempat kerja selalu dalam kondisi bersih untuk mencegah terjadinya kecelakaan besar (major accident); serta
8. Implementasi sistem reward and punishment, yang tercantum dalam Peraturan Perseroan, untuk memastikan kepatuhan terhadap kebijakan K3L.

Sebagai bagian dari upaya tersebut, Perseroan juga membentuk Tim Tanggap Darurat (ERT) untuk memastikan kesiapan menghadapi situasi darurat yang berpotensi menimbulkan dampak terhadap K3L. Perseroan juga memastikan kesiapan tanggap darurat melalui berbagai langkah berikut.

1. Pelatihan Tim Tanggap Darurat (ERT) untuk meningkatkan kemampuan dalam menghadapi situasi darurat.
2. Penyediaan dan pemeriksaan rutin terhadap peralatan serta fasilitas yang diperlukan dalam penanganan keadaan darurat.
3. Sistem proteksi keamanan kebakaran sebagai program prioritas HSE, meliputi:
a. Instalasi proteksi aktif, seperti hydrant, sprinkler, alat pendeteksi api, dan alat pemadam api ringan;
b. Pelatihan pemadaman api secara rutin; serta
c. Pelaksanaan emergency drill setiap tahun untuk memastikan kesiapan seluruh pihak.

4. Using administrative measures, such as work procedures or training, to control potential hazards related to processes, equipment/machinery, raw materials/materials, or behavior.
5. Utilizing Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) as the last layer of protection to protect workers from the remaining risks.

In addition to the implementation of the risk control hierarchy, the Company also adopted additional policies to proactively reduce the risk of work-related accidents. One of these policies is to mandate the use of PPE, such as glasses, helmets, shoes and gloves, for all workers, including those responsible for product distribution. The Company also consistently conducts regular maintenance of all its facilities and infrastructure, as well as enforces strict policies within the factory environment, which include:

1. Prohibiting smoking within the factory premises to prevent potential fire hazards;
2. Conducting routine maintenance of factory equipment to ensure operations run safely and efficiently;
3. Safe work system Implementation, designed to protect employees and maintain the surrounding environment;
4. Preparation of work programs aimed at ensuring Occupational Health, Safety, and the Environment (OHSE), as well as identifying and controlling potential hazards related to the entire production process. The program is continuously improved;
5. Maintenance of the workplace to remain safe and free from potential hazards that can threaten OHSE;
6. Provision of appropriate PPE for employees and visitors, to support their safety while in the Company's environment;
7. Implementation of environmental sanitation policy that are integrated with OHSE policy, by ensuring that the workplace is always in a clean condition to prevent major accidents; and
8. Implementing a reward and punishment system, as stated in the Company Regulations, to ensure compliance with the OHSE policy.

As part of these efforts, the Company also established an Emergency Response Team (ERT) to ensure preparedness to deal with emergency situations that have the potential that may impact on OHSE. The Company also ensures emergency response preparedness through the following several measures.

1. Emergency Response Team (ERT) training to improve the capability to deal with emergency situations.
2. Provision and regular inspection of equipment and facilities required to deal with emergencies.
3. Fire safety protection system as a HSE priority program, including:
a. Active protection installations, such as hydrants, sprinklers, fire detection equipment, and light fire extinguishers;
b. Routine fire extinguishing training; and
c. Conducts emergency drills every year to ensure the preparedness of all parties.
