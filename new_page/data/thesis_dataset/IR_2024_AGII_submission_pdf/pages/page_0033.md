## Peresmian Filing Station Pekanbaru, Riau
Perseroan meresmikan gedung baru di Stasiun Pengisian Pekanbaru yang telah melakukan renovasi serta perluasan area panggung pengisian guna memenuhi kebutuhan pasar dan operasional Perseroan yang kian meningkat setiap waktu.

## Filing Station Opening Ceremony at Pekanbaru, Riau
The Company officially opened a new building at the Pekanbaru Filling Station, which has undergone renovations and expansion of the filling area to meet the increasing market and the Company's operational needs.

## Saham Perseroan Terindeks dalam Indonesian Sharia Stock Index (ISSI) 2024
Saham AGII terpilih masuk sebagai salah satu konstitusi ISSI berdasarkan kriteria oleh Otoritas Jasa Keuangan dan Sekuritas Sharia (DES) untuk periode Juni 2024-November 2024.

## The Company's Shares Indexed in the Indonesian Sharia Stock Index (ISSI) 2024
AGII shares were selected as one of the constituents of the ISSI based on the criteria determined by the Financial Services Authority and Sharia Securities (DES) for the period of June 2024-November 2024.

## Green Ngabuburit: Tree for City Banjarbaru, Jakarta, dan Surabaya
Acara buka puasa bersama yang dilaksanakan di Ruang Terbuka Hijau (RTH) di 3 kota di Indonesia. Selain buka puasa, juga dilaksanakan kegiatan penanaman 5 bibit pohon flamboyan dan philodendron, serta penyerahan 50 bibit pohon palem kipas, lili paris, dan pucuk merah dari Samator Group kepada Kelurahan Manggarai.

## Green Ngabuburit: Tree for City in Banjarbaru, Jakarta, and Surabaya
A joint iftar event held at Green Open Spaces (RTH) in 3 cities in Indonesia. In addition to the iftar, activities included planting 5 flamboyant and philodendron saplings, as well as the donation of 50 saplings of fan palms, paris lilies, and red shoots from the Samator Group to the Manggarai Subdistrict.

## Pelaksanaan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Tahun Buku 2023
RUPS Tahun Buku 2023 dilaksanakan di The Westin Jakarta dan menyetujui beberapa Mata Acara, seperti perubahan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan terkait penambahan kegiatan usaha dan pembagian dividen tunai sebesar Rp33,1 miliar.

## Annual General Meeting of Shareholders for the 2023 Fiscal Year
The AGM for the 2023 Fiscal Year was held at The Westin Jakarta and approved several agenda items, such as amendments to the Articles of Association related to the addition of business activities and the distribution of cash dividends amounting to Rp33.1 billion.
