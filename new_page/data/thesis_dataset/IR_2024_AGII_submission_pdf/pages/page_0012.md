# Cakupan dan Batasan
Laporan Terintegrasi [GRI 2-2] [IR-3F] [IR-3G]

Laporan Terintegrasi Perseroan menyajikan cakupan data terkait aspek lingkungan yang semakin luas setiap tahunnya. Penyampaian informasi ini dilakukan secara bertahap, menyesuaikan dengan perkembangan sistem pencatatan dan pelaporan di berbagai fasilitas operasional.

# Scope and Boundary of the Integrated Report [GRI 2-2] [IR-3F] [IR-3G]

The Integrated Report presents an increasingly broader scope of data related to environmental aspects each year. This information is provided gradually, in line with the development of the recording and reporting systems across various operational facilities.
