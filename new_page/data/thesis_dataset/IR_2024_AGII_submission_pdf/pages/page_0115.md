# Tinjauan Makro Ekonomi

## Macroeconomic Review

Pada tahun 2024, perekonomian global masih mengalami pertumbuhan yang moderat di tengah ketidakpastian yang tinggi. Berdasarkan proyeksi International Monetary Fund (IMF) dalam World Economic Outlook (WEO) edisi Oktober 2024, pertumbuhan ekonomi global diperkirakan mencapai 3,2% pada tahun 2024 dan 2025^{[1]}. Inflasi global menunjukkan tren penurunan dari puncaknya sebesar 9,4% pada kuartal ketiga 2022 menjadi 3,5% pada akhir 2025, meskipun di beberapa negara masih menghadapi tantangan inflasi yang tinggi^{[1]}. Selain itu, risiko ketidakpastian semakin meningkat akibat tensi geopolitik, dinamika politik, potensi perang dagang, serta kebijakan proteksionisme yang dapat berdampak pada stabilitas ekonomi global, arus investasi, dan perdagangan internasional.

Menjelang akhir tahun 2024, sebagian besar harga komoditas global mengalami pelemahan, terutama di sektor energi, pangan, dan pertambangan. Namun, harga minyak sawit mengalami peningkatan seiring dengan tingginya permintaan serta kekhawatiran terhadap kecukupan pasokan^{[2]}. Sejalan dengan moderasi inflasi, beberapa bank sentral di negara maju mulai melonggarkan kebijakan moneternya. The Federal Reserve (The Fed) telah mengisyaratkan kebijakan penurunan suku bunga pada tahun 2024 seiring dengan perlambatan inflasi^{[1]}. Namun, belum terdapat konfirmasi resmi mengenai pemangkasan suku bunga 25 basis poin menjadi 4,50%-4,75% pada Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) November 2024.

In 2024, the global economy continued to experience moderate growth amid high uncertainty. According to the International Monetary Fund (IMF) projections in the October 2024 edition of the World Economic Outlook (WEO), global economic growth was expected to reach 3.2% in both 2024 and 2025^{[1]}. Global inflation showed a declining trend, falling from its peak of 9.4% in third quarter 2022 to 3.5% by the end of 2025, although some countries still face high inflation challenges^{[1]}. In addition, uncertainty risks continue to rise due to geopolitical tensions, political dynamics, potential trade wars, and protectionist policies, all of which could impact global economic stability, investment flows, and international trade.

Towards the end of 2024, most global commodity prices weakened, especially in the energy, food, and mining sectors. However, palm oil prices increased due to high demand and supply concerns^{[2]}. With inflation moderating, several central banks in developed countries have begun to ease monetary policies. The Federal Reserve (The Fed) gave a signal of rate cut in 2024, in line with slowing inflation^{[1]}. However, there has been no official confirmation regarding the expected 25 basis point rate cut to 4.50%-4.75% at the November 2024 Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meeting.
