# Sistem Manajemen HSE Kontraktor (GRI 403-7)

Perseroan menerapkan prosedur K3L yang ketat untuk memastikan kontraktor yang bekerja di lingkungan perusahaan mematuhi standar keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja. Langkah-langkah pengendalian terhadap kontraktor diungkapkan sebagai berikut.

1. Pelatihan Orientasi K3L

Kontraktor yang akan melakukan pekerjaan berisiko tinggi, seperti bekerja di ruang terbatas, ketinggian, atau dengan suhu ekstrem, wajib mengikuti pelatihan orientasi keselamatan dari Unit HSE. Pelatihan mencakup informasi tentang bahaya, formulir izin kerja, dan penggunaan APD.

2. Pengenalan Lokasi Kerja

Manajer kontraktor atau supervisor proyek memberikan pengenalan lokasi, termasuk peralatan darurat dan sistem evakuasi, kepada seluruh pekerja kontraktor sebelum pekerjaan dimulai.

3. Kepatuhan pada Standar Keselamatan

Selama bekerja di area Perseroan, kontraktor dan pekerjaanya wajib mematuhi standar keselamatan yang berlaku. Proyek diawasi oleh Supervisor Proyek, HSE Officer, dan Security, yang akan melakukan tindakan koreksi jika ditemukan penyimpangan terhadap prosedur K3L.

4. Sanksi atas Ketidakpatuhan

Jika kontraktor melanggar prosedur atau standar K3L, General Manager berwenang memberikan sanksi, seperti teguran, surat peringatan, denda, hingga pemutusan kontrak.

# HSE Management System for Contractors (GRI 403-7)

The Company has implemented strict OHSE procedures to ensure that contractors working within the Company environment comply with occupational health and safety standards. Control measures for contractors are described as follows.

1. OHSE Orientation Training

Contractors who will perform high risk work, such as working in limited spaces, heights, or with extreme temperatures, must take part in work safety induction training from the HSE Unit. Training includes information about hazards, work permit forms, and the use of PPE.

2. Workplace Orientation

The contractor's manager or project supervisor provides an introduction to the location, including emergency equipment and evacuation systems, to all contractor workers before work will be carried out.

3. Compliance with Safety Standards

While working in the Company's area, contractors and their workers are required to comply with applicable safety standards. The project is supervised by the project Supervisor, HSE Officer, and Security, who will take corrective action if any deviations from OHSE procedures are found.

4. Sanctions for Non-Compliance

If the contractor violates OHSE procedures or standards, the General Manager has the right to impose sanctions, such as giving a warning, warning letters, fines, and even termination of the contract.
