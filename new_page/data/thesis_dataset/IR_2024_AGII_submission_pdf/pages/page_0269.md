# Keanekaragaman Hayati [POJK F.9, F.10]

Perseroan memahami pentingnya menjaga kelangsungan keanekaragaman hayati sebagai bagian dari komitmen terhadap keberlanjutan. Keanekaragaman hayati mencakup semua bentuk kehidupan, seperti tumbuhan, hewan, mikroorganisme, serta interaksi ekologis yang mendukung ekosistem. Ekosistem ini memberikan manfaat penting bagi manusia, termasuk penyediaan air bersih, udara segar, keamanan pangan, kesehatan, dan kesejahteraan. [GRI 3-3]

Sebagai langkah mitigasi risiko terhadap keanekaragaman hayati, seluruh pabrik Perseroan berlokasi di kawasan industri yang telah dikembangkan dan tidak berdekatan dengan kawasan konservasi lingkungan. Penempatan ini memastikan kegiatan operasional tidak mengancam ekosistem alami atau spesies yang dilindungi.

Langkah keberlanjutan ini juga tercermin dalam distribusi produk gas yang menggunakan tanker dan tabung yang dapat digunakan kembali. Proses distribusi dilakukan sesuai dengan standar keselamatan dan lingkungan untuk mencegah dampak negatif terhadap ekosistem di sekitar wilayah operasional. [GRI 304-1, 304-2] [POJK F.9]

Selain itu, Perseroan juga aktif dalam program penghijauan seperti program penanaman 149 pohon di Ruang Terbuka Hijau (RTH) Banjarbaru, Jakarta, dan Surabaya dalam program "Safari Ramadhan" serta program revitalisasi Sungai Jagir dan sekitarnya. Hal ini menunjukkan komitmen Perseroan terhadap pentingnya menjaga kebersihan lingkungan demi terciptanya ruang yang nyaman bagi berbagai aktivitas. [GRI 304-3] [POJK F.10]

# Biodiversity [POJK F.9, F.10]

The Company recognizes the importance of maintaining biodiversity sustainability as part of its commitment to sustainability. Biodiversity includes all forms of life, such as plants, animals, microorganisms, and the ecological interactions that support ecosystems. These ecosystems provide important benefits to humans, including the provision of clean water, fresh air, food security, health, and welfare. [GRI 3-3]

In order to mitigate risks to biodiversity, all of the Company's factories are located in developed industrial areas and are not adjacent to environmental conservation areas. This placement ensures that operational activities do not threaten natural ecosystems or protected species.

This sustainability measure is also reflected in the distribution of gas products using reusable tankers and cylinders. The distribution process is carried out in accordance with safety and environmental standards to prevent negative impacts on the ecosystem around the operational area. [GRI 304-1, 304-2] [POJK F.9]

In addition, the Company is also actively participated in reforestation programs such as the planting program of 149 trees in the Green Open Space (RTH) of Banjarbaru, Jakarta, and Surabaya in the "Safari Ramadhan" program and the revitalization program of the Jagir River and its surroundings. This shows the Company's commitment to the importance of maintaining environmental cleanliness to create a comfortable space for various activities. [GRI 304-3] [POJK F.10]
