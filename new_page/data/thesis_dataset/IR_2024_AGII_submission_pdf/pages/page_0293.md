# Pernyataan Pertanggungjawaban Laporan Terintegrasi 2024

Responsibility Statement Towards 2024 Integrated Report

Surat Pernyataan Anggota Dewan Komisaris tentang Tanggung Jawab Atas Laporan Terintegrasi 2024 PT Samator Indo Gas Tbk

Statement of Members of the Board of Commissioners on the Responsibility for the 2024 Integrated Report of PT Samator Indo Gas Tbk

Kami, yang bertanda tangan di bawah ini, menyatakan bahwa semua informasi dalam Laporan Terintegrasi PT Samator Indo Gas Tbk tahun 2024 telah dimuat secara lengkap dan bertanggung jawab penuh atas kebenaran isi Laporan Terintegrasi Perseroan.

Demikian pernyataan ini dibuat dengan sebenarnya.

We, the undersigned, hereby declare that all information in the Integrated Report of PT Samator Indo Gas Tbk for year 2024 has been fully contained and we shall be fully responsible to the correctness of contents in the Integrated Report of the Company.

This statement is hereby made in all truthfulness.

Jakarta, April 2025

Dewan Komisaris
Board of Commissioners
