Laporan Keberlanjutan Sustainability Report

Perseroan menetapkan struktur dan besaran kompensasi bagi Direksi dan karyawan berdasarkan prinsip kewajaran, kinerja, serta kondisi industri. Perbandingan kompensasi antara tingkat manajerial tertinggi dan median karyawan dipantau secara berkala untuk memastikan kesetaraan internal dan daya saing eksternal. Pada tahun 2024, terjadi perubahan dalam struktur kompensasi manajemen puncak, sejalan dengan penyesuaian tanggung jawab dan pencapaian kinerja. Namun demikian, Perseroan tidak mengungkapkan angka spesifik rasio kompensasi secara publik. [GRI 2-21]

# Keberagaman Komposisi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi [ESG 0-01]

Perseroan menetapkan keberagaman dalam komposisi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi untuk memastikan bahwa berbagai perspektif, pengalaman, keahlian, dan latar belakang dapat diakomodasi dalam pengambilan keputusan strategis. Keberagaman ini mendukung sinergi antar anggota dan berkontribusi pada kinerja yang optimal. Seluruh anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi yang menjabat telah memenuhi aspek keberagaman, yang dapat dilihat lebih rinci pada profil masing-masing anggota dalam Laporan Terintegrasi ini.

# Pengelolaan Benturan Kepentingan Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi [GRI 2-15] [ESG 0-09]

Perseroan menetapkan pengelolaan benturan kepentingan untuk memastikan bahwa keputusan yang diambil oleh Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dilakukan secara objektif dan adil, serta untuk melindungi kepentingan Perseroan. Beberapa hal yang harus diperhatikan oleh Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi untuk menghindari benturan kepentingan, yaitu:

1. Menghindari adanya benturan kepentingan di dalam melaksanakan tugasnya. Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi tidak akan memanfaatkan jabatan untuk kepentingan pribadi atau untuk kepentingan orang atau pihak lain yang terkait;
2. Menghindari setiap aktivitas yang dapat memengaruhi independensi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dalam tugas pengawasan Perseroan;
3. Mengisi Daftar Khusus yang berisikan kepemilikan saham Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dan/atau keluarganya pada perusahaan lain;
4. Apabila terjadi benturan kepentingan, maka harus diungkapkan, dan Dewan Komisaris maupun Direksi yang bersangkutan tidak boleh melibatkan diri dalam proses pengambilan keputusan Perseroan; serta
5. Membuat pernyataan mengenai benturan kepentingan terhadap hal-hal yang termaktub dalam Rencana Kerja dan Anggaran Perseroan.

The Company determines the compensation structure and amount for the Board of Directors and employees based on the principles of fairness, performance, and industry conditions. The compensation ratio between the highest managerial level and the median employee salary is regularly monitored to ensure internal equity and external competitiveness. In 2024, changes were made to the Top Management compensation structure, aligning with adjustments in responsibilities and performance achievements. However, the Company does not publicly disclose specific compensation ratio figures. [GRI 2-21]

# Diversity of the Composition of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors [ESG 0-01]

The Company establishes diversity in the composition of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors to ensure that various perspectives, experiences, expertise, and backgrounds are accommodated in strategic decision-making. This diversity supports synergy among members and contributes to optimal performance. All members of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors in office meet the aspects of diversity, which can be found in more detail in the profile of each member in this Integrated Report.

# Management of Conflict of Interest of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors [GRI 2-15] [ESG 0-09]

The Company establishes the management of conflicts of interest to ensure that decisions made by the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors are carried out objectively and fairly, and to protect the Company's interests. Several aspects that must be considered by the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors to avoid conflicts of interest include:

1. Avoiding conflicts of interest in carrying out their duties. The Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors will not use their position for personal interests or for the benefit of others or related parties;
2. Avoiding any activities that may affect the independence of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors in supervising the Company;
3. Filling out a Special List containing the share ownership of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors and/or their families in other companies;
4. In case of a conflict of interest, it must be disclosed, and the concerned member of the Board of Commissioners or Board of Directors should not be involved in the Company's decision-making process; and
5. Making a statement regarding conflicts of interest related to matters outlined in the Work Plan and Budget.
