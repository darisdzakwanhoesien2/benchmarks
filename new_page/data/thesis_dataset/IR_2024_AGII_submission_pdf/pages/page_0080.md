# Riwayat Pendidikan

Beliau menyelesaikan pendidikan Sekolah Menengah Atas di SMA Petra 2 Surabaya dan meraih gelar dalam Program Pengembangan Eksekutif bernama "Strategic Action Program" yang diadakan oleh P2EB Universitas Gadjah Mada Yogyakarta pada tahun 2015.

# Riwayat Jabatan

Beliau memulai karier sebagai Direktur PT Samator (1992-2007) dan Komisaris Perseroan (2004-2006).

# Rangkap Jabatan

Per 31 Desember 2024, beliau juga menjabat sebagai Wakil Direktur Utama PT Samator; Komisaris PT Samator Gas Industri, PT Aneka Mega Energi, PT Samabayu Mandala, PT Satyadhika Bakti Finance, PT Arohera, PT Sandana Arohera, PT Samator LNG, PT Samator Land, dan PT Sandana Aneka Medika; serta Direktur Utama PT Sandana, PT Samator Wase Gas, PT Samator Pharmaceutical, PT Sandana Baswara Gas.

# Hubungan Afiliasi

Beliau memiliki hubungan afiliasi dengan anggota Dewan Komisaris lainnya, anggota Direksi, serta Pemegang Saham Utama dan/atau Pengendali.

# Educational Background

He completed high school at Petra 2 High School, Surabaya, and received a degree at the Executive Development Program called "Strategic Action Program" organized by P2EB of Gadjah Mada University, Yogyakarta, in 2015.

# Career Background

He started his career as a Director at PT Samator (1992-2007) and a Commissioner at the Company (2004-2006).

# Concurrent Position

As of December 31, 2024, he also serves as the Vice President Director of PT Samator; Commissioner of PT Samator Gas Industri, PT Aneka Mega Energi, PT Samabayu Mandala, PT Satyadhika Bakti Finance, PT Arohera, PT Sandana Arohera, PT Samator LNG, PT Samator Land, and PT Sandana Aneka Medika; as well as the President Director of PT Sandana, PT Samator Wase Gas, PT Samator Pharmaceutical, and PT Sandana Baswara Gas.
