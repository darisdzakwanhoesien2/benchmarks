# Tantangan dan Kebijakan Strategis yang Berkelanjutan

Di tengah peluang pertumbuhan tersebut, industri gas nasional masih menghadapi sejumlah tantangan, termasuk bagi Perseroan. Indeks PMI Indonesia yang sempat mengalami kontraksi dan tren menurun dibandingkan tahun sebelumnya mencerminkan perlambatan sektor manufaktur, yang secara langsung berdampak pada permintaan gas industri. Tantangan teknis dalam operasional dan fluktuasi harga energi global juga menjadi faktor yang perlu direspons dengan tepat. Di samping itu, tuntutan terhadap pengurangan emisi karbon dan kebutuhan investasi pada teknologi hijau menuntut adaptasi yang progresif dari para pelaku industri, termasuk Perseroan.

Dalam menghadapi ketidakpastian ini, Perseroan menerapkan strategi yang komprehensif dan adaptif melalui beberapa fokus utama berikut.

1. Serve the Market, Set the Trend, and Match Expectation ("Serve, Set, and Match")

Perseroan aktif memperluas portofolio bisnis dengan mendiversifikasi pelanggan pada sektor-sektor strategis seperti sektor kesehatan, energi terbarukan, infrastruktur dan consumer goods. Permintaan gas industri pada sektor-sektor ini diprediksi akan terus meningkat yang didorong oleh kebutuhan layanan kesehatan yang berkelanjutan, transisi global menuju energi bersih serta pembangunan berkelanjutan.

Perseroan juga terus berinovasi dalam pengembangan aplikasi produk dengan mengidentifikasi peluang pasar yang belum tergarap sepenuhnya. Fokus utamanya mendukung transisi menuju ekonomi rendah karbon melalui pengembangan teknologi seperti carbon capture, utilization, and storage (CCUS), hydrogen rendah karbon, co-firing hydrogen, enhanced oil recovery (EOR) dan lainnya. Perseroan juga terus berinovasi dalam mengembangkan beberapa aplikasi gas industri lainnya yang menunjang kebutuhan operasional pelanggan.

Untuk memastikan kepuasan pelanggan, Perseroan menjamin kualitas dan pengiriman produk tepat waktu untuk pelanggan, serta memperhatikan praktik bisnis yang etis dan berkelanjutan demi kesejahteraan masyarakat dan lingkungan.

Perseroan telah menerapkan beberapa strategi di 2024 untuk meningkatkan proses operasional agar lebih efisien dan efektif, seperti:

# Challenges and Sustainable Strategic Policies

Amidst these growth opportunities, the national gas industry still faced a number of challenges, as did the Company. The Indonesian PMI index, which experienced a contraction and a downward trend compared to the previous year, reflected a slowdown in the manufacturing sector, which had a direct impact on demand for industrial gas. Technical challenges in operations and fluctuations in global energy prices were also factors that required a precise response. Also, demands for reducing carbon emissions and the need for investment in green technology demand progressive adaptation from industry players, including the Company.

In order to face this uncertainty, the Company has implemented a comprehensive and adaptive strategy through the following key focuses.

1. Serve the Market, Set the Trend, and Match Expectation ("Serve, Set, and Match")

The Company is actively expanding its business portfolio by diversifying customers in strategic sectors such as healthcare, renewable energy, infrastructure and consumer goods. Demand for industrial gas in these sectors is predicted to continue to increase, driven by the need for sustainable healthcare services, the global transition to clean energy and sustainable development.

The Company also continues to innovate with regards to developing product applications by identifying market opportunities that have not been fully exploited. Its main focus is supporting the transition to a low-carbon economy through the development of technologies such as carbon capture, utilization, and storage (CCUS), low-carbon hydrogen, co-firing hydrogen, Enhanced Oil Recovery (EOR) and others. The Company also continues to innovate through developing several other industrial gas applications that facilitate customer operational needs.

To ensure customer satisfaction, the Company guarantees the quality and timely delivery of products to customers, while paying attention to ethical and sustainable business practices for the welfare of society and the environment.
