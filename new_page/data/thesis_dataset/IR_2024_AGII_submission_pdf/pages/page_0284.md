Selain memastikan kesiapan tanggap darurat, Perseroan juga memprioritaskan komunikasi dan konsultasi sebagai elemen penting dalam pengelolaan K3L. Komunikasi, partisipasi dan konsultasi ini difasilitasi untuk karyawan internal dan juga pemangku kepentingan eksternal seperti kontraktor, pemasok, subkontraktor, pelanggan, badan pemerintah, masyarakat, LSM, konsultan, perguruan tinggi, dan badan sertifikasi yang memiliki akses untuk mengonsultasikan masalah terkait K3L kepada Unit HSE, termasuk kepada pihak eksternal yang relevan. Sebagai bentuk partisipasi, konsultasi, dan komunikasi K3L, Perseroan secara rutin mengadakan kegiatan berikut.

1. Rapat Tengah Tahun, yang dihadiri oleh Direksi, untuk membahas:
a. Pelaksanaan rencana HSE;
b. Pekerjaan yang sedang berlangsung;
c. Sistem Manajemen HSE (SMHSE);
d. Evaluasi SMHSE; serta
e. Tindak lanjut hasil Management Walk Through.

2. Rapat Bulanan Manajemen Proyek HSE, yang mencakup:
a. Evaluasi kinerja K3L;
b. Pembahasan permasalahan di lapangan;
c. Analisis leading indicators;
d. Peninjauan kejadian kecelakaan kerja dan pencemaran lingkungan;
e. Status tindak lanjut permasalahan; serta
f. Pelatihan K3L dan rencana tindak lanjutnya.

3. Briefing Harian, yang dipimpin oleh Pengawas di lokasi kerja untuk membahas isu atau aspek penting terkait K3L yang sedang berjalan. Hasil briefing ini kemudian dilaporkan oleh Supervisor kepada Manajer terkait.
4. Penyediaan media informasi, seperti papan informasi, buletin, dan poster yang menampilkan hasil pengelolaan K3L.
5. Komunikasi melalui Departemen SDM, yang meliputi:
a. Informasi terkait program pelatihan K3L;
b. Peraturan dan persyaratan K3L; serta
c. Hasil inspeksi dan audit K3L.

Melalui berbagai inisiatif ini, Perseroan berhasil mencapai zero accident dan zero fatality pada tahun 2024, yang mencerminkan keberhasilan penerapan K3L di seluruh lingkungan kerja Perseroan.

4. Emergency action/response plans, including prevention and mitigation measures designed for each type of emergency incident. This emergency response procedure is communicated to all relevant parties and reviewed periodically by the HSE Unit.
5. Periodic evacuation drills to ensure preparedness for emergency situation.
6. Post-emergency procedures, which include evaluation and recovery of conditions to minimize impacts and prevent similar incident in the future.

In addition to ensuring emergency response preparedness, the Company also prioritizes communication and consultation as essential elements in the management of OHSE. Communication, participation, and consultation are facilitated for both internal employees and external stakeholders such as contractors, suppliers, subcontractors, customers, Government bodies, communities, NGOs, consultants, universities, and certification bodies who have access to consult regarding OHSE issues with the HSE Unit, including relevant external parties. As a form of participation, consultation, and communication on HSE, the Company regularly conducts the following activities.

1. Mid-year Meeting, attended by the Board of Directors, which discusses:
a. The implementation of HSE plan;
b. Ongoing work;
c. HSE Management System (SMHSE);
d. Evaluation of the SMHSE;
e. Management Walk Through follow-up;

2. Monthly HSE Project Management Meeting, which includes:
a. OHSE performance evaluation;
b. Discussion of problems in the field;
c. Analysis of leading indicators;
d. Review of work accidents and environmental pollution;
e. Status of problem follow-up; and
f. OHSE training and its follow-up;

3. Daily Briefing, led by the Supervisor at the workplace to discuss important issues or aspects related to ongoing OHSE issues. The results of this briefing are then reported by the Supervisor to the relevant Manager.
4. Provision of information media, such as information boards, bulletins, and posters displaying the results of OHSE management.
5. Communication through the HR Department, which includes:
a. Information regarding OHSE training programs;
b. OHSE regulations and requirements; and
c. OHSE inspection and audit results.

Through these various initiatives, the Company successfully achieved zero accidents and zero fatalities in 2024, which indicates the successful implementation of OHSE across the Company's work environment.
