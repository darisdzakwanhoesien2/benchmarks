# Tugas dan Tanggung Jawab

Tugas dan tanggung jawab Dewan Komisaris terkait Fungsi Nominasi dan Remunerasi diuraikan sebagai berikut.

## 1. Fungsi Nominasi

a. Menyusun komposisi dan proses nominasi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan/atau anggota Direksi.
b. Menyusun kebijakan dan kriteria yang dibutuhkan dalam proses nominasi calon anggota Dewan Komisaris dan/atau anggota Direksi.
c. Membantu pelaksanaan evaluasi atas kinerja anggota Dewan Komisaris dan/atau anggota Direksi.
d. Menyusun program pengembangan kemampuan anggota Dewan Komisaris dan/atau Direksi.
e. Menelaah dan mengusulkan calon yang memenuhi syarat sebagai anggota Dewan Komisaris dan/atau anggota Direksi untuk disampaikan kepada RUPS.

## 2. Fungsi Remunerasi

a. Menyusun struktur remunerasi bagi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan/atau anggota Direksi.
b. Menyusun kebijakan atas remunerasi bagi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan/atau anggota Direksi.
c. Menyusun besaran atas remunerasi bagi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan/atau anggota Direksi untuk disampaikan kepada RUPS.

# Kebijakan Sukses Direktsi

Hingga tahun 2024, Perseroan belum memiliki Kebijakan Sukses Direktsi secara formal. Namun, perusahaan menyadari pentingnya keberlanjutan kepemimpinan dan sedang mempersiapkan langkah-langkah untuk menyusun kebijakan suksesi yang akan memastikan kelangsungan kepemimpinan di masa mendatang. Proses suksesi saat ini dilakukan melalui RUPS berdasarkan rekomendasi dari Komisaris Utama. Perseroan berkomitmen untuk memperkenalkan kebijakan suksesi Direksi dalam waktu dekat, dengan melibatkan Dewan Komisaris dan pihak terkait untuk memastikan bahwa proses penggantian dan penunjukan Direksi dilakukan dengan memperhatikan kebutuhan perusahaan dan kriteria yang sesuai.

# Rapat

Pada tahun 2024, rapat Dewan Komisaris yang berhubungan dengan Fungsi Nominasi dan Remunerasi diselenggarakan pada 27 Mei 2024.

# Pelaksanaan Tugas

Pada tahun 2024, Dewan Komisaris telah menjalankan Fungsi Nominasi dan Remunerasi dengan efektif, salah satunya dalam penetapan remunerasi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi.

# Duties and Responsibilities

The duties and responsibilities of the Board of Commissioners related to the Nomination and Remuneration Function are described as follows.

## 1. Nomination Function

a. Develop the composition and nomination process of members of the Board of Commissioners and/or Board of Directors.
b. Develop policies and criteria required for the nomination process of candidates for members of the Board of Commissioners and/or Board of Directors.
c. Assist in the evaluation of the performance of members of the Board of Commissioners and/or Board of Directors;
d. Develop a program to enhance the capabilities of members of the Board of Commissioners and/or Board of Directors.
e. Review and propose candidates who meet the qualifications to be members of the Board of Commissioners and/or Board of Directors to be presented at the GMS.

## 2. Remuneration Function

a. Develop the remuneration structure for members of the Board of Commissioners and/or Board of Directors.
b. Develop the remuneration policy for members of the Board of Commissioners and/or Board of Directors.
c. Develop the remuneration amount for members of the Board of Commissioners and/or Board of Directors to be presented at the GMS.

# Board of Directors Succession Policy

Until 2024, the Company has not yet established a formal Board of Directors Succession Policy. However, the Company acknowledges the importance of leadership continuity and is in the process of preparing steps to develop a succession policy that ensures future leadership sustainability. The current succession process is conducted through the GMS based on the recommendations from the President Commissioner. The Company is committed to introducing a Board of Directors Succession Policy in the near future, involving the Board of Commissioners and relevant parties to ensure that the process of replacing and appointing Directors considers the Company's needs and appropriate criteria.

# Meetings

In 2024, the Board of Commissioners held a meeting related to the Nomination and Remuneration Function on May 27, 2024.

# Implementation of Duties

In 2024, the Board of Commissioners effectively carried out the Nomination and Remuneration Function, including the determination of remuneration for the members of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors.
