# Keputusan yang Perlu Mendapat Persetujuan Dewan Komisaris

Keputusan yang memerlukan persetujuan Dewan Komisaris meliputi:

1. Rancangan Rencana Kerja dan Anggaran Perusahaan (RKAP);
2. Rencana RUPS Tahunan 2023 yang diselenggarakan pada tanggal 24 Juni 2024; serta
3. Key Performance Indicator (KPI) Perseroan.

# Penilaian Kinerja [GRI 2-18] [ESG G-04]

Penilaian kinerja Dewan Komisaris dilakukan setiap tahun untuk menilai efektivitas pengawasan dan kontribusi mereka dalam mengarahkan serta mengawasi operasional dan strategi Perseroan. Penilaian ini juga bertujuan untuk memastikan Dewan Komisaris menjalankan tugasnya secara profesional dan sesuai dengan tujuan jangka panjang perusahaan, serta untuk mengidentifikasi area yang perlu diperbaiki dan memberikan rekomendasi untuk meningkatkan kualitas penerapan GCG.

Sejalan dengan hal tersebut, penilaian kinerja Dewan Komisaris Perseroan mengacu pada POJK 21/2015 dan SEOJK 32/2015, yang mengatur tentang pelaksanaan penilaian kinerja organ perusahaan terbuka, termasuk Dewan Komisaris.

# Prosedur dan Kriteria Penilaian

Penilaian kinerja Dewan Komisaris dilaksanakan melalui metode self-assessment, yang mencakup evaluasi terhadap kinerja individu dan kolektif Dewan Komisaris. Proses ini dilakukan dengan mengacu pada kriteria yang telah ditetapkan, termasuk key performance indicators (KPI) yang telah ditetapkan untuk menilai pencapaian kinerja Dewan Komisaris dalam beberapa aspek penting, seperti:

1. Pelaksanaan tugas pengawasan;
2. Kualitas nasihat yang diberikan kepada Direksi;
3. Efektivitas pengawasan;
4. Keberhasilan dalam mencapai tujuan jangka panjang;
5. Tingkat kehadiran rapat; serta
6. Kepatuhan terhadap peraturan yang berlaku.

# Pihak Penilai

Penilaian kinerja Dewan Komisaris dilakukan oleh masing-masing anggota Dewan Komisaris dengan menggunakan metode self-assessment. Proses ini memberikan kesempatan bagi anggota Dewan Komisaris untuk menilai sejauh mana mereka telah memenuhi tugas dan tanggung jawab yang telah ditetapkan.

Selain itu, Pemegang Saham juga berperan dalam proses penilaian ini, khususnya melalui RUPS. Laporan pengawasan yang disampaikan oleh Dewan Komisaris menjadi dasar bagi Pemegang Saham untuk memberikan masukan dan penilaian terhadap kinerja Dewan Komisaris.

# Decisions Requiring Board of Commissioners' Approval

Decisions that require approval from the Board of Commissioners include:

1. The draft of the Company's Work Plan and Budget (RKAP);
2. The plan for the 2023 Annual General Meeting of Shareholders, held on June 24, 2024; and
3. Key Performance Indicators (KPI).

# Performance Evaluation [GRI 2-18] [ESG G-04]

The performance evaluation of the Board of Commissioners is conducted annually to assess the effectiveness of their supervision and contribution in guiding and supervising the Company's operations and strategy. This evaluation also has a purpose to ensure that the Board of Commissioners carries out its duties professionally and aligns with the Company's long-term goals while identifying areas for improvement and providing recommendations to enhance the quality of GCG implementation.

In line with this, the performance evaluation of the Board of Commissioners refers to POJK 21/2015 and SEOJK 32/2015, which regulate the performance assessment of corporate governance organs in public companies, including the Board of Commissioners.

# Evaluation Procedures and Criteria

The performance evaluation of the Board of Commissioners is conducted through a self-assessment method, which includes an evaluation of both individual and collective performance. This process follows predetermined criteria, including key performance indicators (KPI), to measure the Board of Commissioners' performance in key areas such as:

1. Execution of supervisory duties;
2. Quality of advice provided to the Board of Directors;
3. Effectiveness of the supervision made;
4. Success in achieving long-term objectives;
5. Meeting attendance level; and
6. Compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

# Assessor

The performance evaluation of the Board of Commissioners is conducted by its members through self-assessment, allowing them to reflect on how well they have fulfilled their assigned duties and responsibilities.

Additionally, the Shareholders also play a role in the evaluation process, particularly through the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS). The supervision report presented by the Board of Commissioners serves as a basis for the Shareholders to provide feedback and assess the Board of Commissioners's performance.
