Pengembangan teknologi dalam sistem distribusi mencakup:

1. Sistem Pelacakan Silinder
Suatu sistem inovatif yang dapat memantau identitas dan pergerakan tabung gas terkompresi. Dengan sistem ini, Perseroan dapat memanfaatkan kemampuan retensi aset dan meningkatkan kelayakan informasi mengenai pengembalian aset tersebut, sehingga efisiensi operasional dan pengalaman pelanggan dapat meningkat secara signifikan.

2. Telemetri Internet of Things (IoT)
Telemetri IoT telah diintegrasikan dalam sistem distribusi sebagai pemantau data tingkat persediaan secara real-time untuk setiap tangki gas industri yang terpasang. Dengan sistem ini, proses pengambilan keputusan dalam perencanaan dan pelaksanaan distribusi gas industri menjadi lebih cepat dan akurat.

Strategi Penjualan
Produk Perseroan dijual langsung kepada pelanggan dengan fokus utama pada pemenuhan permintaan mereka. Melalui strategi ini, Perseroan berupaya menjadi pemasok terkemuka yang dapat dipercaya oleh setiap pelanggan. Selain itu, Entitas Anak berperan dalam melayani pasar ritel gas industri guna memastikan bahwa kebutuhan pasar, baik dalam bentuk cair maupun gas, dapat terpenuhi secara optimal. Pendekatan ini tidak hanya memperkuat kehadiran Perseroan di pasar, tetapi juga berkontribusi dalam meningkatkan kepuasan pelanggan melalui layanan yang lebih terfokus dan terintegrasi.

Distribution is carried out through filling stations spread across many regions in Indonesia. The Company and its Subsidiaries continuously develop assets and supply chains to integrate with modern technology, allowing the distribution system to operate synergistically across islands to meet customer needs more efficiently.

Technological development in the distribution system include:

1. Cylinder Tracking System
An innovative cylinder tracking system that monitors the identity and movement of compressed gas cylinders. With this system, the Company can take advantage of asset retention capabilities and provides better visibility into asset returns, significantly improving operational efficiency and customer experience.

2. Internet of Things (IoT) Telemetry
IoT telemetry has been integrated into the distribution system to enable real-time inventory level data monitoring for each installed industrial gas tank. This system enhances decision-making in gas distribution planning and execution, making processes faster and more accurate.

Sales Strategy
The Company sells its products directly to customers, focusing primarily on fulfilling their demands. Through this strategy, the Company strives to be a trusted leading supplier for all its customers. Additionally, its Subsidiaries play a key role in serving the industrial gas retail market, ensuring that market demands whether for liquid or gas forms are met optimally. This approach not only strengthens the Company's market presence but also enhances customer satisfaction through more focused and integrated services.

Business Outlook

Pada tahun 2025, perekonomian Indonesia diproyeksikan tetap tumbuh dengan tingkat ketahanan yang kuat, meskipun dihadapkan pada berbagai tantangan global, seperti volatilitas harga komoditas, peningkatan suku bunga, gangguan rantai pasok, serta risiko terhadap ketahanan pangan dan energi akibat perubahan iklim. Pemerintah menargetkan pertumbuhan ekonomi nasional mencapai 8% pada tahun 2028-2029, sementara pertumbuhan pada 2025 diperkirakan berada di kisaran 5,2%[1].

In 2025, Indonesia's economy is projected to continue growing with strong resilience, despite facing global challenges such as commodity price volatility, rising interest rates, supply chain disruptions, and risks to food and energy security due to climate change. The Government has set a national economic growth target of 8% by 2028-2029, while the growth forecast for 2025 is expected to be around 5.2%[1].
