# Tentang Laporan Terintegrasi [IR-1A]
## About the Integrated Report

Laporan Terintegrasi 2024 PT Samator Indo Gas Tbk (yang selanjutnya disebut "Perseroan" atau "kami") disusun sebagai bentuk pertanggungjawaban dan penyampaian informasi terkait strategi, kinerja, peluang, dan keberlanjutan dalam mengelola isu ekonomi serta lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola (LST) selama periode 1 Januari 2024 hingga 31 Desember 2024, dengan mencantumkan perbandingan periode sebelumnya. Laporan ketiga yang diterbitkan Perseroan ini akan terus dikembangkan dalam penyampaian periodik selanjutnya, yaitu setiap akhir tahun buku. [GRI 2-3] [IR-1C]

Laporan ini memberikan informasi bagi seluruh pemangku kepentingan tentang kontribusi Perseroan dalam mendukung sektor industri nasional melalui penyediaan gas industri berkualitas yang berkelanjutan serta memuat cakupan penerapan prinsip keberlanjutan pada aspek ekonomi, lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola di Perseroan dan Entitas Anak. Pada aspek ekonomi, informasi keuangan disajikan berdasarkan Laporan Keuangan Konsolidasian sehingga mencakup informasi Perseroan dan seluruh Entitas Anak. Dalam aspek lingkungan, mencakup sebagian entitas yang relevan, untuk periode 2022 hingga 2024, sebagaimana diungkapkan dalam pembahasan Cakupan dan Batasan. [GRI 2-2] [IR-1C] [IR-3E][IR-3F] [IR-1G]

Dasar penyusunan Laporan Terintegrasi ini sesuai dengan kriteria yang telah diterbitkan di Indonesia ataupun organisasi berstandar internasional yang independen, antara lain:

1. Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 29/POJK.04/2016 tentang Laporan Tahunan Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik dengan muatan konten sesuai Surat Edaran Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 16/SEOJK.04/2021 tentang Bentuk dan Isi Laporan Tahunan Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik;
2. Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 51/POJK.03/2017 tentang Penerapan Keuangan Berkelanjutan bagi Lembaga Jasa Keuangan, Emiten, dan Perusahaan Publik;
3. Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) Standards 2021 opsi in Accordance; serta
4. Integrated Reporting Framework.

Penyusunan laporan berdasarkan standar tersebut telah diverifikasi oleh pihak internal, tanpa proses penjaminan oleh pihak eksternal yang independen. [IR-1B][IR-1D][IR-1F] [IR-4H] [GRI 2-5] [POJK 0.1]

Sepanjang tahun 2024, tidak terdapat perubahan signifikan pada Laporan Terintegrasi Perseroan. Untuk memenuhi prinsip validitas, apabila ada pernyataan ulang atau pernyataan kembali informasi yang dimuat pada laporan sebelumnya karena alasan tertentu, misalnya perbedaan metodologi atau cara perhitungan, kami memberi tanda: "disajikan kembali. [GRI 2-4]

The 2024 Integrated Report of PT Samator Indo Gas Tbk (hereinafter referred to as "the Company" or "we") is prepared as a form of accountability and communication regarding the strategy, performance, opportunities, and sustainability in managing economic issues as well as environmental, social, and governance (ESG) issues during the period from January 1, 2024, to December 31, 2024, including a comparison with the previous period. This third report published by the Company will continue to be developed in subsequent periodic reports, which will be issued at the end of each fiscal year. [GRI 2-3] [IR-1C]

This report provides information for all stakeholders about the Company's contribution in supporting the national industrial sector through the provision of sustainable, high-quality industrial gas. It also covers the application of sustainability principles in the economic, environmental, social, and governance aspects within the Company and its Subsidiaries. In the economic aspect, financial information is presented based on the Consolidated Financial Statements, covering the Company and all Subsidiaries. In the environmental aspect, it covers some relevant entities for the period from 2022 to 2024, as disclosed in the Coverage and Scope discussion. [GRI 2-2] [IR-1C] [IR-3E][IR-3F] [IR-1G]

The preparation of this Integrated Report refers to criteria published in Indonesia or internationally recognized independent organizations, among others:

1. Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 29/POJK.04/2016 on the Annual Report of Issuers or Public Companies, with content aligned with the Financial Services Authority Circular Letter No. 16/SEOJK.04/2021 on the Form and Content of the Annual Report of Issuers or Public Companies;
2. Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 51/POJK.03/2017 on the Implementation of Sustainable Finance for Financial Services Institutions, Issuers, and Public Companies;
3. Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) Standards 2021, "in Accordance" option; and
4. Integrated Reporting Framework.

The preparation of this report based on these standards has been verified by internal parties, without external independent assurance. [IR-1B][IR-1D][IR-1F] [IR-4H] [GRI 2-5] [POJK 0.1]

Throughout 2024, there were no significant changes in the Integrated Report. To ensure validity, if any restatements or revisions of information from previous reports are made due to certain reasons, such as differences in methodology or calculation methods, we will indicate it with: "r" restated. [GRI 2-4]
