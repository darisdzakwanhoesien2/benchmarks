Langkah-langkah konkret yang diterapkan Perseroan meliputi:

1. Menjamin proses produksi gas industri berjalan aman sesuai standard operating procedure (SOP) dan melalui quality control internal yang ketat sebelum produk dikirimkan ke pelanggan;
2. Melaksanakan inspeksi dan kalibrasi berkala terhadap sarana dan prasarana pemasaran, seperti tabung gas, portable gas supply (PGS), road trailer, dan mobil tangki;
3. Memastikan penggunaan tabung gas yang memenuhi standar keamanan, sekaligus menolak pengisian gas pada tabung yang tidak memenuhi persyaratan keselamatan; dan
4. Menerapkan pedoman penanganan silinder medis yang terkontaminasi eksternal sebagai langkah pencegahan tambahan selama pandemi. Pedoman ini disusun berdasarkan standar yang ditetapkan oleh Asia Industrial Gases Association (AIGA), bertujuan mengurangi risiko penularan virus melalui produk yang didistribusikan kepada pelanggan.

Melalui langkah-langkah tersebut, keamanan dan kualitas produk dapat terjaga dengan baik. Sepanjang tahun 2024, Perseroan tidak mengalami penarikan produk, mencerminkan efektivitas sistem pengelolaan mutu dan keamanan yang diterapkan.

## Dampak Produk dan Layanan (POJK F.28)

Perseroan memastikan semua produk yang ditawarkan telah dinilai secara menyeluruh dengan memberikan informasi lengkap kepada pelanggan. Informasi tersebut disampaikan melalui berbagai media, seperti sosialisasi pelanggan, banana sticker, dan rambu-rambu pada setiap produk.

Sepanjang tahun 2024, tidak terdapat insiden ketidakpatuhan terkait informasi maupun pelabelan produk dan layanan yang diberikan.

The concrete measures implemented by the Company include:

1. Ensuring the industrial gas production process operates safely in compliance with Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) and undergoes rigorous Internal Quality Control before products are delivered to customers;
2. Conducting periodic inspections and calibrations of marketing infrastructure and facilities, such as gas cylinders, portable gas supply (PGS), road trailers, and tank cars;
3. Ensuring the use of gas cylinders that meet safety standards while rejecting the filling of gas cylinders that do not comply with safety requirements; and
4. Implementing the guidelines for handling externally contaminated medical cylinders as an additional preventive measure during the pandemic. These guidelines, based on standards set by the Asia Industrial Gases Association (AIGA), aim to reduce the risk of virus transmission through products distributed to customers.

Through these measures, product safety and quality are well maintained. Throughout 2024, the Company did not experience any product recalls, reflecting the effectiveness of its implemented quality and safety management system.

## Impact of Products and Services (POJK F.28)

The Company ensures that all products have been thoroughly assessed by providing complete information to customers. This information is conveyed through various media, such as customer dissemination programs, banana stickers, and signs on each product.

In 2024, the Company did not receive any reports of non-compliance with regards to product and service information and labeling.
