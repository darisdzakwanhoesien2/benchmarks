# Pengembangan Masyarakat

## Dampak Operasi Terhadap Masyarakat Sekitar
[POJK F.23]

Perseroan menyadari bahwa aktivitas bisnis yang dijalankan membawa pengaruh yang signifikan terhadap masyarakat sekitar. Dampak positif yang dihasilkan meliputi pembukaan lapangan kerja, peningkatan ekonomi lokal, dan perluasan akses pendidikan bagi komunitas setempat. Namun, di balik manfaat tersebut, terdapat pula dampak negatif yang tidak dapat sepenuhnya dihindari, seperti munculnya potensi ketimpangan sosial di masyarakat.

Dalam menghadapi tantangan ini, Perseroan berkomitmen menjalankan program pengembangan komunitas yang berkelanjutan. Melalui inisiatif corporate social responsibility (CSR), berbagai program dirancang sesuai kebutuhan spesifik masyarakat. Program ini bertujuan meminimalkan dampak negatif sekaligus memastikan keberlanjutan manfaat yang dihasilkan bagi komunitas.

# Community Development

## Impact of Operations to the Surrounding Communities [POJK F.23]

The Company acknowledges that its business activities have a significant influence on the surrounding community. The positive impacts include employment opportunities, improved local economy, and expanded access to education for local communities. However, alongside these benefits, there are also negative impacts that cannot be completely avoided, such as the emergence of potential social inequality in society.

In facing this challenge, the Company is committed to running a sustainable community development program. Through Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives, various programs are designed according to the community's specific needs. This Program has the purpose to minimize negative impacts while ensuring the sustainability of the benefits generated for the community.
