In addition to meeting creditor requirements, the Company ensures that its capital structure remains at a level that does not pose a risk to its credit rating. The debt-to-equity ratio serves as a key indicator monitored by the Management to assess the health of the capital structure and evaluate the effectiveness of debt utilization. Through close monitoring of this ratio, the Company can maintain financial stability and ensure its ability to meet financial obligations on a sustainable basis.

# Ikatan Material untuk Investasi Barang Modal Material Commitment for Capital Goods Investment

Selama tahun 2024, terdapat ikatan material yang dilakukan untuk tujuan investasi barang modal sebesar Rp28,47 miliar. Perseroan menggunakan sumber dana internal dan eksternal (pinjaman bank) untuk mendukung kegiatan usahanya. Seluruh ikatan material menggunakan mata uang Rupiah sebagai denominasi. Perseroan senantiasa menjalankan kegiatan usaha dan investasi dengan prinsip kehati-hatian, terutama dalam mengelola risiko fluktuasi mata uang asing.

Over the course of 2024, there were material commitments for capital goods investments amounting to Rp28.47 billion. The Company utilizes both internal funds and external financing (bank loans) to support its business activities. All material commitments are denominated in Rupiah. The Company consistently conducts its business and investment activities with prudence, particularly in managing the risks associated with foreign currency fluctuations.

## Realization of Capital Goods Investment in the Last Fiscal Year

Perseroan melakukan investasi dalam barang modal untuk mendukung aktivitas operasional. Sumber pendanaan untuk pembelanjaan modal rutin, yang tidak termasuk transaksi akuisisi, berasal dari kas operasional, dan pembiayaan perbankan. Setiap pembelian barang modal dilakukan dengan pertimbangan yang matang dan disesuaikan dengan kebutuhan operasional. Funding sources for routine capital expenditures, excluding acquisition transactions, come from operational cash flow, and bank financing.
