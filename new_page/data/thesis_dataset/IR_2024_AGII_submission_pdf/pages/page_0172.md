# Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham
## General Meeting of Shareholders

Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS) merupakan organ Perseroan tertinggi yang mempunyai hak dan kewenangan yang tidak dimiliki oleh Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dalam batasan yang ditentukan dalam ketentuan peraturan perundangan dan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan. Selain itu, RUPS merupakan forum penting bagi Pemegang Saham untuk berpartisipasi dalam pengambilan keputusan strategis perusahaan. RUPS berfungsi sebagai wadah menerima dan menyetujui Laporan Pengawasan Dewan Komisaris, Laporan Pengurusan dan Pengelolaan Direksi, memilih anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, serta membahas isu-isu penting lainnya yang memengaruhi masa depan perusahaan. Dengan demikian, melalui forum ini, Pemegang Saham memiliki hak untuk memberikan suara dan menyampaikan pendapat, yang memastikan bahwa keputusan yang diambil mencerminkan aspirasi mereka.

Pelaksanaan RUPS terbagi dalam 2 jenis, yaitu RUPS Tahunan dan RUPS Luar Biasa. RUPS Tahunan wajib dilaksanakan minimal 6 bulan setelah tahun buku berakhir. Sementara itu, RUPS Luar Biasa dapat dilaksanakan sewaktu-waktu, sesuai dengan kepentingan Pemegang Saham, Dewan Komisaris, dan Direksi Perseroan.

# Kewenangan RUPS

Dalam RUPS, Pemegang Saham berhak memperoleh perlakuan yang sama dalam menyuarakan pendapat dan berkontribusi dalam proses pengambilan keputusan penting dan strategis Perseroan. RUPS memiliki wewenang yang tidak dapat dilimpahkan kepada Dewan Komisaris maupun Direksi. Wewenang RUPS meliputi:

1. Meminta pertanggungjawaban Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi terkait pengelolaan Perseroan;
2. Mengubah Anggaran Dasar Perseroan;
3. Mengangkat dan memberhentikan Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi;
4. Memutuskan pembagian tugas dan wewenang pengurusan Perseroan; serta
5. Memutuskan berbagai hal lain yang dinilai perlu.

Selain itu, Direksi Perseroan berkewajiban untuk menyampaikan hal-hal berikut dalam rangka pelaksanaan RUPS:

1. Laporan Tahunan;
2. Usulan terkait penggunaan laba bersih Perseroan;
3. Usulan terkait penetapan akuntan publik untuk tahun buku yang sedang berjalan berdasarkan arahan dari Dewan Komisaris; serta
4. Hal-hal lain yang memerlukan persetujuan RUPS.

The General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) is the Company's highest governing organ that have the rights and authorities that are not granted to the Board of Commissioners or the Board of Directors, within the limits set by applicable laws and regulations, and the Articles of Association. Additionally, the GMS serves as a crucial forum for the Shareholders to participate in the Company's strategic decision-making process. The GMS serves as a forum to receive and approve the Supervisory Report of the Board of Commissioners, the Management and Administration Report of the Board of Directors, appoint members of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors, and discuss other important issues that affect the Company future. Therefore, through this forum, the Shareholders have the right to vote and express their opinions, which ensures that the decisions taken reflect their aspirations.

GMS implementation is divided into 2 types, namely the Annual GMS and the Extraordinary GMS. The Annual GMS must be held at least 6 months after the end of the financial year. Meanwhile, the Extraordinary GMS can be held at any time, in accordance with the Shareholders, the Board of Commissioners, and the Board of Directors interests.

# GMS Authorities

At the GMS, the Shareholders have the right to equal treatment in expressing their opinions and contributing to the decision-making process on key strategic matters. The GMS has authorities that cannot be delegated to the Board of Commissioners or the Board of Directors. The authorities of the GMS include:

1. Ask the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors for accountability regarding the management of the Company;
2. Amend the Articles of Association;
3. Appoint and dismiss the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors;
4. Decide on the division of duties and authority for the management of the Company; and
5. Decide on various other matters that are deemed necessary.

In addition, the Board of Directors has an obligation to submit the following matters in the context GMS implementation:

1. Annual Report;
2. Proposals related to the use of the Company's net profit;
3. Proposals related to the appointment of a public accountant for the current financial year based on the Board of Commissioners' directives; and
4. Other matters that require approval of the GMS.
