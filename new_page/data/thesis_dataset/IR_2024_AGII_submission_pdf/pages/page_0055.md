## 3. Culture of Collaboration

Perseroan juga mengedepankan budaya kolaborasi yang kuat, di mana setiap tim kerja didorong untuk bekerja sama lintas fungsi melalui indikator utama organisasi yang terpusat (objectives and key results/OKR). Pada tahun 2024, Perseroan memulai inisiatif KPI Reinvented, Kurikulum Training Academy yang baru, dan proyek kolaboratif ESG All In, sebagai langkah utama untuk menerapkan pola pikir tujuan bersama dalam aktivitas sehari-hari di atas pencapaian individu.

Melalui kombinasi strategi ini, Perseroan tidak hanya memperkuat ketahanan usaha, tetapi juga memastikan keberlanjutan pertumbuhan di tengah tantangan industri yang dinamis. Serve, Set, and Match memungkinkan meningkatkan pangsa pasar serta mengurangi ketergantungan pada segmen tertentu melalui diversifikasi pelanggan. Sementara itu, Process Improvement dan Culture of Collaboration memperkuat daya saing serta menciptakan nilai tambah bagi seluruh pemangku kepentingan. Dengan pendekatan yang adaptif dan inovatif, Perseroan menegaskan posisinya sebagai pemain utama dalam industri gas nasional yang siap menghadapi masa depan berkelanjutan.

## 3. Culture of Collaboration

The Company also prioritises a strong culture of collaboration, where each work team is encouraged to work together across functions through centralised organisational key indicators (objectives and key results/OKR). In 2024, the Company started the KPI Reinvented initiative, the new Training Academy Curriculum, and the ESG All In collaborative project, as the main steps to implement a common goal mindset in daily activities over individual achievements.

Through this combination of strategies, the Company has not only strengthened its business resilience, but also ensured sustainable growth amidst dynamic industry challenges. Serve, Set, and Match allows for increasing market share and reducing dependence on certain segments through customer diversification. Meanwhile, the Process Improvement and Culture of Collaboration focuses on strengthening competitiveness and generate added value for all stakeholders. With an adaptive and innovative approach, the Company confirms its position as a major player in the national gas industry, ready to face a sustainable future.
