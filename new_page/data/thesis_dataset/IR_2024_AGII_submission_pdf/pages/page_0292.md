# Kepuasan Pelanggan [POJK F.30]

Perseroan secara rutin mengukur tingkat kepuasan pelanggan terhadap produk dan jasa yang ditawarkan melalui survei kepuasan pelanggan yang diselenggarakan setiap 2 tahun sekali. Survei ini mencakup berbagai aspek layanan, termasuk komunikasi, pengiriman, administrasi penagihan, layanan purna jual, kualitas sarana pemasaran, serta kelengkapan keamanan kerja.

Pada tahun 2024, Perseroan kembali menyelenggarakan survei kepuasan pelanggan dengan hasil sebagai berikut:

1. Customer Satisfaction Index sebesar 89,27%, mencerminkan persentase responden yang menyatakan puas atau sangat puas terhadap layanan Samator Group.
2. Perseroan berhasil meraih skor 4,35 dari total skor maksimal 5 yang berarti telah memenuhi 87,00% dari standar penerimaan untuk kepuasan pelanggan.

# Customer Satisfaction [POJK F.30]

The Company regularly measures the satisfaction levels of their customers with the products and services offered through customer satisfaction surveys biennially. This survey includes various aspects of service, including communication, delivery, billing administration, after-sales service, quality marketing, and job security.

In 2024, the Company conducted another customer satisfaction survey with the following results:

1. Customer Satisfaction Index of 89.27%, reflecting the percentage of respondents stating that they were satisfied or very satisfied with the Company's services.
2. The Company achieved a score of 4.35 out of a maximum total score of 5 which means it has met 87.00% of the acceptance standards for customer satisfaction.
