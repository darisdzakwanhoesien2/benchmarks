# 2. Pengurangan Emisi Cakupan 2

a. Peningkatan Efisiensi Energi
Audit menyeluruh terhadap penggunaan energi di seluruh fasilitas operasional, termasuk pabrik dan stasiun pengisian, akan dimulai pada Kuartal I-2025. Perseroan juga akan mengganti pencahayaan dengan lampu LED dan mengadopsi variable frequency drives (VFD) pada pompa dan motor untuk meningkatkan efisiensi.

b. Pemanfaatan Energi Terbarukan
Pemasangan panel surya (solar PV) akan diperluas di lokasi-lokasi yang memungkinkan untuk memaksimalkan penggunaan energi terbarukan secara langsung.

c. Sertifikasi Energi Terbarukan
Perseroan akan mengakuisisi Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs) sebagai bagian dari upaya mendukung pengadaan energi dari sumber yang lebih berkelanjutan.

Melalui implementasi inisiatif-inisiatif tersebut, Perseroan menargetkan pengurangan emisi gas rumah kaca Cakupan 1 dan 2 sebesar 23,60% hingga tahun 2033, sejalan dengan komitmen jangka panjang terhadap keberlanjutan lingkungan.

# Pemakaian Air [POJK F.8]

Perseroan menggunakan air bersih yang disuplai dari pihak ketiga, yaitu Perusahaan Daerah Air Minum (PDAM), untuk mendukung kebutuhan operasional sehari-hari kantor dan sistem pendingin di pabrik gas. Untuk mengelola penggunaan air secara bijaksana dan mencegah kelangkaan air bersih, Perseroan menerapkan beberapa langkah strategis, seperti:

1. Mendaur ulang air untuk digunakan kembali dalam sistem pendinginan pabrik gas;
2. Mengadakan kampanye hemat air melalui pemasangan brosur di area penggunaan air seperti kamar mandi;
3. Menggunakan kran otomatis untuk meminimalkan pemborosan air;

b. Low-Emission Fuel Consumption
Diesel fuel in generators will be replaced with B40 biodiesel as a more environmentally friendly alternative.

c. Efficiency and Leakage Audits
Production machinery will be audited starting in Q1 2025 to identify energy inefficiencies and potential leaks.

d. Refrigerant Management
Cooling infrastructure will be audited, and a feasibility study will be conducted to replace high-global-warming-potential (high-GWP) refrigerants with lower-impact alternatives.

# 2. Scope 2 Emission Reductions

a. Energy Efficiency Improvements
A comprehensive audit on energy consumption will be conducted across operational facilities, including plants and refilling stations, beginning in Q1 2025. The Company will also replace lighting with LED bulbs and adopt variable frequency drives (VFDs) for pumps and motors to improve efficiency.

b. Renewable Energy Consumption
Solar panel (solar PV) installations will be expanded in suitable locations to maximize direct use of renewable energy.

c. Renewable Energy Certification
The Company will acquire Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs) as part of its efforts to support the procurement of energy from more sustainable sources.

Through the implementation of these initiatives, the Company targets a 23.60% reduction in Scope 1 and 2 Greenhouse Gas Emissions by 2033, in line with its long-term commitment to environmental sustainability.

# Water Use [POJK F.8]

The Company uses clean water supplied by a third party, namely the Local Water Supply Utility (PDAM), to support office daily operational needs and the cooling system at gas plants. To manage water use wisely and prevent clean water shortages, the Company has taken several strategic measures, such as:

1. Recycling water for reuse in gas plant cooling system;
2. Holding a water saving campaign by posting brochures in water use areas such as in bathrooms;
3. Using automatic faucets to minimize water waste;
