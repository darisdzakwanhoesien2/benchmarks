Citizen of Indonesian, born in Purwadadi on October 12, 1968, aged 56 years, and residing in Sleman, Yogyakarta, Indonesia. Holds a Bachelor's degree (S1) in Civil Engineering from University of Ahmad Yani (2019) and has a CRGP professional certification, BNSP (2024). Appointed based on the Board of Directors Decree No. 67/SK/WK/PEN/2024 dated June 28, 2024.

POE is responsible for controlling planning, implementation, and evaluation activities in the production sector, (costs, K3LMP, timelines, management of equipment and company-owned project assets and key materials), as well as he formulates, develops, manages, and evaluates technology, research, knowledge, and innovation to align with the Company's business development. Provides support in the Value Engineering process at the tender, implementation, and commissioning stages. Furthermore, evaluates and monitors the Production function in the Operations Division to ensure Waskita's projects run according to planned costs, quality, and timelines.
