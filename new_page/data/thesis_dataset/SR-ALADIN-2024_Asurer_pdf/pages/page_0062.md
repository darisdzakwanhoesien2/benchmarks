In 2024, the Bank continues to expand its network of partnership collaborations and optimize its intermediary function by increasing third-party fund collection and financing distribution activities. To encourage fund collection, the Bank launched the QRIS CPM feature in 2024 to facilitate payment methods through the mobile banking application, as well as a Corporate Internet Banking platform to simplify transactions for corporate customers. The Bank has also continued to expand its cash deposit and withdrawal services, which are now available at more than 23,000 Alfa Group outlets, including Alfamart, Alfamidi, Lawson, and DAN+DAN across Indonesia.
