Risiko Transisi | Transition Risk

|  Risiko Risk | Dampak Terhadap Bisnis Impact on Business | Dampak Finansial Financial Impact | Langkah Mitigasi Mitigation Steps  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  Regulasi Emisi Emission Regulations | Peningkatan biaya kepatuhan terhadap kebijakan pajak karbon dan sistem perdagangan emisi. Higher costs associated with compliance to carbon tax policies and emissions trading systems. | Biaya tambahan untuk memenuhi regulasi dan potensi denda jika tidak patuh. Additional expenses for adhering to regulations and possible penalties for non-compliance. | Mengadopsi teknologi rendah karbon dan berpartisipasi dalam program perdagangan emisi. Adopt low-carbon technologies and participate in emissions trading programs.  |
|  Perubahan Teknologi Technological Change | Investasi besar dalam teknologi rendah karbon untuk memenuhi standar lingkungan. Major investments in low-carbon technologies to comply with environmental standards. | Peningkatan belanja modal (CAPEX) untuk modernisasi peralatan dan sistem produksi. Increased capital expenditure (CAPEX) aimed at modernizing production equipment and systems. | Mencari pendanaan hijau atau insentif pemerintah dan meningkatkan efisiensi energi. Seek green funding or government incentives and improve energy efficiency.  |
|  Penurunan Permintaan Decreasing Demand | Pergeseran global ke energi terbarukan mengurangi permintaan batubara. The global transition to renewable energy decreases the demand for coal. | Penurunan pendapatan dan risiko aset tambang menjadi tidak bernilai (stranded assets). Reduction in income and the risk of mining assets becoming worthless (stranded assets). | Diversifikasi ke sektor energi terbarukan dan mengembangkan solusi logistik rendah karbon. Diversify into the renewable energy sector and develop low-carbon logistics solutions.  |

|  Risiko Risk | Dampak Terhadap Bisnis Impact on Business | Dampak Financial Financial Impact | Langkah Mitigasi Mitigation Steps  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  Pengawasan Publik Public Oversight | Tekanan dari masyarakat dan investor untuk menerapkan praktik bisnis berkelanjutan. High legal costs, large fines, and potential closure of operations. | Memastikan kepatuhan lingkungan dan melakukan audit keberlanjutan secara berkala. Ensure environmental compliance and conduct regular sustainability audits.  |
