In collecting the data, ABM coordinated with the Human Capital Department in each subsidiary, including PT Tunas Inti Abadi, PT Cipta Kridatama, PT Prima Wiguna Parama, and PT Reswara Minergi Hartama to collect MVC data. PT Sanggar Sarana Baja provided EES data, and PT Cipta Krida Bahari provided ILS data.  |
