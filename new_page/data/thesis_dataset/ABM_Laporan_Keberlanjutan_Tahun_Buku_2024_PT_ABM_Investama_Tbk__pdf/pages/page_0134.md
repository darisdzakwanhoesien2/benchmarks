a. Data tingkat kecelakaan kerja pada tabel di atas mencakup seluruh area operasional dan anak perusahaan ABM.

# Note:

a. The work incident rate data in the table above covers all ABM's operational areas and subsidiaries.
b. The increase in total working hours of employees and contractors is due to the increase in production and the number of employees.
c. The injury rate is calculated using a denominator of 1,000,000 according to the International Labour Organization (ILO) and Ministry of Manpower.
d. Restatement of figures in 2022 adjusts the parameters standardized by the Ministry of Manpower.
