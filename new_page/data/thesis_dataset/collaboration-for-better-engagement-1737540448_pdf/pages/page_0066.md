# Peningkatan Kompetensi Terkait Keberlanjutan [E.2] [2-17]

Guna mendukung penerapan praktik keberlanjutan, Perseroan mendorong seluruh insan Wismilak untuk meningkatkan pengetahuan dan kompetensi terkait keberlanjutan. Tak hanya karyawan, namun anggota manajemen puncak juga diikutsertakan dalam berbagai pelatihan dengan tema yang berbeda namun berkesinambungan terkait isu-isu keberlanjutan.

# Sustainability-Related Competency Development

To support the implementation of sustainability practices, the Company encourages all Wismilak employees to improve their knowledge and competencies related to sustainability. Not only employees, but members of top management are also included in various trainings with different but continuous themes related to sustainability issues.
