Tata Kelola Keberlanjutan Sustainability Governance

# Penerapan Sistem Pelaporan Pelanggaran (WBS)

Wismilak secara terbuka menerima pengaduan atau laporan dari pemangku kepentingan atas hal-hal yang diindikasi melanggar peraturan, kode etik, maupun pelanggaran hukum dan kegiatan tidak beretika lainnya. Perseroan menjalankan sistem pelaporan pelanggaran (whistleblowing system/WBS) yang dikelola oleh Tim Wismilak Whistleblowing System (Tim WWBS). Setiap laporan yang diterima akan ditindaklanjuti sesuai hasil temuan penyelidik dengan jaminan perlindungan terhadap pelapor. Pada tahun 2022, tidak ada pelaporan yang diterima Perseroan.

# Whistleblowing System (WBS)

Wismilak enables public submission of complaint or report from the stakeholders on any indication of violations against the regulations, code of conduct, and law as well as other unethical behaviors. The Company implements a whistleblowing system managed by the Wismilak Whistleblowing System (WWBS) Team. Each report will be followed up in accordance with the findings of the investigator team and the identity of the whistleblower will be protected. Throughout 2022, the Company did not receive any whistleblowing report.
