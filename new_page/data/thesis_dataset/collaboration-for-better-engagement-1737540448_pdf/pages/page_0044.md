# Penilaian Kinerja dan Pengembangan Karir

Penilaian kinerja kepada karyawan tetap dilakukan melalui aplikasi Performance Appraisal Online yang dilakukan oleh atasan secara daring dengan memadukan hasil penilaian berbasis kuantitatif dan kualitatif berdasarkan pencapaian kinerja timnya. Setiap atasan bertanggung jawab untuk menetapkan dan mengomunikasikan hasil penilaian kinerja timnya dan perbaikan yang diharapkan untuk periode kinerja selanjutnya melalui proses evaluasi kinerja. Kebijakan dan Prosedur Performance Appraisal (Penilaian Kinerja) tertuang dalam MOD-OD 004. Atasan juga wajib menyampaikan obyektif Departemen kepada tim yang selaras dengan obyektif Perseroan. Evaluasi kinerja dilakukan melalui aplikasi Perform dengan mekanisme personal feedback dan merupakan masukan bagi proses Tinjauan Karyawan atas pencapaian obyektif (Key Performance Indicator-KPI), Leadership Model, pengembangan karier, dan Perencanaan pengembangan yang akan datang. Hasil penilaian kinerja digunakan sebagai dasar untuk melakukan pemetaan kompetensi, kebutuhan pengembangan, dan promosi terutama pada level Staf, Supervisor dan Manager.

Berdasarkan hasil penilaian kinerja tahun 2022, sebanyak 363 karyawan mendapatkan promosi, non-staf ke staf sebanyak 38 karyawan, dan 9 karyawan dari level supervisor ke level manajerial. Tindak lanjut penilaian kinerja untuk melakukan pemetaan kompetensi diakomodir melalui aplikasi Talent Management System.

# Performance Appraisal and Career Development

Performance appraisals for permanent employees are conducted through the Performance Appraisal Online application conducted by supervisors online by combining quantitative and qualitative-based assessment results based on their team's performance achievements. Each supervisor is responsible for determining and communicating the results of his/her team's performance appraisal and expected improvements for the next performance period through the Performance Appraisal process. The Performance Appraisal Policy and Procedure is contained in MOD-OD 004. Supervisors are also required to convey the Department's objectives to the team that are aligned with the Company's objectives. Performance evaluation is conducted through the Perform application with a personal feedback mechanism and is an input for the Employee Review process on the achievement of objectives (Key Performance Indicator-KPI), Leadership Model, career development, and future development planning. Performance appraisal results are used as the basis for mapping competencies, development needs, and promotions, especially at the Staff, Supervisor and Manager levels.

Based on the results of the performance appraisal in 2022, 363 employees were promoted, 38 employees from non-staff to staff, and 9 employees from supervisor level to managerial level. Follow-up performance appraisal to conduct competency mapping is accommodated through the Talent Management System application.
