Kinerja Sosial Social Performance

Perseroan menjaga hubungan baik dengan masyarakat sekitar melalui kegiatan tanggung jawab sosial atau corporate social responsibility (CSR). Disamping itu, Perseroan juga melakukan pengelolaan dampak lingkunganseperti pengendalian emisi, penghijauan, pencemaran suara (kebisingan) dan limbah buang. Kami memastikan bahwa pengelolaan dampak negatif ini dilakukan sesuai dengan peraturan yang berlaku dan tidak menimbulkan bahaya bagi lingkungan sekitar.

Melalui program Wismilak Berbagi, Perseroan juga memberikan literasi kewirausahaan dan pelatihan untuk masyarakat. Program-program tersebut diharapkan dapat mewujudkan salah satu nilai Perseroan, yaitu "Bersama Meraih Sukses". Untuk mengetahui kemanfaatan kegiatan CSR, Perseroan juga melakukan sosialisasi program kepada berbagai pemangku kepentingan, diantaranya kepada Pemerintah, akademisi, asosiasi maupun komunitas, untuk mendapatkan masukan dan perbaikan dalam program Perseroan selanjutnya. Disamping itu, beberapa kolaborasi program juga dilakukan Wismilak dalam tahun pelaporan,seperti berkolaborasi bersama Gabungan Organisasi Wanita Surabaya (GOW) dalam pelaksanaan program literasi pendidikan, berkolaborasi dengan Asosiasi Pengusaha Indonesia (APINDO) bidang UMKM IKM untuk kegiatan literasi kewirausahaan, dan juga berkolaborasi dengan organisasi internasional Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) dalam program Diplomat Success Challenge (DSC).

The Company maintains good relationships with the surrounding community through corporate social responsibility (CSR) programs. In additions, The Company also manages environmental impacts such as emission control, greening, sound pollution (noise) and waste disposal. We ensure that the negative impact management is carried out in accordance with the applicable regulations and does not pose a threat to the surrounding environment.

Through Wismilak Sharing programs, the Company provides entrepreneurial literacy and training for public. These programs are expected to realize one of the Company's values, "Achieving Success Together". In exploring the benefits of CSR activities, the Company conducts program outreach to various stakeholders, including the Government, academics, associations and the community. This enables us to obtain inputs and make improvements in the Company's future program. In addition, Wismilak carried out several collaborative programs in the reporting period, such as collaborating with the Surabaya Women's Organization Association (GOW) in implementing education and literacy programs, collaborating with the Indonesian Employers' Association in the small business sector for entrepreneurial literacy activities, and also collaborated with the international organization Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) in the Diplomat Success Challenge (DSC) program.
