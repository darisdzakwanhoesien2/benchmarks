Informasi yang tertera dalam label kemasan produk mencantumkan sumber komponen seperti kandungan kadar nikotin dan tar sesuai dengan angka aman yang terakreditasi berdasarkan ketentuan peraturan. Sepanjang tahun pelaporan, Perseroan tidak mendapatkan sanksi maupun peringatan akibat ketidakpatuhan terkait informasi dan pelabelan produk, serta komunikasi pemasaran. Namun Perseroan menerima 33 suara konsumen, di antaranya terdapat 27 masukan, saran, dan apresiasi, serta enam aduan konsumen. Seluruh pengaduan telah direspon, ditindaklanjuti, dan diselesaikan oleh unit Suara Konsumen Wismilak, yang dibantu oleh departemen Quality Assurance dan Public Relations. Perseroan secara terbuka dan transparan menerima setiap keluhan dan masukan melalui kanal Suara Konsumen untuk meningkatkan kinerja. Semua saran akan ditindaklanjuti dalam waktu maksimal tujuh hari kerja sesuai dengan standar layanan. [417-2] [417-3]

The information presented on the product label includes the source of components such as nicotine and tar content according to the accredited safe amount based on regulatory provisions. Throughout the reporting year, the Company did not receive sanctions or warnings due to non-compliance regarding product information and labeling, as well as marketing communications. However, the Company received 33 consumer votes, including 27 inputs, suggestions and appreciation, as well as six consumer complaints. All complaints have been responded, followed up, and resolved by the Wismilak Consumer Voice unit, assisted by the Quality Assurance and Public Relations departments. The Company openly and transparently accepts every complaint and input through the Customer Voice channel to improve performance. All suggestions will be followed up within a maximum of seven working days in accordance with service standards.
