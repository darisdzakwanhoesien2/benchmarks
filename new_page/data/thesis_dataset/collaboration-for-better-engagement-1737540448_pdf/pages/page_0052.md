|  Event: | Wismilak's support for the Nano Coating application for Airlangga University Hospital in Surabaya  |

## Uraian Kegiatan:

Wismilak memberikan dukungan terhadap inovasi anak bangsa salah satunya dalam aplikasi nano coating tembaga untuk Rumah Sakit Universitas Airlangga Surabaya. Nano coating tembaga ini dikembangkan oleh Tim Riset Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember Surabaya (ITS) dan telah diuji di Laboratorium Institute Tropical Desease Universitas Airlangga, bersama Profesor Kazufumi Shimizu dari Universitas Kobe, Jepang dengan hasil terbukti dapat membunuh 90% virus, termasuk COVID-19 dalam 10 menit. Serah terima dilakukan pada tanggal 4 Maret 2022 secara hybrid di RS Universitas Airlangga Surabaya, dihadiri oleh Direktur Rumah Sakit Universitas Airlangga, Prof. Dr. Nasronudin, Sp.PD., KPTI-FINASIM, Wakil Rektor IV ITS, BAMBANG PRAMUJATI, S.T., M.Eng PH.D, Direktur Direktorat Inovasi dan Kawasan Sains Teknologi (DIKST) Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember, Agus Muhamad Hatta ST, MSc Ph.D, Direktur Direktorat Riset dan Pengabdian Kepada Masyarakat (DRPM) Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember, Fadlilatul Taufany, S.T., Ph.D dan Dr. Agung Purniawan S.T., M.Eng selaku Kepala Riset dan Pembina CoFilm.

## Description:

Wismilak provides support for the innovation of the nation's children, one of which is in the application of copper nano coating for Airlangga University Hospital in Surabaya. This copper nano coating was developed by the Surabaya Sepuluh Nopember Institute of Technology (ITS) Research Team and has been tested at the Airlangga University Tropical Design Institute Laboratory, together with Professor Kazufumi Shimizu from Kobe University, Japan with proven results that can kill 90% viruses, include COVID-19 virus in 10 minutes. The handover was carried out on March 4 2022 in a hybrid manner at Airlangga University Hospital in Surabaya, performance by the Director of Airlangga University Hospital, Prof. Dr. Nasronudin, Sp.PD., KPTI-FINASIM, Deputy Chancellor IV ITS, BAMBANG PRAMUJATI, S.T., M.Eng PH.D, Director of the Directorate of Innovation and Science Technology Area (DIKST) Sepuluh Nopember Institute of Technology, Agus Muhamad Hatta ST, MSc Ph.D, Director of the Directorate of Research and Community Service (DRPM) of the Sepuluh Nopember Institute of Technology, Fadlilatul Taufany, S.T., Ph.D and Dr. Agung Purniawan S.T., M.Eng as Head of Research and Advisor of CoFilm.

## Uraian Kegiatan:

Merupakan kegiatan sosial tahunan Perseroan untuk membantu dhuafa dan panti asuhan. Kegiatan ini bertema “Berbagi Kebahagiaan Bersama Dalam Rahmat Allah SWT”, menyalurkan kebutuhan pokok dan juga alat pelindung kesehatan berupa masker.

## Description:

It is the Company's annual social program to help the impoverished community members and orphanages. This activity has the theme “Sharing Happiness in the Grace of Allah SWT”, distributing basic necessities and health protective equipment (masks).
