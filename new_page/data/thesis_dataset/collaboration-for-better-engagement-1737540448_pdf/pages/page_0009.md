# Profil Laporan
## Report Profile

Laporan Keberlanjutan diterbitkan setiap tahun selaras dengan periode pelaporan finansial. Laporan ini menyampaikan kinerja atas pengelolaan dampak lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola (LST). Dalam laporan, terdapat beberapa pencatatan data yang melibatkan PT Gelora Djaja dan PT Gawih Jaya selaku entitas anak, yaitu data karyawan, pelatihan, dan pemeriksaan kesehatan berkala. Sementara, semua data keuangan yang disajikan berasal dari semua entitas anak sebagai data konsolidasian. [2-2][2-3]

Laporan disusun untuk periode 1 Januari hingga 31 Desember 2022 berdasarkan Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (POJK) No. 51/POJK.03/2017 tentang Penerapan Keuangan Berkelanjutan bagi Lembaga Jasa Keuangan, Emiten, dan Perusahaan Publik, mengacu pada Surat Edaran Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (SEOJK) No. 16/SEOJK.04/2021 dan standar Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) dengan kesesuaian with reference to the GRI Standards. Terdapat penyajian kembali informasi terkait peserta pelatihan berdasarkan area kerja yang menyampaikan perhitungan ulang konsolidasi data direksi induk dan anak perusahaan dan data pelatihan pegawai berdasarkan jenjang yang menyampaikan perhitungan data jenjang junior yang dikonsolidasi antara jenjang entry dan basic pada Laporan Keberlanjutan 2021. [2-4]

Perseroan belum melakukan penjaminan eksternal (external assurance) dari pihak independen. Namun, seluruh informasi dalam laporan didapat melalui prosedur yang berlaku, serta telah melalui proses validasi internal. Adapun penyajian informasi disampaikan berdasarkan topik material yang tidak berubah dari tahun sebelumnya, karena tidak ada perubahan model usaha, ataupun perubahan lainnya yang bersifat signifikan. Kami menggunakan pendekatan risiko dan peluang dalam menentukan topik material dan sesuai dengan strategi perusahaan. [C.6] [G.1][2-5]

Pertanyaan dan informasi lebih lanjut atas Laporan Keberlanjutan 2022 dapat disampaikan melalui: [2-3]

Sustainability Report is published annually, in accordance with the period of financial report. This report presents the performance of environmental, social, and governance (ESG) impact management. This Report contains data collection that involved PT Gelora Jaya and PT Gawih Jaya as subsidiaries, encompassing employee, training, and regular medical check-up data. Financial data presented in this report is collected from all subsidiaries as consolidated data.

This report was prepared for the period of January 1 to December 31, 2022, pursuant to Regulation of the Financial Service Authority (POJK) No. 51/POJK.03/2017 concerning Implementation of Sustainable Finance for Financial Institutions, Issuer Companies, and Public Companies, referring to the Circular Letter of the Financial Service Authority (SEOJK) No. 16/SEOJK.04/2021 and with reference to Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) Standards. There is a restatement of information related to training participants based on work area which submits a recalculation of consolidated data on directors of the parent and subsidiary companies and employee training data based on level which conveys junior level data calculations which are consolidated between entry and basic levels in the 2021 Sustainability Report.

The Company has yet to carry out external assurance by an independent party. However, all information delivered in this report was collected in accordance with the applicable procedures and has undergone internal validation process. The information is presented based on the material topics as in the previous report since there were no changes to the business model. We employ risk and opportunity approach in determining our material topics in accordance with the corporate strategy.
