# Occupational Health and Safety

Sistem Manajemen Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja (SMK3) merupakan acuan dalam setiap kegiatan operasional. Kami memastikan seluruh peralatan dan standar operasional prosedur (SOP) pekerjaan telah dievaluasi dan selalu dilaksanakan di bawah supervisi Safety sebagai Sub-Department.

Kesadaran pekerja akan pentingnya K3 dapat memitigasi timbulnya bahaya dan risiko yang terjadi saat bekerja. Perseroan melakukan identifikasi pekerjaan berisiko tinggi menggunakan metode Failure Mode and Effect Analysis (FMEA). Untuk meningkatkan kesadaran pekerja, Perseroan memberikan sosialisasi prosedur, pencegahan, dan mitigasi dampak K3 melalui pelatihan secara berkala. Perseroan telah mengalokasikan dana sebesar Rp21,86 miliar untuk mendukung kegiatan pelatihan K3, safety riding, emergency response, inspeksi alat pemadam api, dan inspeksi lingkungan kerja. Sebanyak 889 pegawai telah mengikuti pelatihan dan simulasi K3 sepanjang tahun 2022.

The Occupational Health and Safety Management System is a reference in each operational activity. We ensure that all equipment and standard operating procedures (SOP) have been evaluated and are always implemented under the supervision of Safety Sub-Department.

Employee awareness of the importance of safety management can mitigate occupational hazards and risks. To identify high-risk jobs, the Company employs Failure Mode and Effect Analysis (FMEA) method. To increase employee awareness, the Company disseminates procedures, prevention measures, and mitigation efforts of OHS impacts through regular training. The Company has allocated Rp21.86 billion to support safety management training activities, safety riding, emergency response, fire extinguisher inspection, and work environment inspection. A total of 889 employees have attended safety management training and simulation throughout 2022.
