Kinerja Sosial Social Performance

Talent Management adalah suatu proses manajemen SDM yaitu :

a. Mengembangkan dan memperkuat karyawan baru pada proses pertama kali masuk perusahaan.
b. Memelihara dan mengembangkan pegawai yang sudah ada di perusahaan.

Talent Management System menggabungkan Performance/ Kinerja dan Potensial. Kinerja diperoleh melalui hasil penilai kinerja sedangkan Potensial/potensi diperoleh melalui asesmen multi dimensional aspek (result agility, self awareness, change agility, mental agility, people agility). Proses tersebut akan menghasilkan identifikasi talent yang dimiliki masing-masing divisi/departemen di lingkungan Perseroan (9-Box), memberikan masukan kepada semua pemangku kepentingan, serta bentuk pengembangan yang tepat untuk masing-masing talent (Individual Development Plan – IDP).

Talent Management is an HR management process that is :

a. Developing and strengthening new employees in the process of first entering the company.
b. Maintaining and developing existing employees in the company.

Talent Management System combines Performance / Performance and Potential. Performance is obtained through the results of performance appraisals while Potential is obtained through a multi-dimensional assessment of aspects (result agility, self awareness, change agility, mental agility, people agility). The process will result in the identification of talents owned by each division/department within the Company (9-Box), providing input to all stakeholders, as well as the right form of development for each talent (Individual Development Plan - IDP).

# Aplikasi Human Resources Information System (HRIS) Human Resources Information System (HRIS) Application

![img-77.jpeg](img-77.jpeg)

Dalam rangka melakukan efektivitas dan efisiensi dalam pengelolaan SDM, serta mendukung kemajuan era digital, Perseroan meluncurkan aplikasi Human Resources Information System (HRIS) yang merupakan solusi perangkat lunak yang membantu memelihara, mengelola, dan memproses informasi rinci karyawan dan aktivitas SDM, serta beberapa aplikasi pendukung yang memudahkan sistem pengelolaan SDM. Aplikasi yang sudah berjalan dengan baik akan terus menerus dijalankan dan diperbaiki di tahun mendatang.

In order to carry out effectiveness and efficiency in HR management, as well as to support the advancement of the digital era, the Company launched the Human Resources Information System (HRIS) application which is a software solution that helps maintain, manage and process detailed employee information and HR activities, as well as several supporting applications. which facilitates the HR management system. Applications that are already running well will continue to be run and improved in the coming year.
