Roadmap TJSL WIKA BETON 2021-2023

WIKA BETON 2021-2023 CSR Roadmap

## 2021

### Penguatan dan Pengembangan TJSL CSR Strengthening and Development

- Sosialisasi kebijakan dan prosedur TJSL;
- Survei potensi program community development (comdev) di lingkungan setiap unit usaha;
- Standardisasi IPAL di seluruh pabrik (HSE);
- Pelaksanaan TJSL sesuai Pilar berbasis Materialitas SDG dan ISO 26000;
- SR dengan akurasi data &amp; informasi optimal;
- Memperoleh penghargaan terkait kinerja TJSL.

- Socializing CSR Policy and Procedure;
- Performing Comdev program potential survey in every business unit;
- Standardizing IPAL in all Plants (HSE);
- Implementing CSR in accordance with the Pilar based on SDG Materiality and ISO 26000;
- SR with data accuracy and optimal information;
- Receiving CSR Award and ASRA nomination

## 2022

### Kinerja Unggul Keberlanjutan Superior Performance Sustainability

- Pelaksanaan, monitoring dan evaluasi program TJSL;
- Adanya sistem informasi yang terintegrasi dengan TJSL;
- Terlaksananya 1 Program Prioritas dalam Comdev TJSL;
- SR dengan akurasi data &amp; informasi optimal;
- Melakukan studi banding dalam praktik TJSL;
- Memperoleh penghargaan terkait kinerja TJSL.

- Implementing CSR program monitoring and evaluation;
- Integrating Information System with CSR;
- Implementing 1 Priority Program in the Comdev CSR;
- Composing CSR with accurate data and optimal information;
- Performing comparative study in CSR practice;

## 2023

### Kinerja Excellent Excellent Performance

- Optimasi program dan kebijakan TJSL;
- Pengembangan sistem TJSL yang terintegrasi dengan kinerja perusahaan;
- Membentuk budaya perusahaan berbasis kinerja terkait TJSL;
- Menjadi perusahaan unggul dalam praktik TJSL berbasis ISO 26000;
- Memperoleh penghargaan terkait kinerja TJSL.
- Berjalannya kegiatan dan monitoring Comdev kepada penerima manfaat &amp; perusahaan;
- Sistem informasi TJSL terintegrasi yang berisikan informasi &amp; penilaian kinerja TJSL;
- Sustainable Report Excellent.

- Optimizing CSR Program &amp; Policy;
- Developing CSR System that integrates with the company performance;
- Shaping Corporate Culture based on CSR-based performance;
- Becoming an Excellent Company in CSR practice based on ISO 26000;
- Implementing Comdev activities and monitoring to beneficiaries &amp; the Company;
- Performing an integrated CSR information system, containing CSR information &amp; performance assessment;
- Achieving Sustainable Report Excellent.

TJSL secara strategis menghasilkan value yang optimal antara pelestarian lingkungan dan kemandirian masyarakat sekitar wilayah operasi. Di samping itu, seluruh kegiatan TJSL disusun sejalan dengan SDGs.

Strategic CSR produced optimal values between environmental preservation and community independence around operational areas. Additionally, other CSR activities are formulated in line with SDGs.
