## Sustainability Strategy

WIKA BETON sustainability strategy is implemented through social responsibility programs, which refer to the 7 core values of ISO 26000, which are corporate governance, human rights (HAM), labor practices, the environment, fair operational procedures, consumer issues, as well as community engagement and empowerment. ISO 26000 has also been implemented in the operational processes of the Company, with 6 out of 7 core aspects being internally implemented.

By referring to the CSR long term plan, the Company successfully perfected the CSR policies and procedures in 2021. In the next phase, the Company will expand the scope of improvement and achieve excellent performance in terms of system and monitoring in 2023.
