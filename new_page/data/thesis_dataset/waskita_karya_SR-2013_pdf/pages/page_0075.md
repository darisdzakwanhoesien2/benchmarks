Kesejahteraan pegawai baik kesehatan maupun keselamatan kerja merupakan salah satu hal penting yang menjadi perhatian Perseroan. Hal ini dilakukan agar pegawai dapat merasa aman dan nyaman dalam bekerja, yang selanjutnya dapat memotivasi mereka agar terus meningkatkan prestasi dan produktifitas kerja guna mencapai keunggulan kompetitif.

Selain penghasilan bulanan berupa gaji dan tunjangan transportasi, pegawai juga memperoleh berbagai tunjangan lain seperti tunjangan hari raya (THR), tunjangan cuti, dan bantuan uang duka bila pegawai atau keluarganya mengalami musibah meninggal dunia. Bentuk kesejahteraan pegawai lain di Waskita, meliputi:

- Jamsostek (Jaminan Kematian, Jaminan Kecelakaan Kerja, Jaminan Hari Tua);
- Medical Check Up;
- Family Gathering;
- Bonus Finalty;
- Jasa Produksi;
- Cuti;
- Perawatan Kesehatan;
- Penggantian Kaca Mata;
- Kupon Makan.

Selama tahun 2013, Perseroan telah mengeluarkan dana kesehatan pegawai sebesar Rp255.260.370 untuk biaya Rumah Sakit Pegawai. Hal ini turun 94.4% dibanding dana kesehatan pegawai tahun 2012 sebesar Rp270.280.674.

Employee Welfare namely health and welfare safety is one of the important things that concern the Company. This is done so that employees can feel safe and comfortable in the work, which in turn can motivate them to continue to improve performance and productivity in order to achieve competitive advantage.

In addition to the monthly income in the form of salary and transport allowance, employees also get other benefits such as festive allowance (THR), leave allowances, reimbursements and assistance when an employee or family of is experiencing a hard time or demising. Other forms of employee welfare in Waskita Karya include:

- Jamsostek (Death Insurance, Accident Insurance, Old Age Security);
- Medical Check Up;
- Family Gathering;
- Penalty Bonus;
- Production Service;
- Leave;
- Health Treatment;
- Replacement of Glasses;
- Eating Coupon.

During 2013, the company has expended IDR 255,260,370 for employee health benefit to pay Employee Hospital or decreased by 94.4% compared to the employee health benefit in 2012 in the amount of IDR270,280,674.
