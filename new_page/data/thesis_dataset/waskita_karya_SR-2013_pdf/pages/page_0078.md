The Company's HQ Energy Consumption Intensity (IKE) for the year 2013 was 186.76 kWh/m2 per year. The figure was still below the standard IKE for office buildings with 250 kWh/m2 per year (standard GBCI).

The Company strives to make energy savings. To that end we continue to make efforts to save energy consumption. Efforts have been undertaken during the year 2013, including efficient use of electric energy, replacing fossil fuel with gas fuel (own use). Besides, we also initiated the use of solar panels to generate electricity which was then used as lighting. [EN5] [EN6]

The company has also conducted a series of indirect efforts aimed at saving energy use, including: Reducing the use of private motor vehicles with car-free day implementation activities at the HQ held at every implementation of GMS and car pooling programs namely a program to go and go back from work together for HQ employees.
