# Kesehatan Kerja

Untuk mencegah timbulnya penyakit baik yang disebabkan oleh lingkungan kerja maupun pola hidup karyawan, Perseroan melakukan beberapa program, diantaranya: monitoring potensi bahaya kesehatan di tempat kerja seperti kebisingan, kadar debu, penerangan, ventilasi, tekanan panas, kandungan gas beracun, getaran di alat berat dan program menuju sehat. Selain itu, Perseroan menyelenggarakan penyuluhan dan pelatihan mengenai pengendalian keletihan bagi pekerja shift. (LAB)

# Occupational Health

To prevent diseases caused by work environment or lifestyle of employees, the Company has made several programs, such as: monitoring of potential health hazards in the workplace such as noise, dust levels, lighting, ventilation, heat stress, content of toxic gases, vibrations in heavy machine and programs to health. In addition, the Company has made counseling and training to deal with fatigue for shift employees. (LAB)
