e. Decentralized organization and marketing network and extensive production areas in Indonesia

Waskita Karya has performed reorganization in order to evaluate the effectiveness of the existing organizational structure. With the new organizational structure, Waskita Karya can realize a decentralized system and empowerment of its Divisions divided by more specific work and geographic area. Some of the benefits of the reorganization done by Waskita Karya are, among others:

- It has a business unit in every province in Indonesia and abroad (Jeddah), with a minimum of personnel;
- Delegation of decision-making process to Divisions;
- Each Division carries out the Company's activities in the field of marketing up to production;
- It becomes more selective in obtaining prefinancing projects (supported by payment guarantee).
