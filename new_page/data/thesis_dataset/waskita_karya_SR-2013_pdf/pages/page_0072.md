## Tanggung Jawab Terhadap Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja

Waskita Karya berkomitmen terhadap Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja (K3) melalui penerapan Sistem Manajemen Keselamatan Dan Kesehatan Kerja (SMK3). SMK3 dilaksanakan dalam rangka perlindungan atas keselamatan bagi karyawan dan pekerja lainnya serta setiap orang yang berada pada tempat kerja. Perseroan menunjang terpeliharanya derajat kesehatan, ketahanan fisik, daya kerja, kenyamanan kerja, keselamatan kerja, produktivitas kerja dan efisiensi; tercapainya keseimbangan antara program pencegahan dan penyembuhan serta memberikan motivasi karyawan agar melaksanakan upaya K3 secara mandiri dengan menekankan upaya-upaya preventif.

Perseroan selalu melakukan monitoring dalam penerapan sistem Standar Manajemen Mutu ISO 9001:2008; ISO 14001:2004; OHSAS 18001:2007; Sistem Manajemen Pengamanan sesuai regulasi yang diberlakukan sejak tahun 2007 yaitu Peraturan Kapolri (PERKAP) Nomor: 24 tahun 2007 dan Sistem Manajemen Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja (SMK3) yang diimplementasikan pada seluruh Unit Kerja.

Secara internal, sistem manajemen keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja juga telah dirumuskan dalam Perjanjian Kerja Bersama antara PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk. dengan Serikat Pekerja Waskita periode tahun 2013-2014 pasal 29 mengenai Keselamatan &amp; Kesehatan Kerja dan Lingkungan, diantaranya.

## Responsibility for Health and Safety

Waskita Karya is committed to the Health and Safety (K3) through the implementation of Safety and Health Management System (SMK3). SMK3 is implemented in order to protect the safety of employees and other employees as well as any persons in the workplace of the Company, support the maintenance of healthy condition, physical endurance, power work, work comfort, safety, productivity and efficiency, achieve a balance between prevention and cure and provide motivation for employees in order to implement self-K3 effort to emphasize preventive measures.

The company is always monitoring the implementation of quality management system standards ISO 9001:2008; ISO 14001:2004; OHSAS 18001:2007; The Security Management System according to regulations enacted since 2007 include Police Chief Regulation No. 24 of 2007 and Health and Safety Management System (SMK3) implemented on all Units of Work.

Internally, the occupational safety and health management system has also been defined in the Collective Labor Agreement between PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk. and Waskita Karya Labor Unions in 2013-2014 period, namely in Article 29 concerning Occupational Safety &amp; Health and Environment, among others.

1) The company provides Safety &amp; Health and Environment (K3LMP) tools in accordance with the provisions of Law No. 1 of 1970 concerning Safety &amp; Health and Environment as well as the applicable government regulations.
