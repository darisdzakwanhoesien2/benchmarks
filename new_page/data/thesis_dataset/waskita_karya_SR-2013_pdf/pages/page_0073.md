a. Kebebasan Berserikat

Waskita sangat menyadari bahwa hubungan kerja yang baik antara Perseroan dan pegawai merupakan prasyarat utama Perseroan dalam mencapai visi, misi dan tujuan Perseroan. Perseroan akan mampu menjalankan kegiatan usahanya dengan lancar dan mampu meraih laba serta tumbuh dan berkembang sesuai dengan target yang ditetapkan oleh pemegang saham, apabila terdapat lingkungan kerja sehat yang saling bersinergi.

2) Employees are entitled to working facilities, and K3LMP tools according to the nature and location of work assignments.

3) Employees shall wear and maintain K3LMP tools provided by the Company, which are given to be used, and not allowed to own, be misused, or transferred to unauthorized parties.

4) The Company may charge compensation in part or in full to the employee, that due to their negligence or deliberation has resulted in loss or damage resulting in the loss of K3LMP tools.

5) Employees shall actively take part in the prevention and mitigation of accidents / fires and pollution in their work environment.

6) Employees shall report any accidents / fires that occur in their work environment, and are required to provide correct information to the officer appointed by the Company to investigate the events in question.

7) For the benefit of the Company and their benefit, personnel shall comply / obey all K3LMP legislations applicable in the Company.

a. Freedom of Association

Waskita Karya is very aware that a good working relationship between the Company and the Company's employees is a major prerequisite in achieving the vision, mission and objectives of the Company. The company will be able to carry out its operations smoothly and is able to make a profit and grow and develop in accordance with the targets set by the shareholders, if there is a synergistic and healthy working environment.
