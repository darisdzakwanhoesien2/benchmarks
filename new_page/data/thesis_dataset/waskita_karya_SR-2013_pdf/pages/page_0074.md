Dalam rangka mendukung hal tersebut, Perseroan menjamin hak pekerjaanya untuk berserikat, berkumpul, dan menyampaikan pendapat. Waskita mendukung kegiatan Pekerja dalam berserikat dengan pembentukan Serikat Pekerja Waskita (Serikat Pekerja). Serikat Pekerja tercatat di Suku Dinas Tenaga Kerja dan Transmigrasi Kodya Jakarta Timur sesuai No. Bukti Pencatatan : 506/IV/P/V/2005 tanggal 18 Mei 2005.

Tenaga Kerja Perseroan yang tergabung dalam Serikat Pekerja (SP) Perseroan telah sesuai berdasarkan Anggaran Dasar dan Anggaran Rumah Tangga Serikat Pekerja Waskita tertanggal 10 November 1998, dan telah didaftarkan sebagaimana dalam Surat Keputusan Kepala Kantor Departemen Tenaga Kerja Kodya Jakarta Timur No.107/W26-K5/04/XII/K/1998 tentang Pendaftaran Serikat Pekerja di Tingkat Perseroan tertanggal 10 Desember 1998. Sampai dengan akhir tahun 2013 jumlah anggota Serikat Pekerja sebanyak 1200 Orang.

b. Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja (K3)
Dalam hal Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja (K3), Waskita memastikan pelaksanaan setiap aktivitas pekerjaan telah sesuai dengan Sistem Manajemen Kesehatan Keselamatan Kerja (SMK3).

In the support of this condition, the Company guarantees the right of employees to organize, assemble and express opinions. Waskita Karya supports employees' activities to associate with the formation of Waskita Karya Labor Union listed in Sub-Office of Manpower and Transmigration of the Municipality of East Jakarta According to Record No.: 506/IV/P/V/2005 dated 18 May 2005.

The Company's employee joining the Labor Union (SP) of the Company has complied with the Statutes and Bylaws of Waskita Karya Labor Union dated 10 November 1998 which have been registered pursuant to Decree of the Head of Office of the Ministry of Labor of East Jakarta Municipality No.107/W26-K5/04/XII/K/1998 concerning Registration of Company Labor Union dated 10 December 1998. Until the end of 2013 the total members of the Labor Union was 1,200 members.

Equality and Equal Employment Opportunity
Waskita Karya treats its employees regardless of gender or race. Application of the principle of non-discrimination is the company's ground to interact with employees within the framework of creating a harmonious industrial relationship.

Recruitment is done in a transparent and fair manner. Prospective employees who have the necessary competencies have the same opportunities to be employees of Waskita Karya. Employee development is done according to the level requirement for all employees to support the achievement of the goals and objectives of the company in the short term and long term.

b. Health and Safety (K3)
In terms of Health and Safety (K3), Waskita Karya ensures that the implementation of all work activities has been in accordance with the Occupational Safety Health Management System (SMK3).
