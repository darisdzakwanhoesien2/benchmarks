Perseroan selalu mengutamakan aspek kesehatan dan keselamatan bagi seluruh karyawan dalam menjalankan operasional. 1. Pasal 14 Hak Istirahat Tahunan, Cuti Besar, Cuti sakit, Cuti Bersalin
2. Pasal 15 Hak Istirahat Khusus
3. Pasal 24 Asuransi Jiwa untuk daerah konflik
4. Pasal 26 Perawatan kesehatan dan Jalan
5. Pasal 27 Alat bantu dengar dan kacamata
6. Pasal 28 Medical checkup
7. Pasal 29 tentang K3L

# Employee Health and Safety

The company always prioritizes health and safety aspects for all employees in carrying out operations. In addition to in the Company's safety regulations, the Collective Labor Agreement which is an agreement between the management and employees' representatives have explicitly contained matters related to health and safety. The content of the CLA includes:

1. Article 14 Rights of Annual Break, Big Leaves, sick leave, maternity leave
2. Article 15 Rights of Special Break
3. Article 24 Life Insurance for conflict areas
4. Article 26 Health treatment and outpatient treatment
5. Article 27 Hearing aids and eyeglasses
6. Article 28 Medical checkup
7. Article 29 About HSE
