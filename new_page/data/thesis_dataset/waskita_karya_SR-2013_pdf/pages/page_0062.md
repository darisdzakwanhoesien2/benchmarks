Benefit pegawai (4.5, EC3, LA3)

Selain dalam bentuk imbal jasa pekerjaan, Perseroan juga menyelenggarakan beberapa program yang ditujukan untuk meningkatkan kesejahteraan pekerja.

Perseroan selalu mendasarkan penentuan kompensasi pada kompetensi dan kinerja pegawai. Semakin tinggi kompetensi dan kinerja pegawai kompensasi yang diterima pegawai juga akan semakin meningkat.

Selain penghasilan bulanan berupa gaji dan tunjangan transportasi, pegawai juga memperoleh berbagai tunjangan lain seperti tunjangan hari raya (THR), tunjangan cuti, dan bantuan uang duka bila pegawai atau keluarganya mengalami musibah meninggal dunia. Bentuk kesejahteraan pegawai lain di Waskita Karya meliputi:

- Jamsostek (Jaminan Kematian, Jaminan Kecelakaan Kerja, Jaminan Hari Tua);
- Medical Check Up;
- Family Gathering;
- Bonus Penalti;
- Jasa Produksi;
- Cuti;
- Perawatan Kesehatan;
- Penggantian Kaca Mata;
- Kupon Makan.

Employee benefit (4.5, EC3, LA3)

In addition to work compensation, the Company also has several programs aimed at improving the welfare of employees.

The Company has always based compensation on the competency and performance of employees. The higher the competence and performance of an employee, the higher the compensation received by the employee.

In addition to the monthly income in the form of salary and transport allowance, employees also get other benefits such as holiday allowance (THR), leave allowances, reimbursements and assistance when an employee or family of the unfortunate death. Other employees in the form of welfare Waskita include:

- Social Security (Death Insurance, Accident Insurance, Old Age Security);
- Medical Check Up;
- Family Gathering;
- Penalty Bonus;
- Production Incentives;
- Leave;
- Health Care;
- Glasses Reimbursement;
- Meals Coupons.
