# Tentang Laporan Ini
About This Sustainability Report

Laporan ini merupakan Laporan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan ke-2 PT Waskita Karya. Setiap tahunnya, Perseroan melaporkan kegiatan pembangunan berkelanjutan dengan mengacu kepada standar Global Report Initiative (GRI). Penggunaan standar ini sangat membantu pelaporan yang memenuhi prinsip-prinsip transparansi, sustainability, dan kejelasan (clarity) atas segala aspek kegiatan yang dilakukan Perseroan di bidang ekonomi, lingkungan, dan sosial.

Laporan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan Perseroan memaparkan kinerja operasional serta komitmennya. Selain itu, dalam laporan ini, dipaparkan juga bagaimana Perseroan memberikan manfaat sosial, ekonomi, dan lingkungan melalui kemitraan dengan pemangku kepentingan khususnya adalah masyarakat di sekitar proyek yang dikerjakan perseroan.

Kinerja operasional tercermin melalui penerapan prinsip-prinsip good corporate governance serta praktek operasional yang secara konsisten mengacu kepada standar keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja yang tinggi. Selain itu, perseroan juga memegang komitmen untuk menjalankan program pengembangan masyarakat yang berkelanjutan dengan secara aktif melaksanakan kegiatan sesuai perencanaan.

Program-program kegiatan tersebut tidak hanya semata-mata sebagai pemenuhan persyaratan AMDAL di bidang sosial dan ekonomi, tetapi juga merupakan upaya proaktif tanggung jawab sosial Perseroan (Corporate Social Responsibility) PT Waskita Karya. Dalam pelaksanaannya, program kegiatan disesuaikan dengan rencana strategis jangka panjang demi kelangsungan hidup masyarakat sekitar proyek.

This report is the 2nd Sustainable Development Report of PT Waskita. Each year, the Company reports its activities of sustainable development with reference to the standard of the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI). The use of this standard is very helpful for a reporting that meets the principles of transparency, sustainability, and clarity of all aspects of the activities conducted by the Company in the economic, environmental and social sectors.

This Sustainable Development Report describes the Company's operating performance and commitment. In addition, this report also describes how the company provides social, economic, and environment benefits through partnerships with stakeholders, in particular the community around a project done by the company.

Operational performance is reflected through the application of the principles of good corporate governance and operational practices that consistently refer to high occupational health and safety standards. In addition, the company is also committed to running a sustainable community development program by actively carrying out activities according to the plan.

The activity programs are not merely as the fulfillment of EIA requirements in the social and economic aspects, but also a proactive CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility) effort of PT Waskita Karya. In practice, the activity program is tailored to the long-term strategic plan for the survival of the community around the project.
