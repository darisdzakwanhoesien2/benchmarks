Pengendalian Dampak Lainnya

Di luar uraian di atas, kami menyadari masih ada potensi ancaman dampak lain yang bisa mempengaruhi kualitas lingkungan. Tentu saja Perseroan juga memberikan perhatian serius demi mengendalikan dan meminimalkan ancaman tersebut.

Waskita Karya senantiasa mempertimbangkan factor lingkungan alam sekitar dalam melaksanakan proyek-proyek Perseroan. Komitmen tersebut diwujudkan dengan penerapan Sistem Manajemen Lingkungan ISO 14001:2004, sehingga polusi yang dihasilkan proyek selalu masih berada dalam batas aman atau dibawah NAB (Nilai Ambang Batas) yang diizinkan.

Perseroan melalui kebijakan manajemen berkomitmen terhadap pemenuhan semua peraturan perundang-undangan terkait dengan lingkungan. Kepatuhan terhadap pelaksanaan manajemen lingkungan menjadi bagian aspek Key Performance Indicator (KPI) dalam menilai kinerja pegawai.

B3 Waste Generated / Imported and its management [EN22] [EN24]

Solid and liquid B3 Waste is stored in special packaging / drums equipped with symbols and labels before being stored temporarily and then submitted to a licensed collector.

Other Impact Control

Beyond the above description, we realize there is still a potential threat of other impacts that could affect the quality of the environment. Of course, the company gives serious attention in order to control and minimize the threat.

Waskita Karya always considers the factors surrounding the environment in implementing the company's projects. The Company through management policy is committed to compliance with all laws and regulations related to the environment. Adherence to the implementation of environmental management aspects is a part of Key Performance Indicator (KPI) in assessing employee performance.
