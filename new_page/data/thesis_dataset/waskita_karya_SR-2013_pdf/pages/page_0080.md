## Pengelolaan Limbah

Upaya menjaga kelestarian lingkungan tak bisa dilepaskan dari usaha bersama untuk meminimalkan ancaman kerusakan yang bisa ditimbulkan dari keberadaan limbah. Karenanya Perseroan memiliki kebijakan terkait pengelolaan limbah. Ada beberapa bentuk limbah yang dihasilkan Perseroan dari kegiatan operasional yang dilakukan, dan pengelolaannya dilakukan dengan memperhatikan karakteristik limbah yang dihasilkan. [EN22]

## Limbah Bukan B3 Dihasilkan dan Pengelolaannya [EN22]

Limbah cair non B3 yang berasal dari kegiatan WC/toilet, pantry dan kantin diolah di STP sebelum dibuang ke badan air penerima. Limbah cair yang telah melewati proses di STP dipastikan telah memenuhi baku mutu limbah cair domestik Peraturan Gubernur DKI Jakarta No.122 tahun 2005.

Perseroan melakukan pengukuran limbah cair non B3 dengan pengambilan contoh uji terhadap kualitas limbah cair yang dihasilkan secara berkala (setiap 6 bulan sekali) sebelum disalurkan ke saluran drainase kota.

Sedangkan limbah padat non B3 (sampah), dilakukan pemisahan sesuai jenis organik dan anorganik. Selanjutnya sampah diserahkan ke Dinas Kebersihan setempat. Khusus untuk logam selanjutnya diserahkan kepada pengumpul khusus sampah logam.

## Waste Management

Efforts to preserve the environment cannot be separated from a concerted effort to minimize the threat of damage that may result from the presence of waste. Therefore the Company has a policy regarding waste management. There are several forms of waste generated from the Company's operations and the management is done by taking into account the characteristics of the waste generated. [EN22]

## Non B3 Waste and its Management [EN22]

Liquid Non B3 waste derived from the activities of WC / toilet, pantry and cafeteria is treated in STP before being discharged into receiving water bodies. Waste liquid that has passed through the STP process has met certain quality standards of domestic wastewater under Jakarta Governor Decree No. 122 of 2005.

The company performs measurements of non-B3 waste water by taking sample of the quality of the wastewater produced periodically (every 6 months) before it is piped to the city drainage.

Meanwhile, non-B3 solid waste (garbage) is separated according to the type of organic and inorganic. Furthermore it is delivered submitted to the local Health Office while metals will be transferred to a special metal garbage collector.
