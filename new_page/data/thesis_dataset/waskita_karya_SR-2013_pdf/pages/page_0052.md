## Pengembangan Kompetensi SDM

Waskita Karya secara berkesinambungan melakukan pembinaan dan pengembangan SDM untuk memenuhi kompetensi perilaku maupun kompetensi teknis yang dipersyaratkan. Pembinaan dan pengembangan dilakukan dengan metode pendidikan, sertifikasi, pelatihan, pengembangan, penugasan khusus maupun melalui mutasi dan promosi.

Secara periodik pegawai yang masuk dalam Talent Pool akan diberikan gambaran mengenai potensi kompetensinya berdasarkan metode assessment center. Berdasarkan hasil assessment center tersebut, pegawai akan mendapatkan saran dan rekomendasi untuk mendapatkan dan melakukan pengembangan diri. Program pengembangan dilakukan secara bervariasi, melalui program training, assignment, job enlargement, job enrichment, dan sebagainya.

Waskita Karya berkomitmen untuk terus meningkatkan kapasitas dan kapabilitas SDM, karena Perseroan menyadari bahwa peningkatan kompetensi akan memberikan kontribusi yang signifikan terhadap peningkatan produktivitas dan kualitas pegawai dan organisasi. Komitmen tersebut diwujudkan dengan meningkatnya anggaran/biaya untuk pelatihan dan pengembangan SDM. Tahun 2013, realisasi biaya pendidikan, pengembangan dan pelatihan SDM meningkat sekitar 8,7% yaitu sebesar Rp6.101.384.514 dibandingkan tahun 2012 sebesar Rp5.612.831.665.

## HR Competency Development

Waskita Karya continuously provides HR training and development to meet the behavioral competencies and technical competencies required. The training and development are carried out by the methods of education, certification, training, development, special assignments or through transfers and promotions.

Periodically, employees included in the Talent Pool will be given an overview of their potential competency based on the assessment center method. Based on the results of the assessment center, the employees will receive advice and recommendations to acquire and develop themselves. The development program is done through a variety of means namely training programs, assignment, job enlargement, job enrichment, and so on.

Waskita Karya is committed to continuously improve the capacity and capability of human resources, because the company recognizes that increased competence will contribute significantly to the improvement of productivity and quality of employees and the organization. The commitment is realized by increasing the budget / costs for training and human resource development. In 2013, the realization of the cost of education, human resource development and training increased by approximately 8.7% in the amount of IDR 6,101,384,514 compared to the year 2012 amounting to IDR 5,612,831,665.
