## Program Beasiswa Bagi Keluarga pegawai

Program beasiswa untuk anak pegawai khusus diberikan kepada anak pegawai yang sudah ada pada tingkat (SMP/SMU/perguruan tinggi). Program ini secara rutin Perseroan buka setiap tahun. Bagi para peserta yang memenuhi syarat dan lolos dalam seleksi akan mendapatkan uang beasiswa.

Hingga saat ini, Perseroan telah memberikan beasiswa kepada anak pegawai sebanyak 52 orang. Beasiswa diberikan kepada anak-anak pegawai yang bersekolah didalam negeri maupun diluar negeri.

Program ini Perseroan diciptakan dengan tujuan memotivasi anak-anak para pegawai agar berprestasi, selain sebagai salah satu apresiasi atas kinerja/kontribusi para pegawai.

Komitmen Terhadap Penegakan Hak Asasi Manusia Perseroan senantiasa menjunjung tinggi hak asasi manusia pada setiap aspek kegiatan operasi Perseroan. Sesuai dengan Undang-Undang Dasar 45 pasal 28, Perseroan memberikan kebebasan berkumpul dan berserikat kepada seluruh pegawai untuk membentuk beberapa serikat pekerja dan serikat buruh di lingkungan Perseroan sebagai wadah komunikasi dan sarana menyampaikan aspirasi.

Seluruh pegawai mendapatkan hak yang sama tanpa membedakan jenis kelamin untuk keselamatan, kesehatan dan lingkungan, peluang karir, pelatihan dan pengembangan, rotasi dan mutasi, menduduki jabatan tertentu di Perseroan, serta benefit dari Perseroan.

## Scholarship Program for Employee Family

Scholarship program for employees' children is specifically given to children of employees who are already at the level of junior high / high school / college. The program is routinely opened by the company every year. Participants who are eligible and qualify for the selection will receive scholarship.

To date, the Company has provided scholarships to 52 employees' children. The scholarships are awarded to employees' children who attend school at home and abroad.

This program is made by the Company to motivate employees' children to excel, as well as an appreciation for the performance / contribution of the employees.

## Commitment to Human Rights Enforcement

The Company has continually upheld human rights in every aspect of the Company's operations. In accordance with the 1945 Constitution Article 28, the Company provides freedom of assembly and association for all employees to form unions in the Company as a means of communication and channeling aspirations.

All employees get the same rights regardless of gender for the safety, health and environment, career opportunities, training and development, rotation and mutation, certain positions in the Company, as well as the benefit of the Company.
