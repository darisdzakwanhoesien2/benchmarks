# Pemilihan Serta Evaluasi Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris (4.2)

Pemilihan Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris ditentukan dalam Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS). Secara umum, kualifikasi yang digunakan dalam penentuan Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris adalah senioritas, latar belakang pendidikan, serta pengalaman kerja.

Evaluasi kinerja Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris dilakukan dalam Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS) dengan melihat kinerja Perseroan di bidang ekonomi, sosial, lingkungan, serta keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja.

# Election and Evaluation of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners (4.2)

Election of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners determined in the General Meeting of Shareholders (AGM). In general, the criteria used in the determination of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners are seniority, educational background, and work experience.

Evaluation of the performance of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners is conducted at a General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) to see the performance of the company in the economic, social, environmental, and occupational safety and health fields.
