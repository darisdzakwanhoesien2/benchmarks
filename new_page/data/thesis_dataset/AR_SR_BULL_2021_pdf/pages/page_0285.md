b. Gas tankers provide maritime transportation of liquified gas, which include among others; LPG, propylene, propane and LNG.

c. Chemical tankers provide maritime transportation of liquid chemical (organic and non-organic) and vegetable oil and animal fats.

d. Others comprise of providing crew and vessels management.

The accounting policies of the reportable segments are the same as the Group's accounting policies described in Note 2. Segment profit represents the profit earned by each segment without allocation of administrative expenses, final income tax, loss on non-functional exchange, increase (decrease) in revaluation of vessels, finance cost and other gain and losses.

This is the measure reported to the Directors as the chief operating decision maker for the purposes of resource allocation and assessment of segment performance.
