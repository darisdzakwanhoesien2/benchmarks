# Efisiensi dan Daur Ulang Air dalam Operasional Pertambangan

Dalam kegiatan pertambangan dan pengolahan, ABM menerapkan berbagai langkah efisiensi penggunaan air. CK memanfaatkan air dari void bekas tambang untuk penyiraman jalan hauling guna mengendalikan debu dan mengurangi pengambilan air dari sumber alami. Air bekas pencucian alat berat di washpad dikelola melalui oil trap untuk memisahkan minyak dan sedimen, kemudian digunakan kembali untuk pencucian unit lainnya. CK juga menerapkan rainwater harvesting dan melakukan pencatatan harian konsumsi air untuk mengidentifikasi potensi pemborosan.

# Efficiency and Water Recycling in Mining Operations

In mining and processing activities, ABM implements various water efficiency measures. CK utilizes water sourced from decommissioned mine voids for hauling road watering to control dust and reduce water extraction from natural sources. Water arising from the washing of heavy equipment on the washpad is managed through an oil trap designed to separate oil and sediments, with the water subsequently being reused for cleaning other units. CK implements rainwater harvesting systems and maintains daily records of water consumption to identify any potential wastage.

# Pengelolaan Air Terpadu di Area Izin Usaha Pertambangan (IUP)

Sebagai pemegang IUP, TIA menerapkan sistem pengelolaan air yang terintegrasi. Air hasil pengendapan di settling pond dimanfaatkan kembali untuk dust suppression dan proses tambang, sementara sedimen yang mengendap digunakan untuk reklamasi lahan. TIA juga mengoperasikan SPARING untuk pemantauan kualitas air secara daring dan real-time, serta menerapkan Zero Discharge Policy, yaitu tidak melakukan pembuangan air ke badan air sebelum memenuhi baku mutu. Pemanfaatan air hujan juga dilakukan untuk mengurangi pengambilan air permukaan.

# Integrated Water Management in the Mining Business License Area (IUP)

As the holder of an IUP, TIA implements an integrated water management system. Water derived from sedimentation in the settling pond is reutilized for dust suppression and mining processes, while the settled sediment is utilized for land reclamation. TIA additionally operates SPARING for online and real-time monitoring of water quality and enforces a Zero Discharge Policy, ensuring that no water is discharged into water bodies until it complies with established quality standards. Furthermore, rainwater harvesting is also being carried out to reduce surface water extraction.

# Pencegahan Air Asam Tambang dan Perlindungan Kualitas Lingkungan

ABM, melalui TIA, menerapkan berbagai langkah pencegahan terhadap potensi Acid Mine Drainage (AMD) dan kontaminasi lingkungan. Pengelolaan overburden dan material berpotensi asam dilakukan melalui reklamasi dan revegetasi dengan penutupan tanah pucuk serta penanaman vegetasi lokal untuk mengurangi kontak air dengan batuan sulfida. Sistem drainase dan settling pond dibangun untuk mengendalikan limpasan air hujan, sementara pemantauan kualitas air melalui SPARING yang terhubung dengan KLHK memastikan parameter seperti pH, TSS, debit, dan logam berat tetap memenuhi baku mutu. Apabila diperlukan, pengapuran dilakukan untuk menetralkan pH, dengan penerapan Zero Discharge Policy sebagai prinsip utama pengelolaan air tambang.

# Prevention of Acid Mine Drainage and Environmental Quality Protection

ABM, through TIA, implements various preventive measures to mitigate potential acid mine drainage (AMD) and environmental contamination. Overburden and potentially acid-generating materials are managed through reclamation and revegetation employing topsoil cover and the planting of local vegetation to reduce water contact with sulfide rocks. Drainage systems and settling ponds are constructed to control runoff, whilst water quality monitoring—conducted through SPARING and coordinated with the Ministry of Environment and Forestry (KLHK)—ensures that parameters such as pH, TSS, flow rate, and heavy metals remain within quality standards. Where deemed necessary, lime treatment is applied to neutralize pH levels, with a Zero Discharge Policy serving as the main principle of mine water management.
