# Manajemen Risiko

Dalam mengelola risiko, termasuk risiko terkait perubahan iklim, ABM menerapkan ISO 31000 sebagai kerangka kerja manajemen risiko yang berlaku secara terpusat di tingkat Perusahaan dan entitas anak. Sistem Manajemen Risiko Grup ABM telah diformalkan melalui Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) Policy No. ABM-PLC-ERM-001 tanggal 21 Agustus 2021 dan SOP No. ABM-SOP-ERM-001 tanggal 25 September 2021, guna memastikan penerapan strategi mitigasi risiko yang konsisten serta dukungan sumber daya yang memadai.

Penerapan manajemen risiko di ABM mengacu pada model tiga lini. Lini pertama terdiri dari Risk Owner dan Risk Champion yang bertanggung jawab langsung atas pengelolaan risiko di masing-masing fungsi dan entitas. Departemen ERM berperan sebagai lini kedua yang mengawasi dan mendukung pelaksanaan manajemen risiko melalui pengembangan kerangka kerja, pendampingan, serta pemantauan implementasi mitigasi risiko. Secara struktural, fungsi ERM berada di bawah Direktur dan dipimpin oleh Division Head Risk Management.

# Risk Management

In managing risks, including those related to climate change, ABM applies ISO 31000 as the risk management framework, which is centrally implemented at the company and subsidiary levels. The ABM Group Risk Management System has been formalized through the Corporate Risk Management Policy (ERM) No. ABM-PLC-ERM-001 dated August 21, 2021, and SOP No. ABM-SOP-ERM-001 dated September 25, 2021, to ensure consistent application of risk mitigation strategies and adequate resource allocation.

The implementation of risk management at ABM refers to the three lines model. The first line includes Risk Owners and Risk Champions, who have direct responsibility for risk management within their respective functions and entities. The ERM Department serves as the second line, overseeing and supporting risk management activities through the development of frameworks, guidance, and the monitoring of risk mitigation efforts. Structurally, the ERM function reports to the Director and is led by the Head of the Risk Management Division.

Efektivitas penerapan manajemen risiko dievaluasi secara berkala di seluruh entitas anak melalui koordinasi dengan entitas induk, rapat ERM rutin, serta pembahasan risiko utama. Proses manajemen risiko mencakup identifikasi risiko utama, perencanaan dan implementasi mitigasi, serta pemantauan efektivitas mitigasi melalui laporan kemajuan, yang menjadi dasar evaluasi dan penguatan pengelolaan risiko Grup ABM secara berkelanjutan. [SEOJK F.27]

The effectiveness of risk management implementation is regularly assessed across all subsidiaries through coordination with the parent entity, routine ERM meetings, and discussions of key risks. The risk management process involves identifying significant risks, planning and executing mitigation measures, and monitoring mitigation effectiveness through progress reports, which form the basis for ongoing evaluation and improvement of ABM Group's risk management practices.
