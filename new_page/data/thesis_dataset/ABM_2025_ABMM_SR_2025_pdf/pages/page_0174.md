# Whistleblowing System (Sistem Pengaduan Pelanggaran)

ABM memiliki mekanisme Whistleblowing System (WBS) yang memungkinkan karyawan dan pemangku kepentingan melaporkan dugaan pelanggaran etika atau korupsi secara aman tanpa risiko pembalasan. Grup ABM menyediakan berbagai kanal pelaporan, termasuk email khusus dan PO Box yang dioperasikan secara independen oleh Komite Audit selama 24/7, serta jalur alternatif melalui atasan, HR, Legal, Compliance, atau Internal Audit.

Sepanjang 2025, ABM menerima sejumlah 33 laporan dugaan pelanggaran etika melalui mekanisme pengaduan yang tersedia. Seluruh laporan ditindaklanjuti melalui proses investigasi rahasia oleh Tim Internal Audit sebagai bentuk respon, serta didukung penetapan tindakan korektif dan langkah remediasi untuk mencegah terulangnya kejadian serupa. Untuk kasus yang kompleks, Perusahaan dapat melibatkan ahli forensik eksternal, dan selama proses investigasi karyawan yang terlibat dapat dikenakan penonaktifan sementara guna menjaga integritas proses.

Apabila dugaan terbukti, ABM menjatuhkan sanksi sesuai Peraturan Perusahaan dan tingkat pelanggaran, mulai dari teguran tertulis hingga pemutusan hubungan kerja. Dalam hal terdapat pelanggaran terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan, Perusahaan menyampaikan laporan kepada otoritas yang berwenang, termasuk OJK dan Bursa Efek Indonesia, sebagai bagian dari komitmen kepatuhan. Hasil penanganan kasus, dengan tetap menjaga kerahasiaan, dapat dikomunikasikan sebagai pembelajaran untuk memperkuat budaya tanpa toleransi terhadap korupsi.

ABM menerapkan kebijakan non-retaliasi dan memastikan setiap pelapor yang menyampaikan laporan dengan itikad baik memperoleh perlindungan. Informasi mengenai mekanisme Whistleblowing System (WBS) dikomunikasikan secara berkelanjutan melalui pelatihan, orientasi karyawan baru, dan berbagai media internal.

# Whistleblowing System (Violation Reporting System)

ABM has implemented a Whistleblowing System (WBS) mechanism that allows employees and stakeholders to report suspected ethical violations or corruption securely, without fear of retaliation. The ABM Group offers multiple reporting channels, including a dedicated email and a PO Box operated independently by the Audit Committee 24/7, as well as alternative channels through supervisors, Human Resources, Legal, Compliance, or Internal Audit.

Throughout 2025, ABM received a total of 33 reports of suspected ethical violations through the available grievance mechanism. All reports are followed up with confidential investigation processes conducted by the Internal Audit Team as a form of response, supported by the implementation of corrective actions and remediation steps to prevent similar incidents from recurring. For complex cases, the Company may involve external forensic experts, and during the investigation, involved employees may be placed on temporary administrative leave to maintain the integrity of the process.

If the suspicion is proven, ABM imposes sanctions in accordance with the Company Regulations and the severity of the violation, which may vary from written warnings to termination of employment. In cases of violations of laws and regulations, the Company reports these to the relevant authorities, including OJK and the Indonesia Stock Exchange, as part of our compliance. The outcomes of case handling, while maintaining confidentiality, may be shared as lessons learned to reinforce a culture of zero tolerance for corruption.

ABM implements a non-retaliation policy and guarantees protection for any whistleblower acting in good faith. Information regarding the Whistleblowing System (WBS) mechanism is continuously communicated through training, new employee orientations, and various internal media.
