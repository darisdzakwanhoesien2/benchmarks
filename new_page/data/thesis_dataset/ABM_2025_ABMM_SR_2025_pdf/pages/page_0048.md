# Pendekatan Manajemen untuk Lingkungan Management Approach for the Environment

ABM mengelola aspek lingkungan sebagai bagian integral dari strategi keberlanjutan Perusahaan untuk meminimalkan dampak negatif, meningkatkan efisiensi sumber daya, serta mendukung ketahanan operasional jangka panjang. Pendekatan ini mengacu pada peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku, standar nasional dan internasional, serta kerangka pelaporan Global Reporting Initiative (GRI), dengan penguatan tata kelola melalui fungsi Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE), manajemen risiko, dan pengawasan Direksi serta Dewan Komisaris.

Secara umum, prinsip ini diwujudkan dalam kebijakan dan implementasi yang terkait dengan aspek-aspek di bawah ini:

## A. Pengelolaan Energi dan Emisi

- **Konsumsi Energi**
ABM menerapkan kebijakan dan prosedur pengelolaan energi di seluruh entitas untuk meningkatkan efisiensi operasional dan menekan emisi, dengan pengukuran konsumsi energi listrik dan bahan bakar secara sistematis, penerapan inisiatif efisiensi, serta pemantauan kinerja melalui indikator yang relevan.

- **Emisi GRK**
ABM mengelola emisi Gas Rumah Kaca sebagai isu material melalui penghitungan Cakupan 1, Cakupan 2, dan sebagian Cakupan 3 menggunakan metodologi IPCC dan GHG Protocol, serta menerapkan berbagai inisiatif mitigasi untuk mengendalikan dan menurunkan emisi.

## B. Pengelolaan Sumber Daya Alam dan Lingkungan

- **Penggunaan Air**
ABM mengelola penggunaan air secara bertanggung jawab melalui izin resmi, pemantauan pengambilan dan pembuangan air, pengolahan air limbah sebelum dilepas ke lingkungan, serta pengujian kualitas air secara berkala.

- **Keanekaragaman Hayati**
ABM berkomitmen melindungi ekosistem di sekitar wilayah operasional melalui pelaksanaan RKL-RPL dan UKL-UPL, rehabilitasi dan reklamasi lahan, serta Perusahaan melakukan pendekatan yang mempertimbangkan karakteristik ekologis setempat.

ABM manages environmental aspects as an integral part of the Company's sustainability strategy, aiming to minimize negative impacts, enhance resource efficiency, and bolster long-term operational resilience. This approach ensures compliance with relevant laws and regulations, as well as national and international standards, including the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) reporting framework. Governance is reinforced through the functions of Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE), risk management, and oversight by the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners.

In general, this principle is actualized through policies and implementations concerning the following aspects:

## A. Energy Management and Emissions

- **Energy Consumption**
ABM implements energy management policies and procedures across all entities to improve operational efficiency and reduce emissions. This includes systematic measurement of electricity and fuel consumption, the implementation of efficiency initiatives, and the monitoring of performance through relevant indicators.

- **GHG Emissions**
ABM manages Greenhouse Gas emissions as a significant material issue by quantifying Scope 1, Scope 2, and some Scope 3 emissions utilizing IPCC and GHG Protocol methodologies. Additionally, the company implements various mitigation initiatives designed to control and reduce emissions.

## B. Management of Natural Resources and the Environment

- **Water Usage**
ABM manages water usage responsibly through official permits, monitoring water extraction and disposal processes, treating wastewater prior to its release into the environment, and conducting regular water quality assessments.

- **Biodiversity**
ABM is committed to protecting the surrounding ecosystem of the operational area through the implementation of RKL-RPL and UKL-UPL, as well as land rehabilitation and reclamation initiatives. Additionally, the company adopts approaches that take into account the local environment's ecological characteristics.
