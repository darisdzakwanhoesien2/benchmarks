Supply Chain

Grup ABM berkomitmen mendorong pertumbuhan berkelanjutan melalui optimalisasi operasional dan ekspansi portofolio investasi di sektor pertambangan. Pendekatan multisektoral diterapkan untuk memenuhi kebutuhan pasar energi, mencakup sumber daya energi, jasa pertambangan dan energi, serta infrastruktur energi terbarukan. Upaya ini juga diperkuat melalui keterlibatan dalam inisiatif berkelanjutan, termasuk proyek carbon offset dan eksplorasi energi terbarukan.

Sebagai gambaran, berikut ilustrasi ekosistem bisnis ABM:

The ABM Group is dedicated to fostering sustainable growth through operational efficiency and the expansion of its investment portfolio in the mining sector. A multi-sectoral strategy is applied to address the requirements of the energy market, encompassing energy resources, mining and energy services, as well as renewable energy infrastructure. These initiatives are further supported by participation in sustainable projects, including carbon offset programs and the development of renewable energy sources.
