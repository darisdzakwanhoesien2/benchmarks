# Pemanfaatan Energi Surya di Entitas Lain Grup ABM

Sebagai bagian dari diversifikasi energi, Grup ABM memanfaatkan energi surya untuk menurunkan konsumsi energi berbasis bahan bakar fosil dan listrik konvensional. CK mengoperasikan Pembangkit Listrik Tenaga Surya (PLTS) off-grid dengan sistem Containerized Battery Energy Storage System (CBESS) di site CK KIM, Provinsi Jambi, dengan kapasitas 638 kWp dan baterai 1 MWh, yang digunakan untuk memenuhi kebutuhan listrik fasilitas tambang seperti kantor, mess, klinik, dan masjid, sehingga mengurangi penggunaan genset berbahan bakar solar.

Inisiatif pemanfaatan energi surya juga diterapkan oleh SSB di Plant Cikupa serta CKB di Samarinda. Di Plant Cikupa, sistem Solar PV yang mulai beroperasi pada Agustus 2025 hingga Desember 2025 telah menghasilkan listrik sebesar 135,37 MWh, setara dengan 17,69% dari total konsumsi listrik plant sebesar 765,04 MWh pada periode tersebut. Pemanfaatan energi surya ini memberikan efisiensi biaya dengan perbandingan tarif listrik PLN sebesar Rp 1.035,76 per kWh dan tarif Solar PV sebesar Rp 828,61 per kWh, sehingga menghasilkan penghematan biaya sebesar Rp 28.041.895,5 selama periode Agustus–Desember 2025.

Selain manfaat ekonomi, penggunaan Solar PV di Plant Cikupa juga memberikan manfaat lingkungan, antara lain penghematan batu bara standar sebesar 62,033 ton, penghindaran emisi CO₂ sebesar 73,664 ton, serta setara dengan penanaman 101 pohon. Implementasi ini berkontribusi pada penurunan konsumsi energi listrik dari jaringan dan pengurangan emisi GRK Cakupan 2 di lingkungan operasional Grup ABM.

# Utilization of Solar Energy in Other Entities of ABM Group

As part of energy diversification, ABM Group utilizes solar energy to reduce fossil fuel-based energy consumption and conventional electricity. CK operates an off-grid Solar Power Plant (SPP) equipped with a Containerized Battery Energy Storage System (CBESS) at the CK KIM site in Jambi Province. This facility has a capacity of 638 kWp and a 1 MWh battery, which are utilized to supply electricity to various mining facilities, including offices, mess halls, clinics, and mosques, thereby reducing the diesel-fueled generator usage.

The solar energy utilization initiative is also undertaken by SSB at Plant Cikupa and CKB in Samarinda. At Plant Cikupa, the Solar PV system, which commenced operations in August 2025 and continued through December 2025, generated 135.37 MWh of electricity, equivalent to 17.69% of the plant's total electricity consumption of 765.04 MWh during that period. This solar energy utilization provides cost efficiency with a comparison between PLN electricity tariffs at Rp 1,035.76 per kWh and Solar PV tariffs at Rp 828.61 per kWh, resulting in cost savings of Rp 28,041,895.50 during August–December 2025.

Beyond economic benefits, the use of Solar PV at Plant Cikupa provides numerous environmental benefits. These include the conservation of 62.033 tons of standard coal, the prevention of 73.664 tons of CO₂ emissions, and equivalent to planting 101 trees. This implementation contributes to reducing electricity consumption from the grid and lowering Scope 2 GHG emissions within ABM Group's operational environment.
