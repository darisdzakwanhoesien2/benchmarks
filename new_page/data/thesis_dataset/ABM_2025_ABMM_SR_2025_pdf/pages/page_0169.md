# Peningkatan Kapabilitas ESG dan Kesiapan IFRS [SEOJK E.2]

Sebagai bagian dari penguatan tata kelola keberlanjutan dan persiapan penerapan IFRS S1 dan S2, ABM melaksanakan rangkaian pelatihan ESG - IFRS sepanjang tahun 2024 - 2025. Sebanyak enam peserta dari fungsi Finance, Risk Management, Corporate Communication, dan Communication mengikuti tiga sesi pelatihan resmi yang diselenggarakan oleh PwC (30 Juni), EY (25 September), dan IAI (3 Oktober). Selain itu, ABM juga mengikuti sesi sosialisasi IFRS Standards Board yang berlangsung di Bali melalui fasilitas daring.

Pelatihan ini memperkuat pemahaman internal terkait pelaporan keberlanjutan dan pengelolaan risiko iklim. Materi pelatihan mencakup dampak curah hujan tinggi terhadap operasional tambang, serta ketergantungan tambang baru di Kalimantan Tengah pada kondisi sungai yang perlu diintegrasikan ke dalam manajemen risiko dan pengungkapan keberlanjutan. Melalui kegiatan ini, ABM meningkatkan kesiapan organisasi dalam mengadopsi standar IFRS dan mengintegrasikannya secara konsisten ke dalam proses bisnis.

# Improving ESG Capabilities and IFRS Readiness [SEOJK E.2]

As part of strengthening sustainability governance and preparing for the implementation of IFRS S1 and S2, ABM conducted a series of ESG - IFRS training sessions throughout 2024 and 2025. Six participants from the Finance, Risk Management, Corporate Communication, and Communication functions attended three official training sessions organized by PwC (June 30), EY (September 25), and IAI (October 3). Furthermore, ABM also participated in an IFRS Standards Board socialization session held in Bali via online facilities.

This training improves internal understanding related to sustainability reporting and climate risk management. The training materials cover the impact of heavy rainfall on mining operations, as well as the dependence of new mines in Central Kalimantan on river conditions, which must be integrated into risk management and sustainability disclosures. Through this activity, ABM improves organizational readiness to adopt IFRS standards and consistently integrate them into business processes.
