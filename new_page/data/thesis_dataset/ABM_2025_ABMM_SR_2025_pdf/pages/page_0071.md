# Pengelolaan dan Mitigasi Dampak Keanekaragaman Hayati

Sebagai tindak lanjut atas hasil identifikasi dampak, ABM dan anak perusahaan menerapkan berbagai upaya mitigasi untuk meminimalkan dampak negatif terhadap keanekaragaman hayati. Upaya tersebut dilaksanakan berdasarkan Hirarki Mitigasi Keanekaragaman Hayati TIA, yang mencakup: [GRI 101-2] [GRI 101-3] [GRI 101-4][GROI 304-2]

1. Penghindaran Dampak melalui penilaian awal lingkungan sebelum pembukaan lahan, pemetaan habitat dan spesies flora-fauna, perencanaan tambang berdasarkan rona awal HTI akasia, serta identifikasi area sensitif dan habitat satwa dilindungi.
2. Pengurangan Dampak melalui pembukaan lahan bertahap, penerapan sistem pengendalian erosi dan sedimentasi, larangan berburu dan memberi makan satwa liar, penjagaan koridor perlintasan satwa, serta pengelolaan kualitas habitat fauna darat, aves, dan biota perairan.
3. Pemulihan dan Restorasi melalui reklamasi dan revegetasi pascatambang, penanaman jenis lokal, fast growing species, MPTS, penanaman mangrove dan cemara di area pesisir, rehabilitasi DAS, serta transplantasi terumbu karang pada jalur pelayaran.
4. Monitoring, Evaluasi, dan Perbaikan Berkelanjutan melalui pemantauan flora dan fauna secara berkala, evaluasi peningkatan jumlah famili dan spesies flora, pemantauan satwa dilindungi, serta kolaborasi dengan ahli dan penerapan SOP pengelolaan biodiversitas.
5. Konservasi Jangka Panjang dengan tujuan memastikan rona akhir pascatambang memiliki daya dukung sebagai habitat flora-fauna, menjaga stabilitas ekosistem, serta meningkatkan nilai ekologis wilayah.

# Management and Mitigation of Biodiversity Impacts

Following the impact identification results, ABM and its subsidiaries implement various mitigation efforts to minimize negative effects on biodiversity. These efforts are based on the Biodiversity Mitigation Hierarchy of TIA, which includes: [GRI 101-2] [GRI 101-3] [GRI 101-4][SEOJK F.9]

1. Impact Avoidance through preliminary environmental assessments prior to land clearing, including the mapping of habitats and flora and fauna species, mine planning based on the initial conditions of the acacia plantation forest (HTI), and the identification of sensitive areas and protected animal habitats.
2. Impact Minimization through phased land clearing, the implementation of erosion and sediment control systems, prohibition of hunting and feeding wild animals, safeguarding wildlife crossing corridors, and managing the habitat quality of terrestrial fauna, birds, and aquatic biota.
3. Restoration and Rehabilitation through post-mining reclamation and revegetation, planting local and fast-growing species, such as MPTS, planting mangroves and casuarinas in coastal areas, rehabilitating watersheds, and transplanting coral reefs along shipping routes.
4. Monitoring, Evaluation, and Continuous Improvement through regular flora and fauna monitoring, evaluating increases in the number of flora families and species, monitoring protected animals, collaborating with experts, and implementing biodiversity management SOPs.
5. Long-term Conservation aims to ensure that the post-mining landscape can support flora and fauna habitats, maintain ecosystem stability, and enhance the area's ecological value.
