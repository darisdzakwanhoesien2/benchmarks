## Mendorong Efisiensi Operasional melalui Studi Lubricant Management System

PWP melakukan studi Lubricant Management System milik CK di site KIM untuk memahami penerapan sistem yang efektif dan teruji dalam pengelolaan pelumas. Hasil studi ini dimanfaatkan sebagai referensi dalam pengembangan sistem Fuel Management di seluruh proyek PWP dengan penyesuaian sesuai kebutuhan masing-masing site. Inisiatif ini diarahkan menuju digitalisasi penuh guna meningkatkan akurasi data, meminimalkan kesalahan manusia, serta mengurangi potensi penyimpangan, sekaligus memperkuat efisiensi, transparansi, dan akuntabilitas operasional.

## Promoting Operational Efficiency through Lubricant Management System Study

PWP conducted a study on CK's Lubricant Management System at the KIM site to understand the implementation of an effective and proven system for lubricant management. The results of this study are used as a reference in developing the Fuel Management system across all PWP projects, with adjustments made according to each site's needs. This initiative is aimed at full digitalization to improve data accuracy, minimize human errors, and reduce potential deviations, while also enhancing operational efficiency, transparency, and accountability.
