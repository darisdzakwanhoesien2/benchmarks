# Implementasi Code of Ethics and Conduct (COEC)

Implementation Code of Ethics and Conduct (COEC)

![img-102.jpeg](img-102.jpeg)

Secara berkesinambungan, ABM menanamkan budaya kerja professional, transparan, dan bertanggung jawab melalui penerapan Code of Ethics and Conduct (COEC) sebagai pedoman nilai, prinsip, dan perilaku bagi seluruh karyawan dan direksi. Karyawan didorong melaporkan pelanggaran COEC melalui dua jalur, yaitu berdiskusi dengan fungsi internal terkait atau menggunakan Whistleblowing System (WBS) yang menjamin kerahasiaan pelapor. Pelanggaran terhadap Peraturan Perusahaan akan dikenakan sanksi bertingkat, mulai dari teguran hingga pemutusan hubungan kerja. Data pelanggaran sepanjang 2025 disajikan pada tabel di bawah ini [SEOJK F.16]

ABM consistently fosters a culture of professionalism, transparency, and accountability through the implementation of the Code of Ethics and Conduct (COEC), serving as a guideline for values, principles, and behaviors for all employees and directors. Employees are encouraged to report any violations of the COEC through two channels: discussing with the relevant internal function or utilizing the Whistleblowing System (WBS), which ensures the confidentiality of the whistleblower. Violations of the Company Regulations will incur tiered sanctions, ranging from warnings to termination of employment. Data regarding violations throughout 2025 are presented in the table below.
