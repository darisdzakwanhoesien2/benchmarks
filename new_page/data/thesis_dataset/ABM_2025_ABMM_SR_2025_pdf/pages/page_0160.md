# Evaluasi kinerja badan tata kelola tertinggi [GRI 2-18]

ABM memiliki dua kerangka evaluasi kinerja badan tata kelola tertinggi, yaitu:

- Evaluasi Kinerja Komite di Bawah Dewan Komisaris

Dewan Komisaris melaksanakan evaluasi kinerja terhadap komite-komite yang berada di bawah pengawasannya sekurang-kurangnya satu kali dalam setahun. Penilaian dilakukan menggunakan berbagai metode, termasuk self-assessment, peer evaluation, atau metode lain yang disetujui dalam rapat internal Dewan Komisaris. Evaluasi ini membandingkan pelaksanaan tugas masing-masing komite dengan target serta indikator kinerja yang ditetapkan dalam rencana kerja tahunan.

Berdasarkan hasil evaluasi tahun 2025, Dewan Komisaris menilai bahwa Komite Audit, Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi, Komite Investasi, serta Komite ESG telah menjalankan peran dan tanggung jawabnya secara efektif. Kinerja komite-komite tersebut dinilai selaras dengan mandat yang diberikan serta target yang ditetapkan dalam rencana kerja tahunan.

- Evaluasi Kinerja Komite di Bawah Direksi

Direksi melakukan evaluasi secara berkala terhadap komite pendukung yang membantu pelaksanaan fungsi manajerial, khususnya Komite Group Executive Management (GEM). Evaluasi dilakukan melalui mekanisme yang terstruktur, antara lain self-assessment, peer evaluation, serta metode lain sesuai keputusan rapat internal Direksi. Proses ini bertujuan memastikan bahwa komite menjalankan peran operasional dan strategis secara efektif serta selaras dengan pedoman kerja yang berlaku.

Evaluasi terhadap Komite GEM dilaksanakan sejalan dengan sistem penilaian kinerja Direksi yang berbasis Key Performance Indicators (KPI), yang mencakup aspek keuangan, operasional, manajemen risiko, tata kelola, serta implementasi strategi keberlanjutan (ESG). # Evaluation of the Highest Governance Body's Performance [GRI 2-18]

ABM has two frameworks for evaluating the performance of the highest governance body:

- Performance Evaluation of the Committee Under the Board of Commissioners

The Board of Commissioners conducts performance evaluations of the committees under its supervision at least once a year. The assessment is carried out using various methods, including self-assessment, peer evaluation, or other methods approved in the Board of Commissioners' internal meeting. This evaluation compares each committee's performance in carrying out its duties with the targets and performance indicators set in the annual work plan.

Based on the 2025 evaluation results, the Board of Commissioners assesses that the Audit Committee, Nomination and Remuneration Committee, Investment Committee, and ESG Committee have effectively performed their roles and responsibilities. The performance of these committees is deemed to be consistent with the mandates and the targets set in the annual work plan.

- Performance Evaluation of the Committee Under the Board of Directors

The Board of Directors conducts periodic evaluations of supporting committees that assist in executing managerial functions, particularly the Group Executive Management (GEM) Committee. The evaluation is conducted via a structured mechanism comprising self-assessment, peer evaluation, and other methods as determined by internal Board meetings. This process aims to ensure that the committee performs its operational and strategic roles effectively and in compliance with applicable work guidelines.

The evaluation of the GEM Committee is conducted in line with the performance assessment system of the Board of Directors, which is based on Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).
