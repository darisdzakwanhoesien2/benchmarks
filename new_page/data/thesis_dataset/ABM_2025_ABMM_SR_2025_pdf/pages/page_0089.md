# Kinerja Sosial

## Partisipasi, Konsultasi, dan Komunikasi K3 [GRI 403-3] [GRI 403-4]

Untuk memperkuat disiplin dan pemahaman K3 secara berkesinambungan, ABM melaksanakan prosedur komunikasi dan konsultasi terhadap seluruh karyawan melalui:

- Pelaksanaan Safety Briefing
Safety briefing dilakukan di setiap shift di seluruh lini operasional Perusahaan.

- Forum Komunikasi K3
Forum komunikasi K3 dibentuk dan dijalankan secara rutin sebagai ruang sosialisasi dan kampanye K3. Forum ini terdiri dari Forum P2K3 yang diadakan setiap bulan. P2K3 merupakan panitia yang beranggotakan perwakilan Manajemen dan perwakilan pekerja, sebagai wadah kerja sama dalam meningkatkan implementasi keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja di lingkungan operasional. Selain itu, pertemuan bulanan dan safety talk juga dilakukan bersama kontraktor dan mitra kerja guna memperkuat budaya keselamatan secara menyeluruh.

- Kampanye K3
Kampanye K3 dilakukan melalui buletin, email, stiker, dan majalah dinding setiap bulan untuk seluruh karyawan. Pemasangan banner dan spanduk K3 dilaksanakan setiap tiga bulan. Program seperti Toolbox Meeting/P5M, peringatan Bulan K3 Nasional, Safety &amp; Health Talk, serta sosialisasi prosedur K3, termasuk penggunaan radio komunikasi dan aplikasi kerja, dilaksanakan secara berkala. Selain itu, setiap karyawan wajib mengikuti induksi K3 setelah kembali dari cuti. Pertemuan bulanan dan safety talk juga dilakukan bersama kontraktor dan mitra kerja.

Sepanjang 2025, P5M dilakukan setiap awal shift, sedangkan HSE Talk diadakan minimal sekali seminggu dengan topik K3 yang bergantian.

## Pelatihan K3 untuk Pekerja [GRI 403-5]

Sepanjang tahun 2025, ABM melaksanakan pelatihan K3 bagi karyawan untuk memastikan kesiapan mereka dalam menjalankan prosedur SMKP, penanganan kejadian kecelakaan kerja, serta pencegahan penyakit akibat kerja. Pelatihan ini dirancang sebagai bagian integral dari sistem manajemen K3 untuk memperkuat kompetensi pekerja dalam mengidentifikasi bahaya, menilai risiko, serta menerapkan pengendalian risiko sesuai prinsip Hazard Identification, Risk Assessment, and Determining Control (HIRADC).

## OHS Participation, Consultation, and Communication [GRI 403-3] [GRI 403-4]

To strengthen OHS (K3) discipline and understanding on an ongoing basis, ABM carries out communication and consultation procedures for all employees through:

- Implementation of Safety Briefings
Safety briefings are conducted in every shift across all of the Company's operational lines.

- OHS Communication Forum
An OHS communication forum is established and implemented regularly as a space for OHS socialization and campaigns. This forum consists of the P2K3 Forum, which is held every month. P2K3 is a committee comprising representatives of Management and worker representatives, serving as a forum for cooperation in improving the implementation of occupational safety and health in the operational environment. In addition, monthly meetings and safety talks are also conducted with contractors and business partners to strengthen the overall safety culture.

- OHS Campaigns
OHS campaigns are carried out through monthly bulletins, emails, stickers, and wall magazines for all employees. The installation of OHS banners and signage is carried out every three months. Programs such as Toolbox Meeting/P5M, the commemoration of National OHS Month, Safety &amp; Health Talk, and the socialization of OHS procedures, including the use of communication radios and work applications, are conducted periodically. In addition, each employee is required to undergo OHS induction upon returning from leave. Monthly meetings and safety talks are also conducted with contractors and business partners.

Throughout 2025, P5M was conducted at the start of every shift, while HSE Talk was held at least once a week with rotating OHS topics.

## OHS (K3) Training for Workers [GRI 403-5]

Throughout 2025, ABM conducted OHS (K3) training for employees to ensure their readiness in implementing SMKP procedures, handling occupational accident incidents, and preventing occupational diseases. This training was designed as an integral part of the OHS management system to strengthen workers' competencies in identifying hazards, assessing risks, and implementing risk controls in accordance with the principles of Hazard Identification, Risk Assessment, and Determining Control (HIRADC).
