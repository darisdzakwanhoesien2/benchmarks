# Respons Cepat ABM untuk Banjir &amp; Longsor Sumatera 2025

ABM's Rapid Response to the 2025 Sumatra Floods and Landslides [GRI 203-2]

![img-88.jpeg](img-88.jpeg)

Menanggapi bencana banjir dan longsor yang melanda Aceh, Sumatera Utara, dan Sumatera Barat pada 2025, Grup ABM bersama entitas dalam Grup TMT melalui kolaborasi dengan Human Initiative mengaktifkan program tanggap darurat untuk memastikan pemenuhan kebutuhan dasar masyarakat terdampak, khususnya kelompok masyarakat yang terdampak secara signifikan oleh bencana. Sebagai bagian dari komitmen kemanusiaan tersebut, Grup TMT menyalurkan bantuan senilai Rp 2,5 milyar kepada masyarakat terdampak di Aceh, khususnya di wilayah Aceh Tamiang dan Aceh Utara yang mengalami dampak paling parah akibat bencana banjir bandang, luapan sungai, dan tanah longsor yang terjadi pada akhir November 2025.

Sebanyak 55 relawan diterjunkan ke wilayah terdampak untuk melaksanakan evakuasi, distribusi bantuan, dan pendirian pos tanggap darurat. Selain dukungan relawan, bantuan yang disalurkan juga mencakup berbagai kebutuhan dasar masyarakat, antara lain paket sembako, perlengkapan hunian darurat (terpal, tikar, paku), peralatan memasak, perlengkapan mandi, paket kebutuhan dasar, perlengkapan tempat tidur (kasur, selimut, bantal, kelambu), pengadaan air bersih melalui water trucking, serta alat kebersihan rumah dan community working tools untuk mendukung proses pembersihan lingkungan pascabencana.

Selain bantuan logistik, dukungan operasional juga diberikan melalui pengerahan alat berat untuk membantu pembukaan akses jalan di wilayah terdampak sehingga proses evakuasi dan distribusi bantuan dapat berjalan lebih efektif. Emergency Response Team (ERT) juga diterjunkan untuk mendukung evakuasi korban, distribusi bantuan kebutuhan dasar, serta pendirian dapur umum bagi masyarakat terdampak.

Inisiatif ini mencerminkan komitmen ABM dalam mendukung pengurangan risiko dan kerugian akibat krisis kemanusiaan, sekaligus memastikan masyarakat terdampak memperoleh bantuan secara cepat, tepat sasaran, dan bermartabat. Selain di Aceh, dukungan kemanusiaan juga disalurkan ke wilayah terdampak lainnya di Sumatera Utara dan Sumatera Barat, termasuk Kabupaten Tapanuli Tengah, Tapanuli Selatan, dan Kabupaten Agam, sebagai bagian dari upaya kolaboratif untuk mempercepat pemulihan masyarakat pascabencana.

In response to the floods and landslides that struck Aceh, North Sumatra, and West Sumatra in 2025, ABM Group together with entities within the TMT Group, in collaboration with Human Initiative, activated an emergency response program to ensure the fulfillment of the basic needs of affected communities, particularly those significantly impacted by the disaster. As part of this humanitarian commitment, TMT Group provided assistance amounting to Rp2.5 billion to affected communities in Aceh, particularly in Aceh Tamiang and North Aceh, which were among the areas most severely impacted by flash floods, river overflows, and landslides that occurred in late November 2025.

A total of 55 volunteers were deployed to the affected areas to conduct evacuations, distribute assistance, and establish emergency response posts. Along with volunteer support, the aid offered also covered various basic needs for affected communities, such as food parcels, shelter kits (tarpaulins, mats, nails), cooking utensils, hygiene kits, dignity kits, bedding kits (mattresses, blankets, pillows, mosquito nets), supply of clean water through water trucking, as well as household cleaning supplies and community tools to assist with post-disaster clean-up efforts.

In addition to logistical aid, operational support has been supplied by deploying heavy equipment to clear roads in the affected areas, thereby facilitating more efficient evacuation and distribution of assistance. An Emergency Response Team (ERT) has also been deployed to assist with evacuations, distribute basic necessities, and establish community kitchens for those affected communities.

This initiative reflects ABM's commitment to supporting the reduction of risks and losses from humanitarian crises, while ensuring that affected communities receive assistance quickly, accurately targeted, and with dignity. Beyond Aceh, humanitarian assistance was also extended to other affected areas in North Sumatra and West Sumatra, including Central Tapanuli, South Tapanuli, and Agam Regency, as part of a collaborative effort to accelerate community recovery after the disaster.
