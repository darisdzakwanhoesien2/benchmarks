# Pendekatan Manajemen Untuk Kinerja Ekonomi

Selain berfokus pada isu keberlanjutan, profitabilitas merupakan elemen penting bagi ABM untuk mendistribusikan nilai ekonomi kepada seluruh pemangku kepentingan. Pada 2025, ABM mencatat kinerja operasional dan keuangan yang positif, meskipun berada di tengah dinamika industri pertambangan dan energi yang semakin kompleks. Sepanjang 2025, perusahaan tetap menunjukkan kemampuan menghasilkan pendapatan dan laba yang memberikan manfaat bagi seluruh pemangku kepentingan, melalui pembayaran pajak, pemberian remunerasi dan fasilitas bagi seluruh karyawan, pembagian dividen, pembayaran utang, serta realisasi dana pengembangan masyarakat dan lingkungan.

Dalam menghadapi tantangan industri untuk menjaga stabilitas kinerja ekonomi, ABM menerapkan peta jalan strategis yang disusun secara komprehensif dan diawasi secara ketat oleh Direksi, baik di tingkat anak perusahaan maupun kantor pusat.

Pengawasan dilakukan melalui peninjauan rutin pada setiap lini kerja dan memastikan seluruh inisiatif berjalan sesuai rencana. Dewan Komisaris turut menjalankan fungsi pengawasan sebagai perwakilan pemegang saham melalui evaluasi berkala atas laporan manajemen dan memastikan arah strategis Perusahaan diimplementasikan dengan efektif.

# Management Approach For Economic Performance

In addition to focusing on sustainability issues, profitability is an important element for ABM in distributing economic value to all stakeholders. By 2025, ABM achieved positive operational and financial performance despite the increasingly complex dynamics in the mining and energy industries. Throughout 2025, the Company consistently demonstrated our ability to generate revenue and profit that benefit all stakeholders through tax payments, employee remuneration and facilities, dividend distributions, debt repayments, and investments in community and environmental development.

In facing industry challenges to maintain economic performance stability, ABM has implemented a comprehensive strategic roadmap that is closely monitored by the Board of Directors, at both the subsidiary level and the head office.

Monitoring is conducted through regular reviews at every work line to ensure all initiatives progress in accordance with established plans. The Board of Commissioners also performs oversight functions as representatives of the shareholders by periodically evaluating management reports and ensuring the effective implementation of the company's strategic directives.
