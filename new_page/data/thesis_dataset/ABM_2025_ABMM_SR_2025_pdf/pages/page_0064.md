Untuk memastikan air buangan memenuhi standar lingkungan, seluruh air limbah dari kegiatan operasional diolah terlebih dahulu sebelum dilepas kembali ke badan air umum. Pada kegiatan pertambangan batu bara, air limbah dialirkan ke kolam pengendapan untuk mengendapkan sedimen. Kegiatan supporting seperti mess dan kantor di area pertambangan yang menghasilkan air limbah domestik sistem pengelolaan air limbahnya sama dengan lini bisnis logistik, manufaktur, dan energi baru terbarukan, yaitu pengelolaan air limbah dilakukan melalui Instalasi Pengolahan Air Limbah (IPAL) yang tersedia di fasilitas operasional atau kawasan industri terkait. [GRI 303-2] [GRI 303-4] [SEOJK F.8] [NDAQ E6]

Pemenuhan baku mutu efluen dijaga melalui pengujian kualitas air secara berkala sesuai dengan ketentuan pemerintah. Parameter pengujian meliputi pH, Total Suspended Solid (TSS), debit aliran, Chemical Oxygen Demand (COD), Biological Oxygen Demand (BOD), serta logam berat seperti Fe, Mn, dan Cd. Sepanjang periode pelaporan, seluruh hasil pengujian berada di bawah ambang batas baku mutu yang ditetapkan dan tidak terdapat kejadian ketidakpatuhan terhadap peraturan pengelolaan air limbah. [GRI 303-4] [SEOJK F.8]

Pemantauan pengambilan, penggunaan, dan pembuangan air dilakukan menggunakan flow meter serta pendekatan pencatatan air yang konsisten di seluruh entitas. Metodologi perhitungan yang digunakan pada tahun pelaporan tetap konsisten dengan tahun sebelumnya untuk menjaga keterbandingan data. Beberapa entitas, seperti TIA, telah menerapkan Sistem Pemantauan Air Limbah Secara Otomatis dan Terus Menerus (SPARING) yang terhubung langsung dengan sistem Kementerian Lingkungan Hidup dan Kehutanan (KLHK) guna memastikan kepatuhan secara real-time. [GRI 303-5] [SEOJK F.8] [NDAQ E6]

Berdasarkan studi air yang dilakukan oleh Bappenas pada tahun 2021, seluruh wilayah operasional ABM tidak berada dalam kategori wilayah dengan tingkat kelangkaan air sebagaimana diidentifikasi oleh Bappenas dan WRI Aqueduct. Meskipun demikian, ABM tetap menerapkan pengelolaan sumber daya air secara hati-hati dan bertanggung jawab, antara lain melalui pemanfaatan air hujan, penggunaan kembali air hasil pengolahan untuk penyiraman jalan operasional, serta penerapan langkah-langkah efisiensi penggunaan air di seluruh area kerja. [GRI 303-1] [GRI 303-5] [SEOJK F.8] [NDAQ E6]

To ensure compliance with environmental standards, all wastewater from operational activities is treated before discharge into public water bodies. In coal mining, wastewater is directed to a settling pond for sedimentation. Domestic wastewater from auxiliary activities, such as mess and office facilities within the mining area, is managed similarly to the logistics, manufacturing, and renewable energy business lines, through Wastewater Treatment Plants (WWTPs) located at operational sites or industrial areas. [GRI 303-2] [GRI 303-4] [SEOJK F.8] [NDAQ E6]

Effluent quality standards are maintained through regular water quality testing in accordance with governmental regulations. The testing parameters include pH, Total Suspended Solids (TSS), flow rate, Chemical Oxygen Demand (COD), Biological Oxygen Demand (BOD), and heavy metals such as Fe, Mn, and Cd. Throughout the reporting period, all test outcomes remained within the prescribed quality standard limits, and there were no violations of wastewater management regulations. [GRI 303-4] [SEOJK F.8]

Monitoring of water extraction, usage, and disposal is conducted employing flow meters and a consistent water accounting approach across all entities. The calculation methodology applied in the reporting year remains consistent with that of the previous year to maintain data comparability. Certain entities, such as TIA, have adopted an Automatic and Continuous Wastewater Monitoring System (SPARING) that is directly connected to the Ministry of Environment and Forestry (KLHK) system to ensure real-time compliance. [GRI 303-5] [SEOJK F.8] [NDAQ E6]

According to water studies conducted by Bappenas in 2021, the entire operational area of ABM is not classified as a water-stressed region, as identified by Bappenas and WRI Aqueduct. Nevertheless, ABM continues to implement careful and responsible water resource management, including the utilization of rainwater, the reuse of processed water for operational road irrigation, and applying water efficiency measures across the work area. [GRI 303-1] [GRI 303-5] [SEOJK F.8] [NDAQ E6]
