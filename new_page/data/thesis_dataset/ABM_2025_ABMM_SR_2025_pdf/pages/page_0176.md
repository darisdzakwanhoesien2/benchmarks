# ABM Meraih IICD Award 2025 untuk Praktik GCG Unggul

Pada tahun 2025, ABM meraih IICD Award 2025 dalam kategori *Best Financial Sector* (Mid Cap) pada ajang "The 16th IICD Corporate Governance Conference and Award: Building Resilience through Good Governance – Thriving in Turbulent Times" yang diselenggarakan oleh Indonesian Institute for Corporate Directorship (IICD). Penghargaan ini diberikan kepada emitten yang dinilai konsisten menerapkan praktik *Good Corporate Governance* (GCG) serta tidak terlibat dalam kasus serius yang bertentangan dengan prinsip tata kelola.

Pencapaian tersebut mencerminkan pengakuan atas penguatan tata kelola ABM, termasuk peran *Corporate Secretary* dalam memastikan efektivitas *Whistleblowing System* (WBS), penerapan *Sistem Manajemen Anti Penyuapan ISO 37001*, serta penguatan ABM *Investment System* sebagai bagian dari kerangka pengambilan keputusan yang transparan, akuntabel, dan berintegritas.

Penilaian dilakukan terhadap 200 emitten dengan kapitalisasi pasar terbesar dan menengah di Bursa Efek Indonesia menggunakan metodologi ASEAN *Corporate Governance Scorecard*, yang mencakup aspek hak pemegang saham, peran pemangku kepentingan, pengungkapan dan transparansi, serta tanggung jawab Dewan. Penghargaan ini semakin memperkuat komitmen ABM untuk terus meningkatkan kualitas tata kelola dan membangun ketahanan perusahaan secara berkelanjutan.

# ABM Receives the IICD Award 2025 for Excellence in GCG Practices

In 2025, ABM was honored with the IICD Award 2025 in the Best Financial Sector (Mid Cap) category at the "16th IICD Corporate Governance Conference and Award: Building Resilience through Good Governance – Thriving in Turbulent Times," organized by the Indonesian Institute for Corporate Directorship (IICD). This award is given to issuers that consistently implement *Good Corporate Governance* (GCG) practices and are not associated with any significant violations conflicting with governance principles.

This achievement reflects recognition of ABM's strengthened governance, including the role of the Corporate Secretary in ensuring the efficacy of the Whistleblowing System (WBS), the implementation of the ISO 37001 Anti-Bribery Management System, and the improvement of the ABM Investment System as part of a transparent, accountable, and integrity-oriented decision-making framework.

The assessment was conducted on 200 issuers with the largest and mid-sized market capitalizations on the Indonesia Stock Exchange, using the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard methodology, which covers shareholder rights, stakeholder roles, disclosure and transparency, and board responsibilities. This award further underscores ABM's commitment to continuously improving governance quality and the development of sustainable corporate resilience.
