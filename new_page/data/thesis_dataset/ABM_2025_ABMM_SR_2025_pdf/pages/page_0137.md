- Keterlibatan pemangku kepentingan dalam konsultasi dan evaluasi bersama
ABM bermitra dengan Kelompok Tani Hutan, BUMDes Tiwingan Sejahtera, Pemerintah Desa Tiwingan Lama, dan pengelola Taman Hutan Raya (Tahura) Sultan Adam, untuk memastikan desain program menjawab kebutuhan lokal.

- Penyediaan pelatihan bagi petani kopi
Berdasarkan hasil konsultasi, ABM menyediakan serangkaian pelatihan mencakup empat bidang utama, berikut dengan 40 petani yang berpartisipasi. Dukungan kepada mereka tidak hanya dalam pengembangan produk tetapi juga peningkatan akses pasar.

This program emphasizes the shared value concept, in which community empowerment strengthens long-term economic resilience and supports the achievement of the SDGs and local economic development.

Below is the Aranio Coffee Program journey, which showcases the involvement and empowerment of local stakeholders throughout the program flow.

- Stakeholder engagement in joint consultations and evaluations
ABM partners with the Forest Farmer Group (Kelompok Tani Hutan), BUMDes Tiwingan Sejahtera, the Tiwingan Lama Village Government, and the management of the Sultan Adam Forest Park (Tahura) to ensure that the program design addresses local needs.

- Provision of training for coffee farmers
Based on the consultation results, ABM provided a series of training programs covering four main areas, with 40 farmers participating. Support for them is provided not only in product development but also in improving market access.
