# Kinerja Lingkungan
Environmental Performance

Untuk mencegah potensi pencemaran akibat tumpahan bahan berbahaya, ABM menerapkan langkah-langkah pengendalian operasional, termasuk penyediaan spill kit di area workshop, TPS B3, dan titik pengisian bahan bakar; penerapan secondary containment pada penyimpanan drum oli dan bahan kimia; serta pelaksanaan SOP dan pelatihan tanggap darurat bagi mekanik dan operator. Di CK, diterapkan pula filtrasi oli untuk mengurangi frekuensi penggantian oli dan menekan potensi tumpahan, serta pemanfaatan kembali solar bekas hasil filtrasi untuk genset. Untuk limbah non-B3, beberapa unit seperti CK DMP dan CK BMB bekerja sama dengan bank sampah guna mencegah penumpukan limbah dan risiko kesehatan lingkungan. [GRI 306-2] [SEOJK F.16] [NDAQ E7]

Terkait fasilitas tailing dan pengelolaan air limpasan, CK tidak memiliki fasilitas tailing karena berperan sebagai kontraktor tambang. Fasilitas pengelolaan air limpasan berada pada pemilik IUP, yaitu TIA, yang mengoperasikan settling pond untuk menampung air limpasan dari area tambang yang mengandung sedimen dan partikel halus. Fasilitas ini berfungsi untuk mengendapkan material padat, menurunkan kekeruhan, serta memastikan kualitas air memenuhi baku mutu sebelum dialirkan lebih lanjut. Pemantauan kualitas air dilakukan secara real-time melalui sistem SPARING yang terhubung dengan server KLHK sesuai Peraturan Menteri LHK No. P.93 Tahun 2018 dan PP No. 22 Tahun 2021, dengan parameter pemantauan meliputi pH, TSS, debit aliran, COD, BOD, serta logam berat (Fe dan Mn). [GRI 306-2] [GRI 306-4] [SEOJK F.16] [NDAQ E7]

Sebagai bagian dari pengendalian risiko lingkungan, TIA menerapkan Zero Discharge Policy, menyediakan spill kit di area kritis, serta melaksanakan simulasi tanggap darurat secara berkala untuk menghadapi potensi tumpahan bahan bakar. Pendekatan ini memastikan integritas fasilitas pengelolaan limbah dan air tetap terjaga serta meminimalkan risiko pencemaran lingkungan. [GRI 306-2] [SEOJK F.16] [NDAQ E7]

At CK, oil filtration processes, such as kidney loop filtration, are employed to extend oil change frequency and decrease the potential for spills. Additionally, used diesel from filtration is repurposed for generators. For non-B3 waste management, various units, including CK DMP and CK BMB, collaborate with waste banks to prevent waste accumulation and environmental health risks. [GRI 306-2] [SEOJK F.16] [NDAQ E7]

Regarding tailings facilities and runoff water management, CK does not have tailings facilities as it operates solely as a mining contractor. The runoff water management facility is owned by the IUP holder, TIA, which operates a settling pond designed to contain runoff water from the mining area containing sediments and fine particles. This facility functions to settle solid materials, reduce turbidity, and ensure that water quality meets the prescribed standards prior to discharge. Water quality monitoring is conducted in real-time via the SPARING system, which is connected to the KLHK server in accordance with Minister of Environment and Forestry Regulation No. P.93 of 2018 and Government Regulation No. 22 of 2021, with monitoring parameters include pH, TSS, flow rate, COD, BOD, and heavy metals (Fe and Mn). [GRI 306-2] [GRI 306-4] [SEOJK F.16] [NDAQ E7]

As part of environmental risk management, TIA implements a Zero Discharge Policy, equips critical areas with spill kits, and regularly conducts emergency response simulations to prepare for potential fuel spills. This approach ensures the integrity of waste and water management facilities is maintained and minimizes the risk of environmental pollution. [GRI 306-2] [SEOJK F.16] [NDAQ E7]
