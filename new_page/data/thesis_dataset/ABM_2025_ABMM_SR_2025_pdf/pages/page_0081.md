# Inisiatif Pengurangan, Pemanfaatan Kembali, dan Daur Ulang Limbah Operasional

ABM menerapkan berbagai inisiatif untuk mengurangi timbulan limbah sekaligus meningkatkan efisiensi operasional melalui prinsip reduce, reuse, dan recycle. Di hampir seluruh site CK, diterapkan filterisasi oli menggunakan sistem kidney loop pada oli hidrolik, transmisi, dan final drive alat berat, sehingga kualitas oli tetap terjaga dan frekuensi penggantian dapat dikurangi. Inisiatif ini berkontribusi langsung pada penurunan volume limbah oli bekas serta penghematan biaya operasional.

Selain itu, solar bekas dari proses penggantian filter bahan bakar dimanfaatkan kembali untuk kebutuhan operasional lain, seperti pengoperasian genset di site, guna mengurangi pemborosan bahan bakar dan timbulan limbah cair. Untuk limbah non-B3, CK BMB dan CK DMP menjalin kerja sama dengan bank sampah, di mana limbah seperti plastik, kertas, dan kardus dikumpulkan dan disalurkan untuk didaur ulang, sehingga mengurangi volume sampah yang dikirim ke tempat pembuangan akhir sekaligus mendukung ekonomi sirkular di masyarakat sekitar.

# Operational Waste Reduction, Reuse, and Recycling Initiative

ABM implements various initiatives to reduce waste generation while simultaneously improving operational efficiency by applying the principles of reduce, reuse, and recycle. At nearly all CK sites, oil filtration is performed using a kidney loop system on hydraulic oil, transmission oil, and final drive of heavy equipment, ensuring oil quality is maintained and replacement frequency is decreased. This initiative directly helps reduce used oil waste volume and operational costs.

Furthermore, used diesel generated from fuel filter replacement processes is reutilized for other operational purposes, such as powering on-site generators, to reduce fuel wastage and liquid waste production. For non-hazardous waste, CK BMB and CK DMP collaborate with waste banks, where waste such as plastics, paper, and cardboard are collected and directed towards recycling, thereby reducing the volume of waste disposed of at final disposal sites and supporting the circular economy within the surrounding community.
