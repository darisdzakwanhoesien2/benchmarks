This report also reflects our strategic sustainability framework through ABM Sustainability Target, which includes focus areas and targets across various aspects, from operations and environment to social and governance. Each focus and target has been aligned with relevant Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Our progress and contributions toward these SDGs are periodically documented within this report, and more comprehensive information regarding the mapping is available on page 36.
