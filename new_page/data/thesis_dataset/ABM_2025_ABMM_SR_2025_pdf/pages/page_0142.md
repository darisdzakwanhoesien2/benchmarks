# Restorasi Ekosistem Pesisir melalui Rehabilitasi Mangrove

Pada tahun 2025, ABM memperkuat komitmen restorasi pesisir melalui program rehabilitasi mangrove yang dilaksanakan bersama CK, CKB, PWP, dan SSB. Dalam rangka memperingati International Biodiversity Day dan dengan tema "Empower the Shoreline, Protect the Future" ABM dan para anak perusahaan memobilisasi karyawan, masyarakat lokal, dan pemangku kepentingan pesisir untuk menanam mangrove di kawasan rawan abrasi. Dengan mengandalkan keahlian teknis dan logistik anak perusahaan, program ini mencakup edukasi, penanaman, dan pemantauan pascatanam untuk memastikan kelangsungan hidup bibit.

Upaya ini diarahkan untuk melindungi pesisir pantai, meningkatkan keanekaragaman hayati, dan menciptakan nilai bersama melalui keterlibatan karyawan dan masyarakat. Pada tahun 2025, program rehabilitasi mangrove dilaksanakan di Mangrove Romokalisari, Surabaya, dengan penanaman 6.000 bibit sebagai bagian dari target total 10.000 mangrove. Selain itu, pada bulan Desember 2025, ABM juga melaksanakan penanaman 10.000 bibit mangrove di kawasan Urban Aquaculture, Desa Mauk, Tangerang.

Secara jangka panjang, program ini diharapkan memberikan manfaat ekologis berkelanjutan, dengan kemampuan mangrove untuk menyerap karbon dan menjaga ekosistem pesisir hingga lebih dari 20 tahun.

# Coastal Ecosystem Restoration through Mangrove Rehabilitation

In 2025, ABM strengthened its coastal restoration commitment through a mangrove rehabilitation program implemented together with CK, CKB, PWP, and SSB. In commemoration of International Biodiversity Day and under the theme "Empower the Shoreline, Protect the Future", ABM and its subsidiaries mobilized employees, local communities, and coastal stakeholders to plant mangroves in abrasion-prone areas. Leveraging the technical and logistical expertise of its subsidiaries, the program includes education, planting, and postplanting monitoring to ensure seedling survival.

These efforts are directed at protecting coastlines, enhancing biodiversity, and creating shared value through employee and community engagement. In 2025, the mangrove rehabilitation program was carried out at Mangrove Romokalisari, Surabaya, with the planting of 6,000 seedlings as part of a total target of 10,000 mangroves. In addition, in December 2025, ABM also planted 10,000 mangrove seedlings in the Urban Aquaculture area, Mauk Village, Tangerang.

In the long term, this program is expected to provide sustainable ecological benefits, with mangroves' ability to absorb carbon and preserve coastal ecosystems for more than 20 years.
