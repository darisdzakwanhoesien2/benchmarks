# Identifikasi Dampak dan Lokasi dengan Risiko Keanekaragaman Hayati

Sejalan dengan karakteristik kegiatan usaha di sektor pertambangan, energi, dan logistik, ABM telah melakukan identifikasi potensi dampak keanekaragaman hayati melalui dokumen Rencana Pengelolaan Lingkungan dan Rencana Pemantauan Lingkungan (RKL-RPL) untuk kegiatan pertambangan serta Upaya Pengelolaan Lingkungan dan Upaya Pemantauan Lingkungan (UKL-UPL) untuk kegiatan non-pertambangan.

Identifikasi dilakukan melalui penilaian rona awal lingkungan (baseline assessment) yang mencakup kondisi habitat, flora, fauna, serta keberadaan spesies dilindungi atau memiliki nilai konservasi penting, dan mencakup seluruh tahapan kegiatan mulai dari persiapan lahan, operasi produksi, hingga pascatambang.

Dari hasil identifikasi tersebut, kegiatan operasional Perusahaan memiliki potensi dampak terhadap keanekaragaman hayati, antara lain penurunan kualitas habitat, gangguan terhadap satwa liar, serta peningkatan tekanan lingkungan akibat aktivitas operasional. Perusahaan mengklasifikasikan dampak berdasarkan tingkat signifikansi, durasi, dan cakupan wilayah untuk membedakan antara dampak aktual dan potensial, serta sebagai dasar penetapan prioritas mitigasi.

Selain aspek lingkungan, ABM melakukan Pemetaan Sosial untuk memahami keterkaitan antara perubahan biodiversitas dengan mata pencaharian, ketersediaan air, dan ketergantungan masyarakat terhadap jasa ekosistem. ABM juga mengidentifikasi apakah wilayah operasional berada di dalam atau berdekatan dengan kawasan sensitif secara ekologis, termasuk area bernilai keanekaragaman hayati tinggi, area dengan integritas ekosistem tertentu, serta wilayah dengan risiko kelangkaan air. Pendekatan ini memastikan pengelolaan operasional tetap memperhatikan perlindungan fungsi ekologis dan keberlanjutan masyarakat sekitar. [GRI 101-4] [GRI 101-5] [GRI 101-6] [SEOJK F.10]

In implementing the Integrated Management Policy, the Company emphasizes the correlation between biodiversity management and efforts to conserve energy, water, and fuel as a unified natural resource management approach. [GRI 101-1] [GRI 101-2][SEOJK F.10]

# Identifying the Impact and Location of Biodiversity Risks

In line with the characteristics of business activities in the mining, energy, and logistics sectors, ABM has identified potential impacts on biodiversity through Environmental Management and Monitoring Plans (RKL-RPL) for mining activities and Environmental Management and Monitoring Efforts (UKL-UPL) for non-mining activities.

The identification was conducted through an initial environmental assessment (baseline assessment) that includes habitat conditions, flora, fauna, as well as the presence of protected species or those of significant conservation value. This assessment encompasses all phases, from land preparation and production operations to post-mining activities.

Based on the results of this identification, the company's operational activities have the potential to impact biodiversity, including habitat quality decline, disturbances to wildlife, and increased environmental pressure due to operational activities. The company classifies impacts based on their significance level, duration, and geographic scope to differentiate between actual and potential impacts, as well as to set mitigation priorities.

Beyond environmental aspects, ABM conducts Social Mapping to understand the relationships among biodiversity changes, livelihoods, water availability, and community dependence on ecosystem services. ABM also identifies whether operational areas are located within or near ecologically sensitive zones, including areas with high biodiversity value, areas with intact ecosystems, and regions at risk of water scarcity. This approach ensures that operational management continues to consider the protection of ecological functions and the sustainability of surrounding communities.
