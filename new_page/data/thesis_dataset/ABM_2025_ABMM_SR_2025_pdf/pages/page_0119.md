# Evaluasi Kinerja, Promosi, dan Perencanaan Sukses (GRI 404-3)

Sebagai bagian dari strategi keberlanjutan kepemimpinan, Grup ABM menempatkan kesiapan suksesor sebagai salah satu KPI Direksi. Pendekatan ini memastikan bahwa pengembangan talentadiakukan secara terintegrasi dan berorientasi jangka panjang.

ABM menerapkan proses Talent Mapping berbasis 9-Box Matrix untuk mengelola potensi dan kinerja karyawan secara objektif. Hasil pemetaan ini ditindaklanjuti melalui Individual Development Plan (IDP) atau Performance Improvement Plan (PIP), yang dirancang dan dimonitor secara berkala guna memastikan setiap karyawan memperoleh pengembangan yang sesuai dengan kebutuhan dan potensinya.

# Performance Evaluation, Promotion, and Succession Planning (GRI 404-3)

As part of its leadership sustainability strategy, the ABM Group places successor readiness (Bench Strength Readiness) as one of the Board of Directors' Key Performance Indicators (KPIs). This approach ensures that talent development is carried out in an integrated manner and is long-term oriented.

ABM implements a Talent Mapping process based on the 9-Box Matrix to manage employee potential and performance objectively. The results of this mapping are followed up through an Individual Development Plan (IDP) or a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP), which are designed and monitored periodically to ensure that each employee receives development that is aligned with their needs and potential.
