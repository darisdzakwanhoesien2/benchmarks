# Peran Aktif Komite ESG untuk Mengawasi Implementasi Keberlanjutan [SEOJK E.1][SEOJK E.5][GRI 2-12][GRI 2-13][GRI 2-14]

The Active Role of the ESG Committee in Overseeing Sustainability Implementation

![img-96.jpeg](img-96.jpeg)

Sebagai bagian dari komitmen ABM terhadap keberlanjutan, Perseroan membentuk Komite ESG yang secara rutin memantau dan mengawasi pengelolaan dampak lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola di seluruh entitas ABM dan anak perusahaan, sejalan dengan kerangka strategis ABM Sustainability Target. Komite ESG berperan dalam memberikan arahan strategis atas implementasi ESG serta memantau progres pelaksanaannya secara berkala. Komite ESG diketuai oleh Komisaris Independen dan didukung oleh Komisaris Independen lainnya serta manajemen senior ABM, dan melaporkan hasil pengawasannya secara berkala kepada Dewan Komisaris melalui ESG Committee Meeting yang diselenggarakan secara kuartal dalam satu tahun pelaporan. Selain itu, Ketua Komite ESG juga menyampaikan laporan dan rekomendasi strategis kepada Direksi (BOD) guna memastikan integrasi aspek ESG dalam pengambilan keputusan operasional dan strategis Perusahaan.

In alignment with ABM's commitment to sustainability, the Company has established an ESG Committee tasked with regularly monitoring and overseeing the management of environmental, social, and governance impacts across all ABM entities and subsidiaries, consistent with the strategic framework of the ABM Sustainability Target. The ESG Committee is responsible for providing strategic guidance on ESG implementation and periodically monitoring its progress. The ESG Committee is chaired by an Independent Commissioner and supported by other Independent Commissioners as well as senior management of ABM, and periodically reports the results of its oversight to the Board of Commissioners through quarterly ESG Committee Meetings held during the reporting year. It reports its oversight findings regularly to the Board of Commissioners. Furthermore, the Chair of the ESG Committee also submits reports and strategic recommendations to the Board of Directors (BOD) to ensure the integration of ESG aspects into the Company's operational and strategic decision-making.
