# Dampak Program Kopi Aranio

[SEOJK F.23]

Berdasarkan SROI yang dilakukan oleh Center for Entrepreneurship, Change, and Third Sector (CECT) Trisakti, petani mengalami peningkatan pengetahuan, akses pasar yang lebih mudah melalui BUMDes, dan peningkatan pendapatan. Program ini juga mendukung terbentuknya rantai nilai kopi desa yang baru, menghasilkan peningkatan produk olahan dan sumber pendapatan baru bagi BUMDes.

Hasil SROI menunjukkan bahwa setiap Rp 1 yang diinvestasikan menghasilkan Rp 3,38 nilai sosial selama 2023-2024. Dampak signifikan, sebesar 99,3%, diterima oleh petani melalui peningkatan pengetahuan, pendapatan, dan stabilitas pasar, serta berkontribusi pada penguatan lembaga desa dan pengelolaan berkelanjutan kawasan Tahura. Seiring dengan berkembangnya komoditas kopi sebagai sumber ekonomi desa, terdapat potensi peningkatan ketergantungan pada komoditas tertentu, sehingga ABM juga mendorong penguatan kapasitas masyarakat dan pengembangan variasi produk agar manfaat ekonomi tetap berkelanjutan.

# Impact of the Aranio Coffee Program

[SEOJK F.23]

Based on the SROI conducted by the Center for Entrepreneurship, Change, and Third Sector (CECT) Trisakti, farmers experienced increased knowledge, easier market access through BUMDes, and increased income. This program also supported the establishment of a new village coffee value chain, resulting in an increase in processed products and new income sources for BUMDes.

The SROI results show that every Rp 1 invested generated Rp 3.38 in social value during 2023-2024. Significant impact, amounting to 99.3%, was received by farmers through increased knowledge, income, and market stability, and also contributed to strengthening village institutions and the sustainable management of the Tahura area. As coffee becomes an increasingly vital economic resource for the village, there is a risk of growing dependence on a single commodity; therefore, ABM also encourages community capacity building and the development of product diversification to ensure that economic benefits remain sustainable.

Dampak signifikan terhadap petani yang teridentifikasi mencakup:

- Peningkatan pendapatan petani dari penjualan kopi
- Pengurangan biaya transportasi karena akses pasar desa
- Peluang kerja baru (misalnya, terbukti dari 5 perempuan pekerja panen)
- Pertumbuhan agroindustri lokal melalui unit pengolahan BUMDes
- Terbentuknya rantai nilai kopi desa sebagai pendorong ekonomi lokal

Significant impacts on farmers that were identified include:

- Increased farmer income from coffee sales
- Reduced transportation costs due to village market access
- New job opportunities (for example, as evidenced by 5 women harvest workers)
- Growth of the local agro-industry through the BUMDes processing unit
- The establishment of a village coffee value chain as a driver of the local economy
