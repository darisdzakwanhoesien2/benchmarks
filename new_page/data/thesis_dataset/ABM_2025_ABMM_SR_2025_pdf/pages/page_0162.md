- Fixed Pay dan Variable Pay
Remunerasi mencakup:
- Fixed pay: gaji pokok dan tunjangan tetap.
- Variable pay: insentif jangka pendek dan jangka panjang.

Insentif jangka pendek diberikan berdasarkan hasil evaluasi kinerja tahunan. Insentif jangka panjang diberikan apabila Perusahaan mencapai target pertumbuhan profitabilitas serta capaian keberlanjutan, dan mengikuti siklus penilaian tiga tahunan sesuai kebijakan Perusahaan.

- Bonus dan tunjangan lainnya:
- Sign-on bonus atau recruitment incentive: ABM tidak memberikan sign-on bonus bagi eksekutif, kecuali dalam kondisi khusus sesuai keputusan KNR.
- Termination payments: Pembayaran terminasi mengikuti ketentuan perundang-undangan serta kontrak kerja eksekutif.
- Clawback: ABM tidak menerapkan mekanisme clawback pada insentif jangka panjang.
- Retirement benefits: menyediakan program pensiun opsional bagi eksekutif yang dirancang untuk mendukung transisi masa pensiun.

# Proses Penentuan Remunerasi
[GRI 2-20]

ABM memiliki ketentuan remunerasi bagi Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, dan karyawan sebagaimana tertuang dalam Risalah Komite Remunerasi Nomor 006/ABM-KNR/X/2020 tanggal 23 Oktober 2020. Penentuan remunerasi, termasuk untuk Direktur Utama, memperhatikan aspek-aspek berikut:

1. Key Performance Indicators (KPI) masing-masing anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi;
2. Kinerja Perseroan;
3. Ukuran bisnis; dan
4. Benchmark remunerasi dengan pasar tenaga kerja.

Sejak 2021, pencapaian ABM dalam bidang Environment, Social, and Governance (ESG) menjadi bagian dari KPI Direktur Utama dan diturunkan secara terstruktur menjadi KPI setiap Direktur serta departemen terkait di seluruh entitas ABM. Capaian KPI ESG ini turut menentukan besaran remunerasi yang diterima Direksi di seluruh Grup.

- Fixed Pay and Variable Pay
Remuneration includes:
- Fixed pay: base salary and fixed allowances.
- Variable pay: short-term and long-term incentives.

Short-term incentives are awarded based on annual performance evaluations. Long-term incentives are granted if the Company achieves profitability growth targets and sustainability goals, following a three-year assessment cycle in accordance with the Company's policies.

- Additional bonuses and allowances:
- Sign-on bonus or recruitment incentive: ABM does not offer sign-on bonuses to executives, except under special circumstances as determined by KNR.
- Termination payments: Termination payments adhere to applicable laws and the executive's employment contract.
- Clawback: ABM does not implement a clawback mechanism for long-term incentives.
- Retirement benefits: Offers optional retirement programs for executives intended to facilitate the transition into retirement.

# Remuneration Determination Process
[GRI 2-20]

ABM has remuneration policies for the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, and employees as outlined in the Remuneration Committee Minutes No. 006/ABM-KNR/X/2020 dated 23 October 2020. The determination of remuneration, including for the President Director, considers the following aspects:

1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for each member of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors;
2. The company's performance;
3. Business size; and
4. Remuneration benchmarks with the labor market.

Since 2021, ABM's achievements in the Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) field have become part of the CEO's KPI and are systematically broken down into KPIs for each Director and related departments across all ABM entities. The achievement of these ESG KPIs also determines the amount of remuneration received by the Board of Directors throughout the Group.
