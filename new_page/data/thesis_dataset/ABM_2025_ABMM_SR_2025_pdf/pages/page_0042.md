- Langkah 4: Penyusunan Matriks Double Materiality

Hasil penilaian sumbu Y dan sumbu X kemudian dipetakan ke dalam matriks double materiality untuk menggambarkan tingkat kepentingan setiap topik ESG dari perspektif pemangku kepentingan dan Perusahaan.

- Langkah 5: Penetapan Topik Material

Berdasarkan matriks materialitas, topik ESG diprioritaskan sebagai fokus utama dalam strategi keberlanjutan, pengelolaan risiko, dan pengungkapan Sustainability Report ABM Investama.

- Step 4: Preparation of the Double Materiality Matrix

The results of the Y-axis and X-axis assessments are then plotted into a double materiality matrix to illustrate the level of importance of each ESG topic from the perspectives of stakeholders and the Company.

- Step 5: Determination of Material Topics

Based on the materiality matrix, ESG topics are prioritized as the main focus in ABM Investama's sustainability strategy, risk management, and Sustainability Report disclosures.
