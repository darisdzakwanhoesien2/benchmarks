# Penguatan Jiwa Volunteerism untuk Karyawan ABM

Sebagai bagian dari komitmen terhadap pembangunan sosial dan penguatan keterlibatan karyawan, ABM mengembangkan program Employee Investment on Social melalui inisiatif volunteer yang dikembangkan menjadi sebuah program terstruktur. Program ini mendorong kontribusi aktif karyawan dalam bentuk waktu, tenaga, dan keahlian untuk mendukung pencapaian tujuan CSR perusahaan, sekaligus memperkuat engagement, kepedulian sosial, serta reputasi Perusahaan.

Salah satu inisiatif utama adalah ABM Food Always In The House (FAITH), program volunteer internal yang memberikan hak cuti volunteer selama satu hari (8 jam) per karyawan setiap tahun, yang dilaksanakan dalam beberapa batch. Program ini dirancang untuk melibatkan seluruh jenjang karyawan, dari staf hingga direksi, dalam berbagai kegiatan sosial yang berdampak langsung bagi masyarakat.

Selain itu, anak perusahaan Grup ABM secara aktif berkontribusi dalam bidang pendidikan melalui kegiatan seperti pengajaran di sekolah kejuruan, kunjungan industri bagi mahasiswa, serta program peningkatan kualitas sumber daya manusia dan kewirausahaan masyarakat. Melalui pendekatan ini, ABM tidak hanya memberikan manfaat sosial yang berkelanjutan, tetapi juga menanamkan nilai kepedulian dan kebersamaan sebagai bagian dari budaya perusahaan.

# Strengthening the Spirit of Volunteerism for ABM Employees

As part of its commitment to social development and strengthening employee engagement, ABM developed the Employee Investment on Social program through volunteer initiatives that have been developed into a structured program. This program encourages employees' active contributions in the form of time, effort, and expertise to support the achievement of the Company's CSR objectives, while also strengthening engagement, social awareness, and the Company's reputation.

One of the main initiatives is ABM FAITH (Food Always in the House), an internal volunteer program that grants one day (8 hours) of volunteer leave entitlement per employee each year, implemented in several batches. This program is designed to involve all levels of employees, from staff to the Board of Directors, in various social activities that have direct impacts on communities.

In addition, ABM Group's subsidiaries actively contribute in the education sector through activities such as teaching at vocational schools, industry visits for university students, and programs to improve the quality of human resources and community entrepreneurship. Through this approach, ABM not only delivers sustainable social benefits, but also instills the values of care and togetherness as part of the Company's culture.
