# Hak Pemegang Saham

Pemegang saham atau kelompok pemegang saham yang memiliki minimal 10% saham berhak mengajukan nominasi anggota Dewan Komisaris atau Direksi kepada KNR. Setiap nominasi diproses sesuai dengan kebijakan dan prosedur KNR yang berlaku.

# Dokumentasi dan Transparansi

Proses nominasi dari tingkat Komisaris Utama hingga General Manager diatur dalam Kebijakan Board &amp; Management dan dapat diakses melalui situs web Perusahaan.

# Proses Nominasi dengan Orientasi ESG

- Proses nominasi dilakukan menggunakan kriteria evaluasi yang terdokumentasi, antara lain integritas, kompetensi strategis, rekam jejak, serta kesesuaian dengan nilai dan prinsip tata kelola Perusahaan.
- KNR mempertimbangkan aspek keberagaman, termasuk pengalaman industri, gender, dan latar belakang profesional, dalam proses seleksi untuk memastikan keberagaman perspektif dalam pengambilan keputusan.
- KNR melakukan penilaian secara independen untuk memastikan tidak terdapat konflik kepentingan dalam usulan calon, serta memastikan seluruh kandidat menjalani uji kelayakan dan kepatutan sesuai dengan peraturan OJK.

# Shareholder Rights

Shareholders or groups of shareholders holding at least 10% of the shares possess the right to submit nominations for members of the Board of Commissioners or the Board of Directors to KNR. Each nomination is processed in accordance with the applicable KNR policies and procedures.

# Documentation and Transparency

The nomination process from the position of Main Commissioner to that of General Manager is regulated in the Board &amp; Management Policy and is accessible through the Company's official website.

# Nomination Process with ESG Orientation

- The nomination process is conducted utilizing documented evaluation criteria, which include integrity, strategic competence, track record, and alignment with the company's values and governance principles.
- KNR takes into account aspects of diversity, including industry experience, gender, and professional background, during the selection process to ensure a variety of perspectives in decision-making.
- KNR performs an independent assessment to confirm the absence of conflicts of interest among the proposed candidates and ensures that all candidates undergo the fit and proper test in accordance with OJK regulations.

# Critical Communications to the Highest Governance Body [GRI 2-16]

ABM has a clear, structured mechanism to ensure that any material incidents or information pertaining to significant social and environmental impacts are promptly communicated to authorized parties. Any material and/or critical incidents across ABM Group's units must be reported immediately to the Board of Directors (BOD) and the Board of Commissioners (BOC) in accordance with the criteria for critical incidents established in the risk management framework and updated periodically. These criteria are classified into three levels: critical incidents, serious impact incidents, and very serious impact incidents, based on their implications for operational, financial, reputational, social, and environmental aspects.
