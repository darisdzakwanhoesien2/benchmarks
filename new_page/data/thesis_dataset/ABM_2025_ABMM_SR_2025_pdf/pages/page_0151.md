Governance and Economy

- Independensi Dewan Direksi dan Komisaris

ABM menekankan pengawasan yang objektif dan pencegahan benturan kepentingan melalui keberadaan komisaris independen, pemisahan peran Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris, serta pembatasan hubungan keluarga atau kepemilikan yang dapat memengaruhi integritas pengambilan keputusan.

- Remunerasi yang Berintegritas

ABM mengintegrasikan prinsip integritas dalam keputusan remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Dewan Direksi yang disesuaikan dengan peran, kinerja, dan tanggung jawab masing-masing.

- Peran Aktif Komite ESG untuk Mengawasi Implementasi Keberlanjutan

Komite ESG yang dibentuk ABM memastikan pemantauan dan pelaporan dampak ESG dilakukan secara rutin dan terukur, baik terhadap ABM maupun anak perusahaan.

B. Di Seluruh Tingkat Manajemen

- Implementasi Code of Ethics and Conduct (COEC)

ABM menetapkan COEC yang mengatur nilai-nilai, prinsip, dan perilaku yang harus dijalankan oleh seluruh karyawan dan jajaran direksi ABM.

- Kebijakan Anti-Suap dan Korupsi

ABM memastikan bahwa setiap karyawan dan pemasok mematuhi Kebijakan Anti-Suap dan Kebijakan Anti-Korupsi untuk mewujudkan praktik bisnis yang bersih berintegritas, serta bebas dari korupsi dan gratifikasi.

Pengawasan atas penerapan governansi ABM dilakukan oleh Komite Tata Kelola Perusahaan dan diperkuat melalui audit rutin untuk memastikan seluruh kebijakan governansi dijalankan secara konsisten dan efektif.

- Independence of the Board of Directors and Commissioners

ABM emphasizes objective oversight and conflict of interest prevention by having independent commissioners, separating roles between the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners, and restricting family or ownership relationships that could influence decision-making integrity.

- Remuneration with Integrity

ABM integrates the principle of integrity into remuneration decisions for the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors, tailored to each member's role, performance, and responsibilities

- Active Role of the ESG Committee in Overseeing Sustainability Implementation

The ESG Committee established by ABM ensures that ESG impacts are monitored and reported regularly and measurably, for both ABM and its subsidiaries.

B. Across All Management Levels

- Implementation of the Code of Ethics and Conduct (COEC)

ABM establishes a COEC that governs the values, principles, and behaviors expected from all employees and management across the organization.

- Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption Policies

ABM ensures that every employee and supplier complies with the Anti-Bribery Policy and Anti-Corruption Policy to uphold clean, integrity-driven business practices free from corruption and gratuities.

The oversight of ABM governance implementation is performed by the Corporate Governance Committee and is strengthened through routine audits to ensure that all governance policies are implemented consistently and effectively.
