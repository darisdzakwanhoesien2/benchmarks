# Membekali Karyawan Menuju Masa Purnabakti yang Siap, Mandiri, dan Bermakna

Sebagai bagian dari komitmen ABM dalam pengembangan SDM dan kesejahteraan karyawan, ABM menyelenggarakan program pensiun untuk membekali karyawan yang mendekati masa purnabakti agar siap secara psikologis, mental, dan perencanaan masa depan. Program ini diikuti oleh 21 peserta dan dirancang untuk membantu peserta memasuki masa pensiun dengan lebih tenang, percaya diri, serta memiliki arah tujuan yang jelas dalam kehidupan setelah purna tugas.

Program disusun berbasis prinsip adult learning dengan pendekatan pembelajaran yang berpusat pada peserta melalui kombinasi ceramah, diskusi, tanya jawab, studi kasus, role play, simulasi, serta perspektif aplikasi praktis. Materi pelatihan mencakup perubahan mental, psikologi dalam mengelola perubahan, membangun pemikiran positif, menemukan kekuatan diri, perubahan paradigma dalam menghadapi second career agar tetap produktif dan bermakna, mengatasi post power syndrome, hingga pengenalan kewirausahaan seperti memulai bisnis kuliner dan memahami skema biaya usaha. Seluruh modul tersebut mendukung peserta dalam memperoleh gambaran komprehensif mengenai transformasi dari karyawan menjadi self-employment, memahami pengelolaan dana pensiun, berbagai pilihan investasi, serta pengelolaan keuangan keluarga yang aman dan efektif.

Untuk memastikan kualitas dan efektivitas program, ABM menerapkan pendekatan evaluasi pelatihan berbasis Kirkpatrick's Evaluation Model pada tingkat reaksi dan pembelajaran melalui kuesioner evaluasi modul, penilaian fasilitator, serta pre-test dan post-test. Pendekatan ini membantu memastikan bahwa program memberikan manfaat nyata bagi peserta, sekaligus memperkuat upaya ABM dalam membangun kapabilitas, kesiapan transisi kerja, dan kesejahteraan jangka panjang karyawan secara berkelanjutan.

# Equipping Employees Toward a Ready, Independent, and Meaningful Retirement

As part of ABM's commitment to Human Resource Development and Employee Welfare, ABM organized a Retirement Program to equip employees approaching retirement so they are psychologically and mentally prepared and able to plan for the future. This program was attended by 21 participants and is designed to help participants enter retirement more calmly, confidently, and with clear direction and purpose in life after completing their service.

The program was developed based on adult learning principles with a participant-centered learning approach through a combination of lectures, discussions, question-and-answer sessions, case studies, role play, simulations, and a practical application perspective. Training materials include mental switching, psychology in managing change, building a positive mindset, discovering personal strengths (self discovery), paradigm shifts in facing a second career to remain productive and meaningful, overcoming post power syndrome, and introductions to entrepreneurship such as starting a culinary business and understanding business cost schemes. All of these modules support participants in obtaining a comprehensive picture of the transformation from employee to self-employment, understanding the management of retirement funds, various investment options, as well as safe and effective family financial management.

To ensure the quality and effectiveness of the program, ABM applied a training evaluation approach based on Kirkpatrick's Evaluation Model at the reaction and learning levels through module evaluation questionnaires, facilitator assessments, as well as pre-tests and post-tests. This approach helps ensure that the program delivers tangible benefits to participants, while also strengthening ABM's efforts to build capability, readiness for work transition, and long-term employee welfare on a sustainable basis.
