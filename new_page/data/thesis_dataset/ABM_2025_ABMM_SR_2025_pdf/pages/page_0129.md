Kinerja Sosial Social Performance

Sejalan dengan pendekatan manajemen yang komprehensif dalam perlindungan hak asasi manusia, ABM menerapkan prinsip kesetaraan dan nondiskriminasi di seluruh wilayah operasional. Perusahaan menyediakan mekanisme pelaporan atas dugaan tindakan diskriminatif dan pelecehan seksual melalui Whistleblowing System (WBS) yang menjamin kerahasiaan pelapor serta penanganan sesuai ketentuan internal dan peraturan yang berlaku. Kebijakan terkait etika di tempat kerja, pencegahan diskriminasi, dan pelecehan seksual diatur dalam Peraturan Perusahaan, ditinjau secara berkala, dan dikelola oleh fungsi Corporate Human Capital. Sepanjang tahun 2025, ABM tidak menerima laporan maupun mencatat adanya insiden diskriminasi atau pelecehan seksual, serta secara konsisten memastikan keselarasan kebijakan dengan regulasi nasional dan standar internasional, termasuk International Labour Organization (ILO).

## Perundingan Kolektif [GRI 2-30] [GRI 402-1] [GRI 407-1] [NDAQ G4]

ABM menjamin dan melindungi kebebasan berserikat sebagai bagian dari komitmen perusahaan terhadap hak asasi manusia dan hubungan industrial yang harmonis. Perusahaan secara terbuka memfasilitasi dialog dengan karyawan melalui berbagai kanal komunikasi, seperti coffee morning, morning briefing, dan town hall, sehingga karyawan dapat menyampaikan pendapat dan aspirasi dengan bebas. Melalui mekanisme komunikasi ini, ABM dapat mengelola potensi risiko terkait kebebasan berserikat dan perundingan kolektif. Perusahaan memastikan seluruh karyawan memiliki hak untuk bergabung dan berpartisipasi dalam kegiatan serikat pekerja.

Komitmen terhadap kebebasan berserikat dan perundingan kolektif juga diwujudkan melalui mekanisme penyusunan dan pelaksanaan Perjanjian Kerja Bersama (PKB) maupun Peraturan Perusahaan (PP), yang disusun dan dirundingkan secara partisipatif antara manajemen dan perwakilan pekerja. Pendekatan ini sejalan dengan Konvensi ILO No. 98 Tahun 1949 tentang Hak Berorganisasi dan Perundingan Bersama yang telah diratifikasi melalui Undang-Undang No. 18 Tahun 1956, guna memberikan kejelasan mengenai hak dan kewajiban antara pengusaha dan pekerja serta tata tertib di lingkungan kerja.

In line with a comprehensive management approach to protecting human rights, ABM applies the principles of equality and non-discrimination across all operational areas. The Company provides a reporting mechanism for alleged discriminatory acts and sexual harassment through the Whistleblowing System (WBS), which ensures reporter confidentiality and handling in accordance with internal provisions and applicable regulations. Policies related to workplace ethics, discrimination prevention, and sexual harassment are regulated in the Company Regulation, reviewed periodically, and managed by the Corporate Human Capital function. Throughout 2025, ABM did not receive any reports or record any incidents of discrimination or sexual harassment, and consistently ensured policy alignment with national regulations and international standards, including the International Labour Organization (ILO).

## Collective Bargaining [GRI 2-30] [GRI 402-1] [GRI 407-1] [NDAQ Q G4]

ABM guarantees and protects freedom of association as part of the Company's commitment to human rights and harmonious industrial relations. The Company openly facilitates dialogue with employees through various communication channels, such as coffee morning, morning briefings, and town halls, so that employees can freely express opinions and aspirations. Through these communication mechanisms, ABM can manage potential risks related to freedom of association and collective bargaining. The Company ensures that all employees have the right to join and participate in labor union activities.

The commitment to freedom of association and collective bargaining is also realized through the mechanism for the preparation and implementation of a Collective Labor Agreement (PKB) and/or Company Regulation (PP), which are prepared and negotiated in a participatory manner between management and employee representatives. This approach is aligned with ILO Convention No. 98 of 1949 concerning the Right to Organize and Collective Bargaining, which has been ratified through Law No. 18 of 1956, to provide clarity regarding the rights and obligations between employers and workers as well as rules and order in the workplace.
