# Identifikasi Bahaya, Penilaian Risiko, dan Investigasi Insiden [GRI 403-2]

Untuk mewujudkan lingkungan kerja yang sehat dan aman, ABM senantiasa melakukan identifikasi bahaya, penilaian risiko, dan investigasi insiden yang merujuk pada regulasi dan standar di atas. Proses identifikasi bahaya dan risiko, serta investigasi insiden telah distrukturkan dalam langkah-langkah berikut ini:

- Persiapan
- Pembentukan tim manajemen risiko K3
- Penugasan dan penentuan proses kerja tim manajemen risiko K3
- Pengumpulan informasi yang relevan

- HIRADC
- Identifikasi bahaya
- Penilaian risiko
- Penetapan upaya pengendalian risiko
- Penetapan tujuan, sasaran, dan program

- Pelaksanaan dan Peninjauan
- Persetujuan manajemen
- Komunikasi dan konsultasi
- Penerapan upaya pengendalian risiko
- Pemantauan dan pemeriksaan rutin
- Peninjauan HIRADC secara berkala

- Pencatatan, penerapan, peninjauan HIRADC, pencapaian kinerja K3, identifikasi bahaya, insiden, dan pelaporan merujuk pada regulasi yang berlaku di Indonesia untuk industri, yaitu:
- Undang-Undang No. 1 Tahun 1970 tentang Keselamatan Kerja
- Peraturan Pemerintah No. 50 Tahun 2012 tentang Penerapan Sistem Manajemen Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja
- Peraturan Menteri ESDM No. 26 Tahun 2018 tentang Pelaksanaan Kaidah Pertambangan yang Baik
- Peraturan Menteri Tenaga Kerja No. 187 Tahun 1999 tentang Pengendalian Bahan Kimia Berbahaya di Tempat Kerja
- Peraturan Menteri Tenaga Kerja No. 03 Tahun 1998 tentang Tata Cara Pelaporan dan Pemeriksaan Kecelakaan
- Peraturan Menteri Tenaga Kerja No. 15 Tahun 2008 tentang P3K di Tempat Kerja
- Regulasi spesifik lainnya, seperti regulasi dari Keputusan Dirjen Minerba, peraturan Kesehatan Lingkungan, serta Standar Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja Perkantoran.

# Hazard Identification, Risk Assessment, and Incident Investigation [GRI 403-2]

To realize a healthy and safe work environment, ABM continuously conducts hazard identification, risk assessment, and incident investigations referring to the regulations and standards above. The hazard and risk identification process, as well as incident investigations, has been structured into the following steps:

- Preparation
- Establishment of an OHS risk management team
- Assignment and determination of work processes for the OHS risk management team
- Collection of relevant information

- HIRADC
- Hazard identification
- Risk assessment
- Determination of risk control measures
- Determination of objectives, targets, and programs

- Implementation and Review
- Management approval
- Communication and consultation
- Implementation of risk control measures
- Routine monitoring and inspections
- Periodic review of HIRADC

- Recording, implementation, and review of HIRADC, OHS performance achievement, hazard identification, incidents, and reporting refer to the applicable regulations in Indonesia for the industry, namely:
- Law No. 1 of 1970 on Occupational Safety
- Government Regulation No. 50 of 2012 on the Implementation of an Occupational Safety and Health Management System
- Minister of Energy and Mineral Resources Regulation (Permen ESDM) No. 26 of 2018 on the Implementation of Good Mining Practices
- Minister of Manpower Regulation No. 187 of 1999 on the Control of Hazardous Chemicals in the Workplace
- Minister of Manpower Regulation No. 03 of 1998 on Procedures for Reporting and Inspecting Accidents
- Minister of Manpower Regulation No. 15 of 2008 on First Aid in the Workplace
- Other specific regulations, such as regulations from Directorate General of Mineral and Coal Decrees, environmental health regulations, and Office Occupational Safety and Health Standards.
