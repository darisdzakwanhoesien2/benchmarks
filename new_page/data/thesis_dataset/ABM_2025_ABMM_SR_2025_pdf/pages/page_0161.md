# Remunerasi yang Berintegritas [GRI 2-19][GRI 2-20][GRI 2-21][NDAQ G3][NDAQ S1]

## Remuneration with Integrity

ABM menetapkan kebijakan remunerasi bagi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi melalui Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi (KNR). Komite ini memastikan kebijakan remunerasi disusun secara transparan, akuntabel, serta selaras dengan ketentuan ketenagakerjaan dan praktik terbaik industri. Penetapan remunerasi dilakukan melalui evaluasi berbasis kinerja, konsultasi dengan pemangku kepentingan internal, serta analisis kesesuaian dengan strategi jangka panjang Perusahaan.

Struktur remunerasi di ABM disusun berdasarkan job band, person grade, serta pencapaian kinerja keberlanjutan yang meliputi indikator ekonomi, lingkungan, dan sosial yang telah terintegrasi dalam sistem penilaian kinerja eksekutif. Integrasi aspek keberlanjutan ini memastikan bahwa kompensasi tidak hanya mencerminkan pencapaian finansial, tetapi juga kontribusi terhadap pengelolaan dampak organisasi dan implementasi strategi ESG.

## Komponen Remunerasi

Komponen remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi terdiri dari:

- Insentif &amp; Anugerah Kinerja (bonus)
- Tunjangan transportasi dan perjalanan dinas
- Cuti tahunan dan tunjangan perjalanan cuti
- Pengobatan, perawatan kesehatan, pemeriksaan medis, dan asuransi jiwa
- Tunjangan pendidikan anak
- Tunjangan utilitas
- Keanggotaan klub
- Benefit lainnya sesuai kebijakan Perusahaan

ABM establishes remuneration policies for members of the Board of Commissioners and Directors via the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NRC). This committee ensures that the remuneration policies are developed in a transparent, accountable manner, and in compliance with labor regulations and industry standards. The determination of remuneration is conducted through performance-based evaluations, consultations with internal stakeholders, and analyses to ensure alignment with the Company's long-term strategy.

The remuneration structure at ABM is structured according to job bands, person grades, and achievement of sustainability performance, which includes economic, environmental, and social indicators integrated into the executive performance assessment system. The integration of sustainability aspects ensures that compensation not only mirrors financial achievements but also acknowledges contributions to managing organizational impacts and implementing ESG strategies.

## Remuneration Components

The remuneration components for the Board of Commissioners and Directors consist of:

- Performance Incentives &amp; Awards (bonuses)
- Transportation and official travel allowances
- Annual leave and holiday travel allowances
- Medical treatment, healthcare, medical examinations, and life insurance
- Child education allowances
- Utility allowances
- Club memberships
- Other benefits in accordance with Company policies
