# Kebijakan Anti-Suap dan Anti-Korupsi [GRI 205-1] [GRI 2-13] [GRI 2-14]

Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption Policy

Grup ABM mengimplementasikan kebijakan dan prosedur anti-suap dan anti-korupsi yang mengacu pada peraturan perundang-undangan nasional, termasuk Undang-Undang No. 31 Tahun 1999 dan Undang-Undang No. 20 Tahun 2001, serta pedoman yang diterbitkan oleh Komisi Pemberantasan Korupsi (KPK). Kebijakan tersebut dievaluasi dan diperbarui secara berkala oleh Departemen Internal Audit untuk memastikan efektivitas dan relevansinya, serta berlaku sebagai kerangka terpadu bagi seluruh entitas Grup ABM.

Perusahaan telah mengidentifikasi area operasional dengan tingkat risiko korupsi yang signifikan, antara lain pengadaan barang dan jasa, pembebasan lahan, pengurusan perizinan, pengelolaan kas dan setaranya, pengelolaan aset dan persediaan, proses klaim, penerimaan gratifikasi, serta aktivitas lain yang berpotensi menimbulkan benturan kepentingan. Risiko-risiko tersebut dapat berdampak pada integritas dan reputasi Perusahaan, sehingga ABM menerapkan pengendalian dan tata kelola yang ketat di seluruh proses bisnis. [GRI 205-1] [POJK E.1]

Pada tahun 2025, seluruh unit bisnis ABM baik kantor pusat maupun anak perusahaan telah menjalani penilaian risiko korupsi, sehingga 100% operasi Perseroan tercakup dalam proses evaluasi risiko. Penilaian dilakukan melalui risk assessment tahunan yang terintegrasi dalam kerangka Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) dan diperkuat dengan pelaksanaan audit internal berbasis risiko secara berkala.

Untuk memitigasi risiko yang teridentifikasi, ABM secara konsisten mengimplementasikan kebijakan anti-suap dan anti-korupsi melalui evaluasi rutin atas risiko korupsi di seluruh proses bisnis. Risiko tersebut diintegrasikan ke dalam Enterprise Risk Management System Dashboard dan dipantau serta dianalisis setiap bulan oleh Departemen Enterprise Risk Management, dengan penguatan pengawasan melalui audit internal yang dilakukan setiap tiga bulan oleh Tim Internal Audit. [GRI 205-1]

Sistem pengendalian internal ABM mengacu pada kerangka Committee on Sponsoring Organization of the Treadway Commission (COSO) yang terdiri dari lima komponen utama dan berlaku secara internasional, guna memastikan kegiatan operasional berjalan efektif, efisien, andal, aman, serta patuh terhadap peraturan yang berlaku. ABM Group implements anti-bribery and anti-corruption policies and procedures in accordance with national laws and regulations, including Law No. 31 of 1999 and Law No. 20 of 2001, as well as guidelines issued by the Corruption Eradication Commission (KPK). These policies are regularly evaluated and updated by the Internal Audit Department to ensure their effectiveness and continued relevance, serving as an integrated framework for all ABM Group entities.

The Company has identified operational areas with significant corruption risks, including procurement of goods and services, land acquisition, licensing procedures, cash management and equivalents, asset and inventory management, claims processing, acceptance of gratifications, and other activities that may give rise to conflicts of interest. Recognizing the potential impact of these risks on the Company's integrity and reputation, ABM implements stringent controls and governance throughout all business processes. [GRI 205-1] [POJK E.1]

By 2025, all ABM business units, including the head office and subsidiaries, have conducted comprehensive corruption risk assessments, ensuring that 100% of the company's operations are encompassed within the risk evaluation process. These assessments are conducted through an annual risk assessment integrated into the Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) framework and reinforced by periodic risk-based internal audits

To address and mitigate identified risks, ABM persistently enforces anti-bribery and anti-corruption policies through routine evaluations of corruption risks across all business processes. These risks are integrated into the Enterprise Risk Management System Dashboard and monitored and analyzed on a monthly basis by the Enterprise Risk Management Department. Oversight is reinforced through internal audits conducted quarterly by the Internal Audit Team. [GRI 205-1]

ABM's internal control system is based on the Committee of Sponsoring Organizations of the Treadway Commission (COSO) framework, which comprises five main components and is internationally acknowledged.
