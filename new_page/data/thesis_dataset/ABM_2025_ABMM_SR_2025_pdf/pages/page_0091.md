## Pengukuran Efektivitas Pelatihan K3

[GRI 404-1] [OJK F.22]

Pada tahun 2025, ABM melakukan pengukuran efektivitas program pelatihan sebagai bagian dari upaya memastikan bahwa pengembangan kompetensi karyawan memberikan dampak nyata terhadap peningkatan keselamatan kerja. Pengukuran ini difokuskan pada pelatihan Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3) yang dilaksanakan di seluruh area operasional, dengan menggunakan pendekatan tiga tingkat evaluasi, yaitu Level 1 evaluasi kembali, Level 2 pasca-uji, dan Level 3 penilaian perilaku. Hasil evaluasi Level 1 menunjukkan persepsi positif peserta terhadap kualitas pelatihan, dengan penilaian terhadap kesesuaian dan kejelasan materi sebesar 34%, kompetensi fasilitator 33%, serta kepuasan terhadap fasilitas 33%.

Sepanjang tahun 2025, ABM telah menyelenggarakan 3.151 pelaksanaan pelatihan K3 dengan total 11.660 jam pembelajaran melalui berbagai metode pembelajaran, termasuk pembelajaran daring, pembelajaran hibrida, pelatihan tatap muka, serta pembelajaran virtual, baik melalui program internal maupun learning implementation yang bekerja sama dengan lembaga eksternal. Program pelatihan ini dirancang untuk memastikan peningkatan kompetensi keselamatan kerja secara berkelanjutan serta mendukung penerapan standar keselamatan di seluruh lini operasional Perusahaan.

Efektivitas pembelajaran juga tercermin pada hasil Level 2, yang menunjukkan peningkatan pemahaman peserta dengan nilai rata-rata pre-test sebesar 63,2 meningkat menjadi 89,9 pada post-test, menghasilkan learning gain sebesar 29,7%. Sementara itu, evaluasi Level 3 menunjukkan bahwa 31,07% pelatihan dinilai sangat efektif, 59,89% efektif, dan 9,04% cukup efektif, tanpa adanya kategori kurang efektif. Materi pelatihan yang diberikan mencakup berbagai topik keselamatan kritis, antara lain ruang terbatas, bekerja di ketinggian, penerapan izin pekerjaan panas, Lock Out Tag Out Try Out (LOTOTO), penanggulangan kebakaran dan tanggap darurat, penguatan komunikasi bahaya, penerapan manajemen kelelahan, serta pelatihan operator alat angkat dan angkut seperti crane, forklift, dan rigging. Selain itu, ABM juga menyelenggarakan pelatihan terkait Implementasi Sistem Manajemen Keselamatan Pertambangan (ISMKP), Pengawas Operasional Pertama (POP) Pertambangan, serta berbagai pelatihan teknis K3 lainnya untuk memastikan seluruh pekerja memiliki kompetensi yang selaras dengan standar keselamatan industri pertambangan.

## OHS Training Effectiveness Measurement

[GRI 404-1] [SEO JK F.22]

In 2025, ABM conducted an effectiveness assessment of its training programs to ensure that employee development initiatives deliver tangible improvements in workplace safety competencies. The assessment focused on Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) training implemented across operational areas, using a three-level evaluation approach consisting of Level 1 (feedback evaluation), Level 2 (post-test), and Level 3 (behavior assessment). Results from the Level 1 evaluation indicated positive participant perceptions of the training quality, with 34% satisfaction related to the relevance and clarity of the materials, 33% for facilitator competence, and 33% for training facilities.

Throughout 2025, ABM delivered 3,151 OHS training sessions with a total of 11,660 learning hours through various learning methods, including e-learning, hybrid learning, in-class training, and virtual learning, implemented through both internal programs and learning implementation in collaboration with external training providers. These training initiatives are designed to continuously enhance safety competencies and support the consistent implementation of safety standards across all operational areas.

The effectiveness of the learning programs is also reflected in the Level 2 evaluation, which showed a significant improvement in participants' knowledge, with the average pre-test score of 63.2 increasing to 89.9 in the post-test, resulting in a 29.7% learning gain. Meanwhile, the Level 3 evaluation indicates that 31.07% of the training programs were rated highly effective, 59.89% effective, and 9.04% moderately effective, with no programs categorized as ineffective. Training topics covered a wide range of critical safety areas, including confined space, working at height, hot work permit, Lock Out Tag Out Try Out (LOTOTO), fire fighting and emergency response, hazard communication, fatigue management, and lifting equipment operation such as crane, forklift, and rigging. In addition, ABM also conducted training on the Implementation of the Mining Safety Management System (ISMKP), First Operational Supervisor (POP) certification, and other technical OHS programs, ensuring that employees maintain competencies aligned with mining industry safety standards.
