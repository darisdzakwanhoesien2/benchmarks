# Menjaga Kesehatan dan Kesejahteraan Pekerja [GRI 403-3] [GRI 403-6] [GRI 403-10] [OJK F.21]

ABM menyediakan layanan kesehatan kerja sebagai bagian dari sistem perlindungan menyeluruh terhadap pekerja di seluruh wilayah operasional. Fasilitas yang disediakan mencakup klinik dan dokter perusahaan yang mudah diakses, pemeriksaan kesehatan berkala termasuk Medical Check Up (MCU), imunisasi dan vaksinasi, serta konsultasi kesehatan. Layanan ini bertujuan untuk mendeteksi dini risiko kesehatan dan memastikan kondisi kerja yang aman dan sehat bagi pekerja.

Selain layanan kuratif dan preventif, ABM menjalankan program promosi kesehatan yang terintegrasi dengan sistem K3 bagi seluruh karyawan termasuk pekerja bukan karyawan. Program ini meliputi kampanye gaya hidup sehat, edukasi pencegahan penyakit akibat kerja, pengelolaan kelelahan, senam kebugaran, serta sosialisasi melalui berbagai media internal. Perusahaan juga melakukan pemantauan dan pengendalian faktor risiko lingkungan kerja seperti debu, kebisingan, getaran, ergonomi, kualitas air minum, dan sanitasi, guna meminimalkan potensi dampak kesehatan jangka pendek maupun jangka panjang.

Sebagai bagian dari penguatan tata kelola K3, ABM memberikan hak kepada pekerja untuk menolak pekerjaan yang berpotensi membahayakan kesehatan dan keselamatannya hingga risiko tersebut dikendalikan. Disiplin dan akuntabilitas pimpinan, pengawas, serta pekerja terus diperkuat untuk memastikan seluruh prosedur K3 dijalankan secara konsisten.

ABM juga secara sistematis mengidentifikasi dan memetakan potensi Penyakit Akibat Kerja (PAK) berdasarkan paparan faktor kimia, fisika, biologi, maupun penyakit infeksi sesuai dengan Peraturan Presiden No. 7 Tahun 2019 tentang Penyakit Akibat Kerja. Sepanjang tahun 2025, tidak terdapat kasus PAK maupun fatalitas akibat PAK, baik pada karyawan maupun pekerja kontraktor, yang mencerminkan efektivitas pengendalian risiko kesehatan kerja dan penerapan program pencegahan secara berkelanjutan.

# Maintaining Workers' Health and Well-Being [GRI 403-3] [GRI 403-6] [GRI 403-10] [OJK F.21]

ABM provides occupational health services as part of a comprehensive protection system for workers across all operational areas. The facilities provided include easily accessible clinics and company doctors, periodic health examinations including Medical Check Up (MCU), immunization and vaccination, and health consultations. These services aim to detect health risks early and ensure safe and healthy working conditions for workers.

In addition to curative and preventive services, ABM implements health promotion programs integrated with the OHS system for all employees, including non-employee workers. These programs include healthy lifestyle campaigns, education on the prevention of occupational diseases, fatigue management, fitness exercises, and socialization through various internal media. The Company also monitors and controls workplace environmental risk factors such as dust, noise, vibration, ergonomics, drinking water quality, and sanitation, to minimize potential short-term and long-term health impacts.

As part of strengthening OHS governance, ABM grants workers the right to refuse work that may endanger their health and safety until the risks are controlled. Leadership discipline and accountability of leaders, supervisors, and workers continue to be strengthened to ensure that all OHS procedures are implemented consistently.

ABM also systematically identifies and maps potential Occupational Diseases (PAK) based on exposure to chemical, physical, biological factors, as well as infectious diseases in accordance with Presidential Regulation (Peraturan Presiden) No. 7 of 2019 on Occupational Diseases. Throughout 2025, there were no cases of occupational diseases (PAK) or fatalities due to PAK, either among employees or contractor workers, reflecting the effectiveness of occupational health risk controls and the continuous implementation of prevention programs.
