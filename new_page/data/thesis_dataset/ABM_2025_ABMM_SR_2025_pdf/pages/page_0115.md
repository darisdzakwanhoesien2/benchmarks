# Transformasi Kepemimpinan dan Talent Management

Leadership Transformation and Talent Management

Untuk menjawab kebutuhan kepemimpinan di tengah ekspansi dan dinamika bisnis, pada tahun 2025 Grup ABM melanjutkan implementasi Leadership Development Program (LDP) yang mencakup seluruh jenjang kepemimpinan, mulai dari level frontline hingga eksekutif. Program ini dirancang untuk memperkuat kepemimpinan strategis, tidak hanya dari sisi kompetensi teknis, tetapi juga kemampuan memimpin tim, mengelola perubahan, dan mendorong inovasi berkelanjutan.

LDP disusun secara berjenjang, mulai dari LDP 1.0 hingga LDP 6.0, serta dilengkapi dengan Leadership Intensive Course (LIC) bagi pimpinan yang belum memperoleh pembekalan kepemimpinan formal. Sepanjang tahun 2025, program ini dilaksanakan dalam beberapa batch dengan cakupan yang terus diperluas di seluruh unit operasional Grup ABM.

To address leadership needs amid business expansion and dynamics, in 2025 the ABM Group continued the implementation of the Leadership Development Program (LDP), which covers all leadership levels, from frontline to executive. This program is designed to strengthen strategic leadership, not only in terms of technical competencies, but also the ability to lead teams, manage change, and drive sustainable innovation.

The LDP is structured in stages, from LDP 1.0 to LDP 6.0, and is complemented by a Leadership Intensive Course (LIC) for leaders who have not yet received formal leadership training. Throughout 2025, this program was implemented in several batches with continuously expanded coverage across all operational units of the ABM Group.
