# Identifikasi Bahaya Signifikan dan Pengendalian Risiko K3 [GRI 403-2][GRI 403-9]

ABM berkomitmen untuk memitigasi risiko keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja guna menjamin perlindungan pekerja dalam melaksanakan kegiatan operasional sekaligus memastikan keberlanjutan usaha. Identifikasi bahaya dan penilaian risiko dilakukan secara sistematis sesuai ISO 45001:2018 dan regulasi yang berlaku, dengan mempertimbangkan aktivitas rutin dan non-rutin, aktivitas seluruh pihak yang memasuki area kerja, faktor manusia, bahaya dari lingkungan eksternal, perubahan organisasi dan proses kerja, kewajiban peraturan perundang-undangan, serta desain tempat kerja dan prosedur operasional.

Berdasarkan hasil identifikasi bahaya, penilaian risiko, pemantauan, dan tindak lanjut oleh tim K3 di seluruh area operasional dan anak perusahaan sepanjang tahun 2025, ditetapkan sejumlah pekerjaan berisiko tinggi dengan potensi dampak signifikan berupa Fatality, Lost Time Injury (LTI), dan Property Damage sebagai berikut:

# Identification of Significant Hazards and OHS (K3) Risk Controls [GRI 403-2][GRI 403-9]

ABM is committed to mitigating occupational safety and health risks to ensure the protection of workers in carrying out operational activities while also ensuring business sustainability. Hazard identification and risk assessment are conducted systematically in accordance with ISO 45001:2018 and applicable regulations, by taking into account routine and non-routine activities, the activities of all parties entering the work area, human factors, hazards from the external environment, organizational and work process changes, statutory and regulatory obligations, as well as workplace design and operational procedures.

Based on the results of hazard identification, risk assessment, monitoring, and follow-up by OHS (K3) teams across all operational areas and subsidiaries throughout 2025, a number of high-risk jobs were determined with significant potential impacts in the form of Fatality, Lost Time Injury (LTI), and Property Damage, as follows:
