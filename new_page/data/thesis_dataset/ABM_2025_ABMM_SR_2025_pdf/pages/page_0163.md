Dengan pendekatan ini, ESG tidak diposisikan sebagai inisiatif terpisah, melainkan menyatu dalam cara perusahaan menjalankan operasi dan mengelola bisnis. Setiap target ESG dirancang agar selaras dengan keberlanjutan operasional, ketahanan bisnis, kepatuhan terhadap regulasi, serta penguatan reputasi perusahaan di mata pemangku kepentingan. Melalui kerangka Enterprise Risk Management (ERM), aspek ESG juga dipetakan sebagai bagian dari risiko strategis dan operasional yang dipantau secara berkala. Dengan demikian, setiap keputusan yang diambil tidak hanya mempertimbangkan kinerja jangka pendek, tetapi juga dampaknya terhadap keberlanjutan dan penciptaan nilai jangka panjang.

## Proses Perancangan Kebijakan Remunerasi

Penentuan kebijakan Compensation &amp; Benefit (C&amp;B) dan Talent Management bagi eksekutif dilakukan berdasarkan filosofi ABM, yaitu meningkatkan motivasi, mendukung sinergi antar entitas, mengembangkan talenta, mendorong pertumbuhan profesional, dan mengoptimalkan biaya. Struktur C&amp;B dirancang untuk:

- Menyelaraskan total penghasilan dengan kinerja bisnis dan pasar;
- Meningkatkan proporsi remunerasi variabel;
- Mendorong budaya kinerja dan sinergi;
- Menstandarkan kebijakan remunerasi;
- Menjaga daya saing pasar; serta
- Memberikan manfaat kesejahteraan yang mendukung pencapaian peran eksekutif.

## Pengawasan dan Persetujuan Remunerasi

Proses penentuan remunerasi diawasi oleh Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi (KNR) sebagai komite independen di bawah Dewan Komisaris. KNR bertanggung jawab memastikan proses penilaian remunerasi dilakukan secara objektif, transparan, dan akuntabel. Penetapan remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi kemudian dimintakan persetujuan melalui Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS) sebagai bentuk keterlibatan pemangku kepentingan utama.

Pada tahun 2025, ABM belum melibatkan pemangku kepentingan lain di luar RUPS dalam proses pengambilan keputusan remunerasi.

With this approach, ESG is not positioned as a separate initiative; rather, it integrates it into the Company operational conduct and manages its business. Each ESG target is designed to align with sustainable operations, business resilience, compliance with regulations, and the strengthening of the Company's reputation among stakeholders. Through the Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) framework, ESG aspects are also mapped as part of strategic and operational risk that are monitored periodically. Consequently, all decisions are made with consideration of not only short-term performance but also their implications for sustainability and long-term value creation.

## Remuneration Policy Design Process

The determination of Compensation &amp; Benefits (C&amp;B) and Talent Management policies for executives is founded upon the ABM philosophy, which seeks to enhance motivation, foster synergy among entities, develop talent, encourage professional growth, and optimize costs. The C&amp;B structure is designed to:

- Align total compensation with business performance and market standards;
- Increase the proportion of variable remuneration;
- Promote a culture of performance and synergy;
- Standardize remuneration policies;
- Maintain market competitiveness; and
- Provide welfare benefits that support the achievement of executive roles.

## Supervision and Approval of Remuneration

The process of determining remuneration is overseen by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NRC), functioning as an independent committee under the Board of Commissioners. The NRC bears the responsibility of ensuring that the remuneration assessment process is conducted with objectivity, transparency, and accountability. The determination of remuneration for the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors is subsequently submitted for approval through the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS), serving as a form of engagement with key stakeholders.

By 2025, ABM has not involved other stakeholders beyond the GMS into the remuneration decision-making process.
