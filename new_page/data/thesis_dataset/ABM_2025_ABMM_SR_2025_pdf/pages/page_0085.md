# Accountability in Occupational Health and Safety (K3)

ABM berkomitmen membangun budaya K3 yang memastikan lingkungan kerja aman, sehat, dan nyaman bagi seluruh pekerja, dengan menerapkan standar Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja sesuai regulasi nasional dan praktik internasional. Pengelolaan K3 dilakukan secara lintas fungsi dan difasilitasi oleh Health, Safety, and Environmental (HSE), sementara evaluasi kinerja dilakukan oleh Direksi melalui penilaian laporan rutin dan capaian Key Performance Indicator (KPI), terutama untuk memastikan pencapaian target zero fatalities, zero Lost Time Injury (LTI), dan zero recordable incident di seluruh lini bisnis dan anak perusahaan.

Pada tahun 2025, ABM senantiasa kembali melakukan evaluasi berkala atas penerapan sistem manajemen K3 di seluruh wilayah operasional sesuai dengan SMK3, ISO 45001, dan SMKP. Evaluasi ini juga mencakup pemantauan dan pelaksanaan audit internal dan eksternal minimal satu kali dalam setahun, diikuti dengan proses sertifikasi dan resertifikasi ISO 45001. Temuan audit selalu ditindaklanjuti oleh ABM untuk perbaikan berkesinambungan.

# Sistem Manajemen Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja Pertambangan (SMKP) [GRI 403-1] GRI 403-7] [GRI 403-8]

Sebagai bagian dari upaya mitigasi risiko Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3) dalam hubungan bisnis, ABM menerapkan pengendalian terintegrasi terhadap seluruh pihak yang berada dalam lingkup operasional, termasuk kontraktor dan mitra kerja. Pendekatan ini memastikan standar K3 ABM dijalankan secara konsisten oleh seluruh tenaga kerja di bawah kendali operasional Perusahaan.

ABM dan seluruh anak perusahaan menerapkan SMKP sesuai peraturan nasional dan diperkuat dengan standar internasional. Peraturan nasional yang dirujuk oleh ABM meliputi ketentuan Permen ESDM No. 26/2018, Kepmen ESDM No. 1827.K/30/MEM/2018, serta Kepdirjen Minerba No. 185.K/37.04/DJB/2019. Untuk memperkuat implementasi peraturan nasional, ABM turut merujuk pada SMK3 dari Kementerian Ketenagakerjaan, SMKP dari Kementerian ESDM, dan ISO 45001 – Occupational Health and Safety Management System.

ABM is committed to building an occupational health and safety (K3) culture that ensures a safe, healthy, and comfortable work environment for all workers, by implementing Occupational Health and Safety standards in accordance with national regulations and international practices. K3 management is conducted cross-functionally and facilitated by Health, Safety, and Environmental (HSE), while performance evaluation is carried out by the Board of Directors through assessments of routine reports and Key Performance Indicator (KPI) achievements, particularly to ensure the achievement of zero fatalities targets, zero Lost Time Injury (LTI), and zero recordable incidents across all business lines and subsidiaries.

In 2025, ABM continuously conducted periodic evaluations of the implementation of the K3 management system across all operational areas in accordance with SMK3, ISO 45001, and SMKP. This evaluation also includes monitoring and conducting internal and external audits at least once a year, followed by the ISO 45001 certification and recertification process. Audit findings are always followed up by ABM for continuous improvement.

# Mining Occupational Safety and Health Management System (SMKP)

[GRI 403-1] [GRI 403-7] [GRI 403-8]

As part of efforts to mitigate Occupational Health and Safety (K3) risks in business relationships, ABM implements integrated controls over all parties within the operational scope, including contractors and business partners. This approach ensures that ABM's K3 standards are implemented consistently by all workers under the Company's operational control.

ABM and all subsidiaries implement SMKP in accordance with national regulations and strengthened by international standards. The national regulations referred to by ABM include the provisions of Permen ESDM No. 26/2018, Kepmen ESDM No. 1827.K/30/ MEM/2018, as well as Kepdirjen Minerba No. 185.K/37.04/ DJB/2019. To strengthen the implementation of national regulations, ABM also refers to SMK3 from the Ministry of Manpower, SMKP from the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources, and ISO 45001 – Occupational Health and Safety Management System.
