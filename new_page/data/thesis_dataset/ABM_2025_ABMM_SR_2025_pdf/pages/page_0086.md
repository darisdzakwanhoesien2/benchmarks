Dalam pengelolaan pihak eksternal, ABM mewajibkan penerapan Contractor Safety Management System (CSMS) sebagai bagian dari proses seleksi dan evaluasi kontraktor, dilengkapi dengan pra-kualifikasi keselamatan dan pengendalian risiko berbasis Hazard Identification, Risk Assessment, and Determined Control (HIRADC). Perusahaan juga memastikan hak pekerja, termasuk kontraktor, untuk menghentikan pekerjaan yang berisiko hingga pengendalian memadai diterapkan. Kesiapsiagaan didukung oleh Tim Tanggap Darurat di seluruh area kerja yang turut berkontribusi dalam penanganan keadaan darurat internal maupun kegiatan kemanusiaan nasional.

In managing external parties, ABM requires the implementation of a Contractor Safety Management System (CSMS) as part of the contractor selection and evaluation process, complemented by safety pre-qualification and risk controls based on Hazard Identification, Risk Assessment, and Determined Control (HIRADC). The Company also ensures workers' rights, including contractors, to stop work that poses risks until adequate controls have been implemented. Preparedness is supported by Emergency Response Teams across all work areas, which also contribute to handling internal emergencies and national humanitarian activities.
