# Realisasi Dana Pemberdayaan Masyarakat [SEOJK F.25]

Pada tahun 2025, ABM merealisasikan dana pemberdayaan masyarakat sebesar Rp 5,11 miliar, menurun dibandingkan realisasi tahun 2024 sebesar Rp 9,59 miliar. Penurunan ini mencerminkan penyesuaian prioritas program dan skala implementasi di masing-masing wilayah operasional selama periode pelaporan.

Secara keseluruhan, dana pemberdayaan ini digunakan untuk mendukung kegiatan di berbagai sektor, meliputi pendidikan, kesehatan, peningkatan pendapatan dan kemandirian ekonomi, sosial dan budaya, serta partisipasi masyarakat dalam pengelolaan lingkungan. Program juga mencakup penguatan kelembagaan komunitas, pembangunan infrastruktur pendukung, serta inisiatif pelestarian lingkungan.

# Community Empowerment Fund Realization [SEOJK F.25]

In 2025, ABM realized community empowerment funds amounting to Rp 5.11 billion, a decrease compared to the 2024 realization of Rp 9.59 billion. This decrease reflects adjustments to program priorities and the scale of implementation in each operational area during the reporting period.

Overall, these empowerment funds were used to support activities across various sectors, including education, health, income improvement and economic self-reliance, social and culture, as well as community participation in environmental management. Programs also include strengthening community institutions, developing supporting infrastructure, and environmental conservation initiatives.

Grup ABM mengelompokkan program pemberdayaan masyarakat ke dalam tiga kategori utama, berdasarkan tujuan program dan target pemangku kepentingan, yaitu:

- Pemberdayaan Ekonomi Masyarakat, Pelestarian Lingkungan, dan Mata Pencaharian

ABM mengembangkan dan melaksanakan berbagai inisiatif masyarakat untuk memperkuat kewirausahaan, keterampilan penghidupan, pelestarian lingkungan, serta kesejahteraan pemangku kepentingan utama. Para pemangku kepentingan utama ini mencakup petani serta kelompok lain yang terdampak secara langsung maupun tidak langsung oleh operasi ABM, seperti perempuan, anak-anak, dan anak usia sekolah.

ABM Group classified community empowerment programs into three main categories, based on program objectives and stakeholder targets, as follows:

- Community Economic Empowerment, Environmental Conservation, and Livelihoods

ABM develops and implements various community initiatives to strengthen entrepreneurship, livelihood skills, environmental conservation, and the welfare of key stakeholders. These key stakeholders include farmers and other groups directly or indirectly affected by ABM's operations, such as women, children, and school-age children.
