Kinerja Lingkungan
Environmental Performance

- Operasi Lingkungan (Pengelolaan Limbah)
ABM mengelola limbah B3 dan non-B3 berdasarkan jenis dan karakteristiknya melalui pemilahan, pencatatan, penyimpanan sementara, serta pengangkutan dan pengolahan oleh pihak ketiga berizin, disertai upaya pencegahan pencemaran dan pemanfaatan kembali limbah tertentu.

Seluruh kebijakan dan implementasi aspek-aspek di atas dipantau secara berkala oleh fungsi HSE dan manajemen, serta dilaporkan kepada Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris sebagai bagian dari mekanisme evaluasi kinerja dan perbaikan berkelanjutan.

# Biaya lingkungan Hidup [SEOJK F.4][NDAQ E10]

Perusahaan mengalokasikan biaya lingkungan sebagai bagian dari komitmen dalam mengelola dampak operasional secara bertanggung jawab. Pada tahun 2025, total biaya lingkungan yang dikeluarkan mencapai Rp13,27 miliar (setara dengan sekitar US$0,79 miliar berdasarkan kurs Rp16.781,90), yang mencakup kegiatan pengelolaan limbah B3 dan non-B3, pemantauan dan pengelolaan kualitas lingkungan, pengukuran lingkungan kerja, serta pelaksanaan program reklamasi, revegetasi, dan pengendalian dampak lingkungan lainnya.

- Environmental Operations (Waste)
ABM manages hazardous and non-hazardous waste according to their types and characteristics through sorting, recording, temporary storage, transportation, and processing by licensed third parties, accompanied by efforts to prevent pollution and reuse of certain waste materials.

All policies and implementation aspects outlined above are regularly monitored by the HSE function and management. These are subsequently reported to the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners as part of the performance evaluation and continuous improvement mechanism.

# Environmental Costs [SEOJK F.4][NDAQ E10]

The Company allocates environmental costs as part of its commitment to responsibly manage the environmental impacts of its operations. In 2025, total environmental expenditure amounted to Rp13.27 billion (equivalent to approximately US$0.79 billion based on an exchange rate of Rp16,781.90), covering hazardous and non-hazardous waste management, environmental quality monitoring and management, workplace environmental measurements, as well as the implementation of reclamation, revegetation, and other environmental impact mitigation programs.
