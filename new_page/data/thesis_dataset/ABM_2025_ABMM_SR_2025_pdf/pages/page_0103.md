Kinerja Sosial Social Performance

ABM juga menetapkan jenis pekerjaan tertentu yang dapat dilakukan oleh tenaga nonkaryawan, yaitu pekerjaan yang tidak berhubungan langsung dengan proses produksi maupun proses bisnis inti perusahaan, seperti layanan kebersihan dan administrasi pendukung. Perusahaan tidak menerapkan skema pekerja paruh waktu, namun menyediakan program magang sebagai sarana pengembangan talenta muda dan pemenuhan kebutuhan operasional jangka menengah.

Dalam pengelolaan karyawan kontrak (PKWT), ABM mematuhi seluruh ketentuan ketenagakerjaan yang berlaku, dengan masa kerja maksimal hingga lima tahun yang dapat terdiri dari beberapa periode kontrak. Setiap perpanjangan kontrak dilakukan secara adil dan transparan berdasarkan evaluasi kinerja serta kebutuhan operasional, guna memastikan kepatuhan hukum sekaligus perlindungan hak-hak karyawan.

Selama tahun pelaporan, Perusahaan melakukan penyesuaian terhadap jumlah dan komposisi karyawan sejalan dengan dinamika operasional. Di situs CK, terjadi perubahan pada kelompok usia produktif yang lebih muda, terutama sebagai dampak penghentian pekerjaan kontrak di Provinsi Kalimantan Selatan dan Kalimantan Timur, serta pelaksanaan Pemutusan Hubungan Kerja (PHK) terhadap karyawan PKWTT di Provinsi Kalimantan Selatan dan Jambi. Kondisi tersebut turut memengaruhi tingkat turnover karyawan serta penurunan jumlah karyawan pada tahun 2025.

Selain itu, peningkatan proporsi karyawan PKWT dipengaruhi oleh penutupan salah satu area pertambangan dan berakhirnya kegiatan operasional di lokasi tersebut. Penyesuaian struktur tenaga kerja dilakukan secara bertahap melalui skema kontrak yang diselaraskan dengan kebutuhan transisi operasional, dengan tetap menjunjung tinggi prinsip kepatuhan terhadap peraturan ketenagakerjaan serta perlindungan hak-hak karyawan.

ABM also determines certain types of work that may be performed by non-employee workers, namely work that is not directly related to the production process or the company's core business processes, such as cleaning services and supporting administration. The Company does not implement a part-time worker scheme, but provides an internship program as a means of developing young talent and meeting medium-term operational needs.

In managing contract employees (PKWT), ABM complies with all applicable labor provisions, with a maximum employment period of up to five years, which may consist of several contract periods. Each contract extension is carried out fairly and transparently based on performance evaluations and operational needs, to ensure legal compliance as well as the protection of employees' rights.

During the reporting year, the Company made adjustments to the number and composition of employees in line with operational dynamics. At CK sites, changes occurred in the younger productive age group, primarily as an impact of the termination of contract work in the Provinces of South Kalimantan and East Kalimantan, as well as the implementation of termination of employment (PHK) for PKWTT employees in the Provinces of South Kalimantan and Jambi. These conditions also affected the employee turnover rate as well as a decrease in the number of employees in 2025.

In addition, the increase in the proportion of PKWT employees was influenced by the closure of one mining area and the end of operational activities at that location. Adjustments to the workforce structure were carried out gradually through contract schemes aligned with the needs of the operational transition, while continuing to uphold the principles of compliance with labor regulations and protection of employees' rights.
