## Strategi

Perubahan iklim mendorong ABM untuk menyesuaikan strategi bisnis guna menjaga ketahanan operasional dan mendukung transisi energi nasional. Risiko iklim yang paling material bagi ABM mencakup risiko fisik, seperti banjir ekstrem, longsor, suhu tinggi, dan kekeringan yang berdampak pada operasi pertambangan, logistik, dan distribusi bahan bakar, serta risiko transisi yang berasal dari perubahan regulasi emisi, perkembangan teknologi rendah karbon, dinamika pasar energi bersih, dan ekspektasi investor.

Sebagai respons, ABM telah menetapkan Kebijakan Perubahan Iklim yang menjadi dasar pengelolaan risiko dan peluang iklim. Kebijakan ini menegaskan pengawasan oleh Dewan Komisaris melalui Komite ESG bersama Direksi, komitmen terhadap kepatuhan regulasi, pelaksanaan aksi nyata pengurangan dampak iklim di seluruh entitas anak, integrasi aksi iklim ke dalam praktik bisnis berkelanjutan, serta pengungkapan yang transparan. Seluruh inisiatif iklim tersebut diarahkan untuk memberikan kontribusi positif terhadap aspek Lingkungan, Sosial, dan Tata Kelola (ESG) serta meningkatkan nilai jangka panjang Perusahaan. [SEOJK F.3]

## Strategy

Climate change is compelling ABM to adapt its business strategies to maintain operational resilience and support the national energy transition. The most significant climate risks for ABM include physical hazards such as severe flooding, landslides, high temperatures, and droughts, which affect mining activities, logistics, and fuel distribution. Additionally, transition risks stem from evolving emission regulations, advancements in low-carbon technologies, dynamics in the clean energy market, and changing investor expectations.

In response, ABM has established a Climate Change Policy that forms the foundation for managing climate-related risks and opportunities. This policy emphasizes oversight by the Board of Commissioners through the ESG Committee alongside the Board of Directors, demonstrates a commitment to regulatory compliance, and includes the implementation of tangible actions to mitigate climate impacts across all subsidiaries, and integrates climate actions into sustainable business practices with transparent disclosure. All these initiatives are aimed at positively contributing to Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) aspects and at increasing the Company's long-term value. [SEOJK F.3]
