# Proses Penilaian Double Materiality
[GRI 3-1]

Penyusunan Laporan Keberlanjutan ini didasarkan pada prinsip-prinsip fundamental yang menjadi acuan dalam mengidentifikasi, mengukur, dan mengungkapkan dampak bisnis ABM terhadap aspek lingkungan, sosial, dan ekonomi. Prinsip-prinsip tersebut meliputi materialitas, konteks keberlanjutan, keseimbangan, komparabilitas, kelengkapan, akurasi, ketepatan waktu, kejelasan, keterlibatan pemangku kepentingan, serta keandalan. Keseluruhan prinsip ini berfungsi sebagai kerangka kerja yang konsisten dan komprehensif untuk memastikan informasi yang disajikan relevan, transparan, dan dapat diandalkan bagi para pemangku kepentingan.

Pada tahun 2025, ABM menerapkan pendekatan double materiality untuk mengidentifikasi dan memprioritaskan topik ESG dengan mempertimbangkan dampak terhadap pemangku kepentingan serta dampak finansial dan operasional terhadap Perusahaan dengan langkah sebagai berikut:

- Langkah 1: Identifikasi Topik ESG

Proses dimulai dengan menyusun daftar awal topik ESG melalui kajian GRI Standards Coal dan SASB sector standards yang relevan dengan kegiatan usaha ABM Investama, termasuk sektor Coal Operations, Road Transport, Biofuel, serta Industrial Machinery &amp; Goods. Langkah ini memastikan keselarasan dengan standar pelaporan keberlanjutan internasional.

- Langkah 2: Penilaian Dampak terhadap Pemangku Kepentingan (Sumbu Y)

Setiap topik ESG dievaluasi berdasarkan dampak aktual dan potensial terhadap pemangku kepentingan. Penilaian dilakukan dengan memetakan topik terhadap GRI dan SASB untuk melihat tingkat relevansinya di berbagai sektor usaha Grup. Hasil penilaian mengelompokkan topik ke dalam dampak tinggi, menengah, dan rendah, yang mencerminkan pengaruhnya terhadap aspek keselamatan, lingkungan, sosial, kepatuhan, dan kepercayaan pemangku kepentingan.

- Langkah 3: Penilaian Dampak Finansial dan Operasional (Sumbu X)

Penilaian dampak finansial dilakukan melalui Focus Group Discussion (FGD) dengan anak perusahaan dan fungsi terkait. Diskusi ini menilai pengaruh setiap topik ESG terhadap kinerja keuangan, keberlanjutan operasional, risiko bisnis, serta biaya dan investasi.

# Double Materiality Assessment Process
[GRI 3-1]

The preparation of this Sustainability Report is based on fundamental principles that serve as references in identifying, measuring, and disclosing ABM's business impacts on environmental, social, and economic aspects. These principles include materiality, sustainability context, balance, comparability, completeness, accuracy, timeliness, clarity, stakeholder engagement, and reliability. All of these principles function as a consistent and comprehensive framework to ensure that the information presented is relevant, transparent, and reliable for stakeholders.

In 2025, ABM applied a double materiality approach to identify and prioritize ESG topics by considering impacts on stakeholders as well as financial and operational impacts on the Company, through the following steps:

- Step 1: Identification of ESG Topics

The process begins by compiling an initial list of ESG topics through a review of the GRI Standards Coal and the SASB sector standards relevant to ABM Investama's business activities, including the Coal Operations, Road Transport, Biofuel, and Industrial Machinery &amp; Goods sectors. This step ensures alignment with international sustainability reporting standards.

- Step 2: Assessment of Impacts on Stakeholders (Y Axis)

Each ESG topic is evaluated based on actual and potential impacts on stakeholders. The assessment is conducted by mapping topics against GRI and SASB to see their level of relevance across the Group's various business sectors. The assessment results group topics into high, medium, and low impacts, reflecting their influence on safety, environmental, social, compliance, and stakeholder trust aspects.

- Step 3: Assessment of Financial and Operational Impacts (X Axis)

The financial impact assessment is conducted through Focus Group Discussions (FGD) with subsidiaries and relevant functions. These discussions assess the influence of each ESG topic on financial performance, operational sustainability, business risks, as well as costs and investments.
