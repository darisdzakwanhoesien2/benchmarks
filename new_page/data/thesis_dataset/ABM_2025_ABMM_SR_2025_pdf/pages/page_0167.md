# ESG Committee Structure

![img-97.jpeg](img-97.jpeg)

Seperti pada tahun sebelumnya, pada tahun 2025, Komite ESG beserta seluruh Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi telah meninjau dan menyetujui Laporan Keberlanjutan 2025, termasuk pengesahan daftar topik material, data kinerja, serta informasi yang diungkapkan. Sepanjang tahun pelaporan, koordinasi implementasi ESG dilakukan melalui pertemuan ESG Working Group yang diselenggarakan secara mingguan untuk memantau perkembangan program, konsolidasi data ESG, serta tindak lanjut atas inisiatif keberlanjutan. Selain itu, pengawasan strategis terhadap implementasi ESG dilakukan melalui ESG Committee Meeting yang dilaksanakan secara kuartal untuk meninjau progres kinerja ESG serta memberikan arahan kepada manajemen.

Pelaksanaan penilaian tata kelola perusahaan (GCG Score) dilakukan melalui mekanisme self-assessment sesuai dengan ketentuan POJK, SEOJK, dan ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS), dengan rincian lebih lanjut disajikan dalam Laporan Tahunan.

Rincian tugas dan tanggung jawab komite, termasuk agenda evaluasi ESG sepanjang 2025, dijabarkan dalam Bab Tata Kelola pada Laporan Tahunan ABM halaman terkait.

Similar to the previous year, in 2025, the ESG Committee, together with the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors, has reviewed and approved the 2025 Sustainability Report. This includes the validation of the list of material topics, performance data, and the disclosed information. Throughout the reporting year, ESG implementation was monitored through weekly ESG Working Group meetings to monitor program progress, consolidate ESG data, and follow up on sustainability initiatives. Additionally, strategic oversight of ESG implementation was conducted through quarterly ESG Committee Meetings to review ESG performance progress and provide guidance to management.

The implementation of the corporate governance assessment (GCG Score) is conducted through a self-assessment mechanism in accordance with POJK, SEOJK, and the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS) regulations, with further details provided in the Annual Report.

Details of the committee's duties and responsibilities, including the ESG evaluation agenda throughout 2025, are outlined in the Governance chapter of the ABM Annual Report, on the relevant pages.
