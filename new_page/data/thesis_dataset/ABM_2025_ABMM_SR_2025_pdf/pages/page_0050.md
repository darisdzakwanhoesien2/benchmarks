# Pengelolaan Energi dan Emisi Energy and Emissions Management

## Konsumsi Energi

Pengelolaan energi merupakan bagian integral dari strategi ABM dalam meningkatkan efisiensi operasional sekaligus menekan emisi gas rumah kaca. Pendekatan ini sejalan dengan komitmen Perusahaan dalam menghadirkan energi yang memberikan manfaat secara berkelanjutan, di mana peningkatan efisiensi energi di setiap lini operasi berkontribusi langsung terhadap pengurangan intensitas emisi. Pengelolaan energi diterapkan secara konsisten di seluruh entitas melalui kebijakan dan prosedur internal yang mengacu pada ketentuan pemerintah, termasuk Peraturan Menteri ESDM No. 8 Tahun 2025 tentang Manajemen Energi, serta praktik terbaik internasional. [GRI 302-1] [SEOJK F.6] [NDAQ E3]

Energi yang digunakan Grup ABM terutama berasal dari bahan bakar minyak untuk pengoperasian alat berat dan kendaraan operasional, serta energi listrik untuk mendukung aktivitas produksi dan fasilitas penunjang. Berbagai inisiatif efisiensi energi dijalankan secara berkelanjutan, antara lain penggunaan *Biosolar* B40 di tahun 2025, pembatasan pengisian ulang bahan bakar harian, standarisasi parameter operasional peralatan, penggantian lampu konvensional dengan lampu LED, pemasangan Penerangan Jalan Umum (PJU) berbasis panel surya, optimalisasi pemanfaatan cahaya alami melalui skylight di fasilitas pergudangan, serta pengendalian konsumsi listrik di *mess karyawan*. Melalui inisiatif ini, ABM telah mengurangi energi sebesar 1,6 juta *Gigajoule*. [GRI 302-1] [GRI 302-4] [SEOJK F.7] [NDAQ E5]

Pengukuran konsumsi energi dilakukan secara sistematis menggunakan kWh meter untuk listrik dan flow meter untuk bahan bakar di masing-masing lokasi operasional, dengan perhitungan yang mengacu pada metodologi dan standar IPCC AR 5 tahun 2014 ( $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$ ,  $\mathrm{N}_{2}\mathrm{O}$ ,  $\mathrm{CH}_{4}$ ) dan GHG Protocol. Faktor konversi energi yang digunakan juga mengacu pada standar tersebut untuk memastikan konsistensi dan keterbandingan data antar periode. [GRI 302-1] [GRI 302-5] [NDAQ E3]

Saat ini, pengumpulan data terkait konsumsi energi dari sumber eksternal masih dalam proses konsolidasi di seluruh entitas usaha ABM. Perseroan terus melakukan koordinasi dengan unit operasional terkait untuk memastikan kelengkapan dan akurasi data sebelum dilakukan pelaporan secara menyeluruh. [GRI 302-2] [NDAQ E4]

## Energy Consumption

Energy management is an integral part of ABM's strategy to improve operational efficiency while reducing greenhouse gas emissions. This approach aligns with the Company's commitment to deliver energy that yields sustainable benefits, wherein increased energy efficiency across all operational facets directly reduces emission intensity. Energy management is systematically implemented across all entities through internal policies and procedures aligned with governmental regulations, including the Minister of Energy and Mineral Resources Regulation No. 8 of 2025 concerning Energy Management, as well as international best practices. [GRI 302-1] [SEOJK F.6] [NDAQ E3]

The energy consumption of the ABM Group mainly comes from fuel oil used to operate heavy equipment and operational vehicles, as well as electricity used for production activities and supporting infrastructure. Numerous energy efficiency initiatives are continually implemented, including adopting Biosolar B40 in 2025, limiting daily fuel refills, standardizing equipment operational parameters, replacing traditional lighting with LED lighting, installing solar-powered Public Street Lighting (PJU), optimizing natural light through skylights in warehouses, and regulating electricity consumption in employee dormitories. These initiatives have led to a reduction in energy consumption by 1.6 million gigajoules. [GRI 302-1] [GRI 302-4] [SEOJK F.7] [NDAQ E5]

Energy consumption measurement is systematically conducted systematically using kWh meters for electricity and flow meters for fuel at each operational site, with calculations based on the methodologies and standards outlined in IPCC AR5 2014 (CO2, N2O, CH4) and the GHG Protocol. The energy conversion factors used also refer to these standards to ensure data consistency and comparability across different periods. [GRI 302-1] [GRI 302-5] [NDAQ E3]

Currently, data collection regarding energy consumption from external sources is still being consolidated across all ABM business entities. The Company continues to coordinate with the relevant operational units to ensure data completeness and accuracy before comprehensive reporting. [GRI 302-2] [NDAQ E4]
