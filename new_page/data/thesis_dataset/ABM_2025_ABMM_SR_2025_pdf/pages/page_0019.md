# Pasar yang Dilayani [GRI 2-1][SEOJK C.4]

Markets Served

ABM memasarkan produk portofolio pertambangannya kepada perusahaan pembangkit listrik di pasar domestik maupun internasional, termasuk Tiongkok, Thailand, Vietnam, dan Filipina. Di tingkat nasional, ABM juga menawarkan layanan lengkap, seperti jasa kontraktor pertambangan, logistik, rekayasa, pasokan bahan bakar, dan energi listrik. Semua layanan ini dirancang untuk memenuhi kebutuhan industri di berbagai wilayah Indonesia.

ABM markets its mining portfolio products to power generation companies in domestic and international markets, including China, Thailand, Vietnam, and the Philippines. At the national level, ABM also offers comprehensive services, including mining contracting, logistics, engineering, fuel supply, and electrical power. All of these services are designed to meet the needs of industries in various regions of Indonesia.
