# Mengatasi Tantangan dalam Akses Pembelajaran melalui Udemy Business

Untuk mempercepat transformasi pembelajaran digital dan memperluas jangkauan pengembangan kompetensi karyawan, ABM mengintegrasikan Udemy Business ke dalam Learning Management System (LMS) sebagai bagian dari inisiatif Digital Learning Transformation. Inisiatif ini menjawab tantangan utama pembelajaran konvensional, seperti keterbatasan kapasitas kelas tatap muka, tingginya biaya pelatihan, keterbatasan waktu karyawan, serta akses pembelajaran yang belum merata khususnya bagi karyawan di site operasional.

Melalui Udemy Business, karyawan Grup ABM memperoleh akses ke ribuan kursus daring yang mencakup kepemimpinan, manajemen, teknologi digital, keselamatan kerja, hingga topik keberlanjutan dan ESG. Platform ini terintegrasi penuh dengan LMS melalui Single Sign-On (SSO), memungkinkan pembelajaran dilakukan secara fleksibel melalui web maupun perangkat seluler, tanpa mengganggu aktivitas operasional.

Integrasi ini juga memungkinkan pelacakan jam pelatihan secara individual melalui sistem learning analytics yang terhubung langsung dengan data karyawan. Setiap aktivitas pembelajaran termasuk durasi belajar, kursus yang diikuti, tingkat penyelesaian, dan progres individu tercatat secara otomatis dan terpusat di LMS. Dengan demikian, ABM dapat memantau total jam pelatihan per karyawan, mendukung penyusunan Individual Development Plan (IDP), serta memastikan pelaporan pengembangan SDM yang lebih akurat, transparan, dan berbasis data.

# Addressing Learning Access Challenges Through Udemy Business

To accelerate the digital learning transformation and expand the reach of employee competency development, ABM integrated Udemy Business into the Learning Management System (LMS) as part of the Digital Learning Transformation initiative. This initiative addresses key challenges of conventional learning, such as limited face-to-face class capacity, high training costs, limited employee time, and learning access that has not been evenly distributed—particularly for employees at operational sites.

Through Udemy Business, ABM Group employees gain access to thousands of online courses covering leadership, management, digital technology, occupational safety, and sustainability and ESG topics. This platform is fully integrated with the LMS through Single Sign-On (SSO), enabling flexible learning via the web and mobile devices without disrupting operational activities.

This integration also enables individual tracking of training hours through a learning analytics system directly connected to employee data. Each learning activity, including learning duration, courses taken, completion rates, and individual progress, is recorded automatically and centrally in the LMS. Accordingly, ABM can monitor total training hours per employee, support the preparation of Individual Development Plans (IDPs), and ensure more accurate, transparent, and data-driven human resource development reporting.
