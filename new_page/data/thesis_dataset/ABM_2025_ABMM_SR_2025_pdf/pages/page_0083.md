# Kinerja Sosial

Social Performance

ABM memastikan penghormatan terhadap hak asasi manusia, terutama pekerja, terintegrasi dalam kinerja sosial organisasi yang berorientasi pada keberlanjutan.

ABM ensures that respect for human rights, especially workers' rights, is integrated into the organization's sustainability-oriented social performance.
