# Pencegahan Benturan Kepentingan [GRI 2-9]

ABM memastikan pencegahan benturan kepentingan melalui penerapan pedoman etika dan tata kelola, pengungkapan rutin melalui pernyataan tertulis, pengawasan oleh Komite Tata Kelola Perusahaan, serta pengaturan transaksi afiliasi yang wajib memenuhi ketentuan regulasi, diaudit, dan diungkapkan secara transparan, termasuk pengungkapan keanggotaan lintas dewan, kepemilikan saham lintas organisasi, serta pemegang saham pengendali. Transparansi atas seluruh transaksi pihak berelasi diungkapkan dan dipublikasikan dalam laporan keuangan konsolidasi sesuai dengan standar akuntansi yang berlaku. Melalui mekanisme pencegahan ini, ABM menjaga integritas, keterbukaan, dan kepercayaan dalam penerapan governansi Perusahaan.

# Independensi Dewan Direksi dan Komisaris

[GRI 2-9][GRI 2-11][GRI 2-15] [NDAQ G.2]

ABM menuangkan kebijakan independensi Dewan Direksi dan Komisaris dalam Board Manual yang bersifat mengikat bagi seluruh anggota perusahaan. Dokumen ini menjadi pedoman hubungan internal Perusahaan dan mencakup tugas, wewenang, mekanisme rapat, pengambilan keputusan, serta pengendalian dan pengawasan.

Board Manual menegaskan bahwa Dewan Komisaris harus bersikap independen dalam melakukan pengawasan, serta melarang Direktur Utama (Chief Executive Officer) merangkap sebagai Komisaris guna menjaga efektivitas fungsi kontrol. Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi tidak diperbolehkan memiliki hubungan keluarga satu sama lain, serta tidak ada lintas kepemilikan saham dengan pemasok dan para pemangku kepentingan lain di luar Grup TMT.

Dalam menjalankan fungsi pengawasan, ABM menunjukkan menempatkan Komisaris Independen untuk mengawasi kinerja Direksi, memberikan masukan secara objektif, dan memastikan penerapan GCG berjalan dengan semestinya.

Pada tahun 2025, ABM memiliki dua Komisaris Independen yang mewakili 50% dari total anggota Dewan Komisaris. Komposisi ini mencerminkan komitmen Perseroan terhadap pengawasan yang independen dan tata kelola yang seimbang.

# Conflict of Interest Prevention [GRI 2-9]

ABM ensures the prevention of conflicts of interest through the implementation of ethical guidelines and governance principles, routine disclosures via written statements, oversight by the Corporate Governance Committee, and regulation of affiliate transactions that must adhere to regulatory standards, be audited, and transparently disclosed, including disclosures of cross-board memberships, cross-organization shareholdings, and controlling shareholders. Transparency regarding all related-party transactions is disclosed and published in consolidated financial reports in accordance with applicable accounting standards. Through this preventive mechanism, ABM upholds integrity, transparency, and trust in the company's governance practices.

# Independence of the Board of Directors and Commissioners

[GRI 2-9][GRI 2-11][GRI 2-15] [NDAQ G.2]

ABM articulates the independence policy of the Board of Directors and Commissioners within the Board Manual, a document that is binding for all company members. This document serves as a guideline for the company's internal relations, encompassing responsibilities, authorities, meeting protocols, decision-making processes, as well as control and supervision.

The Board Manual emphasizes that the Board of Commissioners to operate independently in their supervisory role and explicitly prohibits the President Director (Chief Executive Officer) from concurrently serving as a Commissioner to maintain the effectiveness of the control function. The Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors are barred from possessing familial relationships with one another, and cross-shareholdings with suppliers and other external stakeholders outside the TMT Group are not permitted.

In the conduct of its supervisory responsibilities, ABM appoints and positions Independent Commissioners to oversee the Board of Directors' performance, provide objective input, and ensure the proper implementation of GCG.

By 2025, ABM aims to have two Independent Commissioners, comprising 50% of the total members of the Board of Commissioners. This composition reflects the company's commitment to independent oversight and balanced governance.
