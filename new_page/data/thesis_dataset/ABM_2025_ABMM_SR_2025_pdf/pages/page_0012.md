Terdapat pernyataan ulang, khususnya pada data limbah B3, limbah non-B3, dan konsumsi energi. Pernyataan ini disampaikan sebagai bagian dari komitmen ABM dalam menjaga transparansi, konsistensi, dan akurasi pelaporan sesuai ketentuan pelaporan keberlanjutan yang berlaku.

# Assurance oleh Pihak Eksternal [NDAQ G10.1] [SEOJK G.1] [GRI 2-5]

Perusahaan menunjuk TUV Rheinland Indonesia sebagai pihak independen untuk melaksanakan assurance atas Laporan Keberlanjutan ini, dengan fokus pada verifikasi konten laporan, khususnya terkait topik material. Penunjukan pihak ketiga ini dilakukan untuk memastikan proses assurance berlangsung secara objektif dan bebas dari konflik kepentingan. TUV Rheinland Indonesia tidak memiliki hubungan bisnis dengan ABM, sehingga independensinya terjamin. Sepanjang proses, Top Management, manajemen, dan kontributor data berperan aktif dalam penyediaan informasi dan klarifikasi yang diperlukan.

Kegiatan assurance dilakukan mengacu pada standar AA1000AS v3 (2020) dengan tingkat moderate assurance, serta menerapkan prinsip AA1000AP (2018) yang mencakup inklusivitas, materialitas, responsivitas, dan dampak. Pernyataan assurance lengkap dapat ditemukan pada halaman 211 laporan ini.

# Restatement of Information [SEOJK C.6][GRI 2-4]

For data and information disclosed in the previous report, a restatement has been made, particularly concerning hazardous waste, non-hazardous waste, and energy consumption data. This adjustment was carried out due to a change in the reporting unit from kiloliters to liters, as well as improvements in data collection and recording methodologies to enhance the accuracy and consistency of reporting. This statement is presented as part of ABM's commitment to maintaining transparency, consistency, and accuracy in reporting in accordance with applicable sustainability reporting requirements.

# External Assurance [NDAQ G10.1] [SEOJK G.1] [GRI 2-5]

The company appointed TUV Rheinland Indonesia as an independent party to conduct assurance on this Sustainability Report, focusing on verifying the report's content, especially regarding material topics. This third-party appointment was made to ensure that the assurance process was conducted objectively and free from conflicts of interest. TUV Rheinland Indonesia has no business relationship with ABM, ensuring its independence. Throughout the process, top management, management, and data contributors actively provided the necessary information and clarifications.

The assurance activities were conducted according to the AA1000AS v3 (2020) standard with a moderate assurance level. They also followed the AA1000AP (2018) principles, which include inclusivity, materiality, responsiveness, and impact. The full assurance statement can be found on page 211 of this report.
