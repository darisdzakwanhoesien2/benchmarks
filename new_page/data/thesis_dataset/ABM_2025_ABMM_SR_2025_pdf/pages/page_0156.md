# Nominasi Badan Tata Kelola Tertinggi

[GRI 2-10]

ABM menerapkan proses nominasi yang terstruktur dan mengacu pada ketentuan yang berlaku bagi perusahaan terbuka. Proses ini bertujuan memastikan anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi memiliki kompetensi, integritas, serta kemandirian yang memadai untuk menjalankan fungsi pengawasan dan pengelolaan Perusahaan.

# Proses Nominasi Dewan Komisaris

Sebagai perusahaan publik, ABM wajib memiliki Komisaris Independen dengan jumlah minimal 30% dari total anggota Dewan Komisaris. Komisaris Independen berasal dari pihak eksternal yang bebas dari pengaruh Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, maupun Pemegang Saham Pengendali. Selain itu, sekurangkurangnya satu anggota Dewan Komisaris wajib memiliki latar belakang akuntansi dan/atau keuangan.

Proses nominasi dilakukan oleh Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi (KNR) yang menilai kualifikasi, pengalaman, serta kepatuhan terhadap persyaratan regulator. Rekomendasi KNR selanjutnya disampaikan kepada Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham (RUPS) untuk memperoleh persetujuan pengangkatan.

# Proses Nominasi Direksi

Direksi berjumlah minimal dua orang, termasuk satu Direktur Utama. KNR melakukan penilaian awal terhadap calon Direksi dan mengajukan rekomendasi kepada RUPS. Calon Direksi dievaluasi berdasarkan pengalaman kepemimpinan, pemahaman terhadap risiko, serta kesesuaian dengan kebutuhan bisnis Perusahaan.

# Sumber Calon dan Prioritas Seleksi

ABM memprioritaskan calon yang berasal dari internal Grup, seperti Direktur Entitas Anak atau karyawan yang telah mengikuti pengembangan kepemimpinan. Namun demikian, Perusahaan tetap membuka kesempatan bagi calon eksternal apabila tidak terdapat kandidat internal yang memenuhi persyaratan, terdapat ketentuan hukum tertentu, atau diperlukan perwakilan daerah.

# Nomination of the Highest Governance Body [GRI 2-10]

ABM implements a structured nomination process based on applicable regulations for publicly listed companies. This process aims to ensure that members of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors possess adequate competence, integrity, and independence to carry out the supervisory and management functions of the Company.

# Nomination Process for the Board of Commissioners

As a publicly listed company, ABM is required to appoint an Independent Commissioner constituting at least 30% of the total Board of Commissioners members. The Independent Commissioner must come from an external party who is free from influence by the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, or the Controlling Shareholders. Furthermore, at least one member of the Board of Commissioners must have a background in accounting and/or finance.

The nomination process is conducted by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NRC), which assesses the qualifications, experience, and compliance with regulatory requirements. The NRC's recommendations are subsequently submitted to the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) for the approval of the appointment.

# Board of Directors Nomination Process

The board of directors is composed of a minimum of two individuals, including one President Director. KNR conducts an initial assessment of the prospective directors and submits its recommendations to the GMS. The candidates are assessed based on their leadership experience, risk comprehension, and their suitability to the company's business needs.

# Candidate Sources and Selection Priorities

ABM prioritizes candidates from within the Group, such as subsidiary directors or employees who have participated in leadership development. However, the Company remains open to external candidates if there are no suitable internal candidates, certain legal provisions apply, or regional representation is needed.
