Perubahan Iklim

Climate Change

ABM berkomitmen untuk melaksanakan analisis skenario perubahan iklim sebagai bagian dari penguatan manajemen risiko dan perencanaan strategis jangka panjang. Analisis ini akan menggunakan pendekatan *Representative Concentration Pathways* (RCP) guna memahami potensi dampak perubahan iklim terhadap kinerja operasional, ketahanan aset, serta profil risiko Perusahaan di berbagai wilayah operasional.

Melalui penerapan skenario RCP, ABM akan menilai risiko fisik dan risiko transisi di bawah berbagai tingkat pemanasan global, serta mengidentifikasi implikasinya terhadap operasi pertambangan, logistik, investasi, dan strategi diversifikasi bisnis. Hasil analisis ini akan menjadi dasar dalam pengambilan keputusan strategis, perencanaan mitigasi dan adaptasi iklim, serta peningkatan ketahanan bisnis ABM dalam menghadapi ketidakpastian iklim di masa depan.

ABM is committed to conducting climate scenario analysis as part of strengthening risk management and long-term strategic planning. This analysis will utilize the Representative Concentration Pathways (RCP) approach to evaluate the potential impacts of climate change on operational performance, asset resilience, and the company's risk profile across various operational areas.

Through the implementation of RCP scenarios, ABM will assess physical and transition risks associated with different global warming levels, as well as identify their implications for mining operations, logistics, investments, and business diversification strategies. The results of this analysis will serve as a basis for strategic decision-making, climate mitigation and adaptation planning, and enhancing ABM's business resilience in facing future climate-related uncertainties.
