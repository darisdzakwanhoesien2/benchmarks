## Kebijakan dan Komitmen Pengelolaan Keanekaragaman Hayati

ABM berkomitmen menjaga keanekaragaman hayati di sekitar wilayah operasional melalui perlindungan ekosistem dan rehabilitasi lingkungan yang dilakukan secara bertanggung jawab. Komitmen ini diterapkan untuk memastikan bahwa kegiatan usaha di sektor pertambangan, energi, dan logistik tetap berjalan selaras dengan prinsip keberlanjutan lingkungan.

Komitmen tersebut diformalkan melalui Kebijakan Perlindungan Keanekaragaman Hayati TIA dan Prosedur Pemantauan Tingkat Keanekaragaman Hayati (TIA-PR-HSE-40), yang menjadi dasar pengelolaan dampak keanekaragaman hayati di seluruh tahapan kegiatan, mulai dari perencanaan, operasi, hingga pascatambang.

Pengelolaan keanekaragaman hayati mengacu pada dokumen lingkungan yang berlaku, seperti Rencana Pengelolaan Lingkungan dan Rencana Pemantauan Lingkungan (RKL-RPL) serta Upaya Pengelolaan Lingkungan dan Upaya Pemantauan Lingkungan (UKL-UPL), dengan mempertimbangkan karakteristik ekologis setempat. Pendekatan ini diterapkan sejak tahap perencanaan kegiatan guna meminimalkan potensi dampak terhadap lingkungan.

## Policy and Commitment to Biodiversity Management

ABM is committed to preserving biodiversity within its operational areas through the protection of ecosystems and environmentally responsible rehabilitation. This commitment is implemented to ensure that activities across the mining, energy, and logistics sectors proceed in harmony with principles of environmental sustainability.

This commitment is formalized through the TIA Biodiversity Protection Policy and the Biodiversity Level Monitoring Procedure (TIA-PR-HSE-40), which serve as the basis for managing biodiversity impacts throughout all stages of activities, from planning and operations to post-mining.

Biodiversity management refers to applicable environmental documents, such as the Environmental Management and Monitoring Plan (RKL-RPL) and Environmental Management and Monitoring Efforts (UKL-UPL), taking into account local ecological characteristics. This approach is applied from the planning phase to minimize potential environmental impacts.
