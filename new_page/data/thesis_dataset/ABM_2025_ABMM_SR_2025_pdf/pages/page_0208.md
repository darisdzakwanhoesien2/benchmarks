# Reklamasi dan Penanaman Pohon sebagai Upaya Carbon Offset

Selain pengurangan emisi, ABM melaksanakan program reklamasi lahan dan penanaman pohon sebagai bagian dari strategi mitigasi perubahan iklim. Program ini berfungsi sebagai upaya carbon offset sekaligus mendukung pemulihan lingkungan pascatambang dan perlindungan keanekaragaman hayati.

Hingga tahun pelaporan, kegiatan reklamasi dan revegetasi telah mencakup area seluas 901,26 hektare dengan jumlah penanaman 563.268 pohon di area IUP TIA. Melalui pendekatan ini, ABM berupaya menyeimbangkan dampak operasional dengan pemulihan ekosistem, sekaligus memperkuat ketahanan lingkungan di sekitar wilayah operasional.

# Reclamation and Tree Planting as Carbon Offset Efforts

In addition to emission reductions, ABM implements land reclamation and tree-planting programs as part of our climate change mitigation strategy. These programs serve as carbon offset efforts while also promoting post-mining environmental restoration and biodiversity preservation.

As of the reporting year, reclamation and revegetation activities have covered an area of 901.26 hectares with 563.268 trees planted in IUP TIA area. Through this approach, ABM aims to balance operational impacts with ecosystem restoration, whilst also strengthening environmental resilience around the operational area.

# Mengelola Air untuk Menjaga Keberlanjutan Operasi Tambang

Operasi pertambangan ABM di Kalimantan dan Sumatra menghadapi tingkat curah hujan yang tinggi, sehingga berpotensi meningkatkan risiko banjir dan gangguan operasional. Untuk mengelola risiko tersebut, ABM menerapkan pendekatan manajemen air terpadu yang bertujuan menjaga kelancaran operasi sekaligus melindungi lingkungan sekitar area tambang.

Upaya yang dilakukan mencakup pembangunan dan pemeliharaan saluran drainase, pemantauan kualitas air buangan secara berkala, serta penerapan langkah-langkah pencegahan banjir dan pengendalian sedimen. Melalui pengelolaan air yang sistematis ini, ABM berupaya meminimalkan dampak lingkungan, memastikan kepatuhan terhadap regulasi, serta meningkatkan ketahanan operasional di tengah tantangan iklim yang semakin dinamis.

# Managing Water to Sustain Mining Operations

ABM's mining operations in Kalimantan and Sumatra face high levels of rainfall, which may increase the risk of flooding and operational disruptions. To manage these risks, ABM implements an integrated water management approach aimed at maintaining operational continuity while protecting the surrounding environment in the mining areas.

The efforts include the construction and maintenance of drainage systems, regular monitoring of discharged water quality, as well as the implementation of flood prevention measures and sediment control. Through this systematic water management, ABM strives to minimize environmental impacts, ensure compliance with regulations, and enhance operational resilience amid increasingly dynamic climate challenges.
