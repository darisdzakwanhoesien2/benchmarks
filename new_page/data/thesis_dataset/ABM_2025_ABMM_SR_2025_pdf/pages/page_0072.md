# Kondisi Keanekaragaman Hayati di Wilayah Operasional [GRI 304-3]

Pada tahun 2024, dalam operasional TIA, tidak terdapat spesies yang terdampak secara khusus akibat aktivitas penambangan. Area operasional TIA sebelumnya merupakan bagian dari Hutan Tanaman Industri (HTI) dengan dominasi Acacia Mangium, sehingga tidak menimbulkan dampak spesifik terhadap spesies tertentu.

Kegiatan penambangan telah berlangsung sejak tahun 2009 dan direncanakan berlanjut hingga tahun 2025, sementara upaya pemulihan lingkungan telah dilakukan sejak tahun 2010 dan direncanakan berlanjut hingga tahun 2031. Dengan karakteristik lahan yang dapat dipulihkan, dampak aktivitas penambangan dinilai bersifat sementara dan dapat direhabilitasi sesuai dengan peruntukan lahan pascatambang.

Sejalan dengan upaya pemulihan tersebut, reklamasi dan revegetasi dilaksanakan menggunakan komposisi tanaman yang lebih beragam, yaitu sekitar $60\%$ tanaman pionir serta $40\%$ tanaman lokal dan Multi Purpose Tree Species (MPTS), sehingga Perusahaan berupaya meningkatkan tingkat keanekaragaman hayati dibandingkan dengan kondisi rona awal yang homogen. Pengukuran kuantitatif perubahan kondisi biodiversitas secara jangka panjang masih dikembangkan. [GRI 101-7] [SEOJK F.10]

# Biodiversity Conditions in the Operational Area [GRI 304-3]

In 2024, the operations of TIA did not specifically affect any species during their mining activities. The operational area of TIA was previously part of an Industrial Plantation Forest (HTI) predominantly composed of Acacia mangium, so it did not cause notable impacts on any particular species.

Mining activities have been ongoing since 2009 and are scheduled to continue until 2025, while environmental restoration efforts have been carried out since 2010 and are planned to last until 2031. Given the land's recoverable characteristics, the impact of mining activities is considered temporary and can be rehabilitated based on post-mining land use.

In accordance with these restoration efforts, reclamation and revegetation involve using a more diverse plant composition, with approximately $60\%$ pioneer species and $40\%$ native plants and Multi-Purpose Tree Species (MPTS). The company's goal is to increase biodiversity compared to the initial homogeneous condition. Quantitative measurement of long-term biodiversity changes is currently under development.
