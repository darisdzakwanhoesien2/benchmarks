# Program Bunda Peduli Asupan Sehat (Bunda PAS)

Diluncurkan melalui CKB, ABM bekerja sama dengan tenaga kesehatan lokal dan pemerintah desa di Surabaya, Jawa Timur. Program ini memberikan dukungan gizi langsung, edukasi, dan pemberdayaan masyarakat untuk mengatasi stunting.

Program berjalan di 4 kelurahan di Kecamatan Benowo Tambak Osowilangun, Sememi/Semeni, Kandangan, dan Romokalisari dan menjangkau 50 anak berisiko stunting melalui paket gizi rutin (telur, susu, vitamin), pemantauan pertumbuhan, pelatihan bagi pengasuh, dan koordinasi dengan Puskesmas. Selain itu, program ini juga dijalankan di Jakarta Utara, khususnya di Kelurahan Sukapura, Kecamatan Cilincing. Total kontribusi ABM pada tahun 2025 untuk program ini adalah Rp 65,49 Juta.

Dengan fokus intervensi program ada 1.000 hari pertama kehidupan, ABM memastikan bahwa program ini menyasar akar masalah stunting, yaitu malnutrisi kronis, kurangnya pengetahuan pengasuhan, dan minimnya akses terhadap makanan bergizi, daripada sekadar memberikan bantuan yang tidak menyentuh akar permasalahan (ad-hoc aid).

# Dampak Program Bunda PAS

Berdasarkan SROI yang dilakukan oleh Center for Entrepreneurship, Change, and Third Sector (CECT) Trisakti, program Bunda PAS di Jakarta menghasilkan dampak sosial yang mencakup, antara lain, asupan gizi balita tercukupi dan terpantau secara rutin, peningkatan perkembangan motorik halus dan kasar bagi balita, efisiensi biaya pemenuhan gizi anak, dan penurunan angka stunting di Kecamatan Sukapura.

Analisis Social Return on Investment yang dilakukan oleh ABM dan CECT Sustainability Universitas Trisakti untuk program di Jakarta Utara menunjukkan bahwa setiap Rp 1 yang diinvestasikan untuk program ini menghasilkan nilai dampak sosial sebesar Rp 1,43.

# Bunda Peduli Asupan Sehat (Bunda PAS) Program

Launched through CKB, ABM collaborates with local health workers and village governments in Surabaya, East Java. This program provides direct nutritional support, education, and community empowerment to address stunting.

The program operates in four urban villages in Benowo District—Tambak Osowilangun, Sememi/Semeni, Kandangan, and Romokalisari—and reaches 50 children at risk of stunting through regular nutrition packages (eggs, milk, vitamins), growth monitoring, training for caregivers, and coordination with Puskesmas. In addition, the program is also implemented in North Jakarta, specifically in Sukapura Urban Village, Cilincing District. ABM's total contribution in 2025 for this program was IDR 65.49 million.

With the program intervention focused on the first 1,000 days of life, ABM ensures that this program targets the root causes of stunting, namely chronic malnutrition, lack of caregiving knowledge, and limited access to nutritious food, rather than merely providing assistance that does not address the underlying issues (ad-hoc aid).

# Impacts of the Bunda PAS Program

Based on the SROI conducted by the Center for Entrepreneurship, Change, and Third Sector (CECT) Trisakti, the Bunda PAS program in Jakarta generated social impacts including, among others, toddlers' nutritional intake being adequately fulfilled and routinely monitored, improved fine and gross motor development for toddlers, cost efficiency in meeting children's nutritional needs, and a reduction in stunting rates in Sukapura District.

The Social Return on Investment analysis conducted by ABM and CECT Sustainability Universitas Trisakti for the program in North Jakarta shows that every Rp1 invested in this program generated Rp 1.43 in social impact value.
