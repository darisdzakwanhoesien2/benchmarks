# Keselamatan sebagai Prioritas Bersama

Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3) merupakan prioritas utama dalam seluruh operasional Perusahaan. Sepanjang periode pelaporan, penerapan sistem manajemen K3 yang konsisten di berbagai entitas Grup memperoleh pengakuan dari instansi pemerintah, penyelenggara penghargaan nasional, serta pengakuan internal, yang mencerminkan komitmen Perusahaan dalam menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang aman dan berkelanjutan.

ABM melalui Entitasnya CK menunjukkan komitmen yang kuat dalam penerapan Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3) melalui penguatan sistem manajemen keselamatan serta pengendalian risiko operasional secara konsisten. Atas upaya tersebut, CK memperoleh penghargaan Indonesia Safety Excellence Award (ISEA) sebagai pengakuan atas praktik keselamatan kerja yang unggul dan kepatuhan terhadap standar K3 yang berlaku. Penghargaan ini mencerminkan keberhasilan CK dalam membangun budaya keselamatan yang terintegrasi dalam seluruh aktivitas operasional.

SSB mencatatkan kinerja keselamatan kerja yang positif di berbagai wilayah operasionalnya. Sepanjang periode pelaporan, SSB memperoleh penghargaan Zero Accident untuk area Grasberg yang diberikan oleh PT Freeport Indonesia (PTFI), serta penghargaan Zero Accident untuk area Sebamban dan Tanjung dari Pemerintah Provinsi Kalimantan Selatan melalui instansi ketenagakerjaan terkait. Penghargaan ini diberikan berdasarkan pencapaian jam kerja tanpa kecelakaan serta hasil evaluasi kepatuhan terhadap ketentuan keselamatan kerja yang berlaku di masing-masing wilayah.

ATR menunjukkan konsistensi dalam menjaga kinerja keselamatan kerja melalui pencapaian Zero Accident Hours Periode 2024 yang diberikan oleh CKB. Capaian ini mencerminkan efektivitas penerapan prosedur keselamatan kerja, kedisiplinan tenaga kerja dalam mematuhi standar operasional, serta pengawasan K3 yang berkelanjutan di lingkungan kerja. Pengakuan ini bersifat internal dan didukung oleh pencatatan serta dokumentasi resmi atas kinerja keselamatan kerja selama periode tersebut.

# Safety as a Shared Priority

Occupational safety and health (K3) are the top priority across all of the Company's operations. Throughout the reporting period, the consistent implementation of the OHS (K3) management system across various Group entities received recognition from government institutions, national award organizers, as well as internal recognition, reflecting the Company's commitment to creating a safe and sustainable work environment.

ABM, through its Entity CK, demonstrated a strong commitment to the implementation of occupational safety and health (K3) by strengthening the safety management system and consistently controlling operational risks. For these efforts, CK received the Indonesia Safety Excellence Award (ISEA) as recognition of excellent occupational safety practices and compliance with applicable OHS (K3) standards. This award reflects CK's success in building a safety culture integrated into all operational activities.

SSB recorded positive occupational safety performance across its various operational areas. Throughout the reporting period, SSB received a Zero Accident award for the Grasberg area granted by PT Freeport Indonesia (PTFI), as well as Zero Accident awards for the Sebamban and Tanjung areas from the Provincial Government of South Kalimantan through the relevant manpower agency. These awards were granted based on the achievement of work hours without accidents and the results of evaluations of compliance with applicable occupational safety provisions in each area.

ATR demonstrated consistency in maintaining occupational safety performance through the achievement of Zero Accident Hours for the 2024 Period, granted by CKB. This achievement reflects the effectiveness of implementing occupational safety procedures, workforce discipline in complying with operational standards, as well as ongoing OHS (K3) oversight in the workplace. This recognition is internal in nature and is supported by official recording and documentation of occupational safety performance during the period.
