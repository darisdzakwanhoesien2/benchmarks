## Pengembangan Kompetensi dan Evaluasi Kinerja [GRI 404-1][GRI 404-3]

ABM menyelenggarakan berbagai program pelatihan dan pengembangan kompetensi untuk mendukung peningkatan kapabilitas karyawan di seluruh jenjang jabatan dan fungsi kerja.

Pelatihan dan pengembangan karyawan dilaksanakan secara berkelanjutan sepanjang tahun, disesuaikan dengan kebutuhan kompetensi, tuntutan operasional, serta pengembangan karier jangka panjang.

ABM juga secara berkala melakukan evaluasi kinerja dan pengembangan karier melalui sistem penilaian kinerja yang terstruktur, yang menjadi dasar dalam penetapan program pengembangan kompetensi, promosi, maupun perencanaan suksesi.

## Competency Development and Performance Evaluation [GRI 404-1][GRI 404-3]

ABM organizes various training programs and competency development initiatives to support the enhancement of employee capabilities across all job levels and work functions.

Employee training and development are carried out continuously throughout the year, tailored to competency needs, operational demands, and long-term career development.

ABM also periodically conducts performance evaluation and career development through a structured performance appraisal system, which serves as the basis for determining competency development programs, promotions, and succession planning.
