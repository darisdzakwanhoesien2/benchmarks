Social Performance

- Dukungan untuk Mengentaskan AIDS, TBC, dan Malaria di Kalimantan Selatan
TIA dan CK mendukung inisiatif provinsi Kalimantan Selatan untuk mengentaskan AIDS, TBC, dan Malaria pada 2030. Kontribusi kedua perusahaan mencakup kegiatan pencegahan, layanan Voluntary Counselling and Testing (VCT) di tempat kerja, dan edukasi kesehatan bagi pelajar SMA. Upaya ini sejalan dengan target pencapaian kesehatan nasional dan TPB, sekaligus memperkuat kesadaran komunitas dan kemampuan pencegahan terhadap penyakit tersebut.

- Pemeliharaan Teaching Factory dan Dukungan Rumah Autis
Di bidang pendidikan, dukungan diberikan melalui pemeliharaan fasilitas Teaching Factory serta bantuan perlengkapan sekolah bagi SDN Uhak dan Lurang, Maluku melalui CKB. Komitmen terhadap inklusi juga diwujudkan melalui dukungan berkelanjutan kepada Rumah Autis dengan alokasi dana sebesar Rp 8 juta untuk mendukung operasional dan kebutuhan pembelajaran.

- Pencegahan Stunting
Program pencegahan stunting dilaksanakan di Sememi, Surabaya (75 anak) dengan dukungan senilai Rp 30 juta, serta di Puskesmas Sukapura, Cilincing, Jakarta Utara (53 anak) melalui penyediaan makanan tambahan bergizi bagi anak-anak terdampak. Program ini memperkuat upaya peningkatan kualitas generasi masa depan di wilayah operasional perusahaan.

- Pemberdayaan Perempuan melalui UMKM
Pemberdayaan ekonomi lokal juga dilakukan melalui pembelian dan penyaluran produk batik sasirangan dari 11 ibu di Desa Bunati, Kecamatan Sungai Loban, Kabupaten Tanah Bumbu, sebagai bentuk dukungan terhadap UMKM perempuan dan penguatan ekonomi berbasis komunitas.

- Program Food Always In The House (FAITH)
Melalui program Food Always In The House (FAITH), ABM mendorong keterlibatan aktif karyawan dalam kegiatan volunteering yang berfokus pada praktik urban farming di lahan terbatas, bekerja sama dengan Kebun Kumara. Program ini meliputi penanaman tanaman pangan, tanaman obat, serta tanaman cepat panen, disertai edukasi mengenai media tanam dan teknik pengelolaan kebun sederhana yang dapat diterapkan secara mandiri. Pada tahun 2025, kegiatan ini diikuti oleh 49 karyawan dalam dua batch pelaksanaan, yang terdiri dari 23 peserta pada batch pertama dan 26 peserta pada batch kedua, sebagai wujud komitmen Perusahaan dalam membangun budaya keberlanjutan melalui partisipasi.

- Support for Eliminating AIDS, Tuberculosis, and Malaria in South Kalimantan
TIA and CK supported the South Kalimantan provincial initiative to eliminate AIDS, tuberculosis, and malaria by 2030. The contributions of both companies include prevention activities, Voluntary Counselling and Testing (VCT) services in the workplace, and health education for senior high school students. These efforts are aligned with national health achievement targets and the SDGs, while also strengthening community awareness and prevention capabilities against these diseases.

- Maintenance of the Teaching Factory and Support for Rumah Autis
In the education sector, support was provided through the maintenance of Teaching Factory facilities as well as school supply assistance for SDN Uhak and Lurang, Maluku through CKB. The commitment to inclusion was also realized through ongoing support to Rumah Autis with a fund allocation of Rp 8 million to support operations and learning needs.

- Stunting Prevention
Stunting prevention programs were implemented in Sememi, Surabaya (75 children) with support amounting to Rp 30 million, as well as at Puskesmas Sukapura, Cilincing, North Jakarta (53 children) through the provision of nutritious supplementary food for affected children. This program strengthens efforts to improve the quality of future generations in the Company's operational areas.

- Women's Empowerment through MSMEs
Local economic empowerment was also carried out through the purchase and distribution of sasirangan batik products from 11 women in Bunati Village, Sungai Loban District, Tanah Bumbu Regency, as a form of support for women MSMEs and community-based economic strengthening.

- Food Always In The House (FAITH) Program
Through the Food Always In The House (FAITH) program, ABM encourages active employee involvement in volunteering activities focused on urban farming practices on limited land, in collaboration with Kebun Kumara. This program includes planting food crops, medicinal plants, and fast-harvest crops, accompanied by education on planting media and simple garden management techniques that can be applied independently.
