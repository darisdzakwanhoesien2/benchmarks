Untuk memastikan penerapan prinsip hak asasi manusia secara konsisten, ABM memastikan bahwa karyawan memperoleh pemahaman yang memadai terkait perlindungan hak asasi manusia melalui sosialisasi peraturan perusahaan dan kode etik yang mencakup klausul penghormatan terhadap hak masyarakat adat dan komunitas lokal.

Dalam konteks wilayah operasional, ABM memastikan bahwa seluruh area operasi tidak bersinggungan di dalam kawasan masyarakat adat, kawasan lindung berdasarkan Kategori Manajemen Kawasan Lindung IUCN, Konvensi Ramsar, maupun legislasi nasional. Meskipun demikian, ABM tetap menetapkan dan mengelola kawasan perlindungan keanekaragaman hayati di sejumlah wilayah operasional sebagai bentuk komitmen terhadap kelestarian lingkungan.

Pendekatan perlindungan hak asasi manusia di ABM, baik di lingkungan kerja maupun di sepanjang rantai pasok, diatur melalui kode etik, Peraturan Perusahaan, serta klausul dalam perjanjian kerja dan pakta integritas yang wajib dipatuhi oleh seluruh karyawan dan pemasok. Implementasi kebijakan tersebut secara berkala ditinjau dengan mengacu pada ketentuan regulasi yang berlaku.

ABM juga memastikan bahwa 100% petugas keamanan, termasuk yang berasal dari pihak ketiga, telah menerima pelatihan resmi terkait kebijakan hak asasi manusia dan prosedur pengamanan yang berlaku. The assistance provided by ABM is focused on programs that are relevant to community needs, particularly those related to strengthening local livelihoods that have been sustained from generation to generation, as well as preserving local social and cultural values.

During the reporting period, there were no identified incidents of violations of the rights of Indigenous Peoples or local communities, as evidenced by the absence of reports through the WBS, media coverage, or direct submissions from stakeholders.

To ensure consistent application of human rights principles, ABM ensures that employees obtain adequate understanding related to human rights protection through the socialization of Company Regulations and the Code of Conduct, which includes clauses on respect for the rights of Indigenous Peoples and local communities.

In the context of its operational areas, ABM ensures that all operational areas do not overlap within Indigenous Peoples' areas, protected areas based on IUCN Protected Area Management Categories, the Ramsar Convention, or national legislation. Nevertheless, ABM continues to designate and manage biodiversity protection areas in several operational areas as a form of commitment to environmental sustainability.

ABM's approach to human rights protection, both in the workplace and throughout the supply chain, is regulated through the Code of Conduct, Company Regulations, as well as clauses in employment agreements and integrity pacts that must be complied with by all employees and suppliers. The implementation of these policies is periodically reviewed with reference to applicable regulatory provisions.

ABM also ensures that 100% of security personnel, including those from third parties, have received official training related to the human rights policy and applicable security procedures. This requirement is applied equally to ensure that all security activities are carried out with respect for human rights across all operational areas. [GRI 410-1]
