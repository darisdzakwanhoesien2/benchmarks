# Komitmen ABM terhadap Keselamatan Kerja

Keselamatan dan kesehatan kerja merupakan prioritas utama ABM dalam setiap aktivitas operasional. Pada tahun pelaporan, terjadi satu insiden Lost Time Injury (LTI) di salah satu lokasi operasional SSB, di mana seorang pekerja mengalami cedera pada bagian kepala akibat terpentalnya jig dan jack hydraulic saat proses pembongkaran komponen spring coil. Peristiwa ini menjadi pengingat bahwa setiap aktivitas kerja mengandung risiko yang harus dikelola secara disiplin dan konsisten.

ABM memandang insiden tersebut sebagai pembelajaran bersama untuk memperkuat budaya keselamatan di seluruh lini operasi. Sebagai tindak lanjut, Perusahaan melaksanakan safety stand down meeting, melakukan rekayasa ulang pada jig pembongkaran spring coil dengan penambahan safety guard dan pengikatan jig ke lantai kerja, serta memasang safety sign peringatan bahaya di area terkait. Selain itu, dilakukan identifikasi kesesuaian jig dengan jenis pekerjaan serta proses inquiry job review.

ABM juga memberikan coaching kepada operator dan pengawas guna memastikan pemahaman risiko dan kepatuhan terhadap prosedur kerja aman. Melalui langkah-langkah tersebut, ABM menegaskan komitmennya untuk mencegah kejadian serupa dan terus menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang aman, sehat, dan saling peduli bagi seluruh insan perusahaan.

# ABM's Commitment to Occupational Safety

Occupational safety and health is ABM's top priority in every operational activity. In the reporting year, one Lost Time Injury (LTI) incident occurred at one of SSB's operational sites, where a worker sustained a head injury after a jig and hydraulic jack were ejected during the dismantling process of a spring coil component. This incident serves as a reminder that every work activity carries risks that must be managed with discipline and consistency.

ABM views the incident as a shared learning opportunity to strengthen the safety culture across all operational lines. As a follow-up, the Company conducted a safety stand down meeting, re-engineered the spring coil dismantling jig by adding a safety guard and anchoring the jig to the workshop floor, and installed safety warning signs in the relevant area. In addition, identification was carried out regarding the suitability of the jig for the type of work, as well as an inquiry job review process.

ABM also provided coaching to operators and supervisors to ensure their understanding of risks and compliance with safe work procedures. Through these measures, ABM reaffirms its commitment to preventing similar incidents and to continuously creating a safe, healthy, and caring work environment for all members of the Company.
