Direksi (BOD) melakukan pengawasan atas kinerja keberlanjutan Perusahaan serta memastikan akuntabilitas manajemen terhadap pencapaian target ESG. Dalam pelaksanaannya, Direksi mendelegasikan implementasi ESG kepada Chief Information Officer (CIO) sebagai Chief sustainability untuk memastikan efektivitas penerapan dan integrasi pertimbangan keberlanjutan di seluruh lini bisnis. CIO bersama Sustainability Head secara bulanan menyampaikan perkembangan implementasi ESG dan pengelolaan dampak ekonomi, lingkungan, dan sosial kepada Direksi dan Komite ESG sebagai bagian dari mekanisme pelaporan internal.

Sustainability Head yang didukung oleh ESG Working Group bertanggung jawab atas pelaksanaan dan pengelolaan rutin inisiatif keberlanjutan, di bawah supervisi CIO dan Komite ESG. Koordinasi implementasi ESG di tingkat operasional dilakukan melalui pertemuan ESG Working Group yang diselenggarakan secara mingguan untuk memantau perkembangan program, konsolidasi data ESG, serta tindak lanjut atas inisiatif keberlanjutan di seluruh entitas ABM. Sementara itu, fungsi Corporate Communication bertanggung jawab atas penyusunan, penyampaian kepada pemangku kepentingan, serta publikasi Laporan Keberlanjutan. Melalui struktur ini, ABM memastikan bahwa dampak ESG dapat diidentifikasi, dikelola, dan dievaluasi secara berkesinambungan, sehingga informasi ESG yang disajikan dalam Laporan Keberlanjutan dapat dipertanggungjawabkan.

Laporan Keberlanjutan 2025 telah ditelaah dan disetujui oleh Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, termasuk persetujuan atas topik material serta data dan informasi yang diungkapkan. Proses persetujuan dilakukan melalui peninjauan menyeluruh untuk memastikan akurasi, transparansi, dan kesesuaian dengan standar pelaporan yang berlaku, serta mempertimbangkan masukan pemangku kepentingan dalam penentuan topik material yang relevan.

The Board of Directors (BOD) oversees the Company's sustainability performance and ensures management accountability for achieving ESG targets. In its implementation, the BOD delegates the responsibility of ESG implementation to the Chief Information Officer (CIO) to ensure the effective application and integration of sustainability considerations throughout all business lines. The CIO and the Head of Sustainability regularly report on the progress of ESG implementation and the management of economic, environmental, and social impacts to the Board of Directors and the ESG Committee as part of the internal reporting mechanism.

The Head of Sustainability, supported by the ESG Working Group, bears responsibility for the implementation and ongoing management of sustainability initiatives, under the oversight of the CIO and the ESG Committee. The coordination of ESG implementation at the operational level is conducted through biweekly meetings of the ESG Working Group to track program progress, compile ESG data, and follow up on sustainability initiatives across all ABM entities. recurrently, the Corporate Communication function is responsible for preparing, delivering to stakeholders, and publication of the Sustainability Report. Through this structure, ABM ensures that ESG impacts can be identified, managed, and evaluated continuously, so that the ESG information presented in the Sustainability Report can be accounted for.

The 2025 Sustainability Report has been reviewed and approved by the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors, including the approval of material topics as well as the data and information disclosed. The approval process entails a comprehensive review to ensure accuracy, transparency, and compliance with applicable reporting standards, while duly considering stakeholder input in the determination of pertinent material topics.
