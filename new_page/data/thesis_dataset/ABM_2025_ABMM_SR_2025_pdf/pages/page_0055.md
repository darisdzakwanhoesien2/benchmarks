Melalui langkah ini, ABM tidak hanya memastikan kepatuhan terhadap regulasi yang berlaku, tetapi juga berkontribusi dalam perlindungan lingkungan global, khususnya dalam menjaga keberlanjutan lapisan atmosfer dan mendukung upaya mitigasi perubahan iklim. [GRI 305-6] [SEOJK F.12]

Dalam upaya menurunkan emisi, ABM menerapkan berbagai inisiatif mitigasi di seluruh unit bisnis, antara lain penggunaan biodiesel (B40), peningkatan efisiensi bahan bakar melalui standarisasi parameter operasional, pemanfaatan energi surya dan energi biogas, digitalisasi pelaporan lingkungan, serta rehabilitasi dan reklamasi lahan pascatambang. Di tingkat operasional, CK mengoperasikan Pembangkit Listrik Tenaga Surya (PLTS) off-grid dengan sistem baterai (CBESS) di site CK KIM, Provinsi Jambi, untuk mendukung kebutuhan listrik fasilitas operasional sekaligus mengurangi emisi tidak langsung dan biaya energi jangka panjang. [GRI 305-5] [SEOJK F.12] [NDAQ E1]

Selain itu, penerapan biodiesel B40 pada alat berat dan kendaraan operasional turut berkontribusi pada penurunan intensitas emisi dibandingkan penggunaan solar konvensional. Optimalisasi rute dan ritase melalui pengaturan jarak tempuh dan waktu tunggu alat angkut, termasuk pemanfaatan sistem digital seperti menggunakan MineStar, juga dilakukan untuk meningkatkan efisiensi operasional dan menekan konsumsi bahan bakar. [SEOJK F.11] [NDAQ E1]

Pengelolaan emisi ABM diselaraskan dengan komitmen nasional dan internasional, termasuk target penurunan emisi Indonesia sebesar 31,89% serta target Net Zero Emissions pada tahun 2060, sebagaimana tercantum dalam Nationally Determined Contribution (NDC). ABM juga mendukung tujuan Perjanjian Paris untuk membatasi kenaikan suhu global di bawah 2°C. Dengan menjadikan tahun 2022 sebagai tahun dasar (baseline), ABM menetapkan target penurunan emisi Gas Rumah Kaca (GRK) sebesar 5% yang akan dicapai pada tahun 2030.

Pemantauan dan evaluasi emisi dilakukan secara berkala oleh fungsi Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) di masing-masing entitas anak. Hasil pemantauan tersebut dilaporkan secara rutin kepada manajemen dan Direksi sebagai bagian dari mekanisme tata kelola serta evaluasi Key Performance Indicators (KPI) lingkungan. Through this stage, ABM not only ensures compliance with relevant regulations but also contributes to global environmental preservation, particularly in maintaining the sustainability of atmospheric layers and supporting initiatives aimed at mitigating climate change. [GRI 305-6] [SEOJK F.12]

To reduce emissions, ABM implements various mitigation initiatives across its business units, including the utilization of biodiesel (B40), improving fuel efficiency through the standardization of operational parameters, utilizing solar energy and biogas, digitalizing environmental reporting, and rehabilitating and reclaiming post-mining lands. At the operational level, CK manages an off-grid Solar Power Plant (PLTS) equipped with a battery system (CBESS) at the CK KIM site in Jambi Province to support the site's electricity needs, whilst concurrently reducing indirect emissions and long-term energy costs. [GRI 305-5] [SEOJK F.12] [NDAQ E1]

Furthermore, the implementation of B40 biodiesel in heavy machinery and operational vehicles further facilitates a reduction in emission intensity relative to traditional diesel fuel. Additionally, route optimization and load scheduling aimed at managing travel distances and wait times for transportation equipment, including the use of digital systems such as MineStar are implemented to improve operational efficiency and reduce fuel consumption. [SEOJK F.11] [NDAQ E1]

ABM emission management aligns with both national and international commitments, including Indonesia's emission reduction target of 31.89% and the Net Zero Emissions target by 2060, as outlined in the Nationally Determined Contribution (NDC). Additionally, ABM also supports the objectives of the Paris Agreement to limit global temperature rise below 2°C. Using 2022 as the baseline year, ABM sets a target to reduce greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions by 5% by 2030.

Monitoring and evaluating emissions are regularly carried out by the Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) functions at each subsidiary. The results are routinely reported to management and the Board of Directors as part of governance mechanisms and environmental Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).
