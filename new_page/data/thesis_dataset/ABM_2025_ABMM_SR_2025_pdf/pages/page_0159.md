# Pengetahuan Kolektif Badan Tata Kelola Tertinggi tentang Keberlanjutan [GRI 2-17]

ABM memastikan Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi memiliki pengetahuan kolektif yang memadai untuk mengawasi dan mengarahkan kinerja keberlanjutan Perusahaan. Penguatan pengetahuan tersebut dibangun melalui berbagai pedoman, kebijakan, dan prosedur yang menjadi acuan dalam memahami isu keberlanjutan, tata kelola, etika, serta risiko operasional.

- Pedoman Etika dan Perilaku memberikan dasar pemahaman mengenai etika bisnis dan perilaku kerja yang relevan dengan agenda keberlanjutan. Kewajiban seluruh anggota Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi untuk menandatangani pedoman ini setiap tahun memastikan pemahaman yang konsisten terhadap nilai, standar etika, dan ekspektasi tata kelola ABM.
- Board Manual memperkuat pemahaman Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi terkait struktur tata kelola, mekanisme rapat, proses pengambilan keputusan, serta fungsi pengawasan. Dokumen ini membantu badan tata kelola tertinggi memahami implikasi keberlanjutan terhadap operasi, risiko, dan strategi perusahaan.
- Peraturan Perusahaan menjelaskan hak dan kewajiban, standar perilaku, serta ketentuan ketenagakerjaan yang relevan dengan aspek sosial keberlanjutan. Dengan regulasi yang sudah diselaraskan dengan nilai inti Perusahaan, Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi memiliki pondasi yang jelas dalam memahami implikasi keberlanjutan terhadap kinerja tenaga kerja.
- Kebijakan Perusahaan mencerminkan komitmen ABM terhadap praktik GCG dan pelaksanaan kegiatan bisnis yang sesuai prinsip keberlanjutan. Pembaruan kebijakan di berbagai area, seperti Business Development Logistics, Engineering, Data Science, dan Government Relations, mendukung pemahaman Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi terhadap dinamika isu keberlanjutan lintas fungsi.
- Prosedur Perusahaan (SOP) membantu Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi memahami bagaimana prinsip keberlanjutan dioperasionalkan dalam kegiatan sehari-hari. Dengan lebih dari 113 SOP yang diperbarui secara berkala, badan tata kelola tertinggi memiliki referensi yang memadai untuk memonitor kepatuhan terhadap standar operasional, aspek keselamatan, efisiensi sumber daya, dan pengelolaan risiko.

# The Collective Knowledge of the Highest Governance Body on Sustainability [GRI 2-17]

ABM ensures that the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors possess adequate collective knowledge to oversee and direct the company's sustainability performance. This knowledge is strengthened through various guidelines, policies, and procedures that serve as benchmarks in understanding sustainability issues, governance, ethics, and operational risks.

- The Ethics and Conduct Guidelines provide a foundation for understanding business ethics and work behavior relevant to the sustainability agenda. The obligation for all members of the Board of Commissioners and Directors to annually endorse this guideline each year ensures a consistent understanding of core values, ethical standards, and governance expectations within ABM.
- The Board Manual strengthens the understanding of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors concerning governance structure, meeting mechanisms, decision-making procedures, and oversight functions. This document facilitates the highest governance body in understanding the implications of sustainability on operational activities, risk management, and company strategy.
- The Company Regulations outline rights and obligations, behavioral standards, and employment provisions pertinent to social sustainability aspects. With regulations aligned with the Company's core values, the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors possess a clear understanding of the implications of sustainability on workforce performance.
- The Company Policy reflects ABM's commitment to GCG practices and the implementation of business activities in accordance with sustainability principles. Amendments to policies across diverse sectors, including Business Development Logistics, Engineering, Data Science, and Government Relations, assist the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors in understanding the dynamics of cross-functional sustainability issues.
- The Company Procedures (SOP) help the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors in understanding how sustainability principles are operationalized in daily activities. With over 113 Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) regularly updated, the highest governance body has enough reference materials to monitor compliance with operational standards, safety aspects, resource efficiency, and risk management.
