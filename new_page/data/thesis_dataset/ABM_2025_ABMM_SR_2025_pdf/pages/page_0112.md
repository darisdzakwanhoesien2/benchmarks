BOTB 2025 dilaksanakan melalui proses seleksi dan pengembangan berjenjang, mulai dari penjaringan 30 besar peserta hingga penetapan 15 besar dan pemenang utama. Peserta mengikuti rangkaian pembinaan yang mencakup pengembangan fisik, sikap dan perilaku, kepemimpinan, serta penguatan kompetensi teknis sesuai dengan fungsi masing-masing, termasuk operasi, pemeliharaan peralatan, OSHE, dan layanan teknis. Program ini juga menekankan disiplin, tanggung jawab, daya saing, serta penerapan praktik pertambangan yang baik melalui kombinasi pembelajaran teori, evaluasi kinerja, dan battle knowledge.

Melalui BOTB 2025, PT Cipta Kridatama menunjukkan komitmennya dalam membangun sumber daya manusia unggul, berkarakter, dan siap menghadapi tantangan operasional, sekaligus memastikan keberlanjutan kinerja Perusahaan dalam jangka panjang.

BOTB 2025 was implemented through a tiered selection and development process, starting from screening the Top 30 participants up to determining the Top 15 and the overall winner. Participants undertook a series of coaching activities covering physical development, attitudes and behavior, leadership, and strengthening technical competencies according to their respective functions, including operations, equipment maintenance, OSHE, and technical services. The program also emphasizes discipline, responsibility, competitiveness, and the implementation of good mining practices through a combination of theoretical learning, performance evaluation, and battle knowledge.

Through BOTB 2025, Cipta Kridatama demonstrates its commitment to building superior human resources with strong character and readiness to face operational challenges, while also ensuring the Company's long-term performance sustainability.

# Rekrutmen Lokal Panji dari BOTB untuk Knowledge Transfer dalam Mining Academy

Keberhasilan program BOTB tercermin dari lahirnya talenta internal yang kemudian berperan sebagai penggerak pembelajaran di organisasi atau yang disebut dengan Lokal Panji. Mereka menjadi pengajar dan mentor bagi karyawan lain melalui Mining Academy. Pendekatan ini memperkuat budaya berbagi pengetahuan, meningkatkan produktivitas operasional, serta mendorong peningkatan utilisasi dan efisiensi kerja di lapangan.

Komitmen CK sejalan dengan strategi induk Perusahaan, ABM. yang menempatkan manusia sebagai fondasi utama dalam membangun bisnis yang berkelanjutan. Sepanjang tahun pelaporan, pengembangan kompetensi dilakukan secara konsisten sebagai bagian dari budaya perusahaan, bukan sekadar program jangka pendek. Melalui inisiatif ini, CK meyakini bahwa keberlanjutan industri pertambangan hanya dapat dicapai dengan menumbuhkan pemimpin-pemimpin yang lahir dari lapangan, berintegritas, dan siap menghadapi tantangan masa depan.

# Local Panji Recruitment from BOTB for Knowledge Transfer in the Mining Academy

The success of the BOTB program is reflected in the emergence of internal talents who subsequently serve as learning drivers within the organization, referred to as Local Panji. They act as instructors and mentors for other employees through the Mining Academy. This approach strengthens a knowledge-sharing culture, improves operational productivity, and encourages increased utilization and work efficiency in the field.

CK's commitment is aligned with the parent company's strategy, ABM, which places people as the primary foundation in building a sustainable business. Throughout the reporting year, competency development was carried out consistently as part of the Company's culture, rather than merely a short-term program. Through this initiative, CK believes that the sustainability of the mining industry can only be achieved by fostering leaders who emerge from the field, have integrity, and are prepared to face future challenges.
