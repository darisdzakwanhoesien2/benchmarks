# Perubahan signifikan pada organisasi dan rantai pasokannya [SEOJK C.6]

Selama periode pelaporan, tidak terdapat perubahan signifikan pada struktur organisasi maupun rantai pasokan ABM. Meskipun perusahaan memperluas portofolio bisnis dan mengembangkan ekosistem multi-sektoral, termasuk penambahan dua entitas baru di sektor pertambangan, perubahan tersebut bersifat penguatan kapasitas usaha dan tidak berdampak material terhadap stabilitas operasional maupun alur rantai pasokan yang telah berjalan.

# Significant changes to the organization and its supply chain [SEOJK C.6]

During the reporting period, there were no significant changes to ABM's organizational structure or supply chain. Although the company expanded its business portfolio and developed a multi-sector ecosystem, including the addition of two new entities in the mining sector, these changes were aimed at strengthening business capacity and had no material impact on operational stability or the existing supply chain.

# Keanggotaan dalam Asosiasi [GRI 2-28][SEOJK C.5]

## Membership of Associations

Sebagai bagian dari komitmen terhadap tata kelola dan praktik keberlanjutan, ABM berpartisipasi dalam sejumlah asosiasi industri dan profesional. Keanggotaan tersebut mencakup Asosiasi Emiten Indonesia, Asosiasi Sekretaris Perusahaan Indonesia (ICSA) sejak 2017, Indonesia Center for Sustainable Development (ICSD) sejak 2017, serta Women in Mining &amp; Energy (WIME) sejak 2022. Informasi tambahan mengenai keanggotaan anak perusahaan disajikan dalam Laporan Tahunan.

ABM juga berkontribusi dalam pengembangan standar dan panduan keberlanjutan sektor pertambangan batu bara melalui kerja sama dengan Asosiasi Pertambangan Batu bara Indonesia (APBI-ICMA) sejak 2009, CDP Asia Pacific sejak 2024, dan Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) sejak 2017.

As part of its commitment to governance and sustainability practices, ABM participates in several industry and professional associations. These memberships include the Indonesian Issuers Association, the Indonesian Corporate Secretaries Association (ICSA) since 2017, the Indonesia Center for Sustainable Development (ICSD) since 2017, and Women in Mining &amp; Energy (WIME) since 2022. Additional information regarding subsidiary memberships is presented in the Annual Report.

ABM also contributes to the development of sustainability standards and guidelines for the coal mining sector through its collaboration with the Indonesian Coal Mining Association (APBI-ICMA) since 2009, CDP Asia Pacific since 2024, and the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) since 2017.
