# Klaster Pertanian Berkelanjutan melalui Biochar Three in One (Biotron)

[GRI 203-1; GRI 413-1; GRI 413-2]

Melalui CK, ABM mengembangkan klaster pertanian berkelanjutan berbasis Biotron, teknologi pemulihan tanah. Berkolaborasi dengan BBPP Binuang, ABM memberikan pelatihan, demonstrasi lapangan, dan dukungan teknis bagi petani. Biotron menjawab tantangan kelangkaan pupuk, degradasi tanah, dan penurunan produktivitas, serta menawarkan solusi agroforestri berskala luas untuk pemulihan lahan jangka panjang, termasuk lahan pascatambang. Hasil awal menunjukkan peningkatan kesehatan tanah, peningkatan hasil panen, pengurangan ketergantungan pada pupuk sintetis, dan peningkatan ketahanan petani.

# Sustainable Agriculture Cluster through Biochar Three in One (Biotron)

[GRI 203-1; GRI 413-1; GRI 413-2]

Through CK, ABM developed a sustainable agriculture cluster based on Biotron, a soil restoration technology. In collaboration with BBPP Binuang, ABM provided training, field demonstrations, and technical support for farmers. Biotron addresses challenges related to fertilizer scarcity, soil degradation, and declining productivity, and offers large-scale agroforestry solutions for long-term land restoration, including post-mining land. Initial results indicate improved soil health, increased crop yields, reduced dependence on synthetic fertilizers, and enhanced farmer resilience.
