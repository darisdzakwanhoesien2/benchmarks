# Metrics &amp; Targets

Currently, ABM has established the ABM Target 2025, which includes a commitment to reduce total energy consumption by 15% and total carbon emissions by 5% across all subsidiaries and operational activities of the Company. This target serves as a reference in implementing energy efficiency initiatives and emission reduction strategies throughout the Group.

Seiring dengan perkembangan strategi keberlanjutan dan transisi energi, ABM saat ini sedang mengembangkan target keberlanjutan yang baru untuk periode selanjutnya (tahun 2026). Target tersebut akan disusun dengan mempertimbangkan kinerja historis, potensi operasional, serta perkembangan regulasi dan standar pelaporan keberlanjutan yang berlaku, guna mendukung pencapaian kinerja lingkungan yang berkelanjutan dalam jangka panjang.

In conjunction with the development of sustainability strategies and the energy transition, ABM is currently establishing new sustainability targets for the upcoming period (year 2026). These targets will be formulated with consideration of historical performance, operational potential, in addition to changes in relevant regulations and standards for sustainability reporting, to support the achievement of sustainable environmental performance over the long term.
