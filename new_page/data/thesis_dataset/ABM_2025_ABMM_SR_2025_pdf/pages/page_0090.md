Seluruh program pelatihan K3 juga mendukung penerapan hierarchy of control, yang mencakup eliminasi, substitusi, pengendalian teknis, pengendalian administratif, dan penggunaan Alat Pelindung Diri (APD), guna memastikan pencegahan risiko dilakukan secara sistematis dan berlapis.

Beberapa program pelatihan yang diberikan kepada karyawan mencakup:
- Penanganan kecelakaan kerja
- Manajemen tingkat kelelahan
- Workshop Lock Out Tag Out (LOTO)
- Kepemimpinan Keselamatan yang Efektif
- Pelatihan dan sertifikasi Sistem Manajemen Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (SMK3)
- Refreshment Sistem Manajemen Keselamatan Pertambangan (SMKP)
- Pelatihan dan sertifikasi implementasi dan audit SMKP
- Semiloka dasar-dasar K3 pertambangan
- Semiloka teknik investigasi kecelakaan
- Seminar Zero Accident
- Diklat dan uji kompetensi POP/POM
- Workshop HIRADC, dan CPR
- Praktik dasar kerja aman

Pelatihan tersebut diberikan kepada seluruh tenaga kerja dalam lingkup operasional ABM, baik karyawan tetap maupun tenaga kerja non-karyawan, termasuk kontraktor dan tenaga kerja alih daya. ABM memastikan bahwa seluruh pekerja yang berada di bawah pengendalian operasional Perusahaan memperoleh akses terhadap program pelatihan yang relevan guna mendukung peningkatan kompetensi serta penerapan standar keselamatan dan kinerja yang konsisten di seluruh area kerja.

Pelatihan dilaksanakan secara berkelanjutan dengan mempertimbangkan karakteristik risiko masing-masing fungsi kerja dan area operasional, serta menjadi salah satu indikator evaluasi kinerja K3 di tingkat unit kerja dan korporasi.

All OHS training programs also support the implementation of the hierarchy of control, which includes elimination, substitution, engineering controls, administrative controls, and the use of personal protective equipment (PPE), to ensure that risk prevention is carried out systematically and in layers.

Several training programs provided to employees include:
- Handling of occupational accidents
- Fatigue management
- LOTO (Lock Out Tag Out) Workshop
- Effective Safety Leadership
- Training and certification on the Occupational Safety and Health Management System (SMK3)
- Refresher on the Mining Safety Management System (SMKP)
- Training and certification on SMKP implementation and audit
- Basic mining OHS workshop
- Accident investigation techniques workshop
- Zero Accident seminar
- POP/POM training and competency testing
- HIRADC and CPR workshop
- Basic safe work practices

The training was provided to all workers within ABM's operational scope, both permanent employees and non-employee workers, including contractors and outsourced workers. ABM ensures that all workers under the Company's operational control receive access to relevant training programs to support competency improvement and the consistent application of safety standards and performance across all work areas.

Training is conducted on an ongoing basis by considering the risk characteristics of each work function and operational area, and serves as one of the indicators for evaluating OHS performance at both the work-unit and corporate levels.
