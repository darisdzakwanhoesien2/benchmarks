# Pertimbangan Pemangku Kepentingan untuk Penentuan Remunerasi

- Pandangan pemegang saham disampaikan melalui agenda persetujuan remunerasi dalam RUPS dan hasil pemungutan suara menjadi bagian dari keputusan final Perusahaan.
- ABM menggunakan pihak eksternal independen untuk analisis benchmarking dalam penentuan remunerasi menggunakan Mercer. ABM memastikan pihak tersebut bekerja secara independen tanpa konflik kepentingan dengan Direksi, Dewan Komisaris, atau manajemen senior.

# Rasio Jumlah Kompensasi [GRI 2-21]

Informasi mengenai rasio total kompensasi tahunan individu dengan kompensasi tertinggi dibandingkan median kompensasi karyawan, serta rasio persentase kenaikan kompensasi tahunan, tidak disajikan dalam Laporan Keberlanjutan ini tetapi diungkapkan dalam Laporan Tahunan ABM sebagai bagian dari pengungkapan tata kelola halaman 261.

Meskipun demikian, ABM tetap berkomitmen mengelola sumber daya manusia secara adil, transparan, dan sesuai dengan peraturan ketenagakerjaan yang berlaku. ABM memastikan bahwa struktur kompensasi disusun berdasarkan prinsip kesetaraan internal, evaluasi jabatan, benchmarking pasar, serta mekanisme penilaian kinerja yang diterapkan secara konsisten bagi seluruh karyawan.

Dalam penentuan kompensasi, ABM menggunakan metodologi penghitungan total kompensasi tahunan yang mencakup gaji pokok, tunjangan tetap, serta manfaat lain sesuai kebijakan perusahaan. Data dihimpun melalui sistem penggajian terpusat untuk menjamin akurasi dan konsistensi pelaporan.

ABM akan terus mengevaluasi kelayakan pengungkapan rasio kompensasi di masa mendatang dengan mempertimbangkan perkembangan regulasi, praktik terbaik, dan permintaan dari pemangku kepentingan.

# Stakeholder Considerations for Remuneration Determination

- Shareholders' views are conveyed through the remuneration approval agenda at the GMS, and the voting results become part of the company's final decision.
- ABM employs an independent external party for benchmarking analysis to determine remuneration, using Mercer. ABM ensures this party operates independently, without conflicts of interest with the Board of Directors, the Board of Commissioners, or senior management.

# Total Compensation Ratio [GRI 2-21]

Information concerning the ratio of an individual's total annual compensation to the highest compensation in comparison with the median employee compensation, as well as the percentage increase in annual compensation, is not included in this Sustainability Report; however, it is disclosed in ABM's Annual Report as part of governance disclosures page 261

Nevertheless, ABM remains committed to managing human resources fairly, transparent, and in accordance with relevant labor regulations. ABM ensures that the compensation structure is designed based on the principles of internal equity, job evaluation, market benchmarking, and performance assessment mechanisms, which are applied consistently across all employees.

In determining compensation, ABM employs a methodology for calculating total annual compensation that encompasses base salary, fixed allowances, and additional benefits in accordance with company policies. Data is collected through a centralized payroll system to ensure accuracy and consistency in reporting.

ABM will continue to evaluate the appropriateness of disclosing compensation ratio disclosures in the future, considering regulatory developments, best practices, and stakeholder demands.
