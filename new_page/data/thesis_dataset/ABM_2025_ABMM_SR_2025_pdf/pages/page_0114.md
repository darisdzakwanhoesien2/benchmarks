# Menanamkan Integritas melalui Pelatihan Code of Conduct

Sebagai bagian dari penguatan tata kelola dan budaya integritas, ABM menyelenggarakan pelatihan Code of Conduct bagi seluruh karyawan melalui platform Learning Management System (LMS). Pelatihan ini dirancang sebagai mandatory course untuk memastikan pemahaman yang konsisten atas nilai, prinsip etika, serta standar perilaku yang berlaku di seluruh Grup ABM.

Pelatihan Code of Conduct dilaksanakan secara daring dan terstruktur, mencakup rangkaian pretest, modul pembelajaran, post-test, dan evaluasi, dengan standar kelulusan minimum yang ditetapkan. Akses pelatihan tersedia melalui web maupun aplikasi seluler, sehingga memungkinkan karyawan mengikuti pembelajaran secara fleksibel namun tetap terpantau.

Melalui pendekatan pembelajaran digital ini, ABM memperkuat kesadaran etika, kepatuhan, dan tanggung jawab individu dalam menjalankan peran masing-masing, sekaligus memastikan bahwa prinsip integritas menjadi fondasi perilaku dan pengambilan keputusan di seluruh lini organisasi.

# Instilling Integrity Through Code of Conduct Training

As part of strengthening governance and a culture of integrity, ABM organized Code of Conduct training for all employees through the Learning Management System (LMS) platform. This training is designed as a mandatory course to ensure consistent understanding of the values, ethical principles, and behavioral standards applicable throughout the ABM Group.

The Code of Conduct training is delivered online in a structured manner, comprising a series of pretests, learning modules, post-tests, and evaluations, with a minimum passing standard established. Training access is available via the web and mobile application, enabling employees to participate flexibly while remaining monitored.

Through this digital learning approach, ABM strengthens ethical awareness, compliance, and individual responsibility in carrying out each respective role, while also ensuring that integrity principles serve as the foundation for behavior and decision-making across all lines of the organization.
