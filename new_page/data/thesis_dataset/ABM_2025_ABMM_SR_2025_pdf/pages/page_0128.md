ABM

Bagi karyawan di remote area, waktu bebas tetap diberikan di luar jam kerja, dan lembur tidak pernah diwajibkan, terutama jika kondisi kesehatan atau keselamatan tidak memungkinkan. Pelaksanaan lembur hanya dilakukan berdasarkan persetujuan karyawan.

Pengelolaan jam kerja dilakukan secara wajar, tanpa paksaan, dan memastikan karyawan mendapatkan waktu istirahat yang layak.

- Penghargaan terhadap konsen karyawan

ABM juga memastikan proses rekrutmen, promosi, dan mutasi dilakukan berdasarkan kesepakatan kedua belah pihak tanpa unsur paksaan atau ancaman.

Sebagai penguatan atas komitmen terhadap perlindungan hak asasi manusia, ABM memastikan bahwa seluruh operasi dan pemasok Perusahaan tidak berada pada risiko signifikan terhadap insiden kerja paksa atau wajib kerja. Seluruh kegiatan usaha dijalankan dengan mengacu pada kebijakan hak asasi manusia dan ketenagakerjaan yang berlaku, serta prinsip hubungan kerja yang adil dan saling menghormati di seluruh rantai nilai ABM. Selama periode pelaporan, tidak terdapat insiden pelanggaran yang teridentifikasi terkait kerja paksa maupun pelanggaran hak masyarakat adat.

Dalam pengelolaan jam kerja, ABM memastikan tidak memberlakukan lembur secara berlebihan dan senantiasa mematuhi ketentuan mengenai waktu kerja dan waktu istirahat. Perusahaan tidak melakukan penahanan dokumen pribadi karyawan dan menjunjung tinggi prinsip kepercayaan serta profesionalisme dalam seluruh proses administrasi kepegawaian. Bagi karyawan yang bertugas di wilayah terpencil, ABM memberikan fleksibilitas waktu di luar jam kerja dan memastikan bahwa keputusan untuk melakukan lembur sepenuhnya bersifat sukarela dengan mempertimbangkan faktor kesehatan, keselamatan, dan kondisi lingkungan kerja.

ABM memastikan bahwa seluruh proses rekrutmen, promosi, dan mutasi karyawan dilaksanakan berdasarkan asas kesepakatan bersama tanpa unsur paksaan dan ancaman, baik di seluruh operasi maupun di sepanjang rantai pasok Perusahaan. Pendekatan ini menjadi bagian dari upaya Perusahaan dalam menciptakan hubungan kerja yang adil, setara, dan berkelanjutan.

For employees in remote areas, free time is still provided outside working hours, and overtime is never mandatory, particularly if health or safety conditions do not allow it. Overtime is carried out only based on employee consent.

Working hours are managed reasonably, without coercion, and ensure employees receive adequate rest time.

- Respecting employee consent

ABM also ensures that recruitment, promotion, and transfer processes are carried out based on mutual agreement between both parties, without any element of coercion or threats.

To further strengthen its commitment to protecting human rights, ABM ensures that all of the Company's operations and suppliers are not at significant risk of forced labor or compulsory labor incidents. All business activities are carried out with reference to applicable human rights and labor policies, as well as fair and mutually respectful employment relationship principles throughout the ABM value chain. During the reporting period, there were no identified incidents of violations related to forced labor or violations of Indigenous Peoples' rights.

In managing working hours, ABM ensures that it does not impose excessive overtime and consistently complies with provisions regarding working time and rest time. The Company does not withhold employees' personal documents and upholds the principles of trust and professionalism in all personnel administration processes. For employees assigned to remote areas, ABM provides time flexibility outside working hours and ensures that decisions to work overtime are entirely voluntary, taking into account health, safety, and work environment conditions.

ABM ensures that all recruitment, promotion, and employee transfer processes are carried out based on the principle of mutual agreement without coercion or threats, both across all operations and throughout the Company's supply chain. This approach forms part of the Company's efforts to create fair, equitable, and sustainable employment relationships.
