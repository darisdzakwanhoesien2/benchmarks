ABM

Melalui penerapan prinsip materialitas ganda, ABM menilai secara komprehensif keterkaitan antara aktivitas bisnis dan isu-isu ESG. Pendekatan ini mencakup evaluasi terhadap dampak material kegiatan usaha ABM terhadap lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola, serta dampak material isu-isu ESG terhadap kinerja, posisi keuangan, dan keberlanjutan bisnis Perusahaan.

Pendekatan yang menyeluruh ini memungkinkan ABM untuk mengidentifikasi, memprioritaskan, dan merespons secara efektif berbagai risiko dan peluang terkait keberlanjutan. Dengan demikian, strategi dan langkah-langkah yang diambil mencerminkan komitmen ABM dalam menerapkan praktik bisnis yang bertanggung jawab dan berkelanjutan.

Through the application of the double materiality principle, ABM comprehensively assesses the linkages between business activities and ESG issues. This approach includes an evaluation of the material impacts of ABM's business activities on environmental, social, and governance aspects, as well as the material impacts of ESG issues on the Company's performance, financial position, and business sustainability.

This comprehensive approach enables ABM to identify, prioritize, and respond effectively to various sustainability-related risks and opportunities. Accordingly, the strategies and measures taken reflect ABM's commitment to implementing responsible and sustainable business practices.
