# Sekretaris Perusahaan
## Corporate Secretary

Berdasarkan Surat Keputusan Direksi Perseroan No. 001/SK-DIR/SSD/VII/2020 tertanggal 17 Juli 2020 tentang Pengangkatan Sekretaris Perseroan, Perseroan telah mengangkat GEORGE SAMUEL sebagai Sekretaris Perseroan sesuai dengan Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan Nomor 35/POJK.04/2014 tentang Sekretaris Perseroan Emiten atau Perseroan Publik.

Based on the Decree of the Board of Directors of the Company No. 001 / SK-DIR / SSD / VII / 2020 dated 17 July 2020 concerning the Appointment of the Corporate Secretary, the Company has appointed GEORGE SAMUEL as the Corporate Secretary following the Financial Services Authority Regulation Number 35 / POJK.04 / 2014 concerning Corporate Secretary Issuers or Public Companies.
