# ASPEK UMUM

## Biaya Lingkungan Hidup

Perseroan senantiasa berupaya mencegah dampak buruk dari kegiatan usaha yang dilakukan, dengan mengeluarkan kebijakan yang sejalan dengan tujuan pembangunan berkelanjutan. Sampai dengan saat ini Perseroan tetap konsisten pada upaya memelihara vegetasi, menanam berbagai jenis pohon serta mempertahankan kawasan Sentul City tetap hijau. Dampak dari konsistensi pelaksanaan kebijakan Perseroan tersebut diharapkan memberikan kontribusi terkait kesehatan warga penghuni kawasan Sentul City dan warga sekitar. Pada tahun yang baru berakhir biaya perawatan yang dikeluarkan oleh Perseroan sebesar Rp. 10.836.224.591, dibandingkan biaya perawatan tahun 2021 mengalami mengalami kenaikan sebesar Rp. 1,8 miliar atau sekitar 20%. Biaya perawatan tersebut meliputi Taman Lingkungan, Taman Spine Road, Play Set, Rumput Bahu Jalan, Rumput Ruang Terbuka Hijau, Pohon Jalan, Pohon Penghijauan, Penyapuan, Jogging Track dan Saluran.

# ASPEK MATERIAL

## Penggunaan Material yang Ramah Lingkungan

Material Ramah Lingkungan yang Digunakan Perseroan antara lain;

Kayu Rekayasa untuk memenuhi berbagai kerperluan antara lain untuk membuat bagian-bagian struktural seperti dinding, papan, pintu dan lain-lain. Kayu rekayasa terbuat dari berbagai sisa - limbah kayu, lapisan kayu yang berbeda, potongan kayu, kayu lunak, serat kayu, dan lain-lain, yang dioleh dan dibentuk menjadi bahan yang mempunyai nilai ekonomi relatif tinggi.

## Kayu Rekayasa

Kayu adalah salah satu bahan bangunan paling terkenal yang digunakan di seluruh dunia. Namun dalam proses konversi kayu mentah menjadi papan dan papan kayu, sebagian besar sisa kayu mungkin terbuang sia-sia. Ampas kayu juga dapat digunakan untuk membuat bagian-bagian struktural seperti dinding, papan, pintu dll dalam bentuk kayu rekayasa. Tidak seperti kayu solid, kayu rekayasa mengandung lapisan kayu yang berbeda, biasanya lapisan tengahnya terbuat dari potongan kayu, kayu lunak, serat kayu, dll.

# GENERAL ASPECT

## Environmental Cost of Living

The company always strives to prevent the negative impacts of its business activities by issuing policies that are in line with the goal of sustainable development. Up to this point, the company remains consistent in its efforts to preserve vegetation, plant various types of trees, and maintain the Sentul City area to remain green. The impact of the company's policy implementation consistency is expected to contribute to the health of the residents of the Sentul City area and surrounding residents. In the recently ended year, the maintenance cost incurred by the company amounted to Rp. 10,836,224,591, compared to the maintenance cost in 2021, which increased by Rp. 1.8 billion or approximately 20%. The maintenance cost includes the Environmental Park, Spine Road Park, Play Set, Roadside Grass, Green Open Space Grass, Road Trees, Greenery Trees, Sweeping, Jogging Track, and Channels.

# MATERIAL ASPECT

## Use of Environmentally Friendly Materials

Environmentally Friendly Materials used by the Company include;

Engineered Wood to meet various needs, among others, to make structural parts such as walls, boards, doors and others. Engineered wood is made from various wastes - wood waste, different layers of wood, pieces of wood, soft wood, wood fibers, etc., which are processed and shaped into materials that have relatively high economic value.

## Engineering Wood

Wood is one of the most popular building materials used all over the world. However, in the process of converting raw wood into boards and planks, most of the remaining wood may be wasted. Wood pulp can also be used to make structural parts such as walls, boards, doors etc. in the form of engineered wood. Unlike solid wood, engineered wood contains different layers of wood, usually the middle layer is made of cut wood, softwood, wood grain, etc.

## Non-VOC Paint

Non-VOC paints or eco-friendly paints are recommended to replace paints containing VOC. The presence of Volatile Organic Compounds (VOC) in paint reacts with sunlight and nitrogen oxides resulting in the formation of ozone which can cause severe health problems for the occupants.
