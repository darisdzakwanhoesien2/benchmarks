# Economic Performance Report

The Company continues to work in 2021 with our still relevant strategic guidance: increasing the supply of raw materials, increasing production capacity, and increasing sales, marketing, and distribution.

To drive sales, the Company opened various new trade including online trading to target the upper middle-class consumers and to respond to changes in the way people shop, including shopping for rice. In 2021, online sales increased significantly from the previously just Rp2,682,049,620 to Rp9,968,313,253.

The Company has also added a drying machine and husk crushers machine in Subang. This addition is intended for cost optimization and margin improvement.

To reduce waste, the Company has built a Rice Husk Power Plant in Palembang in 2021. This year, the Company has recorded income from power plant rentals of Rp14.40 billion.

Until the end of 2021, the Company recorded net sales of Rp933.60 billion, down 20% from the achievement in 2020. This sales figure is also below the target which is projected to grow 10-15% from 2020. This is due to decrease in people's purchasing power due activity restrictions. As a consequence, this year's profit for the year also fell to Rp12.53 billion, with a net profit margin of 1.34%, which was also below the target at the beginning of the year.

While on the balance sheet, due to the increase in property, plant and equipment, total assets increase by 9% to Rp989.12 billion, accompanied by an increase in short term bank loans.

The Company continues to maintain the quality of raw material supply and factory operations so as to produce premium quality rice. We also have a quality control system ISO 9001-2015 standards which ensure product quality is maintained. In 2021 none the Company's product was recalled for safety or quality reasons.
