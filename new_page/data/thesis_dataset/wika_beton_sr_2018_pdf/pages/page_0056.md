Perseroan berhasil menurunkan jumlah pemakaian solar hingga 32% di tahun 2018, sedangkan pemakaian diesel, gas alam, dan MFO juga berhasil diturunkan. Namun demikian, pemakaian energi listrik naik 42%, yang disebabkan karena permintaan produksi meningkat dan lokasi pabrik bertambah. [103-3]

The company succeeded in reducing the amount of diesel fuel consumption by 32% in 2018, while diesel, natural gas, and MFO were also reduced significantly through effective and efficient consumption. However, electricity consumption increased by 42% due to the increasing demand for production and more operating plants. [103-3]
