## Praktik Ketenagakerjaan dan Kenyamanan Bekerja

Sumber Daya Manusia (SDM) merupakan aset utama Perseroan dan memiliki peran penting dalam menentukan keberhasilan kegiatan usaha Perseroan. Menyadari hal tersebut, Perseroan berkeyakinan bahwa untuk dapat mencapai misi Perseroan, mutlak diperlukan usaha-usaha yang dapat menunjang pengembangan dan peningkatan kualitas sumber daya manusia sehingga pendayagunaan sumber daya manusia dapat dilakukan secara optimal.

Per tanggal 31 Desember 2017, Perseroan memiliki 1503 karyawan. Berdasarkan komposisi jenjang pendidikan, Perseroan memiliki 488 karyawan Strata 1/Strata 2, 175 karyawan Diploma 3, dan 840 karyawan Sekolah Menengah Umum/Sederajat. Sejalan dengan proyek yang ditangani dan target yang ditetapapkan Perseroan, jumlah karyawan tersebut akan terus bertambah. [G4-LA1]

Sepanjang tahun 2017, Perseroan merekrut 671 karyawan, atau naik 42,16% dibanding tahun 2016, yakni 472 karyawan. Rekrutmen dilakukan di Kantor Pusat maupun di daerah-daerah tempat Waskita Beton Precast beroperasi. Hingga akhir Desember 2017, Perseroan mengoperasikan 11 pabrik (plant) precast, 68 pabrik ready mix/batching plant dan 5 quarry yang memiliki lokasi yang strategis di dekat lokasi proyek-proyek yang dikerjakan Waskita Beton Precast.

Selain bertambahnya jumlah karyawan baru yang masuk, ada juga di antara karyawan Perseroan yang berhenti atau keluar dari pekerjaannya. Per 31 Desember 2017, sebanyak 53 karyawan keluar.

Karyawan yang keluar pada tahun 2017 terdiri dari 64 karyawan pria dan 14 karyawan wanita, mereka berada dalam rentang usia antara 18-55 tahun. Dari jumlah itu, karyawan yang keluar dari Kantor Pusat di Jakarta berjumlah 23 karyawan, dan keluar dari kantor/pabrik/area produksi di daerah sebanyak 55 karyawan.

Sementara itu, berkaitan dengan pelaksanaan cuti melahirkan bagi karyawan perempuan, sepanjang tahun 2017, terdapat 1 karyawan perempuan yang mengajukan cuti melahirkan. Dari jumlah itu, sebanyak semuanya kembali bekerja. [G4-LA3]

## Employment Practices and Work Comfort

Human Resources (HR) is a major asset of the Company and has an important role in determining the success of the Company's business activities. Realizing this, the Company believes that in order to achieve the mission of the Company, efforts to support the development and improvement of human resources quality are required which leads to empowerment of human resources can be performed optimally.

As of December 31, 2017, the Company had 1503 employees. Based on the composition of education level, the Company has 488 employees of bachelor/master degree, 175 employees of Diploma 4/Diploma 3 degree, and 840 employees of Senior High School/equivalents degree. In line with the projects handled and the target set by the Company, the number of employees will continue to grow. [G4-LA1]

Throughout 2017, the Company recruited 671 employees, an increase of 42.16% compared to 2016 of 472 employees. The recruitment was performed in the Head Office and in operational areas of Waskita Beton Precast. Until the end of December 2017, the Company operates 11 precast factory (plant), 68 ready mix factory/batching plant, and 5 stone crusher (quarry) which have strategic locations near the location of the projects undertaken by Waskita Beton Precast.

In addition to increasing the number of new employees, there were also the Company's employees that quit or resigned. As of December 31, 2017, there were 53 employees that resigned.

Employees that resigned in 2017 consisted of 64 male and 14 female employees, in the age range between 18-55 years old. From the total amount, employees that resigned from the Head Office in Jakarta were 23 employees, and from the production office/factory/production area in the region, there were 55 employees.

Meanwhile, with regard to the implementation of maternity leave for female employees, throughout the year 2017, there was 1 female employee that applied for maternity leave. From that number, the employee is back to work. [G4-LA3]
