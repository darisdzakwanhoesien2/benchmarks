Guna mencegah insiden yang terjadi tidak terulang di masa-masa mendatang, Perseroan menetapkan rencana aksi berupa penetapan sistem Safety Performance Indicator. Sistem ini akan menjadi acuan bagian unit kerja sebagai indikator pengeloaan Keselamatan, Kesehatan dan lingkungan hidup. Untuk mencapai indikator yang sesuai dengan standar yang berlaku, Perseroan menetapkan langkah-langkah konkrit seperti sistem reward and punishment, safety observation program, safety leadership dan lain-lain. [G4-LA7]

Rencana aksi ke depan Perseroan/koorporasi dalam pencegahan penyakit akibat kerja dengan melakukan pemetaan risiko penyakit akibat kerja dari setiap pekerjaan, melakukan konsultasi dan partisipasi, menetapkan rencana pemeriksaan karyawan yang berpotensi terkena dampak penyakit akibat kerja secara berkala. Audit industrial hygiene akan menjadi salah satu program unggulan dalam pencegahan penyakit akibat kerja yang sudah diatur dalam Peraturan Perusahaan PT Waskita Beton Precast Tbk, yang sudah disahkan melalui Surat Keputusan Direktur Jenderal Pembinaan Hubungan Industrial dan Jaminan Sosial Tenaga Kerja Nomer Kep.587/PHJSK-PKKAD/PP/V/2015 tanggal 19 Mei 2015.

To prevent the incident to occur in the future, the Company has established an action plan in the form of Safety Performance Indicator system establishment. This system will be a reference to the work units as safety, health and environment management indicator. To reach such indicator, the Company provides reward and punishment system, safety observation program, safety leadership, and so forth. [G4-LA7]

The future action plan of the Company is to prevent occupational diseases caused by work activity through the mapping of disease risk from every work, conducting consultation and participation, establishing an investigation plan of employees with the potential to be affected with the occupational disease regularly. Industrial hygiene audit will be one of the excellent programs in occupational disease prevention caused by work activity stipulated in the Company Regulation of PT Waskita Beton Precast Tbk, validated through the Decree of the General Director of Industrial Relations Development and Labor Social Security Number Kep.587/PHJSK-PKKAD/PP/V/2015 dated May 19, 2015.
