# Pelaksanaan Kepatuhan Lingkungan Hidup

Komitmen Perseroan dalam melakukan bisnis adalah pemenuhan kepada peraturan perundangan yang berlaku di mana kami beroperasi, yang merupakan syarat minimal yang harus dipenuhi. Pelaksanaan Kepatuhan dilaksanakan selama aktivitas bisnis Perseroan dilakukan. Setiap aktivitas Perseroan dimulai dengan analisis risiko lingkungan dan sosial bersama pemangku kepentingan. Aktivitas ini dilaksanakan dalam kerangka kerja Analisis Mengenai Dampak Lingkungan sebagai bagian dari kegiatan untuk memperoleh Izin Lingkungan.

Rincian kegiatan dalam mengelola dampak lingkungan dan dampak sosial dituangkan dalam Rencana Pengelolaan dan Pemantauan Lingkungan (RKL/RPL) atau Upaya Pengelolaan dan Pemantauan Lingkungan (UKL/UPL) sesuai dengan skala dampak. Dokumen RKL/RPL dan UKL/UPL yang disetujui Pemerintah merupakan dasar pelaksanaan kepatuhan Perseroan dalam pengelolaan dampak lingkungan dan sosial.

Setelah dokumen lingkungan diperoleh sebelum aktivitas dilakukan di area kerja, Perseroan melakukan kegiatan identifikasi dan dokumentasi flora dan fauna yang ada di daerah tersebut, kemudian dilakukan pengambilan beberapa jenis spesies tanaman lokal dan langka dengan sistem putaran untuk dikoleksi di Pusat Pembibitan, dan pada gilirannya akan ditanam kembali pada kegiatan revegetasi. Hal ini dilakukan untuk menjamin konservasi lingkungan dan keanekaragaman hayati di areal kerja.

# Compliance with Environment

The Company's commitment on conducting business is the compliance with the prevailing laws and regulations in which we operate that is the minimum requirement that should be complied with. The compliance is implemented during business activity. Every activity of the Company is begun with analysis on social and environmental risk with stakeholders. This activity is carried out in Environmental Impact Assessment framework as a part of activity to obtain Environment Permit.

Detail of activity in managing environmental and social impact is contained in Environmental Monitoring and Management Plan (RKL/RPL) or Environmental Monitoring and Management Effort (UKL/UPL) according to the scale of impact. RKL/RPL and UKL/UPL document approved by the Government are the basis of compliance of the Company in managing social and environmental impact.

After the environmental document was obtained before activities are carried out in working area, the Company conducts identification and documentation of flora and fauna in the area, then some of rare and local plant species are taken with rotation system to be collected in Nursery Center, and in turn will be replanted in revegetation activity. This is carried out to ensure environmental conservation and biodiversity in work area.

# Evaluation on the Effectiveness of Risk Management System

Evaluation on the implementation of risk management is carried out through periodic review and monitoring activities every three months. These activities will provide the effectiveness of risk control and control plan that will be carried out in the next period. One of results of risk management evaluation is by taking into account the development and need of business in the future. Risk management application in use is currently in improvement phase.
