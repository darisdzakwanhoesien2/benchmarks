PT Waskita Beton Precast Tbk | Laporan Keberlanjutan 2017

Untuk program CSR, sepanjang tahun 2017, Perseroan mengalokasikan dana sebesar Rp6,34 miliar. Waskita Precast mengalokasikan dana tersebut sebagai perwujudan dan tanggung jawabnya dalam menjalankan amanat Undang-Undang seperti Undang-Undang No. 25 Tahun 2007 tentang Penanaman Modal, UU No. 40 Tahun 2007 tentang Perseroan Terbatas, serta ISO 26000 yang telah diratifikasi oleh Pemerintah pada tahun 2010 dan dijadikan Standar Nasional (SNI) pada 2012.

Kepedulian terhadap masyarakat di sekitar kantor maupun plant di wujudkan melalui program Tanggung Jawab Sosial Perusahaan dalam bentuk, antara lain: [G4-EC7], [G4-EC8]
- Pemeriksaan kesehatan massal untuk masyarakat sekitar sarana produksi;
- Pembangunan dan perbaikan sarana ibadah;
- Perbaikan akses jalan lingkungan;
- Sosialisasi kesehatan di sarana pendidikan disekitar sarana produksi;
- Menyediakan sarana olahraga untuk karang taruna.

For CSR program, throughout 2017, the Company allocated funds amounting to Rp3.8 billion. The allocation of these funds is the realization and responsibility of Waskita Beton Precast in carrying out the mandate of Laws, such as Law No. 25 of 2007 on Capital Investment, Law No. 40 Year 2007 on Limited Liability Company, as well as ISO 26000 which has been ratified by the government in 2010 and became the National Standard (SNI) in 2012.

The Company's concern for the community around the office or plant is realized through Corporate Social Responsibility program in the form of, among others: [G4-EC7] [G4-EC8]
- Mass medical examination for the community around the production facility;
- Construction and repair of worship facilities;
- Road repair;
- Health dissemination in educational institutions around the production facilities;
- Provide sports facilities for the youth.
