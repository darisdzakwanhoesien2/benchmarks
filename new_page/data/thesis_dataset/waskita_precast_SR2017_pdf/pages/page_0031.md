PT WAKITA BATON PRECAST TBK | LIPORAN KEBERJAYATAN 2017

# Skala Perseroan [G4-9] [G4-10]

Sejalan dengan kinerja Perseroan yang terus meningkat, pada tahun 2017, PT Waskita Beton Precast Tbk, berhasil mencatatkan laba bersih sebesar Rp 1,00 triliun, atau sebesar 57,73% dibandingkan tahun 2016 sebesar Rp 634 miliar. Sedangkan angka penjualan usaha pada tahun 2017 mencapai Rp 7,10 triliun, naik 50,52% dibanding tahun 2016, yakni Rp 4,72 triliun.

Keberhasilan Perseroan itu tak lepas dari dukungan seluruh karyawan yang berdedikasi tinggi dalam bekerja. Hingga 31 Desember 2017, Perseroan memiliki karyawan sebanyak 1503 orang, naik 65% dibanding tahun 2016, yakni 591 orang.

# Ketercakupan dalam Peraturan Perusahaan [G4-11]

Perseroan menghargai kebebasan bagi karyawan dalam berserikat dan berkumpul, termasuk membuka peluang berdirinya serikat pekerja karyawan. Hingga saat ini, Perseroan belum memiliki Serikat Pekerja, meskipun dalam Peraturan Perseroan di Perseroan lain berupa Peraturan Perusahaan (PP) dimungkinkan untuk itu. Peraturan Perusahaan mengatur berbagai hal yang berkaitan dengan hubungan antara pegawai dengan Perseroan sehingga mengikat kedua pihak.

Walau Perseroan belum memiliki serikat pekerja, namun hak mereka untuk berkumpul dan menyampaikan pendapat sudah diatur dalam Peraturan Perusahaan yang mengikat bagi seluruh karyawan dan manajemen Perseroan. Hal itu, diatur dalam Perjanjian Perusahaan, di antaranya mengatur tentang keluh kesah pegawai dan cara penyampaiannya.

# The Company Scale [G4-9] [G4-10]

In line with the Company's performance that continues to increase, in 2017, PT Waskita Beton Precast Tbk managed to record net profit of Rp1.00 trillion or 57.73% compared to that of 2016 which was Rp634 billion. Meanwhile, operating sales in 2017 reached Rp7.10 trillion, increased by 50.52% compared to that of 2016 which was Rp4.72 trillion.

The success is inseparable from the support of all the dedicated employees. As of December 31, 2017, employees of the Company were 1503 employees, an increase by 65% compared to that of 2016 which was 591 employees.

# Coverage in the Collective Labor Agreement [G4-11]

The Company appreciates the freedom for employees to join an union and assemble, including the opportunity to establish employee unions. As of today, the Company does not have an Employee Union, although in the Company Regulations in other companies in the form of the Company Regulations (PP), the union is allowed. The Company Regulations regulates various issues related to the relationship between an employee with the Company, and it is binding for both parties.

Although the Company does not have an Employee Union yet, but the employee's right to assemble and express opinions has been set in the Company Regulations, and the regulations are binding for all employees and management of the Company. This is stipulated in the Collective Labor Agreement, which among others regulates on the employee complaints and media to submit the complaint.
