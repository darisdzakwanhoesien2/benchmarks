## Perubahan Signifikan Selama Masa Pelaporan

Secara keseluruhan, tidak terdapat perubahan penting ataupun perubahan pada prinsip komparabilitas data dari pelaporan pada periode sebelumnya terkait cakupan dan batas-batas aspek material informasi yang disajikan. Oleh karena itu, tidak ada pernyataan ulang (restatement) dalam Laporan Keberlanjutan WSBP tahun 2017. [G4-13] [G4-22] [G4-23]

## Pelibatan Kelompok Kepentingan

G4-24, G4-25, G4-26, G427

Pemangku kepentingan WSBP meliputi seluruh elemen Perseroan, pelanggan, supplier, masyarakat dan komunitas lokal, pemegang saham utama, serta pengendali. Pemetaan pemangku kepentingan WSBP dilakukan berdasarkan relasi yang terbangun selama ini, yaitu adanya hubungan yang saling memengaruhi, aktif, dan responsif. Dalam rangka memfasilitasi keterlibatan para pemangku kepentingan, WSBP mengadakan pelatihan dan rapat secara reguler sesuai kepentingan masing-masing pihak, serta mengadakan RUPS Tahunan dan RUPS Luar Biasa.

## Significant Change During Reporting Period

Overall, there was no significant change or change in data comparability principle from reporting in the previous period related to scope and limitation of information material. Therefore, there is no restatement in Sustainability Report of WSBP in 2017. [G4-13] [G4-22] [G4-23]

## Engagement of Stakeholders

G4-24, G4-25, G4-26, G427

Stakeholders of WSBP include all elements of the Company, customers, suppliers, public, local community, majority and controlling shareholders. Mapping of stakeholders of WSBP is conducted based on the established relation, namely mutually affecting relationship, active, and responsive. To facilitate the engagement of stakeholders, WSBP regularly held training and meeting in accordance with the interest of each parties, and convened the Annual and Extraordinary GMS.
