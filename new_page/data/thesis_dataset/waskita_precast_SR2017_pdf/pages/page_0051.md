# Kian Kokoh Melayani Negeri

Getting Stronger in Serving the Nation

## Nilai Ekonomi Langsung [G4-EC1]

Gencarnya pembangunan infrastruktur yang dipacu pemerintah, membawa dampak yang positif bagi Perseroan. Hal ini ditunjukkan dengan kinerja WSBP yang kian meningkat di tahun ini. Nilai ekonomi langsung yang dihasilkan, yakni Pendapatan Usaha, per 31 Desember 2017 tercatat sebesar Rp7,10 triliun atau mencapai 50,42% dibandingkan tahun 2016 yang sebesar Rp4,72 triliun. Seiring dengan naiknya pendapatan usaha, laba usaha juga mengalami kenaikan yang signifikan. Per 31 Desember 2017, Laba Bersih tercatat sebesar Rp1,00 triliun, atau sebesar 57,52% dibandingkan tahun 2016 sebesar Rp634,82 miliar.

Sementara itu, nilai ekonomi yang didistribusikan, yakni biaya operasional, pada 2017 meningkat yang mencapai Rp5,15 triliun dibandingkan tahun 2016 sebesar Rp3,50 triliun. Peningkatan juga terjadi pada beban untuk upah dan tunjangan karyawan. Jika pada 31 Desember 2016 tercatat sebesar Rp243,59 miliar, maka pada 31 Desember 2017 menjadi sebesar Rp292,09 miliar. Peningkatan terjadi karena penambahan jumlah karyawan yang dibutuhkan oleh Perseroan.

Sementara itu, pajak yang dibayarkan Perseroan atas laba yang diperoleh kepada pemerintah juga mengalami kenaikan. Pada 31 Desember 2016, Waskita Beton Precast mencatat kewajiban pajak kini sebesar Rp335.518 miliar, sedangkan per 31 Desember 2017 mencatat kewajiban sebesar Rp195.445 miliar.

Sebagai Perseroan yang bergerak di bidang infrastruktur, salah satu risiko yang dihadapi Perseroan menyangkut ketersediaan bahan baku material alam, yakni pasir dan split/ batu pecah. Bahan baku ini mempunyai sifat musiman. Saat musim panas/kering, bahan baku tersebut akan banyak/ melimpah dan mudah dipasok, sementara saat musim hujan, berlaku juga sebaliknya. Dengan demikian, perubahan cuaca tersebut mempengaruhi kinerja Perseroan, sekaligus berimplikasi secara finansial. Sesuai hukum ekonomi, saat pasokan bahan material banyak tersedia, maka harganya akan lebih terjangkau dibanding saat bahan material pasokannya terbatas. [G4-EC2]

## Direct Economic Value [G4-EC1]

The incessant infrastructure development driven by the government brings positive impact for the Company. It is demonstrated with the rising performance of WSBP in this year. The generated direct economic value, namely the Revenue, was recorded at Rp7.10 trillion as of December 31, 2017, or increased by 50.42% compared to 2016 which was recorded at Rp4/72 trillion. Along with the increase in revenues, the income also increased significantly. As of December 31, 2017, Net Income was recorded at Rp1.00 trillion, or improved by 57.52% compared to 2016 which amounting to Rp634.82 billion.

Meanwhile, the distributed economic value, namely, operating expenses, in 2017 increased by Rp5,15 trillion compared to 2016 which was at Rp4.72 trillion. The increase also occurred in the expense of employee salary and benefits. If on December 31, 2016, it was amounted to Rp46.134 billion, then on December 31, 2017, it was amounted to Rp97.364 billion. This increase was due to the increase in the number of employees needed by the Company.

Meanwhile, the tax paid on the Company's profits to the government also increased. On December 31, 2016, Waskita Beton Precast recorded current tax liabilities amounting to Rp335.518 billion, while as of December 31, 2017, it was recorded at Rp195.445 billion.

As a company engaging in the infrastructure sector, one of the risks faced by the Company is related to the availability of raw natural materials, namely sand and split/broken stones. These raw materials have a seasonal nature. In summer/dry season, the raw materials will be more/abundant and easily supplied, while in the rainy season, the reverse applies. As a result, the weather changes affect the Company's performance and financial condition. Referring to the laws of economics, when the supply of materials are widely available, then the price will be more affordable compared to the condition of limited supply of materials. [G4-EC2]
