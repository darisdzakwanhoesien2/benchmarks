## Asesmen Pemasok

Perseroan menjunjung tinggi ketentuan dan aturan-aturan yang berlaku di bidang di Indonesia, baik di bidang ketenagakerjaan dan pelestarian lingkungan. Dengan asesmen seperti itu, sepanjang tahun 2017, tidak ada laporan pelanggaran yang dilakukan pemasok berkaitan dengan ketentuan di bidang ketenagakerjaan dan lingkungan. [G4-LA14, G4-LA15, G4-LA16.]

## Persaingan Usaha

Saat ini Perseroan merupakan salah satu produsen yang memiliki reputasi terbaik dan memiliki keunggulan kompetitif dibandingkan 5 (lima) produsen beton precast pesaing. Berdasarkan strategy canvas industri beton precast (Markplus, 2015), keunggulan kompetitif Perseroan terletak pada mutu, lokasi plant, inovasi dan kapasitas finansial.

Sementara itu, berdasarkan kelompok diskusi terbatas/Forum Group Discussion (FGD) yang dilakukan kepada pelanggan internal dan eksternal Perseroan, Perseroan memiliki keunggulan pada faktor harga dan pelayanan eksternal. Dari perspektif pelanggan, Perseroan dinilai mampu menawarkan harga dan produk yang kompetitif dibandingkan dengan pesaing. Selain itu, Perseroan dinilai dapat dipercaya dimana dipersepsikan memiliki kualitas yang sama baik dengan induknya, WASKITA.

Dalam menghadapi persaingan usaha, Perseroan menjunjung tinggi persaingan yang sehat, anti-monopoli, serta selalu berpatokan pada ketentuan dan undang-undang yang berlaku. Komitmen itu dipegang teguh Perseroan agar tidak ada pelanggaran kepatuhan terhadap aturan dan ketentuan dalam menjalankan usaha, termasuk ketika harus bersaing dengan kompetitor. [G4-SO7, G4-SO8]

Untuk memenangkan persaingan usaha secara adil, Perseroan didukung oleh kantor pemasaran yang tersebar di 6 area wilayah penjualan untuk memperoleh informasi dini pasar, memperlancar komunikasi dengan pasar dan health (K3LMP), and employees shall wear and maintain the K3LMP tools provided by the Company. In addition, there are also provisions that employees must take an active part in prevention and mitigation of accidents/fires, environmental pollution and safety in the work environment. In the event of an accident/fire in the workplace, employees are required to report and provide correct information to the officer designated by the Company to investigate the incident. [G4-LA8]

With such assessment, throughout 2017, there were no reports of violations committed by suppliers with regard to the provision in the field of labor and the environment. [G4-LA14, G4-LA15, G4-LA16]

## Business Competition

Presently, the Company is one of the manufacturers with the best reputation that has competitive advantages compared to another 5 (five) closest competitors of precast concrete manufacturers. Based on the precast concrete industry's canvas strategy (Markplus, 2015), the Company's competitive advantage lies in quality, plant location, innovation and financial capacity.

Meanwhile, based on the Forum Group Discussion (FGD) conducted to internal and external customers of the Company, the Company has the advantages on the price factor and external services. From the customer's perspective, the Company is considered to able to offer competitive prices and products compared to competitors. In addition, the Company is assessed to be reliable which is perceived to have the same good quality as its parent, Waskita.

In facing the competition, the Company upholds fair competition, anti-monopoly, and has always relied on the prevailing laws and regulations. The commitment is firmly held by the Company, hence there is no violation of compliance with the rules and regulations of conducting business, including in facing competition with others. [G4-SO7, G4-SO8].

To win the competition fairly, the Company is supported by marketing offices spread across f6 sales area in order to obtain market information earlier, facilitating communication with the market and cover resources to support marketing.
