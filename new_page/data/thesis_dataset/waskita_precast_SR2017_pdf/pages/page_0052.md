Perihal imbalan kerja karyawan, seperti pensiun, uang pisah dan uang penghargaan masa kerja dihitung berdasarkan Undang-Undang Ketenagakerjaan No.13/Tahun 2013. Pada tahun 2017, Waskita Beton Precast mencatat imbalan kerja sebesar Rp9.461 miliar atau 375% dari yang dicatat pada tahun 2016 yaitu sebesar Rp1.991 miliar. [G4-EC3]

Selama tahun pelaporan, Perseroan tidak mendapat bantuan finansial dari pemerintah, baik berupa keringanan pajak, pemberian subsidi, pembebasan pembayaran royalti, bantuan penelitian dan pengembangan, maupun jenis bantuan lainnya. [G4-EC4]

## Dampak Ekonomi Tidak Langsung

Kehadiran Waskita Precast dalam pembangunan infrastruktur di Indonesia tidak hanya diperuntukkan untuk mencari laba sebesar-besarnya. Melainkan, Perseroan berupaya agar keberadaannya juga bermanfaat bagi masyarakat di sekitarnya. Manfaat itu, antara lain, berupa kesempatan untuk menjadi pekerja di lokasi-lokasi plant yang dimiliki Waskita Precast, menjadi pemasok bahan baku material, atau mendapatkan bantuan melalui program tanggung jawab sosial perusahaan (Corporate Social Responsibility).

Hingga 31 Desember 2017, dari total sebanyak 1.503 pegawai, sebanyak 3.789 orang direkrut dari masyarakat di sekitar plant. Mereka menduduki posisi sebagai Staf Teknik, dan staf non teknik Upaya merekrut pegawai dari orang lokal merupakan komitmen Perseroan untuk mengurangi tingkat pengangguran, sekaligus memberdayakan potensi tenaga lokal. Dengan terbukanya lapangan kerja di Waskita Beton Precast, maka kehidupan para pekerja tersebut akan meningkat.

Sementara itu, untuk pemasok bahan baku material, sepanjang tahun 2017, Perseroan menjalin kerjasama dengan 11 pemasok di lokasi operasional plant. Bahan baku yang dipasok berupa PC Bar, Iron Wire, Besi Beton, PC Strand, Shear Concealer, joint plate. Bahan baku material dari para pemasok itu menyumbang 57.8 % dari total kebutuhan bahan baku Perseroan. [G4-EC9]

Regarding the work benefits of the employee, such as pensions, severance payment and gratuity payment are calculated based on Labor Law No.13/of 2013. In 2017, Waskita Beton Precast recorded employment benefits amounted to Rp9.461 billion or increased by 375% compared to 2016 which amounted to Rp1.991 billion. [G4-EC3]

During the period of reporting, the Company did not receive any financial assistance from the government, either in the form of tax relief, subsidies, exemption of royalty payments, research and development aid, or other types of assistance. [G4-EC4]

## Indirect Economic Impacts

The existence of Waskita Beton Precast in infrastructure development in Indonesia is not only to seek maximum profit. In this regard, the Company's desire is that its existence would also be beneficial to the surrounding community. The benefits are, among others, in the form of opportunity to be workers in the plant sites owned by Waskita Beton Precast, to be the supplier of raw materials, or to receive assistance through Corporate Social Responsibility programs.

As of December 31, 2017, from the total of 1,503 employees, as many as 3,789 persons were recruited from the communities around the plant. They occupied position as Engineering Staff, and non-engineering staff. Efforts to recruit employees from local community is the Company's commitment to reduce the unemployment rate, as well as empowering the potential of local people. With the employment opportunities in Waskita Beton Precast, the quality of life of these workers are expected to increase.

Meanwhile, regarding raw material suppliers, throughout 2017 the Company has established a partnership with 11 suppliers in the location of plant operations. The supplied raw materials were in the form of PC Bar, Iron Wire, Concrete Iron, PC Strand, Shear Concealer and joint plate. These raw materials from suppliers contributed to 57.8% of total raw material needed of the Company. [G4-EC9]
