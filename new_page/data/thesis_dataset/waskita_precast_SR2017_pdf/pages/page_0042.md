Umum Pemegang Saham apabila terjadi gejala menurunnya kinerja Perseroan.

Dalam hubungannya dengan Direksi, Komisaris berkewajiban untuk:

1. Meneliti dan menelaah laporan berkala dan laporan tahunan yang disiapkan Direksi serta menandatangannya.
2. Melakukan pengawasan dan memberikan saran atas pengelolaan Perseroan.
3. Melakukan penilaian atas kinerja Direksi.

## Larangan

Komisaris dilarang melakukan transaksi yang mempunyai benturan kepentingan dan mengambil keuntungan pribadi baik secara langsung maupun tidak langsung dari PT Waskita Beton Precast Tbk selain penghasilan yang sah.

## Etika dan Perilaku Direksi

Dalam hubungannya dengan penerapan prinsip-prinsip Good Corporate Governance (GCG), Direksi harus:

1. Memahami prinsip-prinsip GCG dan melaksanakannya sebagai contoh perilaku bagi karyawan;
2. Menghindari timbulnya benturan kepentingan baik secara langsung maupun tidak langsung.

Dalam hubungan dengan tugas pokok, maka Direksi berkewajiban:

1. Mengusahakan dan menjamin terlaksananya usaha dan kegiatan perusahaan sesuai dengan maksud dan tujuan serta kegiatan usahanya;
2. Menyiapkan pada waktunya Rencana Jangka Panjang Perseroan, Rencana Kerja dan Anggaran Perseroan, termasuk rencanarecana lainnya yang berhubungan dengan pelaksanaan usaha dan kegiatan Perseroan serta menyampaikannya kepada Komisaris dan Pemegang Saham untuk mendapatkan pengesahan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham;
3. Memelihara risalah rapat serta menyelenggarakan pembukuan perusahaan sesuai dengan kelaziman yang berlaku bagi suatu Perseroan;
4. Menyusun sistem akuntansi sesuai dengan Standar Akuntansi Keuangan dan berdasarkan prinsip-prinsip pengendalian intern, terutama fungsi pengurusan, pencatatan, penyimpanan

Shareholders whenever there are symptoms of declining performance of the Company.

In the relationship with the Board of Directors, the Board of Commissioners must:

1. Review and analyze the periodic reports and annual reports that have been prepared by the Board of Directors and sign them.
2. Supervise and provide advice on the management of the company.
3. Conduct an assessment of the performance of the Board of Directors.

## Prohibition

The Board of Commissioners is prohibited from making any transactions that have conflict of interest and gaining personal benefit either directly or indirectly from PT Waskita Beton Precast Tbk, other than their legitimate income.

## Ethics and Conduct of the Board for Directors

In relation with the implementation of the principles of Good Corporate Governance (GCG), the Board of Directors must:

1. Understand the GCG principles and implement them as an example for employees.
2. Avoid potential direct or indirect conflicts of interest.

Related with their main duties, the Board of Directors is obliged to:

1. Promote and assure the implementation of the Company's business and activities are pursuant to the goals and objectives as well its business activities.
2. Prepare the Company's Long-Term Plan, Work Plan and Budget, including plans related with the Company's business and activities of the enterprise, and submit them to the Board of Commissioners and the Shareholders to obtain approval from the General Meeting of Shareholders.
3. Maintain the minutes of meeting as well as the Company's bookkeeping in accordance with the prevailing standards in a company.
4. Prepare the accounting system pursuant to the Financial Accounting Standards and based on the principles of internal control, especially the maintenance, recording, storage, and supervision
