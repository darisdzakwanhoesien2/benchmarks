Selain remunerasi yang terkait dengan kinerja dan perkembangan industri, Perseroan memberikan tunjangan lain kepada pegawai dalam bentuk Tunjangan Hari Raya (THR), jaminan kesehatan, uang pensiun dan hak cuti yang ditetapkan sesuai dengan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku.

# Standar Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja

Untuk memenuhi Standar Pelaksanaan K3 sesuai dengan Peraturan Menteri Tenaga Kerja No. 50 Tahun 2012 tentang Sistem Manajemen K3, Perseroan mewajibkan kepada seluruh petugas yang bertanggung jawab atas K3 Perseroan, memiliki sertifikat ahli K3 Umum. Proses sertifikasi bekerjasama dengan Lembaga Sertifikasi Ahli K3 yang terdaftar di Kementrian Ketenagakerjaan dan Transmigrasi. Program sertifikasi ini sangat penting untuk menciptakan budaya "zero accident" di Perseroan. Upaya menuju zero accident merupakan komitmen yang tidak bisa ditawarkan.

In addition to remuneration related to the performance and development of the industry, the Company provides other allowance to the employees in the form of Religious Holiday Allowance (THR) health insurance, pension fund, and rights for leave determined in accordance with the prevailing regulations.

The Company has implemented the "Pension Plan" (JHT), Retirement program of "Pension Savings" and Certain Pension Contribution Program cooperating with competent institutions as a part of the employee welfare fulfillment. The Company also provides health service through Hospitals cooperating with the Company and Health Insurance program for the employees.

# Occupational Health and Safety Standards

To meet the Standards of K3 Implementation, in accordance with the Regulation of the Minister of Manpower No. 50 of 2012 on K3 Management System, the Company requires that all officers responsible for the Company K3, to have certificates of General K3 Expert. The certification process is in collaboration with the Institute of Certification of K3 Expert registered in the Ministry of Manpower and Transmigration.
