5. Management Trainee (MT) Program
The Company will conduct the process of Management Trainee (MT) program to fulfill the needs of long-term employees by selecting new graduates from universities/other educational institutions that have a good reputation and track record both domestic and abroad, held centrally and coordinated by the Department of Human Resources, Systems &amp; IT.
