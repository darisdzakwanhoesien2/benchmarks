- Perseroan memiliki standar dalam merespon atas pengaduan pelanggan.
- Pengaduan masuk melalui call center, email customer care dan website.
- Bagian Customer Care akan memberikan jawaban sebagai respons terhadap keluhan kepada pelanggan bahwa keluhan telah diterima dan akan segera diproses oleh unit bersangkutan.
- Bagian Customer Care menyampaikan pengaduan keluhan dari pelanggan kepada unit terkait.
- Selanjutnya, keluhan pelanggan diproses dan diselesaikan oleh unit terkait. Unit terkait dapat langsung berkomunikasi kepada pelanggan dalam proses penyelesaian keluhan.

Pada tahun 2017, pengaduan yang masuk kepada Perseroan ada sebanyak 0 kasus. Terhadap keluhan itu, Waskita Beton Precast langsung menindak lanjuti dan melakukan perbaikan, termasuk memberikan penggantian kepada pelanggan.

Untuk mengukur respon pelanggan atas pelayanan yang diberikan Perseroan, Waskita Beton Precast telah melakukan survei kepuasan pelanggan pada tahun 2017. Survei tersebut penting dilakukan agar Perseroan bisa melakukan perbaikan yang lebih baik lagi guna memastikan kepuasan pelanggan tetap terjaga.

- The company has a standard in responding to customer complaints.
- Complaints are received through call center by phone, email support, and live chat.
- Customer Care will respond to customer complaint that has been received and will be processed by the unit concerned.
- Customer Care will forward it to related units.
- Subsequently, the customer complaint is processed and settled by the related units. Related units will communicate directly to customers in the settlement process.

In 2017, there is 0 case of complaint to the Company. Towards that complaint, Waskita Beton precast immediately follows up and conducts improvement, including refund to the customers.

To measure the customer response to the Company for the services rendered, Waskita Beton Precast has conducted a customer satisfaction survey in 2017. The survey is important to be conducted for the Company to be able to improve customer satisfaction in order to ensure that the customer satisfaction is maintained.
