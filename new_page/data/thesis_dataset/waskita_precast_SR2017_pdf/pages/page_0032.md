# Penerapan Prinsip Kehati-hatian [G4-14]

Dalam menjalankan roda organisasi, Perseroan berkomitmen untuk menerapkan prinsip kehati-hatian guna menghindarkan dari berbagai risiko. Hal itu dilakukan antara lain dengan mengaktifkan kerja Unit Audit Internal, Komite Audit, Komite Risiko dan Asuransi. Selain itu, Perseroan juga mengikuti berbagai standar sertifikasi Internasional. Misalnya, menerapkan OHSAS 18001:2007 (K3), SMK3 (K3), ISO 14001:2004 (LINGKUNGAN), dan ISO 9001:2015 (MUTU).

# Keanggotaan dalam Asosiasi [G4-16]

Sepanjang tahun 2017, Perseroan sudah bergabung dengan asosiasi yang memiliki kesamaan usaha utama, yakni industri manufaktur beton precast dan ready mix. Diantaranya AP3I (Asosiasi Perseroan Pracetak/ Precast Indonesia), dan IAPPI (Ikatan Asosiasi Pracetak Prategang Indonesia).

# The Prudence Principle Implementation [G4-14]

In running the organization's wheel, the Company is committed to implement the prudence principle in order to avoid risks. This is performed by activating the Internal Audit Unit, Audit Committee, Risk and Insurance Committee. In addition, the Company also follows various international certification standards. For example, implementing the OHSAS 18001: 2007 (K3), SMK3 (K3), ISO 14001: 2004 (ENVIRONMENT), and ISO 9001: 2015 (QUALITY).

# Membership in Associations [G4-16]

Throughout 2017, the Company had joined several associations that have the same main business, namely the precast and ready mix concrete manufacturing industry. Among others are AP3I (Indonesian Association of Precast Company), and IAPPI (Indonesian Association of Prestressed Precast).
