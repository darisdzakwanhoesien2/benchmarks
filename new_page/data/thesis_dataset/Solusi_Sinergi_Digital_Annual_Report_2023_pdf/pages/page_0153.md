## Aspek Ketenagakerjaan

Aspek ketenagakerjaan adalah salah satu fokus utama dalam laporan keberlanjutan, mencerminkan komitmen perusahaan atau organisasi terhadap kesejahteraan dan hak-hak karyawan. Laporan ini memberikan gambaran tentang praktik ketenagakerjaan yang berkelanjutan, mencakup aspek seperti keamanan dan kesehatan kerja, keadilan, serta pengembangan karir.

Dalam laporan keberlanjutan tahun 2023, perusahaan telah menerapkan kesetaraan kepada semua karyawan seperti proses pengrekrutan yang transparan dan adil. Tidak mempekerjakan tenaga kerja dibawah umur atau tenaga kerja paksa sesuai dengan peraturan tenaga kerja, memberikan imbalan dan jasa karyawan sesuai dengan upah minimum regional (UMR), berkomitmen dalam menyediakan tempat kerja yang aman dan nyaman serta penerapan program kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja (K3).

## Water Aspect

In supporting operations, we use water for the needs of employees during the workday, as well as the environment around the Company, such as maintenance of plants and trees around the Office. The Company is committed to the wise use of water resources. Starting from monitoring usage, saving policies are part of the sustainability strategy.

## Aspects of Complaints Related to the Environment

During the reporting period, there were no public complaints related to the environment.

## Employment Aspects

Employment aspects are one of the main focuses in sustainability reports, reflecting a company or organization's commitment to the welfare and rights of employees. This report provides an overview of sustainable employment practices, covering aspects such as occupational safety and health, fairness, and career development.

In the 2023 sustainability report, the company has implemented equality to all employees such as a transparent and fair recruitment process. Not employing underage labor or forced labor in accordance with labor regulations, providing employee rewards and services in accordance with the regional minimum wage (UMR), committing to providing a safe and comfortable workplace and implementing occupational health and safety (K3) programs.
