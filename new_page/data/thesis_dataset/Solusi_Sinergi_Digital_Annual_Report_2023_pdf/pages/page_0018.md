Dengan mengangkat Tema "Integrating Digital Sustainability" di tahun 2023 ini, Perseroan sebagai pelaku bisnis di Industri Infrastruktur telekomunikasi berharap dapat menjadi contoh penerapan prinsip dan praktik keberkelanjutan ke dalam setiap tahapan siklus bisnis dan pengembangan ekosistem digital. Perseroan sepanjang tahun 2023 telah mampu menunjukkan kinerja yang baik. Hal tersebut ditunjukkan melalui kinerja pendapatan dan laba bersih yang positif Kinerja yang baik ini tidak lepas dari pengoperasian secara penuh jaringan serat optik di sepanjang jalur kereta Pulau Jawa yang telah menunjukkan traksi penerimaan pasar yang baik dalam menunjang misi Perseroan untuk menghadirkan layanan konektivitas yang handal dan terjangkau bagi Masyarakat.

Sebagai perusahaan yang tercatat di Pasar Modal Indonesia sejak tahun 2020, Surge dengan konsisten telah menaati setiap kebijakan serta regulasi yang dikeluarkan oleh Otoritas Jasa keuangan (OJK). Serta pelaksanaan tata kelola yang baik dengan mengikuti prosedur dan mekanisme pengawasan untuk memastikan semua aktivitas perusahaan berjalan sesuai dengan prinsip-prinsip etika dan integritas bisnis.

Dalam hal ini, Dewan Komisaris bertanggung jawab dalam memastikan kinerja yang optimal dari manajemen dan Direksi Surge. Dengan terlibat secara langsung dalam mengawasi strategi perusahaan, keputusan manajemen dan menjaga akuntabilitas dalam setiap langkah yang diambil. Sehingga dapat memberikan nilai tambah yang positif bagi pemegang saham dan pemangku kepentingan lainnya.

Through this Annual Report, we submit it as a form of accountability for the management of the Company throughout the financial year.

Therefore, as a representative of the Board of Commissioners, let me present an evaluation of the Company's performance during 2023, while still prioritizing the principles of openness, transparency and compliance.

With the theme "Integrating Digital Sustainability" in 2023, the Company as a business player in the telecommunications infrastructure industry hopes to be an example of applying sustainability principles and practices into every stage of the business cycle and digital ecosystem development. The Company throughout 2023 has been able to show good performance. This is shown through positive revenue and net profit performance. This good performance cannot be separated from the full operation of the fiber optic network along the Java Island railway line which has shown good market acceptance traction in supporting the Company's mission to provide reliable and affordable connectivity services for the community.

As a company listed on the Indonesian Capital Market since 2020, Surge has consistently complied with every policy and regulation issued by the Financial Services Authority (OJK). As well as the implementation of good governance by following procedures and supervisory mechanisms to ensure all company activities run in accordance with the principles of business ethics and integrity.

In this regard, the Board of Commissioners is responsible for ensuring the optimal performance of Surge's management and Board of Directors. By being directly involved in overseeing the company's strategy, management decisions and maintaining accountability in every step taken. So that it can provide positive added value for shareholders and other stakeholders.

In terms of financial performance, the Board of Commissioners considers that the Board of Directors has recorded a good performance.
