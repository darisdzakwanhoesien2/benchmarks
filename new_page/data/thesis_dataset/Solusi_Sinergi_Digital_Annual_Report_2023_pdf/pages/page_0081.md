# Target &amp; Strategi 2024

Tahun 2024 menjadi periode penuh peluang dan tantangan bagi Perseroan. Adapun Perseroan mentargetkan percepatan penetrasi layanan telekomunikasi/konektivitas yang dapat mengoptimalkan utilisasi infrastruktur konektivitas Perseroan. Pada tahun 2024, Perseroan akan mengimplementasikan berbagai kerjasama strategis dengan berbagai stakeholder industri telekomunikasi di Indonesia dalam mewujudkan visi dan misi Perseroan melalui pemerataan konektivitas yang handal dan terjangkau bagi seluruh masyarakat.

Perseroan akan memfokuskan kerjasama strategis dalam mengoptimalkan potensi peningkatan pengguna fixed broadband di sepanjang jalur kereta Pulau Jawa yang saat ini penetrasinya baru mencapai 17%. The year 2024 is a period full of opportunities and challenges for the Company. The Company targets to accelerate the penetration of telecommunication/connectivity services that can optimize the utilization of the Company's connectivity infrastructure. In 2024, the Company will implement various strategic partnerships with various telecommunication industry stakeholders in Indonesia in realizing the Company's vision and mission through equitable distribution of reliable and affordable connectivity for the entire community.

The Company will focus on strategic cooperation in optimizing the potential increase in fixed broadband users along the Java Island railway line, which currently has a penetration of only 17%.
