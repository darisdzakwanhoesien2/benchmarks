# Company Performance

By sticking to its vision and mission, the Company believes it can achieve its best performance and optimize the potential of its digital ecosystem. Since offering its shares publicly on the Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX) under the ticker code WIFI, the Company has continued to grow and sharpen its focus on three business pillars, namely: the telecommunications business pillar, the advertising media business pillar and the digital product business pillar.

Currently, the Company is focusing on operating a reliable, large capacity fiber optic infrastructure along 6,927 km covering railways in Java, provincial roads, and toll roads which are expected to be the motor of the Company's business growth going forward. The Company's infrastructure is expected to help fulfill the need for reliable and affordable internet connectivity for all levels of society in Java.

The reliability of connectivity built by the company by combining fiber optic networks on railways-toll roads-provincial roads is the main key for the company to win business competition in the future which presents reliable and efficient connectivity solutions for all business partners of the Company.
