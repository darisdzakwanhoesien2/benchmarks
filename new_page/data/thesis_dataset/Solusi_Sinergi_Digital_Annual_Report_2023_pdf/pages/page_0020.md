# Perubahan Komposisi Dewan Komisaris

Berdasarkan Keputusan Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Tahunan (RUPST) tanggal 2 Agustus 2023, RUPS telah mengangkat Bapak Hermansjah Haryono sebagai Komisaris Utama perseroan, dan menerima pengunduran diri Komisaris Utama Bapak Rudiantara, terhitung sejak pengangkatan Bapak Hermansjah Haryono efektif sebagai Komisaris, yaitu tanggal 2 Agustus 2023. Segenap Dewan Komisaris mengucapkan terima kasih yang sebesar-besarnya dan memberikan apresiasi yang setinggi-tingginya kepada Bapak Rudiantara atas seluruh kontribusi dan dedikasinya selama menjadi bagian dalam Jajaran Komisaris.

Akhir kata, kami selaku Dewan Komisaris menyampaikan terima kasih kepada jajaran Direksi, manajemen, dan seluruh karyawan, serta semua pihak yang turut berperan serta dengan pikiran, pengalaman, dan kerja keras sepanjang tahun 2023, dan juga pada Pemegang Saham dan semua pemangku kepentingan atas dukungan dan partisipasi dalam meningkatkan pertumbuhan perseroan yang berkelanjutan ini.

# Changes in the Composition of the Board of Commissioners

Based on the Resolution of the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders (AGMS) dated August 2, 2023, the AGMS has appointed Mr. Hermansjah Haryono as President Commissioner of the company, and accepted the resignation of President Commissioner Mr. Rudiantara, effective as of the appointment of Mr. Hermansjah Haryono as Commissioner, namely August 2, 2023. The entire Board of Commissioners would like to express its deepest gratitude and highest appreciation to Mr. Rudiantara for all his contributions and dedication during his time as part of the Board of Commissioners.

Finally, we as the Board of Commissioners would like to thank the Board of Directors, management, and all employees, as well as all parties who participated with their thoughts, experiences, and hard work throughout 2023, and also to the Shareholders and all stakeholders for their support and participation in enhancing the company's sustainable growth.

Atas nama Dewan Komisaris,

On behalf of the Board of Commissioners,

Hermansjah Hartono

Komisaris Utama

President Commissioner and Independent Commissioner
