**Akusisi Entitas Anak (lanjutan)**

PT Laju Inti Digital (“LIDI”)

Berdasarkan Akta Notaris No. 27 dari Notaris Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., tanggal 11 Februari 2021, AMI membeli saham PT Laju Inti Digital (dahulu PT Kereta Teknologi Listrik) dari PT Lintas Maju Maxima sebanyak 200 lembar saham, sehingga total kepemilikan saham AMI atas LIDI menjadi 420 lembar saham atau ekuivalen dengan 70%. Hal ini Akta ini telah mendapat persetujuan Menteri Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui Surat Keputusan No. AHU-AH.01.03-0111664 tanggal 22 Februari 2021.

**Pelepasan Entitas Anak**

PT Jalani Operasional Bersama (“JOB”)

Berdasarkan Akta Pernyataan Keputusan Pemegang Saham Perseroan Terbatas No. 41 tanggal 17 Oktober 2023 dari Notaris Janty Lega, S.H., dan telah mendapat pengesahan dari Kementerian Hukum dan Hak Asasi Manusia Republik Indonesia melalui Surat Keputusan No. AHU-AH.01.09-0175984 tanggal 20 Oktober 2023, Perusahaan melepas seluruh kepemilikan saham JOB kepada pihak ketiga sebanyak 330 lembar, dengan harga jual sebesar Rp 330.000.000. Selisih antara harga pelepasan dengan nilai buku investasi JOB dicatat sebagai “Laba Pelepasan Entitas Anak” dan disajikan sebagai bagian dari penghasilan lain-lain pada laporan laba rugi dan penghasilan komprehensif lain konsolidasian.

**Acquisition of Subsidiaries (continued)**

PT Laju Inti Digital (“LIDI”)

Based on Notarial Deed No. 27 from Notary Kezia Janty Lega, S.H., M.Kn., dated February 11, 2021, AMI purchased shares of PT Laju Inti Digital (formerly PT Kereta Teknologi Listrik) from PT Lintas Maju Maxima totaling 200 shares, therefore the total shares ownership of AMI in LIDI is 420 shares, equivalent to 70%. This deed has been approved by the Minister of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through Decision Letter No. AHU-AH.01.03-0111664 date February 22, 2021.

**Divestment of Subsidiaries**

PT Jalani Operasional Bersama (“JOB”)

Based on the Deed of Limited Liability Company Shareholder Decision Statement No. 41 dated October 17, 2023 from Notary Janty Lega, S.H., and has received approval from the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia through a Decree No. AHU-AH.01.09-0175984 dated October 20, 2023, the Company released all ownership JOB shares to third parties amounted to 330 shares, with a selling price of Rp 330,000,000. The difference between the disposal price and the book value of the JOB investment is recorded as “Profit on Disposal of Subsidiary Entity” and is presented as part of other income in the consolidated statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income.
