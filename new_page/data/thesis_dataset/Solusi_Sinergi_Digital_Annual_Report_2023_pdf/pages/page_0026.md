Fokus Perseroan untuk tahun 2022 adalah optimalisasi ekosistem digital dalam mencapai pertumbuhan yang berkelanjutan. # Strategy Implementation in 2023

The Company's focus for 2022 is to optimize the digital ecosystem to achieve sustainable growth. With the Java Backbone fiber optic network fully operational, the Company focuses on providing reliable and affordable connectivity, the Company collaborates with various stakeholders in the telecommunications industry such as Internet Service Provider (ISP), Cloud Provider, Tower Company, Telco Provider, and others.

So far, the Company has prepared a number of business models to optimize the acceleration of market adoption of connectivity services as well as accelerate the development of the digital ecosystem in Indonesia.
