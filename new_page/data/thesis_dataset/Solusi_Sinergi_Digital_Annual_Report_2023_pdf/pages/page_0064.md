# Tinjauan Operasional

Operational Overview

Perseroan memulai operasi bisnis sejak tahun 2012, dan telah bertransformasi menjadi perusahaan infrastruktur digital terpercaya. Perseroan memiliki 3 pilar bisnis utama yaitu pilar bisnis periklanan, segmen bisnis telekomunikasi, dan segmen bisnis produk digital. Ke depan segmen bisnis telekomunikasi akan menjadi pendorong utama kinerja Perseroan yang juga akan mendukung perkembangan segmen bisnis lainnya.

Sampai saat ini, Perseroan telah mengoperasikan jaringan serat optik sepanjang 6.927 km di Pulau Jawa. Adapun infrastruktur Java Backbone yang dibangun Perseroan mengkombinasikan 3 (tiga) segmen yang berbeda yaitu jalur rel kereta api Pulau Jawa, jalan tol, dan juga jalan provinsi. Adapun kapasitas terpasang sebesar 144 core dengan kapasitas bandwidth maksimum hingga 64 Tbps. Perseroan terus-menerus mengembangkan infrastruktur yang dimiliki dalam menyediakan layanan konektivitas yang handal dan terjangkau bagi seluruh masyarakat.

The Company started business operations in 2012, and has transformed into a trusted digital infrastructure company. The Company has 3 main business pillars, namely advertising business pillar, telecommunication business segment, and digital product business segment. Going forward, the telecommunications business segment will be the main driver of the Company's performance which will also support the development of other business segments.

To date, the Company has operated 6,927 km of fiber optic network in Java. The Java Backbone infrastructure built by the Company combines 3 (three) different segments, namely the Java Island railroad line, toll roads, and also provincial roads. The installed capacity is 144 cores with a maximum bandwidth capacity of up to 64 Tbps. The Company continues to develop its infrastructure to provide reliable and affordable connectivity services for the entire community.
