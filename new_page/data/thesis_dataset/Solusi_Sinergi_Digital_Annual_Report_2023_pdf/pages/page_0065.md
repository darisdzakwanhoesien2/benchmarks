Tinjauan Operational
Operational Overview

Selain itu, Perseroan juga telah mengoperasikan 58 titik lokasi Edge Data Center (EDC) yang terhubung dengan jaringan serat optik Perseroan di sepanjang jalur kereta. Adapun berbagai layanan konektivitas yang dapat diakses pelaku telekomunikasi antara lain Leased Core (Dark Fiber), Leased Line (Bandwidth), Colocation, Content Delivery Network (CDN), Tower and Fiberization, dan layanan lainnya. Saat ini, Perseroan memiliki berbagai klien dari berbagai segmen seperti Data Center, Telco Operator, Internet Service Provider (ISP), dan Cloud Provider.

Kinerja Pertumbuhan Pendapatan Perseroan didukung oleh segmen bisnis periklanan dan telekomunikasi, dimana segmen bisnis periklanan Perseroan mencatat pendapatan bersih sebesar 260 miliar pada periode 31 Desember 2023. Sementara pada segmen bisnis telekomunikasi Perseroan mencatat Pendapatan Bersih sebesar 122 Miliar pada 31 Desember tahun 2023.

In addition, the Company has also operated 58 Edge Data Center (EDC) locations connected to the Company's fiber optic network along the train line. The various connectivity services that can be accessed by telecommunication players include Leased Core (Dark Fiber), Leased Line (Bandwidth), Colocation, Content Delivery Network (CDN), Tower and Fiberization, and other services. Currently, the Company has various clients from various segments such as Data Center, Telco Operator, Internet Service Provider (ISP), and Cloud Provider.

The Company's revenue growth performance was supported by the advertising and telecommunications business segments, where the Company's advertising business segment recorded net revenue of 260 billion as of December 31, 2023. While in the telecommunications business segment, the Company recorded Net Revenue of 122 billion as of December 31, 2023.

# Wilayah Operational Emiten
# Operational Area of the Issuer

Perseroan menjalankan operasi bisnisnya secara nasional, Pada segmen bisnis periklanan dan segmen produk digital, Perseroan memiliki berbagai asset-aset media yang tersebar dan beroperasi di berbagai wilayah di seluruh Indonesia. Sementara pada segmen bisnis telekomunikasi, saat ini Perseroan memiliki wilayah operasional di sepanjang Pulau Jawa yang terdiri atas 3 segmen berbeda yakni jalur kereta, jalur jalan tol, dan jalur jalan provinsi dengan total jaringan sepanjang 6.927 km.yang membentang dari Merak sampai dengan Banyuwangi.

In the advertising business segment and digital product segment, the Company has various media assets spread and operating in various regions throughout Indonesia. While in the telecommunications business segment, the Company currently has operational areas along Java Island consisting of 3 different segments, namely railways, toll roads, and provincial roads with a total network of 6,927 km, stretching from Merak to Banyuwangi.
