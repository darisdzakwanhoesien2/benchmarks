# Sekretaris Perusahaan

Corporate Secretary

Berdasarkan Surat Keputusan Direksi Perseroan No. 002/SK-DIR/SSD/XI/2021 tertanggal 22 November 2021 tentang Pengangkatan Sekretaris Perseroan, Perseroan telah mengangkat GILMAN PRADANA NUGRAHA sebagai Sekretaris Perseroan sesuai dengan Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan Nomor 35/POJK.04/2014 tentang Sekretaris Perseroan Emiten atau Perseroan Publik.

Based on the Decree of the Board of Directors of the Company No. 002 / SK-DIR / SSD / XI / 2021 dated 22 November 2021 concerning the Appointment of the Corporate Secretary, the Company has appointed GILMAN PRADANA NUGRAHA as the Corporate Secretary following the Financial Services Authority Regulation Number 35 / POJK.04 / 2014 concerning Corporate Secretary Issuers or Public Companies.
