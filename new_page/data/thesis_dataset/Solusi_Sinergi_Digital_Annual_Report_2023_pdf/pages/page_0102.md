## Rapat
Berdasarkan Pasal 16 ayat (1) Peraturan OJK No. 33/2014, Direksi wajib mengadakan rapat Direksi secara berkala paling kurang 1 (satu) kali dalam setiap bulan. Pasal 16 ayat (2) mengatur lebih lanjut bahwa rapat Direksi tersebut dapat dilangsungkan apabila dihadiri mayoritas dari seluruh anggota Direksi. Rapat anggota Direksi dilakukan secara bulanan dan rapat tersebut dihadiri oleh seluruh anggota Direksi.

## Meeting
Pursuant to Article 16 paragraph (1) of OJK Regulation No. 33/2014, the Board of Directors is obliged to hold a Board of Directors meeting periodically at least 1 (one) time in every month. Article 16 paragraph (2) further regulates that such meeting of the Board of Directors may be held if attended by a majority of all members of the Board of Directors. Board of Directors. Meetings of the Board of Directors are conducted on a monthly basis and the meetings are attended by all members of the Board of Directors.

### 1. Meeting Agenda
Throughout 2023, the Board of Directors Meeting has discussed several important matters, among others:
- 2024 Business Plan Sustainable Finance Action Plan (RBB and RAKB)
- Operational discussion
- Financial performance discussion
- Business and digital performance
- Internal audit report
- Regulatory Compliance and Financial Crime Compliance report
- Strategy and Policy
- Organizational Structure Changes
- Fraud Risk Management

### 2. Agenda of the Joint Meeting of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners
During 2023, the agenda of the Joint Meeting discussed among others regarding work plans, operational performance, financial performance report, guidelines and work rules of the Board of Directors, and digital updates.
