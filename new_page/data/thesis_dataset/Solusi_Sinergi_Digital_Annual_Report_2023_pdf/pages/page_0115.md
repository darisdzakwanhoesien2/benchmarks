## Periode dan Masa Jabatan Anggota Komite Nominasi &amp; Remunerasi

Anggota Komite Nominasi &amp; Remunerasi diangkat untuk masa kerja 5 (lima) tahun dengan memperhatikan masa jabatan Dewan Komisaris sebagaimana diatur dalam Anggaran Dasar Perseroan. Periode tugas anggota Komite Nominasi &amp; Remunerasi yang berasal dari Dewan Komisaris tidak boleh lebih lama daripada masa jabatan Komisaris dan hanya bisa diangkat kembali hanya untuk 1 (satu) periode berikutnya.

## Piagam Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi

Perseroan juga telah memiliki Piagam Komite Nominasi &amp; Remunerasi tertanggal 17 Juli 2020 sebagai acuan Komite Nominasi &amp; Remunerasi menjalankan tugas dan tanggung jawab sesuai dengan Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan Nomor 34/POJK.04/2014 tentang Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi Emiten atau Perseroan Publik ("POJK 34/2014").

## Frekuensi Rapat dan Kehadiran

Sepanjang 2023, Komite Nominasi &amp; Remunerasi mengadakan 4 (empat) kali rapat formal secara online dengan tingkat kehadiran 100%.

## Term and Period of the Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee Members

Members of the Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee are appointed for a 5 (five) year term by taking into consideration the Board of Commissioners' length of service as stipulated in the Company's Article of Association. The length of service of the Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee Members from the Board of Commissioners may not be longer than the length of service of the Commissioners and can only be reappointed for the next 1 (one) term.

## Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee Charter

The Company also has a Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee Charter dated July 17, 2020, as a reference for the Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee to carry out its duties and responsibilities in accordance with the issuance of Financial Services Authority Regulation Number 34/POJK.04/2014 regarding the Nomination and Remuneration Committee of Issuers or Public Companies ("POJK 34/2014").

## Meeting Frequency and Attendance

Throughout 2023, the Nomination &amp; Remuneration Committee has held 4 (four) times online formal meeting with 100% attendance.
