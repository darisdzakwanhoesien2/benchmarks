# Ikhtisar Saham

Perseroan tercatat di Bursa Efek Indonesia pada tanggal 30 Desember 2020 dengan kode WIFI. Sepanjang tahun 2023 tercatat rata-rata volume perdagangan saham harian sebesar 167.801.459 lembar saham dengan harga saham penutupan pada 29 Desember 2023 sebesar Rp. 154 / lembar saham. Sementara total saham yang beredar sampai dengan 29 Desember 2023 adalah sejumlah 2.359.355.118 lembar saham dengan total kapitalisasi pasar sebesar Rp. 363.340.688.172.

The Company was listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange on December 30, 2020 with the code WIFI. Throughout 2023, the average daily share trading volume was recorded at 167.801.459 shares with a closing share price on December 29, 2023 of Rp. 154 / share. While the total shares outstanding as of December 29, 2023 were 2.359.355.118 shares with a total market capitalization of Rp. 363.340.688.172.
