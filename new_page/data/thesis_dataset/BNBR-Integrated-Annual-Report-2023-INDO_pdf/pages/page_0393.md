g. Receivable from Commissioners and Directors (Note 16)

The Group extended non-interest bearing loans to commissioners and directors for the purchase of houses and vehicles. The balances of the loans to commissioners and directors as of December 31, 2023 and 2022, amounted to Rp0.06 billion and Rp0.11 billion, respectively, and are presented as part of "Other Non-Current Assets" in the consolidated statements of financial position.
