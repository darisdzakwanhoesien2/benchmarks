a. Silvery Moon Investments Ltd

On September 30, 2022, Golden Glades Limited (GGL) transferred its remaining receivables from the Company to Silvery Moon Investment Ltd (SMIL) amounting to USD30.5 million or equivalent to Rp479.9 billion. Due to such transfer, since September 30, 2022, the Company signed an Payable Acknowledgement Agreement with SMIL which was due on September 30, 2023.
