## 3. Keberagaman Karyawan [405-1]
Di tahun 2022, keberagaman PermataBankers ditunjukkan berdasarkan komposisi usia dan gender yang dapat dilihat mulai dari level Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, hingga karyawan. Selain itu, keberagaman karyawan juga ditunjukkan dengan adanya karyawan difabel yang dipekerjakan PermataBank.

Saat ini penyandang disabilitas yang bekerja di PermataBank sebanyak 21 tenaga kerja, jumlah ini sama dengan tahun lalu. Tenaga kerja disabilitas ini bekerja di bagian telesales, divisi bisnis hingga fungsional. PermataBank memberikan perhatian khusus bagi penyandang disabilitas dengan mendukung pengembangan mereka melalui program Permata BRAVE dan bekerja sama dengan lembaga vokasi disabilitas untuk meningkatkan kemampuan dan kemandirian mereka.

## 3. Employee Diversity [405-1]
In 2022, PermataBankers' diversity is shown based on the age and gender composition that can be seen from the level of the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, to employees. In addition, the diversity of employees is also shown by the presence of employees with disabilities that employed by PermataBank.

Currently, people with disabilities who work at PermataBank are 21 employees, this number is the same from the previous year. The employees with disabilities works in telesales, business and functional divisions. PermataBank pays special attention to people with disabilities by supporting their development through the Permata BRAVE program and collaborating with disability vocational institutions to improve their abilities and independence.
