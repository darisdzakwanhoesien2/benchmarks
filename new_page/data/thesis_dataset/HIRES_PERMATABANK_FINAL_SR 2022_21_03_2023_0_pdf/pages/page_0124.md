**Catatan I Note:**
Sepanjang Bank beroperasional, PermataBank hanya memiliki karyawan dengan status permanen dan kontrak. PermataBank tidak mempekerjakan karyawan part-time atau non-guaranteed hours employee (pekerja harian). Karyawan permanen dan kontrak adalah karyawan yang bekerja full time.
During the Bank's operations, PermataBank only has employees with permanent and contract status. PermataBank does not employ part-time employees or non-guaranteed hours employees. Permanent and contract employees are employees who work full time.

Selain memiliki pekerja berstatus karyawan permanen dan kontrak, PermataBank juga memiliki pekerja yang tidak berstatus karyawan, yaitu pekerja magang. Program pemagangan yang diberikan meliputi pemberian teori dan praktik di unit pelatihan PermataBank dengan komposisi paling banyak 25% dari komposisi program pemagangan serta praktik kerja di unit kerja PermataBank, dengan komposisi paling sedikit 75% dari komposisi program pemagangan. [2-8]

In addition to having permanent and contract employee status, PermataBank also has workers who do not have employee status, namely interns. The internship program includes providing theory and practice in the PermataBank training unit with a composition of at the most 25% of the composition of the internship program and work practice in the PermataBank work unit, with a composition of at the least 75% of the composition of the internship program. [2-8]
