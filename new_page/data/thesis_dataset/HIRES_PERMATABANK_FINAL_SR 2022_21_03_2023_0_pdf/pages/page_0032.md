# LAPORAN DIREKSI
REPORT FROM THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS

## Pemangku Kepentingan yang Terhormat,

Sepanjang tahun 2022, perekonomian global masih diliputi dengan ketidakpastian. Pertumbuhan ekonomi dunia masih diperkirakan melambat dan mengalami stagflasi. Pemulihan ekonomi pasca COVID-19 masih terus berlangsung. Perubahan gaya hidup di new normal mempengaruhi segala aspek kehidupan secara signifikan. Hal ini bisa dilihat dari kebiasaan dan perilaku masyarakat, mulai dari cara belajar dan bekerja, sampai cara berbelanja dan berbisnis yang mulai banyak beralih ke digital. Pergeseran perilaku masyarakat ini mendorong akselerasi digital perbankan di Indonesia. Kami percaya, momentum ini menjadi peluang Bank untuk tumbuh sekaligus berkontribusi pada keberlanjutan. Dalam jangka panjang, digitalisasi akan membantu mengurangi emisi Gas Rumah Kaca (GRK) melalui penerapan pola hidup ramah lingkungan, meningkatkan jangkauan inklusi keuangan, dan mempermudah akses perbankan.

## Strategi Merespons Tantangan Keberlanjutan

Perjalanan dalam penerapan keberlanjutan memerlukan komitmen dan strategi yang tepat sehingga penerapan keberlanjutan dapat terlaksana dan terintegrasi dalam kegiatan operasional Bank. Kami menyadari hal tersebut memerlukan waktu, ditambah dengan pemahaman terkait penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan yang masih belum merata di kalangan pemangku kepentingan kami. Kami percaya, PermataBank dapat mengoptimalkan penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan apabila memiliki pemahaman yang sama antara Bank dengan pemangku kepentingan.

## Dear Distinguished Stakeholders,

The global economy continued to be marked by uncertainties throughout 2022. A slowdown of the global economic growth and stagflation had been anticipated, while recovery process post-COVID-19 was ongoing. In a new normal, lifestyle changes have substantial impacts on many facets of life. The change is palpable from people's habits and behaviours, such as how they work and study as well as how they shop and conduct business with increased reliance on digital means. It was this change that propelled Indonesia's digital banking acceleration. We believe that this momentum presents a chance for the Bank to grow while making a sustainable contribution. In the long-term, digitalization will contribute to Greenhouse Gas (GHG) emission reduction through the promotion of eco-friendly lifestyles, expansion of financial inclusion, and easier banking access.

## Strategies to Address Sustainability Challenges

The sustainability journey needs the right commitment and strategy to ensure its implementation and incorporation into the Bank's operational activities. We are aware that this process takes time, particularly given the challenge of varying degree of understanding towards sustainable financing among our stakeholders. We believe, by aligning the Bank and its stakeholders' perception, PermataBank will be able to optimize its sustainable finance practices.
