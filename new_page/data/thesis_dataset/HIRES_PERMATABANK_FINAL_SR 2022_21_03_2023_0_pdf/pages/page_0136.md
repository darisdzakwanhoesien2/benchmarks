Program PermataHati Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) mewujudkan komitmen PermataBank untuk meningkatkan literasi dan inklusi keuangan di Indonesia dengan membangun ekosistem yang berkelanjutan, suportif, dan kolaboratif bagi seluruh lapisan masyarakat, termasuk para penyandang disabilitas. Program-program PermataHati dibagi dalam tiga pilar utama 3E, yaitu:

- **Education (Pendidikan)**
Memberikan edukasi literasi keuangan, serta pelatihan kesiapan dan keterampilan kerja.

- **Empowerment (Pemberdayaan)**
Memberikan pendampingan intensif dan bantuan modal usaha dalam bentuk ilmu dan keterampilan penting sesuai kebutuhan kepada peserta program berprestasi, dan memberikan beasiswa pendidikan kepada anak-anak tidak mampu melalui kerja sama dengan sejumlah mitra strategis.

- **Enhancement (Peningkatan Fasilitas)**
Memberikan bantuan perbaikan fasilitas di sekolah-sekolah, seperti kelengkapan perpustakaan dan komputer, serta membantu rehabilitas bangunan pendidikan yang rusak di wilayah-wilayah terdampak bencana alam. Selain itu, PermataHati juga menyediakan alat bantu mobilitas bagi penyandang disabilitas, seperti alat bantu penglihatan, pendengaran, maupun kaki dan tangan prostesis.

PermataHati Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) programs manifest PermataBank's commitment to strengthening financial literacy and inclusion in Indonesia by developing a sustainable, supportive and collaborative ecosystem for all groups in society including people with disabilities. The PermataHati program comprises three main pillars namely the 3Es, which are:

- **Education**
Providing financial literacy education, job readiness and vocational training.

- **Empowerment**
Extending close mentorship and capital support for top achieving-beneficiaries in the form of knowledge sharing and in-depth essential skills based on the needs of beneficiaries, as well as providing scholarships for underprivileged children in collaboration with a number of strategic partners.

- **Enhancement**
Supporting facility enhancement in schools such as libraries, computers etc, and providing rebuilding efforts to damaged school buildings in areas affected by natural disasters. Furthermore, PermataHati providing enhancement in the area of assistive devices and mobility aids for people with disabilities such as hearing and visual aids as well as prosthetic limbs.
