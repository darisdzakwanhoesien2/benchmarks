# KONFLIK KEPENTINGAN [2-15]

CONFLICTS OF INTEREST [2-15]

PermataBank memiliki Kebijakan Benturan Kepentingan (Conflict of Interest Policy) yang menetapkan pendekatan Bank untuk mengidentifikasi, mengelola, dan memitigasi konflik kepentingan baik yang nyata, yang dirasakan, maupun yang merupakan potensi. Bagi karyawan PermataBank selain mematuhi kebijakan benturan kepentingan ini juga harus mematuhi Kode Etik PermataBank.

Setiap karyawan PermataBank wajib untuk memastikan konflik kepentingan diidentifikasi, dihindari, disekalasi, dan dikelola secara tepat sesuai dengan persyaratan peraturan dan kebijakan Bank, termasuk Kode Etik PermataBank.

Setiap karyawan juga diwajibkan untuk melengkapi pelatihan mengenai konflik kepentingan serta mengikuti penyegaran tahun atas pelatihan tersebut.

Selain itu, perihal benturan kepentingan juga diatur dalam Pedoman Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi (Board Manual of Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors), kutipannya sebagai berikut:

PermataBank has a Conflict of Interest Policy that stipulates the Bank's approach to identify, manage, and mitigate conflicts of interest, both tangible, perceived, and potential. PermataBank employees, in addition to complying with this conflict-of-interest policy, must also comply with the PermataBank Code of Ethics.

Every PermataBank employee is obliged to ensure that conflicts of interest are identified, avoided, escalated, and managed appropriately in accordance with the Bank's regulations and policies, including the PermataBank's Code of Conduct.

Each employee is also required to complete conflict of interest training as well as attend an annual training refreshment.

Moreover, the issue of conflicts of interest is also regulated in the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors Guidelines, with the following quotations:

&gt; Dalam hal terjadi benturan kepentingan, anggota Direksi, anggota Dewan Komisaris, dan Pejabat Eksekutif dilarang mengambil tindakan yang dapat merugikan Bank atau mengurangi keuntungan Bank dan wajib mengungkapkan benturan kepentingan dalam setiap keputusan.

In the event of a conflict of interest, members of the Board of Directors, members of the Board of Commissioners, and Executive Officers are prohibited from taking actions that may harm the Bank or reduce the Bank's profits and are obliged to disclose the conflict of interest in any decision.
