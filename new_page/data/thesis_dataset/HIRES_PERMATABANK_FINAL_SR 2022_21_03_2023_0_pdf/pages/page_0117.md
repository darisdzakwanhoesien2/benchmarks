## 9. Sertifikasi K3
PermataBank juga menyelenggarakan program Sertifikasi Kompetensi bidang LK3 antara lain training sertifikasi Ahli K3 Umum. Pada tahun 2022, 11 orang PermataBankers telah memiliki sertifikasi K3 ini.

## 10. Audit K3
Keefektifan sistem manajemen K3 PermataBank diaudit secara periodik setiap tahun. Di tahun 2022 ini, PermataBank telah melakukan audit sistem manajemen K3 [SMK3] sesuai PP No. 50 Tahun 2012 tentang Penerapan Sistem Manajemen Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja atas 12 kantor cabang (termasuk kantor pusat) dengan tingkat pencapaian penerapan seluruhnya memuaskan. [403-7]

## 11. Penanggulangan COVID-19
Di masa pandemi, PermataBank telah berkomitmen penuh dalam menjaga kesehatan dan keselamatan karyawan, keluarga, nasabah dan seluruh pemangku kepentingan, dan pada saat yang sama memegang teguh mandat dan fungsinya sebagai institusi jasa keuangan. Seluruh unsur PermataBank, karyawan, manajemen, dan Gugus Tugas COVID-19 dengan sigap telah bekerja keras tanpa mengenal lelah secara bergotong royong. Diawali dengan pelaksanaan BCP [Business Continuity Plan] secara masif, lalu penyediaan layanan dan anggaran kesehatan menyeluruh, dilakukannya tindakan track-trace-treatment secara berkesinambungan, hingga penyediaan dan pemberian vaksin.

Pada tahun 2022 PermataBank melanjutkan program Penanggulangan COVID-19 tahun sebelumnya antara lain:
- Penerapan Prokes 6M sesuai SE No.16/2021COVID-19 secara nasional.
- Penerapan BCP.
- Pengaturan kerja secara WFO – WFH.
- Pengaturan perjalanan dinas domestik dan mancanegara.
- Penggunaan pertemuan virtual untuk rapat, training, gathering dan sejenisnya.

a. HSE month campaign via desktop wallpaper across employee PCs/Laptops on 10-16 January 2022.
b. HSE Webinar by speakers from executives of the Ministry of Manpower of the Republic of Indonesia and occupational doctors attended by the Directors and Employees of PermataBank from across Indonesia on 11 February 2022.
c. Photo competition for all employees with the theme of "Application of HSE in PermataBank Work Area" on 18 to 28 January 2022.

## 9. HSE Certification
PermataBank also organizes a Competency Certification program in HSE, including training for General HSE Expert certification. In 2022, 11 PermataBankers already have this HSE certification.

## 10. HSE Audit
The effectiveness of PermataBank's HSE management system is audited periodically every year. In 2022, PermataBank has conducted an audit of the HSE management system in accordance with PP No. 50 Year 2012 regarding Implementation of the Occupational Health and Safety Management System at 12 branch offices (including the head office) with a satisfactory level of achievement in the implementation. [403-7]

## 11. Countermeasures for COVID-19
During the pandemic, PermataBank was fully committed to maintaining the health and safety of its employees, families, customers and all stakeholders, while at the same time upholding its mandate and function as a financial services institution. All elements of PermataBank, employees, management and the COVID-19 Task Force have worked together swiftly and tirelessly. Starting with the massive implementation of BCP [Business Continuity Plan], then providing services and a comprehensive health budget, carrying out continuous track-trace-treatment actions, up to the provision and administration of vaccines.

In 2022 PermataBank will continue the previous year's COVID-19 Management program, including:
- Implementation of the 6M Prokes according to SE No. 16/2021COVID-19 nationally.
- Application of BCP.
- WFO – WFH work arrangements.
- Arrangement of domestic and foreign official travel.
- Use of virtual meetings, training, gatherings and the like.
