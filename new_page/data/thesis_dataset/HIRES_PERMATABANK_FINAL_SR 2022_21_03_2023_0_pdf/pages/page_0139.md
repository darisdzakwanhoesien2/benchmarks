# PERMATABANKIR CILIK

PermataBankir Cilik is a financial literacy education program for primary school students in grade 4 and 5. The program intends to inspire the younger generation to be "Savings Ambassadors" who promote the habit of saving to their communities. This program is equipped with interactive modules on the basics of money management and banking, teaching the students to be financially savvy and responsible from a young age.

Through simulations as well as individual and group tasks, the program also develops leadership skills, creativity, and confidence of the students as Savings Ambassadors.

A total of 167 beneficiaries of PermataBankir Cilik joined in all the series of online activities, such as virtual tours, various workshops, ranging from basic financial literacy to creative story telling through comics.
