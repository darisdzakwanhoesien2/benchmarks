# FOSTERING SUSTAINABILITY FOR STEADY GROWTH

Keberlanjutan adalah semangat PermataBank dalam menjalankan usahanya. Semangat tersebut tercermin dalam langkah-langkah inisiatif yang dilakukan Bank, di mana Bank tidak hanya memikirkan kepentingan saat ini, namun senantiasa berorientasi ke masa depan dengan memperhatikan aspek ekonomi, lingkungan, sosial dan tata kelola.

Berkaitan dengan aspek ekonomi, sebagai lembaga intermediasi, keberlanjutan diwujudkan dengan produk-produk keuangan inklusif dan kegiatan literasi keuangan. Pada aspek sosial, keberlanjutan dilakukan dengan terus mengasah kemampuan sumber daya manusia yang dimiliki, termasuk mengasah kepedulian terhadap sesama seperti yang tercermin dalam nilai PermataBank "Caring".

Di aspek lingkungan, Bank terus berupaya untuk meminimalisir dampak lingkungan melalui kebijakan kredit yang menilai risiko Lingkungan, Sosial, dan Tata Kelola (LST) dan mengontrol pemakaian energi sehingga dengan penuh kesadaran Bank dapat mengatasiejak karbon yang dihasilkan dari kegiatan operasional bisnis Bank. Sementara di aspek tata kelola, Bank senantiasa menjalankan praktik bisnis berkelanjutan sesuai dengan prinsip Good Corporate Governance (GCG) dan nilai-nilai keberlanjutan.

Dengan meletakkan dasar-dasar keberlanjutan di seluruh aspek, PermataBank optimistis akan terus tumbuh dan berkembang, serta keberadaannya memberikan nilai yang besar bagi segenap pemangku kepentingan.

Sustainability is our spirit in performing the Bank's business. This spirit is reflected in our initiatives, where the Bank not only measures on current interests, however is always oriented towards the future by taking into account the economic, environmental, social and governance aspects.

With regard to economic aspects, as an intermediation institution, we deliver sustainability through inclusive financial products and financial literacy activities. On the social aspect, sustainability is carried out by continuing to hone the abilities of our human resources, including to sharpen our concern for others as reflected in the value of PermataBank "Caring".

On the environmental aspect, the Bank continues to strive to minimize environmental impacts through credit policies that are assessed with Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) risks and to control energy consumption, hence the Bank can consciously overcome the carbon footprint resulting from business operations. Meanwhile, in terms of governance, the Bank continues to carry out sustainable business practices in accordance with Good Corporate Governance (GCG) principles and sustainability values.

By laying the foundations of sustainability in all aspects, we are optimistic that we will continue to grow and progress, and also our existence provide great value for all stakeholders.
