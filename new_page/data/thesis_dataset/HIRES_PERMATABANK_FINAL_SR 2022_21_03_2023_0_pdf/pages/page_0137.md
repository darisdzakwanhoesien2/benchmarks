# MEMBERDAYAKAN MASYARAKAT MELALUI PERMATAHATI

EMPOWERING COMMUNITIES WITH PERMATAHATI

Dikelola oleh Departemen Tanggung Jawab Sosial Perusahaan (CSR), PermataHati turut mewujudkan nilai-nilai PermataBank, terutama nilai 'Caring' ('Peduli'), melalui berbagai aktivitas yang disampaikan dan ditopang oleh Employee Volunteers (EVO), sebagai penggerak utama (key enabler) program PermataHati. Menyasar berbagai level pendidikan mulai dari tingkat sekolah dasar hingga sekolah menengah/kejuruan termasuk sekolah luar biasa, program-program PermataHati bersifat berkelanjutan dan mencakup berbagai penerima manfaat seperti guru-guru sekolah, generasi muda, wanita dan para penyandang disabilitas demi menciptakan masyarakat yang berdaya, cerdas finansial, dan siap bersaing.

PermataHati mendukung 6 aspek Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan (SDG), yaitu: No. 1 - Tanpa Kemiskinan; No. 4 - Pendidikan Berkualitas; No. 5 - Kesetaraan Gender; No. 8 - Pekerjaan Layak dan Pertumbuhan Ekonomi; No. 9 - Industri, Inovasi, dan Infrastruktur; serta No. 10 - Berkurangnya Kesenjangan.

Managed by the Corporate Affairs Division under the Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Department, PermataHati realizes PermataBank's values, especially the 'Caring' pillar, through its various activities that are delivered and supported by Employee Volunteers (EVOs) as the key enablers of PermataHati. Targeting various levels of education ranging from primary school to secondary/vocational school levels, including special education schools, PermataHati covers a wide range of beneficiaries, such as school teachers, youth, women, and people with disabilities through sustainable programs aimed to develop communities that are empowered, financially savvy, and competitive.

PermataHati supports 6 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) being, No. 1 - No Poverty; No. 4 - Quality Education; No. 5 - Gender Equality; No. 8 - Decent Work and Economic Growth; No. 9 - Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure; and No. 10 - Reduced Inequalities.
