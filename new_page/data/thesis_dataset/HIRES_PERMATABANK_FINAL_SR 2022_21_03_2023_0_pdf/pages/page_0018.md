# BUDAYA KEBERLANJUTAN [F.1, 2-24]

SUSTAINABILITY CULTURE [F.1, 2-24]

PermataBank memiliki visi dan seperangkat nilai-nilai yaitu I-PRICE (Integrity, Partnership, Responsiveness, Innovation, Caring, and Excellence) sebagai panduan bagi karyawan PermataBank yang tidak hanya dalam bekerja dan berperilaku namun juga sebagai panduan dalam menerapkan praktik keberlanjutan di kegiatan operasional Bank.

Diseminasi budaya keberlanjutan kepada seluruh pemangku kepentingan terus menerus dilakukan untuk mewujudkan visi Bank "menjadi bank pilihan dengan membina kemitraan dan menciptakan nilai bermakna bagi pemangku kepentingan". Diseminasi budaya keberlanjutan sudah dikenalkan sejak proses rekrutmen dan diinternalisasi secara terus-menerus melalui berbagai program dan kegiatan perusahaan.

Dalam rangka pelaksanaan green banking, PermataBank menerapkan budaya tanggung jawab lingkungan serta Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja (K3) kepada seluruh karyawan PermataBank melalui pesan-pesan Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) dan kampanye di media digital yang digalakkan oleh Departemen HSE.

Di bidang sosial, Bank memberikan kesempatan kepada karyawan PermataBank untuk terlibat dalam kegiatan-kegiatan yang bertujuan memberi dampak positif bagi masyarakat melalui program-program CSR PermataHati dan pemberdayaan yang berkelanjutan dalam bentuk Employee Volunteerism (EVO). Keikutsertaan PermataBankers dalam EVO memberikan manfaat positif di dalam hati masing-masing individu dan meningkatkan sense of purpose bagi mereka untuk terus berkarya dan melakukan aksi nyata.

Sedangkan diseminasi budaya keberlanjutan kepada para pemangku kepentingan lainnya dilakukan melalui berbagai program kegiatan dan diinternalisasi dalam kebijakan-kebijakan PermataBank yang mendukung prinsip keberlanjutan.

PermataBank has vision and values namely I-PRICE (Integrity, Partnership, Responsiveness, Innovation, Caring, and Excellence) as a guidance to PermataBank's employees not only in how to do their works and behave but also as a guidance in implementing sustainable practice in the Bank's operational.

To realize the vision to "be the bank of choice, nurturing relationships and creating value for stakeholders", the Bank consistently promotes sustainability culture to all stakeholders as early as the recruitment process. This culture is internalized through the Bank's various programs and activities.

PermataBank also promotes safety and environmental awareness to all employees as part of green banking practices by disseminating Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) communications and campaigns on digital by the HSE Department.

In the social sector, the Bank provides an opportunity for its employees to participate in social and empowerment activities under PermataHati CSR and Employee Volunteerism (EVO) programs. Their participation, especially in EVO programs, not only creates value for the beneficiaries, but also creating a long-lasting impression and enhancing the sense of purpose in each volunteer to continue sharing kindness.

For other stakeholders, the Bank nurtures sustainability culture through various activities and by formalizing the culture through PermataBank's policies which supported sustainability principles.
