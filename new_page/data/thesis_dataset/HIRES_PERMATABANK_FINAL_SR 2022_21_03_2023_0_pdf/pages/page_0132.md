## 4. Keadilan dalam Memberikan Penghargaan

PermataBank menyadari reward dan benefit yang kompetitif akan memberikan peluang pada peningkatan kinerja Perseroan. Dengan tingkat kesejahteraan material dan kepuasan mental yang baik, produktivitas karyawan akan meningkat dan karyawan bertalenta dapat terus dipertahankan.

PermataBank menerapkan kebijakan remunerasi Pay for Performance, dengan mempertimbangkan rasa keadilan, pencapaian Perseroan, dan ketentuan tentang pengupahan yang berlaku. Imbalan atas kinerja dianggarkan sesuai sasaran hasil usaha dan direalisasikan sesuai dengan hasil usaha.

Nilai imbalan yang diterima PermataBankers akan bergantung kepada capaian Bank, Direktorat, dan masing-masing individu. Hal ini sesuai dengan aturan regulator dan prinsip tata kelola yang baik. Dalam penerapannya, PermataBank menetapkan kebijakan remunerasi dan kesejahteraan karyawan dengan mempertimbangkan:

- Kinerja keuangan Perusahaan.
- Kompetensi dan kinerja karyawan (meritocracy).
- Asas kelayakan dan kepantasan sesuai dengan posisi/jabatan.
- Praktik remunerasi yang berlaku di pasar (market competitiveness).
- Kesetaraan internal (internal equity).

## 4. Fairness in Giving Awards

PermataBank realizes that competitive rewards and benefits will provide opportunities to improve the Company's performance. With a good level of material well-being and mental satisfaction, employee productivity will increase and talented employees can continue to be maintained.

PermataBank applies a Pay for Performance remuneration policy, taking into account the sense of fairness, the Company's achievements, and the applicable wage provisions. Rewards for performance are budgeted according to the target of business results and realized in accordance with the business results.

The rewards value received by PermataBankers will depend on the achievements of the Bank, the Directorate, and each individual. This is in accordance with the regulatory and the principles of good governance. In its implementation, PermataBank sets a remuneration and employee welfare policy by considering:

- Financial performance of the Company.
- Competence and performance of employees (meritocracy).
- The principle of eligibility and appropriateness in accordance with the position/title.
- Market competitiveness practices.
- Internal equity.
