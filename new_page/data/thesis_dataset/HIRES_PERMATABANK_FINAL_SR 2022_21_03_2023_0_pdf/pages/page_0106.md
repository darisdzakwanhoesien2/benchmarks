# KINERJA KEBERLANJUTAN SOSIAL

SOCIAL SUSTAINABILITY PERFORMANCE

PermataBank berkomitmen untuk terus mewujudkan kesejahteraan PermataBankers dengan menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang inklusif, dimana keberagaman dijunjung tinggi, hak-hak pekerja dihargai dan pembelajaran dilakukan terus menerus

PermataBank is committed to continuing to realize the welfare of PermataBankers by creating an inclusive work environment, where diversity is upheld, employee's rights are respected and learning is carried out continuously
