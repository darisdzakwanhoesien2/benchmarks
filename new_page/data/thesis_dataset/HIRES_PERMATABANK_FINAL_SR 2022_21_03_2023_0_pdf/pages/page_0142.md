# PERMATAYOUTHPRENEUR

PermataYouthPreneur ["PYP"] adalah program pelatihan, pendampingan dan pengembangan kewirausahaan digital dan literasi keuangan yang ditujukan bagi para siswa/i tingkat SMA/Kejuruan yang menginspirasi generasi muda untuk menjadi cerdas finansial, memiliki motivasi melalui aktivitas yang positif, dan bertanggungjawab serta bermanfaat bagi masyarakat.

Di tahun 2022, PYP kembali diselenggarakan secara daring. Dengan mengangkat tema "Unleash Your Future", PermataHati kembali bermitra dengan Fuzztrack (sebelumnya bernama Arkademy) dalam mengemas dan menyajikan program PYP sebagai platform diskusi pelatihan, dan pendampingan bagi para siswa/i dalam mengembangkan ide tahap awal produk FinTech masing-masing.

Peserta diberikan kelas dan pendampingan dengan beragam modul, mulai dari Business Validation; Product Validation; Software Development; Financial Literacy; hingga Soft Skills dalam bentuk kelas Presentation dan Pitching Skill, yang dibawakan oleh mentor-mentor berpengalaman dari berbagai industri dan startup ternama, seperti Paragon Tech &amp; Innovation, Bukalapak, dan Flip Id, serta didukung oleh OJK dan Direktorat Kemitraan dan Penyelarasan Dunia Usaha dan Dunia Industri (Mitras DUDI) dari Kementerian Pendidikan, Kebudayaan, Riset, dan Teknologi.

Usai mengikuti program, peserta terpilih juga mendapatkan kesempatan meraih beasiswa bootcamp untuk kelas coding yang diselenggarakan oleh Fazztrack.

# PERMATAYOUTHPRENEUR

PermataYouthPreneur ["PYP"] is a training mentorship and development program on digital entrepreneurship and financial literacy that targets senior secondary level students, including vocational high schools. The program aims to inspire youth to be financially smart, motivated through positive activities as well as to be responsible and productive members of society.

With the theme of "Unleash Your Future", the 2022 PYP was held again online. PermataHati maintained its partnership with Fazztrack (formerly called Arkademy) to design and deliver PYP as a platform of discussion, training, and mentoring for the participating students to develop their ideas of developing FinTech products in the embryo stage.

Participants are provided with various modules through classes and mentoring activities, from Business Validation; Product Validation; Software Development to Financial Literacy; to in-class presentation on Soft Skills and Pitching Skills. The courses feature experienced mentors from reputable industries and startups, such as Paragon Tech &amp; Innovation, Bukalapak, Flip Id, and supported by the OJK and the Directorate of Partnership and Alignment with Businesses and Industries (Mitras DUDI) under the Ministry of Education, Culture, Research, and Technology.

At the end of the program, selected participants were given the opportunity to win bootcamp scholarships for coding classes from Fazztrack.
