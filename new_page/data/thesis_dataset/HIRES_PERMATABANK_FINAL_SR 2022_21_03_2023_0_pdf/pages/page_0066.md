# KEBIJAKAN DAN PROSES PENENTUAN REMUNERASI [2-19, 2-20]

POLICY AND REMUNERATION DETERMINATION PROCESS [2-19, 2-20]

![img-44.jpeg](img-44.jpeg)

PermataBank telah memiliki kebijakan yang mengatur remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, termasuk penanggung jawab keuangan berkelanjutan yang tercantum dalam piagam Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi.

Pengertian remunerasi untuk Dewan Komisaris bagi PermataBank adalah kompensasi yang diberikan oleh Bank berdasarkan kontribusi anggota Dewan Komisaris selama periode tertentu. Penentuan nilai remunerasi dan fasilitas yang didapat oleh Dewan Komisaris ditentukan oleh RUPS Tahunan.

Sedangkan penetapan remunerasi untuk Direksi terkait erat dengan kinerja PermataBank. Selain itu, remunerasi juga didasarkan pada perkembangan kondisi perekonomian dalam negeri. Penentuan remunerasi Direksi berdasarkan prinsip-prinsip berikut ini:

a. Nilai remunerasi Direksi bersaing dan senantiasa dibandingkan dengan kompensasi yang berlaku pada pasar industri perbankan secara berkala.
b. Nilai remunerasi bergantung pada komponen tetap dan variabel yang relatif terhadap kinerja perusahaan baik dalam jangka pendek dan panjang serta perekonomian dalam negeri.

Penetapan remunerasi untuk penanggung jawab keuangan berkelanjutan didasarkan pada peraturan dan perundangan yang berlaku, falsafah dan tata nilai Perseroan, prinsip kehati-hatian serta mendukung pelaksanaan strategi bisnis dan pencapaian sasaran jangka pendek dan jangka panjang Perusahaan.

PermataBank has a policy that regulates the remuneration of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors, including the person in charge of sustainable finance as stated in the Remuneration and Nomination Committee charter.

The definition of remuneration for the Board of Commissioners is a compensation given by the Bank based on the contributions of members of the Board of Commissioners during a certain period. The determination of remuneration value and facilities for the Board of Commissioners is determined by the Annual GMS.

Meanwhile, the determination of remuneration for the Board of Directors is closely related to PermataBank's performance. In addition, remuneration is also based on the development of domestic economic conditions. The determination is based on the following principles:

a. The remuneration value of the Board of Directors is competitive and compared with the compensation applicable to the banking industry market periodically.
b. The remuneration value depends on fixed components and variables relative to the company's performance in the short and long term as well as the domestic economy.

The determination of remuneration for the person in charge of sustainable finance is based on the prevailing laws and regulations, the Company's philosophy and values, the principle of prudence and supports the implementation of business strategies and the achievement of the Company's short- and long-term goals.
