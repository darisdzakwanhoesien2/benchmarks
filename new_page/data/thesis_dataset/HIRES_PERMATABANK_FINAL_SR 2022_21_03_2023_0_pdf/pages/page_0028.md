- PermataBank has a whistleblowing mechanism procedure, namely Speak-Up and has reporting procedures and channels for Speak-Ups.
- Information on the number of BOD and BOC meetings and attendance rates is presented in the Corporate Governance Chapter of the 2022 Annual Report.
- Information on the audit committee, the number of audit committee meetings and the attendance rate as disclosed in the Corporate Governance Chapter in the 2022 Annual Report.
- Information on the composition of BOD and BOC by age group.
