# NOMINASI DAN SELEKSI BADAN TATA KELOLA TERTINGGI [2-10]

NOMINATION AND SELECTION OF THE HIGHEST GOVERNING ORGANS [2-10]

PermataBank memiliki Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi yang bertanggung jawab diantaranya merekomendasikan nominasi dan remunerasi untuk Dewan Komisaris. Adapun prosedur pengangkatan anggota Dewan Komisaris sebagai berikut:

1. Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi memberikan rekomendasi kandidat calon anggota Dewan Komisaris.
2. Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi selanjutnya menominasikan calon anggota Dewan Komisaris kepada Dewan Komisaris untuk disampaikan kepada RUPS dan selanjutnya diangkat sebagai anggota Dewan Komisaris.
3. Sebelum efektif menjadi anggota Dewan Komisaris dan dapat menjalankan tugasnya, calon anggota Dewan Komisaris tersebut wajib melalui proses uji penilaian kemampuan dan kepatutan (fit and proper test) oleh OJK.

Sedangkan prosedur pengangkatan anggota Direksi PermataBank sebagai berikut:

1. Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi memberikan rekomendasi kandidat calon anggota Direksi.
2. Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi selanjutnya menominasikan calon anggota Direksi kepada Dewan Komisaris untuk disampaikan kepada RUPS dan selanjutnya diangkat sebagai anggota Direksi.
3. Sebelum efektif menjadi anggota Direksi dan dapat menjalankan tugasnya, calon anggota Direksi tersebut wajib melalui proses uji penilaian kemampuan dan kepatutan (fit and proper test) oleh OJK.

PermataBank Remuneration and Nomination Committee is responsible to provide recommendations on nominations and remuneration for the Board of Commissioners. The appointment procedures for members of the Board of Commissioners are as follows:

1. The Remuneration and Nomination Committee provides recommendations for candidates' members of the Board of Commissioners.
2. The Remuneration and Nomination Committee then nominates candidates' members of the Board of Commissioners to the Board of Commissioners to be submitted to the GMS and subsequently appointed as members of the Board of Commissioners.
3. Before effectively becoming a member of the Board of Commissioners and being able to carry out their duties, prospective members of the Board of Commissioners must go through a fit and proper test process by the OJK.

Meanwhile, the appointment procedures for the members of the Board of Directors of Directors of PermataBank are as follows:

1. The Remuneration and Nomination Committee provides recommendations for candidates' members of the Board of Directors.
2. The Remuneration and Nomination Committee then nominates candidates' members of the Board of Directors to the Board of Commissioners to be submitted to the GMS and next to be appointed as members of the Board of Directors.
3. Before effectively becoming a member of the Board of Directors and being able to carry out their duties, prospective members of the Board of Directors must pass through a fit and proper test by the OJK.
