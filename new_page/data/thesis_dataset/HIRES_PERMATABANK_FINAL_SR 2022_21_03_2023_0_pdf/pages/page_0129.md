# PENGEMBANGAN PERMATABANKERS
PERMATABANKERS DEVELOPMENT

![img-64.jpeg](img-64.jpeg)

Untuk memfasilitasi akses training PermataBankers yang tanpa batas, dimana saja dan kapan saja, PermataBank mengembangkan Platform Digital Learning, salah satunya dengan membangun Future Learning Engine yang telah dilakukan melalui platform online (Instagram Live, Instagram TV), dan learning studio. Dengan adopsi teknologi, Permata Learning Week telah menjangkau lebih dari 10.000 peserta dari seluruh Indonesia dalam tiga hari program.

## Kinerja

### 1. Pelatihan Pegawai
Masih seperti tahun sebelumnya, di tahun 2022 kegiatan belajar mengajar fokus dilakukan secara daring, sejalan dengan digitalisasi proses bisnis bank. 99% PermataBankers telah mendapatkan pelatihan dengan rata-rata pembelajaran 6,1 hari per orang. Jumlah rerata pembelajaran ini meningkat dibandingkan rata-rata 4,73 hari/orang pada 2021.

To facilitate unlimited access to PermataBankers training, anywhere and anytime, PermataBank developed a Digital Learning Platform, one of which is by building a Future Learning Engine, which has been carried out through online platforms (Instagram Live, Instagram TV), and learning studios. With the adoption of technology, Permata Learning Week has reached more than 10,000 participants from all over Indonesia in the three-day program.

## Performance

### 1. Employee Training
As in the previous year, in 2022 teaching and learning activities were focused on online learning, in line with the digitization of bank business processes. 99% PermataBankers has received training with an average of 6.1 days learning per person. This average number of learnings has increased compared to an average of 4.73 days/person in 2021.
