# Pelatihan Siap Kerja dan Pendampingan UMKM

PermataHati kembali bermitra dengan Precious One, sebuah yayasan sosial yang memberdayakan penyandang disabilitas Tuna Rungu dengan memberikan pelatihan keterampilan hingga mempekerjakan mereka dalam sebuah rumah kriya.

Bersama Precious One, di tahun 2022 PermataHati menyelenggarakan 5 (lima) batch pendampingan UMKM disabilitas yang dinamakan Program "BANGGA", dengan total peserta sebanyak 80 UMKM.

Tahun ini "BANGGA" berlanjut dengan "BANGGA MAJU", program yang memberikan pendampingan lanjutan bagi para UMKM terpilih dari program "BANGGA", untuk semakin meningkatkan dan mengembangkan usaha mereka. Sebanyak 22 UMKM mengikuti program "BANGGA MAJU" ini.

Kelas-kelas dan pendampingan program BANGGA dan BANGGA MAJU selain dilakukan secara online, juga disertai kunjungan langsung ke beberapa peserta terpilih. Program pendampingan melibatkan para pembicara dan mentor dengan latar belakang dan keahlian yang beragam, mulai dari Branding, Marketing, Financial Literacy, hingga Sales serta Product dan Business Development.

# Vocational Training and MSMEs Mentoring

PermataHati has continued its partnership with Precious One, a social foundation that empowers hearing impaired people by providing them with practical skills and employment in craft production.

Together with Precious One, in 2022, PermataHati held 5 (five) mentoring programs for MSMEs through a program called "BANGGA", which reached a total of 80 participating MSMEs.

This year, BANGGA developed a program extension called "BANGGA MAJU", providing advanced mentoring and close monitoring to selected MSMEs that completed the basic BANGGA program to help in further strengthening and growing their businesses. In total, 22 participants took part in the "BANGGA MAJU" program.

The BANGGA and BANGGA MAJU mentoring programs were delivered through a combination of online activities and site visits to several selected MSMEs. The mentoring programs involved speakers and mentors with diverse backgrounds and expertise from reputable organizations, whom provided skills and knowledge from Branding, Marketing, Financial Literacy, to Sales as well as Product and Business Development.
