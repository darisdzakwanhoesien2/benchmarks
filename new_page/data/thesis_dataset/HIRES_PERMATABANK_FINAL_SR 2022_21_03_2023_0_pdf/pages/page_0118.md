## 12. Program Vaksin Gotong Royong – Booster

Untuk meningkatkan imunitas karyawan dan keluarga PermataBank juga mendukung Pemerintah dalam penanganan virus COVID-19, melalui program Vaksin Gotong Royong (VGR). Program VGR diadakan secara bertahap di mulai di bulan Februari untuk karyawan yang berada di wilayah Jabodetabek dan kota lainnya.

Pelaksanaan Vaksin Gotong Royong – Booster sebagai berikut:

1. Periode pelaksanaan: 26 Februari – 09 April 2022
2. Lokasi pelaksanaan:

- Procedures for testing and contact tracing.
- Empowering Floor Captains and security officers to supervise the implementation of the COVID Protocol (6M – Wearing masks, Washing hands with soap in running water, Keeping distance, Staying away from crowds, Reducing Mobility, Avoiding eating together).
- Routine disinfection at the Head Office and Branch Offices as needed.
- Cleaning of Air Conditioner (AC) filters at Head Office which is done once a week.
- Conducting paging reminders of the COVID-19 health protocol at the Head Office twice a day.
- Body temperature check before entering the work area building.
- COVID Awareness Staff Communication Series, through Director Messages and President Director Messages.
- PermataBank’s New COVID Center which can be accessed by cell phone.
