# KOMUNIKASI HAL-HAL KRITIS DAN MEKANISME UNTUK SARAN DAN ETIKA [2-16, 2-25, 2-26]

COMMUNICATION OF CRITICAL MATTERS AND MECHANISMS FOR ADVICE AND ETHICS [2-16, 2-25, 2-26]

Sejak tahun 2008 PermataBank telah menerapkan sistem pelaporan pelanggaran atau Whistleblowing System yang dikenal dengan nama Speak Up. Speak Up merupakan sarana partisipasi aktif seluruh karyawan PermataBank untuk menjaga lingkungan kerja yang bebas dari tindakan fraud dengan melaporkan pelanggaran-pelanggaran dan malpraktek, termasuk kecurigaan atas tindakan tersebut.

## Mekanisme Penyampaian Laporan Speak Up

Karyawan PermataBank, nasabah maupun pihak ketiga, dapat menyampaikan laporan terhadap pelanggaran termasuk dugaan pelanggaran dan potensi, rencana atau tindakan pelanggaran terhadap ketentuan yang berlaku yang diketahui di lingkungan PermataBank melalui saluran Speak Up di bawah ini:

Since 2008 PermataBank has implemented a Whistleblowing System known as Speak Up. Speak Up is a means of active participation of all PermataBank employees to maintain a work environment that is free from fraud by reporting violations and malpractices, including suspicion of such actions.

## The Mechanism of the Speak Up Report Submission

PermataBank employees, customers or third parties, can submit reports on violations including allegations of fraud and potentials, plans or actions of fraud against the applicable provisions in the PermataBank environment through the Speak Up channels below:

![img-43.jpeg](img-43.jpeg)

Setiap pelaporan atau pengaduan melalui Speak Up dicatat dalam register dan diklasifikasikan oleh Administrator berdasarkan kategori laporan yang meliputi: dugaan fraud, pelanggaran kode etik, dan laporan umum lain seperti keluh kesah atau saran.

Tata cara pelaporan dan pengelolaan laporan Speak Up serta pihak yang mengelola Speak Up diatur dalam kebijakan dan prosedur Speak Up PermataBank yang selalu dikaji dan diperbaharui secara berkala.

Any reporting or complaint through the Speak Up is registered and classified by the Administrator based on report category that includes: alleged fraud, violations of the code of conduct, and other general reports such as complaints or suggestions.

The Speak Up reports and handling procedures and persons in charge are regulated in the PermataBank Speak Up policies and procedures which are regularly reviewed and updated.
