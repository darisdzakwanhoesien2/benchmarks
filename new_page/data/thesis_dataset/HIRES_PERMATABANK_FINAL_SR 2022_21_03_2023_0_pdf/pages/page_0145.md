# PROGRAM PERMATAHATI CSR [413-1]
PERMATAHATI CSR PROGRAMS [413-1]

- Empowerment (Pemberdayaan), melalui pelatihan dan pendampingan intensif bagi Usaha Mikro Kecil &amp; Menengah (UMKM) milik penyandang disabilitas untuk meningkatkan usaha mereka, dengan berbagai materi, seperti perencanaan keuangan, investasi, branding, marketing, pengembangan usaha, dan berbagai topik terkait lainnya, serta pemberian beasiswa bagi mereka yang duduk di bangku kuliah;
- Enhancement (Peningkatan Fasilitas dan Inklusivitas), melalui kolaborasi dengan mitra-mitra dalam memberikan alat-alat bantu, seperti alat bantu mobilitas, alat bantu visual, dan alat bantu dengar.

- Empowerment, through focused and close mentoring to provide support for Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) owned by people with disabilities to improve their businesses with a wide range of programs on financial planning, investment, branding, marketing and other related topics, as well as scholarship for university students.
- Enhancement, through collaboration with strategic partners in delivering assistive devices such as mobility, visual, and hearing aids.
