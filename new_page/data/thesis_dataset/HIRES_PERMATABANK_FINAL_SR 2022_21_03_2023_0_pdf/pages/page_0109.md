# KESELAMATAN &amp; KESEHATAN KERJA

OCCUPATIONAL HEALTH &amp; SAFETY

Tantangan pandemi masih terus berlangsung meskipun perekonomian mulai pulih dan bergeliat kembali. Tahun 2022, PermataBank masih tetap menerapkan protokol kesehatan yang ketat dengan tingkat kewaspadaan yang sama seperti tahun-tahun sebelumnya. Hal ini dilakukan sebagai upaya untuk mengatasi risiko infeksi COVID-19 pada operasional dan PermataBankers sehingga dapat menekan tingkat infeksi dan operasional Bank dapat berjalan seperti biasa.

Percepatan program vaksinasi terus dilakukan. PermataBank bekerja sama dengan berbagai pihak untuk mencapai 100% vaksinasi kepada seluruh PermataBankers, keluarga, dan mitra. Hingga bulan April 2022, tingkat vaksinasi ketiga [booster] PermataBankers mencapai jumlah 1.245 orang staf karyawan [belum termasuk keluarga, outsource, dan vendor], jumlah ini terus meningkat dari tahun-tahun sebelumnya.

Selain itu, PermataBank juga terus berkolaborasi menciptakan cara-cara kerja baru di tatanan normal baru sehingga kami dapat terus memberikan standar pelayanan terbaik kepada nasabah. Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja [K3] menjadi prioritas tertinggi kami untuk meningkatkan kinerja. Melalui Departemen Security, Safety &amp; Support Management [SSSM] yang berada di bawah Divisi Corporate Real Estate Services [CRES], Direktorat Technology &amp; Operations [T&amp;O], kami mengelola kegiatan K3 sehingga kami dapat memberikan lingkungan kerja yang aman dan nyaman bagi PermataBankers, Nasabah, mitra, dan pemangku kepentingan lainnya.

PermataBank menetapkan visi K3 sebagai "Perseroan yang sehat, aman dan ramah lingkungan untuk mencapai Bisnis Berkelanjutan". Tujuan pengelolaan K3: [F:21]

a. Mencegah insiden dan kecelakaan, kesehatan, dan keselamatan kerja.
b. Mencegah pencemaran lingkungan.
c. Memastikan penggunaan konsumsi sumber daya alam [penggunaan energi dan air] dengan efektif dan optimal.
d. Meningkatkan kualitas lingkungan.
e. Mematuhi peraturan kesehatan, keselamatan dan lingkungan yang berlaku.

The challenges of the pandemic remained even though the economy is recovering and improving. In 2022, PermataBank continued to implement strict health protocols with the same level of vigilance as in previous years. This was done as an effort to overcome the risk of COVID-19 infection in operations and PermataBankers, thereby able to reduce the infection rate and the Bank operations can be performed as usual.

The acceleration of the vaccination program continues to be carried out. PermataBank collaborates with various parties to achieve 100% vaccination for all PermataBankers, families, and partners. Until April of 2022, PermataBankers' third vaccination [booster] has reached 1,245 employees [not including its family, outsource, and vendors], continued to increase from previous years.

Moreover, PermataBank also continues to collaborate to create new ways of working in the new normal era, thereby we can continue to provide excellent services to customers. Occupational Health and Safety [HSE] is our highest priority to improve performance. Through the Departemen Security, Safety &amp; Support Management [SSSM] which is under the Division of Corporate Real Estate Services [CRES], Directorate of Technology &amp; Operations [T&amp;O], we manage HSE activities in order to provide a safe and comfortable working environment for PermataBankers, Customers, partners, and other stakeholders.

PermataBank sets the vision of HSE as "A healthy, safe and environmentally friendly Company to achieve a Sustainable Business". The HSE management objectives are as follows: [F:21]

a. Prevent incidents and accidents, occupational health and safety.
b. Prevents environmental pollution.
c. Ensuring effective and optimal use of natural resource consumption [energy and water consumption].
d. Improving the quality of environment.
e. Complying with applicable health, safety and environmental regulations.
