# 5. Survei Keterikatan Pegawai

Untuk dapat mengetahui keefektifan pengelolaan sumber daya manusia, PermataBank secara rutin mengkaji tingkat keterikatan PermataBankers melalui survei tahunan Change for Better Survey yang diadakan secara berkala oleh konsultan global eksternal. Di tahun 2022, survei dilakukan oleh 95% PermataBankers yang menghasilkan skor keterlibatan 82%, dimana 93% dari karyawan telah menyatakan kebanggaan mereka terhadap PermataBank serta 94% menyatakan PermataBank tempat yang baik untuk berkontribusi.

Secara keseluruhan skor keterikatan pegawai meningkat 1% dibandingkan tahun lalu. Efektivitas diukur dengan dua masukan utama, yaitu indeks Keterikatan dan Pemberdayaan karyawan. Skor tertinggi didapat untuk dimensi terkait dengan arahan strategi bisnis dan kualitas pelayanan kepada nasabah. Peningkatan indeks tercermin di hampir seluruh Direktorat, di seluruh dimensi, serta diatas seluruh norma yang dijadikan acuan.

# 5. Employee Engagement Survey

To determine the effectiveness of human resource management, PermataBank regularly examines the level of attachment of PermataBankers through the annual Change for Better Survey, which is held regularly by external global consultants. In 2022, a survey was conducted by 95% of PermataBankers have resulted in an engagement score of 82%, whereby 93% of employees have expressed pride in PermataBank and 94% stated that PermataBank is a good place to contribute.

Overall employee engagement score increased by 1% compared to the previous year. Effectiveness is measured by two main inputs, namely the Employee Engagement and Empowerment index. The highest score is obtained for dimensions related to the business strategy direction and the quality customer service. The increase in index is reflected in almost all directorates, across all dimensions, as well as above all norms used as a reference.

# 6. Serikat Pekerja dan Perjanjian Kerja Bersama

Penghormatan pada hak-hak karyawan dan norma tenaga kerja, diwujudkan dalam penyelenggaraan hubungan industrial antara manajemen dan serikat pekerja yang mewakili karyawan yaitu Serikat Pekerja Bank Permata (SPBP). Komunikasi dua arah manajemen dan serikat pekerja amatlah penting terutama dalam suasana penuh ketidakpastian akibat pandemi dan ekonomi selama dua tahun terakhir. Setiap dua bulan forum ini membahas isu terkait ketenagakerjaan dalam rangka penciptaan lingkungan kerja yang kondusif. Manajemen dan Serikat Pekerja juga telah bersamasama menyepakati Perjanjian Kerja Bersama (PKB) dengan sukses tanpa adanya isu yang signifikan. Perundingan PKB terselesaikan dalam satu kali tahap perundingan, di mana PermataBank menjadi bank pertama yang sukses melaksanakan perundingan dengan Serikat Pekerja melalui media virtual. Terkait adanya Undang-undang Cipta Kerja, PermataBank juga merupakan bank pertama yang menerapkan pasal hybrid terkait pensiun. [2-30, 407-1]

# 6. Trade Unions and Collective Labour Agreements

Respect for employee rights and labour norms is manifested in the implementation of industrial relations between management and trade unions that represents employees, namely the Bank Permata Workers Union (SPBP). The two-way communication of management and trade unions is prerequisite, particularly in the atmosphere of uncertainty due to the pandemic and the economy over the past two years. Every two months this forum discusses issues related to employment in order to create a favourable work environment. The management and the Trade Unions have also successfully jointly entered into a Collective Labour Agreement (CLA) without any significant issues. The CLA negotiations were completed in one stage of negotiations, where PermataBank became the first bank to successfully carry out negotiations with trade unions through virtual media. On the Job Creation Law, PermataBank is also the first bank to implement a hybrid article related to pensions. [2-30, 407-1]
