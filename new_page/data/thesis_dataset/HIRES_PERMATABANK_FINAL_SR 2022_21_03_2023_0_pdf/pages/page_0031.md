# LAPORAN DIREKSI [D.1, 2-22]

REPORT FROM THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS [D.1, 2-22]

“Di tahun 2022, PermataBank mulai melakukan kajian Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) secara menyeluruh sebagai komitmen kami dalam rangka penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan ke dalam kegiatan operasional Bank. Diharapkan dengan langkah ini PermataBank dapat melakukan penyesuaian di internal Bank agar dapat sejalan dengan prinsip ESG.

PermataBank commenced a comprehensive Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) study in 2022 as part of its commitment to incorporating sustainable finance into the Bank’s operations. This measure was expected to align the Bank’s internal system with the ESG principles.

Meliza Musa Rusli
Direktur Utama | President Director
