Pelatihan ini dilaksanakan intensif selama dua bulan secara online, dengan melibatkan tim ahli hidroponik dari komunitas petani urban di Sleman, Yogyakarta.

Dalam rangka Hari Disabilitas Internasional yang jatuh pada setiap tanggal 3 Desember, PermataHati menyelenggarakan rangkaian kegiatan sebagai berikut:

a. Kampanye Digital untuk Achmad "Bang Dzoel" Zulkarnain (1991 – 2022)

Seorang tokoh disabilitas sekaligus fotografer lepas ternama asal Banyuwangi, influencer dan motivator dalam berbagai kegiatan PermataHati hingga saat ia berpulang pada Agustus 2022 lalu. Beliau terlahir sebagai tuna daksa, namun menggunakan kondisinya untuk memotivasi dan menginspirasi orang lain. Selama hidupnya, ia berhasil menjadi fotografer berprestasi yang dikenal luas baik dalam ranah nasional, maupun internasional.

Sebagai penghormatan PermataHati kepada Bang Dzoel, PermataHati mengadakan kampanye digital Comment Social Responsibility (CSR) "Tribute to Bang Dzoel" melalui akun Instagram @permatahaticsr dengan mengajak para audiens turut berdonasi bagi komunitas fotografi Bang Dzoel di Banyuwangi.

Another strategic partner of PermataHati's PermataBRAVE program, Kita Setara Indonesia, a social foundation based in South Tangerang focuses on empowering and creating equality for people with disabilities. Through "Setara", a basic vocational training program, in 2022, PermataHati and Kita Setara provided culinary skills training to 15 people with disabilities for one week at the Kita Setara Campus, which involved mentors from the Indonesia Culinary Alliance.

PermataHati also briefly collaborated in 2022 with Ngelesin, an education startup, to deliver a short hydroponic cultivation training course through "PANENKU" program to 4 people with disabilities in the Greater Jakarta area. This was an intensive, two-month online training that involved hydroponic experts from the urban farming community in Sleman, Yogyakarta.

To celebrate "International Day for People with Disabilities" which falls on December 3, PermataHati organized a series activities:

a. Digital Campaign in Memoriam of Iconic Achmad "Bang Dzoel" Zulkarnain (1991 – 2022)

A freelance photographer and well-known figure amongst people with disabilities, Banyuwangi-native Bang Dzoel was a PermataHati motivator and influencer who passed away in August 2022. Born with a physical disability, Bang Dzoel used his condition to motivate and inspire people. His life achievements were recognized nationally and internationally.

In tribute to Bang Dzoel, a digital Comment Social Responsibility (CSR) donation campaign titled "Tribute to Bang Dzoel" was held in the @permatahaticsr Instagram account encouraging the public to donate to Bang Dzoel's photography community in Banyuwangi.
