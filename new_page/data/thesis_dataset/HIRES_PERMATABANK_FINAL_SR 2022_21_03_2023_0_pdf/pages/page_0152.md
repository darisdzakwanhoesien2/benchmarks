Pada tahun 2022, PermataHati menyelenggarakan UFE secara daring dengan mengangkat tema "Making A Difference", melibatkan 20 pembicara inspiratif dari berbagai latar belakang bidang dan keahlian. UFE Bootcamp "Making A Difference" yang telah diselenggarakan secara daring pada tanggal 16 Desember 2021 khusus bagi para mitra CSR PermataBank, dan Sustainability Forum "Making A Difference" pada tanggal 10 dan 11 Februari 2022, terbagi dalam 6 sesi Conference dan 5 sesi Masterclass.

UFE "Making A Difference" diikuti oleh lebih dari 1.000 peserta dengan tingkat kepuasan keseluruhan di atas 90%.

In 2022, PermataHati held an online UFE with the theme "Making A Difference", involving 20 inspirational speakers from various backgrounds and expertise. UFE Bootcamp "Making A Difference" was held online on 16 December 2021 and was dedicated for PermataBank CSR partners, while the Sustainability Forum "Making A Difference" was held on 10 and 11 February 2022, consisting of 6 conference sessions and 5 Masterclasses.

UFE "Making A Difference" was attended by more than 1,000 participants with an overall satisfaction rate above 90%.
