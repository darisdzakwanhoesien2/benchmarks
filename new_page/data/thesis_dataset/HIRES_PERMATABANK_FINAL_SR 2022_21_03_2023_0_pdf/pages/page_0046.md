# BIDANG USAHA [C.4, 2-6]

CORE BUSINESS [C.4, 2-6]

PT Bank Permata Tbk memiliki bidang usaha sebagai Bank Umum, yang menawarkan produk dan jasa perbankan untuk membantu masyarakat serta perusahaan agar berhasil menciptakan kesejahteraan dan pertumbuhan bisnis melalui segmen usaha dari Retail Banking, Wholesale Banking, SME, serta produk dan jasa berbasis Syariah.

Selain itu, PermataBank juga memiliki layanan berbasis digital seperti PermataMobile X dan PermataNet untuk melakukan transaksi perbankan.

PT Bank Permata Tbk engages as a Commercial Bank, which offers banking products and services for the community and companies to successfully create business welfare and growth through its business segments from Retail Banking, Wholesale Banking, SME, as well as Sharia-based products and services.

PermataBank also offers digital-based services such as PermataMobile X and PermataNet for banking transactions.
