STRUCTURE AND COMPOSITION OF SUSTAINABLE GOVERNANCE [E.1, 2-9]

Struktur Tata Kelola PermataBank Governance Structure of PermataBank

Dalam pelaksanaan tugasnya, Dewan Komisaris dibantu oleh Komite Dewan Komisaris yang terdiri dari Komite Audit, Komite Pemantau Risiko, dan Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi. Untuk komposisi lengkap Dewan Komisaris dan komite-komite di bawahnya dapat dilihat pada buku Laporan Tahunan PermataBank Bab Tata Kelola Perusahaan halaman 446.

Sementara Direksi dalam pelaksanaan tugasnya dibantu oleh komite-komite yang berada di bawah fungsi Direksi, Satuan Kerja Audit Intern (SKAI), Sekretaris Perusahaan, dan unit kerja lainnya. Untuk komposisi lengkap Direksi dan komite-komite di bawahnya serta organ lainnya dapat dilihat pada buku Laporan Tahunan PermataBank Bab Tata Kelola Perusahaan halaman 446.

Hingga saat ini, PermataBank masih belum memiliki unit khusus yang bertanggung jawab terhadap penerapan keberlanjutan di lingkup Bank. Meskipun begitu, berdasarkan Rencana Aksi Keuangan Berkelanjutan (RAKB) penanggung jawab penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan berada pada organ tata kelola yaitu Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, Divisi Sekretaris Perseroan, serta unit bisnis terkait. Dalam hal ini, Direksi melakukan tinjauan dan memberikan masukan serta memberikan persetujuan atas RAKB yang disusun oleh Divisi Sekretaris Perseroan. Selanjutnya RAKB tersebut dilaporkan dan diajukan kepada Dewan Komisaris untuk dimintakan persetujuan.

In carrying out its duties, the Board of Commissioners is assisted by the Board of Commissioners' Committee consisting of the Audit Committee, Risk Monitoring Committee, and Nomination and Remuneration Committee. For the complete composition of the Board of Commissioners and its committees is presented in the PermataBank Annual Report, Corporate Governance Chapter on page 446.

Meanwhile, the Board of Directors' duties is assisted by the committees under its functions, Internal Audit Unit (IAU), Corporate Secretary, and other units. For the complete composition of the Board of Directors and the committees and other organs is presented in the PermataBank Annual Report, Corporate Governance Chapter on page 446.

PermataBank currently does not establish a special unit responsible for the implementation of sustainability within the Bank. However, based on the Sustainable Finance Action Plan (SFAP), the person in charge of implementing sustainable finance is in the governance organs, namely the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, Corporate Secretary Division, and related business units. In this case, the Board of Directors reviews and provides input and approves the SFAP prepared by the Company's Secretary Division. Furthermore, the SFAP is reported and submitted to the Board of Commissioners for approval.
