# EVALUASI KINERJA BADAN TATA KELOLA TERTINGGI [2-18]

PERFORMANCE EVALUATION OF THE HIGHEST GOVERNANCE ORGANS [2-18]

Dalam sistem tata kelola bank, PermataBank telah memiliki sistem evaluasi atas kinerja badan tata kelola tertinggi Bank, baik itu Dewan Komisaris, Direksi, maupun Penanggung Jawab Keuangan Berkelanjutan.

Penilaian terhadap kinerja anggota Dewan Komisaris Perseroan pada dasarnya dilakukan oleh pemegang saham melalui mekanisme penilaian kinerja individu dan selanjutnya dilakukan penilaian oleh wakil pemegang saham utama (Komisaris Utama).

Dalam pelaksanaannya, PermataBank melalui Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi melakukan penilaian tahunan bagi kinerja Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi berdasarkan pengungkapan kriteria yang telah ditetapkan dalam piagam Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi.

Kriteria penilaian Dewan Komisaris dilakukan berdasarkan capaian kinerja, kehadiran dalam rapat serta penyelesaian tindak lanjut yang disepakati dalam risalah rapat. Sedangkan kriteria penilaian Direksi dilakukan berdasarkan capaian kinerja dari sisi keuangan, kualitas tata kelola, efektivitas operasional serta pengembangan sumber daya manusia selama tahun buku.

Seperti halnya penilaian kinerja kepada Direksi, Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi juga melakukan penilaian kinerja tahunan bagi Direktur Utama. Tinjauan kinerja Direktur Utama bersama dengan anggota Direksi lainnya dipresentasikan dan dibahas dalam Rapat Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi.

## Kriteria dan Penilaian Direksi

Penilaian terhadap kinerja Direksi maupun masing-masing anggota Direksi PermataBank pada dasarnya mencakup:

- Performance Planning
Performance planning digunakan setiap awal tahun. Performance planning Direksi merupakan pelaksanaan dari Visi dan Misi PermataBank serta rencana strategis tahunan PermataBank.

- Performance Feedback dan Coaching
Performance Feedback dan Coaching merupakan suatu proses interaktif melalui rapat antara Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris untuk mendapatkan masukan sehubungan dengan penilaian kinerja Direksi dalam kurun waktu tertentu.

- Penilaian Tengah Tahun dan Akhir tahun
Umumnya, penilaian atas kinerja Direksi dilakukan dua kali dalam setahun.

In the bank governance system, PermataBank has the performance evaluation system for the Bank's highest governance organs, be it the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, or the Person in Charge of Sustainable Finance.

The performance assessment of members of the Board of Commissioners is basically carried out by the shareholders through an individual performance assessment mechanism and is further carried out by the representative of the main shareholder (President Commissioner).

In its implementation, PermataBank Remuneration and Nomination Committee conducts an annual performance assessment of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors based on the disclosure of the criteria set out in the Remuneration and Nomination Committee charter.

The assessment criteria of the Board of Commissioners are based on performance achievements, attendance at meetings and completion of follow-ups agreed upon in the meeting minutes. Meanwhile, the assessment criteria of the Board of Directors are based on performance achievements in terms of finances, quality of governance, operational effectiveness and human resource development during the financial year.

Similar to the Board of Directors, the Remuneration and Nomination Committee also conducts annual performance assessment for the President Director. The performance review of the President Director and other members of the Board of Directors is presented and discussed at the Remuneration and Nomination Committee Meeting.

## Criteria and Assessments of the Board of Directors

The performance assessment of the Board of Directors and each member of the Board of Directors of PermataBank includes:

- Performance Planning
Performance planning is applied at the beginning of each year. The performance planning of the Board of Directors is the implementation of PermataBank's Vision and Mission, as well as annual strategic plan.

- Performance Feedback and Coaching
Performance Feedback and Coaching is an interactive process through a meeting between the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners to obtain input on performance assessment of the Board of Directors within a certain period of time.

- Mid-Year and Year-end Assessments
Generally, the performance assessment of the Board of Directors is carried out twice a year.
