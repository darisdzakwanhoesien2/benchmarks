# Prosedur Penetapan Remunerasi Dewan Komisaris

PermataBank menganut prinsip-prinsip di bawah ini untuk menentukan nilai remunerasi Dewan Komisaris:

a. Remunerasi ditentukan sesuai dengan peraturan perundangan yang berlaku.
b. Remunerasi ditentukan berdasarkan asas keterbukaan, keseimbangan internal serta bersaing dengan tingkat remunerasi di industri perbankan.
c. Remunerasi ditentukan selaras dengan independensi Dewan Komisaris sesuai dengan perannya dalam mengawasi jalannya pengelolaan PermataBank.
d. Remunerasi ditentukan sesuai dengan kontribusi individu.
e. Prosedur Penetapan Remunerasi Direksi.

PermataBank memberikan remunerasi untuk Direksi atas dasar kinerja Direksi baik individual maupun kolektif selama periode tertentu dengan mempertimbangkan perbandingan tingkat remunerasi Direksi industri perbankan. Seperti penentuan remunerasi Dewan Komisaris, remunerasi dan fasilitas yang didapat oleh Direksi juga ditetapkan oleh RUPS Tahunan. Kewenangan RUPS dapat dilakukan oleh Dewan Komisaris atas nama RUPS setelah mendapatkan pendelegasian kewenangan RUPS.

Remunerasi untuk Direksi dirumuskan dalam sistem kompensasi yang diusulkan oleh Komite Remunerasi dan Nominasi yang terdiri dari Komisaris Independen yang menjadi ketua, dengan anggota terdiri dari satu Komisaris Independen, dua Komisaris, dan Pejabat Eksekutif yang mempunyai kapabilitas dalam pengelolaan sumber daya manusia. Hasil dari perumusan tersebut selanjutnya diusulkan dalam RUPS Tahunan untuk mendapatkan persetujuan.

Remunerasi yang diterima oleh Direksi dapat berupa remunerasi tetap serta variabel yang dapat berupa bonus dan/atau insentif jangka panjang.

# Prosedur Penetapan Remunerasi Penanggung Jawab Keuangan Berkelanjutan

Remunerasi untuk penanggung jawab keuangan berkelanjutan ditentukan berdasarkan pada cakupan tugas dan tanggung jawab masing-masing unit yang dikaitkan dengan kinerja dan risiko.

# Remuneration Determination Procedures for the Board of Commissioners

To determine the remuneration value of the Board of Commissioners, the Bank adheres to the following principles:

a. Remuneration is determined in accordance with prevailing laws and regulations.
b. Remuneration is determined based on the principle of transparency, internal balance and competitive with the level of remuneration in the banking industry.
c. Remuneration is determined in line with the independence of the Board of Commissioners in accordance with its role in overseeing the Bank's management.
d. Remuneration is determined in accordance with individual contributions.
e. Remuneration Determination Procedures for the Board of Directors.

PermataBank provides remuneration for the Board of Directors on the basis of the performance of the Board of Directors, both individually and collectively during a certain period by considering the comparison of the remuneration levels of the Board of Directors of the banking industry. Similar to the Board of Commissioners, the remuneration and facilities for the Board of Directors are also determined by the Annual GMS. The authority of the GMS can be carried out by the Board of Commissioners on behalf of the GMS after obtaining the delegation of authority of the GMS.

Remuneration for the Board of Directors is formulated in the compensation system proposed by the Remuneration and Nomination Committee consisting of an Independent Commissioner who is the chairman, with members consisting of one Independent Commissioner, two Commissioners, and Executive Officers that have capabilities in human resource management. The results are then proposed at the Annual GMS for approval.

Remuneration received by the Board of Directors can be in the form of fixed remuneration as well as variables such as bonuses and/or long-term incentives.

# Remuneration Determination Procedures for Persons in Charge of Sustainable Finance

Remuneration for the person in charge of sustainable finance is determined based on the scope of duties and responsibilities of each unit associated with performance and risk.
