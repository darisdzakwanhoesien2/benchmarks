# KINERJA KEBERLANJUTAN LINGKUNGAN

Environmental Sustainability Performance

PermataBank senantiasa memastikan operasional bisnis Bank berjalan tanpa merusak lingkungan. Selain itu, PermataBank juga memiliki kebijakan kredit yang memperhatikan risiko Lingkungan, Sosial dan Tata Kelola (LST) sebagai kontribusi dan wujud komitmen Bank dalam menjaga dan melestarikan lingkungan

PermataBank strives to ensure that the Bank's business operations performed without damaging the environment. PermataBank also has a credit policy that takes into account the Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) risks as a contribution and manifestation of the Bank's commitment to protecting and preserving the environment
