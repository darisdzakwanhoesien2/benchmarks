Prosedur yang dijalankan DELST dan divisi lainnya telah diresmikan dan disetujui oleh Direksi Perseroan dan telah diunggah dalam website Perseroan (https://ptsmi.co.id/perlindungan-lingkungan-sosial) dan disosialisasikan kepada karyawan dan debitur secara berkala. Prosedur ini diharapkan mampu membuat para debitur dapat menjalankan bisnisnya sesuai dengan prinsip yang telah ditetapkan oleh Perseroan, yakni ramah lingkungan dan sosial, serta taat terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan.

The procedures implemented by DELST and other divisions have been formally approved by the Board of Directors and have been published on the Company's official website (https://ptsmi.co.id/perlindungan-lingkungan-sosial). These procedures are regularly disseminated to employees and debtors. Through this initiative, the Company aims to enable debtors to conduct their businesses in an environmentally and socially responsible manner while remaining fully compliant with applicable laws and regulations.
