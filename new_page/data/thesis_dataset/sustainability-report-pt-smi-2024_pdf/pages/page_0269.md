Demografi Karyawan [OJK C.3.]
Per 31 Desember 2024, jumlah karyawan PT SMI tercatat sebanyak 416 orang, mengalami peningkatan sebesar 41 orang atau 10,93% dibandingkan tahun 2023, yang berjumlah 375 orang. Kenaikan ini mencerminkan pertumbuhan perusahaan yang seiring dengan peningkatan jumlah tenaga kerja yang terlibat dalam operasional perusahaan. Karyawan yang dimaksud terdiri dari 412 karyawan Perjanjian Kerja Waktu Tidak Tertentu (PKWTT) dan 4 orang karyawan Perjanjian Kerja Waktu Tertentu (PKWT). Di PT SMI tidak terdapat karyawan dengan jam kerja tidak terjamin (non-guaranteed hours employee). [GRI 2-7, 2-8]

4. Compliance with Labor Regulations and ILO Conventions:
PT SMI's policies regarding minimum employee age and clear working hours are also an implementation of several international regulations, such as ILO Convention No. 138 and No. 182, which aim to eliminate child labor and forced labor. This directly supports SDG 8.7, which requires countries and companies to end all forms of hazardous work for children and protect them from economic exploitation.

5. Positive Impacts and Results:
PT SMI's consistency in implementing clear policies on minimum employee age and working hours has had a positive impact, with no incidents or findings related to child labor or forced labor practices. As a result, PT SMI has not received any fines or sanctions related to non-compliance with regulations governing child labor and forced labor. This effort supports SDG 16.2, which aims to eliminate all forms of violence and exploitation against children.

PT SMI's efforts to ensure compliance with labor regulations prohibiting child labor and forced labor, as well as fair policies on working hours and minimum employee age, contribute to achieving SDG 8, which focuses on decent work and sustainable economic growth. In doing so, PT SMI not only ensures the sustainability of the Company's operations but also commits to actively contributing to positive social impacts by implementing labor standards in accordance with human rights principles. Employee Demographics [OJK C.3.]
As of December 31, 2024, the total number of employees at PT SMI was recorded at 416, an increase of 41 employees or 10.93% compared to 2023, which had 375 employees. This increase reflects the Company's growth, which is in line with the rising number of personnel involved in its operations. The employees consist of 412 Permanent Employees (PKWTT) and 4 Contract Employees (PKWT). There are no non-guaranteed hours employees at PT SMI. [GRI 2-7, 2-8]
