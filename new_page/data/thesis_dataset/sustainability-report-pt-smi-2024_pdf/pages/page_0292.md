Selanjutnya, untuk mengetahui pencapaian target, Perseroan melakukan evaluasi secara berkala antara lain melalui pengukuran parameter kualitas lingkungan dan K3. Hasil evaluasi tahunan disampaikan dalam kinerja K3 Perseroan. Selain evaluasi internal, dalam rangka persiapan pelaksanaan sertifikasi di tahun 2025, Perseroan juga telah menugaskan pihak eksternal untuk melakukan asesmen K3 dengan ruang lingkup sebagai berikut: [GRI 403-7]

1. Safety Management System K3 (SMK3), yang terdiri atas 5 prinsip dengan 12 elemen dan 166 kriteria.
2. Sistem Proteksi Kebakaran Aktif.
3. Sistem Proteksi Kebakaran Pasif.
4. Operation &amp; Maintenance.

Secara garis besar, hasil gap assessment tahun 2024 menunjukkan bahwa $62.5\%$ pelaksanaan K3 Perseroan telah sesuai mengacu pada kriteria Sistem Manajemen K3 (SMK3) berdasarkan Peraturan Pemerintah No. 50 tahun 2012 (64 Kriteria).

To assess the achievement of its targets, the Company conducts regular evaluations, including measurements of environmental quality and OHS parameters. The results of the annual evaluation are presented in the Company's OHS performance report. In addition to internal evaluation, in preparation for the implementation of certification in 2025, the Company has also commissioned an external party to conduct an OHS assessment with the following scope: [GRI 403-7]

1. Occupational Health and Safety Management System (SMK3), comprising 5 principles, 12 elements, and 166 criteria.
2. Active Fire Protection System.
3. Passive Fire Protection System.
4. Operation &amp; Maintenance.

In general, the results of the 2024 assessment indicate that $62.5\%$ of the Company's Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) implementation complies with the criteria of the Occupational Health and Safety Management System (SMK3) based on Government Regulation No. 50 of 2012 (64 Criteria).
