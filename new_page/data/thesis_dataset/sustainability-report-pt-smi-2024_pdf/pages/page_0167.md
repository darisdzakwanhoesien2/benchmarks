Selain butir-butir di atas, Etika Usaha dan Tata Perilaku berisi 11 Ketentuan Pokok sebagai berikut:

1. Interaksi Kerja Sesama Karyawan
2. Kepatuhan Terhadap Hukum, Ketentuan, dan Peraturan Lain
3. Menjaga Keamanan dan Keselamatan, Kesehatan Kerja, dan Lindungan Lingkungan (K3LL)
4. Menjaga Kerahasiaan Data dan Informasi Perseroan
5. Perlindungan dan Penggunaan Aset Secara Layak
6. Menghindari Benturan Kepentingan
7. Larangan Menerima atau Memberi Hadiah
8. Tidak Melakukan KKN dan Larangan Menerima Suap
9. Persaingan Usaha yang Sehat
10. Pengungkapan Informasi Keuangan
11. Kontribusi dan Aktivitas Politik

Untuk menjangkau seluruh Insan PT SMI, Perseroan melakukan sosialisasi Etika Usaha dan Tata Perilaku secara rutin melalui kegiatan Town Hall Gathering, email blast, portal internal, sosial media dan situs web Perseroan. Etika Usaha dan Tata Perilaku juga menjadi salah satu materi wajib yang disampaikan dalam program pengenalan bagi anggota Dewan Komisaris baru, anggota Dewan Pengawas Syariah baru, anggota Direksi baru dan karyawan baru. Program sosialisasi, implementasi koordinasi, serta evaluasi atas pelaksanaan kepatuhan atas Etika Usaha &amp; Tata Perilaku menjadi tanggung jawab Pejabat Etika, yaitu pejabat yang ditetapkan oleh Direksi dengan kewenangan terbatas untuk memutuskan beberapa aspek penegakan etika dan sebagai penasehat atas pertanyaan yang diajukan oleh Insan PT SMI dalam penegakan etika.

Lebih lanjut, dalam Etika Usaha dan Tata Perilaku juga telah diatur tentang media pelaporan pelanggaran, penanganan dugaan pelanggaran, keputusan dugaan pelanggaran, penghargaan dan sanksi atas pelanggaran, serta konsultasi terhadap situasi yang berpotensi menimbulkan pelanggaran Etika Usaha &amp; Tata Perilaku. Informasi selengkapnya tentang Etika Usaha &amp; Tata Perilaku bisa diakses melalui situs: https://www.ptsmi.co.id/cfind/source/files/gcg/pedoman-etika-usaha-dan-tata-perilaku-code-of-conduct-2021.pdf

Sepanjang tahun 2024, Perseroan tidak mencatatkan adanya pelanggaran Etika Usaha dan Tata Perilaku.

In addition to the aforementioned points, the Company's Business Ethics and Code of Conduct include 11 Fundamental Provisions, as follows:

1. Co-Employee Interaction
2. Compliance with Laws, Terms, and Other Regulations
3. Maintaining Security and Safety, Occupational Health, and Environmental Safeguard (K3LL)
4. Maintaining the Confidentiality of Company Data and Information
5. Protection and Proper Use of Assets
6. Avoiding Conflicts of Interest
7. Prohibition of Accepting or Giving Gifts
8. No KKN and Prohibition of Accepting Bribes
9. Fair Business Competition
10. Disclosure of Financial Information
11. Political Contributions and Activities

To reach all members of PT SMI, the Company regularly disseminates information on Business Ethics and Code of Conduct through Town Hall Gatherings, email blasts, the internal portal, social media, and the Company's website. Business Ethics and Code of Conduct are also included as mandatory topics in the orientation program for new members of the Board of Commissioners, the Sharia Supervisory Board, the Board of Directors, and new employees. The dissemination, implementation of coordination, and evaluation of compliance with Business Ethics &amp; Code of Conduct are the responsibility of the Ethics Officer, an official appointed by the Board of Directors with limited authority to decide on certain aspects of ethics enforcement and to serve as an advisor on ethical matters raised by PT SMI personnel.

Furthermore, the Business Ethics and Code of Conduct outlines reporting mechanisms for violations, handling of alleged violations, decisions on alleged violations, as well as rewards, sanctions, and consultation procedures for situations that may lead to breaches of ethical standards. For more detailed information, the Business Ethics and Code of Conduct can be accessed through the following link: https://www.ptsmi.co.id/cfind/source/files/gcg/pedoman-etika-usaha-dan-tata-perilaku-code-of-conduct-2021.pdf

Throughout 2024, the Company did not record any violations of Business Ethics and Code of Conduct.
