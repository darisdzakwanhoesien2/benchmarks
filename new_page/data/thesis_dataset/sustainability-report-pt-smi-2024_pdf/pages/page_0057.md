# Jejak Langkah

# Milestones

|  2009 | • Pendirian PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur (Persero) ("PT SMI") pada tanggal 26 Februari 2009 di bawah koordinasi Kementerian Keuangan dengan mandat menjadi katalis pembangunan infrastruktur Indonesia. • Pembiayaan infrastruktur pertama di sektor irigasi senilai Rp284 miliar. • The establishment of PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur (Persero) ("PT SMI") on February 26, 2009, under the coordination of the Ministry of Finance with a mandate of becoming a catalyst for the infrastructure development in Indonesia. • The first infrastructure financing in the irrigation sector amounted to IDR284 billion.  |
| --- | --- |
|  2010 | • Pembentukan PT IIF, Entitas Anak Perseroan yang dibentuk sebagai perusahaan patungan dengan ADB, IFC, dan DEG serta penambahan modal. • Pemerintah senilai Rp1,0 trilion (Rp600 miliar berasal dari PT SMI). • The establishment of PT IIF, a subsidiary of the Company, established as a joint venture with ADB, IFC, and DEG. Received additional capital. • The Government of IDR1.0 trillion (IDR600 billion from PT SMI).  |
|  2011 | Penugasan oleh Menteri Keuangan sebagai fasilitator penyiapan dua proyek showcase KPBU yaitu proyek Kereta Api Bandara Soekarno-Hatta dan proyek Sistem Penyediaan Air Minum Umbulan. Assigned by the Minister of Finance to act as the facilitator for the preparation of two PPP showcase projects: Soekarno-Hatta Airport Rallink project and Umbulan Water Supply System project.  |
|  2012 | • Peluncuran pilar bisnis ketiga Jasa Konsultasi melengkapi pilar Pembiayaan dan Pengembangan Proyek. • Pembiayaan proyek to Cikampek-Palimanan secara sindikasi bersama 21 lembaga keuangan. • PT Fitch Ratings Indonesia memberikan rating "AA (idn)" untuk Perseroan dengan Outlook Stabil. • Launched the third business pillar of the Advisory to complement the Financing and Project Development pillars. • Syndicated the financing for Cikampek-Palimanan toll road project with 21 financial institutions. • Received "AA (idn)" rating from PT Fitch Ratings Indonesia with Stable Outlook.  |
|  2013 | • Peresmian Indeks Infrastruktur SMinfra18 bekerja sama dengan Bursa Efek Indonesia. • Fitch Ratings juga meningkatkan peringkat National Long-Term Rating Perseroan menjadi 'AA+(idn)' dengan Outlook Stable dan memberikan peringkat international rating (BBB-/Stabil). • Official inauguration of the SMinfra18 infrastructure index in collaboration with the Indonesian Stock Exchange. • Fitch Ratings upgraded the Company's National Long-Term Rating to "AA+ (idn)" with Stable Outlook and awarded the (BBB-/Stable) international rating.  |
|  2014 | • Penghargaan "Finance Project of the Year" untuk Proyek Umbulan pada 7th Global Infrastructure Leadership Forum di New York, Amerika Serikat. • Penerbitan Obligasi pertama Perseroan, senilai Rp1 trilion dengan peringkat idAA+ dari Pefindo. • Received the "Finance Project of the Year" award for the Umbulan Project at the 7th Global Infrastructure Leadership Forum in New York, USA. • Issued the first bonds of the Company in the amount of IDR 1.0 trillion with idAA+ from Pefindo.  |
|  2015 | • Memperoleh izin perluasan sektor pembiayaan dari OJK melalui Surat OJK Nomor S-48/D.05/2015 tanggal 30 April 2015 tentang Permohonan Persetujuan Izin Penambahan Objek Pembiayaan Infrastruktur pada PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur yang meliputi infrastruktur sosial (rumah sakit, pasar, terminal, dan lainnya), infrastruktur kepariwisataan, dan rolling stock. • Pengalihan aset PIP sebesar Rp18,3 trilion memperkuat permodalan Perseroan. • Kerja sama Credit Facility dan Quasi Equity Facility dengan Bank Pembangunan Prancis (AFD) untuk pengembangan sektor energi terbarukan dan perubahan iklim. • Pembiayaan Jalan Tol Trans Sumatera Ruas Medan-Binjal dengan tenor terpanjang (25 tahun) dan grace period 15 tahun. • Obtained a license from OJK to expand its financing sectors by virtue of OJK Letter No. S-48/D.05/2015 dated April 30, 2015, concerning the License Approval Request on the Addition of Infrastructure Financing Objects for PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur, which includes social infrastructure (hospitals, markets, terminals, and others), tourism infrastructure, and rolling stock. • PIP asset transfer at the amount of IDR18.3 trillion to strengthen the Company's capital. • Credit Facility and Quasi Equity Facility cooperation with the French Development Bank (AFD) for the development of renewable energy and climate change sectors. • Financing of the Medan-Binjal sector of the Trans Sumatera Toll Road with the longest tenor, i.e. 25 years and a grace period of 15 years.  |
|  2016 | • Pefindo meningkatkan corporate rating PT SMI menjadi "AAA". • Fitch menaikkan outlook PT SMI menjadi positif dari sebelumnya stable. • Perseroan memperoleh akreditasi Green Climate Fund (GCF), pertama di Kawasan Asia Tenggara. • Aktif dalam pengembangan proyek KPBU Jaringan Serat Optik Palapa Ring Barat. • Penerbitan Obligasi melalui Penawaran Umum Obligasi Berkelanjutan I Sarana Multi Infrastruktur Tahap I Tahun 2016 sebesar Rp5 trilion dari rencana komitmen sebesar Rp30 trilion. • Peluncuran inovasi produk baru Cash Deficiency Support (CDS), diimplementasikan pada pembiayaan proyek Tol Trans Sumatera Ruas Palembang-Indralaya (tenor 25 tahun). • Penandatangan proyek-proyek KPBU pertama dengan dukungan VGF dari Pemerintah (SPAM Umbulan). • Penerapan standar Perlindungan Lingkungan dan Sosial. • Pefindo upgraded the Company's corporate rating to "AAA". • Fitch upgraded the outlook of PT SMI from stable to positive. • Became the first and only company in Southeast Asia to be accredited by the Green Climate Fund (GCF). • Participated in the development of PPP project: Palapa West Ring Optical Fiber Network. • Issued Shelf Registration Bonds Tranche I Year 2016 at the amount of IDR5 trillion from the planned commitment of IDR30 trillion. • Launched a new product, Cash Deficiency Support (CDS), which was implemented for the financing of the Palembang-Indralaya sector of the Trans Sumatera Toll Road (25-year tenor). • Signed the first Public-Private Partnership (PPP) project with Viability Gap Fund (VGF) support from the Government (Umbulan Drinking Water Supply System). • Implemented the Environmental and Social Safeguard Standards.  |
