## Keanekaragaman Hayati

Perseroan menyadari bahwa keanekaragaman hayati memiliki berbagai manfaat bagi kehidupan manusia di antaranya berkontribusi terhadap ketahanan pangan dengan menyediakan berbagai jenis tumbuhan dan hewan. Selain itu, ekosistem yang kaya akan spesies juga berperan dalam menjaga keseimbangan lingkungan, seperti pengaturan iklim, penyerapan karbon, dan penyaringan air. Merujuk manfaat yang sedemikian besar, walau bidang usahanya tidak berdampak langsung terhadap lingkungan dan keanekaragaman hayati. Salah satunya, Perseroan memastikan bahwa lokasi kegiatan usahanya tidak berdekatan atau berada di daerah konservasi ataupun memiliki keanekaragaman hayati. Hal ini dilakukan lewat pelaksanaan Prosedur Perlindungan Lingkungan dan Sosial yang mana salah satu standarnya (ESS 9) bertujuan untuk memastikan bahwa proyek-proyek yang didukung oleh Perseroan tidak menimbulkan dampak negatif signifikan terhadap keanekaragaman hayati di area sekitar proyek. [OJK F.9]

Di sisi lain, kontribusi Perseroan terhadap keanekaragaman hayati dilakukan melalui pelaksanaan Program Tanggung Jawab Sosial dan Lingkungan Badan Usaha Milik Negara (TJSL BUMN) pilar lingkungan. Program direalisasikan dengan melakukan penanaman pohon alpukat, jeruk limau dan mangrove seperti disampaikan dalam tabel off-set karbon dari pohon yang ditanam atau direstorasi secara alami oleh Perseroan di atas. [OJK F.10]

## Pengaduan Masalah Lingkungan [OJK F.16]

PT SMI berkomitmen untuk mematuhi berbagai regulasi terkait lingkungan dalam menjalankan usaha sebagai bagian dari tanggung jawab sosial dan keberlanjutan perusahaan. Komitmen tersebut diwujudkan melalui penerapan praktik-praktik ramah lingkungan yang sejalan dengan peraturan perundangan yang berlaku, seperti pengelolaan energi, emisi, limbah dan air limbah yang bertanggung jawab.

Selaras dengan itu, Perseroan juga menyediakan saluran pengaduan yang bisa dimanfaatkan oleh masyarakat atau pemangku kepentingan yang lain, termasuk apabila terdapat dampak lingkungan dari operasional maupun usaha perusahaan. Pengaduan bisa disampaikan melalui telepon, website maupun email, dan Perseroan berkomitmen untuk menindaklanjuti dan mencari solusi terbaik. Melalui pelaksanaan Pedoman dan Prosedur Pengelolaan Keluhan Masyarakat Terdampak dan Kelompok Pemerhati, PT SMI akan menerima, mencatat, dan mengelola keluhan yang telah disampaikan. Namun demikian, per 31 Desember 2024, Perseroan tidak menerima pengaduan lingkungan. [GRI 2-27][OJK F.16]

## Biodiversity

The Company recognizes that biodiversity provides numerous benefits to human life, including contributing to food security by supplying a variety of plant and animal species. Additionally, ecosystems rich in species diversity play a crucial role in maintaining environmental balance, such as climate regulation, carbon absorption, and water filtration. Given these significant benefits, and despite its business activities having no direct impact on the environment or biodiversity. One of the key steps taken by the Company is ensuring that its business activities are not located near or within conservation areas or regions with high biodiversity, through the implementation of the Environmental and Social Safeguards Procedure, in which one of the standards (ESS 9) aims to ensure that projects supported by the Company do not cause significant negative impacts on biodiversity in the areas surrounding the projects. [OJK F.9]

Furthermore, the Company contributes to biodiversity through the implementation of the State-Owned Enterprises Corporate Social and Environmental Responsibility Program (SOEs CSR) under the environmental pillar. This program is realized through the planting of avocado trees, kaffir lime trees, and mangroves, as detailed in the carbon offset table of trees planted or naturally restored by the Company. [OJK F.10]

## Environmental Complaints [OJK F.16]

PT SMI is committed to complying with various environmental regulations as part of its corporate social responsibility and sustainability efforts. This commitment is demonstrated through the implementation of environmentally friendly practices aligned with applicable laws and regulations, including responsible management of energy, emissions, waste, and wastewater.

In line with this, the Company also provides a complaint channel that can be utilized by the public and other stakeholders, including for reporting environmental impacts resulting from the Company's operations or business activities. Complaints can be submitted via telephone, the Company's website, or email, and the Company is committed to following up and seeking the best solutions. Through the Guidelines and Procedures for Grievance Management of Affected Communities and Observer Groups, PT SMI ensures that all submitted complaints are received, recorded, and addressed appropriately. However, as of December 31, 2024, the Company did not receive any environmental complaints. [GRI 2-27][OJK F.16]
