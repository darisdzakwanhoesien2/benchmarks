## Pelatihan dan Pengembangan Kemampuan Karyawan [GRI F.22]

PT SMI secara berkesinambungan menyelenggarakan berbagai program pendidikan, pelatihan dan sertifikasi untuk meningkatkan kompetensi, sekaligus memacu produktivitas, loyalitas, motivasi dan integritas karyawan. Program pengembangan kemampuan karyawan dilaksanakan sesuai dengan kebutuhan Perseroan dan terbuka bagi seluruh karyawan tanpa membedakan jenis kelamin. Seluruh program pelatihan dan pengembangan karyawan dilaksanakan oleh Divisi Sumber Daya Manusia (DSDM), Unit Kerja SMI University &amp; People Development serta dipantau/dievaluasi lewat berbagai pertemuan dan/ atau rapat komite terkait. [GRI 3-3]

## Pelatihan Karyawan

PT SMI memastikan setiap karyawan memiliki hak dan kesempatan yang setara untuk mengikuti program pelatihan yang diselenggarakan, tanpa membedakan jenjang jabatan maupun jenis kelamin. Pada tahun 2024, DSDM telah melakukan asesmen internal terhadap karyawan pada level Team Leader dan Staf untuk mengidentifikasi kompetensi yang perlu ditingkatkan (competency gap). Hasil asesmen ini menjadi dasar penyusunan Training Need Analysis dan rencana pelatihan yang akan dilakukan sepanjang tahun.

Selain itu, evaluasi pascapelatihan dilakukan dengan menggunakan metodologi Kirkpatrick untuk memastikan efektivitas program pelatihan yang telah dilaksanakan. PT SMI terus menyempurnakan Training Need Analysis dan rencana pelatihan untuk memastikan kesesuaian dengan strategi bisnis dan rencana perusahaan.

## Employee Training and Development [GRI F.22]

PT SMI consistently organizes various education, training, and certification programs to enhance employee competencies while boosting productivity, loyalty, motivation, and integrity. Employee development programs are implemented based on the Company's needs and are open to all employees, regardless of gender. All training and development programs are managed by the Human Resources Division and the SMI University &amp; People Development Work Unit, with monitoring and evaluation conducted through various meetings and/or discussions with relevant committees. [GRI 3-3]

## Employee Training

PT SMI ensures that every employee has equal rights and opportunities to participate in training programs, irrespective of their position or gender. In 2024, HC Division conducted internal assessments for employees at the Team Leader and Staff levels to measure the competency gaps of each employee for further improvement. This assessment serves as the basis for preparing the Training Need Analysis and the employee training plan for the year.

In addition, post-training evaluations are conducted using the Kirkpatrick methodology to ensure the effectiveness of the training programs implemented. PT SMI continues to improve its Training Needs Analysis and training plans to ensure alignment with the Company's business strategy and plans.
