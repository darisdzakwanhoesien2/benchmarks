# Identifikasi Bahaya dan Penilaian Risiko [GRI 403-2]

Sesuai dengan Prosedur SMK3L yang sudah diberlakukan, PT SMI menerapkan standar K3 yang meliputi keselamatan kerja, kesehatan kerja, kesehatan lingkungan kerja perkantoran, dan ergonomi perkantoran. Standar tersebut diterapkan bertujuan untuk mencegah dan mengurangi penyakit akibat kerja dan penyakit lain, mencegah kecelakaan kerja karyawan, serta menciptakan perkantoran yang aman, nyaman dan efisien untuk mendorong produktivitas kerja.

Secara umum, proses identifikasi bahaya dan penilaian risiko yang dijelaskan dalam Prosedur SMK3L dilakukan sebagai berikut:

1. Identifikasi risiko/potensi bahaya K3L.

Dalam melakukan identifikasi ini, beberapa hal yang dipertimbangkan mencakup faktor sosial, jenis kegiatan, histori kejadian, potensi kondisi darurat, desain area kerja dan sebagainya.

2. Penilaian risiko K3L.

Penilaian risiko K3L dilakukan melalui:

a. Pengukuran inherent risk dengan memperhatikan dua variabel yaitu dampak kerugian dan likelihood risiko.
b. Pengukuran control atas risiko K3L yang mencakup design effectiveness dan operating effectiveness.
c. Pengukuran overall assessment (composite risk) K3L.

# Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment [GRI 403-2]

In accordance with the applied Occupational Health, Safety, and Environmental Management System (SMK3L) Procedures, PT SMI implements OHS standards that encompass workplace safety, occupational health, office environmental health, and office ergonomics. These standards are aimed at preventing and reducing occupational diseases and other illnesses, avoiding workplace accidents, and creating a safe, comfortable, and efficient office environment to enhance work productivity.

In general, the process of hazard identification and risk assessment as described in SMK3L Procedure is carried out as follows:

1. Identification of K3L risks/potential hazards.

In carrying out this identification, several things that are considered include social factors, types of activities, history of events, potential emergency conditions, work area design etc.

2. K3L risk assessment.

K3L risk assessment is carried out through:

a. Measuring inherent risk by considering two variables, namely the impact of losses and the likelihood of risk.
b. Measuring control over K3L risks which include design effectiveness and operating effectiveness.
c. Measuring overall assessment (composite risk) K3L.

Hasil dari proses identifikasi bahaya dan penilaian risiko dirangkum dalam dokumen Hazard Identification, Risk Assessment and Risk Control (HIRARC). Dalam dokumen tersebut ditampilkan potensi dampak, tingkat risiko awal, kontrol yang sudah ada, tingkat residual risk serta tingkat risiko akhir. Dari sejumlah tingkat risiko akhir, tidak terdapat risiko yang tingkatnya masih high. Beberapa tingkat risiko akhir yang tingkatnya medium diantaranya adalah risiko kebakaran dan gempa bumi.

The results of the hazard identification and risk assessment process are summarized in the Hazard Identification, Risk Assessment and Risk Control (HIRARC) document. The document displays the potential impact, initial risk level, existing controls, residual risk level and final risk level. Of the final risk levels, there is no risk that is still high. Some of the final risk levels that are medium include fire and earthquake risks.
