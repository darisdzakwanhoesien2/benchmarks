The SMK3L Procedure also explains how the Company's employees can provide input and/or complaints regarding K3L issues, one of which is through the General Affair Service System (GASS) application.

Hal-hal tersebut dilaksanakan oleh Perseroan untuk meyakinkan bahwa pekerjaan yang dilakukan oleh setiap karyawan dalam dilaksanakan dengan aman.

Dalam Prosedur SMK3L belum tertulis secara eksplisit bagaimana karyawan dapat menolak pelaksanaan pekerjaan bila dirasakan ada isu terkait K3L. Hal ini belum diatur diantaranya karena nature pekerjaan di Perseroan yang berbeda dengan sektor risiko tinggi lainnya seperti pertambangan dan manufaktur). Namun demikian dalam praktiknya telah ada preseden di mana karyawan menolak suatu pekerjaan karena alasan kesehatan atau isu K3L lainnya tanpa ada dampak negatif bagi karyawan tersebut.

Secara spesifik, oleh karena operasional Perseroan berada di gedung perkantoran yang tinggi, PT SMI telah melakukan pemetaan dan identifikasi bahaya serta penilaian risiko terjadinya K3. Sulit dipungkiri bahwa karyawan perkantoran yang berlokasi pada gedung perkantoran yang tinggi memiliki risiko keselamatan tersendiri dimana pada saat terjadi situasi darurat seperti kebakaran, gempa, huru-hara dan ancaman teroris perlu upaya pengendalian dan pencegahan melalui pengembangan standar prosedur tanggap darurat yang baik. Keberadaan standar tersebut sangat vital untuk meminimalisir terjadinya kecelakaan kerja yang bersifat fatal dan masal.

These measures are implemented by the Company to ensure that the work carried out by each employee is carried out safely.

In the SMK3L Procedure, it is not explicitly written how employees can refuse to carry out work if they feel there are issues related to K3L. This has not been regulated, among other things, because the nature of work in the Company is different from other high-risk sectors such as mining and manufacturing. However, in practice there have been precedents where employees have refused a job for health reasons or other K3L issues without any repercussions on the employee.

Specifically, as the Company's operations are based in high-rise office buildings, PT SMI has conducted hazard mapping, hazard identification, and risk assessments related to OHS. It is undeniable that employees working in high-rise office buildings face unique safety risks, particularly in emergency situations such as fires, earthquakes, civil disturbances, or terrorist threats. Effective control and prevention measures are essential in such scenarios, achieved through the development of well-structured emergency response procedures. The existence of these standards is critical to minimizing the occurrence of fatal and large-scale workplace accidents.
