Terkait aspek keberlanjutan, Perseroan memiliki kebijakan pemberian remunerasi kepada Direksi terkait kinerja di bidang ekonomi, lingkungan, dan sosial. Hal tersebut ditentukan berdasarkan indikator kinerja utama (IKU) atau KPI (Key Performance Indicators). Direksi terkait dengan aspek lingkungan dan sosial yaitu pada tercapainya program-program SDG Indonesia One yang memiliki dampak potensial positif bagi lingkungan dan masyarakat. KPI Direksi ini juga merupakan KPI korporasi terkait dengan Kerja Sama Pendanaan Melalui Platform SDG Indonesia One (SIO), termasuk Dukungan Terhadap Proyek Geothermal di Indonesia, Sinergi dengan Special Mission Vehicle (SMV) di bawah Kementerian Keuangan, dan Kemitraan dengan Pihak Lain. [GRI 2-18]

Regarding sustainability aspects, the Company has established a remuneration policy for the Board of Directors based on their performance in economic, environmental, and social matters. This remuneration is determined based on the Directors' Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) related to environmental and social aspects, specifically in achieving the SDG Indonesia One programs, which have the potential to generate positive impacts on the environment and society. These KPIs for the Board of Directors are also corporate KPIs related to Funding Cooperation through the SDG Indonesia One (SIO) platform, including Support for Geothermal Projects in Indonesia, Synergy with the Special Mission Vehicle (SMV) under the Ministry of Finance, and Partnerships with Other Parties. [GRI 2-18]

The aforementioned KPIs govern the collaboration between the Company and donor institutions, as well as bilateral, multilateral, and other organizations in channeling grants or other incentives through the SDG Indonesia One platform.
