## Dukungan PT SMI terhadap Net-Zero Emission di Indonesia

Dukungan PT SMI terhadap net-zero emission dilakukan melalui berbagai program dan kebijakan. Selain melakukan penghematan penggunaan energi di perkantoran, baik penghematan BBM maupun listrik, dukungan juga direalisasikan melalui konversi mobil kendaraan operasional yang semula berbahan bakar minyak menjadi mobil listrik.

## PT SMI's Support for Net-Zero Emission in Indonesia

PT SMI's commitment to achieving net-zero emissions is implemented through various programs and policies. In addition to reducing energy consumption in office operations—including fuel and electricity savings—the Company has also taken steps to convert its operational vehicle fleet from fossil fuel-powered cars to electric vehicles (EVs).
