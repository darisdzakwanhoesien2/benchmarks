Financed Emission dihitung mengacu pada standar internasional yang relevan untuk sektor keuangan, yaitu Partnership for Carbon Accounting Financials (PCAF) – Financed Emission Global GHG Accounting and Reporting Standard. Perhitungan mencakup 140 debitur dan investee dengan total outstanding Rp 75 triliun atau mewakili 83% dari total outstanding pembiayaan dan investasi per 31 Desember 2023.

Estimasi financed emission dilakukan dengan menghitung financed emission dari masing-masing debitur dan investee, yaitu dengan mengalikan emisi cakupan 1 dan 2 debitur dengan attribution factor. Emisi debitur dan investee didapatkan dari laporan yang dipublikasikan, laporan ke PT SMI, estimasi menggunakan data teknis (seperti produksi listrik, produksi air, panjang jalan, dsb), dan estimasi menggunakan data keuangan (revenue atau aset). Sedangkan attribution factor didapatkan dari nilai outstanding dibagi dengan nilai investasi proyek (untuk project financing) atau nilai total debt + equity debitur (untuk corporate financing).

Selain financed emission, perhitungan juga dilakukan untuk avoided emission atau emisi yang terhindarkan. Perhitungan avoided emission dilakukan berdasarkan proyek energi terbarukan yang telah beroperasi kemudian dikalikan dengan attribution factor.

The calculation of Financed Emission follows internationally recognized standards for the financial sector, specifically the Partnership for Carbon Accounting Financials (PCAF) – Financed Emission Global GHG Accounting and Reporting Standard. The assessment covers 140 debtors and investees, with a total outstanding amount of IDR 75 trillion, representing 83% of the total outstanding financing and investment as of December 31, 2023.

The estimated financed emission is calculated by determining the financed emissions of each debtor and investee. This is done by multiplying the Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions of the debtor by the attribution factor. Debtor and investee emissions are obtained from various sources, including: published reports, reports submitted to PT SMI, estimates based on technical data (such as electricity production, water production, road length, etc.), estimates based on financial data (such as revenue or total assets). The attribution factor is derived from: outstanding loan value divided by the total project investment value (for project financing), or outstanding loan value divided by the total debt + equity of the debtor (for corporate financing).

In addition to financed emission, calculations are also conducted for avoided emission, referring to the GHG emissions prevented through renewable energy projects that have become operational. The avoided emission is determined by multiplying the operational renewable energy project emissions by the attribution factor.

Selain emisi GRK Cakupan 1, 2 dan 3, Perseroan mendukung penuh kebijakan pemerintah untuk memperketat penggunaan berbagai bahan yang menghasilkan emisi zat perusak lapisan ozon (Ozone Depletion Potential/ODP) atau Bahan Perusak Ozon (BPO). Zat ini biasa terdapat di mesin pendingin ruangan (AC), kulkas, dan tabung pemadam api.

In addition to Scope 1, 2, and 3 GHG emissions, the Company fully supports government policies aimed at restricting the use of substances with Ozone Depletion Potential (ODP), also known as Ozone-Depleting Substances (ODS). These substances are commonly found in air conditioners (AC), refrigerators, and fire extinguishers.
