|  4 Juni 2024
June 4, 2024 | PT SMI berpartisipasi dalam rangkaian acara Asian Infrastructure Forum 2024 (AIF 2024) dengan tema Sustanaible Infrastructure: Transforming Asia's Journey yang diselenggarakan oleh Infrastruktur Asia. Dalam acara ini, PT SMI yang diwakilkan oleh Direktur Pembiayaan dan Investasi PT SMI - Sylvi J. Gani hadir dalam seremoni MoU Exchange atas perpanjangan kedua MoU kolaborasi percepatan pengembangan infrastruktur di Indonesia antara PT SMI dengan Infrastructure Asia (IA).
PT SMI participated in the Asian Infrastructure Forum 2024 (AIF 2024), themed "Sustainable Infrastructure: Transforming Asia's Journey," organized by Infrastructure Asia. Represented by Director of Financing & Investment, Sylvi J. Gani, PT SMI took part in the MoU Exchange Ceremony for the renewal of two MoUs on collaborative efforts to accelerate infrastructure development in Indonesia between PT SMI and Infrastructure Asia (IA). |   |
|  15 Agustus 2024
August 15, 2024 | PT SMI mendukung PT Arkora Hydro Malili dalam membangun PLTM Tomoni 2x5 MW di Tomoni, Luwu Timur, Sulawesi Selatan diwujudkan melalui penandatangan perjanjian pembiayaan sebesar Rp 223 miliar oleh Direktur Pembiayaan & Investasi – Sylvi J. Gani dan Direktur Utama PT PT Arkora Hydro Malili – Ricky Hartono
PT SMI supported PT Arkora Hydro Malili in the development of the Tomoni 2x5 MW Mini Hydro Power Plant (PLTM) in Tomoni, East Luwu, South Sulawesi. This was realized through a financing agreement worth IDR 223 billion, signed by Director of Financing & Investment, Sylvi J. Gani, and President Director of PT Arkora Hydro Malili, Ricky Hartono. |   |
|  22 Agustus 2024
August 22, 2024 | Program Tanggung Jawab Sosial & Lingkungan (TJSL) PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur Persero (PT SMI) selaku pemegang saham mayoritas CCT melaksanakan Program Penghijauan Jalan Tol Cimanggis Cibitung di Area Gerbang Tol Nagrak STA 28+650 - STA 31+250.
Through its Corporate Social and Environmental Responsibility (CSR) program, PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur (Persero) (PT SMI), as the majority shareholder of CCT, initiated the Cimanggis-Cibitung Toll Road Greening Program at Nagrak Toll Gate Area STA 28+650 - STA 31+250. |   |
|  9 September 2024
September 9, 2024 | PT SMI bersama Bank Mega memberikan dukungan kepada PT INKA untuk pengadaan 16 Trainset Kereta Rel Listrik (KRL) melalui penandatangan Perjanjian Kredit Sindikasi senilai Rp 2,1 triliun, di mana porsi pembiayaan dari PT SMI mencapai Rp 850 miliar.
PT SMI and Bank Mega supported PT INKA in the procurement of 16 Electric Multiple Unit (KRL) Trainsets through a Syndicated Loan Agreement worth IDR 2.1 trillion, with PT SMI's financing portion reaching IDR 850 billion. |   |
|  1 Oktober 2024
October 1, 2024 | PT SMI bekerja sama dengan Kementerian Lingkungan Hidup dan Kehutanan (KLHK) serta didukung oleh pendanaan dari Uni Eropa melalui KfW telah menyelesaikan Studi Pemetaan Off-taker Potensial Refuse Derived Fuel (RDF) di Indonesia.
PT SMI, in collaboration with the Ministry of Environment and Forestry (KLHK) and funded by the European Union through KfW, successfully completed a study on mapping potential off-takers for Refuse Derived Fuel (RDF) in Indonesia. |   |
