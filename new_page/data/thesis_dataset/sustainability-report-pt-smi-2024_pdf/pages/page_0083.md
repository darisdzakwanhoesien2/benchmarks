# Perubahan Perusahaan yang Bersifat Signifikan [GRI 2-6][OJK C.6]
## Significant Changes to the Company [GRI 2-6][OJK C.6]

Pada tahun 2024, PT SMI mendapatkan perluasan sektor pembiayaan melalui Peraturan OJK RI Nomor 16 Tahun 2024 tentang Pengawasan PT SMI. Perluasan sektor Perseroan meliputi infrastruktur ekosistem industri, infrastruktur kendaraan bermotor listrik berbasis baterai, dan pembiayaan untuk sektor/bidang penyelenggaraan mitigasi/adaptasi perubahan iklim. Selain itu tidak terdapat perubahan signifikan pada struktur kepemilikan saham maupun aktivitas bisnis utama Perseroan.

In 2024, PT SMI received an expansion of its financing sector through the OJK Regulation No. 16 of 2024 regarding the Supervision of PT SMI. The expansion of the Company's sector includes infrastructure for industrial ecosystems, infrastructure for battery-powered electric vehicles, and financing for the sector/field of climate change mitigation/adaptation activities. Furthermore, there were no significant changes in the shareholding structure or the main business activities of the Company.
