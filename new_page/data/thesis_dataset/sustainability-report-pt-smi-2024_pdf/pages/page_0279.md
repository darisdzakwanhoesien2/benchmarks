# Pelatihan Integrated Biodiversity Assessment Tools (IBAT)

Pelatihan internal terkait dengan penggunaan dan pemanfaatan Integrated Biodiversity Assessment Tools (IBAT) ini diinsiasi oleh DELST bersama dengan Divisi Pembiayaan Publik 2 (DPPU 2), khususnya bagian Project Management Unit (PMU) Geothermal. Materi training disampaikan oleh Biodiversity Specialist DELST, yaitu M. Faesal. Pelatihan diselenggarakan tanggal 27 Juni 2024 dan dihadiri oleh DELST dan DPPU2.

# Training on Integrated Biodiversity Assessment Tools (IBAT)

The internal training on the use and application of the Integrated Biodiversity Assessment Tools (IBAT) was initiated by Environmental, Social, and Technical Evaluation Division (DELST) in collaboration with the Public Financing Division 2 (DPPU 2), specifically the Project Management Unit (PMU) Geothermal. The training session was delivered by DELST's Biodiversity Specialist, M. Faesal. The training was conducted on June 27, 2024, and was attended by representatives from DELST and DPPU 2.
