|  25 – 26 April 2024
April 25-26, 2024 | PT SMI menghadiri Transit Oriented Development (TOD)
Investment Forum 2024 yang diselenggarakan oleh PT MRT Jakarta di Tokyo, Jepang. PT SMI turut berpartisipasi dan melakukan penandatangan Head of Agreement (HoA) bersama PT MRT Jakarta. Selain itu, Direktur Pembiayaan & Investasi PT SMI – Sylvi J. Gani menjadi salah satu pembicara pada sesi panel kategori Financing, dengan mengangkat topik “Creative Financing for Infrastructure Development in Indonesia”.
PT SMI attended the Transit Oriented Development (TOD) Investment Forum 2024, organized by PT MRT Jakarta in Tokyo, Japan. PT SMI actively participated in the event and signed a Head of Agreement (HoA) with PT MRT Jakarta. Additionally, PT SMI’s Director of Financing & Investment, Sylvi J. Gani, was a panel speaker in the Financing category, presenting on “Creative Financing for Infrastructure Development in Indonesia.” |   |
|  13 Mei 2024
May 13, 2024 | PT SMI menandatangani Nota Kesepahaman dengan PT Transportasi Jakarta (PT TJ) dalam rangka penjajakan kerja sama penyediaan fasilitas pembiayaan pengadaan bus listrik bagi para operator bus di bawah pengelolaan PT TJ.
PT SMI signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with PT Transportasi Jakarta (PT TJ) to explore collaboration in providing financing facilities for electric bus procurement for bus operators under PT TJ’s management. |   |
|  19 Mei 2024
May 19, 2024 | Direktur Utama PT SMI - Edwin Syahruzad menghadiri Tri Hita Karana Forum - World Economic Forum dan menjadi pembicara dalam Plenary Session bertajuk Showcasing Blended Financing Mechanisms for Climate and Nature.
President Director of PT SMI, Edwin Syahruzad, attended the Tri Hita Karana Forum - World Economic Forum and was a speaker in the Plenary Session titled “Showcasing Blended Financing Mechanisms for Climate and Nature.” |   |
|  21 Mei 2024
May 21, 2024 | Direktur Operasional dan Keuangan PT SMI - Darwin Trisna Djajawinata memaparkan tentang SDG Indonesia One pada acara Leadership Forum and Technical Workshop on Clean Energy for Indonesian Industrial Zones yang diselenggarakan oleh Net Zero World dan US Department of Commerce.
Director of Operations & Finance at PT SMI, Darwin Trisna Djajawinata, presented SDG Indonesia One at the Leadership Forum and Technical Workshop on Clean Energy for Indonesian Industrial Zones, organized by Net Zero World and the US Department of Commerce. |   |
|  28 Mei 2024
May 28, 2024 | Direktur Manajemen Risiko - Pradana Murti menghadiri Working Group ke-5: Efisien Energi dan Elektrifikasi (E3WG) yang diselenggarakan oleh Just Energy Transition Partnership (JETP) serta menjadi pembicara pada sesi Menavigasi Tantangan dan Strategi Pendanaan untuk Efisiensi Energi dan Elektrifikasi di Indonesia.
Director of Risk Management, Pradana Murti, attended the 5th Working Group on Energy Efficiency and Electrification (E3WG), hosted by the Just Energy Transition Partnership (JETP). He also spoke in a session on “Navigating Challenges and Financing Strategies for Energy Efficiency and Electrification in Indonesia.” |   |
