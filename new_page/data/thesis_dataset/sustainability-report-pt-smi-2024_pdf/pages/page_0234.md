• PT GDE is preparing a new strategy for the Waesano exploration project, covering aspects such as project site planning, accessibility, civil design, and drilling plan.
• A draft pre-feasibility study document was submitted for PT SMI's feedback on September 23. The document is currently under review and will be presented to the Technical Committee before approval by the Joint Committee. PT SMI has provided financial input regarding the project's economic viability, which still requires adjustments.
• To utilize PISP funds, the Waesano project must comply with the governance regulations outlined in Regulation of the Minister of Finance No. 80 of 2022, which require the completion of a Five-Party Exploration Support Agreement and a Project Implementation Manual for PISP funds. PT GDE, PT SMI, and the Joint Committee are currently drafting these documents.
• Until the completion of these documents, Waesano remains in a transitional phase. During this period, the operational site is funded through PISP funds, in accordance with Letter No. S-7/KB.1/2023, dated November 7, 2023, from the Director of State Financial Risk Management, acting as Committee Secretary.  |

## Penyaluran Hibah dalam Bentuk Dukungan Teknis dan Pelaksanaan Kegiatan

Melalui platform SDG Indonesia One, PT SMI senantiasa meningkatkan kerja sama dengan berbagai lembaga, baik bilateral maupun multilateral, untuk mendukung pengembangan energi terbarukan serta proyek-proyek yang berlandaskan Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan. Pada pelaksanaanya, PT SMI yang merupakan Accredited Entity Green Climate Fund mengajukan proposal sebagai bentuk kerja sama, baik untuk penyiapan proyek (Project Preparation Facility) maupun untuk implementasi proyek (Funding Proposal) untuk proyek-proyek terkait mitigasi dan adaptasi perubahan iklim kepada berbagai lembaga tersebut.

## Grant Disbursement in the Form of Technical Assistance

Through the SDG Indonesia One platform, PT SMI continuously strengthens cooperation with various bilateral and multilateral institutions to support the development of renewable energy and projects aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). As an Accredited Entity of the Green Climate Fund (GCF), PT SMI submits proposals for collaboration in the form of both Project Preparation Facility (PPF) funding and Funding Proposals (FP) for the implementation of climate change mitigation and adaptation projects. These proposals are directed to various international institutions and funding partners to advance sustainable infrastructure initiatives.
