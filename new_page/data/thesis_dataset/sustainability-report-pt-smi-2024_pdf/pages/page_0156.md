Penerapan prinsip pertahanan tiga lapis dilakukan dengan sosialisasi kepada setiap karyawan akan pentingnya kesadaran atas potensi risiko-risiko dalam pengelolaan kegiatan operasional dan bisnis Perseroan.

Berdasarkan Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 16 Tahun 2024 tentang Pengawasan PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur (Persero) dan Surat Edaran Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. 27/POJK.05/2021 tentang Penilaian Tingkat Kesehatan Perusahaan Pembiayaan Infrastruktur, Perseroan wajib menerapkan manajemen risiko terhadap 8 (delapan) taksonomi risiko yaitu: Risiko Kredit, Risiko Operasional, Risiko Pasar, Risiko Likuiditas, Risiko Hukum, Risiko Strategis, Risiko Kepatuhan, dan Risiko Reputasi. Selain kedelapan taksonomi risiko tersebut, Perseroan juga melakukan pengelolaan Risiko Penyertaan Modal yang sesuai dengan karakter dan kompleksitas bisnis Perseroan. Dengan demikian, Jenis-jenis risiko yang dihadapi Perseroan berjumlah 9 (sembilan) Jenis. Perseroan juga melakukan pengelolaan risiko terhadap Risiko Syariah dan Risiko Lingkungan dan Sosial yang merupakan bagian dari taksonomi Risiko Kredit dan Risiko Kepatuhan. [GRI 2-25]

The implementation of the Three Lines of Defense principle includes raising awareness among all employees about the importance of recognizing potential risks in managing the Company's operations and business activities.

Based on the Regulation of Financial Services Authority No. 16 of 2024 concerning the Supervision of PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur (Persero) and Circular Letter of the Financial Services Authority No. 27/POJK.05/2021 concerning the Assessment of Health Level of Infrastructure Financing Company, the Company is required to implement risk management for eight (8) risk taxonomies, namely: Credit Risk, Operational Risk, Market Risk, Liquidity Risk, Legal Risk, Strategic Risk, Compliance Risk, and Reputational Risk. In addition to these eight risk taxonomies, the Company also manages Equity Investment Risk in accordance with the character and complexity of its business. Consequently, the total types of risks faced by the Company amount to nine (9). The Company also manages risks related to Sharia Risk and Environmental and Social Safeguards Risk, which are part of the Credit Risk and Compliance Risk taxonomies. [GRI 2-25]
