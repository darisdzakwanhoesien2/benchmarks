# Penilaian Kinerja Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi [GRI 2-18]
Performance Assessment of the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors [GRI 2-18]

Perseroan secara berkala melakukan pengukuran atau evaluasi atas kinerja Dewan Komisaris yang ditetapkan dalam Indikator Kinerja Utama (IKU) yang tercantum dalam Kontrak Kinerja Dewan Komisaris yang diajukan dan disetujui oleh RUPS, kemudian disahkan sebagai bagian dari RKAP Perseroan. Dewan Komisaris menyusun rencana kerja dan target kerja di setiap awal tahun dan melakukan evaluasi mandiri (self assessment) atas pencapaiannya serta melaporkan kepada Pemegang Saham apabila terjadi gejala kemunduran kinerja pada Perseroan. Dewan Komisaris menyampaikan laporan kinerja dan pertanggungjawaban selama satu tahun buku kepada RUPS, dan RUPS memutuskan untuk menerima atau menyampaikan penilaian.

Dalam mengukur kinerja Direksi secara kolegial, Perseroan juga telah menetapkan sejumlah kriteria berdasarkan IKU yang telah disetujui oleh RUPS dan termuat dalam RKAP Perseroan. Dalam proses penilaian kinerja Direksi, Dewan Komisaris menetapkan indikator untuk masing-masing Direksi. Di sisi lain, Direksi juga menetapkan tolok ukur kinerja masing-masing unit kerja dan melakukan penilaian setiap tahun secara transparan untuk mendukung kinerja Perseroan.

The Company regularly measures and evaluates the performance of the Board of Commissioners, as outlined in the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) stated in the Board of Commissioners' Performance Contract, which is submitted and approved by the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) and subsequently ratified as part of the Company's Work Plan and Budget. The Board of Commissioners formulates its work plan and targets at the beginning of each year, conducts self-assessments to evaluate achievements, as well as reports to shareholders if there are signs of performance decline within the Company. The Board of Commissioners submits an annual performance and accountability report to the GMS, which then decides whether to accept or provide further assessment.

To assess the performance of the Board of Directors collegially, the Company has also established criteria based on the KPIs approved by the GMS, which are included in the Company's Work Plan and Budget. In the performance evaluation process, the Board of Commissioners determines indicators for each Director. Meanwhile, the Board of Directors sets performance benchmarks for each work unit and conducts annual transparent assessments to support the overall performance of the Company.
