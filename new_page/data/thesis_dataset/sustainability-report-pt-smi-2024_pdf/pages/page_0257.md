The management of wastewater (effluent) is the responsibility of the Sahid Sudirman Center building management, as is the handling of hazardous and toxic waste (B3 waste), including used lamps and batteries, except for cartridges, which are collected by the provider. With the management measures in place, there were no recorded incidents of waste spills or hazardous substance leaks during the reporting year that could have polluted the environment or endangered workers' health.
