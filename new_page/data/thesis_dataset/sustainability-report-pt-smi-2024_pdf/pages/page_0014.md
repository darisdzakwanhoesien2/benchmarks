ESG Rating Achievement from MSCI

Pada tanggal 7 Mei 2024, MSCI memberikan Provisional Rating ESG kepada PT SMI dengan peringkat AA, yang meliputi tiga aspek kunci: Environmental, Social and Governance. Hal ini menjadikan PT SMI sebagai perusahaan dengan rating ESG tertinggi versi MSCI di Indonesia dan satu-satunya Special Mission Vehicle (SMV) Kementerian Keuangan RI yang menerima rating MSCI. Dalam Klasifikasi MSCI, rating AA masuk dalam kategori tertinggi atau Leader, perusahaan di dalam industri terkait yang dianggap terdepan dalam mengelola risiko dan kebijakan berkenaan dengan ESG. Divisi Keuangan dan Hubungan Investor (DKHI) beserta Divisi Evaluasi Lingkungan Sosial dan Teknik (DELST) mengkoordinasi tahapan persiapan dan pelaksanaan rating sejak awal tahun 2024.

On May 7, 2024, MSCI awarded a Provisional ESG Rating of AA to PT SMI, covering the three key aspects of Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG). This recognition positioned PT SMI as the highest-rated company in Indonesia according to MSCI's ESG ratings and the only Special Mission Vehicle (SMV) under Indonesia's Ministry of Finance to receive an MSCI rating. Under MSCI's classification, an AA rating falls into the highest category, known as "Leader", which is assigned to companies within the relevant industry that are considered to be at the forefront of managing ESG-related risks and policies. The Finance and Investor Relations Division (DKHI), along with the Environmental, Social, and Technical Evaluation Division (DELST), coordinated the preparation and execution of the rating process since early 2024.
