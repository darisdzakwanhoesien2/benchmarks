# Pendelegasian Wewenang [GRI 2-13]
## Delegation of Authority [GRI 2-13]

Direksi dapat mendelegasikan sebagian wewenang pengelolaan Perseroan kepada jajaran di bawahnya sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku dengan pertimbangan untuk menunjang kelancaran tugas dan meningkatkan efisiensi dan efektivitas. Wewenang yang bisa didelegasikan antara lain berkaitan dengan pengelolaan aspek-aspek keberlanjutan yaitu menciptakan keselarasan antara aspek ekonomi, sosial dan lingkungan. Pendelegasian wewenang tersebut tidak melepaskan tanggung jawab Direksi.

Lebih lanjut, Kepala Divisi sebagai penerima pendelegasian wewenang harus melaksanakan dengan penuh tanggung jawab, dan memberikan laporan pelaksanaannya secara berkala kepada Direksi. Dalam implementasinya, pendelegasian wewenang dikaji secara periodik untuk disesuaikan dengan tuntutan perkembangan Perseroan.

The Board of Directors may delegate part of its authority in managing the Company to its subordinate levels in accordance with applicable regulations. This delegation is intended to facilitate the smooth execution of duties while enhancing efficiency and effectiveness. The delegated authority may include aspects related to sustainability management, ensuring alignment between economic, social, and environmental aspects. However, such delegation does not absolve the Board of its overall responsibility.

Furthermore, Heads of Division, as recipients of delegated authority, must carry out their responsibilities with full accountability and submit regular reports to the Board of Directors regarding the implementation of their duties. In practice, the delegation of authority is periodically reviewed to ensure alignment with the Company's evolving needs and strategic direction.
