# Pelaksanaan Pelatihan Karyawan [GRI 404-1, 404-2]

Perseroan mengklasifikan program pelatihan karyawan ke dalam 3 (tiga) jenis yakni public training, in-house training, dan knowledge sharing. Definisi ketiga jenis pelatihan adalah sebagai berikut:

1. Public Training
Public training merupakan program pelatihan karyawan meliputi keikutsertaan karyawan pada program seminar, pelatihan, kursus, lokakarya, workshop, maupun conference yang diselenggarakan oleh pihak penyedia jasa pelatihan di luar Perseroan yang terstruktur dan sudah diverifikasi oleh Divisi Sumber Daya Manusia, baik dari lembaga pelatihan dalam negeri maupun luar negeri.

2. In-House Training
In-House Training adalah program pelatihan karyawan yang diselenggarakan oleh Divisi Sumber Daya Manusia dengan bekerja sama dengan penyedia jasa pelatihan secara terstruktur, dalam rangka meningkatkan kompetensi karyawan secara menyeluruh, yang pelaksanaannya dapat dilakukan di dalam maupun di luar Perseroan.

3. Knowledge Sharing
Knowledge Sharing merupakan program pelatihan karyawan melalui aktivitas saling memberikan dan bertukar pengetahuan dan pengalaman kepada seluruh insan Perseroan, yang dilakukan oleh seluruh komponen Perseroan baik jajaran Direksi, Kepala Divisi, Team Leader, maupun karyawan, sebagai bentuk pembelajaran serta membangun kompetensi dan kapasitas karyawan.

4. e-Learning
Program pelatihan karyawan yang memungkinkan karyawan mengakses kursus, pengajaran, dan materi instruksional secara online melalui situs web https://e-learning.ptsmi.co.id/

# Implementation of Employee Training [GRI 404-1, 404-2]

The Company classifies employee training programs into three categories: Public Training, In-House Training, and Knowledge Sharing. The definitions of these training types are as follows:

1. Public Training
Public training is an employee training program that includes employee participation in seminars, training sessions, courses, workshops, or conferences organized by external training service providers. These programs are structured and verified by the Human Resources Division, whether from domestic or international training institutions.

2. In-House Training
In-house training is an employee training program organized by the Human Resources Division in collaboration with structured training service providers. This program aims to comprehensively enhance employee competencies and can be conducted either within or outside the Company.

3. Knowledge Sharing
Knowledge sharing is an employee training program that involves exchanging knowledge and experiences among all members of the Company. This activity is conducted by various stakeholders, including the Board of Directors, Heads of Division, Team Leaders, and employees, as a form of learning and building employee competencies and capacity.

4. e-Learning
The employee training program enables employees to access courses, instruction, and instructional materials online through the website https://e-learning.ptsmi.co.id/.
