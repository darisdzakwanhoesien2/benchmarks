|  4 | Awareness of gender mainstreaming dan inklusi sosial
Awareness of gender mainstreaming and social inclusion | Awareness gender mainstreaming dan inklusi sosial untuk tahun 2024 dapat terlaksana dengan baik
Gender mainstreaming and social inclusion awareness for 2024 were successfully implemented. | Program awareness gender mainstreaming dan inklusi sosial terlaksana dengan baik
The gender mainstreaming and social inclusion awareness program were successfully implemented. | Target tercapai dengan dilaksanakannya:
1. Gender Assessment terhadap proyek TJSL di Siru dan BARI.
2. Gender Assessment terhadap salah satu Debitur PT Krakatau Tirta Industri.
3. Publikasi Kebijakan Perseroan terkait Gender dan Inklusi Sosial melalui website resmi PT SMI
4. PT SMI ikut serta dalam pertemuan yang digagas oleh Finance in Common Summit's (FiCS) Gender Coalition membahas perkembangan metodologi dalam isu gender secara global.
5. Termutakhirkannya Kelompok Kerja KGIS melalui SK Penunjukan Direksi SK-019/SMI/1024, yang dilanjutkan dengan Pelatihan Lanjutan bagi anggota KK KGIS berkolaborasi dengan Asian Development Bank

Target achieved through the implementation of:
1. Gender Assessment for TJSL projects in Siru and BARI.
2. Gender Assessment for one of PT Krakatau Tirta Industri's debtors.
3. Publication of the Company's Gender and Social Inclusion Policy on PT SMI's official website.
4. PT SMI's participation in a meeting initiated by the Finance in Common Summit's (FiCS) Gender Coalition to discuss global developments in gender methodology.
5. Update of the KGIS Working Group through the issuance of Decree of the Board of Directors SK-019/SMI/1024, followed by Advanced Training for KGIS Working Group members in collaboration with the Asian Development Bank.  |
