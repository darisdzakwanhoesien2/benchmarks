# Whistleblowing System [GRI 2-26]
## Whistleblowing System [GRI 2-26]

PT SMI berkomitmen untuk meningkatkan efektivitas pengendalian internal yang bertujuan untuk mengurangi atau menghindari tindakan pelanggaran di Perseroan.

Untuk mewujudkan hal tersebut, pada tahun 2022, Perseroan menyusun dan menerbitkan Petunjuk Teknis Sistem Pelaporan Pelanggaran (Whistleblowing System WBS). Perseroan meyakini implementasi WBS menjadi salah satu metode pendeteksian dini atas terjadinya pelanggaran, sekaligus mendorong upaya mewujudkan budaya organisasi yang lebih transparan dan akuntabel, mendorong kinerja organisasi, dan melindungi para pemangku kepentingan. Dengan demikian, pelaksanaan tugas pokok dan fungsi organisasi dapat tercapai dengan baik, efektif dan efisien, baik dalam jangka pendek maupun jangka panjang, sehingga reputasi PT SMI semakin meningkat, baik di dalam maupun luar negeri.

Petunjuk Teknis mengatur secara lengkap mulai dari prinsip-prinsip pelaporan pelanggaran, ketentuan pelaporan pelanggaran termasuk di dalamnya tentang komitmen penerapan WBS, perlindungan pelapor, pengelola WBS, mekanisme penanganan pelaporan, media pelaporan, serta proses tindak lanjut atas pengaduan pelanggaran melalui saluran/media WBS. Selain itu, juga terdapat pembahasan tentang sosialisasi dan evaluasi, penghargaan dan sanksi, serta pemulihan nama baik.

Selama tahun 2024, PT SMI tidak menerima adanya laporan pengaduan pelanggaran yang masuk melalui dari saluran Whistleblowing System PT SMI. Pada tahun 2023, Perseroan menerima 1 (satu) laporan melalui WBS dan telah selesai ditindaklanjuti pada bulan Juni 2024 dengan hasil investigasi diyakini tidak terjadi. Sementara itu, pada tahun 2022, tercatat 1 (satu) laporan diterima dan telah selesai ditindaklanjuti pada tahun 2023. PT SMI menjamin seluruh laporan yang disampaikan oleh Pelapor bersifat rahasia dan ditindaklanjuti sesuai dengan peraturan internal maupun perundang-undangan yang berlaku.

PT SMI is committed to enhancing the effectiveness of internal control to mitigate or prevent violations within the Company.

In pursuit of this objective, in 2022, the Company developed and issued the Technical Guidelines for the Whistleblowing System (WBS). PT SMI believes that implementing the WBS serves as an early detection mechanism for violations while fostering a more transparent and accountable organizational culture, improving organizational performance, and safeguarding stakeholders. Through this initiative, the Company's core functions and operational goals can be achieved effectively and efficiently in both the short and long term, further strengthening PT SMI's reputation domestically and internationally.

Technical Guidelines comprehensively regulate all aspects, including the principles of violation reporting, reporting provisions—covering the commitment to implementing the Whistleblowing System (WBS), whistleblower protection, WBS management, reporting handling mechanisms, reporting channels, and the follow-up process for complaints submitted through the WBS channels. Additionally, the guidelines also address dissemination and evaluation, rewards and sanctions, as well as reputation restoration.

Throughout 2024, PT SMI did not receive any violation reports through the PT SMI Whistleblowing System (WBS). In 2023, the Company received one report through WBS, which was fully addressed and concluded in June 2024, with the investigation determining that no violation had occurred. Meanwhile, in 2022, 1 (one) report was recorded and was fully resolved in 2023. PT SMI ensures that all reports submitted by whistleblowers are kept confidential and are handled in accordance with internal regulations as well as prevailing laws and regulations.
