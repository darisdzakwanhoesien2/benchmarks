# Emission Control

Greenhouse gas emissions such as carbon dioxide (CO₂), methane (CH₄) and nitrous oxide (N₂O) play a central role in global warming and climate change. For this reason, the reduction of greenhouse gas emissions is a shared priority to avoid increasing these negative impacts.

PT SMI is committed to restraining the rate of this global phenomenon by managing emissions properly. The real policy carried out is to reduce emissions by carrying out energy efficiency. Emission reduction efforts are also carried out by the Company by actively participating in the reforestation movement and the construction of Solar Power Plants (PLTS) through the Social and Environmental Responsibility Program. [3-3]

In this report, the emissions reported are direct GHG emissions (scope 1) resulting from the use of fuel, indirect GHG emissions (scope 2) sourced from the use of electricity from PLN, and other indirect GHG emissions (scope 3) from official travel. In mid-2024, the Company added scope 3 GHG emission calculation, namely financed emission or GHG emission from financing and investment activities. The results of the financed emission calculation for the 2023 period will be published in this report, while the financed emission for the 2024 period is still in the data collection stage. [GRI 3-3]
