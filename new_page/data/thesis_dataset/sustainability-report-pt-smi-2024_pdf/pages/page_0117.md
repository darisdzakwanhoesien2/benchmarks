# 6. Kajian Penanganan Stunting di Kabupaten Timor Tengah Selatan (TTS)

SMI Institute melakukan kajian mendalam terkait permasalan stunting di Kabupaten Timor Tengah Selatan (TTS), bekerjasama dengan SMERU Research Institute. Kabupaten TTS merupakan wilayah dengan tingkat stunting tertinggi di Indonesia pada 2022 berdasarkan Studi Status Gizi Indonesia (SSGI). Kajian ini bertujuan memberikan pemahaman yang komprehensif tentang penyebab utama serta dampak sosial dan ekonomi dari stunting.

Dalam kajian ini, kami berkolaborasi secara aktif dengan berbagai pemangku kepentingan di tingkat kecamatan, kabupaten, dan provinsi untuk mengidentifikasi akar permasalahan serta merumuskan solusi yang dapat dilakukan. Hasil kajian menghasilkan rekomendasi kebijakan dan program strategis, termasuk pembangunan infrastruktur prioritas yang dapat segera diimplementasikan oleh pemerintah daerah. Hasil kajian ini juga dapat digunakan sebagai dasar kegiatan peningkatan kapasitas pemerintah daerah dalam pengelolaan isu-isu kesehatan dan sosial secara lebih efektif, berkelanjutan, dan terintegrasi.

# 5. Development of PT SMI's Roadmap to Support the National Energy Transition

SMI Institute, in collaboration with the Planning, Environment, and Public Financing divisions of PT SMI, and with support from the Climate Policy Initiative, is currently developing PT SMI's roadmap to support the national energy transition toward a low-carbon economy. This roadmap aims to increase financing allocation to the new and renewable energy sector, accelerating the achievement of carbon-neutral financing targets in the coming years.

# 6. Study on Stunting Management in Timor Tengah Selatan (TTS) Regency

SMI Institute is conducting an in-depth study on stunting issues in Timor Tengah Selatan (TTS) Regency, in collaboration with the SMERU Research Institute. Based on the 2022 Indonesia Nutritional Status Study (SSGI), TTS Regency recorded the highest stunting rate in Indonesia. This study aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of the root causes, as well as the social and economic impacts of stunting.

Ini the study, we actively collaborate with stakeholders at the sub-district, regency, and provincial levels to identify key challenges and formulate effective solutions. The study has resulted in policy recommendations and strategic programs, including priority infrastructure development that can be immediately implemented by local governments. Additionally, the study serves as a foundation for capacity-building initiatives to help local governments manage health and social issues more effectively, sustainably, and in an integrated manner.
