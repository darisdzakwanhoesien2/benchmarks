PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur (Persero) - Laporan Keberlanjutan 2024

Unit TJSL dibentuk melalui Surat Keputusan Direksi Nomor: SK-009/SMI/0524 tanggal 29 Mei 2024 tentang Penunjukan Personalia Unit Tanggung Jawab Sosial dan Lingkungan Pengelolaan TJSL PT SMI juga melibatkan Organ Perseroan yaitu Direksi, Dewan Komisaris, dan divisi-divisi internal terkait, yaitu: Divisi Keuangan dan Hubungan Investor (DKHI), Divisi Akuntansi dan Administrasi Aset (DAA), Divisi Hukum (DH), Divisi Evaluasi Lingkungan Sosial dan Teknik (DELST), Divisi Umum dan Pengadaan (DUP), Divisi Audit Internal (DAI), Divisi Kepatuhan (DK), dan Divisi lainnya yang terkait dengan pelaksanaan Kegiatan TJSL Perseroan.

The CSR Unit was established through Decree of the Board of Directors No. SK-009/SMI/0524, dated May 29, 2024, concerning the Appointment of Personnel for the CSR Unit of PT SMI. The management of CSR also involves the Company's governing organs, including the Board of Directors, Board of Commissioners, and relevant internal divisions, namely: Finance and Investor Relations Division (DKHI), Accounting and Asset Administration Division (DAA), Legal Division (DH), Environmental, Social, and Technical Evaluation Division (DELST), General Affairs and Procurement Division (DUP), Internal Audit Division (DAI), Compliance Division (DK), and other divisions related to the implementation of PT SMI's CSR activities.

Struktur Personalia Unit TJSL

Dalam melaksanakan TJSL di bidang lingkungan, Unit TJSL senantiasa melakukan peninjauan secara langsung ke lokasi sebelum menjalankan program guna menganalisis secara menyeluruh mengenai usulan program TJSL yang benar-benar dibutuhkan oleh masyarakat penerima bantuan. Sementara itu, untuk program TJSL terkait bantuan korban bencana alam maupun musibah lainnya dapat dilakukan secara langsung tanpa perlu mengadakan survei atau kajian ke lokasi mengingat para korban membutuhkan bantuan secara cepat.

Selaras dengan itu, PT SMI melakukan evaluasi atas kinerja program TJSL yang dijalankan secara berkala yang dilakukan oleh pihak ketiga untuk menjaga obyektivitas penilaian evaluasi. Adapun evaluasi yang dilakukan dengan menilai program TJSL melalui muatan: Stakeholder Engagement, Stakeholder Perception, Indeks Kepuasan Masyarakat (IKM), Social Return on Investment (SROI), Sustainability Analysis, SWOT, Creating Shared Value (CSV), serta Efektivitas Dana Salur PUMK. Dengan demikian, hasil evaluasi terhadap program TJSL tersebut sekaligus bisa menjadi masukan untuk meningkatkan mutu pelaksanaan program TJSL agar dapat memberikan manfaat sosial, ekonomi, dan lingkungan yang lebih baik di masa mendatang. Sebagai informasi, nilai IKM rata-rata program TJSL Perseroan adalah 88,43 dengan kategori "sangat baik" sementara nilai SROI rata-rata adalah 2,40.

In implementing CSR programs in the environmental sector, the CSR Unit consistently conducts on-site assessments before implementing programs to thoroughly analyze the proposed CSR initiatives and ensure they address the actual needs of the beneficiary communities. However, for CSR programs related to disaster relief or emergency aid, assistance can be provided immediately without the need for prior surveys or assessments, considering the urgency of support for affected victims.

In line with this, PT SMI conducts regular evaluations of its CSR program performance, carried out by third-party assessors to maintain objectivity in the evaluation process. The evaluation framework includes assessments based on: Stakeholder Engagement, Stakeholder Perception, Community Satisfaction Index (IKM), Social Return on Investment (SROI), Sustainability Analysis, SWOT Analysis, Creating Shared Value (CSV) as well as Effectiveness of MSE Funding Program Fund Distribution. These evaluation results serve as valuable input for enhancing the quality of CSR program implementation, ensuring it delivers greater social, economic, and environmental benefits in the future. For information, the average IKM value of the Company's TJSL program is 88.43 with a category of "very good" while the average SROI value is 2.40.
