3. Meningkatkan reputasi dan kredibilitas Perseroan melalui kegiatan yang dapat memberikan nilai tambah bagi Perseroan.

Selain itu, dalam setiap pelaksanaan program TJSL Perseroan harus memperhatikan dan mempertimbangkan potensi risiko yang dapat terjadi serta upaya mitigasinya. Adapun risiko-risiko yang mungkin terjadi dalam pelaksanaan program TJSL berupa risiko operasional, risiko hukum, risiko reputasi, serta risiko kredit.

## Bentuk Pelaksanaan Program TJSL

Sebagai Special Mission Vehicle, PT SMI berkomitmen mendukung pencapaian TPB/SDGs sejalan dengan mandat yang diterima Perseroan sebagai aktor utama dalam kegiatan pembiayaan kreatif untuk mengimplementasikan value for money dalam upaya mendorong akselerasi pembangunan nasional.

Pada dasarnya, bentuk pelaksanaan Program TJSL Perseroan terdiri dari:

1. Program Pendanaan Usaha Mikro dan Kecil (PUMK)
Program PUMK dilaksanakan dengan persyaratan sebagai berikut:

a. PUMK diberikan kepada perorangan atau badan usaha yang memenuhi kriteria usaha mikro dan usaha kecil sebagaimana diatur dalam ketentuan peraturan perundang-undangan.
b. PUMK berupa pemodalan dalam bentuk pembiayaan konvensional dan/atau pembiayaan syariah;
c. Permodalan diberikan dengan jumlah paling banyak sebesar Rp250.000.000,00 (dua ratus lima puluh juta rupiah) untuk setiap usaha mikro dan usaha kecil.
d. PUMK dapat ditindaklanjuti dengan Pembinaan, dan biayanya dibebankan pada Kegiatan TJSL Perseroan.

2. Program Bantuan TJSL
Program Bantuan TJSL meliputi beberapa kegiatan, yaitu:

a. Kegiatan Penanganan Bencana
Kegiatan Penanganan Bencana adalah kegiatan penanganan bencana alam dan non-alam, termasuk yang disebabkan oleh wabah penyakit, dengan ruang lingkup antara lain:

i) Bantuan bahan-bahan obat-obatan dan/atau bantuan medis, bahan pokok, air bersih, sarana sanitasi dan lainnya.
ii) Bantuan peralatan/fasilitas penunjang penanganan korban bencana, antara lain seperti perahu karet, tenda pengungsi/tempat penampungan sementara dan lainnya.
iii) Kegiatan penanganan pasca bencana, seperti pembangunan sarana pendidikan, infrastruktur sanitasi, infrastruktur penunjang pemulihan bencana, penanganan trauma, serta pendidikan dan/atau pelatihan untuk korban bencana.

3. Enhance the Company's reputation and credibility through activities that add value to the Company.

In addition, in every implementation of the Company's CSR program, potential risks that may occur, as well as the corresponding mitigation efforts, must be considered. The risks that may arise in the implementation of the CSR program include operational risk, legal risk, reputational risk, and credit risk.

## CSR Program Implementation

As a Special Mission Vehicle (SMV), PT SMI is committed to supporting the achievement of the SDGs in line with the mandate received by the Company as a key actor in creative financing activities to implement value for money in efforts to accelerate national development.

The implementation of PT SMI's CSR Program consists of the following key initiatives:

1. MSE Funding Program (PUMK)
MSE Funding Program is carried out under the following conditions:

a. MSE Funding Program is provided to individuals or business entities that meet the criteria for micro and small enterprises as stipulated in applicable laws and regulations.
b. MSE Funding Program is offered in the form of conventional financing and/or sharia-compliant financing.
c. The maximum financing amount is IDR250,000,000 (two hundred fifty million rupiah) for each micro and small business.
d. MSE Funding Program may be followed by business development assistance, with its costs covered under the Company's CSR activities.

2. CSR Assistance Program
The CSR Assistance Program consists of various activities, including:

a. Disaster Management Activities
Disaster management covers both natural and non-natural disasters, including disease outbreaks, with the following scope:

i) Provision of medical supplies and/or medical aid, essential goods, clean water, sanitation facilities, and other necessities.
ii) Provision of equipment and facilities for disaster relief, such as rubber boats, refugee tents/temporary shelters, etc.
iii) Post-disaster recovery efforts, including construction of educational facilities, sanitation infrastructure, disaster recovery infrastructure, trauma management, and education/training for disaster victims.
