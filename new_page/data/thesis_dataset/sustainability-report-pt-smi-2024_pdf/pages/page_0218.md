Standar ESS PT SMI terdiri dari 10 acuan sebagai berikut:
- ESS 1: Asesmen dan pengelolaan risiko dan dampak lingkungan dan sosial
- ESS 2: Ketenagakerjaan dan lingkungan kerja
- ESS 3: Pencegahan dan pengurangan polusi
- ESS 4: Keselamatan, kesehatan dan keamanan
- ESS 5: Pembebasan lahan dan pemukiman kembali secara tidak sukarela
- ESS 6: Pelestarian keanekaragaman hayati dan pengelolaan sumber daya alam
- ESS 7: Masyarakat adat
- ESS 8: Warisan budaya
- ESS 9: Konservasi energi dan penggunaan energi ramah lingkungan
- ESS 10: Konsultasi dan mekanisme penanganan keluhan

Dalam implementasinya, Perseroan melakukan evaluasi risiko lingkungan dan sosial berdasarkan Prosedur Perlindungan Lingkungan dan Sosial. Prosedur ini menjadi panduan atas pelaksanaan kajian pada setiap kegiatan pembiayaan yang dilakukan oleh PT SMI dalam bentuk Environmental and Social Due Diligence (ESDD). Kajian dilakukan untuk mengidentifikasi kesenjangan-kesenjangan yang ada sekaligus merumuskan tindaklanjut berupa rencana tindakan perbaikan (Corrective Action Plan/CAP).

DELST members are permanent representatives in the Risk &amp; Capital Committee, the Project Development &amp; Advisory Services Committee, Financing Committee, Sustainable Financing Committee, and Equity Investment Committee. The division comprises specialists in various fields, including environmental, social, Geographic Information System (GIS), spatial planning, disaster risk mitigation, occupational health and safety, biodiversity, gender equality and social inclusion, energy, regional infrastructure, healthcare, transportation, as well as reviewer of the preparation process for advisory services and project development.

Through DELST, PT SMI implements ESS for projects financed under predetermined standard requirements. This division also provides assistance and fosters understanding among debtors regarding compliance with and commitment to ESS principles. Additionally, it ensures the implementation of ESS for financed projects in accordance with established standards. Furthermore, the division performs role in offering guidance and enhancing debtors' comprehension of ESS principles and commitments.

PT SMI's ESS standards consist of ten references as follows:
- ESS 1: Assessment and Management of Environmental and Social Risks and Impacts
- ESS 2: Labor &amp; Working Conditions
- ESS 3: Pollution Prevention and Abatement
- ESS 4: Safety, Health &amp; Security
- ESS 5: Land Acquisition &amp; Involuntary Resettlement
- ESS 6: Biodiversity Conservation &amp; Natural Resources Management
- ESS 7: Indigenous Peoples
- ESS 8: Cultural Heritage
- ESS 9: Energy Conservation &amp; Environmentally-friendly Energy
- ESS 10: Consultation &amp; Grievance Redress Mechanism

In its implementation, the Company conducts environmental and social risk evaluations based on the Environmental and Social Safeguards Procedure. This procedure serves as a guideline for conducting assessments in every financing activity undertaken by PT SMI through Environmental and Social Due Diligence (ESDD). The assessment is carried out to identify existing gaps and formulate follow-up actions in the form of a Corrective Action Plan (CAP).
