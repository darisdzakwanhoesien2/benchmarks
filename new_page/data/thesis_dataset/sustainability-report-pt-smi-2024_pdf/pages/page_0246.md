PT SMI implemented a hybrid work culture, where some employees work from the office (WFO) while others work from anywhere (WFA).

Despite a 9.5% increase in the number of employees compared to 2023, PT SMI's electricity consumption efficiency improved by 9.1%, as reflected in the year-on-year (2023 vs. 2024) electricity consumption comparison. This trend is illustrated in the average electricity consumption (kWh) per employee per month.
