Untuk mendukung hasil penilaian yang dilakukan oleh Perseroan, PT SMI juga melakukan survei kepada para pemangku kepentingan. Survei melibatkan total 47 responden dari berbagai kelompok pemangku kepentingan, yaitu pemegang saham, akademisi, debitur BUMN dan swasta, LSM, masyarakat (penerima manfaat proyek/TJSL), media, mitra sindikasi pembiayaan, pemerintah daerah vendor/supplier, serta multilateral finance institution dan lembaga donor internasional. Survei tersebut dilakukan untuk mengetahui interest dari pemangku kepentingan terhadap topik keberlanjutan yang dinilai relevan untuk dipublikasikan dalam SR 2024 PT SMI.

To support the assessment conducted by the Company, PT SMI also conducted a survey among stakeholders. The survey involved a total of 47 respondents from various stakeholder groups, including shareholders, academics, SOEs and private debtors, NGOs, communities (beneficiaries of projects, CSR, programs), media, syndication partners in financing, regional/local governments, vendor/supplier, and multilateral finance institution and international donor agencies. The survey was conducted to determine stakeholder interest in sustainability topics that are considered relevant to be published in PT SMI's SR 2024.
