## Kinerja Pilar Bisnis

Melalui 3 pilar bisnis utamanya PT SMI hingga 31 Desember 2024 telah membiayai proyek infrastruktur sejumlah total 510 proyek dengan nilai Rp1.153,24 triliun dan telah memberikan jasa konsultasi dan pengembangan proyek untuk total 206 proyek senilai Rp1.121,58 triliun.

## Business Pilar Performance

Through its 3 (three) main business pillars, as of December 31, 2024, PT SMI has financed a total of 510 infrastructure projects with a total value of Rp1,153.24 trillion and has provided advisory and project development services for 206 projects worth IDR1,121.58 trillion.
