PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur (Persero) - Laporan Keberlanjutan 2024

Implementasi kedelapan prinsip tersebut selaras dengan budaya keberlanjutan PT SMI yaitu I-SPRINT yang merupakan singkatan dari Integrity, Service Excellence, Partnership, Resilience, Innovation dan Trust. Untuk membangun budaya keberlanjutan di kalangan internal Perseroan, PT SMI melakukannya sejak proses rekrutmen karyawan dan secara berkesinambungan dilakukan melalui berbagai media dan kesempatan, seperti situs/website, media sosial, pertemuan antara manajemen dengan pegawai, dan sebagainya. Adapun upaya PT SMI membangun budaya keberlanjutan untuk kalangan eksternal seperti debitur, vendor/mitra kerja, masyarakat dan pemangku kepentingan eksternal lainnya dilakukan melalui berbagai program dan kesempatan seperti vendor gathering, sosialisasi program Tanggung Jawab Sosial dan Lingkungan (TSJL), penandatangan pakta integritas dan sebagainya.

PT SMI meyakini konsistensinya dalam membangun budaya keberlanjutan akan berdampak positif terhadap operasional perusahaan, meningkatkan citra sebagai lembaga keuangan yang bertanggung jawab dan berorientasi pada pembangunan berkelanjutan, sekaligus menguatkan kepercayaan dari para pemangku kepentingan. Selaras dengan itu, implementasi budaya keberlanjutan juga akan mengukuhkan Perseroan dalam mengelola risiko lingkungan dan sosial secara lebih efektif sehingga dapat meminimalkan potensi dampak negatif terhadap proyek infrastruktur yang dibiayai Perseroan melalui para debitur.

The implementation of these eight principles is in line with PT SMI's sustainability culture, known as I-SPRINT, which stands for Integrity, Service Excellence, Partnership, Resilience, Innovation, and Trust. To cultivate a sustainability culture internally within the Company, PT SMI integrates this culture starting from the employee recruitment process and continues consistently through various media and opportunities, such as the website, social media, management-employee meetings, and others. Meanwhile, PT SMI's efforts to build a sustainability culture externally, targeting debtors, vendors/partners, communities, and other external stakeholders, are conducted through various programs and opportunities such as vendor gathering, dissemination of Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) programs, signing of integrity pacts, etc.

PT SMI believes that its consistent efforts in fostering a sustainability culture will positively impact the Company's operations, enhance its image as a responsible financial institution oriented towards sustainable development, and strengthen the trust of stakeholders. In line with this, the implementation of a sustainability culture will also reinforce the Company's ability to manage environmental and social risks more effectively, thereby minimizing potential negative impacts on infrastructure projects financed by the Company through its debtors.
