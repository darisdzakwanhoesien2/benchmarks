Pelatihan Inventarisasi Emisi Gas Rumah Kaca (GRK) untuk Debitur dan internal PT SMI

Pelatihan Inventarisasi Emisi Gas Rumah Kaca (GRK) untuk sektor Pembangkit berbahan bakar fosil diselenggarakan pada tanggal 4-5 November 2024 yang dihadiri oleh 39 peserta, termasuk karyawan PT SMI dan 12 debitur dari sektor Pembangkit Listrik Tenaga Uap (PLTU), Pembangkit Listrik Tenaga Gas dan Uap (PLTGU), serta Pembangkit Listrik Tenaga Gas (PLTG). Materi pelatihan disampaikan oleh ahli dari berbagai institusi, yaitu Kementerian Lingkungan Hidup (Bapak Prasetyadi), Kementerian ESDM (Bapak M. Tomas Triananta, Bapak Hikmat Sandi), Konsultan Insolity (Bapak Randy Ismail), dan PT SMI (Ibu Asri Suciati).

Training on Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Emissions Inventory for Debtors and PT SMI Employees

The Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Emissions Inventory Training for the fossil fuel-based power generation sector was held on November 4-5, 2024. The training was attended by 39 participants, including PT SMI employees and 12 debtors from the Coal-Fired Power Plant (PLTU), Combined Cycle Gas Turbine Power Plant (PLTGU), and Gas-Fired Power Plant (PLTG) sectors. Training materials were delivered by experts from various institutions, including Ministry of Environment – Prasetyadi, Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources– M. Tomas Triananta, Mr. Hikmat Sandi, Insolity Consultant – Randy Ismail, and PT SMI – Asri Suciati
