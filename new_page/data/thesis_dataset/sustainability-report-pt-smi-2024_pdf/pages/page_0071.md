## 6. Penguatan Ekosistem Infrastruktur

Perseroan memberikan dukungan kepada pemerintah, baik pusat maupun daerah, serta stakeholders lainnya dalam rangka penguatan ekosistem infrastruktur yang meliputi kegiatan originasi dan screening proyek, koordinasi, sosialisasi, serta kegiatan peningkatan kapasitas dalam bentuk sharing session yang dilakukan oleh Perseroan atau bersama dengan donor/lembaga multilateral yang bertindak sebagai mitra Perseroan dalam strateginya sebagai Development Partner dan Ecosystem Enabler.

Perseroan juga mendukung kegiatan yang mendorong pengembangan pipeline proyek dari Pemda maupun Kementerian/Lembaga dalam bentuk kegiatan Pre-Project Development Facility (Pre-PDF) proyek KPBU dari Kementerian Keuangan, guna mendukung kesiapan proyek KPBU sebelum PDF diberikan. Di dalam fasilitas ini, Perseroan menyediakan program sharing sessions secara spesifik terkait bahasan aspek tertentu yang bersifat tematik baik dari sisi manajemen proyek maupun dari sisi sektoral.

## 6. Strengthening Infrastructure Ecosystem

Strengthening the Company provides support to both central and regional governments as well as other stakeholders in strengthening the infrastructure ecosystem, which includes project origination and screening, coordination, dissemination, and capacity building activities through sharing sessions held by the Company or co-organized with donors/multilateral organizations acting as the Company's strategic partner in its role as Development Partner and Ecosystem Enabler.

The Company also supports activities that spur the development of project pipeline from the Local Government as well as Ministries/Institutions in the form of Pre-Project Development Facility (Pre-PDF) for PPP projects from the Ministry of Finance, in order to support the readiness of PPP projects before the PDF is provided. In this facility, the Company organizes specific sharing session programs on certain thematic aspects, both from the project management side and from the sectoral side.
