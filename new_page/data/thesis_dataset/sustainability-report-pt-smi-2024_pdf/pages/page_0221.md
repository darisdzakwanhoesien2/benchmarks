# Pelaksanaan FGD atas usulan sistem pemeringkatan ESS

PT SMI melaksanakan Focus Group Discussion (FGD) terkait usulan sistem pemeringkatan (rating) ESS pada tanggal 9 Jul 2024. FGD bertujuan untuk memperoleh masukan terhadap usulan atribut masing-masing variabel perlindungan lingkungan dan sosial (Environmental and Social Safeguard/ESS) PT SMI beserta pembobotannya, termasuk memperoleh masukan terhadap usulan desain hasil penilaian variabel ESS tertimbang PT SMI. FGD ini dihadiri oleh internal PT SMI (DELST) bersama pihak eksternal meliputi akademisi, konsultan, debitur, dan financial institution.

# Implementation of FGD on the Proposed ESS Rating System

PT SMI conducted a Focus Group Discussion (FGD) on July 9, 2024, to discuss the proposed Environmental and Social Safeguard (ESS) rating system. The FGD aimed to gather feedback on the proposed attributes for each environmental and social safeguard variable, along with their weighting. Additionally, it sought input on the design of the weighted ESS variable assessment results for PT SMI. This initiative was attended by internal PT SMI representatives (DELST), along with external stakeholders, including academics, consultants, debtors, and financial institutions, to ensure a comprehensive evaluation and alignment with industry best practices.

Benchmarking oleh PT Tamaris Hydro kepada PT SMI dalam Penerapan Prinsip-Prinsip Environmental, Social, and Governance
Benchmarking diisi dengan pemaparan dan diskusi bersama PT Tamaris Hydro dan tim DELST PT SMI pada tanggal 10 September 2024, di mana PT SMI menyampaikan bagaimana penerapan kebijakan ESG di integrasikan dalam kegiatan bisnis yang mencakup serangkaian aspek pengelolaan isu lingkungan hidup, isu sosial, dan tata kelola perusahaan yang dapat menjadi pertimbangan dalam berinvestasi.

# Benchmarking by PT Tamaris Hydro with PT SMI on the Implementation of Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Principles

The benchmarking session was conducted on September 10, 2024, featuring presentations and discussions between PT Tamaris Hydro and PT SMI's DELST team. During this session, PT SMI shared insights on how ESG policies are integrated into business activities, covering various aspects of environmental management, social issues, and corporate governance. These principles serve as key considerations in investment decision-making and ensure that sustainability is embedded within the Company's operational framework.
