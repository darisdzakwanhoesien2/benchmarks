## Pemantauan Risiko

Perseroan secara konsisten melakukan pemantauan risiko dari pelaksanaan kegiatan operasional dan bisnis sebagai upaya dalam memastikan penerapan tata kelola berjalan secara efektif. Pemantauan risiko dilakukan Risk Taking Divisions (RTD) selaku risk owner dan secara periodik oleh Direktorat Manajemen Risiko. Pemantauan risiko tersebut dilakukan untuk meninjau perkembangan dan perubahan risiko yang terjadi, serta melihat efektivitas pelaksanaan pengendalian risiko Perseroan.

Berbagai hasil pemantauan didokumentasikan dalam bentuk laporan disampaikan secara berkala kepada Direksi, antara lain:

1. Laporan Pengelolaan Risiko di mana di dalamnya terutama membahas Profil Risiko dan Pemantauan Risk Appetite Statement Perseroan (triwulanan).
2. Laporan Portofolio Pembiayaan, Investasi, Treasury, Pengembangan Proyek dan Jasa Konsultasi (bulanan).
3. Laporan Market &amp; Liquidity (bulanan).
4. Laporan Pengelolaan Pembiayaan Khusus (bulanan).
5. Laporan Hasil Pelaksanaan Forum URCM.

Divisi Evaluasi Lingkungan Sosial &amp; Teknik secara berkala membuat pelaporan terkait dengan pengelolaan Risiko Lingkungan dan Sosial melalui pelaporan status pemenuhan Corrective Action Plan (CAP) aspek lingkungan dan sosial. Hasil laporan tersebut selanjutnya digunakan oleh Manajemen untuk melihat kondisi aktual perkembangan operasional dan bisnis, serta dapat menambah pertimbangan manajemen dalam mengambil suatu keputusan.

## Risk Monitoring

The Company consistently monitors risks from operational and business activities as an effort to ensure effective governance implementation. Risk monitoring is conducted by the Risk Taking Divisions (RTD) as the risk owner and periodically by the Directorate of Risk Management. This monitoring aims to review the development and changes in risks, as well as to evaluate the effectiveness of the Company's risk controls.

Monitoring results are documented in reports and regularly presented to the Board of Directors, including:

1. Risk Management Report, primarily discussing the Risk Profile and Monitoring of the Company's Risk Appetite Statement (quarterly).
2. Financing, Investment, Treasury, Project Development, and Consulting Services Portfolio Report (monthly).
3. Market &amp; Liquidity Report (monthly).
4. Special Financing Management Report (monthly).
5. Results of the URCM Forum Implementation Report.

The Environmental, Social &amp; Technical Evaluation Division periodically prepares reports on the management of Environmental and Social Risks through the reporting of the fulfillment status of the Corrective Action Plan (CAP) for environmental and social aspects. The results of these reports are subsequently utilized by Management to assess the actual conditions of operational and business developments and serve as additional considerations in managerial decision-making.
