# Organisasi Tim Tanggap Darurat (Emergency Response Team)

Seiring dengan komitmen untuk merealisasikan lingkungan kerja yang nyaman dan aman sesuai prinsip K3, pada tahun 2018, Perseroan membentuk Emergency Response Team (ERT). Tim terdiri dari perwakilan masing-masing divisi yang susunan organisasinya terdiri dari Ketua ERT, Wakil Ketua ERT, Sekretaris, Koordinator dan anggota Tim Floor Warden, Koordinator dan anggota Tim P3K dan Koordinator, anggota tim Evakuasi, serta Koordinator dan anggota tim Logistik. Per 31 Desember 2024, total anggota ERT sebanyak 46 orang atau 9% dari total karyawan Perseroan. Dalam susunan kepengurusan tersebut, Direktur Operasional dan Keuangan bertindak sebagai penasihat. [GRI 403-4]

# Emergency Response Team Organization

In line with the commitment to creating a comfortable and safe work environment in accordance with OHS principles, the Company established the Emergency Response Team (ERT) in 2018. The team comprises representatives from each division, with an organizational structure consisting of the Head of ERT, Deputy Head of ERT, Secretary, Coordinators and members of the Floor Warden Team, Coordinators and members of the First Aid Team, Coordinators and members of the Evacuation Team, as well as Coordinators and members of the Logistics Team. As of December 31, 2024, the total number of ERT members is 46 people, or 19% of the Company's total employees. Within this organizational structure, the Director of Operations and Finance acts as an advisor. [GRI 403-4]
