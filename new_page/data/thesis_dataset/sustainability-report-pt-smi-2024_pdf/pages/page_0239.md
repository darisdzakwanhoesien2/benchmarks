# Penerapan Green Office [GRI 3-3] [OJK F.5]

Penerapan kantor ramah lingkungan (green office) merupakan kontribusi nyata PT SMI terhadap penanganan perubahan iklim melalui upaya mengurangi jejak karbon dan mendukung praktik berkelanjutan. Dengan menerapkan berbagai inisiatif ramah lingkungan seperti pengurangan penggunaan energi, pengelolaan limbah yang efisien, dan digitalisasi dokumen, Perseroan tidak hanya menciptakan lingkungan kerja yang lebih sehat tetapi juga berkontribusi dalam mengurangi emisi gas rumah kaca. Inisiatif ini sejalan dengan komitmen Perseroan mendukung strategi pemerintah dalam mencapai target pengurangan emisi gas rumah kaca dan transisi menuju ekonomi rendah karbon.

Langkah nyata untuk mewujudkan kantor ramah lingkungan melalui penerapan Lingkungan Kantor Hijau dan Sehat antara lain Perseroan melakukan office transformation dengan mengubah desain kantor mengadopsi konsep Activity Based Workspaces sebagai strategi workplace yang memberikan fleksibilitas tempat kerja bagi karyawan. # Implementation of Green Office [GRI 3-3] [OJK F.5]

The adoption of a green office is PT SMI's tangible contribution to addressing climate change by reducing its carbon footprint and supporting sustainable practices. Through various eco-friendly initiatives such as energy reduction, efficient waste management, and digital document transformation, the Company not only fosters a healthier work environment but also actively contributes to reducing greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions. This initiative aligns with the Company's commitment to supporting government strategies in achieving GHG emission reduction targets and facilitating the transition toward a low-carbon economy.

To realize a green office environment, the Company has implemented the Green and Healthy Office Environment initiative. One key strategy is office transformation, where workplace design is adapted to the Activity-Based Workspaces (ABW) concept. This approach provides greater flexibility for employees while optimizing their workspace to match the nature of their tasks. Additionally, this concept integrates the latest information technology (smart office) to enhance workflow efficiency, workspace design, and construction while incorporating eco-friendly features.

The Company recognizes that the effort to create a green office environment requires the active participation of all PT SMI employees in adopting sustainable and healthy workplace practices. As part of the internalization of this program, the Company has developed several policies related to the implementation of environmentally friendly office initiatives. In its implementation, PT SMI encourages the participation of all employees to take an active role in addressing climate change by adopting a green habit culture in the office environment. This is carried out through the #5 Green Action Campaign, which includes:

1. Save Energy (Electricity &amp; Water)

a. Turn off unused lights and electronic equipment;
b. Setting the use of air conditioning centrally through system-based ITM technology;
c. Use of energy efficient electronic equipment;
d. Utilization of sunlight as alternative lighting for workspace/meetings;
e. Turn off the water faucet after use;
f. Use of automatic water faucets for hand-washing;
g. Use of water fixtures to control water discharge
