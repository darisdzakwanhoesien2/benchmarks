Secara spesifik, Country Platform ETM merupakan suatu pembiayaan campuran (blended finance) yang dirancang untuk mempercepat penghentian pembangkit listrik tenaga batu bara (PLTU) dan membuka investasi untuk energi bersih. Sebagai Country Platform Manager ETM, Perseroan bertugas menyalurkan dukungan fiskal Pemerintah untuk kerangka pendanaan dan pembiayaan transisi energi di Indonesia.

ETM diimplementasikan melalui 2 (dua) skema yaitu Fasilitas Pengurangan Emisi (Carbon Reduction Facility/CRF) dan Fasilitas Energi Bersih (Clean Energy Facility/CEF). Skema CRF digunakan untuk mempercepat pensiun dini PLTU di Indonesia, sedangkan CEF ditujukan untuk mengembangkan atau menginvestasikan pembangunan fasilitas energi hijau. Kedua skema diharapkan menjadi solusi terbaik bagi masyarakat, investor, perekonomian daerah, dan lingkungan.

Sesuai dengan perluasan mandat yang diterima, Perseroan berperan dalam lingkup koordinasi dengan pemangku kepentingan, menyusun kajian komprehensif terkait dukungan fiskal, merumuskan konsep integrasi dukungan fiskal dan fasilitas de-risking dari sumber lain yang sah, serta melakukan langkah-langkah awal yang diperlukan untuk implementasi country platform ini. Untuk itu, Perseroan juga telah mengidentifikasi mekanisme yang sesuai untuk ETM dan memberikan dukungan tidak hanya dari anggaran Pemerintah, melainkan juga memobilisasi pembiayaan dari mitra.

Dalam penerapan di lapangan, Perseroan berkolaborasi dengan berbagai mitra untuk memperoleh dukungan dalam pengembangan kebijakan energi, pendanaan proyek, peningkatan kapasitas, serta pertukaran pengetahuan untuk mendorong transisi energi di antaranya dengan:

1. Mitra Hibah (Bloomberg Philanthropies &amp; ClimateWorks Foundation’s Global Energy Transition Initiative, UK MENTARI, dan Global Energy Alliance for People and Planet);
2. Mitra pembiayaan (ADB, World Bank, Islamic Development Bank, Climate Investment Funds, HSBC, Standard Chartered, dan Japan Bank for International Cooperation);

Mitra pengetahuan &amp; teknis (United States Agency for International Development, Global Green Growth Institute, Climate Policy Initiative, United Nations Development Programme, Rocky Mountain Institute, Climate Bonds Initiative, Tony Blair Institute, dan United Nations Office for Project Services);

Specifically, the ETM Country Platform is a blended finance mechanism designed to accelerate the early retirement of coal-fired power plants (PLTU) and unlock investments in clean energy. As the Country Platform Manager for ETM, PT SMI is responsible for channeling the Government’s fiscal support within the funding and financing framework for Indonesia’s energy transition.

The Energy Transition Mechanism (ETM) is implemented through two financing schemes, namely Carbon Reduction Facility (CRF) and Clean Energy Facility (CEF). CRF is designed to support the early retirement of coal-fired power plants (PLTU) in Indonesia while CEF is aimed at developing or investing in green energy facilities development. Both schemes are expected to provide the best solutions for communities, investors, regional economies, and the environment.

With its expanded mandate, PT SMI performs a crucial role in coordinating with stakeholders, conducting comprehensive studies on fiscal support, developing an integrated framework for fiscal support and risk mitigation facilities from other legitimate sources, and initiating key steps for implementing the ETM Country Platform. To support this effort, PT SMI has identified the appropriate mechanisms for ETM implementation and is committed to mobilizing financing not only from Government funds but also from partners.

In its practice, PT SMI has established strategic collaborations with various partners to obtain support in energy policy development, project financing, capacity building, as well as knowledge exchange to drive the energy transition, including through:

1. Grant Partners (Bloomberg Philanthropies, ClimateWorks Foundation’s Global Energy Transition Initiative, UK MENTARI, and the Global Energy Alliance for People and Planet);
2. Financing Partners (Asian Development Bank (ADB), World Bank, Islamic Development Bank, Climate Investment Funds, HSBC, Standard Chartered, and Japan Bank for International Cooperation);
3. Knowledge and Technical Partners (United States Agency for International Development (USAID), Global Green Growth Institute (GGGI), Climate Policy Initiative, United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), Rocky Mountain Institute, Climate Bonds Initiative, Tony Blair Institute, dan United Nations Office for Project Services);
4. Investment Partner (the Indonesia Investment Authority).
