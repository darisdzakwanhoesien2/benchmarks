Dalam melaksanakan kegiatan usahanya, Perseroan tidak hanya memiliki karyawan tetap dan karyawan kontrak, melainkan juga memiliki kontrak dengan pihak penyedia jasa outsourcing untuk mendukung kegiatan operasional kantor. Tenaga kerja tersebut memberikan dukungan dalam kegiatan general service antara lain seperti office boy/girl, messenger, cleaning service, security, driver dan admin.

Per 31 Desember 2024, Perseroan mempekerjakan tenaga kerja outsource sebanyak 71 orang, naik dibandingkan tahun 2023 yang mencapai 70 orang.

Adapun hak-hak tenaga kerja karyawan tetap dan pekerja outsource terkait remunerasi meliputi: [GRI 401-2]

In carrying out its business activities, the Company employs not only permanent and contract employees but also engages outsourcing service providers to support office operations. These outsourced workers provide support in general services, including but not limited to office boys/ girls, messengers, cleaning services, security, drivers, and administrative staff.

As of December 31, 2024, the Company employed a total of 71 outsourced workers, a increase compared to 70 workers in 2023.

# Kebijakan Cuti

Cuti melahirkan (maternity leave) merupakan hak normatif bagi karyawan perempuan dan karyawan laki-laki yang istrinya melahirkan (paternity leave) yang dijamin pemenuhannya oleh PT SMI. Sesuai regulasi yang berlaku, Perseroan memberikan cuti melahirkan kepada karyawan perempuan selama 3 bulan, yakni 1,5 bulan sebelum melahirkan dan 1,5 bulan setelah melahirkan yang didasarkan pada perhitungan dokter kandungan ataupun bidan yang merawat. # Leave Policy

Maternity leave is a normative right for female employees and male employees whose wives give birth (paternity leave), the fulfillment of which is guaranteed by PT SMI. In accordance with applicable regulations, the Company provides female employees with 3 months of maternity leave, consisting of 1.5 months before childbirth and 1.5 months after childbirth, based on the calculation of the treating obstetrician or midwife.
