## Implikasi Financial Serta Risiko dan Peluang Lain Akibat dari Perubahan Iklim [GRI 3-3, 201-2] [OJK E.5]

Perubahan iklim merupakan tantangan tersendiri bagi pembangunan infrastruktur yang dibiayai PT SMI, terkhusus bagi debitur. Peningkatan frekuensi dan intensitas bencana alam seperti banjir, tanah longsor, dan badai tropis dapat merusak infrastruktur fisik, seperti jalan, jembatan, atau fasilitas energi, sehingga memperpendek masa pakai dan meningkatkan biaya pemeliharaan. Selain itu, kenaikan suhu global dan perubahan pola curah hujan juga dapat memengaruhi efisiensi operasional infrastruktur, misalnya dalam sektor energi dan pengelolaan air. Dalam hal ini, infrastruktur yang tidak dirancang untuk menghadapi dampak perubahan iklim berisiko kehilangan fungsionalitas lebih cepat, menghambat pertumbuhan ekonomi, dan menurunkan kualitas hidup masyarakat.

Untuk menghadapi tantangan tersebut, Perseroan berupaya untuk memastikan bahwa proyek-proyek yang dibiayai telah mengintegrasikan prinsip adaptasi terhadap perubahan iklim sejak tahap perencanaan. Adaptasi tersebut antara lain dilakukan melalui penerapan desain infrastruktur yang tangguh, seperti penguatan struktur bangunan, peninggian jalan di area rawan banjir, dan sistem drainase yang lebih efisien. Seiring dengan itu, Perseroan juga mendorong penerapan teknologi terkini yang dapat memantau dan memitigasi risiko perubahan iklim terhadap infrastruktur. Dengan langkah-langkah ini, selain menjaga keberlanjutan investasi, Perseroan juga turut mendukung ketahanan Indonesia terhadap dampak perubahan iklim melalui proyek pembangunan infrastruktur yang dibiayai.

To reinforce the implementation of these strategic initiatives, the Company has established policies related to occupational health and safety (OHS) and environmental protection, taking into account social aspects for communities through internal regulations, including:

1. Regulation of the Board of Directors No. PD-025/SMI/0622 dated June 20, 2022, concerning Procedures for Environmental and Social Protection;
2. Regulation of the Board of Directors No. PD-014/SMI/1123 dated November 9, 2023, concerning Procedures for Grievance Management of Affected Communities and Observer Groups (Revision - 1);
3. Regulation of the Board of Directors No. PD-022/SMI/0622 dated June 20, 2022, concerning Procedures for Gender Equality and Social Inclusion Mainstreaming; and
4. Regulation of the Board of Directors No. PD-012/SMI/1023 dated October 4, 2023, concerning Procedures for Occupational Safety, Health, and Environmental Management System;
5. Technical Guidelines for Greenhouse Gas Emissions Calculation (Operational Activities), 2024.

## Financial Implications, Risks, and Opportunities Arising from Climate Change [GRI 3-3, 201-2] [OJK E.5]

Climate change poses a significant challenge to the infrastructure development projects financed by PT SMI, particularly for debtors. The increasing frequency and intensity of natural disasters, such as floods, landslides, and tropical storms, can damage physical infrastructure, including roads, bridges, and energy facilities, thereby reducing their lifespan and increasing maintenance costs. Additionally, rising global temperatures and changes in rainfall patterns can impact the operational efficiency of infrastructure, particularly in the energy and water management sectors. In this context, infrastructure that is not designed to face the effects of climate change risks losing its functionality more rapidly, hindering economic growth, and lowering the quality of life for communities.

To address these challenges, the Company strive to ensure have integrated climate change adaptation principles into their projects from the planning stage. This adaptation is performed through the implementation of resilient infrastructure designs, such as strengthening building structures, elevating roads in flood-prone areas, and improving drainage systems. Alongside these measures, the Company also encourages the adoption of the latest technologies that can monitor and mitigate climate change risks to infrastructure. With these steps, in addition to maintaining the sustainability of investments, the Company also supports Indonesia's resilience to the impacts of climate change through the infrastructure development projects it finances.
