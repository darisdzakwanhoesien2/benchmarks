b. Kegiatan Pendidikan/Pelatihan
Kegiatan Pendidikan/Pelatihan dengan ruang lingkup antara lain:
i) Bantuan penunjang pelaksanaan kegiatan pendidikan, seperti peralatan sekolah, peralatan laboratorium, peralatan/sarana olah raga, dan peralatan untuk praktik belajar.
ii) Bantuan biaya pendidikan/beasiswa bagi yang memiliki potensi dan prestasi namun tidak memiliki biaya untuk melanjutkan pendidikan.
iii) Bantuan pembangunan dan/atau rehabilitasi sarana pendidikan, seperti ruang kelas, ruang olah raga, ruang laboratorium, dan ruang praktik belajar.
iv) Kegiatan pelatihan dan peningkatan kapasitas bagi masyarakat.
v) Kegiatan pelaksanaan riset/penelitian yang terkait dengan kegiatan akademik di lembaga pendidikan.

c. Kegiatan Kesehatan
Kegiatan Kesehatan dengan ruang lingkup antara lain:
i) Bantuan penyediaan obat-obatan, peralatan kesehatan untuk penanganan pasien, serta peralatan penunjang kegiatan operasional rumah sakit/puskesmas/klinik.
ii) Bantuan rehabilitasi atau renovasi sentra layanan kesehatan.
iii) Bantuan sarana penunjang perbaikan sanitasi dan air bersih yang bertujuan untuk peningkatan kesehatan masyarakat.

d. Kegiatan Infrastruktur
Kegiatan Infrastruktur dengan ruang lingkup antara lain:
i) Kegiatan pengembangan infrastruktur/fasilitas yang terkait dengan pengembangan energi baru dan terbarukan (EBT) dan transisi energi berskala mikro, seperti penyediaan peralatan pembangkit listrik tenaga air, tenaga surya, tenaga angin, serta sumber energi berkelanjutan lainnya.
ii) Bantuan pembangunan dan rehabilitasi prasarana dan/atau sarana umum, seperti jalan, jembatan, penerangan jalan, taman, dan sarana sosial lainnya.
iii) Bantuan pembangunan atau renovasi panti sosial, panti asuhan, panti jompo, dan fasilitas rehabilitasi sosial lainnya.
iv) Kegiatan atau bantuan lain terkait dengan pengembangan infrastruktur.

e. Kegiatan Sarana Keagamaan
Kegiatan Sarana Keagamaan dengan ruang lingkup antara lain:
i) Bantuan pembangunan atau renovasi tempat peribadatan.
ii) Bantuan penunjang sarana peribadatan dan pelaksanaan kegiatan keagamaan.

b. Education and Training Activities
The scope of education and training activities includes:
i) Support for educational activities, such as school equipment, laboratory equipment, sports facilities, and hands-on learning tools.
ii) Scholarships and financial assistance for individuals with potential and achievements who lack the financial means to continue their education.
iii) Construction and/or rehabilitation of educational facilities, such as classrooms, sports halls, laboratories, and vocational training spaces.
iv) Training and capacity-building programs for the community.
v) Research and studies related to academic activities in educational institutions.

c. Healthcare Activities
The scope of healthcare activities includes:
i) Provision of medicines, medical equipment for patient care, and operational support equipment for hospitals, community health centers (puskesmas), and clinics.
ii) Rehabilitation or renovation of healthcare service centers.
iii) Provision of sanitation and clean water infrastructure to improve public health.

d. Infrastructure Development Activities
The scope of infrastructure development activities includes:
i) Development of micro-scale infrastructure/facilities related to new and renewable energy and energy transition, such as hydropower, solar power, wind power, and other sustainable energy sources.
ii) Construction and rehabilitation of public infrastructure and facilities, including roads, bridges, street lighting, parks, and other social facilities.
iii) Construction or renovation of social care facilities, such as orphanages, elderly care homes, and other social rehabilitation centers.
iv) Other activities or assistances regarding infrastructure development.

e. Religious Facility Activities
The scope of religious facility activities includes:
i) Construction or renovation of places of worship.
ii) Provision of supporting facilities for worship activities and religious events.
