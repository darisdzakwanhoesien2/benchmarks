# 3. Capacity Building mengenai Stunting di Kabupaten Timor Tengah Selatan (TTS)

Kajian Penanganan Stunting di Kabupaten TTS dilaksanakan secara berkesinambungan dengan kegiatan capacity building. Melalui workshop dan FGD di level kecamatan dan kabupaten serta advokasi di level provinsi, kegiatan ini bertujuan untuk meningkatkan pemahaman pemerintah daerah mengenai penyebab, dampak, serta potensi intervensi yang efektif dalam mengatasi isu sosial stunting. Kegiatan ini melibatkan berbagai pemangku kepentingan kunci yang meliputi banyak unsur seperti Badan Perencanaan dan Pembangunan Daerah, Dinas Kesehatan, Tim Percepatan Penurunan Stunting, DPRD, Dinas Perumahan Rakyat, dan Kawasan Permukiman, Organisasi Non-Pemerintah, Asosiasi Profwesi, Akademi, Kepala Puskesmas, Tokoh Masyarakat, Tokoh Agama, Kepala Desa, dan berbagai pihak lainnya. Kegiatan ini juga memetakan peran masing-masing aktor pemerintah, non-pemerintah, serta masyarakat dalam upaya bersama mengatasi tantangan stunting secara berkelanjutan.

## 3. Capacity Building on Stunting Management in Timor Tengah Selatan (TTS) Regency

The Stunting Management Study in TTS Regency is continuously supported by capacity-building activities. Through workshops and focus group discussions (FGDs) at the sub-district and regency levels, as well as advocacy at the provincial level, this initiative aims to enhance local government understanding of the causes, impacts, and potential interventions for addressing stunting. The program involves key stakeholders, including: Regional Development Planning Agency (Bappeda), Health Institution, Stunting Reduction Acceleration Team, Regional House of Representatives (DPRD), Public Housing and Settlement Agency, Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs), Professional Associations and Academia, Community and Religious Leaders, Heads of Public Health Center (Puskesmas), Village Heads, other relevant stakeholders. This initiative also maps out the roles of government, non-governmental organizations, and the community, ensuring a collaborative and sustainable approach to stunting mitigation.

The Public Financing Division also actively conducts outreach and capacity development for local governments.

## 1. Consultation and FGD on PT SMI's Design of Results-Based Loan for Local/Regional Governments

## 2. FGD Pembiayaan Penyediaan Aset BLU

PT SMI menyelenggarakan Focus Group Discussion (FGD) Pembiayaan Penyediaan Aset Badan Layanan Umum (BLU) dengan skema PMK No. 29/PMK.05/2022 di Bogor pada tanggal 13-14 Juni 2024 Peserta FDG adalah perwakilan dari Kementerian Kesehatan dan beberapa BLU yang diundang. ## 2. FGD on Public Service Agency (BLU) Asset Financing

PT SMI organized FGD on BLU Asset Financing under Regulation of the Minister of Finance No. 29/PMK.05/2022, held in Bogor on June 13-14, 2024. Participants included representatives from the Ministry of Health and several BLUs. The discussion focused on plans and timelines for asset procurement, facilitated by PT SMI.
