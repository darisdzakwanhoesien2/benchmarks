Per 31 Desember 2024, terdapat 111 pembangkit EBT yang turut dibiayai PT SMI dan telah beroperasi. Potensi energi terbarukan yang dihasilkan per tahun dari proyek-proyek tersebut diestimasi mencapai 4.338.368 MWh/tahun. Sedangkan potensi emisi GRK yang dapat dihindari mencapai 4 juta ton CO₂e/tahun dengan potensi carbon credit equivalent sebesar Rp235 miliar*. [FS8]

As of December 31, 2024, there were 111 renewable energy plants that were also financed by PT SMI and have been operating. The potential renewable energy generated per year from these projects is estimated to reach 4.338.368 MWh/year. Meanwhile, the potential GHG emissions that can be avoided reach 4 million tons of CO₂e/year with a potential carbon credit equivalent of IDR235 billion*. [FS8]

Perseroan juga menghitung manfaat lingkungan dari proyek transportasi ramah lingkungan yang dibiayai PT SMI, yaitu Light Rail Transit terintegrasi di wilayah Jakarta, Bogor, Depok, dan Bekasi (LRT Jabodetabek). Adanya LRT Jabodebek sebagai salah satu pilihan mass public transportation diestimasi dapat menghemat energi sebesar 2.775 TJ/tahun dan menghindari emisi GRK sebesar 72.841 ton CO₂e/tahun.

The Company also calculated the environmental benefits of an environmentally friendly transportation project financed by PT SMI, namely the integrated Light Rail Transit in Jakarta, Bogor, Depok, and Bekasi (LRT Jabodetabek). The Jabodetabek LRT as one of the mass public transportation options is estimated to save energy by 2,775 TJ/year and avoid GHG emissions by 72,841 tons CO₂e/year.

Sementara itu, proyek-proyek sektor sosial yang dibiayai Perseroan juga telah memberikan berbagai manfaat yang dinikmati oleh masyarakat. Secara kumulatif, proyek air bersih berpotensi telah berdampak pada tersedianya akses air bersih bagi 5,2 juta rumah tangga (setara dengan 20 juta jiwa), proyek irigasi berpotensi telah berdampak pada teralirinya 188.299 ha sawah dan dihasilkannya 979.136 ton gabah kering/tahun, proyek sekolah berpotensi telah berdampak pada tersedianya tambahan kapasitas infrastruktur bagi 3.316 siswa serta proyek infrastruktur Kesehatan berpotensi telah berdampak. [FS7]

Meanwhile, social sector projects financed by the Company have also provided various benefits enjoyed by the community. Cumulatively, clean water projects have the potential to provide access to clean water for 5.2 million households (equivalent to 20 million people), irrigation projects have the potential to have an impact on the irrigation of 188,299 ha of rice fields and the production of 979,136 tons of dry grain/year, school projects have the potential to have an impact on the availability of additional infrastructure capacity for 3,316 students and Health infrastructure projects have the potential to have an impact. [FS7]
