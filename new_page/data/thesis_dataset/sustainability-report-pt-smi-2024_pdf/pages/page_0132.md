# Tujuan Penerapan Tata Kelola Perusahaan Objectives of Corporate Governance Implementation

Dalam rangka memastikan tata kelola perusahaan senantiasa sejalan dengan perkembangan kondisi yang dinamis, tujuan utama penerapan GCG di Perseroan antara lain:

1. Kepatuhan terhadap ketentuan perundang-undangan yang berlaku;
2. Pengelolaan Perseroan dengan lebih profesional, transparan, dan efisien;
3. Penguatan nilai (value) Perseroan;
4. Pengambilan keputusan yang optimal dan dilandasi oleh nilai moral tinggi;
5. Peningkatan daya saing Perseroan;
6. Pengembangan sumber daya Perseroan;
7. Peningkatan pengelolaan risiko usaha Perseroan;
8. Penguatan kesadaran dan tanggung jawab sosial perusahaan; dan
9. Penguatan sikap dan perilaku kerja yang lebih baik menuju perwujudan budaya Perseroan.

To ensure that corporate governance remains aligned with dynamic developments, the main objectives of implementing Good Corporate Governance (GCG) in the Company include:

1. Compliance with the prevailing laws and regulations;
2. Management of the Company in a more professional, transparent, and efficient manner;
3. Strengthening of the Company's values;
4. Optimal decision-making based on strong moral values;
5. Improvement of the Company's competitiveness;
6. Development of the Company's resources;
7. Improvement of the Company's business risk management;
8. Strengthening of the Company's awareness and corporate social responsibility; and
9. Strengthening of work behavior and attitude toward a better corporate culture.
