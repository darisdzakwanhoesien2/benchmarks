## Realisasi Program Tanggung Jawab Sosial [GRI 413-1][OJK F.25]

## Realization of Social Responsibility Programs [GRI 413-1][OJK F.25]

PT SMI's commitment to creating sustainable business practices is demonstrated through various Social and Environmental Responsibility (CSR) programs. To enhance the quality and diversity of these programs, the Company continuously introduces innovations and breakthroughs in designing and implementing CSR initiatives. In line with this effort, the Company conducts social mapping to identify strategic issues and community needs as the basis for developing its CSR programs. This mapping ensures that the programs implemented are more focused, targeted, sustainable, and aligned with community needs while also enhancing the Company's positive image.

In 2024, PT SMI continued several ongoing and sustainable programs that have become the Company's mainstay initiatives. Hence, the Company designed various priority CSR programs grouped into three main areas: social, environmental, and Micro and Small Enterprises Funding (MSE Funding). These three areas form the core focus of the Company's CSR efforts, encompassing programs such as MSE Funding, SMI Berbagi, and other CSR initiatives.
