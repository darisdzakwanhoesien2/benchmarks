September 2024, data from the Minister of Energy and Mineral Resources (ESDM) indicates that the realization of the new and renewable energy (EBT) mix has only reached 13.93% of the target of 19.5% by the end of 2024. This 2024 target aligns with Government Regulation Draft (RPP) No. 79 of 2014 concerning the National Energy Policy (KEN). Indonesia has set a renewable energy mix target of 23% by 2025. According to the Minister of Energy and Mineral Resources, the shortfall in achieving the EBT mix target is due to insufficient investment in the sector.

The focus on the EBT sector is also inseparable from the consideration that the new and renewable energy sector is in line with the sustainable development goals that the government is currently pursuing. More specifically, financing the EBT sector can be a solution to address climate and environmental change by reducing greenhouse gas emissions and dependence on fossil fuels. In the long term, financing the EBT sector contributes to achieving Net Zero Emissions (NZE) by 2060 or sooner.

Regarding sustainable financing, since 2018, PT SMI, through the Sustainable Financing Division (DPB), has been mandated by the government to accelerate the development of the green and sustainable sector. This includes financing activities, grant management, and technical assistance for the development of New and Renewable Energy (EBT) power plants, including geothermal exploration for Geothermal Power Plants (PLTP). Furthermore, the company has implemented general strategies to enhance business performance as part of its responsibility and mandate, as follows:

1. Building cooperation with multilateral institutions;
2. Enhancing coordination with relevant government agencies to encourage the creation a more conducive environment for developing projects that support sustainable development;
3. Increasing internal capacity for sustainable financing; and
4. Coordinating with international organizations that have specialized programs for renewable energy projects (such as de-risking programs) to be implemented in financing facilities for new debtors who require further support.

PT SMI is committed to fulfilling its mandate related to EBT financing to support sustainable development goals while simultaneously reducing greenhouse gas emissions, which contribute to climate change. Moreover, Indonesia has vast renewable energy potential, including geothermal, hydro, solar, and wind power. Currently, Indonesia's potential for renewable energy utilization in electricity generation remains largely untapped. Of the total electricity generation potential of 3,686 GW, only 12,557 MW of renewable energy has been utilized, with bioenergy contributing 3,086 MW.
