## 3. Social Performance

PT SMI's social performance reflects its commitment to fulfilling the rights of both internal and external stakeholders in areas such as human rights, labor rights, occupational health and safety (OHS), community empowerment, etc. The Company's commitment to employees as internal stakeholders is demonstrated through equal treatment, ensuring no incidents of discrimination, providing equal opportunities for career development through education and training programs, and creating a safe and healthy work environment in accordance with OHS principles. Meanwhile, the Company upholds debtor rights as external stakeholders by continuously innovating its products and services and enhancing products/services quality.

Meanwhile, the fulfillment of responsibility to the community is carried out through social responsibility programs includes the disbursement of TJSL funds amounting to IDR 22.33 billion or 100% of the RKAP fund distribution target of IDR 22.33 billion; PUMK Program, the distribution of PUMK funds in 2024 was recorded at IDR 1 billion or 100% of the RKAP of IDR 1 billion; the implementation of training related to sustainable finance was realized at 91.3% of the minimum target of 82.5% of the total training hours in 2024; likewise, the target for awareness of gender mainstreaming and social inclusion was achieved with the implementation of gender assessment of CSR projects in Siru and Bari. Also, gender assessment of one of our debtors, PT Krakatau Tirta Industri, publication of the Company's policies related to gender and social inclusion through PT SMI's official website, and PT SMI participated in a meeting initiated by Finance in Common Summit's (FiCS) Gender Coalition to discuss methodological developments in gender issues globally, update of the Gender Mainstreaming and Social Inclusion Working Group through the issuance of Board of Directors Decree No. SK-019/SMI/1024, followed by Advanced Training for the Working Group members in collaboration with the Asian Development Bank.

In terms of employee competency development, PT SMI spent IDR19.39 billion, an increase compared to 2023, which reached IDR 12.02 billion. As for OHS performance as of December 31, 2024, the total safe working hours were 741,632 hours and no fatalities were recorded.
