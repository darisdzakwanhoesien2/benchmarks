## Portfolio Khusus Proyek Green Climate Fund (GCF) [OJK F.3]

Perseroan berkomitmen untuk mendorong pencapaian SDGs dalam pembangunan infrastruktur yang dapat memberikan manfaat terhadap aspek ekonomi, sosial, dan lingkungan. Komitmen tersebut antara lain diwujudkan dengan Pembiayaan Green Climate Fund (GCF). Implementasi komitmen telah menjadikan PT SMI sebagai entitas pertama di Asia Tenggara yang mendapatkan akreditasi dari GCF sehingga memungkinkan Perseroan selaku entitas terakreditasi (accredited entity) untuk mengakses pendanaan dalam rangka mendukung proyek-proyek terkait mitigasi dan adaptasi perubahan iklim.

## Kriteria Proyek Investasi Green Climate Fund (GCF)

1. Potensi dampak proyek: Potensi untuk berkontribusi terhadap pencapaian tujuan-tujuan GCF.
2. Potensi pergeseran paradigma (Paradigm Shift): Dampak jangka panjang atas investasi GCF.
3. Potensi pembangunan berkelanjutan: Manfaat tambahan, ekonomi, sosial, lingkungan.
4. Kebutuhan penerima: Kerentanan dan kebutuhan pembiayaan dari penerima manfaat.
5. Kepemilikan negara: Kapasitas implementasi proyek, keselarasan dengan strategi dan kebijakan nasional.
6. Efisiensi dan efektivitas: Efektivitas dan efisiensi atas biaya proyek mitigasi perubahan iklim.

## Sektor yang Dapat Didanai

1. Mitigasi Perubahan Iklim
2. Pembangkitan dan akses energi.
3. Transportasi.
4. Kehutanan dan pemanfaatan lahan.
5. Bangunan, perkotaan, industri, dan appliances.

Pada tahun 2024, PT SMI telah menyampaikan concept note dan proposal Project Preparation Facility (PPF) kepada GCF untuk memasilitasi program "Climate Finance Facility to Support Energy Transition in Indonesia" yang telah mendapatkan No-Objection Letter (NOL) dari Kementerian Keuangan.

## Special Portfolio for Green Climate Fund (GCF) Projects [OJK F.3]

PT SMI is committed to advancing the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) by financing infrastructure projects that generate economic, social, and environmental benefits. One of the key initiatives supporting this commitment is the Green Climate Fund (GCF) financing program. Through this initiative, PT SMI became the first accredited entity in Southeast Asia to receive GCF accreditation. This accreditation enables the Company to access GCF funding, supporting projects focused on climate change mitigation and adaptation.

## Green Climate Fund (GCF) Project Criteria

1. Potential impact of the project: Potential to contribute to the achievement of GCF goals.
2. Potential paradigm shift (Paradigm Shift): Long-term impact on GCF investments.
3. Potential for sustainable development: Additional benefits, economic, social, environmental.
4. Recipient's need: Vulnerability and financing needs of beneficiaries.
5. State ownership: Project implementation capacity, alignment with national strategies and policies.
6. Efficiency and effectiveness: Cost effectiveness and efficiency of climate change mitigation projects.

## Eligible Sectors

1. Climate Changes Mitigation
2. Energy generation and access.
3. Transportation.
4. Forestry and land usage.
5. Building, urban, industrial, and appliances.

In 2024, PT SMI submitted a concept note and proposal for the Project Preparation Facility (PPF) to the Green Climate Fund (GCF) to facilitate the "Climate Finance Facility to Support Energy Transition in Indonesia" program, which has received a No-Objection Letter (NOL) from the Ministry of Finance.
