# FINDINGS AND CONCLUSIONS

## ASSURANCE OPINION

On the basis of the methodology described and the assurance work performed, we are satisfied that the specified performance information included in the scope of assurance is accurate, reliable, has been fairly stated and has been prepared, in all material respects, in accordance with the AA1000 Accountability Principles (2018) and Global Reporting Initiative Sustainability Reporting Standards 2021 (In Accordance with) and GRI G4: Financial Services Sector Disclosures (2008 Supplement).

We believe that the organization has chosen an appropriate level of assurance for this stage in their reporting.

## ADHERENCE TO AA1000 ACCOUNTABILITY PRINCIPLES (2018)

### INCLUSIVITY

PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur (Persero) has demonstrated a good commitment to stakeholder inclusivity and stakeholder engagement. A variety of engagement efforts such as survey and communication to employees, customers, investors, suppliers, sustainability experts, and other stakeholders are implemented to underpin the organization's understanding of stakeholder concerns. For future reporting, PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur (Persero) may proactively consider having more direct two-ways involvement of stakeholders during future engagement.

### MATERIALITY

PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur (Persero) has established effective processes for determining issues that are material to the business. Formal review has identified stakeholders and those issues that are material to each group and the report addresses these at an appropriate level to reflect their importance and priority to these stakeholders.

### RESPONSIVENESS

PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur (Persero) has responded to stakeholders issues that affect to its sustainability performance and is released through decisions, actions and performance, as well as communication with stakeholders.
