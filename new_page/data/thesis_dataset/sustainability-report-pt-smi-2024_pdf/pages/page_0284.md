## Penilaian Kinerja [GRI 404-3]

Perseroan melaksanakan penilaian kinerja kepada seluruh karyawan berdasarkan potential dan performance review yang menjadi acuan dalam penentuan program Individual Development Plan. Hasil penilaian sekaligus menjadi acuan bagi Perseroan dalam mempertimbangkan kenaikan jenjang karier dan remunerasi. Penilaian kinerja Perseroan dilakukan dengan menggunakan 2 (dua) metode yaitu:

1. Penilaian Perilaku Kerja (Feedback 360 Degree)
Metode penilaian Feedback 360 Degree melibatkan atasan terkait, rekan kerja, dan bawahan. Penilaian perilaku kerja terdiri dari 2 aspek, yaitu kedisiplinan dan sikap kerja (sikap proaktif, hasil kerja, perencanaan dan pengorganisasian, integritas, dll). Khusus untuk jabatan-jabatan struktural dilakukan juga penilaian perilaku kerja dari aspek kepemimpinan.

2. Penilaian Prestasi Kerja
Penilaian atas pencapaian kinerja Karyawan yang didasarkan pada kesepakatan kerja yang telah ditetapkan melalui metode penilaian Key Performance Indicator yang terdiri dari 4 perspektif penilaian, yaitu financial perspective, business perspective, customer perspective dan learning &amp; growth perspective.

## Performance Assessment [GRI 404-3]

The Company conducts performance assessments for all employees based on potential and performance reviews, which serve as the foundation for determining Individual Development Plans (IDPs). The results of these assessments are also used as a reference for career advancement and remuneration considerations. PT SMI employs two methods for performance assessments:

1. Work Behavior Assessment (360-Degree Feedback)
This method involves feedback from supervisors, peers, and subordinates. Work behavior assessment covers two key aspects, namely discipline and work attitude (including proactiveness, work results, planning and organization, integrity, etc.). Specifically for structural positions, the assessment also includes a leadership assessment.

2. Work Performance Assessment
The assessment of employee performance achievements is based on the work agreement that has been established through the Key Performance Indicator (KPI) assessment method, which consists of four assessment perspectives, i.e. financial perspective, business perspective, customer perspective, and learning &amp; growth perspective.
