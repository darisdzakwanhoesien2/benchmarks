## Menerima Perluasan Mandat sebagai Country Platform Manager ETM untuk Mempercepat Transisi Energi Bersih

Pemerintah menunjuk PT SMI menjadi Country Platform Manager pada penerapan Energi Transition Mechanism (ETM) atau Mekanisme Transisi Energi, yaitu program peningkatan pembangunan infrastruktur energi dan percepatan transisi energi menuju emisi nol bersih/Net Zero Emission (NZE) dengan prinsip adil (just) dan terjangkau (affordable) pada tahun 2060 atau lebih cepat. Indonesia memerlukan ETM karena ekonominya diproyeksikan akan terus tumbuh dan ditargetkan menjadi negara maju pada 2045. Untuk itu, Indonesia membutuhkan energi yang sangat besar tetapi tidak boleh berdampak negatif terhadap lingkungan.

The transformation into a DFI further solidifies PT SMI's long-standing commitment to accelerating national infrastructure development while strengthening its role in building sustainable infrastructure with continuously considering sustainability aspects. The energy transition is expected to enhance the efficiency of environmentally friendly energy use, generating positive impacts on social, economic, and environmental aspects. Ultimately, this transition aims to foster sustainable economic growth.

## Receiving an Expanded Mandate as Country Platform Manager for ETM to Accelerate Clean Energy Transition

The Government of Indonesia has appointed PT SMI as the Country Platform Manager for the implementation of the Energy Transition Mechanism (ETM)—a program aimed at enhancing energy infrastructure development and accelerating the transition to net-zero emissions (NZE) by 2060 or earlier through a just and affordable approach. Indonesia requires ETM because its economy is projected to continue growing, with a target of becoming a developed nation by 2045. To achieve this, Indonesia will need a significant amount of energy, but without causing negative environmental impacts.
