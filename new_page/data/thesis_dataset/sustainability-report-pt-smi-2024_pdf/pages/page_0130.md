PT Sarana Multi Infrastruktur (Persero) - Laporan Keberlanjutan 2024

PT SMI menerapkan prinsip-prinsip GCG yang terdiri dari 6 (enam) prinsip dasar, yaitu: Transparansi, Akuntabilitas, Tanggung Jawab, Kemandirian, Kewajaran dan Kesetaraan dengan implementasi sebagai berikut:

PT SMI adheres to six fundamental principles of Good Corporate Governance (GCG), which are: Transparency, Accountability, Responsibility, Independence, Fairness, and Equality, with the following implementation:

|  Transparansi
Transparency | Merupakan hak-hak para Pemegang Saham atas informasi yang benar dan tepat mengenai Perseroan, ikut dalam pengambilan keputusan mengenai perubahan-perubahan yang mendasar atas Perseroan, dan hak atas keuntungan Perseroan. Transparansi juga diartikan keterbukaan informasi baik dalam proses pengambilan keputusan maupun mengungkapkan informasi tentang Perseroan. It is the rights of the Shareholders to have valid and correct information about the Company, to participate in making decisions regarding fundamental changes to the Company, and the rights to the Company's profits. Transparency also means disclosure of information both in the decision making process and in disclosing information about the Company. | Perseroan mewujudkan komitmen transparansi melalui keterbukaan informasi dan laporan perusahaan bagi publik, serta dalam menetapkan prosedur kerja operasional. Masyarakat umum, pelaku usaha dan investor dapat memperoleh ataupun menyampaikan informasi, serta mengikuti perkembangan usaha Perseroan melalui kanal informasi yang tersedia serta mudah diakses. The Company achieves its commitment to transparency by opening information and Company reports to the public, as well as in establishing operational work procedures. The general public, shareholders, and investors can obtain or present information and follow the Company's business updates through available and easily accessible channels.  |
|  Akuntabilitas
Accountability | Merupakan kejelasan fungsi, pelaksanaan dan pertanggungjawaban organ Perseroan sehingga pengelolaan Perseroan terlaksana secara efektif. It is the clarity of functions, implementation and responsibilities of the Company's organs so that the management of the Company is carried out effectively. | Prinsip akuntabilitas melekat ke setiap pembagian tugas, fungsi, wewenang dan kewajiban baik secara individu maupun kelompok di seluruh lapisan organisasi Perseroan, agar aktivitas usaha dilakukan melalui kinerja yang berintegritas dan beretika profesional serta menjaga komitmen terhadap pencapaian sasaran atau target usaha Perseroan. The accountability principle is inherent to every distribution of tasks, functions, responsibilities, and obligations, both for individuals and groups at every organizational level of the Company, to drive the Company's activities through performances with integrity, professional ethics, and commitment in achieving the Company's business goals or targets.  |
|  Tanggung Jawab
Responsibility | Merupakan kesesuaian di dalam pengelolaan Perusahaan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan dan prinsip korporasi yang sehat. It is a compliance by the management of the Company with the laws and regulations and sound corporate principles. | Perseroan menerapkan prinsip tanggung jawab sebagai salah satu unsur pedoman kerja seluruh unsur organisasi. Setiap proses kerja, pengambilan keputusan maupun tindakan, harus sesuai prosedur, porsi dan tingkat pertanggungjawabannya dalam rangka melindungi kepentingan usaha secara jangka panjang. The Company implements the principle of responsibility as one of the work guidelines for all elements in the organization. The flow, portion, and responsibility level of all work processes, decision making, and actions are determined to protect long-term business interests.  |
|  Kemandirian
Independence | Merupakan keadaan di mana Perseroan dikelola secara profesional tanpa benturan kepentingan dan pengaruh/tekanan dari pihak manapun yang tidak sesuai dengan peraturan perundang-undangan dan prinsip-prinsip korporasi yang sehat. It is the condition in which the Company is managed professionally without conflicts of interest and influence/pressure from any party that does not align with the prevailing laws and regulations and sound corporate principles. | Perseroan berkomitmen kuat untuk menghindari pengaruh dan campur tangan oleh pihak manapun dalam menjalankan usaha. Pengelolaan perusahaan tidak dapat dipengaruhi oleh kepentingan atau tekanan pribadi maupun kelompok tertentu, dan bebas dari benturan kepentingan (conflict of interest) pihak manapun. The Company is strongly committed to avoid domination, influence, and interference by any party in conducting the business. The management of the Company shall not be influenced by interest or pressure from certain parties, and free from conflict of interest from any party.  |
