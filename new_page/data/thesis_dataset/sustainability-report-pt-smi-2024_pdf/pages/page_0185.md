## Kinerja Ekonomi PT SMI Tahun 2024 [OJK F.2, F.3] [GRI 201-1, 203-2]

Kinerja sektor infrastruktur di sepanjang tahun 2024 mengalami tekanan. Merujuk laman Bursa Efek Indonesia, kinerja IDX Infrastructures sudah turun 1,48% sejak awal tahun alias year to date (YTD). Sejak awal Oktober, evaluasi minor BEI terhadap konstitusi IDX Infrastructures juga mulai berlaku hingga 31 Desember 2024. Analis menyebutkan kinerja IDX Infrastructure secara YTD memang masih turun seiring dengan peralihan kepemimpinan dari Presiden Joko Widodo (Jokowi) menuju pemerintahan baru di bawah kepemimpinan Presiden terpilih Prabowo Subianto. Hal tersebut menyebabkan pasar cenderung menunda untuk melakukan investasi di sektor infrastruktur, terutama konstruksi.

Walaupun PT SMI tidak berkait langsung dengan IDX Infrastructures, namun kondisi tersebut memberikan cerminan tentang kinerja sektor infrastruktur di tanah air, termasuk kinerja debitur Perseroan. Lebih lanjut, kondisi tersebut berpotensi berdampak terhadap kinerja PT SMI sebagai Special Mission Vehicle (SMV) di bawah Kementerian Keuangan yang bergerak di bidang pembiayaan pembangunan infrastruktur.

## PT SMI's Economic Performance in 2024 [OJK F.2, F.3] [GRI 201-1, 203-2]

The infrastructure sector faced pressure throughout 2024. According to the Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX), the IDX Infrastructure Index declined by 1.48% year-to-date (YTD). Additionally, since early October 2024, the IDX Infrastructure Index underwent a minor evaluation by IDX, which remains in effect until December 31, 2024. Analysts attribute the YTD decline in infrastructure performance to the leadership transition from President Joko Widodo (Jokowi) to the new administration under President-elect Prabowo Subianto. This transition has led investors to delay infrastructure investments, particularly in the construction sector.

Although PT SMI is not directly linked to the IDX Infrastructures, this situation reflects the overall performance of the infrastructure sector in Indonesia, including the financial health of PT SMI's debtors. Moreover, these market conditions have the potential to impact PT SMI's performance as a Special Mission Vehicle (SMV) under the Ministry of Finance, which focuses on infrastructure development financing.
