## 2. ESS Culture dalam laman PT SMI

Pada pertengahan tahun 2021, PT SMI melakukan pembaharuan website www.ptsmi.co.id, salah satunya adalah pembaharuan pada laman Environmental and Social Safeguard dengan menambahkan konten ESS Culture yang berisi materi-materi mengenai rencana, pencapaian dan informasi relevan lainnya tentang lingkungan, sosial dan K3 yang dapat diakses tidak hanya oleh SMIers namun juga masyarakat umum. Materi ESS Culture pada tahun 2024 di antaranya:

a. Deklarasi Karbon Netral pada ulang tahun PT SMI ke 15
b. Sharing session: Elemen ESS 4 PT SMI – Keselamatan, Kesehatan dan Keamanan
c. Workshop "Perlindungan Biodiversitas dalam Pelaksanaan Proyek Pembangunan Infrastruktur Berkelanjutan di Indonesia"
d. PT Tamaris Hydro melakukan benchmarking penerapan prinsip-prinsip Environmental, Social, Governance (ESG) dengan PT SMI.

Informasi lebih lengkap dapat di akses di laman: https://ptsmi.co.id/ess-cultures.

## Dukungan Terhadap Infrastruktur Rendah Karbon [GRI 2-23, 2-25, 3-3] [GRI FS1, FS2, FS3]

Pembiayaan berkelanjutan yaitu fasilitas untuk mendanai pembangunan infrastruktur yang ramah lingkungan, merupakan aspek penting bagi PT SMI. Dalam implementasinya, pembiayaan ditujukan untuk pembangunan proyek-proyek yang secara aktif berkontribusi dalam mengantisipasi perubahan iklim dan memerangi masalah lingkungan melalui pengelolaan masalah lingkungan, peningkatan kualitas lingkungan, pengurangan ancaman pemanasan global, dan mendukung pembangunan rendah karbon.

## 2. ESS Culture on PT SMI's Website

In mid-2021, PT SMI updated its official website, www.ptsmi.co.id, including an enhancement to the Environmental and Social Safeguards (ESS) page. As part of this update, ESS Culture was introduced, featuring materials on plans, achievements, and other relevant information related to environmental, social, and occupational health and safety (OHS) aspects. This content is accessible not only to SMI employees (SMIers) but also to the general public. In 2024, the ESS Culture materials included the following:

a. Carbon Neutral Declaration in celebration of PT SMI's 15th anniversary
b. Sharing Session on ESS Element 4 – Safety, Health, and Security
c. Workshop on "Biodiversity Protection in Implementing Sustainable Infrastructure Projects in Indonesia"
d. Benchmarking by PT Tamaris Hydro on the implementation of Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles with PT SMI

For more details, visit: https://ptsmi.co.id/ess-cultures.

## Support for Low-Carbon Infrastructure [GRI 2-23, 2-25, 3-3] [GRI FS1, FS2, FS3]

Sustainable financing, which provides funding for environmentally friendly infrastructure development, is an important aspect for PT SMI. In its implementation, financing is directed towards projects that actively contribute to climate change mitigation and address environmental challenges through environmental management, environmental quality improvement, reduction of global warming threats, and support for low-carbon development.
