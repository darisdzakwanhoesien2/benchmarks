## Perubahan Terkait Laporan

Topik material laporan keberlanjutan tahun 2024 tidak mengalami perubahan dibanding tahun sebelumnya. Penetapan hal itu dilakukan dalam Focus Group Discussion (FGD) yang diselenggarakan secara online pada Jumat, 18 Oktober 2024. FGD ini melibatkan pemangku kepentingan internal Perseroan, yaitu karyawan PT SMI. Sebelum diskusi dilakukan, peserta telah mendapatkan bahan diskusi berupa daftar topik material laporan keberlanjutan tahun 2023 sebagai bahan yang hendak di-review serta topik material lengkap GRI Standards 2021 sebagai bahan pembanding dan rujukan.

Dalam diskusi, baik topik material laporan keberlanjutan tahun 2023 maupun keseluruhan topik material GRI Standards dibahas secara mendalam sehingga seluruh peserta diskusi memiliki perspektif yang sama tentang topik material sesuai panduan GRI Standards. Melalui peninjauan tersebut, selain mengidentifikasi dan menilai dampak secara berkesinambungan, sekaligus Perseroan telah memastikan bahwa topik material dalam laporan ini mewakili dampak paling signifikan selama tahun pelaporan. Selanjutnya, berdasarkan peninjauan secara internal dan eksternal, kedua pemangku kepentingan menyepakati untuk tidak melakukan perubahan topik material, terlebih lagi penetapan topik tahun 2023 telah melalui proses yang komprehensif, termasuk dalam pelibatan pemangku kepentingan. ## Changes Related to the Report

There have been no changes related to material topics in the 2024 Sustainability Report compared to the previous year. The determination of these topics was conducted through a Focus Group Discussion (FGD), held online on Friday, October 18, 2024. This FGD involved internal stakeholders of the Company, which is PT SMI employees. Prior to the discussion, participants were provided with preparatory materials, including the list of material topics from the 2023 Sustainability Report, which was subject to review, and the complete list of material topics from the GRI Standards 2021 as a comparison and reference.

During the discussion, both the 2023 material topics and the entire set of material topics from the GRI Standards were examined in depth to ensure that all participants had a shared understanding of the material topics in alignment with the GRI Standards guidelines. Through this review, the Company not only identified and continuously assessed its impacts but also ensured that the material topics presented in this report represent the most significant impacts during the reporting year. Following both internal and external reviews, the stakeholders agreed not to make any changes to the material topics. This decision was further reinforced by the fact that the determination of the 2023 topics had undergone a comprehensive process, including extensive stakeholder engagement. [GRI 3-2]
