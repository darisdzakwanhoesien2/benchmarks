### 1. Kinerja Aspek Ekonomi

RKAP yang disusun secara matang dan realistis berdasarkan prinsip kehati-hatian dan penerapan manajemen risiko di dalamnya antara lain berisi tentang rencana Perseroan untuk meningkatkan kinerja usaha serta strategi untuk merealisasikan rencana tersebut sesuai target dan waktu yang ditetapkan. Selain faktor eksternal dan internal yang dapat memengaruhi kelangsungan usaha, penyusunan rencana kerja juga memperhatikan sejumlah hal seperti prinsip kehati-hatian dan penerapan manajemen risiko sesuai dengan prinsip-prinsip tata kelola perusahaan yang baik.

## Implementation of Sustainable Finance in 2024

The management and employees of PT SMI have worked optimally throughout 2024, striving to deliver the best contributions and achieve outstanding performance across economic, social, and environmental aspects. The strong synergy between PT SMI's management and employees has resulted in significant achievements, as outlined below:

### 1. Economic Performance

PT SMI's economic performance achievements refer to the targets and accomplishments set in the Company's Work Plan and Budget, which is issued annually, and carefully and realistically designed, incorporating the principle of prudence and the application of risk management. It includes the Company's plans to improve business performance and strategies to achieve these plans within the set targets and timeframe. In addition to external and internal factors that may affect business continuity, the work plan also considers several aspects such as the principle of prudence and the application of risk management in accordance with the principles of good corporate governance.

Given the prevailing economic conditions, including Indonesia's stable economic growth in 2024 and the expansion of all 17 business sectors that support this growth, PT SMI recorded a positive economic performance. As of December 31, 2024, the Company reported: Financing and Investment of IDR 96,328 billion, achieving 100.31% of the Company's 2024 Work Plan and Budget target, reflecting a 5.5% increase, or IDR 5,966 billion from the previous year. Operating Revenue of IDR 7,820 billion, achieving 107.38% of the Company's 2024 Work Plan and Budget target, reflecting a 5.14% increase or IDR382 billion from the previous year. Net Income of IDR2,230 billion, achieving 110.86% of the Company's 2024 Work Plan and Budget target, reflecting a 7.31% increase from the previous year or equal to IDR152 billion. Total Assets recorded at IDR120,070 billion, achieving 101.03% of the Company's 2024 Work Plan and Budget target, reflecting a 3.73% increase or IDR4,313 billion from the previous year. Total Equity of IDR43,811 billion, achieving 100.59% of the Company's 2024 Work Plan and Budget target, reflecting a 3.67% increase or equal to IDR1,550 billion from the previous year.
