## Melibatkan Insan Perseroan untuk Ikut Berkontribusi melalui SMI Berbagi

Program SMI Berbagi merupakan kegiatan rutin yang dilaksanakan PT SMI setiap tahun. Program ini merupakan program TJSL yang turut melibatkan Insan Perseroan untuk memberikan manfaat positif kepada masyarakat. Selama tahun 2024, 4 (empat) kegiatan SMI Berbagi adalah sebagai berikut:

1. SMI Berbagi Ramadhan yang dilaksanakan di Yayasan Daarul Rahman, Yayasan Cahaya Alam, dan Yayasan Panji Nusantara di wilayah Jabodetabek dengan memberikan bantuan kepada anak yatim piatu.
2. Kegiatan SMI Berbagi Pendidikan yang diadakan di DOES University, Yogyakarta, dengan melibatkan SMIers dalam kegiatan belajar animasi dan pemberian bantuan kepada anak yatim piatu dari Panti Asuhan Al Wahaab.
3. Pelaksanaan SMI Berbagi Penghijauan berkolaborasi dengan Infrariders Brotherhood sebagai salah satu komunitas insan PT SMI, dan Dinas Kehutanan Provinsi Jawa Barat, melakukan pemberian bantuan peralatan

These six principles serve as the foundation for the Company in planning various CSR activities that align with the objectives of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). The six principles are as follows:

1. Targeted
The Company's CSR activities are implemented with a clear direction to achieve corporate objectives.

2. Measurable Impact
The Company's CSR activities contribute and provide tangible benefits, resulting in positive change or added value for both stakeholders and the Company.

3. Accountability
The implementation of CSR activities must be accountable, ensuring transparency and integrity while preventing misuse and irregularities.

4. Transparency
The Company ensures openness in actions and policies related to CSR implementation, fostering trust among stakeholders.

5. Fairness
The principle of fairness and equality is upheld to fulfill the rights of both stakeholders and the Company in a balanced manner.

6. Integrated
CSR activities are carried out based on risk analysis and business processes that are closely linked to stakeholders.

The implementation of CSR/PKBL programs is carried out continuously, in accordance with the approved work program plans, and refers to ISO 26000 on Social Responsibility Guidelines.

## Engaging the Company's Personnel to Contribute through SMI Berbagi

SMI Berbagi program is an annual activity carried out by PT SMI. This program is part of the CSR initiative, actively involving the Company's personnel in providing positive benefits to the community. During 2024, the 4 (four) activities under the SMI Berbagi program were as follows:

1. SMI Berbagi Ramadhan: Conducted at Daarul Rahman Foundation, Cahaya Alam Foundation, and Panji Nusantara Foundation in the Greater Jakarta area (Jabodetabek), this activity provided assistance to orphans.
2. SMI Berbagi Pendidikan: Held at DOES University, Yogyakarta, this program involved PT SMI personnel (SMIers) in animation learning activities and providing assistance to orphans from Panti Asuhan Al Wahaab.
