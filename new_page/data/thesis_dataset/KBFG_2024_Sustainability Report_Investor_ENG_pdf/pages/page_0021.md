## Physical Risk Scenario Analysis

KB Financial Group used RCP climate change scenario analysis to forecast the size of economic damage in case of a future disaster and identified the physical risk that climate change has on the real estate (commercial and residential use) asset portfolio.

Based on the IPCC Assessment Report, we chose leading disaster types as flooding (heavy rain, river flooding), wildfires, and cyclones in consideration of Korea's climate characteristics and applied a scenario by disaster type. We used the rainfall and disaster damage statistics provided by the Korea Meteorological Administration and Ministry of the Interior and Safety to estimate the damage amount by region, and analyzed the impact that the disaster-caused drop in collateral value has on LGD to quantify the economic damage size, leading to physical risk measurement.
