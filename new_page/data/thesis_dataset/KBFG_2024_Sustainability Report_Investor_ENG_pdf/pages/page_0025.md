Results of an analysis by region indicate that the collateral value decline rate is the highest for Gangwon Province, which showed a relatively high frequency of natural disasters, but actual RWA impact is the highest for Gyeonggi Province, which has a large overall exposure size. In contrast, Seoul has an extremely low collateral value drop rate, leading to an analysis that the RWA increase is limited.
