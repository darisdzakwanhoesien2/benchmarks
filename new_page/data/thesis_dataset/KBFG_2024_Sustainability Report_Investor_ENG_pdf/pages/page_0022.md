## Results of Climate Change Scenario Analysis

KB Financial Group carried out a climate change impact analysis for the next 30 years up to 2050 based on an asset portfolio that calculates credit risk RWA as of the end of 2023. Results of the climate change scenario analysis indicate that transition risk has the most impact under the NGFS net zero scenario, while physical risk has the most impact in the IPCC RCP8.5 scenario.

Results of analyzing short-, mid-, and long-term impact per scenario indicate that there is the highest impact over the long term in all scenarios and transition risk shows a relatively higher overall impact regarding climate risk. This signifies that the negative impact of carbon costs that should be borne by high-carbon industries owing to strengthened carbon policies is greater than climate change's direct impact on borrowers' physical asset value.
