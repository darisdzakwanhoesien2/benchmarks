## Setting Risk Management Monitoring Metrics and Targets

KB Financial Group sets targets for risk management metrics, including the CET1 ratio, BIS ratio, and risk appetite, and periodically monitors them for systematic risk management. As of 2024, we satisfy all target ratios of risk management metrics.
