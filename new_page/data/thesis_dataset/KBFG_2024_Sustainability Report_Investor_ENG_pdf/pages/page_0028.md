## Financial Impact of Transition Risk

The impact of transition risks on the corporate finance portfolio was assessed by estimating the increase rate of borrowers' PD under various scenarios and the resulting changes in loan loss provisions. After analyzing the anticipated financial impact of transition risks on loan loss provisions over the short, medium, and long term, it was determined that the transition to carbon neutrality could have a significant financial impact on the financial statements over the medium to long term, depending on the pace of transition and policy directions.
