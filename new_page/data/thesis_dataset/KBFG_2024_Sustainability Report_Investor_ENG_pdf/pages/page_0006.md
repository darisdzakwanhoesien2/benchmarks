# STRATEGY

## Enterprise Risk Management Strategy

KB Financial Group establishes short- and mid- to long-term management strategies for major risks, and systematically implements response and preventive measures.

Major subsidiaries establish a risk response system per material issue, and the Group establishes a Group risk management system that prevents risk transfer among subsidiaries through integrated risk management.
