# Financed Emissions (Scope 3)

KB Financial Group's financed emissions as of the end of 2023 stood at about 55.91 million tons, an increase of around 6.54 million tons from the end of 2022. This is mainly attributable to a significant expansion of the measurement target scope. After establishing the initial SBTi reduction targets in 2019, we expanded asset groups that are subject to measurement to include residential real estate, automobile loan, and government bond. At the end of 2023, we started including foreign government bonds in the target of measurement, leading to a year-on-year increase in financed emissions.
