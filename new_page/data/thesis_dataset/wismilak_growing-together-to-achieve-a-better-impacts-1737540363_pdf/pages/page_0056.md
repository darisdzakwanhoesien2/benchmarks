Talent Management adalah suatu proses manajemen SDM yang berfungsi untuk:

Talent Management is an HR management process that serves to:

- Mengembangkan dan memperkuat karyawan baru pada proses pertama kali masuk Perseroan.

Develop and reinforce new employees during their initial entry into the Company.

- Memelihara dan mengembangkan karyawan yang sudah ada di Perseroan.

Maintain and develop existing employees within the Company.

Talent Management System menggabungkan Performance/Kinerja dan Potensial.

The Talent Management System combines Performance and Potential.

- Kinerja diperoleh melalui hasil penilai kinerja.

Performance is obtained through performance appraisal results.

- Potensial/potensi diperoleh melalui asesmen multidimensional aspek (result agility, self awareness, change agility, mental agility, people agility).

Potential is evaluated through a multidimensional assessment of aspects such as result agility, self-awareness, change agility, mental agility, and people agility.

# Aplikasi Human Resources Information System (HRIS)

Human Resources Information System (HRIS) Application

Sebagai upaya untuk melakukan efektivitas dan efisiensi dalam pengelolaan SDM maka Perseroan memiliki aplikasi Human Resources Information System (HRIS). Melalui aplikasi HRIS dapat membantu Perseroan untuk memelihara, mengelola, dan memproses informasi rinci karyawan dan aktivitas SDM, serta beberapa aplikasi pendukung yang memudahkan sistem pengelolaan SDM.

To enhance effectiveness and efficiency in human resources management, the Company has implemented a Human Resources Information System (HRIS). The HRIS application assists the Company in maintaining, managing, and processing detailed information about employees and HR activities. Additionally, it integrates several supporting applications that streamline the HR management system.

# Remunerasi dan Kesejahteraan Karyawan [F.20][2-20][401-2][201-3]

# Employee Remuneration and Welfare

Kebijakan mengenai remunerasi, kompensasi, tunjangan, dan apresiasi karyawan diatur dalam Kebijakan Human Resources - Compensation &amp; Benefit (HR-C&amp;B). Perseroan senantiasa memastikan remunerasi yang diterima oleh setiap karyawan setara dan adil tanpa membedakan gender serta besarannya mengacu pada hasil evaluasi kinerja karyawan serta Upah Minimum Regional (UMR).

Perseroan juga telah menyertakan karyawan ke dalam program Jaminan Hari Tua (JHT) dan Jaminan Pensiun (JP) yang dikelola oleh Badan Penyelenggara Jaminan Sosial (BPJS) Ketenagakerjaan. Iuran program JHT dan JP dibayarkan oleh Perseroan dan juga karyawan dengan besaran persentase yang telah diatur dalam peraturan yang berlaku. Keterbukaan informasi mengenai persentase iuran dapat diakses dalam aplikasi milik BPJS Ketenagakerjaan yaitu, Jamsostek Mobile (JMO). [201-3]

Policies regarding employee remuneration, compensation, allowances, and appreciation are outlined in the Human Resources Policy - Compensation &amp; Benefit (HR-C&amp;B). The Company consistently ensures that the remuneration received by each employee is equitable and fair, without discriminating gender, and the amounts are based on the employee performance evaluation and the Regional Minimum Wage (UMR).

The Company has also enrolled employees with more than one year of service in the Old Age Security (JHT) and Pension Security (JP) programs managed by BPJS Ketenagakerjaan. Contributions to the JHT and JP programs are paid by the Company and employees at specified percentage rates as stipulated in the applicable regulations. Information regarding the contribution percentages is transparent and accessible through the BPJS Ketenagakerjaan mobile application, Jamsostek Mobile (JMO). [201-3]
