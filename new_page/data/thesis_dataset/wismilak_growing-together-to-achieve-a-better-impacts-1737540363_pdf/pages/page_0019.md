# Dukungan Wismilak Terhadap Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan (TPB) [2-22][2-23][2-24]

Support for the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

Wismilak mendukung TPB melalui enam tujuan prioritas melalui pemetaan dengan mempertimbangkan keseimbangan kinerja aspek lingkungan, sosial, serta tata kelola (LST).

Wismilak supports the SDGs through six priority objectives by mapping considerations for balancing performance in environmental, social, and governance (ESG) aspects.

![img-55.jpeg](img-55.jpeg)

## Inisiatif | Initiative

1. Penerapan manajemen Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja (K3)
Implementing the principles and management of Occupational Health and Safety (OHS)

2. Program kesehatan seluruh karyawan
Healthcare programs for all employees

3. Pemberian tunjangan, fasilitas dan manfaat untuk mendukung kinerja karyawan
Provision of allowances, facilities, and benefits to support employee performance

## Capstan 2023 | 2023 Outcome

1. Zero fatality akibat kecelakaan kerja
Zero fatalities due to work accidents

2. Wellness program telah berjalan sebanyak 2 program dengan tema Ergonomi saat bekerja dan Menjaga Imunitas &amp; Produktifitas Puasa
The wellness program has conducted 2 programs with the themes of Ergonomics at Work and Maintaining Immunity &amp; Productivity During Fasting
- Program promotif dan preventif kesehatan kerja telah diselenggarakan 3 kali di area Bojonegoro dan 6 kali di area Buntaran Surabaya
Promotional and preventive occupational health programs have been held 3 times in the Bojonegoro area and 6 times in the Buntaran Surabaya area

3. Penerbitan kebijakan dan prosedur untuk kesejahteraan karyawan
Issuance of policies and procedures for employee welfare

## Target 2024 | 2024 Target

1. Nihil kecelakaan kerja
Achieve zero work accidents

2. Pelaksanaan wellness program sebanyak 3 program selama 2024
Implementation of 3 wellness programs in 2024
- Target 4 edukasi promotif preventif oleh dokter umum di Bojonegoro dan 2 edukasi promotif preventif dokter spesialis
Targeting 4 promotive and preventive education by general practitioners in Bojonegoro and 2 promotive and preventive education by specialists

3. Kebijakan dan Prosedur Compensation Benefit yang kompetitif
Competitive Compensation Benefit Policies and Procedures
