# Peningkatan Kompetensi Terkait Keberlanjutan [E.2] [F.1][2-17]

Sustainability-Related Competency Development

Wismilak memberikan kesempatan yang setara kepada seluruh insan untuk meningkatkan kompetensi dan pengetahuan terkait keberlanjutan melalui sosialisasi secara online di sosial media dan mengadakan event internal. Hal ini diberikan secara adil tanpa membedakan jenjang karier, agama, ras, dan gender dalam mendukung penerapan praktik keberlanjutan Perseroan.

Wismilak provides equal opportunities for all individuals to enhance their competencies and knowledge in sustainability through online socialization on social media and holding internal events. This is offered fairly, without discrimination based on career level, religion, race, or gender, supporting the implementation of the Company's sustainability practices.
