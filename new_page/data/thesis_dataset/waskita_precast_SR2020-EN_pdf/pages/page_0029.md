# COMPANY PROFILE

![img-27.jpeg](img-27.jpeg)

PT Waskita Beton Precast Tbk (hereinafter referred to as "the Company") is a company engaging in readymix and precast concrete manufacturing industry. Majority of the Company's shares is held by PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk (hereinafter referred to as "WASKITA"), one of state-owned enterprise (SOE) construction companies.
