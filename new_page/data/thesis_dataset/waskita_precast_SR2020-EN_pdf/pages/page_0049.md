# Employee Composition (102-8)

As of December 31, 2020, the Company had 1,270 employees, a decrease by 4% from that of 2019 at 1,320 employees. The decrease in the number of the Company's employees was an efficiency measure carried out by the Company since before 2020.

Based on their status, WSBP employees are divided into three categories, namely permanent, temporary, and outsourced employees; while based on their working area, the employees are divided into two major groups, namely those who work in the Head Office (PK) and Regional Offices (KP).
