## Trade payables

The Company's trade payables by end of 2021 were Rp1,285.3 billion or decreased by 14%. The decrease in trade payable is mostly due to a decrease in purchase of inventories for the distribution business segment.

## Accrued expenses

As of 31 December 2021, the Company's accrued expenses were Rp186.6 billion, increasing by 62% compared to previous year. The increase was mainly for promotion and marketing program.

## Non-current liabilities

In 2021, the Company's non-current liabilities were Rp159.6 billion or decreased by 10% which was due to decreases in lease liability and liability for employee benefits.

## Utang usaha

Utang usaha Perseroan pada tahun akhir tahun 2021 adalah sebesar Rp1.285,3 milyar atau turun sebesar 14%. Turunnya utang usaha sebagian besar disebabkan oleh penurunan nilai pembelian persediaan untuk segmen bisnis distribusi.

## Beban akrual

Pada tanggal 31 Desember 2021, Perseroan membukukan beban akrual sebesar Rp186,6 milyar, meningkat sebesar 62% dibandingkan tahun lalu. Peningkatan ini terutama untuk program promosi dan marketing.

## Liabilitas jangka panjang

Pada tahun 2021, liabilitas jangka panjang Perseroan adalah sebesar Rp159,6 milyar atau mengalami penurunan sebesar 10%, disebabkan oleh penurunan liabilitas sewa dan liabilitas imbalan kerja karyawan.
