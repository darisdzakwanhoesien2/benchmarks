# Winner of Indonesia Law Awards 2021

SOHO Global Health won The Indonesia Law Awards 2021 for category "Healthcare and Pharmaceutical In-House Team of the Year" from Asian Legal Business (ALB) and Thomson Reuters.

The Indonesia Law Awards were presented to the winners of in-house Legal Team, Law Firms and Top Deals &amp; Deal Makers in Indonesia.

The evaluation of entries for "In-House Team" categories are based on the multiple combined factors such as complexity, innovation/improvement, impact, breadth/significance, and third party awards/recognition.

On top of becoming the winner in 2015, 2019 and 2021, we are also being named a finalist for three consecutive years in 2016-18.

# Pemenang Indonesia Law Awards 2021

SOHO Global Health memenangkan The Indonesia Law Awards 2021 untuk kategori "Healthcare and Pharmaceutical In-House Team of the Year" dari Asian Legal Business (ALB) dan Thomson Reuters.

Indonesia Law Awards diberikan kepada para pemenang In-house Legal Team, Law Firm dan Top Deals &amp; Deal Makers di Indonesia.

Evaluasi untuk kategori "In-House Team" didasarkan pada beberapa faktor gabungan seperti kompleksitas, inovasi/ perbaikan, dampak, keluasan/signifikansi, dan penghargaan/ pengakuan pihak ketiga.

Selain menjadi pemenang pada 2015, 2019, dan 2021, kami juga dinobatkan sebagai finalis selama tiga tahun berturut-turut pada 2016-18.
