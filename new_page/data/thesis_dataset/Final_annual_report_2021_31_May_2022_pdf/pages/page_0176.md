|  4.PT. Tozy Sentosa (“Centro”)  |   |
|  Case Status/Status Perkara | PPG is a Concurrent Creditor of Centro. PPG has receivables that are due and payable to Centro for the pharmaceutical ad OTC products supplied by PPG and Centro is unable to pay the receivables. The bankruptcy application of Centro was applied by another creditor of Centro. PPG adalah Kreditur Konkuren Centro. PPG memiliki piutang yang telah jatuh tempo dan terutang kepada Centro atas produk farmasi dan OTC yang dipasok oleh PPG dan Centro tidak mampu membayar piutang tersebut. Permohonan palil Centro diajukan oleh kreditur Centro yang lain.  |
|  Risks Faced by the Company and Their Impacts on the Company/Risiko yang Dihadapi dan Pengaruhnya terhadap Perseroan | PPG faces the risk of inability to recover its receivables fully, which amounts to IDR 152,250,504. PPG berisiko tidak akan memperoleh pengembalian tagihan secara penuh yang jumlahnya sebesar Rp. 152.250.504.  |
|  Follow-up/Tindak Lanjut | Centro has been declared Bankrupt based on the decision of the Commercial Court at Central Jakarta District Court No. 106/Pdt.Sus.PKPU/2021/PN Niaga, dated May 17, 2021. Currently PPG is waiting for the settlement process from the liquidation team to get payment from PPG's receivables (if any). Centro telah dinyatakan palil berdasarkan putusan Pengadilan Niaga pada Pengadilan Negeri Jakarta Pusat No. 106/Pdt.Sus.PKPU/2021/PN Niaga, tanggal 17 Mei 2021. Saat ini PPG sedang menunggu proses penyelesaian dari tim likuidasi untuk mendapatkan pembayaran dari piutang PPG (jika ada).  |
