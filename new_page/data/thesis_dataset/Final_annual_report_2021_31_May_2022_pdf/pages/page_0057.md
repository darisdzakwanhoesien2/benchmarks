# Head of Internal Audit Unit Profile

Profil Kepala Unit Audit Internal

The Internal Audit Unit is led by a Head of the Internal Audit Unit who is appointed by the Board of Directors through a Decree of the Board of Directors and is responsible to the President Director and acts independently in carrying out his duties and responsibilities.

The Internal Audit Unit is formed based on the Decree of Board of Directors Number 05/SK-Dir/SGH/VI/2020 concerning the Establishment of the Company's Internal Audit Unit dated June 19, 2020.

Unit Audit Internal dipimpin oleh seorang Kepala Unit Audit Internal yang ditunjuk oleh Direksi melalui suatu Surat Keputusan Direksi dan bertanggung jawab kepada Presiden Direktur serta bertindak secara independen dalam melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya.

Unit Audit Internal dibentuk berdasarkan Keputusan Direksi Perseroan Nomor 05/SK-Dir/SGH/VI/2020 tentang Pembentukan Unit Audit Internal Perseroan tertanggal 19 Juni 2020.
