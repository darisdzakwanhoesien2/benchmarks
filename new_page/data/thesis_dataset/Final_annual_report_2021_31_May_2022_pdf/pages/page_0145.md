## Board of Directors Charter

In managing the Company, the Board of Directors follows the guideline from Board of Directors Charter which contains their duties, responsibilities, and authorities.

## Assessment of the performance of the Board of Directors

Referring to the Board of Directors Charter, the evaluation of the performance of the members of the Board of Directors is carried out by the Board Of Commissioners and shareholders of the Company. The Company also has a Policy on Performance Assessment of the Board of Directors, in which this policy is prepared as a guideline that can be used to assess the effectiveness of the implementation of the duties and responsibilities of the Board of Directors and also as a form of accountability for the performance assessment of the Board of Directors of the Company.

## Piagam Direksi

Dalam mengelola Perseroan, Direksi berpedoman pada Piagam Direksi yang memuat tugas, tanggung jawab, dan wewenang Direksi.

## Penilaian terhadap kinerja Direksi

Mengacu pada Piagam Direksi (Board of Directors Charter), penilaian atas kinerja anggota Direksi dilakukan oleh Dewan Komisaris dan pemegang saham Perseroan. Perseroan juga telah memiliki Kebijakan Penilaian atas Kinerja Direksi, yang mana kebijakan ini disusun sebagai pedoman yang dapat digunakan untuk menilai efektivitas pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab Direksi dan juga sebagai bentuk akuntabilitas atas penilaian kinerja Direksi Perseroan.
