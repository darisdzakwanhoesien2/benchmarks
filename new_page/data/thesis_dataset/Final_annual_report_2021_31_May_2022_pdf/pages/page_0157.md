# Qualification or Certification as an Internal Audit Professional

The Head of the Internal Audit Unit is a Certified Public Accountant. His certification was obtained from and issued by the Board of Accountancy in the Philippines. He is also a member of Institute of Internal Auditors and Association of Certified Fraud Examiners.

# Kualifikasi atau Sertifikasi sebagai Profesi Audit Internal

Kepala Unit Audit Internal, telah memiliki sertifikasi sebagai Akuntan Publik Bersertifikat (Certified Public Accountant) yang diperoleh dari dan diterbitkan oleh Board of Accountancy di Filipina. Beliau juga merupakan anggota dari Institute of Internal Auditors dan Association of Certified Fraud Examiners.
