# Significant Changes

Throughout year 2021, there were several significant changes in the Company's shares ownership, in which all changes in the Company's share ownership have been reported by the Company and related shareholders, to the Financial Services Authority ("OJK") in accordance with the provisions of OJK and Indonesia Stock Exchange regulations. The changes in the Company's share ownership are as follows:

- Sale of all shares owned by Eng Liang Tan, as the President Commissioner of the Company and the party who own shares of 5% or more of the paid-up shares in the Company, namely 487,080,805 shares on the transaction date of January 14, 2021, with the aim of internal restructuring of his share ownership. On the same date, namely January 14, 2021, Tiberias 96 Pty Ltd has reported to the OJK that Tiberias 96 Pty Ltd has owned 481,237,031 shares of the Company. The sale of shares and these changes in ownership of the Company's shares did not result in a change of control over the Company because Tiberias 96 Pty Ltd is a company wholly owned (100%) by Eng Lian Tan.

- Sale of all shares of the Company which are directly owned by the Board of Directors of the Company, namely:

(i) Rogelio Paulino Jr. Castillo La O, as the President Director of the Company, has sold all of his shares amounting to 12,542,100 shares on the transaction date of May 19, 2021;

(ii) Piero Brambati, as the Director of the Company, has sold all of his shares amounting to 9,964,000 shares on the transaction date of May 19, 2021;

(iii) Yuliana, as Director of the Company, has sold all of her shares amounting to 6,327,000 shares on the transaction date of May 19, 2021; and

(iv) Henryk Klakurka as Director of the Company, has sold all of his shares amounting to 4,459,400 shares on the transaction date of May 19, 2021.

- Purchase of the Company's shares by Medisia Investment Holdings Pte. Ltd, as the shareholder who owns more than 5% of the Company's shares, a total of 51,651,300 of the Company's shares on the transaction date of May 19, 2021.

# Perubahan yang Bersifat Signifikan

Sepanjang tahun 2021, terdapat beberapa kali perubahan kepemilikan saham Perseroan yang bersifat signifikan, yang mana seluruh perubahan kepemilikan saham Perseroan tersebut telah dilaporkan oleh Perseroan dan pemegang saham terkait, kepada Otoritas Jasa Keuangan ("OJK") sesuai dengan ketentuan peraturan OJK dan Bursa Efek Indonesia. Perubahan kepemilikan saham Perseroan tersebut adalah:

- Penjualan seluruh saham milik Eng Liang Tan, selaku Presiden Komisaris Perseroan dan pihak yang memiliki saham di atas 5% atau lebih saham yang disetor pada Perseroan, yakni sebanyak 487.080.805 lembar saham pada tanggal transaksi 14 Januari 2021, dengan tujuan restrukturisasi internal kepemilikan sahamnya. Pada tanggal yang sama, yakni 14 Januari 2021, Tiberias 96 Pty Ltd telah melakukan pelaporan kepada OJK bahwa Tiberias 96 Pty Ltd telah memiliki saham Perseroan sebanyak 481.237.031 lembar saham. Penjualan saham dan perubahan kepemilikan atas saham Perseroan ini tidak mengakibatkan perubahan pengendali terhadap Perseroan karena Tiberias 96 Pty Ltd merupakan perusahaan yang dimiliki sepenuhnya (100%) oleh Eng Lian Tan.

- Penjualan seluruh saham Perseroan yang dimiliki langsung oleh Direksi Perseroan, yakni:

(i) Rogelio Paulino Jr. Castillo La O, selaku Presiden Direktur Perseroan, telah menjual seluruh saham yang dimilikinya, yakni sebanyak 12.542.100 lembar saham Perseroan pada tanggal transaksi 19 Mei 2021;

(ii) Piero Brambati, selaku Direktur Perseroan, telah menjual seluruh saham yang dimilikinya, yakni sebanyak 9.964.000 lembar saham Perseroan pada tanggal transaksi 19 Mei 2021;

(iii) Yuliana, selaku Direktur Perseroan, telah menjual seluruh saham yang dimilikinya, yakni sebanyak 6.327.000 lembar saham pada tanggal transaksi 19 Mei 2021; dan

(iv) Henryk Klakurka, selaku Direktur Perseroan telah menjual seluruh saham yang dimilikinya, yakni sebanyak 4.459.400 lembar saham Perseroan pada tanggal transaksi 19 Mei 2021.

- Pembelian saham Perseroan oleh Medisia Investment Holdings Pte. Ltd, selaku pemegang saham yang memiliki saham Perseroan di atas 5%, sejumlah 51.651.300 lembar saham Perseroan pada tanggal transaksi 19 Mei 2021.
