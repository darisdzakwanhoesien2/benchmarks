## Claims for income tax refund

The Company's claim for income tax refund as of 31 December 2021 were Rp70.4 billion and decreased by 52% compared to previous year's Rp146.5 billion. The decrease was mostly due to collection of subsidiaries' claim for tax refund for fiscal year 2013.

## Fixed assets, net

The Company's fixed assets as of 31 December 2021 were Rp386 billion, decreasing by 2% compared to previous year's Rp395.5 billion. The decrease was due to normal annual depreciation of the fixed assets.

## Liabilities

The Company booked total liabilities at year end 2021 of Rp1.8 trillion, of which 91% were current liabilities and 9% were non-current liabilities. The total liabilities decreased by Rp160.5 billion or 8% compared to 2020, which was mainly caused by a decrease in current liabilities.

## Current liabilities

In 2021, the Company's current liabilities were Rp1.6 trillion or decreased by 8%. The decrease in the amount of current liabilities was mainly due to a decrease in trade payable which was partly offset by an increase in accrued expenses

## Taksiran pengembalian Pajak

Taksiran pengembalian pajak Perseroan pada tanggal 31 Desember 2021 adalah sebesar Rp70,4 milyar dan mengalami penurunan sebesar 52% dibanding tahun lalu sebesar Rp146,5 milyar. Penurunan ini terutama berasal dari penerimaan pengembalian pajak anak perusahaan tahun pajak 2013.

## Aset tetap, neto

Aset tetap Perseroan pada tanggal 31 Desember 2021 adalah sebesar Rp386 milyar, mengalami penurunan sebesar 2% dibanding tahun lalu sebesar Rp395,5 milyar. Penurunan ini merupakan depresiasi normal tahunan dari aset tetap tersebut.

## Liabilitas

Perseroan membukukan jumlah liabilitas di akhir tahun 2021 sebesar Rp1,8 triliun yang terdiri dari 91% liabilitas jangka pendek dan 9% liabilitas jangka panjang. Jumlah liabilitas mengalami penurunan sebesar Rp160,5 milyar atau 8% dibanding tahun 2020. Penurunan nilai total liabilitas tersebut terutama disebabkan oleh penurunan liabilitas jangka pendek.

## Liabilitas jangka pendek

Pada tahun 2021, liabilitas jangka pendek Perseroan adalah sebesar Rp1,6 triliun atau mengalami penurunan sebesar 8%. Penurunan jumlah liabilitas jangka pendek terutama disebabkan oleh penurunan utang usaha yang sebagian diimbangi oleh kenaikan beban akrual.
