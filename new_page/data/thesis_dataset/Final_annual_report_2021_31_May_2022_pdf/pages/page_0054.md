# Gabriele Isacco Tironi

Member of Audit Committee
Anggota Komite Audit

Appointed as a Member of the Audit Committee based on the Decree of the Company's Board of Commissioners Number 49/SK-Dekom/SGH/V/2021 dated 20 May 2021 concerning the Dismissal and Appointment of the Company's Audit Committee Members, in which the appointment of Gabriele Isacco Tironi is effective as of 20 May 2021.

An Italian citizen, born in Treviglio (BG) Italy, on February 9, 1986, domiciled in Singapore.

He earned a Diploma in Scientific Studies &amp; Information Technology from the Industrial Institute "Galileo Galilei" Crema (CR), Italy, in 2005, then a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) in Management &amp; Production Engineering from Politecnico Di Milano, Italy, in 2008, and Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Management, Economics &amp; Industrial Engineering from Politecnico Di Milano (Italy) &amp; Universidad Politecnica De Madrid (Spain) in 2011.

Started his career from 2008 to 2010 at C.L.O. Servizi Logistici Italy as Logistics &amp; Human Resource Management Intern, he continued his career by joining for more than 8 (eight) years at Pricewaterhousecoopers Advisory Deals Italy and Singapore as M&amp;A Strategy &amp; Operations. Then since 2019 until now, he has been trusted as a member of the Board of Directors at PT Mulia Boga Raya Tbk. In the same year, he joined CVC Asia Pacific (Singapore) Pte. Ltd as Portfolio Management Senior Advisor to date.
