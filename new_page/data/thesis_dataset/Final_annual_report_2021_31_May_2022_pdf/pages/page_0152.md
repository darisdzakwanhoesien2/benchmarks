## Nomination and Remuneration Committee Charter

The Nomination and Remuneration Committee is guided by the Nomination and Remuneration Committee Guidelines which contain (i) membership composition and structure, (ii) term of office, (iii) duties and responsibilities, (iv) work procedures and procedures, (v) policy for holding meetings, (vi) activity reporting system, and (vii) procedures for replacing members of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, as outlined in the Decree of the Company's Board of Commissioners Number 08/SK-Dekom/SGH/VI/2020 dated June 19, 2020 regarding the Establishment of the Company's Nomination and Remuneration Committee Charter.
