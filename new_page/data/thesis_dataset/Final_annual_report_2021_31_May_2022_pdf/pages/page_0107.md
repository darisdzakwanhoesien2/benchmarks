Net revenues from the Alliance segment increased by 16.2% compared to 2020. The increase was mainly due to several new product launches in 2021, such as Pramifrol 0.375 mg, Pramifrol 0.75mg, Celmunos &amp; Teroza 20 mg, although the increase was still below target due to lower demand throughout the Covid-19 pandemic.

Net revenues from the segment Others decreased by 20.7% mainly due to a decrease in revenue of Medical Devices that stopped promoting BBraun products, and only a slightly increase from Unihealth.

In 2021, the Company's cost of revenues amounted to Rp5.5 trillion, or increased by 14.3%. The increase in cost of revenues was mainly attributable to the Distribution segment and was the result of the increase in sales volume.

Cost of revenues for the Professional Product and the Consumer Health segments also increased following a significant increase in sales. In these segments, cost of revenues mainly represents cost of raw materials used in production.

Cost of revenue for the Alliance segment increased compared to the previous year due to the addition of several new products with higher cost of revenues.

Decrease in cost of revenues in the Others segment was due to decreasing sales volume from Medical Devices.

Pada tahun 2021, beban pokok pendapatan perseroan adalah sebesar Rp5,5 trillion atau meningkat 14,3%. Peningkatan beban pokok pendapatatan terutama berasal dari segmen Distribusi, yang dipengaruhi oleh peningkatan volume penjualan.

Beban pokok pendapatan untuk segmen Produk Profesional dan Kesehatan Konsumen, nilainya juga meningkat seiring peningkatan penjualan yang cukup signifikan. Pada segmen ini, beban pokok penjualan sebagian besar merupakan biaya bahan baku yang digunakan dalam produksi.

Beban pokok pendapatan untuk segmen Alliance mengalami kenaikan dibandingkan dengan tahun lalu karena adanya tambahan beberapa produk baru ditahun ini dengan bobot beban pokok pendapatan lebih tinggi.

Penurunan beban pokok pendapatan pada segmen Lainnya karena adanya penurunan volume pendapatan dari Alat Kesehatan.
