|  1 | Public Companies should be equipped with either open or closed voting technical procedures, which focuses on independence and the interest of shareholders.

Perusahaan Terbuka memiliki cara atau prosedur teknis pengumpulan suara (voting) baik secara terbuka maupun tertutup yang mengedepankan independensi, dan kepentingan pemegang saham. | Recommendations have been implemented.

The Company has regulated the procedures and technical procedures for voting in each GMS. The procedures and technical procedures are prepared based on the provisions of the Company's Articles of Association and the applicable laws and regulations.

The procedures and technical procedures are notified to all shareholders in the invitation letter for the GMS and contained in the rules for the GMS, which are distributed to each shareholder present.

The technical procedures and procedures also include regulations regarding voting conducted in private through voting cards given to shareholders, as well as voting through a system provided by KSEI (KSEI eASY).

Information related to these procedures is also available on the Company's Website, as will always be updated from time to time, in the event that the Company's GMS will be held.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.

Perseroan telah mengatur tata cara serta prosedur teknis pengumpulan suara (voting) dalam setiap RUPS. Tata cara serta prosedur teknis tersebut disusun berdasarkan ketentuan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan dan peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku.

Tata cara serta prosedur teknis tersebut diberitahukan kepada seluruh pemegang saham pada surat pemanggilan RUPS serta dimuat dalam tata tertib RUPS, yang dibagikan pada setiap pemegang saham yang hadir.

Tata cara serta prosedur teknis tersebut juga mencakup peraturan terkait pemungutan suara yang dilakukan tertutup melalui kartu suara yang diberikan kepada para pemegang saham, maupun pemungutan suara yang diberikan melalui sistem yang disediakan KSEI (eASY KSEI).

Informasi terkait tata cara tersebut juga tersedia pada Situs Web Perseroan, sebagaimana akan selalu diperbaharui dari waktu ke waktu, dalam hal akan dilaksanakannya RUPS Perseroan.  |
|  2 | All members of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners of the Public Company shall attend the Annual GMS.

Seluruh anggota Direksi dan anggota Dewan Komisaris Perusahaan Terbuka hadir dalam RUPS Tahunan. | Recommendations have been implemented but have not been fully fulfilled due to the Covid-19 pandemic.

The Company always tries to present the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners at each Annual GMS. The Company's Annual GMS for Fiscal Year 2020 was attended by 3 (three) of 4 (four) members of the Board of Directors and 0 of 2 (two) members of the Board of Commissioners. The absence of members of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners occurred due to:

1. there are members of the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners who are domiciled outside the territory of the Republic of Indonesia,
2. there are travel restrictions to the territory of the Republic of Indonesia and the Jakarta area, due to the Covid-19 pandemic,
3. in order to support the Government's efforts to prevent potential transmission of Covid-19 and comply with the Guidelines for the Prevention and Control of Covid-19 as issued by the Ministry of Health of the Republic of Indonesia; as well as
4. there is a high risk to the safety and health of the members of the Board of Directors and members of the Board of Commissioners if the members of the Board of Directors and members of the Board of Commissioners physically attend the GMS of the Company, considering that some members of the Board of Directors and members of the Board of Commissioners who are absent are classified in the category of elderly. They have a higher risk when exposed to the Covid-19 virus.  |
