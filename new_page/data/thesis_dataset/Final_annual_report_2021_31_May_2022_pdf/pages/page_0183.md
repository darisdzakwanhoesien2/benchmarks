|  11 | The Board of Commissioners or the Committee in charge of executing the Nomination and Remuneration function shall prepare succession policy for the Nomination of members of the Board of Directors.

Dewan Komisaris atau Komite yang menjalankan fungsi Nominasi dan Remunerasi menyusun kebijakan suksesi dalam proses Nominasi anggota Direksi. | Recommendations have been implemented.
The Company's Nomination and Remuneration Committee is in charge of compiling the composition and nomination process for members of the Board of Directors and/or Board of Commissioners, as well as formulating policies and criteria in the nomination process for candidates for members of the Board of Directors and/or Board of Commissioners. This is stated in the Company's Nomination and Remuneration Committee Guidelines which are also available on the Company's Website. The succession plan is discussed annually and the implementation of succession is carried out as needed.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.
Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi Perseroan bertugas menyusun komposisi dan proses nominasi anggota Direksi dan/atau Dewan Komisaris, serta menyusun kebijakan dan kriteria dalam proses nominasi calon anggota Direksi dan/atau Dewan Komisaris. Hal tersebut tercantum dalam Pedoman Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi Perseroan yang juga telah tersedia dalam Situs Web Perseroan. Rencana suksesi dibahas setiap tahun dan pelaksanaan suksesi dijalankan sesuai dengan kebutuhan.  |

|  12 | Decisions on the number of members of the Board of Directors shall consider the condition of the respective Public Company and the effectiveness of decision making process.

Penentuan jumlah anggota Direksi mempertimbangkan kondisi Perusahaan Terbuka serta efektifitas dalam pengambilan keputusan. | Recommendations have been implemented.
The Company has a Board of Directors Charter which regulates the number of members of the Board of Directors, where the composition and number of members of the Board of Directors is determined by the GMS with due regard to the purposes and objectives of the Company as stipulated in the Company's Articles of Association, thus enabling effective, precise and fast decision making in the daily management of the Company.

The determination of the number of members of the Board of Directors is also regulated in Article 18 paragraph 1 of the Company's Articles of Association, the number of which is adjusted to the requirements of the prevailing laws and regulations in the Capital Market sector.

Both the Board of Directors Charter and the Company's Articles of Association are available on the Company's Website.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.
Perseroan telah memiliki Piagam Direksi yang mengatur mengenai jumlah anggota dari Direksi, dimana komposisi dan jumlah anggota Direksi ditetapkan oleh RUPS dengan memperhatikan maksud dan tujuan Perseroan sebagaimana diatur dalam Anggaran Dasar Perseroan, sehingga memungkinkan pengambilan keputusan yang efektif, tepat dan cepat dalam pengurusan Perseroan sehari-hari.

Penentuan jumlah anggota Direksi juga diatur dalam Pasal 18 ayat 1 Anggaran Dasar Perseroan yang mana jumlahnya disesuaikan dengan persyaratan dalam peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku di bidang Pasar Modal.

Baik Piagam Direksi maupun Anggaran Dasar Perseroan telah tersedia dalam Situs Web Perseroan.  |
