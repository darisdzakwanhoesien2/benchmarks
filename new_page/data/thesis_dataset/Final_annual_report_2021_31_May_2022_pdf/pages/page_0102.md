## Financial performance

As of 2021, the Company recorded total assets amounting to Rp4 trillion, decreasing by 4% compared to 2020.

## Current assets

The Company's current assets decreased by 2% from Rp3.4 trillion to Rp3.3 trillion in 2021. This is mainly due to decrease in trade receivables which was partly offset by an increase in inventories and cash and cash equivalents.

## Posisi keuangan konsolidasian

Pada tahun 2021, Perseroan mencatatkan total asset sebesar Rp4 trillion, turun sebesar 4% dibandingkan tahun 2020.

## Aset lancar

Aset lancar Perseroan turun sebesar 2% dari Rp3,4 trillion menjadi Rp3,3 trillion di tahun 2021. Hal tersebut terutama disebabkan oleh penurunan saldo piutang usaha yang sebagian diimbangi oleh kenaikan persediaan serta kas dan setara kas.
