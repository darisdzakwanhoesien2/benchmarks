# PT Soho Industri Pharmasi Handed Over Veklury® Donations from Gilead Sciences to Ministry of Health to Support the Ongoing COVID-19 Pandemic

PT Soho Industri Pharmasi handed over donation of 100,000 vials of Veklury® (remdesivir) on October 25, 2021 from Gilead Sciences, Inc. to Ministry of Health (MoH) to support the ongoing Covid pandemic in Indonesia.

The Veklury donations will complement the supply of generic remdesivir provided through Gilead's voluntary licensing program. Gilead is working closely with PT Soho Industri Pharmasi, a subsidiary of PT Soho Global Health, Tbk. (SOHO) and directly with the Governments of Indonesia to coordinate these donations. SOHO's role as Gilead's local distributor in Indonesia is to facilitate the Veklury® donation, given the fact that SOHO is Gilead's partner in selling and marketing of antivirus products in Indonesia.

Gilead has previously donated at least 450,000 vials of Veklury to India, 10,000 vials of Veklury to Georgia and 3000 vials to Armenia. Veklury is approved or authorized for temporary use in approximately 50 countries worldwide.
