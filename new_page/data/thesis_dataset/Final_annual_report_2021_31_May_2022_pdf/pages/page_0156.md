## Internal Audit Unit Reporting

Throughout 2021, the IAU carried out general audits based on the annual audit work plan submitted and approved by the Board of Directors. IAU also conducted special (ad-hoc) audits based on the results of discussions or requests from the management and/or business units.

The audit plan was prepared using a risk-based approach, where audit focus is made for areas with high risks exposure. Further, the audit plan was continuously adjusted based on the changing audit environment and condition.

Audit results were reported to the Board of Directors monthly. A summary of audit results and highlights were reported and presented during quarterly meetings with the Board of Commissioners and Audit Committee.

## Laporan Unit Audit Internal

Sepanjang tahun 2021, UAI melakukan audit umum berdasarkan rencana kerja audit tahunan yang disampaikan ke dan disetujui oleh Direksi. UAI juga melakukan audit khusus (ad-hoc) berdasarkan hasil pembahasan atau permintaan dari manajemen dan/atau unit bisnis.

Rencana audit disusun dengan menggunakan pendekatan berbasis risiko, di mana fokus audit dibuat untuk area dengan eksposur risiko yang tinggi. Selanjutnya, rencana audit terus disesuaikan dengan perubahan lingkungan dan kondisi audit.

Hasil audit dilaporkan kepada Direksi setiap bulan. Ringkasan hasil audit dan sorotan telah dilaporkan dan dipresentasikan dalam rapat triwulanan dengan Dewan Komisaris dan Komite Audit.
