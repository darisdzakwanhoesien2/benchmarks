# Richard Kidarsa

Member of Audit Committee
Anggota Komite Audit

![img-55.jpeg](img-55.jpeg)

Appointed as a Member of the Audit Committee based on the Decree of the Company's Board of Commissioners Number 42/SK-Dekom/SGH/III/2021 dated April 9, 2021 concerning Dismissal and Appointment of Members of the Company's Audit Committee, of which Richard Kidarsa's appointment is effective as of May 1, 2021.

An Indonesian citizen, born in Jakarta on January 13, 1985, domiciled in Singapore.

He earned a Bachelor of Science in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Purdue University in 2006 and a Master of Science in Management Science and Engineering from Stanford University in 2011.

Starting his career at Sandisk Corp, United States, from 2006 to 2010 as a System Engineer and Verification Engineer, he continued his career by joining Morgan Stanley, Singapore, from 2012 to 2016 as an Analyst and Associate.

Since 2016 until now, he has joined CVC Asia Pacific (Singapore) Pte. Ltd. with the position of Investment Executive, then Investment Director until now as Director.

Diangkat sebagai Anggota Komite Audit berdasarkan Surat Keputusan Dewan Komisaris Perseroan Nomor 42/SK-Dekom/SGH/III/2021 tanggal 9 April 2021 tentang Pemberhentian dan Pengangkatan Anggota Komite Audit Perseroan, yang mana pengangkatan Richard Kidarsa berlaku efektif per 1 Mei 2021.

Seorang Warga Negara Indonesia, lahir di Jakarta pada tanggal 13 Januari 1985, berdomisili di Singapura.

Sejak tahun 2016 hingga saat ini, beliau bergabung dengan CVC Asia Pacific (Singapore) Pte. Ltd dengan pernah menempati jabatan sebagai Investment Executive, kemudian Investment Director hingga saat ini menjadi Director.
