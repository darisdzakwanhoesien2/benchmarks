# Audit Committee Profile

Profil Komite Audit

The Company's Audit Committee is appointed based on the decision of the Board of Commissioners and responsible to the Board of Commissioners in assisting and carrying out the duties and functions of the Board of Commissioners. The Company's Audit Committee is acts independently in carrying out its duties and responsibilities.

During 2021, the Company made 2 (two) changes to the Audit Committee based on the Decree of the Company's Board of Commissioners Number 42/SK-Dekom/SGH/III/2021 dated April 9, 2021 and the Decree of the Company's Board of Commissioners Number 49/SK-Dekom/SGH/V/2021 May 20, 2021.

Komite Audit Perseroan diangkat berdasarkan keputusan Dewan Komisaris dan bertanggung jawab kepada Dewan Komisaris dalam membantu dan melaksanakan tugas dan fungsi Dewan Komisaris. Komite Audit Perseroan bertindak secara independen dalam melaksanakan tugas dan tanggung jawabnya.

Selama tahun 2021, Perseroan melakukan 2 (dua) kali perubahan Komite Audit berdasarkan Surat Keputusan Dewan Komisaris Perseroan Nomor 42/SK-Dekom/SGH/III/2021 tanggal 9 April 2021 dan Surat Keputusan Dewan Komisaris Perseroan Nomor 49/SK-Dekom/SGH/V/2021 tanggal 20 Mei 2021.
