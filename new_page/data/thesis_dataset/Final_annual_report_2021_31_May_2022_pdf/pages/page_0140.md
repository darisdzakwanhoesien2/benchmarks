2. During 2021, the attendance rate of members of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee in meeting reached 100%, as explained in more detail in the section on the Nomination and Remuneration Committee.

## Remuneration for the Board of Commissioners

In 2021, the Company paid IDR 400,000,000 for the remuneration of the Board of Commissioners of the Group Companies.

The remuneration procedure, basis of determination, and remuneration structure is determined based on the Board of Commissioner's annual performance.
