# General Shareholders Meeting Year 2021

During 2021, the Company held one (1) time Annual GMS. To hold this GMS, in accordance with the provisions of Article 12 of the Company's Articles of Association and with the provisions of Article 8, Article 10, and Article 17 of POJK No. 15/2020, the Company's Board of Directors has conducted the following:

1. Sent a letter to the Financial Services Authority ("OJK") regarding the plan to hold a GMS and its agenda through letter Number 163/SGH-OJK/III/2021 dated March 17, 2021 regarding: Submission of the Agenda for the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders.

2. Advertised the Announcement on March 25, 2021, with the following details:
- in the Indonesian language newspaper, namely Media Indonesia;
- in the Company's official website (in Indonesian and English version)
- in the Indonesia Stock Exchange website, through the Integrated Electronic Reporting Facility ("SPE"); and
- eASY.KSEI website

3. Advertised the Invitation on April 9, 2021, with the following details:
- in the Indonesian language newspaper, namely Media Indonesia;
- in the Company's official website (in Indonesian and English version)
- in the Indonesia Stock Exchange website, through the Integrated Electronic Reporting Facility ("SPE"); and
- eASY.KSEI website

## Annual GMS

The Annual GMS was attended or the Company's shares in the meeting were 1,257,434,639 or 99.07% of the 1,269,168,239 shares, which are all fully paid-up shares of the Company.

# Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Tahun 2021

Selama tahun 2021, Perseroan menyelenggarakan satu (1) kali RUPS Tahunan. Bahwa untuk menyelenggarakan RUPS ini, sesuai dengan ketentuan Pasal 12 Anggaran Dasar Perseroan dan dengan memperhatikan ketentuan Pasal 8, Pasal 10, dan Pasal 17 POJK No. 15/2020. Direksi Perseroan telah melakukan hal-hal sebagai berikut:

1. Mengirimkan surat kepada Otoritas Jasa Keuangan ("OJK") tentang rencana akan diselenggarakannya RUPS beserta mata acaranya melalui surat Nomor 163/SGH-OJK/III/2021 tanggal 17 Maret 2021 perihal: Penyampaian Mata Acara Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham Tahunan.

2. Memasang iklan Pengumuman pada tanggal 25 Maret 2021, dengan rincian sebagai berikut:
- dalam harian surat kabar berbahasa Indonesia, yaitu Media Indonesia;
- di situs resmi Perseroan (dalam versi bahasa Indonesia dan bahasa Inggris);
- di situs Bursa Efek Indonesia melalui Sarana Pelaporan Elektronik Terintegrasi ("SPE"); dan
- situs eASY.KSEI.

3. Memasang iklan Pemanggilan pada tanggal 9 April 2021, dengan rincian sebagai berikut:
- dalam harian surat kabar berbahasa Indonesia yaitu Media Indonesia;
- di situs resmi Perseroan (dalam versi bahasa Indonesia dan bahasa Inggris);
- di situs Bursa Efek Indonesia melalui Saran Pelaporan Elektronik Terintegrasi ("SPE"); dan
- situs eASY.KSEI.
