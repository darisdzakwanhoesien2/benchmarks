## Board of Commissioners Charter

In supervising the Company's management, the Board of Commissioners follows the guideline from Board of Commissioners Charter which contains their duties, responsibilities, and authorities.

## Assessment of the performance of the Board of Commissioners

Referring to the Board of Commissioners Charter, the evaluation of the performance of the members of the Board of Commissioners is carried out by the Company's shareholders. The Company also has a Policy on Performance Approvaisal of the Board of Commissioners, in which this policy is prepared as a guideline that can be used to assess the effectiveness of the implementation of the duties and responsibilities of the Board of Commissioners and also as a form of accountability for the assessment of the performance of the Board of Commissioners of the Company.

The Company's shareholders periodically evaluate the performance of the Board of Commissioners at least once a year. Performance appraisal is carried out on the implementation of the duties and responsibilities of the Board of Commissioners in supervising and managing the Company as stated in the laws and regulations and the Company's Articles of Association.

## Piagam Dewan Komisaris

Dalam mengawasi pengelolaan Perseroan, Dewan Komisaris berpedoman pada Piagam Dewan Komisaris yang memuat tugas, tanggung jawab dan wewenang Dewan Komisaris.

## Penilaian terhadap kinerja Dewan Komisaris

Mengacu pada Piagam Dewan Komisaris (Board of Commissioners Charter), penilaian atas kinerja anggota Dewan Komisaris dilakukan oleh pemegang saham Perseroan. Perseroan juga telah memiliki Kebijakan Penilaian atas Kinerja Dewan Komisaris, yang mana kebijakan tersebut disusun sebagai pedoman yang dapat digunakan untuk menilai efektivitas pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab Dewan Komisaris dan juga sebagai bentuk akuntabilitas atas penilaian kinerja Dewan Komisaris Perseroan.

Pemegang saham Perseroan secara berkala melakukan evaluasi atas kinerja Dewan Komisaris setidaknya satu kali dalam setahun. Penilaian kinerja dilakukan terhadap pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab Dewan Komisaris dalam mengawasi dan mengelola Perseroan sebagaimana yang tercantum dalam peraturan perundang-undangan dan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan.
