# The economic recovery and improvements in mobility and consumer confidence lead to a significant boost to the FMCG market

Leisure, Education, Transportation, and Health recovered as mobility and vaccination rates increased. Basic needs such as Food &amp; FMCG remained stable and financial-related spending such as Insurance and Saving + Loan showed a declining trend.

Pemulihan ekonomi dan peningkatan mobilitas dan kepercayaan konsumen menghasilkan pertumbuhan signifikan bagi pasar FMCG

Kenyamanan, Pendidikan, Transportasi, dan Kesehatan pulih karena mobilitas dan tingkat vaksinasi meningkat. Kebutuhan dasar seperti Makanan &amp; FMCG tetap stabil sementara pengeluaran terkait keuangan seperti Asuransi dan Tabungan + Pinjaman menunjukkan tren yang menurun.
