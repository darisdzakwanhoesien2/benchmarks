# Andy Nugroho
## Purwohardono

Commissioner
Komisaris

An Indonesian citizen, born in Jakarta, on December 22, 1968. Domiciled in Singapore.

He earned a Bachelor of Science degree from Oregon State University Corvallis, Oregon, United States, in 1991 and continued his education to obtain a Master of Business Administration from The University of Texas, in San Antonio, Texas, United States in 1993.

Starting his career in 1991, he held several positions at Intel Corp, PT SG Securities Indonesia, ABN Amro Bank, and PT Danareksa Securities until 2009. He joined PT Morgan Stanley Asia Indonesia, from 2009 to 2013 as President Director and Head of Investment Banking. He also joined PT Matahari Department Store Tbk as Director from 2014 to 2017. In 2015 he joined PT MAP Active Adiperkasa Tbk as Director until 2020. He also joined PT Softex Indonesia from 2016 to 2020 as Director. Currently, he also holds several positions including Senior Managing Director at CVC Asia Pacific Limited since 2013, Director at PT Link Net Tbk since 2014, Commissioner of PT Siloam International Hospitals Tbk since 2016, and Commissioner of PT MAP Active Adiperkasa Tbk since 2020.
