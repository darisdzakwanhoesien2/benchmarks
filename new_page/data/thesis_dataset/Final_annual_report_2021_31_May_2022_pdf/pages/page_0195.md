Waste Management

Our waste is managed based on the type and material content, which is comprised of solid and liquid waste, and hazardous and toxic and non-hazardous and toxic materials. Our waste management is carried in collaboration with licensed third parties.

Liquid waste is managed through the Waste Water Treatment Plant (WWTP). We ensure that run-off water meets Government established quality standards before being released into bodies of water, or channelled for further processing by industrial estate managers. Waste water quality monitoring is carried out internally and externally and involves an environmental laboratory accredited by the National Accreditation Committee (NAC) and registered with the Ministry of Environment and Forestry (MoEF). Their inspection results are routinely reported to the City/District Environmental Agency and the Provincial Environmental Management Agency and the MoEF.

Penanganan limbah

Limbah kami dikelola berdasarkan jenis dan kandungan bahannya, meliputi limbah padat dan cair, serta bahan berbahaya dan beracun (B3) dan non-B3. Pengelolaan limbah kami dilakukan secara mandiri, atau bekerja sama dengan pihak ketiga yang memiliki izin.

Limbah cair dikelola melalui Instalasi Pengolahan Air Limbah (IPAL). Kami memastikan bahwa air limpasan memenuhi kualitas yang ditetapkan. Pemerintah baku sebelum dibuang ke badan air, atau dialirkan untuk diproses lebih lanjut oleh pengelola kawasan industri. Pemantauan kualitas air limbah dilakukan secara internal dan eksternal dan melibatkan laboratorium lingkungan yang terakreditasi oleh Komite Akreditasi Nasional (KPA) dan terdaftar di Kementerian Lingkungan Hidup dan Kehutanan (KLHK). Hasil pengawasan mereka secara rutin dilaporkan ke Kota/Kabupaten Badan Lingkungan Hidup dan Badan Pengelolaan Lingkungan Hidup Provinsi dan KLHK.
