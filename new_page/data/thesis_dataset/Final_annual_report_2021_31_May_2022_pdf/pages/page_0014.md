## Dear valued Shareholders

Pemegang Saham yang Terhormat,

The world in 2020/21 was firmly in the grip of the coronavirus pandemic. Across the globe many people died from the virus infection, the economy slumped, livelihoods were destroyed. For SOHO Global Health, too, it was a challenging period, yet we came through the pandemic in good shape and at the same time laid the foundation for future growth.

We achieved a great deal in the face of volatile conditions, bringing new products to market for our customers and patients, driving forward SOHO's transformation and taking our sustainability commitment to the next level. We invested substantially in innovation and future growth.

## Shouldering responsibility during the crisis

Looking back to the past year, we have much we can be proud of. We managed to keep the company running successfully despite this major crisis, and continued providing patients and consumers with urgently needed and, in some cases, life-saving products. Our Imboost Force was the most sought after health supplement during the various pandemic waves. At the same time we succeeded in protecting our workforce and minimizing the number of Covid-19 infections in the company, although, sadly, several colleagues passed away due to the severe delta variant that hit Indonesia in the middle of the year.

We would like to recognize in particular our frontline employees and factory workers – their hard work and resilience were critical to get through 2021 and to maintain business continuity. Our businesses in healthcare and distribution are all essential to people's lives and it is our social responsibility to ensure stable production and supply.

This effort wasn't primarily driven by financial considerations. Our overriding aim has been to contribute to ending the pandemic. Right from the start of this emergency, we've been helping in many ways: we donated money, medicines, multivitamins, protective equipment and medical appliances either through government agencies and charity foundations or directly to medical personnel and people in need. On top of that, we've helped raising awareness and providing relevant health information via a series of webinars with experts/Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs).

## Memikul tanggung jawab selama krisis

Melihat kembali ke tahun sebelumnya, kami memiliki banyak hal untuk dibanggakan. Kami mengelola perusahaan agar tetap berjalan dengan sukses ditengah krisis, dan terus melayani pasien dan pelanggan dengan kebutuhan mendesak, bahkan dalam kondisi tertentu, kebutuhan akan produk life-saving. Imboost Force merupakan salah satu produk suplemen kesehatan paling dicari konsumen selama beberapa kali gelombang pandemi. Di saat bersamaan kami berhasil melindungi kesehatan tenaga kerja kami dan meminimalkan paparan infeksi Covid-19 di perusahaan kami, walaupun dengan sangat berduka, kami kehilangan beberapa kolega yang meninggal dunia akibat parahnya infeksi varian delta di Indonesia pada pertengahan tahun lalu.

Kami hendak memberikan penghormatan secara khusus pada karyawan yang berada di garis depan dan para pekerja pabrik – kerja keras dan resiliensi mereka penting untuk keberhasilan kami melalui 2021 dan menjaga keberlangsungan usaha. Kegiatan bisnis kami di bidang kesehatan dan distribusi adalah esensial untuk kehidupan banyak orang dan adalah tanggung jawab sosial kami untuk memastikan stabilitas produksi dan pasokan produk kesehatan.

Segala upaya yang kami lakukan tidak semata-mata berdasarkan pertimbangan finansial. Tujuan utama kami adalah berkontribusi untuk mengakhiri pandemi. Tepat sebelum keadaan darurat ini dimulai, kami telah berkontribusi dalam berbagai cara: kami memberikan donasi berupa dana, obat-obatan, multivitamin, alat perlindungan diri (APD) dan peralatan medis, baik melalui instansi pemerintah dan yayasan amal atau langsung pada tenaga medis dan masyarakat yang membutuhkan. Disamping segala kontribusi tersebut, kami juga melaksanakan peran kami untuk meningkatkan kesadaran akan pentingnya menjaga imun tubuh serta menyediakan informasi kesehatan yang relevan bagi masyarakat melalui serangkaian webinar, dengan narasumber para ahli kesehatan/Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs).
