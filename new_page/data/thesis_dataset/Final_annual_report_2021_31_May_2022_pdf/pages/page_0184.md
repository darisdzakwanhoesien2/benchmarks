|  13 | Decision on the composition of the Board of Directors should consider diversity in the required expertise, knowledge and experience.

Penentuan komposisi anggota Direksi memperhatikan, keberagaman keahlian, pengetahuan, dan pengalaman yang dibutuhkan. | Recommendations have been implemented.
The determination of the composition of the members of the Company's Board of Directors has taken into account the diversity of expertise, knowledge and experience required by the Company. This can be seen in the profiles and work history of the members of the Company's Board of Directors which have been described in the Company's Annual Report for the 2021 Financial Year.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.
Penentuan komposisi anggota Direksi Perseroan telah memperhatikan keberagaman keahlian, pengetahuan dan pengalaman yang dibutuhkan oleh Perseroan. Hal ini dapat terlihat pada profil dan riwayat kerja dari para anggota Direksi Perseroan yang telah diuraikan dalam Laporan Tahunan Perseroan Tahun Buku 2021.  |
|  14 | Board of Directors in charge of accounting or finance shall have the expertise and/or knowledge in accounting.

Anggota Direksi yang membawahi bidang akuntansi atau keuangan memiliki keahlian dan/atau pengetahuan di bidang akuntansi. | Recommendations have been implemented.
The Company's Finance Director, Mr. Piero Brambati, has strong expertise and experience in accounting and finance. This can be seen in his profile and work history in the Profile of the Company's Board of Directors in the Company's Annual Report for the 2021 Financial Year.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.
Direktur Keuangan Perseroan, yakni Bapak Piero Brambati, memiliki keahlian dan pengalaman yang mumpuni dalam bidang akuntansi dan keuangan. Hal ini dapat terlihat pada profil dan riwayat kerja beliau pada bagian Profil Direksi Perseroan dalam Laporan Tahunan Perseroan Tahun Buku 2021.  |

|  15 | The Board of Directors shall have a self assessment policy to measure the performance of the Board of Directors.

Direksi mempunyai kebijakan penilaian sendiri (self assessment) untuk menilai kinerja Direksi. | Recommendations have been implemented.
The Company currently has a Policy for Assessment of the Performance of the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors, the policy also explains aspects of the assessment and performance appraisal system of the Board of Directors.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.
Perseroan saat ini telah memiliki Kebijakan Penilaian atas Kinerja Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi Perseroan, dalam kebijakan tersebut juga menjelaskan perihal aspek-apek penilaian dan sistem penilaian kinerja Direksi.  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  16 | Policy on self assessment to measure the performance of the Board of Directors shall be reported in the Public Company's Annual Report.

Kebijakan penilaian sendiri (self assessment) untuk menilai kinerja Direksi diungkapkan melalui laporan tahunan Perusahaan Terbuka. | Recommendations have been implemented.
The self-assessment policy to assess the performance of the Board of Directors has been disclosed in the Public Company Annual Report.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.
Kebijakan penilaian sendiri (self assessment) untuk menilai kinerja Direksi telah diungkapkan melalui Laporan Tahunan Perusahaan Terbuka.  |
|  17 | The Board of Directors shall have a resignation policy for its members in the event of any financial fraud.

Direksi mempunyai kebijakan terkait pengunduran diri anggota Direksi apabila terlibat dalam kejahatan keuangan. | Recommendations have been implemented.
Policies regarding the resignation of members of the Board of Directors due to involvement in financial crimes or other violations of law have been stated in the Company's Board of Directors Charter and the Company's Articles of Association. The Board of Directors' Charter is available on the Company's Website.

Rekomendasi telah dilaksanakan.
Kebijakan terkait pengunduran diri anggota Direksi karena terlibat dalam kejahatan keuangan maupun pelanggaran hukum lainnya telah tercantum dalam Piagam Direksi Perseroan dan Anggaran Dasar Perseroan. Piagam Direksi telah tersedia di dalam Situs Web Perseroan.  |
