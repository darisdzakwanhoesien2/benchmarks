- Y-o-y sales growth: launch new product / line extensions; increase awareness and trial for our key brands; secure new distribution principals.
- Margin and cash conversion focus: operating leverage and cost reduction initiatives; relentless management of working capital and prepaid taxes.

The overall business prognosis for Indonesia in 2022 could still be impacted by resurgences in Covid-19 pandemic delaying the anticipated recovery and by accelerating inflation as a result of global accommodative monetary policies and geopolitical events. - Group free cash flow generation depends on timely collection of receivables from public hospitals and prepaid taxes, both of which can face delays.
