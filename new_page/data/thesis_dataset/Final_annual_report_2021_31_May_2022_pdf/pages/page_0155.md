# Internal Audit Unit

Unit Audit Internal

The Internal Audit Unit ("IAU") was formed based on the Decree of the Board of Directors Number 05/SK-Dir/SGH/VI/2020 about the Establishment of the Company's Internal Audit Unit dated June 19, 2020.

IAU assists the company's management by reviewing the design and execution of controls, process and providing recommendations on how to improve the operational and financial processes.

The IAU is responsible to the President Director and acts independently in carrying out its duties and responsibilities. The IAU has unrestricted access to all functions and records within the Company, to fully carry out its mandate.

Patrick Lian Abuan Go was appointed as Head of the Internal Audit Unit based on the Decree of the Board of Directors Number 07/SK-Dir/SGH/VI/2020 about the Appointment of Head of Internal Audit Unit dated June 19, 2020.

The detailed biography of the Head of Internal Audit Unit is presented under the the Company Profile section.

## Unit Audit Internal Charter

The IAU is guided by the Internal Audit Unit Charter which contains the vision and mission, scope of work, duties, responsibilities, authorities and Code of Ethics of the Internal Audit Unit, which is outlined in the Decree of the Board of Directors Number 06/SK-Dir/SGH/VI/2020 dated June 19, 2020 about the Charter of Internal Audit Unit.

Unit Audit Internal ("UAI") dibentuk berdasarkan Keputusan Direksi Perseroan Nomor 05/SK-Dir/SGH/VI/2020 tentang Pembentukan Unit Audit Internal Perseroan tertanggal 19 Juni 2020.

UAI membantu manajemen perusahaan dengan meninjau desain dan pelaksanaan kontrol, proses dan memberikan rekomendasi tentang cara meningkatkan proses operasional dan keuangan.
