Innovations in the product development process with green technology include the use of water-based coating media and no longer using organic solvent-based coating media. The comprehensive implementation of the water-based coating media on our products was able to reduce 100% of the impact of the risk of organic solvent contamination on the environment and also the risk of exposure to organic solvents of our employees.

Continuous innovation in the production process involves the application of environmentally friendly technology wherein the herbal extraction process is carried out with environmentally friendly solvents (not organic solvents) and the application of solvent recovery and reuse. It was able to reduce the use and waste of solvents by more than 25%.

After-sales service is also carried out by utilizing technology, information and communication through various available media such as online media, social media and print media.
