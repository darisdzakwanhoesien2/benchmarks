1. PT. Asuransi Himalaya Pelindung (continue/lanjutan)

|  Case Status/Status Perkara | PPG merupakan nasabah dari Asuransi Himalaya. PPG telah mengajukan klaim atas kerusakan produk kepada Asuransi Himalaya sejak tanggal 1 Agustus 2016 sampai 22 Januari 2018, namun PPG sampai saat ini belum juga menerima pembayaran terhadap klaim tersebut. PPG melalui kuasa hukumnya telah melakukan pengaduan ke Otoritas Jasa Keuangan terkait pengajuan klaim ini. Berdasarkan Surat Tanggapan Pengaduan dari Otoritas Jasa Keuangan, dinyatakan bahwa Asuransi Himalaya sudah dicabut izin operasionalnya sehingga harus dibubarkan. Tim likuidasi telah dibentuk untuk penyelesaian klaim yang diajukan PPG.  |
| --- | --- |
|  Risks Faced by the Company and Their Impacts on the Company/ Risiko yang Dihadapi dan Pengaruhnya terhadap Perseroan | PPG faces the risk of inability to recover its receivables fully, which amounts to IDR 659,842,897.
PPG berisiko tidak akan memperoleh pengembalian tagihan secara penuh yang jumlahnya sebesar Rp 659.842.897.  |
|  Follow-up/ Tindak Lanjut | The Liquidation Team was formed based on an announcement from the Financial Services Authority on 23 September 2020 and PPG has submitted the PPG insurance claim to the Liquidation Team of Himayala Insurance through its attorney. Currently, PPG is waiting for the next process of the liquidation process.
Tim Likuidasi sudah dibentuk berdasarkan pengumuman dari Otoritas Jasa Keuangan tanggal 23 September 2020 dan PPG telah mengajukan tagihan klaim asuransi PPG kepada Tim Likuidasi dari Asuransi Himayala melalui kuasa hukumnya. Saat ini PPG sedang menunggu proses selanjutnya dari proses likuidasi tersebut.  |

2. Henny Puspawati

|  Case Status/Status Perkara | PPG has signed Perjanjian Sewa Menyewa (Lease Agreement) with Mrs. Henny for lease of PPG's Warehouse as stated in the Deed No. 1 dated October 1, 2015 ("Lease Agreement"). Pursuant to the Lease Agreement, PPG is required to pay rent for the first five-year lease period in amount IDR 1,775,000,000. This obligation has been carried out by PPG, but in its implementation it was discovered that the Warehouse's IMB is not in accordance with its designation, so that the Warehouse could not be used by PPG, and PPG had asked Mrs. Henny to return the rental fee that had been paid by PPG in the amount of Rp. 1,175,000,000. This amount has been agreed by PPG and Mrs. Henny as stated in the Perjanjian Pengembalian Uang Sewa dated April 2, 2019 ("Rental Fee Repayment Agreement"). It is stated in the Rental Fee Repayment Agreement that Mrs. Henny will return the rental fee after Mrs. Henny's villa in Bali has been sold. However PPG has not received the rental fee until date since Mrs. Henny's villa in Bali has not been sold.  |
