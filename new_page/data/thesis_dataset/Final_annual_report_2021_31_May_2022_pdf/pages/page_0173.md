# PT. Parit Padang Global ("PPG")

|  1. PT. Asuransi Himalaya Pelindung  |   |
| --- | --- |
|  Case Status/Status Perkara | PPG is a policy holder of Asuransi Himalaya. PPG has submitted a claim for product damages to Asuransi Himalaya since 1 August 2016 to 22 January 2018, however, until now, PPG has not yet received the payment for this claim. PPG through its attorney has submitted a complaint to the Financial Services Authority regarding this claim submission. According to the Complaint Response Letter from Financial Services Authority, it is stated that Asuransi Himalaya has had its operational license revoked so that it must be dissolved. A liquidation team has been formed to settle claims that submitted by PPG.  |
