# PT BAKRIE METAL INDUSTRIES

*Senior Management of Business Unit*

Bakrie Metal Industries (BMI) started its steel pipe manufacturing business in 1959 under the name “Talang Tirta”, it originally produces conduit pipe for general use. In 1984 the company expand its business to fabrication service, which specialty is in corrugated steel and bridge. In 1985 BMI established its EPC business with the operation of its heavy industrial steel fabrication facility in Sumunraja, Banten. Today BMI is well known for its high quality steel pipes (oil &amp; gas and general purposes) and nation wide acclaimed EPC and fabrication expertise.
