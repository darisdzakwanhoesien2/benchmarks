Tanjung Jati A Power Plant (PLTU) is coal-fired power plant and it has the capacity of 2 x 660 megawatt (MW). This project is located in West Jawa, in between Cirebon and Indramayu. Construction for Cimanggis-Cibitung toll road will be commenced in two stages. The first stage was to build a 3.2 km toll road that connects Jagorawi/Cijago toll road with Transyogi main road. This first stage has been started since the second quarter of 2015 and up to currently the construction has reached 40% completion. The second stage is to build 23.3 km toll road that connects Raya Transyogi main road to Cibitung with land acquisition being our current focus activity.
