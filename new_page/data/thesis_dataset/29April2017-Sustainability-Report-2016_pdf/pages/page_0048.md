# REMUNERATION

BNBR is committed to implement the provision of fair compensation. We recognize that workforce with specialized skills, motivated, and good interaction capability are crucial for the achievement of growth objectives. We do not discriminate employees based on gender for their salary. Differences in remuneration given is due to the differences in levels, performance assessment's achievement, position / job-title and the length period of employment. Our remuneration structures always comply with government regulations related to minimum wage. Our total remuneration in 2016 amounted to Rp 239 billion. [202-1]
