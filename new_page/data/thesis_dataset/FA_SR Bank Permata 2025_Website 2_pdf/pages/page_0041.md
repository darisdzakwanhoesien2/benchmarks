|  Risiko
Risks | 1. Kaji ulang tahunan ERMF dilaksanakan sebanyak satu kali selama tahun 2025, dengan memastikan prinsip keberlanjutan tetap dikembangkan dalam kerangka kerja manajemen risiko.
2. Pelaksanaan dan pelaporan *piloting Bottom-Up Climate Risk Management & Scenario Analysis* (CRMS) pada rentang waktu Januari – Agustus 2025 termasuk meningkatkan cakupan menjadi 100% portfolio pinjaman serta melakukan pembaharuan data makro ekonomi berdasarkan NGFS phase 5. Dalam perhitungan emisi dari aktivitas pembiayaan, Bank juga meningkatkan kualitas data emisi dengan menambahkan data Tier 2 dari 10 debitur pada segmen korporasi yang diperoleh dari Sustainability Report. Penilaian dampak risiko iklim dilakukan terhadap risiko risiko transisi dan risiko fisik.
3. Melakukan sosialisasi hasil pelaksanaan CRMS kepada unit bisnis dan unit kredit untuk meningkatkan kesadaran dan pemahaman dampak dari risiko iklim.
4. Pengkajian tahunan Risk Appetite Statement (RAS) dan *Risk Tolerance* terhadap semua jenis risiko sebanyak satu kali dalam setahun.
5. Mempresentasikan dan mendiskusikan perkembangan terkini terkait keberlanjutan dan risiko iklim pada rapat komite risiko baik RMC dan RMOC sebanyak 5 agenda sepanjang tahun 2025.
6. Mengadakan Forum Diskusi terkait Risiko Iklim sebanyak lima kali selama tahun 2025 yang dihadiri pada PIC RFO Risiko Iklim.
7. Mempersiapkan prosedur pelaksanaan uji ketahanan risiko iklim dan prosedur perhitungan emisi dari aktivitas pembiayaan.
8. Mempersiapkan integrasi data terkait risiko iklim kedalam sistem internal.
9. Penerapan pelaporan THI dan TKBI sesuai dengan ketentuan regulator.
10. Pengembangan ketentuan internal terkait ESRA dan pembiayaan berkelanjutan.
11. Pengembangan sistem internal Bank untuk mendukung kebutuhan pelaporan antara lain yang mencakup klasifikasi Taksonomi Hijau Indonesia (THI), THI Tematik, dan KUBL. | 1. An annual review of the ERMF will be conducted once during 2025, ensuring that sustainability principles are continuously developed within the risk management framework.
2. Implementation and reporting of the Bottom-Up Climate Risk Management & Scenario Analysis (CRMS) pilot will be conducted between January and August 2025, including increasing coverage to 100% of the loan portfolio and updating macroeconomic data based on NGFS phase 5. In calculating emissions from financing activities, the Bank will also improve the quality of emissions data by adding Tier 2 data from 10 debtors from corporate segment obtained from the Sustainability Report. Climate risk impact assessments will be conducted for transition risks and physical risks.
3. CRMS implementation results will be disseminated to business units and credit units to increase awareness and understanding of the impacts of climate risk.
4. Annual reviews of Risk Appetite Statement (RAS) and Risk Tolerance for all risk types will be conducted once a year.
5. Presenting and discussing the latest developments related to sustainability and climate risk at 5 risk committee meetings for both the RMC and RMOC throughout 2025.
6. Convening five Climate Risk Discussion Forums throughout 2025, attended by the Climate Risk RFO PIC.
7. Preparing procedures for implementing climate risk stress tests and calculating emissions from financing activities.
8. Preparing the integration of climate risk data into internal systems.
9. Implementing THI and TKBI reporting in accordance with regulatory requirements.
10. Developing internal regulations related to ESRA and sustainable financing.
11. Developing the Bank’s internal systems to support reporting requirements, including the Indonesian Green Taxonomy (THI), Thematic THI, and KUBL classifications.  |
