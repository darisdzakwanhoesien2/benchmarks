# Tentang Laporan Keberlanjutan About Sustainability Report

Permata Bank menerbitkan Laporan Keberlanjutan secara tahunan. Laporan Keberlanjutan 2025 mencakup data dan informasi untuk periode pelaporan 1 Januari hingga 31 Desember 2025, disertai dengan penyajian data historis tertentu selama tiga tahun. Laporan ini merupakan kelanjutan dari laporan keberlanjutan yang telah diterbitkan pada periode sebelumnya di tanggal 12 Maret 2025. Seluruh data finansial dan non-finansial yang disajikan dalam laporan ini berasal dari aktivitas Permata Bank, mengingat tidak terdapat entitas anak maupun perusahaan venture bersama yang dimiliki oleh Bank. [2-2][2-3]

Ketentuan dan standar yang digunakan dalam penyusunan laporan ini meliputi Peraturan Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (POJK) No. 51/POJK.03/2017 tentang Penerapan Keuangan Berkelanjutan bagi Lembaga Jasa Keuangan, Emiten, dan Perusahaan Publik serta Surat Edaran Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (SEOJK) No. 16/SEOJK.04/2021 mengenai Bentuk dan Isi Laporan Tahunan Emiten atau Perusahaan Publik. Laporan juga disusun dengan merujuk (with reference) pada Standar Global Reporting Initiative (GRI), Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB) Standards: Commercial Banks, serta penerapan awal Standar IFRS S1 dan S2. Terdapat pengungkapan kembali pada data atau informasi dalam Laporan ini. [2-4]

Melalui proses tata kelola internal, Laporan Keberlanjutan ini telah dikonfirmasi oleh para kontributor data dan pejabat dari unit kerja terkait, sebelum ditelaah dan disetujui oleh Direksi. Selanjutnya, laporan ini menjalani proses penjaminan (assurance) oleh pihak independen eksternal, yang dipilih tanpa benturan kepentingan dan dengan persetujuan pejabat berwenang. Proses penjaminan dilakukan dengan mengacu pada Standar AA1000 tipe 1 untuk keseluruhan laporan dan tipe 2 untuk topik "Keamanan Produk dan data serta Privasi Nasabah" serta "Pengelolaan Lingkungan dan Perubahan Iklim", level moderate. [G.1][2-5]

Permata Bank publishes Sustainability Report annually. The 2025 Sustainability Report covers data and information for the reporting period from 1 January to 31 December 2025, and includes the presentation of certain historical data from the previous three years. This report is a continuation of the sustainability reports published in prior periods, most recently on 12 March 2025. All financial and non-financial data presented in this report are derived from the activities of Permata Bank, as the Bank does not have any subsidiaries or jointly owned venture companies. [2-2] [2-3]

The provisions and standards applied in the preparation of this report include Financial Services Authority Regulation (POJK) No. 51/POJK.03/2017 on the Implementation of Sustainable Finance for Financial Services Institutions, Issuers, and Public Companies, as well as Financial Services Authority Circular Letter (SEOJK) No. 16/SEOJK.04/2021 concerning the Form and Content of Annual Reports of Issuers or Public Companies. This report has also been prepared with reference to the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) Standards, the Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB) Standards: Commercial Banks, and early adoption of IFRS S1 and S2. There is restatement of data or information in this Report. [2-4]

Through internal governance processes, this Sustainability Report has been confirmed by data contributors and officials from the relevant work units, prior to being reviewed and approved by the Board of Directors. Subsequently, this report underwent an assurance process conducted by an independent external party, which was appointed without any conflict of interest and with the approval of the authorised officials. The assurance process was carried out in accordance with the AA1000 Assurance Standard, Type 1 for overall reports and type 2 for "Product and Data Security, and Customer Privacy" and "Environmental Management and Climate Change" topics, moderate level. [G.1][2-5]
