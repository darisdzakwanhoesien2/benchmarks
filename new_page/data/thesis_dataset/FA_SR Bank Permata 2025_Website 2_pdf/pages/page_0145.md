# Adherence to AA1000AP (2018) and GRI Universal Standards

Inclusivity – The Company has involved a limited number of internal stakeholders with consultants in determining material topics by conducting Focus Group Discussions (FGDs). Furthermore, the Company also involves each division in the material topic selection procedure. In the next period, the Company is expected to involve external stakeholders, as well as consider the risks, opportunities, and negative and positive impacts of the Company's business by looking at the interests of stakeholders and shareholders.

Materiality – Approach to determining material topics, the Company has used the Double Materiality Approach (DMA) and has already explained the measurement of impact materiality and financial materiality in the report. However, the Company is expected to consider short-term, medium-term, and long-term perspectives. Furthermore, for the next period, the Company is expected to determine this when planning the budget, to accommodate interests in the budget, the Company's actual actions, and reporting.

Responsiveness – The Company has responded to the needs, issues, concerns, and expectations of stakeholders through Speak Up, a complaint channel accessible to internal and external stakeholders. Monitoring is also carried out in relation to complaints received from customers, related to the detection of significant issues.

Impact – The Company has measured, assessed, and managed its impacts reasonably well in accordance with materiality and has described the level of impact materiality (positive and negative) and the level of financial materiality (risk and opportunity) in the report. Furthermore, the Company is expected to be able to identify more thoroughly the impacts arising from the performance of each material topic.

Statement of Use: "with Reference to the GRI Standards" – Our experts evaluated the Report content against the GRI Universal Standards principles, disclosures, and requirements for reporting. We believe the Report has fulfilled the requirements of its statement of use; publish a GRI Content Index, provide a statement of use, and notify GRI.
