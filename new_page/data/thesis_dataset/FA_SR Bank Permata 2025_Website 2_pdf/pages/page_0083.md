# Inovasi dan Pengembangan Produk dan/atau Jasa Keuangan Berkelanjutan [F.26]

Innovation and Development of Sustainable Finance Products and/or Services

Dalam mendukung peningkatan layanan dan efisiensi operasional, Permata Bank terus mendorong pengembangan digital di seluruh lini bisnis. Inovasi digital juga dimanfaatkan dalam pengembangan produk keuangan berkelanjutan guna menyediakan layanan keuangan yang inklusif, adil, formal, dan bertanggung jawab bagi nasabah serta masyarakat Indonesia. Berbagai produk digital Permata Bank dapat diakses melalui QR Pay, API banking, mobile banking, internet banking PermataNet, digital value chain, model branch, dan super app Permata ME.

Produk dan layanan digital Permata Bank dikembangkan sesuai dengan fungsi dan kebutuhan pengguna. Salah satunya adalah application programming interface (API) banking atau PermataAPI, yang dimanfaatkan oleh Lembaga Keuangan Syariah, termasuk Bank Pembiayaan Rakyat Syariah (BPRS), untuk memberikan kemudahan transaksi kepada UMKM dan Perorangan Berpenghasilan Rendah (PBR) secara lebih digital dan efisien. Selain itu, Permata ME menghadirkan lebih dari 200 fitur layanan keuangan dengan konsep "phygital" yang mengintegrasikan layanan digital dan fisik. Dukungan terhadap UMKM juga diperkuat melalui pemanfaatan Produk digital PermataQR.

Togmpom Commercial Banking turut mengembangkan versi terbaru Permata eBusiness yang memberikan kemudahan akses saldo rekening serta pelaksanaan berbagai transaksi perbankan,seperti pemindahbukuan, SKN, RTGS, BI-FAST, domestik transfer, remitansi, dan pengelolaan rekening. Per akhir tahun 2025, Permata ME mencatat peningkatan jumlah pengguna sebesar  $10\%$  diikuti dengan pengembangan baru berupa fitur QRIS Tap berbasis NFC, QRIS Cross Border Jepang, fitur kelola limit transaksi kartu debit, dan fitur keamanan penguncian rekening secara mandiri.

Per akhir tahun 2025, jumlah pengguna Permata e-Business mencapai 1.776 pengguna, dengan volume transaksi mencapai Rp14,7 Trilun per bulan.

To support service improvements and operational efficiency, Permata Bank continues to drive digital development across all business lines. Digital innovation is also leveraged in the development of sustainable financial products to provide inclusive, fair, formal, and responsible financial services for customers and the Indonesian public. Permata Bank's digital products can be accessed through QR Pay, API banking, mobile banking, PermataNet internet banking, digital value chain, model branches, and the Permata ME super app.

Permata Bank's digital products and services are developed in line with user functions and needs. One example is application programming interface (API) banking, or PermataAPI, which is utilized by Sharia Financial Institutions, including Sharia Rural Banks (BPRS), to provide convenience in conducting transactions to MSMEs and Low-Income Individuals in a more digital and efficiently. In addition, Permata ME provides more than 200 financial service features under a "phygital" concept that integrates digital and physical services. Support for MSMEs is also strengthened through the use of PermataQR digital products.

The Commercial Banking segment also developed the latest version of Permata eBusiness, providing easier access to account balances and enabling various banking transactions such as internal transfers, SKN, RTGS, BI FAST, domestic transfers, remittances, and account management. As of the end of 2025 Permata ME recorded a 10 percent increase in the number of users, followed by new enhancements including the NFC based QRIS Tap feature QRIS Cross Border Japan debit card transaction limit management and a self service account locking security feature.

By the end of 2025, the number of Permata e-Business users will reach 1,776, with a transaction volume of IDR 14.7 trillion per month.
