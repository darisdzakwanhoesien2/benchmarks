# Biaya Pengelolaan Lingkungan
Environmental Management Costs

Pada tahun pelaporan 2025, Permata Bank merealisasikan biaya pengelolaan lingkungan sebesar Rp123.923.400 yang berasal dari pelaksanaan pengelolaan lingkungan di 12 kantor utama. Alokasi biaya tersebut mencakup kegiatan pengelolaan sampah sebagai bagian dari upaya Bank dalam meminimalkan dampak lingkungan dari aktivitas operasional sehari-hari. [F.4]

During the 2025 reporting period, Permata Bank incurred environmental management costs of IDR 123,923,400, arising from the implementation of environmental management activities across 12 main offices. This cost allocation includes environmental monitoring and testing activities, as well as waste management, as part of the Bank's efforts to minimize the environmental impacts of its day-to-day operations.
