# Cybersecurity

Seiring meningkatnya digitalisasi perbankan, Permata Bank memperkuat pengelolaan keamanan siber melalui penerapan kontrol internal yang efektif dan sesuai dengan ketentuan yang berlaku. Bank berupaya memitigasi risiko keamanan informasi yang dapat berdampak pada system, proses, sumber daya manusia, dan pihak ketiga, serta memastikan risiko tersebut tetap berada dalam batas yang terkendali.

Sebagai wujud komitmen terhadap ketahanan siber, Bank memiliki Cyber Security Program, yang berfokus pada penguatan Tata Kelola, Kapabilitas serta keamanan dan ketahanan siber (KKS) sebagai bagian dari upaya menjaga keberlangsungan layanan, perlindungan data, dan stabilitas operasional perusahaan. Program ini secara berkala di bahas pada IT Steering Committee (ITSC) Selain itu, Bank telah memperoleh sertifikasi ISO 27001:2022 Sistem Manajemen Keamanan Informasi pada lingkup Pengembangan dan Operasional Core Banking System. Bank juga melakukan penilaian tahunan atas tingkat maturitas ketahanan dan keamanan siber berdasarkan SEOJK Nomor 29/SEOJK.03/2022 tentang Ketahanan dan Keamanan Siber bagi Bank Umum, dan PADG 24 Tahun 2024 tentang Keamanan Sistem Informasi dan Ketahanan Siber bagi Penyelenggara Sistem Pembayaran dengan telah yang dilaporkan kepada OJK dan Bank Indonesia.

Pengelolaan risiko keamanan siber didelegasikan kepada Unit Kerja Information Security Risk Management di bawah Direktorat Risiko sebagai Lini Pertahanan Kedua, yang bekerja sama dengan Departemen Cyber Security Operations dan Technology Risk Governance &amp; PMO di bawah Direktorat Technology sebagai Lini Pertahanan Pertama. Kedua unit secara proaktif melakukkan identifikasi, pemantauan, dan evaluasi risiko keamanan informasi, serta melaporkan potensi maupun insiden kepada Komite Risiko. Risiko keamanan siber diintegrasikan dengan risiko lainnya dan dikelola melalui kerangka enterprise risk management framework yang diturunkan ke dalam risk type framework, termasuk risiko keamanan informasi.

As banking digitalization continues to expand, Permata Bank strengthens cybersecurity management by implementing effective internal controls in line with applicable requirements. The Bank seeks to mitigate information security risks that may affect systems, processes, human resources, and third parties, and to ensure these risks remain within controlled limits.

As a manifestation of its commitment to cyber resilience, the Bank has established a Cyber Security Program focused on strengthening governance capabilities as well as cyber security and resilience as part of its efforts to ensure service continuity data protection and operational stability. The program is regularly discussed at the IT Steering Committee (ITSC). In addition the Bank has obtained ISO 27001:2022 Information Security Management System certification covering the development and operations of the Core Banking System. The Bank also conducts an annual assessment of its cyber resilience and security maturity level in accordance with SEOJK Number 29/SEOJK.03/2022 concerning Cyber Resilience and Security for Commercial Banks issued by Otoritas Jasa Keuangan and PADG 24 of 2024 concerning Information System Security and Cyber Resilience for Payment System Providers issued by Bank Indonesia with the results reported to Financial Services Authority and Bank of Indonesia.

Cybersecurity risk management is delegated to the Information Security Risk Management unit under the Risk Directorate as the Second Line of Defense, working in coordination with the Cyber Security Operations Department and Technology Risk Governance &amp; PMO under the Technology Directorate as the First Line of Defense. Both units proactively identify, monitor, and evaluate information security risks, and report potential issues and incidents to the Risk Committee. Cybersecurity risk is integrated with other risks and managed through the enterprise risk management framework, which is cascaded into the risk type framework, including information security risk.
