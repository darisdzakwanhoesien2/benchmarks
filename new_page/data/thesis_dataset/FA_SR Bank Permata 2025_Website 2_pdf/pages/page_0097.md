# 4. Permata CERITA (Cinta dan Edukasi daRI kiTA)

Sebagai program unggulan Permata Hati, CERITA dirancang untuk memperluas literasi keuangan bagi siswa usia sekolah, mulai dari TK hingga SMA, termasuk Sekolah Luar Biasa (SLB). Program ini dilaksanakan secara serentak di berbagai kantor cabang Permata Bank di Indonesia dengan melibatkan ratusan hingga ribuan EVO sebagai penggerak utama kegiatan.

![img-86.jpeg](img-86.jpeg)

Melalui CERITA, Permata Bank mendorong pembentukan budaya menabung dan peningkatan kesadaran keuangan sejak dini dengan memperkenalkan pengetahuan dasar mengenai uang, perbankan, serta pentingnya pelestarian lingkungan. Program ini sekaligus menjadi bentuk dukungan Bank terhadap Gerakan Nasional Cerdas Keuangan (GENCARKAN) yang diinisiasi OJK.

![img-87.jpeg](img-87.jpeg)

In its implementation, volunteers are directly involved in various activities, including financial literacy education in schools, support for improving educational facilities, and environmental conservation activities. Environmental activities include tree planting in school areas, environmental conservation such as greening, coral reef planting, sea turtle hatchling release, and mass waste collection activities. In addition, volunteers also act as educators in the BANGGA financial education program for MSME empowerment and other relevant programs. Throughout 2025, a total of 3,720 PermataBankers participated as volunteers.

Volunteer participation is appreciated through the awarding of Smile Points according to the type and level of involvement in activities. All accumulated Smile Points are converted, where every 15 points are converted into 1 educational scholarship for one underprivileged child each year.

# 4. Permata CERITA

As a flagship program of Permata Hati, CERITA is designed to expand financial literacy for school age students, from kindergarten to senior high school, including Special Needs Schools. This program is conducted simultaneously across various Permata Bank branch offices in Indonesia by involving hundreds to thousands of volunteers as the main drivers of activities.

![img-88.jpeg](img-88.jpeg)

Through CERITA, Permata Bank encourages the formation of a saving culture and increased financial awareness from an early age by introducing basic knowledge about money, banking, and the importance of environmental preservation. This program also serves as the Bank's support for the National Financial Literacy Movement initiated by Financial Services Authority.
