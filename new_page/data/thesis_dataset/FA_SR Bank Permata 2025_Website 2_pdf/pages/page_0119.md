Sustainability Governance

|  Climate Risk Analytics | 1. Menyusun kerangka kerja, metodologi, kebijakan, dan/atau prosedur manajemen risiko terkait iklim.
2. Mengintegrasikan pengelolaan risiko terkait iklim ke dalam kerangka kerja manajemen risiko Bank secara keseluruhan.
3. Mengembangkan metodologi untuk identifikasi, pengukuran, pengendalian, dan pemantauan termasuk pelaporan risiko terkait iklim dengan tepat, komprehensif, dan efektif.
4. Melaksanakan analisis skenario iklim dan/atau uji stres secara berkala untuk menilai dampak risiko terkait iklim terhadap Bank dan keterkaitannya dengan risiko utama lainnya (seperti risiko kredit, risiko pasar, risiko likuiditas, risiko operasional), serta dampaknya terhadap permodalan Bank.
5. Menyusun kebijakan atau prosedur terkait emisi dari aktivitas pembiayaan.
6. Secara aktif melakukan kajian dan pengembangan penerapan manajemen risiko terkait iklim sesuai dengan standar dan praktik umum yang diterapkan dalam industri. | 1. Develops frameworks, methodologies, policies, and/or procedures for climate risk management.
2. Integrates climate-related risk management into the Bank's overall risk management framework.
3. Develops methodologies for accurate, comprehensive, and effective identification, measurement, control, monitoring, and reporting of climate-related risks.
4. Conducts periodic climate scenario analysis and/or stress testing to assess the impacts of climate-related risks on the Bank and their linkages to other key risks (such as credit, market, liquidity, and operational risks), including impacts on the Bank's capital.
5. Develops policies or procedures related to emissions from financing activities.
6. Actively reviews and develops climate risk management implementation in line with standards and common industry practices.  |

|  Sustainability Culture Network | 1. Mempromosikan budaya keberlanjutan di wilayah kerja masing-masing.
2. Menyusun inisiatif keberlanjutan sesuai cakupan area kerja masing-masing network.
3. Menyampaikan hasil pelaksanaan program keberlanjutan kepada Divisi Keberlanjutan.
4. Memberikan masukan terkait kebutuhan sumber daya (orang, sistem, infrastruktur) untuk implementasi keberlanjutan. | 1. Promotes sustainability culture within each network's respective working area.
2. Develops sustainability initiatives aligned with each network's scope of work.
3. Reports sustainability program implementation results to the Sustainability Division.
4. Provides input on resource needs (people, systems, infrastructure) for sustainability implementation.  |
