Integrasi aspek keberlanjutan dalam tata kelola Bank juga didorong oleh kesadaran Bahwa kegiatan pembiayaan berpotensi membawa risiko lingkungan dan sosial. Permata Bank menyadari Bahwa penyaluran kredit memiliki potensi paparan risiko lingkungan dan sosial. Risiko tersebut dapat timbul secara langsung maupun tidak langsung, meliputi kerusakan hutan, penurunan keanekaragaman hayati, degradasi lingkungan laut dan sumber daya air, serta risiko sosial berupa pelanggaran hak asasi manusia dan hak-hak tenaga kerja.

Permata Bank menjalankan fungsi keberlanjutan melalui struktur organisasi dan mekanisme tata kelola yang berlaku. Divisi Sustainability berada di bawah Direktorat Corporate Affairs &amp; Sustainability untuk mendukung koordinasi dan pengambilan keputusan. Selain itu, Bank menerapkan terms of reference (TOR) dalam pengaturan keanggotaan Komite Keberlanjutan. Komite ini beranggotakan para Direktur yang relevan dalam upaya keberlanjutan Bank. Dalam pelaksanaannya, Komite Keberlanjutan dapat melibatkan Kepala Divisi lainnya sesuai dengan topik yang dibahas.

The integration of sustainability aspects into the Bank's governance is also driven by the awareness that financing activities may entail environmental and social risks. Permata Bank recognises that lending activities have the potential to expose the Bank to environmental and social risks, which may arise either directly or indirectly, including deforestation, biodiversity loss, degradation of marine environments and water resources, as well as social risks such as violations of human rights and labour rights.

Permata Bank carries out its sustainability function through the existing organisational structure and governance mechanisms. The Sustainability Division operates under the Corporate Affairs &amp; Sustainability Directorate to support coordination and decision-making related to sustainability. In addition, the Bank has established terms of reference (TOR) and membership arrangements for the Sustainability Committee. This committee comprises directors relevant to the Bank's sustainability efforts. In its implementation, the Committee may involve other Division Heads depending on the topics discussed.
