# 2. Kampanye Digital

Penyampaian informasi K3L juga dilakukan melalui media digital signage yang terpasang di Kantor Pusat WTC II &amp; Kantor Operasional Bintaro.

Selain kedua media tersebut, PermataBankers dapat melaporkan insiden atau potensi bahaya terkait pekerjaan melalui formulir pelaporan insiden.

Permata Bank menyediakan wadah kolaboratif antara manajemen dan PermataBankers melalui Panitia Pembina Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (P2K3) atau Komite K3 untuk mengoptimalkan penerapan K3. Pada tahun 2025, Komite K3 beranggotakan 124 orang yang tersebar di 10 kantor utama dan dipimpin oleh Manajemen. Komite ini secara rutin menyelenggarakan pertemuan kuartalan sebagai sarana partisipasi dan konsultasi PermataBankers dalam pengembangan, implementasi, serta evaluasi SMK3. Setiap hasil diskusi dilaporkan kepada pihak berwenang guna memastikan kesesuaian pelaksanaan program K3 dengan prosedur yang berlaku. [403-4]

# Pelatihan K3 [403-5]

## OHS Training

Permata Bank meningkatkan pemahaman dan kesadaran K3 melalui penyelenggaraan pelatihan K3 bagi PermataBankers, termasuk pelatihan wajib bagi petugas tertentu sesuai dengan penunjukan di masing-masing kantor. Materi K3 juga dikembangkan dalam bentuk modul e-learning yang dapat diakses melalui portal internal Permata Bank dan platform PEARL. Pada tahun 2025, Bank melaksanakan simulasi evakuasi secara terbatas serta pelatihan Tanggap Darurat di 185 kantor cabang.

# 2. Digital Campaigns

HSE information is also conveyed through digital signage installed at the WTC II Main Office &amp; Bintaro Operational Office.

In addition to these two channels, PermataBankers can report incidents or potential work-related hazards through an incident reporting form.

Permata Bank provides a collaborative forum between management and PermataBankers through the Occupational Safety and Health Committee, or K3 Committee, to optimize HSE implementation. In 2025, the K3 Committee consisted of 124 members across 10 main offices and was led by Management. The Committee routinely held quarterly meetings as a forum for PermataBankers' participation and consultation in the development, implementation, and evaluation of the OHSMS. The outcomes of discussions were reported to the authorized parties to ensure alignment of the HSE program implementation with applicable procedures. [403-4]

Permata Bank enhances OHS understanding and awareness by providing OHS training for PermataBankers, including mandatory training for designated officers based on their assignments at each office. OHS materials are also developed in the form of e-learning modules accessible through Permata Bank's internal portal and the PEARL platform. In 2025, the Bank conducted limited evacuation drills and Emergency Response training at 185 branch offices.
