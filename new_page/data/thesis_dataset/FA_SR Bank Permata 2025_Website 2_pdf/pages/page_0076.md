# Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja (K3) [F.21]
Occupational Health and Safety (OHS)

## Sistem Manajemen Kesehatan dan Keselamatan K3 (SMK3) [403-1][403-8]
Occupational Health and Safety Management System (OHSMS)

Permata Bank telah menerapkan Sistem Manajemen Kesehatan dan Keselamatan Kerja (SMK3) yang mencakup seluruh (100%) PermataBankers sebagaimana diatur dalam Kebijakan Kesehatan, Keselamatan Kerja, dan Lingkungan (K3L). Penerapan SMK3 bertujuan untuk mewujudkan lingkungan kerja yang layak, aman, dan nyaman bagi karyawan, nasabah, mitra, serta pemangku kepentingan lainnya. Implementasi SMK3 dikelola oleh Departemen Security, Safety &amp; Support Management (SSSM) di bawah Divisi Corporate Real Estate Services (CRES) dalam Direktorat Finance and Sharia Business Unit.

Permata Bank implements an Occupational Health and Safety Management System (OHSMS) covering all (100%) PermataBankers, as set out in the Occupational Health, Safety, and Environment Policy. The implementation of OHSMS aims to create a proper, safe, and comfortable working environment for employees, customers, partners, and other stakeholders. OHSMS implementation is managed by the Security, Safety and Support Management (SSSM) Department under the Corporate Real Estate Services (CRES) Division within the Finance and Sharia Business Unit Directorate.

## Identifikasi Risiko K3 [403-2]
OHS Risk Identification

Permata Bank melaksanakan pengelolaan K3 sesuai dengan Kebijakan K3L melalui identifikasi bahaya dan ancaman kerja, penilaian dan penanganan risiko K3, keamanan (security), serta kelangsungan usaha (business continuity planning/BCP). Penerapan ini mendukung pencegahan kecelakaan kerja, penyakit akibat kerja, dan kerusakan lingkungan. Selama periode pelaporan, identifikasi bahaya dilakukan melalui Building Risk Assessment (BRA) dan HSE Management Audit (HMA) di 16 kantor cabang utama. Bank mencatat 41 temuan BRA, 4 temuan HMA dan seluruhnya (100%) telah diselesaikan di akhir tahun 2025.

Permata Bank manages occupational health and safety (OHS) in accordance with its OHS and Environment (HSE) Policy through the identification of workplace hazards and threats, OHS risk assessment and mitigation, security, and business continuity planning (BCP). This implementation supports the prevention of workplace accidents, occupational diseases, and environmental damage. During the reporting period, hazard identification was conducted through Building Risk Assessments (BRAs) and HSE Management Audits (HMAs) at 16 main branch offices. The Bank recorded 41 BRAs findings and 4 HMAs findings, with all (100%) completed by the end of 2025.
