# Kepatuhan terhadap Hukum dan Peraturan [2-27]

Compliance with Laws and Regulations

Permata Bank menerapkan Kebijakan Kepatuhan yang wajib dipatuhi oleh seluruh karyawan. Pengawasan atas pelaksanaan fungsi kepatuhan dilakukan oleh Direktur Hukum dan Kepatuhan, dengan dukungan Divisi Compliance Regulatory Affairs Advisory, Compliance Monitoring &amp; Training, Anti Money Laundering (AML), serta Cross Border Compliance Advisory. Masing-masing divisi memiliki tugas dan tanggung jawab yang ditetapkan untuk memastikan kepatuhan Bank terhadap peraturan yang berlaku.

Kesadaran dan budaya kepatuhan di Permata Bank dibangun melalui program pembelajaran yang berkelanjutan. Bank secara rutin menyelenggarakan pelatihan wajib berbasis e-learning mengenai budaya kepatuhan bagi seluruh karyawan setiap tahunnya. Upaya ini diperkuat dengan pelaksanaan legal sharing session oleh Divisi Litigasi yang bertujuan meningkatkan pemahaman karyawan atas prinsip prudential banking dan mendorong kepatuhan yang konsisten terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan, ketentuan regulator, serta kebijakan dan prosedur internal.

Untuk memastikan kepatuhan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan yang ditetapkan oleh otoritas pengawas keuangan, termasuk Otoritas Jasa Keuangan dan Bank Indonesia, Permata Bank melalui Divisi Compliance and Regulatory Affairs menjalankan serangkaian aktivitas pengelolaan kepatuhan yang terstruktur, meliputi:

1. Pelaksanaan gap analysis atas peraturan baru yang diterbitkan regulator serta sosialisasi kepada unit kerja terkait untuk memastikan pemenuhan kewajiban sesuai jangka waktu yang ditetapkan, sebagaimana diatur dalam Department Operating Instruction (DOI) Administrasi dan Distribusi Surat serta Pemantauan, Distribusi, dan Sosialisasi Peraturan Regulator.
2. Peninjauan secara berkala atas kebijakan dan prosedur Bank guna memastikan kesesuaian dengan ketentuan yang berlaku, dengan mengacu pada Kebijakan dan Prosedur Panduan Tata Kelola Dokumen.
3. Reviu atas produk Bank, baik yang telah berjalan maupun produk baru, termasuk pelaksanaan pelaporan dan/atau perizinan kepada regulator sesuai Kebijakan dan Prosedur Pengembangan Produk Bank.
4. Pemberian konsultasi kepada unit kerja terkait dalam rangka pemenuhan ketentuan regulator yang berlaku.

Permata Bank implements a Compliance Policy that all employees must adhere to. Oversight of the compliance function is carried out by the Director of Legal and Compliance, with support from the Compliance Regulatory Affairs Advisory, Compliance Monitoring &amp; Training, Anti-Money Laundering (AML), and Cross-Border Compliance Advisory Divisions. Each division has designated duties and responsibilities to ensure the Bank's compliance with applicable regulations.

Compliance awareness and culture at Permata Bank are built through continuous learning programs. The Bank regularly conducts mandatory e-learning on compliance culture for all employees each year. This effort is reinforced by the Litigation Division's legal sharing sessions, which aim to improve employees' understanding of prudential banking principles and encourage consistent compliance with laws and regulations, regulatory requirements, and internal policies and procedures.

To ensure compliance with laws and regulations stipulated by financial supervisory authorities, including the Financial Services Authority (OJK) and Bank of Indonesia, Permata Bank, through its Compliance and Regulatory Affairs Division, carries out a series of structured compliance management activities, including:

1. Conducting gap analyses on new regulations issued by regulators and disseminating them to relevant work units to ensure compliance with established timeframes, as stipulated in the Department Operating Instruction (DOI) on Administration and Distribution of Letters and Monitoring, Distribution, and Dissemination of Regulatory Regulations.
2. Periodic reviews of the Bank's policies and procedures to ensure compliance with applicable regulations, with reference to the Document Governance Guidelines Policy and Procedures.
3. Reviews of the Bank's products, both existing and new, including reporting and/or licensing to regulators in accordance with the Bank's Product Development Policy and Procedures.
4. Providing consultations to relevant work units regarding compliance with applicable regulatory requirements.
