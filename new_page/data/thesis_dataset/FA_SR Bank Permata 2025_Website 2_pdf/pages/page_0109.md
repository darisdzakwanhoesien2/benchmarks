# BANTUAN BENCANA

Menanggapi bencana yang terjadi di Aceh, Sumatera Utara, dan Sumatera Barat, Permata Hati berkolaborasi dengan Unit Pelayanan Zakat dan Dana Kebajikan (UPZDK) Permata Bank Syariah, dan serikat pekerja, berhasil mengumpulkan dan menyalurkan bantuan senilai lebih dari Rp1,9 miliar disalurkan untuk mendukung pemulihan masyarakat terdampak bencana di Aceh dan sejumlah wilayah Sumatra.

![img-103.jpeg](img-103.jpeg)

Bantuan ini disalurkan melalui beberapa mitra, antara lain:

1.  BAZNAS RI

Penyaluran bantuan melalui UPZDK Permata Bank Syariah senilai Rp1,4 miliar kepada BAZNAS RI untuk mendukung penanganan bencana di berbagai wilayah di Sumatra. Penyerahan bantuan telah dilakukan secara simbolis oleh Bapak Rudy Basyir Ahmad selaku Direktur Keuangan &amp; Unit Usaha Syariah Permata Bank.

2.  Dompet Dhuafa

Penyaluran bantuan melalui UPZDK Permata Bank Syariah senilai Rp300 juta kepada Dompet Dhuafa, dalam bentuk dukungan logistik, penggerakkan relawan, tenaga medis, dan tim respon cepat yang bekerja langsung di wilayah terdampak sejak fase awal darurat hingga masa pemulihan.

3.  Human Initiative (HI)

Penyaluran bantuan senilai Rp100 juta dari hasil penggalangan dana melalui partisipasi karyawan, program Permata Hati, UPZDK Permata Bank Syariah, dan SPBP kepada Human Initiative. Dana ini akan digunakan untuk memenuhi kebutuhan dasar di tiga wilayah prioritas: Pidie Jaya (Aceh), Langkat (Sumatra Utara), dan Kota Padang (Sumatra Barat). Penyaluran bantuan dilakukan bersama mitra Human Initiative, dengan fokus pada distribusi bahan makanan dan keperluan kebersihan.

# DISASTER RELIEF

In response to disasters in Aceh, North Sumatra, and West Sumatra, Permata Hati, in collaboration with the Zakat and Benevolent Funds Service Unit (UPZDK) of Permata Bank Syariah and the labor union, raised and distributed more than IDR 1.9 billion to support recovery efforts for affected communities in Aceh and several regions in Sumatra.

![img-104.jpeg](img-104.jpeg)

Assistance was distributed through several partners, including:

1.  BAZNAS RI

Assistance was distributed through UPZDK Permata Bank Syariah in the amount of IDR 1.4 billion to BAZNAS RI to support disaster response in various regions in Sumatra. The handover was conducted symbolically by Mr. Rudy Basyir Ahmad, Director of Finance and Sharia Business Unit of Permata Bank.

2.  Dompet Dhuafa

Assistance was distributed through UPZDK Permata Bank Syariah in the amount of IDR 300 million to Dompet Dhuafa, in the form of logistics support, volunteer mobilization, medical personnel, and rapid response teams working directly in affected areas from the initial emergency phase through recovery.

3.  Human Initiative (HI)

Assistance amounting to IDR 100 million, raised through employee participation, Permata Hati programs, UPZDK Permata Bank Syariah, and SPBP, was distributed to Human Initiative. The funds are used to meet basic needs in three priority areas: Pidie Jaya (Aceh), Langkat (North Sumatra), and Padang City (West Sumatra). Distribution is carried out with Human Initiative, focusing on food supplies and hygiene necessities.
