# Evaluasi Keamanan Produk [F.27][F.29]

# Product Safety Evaluation

Permata Bank memastikan seluruh produk dan layanan digital memenuhi ketentuan regulator sebelum dipasarkan, di mana berdasarkan risk assessment sesuai dengan ketentuan Bank Indonesia, dilakukan audit keamanan TI, untuk tingkat risiko sedang dan tinggi. Evaluasi dilakukan melalui proses penilaian risiko (risk assessment), audit keamanan TI, pengujian keamanan (penetration testing), evaluasi proses dan prosedur operational standar, serta Security Operation Center (SOC) yang beroperasi secara berkelanjutan selama 24 jam sehari dan 7 hari seminggu. Di samping itu, pengujian keamanan TI juga melibatkan pihak ketiga independen yang merupakan anggota Self-Regulatory Organization (SRO) atau Asosiasi Sistem Pembayaran Indonesia (ASPI) yang ditunjuk oleh Bank Indonesia.

Permata Bank juga menerapkan standar keamanan dan perizinan Bank Indonesia dan OJK dalam pengelolaan produk dan layanan baru, termasuk kewajiban kepemilikan laporan perizinan dan tidak adanya temuan terbuka dari penetration test serta audit TI. Layanan yang telah memperoleh izin dimonitor sesuai prosedur SLA TI dan mekanisme change management, yang mensyaratkan pelaksanaan security acceptance test sebelum digunakan kepada dilakukan perubahan. Dengan mekanisme tersebut, sepanjang tahun 2025, tidak terdapat produk dan layanan keuangan yang ditarik dari program penjualan.

Permata Bank ensures that all digital products and services comply with regulatory requirements before being launched to the market. Based on risk assessments in accordance with provisions issued by Bank Indonesia, IT security audits are conducted for products classified as medium and high risk. Evaluations are carried out through risk assessment processes, IT security audits, penetration testing, reviews of operational processes and standard operating procedures, and continuous monitoring by the Security Operation Center (SOC) operating 24 hours a day 7 days a week. In addition IT security testing also involves independent third parties that are members of a Self Regulatory Organization (SRO) or the Asosiasi Sistem Pembayaran Indonesia appointed by Bank Indonesia.

Permata Bank also applies Bank of Indonesia and Financial Services Authority security standards and licensing requirements in managing new products and services, including the requirement to hold the relevant licensing documentation and to have no open findings from penetration testing and IT audits. Licensed services are monitored in accordance with IT SLA procedures and change management mechanisms, which require a security acceptance test to be conducted before use or before any changes are implemented. With these mechanisms in place, throughout 2025 no financial products or services were withdrawn from sales programs.
