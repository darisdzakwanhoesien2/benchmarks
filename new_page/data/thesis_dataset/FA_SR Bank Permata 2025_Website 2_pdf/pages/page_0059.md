Bank menetapkan tahun 2024 sebagai tahun dasar pengukuran emisi guna menjadi rujukan dalam perencanaan program penurunan emisi. Berangkat dari baseline tersebut, Bank merancang sasaran pengurangan energi dan emisi pada kegiatan operasional internal serta aktivitas debitur. Pada tahun 2025, Bank menerapkan inisiatif-inisiatif penurunan emisi, seperti penggantian lampu LED, pengaturan AC, kampanye hemat energi, dan sosialisasi budaya keberlanjutan, sehingga penurunan emisi scope 1 dan scope 2 di 2025 mencapai  $4,90\%$ . [302-5]

Ke depan, upaya penurunan emisi akan terus dilakukan melalui inisiatif-inisiatif internal (termasuk dalam identifikasi sumber emisi utama), maupun skema carbon credit dan pembelian REC. Insisiatif-insiatif ini dilakukan untuk mencapai target Net Zero Emission (NZE) jangka menengah yaitu Net Zero Cakupan 1 &amp; 2 dari aktivitas operasional pada 2030, dan untuk jangka panjang mencapai NZE dari aktivitas pembiayaan pada 2052. Bank saat ini sedang menyusun peta jalan dari NZE sebagai bagian dari strategi jangka menengah untuk menurunkan emisi operasional.

Bank set 2024 as the emissions measurement baseline year to serve as a reference for planning emissions reduction programs. Building on this baseline, the Bank designs energy and emissions reduction targets covering internal operational activities as well as debtors' activities. In 2025, the Bank implemented emissions reduction initiatives, such as replacing lights with LED, optimizing air-conditioning settings, conducting energy-saving campaigns, and socializing a sustainability culture, resulting in a  $4.90\%$  reduction in scope 1 and scope 2 emissions in 2025. [302-5]

Going forward, emissions reduction efforts will continue through internal initiatives (including the identification of key emissions sources), as well as through carbon credit schemes and the purchase of RECs. The Bank will also continue to strengthen sustainable operational practices. These efforts support the achievement of Permata Bank's medium-term NZE targets, namely Net Zero for Scope 1 and Scope 2 operational emissions by 2030, and its long-term target of achieving Net Zero Emissions from financing activities by 2052. The bank is currently developing a roadmap for NZE as part of its medium-term strategy to reduce operational emissions.

# INISIATIF PENGURANGAN KONSUMSI ENERGI DAN EMISI GRK YANG DIHASILKAN (A1B, A1C, A1D, A2, A1D, A2B, A1C)

Initiatives to Reduce Energy Consumption and Resulting GHG Emissions

![img-62.jpeg](img-62.jpeg)

Bank terus melakukan berbagai upaya untuk mengefsienkan penggunaan energi listrik guna menekan emisi dari aktivitas operasional. Sejumlah langkah penghematan energi dijalankan secara konsisten, antara lain:

- Melakukan kampanye penghematan energi melalui digital signage, email blast dan dig kepada seluruh karyawan
- Secara bertahap melakukan penggantian lampu Non LED menjadi lampu LED di seluruh gedung
- Melakukan penyesuaian waktu nyala lampu signage
- Penggantian kendaraan Board menjadi EV
- Penyesuaian waktu nyala peralatan listrik (AC, lift lampu) lebih lambat 30 menit di gedung Bintaro sektor 7 Tangerang.
- Sertifikasi green building untuk Gedung Bintaro sektor 7 Tangerang Selatan dan Merdeka Bandung.
- Penerapan tempat sampah terpilah (organik &amp; nonorganik) di gedung WTC II, Bintaro, Hayam Wuruk dan Merdeka
- Penggunaan water recycle di gedung Bintaro sektor 7 Tangerang
- Penggunaan solar panel di gedung Bintaro sektor 7 Tangerang

The Bank continues to pursue various efforts to improve electricity efficiency in order to reduce emissions from operational activities. A number of energy-saving measures are implemented on an ongoing basis, including:

- Conducting an energy-saving campaign through digital signage, email blasts, and digital displays for all employees.
- Gradually replacing non-LED lights with LED lights throughout the building.
- Adjusting signage light turn-on times.
- Replacing board vehicles with electric vehicles.
- Adjusting electrical equipment (AC, elevators, and lighting) turn-on times by 30 minutes in the Bintaro Sector 7 building in Tangerang.
- Green building certification for the Bintaro Sector 7 building in South Tangerang and Merdeka building in Bandung.
- Implementing separate waste bins (organic and non-organic) in the WTC II, Bintaro, Hayam Wuruk, and Merdeka buildings.
- Implementing water recycling in the Bintaro Sector 7 building in Tangerang.
- Implementing solar panels in the Bintaro Sector 7 building in Tangerang.
