In 2025, Permata Bank implemented the Sustainable Finance Action Plan (RAKB) for the 2024–2028 period through a series of initiatives divided into several key activity segments, such as:

Increased financing for Environmentally Friendly Business Activities (KUBL) by 48.3% compared to 2024. |
