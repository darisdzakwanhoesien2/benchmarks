The data shows that most of Permata Bank's business segments delivered strong performance, hitting all RBB targets by year-end 2025. This achievement highlights the success of the Bank's strategic initiatives and demonstrates its strong operational resilience throughout the year. At the same time, the Bank monitored key performance indicators that offered room for further improvement, particularly in loan growth, which remained a priority while adhering to prudent risk management principles. Strengthening these areas is expected to reinforce the foundation for sustainable growth and enhance the Bank's competitiveness amid an increasingly dynamic and challenging banking landscape.
