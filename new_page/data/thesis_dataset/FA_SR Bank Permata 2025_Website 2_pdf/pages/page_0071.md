# PENGEMBANGAN KOMPETENSI DAN KARIER PERMATABANKERS [F.22][401-2]

Untuk mendukung pencapaian kinerja unggul, Permata Bank mengusung Employee Value Proposition (EVP) Learn, Grow, and Make a Difference sebagai landasan dalam pengembangan PermataBankers. Bank pun menerapkan strategi pengembangan kompetensi yang terstruktur gunna memastikan pencapaian kinerja yang berkelanjutan.

Pengembangan dan pelaksanaan program pelatihan di Permata Bank dikoordinasikan oleh Direktorat People &amp; Culture dengan mengacu pada kerangka manajemen talenta dan strategi Bank. Dalam pelaksanaannya, Direktorat People &amp; Culture bekerja sama dengan unit kerja terkait untuk melakukan analisis kebutuhan pelatihan sehingga program yang diselenggarakan dapat mendukung penguatan kompetensi yang sesuai dengan kebutuhan Bank.

Dalam rangka membangun kapabilitas SDM yang berkelanjutan, Bank memastikan kesetaraan kesempatan kerja dan mendorong PermataBankers untuk mengambil inisiatif dalam pengembangan diri, baik soft skill maupun hard skill. Inisiatif tersebut mencakup penilaian kompetensi, perencanaan pengembangan individu, serta koordinasi dengan atasan agar rencana pengembangan dapat diimplementasikan secara optimal. [F.18]

# COMPETENCY AND CAREER DEVELOPMENT OF PERMATABANKERS [F.22][401-2]

To support strong performance, Permata Bank adopts the Employee Value Proposition (EVP) "Learn, Grow, and Make a Difference" as the foundation for PermataBankers' development. The Bank also implements a structured competency development strategy to support sustainable performance.

The development and implementation of training programs at Permata Bank are coordinated by the People &amp; Culture Directorate, guided by the Bank's talent management framework and strategy. In its implementation, the People &amp; Culture Directorate collaborates with relevant work units to conduct training needs analysis so that the programs implemented can support competency development that aligns with the Bank's needs.

To build sustainable HR capabilities, the Bank ensures equal employment opportunities and encourages PermataBankers to take initiative in self-development, both soft skills and hard skills. These initiatives include competency assessments, individual development planning, and coordination with supervisors to ensure development plans are implemented effectively. [F.18]
