# PENINGKATAN SECURITY AWARENESS DAN KAPASITAS SDM TERKAIT KEAMANAN SIBER

Permata Bank memastikan seluruh PermataBankers, termasuk Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris, memiliki pemahaman yang memadai mengenai keamanan siber. Untuk mendukung hal tersebut, Bank menyediakan publikasi materi serta pelatihan information security awareness yang diwajibkan bagi seluruh karyawan melalui portal e-learning Permata Bank. Unit Kerja Information Security Risk Management ditetapkan sebagai pihak yang bertanggung jawab dalam perumusan, pelaksanaan, dan pemantauan program peningkatan security awareness. Adapun program security awareness yang dilaksanakan antara lain:

- Publikasi materi security awareness melalui surat elektronik, digital signage, dan desktop wallpaper.
Modul e-learning security awareness dan modul khusus phishing awareness.
- Phishing simulation yang dilakukan secara berkala.

Di samping program security awareness, Permata Bank juga memfasilitasi karyawan pada unit kerja terkait keamanan siber untuk mengikuti pelatihan dan sertifikasi yang mendukung pelaksanaan tugasnya. Program pelatihan yang diberikan antara lain:

- Pelatihan softskill atau hardskill sesuai ILDP secara berkala.
- Pelatihan dan sertifikasi sesuai kebutuhan di masing-masing unit kerja, seperti CISA, CISM, CRISC, CISSP, ISO 27001, CEH, Cloud Security, Artificial Intelligence, dan Digital Forensik.

# AUDIT KEAMANAN INFORMASI

Dalam kerangka Three Lines of Defense, Satuan Kerja Audit Internal (SKAI) berperan sebagai Lini Pertahanan Ketiga yang memberikan assurance independen atas efektivitas pengendalian internal Bank, termasuk risiko siber. SKAI menilai kepatuhan manajemen dalam mengidentifikasi, menilai, dan mengelola risiko, serta mengevaluasi kecukupan desain kontrol berdasarkan Ketentuan Internal dan Ketentuan Regulator yang berlaku. Audit atas keamanan informasi dilakukan atas aspek pengelolaan infrastruktur TI, keamanan data, dan risiko siber. Rencana Kerja Tahunan SKAI ditetapkan dengan persetujuan dari Direktur Utama, Komite Audit, dan Dewan Komisaris, dengan prioritas berdasarkan risiko dari masing-masing proses dan audit wajib yang diatur oleh Regulator.

# ENHANCING SECURITY AWARENESS AND EMPLOYEE CAPACITY IN CYBERSECURITY

Permata Bank ensures that all PermataBankers, including the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners, have an adequate understanding of cybersecurity. To support this, the Bank provides publications and information security awareness training that is mandatory for all employees through Permata Bank's e-learning portal. The Information Security Risk Management unit is designated as the party responsible for designing, implementing, and monitoring the security awareness enhancement program. Security awareness programs implemented include:

- Publishing security awareness materials via email, digital signage, and desktop wallpapers.
Security awareness e-learning modules and specialized phishing awareness modules.
Periodic phishing simulations.

In addition to security awareness programs, Permata Bank also facilitates employees in cybersecurity-related units to participate in training and certifications that support their roles. Training programs include:

- Periodic soft-skill and hard-skill training in line with the ILDP.
- Training and certifications aligned with the needs of each work unit such as CISA, CISM, CRISC, CISSP, ISO 27001, CEH, Cloud Security, Artificial Intelligence, and Digital Forensics.

# INFORMATION SECURITY AUDIT

Within the Three Lines of Defense framework, the Internal Audit Unit serves as the Third Line of Defense, providing independent assurance on the effectiveness of the Bank's internal controls, including cybersecurity risk. Internal Audit Unit assesses management's compliance in identifying, assessing, and managing risks, and evaluates the adequacy of control design based on Internal Audit requirements. Audits of IT implementation are also conducted as part of the cybersecurity risk assessment. The Internal Audit Unit Annual Work Plan is determined with the approval of the President Director, Audit Committee, and Board of Commissioners with priorities based on the risks of each process and mandatory audits regulated by the Regulator.
