# Berbagi Ruang

Bertepatan dengan Hari Gajah Sedunia (World Elephant Day) pada 12 Agustus 2025, Permata Bank meluncurkan Film Dokumenter "Berbagi Ruang untuk Bukit Tigapuluh". Karya ini telah kolaborasi dengan WWF Indonesia dan KITE Entertainment, sebagai upaya membangun kesadaran publik akan pentingnya peran masyarakat dalam menjaga jalur pergerakan alami kepada koridor hidupan liar Gajah Sumatra di kawasan hutan. Program as a concrete effort to support social and environmental ecosystem conservation in the Bukit Tigapuluh area of Jambi. The program is implemented in Simerantihan Hamlet, Suo-Suo Village, Tebo Regency, and is part of the Bank's contribution to achieving the 7 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and addressing climate change.

This program aims to maintain the sustainability of the Bukit Tigapuluh ecosystem through Sumatran Elephant conservation, using an approach that respects and protects natural processes as a foundation for addressing social, environmental, and economic challenges. This initiative is also expected to support the survival of local indigenous communities, namely the Talang Mamak and Anak Dalam tribes, so they can live in harmony with wildlife in Bukit Tigapuluh.

As a follow-up to the #DenganHati for Bukit Tigapuluh Program, Permata Bank launched the "Adopt a Tree" initiative, inviting customers and the general public to participate in efforts to rehabilitate deforested land by donating trees in the Bukit Tigapuluh area. Permata Bank, in collaboration with WWF Indonesia, has created a dedicated website for the public to submit tree-planting contributions, with easy payment options available through its banking app.

Through the implementation of the #DenganHati for Bukit Tigapuluh Program and the Adopt a Tree initiative, Permata Bank strives to contribute to the achievement of the 7 SDGs, namely Goal 1 (No Poverty), Goal 4 (Quality Education), Goal 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth), Goal 10 (Reduced Inequality), Goal 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production), Goal 13 (Addressing Climate Change), and Goal 15 (Terrestrial Ecosystems).

# Sharing Space

Coinciding with World Elephant Day on 12 August 2025, Permata Bank launched a documentary film "Berbagi Ruang untuk Bukit Tigapuluh" (Sharing Space for Bukit Tigapuluh). This documentary is a collaboration piece with WWF Indonesia and KITE Entertainment, aimed at raising public awareness on the importance of community involvement in protecting natural movement pathways or wildlife corridors of the Sumatran elephant within forest areas.
