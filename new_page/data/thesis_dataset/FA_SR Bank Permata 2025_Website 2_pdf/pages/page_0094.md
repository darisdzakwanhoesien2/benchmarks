Community Empowerment for Self-Reliance

Permata Bank berkomitmen untuk berkontribusi bagi bumi dan masyarakat Indonesia, melalui pelaksanaan program-program Tanggung Jawab Sosial dan Lingkungan yang diharapkan dapat memberikan dampak positif lebih luas kepada masyarakat, nasabah dan komunitas dalam mendukung terciptanya ekonomi yang berkelanjutan di masyarakat.

Seluruh pelaksanaan program Tanggung Jawab Sosial dan Lingkungan (TJSL) tersebut dikelola melalui Unit Corporate Social &amp; Environmental Responsibility (CSER) "Permata Hati" di bawah Direktorat Corporate Affairs and Sustainability, Divisi Corporate Communication. Program-program Permata Hati dikembangkan berdasarkan tiga pilar utama, yaitu:

1. Pendidikan (Education)
2. Pemberdayaan (Empowerment)
3. Peningkatan Fasilitas dan Inklusi (Enhancement)

Unit CSER menjalin kolaborasi dengan mitra strategis dan berbagai pemangku kepentingan untuk memperkuat strategi dan pelaksanaan program agar memberikan dampak yang lebih luas bagi masyarakat. Kolaborasi tersebut diarahkan untuk:

- Meningkatkan kesadaran dan mendorong inisiatif kegiatan yang selaras dengan prinsip lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola (LST).
- Menegaskan komitmen terhadap pelestarian lingkungan guna mendukung terciptanya bumi yang sehat dan peningkatan kesejahteraan manusia.
- Mendorong literasi keuangan berkelanjutan melalui edukasi kepada masyarakat dan penguatan sinergi dengan komunitas demi membangun generasi masa depan yang berkelanjutan.

Permata Bank is committed to contributing to the earth and Indonesian society through the implementation of Social and Environmental Responsibility programs that are expected to have a broader positive impact on the community, customers, and communities in supporting the creation of a sustainable economy in society.

All implementation of the Social and Environmental Responsibility program is managed through the Corporate Social &amp; Environmental Responsibility (CSER) Unit "Permata Hati" under the Directorate of Corporate Affairs and Sustainability, Corporate Communication Division. Permata Hati programs are developed based on three main pillars, namely:

1. Education
2. Empowerment
3. Facilities Enhancement and Inclusion

The CSER Unit collaborates with strategic partners and various stakeholders to strengthen program strategy and implementation in order to deliver broader impacts for the community. These collaborations are aimed at:

- Increasing awareness and encouraging initiatives aligned with ESG principles.
- Reinforcing the Bank's commitment to environmental conservation to support a healthier planet and improved human well-being.
- Promoting sustainable financial literacy through public education and stronger collaboration with communities to build a more sustainable future generation.
