# Scholarships and Donations

Pada tahun 2025, Permata Bank menyalurkan program beasiswa dan donasi yang dilaksanakan melalui tiga subprogram, yaitu:

## 1. Beasiswa Poin Senyum

Permata Bank menginisiasi program Beasiswa Poin Senyum sebagai wujud komitmen berkelanjutan dalam mendukung akses pendidikan bagi anak-anak yang membutuhkan. Melalui program ini, partisipasi PermataBankers sebagai Employee Volunteer (EVO) dalam kegiatan TJSL dikonversikan menjadi kontribusi tidak langsung berupa beasiswa pendidikan selama satu tahun bagi satu penerima manfaat. Penyaluran Beasiswa Poin Senyum dilakukan melalui sejumlah yayasan dan organisasi pendidikan, antara lain Hoshizora Foundation, Yayasan Nara Kreatif, Red Nose Foundation, Inspiration Factory, SOS Village, serta serikat pekerja untuk putra-putri PermataBankers yang membutuhkan.

In 2025, Permata Bank distributed scholarship and donation programs implemented through three sub-programs, namely:

## 1. Poin Senyum Scholarship

Permata Bank initiated the Poin Senyum Scholarship Program as a manifestation of its ongoing commitment to supporting access to education for children in need. Through this program, the participation of PermataBankers as Employee Volunteers (EVOs) in CSER activities is converted into an indirect contribution in the form of a one-year educational scholarship for each beneficiary. The Poin Senyum Scholarship is distributed through a number of foundations and educational organisations, including the Hoshizora Foundation, Yayasan Nara Kreatif, Red Nose Foundation, Inspiration Factory, as well as the labor union for the children of PermataBankers in need.

Pada tahun 2025, Permata Hati menyalurkan beasiswa dengan total nilai Rp1 miliar kepada 773 penerima manfaat dari jenjang SD, SMP, SMA, hingga perguruan tinggi yang tersebar di Jakarta dan sekitarnya serta Yogyakarta.

In 2025, Permata Hati distributed scholarships with a total value of IDR 1 billion to 773 beneficiaries across primary, junior secondary, senior secondary, and tertiary education levels, located in Jakarta and surrounding areas as well as Yogyakarta.
