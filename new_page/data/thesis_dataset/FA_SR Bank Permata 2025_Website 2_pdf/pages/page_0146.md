## Type 2 Assurance

SRAI verifies the topic of **Product and Data Security**, and **Customer Privacy**, the Company has established internal data security policies approved by the Board of Directors, with regular reporting and monthly quality control meetings. To identify system vulnerabilities, the Company uses regular assessment forms accessible to customers. It also utilizes cyber threat intelligence tools to monitor data leaks and map threat patterns. The Company is currently exploring the use of AI and is expected to implement it to enhance service delivery and improve communication of products and services to customers. Regarding **Environmental Management and Climate Change**, the Company is still developing its Climate Risk Management Stress Test (CRMS), with initial testing conducted using proxy data. In the coming year, it plans to focus on collecting financed Greenhouse Gases (GHG) emissions data by preparing tools to obtain more accurate information. Additionally, Scope 1 and Scope 2 emission calculations have not been externally verified, and portfolio monitoring is conducted monthly by the risk team and reported to management.
