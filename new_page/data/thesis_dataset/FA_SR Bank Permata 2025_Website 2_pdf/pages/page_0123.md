Pengkajian Risiko Iklim dilakukan secara berkelanjutan sepanjang siklus hubungan dengan debitur, mulai dari proses on boarding, aplikasi dan tinjauan kredit, pemantauan dan keterlibatan berkelanjutan, hingga persetujuan produk atau bisnis baru. Dalam proses tersebut, dampak Risiko Iklim dipetakan terhadap risiko keuangan dan nonkeuangan untuk menilai potensi, signifikansi, dan tingkat konsentrasinya. [F.12]

Permata Bank juga melakukan komunikasi aktif dengan debitur serta pihak ketiga yang dianggap signifikan, termasuk pemasok barang, guna memperoleh informasi yang lebih memadai mengenai pemahaman terhadap risiko iklim, rekam jejak, komitmen, dan strategi transisi dalam mengelola risiko tersebut.

Climate Risk assessment is conducted continuously throughout the debtor relationship lifecycle, starting from the onboarding process, credit application and review, ongoing monitoring and engagement, through to the approval of new products or business activities. Within this process, the impact of Climate Risk is mapped against both financial and non-financial risks to assess its potential, materiality, and level of concentration. [F.12]

Permata Bank also engages in active communication with debtors and significant third parties, including goods and service suppliers, in order to obtain more comprehensive information regarding their understanding of climate risks, track records, commitments, and transition strategies in managing such risks.

# Pengelolaan Risiko Iklim [2-13][201-2]

# Climate Risk Management

Sebagai bagian dari penguatan pengelolaan Risiko Iklim, Permata Bank telah mengembangkan Kerangka Kerja Tipe Risiko Iklim (Climate Risk Type Framework) dan menerapkan Taksonomi untuk Keuangan Berkelanjutan Indonesia (TKBI). Penerapan kerangka tersebut didukung oleh pembentukan fungsi Climate Risk Analytics di bawah Divisi Market &amp; Enterprise Risk Management (MERM) yang berfokus pada pengembangan Climate Risk Stress Test (CRST) serta perhitungan Financed Emission. Secara tata kelola, pengelolaan Risiko Iklim berada di bawah pengawasan Direktur Risiko dan Chief of Corporate Affair &amp; Sustainability selaku Risk Framework Owner (RFO), dengan dukungan RFO PIC yang terdiri dari Division Head Market &amp; Enterprise Risk Management (MERM), Division Head Corporate, Commercial, &amp; EBB Credit Risk Management (CCE CRM), dan Division Head Sustainability.

Tahun 2025, Permata Bank melakukan kajian pilot bottom-up Climate Risk Management &amp; Scenario Analysis (CRMS) yang kedua sesuai dengan persyaratan regulator serta mengacu pada panduan yang ditetapkan oleh Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) berdasarkan skenario yang disusun oleh Network for Greening the Financial System (NGFS) yaitu skenario Net Zero 2050, Delayed Transition, dan Current Policy terhadap Risiko Transisi dan Risiko Fisik.

As part of strengthening its climate risk management, Permata Bank has developed a Climate Risk Type Framework and applies the Indonesia Sustainable Finance Taxonomy (TKBI). Implementation of this framework is supported by the establishment of a Climate Risk Analytics function under the Market and Enterprise Risk Management (MERM) Division, which focuses on developing the Climate Risk Stress Test (CRST) and calculating financed emissions. From a governance perspective, climate risk management is overseen by the Risk Director and the Chief of Corporate Affairs &amp; Sustainability as the Risk Framework Owner (RFO), supported by RFO PICs comprising the MERM Division Head; Corporate, Commercial, and EBB Credit Risk Management (CCE CRM) Division Head; and Sustainability Division Head.

In 2025, Permata Bank conducted its second pilot bottom-up Climate Risk Management &amp; Scenario Analysis (CRMS) assessment in line with regulatory requirements and referring to guidance issued by the Financial Services Authority (OJK), using scenarios developed by the Network for Greening the Financial System (NGFS), namely Net Zero 2050, Delayed Transition, and Current Policies, across Transition Risk and Physical Risk.
