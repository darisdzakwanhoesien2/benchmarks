# 5. Sosial Lainnya

UPZDK memberikan berbagai bantuan sosial untuk membantu penerima manfaatseperti bantuan tunai berupa santunan kepada anak yatim piatu, berbagi makanan buka puasa dan takjil bagi lingkungan sekitar, bantuan sembako bagi penerima manfaat yang kesulitan dalam pemenuhan kebutuhan dasar, pemberian tunjangan/insentif kepada guru yang belum memperoleh tingkat kesejahteraan, dan bantuan pemberian sedekah daging kurban kepada para penerima manfaat dan masyarakat di sekitar lingkungan Permata Bank Syariah.

# 6. Kebencanaan dan Kemanusiaan

Melalui kerjasama dengan Mitra Kolaborasi, UPZDK memberikan bantuan tanggap darurat bencana di wilayah Aceh, Sumatera Utara, dan Sumatera Barat yang terdampak banjir bandang &amp; longsor yang mengakibatkan masyarakat kehilangan/rusaknya tempat tinggal dan harta benda, kekurangan makanan dan minuman, fasilitas air bersih serta kebutuhan dasar lainnya. UPZDK memberikan bantuan berupa dapur umum, makanan siap saji, hygiene kit, perlengkapan ibu dan wanita, perlengkapan kebersihan, dan pembangunan sumur untuk mendapatkan air bersih sebagai bentuk kepedulian dan dukungan kepada masyarakat Sumatera yang terdampak bencana.

Penyaluran dana Zakat dan dana Kebajikan melalui program-program tersebut, diharapkan dapat memberikan manfaat nyata kepada penerima manfaat dan juga sebagai bentuk syiar dari Permata Bank melalui Unit Usaha Syariah yang terus berkomitmen untuk memberikan pelayanan perbankan terbaik kepada nasabah, termasuk mengembantugas sosial kepada masyarakat dalam mendukung pertumbuhan yang berkelanjutan.

These 4 Permata Bridges aim to improve accessibility and connectivity between villages, support community mobility to education, health facilities and other social infrastructure, enhance community safety, and promote more equitable and sustainable regional development.

# 5. Other Social Programs

UPZDK provides various forms of social assistance, including cash support for orphans, food distribution for iftar and takjil to surrounding communities, staple food assistance for beneficiaries facing difficulty meeting basic needs, allowances/incentives for teachers with limited welfare levels, and the distribution of qurban meat donations to beneficiaries and communities around Permata Bank Syariah.

# 6. Disaster and Humanitarian Assistance

In cooperation with collaborative partners, UPZDK provided emergency disaster response assistance in Aceh, North Sumatra, and West Sumatra, which were affected by flash floods and landslides resulting in loss and damage to homes and property, shortages of food and drinking water, limited clean water facilities, and other basic needs. Assistance included public kitchens, ready-to-eat meals, hygiene kits, supplies for mothers and women, cleaning supplies, and the construction of wells to provide clean water, as a form of care and support for disaster-affected communities in Sumatra.

The distribution of zakat and benevolent funds through these programs is expected to deliver tangible benefits to beneficiaries and serve as part of Permata Bank's social mission through the Sharia Business Unit, which remains committed to providing the best banking services to customers while fulfilling social responsibilities in support of sustainable growth.

# PEMBERDAYAAN USAHA MIKRO, KECIL, DAN MENENGAH (UMKM) [413-1]

Permata Bank menjalankan pemberdayaan Usaha Mikro, Kecil, dan Menengah (UMKM) bagi penyandang disabilitas melalui program BANGGA, yang berfokus pada pelatihan dan pendampingan kewirausahaan. Program ini diinisiasi bersama mitra strategis Precious One, sebuah yayasan kriya inklusif yang memberdayakan penyandang disabilitas, khususnya Teman Tuli, dan telah dilaksanakan secara berkelanjutan sejak tahun 2021. Inisiatif BANGGA sejalan dengan komitmen diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) Permata Bank dalam menciptakan kesempatan yang setara untuk berkarya, bersaing, dan hidup sejahtera bagi seluruh individu, khususnya penyandang disabilitas.

# MICRO, SMALL, AND MEDIUM ENTERPRISE (MSME) EMPOWERMENT [413-1]

Permata Bank empowers MSMEs for persons with disabilities through the BANGGA program, which focuses on entrepreneurship training and mentoring. The program is initiated together with a strategic partner, Precious One, an inclusive craft foundation that empowers persons with disabilities, particularly the Deaf Community, and has been implemented on an ongoing basis since 2021. The BANGGA initiative aligns with Permata Bank's diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) commitment to create equal opportunities to contribute, compete, and thrive for all individuals, especially persons with disabilities.
