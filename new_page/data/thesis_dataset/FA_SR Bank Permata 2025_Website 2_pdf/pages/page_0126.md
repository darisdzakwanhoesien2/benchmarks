2025 Sustainability Report PT Bank Permata Tbk.

Hasil CRMS di tahun 2025 menunjukkan bahwa Risiko Transisi dan Risiko Fisik berpotensi menekan kinerja keuangan Bank yang tercermin dari penurunan profit after tax (PAT) dan capital adequacy ratio (CAR). Dalam pilot bottom-up CRMS terhadap  $100\%$  portofolio, CAR diproyeksikan menurun menjadi  $28\% - 30\%$  pada horizon jangka pendek (2025-2027) dan menjadi  $28\% - 33\%$  pada horizon jangka panjang (2030-2050), dibandingkan posisi Desember 2024 sebesar  $35\%$ .

![img-115.jpeg](img-115.jpeg)

Penurunan tersebut terutama dipengaruhi oleh peningkatan expected credit loss (ECL), penurunan pendapatan bunga akibat menjigkatnya debitur bermasalah (non-performing loan/NPL), serta kerugian nilai wajar surat berharga seiring perubahan kondisi pasar.

Proyeksi dampak tersebut diperoleh melalui uji ketahanan yang diawali dengan pengelompokan debitur ke dalam subsektor prioritas berdasarkan tingkat risiko tinggi (seperti batubara, pembangkit listrik, migas, dan perkebunan), risiko menengah (seperti industri kimia, transportasi, gula, semen, dan daging &amp; susu), dan risiko rendah (perdagangan &amp; jasa, properti, konstruksi, dan pengolahan makanan). Uji ketahanan dilakukan untuk periode waktu jangka pendek (2025 - 2027) maupun jangka panjang (2030 - 2050) dengan menilai perubahan credit risk grading (CRG) debitur serta implikasi finansial terhadap Permata Bank melalui proyeksi loan impairment (LI), risk weighted asset (RWA), dan capital adequacy ratio (CAR). Hasil uji ketahanan menunjukan sektor pertambangan batubara, perkebunan, dan migas merupakan sektor yang paling terdapat oleh perubahan iklim (penurunan rating customer tertinggi).

Risiko Kredit menjadi risiko terbesar bagi Bank terhadap dampak dari perubahan iklim. Kajian CRMS juga digunakan untuk mengukur dampak lintas risiko, termasuk risiko pasar, risiko likuiditas, dan risiko operasional, walaupun dampaknya tidak signifikanseperti pada risiko kredit.

The 2025 CRMS results indicate that Transition Risk and Physical Risk may put pressure on the Bank's financial performance, as reflected in a decline in profit after tax (PAT) and the capital adequacy ratio (CAR). Under the pilot bottom-up CRMS covering  $100\%$  of the portfolio, CAR is projected to decline to  $28\% - 30\%$  over the short-term horizon (2025 - 2027) and to  $28\% - 33\%$  over the long-term horizon (2030 - 2050), compared with a December 2024 position of  $35\%$ .

![img-116.jpeg](img-116.jpeg)

This decline is primarily driven by higher expected credit loss (ECL), lower interest income due to an increase in troubled debtors (non-performing loans/NPLs), and fair value losses on securities as market conditions change.

These projected impacts are derived through stress testing that begins with categorizing debtors into priority subsectors based on risk levels: high risk (such as coal, power generation, oil and gas, and plantations), medium risk (such as chemicals, transportation, sugar, cement, and meat and dairy), and low risk (trade and services, property, construction, and food processing). Stress testing is conducted for both the short-term (2025 - 2027) and long-term (2030 - 2050) periods by assessing changes in debtor Credit Risk Grading (CRG) and the resulting financial implications for Permata Bank through projections of loan impairment (LI), risk-weighted assets (RWA), and the capital adequacy ratio (CAR). The results indicate that coal mining, plantations, and oil and gas are the sectors most affected by climate change (with the largest decline in customer ratings).

Credit Risk represents the Bank's most significant risk exposure arising from climate change impacts. The CRMS assessment is also used to measure cross-risk impacts, including market risk, liquidity risk, and operational risk, although these impacts are not as significant as those on credit risk.
