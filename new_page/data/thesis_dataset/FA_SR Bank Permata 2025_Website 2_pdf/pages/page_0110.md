# Realisasi Investasi Sosial Permata Bank 2025

# Permata Bank Social Investment Realization 2025

Sepanjang tahun 2025, Permata Bank merealisasikan berbagai program sosial dengan total penyaluran dana sebesar Rp2.678,8 juta. Melalui program-program tersebut, Bank berharap dapat memberikan manfaat yang berkelanjutan bagi seluruh pemangku kepentingan.

Throughout 2025, Permata Bank implemented various social programs with a total fund disbursement of IDR 2,678.8 million. Through these programs, the Bank hopes to deliver sustainable benefits to all stakeholders.

| Program | Dana yang Disalurkan (Rp. Juta)
Funds Disbursed (Rp Million) |
| --- | --- |
| Tanggung Jawab Sosial | Social Responsibility | 2.678,8 |
| Tanggung Jawab Lingkungan | Environmental Responsibility | 1.550 |
| Donasi dan Beasiswa | Donations and Scholarships | 1.571,2 |
| Jumlah | Total | 5.800 |

# Survei Kepuasan Penerima Manfaat

# Beneficiary Satisfaction Survey

Permata Bank secara berkala mengevaluasi efektivitas pelaksanaan program Permata Hati dengan menghimpun umpan balik dari penerima manfaat pada akhir setiap program. Hasil evaluasi tersebut digunakan sebagai dasar perbaikan dan penyempurnaan program di periode berikutnya.

Permata Bank regularly evaluates the effectiveness of the implementation of Permata Hati programs by collecting feedback from beneficiaries at the conclusion of each program. The evaluation results are used as the basis for improvements and enhancements to the programs in subsequent periods.
