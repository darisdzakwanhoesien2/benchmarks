Penggunaan Material Ramah Lingkungan (F.5)

Permata Bank tidak menjalankan aktivitas produksi yang menggunakan bahan baku langsung, melainkan hanya memanfaatkan material pendukung untuk operasional perbankan sehari-hari. Sejalan dengan komitmen terhadap pengelolaan lingkungan, Bank menerapkan digitalisasi proses kerja untuk menekan kebutuhan pencetakan dokumen, termasuk pada layanan pembukaan rekening, transaksi teller, penyampaian laporan, dan administrasi/dokumentasi internal.

Dalam penggunaan material fisik yang masih diperlukan, Bank memprioritaskan kertas yang telah memiliki sertifikasi lingkungan, seperti Programme for the Endorsement of Forest Certification (PEFC).

Selain pengelolaan material, prinsip keberlanjutan juga diterapkan dalam pemilihan gedung perkantoran, di mana gedung Kantor Pusat WTC II Sudirman telah tersertikasi Green Mark, dan gedung Metropolitan Tower (Permata Learning Center) tersertifikasi GREENSHIP Existing Building peringkat Gold.

Pada tahun 2025, terdapat tambahan gedung Permata Bank yang menerima sertifikasi green building, di antaranya:

1. Gedung Permata Bank Bintaro Sektor 7 Tangerang Selatan telah menerima GREENSHIP Certification dengan peringkat Gold oleh Green Building Council Indonesia. Selain itu, telah dilakukan penggantian lampu LED sebanyak kurang lebih 1.200 titik, pemakaian listrik dari solar panel sebesar kurang lebih 4.800 kWh per bulan, kampanye dan sosialisasi hemat energi, pengaturan mati/nyala lampu, AC dan lift, dan monitoring pemakaian energi setiap bulan.
2. Gedung Permata Bank Merdeka Bandung telah menerima GREENSHIP Certification dengan peringkat Gold oleh Green Building Council Indonesia. Sejak tersertifikasi greenship, secara rutin dilakukan kampanye dan sosialisasi hemat energi, dan monitoring pemakaian energi setiap bulan.

Permata Bank does not conduct production activities that directly use raw materials, and instead uses supporting materials for day-to-day banking operations. In line with its commitment to environmental management, the Bank implements digitalization of work processes to reduce the need for document printing, including for account opening services, teller transactions, report submissions, and internal administration and documentation.

Where physical materials remain necessary, the Bank prioritizes paper with environmental certifications such as the Programme for the Endorsement of Forest Certification (PEFC).

Beyond materials management, sustainability principles are also applied in the selection of office buildings, where the WTC II Sudirman Head Office building is certified Green Mark, and the Metropolitan Tower (Permata Learning Center) is certified under GREENSHIP Existing Building with a Gold rating.

In 2025, additional Permata Bank buildings received green building certification, including:

1. Permata Bank Bintaro Sector 7 Building in South Tangerang received a GREENSHIP Certification with a Gold rating from the Green Building Council Indonesia. Furthermore, the Bank has replaced approximately 1,200 LED lights, utilized electricity generated from solar panels of approximately 4,800 kWh per month, implemented energy-saving campaigns and socialization, applied lighting, air-conditioning, and lift on/off scheduling, and monitored energy usage on a monthly basis.
2. Permata Bank Merdeka Bandung Building received a GREENSHIP Certification with a Gold rating from the Green Building Council Indonesia. Since obtaining GREENSHIP certification, the Bank has routinely implemented energy-saving campaigns and socialization and monitored energy usage on a monthly basis.
