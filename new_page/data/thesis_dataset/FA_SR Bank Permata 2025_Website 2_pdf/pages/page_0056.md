# Pelestarian Lingkungan dan Keanekaragaman Hayati [F.9][F.10]

# Environmental Preservation and Biodiversity

Aktivitas operasional Permata Bank dilaksanakan di area yang tidak bersinggungan dengan kawasan konservasi maupun wilayah dengan tingkat keanekaragaman hayati tinggi, sehingga tidak menimbulkan dampak langsung terhadap ekosistem sekitar. Kendati demikian, Bank tetap menunjukkan komitmen terhadap perlindungan lingkungan dan keanekaragaman hayati melalui pelaksanaan berbagai inisiatif keberlanjutan. Penjelasan lebih rinci mengenai upaya tersebut dapat ditemukan pada sub-bab Tanggung Jawab Lingkungan. [304-1]

Permata Bank's operational activities are carried out in areas that do not overlap with conservation zones or regions with high biodiversity value, and therefore do not create direct impacts on surrounding ecosystems. Nevertheless, the Bank continues to demonstrate its commitment to environmental protection and biodiversity through the implementation of various sustainability initiatives. Further details on these efforts are presented in the Environmental Responsibility sub-chapter. [304-1]
