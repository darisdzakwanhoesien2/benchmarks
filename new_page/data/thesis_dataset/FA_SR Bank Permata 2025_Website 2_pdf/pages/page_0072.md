Permata Bank memfokuskan pengembangan kepemimpinan pada penguatan kompetensi yang sejalan dengan nilai-nilai I-PRICE dan sembilan Kompetensi Kepemimpinan Permata Bank. Dalam mendukung pengembangan karier, Bank menerapkan system mutasi dan rotasi sebagai bagian dari pengelolaaan talenta. Sepanjang tahun 2025, sebanyak 140 PermataBankers berpartisipasi dalam program akselerasi. Selain itu, Bank menjadi  $72\%$  posisi pejabat eksekutif melalui promosi internal sebagai wujud komitmen terhadap pengembangan talenta dari dalam.

Permata Bank focuses its leadership development on strengthening competencies aligned with the Bank's I-PRICE values and its nine Permata Bank Leadership Competencies. To support career development, the Bank implements a transfer and rotation system as part of its talent management. Throughout 2025, 140 PermataBankers participated in the acceleration program. Furthermore, the Bank filled  $72\%$  of its executive officer position through internal promotions, demonstrating its commitment to developing talent from within.

Permata Bank menyediakan pengalaman pembelajaran yang terpadu melalui Permata Learning (PEARL) sebagai platform pengembangan kompetensi karyawan. PEARL mengintegrasikan berbagai sarana pembelajaran, termasuk pelatihan dan seminar, konten video, gamifikasi, serta kegiatan coaching dan mentoring. Sejalan dengan transformasi digital, Bank juga meningkatkan kapabilitas pembelajaran daring melalui pemanfaatan learning studio.

Sepanjang tahun 2025, seluruh PermataBankers (100%) mengikuti program pengembangan kompetensi dengan rata-rata jam pelatihan sebesar 74 jam per orang, meningkat dari 71 jam per orang pada tahun 2024. Program tersebut dilaksanakan melalui kombinasi pembelajaran daring dan tatap muka, termasuk kelas onsite, aplikasi, dan media sosial. Untuk mendukung pelaksanaan program, Permata Bank mengalokasikan anggaran pelatihan sebesar Rp65,1 miliar dengan realisasi Rp69,1 miliar, atas meningkat  $6\%$  dibandingkan tahun 2024.

Permata Bank provides an integrated learning experience through Permata Learning (PEARL) as the employee competency development platform. PEARL integrates various learning channels, including training and seminars, video content, gamification, as well as coaching and mentoring activities. In line with digital transformation, the Bank also enhances online learning capabilities through the use of learning studia.

Throughout 2025, all PermataBankers (100%) participated in competency development programs with an average of 74 hours of training per person, up from 71 hours per person in 2024. The program was implemented through a combination of online and face-to-face learning, including on-site classes, applications, and social media. To support the program's implementation, Permata Bank allocated a training budget of IDR 65.1 billion, with realization of IDR 69.1 billion, a  $6\%$  increase compared to 2024.
