# 5. Pengembangan Produk Keuangan Berkelanjutan

Permata Bank di 2025 meluncurkan Program KPR Hijau (Green Mortgage) dengan memberikan hadiah 5 pohon untuk setiap 1 fasilitas KPR Hijau yang disetujui. Program ini berkolaborasi dengan WWF Indonesia. Melalui program ini, terdapat 9 akad KPR Hijau dengan total pohon yang sudah tertanam sebanyak 45 pohon di Bukit Tigapuluh, Jambi.

Pada 15 Oktober 2025, Permata Bank bersama Kota Baru Parahyangan, pengembang perumahan tersertifikasi Greenship di Bandung melaksanakan Ceremony Green Mortgage dengan tema kegiatan “Every Home We Build is a Seed of Hope”. Kegiatan ini merupakan momentum yang sangat bermakna—sebuah bukti komitmen bersama dalam mendukung pembangunan berkelanjutan di Indonesia.

Acara ini dihadiri oleh Nasabah sekaligus pemilik unit Cluster Nilapadmi, Chief of Corporate Affairs Permata Bank, Direktur Kota Baru Parahyangan, Perwakilan dari WWF Indonesia, Green Building Council Indonesia, dan Komunitas Hayu Hejo.

Selain segmen Consumer, saat ini Permata Bank sedang dalam tahap pengembangan produk keuangan berkelanjutan untuk segmen Corporate dan Commercial. Di 2025, Bank telah mulai melakukan persiapan-persiapan dan studi banding dengan peer bank.

# 5. Development of Sustainable Financial Products

In 2025, Permata Bank launched the Green Mortgage Program by providing 5 trees for each approved Green Mortgage facility. This program was implemented in collaboration with WWF Indonesia. Through the program, 9 Green Mortgage agreements were executed, resulting in a total of 45 trees planted in Bukit Tigapuluh, Jambi.

On 15 October 2025, Permata Bank, together with Kota Baru Parahyangan, a Greenship-certified housing developer in Bandung, held a Green Mortgage Ceremony under the theme “Every Home We Build is a Seed of Hope”. The event served as a meaningful milestone and a demonstration of the parties’ shared commitment to supporting sustainable development in Indonesia.

The event was attended by a customer and owner of a unit in the Nilapadmi Cluster, the Chief of Corporate Affairs of Permata Bank, the Director of Kota Baru Parahyangan, representatives of WWF-Indonesia and the Green Building Council Indonesia, and the Hayu Hejo community.

In addition to the Consumer segment, Permata Bank is currently developing sustainable financial products for the Corporate and Commercial segments. In 2025, the Bank began preparatory work and benchmarking with peer banks.
