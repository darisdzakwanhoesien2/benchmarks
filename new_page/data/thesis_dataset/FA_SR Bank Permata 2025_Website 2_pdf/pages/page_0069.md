# KESEJAHTERAAN KARYAWAN

Bank menerapkan kebijakan remunerasi yang kompetitif untuk memastikan karyawan merasa dihargai dan termotivasi dalam memberikan kinerja terbaik. Kesetaraan rasio gaji pokok antara karyawan laki-laki dan perempuan juga diterapkan, dengan tingkat remunerasi yang berada di atas UMR yang ditetapkan. Pemberian remunerasi berbasis kinerja (pay for performance) dilakukan dengan mempertimbangkan beberapa aspek sebagai berikut: [405-2]

- Kinerja keuangan Bank;
- Kompetensi dan kinerja karyawan (meritocracy);
- Asas kelayakan dan kepantasan sesuai dengan posisi/jabatan;
- Praktik remunerasi yang berlaku di pasar; serta
- Kesetaraan internal.

Permata Bank memastikan kesetaraan rasio gaji pokok antara karyawan laki-laki dan perempuan. Selain upah, Bank juga memberikan berbagai bentuk remunerasi dan tunjangan lainnya, antara lain: [405-2]

# EMPLOYEE WELFARE

The Bank implements a competitive remuneration policy to ensure employees feel valued and motivated to deliver their best performance. The Bank also maintains equality in base salary ratios between male and female employees, with remuneration levels above the applicable minimum wage. Performance-based remuneration (pay for performance) is provided by taking into account the following aspects: [405-2]

- The Bank's financial performance;
- Employee competencies and performance (meritocracy);
- Appropriateness and fairness in line with position/role;
- Market competitiveness in remuneration practice; and
- Internal equity.

Permata Bank ensures equal base salary ratios between male and female employees. In addition to wages, the Bank also provides various other forms of remuneration and benefits, including: [405-2]
