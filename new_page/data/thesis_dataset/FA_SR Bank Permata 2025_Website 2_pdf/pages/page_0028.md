# Membangun Budaya Keberlanjutan [F.1]

# Establishing a Sustainability Culture

![img-51.jpeg](img-51.jpeg)

Permata Bank menjadikan visi serta nilai utama I-PRICE sebagai fondasi bagi PermataBankers dalam bekerja, berinteraksi, dan berperilaku. Nilai-nilai tersebut juga menjadi rujukan dalam penguatan budaya keberlanjutan di lingkungan internal Bank. Permata Bank mendorong partisipasi aktif karyawan dalam pengembangan budaya keberlanjutan melalui berbagai kampanye di media digital dan seminar internal, serta program Sustainability Culture yang berfokus pada integrasi nilai keberlanjutan di dalam proses kerja dan gaya hidup sehari-hari karyawan.

Melalui program Sustainability Culture, dibentuk Culture Network yang terdiri dari Culture Champions, Culture Leaders, dan Culture Agents dari setiap perwakilan Direktorat. Di tahun 2025, Culture Network telah mengembangkan 96 program keberlanjutan. Program-program tersebut dijalankan di tingkat Divisi dan dimonitor oleh Culture Champions di masing-masing Direktorat. Untuk memastikan programnya berjalan dengan baik, Divisi Sustainability telah menyelenggarakan 9 Sustainability Culture Workshop bagi seluruh anggota Culture Network sepanjang 2025.

Di akhir periode program, Divisi Sustainability melalui Sustainability Committee Meeting melakukan penilaian terhadap program-program yang memiliki dampak terukur serta relevan dengan komitmen keberlanjutan Permata Bank. Sebagai bentuk apresiasi, Permata Bank memberikan penghargaan kepada Divisi dan Culture Network dengan program terbaik, yang diumumkan melalui Culture Awards Permata Bank.

Selain itu, PermataBankers turut dilibatkan dalam program Employee Volunteers (EVO) sebagai sarana untuk menciptakan dampak positif bagi masyarakat sekaligus menumbuhkan sense of purpose bagi karyawan dalam berkarya dan berkontribusi secara nyata.

Upaya peningkatan kesadaran terhadap 'Sustainability Journey Permata Bank' juga dilakukan melalui pengiriman e-mail blast secara berkala kepada seluruh karyawan, termasuk Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris. Komunikasi internal tersebut memuat poster serta informasi singkat mengenai berbagai inisiatif keberlanjutan yang dijalankan, dengan mengusung tagar #SayangiBumi dan #DenganHati. Sepanjang tahun 2025, Bank melakukan pengiriman 39 e-mail blast Sustainability Journey kepada seluruh karyawan.

![img-52.jpeg](img-52.jpeg)

Permata Bank adopts its vision and core I-PRICE values as the foundation for PermataBankers in working, interacting, and conducting themselves. These values also serve as a reference for strengthening a culture of sustainability within the Bank's internal environment. Permata Bank encourages active employee participation in the development of a sustainability culture through various digital media campaigns, internal seminars, and Sustainability Culture Program that focus on integrating sustainability values into work processes and employees' everyday lifestyles.

Through the Sustainability Culture program, a Culture Network was established, consisting of Culture Champions, Culture Leaders, and Culture Agents from each Directorate. By 2025, the Culture Network had developed 96 sustainability programs. These programs are implemented at the Division level and monitored by Culture Champions in each Directorate. To ensure the program's success, the Sustainability Division held 9 Sustainability Culture Workshops for all Culture Network members throughout 2025.

At the end of the program period, the Sustainability Division, through a Sustainability Committee Meeting, assessed programs that had a measurable impact and were relevant to Permata Bank's sustainability commitments. As a token of appreciation, Permata Bank presented awards to the Divisions and Culture Networks with the best programs, which were announced through the Permata Bank Culture Awards.

In addition, PermataBankers are involved in the Employee Volunteers (EVO) program as a means of creating positive social impact while fostering a sense of purpose among employees in their work and in making tangible contributions.

Efforts to enhance awareness of 'Permata Bank's Sustainability Journey' are also undertaken through the periodic distribution of email blasts to all employees, including the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners. These internal communications feature posters and brief information on various sustainability initiatives implemented by the Bank, using the hashtags #SayangiBumi and #DenganHati. Throughout 2025, the Bank carried out 39 e-mail blast update on sustainability journey activities.
