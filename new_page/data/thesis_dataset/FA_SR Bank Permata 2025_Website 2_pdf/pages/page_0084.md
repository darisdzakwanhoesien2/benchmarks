Dalam mengembangkan produk dan layanan digital, Permata Bank memastikan kepatuhan terhadap prosedur dan kebijakan internal, ketentuan perbankan yang berlaku, serta persetujuan dari OJK dan Bank Indonesia. Upaya tersebut dilakukan untuk menjaga dan melindungi ketersediaan, integritas, dan kerahasiaan informasi dari berbagai potensi gangguan, baik internal maupun eksternal.

In developing digital products and services, Permata Bank ensures compliance with internal procedures and policies, applicable banking regulations, and required approvals from Financial Services Authority and Bank Indonesia. These efforts are undertaken to safeguard and protect the availability, integrity, and confidentiality of information from potential disruptions, both internal and external.

# Dampak atas Produk dan Layanan [F.28][413-2]

Impact of Products and Services

Melalui penyediaan berbagai produk dan layanan keuangan, Permata Bank berupaya memberikan dampak positif bagi peningkatan inklusi keuangan dan pertumbuhan ekonomi. Layanan digital dan keuangan mikro memungkinkan Bank menjangkau masyarakat yang sebelumnya belum memiliki akses ke layanan perbankan formal. Di sisi lain, pembiayaan usaha mikro kepada UMKM berperan dalam menciptakan lapangan kerja dan menekan kesenjangan sosial. Pengembangan produk green bonds serta digitalisasi layanan perbankan juga mendukung upaya pengurangan emisi karbon dengan menurunkan kebutuhan aktivitas perbankan secara fisik.

Sejalan dengan itu, bank juga menyadari bahwa produk dan layanan berpotensi memberikan dampak negatif seperti risiko penipuan dan kebocoran data nasabah. Untuk memitigasinya Bank membekali nasabah dengan edukasi keamanan layanan digital digital sebanyak 41 kali yang terdiri dari 29 kali edukasi melalui akun social media resmi Bank dan 12 kali edukasi yang dikirimkan melalui email blast dan push notifications di tahun 2025.

Through the provision of a range of financial products and services, Permata Bank seeks to deliver positive impacts on financial inclusion and economic growth. Digital services and microfinance enable the Bank to reach communities that previously lacked access to formal banking services. In addition, micro-business financing for MSMEs supports job creation and helps reduce social inequality. The development of green bond products and the digitalization of banking services also support emissions reduction efforts by lowering the need for physical banking activities.

In line with this the Bank also recognizes that its products and services may pose potential negative impacts such as fraud risk and customer data breaches. To mitigate these risks the Bank provided digital service security education 41 times consisting of 29 educational communications through the Bank's official social media accounts and 12 educational messages delivered via email blasts and push notifications in 2025.

# Pemasaran dan Pelabelan [F.17][417-1][417-2][417-3]

Marketing and Labeling

Permata Bank memastikan akses informasi produk dan layanan yang setara dan mudah bagi nasabah melalui berbagai kanal, termasuk situs resmi, kantor cabang, Permata ME, call center, dan media sosial. Selain itu, Bank juga berupaya menyajikan dokumen resmi, seperti brosur, ringkasan informasi produk dan layanan, dan kontrak perjanjian dengan bahasa yang sederhana dan mudah dipahami. Seluruh komunikasi pemasaran ditelaah terlebih dahulu oleh Compliance, Legal, dan Marketing Communication.

Sepanjang tahun 2025, Permata Bank tidak mencatat adanya insiden ketidakpatuhan terkait informasi produk maupun komunikasi pemasaran. Hal ini didukung oleh pelatihan yang diberikan kepada Tim Penjualan agar mampu menjelaskan biaya, risiko, manfaat, serta syarat dan ketentuan produk secara jelas kepada nasabah, dengan mengacu pada Ringkasan Informasi Produk dan Layanan (RIPLAY), baik RIPLAY Umum dan juga RIPLAY Personal.

Permata Bank ensures equal and convenient access to information on products and services for customers through multiple channels, including the official website, branch offices, Permata ME, the call center, and social media. The Bank also strives to present official documents, such as brochures, product and service information summaries, and agreement contracts, in clear and easy-to-understand language. All marketing communications are reviewed in advance by Compliance, Legal, and Marketing Communication.

Throughout 2025, Permata Bank did not record any noncompliance incidents related to product information or marketing communications. This is supported by training provided to the Sales Team to ensure they can clearly explain product fees, risks, benefits, and terms and conditions to customers, with reference to the Product and Service Information Summary (RIPLAY), including both the General RIPLAY and the Personal RIPLAY.
