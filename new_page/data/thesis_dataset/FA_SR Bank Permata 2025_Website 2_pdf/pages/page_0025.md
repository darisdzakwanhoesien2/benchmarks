# Strategi Keberlanjutan [A.1][2-22] Sustainability Strategy

Permata Bank mengintegrasikan kepentingan ekonomi, sosial, dan lingkungan ke dalam aktivitas bisnis dan operasionalnya. Komitmen tersebut selaras dengan visi keberlanjutan Bank, yaitu "Menjadi mitra keuangan terpercaya yang mendorong transformasi menuju ekonomi yang berkelanjutan, inklusif, dan bertanggung jawab." Untuk mendukung visi tersebut, Permata Bank menyusun Rencana Aksi Keuangan Berkelanjutan (RAKB) Periode 2024-2028 yang dirancang gunna memastikan penerapan prinsip lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola (LST) di seluruh kegiatan Bank.

Dalam kerangka RAKB 2024-2028, sejumlah program telah dirancang dan sudah berjalan pada 2025. Program-program tersebut, mencakup penguatan pembiayaan berkelanjutan, serta pengembangan produk dan layanan keuangan yang mendukung pertumbuhan ekonomi berkelanjutan, baik yang memberikan dampak positif terhadap lingkungan maupun mendorong inklusi sosial. Selain itu, Bank juga memperkuat aspek tata kelola melalui pengelolaan risiko terkait perubahan iklim, upaya pencapaian Net Zero Emission (NZE) dari operasional Bank, pengembangan proses pengadaan berkelanjutan, pelaksanaan Sustainability Learning Series, penguatan Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI).

Melalui implementasi RAKB tersebut, Permata Bank berupaya memberikan kontribusi positif bagi lingkungan dan masyarakat, antara lain dengan mendukung transisi menuju masa depan rendah karbon, menjaga keanekaragaman hayati, serta meningkatkan literasi keuangan di masyarakat. Di sisi internal, Bank juga memastikan terciptanya lingkungan kerja yang bertanggung jawab dan kondusif bagi PermataBankers dengan menerapkan budaya keberlanjutan dan menjunjung tinggi etika bisnis.

Permata Bank integrates economic, social, and environmental considerations into its business and operational activities. This commitment is aligned with the Bank's sustainability vision, namely "To become a trusted financial partner that drives transformation towards a sustainable, inclusive, and responsible economy." To support this vision, Permata Bank has developed a Sustainable Finance Action Plan (RAKB) for the 2024-2028 period, which is designed to ensure the implementation of environmental, social, and governance (ESG) principles across all of the Bank's activities.

Within the framework of the 2024-2028 Sustainable Finance Action Plan (RAKB), a number of programs were designed and have been implemented in 2025. These programs include the strengthening of sustainable financing, as well as the development of financial products and services that support sustainable economic growth, both by generating positive environmental impacts and by promoting social inclusion. In addition, the Bank has strengthened governance aspects through the management of climate change-related risks, efforts to achieve Net Zero Emissions (NZE) from the Bank's operations, the development of sustainable procurement processes, the implementation of the Sustainability Learning Series, the strengthening of Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI).

Through the implementation of the Sustainable Finance Action Plan (RAKB), Permata Bank seeks to make a positive contribution to the environment and society, including by supporting the transition towards a low-carbon future, preserving biodiversity balance, and enhancing financial literacy within the community. Internally, the Bank also ensures the creation of a responsible and conducive working environment for PermataBankers by embedding a culture of sustainability and upholding high standards of business ethics.
