# Respons Permata Bank
Permata Bank's Responses
- Membentuk taskforce untuk mempersiapkan pengembangan produk keuangan berkelanjutan untuk segmen Commercial dan Corporate.
- Layanan customer care telah menyelesaikan keluhan nasabah sebanyak 30.201.
- Economic Outlook terlaksana dengan mengangkat tema Driving Resilient Growth Through Sustainability di salah satu panel diskusinya.
- Mengintegrasikan pembahasan keuangan berkelanjutan saat kunjungan rutin ke nasabah korporasi.
- Bank menyalurkan pembiayaan kendaraan listrik (EV) kepada beberapa perusahaan multifinance.
- Established a task force to prepare the development of sustainable finance products for the Commercial and Corporate segments.
- Customer care services resolved 30,201 customer complaints.
- The Economic Outlook event was conducted, featuring Driving Resilient Growth Through Sustainability as one of its panel discussion themes.
- Integrated discussions on sustainable finance into routine visits to corporate customers.
- The Bank provided electric vehicle (EV) financing to several multifinance companies.

# Metode Pelibatan dan Frekuensi
Engagement Method and Frequency
- Komunikasi secara non-digital (diskusi/wawancara), platform digital seperti media sosial dan media elektronik (surel dan SMS) (setiap saat).
- Informasi terkait produk-produk perbankan dalam situs web Permata Bank (setiap saat).
- Layanan customer care melalui PermataTel (setiap saat).
- Program webinar/seminar, seperti acara Economic Outlook yang mengangkat tema keberlanjutan (minimal setahun sekali).
- Communication through digital platforms (social media), non-digital (discussion/interview), and electronic media (email and SMS) (at any time).
- Information about banking products is available on Permata Bank's website (at any time).
- Customer care services via PermataTel (at any time).
- Webinar/seminar programs, such as the Economic Outlook event, which raises the theme of sustainability (at least once a year).

# Respons Permata Bank
Permata Bank's Responses
- Laporan keberlanjutan, Rencana Aksi Keuangan Berkelanjutan dan Climate-Risk Management &amp; Scenario Analysis dikirimkan tepat waktu kepada regulator.
- Membayar pajak sesuai peraturan perundang-undangan secara tepat waktu.
- Sustainability reports, Sustainable Financial Action Plan, and Climate-risk Management &amp; Scenario Analysis are submitted to regulators on time.
- Paying taxes in accordance with laws and regulations in a timely manner.

# Metode Pelibatan dan Frekuensi
Engagement Method and Frequency
- Seluruh laporan terkait pelaksanaan kepatuhan (minimal setahun sekali).
- Penyampaian laporan rutin dan laporan insidentil lainnya kepada regulator sesuai dengan timeline.
- Melaksanakan mandatory e-learning kepada seluruh PermataBankers terlaksana dengan baik (secara berkala).
- Submitting all reports related to compliance implementation (at least once a year).
- Submitting regular and incidental reports to regulators in accordance with the timelines.
- Conducting mandatory e-learning for all PermataBankers and implemented effectively (on a periodic basis).
