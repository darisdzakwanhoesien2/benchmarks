# Manajemen Risiko [E.3]
Risk Management

Permata Bank menerapkan manajemen risiko sebagai bagian dari kerangka pengelolaaan usaha Bank. Seluruh risiko material dikelola berdasarkan enterprise risk management framework (ERMF) melalui rangkaian proses identifikasi, pengukuran, pemantauan, dan pengendalian yang dijalankan secara konsisten di seluruh aktivitas operasional Bank. Pengelolaaan ini ditujukan untuk menjaga ketahanan usaha sekaligus mengendalikan potensi dampak risiko terhadap aspek ekonomi, sosial, lingkungan, dan tata kelola. Strategi manajemen risiko dirancang untuk mendukung kesinambungan usaha dan penciptaan nilai bagi pemangku kepentingan dengan memperhatikan kondisi internal dan eksternal Bank.

Risiko dan peluang bisnis utama yang berkaitan dengan aspek lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola (LST) dipantau serta dinilai melalui Komite Keberlanjutan. Pemantauan tersebut didukung oleh kajian manajemen risiko yang mencakup seluruh aspek operasional Bank, termasuk risiko terkait LST dan ekonomi. Dalam kerangka pengelolaaan risiko tersebut, Permata Bank mengelola 15 tipe risiko utama, yaitu Risiko Kredit, Risiko Pasar, Risiko Likuiditas Jangka Pendek, Risiko Likuiditas Struktural, Risiko Modal, Risiko Operasional, Risiko Keamanan Informasi, Risiko Hukum, Risiko Strategik, Risiko Kepatuhan, Risiko Imbal Hasil, Risiko Investasi, Risiko Transaksi Intra-group, serta Risiko Iklim, sesuai dengan ERMF 2025.

Lebih lanjut, sebagai bagian dari tata kelola risiko, pengelolaaan risiko iklim juga didukung oleh peran Komite Manajemen Risiko (KMR) dan Komite Pemantauan Risiko (KPR). KMR menetapkan kebijakan, kerangka kerja, serta parameter risiko yang mencakup aspek keberlanjutan, termasuk risk appetite dan risk tolerance terkait risiko iklim, dan memastikan penerapannya terintegrasi dalam proses bisnis. Sementara itu, KPR menjalankan fungsi pengawasan independen atas implementasi kebijakan tersebut dengan menilai keselarasan antara arahan yang telah ditetapkan dan praktik pelaksanaannya, serta memberikan rekomendasi kepada Dewan Komisaris untuk memastikan efektivitas tata kelola pengelolaaan risiko iklim.

Dalam kerangka pengelolaaan Risiko Iklim sebagai bagian dari risiko material Bank, Permata Bank melakukan identifikasi dan penilaian Risiko Iklim secara lebih mendalam. Risiko iklim yang dinilai material dikelompokkan ke dalam dua kategori utama, yaitu Risiko Transisi dan Risiko Fisik. Risiko Transisi berkaitan dengan perubahan kebijakan, perkembangan teknologi, serta pergeseran preferensi sosial, sementara Risiko Fisik meliputi banjir, kebakaran, peningkatan suhu, dan kenaikan permukaan air laut. Identifikasi tersebut dilakukan melalui analisis skenario dan uji stres untuk menilai dampaknya terhadap strategi bisnis Bank dalam jangka pendek, menengah, dan panjang.

Permata Bank implements risk management as an integral part of the Bank's business management framework. All material risks are managed in accordance with the enterprise risk management framework (ERMF) through a series of identification, measurement, monitoring, and control processes that are applied consistently across all of the Bank's operational activities. This management approach is intended to safeguard business resilience while controlling the potential impact of risks on economic, social, environmental, and governance aspects. The risk management strategy is designed to support business continuity and value creation for stakeholders, taking into account both the Bank's internal and external conditions.

Key business risks and opportunities related to environmental, social, and governance (ESG) aspects are monitored and assessed through the Sustainability Committee, which convenes at least twice a year. This monitoring is supported by risk management assessments covering all aspects of the Bank's operations, including ESG- and economy-related risks. Within this risk management framework, Permata Bank manages 15 main risk types, namely Credit Risk, Market Risk, Short-Term Liquidity Risk, Structural Liquidity Risk, Capital Risk, Operational Risk, Information Security Risk, Legal Risk, Strategic Risk, Compliance Risk, Rate of Return Risk, Investment Risk, Intra-group Transaction Risk, and Climate Risk, in accordance with the 2025 ERMF.

In addition, as part of risk governance, climate risk management is supported by the roles of the Risk Management Committee (RMC) and the Risk Monitoring Committee (RMOC). The Risk Management Committee establishes policies, frameworks, and risk parameters that incorporate sustainability considerations, including climate risk appetite and climate risk tolerance, and ensures these are integrated into business processes. Meanwhile, the Risk Monitoring Committee provides independent oversight of implementation by assessing alignment between the established direction and execution in practice, and by providing recommendations to the Board of Commissioners to support the effective governance of climate risk management.

Within the framework of Climate Risk management as part of the Bank's material risks, Permata Bank conducts more in-depth identification and assessment of Climate Risk. Climate risks deemed material are categorised into two main groups: Transition Risk and Physical Risk. Transition Risk relates to changes in policy, technological developments, and shifts in social preferences, while Physical Risk includes floods, fires, rising temperatures, and sea-level rise. This identification is carried out through scenario analysis and stress testing to assess the impact on the Bank's business strategy over the short, medium, and long term.
