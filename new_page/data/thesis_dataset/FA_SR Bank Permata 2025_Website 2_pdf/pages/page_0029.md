# Produk, Layanan, dan Kegiatan Usaha yang Dijalankan [C.4][2-6]

Products, Services, and Business Activities

Permata Bank menyediakan layanan perbankan yang mencakup kegiatan usaha utama dan penunjang bagi berbagai segmen, yaitu corporate, commercial, consumer, dan syariah. Seluruh layanan tersebut dijalankan dalam kerangka kegiatan usaha bank umum sesuai dengan ketentuan Pasal 3 Anggaran Dasar Permata Bank. Pengaturan mengenai ruang lingkup kegiatan usaha tersebut dituangkan dalam Akta No. 56 tanggal 30 September 2025 dan telah diberitahukan kepada Menteri Hukum Republik Indonesia berdasarkan suratnya No. AHU-AH.01.03-0240484 dan telah diumumkan dalam BNRI No. 83, TBN No. 29676/2025.

Permata Bank provides banking services encompassing its main and supporting business activities across various segments, namely corporate, commercial, consumer, and sharia. All such services are conducted within the scope of a commercial bank's business activities in accordance with the provisions of Article 3 of Permata Bank's Articles of Association. The regulations regarding the scope of business activities are set out in Deed No. 56 dated September 30, 2025 and have been notified to the Minister of Law of the Republic of Indonesia based on his letter No. AHU-AH.01.03-0240484 and have been announced in BNRI No. 83, TBN No. 29676/2025.

# PRODUK DAN LAYANAN PERMATA BANK

Permata Bank menyediakan fasilitas penghimpunan dan penyaluran dana kepada masyarakat dengan penawaran suku bunga yang kompetitif. Produk yang ditawarkan, antara lain pembiayaan dan permodalan usaha, tabungan, giro, deposito, kartu kredit, pembiayaan kepemilikan rumah, serta layanan perbankan lainnya.

# PERMATA BANK PRODUCTS AND SERVICES

Permata Bank provides facilities for the mobilisation and distribution of funds to the public at competitive interest rates. The products offered include, among others, business financing and working capital facilities, savings accounts, current accounts, time deposits, credit cards, home ownership financing, as well as other banking services.

Unslan lebih rinci mengenai kegiatan usaha serta produk dan layanan Permata Bank disajikan dalam Layanan Tahunan 2025. A detailed description of Permata Bank's business activities, products, and services is presented in 2025 Annual Report.
