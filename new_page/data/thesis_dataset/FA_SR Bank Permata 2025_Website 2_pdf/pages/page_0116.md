# Komite yang Terkait dengan Keberlanjutan
Committees Related to Sustainability

## KOMITE KEBERLANJUTAN

Pada tahun 2023 Bank membentuk Komite Keberlanjutan untuk meningkatkan pengawasan terhadap isu-su keberlanjutan. Komite Keberlanjutan mengadakan pertemuan secara berkala, setidaknya empat kali dalam setahun. Komite keberlanjutan terdiri dari anggota tetap dengan hak suara, yaitu Direktur Utama, Direktur Risiko, dan Direktur Keuangan, serta anggota tetap tanpa hak suara, yang terdiri dari Chief dan Kepala Divisi terkait. Antara lain, Corporate Banking, Commercial Banking, International Business Development &amp; Advisory, Treasury, Sustainability, Corporate Communication, dan Consumer Lending. Komite Keberlanjutan bertugas melakukan pengawasan serta mendorong pelaksanaan inisiatif keberlanjutan di Permata Bank, mengintegrasikan aspek lingkungan, sosial, dan tata kelola (LST) ke dalam strategi, kebijakan, dan praktik bisnis Bank, serta memastikan kepatuhan terhadap peraturan yang berlaku, keselarasan dengan standar industri, dan praktik umum terkait keberlanjutan di sektor perbankan.

## KOMITE PEMANTAU RISIKO

Komite Pemantau Risiko (KPR) berperan mengevaluasi konsistensi antara kebijakan manajemen risiko termasuk risiko iklim, dengan pelaksanaan kebijakan Bank dalam rangka memberikan rekomendasi kepada Dewan Komisaris, serta memonitor dan mengevaluasi pelaksanaan tugas Komite Manajemen Risiko dan Satuan Kerja Manajemen Risiko dalam rangka memberikan rekomendasi kepada Dewan Komisaris. Penjelasan lebih lanjut terkait term of references (TOR) Komite Pemantauan Risiko dapat mengacu pada Laporan Tahunan Permata Bank.

## KOMITE MANAJEMEN RISIKO

Komite Manajemen Risiko (KMR) bertugas untuk mengevaluasi dan merekomendasikan kebijakan manajemen risiko, strategi, risk appetite, toleransi risiko, limit risiko, kerangka kerja manajemen risiko, dan rencana kontinjensi untuk mengantisipasi kondisi abnormal, serta memastikan eksposur risiko untuk semua jenis risiko di termasuk risiko iklim, di Bank tetap sesuai dengan risk appetite.

KMR juga memastikan penerapan kerangka kerja manajemen risiko termasuk kerangka kerja risiko (risk type framework/RTF) iklim di Permata Bank telah berjalan dengan efektif. Penjelasan lebih lanjut terkait terms of references (TOR) Komite Manajemen Risiko dapat mengacu pada Laporan Tahunan Permata Bank.

## SUSTAINABILITY COMMITTEE

In 2023, the Bank established the Sustainability Committee to strengthen oversight of sustainability matters. The Sustainability Committee holds regular meetings, at least four times a year. It comprises permanent voting members, namely the President Director, Risk Director, and Finance Director, as well as permanent non-voting members consisting of relevant Chiefs and Division Heads, including Corporate Banking, Commercial Banking, International Business Development and Advisory, Treasury, Sustainability, Corporate Communication, and Consumer Lending. The Sustainability Committee is responsible for providing oversight and driving the implementation of sustainability initiatives at Permata Bank, integrating environmental, social, and governance (ESG) aspects into the Bank's strategy, policies, and business practices, and ensuring compliance with applicable regulations, alignment with industry standards, and common sustainability practices in the banking sector.

## RISK MONITORING COMMITTEE

The Risk Monitoring Committee (RMOC) evaluates the consistency between risk management policies, including climate risk, and the Bank's policy implementation in order to provide recommendations to the Board of Commissioners. The Committee also monitors and evaluates the execution of duties by the Risk Management Committee and the Risk Management Work Unit in order to provide recommendations to the Board of Commissioners. Further details on the Risk Monitoring Committee's terms of reference (TOR) are presented in Permata Bank's Annual Report.

## RISK MANAGEMENT COMMITTEE

The Risk Management Committee (RMC) evaluates and recommends risk management policies, strategy, risk appetite, risk tolerance, risk limits, the risk management framework, and contingency plans to anticipate abnormal conditions, and ensures the Bank's risk exposures across all risk types, including climate risk, remain within the approved risk appetite.

The RMC also ensures the effective implementation of the risk management framework, including the climate risk type framework (RTF), at Permata Bank. Further details on the Risk Management Committee's terms of reference (TOR) are presented in Permata Bank's Annual Report.
