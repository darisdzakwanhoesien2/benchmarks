Pelaksanaan CRMS dilakukan pada periode Februari hingga Agustus 2025 (6 bulan) dan melibatkan beberapa unit kerja dari berbagai direktorat seperti Risk, Corporate Affairs &amp; Sustainability, Finance &amp; Sharia Business Unit, dan Treasury. Kajian CRMS meliputi analysis skenario uji ketahanan terhadap dampak risiko iklim, Climate Risk Stress Testing (CRST), dan perhitungan emisi dari aktivitas pembiayaan (financed emissions). Karena keterbatasan data emisi debitur, saat ini pengukuran dilakukan menggunakan data perantara (proxy) dari beberapa sektor industri yang sama dengan debitur-debitur Permata Bank.

Permata Bank telah mengembangkan metodologi Transmisi Risiko Iklim yang terintegrasi untuk mengidentifikasi dan mengelola dampaknya terhadap Risiko Kredit, Risiko Pasar, Risiko Likuiditas, dan Risiko Operasional sebagai berikut:

The CRMS was conducted from February to August 2025 (6 months) and involved multiple work units across directorates, including Risk, Corporate Affairs &amp; Sustainability, Finance and Sharia Business Unit, and Treasury. The CRMS covered climate risk resilience scenario analysis, Climate Risk Stress Testing (CRST), and the calculation of financed emissions. Due to limited debtor emissions data, measurement is currently performed using proxy data from several industry sectors that correspond to Permata Bank's debtors.

Permata Bank has developed an integrated Climate Risk Transmission methodology to identify and manage climate-related impacts across Credit Risk, Market Risk, Liquidity Risk, and Operational Risk, as follows:

Hasil kajian CRMS disetujui oleh Direktur Risiko, Chief of Corporate Affairs &amp; Sustainability, Chief of Treasury, dan Direktur Keuangan dan Unit Usaha Syariah serta disampaikan kepada Komite Pemantau Risiko (KPR) dan Komite Manajemen Risiko (KMR) sebagai pembaruan informasi mengenai identifikasi Risiko Iklim dan potensi dampaknya bagi Bank, serta sebagai bahan penyampaian kepada Direksi dan Dewan Komisaris. Sejalan dengan dinamika regulasi Risiko Iklim yang terus berkembang, frekuensi pelaksanaan CRMS ditetapkan dengan mempertimbangkan perkembangan ketentuan yang berlaku.

The CRMS results were approved by the Risk Director, the Chief of Corporate Affairs and Sustainability, the Chief of Treasury, and the Director of Finance and Sharia Business Unit, and were submitted to the Risk Monitoring Committee and the Risk Management Committee as an update on climate risk identification and its potential impacts on the Bank, as well as input for reporting to the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners. In line with evolving climate risk regulations, the frequency of CRMS implementation is determined with due consideration to prevailing requirements.
