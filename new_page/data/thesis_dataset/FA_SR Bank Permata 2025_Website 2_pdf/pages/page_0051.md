# Pengendalian Dampak Lingkungan Environmental Impact Management

Meskipun kegiatan perbankan tidak secara langsung menimbulkan dampak lingkungan yang signifikan, aktivitas operasional sehari-hari tetap memiliki potensi dampak, seperti penggunaan energi, konsumsi kertas, dan pengelolaan limbah. Selain itu, keputusan pembiayaan yang diambil Bank juga dapat menimbulkan dampak lingkungan secara tidak langsung, khususnya apabila pembiayaan disalurkan kepada sektor atau proyek dengan tingkat risiko lingkungan yang tinggi.

Dari hasil pilot bottom-up Climate Risk Management &amp; Scenario Analysis (CRMS) tahun 2025, Permata Bank mengidentifikasi dampak dari risiko iklim terhadap pembiayaan. Dalam jangka panjang, pembiayaan pada sektor intensif karbon berpotensi mendorong peningkatan NPL, ATMR, dan CKPN karena sektor tersebut paling rentan terhadap risiko transisi, serta berkontribusi besar terhadap emisi Bank dari aktivitas pembiayaan. Perburukan kualitas kredit terbesar diproyeksikan terjadi pada sektor pertambangan batubara, perkebunan &amp; kelapa sawit, serta sektor pengolahan.

Hasil Kajian CRMS dipresentasikan dan disosialisasikan kepada manajemen senior, unit bisnis, dan unit terkait lainnya untuk membangun kesadaran terkait risiko iklim walaupun belum menjadi acuan dalam menentukan strategi bisnis. Saat ini Bank fokus pada peningkatan kualitas data terkait risiko iklim, penguatan metodologi pengukuran, serta peningkatan kemampuan SDM sehingga hasil Kajian dan pengukuran lebih kredibel dan dapat digunakan untuk tujuan yang lebih luas.

Dari sisi operasional, Permata Bank mengintegrasikan prinsip pelestarian lingkungan dalam kebijakan operasional dan mendorong penerapan budaya keberlanjutan di seluruh aktivitas internal Bank.

Sebagai bagian dari upaya tersebut, di tahun 2025 Permata Bank melakukan:

1. Kampanye hemat energi melalui berbagai platform internal Bank (e-mail blast, Permata Portal/News, media sosial internal, digital signage).
2. Penggantian lampu ke lampu LED yang lebih hemat energi sebanyak kurang lebih 1.200 pcs.
3. Penggantian kendaraan Dewan Direksi menjadi EV.
4. Penyesuaian waktu nyala peralatan listrik (AC, lift, lampu) lebih lambat 30 menit di Gedung Bintaro Sektor 7 Tangerang.
5. Sertifikasi green building untuk Gedung Bintaro Sektor 7 Tangerang Selatan dan Gedung Merdeka Bandung.

Although banking activities do not directly cause significant environmental impacts, day-to-day operations still have potential impacts, including energy use, paper consumption, and waste management. In addition, the Bank's financing decisions may also create indirect environmental impacts, particularly where financing is extended to sectors or projects with higher environmental risk profiles.

Based on the 2025 bottom-up Climate Risk Management &amp; Scenario Analysis (CRMS) pilot, Permata Bank identified the impacts of climate risk on its financing activities. Over the long term, financing exposure to carbon-intensive sectors may contribute to higher NPLs, RWA, and ECL allowances, as these sectors are the most vulnerable to transition risks and are significant contributors to the Bank's financed emissions. The most significant deterioration in credit quality is projected to occur in the coal mining sector, the plantation and palm oil sector, and the manufacturing sector.

The CRMS assessment results were presented and socialized to senior management, business units, and other relevant units to build awareness of climate risks, although they have not yet been used as a primary reference in determining business strategy. The Bank is currently focused on improving climate risk-related data quality, strengthening measurement methodologies, and enhancing human resource capabilities so that assessment and measurement results are more credible and can be applied for broader purposes.

From an operational perspective, Permata Bank integrates environmental conservation principles into its operational policies and promotes the adoption of a sustainability culture across the Bank's internal activities.

As part of these efforts, in 2025 Permata Bank:

1. Conducted energy conservation campaigns through various internal platforms (e-mail blasts, Permata Portal/News, internal social media, and digital signage).
2. Replaced approximately 1,200 lights with more energy-efficient LED lights.
3. Replaced Board of Directors' vehicles with EVs.
4. Adjustment of electrical equipment start times (AC, elevators, and lights) by 30 minutes in the Bintaro Sector 7 Building, Tangerang.
5. Green building certification for the Bintaro Sector 7 Building in South Tangerang and the Merdeka Building in Bandung.
