# Permasalahan Penerapan Keuangan Berkelanjutan [E.5]

Issues in Sustainable Finance Implementation

Implementasi keuangan berkelanjutan di Permata Bank berlangsung dalam lanskap regulasi dan kondisi eksternal yang terus berubah. Perkembangan ketentuan, serta dinamika ekonomi, sosial, dan lingkungan, turut membentuk tantangan yang dihadapi Bank. Selama tahun 2025, Bank menghadapi sejumlah tantangan utama, antara lain:

- Peningkatan transaksi digital menambah eksposur Bank terhadap berbagai risiko keamanan informasi, sehingga diperlukan penguatan kontrol internal untuk melindungi data Bank dan nasabah dari potensi ancaman siber;
- Masih terbatasnya pemahaman nasabah terkait praktik transaksi digital yang aman meningkatkan risiko terjadinya kebocoran data pribadi;
- Pemahaman mengenai prinsip keuangan berkelanjutan dan aspek Lingkungan, Sosial, dan Tata Kelola (LST) yang belum merata di antara karyawan maupun nasabah;
- Kapasitas internal dalam memahami dan menilai dokumen lingkungan serta risiko iklim sebagai bagian dari persyaratan pemberian kredit masih terbatas;
- Persepsi bisnis berkelanjutan yang masih terbatas terhadap profitabilitas jangka pendek dari seluruh lini pemangku kepentingan dapat berdampak pada lambatnya pertumbuhan portofolio berkelanjutan.

The implementation of sustainable finance at Permata Bank takes place within a constantly changing regulatory landscape and external conditions. Evolving regulations, as well as economic, social, and environmental dynamics, contribute to shaping the challenges the Bank faces. Throughout 2025, the Bank faces several key challenges, including:

- The growing volume of digital transactions can increase the Bank's exposure to information security risks, thereby it is necessary to strengthen internal controls to protect Bank and customer data from potential cyber threats;
- Limited customer awareness of secure digital transaction practices can increase the risk of personal data breaches;
- The level of understanding regarding sustainable finance principles and Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) aspects remains uneven among both employees and customers;
- Internal capacity to interpret and assess environmental documentation and climate-related risks, as part of Bank's credit underwriting requirements, remains limited;
- Limited perception of sustainable business profitability in the short-term among stakeholders can potentially slow the growth of sustainable financing portfolio.
