## Pemangku kepentingan yang terhormat,

Sepanjang 2025, Permata Bank terus memperkuat perannya sebagai institusi keuangan yang mengintegrasikan prinsip keberlanjutan ke dalam strategi bisnis dan pengambilan keputusan. Dengan mencermati dinamika risiko dan peluang usaha yang semakin kompleks, Bank fokus pada upaya penguatan pembiayaan sesuai prinsip keuangan berkelanjutan, pengembangan solusi keuangan yang mendukung transisi ekonomi, serta pengelolaan risiko iklim. Integrasi aspek Lingkungan, Sosial, dan Tata Kelola (LST) dalam kegiatan usaha, yang sejalan dengan arah pencapaian Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan (TPB), dilakukan melalui perluasan pembiayaan inklusif, serta dukungan terhadap upaya penurunan emisi dalam operasional Bank.

## Dear respected stakeholders,

Throughout 2025, Permata Bank continued to strengthen its role as a financial institution that integrates sustainability principles into business strategy and decision-making. In response to increasingly complex risk and business opportunity dynamics, the Bank focused on strengthening financing aligned with sustainable finance principles, developing financial solutions that support the economic transition, and managing climate risk. The integration of Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) aspects into business activities, aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), was carried out through the expansion of inclusive financing and support for emission reduction efforts in the Bank's operations.
