# Penanggung Jawab Keuangan Berkelanjutan [E.1]
# Sustainable Finance Implementation Management

Kinerja keberlanjutan Permata Bank, termasuk pelaksanaan keuangan berkelanjutan, dijalankan melalui pendelegasian tugas, kewenangan, dan tanggung jawab yang jelas pada organisasi tata kelola, yang meliputi:

Permata Bank's sustainability performance, including the implementation of sustainable finance, is carried out through the delegation of clear duties, authorities, and responsibilities to the governance organization, which includes:

Tugas, Wewenang, dan Tanggung Jawab Keuangan Berkelanjutan
Sustainable-Finance-Related Roles, Authorities, and Responsibilities

|  Dewan Komisaris Board of Commissioners | 1. Memberikan hasil revu dan persetujuan atas Rencana Aksi Keuangan Berkelanjutan (RAKB) yang disampaikan kepada regulator secara tahunan. | 1. Reviews and approves the Sustainable Finance Action Plan (RAKB) submitted to regulators on an annual basis.  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|   |  2. Memberikan persetujuan dan evaluasi secara berkala atas strategi (tahunan), kerangka kerja dan kebijakan (minimum setiap tiga tahun sekali), dan target penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan (minimum tahunan) untuk memastikan penciptaan nilai jangka panjang dengan mempertimbangkan peluang, tantangan dan risiko penerapan LST. | 2. Approves and periodically evaluates the strategy (annually), frameworks and policies (at least once every three years), and targets for sustainable finance implementation (at least annually) to ensure long-term value creation, taking into account ESG opportunities, challenges, and risks.  |
|   |  3. Mengawasi pelaksanaan kinerja Bank sesuai dengan prinsip keuangan berkelanjutan. | 3. Oversees the Bank's performance in line with sustainable finance principles.  |
|   |  4. Mengawasi dan mengevaluasi penerapan strategi, kerangka kerja, kebijakan, dan pencapaian target keuangan berkelanjutan dan risiko iklim secara efektif. | 4. Oversees and evaluates the effective implementation of strategy, frameworks, policies, achievement of sustainable finance targets, and climate risk management.  |
|   |  5. Mengevaluasi kecukupan sumber daya, sistem, dan infrastruktur pendukung yang dibutuhkan Bank dalam penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan secara komprehensif dan efektif. | 5. Evaluates the adequacy of resources, systems, and supporting infrastructure required for comprehensive and effective sustainable finance implementation.  |

|   |  2. Menyusun strategi (tahunan), kerangka kerja dan kebijakan (minimum setiap tiga tahun sekali), dan target penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan (minimum tahunan). | 2. Develops strategy (annually), frameworks and policies (at least once every three years), and targets for sustainable finance implementation (at least annually).  |

|   |  5. Memastikan Bank telah mengembangkan dan mengimplementasikan proses untuk mengidentifikasi peluang dan risiko dari penerapan LST dan memahami potensi dampak risiko terkait LST terhadap bisnis Bank dan memperhitungkan risiko tersebut pada strategi bisnis dan kerangka manajemen risiko. | 5. Ensures the Bank develops and implements processes to identify ESG-related opportunities and risks, understands potential impacts on the Bank's business, and incorporates these risks into the Bank's business strategy and risk management framework.  |
|   |  6. Memastikan kecukupan sumber daya, sistem, dan infrastruktur pendukung yang dibutuhkan Bank dalam penerapan keuangan berkelanjutan. | 6. Ensures the adequacy of resources, systems, and supporting infrastructure required for sustainable finance implementation.  |

Evaluates sustainable finance strategy implementation and reports the results to the Board of Commissioners, regulators, and other relevant stakeholders.  |
