Tata Kelola Keberlanjutan Sustainability Governance

Pengelolaan Risiko Iklim di Permata Bank dilakukan melalui penguatan proses, tata kelola, dan koordinasi lintas fungsi. Upaya tersebut mencakup:

- Pembentukan Departemen Climate Risk Analytics pada tahun 2024 yang bertujuan untuk melakukan kajian yang lebih mendalam atas pengelolaan dan mitigasi masing-masing risiko terkait iklim.
- Pembentukan Divisi Sustainability di bawah Direktorat Corporate Affair and Sustainability yang bertanggung jawab untuk merancang mengembangkan keberlanjutan bank secara bankwide agar selaras dengan tujuan Permata Bank untuk mencapai target NZE operasional 2030 dan NZE pembiayaan 2052 serta memastikan kesesuaian dengan regulasi yang berlaku.
- Peningkatan dan pengembangan kapasitas Sumber Daya Manusia (SDM) dalam mengelola dampak risiko iklim melalui pelatihan-pelatihan eksternal.
- Pengembangan dan penerapan proses untuk memahami serta mengidentifikasi potensi dampak Risiko Iklim terhadap kegiatan usaha Bank, sekaligus mengelolanya dalam strategi bisnis dan kerangka manajemen risiko.
- Penetapan peran dan tanggung jawab Direksi, Dewan Komisaris, dan unit kerja terkait sesuai struktur organisasi, termasuk mekanisme koordinasi dan pengawasan dalam pengelolaan risiko iklim.
- Penyusunan kebijakan, prosedur, dan pengendalian internal yang efektif terkait manajemen risiko iklim.
- Integrasi risiko iklim ke dalam sistem pengendalian internal melalui penerapan model tiga lini guna memastikan proses identifikasi, pengukuran, dan mitigasi risiko berjalan secara tepat, komprehensif, dan efektif.
- Pembentukan focus group discussion (FGD) risiko iklim untuk membahas strategi, inisiatif, isu, status kemajuan, serta memperkuat koordinasi antar unit kerja yang berada di bawah lingkup risk framework owner (RFO).
- Penyampaian pembaruan informasi terkait Risiko Iklim kepada Komite Manajemen Risiko dan Komite Pemantau Risiko setidaknya satu kali dalam setahun.

Dalam pengelolaan risiko iklim, Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi menjalankan fungsi pengawasan secara aktif terhadap efektivitas sistem pengendalian internal. Pelaksanaan pengawasan tersebut dilakukan sesuai dengan kewenangan dan tanggung jawab masing-masing, yang didelegasikan kepada komite di bawah Dewan Komisaris serta komite di bawah Direksi. Climate Risk Management at Permata Bank is carried out through strengthening processes, governance, and cross-functional coordination. These efforts include:

- The establishment of the Climate Risk Analytics Department in 2024 aims to conduct more comprehensive studies on climate-related risk management and mitigation.
- Establishing the Sustainability Division under the Corporate Affairs and Sustainability Directorate, which is responsible for designing and advancing bank-wide sustainability in alignment with Permata Bank's targets to achieve Net Zero Emissions (NZE) for operations by 2030 and NZE for financing by 2052, and for ensuring compliance with applicable regulations.
- Enhancing and developing human capital capacity to manage climate risk impacts through external training programs.
- Development and implementation of processes to understand and identify the potential impacts of Climate Risk on the Bank's business activities, while managing them within the business strategy and risk management framework.
- Definition of the roles and responsibilities of the Board of Directors, Board of Commissioners, and relevant work units in accordance with the organizational structure, including coordination and oversight mechanisms for climate risk management.
- Development of effective policies, procedures, and internal controls related to climate risk management.
- Integration of climate risk into the internal control system through the implementation of the three lines model to ensure the processes of risk identification, measurement, and mitigation are carried out appropriately, comprehensively, and effectively.
- Establishment of a climate risk forum to strengthen coordination between work units under the risk framework owner (RFO).
- Providing updates on Climate Risk to the Risk Management Committee and Risk Oversight Committee at least once a year.

In managing climate risk, the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors actively oversee the effectiveness of the internal control system. This oversight is carried out in accordance with their respective authorities and responsibilities, which are delegated to committees under the Board of Commissioners and committees under the Board of Directors.
