## Sustainability Governance

Permata Bank menerapkan tata kelola perusahaan berdasarkan Peraturan OJK No. 17 Tahun 2023 tentang Penerapan Tata Kelola bagi Bank Umum (POJK 17/2023) serta Surat Edaran OJK No. 14/SEOJK.03/2025 tentang Penerapan Tata Kelola Bagi Bank Umum (SEOJK 14/2025) sebagai kerangka utama tata kelola perbankan. Penerapan tata Kelola di Bank berlandaskan pada prinsip keterbukaan (transparency), akuntabilitas (accountability), tanggung jawab (responsibility), independensi (independency), dan kewajaran (fairness), dengan mempertimbangkan aspek keberlanjutan dalam pengelolaan Bank. Selain itu, Permata Bank turut mengadopsi ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS) 2023, best practice, serta regulasi lain yang relevan. Sebagai bagian dari reviu pedoman dan kebijakan tata kelola Bank secara berkala, pada tahun 2025, Permata Bank melakukan penyesuaian Pedoman Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi dengan ketentuan SEOJK 14/2025.

Pada tahun 2025, Permata Bank telah memiliki Kebijakan Keberlanjutan, yang berfungsi sebagai kebijakan payung dalam setiap pelaksanaan keberlanjutan di operasional hingga bisnis Bank. Sejalan dengan visi keberlanjutan untuk menjadi mitra keuangan terpercaya yang mendorong transformasi menuju ekonomi yang berkelanjutan, inklusif, dan bertanggung jawab, Permata Bank mengintegrasikan prinsip keuangan berkelanjutan dalam seluruh aktivitas bisnisnya. Melalui misi keberlanjutan, Bank berkomitmen untuk menyediakan layanan keuangan yang inovatif dan bertanggung jawab, berkontribusi pada pelestarian lingkungan dan kesetaraan sosial, serta membangun bisnis yang transparan, akuntabel, dan beretika melalui pengelolaan risiko keberlanjutan yang efektif dan kolaborasi dengan para pemangku kepentingan.

Komitmen tersebut diimplementasikan melalui empat pilar keberlanjutan, yaitu Sustainable Business, Sustainable Operations, Sustainable Impact for Communities, dan Sustainability Governance. Keempat pilar ini mencakup pengembangan produk dan pembiayaan berkelanjutan, penerapan praktik operasional yang ramah lingkungan dan inklusif, pemberdayaan masyarakat melalui literasi keuangan dan program sosial-lingkungan, serta penguatan tata kelola yang baik, manajemen risiko, dan perlindungan pemangku kepentingan. Seluruh inisiatif keberlanjutan Permata Bank dilandasi oleh nilai inti I-PRICE, yaitu Integrity, Partnership, Responsiveness, Innovation, Caring, dan Excellence, serta selaras dengan Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan (TPB).

Permata Bank implements corporate governance in accordance with the provisions of POJK No. 17 of 2023 on the Implementation of Governance for Commercial Banks, which serves as the main framework for banking governance. In its development, the Bank adjusts its governance policies and procedures to align with evolving sustainability needs and challenges. These adjustments aim to ensure that the principles of transparency, accountability, responsibility, independence, and fairness continue to be applied consistently, while incorporating sustainability considerations into the Bank's management. In addition, Permata Bank has adopted the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS) 2023, best practices, and other relevant regulations. As part of its periodic review of the Bank's governance guidelines and policies, in 2025, Permata Bank updated the Board of Commissioners and Board of Directors Guidelines to align with SEOJK 14/2025.

In 2025, Permata Bank maintains its Sustainability Policy, which serves as an umbrella policy for the implementation of sustainability across the Bank's operations and business. In line with its sustainability vision to become a trusted financial partner that drives transformation toward a sustainable, inclusive, and responsible economy, Permata Bank integrates sustainable finance principles across all business activities. Through its sustainability mission, the Bank is committed to providing innovative and responsible financial services, contributing to environmental conservation and social equity, and building a transparent, accountable, and ethical business through effective sustainability risk management and collaboration with stakeholders.

This commitment is implemented through four sustainability pillars: Sustainable Business, Sustainable Operations, Sustainable Impact for Communities, and Sustainability Governance. These pillars cover the development of sustainable products and financing, the application of environmentally friendly and inclusive operational practices, community empowerment through financial literacy and social-environmental programs, and the strengthening of good governance, risk management, and stakeholder protection. All of Permata Bank's sustainability initiatives are guided by the core I-PRICE values of Integrity, Partnership, Responsiveness, Innovation, Caring, and Excellence; and align with the SDGs.
