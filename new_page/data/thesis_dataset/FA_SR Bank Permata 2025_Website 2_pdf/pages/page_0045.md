Melalui penguatan kapasitas internal, Permata Bank meningkatkan pemahaman dan kompetensi keuangan berkelanjutan melalui kolaborasi lintas unit, termasuk Divisi Sustainability, Divisi Learning & Development, Unit-unit Bisnis, Treasury, dan Risk. Upaya ini menjadi fondasi dalam pengembangan portofolio Kategori Kegiatan Usaha Berkelanjutan (KKUB) melalui berbagai inisiatif yang terintegrasi. Bank menyediakan solusi pembiayaan hijau seperti green mortgage, pembiayaan kendaraan tenaga listrik serta instrumen investasi berkelanjutan, termasuk green bonds. Selain itu, Bank mendorong inklusi keuangan dengan menyediakan pembiayaan bagi perorangan berpenghasilan rendah, serta memperluas akses layanan melalui digitalisasi dan pengembangan aplikasi maupun platform perbankan.

### Penyaluran Pembiayaan Inklusif

Pada tahun 2025, Permata Bank terus berperan aktif dalam mendorong inklusi keuangan melalui penyaluran pembiayaan kepada Usaha Mikro, Kecil, dan Menengah (UMKM) serta Perorangan Berpenghasilan Rendah (PBR). Pembiayaan inklusif dipandang sebagai instrumen strategis untuk memperluas akses layanan keuangan, mendorong pertumbuhan ekonomi, meningkatkan kesejahteraan masyarakat, serta mendukung stabilitas perekonomian nasional.

Sejalan dengan ketentuan regulator, Bank secara konsisten memenuhi Rasio Pembiayaan Inklusif Makroprudensial (RPIM) yang ditetapkan, dengan capaian pada akhir tahun pelaporan berada di atas batas minimum yang ditetapkan, yaitu sebesar 12,47% untuk konvensional dan 10,94% untuk syariah.

### Perluasan Layanan Digital

Di 2025, Permata Bank terus memperkuat pengembangan produk dan layanan perbankan digital sebagai bagian dari upaya menghadirkan solusi keuangan yang inovatif dan ramah lingkungan. Pemanfaatan layanan digital memungkinkan masyarakat, termasuk yang berada di wilayah dengan keterbatasan akses fisik ke kantor Bank, untuk memperoleh layanan keuangan secara mudah dan efisien. Di samping meningkatkan inklusi dan kenyamanan nasabah, digitalisasi juga berkontribusi pada pengurangan penggunaan kertas serta menekan kebutuhan mobilitas fisik, sehingga mendukung upaya pelestarian lingkungan.

Pada tahun 2025, transaksi QRIS pada aplikasi Permata Me mengalami peningkatan sebesar 83% dibandingkan periode sebelumnya.

Through the strengthening of internal capacity, Permata Bank enhances understanding and competencies in sustainable finance through cross-unit collaboration, including the Sustainability Division, Learning & Development Division, Business Units, Treasury, and Risk. These efforts form the foundation for the development of the Sustainable Business Activity Category (Kategori Kegiatan Usaha Berkelanjutan/ KKUB) portfolio through various integrated initiatives. The Bank provides green financing solutions such as green mortgages, electric vehicle financing, and sustainable investment instruments, including green bonds. In addition, the Bank promotes financial inclusion by providing financing to low-income individuals, as well as expanding access to services through digitalisation and the development of banking applications and platforms.

### Inclusive Financing Disbursement

In 2025, Permata Bank continued to play an active role in promoting financial inclusion through the provision of financing to Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) and Low-Income Individuals. Inclusive financing is regarded as a strategic instrument to expand access to financial services, encourage economic growth, improve community welfare, and support national economic stability.

In line with regulatory requirements, the Bank consistently complies with the stipulated Macroprudential Inclusive Financing Ratio (Rasio Pembiayaan Inklusif Makroprudensial/ RPIM), with the achievement at the end of the reporting year exceeding the prescribed minimum threshold, amounting to 12.47% for conventional banking and 10.94% for sharia banking.

### Expansion of Digital Services

In 2025, Permata Bank continued to strengthen the development of digital banking products and services as part of its efforts to deliver innovative and environmentally friendly financial solutions. The use of digital services enables the public, including those in areas with limited physical access to Bank branches, to obtain financial services easily and efficiently. In addition to enhancing financial inclusion and customer convenience, digitalisation also contributes to reducing paper usage and minimising the need for physical mobility, thereby supporting environmental conservation efforts.

In 2025, QRIS transactions on the Permata Me application increased by 83% compared to the previous period.
