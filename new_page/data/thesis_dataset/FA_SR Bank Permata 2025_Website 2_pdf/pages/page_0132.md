5. Managing relationships with regulators and submitting compliance reports in a timely manner in accordance with the Regulatory Relations Management Policy and DOI for Compliance Reporting.

On the other hand, the Legal Advisory Division ensures the Bank's compliance with laws and regulations through the implementation of duties and responsibilities that refer to the Legal Advisory Procedures, namely:

1. Providing legal reviews and issuing opinions on every transaction to be executed, new products and services to be issued by the Bank.
2. Creating standard legal documents for new and/or developed products and services issued by the Bank.
3. Periodically reviewing existing legal document standards in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
4. Develop and update, review legal policy and procedure.
5. Reviewing/providing legal reviews of policies/procedures from other units related to legal matters, upon request by the relevant unit.
6. Providing legal information and knowledge to relevant units on a regular basis.
7. Assisting relevant units in conducting legal reviews and providing opinions on the Bank's corporate actions.

Until the end of 2025, Permata Bank faces civil lawsuits from debtors or other third parties, which are generally related to the execution of collateral by Permata Bank on of debtor debts. None of these legal issues are material and do not affect the Bank's status, position, business continuity, or financial condition.
