Sepanjang tahun 2025, Permata Bank menerima sebanyak 30,201 keluhan nasabah, dan 98% di antaranya telah diselesaikan. Adapun sisanya masih dalam proses penanganan hingga akhir tahun 2025. Keluhan nasabah umumnya terkait dengan layanan e-banking, aplikasi mobile dan kendala transaksi. Pada periode yang sama, Bank tidak menerima pengaduan dari masyarakat yang berkaitan dengan isu lingkungan maupun sosial. [F.16]

Throughout 2025, Permata Bank received a total of 30,201 customer complaints, of which 98% had been resolved. The remaining cases were still in process as of the end of 2025. Customer complaints were generally related to e-banking services mobile applications and transaction issues. During the same period the Bank did not receive any public complaints related to environmental or social issues. [F.16]
