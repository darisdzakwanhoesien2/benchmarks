# SUSTAINABLE LEARNING SERIES

Permata Bank menyelenggarakan berbagai program pengembangan kompetensi yang mencakup pemahaman atas karakteristik produk dan/atau jasa keuangan berkelanjutan serta pengelolaan risikonya. Upaya tersebut dilaksanakan melalui pelatihan, webinar, dan program pembelajaran lainnya yang tergabung dalam Sustainable Learning Series. Adapun tema kegiatan yang diselenggarakan antara lain:

- How to Improving of Good Quality Sustainability Report
- Introduction to Sustainable Finance
- Sustainability Series - Green Financing
- Renewable Energy Projects
- Atomic Green Habits: Shaping Excellence for A Sustainable Tomorrow
- Banking on Net Zero: Turning Climate Risk into Opportunity for Strategic Growth

Sepanjang tahun 2025, Permata Bank memperkuat pemahaman karyawan mengenai keuangan berkelanjutan melalui penyediaan perangkat self-learning pada platform pembelajaran digital. Bank merilis video e-learning ke 6 di Permata Learning (PEARL) yang membahas topik keuangan berkelanjutan dan penerapan aspek LST, yaitu Sustainable Finance Implementation in Permata Bank. Di samping itu, Bank juga menyampaikan 30 email blast Sustainability Journey kepada karyawan sebagai media edukasi berkelanjutan.

# SUSTAINABLE LEARNING SERIES

Permata Bank provides various competency development programs that build understanding of the characteristics of sustainable financial products and/or services and their risk management. These efforts are delivered through training, webinars, and other learning programs under the Sustainable Learning Series. Activity themes conducted include:

- How to Improving of Good Quality Sustainability Report
- Introduction to Sustainable Finance
- Sustainability Series - Green Financing
- Renewable Energy Projects
- Atomic Green Habits: Shaping Excellence for A Sustainable Tomorrow
- Banking on Net Zero: Turning Climate Risk into Opportunity for Strategic Growth

Throughout 2025, Permata Bank strengthened employees' understanding of sustainable finance by providing self-learning materials on its digital learning platform. The Bank released its sixth e-learning video on Permata Learning (PEARL), which discusses sustainable finance and the implementation of ESG, titled Sustainable Finance Implementation in Permata Bank. In addition, the Bank delivered 30 Sustainability Journey email blasts to employees as a continues education channel.
