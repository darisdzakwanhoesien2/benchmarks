# PELATIHAN ANTIKORUPSI DAN ANTI-FRAUD

[205-2]

Menyadari potensi dampak finansial yang dapat ditimbulkan oleh korupsi dan fraud, baik secara langsung maupun tidak langsung terhadap Bank dan nasabah, Permata Bank menempatkan pengelolaan risiko fraud sebagai bagian penting dari tata kelola risiko. Untuk tujuan tersebut, Bank memiliki Divisi Fraud Risk Management (FRM) yang bertanggung jawab untuk menyusun strategi anti fraud secara efektif dan efisien dalam mengelola risiko fraud, memberikan saran dan pendapat kepada seluruh unit di Permata Bank mengenai manajemen risiko fraud melalui namun tidak terbatas pada identifikasi risiko terjadinya Fraud yang melekat pada setiap aktivitas yang berpotensi merugikan Bank, serta melakukan pelaporan fraud ke OJK.

Sosialisasi kebijakan antikorupsi, anti-fraud, dan kode etik di Permata Bank dilakukan melalui berbagai kanal komunikasi dan pembelajaran, seperti refreshment kode etik, program kesadaran anti-fraud, roadshow, email blast, desktop wallpaper, serta kewajiban e-learning terkait SMAP, anti-fraud awareness, dan kode etik. Program ini menjangkau seluruh karyawan, termasuk karyawan baru, karyawan tetap, karyawan tidak tetap/pekerja alih daya. Selain internal, Bank juga menyelenggarakan sosialisasi antikorupsi secara offline dan online kepada pihak ketiga. Sebanyak 49 vendor telah mengikuti sosialisasi tersebut, sebagai bagian dari upaya pencegahan korupsi, dengan ketentuan antikorupsi dan antipenyuapan yang telah dituangkan dalam klausul perjanjian kerja sama.

# ANTI-CORRUPTION AND ANTI-FRAUD TRAINING

[205-2]

Recognizing the potential financial impact that corruption and fraud can have, both directly and indirectly, on the Bank and its customers, Permata Bank places fraud risk management as an important part of its risk governance. To that end, the Bank has a Fraud Risk Management (FRM) Division that is responsible for developing effective and efficient anti-fraud strategies to manage fraud risk, providing advice and opinions to all units at Permata Bank regarding fraud risk management through, but not limited to, identifying the risk of fraud inherent in every activity that could potentially harm the Bank, as well as reporting fraud to the Financial Services Authority.

Permata Bank's anti-corruption, anti-fraud, and code of ethics policies are disseminated through various communication and learning channels, such as code of ethics refreshers, anti-fraud awareness programs, roadshows, email blasts, desktop wallpapers, and mandatory e-learning related to the SMAP (Standard Assessment System), anti-fraud awareness, and the code of ethics. This program reaches to all employees, including new hires, permanent employees, and temporary/outsourced workers. In addition to internal outreach, the Bank also conducts offline and online anti-corruption outreach to third parties. A total of 49 vendors have participated in these outreach programs as part of its corruption prevention efforts, in accordance with the anti-corruption and anti-bribery provisions outlined in the cooperation agreement.

# INSIDEN KORUPSI TAHUN 2025 [205-3]

Sepanjang tahun 2025, terdapat 1 kasus korupsi. Permata Bank telah menindaklanjuti insiden tersebut secara tegas, termasuk dengan pemberian sanksi berupa pemberhentian. Insiden tersebut tidak memiliki pengaruh signifikan pada kelangsungan bisnis bank. Lebih lanjut, tidak terdapat insiden korupsi yang melibatkan mitra bisnis dan tidak terdapat gugatan kasus korupsi yang diajukan oleh publik kepada Permata Bank maupun karyawan Perusahaan.

# CORRUPTION INCIDENTS IN 2025 [205-3]

Throughout 2025, there was one corruption case. Permata Bank followed up firmly, including by imposing sanctions in the form of termination of employment. The incident did not have a significant impact on the continuity of the Bank's business. In addition, there were no corruption incidents involving business partners, and no corruption-related lawsuits were filed by the public against Permata Bank or the Bank's employees.
