# LITERASI KEUANGAN [203-2][FN-CB-240a.4]

Dalam rangka meningkatkan kemampuan masyarakat dalam mengambil keputusan keuangan yang tepat serta mendorong perilaku pengelolaan keuangan yang bertanggung jawab, Permata Bank secara aktif menyelenggarakan kegiatan edukasi literasi dan inklusi keuangan. Kegiatan-kegiatan tersebut dilaksanakan oleh departemen-departemen/unit-unit bisnis serta Unit CSER dilakukan berkolaborasi bersama mitra, pelaku unit jasa keuangan (PUJK) lain, dan komunitas. Pelaksanaan program ini mengacu pada regulasi yang berlaku.

![img-93.jpeg](img-93.jpeg)

Pada tahun 2025, Permata Bank telah merealisasikan lebih dari 300 kegiatan edukasi literasi dan inklusi yang dilakukan dalam skala nasional maupun lokal area/cabang. Bank juga mensupport Gerakan Nasional Cerdas Keuangan (GENCARKAN) yang diinisiasi oleh OJK, melalui berbagai kegiatan yang dilakukan unit - unit bisnis dan kantor - kantor cabang. Permata Bank ditunjuk sebagai Campaign Manager oleh OJK untuk mengadakan Literasi Keuangan di 6 area dengan tingkat literasi rendah, seperti Kabupaten Serang, Kabupaten Kutai Timur, Kota Serang, Kota Sukabumi, Kabupaten Gresik, dan Kota Medan.

Kegiatan yang dilakukan seperti Permata Bankir Cilik, Permata CERITA, Guruku: Permata Finansial di Kota Sukabumi dan Kota Serang, Edukasi Keuangan ke kampus UIN di Kota Serang, kepada atlit layar di Anyer, serta sosialisasi pembayaran digital kepada petani di Sangatta, Kabupaten Kutai Timur.

# FINANCIAL LITERACY [203-2][FN-CB-240a.4]

In order to enhance the public's ability to make sound financial decisions and promote responsible financial management behavior, Permata Bank actively organizes financial literacy and inclusion education activities. These activities are carried out by various departments and business units, as well as the CSER Unit, in collaboration with partners, other financial services institutions, and communities. The implementation of these programs refers to applicable regulations.

![img-94.jpeg](img-94.jpeg)

In 2025, Permata Bank carried out more than 300 financial literacy and inclusion education activities conducted at both national and local branch levels. The Bank also supported the National Financial Literacy Movement (GENCARKAN), initiated by Financial Services Authority, through various programs delivered by business units and branch offices. In addition, Permata Bank was appointed by Financial Services Authority as a Campaign Manager to conduct financial literacy programs across 6 areas with low literacy capability, such as Serang Regency, East Kutai Regency, Serang, Sukabumi, Gresik Regency, and Medan.

The activities conducted included Permata Bankir Cilik, Permata CERITA, and "Guruku: Permata Finansial" in Sukabumi and Serang, financial education sessions at UIN University in Serang, financial education for sailing athletes in Anyer, and digital payment socialization for farmers in Sangatta, East Kutai Regency.
