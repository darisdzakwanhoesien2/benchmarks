PERMATA LEARNING WEEK (PLW)

Permata Learning Week (PLW) kembali diselenggarakan pada 5 – 7 Agustus 2025 sebagai bagian dari penguatan budaya pembelajaran di Permata Bank. Dengan tema “Thrive for Excellence – Leveraging AI to Unlock Potential”. Program ini mencakup 17 sesi pembelajaran yang diikuti oleh 3.721 PermataBankers dengan tingkat partisipasi 18.614 meningkat 47% dari tahun sebelumnya. PLW mengangkat topik keberlanjutan, artificial intelligence (AI) dan well-being, serta dilengkapi berbagai aktivitas interaktif, termasuk AI Photobooth, Aura Reading, Tarot, Art &amp; Crafts, Cooking Class, dan standing booth. Seluruh sesi pembelajaran tersedia di platform PEARL.

PERMATA LEARNING WEEK (PLW)

Permata Learning Week (PLW) was held again on August 5 – 7, 2025 as part of strengthening the learning culture at Permata Bank. With the theme “Thrive for Excellence - Leveraging AI to Unlock Potential”. This program included 17 learning sessions attended by 3,721 PermataBankers with a participation rate of 18,614, an increase of 47% from the previous year. PLW raised the topics of sustainability, artificial intelligence (AI) and well-being, and was equipped with various interactive activities, including AI Photobooth, Aura Reading, Tarot, Arts &amp; Crafts, Cooking Class, and standing booths. All learning sessions are available on the PEARL platform.
