# CUTI KARYAWAN

Karyawan perempuan memperoleh hak cuti melahirkan selama 3 bulan dan cuti keguguran hingga 1,5 bulan, sementara karyawan laki-laki berhak atas cuti ayah selama 5 hari serta cuti keguguran selama 3 hari. Bank memastikan bahwa pengambilan cuti tidak mengakibatkan hilangnya status maupun hak karyawan. Sepanjang tahun 2025, tercatat 249 karyawan, terdiri dari 132 perempuan dan 117 laki-laki, telah memanfaatkan hak cuti melahirkan dan cuti ayah, dengan tingkat kembali bekerja sebesar 100%. [401-3]

Permata Bank memberikan fleksibilitas pengaturan cuti melalui pemberian cuti di luar tanggungan (unpaid leave) berdasarkan ketentuan tertentu, tanpa menghilangkan manfaat asuransi kesehatan dan manfaat lainnya. Bank juga menyediakan cuti khusus, termasuk cuti ibadah, cuti ulang tahun yang dapat diambil satu hari pada hari ulang tahun karyawan, serta cuti CSER bagi karyawan yang terlibat dalam kegiatan tanggung jawab sosial dan lingkungan.

# SURVEI KETERIKATAN KARYAWAN [2-16][2-26]

Permata Bank melakukan evaluasi atas strategi pengelolaan PermataBankers melalui pengukuran tingkat pengalaman karyawan yang dilaksanakan melalui Voice of PermataBankers Survey oleh konsultan global independen. Pada tahun 2025, 82% karyawan menyebut Permata Bank sebagai "Great Place To Work", dan 75% karyawan menyatakan memiliki pengalaman kerja positif.

# PENGADUAN MASALAH KETENAGAKERJAAN [2-26][FN-CB-510a.2]

Permata Bank menerapkan prosedur Penanganan Keluh Kesah untuk memastikan setiap permasalahan karyawan ditangani secara langsung, adil, dan objektif. PermataBankers dapat menyampaikan keluhan ketenagakerjaan secara informal melalui diskusi dengan atasan. Apabila belum terselesaikan, keluhan dapat diteruskan kepada Direktorat People &amp; Culture dan/atau melalui serikat pekerja untuk disampaikan kepada Direktorat People &amp; Culture dengan dukungan advokasi dari serikat pekerja sesuai ketentuan peraturan perundang-undangan. Selain mekanisme informal, Bank juga menyediakan saluran formal melalui whistleblowing system (WBS) yang dapat diakses melalui surat elektronik, telepon, maupun e-form pada situs web Permata Bank.

# EMPLOYEE LEAVE

Female employees are entitled to 3 months of maternity leave and up to 1.5 months of miscarriage leave, while male employees are entitled to 5 days of paternity leave and 3 days of miscarriage leave. The Bank ensures that taking leave does not result in the loss of an employee's status or rights. Throughout 2025, there were 249 employees, consisting of 132 women and 117 men, utilized maternity and paternity leave entitlements, with a 100% return-to-work rate. [401-3]

Permata Bank provides flexibility in leave arrangements through unpaid leave, subject to certain conditions, without eliminating health insurance coverage and other benefits. The Bank also provides special leave, including religious leave, birthday leave that may be taken as one day on the employee's birthday, and CSER leave for employees involved in social and environmental responsibility activities.

# EMPLOYEE ENGAGEMENT SURVEY [2-16][2-26]

Permata Bank evaluates its management strategy for PermataBankers by measuring employee experience levels through the Voice of PermataBankers Survey conducted by an independent global consultant. By 2025, 82% of employees will call Permata Bank a "Great Place to Work," and 75% will report having a positive work experience.

# LABOR-RELATED GRIEVANCES [2-26] [FN-CB-510a.2]

Permata Bank implements a Grievance Handling procedure to ensure that employee issues are addressed promptly, fairly, and objectively. PermataBankers may raise employment-related grievances informally through discussions with their supervisors. If it has not been resolved, the complaint can be forwarded to the People &amp; Culture Directorate and/or through the labor union to be submitted to the People &amp; Culture Directorate supported by the labor union advocacy in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. In addition to informal mechanisms, the Bank also provides formal channels through the whistleblowing system (WBS), accessible via email, telephone, or an e-form on Permata Bank's website.
