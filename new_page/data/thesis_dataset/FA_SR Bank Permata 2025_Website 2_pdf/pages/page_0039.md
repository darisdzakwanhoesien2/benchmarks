# Rencana Aksi Keuangan Berkelanjutan (RAKB)
Sustainable Finance Action Plan (RAKB)

Pelaksanaan keuangan berkelanjutan pada tahun 2025 dijalankan Permata Bank dengan mengacu pada Rencana Aksi Keuangan Berkelanjutan (RAKB) Periode 2024-2028 yang telah ditetapkan sebagai panduan strategis jangka menengah. Progress pencapaian RAKB dilaporkan setiap tahun kepada Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK).

Dalam implementasinya sepanjang periode pelaporan 2025, Bank memfokuskan pelaksanaan RAKB pada sejumlah prioritas strategis. Fokus tersebut mencakup:

1. Meningkatkan portofolio pembiayaan sesuai kriteria keuangan berkelanjutan dari regulator;
2. Mengembangkan produk/solusi pembiayaan berkelanjutan;
3. Mengelola risiko terkait iklim;
4. Mendukung tercapainya net zero emission (NZE) dalam kegiatan operasional Bank;
5. Menerapkan pengadaan berkelanjutan;
6. Melaksanakan seri pembelajaran terkait keberlanjutan;
7. Menerapkan diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI); dan
8. Menciptakan dampak positif bagi lingkungan dan sosial masyarakat.

Ke depan, Permata Bank akan terus melanjutkan implementasi keuangan berkelanjutan melalui 8 fokus tersebut dan ditambah fokus pada pencapaian NZE dalam kegiatan pembiayaan. Permata Bank telah menetapkan target jangka panjang untuk NZE dalam kegiatan operasional (scope 1 dan 2) pada 2030 dan NZE dalam kegiatan pembiayaan pada 2052.

Selama tahun 2025, peningkatan kompetensi karyawan dilakukan secara berkelanjutan sebagai landasan dalam mendukung keberhasilan RAKB, melalui berbagai program pembelajaran di bidang keberlanjutan yang dikembangkan oleh Divisi Sustainability berkoordinasi dengan Divisi Learning &amp; Development. Upaya tersebut diperkuat dengan adanya penyelarasan struktur organisasi, budaya, sistem pengendalian, serta kebijakan dan prosedur operasional agar sejalan dengan prinsip keberlanjutan.

The implementation of sustainable finance in 2025 by Permata Bank was carried out with reference to the Sustainable Finance Action Plan (Rencana Aksi Keuangan Berkelanjutan/RAKB) for the 2024-2028 period, which has been established as a medium-term strategic guideline. Progress on the achievement of the RAKB is reported annually to the Financial Services Authority (OJK).

In its implementation throughout the 2025 reporting period, the Bank focused the execution of the RAKB on several strategic priorities. These priorities include:

1. Increasing the financing portfolio in line with the regulator's sustainable finance criteria;
2. Developing sustainable financing products and solutions;
3. Managing climate-related risks;
4. Supporting the achievement of net zero emissions (NZE) in the Bank's operational activities;
5. Implementing sustainable procurement practices;
6. Conducting a series of learning programs related to sustainability;
7. Implementing diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI); and
8. Creating positive environmental and social impacts for the community.

Going forward, Permata Bank will continue to implement sustainable finance through the 8 focus areas and will further strengthen its focus on achieving NZE in its financing activities. Permata Bank has set long-term targets to achieve NZE in its operational activities (Scope 1 and Scope 2) by 2030, and NZE in its financing activities by 2052.

Throughout 2025, employee competency development will be continuously implemented as a foundation for supporting the success of the Sustainability Action Plan (RAKB), through various sustainability learning programs developed by the Sustainability Division in coordination with the Learning &amp; Development Division. These efforts will be strengthened by aligning the organizational structure, culture, control systems, and operational policies and procedures to align with sustainability principles.
