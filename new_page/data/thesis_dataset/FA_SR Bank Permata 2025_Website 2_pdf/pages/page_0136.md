Speak-Up

Permata Bank menyediakan mekanisme Speak-Up sebagai sarana bagi PermataBankers, nasabah, dan pihak ketiga untuk melaporkan dugaan pelanggaran dan malpraktik, termasuk fraud maupun pelanggaran kode etik. Bank menjamin kerahasiaan atas setiap laporan yang disampaikan melalui Speak-Up, termasuk perlindungan terhadap identitas dan kepentingan seluruh pihak pelapor.

Kerahasiaan dan perlindungan pelapor menjadi prinsip utama dalam pengelolaan Speak-Up. Pelaporan tidak memengaruhi penilaian kinerja maupun perjalanan karier pelapor, dan hanya diketahui oleh pihak yang berwenang, yaitu Administrator Speak-Up, PIC Speak-Up, dan Speak-Up Council. Setiap laporan dicatat oleh Administrator Speak-Up dalam register dan dikategorikan ke dalam dugaan fraud, pelanggaran kode etik, atau laporan umum lainnya, termasuk keluhan dan saran.

Dua dari tiga saluran Speak Up yang dimiliki oleh Permata Bank yaitu formulir elektronik yang terdapat dalam situs web Permata Bank dan nomor telepon, selain dapat digunakan oleh Karyawan Permata Bank, dapat juga digunakan oleh Nasabah maupun Pihak Ketiga lainnya untuk menyampaikan laporan perihal potensi, rencana, atau tindakan pelanggaran.

Permata Bank provides a Speak-Up mechanism as a means for PermataBankers, customers, and third parties to report suspected violations and malpractice, including fraud and violations of the code of ethics. The Bank guarantees the confidentiality of every report submitted through Speak-Up, including protection of the identity and interests of all reporting parties.

Confidentiality and protection of reporters are key principles in managing Speak-Up. Reports do not impact the reporter's performance assessment or career path, and are only known to authorized parties: the Speak-Up Administrator, the Speak-Up PIC, and the Speak-Up Council. Each report is recorded by the Speak-Up Administrator in a register and categorized as suspected fraud, violation of the code of ethics, or other general reports, including complaints and suggestions.

Two of Permata Bank's three Speak-Up channels, namely the electronic form available on the Permata Bank website and the dedicated phone number, can be used not only by Permata Bank employees but also by customers and other third parties to submit reports regarding potential, planned, or actual violations.

The Speak-Up channels are as follows:
