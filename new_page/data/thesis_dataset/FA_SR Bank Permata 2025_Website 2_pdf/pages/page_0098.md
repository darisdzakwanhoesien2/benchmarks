Pelaksanaan CERITA dihadiri oleh Direksi dan pejabat Permata Bank, serta perwakilan OJK, Bank Indonesia (BI), Kementrian Pendidikan Dasar dan Menengah (Kemendikdasmen), dan pemerintah daerah. Selain edukasi literasi keuangan, Bank turut menyalurkan bantuan prasarana sekolah dan lingkungan, meliputi perbaikan ruang kelas, sarana olahraga, perpustakaan, alat bantu ajar, penanaman apotek hidup dan pohon, serta penyediaan tempat sampah. Program CERITA diselenggarakan secara serentak di 23 kota pada 10 Oktober 2025, dengan melibatkan lebih dari 500 EVO dan menjangkau 2.485 siswa penerima manfaat.

# AKTIVITAS UNIT PENGELOLA ZAKAT DAN DANA KEBAJIKAN (UPZDK)

Permata Bank melalui unit usahanya, yaitu Unit Usaha Syariah (UUS), bekerja sama dengan berbagai mitra dalam pelaksanaan berbagai program tanggung jawab sosial melalui Unit Pengelola Zakat dan Dana Kebajikan (UPZDK). Pada tahun 2025, UPZDK menyalurkan dana sebesar Rp17 Miliar yang diberikan kepada 32.121 penerima manfaat yang terbagi dalam asnaf Miskin, Fisabilillah, Gharimin, dan Mualaf serta 26 lembaga/yayasan. Adapun pelaksanaan kegiatan UPZDK terbagi ke dalam 6 pilar program, antara lain:

1. Pendidikan
UPZDK secara aktif dan berkelanjutan dalam upaya memperluas akses dan kualitas pendidikan melalui pemberian program beasiswa kepada mahasiswa di berbagai perguruan tinggi negeri/swasta yang memiliki kendalan dalam finansial, pemberian beasiswa ekonomi penyediaan ruang belajar yang inklusif kepada anak-anak prasejahtera, pemberian dukungan untuk sertifikasi para guru yang memadai dan berbagai program yang dilaksanakan sebagai bentuk komitmen untuk membantu para penerima manfaat dapat melanjutkan pendidikan, meningkatkan kompetensi, serta mendorong terwujudnya kemandirian dan peningkatan kulitas sumber daya manusia.

2. Kesehatan
UPZDK berkomitmen dalam membantu pemenuhan hak dasar kesehatan masyarakat melalui akses layanan kesehatan bagi masyarakat kurang mampu melalui kerja sama dengan berbagai mitra ataupun rumah sakit. Bantuan diberikan melalui pemberian obat-obatan gratis, khitanan massal, penyediaan ambulance gratis, serta pemberian bantuan Kesehatan lainnya sesuai dengan kebutuhan penerima manfaat. Melalui program kesehatan, diharapkan dapat memberikan manfaat, meringankan beban biaya pengobatan, serta mendukung peningkatan kualitas hidup dan kesejahteraan penerima manfaat.

The implementation of CERITA was attended by the Board of Directors and officials of Permata Bank, as well as representatives from Financial Services Authority, Bank Indonesia, the Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education, and local governments. In addition to financial literacy education, the Bank also distributed assistance for school and environmental infrastructure, including classroom improvements, sports facilities, libraries, teaching aids, planting medicinal plants and trees, and providing waste bins. The CERITA program was held simultaneously in 23 cities on 10 October 2025, involving more than 500 volunteers and reaching 2,485 beneficiary students.

# UPZDK ACTIVITIES

Through its business unit, the Sharia Business Unit (SBU), Permata Bank partners with various organizations to implement social responsibility programs through the Zakat and Benevolent Funds Management Unit (UPZDK). In 2025, UPZDK disbursed IDR 17 billion to 32,121 beneficiaries across the asnaf categories of Miskin, Fisabilillah, Gharimin, and Mualaf, as well as 26 institutions/foundations. UPZDK activities are implemented under 6 program pillars, as follows:

1. Education
UPZDK actively and continuously expands access to and improves the quality of education through scholarship programs for students at various public and private universities facing financial constraints, economic scholarships and the provision of inclusive learning spaces for underprivileged children, support for adequate teacher certification, and other programs. These efforts reflect a commitment to helping beneficiaries continue their education, enhance competencies, and promote independence and improved human capital quality.

2. Health
UPZDK is committed to supporting the fulfillment of basic health rights by providing access to health services for underprivileged communities through cooperation with various partners and hospitals. Assistance includes free medicines, mass circumcision programs, free ambulance services, and other health support tailored to beneficiary needs. Through these health programs, UPZDK aims to deliver benefits, ease the burden of medical costs, and support improvements in beneficiaries' quality of life and well-being.
