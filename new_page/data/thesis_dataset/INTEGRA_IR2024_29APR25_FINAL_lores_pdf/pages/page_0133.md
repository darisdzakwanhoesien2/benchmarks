Pada tahun 2024, kebijakan CSR dalam bidang sosial kemasyarakatan difokuskan pada sektor kesehatan, pendidikan, dan pemberdayaan masyarakat, yang diselenggarakan melalui berbagai program seperti donasi sembako, bantuan bahan bangunan, penyediaan lahan PKL gratis di sekitar pabrik, serta bantuan penanganan musibah.

Anggaran dana program CSR Perseroan tahun 2024 mencapai Rp704.338.600 dan telah didistribusikan seluruhnya secara proporsional untuk merealisasikan program-program tersebut di atas. Perseroan bertekad memberikan nilai tambah bagi stakeholder dan memberikan manfaat sebanyak mungkin bagi masyarakat. Beragam kegiatan sosial kemasyarakatan yang telah dijalankan merupakan wujud kepedulian Perseroan kepada masyarakat, khususnya masyarakat di sekitar wilayah operasional Perseroan.

In 2024, our CSR policy in the social community sector was focused on health, education, and community empowerment sectors, organized through various programs, such as donations in the form of basic needs, building materials, and free space areas for street vendors around the factory, as well as assistance for handling disasters.

The Company's CSR program allocation for 2024 reached Rp704,338,600 and has been distributed proportionally to realize the above programs. The Company is determined to provide added value to stakeholders and provide as much benefit as possible to the community. Various social community activities that have been carried out are a form of the Company's concern for the community, especially the community around the Company's operational areas.
