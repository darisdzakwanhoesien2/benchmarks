## Tugas dan Tanggung Jawab Dewan Komisaris

Lingkup tugas pengawasan Dewan Komisaris Perseroan selama tahun 2024 adalah melakukan pengawasan dan penasihatan atas kebijakan dan jalannya pengurusan pada umumnya, baik mengenai Perseroan maupun usaha Perseroan, memberikan nasihat kepada Jajaran Direksi, serta memantau dan memastikan bahwa prinsip-prinsip Tata Kelola Perusahaan yang Baik telah diterapkan secara efektif dan berkelanjutan.

Diskusi dan evaluasi berkala juga kami lakukan untuk menilai hasil kinerja yang tercapai sekaligus memperbaiki berbagai hal dengan segera jika diperlukan. Hal ini kami lakukan baik melalui rapat Dewan Komisaris sebanyak enam kali maupun rapat gabungan dengan Direksi sebanyak empat kali selama tahun 2024.

## Kinerja Direksi Menurut Pandangan Dewan Komisaris

Dalam pandangan kami, Direksi dan PT Integra Indocabinet Tbk telah melakukan berbagai upaya untuk mengatasi berbagai tantangan usaha dan mempertahankan kinerjanya, seperti meningkatkan efisiensi operasional, perluasan pangsa pasar – terutama di negara AS– dan meningkatkan penjualan online. Upaya Perseroan tersebut membuahkan hasil kinerja operasional dan keuangan yang cukup signifikan di tahun 2024.

Penjualan bersih Perseroan di tahun 2024 mencapai Rp2,79 triliun atau naik 27,7% dari tahun 2023, resource quality, and expanding export markets, including penetrating non-traditional markets such as India.

Despite challenges in the domestic market due to a shrinking middle class and weakened purchasing power, industry players remain optimistic about export performance. The global furniture market is projected to reach US$766 billion by the end of 2024, presenting opportunities for Indonesia's furniture exports to grow.

To support this expansion, the Indonesian Furniture and Handicraft Industry Association (Asmindo) has developed a roadmap emphasizing export development and increased domestic market dominance. These initiatives are expected to strengthen Indonesia's position in the global furniture market and drive national economic growth.

## Duties and Responsibilities of the Board of Commissioners

The scope of supervisory of the Board is to conduct supervision and advisory on the policies and general management regarding both the Company and its business, to provide advice to the Board of Directors, to monitor and ensure that the principles of Good Corporate Governance have been effectively and sustainably applied.

Periodic discussions and evaluations are also conducted to assess the achieved performance results while making immediate improvement if needed. We have done it through the meeting of the Board for six times and joint meetings with the Board of Directors for four times in 2024.

## Consideration for the Board of Directors' Performance

In our view, the Board of Directors and PT Integra Indocabinet Tbk have made various efforts to overcome the impact of varied challenges and maintain its performance, such as improving operational efficiency, expanding market share –especially in the US– and increasing online sales. These efforts resulted in significant operational and financial performance in 2024.
