## B. Extraordinary GMS

The Extraordinary GMS fulfilled the quorum with the attended shareholders representing 5,355,495,600 shares or 85.49% of all shares issued and paid-up by the Company.

## Resolutions of Extraordinary GMS:

### 1. First Meeting Agenda

Approved to grant authority to the Board of Directors with the approval of the Board of Commissioners to increase financing facilities and pledge/mortgage the Company assets for business expansion purposes.

### 2. Second Meeting Agenda

Discussion on the feasibility study of additional business fields for the Company based on Article 3 of the Company's Articles of Association.

### 3. Third Meeting Agenda

- Sawmill industry
- Wood preservation industry
- Plywood industry

- Veneer industry
- Wooden building goods industry
- Prefabricated wooden building industry
