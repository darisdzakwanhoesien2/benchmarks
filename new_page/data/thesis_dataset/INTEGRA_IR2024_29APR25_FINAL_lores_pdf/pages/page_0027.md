# Pandangan Dewan Komisaris Terhadap Komite Penunjang Dewan Komisaris

Tugas dan tanggung jawab Dewan Komisaris Perseroan tidak bisa dipisahkan dengan Komite Penunjang Dewan Komisaris. Mengingat Ketua dan Wakil Ketua Komite Penunjang adalah juga anggota Dewan Komisaris, tugas pengawasan Dewan Komisaris menjadi satu kesatuan dengan tugas Komite Penunjang Dewan Komisaris.

Dengan dibantu dua Komite Penunjang, Dewan Komisaris telah melakukan pengawasan dan pemberian arahan kepada Direksi dengan berpedoman pada aturan dan Undang-Undang Perseroan. Di samping itu, Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi terus berkomunikasi secara aktif dan terbuka demi kelancaran kegiatan operasi Perseroan.

Komite Penunjang Dewan Komisaris dalam mengawasi dan mengelola Perseroan terdiri dari Komite Audit dan Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi. Dewan Komisaris memberikan apresiasi atas dukungan Komite dalam meningkatkan efektivitas pelaksanaan tugas dan fungsi Dewan Komisaris sepanjang tahun 2024.

Kedua Komite tersebut telah menunjukkan kinerja yang berkualitas dengan menjunjung standar kompetensi dan kualitas yang baik. Komite Audit telah memastikan terselenggaranya pengendalian internal dan secara efektif membantu Dewan Komisaris dalam pengawasan atas pelaksanaan fungsi audit internal dan eksternal, implementasi tata kelola perusahaan dan kepatuhan terhadap peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku.

Sementara Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi telah melaksanakan tugasnya, antara lain dengan memberikan rekomendasi kepada Dewan Komisaris atas Nominasi dan Remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi, serta memberikan masukan terkait monitoring kepatuhan atas kegiatan Perseroan dan penerapan GCG.

# Consideration for the Board of Commissioners' Supporting Committees

The duties and responsibilities of the Company's Board of Commissioners are inseparable from the Board's Supporting Committee since the Chairman and Vice Chairman of such Committee are also members of the Board of Commissioners. Supervisory tasks of the Board of Commissioners are also the ones of the Supporting Committee.

Backed by two Supporting Committees, the Board of Commissioners has conducted supervision and direction to the Board of Directors based on the rules and laws of the Company. In addition, the Boards of Commissioners and Directors continue to communicate actively and openly for the sake of the Company's operation.

In conducting its duties and responsibilities, the Board of Commissioners is supported by Audit Committee and Nomination and Remuneration Committee. The Board appreciates both Committees' support in improving the effectiveness of the duties and functions implementation of the Board in 2024.

Both Audit Committee and Nomination and Remuneration Committee have demonstrated quality performance by upholding their competence and good quality standards. Audit Committee has ensured the implementation of internal controls and effectively assisted us in supervising internal and external audit functions, corporate governance and compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

Nomination and Remuneration Committee has also carried out its duties well, among others, by providing recommendations to us on nomination and remuneration of the Boards of Commissioners and Directors, as well as providing other recommendations related to compliance monitoring over the Company's activities and GCG implementation.
