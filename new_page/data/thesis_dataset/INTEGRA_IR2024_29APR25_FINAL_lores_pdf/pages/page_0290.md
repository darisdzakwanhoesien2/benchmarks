## 34. PERISTIWA SETELAH TANGGAL PELAPORAN (lanjutan)

Pada Januari-Februari 2025, ITR, Entitas Anak, menerima Surat Ketetapan Pajak Lebih Bayar atas Pajak Pertambahan Nilai untuk masa November-Desember 2024 sebesar Rp 26.377.961.464. Pengembalian telah diterima oleh ITR pada bulan Februari-Maret 2025.

Pada Januari 2025, INT, Entitas Anak, menerima Surat Ketetapan Pajak Lebih Bayar Pajak Penghasilan Badan tahun 2023 sebesar Rp 1.819.280.748. INT juga menerima Surat Ketetapan Pajak Kurang Bayar atas Pajak Penghasilan Pasal 21, 23 dan 4 (2) tahun 2023 sebesar Rp 415.770.749. Jumlah yang dibebankan sebesar Rp 544.616.829 dan dicatat sebagai bagian dari akun "Beban umum dan administrasi" di laporan laba rugi dan penghasilan komprehensif lain konsolidasian. Lebih bayar atas surat ketetapan pajak tersebut dicatat sebagai "Tagihan pajak" di laporan posisi keuangan konsolidasian pada tanggal 31 Desember 2024.

Pada Januari-Februari 2025, INT, Entitas Anak, menerima Surat Ketetapan Pajak Lebih Bayar atas Pajak Pertambahan Nilai untuk masa November-Desember 2024 sebesar Rp 5.840.906.346. Pengembalian telah diterima oleh INT pada bulan Februari dan Maret 2025.

## 34. EVENTS AFTER THE REPORTING DATE (continued)

In January-February 2025, ITR, Subsidiary received a Tax Overpayment Assessment Letter of Value Added Tax for November-December 2024 amounting to Rp 26,377,961,464. The refund was received by ITR in February-March 2025.

In January 2025, INT, Subsidiary, received a Tax Overpayment Assessment Letter of Corporate Income Tax for 2023 amounting to Rp 1,819,280,748. INT also received a Tax Underpayment Assessment Letter for Income Tax art 21, 23 and 4 (2) for 2023 amounting to Rp 415,770,749. The amount charged is Rp 544,616,829 and was presented as part of "General and administrative expenses" in the consolidated statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income. The overpayment from the tax assessment letter was recorded as "Claim for tax refund" in the consolidated statement of financial position as at December 31, 2024.

In January-February 2025, INT, Subsidiary received a Tax Overpayment Assessment Letter of Value Added Tax for November-December 2024 amounting to Rp 5,840,906,346. The refund was received by INT in February and March 2025.
