## 1. UMUM (lanjutan)

### c. Penawaran Umum Efek Utang Perusahaan

Berdasarkan Surat Otoritas Jasa Keuangan No. S-52/D.04/2021, tanggal 8 April 2021, Perusahaan memeroleh pernyataan efektif dari Otoritas Jasa Keuangan untuk melakukan penawaran umum berkelanjutan Obligasi dan Sukuk Mudharabah dengan total penawaran umum masing-masing sebesar Rp 700.000.000.000 dan Rp 300.000.000.000.

Perusahaan telah mencatatkan di Bursa Efek Indonesia Obligasi dan Sukuk Mudharabah Tahap I masing-masing sebesar Rp 450.000.000.000 dan Rp 150.000.000.000 pada tanggal 15 April 2021.

Selanjutnya, pada tanggal 28 Maret 2022, Perusahaan mencatatkan Obligasi dan Sukuk Mudharabah Tahap II masing-masing sebesar Rp 250.000.000.000 dan Rp 109.030.000.000.

### c. Public Offering of the Company's Debt Securities

Based on the letter No. S-52/D.04/2021, dated April 8, 2021, the Company received the effective statement from the Financial Services Authority to execute a public offering of Bonds and Sukuk Mudharabah with a total public offering of Rp 700,000,000,000 and Rp 300,000,000,000, respectively.

The Company has listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange its Bonds and Sukuk Mudharabah I with total amount of Rp 450,000,000,000 and Rp 150,000,000,000, respectively, on April 15, 2021.

Furthermore, on March 28, 2022 the Company has listed its Bonds and Sukuk Mudharabah II with total amount of Rp 250,000,000,000 and Rp 109,030,000,000, respectively.
