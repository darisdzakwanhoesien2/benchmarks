# Tinjauan Umum

Laporan Bank Indonesia mengungkapkan bahwa ketidakpastian pasar keuangan global terus berlanjut seiring dengan melebaranya divergensi pertumbuhan ekonomi dunia. Ekonomi Amerika Serikat (AS) tumbuh lebih kuat dari perkiraan, didorong oleh stimulus fiskal dan investasi teknologi yang meningkatkan produktivitas, sementara kebijakan tarif dan kuatnya ekonomi AS menahan laju disinflasi serta membatasi ekspektasi penurunan Fed Funds Rate (FFR).

Di sisi lain, ekonomi Eropa, Tiongkok, dan Jepang masih melemah akibat rendahnya kepercayaan konsumen dan terbatasnya produktivitas, sementara India terhambat oleh kendala di sektor manufaktur. Meski demikian, proyeksi pertumbuhan ekonomi global pada 2025 meningkat menjadi 3,2%.

Di dalam negeri, ekonomi Indonesia tumbuh sebesar 5,03% di tahun 2024, hampir menyamai pertumbuhan tahun sebelumnya yang mencapai 5,05% sebagaimana dilaporkan Badan Pusat Statistik (BPS) Indonesia. Pertumbuhan ini didukung oleh peningkatan investasi, percepatan proyek infrastruktur, dan belanja kampanye politik menjelang pemilu.

Namun untuk tahun 2025, Kementerian Perindustrian mengantisipasi tantangan seperti potensi tarif dari Amerika Serikat dan melemahnya permintaan global, yang dapat mempengaruhi kinerja sektor industri. Sebagai langkah antisipatif, pemerintah telah menurunkan suku bunga acuan sebesar 50 basis poin sejak September 2024 dan meluncurkan berbagai program stimulus ekonomi, termasuk diskon tarif listrik, penyediaan makanan gratis di sekolah, dan perumahan terjangkau.

# Tinjauan Industri

Sebagaimana dilansir Indonesia.go.id, industri furnitur Indonesia kini membidik pasar nontradisional seperti India yang memiliki potensi besar dan terus berkembang. Dengan langkah strategis dan inovasi produk, Indonesia siap memperluas jangkauan ekspornya ke pasar baru yang menjanjikan ini.

Populasi India yang tinggal di daerah perkotaan kini tercatat meningkat sebanyak 37% dan terus bertambah, sehingga pembangunan hunian kota dan hospitality terus dilakukan. Secara spesifik, pasar furnitur India mencapai US$41 miliar, merupakan konsumen furnitur terbesar ke empat di dunia.

# General Overview

Bank Indonesia report reveals that global financial market uncertainty continues as the divergence in global economic growth widens. The U.S. economy has grown stronger than expected, driven by fiscal stimulus and technological investments that boost productivity. Meanwhile, trade policies and the strength of the U.S. economy have slowed the pace of disinflation and limited expectations for a Fed Funds Rate (FFR) cut.

On the other hand, economic growth in Europe, China, and Japan remains sluggish due to weak consumer confidence and limited productivity, while India faces challenges in its manufacturing sector. Despite these conditions, global economic growth is projected to rise to 3.2% in 2025.

Domestically, Indonesia's economy grew by 5.03% in 2024, nearly matching the 5.05% growth recorded in the previous year, according to Statistics Indonesia (BPS). This growth was supported by increased investment, accelerated infrastructure projects, and political campaign spending ahead of the elections.

However, for 2025, the Indonesia's Ministry of Industry anticipates challenges such as potential U.S. tariffs and weakening global demand, which may impact industrial sector performance. As a proactive measure, the government has reduced the benchmark interest rate by 50 basis points since September 2024 and launched various economic stimulus programs, including electricity tariff discounts, free school meals, and affordable housing initiatives.

# Industry Overview

As reported by Indonesia.go.id, Indonesia's furniture industry is now targeting non-traditional markets such as India, which holds vast potential and continues to grow. Through strategic initiatives and product innovation, Indonesia is ready to expand its export reach into this promising new market.

India's urban population has increased to 37% and continues to grow, driving ongoing development in urban housing and the hospitality sector. Specifically, India's furniture market is valued at US$41 billion, making it the fourth-largest furniture consumer in the world.
