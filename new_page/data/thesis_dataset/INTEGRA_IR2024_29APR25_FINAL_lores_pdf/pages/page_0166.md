PT Belayan River Timber (continued)

Based on the decision from the general meeting of BRT's shareholder which was notarized by the Notarial Deed No. 16 dated March 31, 2023 of Dyah Ayu Ambarwati, S.H., M.Kn., the shareholders agreed to distribute cash dividend amounting to Rp 12,000,000,000 of the 2022 net income.

Based on the decision from the general meeting of INT's shareholder which was notarized by the Notarial Deed No. 19 dated June 28, 2024 of Dyah Ayu Ambarwati, S.H., M.Kn., the shareholders agreed to distribute dividend amounting to Rp 75,000,000,000 for financial year 2023.

Carbon Resources Alliance ApS

CRA was established based on Memorandum of Association on February 23, 2023 with capital amounting to DKK 40,000. Based on the Extraordinary General Shareholders' Meetings of CRA, the shareholders agreed to increase the issued and fully paid share capital from DKK 40,000 to DKK 3,360,000. The investment amount paid by the Company amounted to DKK 2,016,000 (equivalent to Rp 4,552,128,000), so that the percentage of the Company's ownership is 60%.

PT Narkata Rimba

Based on the Notarial Deed of Dyah Ayu Ambarwati, S.H., M.Kn., No. 10 dated July 17, 2023, the Company sold 440,550 NKT's shares to BRT at price of Rp 56,830,950,000.
