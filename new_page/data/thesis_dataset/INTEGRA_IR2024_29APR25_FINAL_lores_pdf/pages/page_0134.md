# Tanggung Jawab Produk

Perseroan menjunjung tinggi komitmen dalam menjaga standar operasional yang tinggi serta tanggung jawab terhadap proses manufaktur dan kualitas produk kayu yang dihasilkan. Komitmen ini tercermin dalam penerapan kontrol kualitas yang ketat, peningkatan proses secara berkelanjutan, serta kepatuhan terhadap praktik terbaik di industri.

Untuk memperkuat kredibilitas dan memastikan kepatuhan terhadap regulasi internasional maupun lokal, Perseroan secara konsisten memperbarui dan memperbaharui sertifikasi penting. Sertifikasi tersebut mencakup ISO 9001:2015 untuk sistem manajemen mutu, Forest Stewardship Council (FSC) sebagai bukti pengelolaan hutan yang bertanggung jawab, serta Sistem Verifikasi Legalitas Kayu (SVLK) yang menjamin bahwa kayu yang digunakan dalam produksi memenuhi standar legalitas dan keberlanjutan. Dengan mempertahankan sertifikasi ini, Perseroan tidak hanya memperkuat daya saing di pasar, tetapi juga menegaskan komitmennya terhadap tanggung jawab produk, keberlanjutan lingkungan dan kepercayaan pelanggan.

Perseroan membuka jalur komunikasi bagi masyarakat untuk melakukan pengaduan terkait isu sosial dan lingkungan di sekitar wilayah operasi. Setiap pengaduan akan ditindaklanjuti fungsi Humas sesuai dengan mekanisme standard operating procedure (SOP) penyelesaian konflik, mulai dari penerimaan dan pencatatan keluhan sesuai dengan klasifikasi (lingkungan, keamanan, hukum, sosial), koordinasi dengan unit kerja terkait untuk membuat rencana tindak lanjut dan menyampaikan jawaban kepada pihak luar, hingga proses verifikasi kebenaran keluhan dengan kunjungan dan pendekatan persuasif. Pelapor/masyarakat diminta mengisi lembar penyelesaian apabila keluhan dianggap selesai dan jika gagal akan dilimpahkan ke pihak berwenang.

# Product Responsibility

The Company upholds a strong commitment to maintaining high operational standards and responsibility in its manufacturing processes and the quality of its wood products. This dedication is reflected in rigorous quality control measures, continuous process improvements, and adherence to best industry practices.

To reinforce its credibility and ensure compliance with international and local regulations, the Company consistently updates and renews essential certifications. These include ISO 9001:2015 for quality management systems, the Forest Stewardship Council (FSC) certification for responsible forest management, and the Timber Legality Verification System (SVLK) certification, which guarantees that the wood used in production meets legal and sustainability standards. By maintaining these certifications, the Company not only strengthens its market competitiveness but also reinforces its commitment to product responsibility, environmental sustainability and customer trust.

# Social and Environmental Complaint Mechanism

In addition to providing product and service information, improving customer satisfaction, as well as maintaining customer loyalty, the Company has a policy on consumer complaint handling which can be accessed via Call Center service through (+62) 318910434 - 36, Fax. (+62) 318911391, or online through the Company's official website at www.integragroup-indonesia.com.

The Company opens a communication channel for the community to file complaints over social and environmental issues around the operating area. Each complaint will be followed up by the Public Relations function in accordance with standard operating procedure (SOP) of conflict resolution, starting from receiving and recording complaints based on classification (environment, security, legal, social), coordination with relevant Work Unit to plan follow-up and respond to external parties, up to the verification to ascertain the truth of complaints through visits and persuasive approaches. The complainant/community is asked to fill in the resolution form if the complaint is deemed resolved. However, if the resolution fails, then it is handed over to the authorities.
