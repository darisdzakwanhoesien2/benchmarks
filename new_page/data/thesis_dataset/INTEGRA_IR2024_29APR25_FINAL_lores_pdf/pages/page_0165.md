## PT Belayan River Timber

## PT Belayan River Timber

Based on the Notarial Deed No. 19 dated November 30, 2023 of Dyah Ayu Ambarwati, S.H., M.Kn., the shareholders of BRT agreed to increase the authorized, issued and fully paid capital from Rp 99,307,000,000 to Rp 406,117,025,500. The Company did not take the investment so the percentage of the Company's ownership decreased to 81.44%.

On October 31, 2023, BRT's shareholders agreed to distribute interim dividend for financial year 2023 amounting of Rp 11,000,000,000.
