## INTERTREND UTAMA

PT Intertrend Utama (ITR) yang mulai beroperasi sejak 1998 menjalankan aktivitas usahanya dengan melayani permintaan pasar yang tinggi untuk outdoor dan indoor furniture serta building components. Produk ITR telah memiliki standar tinggi untuk kualitas dan penyelesaian akhir guna memenuhi tuntutan permintaan pasar global.

Pelanggan utama ITR didominasi oleh Amerika Serikat, sementara untuk building components didominasi oleh pasar Eropa. Untuk memenuhi tingginya permintaan dan potensi pasar AS yang luas untuk produk building component, sejak tahun 2019 ITR mulai fokus pada produksi produk-produk building component dengan meningkatkan kapasitas produksi dan diversifikasi produk baru, yaitu wooden blind.

Tingginya permintaan ekspor menjadikan ITR untuk senantiasa berupaya menghasilkan produk berkualitas tinggi dan ramah lingkungan. Untuk itu, IU telah dibekali dengan sertifikat ISO 900:2015, FSC, GFTN/WWF, dan KOMO.

Established in 1997, BRT carefully plans and conducts its business activities with the support of certified human resources and modern technology such as GPS, theodolites, and computerized data processing systems.

Forest management is carried out using the Indonesia's Selective Logging system, which was later refined by implementing the Indonesia's Selective Logging and Replanting system to ensure the sustainability of natural resources while minimizing environmental damage. To achieve this, BRT consistently implements sustainable forest utilization activities in accordance with FSC (Forest Stewardship Council) certification standards.

With production focused on Meranti, BRT serves as one of the key suppliers for plywood &amp; veneer producing companies as well as furniture &amp; building component companies.

## INTERTREND UTAMA

Established in 1998, PT Intertrend Utama (ITR) was created during the development of Integra Group. This Subsidiary was established to serve a high market demand for outdoor and indoor furniture and building components. ITR focuses on high standards of product quality and finishing to meet the global market demand.

The ITR's main customers are dominantly from the United States, and European countries for building components. However, to meet high demand and potential of wide United States market for building components, ITR began to focus on building component production by increasing its capacity and diversifying new product of wooden blind since 2019.

With high export demand, ITR continuously strives to ensure that its products are high quality and environmentally friendly, as well as certified by ISO 9001:2015, FSC, GFTN/WWF, and KOMO.
