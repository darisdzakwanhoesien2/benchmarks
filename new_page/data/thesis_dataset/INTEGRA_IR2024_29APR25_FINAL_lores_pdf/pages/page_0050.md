# b. Konsesi Hutan

Perseroan menjalankan kegiatan usaha konsesi hutan melalui Entitas Anak, PT Belayan River Timber, yang bertanggung jawab atas konsesi hutan di wilayah Kutai Timur, Kutai Barat dan Kutai Kartanegara, Provinsi Kalimantan Timur. Dalam menjalankan kegiatan usaha konsesi hutan ini, Perseroan dan Entitas Anak telah menerapkan prinsip go-green dan mendapatkan sertifikasi lingkungan seperti Forest Stewardship Council (FSC) dan Sistem Verifikasi Legalitas Kayu (SVLK).

# b. Forest Concession

The Company's Subsidiary, PT Belayan River Timber, undertakes forest concession business activities, covering the forests in Kutai Timur, Kutai Barat and Kutai Kartanegara regions of East Kalimantan Province. In conducting the forestry operations, The Company and its subsidiary has implemented go-green principle and received environment certifications, such as Forest Stewardship Council (FSC) and Wood Legality Verification System (SVLK).
