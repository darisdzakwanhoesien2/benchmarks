# Tinjauan Makroekonomi

Pemulihan ekonomi Indonesia terus berlanjut pada 2024 di tengah dinamika tingginya ketidakpastian global. Pertumbuhan ekonomi Indonesia 2024 tetap baik ditopang oleh permintaan domestik serta stabilitas harga tetap terjaga didukung oleh konsistensi kebijakan moneter Bank Indonesia dan koordinasi erat dengan kebijakan Pemerintah.

Neraca Pembayaran Indonesia juga tetap sehat sejalan dengan rendahnya defisit transaksi berjalan serta meningkatnya surplus neraca transaksi modal dan finansial sehingga mendukung terjaganya stabilitas nilai tukar Rupiah.

Kondisi tersebut juga sejalan dengan laporan Badan Pusat Statistik (BPS) Indonesia yang menyatakan bahwa pertumbuhan ekonomi nasional tahun 2024 hampir menyamai tahun sebelumnya. Ekonomi Indonesia tahun 2024 tumbuh sebesar 5,03%, sedikit lebih rendah dibanding capaian tahun 2023 yang mengalami pertumbuhan sebesar 5,05%.

# Tinjauan Industri

Sepanjang tahun 2024, sektor industri manufaktur di tanah air secara konsisten berada pada level ekspansif. Sebagaimana dilaporkan Bank Indonesia, kinerja Lapangan Usaha (LU) Industri Pengolahan pada triwulan IV 2024 tetap solid dan terus berkembang, tercermin dari Prompt Manufacturing Index – Bank Indonesia (PMI-BI) yang mencapai 51,58%. Sebagian besar komponen penyusunnya menunjukkan ekspansi, termasuk Volume Persediaan Barang Jadi, Volume Total Pesanan, Volume Produksi, dan Penerimaan Barang Pesanan Input.

Dari sisi Sublapangan Usaha (Sub-LU), mayoritas sektor juga berada dalam fase ekspansi, memberikan dorongan positif terhadap PMI-BI. # Macroeconomic Overview

Indonesia's economic recovery continues in 2024 amidst the dynamics of high global uncertainty. Indonesia's economic growth in 2024 remains strong, supported by domestic demand, while price stability is maintained, backed by Bank Indonesia's consistent monetary policies and close coordination with the Government's policies.

Indonesia's Balance of Payments remains healthy, in line with the low current account deficit and the increasing surplus in the capital and financial account, which supports the stability of the Rupiah exchange rate.

This situation is also in line with the report from Statistics Indonesia (BPS), which states that national economic growth in 2024 is almost on par with the previous year. Indonesia's economy in 2024 grew by 5.03%, slightly lower than the 2023 growth rate of 5.05%.

# Industry Overview

Throughout 2024, domestic manufacturing industry consistently remained in an expansionary phase. The performance of Manufacturing Industry in Q4 2024 remained strong and continued to expand, as reflected in Bank Indonesia's Prompt Manufacturing Index (PMI-BI), which reached 51.58%. Most of its key components were in the expansion phase, including Finished Goods Inventory Volume, Total Order Volume, Production Volume, and Input Order Receipts.

From the perspective of Sub-Sectors, the majority also experienced expansion, contributing positively to the PMI-BI. The Furniture Industry recorded the highest index at 58.10%, followed by Machinery &amp; Equipment Industry at 57.19% and Leather, Leather Goods, and Footwear Industry at 57.14%.

Since the beginning of 2024, Indonesia's furniture industry has demonstrated positive performance, as reflected in a 0.50% growth in Gross Domestic Product (GDP) during the first half of the year—a significant rebound after two years of contraction.
