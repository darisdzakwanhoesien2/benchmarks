# PROYEKSI TAHUN 2025

Ke depan, Perseroan tetap optimis dalam memanfaatkan kekuatan pasar perumahan AS yang diproyeksikan akan terus tumbuh secara stabil. Pemangkasan suku bunga yang diharapkan serta potensi penerapan tarif AS terhadap kayu dari Kanada dan Meksiko dapat mendorong pergeseran permintaan ke sumber alternatif, menciptakan angin segar bagi Perseroan. Kolaborasi langsung dengan peritel AS melalui saluran e-commerce dan grosir juga meningkatkan margin dengan menghilangkan perantara serta mengurangi dampak tarif.

Untuk melakukan diversifikasi di luar pasar AS, Perseroan memperluas jangkauan global melalui kemitraan dengan mitra asal Tiongkok, meluncurkan produk furnitur luar ruang berbahan aluminium dan lantai untuk pasar Eropa, serta mengoptimalkan produksi plywood guna meningkatkan efisiensi rantai pasok dan jangkauan pemasaran. Perseroan juga meningkatkan ekspor furnitur kayu ke Timur Tengah dan telah memulai kerja sama pemasaran untuk mempromosikan produk furnitur dan komponen bangunan di pasar-pasar baru.

# PROJECTION FOR 2025

Looking ahead, the Company remains confident in capitalizing on the continued strength of the U.S. housing market, which is projected to grow steadily. Expected rate cuts and potential U.S. tariffs on Canadian and Mexican lumber may further shift demand toward alternative sources, creating a favorable tailwind for the Company. Direct collaboration with U.S. retailers in both e-commerce and wholesale channels also enhances margins by bypassing intermediaries and offsetting tariff impact.

To diversify beyond the U.S., the Company is expanding its global footprint through partnerships with Chinese partners, launching aluminium outdoor furniture and flooring products for European Market, and optimizing plywood operations to improve supply chain efficiency and marketing reach. The Company also scaling up exports of wooden furniture to the Middle East and has initiated a joint marketing collaboration to promote both furniture and building components across new markets.
