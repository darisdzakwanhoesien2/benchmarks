- **INTERKRAFT**
PT Interkraft (ITK) was established in 2002 to serve the high demand for case goods furniture and hotel projects. High demand for global markets, especially from Europe and the United States, requires ITK to produce quality and high standard products with special design of classic styles and carvings.

ITK mainly produces bedroom/case goods furniture for hotel and apartment projects. To meet the market demand for high quality and environmentally friendly products, ITK has the certifications of ISO 9001:2015 and SVLK.

- **CARBON RESOURCES ALLIANCE APS**
