# KOMITE NOMINASI DAN REMUNERASI

Nomination and Remuneration Committee Charter

Komite Nominasi dan Remunerasi adalah komite yang dibentuk oleh dan bertanggung jawab langsung kepada Dewan Komisaris untuk membantu pelaksanaan tugas dan tanggung jawab Dewan Komisaris sehubungan dengan pengawasan atas implementasi kebijakan nominasi dan remunerasi Dewan Komisaris dan Direksi Perseroan.

The Nomination and Remuneration Committee is a committee formed by and directly responsible to the Board of Commissioners to assist the duties and responsibilities of the Board of Commissioners related to monitoring the implementation of the nomination and remuneration policies for the Company's Board of Commissioners and Directors.
