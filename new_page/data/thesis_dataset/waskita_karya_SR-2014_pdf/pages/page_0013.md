# Sambutan Direksi

Foreword From The Board Of Director

Menghadapi hal tersebut, Pemerintah terus mendorong pembangunan nasional, terutama dalam bidang sarana dan prasarana Infrastruktur. Sebagai Perseroan yang bergerak di bidang Jasa Konstruksi, Perseroan memainkan peran penting dalam pembangunan nasional, dan hal tersebut tentu saja merupakan peluang dan sekaligus dorongan untuk terus dapat meningkatkan kinerja dan tetap mampu bersaing dan membawa Perseroan menjadi Perusahaan yang berkelanjutan.

## Kontribusi Terhadap Sosial, Ekonomi dan Lingkungan

Melalui Unit PKBL, Perseroan telah merealisasikan berbagai program bantuan berupa pembangunan instalasi air bersih, revitalisasi sumur bor, penghijauan, perbaikan infrastruktur, pelatihan dan pendidikan, peningkatan kesehatan serta bantuan untuk keperluan ibadah dan daerah yang tertimpa bencana alam di beberapa lokasi yang tersebar di wilayah Indonesia, yang diharapkan dapat membantu Pemerintah dalam mewujudkan kesejahteraan masyarakat. Perseroan terus berupaya untuk dapat membina hubungan yang harmonis dengan berbagai pihak dalam program CSR sehingga tepat sasaran dan bermanfaat.[G4-SO2][G4-S01][G4-EC1]

Pada aspek lingkungan, Perseroan telah menerapkan praktik terbaik prinsip-prinsip keberlanjutan, dengan ditetapkannya status Emerging Member dari World Green Building Council (WGBC), yang merupakan komitmen Perseroan untuk berperan aktif dalam melakukan aktivitas pembangunan gedung dengan konsep Go Green Construction untuk ikut serta menekan dampak merusak lingkungan dan menurunkan emisi gas rumah kaca. [G4-EN1][G4-EN6]

## Ketenagakerjaan, Keselamatan dan Kesehatan Kerja

Perseroan melaksanakan prinsip-prinsip sustainability dalam pengelolaan ketenagakerjaan, kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja yang baik. Dalam bidang ketenagakerjaan, Perseroan memberikan kebebasan berserikat bagi pegawai, untuk menciptakan hubungan industrial yang selaras dan harmonis. Rekruitmen pegawai dilakukan secara transparan dan terbuka, sehingga calon pegawai memiliki peluang yang sama untuk dapat bekerja di Perseroan.

In response to this situation, the Government continues to encourage national development, particularly in the facility and infrastructure sector. As a Company engaged in the Construction Service, the Company plays an important role in the national development, and this certainly is the opportunity and a motivation to always improve its performance so as to be able to compete and lead the Company to sustainability.

## Contribution to Social, Economy, and Environment

Through PKBL unit, the Company has implemented various donation programs in the form of clean water installation development, revitalization of drilling well, green activities, infrastructure development, training and education, health improvement, and donation for places for worship and areas that were affected by natural disaster in several locations across Indonesia. These are expected to help the Government create welfare for the society. The Company continues to develop harmonious relationship with various parties in the CSR programs so that the programs can be beneficial and meet the target. [G4-SO2][G4-S01][G4-EC1]

In the environment aspect, the Company has implemented best practices of sustainability principles with the confirmation of Emerging Member status from the World Green Building Council (WGBC), which reflects the Company's commitment to actively take part in implementing Go-Green Construction concept in building construction. This aims to minimize environmental damage impact and reduce greenhouse gas emission. [G4-EN1][G4-EN6]

## Manpower, Occupational Health and Safety

The Company implements sustainability principles in managing manpower as well as occupational health and safety. In the field of manpower, the Company ensures union freedom for employees to create harmonious industrial relations. Employee recruitment is conducted transparently and openly, thus each candidate for employee has equal opportunities to work in the Company.
