## Semangat Berkelanjutan

Pada saat ini, Indonesia tengah memantapkan langkah dalam menghadapi kerjasama Masyarakat Ekonomi ASEAN (MEA) atau ASEAN Economic Community (AEC) 2015. Hal ini menjadi tonggak awal dari pertarungan terbuka dan persaingan semua Negara anggota ASEAN di seluruh sektor perekonomian yang meliputi perdangan barang, jasa investasi, tenaga terampil dan arus modal yang lebih bebas.

The Company has also completed the development of several prestigious construction projects, which led to the increase in economic growth and brought multiplier effect sourcing from its own development. We are grateful that this development has an impact on the Company's performance in terms of economic, social, and environmental aspects. The Company's growth is an important step to make us develop to become the best, tough, and leading construction company in the future.

## Sustainable Spirit

Currently, Indonesia is strengthening its step in facing the ASEAN Economic Community (AEC) in 2015. This is a milestone in the beginning of an open fight and competition for all ASEAN member countries in all sectors of the economy, which covers goods trade, investment services, provision of skilled labor, and more free capital flow.
