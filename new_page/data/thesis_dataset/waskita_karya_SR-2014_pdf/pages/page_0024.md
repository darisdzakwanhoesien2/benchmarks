# Hubungan Dengan Para Pemasok
Relationship With Suppliers

Bagi Perseroan pemasok didefisikan sebagai mitra kerja yang mendukung Perseroan dalam menjalankan kegiatannya. Perseroan didukung oleh pemasok barang material konstruksi maupun jasa-jasa lainnya. Perseroan terus memastikan dan menjamin setiap pemasok diberlakukan secara adil dan transparan. Pada 2014, Perseroan sudah mengumpulkan data pemasok untuk bisnis Perseroan yang berkelanjutan. Perseroan mengharapkan pada Laporan Berkelanjutan yang akan datang, akan dapat dijabarkan lebih dalam mengenai pemasok. [G4-12]

Perseroan melayani pelanggan dan pemangku kepentingan lainnya dengan bertindak peduli dan proaktif serta tetap mengedepankan kehati-hatian. Pengelolaan Risk Management Perseroan melalui Komite Risiko dan Asuransi serta Internal Audit. [G4-14]

Dalam rangka menerapkan standar baku yang berlaku dalam bisnis, maupun untuk mengukur memantau dan mengukur kinerjanya, Perseroan sesuai dengan standar ISO-9001, OSHAS-18001, GRI 4.0, serta IFRS. [G4-15, G4-16]

The Company defined suppliers as partners who support the Company in carrying out its activities. The Company is supported by the supplier of construction materials and other services. The Company continues to ensure and assure the protections of each supplier are enforced fairly and transparently. In 2014, the Company has been collecting data suppliers to the Company's ongoing business. The Company expects the upcoming Sustainability Report, has been able to describe more about the suppliers. [G4-12]

The Company serves customers and other stakeholders with care and proactive and promote prudence. Management of the Company's Risk Management through Insurance and Risk Committee and Internal Audit. [G4-14]

In order to implement the applicable standards in the business, as well as to monitor and measure performance measure, the Company has been in accordance with ISO-9001, OSHAS-18001, GRI 4.0, as well as IFRS. [G4-15, G4-16]
