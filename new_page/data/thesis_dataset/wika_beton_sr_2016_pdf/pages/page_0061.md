# Komposisi Karyawan Berdasarkan Tingkatan Level Organisasi

The Company's number of employees of the Company is dominated by male employees compared to female employees, while the number of technical employees is still more than non-technical employees. Based on the level of education, the number of undergraduate-educated and postgraduate and post-graduate (S2 and S3) staffs will continue to be improved.
