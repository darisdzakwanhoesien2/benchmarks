# Governance Framework [102-18]

BNBR ensures to implement strategic measures to improve and strengthen corporate governance through a good governance framework which connect relationship between each corporate function, including relations with stakeholders, as a part of our effort to achieve the most effective management to respond to the interests of all of our stakeholders.
