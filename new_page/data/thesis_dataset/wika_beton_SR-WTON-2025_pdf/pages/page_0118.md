Dalam periode pelaporan, WIKA Beton masih berfokus pada pemberian kompensasi jangka pendek kepada Dewan Direksi sesuai dengan ketentuan, kebijakan internal, dan regulasi yang berlaku. Perseroan juga tengah melakukan kajian terkait pengembangan skema kompensasi berbasis kinerja, termasuk long-term incentives (LTI) mengacu pada Peraturan Menteri BUMN No. PER-3/MBU/03/2023, sebagai bagian dari penyelarasan praktik remunerasi dengan tata kelola perusahaan.

During the reporting period, WIKA Beton continued to focus on providing short-term compensation to the Board of Directors in accordance with applicable provisions, internal policies, and regulations. The Company is also conducting an assessment on the development of performance-based compensation schemes, including long-term incentives (LTI), with reference to Minister of State-Owned Enterprises Regulation No. PER-3/MBU/03/2023, as part of aligning its remuneration practices with corporate governance principles.
