2025 SUSTAINABILITY REPORT

WIKA Beton views the reduction of dependence on fossil energy as a strategic step to mitigate the impacts of climate change while strengthening the foundation for long-term sustainability. In line with this approach, the Company continues to expand the utilization of renewable energy across its operational units through the gradual development of solar power installations at several key facilities. In addition to advancing the energy transition, the Company is also strengthening its efficiency efforts by setting a target to reduce energy intensity by 3% by 2025 as part of its commitment to more sustainable energy management.
