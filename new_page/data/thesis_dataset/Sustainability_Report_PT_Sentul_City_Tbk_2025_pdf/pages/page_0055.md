## Mekanisme Pengelolaan Limbah dan Efluen

### Sistem Pengelolaan Air Limbah di Sentul City

#### 1. Sistem Waste Water Reticulation

Berupa jaringan perpipaan yang menampung air limbah dari closet kamar mandi dari masing-masing rumah, yang kemudian dikoneksikan dengan Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) yang berada di 5 titik. Air limbah tersebut kemudian di tampung dan diolah dalam STP dengan sistem anaerob, overflow hasil pengolahan dialirkan ke jaringan drainase kota.

# WASTE AND EFFLUENT ASPECTS

## Waste and Effluent Management Mechanisms

### Wastewater Management System in Sentul City

#### 1. Wastewater Reticulation System

This system consists of a piping network that collects wastewater from toilet fixtures in individual residential units and conveys it to Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs) located at five designated points. The wastewater is subsequently collected and treated in the STPs using an anaerobic process, and the treated effluent is discharged into the municipal drainage network.
