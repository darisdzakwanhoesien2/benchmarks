# 4. Performance Achievements

- Revenue: In 2025, the Company recorded a revenue of IDR 2.76 trillion and a profit of IDR 833 billion.
- Environment: The Company maintained a strong focus on preserving and maintaining various tree species to ensure the Sentul City area remains green and provides fresh air throughout the year.
- Partnership: In 2025, the Company collaborated with Institut Pertanian Bogor (IPB University), specifically with the Landscape Architecture Academic Business Unit. This unit conducted i-Tree Eco-based landscape service research across three clusters in the Sentul City Area, each featuring diverse landscape characteristics. The research concluded that the Company has provided high-quality landscape services and that the Company's private green open spaces serve as ecological assets and socio-economic capital that strengthen the competitiveness of sustainable residential areas.
