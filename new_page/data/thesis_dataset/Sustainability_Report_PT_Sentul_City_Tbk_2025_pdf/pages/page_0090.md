Peresmian Taman Bacaan di Desa Bojong Koneng
Official Inauguration of Bojong Koneng's Community
Reading Park

Perseroan meresmikan Taman Bacaan di Kampung Cibimbin, Desa Bojong Koneng pada tanggal 23 Juli 2025. Peresmian ini dihadiri oleh Bapak Iding Sahrudin, S.K.M., M.H.Kes., Kasi Pendidikan &amp; Kesehatan, dan Bapak Baenuri, Ketua HIMPAUDI Kecamatan Babakan Madang, serta Ibu Indira Ratna, perwakilan dari Kayu Literasi.

The Company launched a community reading park in Cibimbin Hamlet, Bojong Koneng Village, on July 23, 2025. The event was attended by Mr. Iding Sahrudin, S.K.M., M.H.Kes, Section Head of Education and Health, Mr. Baenuri, Head of HIMPAUDI Babakan Madang Subdistrict, and Ms. Indira Ratna, a representative of Kayu Literasi.

Pemberian Bantuan Apresiasi Siswa- siswa Berprestasi
Distribution of Educational Support for High-Achieving
Students

Perseroan memberikan apresiasi kepada siswa-siswa yang berprestasi di beberapa sekolah dasar di Desa Bojong Koneng, yaitu SD Negeri 01 Bojong Koneng, SD Negeri 02 Bojong Koneng dan SD Negeri 04 Bojong Koneng. Kegiatan ini dilaksanakan pada tanggal 26 November 2025.

The Company provided educational support to high-achieving students at several public elementary schools in Bojong Koneng Village, namely SD Negeri 01 Bojong Koneng, SD Negeri 02 Bojong Koneng, and SD Negeri 04 Bojong Koneng. This activity was held on November 26, 2025.
