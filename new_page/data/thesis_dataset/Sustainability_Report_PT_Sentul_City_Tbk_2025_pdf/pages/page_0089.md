**Peresmian Gedung Baru PAUD Cibimbin**
**Official Inauguration of PAUD Cibimbin's New Building**

Perseroan membangun Gedung Baru PAUD Cibimbin di Desa Bojong Koneng, yang telah diresmikan pada tanggal 24 Februari 2025. Peresmian ini dihadiri oleh Bapak Iding Sahrudin, S.K.M., M.H.Kes., Kasi Pendidikan &amp; Kesehatan, dan Bapak Enang Solihin, S.E., Kasi Pemberdayaan Masyarakat Kecamatan Babakan Madang, serta Bapak Wildan, perwakilan Desa Bojong Koneng

The Company constructed a new building for a local pre-school, PAUD Cibimbin, in Bojong Koneng Village, which was officially launched on February 24, 2025. The launching ceremony was attended by Mr. Iding Sahrudin, S.K.M., M.H.Kes, Section Head for Education and Health, and Mr. Enang Solihin, S.E, Head of Community Empowerment for Babakan Madang Subdistrict, as well as Mr. Wildan, a representative of Bojong Koneng Village.
