# Produk Layanan dan Kegiatan Usaha
## Service Products and Business Activities

Maksud dan Tujuan Perseroan adalah berusaha dalam bidang Konstruksi, Real Estat, Perdagangan dan Jasa.

Produk yang dihasilkan adalah bangunan rumah, apartemen, gedung perkantoran, gedung pertokoan dan lain-lain.

## Kegiatan Usaha

Menjalankan usaha real estat dan properti, antara lain meliputi perdagangan yang berhubungan dengan usaha real estat, yaitu penjualan bangunan-bangunan rumah, gedung perkantoran, gedung pertokoan, unit-unit ruangan apartemen, ruangan kantor, ruangan pertokoan dan lain-lain.

The purposes and objectives of the Company are to engage in the Construction, Real Estate, Trading, and Services sectors.

The products include residential houses, apartments, office buildings, commercial buildings, and others.

## Business Activities

Conducting real estate and property business, including trading related to real estate business, such as selling residential buildings, office buildings, commercial buildings, apartment units, office spaces, commercial spaces, and others.

# Keanggotaan pada Asosiasi
## Membership in Associations

1. Perseroan tercatat sebagai anggota Asosiasi Emiten Indonesia (AEI) sejak tanggal 28 Juli 1997 hingga saat ini.
2. Perseroan juga merupakan anggota Persatuan Perusahaan Real Estat Indonesia (REI) sejak tanggal 1 Agustus 1992 hingga saat ini.
3. Perseroan juga tercatat sebagai anggota Asosiasi Sekretaris Perusahaan Indonesia atau Indonesia Corporate Secretary Association (ICSA) hingga saat ini.

1. The Company has been a registered member of the Indonesian Issuers Association (AEI) since July 28, 1997, and remains a member to date.
2. The Company has also been a member of the Real Estate Indonesia Association (REI) since August 1, 1992, and continues to hold such membership.
3. The Company is likewise a member of the Indonesian Corporate Secretary Association (ICSA) and remains an active member to date.
