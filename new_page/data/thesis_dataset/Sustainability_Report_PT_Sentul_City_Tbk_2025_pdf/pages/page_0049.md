# Kinerja Lingkungan Hidup
## Environmental Performance

## ASPEK UMUM

### Biaya Lingkungan Hidup

Perseroan senantiasa berupaya mencegah dampak buruk dari kegiatan usaha yang dilakukan dengan mengeluarkan kebijakan yang sejalan dengan tujuan pembangunan berkelanjutan. Sampai dengan saat ini, Perseroan tetap konsisten pada upaya memelihara vegetasi, menanam berbagai jenis pohon, serta mempertahankan kawasan Sentul City agar tetap hijau. Dampak dari konsistensi pelaksanaan kebijakan Perseroan tersebut diharapkan memberikan kontribusi terkait kesehatan warga penghuni kawasan Sentul City dan warga sekitar. Pada tahun yang baru berakhir, biaya perawatan yang dikeluarkan oleh Perseroan sebesar Rp.949.388.911. Biaya perawatan tersebut meliputi Taman Lingkungan, Taman Spine Road, Play Set, Rumput Bahu Jalan, Rumput Ruang Terbuka Hijau, Pohon Jalan, Pohon Penghijauan, Penyapuan, Jogging Track dan Saluran.

## ASPEK MATERIAL

### Penggunaan Material yang Ramah Lingkungan

**Material Ramah Lingkungan yang Digunakan Perseroan antara lain;**
Kayu Rekayasa untuk memenuhi berbagai keperluan, antara lain untuk membuat bagian-bagian struktural seperti dinding, papan, pintu dan lain-lain. Kayu rekayasa terbuat dari berbagai sisa-limbah kayu, lapisan kayu yang berbeda, potongan kayu, kayu lunak, serat kayu, dan lain-lain, yang diolah dan dibentuk menjadi bahan yang mempunyai nilai ekonomi relatif tinggi.

### Kayu Rekayasa

Kayu adalah salah satu bahan bangunan paling terkenal yang digunakan di seluruh dunia. Namun, dalam proses konversi kayu mentah menjadi kayu dan papan kayu, sebagian besar sisa kayu mungkin terbuang sia-sia. Ampas kayu juga dapat digunakan untuk membuat bagian-bagian struktural seperti dinding, papan, pintu, dll., dalam bentuk kayu rekayasa. Tidak seperti kayu solid, kayu rekayasa mengandung lapisan kayu yang berbeda, biasanya lapisan tengahnya terbuat dari potongan kayu, kayu lunak, serat kayu, dll.

## GENERAL ASPECT

### Environmental Expenditures

The Company works consistently to minimize negative impacts from its business activities by implementing policies that align with the SDGs. To date, the Company has remained committed to preserving vegetation, planting a range of tree species, and maintaining green spaces throughout the Sentul City township. The consistent implementation of the Company's policies is expected to support the well-being of residents in the Sentul City township and surrounding communities. During the reporting year, the Company incurred maintenance costs of IDR 949,388,911 related to landscaping and public area upkeep. These costs covered neighborhood parks, spine-road landscaping, play sets, roadside grass, grass in green open-spaces, street trees, reforestation trees, street sweeping, jogging tracks, and drainage channels.

## MATERIAL ASPECT

### Use of Environmentally Friendly Materials

**Environmentally Friendly Materials Used by the Company include, among others:**
Engineered wood, which is used for a variety of purposes, including the manufacture of structural components such as walls, panels, doors, and others. Engineered wood is produced from various wood residues and wastes—such as layered wood, offcuts, softwood, and wood fibers—which are processed into materials with relatively higher economic value.

### Engineered Wood

Wood is one of the most widely used construction materials in the world. However, during the conversion of raw timber into planks and boards, a significant portion of the wood waste may be discarded. Wood residues can also be processed into engineered wood for the manufacture of structural components such as walls, boards, doors, and other elements. Unlike solid wood, engineered wood consists of multiple layers of wood, typically with a core layer made from wood offcuts, softwood, wood fiber, and similar materials.
