## 2. Sistem Biofill/Septic Tank Individual

Sistem ini berupa pemasangan biofill tank individual di setiap unit rumah yang disesuaikan dengan kapasitas per unit rumah. Untuk unit komersial diakomodasi dengan penyediaan biofill tank atau septic tank individual di masing-masing area komersial. Overflow hasil pengolahan dialirkan ke jaringan drainase kota.

## 2. Biofill System/Individual Septic Tanks

This system involves installing individual biofill tanks at each residential unit, sized to meet each unit's capacity requirements. For commercial units, this is accommodated by providing individual biofill tanks or septic tanks for each commercial area. The treated effluent is discharged into the municipal drainage network.
