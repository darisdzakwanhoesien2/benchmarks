## Upaya Efisiensi Energi

Perseroan berupaya untuk melakukan efisiensi energi dengan cara antara lain:

1. Secara bertahap mengganti atau menggunakan lampu hemat energi untuk penerangan jalan umum di kawasan Sentul City dan mengurangi durasi lampu nyala.
2. Secara bertahap mengganti atau menggunakan lampu hemat energi untuk kantor Perseroan dan mematikan lampu kantor yang tidak dipergunakan atau tidak diperlukan.

Based on the data above, electricity consumption in 2025 across the areas referenced increased compared to 2024 and, similarly, compared to 2023.

Fuel consumption increased in 2025 compared with both 2024 and 2023, exceeding the levels recorded in the previous two years.

Electricity consumption for public street lighting along main roads in 2025 decreased compared to both 2024 and 2023.

Electricity consumption for public street lighting increased in 2024 compared with 2023. In 2025, it decreased by 5,295 kWh relative to 2024. This indicates a decline in 2025 compared to 2024, although consumption remained slightly above the 2023 level. Overall, electricity consumption for public street lighting on main roads and in estate areas remained relatively stable throughout the 2023-2025 period.

## Energy Efficiency Measures

The Company sought to improve energy efficiency through the following measures, among others:

1. Progressively replacing or transitioning to energy-efficient lighting for public street lighting in the Sentul City township, and reducing lighting hours.
2. Progressively replacing or transitioning to energy-efficient lighting for the Company's offices, and switching off office lights that are not in use or not needed.
