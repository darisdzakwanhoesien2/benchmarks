Bantuan Pembangunan Musholla
Assistance for a Prayer Building (Musholla) Construction

Perseroan memberikan bantuan material untuk pembangunan Musholla Al-Furkon di Kampung Cicerewed, Desa Cijayanti yang dilaksanakan pada tanggal 22 Agustus 2025. Penyerahan bantuan diberikan kepada Bapak H. Ijat Sudrajat Pengurus Musholla dan Bapak Ahyar Royani Ketua RW 01 Desa Cijayanti.

The Company provided material assistance for the construction of the Al-Furqon prayer building in Cicerewed Hamlet, Cijayanti Village, on August 22, 2025. The assistance was formally handed over to Mr. H. Ijat Sudrajat, a member of the Musholla's management, and Mr. Ahyar Royani, Head of Neighborhood Unit (RW) 01 of Cijayanti Village.

Pelatihan Cleaning Service
Cleaning Service Training Program

![img-37.jpeg](img-37.jpeg)

Perseroan bekerja sama dengan PT. Binajasa Abadikarya (BIJAK) untuk meningkatkan skill atau keterampilan bagi masyarakat yang pencari kerja. Kegiatan yang dilakukan adalah Pelatihan Cleaning Service di Desa Bojong Koneng pada tanggal 31 Juli dan 30 September 2025. Materi kegiatan ini diberikan oleh 3 narasumber, yaitu Bapak Amir, Bapak Jamil dan Bapak Riski dari PT. Binajasa Abadikarya (BIJAK).

In collaboration with PT Binajasa Abadikarya (BIJAK), the Company hosted a skills-development program for community members seeking employment. The program included cleaning service training sessions held in Bojong Koneng Village on July 31 and September 30, 2025. The training was delivered by three instructors from PT Binajasa Abadikarya (BIJAK): Mr. Amir, Mr. Jamil, and Mr. Riski.

Pemberdayaan UMKM
MSME Empowerment

Perseroan bekerja sama dengan Karang Taruna Desa Bojong Koneng untuk mengembangkan produk UMKM masyarakat lokal. Kegiatan seremonial penyerahan produk lokal UMKM dilaksanakan bersamaan dengan peresmian Gedung Baru Paud Cibimbin pada tanggal 24 Februari 2025. Kegiatan tersebut juga dihadiri oleh Pemerintah Kecamatan Babakan Madang yang diwakili Bapak Iding Sahrudin, S.K.M., M.H.Kes., Kasi Pendidikan &amp; Kesehatan, dan Bapak Enang Solihin, S.E., Kasi Pemberdayaan Masyarakat Kecamatan Babakan Madang, serta Bapak Wildan, perwakilan Desa Bojong Koneng.

The Company collaborated with the Bojong Koneng Youth Organization (Karang Taruna) to support the development of local MSME products. A ceremonial handover of the produced MSME goods was held alongside the launch of the PAUD Cibimbin's new building on February 24, 2025. The event was also attended by representatives of the Babakan Madang Subdistrict, including Mr. Iding Sahrudin, S.K.M., M.H.Kes, Section Head of Education and Health, and Mr. Enang Solihin, S.E., Head of Community Empowerment, as well as Mr. Wildan, a representative of Bojong Koneng Village.
