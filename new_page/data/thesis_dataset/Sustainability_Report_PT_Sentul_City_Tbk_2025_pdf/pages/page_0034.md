# B. Penerapan Keuangan Berkelanjutan

## Bidang Ekonomi

Sepanjang tahun 2025, Perseroan telah bekerja keras dengan mengerahkan sumber daya yang ada sehingga Perseroan berhasil membukukan pendapatan menembus angka Rp2,76 Triliun pada tahun yang baru berakhir.

## Bidang Lingkungan

Penggunaan daya listrik tahun 2025 untuk beberapa gedung utama yang dipergunakan oleh Perseroan adalah sebesar 479.599,00 kWh, lebih besar dibandingkan dengan tahun 2024 sebesar 479.332,80 kWh. Hal tersebut terjadi karena adanya penambahan area yang memerlukan penerangan dan area yang memerlukan pendingin ruangan.

Untuk terus menghemat penggunaan daya listrik, Perseroan saat ini secara bertahap berupaya mempergunakan lampu dan pendingin ruangan hemat energi.

Selain daripada hal tersebut dalam proses pembangunan dan pengembangan kawasan Sentul City, Perseroan juga memanfaatkan material lokal berupa batu andesit yang sangat cocok untuk fondasi. Batu tersebut diperoleh pada saat proses grading atau cut &amp; fill.

# 5. Challenges in Implementing Sustainable Finance

A key challenge in implementing Sustainable Finance is that its importance has not yet been fully embedded within Issuers and Public Companies. Consequently, planned and continuous outreach and training by relevant parties are essential in implementing Sustainable Finance across short, medium, and long-term time frames.

# B. Implementation of Sustainable Finance

## Economic Sector

Throughout 2025, the Company worked diligently on implementing Sustainable Finance by mobilizing its available resources, resulting in a recorded revenue exceeding IDR 2.76 trillion for the year.

## Environmental Sector

Electricity consumption in 2025 for several of the Company's main buildings was 479,599.00 kWh, an increase from 479,332.80 kWh in 2024. This increase was due to the expansion of areas requiring lighting and air conditioning.

To continue reducing electricity consumption, the Company is gradually implementing energy-efficient lighting and air conditioning systems.

In the construction and development of the Sentul City area, the Company utilized local materials, such as andesite stone, which is well-suited for use as a foundation material. This stone is sourced during the grading or cut-and-fill process.
