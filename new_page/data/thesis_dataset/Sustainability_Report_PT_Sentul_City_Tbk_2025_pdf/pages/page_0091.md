## Donor Darah Blood Donation Drives

Perseroan bekerja sama dengan PMI Kabupaten Bogor melaksanakan kegiatan Donor Darah. Sepanjang tahun 2025 perseroan melaksanakan kegiatan Donor darah sebanyak 4 kali yaitu:

1. Tanggal 18 Februari 2025 diikuti oleh 51 pendonor;
2. Tanggal 14 Mei 2025 diikuti oleh 31 pendonor;
3. Tanggal 05 Agustus 2025, diikuti oleh 52 pendonor;
4. Tanggal 05 November 2025, diikuti oleh 39 pendonor.

The Company collaborated with the Indonesian Red Cross (PMI) of Bogor District to organize blood donation drives. Throughout 2025, the Company conducted four blood donation drives, as follows:

1. February 18, 2025, with 51 donors;
2. May 14, 2025, with 31 donors;
3. August 5, 2025, with 52 donors;
4. November 5, 2025, with 39 donors.

## POSYANDU &amp; Penyuluhan Kesehatan Community Health Services (POSYANDU) and Health Education

Perseroan bekerja sama dengan Puskesmas Cijayanti melaksanakan kegiatan POSYANDU dan Penyuluhan Kesehatan yang dilaksanakan di PAUD Sudi dan PAUD Cibimbin. Kegiatan POSYANDU dilaksanakan pada tanggal 28 dan 29 April 2025, sedangkan kegiatan Penyuluhan Kesehatan dilaksanakan pada tanggal 17 dan 18 September 2025.

The Company partnered with the Cijayanti Community Health Center (Puskesmas) to deliver community-based health services and health education programs at PAUD Sudi and PAUD Cibimbin. Community health service (POSYANDU) activities focused on early detection of child growth and development were conducted on April 28-29, 2025, while the health education sessions took place on September 17-18, 2025.
