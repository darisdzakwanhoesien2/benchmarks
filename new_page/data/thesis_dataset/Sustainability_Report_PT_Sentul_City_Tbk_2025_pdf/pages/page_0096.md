Melanjutkan kegiatan "Save Gunung Pancar" yang dimulai tahun 2024 lalu, Perseroan kembali menggelar kegiatan serupa berkolaborasi dengan Balai Besar Pelaksanaan Jalan Nasional DKI Jakarta dan Jawa Barat dari Kementerian PUPR.

Kegiatan ini diselenggarakan pada tanggal 7 Mei 2025 di kawasan Sentul City. Dihadiri oleh Bapak Tjetje Muljanto, Presiden Direktur PT. Sentul City Tbk., Bapak H. Agus Ridhallah - Ketua Kwartir Cabang Gerakan Pramuka Kabupaten Bogor dan Ibu Ayu Sukma, perwakilan Dinas PUPR. Jumlah pohon yang ditanam pada kegiatan tersebut sekitar 500 pohon yang terdiri dari pohon tabebuya, pohon mahoni, pohon kecapi, pohon trembesi, pohon cemara dan pohon pinus.

Following the launch of the "Save Gunung Pancar" program in 2024, the Company organized a similar initiative in collaboration with the National Road Implementation Agency (BBPJN) for the DKI Jakarta and West Java region under the Ministry of Public Works and Housing (PUPR).

The activity was held on May 7, 2025, in the Sentul City area and was attended by Mr. Tjetje Muljanto, President Director of PT Sentul City Tbk; Mr. H. Agus Ridhallah, Head of the Bogor District Scout Movement; and Ms. Ayu Sukma, a representative of the Ministry of Public Works and Housing. Approximately 500 trees were planted during the initiative, including Tabebuia, Mahogany, Kecapi, Trembesi, Pine, and Cypress trees.
