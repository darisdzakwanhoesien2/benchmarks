# Kegiatan Tanggung Jawab Sosial Lingkungan (TJSL)

Social Corporate Responsibility (CSR) Activities

![img-28.jpeg](img-28.jpeg)

Program TJSL yang dilakukan oleh Perseroan berorientasi pada program-program dalam bidang pendidikan, kesehatan, ekonomi, sosial dan lingkungan. Program tersebut dijalankan untuk mendukung pencapaian Tujuan Pembangunan Berkelanjutan (TPB) (Sustainable Development Goals/SDGs) antara lain terkait TPB No. 1 - Tanpa Kemiskinan, TPB No. 2 - Tanpa Kelaparan, TPB No. 3 - Kehidupan Sejahtera dan Sehat, TPB No. 4 - Pendidikan Berkualitas, TPB No. 6 - Air Bersih dan Sanitasi Layak, TPB No. 9 - Industri, Inovasi dan Infrastruktur, TPB No. 11 - Kota dan Komunitas Berkelanjutan, dan TPB No. 15 - Ekosistem Daratan.

The Company's Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives are strategically focused on advancing Education, Healthcare, Economic, Social, and Environmental Development. These programs are designed to support the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), including SDG 1 (No Poverty), SDG 2 (Zero Hunger), SDG 3 (Good Health and Well-Being), SDG 4 (Quality Education), SDG 6 (Clean Water and Sanitation), SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure), SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities and Communities), and SDG 15 (Life on Land).
