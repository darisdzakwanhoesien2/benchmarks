# Kegiatan Lomba Kebersihan Keindahan dan Ketertiban HUT RI Ke- 80
## Community Clean-up Competition for Indonesia's 80th Independence Day

![img-45.jpeg](img-45.jpeg)

Dalam rangka memperingati Hari Ulang Tahun Kemerdekaan RI yang ke-80, Perseroan bekerja sama dengan Pemerintah Kecamatan Babakan Madang menyelenggarakan kegiatan Lomba Kebersihan, Keindahan, dan Ketertiban untuk seluruh desa di Kecamatan Babakan Madang. Adapun hadiah lomba diberikan oleh Bapak Drs. Raden Makmun Nawawi, M.Si. Camat Babakan Madang, kepada masing-masing Kepala Desa pemenang yaitu sebagai berikut:

1. Juara I: Desa Cijayanti Bapak H. Ahmad Paojan, S. Kep.
2. Juara II: Desa Citaringgul Bapak R. Fredy Agustian, S.H.
3. Juara III: Desa Sumur Batu Bapak H. Adi Nurhikmat, S.E.

To celebrate Indonesia's 80th Independence Day, the Company collaborated with the Babakan Madang Subdistrict officials organized a community clean-up competition involving all villages in the subdistrict. The awards were presented by Mr. Drs. Raden Makmun Nawawi, M.Si, Head of Babakan Madang Subdistrict, to the respective village heads of the winning villages, as follows:

1. 1st Place: Cijayanti Village – Mr. H. Ahmad Paojan, S.Kep.
2. 2nd Place: Citaringgul Village – Mr. R. Fredy Agustian, S.H.
3. 3rd Place: Sumur Batu Village – Mr. H. Adi Nurhikmat, S.E.

**Pelatihan Baki Hantaran**
Training on Making Gift Trays from Recycled Materials

Perseroan melaksanakan kegiatan pelatihan daur ulang sampah berupa Baki Hantaran. Pelatihan daur ulang sampah dilaksanakan pada tanggal 17 dan 19 Maret 2025 di Desa Bojong Koneng.

![img-47.jpeg](img-47.jpeg)

The Company carried out a recycling training program focused on creating gift trays from reusable materials. The training sessions were held on March 17 and 19, 2025, in Bojong Koneng Village.
