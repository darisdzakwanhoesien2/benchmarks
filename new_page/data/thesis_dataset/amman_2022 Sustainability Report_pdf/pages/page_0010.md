Our Environmental Expenditure in million USD

As outlined in our Work Plan and Budget (Rencana Kerja dan Anggaran Biaya/RKAB) submitted to the MEMR, our environmental expenditure in 2022 was USD 35,691,572, indicating a 53% increase in realised budget compared to the previous year. The funds were spent on carrying out programmes such as environmental monitoring, land management, acid rock drainage prevention and control, and civil works such as the construction and maintenance of sedimentation ponds.
