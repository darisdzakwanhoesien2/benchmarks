# balé Bisnis

balé Bisnis is the rebranding of BTN Internet Banking Business services, designed to facilitate MSME (Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprise) customers who have a BTN Business Savings Account to manage and grow their businesses. Its unique selling points include:

- Soft token in Balé by BTN
- Single / multiple authorization

- High transfer limits
- Registration SLA of 1 x 24 hours
- Scheduled transactions
- Bulk transfer
- Payroll services
- Cash flow tracker
- Account statement in XLS, PDF, and MT940 formats
