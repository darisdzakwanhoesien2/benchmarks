## Legal Basis for Establishment:

- Emergency Law No. 9 of 1950, dated 9 February 1950, concerning the Amendment of the Postpaarbank Law (Staatsblad 1934 No. 653, 1937 No. 176 and 197, and 1941No. 295) (State Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia No. 12 of 1950).
- Government Regulation instead of Law No. 4 of 1963, dated 22 June 1963, concerning Amendments and Additions to Law No. 26 of 1953 on Bank Tabungan Negara (State Gazette No. 86 of 1953).
- Government Regulation No. 24 of 1992, dated 29 April 1992, concerning the Adjustment of the Legal Form of Bank Tabungan Negara into a Limited Liability Company (Persero).
- Deed of Establishment of the Bank as a Persero No. 136, dated 31 July 1992, drawn up before Notary Muhani Salim, S.H., in Jakarta.
