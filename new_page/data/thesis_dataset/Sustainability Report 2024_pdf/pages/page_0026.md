# ESG Governance

BTN recognizes the importance of good governance as an integral part of the Bank's sustainability efforts. Although this topic is not discussed in depth in this chapter, BTN wishes to emphasize that transparent and accountable corporate governance serves as a solid foundation for the Bank's sustainability initiatives.

A comprehensive explanation of the sustainability governance policies, structure, and practices can be found in the chapter on Sustainability Governance Integration. In this section, the Bank has detailed of risk management, compliance with applicable regulations, and demonstrates Bank's commitment to ethical standards and transparency in every aspect of the Company's operations.

# ESG Process

## Strategy Development

In implementing the ESG framework, BTN has formulated nine (9) strategic steps that are applied inclusively, as follows:

1. Conducting sustainable operational and banking business activities.
2. Developing sustainable and inclusive banking products and portfolio management.
3. Integrating ESG components into the business risk management framework (enterprise risk management).
4. Developing an ESG culture program and responsible leadership.
5. Building a control environment integrated with ESG components.
