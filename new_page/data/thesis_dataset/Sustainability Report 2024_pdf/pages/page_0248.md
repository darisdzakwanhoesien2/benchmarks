# Risk Management Work Unit (SKMR)

The Risk Management Work Unit serves as a unit directly under the responsibility of the Director overseeing the Risk Management function. This unit leads risk management operations and operates independently from the Operational Work Unit, functioning as a risk-taking unit, the Internal Audit Work Unit, and the Compliance Work Unit. The implementation of the Bank's risk management is based on GCG principles, achieved through full participation across the organization in managing risks, reflected within the risk management organization structure. Additionally, the Risk Management Director is responsible for supervising ESG risks during financing processes, ensuring effective management of these risks in accordance with established policies and procedures.

The Board of Commissioners, through the Risk Monitoring Committee meetings, and the Directors, through Risk Management Committee meetings, bear the responsibility to ensure that risk management implementation in the Bank aligns with its characteristics, complexities, and risk profiles. Both committees actively participate in managing risks to mitigate those faced by the Bank, adhering to the Risk Management Implementation Quality Standards (KPMR). These standards include aspects such as risk governance, risk management framework, risk management processes, information management systems adequacy, and adequacy of internal control systems.
