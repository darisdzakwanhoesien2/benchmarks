# Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprise (MSME) Credit

BTN is committed to providing access to capital for all segments of society, with a primary focus on the development of MSMEs that possess potential and viability across various economic sectors. The primary objective of this funding is to support investment and/or working capital financing, while also contributing to the achievement of sustainable development. In this context, BTN plays a role in enhancing the competitiveness of MSME clients, encouraging them to become more productive, competitive, and globally aware through the provision of business capital.

As a responsible economic development agent, BTN facilitates financing for MSME players with the hope of fostering growth in this sector. Through this initiative, the Bank also creates a positive impact on the local and national economy, improving community welfare. The MSME Program, one of the Bank's flagship initiatives, is specifically designed to support MSMEs by providing

working capital and/or investment financing. With a limit of up to Rp5 billion, this program supports the operational and capacity development of MSMEs, ultimately contributing to job creation and community empowerment. Through this program, BTN aims to strengthen the local economy sustainably. [SASB FN-CB-240a.1]

In the reporting year, BTN developed strategies to expand the distribution of MSME credit, including:

1. Enhancement of High Yield Loan (Eagle Project) distribution through:

- Establishment of MSME Partner Units at Sub-Branch Offices (KCP) for the distribution of People's Business Credit (KUR) and Micro SME Credit;
- Segmenting MSME sales to ecosystems or communities based on regional potential through BPI SME.
