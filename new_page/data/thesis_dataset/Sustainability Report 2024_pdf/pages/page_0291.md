# Whistleblowing System

BTN's commitment to fraud prevention is evident through the implementation of an anti-fraud strategy that manages all potential fraud risk. The strategy encompasses the execution of four pillars:

a. Prevention

This covers various tools designed to reduce the likelihood of fraud, including efforts to raise awareness about the importance of anti-fraud measures, conducting vulnerability identification to detect potential fraud triggers, and implementing policies to better understand employees.

b. Detection

This has been designed to identify and uncover potential fraud through various mechanisms that have been established, such as the Whistleblowing System (WBS) policy, surprise audits, surveillance systems, and direct supervision by the Operational Responsibility Officer. Based on the Bank's WBS report data for 2024, a total of 3 complaints were received, all of which were submitted via email or the website. All complaints received have been properly processed and resolved, with appropriate sanctions applied, including administrative sanctions. This information is detailed in the table below.
