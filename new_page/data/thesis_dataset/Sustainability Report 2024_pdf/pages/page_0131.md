Employee Development and Welfare Support

#

The Bank's commitment to developing outstanding human resources goes beyond education and training programs. Selected permanent employees are also given opportunities to pursue higher education. This initiative is implemented in line with General Human Capital Policy KU.3 on Learning and Development Management, designed to enhance employee competencies and create a skilled, resilient, and

responsive workforce tailored to the needs and developments in the banking industry. Additionally, BTN ensures that training is provided for active employees and those nearing retirement. Training programs aim to equip employees preparing for retirement with the skills and readiness to transition into a productive and meaningful post-retirement phase. [GRI 404-2]

# BUMN Learning Festival 2024

BTN hosted the 2024 BUMN Learning Festival (BLF) grand event with the theme "Be Agile, Be a Lifelong Learner," held at Menara BTN. This event encouraged sustainable practices through learning, skill enhancement, and adaptation to the latest business trends. Over 116 BUMNs participated, with a total of 2,500 attendees. The event also featured digital learning exhibitions from each BUMN.

The BLF reflects BTN's commitment to developing exceptional human resources grounded in the strong foundation of AKHLAK (Accountable, Knowledgeable, Harmonious, Adaptive, Kinship). During the event, BTN emphasized the importance of quality human resources in supporting the housing sector—one of the nation's strategic programs—while also contributing positively to the national economy. # LinkedIn Top Companies 2024

BTN's success in developing human resources is reflected in winning the LinkedIn Talent Awards and ranking 3rd in the LinkedIn Top Companies 2024. This award positions BTN as one of the best career development platforms in Indonesia and solidifies the Bank's commitment to building an adaptive and sustainable development ecosystem.

BTN's human capital development strategy focuses on strengthening employee competencies through technology and globally standardized learning platforms.
