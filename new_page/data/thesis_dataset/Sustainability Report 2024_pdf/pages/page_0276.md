# Code of Conduct and Business Ethics

[GR1 2--23, 2--24]

The Code of Conduct reflects BTN's commitment to implementing Good Corporate Governance (GCG) by adhering to the highest standards based on proven best practices in the banking industry that have established credibility and trustworthiness. In the context of ethics, the credibility and trust held by the public, shareholders, and customers are considered significant factors in supporting the Bank's growth and sustainability. Therefore, every business activity carried out by BTN not only complies with applicable laws and regulations but also reflects adherence to norms and ethics prevailing in society and business environments.

Awareness of the importance of good ethical practices plays a key role in building BTN's positive reputation, ultimately strengthening its image as a socially responsible entity that contributes positively to society. To support this goal, BTN is committed to upholding integrity in every action and decision taken. The Bank does so by applying ethical guidelines and clear behavior standards as a foundation for all members of the organization in carrying out their responsibilities. These guidelines ensure that every individual and team at BTN works professionally and in accordance with high ethical standards, aligned with BTN's principles, vision, mission, and established values, while adhering to applicable internal and external regulations.

To ensure that every activity reflects a commitment to ethical principles, BTN has established standards of ethics and conduct aligned with its vision, mission, and values. These standards are implemented through Joint Decision Letter of the Board of Commissioners No. 01/SKPB/KOM/BTN/2024 and Board of Directors No. SKPB-01/DIR/BTN/2024 on BTN's Code of Conduct and Behavior (Code of Conduct). This Code provides clear guidelines for all BTN employees to act in accordance with the upheld values, maintaining integrity and professionalism in all aspects of operations. Non-compliance or deviations from the Code of Conduct can result in significant reputational damage, operational risk, reduced business efficiency and effectiveness, or credit risk, impacting the Bank's ability to manage and provide loans responsibly. Therefore, business ethics play a critical role in maintaining BTN's sustainability and stability.

The Code of Conduct aims to shape behavior that aligns with the ideal standards and fosters a work culture based on BTN's core values. The Code of Conduct consists of four main chapters, which are briefly described as follows:

### 1. Introduction

The Code of Conduct is a codification or compilation of policies, regulations, and agreements jointly made between the Bank and its employees, designed to influence and establish the expected behavior standards for all individuals in the organization, from management to employees. The main objective is to ensure that all activities carried out within the Bank adhere to high ethical standards. The application of the Code of Conduct, in terms of both compliance and breaches, becomes one of the indicators in evaluating employee performance. In other words, as long as employees adhere to the Code of Conduct, it will positively influence their performance evaluations. The Code of Conduct not only serves as a reference for decision-making and policy implementation but also acts as a foundation for managing the Bank's practices. Consistency in applying the Code of Conduct demonstrates BTN's commitment to always conducting its business with high ethical values and good principles. This, in turn, supports the establishment of Good Corporate Governance (GCG).

### 2. Business Ethics Standards

The business ethics standards function as a guide that governs the behavior and interactions of the Bank across various aspects of its activities. These ethical guidelines include relationships between the Bank and related parties, such as employees, customers, suppliers of goods and services, and competitors. In addition, these standards also encompass ethics in relations with work partners, the government, society, the media, and professional organizations. All interactions must be carried out with principles of honesty, transparency, and mutual respect, to maintain the Bank's reputation and integrity.
