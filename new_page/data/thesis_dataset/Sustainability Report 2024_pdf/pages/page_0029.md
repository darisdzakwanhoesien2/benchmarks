When proposing the ESG framework, strategy, and roadmap, BTN takes stakeholder engagement into account. BTN conducts benchmarking with companies that have already implemented ESG, both domestically and internationally. In addition, BTN also organizes ESG workshops with internal participants from 20 work units. This agenda serves as a supporting validator for the draft proposal of BTN's ESG framework, strategy, and initiatives, ensuring the creation of an ESG framework that aligns with BTN's readiness and ambition.

BTN has identified 24 strategic initiatives in its ESG roadmap for the next five years, with ten priority initiatives as follows:

## 1 Implementing Clean Energy Transition

Becoming a pioneer in green mortgage carbon trading.
- Target : Listing on the carbon trading exchange as a seller in green mortgage carbon trading.
- Timeline : 2024-2025

## 2 Sustainable Bank Operations Program

Implementing sustainable bank operations with efficiency in paper and electricity usage, electric vehicles (EVs), and solar panel usage.
- Target : 50% reduction in paper and electricity use within 5 years, 50 EVs, and 20 solar panel installations.
- Timeline : 2024-2028

## 3 Sustainable Procurement Program

Increasing stakeholder and partner awareness and building sustainable procurement policies.
- Target : Sustainable practices with 50 core vendors.
- Timeline : 2024-2026

## 4 Climate Risk Stress Test

Measuring climate risks that impact BTN's financial condition.
- Target : Climate risk stress testing in accordance with OJK and best practices.
- Timeline : 2024

## 5 Developing ESG University

Enhancing the competency, capacity, and capabilities of employees regarding ESG by building an ESG curriculum and training programs.
- Target : 75% of employees in strategic positions.
- Timeline : 2024-2026

## 6 Sustainable Product Offering

Consistently launching sustainable products, including environmental/social bonds and green products.
- Target : Environmental/social bonds: Rp3 trillion and 5 green products.
- Timeline : 2024-2028

## 7 Disbursing Sustainable Credit

Strengthening BTN's role in supporting sustainable credit disbursement (according to OJK's designated sectors), SMEs, and green mortgages.
- Target : Sustainable sector: at least 20% of the credit portfolio; green mortgages: 20%.
- Timeline : 2024-2028

## 8 Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Program

Promoting social aspects within human resources.
- Target : Employment of persons with disabilities: 2% of total employees and increasing the percentage of female talent in management positions: 30%.
- Timeline : 2024-2028

## 9 Developing ESG Policies

Regularly aligning internal regulations and policies with relevant ESG policies according to best practices and regulations.
- Target : Reviewing ESG policies.
- Timeline : 2024-2028

## 10 Becoming Signatories for Sustainable Finance Commitment

Actively engaging in international ESG networks and communities.
- Target : Membership in 4 sustainability institutions (domestic and international).
- Timeline : 2024-2028
