BTN believes that sustainability is not just about meeting standards but about building a better, more sustainable future. With this vision, BTN remains committed to contributing to environmentally friendly workspaces across its operations, supporting national sustainability targets, and providing added value for all stakeholders.

In addition to implementing the green office concept and fulfilling environmental responsibilities, BTN strives to preserve the environment through various conservation programs and activities. This initiative is realized by ensuring that the Bank's operational offices are not located in conservation areas or regions with protected biodiversity. [GRI 3-3] [OJK F.9, F.10]

BTN actively supports and contributes to the Indonesian government by agreeing to a global action plan aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) alongside other global leaders. BTN has incorporated several environmental targets from the SDGs as

benchmarks for environmental preservation efforts, including Goal 6 (Clean Water and Sanitation), Goal 7 (Affordable and Clean Energy), Goal 11 (Sustainable Cities and Communities), Goal 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production), Goal 13 (Climate Action), and Goal 15 (Life on Land). [OJK F.1]

BTN's commitment to implementing best practices and complying with environmental regulations and standards has been proven by the absence of violations resulting in fines or non-monetary sanctions throughout 2024. [GRI 2-27, 3-3, 307-1] [OJK F.16]
