To support the implementation of this policy, the Bank submits reports and data to regulators and/or law enforcement agencies (LEAs) covering the following components:

1. Submitting Financial Transaction Reports to the Financial Transaction Reports and Analysis Center (PPATK), including:
a. Cash Financial Transaction Reports (LTKT)
b. Suspicious Financial Transaction Reports (LTKM)
c. Cross-border Fund Transfer Reports (LTKL) through the Integrated Customer Service User Information System (SIPESAT)

2. Providing Data/Information to Law Enforcement Agencies (LEAs) and Regulators, including:
a. Information System for AML/CTF (SIGAP) to the Financial Services Authority (OJK)
b. AML/CTF Reports via the OJK's APOLO Application
c. Suspected Terrorism Financing Information System (SIPENDAR) to PPATK
d. Electronic Data Exchange (PEDAL) to the Corruption Eradication Commission (KPK)

e. Data Requests Fulfillment by the LPP and Other APGAKUM Agencies, such as the Police, National Narcotics Agency, and Prosecutor's Office.

3. Submitting Reports Related to the Implementation of General Elections and Regional Elections to PPATK, including:
a. Participating in the Collaborative Analysis Team (CAT), which is a collaborative exchange of information between public sector entities such as PPATK, reporting parties, supervisory and regulatory bodies (LPP), and law enforcement agencies.
b. Monitoring the accounts related to Special Campaign Funds (RKDK).
c. Screening against political party treasurers, central and regional campaign committees.
d. Screening against the Permanent Voter List (DCT) of participants in the General and Regional Elections, which is reported to PPATK.
e. Submitting all reports and information requests related to the implementation of the 2024 General and Regional Elections.

# Strategic Awareness Program Initiatives

1. Thematic Refreshment Training on AML, CTF, and PFWMD for Employees through Internal Bank Media, Using Both Print and Digital Platforms.

2. Internalization of AML, CTF, and PFWMD Awareness in 2024.
a. Training of Trainer.
BTN conducts Training of Trainers (TOT) sessions to enhance employees' understanding of APU, PPT, and PPPSPM implementation, particularly for those who interact with customers and Walk-In Customers (WIC), such as customer service representatives. The expected outcome of this program is that participants will continuously internalize and disseminate knowledge to other employees, ensuring a broad, consistent, and interactive understanding of AML, CTF, and PFWMD.
b. Training on Beneficial Owners
BTN provides Beneficial Owner (BO) training to improve employees' understanding of AML, CTF, and PFWMD, particularly in the implementation of Customer Due Diligence (CDD) for prospective customers, existing customers, and WICs who have a Beneficial Owner (BO).

c. Training on Understanding and Implementing AML, CTF, and PFWMD for High-Risk Customers. BTN organizes training sessions to equip employees with the necessary knowledge to conduct Customer Due Diligence (CDD) and Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) for high-risk customers. This initiative aims to enhance vigilance and ensure compliance with AML, CTF, and PFWMD regulations.
