# Employee Safety Management Program [GRI 403-5]

a. All operational offices are equipped with workplace safety equipment such as fire extinguishers (APAR), smoke detectors, diesel pumps, and emergency ladders to ensure the safety of the office environment and employees. The provision of such equipment follows the standards set by the government in the Ministry of Public Works Regulation Number 26/PRT/M/2008 on the Technical Requirements for Fire Protection Systems in Buildings and Environments;

b. BTN regularly inspects, maintains, and replaces all safety equipment. This program aims to monitor and control the safety equipment's feasibility in the Bank's environment;

c. All operators of safety equipment (e.g., lifting and transporting machines) are certified by the

Provincial Employment and Transmigration Office of DKI Jakarta;

d. BTN installs fire evacuation plans and fire safety procedures for all rooms and floors of the building. This facility serves not only as a tool to warn employees in emergencies but also as an educational means for employees on how to handle fires;

e. Conducting training and simulation exercises to prepare for emergency situations caused by disasters and fires in the office environment, including organized evacuation procedures and safe workplace exit protocols to minimize risks and ensure employee safety; and

f. Employees are certified through the "Safety and Health Expert Certification Training (AK3U)" held by BPJS Health.

# Occupational Health and Safety Management System (OHSMS) and Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

According to the Indonesian Ministry of Health Regulation No. 48 of 2016 on Office Health and Safety Standards, OHSMS is "a part of the office building management system as a whole for controlling risks related to work activities to create a safe, efficient, and productive workplace." Implementing OHSMS is designed to protect employees' safety and health through planned, structured, and integrated procedures. Its main goal is to prevent work accidents and occupational diseases that may disrupt productivity and employee welfare.

Comprehensively, BTN has implemented OHSMS along with OHS policies that involve all parties engaged in its operational activities, including 100% of employees, customers, suppliers, and others active in operational areas. All data on implementing this policy is recorded in the Human Capital Management Division's database to ensure that all parties involved receive appropriate protection. In line with this, BTN also develops and implements standard operating procedures (SOPs) to ensure the health and safety of employees and stakeholders in the Bank's operational areas. [GRI 403-1, 403-7, 403-8]

Some BTN employees have been certified as General Occupational Safety and Health Experts (AK3U) since 2020. Employees who have obtained AK3U certification are primarily responsible for ensuring the proper implementation of OHSMS and office OHS standards in the Bank. Additionally, these employees are responsible for identifying hazards by applying the hierarchy of controls, conducting risk assessments, and investigating reported hazardous situations or incidents in the Bank's operational area to prevent recurrence. AK3U certification is a program from the Ministry of Manpower that prepares OHS experts in BTN. Some of the safety and health training topics that have been conducted include: [GRI 403-2, 403-4, 403-5]

a. Support facilities for building operations, such as elevators, generators, air conditioning, and lighting;

b. Routine medical checkups for employees;

c. Work support equipment that should not negatively affect health; and

d. Periodic employee rotation to avoid psychological disruptions.
