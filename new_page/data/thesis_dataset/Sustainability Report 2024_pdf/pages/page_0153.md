# Social Pillar

![img-155.jpeg](img-155.jpeg)

BTN's primary objective is to grow alongside the community through a series of initiatives that contribute to improving social, cultural, religious, and social welfare aspects. These efforts align with Sustainable Development Goals 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5.

## Establishment of a Reading Park for Illiteracy Eradication

As part of its initiative to support education and eradicate illiteracy, BTN Lampung Branch Office has provided CSR assistance in the form of the construction of a Reading Park in Lampung on October 14, 2024.

![img-158.jpeg](img-158.jpeg)

![img-159.jpeg](img-159.jpeg)

## Scholarships for Students from Low-Income Families

On January 11, 2024, BTN provides educational assistance to outsourced workers at the Credit Restructuring, Collection, and Settlement Unit, as part of its corporate social responsibility to support welfare in the education sector.
