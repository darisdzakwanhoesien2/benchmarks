## Credit Risk

Credit risk represents a potential loss due to the failure of counterparties to fulfill their obligations. BTN faces various types of credit risks, such as risks arising from debtor defaults, credit concentration, counterparty risk, settlement risk, and country risk. Credit risk sources stem from credit and financing activities but also include financial instruments such as securities, interbank transactions, trade financing, currency exchange, derivatives, as well as commitments and contingencies. To manage credit risk, BTN applies the Credit and Financing Policy Guidelines, monitors credit exposure, and ensures that credit risks remain within the defined Risk Appetite and Risk Tolerance levels.

As part of effective risk management efforts, BTN regularly conducts Forum Portfolio Quality Reviews to monitor credit quality and communicate the results to the Board of Directors and related units. The Bank has established a Credit Committee and implemented centralized systems such as the Commercial Banking Center (CBC) for the commercial segment and the MSME Processing Centers (SPU) for the MSME segment.
