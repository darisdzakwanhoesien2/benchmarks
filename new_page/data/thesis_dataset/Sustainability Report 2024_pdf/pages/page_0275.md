# Basel Implementation

Basel is an international regulatory framework designed to strengthen the stability of the banking system by improving risk management. In the Indonesian banking sector, Basel implementation is carried out in accordance with OJK regulations to ensure adequate capital, liquidity, and risk management operations.

This framework supports the sustainability of banking businesses by maintaining customer trust and the stability of the financial sector. To implement risk management, particularly the application of Basel II and Basel III, which applies to all commercial banks, BTN has fulfilled the following requirements:

|  Requirement | Status  |
| --- | --- |
|  Implementation of Basel II Pillar 1  |   |
|  The Bank has implemented market risk measurements using the standardized model in accordance with SE OJK No. 38/SEOJK.03/2016, dated September 15, 2016, concerning the Guidelines for Using the Standard Method in Calculating Minimum Capital Requirements for Commercial Banks by Considering Market Risk. The portfolio included in determining the KPMM market risk consists of interest rate trading book portfolios and currency trading book and banking book portfolios for exchange rate risk. The Bank has prepared SEOJK No. 23/SEOJK.03/2022, dated December 7, 2022, regarding the Calculation of Risk-Weighted Assets for Market Risk for Commercial Banks, which has been applied as of January 1, 2024. The Bank has also conducted trial calculations and reporting to OJK for market risk ATMR positions as of June 2023, September 2023, and December 2023. | ☑  |
|  Implementation of Basel II Pillar 2  |   |
|  The Bank submitted the results of the implementation report on Interest Rate Risk in the Banking Book (IRRBB) and the IRRBB calculation report to regulators quarterly in accordance with SE OJK No. 12/SEOJK.03/2018, dated August 21, 2018, concerning the Implementation of Risk Management and Measurement of Risk Using the Standard Method for Interest Rate Risk in the Banking Book (IRRBB). | ☑  |
|  Implementation of Basel III  |   |
