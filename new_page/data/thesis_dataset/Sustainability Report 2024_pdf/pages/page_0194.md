# balé by BTN

balé by BTN is a super app developed as a transformation of the BTN Mobile application. This app is designed to simplify financial transactions for Indonesians, including banking and financial services. balé by BTN emphasizes easy financial management and practical transactions, offering customers greater convenience in managing their daily financial activities. Moreover, this service is a strategic initiative to overcome geographical challenges, ensuring that all segments of society, particularly those in remote areas, can experience the benefits of broader financial inclusion. In 2024, the service successfully reached communities in underserved areas, covering 20,183 users, in line with Presidential Regulation of the Republic of Indonesia Number 63 of 2020.

The advantage of balé by BTN lies in its ability to integrate with various other BTN services, such as BTN Properti, BTN Smart Residence, BTN Merchant, and more. This application also supports recurring payments for various household or business needs, including utilities (electricity, clean water/PDAM, mobile credit and data packages), environmental management fees (IPL), as well as security, parking, and property maintenance services. Additionally, balé by BTN offers exclusive features, including:

1. Online Account Opening, customers can open an account easily and quickly without visiting a branch, anytime and anywhere.
2. Single Sign-On, a unified login system that allows seamless access to all BTN services without switching platforms.
3. Cardless Transactions, this feature enables customers to withdraw cash from BTN ATMs without a physical card, using only the application.
4. Auto Debit System, customers can schedule recurring bill payments automatically, ensuring timely payments without manual intervention.
5. Auto Transfer, this feature simplifies scheduling periodic fund transfers to other accounts, both within BTN and to other banks, as needed.
6. Cashflow Tracker, customers can easily monitor their income and expenses, helping them manage personal finances more effectively.
