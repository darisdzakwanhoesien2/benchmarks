## Driving Transforming and Effectivity through Advanced Analytics (2026--2027)

The targets for the 2026--2027 period focus on transformation and effectiveness through advanced analytics. In balance sheet optimization, BTN will implement optimization models and FTP systems, as well as strengthen credit risk analytics with ESG and external data. ESG initiatives will be integrated into core banking operations, and the governance of subsidiaries will be aligned with global standards. BTN will also continue digitizing processes with data-driven decision-making and enhance model risk management using digital tools. Operational efficiency will be improved with digital file processing and sector prioritization mechanisms.

For operational resilience, BTN will strengthen third-party oversight, data security, and fraud detection systems. Risk management systems and regulatory compliance will also be optimized. BTN will enhance leadership capabilities and the GRC culture through leadership forums, dual career path management, and GRC capacity-building, as well as establish a Risk Academy to address skill gaps. GRC will also be integrated into talent management.

The 2026--2027 target aims to develop an asset and liability risk management framework focusing on balance sheet optimization, cost reduction, and credit risk strengthening through the application of core/non-core models and ESG principle integration in banking operations.
