# Governance Strategy

By implementing a holistic GCG paradigm, BTN ensures alignment between the Bank's goals and interests with those of its stakeholders. This alignment is expected to support BTN's long-term sustainability. To maintain consistency in GCG implementation, BTN has developed a roadmap encompassing four main pillars. The first pillar is the commitment to governance (Commitment on Governance), which serves as the foundation for the subsequent three pillars: Governance Structure, Governance Process, and Governance Outcome.
