# Customer Satisfaction

BTN considers customer satisfaction one of its top priorities. Each year, the Bank strives to improve customer satisfaction levels by offering high-quality products and services that are not only superior in quality but also equitably accessible to all customers without exception. Additionally, the Bank establishes policies and service standards designed not only to maintain existing levels of satisfaction but also to strengthen and enhance customer trust in the Bank. [OJK F.17]

Several initiatives undertaken by BTN to ensure customer satisfaction include:

1. Offering products and services that meet established quality standards;
2. Providing the best services to all customers and addressing every customer complaint fairly and without discrimination;
3. Conducting service promotions that are ethical, fair, honest, and non-misleading, while adhering to commonly accepted social norms;
4. Promoting fair trade practices in accordance with BTN's policies, including: [FSIS]
a. Special Policy No. 2-L on Retail Funding Service Products;
b. Technical Guidelines PT.2-C.1 on Foreign Currency Transfer Service Fees;
5. Ensuring that all employees support the Bank's efforts to achieve customer satisfaction and trust.

Besides providing information on existing products and services, BTN is also committed to offering clear and accurate information on new products and

innovations. This allows customers to make informed and confident decisions. The information covers various essential aspects, including the purpose and type of the product, offered benefits, potential risks, pricing, usage procedures, related fees, and other relevant details that help customers understand the Bank's products and services. [GRI 3-3, 417-1] [OJK F.27]

Furthermore, all marketing and promotional activities conducted by the Bank comply fully with applicable regulations, ensuring that all promoted products and services meet the standards set by relevant authorities. As a result of this approach, during the reporting year, no negative impacts were recorded due to the Bank's products or services. No products or services had to be withdrawn due to safety concerns, and no non-compliance issues were identified regarding information or labeling of the Bank's products and services. Additionally, BTN did not receive any reports of violations related to marketing communications throughout 2024. [GRI 3-3, 417-2, 417-3] [OJK F.28, F.29]

BTN continuously strives to enhance customer satisfaction, and the success is reflected in various external assessments. Notably, BTN secured first place in the Net Promoter Score (NPS) category for the Bank Group (KBMI 3) in the Satisfaction Loyalty Engagement (SLE) survey conducted in 2024, achieving a score of 50.00. This achievement represents an 8.5% increase from the previous year, underscoring high levels of customer satisfaction and loyalty towards BTN's services. [OJK F.30]
