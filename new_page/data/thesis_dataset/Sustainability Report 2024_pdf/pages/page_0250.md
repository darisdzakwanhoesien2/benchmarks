# Integration of ESG Risk Management

BTN has integrated Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) principles into the credit due diligence process by directly involving the Credit Risk Management team in the assessment of ESG issues. Through the Risk Management Unit (SKMR), the bank

ensures that all risks arising from business activities, including credit risk and ESG-related risks, are identified, measured, monitored and reported transparently. The assessment report is submitted to the Board of Directors to ensure responsible risk management.
