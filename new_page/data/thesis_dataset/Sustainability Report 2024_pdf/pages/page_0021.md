# Impact, Double Materiality, and Value Chain [GRI 3-1, 3-2, 3-3]

BTN has adopted the principles of impact, double materiality, and the value chain as a holistic approach to understanding, measuring, and managing the environmental, social, and economic impacts of its activities. The Bank focuses on reporting the most significant impacts on the economy, environment, and society while ensuring that its decisions and policies consider these aspects. In this context, BTN defines material topics as issues that have a significant influence on stakeholders' decisions.
