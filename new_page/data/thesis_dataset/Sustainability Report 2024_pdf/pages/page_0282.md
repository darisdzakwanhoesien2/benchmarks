# Fraud Risk Assessment

The Bank must remain vigilant to any exposure arising from its involvement in business or operations in regions with higher incidents of corruption. This risk includes the potential for involvement in controversies related to corrupt practices, which can damage the Bank's reputation, affect relationships with stakeholders, and result in legal sanctions that harm BTN's operational stability.

As part of its mitigation efforts, the Bank has implemented a Fraud Risk Assessment (FRA). FRA is a method used by BTN to identify and analyze potential fraud risks, whether originating internally within BTN (internal risks) or externally (external risks).
