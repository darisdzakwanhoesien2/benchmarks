# Other Financial Literacy Programs [FSIS]

As part of its commitment to promoting financial literacy, BTN continues to expand its collaborations with educational institutions, government agencies, and local communities to implement financial training programs. These initiatives aim to enhance financial literacy and broaden public access to the financial sector, particularly in property and banking. One of BTN's flagship initiatives is the Financial Inclusion Program, designed to improve financial knowledge and skills

among university students and academic communities. Through this program, BTN seeks to reach unbanked populations by partnering with universities and higher education institutions to provide training and awareness sessions on personal financial management, financial planning, and the importance of financial literacy.
