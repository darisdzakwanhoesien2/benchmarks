# Routine Performance Appraisal and Feedback

In reviewing and evaluating employee performance, BTN follows the General Policy on Human Resources (Human Capital) Number KU.3 regarding Career Management. This policy includes aspects of planning and developing employee careers within the Bank. In determining performance evaluation indicators, BTN does not prioritize subjectivity or differentiate employees based on gender but focuses on everyone's competencies, performance, and potential. The results of these evaluations are an essential factor the Bank considers when formulating career development policies, including decisions related to promotions, transfers, or further development opportunities for the employees concerned.

In 2024, BTN committed to providing performance evaluation feedback to 100% of employees at all levels of positions without exception, objectively, and without gender discrimination. This process is carried out with structured performance evaluations

through semi-annual and annual reviews, using Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to assess individual and team performance. The Continuous Professional Development (CPD) program is also implemented to support open communication about achievements, areas for development, and future career plans. [GRI 404-3]

BTN upholds the principles of equality and inclusion by integrating a performance-based compensation strategy (performance-driven culture), ensuring salary increases based on merit (merit increase), and providing incentives and bonuses as rewards for individual and team achievements. This policy also ensures the equal career development of female workers and adjusts compensation based on position, length of service, workload, and performance. With comprehensive performance evaluations, the CPD program, and performance-based compensation policies, BTN supports career development and employee engagement while preparing for professional growth in line with the company's future strategic needs.
