# Oversight Of Financial Products

BTN is committed to ensuring that all financial products and services meet high standards in terms of fairness, security, and compliance, while also mitigating potential risks. Every product and service, whether new or updated, is designed with customer needs in mind, including features, convenience, reliability, and regulatory compliance. Before launch, all products and services undergo a comprehensive testing process to ensure customer data privacy protection and transaction security in accordance with established standards. This is part of BTN's risk mitigation efforts to prevent service disruptions and maintain data integrity.

The evaluation process for financial products and services is conducted regularly to ensure compliance with security standards and applicable regulations, as well as to identify and mitigate risks that may arise during product and service development. Supervision of this process is carried out by the Business Committee, which consists of Board of Directors and SEVP members. The Business Committee is responsible for the formulation, decision-making, oversight, monitoring, and evaluation of policy implementation and strategic planning related to products and activities launched by BTN. The committee also monitors business developments and provides recommendations for improvements. The Business Committee operates based on Special Policy No. KK.9-D on the Governance Procedures of the Board Committees and plays a key role in product and service oversight and decision-making.

1. Responsible for decisions made during Business Committee Meetings and Board of Directors Meetings;
2. Responsible for reporting significant decisions to the Board of Directors at least once a year;
3. Determining corrective actions related to product development and reporting them to the Board of Directors.

With strict supervision, the Business Committee ensures that both the development and discontinuation of products consider risk management, customer satisfaction, and BTN's long-term strategic objectives. Additionally, BTN also focuses on the social, environmental, and economic impact of its products and services by conducting regular impact assessments through a systematic approach. This ensures that products not only meet quality standards but also provide positive benefits for customers and society.

The assessment process includes input from reviewers and control owners, historical evaluation reports, alignment analysis based on internal and external standards, and follow-up reports related to impact and regulatory compliance. These assessments are conducted at least once a year, or more frequently if significant changes occur in products or regulations. The results of these assessments are used to refine policies and procedures to improve the quality of inclusive and sustainable financial services.

In 2024, product supervision effectiveness was measured through regulatory compliance, customer satisfaction, and financial inclusion achievements. BTN utilizes a customer feedback loop to improve product quality. Regular evaluations ensure that products meet security and compliance standards while delivering maximum benefits and considering social and environmental impacts. A structured oversight system at the board and committee level reinforces BTN's commitment to risk management, ensuring reliable, secure products that contribute to sustainability and data integrity.
