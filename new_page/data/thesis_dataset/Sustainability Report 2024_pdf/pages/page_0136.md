# Protection and Fulfillment of Employee Rights

BTN is fully committed to protecting employee rights, as fulfilling these rights is believed to enhance employee satisfaction. High satisfaction will drive better performance, ultimately contributing to sustainable business outcomes and long-term success.

The Bank regularly negotiates with the Employee Union to fulfill employee rights. These negotiations aim to draft and agree on the Collective Labor Agreement (CLA), valid for two years with the option of a maximum one-year extension. BTN has also registered this CLA with the Ministry of Manpower and Transmigration of the Republic of Indonesia, the authority responsible for ensuring business compliance with labor issues, by the Decree of the Director-General of Industrial Relations and Social Security Decision Number Kep. 4/ 032/PKB-PPJ/HI.00.01/X/2024 The currently applicable CLA is for the period 2024-2026. [GRI 2-30] [OJK F.19]

Although the CLA does not yet comprehensively cover the provisions of the Minister of Manpower Regulation No. 28 of 2014 concerning the Procedure for the Establishment and Ratification of Company Regulations and the Creation and Registration of Collective Labor Agreements, BTN ensures that all employees (100%) are protected through the CLA and are members of the Employee Union.

# Employee Remuneration

BTN defines remuneration as providing rewards to employees that must be carried out fairly without discriminatory practices based on gender, ethnicity, race, religion, skin color, or other forms of diversity. In this regard, the Bank has ensured no difference in the basic salary and remuneration received by female and male employees at the same job level. The amount of remuneration provided is based on performance achievements and the responsibilities

In addition to this, BTN is also firmly committed to preventing forced labor practices in its workplace. This commitment is implemented through the Human Capital Policy Number KU.3 on Workdays and Hours, clearly defining the Bank's operational days and hours. This policy ensures that employees can work, attend, and rest according to the established schedule, with attention to the balance between work and rest time. Suppose an employee is asked to work beyond the designated hours. In that case, the Bank will provide overtime pay by the provisions outlined in the Human Capital Policy Number KU.3 on Compensation and Benefits Management. [GRI 409-1]

The Bank actively communicates with employees to foster good and transparent working relationships. This communication includes information on changes in the organizational structure, the addition of business units, and matters related to personnel management, such as policies, procedures, and career development. All relevant information is easily accessible to employees through LApS, an internal application designed to manage and deliver provisions, rules, and updates applicable to the Bank. This ensures that employees are always up-to-date and can actively participate in the organization's development.
