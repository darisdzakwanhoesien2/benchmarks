As part of this commitment, BTN is expanding its financing scope with a focus on sustainable and eco-friendly housing projects. The Bank recognizes its crucial role in supporting the success of national housing development. Therefore, BTN prioritizes supporting government programs to achieve the target of building three million homes and provide high-quality and environmentally friendly housing financing. One of our key initiatives is Low-Emissions Housing, through which BTN aims to develop 150,000 low-emission houses by 2029 as part of efforts to address global climate change challenges.

BTN initiated the Low-Emission Housing Program with an initiative to use environmentally friendly materials for 10% of construction and plans to increase this percentage to 30% by 2029. The use of innovative materials such as floor decking and paving blocks that contain recycled materials from plastic waste not only helps to reduce plastic waste. Through this program, BTN aims to create a positive environmental impact and enhance the quality of ecosystems surrounding residential areas.

The issuance of the RAKB also serves to communicate sustainability values to all stakeholders while supporting the Bank's performance in alignment with sustainable development objectives.
