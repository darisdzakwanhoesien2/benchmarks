# Evaluation of the Performance of the Highest Governing Body [GRI 2-18]

The evaluation of corporate governance performance is a critical step to ensure the Bank consistently meets the highest standards and adapts to business developments. The evaluation of the highest governing body's performance, related to the implementation of Good Corporate Governance (GCG) and the management of economic, environmental, and social impacts, is conducted through a self-assessment process. This evaluation is performed routinely every

year, specifically at the end of June and December, without involving third parties. The evaluation includes the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, and relevant work units, assessing the factors established to determine the extent to which GCG implementation has been achieved. The self-assessment results with a score of 2 indicate that the Bank's management successfully implements corporate governance and that it is generally deemed good. This assessment reflects the fulfillment of adequate corporate governance principles. Although there are some minor shortcomings in implementing governance principles, overall, these issues are not considered significant and can be improved through appropriate management actions. Detailed information on assessment criteria, evaluation recommendations, and follow-up actions based on the self-assessment results can be found in BTN's Annual Report. [GRI 2-18]

In addition to GCG evaluations, BTN actively participates in the Corporate Governance Perception Index (CGPI) survey organized annually by the Indonesian Institute for Corporate Governance (IICG). CGPI is a research and rating program that evaluates the quality of corporate governance. In 2024, the Bank successfully achieved a CGPI score of 91.52 with the predicate of "Most Trusted Company." This result reflects BTN's

commitment to excellent corporate governance practices and demonstrates that the Bank can be trusted in governance matters.

Apart from the GCG and CGPI evaluations, BTN also actively participates in other assessments through the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS). ACGS is an international assessment tool that aligns corporate governance practices with standards set by the Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development (OECD) based on the OECD Principles, with the aim of supporting Good Corporate Governance practices at BTN.

The ACGS seeks to promote companies in ASEAN countries with high-quality governance, thereby increasing investor trust in companies across the region. Each ASEAN member appoints representatives for the Domestic Ranking Body (DRB) and Corporate Governance (CG) Experts to conduct assessments under the ACGS framework. In Indonesia, DRB and CG Experts are represented by PT RSM Indonesia.
