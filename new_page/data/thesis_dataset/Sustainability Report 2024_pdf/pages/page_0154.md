## Implementation of Mini MBA in Property and School Property of Developer

BTN has implemented the Mini MBA in Property programs for Batch 20, Batch 21, and Batch 22 as part of its property business training for aspiring property developers. This program, conducted in 2024, aims to enhance human resource capacity in the property sector. Additionally, BTN, in collaboration with BPD HIPMI East Java, organized the Mini MBA in Property for HIPMI from September 11-13, 2024.

## BTN Santri Developer Kebangsaan Program

From August 23-30, 2024, BTN successfully implemented the BTN Santri Developer Kebangsaan Batch 4 program. This program, which took place at Pondok Dar El Hikmah in Pekanbaru, Riau, was attended by 80 participants. The goal is to enhance the knowledge and competencies of Santri alumni in the field of property business.
