In delivering digital services, the Bank remains committed to continuous adaptation and security enhancements by implementing a three-pillar approach designed to protect customer data and transactions from potential threats. This approach aims to ensure that digital services remain secure, reliable, and protected from cyber risks. The three pillars include:

1.  People

Strengthening information security from the people aspect includes activities such as:

a.  Security Awareness

BTN has conducted regular security awareness programs for all employees, customers, and third parties collaborating with the Bank. Various media are used to deliver security awareness messages to employees, such as emails, bulletins, desktop wallpapers on employee work devices (updated monthly), as well as online and offline socialization. Educating customers on information security is a priority, ensuring they can conduct transactions securely and remain vigilant against cybercrime to protect their personal information. Regular training is also provided for customers working with BTN to ensure they safeguard both their company's and BTN's data. Socialization efforts for customers and third parties collaborating with BTN are carried out through BTN's e-channels, BTN's social media platforms (Instagram, X, Facebook, etc.), and various offline events or marketing activities organized by the Bank. BTN continuously enhances employee professionalism and cybersecurity competency as customer partners through various training programs and cybersecurity certifications.

b.  Competency Development

Throughout 2024, the Bank has conducted IT Security-related training and certifications to strengthen both soft skills and technical skills, including Certified in Risk and Information Systems Control (CRISC), CompTia Security+, Security Tools on DevSecOps Toolchain, Coaching &amp; Strategic Business Leaders for Department Head, Business Performance Through Effective Coaching for Leaders, BUMN School of Excellence (BSE) - CTO Masterclass Year 2024, Leadership Development: Performance Management Strategies, Problem Solving &amp; Decision Making, Performance

Excellence Program: Strategies for Driving High Performance, Training and Certification in Banking Risk Management, Certified Chief Information Security Officer (CCISO) Training, ISMS Lead Auditor/Lead Implementer ISO 27001:2022 and Microsoft 365 Administrator. In addition, the Bank also conducted forums and workshops including BSSN National Cyber Exercise 2024, Managing Cyber Security Workshop, CISO Forum, External National Cyber Exercise, AI Technology Workshop, Stamp Duty Regulation Implementation Workshop and AI Knowledge Based Project.

2.  Process

To ensure good governance in compliance with applicable standards and regulations, BTN's work processes are based on the latest SOPs, which have been disseminated and consistently implemented by all stakeholders. In line with these standardized operational processes, the Bank has undertaken the following initiatives:

a.  Establishment of the Computer Security Incident Response Team (CSIRT) has collaborated with regulators and is supported by the latest technology and a monitoring system through Security Incident and Event Management (SIEM)

b.  Regular cybersecurity testing processes on network, system, and data security in accordance with regulatory requirements, including

- Cybersecurity testing based on vulnerability analysis, such as conducting vulnerability assessments and penetration tests for new applications, application development, and regular applications.
