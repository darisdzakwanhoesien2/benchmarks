# Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

[GRI 203-1, 203-2, 413-1] [CUK F.23, F.25, F.3]

BTN is committed to creating a positive impact on people's lives and the surrounding environment through its CSR programs. These have been designed not only to empower communities but also to improve their quality of life sustainably while preserving the ecosystem around the Bank. Within the framework of the Sustainable Finance Action Plan (SFAP), CSR serves as a key pillar driving the Bank's continuous innovation and growth alongside society. [GRI 3-3]

To ensure the program's success, BTN aligns its CSR initiatives with ISO 26000 standards on Social

Responsibility. Through this framework, the Bank develops measurable and tangible strategies that contribute to a communities well-being while also generating benefits for the Bank. With this approach, BTN continues to play a strategic role in supporting sustainable development, encompassing social, economic, and environmental aspects. [GRI 3-3]

In this reporting year, BTN has allocated Rp88.97 billion for CSR programs.
