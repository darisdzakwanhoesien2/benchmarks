# Contribution to the State

BTN is committed to transparent and ethical business conduct as part of its social responsibility and regulatory compliance. Tax and dividend payments reflect this commitment and significantly contribute to the national economy. In 2024, BTN contributed Rp4.2 trillion through various taxes paid to support national economic development.
