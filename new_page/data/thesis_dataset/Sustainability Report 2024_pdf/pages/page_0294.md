BTN implements WBS with the aim of ensuring that every report received from whistleblowers is handled seriously, in accordance with established procedures. This involves a process of verification and investigation to ensure the validity of the reports. Based on the reports received, the Bank is responsible for formulating and imposing appropriate sanctions against parties proven to have committed breaches. The purpose of imposing sanctions is to create a deterrent effect, thereby preventing others from committing similar actions that could harm the Bank. The WBS BTN policy is designed

to strengthen internal control mechanisms to support business growth through the following steps:

1. Conducting early detection and prevention of irregularities and breaches; and
2. Gradually creating a work climate that is open, honest, and responsible at BTN.

The BTN WBS team responsible for managing WBS reports consists of:

1. Head of the Internal Audit Division;
2. Deputy Head of the Internal Audit Division; and
3. The WBS unit under the Fraud Investigation &amp; Whistleblowing System Department within the Internal Audit Division.

# Whistleblowing Report Submission Mechanism [GRI 2-16]

BTN provides a dedicated Whistleblowing System (WBS) channel to facilitate stakeholders in reporting violations within the Bank. This mechanism ensures that individuals who have information about misconduct can submit their reports securely and confidentially. Reports can be submitted based on the identified indications of violations, as follows:

1. If the reported party is a BTN employee up to two levels below the Board of Directors and is not part of the BTN WBS Team, the Independent WBS Manager will forward the report to the BTN WBS Team.
2. If the reported party is a BTN employee one level below the Board of Directors and is not part of the WBS Team, the Independent WBS Manager will forward the report to the President Director.

3. If the reported party is a member of the BTN WBS Team, the Independent WBS Manager will forward the report to the President Director.
4. If the reported party is a member of the Board of Directors, the Independent WBS Manager will forward the report to the Board of Commissioners and the WBS Manager at the Ministry of State-Owned Enterprises (BUMN).
5. If the reported party is a Commissioner and/or a Committee under the Board of Commissioners, the Independent WBS Manager will forward the report to an unrelated Commissioner and Committee Member under the Board of Commissioners, as well as the WBS Manager at the Ministry of BUMN (specifically if the reported party is a Commissioner).
