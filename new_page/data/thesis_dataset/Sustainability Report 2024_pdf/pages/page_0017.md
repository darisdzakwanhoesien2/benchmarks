BTN faces several external challenges that could influence its strategies and operations, including changes in government policies related to housing finance incentives, natural disaster risks in key operational areas, global economic uncertainty due to trade wars, and fluctuations in the rupiah exchange rate, which could increase operational costs. Internally, BTN faces dynamics such as changes in ownership structure and declining financial performance. These factors require BTN to adapt swiftly to remain competitive amid an intensifying industry.

In response, BTN has implemented various mitigation measures, including hedging to protect strategic projects from market fluctuations and enhancing communication with the government through industry associations to support policies aligned with the bank's objectives. BTN also prioritizes product diversification, expansion of collaboration networks, and investments in technology, such as the development of efficient digital platforms and cybersecurity enhancements. These efforts are designed to ensure operational efficiency, expand market share, and improve service quality for the community.

# Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

BTN's journey in implementing sustainable finance aligns with its commitment to supporting the achievement of sustainable development targets. BTN is dedicated to supporting the Indonesian government's initiatives to ratify the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), a global agenda initiated by the United Nations to address worldwide challenges, including poverty, climate change, and social inequality, to achieve sustainable social, economic, and environmental welfare.

In response, the Indonesian government has adopted the SDGs through the Sustainable Development Goals as stipulated in Presidential Regulation No. 59 of 2017.
