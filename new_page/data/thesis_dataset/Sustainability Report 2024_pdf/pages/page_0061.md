# BTN's History and Transformation Journey

PT Bank Tabungan Negara (Persero) Tbk, hereinafter referred to as "Bank" or "BTN," is a State-Owned Enterprise (SOE) in the form of a limited liability company operating in the financial and banking services sector. BTN is committed to becoming a bank that provides and supports housing sector financing through three main products: retail banking, business banking, and sharia banking.

BTN was founded as Postpaarbank during the Dutch colonial era in 1897. Postpaarbank was taken over and renamed Tyokin Kyoku or Savings Office during the Japanese occupation. After Indonesia declared independence in 1945, the Government of the Republic of Indonesia took over Tyokin Kyoku and established Kantor Taboengan Pos (Postal Savings Office). In 1950, the government renamed it Bank Tabungan Pos (Postal Savings Bank) under Emergency Law No. 9 of 1950,

effective 9 February 1950. Later, through Government Regulation instead of Law No. 4 of 1963, the Bank was renamed Bank Tabungan Negara, a name it still retains. In 1976, the government initiated a housing program for the public, appointing BTN as the sole institution to distribute housing loans (KPR). The first KPR distribution occurred in 1976 in Tanah Mas Housing Complex, Semarang. On 29 April 1989, BTN began operating as a state-owned commercial bank. Through Government Regulation No. 24 of 1992, BTN was officially designated as a state-owned limited liability company (Persero). The Bank's founding document as a Persero was notarized by Muhani Salim, S.H., in Deed No. 136 on 31 July 1992, ratified by the Ministry of Justice through Decree No. C2-6587.HT.01.01.TH.92 dated 12 August 1992, and published in the State Gazette No. 73 Supplement No. 6A on 11 September 1992.
