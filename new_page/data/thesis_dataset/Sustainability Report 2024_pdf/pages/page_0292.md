d. Monitoring, Evaluation, and Follow-Up

This final pillar includes various actions taken by BTN to monitor and evaluate potential fraud cases while ensuring proper follow-up mechanisms are in place. In the reporting year, BTN recorded 19 internal fraud cases, of which 7 cases were successfully resolved, while the remaining cases are still under resolution. This process demonstrates BTN's efforts to respond to every

potential violation seriously and take necessary steps to address the issues. Additionally, the 19 identified cases have been followed up through appropriate legal channels. This reflects BTN's strong commitment to handling every violation in compliance with applicable laws, ensuring integrity and adherence in all Company activities.
