Every year, BTN prepares a sustainability report covering achievements, challenges, and initiatives related to environmental, social, and governance aspects. This report is based on accurately and validly collected data and information, and through in-depth analysis to assess the impact of various Company activities on sustainability. BTN also conducts internal evaluations to assess the effectiveness of the ESG strategies and policies implemented and to identify areas that require improvement or strengthening.

In these sessions, problem-solving and necessary solutions are discussed to ensure that ESG initiatives continue as planned and meet the established goals. This approach ensures that every challenge is addressed promptly, and sustainability progress is maximized. The results of these evaluations will serve as the basis for refining the Bank's sustainability policies and initiatives in the future. Performance evaluation is conducted in-depth through one-on-one sessions with the relevant work units. Through this approach, BTN is committed to continually improving its ESG performance, ensuring accountability, and maximizing the Company's contribution to supporting broader sustainable development.
