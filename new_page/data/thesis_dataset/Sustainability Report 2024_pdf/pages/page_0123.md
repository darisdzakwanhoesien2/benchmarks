# Synergy in Preparing Competent Human Resources in the Digital Era

As part of its commitment to enhancing human resources (HR) quality and supporting the sustainable housing sector, BTN is also actively engaged in providing literacy to students through public lectures on addressing the economic and digital disruptions currently taking place. In 2024, these literacy activities were conducted at several universities, including Bina Nusantara University (Binus), Semarang State University (Unnes), Andalas University (Unand), Tanjungpura University (Untan), HKBP Nommensen University (UHN), and Islamic University of Indonesia (UII).

During the same event, a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) was signed, marking a strategic step for BTN to expand its involvement in the development of Indonesia's young talent,

particularly in the banking and property sectors. This collaboration supports BTN's mission to "Become the Home for Indonesia's Best Talents" while also contributing to the achievement of the target of building three million houses annually. BTN also provides banking services to advance education, including tuition fee payments, internships for students, public lectures, and other collaborations. Through this partnership, BTN has supported the provision and use of banking services and contributed to the development of the academic community. This strategic move reaffirms BTN's commitment to building superior human resources that will support the development of a sustainable housing sector, contributing to Indonesia's Golden Years in 2045.
