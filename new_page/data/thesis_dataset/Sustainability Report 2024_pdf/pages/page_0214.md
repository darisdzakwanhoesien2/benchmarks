2. Commitment to Implementing Leading Data Protection Standards. BTN has implemented data protection mechanisms that comply with standards outlined in Special Policy (KK) 6-K on Personal Data Protection and Special Policy (KK) 5-B on Information Technology Security. These policies include procedures and security measures designed to ensure data is protected and managed securely. Additionally, BTN has adopted international information security standards, namely ISO 27001:2022 for the Information Security Management System, which provides the best framework for managing and protecting information.

3. Commitment to Notifying Data Subjects Promptly in the Event of Data Changes or Breaches. BTN has established a policy requiring notifications to be sent to data subjects if there is a change in data processing regulations or a data protection failure. This is regulated in Special Policy No. 6-K on Personal Data Protection and Technical Guidelines No. 6-K.4 on Handling Personal Data Protection Failures, which provides guidelines for responding to incidents related to data protection failures.

4. Commitment to Obtaining User Data in a Legal, Transparent Manner with Explicit Consent When Required. BTN ensures that data processing is carried out in accordance with applicable laws and with transparency, as stated in Special Policy No. 6-K on Personal Data Protection. Additionally, the Bank has implemented processes to ensure that explicit and clear consent is obtained from personal data subjects, whether individuals or corporations. Consent is given for data management based on a previously communicated purpose to the data subject, including in any activities involving third parties. This consent must be documented in the Personal Data Processing Record (ROPA).

5. Commitment to Requiring Third Parties Sharing Data to Comply with the Bank's Policies. In addition to business agreements, BTN applies risk management practices related to third-party data handling to ensure all potential risks are

properly managed. Furthermore, the Bank has established compliance and security standards that third parties must adhere to in order to protect the data and information involved in their business engagements. These security standards align with the procedures established by the Bank, as stipulated in Special Policy No. 6-K on Personal Data Protection and Technical Guidelines No. 6-K.5 on Third-Party Cooperation in Personal Data Protection.

BTN does not sell, provide, or lease personal data belonging to data subjects associated with BTN, except for transaction processing purposes, provision of financial services, regulatory reporting, fulfillment of requests from law enforcement authorities, and other exceptions as stipulated by applicable laws and regulations. Processed personal data is minimized, as long as it does not conflict with prevailing regulations.

As a form of transparency to personal data subjects, BTN has published a privacy policy that can be accessed at www.btn.co.id/privacy-policy and other BTN digital platforms. BTN is committed to upholding the rights of personal data subjects in accordance with applicable laws and regulatory requirements.

To address customer concerns related to personal data subject rights, BTN provides multiple channels, including BTN Mobile/app, BTN call center, and customer service at bank branches. The following are the available channels related to personal data subject rights:

- To obtain information related to customers' personal data, accessible via BTN Mobile/app and customer service;
- To request modifications and corrections to customers' personal data, available through customer service; and
- To terminate data processing and delete personal data, available through customer service.

Additionally, BTN has initiated a periodic evaluation process regarding the implementation of personal data protection. This evaluation is conducted by the internal team, but it also involves third parties appointed by the Bank to support this process.
