# Housing Finance Center (HFC) [FS16]

![img-178.jpeg](img-178.jpeg)

The large number of households in Indonesia that have yet to own a home indicates that the housing sector still has significant growth potential, supported by the increasing number of new families and improved accessibility to homeownership. This opportunity is leveraged by the Housing Finance Center (HFC) to address the potential in Indonesia's housing sector. BTN established the Housing Finance Center (HFC), officially launched on October 16, 2014. To further enhance human resource development in the property sector, HFC has also set up a training center at BTN KCP UGM Yogyakarta.

# Vision, Mission, and Pillars of the HFC Program

## Vision

To become a leading center for research, education, and consultancy related to housing finance.

## Mission

To provide excellent, innovative, and integrated services in housing finance research, education, and consultancy for clients.

# HFC Functions

Through HFC, BTN is committed to supporting housing supply and fostering new developers in the housing sector. This effort is realized through three core functions of HFC, which are outlined within three business pillars:

![img-179.jpeg](img-179.jpeg)

1. Learning
The Indonesian Housing Finance Education Center offers a range of educational and training programs.

![img-180.jpeg](img-180.jpeg)

2. Advisory
The Indonesian Property and Housing Finance Consultation Center supports stakeholders (including the public, developers, and government) in the decision-making process.

![img-181.jpeg](img-181.jpeg)

3. Research
The Indonesian Housing Finance Information and Innovation Center provides insights through various scientific studies on the market, industry players, and housing sector trends.
