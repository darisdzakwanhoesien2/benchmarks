## Competency Development Budget

Competency development remains one of BTN's focal points. In 2024, the Bank allocated a larger budget for employee competency development, reflecting a 10% increase compared to 2023. To optimize training implementation, BTN adopts a blended learning approach that combines various methods, such as online learning, virtual classes, and self-learning. This approach aims to enhance educational delivery efficiency without compromising the programs' quality and effectiveness.
