# Employee Health and Safety Management Program

To realize its "Becoming One of the Homes of Indonesia's Best Talent" mission, BTN creates a safe and healthy work environment for all its employees. In line with this commitment, the Bank ensures that all employees receive adequate protection, particularly concerning occupational health and safety (OHS). BTN always strives to maintain high standards regarding workplace safety, with the primary goal of achieving zero work accidents and zero occupational diseases. BTN also recognizes that workplace security and health are essential for protecting employees and directly impacting productivity. A safe and healthy environment boosts motivation and a sense of security among employees, which fosters their trust in the Bank. [GRI 3-3] [OJK F.21]

BTN is obligated to protect employees' welfare, safety, and health, both mentally and physically, as regulated in Law No. 13 of 2003 on Manpower. BTN has established a policy governing the occupational health and safety management system (OHSMS) to fulfill this obligation. This policy serves as a guide to creating a safe and healthy work environment and as a basis for

implementing procedures, training, and risk mitigation actions related to safety and health at the workplace. This policy is documented in several documents, including [GRI 403-1]

a. Special Policy Number 9-AG on Occupational Health and Safety Management System; and
b. Technical Guide Number 9-AG.1 on the Implementation Guidelines for Occupational Health and Safety Management System (OHSMS).

According to the Indonesian Ministry of Health Regulation Number 48 of 2016, implementing OHS in office environments begins with forming and developing the occupational health and safety management system (OHSMS). OHSMS development can be tailored to the office's needs and relevant OHS standards. Aspects to be addressed in the office OHSMS include establishing OHS policies, planning OHS, implementing OHS plans, monitoring and evaluating OHS, and reviewing and improving OHSMS performance. # Employee Health Management Program

To improve employee health quality, BTN provides various health facilities that meet quality standards and have valid practice licenses. These health facilities include various medical services that employees can directly access to maintain their overall health. Additionally, BTN collaborates with external healthcare providers or third parties to give access to medical services that cannot be provided within the bank. The following are health facilities and services accessible to employees: [GRI 403-1, 403-3, 403-6]

a. Periodic medical checkup facilities and treatment services to detect and address specific developing or epidemic diseases;

b. Services to improve employees' health quality, including reimbursement for hospitalization, outpatient care, childbirth expenses, dental care, eyeglasses replacement, hearing aids, laboratory tests, and other medical costs;
c. Polyclinics and doctors are available to employees and their families at any time;
d. Organizing educational seminars on anti-drug, AIDS, and other health-related topics in the workplace; and [GRI 403-5]
e. BPJS Health insurance services for employees and their families.
