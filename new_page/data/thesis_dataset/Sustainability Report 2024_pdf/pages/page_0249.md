The Risk Management Work Unit (SKMR) is led by a Division Head who reports directly to the Director of Risk Management. Currently, SKMR consists of four divisions: the Enterprise &amp; ESG Risk Management Division, the Digital &amp; Operations Risk Management Division, the Wholesale Credit Risk Division, and the Retail Credit Risk Division.

The Risk Management &amp; ESG Work Unit, chaired by BOD-1, acts as a control tower with directive, controlling, and supportive roles in the implementation of ESG principles. As a control tower, the Risk Management &amp; ESG Work Unit not only serves as a second line of defense in oversight but also plays a 1.5-line role in formulating strategies, initiatives, and ESG roadmaps. This work unit monitors the implementation of the ESG roadmap

and continuously evaluates the Bank's defined ESG strategies. Thus, this unit plays a crucial role in ensuring that ESG principles are effectively and consistently integrated into Bank activities.

The Enterprise &amp; ESG Risk Management Division encompasses several departments, including the ESG Department, Integrated Risk Management Department, Credit Portfolio Risk Management Department, Market &amp; Liquidity Risk Management Department, and Model Validation Department.
