This tax will take effect on coal-fired power plants in 2025 at a rate of Rp30 per kg CO₂e and may potentially be expanded to other sectors, including construction. | 1. Increase in NPLs: The carbon tax increases debtor costs, increasing the risk of default and deteriorating credit quality.

3. Decline in ESG Rating: Financing high-emission projects may hinder access to green funding.
4. Tax Risks for the Bank: If BTN is subject to the carbon tax, operational costs will rise, profitability will decline, and financing for high-emission sectors may decrease.  |
