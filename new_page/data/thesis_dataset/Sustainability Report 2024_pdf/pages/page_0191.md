# Inclusive Mortgage Loan

1.  KPR BTN Sejahtera FLPP

KPR BTN Sejahtera FLPP is a homeownership program offered by the Ministry of Housing and Residential Areas for low-income communities (MBR). This program provides low-interest rates and affordable installments, making it accessible for purchasing landed houses or public housing units (flats/apartments). This program serves as a follow-up to the regulations of the Public Housing Savings Management Agency under the State Public Housing Savings Bank Regulation (Perban Tapera) No. 9 of 2021, concerning homeownership financing through the Housing Liquidity Facility.

|  Product Name | Description | Debtor Segmentation | Income Limit | Provisions  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  KPR BTN Sejahtera FLPP | The Housing Liquidity Facility (FLPP) is a financial support program providing liquidity for financing landed houses or apartment units for low-income communities. | Fixed Income - Non-Civil Servants | Unmarried: < Rp7,000,000 Married: up to Rp8,000,000 | 1. The maximum mortgage amount corresponds to the house or apartment selling price, minus the down payment prepared by the low-income communities (MBR). Through this program, MBR is eligible for a Down Payment Assistance Subsidy (SBUM) of Rp4 million (for Java, Sumatra, Kalimantan, and Sulawesi) and Rp10 million (for Papua) provided by the government.
2. The mortgage interest rate paid by the borrower is 5% per year, which already includes life insurance, fire insurance, and credit insurance premiums. The interest rate, as mentioned in point (1), remains fixed throughout the loan tenure (fixed rate), using an annuity-based annual interest calculation method.
3. FLPP program funding sources consist of 75% from the government and 25% from the Bank. BTN collaborates with PT Sarana Multigriya Financial to provide funding allocations for the FLPP program.
4. The mortgage tenure is agreed upon by BTN and the MBR, adjusted according to the borrower's repayment capability, with a maximum term of 20 years.  |

2.  KPR BTN Tapera

KPR BTN Tapera is a homeownership loan facility specifically designed for Civil Servants (ASN) registered as participants of the Public Housing Savings Program (Tapera). This program aims to assist ASN in purchasing their first home under favorable terms and conditions.

|  Product Name | Description | Debtor Segmentation | Income Limit | Provisions  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  KPR BTN Tapera | KPR Tapera is a housing loan facility provided by BTN to low-income households (MBR) participating in Tapera, for purchasing landed houses or apartment units. | Fixed Income - Civil Servants (PNS) | Unmarried or married with income up to Rp8,000,000 | 1. The maximum mortgage amount corresponds to the house or apartment selling price, minus the down payment prepared by the borrower (MBR), who must have been a Tapera participant for at least 12 months.
2. The mortgage interest rate paid by the borrower is 5% per year, which already includes life insurance, fire insurance, and credit insurance premiums. The interest rate, as mentioned in point (1), remains fixed throughout the loan tenure (fixed rate), using an annuity-based annual interest calculation method.
3. KPR Tapera funding sources come from participant contribution savings.
4. The mortgage tenure is agreed upon by BTN and MBR, adjusted according to the borrower's repayment capability, with a maximum term of 30 years.  |
