# Customer Complaint Mechanism

BTN understands that prompt and effective resolution of customer complaints is crucial in providing the best service experience and serves as a key factor in service excellence. As part of its commitment to service quality enhancement, BTN has established a Special Policy No. KK.9-K on Customer Complaints. In accordance with

this policy, the Bank has set up accessible complaint channels across all operational units, ensuring that customers can submit complaints or feedback anytime and from anywhere, without being restricted to the branch where they first registered. Customers can submit complaints both verbally and in writing through the available media channels, including:

# Customer Complaint Media Channels

1

Complaints can be submitted directly to BTN through Regional Offices, Branch Offices, Sub-Branch Offices, and divisions at the Head Office and Board of Directors.

2

Complaints via the Contact Center at 150286 or through BTN's official email: btncontactcenter@btn.co.id. (For complaints via Live Chat, electronic media, or social media, they will be redirected to the official email or contact center).

3

Complaints submitted through the Financial Services Authority (OJK), Bank Indonesia, and other regulatory institutions.

Complaint Handling Process

Customer Submits a Complaint

Customers submit their complaints through the channels provided by BTN.

Customer Receives Complaint Number

BTN records and provides the customer with a ticket number for their complaint.

Complaint Resolution Completed

If the customer agrees with the resolution, the complaint is considered resolved.
