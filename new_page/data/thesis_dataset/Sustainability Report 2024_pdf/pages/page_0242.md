# Remuneration Policy for the Board of Commissioners and Directors

[GRI 2-19]

The determination of remuneration for the Board of Commissioners and Directors refers to Minister of State-Owned Enterprises Regulation No. PER-13/MBU/09/2021, regarding the Sixth Amendment to Minister of State-Owned Enterprises Regulation No. PER-04/MBU/2014 concerning Guidelines for Setting the Income of

Directors, Board of Commissioners, and Supervisory Board of State-Owned Enterprises. Additionally, the remuneration for the Board of Commissioners and Directors is also determined based on the achievement of their objectives and performance in managing the Bank's impact on economic, environmental, and social aspects.
