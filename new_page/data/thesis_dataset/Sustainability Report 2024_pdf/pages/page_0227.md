## Board of Directors

The Board of Directors is the primary organ in governance, fully responsible for managing the Bank to achieve its established objectives and interests. The responsibilities of the Board of Directors include operational management, strategic formulation, and the management and protection of the Bank's assets. In practice, the Board of Directors is required to report its performance results to the Board of Commissioners.

Each member of the Board of Directors must have at least five years of experience in operational functions, holding a position no lower than an executive officer in a bank, and must meet the criteria for integrity, competence, and financial reputation as stipulated by the Financial Services Authority (OJK).
