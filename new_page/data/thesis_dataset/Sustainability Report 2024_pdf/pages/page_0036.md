BTN facilitates access to financing for all segments of society, particularly vulnerable groups, thereby contributing to social and economic stability across various regions.

In the Housing sector, BTN plays a vital role in enhancing access to adequate housing. A total of 61% of BTN's mortgage recipients reside in rural areas, where housing accessibility requires greater attention. The majority of beneficiaries are in the productive age group, with 68.12% aged between 30 and 60 years, aligning with BTN's mission to support family stability and economic well-being. Additionally, BTN demonstrates its

commitment to gender inclusion, with 31% of mortgage financing allocated to women, as part of its efforts to promote gender equality in housing access.
