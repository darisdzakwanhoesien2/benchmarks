# Economic Performance in 2024 [GRI 3-3]

Indonesia's economic performance in 2024 reflects steady recovery efforts amidst various global challenges. According to official data from the Indonesian Statistics Agency (BPS), the country's economic growth in the third quarter of 2024 was 4.95% year-on-year (YoY). In addition, quarter-to-quarter (QtQ) growth reached 1.50% compared to the previous quarter. These achievements indicate a positive trend in national economic activity.

This stable economic recovery underscores the critical role of the banking sector in supporting national growth. Banks in Indonesia are now facing the challenge of integrating sustainability principles, such as Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) factors, into their services and products. Beyond developing innovative, sustainable financial products to drive real sector growth, the banking industry also contributes to climate change mitigation and reducing social inequality. The banking sector can play a more significant role in fostering inclusive and sustainable economic growth with the right strategies.

BTN remains steadfast in its commitment to supporting economic recovery by leveraging existing challenges

as opportunities to accelerate growth aligned with ESG principles. BTN has continued to deliver significant performance, particularly in the property and housing finance sectors, substantially contributing to Indonesia's disbursing mortgage loans (KPR). Throughout 2024, BTN successfully disbursed financing for 199,384 housing units.

BTN consistently plays a strategic role in the housing finance sector as part of its contribution to national economic improvement and strengthening. In 2024, BTN disbursed 118,665 subsidized housing units, amounting to Rp19.45 trillion, capturing 73.87% of the subsidized mortgage market share. This achievement highlights BTN's commitment to supporting the government in providing adequate housing for low-income communities while solidifying its position as a market leader in housing finance in Indonesia.

BTN's efforts and consistency in managing its economic performance this year resulted in a net profit of Rp3.01 trillion, reflecting 85.26% of the target set. Despite challenges affecting performance, BTN remains focused on sustainability strategies expected to strengthen its market position and add value for stakeholders. [OJK F.2]
