# Green Office at the Head Office

Although operational activities have no direct impact on the environment, BTN consistently supports and contributes to environmental preservation and social welfare. This commitment is also a pillar in BTN's Corporate Work Plan and Budget (RAKB) under the theme "Product Pillars Supporting Sustainable Finance."

Concrete steps in environmental preservation and social welfare at the Head Office area are implemented by the Bank through various initiatives, including: [OJK F.1]

a. Conducting regular efforts to prevent environmental pollution through various measures, such as:

- Using environmentally friendly technology for air conditioners (R32, R134A, R410 refrigerants);
- Providing temporary storage for hazardous waste (TPS LB3) with a storage capacity of 3.20 m x 3.50 m x 2.50 m;
- Installing a wastewater treatment plant (IPAL) at the building with a capacity of 220 m³/day and has met the permitted threshold in the Wastewater Discharge Permit issued on July 19, 2018, which is 260 m³/day;
- Has a wastewater treatment plant (WWTP) at Menara 2 BTN with a capacity of 18 m³.

b. Energy-saving efforts, including:

- Utilizing solar energy as a water heating source;
- Limiting water usage through flow regulation in toilets, ablution areas, and others;
- Automating the use of lighting and air conditioning systems through BAS (Building Automation System) technology.

c. Implementing the utilization of two infiltration wells in BTN Tower and 25 infiltration wells in BTN Tower 2;

d. Providing temporary storage facilities (TPS) with a capacity of up to 14 m³ in BTN Tower and 18 m³ in BTN Tower 2, from where the collected waste will be transported to the final processing area (TPA);
e. Having 35 biopore points spread across the BTN Tower area;
f. Conducts a plastic-free campaign in the office by reducing the use of bottled mineral water and single-use utensils in meetings and events, both internal and external, while encouraging awareness of bringing personal drinking bottles;
g. Encouraging employees to use public transportation for commuting to the office. This aligns with government policies mandating that all operational vehicles of state-owned enterprises (BUMN) use non-subsidized fuel with high octane ratings for better environmental sustainability. This was also supported by BTN's circular letter No. 1836/M/PGSD/GS/XI/2013 issued by the General Division and Public Services on November 6, 2013, urging regional offices, branch offices, and sharia branch offices to avoid using RON 88 gasoline (Premium);
h. Supporting and participating in the Earth Hour initiative since 2020.

In implementing the green office concept, BTN adheres to the following regulations and guidelines: [ORI 3-3]

a. Law No. 32 of 2009 on Environmental Protection and Management (UU No. 32 Tahun 2009);
b. Law No. 17 of 2019 on Water Resources;
c. Law No. 18 of 2008 on Waste Management;
d. Government Regulation No. 101 of 2014 on Hazardous Waste Management;
e. Government Regulation No. 74 on the Management of Hazardous and Toxic Substances.
