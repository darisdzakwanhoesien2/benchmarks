# Commitment to Governance Implementation

BTN is fully committed to integrating the principles of Good Corporate Governance (GCG) into all operational aspects. GCG serves as a key foundation for BTN's management, not only as a means of fulfilling legal obligations but also as a shared form of accountability to safeguard the Bank's business. BTN views the implementation of GCG as a strategic step to enhance performance, competitiveness, and business sustainability in the long term.

In the dynamic landscape of the banking sector, BTN continuously strengthens the implementation of GCG to create a balance of control within the Bank. Through effective implementation, GCG helps mitigate risks of fraud and minimizes errors in organizational management. Additionally, BTN's ability to meet stakeholder expectations, both internally and externally, becomes an essential asset to sustain business continuity, in alignment with prevailing regulations and recognized international best practices.

In implementing GCG, BTN refers to various laws, regulations, and relevant GCG guidelines at both national and international levels. The basis for BTN's GCG implementation includes:

- Law No. 40 of 2007 on Limited Liability Companies;
