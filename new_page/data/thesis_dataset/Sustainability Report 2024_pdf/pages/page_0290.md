# Internal Control

The implementation of an internal control system aims to support the achievement of optimal performance for the Bank while also providing added value for stakeholders. The system functions to minimize risk and ensure compliance with applicable regulations. In addition to being a critical component of the Bank's oversight mechanism, the internal control system also acts as a guideline to ensure the Bank's operations run smoothly, efficiently, and in a controlled manner.

In designing the internal control framework, BTN refers to the Committee of the Sponsoring Organizations of the Treadway Commission (COSO)-Internal Control Integrated Framework. This approach aims to ensure adequate controls in various aspects, such as operations, financial reporting, operational effectiveness and efficiency, as well as compliance with applicable regulations. This way, the Bank can minimize potential risks arising during operational and financial processes.

BTN management has established an ongoing monitoring mechanism. The goal of this monitoring is to protect the Bank's assets, ensure that the reports produced are accurate and reliable, and enhance compliance with applicable regulations. Moreover, this system aims to improve cost efficiency, reduce the impact of financial losses, and prevent fraud or breaches of prudential principles in Bank management. By implementing a strong internal control system, BTN can ensure operations run better and safer, thus maintaining long-term business sustainability.

# Gratification Control

BTN recognizes that every employee, across various divisions, may face potential risks related to gratification. Therefore, BTN encourages all employees to actively support the anti-gratification program by reporting regarding any case of gratification &amp; Governance Work Unit. This is a concrete step to reinforce commitment to controlling gratification and maintaining the Bank's integrity. Furthermore, to support the implementation of anti-fraud values, BTN has established a Whistleblowing System (WBS), accompanied by various internalization activities. The system is implemented by third-party independent entities to ensure its execution is objective and transparent, creating a work environment free from irregularities and fraud.

Throughout the reporting period, BTN received 96 reports related to gratification from the Head Office and Branch Offices. These reports encompass various forms of gratification, such as gifts, gratification related to official duties, the provision of food before religious holidays, and refusals of gratification. As part of its commitment to preventing gratification and corruption, BTN continues to strengthen monitoring and risk management within its operations. To maintain its ISO 37001 - SMAP Certification, Surveillance Audit with a scope of 15 work units and Extend Scope in 1 work unit selected based on core business considerations with high corruption risks. This initiative ensures that all bank operations remain aligned with the principles of transparency, integrity, and compliance with applicable regulations. [GRi 205-1]
