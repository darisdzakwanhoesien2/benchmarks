# Personal Data Protection

Personal data protection refers to a series of steps and procedures for managing personal information by ensuring that the data is securely protected according to its classification level. Protection also emphasizes the importance of transparency in every stage of personal data management, meaning that all involved parties must clearly understand how their data is collected, used, and safeguarded. Personal data protection includes establishing obligations that must be fulfilled by entities managing the data, such as the Bank, as well as the rights of individuals whose personal data is being processed to protect their interests.

BTN has implemented personal data protection practices with the primary goal of building and maintaining customer trust, while ensuring compliance with Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection. In terms of personal data protection, BTN has established a Data Privacy Department, which serves as the Personal Data Protection Officer under the Compliance Unit, within the Directorate of Human Capital, Compliance &amp; Legal. This department is actively supervised by the Board of Directors through the Information Technology Steering Committee (KPTI), Risk Management Committee, and Personal Data Protection Implementation Project Steering Committee. The Data Privacy Department is responsible for developing frameworks and policies and implementing personal data protection programs to ensure comprehensive, bank-wide implementation of data protection controls.

The personal data protection and management practices applied by BTN are reflected in policies regulating various aspects of personal data governance, from collection and usage (sharing) to data storage and retention. Below is a list of documents and policies detailing these practices:

- Special Policy (KK) 6-K on Personal Data Protection
- Special Policy (KK) 5-B on Information Technology Security
- Special Policy (KK) 9-Z on Consumer and Community Protection
- Special Policy (KK) 9-J on Archival Management Procedures

- Technical Guidelines (PT) 6-K.1 on Consent of Personal Data Subjects
- Technical Guidelines (PT) 6-K.2 on Personal Data Collection Records
- Technical Guidelines (PT) 6-K.3 on Impact Assessment of Personal Data Protection
- Technical Guidelines (PT) 6-K.4 on Handling Personal Data Protection Failures
- Technical Guidelines (PT) 6-K.5 on Cooperation with Third Parties Regarding Personal Data Protection
- Technical Guidelines (PT) 9-J.1 on the Implementation of Archival Management

As part of its compliance efforts, the Bank ensures the confidentiality and security of personal information through:

1. **Personal Information Retention**

Personal information stored by the Bank must be deleted when it is no longer required for business operations or to fulfill legal and regulatory obligations.

2. **Disclosure of Personal Data**

Personal data may only be shared with third parties if explicit permission or consent has been obtained from the data owner or if required by an authorized official in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. The disclosure of personal data by the Bank is strictly permitted only when it complies with the relevant legal and regulatory provisions.

As the entity responsible for managing personal data, BTN has fulfilled its obligations by undertaking the following actions:

1. **Commitment to Collecting and Processing User Data for Limited Purposes.** In the process of collecting and processing personal data, BTN prioritizes the principle of data minimization, meaning that only data that is strictly necessary and relevant for a specific purpose is collected.
