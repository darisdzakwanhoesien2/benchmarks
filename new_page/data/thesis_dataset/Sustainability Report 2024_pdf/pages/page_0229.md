As part of collective responsibility, each Board member is jointly and fully liable for any losses resulting from errors or negligence in carrying out their duties. However, a Board member may be exempt from liability if they can demonstrate that:

1. The loss was not due to their error or negligence.
2. They performed their duties in good faith, responsibly, and with due diligence in the best interest of the Bank.
3. They had no direct or indirect conflict of interest in the management actions that led to the loss.
4. They took necessary measures to prevent or mitigate the loss.

# ESG Committee

BTN has established ESG Committee, a board-level Committee, by the President Director, with membership comprising the Board of Directors and SEVP. The committee aims to support the achievement of the Company's Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and climate targets. The ESG Committee plays a critical role in ensuring that the Bank's sustainability policies encompass Environmental, Social, and Governance aspects aligned with the Company's strategic objectives. The responsibilities of the ESG Committee include formulating policies, strategies, and objectives for ESG and Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) programs,

focusing on environmental, social, and governance impacts. Additionally, the committee is responsible for overseeing ESG-related risks and opportunities and making strategic decisions regarding ESG and CSR program implementation. Regular meetings are conducted annually. [GRI 2-18] [FS2]
