- Waste Management
Each housing unit is required to provide waste separation facilities, making it easier to recycle and manage waste efficiently.

- Green Open Spaces
A minimum of 10% of the total residential area must be allocated for green open spaces. This is intended to create recreational areas that enhance residents' quality of life while also functioning as rainwater absorption zones and reducing the urban heat effect.

BTN prioritizes sustainability in all aspects of its operations, including providing financing for environmentally friendly housing developments. In addition to focusing on environmental aspects, BTN is committed to ensuring that the homes that are built are accessible to low- and middle-income communities. This means that BTN not only acts as a financial provider but also as a driver of change in creating affordable, healthy, and sustainable housing for all social groups.

Collaboration between BTN and various stakeholders, including the government, developers, and communities, will ensure that low-emission housing developments not only support environmental sustainability but also generate significant social and economic benefits. As part of its Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) efforts and sustainability principles, BTN remains committed to promoting a circular economy, reducing negative environmental impacts, and enhancing community well-being by providing healthier and more sustainable housing. This program is also aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

# Green Bond

BTN has developed an ESG-based Green Bond Portfolio. ESG-based green bonds are issued to finance projects that support environmental sustainability while integrating Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles. In addition to focusing on environmental aspects, such as carbon emission reduction and renewable energy, these green bonds also consider social impacts, including community empowerment and job creation, while ensuring that issuers maintain good and transparent governance. Projects financed through these bonds must comply with strict ESG standards, with transparent reporting on fund utilization and impact.

As of 2024, BTN has established several ESG-based green bond portfolios with a total value of IDR 941.211 billion, consisting of green bond instruments such as SBSN amounting to Rp765.267 billion and SUKBI totaling

Rp125.944 billion. Additionally, BTN has issued social bonds through the Sustainable Bonds VI SMF Phase I and II (2021), each valued at Rp25 billion.

As part of its ongoing commitment, BTN continues to strengthen its role in green financing by developing sustainable financial products, expanding partnerships with the public and private sectors, and integrating ESG principles into every investment decision. In 2025, BTN aims to issue sustainability/social bonds, which are expected to support sustainable projects that have a positive impact on the environment or society.
