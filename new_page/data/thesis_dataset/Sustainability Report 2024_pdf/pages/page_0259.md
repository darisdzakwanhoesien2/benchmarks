# ESG Risk

## Climate Change Risks and Opportunities

Climate change can have a significant impact on the banking sector. One of the consequences is the increased risk of loan defaults by debtors, especially those whose businesses are directly affected by climate change. Therefore, BTN establishes specific environmental criteria before disbursing credit to ensure funds are not allocated to projects or activities that may harm the environment. This step is crucial in mitigating risks associated with financing activities that contribute to environmental degradation.
