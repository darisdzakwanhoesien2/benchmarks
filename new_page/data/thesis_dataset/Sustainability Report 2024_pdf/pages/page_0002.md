# Connecting Finance and Sustainability for Shared Prosperity

BTN is deeply committed to the strategic integration of financial and sustainability aspects, fostering inclusive and sustainable prosperity for all stakeholders. The Bank ensures that every policy, product, and service not only drives economic growth but also creates positive impacts on social well-being and environmental conservation.

The commitment to sustainable finance is reinforced through the adoption of the 5P principles (Planet, People, Peace, Partnership, and Prosperity) as a foundation for developing sustainable financial products and services. One of BTN's key initiatives is the low-emission housing financing program, which supports financial inclusion, circular economy, and mitigates climate change impacts. These initiatives are part of BTN's broader

strategy to achieve the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) in alignment with Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles.

BTN continues to optimize financial resources to strengthen financial inclusion and access to sustainable housing. Sustainable finance implementation encompasses the development of a green portfolio, the integration of environmentally friendly technologies in the housing sector, and collaboration with various stakeholders to create broader economic, social, and environmental benefits. Through these efforts, BTN not only contributes to national economic development but also acts as a catalyst for transforming the housing ecosystem into a more inclusive, innovative, and sustainable landscape.
