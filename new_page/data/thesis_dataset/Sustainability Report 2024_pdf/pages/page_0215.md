# Deletion of Personal Data

In an effort to comply with applicable legal provisions, particularly those related to personal data protection, BTN adheres to the regulations set out in Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection. Although this law outlines the obligation to delete and destroy personal data, it does not explicitly establish a retention period for personal data. Therefore, BTN refers to more detailed internal policies, such as those outlined in KK 9-M on Data Governance Guidelines, which stipulate that determining the data retention period is the responsibility of each data owner. Additionally, BTN also has PT 9-J1 on Archive Management Implementation, which regulates the Archive Retention Schedule based

on the data categories owned. Each data category, including products, services, and specific types of data, is managed by the responsible division owner, who determines the appropriate storage duration.

As part of our commitment to transparent and sustainable governance, BTN is also committed to not obtaining or using customer personal data from other sources without the explicit consent of the customer. This measure is taken to ensure the security and confidentiality of customer personal data, as well as to strengthen public trust in the responsible management of personal data.
