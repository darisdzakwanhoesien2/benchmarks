# Diversity Recruitment Goals

As part of its sustainability commitment, BTN has set quantitative diversity targets in recruitment, with the goal of having 0.5% of new employees from underrepresented groups by 2025, 1% by 2026, and 2% by 2028. These targets include individuals with disabilities and other underrepresented groups, in line with the Bank's efforts to create a more inclusive and diverse work environment.

To support this diversity, BTN provides various facilities to meet the needs of individuals from different backgrounds. Some steps include inclusive training, infrastructure adjustments to create an accessible work environment, and mentoring programs to ensure career success for employees from these groups. BTN has launched several programs aimed at accelerating diversity implementation through various initiatives:

1. Induction Training for All New TKPD Employees: This training covers product knowledge, the Code of Conduct, banking business processes, rights and responsibilities, and company culture, ensuring

they have a shared understanding and can contribute optimally in the workplace.

2. Inclusive Management Training for Managers: Aimed at increasing awareness and applying diversity principles within teams.
3. Periodic Assessments of Diversity Targets: Involves transparent monitoring and cross-functional collaboration.
