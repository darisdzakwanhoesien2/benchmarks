# Diversity, Equality, and Inclusion

## Policies and Management Oversight

BTN upholds the fundamental principles of diversity, equality, and inclusion for all employees following Law Number 13 of 2003 concerning Labor. To ensure these principles are implemented concretely, BTN has developed policies and regulations supporting respect for human rights. These principles are outlined in General Policy No.KU.3 on Human Capital and the Collective

Labor Agreement. BTN ensures that diversity, equality, and inclusion are applied across all processes, including recruitment, training and competency development, career progression, employee remuneration, and welfare. BTN also guarantees that these processes are carried out without discrimination based on gender, religion, ethnicity, or race. [GRI 3-3, 2-23] [OJK C.3, F.18]

## Diversity in the Composition of the Board of Commissioners and Directors [GRI 2-9]

Diversity within the Board of Commissioners and Directors fosters inclusive management and ensures sustainable outcomes. Diverse backgrounds, including age, gender, education, work experience, and expertise at the highest level of governance, enrich the strategic decision-making process. This diversity allows the Bank to assess issues and opportunities from various perspectives, leading to more accurate and innovative decisions. It also supports creating an open and inclusive environment where different viewpoints are valued, ultimately enhancing the Bank's long-term performance and competitiveness.

Although the members of the Board of Commissioners and Directors do not come from underrepresented social groups, they remain committed to listening to their aspirations and acting as representatives for such groups. This approach reflects efforts to foster inclusive governance.
