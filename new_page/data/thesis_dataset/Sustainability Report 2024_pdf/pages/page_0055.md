## Effective Date of Company Name Changes:

The Bank was established under the name "Postspaarbank" in 1897, which was later changed to "Tyokin Kyoku" or "Savings Office" in 1942. After the independence of the Republic of Indonesia, the Bank's name was changed again to "Kantor Taboengan Pos" in 1945. On 9 February 1950, the Government of the Republic of Indonesia renamed it "Bank Tabungan Pos." Effective 22 June 1963, the Bank's name was changed to "Bank Tabungan Negara." Later, in 1992, the Bank officially became a Persero (State-Owned Enterprise).
