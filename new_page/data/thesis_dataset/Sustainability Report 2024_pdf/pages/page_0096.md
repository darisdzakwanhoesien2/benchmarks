# Transformation of Waste Into Sustainable Solutions for the Future

On Friday, October 25, 2024, BTN's Director of Risk Management, Mr. Setiyo Wibowo, along with the BTN ESD team, visited the Galuga Final Disposal Site (TPA) in Bogor. During this visit, they met with @plustikid, a supplier of eco-friendly materials that processes recycled plastic into floor decking and paving blocks. This visit opened up significant opportunities to transform waste piles into sustainable solutions that benefit the environment, society, and the economy.

As part of BTN's commitment to sustainable waste management, this initiative aims to strengthen efforts in reducing carbon footprints and creating a greener future. BTN also aims to promote the development of eco-friendly housing that minimizes negative impacts on the Earth while supporting the circular economy through more efficient waste utilization.

More than just waste management, this is a concrete step toward generating a broad positive impact for both communities and our planet. Through this collaboration, BTN is committed to continued innovation, taking bold actions, and building a more sustainable future for generations to come.
