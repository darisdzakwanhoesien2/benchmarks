# BTN and The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) initiated by the United Nations (UN) in 2015 consist of 17 goals and 169 targets aimed at promoting global sustainability through the integration of economic, social, and environmental aspects.

Indonesia has demonstrated its commitment to achieving the SDGs through the issuance of Presidential Regulation No. 59 of 2017. In this context, BTN contributes to SDG 11 by supporting housing finance to help create inclusive, safe, and affordable cities and settlements by 2030.

BTN's contribution aligns with the mandate of Law No. 14 of 2016 on Housing and Settlement Area Development. Through its financing products, BTN not only addresses sustainability challenges but also strengthens business resilience against sustainability-related risks while supporting the government's program to build three million homes and other inclusive and sustainable national development initiatives.

# KPR BTN Sejahtera: Happy Families, Prosperous Lives

KPR BTN Sejahtera is a subsidized mortgage program under the Housing Finance Liquidity Facility (FLPP) scheme implemented by Indonesia's Ministry of Public Works and Housing (PUPR) for low-income communities. This program offers very low interest rates and affordable installments for purchasing standalone houses and affordable apartments.
