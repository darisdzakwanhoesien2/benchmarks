# Oversight of Customer Complaint Management

The BTN Risk Monitoring Committee, operating at the Board of Commissioners level, oversees customer complaint management as part of operational risk management. In this process, the Committee collaborates with the Director of Operations to receive performance reports on complaints handling from the Experience Division Unit. This initiative aims to identify potential risks and ensure appropriate follow-up actions to improve processes and mitigate emerging risks, enabling effective and efficient customer complaint management.

If a complaint cannot be resolved directly and escalates into a dispute, customers are given the opportunity to escalate their case to a dispute resolution body,

such as arbitration, mediation, or court proceedings, in accordance with applicable regulations. This approach reflects BTN's commitment to maintaining its integrity and credibility, as well as managing legal and reputational risks that may arise from unresolved disputes.

Through this structured approach, BTN remains committed to managing customer complaints with prudence and continuous improvement, aiming to enhance customer satisfaction and loyalty. This commitment underscores the Bank's dedication to providing fair and transparent solutions for customers while continuously improving overall service quality.

# Customer Complaint Handling Process

To further enhance the quality of customer complaint management, BTN collaborates with external parties, such as the Ombudsman and the National Consumer Protection Agency (BPKN), to accelerate the complaint resolution process. This collaboration aims to ensure that each complaint is resolved promptly and accurately, while maintaining the Bank's credibility in delivering the best solutions for customers.
