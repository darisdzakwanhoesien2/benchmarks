The impacts of environmental degradation and climate change are becoming increasingly apparent and have emerged as significant challenges. Recognizing these impacts, BTN is committed to reducing the negative effects on the environment caused by operational activities.

![img-119.jpeg](img-119.jpeg)

Managing the risks of climate change and its effects on ecosystems (climate change and ecosystems) has also become an integral part of BTN's environmental, social, and governance (ESG) framework. Therefore, BTN has designed various programs to mitigate climate change risks. These efforts also serve as the Bank's contribution to supporting the government's commitment to achieving carbon neutrality by 2060 and advancing the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), specifically Goal 13: Climate Action. [see 2-3]

BTN has also ensured the Bank's compliance with environmental and social issues. This commitment involves routine reporting conducted twice a year, separate from the Bank's plans and initiatives, and includes the implementation of the Environmental Management Plan (RKL) and the Environmental Monitoring Plan (RPL), which refer to the Minister of Environment Decree No. 45 of 2005 on Guidelines for Preparing Environmental Monitoring Plans (RPL), as well as other applicable regulations and laws.
