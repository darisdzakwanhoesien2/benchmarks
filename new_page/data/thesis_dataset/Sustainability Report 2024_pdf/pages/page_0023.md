BTN considers the designated material topics as the foundation for decision-making, guidance, and motivation to enhance its role and contribution to achieving sustainability performance, covering economic, social, and environmental aspects. This approach is also aligned with the key priorities of stakeholders, which are essential for the Bank in conducting its business. These considerations are outlined in the following topic boundaries: [GRE 3-1, 3-2, 3-3]
