# General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) [GRI 2-11]

The General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) is the highest organ within the Bank's structure, functioning as a forum for shareholders to exercise their rights, whether to obtain information or provide insights related to the Company's strategic policies. Under Law No. 40 of 2007 on Limited Liability Companies, the GMS is defined as "a corporate organ with authority not granted to the Board of Directors or the Board of Commissioners, within the limits determined by this Law and/or the Company's Articles of Association."

As the highest governance body, the GMS is not part of the Company's executive branch but serves as a platform where the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners present performance reports and accountability for the duties they have carried out. In accordance with BTN's Articles of Association and

applicable regulations, shareholders do not participate in the Bank's operational activities, which are entirely the responsibility of the Board of Directors. Furthermore, the GMS plays a critical role in making strategic decisions that directly impact the sustainability of the Bank's operations and strategic direction. [GRI 2-11]

The Bank holds two types of General Meetings of Shareholders: the Annual GMS and the Extraordinary GMS. The Annual GMS must be conducted no later than six months after the fiscal year ends. Meanwhile, the Extraordinary GMS, also referred to as a Special GMS, may be convened at any time based on the Bank's needs and interests. ## Board of Commissioners

The Board of Commissioners is a governance organ of the Bank responsible for carrying out both comprehensive and specific supervision in accordance with the provisions of the Articles of Association. Additionally, the Board of Commissioners plays a role in providing recommendations to the Board of Directors. Its primary function is to ensure the interests of the Bank and its shareholders are fulfilled while supporting the achievement of BTN's goals, taking into account the interests of all stakeholders.

In performing its supervisory duties, the Board of Commissioners is authorized to establish committees composed of members of the Board of Commissioners. These committees report directly to the Board of Commissioners. The functions and duties of the Board of Commissioners are further regulated by Law No. 40 of 2007 on Limited Liability Companies, Law No. 7 of 1992 on Banking, and other relevant regulations, including provisions set by OJK, Bank Indonesia, and BTN's Articles of Association.
