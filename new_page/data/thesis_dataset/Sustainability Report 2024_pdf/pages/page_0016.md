# ESG Principles

## Sustainable Finance [GRI 2-12, 2-13] [OJK B.3, E.1]

BTN applies the principles of Sustainable Finance in accordance with POJK Regulation No. 51/POJK.03/2017, which came into effect on January 1, 2019. This regulation governs the implementation of sustainable finance for Financial Services Institutions, Issuers, and Public Companies. It emphasizes the importance of having personnel, officers, and/or work units authorized to manage and coordinate activities related to Sustainable Finance. BTN has adjusted its organizational structure to align with these requirements.

BTN has designated the Enterprise &amp; ESG Risk Management as the unit responsible for managing and coordinating Sustainable Finance activities. Implementation involves collaboration across various work units, including the Corporate Secretary, Compliance and Governance, Commercial Banking, Corporate Banking, Digital Banking Product Development, Financial Institution &amp; Capital Market, Human Capital Management, Human Capital Strategy, Investor Relations &amp; Research, IT Strategic Planning &amp; Development, Large Institutional Funding, Learning Management, Mortgage and Secure Loan, Procurement &amp; Fixed Asset Management, Retail Funding, Sharia Banking, Small Medium Enterprise Banking, Subsidized Mortgage, Personal Banking, Marketing Communication, and Data Management. [TCFD Gov.a]

In its implementation, BTN adheres to eight principles of sustainable finance, which serve as regulatory guidelines:

1.  Principle of Responsible Investment
Prioritizing investments in sustainable projects for long-term benefits, considering economic, social, environmental, and governance aspects.

2.  Principle of Sustainable Business Strategy and Practice
Minimizing negative impacts through policies and strategies that integrate sustainability aspects.

3.  Principle of Social and Environmental Risk Management
Incorporating social responsibility and environmental protection into risk management to prevent adverse impacts.

4.  Principle of Governance
Applying governance that is transparent, accountable, responsible, independent, and equitable.

5.  Principle of Informative Communication
Employing effective communication about strategies, performance, and business prospects with stakeholders.

6.  Principle of Inclusivity
Expanding access to banking products and services for all segments of society, particularly underserved communities.

7.  Principle of Priority Sector Development
Supporting priority sectors to achieve sustainable development goals and mitigate climate change.

8.  Principle of Coordination and Collaboration
Strengthening synergy with stakeholders to accelerate improvements in economic prosperity, social welfare, and environmental quality.

## Challenges and Issues in the Implementation of Sustainable Finance [OJK E.5]

Throughout 2024, BTN successfully maintained consistency in implementing sustainable finance without facing significant obstacles. However, the Bank continues to encounter various challenges that require special attention. As a proactive measure, BTN has identified related risks and implemented strategic mitigation steps to ensure the optimal achievement of sustainability objectives.
