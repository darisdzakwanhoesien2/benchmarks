# Risk Monitoring

BTN implements a system and procedures for monitoring that include tracking risk exposure levels, risk tolerance, compliance with internal limits, stress testing results, and consistency in the implementation of established policies and procedures. The results of risk monitoring are presented in periodic reports submitted to management. These reports are used as a basis for risk mitigation and determining necessary actions.

To ensure the smooth operation of the monitoring process, the Bank prepares an effective backup system to prevent disruptions in risk monitoring. Additionally, regular reviews and reassessments are conducted on the backup system to ensure its sustainability and the effectiveness of the implemented risk monitoring process.

# Risk Management Information System

The risk management information system is managed collaboratively by multiple divisions within the Bank. At the initial stage, the primary focus is on collecting and improving the quality of risk and data. This is expected to be developed and gradually applied within the risk management information system. The goal is to ensure that risk measurement and monitoring processes are conducted in an integrated, timely, and compliant manner in accordance with applicable regulations.

# Impact and Risk Assessment

Impact and risk assessment conducted by the Bank is a thorough evaluation process to identify and analyze risks as well as potential impacts arising from certain activities or policies. The main objective of this assessment is to minimize potential risks while understanding how decisions made by the Bank can influence operations and long-term business sustainability. This process includes several key elements, such as:

1. New Product Development

The Bank evaluates risk related to new products, whether funding or credit products. This assessment includes the impact on customers, the Bank's operations, and business aspects such as information technology, compliance, legal, and financial factors to ensure that the products can operate smoothly without causing harmful risks.

2. BTN Technology/Applications

The Bank also assesses potential risks related to the use of technology, particularly in data privacy and cybersecurity. Given the importance of protecting customer personal data and the security of the Bank's information systems, this aspect becomes a primary focus to avoid data breaches or cyberattacks that could harm the Bank and its customers.

3. Responsible Investments

The assessment is also conducted to ensure that the Bank's investments adhere to sustainability principles by considering the social and environmental impacts that may arise. This ensures that the Bank's investments are not only financially beneficial but also socially and ethically responsible.

4. Human Resource Development Policies

Evaluating human resource development policies is crucial to ensure that the Bank can manage and develop its employees effectively without adding operational risk. This includes appropriate training and development to enhance employee competencies and support the Bank's strategic objectives.
