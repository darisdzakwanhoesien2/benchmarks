# Employee Engagement Survey

As a proactive step to identify existing issues, BTN conducts an employee engagement survey annually, covering all employees (100%). This survey aims to measure employee engagement levels and identify areas for improvement.

The results of the 2024 Employee Engagement Survey showed an average score of 93 on a scale of 100. The survey reflects an excellent level of engagement with key indicators including:

1. Benefit;
2. Pay;
3. Performance Management.

4. Talent Management;
5. Learning and Development;
6. Leadership;
7. Empowerment;
8. Supervision;
9. Collaboration and Teamwork;
10. Customer Focus;
11. Efficiency;
12. Competitiveness;
13. Change;
14. Wellness and Stress;
15. Innovation;
16. Sustainable Engagement; and
17. Retention.

BTN utilizes the employee engagement survey findings to develop strategic programs, such as improving well-being services, enhancing benefits, improving talent management programs, enhancing internal company communication, and implementing an innovation

award. These steps aim to improve productivity and job satisfaction, reduce employee turnover, and improve communication between employees and management to support policy improvements and create a positive work culture.
