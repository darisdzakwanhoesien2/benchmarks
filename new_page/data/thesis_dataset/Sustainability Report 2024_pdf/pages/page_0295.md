## Whistleblower Protection Guarantee

BTN is committed to ensuring protection for whistleblowers who report violations in good faith, especially if the violation has the potential to harm the Bank. This protection is implemented through the following policies:

1. Provision of a free and confidential WBS reporting platform. Whistleblowers will receive updates on their report status from the Independent WBS Manager through designated communication channels.
2. Confidentiality of the whistleblower's identity, except in cases where legal proceedings require identity disclosure before a judge.

3. Protection against retaliation by the reported party, which includes:

a. Physical and psychological protection for the whistleblower and their family.
b. Protection of personal and family assets from threats or retaliatory actions.
c. Administrative protection, including safeguards against delayed promotions, unfair dismissals, improper transfers, and job security threats.
d. Legal protection, where necessary, in litigation proceedings in accordance with applicable regulations.

## Complaint Handling System

BTN has established a strategic partnership with the Corruption Eradication Commission (KPK) of the Republic of Indonesia to manage the Whistleblowing System (WBS) as part of a joint effort to combat corruption practices. This collaboration aims to enhance the effectiveness and efficiency of complaint handling conducted both internally by employees and externally by third parties, by adopting an integrated, professional, transparent, and accountable approach. Every step taken within this collaborative framework emphasizes confidentiality and ensures that reports received are handled securely without fear of retaliation. Accordingly, BTN is committed to optimizing policies and internal strategies to comprehensively prevent and combat corruption offenses through a reliable system that fosters a strong anti-corruption culture.
