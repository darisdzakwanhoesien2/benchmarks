# Consumer and Community Protection

BTN has established Special Policy No. KK.9-Z regarding Customer and Community Protection. This policy ensures maximum protection for customers and the public by providing clear, accurate, and transparent information regarding products and/or services offered by the Bank. It includes explanations of all potential consequences and risks that customers or the public may face when deciding to use or benefit from the Bank's products and/or services. By implementing this policy, customers and the public will not only be protected from potential financial losses that may arise from using a product or service but also from legal risks that could emerge in connection with such use. This policy aims to create a sense of security and trust among customers and the public in every transaction or engagement they have with the Bank.

BTN upholds fundamental principles of consumer and community protection to safeguard customers and the public, including:

1. Adequate education;
2. Transparency and openness of information;
3. Fair treatment and responsible business conduct;

4. Protection of assets, privacy, and customer data;
5. Effective and efficient complaint handling and dispute resolution;
6. Compliance enforcement; and
7. Healthy competition.

BTN is committed to protecting customer rights as subjects of personal data in accordance with applicable regulations, such as the Personal Data Protection Law. Customers have essential rights, including the right to receive clear information regarding the collection and use of their personal data, the right to correct or update data if there are errors or omissions, and the right to access and obtain copies of their stored personal data. Additionally, customers have the right to terminate, delete, or erase their personal data if it is no longer needed or if they withdraw their previously given consent.
