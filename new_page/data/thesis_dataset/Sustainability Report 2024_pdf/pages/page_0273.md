# Comprehensive Internal Control System

## Risk Control System

To implement effective risk management, BTN equips itself with a comprehensive internal control system. This system is designed to safeguard the Bank's assets, ensure the availability of reliable financial and managerial reports, enhance compliance with regulations and legal requirements, and minimize risk of loss, as well as deviations to and breaches of prudential principles.

BTN's risk control system adheres to established policies and procedures. The risk control process is tailored to the Bank's risk exposure and the level of risk to be undertaken, based on the predetermined risk tolerance. Risk control is conducted through various mechanisms, such as hedging, issuing guarantees, asset securitization, credit derivatives, and capital augmentation to absorb potential losses.

The implementation of a reliable and effective internal control system is a shared responsibility among all operational work units, supporting units, and the Internal Audit Division. ## Risk Profile Assessment [GRI 2-16]

To provide a comprehensive and sustainable overview of risk, BTN regularly conducts self-assessments of the Bank Soundness Level (TKB) and prepares Risk Profile Reports. TKB assessments, which refer to POJK No. 4/POJK.03/2016 and SE OJK No. 14/SEOJK.03/2017 on Bank Soundness Level Assessments, are conducted semiannually. Meanwhile, the Risk Profile Report is prepared quarterly. TKB assessments encompass several aspects, including the risk profile (consisting of inherent risks and the quality of risk management implementation), profitability, governance, and capital.
