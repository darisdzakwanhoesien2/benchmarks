# Financial Product Complaints Handling

[ORI 413-2] [OJK F.24]

![img-222.jpeg](img-222.jpeg)

BTN recognizes the importance of transparent and open communication in building trust with all stakeholders. Therefore, the Bank prioritizes accessible, responsive, and effective communication channels, allowing stakeholders, particularly local communities, to submit feedback, suggestions, or complaints related to the Bank's operations. These complaint channels serve not only as a means for stakeholders to express concerns or dissatisfaction but also as a dialogue platform enabling the Bank to respond to issues quickly and collaboratively, seeking the best solutions for all parties. [F.24]

Through a consistent commitment to these efforts, BTN aims to foster positive relationships with stakeholders while ensuring that every perspective is acknowledged and addressed appropriately. BTN also ensures that every operational decision's impact is well-managed to avoid significant issues or complaints. During the reporting period, BTN did not receive any complaints related to operational activities or negative impacts caused, demonstrating the Bank's commitment to maintaining effective and responsive communication.
