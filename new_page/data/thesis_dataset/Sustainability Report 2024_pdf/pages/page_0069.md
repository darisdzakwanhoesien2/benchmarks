# Supply Chain [GRI 2-6] [OJK 8.1]

One of the key factors in achieving the success and sustainability of the Bank is the involvement and support of all stakeholders, including suppliers, who ensure the availability of resources, goods, and services supporting operations. Supplier involvement includes providing IT infrastructure, office supplies, furniture, souvenirs, and consulting services. BTN has implemented an integrated electronic procurement system (e-Procurement) to manage the procurement of goods and services. This system is part of the Company's effort to reduce greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions, particularly by minimizing paper usage in daily operations.

Through the Special Policy on Guidelines for Procurement of Goods and/or Services KK.9-H, BTN consistently supports strengthening the local and national economy by prioritizing domestic products while complying with applicable regulations. The Bank collaborates with local suppliers geographically based within Indonesia. If the required goods and services cannot be sourced from local suppliers or due to specific considerations, BTN opts for foreign suppliers based abroad. The Bank's commitment to prioritizing local suppliers aligns with the Circular Letter of the Ministry of SOEs No. SE-10/MBU/08/2020, dated 26 August 2020, on enhancing the role of Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) in procuring goods and services in SOEs. This commitment also aligns with the Regulation on Procurement of Goods and Services in SOEs No. PER-2/MBU/3/2023, regarding Governance and Significant Corporate Activities in SOEs. [GRI 3-3]

For outsourcing arrangements, BTN adheres to Financial Services Authority Regulation (POJK) Number 9/POJK.03/2016 on Prudential Principles for Commercial Banks in Delegating Part of Their Operations to Third Parties, ensuring that core activities are not outsourced. In 2024, the Bank executed 707 procurement transactions involving 939 suppliers, an increase from 452 suppliers in 2023. All 939 registered suppliers in 2024 were local suppliers from Indonesia.
