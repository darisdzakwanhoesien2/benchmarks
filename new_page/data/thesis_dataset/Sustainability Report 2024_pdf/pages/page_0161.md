# UMKM BerKRIYAsi Bazaar

BTN supports the development of MSMEs through various initiatives, including financing and promotion. One of these initiatives is participation in the UMKM BUMN BerKRIYAsi Bazaar held at Sarinah, Jakarta, from October 17-20, 2024. This event, initiated by the Ministry of State-Owned Enterprises, aims to empower MSMEs and encourage national economic growth. The bazaar showcases a variety of innovative products from MSMEs, including batik, accessories, recycled plastic woven items, and furniture.

Through this event, BTN reaffirmed its commitment to supporting
