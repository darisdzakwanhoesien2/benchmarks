# Responsible Debt Collection Policy

As part of BTN's commitment to ensuring responsible and sustainable banking practices, BTN has developed and implemented the Standalone Debt Collection Policy, which includes clear and transparent debt collection procedures. This policy is designed to protect consumer rights while supporting business continuity by upholding high standards of Sustainable Governance (LST). Every employee or authorized party representing the bank in debt collection is required to adhere to high ethical standards and respect debtor rights, ensuring that the collection process is conducted professionally and in compliance with applicable regulations.

The Standalone Debt Collection Policy refers to the Consumer Collection Recovery and Asset Sales Policy, which covers the management of non-performing loans and debt collection procedures. This policy sets out specific steps for managing and collecting debts, including early notification to debtors, transparent communication arrangements, and a structured escalation mechanism. All these steps are designed to ensure that the collection process is conducted professionally, respects debtor privacy and conditions, and remains accessible to all relevant stakeholders.

BTN also provides comprehensive training for employees regarding this policy to enhance their understanding, including specialized training programs such as Leading Collection with Negotiation Skills and Optimizing Collection with Negotiation. The training is designed to develop effective communication skills, conflict management, and guidance on fair treatment of customers. This policy also emphasizes the importance of a constructive communication approach in debt collection, focusing on providing solutions to customers facing financial difficulties, such as restructuring or refinancing options. Through this policy, BTN is committed to ensuring that debt collection is not only effective but also fair, inclusive, and sustainable. Oversight of policy implementation is carried out by the CSRD Division, which is responsible for ensuring that all debt collection activities are conducted in accordance with the principles established in the policy.

# Loan Modification Options

As part of BTN's commitment to consumer protection, the Bank offers loan modification options for customers experiencing financial difficulties. This restructuring program includes adjustments to the payment schedule, temporary interest rate reductions, or principal installment deferment, aiming to provide a fair solution for both parties.

The restructuring process begins with a discussion between the debtor and the account manager. Debtors can also access information regarding loan restructuring through OJK (Indonesia's Financial Services Authority). Once approved, the debtor will receive a Loan Restructuring Agreement Letter outlining the scheme, terms, and applicable conditions.

BTN's restructuring options refer to POJK No. 40 of 2019, which includes:

1. Adjustment of Payment Schedule
2. Temporary Reduction of Loan Interest Rates
3. Deferral of Principal Installments

The application process begins with a debtor's request, which is then reviewed by the Bank's risk management team. Once approved, the debtor will receive an agreement letter detailing the agreed upon scheme, providing extended repayment flexibility and reducing credit risk.
