# Energy

![img-191.jpeg](img-191.jpeg)

The Bank establishes credit policies that require customers in this sector to:

A. Credit financing in the Energy sector is limited only to companies with good ESG implementation.
B. Hold a PROPER rating and/or at least have an Environmental Impact Assessment (AMDAL) report or Environmental Management and Monitoring Effort Report (UKL-UPL) approved by the Environmental Agency (BLH).
C. For financing new coal-fired power plant (PLTU) projects with PLN as the offtaker, the suitability of the financing period must be assessed in line

with the coal phase-out strategy according to the valid PLN Electricity Supply Business Plan (RUPTL).

D. For operational power plants, companies must have Environmental Management System certifications such as ISO 14001/ISO 45001 or other equivalent documents recognized by the Bank.
E. For operational power plants, companies must have a code of conduct, policies related to environmental management (carbon emissions, coal ash, water and waste management) and labor standards.
F. Have no social/environmental conflicts.

# Coal*

![img-193.jpeg](img-193.jpeg)

The Bank establishes credit policies that require customers in this sector to:

A. Credit financing in the Coal sector is limited only to Top-Tier Corporations with good ESG implementation.
B. Hold a minimum PROPER Green rating and/or at least have an Environmental Impact Assessment (AMDAL) report or Environmental Management and Monitoring Effort Report (UKL-UPL) (including reclamation and post-mining guarantees) approved by the Environmental Agency (BLH).
C. Hold Environmental Management System certifications such as ISO 14001/ISO 45001 or other

equivalent documents recognized by the Bank.

D. For financing in the coal mining sector as a supplier for PLTU, the suitability of the financing period must be assessed in line with the coal phase-out strategy according to the valid PLN Electricity Supply Business Plan (RUPTL).
E. Have a roadmap for reducing Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Emissions.
F. Borrowers must comply with loan agreement clauses, including adherence to all applicable environmental regulations and required documentation. Failure to comply will impact loan sustainability.

* Solely as a reference for the Bank, and currently, the Bank has no financing in this sector.
