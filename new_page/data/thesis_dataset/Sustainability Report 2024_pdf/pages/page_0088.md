BTN calculates emissions using an operational control approach based on the methodology of the IPCC Guidelines 2006, updated in 2019, and referring to the ISO 14064 Greenhouse Gases standard. Regarding electricity emissions, BTN refers to factors issued by the Directorate General of Electricity, Ministry of Energy and

Mineral Resources (DJK-ESDM) in 2020. Meanwhile, for fuel emissis, BTN uses factors issued by the Agency for Oil and Gas (Lemigas) under the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources in 2021. Emission calculations include  $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$  gas multiplied by a GWP factor of 1 and exclude biogenic  $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$  emissions. [GRI 305-1, 305-2, 305-3] [OJK F.II] [TCFD Metrics.b]

Information on GHG emissions produced is detailed as follows: [GRI 305-1, 305-2, 305-3] [OJK F.II] [TCFD Metrics.b]
