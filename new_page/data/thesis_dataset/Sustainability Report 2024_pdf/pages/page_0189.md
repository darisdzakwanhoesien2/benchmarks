# Sustainability Linked Loan (SLL)

As part of its efforts to strengthen its commitment to sustainability and support the ESG roadmap, BTN has initiated the development of policies related to the Sustainability Linked Loan (SLL) scheme, to support BTN's ESG goals, which align with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and the transition toward a low-carbon economy. The SLL scheme provides incentives for companies to achieve their sustainability targets, while ensuring that projects financed by BTN generate positive social and environmental impacts.

Incentives are granted to borrowers upon meeting the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and Sustainability Performance Targets (SPTs) agreed upon in the Loan Agreement (PK). The types of incentives provided by BTN include:

1. Pricing Incentive

Pricing incentives are offered to borrowers through an interest rate reduction mechanism, with a specific percentage determined by the Bank's calculations. These incentives are granted through two mechanisms:

- Pricing Incentive - Step Up: An incentive provided at the beginning of the SLL facility period. If the borrower fails to meet the KPIs and SPTs, the incentive will be revoked, and the borrower will be subject to an increased loan interest rate until the end of the loan term.
- Pricing Incentive - Step Down: A tiered incentive structure, where borrowers receive progressive interest rate reductions upon achieving the KPIs and SPTs targets.

2. All in Fee Incentive

This incentive is provided at the beginning of the SLL facility period in the form of a reduction in fees, calculated as a percentage determined by the Bank. However, if the KPIs and SPTs are not met, the Incentives may be revoked, and the debtor will be required to pay the difference in the All-in Fee that was previously reduced if KPIs and SPTs are not achieved.

# KPR Subsidies - An Outstanding Social Product

BTN is committed to supporting the government's efforts to improve public access to banking and financial services by providing inclusive and sustainable financial products. A concrete example of this commitment is the development of the Subsidized Home Ownership Loan (KPR Subsidized), specifically designed for low-income households (MBR) with low-interest rates and affordable financing. BTN also runs financial literacy and inclusion programs, aligned with recommendations from Bank Indonesia and the Financial Services Authority (OJK), to enhance public understanding of sustainable financial planning. By prioritizing sustainability principles, BTN not only expands access to financial services but also contributes to achieving the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) in Indonesia.

As part of its efforts to support sustainable housing development, BTN continues to develop ESG-based financial products focused on green housing financing. These products include financing for energy-efficient and green building-certified homes, ensuring comfort for residents while maintaining environmental sustainability. BTN also ensures that environmental, social, and governance (ESG) aspects are integrated into every housing loan (KPR) product, fostering a more sustainable housing ecosystem.
