# Governance Principles

BTN recognizes that business sustainability is not solely measured by financial performance or profitability growth but also through good internal management, including adherence to GCG principles. By prioritizing GCG principles, the Bank aims to create a comprehensive balance in business governance. This encompasses the interests of business and society, both at the group and individual levels, internally and externally, in the short and long term, to provide optimal benefits for shareholders and other stakeholders.

The effective application of GCG principles is key to ensuring the Bank's resilience in facing the increasingly complex dynamics of competition. For this reason, BTN continues to adopt and adjust the best GCG practices at national, regional, and international levels to ensure relevance and effectiveness in meeting the Bank's needs and industry challenges.

BTN's objectives in implementing GCG principles include:

1. Supporting BTN's vision of "Becoming the Best Mortgage Bank in Southeast Asia by 2025."
2. Supporting BTN's mission, namely:
a. Actively supporting the government in promoting the welfare of Indonesian society through home ownership.
b. Realizing the dreams of millions of Indonesians by providing access to decent housing.
c. Becoming "The Home of Indonesia's Best Talent."
d. Enhancing shareholder value by focusing on profitability growth and sustainability as a blue-chip company with robust risk management principles.
e. Becoming a financial partner for stakeholders in the housing ecosystem by providing integrated solutions and superior services through digital innovation.
3. Providing benefits and added value for shareholders and stakeholders.
4. Maintaining and enhancing the sustainability of a healthy and competitive business over the long term.
5. Increasing investor trust in BTN.

In conducting business and operational activities, BTN consistently applies the principles of Good Corporate

Governance (GCG) at every level of the organization. The implementation of GCG is based on POJK Number 17 of 2023 concerning the Implementation of Governance for Commercial Banks as the primary guideline. The key corporate governance principles applied by BTN include:

1. Transparency
Transparency refers to openness in the decision-making process and the availability of relevant information regarding the Bank to stakeholders.

2. Accountability
Accountability entails clarity in the roles and responsibilities of the Bank's organs, ensuring BTN's performance operates transparently, fairly, effectively, and efficiently.

3. Responsibility
Responsibility is the alignment of the Bank's management with legal and regulatory requirements in the financing sector, as well as with ethical values, standards, principles, and healthy business practices.

4. Independence
Independence represents the state of the Bank being managed independently and professionally, free from conflicts of interest or undue influence and pressure from any party, in compliance with legal and regulatory provisions in the financing sector and ethical values, standards, principles, and healthy business practices.

5. Fairness
Fairness refers to equity, balance, and justice in fulfilling the rights of stakeholders based on agreements, legal and regulatory provisions, and ethical values, standards, principles, and healthy business practices.

GCG serves as a fundamental guideline for improving the Bank's performance and accountability, aiming to create long-term value for shareholders while considering the interests of all stakeholders. Its implementation is based on applicable laws and ethical business practices. As risk complexities increase, the need for good corporate governance becomes essential to ensure business sustainability and resilience.
