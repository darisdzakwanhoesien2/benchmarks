# Collaboration for Mangrove Planting

![img-123.jpeg](img-123.jpeg)

BTN's concern for environmental management is not only demonstrated through internal Bank programs but is also encouraged through efforts to preserve the surrounding environment. One of the proactive steps taken by the Bank is mangrove planting. In 2024, BTN collaborated with the Financial Transaction Reports and Analysis Center (PPATK) to plant 3,000 mangroves in Bali. The Bank supports nature conservation by releasing 1,000 sea turtle hatchlings back into their natural habitat. In addition to contributing to the reduction of climate change impacts, this initiative reflects the Bank's role in biodiversity conservation efforts. The tree planting and hatchling release programs also support the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), particularly Goal 13: Climate Action and Goal 15: Life on Land.
