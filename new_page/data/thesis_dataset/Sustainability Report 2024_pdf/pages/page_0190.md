Throughout 2024, BTN successfully disbursed subsidized housing loans for 118,665 units, with a total value reaching Rp19.4 trillion. Among these, 102,849 units worth Rp16.8 trillion were allocated to the millennial segment, reflecting the enthusiasm of younger generations in homeownership. BTN also expanded its loan coverage to underserved and informal sectors, with 15,192 units worth Rp2.5 trillion disbursed to street vendors, ride-hailing drivers, and workers with irregular incomes.

In terms of gender, loan disbursement for female homebuyers reached 42,114 subsidized KPR units worth Rp6.9 trillion, while for male homebuyers, it reached 76,551 units worth Rp12.5 trillion. BTN's commitment to providing affordable housing access for diverse communities reflects its efforts to promote financial inclusion and equitable housing development across all social groups in Indonesia.
