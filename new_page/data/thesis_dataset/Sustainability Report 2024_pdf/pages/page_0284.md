If a member of the Board of Directors has an interest in a transaction or contract involving the Bank, they are required to disclose this interest during a Board of Directors meeting and are not permitted to vote in the related decision‐making process. If faced with a potential conflict of interest, where the Bank's economic interests do not align with personal economic interests, the member of the Board of Directors must: Prioritize the Bank's interests and ensure that conflicts of interest do not compromise its profitability.Refrain from participating in decision‐making processes when a conflict of interest exists.Disclose the conflict of interest in cases where a decision must still be made under such circumstances.In the case of credit issuance to a member of the Board of Directors, it must be granted on a fair and reasonable basis in accordance with the Bank's applicable regulations, without any preferential treatment compared to other customers, following the arm's length principle.Report any transactions involving the Bank's shares within three (3) business days.

To maintain independence and accountability in management, the Board of Commissioners is not permitted to engage in decision‐making related to the Bank's operational activities, except in cases involving fund provisions for related parties or other matters as stipulated in the Bank's Articles of Association and applicable regulations. The involvement or approval of the Board of Commissioners in such cases does not eliminate the Board of Directors' responsibility for managing the Bank. Additionally, the Board of Commissioners is prohibited from participating in decisions that could create conflicts of interest or violate applicable laws and ethical standards. Therefore, members of the Board of Commissioners must always avoid potential conflicts of interest by adhering to the following principles: Prioritizing the Bank's interests and ensuring that conflicts of interest do not reduce the Bank's profitability.Disclosing share ownership, either personal or by family members, if it reaches five percent or more in the Bank or any other company, whether domestically or internationally.Disclosing family, financial, managerial, or ownership relationships with other members of the Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, controlling shareholders of the Bank, and/or other related parties in the Bank's business activities.Disclosing any concurrent positions held in other entities.Disclosing conflicts of interest when a decision must still be made under such circumstances. The concerned member of the Board of Commissioners must refrain from participating in any decision‐making process related to the matter.Refraining from requesting, accepting, authorizing, or approving any form of compensation, commission, additional payments, services, money, valuable goods, or any other items of economic value for personal, family, or third‐party benefit in relation to the Bank's business activities.

BTN meticulously documents all disclosures made in accordance with applicable regulations to ensure transparency and compliance. Every disclosure related to a conflict of interest is recorded in meeting minutes, which explicitly include key information, such as the identity of the individuals involved, the source of the conflict, the reasons for the conflict, and the considerations underlying decisions related to it. Essentially, all individuals in the Bank are encouraged to avoid situations in which their decisions could be influenced by conflicts of interest, ensuring that every action taken remains objective and focused on the best interests of the Bank. Breachesrelated to conflicts of interest can be reported through the Bank's Whistleblowing System (WBS), which allows individuals to report allegations of misconduct either anonymously or openly. As part of its efforts to reduce the potential for conflicts of interest, all BTN employees are required to sign an Integrity Pact, affirming their commitment to adhering to the values outlined in the Bank's Code of Conduct. Disclosures related to conflicts of interest, including ownership structures in various business entities, financial ties with affiliated parties, and other affiliations, are disclosed in detail in BTN's Annual Report.
