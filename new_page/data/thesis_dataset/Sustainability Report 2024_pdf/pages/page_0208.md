To mitigate the risk of data breaches, BTN has developed a highly secure internal storage system, with stringent controls to ensure the protection of customer information. Additionally, BTN not only relies on security systems to protect data but is also committed to ensuring that customer data will not be used for any purpose beyond the explicit consent of the customer or in compliance with applicable laws. [OJK F.27]

To ensure customer data security, BTN has established an Operational and Digital Risk Management Unit focused on managing operational, information technology (IT) and digital risks. This Unit ensures that all technology services provided by the Bank are secure and reliable by managing risk for each technological solution developed by the Bank.

BTN's commitment to customer privacy and data security also reflects compliance with the following regulations: [GRI 41B-1]

1. Law No. 10 of 1998 on Amendments to Law No. 7 of 1992 concerning Banking;
2. Financial Services Authority (OJK) Regulation No. 1/POJK07/2013 on Consumer Protection in the Financial Services Sector;
3. OJK Circular Letter No. 14/SEOJK/2014 on Confidentiality and Security of Consumer Data and/or Personal Information;

4. Financial Services Authority Regulation (POJK) Number 44 of 2024 on Bank Secrecy;
5. Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection;
6. Special Policy No. KK.9-M on Data Governance Guidelines; and
7. Special Policy No. KK.9-Z on Customer and Community Protection.

In line with these regulations, BTN has issued Special Policy on Information Technology Security No. KK5-B and Special Policy on Personal Data Protection No.6-K. This policy regulates various aspects to ensure that all information regarding personal data and customer data is maintained in accordance with the principle of confidentiality, integrity, and availability, supporting business success. Through this policy, the Bank aims to enhance necessary data protection efforts to ensure smooth operations and create safer, more organized, well-managed, and auditable activities to uphold the company's reputation. [SASB FN-CB-230a.2]

The Bank also identifies data security risks through:

1. Policies in Record Security Management;
2. Retention of sensitive information;
3. Identification of critical records;
4. Storage of critical records;
5. Storage of business source documents;
6. Storage of application transaction data;
7. Destruction of information;
8. Storage of sensitive information; and
9. Storage of security violations and issue information.

# Responsibilities and Oversight of Data Privacy and Security

To ensure effective information security management, BTN has established a dedicated organizational structure with clear roles, responsibilities, and authorities, while implementing a dual control system to separate duties and accountability in IT operations.

The Information Security Organization consists of:

1. Board of Commissioners.
2. Information Technology Steering Committee (KPTI).
3. Director in Charge of Information Technology.
4. Information Security Manager (ISM).

5. Information Asset Owner (IAO).
6. Information Asset Custodian (IAC).
7. Head of Working Unit.
8. Information Security Management System (ISMS) Team.
9. Cyber Security Incident Response Team (CSIRT).
10. Compliance Working Unit.
11. Operational &amp; Digital Risk Management Working Unit.
12. Employees.
13. Third Parties.
