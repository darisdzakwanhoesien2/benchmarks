# Financial Protection Training

As an additional measure for customer financial protection, the Bank has undertaken various initiatives, including training users on IT security, installing authorized and regularly updated software, and implementing password management or standardization to ensure that protected data remains secure. Additionally, the Bank conducts regular evaluations to ensure that its data security architecture is highly effective in protecting customer data. The effectiveness of these efforts are evidenced by the absence of any customer data security complaints, such as loss, theft, or unauthorized disclosure, throughout 2024. 1. Top Management Communication

One of the strategies to foster a security culture among all employees across the Bank's business units is the implementation of the IT Security Index Program, which monitors the effectiveness

of security awareness initiatives in mitigating cybersecurity and information security risks. This program is delivered monthly by Top Management, emphasizing that information security is a critical aspect of maintaining customer trust and Bank's reputation.
