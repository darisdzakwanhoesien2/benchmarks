The Sustainable Finance Action Plan (SFAP) provides strategic guidance for BTN in developing financing portfolios for sustainable business activities, as well as the changes the Bank will make in business processes and risk management. The changes achieved are relevant to integrating ESG risk management into the Bank's business activities.

BTN has implemented several 2024 SFAP programs, as follows:
