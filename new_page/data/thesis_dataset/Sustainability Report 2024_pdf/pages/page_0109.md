This reflects Bank BTN's commitment to enhancing diversity and inclusivity in leadership. To support women's empowerment, Bank BTN has proposed 7 female candidates as BUMN Nominated Talent to the Ministry of State-Owned Enterprises, representing 28% of the total 25 Nominated Talent. This initiative underscores Bank BTN's commitment to continuously encouraging female participation in strategic positions within BUMN, fostering greater diversity, and supporting corporate sustainability.
