To provide a comprehensive and continuous overview of Bank health, BTN regularly conducts self-assessments on the Bank Soundness Level (TKB) and prepares the Risk Profile Report. Assessments of the TKB, which refer to POJK No. 4/POJK.03/2016 and SE OJK No. 14/SEOJK.03/2017 on Bank Soundness Level Assessments, are conducted semiannually. Meanwhile, the Risk Profile Report is prepared quarterly. The TKB assessment encompasses several aspects, including the risk profile (consisting of inherent risks and the quality of risk management implementation), profitability, governance, and capital.

Risk profile assessments include various types of risks, such as credit risk, market risk, liquidity risk, operational risk, legal risk, strategic risk, compliance risk, reputation

risk, and ESG risk. For the Syariah Business Unit, two additional types of risks are assessed: investment return risk and investment risk. This evaluation involves assessing both inherent risks and the quality of risk management implementation in the Bank's operational activities.

In general, based on the self-assessment of the Q4 2024 Risk Profile, BTN aims to achieve a Low to Moderate level, indicating that the composite risk faced by the Bank is relatively low, with a satisfactory quality of risk management implementation. Below are the details of the assessment results:
