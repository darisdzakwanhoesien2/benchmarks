# BTN recognizes that its presence within society must contribute to the well-being of the surrounding communities.

To this end, the Bank actively participates in developing and empowering communities to address various social, economic, and environmental challenges. This initiative underscores BTN's commitment to managing its impact on individuals, communities, and society at large. The Bank's commitment is reflected in the inclusion of people and community as a key component in its ESG Roadmap, serving as a guide to ensure that its business activities are not only financially profitable but also create a positive social impact. This approach aligns with global trends that increasingly emphasize the importance of sustainability and Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) in business operations.

BTN has developed various initiatives that align with sustainable development goals. This commitment is in accordance with Minister of State-Owned Enterprises Regulation Number PER-1/MBU/03/2023, which mandates special assignments and CSR programs for state-owned enterprises. Through this initiative, BTN consistently strengthens its social and environmental responsibility efforts, integrating the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) into the implementation of its CSR programs. These initiatives aim to support poverty reduction, quality education, gender equality, and environmental protection.
