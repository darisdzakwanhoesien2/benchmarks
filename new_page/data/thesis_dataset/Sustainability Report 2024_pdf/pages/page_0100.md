# Green Building Certification for Menara 2 BTN

In November 2024, BTN achieved Green Building certification for environmentally friendly buildings. This certification was awarded by the Green Building Council Indonesia (GBCI), an official member of the World Building Council. This milestone serves as concrete proof of the Bank's commitment to creating environmentally friendly operations and supporting the implementation of ESG principles across BTN's business lines.

Menara 2 BTN achieved the highest Platinum rating after undergoing a rigorous assessment on sustainability aspects, including energy efficiency, water management, the use of eco-friendly materials, air quality, and indoor comfort. BTN's efforts include increasing green areas by 32.1%, achieving energy savings of 21.86% with an Electricity Consumption Intensity (IKE) of 234.41 kWh per meter squared per year, water savings of

26.7% through clean water usage of 36.65 liters per person per day, the use of sustainable materials, implementation of Outdoor Air Introduction, and various other initiatives supporting sustainability principles.

The Platinum certification positions Menara 2 BTN as a role model for environmentally friendly buildings with optimal air quality, lighting, and comfort, which enhance employee productivity. This achievement aligns with BTN's commitment to becoming an ESG Champion in Indonesia's banking industry, as outlined in the BTN ESG Roadmap 2023-2028.
