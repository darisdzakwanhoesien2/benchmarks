# Stakeholder Engagement [GRI 2-29] [OJK E.4]

BTN defines stakeholders as entities or individuals affected by the Bank's activities, products, and services. Conversely, stakeholders also influence the Bank's ability to successfully implement strategies and achieve its objectives. Therefore, BTN actively involves stakeholders in the development of sustainability agendas, considering the Bank's social responsibilities, environmental concerns, and prioritizing effective communication with various strategic partners.

BTN firmly believes that close collaboration with stakeholders is crucial to creating sustainable positive impacts. The stakeholder engagement process is an integral part of the Bank's efforts to meet the needs of each stakeholder group, utilizing available resources responsibly, accountably, and in compliance with applicable regulations.

To identify stakeholder groups, BTN has established specific criteria to ensure constructive and mutually supportive involvement in achieving sustainability goals. These criteria include:

1.  Influence
Stakeholders exert influence over BTN's decisions related to business sustainability.

2.  Dependence
BTN depends on these stakeholders, either directly or indirectly, for its operations and initiatives.

3.  Needs
Stakeholders understand the needs, expectations, and aspirations of various groups related to the Bank's activities, products, or services.

4.  Diversity
Stakeholders possess differing characteristics, backgrounds, and interests that must be understood and respected to ensure inclusive policies and decisions that fairly address the needs of all parties.

5.  Willingness to Participate
Stakeholders demonstrate a willingness to participate in the Bank's decision-making processes and initiatives to achieve mutually beneficial shared goals.
