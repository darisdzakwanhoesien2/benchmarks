# Vision, Mission, and Culture

[ORI 2-23] [OJK C.I]

## Vision

"To Become the Best Mortgage Bank in Southeast Asia by 2025"

## Mission

- Actively supporting the government in advancing the welfare of Indonesian society through homeownership.
- Realizing the dreams of millions of Indonesians by providing proper housing.
- Becoming the Home of Indonesia's Best Talent.
- Enhancing shareholder value by focusing on sustainable profitability growth as a blue chip company with robust risk management principles.
- Becoming a financial partner for stakeholders in the housing ecosystem by providing comprehensive solutions and excellent services through digital innovation.

# Vision and Mission Review by the Board of Commissioners and Directors

The Bank periodically reviews its vision and mission to ensure alignment with the Long-Term Corporate Plan (RJPP) and evolving changes. The latest review was conducted on 27 September 2024 by the Board of Commissioners and Directors. The review confirmed that the new vision and mission formulation align with the internal and external changes the Bank faces. These changes have been integrated into the 2025-2029 BTN RJPP and included in the 2025 Sustainable Finance Action Plan (RAKB).

A significant difference from the previous vision and mission lies in the Bank's focus, which now extends beyond mortgage financing to include the development of a comprehensive banking ecosystem. In this context, BTN has set a new vision for 2025: "The Leading Partner in Financially Empowering Indonesian Families."
