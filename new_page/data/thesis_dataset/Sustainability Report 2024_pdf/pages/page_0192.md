# Electric Vehicle Credit Disbursement

BTN has provided electric vehicle loans to facilitate public access to eco-friendly transportation with various benefits, including affordable installments, unconditional discounts, 0% down payment (DP), flexible loan terms, and waived provisioning and administrative fees. Throughout 2024, BTN financed 52 electric vehicle units, with a total credit value of Rp877,412,000. This initiative is part of BTN's strategy to support the transition to sustainable mobility and reduce the carbon footprint in the transportation sector.

Additionally, BTN ensures that electric vehicle credit financing aligns with ESG principles, promoting green technology and encouraging the adoption of cleaner and more efficient transportation solutions. This product not only improves public access to electric vehicles but also contributes to long-term sustainability by accelerating the shift to clean energy-based transportation in Indonesia.

# Green Initiative Program

BTN is committed to supporting environmental conservation through various initiatives involving active customer participation. One of the flagship programs is "Flash Sale balé Poin Peduli Lingkungan", where customers can exchange their balé points for mangrove tree planting contributions in Mangunharjo, Semarang. This program offers customers the choice to redeem points for various rewards, including the opportunity to participate in environmental activities through mangrove tree planting. By the end of 2024, the program successfully collected a total of 775 mangrove trees from balé point redemptions by customers. BTN targets to reach 1,000 mangrove trees by March 2025 as part of its ongoing sustainability efforts to support coastal ecosystems and mitigate climate change impacts.

Additionally, BTN has introduced the "Tabungan BTN Siap! Dapat Pohon" program, wherein every opening of a Tabungan BTN Siap! account with a minimum deposit of Rp105,000 and a tenure of at least 12 months will contribute to the planting of one mangrove tree.
