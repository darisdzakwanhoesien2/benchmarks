# Data Protection for Vendors and Business Partners

As part of the Bank's commitment to data protection, BTN ensures that the level of data protection implemented also applies to suppliers and business partners who handle the Bank's data or have access to its network. To safeguard data security and privacy, BTN has established measures to ensure that business partners and suppliers comply with information security standards by implementing the following key actions:

1. The Bank manages information security and cybersecurity risks throughout the service provision process by suppliers and business partners, applying comprehensive processes and procedures to ensure security, integrity, and confidentiality of information during IT solution implementation. Suppliers and business partners are also required to comply with information security requirements as stipulated in the cooperation agreement or Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA).
2. The Bank ensures that any amendments to cooperation agreements with suppliers and business partners must be approved by both parties in accordance with the applicable change procedures. Identification and review of information security requirements must be conducted for any changes in the agreed scope of work.
3. The Bank conducts supervision and inspections through regular monitoring of provided services, ensuring that suppliers and business partners submit regular reports on service delivery and remain available to support and provide necessary information for internal audit processes.
4. Upon termination of cooperation, the Bank also implements an exit strategy to ensure that data remains protected even after the relationship with the supplier or business partner has ended.

The regulation of IT security aspects has also been implemented in Special Policy (KK) 5-B on Information Technology Security, ensuring that Third Parties comply with security procedures in accordance with BTN's internal policies.
