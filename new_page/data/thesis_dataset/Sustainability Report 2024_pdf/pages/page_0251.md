The ESG assessment process is conducted according to the Four Eyes Principle, which involves a joint analysis at each stage of the credit application process. These stages include:

1. Initial Analysis under the Four Eyes Principle
The Four Eyes Principle ensures that credit analysis, including ESG considerations, is conducted by two independent units: the business unit and the risk unit. The business unit evaluates prospective borrowers and collects supporting documentation, while the risk unit assesses potential credit risk considering ESG aspects and provides mitigation recommendations if necessary.

2. Credit Proposal Preparation
After the ESG analysis is conducted by both units, the results are integrated into a credit proposal document. This document covers credit risk assessment that considers ESG aspects, mitigation recommendations, and a conclusion on the borrower's creditworthiness.

3. Pre-Credit Committee Meeting (Pre CCM)
During this phase, both the business and risk units present the results of the credit analysis that also considers ESG aspects. The compliance and legal units provide regulatory and legal input.

4. Credit Committee Meeting (CCM)
The CCM is the final decision-making forum for credit proposals. It is where the credit analysis that also considers ESG aspects as agreed in the Pre-CCM, is presented. Decisions are made by consensus, and in the absence of consensus, by majority vote. The results of the decision are documented in the meeting minutes and serve as the basis for issuing the Letter of Credit Approval (SP2K).

BTN also identifies ESG risk triggers, such as situations or indicators that require further evaluation before financing decisions are made. If material risks are identified, such as significant environmental impacts, the case will be escalated to the ESG Committee for the development of appropriate mitigation measures. Once these measures are formulated, they are presented to the Board of Directors for approval and necessary follow-up.

This escalation process ensures that significant ESG risks are managed carefully and responsibly, in line with BTN's commitment to sustainability and good governance principles.

# Adequacy of Risk Management Policies and Procedures and Risk Limit Setting

## Risk Management Policies and Procedures and Risk Limit Setting

To mitigate the negative impact of various risks, BTN has developed risk management policy guidelines designed to anticipate potential impacts from various risks. As part of compliance with Financial Services Authority (OJK) Regulation No. 18/POJK.03/2016 concerning Risk Management Implementation for Commercial Banks, BTN applies effective risk management in handling various types of risks. To meet regulatory requirements, BTN has drafted formal risk management policies documented and reviewed at least annually to ensure effectiveness. This review process aims to ensure all relevant parties involved in risk management, including risk owners and risk managers, understand and apply risk management policies effectively.

As part of efforts to support effective risk management, the Bank has also implemented internal controls by transforming the policy architecture and governance procedures. This restructuring is aimed at ensuring the policies and procedures adopted are aligned with operational needs and prevailing regulations. The policy structure is designed hierarchically, encompassing three levels: general policies, specific policies, and technical regulations. With this structure, the Bank ensures that the policies implemented are not only regulatory-compliant but also support sustainable business operations and sound corporate governance.
