BTN conducts regular reviews of its internal policies to ensure they remain consistent with the latest regulatory requirements. This process includes gap analysis and adjustments to best practices commonly applied in the banking industry, with the goal of improving the quality of risk management practices.

In addition, BTN remains focused on improving its risk management information system. This initiative aims to enhance the quality of the existing risk database, with the expectation that the risk management information system can be continuously developed and gradually implemented. The primary objective of this improvement is to ensure that risk measurement and monitoring processes can be conducted in an integrated manner and presented accurately and promptly.

# Strengthening Risk Management Policies and Procedures

To strengthen risk management policies and procedures, BTN integrates risk management as one of the pillars of BTN's culture, namely governance and risk culture. This pillar plays a strategic role in ensuring that all business processes are carried out prudently and in compliance with applicable standards. The Bank ensures that all BTN

employees adhere to four cultural pillars in their work activities within their respective units. One of the priority pillars is governance and risk culture, which functions to ensure that business implementation is conducted carefully and in full compliance with regulations.
