# Digital Innovation for Modern Services [OJK F.26]

As a leader in financial inclusion in Indonesia, BTN is committed to continuously expanding financial access through strategic, innovative, and technology-driven service channels. As of 2024, BTN has introduced various initiatives to address Indonesia's geographical and demographic challenges, ensuring that all segments of society, particularly those in remote areas, can benefit from broader financial inclusion. Through a technology-driven approach, BTN not only provides access to banking services but also empowers communities to manage their finances more independently.

As part of this commitment, BTN has expanded financial access to more than 20 million Indonesians, including those in underserved segments. Throughout 2024, BTN successfully operated 82 Branch Offices and 536 Conventional Sub-Branch Offices, along with 35 Sharia Branch Offices and 75 Sharia Sub-Branch Offices, serving as financial service points across Indonesia. These financial service points are strategically located and offer personalized, adaptive banking services to meet the needs of customers in both urban and rural areas, including housing financing.

Additionally, BTN provides self-service banking through 1,053 ATMs, 1,018 CRMs, and 1 digital store operating 24/7, enabling customers to conduct transactions at any time without visiting a physical branch. Beyond ATMs and CRMs, BTN has also strengthened its digital

service channels, allowing customers to access banking services without location constraints. The balé by BTN platform, with a user base of 2,220,256, caters to a diverse range of customers. BTN also offers alternative payment channels, including 3,389 EDC merchants and 125,082 QRIS BTN points strategically distributed to facilitate seamless digital and banking transactions. Furthermore, BTN has 5,000 agents across Indonesia, playing a crucial role in extending financial services, particularly in areas previously underserved by traditional banking.

As part of its sustainable financial inclusion strategy, BTN remains committed to continuous technological innovation. One key initiative is the development of an AI-powered mobile banking application, designed to offer a faster, more secure, and more convenient banking experience. This application enables customers to manage their finances more efficiently, from daily transactions to housing loan applications, with a user experience tailored to individual needs.

Through these strategic initiatives, BTN is not only expanding its financial service reach but also ensuring that all societal segments, whether in cities or remote areas, can access the banking services they need. By continuously innovating and leveraging technology, BTN plays an active role in fostering more inclusive and sustainable financial accessibility across Indonesia.
