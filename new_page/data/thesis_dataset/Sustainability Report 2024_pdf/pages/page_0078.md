# Credit Distribution

In 2024, BTN disbursed loans amounting to Rp357.97 trillion, reflecting an increase of Rp39.88 trillion or 12.53% compared to the previous year's figure of Rp318.11 trillion. This achievement fulfilled 97.50% of the 2024 RBB target of Rp367.13 trillion. The performance was primarily driven by housing loans, comprising both Subsidized and Non-Subsidized Mortgages.

The volume of BTN's consumer loan distribution reached 179,539 housing units, marking a decrease of 19,447 units or 9.77% compared to 2023, which recorded 198,986 housing units. This decline was attributed to a reduction in Subsidized Mortgages, Non-Subsidized Mortgages, and Non-Housing Loans, totaling 21,235 units or equivalent to an 11.18% decrease compared to 2023.
