# BTN Drives Economic Growth with Mass KUR and Mortgage Signing Events

PT Bank Tabungan Negara (Persero) Tbk (BTN) successfully held mass mortgage signing events for 4,824 housing units simultaneously across various regions in Indonesia. From January to July 2024, BTN realized 112,000 subsidized mortgage units, including through the Housing Finance Liquidity Facility (FLPP) and Public Housing Savings (Tapera) schemes, out of the national target of 166,000 units. This program highlights BTN's commitment to expanding access to affordable and livable housing, particularly for millennials, who accounted for 89% of subsidized mortgage realization as of June 2024.

BTN also strengthened its support for micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) by disbursing business capital loans totaling Rp2.84 trillion as of June 2024, reflecting a growth of 33.3% compared to the same period the previous year. Of this amount, Rp797.8 billion was allocated for People's Business Loans (KUR), and Rp2.04 trillion for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprise Loans (KUMKM). These loans were targeted at businesses related to the housing ecosystem and leveraged digitalization to simplify access to credit. This strategic initiative underscores BTN's role in fostering economic growth and financial inclusion in Indonesia.
