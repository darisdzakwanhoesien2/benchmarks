# Milestones

## 1897
Founded during Dutch colonial rule as Postpaarbank.

## 1942
Taken over by the Japanese government and renamed Tyokin Kyoku.

## 1945
The Dutch government took over the Kantor Taboengan Pos; employees ceased operations.

## 1946
The Indonesian government reclaimed Tyokin Kyoku and established Kantor Taboengan Pos.

## 1949
Renamed Kantor Tabungan Pos by the Indonesian government.

## 1968
Bank Tunggal was restructured, and Bank Tabungan Negara (BTN) was reestablished.

## 1965
Bank Tunggal was established under the name Bank Negara Indonesia, comprising Bank Tabungan Negara, Bank Indonesia, Bank Koperasi Tani dan Nelayan, Bank Umum Negara, BNI 1946, and Bank Dagang Negara.

## 4 June 1965
Integration of State General Banks and Bank Tabungan Negara into the Central Bank.

## 1963
Renamed as Bank Tabungan Negara and managed under the Minister of Central Bank Affairs.

BTN collaborated with Perum Pos dan Giro to implement the National Savings (Tabanas) and Term Insurance Savings (Taska) programs.

## 1971
### 1974
The Minister of Finance assigned BTN the task of financing public housing.

### 1976
BTN's first mortgage loan (KPR) was made at the Tanah Mas Housing Complex in Semarang City.

### 1986
Obtained a loan from the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (IBRD)/World Bank amounting to USD 266,550,000, signed by the Government of the Republic of Indonesia.

### 1989
Issued bonds for the first time and began general banking activities.

### 1992
Officially became a state-owned limited liability company (PT Persero).

### 1994
Obtained a license to operate as a foreign exchange bank.

## 1950
The Government of the Republic of Indonesia reopened the Post Savings Office under Bank Tabungan Pos Republik Indonesia.

## 1953
Bank Tabungan Pos Republik Indonesia was established as a legal entity under the Ministry of Transportation and authorized to open branch offices.
