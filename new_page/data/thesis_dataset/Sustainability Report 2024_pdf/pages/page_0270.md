# c. Climate Change Mitigation Plan

To mitigate climate change, BTN has designed strategic measures covering GHG reduction, automation of climate risk measurement, and green portfolio policies. In the short term, BTN focuses on aligning climate risk calculations with PSAK standards. The medium-term approach involves monitoring the implementation of decarbonization, while long-term efforts include integrating carbon risk into pricing strategies and underwriting. The total budget for mitigation programs is projected to reach Rp76.02 billion in 2024, focusing on service digitalization, energy efficiency, and corporate social responsibility.

Overall, the Climate Risk Stress Test (CRST) results show that BTN maintains strong financial resilience in facing physical and transition risks. The CAR remains stable despite pressure from these risks. Additionally, the transition plan toward Net Zero Emission offers opportunities to develop green portfolios. However, challenges remain, such as improving debtor data quality, particularly for MSMEs, and strengthening the Bank's human resources and technology capabilities. With the planned mitigation measures, BTN demonstrates a strong commitment to managing climate risks to ensure operational sustainability.

# Risk Measurement and Assessment [GRI 2-16]

BTN uses a risk measurement system to assess risk exposures inherent in business activities, which are then compared to the Bank's risk appetite. This enables BTN to take appropriate mitigation actions and ensure sufficient capital to manage residual risk. Risk measurement is conducted periodically across products, portfolios, and all business activities.

The risk measurement methods employed include standards set by Bank Indonesia and OJK, as well as internal methods developed by BTN. These methods are tailored to the characteristics and complexities of business activities. The risk measurement system is

regularly evaluated and refined as needed to ensure the accuracy of assumptions, fairness, data integrity, and compliance with procedures.

Stress testing is conducted to complement the risk measurement system, aiming to estimate potential losses under adverse market conditions. Through specific scenarios, stress testing measures performance sensitivity to changes in risk factors and identifies significant impacts on portfolios. The results of stress testing are used to review existing policies and limits, enabling the Bank to take appropriate actions should conditions exceed the risk tolerance level.
