# BTN Business Activities [OR 2-6] [OJK C.4]

The scope of the Bank's activities is regulated in Article 3, Paragraph 2 of BTN's Articles of Association, which have undergone several amendments. The latest amendment is in the Deed of Statement of the Resolutions of the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders No. 08, dated 8 April 2021, drawn up before Notary Ashoya Ratam, S.H., M.Kn., in Jakarta. The scope of activities includes general banking activities by applicable laws and regulations, including activities based on Sharia principles.

The Bank's business activities include the following:

1. Collecting funds from the public in the form of deposits such as demand deposits, time deposits, deposit certificates, savings, and/or other equivalent forms.
2. Providing credit.
3. Issuing debt acknowledgment letters.
4. Buying, selling, or guaranteeing certain products on its own risk or for the benefit of and on behalf of its customers, including:
a. Bills of exchange, including those accepted by BTN as a bank, with terms not exceeding customary commercial practices.
b. Debt acknowledgment letters and other commercial papers with terms within customary commercial practices.
c. Treasury notes and government guarantee securities.
d. Bank Indonesia Certificates (SBI).
e. Bonds.
f. Commercial papers with terms as per regulations.
g. Other financial instruments with terms as per regulations.
5. Transferring funds, both for its interests and those of its customers.
6. Placing funds with, borrowing from, or lending funds to other banks using letters, telecommunication media, promissory notes, checks, or other instruments.
7. Receiving payments from securities receivables and setting accounts with third parties.
8. Providing storage facilities for valuables and securities.
9. Acting as a custodian for others based on a contract.
10. Transferring funds from one customer to another in the form of non-listed securities.
11. Providing financing and/or conducting other activities, including those based on Sharia principles stipulated by Bank Indonesia.
12. Engaging in factoring, credit card operations, and trustee services.
13. Conducting foreign exchange activities in compliance with authorized regulations.
14. Investing in shares of banks or other financial companies.
15. Temporary investments are made to resolve loan defaults based on Sharia principles, provided the investments are withdrawn as stipulated by Bank Indonesia.
16. Acting as a founder and manager of pension funds as regulated by law.
17. Providing financial services, commercial banking, and investment banking activities.
18. Conducting business activities based on Sharia principles stipulated by the authorities and regulations.
19. Undertaking other activities commonly performed by banks if they do not violate regulations.

The Bank has implemented all business activities listed in the Articles of Association. In addition to its principal business activities, the Bank engages in supporting activities to strengthen its primary operations, optimize its resources, and adhere to applicable regulations.
