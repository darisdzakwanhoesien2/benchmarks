# Conflict of Interest [ORI 2-15]

BTN acknowledges that interactions between individuals within the organization or with external parties have the potential to create conflicts of interest that could disrupt decision-making processes and implementation. These conflicts of interest undermine the objectivity and integrity of decisions made, thereby potentially harming the Bank and its stakeholders. For this reason, BTN is committed to identifying, managing, and resolving conflicts of interest in a firm and transparent manner. This is crucial to ensure effective business management and the achievement of objectives free from practices that may cause harm, such as corruption, collusion, and nepotism (KKN). Additionally, the Bank strives to build and maintain harmonious relationships with all stakeholders to foster a healthy and sustainable business environment.

Fundamentally, every individual involved in the Bank

must avoid making decisions that could create conflicts of interest that might compromise the objectivity and integrity of those decisions. In situations where a conflict of interest arises, individuals involved are required to disclose the existence. BTN has established policies regarding transactions involving conflicts of interest, which are regulated explicitly in the Code of Conduct and Rules of Procedure for Directors and the Board of Commissioners. If conflicts of interest arise between the Bank and shareholders, members of the Board of Commissioners, Directors, executive officials, or other parties related to BTN, members of the Board of Commissioners, Directors, and executive officials are prohibited from making decisions or actions that could harm or reduce the Bank's profits. Furthermore, it is mandatory to transparently disclose these conflicts of interest and ensure accountability in every decision-making process.

# Conflict of Interest Prevention Policy

Special Policy KK.6-H on Conflict of Interest Management Procedures serves as an umbrella policy regulating mechanisms for managing conflicts of interest within BTN, encompassing Directors, employees, and other related parties. This policy specifically details conflict of interest management within three main areas, namely human resources (HR), procurement processes, and credit. As part of its internal business processes, KK.6-H provides the foundation to ensure that any potential conflicts of interest are handled through consistent mechanisms aligned with Good Corporate Governance principles.

To ensure the effective implementation of this policy, BTN has established provisions that reinforce the integrity and independence of the Board of Directors in carrying out their duties. Each member of the Board of Directors is strictly prohibited from seeking or accepting personal gains, whether directly or indirectly, beyond the remuneration and lawful benefits determined by the General Meeting of Shareholders. They are also forbidden from exploiting the Bank for personal, family, or third-party interests that may harm or diminish the Bank's profitability.
