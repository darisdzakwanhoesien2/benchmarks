As a key enabler, the digitalization strategy aims to minimize manual processing in lending and funding operations, enhance transaction banking capabilities, and expand the sales organization with sharper KPIs. Furthermore, BTN is set to enhance its underwriting model using advanced data analytics and recruit IT and digital talent to support the development of a more modern and reliable banking platform.

### Broaden Product Propositions and Cross-Selling (2026--2027)

The strategic targets for the 2026--2027 period focus on developing a sustainable funding engine, monetizing the housing ecosystem, and expanding product offerings while enhancing cross-selling opportunities. Key initiatives include increasing market penetration for wealth products targeted at cash-rich and hidden affluent customers, launching need-based financial solutions for self-employed professionals, and introducing merchant products for the retail food and restaurant sectors.

In monetizing the housing ecosystem, BTN aims to strengthen acquisition channels through agents for secondary home sales, deepen engagement with anchor corporate clients, and launch bundled products tailored to the construction and real estate sectors. Additionally, BTN will focus on expanding fee-based products and extending KUMK financing to high-growth sectors with low non-performing loan (NPL) risks. To further diversify its services, the Bank plans to establish a joint venture insurance subsidiary offering both life and general insurance.

As a key enabler, the Bank's digitalization strategy will be strengthened by enhancing digital marketing tools and capabilities to drive cross-selling, alongside the development of technology and data infrastructure to support more advanced analytics. Furthermore, BTN will revitalize its underwriting and collection infrastructure to enhance advanced functionalities and optimize omnichannel orchestration.

### Deliver Full Banking Offerings with Superior Customer Experience (2028--2029)

The targets for the 2028--2029 period focus on delivering comprehensive banking offerings with superior customer experiences. To build a sustainable funding engine, BTN will target cross-selling to capture the end-to-end value chain of the housing sector, while expanding the ecosystem to include affluent residential customers, key industries, and top government institutions. Another goal is to increase TB account registrations and utilization from 9% to 20% through a single integrated platform.

In monetizing the housing ecosystem, the strategy will emphasize reducing churn and optimizing repayment processes via centralized counteroffer teams. BTN also plans to enhance the value and profitability of joint finance (B2B) schemes and channeling partnerships to expand its offerings. For diversification, BTN intends to launch a multi-finance subsidiary to support product and service development.

As an enabler, the focus will be on developing holistic service tools, including RM workbench and CRM systems, and deploying advanced technology platforms and analytics to support large-scale operations. Additionally, strengthening the sales organization and service teams across all products and segments will be accompanied by the development of personalized customer journeys through analytics and digitalization.

To achieve these strategic initiatives, BTN has also developed a roadmap for these steps. The aim is to ensure that all objectives are met according to the designated timeline, resources, and priorities. With a systematic roadmap, BTN can identify potential challenges and plan appropriate mitigation measures to ensure effective and efficient implementation in achieving the BTN Corporate Plan 2025--2029.

### Building a Holistic Framework / Actions for Sustainable Growth (2025)

In 2025, BTN will implement a strategy divided into three parts: Support Business Growth & Sustainability, which optimizes the ALM Bank structure to support sustainable growth and enhance the bank's profitability; Drive Resilient & Secure Operational for Business Continuity, which focuses on ensuring strong and secure operations to mitigate cyber risks, improve Service Level Agreement (SLA) effectiveness, and prevent fraud; and Ensure Competency of GRC Talent and Strong GRC Culture, which aims to develop employee talent and instill a strong GRC culture to drive organizational integrity and high performance.
