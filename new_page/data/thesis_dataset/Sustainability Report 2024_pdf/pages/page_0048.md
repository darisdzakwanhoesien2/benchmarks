BTN adopts various policies, standards, and initiatives as references and frameworks for preparing the Sustainability Report, which presents economic, financial, social, and environmental performance and its impacts. This approach reflects the Bank's commitment to supporting the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) adopted by Indonesia (http://sdgsindonesia.or.id/). Furthermore, BTN initiated the early adoption of IFRS S1 and S2 in 2024, enhancing transparency in the reporting of environmental, social, and governance (ESG) factors while supporting efforts to achieve the SDGs.

This Sustainability Report is published in two languages: Indonesian and English and financial data has been audited by a Public Accounting Firm as part of the BTN Annual Report process. All financial information is presented in Indonesian Rupiah unless stated otherwise. Quantitative data is presented in accordance with the principle of comparability for at least the last two years to facilitate stakeholders in analyzing the Bank's performance trends. In addition to the printed version, this report is also available in a digital format, which can be accessed and downloaded via BTN's official website at www.btn.co.id.

# Report Content Determination Process [GRI 3-1, 3-2, 3-3]

The determination of report content is carried out by considering the alignment with the principles of sustainability reporting outlined in the GRI Standards 2021 guidelines, which include:

1. Stakeholder Engagement
Identifying stakeholder groups and focusing on their expectations and interests related to the Bank's business activities.

2. Sustainability Context
Disclosing the performance and impacts of the Bank's operations in the context of sustainability, covering economic, social, and environmental aspects.

3. Materiality
Highlighting the economic, social, and environmental impacts relevant to the Bank's business activities and their significance in stakeholder evaluation and decision-making processes.

4. Completeness
Presenting all material sustainability aspects and their relevant boundaries to enable stakeholders to assess the Bank's sustainability performance during the reporting period.
