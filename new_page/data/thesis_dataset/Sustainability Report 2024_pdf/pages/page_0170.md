From these three main activities, there are several derivative programs:

|  Main Activities of HFC  |   |   |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Learning Function | Mini MBA in Property | The Mini MBA in Property program is a collaboration between HFC and the School of Business and Management at the Bandung Institute of Technology (SBM-ITB). It is held regularly to create new entrepreneurs in the property industry. Since its launch in October 2016, the program has conducted 22 batches and produced over 700 alumni. In 2024, 3 (three) additional batches (Batch 20, 21, and 22) were held. Additionally, the Mini MBA in Property is designed for members of the Indonesian Young Entrepreneurs Association (HIPMI), with previous sessions held in Surabaya.  |
|   |  BTN Santri Developer Kebangsaan Program | This initiative is a joint program between BTN and NU Circle (NUC), offering property development business training for alumni of Islamic boarding schools (pesantren), interfaith organizations, and pesantren alumni located in program implementation areas. To date, the BTN Santri Developer Kebangsaan program has conducted 4 (four) batches and graduated over 300 alumni. In 2024, the program successfully took place at Pesantren Dar El Hikmah in Pekanbaru.  |
|   |  Seminars/Public Lectures/Workshops | HFC actively organizes seminars, public lectures, and workshops in collaboration with institutions, universities, organizations, and associations. These events aim to provide insights and knowledge to audiences on banking business, digitalization in the banking sector, and other relevant topics aligned with current industry developments. In 2024, seminars and public lectures were held at Sepuluh Nopember Institute of Technology (ITS), Andalas University (Unand), Semarang State University (Unnes), Tanjungpura University (Untan), and HKBP Nommensen University (UHN). Additionally, a Market Outlook Property Seminar and the Impact of PPN-DTP Implementation was conducted during the Mini MBA in Property Graduation Ceremony & Alumni Gathering.  |
|  Fungsi Advisory | Advisory Support for ASANU's Housing Development Program | Alumni of the BTN Santri Developer Kebangsaan Program have established the NU Santri Developer Association (ASANU) as a business platform and incubator for program alumni, ensuring its continuity and sustainability. ASANU also plays an active role in facilitating land availability and acquisition for alumni projects. Additionally, BTN supports these alumni by providing access to construction financing and home sales through KPR BTN. In 2024, ASANU Riau Regional Board (DPD ASANU Riau) was successfully established. The planned ASANU projects are located in Banjarnegara and Banyumas.  |
|   |  Development of BTN Property Advisory System (BTN PAS) Application | BTN PAS is a mobile-based application designed as a consultation, mentoring, and communication platform between mentors (instructors) and mentees (participants/alumni of training programs) conducted by the Housing Finance Center (HFC) BTN. Through this platform, mentors and mentees can conduct online consultations on property-related topics. The application serves as a tool for BTN to cultivate new housing developers and strategize business potential.  |
|   |  ABCG Collaboration Program (Academic, Business, Community, and Government) | As an initiative to provide housing solutions for informal MBR (Low-Income Communities), the ABCG Program is a collaborative framework between Academia (Universities), Business (BTN), Community (Community-Based Organizations), and Government (Ministry of Housing and Settlement, Ministry of Agrarian Affairs and Spatial Planning/National Land Agency, and Local Governments). This collaboration aims to support informal sector workers in owning self-built and community-based housing.  |
