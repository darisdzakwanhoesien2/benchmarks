As a financial institution with a primary focus on the housing sector, BTN is committed to fostering a sustainable housing financing ecosystem. To achieve this goal, the Bank recognizes that green mortgages are a strategic initial step.

To accelerate this transformation, BTN has established a strong partnership with the Green Building Council Indonesia. This collaboration not only aims to enhance employee knowledge and understanding of sustainability practices, but also to improve competencies. Through various initiatives, BTN aspires not only to be a leader in the housing sector but also a pioneer in creating lasting positive impacts—both environmentally and socially—that bring long-term benefits and contribute to environmental sustainability.
