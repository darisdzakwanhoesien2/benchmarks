# Oil and Gas

![img-199.jpeg](img-199.jpeg)

The Bank establishes credit policies that require customers in this sector to:

A. Credit financing in the oil and gas sector is limited only to corporations with good ESG implementation.
B. Hold Environmental Management System certifications such as ISO 14001/ISO 45001 or other equivalent documents recognized by the Bank.
C. Have an Environmental Impact Assessment (AMDAL) report or Environmental Management and Monitoring Effort Report (UKL-UPL) (including reclamation and post-mining guarantees) approved by the Environmental Agency (BLH).

D. Implement energy efficiency measures and GHG emissions reduction efforts in accordance with regulatory requirements at the business location. For public companies, hold a Sustainability Report or other equivalent documents.
E. Oil and gas business activities must have documents confirming compliance with all Oil and Gas Safety requirements (including facilities, equipment, workers, general, and environmental safety standards), specifically proven through the Supporting Business Certificate (SKUP).
F. No deforestation practices, including land clearing, and no exploitation.

# Transportation

![img-201.jpeg](img-201.jpeg)

The Bank establishes credit policies that require customers in this sector to:

A. Hold Environmental Management System certifications, such as ISO 14001/ISO 45001 or other

equivalent documents recognized by the Bank.

B. For public companies, they must have a sustainability report.
C. Implement energy efficiency measures and efforts to reduce emissions, with adequate electricity and water supply availability.
