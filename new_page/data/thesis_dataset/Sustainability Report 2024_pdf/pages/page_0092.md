# BTN Plants 200 Mangrove Trees to Commemorate World Mangrove Day

![img-125.jpeg](img-125.jpeg)

As part of the celebration of International Mangrove Day on July 26, 2024, BTN, through its Corporate Social and Environmental Responsibility (TJSL) program, collaborated with Jejakin to plant 200 mangrove trees at Taman Wisata Angke, Pantai Indah Kapuk, Jakarta. This activity is part of BTN's commitment to conducting sustainable and environmentally responsible operations while realizing Clean Banking Operations.

The planting of 200 mangrove trees is estimated to absorb approximately 10.99 tons of  $\mathrm{CO}_{2}\mathrm{e}$ , equivalent to saving 166,667 sheets of paper, according to the research of Xiang et al., 2023. This demonstrates BTN's tangible contribution to reducing its carbon footprint and supporting climate change mitigation efforts.

As part of its commitment to the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), particularly SDG No. 13: Climate Action, BTN supports the preservation of mangrove forests, which have proven to be more effective at absorbing carbon dioxide than tropical rainforests or peatlands. Additionally, this activity contributes to SDG No. 14: Life Below Water, as mangrove forests serve as natural barriers, protecting coastlines from tsunamis, and are vital ecosystems for conserving marine biodiversity.
