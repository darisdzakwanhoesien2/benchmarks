# External Assurance [GRI 2-5] [CJK G.I]

In line with GRI recommendations, BTN has conducted a series of external assurance processes for this Sustainability Report. This process is deemed crucial as it involves an independent third party with high credibility, thereby enhancing the reliability and quality of the report. The selection of the external assurance provider was approved by the Board of Directors, represented by the Risk Management and ESG Division, ensuring there are no conflicts of interest with any party.

BTN entrusted CBC Global Indonesia as the external assurance provider for the 2024 Sustainability Report. The institution holds international certification for assurance standards, specifically the AA1000AS v3 standard, with license number 000-310. The External Assurance Provider was not involved in the preparation of BTN's Sustainability Report. The assurance results on page 302. [GRI 2-5] [CJK G.I]

The Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors reviewed this report to ensure all material topics have been addressed. Following the review process, the Board of Commissioners and the Board of Directors approved and signed this report, as detailed on pages 14 and 15. [GRI 2-14]

# Feedback [CJK G.2, G.3]

A Feedback Form is provided at the end of this report to facilitate two-way communication with readers and users of BTN's Sustainability Report. Through this form, readers and users are encouraged to share feedback, suggestions, and opinions to help improve the quality of future reporting. Any feedback received will be processed and addressed by the Corporate Secretary Division or the Information and Documentation Management Officer (PPID). However, throughout 2024, BTN did not receive any responses from stakeholders regarding the previous year's Sustainability Report.
