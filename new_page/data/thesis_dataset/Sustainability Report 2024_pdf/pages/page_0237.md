# 5. Expanding Service Coverage through Three Subsidiaries

Building a Sharia Bank business ranked #2 in Indonesia; launching and developing a multi-finance company (with loan distribution &gt; Rp2.5 trillion) and insurance companies (Gross Written Premium/GWP &gt; Rp2 trillion).

This aspiration reflects BTN's commitment to integrating innovation, operational efficiency, and sustainability into all aspects of its business. To support this aspiration, BTN has developed the BTN Plan: 1+3+5 strategic initiatives.

## Core strength

### 1. Funding

- Increase low-cost transactional savings through payroll (5% penetration) and mobile banking/balé (3 million customers).
- Develop tailored propositions for 70k+ cash-rich self-employed professionals.
- Promote full-transaction banking for SME and wholesale segments (20% utilization).

## Holistic Banking Propositions

### 1. Housing ecosystem

- Add new customers through cumulative mortgages (1.5 million+) and build comprehensive banking partnerships.
- Improve value chain penetration through 25+ anchor clients.

### 2. Full banking offering

- Cross-sell HYL (5% penetration) and fee-based income to ETB (Existing to Bank) consumers.
- Expand new sectors for SME loans (KUR, KUMKM).

### 3. Financial group

- Become the #2 player in Sharia banking through partnership development and footprint expansion.
- Establish new subsidiaries in life insurance and multi-finance.

## Capabilities to deliver at scale

### 1. Digitization

- Digitize end-to-end customer journeys to accelerate processes and efficiency.
- Drive branch efficiency through automation to achieve CIR &lt; 50%.

### 2. Sales model

Restructure sales and operating models for cross-collaboration (one CST model) and effectiveness (in several mono-lines).

### 3. Upskilling

Conduct bank-wide capability building focused on customer-centric sales.

### 4. Tech &amp; data

- Accelerate modernization of banking platforms.
- Develop and adopt advanced AI assets.

### 5. Risk Management

Strengthen risk underwriting and revamp collections and recovery strategies to maintain CoC &lt; 1.2%.

## Strengthen Engine for Sustainable Funding (2025)

These targets focus on developing a sustainable funding engine and leveraging the housing ecosystem to deepen market penetration and enhance product offerings. The key strategic initiatives include launching an integrated payroll product as part of BTN's efforts to strengthen competitiveness and expand market share, offering housing products tailored for millennials, and enhancing customer acquisition and transactions through BTN Mobile/Balé. Additionally, there is a strong emphasis on boosting retail fund sales, targeting high-volume and high-growth potential segments.

To monetize the housing ecosystem, the strategy includes allocating 60% to subsidized housing schemes and strengthening relationships with 50 key developers. BTN also plans to diversify its banking product portfolio, increase higher-yield returns, and build a solid Sharia banking business post-spin-off.
