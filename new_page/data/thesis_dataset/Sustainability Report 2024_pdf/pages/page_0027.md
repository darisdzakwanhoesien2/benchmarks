# Policy

BTN continues to strive towards implementing sustainable finance to realize its vision as “The Key Partner in Financial Empowerment for Indonesian Families.” To achieve this commitment, the Bank has made sustainability principles the core foundation of every operational activity and decision-making process. As a concrete step, BTN has established General Policy No. KU.10 on Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) and Special Policy No. KK.10-A on the Procedures for Implementing Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG), which serve as the basis for applying ESG principles at BTN, supported by technical guidelines on CRST, GHG Emissions, and Sustainability Reporting. BTN ensures that all components of the Policy and Procedure Architecture for the Bank’s operations and business are aligned with the principles of Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) and the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

# Data and Information

To support the implementation of sustainability principles, BTN systematically collects relevant data and information regarding ESG performance in every aspect of the Bank’s operations. This data collection process includes various indicators reflecting the environmental, social, and governance impacts of the Company’s activities, ensuring the accuracy of the information presented. The Bank adopts various methods to collect data that supports the progress of the ESG roadmap implementation. One of the methods used is the Digital Action Log &amp; Monitoring System (DAMS) application, which allows relevant work units to regularly update the progress of ESG initiatives. This system simplifies the monitoring and management of ESG data efficiently and transparently, ensuring that the information is properly communicated to management.

Furthermore, The Bank also manages detailed data on specific aspects such as electricity use, paper consumption, and others. This step ensures the accuracy and completeness of the data required to support the evaluation and reporting of ongoing ESG initiatives. With this approach, the Bank can continuously monitor progress and make improvements towards achieving the set sustainability goals.

In the reporting year, the Bank developed the BTN Integrated ESG Dashboard System as part of its strategic steps to achieve “BTN Become The ESG Champion”. This initiative aligns with BTN’s roadmap and strategy, namely “Establishing ESG Monitoring, Communication, and Reporting Program.” The ESG Dashboard is designed to provide regular information to stakeholders regarding BTN’s ESG performance, particularly in terms of GHG emissions, sustainable portfolio, and human capital management. The dashboard aims to support integrated control, be paperless, centralized, and enable quick and easy data retrieval as needed.

The collected data is then analyzed to identify areas that need improvement and to measure the achievement of the established sustainability goals. BTN also ensures that all data collected meets transparency and compliance standards, so that the ESG performance reports reflect the Company’s commitment to accountability and social responsibility. With this approach, BTN strives to continuously improve the quality of ESG data collection and reporting, strengthening its sustainability strategy for a more effective and positively impactful outcome for all stakeholders.

As part of its commitment to sustainability, BTN regularly reports and evaluates ESG performance to ensure that efforts made are aligned with the Bank’s long-term goals and have a positive impact on all stakeholders. ESG performance reporting is carried out transparently
