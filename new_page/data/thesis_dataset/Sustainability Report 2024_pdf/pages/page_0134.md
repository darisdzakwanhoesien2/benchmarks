The Talent Acceleration Program (TAP) focuses on leadership aspects, increasing exposure, providing experiences, and strengthening employees' technical competencies. Employee rotation between different roles is implemented with a "dual path" approach, which includes fulfilling the Bank's needs for positions requiring specific expertise (functional) and managerial roles. Employees are allowed to develop in both paths, either in managerial or functional positions. The path selection is based on factors such as the Bank's needs, employee competencies and capabilities, and personal aspirations, ensuring placements align with career development goals.

The Bank has designed a career ladder structure to create clear growth opportunities and motivate employees to develop optimally. This ladder considers job categories, job families, and individual career planning, all based on core competencies, leadership, and job families. In addition, relevant learning curricula are also integrated into the development planning.

In 2024, the Bank provided employee coaching programs for career development. Through this program, employees are trained to enhance skills and abilities relevant to their field. Employees also acquire non-technical skills to develop leadership qualities, overcome challenges and obstacles, improve communication skills, achieve work-life balance, and strengthen motivation and engagement.
