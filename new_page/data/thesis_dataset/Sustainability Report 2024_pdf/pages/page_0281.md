# Anti-Fraud and Conflict of Interest [ORI 2-15]

BTN has implemented a formal and comprehensive policy regarding fraud prevention and anti-corruption, as outlined in specific policies and technical regulations. These policies are designed to prevent fraudulent practices across all Bank operations. As part of this commitment, the President Director and Chief Commissioner of BTN signed BTN's Anti-Bribery Policy in 2023, which is an integral part of the Anti-Bribery Management System (ABMS). The signing of this policy underscores BTN's seriousness in upholding integrity and transparency in all operational aspects.

With the establishment of this policy and guidelines, BTN ensures that all stakeholders, including employees and partners, adhere to strict integrity and anti-corruption standards. The policy encompasses guidelines, procedures, and clear internal controls aimed at preventing bribery. BTN not only commits to meeting legal obligations but also fosters an organizational culture rooted in accountability and ethics. This Anti-Bribery Policy supports BTN in maintaining its reputation and fostering a cleaner and more trustworthy business environment.

# Anti-Fraud

As a foundational element in fostering build sustainable relationships with stakeholders, BTN prioritizes public trust through its commitment to integrity. The Bank strives to conduct all business activities with honesty, fairness, and without tolerance for corruption or fraud. BTN believes that any action violating these core values will significantly impact its reputation and business sustainability. Therefore, the Bank views it as essential not only to consider the financial impact of its decisions but also to weigh their broader implications on public trust, which may not be directly measurable but is invaluable for the Bank's sustainability and growth.

BTN firmly emphasizes that any breaches of its anti-corruption and anti-fraud policies are treated as serious matters requiring stringent actions in accordance with prevailing regulations and policies. Policy Number 6, governs aspects related to Legal, Compliance, AML, PPT, PPPSPM, and Corporate Governance. This policy is applied comprehensively, covering all employees, whether permanent or contractual, and includes top management such as the Board of Commissioners and Directors acting on behalf of BTN. This approach demonstrates BTN's commitment to ensuring that all

actions taken, whether by employees or leadership, consistently reflect integrity and fairness. The ultimate goal is to establish a strong foundation that not only supports business continuity but also builds long-term relationships with all stakeholders, including customers, business partners, and other related parties. [ORI 3-3]

BTN has implemented a Zero Tolerance to Fraud and Zero-Defect program, which is in line with the Financial Services Authority Regulation No. 39/POJK.03/2019 on the Implementation of Anti-Fraud Strategies for Commercial Banks. To support the implementation of this policy, various strategic measures have been implemented to strengthen the prevention, detection, investigation and monitoring of potential fraud in banking operations. BTN conducts socialization regarding the anti-fraud program to all (100%) of employees and communicating anti-corruption policies and procedures to all (100%) active vendors through the signing of the ABMS clause in contracts.
