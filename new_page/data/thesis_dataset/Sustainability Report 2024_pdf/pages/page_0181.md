# Agriculture (Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries)

![img-189.jpeg](img-189.jpeg)

The Bank establishes credit policies that require customers in this sector to:

A. Hold a PROPER Gold or Green rating.
B. Credit will not be granted to new companies involved in land clearing.
C. Have a Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP), with an assessment related to the availability of WWTP operations and compliance with established quality standards.
D. For public companies, they must have a sustainability report.
E. Commit to No Deforestation, No Peat, No Exploitation (NDPE), covering land clearance, land release, and conservation of High Conservation Value (HCV)/High Carbon Stock (HCS) areas and peatlands.
F. For the Palm Oil Plantation subsector, companies must have an ISPO and/or RSPO certification, or at least proof of registration in the form of a receipt from a certification body.
G. For the Palm Oil Plantation subsector without ISPO and/or RSPO certification, the following criteria must be met:

a. Have a Code of Conduct, Environmental &amp; Labor Policies (including Occupational Safety &amp; Health - K3).
b. Have Environmental Management System certifications, such as ISO 14001/ISO 45001 or

other equivalent documents recognized by the Bank.

c. Have Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for fire prevention and fire control, standard fire suppression equipment according to land criteria, fire monitoring towers, and a trained fire-fighting team following the Directorate General of Plantations' standards.
d. Hold a Plantation Business Permit (IUP), including applicable location permits such as IUP Cultivation (IUP-B), IUP Processing (IUP-P), and Plantation Business Registration Certificate (SPUP).

H. Have no social/environmental conflicts within the past three years.
I. For the SME segment, ISPO requirements apply as an affirmative covenant, with compliance to be periodically monitored by the Relationship Manager and subject to internal bank audits.
J. Hold permits for industrial forest management, such as Forest Product Utilization Business Permit (IUPHHK) for Timber and/or Non-Timber Products (IUPHHBK), and Forest Management Work Plan (RKU) approved by the Ministry of Environment and Forestry.
K. Not engage in business activities in areas designated as biodiversity conservation zones.
