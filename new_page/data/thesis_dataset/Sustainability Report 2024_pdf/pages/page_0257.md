Market risk control is performed by evaluating exposure against Risk Appetite and Risk Tolerance, which are periodically revised. To manage exchange rate risk exposure, the Bank sets various limits, including the Net Open Position (NOP), in compliance with PBI No. 17/5/PBI/2015. The Bank also implements SEOJK No. 23/SEOJK.03/2022 related to risk-weighted asset (RWA) calculations for market risk, which has been effective since January 2024. In line with the Basel III implementation, the Bank submits monthly Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) reports and quarterly Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR) reports through the OJK Online Reporting Application (APOLO), in accordance with POJK 19 in the Year 2024 and POJK 20 in the Year 2020, to ensure compliance with liquidity and funding adequacy standards set by the regulator.

## Operational Risk

In implementing operational risk management processes, the Bank has established Operational Risk Tools designed to identify, measure, and control risks that may impact operations and the achievement of objectives. The Operational Risk Tools include:

1. Risk and Control Self-Assessment (RCSA)

RCSA is conducted in each Bank unit to identify and measure operational risks inherent in daily activities. Each unit is required to evaluate the potential risks associated with their tasks and how these risks might affect the achievement of their objectives. RCSA serves as an early mechanism to ensure that identified risks are properly managed.

2. Loss Event Database (LED)

The LED is used to collect data on operational risk incidents occurring in branch offices. Using the criteria set by Basel II, the LED records various types of losses such as actual losses, near losses, and potential losses for each risk incident. This data is critical for analyzing patterns and trends in risk events, helping to design mitigation measures to prevent similar incidents in the future.

3. Key Risk Indicator (KRI)

KRIs are used to monitor and analyze risk trends that may indicate potential operational issues in the future (predictive) or confirm incidents that have already occurred (lagging). With KRIs, the

Bank can quickly identify early warnings related to risks and take necessary preventive actions.

BTN also implements Business Continuity Management (BCM) to ensure the continuity of operations in response to external disruptions such as disasters or IT system failures. The BCM policy encompasses preparedness, disaster response, and operational recovery, with regular simulations and evaluations conducted.

Additionally, to ensure effective control implementation, BTN has established the Business Control Organization, which functions as part of the 1.5th line of defense. This organization focuses on operational risk control for daily activities in various units, such as branch offices (KC), Syariah branch offices (KCS), the Commercial Banking Center (CBC), and the Regional Loan Processing Center (RLPC). This organization comprises various sections, including the Regional Business Control (RBC), Branch Business Control (BBC), and the Commercial Banking Centre Business Control (CBC-BC), tasked with monitoring and managing risks within their respective work units in an integrated and systematic manner.

## Legal Risk

BTN identifies legal risks that may arise from factors such as lawsuits, legal deficiencies, and vulnerabilities in legal aspects attached to various functional activities, including credit, treasury, investments, operations, information technology, and human resource management. Legal risk measurement is conducted by the Risk Management Division in collaboration with the Legal Division. This process results in evaluation reports that include analysis of legal cases, contingencies arising from lawsuits, and the quality of legal agreements in credit documentation.

Legal risk monitoring is performed regularly by evaluating the effectiveness of policy, procedure, and compliance implementation in accordance with regulations and Bank standards. The Legal Division provides legal input and recommendations to all divisions conducting contract reviews with third parties. To support legal risk monitoring, the Bank utilizes the Legal Application System (LApS), which functions to monitor the progress of legal cases, both criminal and non-criminal, occurring at branch offices or headquarters.
