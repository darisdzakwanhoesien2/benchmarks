# BTN Agents [FS16]

BTN Agents is an implementation of the Branchless Financial Services for Financial Inclusion (Laku Pandai), an agency banking program initiated by the Financial Services Authority (OJK). This program aligns with the Indonesian government's National Strategy for Financial Inclusion (SNKI), introduced in June 2012, in order to expand public access to formal financial services, particularly for those without banking access. Additionally, the program seeks to protect communities from burdensome lending practices, which are often prevalent in the informal sector.

BTN Agents provide Laku Pandai services for individuals who are unfamiliar with banking or lack access to formal financial services (unbanked). This program also serves as an opportunity for communities to learn about banking, financial products, and common banking transactions, with easier access and closer proximity to their residences, including in remote areas. Through this service, both new and existing customers can access various banking transactions, such as account opening, bill payments, cash deposits, withdrawals, fund transfers, balance inquiries, mini statements, PIN changes, and product purchases. Additionally, BTN encourages community empowerment within the banking sector by increase their income in the form of participating in becoming an agent.

General Requirements to Become a BTN Agent:

1. Registration can be completed at the nearest BTN branch or sub-branch.
2. Have become a BTN customer and have an active savings account to accommodate deposits, commission payments, and fees.
3. Must have operated a business for at least one year.
4. Must possess valid business permits and licenses (at least a letter of recognition from the local RT authority).
5. Must not be listed on Bank Indonesia's national blacklist.
6. Must pass the Bank's due diligence process.

# SimPel [FS16]

SimPel is one of BTN's flagship programs designed to support financial inclusion by providing easy access to financial services and enhancing financial literacy among students. The program targets students from elementary (SD), middle (SMP), and high school (SMA) levels, aiming to educate them on the importance of saving and financial planning from an early age. With simple account opening and transaction requirements, SimPel seeks to raise financial awareness among school-aged children, helping them understand and manage financial products wisely. As part of BTN's commitment to financial inclusion, this program empowers Indonesia's younger generation to prepare for a more financially secure future.

In 2024, BTN successfully reached over 824,069 participants in both rural and urban areas across Indonesia. The program focuses on digital financial training for students, equipping them with a better understanding of banking products and financial management. Additionally, BTN collaborates with educational institutions and local communities to expand the program's reach and support financial inclusion efforts nationwide.
