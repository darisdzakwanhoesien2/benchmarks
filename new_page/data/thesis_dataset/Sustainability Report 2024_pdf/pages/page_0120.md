# Employee Training on Diversity

To enhance workforce diversity, BTN implements a strategy that includes training and socialization for all employees, from junior staff to senior executive vice presidents, to ensure the understanding and implementing of these principles across the organization. As part of this initiative, BTN organizes training sessions with external speakers to instill the principles of diversity, equality, and inclusion. This training aims to ensure the effective implementation of these principles at all organizational levels.

The BTN Board of Directors fully supports this program through direct oversight by top management to ensure that the diversity policy is integrated into every aspect of the Company's business and operational processes.
