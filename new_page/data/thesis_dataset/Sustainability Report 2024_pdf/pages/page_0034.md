To ensure the effective implementation of the Principles for Responsible Banking (PRB), BTN has conducted an initial impact analysis of its consumer portfolio and is committed to publishing its first PRB report within 18 months of signing. Additionally, BTN has set specific targets for key impact areas identified as sustainability priorities.

## BTN Impact Radar

BTN recognizes the importance of assessing the social, economic, and environmental impacts of its financial activities in implementing the Principles for Responsible Banking. By adopting the Impact Radar framework developed by UNEP FI, BTN can holistically identify and manage both positive and negative impacts.

This framework encompasses the three core pillars of sustainable development—economic, environmental, and social—providing a strong foundation for integrating sustainability into the Bank's business strategy.

This approach is further reinforced by the use of the UNEP FI Portfolio Impact Analysis Tools, which enables BTN to prioritize actions contextually by leveraging connections to various sustainability topics. This tool helps BTN integrate measurable strategic steps to maximize positive impacts and mitigate negative ones. Additionally, the Impact Radar and portfolio analysis tool serve as fundamental resources for developing a comprehensive impact management framework, ensuring that every step aligns with global efforts to achieve the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
