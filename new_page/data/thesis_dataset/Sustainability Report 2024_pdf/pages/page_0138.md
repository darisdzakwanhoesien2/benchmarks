As part of the remuneration policy implementation, BTN provides various employee benefits, including salary, annual salary adjustments, holiday allowances (THR), annual leave allowance, and long-term leave allowance, provided every three years of service. In addition, the Bank also provides health facilities for employees and their family members, covering inpatient and outpatient care, maternity expenses, dental care, general check-ups, eyeglass purchases, among others. BTN is committed to providing equal non-salary benefits for all employees, including contract employees, to improve their quality of life. Remuneration is also variable and can be adjusted under certain conditions, including overtime pay, clothing allowance, sales incentive bonuses, annual bonuses, and location and position allowances for employees in certain organizational positions.

Under Special Policy Number KK.3.B on Remuneration, BTN regulates the provision of Long-Term Incentives (LTI) to improve performance and retain employees. These LTIs can be in the form of shares, cash, or other forms set by the Board of Directors and are designed to link employee rewards to the Company's strategic achievements. BTN also pays special attention to employees' housing and transportation needs by providing special credit facilities to help employees meet these needs. The Bank ensures that all remuneration and benefits are aligned with employment status and that contract employees' rights align with the Company's standards. [GRI 401-2]
