# Employee Competency Development

BTN has provided training and development programs for all employees, including permanent staff, contract employees, part-time workers, and outsourced personnel. With this inclusive approach, Bank BTN aims to foster a work culture that supports continuous development for all workforce members involved in the Company's operations. Throughout 2024, BTN employees have participated in various competency enhancement programs, including training sessions, workshops, conferences, and seminars related to ESG aspects.
