# About the Report

Since 2009, BTN has consistently published an annual Sustainability Report. The 2024 Sustainability Report highlights the Bank's operational performance, encompassing the Head Office, Regional Office, and branch offices across Indonesia, for the period from January 1 to December 31, 2024, aligning with the Consolidated Financial Report as of December 31, 2024. This marks the 16th edition of the report, following the previous edition published in February. It successfully received the Gold Rank award at the ASRRAT 2023. [GRI 2-2, 2-3]

The preparation of the Sustainability Report, which is routinely carried out by BTN, reflects the Bank's commitment and compliance with regulations, particularly Regulation of the Financial Services Authority (POJK) No. 51/POJK.03/2017 concerning the Implementation of Sustainable Finance for Financial Service Institutions, Issuers, and Public Companies. As stated in Article 10, the regulation mandates the preparation of a Sustainability Report for Financial Service Institutions (FSIs), issuers, and public companies. This also serves as evidence of BTN's proactive initiative in publishing Sustainability Reports long before the regulation was enacted.

In the same regulation, a Sustainability Report is defined as a publicly disclosed report containing the economic, financial, social, and environmental performance of FSIs, issuers, and public companies in conducting sustainable business practices. In addition to adhering to POJK No. 51/POJK.03/2017, the preparation of BTN's Sustainability Report also refers to Circular Letter of the Financial Services Authority (SEOJK) No. 16/SEOJK.04/2021 concerning the Form and Content of Annual Reports of Issuers or Public Companies. Based on this OJK Circular Letter, public companies are required to report Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) through an Annual Report or a separate document, such as a Sustainability Report.

BTN has ensured that the data and information presented in this report are accurate and adhere to the principles of report preparation. Should there be any discrepancies between the data or information in previous reports, such as due to changes in measurement methods, such adjustments will be clearly marked as restated to provide clarity.
