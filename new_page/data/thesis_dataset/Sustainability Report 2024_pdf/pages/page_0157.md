# BTN Kediri Strengthens Local Community through CSR Assistance for Social and Environmental Development

On November 14, 2024, BTN organized the handover of Corporate Social Responsibility assistance. This assistance was provided to various social, disability, and environmental groups in Kediri Regency, including the Kediri Disability Association (PDKK), the Srikandi Putri Jambean Women's Farmers Group, and the Insani Bangsa Kediri Association.

The CSR assistance handover is a routine activity for BTN, demonstrating the Company's commitment to social issues, human rights, inclusivity, and financial and infrastructural support for community development at its branch offices. This assistance is expected to contribute to the development and progress of Kediri's growth, while also fostering closer connections between BTN and the community in efforts to enhance the housing ecosystem.

Tree Planting in Green Open Spaces and Public Facilities

On July 26, 2024, BTN collaborated with JEJAKIN in a tree planting activity, where 200 mangrove trees were planted at Angke Wildlife Park, Pantai Indah Kapuk, Jakarta. This program aims to preserve coastal ecosystems and enhance the environmental quality in green open spaces.
