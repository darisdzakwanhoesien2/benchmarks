# Scope 1 and Scope 2 GHG Intensity

GHG intensity indicates the total GHG emissions per unit of activity, output, or other specific metrics. Emission intensity is calculated by dividing the total GHG emissions (Scope 1 and Scope 2) produced by the Bank by the number of employees, expressed in tons of $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$ eq per employee. Additionally, calculations are also performed in tons of $\mathrm{CO}_{2}$ eq per office unit. Information on GHG intensity is detailed in the following table: [GRI 305-4]

The increase in intensity in 2024 is primarily due to the addition of the head office, Menara 2 BTN. Furthermore, there has been an expansion of operational vehicles to enhance the efficiency of the Bank's business operations.

# Scope 3 GHG Emissions

To enhance the comprehensiveness of emission reporting, since 2023 BTN has mapped relevance and calculated Scope 3 GHG emissions. This calculation includes three main categories in the Bank's supply chain activities, namely:

- Purchased goods
- Business travel
- Financed emissions

In the financed emissions category, BTN refers to the standards of The Partnership for Carbon Accounting Financials (PCAF). Financed emissions are generated from financial services provided by BTN, including investments and loans. The Bank has calculated financed emissions per sector, such as construction, energy, transportation, Industrial Process and Production Use (IPPU), and other sectors. Meanwhile, for the other two categories, namely business travel and purchased goods, BTN refers to the standards set by the GHG Protocol. [GRI 305-3] [TCFD Metrics.a]
