This training plays a crucial role in strengthening BTN's ethical culture and ensuring that all actions taken align with values of integrity, transparency, and accountability. By involving all employees and partners, BTN is committed to creating an ethical working environment that protects the Bank's reputation and credibility. The training also aims to raise awareness about the importance of ethics in business interactions and equips participants with skills to address potential conflicts of interest.
