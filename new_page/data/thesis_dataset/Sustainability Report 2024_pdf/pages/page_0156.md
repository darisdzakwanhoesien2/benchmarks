# Environmental Pillar

![img-169.jpeg](img-169.jpeg)

BTN is committed to environmental preservation through activities focused on the management of natural resources and the environment. These efforts are aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals, specifically goals 6, 11, 12, 13, 14, and 15.

## Development of Green Parks and Play Areas Around the BTN Tower

On January 26, 2024, BTN successfully implemented tree planting at the Green BTN Educational Park, located behind the BTN Tower. This park is expected to enhance the quality of the environment while serving as an educational space for the surrounding community.

## Socialization and Waste Sorting in BTN Housing Areas

As a form of environmental and community care, on August 21, 2024, BTN implemented the Waste Bank CSR program in Banten. In this program, BTN conducts socialization and waste sorting activities to raise awareness within BTN housing areas, reduce waste, and maintain a clean environment.

## Development of Clean Water Facilities, Public Toilets, and Sanitation

In support of public health, on June 7, 2024, BTN has constructed clean water facilities, public toilets (MCK), and proper sanitation. A healthy MCK facility has been built in Padang City, West Sumatra.
