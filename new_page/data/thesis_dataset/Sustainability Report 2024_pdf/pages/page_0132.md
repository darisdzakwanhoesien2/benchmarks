As a state-owned bank (BUMN) with a critical role in Indonesia's economy, BTN ensures that its human capital development aligns with the core values of BUMN: AKHLAK (Trustworthy, Competent, Harmonious, Loyal, Adaptive, and Collaborative). With a continuously evolving learning culture, BTN is well-positioned to become a global player, ready to address future challenges and meet the needs of society.
