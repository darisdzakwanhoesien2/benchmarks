# Energy Management Performance

BTN has conducted comprehensive measurement and recording of energy usage, including electricity and fuel (BBM) consumption for the Bank's operational vehicles. These records serve as the basis for measuring efficiency and effectiveness in implementing energy-saving efforts. Energy usage measurement is carried out using a methodology from the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources, based on energy usage (physical units) multiplied by calorific value. Based on this methodology, total energy consumption during the reporting year can be detailed as follows:

Energy Consumption [GRI 3-3, 302-1, 302-2] [GJK F.6]

|  Energy Source | Unit | 2024 | 2023 | 2022  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  Electricity | GJ | 224,920 | 34,276 | 68,829  |
|  Fuel | GJ | 155,332 | 82,321 | 93,403  |
|  Total Energy Usage | GJ | 380,252 | 116,597 | 162,232  |

*) In 2024 there is an additional coverage of branch office data, so the Bank sets 2024 as the baseline year of calculation.

# Energy Intensity

Energy intensity reflects energy usage per unit of activity, output, or specific metrics. Furthermore, energy intensity is presented per employee and per branch office as follows: [GRI 302-3]

Energy Usage Intensity

|  Denominator | 2024 | 2023 | 2022  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  Employees | 29.60 GJ/employee | 9.68 GJ/employee | 13.68 GJ/employee  |
|  Office Unit | 513.85 GJ/office unit | 31.84 GJ/office unit* | 44.33 GJ/office unit*  |

*) Data restated

The increase in intensity in 2024 is primarily due to the addition of the head office, Menara 2 BTN. Furthermore, there has been an expansion of operational vehicles to enhance the efficiency of the Bank's business operations.
