# Employee Well-Being Program

As part of the implementation of the Employee Value Proposition (EVP) and the enhancement of corporate culture programs, in 2024, BTN launched the employee

well-being program through a series of initiatives focusing on both physical and mental health:

|  No | Program Name | Description | Goal  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  1 | Employee Wellness Program (EWP) | A program to improve the physical and mental health of employees by providing a consultancy platform with experts. | Improve BTN employees' health to enhance work performance and support their well-being.  |
|  2 | Employee Challenge | A challenge program for employees to improve their physical health through the Step Challenge and Get Fit Challenge programs.. | Employees can develop a more consistent healthy lifestyle and achieve a healthier and fitter body.  |
|  3 | Health Week and Health Seminars | A program to improve employees' health through mini MCU tests and health seminars over a week with the theme "Enhancing Work-Life Harmony through BTN's Wellbeing Program." | • Employees can develop a more consistent healthy lifestyle and gain insights on healthy living from experts.
• Employees will get the latest health results from the tests.  |
|  4 | Sport Together (Sport – Gether) | A fitness training program conducted in groups through sessions like Poundfit and Cardio Boxing. | Employees can improve their fitness and physical health, leading to a healthier and fitter body.  |
|  5 | Blood Donation | This program is a routine activity conducted every three months throughout 2024 and has expanded its coverage by involving implementation at Regional Offices. | To facilitate BTN employees in maintaining their health.  |
