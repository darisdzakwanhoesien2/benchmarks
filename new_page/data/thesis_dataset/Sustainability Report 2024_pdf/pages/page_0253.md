# Risk Management Policies and Procedures

BTN has established policy guidelines in the area of risk management, known as General Risk Management Policy (KUMR), which meet the minimum requirements mandated by Bank Indonesia Regulations/Financial Services Authority (OJK).

As part of the implementation of KUMR, BTN has also developed more detailed internal policies, including:

1. Guidelines for Reporting and Assessing the Bank's Risk Profile
2. Implementation of Reporting and Assessment of Bank Soundness Level
3. Risk Model Validation Procedures
4. Guidelines for Risk Data Collection for the BTN-Loss Event Database (BTN-LED Tool) Application
5. Guidelines for the Liquidity Risk Measurement Process
6. Guidelines for Calculating and Reporting the Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR)
7. Guidelines for the Market Risk Measurement Process
8. Interest Rate Risk Management in the Banking Book
9. Guidelines for Conducting Stress Testing
10. Risk Model Validation Procedures
11. Liquidity Monitoring Guidelines
12. Risk Management Policy Guidelines (PKMR)
13. Risk &amp; Control Self-Assessment
14. Guidelines for Implementing Recovery Plan Actions
15. Risk Culture

16. Guidelines for Risk Profile Assessment of Branch and Syariah Branch Offices
17. Internal Credit Rating Process (ICR)
18. Guidelines for the Credit Scoring Model Process (CSM)
19. Authority Limits for Commercial and Consumer Credit
20. Guidelines for Calculating Allowance for Impairment Losses (CKPN)
21. Policies for Debtors Affected by the Covid-19 Pandemic
22. Business Continuity Management (BCM)
23. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Business Continuity Plan (BCP)
24. Standard Operating Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)
25. Standard Operating Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

The policies also include ESG risks related to the financing provided. As part of risk management, BTN ensures that every financing decision considers ESG aspects. This includes environmental impacts, compliance with human rights, and adherence to Good Corporate Governance. This aims to mitigate potential risks stemming from financing that do not align with sustainability principles and social responsibility.
