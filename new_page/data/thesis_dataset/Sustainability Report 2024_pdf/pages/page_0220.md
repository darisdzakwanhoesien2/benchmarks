In 2024, BTN successfully improved customer satisfaction, achieving a faster average complaint

resolution time of (3 working days) while also increasing public trust in the Bank's services.

BTN is committed to ensuring that all customer personal data is processed in accordance with the customer's explicit consent. The data will not be used for purposes other than the primary transaction without prior notice or additional consent from the customer.

This is reflected in the attainment of the ISO 9001:2015 Certificate regarding Contact Center Service Quality Standards as a form of validation that BTN's contact center services are standardized and serve as one of the channels for customer complaints.

Additionally, BTN provides a privacy notice accessible on the Bank's official website, offering transparency on how customer data is used and protected. In line with this commitment, BTN does not sell, disclose, or provide customer data to third parties without the customer's consent.
