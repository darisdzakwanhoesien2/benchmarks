The decline in total savings accounts was due to the mass closure of inactive social assistance (bansos) accounts. However, considering the success of financial literacy initiatives in 2024, BTN plans to expand and enhance similar programs in 2025.

One of the key strategies includes introducing SimPel accounts in more schools across Indonesia, with the goal of reaching a wider student demographic in both urban and rural areas. BTN also plans to launch digital-based financial literacy programs, leveraging technology to reach underserved communities in remote locations.
