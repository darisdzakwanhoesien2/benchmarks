The BTN Service Mission was formulated as a detailed elaboration of the Service Vision following the workshop and consists of the following:

1. Fostering an integrated service culture within the organization by adopting a solution-driven approach to achieve service excellence.
2. Establishing consistent service standards across all service channels and customer touchpoints to ensure a seamless and exceptional experience in every interaction.

3. Becoming a trusted financial partner and housing advisor by providing signature services that reflect the authenticity of the organization.
4. Delivering the best service experience, focused on customer expectations, treating them with warmth and care as if they were part of the BTN family.

In addition to the Service Vision and Mission, Service Values have been established as a foundation for support:

![img-185.jpeg](img-185.jpeg)

The term FAMILY was chosen as the service value as it reflects reliable service (fostering and assurance), a uniquely differentiated approach (meaningful and inspiring), and a convenient experience (lighthearted and yearning). The concept embodies BTN's commitment to engaging with customers as part of a family.

As a foundation to support the Service Mission and enhance customer acquisition and loyalty, the Bank has established core values. BTN's core values are defined as Solution-centric, providing added value to customers through basic advice for new customers and advanced advice for existing and churning customers.
