# Emissions of Ozone-Depleting Substances, Persistent Organic Pollutants, and Other Significant Air Emissions

As a company focused on providing financial and investment services, BTN's business activities are not relevant to ozone-depleting substances, persistent organic pollutants (POPs), volatile organic compounds (VOCs), particulate matter (PM), and other significant air emissions. In this regard, BTN has not conducted calculations related to these pollutants. However, BTN remains committed to managing environmental impacts by reducing its carbon footprint and supporting sustainability-driven initiatives. One of these initiatives is

the gradual reduction in refrigerant use as an additional coolant for AC systems. BTN continues to collaborate with partners to support programs that provide positive benefits to the environment in a sustainable manner. [GRI 305-1, 305-2, 305-3, 305-5, 305-6, 305-7] [TCFD Metrics.a, c]

# Water Management

Water is a vital resource for the Bank in carrying out daily operations. It is mainly used for domestic office needs, cleaning toilets, watering plants, and filling fountains. Some of these water needs are fulfilled through a Regional Water Utility Company (PDAM), while others are sourced from deep wells.

BTN recognizes the environmental risks associated with water, including effluents, scarcity, and flooding. Therefore, BTN pays special attention to water management, referring to Law No. 17 of 2019 concerning Water Resources. BTN is committed to using water wisely, aiming to support sustainability without compromising water availability in the surrounding environment. [GRI 3-3]

To maintain ecosystem balance and ensure clean water availability in the future, BTN has implemented efficient and sustainable water management, including:

1. Using PDAM water as the primary source in compliance with local regulations regarding groundwater use;
2. Constructing infiltration wells with a capacity of 48 m³ each, resulting in a total rainwater absorption capacity of 96 m³;
3. Installing water-saving devices on toilets to conserve water;
4. Implementing a sewage treatment plant (STP) system to ensure that discharged water does not harm the environment and has obtained disposal permits from relevant authorities. The waste treatment facility at the BTN Tower has a capacity of 260 m³/day, currently operating at 220 m³/day. These figures align with the Wastewater Disposal Permit issued on July 19, 2018;
