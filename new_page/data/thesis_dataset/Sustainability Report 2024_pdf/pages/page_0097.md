# Paper Usage

In BTN's operational activities, paper is one of the main components supporting activities such as correspondence, memo writing, printing company reports, account registration, transaction recording, and as proof of transactions through ATMs. BTN recognizes the high paper usage in operational activities and its negative impact on the environment, including deforestation and increased waste. Therefore, BTN continuously manages paper waste. [GRI 3-3, 306-1]

BTN implements digital banking to address the challenge of reducing paper usage for environmental sustainability. Digitalization initiatives minimize document use and manage paper usage as follows: [GRI 3-3, 306-2] [OJK F.1]

a. eProc (e-Procurement), digitalization of the end-to-end procurement process as a step toward reducing paper usage (paperless);
b. Monthly account E-statements, proposing two enhancements: the automatic delivery of monthly account statements to customer emails and the option for on-demand account statement requests.;
c. Virtual Debit Card Feature in BTN Mobile to reduce physical ATM card production and minimize paper use in card application processes at Branch Offices;
d. BTN Mobile SBN Feature that allows individual customers to access, purchase, and manage SBN (Government Bonds) investments entirely online;
e. Correspondence activities are conducted using electronic mail (email), eliminating the use of faxes;
f. iFlow, a document management workflow application facilitating information access among divisions and branch offices, covering distribution and disposition processes of documents or letters;
g. Smart Branch, implementing Smart Branch to optimize digitalization and minimize the use of paper forms;
h. Rumah Murah BTN, providing convenience for communities to find affordable housing through the latest digital technology, reducing paper usage in its application process;

i. iLoan, an application used for customer loan processing;
j. iWealth, an application supporting the growth of Bank BTN's business products, including bancassurance, mutual funds, and ORI/SUKRI;
k. Smart Collection, a mobile application for collection officers to conduct surveys and handle customer collections directly via mobile devices;
l. B-Gate, an application for employee attendance, including management activities such as work-from-home monitoring, leave requests, and others;
m. Digital Room for E-Learning Modules (DREAM), an application enabling employees to take part in online learning sessions;
n. iRecruitment, a web-based application used by the Bank to handle recruitment processes for prospective employees;
o. Operational Risk Tools (ORMIS), an integrated system covering operational risk management tools, including RCSA, LED, and KRI;
p. PPDigital, an integrated system for managing internal BTN policies accessible to all employees;
q. iWork, a board of directors' e-MoM application for managing meeting minutes and creating meeting notes;
r. e-Bank Guarantee, an application enabling online issuance of Bank Guarantees, optimizing services and enhancing efficiency;
s. e-Deposito: Offering the option of Deposit ownership proof in the form of Deposit Certificates and Advisories, providing flexibility for customers and reducing reliance on physical documents.

Another initiative undertaken by BTN involves reusing paper for internal memos and shredding important documents that are no longer in use with a paper shredder, collaborating with third parties to repurpose paper that does not fall under the document retention category. At this time, BTN has not utilized eco-friendly materials in its operations, such as recycled paper, nor does it accept recycled material inputs or use reclaimed products. [GRI 3-3, 301-1, 301-2, 301-3, 306-2] [OJK B.1, F.5]
