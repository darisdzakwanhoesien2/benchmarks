# Learning and Competency Development

In human resource management, competency development, both in technical skills (hard skills) and non-technical skills (soft skills), is a crucial element. Efforts to develop competencies are also outlined in Special Policy Number KK.3-A regarding the Human Capital Cycle. This policy emphasizes that employees must continuously develop competencies to perform their work more effectively and efficiently. Of course, the Bank provides training and development based on each employee's role and responsibilities. Furthermore, competency development is also intended to prepare employees to face potential challenges and changes in the workforce and prepare them for higher or strategic positions. BTN also firmly upholds the principle of equality to ensure that all employees, both men and women, have equal opportunities to learn and develop their competencies. Therefore, human capital management focuses on improving technical skills and developing character and interpersonal abilities necessary to face career challenges and opportunities in the future. [ORI 3-3]

To support competency development, BTN has program focuses on three main elements: knowledge, skills, and attitude. By making the Bank a learning organization, BTN encourages employees to continuously improve themselves to perform tasks more effectively, be prepared for change, and prepare for higher positions. To support competency development, BTN applies a systematic approach through the concept of analysis, design, development, implementation, and evaluation (ADDIE), which allows for the design and implementation of measurable development programs. Learning management is also aligned with the Bank's job and business needs using the 70:20:10 approach, where 70% of learning is obtained through practical experience, 20% from interactions with others, and 10% from formal education.

BTN runs an ongoing human resources development program to meet the growing competency and expertise needs. This program is designed with various approaches, including education, training, special assignments, and transfer and promotion programs. All these efforts aim to enhance employees' skills and experience to adapt to changes and increasingly complex job demands. # Employee Talent Development Strategy

In 2024, BTN reaffirmed its commitment to talent development through strategic initiatives by collaborating with third parties to support employee competency development. In addition to enhancing competencies, innovation, and creativity, external development also broadens professional networks, meets standards and regulations, and increases knowledge of industry developments. Below are the strategies and programs implemented by the Bank for employee development in 2024:

1.  Employee Scholarship Program

BTN has an outstanding scholarship program open to all employees to support the development of human resources competencies and capacities. This scholarship is aimed at undergraduate and postgraduate programs, both domestic and abroad, to broaden employees' perspectives and expertise in relevant fields. As part of this commitment, BTN has also partnered with several leading universities, both domestically, such as the University of Indonesia (UI), Master of Management at Gadjah Mada University (MM UGM), and the School of Business and Management at Bandung Institute of Technology (SBM ITB), as well as internationally such as: The University of Sydney, Monash University, International Institute of Management Development (IMD), The University of Melbourne, The University of Edinburgh, serta University of Bristol.
