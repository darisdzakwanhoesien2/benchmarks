Additionally, the Bank assigns Regional Legal Representatives (RLR) to each regional office to provide legal assistance in litigation cases, represent the Bank in legal proceedings, offer legal opinions, and conduct legal watch activities in accordance with Bank Indonesia regulations. RLRs are also responsible for managing and mitigating legal risks within their regions to ensure effective legal risk mitigation efforts.

### Reputation Risk

BTN manages reputation risk through identification, measurement, monitoring, and control involving all business units. Each unit acts as the first line of defense in managing customer relationships.

To identify risks, BTN monitors customer complaints and negative publicity through the Customer Complaint System (SPN), which integrates with branch offices and the Contact Center. Risk measurement includes assessing factors that affect reputation, such as negative publicity and customer complaints.

Monitoring is conducted by the Risk Management Division, which provides recommendations for improvements based on reputation risk evaluations. Control is handled by the Corporate Secretary Division and the Customer Service Division, which resolve customer complaints in line with SLA targets to ensure effective mitigation of reputation risks.

### Strategic Risk

Strategic risk refers to the potential loss arising from inaccurate or inappropriate strategic decisions, as well as the failure to anticipate changes in the business environment. This risk may occur due to strategies that are not aligned with the Bank's vision and mission, incomplete or inadequate strategic plans across organizational levels, or failure to anticipate changes in technology, macroeconomic conditions, competitive dynamics, or policy changes from the government and regulators, such as Bank Indonesia and the Financial Services Authority (OJK). These factors contribute to strategic risk.

BTN identifies strategic risks through the analysis of functional activities, such as credit, treasury, investments, operations, and services, outlined in the business plan by the Corporate Strategy & Planning Division, based on general policies established by the Board of Directors. Strategic risk measurement is conducted by comparing actual results against predetermined performance targets, monitored through Key Performance Indicators (KPIs). Monitoring also involves the Risk Management & ESG Division, which evaluates risk exposures relative to the Bank's Risk Appetite and Risk Tolerance limits.

Strategic risk control is carried out by analyzing discrepancies between actual reports and business plan targets. The Corporate Strategy & Planning Division regularly submits improvement actions to the Board of Directors. The Board of Commissioners, Board of Directors, and relevant divisions review fundamental strategies, business transformations, infrastructure, and human resource management. Reviews of Risk Appetite and Risk Tolerance are conducted at least annually to ensure the Bank's strategies remain relevant and adaptive to the dynamics of the business environment.

### Compliance Risk

The Bank manages compliance risk through the Compliance Regulatory Management System (CRMS) application, which enables employees to access external regulations and provides alerts for reporting obligations to regulators. The Compliance and Governance Division (CMGD) is responsible for monitoring compliance risks, preparing a list of external obligations, and ensuring that internal policies comply with applicable regulations.

For enhanced oversight, Regional Compliance Supervisory Officers (RCSO) in regional offices evaluate branch compliance with regulations and standard operating procedures (SOPs). Compliance risks are reported to the Board of Directors regularly or as required.

Compliance risk measurement is conducted by evaluating the potential impact of non-compliance with regulations. Parameters used include types of breaches, frequency, and compliance history. The Risk Management Division coordinates with the Compliance Division to ensure effective compliance risk management and mitigation actions are implemented when necessary.
