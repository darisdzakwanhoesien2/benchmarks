# Three Lines Model

The Three Lines Model is a critical framework for ensuring effective risk management while supporting the Bank's transparent and integrity-driven governance. This model clearly defines the roles and responsibilities of different organizational lines, all of which contribute to corporate sustainability, including Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) aspects.

# 1. Role of the Governing Body

The governing body bears the primary responsibility to stakeholders for ensuring all organizational activities are conducted with transparency, integrity, and strong leadership. As the highest supervisory authority, it is responsible for formulating policies and ensuring effective risk management to achieve long-term objectives, incorporating ESG principles, such as environmental and social sustainability.

# 2. Role of the First Line

The first line serves as the operational risk manager within the organization. It is tasked with ensuring that established strategies are effectively implemented and that all business decisions are made based on thorough risk analysis and resource optimization. The primary focus is on

achieving organizational objectives and effectively managing risks associated with every activity and operations. The first line also ensures compliance with internal policies and supports operational sustainability by integrating ESG considerations into every decision and process.

# 3. Role of the Second Line

The second line provides specialized support for risk management, focusing on critical areas such as legal and regulatory compliance, internal controls, and sustainability. This line plays a vital role in upholding the ethical standards, business process standards, and organizational conduct are followed, and managing issues related to technology, information, and sustainable quality. It also has broader responsibilities in Enterprise Risk Management, overseeing potential risks that could threaten business continuity and ensuring compliance with applicable laws and regulations.
