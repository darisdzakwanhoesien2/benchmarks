# BTN Sustainable Ecosystem

(OJK A.1, F.1)

Efforts to build a national economy that prioritizes harmony among economic, social, and environmental aspects require proper and adequate resource management. As a banking institution, BTN recognizes its strategic function and role in ensuring that the investments it manages contribute to a sustainable future and have a broad impact on economic growth, environmental conditions, and social well-being.

BTN remains committed to promoting Sustainable Finance by integrating Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) aspects into its business and operational activities. This involves planning on how to manage environmental impacts, address social issues, and maintain ethical governance standards across all its operations. Since 2019, Sustainable Finance has been an integral part of BTN through the issuance of the Sustainable Finance Action Plan (SFAP), which refers to OJK Regulation No. 51 of 2017 on the Implementation of Sustainable Finance for Financial Services Institutions, Issuers, and Public Companies.

BTN firmly believes that sustainability is not a destination but a journey involving various interconnected parties and aspects. The Bank is committed to building and managing an integrated sustainability ecosystem, where social, environmental, and economic factors work in unison to create a lasting positive impact. BTN is confident that long-term value creation for stakeholders can only be achieved through the harmonious collaboration of innovation, social responsibility, and prudent resource management.

To advance Sustainable Finance and support ESG performance, BTN has conducted comprehensive mapping of its sustainability ecosystem, encompassing various aspects, including principles, frameworks, governance, processes, and achieved outcomes. BTN focuses on addressing ESG-related issues in the mortgage sector to achieve long-term sustainability and responsible banking practices.
