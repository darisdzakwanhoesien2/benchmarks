# BTN Smart Residence

BTN Smart Residence is a platform designed to connect residents and property managers for bill payments, fees, information exchange, and complaint resolution.

- Web-based platform for Residence Admin/Property Management
- Mobile-based platform (Android and iOS) for Residents (End Users)

## Benefits of BTN Smart Residence

### Benefits for Property Managers

- Easy
Simplifies bill reconciliation for property managers.
- Prosper
Provides economic benefits for property developers/ managers.
- Communication Platform
Facilitates information updates related to the residence.

### Benefits for Residents

- Convenient bill payments
Enables residents to pay bills anytime, anywhere.
- Complaint submission platform
Allows residents to quickly report complaints to the property manager.
- Residence maintenance fee payments
Facilitates payments for management fees, parking fees, and other residential maintenance costs.

## Features of BTN Smart Residence

- Residence and Tenant Management
Property managers can organize residents based on clusters/towers, blocks/floors, and unit numbers.
- Billing Management
Property managers can input and manage individual residents' bills.
