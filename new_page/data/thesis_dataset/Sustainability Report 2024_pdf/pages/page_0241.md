# Nomination Process for the Highest Governance Body [GRI 2-10]

In the nomination process for members of the highest governance body, the Bank involves shareholders through the GMS mechanism. The appointment and dismissal of each member of the Board of Commissioners and Directors are conducted based on GMS resolutions. The selection and nomination processes are carried out transparently, taking into account recommendations from the Remuneration and Nomination Committee. When evaluating candidates for the Board of Commissioners and Directors, the Bank considers criteria such as integrity, honesty, professionalism, leadership experience, loyalty, and time availability to support the Bank's progress. Additionally, aspects of diversity, expertise, and experience in the fields of economics, environment, and social issues are also important considerations. The Bank ensures that candidates have no financial, familial, or business ties with members of the Board of Commissioners, Directors, or Controlling Shareholders that could compromise their independence. [GRI 2-10]

Based on the Guidelines and Rules of Procedure for the Board of Commissioners No. 18/KOM/BTN/2024, regarding the Guidelines and Rules of Procedure for the Board of Commissioners of PT Bank Tabungan Negara (Persero) Tbk, the Bank has established criteria and requirements for candidates for the Board of Commissioners, which include the following: [GRI 2-10]

1. Candidates eligible to be appointed as members of the Board of Commissioners must meet the requirements at the time of appointment and during their tenure following the provisions of the Bank's Articles of Association and applicable regulations.
2. Members of the Board of Commissioners must meet the capability and integrity assessment requirements under the regulations of the Financial Services Authority regarding the Fit and Proper Test for the Main Parties of Financial Services Institutions and must obtain approval from the Financial Services Authority.
3. At least one (1) member of the Board of Commissioners must reside in Indonesia.
4. Members of the Board of Commissioners who have fulfilled the Fit and Proper Test requirements must have:

a. Integrity;
b. Competence; and
c. A good reputation.

5. Independent Commissioner candidates must have:

a. Adequate knowledge and experience relevant to their position as Independent Commissioners; and
b. Experience in banking and/or financial services.

6. Former members of the Board of Directors or executive officers from entities with ties to the Bank must observe a cooling-off period of at least one (1) year before serving as Independent Commissioners.

7. Cooling-off periods are also required for:

a. Former Chief Executive Officers of the Bank; and
b. Former Directors or executive officers overseeing supervisory functions within the Bank, requiring a minimum of six (6) months before serving as Independent Commissioners.

8. Independent Commissioners must not have financial, managerial, ownership, affiliated, or family relationships with other members of the Board of Commissioners, Directors, or Controlling Shareholders that could affect their independence.
9. Independent Commissioners must not have direct or indirect business relationships with the Bank.
10. Independent Commissioners must not own shares in the Bank, directly or indirectly.
11. Independent Commissioners transitioning from affiliated commissioners must meet the requirements for Independent Commissioners.
12. Independent Commissioners transitioning from affiliated commissioners must observe a cooling-off period of at least one (1) year.
13. The transition of commissioners to Independent Commissioners must obtain approval from the Financial Services Authority by meeting the requirements set by regulations for Financial Services Institutions.
