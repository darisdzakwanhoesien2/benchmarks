# BTN Sustainability Governance Structure

[GRI 2-9, 2-10, 2-11, 2-12, 2-13, 2-18]

BTN has established a governance structure in accordance with the provisions set out in Law No. 40 of 2007 on Limited Liability Companies, which stipulates that the "Corporate Organs consist of the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS), the Board of Directors, and the Board of Commissioners." The formation of these corporate organs is designed to support the implementation of Good Corporate Governance principles through a structured division of roles and responsibilities, as well as the effective execution of

a checks-and-balances mechanism. In operational practices, the GMS, the Board of Commissioners, and the Board of Directors carry out their respective roles while respecting the boundaries of authority as defined by statutory regulations and the Company's Articles of Association. This structure not only reflects BTN's compliance with applicable laws and regulations but also emphasizes the strategic roles of each corporate organ in supporting the effective implementation of governance practices.
