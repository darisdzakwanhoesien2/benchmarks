# Sustainable Finance Action Plan (SFAP) [OJK E1, D1.b]

As a State-Owned Enterprise (SOE) and one of the largest banks in Indonesia, BTN is committed to continuously implementing Sustainable Finance principles through the development of the Sustainable Finance Action Plan (SFAP). BTN's SFAP serves as a strategic guide in developing financing portfolios for sustainable business activities, as well as steps in business processes and risk management.

In addition, BTN actively supports the government's program to achieve the target of three million homes, as part of efforts to strengthen the housing ecosystem in Indonesia. This support is manifested through inclusive and sustainable housing financing, particularly for low-income communities, in line with achieving the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

# Implementation of SFAP 2024

SFAP 2024 focuses on BTN's Five Main Pillars of Sustainable Finance, with programs and activities designed to support target achievement. As part of efforts to strengthen the national housing ecosystem, BTN prioritizes inclusive and sustainable financing for the housing sector, especially for low-income communities. In its implementation, BTN ensures compliance with prudential provisions and effective risk management,

in line with its commitment to building a housing ecosystem that supports community well-being.
