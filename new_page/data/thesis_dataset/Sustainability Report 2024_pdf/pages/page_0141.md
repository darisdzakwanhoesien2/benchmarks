# BTN Jakarta International Marathon

As part of BTN's commitment to achieving its vision of 'Become The ESG Champion' in 2024, the bank has integrated ESG principles through various initiatives focused on environmental sustainability, social impact, and governance. One of the key manifestations of BTN's commitment is the ESG Program, which is an integral part of BTN's Employee Value Proposition. This program provides employees with the opportunity to actively contribute to supporting the bank's sustainability efforts through various ESG-related activities.

A key activity supporting this initiative is the BTN Jakarta International Marathon (JAKIM) 2024, which serves as a platform to promote the ESG campaign. In this event, BTN involved internal employees as volunteers to maintain environmental cleanliness along the marathon track, and set up an ESG Corner Booth to provide information on BTN's sustainability programs and support local MSME empowerment. BTN also collaborated with several foundations to distribute donations that align with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), such as health, education, and the environment.

BTN further demonstrated its commitment to social equality by providing opportunities for disabled runners to participate in the event. Through these efforts, BTN not only shows an active role in environmental conservation but also focuses on social empowerment and inclusivity. The BTN JAKIM 2024 event stands as a tangible example of BTN's efforts in fulfilling its social and environmental responsibilities in line with ESG principles, while also supporting the company's goals to create a positive impact on society and contribute to a more sustainable world.
