The Bank has also implemented other policies related to OHS, including:

Prohibition of drug abuse, psychotropic substances, and addictive substances (narcotics);Prevention of the spread of infectious diseases; andManagement of third-party partners related to OHS.

BTN remains committed to creating a safe and comfortable working environment for all employees by taking preventive measures to maintain their welfare. One of the efforts is to consistently comply with all applicable regulations and standards, both government-mandated and internally adopted by the Bank. Additionally, BTN encourages every employee to actively participate in maintaining workplace safety and health. This encouragement is realized by providing various facilities, such as consultation services, reporting channels for risks and potential hazards, and transparent communication regarding implementing OHSMS and applicable OHS standards. However, BTN has not yet established a formal committee involving management and employee representatives to handle occupational health and safety issues in a more structured manner.

Consistent and well-planned implementation of OHSMS has yielded positive results, with BTN successfully maintaining a workplace free from accidents and occupational diseases. This success reflects the effectiveness of the safety policies applied by BTN. [GRI 403-2, 403-4, 403-9, 403-10]

## Harmonious Industrial Relations

BTN highly values the rights and freedoms of its employees to associate and assemble, which is in line with the Indonesian government's policy of ratifying 19 International Labour Organization (ILO) Conventions. This ratification reflects the country's commitment to international standards protecting workers' rights. Based on the regulations derived from this ratification, BTN provides space for employees to negotiate and actively participate in finding solutions to potential issues that may arise, including working conditions, welfare, and other matters. This provision aims to foster and maintain a harmonious relationship between the Bank and its employees.

BTN is committed to various regulations protecting workers' associate and organization rights. One of these is Article 28E Paragraph (3) of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia, which asserts that freedom of association, assembly, and expression is a human right; Law Number 21 of 2000 on Trade Unions, which states that in realizing freedom of association, workers have the right to form and develop trade unions; and International Labour Organization (ILO) Convention Number 87 of 1948 on Freedom of Association and Protection of the Right to Organize. [GRI 3-3]

BTN has complied with these regulations by establishing a workers' union on the 24 June 1999. This workers' union is officially registered with the DKI Jakarta Department of Manpower with number Kep-2241/W.26/K.2/1000, indicating that BTN legally supports and recognizes employees' rights to associate. Furthermore, the Bank ensures that the existing workers' union operates effectively and according to government mandates, as stipulated in the Ministry of Manpower Regulation No. 28 of 2014 on Procedures for the Establishment and Ratification of Company Regulations and the Creation and Registration of Collective Labor Agreements. The Bank provides freedom of association to all BTN employees, which includes: [GRI 407-1] The freedom to become a member of the BTN Workers' Union;The freedom to express aspirations through the BTN Worker's Union andThe freedom to gather in BTN Workers' Union activities.
