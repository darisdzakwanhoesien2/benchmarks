# Materiality Topic Determination Process

|  Identify Materiality Topic | Materiality Topic Assessment | Materiality Topics Review and Approval  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  • Identify materiality topics in line with BTN's ESG Roadmap by comparing with peer companies, global standards (GRI, SASB, OJK, IFRS, ESRS), and ESG ratings agencies (MSCI, Sustainalytics, S&P Global, CDP), with reference to previous reports and stakeholder feedback.
• These topics will then be further reviewed to identify 10 key topics. The results of the assessment will be used to develop a questionnaire to be distributed to external and internal stakeholders to obtain their views on the importance and priority of these topics. | • The determination of materiality topics uses the principle of Double Materiality Assessment (DMA) by involving stakeholders to assess the impacts and risks of materiality topics that affect the environment and society, and evaluate the extent to which these topics impact financial performance.
• Environmental and societal materiality (impact materiality): The assessment focuses on the impact of the topic on external stakeholders, including the environment and society.
• Financial materiality: The assessment focuses on the potential impact on the company's financial performance and business continuity. | After a thorough assessment, the ESG Committee and the Board of Directors conduct a review of the assessment results. Materiality topics that create value are then aligned with the strategy and relevance to the Bank's risks and opportunities.  |
