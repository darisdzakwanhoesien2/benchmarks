# Significant Changes [GRI 2-6] [OJK C.6]

BTN has undergone significant changes during this reporting period, including its vision, mission, and corporate logo updates. These changes reflect BTN's commitment to adapting to market dynamics and addressing increasingly complex social and environmental challenges. In addition to updating its corporate identity, BTN shifted its business direction. Previously focused primarily on mortgage financing (beyond mortgage), BTN has expanded its business scope to include other sectors to support more inclusive and sustainable economic growth.

## External Initiatives

As part of its commitment to sustainable finance and climate change response, BTN actively participates in external initiatives. The Bank adheres to various guidelines developed by both national and international organizations. BTN has also created a Sustainable Finance Action Plan (RAKB) by POJK No. 51/POJK.03/2017 on implementing Sustainable Finance for Financial Services Institutions, Issuers, and Public Companies.
