# Sustainable Procurement of Goods and/or Services

Sustainable procurement refers to procuring goods and services considering both price and quality and the long-term social, economic, and environmental impacts. Its objective is to support sustainability by reducing negative environmental impacts, ensuring fair working conditions, and promoting inclusive economic growth. Companies can select suppliers that adhere to ethical practices through sustainable procurement, use environmentally friendly products, and optimize resource utilization to achieve broader sustainability goals.

The Bank has established a Special Policy No. KK.9-H on the Guidelines for the Procurement of Goods and/or Services. Based on this policy, the Bank has set several requirements to support the implementation of sustainable procurement of goods and/or services while considering ESG aspects. The requirements include: [GRI 414-1] [FSS]

1. Take maximum measures to protect the environment, both within and outside the work location, and minimize damage and disruptions to the community or property caused by pollution, noise, or other harm.
2. Environmental Management Effort (UKL) and Environmental Monitoring Effort (UPL) documents.
3. Environmental Impact Analysis (AMDAL) documents.
4. Studies/documents on the quality/criteria of environmental damage.
5. Environmental permit documents.
6. Water discharge assessment documents.
7. A declaration document states the Company's commitment to implementing environmentally friendly procurement principles that prioritize environmental sustainability, fulfill social responsibilities, and adhere to good governance per applicable regulations.
8. A declaration document confirming the implementation of child and female employee protection measures.

9. Providing insurance for workers exposed to high risks.
10. Having anti-corruption policies and programs, including integrity pacts, if available, to verified compliance with applicable regulations.
11. Having an integrity pact or anti-corruption policy (if applicable).

To enhance supplier activities and process, BTN also conducts various activities aimed at maintaining the sustainability of the procurement process for goods and/or services, including:

1. **Quality and Quantity**
Monitoring the quality and quantity of goods or services received to ensure that the products or services meet agreed standards and that the required amounts match the contract.

2. **Timeliness**
Evaluating suppliers' compliance in meeting established deadlines to ensure timely procurement without disrupting operations.

3. **Responsiveness**
Assessing suppliers' ability to address requests or issues arising during contract execution, including their willingness and ability to adapt to changes.

4. **Flexibility**
Measuring the extent to which suppliers can adapt to changing needs or unforeseen conditions during the procurement period without compromising quality or efficiency.

5. **Price**
Evaluating the appropriateness of the offered price in relation to the quality and value of goods or services provided ensures that the price paid is proportionate to the benefits received and remains competitive.
