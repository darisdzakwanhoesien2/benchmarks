# Approaches in Sustainable Development

In its effort to support sustainable development goals, BTN implemented the Triple Bottom Line 3P (Profit, People, and Planet) as a core approach to evaluate sustainability performance. This approach ensures that the bank integrates economic, social, and environmental aspects into its operations.

However, in facing global challenges and in line with its commitment to the SDGs, BTN has adopted a broader approach through the 5P framework (People, Planet, Prosperity, Peace, and Partnership). This approach reflects the evolution of sustainability to be more inclusive and emphasizes the importance of collaboration and social stability in achieving sustainable development.

# Stakeholder Theory

Stakeholder Theory is a business management and ethics concept that requires companies to consider the interests of all groups affected by their activities, including employees, suppliers, customers, communities, and governments. This theory emphasizes that a company's responsibilities extend beyond shareholders to encompass the welfare of all stakeholders.

As a business entity, the Bank engages stakeholders across various operational and policy dimensions. Stakeholder engagement is critical for fostering inclusive and sustainable decision-making. By incorporating input from all relevant parties, the bank can effectively implement Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles. This approach not only prioritizes financial profitability but also ensures the social and environmental impacts of business activities are responsibly managed.
