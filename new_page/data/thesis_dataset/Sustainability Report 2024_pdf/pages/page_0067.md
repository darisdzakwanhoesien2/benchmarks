# Operational Areas and Markets Served

[ORI 2-1, 2-6] [OJK C.3]

As of this reporting year, BTN operates across Indonesia, with its Head Office in Jakarta and a network spanning 38 provinces.

By Financial Services Authority Regulation Number 12/POJK.03/2021, issued on 30 July 2021, regarding Commercial Banks, BTN has simplified the categorization of its network under OJK supervision. Previously, bank offices under branch offices were categorized into sub-branch offices and functional offices. BTN has standardized Cash Office status to Sub-Branch Offices and Payment Points to Functional Offices.

BTN's business growth and operational reach have expanded significantly, especially since it became a public company listed on the stock exchange under the BBTN ticker. As of 31 December 2024, BTN serves markets across 38 provinces with the following network: 8 Regional Offices, 82 Branch Offices, 35 Sharia Branch Offices, 536 Sub-Branch Offices, 75 Sharia Sub-Branch Offices, and 4 Functional Offices.
