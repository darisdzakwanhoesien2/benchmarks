# Fair Advertising Policy

As part of its commitment to good corporate governance and sustainable business practices, BTN implements a fair advertising policy (KK.9-Y) on Marketing Communication Activities to build trust with consumers and maintain business integrity. Transparent, accurate, and non-deceptive advertising is a top priority in marketing communications. The bank ensures that all information conveyed in advertisements is accurate, clear, and not exaggerated without accountable evidence. The language used must be easy to understand by consumers and free of superlative claims without valid data. Additionally, the company is committed to not displaying content that is discriminatory or harmful to specific groups in society.

To ensure fair advertising, the company sets communication standards that include the use of language in accordance with the Enhanced Spelling System (EYD) and prohibits terms such as "free" if

there are hidden costs. Information related to money-back guarantees must be explained transparently, and consumer testimonials may only be used if they have been verified with a written statement.

In the advertising process, BTN considers the appropriate audience, selects suitable media, and chooses strategic airing times to avoid negative impacts on consumers. Regular evaluations are conducted to measure the effectiveness of campaigns and ensure compliance with applicable policies. The results of these evaluations serve as the basis for improvements and innovations in future marketing communication strategies. To ensure transparency and accuracy, BTN routinely evaluates advertising content to prevent the spread of misleading or biased information. Additionally, the bank provides training to marketing and communication teams to ensure a deep understanding of ethics and integrity in conveying information. This training includes topics such as marketing ethics, advertising regulations, and corporate social responsibility.
