As a form of prudential principle implementation and risk management inherent in each priority industry, BTN applies the Risk Acceptance Criteria (RAC) to evaluate the eligibility of prospective debtors. BTN has established two types of RAC, namely General RAC and Specific RAC. General RAC serves as the fundamental criteria that must be met by all prospective debtors across all business sectors, while Specific RAC is designed to assess prospective debtors based on the unique characteristics of their business activities. Both types encompass three key aspects, namely Debtor, Technical, and Financial A, each of which has a different evaluation focus. # Credit Policy for High ESG Risk Sectors

As part of BTN's commitment to support sustainability, the Terms &amp; Conditions of the Portfolio Guideline (PG) is designed to manage credit risks related to ESG. This policy ensures that credit distribution is carried out selectively, particularly in priority sectors with high ESG risks, based on conducted ESG risk assessments. Through this guideline, BTN has established clear standards to identify, evaluate, and limit exposure to sectors that have the potential to cause significant negative environmental and social impacts.

Although BTN did not extend credit to the coal and mining sectors throughout 2024, the Bank remains committed to establishing specific terms and conditions for these sectors as part of its ESG-based credit risk management.
