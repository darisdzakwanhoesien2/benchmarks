# ESG Mutual Funds

As a mutual fund sales agent, BTN has established a strategic partnership with Batavia Prosperindo Aset Manajemen, one of Indonesia's leading investment managers, to market ESG-based mutual fund products. This collaboration is part of BTN's commitment to supporting the development of sustainable and socially responsible financial products, offering investment options that are not only profitable but also contribute to sustainability goals.

Some of the flagship products marketed through this partnership include Batavia Global ESG Sharia Equity USD, Mandiri FTSE Indonesia ESG, and Batavia Saham ESG Impact. All three are mutual funds that integrate ESG principles in their investment portfolios and comply with sharia principles. This product focuses on stocks of companies with a strong track record in environmental sustainability, social responsibility, and transparent corporate governance. By prioritizing companies that meet ESG criteria, this product promotes impact-driven investments that benefit both society and the environment.

Through this approach, BTN is committed to identifying climate-related risks and opportunities, establishing a clear green development strategy, and aligning its business model with the promotion of a low-carbon economy to support the achievement of sustainable environmental impacts.

As part of BTN's long-term strategy, this partnership aims to enhance sustainable financial literacy among domestic investors, educating them on the importance of responsible and sustainable investments. BTN will continue to expand ESG-based mutual fund product offerings in the future, aligning with the growing awareness and commitment to sustainability in the investment sector.

# Sustainable Business Activity Loan (KKUB)

As part of its commitment to sustainability and support for the ESG roadmap, BTN has developed a Sustainable Business Activity Loan (KKUB) scheme. In 2024, BTN successfully disbursed KKUB with a total loan portfolio of Rp183.93 trillion. These loans are allocated to support various sustainability-focused sectors, including:

1. Development of the renewable energy sector;
2. Construction of factories utilizing renewable technology through energy efficiency;
3. Sustainable management of biological resources and land use;
4. Drinking Water Supply System (SPAM) projects; and
5. Development of Transit-Oriented Development (TOD) housing integrated with transportation infrastructure and public facilities.

Support for sustainable finance is also implemented by BTN by channeling financing across various categories, including construction loans, investment loans, working capital loans, corporate loans, and short-term loans.
