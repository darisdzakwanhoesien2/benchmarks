# BTN Wins International Awards for ESG Innovation

![img-111.jpeg](img-111.jpeg)

PT Bank Tabungan Negara (Persero) Tbk (BTN) received two prestigious awards at the Global Retail Banking Innovation Awards 2024 organized by The Digital Banker, a Singapore-based global financial industry media and research institution. BTN won the Mortgage Product of the Year – ESG category for its innovation through the Low-Emission Housing program, which aims to build 150,000 environmentally friendly housing units by 2029. The program was recognized for driving circular economic activities by involving eco-friendly material producers, housing developers, and consumers. This achievement highlights BTN's dedication to mitigating climate change impacts and promoting sustainability values.

Additionally, BTN was honored as Highly Acclaimed for Best ESG Initiative for its efforts to integrate environmental, social, and governance principles into its ESG framework. BTN has also embedded ESG principles into its long-term strategy through its 2024-2029 ESG Roadmap, which emphasizes products and policies that support diversity, inclusivity, and sustainability. This includes increasing the proportion of women in senior management and involving people with disabilities. This international recognition serves as a strong motivation for BTN to continue strengthening its role as an ESG Champion in Indonesia's banking industry.
