# Use of Recycled Paper

![img-96.jpeg](img-96.jpeg)

As part of BTN's commitment to sustainability and environmental preservation, the paper used for printing this Sustainability Report is entirely made of recycled paper. This decision represents a concrete step toward supporting initiatives to reduce the use of natural resources and minimize negative environmental impacts.

BTN recognizes that the printing industry and paper usage significantly contribute to deforestation and pollution. Therefore, the Bank is committed to reducing its carbon footprint by utilizing paper made from recycled materials. By choosing recycled paper, the Bank not only reduces reliance on new natural resources but also contributes to reducing the volume of waste in landfills. The production process of recycled paper also lowers energy and water consumption, as well as carbon emissions, making it a more environmentally friendly option compared to using new paper.
