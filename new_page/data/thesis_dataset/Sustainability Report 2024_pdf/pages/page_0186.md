# Low-Emission Housing

BTN is committed to contributing to the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) by developing 150,000 low-emission houses by 2029. This initiative is a major step in BTN's efforts to support the transformation of Indonesia's housing sector into a more environmentally friendly and sustainable industry. In addition, this program aligns with government policies aimed at providing not only livable and healthy housing but also environmentally friendly homes, considering the increasing urgency of climate change as a global challenge.

Through the low-emission housing project, BTN aims to develop housing solutions that integrate sustainability principles, utilizing eco-friendly materials and applying green and smart technology solutions. Based on global housing development standards, BTN hopes to maximize its contribution to addressing the ongoing global climate crisis.

As part of its long-term plan, BTN targets the use of 30% environmentally friendly materials in these housing projects by 2029. In 2024, BTN initiated a pilot project incorporating a minimum of 10% eco-friendly materials. These materials include innovative components such as floor decking containing 3.6 kg of plastic waste and paving blocks containing 2 kg of plastic waste per square meter. This innovation not only helps reduce plastic waste but also enhances the durability and efficiency of construction materials used in these houses.

The low-emission housing initiative is expected to have a significantly positive environmental impact. By using eco-friendly materials and energy-efficient solutions, BTN estimates that it can reduce more than 1.7 million kilograms of plastic waste that would otherwise become landfill waste and cut carbon emissions by 2.42 tons of CO₂. This impact is equivalent to planting 110,000 trees, which will help absorb carbon dioxide from the atmosphere and improve environmental quality around the housing area.

Furthermore, this initiative is expected to absorb carbon emissions across an area of 323 hectares, providing significant benefits for local ecosystem sustainability. In addition, BTN is committed to prioritizing efficient and sustainable resource management principles, encouraging housing developments to meet the following standards:

- Energy Efficiency
Houses constructed must have proper ventilation, high ceilings, and a window-to-wall ratio of 15% to 30%. This aims to enhance natural lighting and air circulation, thereby reducing energy consumption for cooling and artificial lighting.

- Water Usage
BTN applies efficient water usage equipment, such as low-flow faucets and improved sanitation systems, which help reduce water wastage and support better water resource management.
