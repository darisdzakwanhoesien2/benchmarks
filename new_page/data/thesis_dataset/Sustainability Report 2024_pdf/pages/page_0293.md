## Reporting Breaches [GR 2-26]

BTN implements a Violation Reporting System Policy or Whistleblowing System (WBS) based on the Financial Services Authority Regulation (POJK) No. 39/POJK.03/2019 issued on December 19, 2019, regarding the Anti-Fraud Strategy Implementation for Commercial Banks. The WBS system is an integral part of the Bank's internal control efforts, aimed at preventing irregularities

or breaches in operational activities. Through the WBS, BTN strives to detect any irregularities or breaches that may harm the Bank, both from a reputational and legal standpoint. Additionally, the policy aims to create an open and transparent work culture, where employees can report potential breaches without fear or concern.
