## Share Ownership Policy by Members of the Board of Directors and Commissioners

The Board of Commissioners and Directors have disclosed information regarding their share ownership and/or that of their families, amounting to 5% or more in BTN or other companies, both domestically and internationally. This measure is taken to ensure transparency and minimize potential conflicts of interest that could affect corporate governance. Additionally, it reflects BTN's commitment to adhering to the principles of Good Corporate Governance, which is a critical component in implementing sustainability principles.

Regarding share transactions by the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners, BTN adheres to Financial Services Authority (OJK) Regulation No. 11/POJK.04/2017 on the Reporting of Share Ownership or Any Changes in Share Ownership in Public Companies, as well as PT Bursa Efek Indonesia (IDX) Board of Directors' Decision No. KEP-00066/BEI/09-2022 concerning Amendments to

Regulation No. I-E on Information Disclosure Obligations, and the Bank's Articles of Association. Members of the Board of Directors and Board of Commissioners are required to report their share ownership and any changes thereof to OJK—whether direct or indirect—no later than 10 days after the transaction occurs.

As of December 31, 2024, none of BTN's Board of Commissioners or Directors held shares with total ownership of 5% or more, either in BTN or other companies. This demonstrates that there is no significant influence from share ownership on their independence in carrying out their duties and responsibilities, including during strategic decision-making processes. This transparency serves as concrete evidence of BTN's commitment to maintaining integrity and ensuring the Company is managed free from conflicts of interest.
