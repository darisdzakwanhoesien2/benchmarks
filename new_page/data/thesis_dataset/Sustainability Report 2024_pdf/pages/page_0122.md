## 2. Human Resource Capability Development

BTN is committed to continuously developing human resource capabilities through advanced training programs and leadership development. In this regard, BTN collaborates with several leading institutions. These collaborations aim to provide training relevant to industry needs while sharpening leadership skills to prepare employees for increasingly complex challenges in the banking sector. With the support of these institutions, BTN ensures employees gain quality learning experiences and continuously improve their competencies through structured and practical programs.

## 3. BUMN School of Excellence

BTN plays an active role in the BUMN School of Excellence program, which aims to prepare employees as potential candidates for future Directors. This program is designed to identify and develop the best potential among BTN employees, allowing them to attend intensive leadership training and development. Through participation in this program, BTN ensures that employees are technically prepared and possess strong leadership capacities to lead the Company.

## 4. Certification

To support formal recognition of employee competencies, BTN collaborates with professional certification bodies, including the Risk Management Certification Agency (BSMR), the Banking Professional Certification Institute (LSPP), the Islamic Finance Professional Certification Institute (LSP KS), the Indonesian Capital Market Financial Industry Professional Certification Institute (LSP IKEPAMI), and the ACI Financial Markets Association (ACIFMA). Through these certifications, BTN provides employees the opportunity to validate their professional abilities in line with industry standards, thus enhancing the overall quality of human resources to meet the dynamics of the financial industry.

The Bank monitors and evaluates these programs regularly through the senior management team to ensure their effectiveness and alignment with the Company's business strategy. With this initiative, BTN aims to build a strong and sustainable talent pipeline, which will be the foundation for the Company's growth and sustainability in the future. This effort also represents BTN's contribution to supporting the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), particularly in quality education, decent work, and economic growth.
