# Pulp &amp; Paper

![img-203.jpeg](img-203.jpeg)

The Bank establishes credit policies that require customers in this sector to:

A. Hold Environmental Management System certifications, such as ISO 14001/ISO 45001 or other equivalent documents recognized by the Bank.
B. Hold a minimum PROPER Blue rating and/or at least have an Environmental Impact Assessment (AMDAL) report or Environmental Management and Monitoring Effort Report (UKL-UPL) (including reclamation and post-operations guarantees) approved by the Environmental Agency (BLH).
C. For public companies, they must have a sustainability report.
D. Implement energy efficiency measures and efforts to reduce emissions, with adequate electricity and water supply availability.
E. Have a Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP), Industrial Waste Treatment Plant (IPLC), and Hazardous and Toxic Waste (B3) treatment facilities, proven through documents recognized by the Bank.
F. Hold permits for industrial forest management, such as Forest Product Utilization Business Permit (IUPHHK) for Timber and/or Non-Timber Products (IUPHHBK) and

Forest Management Work Plan (RKU) approved by the Ministry of Environment and Forestry.

G. Not engage in business activities in areas designated as high biodiversity conservation zones.
H. For pulp &amp; paper trading, companies must provide self-declaration statements to fulfill principles and commitments, including:

1) Selling products or services that do not harm the environment.
2) Implementing more environmentally friendly, efficient, cost-effective, and energy-saving distribution processes.
3) Allocating budgets for environmentally friendly marketing efforts.
4) Avoiding excessive resource consumption.
5) Holding sustainability certifications, such as Forest Stewardship Council (FSC), Programme for the Endorsement of Forest Certification (PEFC), or equivalent environmental sustainability certifications.
6) No deforestation practices, including land clearing, and no exploitation.

# Biodiversity

![img-205.jpeg](img-205.jpeg)

In the industrial forest sector, Bank BTN has established a binding credit policy for customers in this sector to:

1. Have documents for the management of industrial forest permits such as Business Permit for Forest Timber Product Utilization and/or Business Permit for Non-Timber Forest Product Utilization, and an

approved Business Work Plan (RKU) from the Ministry of Environment and Forestry.

2. Not engage in business activities in areas declared to have high biodiversity, including RAMSAR land material sites and IUCN Cat.1&amp;2 sites.
