# Ratio of Highest to Lowest Salary [ORI 2-21]

BTN strives to distribute remuneration proportionally and to avoid creating significant gaps. The Bank does not implement an unconditional variable remuneration policy (RBV), as such policies do not consider employees' length of service. As part of its commitment, BTN applies a competitive, fair, and proportional remuneration system to ensure that all employees receive compensation according to government regulations. The remuneration policy is routinely reviewed and updated to align with industry dynamics and the Bank's business strategies. Additionally, compliance with banking regulations related to remuneration is implemented. The ratios of the highest to the lowest salaries over the last three years are as follows: [ORI 2-21]
