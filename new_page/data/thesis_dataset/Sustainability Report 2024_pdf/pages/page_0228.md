Proposals for the appointment or replacement of Board members by the Board of Commissioners to the GMS are based on recommendations from the Remuneration and Nomination Committee.

Members of the Board of Directors serve a term of five (5) years, which may be extended for one (1) additional term. Their appointment is formalized through a GMS resolution and takes effect on the date specified in the GMS resolution until the conclusion of the fifth Annual GMS following their appointment. This provision complies with applicable laws and regulations in the capital market sector. Nevertheless, the GMS holds the authority to dismiss members of the Board of Directors before their term ends, provided it adheres to the prevailing legal provisions in the capital market sector.

The Board of Directors is responsible for executing all management actions and overseeing BTN's operations in alignment with the Bank's objectives and goals. The Board represents the Bank both in and outside the court while adhering to applicable laws, the Articles of Association, and resolutions from the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS). In managing the Bank, the Board of Directors is led by the President Director, who acts as the primary coordinator. The President Director ensures effective leadership and strategic decision-making at the Board level to support the Bank's vision, mission, and long-term objectives.
