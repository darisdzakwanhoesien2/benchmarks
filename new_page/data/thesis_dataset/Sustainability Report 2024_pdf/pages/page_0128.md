## Job-Specific Development Programs

In addition to supporting general competency and performance development, BTN is committed to providing training programs tailored for specific job functions. These job-specific development programs are an integral part of the Bank's strategy to create skilled human resources capable of tackling the increasingly complex challenges of the industry and contributing maximally to achieving the Bank's objectives. These programs enhance technical skills and knowledge relevant to employees' tasks and responsibilities in specific positions. As flagship initiatives, these programs aim to produce professionals who are not only competent but also adaptable to the rapidly evolving industry.

In 2024, BTN introduced 418 virtual training programs covering business, credit risk, leadership, and sales and service. This initiative ensures all employees can access technical, managerial, and technology-based innovation training tailored to their needs.
