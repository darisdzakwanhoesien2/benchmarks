## 2. Violence

Acts of violence include any actions, behaviors, or conduct that involve threats, coercion, violence, or arbitrary deprivation of another person's rights in the workplace. These actions may occur publicly or privately and can result in physical, psychological, sexual, or economic harm or damage to the targeted individual. Such behavior has the potential to create an unsafe work environment and negatively affect employee well-being.

## 3. Harassment

Harassment encompasses any attitudes, words, actions, behaviors, or gestures that occur directly in the workplace or through various communication channels contrary to laws or regulations related to human rights. This behavior is typically aimed at intimidating or adversely affecting the physical, psychological/mental, sexual, or economic aspects of the victim. Its impacts may cause someone to feel threatened, humiliated, offended, degraded, or embarrassed, creating a sense of insecurity or stress in the work environment. Consequently, individuals who experience such behavior may struggle to perform their duties and feel they are working in an unconducive or unsupportive atmosphere. Furthermore, such actions may pose serious risks to employee safety, health, and well-being, both physically and mentally.

BTN launched a series of webinars in 2024 on Respectful Workplace Policy as part of the Bank's commitment to fostering mutual respect in the workplace. This policy ensures that everyone at BTN, including the Board of Commissioners, the Board of Directors, and employees, contributes to creating a dynamic, friendly, productive, and discrimination-free work environment, free from violence and harassment. The policy seeks to establish an inclusive workplace atmosphere, value differences, and equality, and uphold human rights. In doing so, BTN encourages performance, growth, and sustainability through:

1. Appreciating and respecting diversity in the workplace, which compromises individuals from various ethnic, racial, national, skin color, age, religion, gender, disability, and individual characteristic backgrounds, to create a productive work atmosphere.
2. Ensure that every Bank employee is treated equally, regardless of their characteristics, and has fair and equal access to facilities and infrastructure.
