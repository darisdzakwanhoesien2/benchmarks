# BTN Cash Management

BTN Cash Management is a service that enables institutional customers to conduct various financial and banking transactions via the internet. These transactions can be performed online and in real-time, without time or location constraints, in a secure and convenient manner.

## Features of BTN Cash Management include:

- Account information
- Funds transfer
- Payment
- Purchase
- Liquidity Management
- MPN Payment Management
- Pertamina Service

# BTN CRM (Cash Recycle Machine)

Unlike ATMs, which only provide cash withdrawal services, CRM (Cash Recycling Machine) enables customers to conduct both cash withdrawals and deposits, facilitating transactions that are more convenient, efficient, and real-time. In delivering these services, BTN is fully committed to ensuring equitable service quality for all customers, including those with specific needs, such as individuals with disabilities. This commitment is demonstrated through the provision of inclusive facilities and infrastructure, such as ATMs and designated lanes equipped with Braille features for visually impaired customers, as well as dedicated access routes for customers using wheelchairs. Thus, Bank BTN ensures that all customers, without exception, can enjoy services comfortably and easily. [FS14]
