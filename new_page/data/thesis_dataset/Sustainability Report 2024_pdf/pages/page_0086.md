# Renewable Energy Usage

BTN recognizes that most of the energy used is fossil-based, which negatively impacts the environment and contributes to climate change. As part of its commitment to improve sustainable business practices, BTN has gradually increased the use of renewable energy sources. As of 2024, 7 (seven) BTN offices have installed solar panels, with energy generation capacity reaching 231 kWp. The 7 (seven) offices that have used solar panels are Karawang KC, Medan KC, Gorontalo KC, Balikpapan KC, Cilegon KC, Bandung KC, and Tower 1 BTN.
