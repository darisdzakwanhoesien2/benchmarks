# Distinguished Shareholders and Stakeholders,

We extend our deepest gratitude to God Almighty for His blessings and guidance, which have enabled PT Bank Tabungan Negara (Persero) Tbk, hereinafter referred to as "BTN" or "the Bank," to successfully navigate 2024 with a commendable performance. Amidst global economic challenges, BTN has consistently demonstrated resilience and commitment in delivering added value for stakeholders. As part of our commitment to transparency and accountability, we are pleased to present the 2024 Sustainability Report, marking the sixteenth edition since its initial publication in 2009.

This report reaffirms our concrete steps in strengthening a sustainable housing ecosystem-based business, while enhancing the Bank's contribution to housing finance for the people. Through initiatives such as the Low-Emission Housing Program, the Bank actively supports the transformation of Indonesia's housing sector towards greater sustainability. This initiative is also aligned with the government's policy of providing decent, healthy, and environmentally friendly housing, addressing global climate change challenges.

As part of the Bank's commitment to ensuring business sustainability, BTN has taken strategic measures, including the implementation of material topics based on the Double Materiality Assessment, ensuring the relevance of sustainability issues in strategic decision-making. Additionally, we are also working to strengthen transparency and accountability.

As a testament to our responsibility and commitment to transparency, this report provides a comprehensive overview of BTN's economic, environmental, social, and governance performance. Furthermore, it outlines the Bank's achievements, strategies, and sustainability commitments throughout the year. More than just a compliance document, this report reflects BTN's strong support for the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) in line with Presidential Regulation No. 59 of 2017.

# Responding to Sustainability Challenges [GRI 2-22, 2-23, 2-24]

In responding to sustainability challenges, the implementation of the Sustainable Finance Action Planhas become one of the key drivers for the success of development in the banking sector. BTN recognizes that sustainable finance plays a crucial role in addressing global challenges across macroeconomic, social, and environmental aspects. Therefore, integrating the Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) roadmap into the Company's structure is a critical step to ensure that all activities and policies adopted not only support economic growth but also generate positive impacts on society and the environment in a sustainable manner.

BTN believes it is on the right path toward achieving its new vision of becoming the "The Leading Partner in Financially Empowering Indonesian Families". BTN is committed to adapting to changes in the banking and property sectors while delivering the best financial services for society. In addition to mortgage financing, the Bank also supports inclusive and sustainable economic growth by integrating sustainability principles into every strategic initiative. BTN strive to expand access to financial services, not only in the housing sector but also across other industries that contribute to Indonesia's economic growth, while ensuring positive impacts for future generations.

# Realizing Sustainable Business [GRI 2-22, 2-23, 2-24]

BTN is committed to supporting sustainable growth in Indonesia by adopting the "Become The ESG Champion" concept as its foundation. BTN believes that integrating sustainability principles and innovation across all business lines will generate positive impacts on the economy, environment, and society. The Bank not only adapts to changes but also strives to be a pioneer in creating a sustainable future. By integrating the balance of the 5P principles (Planet, People, Peace, Partnership, Prosperity), BTN is confident in creating long-term sustainability value for the nation.
