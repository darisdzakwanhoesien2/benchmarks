# BTN's Commitment to the Principles for Responsible Banking

BTN has officially become a signatory of the UN Principles for Responsible Banking (PRB), a unified framework for sustainable banking, developed through a global partnership between banks and the United Nations Environment Program Finance Initiative (UNEP FI). These principles serve as a key framework to ensure that banking strategies and practices align with the vision for the future, as outlined in the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and the Paris Agreement.

![img-61.jpeg](img-61.jpeg)

Principles for Responsible Banking

As the first Indonesian state-owned bank to adopt the PRB, BTN is committed to strengthening its sustainability strategy by integrating aspects into its core business operations. The initiative also represents a strategic step in supporting innovation and initiatives in advancing sustainable finance. BTN has adopted the six core principles of the PRB, which serve as guidelines for its operations and decision-making, as follows:

![img-62.jpeg](img-62.jpeg)

Principle 1: Alignment

BTN will align its business strategy to ensure consistency and contribution to the needs of individuals and societal goals, in accordance with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), the Paris Agreement, and relevant national and regional frameworks.

![img-63.jpeg](img-63.jpeg)

Principle 4: Stakeholders

BTN will proactively and responsibly consult, engage, and partner with relevant stakeholders to achieve societal goals.

![img-64.jpeg](img-64.jpeg)

Principle 2: Impact and Target Setting

BTN will continuously enhance positive impacts while minimizing negative impacts and managing risks to society and the environment arising from the Bank's activities, products, and services. Therefore, BTN will set and publish targets that allow for the most significant impact.

![img-65.jpeg](img-65.jpeg)

Principle 5: Governance and Culture

BTN will implement its commitment to these principles through effective governance and a responsible banking culture.

![img-66.jpeg](img-66.jpeg)

Principle 3: Clients and Customers

BTN will work responsibly with its clients and customers to promote sustainable practices and support economic activities that create shared prosperity for the present and future generations.

![img-67.jpeg](img-67.jpeg)

Principle 6: Transparency and Accountability

BTN will regularly review the implementation of these principles, both individually and collectively, and remain transparent and accountable for the positive and negative impacts, as well as BTN's contribution to societal goals.
