The GRC Roadmap is designed to provide a strategic framework that enables BTN to address regulatory, risk, and governance challenges in an integrated manner. Through a proactive approach, the roadmap ensures that the organization can identify, evaluate, and mitigate risks effectively to maintain operational and financial stability.

Additionally, the roadmap integrates compliance with evolving regulatory requirements, enabling BTN not only to fulfill legal obligations but also to leverage opportunities presented by policy changes to enhance competitiveness. By strengthening corporate governance structures, the roadmap promotes transparency, accountability, and efficiency, which serve as the foundation for sustainable business growth.

As part of the GRC Roadmap, BTN formulated its Corporate Plan 2025--2029, comprising four key interconnected elements that provide clear direction for developing the Bank's performance. These elements are designed not only to strengthen BTN's position in Indonesia's housing market but also to contribute to inclusive and sustainable economic growth. The four elements are: Vision, Mission, and Values that reflect BTN's fundamental goals and core principles;BTN 2029 Aspiration to Achieve the Vision, which outlines the Bank's aspirations and ambitions for the next five years;BTN Plan: 1+3+5 Strategic Initiatives, which detail a structured approach and key priorities for achieving the vision; andStrategic Initiatives, which describe specific initiatives implemented to support the Bank's strategy.

BTN has established a clear vision and mission to strengthen its strategic foundation and achieve its long‐term objectives. BTN's new vision for 2025 is to become Indonesia's primary financial empowerment partner for families. This reflects a commitment to helping Indonesian families manage their finances better, providing greater access to financial services, and promoting family economic empowerment. To realize this vision, BTN has outlined five key missions as strategic guidelines: Becoming a government partner in promoting housing and financial inclusion.Delivering the best customer experience through integrated digital and financial services.Enhancing shareholder value through sustainable profitability growth.Becoming the home of Indonesia's best talents.Implementing effective governance practices and sustainable business innovations to improve community welfare and environmental sustainability.

In addition to its mission, BTN has outlined the 2029 Aspiration to Achieve BTN's Vision, including the following objectives: Developing a Sustainable Savings Engine to Accumulate Total Savings of Rp120 TrillionCapturing 3% of savings from the 20 largest provinces, launching innovative propositions for payroll, first‐time homebuyers, and professional entrepreneurs, and becoming the transactional bank for customers and businesses by enhancing banking transaction capabilities. Empowering Indonesian Families through Housing Loan Distribution to Realize the Dream of Homeownership with a Target of Rp450 TrillionSupporting approximately 1.2 million subsidized mortgage (KPR) customers and 290,000 non‐subsidized mortgage customers, as well as empowering around 27,000 SMEs related to housing through propositions that cover the entire business lifecycle. Becoming a Financial Partner for Consumers and Businesses by Providing a Comprehensive Range of Everyday Banking SolutionsIncreasing consumer product ownership through high‐margin credit (with a target of 8% of the loan portfolio), investment and payment solutions, expanding the portion of non‐housing loans (with a target of 20% of the loan portfolio), and increasing fee‐based income (FBI) (~20% of revenue); enhancing the conversion of mortgage customers to full‐service banking users (largely as primary bank customers). Building a High‐Quality Portfolio with Operational EfficiencyDeveloping efficient and digital operations (target CIR <50%) while strengthening a customer‐centric culture across the bank; maintaining low NPL levels (target NPL <2.3%) across various credit segments, supported by AI‐driven underwriting and collection processes.
