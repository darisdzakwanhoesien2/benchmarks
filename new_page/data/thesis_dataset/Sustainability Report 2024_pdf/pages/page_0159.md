# Micro and Small Business Funding Program (PUMK)

The Bank has a strategy to support the development of capacity and potential for micro and small businesses through the Micro and Small Business Funding Program (PUMK). This program is a key component of the CSR initiative. The main focus is to enhance the competence and capability of small businesses, making them more independent and competitive, thus contributing positively to the local economy and the well-being of surrounding communities. Additionally, the program aims to assist small businesses with potential but facing difficulties in accessing financing through conventional banking channels.

The Bank provides funding through the Micro and Small Business Funding Program (PUMK). PUMK is a type of loan offered by the Bank to small businesses that have not met the requirements to obtain financing from traditional banking institutions (unbankable). The distribution of these funds is carried out by considering the business sector and the geographical area in which the businesses operate, ensuring that the funds are targeted and can provide the maximum impact. During this reporting period, the Bank disbursed Rp14.85 billion in loans to 498 partners managed by Bank BTN. These funds were distributed through a collaboration with third parties responsible for managing and disbursing the funds to beneficiaries, with a fund allocation of Rp10 billion.
