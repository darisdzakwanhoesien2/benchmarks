The Bank continues to transform to enhance sales capacity and customer-oriented services. Currently, a crucial role in providing direct services to customers is offered through branch offices, sub-branch offices, and other networks. As technology evolves, the Bank's services must also adapt and shift toward digital banking solutions. One form of transformation is integrating digital services into branch offices through the implementation of BTN Digital Store.

BTN Digital Store aims to offer customer-centric services. The implementation is expected to deliver a unique service experience, focusing on transaction convenience, effectiveness, and efficiency, ensuring a practical and secure experience. Additionally, BTN Digital Store is helping to increase sales capacity and enhance employee productivity in service and sales activities. To support the implementation of BTN Digital Store, adjustments will be made to organizational structures, roles, and responsibilities within branch and sub-branch offices, and other

networks, including the exclusive authority of BTN Digital Store services.

There are three types of BTN Digital Store branches:

1. Flagship Branch

Flagship Branch is the most comprehensive type of BTN Digital Store, designed to attract customers to the bank while reinforcing BTN's brand image as a modern and innovative bank.

2. Digital Oriented Branch

Digital Oriented Branch is a type of BTN Digital Store designed to introduce digitalization to selected branch locations.

3. Digital First Branch

Digital First Branch is a type of BTN Digital Store designed to provide banking services to customers in strategic locations such as shopping centers, transit hubs, central business districts, campuses, and so on.
