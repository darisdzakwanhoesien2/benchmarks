# Implementation of the Prudence Principle [ORI 2-23]

## Know Your Customer (KYC) and Know Your Employee (KYE)

To strengthen the implementation of prudence principles in its operations, BTN has adopted two primary approaches, namely Know Your Customer (KYC) and Know Your Employee (KYE), which focus on operational risk management across various business activities of the Bank. KYC, which was previously used to verify customer identities, has now evolved into customer due diligence (CDD), a more comprehensive process involving identification, verification, and monitoring activities for customers or prospective customers. This process aims to ensure that all transactions conducted align with the customer's profile, whether they are new customers, walk-in customers (WIC), or

already registered customers, in accordance with the regulations set forth in POJK No. 23/POJK.01/2019, which amends POJK No. 12/POJK.01/2017, updates several provisions related to the implementation of Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Countering the Financing of Terrorism (CFT) programs in the financial services sector. To support the effective implementation of prudence principles, the Bank provides training for its employees, focusing on CDD-related material containing case studies relevant to prudence principles. This initiative aims to enhance employee understanding and awareness of the importance of vigilance in every transaction conducted.

## Anti-Money Laundering (AML), Counter-Terrorism Financing (CTF), and Prevention of Financing the Proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction (PFWMD)

BTN implements the AML (Anti-Money Laundering), CTF (Counter-Terrorism Financing), and PFWMD (Prevention of Financing the Proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction) programs in its customer onboarding process in compliance with applicable laws and regulations in the banking sector. As part of its efforts to enhance the effectiveness of these three programs and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements, the Bank has established a Special Work Unit (SWU) focusing on AML, CTF, and PFWMD at the Head Office. This unit is responsible for supervising and ensuring that these programs are properly implemented across all Business Units. Additionally, the implementation of

these programs at the regional and branch office levels is the responsibility of each Regional Office Head and Branch Manager.

Apart from implementation at the Head Office, BTN also ensures that the AML, CTF, and PFWMD programs are effectively carried out at Regional Offices and Branch Offices. With comprehensive implementation, the Bank strives to improve the effectiveness of these programs so they can operate optimally while ensuring that all business activities comply with regulatory requirements, thereby maintaining adherence to applicable laws.

## Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Counter-Terrorism Financing (CTF) Policy

BTN has comprehensive policies and strategies for implementing anti-money laundering (AML), counter-terrorism financing (CTF), and the Prevention of Financing the Proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction (PFWMD), which have been established by the Board of Directors and adhere to national and international standards. These policies are designed to ensure that every transaction and activity carried out by BTN is free

from any association with money laundering, terrorism financing, or financing the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction. BTN is committed to maintaining clean, transparent, and accountable practices by involving all employees and business partners in training programs to ensure effective understanding and implementation of these policies, thereby creating a safe financial system that prevents misuse.
