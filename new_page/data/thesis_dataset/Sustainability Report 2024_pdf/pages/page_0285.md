# Fair Treatment Policy for Shareholders

As part of managing potential conflicts of interest, BTN has established a policy related to insider trading, which is regulated under Special Policy Number KK.9-EE on Investor Relations Activities as well as the Code of Conduct for the Board of Directors and the Board of Commissioners. These regulations strictly prohibit Bank Insiders from engaging in insider trading through the purchase or sale of the Bank's securities or those of other companies transacting with BTN. The term Bank Insider refers to the following individuals:

1. Commissioners, Directors, or employees of the Bank.
2. The Bank's major shareholders.
3. Individuals who, due to their position, profession, or business relationship with the Bank, have access to inside information.

4. Individuals who, within the last six months, no longer fall under the categories mentioned in points 1, 2, and 3 above.

The prohibition on Bank Insiders from trading BTN securities is based on the principle of transparency in the capital market, ensuring equal access to information for investment decision-making. Bank Insiders are strictly prohibited from trading BTN securities if they possess material non-public information (inside information or material facts). Transactions may only be conducted after such material facts have been officially disclosed to the public
