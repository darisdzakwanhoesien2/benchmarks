# BTN Sustainability Report Content Determination Process

![img-94.jpeg](img-94.jpeg)

A review of the report's content is the next step, aimed at ensuring compliance with the eight (8) principles of Sustainability Reporting recommended by the GRI, including:

1.  Accuracy
The information presented must be accurate and detailed to enable stakeholders to evaluate the Company's performance effectively.

2.  Balance
The Company's performance must include both positive and negative aspects to ensure it is assessed rationally and comprehensively.

3.  Clarity
The information presented must be easy to understand and accessible to stakeholders.

4.  Comparability
The information presented must be consistent and allow stakeholders to assess changes in the Bank's performance over a specific time frame by including data and information for several years preceding the reporting period.

5.  Completeness
The report must provide sufficient information to allow for the assessment of the Company's impact over the reporting period.

6.  Sustainability
The information presented must report the impact in the broader context of sustainable development.

7.  Timeliness
The report must be published regularly so that stakeholders can make timely decisions based on the information provided.

8.  Reliability
The report must include and utilize verified information and processes in preparing the report, reflecting the quality and materiality of the information presented.
