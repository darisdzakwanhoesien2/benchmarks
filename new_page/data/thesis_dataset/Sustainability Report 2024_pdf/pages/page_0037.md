# Awards in 2024

Throughout 2024, BTN achieved various awards that reflect its accomplishments and commitment across multiple areas. A complete list of these awards can be found in BTN's Annual Report. Among the recognitions received, several are related to BTN's performance and efforts in Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG), underscoring the bank's dedication to sustainability and social responsibility.
