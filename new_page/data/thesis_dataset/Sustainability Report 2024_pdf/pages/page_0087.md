# Emission Management

## Greenhouse Gas Emissions

As a company contributing to greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions, BTN is committed to supporting efforts to reduce them by placing GHG emission management under the Climate Change and Ecosystems pillar, setting a target to reduce emissions by at least 30% from operational activities over the next five years (2023-2029). BTN has contributed to supporting the government in achieving carbon neutrality (Net Zero Emission/NZE) by 2060, in line with the Enhanced Nationally Determined Contributions (NDC) target of 43.20%. To strengthen its commitment to reducing the negative impacts of climate change risks, BTN is in the process of setting a Net Zero Emission target. Additionally, BTN has developed an ESG roadmap for 2023-2029, which includes energy efficiency targets, digitalization to reduce paper usage, and operational efficiency improvements in the short, medium, and long terms. [GRI 3-3]

BTN considers GHG emissions inventory an essential step to measure emissions generated by activities that potentially contribute to global warming (Global Warming Potential/GWP), including CO₂, N₂O, CH₄, and CFC. The results of the emissions calculation are presented in CO₂ equivalent units (CO₂ eq). In line with the initiatives implemented previously, BTN continues to take strategic steps to reduce generated emissions. This year, BTN realized various initiatives, such as increasing solar panel adoption and expanding the use of electric vehicles. As of this reporting period, BTN has installed solar panels in seven offices and utilized electric vehicles. [GRI 3-3]

As part of its broader sustainability efforts, BTN has established an ESG Committee led by the President Director, with members consisting of the Board of Directors and SEVPs. This committee plays a key role

in supporting the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and the company's climate targets, while ensuring that sustainability policies encompass environmental, social, and governance (ESG) aspects. The committee also oversees ESG-related risks and opportunities and makes strategic decisions during its annual meetings. During the reporting year, the ESG Committee held five (5) meetings. Additionally, BTN has an Enterprise &amp; ESG Risk Management Division, led by the Head of Division, which is directly responsible for implementing and monitoring BTN's ESG initiatives, such as carbon emission reduction, supporting social aspects, and conducting Climate Risk Stress Tests to assess the impact of climate change on the company's sustainability. Through these efforts, BTN is committed to continuously integrating sustainability principles into its operations and creating long-term positive impacts for society and the environment.
