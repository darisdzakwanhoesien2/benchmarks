In accordance with the Service Blueprint, improvements will focus on the aspects of People, Process, and Physical Evidence to establish new standards across all service channels.

The implementation plan for the BTN Service Roadmap 2023-2025 is divided into:

1. Quick Win
- Solution-Centric Response Mechanism
- Service-focused Squad
- FinEase Acceleration
- BTN Care

2. Mid-Term
- Service Competency Enhancement Program (S-CEP)
- BTN Mobile Advancement
- CRM Empowerment
- BTN Housing &amp; Mortgage Advisory

3. Long-Term
- Hackathon
- BTN Heritage

Based on the Service Programs Prioritization established during the BTN Service Roadmap 2023-2025 Workshop, four Quick Win service programs have been agreed upon for execution under the Roadmap's Pilot Project, namely:

1. Solution-Centric Response Mechanism
Implementation of service recovery procedures when BTN provides services below the required standard, and development of a service recovery room for customers submitting hard complaints.

2. Service-focused Squad
Formation of a Service-Focused Squad to assist customers without requiring direct interaction with Customer Service (CS) and tellers, and establishment of a service organization to implement service standards and foster a learning culture and environment.

3. FinEase Acceleration
Acceleration of the account opening process, improvements in consumer credit agreement service standards, standardization of branch office premises (layout adjustments, queue machines, and Loan Services uniforms), and script development.

4. BTN Care
Ensuring the availability of facilities for people with disabilities, the elderly, pregnant/nursing mothers, and children, such as disability ramps, handrails, wheelchairs, walking aids, priority seats, kids' corners, baby-feeding rooms, accessible toilets, guiding blocks, and other necessary amenities.

The Pilot Project has been approved for implementation at five (5) branch offices (both Conventional and Sharia), namely the Bekasi Branch Office, Tangerang Branch Office, Karawang Branch Office, Bandung Sharia Branch Office, and Yogyakarta Sharia Branch Office.
