# Respectful Workplace Policy

BTN has established guidelines to create a safe work environment that respects and protects human dignity, emphasizes mutual respect, and is free from discrimination, exclusion, harassment, and physical or mental violence for all employees and stakeholders. These guidelines are outlined in Special Policy Number KK.3-C, on Employment Regulations and Administration. The primary aim of this policy is to create an inclusive, conducive, and productive work environment that promotes the Bank's sustainability and upholds human rights. To achieve this, the policy includes preventive measures designed to avoid various issues related to unfair treatment or employee harm, covering:

1. Discrimination

BTN is fully committed to implementing an anti-discrimination policy by adopting principles, policies, and practices of diversity and gender

inclusion. This includes avoiding all forms of differentiation, marginalization, restriction, and/or exclusion, whether directly or indirectly. The mentioned discriminatory actions can be based on various factors such as religion, ethnicity, race, group, social class, and social status, including marital status, economic status, gender, language, political preferences, and disabilities. All forms of discrimination can negatively impact or even eliminate the recognition of individuals' human rights and fundamental freedoms. As a result, it may create inequality in opportunities and differential treatment toward specific individuals or groups within the Bank's activities. Therefore, BTN is committed to avoiding all forms of injustice in social interactions and all activities involving employees in the workplace.
