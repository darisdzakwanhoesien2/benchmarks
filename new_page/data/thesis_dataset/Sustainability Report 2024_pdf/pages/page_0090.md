In managing Scope 3 emissions, BTN has implemented various strategies, including enhancing engagement with business partners to disclose GHG values from emissions generated by products transacted to and from the Bank. In 2024, BTN developed the 2024-2029 ESG Roadmap, outlining various initiatives to reduce the company's overall emissions. Some of the initiatives already implemented include the digitalization of operational activities to reduce paper usage, the implementation of sustainable procurement, sustainable waste management, and the application of a Portfolio Guideline that contains ESG principles. These measures ensure that BTN's credit portfolio avoids funding sectors with high environmental risks while helping to keep financed emissions under control.
