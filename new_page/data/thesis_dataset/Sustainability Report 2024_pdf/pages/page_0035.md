BTN Impact Radar

The BTN Impact Radar illustrates the results of an impact analysis supported by the Portfolio Impact Analysis Tool, specifically focusing on areas and impact topics relevant to BTN's portfolio, such as the housing and financial sectors. BTN applies this approach to assess the impact of its financial activities, with a particular emphasis on its consumer portfolio, which accounts for 70% of the Bank's total portfolio. This analysis has identified two key areas with significant impact—

Finance and Housing—which are strategic priorities for BTN in supporting sustainable development.

In the Finance sector, the analysis shows that BTN has successfully promoted financial inclusion by supporting financing for low-income communities.
