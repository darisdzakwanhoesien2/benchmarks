Sustainable Environmental Management

Employee Development and Welfare Support

To support sustainability, BTN also provides guidance and supervision to suppliers to ensure they meet sustainability standards in the procurement process. These activities are conducted through letters and socialization sessions to ensure suppliers understand and comply with sustainability principles, such as reducing environmental impacts, implementing fair social practices, and maintaining high ethical standards throughout the procurement process.

Additionally, in its procurement process, the Bank ensures that all goods and services are procured through procedures adhering to Good Corporate Governance (GCG) principles, namely transparency, accountability, responsibility, independence, and fairness. BTN regularly conducts audits and evaluations to ensure all procurement stages are conducted fairly and efficiently, free from potential misuse, and support sustainable resource management in compliance with applicable regulations.

As part of these efforts, during the reporting period, BTN also strengthened its engagement with suppliers through a Vendor Gathering event, presented as a breakfast meeting on 4 September 2024, with the theme "Implementation Integrated End to End Procurement." The event addressed important topics such as introducing the End-to-End Procurement application, module presentation, and implementation phases. This initiative reflects the Bank's commitment to promoting improved ESG performance throughout its supply chain, focusing on digitalization, paperless operations, transparency, and monitoring.
