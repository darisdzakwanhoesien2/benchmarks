3. Policy on Employee Conduct Standards
- Employees must avoid conditions, situations, or impressions of a conflict of interest or abuse of authority.
- Members of the Board of Commissioners, Directors, and Bank employees are prohibited from giving or receiving anything of value, whether directly or indirectly, from business partners that could influence decision-making.
- The Bank is committed to engaging in fair and ethical business competition in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. The Bank avoids monopolistic practices, provides equal opportunities to all business players, and promotes sustainable business innovation while complying with all prevailing laws and regulations.
- Bank organs and employees must adhere to laws, regulations, and Bank policies.

4. Implementation and Enforcement
All BTN employees are obligated to report any breaches or deviations from the Corporate Code of Conduct to the Human Capital Management &amp; Culture Specialist Division. BTN guarantees that the reporter's identity will be protected with full confidentiality to avoid retaliation or negative repercussions. Upon receiving the report, the Human Capital Management Division conducts a thorough analysis and submits its evaluation

to the Board of Directors and/or Commissioners, depending on their responsibilities and authority. Based on the assessment results, the Board of Directors and Commissioners will decide the necessary actions, such as coaching, disciplinary sanctions, or corrective actions. In addition, the Bank continues to develop and establish preventive procedures and policies that all supervisors must follow within the work environment to ensure that similar breaches do not recur in the future. [GRI 2-16]

The Business Ethics and Code of Conduct document is accessible to all parties through BTN's official website to ensure transparency and accountability. This code not only outlines the expected standards of conduct within a business context but also emphasizes respect for human rights, in line with the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights (UNGP BHR). It affirms the Bank's commitment to operating responsibly and upholding the rights of individuals in all business activities. Furthermore, all decisions and actions taken by the Bank adhere to the principles of prudence, meaning that every decision and activity is conducted with careful consideration and accountability. BTN's Code of Ethics also requires all parties involved to perform due diligence, including customer verification, business partner inspections, and assessments of other related parties, to ensure compliance with ethical standards and laws established by BTN. [GRI 2-23]

Compliance with the Code of Conduct

The Code of Conduct applies to all members of the highest governance bodies and employees in every business activity of the Bank, including interactions with stakeholders. The implementation of this code is regulated under the Joint Decision Letter of the Board of Commissioners No. 01/SKPB/KOM/BTN/2024 and Board of Directors No. SKPB-01/DIR/BTN/2024 concerning the Code of Conduct of PT Bank Tabungan Negara (Persero) Tbk. The Bank requires every individual in the organization to sign an Integrity Pact, aimed at ensuring the Code of Conduct is executed with full

accountability. The successful implementation of the Code of Conduct is the responsibility of the leaders in each work unit, who must ensure that all employees understand and properly adhere to the code within their work environment.

BTN implements the Code of Conduct comprehensively, covering education, campaigns, application, reporting, and follow-up actions for Code of Conduct breaches. [GRI 2-23]
