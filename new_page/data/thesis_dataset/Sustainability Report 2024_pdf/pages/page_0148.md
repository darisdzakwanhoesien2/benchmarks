# Social and Economic Compliance

Social and economic compliance is essential in carrying out BTN's responsibilities. As part of this commitment, the Bank is determined always to respect and adhere to various ethical standards and applicable legal regulations to ensure that all operational activities are carried out responsibly. By maintaining compliance, BTN protects the business from potential legal risks and non-monetary sanctions and strengthens the trust of stakeholders, including customers, employees, partners, and the public, in the Bank's integrity and credibility.

BTN has a Special Number KK9-AG on the Occupational Health and Safety Management System (OHSMS) to ensure social compliance. Through this policy, BTN is fully committed to:

1. Preventing and reducing work-related accidents and occupational diseases by involving management, bank employees, and/or trade unions;
2. Creating a safe, comfortable, and efficient workplace to promote productivity;
3. Supporting the creation of added value for the Bank; and
4. Enhancing independence, responsibility, and professionalism.

BTN is committed to complying with applicable labor regulations and established occupational health and safety (OHS) standards to ensure social compliance. In this regard, BTN implements policies that support employee welfare and fair operational sustainability, such as employing workers with transparent and regulated contracts, prohibiting forced labor or underage workers, and upholding equal treatment for employees and customers without discrimination. Additionally, the Bank strives to create a safe, comfortable, and healthy work environment by applying OHS principles in its operations. On the other hand, BTN also prioritizes the economic sector, which is reflected in the application of healthy competition, the prohibition of monopoly practices, and an anti-corruption stance. The Bank also ensures that the wages given to employees meet reasonable standards and reflect the value of their contributions. [GRI 3-3] [SASB FN-CB-510a.1]

BTN's commitment to implementing best practices and adhering to social and economic regulations and provisions is evident in the absence of fines or non-monetary sanctions. Furthermore, BTN has not reported any cases to Indonesia's dispute resolution mechanisms. These mechanisms include labor, product legality, and other disputes. [GRI 2-27, 419-1]
