## Enhanced Due Diligence and High Risk Jurisdiction

BTN implements the principle of Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) and countermeasures that are appropriate and proportional to manage and reduce risks arising from business relationships, transactions, prospective customers, walk‐in customers (WIC), and/or customers originating from countries classified as high‐risk, as identified and published by the Financial Action Task Force (FATF). To monitor High‐Risk Jurisdictions, the Bank utilizes the AML,CTF, and PFWMD systems accessible to all staff involved in this oversight, including front‐line personnel such as customer service representatives.

Based on the list of countries deemed high‐risk under FATF regulations, BTN is committed to implementing appropriate preventive measures, such as countermeasures and Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD), and reporting any suspicious activities through the AML,CTF, and PFWMD applications. This approach applies to prospective customers, customers, beneficial owners, conductors, and/or walk‐in customers (WIC) originating from countries or jurisdictions categorized as High‐Risk Jurisdictions subject to a Call for Action, according to the risk level set by FATF. As such, all business relationships and transactions with these parties undergo stricter scrutiny. Additionally, BTN applies EDD to prospective customers, customers, beneficial owners, conductors, and/or WICs from countries or jurisdictions identified as having significant deficiencies in anti‐money laundering and counter‐terrorism financing systems, as determined by FATF. This reflects BTN's proactive efforts to identify and mitigate potential risks arising from relationships with these parties, ensuring adherence to applicable regulations and high standards of prudence.
