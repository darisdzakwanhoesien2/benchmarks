# Internal Control Over Financial Reporting (ICOFR)

As part of efforts to improve sustainable corporate governance, BTN has completed a Diagnostic Review of its Internal Control Over Financial Reporting (ICOFR). This process represents a strategic initiative aligned with the directives of the Ministry of State-Owned Enterprises (KBUMN) based on KBUMN Letter No. S-4/DKU.MBU/02/2024. As part of efforts to strengthen corporate governance, BTN's ICOFR Diagnostic Review project is implemented through a systematic approach consisting of four main stages:

## Stages of Diagnostic Review for ICOFR at BTN

1.  Mapping BTN's corporate structure and assessing risks of material misstatements due to structural complexity. The results serve as a basis for understanding potential risks in financial reporting.

2.  Identifying significant accounts in BTN's financial statements. Each account is analyzed to assess the risks attached, ensuring all elements affecting internal controls are appropriately mapped.

3.  Conducting diagnostic procedures to evaluate the effectiveness of internal controls in various areas. This process includes interviews with process owners, document reviews of policies and procedures, and in-depth analysis of control implementation.

4.  Comprehensive improvement recommendations are drafted. An implementation roadmap is also designed as a strategic guide for executing improvements in a phased and measurable manner.

Throughout the process, the diagnostic procedures include in-depth interviews, regulatory documents and supporting materials reviews, and identifying areas of control that require improvement. This approach ensures that each step contributes significantly to strengthening BTN's internal controls to support transparent and accountable corporate governance.

In conducting the Diagnostic Review of Internal Control Over Financial Reporting (ICOFR), BTN identified 13 key areas as primary focuses, covering Entity Level Control, IT General Control, and processes in financial, credit, treasury, and HR functions. This process is designed to provide a comprehensive assessment of the effectiveness of internal controls across various operational lines of the Bank.

## Diagnostic Review Procedure for ICOFR at BTN

- Understanding processes and controls through interviews with Process Owners and reviews of policy and procedure documents.
- Workthrough
- Controls identified are confirmed through supporting documents for implementation.
- Confirmation
- Reviewing samples of transaction documents to assess the adequacy of controls within a transaction cycle.
- Document Review
- Identifying control areas that require improvement and obtaining confirmation from Process Owners for approval.
- Identification of Areas for Improvement
