Employee Development and Welfare Support

#

In addition to retaining the best talent, applying the Bank's remuneration system also aims to meet the needs for human capital. This remuneration serves as a reward and encourages optimal and sustainable performance in line with the Bank's strategic objectives. With a performance and risk-based approach, BTN ensures that remuneration is directly linked to the employee's work results, whether individually, within a work unit, or at the Bank's overall performance level. In line with this approach, the wage structure and scale implementation are adjusted according to applicable regulations. All these factors are then aligned with the Bank's financial capacity to ensure that the remuneration provided is in balance with the Bank's financial ability.

BTN is committed to ensuring employee welfare through a fair, transparent, performance-based remuneration policy. The salary structure ensures that employees' minimum salary remains above the Regional Minimum Wage while adhering to government policies by the Job Creation Law Number 6 of 2023 on the Establishment of Government Regulations in Lieu of Law No. 2 of 2022 on Job Creation on Minimum Wage by consistently adjusting employee salaries. Additionally, employee salaries are reviewed at least once a year. BTN also strives to maintain competitive wages by ensuring the salary percentile is above P60 at all job levels. This remuneration policy aims to retain the best talent and encourage optimal performance in line with the Bank's strategic achievements. [GRI 202-1] [OJK F.20]

With a performance and risk-based approach, the remuneration is directly linked to employee work results, whether at the individual level, work unit level, or the Bank's overall performance. The wage structure and scale are adjusted according to applicable regulations and the Bank's financial capacity. BTN also emphasizes that the remuneration policy does not differentiate based on gender, race, ethnicity, religion, or other backgrounds and provides remuneration based on a person's grade, performance, and position without discrimination.

In line with the Bank's principle of promoting gender equality in wage management, the average salary between men and women is the same. The median salary comparison between male and female employees is 1:1. This represents the Bank's commitment to providing equal pay without gender discrimination.

This policy demonstrates BTN's efforts to oversee the fair distribution of compensation, whether for employees with the highest salaries or most other employees, to create an inclusive and equitable working environment.
