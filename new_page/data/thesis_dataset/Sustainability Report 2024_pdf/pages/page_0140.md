# BTNers Club

BTNers Club is a platform that coordinates various sports, arts, and spiritual communities for all BTN employees. Currently, BTNers Club oversees 26 communities designed to accommodate the interests and hobbies of BTN employees, fostering a balance between personal life and work (work-life harmony). This initiative is expected to encourage innovation, creativity, and collaboration, ultimately enhancing productivity and sustainable performance at BTN.

As part of the Employee Wellbeing Program (EWP), BTNers Club aligns with the Employee Wellbeing policy implemented at state-owned enterprises (BUMN). Through this club, the bank strives to create a more collaborative work environment that supports the wellbeing of all employees, fostering a productive and harmonious atmosphere.

BTNers Club plays a key role in driving the social pillar of the EWP program by providing a platform for employees to develop skills, collaborate, and engage in beneficial activities outside of work. The available communities offer a space for exploring interests and personal development, which is expected to improve overall wellbeing and inspire individuals to innovate in their work.
