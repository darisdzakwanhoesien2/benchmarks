# Human Resource Management

BTN has a strategy to realize its vision through the mission of “Becoming the Home of Indonesia’s Best Talent.” This strategy is implemented through short- and medium-term human resource (HR) development programs aligned with the BTN Corporate Plan for 2024-2029, as follows:

1.  Ability to Attract Top Talents
Becoming a top-of-mind employer for external candidates from outside the Bank and leading universities in Indonesia.

2.  Ability to Retain Top Talents
Retaining top talent to grow and develop with BTN.

3.  Talents’ Capability Readiness
Equipping talent with the capabilities to achieve the Bank’s current and future strategic goals.

4.  Employee Productivity
Achieving employee productivity levels above the average of prominent organizations.

To achieve these aspirations, BTN has formulated the following strategies and initiatives:

1.  Become the top workplace choice for the best talent.
2.  Strengthen HR capabilities, productivity, and competitiveness through integrated employee development programs aligned with current and future business needs and
3.  Focus on value creation and becoming a business partner.

BTN implements various human capital strategy initiatives as part of its strategy and initiatives, including:

1.  Aligning remuneration systems and incentive programs to attract, retain, and motivate employees.
2.  Implementing multi-sourcing strategies and running orientation programs for new employees and internal transfers.
3.  Activating the four pillars of Employee Value Proposition (EVP) — Learn, Grow, Contribute, and Engage — to enhance BTN’s reputation.
4.  Managing succession planning to ensure the fulfillment of critical/key positions and address generational gaps.
5.  Detailing dual career paths (specialist and general) for critical positions in alignment with BTN’s strategic goals.
6.  Implementing predictive and productivity-based workforce planning.
7.  Applying systematic productivity measurement methodologies and metrics for business and support segments.
8.  Establishing integrated performance management principles and governance, including multi-source performance appraisals to ensure comprehensive objectivity.
9.  Refining learning strategies, implementing consistent mentoring, and conducting interventions to foster a learning culture.
