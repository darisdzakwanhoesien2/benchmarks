# Oversight for Ethics Issues

In an effort to reinforce good governance and ensure compliance with ethical principles, BTN has implemented strict oversight of fraud-related issues and ethical breaches through the Anti-Fraud Subcommittee, which operates under the Risk Management Committee. This subcommittee plays a strategic role in establishing policies, strategies, and a fraud risk management framework aimed at preventing, detecting, and addressing potential breaches. The Anti-Fraud Subcommittee comprises both permanent and non-permanent members from various directorates, including Risk Management, Operations, Information Technology, and Legal, with the Head of the Operational and Digital Risk Management Unit serving as the secretary.

As part of its commitment to fraud risk management, the Anti-Fraud Subcommittee is responsible for implementing necessary corrective measures and reporting significant decisions to the Board of Directors. In carrying out its duties, the committee develops fraud risk policies and tolerance limits, evaluates the

effectiveness of existing fraud prevention strategies, and ensures that any risks remain within acceptable limits. The Anti-Fraud Subcommittee holds regular meetings at least once every three months or more frequently if necessary, with decisions made through deliberation or voting when consensus cannot be reached. All decisions are documented in meeting minutes, signed by the chairperson and all committee members, as a measure of transparency and accountability.

To ensure the effective implementation of anti-fraud policies, the Anti-Fraud Subcommittee is supported by a Supporting Group, responsible for formulating strategic recommendations, collecting data, and evaluating policy implementation. Additionally, the Operational and Digital Risk Management Unit plays a key role in monitoring and following up on decisions made during committee meetings. Through this structured oversight mechanism, BTN remains committed to fostering a high-integrity work environment, mitigating fraud risks, and ensuring that good governance principles are consistently applied across all operational areas

# Employee Training on Ethical Standards

As part of its commitment to maintaining high ethical standards, the Bank organized a Code of Ethics training program in 2024 for all employees, including permanent and contract employees, as well as development partners. The purpose of this training has been to ensure that all involved parties understand, internalize, and

adhere to the ethical principles enforced by the Bank, enabling them to apply these standards in their daily activities. Additionally, every new employee is required to undergo business ethics training as part of their orientation to ensure they understand and implement the established ethical standards.
