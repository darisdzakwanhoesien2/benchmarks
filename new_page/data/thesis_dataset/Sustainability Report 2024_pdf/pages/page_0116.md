# Recruitment of New Employees and Employee [GRI 401-1]

To meet workforce needs, BTN conducts recruitment through two channels: internal and external. The internal channel includes recruiting and selecting employees who meet specific criteria and are ready to fill certain positions. Meanwhile, external recruitment involves candidates who may be pre-graduates, fresh graduates, or individuals with professional experience (experienced &amp; professional hires). BTN prioritizes local employees, namely those of Indonesian nationality. All employees, including senior management, are part of the local community. Additionally, BTN has targeted recruitment initiatives for vulnerable groups such as women, minorities, indigenous communities, and persons with disabilities.

To support this policy, BTN refers to Special Policy Number KK.3-A on Human Capital Cycles states that the selection process must include clear and structured stages to ensure recruitment is conducted fairly, transparently, and by applicable standards. The stages are as follows: [GRI 3-3, 202-2]

## 1. Application Screening

Applications are managed, structured, and straightforward, enabling candidates to navigate the recruitment process efficiently. The requirements for this stage include:

- Fulfillment of criteria
- Completeness of documents
- Verification of application data authenticity

## 2. Candidate Assessment

This process may consist of one or several methods, including but not limited to:

- Speed dating interview, brief interviews with candidates
- Paper-based test, written questions related to psychometrics, scholastics, knowledge, and/or specific skills
- Skills-based approaches, validation of candidates' skills through demonstrations or presentations of their knowledge or abilities
- Gamification, selection in the form of game simulations to assess candidate alignment with the Bank's required values
- Competency/hackathons, competition skills relevant to the Bank's needs
- Job audition, candidates complete specific projects assigned by the Bank
- Case study, candidates solve case studies based on their capabilities
- Pre-employee screening, profile screening based on the Know Your Employee (KYE) principle to mitigate operational risks

BTN has a specific mechanism for appointing new employees as permanent staff. New employees who pass the selection stages and are appointed permanent employees receive remuneration by applicable labor regulations.
