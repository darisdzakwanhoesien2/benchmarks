# Mining*

![img-197.jpeg](img-197.jpeg)

The Bank establishes credit policies that require customers in this sector to:

A. Credit financing in the mining sector is limited only to corporations with good ESG implementation.
B. Hold a minimum PROPER Green rating and/or at least have an Environmental Impact Assessment (AMDAL) report or Environmental Management and Monitoring Effort Report (UKL-UPL) (including reclamation and post-mining guarantees) approved by the Environmental Agency (BLH).

C. Hold Environmental Management System certifications such as ISO 14001/ISO 45001 or other equivalent documents recognized by the Bank.
D. Implement Good Mining Practice (GMP) in compliance with applicable laws and regulations, including submission of an Annual Work Plan &amp; Budget (RKAB) approved by the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources (ESDM), and/or any other required documents related to Good Mining Practice.
E. Have an internal policy (code of conduct) related to environmental and labor standards.

* Solely as a reference for the Bank, and currently, the Bank has no financing in this sector.
