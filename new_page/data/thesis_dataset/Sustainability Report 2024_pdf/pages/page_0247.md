# Risk Management [ORI 2-23] [OJK E.3]

As a bank focused on providing credit and financing for housing, BTN's portfolio of assets primarily consists of Mortgage Loans (KPR). The Bank recognizes that various internal and external factors, such as changes in the business climate, inflation rates, and interest rate fluctuations, can impact this portfolio. Therefore, to minimize potential negative effects, sustainable risk management has become one of the Bank's top priorities.

The risk management implemented by BTN is grounded in the principles of Good Corporate Governance, which serve as the foundation for every risk management policy and procedure. Through the application of GCG, the Bank ensures that risk management policies

and procedures are conducted with transparency, accountability, and optimal sustainability. This approach not only supports strategic decision-making but also ensures that all risk management processes are carried out according to high ethical and integrity standards.

To control risks and ensure the Bank's healthy performance and achievement of objectives, BTN applies a comprehensive risk management system.
