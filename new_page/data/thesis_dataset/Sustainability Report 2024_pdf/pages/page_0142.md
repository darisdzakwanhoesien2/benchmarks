# Maternity Leave

BTN provides maternity leave for all employees who undergo or accompany the birth process. This provision is outlined in Special Policy 3. C regarding Regulations and Administration. Female employees are entitled to apply for maternity leave for 3 (three) months. Meanwhile, male employees accompanying their wives during childbirth are entitled to 5 (five) days of paternity leave.

Additionally, BTN also provides miscarriage leave for female employees for 45 days, calculated from the date of miscarriage, to support physical and emotional recovery. Furthermore, BTN sets provisions for childbearing leave that can be granted for up to 30 days, with certain conditions. [GRI 401-3]

# Retirement Preparation Program

As a manifestation of BTN's concern for the welfare of employees approaching retirement, the Bank has a Retirement Preparation Program (MPP). The MPP is carried out over 12 months and is designed to provide skills training and comprehensive mental preparation so employees can face life after retirement more effectively. The training offered not only focuses on technical or professional aspects but also involves developing emotional, spiritual, and intellectual aspects. With an approach that includes spiritual quotient (SQ), emotional quotient (EQ), and intelligence quotient (IQ), this program aims to create a retirement experience that offers happiness, health, and well-being. This program is intended for permanent employees who have reached age 55 as a form of appreciation and recognition for their contributions during their tenure at BTN. [GRI 404-2]

The training under the MPP is conducted using various methods specifically designed to ensure that the benefits provided are achieved effectively and optimally. Some of the methods used include:

1.  Experimental Learning
This method focuses on forming understanding and insight through simulation experiences and interaction with other participants. It is expected to inspire and provide insights to change attitudes and behaviors toward more effective and productive directions.

2.  Quantum Learning
This method focuses on participants' aspirations and two-way communication, supported by a comfortable and enjoyable training environment. This is intended to encourage participants to change their attitudes and behaviors positively.
