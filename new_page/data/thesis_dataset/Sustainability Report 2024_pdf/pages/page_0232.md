In addition, the Bank adopts various best governance standards at regional and international levels and continues to enhance the scope of GCG implementation, including:

1. Corporate governance principles developed by the Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development (OECD);
2. ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard published by the ASEAN Capital Market Forum (ACMF);
3. Indonesian Corporate Governance Guidelines (PUGKI) developed by the National Committee on Governance Policy (KNKG);
4. Banking GCG Guidelines for Indonesia developed by the National Committee on Governance Policy (KNKG); and
5. Principles for Enhancing Corporate Governance published by the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision.

The implementation of GCG has become a strategic element in achieving the goal of moving beyond corporate governance to true business value. BTN has shifted its governance focus from a compliance-driven approach to a value-driven approach across all business lines. This step aligns the Bank's values with strengthening sustainable GCG practices. Since 2014, the Corporate Governance Perception Index (CGPI) score and the ASEAN Corporate Governance Scorecard (ACGS) have shown significant growth, reflecting consistency in enhancing performance and governance practices at the Bank.

# Governance Commitment

BTN is committed to continuously integrating the principles of GCG into all operational aspects, adhering to applicable regulations, and maintaining openness to improvements in achieving alignment with the best practices. This commitment aims to support BTN's vision and create optimal value for shareholders and other stakeholders.

The implementation of GCG is a key element in assessing BTN's performance by shareholders while ensuring that the Bank is managed transparently and professionally, safeguarding the interests of all shareholders. As a State-Owned Enterprise (SOE) and public company, BTN is determined to continuously enhance GCG implementation to meet the expectations of all stakeholders.
