Retirement benefits are rights the Bank gives to permanent employees who meet the retirement age requirements, which are 56 years for general employees and 36 years for employees in specific positions such as tellers, customer service staff, and secretaries.

The Bank emphasizes the welfare of employees nearing retirement by providing preparation and special incentives. This is outlined in the General Policy on Human Number KU.3 regarding the Management

of Employment Termination. Based on this policy, permanent employees aged 56 are entitled to receive pension benefits as part of recognizing their contributions during their tenure at BTN. The following are the pension benefits provided:

- Define Benefit Program, providing retirement benefits based on a formula set by the Pension Fund;
- Defined Contribution Program, providing retirement benefits from the accumulation of the permanent employee's contribution balance, consisting of 2.5% from the employee's salary, 7.5% from the Bank, and the development results;
- Referring to the Director's Decree regulating the Pension Fund Regulations of PT Bank Tabungan Negara (Persero) Tbk, the maximum pension benefit is 80% of the basic pension income. [GRI 201-3]

Employee Complaint Handling

Complaint Management

BTN recognizes that a harmonious and open relationship between employees and management is key to creating a healthy and conducive work environment. Therefore, BTN is committed to ensuring the presence of effective and transparent communication channels. As a concrete step, the Bank has developed a digital employee platform called BTNBest designed to facilitate employees' access to all information and personnel matters. Additionally, BTN provides a whistleblowing system (WBS) facility managed by an independent third party. This system is a secure channel for employees to report potential violations or fraudulent actions, as the Bank guarantees the whistleblower's confidentiality. This facility allows employees to report violations without fear of retaliation, such as termination, demotion, or intimidation. BTN also uses the WBS to manage employee complaints regarding labor practices and human rights issues by Technical Guidelines Number 7-B.2 on the Management of the Whistleblowing System. The whistleblowing system (WBS) facility is accessible to all employees flexibly, with access available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

In Technical Guidelines Number 7-B.2 regarding the Management of the Whistleblowing System, the employee complaint mechanism is outlined as follows:

- The WBS process and mechanism are the responsibility of the President Director and the WBS Manager, which consists of the WBS Committee and the WBS Monitoring Committee;
- The WBS Committee consists of several divisions and the WBS management unit, while the WBS Monitoring Committee consists of the President Commissioner and other commissioners at the Bank;
- Unless the reported party is a member of the Board of Directors or a person with a special relationship with the board and/or members of the WBS committee, the report may be submitted to the WBS Monitoring Committee; and
- If the reported party is a member of the Board of Commissioners or someone with a special relationship with the Commissioner, the report may be submitted to the President Director.

Throughout the reporting year, BTN did not receive any complaints about the work environment, indicating the effectiveness of the Company's policy in creating a healthy, safe, and harmonious work environment for all employees.
