# People's Business Credit (KUR)

Since 2017, BTN has supported government policies aimed at enhancing business capital access to accelerate real sector development. In line with this, the Bank has placed a strong focus on empowering MSMEs by distributing People's Business Credit (KUR), including Micro KUR and Small KUR. This program is designed to provide capital access to micro, small, and medium enterprises, particularly those operating in the construction, housing, and other supporting sectors. Through KUR, BTN offers financing options that can be used for working capital and/or investment, strengthening the operational capacity of debtors. The program also provides opportunities for debtors without additional collateral to obtain financing. KUR is not only intended for individual entrepreneurs and small businesses but also for business entities and productive business groups that meet eligibility criteria.

In the reporting year, BTN has implemented several strategies to enhance the distribution of People's Business Credit (KUR), including:

1. Enhancing High Yield Loan Distribution (Eagle Project)
- Increasing KUR realization through direct selling to mortgage (KPR) and consumer loan debtors.
- Segmenting KUR sales by targeting ecosystems and communities based on regional potential through BPI SME.
