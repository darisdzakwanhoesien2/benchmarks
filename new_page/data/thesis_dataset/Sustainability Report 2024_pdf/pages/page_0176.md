# BTN Service Roadmap 2023 – 2025

In order to realize BTN's vision of becoming the "Primary Partner in Empowering Indonesian Families Financially" and to fulfill one of its missions to "Provide the Best Customer Experience through Integrated Digital and Financial Services," the Bank has established a service vision: "To deliver a reliable, uniquely differentiated, and convenient service beyond customer expectations," along with 19 key initiatives. Based on this, BTN has developed a Service Roadmap that will serve as a guide in achieving BTN's Service Vision.

The development process of the 2023-2025 Service Roadmap began with a survey to gather Voice

of Customer (VoC) insights through Customer Experience Index (CEI) measurements. To follow up on the established BTN Service Roadmap 2023-2025 strategy, the Customer Experience Division conducted a workshop attended by the Board of Directors, SEVPs, relevant Division Heads (including product and channel owners), Regional Office Heads of Regions 1 and 2, and a team of consultants. This workshop served as a platform for socialization, discussion, and obtaining guidance and approval from the Board of Directors regarding the proposed Service Roadmap.
