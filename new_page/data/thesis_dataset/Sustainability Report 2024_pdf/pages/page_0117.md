## Employee Recruitment and Turnover

In 2024, BTN aimed to recruit 1,000 employees as part of its human resource development strategy to support long-term growth and sustainability. By year-end, the

recruitment reached 1,231 employees, reflecting BTN's commitment to providing career opportunities and strengthening organizational capacity to face business challenges while creating positive societal impacts. For 2025, BTN has set a recruitment target of 618 employees. Below is the data for new employees recruited through the Human Capital Management Unit:

The workforce also decreases due to retirement, resignations, contract expiration, death, and other reasons. BTN manages employee termination processes based on the guidelines outlined in Special

Policy Number KK.3-A on the Human Capital Cycle. This policy includes clear procedures for handling various termination situations to ensure smooth operational activities and fair processes for all employees.
