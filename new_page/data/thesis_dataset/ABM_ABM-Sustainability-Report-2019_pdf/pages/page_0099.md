## Program Bantuan Persiapan Pensiun

Sebelum karyawan pensiun, Perusahaan memberikan bantuan persiapan dengan menyelenggarakan program/kegiatan persiapan pensiun. Program bertujuan agar karyawan yang akan pensiun dapat melanjutkan aktivitasnya, terutama mendapatkan sumber penghasilan alternatif untuk membiayai kehidupan mereka. Selama tahun 2019, program bantuan persiapan pensiun diselenggarakan oleh fungsi Human Capital perusahaan. (404-2)

## Pension Preparation Assistance Program

Before employees retire, the Company provides preparation assistance by organizing pension preparation programs/activities. The programs are aimed at preparing soon-to- retire employees on how to remain active, and how to find alternative sources of income to finance their lives. During 2019, the retirement preparation assistance programs was organized by Human Capital Function. (404-2)
