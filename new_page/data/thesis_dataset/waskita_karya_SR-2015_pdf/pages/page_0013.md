# PENCAPAIAN 2015

Sepanjang 2015, Perseroan telah melaksanakan berbagai kebijakan strategis guna menghadapi perubahan dan dinamika bisnis Jasa Konstruksi. Beberapa kebijakan strategis yang dilakukan dalam tahun 2014 antara lain melakukan peningkatan modal dengan Penyertaan Modal Negara (PMN) dan tambahan dana publik melalui mekanisme rights issue sebesar Rp5,3 triliun.

Dear Shareholders and Stakeholders,

PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk (Company) is grateful to present this 2015 Sustainability Report for the second time. This report becomes track record of the Company in an effort to implement sustainability principles. We believe that all efforts that we have exerted continue to be useful for the stakeholders.

# ACHIEVEMENT IN 2015

Throughout 2015, the Company has implemented various strategic policies to deal with changes and business dynamics in the Construction Industry. Several strategic policies that we implemented in 2014 were, among others, increasing State Capital Participation (PMN) and additional public funds through mechanism of right issue amounted to Rp5.3 trillion.
