Perseroan melakukan go public pada Desember 2012 dan menjadi PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk. Saat ini, Perseroan telah menjelma menjadi salah satu perusahaan konstruksi terbesar di Indonesia.

The Company listed its shares on December 2012 and became PT Waskita Karya (Persero) Tbk. Currently, the Company is considered as one of the most prominent construction company in Indonesia.
