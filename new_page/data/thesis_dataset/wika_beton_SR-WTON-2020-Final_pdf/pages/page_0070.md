Concrete production factory generates solid waste in the forms of casting and soil. Casting can be used as the foundation of resident housing and utilized as raw material for paving blocks as road compactor. The soil not utilized by the company can be used for the backfilling of roads, paddy fields, empty lands, and building foundations.
