Strategi Keberlanjutan Sustainability Strategy

Dari sisi tata kelola, Bank Aladin Syariah telah membentuk Komite Pemantau Risiko yang merupakan organ pendukung Dewan Komisaris dan Komite Manajemen Risiko pada tingkat Direksi guna mengelola risiko secara efektif dan proaktif.

In terms of governance, Bank Aladin Syariah has formed a Risk Monitoring Committee which is a supporting organ for the Board of Commissioners and a Risk Management Committee at the Board of Directors level to manage risk effectively and proactively.
