Recommendations: practical checklist for ESG/climate disclosure analytics (12–18 items, 3–5 categories)

Category: Data readiness

Item 1: Maintain a documented data provenance log for every document with source, version, and license; test by producing a per-item provenance trail for a sample extraction.
Item 2: Define and enforce language/domain scope mappings (e.g., GHG scopes, baselines) with explicit canonical labels; verify via a crosswalk report against a gold-standard subset.
Item 3: Build and maintain a multilingual glossary with region-specific terminology; validate by sampling non-English disclosures and confirming correct term normalization.
Category: Modeling and extraction

Item 4: Use a structured JSON/ schema for all model outputs and validate conformance in a post-process step; test by feeding known-good samples and ensuring zero-schema violations.
Item 5: Implement provenance-attached extractions where every field includes the exact source passage (page/section/footnote) and a confidence score; verify by random audits linking fields to passages.
Item 6: Employ a two-stage extraction check: (a) an LLM/sklearn-based extractor produces candidate fields, (b) a deterministic validator checks type, range, and cross-field consistency; test with edge-case documents.
Category: Evaluation and benchmarking

Item 7: Establish a ground-truth benchmark corpus with expert-adjudicated records for targets, baselines, and years across at least three jurisdictions; require inter-annotator agreement above a pre-specified threshold.
Item 8: Report field-level F1, record-level exact-match, evidence correctness, provenance fidelity, and abstention rates for every release; validate improvements with ablation tests.
Item 9: Measure end-to-end evidence-groundedness by computing the fraction of extractions that have corroborating passages and correct anchors; perform periodic re-annotation to monitor drift.
Category: Reliability, governance, and deployment

Item 10: Institute canary deployments with versioned prompts and models; compare performance against a baseline on a fixed set of canary documents before broader rollout.
Item 11: Maintain an audit log of prompts, model configurations, retrieval settings, and validation results; test by reproducing past extractions from the logs.
Item 12: Implement abstention policy with explicit rules (confidence threshold, missing provenance, or conflicting signals) and document the rationale; verify that abstentions occur only according to policy.
Note: This checklist emphasizes testable, auditable, and repeatable practices focused on data lineage, structured outputs, evidence grounding, multilingual considerations, and deployment governance. Each item is designed so that practitioners can execute a concrete test or generate a reproducible artifact to demonstrate compliance with the guideline.
