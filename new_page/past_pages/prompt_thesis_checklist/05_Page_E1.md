E1. Overall Thesis Structure

Determine the actual structure from the supplied thesis files before judging missing chapters.

For the current thesis, the likely chapter mapping is:

- front matter: `foreword.md`, `abstract.md`, `tiivistelma.md`, `abbreviations.md`
- Chapter 1: `introduction.md`
- Chapter 2: `relatedwork.md`
- Chapter 3: `implementation.md`
- Chapter 4: `experiments.md`
- Chapter 5: `discussion.md`
- Chapter 6: `summary.md`
- Chapter 7: `appendices.md`

Evaluate whether the progression is logical across:

Abstract
Introduction
Background or theoretical foundation
Related work
Methodology
Implementation
Experimental setup
Results
Discussion
Limitations or threats to validity
Conclusion
References
Appendices

Check whether:

each chapter has a clearly defined purpose;
background and related work are properly distinguished;
methodology is separated from results;
implementation details are placed appropriately;
results are separated from interpretation where required;
discussion returns to the research questions;
the conclusion does not introduce new results;
appendix material is referenced from the main text;
essential methodological information has not been relegated entirely to appendices.

Thesis-specific structural checks:

- whether Chapter 3 combines methodology and implementation coherently or needs clearer internal separation;
- whether Chapter 4 properly carries both results and evaluation evidence since there is no separate results chapter;
- whether Chapter 5 repeats too much of Chapter 4 instead of deepening interpretation;
- whether placeholder front matter currently makes the thesis structurally incomplete.
