D3. Typography and Layout

Assess, where observable:

page margins;
font type and size;
heading hierarchy;
line spacing;
paragraph spacing;
indentation;
page numbering;
caption format;
figure and table placement;
table width and readability;
landscape-page handling;
equation formatting and numbering;
orphan and widow lines;
blank pages;
excessive white space;
inconsistent alignment;
oversized or unreadably small content;
consistency of appendix formatting;
bibliography formatting;
compliance with the required thesis template.

When formatting cannot be assessed from extracted text, explicitly state that the source PDF or compiled thesis must be inspected manually.
