D1. Academic and Engineering Language

Evaluate whether the writing is:

professional;
precise;
concise;
technically appropriate;
grammatically correct;
logically connected;
suitable for an engineering and scientific audience.

Identify:

exaggerated or promotional language;
vague claims;
conversational expressions;
unnecessary adjectives;
unsupported value judgments;
repetition;
excessively long sentences;
sentence fragments;
ambiguous pronouns;
inconsistent tense;
inconsistent singular and plural forms;
incorrect article usage;
inconsistent capitalization;
improper use of abbreviations;
undefined technical terms;
excessive passive voice where responsibility becomes unclear;
excessive first-person language;
imprecise verbs such as “does,” “handles,” “uses,” or “works” when a technical verb is needed.

For each major linguistic issue, provide:

Original sentence
Problem
Revised sentence
Reason for revision

Prioritize recurring patterns instead of listing every minor typo.
