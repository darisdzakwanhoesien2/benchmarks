Section 7. Implementation-Oriented Synthesis (Repository-Ready)

This final section translates the review into a concrete, reproducible pipeline suitable for a repository, emphasizing an implementation-first, evaluation-first stance for OCR-derived bilingual Indonesian ESG disclosures.

7.1 Recommended corpora

Two complementary corpora are recommended for a robust, repository-ready analysis:

Statement-level corpus (ABSA-extracted)

Advantages: Provides high-resolution signals aligned to specific ESG claims, metrics, or disclosures; compatible with ABSA labeling to map aspects (environmental, social, governance) to sentiment cues; facilitates fine-grained governance insights and precise auditing/triage workflows.
Risks: Very short text units per item may yield sparse topic signals; ABSA labeling quality depends on annotation consistency and availability of domain-specific training data; potential overfitting to a limited set of aspects if not balanced.
Recommended unit size: 1–3 sentences per statement, with a minimum of 5–15 tokens after preprocessing. In practice, extract statements via structural cues and/or sentence-boundary detection within the OCR’d text, ensuring language tags are preserved for bilingual processing.
OCR-chunk corpus

Advantages: Balances granularity with robustness by aggregating across OCR-derived chunks (e.g., section- or page-level chunks); mitigates sparse-signal issues while maintaining cross-sectional interpretability; supports cross-language coherence when chunks span both languages.
Risks: Boundary artifacts from page/column cuts can create artificial topic signals; chunk size selection influences topic granularity and noise sensitivity; boilerplate leakage remains a danger if the chunk includes template language.
Recommended unit size: 200–600 tokens per chunk (adjusted to document length and language distribution), with chunks aligned to natural structural breaks (sections, subsections) when possible, and with language labels attached.

7.3 Model comparison design

Baseline set (recommended):

LDA (probabilistic BoW baseline)
NMF on TF-IDF (matrix factorization baseline)
Embedding-based clustering (e.g., BERTopic or ETM with multilingual embeddings)
Cross-corpus comparison:

Evaluate both statement-level and OCR-chunk corpora with the same model configurations where possible to disentangle unit-effects from model-effects.
Keep a consistent evaluation protocol across corpora: same topic counts, identical preprocessing settings where applicable, and identical external validation mappings (ESG pillars, ABSA aspects).
Evaluation and reporting approach:

Coherence and stability as intrinsic metrics, plus extrinsic ABSA/pillar alignment metrics (see Section 5 for details).
Report per-model results by corpus unit, including:
Coherence (C_V, NPMI), topic diversity, and top-word overlap
Stability across seeds and folds (NMI, Jaccard similarity of top-k words)
ABSA-alignment indicators (entropy/concentration of pillar distributions)
ESG taxonomy mapping success rate (percentage of topics confidently mapped to pillars/aspects)
Comparison design guidelines:
Pre-register model configurations and evaluation plan to prevent p-hacking; document any deviations in the reproducibility bundle.
Use statistical significance tests where appropriate to compare coherence or alignment metrics across models and corpora.
Include qualitative case studies highlighting topics with strong vs. weak ABSA alignment to illustrate practical interpretability.
Ensure multilingual parity by reporting language-wise performance and cross-language topic coherence.
7.4 Thesis-ready result formats

Tables:

Model comparison table: rows = models (LDA, NMF, BERTopic/ETM, etc.); columns = corpus unit, number of topics, coherence, stability, ABSA alignment score, pillar alignment accuracy
Topic panels: per-topic label, top keywords, exemplar passages, ABSA/aspect mapping, ESG pillar
Temporal evolution table: cross-year topic prevalence, rate of change per pillar
Sector distribution table: topic distribution by sector (if sector labels available)
Figures:

Topic panels (grid of topics with wordcloud-like visuals and representative exemplars)
Temporal charts: topic prevalence over years, pillar distribution over time
Sector charts: proportion of topics per ESG pillar per sector
Pipeline schematic: high-level data flow from OCR input to ABSA-aligned topics
Ablation study charts: effect of preprocessing choices on coherence and ABSA alignment
Error analysis visuals: boilerplate-driven topics vs. content-driven topics (qualitative sampling)
Documentation and reproducibility:

Include a README describing how to reproduce the pipeline, including environment setup, data access notes, and run commands
Provide a small, canned reproduceable example dataset (synthetic or redacted) for demonstration
Ensure all exports are versioned and linked to their corresponding manifest entries for traceability
