Section 8. Synthesis: Core Thesis and Practical Path

Main thesis (restate)
Topic modelling is a valuable tool for ESG disclosure analysis only when it is executed with rigor around reproducibility and evaluation beyond coherence. Baseline coherence metrics alone are insufficient to guarantee useful, interpretable, and auditable insights from OCR-derived, bilingual Indonesian ESG reports. A reproducible pipeline combined with extrinsic validation (ABSA alignment, ESG taxonomy mapping, temporal/sector signals) is essential to transform topic signals into credible governance and decision-support outputs.

Summary of the recommended path (concise synthesis)

Unit-of-analysis decisions: Employ a dual-unit strategy that leverages both statement-level ABSA-extracted passages for high-resolution interpretability and OCR-chunk units (section/page-derived) for robust, cross-document comparability and temporal analysis.
Baselines plus embedding models: Start with solid baselines (LDA and TF-IDF-based NMF) to establish a reproducible floor, then incorporate embedding-based clustering to capture semantic coherence across bilingual content, and finally explore neural hybrids when the evaluation plan supports end-to-end interpretability with taxonomy alignment.
Stability and alignment evaluation: Implement a reproducible stability protocol (multi-seed runs, resampling, topic-overlap metrics) and extrinsic ABSA-alignment analyses (pillar distribution, ABSA aspects, entropy/concentration metrics) to ensure topics are not only coherent but also interpretable and governance-relevant.
Standardized artifact export: Export a repository-ready set of artifacts with explicit metadata, reproducibility bundles, and versioned preprocessing and model configurations to enable auditability, re-use, and cross-year/ cross-sector comparisons.
Temporal and sector insights: Align topics with ESG taxonomies and ABSA signals to yield interpretable temporal trajectories and sector-specific patterns, supporting auditing, regulatory oversight, and strategic decision-making.
One-sentence concluding impact
By combining reproducible workflows with evaluation beyond coherence, ESG disclosure analysis becomes more interpretable, auditable, and capable of yielding reliable temporal and sectoral insights in Indonesian bilingual, OCR-derived contexts
