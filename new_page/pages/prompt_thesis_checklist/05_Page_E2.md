E2. Heading and Subsection Balance

Check for:

single subsections, such as Section 2.1 without Section 2.2;
headings containing only one short paragraph;
overly deep heading levels;
inconsistent heading style;
vague headings such as “Other,” “Additional,” or “General”;
duplicated or overlapping sections;
disproportionate chapter lengths;
sections whose content does not match their heading;
sections that should be merged, divided, moved, or renamed.
