B4. Critical Discussion

Evaluate whether the discussion:

interprets rather than merely repeats results;
compares findings analytically with related work;
explains agreements and disagreements with prior studies;
discusses unexpected findings;
examines failure cases;
separates observations from speculation;
evaluates how well the original requirements were fulfilled;
explains the scientific and practical significance of the results;
considers alternative explanations;
discusses limitations without undermining the entire thesis;
avoids exaggerated generalization;
identifies conditions under which the method may fail;
explains what can and cannot be concluded.

Identify sections where the discussion reads like another results summary and suggest the analytical questions that should be addressed.
