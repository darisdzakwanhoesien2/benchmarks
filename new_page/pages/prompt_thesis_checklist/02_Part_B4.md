B4. Critical Discussion

Use `discussion.md` as the main target, but verify whether the interpretation also depends on evidence in `experiments.md` and claims repeated in `summary.md`.

For this thesis, pay particular attention to whether the discussion:

- maps findings back to the four declared research questions;
- distinguishes strong operational evidence from weaker validation evidence;
- treats the Indonesian thesis-facing subset as the boundary of generalization;
- acknowledges the difference between diagnostic usefulness and benchmark superiority.

Evaluate whether the discussion:

interprets rather than merely repeats results;
compares findings analytically with related work;
explains agreements and disagreements with prior studies;
discusses unexpected findings;
examines failure cases;
separates observations from speculation;
evaluates how well the original requirements were fulfilled;
explains the scientific and practical significance of the results;
considers alternative explanations;
discusses limitations without undermining the entire thesis;
avoids exaggerated generalization;
identifies conditions under which the method may fail;
explains what can and cannot be concluded.

Identify sections where the discussion reads like another results summary and suggest the analytical questions that should be addressed.

Additional thesis-specific checks:

- whether terms like "validated", "significant", "strong", or "robust" are used too broadly for pilot-level evidence;
- whether limitations are grouped under internal validity, construct validity, external validity, and reproducibility where possible;
- whether future-work items address the actual weakest rigor points instead of only adding more system features.
