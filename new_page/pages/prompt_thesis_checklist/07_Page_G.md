Part G: Independent Performance and Scope of Supervision

Assess what the thesis itself demonstrates about independent performance.

Look for evidence that the author independently:

defined or refined the research problem;
selected and justified methods;
developed or adapted the system;
designed experiments;
prepared or annotated data;
analysed results;
identified limitations;
made technical decisions;
solved implementation problems;
interpreted findings;
documented the work reproducibly.

Identify passages suggesting substantial external assistance with:

defining the topic;
structuring the thesis;
selecting methods;
implementing the system;
evaluating results;
interpreting results;
writing central parts of the thesis.

Do not speculate about private supervision that is not documented. Assess only the evidence available in the thesis and contribution statement.

Report:

| Area | Evidence of Independence | Evidence of External Assistance | Assessment | Clarification Needed |

Use the following assessments:

Strong evidence of independence
Adequate evidence of independence
Independence insufficiently documented
Possible over-reliance on external assistance
Cannot be assessed

Suggest how the author can document their independent decisions more clearly without misrepresenting collaboration.
