D4. Accessibility

Assess:

logical heading hierarchy;
descriptive figure captions;
alternative text for figures;
alternative descriptions for complex tables;
meaningful hyperlink text;
sufficient explanation of information conveyed through colour;
whether colour is the only means of distinguishing categories;
readability of figures at normal zoom;
table header clarity;
document reading order;
accessibility of mathematical content;
contrast and labelling;
whether screenshots contain unreadable text.
