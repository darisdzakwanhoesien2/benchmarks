Part B: Achievement of Aims and Scientific Rigor
B1. Alignment of Objectives, Research Questions, Methods, Results, and Conclusions

Create a traceability matrix with the following columns:

| Objective or Research Question | Promised in Introduction | Method Used | Evidence Produced | Result Reported | Discussed Critically | Answered in Conclusion | Status | Required Revision |

Use these status labels:

Fully achieved
Partially achieved
Not achieved
Cannot be verified
Scope changed without sufficient explanation

Assess whether:

every research question is explicitly answered;
every objective has an associated method and result;
methods directly address the stated objectives;
conclusions follow from the reported evidence;
the abstract accurately reflects the actual work;
uncompleted objectives are acknowledged honestly;
exploratory observations are not presented as validated findings;
anticipated outcomes are not mixed with obtained results;
objectives were changed during the project without explanation;
contributions are concrete, demonstrated, and appropriately limited.

Flag claims using words such as “proves,” “demonstrates,” “significantly improves,” “outperforms,” “robust,” “generalizable,” “effective,” and “state-of-the-art” when the evidence is insufficient.
