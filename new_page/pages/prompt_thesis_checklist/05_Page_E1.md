E1. Overall Thesis Structure

Evaluate whether the progression is logical across:

Abstract
Introduction
Background or theoretical foundation
Related work
Methodology
Implementation
Experimental setup
Results
Discussion
Limitations or threats to validity
Conclusion
References
Appendices

Check whether:

each chapter has a clearly defined purpose;
background and related work are properly distinguished;
methodology is separated from results;
implementation details are placed appropriately;
results are separated from interpretation where required;
discussion returns to the research questions;
the conclusion does not introduce new results;
appendix material is referenced from the main text;
essential methodological information has not been relegated entirely to appendices.
