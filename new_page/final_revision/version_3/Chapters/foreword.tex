This thesis was shaped by a sustained interest in applied natural language
processing, aspect-based sentiment analysis, and the practical challenge of
turning complex corporate sustainability reports into auditable analytical
evidence. The work brought together research design, software implementation,
and iterative evaluation in the context of Environmental, Social, and
Governance (ESG) disclosure analysis.

I would like to express my sincere gratitude to my supervisors, Dr. Mourad
Oussalah and Mehrdad Rostami, at the Center for Machine Vision and Signal
Analysis (CMVS), University of Oulu. Your guidance, support, and scientific
standards provided the foundation for this work. The freedom to explore
unconventional approaches while maintaining methodological discipline
substantially shaped both the direction of the thesis and my development as a
researcher.

I am deeply grateful to my family for their constant encouragement, patience,
and confidence in me throughout this process. Your support during demanding
periods of study and research made it possible to complete this work.

I also gratefully acknowledge the financial support provided through the
University of Oulu Scholarship. This support contributed to the continuity of
the research and to the computational resources required for the thesis.

This thesis reflects curiosity-driven research, sustained engineering effort,
and persistence in addressing a difficult interdisciplinary problem. I hope the
work provides a useful foundation for more transparent, reproducible, and
practically relevant ESG disclosure analysis.
