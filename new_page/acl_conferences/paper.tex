\maketitleAbstract

Corporate sustainability reports are increasingly multimodal, utilizing "visual rhetoric" to influence stakeholder perceptions  \parencite{bingjie_li_487d69fd}. However, verifying these claims against real-world events remains a challenge due to the heterogeneity of data. We propose a unified framework that integrates internal report analysis with external verification. Our approach utilizes \textbf{Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation}  \parencite{mohammad_mahdavi_619c09a8} to extract claims, \textbf{MAFT}  \parencite{kazuya_kakizaki_4193ad80} to process external web-based evidence, and \textbf{knowledge-driven neural models}  \parencite{abir_naskar_17efeb7b} to detect sustainability violations in news and social media.
\begin{center}\rule{\linewidth}{0.5pt}\end{center}
1. Introduction
\begin{itemize}
\item 

\textbf{Background:} The rise of Environmental, Social, and Governance criteria as fundamental pillars for investment  \parencite{g_saipooja_m_n_488bfdd6}.\item 

\textbf{Problem Statement:} Corporate "impression management" leads to greenwashing, where visually dense layouts (charts, images) are used to present a more favorable image than reality  \parencite{bingjie_li_487d69fd}.\item 

\textbf{Challenges:} Traditional text-only models fail to parse complex page layouts, including greenhouse gas emission-reduction targets embedded in charts  \parencite[]{tanay_gupta_aafe0c6a, lars_wilhelmi_8d7e860b}.\item 

\textbf{Contribution:} A pipeline that bridges internal corporate disclosures with "outside-in" verification using external news and social media.
\end{itemize}
\begin{center}\rule{\linewidth}{0.5pt}\end{center}
2. Related Work
\begin{itemize}
\item 

\textbf{2.1 ESG Information Extraction:} Review of Vision Language Models for extracting metrics from visually dense layouts  \parencite{tanay_gupta_aafe0c6a}. Reference to the 0.82 F1-score achieved by combining text and image modalities for GHG target extraction  \parencite{lars_wilhelmi_8d7e860b}.\item 

\textbf{2.2 RAG and Agentic Architectures:} Discussion of AuditGPT  \parencite{abhijeet_b__moghe_963e6c96} and EmeraldMind  \parencite{kaoukis__georgios_35bab107} for automated auditing and greenwashing detection using domain-specific knowledge graphs.\item 

\textbf{2.3 Multimodal Verification:} The evolution from text-based RAG to multimodal RAG, which provides a 6.5\% improvement in mean average precision for ESG analysis  \parencite{g_saipooja_m_n_488bfdd6}.
\end{itemize}
\begin{center}\rule{\linewidth}{0.5pt}\end{center}
3. Methodology
\begin{itemize}
\item 

\textbf{3.1 Internal Claim Extraction:} Utilizing a system that retrieves both textual and visual evidence from internal reports to identify verifiable sustainability objectives  \parencite{mohammad_mahdavi_619c09a8}.\item 

\textbf{3.2 External Evidence Textualization:}
\begin{itemize}
\item 

Applying the \textbf{MAFT} framework to convert external multimodal content (news videos, images, social media audio) into text-based representations  \parencite{kazuya_kakizaki_4193ad80}.\item 

Leveraging web APIs and LLMs to collect real-time data from the open web  \parencite{kazuya_kakizaki_4193ad80}.
\end{itemize}\item 

\textbf{3.3 Sustainability Event Detection:}
\begin{itemize}
\item 

Deployment of \textbf{knowledge-driven neural models} utilizing a multi-tasking sequence labeling architecture  \parencite{abir_naskar_17efeb7b}.\item 

Detection of organizational violations and incidents reported by regulatory agencies or social media users, aligned with Global Reporting Initiative standards  \parencite{abir_naskar_17efeb7b}.
\end{itemize}\item 

\textbf{3.4 Verification and Verdict Generation:}
\begin{itemize}
\item 

Integration of visual forensics to detect synthetic or out-of-context media  \parencite{vinh_nguyen_phan_020d3d5e}.\item 

Generation of "justification-centric" verdicts supported by traceable references  \parencite[]{kaoukis__georgios_35bab107, abhijeet_b__moghe_963e6c96}.
\end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
\begin{center}\rule{\linewidth}{0.5pt}\end{center}
4. Experimental Setup
\begin{itemize}
\item 

\textbf{4.1 Datasets:}
\begin{itemize}
\item 

\textbf{Internal:} The NFIVI dataset for visual representation analysis  \parencite{bingjie_li_487d69fd}.\item 

\textbf{External:} The ESG-FTSE news corpus  \parencite{mariya_pavlova_259be499} and custom annotated sustainability incident corpora (2015–2022)  \parencite{abir_naskar_17efeb7b}.
\end{itemize}\item 

\textbf{4.2 Baselines:} Comparison against text-only RAG and standard VLM extraction artifacts  \parencite{lars_wilhelmi_8d7e860b}.\item 

\textbf{4.3 Evaluation Metrics:} Precision, Recall, F1-score for event detection, and explanation quality for fact-checking reports  \parencite[]{kaoukis__georgios_35bab107, kazuya_kakizaki_4193ad80}.
\end{itemize}

5. Results and Analysis
\begin{itemize}
\item 

\textbf{Performance Improvements:} Quantifying the benefits of the integrated pipeline (e.g., matching or exceeding the 6.5\% precision gain observed in modified multimodal RAG  \parencite{g_saipooja_m_n_488bfdd6}).\item 

\textbf{Modality Comparison:} Analyzing how combining text and image modalities reduces "inference-based hallucinations" in visually dense sustainability reports  \parencite{lars_wilhelmi_8d7e860b}.\item 

\textbf{Case Studies:} Demonstrating the detection of a "sustainability violation" from news/social media that contradicts a corporate report claim.
\end{itemize}

6. Discussion
\begin{itemize}

\textbf{Interpretability:} The role of MAFT in generating detailed, interpretable verification processes  \parencite{kazuya_kakizaki_4193ad80}.\item 

\textbf{Responsible AI:} The importance of "responsible abstention" when external evidence is insufficient to verify a claim  \parencite{kaoukis__georgios_35bab107}.\item
