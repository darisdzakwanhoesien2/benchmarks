Prioritized analysis plan — what to do next

Ordered by thesis impact · estimated effort · data inputs required

P1
Run ClimateBERT locally over all 272 valid Arcee records
Currently the "91 co-occurrences" statistic is computed from LLM-assigned labels embedded during extraction — not actual ClimateBERT model outputs. Running the 13 available ClimateBERT models (climate-detector, climate-commitment, netzero-reduction, tcfd, sentiment, specificity, environmental-claims, etc.) over all 272 valid records produces a genuine per-record comparison table.
tone_records_flat.csv ClimateBERT API / local HF models 272 text records
Expected output: 272 × 13 matrix of CB model scores.
Key metric: % of "commitment" tone records where CB climate-commitment = "yes" (current proxy: 34.3%)
Answers: RQ3 fully · strengthens RQ4 (false negative identification)
Effort: 1–2 days · high thesis impact
P2
Expert annotation of 30–50 stratified records → compute IAA
Stratify sample by: tone (10 commitment, 10 action, 10 outcome, 10 none) × 2 languages. Two annotators label: aspect, ESG pillar, tone (commitment/action/outcome/none), sentiment (positive/neutral/negative). Compute Cohen's κ for each dimension. This is the only way to compute F1 and validate the taxonomy.
tone_records_flat.csv (sample 50) 2 human annotators annotation guidelines document
Expected: κ ≥ 0.70 for tone (good), ≥ 0.60 for aspect (acceptable).
Key metric: tone classification F1 per category (target: F1 ≥ 0.65 per tone class).
Answers: RQ2 (validates taxonomy) · RQ4 (error rate quantification)
Effort: 2–3 weeks · critical for thesis credibility
P3
Prompt stability analysis: ensemble majority-vote on PTBA (5 prompts available)
PTBA has records from 5 different prompt templates — the only document with this coverage. For each PTBA record that appears across multiple prompts, apply majority vote across 3 prompts and measure whether the ensemble reduces commitment rate variance. This directly demonstrates ensemble strategy effectiveness for RQ6.
tone_records_flat.csv (PTBA rows) 5 prompt variants for PTBA
PTBA commitment rate range: 24–62% across 4 prompts (38pp spread).
Majority vote of 3 prompts expected to reduce variance by ~40–60%.
Answers: RQ6 (ensemble strategy effectiveness)
Effort: 1 day computation · medium thesis impact
P4
Bilingual asymmetry analysis: formal test of Indonesian vs English outcome rate
The bilingual asymmetry finding (Indonesian outcome 7.9% vs English 21.8%) is computed but not tested for statistical significance. Apply a two-proportion z-test (or Fisher's exact test given small counts). Also break down by document to check whether the effect is consistent or driven by one document.
tone_records_flat.csv language detection (keyword-based) scipy.stats for z-test
Indonesian outcome: 7.9% (n≈105) vs English: 21.8% (n≈118).
Two-proportion z-test expected p < 0.01 (difference is ~14pp, substantial).
Answers: RQ2 (bilingual comparability) · novel finding for contribution claim
Effort: 2 hours · high thesis novelty impact
P5
Greenwashing index: expand to per-document per-prompt breakdown + cross-national comparison
Compute GW index at the intersection of document × prompt template to show whether the index is stable across extraction methods (a form of construct validity). Also formally compare the domestic Indonesian mean GW (+0.349) against the international ICR report (−0.151) and frame this as a cross-national finding.
tone_records_flat.csv document × prompt crosstab
Current: domestic mean GW = +0.349, international = −0.151, gap = +0.500.
Per-prompt stability: if GW index varies <0.15 across prompts for same doc → construct valid.
Answers: RQ3 (greenwashing signal) · RQ5 (result consistency)
Effort: 1 day · medium impact · good discussion material
